Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior for the entire bar.  
The bar is a stepped circular bar composed of two segments subjected to an axial tensile load of P = 35 000 N.  
Segment 1 (left) has length L₁ = 400 mm and diameter d₁ = 16 mm.  
Segment 2 (right) has length L₂ = 800 mm and diameter d₂ = 40 mm.  
Both segments are made of structural steel with Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa (the same for both segments).  
The axial stress in each segment is σᵢ = P / Aᵢ, where Aᵢ = π dᵢ² / 4 is the cross‑sectional area of segment i.  
The axial strain in each segment is εᵢ = σᵢ / E.  
The elongation of each segment is δᵢ = εᵢ · Lᵢ = (P Lᵢ) / (Aᵢ E).  
The total elongation of the stepped bar is the sum δ_total = δ₁ + δ₂.  
Present the stress values σ₁ and σ₂ and the total elongation δ_total using the given data and the governing relations above.
