Determine the midspan deflection of a simply supported Euler‑Bernoulli beam of length L = 2500 mm subjected to a point load P = 2500 N applied at the midspan.  
Determine the slope at each support of the same beam.  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior, constant cross‑section, and neglect shear deformation, beam self‑weight, axial loads, and temperature effects.  
Use Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa and second moment of area I = 1 200 000 mm⁴.  
Apply the governing relation for a simply supported beam with a central point load: midspan deflection δ = (P L³)/(48 E I).  
Apply the governing relation for the support slope: θ = (P L²)/(16 E I).
