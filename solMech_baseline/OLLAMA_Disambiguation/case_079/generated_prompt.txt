Assume a solid circular shaft of uniform cross‑section, made of a homogeneous isotropic material with shear modulus G = 76 923.1 MPa, undergoing small‑deformation linear‑elastic torsion. The shaft is fixed at one end and a torque T = 600 000 N·mm is applied at the other end. The shaft length is L = 800 mm. The maximum allowable angle of twist is φ_allow = 1.5° (convert to radians for calculations). Use the torsional relation φ = T L / (G J) to determine the required polar moment of inertia J. Then compute the shaft diameter d from J = π d⁴ / 32. Present the governing equations, the conversion of φ_allow to radians, the calculated J, and the resulting shaft diameter d that satisfies the angle‑of‑twist limit. State that no additional stress constraints are considered in this design.
