Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior for the column.  
The column is solid, circular with length L = 2000 mm and diameter d = 20 mm (radius r = 10 mm).  
End supports are pinned‑pinned, giving an effective length factor K = 1.0.  
Material is structural steel with Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa and yield strength σ_y = 250 MPa (used as the compressive strength).  
The column is loaded only by an axial compressive force; no bending or lateral loads are present.  
The moment of inertia of the circular cross‑section is I = π d⁴ / 64.  
Euler critical buckling load is P_cr = (π² E I) / (K L)².  
The allowable axial load is P_allow = P_cr / n, where the factor of safety n = 2.0.  
The factor of safety with respect to buckling is evaluated as n = P_cr / P_applied; using the assumed design load equal to P_allow gives n = 2.0.  
If a specific applied load is later provided, the actual safety factor can be computed by n_actual = P_cr / P_applied.
