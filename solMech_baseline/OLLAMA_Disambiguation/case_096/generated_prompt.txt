Use small‑deformation, linear‑elastic Euler‑Bernoulli beam theory.  
The beam is a prismatic cantilever of length L = 800 mm, fixed at one end and free at the other.  
The material is isotropic with Young’s modulus E = 69 000 MPa (69 000 N/mm²).  
The cross‑section has a constant second moment of area I = 800 000 mm⁴.  
A vertical point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end acting downward.  
No other loads or moments are present.  
The shear force distribution is V(x) = ‑P for 0 ≤ x ≤ L.  
The bending moment distribution is M(x) = ‑P (L ‑ x) for 0 ≤ x ≤ L.  
The slope of the deflection curve is θ(x) = ‑P (L x ‑ x²/2) / (E I).  
The deflection curve is v(x) = ‑P (L x²/2 ‑ x³/6) / (E I).  
The tip deflection is v(L) = ‑P L³ / (3 E I).  
The tip rotation is θ(L) = ‑P L² / (2 E I).  
The maximum normal bending stress is σ_max = M_max c / I, where M_max = P L and c is the distance from the neutral axis to the outer fiber.  
All results are expressed in consistent units (N, mm).
