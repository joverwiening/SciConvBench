Determine the axial stress in Bar 1 and Bar 2.  
Determine the axial strain (common to both bars) and the total axial elongation of the composite bar.  
Determine the load carried by each bar and the proportion of the total load taken by each bar.  
Determine the equivalent axial stiffness of the parallel bar assembly.  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior for both materials.  
Assume the two bars are perfectly bonded to the rigid plates so that they experience the same axial deformation (compatibility).  
Assume the plates are rigid and the axial tensile load of 20 000 N is applied uniformly to the assembly.  
Assume each bar has length L = 400 mm, cross‑sectional areas A₁ = 300 mm² and A₂ = 250 mm², and Young’s moduli E₁ = 200 000 MPa and E₂ = 110 000 MPa respectively.  
Use equilibrium: P = P₁ + P₂, where P₁ = σ₁A₁ and P₂ = σ₂A₂.  
Use compatibility: ε₁ = ε₂ = δ/L, where δ is the axial elongation.  
Use constitutive relations: σ₁ = E₁ ε, σ₂ = E₂ ε.  
From these relations compute ε = P / (E₁A₁ + E₂A₂), then σ₁, σ₂, δ = εL, P₁ = σ₁A₁, P₂ = σ₂A₂, and the equivalent stiffness k_eq = E₁A₁/L + E₂A₂/L.
