Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic, isotropic behavior of the rod. 
The material has Young’s modulus E = 105 000 MPa and Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.34. 
An axial tensile load P = 25 000 N is applied uniformly along the rod axis. 
The rod’s initial diameter is d₀ = 30 mm, giving a cross‑sectional area A = πd₀²/4 ≈ 7.07×10² mm². 
No lateral load is applied (lateral load = 0). 
The axial stress is σ = P/A. 
The axial strain is εₐₓ = σ/E. 
The lateral strain due to the Poisson effect is ε_lat = −ν εₐₓ. 
The resulting change in diameter is Δd = ε_lat · d₀. 
Assume uniform stress distribution, constant cross‑section, negligible temperature effects, and no body forces.
