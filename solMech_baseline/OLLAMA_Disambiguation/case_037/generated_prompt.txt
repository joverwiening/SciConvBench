Determine the deflection and slope of a simply supported beam under a uniformly distributed transverse load using small‑deformation, linear‑elastic beam theory.  
Assume the beam length is L = 4500 mm, the uniform load intensity is w = 0.3 N/mm, the Young’s modulus is E = 200 000 MPa, and the second moment of area is I = 2 000 000 mm⁴.  
No additional distributed loads are present.  
Use the Euler‑Bernoulli beam equation E I d⁴y/dx⁴ = w with boundary conditions y(0)=0, y(L)=0 (zero deflection at supports) and bending moment zero at x=0 and x=L.  
Derive the expressions for the slope θ(x)=dy/dx and deflection y(x) along the beam, and provide the maximum mid‑span deflection and slope.  
State any necessary assumptions explicitly, such as constant cross‑section, isotropic material, and neglect of shear deformation.
