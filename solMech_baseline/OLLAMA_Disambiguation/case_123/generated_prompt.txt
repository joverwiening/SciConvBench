Determine the thermal strain for a prismatic AISI 1020 rod that is fully restrained axially and subjected to a uniform temperature increase of ΔT = 50 °C.  
Assume small deformations, linear‑elastic isotropic behavior, uniform temperature distribution, and that the rod’s ends are fixed so that the total axial strain is zero.  
Use the coefficient of thermal expansion α = 2 × 10⁻⁵ °C⁻¹, Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa, and a circular cross‑section with diameter d = 20 mm (area A = πd²/4).  
Compute the free thermal strain ε_th = α ΔT.  
Since the rod is fully restrained, set the total axial strain ε_total = 0 and obtain the mechanical strain ε_mech = ε_total − ε_th = −ε_th.  
Calculate the induced axial stress σ_res = E ε_mech.  
Determine the reaction force due to thermal restraint F_res = σ_res A and compare it with the externally applied axial load F ≈ 62.8 kN.  
Present the governing relations clearly and state any additional assumptions required for the analysis.
