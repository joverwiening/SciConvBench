Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior for an isotropic rod made of material with Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa and Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.29.  
The rod has an initial length L = 1200 mm and an initial circular cross‑section with diameter d0 = 25 mm, giving an area A = π d0²/4 ≈ 4.91 × 10² mm².  
An axial tensile load P = 45 000 N is applied through the centroid of the rod; no lateral load is applied (lateral load = 0).  
The lateral surfaces of the rod are free, i.e., there are no lateral constraints.  
Compute the axial stress σ = P/A.  
Compute the axial strain ε = σ/E.  
Compute the lateral (transverse) strain ε_lat = –ν ε due to the Poisson effect.  
Determine the change in diameter Δd = ε_lat · d0 and the corresponding change in cross‑sectional area.  
Report the axial deformation ΔL = ε · L.  
Present all governing relations clearly and state any additional assumptions required for the analysis.
