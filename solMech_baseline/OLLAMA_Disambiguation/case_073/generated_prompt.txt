Determine the twist angle of a solid circular shaft using the energy method (Castigliano’s theorem).  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior.  
The shaft has a radius of 15 mm (diameter 30 mm).  
The shaft length is 1000 mm.  
The applied torque magnitude is 450 000 N·mm.  
The material shear modulus is 25 939.8 MPa.  
Assume the shaft is fixed (clamped) at one end and the torque is applied at the free end.  
Use the polar moment of inertia for a solid circular shaft: \(J = \pi r^{4}/2\).  
Compute the twist angle as \(\theta = \dfrac{T\,L}{G\,J}\).  
Report the twist angle in radians (dimensionless).  
Optionally, also compute the maximum shear stress \(\tau_{\max}=T\,r/J\) and the torsional strain energy \(U = \tfrac{1}{2}T\,\theta\).
