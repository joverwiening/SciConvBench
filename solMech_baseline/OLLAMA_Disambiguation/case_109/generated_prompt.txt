Determine the midspan vertical deflection of a simply supported beam subjected to a central point load using Castigliano’s theorem. Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior, isotropic material, constant cross‑section, and that shear deformation is negligible. The beam length is L = 2500 mm. The applied central point load is P = 1000 N. The Young’s modulus of the material is E = 200 000 MPa. The second moment of area of the cross‑section is I = 3 000 000 mm⁴. Use the bending moment diagram for a simply supported beam with a central point load to obtain the strain‑energy expression U = ∫ M²/(2EI) dx over the beam length. Apply Castigliano’s principle δ = ∂U/∂P to derive the governing relation for the midspan deflection and present the final expression, optionally evaluating the numerical deflection.
