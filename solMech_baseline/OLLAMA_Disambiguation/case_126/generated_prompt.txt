Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior for an isotropic, homogeneous solid circular shaft.  
Take the shaft radius as 15 mm, length as 1200 mm, and applied torque as 400 000 N·mm.  
Assume no transverse shear force is applied (shear force = 0).  
Assume one end of the shaft is fixed and the torque is applied at the other end.  
Use the polar moment of inertia for a solid circular shaft: J = π r⁴ / 2.  
Compute the maximum shear stress as τ_max = T · r / J.  
Compute the angle of twist as θ = T · L / (J · G), where G is the shear modulus of the material.  
State that the shear stress varies linearly from zero at the center to τ_max at the outer surface, and that the angle of twist is uniform along the shaft length.  
If material properties are required, denote them symbolically (e.g., G) and note that they must be supplied to obtain numerical values for τ_max and θ.
