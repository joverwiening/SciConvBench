Determine the cantilever beam deflection under a uniformly distributed transverse load using small‑deformation, linear‑elastic solid mechanics.  
Assume the beam is prismatic with constant flexural rigidity EI, the material follows Hooke’s law, and plane sections remain plane.  
The beam length is L = 1000 mm, the second moment of area is I = 2 000 000 mm⁴, Young’s modulus is E = 200 000 MPa, and the uniform load intensity is w = 1.5 N/mm.  
Use the bending moment distribution M(x) = ‑w (L ‑ x)² / 2 for 0 ≤ x ≤ L, with shear V(x) = w (L ‑ x).  
Apply the beam curvature relation EI d²y/dx² = M(x) and integrate twice, imposing the cantilever boundary conditions y(0) = 0 and dy/dx(0) = 0.  
From the integration obtain the deflection curve y(x) = w x² (6L² ‑ 4Lx + x²) / (24 EI).  
Compute the tip deflection y(L) = w L⁴ / (8 EI).  
Also compute the tip slope dy/dx|_{x=L} = w L³ / (6 EI).  
Report the tip deflection, tip slope, and the full deflection expression as the relevant response quantities.
