Assume small deformation and linear‑elastic behavior for the prismatic bar.  
The total length of the bar is L = 1400 mm, composed of segments AB = 700 mm, BC = 400 mm, and CD = 300 mm.  
The cross‑sectional area is A = 500 mm².  
The material Young’s modulus is E = 200 000 MPa.  
The left end A is fixed with prescribed displacement u_A = 0.  
The right end D is free except for the applied axial point load.  
A point load of +15 000 N acting to the right is applied at point B (x = 700 mm).  
A point load of –12 000 N acting to the left is applied at point C (x = 1100 mm).  
A point load of +10 000 N acting to the right is applied at point D (x = 1400 mm).  
Determine the axial force distribution N(x) along the bar, the normal stress σ(x) = N(x)/A, the axial strain ε(x) = σ(x)/E, and the axial displacement u(x) obtained by integrating ε(x) with the boundary condition u(0)=0.  
Present the governing relations clearly, including equilibrium of axial forces, stress‑strain law, and compatibility (displacement) condition.
