Consider a bar of length L = 800 mm with linearly varying cross‑sectional area A(x)=A₀(1 + k x/L) where A₀ = 250 mm² and k = 0.6.  
The material is linear‑elastic with Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa.  
A static axial tensile load P = 12 000 N is applied at the right end (x = L).  
The left end (x = 0) is fixed, i.e., axial displacement u(0)=0.  
Determine the axial deformation, strain, and stress distributions along the bar.  
Use equilibrium σ(x)=P/A(x), strain ε(x)=σ(x)/E, and integrate ε(x)=du/dx with the boundary condition u(0)=0 to obtain displacement u(x).  
Present the expressions for σ(x), ε(x), and u(x) and compute the total elongation Δ = u(L).
