Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior of an isotropic, homogeneous material.  
The rod is straight, prismatic, circular with diameter 25 mm and gage length 800 mm.  
The axial tensile load applied to the rod is 20 000 N.  
The material Young’s modulus is 200 000 MPa.  
The material yield strength is 250 MPa.  
Compute the average normal stress in the rod using σ = P / A where A = π d² /4.  
Compute the average axial strain using ε = σ / E.  
Compute the total elongation of the gage length using ΔL = ε L.  
Evaluate the factor of safety against yielding using FS = σ_yield / σ.  
State all governing relations and the numerical results for stress, strain, elongation, and factor of safety.  
Assume temperature effects are negligible and that the load is applied axially and uniformly.
