Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior of the column.  
The material Young’s modulus is E = 200 000 MPa.  
The column length is L = 2000 mm.  
The second moment of area of the cross‑section is I = 800 000 mm⁴.  
The end conditions are fixed‑fixed, giving an effective length factor K = 0.5.  
The Euler critical buckling load is Pcr = (π² E I) / (K L)².  
Substituting the values: Pcr = (π² × 200 000 MPa × 800 000 mm⁴) / (0.5 × 2000 mm)² ≈ 1.58 × 10⁶ N.  
A buckling/instability check is performed by comparing the applied compressive load Papp to Pcr; the column remains stable if Papp < Pcr and buckles if Papp ≥ Pcr.  
No additional loads, imperfections, or geometric nonlinearities are considered.  
All governing relations are presented as above.
