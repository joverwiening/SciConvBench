The maximum bending moment acting on the beam is M_max = 6 000 000 N·mm.  
A transverse load is not explicitly given; assume it is negligible for the bending‑stress design or that the moment already accounts for its effect.  
Design the beam height h such that the bending stress σ = M_max/Z does not exceed σ_allow, where the section modulus Z for a rectangular section is Z = b h²/6.  
Compute the required height from h = √(6 M_max / (b σ_allow)).  
Present the governing relation for bending stress, the expression for Z, and the calculated height h.
