Determine the midspan vertical deflection of a simply supported Euler‑Bernoulli beam subjected to a single point load of 2500 N applied at the midpoint.  
Determine the slope (rotation) of the beam at each support under the same loading.  
Assume the beam is prismatic with constant second moment of area I = 3 500 000 mm⁴ and Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa.  
Assume small deformations, linear‑elastic material behavior, and that shear deformation is negligible (Euler‑Bernoulli theory).  
Assume the beam length is L = 2500 mm and the supports are simple (pin and roller) providing no moment resistance.  
Use the governing relation for a simply supported beam with a central point load: deflection at midspan δ = (P L³)/(48 E I).  
Use the governing relation for the support slope θ = (P L²)/(16 E I).
