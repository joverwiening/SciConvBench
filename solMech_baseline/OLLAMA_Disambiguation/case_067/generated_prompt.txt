Determine the shear stress distribution and the angle of twist for a solid circular shaft.  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic, isotropic material behavior and that the shaft has a constant circular cross‑section along its length.  
The shaft length is L = 400 mm.  
The shaft diameter is d = 35 mm.  
The applied torque is T = 250 000 N·mm.  
The shear modulus of the material is G = 77 519.4 MPa.  
No transverse shear force is applied (shear force = 0 N).  
Use the polar moment of inertia J = π d⁴ / 32 for a solid circular section.  
Compute the shear stress at a radial position r as τ(r) = T r / J, with the maximum shear stress occurring at the outer surface r = d⁄2.  
Calculate the angle of twist θ over the length L as θ = T L / (J G).  
State all governing relations clearly and list any additional assumptions required for the analysis.
