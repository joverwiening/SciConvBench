Consider a simply supported beam of length 3000 mm with supports at A (x = 0) and B (x = 3000 mm).  
The beam is subjected only to a vertical downward point load P = 12000 N applied at x = 1500 mm (mid‑span).  
The beam’s self‑weight and any other loads are neglected.  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic solid mechanics.  
Use the sign convention: positive shear V upward on the left side of a cut, positive bending moment M sagging (tension in the bottom fibers).  
Express internal shear V in N and bending moment M in N·mm.  
Compute reactions at supports: RA = RB = P/2 = 6000 N upward.  
For 0 ≤ x < 1500 mm, internal shear V(x) = RA = 6000 N.  
For 1500 mm < x ≤ 3000 mm, internal shear V(x) = RA − P = −6000 N.  
For 0 ≤ x ≤ 1500 mm, internal bending moment M(x) = RA·x = 6000·x N·mm.  
For 1500 mm ≤ x ≤ 3000 mm, internal bending moment M(x) = RA·x − P·(x − 1500) = 6000·x − 12000·(x − 1500) N·mm.  
The maximum positive bending moment occurs at mid‑span x = 1500 mm and equals Mmax = 6000·1500 = 9.0 × 10⁶ N·mm.  
The shear diagram changes sign at the load location x = 1500 mm.
