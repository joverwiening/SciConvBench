Determine the midspan vertical deflection of a simply supported prismatic beam using Castigliano’s theorem.  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior and that the beam material is homogeneous and isotropic.  
Assume the beam has a constant second moment of area I along its length and is loaded by a single vertical point load P applied exactly at the midspan.  
Use the beam length L = 3000 mm, point load magnitude P = 2000 N, Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa, and second moment of area I = 3 000 000 mm⁴.  
Apply the governing relation for midspan deflection of a simply supported beam under a central point load derived from Castigliano’s theorem: δ = (P L³) / (48 E I).  
Present the derivation of the strain‑energy expression, the differentiation step, and the final deflection formula clearly.  
State any additional simplifying assumptions required (e.g., neglecting shear deformation, weight of the beam, and temperature effects).
