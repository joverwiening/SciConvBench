Assume a small‑deformation, linear‑elastic solid.  
Assume the column has a rectangular cross‑section with width b = 100 mm and height h = 250 mm.  
Assume the material is isotropic with Young’s modulus E = 69 000 MPa.  
Assume the column ends are fixed‑fixed, giving an effective length factor K = 0.5.  
Assume buckling will occur about the weak axis (the axis with the minimum second moment of area).  

Compute the second moment of area about the weak axis: I_min = b · h³ / 12 = 100 mm · (250 mm)³ / 12 = 2.0833 × 10⁷ mm⁴.  

The Euler critical buckling load is: P_cr = (π² · E · I_min) / (K · L)², where L = 3000 mm and K = 0.5, so the effective length is K · L = 1500 mm.  

State the governing relation for the buckling/instability check: the column is stable if the applied compressive load P_applied < P_cr; otherwise buckling is expected.  

If a safety factor is required, define it as SF = P_cr / P_applied.
