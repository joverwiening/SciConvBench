Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic solid mechanics for a rectangular beam.  
The beam cross‑section has width b = 40 mm and height h = 80 mm.  
The material is typical structural steel with Young’s modulus E = 200 GPa, Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.30, giving shear modulus G ≈ 77 GPa.  
A transverse shear force V = 25 kN acts on the beam.  
Evaluate the transverse shear stress τ at the point y = 20 mm above the neutral axis (y = 0).  

Use the governing relation for transverse shear stress in a rectangular section:  
τ(y) = (V · Q) / (I · b), where I = b h³ / 12 is the second moment of area about the neutral axis, and Q = A′ · ȳ′ is the first moment of the area above the point y (A′ is the area of the portion of the cross‑section above y and ȳ′ is the distance from its centroid to the neutral axis).  

For a rectangular section this simplifies to:  
τ(y) = (3 V) / (2 b h) · [1 – (2y / h)²].  

State any additional assumptions needed (e.g., beam is prismatic, loading is static, plane sections remain plane).  
Present the governing relation clearly and indicate that the transverse shear stress at y = 20 mm is to be computed using the above formula with the given numerical values.
