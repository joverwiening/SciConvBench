Determine the relevant response quantities for a hollow circular shaft under torsion, assuming small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior.  
Assume the shaft material is isotropic, homogeneous AISI 1020 steel with shear modulus \(G = 76\,923.1\ \text{MPa}\).  
Assume the shaft has a constant circular cross‑section, no axial loads, and the torque is applied as a pure torsional moment.  
Use the outer radius \(r_o = 30\ \text{mm}\) and inner radius \(r_i = 24\ \text{mm}\).  
The applied torque magnitude is \(T = 450{,}000\ \text{N·mm}\).  
The shaft length is \(L = 800\ \text{mm}\).  
Compute the polar moment of inertia \(J = \frac{\pi}{2}(r_o^4 - r_i^4)\).  
Compute the torsional rigidity \(GJ\).  
Determine the shear stress distribution \(\tau(r) = \frac{T\,r}{J}\) for \(r_i \le r \le r_o\).  
Find the maximum shear stress at the outer surface \(\tau_{\max} = \frac{T\,r_o}{J}\).  
Calculate the angle of twist \(\theta = \frac{T L}{G J}\).  
Present all governing relations clearly and list the resulting numerical values for \(J\), \(GJ\), \(\tau_{\max}\), and \(\theta\).
