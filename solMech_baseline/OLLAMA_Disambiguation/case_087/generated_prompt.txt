Determine the percent elongation using the relation % elongation = (Lf − L0)/L0 × 100 %.  
Determine the reduction of area using the relation % reduction = (A0 − Af)/A0 × 100 %.  
Assume the specimen deforms uniformly along the gauge length and that the measured final dimensions are taken after unloading.  
Assume small‑deformation linear‑elastic behavior up to the proportional limit, after which plastic deformation governs the ductility measures.  
Assume the material is isotropic and the cross‑section remains circular throughout the test.  
Use the given values: L0 = 50 mm, Lf = 66 mm, A0 ≈ 113.1 mm², Af ≈ 72.4 mm².
