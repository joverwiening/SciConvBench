Determine the bending stress distribution in a rectangular beam with cross‑section width b = 50 mm and height h = 100 mm subjected to a bending moment M = 3 000 000 N·mm.  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic, isotropic material behavior; plane sections remain plane; the neutral axis coincides with the centroid of the rectangular section.  
Compute the second moment of area I = b h³ / 12.  
Express the bending stress at any fiber located a distance y from the neutral axis as σ(y) = M y / I, with y measured positive toward the tensile side.  
State the maximum bending stress occurring at the extreme fibers (y = ±h/2) as σ_max = M (h/2) / I.  
Present the governing relation σ(y) = M y / I and the derived numerical values for I, the section modulus Z = I / (h/2), and σ_max.  
Include a brief note that the sign of σ indicates tension or compression according to the chosen sign convention.
