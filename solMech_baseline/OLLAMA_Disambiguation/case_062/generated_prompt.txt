Determine the thermal strain for a fully axially restrained prismatic AISI 1020 steel rod subjected to a uniform temperature increase of ΔT = 65 °C.  
Assume small deformations, linear‑elastic material behavior, uniform temperature distribution, and that both ends of the rod are fixed so that the total axial strain is zero.  
Use the rod diameter d = 35 mm to compute the cross‑sectional area A = πd²/4.  
Compute the thermal strain as ε_thermal = α ΔT with α = 1.0 × 10⁻⁵ °C⁻¹.  
Because the rod is fully restrained, the total strain ε_total = 0, so the mechanical strain must cancel the thermal strain: ε_mechanical = −ε_thermal.  
Relate the mechanical strain to the axial stress by Hooke’s law: ε_mechanical = σ/E, with E = 200 000 MPa.  
Obtain the axial stress σ = −E ε_thermal and the reaction force F = σ A, which should match the given value of approximately 1.25 × 10⁵ N (125 kN).  
Present the governing relations:  
ε_total = ε_thermal + ε_mechanical = 0.  
ε_thermal = α ΔT.  
ε_mechanical = σ/E.  
σ = F/A, A = πd²/4.  
Report the thermal strain, the induced mechanical strain, the resulting axial stress, and confirm the reaction force.
