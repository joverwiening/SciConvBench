Determine the transverse shear stress distribution in a rectangular beam with width b = 50 mm and height h = 80 mm under a shear force V = 20 000 N.  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior and that the material is typical structural steel with Young’s modulus E ≈ 200 GPa and Poisson’s ratio ν ≈ 0.30.  
Use the standard shear stress formula τ(y) = V Q(y) / (I b), where I = b h³/12 is the second moment of area and Q(y) = b ∫_y^{h/2} (h/2 − y′) dy′ is the first moment of the area above the point of interest.  
Evaluate τ at the location y = 35 mm measured upward from the neutral axis.  
Also compute the maximum transverse shear stress, which occurs at the neutral axis (y = 0).  
State any additional assumptions required for the analysis.
