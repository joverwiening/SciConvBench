Assume the bar is prismatic with constant cross‑section A = 200 mm² and length L = 3500 mm.  
The material is linear‑elastic with Young’s modulus E = 69 000 MPa and density ρ = 8900 kg/m³.  
The bar is fixed at its top end and free at the bottom; only its self‑weight acts.  
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².  
Convert all quantities to SI units (L = 3.5 m, A = 2.0 × 10⁻⁴ m², E = 6.9 × 10¹⁰ Pa).  
The axial force at a distance x from the top is F(x) = ρ A g (L − x).  
The axial stress distribution is σ(x) = F(x)/A = ρ g (L − x).  
The axial strain is ε(x) = σ(x)/E.  
The total elongation is δ = ∫₀ᴸ ε(x) dx = ρ g L²/(2E).  
Present the final elongation δ in millimetres.
