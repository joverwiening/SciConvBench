Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior for an isotropic, homogeneous 6061‑T6 aluminum rod.  
The rod is straight, prismatic, and has a circular cross‑section with a diameter of 30 mm and a gauge length of 800 mm.  
An axial tensile load of 15 000 N is applied uniformly at the ends, producing a uniform axial stress distribution.  

Use the governing relations:  
- Cross‑sectional area \(A = \pi d^{2}/4\).  
- Average normal stress \(\sigma = P/A\).  
- Axial strain \(\varepsilon = \sigma/E\).  
- Elongation \(\Delta L = \varepsilon L\).  
- Factor of safety with respect to yielding \(FS = \sigma_{y}/\sigma\), where \(\sigma_{y}=275\) MPa.  

Neglect temperature effects, residual stresses, and any buckling or lateral loads.  
Report the computed average normal stress in MPa, the axial strain (dimensionless), the elongation in mm, and the factor of safety.
