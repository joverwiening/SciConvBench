Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior and that plane sections remain plane.  
The bar has a rectangular cross‑section with width b = 30 mm and height h = 100 mm.  
An axial tension load P = 50 000 N is applied along the longitudinal axis.  
A transverse load V = 5 000 N is applied perpendicular to the longitudinal axis.  
A transverse shear load V = 5 000 N is applied on the cross‑section.  
The cross‑sectional area is A = b · h.  
Average normal stress at any section is σ̄ = P / A.  
Average shear stress at any section is τ̄ = V / A.  
The transverse load produces a bending moment about the neutral axis, but the average normal stress due to bending over the whole section is zero, so only the axial load contributes to the average normal stress.  
Use the governing relations σ̄ = P / (b h) and τ̄ = V / (b h) to compute the required response quantities.
