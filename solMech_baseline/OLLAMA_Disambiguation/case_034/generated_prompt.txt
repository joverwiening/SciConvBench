Compute the mid‑span vertical deflection of a simply supported Euler‑Bernoulli beam of length 3000 mm subjected to a central point load of 4000 N.  
Compute the slope (rotation) at each support of the same beam.  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior.  
Use Young’s modulus of 69 000 MPa and a second moment of area of 3 500 000 mm⁴.  
Assume no additional shear or distributed loads.  
Apply the standard beam bending formulas: δ = P L³ / (48 E I) for mid‑span deflection and θ = P L² / (16 E I) for support slope.  
Present the numerical results in millimetres for deflection and radians for slope.
