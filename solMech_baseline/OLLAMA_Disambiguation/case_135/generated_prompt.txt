Determine the shear stress in the adhesive layer using τ = V / A.  
Assume the adhesive layer is a rectangular plate 40 mm × 100 mm with thickness t = 20 mm, giving cross‑sectional area A = 4000 mm².  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior and that the bonded bodies are rigid, resulting in a uniform shear stress distribution.  
Treat the adhesive as isotropic with shear modulus G = 39 179.1 MPa and assume a Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.35.  
Compute the Young’s modulus as E = 2 G (1 + ν).  
Calculate the shear strain as γ = τ / G.  
Obtain the shear deformation (relative displacement) of the adhesive layer as δ = γ · t = V t / (A G).  
State that temperature effects are neglected.
