Determine the Euler critical buckling load for a cantilever (fixed–free) column with length L = 1000 mm, Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa, and second moment of area I = 1 200 000 mm⁴.  
Assume the column has a solid circular cross‑section.  
Compute the circular diameter d from I using I = π d⁴/64, then compute the cross‑sectional area A = π d²/4.  
Use the effective length factor K = 2 for fixed–free end conditions, so the effective length Le = K L = 2 L.  
Calculate the Euler critical load as Pcr = π² E I / Le² = π² E I / (4 L²).  
State the governing relation for buckling instability check: the column is stable if the applied axial compressive load P satisfies P < Pcr; otherwise buckling occurs.
