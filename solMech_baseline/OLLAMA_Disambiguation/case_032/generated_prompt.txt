Determine the midspan deflection and the support slope of a simply supported Euler‑Bernoulli beam subjected only to a central point load.  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior, a prismatic beam with constant second moment of area, no shear loading, and neglect shear deformation effects.  
Use the given parameters: point load magnitude P = 1500 N, beam length L = 3000 mm, Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa, second moment of area I = 3 500 000 mm⁴.  
Apply the governing relations for a simply supported beam with a midspan point load: midspan deflection δ = (P L³)/(48 E I); support slope θ = (P L²)/(16 E I).  
Report the midspan deflection in millimetres to three significant figures (or the nearest 0.01 mm) and the support slope in radians to three significant figures (or the nearest 0.0001 rad).
