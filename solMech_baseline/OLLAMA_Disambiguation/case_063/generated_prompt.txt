Determine the thermal strain for a prismatic rod that is fully restrained axially under a uniform temperature increase of ΔT = 35 °C.  
Assume the material has a coefficient of thermal expansion α = 1.0 × 10⁻⁵ °C⁻¹ and Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa.  
The rod has a circular cross‑section with diameter d = 20 mm, giving an area A = πd²/4.  
Because axial displacement is fully restrained, the total axial strain ε_total = 0.  
Thus the mechanical strain ε_mech must cancel the free thermal strain ε_th = αΔT, giving ε_mech = −αΔT.  
The resulting axial stress is σ = E ε_mech = −E α ΔT.  
The reaction (axial) force required to enforce the restraint is P = σ A, which evaluates to approximately 22 kN.  
State these governing relations clearly and note that no axial deformation occurs due to the full restraint.
