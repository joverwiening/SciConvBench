Determine the axial response of a prismatic bar subjected to multiple axial point loads.  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior, one‑dimensional axial loading only, and neglect temperature effects, shear deformation, and any transverse loads.  
The bar consists of three contiguous segments: AB = 500 mm, BC = 600 mm, and CD = 400 mm, giving a total length L = 1500 mm.  
The cross‑sectional area is constant at A = 300 mm².  
The material Young’s modulus is E = 200 000 MPa.  
Apply the following axial point loads: 18 kN acting to the right at point B, –12 kN (12 kN acting to the left) at point C, and 16 kN acting to the right at point D.  
Boundary conditions: the left end A is fixed (axial displacement u_A = 0); the right end D is free except for the applied 16 kN load.  
Compute the internal axial force distribution, axial stress, axial strain, and axial displacement field along the bar, and present the governing equilibrium and compatibility relations clearly.
