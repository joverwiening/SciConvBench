Determine the response of a cantilever beam using Euler‑Bernoulli beam theory under small‑deformation, linear‑elastic conditions. 
Assume the beam has a constant rectangular cross‑section, negligible self‑weight, and no thermal effects. 
The beam length is L = 800 mm. 
A vertical point load P = 2500 N is applied at the free end. 
The material Young’s modulus is E = 69 000 MPa. 
The second moment of area of the cross‑section is I = 1 600 000 mm⁴. 
Use the governing differential equation EI d²w/dx² = M(x) with bending moment M(x) = ‑P(L ‑ x) for 0 ≤ x ≤ L. 
Integrate to obtain the slope θ(x) and deflection w(x) subject to the cantilever boundary conditions w(0)=0 and θ(0)=0. 
Calculate the tip deflection w(L) and tip rotation θ(L). 
Determine the shear force distribution V(x) = ‑P (constant) and bending moment distribution M(x) as given. 
Compute the maximum bending stress σ_max = M_max c/I, where c is the distance from the neutral axis to the outer fiber. 
Present all governing relations, intermediate expressions, and final numerical results for tip deflection, tip rotation, shear, moment, and maximum stress.
