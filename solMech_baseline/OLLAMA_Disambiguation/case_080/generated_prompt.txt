Determine the shaft diameter d such that the angle of twist φ does not exceed the allowable value φ_allow = 0.0131 rad over the length L = 1500 mm when subjected to a torque T = 1,000,000 N·mm. Use the torsion relation φ = T L / (J G) where the polar moment of inertia for a solid circular shaft is J = π d⁴ / 32 and the shear modulus is G = 25 939.8 MPa. Solve for d from d = [ (32 T L) / (π G φ_allow) ]¹⁄⁴. Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior, isotropic material, uniform circular cross‑section, pure torsion without bending, no stress concentrations, and neglect temperature effects. State the resulting diameter and verify that the corresponding shear stress τ = T c / J (with c = d/2) is within the material’s allowable shear stress if needed.
