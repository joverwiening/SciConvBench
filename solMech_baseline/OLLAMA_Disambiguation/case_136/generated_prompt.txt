Determine the internal shear force and bending moment distributions for a simply supported beam of span 6000 mm.  
The beam is subjected to a single vertical point load of magnitude 20000 N applied downward at a distance of 2000 mm from the left support.  
No axial tension load is applied to the beam.  
No external moment (point or distributed) is applied to the beam.  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior, neglect beam self‑weight, and treat the beam as prismatic with constant flexural rigidity.  
Apply static equilibrium to find the support reactions: the left reaction equals (P·(L‑a))/L and the right reaction equals (P·a)/L, where a = 2000 mm.  
Define the shear force V(x) as piecewise: for 0 ≤ x < a, V(x)=R_A; for a < x ≤ L, V(x)=R_A‑P.  
Define the bending moment M(x) as piecewise: for 0 ≤ x < a, M(x)=R_A·x; for a < x ≤ L, M(x)=R_A·x‑P·(x‑a).  
Present the governing relations: equilibrium equations ΣF_y=0, ΣM=0, the shear‑moment relationship dM/dx = V, and the curvature‑moment relation M/EI = d²w/dx².  
State that the internal shear and bending moment are obtained directly from the above piecewise expressions.
