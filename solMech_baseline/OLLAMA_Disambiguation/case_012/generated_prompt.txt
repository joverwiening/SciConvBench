Determine the required beam height h for a simply supported rectangular beam with width b = 30 mm, span L = 2000 mm, subjected to a central point load P = 3000 N, made of 6061‑T6 aluminum (Young’s modulus E = 69 000 MPa, yield stress σ_y = 276 MPa) and a factor of safety FS = 2.0, such that the maximum deflection does not exceed δ_allow = 3 mm and the maximum bending stress does not exceed the allowable stress σ_allow = σ_y/FS = 138 MPa.  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior and use Euler‑Bernoulli beam theory.  
Assume no other loads are present on the beam.  
Use the moment of inertia I = b h³/12 and the section modulus S = b h²/6 for the rectangular cross‑section.  
Compute the maximum bending moment as M_max = P L/4 and the corresponding maximum bending stress as σ_max = M_max/S.  
Compute the maximum deflection as δ_max = P L³/(48 E I).  
Solve for the height h that satisfies σ_max ≤ σ_allow and δ_max ≤ δ_allow, and adopt the larger h obtained from the two criteria.  
Present the governing equations, the calculated required height h, and verify that both the deflection and stress limits are met.
