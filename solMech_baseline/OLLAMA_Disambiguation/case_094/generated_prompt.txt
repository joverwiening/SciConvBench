Consider a simply supported Euler‑Bernoulli beam of span L = 3500 mm.  
The beam is subjected to a uniformly distributed transverse load w = 0.3 N/mm over the entire span.  
A point load P = 1200 N is applied at the mid‑span (x = L/2).  
No other distributed loads are present; assume none.  
The beam has constant flexural rigidity EI = 6.0 × 10¹¹ N·mm².  
Use linear‑elastic small‑deformation theory and superposition to determine the maximum vertical deflection at mid‑span.  
Compute the deflection contribution from the uniform load using the standard simply supported beam formula.  
Compute the deflection contribution from the mid‑span point load using the standard simply supported beam formula.  
Sum the two contributions to obtain the total maximum deflection.  
Present the governing differential equation d⁴v/dx⁴ = q(x)/(EI) and the boundary conditions v(0)=v(L)=0, M(0)=M(L)=0, and the final numerical value of the mid‑span deflection.
