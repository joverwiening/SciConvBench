Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior for the AISI steel rod. 
Use Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa and Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.30. 
The rod has an initial diameter d₀ = 20 mm giving a cross‑sectional area A = 100π mm² ≈ 314.2 mm². 
Apply an axial tensile load P = 35 000 N and no lateral load (lateral load = 0). 
Assume the load is applied uniformly and the rod is prismatic with constant cross‑section. 
Determine the axial stress σ = P/A. 
Compute the axial strain ε = σ/E. 
Compute the transverse strain ε_t = –ν ε due to the Poisson effect. 
Determine the change in diameter Δd = ε_t d₀. 
Present the governing relations σ = E ε, ε_t = –ν ε, and Δd = ε_t d₀. 
Neglect temperature effects and assume the rod ends are free to contract laterally.
