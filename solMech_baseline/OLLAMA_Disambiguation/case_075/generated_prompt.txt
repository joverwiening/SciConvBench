Assume the adhesive layer is a rectangular plate with length 80 mm and width 50 mm, giving an area A = 4000 mm² and thickness t = 20 mm.  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior for the adhesive.  
Assume the material shear modulus is G = 39 179.1 MPa; if needed, Young’s modulus and Poisson’s ratio can be taken as E = 2G(1+ν) with ν ≈ 0.35.  
Assume a uniform in‑plane shear force V = 8000 N is applied, producing a uniform average shear stress τ = V/A across the adhesive layer.  
Use the linear‑elastic shear constitutive relation τ = G γ to obtain the average shear strain γ = τ/G.  
Compute the shear deformation (relative displacement) δ = γ t = (τ t)/G.  
Present the governing equations τ = V/A, γ = τ/G, and δ = γ t, and report the numerical values of τ, γ, and δ based on the given data.
