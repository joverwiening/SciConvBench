Assume a small‑deformation, linear‑elastic Euler‑Bernoulli beam model.  
Assume the beam material is isotropic with Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa.  
Assume the beam has a rectangular cross‑section whose second moment of area is I = 1 200 000 mm⁴.  
Consider a cantilever beam of length L = 1800 mm fixed at one end and free at the other.  
Apply a vertical point load P = 2000 N at the free end; no additional moments or loads are present.  
Neglect shear deformation and axial effects.  
Use the governing relation for tip deflection of a cantilever under an end point load: δ = P L³ / (3 E I).  
Compute the deflection δ in millimeters (mm) using the given values.  
Present the final deflection value in mm and list any intermediate quantities if needed.
