Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior for an isotropic, homogeneous material.  
The shaft is a hollow circular cylinder with outer radius 25 mm and inner radius 22 mm.  
The shaft length is 800 mm.  
A constant torque of 600 000 N·mm is applied; no transverse shear force is present.  
The material shear modulus is 77519.4 MPa.  
Use the polar moment of inertia \(J = \frac{\pi}{2}\,(r_o^{4}-r_i^{4})\).  
Compute the shear stress distribution as \(\tau(r)=\frac{T\,r}{J}\) for \(r_i \le r \le r_o\).  
Determine the maximum shear stress at the outer surface using \(\tau_{\max}= \frac{T\,r_o}{J}\).  
Calculate the angle of twist over the length as \(\phi = \frac{T\,L}{G\,J}\).  
Assume the torque is applied uniformly along the shaft and the ends are free to rotate (no additional constraints).  
Present the governing relations clearly and state any additional assumptions made.
