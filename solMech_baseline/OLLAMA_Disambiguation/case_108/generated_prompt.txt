Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior for the beam. 
Consider a simply supported beam of length L = 3500 mm. 
The beam is subjected to a uniformly distributed transverse load w = 0.5 N/mm. 
No additional distributed load is present (assume zero). 
The material Young’s modulus is E = 200 000 MPa. 
The second moment of area of the cross‑section is I = 1 500 000 mm⁴. 
Determine the bending moment distribution, shear force distribution, slope, and deflection along the beam. 
Use Euler‑Bernoulli beam theory with the governing relation EI d²y/dx² = M(x). 
For a simply supported beam under uniform load, the bending moment is M(x) = w x (L − x)/2. 
Integrate the moment equation to obtain the slope θ(x) = dy/dx and the deflection y(x), applying the boundary conditions y(0) = y(L) = 0. 
Provide the analytical expressions for the maximum deflection at mid‑span and the maximum slope at the supports. 
Present all results in consistent units (deflection in mm, slope in radians).
