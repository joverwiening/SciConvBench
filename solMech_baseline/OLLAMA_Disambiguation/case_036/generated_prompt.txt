Design a solid circular column of structural steel with Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa and assumed yield stress σ_y = 250 MPa. 
The column length is L = 1800 mm and the effective‑length factor is K = 1.0 (pinned‑pinned ends). 
The column must support an axial compressive load P = 30 000 N. 
The required factor of safety against buckling is n = 2.0. 
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior and that buckling is governed by Euler’s formula for a pinned‑pinned column. 
Use the Euler critical load P_cr = (π² E I)/(K L)², where I = π d⁴/64 for a solid circular cross‑section of diameter d. 
Select the minimum diameter d such that the design load P multiplied by the safety factor n does not exceed the Euler critical load (n P ≤ P_cr). 
Calculate the factor of safety as FS = P_cr / P and verify that FS ≥ 2.0. 
Present the governing relations, the chosen diameter, and the computed factor of safety. 
State that the column is assumed to remain within the elastic range and that material yielding is not reached under the design load.
