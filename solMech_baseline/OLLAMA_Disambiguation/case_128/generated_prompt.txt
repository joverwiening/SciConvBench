Determine the shear stress and angle of twist for a solid circular shaft of length 600 mm and diameter 50 mm.  
Assume the shaft is made of structural steel with shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.  
Assume the shaft is subjected only to a torque of 500 000 N·mm and no transverse shear force.  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior, isotropic homogeneous material, and a uniform cross‑section.  
Use the torsion formula τ = T·c / J, where c = d⁄2 and J = π d⁴⁄32, to compute the maximum shear stress.  
Use the angle‑of‑twist relation θ = T·L / (J·G) to compute the twist over the shaft length.  
Present the governing relations clearly and state all assumptions used in the analysis.
