Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior.  
The shaft is a solid circular shaft with length L = 1000 mm and radius r = 15 mm (diameter = 30 mm).  
The material is AISI 1020 steel with shear modulus G = 76 923.1 MPa.  
The applied torque is T = 250 000 N·mm and there is no transverse shear force (0 N).  
The shaft is fixed against rotation at one end and free at the other (cantilever condition).  
Compute the shear stress using τ = T·r / J, where the polar moment of inertia J = π r⁴ / 2.  
Compute the angle of twist using φ = T·L / (J·G).  
Report the shear stress in MPa and the angle of twist in degrees.
