Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior.  
The bar has a rectangular cross‑section with width b = 40 mm and height h = 120 mm.  
The axial tension force applied to the bar is P = 30 000 N.  
The transverse shear force applied to the bar is V = 12 000 N.  
The bending moment acting on the bar is assumed to be zero.  
The length of the bar is taken as L = 1000 mm (1 m).  
The cross‑sectional area is A = b h.  
The average normal stress at any section is σ̄ = P / A.  
The average shear stress at any section is τ̄ = V / A.  
No other loads, moments, or material‑specific effects are considered.
