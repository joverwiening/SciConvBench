Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior for the shaft.  
The shaft is a solid circular bar with radius = 10 mm (diameter = 20 mm) and length = 1000 mm.  
The material is AISI 1020 steel with shear modulus G = 76 923.1 MPa and yield strength σ_y ≈ 350 MPa.  
The applied torque is T ≈ 39.8 kN·mm (≈ 39.8 N·m) and the transmitted power is P = 5 kW at angular speed ω = 2π·(1200/60) = 40π rad/s.  
Power–torque conversion is verified by the relation P = T·ω.  
The polar moment of inertia for a solid circular section is J = π d⁴/32.  
Maximum shear stress due to torsion is τ_max = T·c / J = 16 T / (π d³), where c = radius.  
Allowable shear stress for the safety check is τ_allow = σ_y / SF = 350 MPa / 2.0 = 175 MPa.  
The torsional safety check requires τ_max ≤ τ_allow.  
Angle of twist over the shaft length is θ = T L / (J G).  
Twist per unit length is dθ/dx = T / (J G).  
Assume the shaft has a uniform cross‑section, no stress concentrations, isotropic material properties, and temperature effects are negligible.
