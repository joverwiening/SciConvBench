Assume a prismatic cantilever beam of length 1500 mm with a constant cross‑section.  
The beam material is linear‑elastic with Young’s modulus 200 000 MPa.  
The second moment of area of the cross‑section is 800 000 mm⁴.  
A vertical point load of 500 N is applied at the free end of the cantilever.  
Adopt small‑deformation theory and neglect shear deformation, axial effects, and temperature changes.  
Use Castigliano’s theorem to determine the end deflection by differentiating the strain‑energy with respect to the applied load.  
The bending moment distribution is M(x)=P·(L−x) for 0 ≤ x ≤ L.  
The strain‑energy due to bending is U=∫₀ᴸ M(x)²/(2EI) dx.  
The end deflection δ is given by δ=∂U/∂P, which simplifies to δ = P L³/(3 E I).  
Present the governing relations clearly and state all assumptions used.
