Determine the shear stress distribution and the angle of twist for a solid circular shaft.  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior, isotropic material, constant circular cross‑section, and pure torsion with no axial loads.  
The shaft has a diameter of 45 mm (radius 22.5 mm) and a length of 1000 mm.  
The applied torque is 400 000 N·mm.  
The material shear modulus is 76 923.1 MPa (N/mm²).  
Use the polar moment of inertia J = π d⁴ / 32 for a solid circular section.  
Compute the shear stress at a radial position r as τ(r) = T r / J, with the maximum shear stress τ_max = T c / J where c is the outer radius.  
Calculate the angle of twist φ = T L / (J G).  
Present the governing relations clearly and state any additional assumptions required for the analysis.
