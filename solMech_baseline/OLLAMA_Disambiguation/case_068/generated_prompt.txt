Determine the shear stress distribution in a solid circular shaft of radius 15 mm subjected to a torque of 500 000 N·mm, assuming no additional shear force. 
Determine the angle of twist of the shaft over its length of 800 mm under the same torque. 
Assume the shaft material is homogeneous, isotropic 6061‑T6 aluminum with shear modulus G = 25 939.8 MPa. 
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior and that temperature effects are negligible. 
Use the polar moment of inertia J = π r⁴ / 2 for a solid circular cross‑section. 
Compute the maximum shear stress as τ_max = T r / J. 
Compute the angle of twist as θ = T L / (J G). 
State all governing relations clearly and present the final numerical values for τ_max (in MPa) and θ (in radians or degrees).
