Determine the cantilever beam deflection under a uniformly distributed transverse load using small‑deformation, linear‑elastic assumptions.  
Assume the beam is prismatic, the material is homogeneous and isotropic, plane sections remain plane, and shear deformation is neglected (Euler‑Bernoulli beam theory).  
The beam length is L = 2000 mm, the uniform load intensity is w = 1.0 N/mm acting downward, the Young’s modulus is E = 200 000 MPa, and the second moment of area is I = 1 500 000 mm⁴.  
Use the governing differential equation E I d⁴y/dx⁴ = w for a uniformly distributed load on a cantilever.  
Apply the cantilever boundary conditions: at the fixed end (x = 0) deflection y = 0 and slope dy/dx = 0; at the free end (x = L) bending moment M = 0 and shear V = 0.  
Derive the deflection curve:  
y(x) = [w x² (6L² − 4L x + x²)] / (24 E I).  
Compute the tip deflection (maximum deflection) at x = L:  
δ_max = w L⁴ / (8 E I).  
Present the governing relations, the derived deflection expression, and the numerical value of the tip deflection using the given parameters.
