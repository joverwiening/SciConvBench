Determine the torsional response of a solid circular shaft of length 1200 mm and radius 15 mm made of AISI 1020 steel with shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa. Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior and that the left end of the shaft is fixed (no rotation). Apply a torque T₁ = +200 000 N·mm at the free right end (x = 1200 mm) and a torque T₂ = ‑200 000 N·mm at x = 400 mm from the left end. Use the polar moment of inertia J = π r⁴/2 for a solid circular cross‑section. Compute the internal torque distribution along the shaft, the resulting shear stress τ(r) = T·r/J, the maximum shear stress at the outer surface, and the angle of twist θ(x) obtained by integrating dθ/dx = T(x)/(G J). State any additional assumptions such as neglecting axial loads, bending, and stress concentrations. Present the governing relations clearly.
