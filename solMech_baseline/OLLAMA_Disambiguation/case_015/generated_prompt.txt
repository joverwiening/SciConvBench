Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior and apply Euler‑Bernoulli beam theory.  
Consider a cantilever beam of constant rectangular cross‑section, length L = 1200 mm, fixed at one end and free at the other.  
A vertical point load P = 2000 N is applied at the free end; no other loads or moments are considered.  
The beam material has Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa and second moment of area I = 2 000 000 mm⁴.  
Neglect shear deformation and axial effects.  
Use the governing relation d²v/dx² = M/(E I), where the bending moment M(x) = ‑P·(L ‑ x) for 0 ≤ x ≤ L.  
Integrate the moment curvature equation to obtain the slope θ(x) and deflection v(x), applying the boundary conditions v(0)=0 and θ(0)=0 at the fixed end.  
The tip deflection is v(L) = P L³ / (3 E I).  
The tip rotation is θ(L) = P L² / (2 E I).  
Present the governing equations, the derived expressions for slope and deflection along the beam, and compute the numerical values of tip deflection and rotation using the given parameters.
