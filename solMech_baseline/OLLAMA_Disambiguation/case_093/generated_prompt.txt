Determine the vertical deflection at the point load location (2000 mm from the left support) of a simply supported prismatic beam of total length 3000 mm using the unit‑load (virtual work) method.  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior with a constant Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa and a constant second moment of area I (to be specified by the user or taken as given).  
Assume the beam has no distributed loading and no applied moment; the only external load is a vertical point load P = 800 N applied at the specified location.  
Neglect shear deformation and axial effects; use Euler‑Bernoulli beam theory.  
Compute the bending moment diagram for the real loading, then apply a unit vertical load at the same point to obtain the virtual bending moment diagram.  
Use the unit‑load method integral δ = ∫₀ᴸ (M·m)/(E I) dx, where M(x) is the real bending moment, m(x) is the virtual bending moment due to the unit load, and integrate over the beam length.  
Present the governing relations, the expressions for M(x) and m(x) in each beam segment, and the final integral expression for the deflection at the point load location.  
State any additional required beam property (e.g., I) and note that the numerical evaluation of the integral will yield the deflection value.
