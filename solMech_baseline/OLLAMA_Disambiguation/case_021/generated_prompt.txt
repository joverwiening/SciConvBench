Determine the stress distribution and total angle of twist for a stepped solid circular shaft under the given torque.  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior and that the shaft material is homogeneous with shear modulus G = 25 939.8 MPa.  
Assume no external shear force is applied (shear force = 0).  
Segment AB has length L₁ = 300 mm and radius r₁ = 10 mm; its polar moment of inertia is J₁ = π r₁⁴ / 2.  
Segment BC has length L₂ = 500 mm and radius r₂ = 15 mm; its polar moment of inertia is J₂ = π r₂⁴ / 2.  
The applied torque is constant along the shaft, T = 300 000 N·mm.  
For each segment, the shear stress distribution is τ(r) = T · r / J, where r is the radial coordinate within that segment.  
The maximum shear stress in each segment occurs at its outer radius: τ_max,AB = T · r₁ / J₁, τ_max,BC = T · r₂ / J₂.  
The angle of twist for each segment is θ_i = T · L_i / (J_i · G).  
The total angle of twist of the shaft is the sum θ_total = θ_AB + θ_BC.  
Present the calculated shear stress values for each segment and the total twist in consistent units.
