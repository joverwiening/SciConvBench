Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior for a homogeneous isotropic rectangular beam.  
The beam cross‑section has width b = 50 mm and height h = 120 mm.  
The applied bending moment is M = 4 000 000 N·mm about the axis perpendicular to the width.  
The neutral axis coincides with the centroid of the cross‑section; the y‑coordinate is measured from the neutral axis positive upward.  
The second moment of area of the rectangular section about the neutral axis is I = b h³ / 12 = 7 200 000 mm⁴.  
The bending stress distribution follows σ(y) = – M y / I.  
At the centroid (y = 0 mm) the bending stress is σ = 0 N/mm².  
At the top fiber (y = +60 mm) the bending stress is σ = –33.33 N/mm² (compressive).  
At the bottom fiber (y = –60 mm) the bending stress is σ = +33.33 N/mm² (tensile).
