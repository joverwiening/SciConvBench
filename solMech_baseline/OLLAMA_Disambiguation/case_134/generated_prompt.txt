Consider a rectangular adhesive layer with area 3000 mm² and thickness 8 mm.  
Apply an in‑plane shear force V = 12 000 N uniformly across the layer.  
Assume the shear stress is uniform: τ = V / A.  
Use shear modulus G = 39 179.1 MPa.  
Assume Young’s modulus E ≈ 3000 MPa and Poisson’s ratio ν ≈ 0.35 for any auxiliary calculations.  
Treat the two bonded bodies as rigid plates.  
Adopt small‑deformation, linear‑elastic assumptions.  
Compute shear strain γ = τ / G (dimensionless).  
Compute shear deformation Δ = γ × t and report Δ in mm.  
Report shear strain as a dimensionless quantity (may be expressed in microstrain) and shear deformation in mm.
