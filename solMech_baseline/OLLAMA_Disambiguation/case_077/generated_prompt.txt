Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior.  
Assume the material is structural steel with Young’s modulus E ≈ 210 GPa and Poisson’s ratio ν ≈ 0.30.  
The bar has a rectangular cross‑section of width b = 50 mm and height h = 120 mm.  
The axial tensile force applied to the bar is P = 40 000 N.  
The transverse shear force applied to the bar is V = 15 000 N.  
The cross‑sectional area is A = b · h.  
The average normal stress at the section is σ̄ = P / A.  
The average shear stress at the section is τ̄ = V / A.  
Use the governing relations σ̄ = P / (b h) and τ̄ = V / (b h).  
No bending moment is considered, so the normal stress distribution is uniform across the section.
