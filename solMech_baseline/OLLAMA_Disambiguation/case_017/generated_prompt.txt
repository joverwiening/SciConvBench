Determine the deflection of a simply supported Euler‑Bernoulli beam of length L = 3000 mm subjected to a uniformly distributed transverse load w = 0.3 N/mm acting over the entire span and a point load P = 1200 N applied at the midspan (x = 1500 mm). Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior with constant flexural rigidity EI throughout the beam. Neglect shear deformation and axial effects, and treat the material as isotropic and homogeneous. Use the governing differential equation EI d⁴v/dx⁴ = q(x), where q(x) = w + P δ(x − L/2). Apply superposition by solving separately for the uniform load and the point load, then add the two deflection contributions to obtain the total deflection v(x). Compute the support reactions, shear force diagram, and bending moment diagram for each loading case as intermediate steps. Express the final deflection curve v(x) in terms of x, w, P, L, and EI, and provide the maximum deflection value and its location. State that the results are valid only under the stated assumptions and for the given loading configuration.
