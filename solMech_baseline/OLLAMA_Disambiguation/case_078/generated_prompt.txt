Design a solid circular shaft of diameter d such that the angle of twist does not exceed φ_allow = 0.01745 rad under a torque T = 800 000 N·mm applied over a length L = 1200 mm. Assume the shaft material is isotropic, homogeneous, and behaves as a linear‑elastic solid with shear modulus G = 77 519.4 MPa. Use the torsion relation φ = TL/(J G) where the polar moment of inertia for a solid circular shaft is J = π d⁴/32. Solve for the required diameter: d = [ (16 T L) / (π G φ_allow) ]^(1/3). State that small‑deformation theory applies, the torque is constant along the shaft, and the shaft is fixed at one end and free at the other (or both ends appropriately restrained) so that the angle‑of‑twist formula is valid. Present the governing equations and the final expression for d.
