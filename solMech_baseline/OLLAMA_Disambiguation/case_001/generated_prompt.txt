Calculate the elongation of segment 2 as δ₂ = ε₂ · L₂.  
Obtain the total elongation of the stepped bar as δ_total = δ₁ + δ₂.  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior, isotropic material, constant cross‑section within each segment, and neglect stress concentrations at the step.
