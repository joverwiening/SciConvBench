The material Young’s modulus is 200 000 MPa. The second moment of area of the cross‑section is 3 000 000 mm⁴. Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior, plane sections remain plane, neglect shear deformation, and use the Euler‑Bernoulli beam theory. Present the governing differential equation EI d⁴v/dx⁴ = w and the appropriate boundary conditions for a simply supported beam, and express the resulting formulas for the maximum deflection and the slope at the supports.
