Determine the axial deformation of a linearly tapered bar subjected to a constant axial tensile load.  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior, neglect temperature effects, shear deformation, and any end constraints other than the applied axial load.  
The bar length is L = 800 mm.  
The applied axial load is P = 30 000 N (tension).  
The cross‑sectional area varies linearly from A(0) = A₀ = 250 mm² at x = 0 to A(L) = 450 mm² at x = L, giving a taper factor k = 0.8 and A(x) = A₀ [1 + k x/L].  
The material Young’s modulus is E = 200 000 MPa.  
Use equilibrium (constant axial force) to write the axial stress as σ(x) = P / A(x).  
Compute the axial strain as ε(x) = σ(x) / E.  
Obtain the total axial deformation δ by integrating the strain over the length: δ = ∫₀ᴸ ε(x) dx = ∫₀ᴸ [P / (E A₀ (1 + k x/L))] dx.  
Present the governing relations, the expression for δ, and evaluate the deformation using the given numerical values.
