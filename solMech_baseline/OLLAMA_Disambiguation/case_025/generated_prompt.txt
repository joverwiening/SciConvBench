Assume a cantilever beam of length 1500 mm made of material with Young’s modulus 69 000 MPa.  
The beam has a rectangular cross‑section with moment of inertia I = 2 500 000 mm⁴ (for example width b ≈ 100 mm and height h ≈ 86.6 mm).  
A point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end of the cantilever.  
Use small‑deformation, linear‑elastic solid mechanics and Castigliano’s theorem to determine the end deflection.  
Apply the governing relation δ = P L³ / (3 E I).  
Report the calculated end deflection in millimetres.
