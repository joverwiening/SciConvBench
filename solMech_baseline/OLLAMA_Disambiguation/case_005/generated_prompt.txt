Determine the axial response of a prismatic bar under the given loading and boundary conditions.  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior, constant cross‑section, and that the material is homogeneous and isotropic.  
Use Young’s modulus \(E = 200{,}000\ \text{MPa}\) and cross‑sectional area \(A = 200\ \text{mm}^2\).  
The total bar length is \(L = 1800\ \text{mm}\).  
Apply point loads: +18 000 N at \(x = 700\ \text{mm}\) (to the right), –8 000 N at \(x = 1300\ \text{mm}\) (to the left), and +20 000 N at the right end \(x = 1800\ \text{mm}\).  
Prescribe the left end displacement \(u(0) = 0\) (fixed support) and apply the right‑end axial force of +20 000 N (force boundary condition).  
Compute the axial reaction force at the fixed end, the internal axial force distribution along the bar, the axial stress \(\sigma(x) = N(x)/A\), the axial strain \(\varepsilon(x) = \sigma(x)/E\), and the axial displacement field \(u(x)\) using compatibility \( \varepsilon = du/dx\).  
Present the governing relations: equilibrium of forces, constitutive law \(\sigma = E\,\varepsilon\), strain‑displacement relation \(\varepsilon = du/dx\), and the boundary conditions.  
Summarize the results in terms of the requested response quantities without performing the numerical calculations.
