Assume a prismatic steel beam with Young’s modulus E ≈ 200 GPa.  
The beam has a rectangular cross‑section with width b = 50 mm and height h = 100 mm.  
The beam length is taken as L = 1.0 m (the length is not required for the flexure stress calculation).  
Adopt small‑deformation, linear‑elastic solid mechanics assumptions.  
Compute the second moment of area of the rectangular section as I = b h³ / 12.  
Use the flexure formula σ = M y / I, where y is the distance from the neutral axis (y = h⁄2 for the extreme fiber).  
State that the maximum bending stress occurs at the outermost fibers and is σ_max = M (h⁄2) / I.  
If axial loads are present, add the axial stress σ_axial = P / A (with A = b h) to the bending stress to obtain the combined stress.  
Present all governing relations clearly and note that the neutral axis coincides with the centroid of the rectangular section.
