The rod is a straight prismatic circular bar with a diameter of 12 mm and a gauge length of 800 mm.  
The axial tensile load applied to the rod is 15 000 N.  
The material of the rod is AISI 1020 steel with a yield strength of 350 MPa and a Young’s modulus of 200 000 MPa.  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior, uniform stress and strain distribution over the cross‑section, and neglect temperature effects and residual stresses.  
Use the cross‑sectional area A = π d²/4 to compute the average normal stress σ = P / A.  
Compute the average normal strain ε = σ / E.  
Determine the elongation ΔL = ε L, where L is the original gauge length.  
Evaluate the factor of safety with respect to yielding as FS = σ_y / σ, where σ_y is the yield strength.
