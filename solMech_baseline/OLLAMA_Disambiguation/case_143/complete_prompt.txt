Use N–mm–MPa units.

A solid circular shaft of diameter d = 25 mm carries an axial tensile force P = 15000 N and a torque T = 400000 N·mm simultaneously.

(a) Compute the average axial stress σ = P/A.
(b) Compute the torsional shear stress at the outer surface τ = T c / J.
(c) Using plane stress (σx = σ, σy = 0, τxy = τ), compute the principal stresses and the maximum in-plane shear stress.
