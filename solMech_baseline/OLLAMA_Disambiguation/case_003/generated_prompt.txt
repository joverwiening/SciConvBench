Assume small deformation, linear‑elastic behavior.  
The bar is subjected to an axial tensile load P = 20 000 N.  
Segment 1 has length L₁ = 500 mm and diameter d₁ = 30 mm.  
Segment 2 has length L₂ = 600 mm and diameter d₂ = 25 mm.  
Both segments are made of 6061‑T6 aluminum with Young’s modulus E = 69 000 MPa.  
The cross‑sectional area of each segment is A₁ = π d₁² / 4 and A₂ = π d₂² / 4.  
The axial stress in each segment is σ₁ = P / A₁ and σ₂ = P / A₂.  
The axial elongation of each segment is ΔL₁ = P L₁ / (A₁ E) and ΔL₂ = P L₂ / (A₂ E).  
The total elongation of the stepped bar is ΔL = ΔL₁ + ΔL₂.
