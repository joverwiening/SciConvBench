Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior for the beam.  
Assume a rectangular cross‑section with width b = 100 mm and height h ≈ 67 mm, giving a second moment of area I = 2 500 000 mm⁴.  
Assume the material Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa (200 000 N/mm²).  
Assume the applied bending moment magnitude M = 3 000 000 N·mm.  
Assume plane sections remain plane and perpendicular to the neutral axis after bending.  
The curvature κ is given by κ = M / (E I).  
The radius of curvature R is the reciprocal of curvature, R = 1 / κ.  
Compute κ using the provided values: κ = 3 000 000 N·mm / (200 000 N/mm² × 2 500 000 mm⁴).  
Compute R as the inverse of the calculated κ.  
Present the governing relations and the numerical results for κ and R, and state the above assumptions clearly.
