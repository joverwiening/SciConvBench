Determine the required diameter of a solid circular column using Euler buckling theory.  
Use the relation \(P_{cr}= \dfrac{\pi^{2}EI}{(KL)^{2}}\) with \(I=\dfrac{\pi d^{4}}{64}\).  
Require that \(P_{cr}\ge n\,P\) where \(n=2.5\) and \(P=25\,000\text{ N}\).  
Solve for the diameter \(d\) from \(d=\left[\dfrac{64\,n\,P\,(KL)^{2}}{\pi^{3}E}\right]^{1/4}\).  
Substitute \(E=69\,000\text{ MPa}=69\,000\text{ N/mm}^{2}\), \(L=2200\text{ mm}\), \(K=2.0\), \(n=2.5\), and \(P=25\,000\text{ N}\) to obtain the required \(d\) in millimetres.  
Compute the critical load \(P_{cr}\) with the obtained diameter and evaluate the factor of safety \(FS=P_{cr}/P\) to confirm it meets or exceeds 2.5.  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior, isotropic material, perfectly straight column, central axial loading, fixed‑free end condition, and neglect initial imperfections, temperature effects, and residual stresses.
