The shaft is a solid circular shaft of diameter 30 mm (radius 15 mm).  
The shaft material is assumed to be isotropic, homogeneous steel with Young’s modulus \(E = 210\;\text{GPa}\) and Poisson’s ratio \(\nu = 0.30\).  
Small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior is assumed.  
The shaft is subjected to a bending moment \(M = 4{,}000{,}000\;\text{N·mm}\) about a principal axis and a torsional torque \(T = 400{,}000\;\text{N·mm}\); no axial force is applied.  
The second moment of area of the circular cross‑section is \(I = \pi d^{4}/64\).  
The polar moment of inertia is \(J = \pi d^{4}/32\).  
The bending stress distribution is \(\sigma_{b}(y) = M\,y/I\) where \(y\) is the distance from the neutral axis.  
The torsional shear stress is \(\tau = T\,r/J\) where \(r\) is the radial coordinate.  
The combined stress state at any point on the outer surface (\(y = r = 15\;\text{mm}\)) is \(\sigma_{x} = \sigma_{b,\max}\) and \(\tau_{xy} = \tau_{\max}\).  
The principal normal stresses are \(\sigma_{1,2} = \frac{\sigma_{x}}{2} \pm \sqrt{\left(\frac{\sigma_{x}}{2}\right)^{2} + \tau_{xy}^{2}}\).  
The shear modulus is \(G = E/[2(1+\nu)]\).  
The normal strain in the axial direction is \(\varepsilon_{x} = \sigma_{x}/E\).  
The shear strain is \(\gamma_{xy} = \tau_{xy}/G\).  
The principal strain values are obtained from the strain transformation using \(\varepsilon_{1,2} = \frac{\varepsilon_{x}}{2} \pm \sqrt{\left(\frac{\varepsilon_{x}}{2}\right)^{2} + \left(\frac{\gamma_{xy}}{2}\right)^{2}}\).  
All governing relations are to be presented clearly, and the response quantities to be reported include the maximum bending stress, maximum torsional shear stress, combined stress components, principal stresses, principal strains, and the corresponding strain components.
