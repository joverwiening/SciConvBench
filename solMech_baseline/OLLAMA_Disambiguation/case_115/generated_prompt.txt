Determine the torsional response of a hollow circular shaft assuming small‑deformation, linear‑elastic, isotropic material behavior.  
Assume the shaft is subjected to a constant torque of 600 000 N·mm, no transverse shear force, and that the torque is applied uniformly along the shaft length of 1200 mm.  
Use the given geometric dimensions: outer radius 30 mm, inner radius 22 mm.  
Use the material shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa (constant throughout the shaft).  
Compute the polar moment of inertia J = (π/2)·(r_o⁴ − r_i⁴).  
Calculate the torsional shear stress distribution τ(r) = T·r / J for any radius r between r_i and r_o.  
Find the maximum shear stress τ_max at the outer surface (r = r_o).  
Determine the torsional rigidity G·J.  
Compute the angle of twist φ = T·L / (G·J) over the shaft length L.  
State that the transverse shear stress is zero due to the absence of transverse shear loading.  
Present all governing equations clearly and list the resulting numerical values for τ_max, G·J, and φ using the provided data.
