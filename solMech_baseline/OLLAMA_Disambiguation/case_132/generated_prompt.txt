Determine the required rectangular beam height for a simply supported beam subjected to a maximum bending moment of 3 000 000 N·mm, given a beam width of 40 mm and an allowable bending stress of 90 MPa.  

Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior, isotropic material, rectangular cross‑section, pure bending, and neglect shear stress effects.  

Use the governing relations:  
- Second moment of area I = b h³ / 12.  
- Distance from neutral axis to extreme fiber c = h / 2.  
- Bending stress σ = M c / I = 6 M / (b h²).  
- Enforce σ ≤ σ_allow.  

Solve for the minimum height h: h ≥ sqrt(6 M_max / (b σ_allow)).  

Compute h ≥ sqrt(6 × 3 000 000 N·mm / (40 mm × 90 N/mm²)) = sqrt(5 000 mm²) ≈ 70.7 mm.  

Select a beam height h of at least 71 mm (or the next standard size) to satisfy the allowable bending stress criterion.
