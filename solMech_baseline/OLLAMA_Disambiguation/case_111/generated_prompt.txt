Assume the material is a typical structural steel with a compressive yield strength of 250 MPa.  
Assume the applied axial compressive load on the column is 3 kN, representing a realistic service load for a slender column of this size.  
Use the given column length L = 1500 mm, diameter d = 30 mm (radius r = 15 mm), effective‑length factor K = 2.0 (fixed‑free condition), and Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa.  
Compute the cross‑sectional area A = π r² = 706.86 mm².  
Compute the second moment of area I = π r⁴ / 4 = 39 760.8 mm⁴.  
Calculate the Euler critical buckling load Pcr = π² E I / (K L)².  
Determine the allowable column load as Pallow = Pcr / n, where the target factor of safety n = 2.0.  
Evaluate the actual factor of safety against buckling as FS = Pcr / Papplied, using the assumed applied load Papplied = 3 kN.  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior, no initial geometric imperfections, and neglect temperature effects.  
Present the governing relations, computed values for Pcr, Pallow, and FS, and state that the column is safe if FS ≥ n.
