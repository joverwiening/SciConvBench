Determine the axial stress in Bar 1 and Bar 2, the common axial strain, and the total elongation of the composite bar system under the given axial load.  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior for both materials.  
Assume the two bars are perfectly bonded to the rigid plates so that they experience the same axial deformation (common elongation).  
Use the following material and geometric properties:  
- Bar 1 (AISI 1020 steel): Young’s modulus E₁ = 200 000 MPa, cross‑sectional area A₁ = 300 mm².  
- Bar 2 (6061‑T6 aluminum): Young’s modulus E₂ = 69 000 MPa, cross‑sectional area A₂ = 200 mm².  
Both bars have equal length L = 500 mm.  
The total axial force applied to the combined bar system is P = 20 000 N.  
Compute the equivalent axial stiffness of the parallel arrangement, the distribution of the axial force between the two bars based on their axial stiffnesses, the resulting axial stresses σ₁ and σ₂, the common axial strain ε, and the total elongation δ of the system.  
Present the governing relations clearly, including equilibrium (P = P₁ + P₂), compatibility (ε₁ = ε₂ = δ/L), and constitutive laws (σ = E ε).  
State any additional assumptions made, such as neglecting shear lag, temperature effects, and assuming the rigid plates do not deform.
