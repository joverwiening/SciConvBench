Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior and Euler‑Bernoulli beam theory.  
The beam is a cantilever of length L = 1200 mm with a constant cross‑section having second moment of area I = 2 500 000 mm⁴.  
The material has Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa.  
A point load P = 800 N is applied at the free end of the cantilever.  
Neglect shear deformation and axial effects.  
Determine the tip deflection using the governing relation δ = P L³/(3 E I).  
Present the deflection result in millimetres.
