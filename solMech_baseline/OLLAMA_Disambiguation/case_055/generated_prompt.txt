Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior for the bar.  
The bar length is L = 1200 mm.  
The material Young’s modulus is E = 69 000 MPa.  
A static axial tensile load of magnitude P = 20 000 N is applied at the right end (x = L).  
The left end (x = 0) is constrained so that axial translation is prevented (fixed or simply‑supported support).  
The cross‑sectional area varies linearly along the length according to A(x)=A₀ [1 + (k x)/L] with A₀ = 200 mm², k = 0.6, and L = 1200 mm.  
Neglect temperature effects, shear deformation, and any geometric non‑linearity.  
The axial stress distribution is σ(x)=P / A(x).  
The axial strain distribution is ε(x)=σ(x) / E = P / [E A(x)].  
The axial displacement of the loaded end is u(L)=∫₀ᴸ ε(x) dx = ∫₀ᴸ [P / (E A(x))] dx, which evaluates to u(L)= (P L) / [E A₀ k] ln(1 + k).  
Report the stress σ(x), strain ε(x), and total axial deformation u(L) using the above relations.
