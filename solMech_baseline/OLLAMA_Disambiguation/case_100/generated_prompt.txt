Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior and Euler‑Bernoulli beam theory (shear deformation neglected). 
The cantilever beam has length L = 1500 mm and is subjected to a point load P = 500 N applied at the free end. 
The material Young’s modulus is E = 200 000 MPa. 
The second moment of area about the bending axis is I = 2 500 000 mm⁴ (a rectangular cross‑section with b = 100 mm and h ≈ 224 mm may be used to achieve this I). 
Use Castigliano’s theorem to obtain the tip deflection, which for an Euler‑Bernoulli cantilever under an end point load is δ = P L³ / (3 E I). 
Report the computed tip deflection in millimetres (mm).
