Determine the torsional response of a hollow circular shaft assuming small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior.  
Assume the shaft material is homogeneous, isotropic, and follows Hooke’s law in shear.  
Assume Saint‑Venant torsion conditions: the shaft is prismatic, ends are free to rotate, and warping is unrestricted.  
Outer radius ro = 25 mm.  
Inner radius ri = 21 mm.  
Applied torque magnitude T = 450 000 N·mm.  
Shear modulus G = 77 519.4 MPa.  
Shaft length L = 1200 mm.  
No transverse shear force is considered in the analysis.  
Compute the polar moment of inertia J = (π/2)·(ro⁴ − ri⁴).  
Compute the torsional rigidity G·J.  
Determine the shear stress distribution τ(r) = T·r / J for ri ≤ r ≤ ro.  
Report the maximum shear stress τmax = T·ro / J occurring at the outer surface.  
Calculate the angle of twist φ = T·L / (G·J).  
Present the governing equations and the numerical values of J, G·J, τmax, and φ.
