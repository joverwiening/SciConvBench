Analyze a stepped circular bar under axial tension using small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The bar has two segments in series, fixed at the left end, with axial load P = 35000 N applied at the right end.
Segment 1 (left): diameter d1 = 16 mm, length L1 = 400 mm.
Segment 2 (right): diameter d2 = 40 mm, length L2 = 800 mm.
Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa for both segments (homogeneous material assumed unless stated otherwise).
Assume the bar is prismatic within each segment, the load is purely axial, and stress is uniformly distributed over each cross-section (Saint-Venant assumption away from the step).
Compute the cross-sectional area of each segment using A = π d² / 4.
Determine the axial stress in each segment using σ = P / A.
Determine the elongation of each segment using δ = P L / (A E).
Determine the total elongation as the sum of the elongations of both segments.
State all governing relations clearly and list any additional assumptions made.
