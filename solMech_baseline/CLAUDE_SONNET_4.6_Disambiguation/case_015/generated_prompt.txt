Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Assume an Euler-Bernoulli beam model (plane sections remain plane, shear deformation neglected).
The beam is a cantilever: fixed at one end, free at the other.
A downward point load P = 2000 N is applied at the free end.
The beam length is L = 1200 mm.
Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa (N/mm²).
Second moment of area I = 2000000 mm⁴.
Flexural rigidity EI = 200000 × 2000000 = 4 × 10¹¹ N·mm².
Determine the relevant response quantities: maximum deflection at the free end, the deflection curve (elastic curve) along the beam, the maximum slope, the reaction force and reaction moment at the fixed support, the bending moment distribution, and the shear force distribution.
State all additional assumptions made (e.g., self-weight neglected, no applied moment unless otherwise noted, beam is prismatic with constant EI).
Present the governing differential equation EI·d²v/dx² = M(x) and its boundary conditions clearly.
Derive and present closed-form expressions for shear force V(x), bending moment M(x), slope θ(x), and deflection v(x).
Evaluate and report the maximum deflection at the free end using δ_max = PL³/(3EI).
