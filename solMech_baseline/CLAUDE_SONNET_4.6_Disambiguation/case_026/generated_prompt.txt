The task is to solve a cantilever beam problem using Castigliano's theorem to find the deflection at the free end due to an end point load.

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam is a cantilever (fixed at one end, free at the other).
The beam has a constant second moment of area I = 800000 mm⁴.
The beam length is L = 1500 mm.
A point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end.
The elastic modulus is E = 200000 MPa.
Use Castigliano's second theorem applied to bending strain energy only (shear strain energy neglected under Euler-Bernoulli beam theory).
The bending moment distribution M(x) along the beam is to be expressed as a function of position x measured from the free end.
Compute the strain energy U due to bending, then differentiate with respect to P to obtain the end deflection.
State all assumptions clearly, present the governing relations (moment distribution, strain energy integral, Castigliano differentiation), and give the numerical result for the end deflection in mm.
