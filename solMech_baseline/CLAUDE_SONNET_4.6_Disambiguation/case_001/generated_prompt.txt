The problem is a stepped circular bar under axial tension, to be solved using small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The bar has two segments in series, fixed at the left end, with axial load applied at the right (free) end.
Segment 1 (left): diameter d1 = 25 mm, length L1 = 300 mm.
Segment 2 (right): diameter d2 = 35 mm, length L2 = 1000 mm.
Applied axial tensile load P = 30000 N at the free (right) end.
Both segments are the same material: AISI 1020 steel, E = 200000 MPa.
Determine the axial stress in each segment.
Determine the axial elongation of each segment and the total elongation of the bar.
State any additional assumptions made (e.g., uniform cross-section within each segment, no stress concentrations at the step, uniaxial stress state).
Present the governing relations clearly (cross-sectional area, normal stress formula, and axial deformation formula for each segment).
