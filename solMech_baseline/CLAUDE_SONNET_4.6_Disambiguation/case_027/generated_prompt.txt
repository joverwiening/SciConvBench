A vertical steel rod is subjected to impact loading from a dropped weight with no pre-existing static axial load.
The dropped weight is W = 800 N.
The drop height is h = 150 mm.
The rod has length L = 1500 mm and diameter d = 16 mm.
Compute the cross-sectional area as A = π d² / 4.
The material is AISI 1020 steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics throughout.
Assume the rod is fixed at one end and the weight strikes the free end axially.
Assume no pre-existing static axial load acts on the rod before impact.
Assume the impacting weight does not rebound and remains in contact with the rod after impact (perfectly plastic impact assumption for energy method).
Assume the mass of the rod itself is negligible compared to the dropped weight.
Determine the static deflection δ_st = WL / (AE).
Determine the impact factor n = 1 + sqrt(1 + 2h / δ_st).
Determine the maximum dynamic deflection δ_max = n · δ_st.
Determine the maximum dynamic axial force P_max = n · W.
Determine the maximum dynamic axial stress σ_max = P_max / A.
State all additional assumptions used and present the governing relations clearly.
