The bar is fixed at the top and free at the bottom (hanging bar), which is the standard assumption for self-weight elongation unless otherwise stated.
Determine the axial stress distribution σ(x) along the bar as a function of position x measured from the free (bottom) end.
Determine the axial strain distribution ε(x) using Hooke's law.
Determine the total elongation (deformation) δ of the bar by integrating the strain over the length, yielding δ = γL²/(2E).
