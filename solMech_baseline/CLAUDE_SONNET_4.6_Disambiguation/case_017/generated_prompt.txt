Material and section properties are E = 200000 MPa and I = 3,000,000 mm⁴, giving flexural rigidity EI = 6 × 10¹¹ N·mm².
Determine the deflection response using the principle of superposition, combining the individual deflection solutions for each load case.
State the governing Euler-Bernoulli relation EI d⁴v/dx⁴ = q(x) clearly.
Present the standard closed-form deflection formulae for each load case (UDL on simply supported beam; central point load on simply supported beam).
Superpose the two deflection fields to obtain the total deflection, with particular focus on the maximum deflection at midspan.
