Analyze a prismatic bar under axial point loads using small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The bar has three segments: AB (700 mm), BC (600 mm), and CD (500 mm).
Point A is the fixed (pinned/fixed wall) left end; D is the free right end.
Point loads are: +18000 N (rightward) at B, -8000 N (leftward) at C, +20000 N (rightward) at D.
The cross-sectional area is uniform: A = 200 mm².
The elastic modulus is uniform: E = 200000 MPa.
Determine the internal axial force in each segment (AB, BC, CD).
Determine the axial stress in each segment.
Determine the axial deformation (elongation or contraction) of each segment.
Determine the total elongation of the bar and the displacement at each point (B, C, D) relative to the fixed end A.
State all additional assumptions (e.g., self-weight neglected, loads are concentric, plane sections remain plane).
Present the governing relations clearly, including equilibrium, stress-force, and deformation-displacement relations.
