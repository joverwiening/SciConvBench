Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The shaft is a solid circular cross-section with diameter 30 mm.
The shaft has total length 1200 mm.
The shaft is a cantilever: fixed at x = 0, free at x = 1200 mm.
Two external torques are applied: T1 = 200000 N·mm at x = 1200 mm (free end), and T2 = -200000 N·mm at x = 400 mm.
There is no additional transverse (shear force) loading beyond what is implied by the torques; focus is on torsional response.
The shear modulus is G = 76923.1 MPa (AISI 1020 steel).
Determine the internal torque distribution T(x) along the shaft by sectioning and equilibrium.
Determine the polar second moment of area J for the solid circular cross-section.
Determine the maximum shear stress in each segment using the torsion formula τ = T·r / J, where r = 15 mm.
Determine the angle of twist in each segment using φ = T·L / (G·J).
Determine the total angle of twist at the free end (x = 1200 mm) by summing segment contributions.
State all additional assumptions made (e.g., prismatic shaft, homogeneous and isotropic material, Saint-Venant torsion theory applies, cross-sections remain plane).
