Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Two bars are arranged in parallel between two rigid plates.
Both bars have equal length L = 500 mm.
Bar 1 is AISI 1020 steel with cross-sectional area A1 = 300 mm² and elastic modulus E1 = 200000 MPa.
Bar 2 is 6061-T6 aluminum with cross-sectional area A2 = 200 mm² and elastic modulus E2 = 69000 MPa.
A total tensile load P = 20000 N is applied to the top rigid plate.
The bottom rigid plate is fixed (reaction support).
Determine the relevant response quantities for the composite bar in parallel configuration.
Response quantities to determine include: axial stiffness of each bar, load shared by each bar, axial stress in each bar, and axial deformation (elongation) of the assembly.
State any additional assumptions needed and present the governing relations clearly.
The governing relations must include compatibility (equal deformation of both bars), force equilibrium (sum of bar forces equals total applied load), and the force-deformation relation for each bar.
