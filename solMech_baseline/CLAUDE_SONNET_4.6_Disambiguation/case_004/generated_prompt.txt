Analyze a prismatic bar under axial loading using small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The bar has three segments: AB (500 mm), BC (600 mm), and CD (400 mm).
Point A is the fixed (wall) support at the left end.
Point D is the free right end.
Point loads applied: +18000 N at B, −12000 N at C, +16000 N at D (positive = rightward/tensile direction).
The cross-sectional area is uniform throughout: A = 300 mm².
The elastic modulus is E = 200000 MPa.
Determine the support reaction at A.
Determine the internal axial force in each segment (AB, BC, CD) using method of sections and equilibrium.
Determine the axial stress in each segment.
Determine the axial deformation (elongation or contraction) of each segment using δ = PL/(AE).
Determine the total elongation of the bar and the displacement at each point (B, C, D) relative to the fixed end A.
State all assumptions clearly, including small deformation, linear elasticity, prismatic geometry, and uniaxial stress state.
Present the governing relations: equilibrium (ΣF = 0), stress (σ = P/A), and deformation (δ = PL/AE).
