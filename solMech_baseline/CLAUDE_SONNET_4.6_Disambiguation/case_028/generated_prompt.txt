Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics throughout.
The rod is vertical and fixed at one end (base); the dropped weight strikes the free (top) end axially.
The dropped weight is W = 500 N (the weight itself, not a separate mass plus gravity distinction needed beyond W).
The drop height is h = 200 mm.
The rod length is L = 1200 mm.
The rod has a circular cross-section with diameter d = 30 mm, so A = π(30)²/4.
The elastic modulus is E = 200 000 MPa.
There is no separate static axial load; the only loading is the impact from the dropped weight.
Use the energy method (conservation of energy) to derive the equivalent static load and impact factor.
State all additional assumptions (mass of rod neglected, no energy loss on impact, weight remains in contact with rod after impact, stress is uniform over cross-section).
Determine the following response quantities: static deflection δ_st, impact factor n, maximum dynamic deflection δ_max, maximum dynamic stress σ_max, and the equivalent static (impact) force P_eq.
