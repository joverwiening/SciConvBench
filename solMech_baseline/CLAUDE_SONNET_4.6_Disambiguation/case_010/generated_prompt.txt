Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The system consists of two bars connected in parallel between two rigid plates.
Bar 1 is structural steel with cross-sectional area A1 = 150 mm² and elastic modulus E1 = 200000 MPa.
Bar 2 is cast iron with cross-sectional area A2 = 200 mm² and elastic modulus E2 = 110000 MPa.
Both bars have equal length L = 800 mm.
A total axial tensile load P = 30000 N is applied to the top rigid plate.
The rigid plates enforce equal deformation (compatibility) in both bars.
Determine the relevant response quantities: load shared by each bar, stress in each bar, and axial deformation of the assembly.
State all governing relations clearly, including compatibility, equilibrium, and force-deformation relations.
