Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Assume an Euler-Bernoulli beam model.
The beam is a cantilever (fixed at one end, free at the other).
A downward point load P = 2500 N is applied at the free end.
The beam length is L = 800 mm.
Young's modulus E = 69000 MPa.
Second moment of area I = 1,600,000 mm⁴.
Flexural rigidity EI = 69000 × 1,600,000 N·mm².
There is no moment load to consider for this specific case (focus is on end point load only).
Determine the deflection response: derive and state the governing Euler-Bernoulli bending equation, the boundary conditions, the deflection curve v(x), the maximum deflection at the free end, the slope at the free end, and the bending moment and shear force distributions along the beam.
State all additional assumptions made (e.g., no shear deformation, prismatic cross-section, homogeneous material, self-weight neglected).
Present all governing relations clearly and in symbolic form before substituting numerical values.
