Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics throughout.
The system consists of two bars arranged in parallel between two rigid plates.
Both bars have equal length L = 400 mm.
Bar 1 is AISI 1020 steel with cross-sectional area A1 = 300 mm² and elastic modulus E1 = 200000 MPa.
Bar 2 is cast iron with cross-sectional area A2 = 250 mm² and elastic modulus E2 = 110000 MPa.
A total tensile load P = 20000 N is applied to the top rigid plate.
The bottom plate is fixed (fully restrained).
The two rigid plates enforce equal deformation (compatibility) in both bars.
Determine the relevant response quantities for composite bars in parallel: the force carried by each bar, the stress in each bar, and the common axial deformation (elongation).
State any additional assumptions needed and present the governing relations (equilibrium, compatibility, and force-deformation) clearly.
