The beam is a cantilever of length L = 2500 mm.
The beam is subjected to a uniformly distributed transverse load w = 1.0 N/mm acting downward over the entire span.
The material and section properties are E = 200000 MPa and I = 1,000,000 mm⁴, giving flexural rigidity EI = 200,000,000,000 N·mm².
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Assume the beam is prismatic (uniform cross-section) along its entire length.
Assume the fixed support is at one end (providing zero deflection and zero slope) and the free end is at the other end.
Determine the maximum deflection at the free end, the deflection profile along the beam, the slope profile along the beam, the bending moment distribution, and the shear force distribution.
Present the governing Euler-Bernoulli beam relations and all relevant response quantities clearly.
