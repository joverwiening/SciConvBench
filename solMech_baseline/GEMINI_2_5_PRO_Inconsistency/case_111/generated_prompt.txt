Solve a mechanics of materials problem for a straight, prismatic circular rod.
The rod is made of structural steel.
The rod has a diameter of 12 mm and a gage length of 800 mm.
The rod is loaded in tension by an axial force of 30000 N applied through the centroid.
Use consistent N–mm–MPa units for all calculations.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.
Use a Young’s modulus of E = 200000 MPa.
Use a yield strength of Sy = 250 MPa.
Determine the average normal stress in the rod.
Determine the elastic normal strain.
Determine the axial elongation of the gage length.
Compute the factor of safety against yielding using the definition n = Sy / σ_avg.
