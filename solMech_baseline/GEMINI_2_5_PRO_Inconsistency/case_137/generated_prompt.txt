The problem involves a solid circular shaft with a diameter of 45 mm.
The shaft is subjected to a bending moment of 2,000,000 N·mm and a torque of 500,000 N·mm.
The analysis is for the stress state at the outer surface of the shaft at the location of maximum bending stress.
For part (a), compute the maximum bending normal stress using the formula σ = M * c / I, with I = π * d^4 / 64 and c = d/2.
For part (b), compute the torsional shear stress at the surface using the formula τ = T * c / J, with J = π * d^4 / 32.
For part (c), treat the surface stress state as plane stress with σx equal to the bending stress from part (a), σy = 0, and τxy equal to the torsional shear stress from part (b).
Using this plane stress state, compute the principal stresses and the maximum in-plane shear stress.
