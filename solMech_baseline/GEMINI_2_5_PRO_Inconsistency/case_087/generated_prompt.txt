Use N–mm–MPa units for all calculations.
Assume linear-elastic behavior for the material.
The analysis is for a stepped circular bar made of structural steel.
The bar is fixed at its left end.
A tensile load of 25000 N is applied at the right end.
The bar has two segments, labeled 1 and 2 from left to right.
Segment 1 has a length of 300 mm and a diameter of 12 mm.
Segment 2 has a length of 600 mm and a diameter of 30 mm.
The Young's modulus of the steel is 200000 MPa.
Determine the normal stress in each segment.
Determine the total elongation of the bar.
Calculate the stress in each segment using the applied force and the segment's cross-sectional area.
Calculate the total elongation by summing the elongations of the two individual segments.
