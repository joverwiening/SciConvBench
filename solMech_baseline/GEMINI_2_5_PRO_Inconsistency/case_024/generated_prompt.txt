Analyze a cantilever beam of length L = 1500 mm, fixed at x = 0.
The beam carries a uniform distributed load w = 1.5 N/mm downward over its entire span.
The beam is made of structural steel with a Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The beam's cross-section has a moment of inertia I = 1500000 mm^4.
Use Euler–Bernoulli beam theory for the analysis.
Use N–mm–MPa units.
Calculate the maximum deflection at the free end.
Calculate the slope at the free end.
Do not replace the distributed load with its resultant point load.
