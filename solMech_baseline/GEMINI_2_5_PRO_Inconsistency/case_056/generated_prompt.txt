Compute the elongation of a bar using the axial deformation integral δ = ∫[0→L] P/(A(x)E) dx.
Use N–mm–MPa units.
The bar has a length L = 800 mm.
The bar is subjected to a constant axial tensile force P = 30000 N.
The material is structural steel with a constant Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The bar’s cross-sectional area varies linearly along its length according to the function A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L).
The variable x is measured from the left end of the bar.
The initial cross-sectional area is A0 = 250 mm^2.
The constant k is 0.8.
