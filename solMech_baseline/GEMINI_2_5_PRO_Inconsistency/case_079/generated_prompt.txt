Use N, mm, and MPa as the units for all calculations and results.
The shaft is a solid circular shaft with a diameter (d) of 30 mm.
The shaft is subjected to a bending moment (M) of 4,000,000 N·mm.
The shaft is subjected to a torque (T) of 400,000 N·mm.
The analysis is for the stress state at the outer surface of the shaft at the location of maximum bending stress.
For part (a), compute the maximum bending normal stress (σ).
Use the formula σ = M * c / I for the bending stress calculation.
The distance from the neutral axis to the outer surface (c) is d/2.
The area moment of inertia (I) is π * d^4 / 64.
For part (b), compute the torsional shear stress (τ) at the surface.
Use the formula τ = T * c / J for the torsional shear stress calculation.
The polar moment of inertia (J) is π * d^4 / 32.
For part (c), treat the surface stress state as plane stress.
Set the normal stress in the x-direction (σx) equal to the bending stress (σ) calculated in part (a).
Set the normal stress in the y-direction (σy) to 0.
Set the shear stress (τxy) equal to the torsional shear stress (τ) calculated in part (b).
