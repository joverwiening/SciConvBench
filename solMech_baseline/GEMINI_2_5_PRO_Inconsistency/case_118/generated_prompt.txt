Use N-mm-MPa as the system of units.
A motor delivers 5 kW of power to a solid circular shaft.
The shaft rotates at a constant speed of 1200 rpm.
The shaft is made of AISI 1020 steel with a shear modulus (G) of 76923.1 MPa.
The shaft has a uniform diameter (d) of 20 mm.
The length of the shaft (L) over which the analysis is to be performed is 1000 mm.
Assume the shaft is supported by bearings that allow it to rotate freely.
Assume the power is delivered at one end and taken off at the other, resulting in a constant torque along the length.
Compute the transmitted torque (T) using the relationship P = T·ω.
Calculate the angular velocity (ω) using the formula ω = 2π(rpm/60).
Compute the maximum shear stress in the shaft using torsion theory.
Compute the angle of twist over the shaft's length L.
Express the final angle of twist in degrees.
