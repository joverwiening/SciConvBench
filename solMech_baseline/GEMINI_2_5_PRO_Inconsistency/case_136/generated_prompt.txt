The units for the analysis are N, mm, and MPa.
The bar has a rectangular cross section.
The width of the cross section is 30 mm.
The height of the cross section is 100 mm.
The internal resultant normal force is 50000 N in tension.
The internal resultant transverse shear force is 5000 N.
The transverse shear force acts parallel to the 30 mm width.
Assume uniform stress distributions for both normal and shear stresses.
Compute the average normal stress, σ_avg.
Compute the average shear stress, τ_avg.
Compute the magnitude of the maximum in-plane shear stress using the formula τ_max = sqrt((σ_avg/2)^2 + τ_avg^2).
