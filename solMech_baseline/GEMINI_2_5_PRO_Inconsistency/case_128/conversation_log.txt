Q: The provided shear force V results in a non-zero shear stress. Should this shear stress be included when computing the principal stresses?
A: Yes, you should include the shear stress when computing the principal stresses. Treat the stress state as plane stress where σx = P/A, σy = 0, and τxy = V/A.
