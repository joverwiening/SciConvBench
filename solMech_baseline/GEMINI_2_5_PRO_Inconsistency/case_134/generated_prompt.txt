Determine the internal shear force V and bending moment M for a simply supported beam AB.
The beam has a span L of 3000 mm.
A concentrated vertical load P of 12000 N acts downward at a point 1500 mm from support A.
The analysis should be performed at a cross-section located x = 1500 mm from support A.
Use the statics sign convention for the results.
Positive shear V is defined as upward on the left cut face.
Positive bending moment M is defined as causing sagging (tension at the bottom fibers).
Provide the final value for V in Newtons (N).
Provide the final value for M in Newton-millimeters (N·mm).
