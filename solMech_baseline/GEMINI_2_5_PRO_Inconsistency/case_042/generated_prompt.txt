A prismatic bar has a length of 800 mm and a cross-sectional area of 300 mm^2.
The bar is made of structural steel with a Young's modulus of 200,000 MPa.
A tensile load of 8000 N is applied suddenly to the bar.
The material response is assumed to be linear elastic.
Use energy methods to determine the maximum stress in the bar.
The calculation should compare the work done by the suddenly applied load to the strain energy stored in the bar.
Use N, mm, and MPa as the system of units.
