Use N-mm-MPa units for all calculations and results.
The prismatic bar has a constant cross-sectional area of 300 mm^2.
The bar is composed of three segments: AB, BC, and CD.
The length of segment AB is 500 mm.
The length of segment BC is 600 mm.
The length of segment CD is 400 mm.
The material is AISI 1020 steel with a Young's modulus of 200000 MPa.
An axial force of +18000 N is applied at point B.
An axial force of -12000 N is applied at point C.
An axial force of +16000 N is applied at point D.
The positive direction for forces is to the right.
The left end of the bar, point A, is fixed.
Perform a segmental analysis where the internal force is calculated for each of the three segments individually.
Calculate the internal axial force for each segment (AB, BC, and CD).
Calculate the normal stress for each segment (AB, BC, and CD).
Assume tensile forces and stresses are positive.
Calculate the total displacement of point D relative to the fixed point A.
