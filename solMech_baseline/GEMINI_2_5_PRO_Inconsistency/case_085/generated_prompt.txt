Analyze a stepped circular bar using N–mm–MPa units.
The material behavior is linear-elastic.
The bar is made of AISI 1020 steel with a Young's Modulus (E) of 200000 MPa.
The bar consists of two segments.
Segment 1 has a length (L1) of 300 mm and a diameter (d1) of 12 mm.
Segment 2 has a length (L2) of 800 mm and a diameter (d2) of 30 mm.
The bar is fixed at the left end of Segment 1.
An axial tensile load (P) of 35000 N is applied to the right end of Segment 2.
Calculate the normal stress in each segment individually based on its own cross-sectional area.
Calculate the total elongation of the bar as the sum of the individual elongations of each segment.
