Q: The flexure formula `σ = M y / I` calculates a stress that varies with the distance `y` from the neutral axis. How should I apply this formula to find a stress that is uniform over the section depth?
A: Disregard the instruction to treat the stress as uniform. Apply the flexure formula as it is normally used, where stress varies linearly with the distance from the neutral axis.
