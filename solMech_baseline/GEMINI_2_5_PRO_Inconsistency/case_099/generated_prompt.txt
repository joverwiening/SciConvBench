Use N–mm–MPa units.
The bar is a vertical AISI 1020 steel rod.
The length of the rod is L = 800 mm.
The diameter of the rod is d = 25 mm.
A weight W = 1500 N is dropped from a height h = 200 mm onto a collar at the bottom end of the rod.
Assume the bar behaves like a linear axial spring.
Neglect the mass of the bar.
The modulus of elasticity for the steel is E = 200000 MPa.
The cross-sectional area is A = π * d^2 / 4.
Calculate the maximum axial force, P_max, using the energy conservation equation W * (h + δ_max) = (P_max^2 * L) / (2 * A * E).
The maximum deflection is δ_max = (P_max * L) / (A * E).
After solving for P_max, compute the maximum normal stress using the formula σ_max = P_max / A.
