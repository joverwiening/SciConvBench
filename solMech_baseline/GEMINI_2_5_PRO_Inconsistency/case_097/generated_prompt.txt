Use N, mm, and MPa as the system of units.
The problem involves a cantilever beam of length L = 1500 mm.
The beam is fixed at position x = 0 and free at position x = L.
The beam is made of AISI 1020 steel, with a Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The beam has a constant area moment of inertia I = 2500000 mm^4.
A downward point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end of the beam.
Calculate the vertical deflection at the free end.
The calculation must use Castigliano’s theorem.
The bending strain energy U is to be calculated using the formula U = ∫[0→L] M(x)^2/(2EI) dx.
The bending moment M(x) must be formulated as a function of the position x along the beam.
The deflection is to be found by first integrating to find U and then differentiating U with respect to the load P.
A memorized deflection formula should not be used to obtain the result.
