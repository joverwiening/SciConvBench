Solve a mechanics of materials problem for a straight, prismatic circular rod.
The rod is made of 6061-T6 aluminum.
The rod has a diameter of 12 mm.
The rod has a gage length of 800 mm.
The rod is loaded in tension by an axial force of 15000 N applied through the centroid.
Use a Young’s modulus (E) of 69000 MPa.
Use a yield strength (Sy) of 275 MPa.
Use consistent N–mm–MPa units.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.
Calculate the average normal stress in the rod.
Calculate the elastic normal strain.
Calculate the axial elongation of the gage length.
Calculate the factor of safety against yielding using the formula n = Sy/σ_avg.
