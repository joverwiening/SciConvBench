Use N–mm–MPa units for all calculations.
Treat the Young's modulus, E, as a constant value of 200000 MPa.
The bar has a length, L, of 800 mm.
A constant axial tensile force, P, of 12000 N is applied to the bar.
The cross-sectional area, A(x), varies linearly along the bar's length, x.
The area function is A(x) = A0 * (1 + k * x / L).
The initial cross-sectional area, A0, is 250 mm^2.
The constant k is 0.6.
Compute the total elongation of the bar.
Calculate the elongation using the axial deformation integral δ = ∫[0→L] P/(A(x)E) dx.
Do not use the arithmetic-mean area approximation for the calculation.
