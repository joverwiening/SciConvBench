Use N–mm–MPa units.
The beam is a cantilever beam of length L = 1500 mm.
The beam is fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
The beam is made of AISI 1020 steel with a Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The beam has a constant moment of inertia I = 800000 mm^4.
A downward point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end.
Use Castigliano’s theorem to compute the vertical deflection at the free end.
The bending strain energy is given by U = ∫[0→L] M(x)^2/(2EI) dx.
The bending moment M(x) must be set up as a function of position x along the beam.
First, integrate the expression for strain energy U.
Second, differentiate the resulting expression for U with respect to the load P to find the deflection.
Do not use a memorized deflection formula for the calculation.
