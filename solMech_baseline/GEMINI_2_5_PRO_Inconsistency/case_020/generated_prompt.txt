Use N-mm-MPa units for all calculations and results.
The solid circular shaft has a diameter of 35 mm and a total length of 1200 mm.
The shaft is fixed at the end x = 0.
A positive torque of 200000 N·mm is applied at the free end, x = 1200 mm.
A negative torque of -250000 N·mm is applied at x = 800 mm.
The material is structural steel with a shear modulus G of 77519.4 MPa.
The analysis will be performed by dividing the shaft into two segments.
Segment 1 spans from x = 0 to x = 800 mm.
Segment 2 spans from x = 800 mm to x = 1200 mm.
For each segment, construct the internal torque.
For each segment, compute the maximum shear stress.
Compute the angle of twist for each segment.
Sum the angles of twist of the two segments to find the total angle of twist at the free end (x = 1200 mm) relative to the fixed end.
