Q: How should I calculate the normal stress, given that it should be identical in the two segments despite their different diameters?

Q: How should I interpret the instruction to "enforce a common elongation for the segments" when calculating the total deformation?
A: Do not enforce a common elongation. The total elongation is the sum of the individual elongations of each segment.
