Compute the elongation of a bar using the axial deformation integral δ = ∫[0→L] P/(A(x)E) dx.
Use N–mm–MPa units for the calculation.
The length of the bar, L, is 1200 mm.
The constant axial tensile force, P, is 20000 N.
The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with a constant Young's modulus, E, of 69000 MPa.
The cross-sectional area, A(x), varies linearly along the bar's length according to the formula A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L).
The variable x is measured from the left end of the bar.
The initial cross-sectional area, A0, is 200 mm^2.
The constant k is 0.6.
The integration limits are from x = 0 to x = L.
Do not use the arithmetic-mean area method for the calculation.
