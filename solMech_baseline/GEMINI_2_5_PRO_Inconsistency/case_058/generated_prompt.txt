The column is a straight column made of AISI 1020 steel.
The column length L is 2000 mm.
The modulus of elasticity E is 200000 MPa.
The moment of inertia I is 1200000 mm^4.
The end conditions are fixed-pinned, resulting in an effective length factor K of 0.7.
The calculation assumes Euler buckling applies.
Compute the Euler critical buckling load, P_cr, using the formula P_cr = π^2 * E * I / (K * L)^2.
Report the final value for P_cr in Newtons (N).
Also report the final value for P_cr in kilonewtons (kN).
