Use N–mm–MPa units.
The modulus of elasticity, E, is a constant value of 200000 MPa.
A bar of length L = 500 mm is subjected to a constant axial tensile force P = 12000 N.
The bar’s cross-sectional area varies linearly along its length according to the function A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L).
The variable x is measured from the left end of the bar.
The initial cross-sectional area, A0, is 200 mm^2.
The constant k is 1.0.
Compute the elongation of the bar using the axial deformation integral δ = ∫[0→L] P/(A(x)E) dx.
Do not use the arithmetic-mean area for the calculation.
