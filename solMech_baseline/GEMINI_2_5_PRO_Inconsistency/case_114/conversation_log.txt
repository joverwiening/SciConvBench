Q: You have provided two conflicting methods for the calculation: one that superposes terms as if the product of inertia were zero, and another that uses the full unsymmetric bending formula. Which of these two methods should I use?
A: Use the unsymmetric bending stress formula:
σ = -(My·Iz − Mz·Iyz)/(Iy·Iz − Iyz^2) · z + -(Mz·Iy − My·Iyz)/(Iy·Iz − Iyz^2) · y.
