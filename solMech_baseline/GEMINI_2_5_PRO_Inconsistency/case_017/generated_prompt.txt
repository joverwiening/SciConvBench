Compute the deflection at midspan for a simply supported beam.
The beam has a span L of 3000 mm.
The beam is made of AISI 1020 steel with a Young's modulus E of 200000 MPa.
The beam has a constant moment of inertia I of 3000000 mm^4.
The beam carries two loads simultaneously.
The first load is a uniform distributed load w of 0.3 N/mm over the full span.
The second load is a downward point load P of 1200 N at midspan.
Use N–mm–MPa units.
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory.
Calculate the total deflection by superposing the deflections from two standard cases.
The first case is a simply supported beam with a uniform distributed load.
The second case is a simply supported beam with a point load at midspan.
