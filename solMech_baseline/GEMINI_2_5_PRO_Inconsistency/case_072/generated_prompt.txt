Use N–mm–MPa units.
The solid circular shaft has a diameter d = 30 mm.
The shaft has a length L = 1000 mm.
The shaft is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with a shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa.
The shaft is subjected to a constant torque T = 450000 N·mm.
Use the torsional strain energy expression U = ∫[0→L] T^2/(2GJ) dx.
In the strain energy expression, use the polar moment of inertia J = π d^4/32.
Use Castigliano’s theorem, φ = ∂U/∂T, to compute the angle of twist between the ends of the shaft.
