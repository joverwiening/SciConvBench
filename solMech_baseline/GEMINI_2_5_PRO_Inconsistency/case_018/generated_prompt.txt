Use N-mm-MPa as the system of units.
The shaft is a solid circular shaft.
The shaft diameter is 40 mm.
The total length of the shaft is 1500 mm.
The shaft is fixed at the end x = 0.
A torque, T1, of 300000 N·mm is applied at the free end, x = 1500 mm, in the positive sense.
A second torque, T2, of -150000 N·mm is applied at x = 750 mm in the negative sense.
The shaft material is 6061-T6 aluminum.
The shear modulus, G, for the material is 25939.8 MPa.
The analysis must be performed by dividing the shaft into two segments.
The first segment runs from x = 0 to x = 750 mm.
The second segment runs from x = 750 mm to x = 1500 mm.
Determine the internal torque for each of the two segments.
Calculate the maximum shear stress within each of the two segments.
Calculate the total angle of twist at the free end (x = 1500 mm) by summing the twist of each segment.
