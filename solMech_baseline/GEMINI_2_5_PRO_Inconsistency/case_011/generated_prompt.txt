Use N-mm-MPa as the system of units.
The column is a solid circular column made of structural steel.
The column has a diameter d = 25 mm.
The column has a length L = 2000 mm.
The material's Young’s modulus is E = 200000 MPa.
The material's yield strength is Sy = 250 MPa.
The column's effective length factor is K = 0.7.
The column supports a compressive load P = 25000 N.
First, perform a yielding check.
Compute the average compressive stress using the formula σ_avg = P/A, where A is the cross-sectional area.
Compare the computed average compressive stress σ_avg to the yield strength Sy.
Second, perform an Euler buckling check.
Compute the Euler critical buckling load using the formula P_cr = π^2 * E * I / (K * L)^2.
For the buckling calculation, use the area moment of inertia for a solid circular cross section, I = π * d^4 / 64.
Compare the computed Euler critical buckling load P_cr to the applied load P.
Finally, based on the results of the yielding and buckling checks, state which limit state is more critical for this column.
