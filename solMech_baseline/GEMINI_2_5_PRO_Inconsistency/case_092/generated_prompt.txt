Model a cantilever beam of length L = 1800 mm.
The beam is fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
The beam is made of structural steel with a Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The beam has a constant second moment of area I = 1200000 mm^4.
A downward point load P = 2000 N is applied at the free end.
Use N–mm–MPa as the system of units.
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory is applicable.
Use the linearly varying bending moment for the calculation.
Determine the slope at the free end.
Determine the vertical deflection at the free end.
Report the slope in radians.
Report the vertical deflection in mm.
