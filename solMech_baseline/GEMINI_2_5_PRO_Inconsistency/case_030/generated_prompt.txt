Use N–mm–MPa units.
Assume linear elastic isotropic behavior for the material.
A structural steel rod has an initial diameter of 25 mm.
The initial length of the rod is 1200 mm.
The rod is subjected to an axial tensile load of 45000 N applied through the centroid.
The Young's modulus for the steel is 200000 MPa.
The Poisson’s ratio for the steel is 0.29.
Do not assume a plane strain condition.
Compute the average axial normal stress.
Compute the axial strain.
Compute the lateral (diametral) strain.
