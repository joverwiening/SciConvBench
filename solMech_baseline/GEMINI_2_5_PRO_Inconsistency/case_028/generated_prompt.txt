Use N-mm-MPa units for all calculations.
Model the bar as a linear axial spring.
Neglect the mass of the bar.
The vertical structural steel rod has a length L of 1200 mm.
The diameter d of the rod is 30 mm.
The rod is fixed at its top end.
A weight W of 500 N is dropped from a height h of 200 mm onto a collar at the bottom end.
The Young's modulus E for the steel is 200000 MPa.
Calculate the cross-sectional area A using the formula A = (π * d^2) / 4.
Calculate the maximum axial force P_max by solving the energy conservation equation W * (h + δ_max) = (P_max^2 * L) / (2 * A * E).
The maximum deflection δ_max is defined as δ_max = (P_max * L) / (A * E).
After finding P_max, calculate the maximum normal stress σ_max using the formula σ_max = P_max / A.
