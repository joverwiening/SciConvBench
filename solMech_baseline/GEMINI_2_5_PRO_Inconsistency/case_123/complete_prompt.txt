Use N–mm–MPa units. Assume the shaft is linearly elastic and has a circular solid cross section.

A solid circular shaft made of brass has diameter d = 30 mm and length L = 1200 mm. It transmits a torque T = 400000 N·mm.

Using J = π d^4/32 and τ_max = T c / J (with c = d/2), determine the maximum shear stress in the shaft. Also compute the angle of twist over the length, φ = TL/(JG), using G = 39179.1 MPa. Report φ in degrees.
