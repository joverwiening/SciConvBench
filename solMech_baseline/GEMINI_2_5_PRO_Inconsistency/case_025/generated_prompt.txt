Use N–mm–MPa units.
A cantilever beam has a length L = 1500 mm.
The beam is fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
The beam is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with a modulus of elasticity E = 69000 MPa.
The beam has a constant moment of inertia I = 2500000 mm^4.
A downward point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end.
Compute the vertical deflection at the free end using Castigliano’s theorem.
The bending strain energy is U = ∫[0→L] M(x)^2/(2EI) dx.
The bending moment M(x) must be expressed as a function of the position x along the beam.
Derive the function for the bending moment M(x) based on the described cantilever beam with a point load at the free end.
Do not use a memorized deflection formula for the final answer.
The deflection is found by first setting up the strain energy integral and then differentiating the energy U with respect to the load P.
