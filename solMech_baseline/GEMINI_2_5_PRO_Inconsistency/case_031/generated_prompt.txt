Compute the elongation of a vertical bar due to its own weight.
Use N-mm-MPa as the system of units.
The bar is vertical, prismatic, and linearly elastic.
The bar has a length L of 3500 mm.
The bar has a constant cross-sectional area A of 200 mm^2.
The bar hangs from a rigid support at its top end.
The bar's material has a modulus of elasticity E of 69000 MPa.
The material density ρ is 8900 kg/m^3.
The gravitational acceleration g is 9.81 m/s^2.
The only load acting on the bar is its own weight.
Calculate the elongation by integrating the axial force over the length of the bar.
The axial force at a distance x from the top is P(x) = ρ * g * A * (L - x).
The elongation δ is given by the integral of P(x) / (A * E) from x=0 to x=L.
