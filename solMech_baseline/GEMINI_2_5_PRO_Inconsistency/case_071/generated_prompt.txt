Use N–mm–MPa units.
A solid circular shaft has a diameter d = 25 mm.
The shaft has a length L = 1000 mm.
The shaft is made of a material with a shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa.
The shaft is subjected to a constant torque T = 350000 N·mm.
Use the polar moment of inertia J = π d^4/32.
Calculate the torsional strain energy using the expression U = ∫[0→L] T^2/(2GJ) dx.
Compute the angle of twist between the ends using Castigliano’s theorem, φ = ∂U/∂T.
