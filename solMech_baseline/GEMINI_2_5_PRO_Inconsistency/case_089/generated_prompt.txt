Use N–mm–MPa units.
Analyze a prismatic bar with a constant cross-sectional area of 500 mm^2 and a Young's modulus of 200000 MPa.
The bar is fixed at its left end, point A.
The bar has three segments: AB of length 700 mm, BC of length 400 mm, and CD of length 300 mm.
Axial forces are applied as follows: +15000 N at B, -12000 N at C, and +10000 N at D, with positive forces acting to the right.
Determine the unique internal axial force for each of the three segments (AB, BC, CD).
Determine the unique normal stress for each of the three segments.
Determine the displacement of the right end, point D, relative to the fixed end A.
