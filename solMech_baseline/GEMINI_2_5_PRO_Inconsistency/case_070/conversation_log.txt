Q: The instruction to take the principal stresses as σ_x and 0 conflicts with the presence of a shear force V. Which instruction should take priority?
A: Ignore the instruction to take the principal stresses as σ_x and 0. You should compute the principal stresses based on the full plane stress state, which includes the shear stress τ calculated from the shear force V.
