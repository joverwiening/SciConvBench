Use N-mm-MPa as the system of units.
The prismatic bar has a rectangular cross section.
The width of the cross section is 40 mm.
The height of the cross section is 120 mm.
The internal resultant normal force is 30000 N in tension.
The internal resultant transverse shear force is 12000 N.
The shear force acts parallel to the 40 mm width.
Assume a uniform stress distribution for both normal and shear stresses.
Calculate the average normal stress, σ_avg, on the cross section.
Calculate the average shear stress, τ_avg, on the cross section.
Compute the magnitude of the maximum in-plane shear stress using the formula τ_max = sqrt((σ_avg/2)^2 + τ_avg^2).
