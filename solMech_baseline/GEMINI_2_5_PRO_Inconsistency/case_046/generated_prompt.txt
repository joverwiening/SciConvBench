Solve a mechanics of materials problem for a structural steel rod.
The rod is a straight, prismatic circular rod.
The diameter of the rod is 25 mm.
The gage length of the rod is 800 mm.
The rod is loaded in tension by an axial force of 20000 N applied through the centroid.
Use consistent N–mm–MPa units for all calculations.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.
The Young’s modulus for the structural steel is 200000 MPa.
The yield strength for the structural steel is 250 MPa.
Determine the average normal stress in the rod.
Determine the elastic normal strain in the rod.
Determine the axial elongation of the gage length.
Compute the factor of safety against yielding using the definition n = Sy / σ_avg.
