Use N–mm–MPa units.
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory.
The beam is simply supported with a span L = 3000 mm.
The beam is made of structural steel with E = 200000 MPa.
The beam has a constant second moment of area I = 2000000 mm^4.
The beam carries two loads simultaneously: a uniform distributed load w = 0.3 N/mm over the full span, and a downward point load P = 3000 N at midspan.
Compute the deflection at midspan by superposition of two load cases.
For the first load case, calculate the deflection contribution from the distributed load by replacing the uniform load with its single resultant force (w*L) acting at midspan.
For the second load case, calculate the deflection contribution from the point load P.
The total deflection is the sum of the deflections from these two cases.
