Use N–mm–MPa units.
The beam is simply supported at x=0 and x=2400 mm.
The beam span is L = 2400 mm.
The modulus of elasticity is E = 200000 MPa.
The moment of inertia is I = 3500000 mm^4.
A downward point load of P = 1200 N acts at x = 1200 mm.
Compute the deflection at the load point, x = 1200 mm.
Use the unit-load (virtual work) method for beam deflection.
The formula to use is δ = ∫[0→L] (M(x)·m(x))/(EI) dx.
M(x) is the real bending moment diagram from the 1200 N load.
m(x) is the virtual moment diagram from a unit downward load applied at x = 1200 mm.
