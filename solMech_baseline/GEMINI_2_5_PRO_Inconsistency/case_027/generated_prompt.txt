Use N–mm–MPa units.
Assume the bar behaves like a linear axial spring.
Neglect the mass of the bar.
The vertical rod is made of AISI 1020 steel with a length L of 1500 mm and a diameter d of 16 mm.
The rod is fixed at its top end.
A weight W of 800 N is dropped from a height h of 150 mm onto a collar at the bottom end.
The modulus of elasticity E for the steel is 200000 MPa.
Calculate the cross-sectional area A using the formula A = π * d^2 / 4.
Solve for the maximum axial force P_max using the energy conservation equation W(h + δ_max) = (P_max^2 * L) / (2 * A * E).
In the energy equation, the maximum deflection δ_max is defined as P_max * L / (A * E).
After finding P_max, compute the maximum normal stress σ_max using the formula σ_max = P_max / A.
