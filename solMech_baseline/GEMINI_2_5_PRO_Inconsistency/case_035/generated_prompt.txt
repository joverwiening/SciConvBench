Determine the minimum required diameter d for a solid circular column.
Use N–mm–MPa units.
Assume Euler buckling.
The column has a length L of 2200 mm.
The column is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with a Young's modulus E of 69000 MPa.
The column must support a compressive load P of 25000 N.
The factor of safety against buckling n is 2.5.
The effective length factor K is 2.0.
The critical load P_cr must satisfy the condition P_cr ≥ n·P.
Use the formula P_cr = π^2 E I / (K L)^2 for the critical buckling load.
Use the formula I = π d^4 / 64 for the area moment of inertia of the solid circular cross-section.
