The beam span L is 6000 mm.

The calculation should be performed at a cross-section located x = 4500 mm from support A.
Use the statics sign convention where positive shear V is upward on the left cut face.
Use the statics sign convention where positive bending moment M causes sagging.
Provide the final value for V in Newtons (N).
Provide the final value for M in Newton-millimeters (N·mm).
