A negative torque T2 = -200,000 N·mm is applied at x = 400 mm.
The material is AISI 1020 steel with a shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa.
The analysis must be performed on a segment-by-segment basis.
First, determine the internal torque in the segment from x = 0 to x = 400 mm.
Second, determine the internal torque in the segment from x = 400 mm to x = 1200 mm.
Third, calculate the maximum shear stress within the segment from x = 0 to x = 400 mm.
Fourth, calculate the maximum shear stress within the segment from x = 400 mm to x = 1200 mm.
Finally, compute the total angle of twist at the free end (x = 1200 mm) relative to the fixed end (x = 0) by summing the twists of each segment.
