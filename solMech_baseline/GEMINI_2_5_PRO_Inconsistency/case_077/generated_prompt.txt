Determine the minimum required diameter, d, for a solid circular shaft.
The shaft has a length L of 800 mm.
The shaft is made of AISI 1020 steel with a shear modulus G of 76923.1 MPa.
The shaft must transmit a torque T of 600000 N·mm.
The allowable angle of twist, φ_allow, is 1.5 degrees over the length L.
Use the formula φ = T * L / (J * G) to calculate the angle of twist.
Use the formula J = π * d^4 / 32 for the polar moment of inertia.
Use the angle of twist limit of 1.5 directly in the formula for φ without converting it to radians.
The required units for the final calculation are N–mm–MPa.
