Perform a stress and strain analysis of a solid circular shaft under combined loading.
The analysis must adhere to the principles of small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The shaft has a radius of 12.5 mm.
The shaft is subjected to a tensile axial load of 15000 N.
The shaft is also subjected to a torque of 400000 N·mm.
The primary goal is to determine the response quantities resulting from the combined axial and torsional loads.
The evaluation must include principal stresses and principal strains.
Assume the shaft is made of a material with properties typical of steel, such as a Young's Modulus of 200 GPa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.3.
All governing relations used must be clearly presented.
Any other assumptions made during the analysis must be explicitly stated.
