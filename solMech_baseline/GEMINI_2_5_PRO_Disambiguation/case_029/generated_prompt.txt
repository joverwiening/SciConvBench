Analyze a rod under small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics assumptions.
The rod material is isotropic.
The rod has a circular cross-section with an initial diameter of 20 mm.
The initial length of the rod is 1000 mm.
The material has a Young's Modulus of 105000 MPa.
The material has a Poisson's ratio of 0.34.
The rod is subjected to a single axial tensile load of 25000 N.
All lateral loads are to be ignored.
Determine the average axial normal stress.
Determine the axial strain.
Determine the lateral (diametral) strain.
Determine the change in the diameter of the rod.
State any additional assumptions made and present the governing relations clearly.
