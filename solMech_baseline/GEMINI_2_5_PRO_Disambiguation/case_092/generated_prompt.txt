The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
A prismatic bar is fixed at its left end, A, and free at its right end, D.
The bar has a constant cross-sectional area of 500 mm^2.
The bar is made of a material with a Young's Modulus of 200000 MPa.
The distance from the fixed end A to point B is 700 mm.
The distance from point B to point C is 400 mm.
The distance from point C to point D is 300 mm.
An axial point load of +15000 N is applied at point B, where positive is directed to the right.
An axial point load of -12000 N is applied at point C.
An axial point load of +10000 N is applied at point D.
Determine the internal axial force in each segment: AB, BC, and CD.
Determine the normal stress in each segment: AB, BC, and CD.
Determine the displacement of point D relative to point A.
State any additional assumptions made and present the governing relations clearly.
