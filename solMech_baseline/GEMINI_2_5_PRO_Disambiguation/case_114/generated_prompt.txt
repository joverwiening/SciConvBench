The task is to analyze a straight prismatic circular rod under a tensile axial load.
The analysis should assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The axial load is 30000 N.
The rod has a diameter of 12 mm.
The rod has a length of 800 mm.
The rod is made of structural steel.
The Young's Modulus for the steel is 200000 MPa.
The yield strength for the steel is 250 MPa.
Determine the average normal stress in the rod.
Determine the axial strain in the rod.
Determine the total elongation of the rod.
Evaluate the factor of safety with respect to yielding.
State any additional assumptions made during the analysis.
Clearly present the governing equations used for the calculations.
