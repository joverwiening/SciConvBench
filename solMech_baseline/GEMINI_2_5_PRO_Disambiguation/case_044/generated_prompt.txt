Analyze a 6061-T6 aluminum rod under uniaxial tension.
The analysis will assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The material is assumed to be isotropic and homogeneous.
The initial length of the rod is 500 mm.
The initial diameter of the rod is 40 mm.
The rod is subjected to an axial tensile load of 35,000 N.
Use a Young's Modulus of 69,000 MPa for the material.
Use a Poisson's ratio of 0.33 for the material.
Determine the following response quantities: axial stress, axial strain, lateral strain, change in length, and change in diameter.
The final output should clearly state the governing relations used for the calculations.
