The analysis will be based on small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The component is a solid circular column with a diameter of 20 mm and a length of 2000 mm.
The column is made of structural steel with a Young's modulus of 200,000 MPa.
A reasonable value for the yield strength of the structural steel is to be assumed.
The end support conditions correspond to an effective length factor K of 1.0.
A reasonable value for the applied compressive axial load is to be assumed.
The allowable column load based on Euler buckling is to be determined.
A factor of safety of 2.0 shall be used to calculate the allowable load.
The factor of safety with respect to buckling for the assumed applied load is to be evaluated.
All additional assumptions made must be stated.
The governing relations must be presented clearly.
