Analyze a composite bar system under mechanical loading using small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The system consists of two bars in parallel, positioned between two rigid plates.
The first bar has a Young's modulus of 200,000 MPa, a length of 800 mm, and a cross-sectional area of 150 mm^2.
The second bar has a Young's modulus of 110,000 MPa, a length of 800 mm, and a cross-sectional area of 200 mm^2.
A total tensile load of 30,000 N is applied to the rigid plates.
Determine the stress in each of the two bars.
Determine the strain in the composite system.
Determine the force carried by each bar.
Determine the total elongation of the system.
State any additional assumptions required for the analysis.
Present the governing relations clearly.
