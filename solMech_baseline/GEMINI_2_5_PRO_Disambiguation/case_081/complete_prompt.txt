Use N–mm–MPa units. Consider the stress state at the outer surface of the circular shaft at the location of maximum bending stress.

A solid circular shaft of diameter d = 30 mm is subjected simultaneously to a bending moment M = 4000000 N·mm and a torque T = 400000 N·mm at a critical section.

(a) Compute the maximum bending normal stress at the surface using σ = M c / I with I = π d^4/64 and c = d/2.
(b) Compute the torsional shear stress at the surface using τ = T c / J with J = π d^4/32.
(c) Treating the surface stress state as plane stress with σx = σ (from bending), σy = 0, and τxy = τ (from torsion), compute the principal stresses and the maximum in-plane shear stress.
