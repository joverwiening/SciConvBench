The analysis is for a linear-elastic, small-deformation solid mechanics problem.
The object is a rectangular beam with a width of 40 mm and a height of 100 mm.
The beam is subjected to a sagging internal bending moment of 4,000,000 N·mm at the cross-section of interest.
This moment causes the top surface of the beam to be in compression.
Determine the bending stress as a function of the vertical distance from the neutral axis.
State all necessary assumptions made for the analysis.
