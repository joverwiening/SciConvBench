Analyze a cantilever beam using small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics theory.
The beam has a length of 2500 mm.
The beam's material has a Young's Modulus of 200000 MPa.
Assume the beam has a solid rectangular cross-section with a height of 100 mm and a width of 50 mm.
A uniformly distributed transverse load of 1.0 N/mm acts downward over the entire length of the beam.
Determine the relevant response quantities, with a focus on deflection.
The response quantities to determine are the equations for deflection, slope, shear force, and bending moment as a function of position.
Also determine the maximum values for deflection, slope, shear force, bending moment, and bending stress, and state their locations.
Present the governing relations used in the analysis clearly.
