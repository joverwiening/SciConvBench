Analyze a prismatic beam under small-deformation, linear-elastic conditions.
The beam has a rectangular cross-section with a height of 100 mm and a width of 50 mm.
The analysis is to be performed at a single critical cross-section where a bending moment is applied.
The magnitude of the bending moment is 3,000,000 N·mm.
The bending moment is applied about the centroidal axis parallel to the 50 mm width.
Assume the beam material is steel.
Assume the Young's Modulus for the steel is 200 GPa.
State any additional assumptions made, such as the material being homogeneous and isotropic.
Clearly present the governing relations, including the flexure formula.
Determine the relevant response quantities, which should include the maximum normal stress and the radius of curvature at the section.
