Analyze a prismatic bar under axial loading using small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics theory.
The total length of the bar is 1200 mm.
The left end of the bar, point A, is fixed.
A point load of 18000 N is applied to the right at point B, located 300 mm from the fixed end A.
A point load of 12000 N is applied to the left at point C, located 900 mm from the fixed end A.
A point load of 10000 N is applied to the right at the free end, point D.
The Young's Modulus for the bar's material must be provided.
The constant cross-sectional area of the bar must be provided.
Determine the axial force as a function of position along the bar.
Determine the normal stress as a function of position along the bar.
Determine the axial displacement as a function of position along the bar.
Present the governing relations used in the analysis.
State any additional assumptions made during the analysis.
