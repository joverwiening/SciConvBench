Determine the response quantities for a prismatic bar subjected to multiple axial point loads.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The bar is prismatic and has a total length of 1500 mm.
The left end of the bar, A, is fixed.
A point load of 18000 N is applied to the right at point B, located 500 mm from the fixed end A.
A point load of 12000 N is applied to the left at point C, located 1100 mm from the fixed end A.
A point load of 16000 N is applied to the right at the right end, D, located 1500 mm from the fixed end A.
The Young's Modulus of the bar's material is 200000 MPa.
The cross-sectional area of the bar is constant, but its value has not been provided.
The response quantities to determine are the axial force, normal stress, normal strain, and axial displacement along the bar.
Present the governing relations clearly.
