Perform an analysis of a solid circular shaft under the assumption of small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The shaft has a length of 800 mm.
The shaft has a radius of 15 mm.
The shaft's material has a shear modulus of 25,939.8 MPa.
A torque of 500,000 N·mm is applied to the shaft.
No transverse shear force is applied.
Assume the shaft is fixed at one end, and the torque is applied to the other free end.
Determine the maximum shear stress in the shaft.
Determine the angle of twist of the free end relative to the fixed end.
Clearly present the governing relations used in the analysis.
State any additional assumptions made.
