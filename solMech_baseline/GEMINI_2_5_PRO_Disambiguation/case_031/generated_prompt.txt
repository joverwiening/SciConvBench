Determine the elongation of a vertical bar due to its own weight.
The analysis should assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The bar has a constant cross-sectional area of 200 mm^2.
The length of the bar is 3500 mm.
The material's Young's Modulus is 69000 MPa.
The material's density is 8900 kg/m^3.
Use an acceleration due to gravity of 9.81 m/s^2.
The only required response quantity is the total elongation of the bar.
