Determine the response quantities for a solid circular shaft under combined axial and torsional loading.
The analysis should also evaluate principal stress and principal strain measures.
The shaft has a diameter of 25 mm.
The shaft is subjected to an axial tensile force of 25,000 N.
The shaft is subjected to a torque of 300,000 N·mm.
Assume the analysis is for a point on the outer surface of the shaft.
The analysis must use the assumption of small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Assume the shaft material is steel.
Use a Young's modulus of 200 GPa for the steel.
Use a Poisson's ratio of 0.3 for the steel.
The final output must state all assumptions made.
The final output must clearly present all governing relations used in the calculations.
