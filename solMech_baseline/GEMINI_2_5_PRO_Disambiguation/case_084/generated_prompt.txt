The analysis will be conducted under the assumption of small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The task is to determine the shear flow and required fastener spacing for a built-up beam.
The beam has a built-up I-beam cross-section, constructed from three rectangular plates.
Assume an overall beam height of 300 mm.
Assume a flange width of 150 mm.
Assume a flange thickness of 10 mm.
Assume a web thickness of 8 mm.
The internal shear force at the critical section is 25000 N.
Each flange is connected to the web by a single row of fasteners.
The allowable shear force for a single fastener is 1800 N.
The analysis must present the governing relations clearly.
The final output should be the shear flow at the flange-web interface and the maximum allowable spacing for the fasteners.
