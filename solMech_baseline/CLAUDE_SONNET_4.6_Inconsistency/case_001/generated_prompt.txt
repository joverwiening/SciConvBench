Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.
Assume linear-elastic behavior.
The bar is a stepped circular bar made of AISI 1020 steel.
The bar is fixed at its left end and loaded in tension at its right end by P = 30000 N.
Segment 1 (left) has length L1 = 300 mm and diameter d1 = 25 mm.
Segment 2 (right) has length L2 = 1000 mm and diameter d2 = 35 mm.
Use E = 200000 MPa for both segments.
The same axial force P = 30000 N passes through both segments.
Calculate the normal stress in each segment using σ = P/A, where A is the respective cross-sectional area of that segment.
For the total elongation, calculate the elongation of each segment separately using δ = PL/(AE) and sum them to get the total elongation between the fixed end and the load application point.
Determine (i) the normal stress in each segment and (ii) the total elongation of the bar.
