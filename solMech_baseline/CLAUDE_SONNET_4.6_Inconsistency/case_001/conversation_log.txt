Q: The problem statement says "take the normal stress to be identical in the two segments," but for a stepped bar with different cross-sectional areas carrying the same axial force, the stresses in the two segments are generally different (σ = P/A, and A₁ ≠ A₂).

Which interpretation should I follow for the stress calculation?
A: The stresses are different in each segment. Calculate σ = P/A for each segment using their respective cross-sectional areas.
