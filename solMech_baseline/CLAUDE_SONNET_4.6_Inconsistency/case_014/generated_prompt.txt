Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory (small deflection, linear elastic).
The beam is a cantilever of length L = 1200 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
Material is structural steel with E = 200000 MPa.
The constant second moment of area is I = 2500000 mm^4.
A downward point load P = 800 N is applied at the free end.
No external couple is applied at the free end.
Use the standard cantilever tip-load formulas: slope at the free end = PL²/(2EI) and deflection at the free end = PL³/(3EI).
Compute (i) the slope at the free end in radians (and convert to degrees) and (ii) the vertical deflection at the free end in mm.
Ignore any instruction to use a constant moment or to set the free-end rotation to zero.
