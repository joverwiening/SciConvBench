The shaft is a stepped solid circular shaft made of 6061-T6 aluminum with two segments in series.
Segment AB has length L1 = 400 mm and diameter d1 = 25 mm.
Segment BC has length L2 = 900 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm.
End A is fixed and torque T = 750,000 N·mm is applied at end C.
The shaft is statically determinate: both segments carry the full internal torque T = 750,000 N·mm.
Use G = 25,939.8 MPa throughout.
Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Determine the maximum shear stress in segment AB using the torsion formula τ = T·c / J, where c = d1/2 and J = π·d1⁴/32.
Determine the maximum shear stress in segment BC using the torsion formula τ = T·c / J, where c = d2/2 and J = π·d2⁴/32.
Identify which segment governs (has the higher shear stress).
Determine the angle of twist of segment AB using φ1 = T·L1 / (G·J1).
Determine the angle of twist of segment BC using φ2 = T·L2 / (G·J2).
Determine the total angle of twist at C relative to A as φ_total = φ1 + φ2.
Report all stresses in MPa and all angles in radians.
