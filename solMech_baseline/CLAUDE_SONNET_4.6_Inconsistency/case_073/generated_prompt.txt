Use N, mm, and MPa units throughout.
A simply supported beam has a maximum bending moment of M_max = 6,000,000 N·mm.
The cross section is rectangular with width b = 60 mm and unknown height h.
The allowable bending stress is σ_allow = 170 MPa.
Use the standard bending stress formula σ_max = M_max · (h/2) / (b · h³ / 12).
Set σ_max equal to σ_allow and solve for the minimum required height h.
