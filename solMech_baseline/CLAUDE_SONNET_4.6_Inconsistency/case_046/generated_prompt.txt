Solve a mechanics of materials problem using consistent N–mm–MPa units and small-deformation, linear-elastic assumptions.
Use Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa for structural steel.
The rod is straight, prismatic, and circular with a diameter of 25 mm and a gage length of 800 mm.
The rod is loaded in tension by an axial force of 20000 N applied through the centroid.
Determine the average normal stress in the rod using σ_avg = P/A.
Determine the elastic normal strain using ε = σ_avg / E.
Determine the axial elongation of the gage length using δ = ε × L.
Use a yield strength Sy = 250 MPa.
Compute the factor of safety against yielding defined as n = Sy / σ_avg.
Report all results in consistent N–mm–MPa units.
