Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
A motor delivers power P = 5 kW to a solid circular shaft rotating at 1200 rpm.
The shaft is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa.
The shaft diameter is d = 20 mm and its length is L = 1200 mm.
Part (a): Compute the transmitted torque T using P = T·ω, where ω = 2π(rpm/60). Convert P from kW to N·mm/s (1 kW = 1,000,000 N·mm/s). Do not substitute rpm directly for ω; use ω = 2π(1200/60).
Part (b): Using torsion theory, compute the maximum shear stress τ = T·c/J, where c = d/2 and J = πd⁴/32.
Part (c): Compute the angle of twist using φ = TL/(GJ), treating the shaft as fixed at one end with torque applied at the other (statically determinate).
