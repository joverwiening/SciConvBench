Q: When computing the angle of twist in part (c), should the shaft be treated as free to twist at one end (i.e., fixed at one end, torque applied at the other), or is it truly fixed at both ends (making it statically indeterminate)?
A: Treat it as fixed at one end with torque applied at the other (statically determinate). Use φ = TL/(GJ) directly.
