Q: I notice an inconsistency in the problem statement that I want to clarify before proceeding.

For a standard cantilever beam with a point load P at the free end, the bending moment varies linearly from PL at the fixed end to zero at the free end — not a constant moment PL over the full span. However, your problem statement instructs to assume a constant moment PL over the full span for the deflection calculation.

Should I follow the instruction as stated (constant moment PL over the full span), or should I use the standard Euler–Bernoulli cantilever beam formulas (linearly varying moment, giving slope = PL²/2EI and deflection = PL³/3EI)?
A: Use the standard Euler–Bernoulli cantilever beam formulas with linearly varying moment — slope = PL²/2EI and deflection = PL³/3EI.
