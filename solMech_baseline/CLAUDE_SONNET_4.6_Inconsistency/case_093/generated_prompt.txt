Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory (small deflection, linear elastic).
The beam is a cantilever of length L = 800 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with E = 69000 MPa.
The constant second moment of area is I = 800000 mm^4.
A downward point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end.
Use the standard linearly varying bending moment for a cantilever with a tip point load.
Use the standard formulas: slope at free end = PL²/(2EI) and deflection at free end = PL³/(3EI).
Determine (i) the slope at the free end, reported in radians (and degrees).
Determine (ii) the vertical deflection at the free end, reported in mm.
