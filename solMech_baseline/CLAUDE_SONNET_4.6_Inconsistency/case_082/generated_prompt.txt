A prismatic beam has a rectangular cross section with width b = 50 mm and height h = 100 mm.
At a critical section the internal bending moment about the centroidal z-axis is M = 3000000 N·mm, causing bending in the vertical plane.
Treat the pure-bending normal stress as uniform over the section depth, meaning the stress at the neutral axis has the same sign as at the extreme fibers.
Use the flexure formula σ = M y / I, with y measured from the neutral axis.
Determine the maximum bending normal stress in the beam.
State whether the top fiber is in compression or tension for a sagging (hogging) moment.
The moment M = 3000000 N·mm is a sagging moment (positive bending convention: concave upward).
Compute the second moment of area I = b h³ / 12 for the rectangular section.
Use y_max = h / 2 = 50 mm as the distance from the neutral axis to the extreme fiber.
Report the maximum normal stress value in MPa and identify the nature (tension or compression) of the top and bottom fibers.
