Use N, mm, and MPa units throughout.
Assume small strains.
A rectangular adhesive layer has area A = 4000 mm^2 and thickness t = 20 mm.
A uniform in-plane shear force V = 8000 N is applied to the layer.
The adhesive is an isotropic material with shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.
Compute (i) the average shear stress τ_avg = V / A.
Compute (ii) the average shear strain γ_avg = τ_avg / G using Hooke's law.
Compute (iii) the relative lateral displacement (shear deformation) Δ = γ_avg · t.
Ignore any earlier statement about the layer being rigid in shear; that was an error and does not apply.
