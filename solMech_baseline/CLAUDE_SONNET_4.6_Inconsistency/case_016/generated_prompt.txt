Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory (small deflection, linear elastic).
The beam is a cantilever of length L = 800 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with elastic modulus E = 69000 MPa.
The constant second moment of area is I = 1600000 mm^4.
A downward point load P = 2500 N is applied at the free end (x = L).
No external couple is applied at the free end.
Use standard cantilever beam theory: the bending moment varies linearly from P·L at the fixed end to zero at the free end.
Calculate (i) the slope (rotation) at the free end in radians (and convert to degrees).
Calculate (ii) the vertical deflection at the free end in mm.
