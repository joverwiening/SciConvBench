A bar of length L = 500 mm has a constant axial tensile force P = 12000 N applied to it.
The material is AISI 1020 steel with E = 200000 MPa, treated as constant.
The cross-sectional area varies linearly as A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L), with A0 = 200 mm^2 and k = 1.0.
x is measured from the left end.
Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Compute the elongation using the axial deformation integral δ = ∫[0→L] P/(A(x)·E) dx.
Replace A(x) with the arithmetic-mean area (treating it as exact) for the deformation calculation.
Compute and report the final elongation δ in mm.
