Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory (small deflection, linear elastic).
The beam is a cantilever of length L = 1800 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
Material is structural steel with E = 200000 MPa.
The constant second moment of area is I = 1200000 mm^4.
A downward point load P = 2000 N is applied at the free end.
Use the standard cantilever beam approach: the bending moment varies linearly along the span, being zero at the free end and maximum (P·L) at the fixed end.
The free-end rotation is not zero; it is one of the results to be calculated.
Determine (i) the slope (rotation) at the free end in radians and degrees.
Determine (ii) the vertical deflection at the free end in mm.
