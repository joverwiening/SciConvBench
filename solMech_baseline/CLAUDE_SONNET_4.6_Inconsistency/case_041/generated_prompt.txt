A straight column made of AISI 1020 steel has length L = 1000 mm and constant flexural rigidity EI with E = 200000 MPa and I = 1200000 mm^4.
The end conditions are fixed–free (cantilever), so the effective length factor is K = 2.0.
Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Assume Euler buckling applies (column is slender and remains elastic).
Compute the Euler critical buckling load using the formula P_cr = π^2 × E × I / (K × L)^2.
Report P_cr in both N and kN.
