Assume Euler buckling applies.
The column has a solid circular cross section.
The column length is L = 1800 mm.
The material is structural steel with E = 200000 MPa.
The compressive load is P = 30000 N.
The factor of safety against buckling is n = 2.0.
The effective length factor is K = 1.0.
The critical buckling load formula is P_cr = π² E I / (K L)².
The second moment of area is I = π d⁴ / 64.
The design requirement is P_cr ≥ n · P, meaning P_cr ≥ 2.0 × 30000 = 60000 N.
Solve for the minimum required diameter d by setting P_cr = n · P and solving for d.
Report the minimum required diameter d in mm.
