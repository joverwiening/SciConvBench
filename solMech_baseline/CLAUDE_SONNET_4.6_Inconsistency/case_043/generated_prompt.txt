Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
A solid circular column has diameter d = 20 mm and length L = 2000 mm.
The material is structural steel with E = 200000 MPa.
The effective length factor is K = 1.0.
Assume Euler buckling governs.
For the circular section, use the polar moment J = π d^4 / 32 in the Euler buckling expression instead of the second moment of area I.
The Euler critical load formula becomes P_cr = π^2 E J / (K L)^2.
Part (a): Compute the Euler critical load P_cr using the formula above.
Part (b): Apply a factor of safety n = 2.0 and determine the allowable axial compressive load P_allow = P_cr / n.
