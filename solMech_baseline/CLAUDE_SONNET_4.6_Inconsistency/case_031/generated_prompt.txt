Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
The bar is vertical, prismatic, and linearly elastic.
Bar length L = 3500 mm.
Cross-sectional area A = 200 mm^2.
Modulus of elasticity E = 69000 MPa.
Material density ρ = 8900 kg/m^3.
Gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s^2.
Only self-weight acts; ignore external loads.
Compute the elongation of the bar due to its own weight using two methods and compare them.
Method 1: Use the exact integration approach with axial force P(x) = ρ g A (L − x), integrating δ = ∫₀ᴸ P(x)/(A E) dx, where x is measured downward from the top.
Method 2: Replace the distributed self-weight with a single concentrated load equal to the total weight acting at the free end, and compute elongation as δ = W L / (A E).
Perform careful unit conversions between m and mm as needed so all quantities are consistent in N–mm–MPa units.
Report the elongation result in mm.
