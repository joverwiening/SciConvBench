A motor delivers power P = 5 kW = 5,000,000 N·mm/s to a solid circular shaft rotating at 1200 rpm.
The shaft is made of AISI 1020 steel with shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa.
The shaft diameter is d = 20 mm and its length between fixed ends is L = 1000 mm.

Part (a): Compute the transmitted torque T using P = T·ω, where ω = 2π(1200/60) rad/s.

Part (b): Using torsion theory, compute the maximum shear stress in the shaft using τ = T·c/J, where c = d/2 and J = π·d⁴/32.

Part (c): Compute the angle of twist over the length L using φ = T·L/(G·J).

All calculations must be carried out in consistent N–mm–MPa units.
Ignore the instruction to substitute rpm directly for ω; use the correctly converted ω = 2π(rpm/60) in rad/s.
