Compute the elongation of a bar using the axial deformation integral δ = ∫[0→L] P/(A(x)·E) dx.
Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Bar length L = 800 mm.
Constant axial tensile force P = 12000 N.
Material is AISI 1020 steel with E = 200000 MPa, treated as constant.
Cross-sectional area varies linearly as A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L), where x is measured from the left end.
A0 = 250 mm^2 and k = 0.6.
Replace A(x) with the arithmetic-mean area (computed from A at x=0 and A at x=L) and treat that mean area as exact and constant over the full length for the deformation calculation.
Evaluate the integral δ = ∫[0→L] P/(A_mean·E) dx using this constant mean area.
Report the resulting elongation δ in mm.
