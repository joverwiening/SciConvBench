A cantilever beam has length L = 1500 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with E = 69000 MPa.
The constant second moment of area is I = 2500000 mm^4.
A downward point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end.
Use N, mm, and MPa units throughout.
For the strain energy integral, treat the bending moment as the constant value M = P·L over the entire span from x = 0 to x = L, instead of the actual distribution P·(L − x).
Set up the strain energy as U = ∫[0 to L] (P·L)^2 / (2EI) dx.
Integrate this expression over the full length L.
Differentiate U with respect to P to obtain the vertical deflection at the free end using Castigliano's theorem.
Do not use a memorized deflection formula; derive the result through the integration and differentiation steps described above.
