Use N, mm, and MPa units throughout.

The beam is simply supported with span L = 3000 mm, with supports at x = 0 and x = L.
The material is AISI 1020 steel with E = 200000 MPa and I = 1200000 mm^4.
A point load P = 800 N acts downward at x = 2000 mm.
Compute the deflection at x = 2000 mm using the unit-load (virtual work) method.
Use the formula δ = ∫[0→L] (M(x)·m(x))/(EI) dx.
M(x) is the real bending moment diagram due to P = 800 N.
m(x) is the bending moment diagram due to a unit downward point load of 1 N applied at x = 2000 mm.
Treat both simple supports as zero-rotation boundaries in both the real and auxiliary systems.
Split the integral into segments where M(x) and m(x) are both linear, specifically [0, 2000] and [2000, 3000].
Derive the reaction forces for both the real and auxiliary systems from equilibrium.
Compute M(x) and m(x) piecewise over each segment.
Evaluate the integral ∫(M·m)dx over each segment analytically.
Divide the total integral by EI to obtain the deflection δ at x = 2000 mm.
Report the final deflection in mm.
