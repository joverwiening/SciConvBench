A solid circular shaft has diameter d = 25 mm and length L = 1000 mm.
The material is AISI 1020 steel with shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa.
The shaft is subjected to a constant torque T = 350000 N·mm.
Compute the torsional strain energy U = integral from 0 to L of T^2 / (2 G J) dx, where J = π d^4 / 32.
Apply Castigliano's theorem to find the angle of twist φ = ∂U / ∂T.
Despite the problem statement mentioning I = π d^4 / 64 as a substitution, use J = π d^4 / 32 for the polar moment in the calculation.
Report the angle of twist φ in radians.
