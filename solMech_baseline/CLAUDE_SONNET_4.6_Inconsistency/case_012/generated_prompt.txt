Use N, mm, and MPa units throughout.
Assume small deflection and Euler–Bernoulli beam theory.
The beam is simply supported with span L = 2000 mm.
The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with E = 69000 MPa.
A concentrated load P = 3000 N is applied at midspan.
The cross section is rectangular with width b = 30 mm and unknown height h.
The maximum allowable midspan deflection is δ_allow = 3 mm.
Use the central point load deflection formula: δ_max = P L³ / (48 E I).
Use the moment of inertia formula: I = b h³ / 12.
Determine the minimum required height h such that δ_max does not exceed δ_allow.
Ignore the equivalent uniform load substitution; it was a mistake and is not part of the requirement.
