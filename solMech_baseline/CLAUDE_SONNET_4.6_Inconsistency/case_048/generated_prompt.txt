Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Assume linear elasticity.
The shaft is hollow circular with outer diameter do = 60 mm and inner diameter di = 48 mm (wall thickness t = 6 mm).
Shaft length is L = 800 mm.
Applied constant torque is T = 450000 N·mm.
Model the hollow shaft as a thin-walled closed tube and use the mean-area shear-flow relation to compute shear stress and twist.
The mean diameter is dm = (do + di) / 2 = 54 mm, so the mean radius is rm = 27 mm.
The mean enclosed area is Am = π × rm² = π × 27².
The shear flow is q = T / (2 × Am).
The maximum shear stress is τ = q / t, where t = 6 mm.
Also compute the maximum shear stress using the standard torsion formula τ = T × (do/2) / J, where J = π(do⁴ − di⁴) / 32.
Compute the angle of twist using φ = T × L / (J × G), where G = 76923.1 MPa.
Report (i) the maximum shear stress at the outer surface and (ii) the angle of twist over the full length L = 800 mm.
Present both the thin-walled shear-flow result and the exact J-based result for comparison.
