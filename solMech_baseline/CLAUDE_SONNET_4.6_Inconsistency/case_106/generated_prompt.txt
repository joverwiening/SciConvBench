A simply supported beam of span L = 2500 mm is made of structural steel with E = 200000 MPa and constant I = 3000000 mm^4.
A point load P = 1000 N is applied downward at midspan.
Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Define M(x) piecewise over the full span: M(x) = (P/2)x for 0 ≤ x ≤ L/2, and M(x) = (P/2)(L - x) for L/2 ≤ x ≤ L.
Form the strain energy U = ∫ M(x)^2 / (2EI) dx integrated over the full span using the piecewise moment field.
Apply Castigliano's theorem by differentiating U with respect to P to obtain the vertical deflection at midspan.
Compute the numerical value of the midspan deflection in mm.
