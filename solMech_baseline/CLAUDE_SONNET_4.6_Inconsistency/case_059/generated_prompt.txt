Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.

The column is straight and made of structural steel.
Column length is L = 2500 mm.
Flexural rigidity uses E = 200000 MPa and I = 1800000 mm^4.
End conditions are pinned–pinned with effective length factor K = 1.0.
