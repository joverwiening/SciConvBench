Q: At the outer surface where bending stress is maximum, should the torsional shear stress τ be included in the plane stress state (i.e., τxy = τ from torsion is non-zero), or should it be set to zero as stated in the problem introduction?
A: τxy = τ from torsion is non-zero and should be included in the plane stress state. The introduction statement about shear stress vanishing is not part of the complete requirement — ignore it.
