A simply supported beam AB has a span L = 6000 mm.
A concentrated vertical downward load P = 20000 N acts at 2000 mm from support A and 4000 mm from support B.
There are no other loads on the beam.
Determine the internal shear force V and bending moment M at a cross section located x = 4500 mm from support A.
Use N and N·mm as units for V and M respectively.
Use statics sign convention: positive shear V is upward on the left cut face, and positive bending moment M causes sagging (tension at the bottom fibers).
Analyze the free body diagram of the left portion of the beam (from A to the cut at x = 4500 mm) to find V and M at that section.
