Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
The bar is a vertical AISI 1020 steel rod fixed at its top end.
Rod length L = 800 mm.
Rod diameter d = 25 mm.
Cross-sectional area A = π d^2 / 4.
Elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
A weight W = 1500 N is dropped from height h = 200 mm onto a collar at the bottom end.
The rod behaves as a linear axial spring; neglect the mass of the bar.
Use the suddenly applied-load result P_max = 2W to find the peak axial force.
Compute P_max = 2 × 1500 N.
Compute the maximum normal stress σ_max = P_max / A.
Report P_max in N and σ_max in MPa.
