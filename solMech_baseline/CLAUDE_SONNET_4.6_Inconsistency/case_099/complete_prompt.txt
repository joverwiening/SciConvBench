Use N–mm–MPa units. Assume the bar behaves like a linear axial spring and neglect the mass of the bar.

A vertical AISI 1020 steel rod of length L = 800 mm and diameter d = 25 mm is fixed at its top end. A weight W = 1500 N is dropped onto a collar attached to the bottom end from a height h = 200 mm, causing an impact load that stretches the rod.

Let P_max be the maximum axial force in the rod during the impact. Use energy conservation:
W(h + δ_max) = (P_max^2 L)/(2AE),
with δ_max = P_max L/(AE), A = π d^2/4, and E = 200000 MPa.

Solve for P_max and then compute the maximum normal stress σ_max = P_max/A.
