Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Assume linear elasticity.
The shaft is hollow circular with outer diameter do = 60 mm and inner diameter di = 44 mm (wall thickness t = 8 mm).
Shaft length is L = 1200 mm.
Applied constant torque is T = 600000 N·mm.
Model the hollow shaft as a thin-walled closed tube and use the mean-area shear-flow relation to compute shear stress and twist.
The mean diameter is dm = (do + di) / 2 = 52 mm, so the mean radius is rm = 26 mm.
The mean enclosed area is Am = π × rm² = π × 26².
The shear flow is q = T / (2 × Am).
The maximum shear stress at the outer surface is τ = q / t, where t = 8 mm.
Compute the polar moment of inertia using J = π(do⁴ − di⁴) / 32 with do = 60 mm and di = 44 mm.
Use shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.
Compute the angle of twist using φ = T × L / (G × J), expressed in radians.
Report (i) the maximum shear stress τ in MPa and (ii) the angle of twist φ in radians.
