Solve a constrained thermal stress problem for a prismatic structural steel rod using N–mm–MPa units.
Assume linear elastic behavior and uniform temperature change.
The rod has length L = 1200 mm and diameter d = 20 mm.
The rod is placed between two rigid, unyielding supports so that its total change in length is zero.
The rod experiences a uniform temperature increase of ΔT = 35 °C.
Use E = 200000 MPa and coefficient of thermal expansion α = 1e-05 1/°C.
Because the supports are rigid and the total deformation is zero, thermal stress does develop.
Ignore the contradictory statement in the original problem about thermal stress vanishing.
Determine (i) the thermal stress that develops in the rod and state whether it is tensile or compressive.
Determine (ii) the axial force carried by the rod corresponding to that thermal stress.
