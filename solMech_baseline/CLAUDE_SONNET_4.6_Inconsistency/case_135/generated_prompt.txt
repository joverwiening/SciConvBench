Work in N–mm–MPa units.
A short prismatic bar has a rectangular cross section of width b = 40 mm and height h = 120 mm.
The cross-sectional area is A = b × h = 40 × 120 = 4800 mm².
The internal resultant normal force is P = 30000 N (tension).
The internal resultant transverse shear force is V = 12000 N, acting parallel to the width b.
Compute the average normal stress as σ_avg = P / A.
Compute the average shear stress as τ_avg = V / A (uniform/average value over the full cross section).
Compute the magnitude of the maximum in-plane shear stress using τ_max = sqrt((σ_avg / 2)² + τ_avg²).
Report all results in MPa.
