A solid circular shaft has diameter d = 30 mm and length L = 1000 mm.
The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa.
The applied torque is constant at T = 450000 N·mm.
Compute the torsional strain energy using U = ∫[0→L] T²/(2GJ) dx.
Use the polar moment of inertia J = π d⁴/32.
Apply Castigliano's theorem, φ = ∂U/∂T, to find the angle of twist between the two ends.
Despite the earlier mention of using I = π d⁴/64 in place of J, the problem confirms to use J = π d⁴/32 for the calculation.
Report the final angle of twist in radians.
