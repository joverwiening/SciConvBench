Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Assume Euler buckling applies; the column is slender and remains elastic.
The column is made of AISI 1020 steel.
The column length is L = 2000 mm.
The modulus of elasticity is E = 200000 MPa.
The second moment of area is I = 1200000 mm^4.
The end conditions are fixed–pinned with effective length factor K = 0.7.
Compute the Euler critical buckling load using the formula P_cr = π^2 × E × I / (K × L)^2.
Report P_cr in both N and kN.
