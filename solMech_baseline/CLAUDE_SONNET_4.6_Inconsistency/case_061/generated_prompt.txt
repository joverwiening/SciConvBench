Solve a thermal stress problem for a prismatic rod in N, mm, and MPa units.
The rod is made of AISI 1020 steel.
The rod has length L = 1200 mm and diameter d = 35 mm.
The rod is placed between two rigid, unyielding supports so that its total change in length is constrained to be zero.
The rod experiences a uniform temperature increase of ΔT = 65 °C.
Use E = 200000 MPa and α = 1e-05 1/°C.
Assume linear elastic behavior.
Use the physically correct interpretation: rigid supports enforce zero total deformation, so a compressive thermal stress develops.
Determine (i) the thermal stress that develops in the rod and state whether it is tensile or compressive.
Determine (ii) the axial force carried by the rod corresponding to that stress.
