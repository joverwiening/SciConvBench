Part (a): Treat transverse shear stress as uniform and compute the maximum transverse shear stress using τ_max = 1.5 V/A, where A = b times h.
Part (b): Using the shear formula τ = VQ/(I b), compute the shear stress at y = 26.667 mm from the neutral axis (y positive upward, neutral axis at y = 0).
