Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory (small deflection, linear elastic).
The beam is a cantilever of length L = 1200 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
The material is AISI 1020 steel with E = 200000 MPa.
The constant second moment of area is I = 2000000 mm^4.
A downward point load P = 2000 N is applied at the free end (x = L).
Use the standard Euler–Bernoulli cantilever solution with bending moment varying as M(x) = P·(L − x).
Determine (i) the slope (rotation) at the free end in radians and in degrees.
Determine (ii) the vertical deflection at the free end in mm.
