Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
The bar is a vertical AISI 1020 steel rod fixed at its top end.
Rod length L = 1500 mm.
Rod diameter d = 16 mm.
Cross-sectional area A = π d^2 / 4.
Elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
A weight W = 800 N is dropped from height h = 150 mm onto a collar at the bottom end, stretching the rod.
Neglect the mass of the bar and treat the rod as a linear axial spring.
Use the suddenly applied-load result P_max = 2W for the peak axial force (the drop height affects only contact duration, not the peak force under this assumption).
Compute P_max = 2 × 800 = 1600 N.
Compute A = π × 16^2 / 4.
Compute maximum normal stress σ_max = P_max / A.
Report P_max in N and σ_max in MPa.
