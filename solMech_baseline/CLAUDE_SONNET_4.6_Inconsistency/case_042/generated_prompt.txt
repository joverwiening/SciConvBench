Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The bar is prismatic with length L = 800 mm and cross-sectional area A = 300 mm².

The material is structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.

Assume linear elastic axial response.

A tensile load P = 8000 N is applied suddenly (as a step load, not gradually ramped).

Use the energy method for a linear spring to determine the maximum stress during the transient.

Set the work done by the suddenly applied constant load equal to the strain energy stored in the bar at maximum deflection.

The energy balance gives a dynamic amplification factor of 2 for a suddenly applied constant load.

Compute the maximum dynamic stress as 2 × (P/A).

Report the static stress P/A and the maximum dynamic stress 2 × (P/A) in MPa.

Also compute the maximum dynamic deformation and strain energy stored at that instant.
