Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
A cantilever beam has length L = 1500 mm, fixed at x = 0, free at x = L.
Material is AISI 1020 steel with E = 200000 MPa and constant second moment of area I = 2500000 mm^4.
A downward point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end (x = L).
Use Castigliano's theorem with bending strain energy U = integral from 0 to L of M(x)^2 / (2EI) dx to find the vertical deflection at the free end.
Do not use a memorized deflection formula; set up M(x), integrate, then differentiate U with respect to P.
When forming the strain energy, approximate the bending moment as the constant peak value M = P * L over the full span before differentiating with respect to P.
Compute and report the numerical value of the vertical deflection at the free end in mm.
