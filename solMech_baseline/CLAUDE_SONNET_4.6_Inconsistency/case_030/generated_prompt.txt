Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.
The rod has an initial diameter d0 = 25 mm and a length L = 1200 mm.
An axial tensile load P = 45000 N is applied through the centroid.
Material properties are E = 200000 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.29.
Treat the problem as standard uniaxial stress with free lateral contraction governed by ν = 0.29.
Assume linear elastic isotropic behavior.
Compute (i) the average axial normal stress σ = P/A, where A is the cross-sectional area based on d0.
Compute (ii) the axial strain ε_axial = σ / E.
Compute (iii) the lateral (diametral) strain ε_lateral = −ν × ε_axial.
Compute (iv) the change in diameter Δd = ε_lateral × d0.
Report all results in consistent N–mm–MPa units.
