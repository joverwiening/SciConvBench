Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
The bar is prismatic with constant cross-sectional area A = 300 mm² and modulus E = 200000 MPa (AISI 1020 steel).
The bar has four points: A (left end, fixed, u_A = 0), B, C, and D (right end).
Segment lengths are AB = 500 mm, BC = 600 mm, CD = 400 mm.
Axial forces applied (positive = rightward): at B, +18000 N; at C, −12000 N; at D, +16000 N.
No force is applied at A other than the reaction at the fixed support.
Determine the internal axial force in each segment (AB, BC, CD) using method of sections and equilibrium.
Compute the normal stress in each segment as σ = F/A.
Compute the displacement of point D relative to A as u_D = sum of (F·L)/(A·E) for each segment.
Treat each segment's internal force as uniform (prismatic bar, no distributed loads).
The instruction to "treat the internal axial force as uniform along the full length and compute one stress level from the net applied load" refers to a simplified single-stress check using the net load, but still solve segment by segment as described above.
