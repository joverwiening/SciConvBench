A solid circular column has diameter d = 30 mm and length L = 1500 mm.
The material is structural steel with E = 200000 MPa.
The effective length factor is K = 2.0, giving an effective length KL = 3000 mm.
Assume Euler buckling governs.
Use the standard area moment of inertia I = πd⁴/64 in the Euler buckling formula.
Part (a): Compute the Euler critical load P_cr = π²EI/(KL)².
Part (b): Apply a factor of safety n = 2.0 and determine the allowable axial compressive load P_allow = P_cr/n.
