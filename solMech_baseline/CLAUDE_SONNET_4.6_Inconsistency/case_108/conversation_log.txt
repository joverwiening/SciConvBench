Q: What value should be used for the moment of inertia in the Euler buckling formula — specifically, should the standard area moment of inertia I = πd⁴/64 be used, or do you want the polar moment J = πd⁴/32 substituted in place of I?
A: Use the standard area moment of inertia I = πd⁴/64 in the Euler buckling formula.
