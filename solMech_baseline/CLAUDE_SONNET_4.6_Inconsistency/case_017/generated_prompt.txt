Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory.
Use superposition to compute the midspan deflection.
The beam is simply supported with span L = 3000 mm.
Material is AISI 1020 steel with E = 200000 MPa.
The cross-section has constant second moment of area I = 3000000 mm^4.
The beam carries two loads simultaneously.
Load 1 is a uniform distributed load w = 0.3 N/mm over the full span.
Load 2 is a downward point load P = 1200 N at midspan.
For the distributed-load contribution, replace the uniform load by its single resultant W = wL acting at midspan and use the point-load midspan deflection formula for that resultant.
Superpose the midspan deflection from the resultant of the distributed load (treated as a point load at midspan) with the midspan deflection from the actual point load P.
Use the standard midspan deflection formula for a simply supported beam with a central point load: delta = PL^3 / (48EI).
Compute and report the individual deflection contributions and the total midspan deflection.
