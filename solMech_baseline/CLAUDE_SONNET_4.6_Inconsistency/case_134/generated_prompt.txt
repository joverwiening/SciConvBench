A simply supported beam AB has a span L = 3000 mm.
A concentrated vertical load P = 12000 N acts downward at 1500 mm from support A (midspan).
There are no other loads.
Determine the internal shear force V and bending moment M at a cross section located x = 1500 mm from support A.
Use statics sign convention: positive shear is upward on the left cut face, and positive bending moment causes sagging (tension at the bottom fibers).
Provide V in N and M in N·mm.
