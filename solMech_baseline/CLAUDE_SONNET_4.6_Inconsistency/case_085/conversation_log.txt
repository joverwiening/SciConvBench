Q: The problem statement says "take the normal stress to be identical in the two segments," but for a stepped bar with different cross-sectional areas carrying the same axial force, the stresses in the two segments are generally different. 

Should I follow the physically correct approach (stress = P/A computed separately for each segment, giving different stresses), or should I strictly follow the special instruction as written and treat the stress as the same single value in both segments?
A: Use the physically correct approach: compute stress as P/A separately for each segment, which will give different stress values in the two segments.
