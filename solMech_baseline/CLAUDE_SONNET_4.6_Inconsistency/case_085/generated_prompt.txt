Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.
Assume linear-elastic behavior.
The bar is a stepped circular bar made of AISI 1020 steel, fixed at its left end and loaded in tension at its right end by P = 35000 N.
Segment 1 (left) has length L1 = 300 mm and diameter d1 = 12 mm.
Segment 2 (right) has length L2 = 800 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm.
Use E = 200000 MPa for both segments.
The same axial force P = 35000 N passes through both segments.
Compute the normal stress in each segment separately using sigma = P/A, where A is the cross-sectional area of that segment; the two stresses will be different.
Compute the elongation of each segment using delta = PL/(AE), then sum them to get the total elongation of the bar between the fixed end and the load application point.
Determine (i) the normal stress in each segment and (ii) the total elongation of the bar.
