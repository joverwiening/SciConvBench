Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.
Assume linear elastic isotropic material behavior.
The rod is made of AISI 1020 steel with initial diameter d0 = 20 mm and length L = 1200 mm.
An axial tensile load P = 35000 N is applied through the centroid.
Use elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.30.
Use the standard uniaxial stress state; do not apply any plane strain constraint.
Compute (i) the average axial normal stress σ = P/A, where A is the cross-sectional area.
Compute (ii) the axial strain ε_axial = σ / E.
Compute (iii) the lateral (diametral) strain ε_lateral = −ν × ε_axial.
Compute (iv) the change in diameter Δd = ε_lateral × d0.
