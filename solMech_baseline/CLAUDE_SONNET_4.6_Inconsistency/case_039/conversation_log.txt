Q: You have stated to assume plane strain so that lateral strain is zero, but you are also asking to compute the lateral (diametral) strain and change in diameter — which would both be zero under that assumption.

Should the plane strain assumption be removed, and instead the standard uniaxial stress state be used (where lateral strain = −ν × axial strain)?
A: Yes, remove the plane strain assumption. Use the standard uniaxial stress state where lateral strain = −ν × axial strain.
