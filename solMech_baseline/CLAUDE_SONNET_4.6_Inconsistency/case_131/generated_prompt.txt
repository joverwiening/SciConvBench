Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Assume small strains.
The adhesive layer is rectangular with area A = 3000 mm² and thickness t = 8 mm.
A uniform in-plane shear force V = 12000 N is applied to the layer.
Compute the average shear stress as τ_avg = V / A.
The material is isotropic with shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.
Compute the average shear strain as γ_avg = τ_avg / G.
Compute the relative lateral displacement (shear deformation) as Δ = γ_avg · t.
Report three results: (i) τ_avg, (ii) γ_avg, and (iii) Δ.
Treat the adhesive as rigid in shear only in the sense that the formulation uses the standard shear stress–strain relation without any additional compliance correction.
Do not introduce any other assumptions beyond small strains and the given formulas.
