A bar of length L = 800 mm has a constant axial tensile force P = 30000 N applied along it.
The material is structural steel with E = 200000 MPa, treated as constant.
The cross-sectional area varies linearly as A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L), with A0 = 250 mm^2 and k = 0.8.
Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Compute the elongation using the axial deformation integral δ = ∫[0→L] P/(A(x)·E) dx.
Also compute the elongation using the arithmetic-mean area approximation, replacing A(x) with A_mean = (A(0) + A(L))/2 and treating it as exact.
Report both results and compare them.
