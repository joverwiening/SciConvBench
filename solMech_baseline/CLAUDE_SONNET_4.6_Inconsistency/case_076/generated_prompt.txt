The shaft must transmit a torque T = 800000 N·mm.
The allowable angle of twist is φ_allow = 1.0°, which must be converted to radians: φ = π/180 ≈ 0.01745 rad.
Use the formula φ = TL/(JG) with J = πd^4/32.
Solve for the minimum required diameter d.
Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
