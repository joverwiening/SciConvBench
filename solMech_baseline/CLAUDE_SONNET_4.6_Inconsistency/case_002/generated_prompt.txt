Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.
The bar is a stepped circular bar made of structural steel.
The bar is fixed at its left end and loaded in tension at its right end by P = 35000 N.
Segment 1 (left) has length L1 = 400 mm and diameter d1 = 16 mm.
Segment 2 (right) has length L2 = 800 mm and diameter d2 = 40 mm.
Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa applies to both segments.
The same axial force P = 35000 N passes through both segments.
Use the standard approach: compute normal stress in each segment as sigma = P/A, where A is the cross-sectional area of that segment.
This gives different stress values in the two segments.
Compute the cross-sectional area of each segment from its diameter using A = pi*d^2/4.
Compute the elongation of each segment using delta = P*L/(A*E).
Compute the total elongation as the sum of the two individual elongations.
Determine (i) the normal stress in each segment and (ii) the total elongation of the bar between the fixed end and the load application point.
