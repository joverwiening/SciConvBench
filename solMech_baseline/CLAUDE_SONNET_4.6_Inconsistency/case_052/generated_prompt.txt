Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.

A motor delivers power P = 10 kW to a solid circular shaft rotating at 1800 rpm.
The shaft is made of AISI 1020 steel with shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa.
The shaft diameter is d = 35 mm and its length between fixed ends is L = 1000 mm.

Part (a): Compute the transmitted torque T using P = T·ω, where ω = 2π(rpm/60). Convert P = 10 kW to N·mm/s (i.e., 10,000 W = 10,000,000 N·mm/s) and compute ω from 1800 rpm. Do not substitute rpm directly for ω; use ω = 2π(1800/60).

Part (b): Using torsion theory, compute the maximum shear stress τ_max = T·c / J, where c = d/2 and J = π·d⁴/32. Express the result in MPa.

Part (c): Compute the angle of twist φ = T·L / (G·J) over the length L = 1000 mm. Express the result in radians.
