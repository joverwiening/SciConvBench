A bar of length L = 1200 mm has a constant axial tensile force P = 20000 N applied to it.
The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with E = 69000 MPa, treated as constant.
The cross-sectional area varies linearly as A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L), with A0 = 200 mm² and k = 0.6.
Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Compute the elongation using the axial deformation integral δ = ∫[0→L] P/(A(x)·E) dx evaluated exactly (i.e., integrate 1/(1 + k·x/L) analytically).
Also compute the elongation using the arithmetic-mean area approximation, where A_mean = A0·(1 + k/2), and compare the two results.
