Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.
The beam has a rectangular cross section with width b = 50 mm and height h = 80 mm.
The internal shear force at the section is V = 20000 N.
For part (a), compute the maximum transverse shear stress using τ_max = 1.5 × V/A, where A = b × h.
For part (b), compute the shear stress at y = 35 mm from the neutral axis using τ = VQ/(I × b).
Use I = b × h³/12 for the second moment of area.
Compute Q as the first moment of the area above the level y = 35 mm about the neutral axis.
