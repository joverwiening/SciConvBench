Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.
Apply Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.
The beam is a cantilever of length L = 1000 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
The beam carries a uniformly distributed load w = 1.5 N/mm acting downward over the entire span.
The material is AISI 1020 steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The second moment of area is I = 2000000 mm^4.
Use the standard exact Euler-Bernoulli cantilever formulas for a UDL over the full span.
Calculate the maximum deflection, which occurs at the free end (x = L).
Calculate the slope at the free end (x = L).
Do not substitute the UDL with a point load resultant at midspan.
