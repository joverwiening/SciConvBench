Q: At the outer surface where bending stress is maximum, should the torsional shear stress τ be included in the plane stress state (as described in part c), or should it be set to zero (as stated in the middle paragraph)?
A: The torsional shear stress τ should be included in the plane stress state for part (c). The middle paragraph in the incomplete requirement is not part of the complete requirement — ignore it. Use σx = σ (bending), σy = 0, and τxy = τ (torsion) as stated in part (c).
