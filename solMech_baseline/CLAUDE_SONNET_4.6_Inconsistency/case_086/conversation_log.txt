Q: The problem statement says "take the normal stress to be identical in the two segments" and also asks to "determine the normal stress in each segment" — these two instructions seem to conflict with the standard approach (where stress differs because the cross-sectional areas differ).

Which interpretation should I follow: should I compute the actual (different) normal stress in each segment using σ = P/A, or should I treat the stress as identical across both segments as stated?
A: Compute the actual normal stress in each segment using σ = P/A. The stresses will differ because the cross-sectional areas differ.
