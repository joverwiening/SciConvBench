The beam is made of structural steel with E = 200000 MPa.
The second moment of area about the neutral axis is I = 4000000 mm⁴.
The bending moment at the section is M = 3000000 N·mm.
Compute the curvature κ = M / (E × I) in units of 1/mm.
Compute the radius of curvature ρ = 1 / κ in mm.
Use only the formula κ = M/(EI) with the given I; there is no polar second moment J involved.
