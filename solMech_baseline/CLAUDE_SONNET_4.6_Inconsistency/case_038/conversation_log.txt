Q: Because the loading is symmetric, take the end slopes to be zero in addition to the support deflections.

This condition overconstrains a simply supported beam (which normally has non-zero end slopes). Could you clarify what boundary conditions you actually want applied — for example, should the beam be treated as fully fixed (clamped) at both ends rather than simply supported?
A: No, the beam is simply supported — do not treat it as fixed/clamped. Ignore that statement about end slopes being zero; it's incorrect. Use standard simply supported boundary conditions: zero deflection at x = 0 and x = L, with non-zero end slopes.
