Work in N–mm–MPa units.
A short prismatic bar has a rectangular cross section of width b = 30 mm and height h = 100 mm.
At a particular cross section the internal resultant normal force is P = 50000 N (tension) and the internal resultant transverse shear force is V = 5000 N (acting parallel to the width b).
The cross-sectional area is A = b × h = 30 × 100 = 3000 mm².
Compute the average normal stress as σ_avg = P / A.
Compute the average shear stress as τ_avg = V / A.
Compute the magnitude of the maximum in-plane shear stress using τ_max = sqrt((σ_avg / 2)² + τ_avg²).
Do not use the parabolic shear stress distribution; use only the average (uniform) transverse shear stress for τ_avg.
All stresses are in MPa.
