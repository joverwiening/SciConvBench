Q: The problem statement says "use a unit moment … to generate m(x)" for the auxiliary system, but the standard unit-load method for finding a **deflection** uses a unit **point load** (not a unit moment) at the point of interest — a unit moment would give a rotation, not a deflection.

Which auxiliary loading do you want used for the m(x) diagram: a unit point load at x = 900 mm, or a unit moment at x = 900 mm?
A: Use a unit point load at x = 900 mm. The incomplete requirement's mention of "unit moment" was an error in that version. The complete requirement simply says to apply the unit load at the point and direction of the desired deflection, which is a unit downward point load at x = 900 mm.
