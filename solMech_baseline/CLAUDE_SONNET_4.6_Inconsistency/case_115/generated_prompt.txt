Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
The centroidal coordinates of the point are y = 40 mm and z = 15 mm.
The second moments of area about centroidal axes are Iy = 2500000 mm^4, Iz = 1200000 mm^4, and Iyz = 800000 mm^4.

Two results are required.
First, compute the normal stress using simple superposition as if the product of inertia were zero: σ_simple = My·z/Iy + Mz·y/Iz.
Second, compute the normal stress using the unsymmetric bending formula: σ = −(My·Iz − Mz·Iyz)/(Iy·Iz − Iyz^2)·z − (Mz·Iy − My·Iyz)/(Iy·Iz − Iyz^2)·y.
Report both stress values in MPa with sign (tension positive).
