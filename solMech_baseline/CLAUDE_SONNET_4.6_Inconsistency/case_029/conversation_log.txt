Q: You have stated "Assume plane strain in the two transverse directions so that the lateral strain is zero," which would make the lateral strain and change in diameter both zero — but you also ask to compute a non-trivial lateral strain and Δd.

Should the plane-strain constraint be ignored, and instead the standard uniaxial stress assumption be used (where lateral strain = −ν × axial strain)?
A: Yes, ignore the plane-strain constraint. Use the standard uniaxial stress assumption where lateral strain = −ν × axial strain.
