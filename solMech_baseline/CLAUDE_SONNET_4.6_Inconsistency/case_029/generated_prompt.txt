Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.
The brass rod has an initial diameter d0 = 20 mm and a length L = 1000 mm.
An axial tensile load P = 25000 N is applied through the centroid.
Material properties are E = 105000 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.34.
Assume linear elastic isotropic behavior and standard uniaxial stress conditions.
Ignore the plane-strain constraint.
Compute (i) the average axial normal stress σ = P/A, where A is the cross-sectional area based on d0.
Compute (ii) the axial strain ε_axial = σ / E.
Compute (iii) the lateral strain ε_lateral = −ν × ε_axial.
Compute (iv) the change in diameter Δd = ε_lateral × d0.
