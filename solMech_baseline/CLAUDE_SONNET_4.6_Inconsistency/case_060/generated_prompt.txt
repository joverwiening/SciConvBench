Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Assume Euler buckling applies (column is slender and remains elastic).
The column is straight and made of structural steel.
Column length is L = 2000 mm.
Flexural rigidity uses E = 200000 MPa and I = 800000 mm^4.
End conditions are fixed–fixed with effective length factor K = 0.5.
Compute the Euler critical buckling load using the formula P_cr = π^2 E I / (K L)^2.
The effective length to use in the formula is K × L = 0.5 × 2000 = 1000 mm.
Report the result for P_cr in both N and kN.
