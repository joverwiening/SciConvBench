Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
The bar is vertical, prismatic, and linearly elastic.
Bar length L = 3000 mm.
Cross-sectional area A = 300 mm².
Modulus of elasticity E = 69000 MPa.
Material density ρ = 8900 kg/m³.
Gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Only self-weight acts; there are no external loads.
For the elongation calculation, replace the distributed self-weight with a single concentrated load equal to the total weight acting at the free end, and use that as the exact axial force.
Compute the elongation of the bar due to its own weight using the formula δ = ∫₀ᴸ P(x)/(A E) dx, where P(x) = ρ g A (L − x), with x measured downward from the top.
Apply careful unit conversions between meters and millimeters as needed.
