Q: You have stated to assume plane strain in the transverse directions so that the lateral strain is zero, but you have also asked to compute the lateral (diametral) strain and the change in diameter Δd.

Under strict plane strain, both of those quantities would be zero by definition. Should the plane strain assumption be disregarded for parts (iii) and (iv), and instead the standard uniaxial relations (using ν = 0.33) be used to compute the lateral strain and Δd?
A: Disregard the plane strain assumption entirely. Use the standard uniaxial relations with ν = 0.33 for all four parts, including the lateral strain and change in diameter.
