Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.
The rod is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with initial diameter d0 = 40 mm and length L = 500 mm.
An axial tensile load P = 35000 N is applied through the centroid.
Use elastic modulus E = 69000 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.33.
Assume linear elastic isotropic behavior under standard uniaxial loading conditions.
Disregard any plane strain assumption entirely.
Use standard uniaxial relations for all four parts.
Compute (i) the average axial normal stress σ = P/A, where A is the cross-sectional area based on d0.
Compute (ii) the axial strain εx = σ / E.
Compute (iii) the lateral strain εlat = −ν · εx.
Compute (iv) the change in diameter Δd = εlat · d0.
