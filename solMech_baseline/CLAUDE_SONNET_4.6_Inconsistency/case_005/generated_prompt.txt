Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
The bar is prismatic with constant cross-sectional area A = 200 mm² and modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The bar has four points: A (left end, fixed), B, C, and D (right end).
Segment lengths are AB = 700 mm, BC = 600 mm, CD = 500 mm.
Applied axial forces (positive = rightward): at B, +18000 N; at C, −8000 N; at D, +20000 N.
Boundary condition: displacement at A is zero (u_A = 0).
The bar is statically determinate (free at D, fixed only at A).
Determine the internal axial force in each segment (AB, BC, CD) using the method of sections (cut and sum forces from one side).
Determine the normal stress in each segment as σ = F / A.
Determine the axial displacement at D relative to A by summing the elongations of all three segments: u_D = Σ (F_i × L_i) / (A × E).
Report internal forces in N, stresses in MPa, and displacement in mm.
