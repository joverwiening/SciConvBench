Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.
Assume small strains.
The adhesive layer is rectangular with area A = 4000 mm^2 and thickness t = 20 mm.
A uniform in-plane shear force V = 12000 N is applied to the layer.
The material is isotropic with shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.
Compute (i) the average shear stress τ_avg = V / A.
Compute (ii) the average shear strain γ_avg = τ_avg / G.
Compute (iii) the relative lateral displacement (shear deformation) Δ = γ_avg · t.
Use elastic deformation based on G and γ_avg for all calculations; ignore any rigid-in-shear assumption.
