A stepped solid circular shaft is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with two segments in series.
Use N, mm, and MPa units throughout.
Segment AB has length L1 = 300 mm and diameter d1 = 20 mm.
Segment BC has length L2 = 500 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm.
End A is fixed and torque T = 300,000 N·mm is applied at end C.
No intermediate torques are applied, so the internal torque in both segments is T = 300,000 N·mm.
Use shear modulus G = 25,939.8 MPa for both segments.
Determine the maximum shear stress in each segment using the torsion formula τ = T·c / J, where c is the outer radius and J = π·d⁴/32.
Identify which segment governs (has the higher shear stress).
Determine the angle of twist in each segment using φ = T·L / (G·J).
Sum the two segment twists to obtain the total angle of twist at C relative to A.
