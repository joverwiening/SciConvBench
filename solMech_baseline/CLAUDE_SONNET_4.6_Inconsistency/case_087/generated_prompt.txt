Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.
The bar is made of structural steel with E = 200000 MPa.
The bar is fixed at its left end and loaded in tension at its right end by P = 25000 N.
Segment 1 (left) has length L1 = 300 mm and diameter d1 = 12 mm.
Segment 2 (right) has length L2 = 600 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm.
The same axial force P = 25000 N passes through both segments.
Compute the normal stress in each segment using σ = P/A, where A is the cross-sectional area of that segment; the stresses will differ because the areas differ.
Compute the cross-sectional area of each segment as A = π·d²/4.
Compute the elongation of each segment using δ = P·L/(A·E).
Compute the total elongation as the sum of the elongations of the two segments.
Report (i) the normal stress in each segment and (ii) the total elongation of the bar between the fixed end and the load application point.
