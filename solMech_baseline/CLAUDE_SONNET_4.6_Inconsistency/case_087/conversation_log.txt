Q: The problem statement says "take the normal stress to be identical in the two segments" and also asks to "determine the normal stress in each segment" — these two instructions seem to conflict with the standard approach (where stress differs because the cross-sectional areas differ).

Which interpretation should I follow: should I compute the actual stress in each segment using its own cross-sectional area (σ = P/A, giving different stresses), or should I treat the stresses as equal and work backwards from that assumption?
A: Compute the actual stress in each segment using its own cross-sectional area (σ = P/A), giving different stresses for each segment. The same axial force P passes through both segments, but the stresses differ because the areas differ.
