Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
The bar is a vertical structural steel rod, length L = 1200 mm, diameter d = 30 mm, fixed at its top end.
A weight W = 500 N is dropped onto a collar at the bottom end from height h = 200 mm.
Neglect the mass of the bar and treat it as a linear axial spring.
Use A = π d^2 / 4 for the cross-sectional area.
Use E = 200000 MPa.
Apply the suddenly applied-load result P_max = 2W to find the maximum axial force.
Compute the maximum normal stress as σ_max = P_max / A.
Report P_max in Newtons and σ_max in MPa.
