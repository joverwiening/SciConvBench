Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.

A cantilever beam has length L = 1500 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
The material is AISI 1020 steel with E = 200000 MPa and constant second moment of area I = 800000 mm^4.
A downward point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end (x = L).

When forming the bending moment for the strain energy integral, use the constant peak value M = P * L over the full span (i.e., do not use the position-varying expression M(x) = P*(L - x); instead treat M as the constant P*L for all x from 0 to L).

Set up the strain energy as U = integral from 0 to L of M^2 / (2*E*I) dx, substituting M = P*L (constant).
Perform the integration over the full span length L.
Differentiate U with respect to P to obtain the vertical deflection at the free end using Castigliano's theorem.
Substitute the numerical values P = 500 N, L = 1500 mm, E = 200000 MPa, I = 800000 mm^4 to compute the final numerical deflection in mm.
Do not use a memorized deflection formula such as PL^3/(3EI).
