A solid circular shaft has diameter d = 30 mm.
At a critical section, the shaft is subjected simultaneously to a bending moment M = 4000000 N·mm and a torque T = 400000 N·mm.
Consider the stress state at the outer surface at the location of maximum bending stress.
The torsional shear stress is included in the plane stress state; the middle paragraph stating it vanishes is an error and should be ignored.
Part (a): Compute the maximum bending normal stress using σ = M·c / I, where I = π·d^4 / 64 and c = d / 2.
Part (b): Compute the torsional shear stress at the outer surface using τ = T·c / J, where J = π·d^4 / 32.
Part (c): Treat the stress state as plane stress with σx = σ from bending, σy = 0, and τxy = τ from torsion.
Compute the two principal stresses σ1 and σ2 using the standard plane stress principal stress formulas.
Compute the maximum in-plane shear stress using τmax = sqrt(((σx − σy)/2)^2 + τxy^2).
