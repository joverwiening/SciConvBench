Q: At the outer surface where bending stress is maximum, should the torsional shear stress τ be included in the plane stress state (as stated in parts b and c), or should it be taken as zero (as stated in the middle paragraph)?
A: The torsional shear stress τ should be included in the plane stress state. Parts (b) and (c) are correct — τxy = τ from torsion is included. Ignore the middle paragraph that says the shear stress vanishes; that is an error in the incomplete version.
