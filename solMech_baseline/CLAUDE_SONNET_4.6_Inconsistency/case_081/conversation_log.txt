Q: At the outer surface of the shaft, the axial stress and the torsional shear stress both act simultaneously — but the problem statement says "take the torsional shear stress to vanish" when evaluating principal stresses, which seems to contradict part (c) where τxy = τ is used.

Could you clarify: should the principal stress calculation in part (c) include the torsional shear stress τ, or should it be set to zero?
A: Include the torsional shear stress τ in part (c). The principal stress calculation should use σx = σ, σy = 0, and τxy = τ (not zero).
