Determine the normal stress in each segment of the stepped circular bar made of 6061‑T6 aluminum under a tensile load of 20000 N.  
Use N‑mm‑MPa units and assume linear‑elastic behavior with Young’s modulus E = 69000 MPa.  
Segment 1 (left) has length L1 = 500 mm and diameter d1 = 30 mm.  
Segment 2 (right) has length L2 = 600 mm and diameter d2 = 25 mm.  
Assume the same axial force passes through both segments, so the normal stress is identical in the two segments.  
Enforce a common elongation for the segments before combining their stiffnesses.  
Calculate the total elongation of the bar between the fixed end and the load application point.
