Consider a simply supported beam with span L = 3000 mm made of structural steel with modulus of elasticity E = 200000 MPa and constant second moment of area I = 2000000 mm⁴.  
The beam is subjected to a uniform distributed load w = 0.3 N/mm over the full span and a downward point load P = 3000 N applied at midspan.  
Replace the uniform distributed load by its single resultant w L acting at midspan for the purpose of deflection calculation.  
Compute the midspan deflection by superposing the standard deflection cases for the resultant load and the point load.  
Round the final midspan deflection to three decimal places (0.001 mm).
