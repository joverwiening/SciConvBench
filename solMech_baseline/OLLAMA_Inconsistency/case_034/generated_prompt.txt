Use N–mm–MPa units.  
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory and neglect shear deformation.  
A simply supported beam of span L = 3000 mm is made of 6061‑T6 aluminum with E = 69000 MPa and constant I = 3500000 mm⁴.  
A downward point load P = 4000 N acts at midspan.  
By symmetry, the slope vanishes at both supports and at midspan.  
Compute the maximum deflection at midspan and the slope at the supports.  
Report the deflection in millimetres to three decimal places.  
Report the slope in radians to five decimal places as a single negative value indicating downward rotation.
