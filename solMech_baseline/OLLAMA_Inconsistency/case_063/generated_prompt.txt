Use N–mm–MPa units. The rectangular beam has width b = 50 mm and height h = 100 mm, with the neutral axis at the centroid. The internal sagging bending moment is M = 3000000 N·mm. Treat the pure‑bending normal stress as varying linearly over the section depth, using the conventional sign convention: a positive (sagging) moment produces compressive stress (negative) at the top fiber and tensile stress (positive) at the bottom fiber. Report tensile stresses as positive and compressive stresses as negative. Compute the bending stress σ at a point located y = 25 mm above the neutral axis.
