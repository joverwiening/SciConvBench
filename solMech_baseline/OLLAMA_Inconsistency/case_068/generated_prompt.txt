Use N–mm–MPa units for all calculations.  
Take the Young’s modulus E as 200000 MPa.  
Take the second moment of area I as 4000000 mm^4.  
Assume the polar second moment of area J is equal to I, i.e., J = 4000000 mm^4.  
Use the bending moment M = 3000000 N·mm.  
Compute the curvature κ using κ = M/(E I).  
Compute the radius of curvature ρ as the reciprocal of curvature, ρ = 1/κ, and express ρ in millimetres.
