Use N–mm–MPa units.  
Model the cantilever beam of length L = 1500 mm, modulus E = 200000 MPa, second moment of area I = 2500000 mm^4, made of AISI 1020 steel, with a downward point load P = 500 N applied at the free end (x = L).  
Assume the bending moment M(x) equals the constant peak value M = P·L over the entire span for the purpose of forming the strain energy.  
Form the bending strain energy U = ∫₀ᴸ M(x)²/(2 E I) dx using the assumed constant moment.  
Apply Castigliano’s theorem: differentiate U with respect to the load P to obtain the vertical deflection at the free end.  
Express the resulting deflection in millimeters, rounded to three decimal places as a reasonable best‑practice assumption.
