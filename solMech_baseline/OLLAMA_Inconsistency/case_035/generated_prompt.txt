Use N–mm–MPa units. Assume Euler buckling and a solid circular cross section. A column of length L = 2200 mm made of 6061‑T6 aluminum (E = 69000 MPa) must support a compressive load of P = 25000 N with a factor of safety n = 2.5 against buckling. The effective length factor is K = 2.0. Interpret the required factor of safety against buckling as P_cr ≥ n·P. Determine the minimum required diameter d for a solid circular column using P_cr = π^2 E I/(K L)^2, I = π d^4/64, and require P_cr ≥ n·P. Provide the diameter d in millimetres.
