Use N‑mm‑MPa units.  
The shaft is a solid circular shaft with diameter 30 mm and total length 1200 mm, fixed at x = 0.  
A torque T1 = 200000 N·mm is applied at the free end x = L in the positive sense.  
A torque T2 = ‑200000 N·mm is applied at x = 400 mm (a = 400 mm) in the opposite sense.  
The material is AISI 1020 steel with shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa.  
Because the shaft diameter is constant, take a single internal torque equal to the algebraic sum of the applied torques over the full length when computing both stress and twist.  
Construct the internal torque distribution for the segment 0 → 400 mm and for the segment 400 mm → 1200 mm.  
Compute the maximum shear stress in each segment.  
Compute the angle of twist at x = L relative to the fixed end by summing the twist of each segment, and report the angle in radians.  
Report all numerical results with three significant figures (angle of twist to the nearest 0.001 rad).
