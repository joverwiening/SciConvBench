Compute the engineering stress σ as the axial load P divided by the initial cross‑sectional area A, where A = π × (d0 / 2)² and d0 = 12.5 mm.  
Compute the engineering strain ε as the elongation ΔL divided by the original gage length L0, where ΔL = 0.12 mm and L0 = 50 mm.  
Determine the modulus of elasticity E by dividing the engineering stress by the engineering strain (E = σ / ε).  
Use N–mm–MPa units throughout the calculations.  
Assume the cross‑sectional area remains constant (ignore Poisson’s effect) when calculating the engineering stress.
