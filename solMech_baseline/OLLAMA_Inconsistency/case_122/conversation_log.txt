Q: What is the distance from the neutral axis to the top and bottom fibers (i.e., the value of y at the top and bottom surfaces)?
A: The neutral axis is at the centroid, so the distance to each extreme fiber is half the height:

- Top fiber: \(y = +\dfrac{h}{2} = +\dfrac{120\ \text{mm}}{2} = +60\ \text{mm}\)  
- Bottom fiber: \(y = -\dfrac{h}{2} = -\dfrac{120\ \text{mm}}{2} = -60\ \text{mm}\)
