A bar of length L = 1200 mm is subjected to a constant axial tensile force P = 20000 N. 
The material is 6061‑T6 aluminum with E = 69000 MPa. 
The bar’s cross‑sectional area varies linearly along its length according to A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L), where A0 = 200 mm² and k = 0.6. 
Because the area varies linearly, replace A(x) by the arithmetic‑mean area and treat that as exact in the axial‑deformation calculation. 
Compute the elongation using the axial deformation integral δ = ∫₀ᴸ P/(A(x)E) dx, but with the arithmetic‑mean area substituted for A(x). 
Report the final elongation in millimeters, rounded to a reasonable number of decimal places based on best practices.
