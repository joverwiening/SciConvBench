Compute the average axial normal stress, axial strain, lateral (diametral) strain, and change in diameter Δd for a 6061‑T6 aluminum rod with initial diameter 40 mm and length 500 mm, subjected to an axial tensile load of 35000 N applied through the centroid. Use linear elastic isotropic behavior with E = 69000 MPa and Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.33. Assume plane strain in the two transverse directions so that the lateral strain is zero. Use N‑mm‑MPa units.
