Use N–mm–MPa units. Assume the two bars are perfectly bonded to rigid plates so that they undergo the same elongation (compatibility).

Two prismatic bars of equal length L = 800 mm are mounted in parallel between two rigid plates. Bar 1 is structural steel with area A1 = 150 mm^2 and modulus E1 = 200000 MPa. Bar 2 is cast iron with area A2 = 200 mm^2 and modulus E2 = 110000 MPa. A total tensile load P = 30000 N is applied to the top plate and is shared by the two bars.

Because both bars span the same distance between rigid plates, split the total load equally between them and obtain the plate displacement by adding the two bar elongations.

Determine the force carried by each bar, the stress in each bar, and the common elongation of the bars.
