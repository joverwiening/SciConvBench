Use N–mm–MPa units.  
A prismatic bar with constant cross‑sectional area A = 500 mm² and modulus of elasticity E = 200000 MPa is fixed at the left end A (u_A = 0).  
The bar is divided into three segments: AB = 300 mm, BC = 600 mm, and CD = 300 mm.  
Axial point forces are applied at the points: at B a force of +18000 N, at C a force of –12000 N, and at D a force of +10000 N (positive direction to the right).  
Treat the internal axial force as uniform along the entire bar and compute it from the net applied load.  
Determine the internal axial force in each segment (AB, BC, CD), the normal stress in each segment, and the signed displacement of point D relative to A (positive to the right).
