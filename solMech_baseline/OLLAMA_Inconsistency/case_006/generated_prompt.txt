Use N–mm–MPa units. Compute the deflection at x = 1200 mm for a simply supported beam of span L = 2400 mm. The beam material has E = 200000 MPa and I = 3500000 mm^4. A downward point load P = 1200 N acts at x = 1200 mm. Apply the unit-load (virtual work) method with a unit moment at the point of interest to generate m(x). Treat the simple supports as zero-rotation boundaries in both the real and auxiliary systems. Use δ = ∫[0→L] (M(x)·m(x))/(EI) dx to obtain the deflection.
