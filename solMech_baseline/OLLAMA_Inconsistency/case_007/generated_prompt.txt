Use N–mm–MPa units.  
Consider a simply supported beam of span 1800 mm with supports at x = 0 and x = 1800 mm made of AISI 1020 steel (E = 200000 MPa, I = 3500000 mm^4).  
Apply a downward point load of 2000 N at x = 900 mm.  
Treat both supports as zero‑rotation boundaries in the real and auxiliary systems.  
In the auxiliary (virtual) system, apply a unit moment at x = 900 mm in the positive (counter‑clockwise) direction, producing a sagging bending moment.  
Compute the real bending moment diagram M(x) due to the point load and the virtual bending moment diagram m(x) due to the unit moment.  
Evaluate the deflection at x = 900 mm using the virtual‑work formula δ = ∫0^L [M(x)·m(x)]/(E I) dx.  
Report the resulting deflection in millimetres, rounded to three decimal places.
