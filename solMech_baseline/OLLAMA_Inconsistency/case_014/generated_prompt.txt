Use N–mm–MPa units and assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory (small deflection, linear elastic).  
Consider a cantilever beam of length L = 1200 mm fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.  
The beam material is structural steel with Young’s modulus E = 200000 MPa and constant second moment of area I = 2500000 mm⁴.  
A downward point load P = 800 N is applied at the free end.  
Since no external couple is applied at the free end, take the free‑end rotation to be zero and use the constant moment P·L over the full span for the deflection calculation.  
Determine the slope at the free end and report it in either radians or degrees.  
Determine the vertical deflection at the free end and report it in millimetres.
