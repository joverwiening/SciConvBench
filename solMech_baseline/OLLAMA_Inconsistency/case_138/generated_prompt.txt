Use N–mm–MPa units. Compute the maximum bending normal stress at the outer surface of a solid circular shaft of diameter 35 mm subjected to a bending moment of 3 000 000 N·mm using σ = M c / I with I = π d⁴/64 and c = d/2. Compute the torsional shear stress at the outer surface using τ = T c / J with J = π d⁴/32 for a torque of 500 000 N·mm. Treat the surface stress state as plane stress with σx equal to the bending stress, σy = 0, and τxy equal to the torsional shear stress, and compute the principal stresses and the maximum in‑plane shear stress. Report all stress values to three significant figures (or equivalently to two decimal places in MPa).
