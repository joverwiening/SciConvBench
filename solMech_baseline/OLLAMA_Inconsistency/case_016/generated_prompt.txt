Determine the slope at the free end of a cantilever beam of length 800 mm fixed at x=0 and free at x=800 mm, made of 6061‑T6 aluminum with Young’s modulus 69000 MPa and second moment of area 1600000 mm^4, subjected to a downward point load of 2500 N at the free end. Use Euler‑Bernoulli beam theory with small deflection and linear elastic assumptions, assume the free‑end rotation is zero and a constant bending moment equal to P·L over the full span for the deflection calculation. Report the slope at the free end in radians (or degrees) and the vertical deflection at the free end in millimetres. Use N‑mm‑MPa consistent units.
