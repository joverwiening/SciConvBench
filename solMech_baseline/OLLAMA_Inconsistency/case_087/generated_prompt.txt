Use N–mm–MPa units and assume linear‑elastic behavior.  
A stepped circular bar of structural steel is fixed at its left end and loaded in tension at its right end by a force P = 25000 N.  
Segment 1 (left) has length L1 = 300 mm and diameter d1 = 12 mm.  
Segment 2 (right) has length L2 = 600 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm.  
The Young’s modulus of the material is E = 200000 MPa.  
Assume the axial force is the same in both segments, so the normal stress is identical in the two segments.  
For deformation, enforce a common elongation for the segments before combining their stiffnesses.  
Determine (i) the normal stress in each segment, expressed in MPa, and (ii) the total elongation of the bar between the fixed end and the load application point, expressed in millimetres.
