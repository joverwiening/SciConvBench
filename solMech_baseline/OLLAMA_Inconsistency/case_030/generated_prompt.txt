Use N–mm–MPa units and assume linear elastic isotropic behavior.  
The structural steel rod has an initial diameter of 25 mm and a length of 1200 mm.  
It is subjected to an axial tensile load of 45000 N applied through the centroid.  
Material properties are Young’s modulus E = 200000 MPa and Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.29.  
Assume plane strain in the two transverse directions so that the lateral strain is zero.  
Compute the average axial normal stress.  
Compute the axial strain.  
Compute the lateral (diametral) strain.
