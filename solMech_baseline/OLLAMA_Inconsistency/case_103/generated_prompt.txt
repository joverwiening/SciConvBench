Use N–mm–MPa units throughout the analysis.  
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory and neglect shear deformation.  
Consider a simply supported beam with span L = 2500 mm made of AISI 1020 steel, having Young’s modulus E = 200000 MPa and constant second moment of area I = 3500000 mm⁴.  
A vertical point load P = 2500 N is applied downward at the midspan of the beam.  
By symmetry, impose that the slope of the deflection curve vanishes at both supports and at the midspan.  
Compute the maximum vertical deflection of the beam, which occurs at the midspan.  
Compute the slope of the beam at the supports and report this slope in radians.
