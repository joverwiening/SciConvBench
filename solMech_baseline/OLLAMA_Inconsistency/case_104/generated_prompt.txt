Use N–mm–MPa units and Euler–Bernoulli beam theory. Consider a simply supported beam with span L = 2500 mm. The beam carries a uniform distributed load w = 0.7 N/mm over the full span. The beam material is structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa and second moment of area I = 2000000 mm^4. Because the loading is symmetric, assume the end slopes are zero in addition to the support deflections. Determine the maximum vertical deflection of the beam and the location along the span where it occurs. Compute the slope of the beam at x = 0 (left support). Report the deflection and slope values to three decimal places (deflection in mm, slope in radians) and the location of the maximum deflection to the nearest millimeter.
