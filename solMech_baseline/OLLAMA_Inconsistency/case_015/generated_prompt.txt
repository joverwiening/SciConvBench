Use N–mm–MPa units and assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory (small deflection, linear elastic).  
Consider a cantilever beam of length L = 1200 mm fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.  
The beam material is AISI 1020 steel with Young’s modulus E = 200000 MPa and constant second moment of area I = 2000000 mm⁴.  
A downward point load P = 2000 N is applied at the free end.  
Calculate the actual slope at the free end resulting from the applied load (do not prescribe zero rotation).  
Calculate the vertical deflection at the free end resulting from the applied load.  
Report the slope with its sign (negative for downward clockwise rotation) in radians to three decimal places.  
Report the vertical deflection with its sign (negative for downward displacement) in millimetres to two decimal places.
