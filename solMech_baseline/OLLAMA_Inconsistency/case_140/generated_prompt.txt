Use N‑mm‑MPa units throughout.  
The shaft is solid circular with diameter 25 mm.  
Apply axial tensile force P = 15000 N.  
Apply torque T = 400000 N·mm.  
Compute average axial stress σ = P/A where A is the cross‑sectional area.  
Compute torsional shear stress τ = T c / J where c = d/2 and J = π d⁴ / 32.  
Assume torsional shear stress vanishes at the outer surface for principal stress evaluation, so plane‑stress state is σx = σ, σy = 0, τxy = τ.  
Determine the principal stresses and the maximum in‑plane shear stress from that state.  
Report all stress results in MPa and torque‑related quantity in N·mm, rounded to three significant figures (nearest 0.01 MPa for stresses and 0.1 N·mm for torque‑related quantities).
