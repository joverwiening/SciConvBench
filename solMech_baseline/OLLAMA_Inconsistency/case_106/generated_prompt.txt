Use N–mm–MPa units.  
The beam is simply supported with span L = 2500 mm, made of structural steel with E = 200000 MPa and constant I = 3000000 mm^4.  
A point load P = 1000 N is applied downward at midspan.  
Define the bending moment distribution M(x) piecewise over 0 ≤ x ≤ L for the simply supported beam with a central point load.  
Compute the strain energy U = ∫ M(x)^2/(2EI) dx using the piecewise M(x).  
Apply Castigliano’s theorem to obtain the vertical deflection at midspan.  
Report the deflection in millimeters, rounded to three decimal places (nearest 0.001 mm).
