Determine the internal shear force V and bending moment M at a cross section located 4500 mm from support A on a simply supported beam AB with span 6000 mm. The beam carries a single downward concentrated load P of 20000 N applied 2000 mm from support A (and 4000 mm from support B). Use N for shear force and N·mm for bending moment. Use the statics sign conventions: positive shear V is upward on the left cut face, and positive bending moment M is sagging (tension at the bottom fibers). Provide V in N and M in N·mm.
