Compute the maximum deflection at midspan of a simply supported beam with span L = 3000 mm, made of AISI 1020 steel (E = 200000 MPa), constant second moment of area I = 3500000 mm⁴, subjected to a downward point load P = 1500 N at midspan, using Euler–Bernoulli beam theory and neglecting shear deformation. Express the deflection in millimeters and round to three decimal places. Compute the slope at the supports under the same conditions, express the slope in radians and round to five decimal places. Include the units (mm for deflection, radians for slope) in the answer.
