Use N–mm–MPa units.
Consider a cantilever beam of length L = 1500 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
Material is AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The second moment of area is constant I = 800000 mm^4.
Apply a downward point load P = 500 N at the free end.
Approximate the bending moment distribution M(x) as the constant peak value M = P·L over the entire span for the strain energy calculation.
Form the bending strain energy U = ∫ from 0 to L of [M(x)^2/(2 E I)] dx using the constant M.
Apply Castigliano’s theorem: the vertical deflection at the free end δ = ∂U/∂P.
Differentiate U with respect to P, evaluate at the given load, and compute δ.
Express the final vertical deflection δ in millimetres (mm).
