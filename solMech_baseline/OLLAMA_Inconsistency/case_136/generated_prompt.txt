Work in N–mm–MPa units.

A short prismatic bar has a rectangular cross section of width b = 30 mm and height h = 100 mm. At a particular cross section the internal resultant normal force is P = 50000 N (tension) and the internal resultant transverse shear force is V = 5000 N (acting parallel to the width b).

Because both resultants act over the same area, take the maximum in‑plane shear to be the average transverse shear stress alone.

Assuming uniform stress distributions for both (use average values), compute the average normal stress σ_avg and the average shear stress τ_avg on the cross section. Then compute the magnitude of the maximum in‑plane shear stress at the point using τ_max = sqrt((σ_avg/2)^2 + τ_avg^2).
