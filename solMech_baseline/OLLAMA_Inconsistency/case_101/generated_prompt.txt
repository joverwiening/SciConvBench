Use N–mm–MPa units for all calculations.  
Consider a vertical prismatic bar of length 3000 mm and constant cross‑sectional area 300 mm² hanging from a rigid support at its top.  
The bar material has a modulus of elasticity E = 69000 MPa.  
The material density is ρ = 8900 kg/m³ and gravitational acceleration is g = 9.81 m/s².  
Replace the distributed self‑weight by the exact axial force distribution P(x) = ρ g A (L − x), where x is measured downward from the top.  
Compute the elongation δ by integrating δ = ∫₀ᴸ P(x)/(A E) dx over the length of the bar, performing all necessary unit conversions between meters and millimetres.  
Report the final elongation in millimetres, rounded to three significant figures.
