Compute the normal strains εx, εy, εz and the engineering shear strain γxy for an isotropic linear elastic material with Young’s modulus E = 69000 MPa and Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.33. Use MPa for stress and treat strain as dimensionless. The stress state at the point is σx = 120 MPa, σy = 120 MPa, σz = 0 MPa, τxy = 75 MPa, and all other shear stresses are zero. Assume the shear modulus G is given by G = E/(1 + ν) for converting τxy to γxy. Set εz = 0 because σz = 0. Apply the generalized Hooke’s law to obtain εx, εy, εz and γxy.
