Use N–mm–MPa units.  
Assume linear elastic behavior and uniform temperature change.  
A prismatic AISI 1020 steel rod of length 800 mm and diameter 20 mm is placed between two rigid, unyielding supports so that its total change in length is constrained to zero.  
The rod experiences a uniform temperature increase of ΔT = 50 °C.  
Material properties: Young’s modulus E = 200000 MPa and coefficient of thermal expansion α = 2e‑05 1/°C.  
Determine the thermal stress that develops in the rod using σ = −E α ΔT, and indicate whether it is tensile or compressive.  
Determine the corresponding axial force carried by the rod using the calculated stress and the rod’s cross‑sectional area.  
Report the thermal stress in MPa and the axial force in newtons.
