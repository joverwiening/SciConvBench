Determine the normal stress in each segment of the stepped circular bar.  
Determine the total elongation of the bar between the fixed end and the load application point.  
Use N–mm–MPa units and assume linear‑elastic behavior.  
The bar is fixed at its left end and loaded in tension at its right end by a force P = 35000 N.  
Segment 1 (left) has length L1 = 400 mm and diameter d1 = 16 mm.  
Segment 2 (right) has length L2 = 800 mm and diameter d2 = 40 mm.  
The material Young’s modulus is E = 200000 MPa.  
Assume the same axial force passes through both segments, so the normal stress is identical in the two segments.  
For the deformation calculation, enforce a common elongation for the segments before combining their stiffnesses.
