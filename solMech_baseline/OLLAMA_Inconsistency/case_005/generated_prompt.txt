Use N–mm–MPa units. 
The bar has a constant cross‑sectional area A = 200 mm² and Young’s modulus E = 200000 MPa. 
Segment lengths are AB = 700 mm, BC = 600 mm, and CD = 500 mm. 
Applied axial forces are: at B = +18000 N, at C = ‑8000 N, and at D = +20000 N, with positive defined to the right. 
The left end A is fixed (u_A = 0). 
Treat the internal axial force as uniform along each segment and report it as positive when tensile (acting to the right). 
Compute the normal stress in each segment from the internal axial force divided by the area and report it as positive for tension. 
Report the displacement of point D relative to A as positive to the right (tensile direction) in millimetres. 
Round forces and stresses to the nearest whole number and round the displacement to three decimal places.
