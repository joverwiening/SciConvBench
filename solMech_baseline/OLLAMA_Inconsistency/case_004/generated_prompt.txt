Use N–mm–MPa units. The bar has constant cross‑sectional area A = 300 mm² and Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa. The bar is divided into segments AB = 500 mm, BC = 600 mm, and CD = 400 mm. The left end A is fixed (u_A = 0). Point loads are applied: 18 000 N at B (positive to the right), –12 000 N at C (negative to the right), and 16 000 N at D (positive to the right). Treat the internal axial force as uniform along the whole bar and compute one stress level from the net applied load. Determine the internal axial force in each segment (AB, BC, CD), the normal stress in each segment, and the displacement of point D relative to A, reporting the displacement of D as a positive value when it moves to the right.
