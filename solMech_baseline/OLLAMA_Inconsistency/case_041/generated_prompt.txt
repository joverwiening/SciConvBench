Use N–mm–MPa units. Assume Euler buckling applies (column is slender and remains elastic). A straight column made of AISI 1020 steel has length L = 1000 mm and constant flexural rigidity EI with E = 200000 MPa and I = 1200000 mm^4. The end conditions are fixed–free (cantilever), so the effective length factor is K = 2.0. Treat the effective-length factor as affecting only the mode shape, so evaluate the Euler load from π^2 E I / L^2 using the listed flexural rigidity. Compute the Euler critical buckling load P_cr = π^2 E I / (K L)^2. Report P_cr in N and in kN. Use three significant figures (or two decimal places) for the reported Euler critical buckling load values.
