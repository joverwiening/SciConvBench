Use N–mm–MPa units. Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory and apply superposition. Consider a simply supported beam with span L = 3500 mm, modulus of elasticity E = 200000 MPa, and constant second moment of area I = 3000000 mm^4. The beam is subjected to a uniform distributed load w = 0.3 N/mm over the entire span and a downward point load P = 1200 N applied at midspan. Replace the uniform load by its resultant wL acting at midspan and superpose its deflection with that of the point‑load case. Compute the total midspan deflection and express the result in millimeters, rounded to a reasonable number of decimal places (e.g., three).
