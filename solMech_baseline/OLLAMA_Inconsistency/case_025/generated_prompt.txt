Use N–mm–MPa units. Compute the vertical deflection at the free end of a cantilever beam of length 1500 mm that is fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L. The beam is made of 6061‑T6 aluminum with Young’s modulus E = 69000 MPa and constant second moment of area I = 2500000 mm⁴. A downward point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end. Form the bending strain energy by assuming the bending moment is the constant peak value M = P L over the entire span, set up M(x) accordingly, integrate U = ∫₀ᴸ M(x)²/(2EI) dx, then differentiate U with respect to P using Castigliano’s theorem to obtain the vertical deflection at the free end. Do not use a memorized deflection formula; derive it from the energy expression.
