Use N–mm–MPa units. Assume the two bars are perfectly bonded to rigid plates so that they undergo the same elongation (compatibility). Two prismatic bars of equal length L = 500 mm are mounted in parallel between two rigid plates. Bar 1 is AISI 1020 steel with cross‑sectional area A₁ = 300 mm² and modulus of elasticity E₁ = 200 000 MPa. Bar 2 is 6061‑T6 aluminum with cross‑sectional area A₂ = 200 mm² and modulus of elasticity E₂ = 69 000 MPa. A total tensile load P = 20 000 N is applied to the top plate and is shared by the two bars. Because both bars span the same distance between rigid plates, split the total load equally between them and obtain the plate displacement by adding the two bar elongations. Determine the force carried by each bar, the stress in each bar, and the common elongation of the bars.
