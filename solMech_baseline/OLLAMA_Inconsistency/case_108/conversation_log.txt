Q: Should I use the polar moment J = π d⁴/32 directly in the Euler formula, even though Euler buckling normally uses the area moment of inertia I?
A: No. For Euler buckling you should use the area moment of inertia I (for a solid circular section I = π d⁴/64), not the polar moment J.
