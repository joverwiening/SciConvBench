Use N‑mm‑MPa units throughout the calculations.  
Assume the shaft is linearly elastic and has a solid circular cross‑section.  
The shaft is made of structural steel with diameter d = 35 mm and length L = 400 mm.  
The shaft transmits a torque T = 250 000 N·mm.  
Use the bending second moment I = π d⁴/64 in the torsion relations for both shear stress and twist.  
Use the polar moment of inertia J = π d⁴/32.  
Compute the maximum shear stress using τ_max = T c / J, where c = d/2.  
Compute the angle of twist over the length using φ = T L / (J G), with shear modulus G = 77 519.4 MPa.  
Report both the maximum shear stress and the angle of twist to three decimal places.  
Report the angle of twist φ in degrees.
