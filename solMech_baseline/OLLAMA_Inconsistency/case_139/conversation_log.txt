Q: What Poisson’s ratio should be used to determine the current cross‑sectional area, or should the area be assumed unchanged?
A: The complete requirement does not specify a Poisson’s ratio. Make a reasonable assumption based on best practice: use the original cross‑sectional area (i.e., assume the area is unchanged) when calculating the engineering stress.
