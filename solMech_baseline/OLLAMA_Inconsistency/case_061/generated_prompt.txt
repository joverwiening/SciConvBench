Determine the thermal stress that develops in a prismatic AISI 1020 steel rod and indicate whether it is tensile or compressive. Determine the corresponding axial force carried by the rod. Use N–mm–MPa units. Assume linear elastic behavior and a uniform temperature change. The rod has length L = 1200 mm and diameter d = 35 mm and is placed between two rigid, unyielding supports so that its total change in length is constrained to be zero. The rod experiences a uniform temperature increase of ΔT = 65 °C. Use Young’s modulus E = 200000 MPa and coefficient of thermal expansion α = 1 × 10⁻⁵ 1/°C. Take the actual axial strain to be the free thermal strain α ΔT. Report the thermal stress and axial force with three significant figures.
