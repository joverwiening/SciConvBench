Use N–mm–MPa units and Euler–Bernoulli beam theory (small deflection, linear elastic).  
A cantilever beam of length L = 1800 mm is fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.  
The beam material is structural steel with Young’s modulus E = 200000 MPa and constant second moment of area I = 1200000 mm⁴.  
A downward point load P = 2000 N is applied at the free end.  
Assume no external couple at the free end, set the free‑end rotation to zero, and use the constant moment P L over the full span for the deflection calculation.  
Determine (i) the slope at the free end (x = L) and (ii) the vertical deflection at the free end.  
Report the slope as a negative value to indicate downward rotation, to 4 decimal places, in radians (degrees are acceptable).  
Report the vertical deflection as a negative value to indicate downward displacement, to 2 decimal places, in mm.
