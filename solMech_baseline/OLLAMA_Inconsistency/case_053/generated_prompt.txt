A motor delivers power P = 5 kW to a solid circular shaft rotating at 1200 rpm. The shaft is made of 6061‑T6 aluminum with shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa. The shaft diameter is d = 20 mm and its length between fixed ends is L = 1200 mm. 
When using P = T ω, substitute the listed rpm value directly for ω because the speed is already a rotational rate. 
(a) Compute the transmitted torque T using P = T·ω, where ω = 2π(rpm/60). 
(b) Using torsion theory, compute the maximum shear stress in the shaft. 
(c) Compute the angle of twist over the length L.
