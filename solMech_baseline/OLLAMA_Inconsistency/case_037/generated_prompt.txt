Use N–mm–MPa units and Euler–Bernoulli beam theory.  
Consider a simply supported beam of span L = 4500 mm carrying a uniform distributed load w = 0.3 N/mm over the full span.  
The beam material is structural steel with modulus of elasticity E = 200000 MPa and second moment of area I = 2000000 mm⁴.  
Because the loading is symmetric, impose zero end slopes in addition to the simple‑support deflection condition.  
Determine the maximum deflection of the beam, report its magnitude as a positive value, and give the location of this maximum deflection as the distance from the left support in millimetres (also optionally as a fraction of the span).  
Compute the slope at the left support (x = 0) and report it in radians.  
Round all numerical results to three decimal places (three significant figures).
