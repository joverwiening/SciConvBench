Determine the minimum required height h for a simply supported beam of span L = 2000 mm made of 6061‑T6 aluminum (E = 69000 MPa) with width b = 30 mm, carrying a concentrated load P = 3000 N at midspan, such that the maximum deflection does not exceed δ_allow = 3 mm. Use the point‑load deflection formula δ_max = P L^3/(48 E I) with I = b h^3/12. Use N‑mm‑MPa units.
