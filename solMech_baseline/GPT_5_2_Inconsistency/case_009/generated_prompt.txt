Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model two prismatic bars in parallel between rigid plates with equal length L = 400 mm.
Bar 1 is AISI 1020 steel with area A1 = 300 mm^2 and modulus E1 = 200000 MPa.
Bar 2 is cast iron with area A2 = 250 mm^2 and modulus E2 = 110000 MPa.
Apply a total tensile load P = 20000 N to the top plate shared by the two bars.
Enforce compatibility by requiring both bars to have the same elongation (same plate displacement).
Enforce equilibrium by requiring the sum of the bar forces to equal the applied load.
Determine the force carried by each bar.
Determine the stress in each bar.
Determine the common elongation of the bars as an absolute elongation in mm.
Use reasonable rounding best practices (e.g., forces to nearest N, stresses and elongation to about 3 significant figures).
