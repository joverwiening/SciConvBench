Q: When you say “take the normal stress to be identical in the two segments,” do you want me to follow that instruction even though the two diameters are different?
A: No—don’t follow that. With different diameters, the axial force is the same in both segments, but the normal stress is **not** identical: use \(\sigma_i = P/A_i\) for each segment separately.

Q: For part (ii), should the total elongation be computed as the sum of the two segment elongations, \(\Delta = \Delta_1 + \Delta_2\), using \(\Delta_i = \dfrac{P L_i}{A_i E}\)?
A: Yes. Compute the total elongation as the sum of the elongations of the two segments in series:

\[
\Delta = \Delta_1 + \Delta_2,\qquad 
\Delta_i = \frac{P\,L_i}{A_i\,E},\qquad 
A_i=\frac{\pi d_i^2}{4}.
\]
