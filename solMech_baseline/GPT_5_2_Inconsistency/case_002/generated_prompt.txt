Use N–mm–MPa units and assume linear-elastic behavior with E = 200000 MPa.
Model a stepped circular steel bar fixed at the left end and loaded in tension at the right end by P = 35000 N.
Use segment 1 (left) with L1 = 400 mm and d1 = 16 mm.
Use segment 2 (right) with L2 = 800 mm and d2 = 40 mm.
Assume the same axial force P passes through both segments.
Compute the normal stress in each segment separately using σi = P/Ai with Ai = (π di^2)/4.
Compute the total elongation as the sum of the segment elongations in series: Δ = Δ1 + Δ2.
Compute each segment elongation using Δi = (P Li)/(Ai E).
