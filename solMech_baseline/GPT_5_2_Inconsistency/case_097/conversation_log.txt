Q: When you say “take the bending moment to be the constant peak value \(M=PL\) over the full span,” do you want me to use \(M(x)=PL\) for all \(0\le x\le L\) (instead of the usual \(M(x)=P(L-x)\))?
A: No—use the actual bending moment distribution for a cantilever with an end point load:  
\[
M(x)=P(L-x),\quad 0\le x\le L
\]
Do **not** replace it with a constant \(M(x)=PL\) over the span.
