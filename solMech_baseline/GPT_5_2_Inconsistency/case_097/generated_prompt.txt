Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model a cantilever beam of length L = 1500 mm fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
Use material AISI 1020 steel with E = 200000 MPa.
Use constant second moment of area I = 2500000 mm^4.
Apply a downward point load P = 500 N at the free end.
Take the internal bending moment distribution as M(x) = P(L − x) for 0 ≤ x ≤ L.
Neglect shear deformation and include only bending strain energy.
Form the bending strain energy as U = ∫(0→L) M(x)^2/(2EI) dx.
Use Castigliano’s theorem to compute the free-end vertical deflection as δ = ∂U/∂P.
Report the free-end vertical deflection as positive downward (same direction as P).
Do not use a memorized deflection formula; set up M(x), integrate U, then differentiate with respect to P.
