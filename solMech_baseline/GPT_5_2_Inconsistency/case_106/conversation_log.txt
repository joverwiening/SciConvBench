Q: Do you want me to follow your instruction to use the constant maximum moment \(M=PL/4\) over the entire span when computing \(U\), or should I instead use the correct piecewise bending moment \(M(x)\) for a midspan point load?
A: Use the correct **piecewise bending moment \(M(x)\)** for a midspan point load, defined over \(0 \le x \le L\), and compute the strain energy with  
\[
U=\int_0^L \frac{M(x)^2}{2EI}\,dx
\]
(do **not** use a constant \(M=PL/4\) over the full span).

Q: What sign convention should I use for the midspan deflection (take downward deflection as positive, or upward as positive)?
A: Take the midspan deflection **downward as positive** (since the applied point load is downward).
