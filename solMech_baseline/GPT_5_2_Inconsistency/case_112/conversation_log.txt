Q: What value should I use for the mean radius r̄ of the thin-walled tube (e.g., r̄ = (do + di)/4)?
A: Don’t use a thin-walled mean radius here. The COMPLETE requirement says to use the polar moment

\[
J=\frac{\pi\left(d_o^4-d_i^4\right)}{32}
\]

and compute stress/twist from that (i.e., \( \tau_{\max}=T r_o/J\) with \(r_o=d_o/2\), and \(\theta=TL/(JG)\)).
