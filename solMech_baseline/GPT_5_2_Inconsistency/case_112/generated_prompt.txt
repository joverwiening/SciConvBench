Use N–mm–MPa units and assume linear elasticity.
Analyze a hollow circular structural-steel shaft with outer diameter do = 60 mm, inner diameter di = 44 mm, and length L = 1200 mm.
Apply a constant torque magnitude T = 600000 N·mm.
Do not use a thin-walled mean-radius/mean-area shear-flow approach for calculations.
Use the polar moment of inertia \(J=\pi(do^4-di^4)/32\).
Compute the maximum shear stress at the outer surface using \(\tau_{max}=T r_o/J\) with \(r_o=do/2\).
Compute the angle of twist over the length using \(\theta=TL/(JG)\).
Use shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.
Report the angle of twist as a positive magnitude (ignore sign).
