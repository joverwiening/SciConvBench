Use millimeter units throughout.
Given a ductile metal tension specimen with initial gage length L0 = 50 mm and initial diameter d0 = 12 mm.
After fracture, use the fitted-together gage length Lf = 66 mm.
Use the minimum neck diameter at fracture df = 9.6 mm.
Compute percent elongation using the standard engineering definition %EL = 100 (Lf − L0) / L0.
Compute percent reduction of area using the true area-based definition %RA = 100 (A0 − Af) / A0 with A = (π d^2) / 4.
Do not compute or report the nonstandard percent elongation with Lf in the denominator.
Round the final %EL and %RA to one decimal place.
