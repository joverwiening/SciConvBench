Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model a solid circular shaft with diameter d = 30 mm and total length L = 1200 mm.
The shaft is fixed at x = 0.
Apply torque T1 = +200000 N·mm at x = L in the positive sense.
Apply torque T2 = −200000 N·mm at x = a = 400 mm in the negative sense.
Use AISI 1020 steel with shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa.
Use the sign convention where positive torque is the sense of T1, and internal torque T(x) is positive when it acts in that same sense on the cut section when looking from x = 0 toward +x.
Take the angle of twist θ as positive in the same rotational sense as T1, and report θ(L) in radians.
Report maximum shear stress as a nonnegative magnitude in each segment.
Construct the internal torque separately in each segment (0→400 mm and 400→1200 mm) based on the algebraic sum of applied torques to the right/left of the cut.
(a) Construct the internal torque in each segment (0→a and a→L).
(b) Compute the maximum shear stress in each segment using the segment internal torques.
(c) Compute the angle of twist at x = L relative to the fixed end by summing the twist of each segment using the segment internal torques.
