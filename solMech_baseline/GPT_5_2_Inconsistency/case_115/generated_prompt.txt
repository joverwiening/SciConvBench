Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Given a beam cross section with centroidal coordinates of the point (y, z) = (40 mm, 15 mm).
Use centroidal second moments of area Iy = 2,500,000 mm^4, Iz = 1,200,000 mm^4, and product of inertia Iyz = 800,000 mm^4.
Use internal bending moments My = 3,000,000 N·mm about the y-axis and Mz = 2,000,000 N·mm about the z-axis.
Assume a standard right-handed centroidal y–z axis system with the point located at +y and +z as given.
Compute the normal stress using the unsymmetric bending stress formula σ = -((My·Iz − Mz·Iyz)/(Iy·Iz − Iyz^2))·z - ((Mz·Iy − My·Iyz)/(Iy·Iz − Iyz^2))·y.
Report the final normal stress σ in MPa.
State the sign of σ with tension positive.
Do not compute or report the “ignore product of inertia” superposition result.
