Q: When you say “treat the pure-bending normal stress as uniform over the section depth,” what constant value should I use for the stress magnitude (e.g., equal to the stress at the extreme fiber from σ = My/I)?
A: Don’t treat it as uniform—use the standard linear flexure distribution from **σ = M y / I**. So there is **no constant stress value** to assume. The maximum stress is at the extreme fiber with **y = h/2 = 50 mm**.

Q: For the sign convention, should I take a sagging bending moment as positive (causing the beam to curve concave up)?
A: Yes—take a **sagging** bending moment as **positive** (beam curves **concave up**). With that convention, the **top fiber is in compression** and the **bottom fiber is in tension**.
