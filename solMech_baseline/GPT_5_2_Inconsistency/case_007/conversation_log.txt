Q: When you say “treat the simple supports as zero-rotation boundaries,” do you want me to enforce θ(0)=θ(L)=0 in the auxiliary (unit-load) system even though that makes the supports fixed rather than simply supported?
A: No. Do **not** enforce zero rotation at the simple supports.

Both the real beam and the auxiliary (unit-load) beam are **simply supported**, so use the standard simply supported boundary conditions: **deflection zero at x = 0 and x = L**, with **free rotation** (i.e., end moments are zero).
