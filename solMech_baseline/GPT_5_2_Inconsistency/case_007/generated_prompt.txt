Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model a simply supported beam with supports at x=0 and x=1800 mm.
Use material and section properties E=200000 MPa and I=3500000 mm^4.
Apply a real downward point load P=2000 N at x=900 mm.
Compute the vertical deflection at x=900 mm using the unit-load (virtual work) method.
In the auxiliary system, keep the beam simply supported with deflection zero at x=0 and x=1800 mm and free rotation at both ends (end moments zero).
In the auxiliary system, apply a unit vertical point force of 1 N downward at x=900 mm.
Form the real bending moment diagram M(x) and the auxiliary bending moment diagram m(x).
Evaluate δ = ∫(0→L) (M(x)·m(x))/(E I) dx to obtain the deflection at x=900 mm.
