Q: What is the arithmetic-mean area value you want to use for A(x) (i.e., the average of A at x = 0 and x = L)?
A: Use the arithmetic mean of the end areas:

- \(A(0)=A_0=250\ \text{mm}^2\)
- \(A(L)=A_0(1+k)=250(1+0.6)=400\ \text{mm}^2\)

So,
\[
A_{\text{mean}}=\frac{A(0)+A(L)}{2}=\frac{250+400}{2}=325\ \text{mm}^2.
\]
