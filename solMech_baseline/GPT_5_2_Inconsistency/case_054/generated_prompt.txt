Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Treat E as constant with E = 200000 MPa.
Model a bar of length L = 800 mm under constant axial tensile force P = 12000 N.
Use the linearly varying area definition A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L) with A0 = 250 mm^2 and k = 0.6.
Replace A(x) by the arithmetic-mean area and treat that as exact for the axial-deformation calculation.
Use A(0) = 250 mm^2 and A(L) = 400 mm^2.
Use the arithmetic-mean area A_mean = 325 mm^2 in place of A(x).
Compute elongation using the axial deformation integral δ = ∫[0→L] P/(A(x)E) dx with A(x) replaced by A_mean.
Report the elongation δ in millimeters (mm).
