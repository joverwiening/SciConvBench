Compute the arithmetic-mean area as the average of the end areas: A_mean = (A(0) + A(L)) / 2.
Using the axial deformation integral δ = ∫[0→L] P/(A(x)E) dx, compute the elongation of the bar using the constant area A_mean approximation.
