Use N–mm–MPa units.
Assume linear elastic behavior.
Assume a uniform temperature increase.
Model a prismatic AISI 1020 steel rod with length 800 mm and diameter 20 mm.
Use E = 200000 MPa.
Use coefficient of thermal expansion α = 2e-05 1/°C.
Use temperature rise ΔT = 50 °C.
Enforce the zero total change in length constraint due to two rigid, unyielding supports.
Ignore the incorrect statement that thermal stress vanishes.
Use the sign convention tension positive (+) and compression negative (−) for both stress and axial force.
Report the thermal stress as compressive (negative).
Report the axial force as the internal force in the rod with sign, equal in magnitude and opposite in direction to each support reaction.
Provide (i) the thermal stress in the rod indicating tensile or compressive, and (ii) the corresponding axial force carried by the rod.
Round final stress and force values to 3 significant figures.
