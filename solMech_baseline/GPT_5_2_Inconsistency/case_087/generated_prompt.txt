Use N–mm–MPa units and linear-elastic behavior.
Model a stepped circular structural-steel bar fixed at the left end and loaded in tension at the right end by P = 25000 N.
Use E = 200000 MPa.
Segment 1 is the left segment with L1 = 300 mm and d1 = 12 mm.
Segment 2 is the right segment with L2 = 600 mm and d2 = 30 mm.
Treat the two segments as connected in series end-to-end so the same axial force P passes through both segments.
Compute the normal stress in each segment as σi = P/Ai with Ai = (π/4)di^2.
Compute each segment elongation as δi = PLi/(AiE).
Compute total elongation as δtotal = δ1 + δ2.
Report stresses in MPa and total elongation in mm.
