Use N–mm–MPa units and assume linear-elastic behavior.
Model a stepped circular bar of AISI 1020 steel fixed at the left end and loaded in tension at the right end by P = 35000 N.
Use segment 1 (left): L1 = 300 mm and d1 = 12 mm.
Use segment 2 (right): L2 = 800 mm and d2 = 30 mm.
Use Young’s modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Compute the normal stress in each segment using the same axial force through both segments with different areas: σ1 = P/A1 and σ2 = P/A2.
Compute the total elongation as the sum of elongations of the two segments in series: δ = δ1 + δ2 with δi = (P Li)/(Ai E).
Report (i) σ1 and σ2 and (ii) δ between the fixed end and the load application point.
