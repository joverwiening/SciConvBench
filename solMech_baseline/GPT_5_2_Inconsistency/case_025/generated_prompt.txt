Use the N–mm–MPa unit system.
Model a cantilever beam of length L = 1500 mm fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
Use material 6061-T6 aluminum with E = 69000 MPa.
Use constant second moment of area I = 2,500,000 mm^4.
Apply a downward point load P = 500 N at the free end x = L.
Use the actual bending moment distribution M(x) = P(L − x) for 0 ≤ x ≤ L.
Form the bending strain energy U = ∫(0→L) M(x)^2/(2EI) dx.
Compute the vertical deflection at the free end by Castigliano’s theorem as δ = ∂U/∂P.
Report the final vertical deflection in millimeters (mm).
