Use the N–mm–MPa unit system.
Model two prismatic bars in parallel between rigid plates with equal length L = 800 mm.
Use Bar 1 (structural steel) with A1 = 150 mm^2 and E1 = 200000 MPa.
Use Bar 2 (cast iron) with A2 = 200 mm^2 and E2 = 110000 MPa.
Apply a total tensile load P = 30000 N to the top plate.
Assume perfect bonding to rigid plates so both bars have the same elongation (compatibility), and do not split the load equally.
Determine the load split using equilibrium (F1 + F2 = P) and compatibility (δ1 = δ2) with Hooke’s law (δ = FL/(AE)).
Treat tension as positive so F1, F2, σ1, σ2, and δ are positive in tension.
Compute the force carried by each bar, the stress in each bar, and the common elongation δ.
Include intermediate equations (equilibrium, compatibility, and Hooke’s law) along with final numerical results.
Report δ in mm.
Round forces to the nearest N and stresses and elongation to about 3 significant figures.
