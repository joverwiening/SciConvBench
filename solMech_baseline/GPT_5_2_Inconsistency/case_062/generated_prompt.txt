Use N–mm–MPa units.
Assume linear elastic behavior.
Assume a uniform temperature change.
Model a prismatic structural steel rod with length L = 1200 mm and diameter d = 20 mm.
The rod is placed between two rigid, unyielding supports such that the total change in length is constrained to be zero.
Apply a uniform temperature increase of ΔT = 35 °C.
Use E = 200000 MPa.
Use coefficient of thermal expansion α = 1e-05 1/°C.
Enforce the zero total change in length constraint to determine the thermal stress (do not assume thermal stress vanishes).
Use the sign convention: tension positive (+) and compression negative (−) for both stress and axial force.
Determine (i) the thermal stress that develops in the rod and indicate whether it is tensile or compressive.
Determine (ii) the corresponding axial force carried by the rod.
Report the axial force as a signed value and also state its magnitude with the word “compression” or “tension.”
