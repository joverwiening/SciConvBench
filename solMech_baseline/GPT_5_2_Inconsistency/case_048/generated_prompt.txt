Use N–mm–MPa units and assume linear elasticity.
Analyze a hollow circular shaft made of AISI 1020 steel with outer diameter do = 60 mm, inner diameter di = 48 mm, and length L = 800 mm.
The shaft is subjected to a constant torque T = 450000 N·mm.
Compute the maximum shear stress at the outer surface using τmax = T ro / J with ro = do/2.
Compute the angle of twist over the length using θ = TL / (J G).
Use J = π(do^4 − di^4)/32.
Use G = 76923.1 MPa.
Report maximum shear stress in MPa.
Report angle of twist in radians.
