Q: Where in the cross section should the shear stress direction be taken as positive (i.e., what sign convention for τxy should I use)?
A: The complete requirement doesn’t specify a sign convention for \( \tau_{xy} \). Make a reasonable assumption based on best practices—use the standard mechanics convention: take \( \tau_{xy} \) as positive when it acts on the \(+x\) face in the \(+y\) direction (and on the \(+y\) face in the \(+x\) direction). For this problem you can take \( \tau_{xy}=+V/A \); the principal stress magnitudes and \( \tau_{\max} \) won’t change if you choose the opposite sign.
