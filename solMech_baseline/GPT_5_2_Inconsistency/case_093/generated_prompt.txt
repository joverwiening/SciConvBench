Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory with small deflection and linear elastic behavior.
Model a cantilever beam of length L = 800 mm fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
Use material properties for 6061-T6 aluminum with E = 69000 MPa.
Use constant second moment of area I = 800000 mm^4.
Apply a downward point load at the free end with magnitude P = +500 N.
Use standard cantilever-with-end-point-load boundary conditions: at x = 0, y = 0 and θ = 0; at x = L, M = 0 and V = −P.
Do not assume the free-end rotation is zero.
Adopt a sign convention where downward load and downward deflection are positive, and report the free-end vertical deflection as a positive downward value.
Determine the slope at the free end and report it in radians (or degrees).
Determine the vertical deflection at the free end and report it in mm.
