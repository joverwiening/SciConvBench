Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model a stepped solid circular structural-steel shaft with two uniform segments in series: AB with L1 = 300 mm and d1 = 25 mm, and BC with L2 = 700 mm and d2 = 30 mm.
Apply torque T = 450000 N·mm at end C with end A fixed.
Assume the same internal torque acts in each segment (do not distribute torque by stiffness).
Use shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.
Neglect stress concentrations at the diameter step.
Compute the nominal maximum torsional shear stress at the outer surface in each segment using τmax = Tc/J = 16T/(π d^3).
Identify which segment has the larger τmax and therefore governs.
Compute the total angle of twist at C relative to A as the sum of segment twists: θtotal = (T L1)/(J1 G) + (T L2)/(J2 G), with Ji for a solid circular shaft.
Report shear stresses in MPa to 3 significant figures.
Report total angle of twist to 3 significant figures in radians, optionally also in degrees to 3 significant figures.
