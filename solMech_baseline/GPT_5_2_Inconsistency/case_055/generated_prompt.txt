Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Treat the modulus of elasticity as constant at E = 69000 MPa for 6061-T6 aluminum.
Model a bar of length L = 1200 mm under a constant axial tensile force P = 20000 N along the entire length.
Neglect self-weight and any other axial load variation.
Use the exact linearly varying cross-sectional area A(x) = 200·(1 + 0.6·x/1200) mm² with x measured from the left end.
Compute the elongation using the axial deformation integral δ = ∫ from 0 to L of P/(A(x)E) dx.
Report the elongation in millimeters (mm).
Round the reported elongation to 3 significant figures.
