Use N–mm–MPa units and assume linear elasticity.
Analyze a hollow circular structural-steel shaft with outer diameter do = 50 mm, inner diameter di = 42 mm, length L = 1200 mm, under constant torque T = 450000 N·mm.
For the thin-walled closed-tube (mean-area) modeling step, use the mid-thickness mean radius rm = (ro + ri)/2 = (do + di)/4 = 23 mm.
Compute (i) the maximum shear stress at the outer surface using the solid-shaft torsion formula τmax = T ro / J with ro = do/2.
Compute (ii) the angle of twist using θ = T L / (J G).
Use J = π(do^4 − di^4)/32.
Use shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.
Do not use Poisson’s ratio.
Report the angle of twist in radians, optionally also giving degrees in parentheses.
