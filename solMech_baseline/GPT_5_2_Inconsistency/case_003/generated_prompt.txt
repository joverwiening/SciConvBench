Use N–mm–MPa units and linear-elastic behavior.
Model a stepped circular bar made of 6061-T6 aluminum with E = 69000 MPa.
The bar is fixed at the left end.
A single axial tensile load P = 20000 N is applied at the free right end with no other axial loads along the bar.
Segment 1 (left) has length L1 = 500 mm and diameter d1 = 30 mm.
Segment 2 (right) has length L2 = 600 mm and diameter d2 = 25 mm.
Assume the internal axial force is P in both segments.
Compute normal stress in each segment using σi = P/Ai with Ai = (π di^2)/4, so stresses differ between segments.
Compute total elongation as δ = δ1 + δ2 with δi = (P Li)/(Ai E).
Determine (i) the normal stress in each segment and (ii) the total elongation between the fixed end and the load application point.
