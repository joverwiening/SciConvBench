Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory with small deflection and linear elastic behavior.
Model a cantilever beam of length L = 800 mm fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
Use material properties for 6061-T6 aluminum with E = 69000 MPa.
Use constant second moment of area I = 1600000 mm^4.
Apply a downward point load P = 2500 N at the free end x = L.
Use standard cantilever-with-tip-point-load boundary conditions.
Use the corresponding bending moment distribution M(x) = P(L − x).
Determine the slope at the free end.
Report the free-end slope as a positive magnitude in radians, optionally also in degrees.
Determine the vertical deflection at the free end.
Report the free-end vertical deflection as a positive magnitude in mm.
