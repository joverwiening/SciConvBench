At axial load P = 15000 N, the measured elongation over the gage length is ΔL = 0.15 mm in the linear-elastic region.
Compute engineering stress at this load using the original cross-sectional area A0 based on d0, assuming the diameter remains d0 (do not apply volume constancy or any area reduction).
Compute engineering strain at this load using the original gage length, ε = ΔL/L0 (do not use L0 + ΔL in the denominator).
