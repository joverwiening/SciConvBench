Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Given a rectangular beam cross section with width b = 50 mm and height h = 80 mm.
At the section of interest, the internal shear force is V = 20000 N.
Assume transverse shear stress is treated as uniform through the depth for part (a), and take the maximum shear at the outer fibers using τ_max = 1.5 V/A.
Compute A as the full cross-sectional area.
For part (b), compute the shear stress using τ = VQ/(I b).
Use I = b h^3/12 for the rectangle about the neutral axis.
Evaluate τ at y = +35 mm, where y is measured from the neutral axis with y positive upward.
Use the constant section width b = 50 mm in τ = VQ/(I b).
Use the top surface at y = +40 mm and bottom surface at y = −40 mm.
Compute Q as the first moment of area of the portion of the cross section above the level y, so for y = +35 mm take the area from y = +35 mm to y = +40 mm.
Report τ in part (b) as a magnitude only in MPa.
