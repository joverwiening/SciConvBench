Q: Do you want me to follow your instruction to enforce zero rotation at the free end (even though a cantilever with an end point load normally has a nonzero free-end slope)?
A: No. Do **not** enforce zero rotation at the free end.

Use the standard cantilever-with-end-point-load boundary conditions: fixed end has zero deflection and zero slope; free end has zero moment and zero shear (with the applied point load providing the shear).

Q: To confirm before I proceed: should I compute the slope and deflection using the standard Euler–Bernoulli cantilever end-load formulas (equivalently integrating with M(x)=P(L−x)) rather than using a constant moment PL over the span?
A: Yes—use the standard Euler–Bernoulli cantilever with an end point load: integrate with the bending moment distribution \(M(x)=P(L-x)\) (with \(x\) from the fixed end). Do **not** use a constant moment \(PL\) over the span.
