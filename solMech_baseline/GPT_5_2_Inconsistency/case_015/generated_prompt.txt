Use N–mm–MPa units.
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory with small deflection and linear elastic behavior.
Model a cantilever beam of length L = 1200 mm fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
Use material properties for AISI 1020 steel with E = 200000 MPa.
Use constant second moment of area I = 2000000 mm^4.
Apply a downward point load P = 2000 N at the free end x = L.
Use standard cantilever-with-end-point-load boundary conditions: at x = 0, deflection y(0) = 0 and slope y′(0) = 0; at x = L, bending moment is zero and shear is provided by the applied point load.
Compute using the standard Euler–Bernoulli formulation by integrating with bending moment distribution M(x) = P(L − x), with x measured from the fixed end.
Do not enforce zero rotation at the free end.
Do not use a constant moment PL over the span.
Determine the slope at the free end and report it in radians (or degrees).
Determine the vertical deflection at the free end and report it as a positive magnitude in mm.
