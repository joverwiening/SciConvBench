Use N–mm–MPa units.
Assume Euler buckling applies because the column is slender and remains elastic.
Use a straight column made of AISI 1020 steel with length L = 2000 mm.
Use E = 200000 MPa and I = 1200000 mm^4.
Use fixed–pinned end conditions with effective length factor K = 0.7 exactly as given.
Compute the Euler critical buckling load using \(P_{cr} = \pi^2 E I / (K L)^2\).
Use \(\pi = 3.141592653589793\).
Report \(P_{cr}\) in N and in kN.
Round the reported values to 3 significant figures in both N and kN.
