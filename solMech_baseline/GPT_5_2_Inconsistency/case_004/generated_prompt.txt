Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model a prismatic axial bar with constant cross-sectional area A = 300 mm^2.
Use AISI 1020 steel with modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Use geometry with points A (left end), B, C, D (right end) and segment lengths AB = 500 mm, BC = 600 mm, CD = 400 mm.
Apply axial point loads with positive to the right: at B, +18000 N; at C, −12000 N; at D, +16000 N.
Impose boundary condition u_A = 0 at the fixed left end A.
Assume no distributed axial load along the bar and neglect self-weight.
Determine the axial reaction at A from overall equilibrium with the applied loads.
Compute the internal axial force separately in each segment AB, BC, and CD using equilibrium with the applied point loads.
Use sign convention for internal force N: N > 0 in tension and N < 0 in compression.
Compute normal stress in each segment as σ = N/A with sign convention σ > 0 in tension and σ < 0 in compression.
Compute the signed displacement of point D relative to A with positive to the right using piecewise-constant internal force: u_D = Σ (N_i L_i)/(A E) over segments AB, BC, and CD.
