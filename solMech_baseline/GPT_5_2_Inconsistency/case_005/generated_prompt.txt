Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model a prismatic axial bar with constant cross-sectional area A = 200 mm^2 and modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Use segment lengths AB = 700 mm, BC = 600 mm, and CD = 500 mm.
Apply point loads (positive to the right) of +18000 N at B, −8000 N at C, and +20000 N at D.
Fix the left end at A with u_A = 0 and include the axial reaction at A from equilibrium.
Assume there are no other axial loads or reactions besides the loads at B, C, D and the reaction at A.
Neglect self-weight.
Use the sign convention tension positive (+) and compression negative (−) for internal axial force N and normal stress σ.
Compute the internal axial force separately in each segment AB, BC, and CD using equilibrium with the applied point loads.
Compute the normal stress in each segment using σ = N/A with the same sign convention.
Compute the displacement of point D relative to A using piecewise-constant internal force: u_D − u_A = Σ (N_i L_i)/(A E) over segments AB, BC, CD.
Report u_D as a signed displacement with positive to the right.
Round final forces and stresses to 3 significant figures and displacement to 3 significant figures in mm unless an intermediate value is exact.
