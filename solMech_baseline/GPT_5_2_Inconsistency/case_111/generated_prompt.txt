Solve a linear-elastic, small-deformation axial tension problem for a straight, prismatic solid circular structural-steel rod.
Use consistent N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Use Young’s modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Use rod diameter d = 12 mm and gage length L = 800 mm.
Use an axial tensile force applied through the centroid P = +30000 N with tension taken as positive.
Compute the cross-sectional area A of the solid circular rod and show this intermediate step.
Compute the average normal stress σ_avg = P/A and report it as positive.
Compute the elastic axial strain ε = σ_avg/E and report it as a dimensionless value and also in microstrain (με).
Compute the axial elongation of the gage length δ = εL and report it as positive.
Use yield strength S_y = 250 MPa.
Compute the factor of safety against yielding using n = S_y / σ_avg.
Include at least a brief unit consistency check.
Report final numerical results to about 3 significant figures.
