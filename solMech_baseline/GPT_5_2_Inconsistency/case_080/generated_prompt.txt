Given a standard tension-test specimen with initial diameter d0 = 12.5 mm and initial gage length L0 = 50 mm.
Given that during the linear-elastic portion of the test an axial load P = 12000 N produces an elongation ΔL = 0.12 mm over the gage length.
Compute the engineering stress at this load using the initial cross-sectional area A0 = (π d0^2)/4.
Compute the engineering strain at this load using the standard definition ε = ΔL/L0.
Determine the modulus of elasticity from E = σ/ε using the computed engineering stress and engineering strain.
