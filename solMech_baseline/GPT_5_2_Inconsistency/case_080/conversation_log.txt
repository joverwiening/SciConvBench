Q: When you say “current cross-sectional area,” should I compute it assuming the specimen volume stays constant during elastic deformation (i.e., \(A\,L = A_0\,L_0\))?
A: No. Use the **initial cross-sectional area** \(A_0 = \frac{\pi d_0^2}{4}\) for engineering stress. Do **not** apply constant-volume (that would be for true stress/strain approximations, not engineering values).

Q: For the engineering strain, should I use the original gage length definition \( \varepsilon = \Delta L / L_0 \), or do you want the nonstandard definition using the current elongated length \( \varepsilon = \Delta L / (L_0+\Delta L) \)?
A: Use the **standard engineering strain definition**:

\[
\varepsilon = \frac{\Delta L}{L_0}
\]

Do **not** use \(\Delta L/(L_0+\Delta L)\).
