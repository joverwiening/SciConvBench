A standard tension-test specimen has an initial diameter d0 = 12.5 mm and an initial gage length L0 = 50 mm. During the linear-elastic portion of the test, an axial load of P = 12000 N produces an elongation of ΔL = 0.12 mm over the gage length.

Compute the engineering stress and engineering strain at this load, and determine the modulus of elasticity E from the slope E = σ/ε.
