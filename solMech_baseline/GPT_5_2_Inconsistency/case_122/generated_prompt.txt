Use N–mm–MPa units.
Model a rectangular beam cross-section with width b = 50 mm and height h = 120 mm.
At the section, use internal bending moment M = 4,000,000 N·mm with sagging taken as M > 0.
Measure y from the neutral axis (centroid), with y positive upward.
Use the standard linear bending stress distribution σ(y) = −(M y / I).
Use the sign convention that for sagging M > 0 the top fiber (y = +h/2) is in compression (negative σ) and the bottom fiber (y = −h/2) is in tension (positive σ).
Use the centroidal second moment of area about the horizontal neutral axis I = b h^3 / 12 = 7.20×10^6 mm^4.
Compute σ at y = 0 mm.
Compute σ at the top fiber using y = +h/2.
Compute σ at the bottom fiber using y = −h/2.
