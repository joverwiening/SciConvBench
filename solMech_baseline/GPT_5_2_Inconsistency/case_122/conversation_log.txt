Q: When you say “treat the pure-bending normal stress as uniform over the section depth,” what constant stress distribution should I use (e.g., set σ(y)=σmax everywhere, or set σ(y)=σNA everywhere)?
A: Don’t use a uniform stress distribution. Use the standard linear bending stress distribution:

\[
\sigma(y)= -\frac{M\,y}{I}
\]

So at \(y=0\), \(\sigma=0\); at the top and bottom fibers use \(y=\pm h/2\).

=== 2026-04-12 21:14:15 ===
Q: Confirm the sign convention for sagging moment: should the top fiber be in compression (negative σ) and the bottom fiber in tension (positive σ) with y positive upward?
A: Yes. With \(y\) positive upward and a sagging moment \(M>0\), use \(\sigma(y)=-(My/I)\): the top fiber (\(y=+h/2\)) is in compression (negative \(\sigma\)) and the bottom fiber (\(y=-h/2\)) is in tension (positive \(\sigma\)).

Q: What value should I use for the second moment of area \(I\) about the centroidal horizontal axis: \(I=\frac{b h^{3}}{12}\)?
A: Yes—use the centroidal (neutral-axis) second moment of area for a rectangle about the horizontal axis:

\[
I=\frac{b h^{3}}{12}=\frac{50\,(120)^3}{12}=7.20\times 10^{6}\ \text{mm}^4.
\]
