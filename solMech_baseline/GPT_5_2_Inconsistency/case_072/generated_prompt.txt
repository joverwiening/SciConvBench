Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model a solid circular shaft with diameter d = 30 mm and length L = 1000 mm made of 6061-T6 aluminum with shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa.
Apply a constant torque T = 450000 N·mm along the shaft.
Compute the torsional strain energy using U = ∫ from 0 to L of T^2/(2 G J) dx.
Use the polar moment of inertia J = π d^4/32 throughout and do not substitute the bending second moment I = π d^4/64.
Use Castigliano’s theorem to obtain the angle of twist as φ = ∂U/∂T.
Report the final angle of twist between the ends in radians.
Round the final result to 3 significant figures.
