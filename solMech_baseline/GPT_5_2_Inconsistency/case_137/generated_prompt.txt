Analyze the stress state at the outer surface of a solid circular shaft at the location of maximum bending stress.
Given diameter d = 45 mm, bending moment M = 2,000,000 N·mm, and torque T = 500,000 N·mm at the critical section.
For (a), compute the maximum bending normal stress at the surface using σ = M c / I with I = π d^4/64 and c = d/2.
For (b), compute the torsional shear stress at the surface using τ = T c / J with J = π d^4/32 and c = d/2.
For (c), treat the surface stress state as plane stress with σx = +σ from part (a) (tensile), σy = 0, and τxy = τ from part (b).
For (c), compute the principal stresses in MPa using the convention σ1 ≥ σ2 and take σ3 = 0 for plane stress.
For (c), compute the maximum in-plane shear stress as τmax = (σ1 − σ2)/2 in MPa.
