Use N–mm–MPa units.
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory with small deflection and linear elastic behavior.
Model a cantilever beam of length L = 1200 mm fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
Use material properties E = 200000 MPa and constant second moment of area I = 2500000 mm^4.
Apply a point load P = 800 N at the free end acting downward in the negative y-direction.
Use standard cantilever-with-end-point-load boundary conditions: y(0)=0 and θ(0)=0 at the fixed end, and M(L)=0 and V(L)=P at the free end.
Do not impose zero rotation at the free end.
Use the sign convention where downward deflection is negative and the corresponding free-end rotation is negative (clockwise) for this load case.
Determine (i) the slope at the free end θ(L) and (ii) the vertical deflection at the free end y(L).
Report slope in radians and optionally include the degree equivalent in parentheses.
Report deflection in mm with a negative sign for downward.
Round final numeric results to 3 significant figures (or about 3 decimal places where appropriate).
