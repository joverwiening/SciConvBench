Use N–mm–MPa units and assume linear-elastic behavior.
Analyze a stepped circular bar made of AISI 1020 steel fixed at the left end and loaded in tension at the right end by P = 30000 N.
Use segment 1 (left): L1 = 300 mm and d1 = 25 mm.
Use segment 2 (right): L2 = 1000 mm and d2 = 35 mm.
Use E = 200000 MPa (= 200000 N/mm^2).
Assume the same axial force P passes through both segments.
Compute normal stresses separately as σ1 = P/A1 and σ2 = P/A2 with Ai = (π di^2)/4.
Compute total elongation as the sum of series elongations δ = δ1 + δ2 with δi = (P Li)/(Ai E).
Report stresses and elongation to 3 significant figures.
