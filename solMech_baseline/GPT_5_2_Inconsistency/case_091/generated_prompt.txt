Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory applies.
A simply supported beam has span L = 3500 mm.
Material is structural steel with E = 200000 MPa.
Second moment of area is constant with I = 3000000 mm^4.
The beam carries a uniform distributed load w = 0.3 N/mm over the full span.
The beam also carries a downward point load P = 1200 N at midspan.
For the distributed-load contribution, replace the uniform load by a single resultant load wL acting at midspan.
Compute the midspan deflection by superposition of the two standard midspan point-load cases (one from P and one from wL).
Report the midspan deflection as a positive downward magnitude.
Report the final midspan deflection in millimeters (mm).
Round the final midspan deflection to 3 decimal places.
