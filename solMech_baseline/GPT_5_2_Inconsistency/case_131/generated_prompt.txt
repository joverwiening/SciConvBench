Use N–mm–MPa units and assume small strains.
Given a rectangular adhesive layer with area A = 3000 mm^2 and thickness t = 8 mm.
Given a uniform in-plane shear force V = 12000 N producing an average shear stress τ_avg = V/A.
Treat the adhesive as deformable in shear using γ_avg = τ_avg/G.
Use an isotropic shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.
Compute (i) the average shear stress τ_avg, (ii) the average shear strain γ_avg, and (iii) the relative lateral displacement between faces Δ = γ_avg·t.
Report τ_avg in MPa, γ_avg as dimensionless, and Δ in mm.
