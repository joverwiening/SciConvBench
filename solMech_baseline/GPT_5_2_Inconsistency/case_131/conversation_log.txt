Q: When you say “treat the adhesive layer as rigid in shear so the two faces undergo no relative slip,” should I instead assume the usual uniform shear deformation relation \( \gamma_{\text{avg}}=\tau_{\text{avg}}/G \) (i.e., deformable in shear)?
A: Assume the usual deformable-in-shear relation:  
\[
\gamma_{\text{avg}}=\frac{\tau_{\text{avg}}}{G}
\]
Do **not** assume “rigid in shear / no relative slip” (that statement is not part of the complete requirement).
