Q: When you say “treat the pure-bending normal stress as uniform over the section depth,” do you mean to ignore the usual linear variation (σ = My/I) and instead take σ to be a constant equal to the extreme-fiber stress magnitude throughout the depth?
A: No. Use the usual linear bending-stress distribution:  
\[
\sigma(y)= -\frac{M\,y}{I}
\]
(with \(y\) positive upward and \(M\) sagging). Do **not** assume uniform stress over the depth.

=== 2026-04-12 21:02:01 ===
Q: What sign convention do you want for “sagging” in the formula (i.e., should a sagging moment be taken as \(M=+3{,}000{,}000\ \text{N·mm}\) in \(\sigma(y)=-My/I\))?
A: Take sagging as **positive**: use \(M=+3{,}000{,}000\ \text{N·mm}\) in  
\[
\sigma(y)=-\frac{My}{I},
\]
with \(y\) positive upward (so \(\sigma<0\) at the top fiber and \(\sigma>0\) at the bottom fiber).

=== 2026-04-12 21:02:04 ===
Q: What reference do you want for the “top fiber” and “bottom fiber” y-values (i.e., should I take them as \(y=+h/2=+50\) mm and \(y=-h/2=-50\) mm measured from the centroid)?
A: Yes—measure from the neutral axis (centroid):  
- Top fiber: \(y=+h/2=+50\) mm  
- Bottom fiber: \(y=-h/2=-50\) mm
