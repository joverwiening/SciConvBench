Use N–mm–MPa units.
Assume linear elastic behavior.
Assume a uniform temperature increase.
Model a prismatic AISI 1020 steel rod with length L = 1200 mm and diameter d = 35 mm.
The rod is placed between two rigid, unyielding supports such that the total change in length is zero (fully restrained).
Use E = 200000 MPa.
Use coefficient of thermal expansion α = 1e-05 1/°C.
Use temperature increase ΔT = 65 °C.
Ignore the statement claiming thermal stress vanishes; instead use the fully restrained relation σ = E α ΔT with compressive sign.
Use the sign convention tension positive and compression negative.
Determine (i) the thermal stress in the rod and indicate it is compressive (negative).
Determine (ii) the corresponding axial force carried by the rod.
Report the axial force as the internal force in the rod, equal in magnitude and opposite in direction to the support reactions, with compression negative.
