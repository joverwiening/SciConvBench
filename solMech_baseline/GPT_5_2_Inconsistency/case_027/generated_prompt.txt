Use the N–mm–MPa unit system.
Model the AISI 1020 steel rod as a linear elastic axial spring and neglect the rod mass.
Rod geometry is L = 1500 mm and d = 16 mm.
Rod is fixed at the top end with a collar attached at the bottom end.
A weight W = 800 N is dropped onto the collar from height h = 150 mm.
Neglect the rod’s self-weight in the energy balance.
Use energy conservation: W(h + δ_max) = (P_max^2 L)/(2AE).
Use δ_max = P_max L/(AE).
Use A = π d^2/4.
Use E = 200000 MPa.
Solve the resulting equation for P_max.
Compute σ_max = P_max/A.
Report P_max numerically in N.
Report σ_max numerically in MPa.
