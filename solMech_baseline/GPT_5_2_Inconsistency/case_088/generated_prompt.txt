Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model a prismatic axial bar with constant cross-sectional area A = 500 mm^2 and modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Use the geometry AB = 300 mm, BC = 600 mm, and CD = 300 mm with points ordered A–B–C–D from left to right.
Apply point axial loads with positive to the right: at B, +18000 N; at C, −12000 N; at D, +10000 N.
Impose the boundary condition u_A = 0 at the fixed left end A.
Assume static equilibrium of the entire bar by introducing an unknown axial reaction at A such that the sum of axial forces on the whole bar is zero.
Compute the internal axial force separately in each segment AB, BC, and CD based on the applied point loads and the reaction at A.
Use the sign convention that internal axial force N is positive in tension and negative in compression.
Compute the normal stress in each segment using the segment internal force and the constant area.
Compute the displacement of point D relative to A, reporting displacement positive to the right.
