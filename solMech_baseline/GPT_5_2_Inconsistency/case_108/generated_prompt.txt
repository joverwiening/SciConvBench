Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Analyze a solid circular structural-steel column with diameter d = 30 mm and length L = 1500 mm.
Use modulus of elasticity E = 200000 MPa.
Use effective length factor K = 2.0 corresponding to a fixed–free (cantilever) end condition.
Assume Euler buckling governs.
Use the standard Euler buckling formula with the area moment of inertia for bending.
Use I = π d^4 / 64 for the solid circular section.
Compute the Euler critical load P_cr.
Use factor of safety n = 2.0.
Compute the allowable axial compressive load as P_allow = P_cr / n.
Report P_cr in newtons (N).
