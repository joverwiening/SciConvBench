Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory and use superposition.
Model a simply supported beam with span L = 3000 mm.
Use AISI 1020 steel with E = 200000 MPa.
Use constant second moment of area I = 3000000 mm^4.
Apply two simultaneous loads: a uniform distributed load w = 0.3 N/mm over the full span and a downward point load P = 1200 N at midspan.
Do not approximate the UDL by a single resultant; use the standard simply supported UDL deflection case.
Use the standard simply supported midspan point-load deflection case for the point load.
Compute the midspan deflection by superposing the two standard-case midspan deflections.
Report midspan deflection as negative for downward deflection.
Ignore shear deformation and include only bending deflection.
Round the final midspan deflection to three decimal places in mm.
