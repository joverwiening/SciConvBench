Use N–mm–MPa units and assume linear elasticity.
A hollow circular brass shaft has outer diameter do = 40 mm and inner diameter di = 30 mm.
The shaft length is L = 1000 mm.
The applied constant torque is T = 300000 N·mm.
Use G = 39179.1 MPa.
Use the polar moment of inertia J = π(do^4 − di^4)/32.
For part (i), compute the maximum shear stress at the outer surface using τmax = T c / J with c = do/2.
For part (ii), compute the angle of twist using θ = T L / (J G).
Although the member is hollow and thin-walled assumptions were discussed, report (i) and (ii) using the solid-mechanics torsion relations with the given J.
Use the mean radius rm = (do + di)/4 = 17.5 mm and mean enclosed area Am = π rm^2 as a reasonable thin-walled modeling assumption if any mean-area quantities are referenced.
