Q: When you say “take the end slopes to be zero in addition to the support deflections,” do you want me to enforce boundary conditions θ(0)=0 and θ(L)=0 (i.e., fixed ends) rather than the usual simply supported conditions?
A: No—do **not** enforce zero end slopes. Keep the beam **simply supported**: \(v(0)=0\) and \(v(L)=0\) (with zero end moments, \(M(0)=M(L)=0\), if you use moment boundary conditions).

The “end slopes zero” statement is **not** part of the complete requirement and should be ignored; instead, compute the actual slope at the left support \( \theta(0) \) from the simply supported solution.
