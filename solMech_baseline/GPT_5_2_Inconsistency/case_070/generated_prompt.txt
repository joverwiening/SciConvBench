Use N–mm–MPa units.
Use average stress approximations with uniform stresses over the cross-section.
Use a rectangular prismatic member with width b = 50 mm and height h = 80 mm.
At the cross section, use internal resultant normal force P = 40000 N in tension.
At the cross section, use internal shear force V = 12000 N acting parallel to the width.
Compute cross-sectional area as A = b h.
Compute average normal stress as σ = P/A.
Compute average shear stress magnitude as τ = V/A.
Assume plane stress at a point with σx = σ, σy = 0, τxy = +τ.
Compute the principal stresses from the plane-stress state.
Compute the maximum in-plane shear stress from the plane-stress state.
Report the principal stresses and the maximum in-plane shear stress to one decimal place (0.1 MPa).
