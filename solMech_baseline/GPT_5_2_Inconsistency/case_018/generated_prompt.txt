Use the N–mm–MPa unit system.
Model a solid circular shaft with diameter d = 40 mm and total length L = 1500 mm, fixed at x = 0.
Apply torque T1 = +300000 N·mm at x = L in the positive sense.
Apply torque T2 = −150000 N·mm at x = 750 mm in the negative sense (opposite the positive sense).
Use the sign convention where positive torque is the sense of T1 and negative torque is the opposite sense, keeping the viewing direction consistent along the shaft.
Assume the shaft is prismatic with constant diameter and constant shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa along the entire length.
For (a), construct the piecewise internal torque in each segment 0→750 mm and 750→1500 mm based on the applied torques, and report internal torques in N·mm with signs.
For (b), compute the maximum shear stress in each segment using the corresponding segment internal torque, and report stresses in MPa.
For (c), compute the angle of twist at x = L relative to the fixed end by summing the twist of each segment using the segment internal torques, and report the final angle in radians with a sign consistent with the torque sign convention.
