Use the N–mm–MPa unit system.
Analyze a stepped solid circular shaft of 6061-T6 aluminum with two segments in series.
Segment AB has length L1 = 300 mm and diameter d1 = 20 mm.
Segment BC has length L2 = 500 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm.
End A is fixed.
A torque T = 300000 N·mm is applied at end C.
Assume the same internal torque acts through both segments: TAB = TBC = 300000 N·mm.
Use shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa.
Determine the maximum shear stress in each segment in MPa and identify which segment governs.
Determine the total angle of twist at C relative to A by summing the twists of segments AB and BC.
Report the total angle of twist primarily in radians, optionally also in degrees in parentheses.
