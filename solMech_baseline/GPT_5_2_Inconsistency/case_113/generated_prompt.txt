Use N–mm–MPa units and assume linear elasticity.
Analyze a hollow circular shaft of structural steel with outer diameter do = 50 mm, inner diameter di = 44 mm, wall thickness t = 3 mm, and length L = 800 mm.
Apply a constant torque T = 600000 N·mm.
Model the member as a thin-walled closed tube using the mean-area shear-flow relation, using the mean diameter dm = (do + di)/2 and mean enclosed area Am = π(dm^2)/4 = πrm^2, not based on do.
Compute (i) the maximum shear stress at the outer surface using the solid-shaft torsion formula τmax = (T ro)/J with ro = do/2 and J = π(do^4 − di^4)/32.
Compute (ii) the angle of twist over the length using θ = TL/(GJ) with G = 77519.4 MPa.
Report final numeric answers to 3 significant figures.
