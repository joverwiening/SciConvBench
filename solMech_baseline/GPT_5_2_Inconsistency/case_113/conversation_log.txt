Q: What value should I use for the mean radius (or equivalently the mean enclosed area Am) in the thin-walled closed-tube shear-flow relation: based on the mean diameter dm = (do + di)/2, or based on the outer diameter do?
A: Use the **mean diameter**:  
\( d_m = (d_o + d_i)/2 \) (equivalently \( r_m = (r_o + r_i)/2 \)), so the mean enclosed area is  
\( A_m = \pi (d_m^2)/4 = \pi r_m^2 \).  

Do **not** base \(A_m\) on the outer diameter \(d_o\).

Q: Do you want the maximum shear stress computed using the thin-walled shear-flow result (q = T/(2Am), τ = q/t) or using the solid-shaft torsion formula with J (τmax = T·ro/J)?
A: Use the **solid-shaft torsion formula with \(J\)** for the maximum shear stress at the **outer surface**:

\[
\tau_{\max} = \frac{T\, r_o}{J}, \quad J=\frac{\pi(d_o^4-d_i^4)}{32}.
\]

Use \(GJ\) with \(\theta = \dfrac{TL}{GJ}\) for the angle of twist.
