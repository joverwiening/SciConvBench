Use N–mm–MPa units.
Evaluate the normal stress at a point on a beam cross section located at centroidal coordinates y = 30 mm and z = 15 mm.
Use centroidal second moments of area Iy = 2,500,000 mm^4, Iz = 1,200,000 mm^4, and product of inertia Iyz = 800,000 mm^4.
Use internal bending moments My = 4,000,000 N·mm about the y-axis and Mz = 2,000,000 N·mm about the z-axis.
Assume +y and +z are the positive centroidal coordinate directions consistent with the given point coordinates.
Compute only the unsymmetric bending normal stress using σ = -((My·Iz − Mz·Iyz)/(Iy·Iz − Iyz^2))·z - ((Mz·Iy − My·Iyz)/(Iy·Iz − Iyz^2))·y.
Report σ in MPa and state its sign with tension taken as positive.
