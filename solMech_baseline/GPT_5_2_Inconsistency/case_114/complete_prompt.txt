At a point on a beam’s cross section, the centroidal coordinates of the point are (y, z) = (30 mm, 15 mm). The cross section has the following second moments of area about centroidal axes: Iy = 2500000 mm^4, Iz = 1200000 mm^4, and product of inertia Iyz = 800000 mm^4.

The internal bending moments at the section are My = 4000000 N·mm about the y-axis and Mz = 2000000 N·mm about the z-axis.

Using the unsymmetric bending stress formula
σ = -(My·Iz − Mz·Iyz)/(Iy·Iz − Iyz^2) · z + -(Mz·Iy − My·Iyz)/(Iy·Iz − Iyz^2) · y,
compute the normal stress σ at the point. State the sign (tension positive).
