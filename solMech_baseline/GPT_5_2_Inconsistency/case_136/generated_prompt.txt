Work in N–mm–MPa units.
Use a rectangular cross section with width b = 30 mm and height h = 100 mm.
Use the full cross-sectional area A = b h = 3000 mm^2 for all average stress calculations.
Use internal resultant normal force P = 50000 N in tension.
Take average normal stress as positive in tension and compute σ_avg = +P/A.
Use internal resultant transverse shear force V = 5000 N acting parallel to width b.
Treat average shear stress as a magnitude and compute τ_avg = V/A > 0.
Assume uniform stress distributions for both normal and shear stresses (use average values).
Because both resultants act over the same area, take the maximum in-plane shear to be based on the average transverse shear stress alone.
Compute the magnitude of the maximum in-plane shear stress using τ_max = sqrt((σ_avg/2)^2 + τ_avg^2).
