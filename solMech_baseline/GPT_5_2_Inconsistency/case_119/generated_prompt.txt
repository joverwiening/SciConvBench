Model a bar of length L = 500 mm under a constant axial tensile force P = 12000 N.
Use a linearly varying cross-sectional area A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L) with x measured from the left end, A0 = 200 mm^2, and k = 1.0.
Replace the varying area A(x) by an arithmetic-mean area and treat that replacement as exact for the axial-deformation calculation.
Make a reasonable best-practice assumption for the arithmetic-mean area definition for a linearly varying area.
Compute the elongation using the axial deformation integral δ = ∫[0→L] P/(A(x)E) dx, using the arithmetic-mean area in place of A(x).
