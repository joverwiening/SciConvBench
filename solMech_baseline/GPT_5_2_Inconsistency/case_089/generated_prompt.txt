Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model a prismatic axial bar with constant cross-sectional area A = 500 mm^2 and modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Use segment lengths AB = 700 mm, BC = 400 mm, and CD = 300 mm.
Apply point loads (positive to the right): at B, +15000 N; at C, −12000 N; at D, +10000 N.
Impose boundary condition u_A = 0 at the fixed left end A.
Do not assume a uniform internal axial force over the full length.
Compute internal axial force separately in each segment AB, BC, and CD, allowing jumps at B and C due to the applied point loads.
Use the sign convention N > 0 in tension and N < 0 in compression.
Compute normal stress in each segment as σ = N/A using the segment’s internal axial force.
Compute the displacement of point D relative to A as u_D − u_A by summing axial deformations of each segment, using positive displacement to the right.
