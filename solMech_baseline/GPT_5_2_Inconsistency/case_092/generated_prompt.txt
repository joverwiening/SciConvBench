Use N–mm–MPa units.
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory with small deflection and linear elastic behavior.
Analyze a cantilever beam of length L = 1800 mm fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
Use material modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Use constant second moment of area I = 1200000 mm^4.
Apply a downward point load P = 2000 N at the free end (x = L).
Use standard cantilever boundary conditions for a tip point load: y(0)=0 and θ(0)=0 at the fixed end, and M(L)=0 and V(L)=-P at the free end.
Use the usual sign convention where downward deflection is negative, and report the free-end deflection as a negative value with slope sign consistent with that convention.
Determine (i) the slope at the free end and (ii) the vertical deflection at the free end.
Report slope in radians, optionally including degrees in parentheses.
Provide only the final numerical values with reasonable rounding (e.g., about 3 significant figures or sensible decimal places).
