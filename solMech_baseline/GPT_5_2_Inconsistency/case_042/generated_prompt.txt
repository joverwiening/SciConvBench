Use N–mm–MPa units.
Assume the bar’s axial response is linear elastic.
Model the prismatic bar as an equivalent linear axial spring with stiffness k = AE/L and a lumped mass at the loaded end.
Use L = 800 mm, A = 300 mm^2, E = 200000 MPa.
Apply a tensile load P = 8000 N as a step load applied instantaneously from 0 to P and then held constant at P thereafter.
Use an undamped SDOF step-load energy method comparing the work done by the suddenly applied load to the strain energy stored in the bar.
Determine the maximum stress in the bar during the transient.
Also compute the static stress P/A and report the maximum stress both as a numerical value in MPa and as a factor times the static stress P/A.
