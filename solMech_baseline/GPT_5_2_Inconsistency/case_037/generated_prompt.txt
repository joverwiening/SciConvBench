Use N–mm–MPa units and Euler–Bernoulli beam theory.
Analyze a simply supported beam with span L = 4500 mm under a uniform distributed load w = 0.3 N/mm over the full span.
Use material properties E = 200000 MPa and second moment of area I = 2000000 mm^4.
Apply simply supported boundary conditions v(0)=0 and v(L)=0.
Apply zero end moment boundary conditions M(0)=0 and M(L)=0, equivalently v''(0)=0 and v''(L)=0.
Do not enforce zero end slopes; compute slopes from the simply supported solution.
Adopt the sign convention that downward deflection is positive and report the maximum deflection as a positive value.
Determine the maximum deflection and the location where it occurs, reporting the location as x from the left support in mm.
