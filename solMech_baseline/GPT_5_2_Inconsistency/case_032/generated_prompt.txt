Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory and neglect shear deformation.
Analyze a simply supported beam with span L = 3000 mm.

Apply a downward point load P = 1500 N at midspan (x = L/2).
Take downward deflection v as positive.
Use standard simply supported boundary conditions v(0) = 0 and v(L) = 0.
Use symmetry to enforce zero slope at midspan only: v'(L/2) = 0.
Do not impose zero slope at the supports.
Compute the maximum deflection at midspan v(L/2) and report it as a positive value.
Compute the slope at the supports and report it in radians (dimensionless).
