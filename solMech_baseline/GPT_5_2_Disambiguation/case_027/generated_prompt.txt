Model the dropped weight impact as sticking/no-rebound.
Neglect the rod’s mass and treat the rod as a linear axial spring.

Assume no pre-existing axial load other than the dropped weight impact.
Determine the maximum axial force during impact P_max (peak impact force).
Determine the corresponding maximum normal stress σ_max = P_max / A.
Compute P_max using the specified energy balance W(h + δ_max) = (P_max^2 L)/(2 A E).
Use the specified compatibility relation δ_max = (P_max L)/(A E).
