Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic torsion of a solid circular shaft.
Model a stepped shaft with two segments in series: AB with L1 = 400 mm and d1 = 30 mm, and BC with L2 = 700 mm and d2 = 30 mm.
Apply a single torque T = 600,000 N·mm at end C about the shaft axis.
Assume no transverse shear force is applied.
Impose boundary conditions: end A is fixed against rotation, end C is otherwise free except for the applied torque, and point B is only an internal interface with no external load/support.
Assume internal torque is constant through both segments: T_AB = T_BC = 600,000 N·mm.
Use shear modulus G = 77,519.4 MPa (N/mm²).
Neglect stress-concentration effects at the step and use nominal torsional shear stress in each segment.
Compute maximum torsional shear stress using τ_max = Tc/J with c = d/2 and J = π d^4/32 for each segment.
Compute total angle of twist at C relative to A using θ_CA = Σ (T L / (J G)) over the segments, reporting θ_CA as the primary twist result.
