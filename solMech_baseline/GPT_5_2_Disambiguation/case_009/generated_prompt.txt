Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic axial behavior.
Model two prismatic bars mounted in parallel between two rigid plates.
Assume the bars are perfectly bonded to the rigid plates so both bars undergo the same elongation (compatibility).
Use bar length L = 400 mm for both bars.
Use Bar 1 as AISI 1020 steel with cross-sectional area A1 = 300 mm^2 and elastic modulus E1 = 200,000 MPa.
Use Bar 2 as cast iron with cross-sectional area A2 = 250 mm^2 and elastic modulus E2 = 110,000 MPa.
Apply a total tensile load P = 20,000 N to the top rigid plate, shared by both bars through the rigid plates.
Enforce equilibrium as P = P1 + P2, where P1 and P2 are the axial forces carried by Bars 1 and 2.
Enforce compatibility as δ1 = δ2, with δi = Pi L / (Ai Ei) for each bar i.
Use constitutive relations σi = Pi / Ai and εi = δi / L = σi / Ei.
Determine the relevant response quantities for composite bars in parallel, including load share (P1, P2), stresses (σ1, σ2), common strain (ε), and common elongation (δ), and present the governing relations clearly.
