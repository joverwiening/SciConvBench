Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.
Model the member as an Euler–Bernoulli cantilever beam with constant flexural rigidity and neglect shear deformation.
Use Castigliano’s theorem with bending strain energy only: \(U=\int_{0}^{L}\frac{M(x)^2}{2EI}\,dx\).
Beam length is \(L=1500\) mm.
Material modulus is \(E=200000\) MPa \(=200000\) N/mm².
Second moment of area is \(I=800000\) mm⁴.
Flexural rigidity is \(EI=1.6\times 10^{11}\) N·mm².
Apply only a downward point load at the free end: \(P=500\) N at \(x=L\).
Determine the free-end vertical deflection due to \(P\) using Castigliano’s theorem.
Report the free-end vertical deflection in mm with the sign convention that downward deflection is negative.
