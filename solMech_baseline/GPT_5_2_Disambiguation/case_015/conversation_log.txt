Q: What response quantities do you want reported for the cantilever under the end point load (e.g., deflection curve \(w(x)\), slope \(\theta(x)\), bending moment \(M(x)\), shear \(V(x)\), maximum deflection \(w(L)\), maximum stress \(\sigma_{\max}\))?

Q: What sign convention should I use for positive deflection and positive rotation (e.g., downward deflection positive, clockwise rotation positive)?
A: The complete requirement doesn’t specify a sign convention. Make a reasonable assumption based on best practices.

Use the common beam convention:
- Positive deflection \(w\): **downward**
- Positive rotation/slope \(\theta\): **clockwise** (i.e., \(dw/dx>0\) when \(w\) is positive downward)
