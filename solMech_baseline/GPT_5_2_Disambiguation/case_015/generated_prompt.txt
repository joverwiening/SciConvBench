Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic Euler–Bernoulli beam theory.
Model a cantilever beam of length L = 1200 mm with a fixed support at x = 0 and a free end at x = L.
Use material modulus E = 200,000 MPa (N/mm^2).
Use second moment of area I = 2,000,000 mm^4.
Use flexural rigidity EI = 4.0 × 10^11 N·mm^2.
Apply a downward point load P = 2000 N at the free end x = L.
Apply no end moment at x = L, i.e., M(L) = 0.
Report only the free-end slope θ(L) in radians with degrees optional.
Report only the free-end vertical deflection w(L) in mm.
Use the sign convention: positive deflection w is downward.
Use the sign convention: positive rotation/slope θ is clockwise.
