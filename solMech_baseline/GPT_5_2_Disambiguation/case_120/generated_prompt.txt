Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic axial bar behavior with constant Young’s modulus.
Use a prismatic bar of length L = 500 mm subjected to a constant tensile axial force P = 12,000 N.
Use Young’s modulus E = 200,000 MPa in consistent N–mm–MPa units.
Use a linearly varying cross-sectional area A(x) = A0(1 + k x/L) with A0 = 200 mm^2 and k = 1.0, so A(0) = 200 mm^2 and A(L) = 400 mm^2.
Compute only the total axial elongation between x = 0 and x = L.
Use the governing relation for elongation under axial force with variable area: δ = ∫0^L [P/(A(x)E)] dx.
If a displacement reference is needed for interpretation, take u(0) = 0 as a reasonable convention, but do not compute u(x), σ(x), or ε(x).
