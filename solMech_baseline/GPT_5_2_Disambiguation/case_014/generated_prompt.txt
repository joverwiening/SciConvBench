Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic Euler–Bernoulli beam theory.
Model a cantilever beam fixed at x=0 and free at x=L.
Use beam length L = 1200 mm.
Use material modulus E = 200,000 MPa (N/mm^2).
Use second moment of area I = 2,500,000 mm^4.
Use flexural rigidity EI = 5.0 × 10^11 N·mm^2.
Apply a single downward point load P = 800 N at the free end x=L.
Assume there is no applied end moment at x=L.
Determine only the free-end (tip) response quantities: slope θ(L) and vertical deflection w(L).
