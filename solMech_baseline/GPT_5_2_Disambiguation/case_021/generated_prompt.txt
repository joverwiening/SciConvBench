Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic torsion of a stepped solid circular shaft made of 6061-T6 aluminum with shear modulus G = 25,939.8 MPa.
Model the shaft as two prismatic segments in series with end A fixed and torque applied at end C so the internal torque is constant through both segments.
Use segment AB with length L1 = 300 mm and diameter d1 = 20 mm.
Use segment BC with length L2 = 500 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm.
Apply a purely torsional load T = 300,000 N·mm at end C with no transverse/lateral shear force.
Neglect stress concentrations at the diameter step and use Saint-Venant torsion with the torsional shear stress distribution for a solid circular shaft in each segment based on its own diameter.
Determine the torsional shear stress in each segment and the total angle of twist from A to C.
State any additional assumptions needed and present the governing relations clearly.
