Use N–mm–MPa units. Assume small deflection and Euler–Bernoulli beam theory.

A simply supported beam of span L = 2000 mm made of 6061-T6 aluminum (E = 69000 MPa) carries a concentrated load P = 3000 N at midspan. The beam has a rectangular cross section of width b = 30 mm and unknown height h. The maximum deflection at midspan must not exceed δ_allow = 3 mm.

Determine the minimum required height h. You may use the formula for a simply supported beam with a central point load:
δ_max = P L^3/(48 E I), with I = b h^3/12.
