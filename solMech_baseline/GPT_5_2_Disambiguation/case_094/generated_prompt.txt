Assume a small-deformation, linear-elastic Euler–Bernoulli beam with constant E and I, neglecting shear deformation.
Use a simply supported (pin–roller) beam of span L = 3500 mm.
Apply a uniformly distributed load w = 0.3 N/mm downward over the full span 0 ≤ x ≤ L.
Apply a point load P = 1200 N downward at midspan x = L/2 = 1750 mm.
Assume there is no additional non-uniform distributed load beyond the uniform load (q(x) = w constant).
Use material modulus E = 200000 MPa and second moment of area I = 3,000,000 mm^4 (constant).
Use the sign convention that downward deflection is negative.
Determine only the midspan deflection v(L/2) numerically in consistent N–mm units.
Compute v(L/2) by superposition of standard simply supported solutions for a full-span UDL and a midspan point load.
State any additional assumptions needed and present the governing relations clearly.
