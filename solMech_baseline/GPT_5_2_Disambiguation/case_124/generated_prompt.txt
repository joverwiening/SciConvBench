Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Analyze a rectangular beam section subjected to a bending moment.
Use a rectangular cross-section with width b = 40 mm and height h = 100 mm.
Use an internal bending moment at the section M = 4,000,000 N·mm with sagging taken as positive.
Adopt the Euler–Bernoulli (plane sections remain plane) bending assumption with the neutral axis through the centroid.
Compute bending normal stress as a function of fiber location y measured from the neutral axis with y positive upward.
Report stress with tension positive and compression negative.
For a positive (sagging) moment, take the top fiber to be in compression (negative stress) and the bottom fiber to be in tension (positive stress).
Evaluate and report bending stress at y = 0 mm, y = +50 mm (top extreme fiber), and y = −50 mm (bottom extreme fiber).
State any additional assumptions needed and present the governing relations clearly, including the second moment of area for the rectangle and the flexure formula relating σ(y), M, I, and y.
