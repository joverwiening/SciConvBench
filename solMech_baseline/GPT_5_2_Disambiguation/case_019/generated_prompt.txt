Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic torsion of a circular shaft.
Use a solid, uniform shaft of diameter d = 30 mm over the full length.
Use x measured from the fixed end at x = 0 to the free end at x = L = 1200 mm.
Use a segment break at a = 400 mm, defining segments 0→a and a→L.
Apply only two concentrated torques with the given sign convention.
Apply T2 = −200,000 N·mm at x = a = 400 mm.
Apply T1 = +200,000 N·mm at x = L = 1200 mm.
Include no distributed torsional loading and no transverse shear forces.
Use the same polar moment of inertia in both segments, J = (π d^4)/32 ≈ 7.952×10^4 mm^4.
Use shear modulus G = 76,923.1 MPa (N/mm²) for AISI 1020 steel.
Determine (a) the internal torque distribution T(x) for 0→a and for a→L.
Determine (b) the maximum shear stress in each segment, τ_max.
Determine (c) the angle of twist at the free end θ(L) relative to the fixed end by summing the twist contributions of each segment.
Present any additional assumptions needed and state the governing relations clearly.
