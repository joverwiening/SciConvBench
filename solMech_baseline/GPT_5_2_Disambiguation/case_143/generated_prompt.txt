Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Analyze a solid circular shaft under combined axial tension and torsion.
Use shaft diameter d = 25 mm and outer radius c = 12.5 mm.
Use axial load P = 15,000 N in tension with σx > 0.
Use applied torque magnitude T = 400,000 N·mm.
Use torsional shear stress at the outer surface as τxy = +T c / J per the chosen positive torsion convention.
For parts (a)–(c), do not use material properties.
Treat the stress state as plane stress with σx = P/A, σy = 0, τxy = T c / J.
Report for (a) the average axial normal stress σ = P/A.
Report for (b) the torsional shear stress at the outer surface τ = T c / J.
Report for (c) the principal stresses σ1 and σ2 from the plane-stress state.
Report for (c) the maximum in-plane shear stress τmax from the plane-stress state.
Report only magnitudes for σ1, σ2, and τmax, not the principal stress orientation angle.
Do not report von Mises stress or principal strains for parts (a)–(c).
If strains are needed beyond parts (a)–(c), assume reasonable typical steel properties for E and ν.
