Model a simply supported Euler–Bernoulli beam with bending-only deformation and neglect shear deformation.

Beam length is \(L=3000\) mm.
Material modulus is \(E=200000\) MPa \(=\) N/mm².
Second moment of area is \(I=1{,}500{,}000\) mm\(^4\).
Flexural rigidity is \(EI=3.0\times 10^{11}\) N·mm\(^2\).
Apply a single concentrated load \(P=2000\) N downward at midspan \(x=L/2\).
Include no other loads or applied end moments.
