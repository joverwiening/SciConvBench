Apply concentrated torques at x = a and x = L with the given sign convention: T2 = -250,000 N·mm at x = 800 mm and T1 = +200,000 N·mm at x = 1200 mm.
Impose a built-in boundary condition at x = 0: θ(0) = 0.
Use shear modulus G = 77,519.4 MPa in the consistent N–mm–MPa unit system.
Determine (a) the internal torque distribution by segment, giving constant internal torque values for 0→a and a→L.
Determine (b) the maximum shear stress in each segment, τmax,1 and τmax,2, using the torsion relation for a solid circular shaft.
Determine (c) the angle of twist at the free end x = L relative to x = 0 by summing the twist contributions of each segment (no need to produce θ(x) as a full function).
