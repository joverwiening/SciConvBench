Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic axial bar behavior.
Model a vertical, prismatic bar of length L = 3000 mm with constant cross-sectional area A = 300 mm^2.
Use material properties E = 69000 MPa and density ρ = 8900 kg/m^3.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s^2.
Use an axial coordinate x measured downward from the top support, with x = 0 at the top and x = L at the bottom.
Apply boundary conditions: the top end is fixed with u(0) = 0, and the bottom end is free with no applied end load.
Include only self-weight as the loading; ignore all other external loads.
Determine the relevant response quantity: the total elongation of the bar due to self-weight.
Present any additional assumptions needed and clearly state the governing relations, optionally including the internal axial force relation P(x) = ρ g A (L − x).
