Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic axial behavior for two prismatic bars connected in parallel between two rigid plates.
Use Bar 1 as structural steel with A1 = 150 mm^2 and E1 = 200,000 MPa.
Use Bar 2 as cast iron with A2 = 200 mm^2 and E2 = 110,000 MPa.
Use common bar length L = 800 mm.
Assume both bars are perfectly attached to the rigid plates at both ends so the end plates enforce equal elongation in both bars (compatibility).
Apply a total tensile load P = 30,000 N to the top rigid plate.
Assume the load is purely axial and centered so there is no bending or eccentricity.
Assume mechanical loading only with no initial strains and no temperature change.
Determine the force carried by each bar, the stress in each bar, and the common elongation of the bars.
State any additional assumptions needed and present the governing relations clearly for composite bars in parallel.
