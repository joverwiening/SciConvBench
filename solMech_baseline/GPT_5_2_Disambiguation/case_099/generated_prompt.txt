Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Model the member as a cantilever beam fixed at x=0 and free at x=L.
Use static Euler–Bernoulli beam theory with small slopes/small deflections.
Neglect shear deformation and rotary inertia.
Use N–mm–MPa units with E in MPa (N/mm^2).
Beam length is L = 2000 mm.
Material modulus is E = 200000 MPa.
Second moment of area is I = 1,500,000 mm^4, so EI = 3.0×10^11 N·mm^2.
Apply a uniformly distributed transverse load w = 1.0 N/mm acting downward over the entire span 0 ≤ x ≤ L, with downward taken as negative if +y is upward.
Determine and report only the maximum deflection at the free end v(L) and the slope at the free end θ(L).
State any additional assumptions needed and present the governing relations clearly.
