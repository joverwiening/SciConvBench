Assume a simply supported Euler–Bernoulli beam with small deflection, linear-elastic behavior, and constant flexural rigidity EI.
Use bending strain energy only and neglect shear deformation.
Beam length is L = 3000 mm.
Material modulus is E = 200000 MPa.
Second moment of area is I = 3,000,000 mm^4, so EI = 6.0 × 10^11 N·mm^2.
Apply a single concentrated load P = 2000 N downward at midspan (x = L/2).
No other loads or applied end moments are present.
Determine the midspan deflection using Castigliano’s theorem with U = ∫_0^L M(x)^2/(2EI) dx and clearly state the governing relations and any additional assumptions if needed.
