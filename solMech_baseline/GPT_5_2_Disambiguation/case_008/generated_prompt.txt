Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic axial behavior for a composite bar system consisting of two prismatic members in parallel between two rigid plates.
Use two members: Bar 1 is AISI 1020 steel with A1 = 300 mm² and E1 = 200,000 MPa, and Bar 2 is 6061-T6 aluminum with A2 = 200 mm² and E2 = 69,000 MPa.
Both bars have the same length L = 500 mm.
Assume both bars are perfectly bonded to both rigid plates so they undergo the same elongation δ (compatibility).
Apply a concentric axial tensile load P = 20,000 N to the plates so the load is shared by the two bars with no bending or eccentricity.
Assume no initial strains and no thermal loading.
Use N–mm–MPa units.
Determine and report the force carried by each bar (P1 for steel, P2 for aluminum), the axial stress in each bar (σ1, σ2), and the common elongation δ.
Optionally (if included by best practice), also report the common strain ε = δ/L and the equivalent axial stiffness keq = P/δ.
State any additional assumptions needed and present the governing relations clearly, including equilibrium (P = P1 + P2), compatibility (δ1 = δ2 = δ), and constitutive/axial deformation relations (σi = Pi/Ai and δ = Pi L/(Ai Ei)).
