Determine the Euler–Bernoulli (small-deformation, linear-elastic) response of a simply supported beam with constant properties under a single midspan point load.
Use span length L = 2500 mm.
Use Young’s modulus E = 200,000 MPa (N/mm^2).
Use second moment of area I = 3,500,000 mm^4.
Use flexural rigidity EI = 7.0×10^11 N·mm^2.
Apply a single downward point load P = 2500 N at midspan x = L/2 = 1250 mm.
Include no other shear loading beyond the shear force implied by the point load.
Assume constant cross-section and constant E and I over 0 ≤ x ≤ L.
Neglect shear deformation (do not use Timoshenko theory, G, or shear area).
Use sign convention: downward deflection v is negative and counterclockwise rotation θ is positive.
Report the midspan deflection v(L/2) numerically in mm.
Report the support slopes θ(0) and θ(L) numerically in radians, noting they are equal magnitude and opposite sign by symmetry.
State any additional assumptions needed and present the governing relations clearly.
