Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Analyze a prismatic circular rod with diameter d = 20 mm (area A = 100π ≈ 314.16 mm²) and length L = 1200 mm.
Use material properties E = 200,000 MPa and coefficient of thermal expansion α = 1.0×10^-5 1/°C.
Apply a uniform temperature increase along the entire rod of ΔT = 35 °C.
Assume the rod is fully restrained between two rigid, unyielding supports with both ends fixed axially so the total change in length is zero (ΔL = 0, equivalently average total axial strain ε_total = 0).
Assume no externally applied axial mechanical load; the only loading is thermal, and any axial force is the induced reaction force.
Determine the relevant response quantities for the fully restrained thermal strain case, including thermal strain, mechanical strain, total strain compatibility, induced axial stress, and induced axial reaction force.
State any additional assumptions needed and present the governing relations clearly.
