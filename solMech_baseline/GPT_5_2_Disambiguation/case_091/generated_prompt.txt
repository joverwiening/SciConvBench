Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic axial bar behavior.
Use a prismatic bar with constant cross-sectional area A = 500 mm².
Use structural steel with Young’s modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Use bar points A–B–C–D with segment lengths AB = 300 mm, BC = 600 mm, CD = 300 mm (total 1200 mm).
Apply axial point loads with positive direction to the right: at B, +18000 N; at C, -12000 N; at D, +10000 N.
Impose boundary conditions: A is fixed with u_A = 0; D is free (no displacement constraint) with only the applied +10000 N load.
Assume no other point loads within segments.
Assume no initial strains and no thermal effects.
Use internal axial force sign convention: N > 0 in tension, N < 0 in compression.
Determine and report only the internal axial force in each segment (N_AB, N_BC, N_CD), the normal stress in each segment (σ_AB, σ_BC, σ_CD), and the displacement of point D relative to A (u_D with u_A = 0).
State any additional assumptions needed and present the governing relations clearly.
