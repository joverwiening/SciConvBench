Assume an Euler–Bernoulli cantilever beam with small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.
Use beam length L = 800 mm.
Use material modulus E = 69000 MPa.
Use second moment of area I = 800000 mm^4.
Use flexural rigidity EI = 5.52×10^10 N·mm^2.
Apply a downward point load P = 500 N at the free end x = L.
Apply no end moment at the free end, i.e., M(L) = 0 N·mm.
Report only the free-end slope θ(L) in radians, optionally also in degrees.
Report only the free-end vertical deflection y(L) in mm.
Report y(L) as a positive magnitude corresponding to downward deflection.
State any additional assumptions needed and present the governing relations clearly.
