Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic Euler–Bernoulli beam theory.
Use a simply supported (pin–roller) beam with span length L = 3000 mm.
Apply a uniformly distributed load w = 0.3 N/mm downward over the entire span 0 ≤ x ≤ L.
Apply a point load P = 1200 N downward at midspan x = L/2 = 1500 mm.
Assume no additional distributed load beyond the constant UDL.
Use material/section properties E = 200000 N/mm² and I = 3,000,000 mm⁴, so EI = 6.0×10^11 N·mm².
Use the sign convention that downward loads and downward deflections are positive.
Determine the midspan deflection v(L/2) in mm by superposition of the UDL contribution and the midspan point-load contribution.
State any additional assumptions needed and present the governing relations clearly.
