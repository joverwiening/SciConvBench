Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Analyze a short prismatic bar with a rectangular cross section at the section of interest.
Use cross-sectional dimensions b = 40 mm and h = 120 mm, with area A = b h = 4800 mm^2.
At the section of interest, apply an axial (normal) force P = 30,000 N in tension, taken as positive.
At the section of interest, apply a transverse shear force V = 12,000 N acting parallel to the width b direction.
Assume the bending moment at the section is M = 0 and do not include any bending stress contribution.
Assume a uniform shear stress distribution over the cross-sectional area for purposes of average shear stress.
Compute the average normal stress as σ_avg = P/A = P/(b h).
Compute the average shear stress as τ_avg = V/A = V/(b h).
State any additional assumptions needed to justify using average (uniform) stress measures at the section.
Present the governing relations clearly and report the average normal stress and average shear stress at the section.
