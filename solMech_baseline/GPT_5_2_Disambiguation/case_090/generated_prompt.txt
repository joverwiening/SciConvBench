Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic axial bar behavior.
Analyze a stepped circular bar in uniaxial tension with the left end fixed and an applied tensile load P = 25,000 N at the right end.
Use two prismatic segments carrying the same axial force P.
Use Segment 1 geometry: L1 = 300 mm and d1 = 12 mm.
Use Segment 2 geometry: L2 = 600 mm and d2 = 30 mm.
Use Young’s modulus E = 200,000 MPa.
Neglect any deformation and stress concentration effects in the shoulder/transition region between diameters.
Determine the axial normal stress in each segment using σi = P/Ai with Ai = (π/4)di^2.
Determine the axial elongation of each segment using δi = PLi/(AiE).
Determine the total elongation as δtotal = δ1 + δ2.
State any additional assumptions needed consistent with the above (e.g., uniform stress/strain in each segment, load applied concentrically, no bending).
