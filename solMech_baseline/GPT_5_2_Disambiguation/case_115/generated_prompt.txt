Use applied torque T = 600,000 N·mm.
Use shaft outer diameter do = 60 mm.
Use shaft inner diameter di = 44 mm.
Use shaft length L = 1200 mm.
Use shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.
Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Use polar moment of inertia J = π(do^4 − di^4)/32.
Determine the maximum shear stress at the outer surface τmax at r = do/2.
Determine the total angle of twist over the full length θ.
Report θ primarily in radians, optionally also in degrees.
