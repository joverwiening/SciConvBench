Compute the work of adhesion using the Young‑Dupre equation:  
Wₐd = γ_LV · (1 + cos θ).  

Take the water liquid‑vapor surface tension γ_LV = 72.8 mN·m⁻¹.  

Calculate cos θ for θ = 72°, which is approximately 0.309.  

Add 1 to this value: 1 + 0.309 ≈ 1.309.  

Multiply by γ_LV: 72.8 mN·m⁻¹ × 1.309 ≈ 95.3 mN·m⁻¹.  

Report the work of adhesion as about 95 mN·m⁻¹ (or 95 mJ·m⁻²).
