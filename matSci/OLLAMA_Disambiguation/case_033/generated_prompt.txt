Determine the fraction of liquid present just above the eutectic temperature for a binary alloy using the lever rule.

Use the overall alloy composition C0 = 25.37 % B, the composition of the α solid phase at the eutectic Cαe = 7.90 % B, and the composition of the liquid at the eutectic Ce = 47.78 % B.

Calculate the liquid fraction fL with the formula:
fL = (C0 – Cαe) / (Ce – Cαe).

Compute the numerator: 25.37 % – 7.90 % = 17.47 %.
Compute the denominator: 47.78 % – 7.90 % = 39.88 %.
Divide: fL = 17.47 % / 39.88 % ≈ 0.438.

State the result as a decimal or percentage: the liquid fraction just above the eutectic is approximately 0.44 (44 %).
