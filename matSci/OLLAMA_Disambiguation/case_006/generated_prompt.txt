Use the fracture mechanics relation σ_c = K_Ic / (Y · √(π a)).  
Convert the crack length to meters: a = 2.968 mm = 0.002968 m.  
Calculate √(π a) = √(π · 0.002968 m) ≈ 0.0966 m^0.5.

Divide the fracture toughness by this product: σ_c = 105.8 MPa·√m / 0.0947 m^0.5 ≈ 1.12 × 10³ MPa.
