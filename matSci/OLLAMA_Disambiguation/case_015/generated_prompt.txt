Assume the crystal has a face‑centered cubic unit cell (Z = 4).  
Convert the lattice parameter to centimeters: a = 0.6067 nm = 6.067 × 10⁻⁸ cm.  
Calculate the unit‑cell volume: a³ = (6.067 × 10⁻⁸ cm)³ = 2.235 × 10⁻²² cm³.  
Use the density formula ρ = (Z · M) / (N_A · a³).  
Substitute the values: ρ = (4 × 139.63 g mol⁻¹) / (6.022 × 10²³ mol⁻¹ × 2.235 × 10⁻²² cm³) ≈ 4.15 g cm⁻³.
