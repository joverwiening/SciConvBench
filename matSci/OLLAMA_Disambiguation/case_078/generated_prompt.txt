Determine the fraction of eutectic microconstituent (f_eutectic) present just below the eutectic temperature for a hypoeutectic binary alloy with overall composition C0 = 30 wt% B, eutectic composition Ce = 40 wt% B, and α‑phase composition at the eutectic temperature Cα = 12 wt% B.  
Use the lever rule:  
f_eutectic = (C0 – Cα) / (Ce – Cα).  
Calculate the numerator: 30 wt% B – 12 wt% B = 18 wt% B.  
Calculate the denominator: 40 wt% B – 12 wt% B = 28 wt% B.  
Compute the fraction: f_eutectic = 18 / 28 = 0.6429 (approximately).  
State the result: The eutectic microconstituent constitutes about 64 % of the alloy just below the eutectic temperature.
