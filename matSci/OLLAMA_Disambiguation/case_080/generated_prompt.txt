Use the Nernst–Einstein relation σ = n z² e² D / (k_B T).  
Take n = 4.0×10²⁷ m⁻³, z = 1, e = 1.602×10⁻¹⁹ C, D = 1.2×10⁻¹¹ m²/s, k_B = 1.381×10⁻²³ J/K, and T = 800 K.  
Calculate the numerator: n e² D = (4.0×10²⁷)(1.602×10⁻¹⁹)²(1.2×10⁻¹¹) ≈ 1.23×10⁻²¹.  
Calculate the denominator: k_B T = (1.381×10⁻²³)(800) ≈ 1.10×10⁻²⁰.  
Divide numerator by denominator: σ ≈ (1.23×10⁻²¹) / (1.10×10⁻²⁰) ≈ 0.11 S/m.  
Thus the estimated ionic conductivity of the solid electrolyte at 800 K is approximately 0.11 S · m⁻¹.
