Calculate the open‑circuit thermoelectric voltage by multiplying the Seebeck coefficient by the temperature difference.  
Use a Seebeck coefficient of +180 µV/K and a temperature difference of 60 K.  
The resulting voltage is 180 µV/K × 60 K = 10 800 µV, which equals 10.8 mV.  
State the open‑circuit voltage as +10.8 mV.
