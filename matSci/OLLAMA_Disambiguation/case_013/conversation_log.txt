Q: The weight‑average molecular weight \(M_w\) is calculated using  

\[
M_w=\frac{\sum_i N_i M_i^{2}}{\sum_i N_i M_i}
\]

where \(M_i\) is the molecular weight of each species and \(N_i\) is the number of molecules of that species.

1. Compute the products \(N_i M_i\):
- \(93 \times 21\,640 = 2\,012\,520\)
- \(40 \times 74\,810 = 2\,992\,400\)
- \(170 \times 135\,292 = 22\,999\,640\)
- \(48 \times 141\,802 = 6\,806\,496\)

\(\displaystyle \sum N_i M_i = 34\,811\,056\)

2. Compute the products \(N_i M_i^{2}\):
- \(93 \times 21\,640^{2} = 43\,550\,932\,800\)
- \(40 \times 74\,810^{2} = 223\,861\,444\,000\)
- \(170 \times 135\,292^{2} = 3\,111\,667\,294\,880\)
- \(48 \times 141\,802^{2} = 965\,174\,745\,792\)

\(\displaystyle \sum N_i M_i^{2} = 4\,344\,254\,417\,472\)

3. Calculate \(M_w\):

\[
M_w = \frac{4\,344\,254\,417\,472}{34\,811\,056} \approx 1.25 \times 10^{5}\ \text{g/mol}
\]

**Result:** The weight‑average molecular weight \(M_w\) is approximately **124 800 g/mol**.
A: The weight‑average molecular weight is  

\[
M_w \approx 1.25\times10^{5}\ \text{g/mol} \;\;(\text{about }124{,}800\ \text{g/mol}).
\]
