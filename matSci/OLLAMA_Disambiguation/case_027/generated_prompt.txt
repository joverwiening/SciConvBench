Determine the diffusion activation energy using the Arrhenius relation D = D0 exp(–Ea/(R T)).  
Take natural logarithms of the two diffusion coefficients: ln D1 and ln D2.  
Compute the temperature reciprocals: 1/T1 and 1/T2.  
Calculate the slope: (ln D2 – ln D1) / (1/T2 – 1/T1).  
Multiply the negative of this slope by the gas constant R (8.314 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹) to obtain Ea in joules per mole.  
Convert Ea to kilojoules per mole by dividing by 1000.  
The resulting diffusion activation energy is approximately 135 kJ mol⁻¹.
