Use the FCC density formula ρ = (Z·M) / (N_A·a³) where Z = 4 atoms per unit cell.  
Rearrange to solve for the lattice parameter: a = [(Z·M) / (ρ·N_A)]^(1/3).  
Substitute the given values: Z = 4, M = 197.35 g mol⁻¹, ρ = 7.754 g cm⁻³, N_A = 6.022 × 10²³ mol⁻¹.  
Calculate a³ = (4 × 197.35) / (7.754 × 6.022 × 10²³) cm³ = 1.69 × 10⁻²² cm³.  
Take the cube root: a = (1.69 × 10⁻²²)¹ᐟ³ cm = 5.55 × 10⁻⁸ cm.  
Convert centimeters to nanometers (1 cm = 10⁷ nm): a = 5.55 × 10⁻⁸ cm × 10⁷ nm cm⁻¹ = 0.555 nm.  
The lattice parameter a is 0.555 nm.
