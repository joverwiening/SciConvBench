Calculate the corrosion penetration rate as follows.

1. Compute the volume of steel lost: volume loss (cm³) = mass loss (g) ÷ steel density (g/cm³).  
   Use mass loss = 0.084 g and density = 7.87 g/cm³.

2. Determine the average penetration depth on the coupon surface: penetration depth (cm) = volume loss (cm³) ÷ exposed surface area (cm²).  
   Use surface area = 12.0 cm².

3. Convert the penetration depth to millimetres: depth (mm) = penetration depth (cm) × 10.

4. Convert the exposure time to years: exposure time (y) = exposure duration (hours) ÷ (24 h / day × 365 days / year).  
   Use exposure duration = 240 h.

5. Compute the corrosion penetration rate: rate (mm/y) = depth (mm) ÷ exposure time (y).
