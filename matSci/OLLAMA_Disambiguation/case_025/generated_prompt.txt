Calculate the equilibrium vacancy concentration (vacancies per cubic meter) for the given crystalline solid as follows:

1. Convert the density to kilograms per cubic meter: ρ = 9.434 g cm⁻³ = 9.434 × 10³ kg m⁻³.
2. Convert the atomic weight to kilograms per mole: M = 191.53 g mol⁻¹ = 1.9153 × 10⁻¹ kg mol⁻¹.
3. Compute the number of atoms per cubic meter:  
   N_atoms = (ρ / M) × N_A = (9.434 × 10³ kg m⁻³ / 1.9153 × 10⁻¹ kg mol⁻¹) × 6.022 × 10²³ mol⁻¹.
4. Convert the vacancy formation energy to joules per mole: Q_v = 121.4 kJ mol⁻¹ = 1.214 × 10⁵ J mol⁻¹.
5. Compute the Boltzmann factor at T = 985 K: exp_factor = exp(‑Q_v / (R T)) where R = 8.314 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹.
6. Calculate the equilibrium vacancy concentration:  
   N_vacancies = N_atoms × exp_factor.
7. Report N_vacancies as the number of vacancies per cubic meter (vacancies m⁻³).
