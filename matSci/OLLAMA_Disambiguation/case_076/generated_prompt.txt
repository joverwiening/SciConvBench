Use the Larson–Miller relation P = T (C + log10 t_r).  
Convert the temperature to Kelvin: T = 700 °C + 273 = 973 K.  
Insert the known values: 23 500 = 973 (20 + log10 t_r).  
Solve for log10 t_r: log10 t_r = 23 500 / 973 – 20 ≈ 24.15 – 20 = 4.15.  
Calculate the rupture life: t_r = 10^4.15 ≈ 1.4 × 10^4 hours.  
Thus the estimated rupture life at 700 °C is about 14 000 hours.
