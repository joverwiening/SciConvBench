Calculate the theoretical specific capacity of FePO₄ using the formula: capacity (mAh g⁻¹) = (x × F × 1000) / M, where x is the number of Li moles exchanged per mole of FePO₄, F is Faraday’s constant (96 485 C mol⁻¹ ≈ 26.801 Ah mol⁻¹), and M is the molar mass of FePO₄ (≈150.8 g mol⁻¹).  
Substitute x = 1 mol, F = 26.801 Ah mol⁻¹, and M = 150.8 g mol⁻¹.  
Compute capacity = (1 × 26.801 Ah mol⁻¹ × 1000) / 150.8 g ≈ 177.7 mAh g⁻¹.  
State that the theoretical specific capacity of FePO₄ with one Li exchanged per formula unit is approximately 178 mAh g⁻¹.
