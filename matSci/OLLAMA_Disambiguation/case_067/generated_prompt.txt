Use the body‑centered cubic (BCC) crystal structure and the lattice parameter a = 0.286 nm.  
Identify the close‑packed direction for BCC, which is the <111> direction (the body diagonal).  
The length of one body diagonal is a √3, and two atoms (one at a corner and one at the body centre) lie on this line per full diagonal length.  
Compute the linear density as the number of atoms divided by the length of the diagonal:  
Linear density = 2 / (a √3).  
Substitute a = 0.286 nm (0.286 × 10⁻⁹ m):  
Linear density = 2 / (0.286 × 10⁻⁹ m × 1.732) ≈ 4.04 × 10⁹ atoms · m⁻¹, which is about 4.04 atoms per nanometre.
