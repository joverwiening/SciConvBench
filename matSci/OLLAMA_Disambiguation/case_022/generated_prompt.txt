Use the formula ρ = (Z·M) / (N_A·a³), where ρ is the density, Z is the number of atoms per FCC unit cell (4), M is the atomic weight, N_A is Avogadro’s number, and a is the lattice parameter in centimeters.  
Solve for a: a = [(Z·M) / (ρ·N_A)]^(1/3).  
Insert the values: Z = 4, M = 85.15 g mol⁻¹, ρ = 13.931 g cm⁻³, N_A = 6.022 × 10²³ mol⁻¹.  
Compute a³ = (4 × 85.15) / (13.931 × 6.022 × 10²³) ≈ 4.06 × 10⁻²³ cm³.  
Take the cube root: a ≈ 3.45 × 10⁻⁸ cm.  
Convert to nanometers (1 cm = 10⁷ nm): a ≈ 0.345 nm.  
Thus the lattice parameter of the FCC metal is approximately 0.345 nm.
