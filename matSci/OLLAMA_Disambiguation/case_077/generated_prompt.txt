Use the lever rule for a hypoeutectoid steel cooled just below the eutectoid temperature.  
Let the overall carbon content be C₀ = 0.60 wt % C.  
Use the carbon content of ferrite at the eutectoid temperature C_α = 0.022 wt % C.  
Use the carbon content of austenite (and thus of pearlite) at the eutectoid temperature C_γ = 0.76 wt % C.  
Calculate the mass fraction of proeutectoid ferrite (W_f) as  
W_f = (C_γ – C₀) / (C_γ – C_α).  
Calculate the mass fraction of pearlite (W_p) as  
W_p = 1 – W_f.  
Report the two mass fractions as percentages.
