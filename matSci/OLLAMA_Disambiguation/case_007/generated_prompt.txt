Calculate the difference between the overall composition and the α‑phase composition: 14.36 % B − 4.63 % B = 9.73 % B.  
Calculate the difference between the liquid composition and the α‑phase composition: 40.31 % B − 4.63 % B = 35.68 % B.  
Divide the first difference by the second to obtain the liquid fraction: 9.73 % B / 35.68 % B ≈ 0.273, or about 27 % of the alloy.
