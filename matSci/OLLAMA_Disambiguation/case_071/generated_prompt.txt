Calculate the diffusive flux using Fick’s first law: J = -D · (ΔC / Δx).  
Use D = 2.2 × 10⁻¹¹ m²/s, ΔC = 0.10 mol/m³, and membrane thickness Δx = 1.5 mm = 0.0015 m.  
Compute ΔC / Δx = 0.10 / 0.0015 = 66.67 mol/m⁴.  
Multiply by D: D · (ΔC / Δx) = 2.2 × 10⁻¹¹ × 66.67 = 1.47 × 10⁻⁹ mol/(m²·s).  
Apply the negative sign to indicate direction from high to low concentration: J = –1.47 × 10⁻⁹ mol/(m²·s).  
Report the magnitude of the steady diffusive flux as 1.5 × 10⁻⁹ mol/(m²·s) directed from the higher to the lower concentration side.
