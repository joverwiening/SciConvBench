Calculate the total charge Q = I·t = 2.074 A × 10.8 min × 60 s/min = 1.344 × 10³ C.  
Determine the moles of electrons nₑ = Q/F = 1.344 × 10³ C / 96 485 C·mol⁻¹ = 1.393 × 10⁻² mol.  
Assuming one electron per metal atom, compute the deposited mass m = M·nₑ = 103.65 g·mol⁻¹ × 1.393 × 10⁻² mol = 1.44 g.  
Compute the deposited volume V = m/ρ = 1.44 g / 16.041 g·cm⁻³ = 8.99 × 10⁻² cm³.  
Calculate the average coating thickness t = V/A = 8.99 × 10⁻² cm³ / 147.06 cm² = 6.12 × 10⁻⁴ cm = 6.12 μm.
