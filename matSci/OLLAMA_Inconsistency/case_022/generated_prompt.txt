Use the BCC unit‑cell population (n = 2).  
Given: atomic weight A = 96.98 g mol⁻¹, theoretical density ρ = 5.414 g cm⁻³, Avogadro constant N_A = 6.022 × 10²³ mol⁻¹.  
Compute the cubic lattice parameter a from the relation ρ = nA/(N_A a³).  
Rearrange to a³ = nA/(N_A ρ).  
Calculate a = [ n × A / (N_A × ρ) ]¹ᐟ³.  
Convert a from centimeters to nanometres (1 cm = 10⁷ nm).  
The resulting lattice parameter is a ≈ 0.39 nm.
