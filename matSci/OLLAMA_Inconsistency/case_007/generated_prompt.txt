Use the fracture toughness K_Ic = 105.8 MPa·√m, geometry factor Y = 0.98, and crack size a = 2.968 mm (0.002968 m). Compute √(πa) = √(π × 0.002968 m) ≈ 0.0966 √m. Divide K_Ic by Y × √(πa): σ = 105.8 MPa·√m / (0.98 × 0.0966) ≈ 1.12 × 10³ MPa, i.e., approximately 1118 MPa. The critical applied stress for fracture is about 1118 MPa.
