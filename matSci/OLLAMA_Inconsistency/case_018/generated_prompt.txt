Use the Griffith equation for a central crack: a = 2 E γ_s / (π σ_f²).  
Insert the given values: E = 240.3 GPa = 240.3 × 10⁹ Pa, γ_s = 2.576 J/m², σ_f = 529.7 MPa = 529.7 × 10⁶ Pa.  
Calculate a = (2 × 240.3 × 10⁹ Pa × 2.576 J/m²) / (π × (529.7 × 10⁶ Pa)²) ≈ 1.4 × 10⁻⁶ m.  
Convert to micrometers: a ≈ 1.4 μm.
