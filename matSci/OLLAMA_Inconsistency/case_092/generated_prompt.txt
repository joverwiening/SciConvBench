The work of adhesion W_A is calculated using the Young–Dupré equation: W_A = γ_LV (1 + cosθ).  
Convert the surface tension to joules per square meter: γ_LV = 72.8 mN/m = 0.0728 J/m².  
Compute the cosine of the contact angle: cos 30° = 0.866.  
Calculate W_A: W_A = 0.0728 J/m² × (1 + 0.866) = 0.0728 J/m² × 1.866 ≈ 0.1358 J/m² = 135.8 mJ/m².  
Since the result is positive, the correct sign of the work of adhesion is positive, not negative.  
Therefore, the reported value of –10 mJ/m² has an incorrect sign; the correct sign is +.
