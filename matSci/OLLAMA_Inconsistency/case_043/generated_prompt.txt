Assume a carburizing temperature of 900 °C, at which the carbon diffusion coefficient in austenite is D = 1.0 × 10⁻¹¹ m²/s.  
Use the semi‑infinite diffusion solution: (C(x,t) − C₀)/(C_s − C₀) = erfc[x / (2√(Dt))].  
Set the initial bulk carbon content C₀ = 0.20 wt% C, the surface carbon concentration C_s = 0.90 wt% C, the target depth x = 0.6 mm = 0.0006 m, and the desired carbon content at that depth C(x,t) = 0.60 wt% C.  
Calculate the required erfc argument: (0.60 − 0.20)/(0.90 − 0.20) ≈ 0.5714, which corresponds to erfc⁻¹(0.5714) ≈ 0.40.  
Solve for time: x/(2√(Dt)) = 0.40 → √(Dt) = x/0.8 = 0.00075 m → Dt = 5.625 × 10⁻⁷ m² → t = Dt/D ≈ 5.625 × 10⁴ s.  
Convert to hours: t ≈ 56 250 s ≈ 15.6 hours.  
Therefore, the estimated carburizing time to achieve 0.60 wt% C at 0.6 mm depth under the stated conditions is approximately 15.5–16 hours.
