According to the Curie law for a paramagnet, the magnetic susceptibility χ is inversely proportional to temperature (χ = C/T, where C is a positive constant).  
Therefore, when the temperature rises from 300 K to 600 K, the susceptibility decreases.  
Specifically, χ at 600 K is half of χ at 300 K (χ600 K = χ300 K × 300 K/600 K = 0.5 χ300 K).  
Thus the correct trend is a decrease in susceptibility with increasing temperature.
