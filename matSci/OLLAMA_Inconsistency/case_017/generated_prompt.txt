Use E = 245.0 GPa, which is 2.45 × 10¹¹ Pa.  
Use γₛ = 16.457 J/m².  
Use the half‑crack length a = 107.86 µm, which is 1.0786 × 10⁻⁴ m.  
Compute the fracture stress with the Griffith relation σ_f = √(2 E γₛ / (π a)).  
Calculate the numerator: 2 × 2.45 × 10¹¹ Pa × 16.457 J/m² = 8.06 × 10¹².  
Calculate the denominator: π × 1.0786 × 10⁻⁴ m = 3.39 × 10⁻⁴.  
Form the ratio: 8.06 × 10¹² / 3.39 × 10⁻⁴ = 2.38 × 10¹⁶.  
Take the square root: √(2.38 × 10¹⁶) = 1.54 × 10⁸ Pa.  
Convert to MPa: σ_f ≈ 154 MPa.
