Calculate the resolved shear stress τ on the {110}<111> slip system using the formula τ = σ · cos φ · cos λ. Use the applied uniaxial stress σ = 80 MPa, the angle between the loading direction and the slip plane normal φ = 45°, and the angle between the loading direction and the slip direction λ = 45°. Compute cos 45° = 0.7071, multiply the two cosine values to obtain 0.5, and then multiply by the applied stress: τ = 80 MPa × 0.5 = 40 MPa. Report the resolved shear stress as 40 MPa.
