Calculate the porosity using the formula: porosity = (1 − bulk density / theoretical density) × 100 %.  
Insert the given values: bulk density = 3.25 g/cm³, theoretical density = 3.98 g/cm³.  
Compute the ratio: 3.25 / 3.98 ≈ 0.817.  
Subtract from 1: 1 − 0.817 ≈ 0.183.  
Multiply by 100 to obtain the percentage: 0.183 × 100 ≈ 18.3 %.  
The sintered alumina body has a porosity of approximately 18.3 %.
