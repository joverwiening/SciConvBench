Calculate the natural logarithm of the ratio of the two diffusion coefficients: ln(D2/D1).  
Compute the difference of the reciprocals of the temperatures: (1/T2 − 1/T1).  
Determine the activation energy using Q_d = –R · ln(D2/D1) / (1/T2 − 1/T1).  
Substitute R = 8.314 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹, D1 = 4.76×10⁻¹⁸ m²/s, T1 = 866 K, D2 = 1.86×10⁻¹² m²/s, and T2 = 1438 K.  
The activation energy Q_d is approximately 2.34×10⁵ J mol⁻¹, or 234 kJ mol⁻¹.
