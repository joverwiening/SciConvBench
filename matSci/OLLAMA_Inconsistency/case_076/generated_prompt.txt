Calculate linear shrinkage using the formula (initial length − final length) ÷ initial length × 100 %.  
With an initial length of 50.0 mm and a final length of 52.0 mm, the calculation yields (50.0 mm − 52.0 mm) ÷ 50.0 mm × 100 % = ‑4.0 %.  
The negative sign indicates a 4.0 % linear expansion, not a shrinkage.  
Therefore the correct interpretation is that the ceramic compact expanded by 4.0 % in length during sintering.
