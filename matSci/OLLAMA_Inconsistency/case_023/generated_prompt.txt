Calculate the lattice parameter a for the FCC metallic crystal using the given data.

1. Use the density formula ρ = n A / (N_A a³) with n = 4 atoms per unit cell, atomic weight A = 197.35 g mol⁻¹, Avogadro’s number N_A = 6.02214076 × 10²³ mol⁻¹, and the theoretical density ρ = 7.754 g cm⁻³.  
2. Rearrange the formula to solve for a: a = (n A / (N_A ρ))^(1/3).  
3. Compute the numerator: n A = 4 × 197.35 = 789.40 g mol⁻¹.  
4. Compute the denominator: N_A ρ = (6.02214076 × 10²³ mol⁻¹) × 7.754 g cm⁻³ = 4.672 × 10²⁴ g cm⁻³ mol⁻¹.  
5. Calculate a³: a³ = 789.40 / (4.672 × 10²⁴) = 1.689 × 10⁻²² cm³.  
6. Take the cube root: a = (1.689 × 10⁻²²)^(1/3) = 5.57 × 10⁻⁸ cm.  
7. Convert a from centimeters to nanometers: 1 cm = 10⁷ nm, so a = 5.57 × 10⁻⁸ cm × 10⁷ nm cm⁻¹ = 0.557 nm.  

The lattice parameter a is 0.557 nm.
