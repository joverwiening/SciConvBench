Convert the total crack length 2a = 4.0 mm to the half‑crack length a = 2.0 mm (0.002 m).  
Calculate the critical stress σ_c using the fracture mechanics relation σ_c = K_Ic / (Y √(π a)).  
Insert K_Ic = 35 MPa·√m, Y = 1.0, and a = 0.002 m into the formula.  
Compute σ_c ≈ 35 MPa·√m / (1.0 × √(π × 0.002 m)) ≈ 4.4 × 10² MPa (≈ 440 MPa).
