Calculate the band gap energy using the relation E = hc/λ.  
Use Planck’s constant h = 4.135667696 × 10⁻¹⁵ eV·s and the speed of light c = 2.99792458 × 10⁸ m/s.  
Convert the absorption edge wavelength λ = 900 nm to meters (λ = 9.00 × 10⁻⁷ m).  
Compute E = (4.135667696 × 10⁻¹⁵ eV·s × 2.99792458 × 10⁸ m/s) / (9.00 × 10⁻⁷ m) ≈ 1.38 eV.
