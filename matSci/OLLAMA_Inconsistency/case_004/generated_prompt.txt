Calculate the number of atomic sites per cubic meter: N = (density / atomic weight) × N_A × 1 000 000 = (3.380 g cm⁻³ / 169.90 g mol⁻¹) × 6.022×10²³ mol⁻¹ × 1 000 000 cm³ m⁻³ ≈ 1.20×10²⁸ atoms m⁻³.  
Convert the vacancy formation energy to a per‑atom value by dividing by Avogadro’s number, which is equivalent to using the gas constant: exponent = –Q_v / (R T) = –87 600 J mol⁻¹ / (8.314 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹ × 1443 K) ≈ –7.30.  
Compute the exponential factor: exp(–7.30) ≈ 6.8×10⁻⁴.  
Multiply by N to obtain the equilibrium vacancy concentration: n_v = N × exp(–Q_v/(k_B T)) ≈ 1.20×10²⁸ m⁻³ × 6.8×10⁻⁴ ≈ 8.0×10²⁴ vacancies m⁻³.
