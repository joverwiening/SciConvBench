Calculate the elastic modulus using the formula E ≈ 3 νₑ R T.  
Convert the crosslink density to SI units: νₑ = 4.0 × 10⁻⁵ mol cm⁻³ = 40 mol m⁻³.  
Use the universal gas constant R = 8.314 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹ and the temperature T = 298 K.  
Compute E = 3 × 40 mol m⁻³ × 8.314 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹ × 298 K ≈ 2.97 × 10⁵ Pa.  
Report the modulus as approximately 3.0 × 10⁵ Pa (0.30 MPa).
