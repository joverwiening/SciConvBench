Calculate the volume percent crystallinity X_c using the formula X_c = [ρ_c (ρ_s − ρ_a)] / [ρ_s (ρ_c − ρ_a)] × 100.

Substitute the given densities:
ρ_c = 1.4240 g/cm³, ρ_s = 1.1973 g/cm³, ρ_a = 0.8845 g/cm³.

Compute the numerator:
ρ_c (ρ_s − ρ_a) = 1.4240 × (1.1973 − 0.8845) = 1.4240 × 0.3128 = 0.4454.

Compute the denominator:
ρ_s (ρ_c − ρ_a) = 1.1973 × (1.4240 − 0.8845) = 1.1973 × 0.5395 = 0.6459.

Form the ratio and convert to percent:
X_c = (0.4454 / 0.6459) × 100 = 0.6896 × 100 ≈ 68.96 %.

Report the result as approximately 69 % volume crystallinity.
