Calculate the equilibrium constant using the relation ΔG° = -RT ln K.  
Convert ΔG° to joules: –12 kJ mol⁻¹ = –12000 J mol⁻¹.  
At 298 K, use R = 8.314 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹.  
Compute ln K = –ΔG°/(RT) = 12000 J mol⁻¹ / (8.314 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹ × 298 K) ≈ 4.85.  
Determine K = e^(4.85) ≈ 1.3 × 10², which is greater than 1.  
Therefore, the equilibrium constant should be greater than 1.
