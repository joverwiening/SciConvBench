Use the atomic mass of zirconium (Zr) as 91.22 g/mol and the atomic mass of oxygen (O) as 16.00 g/mol.  
Calculate the molar mass of one formula unit of ZrO₂ as 91.22 + 2 × 16.00 = 123.22 g/mol.  
Multiply by the number of formula units per unit cell (8) to obtain the mass per mole of unit cells: 8 × 123.22 = 985.76 g/mol.  
Convert this mass to grams per unit cell by dividing by Avogadro’s number (6.022 × 10²³ mol⁻¹): mass = 985.76 / (6.022 × 10²³) ≈ 1.64 × 10⁻²¹ g.  
Convert the lattice parameter a = 0.509 nm to centimeters (1 nm = 1 × 10⁻⁷ cm), giving a = 5.09 × 10⁻⁸ cm.  
Compute the unit‑cell volume as a³ = (5.09 × 10⁻⁸ cm)³ ≈ 1.32 × 10⁻²² cm³.  
Determine the theoretical density by dividing the mass per unit cell by the unit‑cell volume: density = (1.64 × 10⁻²¹ g) / (1.32 × 10⁻²² cm³) ≈ 12.4 g · cm⁻³.
