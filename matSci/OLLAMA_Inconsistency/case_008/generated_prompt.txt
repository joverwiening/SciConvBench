The weight fraction of liquid just above the eutectic temperature is calculated with the lever rule: (C0 – Cαe) / (Ce – Cαe). Substituting the given values gives (14.36 wt% B – 4.63 wt% B) / (40.31 wt% B – 4.63 wt% B) = 9.73 / 35.68 ≈ 0.273. Therefore, approximately 27 % (by weight) of the alloy is liquid and will transform into the eutectic microconstituent on cooling.
