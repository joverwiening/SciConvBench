Use the vacancy formation energy Q_v = +0.85 eV per atom and temperature T = 1200 K.  
Take the Boltzmann constant k = 8.617 × 10⁻⁵ eV K⁻¹.  
Compute the exponent: –Q_v/(k T) = –0.85 eV / (8.617 × 10⁻⁵ eV K⁻¹ × 1200 K) ≈ –8.22.  
Calculate the vacancy fraction: N_v/N = exp(–8.22) ≈ 2.7 × 10⁻⁴.  
Thus the equilibrium vacancy fraction at 1200 K is approximately 2.7 × 10⁻⁴ (about 0.027 %).
