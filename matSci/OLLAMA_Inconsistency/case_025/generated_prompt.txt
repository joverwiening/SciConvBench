Use the density formula ρ = n A / (N_A a³) with n = 2 (BCC), atomic weight A = 183.72 g mol⁻¹, Avogadro’s number N_A = 6.02214076 × 10²³ mol⁻¹, and the given theoretical density ρ = 16.416 g cm⁻³.  
Solve for the lattice parameter: a = (n A / (N_A ρ))^(1/3).  
Calculate a³ = (2 × 183.72) / (6.02214076 × 10²³ × 16.416) cm³ ≈ 3.718 × 10⁻²³ cm³.  
Take the cube root: a ≈ (3.718 × 10⁻²³)^(1/3) cm ≈ 3.34 × 10⁻⁸ cm.  
Convert to nanometres (1 cm = 10⁷ nm): a ≈ 0.334 nm.
