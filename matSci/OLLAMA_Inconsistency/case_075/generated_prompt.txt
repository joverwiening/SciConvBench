The transverse modulus of the composite is calculated using the rule of mixtures: E_T = V_f E_f + (1 – V_f) E_m.  
Assume a typical carbon‑fiber/epoxy composite with a fiber volume fraction V_f of 0.60, a fiber modulus E_f of 230 GPa, and a matrix modulus E_m of 3.5 GPa.  
Substituting these values gives E_T = 0.60 × 230 GPa + 0.40 × 3.5 GPa = 138 GPa + 1.4 GPa = 139.4 GPa.  
Therefore, the transverse modulus E_T is approximately 139 GPa.
