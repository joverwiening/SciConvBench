Calculate the weight-average molecular weight M_w in g/mol for the polymer sample.
Use the provided molecular weights (M_i) and corresponding number of molecules (N_i).
For each molecular weight, compute the product of M_i and N_i.
Sum all the N_i values to get the total number of molecules.
Sum all the (M_i * N_i) products to get the total weight.
For each molecular weight, compute the product of M_i and (M_i * N_i).
Sum all the (M_i * (M_i * N_i)) products.
Divide the sum of (M_i * (M_i * N_i)) by the sum of (M_i * N_i) to obtain M_w.
Report the final M_w value in g/mol.
