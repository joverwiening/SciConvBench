Q: What is the type of flaw (e.g., internal penny-shaped crack, surface crack, through-thickness crack)?
A: The complete requirement specifies using the Griffith relation σ_f = sqrt(2Eγ_s / (πa)). This formula is for a through-thickness crack of length 2a in an infinite plate.
