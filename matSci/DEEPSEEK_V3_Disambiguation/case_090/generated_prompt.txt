Estimate the axial coefficient of thermal expansion (CTE) for a unidirectional continuous-fiber composite.
Assume the fiber material is carbon (graphite).
Assume the matrix material is epoxy.
Assume a fiber volume fraction (V_f) of 0.60.
Assume the fibers are perfectly aligned with the axis of interest (axial/isostrain condition).
Assume a linear, room-temperature (approximately 20°C to 100°C) estimation is sufficient.
Use the rule-of-mixtures for axial CTE: α_c = (E_f * V_f * α_f + E_m * V_m * α_m) / (E_f * V_f + E_m * V_m), where V_m = 1 - V_f.
Assume typical material properties: for carbon fiber, E_f = 230 GPa and α_f = -0.5e-6 /°C; for epoxy matrix, E_m = 3.5 GPa and α_m = 55e-6 /°C.
Calculate the axial CTE α_c using the provided formula and assumed properties.
Report the calculated value in units of 10^-6 per degree Celsius (ppm/°C).
