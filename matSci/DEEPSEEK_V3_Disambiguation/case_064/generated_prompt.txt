Assume the FCC single crystal is made of aluminum, a common FCC metal used in such examples.
The tensile stress is 65 MPa.
The tensile axis is oriented at an angle φ = 54.7° to the slip plane normal.
The tensile axis is oriented at an angle λ = 45.0° to the slip direction.
The resolved shear stress (τ_RSS) is calculated using the Schmid Law: τ_RSS = σ * cos(φ) * cos(λ).
Calculate cos(54.7°) and cos(45.0°).
Multiply the stress by these two cosines to obtain the resolved shear stress.
Report the resolved shear stress in MPa.
