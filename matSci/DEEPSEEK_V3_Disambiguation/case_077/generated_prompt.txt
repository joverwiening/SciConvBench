The steel has an overall composition of 0.60 wt% C.
The steel is slowly cooled to just below the eutectoid temperature.
The eutectoid composition is 0.76 wt% C.
The composition of proeutectoid ferrite is 0.022 wt% C.
Calculate the mass fraction of proeutectoid ferrite using the lever rule between the overall composition (0.60 wt% C) and the eutectoid composition (0.76 wt% C), with the ferrite composition (0.022 wt% C) as the other end of the tie line.
The mass fraction of proeutectoid ferrite is (0.76 - 0.60) / (0.76 - 0.022).
Calculate the mass fraction of pearlite as 1 minus the mass fraction of proeutectoid ferrite.
The mass fraction of pearlite is (0.60 - 0.022) / (0.76 - 0.022).
