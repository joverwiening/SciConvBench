The system is a hypoeutectic binary alloy with an overall composition C_0 of 30 wt% B.
The eutectic composition C_e is 40 wt% B.
The composition of the primary alpha phase at the eutectic temperature C_αe is 12 wt% B.
Calculate the fraction of the eutectic microconstituent using the lever rule applied to the alpha phase and the eutectic composition.
The fraction of primary alpha phase is (C_e - C_0) / (C_e - C_αe).
The fraction of the eutectic microconstituent is 1 minus the fraction of primary alpha phase.
Perform the calculation: fraction of primary alpha = (40 - 30) / (40 - 12) = 10 / 28 ≈ 0.357.
Therefore, the fraction of the eutectic microconstituent is 1 - 0.357 = 0.643 or 64.3%.
