The simulation models a p-type thermoelectric leg.
The leg experiences a temperature difference of ΔT = 60 K.
The Seebeck coefficient of the p-type material is S = +180 μV/K.
Assume the cold end temperature is 300 K and the hot end temperature is 360 K.
Calculate the open-circuit thermoelectric voltage as V_oc = S * ΔT.
The calculated open-circuit voltage is +10.8 mV.
