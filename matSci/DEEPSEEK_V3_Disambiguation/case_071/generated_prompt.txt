The diffusive flux is calculated using Fick's first law.
The ceramic membrane thickness is 1.5 mm.
The diffusivity D is 2.2×10^-11 m^2/s.
The concentration difference is 0.10 mol/m³.
The thickness must be converted to meters: 1.5 mm = 0.0015 m.
The flux J is calculated as J = -D * (ΔC / Δx).
The flux magnitude is (2.2×10^-11 m^2/s) * (0.10 mol/m³) / (0.0015 m).
The resulting diffusive flux is 1.47×10^-9 mol/(m²·s).
