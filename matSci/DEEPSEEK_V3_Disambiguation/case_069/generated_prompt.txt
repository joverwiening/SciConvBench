Assume a film thickness of 1 micrometer.
Assume a substrate thickness of 1 millimeter.
Assume the Young's modulus of aluminum is 70 GPa.
Assume the Young's modulus of the ceramic substrate is 300 GPa.
Assume the Poisson's ratio of aluminum is 0.33.
Assume the Poisson's ratio of the ceramic substrate is 0.22.
Assume the stress-free bonding temperature is 300°C (573 K).
The temperature change for cooling is -180 K.
Calculate the thermal-mismatch strain in the film using the formula for a thin film on a thick substrate: ε = (α_s - α_f) * ΔT.
The strain is ε = (8e-6 - 23e-6) K⁻¹ * (-180 K).
The resulting thermal-mismatch strain is 0.0027 (or 2700 microstrain).
