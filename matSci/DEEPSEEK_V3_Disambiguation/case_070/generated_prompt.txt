Assume the substrate is rigid and infinitely thick compared to the film.
Assume the film is isotropic and linearly elastic.
Assume the film is perfectly bonded to the substrate.
Assume the stress state is biaxial, with no stress in the direction normal to the film.
Use the formula for biaxial thermal stress in a thin film on a rigid substrate: σ = (E_f / (1 - ν_f)) * (α_s - α_f) * ΔT.
Substitute the given values: E_f = 160 GPa, ν_f = 0.28, α_f = 14×10^-6 K^-1, α_s = 6×10^-6 K^-1, and ΔT = -250 K.
Calculate the stress: σ = (160 × 10^9 Pa / (1 - 0.28)) * (6×10^-6 - 14×10^-6) K^-1 * (-250 K).
Compute the result: σ ≈ 444 MPa (tensile).
