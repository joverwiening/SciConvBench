The diffusion coefficient follows the Arrhenius equation: D = D0 * exp(-Q / (R * T)).
Given D1 = 4.27e-15 m^2/s at T1 = 771 K.
Given D2 = 1.13e-11 m^2/s at T2 = 1233 K.
Given R = 8.314 J/(mol·K).
Take the natural logarithm of the Arrhenius equation: ln(D) = ln(D0) - Q/(R*T).
For the two data points, subtract the equations to eliminate ln(D0): ln(D2/D1) = -Q/(R) * (1/T2 - 1/T1).
Solve for the activation energy Q: Q = -R * ln(D2/D1) / (1/T2 - 1/T1).
Calculate ln(D2/D1) = ln(1.13e-11 / 4.27e-15) = ln(2646.37) = 7.881.
Calculate (1/T2 - 1/T1) = (1/1233 - 1/771) = (0.0008110 - 0.001297) = -0.0004860.
Calculate Q = -8.314 * 7.881 / (-0.0004860) = 134,800 J/mol.
Convert Q to kJ/mol: Q = 134.8 kJ/mol.
Report the diffusion activation energy as 134.8 kJ/mol.
