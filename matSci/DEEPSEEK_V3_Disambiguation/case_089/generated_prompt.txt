The composite is unidirectional with 55% carbon fiber volume fraction and 45% epoxy matrix volume fraction.
The fibers are aligned along the axis for which thermal conductivity is being estimated.
Use the rule of mixtures for the longitudinal thermal conductivity: k_eff = V_f * k_f + V_m * k_m.
Assume a typical thermal conductivity for carbon fibers (k_f) as 100 W/m·K along the fiber axis.
Assume a typical thermal conductivity for epoxy (k_m) as 0.2 W/m·K.
Calculate the effective longitudinal thermal conductivity: k_eff = (0.55 * 100) + (0.45 * 0.2) = 55 + 0.09 = 55.09 W/m·K.
Report the estimated effective thermal conductivity along the fibers as 55.1 W/m·K.
