The task is to estimate the Young's modulus of an elastomer.
The given crosslink density is ν_e = 4.5×10^-5 mol/cm^3.
The specified temperature is T = 298 K.
Use the rubber-elasticity approximation E ≈ 3ν_eRT.
Calculate the result using the gas constant R = 8.314 J/(mol·K).
Report the final Young's modulus in appropriate units.
