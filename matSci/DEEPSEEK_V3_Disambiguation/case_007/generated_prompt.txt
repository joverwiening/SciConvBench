Assume the β phase composition at the eutectic temperature is 100 wt% B, as it is the terminal phase of the binary system.
Use the lever rule on the α + L region of the phase diagram just above the eutectic temperature.
The fraction of liquid is given by (C0 - C_αe) / (C_e - C_αe).
Substitute the given values: C0 = 14.36 wt% B, C_αe = 4.63 wt% B, C_e = 40.31 wt% B.
Calculate the fraction of liquid: (14.36 - 4.63) / (40.31 - 4.63) = 9.73 / 35.68.
The fraction of liquid is approximately 0.2727 or 27.27%.
This fraction of liquid will solidify as the eutectic microconstituent upon cooling through the eutectic temperature.
