The task is to calculate the activation energy for diffusion in kJ/mol.
The diffusion coefficient D1 is 4.76×10^-18 m^2/s at temperature T1 = 866 K.
The diffusion coefficient D2 is 1.86×10^-12 m^2/s at temperature T2 = 1438 K.
The diffusion follows Arrhenius behavior: D = D0 * exp(-Q / (R * T)).
The gas constant R is 8.314 J/mol·K.
Calculate the activation energy Q using the two-point form of the Arrhenius equation: ln(D2/D1) = -(Q/R) * (1/T2 - 1/T1).
Solve the equation for Q in units of J/mol.
Convert the resulting activation energy Q from J/mol to kJ/mol.
Report the final activation energy value in kJ/mol.
