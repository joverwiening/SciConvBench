The task is to estimate the average grain diameter d for a ferritic steel.
The steel obeys the Hall-Petch relationship: σ_y = σ_0 + k_y * d^(-1/2).
The given yield strength σ_y is 420 MPa.
The given Hall-Petch constant k_y is 0.74 MPa·m^(1/2).
The given intrinsic lattice friction stress σ_0 is 120 MPa.
Rearrange the Hall-Petch equation to solve for d: d^(-1/2) = (σ_y - σ_0) / k_y.
Calculate the term (σ_y - σ_0): 420 MPa - 120 MPa = 300 MPa.
Calculate d^(-1/2): 300 MPa / 0.74 MPa·m^(1/2) ≈ 405.4054 m^(-1/2).
Calculate d by squaring the reciprocal: d = (1 / 405.4054 m^(-1/2))^2.
Perform the calculation: d ≈ (0.0024667 m^(1/2))^2 ≈ 6.084e-6 m.
Convert the result to micrometers: d ≈ 6.084e-6 m * 1e6 µm/m ≈ 6.08 µm.
Report the estimated average grain diameter as approximately 6.1 micrometers.
