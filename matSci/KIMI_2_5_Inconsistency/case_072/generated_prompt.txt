The fully dense ceramic material has a thermal conductivity of 5.0 W/m·K.
The porous ceramic at 30% porosity has a thermal conductivity of 3.5 W/m·K.
The absolute conductivity reduction due to porosity is 1.5 W/m·K (calculated as 5.0 minus 3.5).
The relative conductivity reduction is 30% (calculated as 1.5 divided by 5.0, multiplied by 100).
The porosity-induced reduction in thermal conductivity is consistent with physical expectations, as introducing voids replaces thermally conductive solid material with insulating air.
