The simulation calculates the time required for carbon diffusion in a steel carburization process.

The steel foil has a thickness of 0.5 mm and is treated as a semi-infinite solid.

Carburization occurs from one face only.

The target depth for carbon composition is 0.8 mm below the surface.

The target carbon composition at 0.8 mm depth is 0.30 weight percent carbon.

The surface carbon composition is maintained at 0.90 weight percent carbon during the carburizing process.

The initial carbon composition of the steel foil is 0.20 weight percent carbon.

The carburizing temperature is 927 degrees Celsius (1200 Kelvin).

The diffusion coefficient of carbon in austenite at 927°C is approximately 1.28 × 10^-11 m²/s, calculated using D = D₀ exp(-Q/RT) with D₀ = 2.0 × 10^-6 m²/s and Q = 142 kJ/mol.

The solution uses the error function solution to Fick's second law for diffusion in a semi-infinite solid: (C(x,t) - C₀)/(Cs - C₀) = 1 - erf(x/(2√(Dt))).

The simulation solves for time t when the composition at x = 0.8 mm reaches 0.30 wt% C.

The calculated time required is approximately 7.1 hours.
