The user wants to calculate the atomic radius r of an FCC metal given the lattice parameter a = 0.408 nm, using the formula a = 4r/√3.

Rearranging the formula to solve for r:
r = a√3 / 4

Substituting a = 0.408 nm:
r = (0.408 nm × √3) / 4
r = (0.408 nm × 1.732) / 4
r = 0.7067 nm / 4
r = 0.1767 nm
