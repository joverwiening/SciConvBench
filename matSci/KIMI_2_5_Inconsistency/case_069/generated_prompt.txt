The elastomer modulus E shall be calculated using the formula E ≈ 3ν_eRT.

The crosslink density is ν_e = 4.0×10^-5 mol/cm^3.

The temperature is T = 298 K.

The gas constant is R = 8.314 J/(mol·K) = 8.314×10^6 dyn·cm/(mol·K).

Unit consistency requires converting ν_e to mol/m^3 for SI units or using cgs units throughout.

In SI units: ν_e = 4.0×10^-5 mol/cm^3 = 4.0×10^-5 × 10^6 mol/m^3 = 40 mol/m^3.

The modulus calculation yields E = 3 × (40 mol/m^3) × (8.314 J/(mol·K)) × (298 K) = 2.98×10^5 Pa ≈ 0.30 MPa.

In cgs units: E = 3 × (4.0×10^-5 mol/cm^3) × (8.314×10^6 dyn·cm/(mol·K)) × (298 K) = 2.98×10^6 dyn/cm^2 = 2.98×10^5 Pa.

The final modulus value is E ≈ 0.30 MPa or approximately 3.0×10^5 Pa.
