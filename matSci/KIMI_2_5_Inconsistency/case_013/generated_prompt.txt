Calculate the number-average molecular weight M_n using the formula M_n = Σ(N_i × M_i) / Σ(N_i).

Use the following data:
N1 = 96, M1 = 56526 g/mol
N2 = 131, M2 = 92809 g/mol
N3 = 153, M3 = 115856 g/mol
N4 = 146, M4 = 126029 g/mol

Compute the numerator as (96 × 56526) + (131 × 92809) + (153 × 115856) + (146 × 126029).
Compute the denominator as 96 + 131 + 153 + 146.
Divide the numerator by the denominator to obtain M_n in g/mol.
