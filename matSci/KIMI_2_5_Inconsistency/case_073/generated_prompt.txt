Calculate the fiber weight fraction w_f using the rule of mixtures for composites.

The fiber weight fraction is defined as the mass of fibers divided by the total mass of the composite.

Use the fiber volume fraction V_f = 0.60, fiber density ρ_f = 1.8 g/cm^3, and matrix density ρ_m = 1.2 g/cm^3.

Calculate the composite density ρ_c using the volume fraction rule: ρ_c = V_f × ρ_f + (1 - V_f) × ρ_m.

Calculate the mass of fibers per unit volume: m_f = V_f × ρ_f.

Calculate the mass of matrix per unit volume: m_m = (1 - V_f) × ρ_m.

Calculate the fiber weight fraction: w_f = m_f / (m_f + m_m) = (V_f × ρ_f) / ρ_c.

Substitute the given values: ρ_c = (0.60 × 1.8) + (0.40 × 1.2) = 1.08 + 0.48 = 1.56 g/cm^3.

Calculate w_f = (0.60 × 1.8) / 1.56 = 1.08 / 1.56 = 0.6923, or approximately 0.692 (69.2%).
