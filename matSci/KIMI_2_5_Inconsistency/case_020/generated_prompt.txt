The critical fiber length formula is l_c = σ_f d / (2τ_c).

The fiber tensile strength is σ_f = 3690.4 MPa.

The fiber diameter is d = 11.05 μm (note: the problem states "fiber radius is d" but uses d in the formula which is standard for diameter; the calculation uses d = 11.05 μm as the diameter).

The fiber-matrix interfacial shear strength is τ_c = 15.33 MPa.

Calculate the critical fiber length: l_c = (3690.4 MPa × 11.05 μm) / (2 × 15.33 MPa) = 40778.92 / 30.66 = 1330.0 μm.
