The alloy composition is C0 = 14.36 wt% B, which lies between C_αe = 4.63 wt% B and C_e = 40.31 wt% B, confirming this is a hypoeutectic alloy (not hypereutectic as stated, since C0 < C_e).

Apply the lever rule just above the eutectic temperature to find the weight fraction of liquid.

The liquid phase has composition C_e = 40.31 wt% B.
The α phase has composition C_αe = 4.63 wt% B.

The weight fraction of liquid equals (C0 - C_αe) / (C_e - C_αe).

Calculate the numerator: 14.36 - 4.63 = 9.73 wt% B.

Calculate the denominator: 40.31 - 4.63 = 35.68 wt% B.

The weight fraction of liquid is 9.73 / 35.68 = 0.2727, or approximately 0.273.

Therefore, the weight fraction of the alloy that is liquid just above the eutectic temperature is 0.273 (or 27.3%), and this entire amount will transform into eutectic microconstituent upon cooling through the eutectic temperature.
