The elastic constants E, ν, and G must satisfy the relationship G = E / [2(1 + ν)] for isotropic linear elastic materials.

Calculate the theoretical shear modulus from the given Young's modulus and Poisson's ratio:
G_theoretical = 210 GPa / [2(1 + 0.30)] = 210 GPa / 2.6 = 80.77 GPa

Compare with the assigned value:
G_assigned = 60 GPa

The assigned value of G = 60 GPa does not equal the theoretical value of 80.77 GPa calculated from E and ν.

The percent difference is (80.77 - 60) / 80.77 × 100% = 25.7%.
