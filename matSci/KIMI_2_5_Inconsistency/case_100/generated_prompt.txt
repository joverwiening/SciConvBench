The task is to determine whether the equilibrium constant K should be greater or less than 1 given ΔG° = -12 kJ/mol at 298 K.

Use the thermodynamic relationship ΔG° = -RT ln K, where R = 8.314 J/(mol·K) and T = 298 K.

Calculate ln K = -ΔG°/(RT) = -(-12000 J/mol)/(8.314 J/(mol·K) × 298 K) = 12000/2477.57 = 4.84.

Since ln K = 4.84 is positive, K = e^4.84 = 127.

Conclude that K should be greater than 1.

Explain that a negative ΔG° indicates a spontaneous reaction under standard conditions, which corresponds to K > 1, meaning products are favored at equilibrium.
