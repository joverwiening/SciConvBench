The user states the material is a simple paramagnet with χ = C/T.

The user also states that susceptibility increases as temperature rises from 300 K to 600 K.

These two statements are contradictory.

For a simple paramagnet obeying χ = C/T, susceptibility is inversely proportional to temperature.

As temperature increases from 300 K to 600 K, susceptibility must decrease by a factor of 2.

The correct trend is that susceptibility decreases with increasing temperature.

The statement that susceptibility increases from 300 K to 600 K is physically incorrect for a simple paramagnet.

The susceptibility at 300 K is χ_300 = C/300.

The susceptibility at 600 K is χ_600 = C/600 = χ_300/2.

Therefore, susceptibility decreases as temperature rises.
