The Vickers hardness test uses a 136° diamond pyramid indenter.
The applied load is 500 g, which equals 0.5 kg.
The indentation diagonal length is 0.42 mm.
The Vickers hardness number (HV) is calculated using the formula: HV = 1.8544 × (F / d²), where F is the load in kgf and d is the mean diagonal in mm.
Substituting the values: HV = 1.8544 × (0.5 / 0.42²) = 1.8544 × (0.5 / 0.1764) = 1.8544 × 2.834 = 5.25.
The Vickers hardness is approximately 5.25 HV (or more precisely, 5.254 HV when calculated with full precision).
