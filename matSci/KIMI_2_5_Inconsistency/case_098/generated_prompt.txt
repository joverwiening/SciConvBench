A defect pair consisting of one cation vacancy and one anion vacancy is described as a Schottky defect.

A Frenkel defect consists of a vacancy-interstitial pair where an ion moves from its lattice site to an interstitial site, creating both a vacancy and an interstitial defect of the same ion type.

A Schottky defect consists of a pair of cation and anion vacancies that maintain charge neutrality in the crystal lattice.
