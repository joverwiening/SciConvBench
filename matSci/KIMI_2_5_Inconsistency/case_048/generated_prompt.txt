The lever rule shall be applied to determine phase fractions for an alloy in the α+β two-phase field.

The tie line spans from C_α = 25 wt% B to C_β = 70 wt% B at the specified temperature.

The alloy composition is C_0 = 40 wt% B, which lies within the α+β two-phase field.

The fraction of α phase shall be calculated as W_α = (C_β - C_0) / (C_β - C_α) = (70 - 40) / (70 - 25) = 30 / 45 = 0.667 or 66.7%.

The fraction of β phase shall be calculated as W_β = (C_0 - C_α) / (C_β - C_α) = (40 - 25) / (70 - 25) = 15 / 45 = 0.333 or 33.3%.

The sum of phase fractions shall equal 1.0 (or 100%), which is verified: 0.667 + 0.333 = 1.0.
