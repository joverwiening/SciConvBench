The Nernst equation for the Cu²⁺/Cu electrode is E = E° + (0.0592/2) log a(Cu²⁺).

When a(Cu²⁺) decreases from 1 to 10⁻², the term log a(Cu²⁺) changes from log(1) = 0 to log(10⁻²) = -2.

The correction term (0.0592/2) log a(Cu²⁺) becomes (0.0592/2) × (-2) = -0.0592 V.

Therefore, the reduction potential decreases by approximately 0.059 V, making it more negative, not more positive.

The correct trend is that lowering a(Cu²⁺) makes the reduction potential more negative (less positive).

The statement claiming it becomes "more positive" is incorrect.
