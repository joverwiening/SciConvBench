The lattice parameter of the FCC metal is a = 0.361 nm.
The Burgers vector of the perfect dislocation is b = a/2 <111>.
The magnitude of the Burgers vector |b| is calculated as follows.
The direction <111> has components [1, 1, 1].
The magnitude of the direction vector is sqrt(1^2 + 1^2 + 1^2) = sqrt(3).
Therefore, |b| = (a/2) * sqrt(3) = (0.361 nm / 2) * sqrt(3) = 0.1805 nm * 1.732 = 0.313 nm.
The final answer for |b| is approximately 0.313 nm or 3.13 × 10^-10 m.
