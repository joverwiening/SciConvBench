The mass ordering is physically inconsistent and violates Archimedes' principle.

The dry mass of the porous ceramic is 12.4 g.

The saturated mass of the sample is 13.1 g.

The immersed mass of the saturated sample is 13.6 g.

For a submerged object, the immersed mass (apparent mass) must be less than the actual mass of the object due to buoyant force.

The saturated mass (13.1 g) represents the actual mass of the sample plus absorbed liquid.

The immersed mass (13.6 g) is greater than the saturated mass (13.1 g), which is physically impossible.

The buoyant force would reduce the apparent mass, so the immersed mass should be significantly less than 13.1 g, not greater.

Therefore, the reported mass values contain an error: either the saturated mass and immersed mass values are swapped, or one or more measurements are incorrect.
