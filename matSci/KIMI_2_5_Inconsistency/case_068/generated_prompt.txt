The degree of polymerization (DP) is calculated as the ratio of number-average molecular weight to repeat-unit molecular weight.

Calculate DP using the formula: DP = M_n / M_0.

Substitute the given values: DP = 78 g/mol / 104 g/mol.

Perform the division: DP = 0.75.

Report the degree of polymerization as 0.75 or 3/4.

Note: A DP value less than 1 indicates the average chain length is shorter than one repeat unit, which is physically unusual for a polymer and suggests either the substance is not truly polymeric, consists mainly of monomer/unreacted starting material, or the given values may represent a specific oligomeric state or measurement condition.
