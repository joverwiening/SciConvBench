The total surface energy of a solid is the sum of its dispersive and polar components. Given values of 24 mN/m (dispersive) and 18 mN/m (polar), the correct total should be 42 mN/m, not 30 mN/m.


The dispersive surface-energy component is 24 mN/m.
The polar surface-energy component is 18 mN/m.
The total surface energy is calculated as the sum of dispersive and polar components: 24 mN/m + 18 mN/m = 42 mN/m.
The reported value of 30 mN/m is incorrect.
The correct total surface energy is 42 mN/m.
