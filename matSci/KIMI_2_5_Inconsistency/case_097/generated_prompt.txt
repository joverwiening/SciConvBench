The defect described is a Frenkel defect.

A Schottky defect consists of paired cation and anion vacancies, maintaining charge neutrality in the crystal.

A Frenkel defect consists of a vacancy-interstitial pair where an atom or ion is displaced from its regular lattice site to an interstitial site, creating both a vacancy and an interstitial simultaneously. In ionic crystals, this typically involves cations due to their smaller size compared to anions.
