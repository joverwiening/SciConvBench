The correct electron count is n = 1 electron per formula unit.

The reaction FePO₄ + Li⁺ + e⁻ ↔ LiFePO₄ explicitly shows one electron on the reactant side.

In FePO₄, iron exists as Fe³⁺ (since phosphate is PO₄³⁻). In LiFePO₄, iron exists as Fe²⁺ (since lithium is Li⁺ and phosphate is PO₄³⁻). The reduction from Fe³⁺ to Fe²⁺ requires exactly one electron per iron atom.

The value n = 2 electrons per formula unit is incorrect for this reaction. This may stem from confusion with other battery chemistries or a misapplication of the theoretical specific capacity formula C = (nF)/(3.6M), where n must correctly reflect the actual electron transfer.
