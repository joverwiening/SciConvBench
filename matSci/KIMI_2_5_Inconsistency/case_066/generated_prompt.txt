The miscible polymer blend consists of 50 wt% polymer A and 50 wt% polymer B.
The glass transition temperature of polymer A is 373 K (100°C).
The glass transition temperature of polymer B is 293 K (20°C).
The Fox equation 1/T_g = w_A/T_g,A + w_B/T_g,B is applied using absolute temperature in Kelvin.
The weight fractions are w_A = 0.50 and w_B = 0.50.
Calculate 1/T_g = 0.50/373 K + 0.50/293 K.
Compute the weighted reciprocals: 0.50/373 = 0.001340 and 0.50/293 = 0.001706.
Sum the weighted reciprocals: 0.001340 + 0.001706 = 0.003046 K^-1.
Invert to find T_g: T_g = 1/0.003046 = 328.3 K.
Convert the result to Celsius if needed: T_g = 328.3 - 273.15 = 55.2°C.
The estimated glass transition temperature of the blend is approximately 328 K or 55°C.
