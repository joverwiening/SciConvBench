The user wants to evaluate the theoretical density of a ceramic crystal using the formula ρ = ZM/(N_A a^3).

Given parameters:
Z = 4 formula units per unit cell (note: this contradicts the stated 8 formula units, but the user explicitly specifies to use Z = 4 in the calculation)
M = 139.63 g/mol (molar mass of one formula unit)
N_A = 6.022×10^23 mol^-1 (Avogadro's number)
a = 0.6067 nm = 0.6067×10^-7 cm = 6.067×10^-8 cm (lattice parameter, converted to cm for final units of g/cm^3)

Calculation steps:
First convert the lattice parameter to cm: a = 0.6067 nm = 0.6067 × 10^-7 cm = 6.067 × 10^-8 cm.
Calculate a^3: (6.067 × 10^-8 cm)^3 = 2.233 × 10^-22 cm^3.
Calculate the numerator: Z × M = 4 × 139.63 g/mol = 558.52 g/mol.
Calculate the denominator: N_A × a^3 = (6.022 × 10^23 mol^-1) × (2.233 × 10^-22 cm^3) = 134.47 cm^3/mol.
Calculate ρ: (558.52 g/mol) / (134.47 cm^3/mol) = 4.153 g/cm^3.

The theoretical density is 4.15 g/cm^3 (or more precisely, 4.153 g/cm^3).
