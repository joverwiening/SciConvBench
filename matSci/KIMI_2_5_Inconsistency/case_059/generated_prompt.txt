The Larson-Miller parameter equation is P = T(C + log10 t_r), where P is dimensionless, T is temperature in Kelvin, C is a material constant, and t_r is rupture life in hours.

The evaluation temperature is T = 700 K (not 973 K, which would be 700°C).

Given values are C = 20 and P = 23,500.

Rearrange the equation to solve for log10 t_r: log10 t_r = (P/T) - C.

Substitute the values: log10 t_r = (23,500/700) - 20 = 33.571 - 20 = 13.571.

Calculate t_r: t_r = 10^13.571 hours.

Compute 10^13.571 = 10^0.571 × 10^13 = 3.72 × 10^13 hours.

The estimated rupture life t_r is approximately 3.7 × 10^13 hours (or more precisely, 3.72 × 10^13 hours).
