The transformation follows the Avrami equation X = 1 - exp(-k t^n).

The time unit in the equation is minutes, but the rate constant k is given in s^-n.

First, convert k to consistent units of min^-n.

There are 60 seconds in 1 minute, so k in min^-n equals (7.83×10^-3 s^-n) × (60 s/min)^n.

Calculate k in min^-n: 7.83×10^-3 × (60)^2.81 = 7.83×10^-3 × 60^2.81.

60^2.81 = exp(2.81 × ln(60)) = exp(2.81 × 4.0943) = exp(11.505) = 9.93×10^4.

So k = 7.83×10^-3 × 9.93×10^4 min^-n = 777.5 min^-n.

Rearrange the Avrami equation to solve for t: X = 1 - exp(-k t^n), so exp(-k t^n) = 1 - X, thus -k t^n = ln(1 - X), and t^n = -ln(1 - X)/k.

So t^n = 0.3202 / 777.5 = 4.118×10^-4 min^n.
