Calculate the number of atomic sites per unit volume N using the formula N = (N_A × ρ) / A_w, where ρ is density and A_w is atomic weight.
Convert density from g/cm³ to kg/m³: 3.380 g/cm³ = 3380 kg/m³.
Convert atomic weight from g/mol to kg/mol: 169.90 g/mol = 0.16990 kg/mol.
Calculate N = (6.022×10²³ mol⁻¹ × 3380 kg/m³) / 0.16990 kg/mol = 1.197×10²⁸ m⁻³.
Convert vacancy formation energy from kJ/mol to J/mol: 87.6 kJ/mol = 87600 J/mol.
Calculate the dimensionless exponent term Q_v / (R × T) = 87600 J/mol / (8.314 J/mol·K × 1443 K) = 7.300.
Calculate the Boltzmann factor exp(-Q_v / R T) = exp(-7.300) = 6.76×10⁻⁴.
Calculate the equilibrium vacancy concentration n_v = N × exp(-Q_v / R T) = 1.197×10²⁸ m⁻³ × 6.76×10⁻⁴ = 8.09×10²⁴ m⁻³.
Report the final answer as approximately 8.1×10²⁴ vacancies per m³.
