The biaxial thermal stress in the thin film will be calculated using the thermal stress equation for a constrained film.
The thermal stress is calculated as σ = -E_f * α_f * ΔT / (1 - ν_f).
Substituting the given values: σ = -(160 × 10^9 Pa) * (14 × 10^-6 K^-1) * (-250 K) / (1 - 0.28).
The calculation will result in the biaxial thermal stress magnitude and sign.
The negative temperature change (cooling) and positive thermal expansion coefficient will produce a compressive stress in the film.
The final biaxial thermal stress will be calculated to determine the magnitude of stress induced by the thermal excursion.
