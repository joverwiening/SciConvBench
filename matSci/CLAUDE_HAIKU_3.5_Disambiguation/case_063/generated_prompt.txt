The task is to estimate the average grain diameter for a ferritic steel using the Hall-Petch equation.
The yield strength (σ_y) is 420 MPa.
The Hall-Petch slope (k_y) is 0.74 MPa·m^(1/2).
The initial yield strength (σ_0) is 120 MPa.
Rearrange the Hall-Petch equation to solve for grain diameter (d): d = [(σ_y - σ_0) / k_y]^(-2).
Substitute the values: d = [(420 MPa - 120 MPa) / (0.74 MPa·m^(1/2))]^(-2).
Calculate the average grain diameter.
The result will be in meters (m).
