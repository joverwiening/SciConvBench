Determine the electrical resistivity of a metallic wire using the following specifications.
The wire length is 1.470 meters.
The wire cross-sectional diameter is 2.624 millimeters.
The measured wire resistance is 0.249 ohms.
Assume the measurement was taken at standard room temperature (20°C or 293.15 K).
Calculate the electrical resistivity using the formula: ρ = R * (A / L), where:
ρ is electrical resistivity (Ω·m)
R is measured resistance (0.249 Ω)
A is wire cross-sectional area (calculated from diameter)
L is wire length (1.470 m)
Report the final electrical resistivity value in Ω·m units.
