The corrosion penetration rate will be calculated as follows:

Mass loss = 0.084 g
Coupon thickness = 3 mm
Surface area = 12.0 cm^2
Exposure time = 240 h

Convert exposure time to years: 240 h = 0.0274 years

Assume steel density is 7.85 g/cm^3

Calculate corrosion penetration rate using the formula:
Penetration rate = (Mass loss / (Density * Surface Area * Time)) * Conversion factor

Penetration rate = (0.084 g / (7.85 g/cm^3 * 12.0 cm^2 * 0.0274 years)) * 1000 mm/m
                 = 0.41 mm/year

The estimated corrosion penetration rate is 0.41 mm/year.
