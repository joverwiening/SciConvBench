Q: What is the method or formula you would like me to use to calculate the fiber volume fraction V_f?
A: Use the rule of mixtures formula for volume fraction, which relates weight fraction, densities, and volume fraction:

V_f = (W_f * ρ_m) / (W_f * ρ_m + (1 - W_f) * ρ_f)

Where:
W_f = 0.361 (fiber weight fraction)
ρ_m = 2.035 g/cm^3 (matrix density)
ρ_f = 4.277 g/cm^3 (fiber density)
