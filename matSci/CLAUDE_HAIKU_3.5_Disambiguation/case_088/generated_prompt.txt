The theoretical specific capacity of the FePO_4 cathode will be calculated as follows.
x moles of Li are exchanged per mole of active material.
1 mole of electrons is exchanged per mole of Li.
The charge of an electron is 1.602 × 10^-19 coulombs.
Theoretical specific capacity will be calculated by multiplying: x * 1 * (1.602 × 10^-19 C/electron) * (1 F/1 C) * (3600 s/1 h) * (1 mAh/1000 C).
The result will be expressed in milliamp-hours per gram (mAh/g) of active material.
