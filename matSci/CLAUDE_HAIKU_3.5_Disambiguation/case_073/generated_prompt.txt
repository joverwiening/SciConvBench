Solve for K in the grain growth equation D^n - D_0^n = Kt using the following parameters.
Initial grain diameter (D_0) is 12 μm.

Exponent (n) is 2.
Time (t) is 2.0 hours.
Rearrange the equation to solve for K: K = (D^n - D_0^n) / t.
Substitute the values: K = (36^2 - 12^2) / 2.0.
