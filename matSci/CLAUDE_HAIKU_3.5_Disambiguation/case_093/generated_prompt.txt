The task is to estimate the glass transition temperature of a polymer blend using the Fox equation.

Polymer blend composition:
35 wt% polymer A with Tg = 373 K
65 wt% polymer B with Tg = 263 K

Fox equation calculation steps:
Calculate the reciprocal of the blend's glass transition temperature using the weight fractions and individual Tg values.
1/Tg(blend) = (w1/Tg1) + (w2/Tg2)
Where:
w1 = weight fraction of polymer A = 0.35
Tg1 = glass transition temperature of polymer A = 373 K
w2 = weight fraction of polymer B = 0.65
Tg2 = glass transition temperature of polymer B = 263 K

Perform the calculation by substituting the values into the Fox equation.
The result will provide the estimated glass transition temperature of the polymer blend.
