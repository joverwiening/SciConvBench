The effective thermal conductivity of the unidirectional composite will be calculated using the parallel rule of mixtures.
The volume fraction of carbon fibers is 0.55.
The volume fraction of epoxy is 0.45.
The thermal conductivity of carbon fibers is 18 W/m·K.
The thermal conductivity of epoxy is 0.25 W/m·K.
The effective thermal conductivity is calculated by: k_eff = (0.55 * 18) + (0.45 * 0.25).
The effective thermal conductivity along the fiber direction is 10.025 W/m·K.
