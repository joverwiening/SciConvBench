The electrical conductivity of the n-type semiconductor will be calculated as follows.
Electron concentration (n) = 2.0 × 10^21 m^-3
Electron mobility (μn) = 0.12 m^2/V·s
Electron charge (e) = 1.6 × 10^-19 C
Electrical conductivity (σ) will be calculated using the formula: σ = n × e × μn
The calculation will be performed at 300 K.
The result will be expressed in units of siemens per meter (S/m).
