The Burgers vector magnitude for a perfect dislocation in an FCC metal with lattice parameter a = 0.404 nm will be calculated as follows.
The Burgers vector magnitude is b = a/2 * √2.
Substitute the lattice parameter: b = (0.404 nm)/2 * √2.
Calculate the Burgers vector magnitude: b = 0.286 nm.
The direction of the Burgers vector is along the <110> crystallographic direction.
The calculation assumes a perfect edge or screw dislocation in the FCC crystal structure.
