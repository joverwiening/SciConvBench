I will assume standard room temperature of 300 K for this band gap estimation.

To calculate the band gap energy from the wavelength, I'll use the photon energy equation E = hc/λ:

h (Planck's constant) = 4.136 × 10^-15 eV·s
c (speed of light) = 3 × 10^8 m/s
λ = 620 nm = 620 × 10^-9 m

E = (4.136 × 10^-15 eV·s) × (3 × 10^8 m/s) / (620 × 10^-9 m)
  = 2.00 eV
