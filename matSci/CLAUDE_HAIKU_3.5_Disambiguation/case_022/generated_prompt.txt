Calculate the lattice parameter for a face-centered cubic (FCC) metal with the following parameters:
Atomic weight = 85.15 g/mol
Theoretical density = 13.931 g/cm³
Avogadro's number = 6.022 × 10^23 mol^-1
Number of atoms per unit cell = 4

Calculation steps:
Determine the volume of the unit cell using the density and atomic weight.
Calculate the edge length (lattice parameter) of the cubic unit cell.
Express the final lattice parameter in nanometers (nm).
