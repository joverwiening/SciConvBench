I will assume the reference temperature is 300 K (room temperature), which is a standard practice in thermoelectric material characterization.


The open-circuit thermoelectric voltage will be calculated as follows:
The thermoelectric leg is a p-type material.
The temperature difference (ΔT) is 60 K.
The Seebeck coefficient (S) is +180 μV/K.
The reference temperature is 300 K.
The open-circuit voltage is calculated by multiplying the Seebeck coefficient by the temperature difference.
V = S * ΔT = (+180 μV/K) * (60 K) = 10,800 μV = 10.8 mV.
