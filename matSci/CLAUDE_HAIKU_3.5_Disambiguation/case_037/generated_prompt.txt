Determine the steady heat-transfer rate through a two-layer composite wall using thermal resistance in series.

The wall consists of two layers:
First layer: 10.91 mm thick, thermal conductivity k1 = 4.69 W/m·K
Second layer: 8.09 mm thick, thermal conductivity k2 = 6.75 W/m·K
Total wall thickness: 19.00 mm
Wall area: 0.0782 m²
Temperature difference: 170.6°C

Calculate heat transfer rate using the thermal resistance formula:
Q = (ΔT) / (R1 + R2)
Where:
R1 = L1 / (k1 * A)
R2 = L2 / (k2 * A)
Q = heat transfer rate in watts
L1 = first layer thickness
L2 = second layer thickness
k1 = first layer thermal conductivity
k2 = second layer thermal conductivity
A = wall area
ΔT = temperature difference

Solve the equation step by step to find the steady heat-transfer rate in watts.
