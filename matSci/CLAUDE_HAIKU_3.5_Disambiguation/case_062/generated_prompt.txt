Calculate the fiber volume fraction using the rule of mixtures with the following parameters.
Use the weight fraction of 0.795 for the fibers.
Use the fiber density of 3.711 g/cm^3.
Use the matrix density of 1.609 g/cm^3.
Apply the volume fraction calculation formula: Vf = Wf * (ρm / (Wf * ρm + Wm * ρf)), where:
Vf is the fiber volume fraction
Wf is the fiber weight fraction (0.795)
Wm is the matrix weight fraction (1 - Wf = 0.205)
ρm is the matrix density (1.609 g/cm^3)
ρf is the fiber density (3.711 g/cm^3)
Report the final calculated fiber volume fraction as a decimal value.
