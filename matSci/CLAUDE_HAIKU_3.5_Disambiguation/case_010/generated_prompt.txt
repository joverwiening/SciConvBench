The electrical resistivity of the wire material will be calculated using the formula: ρ = (R * A) / L
Where:
R = Electrical resistance = 11.705 Ω
A = Cross-sectional area = 2.963 × 10⁻⁶ m²
L = Wire length = 0.492 m

Calculation steps:
ρ = (11.705 Ω * 2.963 × 10⁻⁶ m²) / 0.492 m
ρ = 7.05 × 10⁻⁵ Ω·m
