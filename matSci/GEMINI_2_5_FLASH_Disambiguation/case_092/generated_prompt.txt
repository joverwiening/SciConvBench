The degree of crystallinity is estimated by dividing the melting endotherm of the sample by the melting enthalpy of 100% crystalline polypropylene.
The melting endotherm of the semicrystalline polypropylene sample is 78 J/g.
The melting enthalpy of 100% crystalline polypropylene is 209 J/g.
The degree of crystallinity is (78 J/g / 209 J/g) * 100%.
