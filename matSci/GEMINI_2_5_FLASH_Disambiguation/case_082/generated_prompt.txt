The semiconductor material is assumed to be Silicon (Si).
The electron concentration (n) is 2.0×10^21 m^-3.
The temperature is 300 K.
The electron mobility (μ_n) for n-type Silicon at 300 K is assumed to be 0.14 m^2/(V·s).
The elementary charge (e) is 1.602 × 10^-19 C.
The electrical conductivity (σ) is calculated using the formula σ = n * e * μ_n.
The calculated electrical conductivity is 44.86 S/m.
