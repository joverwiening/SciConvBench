The total charge passed is calculated from the current and time.
The moles of electrons transferred are calculated using the total charge and Faraday's constant.
The moles of metal deposited are calculated using the moles of electrons and the number of electrons transferred per mole of metal.
The mass of metal deposited is calculated using the moles of metal and its molar mass.
The volume of metal deposited is calculated using its mass and density.
The average coating thickness is calculated by dividing the volume of metal by the given area.
The final thickness is expressed in micrometers.
