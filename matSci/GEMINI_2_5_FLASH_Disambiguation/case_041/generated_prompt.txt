The total charge passed is calculated by multiplying the current by the time in seconds. The moles of electrons transferred are determined by dividing the total charge by the Faraday constant. The moles of deposited metal are found by dividing the moles of electrons by the number of electrons transferred per mole of metal. The mass of the deposited metal is calculated by multiplying the moles of deposited metal by its molar mass. The volume of the deposited metal is obtained by dividing its mass by its density. The average coating thickness in centimeters is determined by dividing the volume of the deposited metal by the given area. The final average coating thickness is expressed in micrometers.
