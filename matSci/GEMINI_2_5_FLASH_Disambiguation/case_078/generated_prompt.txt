The overall composition of the hypoeutectic binary alloy is 30 wt% B.
The eutectic composition of the alloy is 40 wt% B.
The composition of the alpha phase at the eutectic temperature is 12 wt% B.
The fraction of eutectic microconstituent present just below the eutectic temperature is calculated using the lever rule.
The fraction of eutectic microconstituent is (40 wt% B - 30 wt% B) / (40 wt% B - 12 wt% B).
The fraction of eutectic microconstituent is 0.357.
