The crystallinity of the semicrystalline polymer is calculated using the formula X_c = (ρ_c * (ρ_s - ρ_a)) / (ρ_s * (ρ_c - ρ_a)).
The density of the semicrystalline polymer (ρ_s) is 1.0383 g/cm^3.
The density of the amorphous phase (ρ_a) is 1.0015 g/cm^3.
The density of the crystalline phase (ρ_c) is 1.2157 g/cm^3.
