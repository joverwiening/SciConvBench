The composite is a unidirectional material.
The fiber volume fraction is 55%.
The matrix volume fraction is 45%.
The thermal conductivity of the carbon fibers is 18 W/m·K.
The thermal conductivity of the epoxy matrix is 0.25 W/m·K.
The effective thermal conductivity along the fibers is estimated using the rule of mixtures.
The calculation is performed as (0.55 * 18 W/m·K) + (0.45 * 0.25 W/m·K).
The estimated effective thermal conductivity along the fibers is 10.0125 W/m·K.
