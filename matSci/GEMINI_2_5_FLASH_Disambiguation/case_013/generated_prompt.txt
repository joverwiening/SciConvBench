The given data points are (21640 g/mol, 93), (74810 g/mol, 40), (135292 g/mol, 170), and (141802 g/mol, 48).
Calculate the weight-average molecular weight Mw using the formula Mw = sum(Ni * Mi^2) / sum(Ni * Mi).
The result should be expressed in g/mol.
