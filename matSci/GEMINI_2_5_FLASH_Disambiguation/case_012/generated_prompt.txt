The input data consists of molecular weights and their corresponding number of molecules.
The molecular weights are 56526 g/mol, 92809 g/mol, 115856 g/mol, and 126029 g/mol.
The corresponding number of molecules are 96, 131, 153, and 146, respectively.
Calculate the sum of the products of each molecular weight and its corresponding number of molecules.
Calculate the sum of all the number of molecules.
Divide the sum of products by the sum of the number of molecules.
The result is the number-average molecular weight M_n.
The unit for M_n is g/mol.
