Q: What is the method or formula you would like me to use to calculate the fiber volume fraction V_f from the given weight fractions and densities?
A: Use the volume fraction conversion formula:

V_f = w_f / (ρ_f) / [w_f / (ρ_f) + w_m / (ρ_m)]

Where:
- w_f = 0.360 (fiber weight fraction)
- ρ_f = 5.456 g/cm³ (fiber density)
- w_m = 1 - w_f = 0.640 (matrix weight fraction)
- ρ_m = 1.774 g/cm³ (matrix density)
