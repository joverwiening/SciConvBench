Using the Nernst equation: E = E° + (0.0592/2) log a_(Cu2+) at 25°C
Let's use E° = +0.34 V (standard reduction potential for Cu^2+/Cu)

1. At a_(Cu2+) = 1:
E = 0.34 + (0.0592/2) log(1)
E = 0.34 + 0 = 0.34 V

2. At a_(Cu2+) = 10^-2:
E = 0.34 + (0.0592/2) log(10^-2)
E = 0.34 + (0.0592/2)(-2)
E = 0.34 - 0.0592 = 0.2808 V

Comparing the two potentials:
0.34 V (at a_(Cu2+) = 1) > 0.2808 V (at a_(Cu2+) = 10^-2)
