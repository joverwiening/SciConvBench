The magnitude of the Burgers vector |b| is calculated as follows.
The lattice parameter a is 0.361 nm.
The Burgers vector is b = a/2 <110>.
Calculate |b| by determining the magnitude of the <110> direction in the FCC lattice.
|b| = (a/2) * √(1² + 1² + 0²).
|b| = (0.361 nm / 2) * √2.
|b| = 0.1805 nm * √2.
|b| = 0.255 nm.
