The specification for calculating the porosity of the sintered alumina body is as follows:
Use the porosity formula: Porosity = (1 - Bulk Density / Theoretical Density) × 100%.
Use the given values: Bulk Density = 4.25 g/cm^3 and Theoretical Density = 3.98 g/cm^3.
Calculate the porosity by dividing the bulk density by the theoretical density.
Subtract the result from 1.
Multiply the result by 100 to express the porosity as a percentage.
