The carburization problem specification is as follows:

The problem involves a 0.5 mm thick steel foil carburized from one face.
Treat the foil as a semi-infinite solid.
Determine the time required to reach a target carbon composition of 0.8% at a depth of 0.8 mm.
Use standard carburization modeling techniques to calculate the required time.
Assume the initial carbon composition of the steel foil is its base composition before carburization.
