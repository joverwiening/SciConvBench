The specification for analyzing the wettability of the polymer surface is as follows:

Measure the water contact angle of the polymer surface at 95°.
Interpret the contact angle as indicating a hydrophilic surface.
Note that the angle is between 90° and 180°, suggesting partial wetting characteristics.
Classify the surface as having intermediate wettability between hydrophobic and fully wettable surfaces.
Recognize that the surface will partially wet when in contact with water, allowing some water spreading but not complete wetting.
Understand that the 95° contact angle implies moderate surface energy and partial water interaction.
Recommend further investigation of surface chemistry and potential applications based on this wettability characteristic.
