Q: What is the specific calculation method you want me to use for verifying the number-average molecular weight (M_n) calculation?
A: The formula for calculating M_n is already specified in the requirement: M_n = Σ(N_i M_i^2) / Σ(N_i M_i). Use this exact formula to calculate the number-average molecular weight.
