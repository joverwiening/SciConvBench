The specification for determining the correctness of reported sintered oxide material properties is:

Verify the physical consistency of reported material density values.
Check that the relative density is less than or equal to 1.0.
Confirm that the relative density (0.88) and total porosity (12%) are mutually compatible.
Reject any density values that exceed the theoretical solid density of the material.
Ensure all reported material properties adhere to fundamental physical principles.
