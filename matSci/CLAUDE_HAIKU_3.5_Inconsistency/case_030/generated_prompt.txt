Determine the fraction of empty space in a BCC crystal structure.

The crystal structure has an atomic packing factor (APF) of 0.68.
The fraction of empty space is the complement of the atomic packing factor.
Calculate the empty space fraction by subtracting the APF from 1.0.
The fraction of empty space is 1.0 - 0.68 = 0.32 or 32%.

Calculation steps:
Atomic Packing Factor (APF) = 0.68
Fraction of Empty Space = 1.0 - APF = 1.0 - 0.68 = 0.32
Percentage of Empty Space = 32%
