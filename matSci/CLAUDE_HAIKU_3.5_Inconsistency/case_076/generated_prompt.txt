The specification for interpreting the ceramic compact linear shrinkage is:

Initial length is 50.0 mm.
Final length is 48.0 mm.
Linear shrinkage is calculated as: ((Initial Length - Final Length) / Initial Length) × 100%.
The linear shrinkage calculation is: ((50.0 mm - 48.0 mm) / 50.0 mm) × 100% = 4.0%.
The result represents a 4.0% reduction in length during the sintering process.
The calculation confirms the original problem statement of a 4.0% linear shrinkage.
