Q: What calculation method would you like me to use to verify the weight-average molecular weight (M_w)?
A: Use the formula M_w = Σ(N_i M_i) / ΣN_i, which is already specified in the requirement. Simply multiply each number of molecules (N_i) by its corresponding molecular weight (M_i), sum those products, then divide by the total number of molecules.
