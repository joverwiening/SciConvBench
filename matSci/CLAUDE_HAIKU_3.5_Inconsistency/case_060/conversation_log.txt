Q: What is the specific formula you want me to use to calculate the Vickers hardness number (HV)?
A: The standard Vickers hardness formula is:

HV = (1.854 × Load) / (Diagonal Length)²

Use the given load of 500 g and the indentation diagonal of 0.42 mm to calculate the Vickers hardness number.
