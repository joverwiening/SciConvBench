Q: What is the relationship between the standard Gibbs free-energy change (ΔG°) and the equilibrium constant (K) that would help determine whether K should be greater or less than 1?
A: The relationship between ΔG° and K is given by the equation: ΔG° = -RT ln(K)

At 298 K, since ΔG° is negative (-12 kJ/mol), ln(K) will be positive. This means K will be less than 1.
