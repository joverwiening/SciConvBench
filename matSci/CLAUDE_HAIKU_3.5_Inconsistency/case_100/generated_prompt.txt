The problem requires determining the relationship between standard Gibbs free-energy change and equilibrium constant.
The equation ΔG° = -RT ln(K) relates standard Gibbs free-energy change to the equilibrium constant.
At 298 K, ΔG° = -12 kJ/mol, which is negative.
A negative ΔG° indicates ln(K) will be positive.
When ln(K) is positive, K will be less than 1.
The equilibrium constant K = 0.008 confirms this, as it is indeed less than 1.
The reaction will favor the reactants at equilibrium due to K < 1.
