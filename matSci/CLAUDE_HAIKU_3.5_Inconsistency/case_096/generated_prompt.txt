The specific surface area of spherical powders is inversely proportional to their particle diameter.
For the two powders with mean diameters of 0.5 μm and 5 μm:
The 0.5 μm powder will have a higher specific surface area compared to the 5 μm powder.
This trend is consistent with the fundamental relationship that smaller particles have more surface area per unit volume.
The specific surface area increases as the particle diameter decreases.
