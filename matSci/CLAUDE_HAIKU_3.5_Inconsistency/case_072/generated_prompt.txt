The thermal conductivity reduction due to porosity will be estimated using the Maxwell-Eucken equation.
The fully dense ceramic thermal conductivity is 2.0 W/m·K.
The porous ceramic thermal conductivity is 3.5 W/m·K at 30% porosity.
The thermal conductivity of air is 0.026 W/m·K.
The Maxwell-Eucken equation will be applied to model the effective thermal conductivity as a function of porosity.
The calculation will use the thermal conductivities of the dense ceramic matrix, air, and the 30% porosity volume fraction.
The result will quantify the porosity-induced reduction mechanism in thermal conductivity.
