The task is to determine Young's modulus for a cylindrical tensile sample.
The sample has an original cross-sectional diameter of 11.84 mm.
The sample has an original gauge length of 76.29 mm.
A load of 18.93 kN is applied to the sample.
The load causes an elastic extension of 0.0628 mm.
Calculate the stress using the applied load and the original cross-sectional area.
Calculate the strain using the elastic extension and the original gauge length.
Determine Young's modulus by dividing the calculated stress by the calculated strain.
Express the final result for Young's modulus in units of GPa.
