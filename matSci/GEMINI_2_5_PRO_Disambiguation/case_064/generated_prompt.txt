The material is an FCC single crystal.
A uniaxial tensile stress of 65 MPa is applied to the crystal.
The tensile load is applied along the [001] crystallographic direction.
The slip systems are of the {111}<110> family.
The active slip system is the one with the maximum Schmid factor.
The maximum Schmid factor for this loading condition is 0.408.
The resolved shear stress is calculated by multiplying the applied tensile stress by the maximum Schmid factor.
The resolved shear stress on the active slip system is 26.5 MPa.
