Calculate the percent crystallinity of the semicrystalline polymer.
Use the sample density (ρ_s) of 1.0383 g/cm^3.
Use the amorphous density (ρ_a) of 1.0015 g/cm^3.
Use the crystalline density (ρ_c) of 1.2157 g/cm^3.
Apply the formula: % Crystallinity = [ (ρ_s - ρ_a) / (ρ_c - ρ_a) ] * 100.
The resulting percent crystallinity is 17.18%.
