The calculation is for a 0.60 wt% C steel.
The steel is cooled to just below the eutectoid temperature.
The eutectoid composition is 0.76 wt% C.
The carbon concentration in ferrite is 0.022 wt% C.
The mass fraction of proeutectoid ferrite is determined to be 0.217.
The mass fraction of pearlite is determined to be 0.783.
