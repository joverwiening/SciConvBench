The specimen is a cylinder.
The original cross-sectional diameter is 18.16 mm.
The original gauge length is 37.69 mm.
An axial tensile load of 18.90 kN is applied.
The gauge length increases by 0.0206 mm under this load.
The material behavior is assumed to be linear elastic.
Calculate the cross-sectional area from the original diameter.
Calculate the axial stress by dividing the applied load by the cross-sectional area.
Calculate the axial strain by dividing the increase in gauge length by the original gauge length.
Calculate Young's modulus by dividing the axial stress by the axial strain.
Express the final result for Young's modulus in GPa.
