Calculate the electrical conductivity of a semiconductor.
The semiconductor material is Silicon.
The semiconductor is n-type.
The temperature is 300 K.
The electron concentration is 2.0 x 10^21 m^-3.
The intrinsic carrier concentration of Silicon at 300 K is 1.0 x 10^16 m^-3.
The electron mobility is 0.1333 m^2/(V·s).
The hole mobility is 0.0456 m^2/(V·s).
The elementary charge is 1.602 x 10^-19 C.
The hole concentration is calculated using the mass action law p = n_i^2 / n.
The conductivity is calculated using the formula σ = q * (n * μ_n + p * μ_p).
