Estimate the degree of crystallinity for a semicrystalline polypropylene sample.
The measured melting endotherm of the sample is 78 J/g.
The reference enthalpy of fusion for 100% crystalline polypropylene is 209 J/g.
Calculate the degree of crystallinity by dividing the sample's melting endotherm by the reference enthalpy of fusion and multiplying by 100.
The resulting degree of crystallinity is 37.3%.
