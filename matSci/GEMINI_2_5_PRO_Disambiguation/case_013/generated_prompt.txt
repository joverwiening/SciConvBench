Calculate the weight-average molecular weight (M_w) for the polymer sample.
The sample consists of four species with specified molecular weights (Mi) and number of molecules (Ni).
Species 1 has Mi = 21640 g/mol and Ni = 93.
Species 2 has Mi = 74810 g/mol and Ni = 40.
Species 3 has Mi = 135292 g/mol and Ni = 170.
Species 4 has Mi = 141802 g/mol and Ni = 48.
Use the formula M_w = (Σ Ni * Mi^2) / (Σ Ni * Mi).
The final calculated weight-average molecular weight is 124795.25 g/mol.
