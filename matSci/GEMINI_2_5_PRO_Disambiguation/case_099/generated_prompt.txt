Estimate the open-circuit thermoelectric voltage for a p-type leg.
The material of the leg is Bismuth Antimony Telluride ((Bi,Sb)2Te3).
The temperature at the hot end of the leg is 400 K.
The temperature at the cold end of the leg is 300 K.
The Seebeck coefficient of the material is assumed to be a constant 200 microvolts/Kelvin.
Calculate the voltage as the product of the Seebeck coefficient and the temperature difference.
