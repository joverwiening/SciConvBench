Calculate the biaxial thermal stress in a thin film on a rigid substrate.
The change in temperature is -250 K.
The Young's modulus of the film is 160 GPa.
The Poisson's ratio of the film is 0.28.
The coefficient of thermal expansion of the film is 14e-6 K^-1.
The coefficient of thermal expansion of the substrate is 6e-6 K^-1.
Use the formula for biaxial stress: σ = [E / (1 - ν)] * (α_substrate - α_film) * ΔT.
