Calculate the number-average molecular weight, M_n, for a polymer sample.
The sample has a molecular-weight distribution consisting of four species.
The first species has a molecular weight of 56526 g/mol and a count of 96.
The second species has a molecular weight of 92809 g/mol and a count of 131.
The third species has a molecular weight of 115856 g/mol and a count of 153.
The fourth species has a molecular weight of 126029 g/mol and a count of 146.
Use the formula M_n = Σ(Ni * Mi) / Σ(Ni), where Ni is the number of molecules of species i and Mi is the molecular weight of species i.
Express the final result in g/mol.
