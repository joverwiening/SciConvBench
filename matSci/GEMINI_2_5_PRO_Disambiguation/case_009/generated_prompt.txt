A composite wall consists of two layers, Layer 1 and Layer 2, arranged in series.
Layer 1 has a thickness of 9.74 mm.
The thermal conductivity of Layer 1 is 12.31 W/m·K.
Layer 2 has a thickness of 13.27 mm.
The thermal conductivity of Layer 2 is 107.49 W/m·K.
The total area of the wall is 0.0725 m².
One outer surface of the composite wall is maintained at a constant temperature of 198.1°C.
The other outer surface of the composite wall is maintained at a constant temperature of -25.4°C.
Contact resistance between the two layers is to be neglected.
Calculate the steady-state heat transfer rate through the composite wall.
The final result must be expressed in Watts (W).
