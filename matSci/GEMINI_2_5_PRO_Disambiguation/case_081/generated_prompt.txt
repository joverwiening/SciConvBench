Calculate the carrier concentration of a rectangular semiconductor sample.
The current (I) through the sample is 15 mA.
The magnetic field (B) is 0.80 T.
The Hall voltage (V_H) is 24 mV.
The thickness (t) of the sample is 0.50 mm.
Use the elementary charge (q) value of 1.602 x 10^-19 C.
The carrier concentration (n) is determined by the formula n = (I * B) / (V_H * q * t).
