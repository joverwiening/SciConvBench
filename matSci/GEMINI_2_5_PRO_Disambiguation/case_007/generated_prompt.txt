Calculate the fraction of liquid in the binary alloy just above the eutectic temperature using the lever rule.
The overall alloy composition is 14.36% B.
The solid α phase composition is 4.63% B.
The liquid phase composition is 40.31% B.
The fraction of liquid is calculated as (14.36 - 4.63) / (40.31 - 4.63).
The resulting fraction of liquid is 0.273.
