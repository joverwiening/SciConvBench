Calculate the steady-state heat transfer rate through a composite wall.
The wall consists of two layers in series.
The wall area is 0.2423 m^2.
The first layer has a thickness of 16.79 mm.
The first layer has a thermal conductivity of 24.68 W/m·K.
The second layer has a thickness of 8.73 mm.
The second layer has a thermal conductivity of 94.15 W/m·K.
The temperature on one outer surface of the wall is 134.0°C.
The temperature on the other outer surface of the wall is -26.8°C.
The final result must be expressed in Watts.
