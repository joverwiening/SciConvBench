The system consists of an aluminum film bonded to an Alumina (Al₂O₃) ceramic substrate.
The coefficient of thermal expansion for the aluminum film is 23×10⁻⁶ K⁻¹.
The coefficient of thermal expansion for the Alumina substrate is 8×10⁻⁶ K⁻¹.
The system undergoes a uniform cooling of 180 K.
The thermal-mismatch strain in the film is calculated.
The final estimated thermal-mismatch strain in the aluminum film is 0.0027.
