The task is to compute the theoretical density of a ceramic crystal in g/cm^3.
The crystal structure is a cubic unit cell.
The number of formula units per unit cell (n) is 1.
The molar mass (M) is 101.97 g/mol.
The cell edge length (a) is 0.6330 nm.
Avogadro's number (N_A) is 6.022 x 10^23 mol^-1.
First, convert the cell edge length from nanometers to centimeters.
Next, calculate the volume of the unit cell (V_c) in cm^3 using the formula V_c = a^3.
Finally, compute the theoretical density (ρ) using the formula ρ = (n * M) / (V_c * N_A).
Report the final result in g/cm^3.
