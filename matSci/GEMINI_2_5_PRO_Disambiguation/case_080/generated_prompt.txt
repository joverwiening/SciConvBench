Calculate the ionic conductivity of the solid electrolyte using the Nernst-Einstein relation.
The formula for the Nernst-Einstein relation is σ = (n * (z*e)^2 * D) / (k_B * T).
The mobile-ion number density (n) is 4.0 × 10^27 m^-3.
The diffusivity (D) is 1.2 × 10^-11 m^2/s.
The temperature (T) is 800 K.
The valence of the mobile ion (z) is 1.
Use the elementary charge (e) value of 1.602 × 10^-19 C.
Use the Boltzmann constant (k_B) value of 1.381 × 10^-23 J/K.
Express the final ionic conductivity in units of Siemens per meter (S/m).
