The task is to calculate the theoretical density of a ceramic crystal.
The number of formula units per unit cell is 8.
The molar mass of one formula unit is 139.63 g/mol.
The lattice parameter of the cubic unit cell is 0.6067 nm.
Avogadro's number is 6.022 x 10^23 mol^-1.
Convert the lattice parameter from nanometers to centimeters.
Calculate the volume of the unit cell by cubing the lattice parameter in centimeters.
Calculate the theoretical density using the formula: density = (number of formula units * molar mass) / (unit cell volume * Avogadro's number).
The final result must be expressed in g/cm^3.
