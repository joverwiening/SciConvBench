The task is to calculate the cubic lattice parameter.
The crystal structure is Body-Centered Cubic (BCC).
The number of atoms per unit cell (n) for a BCC structure is 2.
The atomic weight (A) is 129.68 g/mol.
The theoretical density (ρ) is 14.368 g/cm^3.
Avogadro's number (N_A) is 6.022 × 10^23 atoms/mol.
Calculate the volume of the unit cell (Vc) using the formula Vc = (n * A) / (ρ * N_A).
Calculate the lattice parameter (a) by taking the cube root of the unit cell volume.
Convert the calculated lattice parameter from centimeters to nanometers.
Report the final lattice parameter in nanometers.
