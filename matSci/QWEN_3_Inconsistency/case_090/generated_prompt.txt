Copper is electroplated from Cu²⁺ ions by gaining 2 electrons per ion to form neutral Cu atoms.  
The oxidation state of copper changes from +2 to 0 during the electroplating process.  
Faraday's law is applied using n = 2 moles of electrons per mole of copper deposited.  
A current of 2.0 A is applied for 30 minutes to deposit copper.  
The mass of copper deposited is calculated using the formula: m = (M * I * t) / (n * F), where M is the molar mass of copper, I is current, t is time in seconds, n = 2, and F is Faraday's constant.
