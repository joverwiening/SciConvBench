Q: What is the physical significance of a relative permittivity value less than 1 for a passive dielectric material?
A: A relative permittivity less than 1 is not physically consistent for a passive dielectric material. In reality, passive dielectrics have relative permittivity ≥ 1, as ε_r < 1 would imply a reduction in electric field strength compared to vacuum, which is not possible for passive, linear, isotropic materials. The reported value of ε_r = 0.82 is therefore unphysical. Note: The correct value in the complete requirement is ε_r = 8.2, which is physically reasonable.
