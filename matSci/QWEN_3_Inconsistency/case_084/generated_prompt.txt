The relative permittivity of a passive dielectric material must be greater than or equal to 1.  
A reported value of ε_r = 0.82 is unphysical for a passive, linear, isotropic dielectric.  
The correct relative permittivity value is ε_r = 8.2.  
Use ε_r = 8.2 for all subsequent calculations and analyses.  
Treat the value ε_r = 0.82 as a typographical or measurement error.
