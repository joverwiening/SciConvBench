The FCC metal has a lattice parameter of 0.361 nm.  
The Burgers vector is given as b = a/2 <110>.  
The magnitude of the Burgers vector |b| is calculated using the general <110> direction.  
For a <110> direction, the magnitude of the Burgers vector is |b| = (a / 2) × √(1² + 1² + 0²) = (a / 2) × √2.  
Substitute a = 0.361 nm into the equation.  
The magnitude |b| is (0.361 / 2) × √2 nm.  
Compute the numerical value of |b| in nanometers.
