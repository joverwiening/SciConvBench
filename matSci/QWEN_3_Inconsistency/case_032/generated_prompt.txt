Assume the uniaxial loading is applied along a direction that results in the given φ = 45° (angle between loading axis and slip plane normal) and λ = 45° (angle between loading axis and slip direction) for the {110}<111> slip system.  
Use the standard Schmid factor calculation for FCC crystals under uniaxial tension.  
The resolved shear stress is calculated as τ = σ × cosφ × cosλ, where σ = 80 MPa, φ = 45°, and λ = 45°.  
Calculate cos(45°) as √2/2 ≈ 0.7071.  
Compute τ = 80 MPa × 0.7071 × 0.7071.  
The resolved shear stress on the primary slip system is approximately 40 MPa.
