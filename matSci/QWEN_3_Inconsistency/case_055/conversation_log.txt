Q: What is the relationship or condition that defines consistency among the elastic constants E (Young's modulus), G (shear modulus), and ν (Poisson's ratio) for an isotropic material?
A: For an isotropic material, the elastic constants E, G, and ν are related by the equation:  
G = E / (2(1 + ν)).  

Consistency is verified if the given values satisfy this equation.
