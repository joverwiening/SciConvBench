Q: What is the formula or method to calculate the fiber weight fraction \( w_f \) using the given fiber volume fraction \( V_f \), fiber density \( \rho_f \), and matrix density \( \rho_m \)?
A: The fiber weight fraction \( w_f \) is calculated using the formula:

\[
w_f = \frac{V_f \rho_f}{V_f \rho_f + (1 - V_f) \rho_m}
\]

Substitute \( V_f = 0.60 \), \( \rho_f = 1.8 \, \text{g/cm}^3 \), and \( \rho_m = 1.2 \, \text{g/cm}^3 \) into the formula to determine \( w_f \).
