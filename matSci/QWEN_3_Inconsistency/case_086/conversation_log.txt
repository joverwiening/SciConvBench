Q: What is the expected state of a superconductor with a critical temperature (T_c) of 9.2 K when measured at 12 K?
A: The measurement temperature in the complete requirement is 4.2 K, not 12 K. At 4.2 K, which is below T_c = 9.2 K, the superconductor is in the zero-resistance superconducting state.
