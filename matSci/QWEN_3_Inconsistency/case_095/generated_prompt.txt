The theoretical maximum relative density for a sintered oxide material is 1.0.  
The reported relative density of the sintered oxide is 0.88.  
Total porosity is calculated as 1 minus the relative density.  
For a relative density of 0.88, the total porosity is 1 - 0.88 = 0.12 or 12%.  
The reported values of relative density (0.88) and total porosity (12%) are consistent and correct.
