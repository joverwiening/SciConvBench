The dry mass of the porous ceramic is 12.4 g.
The saturated mass is measured after the sample has been fully saturated with water and is weighed while still wet on the surface, yielding 13.1 g.
The immersed mass is measured with the sample fully submerged in water, yielding 13.6 g.
The mass ordering is physically inconsistent because the immersed mass cannot be greater than the saturated mass for a fully submerged sample in a Archimedes density measurement.
