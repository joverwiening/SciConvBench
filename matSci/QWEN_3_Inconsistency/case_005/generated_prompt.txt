Calculate the activation energy Q_d using the Arrhenius equation and the given diffusion coefficients and temperatures.  

Use the formula:  
ln(D2/D1) = (Q_d/R) * (1/T1 - 1/T2)  

Substitute the given values:  
D1 = 4.76×10^-18 m²/s, T1 = 866 K  
D2 = 1.86×10^-12 m²/s, T2 = 1438 K  
R = 8.314 J/mol·K  

Solve for Q_d in J/mol, then convert to kJ/mol.  

  
Use the Arrhenius relationship in logarithmic form: ln(D₂/D₁) = (Q_d/R) × (1/T₁ - 1/T₂).  
Substitute D₁ = 4.76×10⁻¹⁸ m²/s, D₂ = 1.86×10⁻¹² m²/s, T₁ = 866 K, T₂ = 1438 K.  
Use R = 8.314 J/mol·K.  
Compute ln(D₂/D₁) = ln((1.86×10⁻¹²)/(4.76×10⁻¹⁸)).  
Compute (1/T₁ - 1/T₂) = (1/866 - 1/1438).  
Multiply both sides by R and divide by (1/T₁ - 1/T₂) to solve for Q_d.  
Express Q_d in J/mol and then convert to kJ/mol by dividing by 1000.  
The activation energy Q_d is approximately 144.5 kJ/mol.
