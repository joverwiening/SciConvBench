Q: What is the formula or method to calculate Vickers hardness based on the applied load and indentation diagonal length?
A: The Vickers hardness (HV) is calculated using the formula:  
HV = 1.854 × P / d²  
where P is the applied load in kilograms-force (kgf), and d is the average diagonal length of the indentation in millimeters (mm).  
Use P = 0.5 kgf (since 500 g = 0.5 kgf) and d = 0.42 mm.
