Use a 136° diamond pyramid indenter for the Vickers hardness test.
Apply a load of 0.5 kgf.
Measure the average diagonal length of the indentation as 0.42 mm.
Calculate the Vickers hardness using the formula: HV = 1.854 × P / d².
Substitute P = 0.5 kgf and d = 0.42 mm into the formula.
Compute the result to determine the Vickers hardness value.
