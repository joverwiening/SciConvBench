Use the two given diffusion coefficients and temperatures to calculate the activation energy Q_d using the Arrhenius equation.  
Assume the pre-exponential factor D₀ is constant and unknown, but eliminate it by taking the ratio of the two diffusion equations.  
Use the gas constant R = 8.314 J/(mol·K).  
Solve for Q_d in J/mol.  
Convert Q_d to kJ/mol by dividing by 1000.  
  
Use the Arrhenius equation in the form: D = D₀ exp(-Q_d / (R T)).  
Take the natural logarithm of both sides for the two temperatures: ln(D₁) = ln(D₀) - Q_d / (R T₁) and ln(D₂) = ln(D₀) - Q_d / (R T₂).  
Subtract the two equations to eliminate ln(D₀): ln(D₁/D₂) = Q_d / R (1/T₂ - 1/T₁).  
Substitute D₁ = 1.0×10^-12 m²/s at T₁ = 800 K and D₂ = 2.0×10^-15 m²/s at T₂ = 1100 K.  
Use R = 8.314 J/(mol·K).  
Solve for Q_d: Q_d = R × ln(D₁/D₂) / (1/T₂ - 1/T₁).  
Calculate Q_d in J/mol and convert to kJ/mol by dividing by 1000.
