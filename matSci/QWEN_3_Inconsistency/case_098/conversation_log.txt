Q: What is the definition of a Frenkel defect in terms of the specific point defects involved?
A: A Frenkel defect involves a single ion (usually a cation) leaving its lattice site and occupying an interstitial site, creating a vacancy-interstitial pair of the same ion type. It does not involve both a cation vacancy and an anion vacancy.
