Q: What is the physical meaning of each variable in the thermal-shock parameter R ∝ σ_f k/(Eα)?
A: σ_f is the fracture strength, k is the thermal conductivity, E is the Young’s modulus, and α is the coefficient of thermal expansion.

=== 2026-07-26 00:12:26 ===
Q: Does increasing the coefficient of thermal expansion α actually improve thermal-shock resistance, or is there an error in the initial conclusion?
A: Increasing the coefficient of thermal expansion α does not improve thermal-shock resistance. Since α appears in the denominator of the thermal-shock parameter R ∝ σ_f k/(Eα), increasing α decreases R, which means thermal-shock resistance worsens. The initial conclusion that increasing α improves resistance is incorrect.
