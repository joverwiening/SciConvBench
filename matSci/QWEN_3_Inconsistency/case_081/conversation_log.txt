Q: What is the relationship between the absorption edge and the band gap in a semiconductor? Specifically, should the band gap be calculated directly from the absorption edge wavelength using the photon energy formula?
A: Yes, the band gap should be calculated directly from the absorption edge wavelength using the photon energy formula. The absorption edge corresponds to the minimum energy needed to excite an electron, which is the band gap energy.
