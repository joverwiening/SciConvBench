Use the lever rule to calculate the weight fraction of liquid phase.  
The overall composition of the alloy is 14.36 wt% B.  
The composition of the α phase at the eutectic temperature is 4.63 wt% B.  
The composition of the eutectic liquid at the eutectic temperature is 40.31 wt% B.  
The weight fraction of liquid is given by (C0 - C_αe) / (C_e - C_αe).  
Substitute the values: (14.36 - 4.63) / (40.31 - 4.63) = 9.73 / 35.68.  
Calculate the result to obtain the weight fraction of liquid.  
The weight fraction of liquid just above the eutectic temperature is approximately 0.2727.  
This liquid fraction will transform into the eutectic microconstituent upon cooling below the eutectic temperature.
