Q: What is the expected behavior of glass transition temperatures in a fully miscible binary polymer blend according to thermodynamic principles?
A: In a fully miscible binary polymer blend, thermodynamic principles predict a single, composition-dependent glass transition temperature (Tg) that is intermediate between the Tgs of the individual components. The observation of two distinct Tgs (315 K and 375 K) indicates phase separation and is inconsistent with full miscibility. Therefore, the blend is immiscible.
