Q: What is the formula or method you would like me to use to estimate the degree of crystallinity?
A: Use the density-based formula for degree of crystallinity:  
\( X_c = \frac{\rho_c (\rho - \rho_a)}{\rho (\rho_c - \rho_a)} \)  
where \( \rho \) is the measured density, \( \rho_a \) is the amorphous density, and \( \rho_c \) is the crystalline density.
