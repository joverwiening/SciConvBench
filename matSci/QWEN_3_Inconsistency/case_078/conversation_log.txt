Q: What is the relationship between the sign of the Hall coefficient and the carrier type in a material?
A: The sign of the Hall coefficient indicates the carrier type: a positive sign corresponds to holes (p-type), and a negative sign corresponds to electrons (n-type). In this case, since the Hall coefficient is positive (+4.0×10⁻⁴ m³/C), the majority carriers are holes.
