Q: What is the correct definition of a Schottky defect in an ionic crystal?
A: A Schottky defect in an ionic crystal consists of a pair of vacancies—one cation vacancy and one anion vacancy—maintaining charge neutrality. The description of a cation vacancy and a nearby cation interstitial does not match a Schottky defect; this is more characteristic of a Frenkel defect.
