The applied tensile stress is 100 MPa.  
The angle between the tensile axis and the slip plane normal (φ) is 30°.  
The angle between the tensile axis and the slip direction (λ) is 45°.  
The Schmid factor is calculated as cos(φ) × cos(λ).  
cos(30°) is approximately 0.866.  
cos(45°) is approximately 0.707.  
The Schmid factor is 0.866 × 0.707 = 0.612.  
The stated maximum Schmid factor is 0.5, which does not match the calculated value.  
Using the stated Schmid factor of 0.5 for consistency with the problem.  
The resolved shear stress is calculated as σ × cos(φ) × cos(λ).  
The resolved shear stress is 100 MPa × 0.5 = 50 MPa.
