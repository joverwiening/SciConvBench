Q: What is the relationship between the standard Gibbs free energy change (ΔG°) and the equilibrium constant (K) that should be used for this analysis?
A: Use the equation ΔG° = -RT ln K, where R is the gas constant (8.314 J/mol·K), T is temperature in Kelvin (298 K), and K is the equilibrium constant. A negative ΔG° means K > 1; a positive ΔG° means K < 1. Here, ΔG° = -12 kJ/mol, so K should be greater than 1.
