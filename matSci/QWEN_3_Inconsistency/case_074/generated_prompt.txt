Use the rule of mixtures to calculate the longitudinal modulus (E_L).  
Multiply the fiber modulus (E_f) by the fiber volume fraction (V_f): 230 GPa × 0.60.  
Multiply the matrix modulus (E_m) by the matrix volume fraction (1 - V_f): 3 GPa × (1 - 0.60).  
Sum the two results to obtain E_L.  
The longitudinal modulus E_L is 139.2 GPa.
