The user is asking to determine the correct trend for the reduction potential of a Cu²⁺/Cu electrode when the activity of Cu²⁺ is lowered from 1 to 10⁻².
The given Nernst equation is E = E° + (0.0592/2) log a_(Cu²⁺).
For a reduction reaction (Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu), a decrease in reactant activity (a_(Cu²⁺)) makes the log term more negative.
Adding a negative number to E° makes the reduction potential E less positive (more negative).
Therefore, lowering a_(Cu²⁺) from 1 to 10⁻² makes the reduction potential more negative, not more positive.
The correct trend is that the reduction potential becomes more negative.
