The alloy composition is 40 wt% B.
The α+β tie line at the given temperature spans from C_α = 25 wt% B to C_β = 70 wt% B.
The lever rule is applied to the tie line.
The fraction of the β phase, W_β, is calculated as (C_0 - C_α) / (C_β - C_α).
Substitute the values: W_β = (40 - 25) / (70 - 25).
Calculate W_β = 15 / 45.
The result is W_β = 1/3 or approximately 0.333.
The fraction of the α phase, W_α, is calculated as (C_β - C_0) / (C_β - C_α).
Substitute the values: W_α = (70 - 40) / (70 - 25).
Calculate W_α = 30 / 45.
The result is W_α = 2/3 or approximately 0.667.
The sum of the phase fractions, W_α + W_β, equals 1.
