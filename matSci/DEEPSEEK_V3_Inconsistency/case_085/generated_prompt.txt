The paramagnet obeys the Curie law, χ = C/T, where χ is the magnetic susceptibility, C is the Curie constant, and T is the absolute temperature.
According to the Curie law, susceptibility is inversely proportional to temperature.
Therefore, as temperature increases from 300 K to 600 K, the susceptibility χ decreases.
The statement that susceptibility increases as temperature rises from 300 K to 600 K is incorrect for a paramagnet obeying χ = C/T.
The correct trend is that susceptibility decreases with increasing temperature.
