The standard Gibbs free-energy change ΔG° is -12 kJ/mol.
The equilibrium constant K is stated to be 0.008.
The relationship ΔG° = -RT ln(K) is used.
For a negative ΔG°, the term -RT ln(K) must also be negative, requiring ln(K) to be positive.
A positive ln(K) means K must be greater than 1.
The stated K of 0.008 is less than 1, which contradicts the calculated requirement.
Therefore, based on the given ΔG°, K should be greater than 1.
