Assume the crystal structure is FCC.
Assume the slip system is the primary {111}<110> system.
Assume the tensile axis is oriented such that the angle between the tensile axis and the slip plane normal (φ) is 30° and the angle between the tensile axis and the slip direction (λ) is 45°.
Use the Schmid's Law formula: τ = σ * cos(φ) * cos(λ).
Calculate cos(30°) as approximately 0.8660.
Calculate cos(45°) as approximately 0.7071.
Multiply the applied tensile stress (σ = 100 MPa) by 0.8660 and then by 0.7071.
The resolved shear stress (τ) is approximately 61.2 MPa.
