The reported values are contradictory.
Relative density is defined as the bulk density of the material divided by the true density of the solid phase.
Relative density is a dimensionless ratio and must be less than or equal to 1.
A reported relative density of 1.08 is greater than 1, which is physically impossible.
Total porosity is defined as the volume fraction of pores in the material.
For a single-phase material, total porosity plus relative density must equal 1.
Given a relative density of 1.08, the calculated total porosity would be -0.08 or -8%, which is also physically impossible.
Therefore, the reported values cannot both be correct.
