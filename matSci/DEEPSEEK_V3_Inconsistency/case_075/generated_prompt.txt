Assume a fiber volume fraction (V_f) of 0.6.
Assume a matrix volume fraction (V_m) of 0.4.
Assume a fiber modulus (E_f) of 85 GPa.
Assume a matrix modulus (E_m) of 3.5 GPa.
Calculate the transverse modulus (E_T) using the rule of mixtures: E_T = V_f * E_f + V_m * E_m.
The calculated value of E_T is 51.4 GPa.
