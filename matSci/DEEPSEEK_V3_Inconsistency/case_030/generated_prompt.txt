The atomic packing factor (APF) is defined as the fraction of volume in a crystal structure that is occupied by atoms.
For a body-centered cubic (BCC) structure, the theoretical atomic packing factor is approximately 0.68.
The user reports an atomic packing factor of 0.74 for a metal identified as BCC.
The fraction of empty space in a crystal is equal to 1 minus the atomic packing factor.
Calculate the empty space fraction: 1 - 0.74 = 0.26.
