The Vickers hardness test uses a 136° diamond pyramid indenter.
The applied load is 500 grams-force.
The measured indentation diagonal is 0.42 mm.
Calculate the Vickers hardness number (HV) using the standard formula HV = 1.8544 * (Load in kgf) / (d^2), where d is the diagonal length in mm.
Convert the 500 g load to 0.5 kgf.
Perform the calculation: HV = 1.8544 * 0.5 / (0.42^2).
