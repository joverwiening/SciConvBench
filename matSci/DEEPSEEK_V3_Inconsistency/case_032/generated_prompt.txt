Assume the uniaxial loading direction is along the [001] crystal axis.
Calculate the Schmid factor for the primary slip system {110}<111>.
The Schmid factor is given by cos(φ) * cos(λ), where φ = 45° and λ = 45°.
cos(45°) = √2/2 ≈ 0.7071.
The Schmid factor is therefore 0.7071 * 0.7071 = 0.5.
The resolved shear stress is the product of the applied uniaxial stress (80 MPa) and the Schmid factor.
The resolved shear stress is 80 MPa * 0.5 = 40 MPa.
