The engineering stress is calculated as load divided by final cross-sectional area.
The load P is 42 kN.
The final cross-sectional area A_f is 115 mm^2.
Convert the load to consistent units: 42 kN = 42000 N.
Convert the area to consistent units: 115 mm^2 = 115 * 10^-6 m^2 = 0.000115 m^2.
Calculate the engineering stress: 42000 N / 0.000115 m^2 = 365217391.3 Pa.
Express the engineering stress in a more conventional unit: 365.2 MPa.
