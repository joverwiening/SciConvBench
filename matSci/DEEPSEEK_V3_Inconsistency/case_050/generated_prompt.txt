Assume the first metal is Copper (Cu).
Assume the second metal is Nickel (Ni).
Assume the atomic radius of Copper is 0.128 nm.
Assume the atomic radius of Nickel is 0.125 nm.
Assume both metals have the same Face-Centered Cubic (FCC) crystal structure.
The Hume-Rothery rule states that for complete solid solubility, the atomic size difference should be less than 15%.
The given atomic radii difference between Copper and Nickel is approximately 2.4%, which is within the 15% limit.
The scenario posits a 25% difference, which exceeds the Hume-Rothery size factor limit.
Assess the expectation by stating that complete solid solubility is not predicted based on the size factor alone for a 25% difference.
The assessment must note that other Hume-Rothery rules (valence, electronegativity, crystal structure) could, in principle, be favorable, but the size rule violation is primary.
The final conclusion is that the expectation of complete solid solubility is incorrect if based solely on a 25% size difference.
