Assume the β phase composition at the eutectic temperature is 100 wt% B.
Use the lever rule to calculate the weight fraction of liquid just above the eutectic temperature.
Apply the lever rule between the α phase composition (C_αe = 4.63 wt% B) and the eutectic liquid composition (C_e = 40.31 wt% B).
The overall composition (C0 = 14.36 wt% B) is the fulcrum.
The weight fraction of liquid, W_L, is given by (C0 - C_αe) / (C_e - C_αe).
Substitute the values: (14.36 - 4.63) / (40.31 - 4.63).
Calculate the result: (9.73) / (35.68) ≈ 0.2727.
The weight fraction of the alloy that is liquid just above the eutectic temperature is approximately 0.273 or 27.3%.
This liquid fraction will transform into the eutectic microconstituent upon cooling.
