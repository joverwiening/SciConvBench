The user describes a scenario where copper is electroplated from Cu^2+ ions.
Faraday's law is applied with n = 1 electron per Cu atom to compute deposited mass.
A current of 2.0 A is applied for 30 minutes.
The task is to determine the correct electron number (n) for this reaction.
The correct electron number for the reduction of Cu^2+ to Cu(s) is 2 electrons per copper atom.
The specification is to use n = 2 in the calculation.
