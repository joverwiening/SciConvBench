Assume a total sample length of 10 mm, with the initial interface at the midpoint (x = 0 mm).
Assume the material system is a generic binary metallic alloy (e.g., Cu-Ni).
Assume the diffusion coefficient D is 1e-12 m²/s.
Assume the annealing temperature is 1000 K.
Assume the annealing time is 100 hours (360,000 seconds).
Assume the concentration profile follows the error function solution to Fick's second law for a diffusion couple.
The initial left composition is 20 at% B, and the initial right composition is 80 at% B.
The profile after annealing spans from 10 at% B to 90 at% B.
Define the profile width as the distance between the points where the concentration reaches 20 at% B and 80 at% B.
Calculate the interdiffusion distance from this profile width using the error function solution.
The interdiffusion distance is estimated as the diffusion length, proportional to the square root of the product of the diffusion coefficient and time.
