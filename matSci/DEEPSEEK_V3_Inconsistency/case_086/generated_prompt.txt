The superconductor has a critical temperature (T_c) of 9.2 K.
The measurement is taken at a temperature of 12 K.
Since the measurement temperature (12 K) is higher than the critical temperature (9.2 K), the superconductor is not in its superconducting state.
The material is in the normal, resistive state at 12 K.
The description of it being in the zero-resistance superconducting state at 12 K is incorrect.
