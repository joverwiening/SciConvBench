The task is to estimate the reduction in thermal conductivity for a porous ceramic using a linear model.
The fully dense material (0% porosity) has a thermal conductivity of 2.0 W/m·K.
The material at 30% porosity has a thermal conductivity of 3.5 W/m·K.
Apply a linear relationship between porosity and thermal conductivity across the range of 0% to 30% porosity.
Calculate the slope of the conductivity-porosity line using the two given data points.
Report the estimated conductivity reduction per unit increase in porosity.
