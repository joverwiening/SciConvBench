The photon energy is calculated using E = hc/λ.
The work function of the coated metal is 4.8 eV.
The incident light has a wavelength of 300 nm.
The maximum kinetic energy of the emitted photoelectrons is stated to be 0.8 eV.
Emission should occur if the photon energy is greater than the work function.
The photon energy is approximately 4.133 eV.
Since 4.133 eV is less than the work function of 4.8 eV, emission should not occur.
The stated maximum kinetic energy of 0.8 eV is therefore contradictory.
The correct conclusion is that emission should not occur.
