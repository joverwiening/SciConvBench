The incident X-rays have a wavelength of 0.1172 nm.
The crystal is cubic with a lattice parameter of 0.4045 nm.
The reflection is from the (311) crystallographic planes.
Use the order of reflection, n, equal to 2 in Bragg's law.
Calculate the Bragg angle, θ, for this reflection.
Calculate the diffraction angle, 2θ, in degrees.
The interplanar spacing d for the (311) planes in a cubic crystal is a / sqrt(h^2 + k^2 + l^2).
Substitute h=3, k=1, l=1, and a=0.4045 nm to find d.
Apply Bragg's law: nλ = 2d sin(θ).
Solve for sin(θ) and then for θ.
Multiply θ by 2 to find the diffraction angle 2θ.
