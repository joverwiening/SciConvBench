Assume the molecular weight of each polymer is 50,000 g/mol.
Assume the blend composition is 50/50 by weight.
The blend is described as fully miscible.
Differential Scanning Calorimetry (DSC) shows two distinct glass transition temperatures (Tg) at 315 K and 375 K.
A single, composition-dependent Tg is expected for a miscible blend.
The observation of two distinct Tgs indicates phase separation.
Therefore, assess the blend as immiscible based on the DSC data.
The initial description of full miscibility is incorrect or refers to a different condition.
