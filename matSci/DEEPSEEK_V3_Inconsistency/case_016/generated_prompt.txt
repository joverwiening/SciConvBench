The theoretical density of the ceramic crystal is calculated using the formula ρ = ZM/(N_A a^3).
The number of formula units per unit cell, Z, is 4.
The molar mass of one formula unit, M, is 139.63 g/mol.
Avogadro's number, N_A, is 6.022×10^23 mol^-1.
The lattice parameter, a, is 0.6067 nm.
Convert the lattice parameter from nanometers to centimeters: a = 0.6067 × 10^-7 cm.
Calculate a^3: (0.6067 × 10^-7 cm)^3 = 2.233 × 10^-22 cm^3.
Calculate the numerator: Z * M = 4 * 139.63 g/mol = 558.52 g/mol.
Calculate the denominator: N_A * a^3 = (6.022×10^23 mol^-1) * (2.233 × 10^-22 cm^3) = 134.5 cm^3/mol.
Calculate ρ: 558.52 g/mol / 134.5 cm^3/mol = 4.153 g/cm^3.
The theoretical density is 4.153 g/cm^3.
