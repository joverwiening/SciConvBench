The reported static water contact angle of 195° is physically impossible, as the maximum possible contact angle on any smooth, homogeneous surface is 180°.
A contact angle greater than 180° would indicate the water droplet is not resting on the surface but is instead a floating sphere, which is not a valid measurement for wettability.
Therefore, the reported value of 195° is erroneous and cannot be used to comment on wettability.
For a valid assessment, a correct measurement of the static water contact angle must be obtained.
