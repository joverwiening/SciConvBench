Use the Avrami equation X = 1 − exp(−k t^n).
Rearrange to exp(−k t^n) = 1 − X.
Take natural log to get −k t^n = ln(1 − X).
Solve for time as t = [−ln(1 − X)/k]^(1/n).
Substitute X = 0.274, k = 7.83×10^-3 s^-n, n = 2.81.
Compute t = [−ln(0.726)/(7.83×10^-3)]^(1/2.81) = 3.56 s.
Report the required holding time as 3.56 s.
