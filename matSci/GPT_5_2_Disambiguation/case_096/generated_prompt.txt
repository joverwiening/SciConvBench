Use the relation H_c(T) = H_c(0)[1 − (T/T_c)^2].
Set T = 4.2 K, H_c(0) = 0.080 T, and T_c = 7.20 K.
Compute (T/T_c) = 4.2/7.20 = 0.5833.
Square it to get (T/T_c)^2 = 0.3403.
Compute the bracket term 1 − (T/T_c)^2 = 0.6597.
Compute H_c(4.2 K) = 0.080 T × 0.6597 = 0.0528 T.
Report the critical field at 4.2 K as H_c ≈ 5.28 × 10^−2 T (≈ 52.8 mT).
