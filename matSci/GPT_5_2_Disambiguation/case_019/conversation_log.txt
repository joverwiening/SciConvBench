Q: Which critical fiber length formula should I use (e.g., \(l_c=\frac{\sigma_f d}{2\tau_c}\))?
A: Use the formula:

\[
l_c=\frac{\sigma_f\, d}{2\tau_c}
\]

where \(d\) is the fiber diameter.
