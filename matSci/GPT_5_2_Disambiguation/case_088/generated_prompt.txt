Assume x = 1 mol Li exchanged per mol FePO₄, corresponding to 1 mol e⁻ per mol FePO₄.
Use the theoretical capacity formula Q = (x·F)/(3.6·M), where F = 96485 C/mol e⁻ and M is the molar mass in g/mol.
Compute the molar mass of FePO₄ as M = 55.845 + 30.974 + 4·15.999 = 150.815 g/mol.
Compute the theoretical specific capacity as Q = (1·96485)/(3.6·150.815) = 177.8 mAh/g.
Report the estimate as approximately 178 mAh/g (based on FePO₄ active material mass).
