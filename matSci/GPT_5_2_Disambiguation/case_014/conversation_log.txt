Q: Should the crystallinity be reported as a mass fraction (weight percent) based on densities?
A: Report it as **percent crystallinity (X₍c₎ × 100)** using the given density-based formula:
\[
X_c=\frac{\rho_c(\rho_s-\rho_a)}{\rho_s(\rho_c-\rho_a)}\times 100
\]
So it should be a **percentage**, not left as a raw fraction.
