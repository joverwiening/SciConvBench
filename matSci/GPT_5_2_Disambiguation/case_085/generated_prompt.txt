Compute corrosion penetration rate (CPR) for a steel coupon from mass loss.
Use mass loss W = 0.084 g.
Use exposed surface area A = 12.0 cm².
Use exposure time t = 240 hours.
Use steel density ρ = 7.87 g/cm³.
Compute thickness loss in cm as Δx = W / (ρ·A).
Convert thickness loss to mm as Δx_mm = 10·Δx.
Convert exposure time to years as t_y = 240 / 8760.
Compute corrosion penetration rate in mm/y as CPR = Δx_mm / t_y.
Report the final CPR value numerically with appropriate significant figures.
