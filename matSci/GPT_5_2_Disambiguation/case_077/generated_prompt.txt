Use the lever rule at just below the eutectoid temperature, where the microconstituents are proeutectoid ferrite and pearlite.
Treat pearlite as having the eutectoid composition Cₑ = 0.76 wt% C.
Treat proeutectoid ferrite as having composition Cα = 0.022 wt% C.
Use the alloy composition C0 = 0.60 wt% C.
Compute mass fraction of proeutectoid ferrite as Wα(proeutectoid) = (Cₑ − C0) / (Cₑ − Cα) = (0.76 − 0.60) / (0.76 − 0.022) = 0.16 / 0.738 = 0.217 (≈ 0.22).
Compute mass fraction of pearlite as Wpearlite = 1 − Wα(proeutectoid) = 1 − 0.217 = 0.783 (≈ 0.78).
