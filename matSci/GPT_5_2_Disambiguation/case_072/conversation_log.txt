Q: What carburizing boundary condition should be assumed at the surface: constant surface concentration at 0.95 wt% C for all times (semi-infinite solid, error-function solution)?
A: Yes—assume a **semi-infinite solid** with a **constant surface concentration boundary condition**:  
- \( C_s = 0.95 \,\text{wt\% C} \) held constant for all \( t>0 \)  
- Initial uniform concentration \( C_0 = 0.20 \,\text{wt\% C} \)  
- Use the **error-function (erf) solution** for constant surface concentration.
