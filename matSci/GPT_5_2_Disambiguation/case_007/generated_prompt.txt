Treat the alloy as hypoeutectic so that just above the eutectic temperature the phases present are α + liquid only.
Use the lever rule on the α–liquid tie line at the eutectic temperature.
Use C0 = 14.36%B as the overall composition.
Use Cαe = 4.63%B as the α-phase composition at the eutectic temperature.
Use Ce = 40.31%B as the liquid composition at the eutectic temperature.
Compute the liquid fraction just above the eutectic temperature as fL = (C0 − Cαe) / (Ce − Cαe).
Report fL numerically and interpret it as the fraction of the alloy that will transform into eutectic microconstituent upon cooling through the eutectic temperature.
