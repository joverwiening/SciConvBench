Q: What is the metal’s atomic number density (atoms per cubic meter)?
A: Atomic number density:
\[
n=\frac{\rho}{A}N_A
\]
with \(\rho=7.87\,\text{g/cm}^3=7.87\times10^6\,\text{g/m}^3\), \(A=55.85\,\text{g/mol}\), \(N_A=6.022\times10^{23}\,\text{mol}^{-1}\).

\[
n=\frac{7.87\times10^6}{55.85}\times 6.022\times10^{23}\approx 8.49\times10^{28}\ \text{atoms/m}^3
\]

Q: What value should I use for the Bohr magneton μB in A·m² (J/T)?
A: Use the standard Bohr magneton value:
\[
\mu_B = 9.274\times 10^{-24}\ \text{A·m}^2 \;(\text{J/T})
\]
(You can use \(9.274009994\times10^{-24}\) A·m² for higher precision.)
