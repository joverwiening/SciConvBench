Compute the composite axial coefficient of thermal expansion α1 for a continuous unidirectional fiber composite using the axial isostrain (Voigt) stiffness-weighted rule of mixtures.
Use fiber properties αf = -0.5×10^-6 K^-1 and Ef = 230 GPa.
Use matrix properties αm = 55×10^-6 K^-1 and Em = 3.5 GPa.
Use fiber volume fraction Vf = 0.60 and matrix volume fraction Vm = 0.40.
Assume perfect bonding and uniform axial strain in fiber and matrix (isostrain condition).
Compute the axial modulus by Voigt: E1 = Ef·Vf + Em·Vm.
Compute the axial CTE by stiffness-weighted Voigt: α1 = (Ef·Vf·αf + Em·Vm·αm) / (Ef·Vf + Em·Vm).
Report α1 in K^-1 (and optionally in ×10^-6 K^-1).
