Q: Assuming a central through-crack in an infinite plate (Griffith: σ = sqrt((2Eγs)/(πa))), should the “critical flaw size” be reported as the half-crack length a or the full crack length 2a?
A: Report it as the **half-crack length parameter \(a\)** (not the full crack length \(2a\)). The complete requirement explicitly asks for “the critical flaw size parameter \(a\) in μm” using \(\sigma_f=\sqrt{\frac{2E\gamma_s}{\pi a}}\).
