Compute the fraction of eutectic microconstituent just below the eutectic temperature for a hypoeutectic binary alloy with overall composition C0 = 30 wt% B.
Use the eutectic composition CE = 40 wt% B.
Use the α-phase composition at the eutectic temperature CαE = 12 wt% B.
Assume the eutectic microconstituent fraction equals the fraction of liquid present just above the eutectic temperature.
Apply the lever rule on the α + L tie line at TE: f_eutectic = f_L = (C0 − CαE) / (CE − CαE).
Calculate f_eutectic = (30 − 12) / (40 − 12) = 18/28 = 0.6429.
Report the eutectic microconstituent fraction as 0.643 (about 64.3%).
