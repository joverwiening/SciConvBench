Use the Hall relation V_H = (I B) / (n q t).
Take I = 15 mA, B = 0.80 T, V_H = 24 mV, t = 5.0 × 10^-4 m, q = 1.602 × 10^-19 C.
Solve for n: n = (I B) / (q t V_H).
Compute n = (0.015 × 0.80) / (1.602 × 10^-19 × 5.0 × 10^-4 × 0.024) = 6.24 × 10^21 m^-3.
Report the carrier concentration as n ≈ 6.2 × 10^21 m^-3 (≈ 6.2 × 10^15 cm^-3).
