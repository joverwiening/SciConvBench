Use Vegard’s law for a substitutional binary alloy: a = x_A a_A + x_B a_B with x_A + x_B = 1.
Rewrite as a = (1 − x_B) a_A + x_B a_B.
Solve for x_B: x_B = (a − a_A) / (a_B − a_A).
Substitute values: x_B = (0.389 − 0.404) / (0.361 − 0.404) = (−0.015) / (−0.043) ≈ 0.349.
Report the estimated atomic fraction of component B as x_B ≈ 0.35 (about 35 at.% B).
