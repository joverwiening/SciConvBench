Compute the steady 1-D conduction heat-transfer rate through a two-layer composite wall with layers in series.
Use layer 1 thickness L1 = 16.79 mm and thermal conductivity k1 = 24.68 W/m·K.
Use layer 2 thickness L2 = 8.73 mm and thermal conductivity k2 = 94.15 W/m·K.
Use wall area A = 0.2423 m².
Use surface temperatures T_hot = 134.0 °C and T_cold = -26.8 °C.
Assume perfect thermal contact between layers and neglect contact resistance, convection, and radiation.
Convert thicknesses to meters before calculations.
Compute total thermal resistance R_total = L1/(k1·A) + L2/(k2·A).
Compute temperature difference ΔT = T_hot − T_cold.
Compute heat-transfer rate q = ΔT / R_total.
Report q in watts (W).
