Q: Which definition of crystallinity should I use: weight fraction (mass crystallinity) or volume fraction?
A: Use the **percent crystallinity \(X_c\)** given by the provided **density-based formula**:

\[
X_c=\frac{\rho_c(\rho_s-\rho_a)}{\rho_s(\rho_c-\rho_a)}\times 100
\]

So, use **\(X_c\) as defined by that equation** (the density-method crystallinity), not a separate choice between weight- or volume-fraction definitions.
