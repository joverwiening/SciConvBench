Assume spherical particles with density ρ = 6.2 g/cm^3 and average diameter d = 0.80 μm.
Use the specific surface area relation for monodisperse spheres: SSA = 6/(ρ d), where d is in cm and SSA is in cm^2/g.
Convert diameter to cm: d = 0.80 μm = 0.80×10^-4 cm = 8.0×10^-5 cm.
Compute SSA: SSA = 6 / (6.2 × 8.0×10^-5) = 1.2097×10^4 cm^2/g.
Convert to m^2/g using 1 m^2/g = 10^4 cm^2/g: SSA = 1.2097 m^2/g.
Report the estimate as SSA ≈ 1.21 m^2/g.
