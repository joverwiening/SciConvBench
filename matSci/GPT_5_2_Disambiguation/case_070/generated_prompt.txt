Assume the film is perfectly bonded to a much thicker rigid substrate so the in-plane strain of the film follows the substrate.
Compute the thermal mismatch strain as ε_m = (α_s − α_f)ΔT.
Use α_f = 14×10⁻⁶ K⁻¹, α_s = 6×10⁻⁶ K⁻¹, and ΔT = −250 K to get ε_m = (6−14)×10⁻⁶×(−250) = +0.002.
Compute the biaxial thermal stress as σ = (E_f/(1−ν_f)) ε_m.
Use E_f/(1−ν_f) = 222 GPa to get σ = 222 GPa × 0.002 = 0.444 GPa.
Report the estimated biaxial thermal stress as approximately +0.44 GPa (tensile in the film for the given sign convention).
