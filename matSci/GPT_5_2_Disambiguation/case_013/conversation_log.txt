Q: In the distribution “21640 g/mol (93), …”, what do the numbers in parentheses represent (e.g., number of molecules/chains, mass/weight fraction, or something else)?
A: They represent the number of polymer chains (counts) at that molecular weight: e.g., \(N_1 = 93\) chains with \(M_1 = 21640\) g/mol, \(N_2 = 40\), \(N_3 = 170\), \(N_4 = 48\).
