Compute the steady 1D diffusive molar flux through a planar ceramic membrane of thickness L = 1.5 mm.
Use constant diffusivity D = 2.2×10^-11 m^2/s.
Use boundary concentrations C1 = 0.12 mol/m^3 at x = 0 and C2 = 0.02 mol/m^3 at x = L.
Assume a linear concentration profile so dC/dx = (C2 − C1)/L.
Apply Fick’s first law J = −D(dC/dx).
Report J in mol/(m^2·s) with sign indicating direction from high to low concentration.
Calculate J = −(2.2×10^-11)×((0.02 − 0.12)/0.0015) = +1.47×10^-9 mol/(m^2·s), directed from the 0.12 mol/m^3 side toward the 0.02 mol/m^3 side.
