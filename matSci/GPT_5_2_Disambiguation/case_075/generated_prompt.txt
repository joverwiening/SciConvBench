Compute fatigue cycles by integrating Paris law: N = ∫(a_i→a_f) da / (C(ΔK(a))^m).

Evaluate the integral analytically as N = [1/(C (Y Δσ)^m π^(m/2))] · [(a^(1−m/2))/(1−m/2)] from a_i to a_f.
