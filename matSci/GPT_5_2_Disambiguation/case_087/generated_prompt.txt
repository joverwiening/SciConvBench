Compute the work of adhesion between water and the treated polymer using the Young–Dupré equation Wadh = γLV(1 + cos θ).
Use θ = 72° as the equilibrium contact angle measured through the water phase.
Use γLV = 0.0728 N/m (72.8 mN/m) at 20 °C.
Calculate cos(72°) ≈ 0.3090.
Compute Wadh = 0.0728 × (1 + 0.3090) N/m = 0.0728 × 1.3090 N/m ≈ 0.0953 N/m.
Report the work of adhesion as Wadh ≈ 0.0953 J/m² (since 1 N/m = 1 J/m²).
