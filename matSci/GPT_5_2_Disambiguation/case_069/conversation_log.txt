Q: What coefficients of thermal expansion (CTE) should I use for the aluminum film and the ceramic substrate?
A: Use:
- Aluminum film CTE: \( \alpha_f = 23 \times 10^{-6}\ \text{K}^{-1} \)
- Ceramic substrate CTE: \( \alpha_s = 8 \times 10^{-6}\ \text{K}^{-1} \)

Q: What assumption should I use for the film’s in-plane constraint: fully constrained to match the substrate (so mismatch strain = (αf − αs)ΔT), or free-standing (so strain = αfΔT)?
A: Assume the film is fully constrained in-plane by the bonded ceramic substrate, so use the mismatch strain:

\[
\varepsilon_{\text{mismatch}} = (\alpha_f - \alpha_s)\,\Delta T
\]
