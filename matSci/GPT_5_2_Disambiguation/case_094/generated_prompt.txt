Use the rubber elasticity relation for shear modulus: G = ν_e R T.
Convert ν_e = 4.5×10^-5 mol/cm^3 to mol/m^3 using 1 cm^3 = 1×10^-6 m^3, giving ν_e = 45 mol/m^3.
Use R = 8.314 J/(mol·K) and T = 298 K.
Compute G = 45×8.314×298 Pa ≈ 1.115×10^5 Pa.
Assume an incompressible elastomer with Poisson’s ratio ν ≈ 0.5 so that E = 3G.
Compute Young’s modulus E ≈ 3×1.115×10^5 Pa ≈ 3.35×10^5 Pa.
Report the estimate as E ≈ 0.335 MPa (≈ 3.35×10^5 Pa).
