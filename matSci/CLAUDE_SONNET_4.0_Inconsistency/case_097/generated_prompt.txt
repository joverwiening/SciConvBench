The described defect is a Frenkel defect, not a Schottky defect.
A Frenkel defect consists of one cation vacancy and one nearby cation interstitial.
A Schottky defect consists of equal numbers of cation and anion vacancies with no interstitials.
The defect type should be identified as a Frenkel defect.
This correction applies to ionic crystals in general, regardless of specific structure type.
