Rearrange the equation to isolate t: 0.274 = 1 - exp(-k t^n), so exp(-k t^n) = 0.726.
Take the natural logarithm of both sides: -k t^n = ln(0.726).
Solve for t: t^n = -ln(0.726)/k, then t = [-ln(0.726)/k]^(1/n).
