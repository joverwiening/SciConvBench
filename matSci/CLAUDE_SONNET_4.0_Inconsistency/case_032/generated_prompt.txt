Calculate the resolved shear stress for an FCC crystal under the following conditions.
The crystal has primary slip on the {110}<111> slip system.
Apply uniaxial loading of 80 MPa along the [001] crystallographic direction.
Use φ = 45° as the angle between the loading direction and the slip plane normal.
Use λ = 45° as the angle between the loading direction and the slip direction.
Calculate the resolved shear stress using the Schmid factor formula: τ = σ × cos(φ) × cos(λ).
The resolved shear stress equals 80 MPa × cos(45°) × cos(45°) = 40 MPa.
