Calculate Young's modulus for a cylindrical tensile specimen using the following parameters and method.
The specimen has an original diameter of 18.16 mm.
The specimen has a gauge length of 37.69 mm.
A load of 18.90 kN is applied in the elastic regime.
The gauge length increases by 0.0206 mm under this load.
Use the standard circle area formula A_0 = πd²/4 to calculate the original cross-sectional area.
Calculate the engineering stress as σ = F/A_0 where F is the applied load.
Calculate the engineering strain as ε = ΔL/L_0 where ΔL is the change in gauge length and L_0 is the original gauge length.
Apply linear elasticity assumption with Young's modulus E = σ/ε.
Express the final result in GPa.
