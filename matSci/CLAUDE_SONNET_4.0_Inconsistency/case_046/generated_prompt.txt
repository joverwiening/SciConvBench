Determine the primary phase that forms when cooling a binary eutectic alloy with overall composition C_0 = 28 wt% B.
The eutectic composition is C_e = 42 wt% B.
The alloy is described as hypereutectic.
Assume that at the eutectic temperature, the α phase is nearly pure A (approximately 0 wt% B) and the β phase is nearly pure B (approximately 100 wt% B).
Compare the overall composition C_0 to the eutectic composition C_e to determine which primary phase forms.
If C_0 < C_e, primary α forms during cooling.
If C_0 > C_e, primary β forms during cooling.
Apply this comparison to determine whether primary α or primary β forms for the given alloy composition.
