The simulation models a standard three-point bending test of a rectangular bar.
The bar has dimensions: length L = 40 mm, width b = 4 mm, and thickness d = 3 mm.
The test uses two simple supports positioned at the ends of the 40 mm span.
A concentrated load P = 120 N is applied at the center of the span (20 mm from each support).
The load is applied perpendicular to the top surface of the bar.
The supports allow rotation but prevent vertical displacement.
The simulation calculates the maximum flexural stress using the given formula σ_f = 3PL/(4bd^2).
The simulation compares this calculated stress with the theoretical maximum flexural stress for three-point bending.
The theoretical formula for maximum flexural stress in three-point bending is σ_max = 3PL/(2bd^2).
The simulation determines whether the given formula σ_f = 3PL/(4bd^2) matches the correct theoretical formula.
The simulation reports the discrepancy between the given formula and the standard three-point bending formula.
