Calculate the elastic modulus E of an elastomer using the formula E ≈ 3ν_eRT.
Use crosslink density ν_e = 4.0×10^-5 mol/cm^3.
Use temperature T = 298 K (converted from 298°C).
Use gas constant R = 8.314 J/(mol·K).
Substitute the values into E = 3 × (4.0×10^-5 mol/cm^3) × (8.314 J/(mol·K)) × (298 K).
Perform the multiplication to obtain the modulus value.
Express the final result in appropriate units (Pa or N/m²).
