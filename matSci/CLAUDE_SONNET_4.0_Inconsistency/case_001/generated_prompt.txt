Calculate the density using the formula ρ = nA/(N_A a^3) where n is the number of atoms per unit cell for BCC structure.
Use n = 2 atoms per unit cell since the metal has a BCC structure.
Use A = 129.68 g/mol as the atomic weight.
Use N_A = 6.022×10^23 mol^-1 as Avogadro's number.
Use ρ = 14.368 g/cm^3 as the theoretical density.
Rearrange the density formula to solve for the lattice parameter: a^3 = nA/(N_A ρ).
Substitute the values: a^3 = (2 × 129.68)/(6.022×10^23 × 14.368).
Calculate a^3 and then take the cube root to find the lattice parameter a.
Convert the final lattice parameter from cm to nm by multiplying by 10^7.
