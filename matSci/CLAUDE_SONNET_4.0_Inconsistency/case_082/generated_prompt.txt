For a material with a direct allowed band gap, use the Tauc exponent n = 1/2.
The correct Tauc plot construction is (αhν)^(1/2) versus hν.
This corresponds to the absorption coefficient relationship α ∝ (hν - Eg)^(1/2) for direct allowed transitions.
The band gap energy Eg is determined by extrapolating the linear portion of the (αhν)^(1/2) versus hν plot to the x-axis intercept.
