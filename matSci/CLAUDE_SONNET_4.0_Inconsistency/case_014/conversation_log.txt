Q: What is the correct formula you want me to use for calculating the weight-average molecular weight?
A: The correct formula for weight-average molecular weight is:

M_w = Σ(N_i × M_i²) / Σ(N_i × M_i)

The formula you mentioned, M_w = Σ(N_i M_i) / ΣN_i, is actually for number-average molecular weight (M_n), not weight-average molecular weight.
