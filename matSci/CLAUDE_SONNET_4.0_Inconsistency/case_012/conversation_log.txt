Q: What specific calculation steps or methodology would you like me to follow to solve this electrodeposition thickness problem?
A: Use the standard electrodeposition approach: calculate total charge (Q = I×t), then mass deposited (m = Q×M/(n×F)), then volume (V = m/density), and finally thickness (thickness = V/area). Convert the final result to micrometers.
