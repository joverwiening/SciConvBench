The simulation models carburization of a 0.5 mm thick steel foil treated as a semi-infinite solid.
The initial carbon content of the steel is 0.1 weight percent.
The carburizing temperature is 950 degrees Celsius.
The carbon concentration at the carburized surface is maintained at 1.2 weight percent.
The target carbon composition at 0.8 mm depth is 0.4 weight percent.
The diffusion coefficient of carbon in steel at 950°C is 1.28 × 10^-11 m²/s.
The simulation calculates the time required for the carbon concentration to reach 0.4 weight percent at a depth of 0.8 mm from the carburized surface.
Use Fick's second law with the complementary error function solution for semi-infinite diffusion.
The boundary conditions are constant surface concentration and zero flux at infinite depth.
Output the required carburizing time in hours.
