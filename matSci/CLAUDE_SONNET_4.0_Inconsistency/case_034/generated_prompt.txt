A dislocation has Burgers vector parallel to its line direction and is described as a pure screw dislocation.
Determine whether the dislocation is edge or screw.
By definition, a pure screw dislocation is one where the Burgers vector is parallel to the dislocation line direction.
Since the given dislocation has its Burgers vector parallel to its line direction, it matches the definition of a screw dislocation.
Therefore, the dislocation is a screw dislocation.
