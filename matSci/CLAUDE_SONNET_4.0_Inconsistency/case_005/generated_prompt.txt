Calculate the activation energy for diffusion using the ratio method with the given data.
Use the Arrhenius equation D = D_0 exp(-Q_d/RT) for both temperature conditions.
Set up the ratio D2/D1 = exp(-Q_d/R * (1/T2 - 1/T1)) to eliminate D_0.
Substitute the given values: D1 = 4.76×10^-18 m^2/s at T1 = 866 K and D2 = 1.86×10^-12 m^2/s at T2 = 1438 K.
Use R = 8.314 J/mol·K in the calculations.
Take the natural logarithm of both sides to solve for Q_d.
Rearrange to get Q_d = -R * ln(D2/D1) / (1/T2 - 1/T1).
Calculate the numerical result and convert from J/mol to kJ/mol.
