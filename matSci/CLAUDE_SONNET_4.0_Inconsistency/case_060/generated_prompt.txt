Calculate the Vickers hardness using a 136° diamond pyramid indenter.
Apply a load of 500 grams (0.5 kg).
Measure the diagonal of the square indentation as 0.42 mm.
Use the Vickers hardness formula: HV = 1.854 × F / d², where F is the load in kg and d is the diagonal length in mm.
Substitute the values: HV = 1.854 × 0.5 / (0.42)².
Calculate the result: HV = 0.927 / 0.1764 = 5.26 HV.
Report the final Vickers hardness value as approximately 5.3 HV.
