Calculate the carburizing time for a steel surface using the semi-infinite diffusion solution.
The steel surface is held at 0.90 wt% carbon concentration.
The initial carbon content throughout the steel is 0.20 wt% carbon.
The target carbon content at 0.6 mm depth is 1.10 wt% carbon.
Assume a carburizing temperature of 927°C (1200 K) based on typical industrial carburizing practices.
Use a diffusion coefficient of carbon in steel of 1.28 × 10^-11 m²/s at 927°C from standard literature values.
Apply the semi-infinite diffusion equation: C(x,t) = C₀ + (Cs - C₀) × erfc(x/(2√(Dt))).
Where C₀ is initial concentration, Cs is surface concentration, x is depth, D is diffusion coefficient, and t is time.
Solve for time t when the carbon concentration at x = 0.6 mm equals 1.10 wt%.
Present the calculated carburizing time in hours.
