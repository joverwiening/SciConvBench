Calculate the resolved shear stress using the Schmid factor formula.
The slip plane normal makes an angle φ = 45° with the tensile axis.
The slip direction makes an angle λ = 45° with the tensile axis.
The applied tensile stress is σ = 100 MPa.
Use the Schmid factor formula: m = cos(φ) × cos(λ).
Calculate m = cos(45°) × cos(45°) = 0.707 × 0.707 = 0.5.
Calculate the resolved shear stress using τ = m × σ.
The resolved shear stress is τ = 0.5 × 100 MPa = 50 MPa.
