Calculate the thermal conductivity reduction for a porous ceramic material.
Use a fully dense ceramic with thermal conductivity of 5.0 W/m·K as the baseline.
The porous ceramic has 30% porosity and thermal conductivity of 3.5 W/m·K.
Calculate the conductivity reduction as the difference between the fully dense and porous material conductivities.
Express the reduction both as an absolute value in W/m·K and as a percentage of the baseline conductivity.
The conductivity reduction is 5.0 - 3.5 = 1.5 W/m·K.
The percentage reduction is (1.5 / 5.0) × 100% = 30%.
