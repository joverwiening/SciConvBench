Q: What method or formula would you like me to use to calculate the degree of crystallinity from these density values?
A: Use the standard density-based formula for degree of crystallinity: χc = (ρ - ρa)/(ρc - ρa), where ρ is the measured density, ρa is the amorphous density, and ρc is the crystalline density.
