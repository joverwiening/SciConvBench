Calculate the glass transition temperature of a polymer blend using the Fox equation.
The blend contains 65 wt% polymer A and 35 wt% polymer B.
Polymer A has a glass transition temperature of 370 K.
Polymer B has a glass transition temperature of 250 K.
Apply the Fox equation: 1/T_g,blend = (w_A/T_g,A) + (w_B/T_g,B).
Use weight fractions w_A = 0.65 and w_B = 0.35 in the calculation.
Report the final glass transition temperature of the blend in Kelvin.
