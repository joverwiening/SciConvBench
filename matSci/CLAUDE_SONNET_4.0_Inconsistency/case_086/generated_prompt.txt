Determine the expected state of a superconductor with critical temperature T_c = 9.2 K when measured at 4.2 K.
Since the measurement temperature (4.2 K) is below the critical temperature (9.2 K), the superconductor is expected to be in the superconducting state.
The superconductor should exhibit zero electrical resistance at 4.2 K.
The superconductor should also exhibit the Meissner effect (expulsion of magnetic fields) at 4.2 K.
