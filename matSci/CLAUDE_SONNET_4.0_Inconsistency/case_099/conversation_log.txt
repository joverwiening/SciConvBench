Q: What is the oxidation state of iron in FePO₄ and what is the oxidation state of iron in LiFePO₄?
A: In FePO₄, iron is in the +3 oxidation state (Fe³⁺). In LiFePO₄, iron is in the +2 oxidation state (Fe²⁺).

Q: What is the balanced chemical equation for the complete reaction when one formula unit of FePO₄ is converted to LiFePO₄?
A: FePO₄ + Li⁺ + e⁻ → LiFePO₄

This equation is already balanced as written - one formula unit of FePO₄ requires one lithium ion and one electron to form one formula unit of LiFePO₄.
