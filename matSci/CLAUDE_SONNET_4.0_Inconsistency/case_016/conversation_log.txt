Q: What value should be used for M in the density formula - the molar mass of one formula unit (139.63 g/mol) or should it be calculated differently based on the 8 formula units per unit cell?
A: Use M = 139.63 g/mol (the molar mass of one formula unit). The Z value in the formula already accounts for the number of formula units per unit cell.
