Q: What is the specific relationship or formula you want me to use to calculate the fiber weight fraction from the given densities and fiber volume fraction?
A: Use the standard relationship between weight fraction and volume fraction for composites:

w_f = (V_f × ρ_f) / (V_f × ρ_f + V_m × ρ_m)

where V_m = 1 - V_f
