The grain growth equation is D^n - D_0^n = Kt.
The initial grain size D_0 is 12 μm.
The final grain size D is 36 μm after annealing time t = 2.0 hours.
The grain growth exponent n is 2.
Substitute values: (36)^2 - (12)^2 = K × 2.0.
Calculate (36)^2 = 1296 μm^2 and (12)^2 = 144 μm^2.
Compute the difference: 1296 - 144 = 1152 μm^2.
Solve for K: K = 1152 / 2.0 = 576 μm^2/h.
The value of K is 576 μm^2/h.
