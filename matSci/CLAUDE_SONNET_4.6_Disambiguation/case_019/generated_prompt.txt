Calculate the critical fiber length for short-fiber reinforcement using the standard formula.

The formula for critical fiber length is: l_c = (σ_f * d) / (2 * τ_c)

Use fiber diameter d = 11.05 μm.
Use fiber-matrix interfacial shear strength τ_c = 15.33 MPa.

Compute the numerator: 3690.4 * 11.05 = 40,778.92
Compute the denominator: 2 * 15.33 = 30.66
