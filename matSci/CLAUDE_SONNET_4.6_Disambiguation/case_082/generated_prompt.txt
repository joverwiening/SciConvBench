Given values: electron concentration n = 2.0×10^21 m^-3, electron mobility μ_n = 0.12 m²/V·s, temperature = 300 K.
Use the formula for electrical conductivity: σ = n × e × μ_n.
Use the elementary charge e = 1.6×10^-19 C.
Substitute the values: σ = (2.0×10^21) × (1.6×10^-19) × (0.12).
Compute the result step by step and express the final answer in units of S/m (siemens per meter).
