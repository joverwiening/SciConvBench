The steel is a hypoeutectoid steel with 0.60 wt% C composition.
The eutectoid point in the Fe-Fe3C system is at 0.76 wt% C.
Since 0.60 wt% C is less than 0.76 wt% C, the steel is hypoeutectoid, so proeutectoid ferrite forms first.
The proeutectoid ferrite phase boundary (alpha/alpha+pearlite) is at 0.022 wt% C.
The eutectoid composition is 0.76 wt% C.
Use the lever rule applied across the alpha + pearlite two-phase region just below the eutectoid temperature.

The overall composition C0 = 0.60 wt% C.
The alpha (ferrite) boundary composition C_alpha = 0.022 wt% C.
The eutectoid composition C_eutectoid = 0.76 wt% C.

Calculate the mass fraction of pearlite using the lever rule.
Mass fraction of pearlite = (C0 - C_alpha) / (C_eutectoid - C_alpha).
Mass fraction of pearlite = (0.60 - 0.022) / (0.76 - 0.022).
Mass fraction of pearlite = 0.578 / 0.738.
Mass fraction of pearlite = 0.783 (approximately 78.3%).

Calculate the mass fraction of proeutectoid ferrite.
Mass fraction of proeutectoid ferrite = 1 - mass fraction of pearlite.
Mass fraction of proeutectoid ferrite = 1 - 0.783.
Mass fraction of proeutectoid ferrite = 0.217 (approximately 21.7%).
