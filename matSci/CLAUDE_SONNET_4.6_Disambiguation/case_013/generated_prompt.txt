A polymer sample has four molecular-weight fractions.
The fractions are defined by their molecular weight M_i in g/mol and their number of chains N_i as follows: M_1 = 21640 g/mol with N_1 = 93, M_2 = 74810 g/mol with N_2 = 40, M_3 = 135292 g/mol with N_3 = 170, M_4 = 141802 g/mol with N_4 = 48.
Calculate the weight-average molecular weight M_w in g/mol.
Use the formula M_w = sum(N_i * M_i^2) / sum(N_i * M_i).
Compute N_i * M_i for each fraction and sum them to get the denominator.
Compute N_i * M_i^2 for each fraction and sum them to get the numerator.
Divide the numerator by the denominator to obtain M_w.
Report the final result in g/mol.
