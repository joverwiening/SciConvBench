The material is zirconia (ZrO₂).
The bulk density of the specimen is 5.42 g/cm³.
The theoretical (true) density of zirconia is 6.10 g/cm³.
Calculate total porosity using the formula: Porosity (%) = (1 - bulk density / theoretical density) × 100.
Substitute the values: Porosity (%) = (1 - 5.42 / 6.10) × 100.
Compute the ratio: 5.42 / 6.10 = 0.88852 (approximately).
Subtract from 1: 1 - 0.88852 = 0.11148.
Multiply by 100: total porosity ≈ 11.15%.
Report the result as approximately 11.1% total porosity.
