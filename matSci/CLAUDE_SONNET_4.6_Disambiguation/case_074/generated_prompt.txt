Use Norton's creep law in the form: strain rate = A multiplied by stress raised to the power n.
The material is a nickel alloy at 950°C.
The applied stress is 110 MPa.
The Norton's law constant A is 2.5×10^-18 MPa^-n s^-1.
The stress exponent n is 4.6.
Compute 110 raised to the power 4.6 first.
Multiply the result by A = 2.5×10^-18 to obtain the creep strain rate in units of s^-1.
Report the final numerical value of the creep strain rate in s^-1.
No temperature dependence calculation is required as A is already given at 950°C.
