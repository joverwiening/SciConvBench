Calculate the thermal-mismatch strain in an aluminum film bonded to a ceramic substrate subjected to a temperature change of -180 K.
Use the aluminum film CTE of α_f = 23×10^-6 K^-1.
Use the ceramic substrate CTE of α_s = 8×10^-6 K^-1.
The temperature change is ΔT = -180 K (cooling).
Assume the substrate is thick and rigid compared to the film, so the substrate dictates the dimensional change.
Compute the mismatch strain using the formula: ε_mismatch = (α_f - α_s) × ΔT.
Substitute values: ε_mismatch = (23×10^-6 - 8×10^-6) × (-180).
Simplify the CTE difference: Δα = 15×10^-6 K^-1.
Compute the final result: ε_mismatch = 15×10^-6 × (-180) = -2.7×10^-3.
Report the thermal-mismatch strain in the film as -2.7×10^-3 (dimensionless), indicating the film is in a state of compressive strain relative to its free thermal contraction, meaning the film is under biaxial tension because the substrate constrains it from contracting as much as it would freely.
