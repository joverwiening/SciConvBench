The applied tensile stress is 65 MPa.
The angle between the tensile axis and the slip plane normal (phi) is 54.7°.

Compute cos(54.7°) ≈ 0.5774 and cos(45.0°) ≈ 0.7071.
Multiply the two cosine values to get the Schmid factor: 0.5774 × 0.7071 ≈ 0.4082.
Compute the resolved shear stress as the applied tensile stress multiplied by the Schmid factor: 65 MPa × 0.4082 ≈ 26.5 MPa.
