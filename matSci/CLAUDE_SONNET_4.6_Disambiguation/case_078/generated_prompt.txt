The alloy is a hypoeutectic binary A-B system.
The overall composition is C_0 = 30 wt% B.
The eutectic composition is C_eutectic = 40 wt% B.
The maximum solid solubility of B in the alpha phase is C_alpha = 12 wt% B.
The temperature of interest is just below the eutectic temperature.
Apply the lever rule to find the fraction of eutectic microconstituent.
The eutectic microconstituent forms from the liquid that reaches the eutectic composition.
Use the tie line that spans from C_alpha (12 wt% B) to C_eutectic (40 wt% B).
The fraction of eutectic microconstituent is calculated as: f_eutectic = (C_0 - C_alpha) / (C_eutectic - C_alpha).
Substitute the values: f_eutectic = (30 - 12) / (40 - 12).
Compute the numerator: 30 - 12 = 18.
Compute the denominator: 40 - 12 = 28.
Divide to get the result: f_eutectic = 18 / 28 = 0.643.
The fraction of eutectic microconstituent present just below the eutectic temperature is approximately 0.643, or 64.3%.
The remaining fraction, approximately 0.357 or 35.7%, is the pro-eutectic (primary) alpha phase.
