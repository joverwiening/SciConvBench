Estimate the effective thermal conductivity of a unidirectional composite along the fiber direction using the Rule of Mixtures.
The composite contains 55 vol% carbon fibers (volume fraction V_f = 0.55) and 45 vol% epoxy matrix (volume fraction V_m = 0.45).
Use a thermal conductivity of k_f = 18 W/m·K for the carbon fibers.
Use a thermal conductivity of k_m = 0.25 W/m·K for the epoxy matrix.
Apply the longitudinal Rule of Mixtures formula: k_eff = V_f * k_f + V_m * k_m.
Calculate k_eff = (0.55 * 18) + (0.45 * 0.25).
Compute each term: 0.55 * 18 = 9.9 W/m·K and 0.45 * 0.25 = 0.1125 W/m·K.
Sum the terms to obtain k_eff = 9.9 + 0.1125 = 10.0125 W/m·K.
Report the final effective thermal conductivity along the fiber direction as approximately 10.01 W/m·K.
