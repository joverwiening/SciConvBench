A plating process deposits metal onto an area of 111.01 cm^2.
The current applied is 2.479 A for 14.6 minutes.
Convert time to seconds: 14.6 min × 60 = 876 seconds.
Calculate total charge Q = current × time = 2.479 A × 876 s = 2171.604 C.
The metal has molar mass M = 151.13 g/mol, density ρ = 10.754 g/cm^3, and valence n = 3.
Use Faraday's constant F = 96485 C/mol.
Assume 100% current efficiency.
Calculate mass deposited using Faraday's law: mass = (Q × M) / (n × F) = (2171.604 × 151.13) / (3 × 96485).
Compute numerator: 2171.604 × 151.13 = 328,246.45 g·C/mol.
Compute denominator: 3 × 96485 = 289,455 C/mol.
Mass = 328,246.45 / 289,455 = 1.13405 g.
Calculate volume of deposited metal: volume = mass / density = 1.13405 g / 10.754 g/cm^3 = 0.105455 cm^3.
Calculate thickness: thickness = volume / area = 0.105455 cm^3 / 111.01 cm^2 = 9.4996 × 10^-4 cm.
Convert thickness to micrometers: 9.4996 × 10^-4 cm × 10^4 μm/cm = 9.4996 μm.
Report the average coating thickness as approximately 9.50 μm.
