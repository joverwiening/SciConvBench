Calculate the corrosion penetration rate (CPR) for a steel coupon using the weight-loss method.

The mass loss of the steel coupon is 0.084 g.
The exposed surface area is 12.0 cm².
The exposure duration is 240 hours.
The density of the steel is 7.87 g/cm³.

Use the standard corrosion penetration rate formula: CPR = (K × W) / (A × T × D), where W is mass loss in grams, A is area in cm², T is exposure time in hours, D is density in g/cm³, and K is the unit conversion constant.

Use K = 87,600 to yield CPR in mm/year.

Substitute the values: CPR = (87,600 × 0.084) / (12.0 × 240 × 7.87).

Compute the numerator: 87,600 × 0.084 = 7,358.4.

Compute the denominator: 12.0 × 240 × 7.87 = 22,665.6.

Divide to obtain CPR: 7,358.4 / 22,665.6 ≈ 0.3247 mm/year.

Report the final corrosion penetration rate as approximately 0.325 mm/year.
