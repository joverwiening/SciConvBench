The system is a binary alloy at the eutectic temperature, analyzed using the lever rule.
The overall alloy composition is C0 = 14.36 wt% B.
The alpha phase boundary composition at the eutectic temperature is C_αe = 4.63 wt% B.
The eutectic liquid composition at the eutectic temperature is C_e = 40.31 wt% B.
Just above the eutectic temperature, two phases are present: alpha (solid) and liquid.
Apply the lever rule to find the fraction of liquid just above the eutectic temperature.
The lever rule for the liquid fraction is: f_L = (C0 - C_αe) / (C_e - C_αe).
Substitute the values: f_L = (14.36 - 4.63) / (40.31 - 4.63).
Calculate the numerator: 14.36 - 4.63 = 9.73.
Calculate the denominator: 40.31 - 4.63 = 35.68.
Divide: f_L = 9.73 / 35.68 = 0.2727.
The fraction of the alloy that is liquid just above the eutectic temperature is approximately 0.2727, or 27.27%.
This liquid fraction will fully transform into eutectic microconstituent upon cooling through the eutectic temperature.
Therefore, approximately 27.3% of the alloy becomes eutectic microconstituent, and the remaining approximately 72.7% is primary alpha phase.
