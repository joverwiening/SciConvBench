The task is to calculate the average electrodeposition coating thickness given electrochemical and material parameters.

The deposited metal has molar mass M = 181.05 g/mol and density ρ = 18.511 g/cm³.
The plating current is I = 2.744 A applied for t = 18.3 min = 18.3 × 60 = 1098 s.
The coated area is A = 90.04 cm².
Faraday's constant is F = 96485 C/mol.
Current efficiency is 100%.

Determine the number of electrons transferred per metal atom (n) from the molar mass.
The molar mass 181.05 g/mol corresponds to Tantalum (Ta, atomic mass ≈ 180.95 g/mol), which deposits with n = 5 electrons (Ta⁵⁺ + 5e⁻ → Ta).
Use n = 5.

Calculate total charge: Q = I × t = 2.744 × 1098 = 3013.01 C.
Calculate moles of electrons: mol_e = Q / F = 3013.01 / 96485 = 0.031228 mol.
Calculate moles of metal deposited: mol_metal = mol_e / n = 0.031228 / 5 = 0.0062456 mol.
Calculate mass of deposited metal: m = mol_metal × M = 0.0062456 × 181.05 = 1.13063 g.
Calculate volume of deposited metal: V = m / ρ = 1.13063 / 18.511 = 0.061078 cm³.
Calculate average thickness: d = V / A = 0.061078 / 90.04 = 0.0006783 cm.
Convert to micrometers: d = 0.0006783 cm × 10⁴ μm/cm = 6.783 μm.

The average coating thickness is approximately 6.78 μm.
