The active material is FePO₄ (iron phosphate) used as a cathode.
The electrochemical reaction involves the exchange of x = 1 mole of Li and 1 mole of electrons per mole of FePO₄.
Use the molar mass of FePO₄: M = 55.845 (Fe) + 30.974 (P) + 4 × 15.999 (O) = 150.815 g/mol.
Use Faraday's constant F = 96485 C/mol.
Calculate the theoretical specific capacity using the formula Q = (n × F) / M, where n = 1 mol electrons per mol FePO₄.
Compute Q in C/g first: Q = 96485 / 150.815 = 639.8 C/g.
Convert to mAh/g by dividing by 3.6: Q = 639.8 / 3.6 ≈ 177.7 mAh/g.
Report the theoretical specific capacity of FePO₄ as approximately 178 mAh/g.
