Calculate the carrier concentration of a rectangular semiconductor sample using the Hall effect.
The current through the sample is I = 15 mA (0.015 A).
The applied magnetic field is B = 0.80 T.
The measured Hall voltage is V_H = 24 mV (0.024 V).
The thickness of the sample in the direction parallel to the magnetic field is t = 0.50 mm (0.0005 m).
Use the Hall effect formula: n = (I * B) / (V_H * t * e), where e is the elementary charge (1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C).
Substitute the values: n = (0.015 × 0.80) / (0.024 × 0.0005 × 1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹).
Compute the numerator: 0.015 × 0.80 = 0.012.
Compute the denominator: 0.024 × 0.0005 × 1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ = 1.9224 × 10⁻²⁴.
Divide to obtain the carrier concentration: n = 0.012 / 1.9224 × 10⁻²⁴ ≈ 6.24 × 10²¹ carriers per cubic meter.
Express the final answer as n ≈ 6.24 × 10²¹ m⁻³.
