Estimate the fracture stress of a brittle ceramic using the Griffith fracture criterion.

Use the Griffith equation for fracture stress: σ_f = sqrt((2 * E * γ_s) / (π * a)).

E is the elastic modulus, given as 245.0 GPa (convert to 245.0 × 10^9 Pa).
γ_s is the surface energy, given as 16.457 J/m^2.
a is the half-crack length (flaw size), given as 107.86 μm (convert to 107.86 × 10^-6 m).
The flaw size provided is treated as the half-crack length a in the Griffith equation.

Compute the numerator: 2 * E * γ_s = 2 * (245.0 × 10^9) * (16.457) = 8.064 × 10^12.
Compute the denominator: π * a = π * (107.86 × 10^-6) = 3.38875 × 10^-4.
Divide: (8.064 × 10^12) / (3.38875 × 10^-4) = 2.3797 × 10^16.
Take the square root: σ_f = sqrt(2.3797 × 10^16) = 1.5427 × 10^8 Pa.
Convert to MPa: σ_f = 154.27 MPa.
