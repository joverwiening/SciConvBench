The sample is cylindrical, so the cross-sectional area is circular.
The diameter of the cross-section is 11.84 mm.
Calculate the cross-sectional area using A = π × (d/2)².
A = π × (11.84/2)² = π × (5.92)² = π × 35.0464 ≈ 110.106 mm².
The applied load is 18.93 kN = 18930 N.
Calculate engineering stress using σ = F / A.
σ = 18930 / 110.106 ≈ 171.927 MPa.
The gauge length is 76.29 mm and the elastic extension is 0.0628 mm.
Calculate engineering strain using ε = ΔL / L₀.
ε = 0.0628 / 76.29 ≈ 8.2313 × 10⁻⁴.
Calculate Young's modulus using E = σ / ε.
E = 171.927 MPa / 8.2313 × 10⁻⁴ ≈ 208,843 MPa.
Convert to GPa by dividing by 1000.
E ≈ 208.84 GPa.
