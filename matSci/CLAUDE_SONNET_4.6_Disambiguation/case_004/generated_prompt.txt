The system follows Arrhenius diffusion behavior: D = D0 * exp(-Qd / (R * T)).
Two data points are given: D1 = 4.76×10^-18 m^2/s at T1 = 866 K, and D2 = 1.86×10^-12 m^2/s at T2 = 1438 K.
Use R = 8.314 J/mol·K.
Derive the activation energy Qd by taking the ratio of the two Arrhenius expressions to eliminate D0.
Apply the relation: ln(D1/D2) = (-Qd/R) * (1/T1 - 1/T2).
Rearrange to solve for Qd: Qd = -R * ln(D1/D2) / (1/T1 - 1/T2).
Compute ln(D1/D2) = ln(4.76×10^-18 / 1.86×10^-12) = ln(2.559×10^-6) = -12.877.
Compute 1/T1 = 1/866 = 1.1547×10^-3 K^-1.
Compute 1/T2 = 1/1438 = 6.9541×10^-4 K^-1.
Compute (1/T1 - 1/T2) = 1.1547×10^-3 - 6.9541×10^-4 = 4.5929×10^-4 K^-1.
Compute Qd = -8.314 * (-12.877) / (4.5929×10^-4).
Compute numerator: 8.314 * 12.877 = 107.06 J/mol.
Compute Qd = 107.06 / 4.5929×10^-4 = 233,100 J/mol.
Convert to kJ/mol: Qd ≈ 233.1 kJ/mol.
The activation energy for diffusion is approximately 233 kJ/mol.
