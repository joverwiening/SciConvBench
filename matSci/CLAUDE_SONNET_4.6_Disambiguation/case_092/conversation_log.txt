Q: What value should I use for the theoretical heat of fusion of 100% crystalline polypropylene (ΔHf°)?

Common literature values range from 138 J/g to 209 J/g — do you have a specific value you'd like to use, or should I use a standard reference value (e.g., 207 J/g from Wunderlich, or 138 J/g from another source)?
A: Use ΔH_f^0 = 209 J/g.
