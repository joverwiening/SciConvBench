Estimate the degree of crystallinity of a semicrystalline polypropylene sample using DSC melting data.
The measured melting enthalpy of the sample is 78 J/g.
The theoretical heat of fusion for 100% crystalline polypropylene is 209 J/g.
Calculate the degree of crystallinity using the formula: Xc = (ΔHm / ΔHf°) × 100%.
Xc = (78 / 209) × 100%.
Report the final degree of crystallinity as a percentage, rounded to one decimal place.
No corrections for sample mass, heating rate, or baseline are required unless otherwise stated.
