The task is to solve the Avrami (JMAK) equation for time given a specific transformed fraction.

Use the Avrami equation: X = 1 - exp(-k * t^n)
The transformed fraction X is given as 0.274.
The rate constant k is given as 7.83 × 10^-3.
The Avrami exponent n is given as 2.81.
Rearrange the equation to solve for t: t = [(-ln(1 - X)) / k]^(1/n)
Compute 1 - X = 1 - 0.274 = 0.726.
Compute ln(0.726) = -0.32017 (approximately).
Compute -ln(0.726) = 0.32017.
Divide by k: 0.32017 / (7.83 × 10^-3) = 40.89 (approximately).
Compute the exponent 1/n = 1 / 2.81 = 0.35587 (approximately).
Compute t = (40.89)^(0.35587).
Calculate (40.89)^(0.35587): take natural log of 40.89 = 3.7102, multiply by 0.35587 = 1.3203, exponentiate to get e^1.3203 = 3.744 (approximately).
The time required to reach a transformed fraction of 0.274 is approximately 3.74 seconds.
