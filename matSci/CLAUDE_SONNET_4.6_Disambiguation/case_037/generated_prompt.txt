The system is a two-layer composite wall with heat conduction in series (1D steady-state).
Layer 1 has a thickness of 10.91 mm (0.01091 m) and thermal conductivity k1 = 4.69 W/m·K.
Layer 2 has a thickness of 8.09 mm (0.00809 m) and thermal conductivity k2 = 6.75 W/m·K.
The wall cross-sectional area is A = 0.0782 m².
The outer surface of Layer 1 is held at T1 = 191.8°C and the outer surface of Layer 2 is held at T2 = 21.2°C.
Assume no contact resistance between the two layers.
Assume steady-state, one-dimensional heat conduction with no heat generation.

Calculate the thermal resistance of each layer using R = L / (k · A).
R1 = 0.01091 / (4.69 × 0.0782) = 0.01091 / 0.366758 = 0.029749 K/W.
R2 = 0.00809 / (6.75 × 0.0782) = 0.00809 / 0.52785 = 0.015326 K/W.
Total resistance R_total = R1 + R2 = 0.029749 + 0.015326 = 0.045075 K/W.
Calculate the temperature difference: ΔT = 191.8 - 21.2 = 170.6°C (= 170.6 K).
Calculate the heat transfer rate: Q = ΔT / R_total = 170.6 / 0.045075 = 3785.1 W.

The steady heat-transfer rate through the composite wall is approximately 3785 W.
