Calculate the equilibrium potential of the Cu²⁺/Cu electrode at 25°C using the Nernst equation.
The standard reduction potential for Cu²⁺/Cu is E° = +0.337 V (vs. SHE).
The half-reaction is Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu.
The number of electrons transferred (n) is 2.
The activity of solid Cu is 1 (pure solid).
The activity of Cu²⁺ ions is 0.010.
The temperature is 25°C (298.15 K).
Use the Nernst equation: E = E° - (RT/nF) × ln(1/[Cu²⁺]).
Use R = 8.314 J/(mol·K), F = 96485 C/mol.
Compute (RT/nF) = (8.314 × 298.15) / (2 × 96485) ≈ 0.012856 V.
Compute ln(1/0.010) = ln(100) ≈ 4.6052.
Compute the correction term: 0.012856 × 4.6052 ≈ 0.05921 V.
Subtract the correction from E°: E = 0.337 - 0.05921 ≈ 0.2778 V.
Report the equilibrium potential as approximately +0.278 V vs. SHE.
