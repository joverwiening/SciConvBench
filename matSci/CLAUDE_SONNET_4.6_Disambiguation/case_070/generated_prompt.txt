The task is to estimate the biaxial thermal stress in a thin film on a rigid substrate subjected to a thermal excursion.

The thin film has a Young's modulus E_f = 160 GPa and a Poisson's ratio ν_f = 0.28.
The coefficient of thermal expansion of the film is α_f = 14×10^-6 K^-1.
The coefficient of thermal expansion of the substrate is α_s = 6×10^-6 K^-1.
The thermal excursion is ΔT = -250 K.
The substrate is rigid, meaning it constrains the film and does not deform itself.
Compute the biaxial modulus of the film as M_f = E_f / (1 - ν_f).
Substitute M_f = 160 GPa / (1 - 0.28) = 160 / 0.72 ≈ 222.2 GPa.
Compute the mismatch strain as ε_mismatch = (α_s - α_f) × ΔT.
Substitute ε_mismatch = (6×10^-6 - 14×10^-6) × (-250) = (-8×10^-6) × (-250) = +2×10^-3 (dimensionless).
Compute the biaxial thermal stress as σ = M_f × ε_mismatch.
Substitute σ = 222.2 GPa × 2×10^-3 = +0.4444 GPa ≈ +444 MPa.
The positive sign indicates the stress in the film is tensile.
Report the final result as a biaxial thermal stress of approximately +444 MPa (tensile) in the thin film.
