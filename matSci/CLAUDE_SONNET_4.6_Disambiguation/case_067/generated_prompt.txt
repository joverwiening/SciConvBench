Compute the linear density of atoms along the close-packed direction of a BCC crystal structure.
The lattice parameter is a = 0.286 nm.
The close-packed direction in BCC is the body diagonal, which is the [111] direction.
The body diagonal length is given by the formula: body diagonal = a√3.
Calculate the body diagonal length as 0.286 × √3 nm.
Along the [111] body diagonal in BCC, there are 2 atoms: one-half of each corner atom at the two ends (contributing 2 × 1/2 = 1) plus the full body-center atom (contributing 1), giving a total of 2 atoms per body diagonal length.
Compute linear density as: LD = number of atoms along the direction / length of the direction.
LD = 2 atoms / (0.286 × √3 nm).
Express the final answer in units of atoms per meter (or atoms per nm as appropriate).
Report the numerical result of the linear density.
