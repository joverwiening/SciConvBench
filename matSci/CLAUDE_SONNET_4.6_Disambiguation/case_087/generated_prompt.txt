Use the Young-Dupré equation to compute the work of adhesion: W_adhesion = γ_LV × (1 + cos θ).
The liquid-vapor surface tension of water is γ_LV = 72.8 mN/m.
The contact angle is θ = 72°.
Compute cos(72°) = 0.3090.
Compute 1 + cos(72°) = 1.3090.
Compute W_adhesion = 72.8 mN/m × 1.3090 = 95.30 mN/m.
Report the work of adhesion as W_adhesion ≈ 95.3 mJ/m².
