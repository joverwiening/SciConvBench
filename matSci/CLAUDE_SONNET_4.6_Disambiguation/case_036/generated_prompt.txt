The task is to solve the Avrami (JMAK) equation for time given a specific fraction transformed.

The equation is X = 1 - exp(-k * t^n).
Given values are X = 0.848, k = 9.27×10^-5, and n = 3.60.
Rearrange the equation to solve for t: t = [(-ln(1 - X)) / k]^(1/n).
Compute (1 - X): 1 - 0.848 = 0.152.
Compute -ln(0.152): -ln(0.152) = ln(1/0.152) = ln(6.5789...) ≈ 1.88429.
Divide by k: 1.88429 / 9.27×10^-5 ≈ 20,327.8.
Raise to the power (1/n) = (1/3.60) ≈ 0.27778: t = (20,327.8)^(0.27778).
Compute ln(20,327.8) ≈ 9.9196.
Multiply by 0.27778: 9.9196 × 0.27778 ≈ 2.7554.
Compute t = exp(2.7554) ≈ 15.73.
The time needed to reach X = 0.848 is approximately 15.73 seconds.
