The specimen is cylindrical, so the cross-sectional area is circular.
The initial diameter is 11.79 mm, giving a radius of 5.895 mm.
Calculate the cross-sectional area using A = π × r², which gives A = π × (5.895)² mm².
A = π × 34.751025 = 109.185 mm² (approximately).
The applied load is 16.21 kN = 16210 N.
Calculate the engineering stress using σ = F / A = 16210 / 109.185 N/mm².
σ = 148.47 MPa (approximately).
The gauge length is 44.24 mm and the elongation is 0.0342 mm.
Calculate the engineering strain using ε = ΔL / L₀ = 0.0342 / 44.24.
ε = 7.732 × 10⁻⁴ (dimensionless).
Calculate Young's modulus using E = σ / ε = 148.47 / 7.732 × 10⁻⁴ MPa.
E = 192,000 MPa approximately.
Convert to GPa by dividing by 1000.
