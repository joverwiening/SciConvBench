Use the Hall–Petch equation: σ_y = σ_0 + k_y × d^(-1/2).
The given values are σ_y = 420 MPa, σ_0 = 120 MPa, and k_y = 0.74 MPa·m^(1/2).
Rearrange to solve for d: d^(-1/2) = (σ_y - σ_0) / k_y.
Compute the numerator: 420 MPa - 120 MPa = 300 MPa.
Compute d^(-1/2): 300 MPa / 0.74 MPa·m^(1/2) = 405.405 m^(-1/2).
Compute d^(1/2): 1 / 405.405 m^(-1/2) = 0.002467 m^(1/2).
Compute d: (0.002467)^2 = 6.085 × 10^(-6) m.
Convert to micrometers: d ≈ 6.1 µm.
