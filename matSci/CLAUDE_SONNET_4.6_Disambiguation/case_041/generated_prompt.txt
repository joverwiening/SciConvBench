The task is to calculate the average electrodeposition coating thickness given electrochemical and material parameters.

Given values:
- Electrode area: A = 147.06 cm²
- Current: I = 2.074 A
- Time: t = 10.8 min = 648 s
- Molar mass of deposited metal: M = 103.65 g/mol
- Density of deposited metal: ρ = 16.041 g/cm³
- Current efficiency: 100% (n electrons assumed from Faraday's law)
- Faraday's constant: F = 96485 C/mol
- The valence (n) must be determined from standard electrodeposition context; since M ≈ 103.65 g/mol and ρ ≈ 16.041 g/cm³ match Rhodium (Rh, M = 102.91) closely but more precisely match a scenario where n = 2 is the standard assumption for this problem.

Step 1: Calculate total charge Q.
Q = I × t = 2.074 A × 648 s = 1343.952 C

Step 2: Calculate moles of electrons transferred.
Assume n = 2 (standard valence for this problem context).
moles of electrons = Q / F = 1343.952 / 96485 = 0.013930 mol

Step 3: Calculate moles of metal deposited.
moles of metal = moles of electrons / n = 0.013930 / 2 = 0.006965 mol

Step 4: Calculate mass of deposited metal.
mass = moles × M = 0.006965 × 103.65 = 0.72193 g

Step 5: Calculate volume of deposited metal.
volume = mass / ρ = 0.72193 / 16.041 = 0.045007 cm³

Step 6: Calculate average thickness.
thickness = volume / area = 0.045007 / 147.06 = 3.0605 × 10⁻⁴ cm

Step 7: Convert to micrometers.
thickness = 3.0605 × 10⁻⁴ cm × 10⁴ μm/cm = 3.0605 μm
