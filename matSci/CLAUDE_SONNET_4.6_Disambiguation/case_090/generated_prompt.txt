Estimate the axial coefficient of thermal expansion (CTE) of a continuous-fiber composite using the Schapery rule of mixtures for the axial direction.

Use the following input values:
Fiber volume fraction V_f = 0.60
Matrix volume fraction V_m = 1 - V_f = 0.40
Fiber modulus E_f = 230 GPa
Matrix modulus E_m = 3.5 GPa
Fiber axial CTE α_f = -0.5×10^-6 K^-1
Matrix CTE α_m = 55×10^-6 K^-1

Apply the Schapery formula for the axial CTE:
α_1 = (α_f × E_f × V_f + α_m × E_m × V_m) / (E_f × V_f + E_m × V_m)

Compute the numerator as (α_f × E_f × V_f) + (α_m × E_m × V_m).
Compute the denominator as (E_f × V_f) + (E_m × V_m), which is also the rule-of-mixtures axial modulus E_1.
Divide the numerator by the denominator to obtain α_1 in units of K^-1.
Report the final value of α_1 in units of 10^-6 K^-1.
Show all intermediate numerical steps clearly.
