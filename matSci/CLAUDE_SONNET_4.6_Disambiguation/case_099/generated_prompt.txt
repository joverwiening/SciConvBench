The task is to estimate the open-circuit thermoelectric voltage of a p-type thermoelectric leg.
The material is p-type with a Seebeck coefficient S = +180 µV/K.
The temperature difference across the leg is ΔT = 60 K.
Use the open-circuit thermoelectric voltage formula: V = S × ΔT.
Substitute the values: V = 180 µV/K × 60 K.
Calculate the result: V = 10,800 µV = 10.8 mV.
The open-circuit thermoelectric voltage is 10.8 mV.
The positive sign of S confirms the hot end is at higher electric potential for a p-type material, consistent with conventional current flowing from hot to cold inside the leg.
No internal resistance or load is considered since the circuit is open.
