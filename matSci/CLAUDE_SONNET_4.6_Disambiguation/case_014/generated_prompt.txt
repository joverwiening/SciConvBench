Calculate the degree of crystallinity of a semicrystalline polymer sample using both the volume fraction method and the weight fraction method.

Given values:
- Measured density of the semicrystalline sample: ρ_s = 1.1973 g/cm³
- Density of completely amorphous phase: ρ_a = 0.8845 g/cm³
- Density of completely crystalline phase: ρ_c = 1.4240 g/cm³

Step 1: Calculate volume fraction crystallinity (X_v).
Use the formula: X_v = (ρ_s - ρ_a) / (ρ_c - ρ_a)
Substitute values: X_v = (1.1973 - 0.8845) / (1.4240 - 0.8845)
Compute numerator: 1.1973 - 0.8845 = 0.3128
Compute denominator: 1.4240 - 0.8845 = 0.5395
Compute X_v: 0.3128 / 0.5395 = 0.5797
Express as percentage: X_v ≈ 57.97% ≈ 58.0%

Step 2: Calculate weight fraction crystallinity (X_w).
Use the formula: X_w = (ρ_c / ρ_s) × (ρ_s - ρ_a) / (ρ_c - ρ_a)
This is equivalent to: X_w = (ρ_c / ρ_s) × X_v
Substitute values: X_w = (1.4240 / 1.1973) × 0.5797
Compute ratio: 1.4240 / 1.1973 = 1.1894
Compute X_w: 1.1894 × 0.5797 = 0.6893
Express as percentage: X_w ≈ 68.93% ≈ 68.9%

Final Results:
- Volume fraction crystallinity: X_v ≈ 58.0%
- Weight fraction crystallinity: X_w ≈ 68.9%
