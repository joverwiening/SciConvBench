Use X-rays with wavelength λ = 0.0762 nm.
The crystal is cubic with lattice parameter a = 0.5313 nm.
The reflecting planes are the (111) planes.
Calculate the interplanar spacing d for the (111) planes using d = a / sqrt(h² + k² + l²), where h=1, k=1, l=1.
Apply Bragg's Law for first-order reflection: nλ = 2d sinθ, where n = 1.
Solve for θ (the Bragg angle).
The required output is the full diffraction angle 2θ expressed in degrees.
