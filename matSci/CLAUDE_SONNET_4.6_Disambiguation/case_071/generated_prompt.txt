Steady-state diffusion occurs across a ceramic membrane of thickness 1.5 mm (0.0015 m).
The diffusivity D is 2.2×10^-11 m²/s.
The concentration on the high side is 0.12 mol/m³ and on the low side is 0.02 mol/m³.
The concentration difference ΔC is 0.12 − 0.02 = 0.10 mol/m³.
Apply Fick's First Law: J = D × (ΔC / Δx).
Calculate the concentration gradient: ΔC / Δx = 0.10 mol/m³ ÷ 0.0015 m = 66.67 mol/m⁴.
Calculate the diffusive flux: J = 2.2×10^-11 m²/s × 66.67 mol/m⁴.
Report the final result: J = 1.467×10^-9 mol/(m²·s).
Treat the flux as positive, directed from the high-concentration face to the low-concentration face.
