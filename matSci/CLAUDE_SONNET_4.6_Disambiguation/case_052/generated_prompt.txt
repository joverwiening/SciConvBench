Compute the theoretical density of a ceramic crystal with a cubic unit cell.
The molar mass of one formula unit is 101.97 g/mol.
The number of formula units per unit cell (Z) is 1.
The unit cell edge length is 0.6330 nm, which must be converted to cm: 0.6330 nm = 0.6330 × 10^-7 cm.
Compute the unit cell volume as a = (0.6330 × 10^-7 cm)^3.
Use Avogadro's number N_A = 6.022 × 10^23 mol^-1.
Apply the density formula: density = (Z × M) / (N_A × a^3), where M is the molar mass, a^3 is the unit cell volume.
Substitute Z = 1, M = 101.97 g/mol, a = 0.6330 × 10^-7 cm, N_A = 6.022 × 10^23 mol^-1.
Calculate a^3 = (0.6330 × 10^-7)^3 = 2.5357 × 10^-22 cm^3.
Calculate the denominator: 6.022 × 10^23 × 2.5357 × 10^-22 = 1.5270 × 10^2 = 152.70 cm^3/mol.
Calculate density = 101.97 / 152.70 = approximately 0.6678 g/cm^3.
Report the final theoretical density as approximately 0.668 g/cm^3.
