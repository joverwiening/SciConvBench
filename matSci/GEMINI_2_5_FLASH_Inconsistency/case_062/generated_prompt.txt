The thermal-shock parameter R is inversely proportional to the coefficient of thermal expansion α, as indicated by the formula R ∝ σ_f k/(Eα).
An increase in α will result in a decrease in the thermal-shock parameter R.
A decrease in the thermal-shock parameter R implies a reduction in thermal-shock resistance.
Therefore, based on the given formula, increasing α qualitatively reduces the thermal-shock resistance of the ceramic.
This analysis contradicts the conclusion that increasing α improves thermal-shock resistance.
