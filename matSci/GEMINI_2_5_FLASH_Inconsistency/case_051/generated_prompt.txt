The simulation models carbon diffusion in a semi-infinite steel solid.
The initial carbon composition of the steel is 0.15 weight percent.
The surface carbon composition is maintained at 0.9 weight percent.
The carburization process occurs at a constant temperature of 927 degrees Celsius.
The simulation calculates the time required for the carbon composition to reach 0.5 weight percent at a depth of 0.8 mm from the carburized surface.
