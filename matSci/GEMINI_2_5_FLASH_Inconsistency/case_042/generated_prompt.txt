The concentration at x = 0 is 0.12 mol/m^3.
The concentration at x = L is 0.02 mol/m^3.
The diffusion coefficient D is 3.0×10^-11 m^2/s.
The length L is 1 mm.
The concentration gradient dC/dx will be calculated as (concentration at L - concentration at 0) / L.
Fick's first law will be applied to calculate the diffusion flux J as -D * (dC/dx).
The diffusion flux J will be reported in mol/(m^2·s).
