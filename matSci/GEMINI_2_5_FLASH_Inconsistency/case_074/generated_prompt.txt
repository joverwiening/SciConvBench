The longitudinal modulus of a unidirectional composite is calculated using the rule of mixtures.
The fiber modulus is 230 GPa.
The matrix modulus is 3 GPa.
The fiber volume fraction is 0.60.
The matrix volume fraction is derived as 1 minus the fiber volume fraction.
The longitudinal modulus is calculated as the sum of (fiber modulus multiplied by fiber volume fraction) and (matrix modulus multiplied by matrix volume fraction).
The reported longitudinal modulus of 2.5 GPa is disregarded.
The calculated longitudinal modulus is 139.2 GPa.
