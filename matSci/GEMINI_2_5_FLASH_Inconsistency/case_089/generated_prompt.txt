The Nernst equation for the Cu^2+/Cu electrode at 25°C is E = E° + (0.0592/2) log a_(Cu2+).
When a_(Cu2+) is 1, the reduction potential E is E°.
When a_(Cu2+) is 10^-2, the reduction potential E is E° - 0.0592.
Lowering a_(Cu2+) from 1 to 10^-2 makes the reduction potential more negative.
The statement that lowering a_(Cu2+) from 1 to 10^-2 makes the reduction potential more positive is incorrect.
