A binary diffusion couple is prepared with 20 at% B on the left and 80 at% B on the right.
After annealing, the concentration profile spans from 20 at% B to 80 at% B.
The physical distance corresponding to the concentration span from 20 at% B to 80 at% B is assumed to be 100 micrometers.
The interdiffusion distance is estimated as the physical distance of the concentration profile span.
The estimated interdiffusion distance is 100 micrometers.
