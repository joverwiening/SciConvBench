Q: For a cubic crystal, the interplanar spacing d_100 is equal to the lattice parameter 'a'. How should I interpret the given values where d_100 = 0.180 nm and a = 0.360 nm?
A: The interplanar spacing of the (100) planes is reported as d_(100) = 0.360 nm, and the lattice parameter a = 0.360 nm.
