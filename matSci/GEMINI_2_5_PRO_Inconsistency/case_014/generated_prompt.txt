A polymer sample has four components.
The first component consists of 93 molecules, each with a molar mass of 21640 g/mol.
The second component consists of 40 molecules, each with a molar mass of 74810 g/mol.
The third component consists of 170 molecules, each with a molar mass of 135292 g/mol.
The fourth component consists of 48 molecules, each with a molar mass of 141802 g/mol.
Calculate the weight-average molecular weight, M_w, for this sample.
Use the formula M_w = Σ(N_i * M_i^2) / Σ(N_i * M_i).
The final result must be expressed in units of g/mol.
