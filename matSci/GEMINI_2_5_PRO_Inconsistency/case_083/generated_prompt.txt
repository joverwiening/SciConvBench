The metal is illuminated by light with a wavelength of 240 nm.
The task is to determine if photoelectric emission will occur.
Calculate the energy of a single photon from the illuminating light using the relation E = hc/λ.
Use the value hc = 1240 eV·nm for the calculation.
Compare the calculated photon energy to the 4.8 eV work function of the metal.
State a final conclusion on whether emission will occur based on the comparison.
