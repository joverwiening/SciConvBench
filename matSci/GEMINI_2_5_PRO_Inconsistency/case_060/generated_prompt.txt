The task is to calculate the Vickers hardness number.
The applied load is 500 grams.
The resulting indentation has a diagonal of 0.42 millimeters.
The indenter is a 136-degree diamond pyramid.
Use the standard Vickers hardness formula HV = 1.854 * (F / d^2).
The load F must be in units of kilogram-force (kgf).
The diagonal d must be in units of millimeters (mm).
Convert the 500 gram load to kilogram-force.
Substitute the values for F and d into the formula.
Calculate and report the resulting Vickers hardness number.
