The expectation of complete substitutional solid solubility is incorrect.
The Hume-Rothery size factor rule is the basis for this assessment.
This rule states that for complete solid solubility, the difference in atomic radii between the two elements must be less than 15%.
The stated 25% difference significantly exceeds this threshold.
A large atomic size difference introduces substantial lattice strain into the crystal structure.
This lattice strain increases the internal energy of the solid solution, making it energetically unfavorable to form.
Consequently, the two metals are expected to have negligible or very limited solid solubility, not complete solubility.
