The system is a miscible polymer blend.
The blend consists of 50 wt% component A and 50 wt% component B.
The glass transition temperature of component A is 373 K.
The glass transition temperature of component B is 293 K.
The Fox equation is used to estimate the blend's glass transition temperature.
The equation is 1/T_g = (w_A / T_g,A) + (w_B / T_g,B).
The calculation is performed using temperatures in Kelvin.
The final estimated glass transition temperature is reported in Celsius.
