The problem is to calculate the time required for a carburization process.
The material is a 5.0 mm thick steel plate.
The plate is treated as a semi-infinite solid for the diffusion calculation.
The carburization is performed from one face of the plate.
The initial carbon concentration of the steel is 0.2 wt%.
The carburization process is conducted at a constant temperature of 927 degrees Celsius.
A constant carbon concentration of 0.9 wt% is maintained at the surface of the plate.
The objective is to find the time required to achieve a target carbon concentration of 0.4 wt%.
This target concentration must be reached at a depth of 0.8 mm from the surface.
The calculation should use the error function solution to Fick's second law of diffusion.
