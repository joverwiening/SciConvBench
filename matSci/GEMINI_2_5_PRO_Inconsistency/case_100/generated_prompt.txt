A negative standard Gibbs free-energy change (ΔG°) indicates a spontaneous reaction.
Spontaneous reactions favor the formation of products at equilibrium.
The equilibrium constant (K) for a reaction that favors products is greater than 1.
Given that ΔG° is -12 kJ/mol, the reaction is spontaneous.
Therefore, the equilibrium constant K should be greater than 1.
The stated value of K = 0.008 is inconsistent with the provided ΔG°.
