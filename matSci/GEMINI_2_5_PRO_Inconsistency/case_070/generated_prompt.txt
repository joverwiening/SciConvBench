A miscible polymer blend must exhibit a single glass transition temperature (Tg).
The provided differential scanning calorimetry (DSC) data shows two distinct glass transitions.
The observed glass transitions are at 315 K and 375 K.
These temperatures correspond to the glass transition temperatures of the individual pure polymer components.
The presence of two distinct Tgs, identical to the pure components, indicates the blend is phase-separated.
Therefore, the polymer blend is assessed as immiscible based on the experimental data.
The initial description of the blend as "fully miscible" is contradicted by the DSC results.
