Calculate the critical magnetic field, H_c(T), for a type-I superconductor.
The critical magnetic field at 0 K, H_c(0), is 0.080 T.
The critical temperature, T_c, is 7.2 K.
The calculation will be performed at a temperature, T, of 4.0 K.
Use the formula H_c(T) = H_c(0) * [1 - (T / T_c)^2].
The final result is the value of H_c(4.0 K).
