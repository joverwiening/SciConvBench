Calculate the electrical resistivity of a wire.
The wire has a length of 0.492 meters.
The wire has a diameter of 1.944 millimeters.
The wire has an electrical resistance of 11.705 ohms.
Calculate the circular cross-sectional area using the formula A = π(d/2)², where 'd' is the diameter.
Calculate the electrical resistivity using the formula ρ = RA/L, where R is resistance, A is the cross-sectional area, and L is the length.
The final result for electrical resistivity must be expressed in ohm-meters.
