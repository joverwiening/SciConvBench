The particles are assumed to be solid, non-porous spheres.
The material for both powders is identical, implying they have the same density.
Specific surface area is defined as the total surface area per unit of mass.
For a sphere, specific surface area is inversely proportional to its diameter.
Therefore, the powder with the smaller particle diameter will have the higher specific surface area.
The 0.5 μm powder has a higher specific surface area than the 5 μm powder.
The specific surface area of the 0.5 μm powder is 10 times greater than that of the 5 μm powder.
