The material is a paramagnet.
The magnetic susceptibility of the material is governed by the Curie Law, χ = C/T.
In this equation, C is the positive Curie constant and T is the absolute temperature.
As temperature increases, the magnetic susceptibility must decrease.
The initial statement that susceptibility increases as temperature rises from 300 K to 600 K is incorrect.
The correct trend is that susceptibility is inversely proportional to temperature.
