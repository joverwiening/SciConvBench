A binary diffusion couple is prepared with an initial concentration of 20 at% B on the left side and 80 at% B on the right side.
The total length of the diffusion couple is assumed to be 10 mm.
After an annealing process, a concentration profile is formed.
The profile width is defined as the spatial distance between the location of the 20 at% B concentration and the location of the 80 at% B concentration.
A reasonable value for this measured profile width is assumed to be 200 micrometers.
The interdiffusion distance is estimated to be equal to the profile width.
The final estimated interdiffusion distance is 200 micrometers.
