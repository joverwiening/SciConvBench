The work of adhesion, W_A, is defined by the Young-Dupré equation as W_A = γ_LV * (1 + cos(θ)).
The liquid-vapor surface tension, γ_LV, is given as 72.8 mN/m, which is a positive value.
The contact angle, θ, is given as 30°.
For any real contact angle, the term (1 + cos(θ)) is greater than or equal to zero.
Since the contact angle is 30°, the term (1 + cos(30°)) is positive.
The work of adhesion is the product of two positive quantities, γ_LV and (1 + cos(θ)).
Therefore, the correct sign for the work of adhesion must be positive.
The reported value of W_A = -10 mJ/m^2 is physically incorrect.
