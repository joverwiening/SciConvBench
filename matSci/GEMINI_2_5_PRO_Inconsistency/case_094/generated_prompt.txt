The mass ordering is physically inconsistent.
The saturated mass (13.1 g) is correctly greater than the dry mass (12.4 g), as it includes the mass of the fluid absorbed into the pores.
The immersed mass is the apparent mass of the saturated object when submerged and is equal to the saturated mass minus the buoyant force.
Since the buoyant force must be positive, the immersed mass must be less than the saturated mass.
The provided immersed mass (13.6 g) is greater than the saturated mass (13.1 g), which violates physical principles.
