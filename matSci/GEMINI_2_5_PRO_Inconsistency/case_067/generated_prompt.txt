Estimate the degree of crystallinity of the semicrystalline polymer.
The measured density of the polymer is 1.22 g/cm^3.
The amorphous density of the polymer is 1.05 g/cm^3.
The crystalline density of the polymer is 1.30 g/cm^3.
Calculate the degree of crystallinity as a weight percentage.
Use the formula: %Crystallinity = [ρ_c * (ρ_s - ρ_a)] / [ρ_s * (ρ_c - ρ_a)] * 100, where ρ_c is crystalline density, ρ_s is measured density, and ρ_a is amorphous density.
