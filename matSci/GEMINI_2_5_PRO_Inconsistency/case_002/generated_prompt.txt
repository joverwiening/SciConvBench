Calculate the cubic lattice parameter 'a'.
The theoretical density (ρ) is 18.499 g/cm^3.
The atomic weight (A) is 76.45 g/mol.
The number of atoms per unit cell (n) is 4.
Avogadro's number (N_A) is 6.022 x 10^23 atoms/mol.
Use the formula ρ = nA / (N_A * a^3) to solve for 'a'.
The final result for the lattice parameter 'a' must be expressed in nanometers (nm).
