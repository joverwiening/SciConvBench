The goal is to estimate the carburizing time for a steel surface.
The model used is the semi-infinite diffusion solution.
The initial carbon concentration of the steel is 0.20 wt% C.
The surface of the steel is held at a constant carbon concentration of 0.90 wt% C.
The target carbon concentration is 0.60 wt% C at a depth of 0.6 mm.
The carburizing process temperature is assumed to be 927°C (1200 K).
The diffusion coefficient for carbon in austenite at this temperature is calculated using standard material constants.
The pre-exponential factor (D0) is 2.3 x 10^-5 m^2/s.
The activation energy (Qd) is 148,000 J/mol.
The resulting diffusion coefficient (D) is 8.35 x 10^-12 m^2/s.
The required carburizing time is calculated to be approximately 18.7 hours.
