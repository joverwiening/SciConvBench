The relative permittivity of a passive dielectric material must be greater than or equal to 1.
A value less than 1 would imply the material's permittivity is lower than that of a vacuum, which is physically impossible for a passive medium.
The reported relative permittivity is 0.82.
This value is less than 1.
Therefore, the reported value of 0.82 for a passive ceramic capacitor is not physically consistent.
