The reaction is FePO_4 + Li^+ + e^- ↔ LiFePO_4.
In the reactant FePO_4, the oxidation state of iron (Fe) is +3.
In the product LiFePO_4, the oxidation state of iron (Fe) is +2.
The change in the oxidation state of iron from +3 to +2 requires the gain of one electron.
Therefore, the correct number of electrons (n) transferred per formula unit for this reaction is 1.
