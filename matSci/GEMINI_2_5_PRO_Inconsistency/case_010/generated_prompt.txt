The system is a composite wall with two layers arranged in series.
The heat transfer is steady-state and one-dimensional.
Layer 1 has a thickness of 9.74 mm.
Layer 1 has a thermal conductivity of 12.31 W/m·K.
Layer 2 has a thickness of 13.27 mm.
Layer 2 has a thermal conductivity of 107.49 W/m·K.
The total area of the wall for heat transfer is 0.0725 m^2.
The outer surface of one layer is maintained at a constant temperature of 198.1°C.
The outer surface of the other layer is maintained at a constant temperature of -25.4°C.
The thermal contact resistance between the two layers is neglected.
The objective is to calculate the total heat transfer rate through the wall in Watts.
The calculation must treat the layers as thermal resistances in series.
