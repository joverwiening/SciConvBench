The Nernst equation for the Cu^2+/Cu electrode at 25°C is E = E° + (0.0592/2) * log(a_Cu2+).
When the activity of Cu^2+ is 1, the term log(a_Cu2+) is 0, and the potential E equals the standard potential E°.
When the activity of Cu^2+ is lowered to 10^-2, the term log(a_Cu2+) is -2.
The potential E becomes E° + (0.0592/2) * (-2), which simplifies to E° - 0.0592 V.
The resulting potential is more negative than the standard potential E°.
The correct trend is that lowering the activity of Cu^2+ makes the reduction potential more negative.
