The Basquin equation relates stress amplitude (S) to cycles to failure (N) in the form S = A * N^b.
The first data point is a stress amplitude of 260 MPa at 10^5 cycles to failure.
The second data point is a stress amplitude of 180 MPa at 10^7 cycles to failure.
Calculate the fatigue strength exponent (b) using these two data points.
Calculate the fatigue strength coefficient (A) using these two data points.
The resulting fatigue strength exponent (b) is -0.080.
The resulting fatigue strength coefficient (A) is 653 MPa.
