Identify the task as a lever rule calculation for a binary alloy.
The alloy is hypoeutectic.
The overall alloy composition is C0 = 14.36 wt% B.
The composition of the solid alpha phase just above the eutectic temperature is C_α = 4.63 wt% B.
The composition of the liquid phase just above the eutectic temperature is C_L = 40.31 wt% B.
Calculate the weight fraction of the liquid phase, W_L, using the lever rule.
The formula for the weight fraction of liquid is W_L = (C0 - C_α) / (C_L - C_α).
This calculated weight fraction of liquid is equivalent to the weight fraction of the eutectic microconstituent that forms upon cooling.
Provide the final calculated value as the answer.
