Use the Larson–Miller relation P = T(C + log10 t_r).
Set P = 23500, T = 973 K, and C = 20.
Rearrange to log10 t_r = (P/T) − C.
Compute (P/T) = 23500/973 ≈ 24.151.
Compute log10 t_r ≈ 24.151 − 20 = 4.151.
Compute t_r = 10^4.151 ≈ 1.42 × 10^4 hours.
Report the estimated rupture life as approximately 14,200 hours.
