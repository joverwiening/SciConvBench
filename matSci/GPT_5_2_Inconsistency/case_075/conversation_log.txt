Q: For the transverse modulus of a unidirectional composite, do not use the Voigt (iso-strain) rule of mixtures E_T = V_f E_f + V_m E_m.
Use the Reuss (iso-stress) relation for transverse loading: 1/E_T = V_f/E_f + V_m/E_m.
Use V_f = 0.60 and V_m = 0.40.
Use E_f = 230 GPa and E_m = 3.5 GPa.
Compute 1/E_T = 0.60/230 + 0.40/3.5 = 0.0026087 + 0.1142857 = 0.1168944 (1/GPa).
Compute E_T = 1/0.1168944 = 8.55 GPa (approximately).
A: Use the **inverse rule of mixtures (Reuss/iso-stress)** for transverse modulus:

\[
\frac{1}{E_T}=\frac{V_f}{E_f}+\frac{V_m}{E_m}
\]

With \(V_f=0.60,\; V_m=0.40,\; E_f=230\text{ GPa},\; E_m=3.5\text{ GPa}\):

\[
\frac{1}{E_T}=\frac{0.60}{230}+\frac{0.40}{3.5}=0.0026087+0.1142857=0.1168944\;(\text{GPa}^{-1})
\]

\[
E_T=\frac{1}{0.1168944}=8.55\text{ GPa}\;(\approx 8.6\text{ GPa})
\]
