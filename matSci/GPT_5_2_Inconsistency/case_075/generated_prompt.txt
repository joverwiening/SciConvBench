Use the inverse rule of mixtures (Reuss/iso-stress) for the transverse modulus of a unidirectional composite.
Use 1/E_T = V_f/E_f + V_m/E_m.
Use V_f = 0.60 and V_m = 0.40.
Use E_f = 230 GPa and E_m = 3.5 GPa.
Compute 1/E_T = 0.60/230 + 0.40/3.5 = 0.0026087 + 0.1142857 = 0.1168944 GPa^-1.
Compute E_T = 1/0.1168944 = 8.55 GPa (approximately 8.6 GPa).
