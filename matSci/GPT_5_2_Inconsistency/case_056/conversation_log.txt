Q: What is the definition of φ and λ you want me to use (i.e., φ = angle between tensile axis and slip-plane normal, and λ = angle between tensile axis and slip direction)?
A: Use the standard Schmid-law definitions:

- **φ** = angle between the **tensile axis** and the **slip-plane normal**  
- **λ** = angle between the **tensile axis** and the **slip direction**
