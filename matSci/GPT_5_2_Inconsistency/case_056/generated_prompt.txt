Use Schmid’s law with τRSS = σ cos(φ) cos(λ).
Use φ = 30° and λ = 45°.
Compute the Schmid factor m = cos(30°) cos(45°) = (0.8660)(0.7071) ≈ 0.612.
Compute the resolved shear stress at σ = 100 MPa as τRSS = 100 MPa × 0.612 ≈ 61.2 MPa.
Note that φ = 30° and λ = 45° do not produce the maximum Schmid factor of 0.5; the maximum m = 0.5 occurs at φ = λ = 45°.
