Use the Young–Dupré relation with the convention that W_A is the work required to separate the liquid from the solid.
Compute W_A = γ_LV(1 + cos θ).
With γ_LV = 72.8 mN/m and θ = 30°, cos 30° = 0.866.
Compute W_A = 72.8 mN/m × (1 + 0.866) = 135.8 mN/m = 135.8 mJ/m^2.
Conclude W_A must be positive under this convention.
State that a reported value W_A = −10 mJ/m^2 has the wrong sign for this definition.
