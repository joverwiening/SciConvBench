Q: What thermal conductivity value should I treat as the baseline “fully dense” reference for calculating the reduction: 2.0 W/m·K as listed, or 3.5 W/m·K as the higher value at 30% porosity?
A: Use **5.0 W/m·K** as the baseline fully dense reference (not 2.0). The porous value is **3.5 W/m·K at 30% porosity**.
