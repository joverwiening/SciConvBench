Compute the elastomer modulus using E = 3 ν_e R T.
Use ν_e = 4.0×10^-5 mol/cm^3.
Convert ν_e to SI units: ν_e = 4.0×10^-5 mol/cm^3 = 40 mol/m^3.
Use T = 298 K.
Use R = 8.314 J·mol⁻¹·K⁻¹.
Report E in SI pressure units (Pa) and also in MPa as a best-practice engineering unit.
Calculate E = 3 × 40 × 8.314 × 298 Pa = 2.97×10^5 Pa = 0.297 MPa.
