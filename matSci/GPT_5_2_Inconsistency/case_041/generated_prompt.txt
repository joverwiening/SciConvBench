Use the Arrhenius relation D = D0 exp(−Qd/(R T)).
Use R = 8.314 J/(mol·K).
Use the two data points: D1 = 1.0×10^-12 m^2/s at T1 = 800 K, and D2 = 2.0×10^-15 m^2/s at T2 = 1100 K.
Eliminate D0 by taking the ratio: ln(D1/D2) = (Qd/R)(1/T2 − 1/T1).
Compute ln(D1/D2) = ln((1.0×10^-12)/(2.0×10^-15)) = ln(500) = 6.2146.
Compute (1/T2 − 1/T1) = (1/1100 − 1/800) = −3.4091×10^-4 K^-1.
Solve Qd = R·ln(D1/D2)/(1/T2 − 1/T1) = 8.314×6.2146/(−3.4091×10^-4) = −1.515×10^5 J/mol.
Report the activation energy magnitude as Qd = 1.52×10^5 J/mol = 152 kJ/mol.
