Q: Do you want the resolved shear stress computed using Schmid’s law τ = σ cosφ cosλ?
A: Yes—use Schmid’s law: \( \tau = \sigma \cos\phi \cos\lambda \), with \(\sigma = 80\) MPa, \(\phi = 45^\circ\), and \(\lambda = 45^\circ\) (for the FCC \(\{111\}\langle 110\rangle\) slip system).
