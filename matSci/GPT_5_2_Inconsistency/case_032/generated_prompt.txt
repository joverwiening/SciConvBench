Use Schmid’s law τ = σ cosφ cosλ.
Set σ = 80 MPa, φ = 45°, and λ = 45°.
Compute cos45° = √2/2 ≈ 0.7071.
Compute the Schmid factor m = cosφ cosλ = 0.7071 × 0.7071 = 0.5.
Compute the resolved shear stress τ = 80 MPa × 0.5 = 40 MPa.
Report the resolved shear stress on the specified slip system as 40 MPa.
