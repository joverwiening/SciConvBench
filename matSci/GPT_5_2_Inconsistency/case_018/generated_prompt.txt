Use the central crack Griffith relation σ_f = sqrt(2Eγ_s/(πa)).
Use σ_f = 529.7 MPa = 5.297×10^8 Pa.
Use E = 240.3 GPa = 2.403×10^11 Pa.
Use γ_s = 2.576 J/m^2.
Rearrange to a = 2Eγ_s/(πσ_f^2).
Compute a = (2×2.403×10^11×2.576)/(π×(5.297×10^8)^2) m.
Evaluate a ≈ 1.404×10^-6 m.
Convert to micrometers: a ≈ 1.404 μm.
