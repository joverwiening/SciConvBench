Use the Hall–Petch relation σ_y = σ_0 + k_y d^(-1/2).
Take σ_0 = 120 MPa.
Take k_y = +0.70 MPa·m^(1/2).
Use grain size d = 9 μm = 9×10^(-6) m.
Compute d^(-1/2) = 1/√(9×10^(-6) m) = 1/(3×10^(-3) m^(1/2)) = 333.33 m^(-1/2).
Compute the Hall–Petch increment k_y d^(-1/2) = (0.70 MPa·m^(1/2))(333.33 m^(-1/2)) = 233.33 MPa.
Compute yield strength σ_y = 120 MPa + 233.33 MPa = 353.33 MPa.
Report the estimated yield strength as approximately 353 MPa (≈ 3.53×10^2 MPa).
