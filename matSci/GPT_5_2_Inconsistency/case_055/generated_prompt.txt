Use the isotropic linear-elastic consistency relation G = E / (2(1+ν)).
Substitute E = 210 GPa and ν = 0.30 to get Gcalc = 210 / (2(1.30)) = 210 / 2.6 = 80.77 GPa.
Compare Gcalc = 80.77 GPa to the assigned G = 60 GPa.
Conclude the constants are not mutually consistent because the assigned shear modulus differs from the isotropic value by 20.77 GPa (about 25.7% low relative to 80.77 GPa).
