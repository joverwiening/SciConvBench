Use the standard definition: relative density = bulk (apparent) density ÷ theoretical (fully dense) density.
Interpret total porosity as the total pore volume fraction in the bulk.
Compute solid volume fraction as 1 − porosity = 1 − 0.12 = 0.88.
For a porous body, bulk density equals theoretical density times the solid fraction, so relative density should equal 0.88.
