Use the direct-allowed Tauc relation (αhν) ∝ (hν − Eg)^(1/2).
Plot (αhν)^2 versus hν for a direct allowed transition.
This corresponds to using n = 1/2 in the common form (αhν)^(1/n) versus hν.
Do not use (αhν)^(1/2) versus hν for a direct allowed gap, because that corresponds to an indirect allowed transition (n = 2).
