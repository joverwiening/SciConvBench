Use standard reduction potentials for Zn2+/Zn and Cu2+/Cu under standard conditions.
Assign the anode as the electrode where oxidation occurs.
Assign the cathode as the electrode where reduction occurs.
Oxidation occurs at the zinc electrode: Zn(s) → Zn2+(aq) + 2e−, so Zn is the anode.
Reduction occurs at the copper electrode: Cu2+(aq) + 2e− → Cu(s), so Cu is the cathode.
Conclude that labeling Cu as the anode and Zn as the cathode is incorrect for a standard Zn/Cu galvanic cell.
