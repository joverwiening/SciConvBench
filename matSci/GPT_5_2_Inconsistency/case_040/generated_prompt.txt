Use cubic fluorite ZrO2 with Z = 8 formula units per unit cell.
Use lattice parameter a = 0.509 nm.
Use molar mass M(ZrO2) = 123.22 g/mol.
Convert a to cm: a = 0.509 nm = 0.509 × 10^-7 cm = 5.09 × 10^-8 cm.
Compute unit-cell volume: V = a^3 = (5.09 × 10^-8 cm)^3 = 1.318 × 10^-22 cm^3.
Use Avogadro’s number NA = 6.022 × 10^23 mol^-1.
Compute mass per unit cell: m = (Z × M) / NA = (8 × 123.22 g/mol) / (6.022 × 10^23 mol^-1) = 1.637 × 10^-21 g.
Compute theoretical density: ρ = m / V = (1.637 × 10^-21 g) / (1.318 × 10^-22 cm^3) = 12.42 g/cm^3.
