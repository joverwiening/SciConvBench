Q: In Bragg’s law, should I treat “use n = 2” as the diffraction order (n) even though you also say “first-order reflection,” or should I use n = 1 for first order?
A: Use **n = 1** (first-order reflection). Ignore the “use n = 2” wording—it’s not part of the complete requirement.
