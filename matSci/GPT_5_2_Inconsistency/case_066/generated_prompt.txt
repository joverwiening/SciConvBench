Use the Fox equation in absolute temperature: 1/Tg = wA/Tg,A + wB/Tg,B.
Set wA = 0.5 and wB = 0.5.
Use Tg,A = 373 K and Tg,B = 293 K.
Compute 1/Tg = 0.5/373 + 0.5/293 = 0.003052 K^-1 (approximately).
Compute Tg = 1/0.003052 = 327.6 K (approximately).
Convert to °C: Tg = 327.6 − 273.15 = 54.4 °C (approximately).
