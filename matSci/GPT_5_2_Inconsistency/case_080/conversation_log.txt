Q: Which value should we treat as authoritative for intrinsic silicon at 300 K: n_i = 1.0×10^16 m^-3 or n = p = 1.0×10^20 m^-3?
A: Treat **\(n_i = 1.0\times 10^{16}\ \text{m}^{-3}\)** as authoritative for intrinsic silicon at 300 K. Therefore the correct intrinsic carrier concentrations are:

- \(n = n_i = 1.0\times 10^{16}\ \text{m}^{-3}\)
- \(p = n_i = 1.0\times 10^{16}\ \text{m}^{-3}\)
