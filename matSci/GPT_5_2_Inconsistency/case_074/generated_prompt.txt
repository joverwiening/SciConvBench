Compute the longitudinal modulus E_L of a unidirectional composite using the rule of mixtures.
Use E_f = 230 GPa, E_m = 3 GPa, and V_f = 0.60.
Compute the matrix volume fraction as V_m = 1 − V_f.
Compute E_L = E_f·V_f + E_m·V_m.
Ignore any separately “reported” longitudinal modulus value.
Report E_L in GPa.
