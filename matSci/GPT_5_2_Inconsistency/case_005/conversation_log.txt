Q: Do you want the Arrhenius form to be treated as D = D0·exp(−Qd/(R·T)) (the physically standard form) or exactly as written D = D0·exp(Qd/(R·T))?
A: Use the physically standard Arrhenius form:

\[
D = D_0 \exp\!\left(-\frac{Q_d}{RT}\right)
\]

(i.e., the exponent has a negative sign).
