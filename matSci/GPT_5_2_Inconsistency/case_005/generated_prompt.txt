Use the Arrhenius relation D = D0·exp(−Qd/(R·T)).
Given D1 = 4.76×10^-18 m^2/s at T1 = 866 K, and D2 = 1.86×10^-12 m^2/s at T2 = 1438 K.
Compute Qd from ln(D2/D1) = (Qd/R)·(1/T1 − 1/T2).
Calculate ln(D2/D1) = ln(1.86×10^-12 / 4.76×10^-18) = ln(3.9076×10^5) = 12.875.
Calculate (1/T1 − 1/T2) = (1/866 − 1/1438) = 0.0004589 K^-1.
Compute Qd = R·ln(D2/D1) / (1/T1 − 1/T2) = 8.314×12.875 / 0.0004589 = 2.33×10^5 J/mol.
Report activation energy Qd ≈ 233 kJ/mol.
