Use Bragg’s law nλ = 2d sin(θ) with n = 1.
Substitute λ = 0.30 nm and d = 0.12 nm to get sin(θ) = λ/(2d) = 0.30/(0.24) = 1.25.
Since sin(θ) cannot exceed 1, no first-order Bragg reflection is physically possible for these values.
Therefore, the diffraction angle 2θ is not defined (no real solution).
