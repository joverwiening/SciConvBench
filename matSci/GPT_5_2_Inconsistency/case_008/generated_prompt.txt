Use a hypereutectic binary eutectic system at the eutectic temperature Te.
Given overall composition C0 = 14.36 wt% B.
Given eutectic liquid composition Ce = 40.31 wt% B.
Given α composition at Te is Cαe = 4.63 wt% B.
Assume β composition at Te is Cβe ≈ 100 wt% B (not needed for the asked liquid fraction just above Te).
Interpret “just above the eutectic temperature” for a hypereutectic alloy as the two-phase field L + α.
Compute the weight fraction of liquid just above Te using the lever rule on the L + α tie line at Te.
Set wL = (C0 − Cαe) / (Ce − Cαe).
Substitute values: wL = (14.36 − 4.63) / (40.31 − 4.63).
Compute numerator = 9.73.
Compute denominator = 35.68.
Compute wL = 9.73 / 35.68 = 0.2727.
Report the liquid weight fraction just above Te as wL ≈ 0.273 (≈ 27.3 wt%).
State that this same fraction (≈ 27.3 wt%) will transform into the eutectic microconstituent upon cooling through Te.
