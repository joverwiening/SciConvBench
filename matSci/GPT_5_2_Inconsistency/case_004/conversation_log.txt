Q: What crystal structure should be assumed to determine the number of atomic sites N (e.g., FCC, BCC, HCP), or should N be taken as the total number of atoms per m^3 computed directly from density and atomic weight (i.e., N = ρ N_A / A)?
A: Take **N as the total number of atomic sites per m³ computed directly from density and atomic weight**, i.e.:

\[
N=\frac{\rho N_A}{A}
\]

No crystal structure assumption (FCC/BCC/HCP) is needed.
