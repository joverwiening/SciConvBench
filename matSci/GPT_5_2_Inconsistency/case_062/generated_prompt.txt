Write a short, exam-style qualitative paragraph discussing how thermal expansion coefficient affects thermal-shock resistance for a ceramic.
State that in R ∝ σ_f k/(E α), increasing α (with σ_f, k, and E fixed) decreases R and therefore worsens thermal-shock resistance.
Explain qualitatively that higher α produces larger thermal strains for a given temperature change, so temperature gradients during shock create larger strain mismatch and higher thermal stresses.
Conclude that higher thermal stresses increase the likelihood of crack initiation/propagation, reducing thermal-shock resistance.
