Q: What is the exact value you want used for the Faraday-law formula: m = (Q·M)/(n·F) with n = 4, or do you want m = (Q·M)/F exactly as written?
A: Use the correct Faraday-law form **including** the electron stoichiometry:  
\[
m=\frac{Q\,M}{nF}
\]
with **n = 4** and **F = 96485 C/mol**.
