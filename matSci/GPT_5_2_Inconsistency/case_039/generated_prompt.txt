Use vacancy formation energy Q_v = +0.85 eV per atom.
Use temperature T = 1200 K.
Use Boltzmann constant k = 8.617333262×10^-5 eV/K.
Compute kT = k×T = 0.10341 eV.
Compute exponent = -Q_v/(kT) = -0.85/0.10341 = -8.218.
Compute equilibrium vacancy fraction N_v/N = exp(exponent) = exp(-8.218) = 2.70×10^-4.
