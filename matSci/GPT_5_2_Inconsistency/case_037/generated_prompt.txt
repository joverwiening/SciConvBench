Use a cubic crystal with lattice parameter a = 0.360 nm.
Use interplanar spacing for the (100) planes as d(100) = 0.360 nm.
Assume the candidate structure to verify is simple cubic.
Apply the cubic interplanar spacing relation d(hkl) = a / sqrt(h^2 + k^2 + l^2).
Substitute (hkl) = (100) to obtain d(100) = a / sqrt(1) = a.
Compute d(100) from a as 0.360 nm and compare it to the given d(100) = 0.360 nm.
Conclude the values are consistent with a simple cubic structure.
