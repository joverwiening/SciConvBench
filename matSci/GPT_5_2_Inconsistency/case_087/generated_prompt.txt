Compute the type-I critical field using \(H_c(T)=H_c(0)\left[1-\left(\frac{T}{T_c}\right)^2\right]\).

Compute \(H_c(4.0\ \text{K})=0.080\,[1-0.3086]\approx0.080\times0.6914\approx0.0553\ \text{T}\).
