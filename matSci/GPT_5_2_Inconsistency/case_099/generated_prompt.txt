Use the balanced intercalation reaction FePO4 + Li+ + e− ↔ LiFePO4.
Assign oxidation states: in FePO4, Fe is +3 (PO4 is −3 overall); in LiFePO4, Li is +1 and PO4 is −3 overall, so Fe is +2.
The Fe3+ → Fe2+ reduction corresponds to transfer of exactly 1 electron per FePO4 (and per LiFePO4) formula unit.
Set n = 1 electron per formula unit for theoretical specific capacity calculations for this reaction.
