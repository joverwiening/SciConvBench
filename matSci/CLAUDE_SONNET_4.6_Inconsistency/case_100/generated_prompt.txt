The task is to determine whether the equilibrium constant K is greater or less than 1, given ΔG° = -12 kJ/mol at T = 298 K, and to calculate K using the correct relationship.

Use the relationship ΔG° = -RT ln(K).
Use R = 8.314 J/(mol·K) and T = 298 K.
Ignore the stated value K = 0.008 entirely, as it is contradictory and not part of the problem.
Rearrange the equation to ln(K) = -ΔG° / (RT).
Substitute the values: ln(K) = -(-12,000 J/mol) / (8.314 J/(mol·K) × 298 K).
Calculate the denominator: 8.314 × 298 = 2477.572 J/mol.
Calculate ln(K) = 12,000 / 2477.572 ≈ 4.843.
Calculate K = e^4.843 ≈ 127.
Since ΔG° is negative, ln(K) is positive, which means K is greater than 1.
