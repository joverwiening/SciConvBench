The task is an academic mathematical consistency check of two reported values for a sintered oxide material.
The two reported values are: relative density = 1.08 and total porosity = 12% (i.e., 0.12).
Use the standard relationship between relative density and total porosity: relative density + total porosity = 1, or equivalently, total porosity = 1 − relative density.
Substitute the reported relative density into the standard relationship to calculate the expected total porosity.
Expected total porosity = 1 − 1.08 = −0.08, which is −8%.
Compare the calculated expected total porosity (−8%) with the reported total porosity (12%).
Since −8% ≠ 12%, the two values are not consistent with each other.
Also note that a relative density of 1.08 implies the material is 8% denser than the theoretical density, which is physically impossible for a sintered oxide (relative density cannot exceed 1.0 for a real material).
Similarly, a negative porosity of −8% is physically meaningless.
Conclude that the two reported values cannot both be correct and are mutually inconsistent under standard porosity-density relationships.
State that at least one of the reported values must be in error.
