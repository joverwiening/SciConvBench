Perform a Vickers hardness calculation using a square-based diamond pyramid indenter with a 136° included face angle.
The applied load is 500 gf (0.5 kgf).
The measured indentation diagonal is 0.42 mm.
Since the Vickers indenter produces a square indentation, two diagonals are measured and averaged; here a single diagonal value of 0.42 mm is given, so use d = 0.42 mm as the mean diagonal.
Use the standard Vickers hardness formula: HV = 1.8544 × F / d², where F is the load in kgf and d is the mean diagonal in mm.
Substitute values: HV = 1.8544 × 0.5 / (0.42)².
Calculate d² = 0.42 × 0.42 = 0.1764 mm².
Calculate HV = 0.9272 / 0.1764 = approximately 5.26 × 10¹ HV.
Round the result to a reasonable number of significant figures consistent with the given data.
Report the final answer as HV ≈ 53 (in units of kgf/mm², which is the conventional Vickers hardness number).
