The crystal structure is FCC with n = 4 atoms per unit cell.
The atomic weight A is 197.35 g/mol.
The theoretical density ρ is 7.754 g/cm³.
Avogadro's number N_A is 6.022 × 10²³ atoms/mol.
Use the density formula ρ = nA / (N_A · a³) to solve for the lattice parameter a.
Rearrange the formula to a³ = nA / (N_A · ρ).
Substitute the values: a³ = (4 × 197.35) / (6.022 × 10²³ × 7.754).
Compute the numerator: 4 × 197.35 = 789.4 g/mol.
Compute the denominator: 6.022 × 10²³ × 7.754 = 4.670 × 10²⁴ g/cm³ per mol.
Divide: a³ = 789.4 / (4.670 × 10²⁴) = 1.6904 × 10⁻²² cm³.
Take the cube root: a = (1.6904 × 10⁻²²)^(1/3).
Calculate: a ≈ 5.529 × 10⁻⁸ cm.
Convert to nanometers by multiplying by 10⁷ nm/cm: a ≈ 0.5529 nm.
Report the final answer as a ≈ 0.553 nm.
