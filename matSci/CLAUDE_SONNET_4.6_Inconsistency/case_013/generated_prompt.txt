Calculate the number-average molecular weight M_n for a polymer sample using the formula M_n = Σ(N_i × M_i) / Σ(N_i).

The data is as follows:
N1 = 96, M1 = 56526 g/mol
N2 = 131, M2 = 92809 g/mol
N3 = 153, M3 = 115856 g/mol
N4 = 146, M4 = 126029 g/mol

Compute the numerator as the sum of each N_i multiplied by its corresponding M_i.
Compute the denominator as the sum of all N_i values.
Divide the numerator by the denominator to obtain M_n in g/mol.
Report the final value of M_n in g/mol.
