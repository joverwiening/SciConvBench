Q: I need to flag a contradiction in your request before proceeding.

The standard definition of number-average molecular weight is:

M_n = Σ(N_i × M_i) / Σ(N_i)

However, you have specified the formula:

M_n = Σ(N_i × M_i²) / Σ(N_i × M_i)

These are two different formulas. The one you wrote, Σ(N_i M_i²) / Σ(N_i M_i), is actually the standard definition of the weight-average molecular weight (M_w).

Which formula do you want applied to your data — the one you explicitly wrote (which gives M_w), or the standard M_n formula Σ(N_i M_i) / Σ(N_i)?
A: Use the standard M_n formula: Σ(N_i × M_i) / Σ(N_i).
