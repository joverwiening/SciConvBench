ρ_a = 0.8845 g/cm^3 (density of completely amorphous state)
ρ_c = 1.4240 g/cm^3 (density of completely crystalline state)

Use the formula exactly as given:
X_c = [ρ_c × (ρ_s - ρ_a)] / [ρ_s × (ρ_c - ρ_a)] × 100

1.1973 - 0.8845 = 0.3128

Step 2: Multiply by ρ_c.
1.4240 × 0.3128 = 0.445427

Step 3: Compute the denominator term (ρ_c - ρ_a).
1.4240 - 0.8845 = 0.5395

Step 4: Multiply by ρ_s.
1.1973 × 0.5395 = 0.645933

Step 5: Divide numerator by denominator.
0.445427 / 0.645933 = 0.68955

Step 6: Multiply by 100 to get percent.
0.68955 × 100 = 68.955%
