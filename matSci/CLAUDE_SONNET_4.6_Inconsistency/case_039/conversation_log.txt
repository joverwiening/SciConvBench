Q: The vacancy formation energy Q_v = -0.85 eV/atom is negative, which is physically incorrect.

Vacancy formation energy must be positive because energy is required to remove an atom from a lattice site and place it on the surface — a negative value would imply vacancies form spontaneously without any energy cost, which is unphysical.

Should I treat Q_v as a positive value of 0.85 eV/atom (a likely sign error), or did you intend a different value entirely?
A: Yes, it's a sign error. Use Q_v = +0.85 eV/atom.
