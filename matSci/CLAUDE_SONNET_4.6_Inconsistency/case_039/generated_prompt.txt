Calculate the equilibrium vacancy fraction for a metal using the formula N_v/N = exp(-Q_v / (k * T)).
Use a vacancy formation energy Q_v = 0.85 eV/atom.
Use temperature T = 1200 K.
Use Boltzmann's constant k = 8.617 × 10^-5 eV/K.
Compute the exponent as -Q_v / (k * T) = -0.85 / (8.617 × 10^-5 × 1200).
Evaluate k * T = 0.10340 eV.
Evaluate the exponent = -0.85 / 0.10340 = -8.2205.
Evaluate N_v/N = exp(-8.2205) ≈ 2.69 × 10^-4.
Report the equilibrium vacancy fraction as approximately 2.69 × 10^-4 (roughly 1 vacancy per 3700 lattice sites).
