The specimen is cylindrical and loaded in uniaxial tension.
The original diameter is d = 18.16 mm.
The gauge length is L₀ = 37.69 mm.
The applied load is F = 18.90 kN = 18900 N.
The increase in gauge length under that load is ΔL = 0.0206 mm.
The test is in the elastic regime and linear elasticity is assumed.
Compute the original cross-sectional area using A₀ = π × d² / 4, with d in metres.
Convert d to metres: d = 18.16 × 10⁻³ m.
Compute A₀ = π × (18.16 × 10⁻³)² / 4 in m².
Compute engineering stress σ = F / A₀ in Pascals.
Compute engineering strain ε = ΔL / L₀ = 0.0206 / 37.69 (dimensionless, both in mm).
Compute Young's modulus E = σ / ε in Pascals.
Convert E to GPa by dividing by 10⁹.
Report the final value of Young's modulus in GPa.
