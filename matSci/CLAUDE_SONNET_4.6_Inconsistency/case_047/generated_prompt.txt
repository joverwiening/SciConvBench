The steel contains 0.95 wt% C, which places it in the hypereutectoid region of the iron-carbon phase diagram (eutectoid point is at 0.76 wt% C).
On slow cooling from the austenite region, the steel crosses the Acm line before reaching the eutectoid temperature.
At the Acm line, the solubility limit of carbon in austenite is exceeded.
The proeutectoid phase that forms is cementite (Fe3C), also called iron carbide.
Cementite precipitates at the prior austenite grain boundaries as a network before the remaining austenite transforms to pearlite at the eutectoid temperature.
The proeutectoid phase expected on slow cooling for a 0.95 wt% C hypereutectoid plain-carbon steel is proeutectoid cementite (Fe3C).
