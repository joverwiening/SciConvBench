The elastomer has a crosslink density ν_e = 4.0×10^-5 mol/cm^3.
The temperature is T = 298 K.
The gas constant is R = 8.314 J/(mol·K).
Use the formula E ≈ 3ν_eRT to estimate the Young's modulus of the elastomer.
Convert ν_e to SI units: 4.0×10^-5 mol/cm^3 = 4.0×10^-5 × 10^6 mol/m^3 = 40 mol/m^3.
Compute E = 3 × 40 mol/m^3 × 8.314 J/(mol·K) × 298 K.
Report the final value of E in Pascals (Pa).
