The crystal has a BCC structure with 2 atoms per unit cell (n = 2).
The atomic weight is A = 96.98 g/mol.
The theoretical density is ρ = 5.414 g/cm³.
Avogadro's number is N_A = 6.022 × 10²³ atoms/mol.
Use the theoretical density formula: ρ = nA / (N_A × a³).
Rearrange the formula to solve for the lattice parameter: a³ = nA / (N_A × ρ).
Substitute n = 2, A = 96.98 g/mol, N_A = 6.022 × 10²³ mol⁻¹, and ρ = 5.414 g/cm³ into the rearranged formula.
Compute a³ = (2 × 96.98) / (6.022 × 10²³ × 5.414).
Calculate the numerical value of a³ in cm³.
Take the cube root of a³ to obtain the lattice parameter a in cm.
Convert the result from cm to nm using 1 cm = 10⁷ nm.
Report the final value of a in nm.
