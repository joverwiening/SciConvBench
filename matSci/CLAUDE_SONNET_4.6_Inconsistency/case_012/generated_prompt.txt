Calculate the average electrodeposition coating thickness in micrometers.

Given values:
Area = 90.04 cm^2
Current = 2.744 A
Time = 18.3 min
Molar mass M = 181.05 g/mol
Density rho = 18.511 g/cm^3
Electrons per atom n = 4
Faraday constant F = 96485 C/mol
Current efficiency = 100%

Step 1: Convert time to seconds.
t = 18.3 min × 60 s/min = 1098 s

Step 2: Calculate total charge Q.
Q = I × t = 2.744 A × 1098 s = 3013.01 C

Step 3: Calculate mass deposited using m = Q × M / (n × F).
m = (3013.01 C × 181.05 g/mol) / (4 × 96485 C/mol)
m = 545,306.6 / 385,940
m = 1.41292 g

Step 4: Calculate volume of deposited metal.
V = m / rho = 1.41292 g / 18.511 g/cm^3 = 0.076327 cm^3

Step 5: Calculate average thickness.
thickness = V / Area = 0.076327 cm^3 / 90.04 cm^2 = 0.00084771 cm

Step 6: Convert thickness to micrometers.
1 cm = 10000 μm
thickness = 0.00084771 cm × 10000 μm/cm = 8.4771 μm

The average coating thickness is 8.48 μm.
