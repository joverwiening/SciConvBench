The task is to compute the true strain for a specimen under uniaxial elongation.
Use the true strain formula: ε_t = ln(L / L₀).
The initial length L₀ is 50.0 mm.
The final length L is 57.5 mm.
Compute the ratio L / L₀ = 57.5 / 50.0 = 1.15.
Compute the natural logarithm: ε_t = ln(1.15).
The result is ε_t ≈ 0.1398 (dimensionless).
Round the final answer to four significant figures: ε_t ≈ 0.1398.
