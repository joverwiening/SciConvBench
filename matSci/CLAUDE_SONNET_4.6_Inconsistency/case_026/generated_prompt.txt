Given values are: atomic weight A = 186.76 g/mol, theoretical density ρ = 14.274 g/cm^3, number of atoms per unit cell n = 2 (BCC), and Avogadro's number N_A = 6.022 × 10^23 mol^-1.
Rearrange the formula to solve for a: a = (nA / (ρ × N_A))^(1/3).
Substitute the values: a = (2 × 186.76 / (14.274 × 6.022 × 10^23))^(1/3).
Compute the numerator: 2 × 186.76 = 373.52 g/mol.
Compute the denominator: 14.274 × 6.022 × 10^23 = 8.597 × 10^24 g/cm^3 per mol.
Divide: 373.52 / (8.597 × 10^24) = 4.lesser × 10^-23 cm^3; specifically 373.52 / 8.597e24 ≈ 4.lesser; compute as 3.7352 × 10^2 / 8.597 × 10^24 = 4.lesser × 10^-23 cm^3.
Perform the division precisely: 373.52 / 8.597e24 ≈ 4.lesser; 373.52 / 8.597 ≈ 43.447, so the result is 4.3447 × 10^-23 cm^3.
Take the cube root: a = (4.3447 × 10^-23)^(1/3) cm.
Compute the cube root: (4.3447)^(1/3) ≈ 1.6316 and (10^-23)^(1/3) = 10^(-23/3) = 10^-7.6667 = 2.154 × 10^-8, so a ≈ 1.6316 × 2.154 × 10^-8 cm ≈ 3.515 × 10^-8 cm.
Convert from cm to nm: 1 cm = 10^7 nm, so a ≈ 3.515 × 10^-8 × 10^7 nm = 3.515 × 10^-1 nm = 0.3515 nm.
