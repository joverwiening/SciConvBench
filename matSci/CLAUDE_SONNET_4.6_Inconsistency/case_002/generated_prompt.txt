The material has a BCC crystal structure for the purpose of this calculation (the problem states to use the BCC unit-cell population), despite the initial mention of FCC.
Use n = 2 atoms per unit cell, as BCC contains 2 atoms per unit cell.
Use atomic weight A = 76.45 g/mol.
Use theoretical density ρ = 18.499 g/cm³.
Use Avogadro's number N_A = 6.022 × 10²³ mol⁻¹.
Apply the density formula: ρ = (n × A) / (N_A × a³).
Rearrange the formula to solve for the lattice parameter: a³ = (n × A) / (N_A × ρ).
Substitute values: a³ = (2 × 76.45) / (6.022 × 10²³ × 18.499).
Compute the numerator: 2 × 76.45 = 152.90 g/mol.
Compute the denominator: 6.022 × 10²³ × 18.499 = 1.11401 × 10²⁵ g/cm³ per mol.
Compute a³ = 152.90 / (1.11401 × 10²⁵) = 1.3726 × 10⁻²³ cm³.
Take the cube root: a = (1.3726 × 10⁻²³)^(1/3).
Convert the result from cm to nm by multiplying by 10⁷ (since 1 cm = 10⁷ nm).
Compute a = 2.380 × 10⁻⁸ cm = 0.2380 nm.
Report the cubic lattice parameter as a ≈ 0.2380 nm.
