The electrochemical reaction for copper electroplating is Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu.
Use n = 2 electrons per copper atom, as each Cu²⁺ ion requires 2 electrons to be reduced to Cu.
Apply a current of 2.0 A for 30 minutes (1800 seconds).
Calculate the total charge Q using Q = I × t, giving Q = 2.0 × 1800 = 3600 coulombs.
Calculate the number of moles of electrons using moles of electrons = Q / F, where F = 96485 C/mol.
Calculate the moles of copper deposited using moles of Cu = moles of electrons / n = moles of electrons / 2.
Calculate the deposited mass using mass = moles of Cu × molar mass of Cu, where the molar mass of Cu = 63.546 g/mol.
Report the final deposited mass of copper in grams.
Do not use n = 1 anywhere in the calculation.
