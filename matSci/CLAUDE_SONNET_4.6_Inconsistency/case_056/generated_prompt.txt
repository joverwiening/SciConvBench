The task is a numerical calculation of resolved shear stress in a single crystal under uniaxial tension.

Use the Schmid Law formula: τ = σ × cos(φ) × cos(λ).

The applied tensile stress σ is 100 MPa.

The angle between the tensile axis and the slip plane normal φ is 45°.

The angle between the tensile axis and the slip direction λ is 45°.

Calculate the Schmid factor as cos(45°) × cos(45°) = (0.7071) × (0.7071) = 0.5.

Calculate the resolved shear stress as τ = 100 MPa × 0.5 = 50 MPa.

The resolved shear stress is 50 MPa.
