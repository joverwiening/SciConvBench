The polymer system is a two-phase semicrystalline mixture with an amorphous phase and a crystalline phase.

The measured (bulk) density of the sample is 1.22 g/cm³.
The amorphous phase density is 1.05 g/cm³.
The crystalline phase density is 1.30 g/cm³.

Confirm that 1.22 g/cm³ lies between 1.05 and 1.30 g/cm³, which is physically consistent.

Use the volume-fraction-based two-phase mixing rule to estimate the degree of crystallinity.

The degree of crystallinity by volume fraction is defined as: χ_v = (ρ_measured − ρ_amorphous) / (ρ_crystalline − ρ_amorphous).

Substitute the values: χ_v = (1.22 − 1.05) / (1.30 − 1.05) = 0.17 / 0.25 = 0.68.

Report the degree of crystallinity as approximately 68% by volume.

Optionally, also compute the mass-fraction-based degree of crystallinity using: χ_m = (ρ_crystalline / ρ_measured) × (ρ_measured − ρ_amorphous) / (ρ_crystalline − ρ_amorphous).

Substitute the values: χ_m = (1.30 / 1.22) × (0.17 / 0.25) = 1.0656 × 0.68 ≈ 0.724.

Report the mass-fraction degree of crystallinity as approximately 72.4% if the mass-fraction definition is required.

State clearly which definition (volume fraction or mass fraction) is being used in the final answer.
