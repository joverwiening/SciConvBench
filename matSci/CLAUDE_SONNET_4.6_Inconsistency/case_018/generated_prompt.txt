Calculate the critical flaw size parameter a in micrometers for a brittle glass with a central crack using Griffith fracture theory.
Use the Griffith fracture criterion for a central crack: σ_f = sqrt(2 * E * γ_s / (π * a)).
The applied tensile stress is σ_f = 529.7 MPa = 529.7 × 10^6 Pa.
Young's modulus is E = 240.3 GPa = 240.3 × 10^9 Pa.
Surface energy is γ_s = 2.576 J/m^2.
Rearrange the formula to solve for a: a = 2 * E * γ_s / (π * σ_f^2).
Substitute the given values into the rearranged formula.
Compute σ_f^2 = (529.7 × 10^6)^2 Pa^2.
Compute the numerator as 2 * (240.3 × 10^9) * 2.576.
Compute the denominator as π * σ_f^2.
Divide the numerator by the denominator to obtain a in meters.
Convert the result from meters to micrometers by multiplying by 10^6.
Report the final value of a in micrometers.
