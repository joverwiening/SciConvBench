Q: Before I finalize the specification, I need to clarify one critical point.

The NaCl (rock salt) structure has a well-established coordination number of 6 for both cations and anions — each ion is surrounded by 6 nearest neighbors of the opposite type. A coordination number of 8 is characteristic of the CsCl structure, not the NaCl structure.

Are you asking me to:
1. Work with the **actual NaCl structure** (coordination number = 6), treating the "CN = 8" as an error in the problem, or
2. Work with the **stated CN = 8** as given (which would correspond to a CsCl-type structure, not NaCl)?
A: The coordination number is 6, not 8. Work with CN = 6 for the NaCl structure.
