Use the Hall-Petch equation: σ_y = σ_0 + k_y · d^(-1/2).
Use σ_0 = 120 MPa.
Use k_y = +0.70 MPa·m^(1/2).
Use d = 9 μm = 9 × 10^(-6) m.
Compute d^(-1/2) = (9 × 10^(-6))^(-1/2) = 1 / (3 × 10^(-3) m^(1/2)) = 333.33 m^(-1/2).
Compute k_y · d^(-1/2) = 0.70 MPa·m^(1/2) × 333.33 m^(-1/2) = 233.33 MPa.
Compute σ_y = 120 MPa + 233.33 MPa = 353.33 MPa.
