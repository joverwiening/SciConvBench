Q: Which specific surface area formula should be used to determine the trend: the standard geometric formula for spherical particles (S = 6 / (ρ · d)), or do you want to assume unit density (ρ = 1) and compare surface area per unit volume instead?
A: Use the standard formula for spherical particles. Since both powders are the same material, density is the same for both, so just compare based on diameter — smaller diameter means higher specific surface area.
