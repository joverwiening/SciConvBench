The task is to compute the engineering stress using a non-standard definition where stress equals load divided by the final (deformed) cross-sectional area.

Given values are load P = 42 kN and final cross-sectional area A_f = 115 mm^2.
Convert P from kilonewtons to newtons: 42 kN = 42,000 N.
Apply the given formula: Engineering stress = P / A_f.
Compute: Engineering stress = 42,000 N / 115 mm^2.
Result: Engineering stress = 365.22 MPa (rounded to two decimal places).
Note that this definition uses the deformed area A_f instead of the original area A_0, which differs from the conventional definition of engineering stress but matches the problem statement exactly.
The final answer is approximately 365.22 MPa.
