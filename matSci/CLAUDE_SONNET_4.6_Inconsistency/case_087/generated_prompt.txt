A type-I superconductor has a critical field at absolute zero of H_c(0) = 0.080 T and a critical temperature of T_c = 7.2 K.
The temperature of interest is T = 4.0 K.
Use the standard parabolic approximation formula: H_c(T) = H_c(0) * [1 - (T / T_c)^2].
Substitute the values: H_c(4.0) = 0.080 * [1 - (4.0 / 7.2)^2].
Compute (4.0 / 7.2)^2 = (0.5556)^2 = 0.3086.
Compute 1 - 0.3086 = 0.6914.
Compute H_c(4.0) = 0.080 * 0.6914 = 0.05531 T.
Round the result to three significant figures: H_c(4.0) ≈ 0.0553 T.
