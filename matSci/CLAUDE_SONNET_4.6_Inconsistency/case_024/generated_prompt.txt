Use the density formula ρ = nA / (N_A × a^3) to find the lattice parameter a.
Given values are: ρ = 13.931 g/cm^3, A = 85.15 g/mol, N_A = 6.022×10^23 mol^-1, n = 4 (FCC unit cell).
Rearrange the formula to solve for a: a^3 = nA / (N_A × ρ).
Substitute the values: a^3 = (4 × 85.15) / (6.022×10^23 × 13.931).
Compute the numerator: 4 × 85.15 = 340.60 g/mol.
Compute the denominator: 6.022×10^23 × 13.931 = 8.3893×10^24 g/cm^3 per mol^-1, i.e., 8.3893×10^24 cm^-3.
Divide: a^3 = 340.60 / 8.3893×10^24 = 4.0601×10^-23 cm^3.
Take the cube root: a = (4.0601×10^-23)^(1/3) cm.
Compute the cube root: a ≈ 3.4380×10^-8 cm.
Convert to nanometers (1 cm = 10^7 nm): a ≈ 0.3438 nm.
Report the final answer as a ≈ 0.344 nm.
