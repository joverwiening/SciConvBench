Q: The formula for the area of a circle is A = π(d/2)² = πd²/4, but you've specified to use A = πd² (without dividing by 4).

Should I use A = πd² exactly as stated (which would give a non-standard result), or did you mean the standard formula A = π(d/2)² = πd²/4?
A: Use the standard formula A = π(d/2)² = πd²/4.
