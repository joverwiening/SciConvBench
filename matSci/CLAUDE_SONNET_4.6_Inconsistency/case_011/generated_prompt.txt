Calculate the electrical resistivity of a wire using the formula ρ = R·A / L.
The wire has a length L = 0.492 m.
The wire has a diameter d = 1.944 mm = 0.001944 m.
The wire has an electrical resistance R = 11.705 Ω.
Calculate the cross-sectional area using the standard formula A = π(d/2)² = πd²/4.
Compute A = π × (0.001944/2)² = π × (0.000972)².
Compute A = π × 9.44784 × 10⁻⁷ = 2.9685 × 10⁻⁶ m².
Compute ρ = R × A / L = 11.705 × 2.9685 × 10⁻⁶ / 0.492.
Express the final result in units of Ω·m.
