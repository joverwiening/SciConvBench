Calculate the critical fiber length using the formula l_c = (σ_f × d) / (2 × τ_c).
Use σ_f = 3690.4 MPa as the fiber tensile strength.
Use d = 11.05 μm as the fiber diameter (the variable labeled as fiber radius in the problem is used directly as diameter in the formula as given).
Use τ_c = 15.33 MPa as the fiber-matrix interfacial shear strength.
Substitute into the formula: l_c = (3690.4 × 11.05) / (2 × 15.33).
Compute the numerator: 3690.4 × 11.05 = 40,778.92 MPa·μm.
Compute the denominator: 2 × 15.33 = 30.66 MPa.
Divide: l_c = 40,778.92 / 30.66 = 1329.9 μm.
Report the critical fiber length as approximately 1329.9 μm.
