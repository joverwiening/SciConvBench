The polymer has a number-average molecular weight M_n = 78,000 g/mol (interpreting the value as 78 kg/mol, which is the physically meaningful scale for a polymer).
The repeat-unit molecular weight is M_0 = 104 g/mol.
The degree of polymerization DP is defined as DP = M_n / M_0.
Calculate DP = 78,000 g/mol ÷ 104 g/mol = 750.
The degree of polymerization is 750.
