The crystal is BCC with atomic weight A = 183.72 g/mol.
The theoretical density is ρ = 16.416 g/cm³.
The number of atoms per unit cell is n = 2 (BCC).
Avogadro's number is N_A = 6.022 × 10²³ atoms/mol.
Use the formula ρ = nA / (N_A · a³) to solve for the lattice parameter a.
Rearrange the formula to a³ = nA / (N_A · ρ).
Substitute values: a³ = (2 × 183.72) / (6.022 × 10²³ × 16.416).
Compute the numerator: 2 × 183.72 = 367.44 g/mol.
Compute the denominator: 6.022 × 10²³ × 16.416 = 9.881 × 10²⁴ g/cm³ · mol⁻¹ · (atoms, dimensionless).
Compute a³ = 367.44 / 9.881 × 10²⁴ = 3.719 × 10⁻²³ cm³.
Take the cube root: a = (3.719 × 10⁻²³)^(1/3) cm.
Compute a = 3.336 × 10⁻⁸ cm.
Convert to nanometers: 1 cm = 10⁷ nm, so a = 3.336 × 10⁻⁸ × 10⁷ nm = 0.3336 nm.
The lattice parameter a is approximately 0.3336 nm.
