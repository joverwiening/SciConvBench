The material is sintered alumina.
The bulk density is 3.25 g/cm³.
The theoretical (true) density is 3.98 g/cm³.
Calculate porosity using the formula: Porosity (%) = ((theoretical density - bulk density) / theoretical density) × 100.
Substitute the values: Porosity (%) = ((3.98 - 3.25) / 3.98) × 100.
Compute the numerator: 3.98 - 3.25 = 0.73.
Divide: 0.73 / 3.98 = 0.18342...
Multiply by 100: porosity ≈ 18.34%.
Report the final answer as approximately 18.34% porosity.
