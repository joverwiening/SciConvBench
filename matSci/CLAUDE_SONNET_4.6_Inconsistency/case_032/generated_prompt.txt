Calculate the resolved shear stress for an FCC crystal under uniaxial loading.

The applied uniaxial stress is 80 MPa.

The slip system is the correct FCC slip system: slip plane {111} and slip direction <110>.

The angle between the loading axis and the slip plane normal (φ) is 45°.

The angle between the loading axis and the slip direction (λ) is 45°.

Use the Schmid Law formula: τ = σ × cos(φ) × cos(λ).

Compute cos(45°) = √2/2 ≈ 0.7071 for both angles.

Compute the Schmid factor: cos(45°) × cos(45°) = 0.5.

Compute the resolved shear stress: τ = 80 MPa × 0.5 = 40 MPa.

The resolved shear stress on the {111}<110> slip system is 40 MPa.
