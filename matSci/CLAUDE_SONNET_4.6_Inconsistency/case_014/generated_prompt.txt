Calculate the weight-average molecular weight M_w for a polymer sample using the correct formula M_w = Σ(N_i × M_i²) / Σ(N_i × M_i).

The polymer distribution is as follows:
Component 1: N1 = 93, M1 = 21640 g/mol
Component 2: N2 = 40, M2 = 74810 g/mol
Component 3: N3 = 170, M3 = 135292 g/mol
Component 4: N4 = 48, M4 = 141802 g/mol

Step 1: Calculate N_i × M_i for each component.
N1 × M1 = 93 × 21640 = 2,012,520
N2 × M2 = 40 × 74810 = 2,992,400
N3 × M3 = 170 × 135292 = 22,999,640
N4 × M4 = 48 × 141802 = 6,806,496

Step 2: Calculate Σ(N_i × M_i).
Σ(N_i × M_i) = 2,012,520 + 2,992,400 + 22,999,640 + 6,806,496 = 34,811,056

Step 3: Calculate N_i × M_i² for each component.
N1 × M1² = 93 × (21640)² = 93 × 468,288,960 = 43,550,873,280 (≈ 4.3551 × 10¹⁰)
N2 × M2² = 40 × (74810)² = 40 × 5,596,536,100 = 223,861,444,000 (≈ 2.2386 × 10¹¹)
N3 × M3² = 170 × (135292)² = 170 × 18,303,933,064 = 3,111,668,620,880 (≈ 3.1117 × 10¹²)
N4 × M4² = 48 × (141802)² = 48 × 20,107,807,604 = 965,174,765,000 (≈ 9.6517 × 10¹¹)

Step 4: Calculate Σ(N_i × M_i²).
Σ(N_i × M_i²) = 43,550,873,280 + 223,861,444,000 + 3,111,668,620,880 + 965,174,765,000 = 4,344,255,703,160 (≈ 4.3443 × 10¹²)

Step 5: Calculate M_w.
M_w = Σ(N_i × M_i²) / Σ(N_i × M_i) = 4,344,255,703,160 / 34,811,056 ≈ 124,797 g/mol

The weight-average molecular weight M_w is approximately 124,797 g/mol.
