Calculate the theoretical density of a ceramic crystal using the formula ρ = ZM / (N_A × a^3).

Use Z = 4 (number of formula units per unit cell as specified in the formula evaluation, overriding the 8 units stated in the description).
Use M = 139.63 g/mol (molar mass of one formula unit).
Use N_A = 6.022 × 10^23 mol^-1 (Avogadro's number).
Use a = 0.6067 nm = 0.6067 × 10^-7 cm (lattice parameter converted to centimeters).

Step 1: Convert the lattice parameter to centimeters.
a = 0.6067 nm = 0.6067 × 10^-7 cm = 6.067 × 10^-8 cm.

Step 2: Calculate a^3.
a^3 = (6.067 × 10^-8 cm)^3 = (6.067)^3 × 10^-24 cm^3.
(6.067)^3 = 6.067 × 6.067 × 6.067.
6.067 × 6.067 = 36.808.
36.808 × 6.067 = 223.35.
a^3 = 223.35 × 10^-24 cm^3 = 2.2335 × 10^-22 cm^3.

Step 3: Calculate the numerator ZM.
ZM = 4 × 139.63 = 558.52 g/mol.

Step 4: Calculate the denominator N_A × a^3.
N_A × a^3 = 6.022 × 10^23 × 2.2335 × 10^-22.
= 6.022 × 2.2335 × 10^(23-22).
= 6.022 × 2.2335 × 10^1.
6.022 × 2.2335 = 13.450.
N_A × a^3 = 13.450 × 10^1 = 134.50 cm^3/mol.

Step 5: Calculate density ρ.
ρ = 558.52 / 134.50 = 4.152 g/cm^3.

The theoretical density of the ceramic crystal is approximately 4.15 g/cm^3.
