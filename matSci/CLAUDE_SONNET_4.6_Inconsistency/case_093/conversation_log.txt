Q: The total surface energy is typically calculated by combining the dispersive and polar components. Which combining rule should be used to determine the correct total surface energy — the simple additive rule (γ = γ^d + γ^p), or the geometric mean (Owens-Wendt) approach?
A: Simple additive rule: γ = γ^d + γ^p.
