Q: The formula you've provided contains a contradiction.

The relation `a = 4r/√3` is for **BCC** metals, not FCC.

For an **FCC** metal, the correct relation is `a = 4r/√2` (or equivalently, `r = a√2/4`).

Which formula should be used: the FCC formula `a = 4r/√2`, or the BCC formula `a = 4r/√3`?
A: Use the FCC formula `a = 2√2 r`.
