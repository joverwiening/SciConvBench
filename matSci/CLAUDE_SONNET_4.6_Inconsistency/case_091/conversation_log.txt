Q: The reported static water contact angle of 195° is physically impossible.

Contact angles are measured between the tangent to the liquid-air interface at the three-phase contact line and the solid surface. By geometric definition, a contact angle can only range from 0° to 180°.

- 0° means complete wetting (water spreads fully).
- 180° means perfect non-wetting (theoretical limit, water forms a perfect sphere touching the surface at a single point).

Can you clarify whether the reported value of 195° is a typo or misreading, and provide the intended angle — for example, did you mean 95° (hydrophobic) or 175° (superhydrophobic)?
A: Yes, it's a typo — the correct value is **95°**.
