A unidirectional composite is given with the following material properties: fiber modulus E_f = 230 GPa, matrix modulus E_m = 3 GPa, and fiber volume fraction V_f = 0.60.
The matrix volume fraction V_m is 1 - V_f = 0.40.
The reported longitudinal modulus of 2.5 GPa is intentionally incorrect and must be identified as wrong.
Ignore the reported value of 2.5 GPa entirely.
Apply the Rule of Mixtures to determine the correct longitudinal modulus: E_L = E_f * V_f + E_m * V_m.
Substitute the values: E_L = (230 GPa)(0.60) + (3 GPa)(0.40).
Calculate each term: (230)(0.60) = 138 GPa and (3)(0.40) = 1.2 GPa.
Sum the terms: E_L = 138 + 1.2 = 139.2 GPa.
State the correct longitudinal modulus as E_L = 139.2 GPa.
Conclude that the reported value of 2.5 GPa is incorrect by a large margin and does not match the Rule of Mixtures result.
