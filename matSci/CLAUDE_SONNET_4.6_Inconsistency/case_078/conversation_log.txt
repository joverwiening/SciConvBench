Q: This is a contradiction that needs to be resolved before I can write a specification.

Which piece of information should be treated as correct and authoritative for this problem: the **sign of the Hall coefficient** (+), or the claim that **electrons are the majority carriers**?
A: The sign of the Hall coefficient is the authoritative information. The majority carrier type should be determined from R_H = +4.0×10⁻⁴ m³/C, which means holes are the majority carriers.
