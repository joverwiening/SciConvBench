Calculate the fiber weight fraction w_f purely from the given volume fraction and densities.
Use V_f = 0.60, ρ_f = 1.8 g/cm³, ρ_m = 1.2 g/cm³.
The matrix volume fraction is V_m = 1 - V_f = 0.40.
Assume a unit total volume of 1 cm³ for the composite.
The mass of fiber is m_f = V_f × ρ_f = 0.60 × 1.8 = 1.08 g.
The mass of matrix is m_m = V_m × ρ_m = 0.40 × 1.2 = 0.48 g.
The total mass is m_total = m_f + m_m = 1.08 + 0.48 = 1.56 g.
Calculate w_f as w_f = m_f / m_total = 1.08 / 1.56.
Report the final result as w_f ≈ 0.692 (or 69.2%).
Do not use or reference the stated w_f = 0.40 anywhere in the calculation.
