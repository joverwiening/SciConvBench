The system is a miscible polymer blend with 50 wt% component A and 50 wt% component B.
T_g,A = 373 K and T_g,B = 293 K.
w_A = 0.5 and w_B = 0.5.
Apply the Fox equation: 1/T_g = w_A/T_g,A + w_B/T_g,B.
Calculate 1/T_g = 0.5/373 + 0.5/293.
0.5/373 = 0.001340 K⁻¹.
0.5/293 = 0.001706 K⁻¹.
Sum: 1/T_g = 0.001340 + 0.001706 = 0.003046 K⁻¹.
T_g = 1/0.003046 = 328.3 K.
Convert to Celsius: T_g = 328.3 - 273.15 = 55.1°C.
The estimated glass transition temperature of the blend is approximately 328 K or 55°C.
