The task is to identify and correct the error in the Tauc plot construction for a material with a direct allowed band gap.

The Tauc relation is given by (αhν)^(1/n) = A(hν - Eg), where n depends on the nature of the optical transition.

For a direct allowed transition, the correct exponent n is 1/2, meaning the plot must be constructed as (αhν)^2 versus hν, not (αhν)^(1/2) versus hν.

The exponent used in the given plot is 1/2, which corresponds to n = 2, and that form applies to indirect allowed transitions, not direct allowed transitions.

The error is that the plot uses (αhν)^(1/2), which is the Tauc form for an indirect allowed band gap material.

The correct Tauc plot for a direct allowed band gap must use (αhν)^2 on the y-axis versus hν on the x-axis.

To extract the band gap, extrapolate the linear region of the corrected (αhν)^2 versus hν plot to the x-axis intercept, where (αhν)^2 = 0.

The x-axis intercept of that extrapolated linear region gives the direct allowed optical band gap energy Eg.

State explicitly in the correction that using (αhν)^(1/2) for a direct allowed transition will yield an incorrect and physically meaningless band gap value.
