X-rays of wavelength λ = 0.20 nm are incident on crystal planes with spacing d = 0.12 nm.
The first-order Bragg reflection (n = 1) is observed.
Apply Bragg's Law: nλ = 2d·sin(θ), where θ is the glancing angle.
Substitute values: sin(θ) = (1 × 0.20) / (2 × 0.12) = 0.20 / 0.24 = 0.8333.
Calculate θ = arcsin(0.8333) ≈ 56.4°.
The diffraction angle is 2θ ≈ 112.8°.
Report the final answer as 2θ ≈ 113°.
