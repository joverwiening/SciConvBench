Perform a one-time plane-strain fracture toughness validity check for a compact-tension specimen using the ASTM criterion.

Given values are: thickness B = 3 mm, yield strength σ_y = 900 MPa, measured toughness K_Ic = 40 MPa√m.

The ASTM plane-strain validity criterion requires B ≥ 2.5(K_Ic / σ_y)².

Step 1: Compute the ratio K_Ic / σ_y.
K_Ic / σ_y = 40 MPa√m / 900 MPa = 0.04444 √m.

Step 2: Square the ratio.
(K_Ic / σ_y)² = (0.04444)² = 0.001975 m.

Step 3: Multiply by 2.5 to get the minimum required thickness.
Minimum B = 2.5 × 0.001975 m = 0.004938 m = 4.938 mm ≈ 4.94 mm.

Step 4: Compare the actual thickness to the minimum required thickness.
Actual B = 3 mm.
Required B = 4.94 mm.
Since 3 mm < 4.94 mm, the condition B ≥ 2.5(K_Ic / σ_y)² is NOT satisfied.

Step 5: State the conclusion.
The plane-strain validity criterion is NOT met for this specimen.
The specimen thickness of 3 mm is insufficient; a minimum thickness of approximately 4.94 mm is required to declare a valid plane-strain K_Ic under the ASTM criterion.
The measured value of 40 MPa√m cannot be reported as a valid K_Ic; it should be treated as a conditional or apparent toughness value (often denoted K_Q).
To obtain a valid K_Ic, either use a thicker specimen (B ≥ 4.94 mm) or a material with higher yield strength, or accept that the test does not meet plane-strain conditions.
