Calculate the ionic conductivity of a solid electrolyte using the Nernst-Einstein relation.
The mobile ions are monovalent with charge z = 1.
The mobile-ion number density is n = 4.0×10^27 m^-3.
The diffusivity is D = 1.2×10^-11 m^2/s.
The temperature is 800 K.
Use the elementary charge e = 1.602×10^-19 C.
Use Boltzmann constant k_B = 1.381×10^-23 J/K.
Apply the Nernst-Einstein relation: σ = (n × z^2 × e^2 × D) / (k_B × T).
Calculate the final ionic conductivity in S/m.
