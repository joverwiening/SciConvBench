Calculate the time required for carbon diffusion in steel using Fick's second law.
The steel surface is maintained at 0.95 wt% carbon concentration.
The initial carbon content throughout the steel is 0.20 wt% carbon.
The target carbon concentration is 0.40 wt% carbon at a depth of 0.80 mm from the surface.
The diffusion coefficient is 1.6×10^-11 m^2/s.
Assume the temperature is 927°C (1200 K), which is typical for industrial carburization processes.
Use the semi-infinite solid solution to Fick's second law with constant surface concentration.
Apply the complementary error function (erfc) solution: C(x,t) = Cs - (Cs - C0) × erfc(x/(2√(Dt))).
Solve for time t when the concentration at x = 0.80 mm reaches 0.40 wt% carbon.
Express the final answer in hours with appropriate significant figures.
