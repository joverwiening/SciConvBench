Calculate the steady-state diffusive flux across a ceramic membrane using Fick's first law.
The membrane thickness is 1.5 mm (0.0015 m).
The diffusivity D is 2.2×10^-11 m^2/s.
The concentration on one face is 0.12 mol/m³.
The concentration on the opposite face is 0.02 mol/m³.
Use the equation J = -D × (dC/dx) where dC/dx is the concentration gradient across the membrane.
The concentration gradient is (0.02 - 0.12) mol/m³ divided by 0.0015 m.
Calculate the final diffusive flux in units of mol/(m²·s).
