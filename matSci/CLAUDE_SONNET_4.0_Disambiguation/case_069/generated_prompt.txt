Calculate the thermal-mismatch strain in an aluminum film bonded to a ceramic substrate.
The aluminum film has a coefficient of thermal expansion of 23×10^-6 K^-1.
The ceramic substrate has a coefficient of thermal expansion of 8×10^-6 K^-1.
The system is cooled by 180 K from its initial bonding temperature.
Use the thermal-mismatch strain formula: ε = (α_f - α_s) × ΔT.
The film is constrained by the substrate, so the substrate's thermal expansion behavior dominates.
Calculate the difference in thermal expansion coefficients between the film and substrate.
Multiply this difference by the temperature change to obtain the thermal-mismatch strain.
Express the final result in appropriate strain units.
