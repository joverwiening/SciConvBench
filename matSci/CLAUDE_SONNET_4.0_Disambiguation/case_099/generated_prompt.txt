Calculate the open-circuit thermoelectric voltage for a p-type thermoelectric leg.
Use a temperature difference of ΔT = 60 K between the hot and cold ends.
Use a Seebeck coefficient of S = +180 μV/K for the p-type material.
Apply the thermoelectric voltage formula V = S × ΔT.
The expected result is V = (+180 × 10⁻⁶ V/K) × (60 K) = +10.8 mV.
Report the voltage with appropriate units and sign convention.
