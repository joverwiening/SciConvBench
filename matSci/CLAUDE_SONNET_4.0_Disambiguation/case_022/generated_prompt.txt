Calculate the lattice parameter for a face-centered cubic (FCC) metal crystal.
Use the given atomic weight of 85.15 g/mol.
Use the given theoretical density of 13.931 g/cm³.
Use Avogadro's number N_A = 6.022×10²³ mol⁻¹.
The crystal structure is face-centered cubic with 4 atoms per unit cell.
Apply the relationship between density, atomic weight, number of atoms per unit cell, and unit cell volume.
Use the formula: density = (number of atoms per unit cell × atomic weight) / (N_A × unit cell volume).
For a cubic unit cell, the unit cell volume equals the lattice parameter cubed (a³).
Solve for the lattice parameter a using: a³ = (4 × 85.15) / (6.022×10²³ × 13.931).
Convert the final result from cm to nm by multiplying by 10⁷.
