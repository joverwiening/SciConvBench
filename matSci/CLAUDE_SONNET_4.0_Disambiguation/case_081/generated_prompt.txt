Calculate the carrier concentration for a rectangular semiconductor sample using the Hall effect.
The sample carries a current I = 15 mA.
The magnetic field strength is B = 0.80 T.
The measured Hall voltage is 24 mV.
The sample thickness is t = 0.50 mm.
Assume the sample has typical semiconductor dimensions with length = 10 mm and width = 5 mm.
Use the Hall effect formula: n = IB/(qVH*t) where n is carrier concentration, q is elementary charge, and VH is Hall voltage.
Calculate the numerical result and express the carrier concentration in carriers per cubic meter.
