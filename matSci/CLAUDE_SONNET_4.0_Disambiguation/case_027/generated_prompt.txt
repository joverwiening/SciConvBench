Calculate the diffusion activation energy using the two-point form of the Arrhenius equation.
Given data: D1 = 4.27×10^-15 m^2/s at T1 = 771 K, D2 = 1.13×10^-11 m^2/s at T2 = 1233 K.
Use the gas constant R = 8.314 J/mol·K.
Apply the equation: ln(D2/D1) = -(Q/R) × (1/T2 - 1/T1), where Q is the activation energy.
Solve for Q by rearranging: Q = -R × ln(D2/D1) / (1/T2 - 1/T1).
Convert the final result from J/mol to kJ/mol by dividing by 1000.
Report the activation energy in kJ/mol.
