Calculate the theoretical density of a ceramic material using the formula: density = (Z × M) / (a³ × N_A).
Use Z = 8 formula units per unit cell.
Use M = 182.07 g/mol as the formula-unit molar mass.
Use a = 0.6300 nm = 6.300 × 10^-8 cm as the lattice parameter.
Use N_A = 6.022 × 10^23 mol^-1 as Avogadro's number.
Convert the lattice parameter from nanometers to centimeters by multiplying by 10^-7.
Calculate a³ = (6.300 × 10^-8 cm)³.
Substitute all values into the density formula.
Express the final answer in g/cm³.
