Calculate the Young's modulus of an elastomer using rubber elasticity theory.
Use a crosslink density of 4.5×10^-5 mol/cm^3.
Set the temperature to 298 K.
Assume the material is natural rubber or a similar general elastomer.
Apply the rubber elasticity equation E = 3νeRT where E is Young's modulus, νe is crosslink density, R is the gas constant, and T is absolute temperature.
Use R = 8.314 J/(mol·K) for the gas constant.
Convert units appropriately to obtain Young's modulus in Pa or MPa.
Report the final calculated value with appropriate significant figures.
