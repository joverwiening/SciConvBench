At the eutectic temperature, the eutectic liquid composition is C_e = 40.31% B.
Assume the temperature is just a few degrees above the eutectic temperature where the phase compositions remain essentially the same as at the eutectic temperature.
Apply the lever rule to determine the fraction of liquid phase present.
The fraction of liquid calculated represents the portion that will transform into eutectic microconstituent upon cooling through the eutectic temperature.
Express the final answer as both a decimal fraction and a percentage.
