Calculate Young's modulus for a cylindrical tensile specimen with the following specifications:
The specimen has a circular cross-section with diameter 18.16 mm.
The original gauge length is 37.69 mm.
A tensile load of 18.90 kN is applied in the elastic regime.
The gauge length increases by 0.0206 mm under this load.
Assume linear elastic behavior throughout the loading.
Calculate the cross-sectional area using the circular diameter.
Calculate the stress as force divided by cross-sectional area.
Calculate the strain as change in length divided by original gauge length.
Calculate Young's modulus as stress divided by strain.
Express the final answer in GPa.
