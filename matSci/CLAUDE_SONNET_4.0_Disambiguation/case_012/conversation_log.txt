Q: What do the numbers in parentheses represent - are they the number of molecules, molar amounts, or weight fractions for each molecular weight?
A: The numbers in parentheses represent the number of molecules (N) for each molecular weight. So N1 = 96, N2 = 131, N3 = 153, and N4 = 146.
