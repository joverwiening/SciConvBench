Calculate the number-average molecular weight (M_n) for a polymer sample with the following data.
The sample contains four molecular weight fractions with their corresponding number of molecules.
Fraction 1 has molecular weight 56526 g/mol with 96 molecules.
Fraction 2 has molecular weight 92809 g/mol with 131 molecules.
Fraction 3 has molecular weight 115856 g/mol with 153 molecules.
Fraction 4 has molecular weight 126029 g/mol with 146 molecules.
Use the formula M_n = (sum of N_i × M_i) / (sum of N_i) where N_i is the number of molecules and M_i is the molecular weight for each fraction.
Calculate the total number of molecules by summing all N_i values.
Calculate the sum of N_i × M_i for all fractions.
Divide the sum of N_i × M_i by the total number of molecules to get M_n.
Express the final answer in g/mol.
