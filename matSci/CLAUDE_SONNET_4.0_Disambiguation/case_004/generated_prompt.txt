Calculate the activation energy for diffusion using the Arrhenius equation for the given data.
Given diffusion coefficient D1 = 4.76×10^-18 m^2/s at temperature T1 = 866 K.
Given diffusion coefficient D2 = 1.86×10^-12 m^2/s at temperature T2 = 1438 K.
Use the Arrhenius relationship D = D0 * exp(-Q/RT) where Q is the activation energy.
Apply the two-point form of the Arrhenius equation: ln(D2/D1) = -(Q/R) * (1/T2 - 1/T1).
Use the gas constant R = 8.314 J/mol·K.
Solve for the activation energy Q in kJ/mol.
Calculate ln(D2/D1) = ln(1.86×10^-12 / 4.76×10^-18).
Calculate (1/T2 - 1/T1) = (1/1438 - 1/866).
Substitute these values into the equation to find Q.
Convert the final result from J/mol to kJ/mol by dividing by 1000.
