Calculate the work of adhesion between water and a treated polymer surface using the Young-Dupré equation.
Use a contact angle of 72° for water on the treated polymer surface.
Use a liquid-vapor surface tension of water γ_LV = 72.8 mN/m at 20°C.
Apply the Young-Dupré equation: W_A = γ_LV(1 + cos θ) where W_A is the work of adhesion and θ is the contact angle.
Substitute the given values: W_A = 72.8 mN/m × (1 + cos 72°).
Calculate cos 72° ≈ 0.309.
Compute the final result: W_A = 72.8 mN/m × (1 + 0.309) = 72.8 mN/m × 1.309 ≈ 95.3 mN/m.
Report the work of adhesion as approximately 95.3 mN/m.
