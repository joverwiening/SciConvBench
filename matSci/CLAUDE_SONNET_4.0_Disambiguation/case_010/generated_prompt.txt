Calculate the electrical resistivity of a wire material using the following parameters:
Wire length is 0.492 meters.
Wire has a circular cross-section with diameter of 1.944 mm.
Wire electrical resistance is 11.705 Ω.
Use the formula ρ = R × A / L where ρ is resistivity, R is resistance, A is cross-sectional area, and L is length.
Calculate the cross-sectional area using A = π × (d/2)² where d is the diameter.
Express the final answer in Ω·m units.
