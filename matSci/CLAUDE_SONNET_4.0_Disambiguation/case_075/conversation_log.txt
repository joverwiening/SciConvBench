Q: What material is the plate made of, so I can determine the appropriate Paris law constants (C and m) for the fatigue crack growth calculation?
A: The complete requirement specifies to assume Paris law da/dN = C(ΔK)^m with C = 2.8×10^-11 (SI units) and m = 3.1. Use these values for the calculation.
