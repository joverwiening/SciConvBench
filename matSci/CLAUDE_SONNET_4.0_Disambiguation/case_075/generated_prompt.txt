Calculate the number of fatigue cycles for crack growth using Paris law with the following parameters:
Initial crack length a_i = 2.0 mm.
Final crack length a_f = 6.0 mm.
Stress range Δσ = 180 MPa.
Geometry factor Y = 1.12.
Paris law constants C = 2.8×10^-11 (SI units) and m = 3.1.
Use Paris law equation da/dN = C(ΔK)^m where ΔK = Y × Δσ × √(π × a).
Integrate the Paris law equation from initial to final crack length to determine total cycles N.
Express the final answer as the number of cycles required for the crack to grow from 2.0 mm to 6.0 mm.
