Calculate the theoretical density of a ceramic crystal with the following properties:
The crystal has a cubic unit cell structure.
The unit cell contains exactly 1 formula unit.
Each formula unit has a molar mass of 101.97 g/mol.
The unit cell edge length is 0.6330 nm.
Use Avogadro's number N_A = 6.022×10^23 mol^-1 in the calculation.
Express the final answer in units of g/cm^3.
Use the density formula: density = (Z × M) / (N_A × V) where Z is the number of formula units per unit cell, M is the molar mass, N_A is Avogadro's number, and V is the unit cell volume.
Convert the edge length from nanometers to centimeters before calculating the volume.
Calculate the unit cell volume as the cube of the edge length.
