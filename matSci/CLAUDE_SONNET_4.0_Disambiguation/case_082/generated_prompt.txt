Calculate the electrical conductivity of an n-type semiconductor at 300 K.
Use electron concentration n = 2.0×10^21 m^-3.
Use electron mobility μ_n = 0.12 m²/V·s.
Apply the formula σ = n × e × μ_n where e is the elementary charge (1.602×10^-19 C).
Express the final result in S/m (siemens per meter).
