Calculate the weight-average molecular weight (M_w) for a polymer sample with the following molecular weight distribution.
The sample contains four molecular weight fractions with their respective number of molecules.
Fraction 1 has molecular weight 21640 g/mol with 93 molecules.
Fraction 2 has molecular weight 74810 g/mol with 40 molecules.
Fraction 3 has molecular weight 135292 g/mol with 170 molecules.
Fraction 4 has molecular weight 141802 g/mol with 48 molecules.
Use the formula M_w = (sum of N_i × M_i²) / (sum of N_i × M_i) where N_i is the number of molecules and M_i is the molecular weight for each fraction.
Calculate the numerator by summing N_i × M_i² for all four fractions.
Calculate the denominator by summing N_i × M_i for all four fractions.
Divide the numerator by the denominator to obtain M_w in g/mol.
Report the final answer as the weight-average molecular weight in g/mol.
