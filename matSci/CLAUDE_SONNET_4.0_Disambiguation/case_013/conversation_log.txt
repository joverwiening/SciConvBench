Q: What do the numbers in parentheses represent - are they the number of molecules at each molecular weight, or are they weight fractions, or something else?
A: The numbers in parentheses represent the number of molecules (N) at each molecular weight. So N1 = 93, N2 = 40, N3 = 170, and N4 = 48.
