Calculate the fraction of liquid in a binary alloy just above the eutectic temperature using the lever rule.
The alloy composition is C0 = 25.37% B.
The alpha phase composition at the eutectic temperature is C_αe = 7.90% B.
The liquid phase composition at the eutectic temperature is C_e = 47.78% B.
Analyze the condition infinitesimally above the eutectic temperature where both alpha and liquid phases coexist.
Apply the lever rule formula: fraction of liquid = (C0 - C_αe) / (C_e - C_αe).
Express the final answer as both a decimal and percentage.
