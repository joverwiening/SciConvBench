Calculate Young's modulus for a cylindrical tensile test specimen.
The specimen has a circular cross-section with diameter 11.79 mm.
The gauge length is 44.24 mm.
Apply an elastic load of 16.21 kN to the specimen.
Measure the resulting elongation of 0.0342 mm over the gauge section.
Calculate the cross-sectional area using the diameter.
Calculate the stress by dividing the load by the cross-sectional area.
Calculate the strain by dividing the elongation by the gauge length.
Calculate Young's modulus by dividing stress by strain.
Express the final result in GPa.
