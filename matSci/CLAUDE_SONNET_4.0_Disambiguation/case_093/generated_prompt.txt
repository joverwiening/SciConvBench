Calculate the glass transition temperature of a miscible polymer blend using the Fox equation.
The blend contains 35 wt% polymer A and 65 wt% polymer B.
Polymer A has a glass transition temperature of 373 K.
Polymer B has a glass transition temperature of 263 K.
Apply the Fox equation: 1/Tg = (w1/Tg1) + (w2/Tg2) where w1 and w2 are weight fractions and Tg1 and Tg2 are the glass transition temperatures of the individual polymers.
Convert weight percentages to weight fractions: w1 = 0.35 for polymer A and w2 = 0.65 for polymer B.
Calculate the blend glass transition temperature and report the result in Kelvin.
