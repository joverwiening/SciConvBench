Calculate the mass fractions of proeutectoid ferrite and pearlite for a 0.60 wt% C steel cooled slowly to just below the eutectoid temperature.
Use an eutectoid composition of 0.76 wt% C.
Use a ferrite carbon solubility of 0.022 wt% C at the eutectoid temperature.
Apply the lever rule to determine the mass fraction of proeutectoid ferrite using the formula: mass fraction of proeutectoid ferrite = (C_e - C_0) / (C_e - C_α).
Apply the lever rule to determine the mass fraction of pearlite using the formula: mass fraction of pearlite = (C_0 - C_α) / (C_e - C_α).
Where C_0 is the overall steel composition (0.60 wt% C), C_e is the eutectoid composition (0.76 wt% C), and C_α is the ferrite carbon solubility (0.022 wt% C).
Verify that the sum of both mass fractions equals 1.0.
