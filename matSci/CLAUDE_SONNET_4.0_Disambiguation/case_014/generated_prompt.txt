Calculate the percent crystallinity of a semicrystalline polymer sample using the density method.
Given values are sample density ρ_s = 1.1973 g/cm³, amorphous density ρ_a = 0.8845 g/cm³, and crystalline density ρ_c = 1.4240 g/cm³.
Use the formula X_c = [ρ_c(ρ_s - ρ_a)] / [ρ_s(ρ_c - ρ_a)] × 100 to calculate the percent crystallinity.
Substitute the given density values into the formula and perform the calculation.
Express the final result as a percentage with appropriate significant figures.
