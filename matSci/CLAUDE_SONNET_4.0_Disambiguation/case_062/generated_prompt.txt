Calculate the fiber volume fraction for a composite material.
Given fiber weight fraction is 0.795.
Given fiber density is 3.711 g/cm³.
Given matrix density is 1.609 g/cm³.
Use the relationship between weight fraction and volume fraction: V_f = (w_f / ρ_f) / ((w_f / ρ_f) + ((1 - w_f) / ρ_m)).
Where V_f is fiber volume fraction, w_f is fiber weight fraction, ρ_f is fiber density, and ρ_m is matrix density.
Substitute the given values and solve for the fiber volume fraction.
