Calculate the theoretical specific capacity for an FePO₄ cathode that exchanges 1 mole of Li per mole of active material.
Use the formula: Theoretical specific capacity = (x × F) / M, where x is moles of Li exchanged, F is Faraday's constant (96,485 C/mol), and M is the molecular weight of FePO₄.
Calculate the molecular weight of FePO₄ using atomic weights: Fe = 55.845 g/mol, P = 30.974 g/mol, O = 15.999 g/mol.
The molecular weight of FePO₄ = 55.845 + 30.974 + (4 × 15.999) = 150.815 g/mol.
Substitute values: Theoretical specific capacity = (1 × 96,485) / 150.815 = 639.7 mAh/g.
Express the final answer as approximately 640 mAh/g.
