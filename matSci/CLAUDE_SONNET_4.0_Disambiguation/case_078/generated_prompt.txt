Calculate the fraction of eutectic microconstituent in a hypoeutectic binary alloy with overall composition C_0 = 30 wt% B.
Use the eutectic composition C_e = 40 wt% B.
Use the α-phase composition at eutectic temperature C_αe = 12 wt% B.
Apply the lever rule to determine the fraction of eutectic microconstituent.
The fraction of eutectic microconstituent equals (C_0 - C_αe) / (C_e - C_αe).
Substitute the values: (30 - 12) / (40 - 12) = 18 / 28 = 0.643.
The fraction of eutectic microconstituent present just below the eutectic temperature is 0.643 or 64.3%.
