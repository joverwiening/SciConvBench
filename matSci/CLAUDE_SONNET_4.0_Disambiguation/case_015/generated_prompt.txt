Calculate the theoretical density of a ceramic crystal with the following properties:
The crystal has a cubic unit cell structure.
The molar mass of one formula unit is 139.63 g/mol.
The lattice parameter is 0.6067 nm.
There are 8 formula units per cubic unit cell.
Use Avogadro's number N_A = 6.022×10^23 mol^-1.
Express the final answer in g/cm^3.
Use the density formula: density = (Z × M) / (N_A × V) where Z is the number of formula units per unit cell, M is the molar mass, N_A is Avogadro's number, and V is the unit cell volume.
Calculate the unit cell volume as a^3 where a is the lattice parameter.
Convert units appropriately to obtain the density in g/cm^3.
