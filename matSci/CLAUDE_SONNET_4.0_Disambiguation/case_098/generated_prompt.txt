Calculate the specific surface area of a powder consisting of spherical particles.
Use a particle density of 6.2 g/cm³.
Use an average particle diameter of 0.80 μm.
Assume all particles are perfect spheres with uniform density.
Calculate the surface area to volume ratio for a single sphere.
Convert the result to specific surface area in units of m²/g.
The specific surface area equals 6 divided by (density × diameter) when diameter is in meters and density is in kg/m³.
