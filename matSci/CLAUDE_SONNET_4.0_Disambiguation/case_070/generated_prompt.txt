Calculate the biaxial thermal stress in a thin film on a rigid substrate using the given parameters.
The thermal excursion is ΔT = -250 K (cooling).
The film coefficient of thermal expansion is α_f = 14×10^-6 K^-1.
The substrate coefficient of thermal expansion is α_s = 6×10^-6 K^-1.
The film elastic modulus is E_f = 160 GPa.
The film Poisson's ratio is ν_f = 0.28.
Use the biaxial thermal stress formula: σ = E_f/(1-ν_f) × (α_f - α_s) × ΔT.
The substrate is assumed to be much thicker than the film, making it effectively rigid.
Calculate the mismatch strain as (α_f - α_s) × ΔT.
Report the final stress value with appropriate units and sign convention.
