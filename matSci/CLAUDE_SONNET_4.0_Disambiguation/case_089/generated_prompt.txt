Apply the parallel model equation: k_parallel = V_f × k_f + V_m × k_m.
Use V_f = 0.55 for fiber volume fraction and V_m = 0.45 for matrix volume fraction.
Calculate the result and express the effective thermal conductivity in W/m·K.
