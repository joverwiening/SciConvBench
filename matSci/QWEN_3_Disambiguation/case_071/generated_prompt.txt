Calculate the diffusive flux using Fick's first law of diffusion.  
Use the formula: Flux = -D × (concentration gradient).  
Substitute D = 2.2×10^-11 m²/s.  
Substitute concentration gradient = -66.67 mol/m⁴.  
Compute the flux as Flux = -(2.2×10^-11) × (-66.67).  
The diffusive flux is positive and equal to 1.467×10^-9 mol/(m²·s).
