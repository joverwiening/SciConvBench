The overall composition of the alloy is 30 wt% B.
The eutectic composition is 40 wt% B.
The composition of the alpha phase at the eutectic temperature is 12 wt% B.
Use the lever rule to calculate the fraction of liquid phase at the eutectic temperature.
The fraction of liquid phase is (30 - 12) / (40 - 12).
The fraction of eutectic microconstituent is equal to the fraction of liquid phase just above the eutectic temperature.
The fraction of eutectic microconstituent just below the eutectic temperature is 0.643.
