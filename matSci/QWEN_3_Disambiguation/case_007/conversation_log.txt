Q: What is the temperature range or specific condition just above the eutectic temperature that should be considered for the phase fraction calculation?
A: Just above the eutectic temperature, the system is at an infinitesimally higher temperature where the liquid phase still exists, and the alloy consists of solid α phase particles in equilibrium with the remaining liquid. Use the lever rule at this condition to calculate the weight fraction of liquid.
