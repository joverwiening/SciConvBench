Use the density of the semicrystalline polymer sample, ρ = 1.0383 g/cm³.
Use the density of the amorphous phase, ρ_a = 1.0015 g/cm³.
Use the density of the crystalline phase, ρ_c = 1.2157 g/cm³.
Calculate the mass fraction crystallinity using the formula: X_c = (ρ - ρ_a) / (ρ_c - ρ_a) * (ρ_c / ρ).
The result is the crystallinity of the semicrystalline polymer in mass fraction.
