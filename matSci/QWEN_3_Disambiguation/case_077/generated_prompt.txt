Use the lever rule to calculate the mass fractions of proeutectoid ferrite and pearlite.  
The initial carbon concentration is 0.60 wt% C.  
The eutectoid carbon concentration is 0.76 wt% C.  
The solubility of ferrite at the eutectoid temperature is 0.022 wt% C.  
Calculate the mass fraction of proeutectoid ferrite using (0.76 - 0.60) / (0.76 - 0.022).  
Calculate the mass fraction of pearlite using (0.60 - 0.022) / (0.76 - 0.022).  
The sum of the mass fractions must equal 1.
