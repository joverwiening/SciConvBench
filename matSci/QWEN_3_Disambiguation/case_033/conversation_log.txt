Q: What is the temperature condition or phase rule (e.g., lever rule application) to be used for determining the fraction of liquid just above the eutectic temperature?
A: Use the lever rule at a temperature just above the eutectic temperature, where the alloy is in the α + L (liquid) two-phase region. The weight fraction of liquid is calculated using the lever rule with the overall composition C0, the α-phase composition C_αe, and the liquid composition C_e.
