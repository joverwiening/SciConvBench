The FCC single crystal is loaded in tension to a stress of 65 MPa.
The tensile axis is oriented such that the slip-plane normal makes an angle φ = 54.7° with the tensile axis.
The slip direction makes an angle λ = 45.0° with the tensile axis.
The resolved shear stress is calculated using the formula τ = σ × cos(φ) × cos(λ).
The resolved shear stress on the active slip system is τ = 65 MPa × cos(54.7°) × cos(45.0°).
The resolved shear stress is approximately 26.5 MPa.
