Calculate the activation energy using the Arrhenius equation with the given diffusion coefficients and temperatures.  
Use the formula:  
ln(D2/D1) = -(Ea/R) * (1/T2 - 1/T1).  
Convert the activation energy from J/mol to kJ/mol.  
The temperatures are in Kelvin.  
The gas constant R is 8.314 J/mol·K.  
The diffusion coefficient D1 is 4.76×10^-18 m²/s at T1 = 866 K.  
The diffusion coefficient D2 is 1.86×10^-12 m²/s at T2 = 1438 K.  
The activation energy Ea is to be calculated in kJ/mol.

  
Use the Arrhenius equation in the form ln(D2/D1) = -(Ea/R) * (1/T2 - 1/T1).  
Substitute D1 = 4.76×10^-18 m²/s and D2 = 1.86×10^-12 m²/s.  
Substitute T1 = 866 K and T2 = 1438 K.  
Use R = 8.314 J/mol·K.  
Calculate ln(D2/D1) = ln((1.86×10^-12)/(4.76×10^-18)).  
Calculate (1/T2 - 1/T1) = (1/1438 - 1/866).  
Solve for Ea in J/mol using Ea = -[ln(D2/D1) * R] / (1/T2 - 1/T1).  
Convert Ea from J/mol to kJ/mol by dividing by 1000.  
Report the activation energy in kJ/mol.
