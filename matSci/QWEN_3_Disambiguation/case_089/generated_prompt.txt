Use the rule of mixtures for unidirectional composites to estimate the effective thermal conductivity along the fiber direction.
Multiply the volume fraction of carbon fibers (0.55) by their thermal conductivity (18 W/m·K).
Multiply the volume fraction of epoxy (0.45) by its thermal conductivity (0.25 W/m·K).
Sum the two products to obtain the effective thermal conductivity.
The effective thermal conductivity along the fibers is calculated as (0.55 × 18) + (0.45 × 0.25) W/m·K.
