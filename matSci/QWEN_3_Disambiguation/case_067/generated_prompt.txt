The metal has a body-centered cubic (BCC) crystal structure.  
The lattice parameter is a = 0.286 nm.  
The close-packed direction for linear density calculation is [111].  
Atoms are considered as hard spheres.  
The linear density is defined as the number of atoms per unit length along the [111] direction.  
The [111] direction in BCC passes through the body center atom and connects opposite corners of the cube.  
The length of the [111] direction vector is √3 times the lattice parameter a.  
There are 2 atoms along the [111] direction: one full atom at the body center and two half atoms at the corners, contributing 1 atom in total.  
The total number of atoms fully lying along the [111] direction is 2.  
The linear density is calculated as 2 / (√3 × a).  
The result is expressed in units of atoms per nanometer.
