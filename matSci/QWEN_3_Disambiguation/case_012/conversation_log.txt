Q: What is the meaning of the numbers in parentheses following each molecular weight? For example, what does (96) represent for the 56526 g/mol component?
A: The numbers in parentheses represent the number of molecules (or mole fraction units) for each molecular weight. For example, (96) means there are 96 molecules (or units) with a molecular weight of 56526 g/mol.
