Calculate the number-average molecular weight \( M_n \) using the formula:  
\( M_n = \frac{\sum (M_i \times N_i)}{\sum N_i} \),  
where \( M_i \) is the molecular weight of each component and \( N_i \) is the number of molecules of that component.  
Use the given data:  
\( M_1 = 56526 \) g/mol, \( N_1 = 96 \),  
\( M_2 = 92809 \) g/mol, \( N_2 = 131 \),  
\( M_3 = 115856 \) g/mol, \( N_3 = 153 \),  
\( M_4 = 126029 \) g/mol, \( N_4 = 146 \).  
Compute the sum of \( M_i \times N_i \) for all components.  
Compute the total sum of \( N_i \) for all components.  
Divide the total weighted molecular weight by the total number of molecules to obtain \( M_n \).  
Report \( M_n \) in g/mol.
