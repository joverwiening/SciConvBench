Use a rectangular semiconductor sample with a thickness of 0.50 mm in the direction perpendicular to both the current and the magnetic field.
Apply a current of 15 mA along the length of the sample.
Apply a magnetic field of 0.80 T perpendicular to the current direction.
Measure the Hall voltage as 24 mV across the thickness of the sample.
Calculate the carrier concentration using the formula: n = (I * B) / (V_H * e * d), where I is the current, B is the magnetic field, V_H is the Hall voltage, e is the elementary charge (1.602e-19 C), and d is the thickness of the sample.
Ensure all units are in SI: convert mA to A, mV to V, and mm to m before calculation.
