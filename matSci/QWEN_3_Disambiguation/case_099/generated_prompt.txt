Assume the cold side temperature is 300 K (27°C), a common reference temperature for thermoelectric device analysis.

The hot side temperature is 360 K (87°C), calculated as the cold side temperature plus ΔT (300 K + 60 K).

The Seebeck coefficient of the p-type material is +180 μV/K.

The open-circuit thermoelectric voltage is calculated using the formula: V = S × ΔT.

The open-circuit voltage is 10.8 mV (180 μV/K × 60 K).
