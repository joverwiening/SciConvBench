Q: What is the number of atoms per unit volume (in atoms/m³) for the ferromagnetic metal?
A: Use the density (7.87 g/cm³) and atomic weight (55.85 g/mol) to calculate the number of atoms per unit volume. First, convert density to kg/m³: 7.87 g/cm³ = 7870 kg/m³. Then use Avogadro's number (6.022×10²³ atoms/mol) and atomic weight to find the atomic density:

n = (density × Avogadro's number) / atomic weight  
n = (7.87 g/cm³ × 6.022×10²³ atoms/mol) / 55.85 g/mol  
Convert cm³ to m³: 7.87 g/cm³ = 7.87×10⁶ g/m³

So:  
n = (7.87×10⁶ g/m³ × 6.022×10²³ atoms/mol) / 55.85 g/mol ≈ 8.49×10²⁸ atoms/m³
