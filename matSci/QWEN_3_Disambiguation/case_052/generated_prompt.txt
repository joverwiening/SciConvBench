The ceramic crystal has a cubic unit cell with 1 formula unit per unit cell.  
The molar mass of one formula unit is 101.97 g/mol.  
The unit cell edge length is 0.6330 nm.  
Convert the unit cell edge length to centimeters: 0.6330 nm = 6.330 × 10⁻⁸ cm.  
Calculate the volume of the unit cell: (6.330 × 10⁻⁸ cm)³ = 2.536 × 10⁻²² cm³.  
Calculate the mass of one formula unit: 101.97 g/mol ÷ (6.022 × 10²³ mol⁻¹) = 1.693 × 10⁻²² g.  
The theoretical density is the mass of one formula unit divided by the unit cell volume: (1.693 × 10⁻²² g) ÷ (2.536 × 10⁻²² cm³).  
Compute the result to obtain the theoretical density in g/cm³.
