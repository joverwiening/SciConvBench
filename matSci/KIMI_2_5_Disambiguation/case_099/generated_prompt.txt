The thermoelectric leg is p-type with a Seebeck coefficient of +180 μV/K.
The temperature difference across the leg is ΔT = 60 K.
Assume a cold side temperature of T_cold = 300 K and a hot side temperature of T_hot = 360 K to satisfy the 60 K difference.
Calculate the open-circuit thermoelectric voltage using V = S × ΔT.
Substitute the values: V = (180 × 10^-6 V/K) × (60 K) = 0.0108 V = 10.8 mV.
Report the estimated open-circuit thermoelectric voltage as approximately 10.8 mV.
