Calculate the equilibrium potential for the Cu²⁺/Cu electrode using the Nernst equation at 25°C.
Use the standard reduction potential E° = +0.340 V versus SHE for the Cu²⁺/Cu half-reaction.
Use the Cu²⁺ activity of 0.010 in the calculation.
Apply the Nernst equation: E = E° - (RT/nF)ln(Q), where n = 2 for the Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu reaction.
At 25°C (298.15 K), use the simplified form: E = E° - (0.05916 V/n)log₁₀(Q).
For the reduction half-reaction, Q = 1/a(Cu²⁺) = 1/0.010 = 100.
Substitute values: E = 0.340 V - (0.05916 V/2) × log₁₀(100).
Calculate: E = 0.340 V - (0.02958 V × 2) = 0.340 V - 0.05916 V = 0.281 V.
Report the final equilibrium potential as approximately +0.281 V versus SHE.
