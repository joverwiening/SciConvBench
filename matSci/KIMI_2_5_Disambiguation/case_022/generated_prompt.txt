The metal has a cubic crystal structure.
The atomic weight of the metal is 85.15 g/mol.
The theoretical density of the metal is 13.931 g/cm^3.
Avogadro's number N_A is 6.022×10^23 mol^-1.
The lattice parameter must be calculated in nanometers (nm).
The relationship between density, atomic weight, and lattice parameter for a cubic structure is: ρ = (n × A) / (N_A × a^3), where n is the number of atoms per unit cell.
For a simple cubic structure, n = 1.
For a body-centered cubic (BCC) structure, n = 2.
For a face-centered cubic (FCC) structure, n = 4.
The lattice parameter a is calculated as: a = (n × A / (N_A × ρ))^(1/3).
The result must be converted from cm to nm by multiplying by 10^7.
The final answer must be reported in nm with appropriate significant figures.
