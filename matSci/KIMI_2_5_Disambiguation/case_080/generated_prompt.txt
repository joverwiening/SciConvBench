The ionic conductivity shall be estimated using the Nernst-Einstein relation.

The Nernst-Einstein relation states that ionic conductivity sigma equals (n * q^2 * D) / (k_B * T), where n is the mobile-ion number density, q is the elementary charge, D is the diffusivity, k_B is the Boltzmann constant, and T is the absolute temperature.

The given values are n = 4.0×10^27 m^-3, D = 1.2×10^-11 m^2/s, and T = 800 K.

The elementary charge q equals 1.602×10^-19 C.

The Boltzmann constant k_B equals 1.381×10^-23 J/K.

The calculation proceeds as sigma = (4.0×10^27 m^-3 × (1.602×10^-19 C)^2 × 1.2×10^-11 m^2/s) / (1.381×10^-23 J/K × 800 K).

The numerator equals 4.0×10^27 × 2.566×10^-38 × 1.2×10^-11 = 1.23×10^-21 C^2·m^-1·s^-1.

The denominator equals 1.105×10^-20 J.

The resulting conductivity equals approximately 0.11 S/m or 0.11 Ω^-1·m^-1.
