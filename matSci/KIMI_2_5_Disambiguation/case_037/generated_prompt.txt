The composite wall consists of two layers arranged in series for heat conduction.

The first layer has a thickness of 10.91 mm and a thermal conductivity of 4.69 W/m·K.

The second layer has a thickness of 8.09 mm and a thermal conductivity of 6.75 W/m·K.

The wall area is 0.0782 m².

The hot surface temperature is 191.8°C.

The cold surface temperature is 21.2°C.

The thermal resistance of the first layer is calculated as R1 = L1 / (k1 * A) = 0.01091 / (4.69 * 0.0782) = 0.02976 K/W.

The thermal resistance of the second layer is calculated as R2 = L2 / (k2 * A) = 0.00809 / (6.75 * 0.0782) = 0.01533 K/W.

The total thermal resistance is R_total = R1 + R2 = 0.02976 + 0.01533 = 0.04509 K/W.

The temperature difference across the wall is delta_T = 191.8 - 21.2 = 170.6°C.

The steady heat-transfer rate is Q = delta_T / R_total = 170.6 / 0.04509 = 3783.5 W.
