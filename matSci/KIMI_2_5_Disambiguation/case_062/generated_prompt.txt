The fiber volume fraction is computed using the rule of mixtures for density.

The mass fraction of fibers is given as 0.795.
The mass fraction of matrix is 0.205.

The density of the composite is calculated as the harmonic mean based on mass fractions:
1/ρ_c = w_f/ρ_f + w_m/ρ_m

Substitute the values:
1/ρ_c = 0.795/3.711 + 0.205/1.609 = 0.2142 + 0.1274 = 0.3416 g/cm³

Therefore:
ρ_c = 1/0.3416 = 2.928 g/cm³

The fiber volume fraction is then:
V_f = (w_f × ρ_c) / ρ_f = (0.795 × 2.928) / 3.711 = 0.627

The fiber volume fraction is 0.627 or 62.7%.
