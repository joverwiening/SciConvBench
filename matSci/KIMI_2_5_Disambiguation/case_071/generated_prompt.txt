The system involves steady one-dimensional diffusion across a flat ceramic membrane.

Fick's first law governs the diffusive flux: J = -D × (dC/dx).

For steady diffusion across a plane wall with constant diffusivity, the concentration profile is linear and the flux equation simplifies to: J = D × (C1 - C2) / L.

The membrane thickness L is 1.5 mm, which equals 1.5 × 10^-3 m.

The diffusivity D is 2.2 × 10^-11 m²/s.

The concentration on the high-concentration face C1 is 0.12 mol/m³.

The concentration on the low-concentration face C2 is 0.02 mol/m³.

The concentration difference (C1 - C2) is 0.10 mol/m³.

Calculate the diffusive flux: J = (2.2 × 10^-11 m²/s) × (0.10 mol/m³) / (1.5 × 10^-3 m).

The diffusive flux J equals 1.47 × 10^-9 mol/(m²·s), or equivalently 1.47 nmol/(m²·s).
