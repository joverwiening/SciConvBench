The powder consists of spherical particles with density ρ = 6.2 g/cm³.
The average particle diameter is d = 0.80 μm.
The specific surface area S is defined as the total surface area per unit mass of powder.
For spherical particles, the volume of a single particle is V = (π/6)d³.
The mass of a single particle is m = ρV = (π/6)ρd³.
The surface area of a single particle is A = πd².
The specific surface area is S = A/m = πd² / [(π/6)ρd³] = 6/(ρd).
Convert units: ρ = 6.2 g/cm³ = 6.2 × 10⁶ g/m³, d = 0.80 μm = 0.80 × 10⁻⁶ m.
Calculate S = 6 / [(6.2 × 10⁶ g/m³)(0.80 × 10⁻⁶ m)] = 6 / (6.2 × 0.80 g/m²) = 6 / 4.96 m²/g = 1.21 m²/g.
The estimated specific surface area is approximately 1.2 m²/g.
