The composite wall consists of two layers arranged in series.
Layer 1 has a thickness of 9.74 mm (0.00974 m) and thermal conductivity of 12.31 W/m·K.
Layer 2 has a thickness of 13.27 mm (0.01327 m) and thermal conductivity of 107.49 W/m·K.
The wall area is 0.0725 m².
The outer surface temperature of Layer 1 is maintained at 198.1°C.
The outer surface temperature of Layer 2 is maintained at -25.4°C.
Contact resistance between the layers is neglected.
Calculate the thermal resistance of Layer 1 using R1 = L1 / (k1 * A).
Calculate the thermal resistance of Layer 2 using R2 = L2 / (k2 * A).
Calculate the total thermal resistance as R_total = R1 + R2.
Calculate the temperature difference across the composite wall as ΔT = T_hot - T_cold = 198.1°C - (-25.4°C) = 223.5°C.
Calculate the steady heat-transfer rate using Q = ΔT / R_total.
Report the final heat-transfer rate in Watts (W).
