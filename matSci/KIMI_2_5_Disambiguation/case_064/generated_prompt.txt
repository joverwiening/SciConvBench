The resolved shear stress on the active slip system shall be calculated using Schmid's Law.

The applied tensile stress is 65 MPa.

The angle between the slip-plane normal and the tensile axis is 54.7 degrees.

The angle between the slip direction and the tensile axis is 45.0 degrees.

The resolved shear stress equals the applied tensile stress multiplied by the cosine of the angle between the slip-plane normal and tensile axis multiplied by the cosine of the angle between the slip direction and tensile axis.

The calculation is: τ = σ × cos(φ) × cos(λ) = 65 MPa × cos(54.7°) × cos(45.0°).

The resolved shear stress on the active slip system is approximately 26.5 MPa.
