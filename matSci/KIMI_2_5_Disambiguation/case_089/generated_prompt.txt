The composite is a unidirectional fiber-reinforced material with continuous carbon fibers aligned in one direction.

The fiber volume fraction is 55% (0.55).

The matrix volume fraction is 45% (0.45).

The thermal conductivity of the carbon fibers is 18 W/m·K.

The thermal conductivity of the epoxy matrix is 0.25 W/m·K.

For thermal conduction along the fibers (longitudinal direction), use the rule of mixtures (parallel model).

Calculate the effective thermal conductivity using: k_eff = V_f * k_f + V_m * k_m.

Substitute values: k_eff = (0.55 × 18) + (0.45 × 0.25).

Compute: k_eff = 9.9 + 0.1125 = 10.0125 W/m·K.

Report the effective thermal conductivity along the fibers as approximately 10.0 W/m·K.
