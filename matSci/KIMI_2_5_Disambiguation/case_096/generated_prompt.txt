The critical field H_c at temperature T follows the relation H_c(T) = H_c(0)[1 - (T/T_c)^2].

The critical field at zero temperature H_c(0) is 0.080 T.

The critical temperature T_c is 7.20 K.

The temperature of interest T is 4.2 K.

Calculate the ratio T/T_c = 4.2 / 7.20 = 0.5833.

Calculate (T/T_c)^2 = (0.5833)^2 = 0.3403.

Calculate 1 - (T/T_c)^2 = 1 - 0.3403 = 0.6597.

Calculate H_c(4.2 K) = 0.080 T × 0.6597 = 0.0528 T.

The critical field at 4.2 K is approximately 0.053 T or 5.3 × 10^-2 T.
