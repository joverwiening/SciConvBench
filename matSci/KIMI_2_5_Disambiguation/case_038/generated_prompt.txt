The steady heat-transfer rate through a composite wall in series is calculated using the total thermal resistance.

Calculate the thermal resistance of layer 1: R1 = L1 / (k1 * A) = 0.01679 m / (24.68 W/m·K * 0.2423 m²) = 0.002807 K/W.

Calculate the thermal resistance of layer 2: R2 = L2 / (k2 * A) = 0.00873 m / (94.15 W/m·K * 0.2423 m²) = 0.0003827 K/W.

Calculate total thermal resistance: R_total = R1 + R2 = 0.003190 K/W.

Calculate temperature difference: ΔT = T1 - T2 = 134.0°C - (-26.8°C) = 160.8°C = 160.8 K.

Calculate heat-transfer rate: Q = ΔT / R_total = 160.8 K / 0.003190 K/W = 50407 W.

The steady heat-transfer rate is 50407 W (or approximately 50.4 kW).
