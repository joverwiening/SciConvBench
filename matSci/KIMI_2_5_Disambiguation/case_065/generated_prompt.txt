The crystal structure is Face-Centered Cubic (FCC) with lattice parameter a = 0.404 nm.

The Burgers vector of a perfect dislocation in an FCC crystal lies along the close-packed direction and has the form b = (a/2)<110>.

The magnitude of the Burgers vector is calculated as |b| = (a/2) * sqrt(1^2 + 1^2 + 0^2) = (a/2) * sqrt(2) = a/sqrt(2).

Substituting a = 0.404 nm: |b| = 0.404 nm / sqrt(2) = 0.286 nm (or 2.86 x 10^-10 m, or approximately 0.29 nm).
