The plating process deposits metal onto an area of 111.01 cm^2 at a current of 2.479 A for 14.6 minutes.
The metal has a molar mass of 151.13 g/mol and a density of 10.754 g/cm^3.
Assume 100% current efficiency.
Use Faraday's constant F = 96485 C/mol.
Compute the average coating thickness in micrometers (μm).

The calculation proceeds as follows:
First, calculate the total charge passed: Q = I × t = 2.479 A × (14.6 × 60) s = 2171.604 C.
Then, calculate moles of electrons: n_e = Q / F = 2171.604 / 96485 = 0.022507 mol e⁻.
Assuming the metal ion has charge +2 (common for many plating metals), moles of metal deposited = n_e / 2 = 0.0112535 mol.
Mass of metal deposited = moles × molar mass = 0.0112535 × 151.13 = 1.7008 g.
Volume of metal deposited = mass / density = 1.7008 / 10.754 = 0.15816 cm³.
Thickness = Volume / Area = 0.15816 / 111.01 = 0.0014247 cm = 14.247 μm.

The average coating thickness is 14.2 μm (or more precisely, 14.25 μm).
