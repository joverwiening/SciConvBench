The electrical conductivity of an n-type semiconductor is determined by the majority carrier concentration and mobility.

Use the formula for electrical conductivity: σ = n × e × μₙ.

Substitute the given values: n = 2.0 × 10²¹ m⁻³, e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C, and μₙ = 0.12 m²/V·s.

Calculate: σ = (2.0 × 10²¹ m⁻³) × (1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C) × (0.12 m²/V·s).

The electrical conductivity is σ = 38.4 (Ω·m)⁻¹ or 38.4 S/m.
