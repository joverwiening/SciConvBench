The Larson-Miller parameter equation is P = T(C + log10 t_r).

The given values are:
P = 23,500
T = 700°C = 973.15 K (temperature must be in absolute units, Kelvin)
C = 20

Rearrange the equation to solve for log10 t_r:
log10 t_r = (P / T) - C

Substitute the values:
log10 t_r = (23,500 / 973.15) - 20
log10 t_r = 24.148 - 20
log10 t_r = 4.148

Calculate t_r:
t_r = 10^4.148
t_r = 14,060 hours

The estimated rupture life is approximately 14,060 hours.
