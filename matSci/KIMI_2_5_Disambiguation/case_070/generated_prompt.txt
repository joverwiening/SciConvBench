The biaxial thermal stress in a thin film on a rigid substrate is calculated using the Stoney equation approximation for thermal mismatch.

The biaxial modulus of the film is M_f = E_f / (1 - ν_f) = 160 GPa / (1 - 0.28) = 222.22 GPa.

The thermal strain mismatch between film and substrate is Δε = (α_f - α_s) × ΔT = (14×10^-6 K^-1 - 6×10^-6 K^-1) × (-250 K) = -2.0×10^-3.

The biaxial thermal stress in the film is σ_f = M_f × Δε = 222.22 GPa × (-2.0×10^-3) = -444.4 MPa.

The negative sign indicates compressive stress.

The estimated biaxial thermal stress in the film is 444 MPa (compressive).
