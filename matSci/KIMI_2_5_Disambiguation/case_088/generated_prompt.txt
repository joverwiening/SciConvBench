The theoretical specific capacity of an FePO₄ cathode is calculated as follows.

The specific capacity formula is: Specific Capacity (mAh/g) = (n × F) / (M × 3600) × 1000, where n is the number of electrons transferred, F is Faraday's constant (96485 C/mol), and M is the molar mass.

For FePO₄ with x = 1 mole of Li exchanged per mole of active material, one electron is transferred per formula unit (n = 1).

The calculation is: (1 × 96485 C/mol) / (150.82 g/mol × 3600 s/h) × 1000 mAh/Ah = 177.6 mAh/g.

Alternatively expressed: (96485 × 1000) / (150.82 × 3600) = 177.6 mAh/g.

The theoretical specific capacity of the FePO₄ cathode is approximately 178 mAh/g (or more precisely, 177.6 mAh/g).
