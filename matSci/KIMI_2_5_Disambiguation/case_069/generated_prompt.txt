The thermal-mismatch strain in the film is calculated as the difference in thermal contraction between the film and the substrate when cooled.

The thermal strain in the aluminum film if it were free to contract would be ε_f = α_f × ΔT = 23×10^-6 K^-1 × 180 K = 4.14×10^-3.

The thermal strain in the ceramic substrate if it were free to contract would be ε_s = α_s × ΔT = 8×10^-6 K^-1 × 180 K = 1.44×10^-3.

Since the film is bonded to the substrate, the film is constrained and the thermal-mismatch strain is the difference between what the film would contract and what the substrate actually contracts.

The thermal-mismatch strain in the film is ε_mismatch = (α_f - α_s) × ΔT = (23×10^-6 K^-1 - 8×10^-6 K^-1) × 180 K = 15×10^-6 K^-1 × 180 K = 2.7×10^-3 or 0.0027.

This is a tensile strain in the film since the aluminum wants to contract more than the ceramic substrate allows.
