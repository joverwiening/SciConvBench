The critical fiber length (l_c) for short-fiber reinforcement is calculated using the formula: l_c = (σ_f * d) / (2 * τ_c), where σ_f is the fiber tensile strength, d is the fiber diameter, and τ_c is the fiber-matrix interfacial shear strength.

Substitute the given values: σ_f = 3690.4 MPa, d = 11.05 μm, and τ_c = 15.33 MPa.

Calculate the numerator: σ_f * d = 3690.4 MPa * 11.05 μm = 40778.92 MPa·μm.

Calculate the denominator: 2 * τ_c = 2 * 15.33 MPa = 30.66 MPa.

Divide to find l_c: l_c = 40778.92 / 30.66 = 1329.38 μm.

The critical fiber length is 1329.38 μm.
