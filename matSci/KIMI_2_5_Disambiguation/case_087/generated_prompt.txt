Calculate the work of adhesion using the Young-Dupré equation: W_a = γ_LV(1 + cos θ).

Use the liquid-vapor surface tension of water γ_LV = 72.8 mN/m.

Use the contact angle θ = 72°.

Convert the contact angle to radians for calculation if required by the computation method, or use degree mode in the calculator.

Compute cos(72°) = 0.3090.

Compute (1 + cos θ) = 1 + 0.3090 = 1.3090.

Compute W_a = 72.8 mN/m × 1.3090 = 95.3 mN/m.

Express the final answer in units of mN/m (equivalent to mJ/m²).

The work of adhesion is approximately 95.3 mJ/m².
