The equilibrium number of vacancies in a crystalline solid is determined by the Boltzmann distribution and depends on the vacancy formation energy and temperature.

Calculate the number of lattice sites per unit volume N using the formula N = (ρ × N_A) / A, where ρ is density, N_A is Avogadro's number, and A is atomic weight.

Substitute the given values: N = (9.434 g/cm³ × 6.022×10²³ mol⁻¹) / (191.53 g/mol) = 2.965×10²² atoms/cm³.

Convert the vacancy formation energy to consistent units: E_v = 121.4 kJ/mol = 121400 J/mol.

Calculate the dimensionless ratio E_v / (R × T) = 121400 / (8.314 × 985) = 14.82.

Calculate the vacancy concentration ratio: exp(-E_v / RT) = exp(-14.82) = 3.63×10⁻⁷.

Calculate the equilibrium number of vacancies per cm³: n_v = N × exp(-E_v / RT) = 2.965×10²² × 3.63×10⁻⁷ = 1.08×10¹⁶ vacancies/cm³.

The equilibrium number of vacancies at 985 K is approximately 1.1×10¹⁶ vacancies per cubic centimeter.
