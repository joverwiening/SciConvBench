The ceramic has a cubic unit cell structure.
The formula-unit molar mass is 182.07 g/mol.
The lattice parameter is 0.6300 nm.
The theoretical density shall be calculated in g/cm^3.
Use Avogadro's number N_A = 6.022×10^23 mol^-1.
Assume Z = 4 formula units per unit cell (typical for rock salt or similar cubic structures).
Convert lattice parameter to cm: a = 0.6300 nm = 0.6300 × 10^-7 cm = 6.300 × 10^-8 cm.
Calculate unit cell volume: V = a^3 = (6.300 × 10^-8 cm)^3 = 2.50047 × 10^-22 cm^3.
Calculate mass of unit cell: m = Z × M / N_A = 4 × 182.07 g/mol / (6.022 × 10^23 mol^-1) = 1.2094 × 10^-21 g.
Calculate theoretical density: ρ = m / V = (1.2094 × 10^-21 g) / (2.50047 × 10^-22 cm^3) = 4.837 g/cm^3.
