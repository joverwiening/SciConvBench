The creep strain rate shall be calculated using Norton's law: \dot{ε} = Aσ^n.

The constant A shall be 2.5×10^-18 MPa^-n s^-1.

The stress exponent n shall be 4.6.

The applied stress σ shall be 110 MPa.

The calculation shall be: \dot{ε} = (2.5×10^-18) × (110)^4.6.

The creep strain rate \dot{ε} shall be approximately 1.03×10^-8 s^-1 (or 10.3×10^-9 s^-1, or about 10.3 ns^-1).
