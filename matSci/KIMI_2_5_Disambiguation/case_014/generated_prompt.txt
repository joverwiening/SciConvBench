The task is to calculate the mass fraction crystallinity of a semicrystalline polymer sample.

Use the two-phase model where the semicrystalline polymer is treated as a mixture of amorphous and crystalline phases.

Apply the rule of mixtures for specific volume: 1/ρ_s = w_c/ρ_c + (1-w_c)/ρ_a, where w_c is the mass fraction crystallinity.

Rearrange to solve for w_c: w_c = (ρ_c/ρ_s) × (ρ_s - ρ_a)/(ρ_c - ρ_a).

Substitute the given values: ρ_s = 1.1973 g/cm³, ρ_a = 0.8845 g/cm³, ρ_c = 1.4240 g/cm³.

Calculate ρ_s - ρ_a = 1.1973 - 0.8845 = 0.3128 g/cm³.

Calculate ρ_c - ρ_a = 1.4240 - 0.8845 = 0.5395 g/cm³.

Calculate the ratio (ρ_s - ρ_a)/(ρ_c - ρ_a) = 0.3128/0.5395 = 0.5798.

Calculate ρ_c/ρ_s = 1.4240/1.1973 = 1.1893.

Compute w_c = 1.1893 × 0.5798 = 0.6896, or 68.96%.

Report the crystallinity as approximately 69% or more precisely 0.690 (mass fraction).
