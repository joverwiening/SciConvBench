The degree of crystallinity (Xc) of a semicrystalline polymer is calculated using the ratio of the measured heat of fusion to the heat of fusion of 100% crystalline material.

Calculate the degree of crystallinity using the formula: Xc = (ΔHf / ΔH°f) × 100%.
