The hypoeutectic binary alloy has overall composition C_0 = 30 wt% B.
The eutectic composition is C_E = 40 wt% B.
The composition of the alpha phase at the eutectic temperature is C_αe = 12 wt% B.
The fraction of eutectic microconstituent present just below the eutectic temperature is calculated using the lever rule applied to the eutectic microconstituent.
The formula for the fraction of eutectic microconstituent is: f_eutectic = (C_0 - C_αe) / (C_E - C_αe).
Substituting the values: f_eutectic = (30 - 12) / (40 - 12) = 18 / 28 = 0.643 or 64.3%.
The fraction of eutectic microconstituent present just below the eutectic temperature is 0.643 or 64.3%.
