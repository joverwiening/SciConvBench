The diffusion coefficient follows Arrhenius behavior: D = D₀ exp(-Q/RT), where Q is the activation energy.

Take the ratio of the two diffusion coefficients to eliminate D₀:
D₂/D₁ = exp[-Q/R × (1/T₂ - 1/T₁)]

Solve for Q:
ln(D₂/D₁) = -Q/R × (1/T₂ - 1/T₁)
Q = R × ln(D₂/D₁) / (1/T₁ - 1/T₂)

Convert temperatures to Kelvin:
T₁ = 771 + 273 = 1044 K
T₂ = 1233 + 273 = 1506 K

Calculate the temperature term:
1/T₁ - 1/T₂ = 1/1044 - 1/1506 = 9.579×10⁻⁴ - 6.640×10⁻⁴ = 2.939×10⁻⁴ K⁻¹

Calculate the logarithm term:
ln(D₂/D₁) = ln(1.13×10⁻¹¹ / 4.27×10⁻¹⁵) = ln(2646.4) = 7.881

Calculate Q in J/mol:
Q = 8.314 × 7.881 / 2.939×10⁻⁴ = 223,100 J/mol

Convert to kJ/mol:
Q = 223.1 kJ/mol
