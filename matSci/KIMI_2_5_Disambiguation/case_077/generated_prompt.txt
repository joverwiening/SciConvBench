The steel composition is 0.60 wt% C, which is hypoeutectoid (below 0.76 wt% C).

The eutectoid composition is 0.76 wt% C.

The maximum solubility of carbon in ferrite is 0.022 wt% C.

Use the lever rule to calculate mass fractions.

The mass fraction of proeutectoid ferrite equals (0.76 minus 0.60) divided by (0.76 minus 0.022), which equals 0.16 divided by 0.738, giving 0.217 or 21.7%.

The mass fraction of pearlite equals (0.60 minus 0.022) divided by (0.76 minus 0.022), which equals 0.578 divided by 0.738, giving 0.783 or 78.3%.

Alternatively, the pearlite mass fraction can be found as 1 minus 0.217 equals 0.783.
