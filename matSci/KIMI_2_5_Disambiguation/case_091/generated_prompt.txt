The total porosity of a ceramic specimen is calculated using the relationship between bulk density and theoretical density.

Use the theoretical density of fully dense zirconia: 6.10 g/cm^3.

Use the measured bulk density of the specimen: 5.42 g/cm^3.

Calculate total porosity using the formula: Total Porosity = (1 - bulk density / theoretical density) × 100%.

Perform the calculation: (1 - 5.42 / 6.10) × 100% = (1 - 0.8885) × 100% = 11.15%.

Report the total porosity as approximately 11.1% or 11.15%.
