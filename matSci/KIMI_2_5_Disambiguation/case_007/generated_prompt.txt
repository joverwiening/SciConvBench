The alloy has overall composition C0 = 14.36% B.
The α phase composition at eutectic temperature is C_αe = 4.63% B.
The eutectic liquid composition is C_e = 40.31% B.
Just above the eutectic temperature, the alloy consists of proeutectic α phase and liquid.
Apply the lever rule to find the mass fraction of liquid.
The lever arm for liquid is (C0 - C_αe) = (14.36 - 4.63) = 9.73.
The total lever arm is (C_e - C_αe) = (40.31 - 4.63) = 35.68.
The mass fraction of liquid equals (C0 - C_αe) / (C_e - C_αe) = 9.73 / 35.68 = 0.2727.
The fraction of liquid is 0.273 or 27.3%.
This liquid will transform into eutectic microconstituent upon cooling through the eutectic temperature.
