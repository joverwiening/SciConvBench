The Hall-Petch equation is σ_y = σ_0 + k_y d^(-1/2).

Given values are σ_y = 420 MPa, σ_0 = 120 MPa, and k_y = 0.74 MPa·m^(1/2).

Rearrange the equation to solve for grain diameter d.

Substitute the known values: 420 = 120 + 0.74 * d^(-1/2).

Subtract σ_0 from both sides: 300 = 0.74 * d^(-1/2).

Divide by k_y: d^(-1/2) = 300 / 0.74 = 405.405 m^(-1/2).

Square both sides: d^(-1) = 164,351.35 m^(-1).

Take the reciprocal: d = 6.085 × 10^(-6) m.

Convert to micrometers: d = 6.1 μm.

The average grain diameter is approximately 6.1 micrometers.
