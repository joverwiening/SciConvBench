The fracture stress σ_f for a brittle ceramic is calculated using the Griffith fracture criterion.

The formula for fracture stress is: σ_f = sqrt((2 * E * γ_s) / (π * a))

Where:
E = 245.0 GPa = 245.0 × 10^9 Pa
γ_s = 16.457 J/m^2
a = 107.86 μm = 107.86 × 10^-6 m

Calculation:
σ_f = sqrt((2 × 245.0 × 10^9 Pa × 16.457 J/m^2) / (π × 107.86 × 10^-6 m))
σ_f = sqrt((8.06453 × 10^12 Pa·J/m^2) / (3.388 × 10^-4 m))
σ_f = sqrt(2.3803 × 10^16 Pa^2)
σ_f = 1.543 × 10^8 Pa
σ_f = 154.3 MPa

The estimated fracture stress is 154.3 MPa.
