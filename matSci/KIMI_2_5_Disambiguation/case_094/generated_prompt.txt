The elastomer has a crosslink density ν_e of 4.5×10^-5 mol/cm^3.
The absolute temperature is 298 K.
Use the rubber elasticity theory formula for Young's modulus: E = 3ν_eRT.
Convert ν_e to mol/m^3: ν_e = 4.5×10^-5 mol/cm^3 = 4.5×10^-5 × 10^6 mol/m^3 = 45 mol/m^3.
Use the gas constant R = 8.314 J/(mol·K).
Calculate E = 3 × 45 mol/m^3 × 8.314 J/(mol·K) × 298 K.
The estimated Young's modulus is approximately 3.35×10^5 Pa or 0.335 MPa.
