The system is a binary alloy with composition C0 = 25.37% B.

The α phase solubility limit at the eutectic temperature is C_αe = 7.90% B.

The eutectic composition is Ce = 47.78% B.

The eutectic reaction is L → α + β, where liquid transforms to two solid phases at the eutectic temperature.

Just above the eutectic temperature, the alloy consists of α phase and liquid phase only.

Apply the lever rule to the α-L region of the phase diagram using the tie line at the eutectic temperature.

The fraction of liquid just above the eutectic temperature equals (C0 - C_αe) / (Ce - C_αe).

Calculate the fraction of liquid: (25.37 - 7.90) / (47.78 - 7.90) = 17.47 / 39.88 = 0.4381.

The fraction of liquid just above the eutectic temperature is 0.438, or 43.8%.
