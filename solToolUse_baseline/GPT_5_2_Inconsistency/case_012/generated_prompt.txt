Solve Solid Mechanics Problem 16: a quarter plate with a quarter hole under plane-strain small-strain von Mises elastoplasticity.
Use the symmetric quarter domain [0, 100] x [0, 180] mm with a quarter hole of radius 50 mm centered at the origin.
Use small-strain kinematics with displacement unknown u = (u_x, u_y) and strain epsilon = 0.5*(grad(u) + grad(u)^T).
Use associative von Mises plasticity with perfect plasticity (no isotropic or kinematic hardening) and yield stress sigma_Y = 243 MPa.
Use Lamé parameters lambda = 19.44 GPa and mu = 29.17 GPa.
Use plane strain with unit out-of-plane thickness equal to 1 mm.
Impose symmetry boundary conditions on x = 0: u_x = 0 with zero tangential traction.
Impose symmetry boundary conditions on y = 0: u_y = 0 with zero tangential traction.
Before applying the top displacement, fully clamp x = 0 and y = 0 in both displacement components.
On y = 180 mm prescribe displacement u = (0, 1) mm.
Ramp the prescribed top displacement linearly from 0 to 1 mm over the load steps.
Use a reasonable default of about 20 equal load increments and adjust if Newton convergence has trouble.
On x = 100 mm and on the hole boundary, apply traction-free boundary conditions.
Use a locally refined mesh near the hole with target element size about 2 mm on the hole boundary, grading to about 8–10 mm in the far field, with about 15–20 elements along the quarter-hole arc.
Use continuous Lagrange P2 (quadratic) elements for the displacement space.
Use an incremental return-mapping update consistent with plane strain perfect plasticity.
Use Newton iterations for the incremental solve with relative residual tolerance 1e-8, absolute residual tolerance 1e-10, and maximum 25 Newton iterations per load step.
Initialize internal plastic variables to zero everywhere at the start, including plastic strain tensor and equivalent plastic strain, and initialize any stored previous-step stress or plastic multiplier to zero if used.
Save the resulting displacement field to XDMF with filename case_012_displacement.xdmf.
