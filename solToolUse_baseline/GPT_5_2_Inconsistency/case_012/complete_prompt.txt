Task:
Solve Solid Mechanics Problem 16 from the paper: a quarter plate with a quarter hole under plane-strain elastoplasticity.
Case details:

Model:
Small-strain von Mises elastoplasticity with associative flow and perfect plasticity in plane strain.
Unknown:

lambda = 19.44 GPa, mu = 29.17 GPa, yield stress sigma_Y = 243 MPa.
Boundary conditions:
On x = 0 impose symmetry: u_x = 0 and zero tangential traction.
On y = 0 impose symmetry: u_y = 0 and zero tangential traction.
On y = 180 mm prescribe u = (0, 1) mm.
On x = 100 mm and on the hole boundary: traction-free.
Outputs:
Save the resulting displacement field to XDMF.
Implementation notes:
Impose symmetry only on the normal displacement component on x = 0 and y = 0; do not fully clamp those edges.
Use an incremental return-mapping update that is consistent with plane strain perfect plasticity.
