Case ID: case_014
Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
Solver: small-strain linear elasticity in legacy FEniCS (dolfin); solve the 2D plane-stress variational problem with a vector-valued displacement space and a direct linear solver.
Task:
Analyze stress in a semi-circular domain with two fixed points on the base and symmetric vertical point loads under gravity. Implement plane stress elasticity with E=70e9 Pa, ν=0.33. Compute displacement and von Mises stress distributions in a complex constraint configuration.
Case details:
Material: E=70e9, ν=0.33, ρ=2700 kg/m^3, g=9.81 m/s^2. Geometry: Half-disk (radius 1m). Loads: -3 kN at (±0.707,0.707), gravity (-y). Constraints: Points (-1,0) and (1,0) fixed. Mesh: 64 elements.
Implementation notes:
Treat the 2D domain as plane stress with unit thickness and use the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
Compute von Mises from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
Represent each concentrated load with PointSource on the appropriate displacement component instead of smearing it over an edge.
Apply the two symmetric point loads at the stated arc points and fix only the two base points.
