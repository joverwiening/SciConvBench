Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
Solver: small-strain linear elasticity in legacy FEniCS (dolfin); solve the 2D plane-stress variational problem with a vector-valued displacement space and a direct linear solver.
Task:
Analyze stress in a parallelogram-shaped domain with fixed left/right sloped edges and inward normal traction on the horizontal top edge under gravity. Implement plane stress elasticity with E=70e9 Pa, ν=0.33. Compute displacement and von Mises stress distributions.
Case details:
Material: E=70e9, ν=0.33, ρ=2700 kg/m^3, g=9.81 m/s^2. Geometry: Parallelogram vertices (0,0)-(1,0)-(1.2,0.3)-(0.2,0.3). Loads: Inward normal traction σ=5000 Pa (top edge), gravity (-y). Boundaries: Left/right slopes fixed. Mesh: 72 elements.
Implementation notes:
Treat the 2D domain as plane stress with unit thickness and use the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
Compute von Mises from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
When the prompt says normal traction or inward normal traction, interpret the load in the local boundary-normal direction rather than as a fixed Cartesian vector.
Fix both sloped side edges and apply the top load as inward normal traction on the horizontal top edge.
