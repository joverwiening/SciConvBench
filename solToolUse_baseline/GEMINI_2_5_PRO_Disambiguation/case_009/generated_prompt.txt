Simulate stress in a perforated rectangular plate using FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The simulation will solve a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem under plane-stress conditions.
The geometry is a rectangle defined by corners (0, 0) and (1, 0.5) meters, with a circular hole of radius 0.1 meters centered at (0.5, 0.25).
The mesh will be generated using the `mshr` tool with a resolution parameter of 64.
The material is aluminum with Young's modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, and density ρ=2700 kg/m^3.
The vector-valued displacement function space will use second-degree Lagrange elements.
The left edge of the plate (x=0) is clamped, meaning displacement is fixed at zero.
The right, top, and bottom edges of the plate are traction-free.
A body force due to gravity will be applied, with g=9.81 m/s^2 acting in the negative y-direction.
A uniform pressure of 1e5 Pa will be applied as a normal traction on the boundary of the circular hole.
The linear system will be solved using the 'lu' direct solver.
The simulation will compute the displacement field, the in-plane Cauchy stress tensor, and the von Mises stress field.
The final outputs will be the displacement and von Mises stress patterns.
