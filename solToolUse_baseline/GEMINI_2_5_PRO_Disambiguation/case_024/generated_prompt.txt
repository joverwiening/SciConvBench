Use the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin) to solve a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem.
The geometry is a rectangular plate defined by the domain (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) meters.
The plate has a centered circular hole with a radius of 0.05 meters.
The material has a Young's modulus of 200 GPa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.30.
The left edge of the plate (x=0) is fixed, with displacement u=(0,0).
The right edge of the plate (x=1.0) has an applied line traction of t = (2 MPa*m, 0).
All other external boundaries, including the hole boundary, are traction-free.
Use second-order Lagrange elements for the vector-valued displacement function space.
Use a mesh that is refined around the hole.
Use a direct linear solver to solve the system.
Compute the von Mises stress field and save it as an image file named `q3_vm.png`.
Save the displacement field in XDMF format with the filename `q3_displacement.xdmf`.
Report the maximum von Mises stress value found on the boundary of the hole.
Report the stress concentration factor Kt, calculated as the maximum von Mises stress on the hole boundary divided by 2 MPa.
