Simulate the static linear elastic deformation of a cantilever plate with a circular hole in 2D plane stress using FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The geometry is a rectangle defined by the corners (0,0) and (4,1).
There is a circular hole with a radius of 0.3 centered at (1.0, 0.5).
The mesh should be generated such that the circular hole boundary is approximated by 72 segments.
The function space is a second-order vector Lagrange space for the displacement.
The material properties are Young's modulus E = 210e3 and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.3.
The constitutive law is for a plane-stress condition with unit thickness.
The left edge (x=0) is clamped, with the boundary condition u = (0, 0).
A uniform downward traction t = (0, -1e5) is applied to the right edge (x=4.0).
There are no body forces.
Solve the resulting linear system using a direct solver.
Compute the displacement field and the von Mises stress field.
