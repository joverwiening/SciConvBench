Use the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
Solve a quasi-incompressible finite-strain hyperelasticity problem.
The problem is a two-hole strip with follower pressure and prescribed right-edge displacement.
The model is a mixed displacement-pressure neo-Hookean formulation solved with Newton's method.
The analysis is performed under plane strain conditions.
The geometry is a rectangular strip defined by the domain Omega = (0, 1.0) m x (0, 0.20) m.
The strip contains two circular holes, each with a radius of 0.04 m.
The center of the first hole is at (0.40, 0.10) m.
The center of the second hole is at (0.60, 0.10) m.
The material is a large-deformation isotropic quasi-incompressible neo-Hookean solid.
The material has a Young's modulus E of 5 MPa.
The material has a Poisson's ratio nu of 0.49.
The left edge of the strip (x = 0) is fixed, with displacement u_x = 0 and u_y = 0.
The right edge of the strip (x = 1.0) has a prescribed displacement of u_x = 0.060 m and u_y = 0.
A uniform follower pressure of 0.10 MPa is applied to the boundaries of both holes.
The top and bottom edges of the strip are traction-free.
The displacement field u and the hydrostatic pressure p are the unknown fields.
Use a 2nd-degree Lagrange element for the displacement field.
Use a 1st-degree Lagrange element for the pressure field.
Use a characteristic mesh size of 0.01 for the domain discretization.
Apply the boundary conditions and loads over 10 incremental steps.
Compute the von Mises stress from the Cauchy stress tensor.
