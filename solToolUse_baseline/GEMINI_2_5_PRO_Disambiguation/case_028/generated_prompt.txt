The task is to perform a finite element analysis of an L-shaped structure using FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The analysis will solve a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem under plane stress conditions.
The geometry is an L-shaped domain defined by a 1x1 square with a 0.5x0.5 square removed from the top-right corner.
The material has a Young's modulus E = 70e9 Pa, a Poisson's ratio ν = 0.33, and a density ρ = 2700 kg/m^3.
The structure is subjected to a body force due to gravity, with g = 9.81 m/s^2 acting in the negative y-direction.
A horizontal shear traction of 2.65e4 Pa is applied on the boundary segment defined by y=1 and x>0.5.
The left boundary of the domain, defined by x=0, is fully fixed.
The domain will be meshed with an unstructured triangular mesh with a resolution of approximately 64 elements per edge.
The vector-valued displacement field will be approximated using continuous Lagrange finite elements of polynomial degree 2.
The linear system of equations will be solved using a direct solver.
After solving for the displacement field, the in-plane Cauchy stress tensor and the scalar von Mises stress field will be calculated.
A plot of the displacement magnitude will be generated.
A plot of the von Mises stress distribution will be generated.
The maximum value of the von Mises stress in the domain will be printed to the console.
