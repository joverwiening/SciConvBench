Simulate stress in a perforated rectangular plate using the FEniCS legacy (dolfin) library.
The simulation will solve a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem under plane-stress conditions.
Use a direct linear solver for the system of equations.
The geometry is a rectangle of 1.0m by 0.5m.
The rectangle contains a circular hole with a radius of 0.1m centered at (0.5, 0.25).
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa, a Poisson's ratio of 0.33, and a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
The left boundary of the plate (x=0) is fixed, meaning zero displacement.
A body force representing gravity (g=9.81 m/s^2) is applied in the negative y-direction.
A pure counter-clockwise torque of 800 Nm is applied on the right boundary (x=1).
The torque is implemented as a surface traction couple centered at y=0.25.
A traction of +2.0e6 Pa in the y-direction is applied on the segment from y=0.25m to y=0.27m.
A traction of -2.0e6 Pa in the y-direction is applied on the segment from y=0.23m to y=0.25m.
The domain is meshed with approximately 64 elements per edge.
The simulation will assume a unit thickness of 1.0m.
The displacement field and the von Mises stress field should be computed and analyzed.
The von Mises stress is to be calculated from the in-plane Cauchy stress components.
The problem uses a vector-valued function space for the displacement.
