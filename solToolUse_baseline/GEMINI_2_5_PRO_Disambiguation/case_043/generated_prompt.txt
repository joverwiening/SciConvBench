Solve a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem using the FEniCS legacy (dolfin) library.
The analysis domain is a parallelogram defined by the vertices (0,0), (1,0), (1.2,0.3), and (0.2,0.3).

The left and right sloped edges of the parallelogram are fixed, meaning both x and y displacement components are zero.
An inward normal traction of 5000 Pa is applied to the top edge.
The bottom edge is a free surface with no applied tractions or displacement constraints.
A mesh of 72 triangular elements should be used, created with 9 divisions along the bottom edge and 4 divisions along the sloped side.
The vector-valued displacement space should use second-degree polynomial basis functions.
The variational problem should be solved for the displacement field using a direct linear solver.
The final outputs should be the displacement field and the von Mises stress distribution.
