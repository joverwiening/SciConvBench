Solve a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The geometry is an L-shaped domain created by removing a 0.5x0.5 square from the top-right corner of a 1x1 unit square.
The domain vertices are (0,0), (1,0), (1,0.5), (0.5,0.5), (0.5,1), and (0,1).
The material has a Young's modulus E of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio ν of 0.33.
The material has a density ρ of 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force representing gravity acts in the negative y-direction, with g = 9.81 m/s^2.
The left boundary of the domain (x=0) is fixed, meaning the displacement is zero.
A linearly varying vertical traction is applied to the right boundary (x=1, for 0 <= y <= 0.5).
The traction vector is T = (0, σ_yy) where the magnitude is given by the expression σ_yy = -2.65e4 * y.
All other boundaries (top, bottom, and the two inner re-entrant boundaries) are traction-free.
The domain is meshed with a resolution equivalent to 64 elements along the outer unit-length edges.
The problem is solved for the displacement field using a direct linear solver.
After solving for the displacement, the in-plane Cauchy stress tensor is computed.
Finally, the von Mises stress is calculated from the Cauchy stress components.
