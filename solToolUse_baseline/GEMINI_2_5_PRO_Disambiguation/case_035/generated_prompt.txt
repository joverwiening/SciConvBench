Use the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin) for the simulation.
Solve a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem.
The analysis will be based on a plane stress assumption.
The domain is a closed polygon defined by the vertices (0,0), (0.8,0), (0.8,0.2), (0.5,0.2), (0.5,0.5), (0.3,0.5), (0.3,0.2), and (0,0.2).
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.33.
The material has a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
The bottom edge of the domain, from (0,0) to (0.8,0), is fixed.
A body force due to gravity, with g=9.81 m/s^2, acts on the domain.
A vertical point load of -5000 N is applied at the coordinate (0.4, 0.5).
The point load should be implemented using a PointSource on the y-component of the displacement.
The mesh must contain exactly 72 triangular elements.
The mesh must have a vertex located at the point load application coordinate (0.4, 0.5).
Use second-order Lagrange elements for the vector-valued displacement space.
Use a direct linear solver for the system of equations.
Compute the displacement magnitude field as an output.
Compute the von Mises stress field from the in-plane Cauchy stress components as an output.
