Solve a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem using FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The geometry is a rectangular domain defined by (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) meters.
Create a structured mesh of 80x16 elements over the domain.
Define two material subdomains.
The top subdomain, from y=0.10 to y=0.20, is aluminum.
The bottom subdomain, from y=0.00 to y=0.10, is steel.
The material properties for aluminum are Young's modulus E = 70 GPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.30.
The material properties for steel are Young's modulus E = 200 GPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.30.
Use a continuous function space for the displacement field to enforce the perfect bond at the interface.
Use second-degree Lagrange elements for the displacement field's basis functions.
Apply a fixed boundary condition (u_x = 0, u_y = 0) on the left edge (x = 0).
Apply a uniform downward line traction of t = (0, -5000) N/m on the right edge (x = 1).
The top and bottom edges are traction-free.
Use the plane-stress constitutive relation, assuming unit thickness.
Solve the resulting linear system using a direct solver.
