A horizontal traction of +1000 Pa is applied in the x-direction on the top edge (y=0.5).
The problem is modeled under plane stress conditions with a unit thickness.
The mesh should consist of approximately 72 triangular elements.
Use a vector-valued function space for the displacement.
Use second-degree Lagrange polynomials for the finite element basis functions.
