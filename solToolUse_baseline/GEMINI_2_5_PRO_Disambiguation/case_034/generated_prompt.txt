Use the FEniCS legacy (dolfin) library.
Solve a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem.
The analysis is for an L-shaped domain.
The domain is defined by a 1x1 square with a 0.5x0.5 square removed from the top-right corner.
The mesh should have a resolution of 64 elements per unit edge length.
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.33.
The material has a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force due to gravity (g=9.81 m/s^2) acts in the negative y-direction.
The model should use a plane stress constitutive relation with unit thickness.
The left boundary (x=0) is fixed, with zero displacement.
A spatially varying vertical traction is applied to the right boundary (x=1, for y from 0 to 0.5).
This vertical traction varies linearly from 0 Pa at y=0 to -1.325e4 Pa at y=0.5.
All other boundaries are traction-free.
The linear system should be solved using a direct solver.
The primary outputs are the displacement and stress distributions.
Calculate the von Mises stress from the in-plane Cauchy stress components.
