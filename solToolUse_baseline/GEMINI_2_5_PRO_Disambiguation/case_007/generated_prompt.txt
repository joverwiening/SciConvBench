Use the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
Solve a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem.
The problem domain is a rectangle from (0, 0) to (1.0, 0.2) with a rectangular notch removed.
The removed notch is defined by the region from (0, 0.08) to (0.06, 0.12).
The material has a Young's modulus of 200 GPa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.30.
The model is based on a plane-stress assumption.
Use second-degree Lagrange elements for the vector-valued displacement field.
Generate a mesh with approximately 50 elements across the plate's width of 0.2m.
The left boundary, defined by x=0 and y outside the range [0.08, 0.12], is fixed with zero displacement.
The right boundary, defined by x=1.0, has a line traction of (2e6, 0) N/m applied.
All other boundaries, including the top, bottom, and notch surfaces, are traction-free.
Use a direct linear solver to solve the system.
Compute the von Mises stress field from the resulting displacement.
Save the von Mises stress field as a PNG image named `q5_vm.png`.
Save the displacement field in XDMF format with the filename `q5_displacement.xdmf`.
