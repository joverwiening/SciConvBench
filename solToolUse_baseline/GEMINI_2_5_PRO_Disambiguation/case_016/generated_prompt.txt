Simulate 2D plane-stress linear elasticity on a cross-shaped plate with four circular holes using FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The geometry is the union of a horizontal bar from x = -1 to 1 with a width of 0.6 and a vertical bar from y = -1 to 1 with a width of 0.6.
Four circular holes are cut from the plate.
The radius of each hole is 0.12.
The centers of the holes are located at (0.6, 0), (-0.6, 0), (0, 0.6), and (0, -0.6).
The mesh resolution parameter is 100.
Use a second-order vector Lagrange function space for the displacement.
The material has a Young's modulus of 210000 and a Poisson's ratio of 0.3.
The problem is treated as plane-stress with unit thickness.
The left edge of the plate (x = -1) is clamped, with displacement u = (0, 0).
A downward traction vector t = (0, -5e4) is applied to the right edge of the plate (x = 1).
Solve the system using a direct linear solver.
Compute and output the displacement field.
Compute and output the von Mises stress field.
