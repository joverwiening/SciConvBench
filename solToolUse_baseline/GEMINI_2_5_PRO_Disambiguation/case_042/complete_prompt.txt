Task:
Solve Solid Mechanics Problem 11 from the paper: a nearly incompressible perforated strip in plane strain.
Case details:
Geometry:
Let Ω = (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) m be a rectangular strip with a circular hole of radius a = 0.04 m centred at (0.50, 0.10).
Model:
Small-strain linear elasticity in plane strain.
Material:
Young's modulus E = 5 MPa, Poisson's ratio ν = 0.49.
Boundary conditions:
Left edge (x = 0): fixed, u_x = 0, u_y = 0.
Right edge (x = 1.0): prescribed displacement u_x = 0.001 m, u_y = 0.
Top and bottom edges, and the hole boundary: traction-free.
Outputs:
Compute the von Mises field and save q11_vm.png.
Save a colour map of u_x as q11_ux.png.
Save the displacement field in XDMF format.
Implementation notes:
Because ν is close to 0.5, use a mixed displacement-pressure formulation rather than a displacement-only element pair.
Treat the 2D strip as plane strain.
