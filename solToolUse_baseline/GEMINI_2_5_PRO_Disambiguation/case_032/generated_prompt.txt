Solve a 3D nonlinear hyperelasticity problem using the legacy FEniCS (dolfin) library.
The geometry is a unit cube, meshed with `UnitCubeMesh(24, 16, 16)`.
Use a `VectorFunctionSpace` with first-order Lagrange elements.
The material model is compressible neo-Hookean with Young's modulus E = 10.0 and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.3.
The strain energy density function is `psi = (mu/2) * (I_1 - 3) - mu * ln(J) + (lambda/2) * (ln(J))^2`, where `I_1 = tr(C)`.
The Lamé parameters `mu` and `lambda` are derived from the provided E and ν.
The kinematics are defined by `F = I + grad(u)`, `C = F^T F`, and `J = det(F)`.
The problem is to be solved for the displacement field `u` by minimizing the total potential energy using Newton's method.
The face at `x = 0` is clamped, meaning the displacement is zero.
A prescribed rotational displacement is applied to the face at `x = 1`.
The displacement `u = (u_x, u_y, u_z)` on the face `x = 1` is given by `u_x = 0`, `u_y = 0.5 * ( (y - 0.5)*cos(pi/3) - (z - 0.5)*sin(pi/3) + 0.5 - y )`, and `u_z = 0.5 * ( (y - 0.5)*sin(pi/3) + (z - 0.5)*cos(pi/3) + 0.5 - z )`.
A constant body force `B = (0, -0.5, 0)` is applied throughout the domain.
A constant traction `T = (0.1, 0, 0)` is applied on all faces except for `x = 0` and `x = 1`.
