Solve a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The analysis uses a plane stress assumption.
The domain is a parallelogram with vertices at (0,0), (1,0), (1.2,0.3), and (0.2,0.3).
The material has a Young's modulus E of 70e9 Pa, a Poisson's ratio ν of 0.33, and a density ρ of 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force due to gravity (g=9.81 m/s^2) acts in the negative y-direction.
A horizontal traction vector of (6000, 0) Pa is applied to the left slope, the boundary connecting (0,0) and (0.2,0.3).
The right slope, the boundary connecting (1,0) and (1.2,0.3), is fixed with a zero-displacement Dirichlet boundary condition.
The domain is meshed with 72 triangular elements.
The vector function space for displacement uses continuous Lagrange elements of polynomial degree 2.
The linear system is solved using a direct solver.
Compute the displacement field throughout the domain.
Compute the von Mises stress distribution from the in-plane Cauchy stress components.
The implementation should assume a unit thickness for the domain.
