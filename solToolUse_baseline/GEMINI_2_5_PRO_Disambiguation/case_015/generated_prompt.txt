Solve a 2D plane strain solid mechanics problem using the FEniCS legacy (dolfin) library.
The model is an incompressible, finite-strain, neo-Hookean hyperelastic solid.
The geometry is a rectangular strip defined by the domain (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) meters.
The strip has a circular hole of radius 0.04 meters centered at (0.50, 0.10) meters.
The material's Young's modulus is 5 MPa and its Poisson's ratio is 0.5.
A mixed displacement-pressure formulation is used, with displacement `u` and pressure `p` as the unknown fields.
The displacement field is discretized using second-degree Lagrange elements (P2).
The pressure field is discretized using first-degree Lagrange elements (P1).
The mesh is refined around the hole to accurately capture stress concentrations.
The left boundary (x=0) is fixed, with zero displacement in all directions.
The top, bottom, and right boundaries are traction-free.
A follower pressure load with a magnitude of 0.10 MPa is applied to the hole boundary.
The non-linear problem is solved using Newton's method.
The total load is applied incrementally over multiple steps to ensure convergence.
The final displacement field is saved in XDMF format.
A plot of the deformed geometry, with a magnification factor of 5, is saved as `q13_def.png`.
The Cauchy stress tensor is computed from the solution.
The von Mises stress field is calculated from the Cauchy stress.
A plot of the von Mises stress field is saved as `q13_vm.png`.
