Simulate 2D plane-stress linear elasticity in an L-shaped domain using FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The L-shaped domain is created from a 1x1 square with the top-right 0.5x0.5 square removed.
Use a mesh resolution of 64 elements per unit edge length.
The finite element basis functions for the displacement field will be second-degree Lagrange polynomials.
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa, a Poisson's ratio of 0.33, and a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
The simulation must include a body force due to gravity, with g=9.81 m/s^2.
The boundary at x=0 is clamped, with zero displacement.
The boundary at y=0 is clamped, with zero displacement.
A horizontal traction of 2.65e4 Pa is applied to the right edge at x=1.
The problem will be solved using a direct linear solver.
The simulation should output the displacement field and the von Mises stress field.
The results should be saved in a format suitable for visualization.
