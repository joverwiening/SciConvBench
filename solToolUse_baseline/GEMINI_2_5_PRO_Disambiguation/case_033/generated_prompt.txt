The task is to solve a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem using FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The domain is a right triangle with vertices at (0,0), (1,0), and (0,0.5).
The material's Young's modulus is 70e9 Pa and its Poisson's ratio is 0.33.
The material's density is 2700 kg/m^3.
A gravitational body force is applied, with g=9.81 m/s^2 acting in the negative y-direction.
The boundary along the line x+2y=1 is clamped with zero displacement.
A traction vector of (4000, 0) Pa is applied on the boundary where x=0.
The boundary where y=0 is a free surface with zero traction.
A mesh with approximately 64 elements should be used.
The displacement function space should use second-degree Lagrange elements.
The linear system of equations should be solved with a direct solver.
The final outputs should be the displacement field and the von Mises stress field.
