Analyze stress in a semi-circular domain using the FEniCS legacy (dolfin) library.
The analysis will solve the 2D small-strain linear elasticity variational problem under plane stress conditions.
The geometry is a semi-circle with a radius of 1 meter, located in the y>=0 plane.
The material has a Young's modulus (E) of 70e9 Pa, a Poisson's ratio (ν) of 0.33, and a density (ρ) of 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force representing gravity (g=9.81 m/s^2) acts on the domain.
A concentrated vertical point load of -5000 N is applied at the apex of the semi-circle, located at (0, 1).
The base of the semi-circle (at y=0) is fixed, meaning displacement is zero.
The finite element mesh for the domain must contain exactly 64 elements.
The vector-valued displacement space will be discretized using second-degree Lagrange elements.
The concentrated point load will be implemented using a `PointSource` on the y-component of the displacement.
The resulting linear system will be solved using a direct solver.
The displacement distribution vector will be computed.
The von Mises stress distribution will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
The computed displacement field will be saved to a file for visualization.
