Use the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
Solve a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem.
The analysis is based on a plane-stress assumption.
The L-shaped domain is formed by a 1x1 square with its bottom-left corner at (0,0) from which a 0.5x0.5 square in the top-right corner is removed.
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.33.
Include a body force due to gravity, with a density of 2700 kg/m^3 and gravitational acceleration of 9.81 m/s^2 acting in the negative y-direction.
The left boundary (x=0) is fixed (zero displacement).
Apply a vertically varying traction on the right boundary (x=1, 0 <= y <= 0.5).
The traction vector is (0, -2.65e4*y).
All other boundaries are traction-free.
Generate a mesh with 64 elements along the outer edges of length 1.0.
Use second-degree Lagrange elements for the vector-valued displacement function space.
Use a direct solver to solve the linear system.
Compute the von Mises stress from the resulting in-plane Cauchy stress field.
The domain has a unit thickness.
