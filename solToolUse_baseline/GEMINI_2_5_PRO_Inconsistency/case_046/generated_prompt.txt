Solve a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The domain is a rectangle of size 1.0m by 0.5m.
The rectangle contains a circular hole of radius 0.1m centered at (0.5m, 0.25m).
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.33.
The material has a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force due to gravity, with g=9.81 m/s^2, acts in the negative y-direction.
The left edge of the rectangle (x=0) is fixed with zero displacement.
A uniform downward traction is applied to the full right edge of the rectangle (x=1).
The total resultant of this traction is a 5 kN force in the negative y-direction.
The top, bottom, and circular hole boundaries are traction-free.
The model assumes a unit thickness of 1m.
The domain is to be meshed with approximately 72 elements.
A suitable mesh generation tool, such as mshr, should be used to create the mesh for the geometry.
The vector-valued displacement function space will use second-degree Lagrange elements.
The linear system will be solved using a direct solver.
Compute the displacement vector field distribution.
Compute the von Mises stress scalar field distribution from the in-plane Cauchy stress.
Save the resulting displacement and von Mises stress distributions in a format suitable for visualization, such as VTK.
