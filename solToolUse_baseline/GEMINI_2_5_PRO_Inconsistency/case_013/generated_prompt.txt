The problem is a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem under plane stress conditions.
The geometry is a rectangle defined by (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) meters.
Create a structured mesh of the rectangle with 100 elements in the x-direction and 20 elements in the y-direction.
The mesh should be composed of triangular elements.
The displacement field should be approximated using Lagrange finite elements of polynomial degree 1.
The material has a Poisson's ratio of 0.30.
The Young's modulus is spatially varying according to the formula E(y) = 100e9 + 100e9 * (y / 0.20) Pascals.
The left boundary (x=0) is fixed, with displacement (u_x, u_y) = (0, 0).
The right boundary (x=1) has a uniform traction of (2e6, 0) N/m applied.
The top (y=0.20) and bottom (y=0) boundaries are traction-free.
The linear system should be solved using a direct solver.
