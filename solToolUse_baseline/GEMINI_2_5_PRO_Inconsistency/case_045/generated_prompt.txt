Solve a 2D plane-stress solid mechanics problem using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The geometry is a rectangular domain defined by (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) meters.
The domain consists of two subdomains bonded together.
The top subdomain, spanning (0, 1.0) x (0.10, 0.20) m, is made of aluminium.
The bottom subdomain, spanning (0, 1.0) x (0.00, 0.10) m, is made of steel.
The material properties for aluminium are Young's modulus E = 70 GPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.30.
The material properties for steel are Young's modulus E = 200 GPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.30.
The model is small-strain linear elasticity under plane-stress conditions with unit thickness.
Discretize the domain using a structured mesh with 80 cells in the x-direction and 16 cells in the y-direction.
Use continuous vector-valued Lagrange elements of polynomial degree 2 for the displacement field.
The interface between the two materials at y = 0.10 m is a perfect bond.
Apply a fixed boundary condition (u_x = 0, u_y = 0) on the left edge at x = 0.
Apply a uniform downward line traction of t = (0, -5000) N/m on the right edge at x = 1.
The top and bottom edges of the domain are traction-free.
Solve the resulting linear system using a direct solver.
Assign material properties to the subdomains using cell markers or a DG0 function space.
