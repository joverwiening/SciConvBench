Solve a quasi-incompressible finite-strain hyperelasticity problem.
The problem is Solid Mechanics Problem 14.
The geometry is a rectangular strip defined by the domain (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) meters.
The strip contains two circular holes, each with a radius of 0.04 m.
The first hole is centered at (0.40, 0.10) m.
The second hole is centered at (0.60, 0.10) m.
The model is a large-deformation isotropic quasi-incompressible neo-Hookean solid.
The analysis is performed under plane strain conditions.
The unknown fields are the displacement vector u and the hydrostatic pressure p.
The material's Young's modulus is 5 MPa.
The material's Poisson's ratio is 0.49.
The left edge (x = 0) is fixed, with u_x = 0 and u_y = 0.
The right edge (x = 1.0) has a prescribed displacement of u_x = 0.060 m and u_y = 0.
The top and bottom edges of the strip are traction-free.
Each hole boundary is subjected to a uniform follower pressure of 0.10 MPa.
The follower pressure is applied using the current normals of the deformed hole boundaries.
The total load and prescribed displacement are to be applied incrementally over 10 equal steps.
A mixed displacement-pressure formulation is to be used.
The displacement field is approximated with continuous Lagrange elements of polynomial degree 2.
The pressure field is approximated with continuous Lagrange elements of polynomial degree 1.
The mesh should be generated with a finer resolution around the holes.
A characteristic element size of approximately 0.005 m should be used near the holes.
The non-linear system is to be solved using Newton's method.
