Solve a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem under the plane-stress assumption.
The domain is an L-shape formed by a unit square (0,1)x(0,1) with the bottom-right 0.5x0.5 square removed.
The material properties are Young's modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, and density ρ=2700 kg/m^3.
A body force due to gravity (g=9.81 m/s^2) acts in the negative y-direction.
The left boundary of the domain (x=0) is fixed with a zero-displacement Dirichlet boundary condition.
A horizontal shear traction of 2.65e4 Pa is applied to the top boundary segment defined by y=1 and x>0.5.
The domain should be meshed with a resolution corresponding to 64 elements per unit length.
The resulting linear system should be solved with a direct solver.
