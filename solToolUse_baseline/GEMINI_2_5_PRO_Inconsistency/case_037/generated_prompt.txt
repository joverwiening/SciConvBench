Use the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin) to solve a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem.
The simulation will model a perforated rectangular plate under plane stress conditions.
The geometry is a 1m by 0.5m rectangle, defined by the corners (0,0) and (1,0.5).
A circular hole with a radius of 0.1m is centered at (0.5, 0.25).
A semicircular notch with a radius of 0.02m is cut into the right edge of the plate, centered at (1, 0.25).
The mesh resolution should be approximately 64 elements per meter.
The material's Young's modulus is E=70e9 Pa and Poisson's ratio is ν=0.33.
The material's density is ρ=2700 kg/m^3.
The model assumes a unit thickness of 1m.
The left boundary of the plate (x=0) is fixed with a zero-displacement Dirichlet condition.
A body force representing gravity, f = (0, -ρ*g) with g=9.81 m/s^2, is applied to the domain.
A traction load is applied to the boundary of the semicircular notch.
The traction vector is tangential to the arc at every point along the arc.
The magnitude of the traction is constant along the arc, calculated as t = M / (2 * pi * r), where the torque M is 800 Nm (treated as 800 N for unit thickness) and the arc radius r is 0.02 m.
The top, bottom, inner hole, and the remainder of the right edge boundaries are all traction-free.
The linear system should be solved using a direct solver.
The simulation should compute the displacement field and the von Mises stress field.
