The bottom edge (y=0) is traction-free.
The boundary of the circular hole is traction-free.
The mesh should be generated with a characteristic density of approximately 64 elements per outer edge.
The mesh should be refined near the circular hole to capture stress concentration.
Use vector-valued Lagrange finite elements of polynomial degree 2 for the displacement space.
Solve the linear system using a direct solver.
Compute the von Mises stress from the resulting in-plane Cauchy stress field.
