Use the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin) to solve a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem under the plane-stress assumption.
The domain is a polygon defined by the vertices (0,0), (0.8,0), (0.8,0.2), (0.5,0.2), (0.5,0.5), (0.3,0.5), (0.3,0.2), and (0,0.2).
The mesh of the domain should contain approximately 72 elements.
The mesh must have a vertex located at the point (0.4, 0.5).
The material's Young's modulus is 70e9 Pa and its Poisson's ratio is 0.33.
The material's density is 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force due to gravity, with g=9.81 m/s^2, acts in the negative y-direction.
A concentrated vertical point load of -5 kN is applied at the vertex (0.4, 0.5) using a `PointSource`.
The bottom boundary of the domain, where y=0, is to be fixed (clamped).
The displacement field should be discretized using second-order Lagrange elements (P2).
The resulting linear system of equations should be solved using a direct solver.
Compute the displacement magnitude field from the solution.
Compute the von Mises stress field from the in-plane Cauchy stress components.
Save the computed displacement and von Mises stress fields to files suitable for visualization.
