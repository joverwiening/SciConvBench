Solve a small-strain linear elasticity problem.

The Cartesian components of `u0` are `u_x = r^q * (c1*cos(q*theta) - c2*cos((q-2)*theta))` and `u_y = r^q * (c1*sin(q*theta) - c2*sin((q-2)*theta))`.
The parameter `q` is `0.5430755788367`.
The parameter `kappa` for plane strain is `3 - 4*ν`, where `ν` is Poisson's ratio derived from the Lamé parameters.
The constants are `c1 = (1+q)/(1-q+kappa)` and `c2 = (1-q)/(1-q+kappa)`.
Enforce Dirichlet boundary conditions on the outer boundary of the square and on the slit faces using the exact displacement field `u0`.
Solve the resulting linear system using the GMRES iterative solver.
Use the ILU preconditioner for the GMRES solver.
After computing the numerical displacement field `u`, calculate the L2 error between `u` and the exact solution `u0`.
