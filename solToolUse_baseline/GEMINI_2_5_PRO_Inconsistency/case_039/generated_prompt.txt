Simulate stress in a perforated rectangular plate using the FEniCS legacy (dolfin) library.
The simulation will solve a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem under plane-stress conditions.
The geometry is a rectangle of 1m by 0.5m, with a circular hole of radius 0.1m centered at (0.5, 0.25).
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.33.
Body forces due to gravity will be neglected in the simulation.
The boundary condition on the left edge (x=0) is a vertical roller constraint (uy=0), with horizontal displacement (ux) unconstrained.
The boundary condition on the right edge (x=1) is a horizontal roller constraint (ux=0).
A uniform vertical compressive traction of -2500 Pa is applied to both the top (y=0.5) and bottom (y=0) edges.
The boundary of the central hole is traction-free.
The mesh will be generated with a resolution parameter of 64.
The displacement vector field will be discretized using continuous Lagrange elements of degree 2.
The resulting linear system will be solved using a direct solver.
The simulation must compute the displacement field, the in-plane Cauchy stress field, and the von Mises stress field.
