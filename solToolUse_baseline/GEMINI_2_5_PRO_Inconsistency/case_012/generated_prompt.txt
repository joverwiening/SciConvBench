Solve a solid mechanics problem using the FEniCS legacy (dolfin) library.
The problem is a quarter plate with a quarter hole under plane-strain elastoplasticity.
The geometry is a symmetric quarter domain defined by [0, 100] x [0, 180] mm with a quarter hole of radius 50 mm at the origin.
Model the material using small-strain von Mises elastoplasticity with associative flow and perfect plasticity under plane strain conditions.
The material properties are lambda = 19.44 GPa, mu = 29.17 GPa, and yield stress sigma_Y = 243 MPa.
The unknown field is the displacement vector u.
Use a triangular mesh with a characteristic mesh size of 5 mm.
Use a continuous Galerkin function space of polynomial degree 2 for the displacement.
The solver should use an incremental return-mapping algorithm for the plasticity update, consistent with plane strain perfect plasticity.
The incremental solve should use Newton iterations.
Apply a symmetry boundary condition on the boundary at x = 0 by setting u_x = 0.
Apply a symmetry boundary condition on the boundary at y = 0 by setting u_y = 0.
The boundaries at x = 100 mm and on the hole are traction-free.
Apply a prescribed displacement of u = (0, 1) mm on the boundary at y = 180 mm.
Apply the final prescribed displacement over 50 equal increments.
