Use the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin) to solve a solid mechanics problem.
The problem is a 2D small-strain linear elasticity simulation.
The simulation uses the plane-stress variational formulation.
The geometry is a rectangular plate defined by the domain (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) meters.
A circular hole with a radius of 0.05 meters is centered in the plate at (0.50, 0.10) meters.
The material's Young's modulus is 200 GPa.
The material's Poisson's ratio is 0.30.
The displacement field uses a vector-valued function space.
A reasonable polynomial degree, such as 2, will be used for the function space.
A reasonable mesh density will be used, resulting in approximately 5000-10000 cells.
The left edge of the plate (x=0) is fixed, with zero displacement.
The right edge of the plate (x=1.0) has an applied line traction of (2 MPa*m, 0).
The top, bottom, and circular hole boundaries are traction-free.
A direct linear solver will be used to solve the problem.
Compute the von Mises stress field under plane-stress conditions.
Save a plot of the von Mises stress field to the file `q3_vm.png`.
Save the displacement field to an XDMF file with a reasonable name, such as `q3_displacement.xdmf`.
Report the maximum von Mises stress value found on the boundary of the circular hole.
Report the stress concentration factor Kt, calculated as the maximum von Mises stress on the hole boundary divided by 2 MPa.
