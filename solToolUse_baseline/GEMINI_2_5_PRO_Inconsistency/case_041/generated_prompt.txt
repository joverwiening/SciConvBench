Use the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin) to solve the problem.
The problem is a finite-strain Saint-Venant-Kirchhoff elasticity analysis in plane strain.
The geometry is a rectangular strip defined by the domain (0, 1.20) x (0, 0.20) meters.
The strip contains three circular holes of radius 0.03 meters.
The centers of the holes are located at (0.30, 0.10), (0.60, 0.10), and (0.90, 0.10) meters.
The unknown to be solved for is the displacement field u.
The material model is Saint-Venant-Kirchhoff elasticity.
The second Piola-Kirchhoff stress S is defined as S = lambda*tr(E_GL)*I + 2*mu*E_GL.
The Green-Lagrange strain tensor E_GL is defined as E_GL = 0.5*(F^T*F - I), where F = I + grad(u).
The material's Lamé parameters are lambda = 5.769 MPa and mu = 3.846 MPa.
The left edge of the strip (x = 0) is fixed with u = (0, 0).
The right edge of the strip (x = 1.20) has a prescribed displacement of u = (0.012, 0).
The top, bottom, and hole boundaries are traction-free.
The prescribed displacement on the right edge will be applied incrementally over 10 load steps.
A Newton-Raphson method will be used to solve the nonlinear system at each load step.
After each converged load step, check the stopping criterion.
The simulation must stop if the maximum principal Green-Lagrange strain anywhere in the domain exceeds 0.03.
The mesh should have a characteristic element size of approximately 0.005.
