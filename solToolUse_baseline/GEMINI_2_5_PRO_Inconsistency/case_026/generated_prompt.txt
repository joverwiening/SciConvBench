Solve a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem using FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The analysis will be conducted under plane stress assumptions with unit thickness.
The domain is a right triangle with vertices at (0,0), (1,0), and (0,0.5).
The mesh should be generated with 64 triangular elements.
The material properties are Young's modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, and density ρ=2700 kg/m^3.
A body force due to gravity (g=9.81 m/s^2) is applied in the negative y-direction.
The displacement field is approximated using a vector-valued function space with a polynomial degree of 2.
The left edge of the domain (x=0) is clamped, enforcing a zero-displacement Dirichlet boundary condition.
The bottom edge of the domain (y=0) is treated as a free surface with zero applied traction.
A sinusoidal vertical traction is applied on the hypotenuse (x+2y=1).
The traction vector is t = (0, -5e3*sin(πx)), where the spatial variation depends on the global coordinate 'x'.
The linear system will be solved using a direct solver.
Compute the displacement vector field.
Compute the von Mises stress distribution from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
Present the results as plots of the displacement magnitude and the von Mises stress.
Save the computed displacement and von Mises stress fields to a file for visualization.
