Use the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
Solve a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem under the plane stress assumption.
The geometry is a rectangle of size 4.0 by 1.0.
A circular hole of radius 0.3 is centered at coordinates (1.0, 0.5).
Generate the mesh using a global mesh density parameter of 72.
Use a second-order vector Lagrange function space for the displacement.
The material has a Young's modulus E of 210e3 and a Poisson's ratio ν of 0.3.
Use the plane stress constitutive law, with Lamé parameters mu = E/(2*(1+nu)) and lambda = E*nu/(1-nu**2).
Apply a clamped boundary condition (u=0) on the left edge (x=0).
Apply a uniform downward traction vector t = (0, -1e5) on the right edge (x=4.0).
The boundary of the circular hole is traction-free.
There are no body forces.
Use a direct linear solver to find the displacement field.
After solving for displacement, compute the von Mises stress field.
