Solve a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The domain is a parallelogram defined by the vertices (0,0), (1,0), (1.2,0.3), and (0.2,0.3).
The material properties are: Young's modulus E = 70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν = 0.33, and density ρ = 2700 kg/m^3.
The mesh consists of 72 triangular elements, generated in a structured manner.
The displacement is represented using a vector-valued function space with continuous first-degree Lagrange elements.
A gravitational body force corresponding to g = 9.81 m/s^2 acts in the negative y-direction.
A horizontal traction of (6000, 0) Pa is applied on the left inclined boundary.
The right inclined boundary is fixed with a zero-displacement Dirichlet condition.
The top and bottom boundaries are traction-free.
The resulting linear system of equations shall be solved using a direct solver.
The primary outputs are the displacement field and the von Mises stress distribution.
The von Mises stress should be calculated from the in-plane Cauchy stress components.
