Implement a solver for a 3D compressible neo-Hookean solid using FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The geometry is a unit cube, meshed with 16x16x16 cells.
Use a first-order vector Lagrange function space for the displacement field.
The material is defined by Young's modulus E = 10.0 and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.3.
The strain energy density function is Ψ = (μ/2)*(tr(C) - 3) - μ*ln(J) + (λ/2)*(ln(J))^2.
The face at x = 0 is clamped.
A body force b = (0, -0.5, 0) is applied to the entire domain.
A boundary traction h = (0.1, 0, 0) is applied on the face at x = 1.
The total potential energy is minimized for the displacement field using Newton's method.
The body force and boundary traction are applied all at once, not incrementally.
Use a reasonable absolute tolerance for the Newton solver's convergence.
Use a reasonable linear solver for the Newton iterations.
