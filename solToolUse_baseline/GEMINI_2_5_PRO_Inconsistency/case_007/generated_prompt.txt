Use the FEniCS legacy (dolfin) library to solve a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem.
The analysis will use a plane-stress formulation.
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa, a Poisson's ratio of 0.33, and a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force due to gravity (g=9.81 m/s^2) acts in the negative y-direction.
The geometry is an 8-vertex stepped polygon defined by the coordinates: (0.0, 0.0), (0.8, 0.0), (0.8, 0.2), (0.5, 0.2), (0.5, 0.4), (0.3, 0.4), (0.3, 0.2), and (0.0, 0.2).
The bottom boundary of the domain (y=0) is fixed, with zero displacement in all directions.
The vertical stem boundaries at x=0.3 (for 0.2 ≤ y ≤ 0.4) and x=0.5 (for 0.2 ≤ y ≤ 0.4) have a roller condition with zero vertical displacement (u_y=0).
A shear traction of 5000 Pa is applied in the positive x-direction on the top edge segment defined by 0.3 ≤ x ≤ 0.5 at y=0.4.
The domain should be meshed with approximately 72 triangular elements.
The vector-valued displacement space will be discretized using second-degree Lagrange elements.
The resulting linear system will be solved using a default direct solver.
The outputs of the analysis will be the displacement field and the von Mises stress distribution.
The von Mises stress will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress tensor.
