Use the FEniCS legacy (dolfin) library to solve a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem.
The analysis domain is a rectangle defined by corners (0,0) and (1,0.5), with a circular hole of radius 0.1 centered at (0.5, 0.25).
The mesh for the domain must consist of exactly 72 elements.
The problem is to be solved under the plane stress assumption with unit thickness.
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.33.
The material has a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force due to gravity, with g=9.81 m/s^2, acts in the negative y-direction.
The left edge of the plate (x=0) is fixed.
The bottom edge of the plate (y=0) is fixed.
The right edge of the plate (x=1) is traction-free.
The boundary of the circular hole is traction-free.
A traction vector of (1000, 0) Pa is applied to the top edge of the plate (y=0.5).
Use a direct linear solver for the system of equations.
The vector-valued displacement field should be approximated using continuous Lagrange elements of polynomial degree 2.
Compute the displacement distribution throughout the domain.
Compute the von Mises stress distribution from the resulting in-plane Cauchy stress field.
