Use the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin) to perform the simulation.
Solve a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem.
The simulation domain is a rectangle of size 1.0m by 0.5m.
A circular hole with a radius of 0.1m is centered at (0.5, 0.25) within the rectangle.
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.33.
The material has a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
The problem should be treated under plane stress assumptions with a unit thickness.
The left edge of the rectangle (x=0) is clamped, meaning displacement is zero.
A pressure of 1e5 Pa is applied on the boundary of the hole, directed towards the center of the hole.
A body force due to gravity (g = 9.81 m/s^2) is applied in the negative y-direction.
The mesh should be generated with a resolution corresponding to 64 elements along the exterior boundary.
Use a vector-valued function space with a polynomial degree of 2 for the displacement.
Solve the resulting linear system using a direct solver.
Compute the displacement field.
Compute the von Mises stress field from the in-plane Cauchy stress tensor.
Save the resulting displacement and von Mises stress fields in XDMF format.
