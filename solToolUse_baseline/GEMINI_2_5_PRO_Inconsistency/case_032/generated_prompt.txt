Use the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin) to solve a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem.
The simulation domain is a right triangle with vertices at (0,0), (1,0), and (0,0.5).
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa, a Poisson's ratio of 0.33, and a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
The analysis will include gravity loading, with the gravitational acceleration of 9.81 m/s^2 acting in the negative y-direction.
The sloping edge of the triangle, defined by the line x + 2y = 1, is a clamped boundary with zero displacement.
A normal traction of 4000 Pa is applied in the positive x-direction on the vertical edge at x = 0.
The bottom edge of the triangle, at y = 0, is a free surface with no applied tractions.
The mesh should be generated to have approximately 64 triangular elements.
Use a direct solver for the linear system of equations.
The displacement field should be discretized using continuous Lagrange finite elements of order 2.
The primary outputs of the simulation are the displacement field and the von Mises stress distribution.
The von Mises stress should be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress components.
