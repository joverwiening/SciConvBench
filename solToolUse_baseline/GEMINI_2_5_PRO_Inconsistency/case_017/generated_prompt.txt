Use the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin) for the simulation.
Solve a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem.
The domain is a cross-shaped plate with four circular holes.
The cross shape is the union of a horizontal rectangle from x=-1 to 1 with width 0.6 and a vertical rectangle from y=-1 to 1 with width 0.6.
Four circular holes, each with a radius of 0.12, are centered at (0.6, 0), (-0.6, 0), (0, 0.6), and (0, -0.6).
Generate a mesh for this domain with a resolution corresponding to approximately 100 cells along the longest dimension.
Use a vector function space composed of second-order continuous Lagrange elements for the displacement field.
The material has a Young's modulus E of 210000 and a Poisson's ratio ν of 0.3.
Apply the plane-stress constitutive law assuming unit thickness.
The left edge of the plate, defined by x = -1, is clamped with a zero-displacement boundary condition u = (0, 0).
A uniform downward traction vector t = (0, -5e4) is applied to the right edge of the plate, defined by x = 1.
All other boundaries, including the surfaces of the four holes and the remaining outer edges of the cross, are traction-free.
Solve the resulting linear system using a direct solver.
The final outputs of the simulation are the displacement vector field and the von Mises stress scalar field.
