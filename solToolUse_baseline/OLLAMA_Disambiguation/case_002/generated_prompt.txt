Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve a 2D plane‑stress linear elasticity problem on a right triangular domain with vertices (0,0), (1,0) and (0,0.5).  
Generate a uniform triangular mesh of exactly 64 elements covering the domain.  
Apply a clamped boundary condition (zero displacement) on the vertical edge x = 0.  
Apply an upward normal traction of magnitude 3000 Pa on the sloping boundary defined by x + 2y = 1.  
Include the gravity body force ρ g with ρ = 2700 kg/m³ and g = 9.81 m/s² as a volumetric load.  
Use a vector‑valued displacement function space and the reduced plane‑stress constitutive law with Young’s modulus E = 70e9 Pa, Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.33, and unit thickness.  
Solve the variational problem with the default direct linear solver (PETSc LU).  
Compute the displacement magnitude field and the von Mises stress field from the in‑plane Cauchy stress.  
Save the displacement magnitude to results/displacement.xdmf and the von Mises stress to results/von_mises_stress.xdmf in XDMF format.
