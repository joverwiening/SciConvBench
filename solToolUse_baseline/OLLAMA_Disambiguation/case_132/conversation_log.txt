Q: Should any output files (such as .dat, .frd, or other result files) be listed in the “artifacts” array of the results.json?
A: No. The complete requirement only mandates that the `artifacts` field be present (as an array); it does not require you to list any result files there. You may leave the array empty, or optionally add files such as the `.dat`, `.frd`, or `.sta` if you wish, but it is not required by the specification.
