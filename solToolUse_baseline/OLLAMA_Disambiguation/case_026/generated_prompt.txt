Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve a 2D plane‑stress linear elasticity problem on the right triangular domain with vertices (0,0), (1,0), and (0,0.5).  
Material properties are Young’s modulus E = 70e9 Pa, Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.33, density ρ = 2700 kg/m³, and gravity g = 9.81 m/s² acting in the negative y‑direction.  
Assume unit thickness and employ the reduced plane‑stress constitutive law.  
Apply a clamped Dirichlet condition u = (0,0) on the left edge x = 0.  
Apply a body force f = (0, -ρ g) throughout the domain.  
Apply a vertical Neumann traction on the hypotenuse x+2y = 1 given by t_y(x) = -5.0e3 sin(π x) Pa, with no horizontal component.  
Generate a uniform structured mesh of exactly 64 right‑triangular elements by dividing the base [0,1] into 4 equal segments and the vertical edge [0,0.5] into 8 equal segments, then splitting each rectangle into two triangles.  
Use a direct linear solver to solve the variational problem.  
Compute the Cauchy stress tensor from the displacement solution and evaluate the von Mises stress from the in‑plane stress components.  
Save the displacement field and von Mises stress distribution in VTK (or XDMF) format using the base filename “results”.  
Additionally write CSV files named results_disp.csv and results_stress.csv containing nodal values of displacement and von Mises stress.
