Use legacy FEniCS (dolfin) to solve a 2D plane‑stress linear elasticity problem.  
Create the geometry of a 1 m × 0.5 m rectangle with a circular hole of radius 0.1 m centered at (0.5, 0.25).  
Generate the mesh automatically with mshr using 64 elements per edge.  
Define material properties: Young’s modulus E = 70 GPa, Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.33, density ρ = 2700 kg/m³, and assume unit thickness for plane‑stress.  
Apply a body force f = (0, −ρ g) with g = 9.81 m/s² (negative y‑direction).  
Apply an internal pressure p = 1e5 Pa on the hole boundary as an inward‑normal traction t = −p n.  
Impose a clamped (zero displacement) boundary condition on the left edge x = 0.  
Use a direct linear solver to solve the variational formulation with a vector‑valued displacement space.  
Compute the von Mises stress from the in‑plane Cauchy stress field.  
Export the displacement field and von Mises stress field to XDMF file `results.xdmf` (with accompanying `results.h5`) and also to CSV files `displacement.csv` and `von_mises.csv`.
