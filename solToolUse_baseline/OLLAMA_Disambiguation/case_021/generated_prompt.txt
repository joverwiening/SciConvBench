Apply a body force f = (0, ‑ρ g) representing gravity.  
Impose Dirichlet boundary conditions u_x = 0 and u_y = 0 on the left boundary.  
Leave the hole boundary traction‑free.  
Solve the variational problem with a direct linear solver.  
Compute the in‑plane Cauchy stress tensor and evaluate the von Mises stress from it.
