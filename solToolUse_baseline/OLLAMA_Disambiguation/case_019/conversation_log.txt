Q: What is the exact displacement vector to apply on the \(x_1 = 1\) face corresponding to a 60° counter‑clockwise rotation about the \(x_1\)-axis?
A: For a point \((x_{1},y,z)=(1,y,z)\) on the right face the rotation is performed about the line \((x_{1},0.5,0.5)\) (the cube centreline) by \(\theta =60^{\circ}= \pi/3\) counter‑clockwise.  
Let  

\[
y_{0}=y-0.5,\qquad z_{0}=z-0.5,
\]

\[
\cos\theta =\tfrac12,\qquad \sin\theta =\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\approx0.8660254 .
\]

After rotation the new coordinates are  

\[
\begin{aligned}
y_{\text{new}} &= y_{0}\cos\theta - z_{0}\sin\theta +0.5,\\
z_{\text{new}} &= y_{0}\sin\theta + z_{0}\cos\theta +0.5,
\end{aligned}
\]

while \(x_{1}\) remains unchanged.  

Hence the exact displacement vector \(\mathbf{u}=(u_{1},u_{2},u_{3})\) to be prescribed on the face \(x_{1}=1\) is  

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
u_{1}&=0,\\[4pt]
u_{2}&= \bigl(y-0.5\bigr)\cos\theta - \bigl(z-0.5\bigr)\sin\theta +0.5 - y,\\[4pt]
u_{3}&= \bigl(y-0.5\bigr)\sin\theta + \bigl(z-0.5\bigr)\cos\theta +0.5 - z .
\end{aligned}}
\]

In numerical form (using \(\cos60^{\circ}=0.5\) and \(\sin60^{\circ}=0.8660254\)):

\[
\begin{aligned}
u_{2}&=0.5\,(y-0.5)-0.8660254\,(z-0.5)+0.5-y,\\
u_{3}&=0.8660254\,(y-0.5)+0.5\,(z-0.5)+0.5-z .
\end{aligned}
\]
