Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve a small‑strain linear elasticity problem in 2D plane‑stress for a semi‑circular domain of radius 1 m (y ≥ 0).  
Treat the domain as having unit thickness and employ the reduced plane‑stress constitutive law with material parameters E = 70 × 10⁹ Pa, ν = 0.33, density ρ = 2700 kg/m³, and gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s² acting in the negative y‑direction.  
Apply an inward normal pressure of magnitude 20 kPa on the curved arc of the semi‑circle.  
Fix the entire base line y = 0 by constraining both displacement components to zero.  
Generate a mesh with 8 divisions in the radial direction and 8 divisions in the angular direction (total 64 elements).  
Use a vector‑valued displacement function space and solve the variational problem with a direct linear solver.  
Compute the von Mises stress from the in‑plane Cauchy stress field.  
Save the displacement field and the von Mises stress field in XDMF format (with accompanying HDF5 files) and also in VTK format for quick visual checks.  
Store all output files in the directory `results/` using the base name `solution`, resulting in files `results/solution_displacement.xdmf`, `results/solution_displacement.h5`, `results/solution_vonMises.xdmf`, `results/solution_vonMises.h5`, and the corresponding VTK files `results/solution_displacement.vtk` and `results/solution_vonMises.vtk`.
