Q: What is the prescribed rotational displacement field on the x = 1 face (e.g., rotation angle and axis)?
A: The displacement on the \(x=1\) face is obtained by rotating each point on that face \(60^\circ\) about the line through the face centre \((y_0,z_0)=(0.5,0.5)\) (i.e., the \(x\)-axis through that centre) and then scaling the resulting displacement vector by 0.5.

Q: On which part(s) of the boundary should the traction \(\mathbf{T} = (0.1, 0, 0)\) be applied?
A: Apply the traction \(\mathbf{T} = (0.1,0,0)\) on the natural boundary—that is, on all faces of the cube except the Dirichlet‑prescribed faces \(x=0\) (clamped) and \(x=1\) (prescribed rotational displacement). In practice this means the four remaining faces \(y=0\), \(y=1\), \(z=0\) and \(z=1\).
