Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve a 2D plane‑stress linear elasticity problem.  
Create a rectangular domain 1 m by 0.5 m with a circular hole of radius 0.1 m centered at (0.5, 0.25) using mshr and generate a mesh containing approximately 72 triangular elements.  
Define material properties: Young’s modulus E = 70e9 Pa, Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.33, density ρ = 2700 kg/m³, and gravity g = 9.81 m/s²; treat the domain as plane stress with unit thickness.  
Apply Dirichlet boundary conditions fixing the left edge (x = 0) and the bottom edge (y = 0).  
Apply a horizontal traction of +1000 Pa (σₓₓ) on the top edge (y = 0.5) and –1000 Pa (σₓₓ) on the bottom edge (y = 0) as Neumann boundary conditions, and include the body force due to gravity.  
Assemble the variational formulation using a vector‑valued displacement function space and solve the linear system with PETSc’s LU direct solver (MUMPS).  
Compute the Cauchy stress tensor from the displacement solution and evaluate the von Mises stress from the in‑plane stress components.  
Save the displacement field to `displacement.xdmf` and the von Mises stress field to `von_mises.xdmf` in XDMF format.
