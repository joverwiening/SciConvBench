Simulate a 2D plane‑stress linear elasticity problem using legacy FEniCS (dolfin) with a direct linear solver.  
The domain is a rectangle 1 m by 0.5 m with a circular hole of radius 0.1 m centered at (0.5, 0.25).  
Generate a uniform triangular mesh with 64 divisions along each edge.  
Use a quadratic (P2) vector‑valued displacement space.  
Material properties: Young’s modulus E = 70e9 Pa, Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.33, density ρ = 2700 kg/m³.  
Assume unit thickness and apply the reduced plane‑stress constitutive law.  
Apply a pressure of 1e5 Pa on the hole boundary acting inward, using traction t = +p n where n is the outward normal of the solid.  
Clamp the left edge (x = 0) with zero displacement.  
Apply gravity as a body force f = −ρ g e_y in the negative y‑direction (g = 9.81 m/s²).  
Compute the von Mises stress from the in‑plane Cauchy stress field.  
Save the displacement field, the full stress tensor field, and the von Mises stress field in VTK format.  
Use the filenames displacement.vtk, stress.vtk, and von_mises.vtk.  
Place all output files in a dedicated results directory, e.g., results/.
