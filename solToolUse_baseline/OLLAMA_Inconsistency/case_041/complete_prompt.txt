Case ID: case_041
Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
Solver: finite-strain Saint-Venant-Kirchhoff elasticity in legacy FEniCS (dolfin); use load stepping and Newton iterations in plane strain.
Task:
Solve Solid Mechanics Problem 15 from the paper: a three-hole strip under finite-strain Saint-Venant-Kirchhoff elasticity.
Case details:
Geometry:
Let Ω = (0, 1.20) x (0, 0.20) m be a rectangular strip with three circular holes of radius a = 0.03 m centred on the midline y = 0.10 m at x = 0.30 m, 0.60 m, and 0.90 m.
Model:
Finite-strain Saint-Venant-Kirchhoff elasticity in plane strain.
Unknown:
Displacement u = (u_x, u_y).
Definitions:
F = I + grad(u), E_GL = 0.5*(F^T*F - I), S = lambda*tr(E_GL)*I + 2*mu*E_GL, J = det(F), and Cauchy stress sigma = (1/J)*F*S*F^T.
Material:
Lamé parameters lambda = 5.769 MPa, mu = 3.846 MPa.
Boundary conditions:
Left edge (x = 0): fixed, u_x = 0, u_y = 0.
Right edge (x = 1.20): prescribed displacement u_x = 0.012 m, u_y = 0.
Top, bottom, and hole boundaries: traction-free.
Outputs:
Save a deformed-configuration plot as q15_def.png.
Compute the maximum principal Green-Lagrange strain E_max and save q15_Emax.png.
Compute the deviatoric second Piola stress invariant and save q15_vmS.png.
Export final u and E_max to XDMF.
Stopping rule:
Use load stepping and stop advancing if E_max exceeds 0.03.
Implementation notes:
Use the Green-Lagrange strain in the Saint-Venant-Kirchhoff constitutive model, not the infinitesimal strain.
Evaluate the stopping criterion with the maximum principal Green-Lagrange strain after each converged load step.
