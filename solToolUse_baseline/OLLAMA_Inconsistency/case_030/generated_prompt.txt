Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve a 2D plane‑stress linear elasticity problem on an L‑shaped domain (outer square 1 × 1 m, inner square 0.5 × 0.5 m removed at the lower‑left corner).

Treat the problem as plane stress with unit thickness and use the reduced plane‑stress constitutive law.  
Apply clamped (zero displacement) boundary conditions on the outer left edge (x = 0), outer bottom edge (y = 0), and on the two faces of the cutout adjacent to the re‑entrant corner.  
Apply a uniform horizontal traction σₓₓ = 2.65 × 10⁴ Pa on the entire right edge (x = 1).  
Solve the variational problem with a direct linear solver.  
Compute the in‑plane Cauchy stress tensor and evaluate the von Mises stress from it.  
Save the displacement field to displacement.xdmf (with displacement.h5) and the von Mises stress field to von_mises.xdmf (with von_mises.h5) using XDMF/HDF5 format.
