Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve a 2D plane‑stress linear elasticity problem on a half‑disk of radius 1 m (y ≥ 0).  
Generate a mesh with 8 radial divisions and 8 angular divisions, giving a total of 64 elements.

Treat the domain as plane stress with unit thickness and use the reduced plane‑stress constitutive law.  
Apply a fixed (clamped) boundary condition on the base y = 0 for both displacement components.  
Apply a vertical point load of ‑5 kN at the apex (0, 1) using a PointSource on the vertical displacement component.  
Solve the variational problem with a direct linear solver.  
Compute the von Mises stress from the in‑plane Cauchy stress field.  
Write the displacement field to both `displacement.xdmf` and `displacement.vtk`.  
Write the von Mises stress field to both `von_mises.xdmf` and `von_mises.vtk`.
