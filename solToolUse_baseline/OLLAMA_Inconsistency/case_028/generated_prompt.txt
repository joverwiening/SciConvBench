Use FEniCS legacy dolfin to solve a small‑strain linear elasticity problem in 2D plane‑stress.  
Create an L‑shaped domain by generating a 1 × 1 RectangleMesh with 64 elements per side and removing the lower‑left 0.5 × 0.5 square to form a cutout.  
Use a vector‑valued continuous Lagrange function space for the displacement field.  
Material parameters: Young’s modulus E = 70 e9 Pa, Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.33, density ρ = 2700 kg/m³, gravity g = 9.81 m/s².  
Apply a body force ρ g in the negative y‑direction.  
Impose Dirichlet boundary condition u = (0,0) on the left boundary x = 0.  
Apply a horizontal shear traction t = (2.65 × 10⁴ Pa, 0) as a Neumann condition on the portion of the top edge y = 1 where x > 0.5.  
Treat all other external boundaries (y = 0, x = 1, and the portion of y = 1 where x ≤ 0.5) and the interior cutout boundaries as traction‑free natural boundaries.  
Use the reduced plane‑stress constitutive law with unit thickness.  
Assemble the variational problem and solve with a direct linear solver.  
Compute the Cauchy stress tensor from the displacement gradient and material law.  
Compute the von Mises stress from the in‑plane stress components.  
Save the displacement field to an XDMF file (e.g., displacement.xdmf) and the von Mises stress field to an XDMF file (e.g., von_mises.xdmf) for visualization in ParaView or VisIt.
