The expectation that complete substitutional solid solubility is predicted is incorrect.
The Hume-Rothery size factor rule governs this prediction.
This rule states that for complete solid solubility, the difference in atomic radii between the two elements must be less than 15%.
A 25% difference in atomic radii is significantly larger than the 15% threshold.
This large size difference would introduce excessive lattice strain into the crystal structure.
Therefore, based on the size factor alone, the prediction should be for limited or negligible substitutional solid solubility.
