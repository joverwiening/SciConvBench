The analysis assumes a linear-elastic, small-deformation, plane stress condition.
The material is assumed to be steel with a Young's Modulus of 200 GPa.
The material is assumed to have a Poisson's Ratio of 0.3.
The initial stress state is defined by an average normal stress of 10 MPa in one direction and zero in the perpendicular direction.
The initial stress state includes an average shear stress of 3 MPa.
Calculate the first principal stress (σ₁).
Calculate the second principal stress (σ₂).
Calculate the orientation of the principal stress planes (θ_p).
Calculate the maximum in-plane shear stress (τ_max_inplane).
Calculate the first principal strain (ε₁).
Calculate the second principal strain (ε₂).
The governing relations for principal stresses are σ₁,₂ = (σ_x + σ_y)/2 ± √[((σ_x - σ_y)/2)² + τ_xy²].
The governing relation for principal plane orientation is tan(2θ_p) = 2τ_xy / (σ_x - σ_y).
The governing relations for principal strains are ε₁ = (1/E) * (σ₁ - ν*σ₂) and ε₂ = (1/E) * (σ₂ - ν*σ₁).
