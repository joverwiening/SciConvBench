The simulation will be performed using the laplacianFoam solver.
The computational domain is a 1m by 1m square.
The mesh will consist of 20 cells in the x-direction and 20 cells in the y-direction.
The mesh will be one cell thick in the z-direction.
The left boundary patch will have a fixed temperature of 400 K.
The right boundary patch will have a fixed temperature of 300 K.
The top and bottom boundary patches will have a zero-gradient temperature condition.
The frontAndBack boundary patches will be of type 'wall' with a zero-gradient temperature condition.
The thermal diffusivity of the material will be 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The simulation will start at time 0 and end at time 100000 s to ensure a steady-state solution is reached.
The time step (deltaT) will be 100 s.
The time derivative scheme (ddtSchemes) will be Euler.
The gradient scheme (gradSchemes) will be Gauss linear.
The Laplacian scheme (laplacianSchemes) will be Gauss linear corrected.
The linear solver for temperature will be the Preconditioned Conjugate Gradient (PCG) method.
The preconditioner for the temperature solver will be the Diagonal Incomplete-Cholesky (DIC).
The solver tolerance for temperature will be 1e-6.
The solver relative tolerance for temperature will be 0.
Data will be written at intervals of 10000 s.
