The simulation uses FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is 2D small-strain linear elasticity.
The analysis is performed under the plane stress assumption.
The geometry is a rectangle of 1.0m by 0.5m.
A circular hole with a radius of 0.1m is centered at (0.5, 0.25).
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa.
The material has a Poisson's ratio of 0.33.
The material has a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force due to gravity (g=9.81 m/s^2) is applied in the negative y-direction.
The left edge (x=0) is fixed with a zero-displacement Dirichlet boundary condition.
The bottom edge (y=0) is fixed with a zero-displacement Dirichlet boundary condition.
The top edge (y=0.5) has a horizontal traction of +1000 Pa applied in the x-direction.
The right edge (x=1) is a free boundary with zero traction.
The boundary of the circular hole is a free boundary with zero traction.
The domain is discretized into a mesh of approximately 72 elements using a standard mesh generator like mshr.
The variational problem is solved for the displacement vector using a direct linear solver.
The displacement field is computed and stored.
The von Mises stress distribution is computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress tensor.
