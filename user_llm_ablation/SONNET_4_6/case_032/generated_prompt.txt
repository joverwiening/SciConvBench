The simulation will be performed using FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is a 2D small-strain linear elasticity analysis under the plane stress assumption.
The domain is a right triangle with vertices at (0,0), (1,0), and (0,0.5).
The domain will be discretized with a mesh of 64 elements.
A vector-valued function space will be used for the displacement field.
The material has a Young's modulus E of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio ν of 0.33.
A body force representing gravity will be applied, defined by a density ρ of 2700 kg/m^3 and g of 9.81 m/s^2.
A normal traction vector t = (4000, 0) Pa is applied as a Neumann condition on the vertical edge at x=0.
The sloping edge defined by x + 2y = 1 is clamped, with a Dirichlet boundary condition of u = (0, 0).
The bottom edge at y=0 is a free surface with zero traction.
The resulting linear system will be solved using a direct solver.
The final outputs will be the displacement field and the von Mises stress distribution.
