The analysis pertains to a short prismatic bar under specified loading conditions.
The material behavior is linear-elastic, and deformations are assumed to be small.
The bar has a rectangular cross-section with a width of 40 mm and a height of 120 mm.
The cross-sectional area is 4800 square millimeters.
A tensile axial force of 30,000 N is applied uniformly over the cross-section.
A transverse shear force of 12,000 N is applied to the cross-section.
The shear force acts parallel to the 40 mm width dimension.
No bending moment or torsional moment is present at the cross-section of interest.
The loads are assumed to be applied through the centroid of the cross-section.
Determine the average normal stress acting on the cross-section.
The governing relation for average normal stress is the axial force divided by the cross-sectional area.
Determine the average shear stress acting on the cross-section.
The governing relation for average shear stress is the transverse shear force divided by the cross-sectional area.
Present the final calculated values for both average normal stress and average shear stress.
