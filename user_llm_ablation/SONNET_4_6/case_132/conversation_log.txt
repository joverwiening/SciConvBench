Q: For the Rusanov flux at the interface between cells j and j+1, should the wave speed `alpha` be computed as the local maximum `max(|sin(u_j)|, |sin(u_{j+1})|)`?
A: The complete requirement says `alpha=max|sin(u)|` globally (over all cells), not locally per interface. Use the global maximum of |sin(u)| across all grid points.
