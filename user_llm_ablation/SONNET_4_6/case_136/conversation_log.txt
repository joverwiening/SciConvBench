Q: You have provided two conflicting instructions: first, to take the maximum in-plane shear as the average transverse shear stress alone, and second, to compute it using a formula that combines both normal and shear stress. Which instruction should I follow?
A: Use the formula τ_max = sqrt((σ_avg/2)^2 + τ_avg^2).
