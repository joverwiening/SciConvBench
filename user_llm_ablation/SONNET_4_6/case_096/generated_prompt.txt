The simulation will model 2D Rayleigh-Benard convection.
The computational domain is a square cavity with a height of 0.1 meters.
The width of the cavity is 0.1 meters.
The fluid is air with a kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The Prandtl number of the fluid is 0.71.
The Boussinesq approximation for buoyancy will be used.
The bottom wall is maintained at a constant temperature of 294.0 K.
The top wall is maintained at a constant temperature of 293.0 K.
The side walls have a zeroGradient thermal boundary condition.
A noSlip velocity boundary condition is applied to all walls.
The magnitude of gravitational acceleration is 9.81 m/s^2.
