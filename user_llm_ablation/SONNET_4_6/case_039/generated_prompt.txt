The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The model is a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem.
The geometry is a rectangle with corners at (0,0) and (1.0, 0.5) meters.
A circular hole with a radius of 0.1 meters is centered at (0.5, 0.25).
The material's Young's modulus is 70e9 Pa and its Poisson's ratio is 0.33.
No body forces will be applied.
The mesh resolution will correspond to 64 element divisions along the 1.0-meter-long edges of the domain.
The vector-valued displacement space will use second-degree Lagrange elements.
The left boundary (x=0) is constrained with `uy = 0`.
The right boundary (x=1) is constrained with `ux = 0`.
A uniform compressive traction of -2500 Pa in the y-direction is applied to the top boundary (y=0.5).
A uniform compressive traction of -2500 Pa in the y-direction is applied to the bottom boundary (y=0).
The boundary of the circular hole is traction-free.
The linear system will be solved using a direct solver.
The simulation will compute the displacement field and the von Mises stress field.
