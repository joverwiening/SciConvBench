Retrieve the primitive crystallographic cell for BaTiO3 with space group P4mm.
Use the LDA pseudopotential files ba.upf, ti.upf, and o.upf from PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard.
Determine the plane-wave cutoff energy (ecutwfc) and k-point sampling grid required to converge the total energy to 1 meV/atom.
The kinetic energy cutoff for the charge density (ecutrho) will be four times the converged ecutwfc.
Perform a variable-cell relaxation (vc-relax) using the converged ecutwfc and k-point grid to fully relax the lattice vectors and atomic positions.
The total energy convergence threshold for the vc-relax ionic step will be 5.0e-5 Ry.
The force convergence threshold for the vc-relax ionic step will be 1.0e-4 Ry/Bohr.
Perform a self-consistent field (scf) calculation on the relaxed structure using the converged ecutwfc and k-point grid.
The electronic convergence threshold for the scf calculation will be 5.0e-6 Ry.
Perform a non-self-consistent field (nscf) calculation using the charge density from the scf step.
The nscf calculation will use a gamma-centered k-point grid that is double the density of the converged scf grid in each direction.
Perform a bands calculation using the charge density from the scf step.
The bands calculation will use the high-symmetry path Gamma-X-M-Gamma-Z-R-A-Z with 50 points per segment.
Perform band structure post-processing on the output of the bands calculation.
Perform density of states (DOS) post-processing on the output of the nscf calculation.
The final DOS will be calculated for an energy range of -10 eV to +10 eV relative to the Fermi level.
