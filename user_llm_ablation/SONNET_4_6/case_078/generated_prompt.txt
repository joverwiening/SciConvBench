Calculate the fraction of the eutectic microconstituent for a binary alloy just below the eutectic temperature.
The overall composition of the alloy (C_0) is 30 wt% B.
The eutectic composition (C_e) is 40 wt% B.
The composition of the primary alpha phase at the eutectic temperature (C_αe) is 12 wt% B.
Apply the lever rule for microconstituents to solve for the fraction.
The fraction of the eutectic microconstituent (W_e) is calculated using the formula W_e = (C_0 - C_αe) / (C_e - C_αe).
The resulting fraction of the eutectic microconstituent is 0.643.
