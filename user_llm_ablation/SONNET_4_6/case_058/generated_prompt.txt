The simulation will use the `compressibleInterFoam` solver.
The domain is a vertical cylinder with a diameter of 1 meter and a height of 2 meters.
A gas injector with a 1 centimeter diameter is centered on the bottom face of the cylinder, oriented vertically upwards.
The gas phase is air, modeled as a compressible fluid using the `perfectGas` equation of state.
The liquid phase is water, modeled as an incompressible fluid.
The gas injector inlet has a total pressure of 1,000,000 Pa.
The gas enters at a velocity corresponding to Mach 0.8.
The top boundary of the cylinder is a pressure outlet set to atmospheric pressure (101325 Pa).
All other domain boundaries are no-slip walls.
Initially, the cylinder is filled with quiescent water to a height of 1.5 meters.
The volume above the water is initially filled with quiescent air at atmospheric pressure.
The initial temperature for the entire domain is 300 K.
The simulation is transient.
The objective is to observe the penetration and expansion of the gas jet in the water.
