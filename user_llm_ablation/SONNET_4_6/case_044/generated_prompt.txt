The simulation is a turbulent buoyant cavity flow.
The k-epsilon turbulence model is used.
The geometry is a 2D square cavity with dimensions 0.10 m by 0.10 m.
The left wall is a hot wall with a constant temperature of 303 K.
The right wall is a cold wall with a constant temperature of 293 K.
The top and bottom walls have a zeroGradient thermal boundary condition.
All walls have a noSlip velocity boundary condition.
The working fluid is air.
The kinematic viscosity of the air is 1.5e-5 m²/s.
The Prandtl number of the air is 0.71.
The thermal expansion coefficient of the air is 3.33e-3 1/K.
The reference density of the air is 1.0 kg/m³.
The reference temperature for the Boussinesq approximation is 298 K.
The gravity vector is (0, -9.81, 0) m/s².
