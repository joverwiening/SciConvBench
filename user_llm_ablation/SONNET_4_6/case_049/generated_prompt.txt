Solve the steady-state one-dimensional heat conduction equation d2T/dx2 = 0.
The simulation domain is a slab with a length of 1 meter.
The coordinate system originates at x=0 on the left boundary and extends to x=1 on the right boundary.
The temperature at the left boundary (x=0) is fixed at 100 degrees Celsius.
The temperature at the right boundary (x=1) is fixed at 0 degrees Celsius.
The final output will be the temperature profile T(x) across the slab.
