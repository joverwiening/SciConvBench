The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The problem to be solved is 2D small-strain linear elasticity under plane stress conditions.
The geometry is a 2D polygonal domain defined by the following 8 vertices in order: (0,0), (1,0), (1,0.5), (0.8,0.5), (0.8,1), (0.2,1), (0.2,0.5), (0,0.5).
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.33.
The material has a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force representing gravity acts in the negative y-direction, with g=9.81 m/s^2.
The bottom boundary of the domain, defined by the segment between (0,0) and (1,0), is fixed with zero displacement.
A vertical traction of -10 kPa is applied on the top boundary, defined by the segment between (0.2,1) and (0.8,1).
The domain is to be meshed with approximately 72 triangular elements.
The displacement field is represented using a vector-valued function space.
The linear system is to be solved using a direct solver.
The simulation will compute the displacement magnitude field.
The simulation will compute the von Mises stress field from the in-plane Cauchy stress components.
The plane stress constitutive law will be used.
The domain is assumed to have a unit thickness.
