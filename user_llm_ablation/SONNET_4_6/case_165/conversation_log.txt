Q: How should the viscous stress terms be discretized?
A: Compute v_x with a centered difference on the cell-centered v values, then sigma = (zeta + 4/3*eta)*v_x. Approximate sigma at interfaces by averaging neighboring cell-centered sigma values. Add viscous contributions to the momentum and energy fluxes at interfaces as F_visc = [0, -sigma, -v*sigma] (using interface-averaged v and sigma). Total interface flux: F_total = F_hat + F_visc.
