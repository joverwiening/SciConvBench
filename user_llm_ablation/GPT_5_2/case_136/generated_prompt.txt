Use N-mm-MPa units for all calculations.
The prismatic bar has a rectangular cross-section.
The cross-section width is 30 mm.
The cross-section height is 100 mm.
The internal resultant normal force is 50000 N in tension.
The internal resultant transverse shear force is 5000 N.
The shear force acts parallel to the 30 mm width dimension.
Assume uniform stress distributions for both normal and shear stresses.
Calculate the cross-sectional area A.
Calculate the average normal stress using the formula σ_avg = P / A.
Calculate the average shear stress using the formula τ_avg = V / A.
Calculate the magnitude of the maximum in-plane shear stress using the formula τ_max = sqrt((σ_avg/2)^2 + τ_avg^2).
