Q: You stated to take the maximum in-plane shear as the average transverse shear stress, but then provided a different formula for it. Which method should be used to calculate the maximum in-plane shear stress?
A: Use the formula method.

Compute the average stresses first:
- \( \sigma_{\text{avg}} = \dfrac{P}{A} \)
- \( \tau_{\text{avg}} = \dfrac{V}{A} \)

Then compute the maximum in-plane shear stress magnitude using:
\[
\tau_{\max}=\sqrt{\left(\frac{\sigma_{\text{avg}}}{2}\right)^2+\tau_{\text{avg}}^2}
\]

Do **not** set \( \tau_{\max} = \tau_{\text{avg}} \) in this problem.
