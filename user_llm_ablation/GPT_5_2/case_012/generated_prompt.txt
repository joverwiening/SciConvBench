Calculate the number-average molecular weight (M_n) for a polymer sample.
The sample consists of four molecular weight components.
The first component has a molecular weight of 56526 g/mol and a population of 96 molecules.
The second component has a molecular weight of 92809 g/mol and a population of 131 molecules.
The third component has a molecular weight of 115856 g/mol and a population of 153 molecules.
The fourth component has a molecular weight of 126029 g/mol and a population of 146 molecules.
The number-average molecular weight is calculated by dividing the sum of the products of each molecular weight and its corresponding population by the sum of the populations.
The final result must be expressed in units of g/mol.
