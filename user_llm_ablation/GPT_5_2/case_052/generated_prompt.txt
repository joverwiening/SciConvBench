The governing equation is the steady-state one-dimensional heat conduction equation, d2T/dx2 = 0.
The simulation domain is a one-dimensional slab.
The length of the slab is 1 meter.
The boundary condition at the left end of the slab (x=0) is a fixed temperature of 100 degrees Celsius.
The boundary condition at the right end of the slab (x=1) is a fixed temperature of 20 degrees Celsius.
The thermal diffusivity of the material is 1.11e-4 m^2/s.
The objective is to determine the temperature distribution T(x) along the slab.
