The simulation is a 2D steady-state turbulent buoyant cavity flow.
Use the buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam solver.
The domain is a square cavity with dimensions 0.10 m width, 0.10 m height, and 0.01 m depth.
The mesh resolution is 100 cells in the x-direction, 100 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
Use the k-epsilon turbulence model.
The fluid is modeled using the Boussinesq approximation with constant properties.
The reference density (rho0) is 1.0 kg/m³.
The kinematic viscosity (nu) is 4.816e-4 m²/s to achieve the target Rayleigh number.
The Prandtl number (Pr) is 0.71.
The thermal expansion coefficient (beta) is 3.33e-3 1/K.
The reference temperature (T0) is 298 K.
The gravitational acceleration vector is (0, -9.81, 0) m/s².
The left vertical wall (hotWall) has a fixed temperature of 303 K and a no-slip velocity condition.
The right vertical wall (coldWall) has a fixed temperature of 293 K and a no-slip velocity condition.
The top horizontal wall (topWall) is adiabatic (zeroGradient temperature) and has a no-slip velocity condition.
The bottom horizontal wall (bottomWall) is adiabatic (zeroGradient temperature) and has a no-slip velocity condition.
The front and back patches are set to `empty` to enforce 2D behavior.
The simulation runs for 5000 pseudo-time iterations with a time step (deltaT) of 1.
