The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The problem is a 2D small-strain linear elasticity simulation under the plane stress assumption.
The geometry is a rectangle defined by corners (0,0) and (1,0.5).
A circular hole with a radius of 0.1 is centered at (0.5, 0.25).
The mesh will be generated using `mshr` with a global resolution of 64.
The material has a Young's modulus (E) of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio (ν) of 0.33.
The vector-valued displacement function space will use Lagrange elements of polynomial degree 2.
A boundary condition is applied on the left edge (x=0) to fix the vertical displacement (uy = 0).
A boundary condition is applied on the right edge (x=1) to fix the horizontal displacement (ux = 0).
A uniform vertical traction of -2500 Pa is applied to the top edge (y=0.5).
A uniform vertical traction of -2500 Pa is applied to the bottom edge (y=0.0).
The boundary of the circular hole is traction-free.
Body forces such as gravity are not included in the simulation.
The linear variational problem will be solved using a direct solver.
After solving for the displacement field, compute the von Mises stress from the in-plane Cauchy stress components.
