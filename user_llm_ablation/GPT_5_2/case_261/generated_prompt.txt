Obtain the primitive crystal structure for CoSb3 with space group Im-3 from the Materials Project database.
Use the provided LDA pseudopotentials: co.upf and sb.upf.
Perform a convergence test for the plane-wave cutoff energy (ecutwfc) to achieve a total energy convergence of 1.0e-4 Ry.
Perform a convergence test for the k-point sampling grid to achieve a total energy convergence of 1.0e-4 Ry.
Use the converged ecutwfc and k-point grid for all subsequent calculations.
The k-point grid must be a Monkhorst-Pack grid with a half shift.
Perform a variable-cell relaxation (vc-relax) on the initial structure.
The vc-relax calculation must use non-spin-polarized settings.
The vc-relax calculation must use Marzari-Vanderbilt smearing with a degauss value of 0.01 Ry.
The vc-relax calculation must use an electronic convergence threshold (conv_thr) of 1.0e-8 Ry.
The vc-relax calculation must use a total energy convergence threshold (etot_conv_thr) of 1.0e-8 Ry.
The vc-relax calculation must use a force convergence threshold (forc_conv_thr) of 1.0e-7 Ry/Bohr.
After the vc-relax is complete, perform a self-consistent field (scf) calculation on the fully relaxed structure.
The scf calculation must use the same ecutwfc, k-point grid, pseudopotentials, smearing, and spin settings as the vc-relax calculation.
The scf calculation must use an electronic convergence threshold (conv_thr) of 1.0e-8 Ry.
The scf calculation must use a total energy convergence threshold (etot_conv_thr) of 1.0e-8 Ry.
Return the final relaxed structure from the vc-relax calculation.
