The domain is a cube spanning from (0,0,0) to (1,1,1).
An obstacle is defined by a box from (0.375, 0.375, 0) to (0.625, 0.625, 0.25).
Gravity is set to (0, 0, -9.81).
The simulation models two phases, water and air, using the Volume of Fluid method.
Water has a density of 1000 kg/m^3 and a kinematic viscosity of 1.0e-6 m^2/s.
Air has a density of 1 kg/m^3 and a kinematic viscosity of 1.48e-5 m^2/s.
The surface tension between water and air is 0.07 N/m.
The mesh is a uniform grid of 32x32x32 cells.
The total simulation time is 0.1 seconds.
The initial velocity field for the entire domain is (0, 0, 0).
The initial water volume is a rectangular prism from (0, 0, 0) to (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75).
The top face at z=1 is an open atmosphere boundary.
The pressure (p_rgh) at the top face is a totalPressure condition with a uniform reference pressure of 0.
The velocity at the top face is a pressureInletOutletVelocity condition.
The phase fraction (alpha.water) at the top face is an inletOutlet condition, with an inlet value of 0 for air.
The remaining five faces of the domain are no-slip walls.
The velocity at the wall faces is a fixed value of (0, 0, 0).
The pressure (p_rgh) at the wall faces is a fixedFluxPressure condition.
The phase fraction (alpha.water) at the wall faces is a zeroGradient condition.
The time step is dynamically adjusted to maintain a maximum Courant number of 0.5.
Simulation results are written to disk every 0.01 seconds of simulation time.
