The goal is to calculate the density of states for ferrimagnetic Fe3O4.
The calculation will use the LDA exchange-correlation functional.
Use the pseudopotential files PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/fe.upf and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/o.upf.
The starting structure is the 14-atom primitive cell of Fe3O4 in the Fd-3m space group, correcting the provided metadata of 7 atoms.
Initialize the structure with ferrimagnetic ordering, with antiparallel spins on the tetrahedral and octahedral Fe sites.
Set the initial magnetic moment for Fe atoms on the tetrahedral sites to a positive value and for Fe atoms on the octahedral sites to a negative value.
The plane-wave cutoff energy (ecutwfc) and k-point grid density must be systematically converged to achieve a total energy accuracy of 1 meV per atom.
Use a gamma-centered k-point grid for all calculations.
Use Marzari-Vanderbilt smearing for electronic occupations with a smearing width (degauss) of 0.02 Ry.
The calculation workflow consists of six sequential steps.
First, perform a variable-cell relaxation (vc-relax) to fully relax both the lattice vectors and atomic positions.
Second, perform a self-consistent field (scf) calculation on the relaxed structure to obtain the ground-state charge density.
Third, perform a non-self-consistent field (nscf) calculation using a denser k-point grid suitable for DOS calculations.
Fourth, perform a bands calculation on the relaxed structure along the Γ–X–W–K–Γ–L–U–W–L–K high-symmetry path.
Fifth, perform post-processing on the bands calculation output.
Sixth, perform post-processing on the nscf calculation output to generate the final density of states.
The final deliverable is the total density of states.
