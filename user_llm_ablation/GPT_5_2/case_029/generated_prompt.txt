The simulation solves a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem.
The simulation environment is FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The geometry is a rectangle with corners at (0,0) and (1,0.5).
A circular hole with a radius of 0.1 is centered at (0.5, 0.25).
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.33.
A body force due to gravity is applied, with density ρ=2700 kg/m^3 and g=9.81 m/s^2 in the negative y-direction.
The left edge of the plate (x=0) is fixed with a zero-displacement Dirichlet boundary condition.
The bottom edge of the plate (y=0) is fixed with a zero-displacement Dirichlet boundary condition.
The right edge of the plate (x=1) is traction-free.
The boundary of the circular hole is traction-free.
A shear traction vector of (1000, 0) Pa is applied to the top edge of the plate (y=0.5).
The domain is meshed with an unstructured triangular mesh containing approximately 72 elements.
The vector-valued displacement field is discretized using continuous Lagrange elements of polynomial degree 2.
The linear system is solved using a direct solver.
The primary outputs are the displacement field and the von Mises stress field.
The von Mises stress is calculated from the in-plane Cauchy stress components.
