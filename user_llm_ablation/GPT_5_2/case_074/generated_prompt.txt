The objective is to determine the minimum required height for a rectangular beam.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam has a rectangular cross-section with a constant width of 60 mm.
The beam is simply supported.
The maximum applied bending moment is 6,000,000 N·mm.
The allowable bending stress for the beam material is 170 MPa.
The material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The governing relation is the flexure formula, σ_max = M_max / S.
For a rectangular cross-section, the section modulus is S = (b * h^2) / 6.
The minimum required height (h) is calculated by setting the maximum stress to the allowable stress.
The final design equation is h = sqrt((6 * M_max) / (b * σ_allow)).
