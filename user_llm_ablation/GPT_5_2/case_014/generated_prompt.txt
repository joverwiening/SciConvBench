The simulation is a 2-D laminar counter-flow diffusion flame.
The solver to be used is reactingFoam.
The domain is a rectangle spanning from x=0 to 0.02 m, y=-0.01 to 0.01 m, and one cell thick in the z-direction.
The mesh consists of 100 cells in the x-direction and 40 cells in the y-direction.
The boundary at x=0 is named 'fuel'.
The boundary at x=0.02 is named 'air'.
The boundaries at y=-0.01 and y=0.01 are named 'outlet'.
The front and back boundaries are of type 'empty'.
The simulation end time is 0.5 s.
The time step is 1e-4 s.
Results are written every 0.05 s.
The operating pressure is 101325 Pa.
The momentum transport model is 'laminar'.
The combustion model is 'laminar'.
The chemistry type is 'EulerImplicit' with the 'Rosenbrock43' ODE solver.
The thermophysical model is 'hePsiThermo' using 'janaf' thermodynamics.
The species transport model is 'Sutherland'.
The species mixing model is 'coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture'.
The chemical reaction mechanism is GRI-Mech 3.0.
At time 0, the temperature field is initialized to a uniform value of 2000 K.
At time 0, the pressure field is initialized to a uniform value of 101325 Pa.
At time 0, the velocity field is initialized by linear interpolation between the fuel and air boundaries.
At time 0, the species mass fraction fields are initialized by linear interpolation between the fuel and air boundaries.

The 'air' boundary has a fixed velocity of (-0.1 0 0) m/s.
