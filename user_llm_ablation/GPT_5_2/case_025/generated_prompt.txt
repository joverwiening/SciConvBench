The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The problem is a 2D small-strain linear elasticity analysis under plane stress conditions.
The polygonal domain is defined by the eight vertices: (0.0, 0.0), (1.0, 0.0), (1.0, 0.1), (0.8, 0.1), (0.8, 0.2), (0.2, 0.2), (0.2, 0.1), and (0.0, 0.1).
The domain is to be meshed with exactly 72 triangular elements.
The vector-valued displacement space will use first-order continuous Lagrange basis functions.
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa, a Poisson's ratio of 0.33, and a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force representing gravity (g=9.81 m/s^2) is applied in the negative y-direction.
A vertical traction of -10 kPa is applied to the entire top boundary of the domain (the segment where y=0.2).
The bottom boundary of the domain (the segment where y=0) is fixed, with zero displacement in all directions.
The resulting linear system of equations will be solved using a direct solver.
The simulation will compute the displacement magnitude field.
The simulation will compute the von Mises stress field.
