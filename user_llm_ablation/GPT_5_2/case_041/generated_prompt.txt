The simulation domain is a 2D rectangular channel.
The channel is 2.2 meters long and 0.41 meters high.
A cylinder with a diameter of 0.1 meters is placed in the channel.
The center of the cylinder is located at (0.2, 0.2) meters.
The simulation is for 2D laminar, incompressible flow.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 1e-3 m^2/s.
The inlet velocity is a uniform 1 m/s, corresponding to a Reynolds number of 100.
The simulation must capture the transient von Kármán vortex street.
The solver application is pimpleFoam.
The simulation starts at time 0 and ends at time 8 seconds.
The initial time step is 0.005 seconds.
The time step is adjusted automatically to maintain a maximum Courant number of 0.9.
Simulation results are written to disk every 0.1 seconds of simulation time.
The velocity boundary condition at the inlet is a fixed value of (1 0 0) m/s.
The velocity boundary condition at the outlet is zero-gradient.
The velocity boundary condition on the cylinder wall is no-slip.
The velocity boundary condition on the top and bottom walls is no-slip.
The velocity boundary condition for the front and back patches is empty.
The pressure boundary condition at the inlet is zero-gradient.
The pressure boundary condition at the outlet is a fixed value of 0.
The pressure boundary condition on the cylinder wall is zero-gradient.
The pressure boundary condition on the top and bottom walls is zero-gradient.
The pressure boundary condition for the front and back patches is empty.
The temporal derivative scheme is second-order backward.
The gradient scheme is Gauss linear.
The convection scheme for velocity is Gauss linearUpwind grad(U).
The Laplacian scheme is Gauss linear corrected.
The surface-normal gradient scheme is corrected.
The PIMPLE algorithm uses 2 pressure-velocity correctors per time step.
The linear solver for the pressure equation is GAMG with a tolerance of 1e-7 and a relative tolerance of 0.01.
The linear solver for the velocity equation is smoothSolver with a tolerance of 1e-8 and a relative tolerance of 0.1.
