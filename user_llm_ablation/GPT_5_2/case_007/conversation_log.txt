Q: Since Bernoulli's equation is invalid across a hydraulic jump due to energy loss, shall we use the physically correct momentum equation instead?
A: Yes—use the momentum equation (specific force/momentum function) to compute the sequent depth \(y_2\). Do not apply Bernoulli across the hydraulic jump because mechanical energy is not conserved through the jump.
