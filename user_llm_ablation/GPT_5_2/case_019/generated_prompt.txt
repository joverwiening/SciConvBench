The simulation is a 2D flow past a circular cylinder.
The domain is a rectangle from x = -5 m to x = 5 m and y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m.
The cylinder has a diameter of 2 m and is centered at (x=0, y=0).
The front and back patches are of type empty to ensure a 2D simulation.
The inlet boundary condition at x = -5 m is a uniform velocity of (1 0 0) m/s.
The outlet boundary condition at x = 5 m has a zero gradient for velocity and a reference pressure of 0.
The top and bottom walls have a no-slip velocity condition and a zero gradient pressure condition.
The cylinder wall has a no-slip velocity condition and a zero gradient pressure condition.
The initial condition for the entire domain is a quiescent fluid with velocity (0 0 0) m/s and pressure 0.
The flow is laminar.
The base kinematic viscosity is set to 0.01 m²/s.
The viscosity model is generalisedNewtonian using the crossPowerLaw model.
The crossPowerLaw model parameters are nu0 = 100 m²/s, nuInf = 10 m²/s, m = 0.4, and n = 3.
The solver will use the crossPowerLaw model to determine the effective viscosity, which will vary between 10 m²/s and 100 m²/s.
