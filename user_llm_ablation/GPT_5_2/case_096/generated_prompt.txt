The simulation is for two-dimensional Rayleigh-Benard convection.
The fluid is air-like with constant properties.
The kinematic viscosity is 1.5e-5 m²/s.
The Prandtl number is 0.71.
The thermal expansion coefficient is 3.33e-3 1/K.
The reference density is 1.0 kg/m³.
The reference temperature is 293.5 K.
The simulation domain is a square cavity with dimensions of 0.1 m by 0.1 m.
The mesh consists of 50 cells in the x-direction and 50 cells in the y-direction.
Gravitational acceleration is 9.81 m/s² and acts in the negative y-direction.
The bottom wall is held at a constant temperature of 294.0 K.
The top wall is held at a constant temperature of 293.0 K.
The side walls are adiabatic.
A no-slip velocity boundary condition is applied to all walls.
The initial velocity field is zero everywhere.
The initial temperature field is a uniform 293.5 K everywhere.
