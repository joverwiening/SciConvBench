The simulation will model the injection of a compressible gas into a liquid.
Use the compressibleInterFoam solver.
The simulation is transient.
The geometry is a 2D axisymmetric cylinder representing the water tank.
The tank has a radius of 0.5 meters and a height of 1 meter.
A gas injector nozzle is located at the center of the tank's bottom surface.
The nozzle diameter is 0.01 meters.
The gas phase is Nitrogen, modeled using the perfectGas equation of state.
The liquid phase is water, modeled using the perfectFluid equation of state.
The tank is initially filled with quiescent water at a temperature of 293 K.
The initial pressure in the tank is 101325 Pa.
Nitrogen gas is injected vertically upwards from the nozzle.
The gas enters at a Mach number of 0.8.
The total pressure of the injected gas at the nozzle inlet is 1,013,250 Pa.
The static temperature of the injected gas at the nozzle inlet is 293 K.
The tank side and bottom walls are no-slip adiabatic walls.
The top boundary of the tank is a pressure outlet with a fixed pressure of 101325 Pa.
The k-omega SST turbulence model will be used.
The simulation will run for a total duration of 0.5 seconds.
The goal is to observe the gas jet penetration and expansion in the water.
