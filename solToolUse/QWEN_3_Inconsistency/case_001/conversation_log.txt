Q: What is the spatial variation of the linearly varying vertical traction: is it based on the x-coordinate or the y-coordinate of the loaded segment?
A: The spatial variation of the linearly varying vertical traction is based on the x-coordinate. The traction σ_yy(x) = -8000x Pa is applied on the top segment where 0.3 ≤ x ≤ 0.5, and the variation is explicitly with respect to x.
