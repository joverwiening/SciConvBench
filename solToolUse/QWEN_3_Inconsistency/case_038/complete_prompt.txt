Case ID: case_038
Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
Solver: small-strain linear elasticity in legacy FEniCS (dolfin); solve the 2D plane-stress variational problem with a vector-valued displacement space and a direct linear solver.
Task:
Analyze stress in a semi-circular domain (radius 1m) with fixed base and inward normal pressure on the arc under gravity. Implement plane stress elasticity with E=70e9 Pa, ν=0.33. Compute displacement and von Mises stress distributions.
Case details:
Material: E=70e9, ν=0.33, ρ=2700 kg/m^3, g=9.81 m/s^2. Geometry: Half-disk (y≥0). Loads: Gravity (-y), inward pressure σ_n=-20 kPa. Boundaries: y=0 fixed. Mesh: 64 elements.
Implementation notes:
Treat the 2D domain as plane stress with unit thickness and use the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
Compute von Mises from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
For inward pressure on a hole or arc, apply t = -p*n with n the outward normal of the solid.
Treat the arc load as an inward pressure on the semicircular boundary, not as a vertical traction.
