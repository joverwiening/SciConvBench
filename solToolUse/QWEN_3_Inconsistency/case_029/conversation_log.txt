Q: What is the exact direction and sign convention for the ±1000 Pa normal tractions on the top and bottom edges? Specifically, is +1000 Pa on the top edge directed downward (negative y) and -1000 Pa on the bottom edge directed upward (positive y), or is it the opposite?
A: +1000 Pa on the top edge is directed inward, which is downward (negative y-direction).  
-1000 Pa on the bottom edge is also directed inward, which is upward (positive y-direction).  

Thus, both tractions are compressive (pushing into the domain).
