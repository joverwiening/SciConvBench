Q: What is the exact expression for the reduced plane-stress constitutive law to be used, given E=70e9 Pa and ν=0.33?
A: Use the plane-stress constitutive matrix:

\[
\mathbf{C} = \frac{E}{1 - \nu^2}
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & \nu & 0 \\
\nu & 1 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & \frac{1 - \nu}{2}
\end{bmatrix}
\]

with \( E = 70 \times 10^9 \) Pa and \( \nu = 0.33 \).

Q: What is the exact method or function in legacy FEniCS (dolfin) to be used for distributing the vertical point load as a uniform traction over the hypotenuse, given that concentrated loads are not allowed?
A: Do not distribute the point load over the hypotenuse. Instead, apply the vertical point load as a concentrated force at the centroid (0.5, 0.25) using the `PointSource` function in legacy FEniCS (dolfin) on the y-component of the displacement vector.
