Q: What is the specific range of the top boundary where the linearly varying vertical traction is applied, and what are the traction values at the start and end of that range?
A: The linearly varying vertical traction is applied on the top segment where 0.3 ≤ x ≤ 0.5.

The traction formula is σ_yy(x) = -8000x Pa, so:
- At x = 0.3: σ_yy = -2400 Pa
- At x = 0.5: σ_yy = -4000 Pa
