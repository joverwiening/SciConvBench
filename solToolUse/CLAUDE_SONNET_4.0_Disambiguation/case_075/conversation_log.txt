Q: What specific material properties are missing from the EL material definition that are required for the eigenvalue analysis?
A: The EL material definition is missing the *ELASTIC properties. The complete requirement shows:

```
*MATERIAL,NAME=EL
*ELASTIC
210000.,.3
*DENSITY
7.8E-9
*EXPANSION
12.E-6
```

But the incomplete version only has *DENSITY and *EXPANSION. The *ELASTIC line with Young's modulus (210000.) and Poisson's ratio (.3) is required for the eigenvalue analysis.
