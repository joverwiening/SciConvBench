Q: What specific plane stress constitutive law formulation should be used for the reduced stiffness matrix (e.g., should I use the standard plane stress reduction where σ_zz = 0, or do you have a specific matrix form in mind)?
A: Use the standard plane stress reduction where σ_zz = 0. This gives the typical 2D plane stress constitutive matrix with the reduced stiffness coefficients.
