Q: What is the exact direction of the ±1000 Pa tractions on the top and bottom edges - should the top edge have +1000 Pa (downward into the plate) and bottom edge have -1000 Pa (upward into the plate), or the reverse?
A: The ±1000 Pa values should be interpreted as inward and outward normal tractions on the horizontal top and bottom edges. Apply +1000 Pa as an inward normal traction on the top edge (downward into the plate) and -1000 Pa as an outward normal traction on the bottom edge (upward out of the plate).
