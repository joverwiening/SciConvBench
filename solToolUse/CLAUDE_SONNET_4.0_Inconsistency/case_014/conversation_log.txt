Q: What should be the magnitude of the uniform pressure patch that replaces the two symmetric point loads of -3 kN each?
A: The complete requirement specifies to "Apply the two symmetric point loads at the stated arc points" using PointSource, not to replace them with a uniform pressure patch. Use the -3 kN point loads directly at (±0.707, 0.707).
