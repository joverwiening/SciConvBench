Case ID: case_024
Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
Solver: small-strain linear elasticity in legacy FEniCS (dolfin); solve the 2D plane-stress variational problem with a vector-valued displacement space and a direct linear solver.
Task:
Solve Solid Mechanics Problem 3 from the paper: a plate with a centred circular hole in plane stress.
Case details:
Geometry:
Let Ω = (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) m be a rectangular plate with a centred circular hole of radius a = 0.05 m at (0.50, 0.10).
Model:
Plane-stress linear elasticity for displacement u = (u_x, u_y) in Ω.
Material:
Young's modulus E = 200 GPa, Poisson's ratio ν = 0.30.
Boundary conditions:
Left edge (x = 0): fixed, u_x = 0, u_y = 0.
Right edge (x = 1.0): traction t = (2 MPa*m, 0).
All other external boundaries, including the hole boundary: traction-free.
Outputs:
Compute the plane-stress von Mises field and save q3_vm.png.
Save the displacement field in XDMF format.
Report the maximum von Mises stress on the hole boundary and the stress concentration factor Kt = sigma_max / 2 MPa.
Implementation notes:
Treat the 2D strip as plane stress with unit thickness and use the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
Interpret t = (2 MPa*m, 0) as a line traction per unit thickness on x = 1.
Leave the hole boundary traction-free.
