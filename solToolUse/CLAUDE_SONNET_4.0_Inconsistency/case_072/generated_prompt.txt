The simulation models two cubes stacked vertically using C3D8 elements with mortar contact method.
The contact allows separation under tension but prevents penetration between the cube surfaces.
Boundary conditions fix the bottom cube nodes in z-direction and some nodes in x-y directions.
A distributed load of 100 units is applied to surface 2 in the z-direction.
Material properties include elastic modulus of 210000 and Poisson's ratio of 0.
Output requests include nodal displacements and element stresses.
