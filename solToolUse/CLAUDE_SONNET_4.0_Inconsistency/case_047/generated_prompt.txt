The material is treated as ordinary rate-independent linear elasticity.
The beam geometry is defined by nodes with coordinates ranging from approximately (43.75, 0, 0.75) to (-7.4, 56.4, 0).

Several node sets (Nsets) and element sets (Elsets) are created for different parts of the beam: set2, set5, set6, and set7.
A solid section is assigned to the element set 'set2' with the material 'johnsoncooksteel'.

Boundary conditions are applied to restrict movement: set6 in the x-direction, set5 in the y-direction, and set7 in the z-direction.

The amplitude curve "Druckkurve1820mm von DDT" starts at 140 at time 0.0 and drops to 80 at time 0.000125.
The simulation step is defined with non-linear geometry considerations and a dynamic explicit procedure with time increments from 1e-6 to 0.00001.
