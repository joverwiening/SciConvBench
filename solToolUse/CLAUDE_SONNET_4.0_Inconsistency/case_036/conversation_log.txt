Q: What is the magnitude and direction of the tangential shear traction that should be applied along the left slope?
A: The complete requirement specifies a horizontal traction of σ_xx=6000 Pa on the left slope, not a tangential shear traction. Apply the 6000 Pa as a horizontal traction (in the x-direction) along the left slope boundary.
