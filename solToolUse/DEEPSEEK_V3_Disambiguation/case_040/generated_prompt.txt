Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve a quasi-incompressible finite-strain hyperelasticity problem.
Solve Solid Mechanics Problem 14: a two-hole strip with follower pressure and prescribed right-edge displacement.
Define the domain geometry as a rectangular strip Ω = (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) meters.
Create two circular holes of radius a = 0.04 m centered at (0.40, 0.10) m and (0.60, 0.10) m.
Use a plane strain assumption.
Model the material as a large-deformation isotropic quasi-incompressible neo-Hookean solid.
Use a Young's modulus E of 5 MPa and a Poisson's ratio ν of 0.49.
Use a mixed displacement-pressure formulation with unknowns u (displacement) and p (hydrostatic pressure).
Use a Taylor-Hood (P2-P1) finite element space for the mixed formulation.
Generate a mesh with a global element size of 0.01 m.
Apply a fixed boundary condition on the left edge (x=0): u_x = 0 and u_y = 0.
Apply a prescribed displacement on the right edge (x=1.0): u_x = 0.060 m and u_y = 0.
Apply a uniform follower pressure of 0.10 MPa normal to the deformed boundaries of both holes.
Leave the top and bottom edges traction-free.
Solve the nonlinear problem using Newton's method with load stepping.
Apply the total load in 20 equal increments.
Set the convergence tolerance for Newton's method to 1.0e-8 on the residual norm.
Set the maximum number of Newton iterations allowed per load step to 20.
