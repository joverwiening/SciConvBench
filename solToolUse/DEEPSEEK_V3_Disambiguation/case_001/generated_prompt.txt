Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
Solve a small-strain linear elasticity problem in 2D under plane stress conditions.
The material has Young's modulus E = 70e9 Pa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.33.
The material density is ρ = 2700 kg/m³.
The domain is a polygon defined by vertices (0,0), (0.8,0), (0.8,0.2), (0.5,0.2), (0.5,0.5), (0.3,0.5), (0.3,0.2), and (0,0.2).
Apply a fixed (Dirichlet) boundary condition of zero displacement on the bottom edge where y=0.
Apply a vertical traction load on the top boundary segment where 0.3 ≤ x ≤ 0.5.
The vertical traction is defined as σ_yy(x) = -8000 * x Pa.
Apply a gravity body force vector of (0, -ρ*g) throughout the domain, with g = 9.81 m/s².
Use a mesh with 72 elements.
Use a vector-valued displacement function space.
Use a direct linear solver.
Compute the displacement field.
Compute the von Mises stress field from the in-plane Cauchy stress components.
Implement the reduced constitutive law for plane stress.
Assume a unit thickness of 1 meter for the 2D domain.
