The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane stress.
The domain is a parallelogram with vertices (0,0), (1,0), (1.2,0.3), (0.2,0.3).
The material properties are Young's modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, density ρ=2700 kg/m^3, and gravitational acceleration g=9.81 m/s^2.
The loads include gravity acting in the negative y-direction and a horizontal point load of -5 kN applied at (1.1, 0.15).
The boundary condition is fixed (clamped) on the inclined edge connecting vertices (0,0) and (0.2,0.3).
The mesh will consist of 72 triangular elements.
The solver will be a direct linear solver.
The 2D domain will be treated as plane stress with unit thickness, using the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
The outputs to be computed are the displacement field and the von Mises stress distribution, derived from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
