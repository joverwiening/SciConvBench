The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The solver will implement small-strain von Mises elastoplasticity.
The solver will use return mapping, symmetry conditions, and Newton iterations for the incremental solve.
The task is to solve Solid Mechanics Problem 16 from the specified paper.
The problem involves a quarter plate with a quarter hole under plane-strain elastoplasticity.
The geometry is a symmetric quarter domain [0, 100] x [0, 180] mm.
A quarter hole of radius 50 mm is located at the origin.
The model is small-strain von Mises elastoplasticity with associative flow and perfect plasticity in plane strain.
The unknown is the displacement field u = (u_x, u_y).
The strain epsilon is defined as 0.5*(grad(u) + grad(u)^T).
The material properties are lambda = 19.44 GPa, mu = 29.17 GPa, and yield stress sigma_Y = 243 MPa.
On the boundary x = 0, symmetry conditions are imposed: u_x = 0 and zero tangential traction.
On the boundary y = 0, symmetry conditions are imposed: u_y = 0 and zero tangential traction.
On the boundary y = 180 mm, a prescribed displacement u = (0, 1) mm is applied.
On the boundary x = 100 mm and on the hole boundary, traction-free conditions are applied.
The resulting displacement field will be saved to XDMF format.
The implementation will use an incremental return-mapping update consistent with plane strain perfect plasticity.
