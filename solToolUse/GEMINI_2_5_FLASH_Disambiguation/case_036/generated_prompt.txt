The simulation will be implemented using FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is 2D small-strain linear elasticity under plane stress conditions.
The computational domain is a parallelogram with vertices at (0,0), (1,0), (1.2,0.3), and (0.2,0.3).
The material properties are Young's modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, and density ρ=2700 kg/m^3.
Body forces due to gravity act in the negative y-direction with g=9.81 m/s^2.
A horizontal traction vector of (6000, 0) N/m^2 is applied to the left inclined edge, which connects (0.2,0.3) to (0,0).
The right inclined edge, which connects (1,0) to (1.2,0.3), is fixed, meaning displacement is zero in both x and y directions.
The mesh will consist of 72 triangular elements.
The variational problem will use a vector-valued displacement function space.
A direct linear solver will be employed.
The constitutive law for plane stress will be used, assuming unit thickness.
The simulation will compute and output the displacement field.
The simulation will compute and output the von Mises stress distribution derived from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
