The problem involves solving small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane stress using FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The domain Ω is a rectangular plate (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) m with a semicircular notch of radius 0.05 m removed from the top edge.
The notch center is located at (0.5, 0.2) m.
The material properties are Young's modulus E = 200 GPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.30.
The constitutive law for plane stress with unit thickness must be used.
The displacement field u = (u_x, u_y) is defined in Ω.
Boundary conditions are as follows:
The bottom edge (y = 0) is fixed, meaning u_x = 0 and u_y = 0.
A downward line traction t = (0, -10 MPa*m) per unit thickness is applied to the top edge (y = 0.2) for x in [0, 0.45) and x in (0.55, 1.0].
Both vertical sides (x = 0 and x = 1) and the notch arc are traction-free.
The variational problem should be solved using a vector-valued displacement space and a direct linear solver.
The outputs required are the plane-stress von Mises field, saved as q6_vm.png, and the displacement field, saved in XDMF format.
