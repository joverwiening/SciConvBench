The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The solver will implement small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane-stress.
The solver will use a vector-valued displacement space.
The solver will use a direct linear solver.
The problem domain is a rectangular plate Ω = (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) m.
The mesh will be a uniform structured mesh with 20 subdivisions in x and 4 subdivisions in y.
The model is plane-stress linear elasticity for displacement u = (u_x, u_y).
The material properties are Young's modulus E = 200 GPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.30.
The left edge (x = 0) has fixed boundary conditions, with u_x = 0 and u_y = 0.
The right edge (x = 1) has prescribed displacement boundary conditions, with u_x = 0.001 m and u_y = 0.
The top edge (y = 0.20) and bottom edge (y = 0) are traction-free.
A colour map of the u_x displacement component will be saved as q1_ux.png.
The resulting displacement field will be saved in XDMF format.
The 2D strip will be treated as plane stress with unit thickness.
The reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be used.
