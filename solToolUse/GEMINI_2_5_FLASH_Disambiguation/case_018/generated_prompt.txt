The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane stress.
The variational problem will use a vector-valued displacement space.
A direct linear solver will be used.
The task is to simulate the static linear elastic deformation of a cantilever plate with a circular hole.
The geometry is a rectangle of dimensions 4.0 units by 1.0 unit.
A circular hole is centered at (1.0, 0.5) with a radius of 0.3 units.
The mesh resolution will be 72.
The function space will be second-order vector Lagrange.
The material properties are Young's Modulus E = 210e3 and Poisson's Ratio ν = 0.3.
The left edge (x = 0) is clamped, meaning displacement u = 0.
A uniform downward traction of magnitude 1e5 per unit thickness is applied on the right edge (x = 4.0).
No body forces are present.
The simulation will compute the displacement field.
The simulation will compute the von Mises stress field.
The plate will be treated as a 2D plane-stress problem with unit thickness.
The reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be used.
