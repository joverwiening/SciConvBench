The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is a 2D plane-stress small-strain linear elasticity problem.
The domain is a 1 meter by 0.5 meter rectangle with a central circular hole of 0.1 meter radius located at (0.5, 0.25).
The material properties are Young's Modulus E = 70e9 Pa, Poisson's Ratio ν = 0.33, and density ρ = 2700 kg/m^3.
Gravity loading is applied with g = 9.81 m/s^2 in the negative y-direction.
A downward concentrated point load of 5 kN is applied at the coordinate (1, 0.25).
The left edge of the rectangular plate (x=0) is fixed, meaning all displacement components are zero.
The problem will be solved using a direct linear solver.
The mesh will consist of 72 triangular finite elements.
The finite elements will be quadratic (P2).
The 2D domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness, using the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
The simulation will compute and output the displacement field.
The simulation will compute and output the von Mises stress distribution derived from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
