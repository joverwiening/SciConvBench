The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve a 2D plane-stress small-strain linear elasticity problem.
The domain is a stepped polygon defined by the 10 vertices (0,0), (1.0,0), (1.0,0.5), (0.7,0.5), (0.5,0.5), (0.5,1.0), (0.7,1.0), (0.3,1.0), (0.3,0.5), and (0,0.5), listed in counter-clockwise order.
The material properties are Young's Modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's Ratio ν=0.33, density ρ=2700 kg/m^3.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction with g=9.81 m/s^2.
A horizontal traction of 5000 Pa is applied on the top boundary segment where 0.3 ≤ x ≤ 0.5 (at y=1.0).
The bottom boundary (y=0, 0 ≤ x ≤ 1.0) is fixed (u_x=0, u_y=0).
The vertical stem at x=0.3 (for 0.5 ≤ y ≤ 1.0) has a prescribed displacement u_y=0.
The vertical stem at x=0.5 (for 0.5 ≤ y ≤ 1.0) has a prescribed displacement u_y=0.
The domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness, and the reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be used.
The mesh will consist of 72 elements.
A direct linear solver will be used.
The simulation will compute and output the displacement field and the von Mises stress distribution, derived from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
