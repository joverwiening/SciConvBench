The simulation uses FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The solver implements small-strain linear elasticity.
The problem is a 2D plane-stress variational problem.
The displacement space is vector-valued.
A direct linear solver is used.
The material properties are Young's modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, density ρ=2700 kg/m^3.
Gravity g=9.81 m/s^2 acts in the negative y-direction.
The geometry is a rectangular plate with dimensions 1m by 0.5m.
A circular hole with a radius of 0.1m is centered at (0.5m, 0.25m).
The mesh has 64 elements per edge.
The left boundary (x=0) is fixed (zero displacement).
A sinusoidal vertical traction is applied to the right edge (x=1).
The amplitude of the sinusoidal vertical traction is 6000 Pa.
The frequency of the sinusoidal vertical traction is 4π radians per meter.
A constant shear traction is applied to the top edge (y=0.5).
The magnitude of the constant shear traction is 1000 Pa.
The constant shear traction is in the positive x-direction.
The 2D domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness.
The reduced plane-stress constitutive law is used.
The hole boundary is traction-free.
The simulation computes displacement and stress fields.
Von Mises stress is computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
