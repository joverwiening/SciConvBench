The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The solver will implement small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane-stress.
A vector-valued displacement space will be used.
A direct linear solver will be employed.
The domain is a semi-circular region with radius 1m, defined by y≥0.
The material properties are Young's Modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, and density ρ=2700 kg/m^3.
Gravity g=9.81 m/s^2 acts as a body force.
A concentrated vertical point load of -5 kN is applied at the apex of the semi-circle.
The boundary condition on the straight edge (y=0) is fixed, meaning all displacement components are zero.
The 2D domain will be treated as plane stress with unit thickness, using the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
The mesh will consist of 64 quadrilateral elements, generated by dividing the radius into 4 segments and the 180-degree arc into 16 segments.
Displacement and von Mises stress distributions will be computed.
Von Mises stress will be calculated from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
