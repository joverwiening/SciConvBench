The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane-stress.
The geometry is an L-shaped domain defined by a 1x1 square with the top-right 0.5x0.5 square removed, specifically the region [0,1]x[0,1] excluding [0.5,1]x[0.5,1].
The material properties are Young's Modulus E=70e9 Pa and Poisson's Ratio ν=0.33.
The density ρ=2700 kg/m^3 and gravity g=9.81 are provided but not used in this static analysis.
Boundary conditions include clamped (zero displacement) on the outer edges x=0 (for y in [0,1]) and y=0 (for x in [0,1]).
A horizontal traction of σ_xx=2.65e4 Pa is applied to the outer edge x=1 (for y in [0,0.5]).
The domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness.
The mesh resolution is 64 elements per unit length along the outer edges.
The problem will be solved using a direct linear solver.
The outputs will be the displacement field and the von Mises stress field, computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress.
