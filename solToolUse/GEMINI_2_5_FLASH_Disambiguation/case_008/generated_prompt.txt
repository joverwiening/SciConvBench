The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve a 2D plane-stress small-strain orthotropic elasticity problem.
The domain is a rectangular plate Ω = (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) m.
A structured mesh of 50 x 25 elements will be used over the domain.
The model will solve for the displacement field u = (u_x, u_y).
The material is orthotropic with local properties E1 = 40 GPa, E2 = 10 GPa, G12 = 5 GPa, and nu12 = 0.25.
The local material axes are rotated by 30 degrees anticlockwise with respect to the global x-y axes.
The standard plane-stress reduced stiffness matrix [Q] will be used and rotated to the global frame.
The bottom edge (y = 0) is fixed, with u_x = 0 and u_y = 0.
The top edge (y = 0.20) has a uniform downward traction t = (0, -10 MPa*m), interpreted as a line traction per unit thickness.
The vertical sides (x = 0 and x = 1) are traction-free.
A direct linear solver will be used.
A colour map of the u_x displacement field will be saved as q8_ux.png.
A colour map of the von Mises stress will be saved as q8_vm.png.
The displacement and stress fields will be written to q8_solution.xdmf.
