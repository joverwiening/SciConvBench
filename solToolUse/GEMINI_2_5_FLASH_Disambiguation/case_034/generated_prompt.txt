The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve a 2D plane-stress small-strain linear elasticity problem.
The problem will be solved using a vector-valued displacement space and a direct linear solver.
The geometry is an L-shaped structure with outer dimensions 1x1 and an inner cutout of 0.5x0.5.
The mesh will have 64 elements per edge.
The material properties are Young's Modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, density ρ=2700 kg/m^3, and gravitational acceleration g=9.81 m/s^2.
The structure is subjected to gravity loading, acting as a body force in the negative y-direction.
The left boundary (x=0) has both x and y displacement components fixed to zero.
On the right edge (x=1), a spatially varying vertical traction σ_yy(y) = -2.65e4*y Pa is applied.
The horizontal component of traction on the right edge (x=1) is zero.
The top, bottom, and inner edges of the L-shaped structure are traction-free.
The 2D domain will be treated as plane stress with unit thickness, and the reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be used.
The simulation will compute the stress and displacement distributions.
The von Mises stress will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
