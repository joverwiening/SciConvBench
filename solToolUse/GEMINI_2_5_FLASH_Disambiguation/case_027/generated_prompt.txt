The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane stress.
A vector-valued displacement space will be used.
A direct linear solver will be employed.
The computational domain is a right triangle with vertices at (0,0), (1,0), and (0,0.5).
The material properties are Young's Modulus E = 70e9 Pa, Poisson's Ratio ν = 0.33, density ρ = 2700 kg/m^3, and gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s^2.
The domain will be treated as plane stress with unit thickness, using the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
The mesh will be generated with a resolution parameter of 64.
The boundary condition on the base (y=0) is clamped, meaning zero displacement in both x and y directions.
A horizontal shear traction of τ_x = 2000 Pa is applied along the sloping edge (defined by x+2y=1).
The vertical edge (x=0) is a free surface with zero traction.
Gravity loading is applied as a body force throughout the domain, with f = (0, -ρg).
The displacement magnitude distribution will be computed.
The von Mises stress distribution will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
