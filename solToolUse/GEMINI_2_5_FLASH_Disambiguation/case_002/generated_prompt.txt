The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane-stress.
The domain is a right triangle with vertices at (0,0), (1,0), and (0,0.5).
The material properties are Young's modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, density ρ=2700 kg/m^3, and gravitational acceleration g=9.81 m/s^2.
The domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness.
The reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be used.
The body force due to gravity acts in the negative y-direction.
The boundary condition on the vertical edge (x=0) is clamped (zero displacement in both x and y directions).
The boundary condition on the bottom edge (y=0) is zero traction.
The boundary condition on the sloping edge (x+2y=1) is an upward normal traction with a magnitude of 3000 Pa.
The mesh will consist of 64 total cells.
The problem will be solved using a vector-valued displacement space and a direct linear solver.
The outputs will be the displacement magnitude distribution and the von Mises stress distribution.
Von Mises stress will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
