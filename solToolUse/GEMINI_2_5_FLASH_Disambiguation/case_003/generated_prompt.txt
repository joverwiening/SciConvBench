The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve a 2D plane-stress small-strain linear elasticity variational problem.
The domain is an L-shaped structure defined as the unit square [0,1]x[0,1] with the top-right square [0.5,1]x[0.5,1] removed.
The material properties are Young's modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson ratio ν=0.33, and density ρ=2700 kg/m^3.
Gravity loading is applied as a body force in the negative y-direction, with g=9.81 m/s^2.
A linearly varying vertical traction σ_yy(y) = -2.65e4*y Pa is applied on the outer right edge (x=1) for y in the range [0, 0.5].
The left boundary (x=0) is fixed, meaning displacement components u_x and u_y are zero.
The domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness, and the reduced plane-stress constitutive law is used.
The mesh resolution will be 64 elements per characteristic edge length.
The problem will be solved using a direct linear solver.
The simulation will compute and output the displacement and stress distributions.
Von Mises stress will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
