The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The solver will implement small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane-stress.
The problem will use a vector-valued displacement space and a direct linear solver.
The domain is a right triangle with vertices at (0,0), (1,0), and (0,0.5).
The material properties are Young's Modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, density ρ=2700 kg/m^3, and gravitational acceleration g=9.81 m/s^2.
Gravity loading will be applied in the negative y-direction.
The left edge (x=0) of the triangle is clamped, meaning displacement is zero in both x and y directions.
A sinusoidal vertical traction T_y = -5e3*sin(πx) Pa will be applied on the hypotenuse (defined by the equation x+2y=1).
The mesh will be generated with a resolution parameter of 64.
The 2D domain will be treated as plane stress with unit thickness, using the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
Displacement and von Mises stress distributions will be computed.
Von Mises stress will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
The spatial variation of the traction field will be evaluated using the global coordinate x.
