The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The solver will implement incompressible finite-strain hyperelasticity using a mixed displacement-pressure neo-Hookean formulation with Newton's method.
The task is to solve Solid Mechanics Problem 12 from the paper, which is an incompressible neo-Hookean perforated strip in plane strain under prescribed displacement.
The geometry is a rectangular strip Ω = (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) m with a circular hole of radius a = 0.04 m centered at (0.50, 0.10).
The model is a large-deformation isotropic incompressible neo-Hookean solid with unknowns displacement u = (u_x, u_y) and hydrostatic pressure p.
The problem is set in plane strain.
The material properties are Young's modulus E = 5 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.5.
The standard incompressible neo-Hookean strain energy will be used with a pressure field p imposing J = 1 in a mixed (u, p) formulation.
The boundary conditions are as follows: the left edge (x = 0) is fixed with u_x = 0 and u_y = 0.
The right edge (x = 1.0) has a prescribed horizontal displacement of u_x = 0.060 m and u_y = 0.
The top, bottom, and the hole boundary are traction-free.
The outputs will include a color map of pressure p saved as q12_p.png.
A color map of von Mises stress computed from the Cauchy stress will be saved as q12_vm.png.
The displacement field will be saved in XDMF format.
The implementation will use F = I + grad(u) and J = det(F).
A mixed displacement-pressure formulation will be used, and pressure will not be eliminated by a nearly incompressible displacement-only approximation.
