The simulation uses FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane-stress.
The domain is a semi-circular region with radius 1 meter, defined by y>=0.
The material properties are Young's modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, density ρ=2700 kg/m^3.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction with g=9.81 m/s^2.
An inward normal pressure of 20 kPa is applied uniformly along the curved arc boundary.
The straight boundary at y=0 is fixed, meaning all displacement components are zero.
The mesh is generated with a resolution corresponding to 64 segments along the curved arc boundary.
The variational problem uses a vector-valued displacement space.
A direct linear solver is used.
The 2D domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness, using the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
The simulation computes the displacement field.
The simulation computes the von Mises stress distribution from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
