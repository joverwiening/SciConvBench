The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane-stress.
The variational problem will use a vector-valued displacement space.
A direct linear solver will be used.
The material properties are Young's Modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, density ρ=2700 kg/m^3, and gravitational acceleration g=9.81 m/s^2.
The geometry is an 8-vertex stepped polygon defined by the vertices (0.0, 0.0), (1.0, 0.0), (1.0, 1.0), (0.7, 1.0), (0.7, 0.7), (0.3, 0.7), (0.3, 1.0), and (0.0, 1.0).
Gravity acts as a body force.
A vertical traction of 10 kPa is applied to the top boundary segment where 0.3 ≤ x ≤ 0.5.
The bottom edge of the polygon (y=0) is fixed.
The mesh will consist of 72 elements.
The 2D domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness.
The reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be used.
The simulation will compute the displacement magnitude distribution.
The simulation will compute the von Mises stress distribution from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
