The problem is a 2D plane-stress small-strain linear elasticity simulation using FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The domain is a rectangular plate Ω = (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) m.
The domain is split into two perfectly bonded material subdomains across y = 0.10 m.
The mesh is a structured 80 x 16 grid over Ω.
The model uses small-strain linear elasticity for displacement u = (u_x, u_y).
The material properties for the subdomain above y = 0.10 m (Ω_Al) are Young's modulus E = 70 GPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.30.
The material properties for the subdomain below y = 0.10 m (Ω_Steel) are Young's modulus E = 200 GPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.30.
A perfect bond is enforced at the interface y = 0.10 m, ensuring continuity of displacement and traction.
The left edge (x = 0) is fixed, with u_x = 0 and u_y = 0.
The right edge (x = 1) has a uniform downward line traction t = (0, -5000) N/m.
The top and bottom edges are traction-free.
The simulation should use the reduced plane-stress constitutive law, treating the 2D strip with unit thickness.
The solution will be obtained using a direct linear solver.
A colour map of the magnitude of displacement |u| will be saved as q7_disp.png.
The displacement field will be saved in XDMF format.
