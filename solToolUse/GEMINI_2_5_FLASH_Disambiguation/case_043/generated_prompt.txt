The simulation uses FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve a 2D plane-stress small-strain linear elasticity problem.
The domain is a parallelogram defined by vertices (0,0), (1,0), (1.2,0.3), and (0.2,0.3).
The material properties are Young's modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, and density ρ=2700 kg/m^3.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction with g=9.81 m/s^2.
An inward normal traction of 5000 Pa is applied to the horizontal top edge.
Both x and y components of displacement are fixed to zero on the left and right sloped edges.
The problem uses a vector-valued displacement space and a direct linear solver.
The mesh consists of 72 elements.
The 2D domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness, using the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
The simulation computes and outputs the displacement field and the von Mises stress distribution, derived from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
