The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is a 2D plane-strain nearly incompressible small-strain elasticity problem.
A mixed displacement-pressure formulation will be used with a direct saddle-point solve.
The geometry is a rectangular strip Ω = (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) m with a circular hole.
The circular hole has a radius of 0.04 m and is centered at (0.50, 0.10).
The material properties are Young's modulus E = 5 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.49.
The finite element pair for the mixed formulation will be Taylor-Hood (P2-P1), using quadratic elements for displacement and linear elements for pressure.
Boundary conditions are as follows:
The left edge (x = 0) is fixed, with u_x = 0 and u_y = 0.
The right edge (x = 1.0) has a prescribed displacement of u_x = 0.001 m and u_y = 0.
The top edge (y = 0.20), bottom edge (y = 0), and the hole boundary are traction-free.
The simulation will compute the von Mises stress field.
A colour map of the von Mises field will be saved as q11_vm.png.
A colour map of the u_x displacement component will be saved as q11_ux.png.
The displacement field will be saved in XDMF format.
