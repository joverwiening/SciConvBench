The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve a 2D plane-stress small-strain linear elasticity problem.
The problem will be solved using a vector-valued displacement space and a direct linear solver.
The material properties are Young's Modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, density ρ=2700 kg/m^3, and gravitational acceleration g=9.81 m/s^2.
The geometry is a polygon defined by the vertices (0,0), (0.8,0), (0.8,0.2), (0.5,0.2), (0.5,0.5), (0.3,0.5), (0.3,0.2), and (0,0.2).
The bottom edge of the domain is fixed.
Loads include gravity acting on the entire domain and a vertical point load of 5 kN applied at coordinates (0.4, 0.5).
The mesh will consist of 72 elements.
The 2D domain will be treated as plane stress with unit thickness, using the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
The simulation will compute the displacement magnitude distribution and the von Mises stress distribution.
Von Mises stress will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
