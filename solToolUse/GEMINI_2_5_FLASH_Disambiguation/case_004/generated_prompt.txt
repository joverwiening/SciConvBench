The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The solver will implement small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane-stress.
A vector-valued displacement space will be used.
A direct linear solver will be employed.
The material properties are Young's Modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, density ρ=2700 kg/m^3, and gravitational acceleration g=9.81 m/s^2.
The geometry is a stepped polygon defined by the 8 vertices: (0,0), (1,0), (1,0.5), (0.75,0.5), (0.75,1), (0.25,1), (0.25,0.5), (0,0.5).
Gravity acts as a body force in the negative y-direction.
The boundary at y=0 is fixed, meaning displacement is zero.
An elastic support acts on the top boundary segment where 0.3 <= x <= 0.5.
The spring stiffness for the elastic support is k=1e6 N/m^2.
All other boundaries not explicitly mentioned are traction-free.
The mesh will consist of 72 elements.
The 2D domain will be treated as plane stress with unit thickness, using the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
Displacement and von Mises stress distributions will be computed.
Von Mises stress will be calculated from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
