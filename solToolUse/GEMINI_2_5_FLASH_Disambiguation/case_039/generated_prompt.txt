The simulation uses FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve a 2D plane-stress small-strain linear elasticity problem.
The domain is a 1 meter by 0.5 meter rectangular plate with a circular hole of 0.1 meter radius centered at (0.5, 0.25) meters.
The material properties are Young's Modulus E = 70e9 Pa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.33.
The problem uses a vector-valued displacement space.
A direct linear solver is used.
The mesh is generated with approximately 64 divisions along the 1 meter length and 32 divisions along the 0.5 meter width, implying a characteristic element size of 1/64 meter.
Boundary conditions include a uniform vertical compression of σ_yy = -2500 Pa applied to the top (y=0.5) and bottom (y=0) edges.
The left edge (x=0) has a vertical displacement constraint (uy=0).
The right edge (x=1) has a horizontal displacement constraint (ux=0).
The 2D domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness, using the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
The simulation computes the displacement field and the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
Von Mises stress is computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
