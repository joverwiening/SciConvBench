The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane-stress.
The geometry is a cross-shaped plate consisting of a horizontal bar from x = -1 to 1 and a vertical bar from y = -1 to 1, both 0.6 units wide.
Four circular holes are cut into the plate, each with a radius of 0.12.
The holes are centered at (0.6, 0), (-0.6, 0), (0, 0.6), and (0, -0.6).
The mesh resolution is 100.
The function space is second-order vector Lagrange.
The material properties are Young's modulus E = 210000 and Poisson ratio ν = 0.3.
The boundary condition is a clamped left edge, where the displacement u = (0, 0) on x = -1.
A downward traction with a magnitude of 5e4 is applied on the right edge (x = 1).
The problem will be solved using a direct linear solver.
The simulation will compute the displacement and von Mises stress fields.
The cross-shaped plate is treated as a 2D plane-stress problem with unit thickness.
The reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be used.
