The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is a 2D plane-stress small-strain linear elasticity variational problem.
The displacement space is vector-valued.
A direct linear solver will be used.
The geometry is an L-shaped structure with outer dimensions 1x1 and an inner cutout of 0.5x0.5.
The left boundary (x=0) is fixed.
A vertical point load of -2.65e4 N is applied at coordinates (0.5, 0.5).
Material properties are E=70e9 Pa, ν=0.33, ρ=2700 kg/m^3.
A gravity body force is included with g=9.81 m/s^2 acting in the (0, -1) direction.
The mesh will have 64 elements per edge.
The 2D domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness.
The reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be used.
The simulation will analyze stress and displacement patterns.
Von Mises stress will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
