The problem is a quasi-incompressible finite-strain hyperelasticity problem solved using FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The solver uses a mixed displacement-pressure neo-Hookean formulation with Newton's method.
The problem is Solid Mechanics Problem 14 from the paper, a two-hole strip with follower pressure and prescribed right-edge displacement.
The domain Ω is a rectangular strip (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) m.
The domain contains two circular holes of radius 0.04 m centered at (0.40, 0.10) m and (0.60, 0.10) m.
The model is a large-deformation isotropic quasi-incompressible neo-Hookean solid.
The unknowns are displacement u = (u_x, u_y) and hydrostatic pressure p.
The problem is solved under plane strain conditions.
The material properties are Young's modulus E = 5 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.49.
The left edge (x = 0) is fixed with u_x = 0 and u_y = 0.
The right edge (x = 1.0) has a prescribed displacement of u_x = 0.060 m and u_y = 0.
The hole boundaries have a follower pressure load of 0.10 MPa applied.
The top and bottom edges are traction-free.
A colour map of the hydrostatic pressure p must be saved as q14_p.png.
A colour map of the von Mises stress, derived from the Cauchy stress, must be computed and saved as q14_vm.png.
The displacement field must be saved in XDMF format.
A mixed displacement-pressure formulation consistent with quasi-incompressibility in plane strain must be used.
Displacement fields will be approximated using quadratic Lagrange finite elements (P2).
Pressure fields will be approximated using linear Lagrange finite elements (P1).
The computational mesh will have a characteristic element size of approximately 0.01 m, with local refinement around the circular holes.
