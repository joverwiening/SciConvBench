The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve a small-strain linear elasticity problem.
The problem will be formulated as a 2D plane-stress problem with a vector-valued displacement space.
A direct linear solver will be used.
The geometry is a rectangular plate with dimensions 1 meter in x and 0.5 meters in y.
A circular hole with a radius of 0.1 meters is centered at (0.5, 0.25) meters.
Material properties are Young's Modulus E = 70e9 Pa, Poisson's Ratio ν = 0.33, and density ρ = 2700 kg/m^3.
Gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s^2 acts in the negative y-direction.
The left edge of the plate (x=0) is clamped, meaning all displacement components are zero.
An internal pressure of 1e5 Pa is applied normally outwards on the boundary of the circular hole.
The mesh resolution will be approximately 64 elements along the 1-meter edge and 32 elements along the 0.5-meter edge.
The reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be applied, assuming unit thickness for the 2D domain.
The simulation will calculate and visualize displacement patterns.
The simulation will calculate and visualize stress patterns.
Von Mises stress will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field and visualized.
