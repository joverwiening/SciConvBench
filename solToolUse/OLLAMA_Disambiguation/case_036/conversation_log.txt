Q: Which edges of the parallelogram correspond to the left slope where the horizontal traction σₓₓ = 6000 Pa is applied, and to the right slope that should be fixed?
A: - **Left slope (horizontal traction σₓₓ = 6000 Pa):** the inclined edge that runs from the lower‑left vertex (0, 0) to the upper‑left vertex (0.2, 0.3).

- **Right slope (fixed edge):** the inclined edge that runs from the lower‑right vertex (1, 0) to the upper‑right vertex (1.2, 0.3).
