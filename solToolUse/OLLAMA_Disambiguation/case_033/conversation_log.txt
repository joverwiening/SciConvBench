Q: How many divisions along the x‑ and y‑directions should be used to create the triangular mesh (approximately 64 elements) for the right‑triangle domain?
A: Make a reasonable assumption based on best practices: use 8 divisions in the x‑direction and 8 divisions in the y‑direction (an 8 × 8 structured grid, then triangulated and trimmed to the right‑triangle) – this yields roughly 64 triangular elements.
