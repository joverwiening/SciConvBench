Generate a mesh with approximately 200 cells in the x‑direction and 40 cells in the y‑direction (global cell size ≈ 5 mm) and locally refine the mesh around the hole so that cells in a band of a few radii around the hole have a size of about 1 mm.  
Define a mixed displacement–pressure finite‑element space suitable for the nearly incompressible formulation (e.g., Taylor‑Hood: vector P2 for displacement and scalar P1 for pressure).  
Set material parameters: Young’s modulus E = 5 MPa, Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.49, and compute the Lamé parameters for plane strain.  
Apply Dirichlet boundary conditions: on the left edge (x = 0) enforce uₓ = 0, u_y = 0; on the right edge (x = 1.0) enforce uₓ = 0.001 m, u_y = 0. All other boundaries, including the top, bottom, and hole surface, are traction‑free.  
Formulate the weak form of the mixed displacement‑pressure elasticity problem and solve the resulting saddle‑point linear system with a direct solver (e.g., PETSc MUMPS).  
Compute the Cauchy stress tensor from the displacement solution, then evaluate the von Mises stress field.
