Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve the incompressible finite‑strain hyperelasticity problem with a mixed displacement‑pressure formulation and Newton’s method.  
Define the domain Ω = (0, 1.0) m × (0, 0.20) m with a circular hole of radius a = 0.04 m centred at (0.50, 0.10).  
Model the material as a plane‑strain, isotropic, incompressible neo‑Hookean solid with Young’s modulus E = 5 MPa and Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.5.  
Apply displacement boundary conditions: u_x = 0 and u_y = 0 on the left edge (x = 0).  
Apply a follower pressure of magnitude 0.10 MPa on the hole boundary.  
Leave the right, top, and bottom edges traction‑free.  
Formulate the weak form using the mixed displacement‑pressure incompressible neo‑Hookean formulation and solve the nonlinear system with Newton iterations.  
After convergence, compute the Cauchy stress tensor, then evaluate the von Mises stress field from it.
