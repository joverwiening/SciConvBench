Solver: incompressible finite-strain hyperelasticity in legacy FEniCS (dolfin); solve the mixed displacement-pressure neo-Hookean problem with Newton's method.
Task:
Solve Solid Mechanics Problem 13 from the paper: an incompressible neo-Hookean perforated strip with follower pressure on the hole boundary.
Case details:
Geometry:
Let Ω = (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) m be a rectangular strip with a circular hole of radius a = 0.04 m centred at (0.50, 0.10).
Model:
Large-deformation isotropic incompressible neo-Hookean solid with unknowns displacement u = (u_x, u_y) and hydrostatic pressure p.
Plane strain.
Material:
Young's modulus E = 5 MPa, Poisson's ratio ν = 0.5.
Boundary conditions and loads:
Left edge (x = 0): fixed, u_x = 0, u_y = 0.
Hole boundary: uniform follower pressure P_hole = 0.10 MPa applied as traction sigma*n = -P_hole*n in the deformed configuration.
Right, top, and bottom edges: traction-free.
Outputs:
Save a magnified (x5) deformed plot as q13_def.png.
Compute the von Mises field from the Cauchy stress and save q13_vm.png.
Save the displacement field in XDMF format.
Implementation notes:
Apply the hole load as follower pressure using the current normal of the deformed configuration, not as a dead load with the reference normal.
Use the mixed displacement-pressure incompressible neo-Hookean formulation and Newton iterations.
