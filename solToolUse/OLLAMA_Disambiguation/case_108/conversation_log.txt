## General rule for a varying amplitude in Abaqus

1. **Define the amplitude** (tabular, periodic, etc.)  

   ```abaqus
   *Amplitude, name=MyAmp, definition=Tabular
   0., 0.
   1., 1.
   2., 2.
   …
   ```

2. **Create the load (or boundary) and reference the amplitude**  

   - Nodal force:  

     ```abaqus
     *Cload, nset=LOAD, amplitude=MyAmp
     5, 1., 0., 0.
     ```
   - Surface pressure:  

     ```abaqus
     *Dload, elset=EALL, amplitude=MyAmp
     P, 1.0                ** pressure = 1.0 × MyAmp(t)
     ```
   - Prescribed displacement:  

     ```abaqus
     *Boundary, nset=FIX, amplitude=MyAmp
     1, 1, 1, 0.0          ** Ux = 0.0 × MyAmp(t)  (example)
     ```

3. **Define the step** – the time‑period belongs to the *STATIC* (or *DYNAMIC*) line, **not** to the `*STEP` line.  

   ```abaqus
   *Step, nlgeom=YES
   *Static
   0., 3., 1e-05, 1.
   ```

4. **Run the analysis** – Abaqus will multiply the load magnitude by the amplitude value at each increment of the step.
