Q: Which segment of the top edge (y = 1) should have the horizontal shear traction σₓy = 2.65 × 10⁴ Pa applied? (e.g., the interval of x‑coordinates where it is applied)
A: Apply the horizontal shear traction σₓy = 2.65 × 10⁴ Pa on the top‑right segment of the edge y = 1, i.e. for x > 0.5 (the interval 0.5 < x ≤ 1).
