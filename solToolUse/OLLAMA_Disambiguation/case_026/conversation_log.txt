Q: What is the exact mathematical expression (amplitude and wavelength) for the sinusoidal vertical traction applied on the hypotenuse (the line x + 2y = 1)?
A: The vertical traction on the hypotenuse \(x+2y=1\) is  

\[
t_y(x)= -5\times10^{3}\,\sin(\pi x)\;\text{Pa}.
\]

* **Amplitude:** \(5\,\text{kPa}\) (the magnitude of the sinusoid).  
* **Wavelength:** The sine term \(\sin(\pi x)\) has a spatial period \(L = \frac{2\pi}{\pi}=2\) (in the global \(x\)-direction).
