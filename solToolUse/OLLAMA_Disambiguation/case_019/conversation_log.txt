Q: What is the spatial variation function for the Lamé parameter λ (e.g., λ as a function of coordinates)?
A: The Lamé parameter varies linearly with the reference coordinate x₁:

\[
\lambda(x_1) = 5.8\,x_1 \;+\; 5.7\,(1 - x_1)
\]

which simplifies to \(\lambda(x_1) = 0.1\,x_1 + 5.7\).

Q: Around which axis should the face x₁ = 1 be rotated by 60 degrees?
A: The face \(x_1 = 1\) should be rotated by 60° about the cube’s centreline—that is, about the \(x_1\)‑axis passing through the centre of the cube.
