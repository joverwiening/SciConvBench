Solver: incompressible finite-strain hyperelasticity in legacy FEniCS (dolfin); solve the mixed displacement-pressure neo-Hookean problem with Newton's method.
Task:
Solve Solid Mechanics Problem 12 from the paper: an incompressible neo-Hookean perforated strip in plane strain under prescribed displacement.
Case details:
Geometry:
Let Ω = (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) m be a rectangular strip with a circular hole of radius a = 0.04 m centred at (0.50, 0.10).
Model:
Large-deformation isotropic incompressible neo-Hookean solid with unknowns displacement u = (u_x, u_y) and hydrostatic pressure p.
Plane strain.
Material:
Young's modulus E = 5 MPa, Poisson's ratio ν = 0.5.
Use the standard incompressible neo-Hookean strain energy with a pressure field p imposing J = 1 in a mixed (u, p) formulation.
Boundary conditions:
Left edge (x = 0): fixed, u_x = 0, u_y = 0.
Right edge (x = 1.0): prescribed displacement u_x = 0.060 m, u_y = 0.
Top, bottom, and the hole boundary: traction-free.
Outputs:
Save a colour map of p as q12_p.png.
Compute and save a colour map of von Mises stress from the Cauchy stress as q12_vm.png.
Save the displacement field in XDMF format.
Implementation notes:
Use F = I + grad(u), J = det(F), and a mixed displacement-pressure formulation; do not eliminate pressure by a nearly incompressible displacement-only approximation.
Compute von Mises from the Cauchy stress, not from a Piola stress tensor.
