Q: What is the exact linear expression (including magnitude and direction) for the vertical traction applied on the right edge (x = 1) as a function of the vertical coordinate y?
A: The vertical traction on the right edge \(x=1\) is  

\[
\mathbf{t}(y)=\bigl(0,\; -2.65\times10^{4}\; y\bigr)\;\text{Pa},
\]

i.e. a purely vertical (downward) traction whose magnitude varies linearly with the coordinate \(y\) as \(-2.65\times10^{4}\,y\) Pa.
