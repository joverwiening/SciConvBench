`*EXPANSION` tells Abaqus how the material length changes with temperature.  
For every expansion definition Abaqus must know the temperature at which the
coefficient of thermal expansion (CTE) was measured – the **reference
temperature**.

miss‑spelled) Abaqus cannot find that temperature, assumes a default value of

```

implicitly (0 °C).  It becomes an *error* when the material also contains a
plasticity definition (`*PLASTIC`) **and** no linear‑elastic part (`*ELASTIC`)
is present – a material that can yield must first be defined as elastic.
