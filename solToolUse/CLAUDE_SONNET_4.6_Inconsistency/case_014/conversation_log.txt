Q: I notice a contradiction in the specification: it first says to replace the point loads with a uniform pressure patch over the upper quarter of the arc, then immediately says to apply the two symmetric point loads at the stated arc points. Which loading condition should be used?
A: Use PointSource to apply the two symmetric point loads at (±0.707, 0.707), not a pressure patch.
