Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem on a semi-circular domain.

The domain is a half-disk of radius 1 m (the flat edge lies along y=0, the curved arc is the upper half).

Use a mesh resolution of 64 elements (mshr Circle/Rectangle or equivalent, refined to approximately 64 divisions).

Material properties are: Young's modulus E = 70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio nu = 0.33, density rho = 2700 kg/m^3, gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s^2.

Use the plane-stress reduced constitutive law with unit thickness.

The plane-stress constitutive tensor relates in-plane strains to in-plane stresses using the standard plane-stress reduction: sigma_xx = E/(1-nu^2)*(eps_xx + nu*eps_yy), sigma_yy = E/(1-nu^2)*(eps_yy + nu*eps_xx), sigma_xy = E/(1+nu)*eps_xy.

Define a vector-valued continuous Lagrange finite element space (degree 2) for the displacement field on the half-disk mesh.

Apply Dirichlet boundary conditions fixing both displacement components (ux=0, uy=0) at the two base corner points (-1, 0) and (1, 0) using a PointBC or SubDomain that selects vertices within a small tolerance of those coordinates.

Apply a body force corresponding to gravity in the negative y-direction: f = (0, -rho*g) distributed over the entire domain.

Apply two concentrated point loads using FEniCS PointSource: a force of (0, -3000) N at (0.707, 0.707) and a force of (0, -3000) N at (-0.707, 0.707).

Assemble the stiffness matrix and load vector from the standard linear elasticity variational form: a(u,v) = integral of sigma(u):epsilon(v) dx, L(v) = integral of f·v dx plus the PointSource contributions.

Solve the assembled linear system using a direct linear solver (e.g., LU via dolfin's default direct solver or PETSc LU).

After solving, compute the in-plane Cauchy stress tensor components sigma_xx, sigma_yy, sigma_xy by projecting the stress expression onto a suitable function space (e.g., DG0 or CG1 tensor space).

Compute the von Mises stress from the in-plane stress components using: von_mises = sqrt(sigma_xx^2 - sigma_xx*sigma_yy + sigma_yy^2 + 3*sigma_xy^2).

Output or save the displacement field and the von Mises stress field (e.g., to XDMF or VTK files, or print the maximum values).

Print the maximum displacement magnitude and the maximum von Mises stress value to the console.
