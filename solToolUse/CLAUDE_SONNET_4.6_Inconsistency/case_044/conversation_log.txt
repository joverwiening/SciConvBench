Q: I need to clarify one potential contradiction in the specification before finalizing.

The specification states the top edge is horizontal, and then says "apply the compressive load as a horizontal traction along the edge rather than as a vertical normal traction." However, for a horizontal edge, the inward normal direction is vertical (pointing downward into the domain), not horizontal. A horizontal traction would be tangential to a horizontal edge, not a normal traction.

Which interpretation is correct for the top edge load: apply the traction in the vertical direction (true inward normal to the horizontal top edge, i.e., downward, T = (0, -5000) Pa), or apply it in the horizontal direction (tangential to the top edge, T = (-5000, 0) or (5000, 0) Pa)?
A: Apply the traction in the true inward normal direction to the horizontal top edge — that is, vertically downward: T = (0, -5000) Pa.
