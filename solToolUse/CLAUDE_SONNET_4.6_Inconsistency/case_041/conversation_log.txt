Q: I notice a contradiction in the problem specification. The stopping rule is stated in two different ways with two different quantities:

First, it says "stop advancing if E_max (maximum principal Green-Lagrange strain) exceeds 0.03."

Then, it says "Monitor the stopping rule with the von Mises Cauchy stress and stop when it exceeds 0.03."

Which quantity and threshold should actually govern the load-stepping stopping rule: the maximum principal Green-Lagrange strain E_max exceeding 0.03, or the von Mises Cauchy stress exceeding a certain value?
A: Use the maximum principal Green-Lagrange strain E_max exceeding 0.03 as the stopping criterion. Also, use the Green-Lagrange strain in the Saint-Venant-Kirchhoff constitutive model, not the infinitesimal strain.
