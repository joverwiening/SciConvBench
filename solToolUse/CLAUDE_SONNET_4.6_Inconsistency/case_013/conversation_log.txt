Q: I notice a contradiction in the problem specification regarding the Young's modulus variation.

In the geometry and material section, the modulus is defined as E(y) = 100 GPa + 100 GPa*(y/0.20), varying with height y over the plate's height of 0.20 m. However, in the final line, it says to evaluate the graded modulus as E(x) = 100 GPa + 100 GPa*(x/1.0), varying with x over the plate's length of 1.0 m.

Which spatial variation should be used for Young's modulus: E varying with y (the height direction) or E varying with x (the length direction)?
A: E should vary with y, not x. Use E(y) = 100 GPa + 100 GPa*(y/0.20).
