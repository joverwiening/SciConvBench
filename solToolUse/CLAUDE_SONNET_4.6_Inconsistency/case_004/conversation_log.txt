Q: I need to clarify a contradiction in the boundary conditions before writing the final specification.

The task states two conflicting rules:

Rule 1: "Apply the varying traction only on the outer right edge x = 1; the re-entrant inner boundaries are traction-free."

Rule 2: "Apply the linearly varying vertical traction on every boundary segment whose outward normal has a positive x-component, including the vertical face of the cutout."

Which rule should take precedence — traction applied ONLY on the outer right edge x = 1 (with the inner cutout vertical face left traction-free), or traction applied on ALL faces whose outward normal has a positive x-component (including the inner cutout vertical face at x = 0.5)?
A: Apply the traction **only on the outer right edge x = 1**; the re-entrant inner boundaries are traction-free. Rule 1 takes precedence.
