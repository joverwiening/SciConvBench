Q: The load expression for the right edge is written as σ_yy = -6000·sin(4πy), which uses the vertical coordinate y, but the final instruction says to evaluate the sine factor with the coordinate x on the boundary x = 1. These two directives contradict each other.

To confirm: should the sinusoidal traction on the right edge be implemented as -6000·sin(4π·x[1]) (using the vertical/y coordinate, consistent with the mathematical expression), or as -6000·sin(4π·x[0]) (using the horizontal/x coordinate, as stated in the final instruction)?
A: Use -6000·sin(4π·x[1]), i.e., the vertical coordinate y.
