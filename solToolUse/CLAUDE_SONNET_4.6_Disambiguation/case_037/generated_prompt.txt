Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) for the entire implementation.

The domain is a 2D rectangular plate of width 1.0 m and height 0.5 m, with a circular hole of radius 0.1 m centered at (0.5, 0.25).

Mesh the domain using the mshr module with a resolution of 64 elements per edge (mshr resolution=64).

Use plane-stress formulation with unit thickness (t=1.0 m).

Material properties are: Young's modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, density ρ=2700 kg/m^3, gravitational acceleration g=9.81 m/s^2.

Define the plane-stress constitutive law: λ_eff = E*ν/(1-ν^2), μ = E/(2*(1+ν)), and stress σ(u) = λ_eff*tr(ε)*I + 2*μ*ε where ε = sym(grad(u)).

Use a vector-valued continuous Lagrange finite element space of degree 2 (VectorFunctionSpace, "CG", 2) for the displacement field.

Apply a homogeneous Dirichlet boundary condition (zero displacement) on the entire left boundary (x=0).

Apply a body force f = (0, -ρ*g) uniformly over the entire domain to represent gravity.

The torque traction is applied on a small arc of the outer plate boundary near the point (1.0, 0.25).

Define the torque arc as the portion of the outer boundary where the distance from the point (1.0, 0.25) is less than or equal to r=0.02 m.

Use an angular range of ±90 degrees (π/2 radians) centered on the angle pointing toward (1.0, 0.25) from the plate interior as the reasonable best-practice assumption for the arc extent; since the arc is on the right edge near (1.0, 0.25), identify arc points as those on the outer boundary satisfying sqrt((x-1.0)^2 + (y-0.25)^2) <= 0.02.

Convert the torque M=800 Nm to a tangential traction magnitude using t_θ = M / (2*π*r) where r=0.02 m.

The tangential traction direction at each point on the arc is the unit tangent vector perpendicular to the radial direction from (1.0, 0.25), oriented to produce a counterclockwise (positive) torque: t_vec = (-sin(θ), cos(θ)) where θ = atan2(y-0.25, x-1.0).

Implement the traction as a Neumann boundary condition by marking the arc subdomain with a MeshFunction and integrating t_vec * t_θ over ds(arc_marker) in the weak form.

The weak form is: a(u,v) = ∫ σ(u):ε(v) dx, L(v) = ∫ f·v dx + ∫_arc t_θ * t_vec · v ds.

Solve the linear system using a direct solver (LUSolver or the default direct backend via solve(a==L, u, bc)).

After solving, compute the full Cauchy stress tensor σ by projecting σ(u) onto a tensor function space (TensorFunctionSpace, "DG", 0).

Compute von Mises stress as: σ_vm = sqrt(σ_xx^2 - σ_xx*σ_yy + σ_yy^2 + 3*σ_xy^2) using the in-plane stress components only, consistent with plane-stress.

Output and save the displacement field u, the von Mises stress field, and the full stress tensor field to VTK or XDMF files for visualization.

Print the maximum displacement magnitude and the maximum von Mises stress value to the console.
