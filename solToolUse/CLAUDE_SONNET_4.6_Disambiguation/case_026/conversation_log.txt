Q: What is the exact mathematical form of the sinusoidal vertical traction on the hypotenuse — specifically, what is the amplitude, what spatial variable or expression is the argument of the sine function, and what are the units?
A: The traction is σ_yy = -5e3 * sin(π * x) Pa, where x is the global x-coordinate.
