Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) for the entire implementation.
Solve a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem on a semi-circular (half-disk) domain with radius 1 m (y >= 0).
Use material properties: Young's modulus E = 70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio nu = 0.33, density rho = 2700 kg/m^3, gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s^2.
Generate the mesh using mshr with a half-disk geometry centered at the origin with radius 1 m, using a mesh resolution of 64.
Define a vector-valued continuous Lagrange finite element space of degree 2 (P2) for the displacement field on this mesh.
Apply the reduced plane-stress constitutive law: sigma = E/(1-nu^2) * ((1-nu)*eps + nu*tr(eps)*I) where eps is the symmetric gradient of displacement and I is the 2x2 identity.
Assume unit thickness (t = 1 m) for the plane-stress formulation.
Apply a body force due to gravity: f = (0, -rho * g) as a Constant body force vector in the variational form.
Apply a concentrated vertical point load of -5000 N (downward) at the apex point (0, 1) using a PointSource object targeting the y-component of the load vector, applied to the assembled linear form.
Use a geometric tolerance of 1e-10 m when identifying the apex node at (0, 1) for the PointSource application, which is standard best practice in dolfin.
Define the Dirichlet boundary condition as zero displacement (u = (0, 0)) on the flat base where y = 0, identified using a tolerance of 1e-14.
Formulate the standard bilinear form a(u, v) = integral of inner(sigma(u), sym(grad(v))) dx and linear form L(v) = inner(f, v) dx.
Assemble the system matrix and right-hand side vector, then apply the PointSource to the right-hand side before applying Dirichlet boundary conditions.
Solve the resulting linear system using a direct solver (LU factorization) via dolfin's LUSolver or the default direct solve.
After solving, compute the von Mises stress from the in-plane Cauchy stress components: von_mises = sqrt(s11^2 - s11*s22 + s22^2 + 3*s12^2) where sij are the in-plane stress tensor components projected onto a scalar DG0 or CG1 function space.
Project the von Mises stress expression onto a scalar function space for visualization and output.
Save the displacement field and von Mises stress field to separate XDMF or VTK files named displacement.pvd and von_mises.pvd respectively for post-processing in ParaView.
Print the maximum von Mises stress value and the maximum displacement magnitude to the console.
