Q: What is the prescribed rotational displacement field to be imposed on the x = 1 face (e.g., the rotation angle, axis of rotation, and how the displacement is computed from that rotation)?
A: The rotation is 60 degrees about the x-axis (i.e., the axis pointing in the x-direction) through the face centre (y0, z0) = (0.5, 0.5). The displacement is computed as the difference between the rotated and original positions of each point on the face, then scaled by 0.5.

Concretely, for a point (y, z) on the face x = 1:
- dy = (y - y0)*cos(60°) - (z - z0)*sin(60°) + y0 - y
- dz = (y - y0)*sin(60°) + (z - z0)*cos(60°) + z0 - z
- dx = 0

Then the prescribed displacement is (0, 0.5*dy, 0.5*dz).
