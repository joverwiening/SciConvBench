Q: I have one clarifying question before finalizing the specification.

The top boundary of the stepped polygon consists of two horizontal segments: y=0.5 from x=0.3 to x=0.5, and y=0.2 from x=0 to x=0.3 and from x=0.5 to x=0.8. Which segment (or segments) does the linearly varying vertical traction act on, and what is the spatial variation of that traction (e.g., t_y = some function of x, with what start and end values)?
A: The traction acts only on the segment where y=0.5, 0.3≤x≤0.5. The vertical traction is σ_yy(x) = -8000x Pa, varying linearly with x.
