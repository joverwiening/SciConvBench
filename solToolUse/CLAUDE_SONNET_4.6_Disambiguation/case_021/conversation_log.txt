Q: What are the exact magnitudes and mathematical expressions for the sinusoidal vertical traction on the right edge and the constant shear traction on the top edge (e.g., t_y = A·sin(π·y/H) on x=1, and t_x = τ₀ on y=0.5)?
A: On the right edge (x=1): σ_yy = -6000·sin(4πy) Pa (vertical traction)
On the top edge (y=0.5): τ_xy = 1000 Pa (constant shear traction)
