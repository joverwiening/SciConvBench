Q: What is the spatial variation of Young's modulus across the plate — for example, does it vary along x, along y, or both, and what is the functional form (e.g., exponential, power-law) and the values at the boundaries?
A: Young's modulus varies with height (y only): E(y) = 100 GPa + 100 GPa*(y/0.20), for y in [0, 0.20] m. So it's linear, going from 100 GPa at y=0 to 200 GPa at y=0.20.
