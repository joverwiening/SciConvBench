Q: What is the exact traction formula on the right edge (x=1) — for example, is it t = (0, t₀·y) with a specific value of t₀, or a different linear function of y (or x)?
A: The traction on the right edge (x=1) is a vertical traction σ_yy = -2.65e4 * y Pa, so t = (0, -2.65e4 * y).
