Q: What is the magnitude and direction of the pressure load on the arc — specifically, what is the pressure value in Pascals, and should it act inward (compressive, pushing toward the center) or outward (tensile, pulling away from the center)?
A: Inward pressure, σ_n = -20 kPa (i.e., 20,000 Pa compressive, pushing toward the center). Apply as t = -p*n where p = 20000 Pa and n is the outward normal.
