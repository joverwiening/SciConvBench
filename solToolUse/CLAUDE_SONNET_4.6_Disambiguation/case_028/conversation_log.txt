Q: What should the exact location of the loaded portion on y=1 be — specifically, what is the x-range where the shear traction σ_xy=2.65e4 Pa is applied (e.g., from x=0 to x=0.5, from x=0.5 to x=1, or the full x=0 to x=1)?
A: The shear traction is applied on y=1 where x > 0.5, so the segment from x=0.5 to x=1.
