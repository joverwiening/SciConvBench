Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
Solve a small-strain linear elasticity problem in 2D under plane stress conditions.
The domain is a 1 m by 0.5 m rectangle with a circular hole of radius 0.1 m centered at (0.5, 0.25).
Use a unit thickness of 1 m for the plane stress formulation.
The material properties are Young's modulus E = 70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν = 0.33, density ρ = 2700 kg/m³, and gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Apply a body force representing gravity in the negative y-direction.
Fix all degrees of freedom on the left edge (x = 0).
Apply a uniform traction on the right edge (x = 1, y from 0 to 0.5) such that the total resultant force is 5 kN in the negative y-direction.
Leave the boundary of the circular hole traction-free.
Use a mesh of 72 triangular elements.
Use a vector-valued continuous Lagrange finite element space of polynomial degree 1 for the displacement.
Use the direct linear solver (MUMPS/LU) via the 'solve' command with default parameters.
Compute the displacement field solution.
Compute the in-plane Cauchy stress tensor from the displacement field.
Compute the von Mises stress field from the in-plane Cauchy stress components.
Output or visualize the displacement and von Mises stress distributions.
