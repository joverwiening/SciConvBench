Q: What is the exact shear loading pattern you want to apply?
A: The ±1000 Pa values are normal tractions on the top and bottom edges. The top edge has an inward normal traction of -1000 Pa (compressive), and the bottom edge has an outward normal traction of +1000 Pa (tensile). This creates a shear pattern. Gravity acts as a body force in the negative y-direction.
