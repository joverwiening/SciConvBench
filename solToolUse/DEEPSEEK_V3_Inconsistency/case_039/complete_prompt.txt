Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
Solver: small-strain linear elasticity in legacy FEniCS (dolfin); solve the 2D plane-stress variational problem with a vector-valued displacement space and a direct linear solver.
Task:
Simulate stress in a perforated rectangular plate with asymmetric constraints: vertical fixation on left edge (uy=0), horizontal fixation on right edge (ux=0), and uniform vertical compression on top/bottom. Use linear elasticity with E=70e9 Pa, ν=0.33. Analyze displacement and stress patterns under combined constraints and surface loading.
Case details:
Material: E=70e9, ν=0.33, ρ=2700 kg/m^3, g=9.81 m/s^2. Geometry: 1x0.5 plate with 0.1m radius hole at (0.5,0.25). Loads: σ_yy=-2500 Pa (top+bottom). Constraints: left (uy=0), right (ux=0). Mesh: 64 elements/edge.
Implementation notes:
Treat the 2D domain as plane stress with unit thickness and use the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
Compute von Mises from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
Leave the hole boundary traction-free unless a pressure load is explicitly prescribed.
Preserve the asymmetric support: u_y = 0 on the left edge and u_x = 0 on the right edge.
