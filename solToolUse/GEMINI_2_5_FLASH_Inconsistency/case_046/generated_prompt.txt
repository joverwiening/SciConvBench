The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve a 2D plane-stress small-strain linear elasticity problem.
The displacement field will be represented using quadratic (P2) vector-valued finite elements.
A direct linear solver will be used.
The material properties are Young's Modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, density ρ=2700 kg/m^3, and gravitational acceleration g=9.81 m/s^2.
The geometry is a 1 meter by 0.5 meter rectangular plate with a central circular hole of 0.1 meter radius centered at (0.5, 0.25) meters.
The 2D domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness, and the reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be applied.
Gravity loading will be applied in the negative y-direction.
A total downward load of 5 kN will be applied as a uniform traction distribution on the entire right edge (x=1).
The left edge (x=0) will have all displacement components fixed to zero.
The boundary of the circular hole will be traction-free.
All other exterior edges not explicitly loaded or fixed will be traction-free.
The mesh will consist of 72 elements.
The simulation will compute and output the displacement distribution.
The simulation will compute and output the von Mises stress distribution, derived from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
