The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is a 2D plane-strain small-strain linear elasticity problem.
The domain Ω is a rectangular strip (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) m.
A structured mesh of 100 x 20 elements will be used over Ω.
The material properties are Young's modulus E = 5 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.49.
The formulation will use a displacement-only vector element of polynomial degree 2.
The left edge (x = 0) is fixed with u_x = 0 and u_y = 0.
The right edge (x = 1.0) has a prescribed displacement of u_x = 0.03 m and u_y = 0.
The top and bottom edges are traction-free.
The linear system will be solved using a direct solver.
A colour map of the magnitude of the displacement field |u| will be saved as q10_disp.png.
The displacement field will be saved in XDMF format.
