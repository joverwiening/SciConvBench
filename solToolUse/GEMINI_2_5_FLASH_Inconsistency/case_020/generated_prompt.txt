The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The solver will implement small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane-stress.
The displacement space will be vector-valued.
A direct linear solver will be used.
The task is to solve Solid Mechanics Problem 2 from the paper, which is a rectangular plate in plane stress loaded by a uniform top-edge traction.
The geometry is a rectangular plate Ω = (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) m.
The mesh will be a uniform structured mesh with 40 subdivisions in x and 8 subdivisions in y.
The model is plane-stress linear elasticity for displacement u = (u_x, u_y) in Ω.
The material properties are Young's modulus E = 200 GPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.30.
The left edge (x = 0) has fixed boundary conditions, with u_x = 0 and u_y = 0.
The top edge (y = 0.20) will have a traction derived from a 3D pressure of 2000 Pa acting on the top face.
Given the unit thickness for the 2D plane-stress strip, this corresponds to a 2D traction t = (0, -2000) N/m.
The right edge (x = 1.0) and bottom edge (y = 0) are traction-free.
The 2D strip will be treated as plane stress with unit thickness, and the reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be used.
A colour map of the u_y displacement component will be saved as q2_uy.png.
The resulting displacement field will be saved in XDMF format.
