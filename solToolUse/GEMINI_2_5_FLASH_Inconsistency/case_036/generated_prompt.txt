The simulation will be implemented using FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is a 2D small-strain linear elasticity plane-stress variational problem.
The material properties are Young's Modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, density ρ=2700 kg/m^3, and gravitational acceleration g=9.81 m/s^2.
The domain is a parallelogram with vertices at (0,0), (1,0), (1.2,0.3), and (0.2,0.3).
The domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness, using the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
The mesh will consist of 72 quadrilateral elements.
The right inclined edge (connecting (1,0) to (1.2,0.3)) is fixed, meaning all displacement components are zero.
A body force due to gravity acts in the negative y-direction.
A tangential shear traction with a magnitude of 6000 Pa is applied along the left inclined slope (connecting (0,0) to (0.2,0.3)).
The problem will be solved using a vector-valued displacement space and a direct linear solver.
The output will include the displacement field and the von Mises stress distribution, computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
