Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) for the simulation.
Solve Solid Mechanics Problem 15 from the paper: a three-hole strip under finite-strain Saint-Venant-Kirchhoff elasticity.
The solver uses load stepping and Newton iterations in plane strain.
The domain Ω is a rectangular strip (0, 1.20) x (0, 0.20) m.
The domain contains three circular holes of radius a = 0.03 m.
The holes are centered on the midline y = 0.10 m at x = 0.30 m, 0.60 m, and 0.90 m.
The model is finite-strain Saint-Venant-Kirchhoff elasticity in plane strain.
The unknown is the displacement u = (u_x, u_y).
The deformation gradient F is defined as I + grad(u).
The Green-Lagrange strain tensor E_GL is defined as 0.5*(F^T*F - I).
The second Piola-Kirchhoff stress tensor S is defined as lambda*tr(E_GL)*I + 2*mu*E_GL.
The Jacobian J is defined as det(F).
The Cauchy stress sigma is defined as (1/J)*F*S*F^T.
The Saint-Venant-Kirchhoff constitutive update uses the Green-Lagrange strain tensor E_GL.
The Lamé parameter lambda is 5.769 MPa.
The Lamé parameter mu is 3.846 MPa.
The left edge (x = 0) is fixed with u_x = 0 and u_y = 0.
The right edge (x = 1.20) has a prescribed displacement of u_x = 0.012 m and u_y = 0.
The top boundary (y = 0.20), bottom boundary (y = 0), and all hole boundaries are traction-free.
Apply the total prescribed displacement over 20 load steps.
Stop advancing if the maximum principal Green-Lagrange strain E_max exceeds 0.03.
