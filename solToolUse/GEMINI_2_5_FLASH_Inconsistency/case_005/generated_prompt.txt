The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The solver will implement small-strain linear elasticity.
The problem will be a 2D plane-stress variational problem.
A vector-valued displacement space will be used.
A direct linear solver will be employed.
The material properties are E=70e9 Pa, ν=0.33, ρ=2700 kg/m^3.
The acceleration due to gravity is g=9.81 m/s^2.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction.
The geometry is a 1m x 0.5m rectangular plate.
A circular hole with a 0.1m radius is located at (0.5,0.25).
A leftward point load of -3000N (x-component) is applied at (1,0.2).
A rightward point load of +3000N (x-component) is applied at (1,0.3).
These point loads are represented by PointSource terms.
The left edge of the plate is clamped, constraining displacement in both the x and y directions.
The hole boundary is traction-free.
The mesh will have 64 elements along the 1m outer edge.
The mesh will have 64 elements along the 0.5m outer edge.
The 2D domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness.
The reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be used.
Displacement patterns will be computed.
Von Mises stress patterns will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
