The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The solver will implement small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane stress.
The problem will use a vector-valued displacement space and a direct linear solver.
The geometry is a rectangle with dimensions 4.0 by 1.0.
A circular hole of radius 0.3 is centered at (1.0, 0.5) within the rectangle.
The mesh will be generated with a resolution parameter of 72.
The function space will be second-order vector Lagrange.
The material properties are Young's modulus E = 210e3 and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.3.
The constitutive law will be the reduced plane-stress formulation.
The left edge (x = 0) will have a clamped boundary condition (u = 0).
A uniform downward traction t = (0, -1e5) per unit thickness will be applied as a Neumann boundary condition on the right edge (x = 4.0).
The hole boundary will be traction-free.
There will be no body forces.
The simulation will compute the displacement field.
The simulation will compute the von Mises stress field.
