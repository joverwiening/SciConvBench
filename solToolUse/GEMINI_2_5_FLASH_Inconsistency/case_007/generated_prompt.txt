The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is a 2D plane-stress small-strain linear elasticity variational problem.
A vector-valued displacement space will be used.
A direct linear solver will be used.
The material properties are Young's Modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, density ρ=2700 kg/m^3, and gravitational acceleration g=9.81 m/s^2.
The 2D domain is a stepped polygon defined by 8 vertices in counter-clockwise order: (0.0, 0.0), (1.0, 0.0), (1.0, 0.5), (0.5, 0.5), (0.5, 1.0), (0.3, 1.0), (0.3, 0.5), (0.0, 0.5).
The domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness, and the reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be used.
A horizontal traction of σ_xx=5000 Pa is applied to the top edge segment where 0.3≤x≤0.5.
Gravity acts as a body force in the negative y-direction.
The boundary at y=0 is fixed, meaning both u_x=0 and u_y=0 are imposed.
On the vertical stem faces at x=0.3 and x=0.5, a zero normal displacement u_y=0 is imposed.
The mesh will consist of 72 elements.
The simulation will compute displacement and von Mises stress distributions.
Von Mises stress will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
