The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane stress.
The domain is a right triangle with vertices at (0,0), (1,0), and (0,0.5).
The material properties are Young's Modulus E = 70e9 Pa, Poisson's Ratio ν = 0.33, density ρ = 2700 kg/m^3, and gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s^2.
The constitutive law for plane stress with unit thickness will be used.
The mesh will be generated with a resolution corresponding to 64 divisions along the hypotenuse.
The left edge (x=0) of the domain is clamped, meaning displacement is fixed to zero (Dirichlet boundary condition).
A body force due to gravity acts in the negative y-direction, with magnitude ρg.
A sinusoidal vertical traction is applied to the hypotenuse (x + 2y = 1).
The traction is defined as a surface stress component σ_yy = -5e3 * sin(πx) Pa, acting in the negative y-direction.
The variational problem will use a vector-valued displacement space.
A direct linear solver will be used.
The simulation will compute the displacement field.
The simulation will compute the von Mises stress distribution from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
