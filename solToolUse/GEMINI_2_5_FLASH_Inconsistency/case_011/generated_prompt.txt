The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The solver will implement small-strain linear elasticity.
The problem will be solved as a 2D plane-stress variational problem.
A vector-valued displacement space will be used.
A direct linear solver will be employed.
The geometry is a rectangular plate of dimensions 1 meter by 0.5 meters.
The plate contains a circular hole of 0.1 meter radius centered at (0.5, 0.25) meters.
The material properties are Young's Modulus E = 70e9 Pa, Poisson's Ratio ν = 0.33, and density ρ = 2700 kg/m^3.
Gravity loading will be included with g = 9.81 m/s^2 acting in the negative y-direction.
The left edge of the plate (x=0) is clamped, meaning displacement is fixed to zero.
An internal pressure of 1e5 Pa is applied on the boundary of the hole.
The pressure acts inward towards the hole's center, defined by a force vector t = -p*n where n is the outward normal of the solid.
The mesh will be generated using `mshr.generate_mesh` with a resolution parameter of 64.
The 2D domain will be treated as plane stress with unit thickness.
The reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be used.
The simulation will calculate displacement and stress patterns.
Von Mises stress will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
