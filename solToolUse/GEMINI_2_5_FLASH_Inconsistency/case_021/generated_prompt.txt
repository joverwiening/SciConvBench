The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is a small-strain linear elasticity 2D plane-stress variational problem.
A vector-valued displacement space will be used.
A direct linear solver will be employed.
The material properties are Young's Modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, density ρ=2700 kg/m^3, and gravitational acceleration g=9.81 m/s^2.
The geometry is a rectangular plate of dimensions 1m by 0.5m.
A circular hole with a radius of 0.1m is located at the center (0.5, 0.25) of the plate.
The left boundary (x=0) is fixed, meaning displacement is zero.
A sinusoidal vertical traction σ_yy = -6000*sin(4πy) Pa is applied to the right edge (x=1).
A constant shear traction τ_xy=1000 Pa is applied to the top edge (y=0.5).
The hole boundary is traction-free.
Gravity loading is applied as a body force.
The 2D domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness, using the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
The mesh will have a density equivalent to 64 elements per edge.
The simulation will compute displacement and stress fields.
Von Mises stress will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
