The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The solver will implement small-strain linear elasticity.
The problem is a 2D plane-stress variational problem.
A vector-valued displacement space will be used.
A direct linear solver will be used.
The geometry is a rectangular plate of dimensions 1m by 0.5m.
A circular hole with a radius of 0.1m is located at the center (0.5m, 0.25m) of the plate.
The material properties are Young's Modulus E=70e9 Pa and Poisson's ratio ν=0.33.
The 2D domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness.
The reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be used.
A uniform vertical compression load σ_yy=-2500 Pa is applied to the top and bottom edges of the plate.
The left edge of the plate has a displacement constraint of u_y = 0.
The right edge of the plate has a displacement constraint of u_x = 0.
The hole boundary is traction-free.
The mesh will have 64 elements per edge.
The simulation will analyze displacement and stress patterns.
Von Mises stress will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
