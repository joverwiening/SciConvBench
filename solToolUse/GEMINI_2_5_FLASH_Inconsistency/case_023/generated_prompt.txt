The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The solver will implement small-strain linear elasticity.
The problem is a 2D plane-stress variational problem.
A vector-valued displacement space will be used.
A direct linear solver will be employed.
The domain is a semi-circular region with a radius of 1 meter, located in the y≥0 plane.
The material properties are Young's Modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, and density ρ=2700 kg/m^3.
Gravity g=9.81 m/s^2 acts in the negative y-direction.
A vertical point load of -5 kN is applied at the apex of the semi-circle, located at (0,1).
The base of the semi-circle, along y=0, is clamped (fixed displacement).
The 2D domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness.
The reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be used.
The mesh will be generated using 64 as the characteristic resolution parameter.
The simulation will compute the displacement distribution.
The simulation will compute the von Mises stress distribution from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
