The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is a 2D plane-stress variational problem for small-strain linear elasticity.
A vector-valued displacement space will be used.
A direct linear solver will be used.
The geometry is an L-shaped structure with outer dimensions 1x1 and inner cutout dimensions 0.5x0.5.
The material properties are Young's Modulus E=70e9 Pa and Poisson's ratio ν=0.33.
The density ρ=2700 kg/m^3 is provided but gravity will not be included as a body force.
The boundary conditions are: displacement u=0 on the edges x=0 and y=0.
Displacement u=0 on the two cutout faces adjacent to the re-entrant corner.
A horizontal traction of σ_xx=2.65e4 Pa is applied on the edge x=1.
The mesh resolution will be 64 elements per edge.
The 2D domain will be treated as plane stress with unit thickness, using the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
The simulation will calculate displacement patterns.
The simulation will calculate von Mises stress from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
