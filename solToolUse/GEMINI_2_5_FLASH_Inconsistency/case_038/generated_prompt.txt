The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The solver will implement small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane-stress.
The variational problem will use a vector-valued displacement space.
A direct linear solver will be used.
The domain is a semi-circular half-disk with radius 1m, centered at (0,0) for y≥0.
The material properties are Young's Modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, and density ρ=2700 kg/m^3.
Gravity g=9.81 m/s^2 acts in the negative y-direction.
The boundary condition on the flat base (y=0) is fixed displacement.
The load on the semi-circular arc is an inward normal pressure of 20 kPa (σ_n=-20 kPa).
The arc load will be applied as a traction t = -p*n, where p=20 kPa and n is the outward normal of the solid.
The 2D domain will be treated as plane stress with unit thickness.
The reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be used.
The mesh will be generated using a global resolution parameter of 64 for the semi-circular domain.
The simulation will compute and output the displacement and von Mises stress distributions.
Von Mises stress will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
