The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The solver will implement small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane-stress.
A vector-valued displacement space will be used.
A direct linear solver will be used.
The material properties are Young's modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, density ρ=2700 kg/m^3, and gravitational acceleration g=9.81 m/s^2.
The geometry is a polygon defined by vertices (0,0)-(0.8,0)-(0.8,0.2)-(0.5,0.2)-(0.5,0.5)-(0.3,0.5)-(0.3,0.2)-(0,0.2).
A point load of -5 kN will be applied in the y-component at coordinates (0.4,0.5).
Gravity will be applied as a body force in the negative y-direction.
The boundary condition at y=0 is fixed (zero displacement).
The 2D domain will be treated as plane stress with unit thickness.
The reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be used.
The mesh will consist of linear triangular elements.
The mesh will be generated using `mshr.Polygon` and `mshr.generate_mesh` with a resolution of 10 to yield approximately 72 elements.
The displacement magnitude distribution will be computed.
The von Mises stress distribution will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
