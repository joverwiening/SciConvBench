The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is a 2D plane-stress small-strain linear elasticity problem.
The domain is a right triangle with vertices at (0,0), (1,0), and (0,0.5).
The material properties are Young's Modulus E = 70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν = 0.33, density ρ = 2700 kg/m^3, and gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s^2.
A body force due to gravity acts in the negative y-direction.
The vertical edge at x=0 is clamped, meaning displacement components u_x and u_y are zero.
The sloping boundary defined by x + 2y = 1 has an upward normal traction of 3000 Pa.
The bottom edge at y=0 is a free surface, meaning no essential boundary conditions are applied.
The problem uses a vector-valued displacement space.
A direct linear solver will be used.
The 2D domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness, and the reduced plane-stress constitutive law is applied.
The mesh will consist of approximately 64 elements.
The output will include the displacement magnitude distribution.
The output will include the von Mises stress distribution, computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
