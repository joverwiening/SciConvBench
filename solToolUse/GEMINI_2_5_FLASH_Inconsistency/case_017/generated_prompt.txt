The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is 2D plane-stress linear elasticity.
The geometry is a cross-shaped plate with a horizontal bar from x = -1 to 1 and a vertical bar from y = -1 to 1, both 0.6 units wide.
The plate has four circular holes of radius 0.12 located at (0.6, 0), (-0.6, 0), (0, 0.6), and (0, -0.6).
The plate has a unit thickness.
The mesh will have a target maximum element edge length of 0.02.
The function space is a second-order vector Lagrange space.
The material properties are Young's modulus E = 210000 and Poisson ratio ν = 0.3.
The reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be used.
The left edge (x = -1) is clamped, meaning the displacement u = (0, 0).
A downward traction t = (0, -5e4) is applied only on the right edge (x = 1).
All circular hole boundaries are traction-free.
The instruction to impose zero normal displacement on hole boundaries for symmetry exploitation is disregarded.
The solver is a direct linear solver.
The simulation will compute the displacement field and the von Mises stress field.
