The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The solver will implement small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane stress.
The variational problem will use a vector-valued displacement space.
A direct linear solver will be used.
The problem is a plate with a centred circular hole.
The domain Ω is a rectangle from (0, 0) to (1.0, 0.20) m.
A centred circular hole of radius 0.05 m is located at (0.50, 0.10) m.
The material properties are Young's modulus E = 200 GPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.30.
The left edge (x = 0) has fixed boundary conditions: u_x = 0 and u_y = 0.
The right edge (x = 1.0) has a line traction of (2 MPa, 0) per unit thickness.
All other external boundaries, including the hole boundary, are traction-free.
The 2D strip is treated as plane stress with unit thickness.
The reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be used.
The mesh will be an unstructured triangular mesh.
The displacement field will be approximated using P2 (quadratic) Lagrange elements.
The mesh will be locally refined around the hole such that there are at least 8 elements across the radius of the hole.
The plane-stress von Mises field will be computed.
The computed von Mises field will be saved as q3_vm.png.
The displacement field will be saved in XDMF format.
The maximum von Mises stress on the hole boundary will be reported.
The stress concentration factor Kt = sigma_max / 2 MPa will be reported.
