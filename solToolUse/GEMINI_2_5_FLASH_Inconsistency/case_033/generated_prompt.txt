Implement a DOLFIN solver for a 3D compressible neo-Hookean solid on the unit cube.
Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The solver uses Newton's method to minimize the total potential energy for the displacement field.
The geometry is a unit cube.
The mesh is a UnitCube mesh with 16x16x16 cells.
The function space for displacement is VectorFunctionSpace(mesh, "Lagrange", 1).
The material properties are Young's modulus E = 10.0 and Poisson ratio ν = 0.3.
Kinematics are defined by the deformation gradient F = I + grad(u), the right Cauchy-Green deformation tensor C = F^T F, and the Jacobian J = det(F).
The material response is compressible.
The boundary condition is clamping on the face x = 0.
A body force b = (0, -0.5, 0) is applied.
A boundary traction h = (0.1, 0, 0) is applied on the face x = 1.
