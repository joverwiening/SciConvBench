The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane stress.
The domain is a right triangle with vertices at (0,0), (1,0), and (0,0.5).
The material properties are Young's Modulus E = 70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν = 0.33, density ρ = 2700 kg/m^3, and gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s^2.
The domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness, and the reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be used.
The mesh will be generated such that the longest edge of the triangular domain is divided into 64 segments.
Boundary conditions: The edge at y=0 is clamped, meaning displacement is zero in both x and y directions.
Loading conditions: A body force due to gravity acts in the negative y-direction. A horizontal shear traction of 2000 Pa acts along the sloping edge (defined by x+2y=1) in the positive x-direction.
The solver will use a vector-valued displacement space and a direct linear solver.
The required outputs are the displacement magnitude distribution and the von Mises stress distribution.
Von Mises stress will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
