The problem is small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane stress.
The domain is a half-disk with a radius of 1 meter.
The material properties are Young's Modulus E = 70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν = 0.33, and density ρ = 2700 kg/m^3.
Gravity g = 9.81 m/s^2 acts in the negative y-direction.
Two point loads of -3 kN each are applied at coordinates (0.707, 0.707) and (-0.707, 0.707).
The points (-1,0) and (1,0) on the base of the half-disk are fixed (zero displacement).
The mesh will consist of 64 cells.
The displacement field will be approximated using quadratic (P2) Lagrange elements.
The problem will be solved using a direct linear solver.
The simulation will compute the displacement and von Mises stress distributions.
Von Mises stress will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
The 2D domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness, and the reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be used.
The implementation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
