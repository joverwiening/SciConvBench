The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is small-strain von Mises elastoplasticity in plane strain.
The geometry is a symmetric quarter domain [0, 100] mm by [0, 180] mm with a quarter hole of radius 50 mm at the origin.
The model uses associative flow and perfect plasticity.
The unknown is the displacement field u = (u_x, u_y).
The strain epsilon is defined as 0.5*(grad(u) + grad(u)^T).
Material properties are lambda = 19.44 GPa, mu = 29.17 GPa, and yield stress sigma_Y = 243 MPa.
Boundary conditions are: u_x = 0 on x = 0 mm.
Boundary conditions are: u_y = 0 on y = 0 mm.
Boundary conditions are: u = (0, 1) mm on y = 180 mm.
Boundary conditions are: traction-free on x = 100 mm and on the hole boundary.
The solver uses an incremental return-mapping update consistent with plane strain perfect plasticity.
Newton iterations are used for the incremental solve.
The prescribed displacement on the y = 180 mm boundary will be applied in 50 load steps.
The resulting displacement field will be saved to XDMF format.
