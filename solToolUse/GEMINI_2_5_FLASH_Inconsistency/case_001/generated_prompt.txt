The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The solver will implement small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane stress.
A vector-valued displacement space will be used.
A direct linear solver will be employed.
The material properties are E=70e9 Pa, ν=0.33, ρ=2700 kg/m^3.
Gravity g=9.81 m/s^2 will act in the negative y-direction as a body force.
The geometry is a stepped polygon defined by vertices (0,0)-(0.8,0)-(0.8,0.2)-(0.5,0.2)-(0.5,0.5)-(0.3,0.5)-(0.3,0.2)-(0,0.2).
The bottom edge at y=0 will have fixed displacement (zero in both x and y directions).
A linearly varying vertical traction σ_yy(x) = -8000x Pa will be applied to the top segment where 0.3≤x≤0.5 (which is at y=0.5).
The 2D domain will be treated as plane stress with unit thickness.
The reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be used.
The mesh will consist of 72 elements.
Displacement and von Mises stress distributions will be computed.
Von Mises stress will be calculated from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
