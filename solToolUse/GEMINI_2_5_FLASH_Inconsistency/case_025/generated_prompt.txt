The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane stress.
The solver will use a vector-valued displacement space and a direct linear solver.
The material properties are Young's Modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, density ρ=2700 kg/m^3, and gravitational acceleration g=9.81 m/s^2.
The 2D domain is a stepped polygon defined by the 8 vertices: (0.0, 0.0), (1.0, 0.0), (1.0, 1.0), (0.7, 1.0), (0.7, 0.7), (0.3, 0.7), (0.3, 1.0), (0.0, 1.0).
The domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness, using the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
A body force due to gravity acts in the negative y-direction.
A vertical traction of σ_yy=-10 kPa is applied to the top boundary segment where 0.3 ≤ x ≤ 0.5.
The bottom edge of the domain (y=0) is fixed, meaning displacement is zero in both x and y directions.
The domain will be discretized with a mesh containing approximately 72 triangular elements.
The simulation will compute the displacement magnitude distribution.
The simulation will compute the von Mises stress distribution from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
