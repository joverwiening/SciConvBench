The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane stress.
The variational problem will use a vector-valued displacement space.
A direct linear solver will be used.
The material properties are Young's modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, density ρ=2700 kg/m^3, and gravitational acceleration g=9.81 m/s^2.
The domain is a parallelogram with vertices (0,0), (1,0), (1.2,0.3), and (0.2,0.3).
The domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness, using the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
Boundary conditions include fixed displacement (u=0) on the left sloped edge (connecting (0,0) and (0.2,0.3)) and the right sloped edge (connecting (1,0) and (1.2,0.3)).
Loads include gravity acting in the negative y-direction.
An inward normal traction of 5000 Pa is applied as a vertical downward load on the horizontal top edge (connecting (0.2,0.3) and (1.2,0.3)).
The mesh will consist of 72 triangular finite elements with a polynomial degree of 2 for the displacement field.
The simulation will compute the displacement field and the von Mises stress distribution.
Von Mises stress will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
