The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is a 2D plane-stress small-strain linear elasticity variational problem.
The displacement field will be represented by a vector-valued function space.
A direct linear solver will be used.
The L-shaped domain has outer dimensions from x=0 to x=1 and y=0 to y=1.
A 0.5x0.5 square is removed from the top-right corner, so the domain excludes x in (0.5, 1] and y in (0.5, 1].
The material properties are Young's modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson ratio ν=0.33, and density ρ=2700 kg/m^3.
The domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness, using the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
The mesh will have a characteristic element size of 1/64.
Gravity loading is applied in the negative y-direction with g=9.81 m/s^2.
The left boundary (x=0) is fixed, meaning displacement is zero in both x and y directions.
A vertically increasing traction is applied only on the outer right edge (x=1).
The traction magnitude is given by σ_yy(y) = -2.65e4 * y Pa, where the negative sign indicates compression.
All re-entrant inner boundaries are traction-free.
The simulation will compute displacement and stress distributions.
The von Mises stress will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
