The simulation uses FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The solver implements small-strain linear elasticity.
The problem is a 2D plane-stress variational problem.
A vector-valued displacement space is used.
A direct linear solver is used.
The material properties are Young's Modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, and density ρ=2700 kg/m^3.
The geometry is a 1 meter by 0.5 meter rectangle with a 0.1 meter radius circular hole centered at (0.5, 0.25) meters.
The 2D domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness.
The reduced plane-stress constitutive law is used.
The mesh has 72 elements.
The left edge (x=0) is fixed, meaning displacement is zero in all directions.
The bottom edge (y=0) is fixed, meaning displacement is zero in all directions.
The hole boundary is traction-free.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction with g=9.81 m/s^2.
The top edge (y=0.5) is subjected to an inward normal traction of 1000 Pa, meaning the traction vector is (0, -1000) Pa on the top edge.
Displacement and von Mises stress distributions are computed.
Von Mises stress is computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
