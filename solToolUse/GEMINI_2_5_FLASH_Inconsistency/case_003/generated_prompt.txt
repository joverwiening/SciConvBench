The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The solver will implement small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane-stress.
The displacement function space will be vector-valued and use a quadratic (P2) polynomial degree.
A direct linear solver will be used.
The problem involves a rectangular plate with two circular holes.
The domain Ω is defined as (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) m.
Two circular holes, each with a radius of 0.04 m, are centered at (0.33, 0.10) m and (0.67, 0.10) m.
The material properties are Young's modulus E = 200 GPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.30.
The constitutive response will use the reduced plane-stress formulation for a unit thickness.
The left edge (x = 0) is clamped, with u_x = 0 and u_y = 0.
The right edge (x = 1.0) has a traction t = (2 MPa, 0) applied as a line traction per unit thickness.
All remaining boundaries (top, bottom, and both hole boundaries) are traction-free.
The mesh will have a maximum element size of 0.01 m.
The plane-stress von Mises field will be computed.
The computed von Mises field will be saved as q4_vm.png.
The displacement field will be saved in XDMF format.
The maximum von Mises stress on the hole boundaries will be reported.
The stress concentration factor Kt, calculated as sigma_max / 2 MPa, will be reported.
