The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The solver will implement small-strain linear elasticity.
The problem is a 2D plane-stress variational problem.
A vector-valued displacement space will be used.
A direct linear solver will be employed.
The material properties are Young's Modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, and density ρ=2700 kg/m^3.
The gravitational acceleration g=9.81 m/s^2 will be applied as a body force in the negative y-direction.
The geometry is a 1m x 0.5m rectangular plate.
The plate has a circular hole of radius 0.1m centered at (0.5, 0.25)m.
The plate also has a semi-circular indentation on its right edge.
This indentation has a radius of 0.02m and is centered at (1, 0.25)m.
The indentation curves inwards, connecting points (1, 0.23)m and (1, 0.27)m on the right edge.
The 2D domain is treated as plane stress with unit thickness.
The reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be used.
The left boundary (x=0) is fixed, meaning displacement is zero.
The boundary of the circular hole is traction-free.
A tangential traction is applied to the semi-circular indentation.
The tangential traction t_θ is calculated as M/(2πr), where M=800 Nm and r=0.02m.
The traction is applied in the local tangent direction of the indentation arc.
All other external boundaries (top, bottom, and the straight segments of the right edge) are traction-free.
The mesh density will correspond to approximately 64 elements along the 1m edge of the bounding box.
The simulation will compute displacement and stress patterns.
Von Mises stress will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
