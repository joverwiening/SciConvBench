The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The solver will implement small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane stress.
The variational problem will use a vector-valued displacement space.
A direct linear solver will be used.
The geometry is a rectangular plate Ω = (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) m.
A semicircular notch of radius a = 0.05 m is removed from the top edge.
The notch centre is at (0.5, 0.2).
The model is plane-stress linear elasticity for displacement u = (u_x, u_y) in Ω.
The material properties are Young's modulus E = 200 GPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.30.
The bottom edge (y = 0) is fixed, with u_x = 0 and u_y = 0.
The top edge segments x in [0, 0.45) union (0.55, 1.0] have a traction t = (0, -10 MPa*m).
This traction is interpreted as a line traction per unit thickness.
Both vertical sides (x = 0 and x = 1) are traction-free.
The semicircular notch arc is traction-free.
The 2D strip is treated as plane stress with unit thickness.
The reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be used.
The plane-stress von Mises field will be computed and saved as q6_vm.png.
The displacement field will be saved in XDMF format.
