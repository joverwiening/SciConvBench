Case ID: case_015
Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
Solver: small-strain linear elasticity in legacy FEniCS (dolfin); solve the 2D plane-stress variational problem with a vector-valued displacement space and a direct linear solver.
Task:
Solve Solid Mechanics Problem 6 from the paper: a plate with a semicircular top-edge notch in plane stress.
Case details:
Geometry:
Let Ω = (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) m be a rectangular plate with a semicircular notch of radius a = 0.05 m removed from the top edge. The notch centre is at (0.5, 0.2).
Model:
Plane-stress linear elasticity for displacement u = (u_x, u_y) in Ω.
Material:
Young's modulus E = 200 GPa, Poisson's ratio ν = 0.30.
Boundary conditions:
Bottom edge (y = 0): fixed, u_x = 0, u_y = 0.
Top edge segments x in [0, 0.45) union (0.55, 1.0]: traction t = (0, -10 MPa*m).
Both vertical sides (x = 0 and x = 1) and the notch arc: traction-free.
Outputs:
Compute the plane-stress von Mises field and save q6_vm.png.
Save the displacement field in XDMF format.
Implementation notes:
Treat the 2D strip as plane stress with unit thickness and use the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
Apply the top load only on the two top segments outside the notch opening.
Interpret t = (0, -10 MPa*m) as a line traction per unit thickness.
