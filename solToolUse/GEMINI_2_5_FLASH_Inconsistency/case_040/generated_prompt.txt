The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The solver will implement quasi-incompressible finite-strain hyperelasticity in legacy FEniCS (dolfin).
The problem is a mixed displacement-pressure neo-Hookean problem solved with Newton's method.
The task is to solve Solid Mechanics Problem 14 from the paper, which is a two-hole strip with follower pressure and prescribed right-edge displacement.
The geometry is a rectangular strip Ω = (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) m.
The strip has two circular holes of radius a = 0.04 m.
The holes are centred at (0.40, 0.10) m and (0.60, 0.10) m.
The model is a large-deformation isotropic quasi-incompressible neo-Hookean solid.
The unknowns are displacement u = (u_x, u_y) and hydrostatic pressure p.
The simulation will be performed under plane strain conditions.
The material properties are Young's modulus E = 5 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.49.
The left edge (x = 0) is fixed with u_x = 0 and u_y = 0.
The right edge (x = 1.0) has a prescribed displacement of u_x = 0.060 m and u_y = 0.
Each hole boundary has a uniform follower pressure P_hole = 0.10 MPa.
The top and bottom edges are traction-free.
The two hole pressures will be applied using the reference normals of the undeformed holes.
A mixed displacement-pressure formulation consistent with quasi-incompressibility in plane strain will be used.
A colour map of pressure p will be saved as q14_p.png.
A colour map of von Mises stress, computed from the Cauchy stress, will be saved as q14_vm.png.
The displacement field will be saved in XDMF format.
The characteristic element size for the mesh will be h = 0.01 m.
