The simulation will use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve a 2D plane-stress variational problem for small-strain linear elasticity.
A vector-valued displacement space will be used, and a direct linear solver will be employed.
The domain is a parallelogram with vertices at (0,0), (1,0), (1.2,0.3), and (0.2,0.3).
The material properties are Young's Modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, and density ρ=2700 kg/m^3.
Gravity g=9.81 m/s^2 acts in the negative y-direction.
A concentrated horizontal load of -5 kN is applied at the point (1.1, 0.15).
This concentrated load will be represented using a PointSource on the appropriate displacement component.
The left inclined edge, defined by the line y=1.5x, is fixed, meaning all displacement components are zero along this boundary.
All other edges of the parallelogram are free boundaries, meaning no displacement constraints or external forces are applied to them (other than the specified point load and gravity).
The mesh will consist of 72 elements.
The 2D domain will be treated as plane stress with unit thickness, and the reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be used.
The simulation will compute displacement and von Mises stress distributions.
Von Mises stress will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
