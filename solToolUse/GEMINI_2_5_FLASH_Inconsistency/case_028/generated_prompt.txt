The simulation will be implemented using FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is small-strain linear elasticity in 2D plane stress.
A vector-valued displacement space will be used.
A direct linear solver will be employed.
The geometry is an L-shaped domain defined by an outer 1x1 unit square with a 0.5x0.5 unit square removed from its top-right corner.
The material properties are Young's modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson ratio ν=0.33, and density ρ=2700 kg/m^3.
Gravity loading will be applied as a body force with g=9.81 m/s^2 in the negative y-direction.
The left boundary (x=0) is fixed, meaning displacement is zero.
A horizontal shear traction of σ_xy=2.65e4 Pa will be applied on the top-right edge segment where y=1 and x > 0.5.
The mesh will have a resolution equivalent to dividing the outer 1x1 square into 64x64 cells, meaning a characteristic element size of 1/64.
The 2D domain will be treated as plane stress with unit thickness, and the reduced plane-stress constitutive law will be used.
The von Mises stress will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
Displacement and stress patterns will be visualized.
