The task is to implement a small-strain linear elasticity solver in legacy FEniCS (dolfin) for a 2D plane-stress semi-circular domain with the following specifications:

The domain is a half-disk of radius 1 meter with y ≥ 0.

The mesh consists of 64 triangular elements.

The boundary at y = 0 (the base) is fixed with zero displacement Dirichlet boundary condition.

The curved arc boundary has an inward normal pressure of -20000 Pa applied as a Neumann boundary condition.

Body force due to gravity is applied with density ρ = 2700 kg/m³ and gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s² acting in the negative y-direction.

The material follows plane stress elasticity with Young's modulus E = 70 × 10⁹ Pa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.33.

The constitutive law uses the reduced plane-stress stiffness tensor with unit thickness.

The displacement field is solved using a vector-valued Lagrange finite element space of degree 1.

A direct linear solver (MUMPS or default LU) is used to solve the variational problem.

The von Mises equivalent stress is computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress components σ_xx, σ_yy, and σ_xy.

The output includes the displacement magnitude distribution and the von Mises stress distribution visualized or saved to file.
