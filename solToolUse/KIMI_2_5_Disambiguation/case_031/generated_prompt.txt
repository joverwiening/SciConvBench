Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem on a parallelogram domain.
The domain vertices are (0,0), (1,0), (1.2,0.3), (0.2,0.3).
Material properties are Young's modulus E = 70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν = 0.33, density ρ = 2700 kg/m^3, and gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s^2.
The left inclined edge connecting (0,0) to (0.2,0.3) is fully clamped (zero displacement).
A horizontal point load of -5000 N (negative x-direction) is applied at point (1.1, 0.15).
Body force due to gravity acts in the negative y-direction with magnitude ρ*g.
Use a vector-valued Lagrange finite element space of degree 1 for displacement.
The mesh consists of 72 triangular elements.
Implement the plane-stress constitutive law with unit thickness: σ = (E/(1-ν^2)) * [1 ν 0; ν 1 0; 0 0 (1-ν)/2] * ε, where ε is the symmetric gradient of displacement.
Solve the variational problem: find u in V such that ∫ σ(u) : ε(v) dx = ∫ f·v dx + F_point·v(x_point) for all v in V, where f is the body force and F_point is the point load.
Use a direct linear solver (MUMPS or default LU).
Compute the Cauchy stress tensor from the displacement solution.
Compute the von Mises equivalent stress from the in-plane stress components: σ_vm = sqrt(σ_xx^2 + σ_yy^2 - σ_xx*σ_yy + 3*σ_xy^2).
Output the displacement magnitude and von Mises stress distributions for visualization.
