Q: What is the specific spatial variation of the vertical traction on the top boundary (e.g., linearly increasing from left to right, maximum value at a specific location, or a mathematical expression in terms of x and y coordinates)?
A: σ_yy(x) = -8000x Pa, applied only on the top segment where 0.3 ≤ x ≤ 0.5. The traction varies linearly with x, is purely vertical (negative y-direction), and is zero outside this segment.
