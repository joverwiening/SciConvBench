Solve Solid Mechanics Problem 9 from the paper: a functionally graded plate in plane stress.

Geometry:
Let Ω = (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) m be a rectangular plate in 2D plane stress.

Mesh:
Structured mesh 100 x 20 over Ω.

Model:
Linear elasticity for displacement u = (u_x, u_y).
Use small-strain formulation.

Material:
Poisson's ratio ν = 0.30.
Young's modulus varies spatially as E(y) = 100 GPa + 100 GPa*(y/0.20) for y in [0, 0.20] m.
The modulus varies with height y only, not with x.

Boundary conditions:
Left edge (x = 0): fixed, u_x = 0, u_y = 0.
Right edge (x = 1): uniform traction t = (2e6 Pa*m, 0) per unit thickness.
Top and bottom edges: traction-free.

Constitutive law:
Use plane-stress reduced constitutive law with unit thickness.

Solver:
Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
Solve the 2D plane-stress variational problem with a direct linear solver.

Outputs:
