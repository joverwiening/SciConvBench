The simulation shall model a 2D L-shaped structure using legacy FEniCS (dolfin) with small-strain linear elasticity under plane-stress conditions.

The geometry consists of an outer square of size 1x1 m with an inner square of size 0.5x0.5 m removed from the top-right corner, creating an L-shape.

The mesh shall be generated with 64 elements per edge using the FEniCS mesh generation capabilities or external mesh tools compatible with dolfin.

The function space shall be a vector-valued Lagrange space of degree 1 (P1) for displacement, with 2 components for the 2D problem.

The constitutive law shall use the plane-stress reduced formulation: the stress-strain relationship is sigma = E/(1-nu^2) * [(1-nu)*eps + nu*tr(eps)*I] for the in-plane components, with sigma_zz = 0.

Material properties are: Young's modulus E = 70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio nu = 0.33, density rho = 2700 kg/m^3, gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s^2.

The body force term shall include gravity acting in the negative y-direction: b = (0, -rho*g).

Boundary conditions shall fix the left boundary (x=0) with DirichletBC setting displacement to zero for both components.

A concentrated point load of magnitude -2.65e4 N shall be applied in the y-direction (downward) at the point location (0.5, 0.5) using a PointSource on the y-component of the displacement field.

The variational problem shall be formulated as: find u in V such that a(u,v) = L(v) for all v in V, where a(u,v) = inner(sigma(u), epsilon(v))*dx and L(v) = dot(b, v)*dx + point_load contribution.

The linear system shall be solved using a direct solver (default LU solver in FEniCS).

Post-processing shall compute the von Mises equivalent stress from the in-plane Cauchy stress components: sigma_vm = sqrt(sigma_xx^2 + sigma_yy^2 - sigma_xx*sigma_yy + 3*sigma_xy^2).

The solution shall output displacement magnitude, displacement components (u_x, u_y), and von Mises stress fields for visualization and analysis.
