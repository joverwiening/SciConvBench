The simulation shall model a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem using FEniCS legacy (dolfin).

The geometry consists of a rectangular domain with width 4.0 and height 1.0, containing a circular hole of radius 0.3 centered at coordinates (1.0, 0.5).

The mesh shall be generated with a resolution parameter of 72, using the FEniCS mshr module or gmsh with appropriate refinement near the hole boundary.

The function space shall be a second-order vector Lagrange space (VectorFunctionSpace with degree 2) for the displacement field.

Material properties are Young's modulus E = 210000 and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.3, with unit thickness assumed.

The constitutive law shall use the reduced plane-stress formulation: the stress-strain relation is σ = 2με + λ_tr(ε)I, where λ = 2μλ/(λ+2μ) is the effective plane-stress Lamé parameter, with μ = E/(2(1+ν)) and λ = Eν/((1+ν)(1-2ν)).

Boundary conditions are: clamped displacement u = (0, 0) on the left edge at x = 0; traction boundary condition t = (0, -100000) applied on the right edge at x = 4.0; traction-free boundaries on all remaining edges including the hole boundary.

No body forces are applied.

The variational problem is: find u in V such that ∫Ω σ(u):ε(v) dx = ∫Γ_right t·v ds for all v in V_0, where V_0 contains functions vanishing on x = 0.

The solver shall use a direct linear solver (default MUMPS or LU) via solve(a == L, u, bc).

Post-processing shall compute the von Mises equivalent stress as σ_vm = sqrt(σ_11^2 - σ_11σ_22 + σ_22^2 + 3σ_12^2) in the plane-stress case, projected to a suitable function space for visualization.

Output shall include the displacement field and von Mises stress field, with optional plotting or XDMF/HDF5 export.
