Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem for a rectangular plate with a centered circular hole.

The domain is a rectangular plate Ω = (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) m with a circular hole of radius a = 0.05 m centered at (0.5, 0.1) m.

The material has Young's modulus E = 200 GPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.30.

The variational problem uses a vector-valued displacement function space with Lagrange elements of degree 1.

The left edge at x = 0 is fully fixed with Dirichlet boundary conditions u_x = 0 and u_y = 0.

The right edge at x = 1.0 has a traction boundary condition t = (2e6, 0) Pa applied.

All other external boundaries and the hole boundary are traction-free.

The plane-stress constitutive law is used with the reduced stiffness tensor for plane stress conditions.

A direct linear solver (MUMPS or default LU) is used to solve the linear system.

The mesh is generated with a characteristic element size that provides adequate resolution around the hole, using approximately 32 elements around the hole circumference and graded refinement toward the hole boundary with a minimum element size of approximately 0.002 m near the hole and maximum size of 0.02 m away from the hole.

The von Mises stress field is computed from the plane-stress stress tensor using the standard formula σ_vm = sqrt(σ_xx^2 - σ_xx*σ_yy + σ_yy^2 + 3*σ_xy^2).

The von Mises stress field is plotted and saved to file q3_vm.png.

The displacement field is saved in XDMF format for visualization.

The maximum von Mises stress on the hole boundary is computed by evaluating the von Mises stress at all degrees of freedom on the hole boundary and taking the maximum value.

The stress concentration factor is computed as Kt = σ_max / 2e6 where σ_max is the maximum von Mises stress on the hole boundary.

The theoretical stress concentration factor for this geometry is Kt_theoretical = 3 based on classical elasticity solutions for an infinite plate with a circular hole under uniaxial tension.
