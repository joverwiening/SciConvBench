Impose homogeneous Dirichlet boundary conditions u = (0,0) on the entire outer left edge x = 0 and the entire outer bottom edge y = 0, including portions adjacent to the re-entrant corner.
Apply a Neumann traction on the outer right edge x = 1 only, with traction vector t = (2.65e4, 0) Pa.
Leave all inner cutout boundaries traction-free.
Formulate the weak form for linear elasticity with body force and boundary traction contributions.
Solve the resulting linear system using a direct linear solver available in dolfin.
Compute the in-plane Cauchy stress tensor field from the displacement solution using the plane-stress constitutive relation.
