Q: What is the intended assignment of materials to the two subdomains (i.e., which material occupies 0 ≤ y < 0.10 m and which occupies 0.10 ≤ y ≤ 0.20 m)?
A: Bottom half \(0 \le y < 0.10\ \text{m}\) is **steel** \(\Omega_{\text{Steel}}\).  
Top half \(0.10 \le y \le 0.20\ \text{m}\) is **aluminium** \(\Omega_{\text{Al}}\).
