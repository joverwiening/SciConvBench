Q: What is the exact spatial function E(x,y) for the Young’s modulus field in “Problem 9” (please provide the formula and any parameter values)?
A: Young’s modulus varies **with height only** (not with \(x\)):

\[
E(x,y) = E(y) = 100\,\text{GPa} + 100\,\text{GPa}\left(\frac{y}{0.20}\right), \quad y \in [0,\,0.20]\,\text{m}.
\]

So \(E=100\) GPa at \(y=0\) and \(E=200\) GPa at \(y=0.20\) m.
