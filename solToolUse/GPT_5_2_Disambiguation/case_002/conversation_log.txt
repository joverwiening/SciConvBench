Q: What is the exact traction vector (magnitude and direction) to apply on the sloping boundary x+2y=1 (e.g., t = t0*n with t0 = ? Pa, or a specified vector in global coordinates)?
A: Apply a **pure normal traction** on the sloping edge \(x+2y=1\):

- Magnitude: \(t_0 = 3000\ \text{Pa}\).
- Direction: along the **local outward unit normal** \(\mathbf{n}\) of that boundary (not a fixed global \(y\)-direction).

So use
\[
\mathbf{t} = t_0\,\mathbf{n} = 3000\,\mathbf{n}\ \text{(Pa)}.
\]

For the line \(x+2y=1\), an outward unit normal can be taken as
\[
\mathbf{n}=\frac{(1,\,2)}{\sqrt{5}},
\]
so equivalently
\[
\mathbf{t}=\frac{3000}{\sqrt{5}}(1,\,2)\ \text{Pa}.
\]
