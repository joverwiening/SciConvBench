Q: What is the exact mathematical expression (including magnitude, units, and the x-interval on the top boundary where it applies) for the linearly varying vertical traction?
A: The vertical traction on the top boundary is prescribed as an in-plane normal stress:

- **Expression:** \(\sigma_{yy}(x) = -8000\,x\) **Pa**
- **Where applied:** only on the **top segment** with \(0.3 \le x \le 0.5\) (i.e., along \(y=0.5\) over that x-interval)
- **Direction/sign:** negative means **downward** (compressive in \(y\)).
