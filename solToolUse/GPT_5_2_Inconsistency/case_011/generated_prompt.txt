Use legacy FEniCS (dolfin) to solve a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem on a perforated rectangular plate.
Model the domain as a rectangle of width 1.0 m and height 0.5 m with a circular hole of radius 0.1 m centered at (0.5, 0.25).
Generate a conforming 2D mesh with approximately 64 elements along each outer rectangle edge (use a mesh generator such as mshr).
Use a vector-valued continuous Galerkin displacement space of first order (e.g., VectorFunctionSpace(mesh, "CG", 1)).
Assume plane stress with unit thickness.
Use isotropic linear elastic material parameters E = 70e9 Pa and ν = 0.33.
Use the reduced plane-stress constitutive law with Lamé-type parameters λ_ps = E ν / (1 - ν^2) and μ = E / (2 (1 + ν)).
Define strain as ε(u) = sym(grad(u)).
Define Cauchy stress as σ(u) = 2 μ ε(u) + λ_ps tr(ε(u)) I.
Apply a clamped boundary condition u = (0, 0) on the left edge x = 0.
Apply internal pressure on the hole boundary with magnitude p = 1e5 Pa using traction t = +p n, where n is the outward normal of the solid domain so the traction acts outward from the cavity.
Include gravity as a body force b = (0, -ρ g) with ρ = 2700 kg/m^3 and g = 9.81 m/s^2.
Formulate the variational problem: find u in V such that ∫_Ω σ(u):ε(v) dx = ∫_Ω b·v dx + ∫_(∂Ω_hole) t·v ds for all v in V.
Use appropriate boundary markers to integrate the traction only over the hole boundary.
Solve the resulting linear system with a direct linear solver available in dolfin (e.g., LU).
Postprocess the in-plane Cauchy stress field σ(u) over the domain.
Compute the 2D von Mises stress from the in-plane stress components as σ_vm = sqrt(σ_xx^2 - σ_xx σ_yy + σ_yy^2 + 3 σ_xy^2).
Output displacement and stress fields suitable for visualization (e.g., XDMF or VTK), including u, σ components, and von Mises.
Report qualitative displacement and stress patterns resulting from the combined pressure traction and gravity body force.
