Q: What thickness (out-of-plane) should be assumed for the plane-stress lamina when converting the given top-edge line traction into equivalent 2D units for assembly?
A: Assume **unit thickness**, i.e. **thickness = 1.0 m**. The given top-edge load is already a **line traction per unit thickness**, so apply it directly as \(t = (0,\,-10\,\text{MPa}\cdot\text{m})\) on \(y=0.20\) without any additional thickness scaling.
