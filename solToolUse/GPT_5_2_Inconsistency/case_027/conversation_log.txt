Q: For plane-stress, what exact constitutive relation should be used to compute the in-plane Cauchy stress σ(u): the 2D plane-stress Lamé-parameter form with λps = 2μλ/(λ+2μ) and μ = E/(2(1+ν)), or an explicit 3×3 plane-stress matrix (σxx, σyy, σxy) = Dps (εxx, εyy, 2εxy)?
A: Use the **reduced plane-stress constitutive law** for the **in-plane Cauchy stress**. Either implementation is fine as long as it is plane-stress (σzz = 0), but implement it in **Lamé form with the plane-stress-modified λ**:

- \( \mu = \dfrac{E}{2(1+\nu)} \)
- \( \lambda = \dfrac{E\nu}{(1+\nu)(1-2\nu)} \) (3D)
- **Plane-stress effective** \( \lambda_{ps} = \dfrac{2\mu\lambda}{\lambda + 2\mu} \)

Then compute
\[
\sigma(u) = 2\mu\,\varepsilon(u) + \lambda_{ps}\,\text{tr}(\varepsilon(u))\,I
\]
with \(\varepsilon(u)=\tfrac12(\nabla u + \nabla u^T)\) in 2D.
