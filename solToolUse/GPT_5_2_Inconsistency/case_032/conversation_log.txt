Q: What is the exact traction vector t(x,y) (components in global x–y) to apply on the boundary x=0 to represent the “horizontal shear traction” of magnitude 4000 Pa?
A: Apply a constant traction tangent to the vertical edge \(x=0\), i.e. purely horizontal in global coordinates:

\[
\mathbf{t}(x,y)=\begin{bmatrix}4000\\0\end{bmatrix}\ \text{Pa}\quad \text{on } x=0.
\]

(So \(t_x=4000\) Pa, \(t_y=0\) Pa.)

Q: What body force vector b (in N/m^3) should be used for gravity in the plane-stress model (e.g., b = (0, -ρg) or another direction/sign convention)?
A: Use gravity acting downward in the global \(y\)-direction:

\[
\mathbf{b} = \begin{bmatrix}0\\ -\rho g\end{bmatrix}\ \text{N/m}^3
= \begin{bmatrix}0\\ -2700\cdot 9.81\end{bmatrix}
= \begin{bmatrix}0\\ -26487\end{bmatrix}\ \text{N/m}^3.
\]
