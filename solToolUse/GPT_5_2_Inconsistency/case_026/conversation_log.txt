Q: For the sinusoidal traction on the hypotenuse, should the sine argument use the global x coordinate as written (sin(πx)) or the local arclength coordinate along the hypotenuse (sin(πs))?
A: Use the **global x coordinate as written**: apply the traction as  
\[
\sigma_{yy}(x,y) = -5\times 10^3 \,\sin(\pi x)\ \text{Pa}
\]
on the hypotenuse \(x+2y=1\). Do **not** reparameterize it by local arclength.
