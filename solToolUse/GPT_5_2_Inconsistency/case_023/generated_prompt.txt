Use the reduced plane-stress constitutive law to compute the in-plane Cauchy stress σ(u) from ε(u).
Apply a body force corresponding to gravity in the negative y direction: f = (0, -ρ g).

Apply a concentrated vertical point load at the apex point (0, 1) using PointSource on the vertical displacement component.
Set the PointSource magnitude to -5000 N as a total force applied to the y-component in the unit-thickness plane-stress model.
Formulate and solve the variational problem for static equilibrium with a direct linear solver.
Compute the displacement field u over the domain.
Compute the in-plane Cauchy stress field σ(u) over the domain.
Compute the von Mises stress from the in-plane Cauchy stress field using the plane-stress 2D von Mises expression based on σ_xx, σ_yy, and σ_xy.
