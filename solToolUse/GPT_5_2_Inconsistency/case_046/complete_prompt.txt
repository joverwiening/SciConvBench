Case ID: case_046
Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
Solver: small-strain linear elasticity in legacy FEniCS (dolfin); solve the 2D plane-stress variational problem with a vector-valued displacement space and a direct linear solver.
Task:
Analyze stress in a rectangular plate with central circular hole under gravity loading and point load. Implement linear elasticity with E=70e9 Pa, ν=0.33 using plane stress formulation. Fix left edge, apply 5 kN downward point load at (1,0.25), and compute displacement/von Mises stress distributions.
Case details:
Material: E=70e9, ν=0.33, ρ=2700 kg/m^3, g=9.81 m/s^2. Geometry: 1x0.5 rectangle with 0.1m radius hole at (0.5,0.25). Loads: Gravity (-y), -5 kN at (1,0.25). Boundaries: x=0 fixed. Mesh: 72 elements.
Implementation notes:
Treat the 2D domain as plane stress with unit thickness and use the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
Compute von Mises from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
Represent each concentrated load with PointSource on the appropriate displacement component instead of smearing it over an edge.
Leave the hole boundary traction-free unless a pressure load is explicitly prescribed.
Apply the stated outer-edge load only on the named boundary; all other unloaded exterior edges are traction-free.
