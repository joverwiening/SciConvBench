Q: What constitutive split should be used for the mixed u–p formulation: u–p as a Lagrange multiplier for incompressibility with p = −λ tr(ε(u)) (i.e., σ = 2μ ε(u) − p I), or a three-field-like split where p approximates the volumetric stress with σ = 2μ dev(ε(u)) + p I?
A: Use the **standard mixed incompressible-elasticity Lagrange-multiplier form**:

- Stress: \(\sigma(u,p) = 2\mu\,\varepsilon(u) - p\,I\)

Do **not** use the alternative “\(p I\) with deviatoric strain” split.
