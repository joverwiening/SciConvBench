Q: For the linearly varying traction, should σ_yy vary with x as σ_yy(x) = -8000·x Pa (as stated earlier), or vary with y on the loaded segment (as stated at the end)?
A: It should vary with **x**: apply the vertical traction on the top segment \(0.3 \le x \le 0.5\) as  
\[
\sigma_{yy}(x) = -8000\,x \ \text{Pa},
\]
evaluated using the **global x-coordinate**.
