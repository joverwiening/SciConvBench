Q: What plane-stress constitutive relation should I use in code (i.e., the explicit 2D stress–strain matrix or Lamé-parameter form for plane stress) for E=70e9 and ν=0.33?
A: Use the **plane-stress** linear elastic law (unit thickness), i.e. eliminate \(\sigma_{zz}=0\). In Lamé-like form for 2D:

- Define the plane-stress “effective” Lamé parameters
\[
\mu = \frac{E}{2(1+\nu)}, \qquad \lambda_{ps}=\frac{E\nu}{1-\nu^2}.
\]
Then for in-plane strain \(\varepsilon(u)=\tfrac12(\nabla u+\nabla u^T)\),
\[
\sigma = 2\mu\,\varepsilon + \lambda_{ps}\,\text{tr}(\varepsilon)\,I_{2\times 2}.
\]

For \(E=70\times 10^9\) Pa, \(\nu=0.33\):
- \(\mu = 2.631578947\times 10^{10}\) Pa
- \(\lambda_{ps} = 2.586733733\times 10^{10}\) Pa

Equivalent 2D matrix (Voigt with \([\varepsilon_{xx},\varepsilon_{yy},\gamma_{xy}]^T\), \(\gamma_{xy}=2\varepsilon_{xy}\)):
\[
\begin{bmatrix}\sigma_{xx}\\ \sigma_{yy}\\ \tau_{xy}\end{bmatrix}
=
\frac{E}{1-\nu^2}
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & \nu & 0\\
\nu & 1 & 0\\
0 & 0 & \frac{1-\nu}{2}
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}\varepsilon_{xx}\\ \varepsilon_{yy}\\ \gamma_{xy}\end{bmatrix}.
\]
