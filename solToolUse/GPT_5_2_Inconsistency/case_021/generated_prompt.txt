Use legacy FEniCS (dolfin) to solve a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem in plane stress on a perforated rectangular plate.
Model the domain as a rectangle [0,1]×[0,0.5] with a circular hole of radius 0.1 centered at (0.5,0.25) removed.
Generate a mesh with nominal resolution corresponding to 64 elements per outer edge.
Use a vector-valued continuous Galerkin displacement space (e.g., VectorFunctionSpace(mesh,"CG",1)).
Use unit thickness and a plane-stress constitutive law.
Set material parameters E=70e9 Pa and ν=0.33.
Define μ = E/(2(1+ν)) and λ_ps = Eν/(1−ν^2).
Define strain ε(u)=sym(grad(u)).
Define in-plane Cauchy stress σ(u)=2μ ε(u)+λ_ps tr(ε(u)) I where I is the 2×2 identity.
Apply gravity as a body force per unit volume f=(0,−ρg) with ρ=2700 kg/m^3 and g=9.81 m/s^2, i.e., f=(0,−26487) N/m^3.
Apply a fully clamped Dirichlet boundary condition u=(0,0) on the left boundary x=0.
Apply a Neumann traction on the right boundary x=1 with traction vector t=(0, t_y(y)) where t_y(y)=−6000*sin(4*pi*y) Pa.
Apply a Neumann traction on the top boundary y=0.5 corresponding to constant shear τ_xy=1000 Pa, i.e., traction vector t=(1000,0) on y=0.5.
Apply no traction on the hole boundary (traction-free) unless explicitly prescribed (none is prescribed here).
Assemble the variational form: find u such that ∫_Ω σ(u):ε(v) dx = ∫_Ω f·v dx + ∫_{x=1} t·v ds + ∫_{y=0.5} t·v ds for all test functions v.
Solve the resulting linear system using a direct linear solver.
Post-process the in-plane Cauchy stress field σ(u).
Compute von Mises stress from the in-plane stress components as σ_vm = sqrt(σ_xx^2 − σ_xx σ_yy + σ_yy^2 + 3 τ_xy^2).
Output displacement and stress fields (including von Mises) for visualization.
