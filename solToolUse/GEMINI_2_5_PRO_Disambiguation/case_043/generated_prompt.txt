The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The analysis is a 2D plane stress linear elasticity problem.
The domain is a parallelogram defined by the vertices (0,0), (1,0), (1.2, 0.3), and (0.2, 0.3).
A structured mesh of 72 triangular elements will be generated by specifying 12 divisions along the horizontal direction and 3 divisions along the sloped direction.
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa, a Poisson's ratio of 0.33, and a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force representing gravity acts in the negative y-direction, with g = 9.81 m/s^2.
An inward normal traction of 5000 Pa is applied to the top boundary connecting vertices (0.2, 0.3) and (1.2, 0.3).
The left sloped boundary connecting (0,0) and (0.2, 0.3) is fixed with zero displacement in both x and y directions.
The right sloped boundary connecting (1,0) and (1.2, 0.3) is fixed with zero displacement in both x and y directions.
The displacement field is discretized using a continuous Lagrange vector-valued function space of polynomial order 2.
The linear variational problem is solved using a direct solver.
The displacement field and the von Mises stress field are the required outputs.
