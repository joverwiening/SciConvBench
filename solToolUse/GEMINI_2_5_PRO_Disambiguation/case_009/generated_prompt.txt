The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The problem is a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity simulation.
The domain is a rectangle defined by corners (0,0) and (1.0, 0.5) meters.
A circular hole with a radius of 0.1 meters is centered at (0.5, 0.25) meters and subtracted from the rectangle.
A 2D mesh will be generated for this domain with a boundary resolution of 64 elements.
The displacement vector function space will use continuous Lagrange elements of polynomial degree 2.
The material's Young's modulus is 70e9 Pa.
The material's Poisson's ratio is 0.33.
The material's density is 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force representing gravity is applied over the entire domain, defined as a vector (0, -2700 * 9.81).
A normal pressure of 1e5 Pa is applied to the boundary of the circular hole, directed outwards from the center of the hole.
The left boundary of the rectangle (x=0) is clamped, with displacement fixed to (0, 0).
The system will be solved for the displacement vector using a direct linear solver.
The in-plane Cauchy stress tensor will be calculated from the displacement field.
The von Mises stress will be computed from the calculated stress tensor.
The final displacement vector field and von Mises scalar field will be saved to a file for visualization.
