Use the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin) to perform the simulation.
Solve the mixed displacement-pressure formulation for a finite-strain incompressible neo-Hookean solid.
Use Newton's method to solve the non-linear system.
The geometry is a 2D rectangle defined by corners (0, 0) and (1.0, 0.2).
A circular hole of radius 0.04 is centered at (0.5, 0.1) within the rectangle.
Generate a mesh of the geometry with a characteristic element size of 0.01.
Assume a plane strain condition.
The material is neo-Hookean with Young's modulus E = 5 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.5.
Use continuous Lagrange elements of degree 2 for the displacement vector field (P2).
Use continuous Lagrange elements of degree 1 for the pressure scalar field (P1).
Apply a fixed boundary condition (u_x = 0, u_y = 0) on the left edge (x=0).
The top (y=0.2), bottom (y=0), and right (x=1.0) edges are traction-free.
Apply a uniform follower pressure of 0.10 MPa normal to the deformed boundary of the hole.
Compute the von Mises stress field from the resulting Cauchy stress tensor.
Save the displacement field to a file named `q13_displacement.xdmf`.
Create a plot of the deformed mesh, with deformation scaled by a factor of 5, and save it as `q13_def.png`.
Create a contour plot of the von Mises stress field on the deformed geometry and save it as `q13_vm.png`.
