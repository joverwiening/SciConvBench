The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The problem is a 2D small-strain linear elasticity analysis under the plane stress assumption.
The domain is an L-shape defined by the vertices (0,0), (1,0), (1,0.5), (0.5,0.5), (0.5,1), and (0,1).
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.33.
A uniform body force representing gravity is applied in the negative y-direction, calculated as (0, -ρ*g) with ρ=2700 kg/m^3 and g=9.81 m/s^2.
The left boundary, defined by x=0, is fixed with a zero-displacement Dirichlet boundary condition.
The right boundary, defined by x=1 and 0 ≤ y ≤ 0.5, is subjected to a traction vector T = (0, -2.65e4 * y).
All other boundaries, including the top, bottom, and internal re-entrant edges, are traction-free.
The domain is meshed with triangular elements using a mesh resolution of 64.
The resulting linear system of equations is solved using a direct solver.
The primary outputs to be computed are the displacement field, the Cauchy stress tensor, and the von Mises stress field.
