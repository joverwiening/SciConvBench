Solver: small-strain linear elasticity in legacy FEniCS (dolfin); solve the 2D plane-stress variational problem with a vector-valued displacement space and a direct linear solver.
Task:
Simulate the static linear elastic deformation of a cantilever plate with a circular hole in 2D plane stress. Clamp the left edge, apply a uniform downward traction on the right edge, and compute the displacement and von Mises stress fields.
Case details:
Geometry: rectangle 4.0 x 1.0 with a circular hole of radius 0.3 centred at (1.0, 0.5). Mesh resolution: 72. Function space: second-order vector Lagrange. Material: E = 210e3, ν = 0.3. Loading: right-edge line traction t = (0, -1e5) per unit thickness on x = 4.0. Boundary conditions: u = 0 on x = 0. Hole boundary traction-free. No body force.
Implementation notes:
Treat the plate as a 2D plane-stress problem with unit thickness and use the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
Leave the hole boundary traction-free and apply the downward right-edge traction as a Neumann boundary condition.
