The simulation will be implemented using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The problem to be solved is 2D small-strain linear elasticity under the plane stress assumption.
The domain is a semi-circle in the y>=0 plane, centered at the origin, with a radius of 1 meter.
The mesh will be generated with a target of 64 triangular elements.
The curved boundary of the semi-circle will be defined using 32 segments.
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa, a Poisson's ratio of 0.33, and a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
A uniform gravitational body force will be applied in the negative y-direction, with g=9.81 m/s^2.
The flat base of the semi-circle (y=0) is fixed, corresponding to a zero-displacement Dirichlet boundary condition.
An inward-acting normal pressure of 20e3 Pa is applied to the curved arc of the semi-circle.
The displacement field will be approximated using continuous Lagrange vector elements of polynomial degree 2.
The linear system will be solved using a direct solver.
The simulation will compute the displacement vector field.
The simulation will compute the von Mises stress field from the in-plane Cauchy stress components.
