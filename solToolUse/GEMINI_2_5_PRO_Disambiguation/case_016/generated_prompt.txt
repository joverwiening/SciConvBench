The simulation uses the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The physical model is 2D small-strain linear elasticity under plane-stress conditions.
The geometry is a cross shape formed by the union of a horizontal rectangle and a vertical rectangle.
The horizontal rectangle spans from x=-1 to x=1 and y=-0.3 to y=0.3.
The vertical rectangle spans from x=-0.3 to x=0.3 and y=-1 to y=1.
Four circular holes are removed from the cross-shaped domain.
The centers of the four circular holes are located at (0.6, 0), (-0.6, 0), (0, 0.6), and (0, -0.6).
The radius of each circular hole is 0.12.
The computational mesh is generated with a resolution parameter of 100.
The function space for the displacement vector is the second-order vector Lagrange element space.
The material's Young's modulus is 210000.
The material's Poisson's ratio is 0.3.
The left edge of the plate, defined by x = -1, is clamped with a zero-displacement boundary condition.
A traction vector of (0, -50000) is applied to the right edge of the plate, defined by x = 1.
The linear system is solved using a direct solver.
The simulation must compute the displacement field.
The simulation must compute the von Mises stress field as a post-processing step.
