The problem is a 2D linear elasticity analysis under the plane stress assumption.
The simulation domain is a right triangle with vertices at (0,0), (1,0), and (0,0.5).
Generate a triangular mesh of the domain with a resolution resulting in approximately 64 elements.
The displacement vector field is represented using a vector-valued function space with continuous Lagrange finite elements of polynomial degree 2.
The material's Young's Modulus is 70e9 Pa.
The material's Poisson's Ratio is 0.33.
The material's density is 2700 kg/m^3.
A constant gravitational body force of g = 9.81 m/s^2 acts in the negative y-direction.
A clamped boundary condition (u=0) is applied to the left edge of the domain (x=0).
A surface traction vector T = (0, -5e3 * sin(πx)) Pa is applied to the hypotenuse boundary (x+2y=1).
The bottom edge of the domain (y=0, for x>0) is traction-free.
Solve the variational problem using a direct linear solver.
