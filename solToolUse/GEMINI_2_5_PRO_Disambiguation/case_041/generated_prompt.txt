The simulation will be implemented using the FEniCS legacy library, dolfin.
The problem to be solved is 2D linear elasticity under the plane strain assumption.
The computational domain is a square defined by the corners (-1, -1) and (1, 1), with an internal slit along the line segment from (0, 0) to (1, 0).
The mesh will be generated with a characteristic resolution of 30, featuring refinement towards the crack tip at (0, 0).
The first Lamé parameter, lambda, is set to 0.5444837367825.
The second Lamé parameter, mu, is set to 0.36337378606, which is derived assuming a Poisson's ratio of 0.3.
The finite element formulation will use vector-valued, continuous Lagrange elements of degree 2.
An exact displacement field, u0, is defined to manufacture a solution.
The exact solution u0 is expressed in polar coordinates (r, theta) centered at the origin (0,0).
The x-component of the exact displacement is u0_x = r^q * (-cos((q+1)*theta) + (kappa+1)/(q+1) * cos((q-1)*theta)).
The y-component of the exact displacement is u0_y = r^q * (sin((q+1)*theta) - (kappa-1)/(q+1) * sin((q-1)*theta)).
The exponent q in the exact solution is 0.5430755788367.
The material constant kappa in the exact solution is 1.8, consistent with plane strain and a Poisson's ratio of 0.3.
A body force f is to be computed as f = -div(sigma(u0)), where sigma(u0) is the stress tensor corresponding to the exact displacement field.
The variational problem's right-hand side will be the integral of the dot product of the body force f and the test function v over the domain.
Dirichlet boundary conditions are to be applied on all external boundaries of the domain.
The values for the Dirichlet boundary conditions are prescribed by the exact displacement field u0.
The linear system of equations will be solved using the GMRES iterative solver.
The GMRES solver will be preconditioned using an Incomplete LU (ILU) factorization.
