The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The problem is a 2D linear elasticity analysis under the plane stress assumption.
The domain is a right triangle defined by the vertices (0,0), (1,0), and (0,0.5).
A triangular mesh of the domain will be generated with a resolution parameter of 8.
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa, a Poisson's ratio of 0.33, and a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force representing gravity, f = (0, -2700 * 9.81), is applied over the entire domain.
The boundary at y=0 is clamped, meaning displacement is fixed at (0,0).
The boundary at x=0 is a roller, meaning the x-component of displacement is fixed at 0.
A horizontal shear traction of t = (2000, 0) Pa is applied to the sloping boundary defined by x + 2y = 1.
The displacement field is solved for using a direct linear solver.
The displacement magnitude is computed from the resulting displacement vector.
The in-plane Cauchy stress tensor is computed from the displacement field.
The von Mises stress is computed from the components of the Cauchy stress tensor.
The final outputs are the displacement magnitude and von Mises stress fields.
