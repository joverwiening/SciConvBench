The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The physical model is 2D small-strain linear elasticity under plane stress conditions.
The domain is a rectangle defined by corners (0,0) and (1,0.5) meters.
A circular hole with a radius of 0.1 meters is centered at (0.5, 0.25) meters.
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa.
The material has a Poisson's ratio of 0.33.
The material has a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force representing gravity is included.
The gravitational acceleration is 9.81 m/s^2, acting in the negative y-direction.
The left boundary (x=0) is constrained to have zero displacement in the y-direction.
The right boundary (x=1) is constrained to have zero displacement in the x-direction.
A uniform compressive traction of 2500 Pa is applied to the top boundary (y=0.5).
A uniform compressive traction of 2500 Pa is applied to the bottom boundary (y=0).
The finite element mesh will have approximately 64 elements along each outer edge of the rectangle.
The linear system will be solved using a direct solver.
The displacement vector field will be computed.
The in-plane Cauchy stress tensor will be computed from the displacement field.
The von Mises stress will be computed from the Cauchy stress tensor.
