The simulation will be implemented using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The physical model is small-strain linear elasticity under the plane stress assumption.
The geometry is a 2D L-shaped domain defined by a 1x1 square with a 0.5x0.5 square removed from the top-right corner.
The mesh will be generated from a uniform 64x64 grid on the 1x1 unit square, with cells whose centroids are in the region x > 0.5 and y > 0.5 removed.
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa, a Poisson's ratio of 0.33, and a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force representing gravity acts in the negative y-direction, calculated as f = (0, -ρ*g) with g = 9.81 m/s^2.
A clamped boundary condition (u=0) is applied to the left edge (x=0) and the bottom edge (y=0).
A horizontal traction vector T = (2.65e4, 0) Pa is applied to the right edge (x=1, 0 <= y <= 0.5).
All other boundaries, including the top edge and the inner edges of the L-shape, are traction-free.
The variational problem for the displacement vector field will be solved using a direct linear solver.
After solving for displacement, the in-plane Cauchy stress tensor will be calculated.
The von Mises stress will be computed from the Cauchy stress tensor.
The final outputs of the simulation are the displacement vector field and the von Mises scalar stress field.
