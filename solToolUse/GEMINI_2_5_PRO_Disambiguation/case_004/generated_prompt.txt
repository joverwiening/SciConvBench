The simulation will be implemented using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The physical model is 2D small-strain linear elasticity under the plane stress assumption.
The geometry is a 2D polygon defined by the following 8 vertices in order: (0.0, 0.2), (0.3, 0.2), (0.3, 0.1), (0.7, 0.1), (0.7, 0.2), (1.0, 0.2), (1.0, 0.0), (0.0, 0.0).
A triangular mesh will be generated for the domain with a resolution resulting in approximately 72 elements.
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa, a Poisson's ratio of 0.33, and a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force due to gravity acts on the domain, with g = 9.81 m/s^2 in the negative y-direction.
The displacement vector field is discretized using continuous Lagrange elements of degree 2.
The bottom boundary, where y=0, is fixed with a homogeneous Dirichlet boundary condition.
An elastic foundation with a stiffness of 1e6 N/m^2 provides support on the top boundary segment where 0.3 <= x <= 0.5.
All other boundaries are traction-free.
The linear system will be solved using a direct solver.
The simulation will compute and store the displacement vector field.
The simulation will compute and store the von Mises stress field, derived from the in-plane Cauchy stress components.
The problem assumes a unit thickness for the plane stress formulation.
