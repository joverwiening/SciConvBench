The simulation will solve a 2D plane stress linear elasticity problem using the FEniCS legacy (dolfin) library.
The polygonal domain is defined by the following 8 vertices in order: (0.0, 0.0), (1.0, 0.0), (1.0, 0.2), (0.6, 0.2), (0.6, 0.4), (0.2, 0.4), (0.2, 0.2), (0.0, 0.2).
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.33.
A body force representing gravity is applied, with a density of 2700 kg/m^3 and a gravitational acceleration of 9.81 m/s^2 in the negative y-direction.
The entire bottom boundary, where y=0, is fixed with a zero-displacement Dirichlet boundary condition.
A vertical traction of -10,000 Pa is applied to the portion of the top boundary where 0.3 <= x <= 0.5.
All other boundaries are traction-free.
The domain will be meshed with a target of 72 triangular elements.
The vector-valued displacement function space will use continuous Lagrange elements of polynomial degree 2.
The linear system will be solved using a direct solver.
The simulation will compute the displacement magnitude field.
The simulation will compute the von Mises stress field from the in-plane Cauchy stress components.
