The simulation will be executed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The problem to be solved is 2D small-strain linear elasticity.
The variational formulation will be for plane stress.
The geometry is a rectangular domain defined by the corners (0, 0) and (1.0, 0.2) meters.
A circular hole with a radius of 0.05 meters is centered within the rectangular domain at (0.5, 0.1).
The material has a Young's modulus of 200 GPa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.3.
The left boundary of the rectangle (x=0) is fixed, with displacement u=(0,0).
The right boundary of the rectangle (x=1.0) has an applied traction of t=(2e6, 0) N/m.
All other boundaries, including the top, bottom, and the circular hole, are traction-free.
The domain will be discretized with a mesh having a characteristic element size of 0.01.
The vector-valued displacement function space will use Lagrange elements of polynomial degree 2.
The linear system will be solved using a direct solver.
The displacement field will be saved to an XDMF file named `q3_displacement.xdmf`.
The von Mises stress field will be computed.
The von Mises stress field will be saved as an image file named `q3_vm.png`.
The maximum von Mises stress on the boundary of the circular hole will be calculated and reported.
The stress concentration factor, Kt, will be calculated by dividing the maximum von Mises stress by 2 MPa and reported.
