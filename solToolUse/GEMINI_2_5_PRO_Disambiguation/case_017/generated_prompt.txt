The simulation will be implemented using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The physical model is small-strain linear elasticity.
The problem is solved as a 2D plane-stress variational problem.
The domain is an L-shaped polygon defined by the vertices (0,0), (1,0), (1,0.5), (0.5,0.5), (0.5,1), and (0,1).
The domain has a unit thickness of 1.0.
A triangular mesh of the domain will be generated with a resolution parameter of 64.
The displacement vector field will be discretized using continuous, second-order Lagrange elements.
Young's modulus is 70e9 Pa.
Poisson's ratio is 0.33.
Density is 2700 kg/m^3.
The plane-stress constitutive relation will be used.
A body force due to gravity is applied, with g = 9.81 m/s^2 acting in the negative y-direction.
The left boundary, defined by x=0, is fixed with a displacement of (0,0).
A vertical point load of -2.65e4 N is applied at the coordinate (0.5, 0.5) using a PointSource.
The resulting linear system will be solved using a direct solver.
The in-plane Cauchy stress tensor field will be computed from the displacement solution.
The von Mises stress will be computed from the Cauchy stress tensor.
The displacement field and von Mises stress field will be saved for visualization.
