The simulation solves a small-strain von Mises elastoplasticity problem in FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem domain is a 2D quarter plate defined by the rectangle [0, 100] x [0, 180] mm, with a quarter-circular hole of radius 50 mm centered at the origin.
The analysis is performed under plane-strain conditions.
The material is governed by perfect plasticity with an associative flow rule.
The Lame parameters are lambda = 19.44 GPa and mu = 29.17 GPa.
The von Mises yield stress is sigma_Y = 243 MPa.
The mesh is unstructured with a characteristic element size of 5 mm.
The displacement field is discretized using continuous Lagrange elements of order 2.
A symmetry boundary condition (u_x = 0) is applied on the boundary x = 0.
A symmetry boundary condition (u_y = 0) is applied on the boundary y = 0.
The boundaries at x = 100 mm and on the circular hole are traction-free.
A total vertical displacement of 1 mm is applied to the top boundary at y = 180 mm.
The total displacement is applied over 40 equal increments.
Each load increment is solved using a Newton-Raphson method.
The Newton solver uses a relative tolerance of 1e-8 and an absolute tolerance of 1e-9.
The plasticity update is performed using a return-mapping algorithm.
The final displacement field at the end of the last increment is saved to a file in XDMF format.
