The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The physical model is 2D small-strain linear elasticity under the plane stress assumption.
The domain is a right triangle with vertices at (0,0), (1,0), and (0,0.5).
The triangular domain will be meshed with approximately 64 elements.
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.33.
A body force due to gravity is applied, with a density of 2700 kg/m^3 and gravitational acceleration of 9.81 m/s^2 in the negative y-direction.
The vertical boundary at x=0 is clamped, with zero displacement in all directions.
The horizontal boundary at y=0 is a free surface with zero applied traction.
A normal traction of 3000 Pa is applied to the sloping boundary defined by the line x + 2y = 1.
The variational problem for the vector displacement field will be solved using a direct linear solver.
The simulation will compute the displacement magnitude field.
The simulation will compute the von Mises stress field from the in-plane Cauchy stress components.
