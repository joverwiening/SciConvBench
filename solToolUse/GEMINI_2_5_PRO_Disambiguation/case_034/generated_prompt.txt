Use the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
Solve a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem.
The domain is a polygon defined by the vertices (0,0), (1,0), (1,1), (0.5,1), (0.5,0.5), and (0,0.5) in order.
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.33.
The material has a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force representing gravity is applied in the negative y-direction, with g=9.81 m/s^2.
The left boundary, where x=0, is fixed with a zero-displacement Dirichlet condition.
A linearly varying vertical traction is applied to the right boundary, where x=1.
The traction vector on the right boundary is (0, -2.65e4 * y) Pa.
The top, bottom, and inner boundaries of the domain are traction-free.
Generate a triangular mesh of the domain with a resolution of 64.
The problem is solved for the vector displacement field.
Use a direct linear solver to solve the system of equations.
Assume a unit thickness for the plane-stress formulation.
After solving for displacement, compute the in-plane Cauchy stress tensor.
From the Cauchy stress tensor, compute the von Mises stress field.
