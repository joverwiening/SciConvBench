The simulation will be implemented using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
Solve a static, nonlinear, finite-strain elasticity problem on a UnitCube mesh with a resolution of 8x8x8 cells.
The material model is Saint-Venant-Kirchhoff.
The shear modulus mu is a constant with a value of 3.8461 N/m^2.
The Lamé parameter lambda is spatially varying, defined by the expression lambda = 5.8*x1 + 5.7*(1 - x1).
The boundary face at x1=0 is fixed with a zero-displacement condition.
The boundary face at x1=1 is subjected to a prescribed displacement corresponding to a 60-degree rotation.
The axis of rotation for the x1=1 face is the line parallel to the x1-axis passing through x0=0.5 and x2=0.5.
The nonlinear variational problem will be solved using Newton's method.
