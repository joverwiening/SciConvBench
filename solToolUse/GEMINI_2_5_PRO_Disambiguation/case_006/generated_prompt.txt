The simulation will be implemented using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The physical model is 2D small-strain linear elasticity under the plane stress assumption.
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa, a Poisson's ratio of 0.33, and a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
A uniform gravity load of 9.81 m/s^2 acts in the negative y-direction.
The domain is a 2D polygon defined by the vertices (0.0, 0.0), (0.8, 0.0), (0.8, 0.2), (0.5, 0.2), (0.5, 0.4), (0.3, 0.4), (0.3, 0.2), and (0.0, 0.2).
The domain is meshed with approximately 72 triangular elements.
The bottom boundary, defined by y=0, is fixed with zero displacement in all directions.
The vertical boundary segment at x=0.3 between y=0.2 and y=0.4 is constrained to have zero displacement in the y-direction.
The vertical boundary segment at x=0.5 between y=0.2 and y=0.4 is constrained to have zero displacement in the y-direction.
A horizontal traction of 5000 Pa is applied on the top boundary segment, defined by y=0.4 between x=0.3 and x=0.5.
The displacement is solved for using a direct linear solver.
The final outputs are the computed displacement vector field and the von Mises stress scalar field.
