Use the FEniCS legacy (dolfin) library for the simulation.
The analysis is a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem under the plane stress assumption.
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.33.
The material has a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
The domain is a parallelogram with vertices at (0,0), (1,0), (1.2,0.3), and (0.2,0.3).
The mesh is a uniform grid of 72 triangular elements, created by dividing the parallelogram into a 6x6 grid of cells.
The function space for the vector displacement field is composed of continuous Lagrange elements of polynomial degree 2.
A body force due to gravity acts in the negative y-direction, with g=9.81 m/s^2.
A horizontal point load of -5000 N is applied at the coordinate (1.1, 0.15).
The boundary corresponding to the edge between vertices (0,0) and (0.2,0.3) is clamped, enforcing zero displacement.
Solve the linear variational problem using a direct solver.
Compute the displacement field vector.
Compute the von Mises stress distribution from the in-plane Cauchy stress tensor.
