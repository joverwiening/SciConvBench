Use the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
Solve a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem under the plane stress assumption.
The geometry is a polygon defined by the vertices (0,0), (0.8,0), (0.8,0.2), (0.5,0.2), (0.5,0.5), (0.3,0.5), (0.3,0.2), and (0,0.2).
The mesh should contain approximately 72 elements.
The material's Young's modulus is 70e9 Pa.
The material's Poisson's ratio is 0.33.
The material's density is 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force due to gravity is applied, with g = 9.81 m/s^2 acting in the negative y-direction.
A vertical point load of -5000 N is applied at the coordinate (0.4, 0.5).
The bottom boundary, from (0,0) to (0.8,0), is fixed with a zero-displacement Dirichlet boundary condition.
The vector-valued displacement function space uses second-order Lagrange elements.
The linear system is solved using a direct solver.
The primary outputs are the displacement magnitude field and the von Mises stress field.
The von Mises stress is calculated from the in-plane Cauchy stress components.
