The simulation will be implemented using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The problem is a 2D linear elasticity analysis under the plane stress assumption.
The geometry is a semi-circle in the upper half-plane (y>=0) with a radius of 1 meter, centered at the origin (0,0).
A mesh of the semi-circular domain will be generated with approximately 64 triangular elements.
The material is linear elastic and isotropic with a Young's modulus of 70 GPa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.33.
The material has a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force due to gravity (g = 9.81 m/s^2) is applied in the negative y-direction.
A concentrated vertical point load of -5000 N is applied at the apex of the semi-circle, at the coordinate (0, 1).
The base of the semi-circle (the boundary at y=0) is fixed, meaning displacement is zero in all directions.
The displacement field will be discretized using continuous Lagrange elements of polynomial degree 2.
The resulting linear system of equations will be solved using a direct solver.
The simulation will compute the vector displacement field over the domain.
The simulation will compute the von Mises stress field from the resulting in-plane Cauchy stress components.
