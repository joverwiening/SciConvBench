The simulation will be implemented using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The problem to be solved is 2D small-strain linear elasticity under plane stress conditions.
The computational domain is an L-shaped polygon with vertices at (0,0), (1,0), (1,0.5), (0.5,0.5), (0.5,1), and (0,1).
The domain will be meshed with a uniform triangular mesh corresponding to a resolution of 64 elements per unit length edge.
The displacement vector field will be discretized using continuous Lagrange elements of polynomial degree 2.
The material's Young's modulus is 70e9 Pa.
The material's Poisson's ratio is 0.33.
The material's density is 2700 kg/m^3.
A uniform body force representing gravity acts on the domain, with g=9.81 m/s^2 directed in the negative y-direction.
The left boundary, defined by x=0, is fixed with a zero-displacement Dirichlet boundary condition.
A shear traction vector of (2.65e4, 0) Pa is applied on the boundary defined by y=1 and x > 0.5.
The plane stress constitutive law will be used to relate stress and strain.
The simulation will solve the standard linear elasticity variational problem for the displacement vector field.
The resulting linear system of equations will be solved using a direct solver.
After solving for the displacement field, the in-plane Cauchy stress tensor will be computed.
The von Mises stress will be calculated from the Cauchy stress tensor.
The magnitude of the displacement vector will be calculated.
The displacement magnitude will be visualized as a contour plot.
The von Mises stress will be visualized as a contour plot.
