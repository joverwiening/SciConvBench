The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The problem to be solved is 2D small-strain linear elasticity under the plane stress assumption.
The geometry is a rectangular plate of 1.0m width and 0.5m height.
A circular hole with a radius of 0.1m is centered at (0.5, 0.25).
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.33.
The material has a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force due to gravity (g = 9.81 m/s^2) acts in the negative y-direction.
The left edge of the plate (x=0) is fixed, with zero displacement in all directions.
A sinusoidal vertical traction T_y = -6000*sin(4*pi*y) Pa is applied to the right edge (x=1).
A constant shear traction T_x = 1000 Pa is applied to the top edge (y=0.5).
The bottom edge of the plate (y=0) is traction-free.
The boundary of the circular hole is traction-free.
The mesh resolution should be equivalent to 64 elements per edge of the exterior rectangle.
The displacement field is represented by a vector-valued function space.
The linear system will be solved using a direct solver.
The simulation will compute the displacement vector field.
The simulation will compute the in-plane Cauchy stress tensor field.
The simulation will compute the von Mises stress field from the Cauchy stress.
