The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The problem is a 2D small-strain linear elasticity analysis under plane stress conditions.
The domain is a parallelogram defined by the vertices (0,0), (1,0), (1.2,0.3), and (0.2,0.3).
The domain will be meshed with 72 triangular elements.
The material has a Young's modulus E of 70e9 Pa, a Poisson's ratio ν of 0.33, and a density ρ of 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force representing gravity is applied in the negative y-direction, calculated with g = 9.81 m/s^2.
The left sloped edge, connecting vertices (0,0) and (0.2,0.3), is fixed with zero displacement in all directions.
The right sloped edge, connecting vertices (1,0) and (1.2,0.3), is fixed with zero displacement in all directions.
A constant, vertically downward normal traction of 5000 Pa is applied to the top horizontal edge, connecting (0.2,0.3) and (1.2,0.3).
The vector function space for the displacement solution will use continuous Lagrange elements of polynomial degree 2.
The linear system will be solved using a direct solver.
The simulation will compute the displacement field.
The simulation will compute the von Mises stress distribution from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
