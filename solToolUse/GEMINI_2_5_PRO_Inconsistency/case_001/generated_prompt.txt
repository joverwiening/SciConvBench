The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The problem is a 2D small-strain linear elasticity analysis under plane stress conditions.
The geometry is a closed polygon defined by the vertices (0,0), (0.8,0), (0.8,0.2), (0.5,0.2), (0.5,0.5), (0.3,0.5), (0.3,0.2), and (0,0.2).
The domain will be meshed with a resolution that generates approximately 72 triangular elements.
The material has a Young's modulus E of 70e9 Pa, a Poisson's ratio ν of 0.33, and a density ρ of 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force representing gravity acts in the negative y-direction, with g = 9.81 m/s^2.
The bottom boundary of the domain, where y=0, is fixed with a zero-displacement Dirichlet condition.
A linearly varying vertical traction is applied to the top boundary segment defined by 0.3 ≤ x ≤ 0.5 and y=0.5.
The traction vector is T = (0, -8000*x).
The vector-valued displacement function space will use continuous Lagrange elements of polynomial order 2.
The linear system will be solved using a direct solver with LU decomposition.
The final outputs will be the computed displacement field and the von Mises stress distribution.
