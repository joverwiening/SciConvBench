The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The analysis solves a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem.
The domain is a parallelogram defined by the vertices (0,0), (1,0), (1.2,0.3), and (0.2,0.3).
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.33.
The material has a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force due to gravity of 9.81 m/s^2 is applied in the negative y-direction.
A horizontal point load of -5000 N is applied at the coordinate (1.1, 0.15) using a PointSource.
The left inclined boundary, connecting (0,0) and (0.2,0.3), is fixed with a zero-displacement Dirichlet boundary condition.
The top, bottom, and right boundaries are traction-free.
The domain is meshed with 72 triangular elements.
The vector-valued displacement field is discretized using continuous Lagrange elements of polynomial degree 2.
The linear system is solved using a direct solver.
The simulation will compute the displacement vector field.
The simulation will compute the von Mises stress field from the in-plane Cauchy stress components.
