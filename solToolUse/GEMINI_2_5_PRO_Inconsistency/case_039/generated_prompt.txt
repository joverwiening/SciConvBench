The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The physical model is 2D small-strain linear elasticity under the plane stress assumption.
The geometry is a rectangle defined by corners at (0, 0) and (1.0, 0.5) meters.
A circular hole with a radius of 0.1 meters is centered at (0.5, 0.25) meters.
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.33.
The material has a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
The mesh will be generated for the perforated plate geometry using the mshr tool with a global resolution of 64.
A Dirichlet boundary condition of `uy = 0` is applied to the left edge of the plate (x=0).
A Dirichlet boundary condition of `ux = 0` is applied to the right edge of the plate (x=1).
The boundary of the circular hole is traction-free.
A uniform vertical compressive traction equivalent to a stress of -2500 Pa is applied to the top edge (y=0.5).
A uniform vertical compressive traction equivalent to a stress of -2500 Pa is applied to the bottom edge (y=0).
A body force due to gravity (g = 9.81 m/s^2) is included, acting in the negative y-direction.
The displacement field is solved for using a direct linear solver.
The displacement field and the von Mises stress field will be computed and saved as the primary outputs.
