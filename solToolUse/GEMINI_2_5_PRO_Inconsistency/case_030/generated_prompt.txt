The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The problem is a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem under the plane stress assumption.
The geometry is an L-shaped domain defined by a 1x1 square with a 0.5x0.5 square removed from the top-right corner.
The mesh will be generated with a resolution of 64 elements along the outer edges of length 1.0 and 32 elements along the inner edges of length 0.5.
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa, a Poisson's ratio of 0.33, and a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
A gravitational body force corresponding to g=9.81 m/s^2 will be applied in the negative y-direction.
The outer left edge (x=0) is clamped, with zero displacement.
The outer bottom edge (y=0) is clamped, with zero displacement.
A horizontal traction vector of (2.65e4, 0) Pa is applied to the right edge (x=1).
All other boundaries, including the inner edges forming the re-entrant corner, are traction-free.
The vector-valued displacement function space will use continuous Lagrange elements of polynomial degree 2.
The linear system will be solved using a direct solver.
The simulation will compute the displacement field and the von Mises stress field.
The von Mises stress will be calculated from the in-plane Cauchy stress components.
