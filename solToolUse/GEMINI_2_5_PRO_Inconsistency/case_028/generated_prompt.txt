The simulation uses the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The problem is a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity analysis.
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa, a Poisson's ratio of 0.33, and a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
The geometry is an L-shaped domain defined by the vertices (0,0), (1,0), (1,1), (0.5,1), (0.5,0.5), and (0,0.5).
The mesh is generated with a resolution of 64 elements along the exterior boundary.
A body force representing gravity is applied in the negative y-direction, with g=9.81 m/s^2.
The left boundary, where x=0, is fixed with a zero-displacement Dirichlet condition.
A horizontal shear traction of 2.65e4 Pa is applied in the positive x-direction on the top-right boundary, defined by y=1 and x>0.5.
The displacement field is solved for using a vector-valued function space.
The linear system is solved using a direct solver.
The in-plane Cauchy stress tensor is computed from the displacement solution.
The von Mises stress is calculated from the in-plane Cauchy stress components.
The final output includes visualizations of the displacement magnitude and the von Mises stress field.
