The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy (dolfin) library.
The problem is a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem under the plane stress assumption.
The geometry is an L-shaped domain created by taking a 1x1 square with its bottom-left corner at (0,0) and removing the 0.5x0.5 square from the top-right quadrant.
The domain will be meshed with triangular elements, with a resolution resulting in approximately 64 elements along the outer edges of length 1.0.
The material has a Young's modulus E = 70e9 Pa, a Poisson's ratio ν = 0.33, and a density ρ = 2700 kg/m^3.
The left boundary, defined by x=0, is fixed with a zero-displacement Dirichlet boundary condition.
All other boundaries are traction-free.
A body force representing gravity is applied in the negative y-direction, defined by the vector f = (0, -ρ*g), where g = 9.81 m/s^2.
A concentrated point load of -2.65e4 N is applied in the y-direction at the re-entrant corner (0.5, 0.5) using a `PointSource`.
The variational problem for the vector displacement field will be solved using a direct linear solver.
After solving for the displacement, the in-plane Cauchy stress tensor will be computed.
The von Mises stress will be calculated from the Cauchy stress tensor components.
The simulation assumes a unit thickness of 1.0 for the 2D domain.
