The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The analysis will solve a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem under the plane stress assumption.
The geometry is a rectangular plate of 1.0m by 0.5m.
A circular hole with a radius of 0.1m is centered at (0.5, 0.25).
The material has a Young's modulus E=70e9 Pa, a Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, and a density ρ=2700 kg/m^3.
A mesh will be generated with a resolution of approximately 64 elements along the outer boundary edges.
The left edge of the plate (x=0) is clamped, such that displacement is zero in all directions.
The top edge (y=0.5), bottom edge (y=0), and the boundary of the circular hole are traction-free.
The right edge of the plate (x=1) is also traction-free.
A body force representing gravity, f = (0, -ρ*g), is applied over the entire domain, with g=9.81 m/s^2.
A point load of -3000 N in the x-direction is applied at the coordinate (1, 0.2) using a PointSource.
A point load of +3000 N in the x-direction is applied at the coordinate (1, 0.3) using a PointSource.
The variational problem will be solved for the displacement vector field using a direct linear solver.
After solving, the displacement field and the von Mises stress will be computed and visualized.
