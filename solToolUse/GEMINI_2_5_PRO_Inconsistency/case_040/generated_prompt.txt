The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The problem is a quasi-incompressible finite-strain hyperelasticity problem solved with a mixed displacement-pressure formulation.
The geometry is a rectangular strip defined by the domain (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) meters.
Two circular holes of radius 0.04 m are removed from the strip.
The first hole is centered at (0.40, 0.10) m.
The second hole is centered at (0.60, 0.10) m.
The domain is discretized with an unstructured triangle mesh with a characteristic element size of 0.005 m.
The displacement field is discretized using continuous vector-valued Lagrange elements of degree 2.
The pressure field is discretized using continuous Lagrange elements of degree 1.
The material model is a quasi-incompressible neo-Hookean solid under plane strain conditions.
The material has a Young's modulus E = 5 MPa and a Poisson's ratio ν = 0.49.
The left edge of the strip (x = 0) is fixed, with displacement components u_x and u_y set to zero.
The right edge of the strip (x = 1.0) has a prescribed displacement of u_x = 0.060 m and u_y = 0 m.
The top and bottom edges of the strip are traction-free.
A uniform follower pressure of P_hole = 0.10 MPa is applied to the boundary of each hole.
The follower pressure load is calculated using the normals of the undeformed reference geometry.
The prescribed displacement and follower pressures are applied incrementally over 10 equal steps.
The non-linear system at each step is solved using Newton's method.
The Newton solver will use an absolute tolerance of 1e-9, a relative tolerance of 1e-10, and a maximum of 20 iterations.
The final displacement field is saved to a file in XDMF format.
A color map of the final hydrostatic pressure field is saved as q14_p.png.
The Cauchy stress tensor is computed from the final solution.
The von Mises stress is calculated from the Cauchy stress tensor.
A color map of the final von Mises stress field is saved as q14_vm.png.
