The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The problem to be solved is 2D small-strain linear elasticity.
The analysis will assume plane stress conditions with a unit thickness of 1.0.
The geometry is an L-shaped domain defined by the vertices (0,0), (1,0), (1,0.5), (0.5,0.5), (0.5,1), and (0,1).
An unstructured triangular mesh will be generated with a resolution parameter of 64.
The material's Young's modulus is 70e9 Pa and its Poisson's ratio is 0.33.
The material density is 2700 kg/m^3.
A gravitational body force of g=9.81 m/s^2 acts in the negative y-direction.
The left boundary, where x=0, is fixed with a zero-displacement Dirichlet condition.
A varying traction is applied to the right boundary, defined by x=1 and 0 <= y <= 0.5.
The applied traction vector is (0, T_y), where the magnitude is T_y = -2.65e4 * y Pa.
All other boundaries, including the top, bottom, and internal edges, are traction-free.
The linear variational problem will be solved for the displacement field using a direct solver.
The in-plane Cauchy stress tensor will be computed from the displacement field.
The von Mises stress will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress tensor.
