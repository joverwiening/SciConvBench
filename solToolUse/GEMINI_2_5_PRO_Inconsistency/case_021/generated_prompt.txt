Case ID is case_021.
Use the FEniCS legacy (dolfin) library.
The simulation solves a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem.
The model is based on the plane stress assumption.
The geometry is a rectangle with dimensions 1.0m by 0.5m.
A circular hole with a radius of 0.1m is centered at (0.5m, 0.25m).
The material's Young's modulus is 70e9 Pa.
The material's Poisson's ratio is 0.33.
The material's density is 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force representing gravity is applied, with g = 9.81 m/s^2 acting in the negative y-direction.
The mesh should be generated with a resolution equivalent to 64 elements along the 1m edge.
The left boundary of the plate (x=0) is fixed, with zero displacement in all directions.
The top boundary of the plate (y=0.5) has a constant shear traction of 1000 Pa applied in the positive x-direction.
The right boundary of the plate (x=1) has a sinusoidal vertical traction applied, defined by the expression T_y = -6000 * sin(4 * pi * y) Pa.
The bottom boundary of the plate (y=0) is traction-free.
The boundary of the circular hole is traction-free.
The problem shall be solved using a direct linear solver.
The primary outputs are the displacement field and the von Mises stress field.
