The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The problem is a 2D small-strain linear elasticity analysis under the plane stress assumption.
The geometry is a rectangle of size 1.0m by 0.5m.
A circular hole with a radius of 0.1m is centered at (0.5m, 0.25m).
The mesh for this geometry will be generated programmatically.
The mesh will consist of approximately 72 triangular elements.
The mesh should be generated with a higher element density near the circular hole.
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.33.
The material has a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force due to gravity (g = 9.81 m/s^2) acts in the negative y-direction.
The left edge of the rectangle (x=0) is fixed, with zero displacement in all directions.
A uniform traction of (0, -10000) Pa is applied to the right edge of the rectangle (x=1).
The top, bottom, and circular hole boundaries are traction-free.
The displacement vector function space will use continuous Lagrange elements of polynomial degree 2.
The linear system will be solved using a direct solver.
The simulation will compute the displacement vector field.
The simulation will compute the von Mises stress field from the in-plane Cauchy stress components.
The resulting displacement and von Mises stress fields will be saved to XDMF files for visualization.
