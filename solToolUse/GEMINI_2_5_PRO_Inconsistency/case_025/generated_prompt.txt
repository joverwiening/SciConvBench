The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The analysis solves a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem under plane stress conditions.
The polygonal domain is defined by the vertices (0,0), (1,0), (1,0.1), (0.5,0.1), (0.5,0.3), (0.3,0.3), (0.3,0.1), and (0,0.1).
The domain is meshed with approximately 72 triangular elements.
The material properties are Young's modulus E=70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν=0.33, and density ρ=2700 kg/m^3.
A uniform body force representing gravity acts in the negative y-direction, with g=9.81 m/s^2.
The boundary at y=0 is fixed, with zero displacement in all directions.
A uniform vertical traction of -10 kPa is applied to the top horizontal boundaries located at y=0.3 for 0.3<=x<=0.5, at y=0.1 for 0<=x<=0.3, and at y=0.1 for 0.5<=x<=1.
The linear system is solved using a direct solver.
The simulation must compute the displacement magnitude field.
The simulation must compute the von Mises stress field from the in-plane Cauchy stress components.
