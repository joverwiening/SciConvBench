The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The physical model is 2D small-strain linear elasticity under the plane stress assumption.
The geometry is an L-shaped domain defined by the vertices (0,0), (1,0), (1,1), (0.5,1), (0.5,0.5), and (0,0.5).
The mesh will be a uniform triangular mesh with a resolution corresponding to 64 elements along each outer unit edge.
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.33.
A body force representing gravity is applied, calculated as f = (0, -rho*g) with rho=2700 kg/m^3 and g=9.81 m/s^2.
The left boundary, defined by x=0, is fixed with a zero-displacement Dirichlet boundary condition.
A linearly varying vertical traction T = (0, -2.65e4 * y) is applied on the outer right boundary, defined by x=1.
All other boundaries, including the inner re-entrant boundaries, are traction-free.
The linear system will be solved using a direct solver.
The primary outputs are the displacement vector field and the von Mises stress scalar field, computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress tensor.
