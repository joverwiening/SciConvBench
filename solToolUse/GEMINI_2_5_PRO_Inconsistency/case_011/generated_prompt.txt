The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
Solve a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem under the plane stress assumption.
The geometry is a rectangle with corners at (0,0) and (1.0, 0.5).
A circular hole of radius 0.1 is centered at (0.5, 0.25).
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.33.
The material has a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
The finite element space for the vector displacement is composed of continuous Lagrange elements of polynomial degree 2.
The mesh will be generated with 64 element divisions along the edges parallel to the x-axis and 32 element divisions along the edges parallel to the y-axis.
The left boundary of the rectangle (x=0) is clamped, meaning all displacement components are zero.
A body force due to gravity is applied, with the gravitational acceleration vector defined as (0, -9.81) m/s^2.
A pressure of 1e5 Pa is applied to the boundary of the circular hole.
The pressure acts as a traction force pointing into the solid material, defined by the vector t = -p*n, where p is the pressure magnitude and n is the outward unit normal from the solid surface.
The linear system of equations will be solved using a direct solver.
The simulation must compute the displacement field.
The simulation must compute the Cauchy stress tensor field.
The simulation must compute the von Mises stress field from the in-plane stress components.
The results, including displacement and von Mises stress, should be saved to a file for visualization.
