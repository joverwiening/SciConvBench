The simulation will be implemented using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
Solve a 2D linear elasticity problem under the plane stress assumption.
The domain is a semi-circle in the y>=0 plane with a radius of 1.0 meter, centered at the origin (0,0).
Generate a mesh of the domain containing exactly 64 triangular elements.
The circular arc of the semi-circle boundary will be approximated using 32 segments for mesh generation.
Use a vector-valued function space with continuous Lagrange elements of polynomial degree 2 for the displacement field.
The material has a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa, a Poisson's ratio of 0.33, and a density of 2700 kg/m^3.
Apply a Dirichlet boundary condition to fix the displacement to (0, 0) on the boundary defined by y=0.
Include a body force representing gravity, calculated as a vector (0, -2700 * 9.81) N/m^3.
Apply a concentrated vertical load of -5000 N at the apex point (0, 1) using a PointSource on the second component of the function space.
Formulate the variational problem using the plane stress constitutive law.
Solve the resulting linear system using a direct solver.
Compute the displacement solution vector.
Compute the in-plane Cauchy stress tensor field from the displacement solution.
Compute the von Mises stress field from the Cauchy stress tensor.
The final outputs are the computed displacement and von Mises stress distributions.
