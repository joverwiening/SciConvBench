The simulation will be performed using FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is a finite-strain Saint-Venant-Kirchhoff elasticity analysis in plane strain.
The solver will use Newton iterations within a load-stepping scheme.
The geometry is a rectangle defined by (0, 1.20) x (0, 0.20) meters.
The rectangle contains three circular holes of radius 0.03 meters.
The hole centers are located at (0.30, 0.10), (0.60, 0.10), and (0.90, 0.10) meters.
The material is defined by Lamé parameters lambda = 5.769 MPa and mu = 3.846 MPa.
The constitutive law is S = lambda*tr(E_GL)*I + 2*mu*E_GL, where E_GL is the Green-Lagrange strain tensor.
The displacement function space will use continuous Lagrange elements of polynomial degree 2.
The characteristic mesh element size will be 0.01 meters.
The left boundary (x=0) is fixed with displacement u = (0, 0).
The right boundary (x=1.20) has a prescribed displacement of u_x = 0.012 m and u_y = 0.
The top, bottom, and hole boundaries are traction-free.
The total prescribed displacement will be applied over 10 equal load steps.
The simulation will stop advancing load steps if the maximum principal Green-Lagrange strain, E_max, exceeds a value of 0.03.
A plot of the deformed configuration will be saved as q15_def.png.
A plot of the maximum principal Green-Lagrange strain will be saved as q15_Emax.png.
A plot of the deviatoric second Piola-Kirchhoff stress invariant will be saved as q15_vmS.png.
The final displacement field u will be exported to an XDMF file.
The final maximum principal Green-Lagrange strain field E_max will be exported to an XDMF file.
