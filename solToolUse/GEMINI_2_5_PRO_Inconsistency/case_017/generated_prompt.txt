The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The problem is a 2D small-strain linear elasticity simulation under plane-stress conditions.
The geometry is a cross shape formed by the union of two rectangles, with four circular holes removed.
The horizontal rectangle spans from x=-1 to x=1 and y=-0.3 to y=0.3.
The vertical rectangle spans from y=-1 to y=1 and x=-0.3 to x=0.3.
Four circular holes, each with a radius of 0.12, are centered at (0.6, 0), (-0.6, 0), (0, 0.6), and (0, -0.6).
A 2D triangular mesh will be generated for this geometry.
The mesh resolution parameter will be set to 100, corresponding to the number of cells along the longest dimension of the domain.
The material has a Young's modulus E of 210000 and a Poisson's ratio ν of 0.3.
The displacement solution space is a vector-valued function space using second-order continuous Lagrange elements.
A clamped boundary condition, u = (0, 0), is applied to the left edge of the cross, defined by x = -1.
A downward traction vector, t = (0, -5e4), is applied to the right edge of the cross, defined by x = 1.
The boundaries of the four circular holes are traction-free.
The linear system will be solved using a direct solver.
The simulation will compute the displacement vector field.
The simulation will compute the von Mises stress field as a post-processing step.
