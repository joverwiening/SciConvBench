The simulation uses the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The problem is a 2D static linear elasticity analysis under plane stress conditions.
The geometry is a rectangle defined by corners (0,0) and (4,1), with a circular hole of radius 0.3 centered at (1.0, 0.5).
The mesh is generated such that the circular hole boundary is discretized with 72 segments.
The material is isotropic with Young's modulus E = 210e3 and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.3.
The plane stress constitutive law is used, with Lamé parameters λ = (E*ν)/(1-ν^2) and μ = E/(2*(1+ν)).
The function space for the vector displacement field is the second-order Lagrange element family.
A Dirichlet boundary condition clamps the left edge (x=0), setting the displacement vector to (0,0).
A Neumann boundary condition applies a uniform downward traction vector t = (0, -1e5) on the right edge (x=4.0).
All other boundaries, including the hole, top, and bottom edges, are traction-free.
There are no body forces.
The linear variational problem is solved using a direct solver.
The simulation must compute the displacement vector field.
The simulation must compute the von Mises stress field.
