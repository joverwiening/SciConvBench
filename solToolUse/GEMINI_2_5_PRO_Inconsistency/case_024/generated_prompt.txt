The simulation solves a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem.
The simulation environment is FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The geometry is a rectangular domain defined by the corners (0, 0) and (1.0, 0.20) meters.
A circular hole with a radius of 0.05 meters is centered at (0.50, 0.10) meters.
The material is linear elastic with Young's modulus E = 200 GPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.30.
The finite element mesh has a characteristic element size of 0.005 near the hole boundary, transitioning to 0.02 at the external boundaries.
The displacement field is discretized using continuous Lagrange elements of polynomial degree 2.
A Dirichlet boundary condition fixes the displacement to (0, 0) on the left edge (x = 0).
A Neumann boundary condition applies a traction vector of (2.0e6, 0.0) N/m^2 on the right edge (x = 1.0).
The top edge (y = 0.20), bottom edge (y = 0), and the circular hole boundary are traction-free.
The linear system is solved using a direct solver.
The von Mises stress field is computed from the displacement solution.
The von Mises stress field is saved as a PNG image file named `q3_vm.png`.
The displacement vector field is saved in XDMF format to a file named `q3_displacement.xdmf`.
The maximum von Mises stress on the circular hole boundary is calculated and reported.
The stress concentration factor, Kt, is calculated as the maximum von Mises stress on the hole boundary divided by 2.0e6 Pa, and reported.
