Use the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The simulation domain is a unit cube.
The mesh is a UnitCubeMesh with 16 cells in each direction.
The function space for displacement is a VectorFunctionSpace using first-order Lagrange elements.
The material is compressible neo-Hookean.
The Young's modulus E is 10.0.
The Poisson's ratio nu is 0.3.
The Lamé parameters mu and lambda shall be derived from E and nu.
The kinematic variables are the deformation gradient F, the right Cauchy-Green tensor C, its first invariant Ic, and the determinant of the deformation gradient J.
The strain energy density function is psi = (mu/2)*(Ic - 3) - mu*ln(J) + (lmbda/2)*(ln(J))**2.
The total potential energy of the system must be minimized.
A constant body force b = (0, -0.5, 0) is applied over the entire domain.
A constant traction h = (0.1, 0, 0) is applied on the boundary face defined by x = 1.
The boundary face defined by x = 0 is clamped, with zero displacement.
The nonlinear problem is to be solved using Newton's method.
