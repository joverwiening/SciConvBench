The simulation uses FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
The problem is incompressible finite-strain hyperelasticity.
The model is a mixed displacement-pressure neo-Hookean formulation.
The nonlinear system is solved with Newton's method.
The geometry is a rectangle defined by corners (0, 0) and (1.0, 0.20) meters.
A circular hole with a radius of 0.04 meters is centered at (0.50, 0.10) meters.
The model assumes a plane strain condition.
Use continuous Lagrange elements of polynomial degree 2 for the displacement field.
Use continuous Lagrange elements of polynomial degree 1 for the pressure field.
The characteristic element size for the mesh is 0.01 meters.
The material is an isotropic incompressible neo-Hookean solid.
The Young's modulus E is 5 MPa.
The Poisson's ratio ν is 0.5.
The left edge (x=0) is fixed, with zero displacement in all directions.
The right, top, and bottom edges are traction-free.
A uniform follower pressure of 0.10 MPa is applied to the hole boundary.
The follower pressure is applied normal to the deformed hole surface.
The von Mises stress is computed from the Cauchy stress tensor.
A plot of the von Mises stress field is saved as q13_vm.png.
A plot of the deformed configuration, with deformation magnified by a factor of 5, is saved as q13_def.png.
The displacement field is saved in XDMF format to a file named q13_disp.xdmf.
