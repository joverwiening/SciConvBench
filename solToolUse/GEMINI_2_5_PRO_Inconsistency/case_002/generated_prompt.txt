The simulation will be implemented using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The problem is a 2D small-strain linear elasticity analysis under the plane stress assumption.
The domain is a right triangle with vertices at (0, 0), (1, 0), and (0, 0.5).
A triangular mesh with approximately 64 elements will be generated for the domain.
The vector-valued displacement space will be discretized using continuous Lagrange finite elements of polynomial degree 2.
The material's Young's modulus is 70e9 Pa.
The material's Poisson's ratio is 0.33.
The material's density is 2700 kg/m^3.
A gravitational body force corresponding to an acceleration of 9.81 m/s^2 acts in the negative y-direction.
The vertical boundary at x=0 is clamped, with zero displacement.
The bottom boundary at y=0 is a free surface with zero applied traction.
A normal traction of 3000 Pa is applied to the sloping boundary defined by x + 2y = 1.
The linear system of equations will be solved using a direct solver.
The magnitude of the displacement vector field will be computed and stored.
The von Mises stress field will be computed from the in-plane Cauchy stress components and stored.
