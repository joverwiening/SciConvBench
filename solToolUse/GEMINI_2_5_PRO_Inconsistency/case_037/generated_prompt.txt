The simulation will be implemented using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The problem to be solved is 2D small-strain linear elasticity under the plane stress assumption.
The material has a Young's modulus E of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio ν of 0.33.
The material density ρ is 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force representing gravity is applied, with g = 9.81 m/s^2 acting in the negative y-direction.
The geometry is a rectangular plate, modified by a hole and a loaded arc.
The plate's bounding box extends from (0,0) to (1,0.5).
A circular hole with a radius of 0.1m is centered at (0.5, 0.25).
A circular arc is defined on the right side of the plate by a circle with a radius of 0.02m centered at (0.98, 0.25).
This arc serves as the loaded boundary and extends over an angular range of 90 degrees, from -45 to +45 degrees relative to the horizontal.
The left boundary of the plate (x=0) is fixed with zero displacement.
The boundary of the central hole is traction-free.
A tangential traction is applied to the loaded circular arc.
The magnitude of this traction is calculated using the formula `t_θ = M/(2πr)`, with M=800 Nm and r=0.02m.
The traction vector at each point on the arc is oriented tangentially to the arc.
All other external boundaries are traction-free.
The mesh resolution will be set to 64 elements along the major edges of the domain.
The linear system will be solved using a direct solver.
The simulation will compute the displacement vector field and the von Mises stress field.
