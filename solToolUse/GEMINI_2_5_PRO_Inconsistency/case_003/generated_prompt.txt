Use FEniCS legacy version (dolfin).
The model is 2D plane-stress linear elasticity.
The domain is a rectangle defined by (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) meters.
The domain contains two circular holes of radius 0.04 meters.
The first hole is centered at (0.33, 0.10) meters.
The second hole is centered at (0.67, 0.10) meters.
The material has a Young's modulus of 200 GPa.
The material has a Poisson's ratio of 0.30.
The constitutive law uses the plane-stress formulation with Lamé parameters mu = E/(2*(1+nu)) and lambda = E*nu/(1-nu^2).
The displacement field is discretized using continuous Lagrange elements of polynomial degree 2.
The mesh will be generated with a characteristic element length of 0.004 near the hole boundaries, corresponding to approximately 64 elements on each circumference.
The mesh will have a maximum characteristic element length of 0.02 away from the holes.
The left boundary (x=0) is clamped, with displacement u = (0, 0).
The right boundary (x=1.0) has an applied line traction of t = (2e6, 0) N/m.
The top, bottom, and hole boundaries are traction-free.
The linear system is solved using a direct solver.
Compute the von Mises stress field over the domain.
The von Mises stress is calculated using the plane-stress formula sqrt(s_xx^2 - s_xx*s_yy + s_yy^2 + 3*s_xy^2).
Save a plot of the von Mises stress field to a file named q4_vm.png.
Save the displacement vector field to an XDMF file.
Calculate the maximum von Mises stress value on the boundaries of the two holes.
Calculate the stress concentration factor Kt by dividing the maximum von Mises stress by the nominal stress of 2 MPa.
Report the maximum von Mises stress and the stress concentration factor Kt.
