The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The analysis is a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem under plane stress conditions.
The geometry is a stepped polygon defined by the 8 vertices: (0.0, 0.0), (0.8, 0.0), (0.8, 0.1), (0.5, 0.1), (0.5, 0.2), (0.3, 0.2), (0.3, 0.1), and (0.0, 0.1).
A mesh with approximately 72 elements will be generated for this domain.
The material has a Young's modulus E = 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio ν = 0.33.
A body force due to gravity is applied, with density ρ = 2700 kg/m^3 and gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s^2 acting in the negative y-direction.
The bottom boundary, defined by y=0, is fixed, meaning both displacement components u_x and u_y are zero.
The vertical boundary segments at x=0.3 (for 0.1 ≤ y ≤ 0.2) and x=0.5 (for 0.1 ≤ y ≤ 0.2) have their vertical displacement constrained to zero (u_y = 0).
A horizontal traction vector T = (5000, 0) Pa is applied on the top boundary segment defined by y=0.2 for 0.3 ≤ x ≤ 0.5.
The problem will be solved for the vector displacement field using a direct linear solver.
After solving for the displacement, the in-plane Cauchy stress tensor will be computed.
From the stress tensor, the von Mises stress distribution will be calculated over the domain.
The final outputs will be the displacement field and the von Mises stress distribution.
