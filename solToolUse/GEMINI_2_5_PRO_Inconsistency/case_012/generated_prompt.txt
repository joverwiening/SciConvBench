The simulation will be performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The problem to be solved is small-strain von Mises elastoplasticity under plane strain conditions.
The geometry is a 2D quarter plate defined by the domain [0, 100] x [0, 180] mm, with a quarter-circular hole of radius 50 mm at the origin.
The domain will be meshed with second-order Lagrange triangular elements.
The characteristic element size for the mesh will be 5 mm.
The material model is associative, perfect plasticity with a von Mises yield criterion.
The Lamé parameter lambda is 19.44 GPa.
The shear modulus mu is 29.17 GPa.
The yield stress sigma_Y is 243 MPa.
On the boundary x = 0, the x-component of displacement is constrained to zero (u_x = 0).
On the boundary y = 0, the y-component of displacement is constrained to zero (u_y = 0).
The boundaries at x = 100 mm and on the circular hole are traction-free.
A final displacement of u_y = 1 mm and u_x = 0 is prescribed on the boundary at y = 180 mm.
The prescribed displacement will be applied over 20 equal increments.
Each load increment will be solved using a Newton-Raphson method.
The plasticity update will be handled by a return-mapping algorithm.
The final total displacement field will be saved to an XDMF file.
