Implementation notes:
Treat the 2D domain as plane stress with unit thickness and use the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
Compute von Mises from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
Represent each concentrated load with PointSource on the appropriate displacement component instead of smearing it over an edge.
Apply the concentrated load at the specified re-entrant-corner point and keep the other non-supported boundaries traction-free.
