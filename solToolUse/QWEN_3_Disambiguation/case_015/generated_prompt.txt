Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve the incompressible finite-strain hyperelasticity problem using the mixed displacement-pressure formulation.  
Model the solid as a plane strain, large-deformation, isotropic incompressible neo-Hookean material with displacement u and hydrostatic pressure p as unknowns.  
Define the domain Ω as a rectangular strip (0, 1.0) × (0, 0.20) m with a circular hole of radius 0.04 m centered at (0.50, 0.10) m.  
Set the material properties: Young’s modulus E = 5 MPa and Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.5.  
Apply a fixed boundary condition on the left edge (x = 0): u_x = 0 and u_y = 0.  
Apply a uniform follower pressure of magnitude P_hole = 0.10 MPa on the hole boundary, directed inward normal to the deformed boundary as a traction σ·n = −P_hole n, where n is the outward unit normal in the deformed configuration.  
Set the right, top, and bottom edges as traction-free.  
Solve using Newton’s method.  
Compute the von Mises stress field from the Cauchy stress tensor.
