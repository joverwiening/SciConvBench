Q: What is the magnitude and direction of the pressure load applied on the hole boundary?
A: The pressure load on the hole boundary is a uniform follower pressure with magnitude \( P_{\text{hole}} = 0.10 \) MPa. It is applied in the direction normal to the deformed boundary, inward, as a traction \( \boldsymbol{\sigma} \cdot \mathbf{n} = -P_{\text{hole}} \, \mathbf{n} \), where \( \mathbf{n} \) is the outward unit normal in the deformed configuration.
