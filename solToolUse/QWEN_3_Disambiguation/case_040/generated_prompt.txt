Use the FEniCS legacy (dolfin) library to solve the quasi-incompressible finite-strain hyperelasticity problem using a mixed displacement-pressure formulation.  
Model the solid as a large-deformation isotropic quasi-incompressible neo-Hookean material under plane strain conditions.  
Define the domain Ω as a rectangle (0, 1.0) × (0, 0.20) m with two circular holes of radius 0.04 m centered at (0.40, 0.10) m and (0.60, 0.10) m.  
Set the material parameters: Young's modulus E = 5 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.49.  
Use a mixed function space with displacement u = (u_x, u_y) and hydrostatic pressure p.  
Apply boundary condition u_x = 0, u_y = 0 on the left edge (x = 0).  
Apply boundary condition u_x = 0.060 m, u_y = 0 on the right edge (x = 1.0).  
Apply follower pressure loads on the boundaries of the two circular holes.  
Set traction-free conditions on the top and bottom edges (y = 0 and y = 0.20).  
Solve the nonlinear problem using Newton's method.  
Compute the Cauchy stress tensor from the constitutive model.  
Extract and compute the von Mises stress from the Cauchy stress.
