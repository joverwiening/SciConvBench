The simulation shall solve a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem using FEniCS legacy (dolfin).

The geometry is an L-shaped domain defined as the unit square [0,1]x[0,1] minus the inner square [0.5,1]x[0.5,1], with unit thickness.

The mesh shall be generated with 64 elements per edge using the FEniCS mesh generation capabilities.

The function space shall be a vector-valued Lagrange space of degree 1 for displacement, with 2 components (u_x, u_y).

Material properties are: Young's modulus E = 70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν = 0.33, density ρ = 2700 kg/m^3, gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s^2.

The plane-stress constitutive law shall be used with the reduced stiffness tensor: the Lamé parameters are computed as λ = E*ν/((1+ν)*(1-ν)) and μ = E/(2*(1+ν)), and the stress-strain relation is σ = 2με + λ*tr(ε)*I with the plane-stress modification that σ_zz = 0.

The variational form is: find u in V such that ∫_Ω σ(u):ε(v) dx = ∫_Ω ρ*g*v_y dx + ∫_Γ_t t·v ds for all v in V, where t is the applied traction.

The left boundary at x = 0 shall be fixed with Dirichlet boundary condition u = (0, 0).

The traction boundary condition applies only on the outer right edge where x = 1 and y ∈ [0, 1]. The traction is purely vertical (y-direction) with magnitude σ_yy(y) = -2.65e4*y Pa, so the traction vector is t = (0, -2.65e4*y). The negative sign indicates downward traction.

All other boundaries are traction-free, including the top edge (y = 1), the bottom edge (y = 0), the horizontal face of the cutout (y = 0.5, x ∈ [0.5, 1]), and the vertical face of the cutout (x = 0.5, y ∈ [0.5, 1]).

The linear solver shall be a direct solver (MUMPS or default LU solver in FEniCS).

The solution shall compute: displacement field u(x,y), strain field ε = 0.5*(∇u + ∇u^T), stress field σ from the constitutive law, and von Mises equivalent stress σ_vm = sqrt(σ_xx^2 - σ_xx*σ_yy + σ_yy^2 + 3*σ_xy^2).

Output shall include the displacement magnitude, the von Mises stress distribution, and the stress components σ_xx, σ_yy, σ_xy.
