Q: I need to clarify a contradiction in the boundary condition specification.

The case states: "Apply the varying traction only on the outer right edge x = 1; the re-entrant inner boundaries are traction-free."

But then it also states: "Apply the linearly varying vertical traction on every boundary segment whose outward normal has a positive x-component, including the vertical face of the cutout."

These two statements contradict each other. The vertical face of the cutout (at x = 0.5) has an outward normal with a positive x-component, so it would receive traction under the second rule, but the first rule explicitly says the re-entrant inner boundaries are traction-free.

Which boundary condition specification should I follow: should the traction be applied only on the outer right edge x = 1, or should it also be applied on the vertical face of the cutout (x = 0.5) where the outward normal has a positive x-component?
A: Apply the varying traction **only on the outer right edge x = 1**. The re-entrant inner boundaries (including the vertical face of the cutout at x = 0.5) are **traction-free**.
