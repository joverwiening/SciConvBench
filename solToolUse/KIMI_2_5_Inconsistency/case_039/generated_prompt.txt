The simulation shall use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem on a perforated rectangular plate.

The domain is a 1.0 m by 0.5 m rectangle with a circular hole of radius 0.1 m centered at (0.5, 0.25).

The mesh shall be generated with 64 elements per edge using the FEniCS mesh generation capabilities.

The material properties are: Young's modulus E = 70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν = 0.33, density ρ = 2700 kg/m³, and gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².

The constitutive law shall be the reduced plane-stress elasticity tensor for unit thickness.

The boundary conditions are: left edge (x = 0) has u_y = 0 (vertical fixation), right edge (x = 1) has u_x = 0 (horizontal fixation), top edge (y = 0.5) has traction σ_yy = -2500 Pa, and bottom edge (y = 0) has traction σ_yy = -2500 Pa.

The hole boundary shall be traction-free.

The variational formulation shall use a vector-valued Lagrange finite element space of degree 1 for displacement.

The solver shall use a direct linear solver (MUMPS or default LU) to solve the linear system.

The solution shall compute displacement field, strain field, stress field, and von Mises equivalent stress from the in-plane Cauchy stress components.

The von Mises stress shall be computed as sqrt(σ_xx² + σ_yy² - σ_xx*σ_yy + 3*σ_xy²) for plane stress.

Output shall include visualization of displacement magnitude, stress components σ_xx, σ_yy, σ_xy, and von Mises stress.
