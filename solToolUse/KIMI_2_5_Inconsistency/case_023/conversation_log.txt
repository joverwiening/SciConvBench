Q: What is the radius of the uniform pressure patch that should replace the point load at the apex?
A: The complete requirement does not specify a radius for a uniform pressure patch. Instead, it specifies to use PointSource for the concentrated load: "Represent each concentrated load with PointSource on the appropriate displacement component instead of smearing it over an edge."

Do not replace the point load with a pressure patch; use PointSource as specified.
