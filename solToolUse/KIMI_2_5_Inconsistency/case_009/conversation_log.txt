Q: I need to clarify the boundary conditions, as there appears to be a contradiction in the specification.

The description states: "Left outer boundary (x = 0): fixed, u_x = 0, u_y = 0" and separately says "Clamp not only the left outer boundary but also the two notch faces so the cutout behaves like a rigid fixture slot."

However, the notch is cut *from* the left edge, meaning the notch faces are part of the boundary at x=0. The notch region is (0, 0.06) x (0.08, 0.12), so the notch faces are at y=0.08 and y=0.12 for x in [0, 0.06].

Should I clamp:
- The entire left edge at x=0 (including the notch opening), OR
- Only the left edge portions outside the notch (y in [0, 0.08] U [0.12, 0.20]) plus the two vertical notch faces at y=0.08 and y=0.12 for x in [0, 0.06]?
A: Clamp the entire left edge at x=0, including the notch opening. The notch faces (the vertical faces at y=0.08 and y=0.12 for x in [0, 0.06]) should remain traction-free, not clamped.

The complete requirement states: "Left outer boundary (x = 0): fixed, u_x = 0, u_y = 0" and "Top and bottom edges, and the notch boundaries: traction-free." The incomplete requirement's mention of clamping the notch faces is not in the complete requirement.
