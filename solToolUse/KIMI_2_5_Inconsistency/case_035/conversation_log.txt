Q: What is the width of the upper notch segment over which the uniform traction patch should be applied to replace the point load?
A: The complete requirement does not specify replacing the point load with a uniform traction patch. It states: "Represent each concentrated load with PointSource on the appropriate displacement component instead of smearing it over an edge."

Apply the point load as a PointSource at (0.4, 0.5) on the y-component, not as a distributed traction.
