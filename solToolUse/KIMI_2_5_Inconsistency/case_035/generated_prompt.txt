The simulation solves a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem using legacy FEniCS (dolfin).

The domain is a polygon with vertices (0,0), (0.8,0), (0.8,0.2), (0.5,0.2), (0.5,0.5), (0.3,0.5), (0.3,0.2), (0,0.2).

The mesh contains 72 triangular elements.

Material properties are: Young's modulus E = 70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν = 0.33, density ρ = 2700 kg/m³, gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².

The constitutive law uses plane-stress reduced elasticity with unit thickness.

Boundary conditions fix the bottom edge (y=0) with Dirichlet boundary condition u = (0,0).

A vertical point load of -5 kN (downward) is applied at point (0.4, 0.5) on the y-component using PointSource.

Body force from gravity acts in the negative y-direction with magnitude ρ*g.

The variational problem uses a vector-valued Lagrange displacement space of degree 1.

The bilinear form implements the plane-stress stiffness: a(u,v) = ∫ σ(u) : ε(v) dx, where σ = 2με + λ*tr(ε)I with plane-stress modified Lamé parameters.

The linear form includes contributions from gravity body force and the PointSource.

A direct linear solver (LU) solves the assembled system.

Post-processing computes von Mises stress from the in-plane Cauchy stress components: σ_vm = sqrt(σ_xx² - σ_xx*σ_yy + σ_yy² + 3*σ_xy²).

Output includes displacement magnitude distribution and von Mises stress distribution over the domain.
