Q: What is the thickness of the plate to use for converting the line traction to force per unit area?
A: Use unit thickness (1 m). The traction t = (2 MPa·m, 0) is already given as a line traction per unit thickness, so for a 2D plane-stress model with unit thickness, the applied traction on the right edge is simply (2, 0) MPa.
