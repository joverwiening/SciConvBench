Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem.
The domain is a rectangular plate of dimensions 1.0 m (width in x) by 0.5 m (height in y) with a circular hole of radius 0.1 m centered at coordinates (0.5, 0.25).
The left edge at x=0 is fully clamped (zero displacement).
Apply an internal pressure of 1e5 Pa acting inward normal to the hole boundary, implemented as traction t = +p*n where n is the outward normal of the solid domain.
Apply gravity body force in the negative y-direction with magnitude rho*g where rho=2700 kg/m^3 and g=9.81 m/s^2.
Use material properties Young's modulus E=70e9 Pa and Poisson's ratio nu=0.33.
Treat the problem as plane stress with unit thickness.
Use a vector-valued FunctionSpace of degree 1 for displacement.
Define the plane-stress constitutive law: epsilon = 0.5*(grad(u) + grad(u).T), sigma = E/(1+nu)/(1-nu) * ((1-nu)*epsilon + nu*tr(epsilon)*Identity(2)).
Assemble the variational form: inner(sigma, epsilon(v))*dx - dot(t, v)*ds(1) - dot(f, v)*dx where ds(1) marks the hole boundary and f=(0, -rho*g).
Solve using a direct linear solver (default LU).
Compute von Mises stress from the in-plane Cauchy stress: dev = sigma - tr(sigma)/2*Identity(2), von_mises = sqrt(3.0/2.0*inner(dev, dev)).
Generate the mesh with 64 elements per edge using mshr or external mesh generation with refined resolution near the hole.
Output displacement magnitude and von Mises stress fields for visualization.
