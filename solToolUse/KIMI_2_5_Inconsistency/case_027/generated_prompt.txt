The simulation solves a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem on a right triangular domain using FEniCS legacy (dolfin).

The domain is defined by vertices (0,0), (1,0), and (0,0.5).

The mesh consists of 64 triangular elements.

Material properties are: Young's modulus E = 70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν = 0.33, density ρ = 2700 kg/m³, and gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².

The constitutive law uses the reduced plane-stress formulation with unit thickness.

The boundary condition clamps the base at y = 0 (zero displacement).

The body force applies gravity in the negative y-direction with magnitude ρ*g.

The traction boundary condition applies a uniform horizontal force of τ_x = 2000 Pa in the global positive x-direction on the sloping edge defined by x + 2y = 1.

The variational problem uses a vector-valued Lagrange displacement space of degree 1.

The solver uses a direct linear solver (MUMPS or default LU) to solve the resulting linear system.

The post-processing computes the displacement magnitude as sqrt(u_x² + u_y²).

The post-processing computes the Cauchy stress tensor from the strain using the plane-stress constitutive relation.

The post-processing computes the von Mises stress from the in-plane stress components as sqrt(σ_xx² - σ_xx*σ_yy + σ_yy² + 3*σ_xy²).

The output includes the displacement field, displacement magnitude field, and von Mises stress field for visualization.
