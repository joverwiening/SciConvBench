The simulation shall use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem.

The geometry shall be a rectangle of dimensions 4.0 by 1.0 with a circular hole of radius 0.3 centered at coordinates (1.0, 0.5).

The mesh shall be generated with a resolution parameter of 72.

The function space shall be a second-order vector Lagrange space (VectorFunctionSpace with degree 2).

The material properties shall be Young's modulus E = 210e3 and Poisson's ratio nu = 0.3.

The Lamé parameters shall be computed as mu = E/(2*(1+nu)) and lambda_plane_stress = E*nu/(1-nu**2) for the plane stress formulation.

The left edge at x = 0 shall be clamped with zero displacement (Dirichlet boundary condition u = 0).

The right edge at x = 4.0 shall have a uniform downward traction applied as a Neumann boundary condition with traction vector t = (0, -1e5) per unit thickness.

The hole boundary shall remain traction-free with no boundary condition applied.

No body force shall be included in the formulation.

The variational problem shall use the standard linear elasticity bilinear form with the plane stress constitutive law: sigma = lambda_plane_stress * tr(epsilon) * Identity(2) + 2 * mu * epsilon, where epsilon is the symmetric gradient of displacement.

The linear system shall be solved using a direct linear solver (default MUMLU or LU in FEniCS).

The output shall include the displacement field and the von Mises stress field computed from the stress tensor.
