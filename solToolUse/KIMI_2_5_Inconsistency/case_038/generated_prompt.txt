The simulation shall use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve a 2D plane-stress linear elasticity problem on a semi-circular domain.

The domain is a half-disk of radius 1 meter with y ≥ 0.

The mesh shall contain 64 triangular elements with P1 (linear) Lagrange shape functions for the vector-valued displacement field.

Material properties are: Young's modulus E = 70 × 10^9 Pa, Poisson's ratio ν = 0.33, density ρ = 2700 kg/m³, gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².

The constitutive law shall be the reduced plane-stress elasticity tensor relating in-plane strains to in-plane stresses for a unit thickness domain.

Boundary conditions: the base at y = 0 is fully fixed (Dirichlet: u = 0).

Loads: body force due to gravity acts in the negative y-direction with magnitude ρg; a uniform vertical traction (0, -20 kPa) is applied on the curved arc boundary as a substitute for inward normal pressure.

The variational problem uses the standard linear elasticity weak form with the plane-stress stiffness tensor.

The solver shall use a direct linear solver (MUMPS or default LU) to compute the displacement field.

Post-processing: compute the in-plane Cauchy stress tensor from the displacement gradients and constitutive law; compute the von Mises equivalent stress from the in-plane stress components as σ_vm = sqrt(σ_xx² - σ_xx*σ_yy + σ_yy² + 3*σ_xy²).

Output: displacement magnitude distribution and von Mises stress distribution over the domain.
