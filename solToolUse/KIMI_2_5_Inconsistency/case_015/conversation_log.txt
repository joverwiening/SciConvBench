Q: I need to clarify a contradiction in the boundary conditions.

The case details state: "Top edge segments x in [0, 0.45) union (0.55, 1.0]: traction t = (0, -10 MPa*m)" and separately "Both vertical sides (x = 0 and x = 1) and the notch arc: traction-free."

However, the instruction also says: "Apply the downward top load continuously across the entire top boundary, including the semicircular notch arc."

These are contradictory - the first specifies traction only on the flat top segments excluding the notch, while the instruction says to include the notch arc.

Which boundary condition should I use: traction only on the flat top segments excluding the notch (as originally specified), or traction applied continuously across the entire top boundary including the semicircular notch arc?
A: Apply the top load only on the two top segments outside the notch opening, i.e., x in [0, 0.45) union (0.55, 1.0]. The notch arc should remain traction-free.
