Solve an incompressible neo-Hookean perforated strip problem using FEniCS legacy (dolfin).

The domain is a rectangular strip Ω = (0, 1.0) x (0, 0.20) m with a circular hole of radius a = 0.04 m centered at (0.50, 0.10).

Use a mixed displacement-pressure formulation with P2-P1 Taylor-Hood elements: quadratic (P2) for displacement and linear (P1) for pressure.

The material is a large-deformation isotropic incompressible neo-Hookean solid with Young's modulus E = 5 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.5 (incompressible limit), under plane strain conditions.

Apply boundary conditions as follows: fix the left edge (x = 0) with u_x = 0 and u_y = 0; apply a uniform follower pressure P_hole = 0.10 MPa on the hole boundary as traction sigma*n = -P_hole*n in the deformed configuration; leave the right, top, and bottom edges traction-free.

Apply the hole load as a dead pressure using the reference normal of the undeformed hole boundary.

Use Newton's method with a maximum of 25 iterations and a relative residual convergence tolerance of 1e-8.

Use a mesh with characteristic element size h = 0.01 m globally, with refined resolution near the hole boundary to capture stress concentrations.

Compute the von Mises equivalent stress from the Cauchy stress tensor.
