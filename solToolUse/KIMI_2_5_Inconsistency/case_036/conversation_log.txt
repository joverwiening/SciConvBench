Q: What is the angle of inclination of the left and right edges of the parallelogram (or equivalently, what is the slope/height ratio) so I can correctly compute the tangential direction for applying the shear traction?
A: The parallelogram vertices are (0,0)-(1,0)-(1.2,0.3)-(0.2,0.3). The left edge runs from (0,0) to (0.2,0.3), and the right edge runs from (1,0) to (1.2,0.3). Both edges have the same inclination: the horizontal run is 0.2 and the vertical rise is 0.3, giving a slope of 0.3/0.2 = 1.5 (or equivalently, an angle of arctan(1.5) ≈ 56.31° from horizontal).

However, note that the COMPLETE requirement specifies "horizontal traction on left slope" and to "clamp the right inclined edge and apply the horizontal traction only on the left slope" — this means the traction is applied as a global horizontal load (σ_xx = 6000 Pa), not as a tangential shear traction. The incomplete requirement mentions tangential shear traction, but the complete requirement overrides this with horizontal traction.
