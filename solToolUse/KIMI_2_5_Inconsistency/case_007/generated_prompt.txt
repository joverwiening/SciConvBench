Solve using a direct linear solver (default LU solver in FEniCS).

Post-process to compute von Mises stress from the in-plane Cauchy stress components: σ_vm = sqrt(σ_xx^2 + σ_yy^2 - σ_xx*σ_yy + 3*σ_xy^2). Project the von Mises stress to a scalar function space for visualization.

Output the displacement field and von Mises stress distribution.
