Q: What mesh resolution should be used for the half‑disk (e.g., number of radial divisions and angular divisions or total element count)?
A: Use a mesh with a total of 64 finite elements for the half‑disk. (The requirement only specifies the overall element count; you may choose a reasonable subdivision—e.g., 8 radial × 8 angular divisions—to achieve 64 elements.)
