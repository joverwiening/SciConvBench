Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) with a direct linear solver (LU).  
Create a 2‑D mesh of the half‑disk of radius 1 m (center at the origin, y ≥ 0) consisting of 64 triangular elements.  
Define a vector‑valued function space for the displacement field with unit thickness.  
Treat the problem as plane‑stress and use the reduced constitutive matrix  
\(D = \frac{E}{1-\nu^{2}}\begin{bmatrix}1 & \nu & 0\\ \nu & 1 & 0\\ 0 & 0 & \frac{1-\nu}{2}\end{bmatrix}\)  
with \(E = 70\text{e}9\) Pa and \(\nu = 0.33\).  
Apply a body force \(\mathbf{f}= (0,-\rho g)\) with \(\rho = 2700\) kg/m³ and \(g = 9.81\) m/s².  
Impose Dirichlet boundary conditions fixing the displacement to zero at the two base points \((-1,0)\) and \((1,0)\).  
Apply two symmetric point loads of \(-3\) kN each at the arc points \((\pm0.707,0.707)\) using PointSource objects in the vertical (negative y) direction.  
Formulate the weak form of the linear elasticity variational problem, assemble the system, and solve for the displacement field.  
Compute the Cauchy stress tensor \(\boldsymbol{\sigma}=D:\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}(\mathbf{u})\) and evaluate the von Mises stress  
\(\sigma_{vm}= \sqrt{\sigma_{xx}^{2}-\sigma_{xx}\sigma_{yy}+\sigma_{yy}^{2}+3\sigma_{xy}^{2}}\)  
at each mesh cell.  
Export the displacement vector field and the von Mises stress field to VTK (or XDMF) files for post‑processing.
