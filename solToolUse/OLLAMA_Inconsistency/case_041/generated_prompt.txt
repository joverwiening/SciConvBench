Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to implement a finite‑strain Saint‑Venant‑Kirchhoff elasticity solver in plane strain with load stepping and Newton iterations.
Define the rectangular domain Ω = (0, 1.20) m × (0, 0.20) m and subtract three circular holes of radius a = 0.03 m centred at (0.30, 0.10) m, (0.60, 0.10) m and (0.90, 0.10) m.
Define the displacement field u = (u_x, u_y) and compute the deformation gradient F = I + grad(u).
Compute the Green‑Lagrange strain E_GL = 0.5*(Fᵀ·F – I) and the second Piola‑Kirchhoff stress S = λ·tr(E_GL)·I + 2·μ·E_GL with λ = 5.769 MPa and μ = 3.846 MPa.
Compute the Jacobian J = det(F) and the Cauchy stress σ = (1/J)·F·S·Fᵀ.
Apply Dirichlet boundary conditions: u_x = 0, u_y = 0 on the left edge x = 0; u_x = 0.012 m, u_y = 0 on the right edge x = 1.20 m.
Treat the top edge, bottom edge and all hole boundaries as traction‑free (natural Neumann condition σ·n = 0).
Advance the prescribed right‑edge displacement in load steps of Δu_x = 0.001 m (i.e., twelve equal increments to reach the total 0.012 m) and solve each step with Newton’s method until convergence.
After each load step compute the principal values of the Green‑Lagrange strain tensor and determine the maximum principal strain E_max.
Terminate the load‑stepping procedure as soon as E_max exceeds 0.03.
Post‑process the final converged solution: plot the deformed configuration and save as q15_def.png.
Compute the field of maximum principal Green‑Lagrange strain, plot it, and save as q15_Emax.png.
Compute the deviatoric second‑Piola stress invariant (von Mises equivalent of S), plot it, and save as q15_vmS.png.
Export the final displacement field u and the scalar field E_max to XDMF files for further analysis.
