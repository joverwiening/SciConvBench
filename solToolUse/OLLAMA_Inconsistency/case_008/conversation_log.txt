Q: How would you like the 64‑element triangular mesh to be generated—specify the number of uniform subdivisions along the two legs of the right triangle.
A: Use 8 uniform subdivisions along the horizontal leg (x‑direction) and 4 uniform subdivisions along the vertical leg (y‑direction). This yields 8 × 4 = 32 rectangular cells, each split into two triangles, for a total of 64 triangular elements.
