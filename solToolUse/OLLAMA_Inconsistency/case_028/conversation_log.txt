Q: Should the horizontal shear traction be applied only on the portion of the top edge where x > 0.5, or on the entire top edge y = 1?
A: Apply the horizontal shear traction only on the top‑right segment y = 1 where x > 0.5. The rest of the outer edges are natural (except the clamped left edge).
