Q: Should the vertical load at the re‑entrant corner be applied as a concentrated point force or replaced by an equivalent uniform traction distributed over the two short edges that meet at that corner?
A: Apply the vertical load as a concentrated point force (using a PointSource on the y‑displacement component) at the re‑entrant‑corner point, not as a uniform traction over the adjacent edges.
