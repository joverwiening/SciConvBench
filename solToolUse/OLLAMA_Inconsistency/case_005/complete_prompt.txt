Case ID: case_005
Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin).
Solver: small-strain linear elasticity in legacy FEniCS (dolfin); solve the 2D plane-stress variational problem with a vector-valued displacement space and a direct linear solver.
Task:
Analyze stress in a perforated rectangular plate with opposing horizontal point loads and gravity loading. Implement linear elasticity using E=70e9 Pa, ν=0.33. Apply leftward force at (1,0.2) and rightward force at (1,0.3) while clamping left edge. Compute displacement and von Mises stress patterns.
Case details:
Material: E=70e9, ν=0.33, ρ=2700 kg/m^3, g=9.81 m/s^2. Geometry: 1x0.5 plate with 0.1m radius hole at (0.5,0.25). Loads: -3000N at (1,0.2), +3000N at (1,0.3) (x-components). Mesh: 64 elements/edge.
Implementation notes:
Treat the 2D domain as plane stress with unit thickness and use the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
Compute von Mises from the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
Represent each concentrated load with PointSource on the appropriate displacement component instead of smearing it over an edge.
Leave the hole boundary traction-free unless a pressure load is explicitly prescribed.
Apply the two horizontal point loads as equal and opposite PointSource terms at the two stated boundary locations.
