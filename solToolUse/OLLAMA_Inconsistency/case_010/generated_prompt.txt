Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) to solve a small‑strain orthotropic plane‑stress elasticity problem.  
Define the rectangular domain Ω = (0, 1.0) m × (0, 0.20) m.  
Generate a structured mesh with 50 elements in the x‑direction and 25 elements in the y‑direction over Ω.  
The local material axes (1‑2) are rotated 30° anticlockwise with respect to the global x‑y axes.  
Local orthotropic material constants are E1 = 40 GPa, E2 = 10 GPa, G12 = 5 GPa, ν12 = 0.25.  
Construct the plane‑stress reduced stiffness matrix Q in the material frame and rotate it to the global frame by –30°.  
Impose displacement boundary conditions u_x = 0 and u_y = 0 on the bottom edge y = 0.  
Apply a uniform downward traction t = (0, –10 MPa) on the top edge y = 0.20, interpreted as traction per unit thickness.  
The vertical side edges x = 0 and x = 1 are traction‑free.  
Assemble and solve the variational problem using a direct linear solver.  
Compute the von Mises stress from the stress tensor.  
Save a colour map of the x‑component of displacement u_x as q8_ux.png.  
Save a colour map of the von Mises stress as q8_vm.png.  
Write the displacement field and the stress field to q8_solution.xdmf.
