Q: Should the Young’s modulus vary with the vertical coordinate y (E(y) = 100 GPa + 100 GPa·(y/0.20)) or with the horizontal coordinate x (E(x) = 100 GPa + 100 GPa·(x/1.0))?
A: The Young’s modulus should vary with the vertical coordinate y, i.e. E(y) = 100 GPa + 100 GPa·(y/0.20).
