Use FEniCS legacy (dolfin) with Python.  
Create a triangular mesh of the parallelogram with vertices (0,0), (1,0), (1.2,0.3), (0.2,0.3) consisting of 72 elements.  
Define a continuous Galerkin vector function space (degree 1) for the displacement field **u**.  
Set material parameters: Young’s modulus E = 70e9 Pa, Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.33, density ρ = 2700 kg/m³, gravity g = 9.81 m/s².  
Use the plane‑stress constitutive matrix  
\(C = \frac{E}{1-\nu^{2}}\begin{bmatrix}1 & \nu & 0\\ \nu & 1 & 0\\ 0 & 0 & \frac{1-\nu}{2}\end{bmatrix}\)  
for unit thickness.  
Define strain ε(u) = sym(∇u) and stress σ(u) = C : ε(u).  
Apply body force **f** = (0, ‑ρ g).  
Apply a traction on the horizontal top edge (y = 0.3) equal to **t** = (0, ‑5000) Pa (inward normal, i.e., downward).  
Impose Dirichlet boundary conditions **u** = (0,0) on both sloped side edges: the line from (0,0) to (0.2,0.3) and the line from (1,0) to (1.2,0.3).  
Form the variational problem  
\(a(u,v) = \int_{\Omega} \sigma(u):\varepsilon(v)\,dx\)  
\(L(v) = \int_{\Omega} f\cdot v\,dx + \int_{\Gamma_{\text{top}}} t\cdot v\,ds\).  
Assemble the system and solve with a direct linear solver (e.g., LU).  
Compute the Cauchy stress field σ(u) and evaluate the von Mises stress  
\(\sigma_{VM} = \sqrt{\sigma_{xx}^{2} - \sigma_{xx}\sigma_{yy} + \sigma_{yy}^{2} + 3\sigma_{xy}^{2}}\).  
Export the displacement field and the von Mises stress to VTK files for visualization.
