A 2D, incompressible, steady-state simulation of airflow over a symmetric airfoil will be set up.
The simpleFoam solver will be used.
The computational domain will have a C-grid topology around the airfoil.
The mesh will be one cell thick in the z-direction.
In the `system/blockMeshDict` file, the front and back patches for this 2D simulation will be defined as walls.
The `boundary` entry for the `frontAndBack` patch will specify `type wall` and include faces `(0 4 7 3)` and `(1 2 6 5)`.
For boundary conditions in the `0/U` file, a uniform inlet velocity of (10 0 0) m/s will be applied.
A zero-gradient condition will be applied at the outlet for velocity.
A `noSlip` condition will be applied on the airfoil surface for velocity.
A `noSlip` condition will be applied on the `frontAndBack` patches for velocity.
For pressure in `0/p`, a `zeroGradient` condition will be used for the inlet.
A `zeroGradient` condition will be used for the airfoil surface for pressure.
A `fixedValue uniform 0` condition will be used at the outlet for pressure.
A `zeroGradient` condition will be used on the `frontAndBack` patches for pressure.
The specific geometry or profile of the symmetric airfoil needs to be provided.
A specific numerical value for the chord length of the airfoil needs to be provided.
