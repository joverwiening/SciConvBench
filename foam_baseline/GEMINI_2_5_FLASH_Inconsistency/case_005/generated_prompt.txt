The simulation is a 2D gas–liquid bubble column using twoPhaseEulerFoam (Euler–Euler).
The domain is rectangular, spanning x from 0 to 0.15 m, y from 0 to 1.00 m, and z from 0 to 0.10 m.
The height of the domain is along the +y direction.
The domain is thin in the z direction.
A structured hex mesh with 25 × 75 × 1 cells is used.
The mesh uses simpleGrading (1 1 1).
The blockMeshDict defines inlet, outlet, and walls patches.
The patches use 2D treatment with empty faces in the z direction.
The continuous liquid phase is water.
The water phase uses heRhoThermo with the rPolynomial equation of state.
The rPolynomial equation of state for water has C coefficients (0.001278 -2.1055e-06 3.9689e-09 4.3772e-13 -2.0225e-16).
The molecular weight of water is 18.
The specific heat capacity Cv of water is 4195.
The dynamic viscosity of water is 3.645e-4 Pa·s.
The Prandtl number for water is 2.289.
The dispersed gas phase is air.
The air phase uses heRhoThermo with the perfectGas equation of state.
The molecular weight of air is 28.9.
The specific heat capacity Cp of air is 1007.
The dynamic viscosity of air is 1.84e-05 Pa·s.
The Prandtl number for air is 0.7.
Gravity is set to (0 -9.81 0) m/s².
The bubble size model for air uses an isothermal diameter d0 = 3e-3 m.
The bubble size model for water uses a constant diameter d = 1e-4 m.
Momentum transport is configured as laminar for both phases.
The simulationType is laminar in the respective momentumTransport files.
The multiphase interaction models from phaseProperties define phases as (air water).
Water is the reference phase.
The residualAlpha for both phases is 1e-6.
Blending uses a linear scheme.
The minFullyContinuousAlpha for blending is 0.7.
The minPartlyContinuousAlpha for blending is 0.3 for default or 0.5 for drag.
Surface tension between air and water is constant with sigma = 0.07.
The drag model uses SchillerNaumann for air_dispersedIn_water, water_dispersedIn_air, and air_segregatedWith_water configurations.
Virtual mass uses constantCoefficient with Cvm = 0.5 for both directions.
Heat transfer employs the RanzMarshall model for both directions.
The residualAlpha for heat transfer is 1e-4.
Initial conditions at t = 0 set the velocity of water to uniform (0 0 0).
Initial conditions at t = 0 set the velocity of air to uniform (0 0.1 0).
Initial conditions at t = 0 set the volume fraction of water to uniform 1.
Initial conditions at t = 0 set the pressure p_rgh to uniform 1e5.
Initial conditions at t = 0 set the temperature of air to uniform 300.
Initial conditions at t = 0 set the temperature of water to uniform 350.
Phase distribution uses default field values where the volume fraction of air is 1 and water is 0.
In a box region from (0 0 0) to (0.15 0.701 0.1), the volume fraction of air is 0 and water is 1.
At the inlet boundary, the velocity of air is fixedValue (0 0.1 0).
At the inlet boundary, the velocity of water is fixedValue (0 0 0).
At the inlet boundary, the volume fraction of air is fixedValue 0.5.
At the inlet boundary, the volume fraction of water is fixedValue 0.5.
At the inlet boundary, the temperature of air is fixedValue 300.
At the inlet boundary, the temperature of water is fixedValue 350.
At the wall boundaries, the velocity of air is fixedValue (0 0 0).
At the wall boundaries, the velocity of water is fixedValue (0 0 0).
At the wall boundaries, the volume fraction of air is zeroGradient.
At the wall boundaries, the volume fraction of water is zeroGradient.
At the wall boundaries, the pressure p_rgh is fixedFluxPressure.
At the wall boundaries, the temperature of air is zeroGradient.
At the wall boundaries, the temperature of water is zeroGradient.
The outlet boundary condition for the velocity of air is not specified.
The outlet boundary condition for the velocity of water is not specified.
The outlet boundary condition for the volume fraction of air is not specified.
The outlet boundary condition for the volume fraction of water is not specified.
The outlet boundary condition for the pressure p_rgh is not specified.
