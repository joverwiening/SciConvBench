The simulation will model buoyancy-driven heat transfer.
It will be performed in a 2D channel.
The `buoyantPimpleFoam` solver will be used.
The bottom wall temperature will be a fixed value of 400 K.
The top wall temperature will have a zero gradient boundary condition.
The air will be initialized at 300 K.
Gravity will act in the negative y-direction.
The length of the 2D channel is not specified.
