The task is to set up a transient simulation of turbulent flow over a 2D cylinder in a channel.
The simulation will use the pimpleFoam solver.
The simulation will be configured to use both Large Eddy Simulation (LES) and a Reynolds-Averaged Navier-Stokes (RANS) model simultaneously.
The `constant/turbulenceProperties` dictionary will specify `simulationType LES;`.
The LES sub-dictionary will contain `LESModel Smagorinsky;` and `delta cubeRootVol;`.
The RAS sub-dictionary will contain `RASModel kOmegaSST;`, `turbulence on;`, and `printCoeffs on;`.
Standard air properties for an incompressible fluid will be used.
The inlet velocity is 10 m/s.
The cylinder and channel walls will have noSlip boundary conditions.
The diameter of the 2D cylinder is not specified.
