Set up a laminar counter-flow diffusion flame in a 2-D opposed-jet configuration.
Use the reactingFoam solver.
The computational domain spans x from 0 to 0.02 meters, y from -0.01 to 0.01 meters, and z from -0.01 to 0.01 meters.
The grid resolution is 100 points in x, 40 points in y, and 1 cell in z.
The left boundary at x = 0 is named fuel.
The right boundary at x = 0.02 is named air.
The top and bottom boundaries are named outlet.
The FrontAndBack faces are empty.
At the fuel inlet boundary, inject methane (CH4) at a temperature of 293 K.
The velocity at the fuel inlet boundary is 0.1 m/s.
At the air inlet boundary, supply oxidizer as air (O2/N2) at a temperature of 293 K.
The mass fraction of O2 at the air inlet is 0.23, with the balance being N2.
The velocity at the air inlet boundary is -0.1 m/s.
Initialize the entire domain with a temperature of 2000 K at time 0.
Use EulerImplicit for chemistry type.
Use Rosenbrock43 as the ODE solver.
The combustion model is laminar.
Momentum transport is laminar.
Use compressible finite-rate chemistry.
Use hePsiThermo for thermodynamics.
Use coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture for mixing.
Use Sutherland for transport properties.
Use JANAF for thermodynamics data.
Set viscosity to 0 everywhere to represent an inviscid flame.
The simulation timestep is 1e-4 seconds.
The simulation end time is 0.5 seconds.
Results are written every 0.05 seconds.
