Simulate incompressible water flow in a channel with a prescribed moving wall (simplified peristaltic pumping) using a dynamic mesh.
Use a 2D mesh extruded one cell in the spanwise direction with `frontAndBack` as `empty`.
The water kinematic viscosity is `nu = 1e-6 m^2/s`.
For mesh motion, use `dynamicMotionSolverFvMesh` with `displacementLaplacian` or a similar solver in `constant/dynamicMeshDict`.
Prescribe motion on the `movingWall` via `0/pointDisplacement` using `oscillatingDisplacement` or a similar method.
All other boundaries for mesh motion are `fixedValue uniform (0 0 0)`.
For flow boundary conditions on `0/U`, the inlet is `fixedValue`.
For flow boundary conditions on `0/U`, the outlet is `zeroGradient`.
For flow boundary conditions on `0/U`, stationary walls are `noSlip`.
For flow boundary conditions on `0/U`, the `movingWall` uses `movingWallVelocity`.
For flow boundary conditions on `0/p`, the outlet is `fixedValue uniform 0`.
For flow boundary conditions on `0/p`, the inlet and walls are `zeroGradient`.
In `system/controlDict`, set `application solidDisplacementFoam;`.
