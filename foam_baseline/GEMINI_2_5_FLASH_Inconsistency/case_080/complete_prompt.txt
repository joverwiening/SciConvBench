Set up a simulation of a three-phase fluidized bed in a cylindrical column. The phases are air (gas), water (liquid), and sand (solid particles). The solver to be used is `multiphaseEulerFoam`. The column has a diameter of 0.1 m and a height of 1 m. Gravity acts in the negative y-direction (0 -9.81 0). The phases are defined in `phaseProperties` as `(air water sand)`. Air is treated as a perfect gas, water as an incompressible liquid, and sand as a constant density solid with a diameter of 500 microns. The simulation should be transient, running for 10 seconds. Initial conditions consist of a bed of sand settled at the bottom 0.2 m of the column, saturated with water (`alpha.sand = 0.6`, `alpha.water = 0.4`). The rest of the column is filled with water (`alpha.water = 1`). Air is injected from the bottom inlet at 0.5 m/s. Use standard drag models like Gidaspow for the interactions between the phases.
