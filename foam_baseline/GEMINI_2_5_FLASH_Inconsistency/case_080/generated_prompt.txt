A simulation of a three-phase fluidized bed in a cylindrical column will be set up.
The phases are air (gas), water (liquid), and sand (solid particles).
The solver to be used is twoPhaseEulerFoam.
The column has a diameter of 0.1 m and a height of 1 m.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction with a value of (0 -9.81 0).
The phases are defined in phaseProperties as (air water sand).
Air is treated as a perfect gas.
Water is treated as an incompressible liquid.
Sand is treated as a constant density solid with a diameter of 500 microns.
The simulation will be transient.
The simulation will run for 10 seconds.
Initial conditions consist of a bed of sand settled at the bottom 0.2 m of the column.
In the sand bed, the volume fraction of sand (alpha.sand) is 0.6.
In the sand bed, the volume fraction of water (alpha.water) is 0.4.
The rest of the column is filled with water, where the volume fraction of water (alpha.water) is 1.
Air is injected from the bottom inlet at 0.5 m/s.
Standard drag models like Gidaspow will be used for the interactions between the phases.
