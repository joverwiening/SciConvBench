The simulation will model 3D, incompressible, turbulent flow around a cube mounted on the floor of a channel.
The `simpleFoam` solver will be used.
A RANS `kEpsilon` turbulence model will be employed.
A fully 3D mesh will be used, ensuring more than one cell in the spanwise direction.
The two spanwise side patches, `left` and `right`, will be treated as symmetry boundaries to emulate an infinitely wide channel.
In `system/blockMeshDict`, the `left` and `right` patches will be defined with `type symmetryPlane;`.
`noSlip` boundary conditions will be applied to all solid walls, including the floor, the cube, and the top wall.
A fixed inlet velocity will be set on the `inlet` patch.
A fixed outlet pressure will be set on the `outlet` patch.
