The simulation is a 3D dam break.
The simulation includes three phases: water, air, and a light oil.
The domain is a rectangular tank of size 1m x 0.6m x 0.4m.
Initially, a column of water (0.2m x 0.6m x 0.2m) is in one corner.
A layer of oil (0.2m x 0.6m x 0.05m) is initially on top of the water column.
The rest of the domain is initially filled with air.
The `twoPhaseEulerFoam` solver is used for this transient simulation.
Gravity acts in the -y direction.
The phases are defined in `transportProperties` as `air`, `water`, and `oil`.
The `setFieldsDict` utility is used to initialize the phase fractions `alpha.air`, `alpha.water`, and `alpha.oil` in their respective regions.
All walls have no-slip boundary conditions.
The top boundary is an atmospheric inlet/outlet.
