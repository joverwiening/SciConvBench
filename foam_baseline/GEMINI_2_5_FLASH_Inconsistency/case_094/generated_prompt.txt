Simulate the steady-state, compressible flow of natural gas.
The flow is through a pipe with a porous baffle.
The inlet total pressure is 2e5 Pa.
The outlet static pressure is 1e5 Pa.
The gas is modeled as a perfect gas.
The porous zone is defined in the fvOptions dictionary with Darcy-Forchheimer coefficients.
The solver to be used is porousSimpleFoam.
The kinematic viscosity 'nu' must be defined in transportProperties.
