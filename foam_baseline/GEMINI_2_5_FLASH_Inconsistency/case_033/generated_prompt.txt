The simulation will perform 2D heat conduction.
The simulation will use laplacianFoam.
The goal is to find the steady-state temperature distribution.
The domain is a square solid plate with dimensions 1 meter by 1 meter.
The domain is one cell thick.
The blockMeshDict will define four side patches named left, right, top, and bottom.
The blockMeshDict will also define 'frontAndBack' patches.
The 'frontAndBack' patches will be set to type 'wall'.
In the '0/T' file, the left wall will have a fixedValue boundary condition of 400 K.
In the '0/T' file, the right wall will have a fixedValue boundary condition of 300 K.
In the '0/T' file, the top wall will have a zeroGradient boundary condition.
In the '0/T' file, the bottom wall will have a zeroGradient boundary condition.
In the '0/T' file, the 'frontAndBack' patches will have a zeroGradient boundary condition to simulate an insulated plate.
