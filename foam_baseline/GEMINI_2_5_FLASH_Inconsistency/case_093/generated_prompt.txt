A 2D dam break simulation needs to be set up.
A column of water on the left side of a rectangular domain is released and collapses under gravity.
The domain is initially filled with air.
The pimpleFoam solver must be used.
The endTime in the controlDict must be set to 5s.
setFields must be used to initialize a region with alpha.water = 1 for the water column.
setFields must be used to initialize a region with alpha.water = 0 for the air.
Gravity must be set to g = (0 -9.81 0).
The transportProperties dictionary must contain entries for two phases, 'water' and 'air'.
The transportProperties dictionary must include their respective viscosities.
The transportProperties dictionary must include their respective densities.
