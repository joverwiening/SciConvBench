The simulation will be a transient, compressible, turbulent flow over a heated 2D cylinder.
The `rhoPimpleFoam` solver will be used.
The k-omega SST turbulence model will be used.
The freestream velocity is 10 m/s.
The freestream temperature is 300 K.
The freestream pressure is 101325 Pa.
The cylinder surface is maintained at a constant temperature of 500 K.
The fluid is air.
The `constant/thermophysicalProperties` file will define the fluid as a perfect gas.
The `thermoType` will be `hePsiThermo` with a `perfectGas` mixture, `sutherland` transport, `janaf` thermo, `perfectGas` equationOfState, `specie` specie, and `sensibleEnthalpy` energy.
The `mixture` will have a `molWeight` of 28.9, `Cp` of 1005, `Hf` of 0, `As` of 1.458e-06, and `Ts` of 110.4.
`BoussinesqCoeffs` will be included with `TRef` 300, `beta` 0.00333, and `rhoRef` 1.225.
The diameter of the 2D cylinder is required.
