Set up a transient, compressible, turbulent flow over a heated 2D cylinder using `rhoPimpleFoam` and the k-omega SST turbulence model. The freestream velocity is 10 m/s, temperature is 300 K, and pressure is 101325 Pa. The cylinder surface is maintained at a constant 500 K. The fluid is air. In the `constant/thermophysicalProperties` file, define the fluid as a perfect gas.

thermoType

    type            hePsiThermo;
    mixture         pureMixture;
    transport       sutherland;
    thermo          janaf;
    equationOfState perfectGas;
    specie          specie;
    energy          sensibleEnthalpy;
}
