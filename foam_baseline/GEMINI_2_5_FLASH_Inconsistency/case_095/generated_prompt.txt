Set up a 2D, steady-state, incompressible, turbulent simulation of airflow.
The simulation uses a NACA0012 airfoil at a 5-degree angle of attack.
The simpleFoam solver will be used.
The k-omega SST turbulence model will be used.
The computational domain is a C-grid type, one cell thick in the z-direction.
The mesh is defined in constant/polyMesh.
The boundary patches are inlet, outlet, airfoil, and frontAndBack.
For the inlet patch, the velocity U is a fixedValue uniform (10 0 0).
For the inlet patch, k and omega are calculated from a turbulence intensity of 1% and a viscosity ratio of 10.
For the inlet patch, pressure p is zeroGradient.
For the outlet patch, velocity U is inletOutlet.
For the outlet patch, k and omega are inletOutlet.
For the outlet patch, pressure p is a fixedValue uniform 0.
For the airfoil patch, velocity U is noSlip.
For the airfoil patch, k is kqRWallFunction.
For the airfoil patch, omega is omegaWallFunction.
For the airfoil patch, pressure p is zeroGradient.
The frontAndBack patches must be treated as solid walls.
The type of frontAndBack patches should be set to wall in constant/polyMesh/boundary.
For the frontAndBack patches, velocity U is noSlip.
For the frontAndBack patches, pressure p, k, and omega are zeroGradient.
The specific numerical value for the kinematic viscosity of air has not been provided.
