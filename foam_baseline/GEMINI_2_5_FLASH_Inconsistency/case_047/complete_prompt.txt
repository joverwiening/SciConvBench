Configure a steady-state turbulent pipe flow simulation using simpleFoam with the k-epsilon RANS model. The fluid is water with a kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m²/s. The pipe diameter is 0.1 m and the inlet velocity is 1 m/s. For the initial conditions in the `0` directory, initialize the turbulent kinetic energy (k) and the turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) to small, physically plausible values based on an estimated turbulence intensity (e.g., 5%). For an inlet velocity of 1 m/s, this corresponds to k ≈ 0.00375 m²/s² and epsilon ≈ 0.001 m²/s³. In the `0/k` file, set `internalField uniform 0.00375;` and in `0/epsilon`, set `internalField uniform 0.001;`. For boundary conditions, use `kqRWallFunction` for k and `epsilonWallFunction` for epsilon on the pipe wall.
