Configure a steady-state turbulent pipe flow simulation.
Use the simpleFoam solver.
Employ the k-epsilon RANS model.
The fluid is water.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 1e-6 m²/s.
The pipe diameter is 0.1 m.
The inlet velocity is 1 m/s.
Initialize the turbulent kinetic energy (k) to zero in the 0/k file by setting internalField to uniform 0;.
Initialize the turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) to zero in the 0/epsilon file by setting internalField to uniform 0;.
Apply kqRWallFunction for k on the pipe wall boundary.
Apply epsilonWallFunction for epsilon on the pipe wall boundary.
