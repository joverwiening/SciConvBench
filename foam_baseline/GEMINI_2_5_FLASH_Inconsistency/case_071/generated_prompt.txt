Simulate a 2D dam break case.
The interFoam solver will be used.
The domain is a rectangle with dimensions 0.6m length and 0.4m height.
The height is oriented along the Y-axis, which represents the vertical direction.
blockMesh will create the geometry with a single block.
The initial condition is a column of water occupying the region x < 0.15m and y < 0.3m.
The rest of the domain is air.
The two phases are water and air with standard properties.
The gravity vector is `g (0 -9.81 0);`.
For boundary conditions:
U has `noSlip` on the bottom, left, and right walls.
U has `pressureInletOutletVelocity` on the top atmosphere patch.
p_rgh has `fixedFluxPressure` on the bottom, left, and right walls.
p_rgh has `totalPressure` on the top atmosphere patch.
alpha.water has `zeroGradient` on the bottom, left, and right walls.
alpha.water has `inletOutlet` on the top atmosphere patch.
The front and back patches are of type `empty`.
The simulation runs for 2 seconds.
