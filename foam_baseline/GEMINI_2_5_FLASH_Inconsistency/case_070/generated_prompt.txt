A 2D simulation of natural convection in a tall, sealed cavity will be set up.
The buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam solver will be used.
Gravity will act in the negative y-direction.
The fluid for the simulation will be air.
The left wall of the cavity will be hot.
The right wall of the cavity will be cold.
The top wall of the cavity will be adiabatic.
The bottom wall of the cavity will be adiabatic.
The temperature boundary condition for the leftWall will be fixedValue uniform 350.
The temperature boundary condition for the rightWall will be fixedValue uniform 300.
The temperature boundary condition for the topWall will be zeroGradient.
The temperature boundary condition for the bottomWall will be zeroGradient.
The dimensions of the cavity will be determined by reasonable assumptions based on best practices.
The kinematic viscosity of air will be determined by reasonable assumptions based on best practices.
The Prandtl number of air will be determined by reasonable assumptions based on best practices.
