The task is to simulate a 2D dam break problem.
The `interFoam` solver will be used.
The simulation involves two incompressible, immiscible fluids, water and air.
The computational domain is a rectangular tank 0.5 meters long and 0.5 meters high.
The domain is extruded by 0.01 meters in the z-direction to create a single layer of cells.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction with a magnitude of 9.81 m/s^2, specified as (0 -9.81 0).
Initially, a column of water (alpha.water=1) occupies the region where x is less than 0.15 meters.
The rest of the domain is initially filled with air (alpha.water=0).
The initial field setup will be performed using `setFieldsDict`.
The density of water is assumed to be 1000 kg/m^3.
The density of air is assumed to be 1.225 kg/m^3.
The kinematic viscosity of water is assumed to be 1e-6 m^2/s.
The kinematic viscosity of air is assumed to be 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The surface tension coefficient between water and air is assumed to be 0.07 N/m.
The domain will be discretized with 100 cells in the x-direction.
The domain will be discretized with 100 cells in the y-direction.
The simulation end time is set to 5 seconds.
The maximum Courant number for time stepping is set to 0.5.
The boundary named `leftWall` is of type `wall`.
The boundary named `rightWall` is of type `wall`.
The boundary named `lowerWall` is of type `wall`.
The boundary named `atmosphere` is of type `patch`.
The boundary named `frontAndBack` is of type `symmetry`.
For the velocity field U, `noSlip` boundary conditions are applied on `leftWall`, `rightWall`, and `lowerWall`.
For the velocity field U, `pressureInletOutletVelocity` boundary conditions are applied on `atmosphere`.
For the velocity field U, `symmetry` boundary conditions are applied on `frontAndBack`.
For the modified pressure field p_rgh, `fixedFluxPressure` boundary conditions are applied on `leftWall`, `rightWall`, `lowerWall`, and `frontAndBack`.
For the modified pressure field p_rgh, `totalPressure` boundary conditions are applied on `atmosphere`.
For the volume fraction field alpha.water, `zeroGradient` boundary conditions are applied on `leftWall`, `rightWall`, `lowerWall`, and `frontAndBack`.
For the volume fraction field alpha.water, `inletOutlet` boundary conditions are applied on `atmosphere`.
