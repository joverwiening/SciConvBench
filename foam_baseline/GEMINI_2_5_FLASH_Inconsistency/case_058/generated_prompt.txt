The task is to model the injection of a high-pressure, compressible gas into a tank of water.
The gas enters at Mach 0.8.
Compressibility effects of the gas are significant.
The `interFoam` solver will be used.
The gas phase will be defined using the `perfectGas` equation of state.
The water phase will be defined as incompressible.
The simulation is transient.
The goal is to observe the gas jet penetration and expansion in the water.
The dimensions of the water tank are not specified.
