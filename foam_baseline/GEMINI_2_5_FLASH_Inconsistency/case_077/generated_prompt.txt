The simulation will model a transient thermal plume rising in a 2D domain.
The `buoyantPimpleFoam` solver will be used.
A small, hot patch at the bottom center of the domain will be held at 500 K.
The surrounding fluid is initially at 300 K.
Gravity is set to (0 -9.81 0).
The simulation is expected to show large density variations as the hot air rises.
In `constant/thermophysicalProperties`, the fluid properties are configured as `thermoType hePsiThermo; mixture pureMixture; transport sutherland; thermo hConst; equationOfState Boussinesq; BoussinesqCoeffs { T0 300; beta 0.00333; rho0 1.176; } specie hConst; energy sensibleEnthalpy;`.
The Sutherland model for viscosity uses `As 1.4792e-06;` and `Ts 116;`.
The initial temperature field is `uniform 300;`.
The hot patch boundary condition is `fixedValue uniform 500;`.
All other boundaries are open to the atmosphere.
The dimensions of the 2D domain are not specified.
