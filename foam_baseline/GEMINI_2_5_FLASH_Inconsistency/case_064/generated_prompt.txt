The task is to model a flexible flap in a channel with fluid–structure interaction.
The geometry is 2D, extruded one cell in z with frontAndBack empty.
The channel length is 1.0 m.
The channel height is 0.2 m.
The flap length is 0.05 m.
The flap thickness is 0.005 m.
The flap is attached to the bottom wall at x = 0.4 m.
The fluid is incompressible water.
The fluid kinematic viscosity is 1e-6 m^2/s.
The inlet fluid velocity is U = (1 0 0) with a fixedValue boundary condition.
The outlet fluid pressure is p = 0 with a fixedValue boundary condition.
The channel walls have a noSlip boundary condition.
The solid flap is linear elastic.
The solid Young's Modulus is 1e6 Pa.
The solid Poisson's Ratio is 0.45.
The solid density is 1200 kg/m^3.
The flap base has a fixed displacement boundary condition.
The flap surface has a coupled interface boundary condition.
The fluid solver is pimpleFoam.
The solid solver is pimpleFoam, using the same single solver as the fluid on a combined mesh.
There is no interface coupling, meaning no displacement or traction exchange, in a single-region setup.
