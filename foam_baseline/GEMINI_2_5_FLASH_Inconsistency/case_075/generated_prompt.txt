The simulation will be a 2D bubble column.
The simulation will use twoPhaseEulerFoam.
The column width in the X-direction is 0.5 meters.
The column height in the Y-direction is 2 meters.
The column is initially filled with water.
Air is injected from a sparger at the bottom center.
The goal is to simulate the rise of air bubbles due to buoyancy.
The dispersed phase is air.
The continuous phase is water.
The gravity vector is `g (0 -9.81 0);`.
The bottom boundary is an inlet patch.
At the bottom inlet patch, `alpha.air` is `fixedValue 1`.
At the bottom inlet patch, `U.air` is `fixedValue (0 0.1 0)`.
The top boundary is an outlet.
The side walls are `noSlip`.
The front and back patches are `empty`.
