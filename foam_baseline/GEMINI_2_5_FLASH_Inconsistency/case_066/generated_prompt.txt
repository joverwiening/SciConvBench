Simulate steady-state, incompressible, turbulent flow and heat transfer.
The simulation will be performed in a 2D heated pipe.
The `buoyantSimpleFoam` solver will be used.
The pipe has a diameter of 0.1m.
The pipe has a length of 2m.
The k-epsilon turbulence model will be used.
The fluid is air with standard properties.
The density of air needs to be determined by a reasonable assumption based on best practices.
The inlet velocity is uniform at 1 m/s in the x-direction.
The inlet temperature is 300 K.
The outlet is at a fixed gauge pressure of 0 Pa.
The pipe wall temperature boundary condition is `zeroGradient` (adiabatic).
The front and back patches should be of type 'empty'.
