The simulation will use the simpleFoam solver.
The simulation will model steady, incompressible, laminar flow of water.
The water properties include a kinematic viscosity nu of 1e-6 m^2/s.
The geometry is a 2D channel with a rigid, stationary obstacle.
The channel has a length of 1 meter and a width of 0.1 meter.
The obstacle is a square block with a side length of 0.01 meter.
The mesh is a 2D mesh extruded one cell in the spanwise direction.
The frontAndBack boundary condition is set to empty.
For the velocity field U, the inlet has a fixedValue of uniform (0.1 0 0).
For the velocity field U, the outlet has a zeroGradient boundary condition.
For the velocity field U, the walls and obstacle have a noSlip boundary condition.
For the velocity field U, the frontAndBack boundary condition is empty.
For the pressure field p, the inlet has a zeroGradient boundary condition.
For the pressure field p, the outlet has a fixedValue of uniform 0.
For the pressure field p, the walls and obstacle have a zeroGradient boundary condition.
For the pressure field p, the frontAndBack boundary condition is empty.
