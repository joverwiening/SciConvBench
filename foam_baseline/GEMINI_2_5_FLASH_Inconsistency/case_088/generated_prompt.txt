The task is to model a steady-state buoyant flow in a solar chimney.
The solver to be used is buoyantSimpleFoam.
The air is heated at the bottom and rises due to density changes.
Gravity is defined in the constant/g file.
The Boussinesq model will be used for buoyancy.
The constant/thermophysicalProperties file contains the following: thermoType hePsiThermo, specie molWeight 28.9, equationOfState Boussinesq, thermodynamics Cp 1005, Hf 0, transport mu 1.8e-05, Pr 0.7, BoussinesqCoeffs TRef 300, beta 0.00333, rhoRef 1.176.
The solar chimney has a height of 10 meters.
The solar chimney has a width of 1 meter.
The temperature at the bottom of the solar chimney is 320 Kelvin.
The temperature at the top of the solar chimney is 300 Kelvin.
Heating at the bottom of the solar chimney is applied as a fixed temperature boundary condition.
The velocity boundary condition at the bottom of the solar chimney is fixedValue for pressure (0 Pa gauge) and zeroGradient for velocity.
The velocity boundary condition at the top of the solar chimney is fixedValue for pressure (0 Pa gauge) and zeroGradient for velocity.
The side walls of the chimney have a noSlip velocity boundary condition.
The side walls of the chimney have a zeroGradient temperature boundary condition.
