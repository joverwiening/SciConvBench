A transient simulation of 2D vortex shedding behind a cylinder will be set up.
The Reynolds number for the simulation is 100.
The domain is a rectangular channel with a circular cylinder offset from the center.
The pimpleFoam solver will be used.
The fluid is incompressible.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 0.01 m^2/s.
The inlet velocity is (1 0 0) m/s.
The cylinder diameter is 1 m.
The 'inlet' boundary will have a fixedValue U condition.
The 'outlet' boundary will have zeroGradient U and fixedValue p=0 conditions.
The 'topAndBottom' boundaries will have a slip condition.
The 'cylinder' boundary will have a noSlip condition.
The 'frontAndBack' boundaries will have an empty condition.
The simulation will run from t=0 to t=200 s.
The time step for the simulation is 0.1 s.
The goal of the simulation is to observe the von Kármán vortex street and calculate the Strouhal number.
