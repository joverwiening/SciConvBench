The static deflection of a 2D cantilever beam under a downward traction load will be simulated using linear elasticity.
The beam is 1.0 m long and 0.1 m high.
A structured mesh of 100 × 10 cells will be used.
The material is aluminum with Young's modulus E = 70e9 Pa and Poisson's ratio nu = 0.33.
The solver to be used is solidDisplacementFoam.
The application entry in system/controlDict will be solidDisplacementFoam.
Boundary conditions in 0/D are as follows:
The fixedEnd at x=0 has a fixedValue uniform (0 0 0).
The loadEnd at x=1 has a tractionDisplacement with traction uniform (0 -10000 0) and pressure uniform 0.
The top and bottom surfaces have a zeroGradient boundary condition.
