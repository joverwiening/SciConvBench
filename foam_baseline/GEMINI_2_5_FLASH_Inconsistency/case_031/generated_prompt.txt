The simulation will be a 2D incompressible laminar flow using the icoFoam solver.
The geometry is a rectangular channel with a length of 0.5 m in the x-direction and a height of 0.1 m in the y-direction.
The mesh will be one cell thick in the z-direction.
The blockMeshDict will define the following patches: inlet, outlet, topWall, bottomWall, and frontAndBack.
The frontAndBack patches will have a 'type wall;' specification.
The simulation will model Poiseuille flow.
In the '0/U' file, the inlet will have a parabolic velocity profile with a maximum of 1 m/s.
The outlet in '0/U' will have a zeroGradient boundary condition.
The topWall and bottomWall in '0/U' will have noSlip boundary conditions.
The frontAndBack patches in '0/U' will also have noSlip boundary conditions.
In the '0/p' file, the outlet will have a fixedValue of 0.
All other patches in '0/p', including inlet, topWall, bottomWall, and frontAndBack, will have zeroGradient boundary conditions.
