Simulate thermo-elastic deformation of a solid aluminum block.
The block has dimensions of 0.1 meters in length, 0.1 meters in width, and 0.1 meters in height.
The material properties for aluminum are Young's modulus E = 69e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio nu = 0.33, density rho = 2700 kg/m^3, thermal expansion coefficient alpha = 2.3e-5 1/K, and reference temperature T0 = 300 K.
The goal is to compute the displacement and stress due to thermal expansion.
A temperature field T is available and used to drive thermal strain.
The face at x = 0 meters is the fixedFace.
The fixedFace is fixedValue uniform (0 0 0) for displacement.
All other faces have zeroGradient for displacement.
The face at x = 0.1 meters is the heatedFace.
The heatedFace has a fixedValue uniform 400 K for temperature.
The fixedFace has zeroGradient for temperature.
All other faces have zeroGradient for temperature.
The application in system/controlDict is set to buoyantPimpleFoam.
