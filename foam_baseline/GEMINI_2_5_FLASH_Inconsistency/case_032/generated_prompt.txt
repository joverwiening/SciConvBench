The simulation will be a 2D steady-state, turbulent, incompressible flow.
The flow is over a NACA 0012 airfoil at a 5-degree angle of attack.
The simpleFoam solver will be used.
The k-omega SST turbulence model will be used.
The mesh is a C-grid extruded one cell in the z-direction to create the 2D domain.
In blockMeshDict, the front and back patches are named 'frontAndBack' and are of type 'symmetry'.
The boundary conditions for U are: inlet with a fixed velocity of (10 0 0.1) m/s, outlet with zeroGradient, airfoil surface as a no-slip wall, and 'symmetry' for the 'frontAndBack' patches.
The boundary conditions for p are: outlet with zeroGradient, and 'symmetry' for the 'frontAndBack' patches.
The boundary condition for pressure at the inlet is not specified.
The boundary conditions for k are: outlet with zeroGradient, and 'symmetry' for the 'frontAndBack' patches.
The boundary conditions for omega are: outlet with zeroGradient, and 'symmetry' for the 'frontAndBack' patches.
The simulation aims to calculate the 2D lift and drag coefficients.
