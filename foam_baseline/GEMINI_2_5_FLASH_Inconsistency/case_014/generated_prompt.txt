The task is to perform a structural stress analysis using the solidDisplacementFoam solver.
The geometry is a 2D plate with a circular hole, modeled as a quarter-symmetry domain.
The domain dimensions are 2x2 units in the x-y plane and 0.5 units thick in the z-direction, with convertToMeters set to 1.
The circular hole has a radius of 0.5 units and is centered at the origin.
A structured mesh is to be used, consisting of 5 blocks: two blocks near the hole with 10x10x1 cells, one outer block with 20x10x1 cells, and two other outer blocks with 20x20x1 and 10x20x1 cells respectively.
Symmetry boundary conditions are applied on the 'left' and 'down' patches.
A traction load of 10000 N/m² in the x-direction is applied on the 'right' patch.
Zero traction is applied on the 'up' and 'hole' patches.
Empty conditions are applied on the 'frontAndBack' patches for 2D analysis.
Material properties for steel are: density 7854 kg/m³, Poisson's ratio 0.3, Young's modulus 2e11 Pa, thermal conductivity 60.5 W/m·K, and thermal expansion coefficient 1.1e-05 /K.
The initial temperature is 300 K, with zero gradient boundary conditions on all physical boundaries.
The analysis is steady-state, running from time 0 to 100 with a timestep of 1.
Results should be output every 20 timesteps.
The GAMG solver with a tolerance of 1e-06 is to be used for displacement and temperature fields.
Plane stress analysis is enabled with compact normal stress calculation.
The provided blockMeshDict content must be used for the simulation.
