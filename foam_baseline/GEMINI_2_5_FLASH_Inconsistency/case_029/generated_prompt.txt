Simulate the transient von Kármán vortex street for 2D flow past a circular cylinder.

Use the `pimpleFoam` transient solver.
The cylinder diameter is 1 meter.
The computational domain extends 15 diameters upstream, 30 diameters downstream, and 15 diameters in the transverse direction from the cylinder center.

Apply appropriate boundary conditions for external flow around a cylinder.

The initial time step is 0.005.
