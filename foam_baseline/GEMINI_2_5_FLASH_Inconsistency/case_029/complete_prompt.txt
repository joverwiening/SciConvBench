Simulate the transient von Kármán vortex street for 2D flow past a circular cylinder at Reynolds number Re = 150. Use a transient time step (not steady iterations) and write results frequently enough to resolve the shedding.

In `system/controlDict`, set:
```foam
application     pimpleFoam;

startFrom       startTime;
startTime       0;

stopAt          endTime;
endTime         200;

deltaT          0.005;

adjustTimeStep  yes;
maxCo           0.9;

writeControl    runTime;
writeInterval   1;
```
