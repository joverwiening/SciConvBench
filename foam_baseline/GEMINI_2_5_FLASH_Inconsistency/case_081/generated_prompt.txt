The simulation will use the twoPhaseEulerFoam solver.
The simulation is transient.
The domain is a 2D gas-liquid bubble column.
The domain is a rectangle of 0.5 m width and 2 m height.
The two phases are air and water.
The front and back planes will have an empty boundary condition type.
The density of air is 1.225 kg/m^3.
The density of water is 998.2 kg/m^3.
The dynamic viscosity of air is 1.825e-5 Pa.s.
The dynamic viscosity of water is 1.003e-3 Pa.s.
At the bottom 'inlet' boundary, both air and water are introduced.
The boundary condition for alpha.air at the inlet patch is type fixedValue; value uniform 0.8;.
The boundary condition for alpha.water at the inlet patch is type fixedValue; value uniform 0.2;.
The velocity of air at the inlet is (0 0.2 0) m/s.
The velocity of water at the inlet is (0 0.01 0) m/s.
The top boundary is an outlet.
The boundary condition for alpha.air at the top outlet boundary is type outletPhaseMeanVelocity;.
The boundary condition for alpha.water at the top outlet boundary is type outletPhaseMeanVelocity;.
The boundary condition type for U.air at the top outlet boundary is not yet specified.
The sides are no-slip walls.
