Configure a simulation for the separation of three immiscible fluids: oil, water, and gas.
The simulation uses the Volume of Fluid (VOF) method in a horizontal gravity separator.
The solver `interFoam` is specified in `controlDict`.
Phase definitions, including densities and viscosities, are located in `transportProperties`.
The initial state is a stratified mixture, configured using `setFieldsDict`.
The `alpha.water` field represents the volume fraction of water.
The simulation tracks the interface between the three fluids as they separate over time under gravity.
Gravity is set to (0 -9.81 0).
