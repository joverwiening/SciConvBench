Set up a 2D OpenFOAM interFoam (VOF multiphase) dam-break case of a water column collapsing under gravity in a tank of air.
Assume a reasonable OpenFOAM version based on best practices.
Use a 2D tank domain of 0.584 m in x by 0.584 m in y.
Initialize a water column of width 0.146 m in x and height 0.292 m in y.
Place the initial water column in the bottom-left corner spanning x = 0 to 0.146 m and y = 0 to 0.292 m.
Use a uniform blockMesh grid of 58 × 58 cells over the 0.584 m × 0.584 m domain, giving Δx = Δy = 0.584/58 ≈ 0.01007 m.
Use an open-to-atmosphere top boundary patch named atmosphere with U = pressureInletOutletVelocity, p_rgh = totalPressure with p0 uniform 0, and alpha.water = inletOutlet with inletValue 0.
Use a left wall boundary patch named leftWall with U = noSlip, p_rgh = fixedFluxPressure, and alpha.water = zeroGradient.
Use a right wall boundary patch named rightWall with U = noSlip, p_rgh = fixedFluxPressure, and alpha.water = zeroGradient.
Use a bottom wall boundary patch named lowerWall with U = noSlip, p_rgh = fixedFluxPressure, and alpha.water = zeroGradient.
Use a front and back boundary patch named frontAndBack set to empty for U, p_rgh, and alpha.water to enforce a 2D case.
Specify the simulation end time for the dam-break run.
