Q: What is the geometry and size of the fluidized bed you want to simulate (e.g., column diameter and height)?
A: Use a 3D rectangular bed: **0.1 m (x) × 0.5 m (y, height) × 0.1 m (z)**. Gas enters from the **bottom** distributor and exits at the **top**.

Q: What gas and solid particle properties should I use (gas density/viscosity, particle density, particle diameter, and solids volume fraction or initial bed height)?
A: - **Gas (air) properties:**  
  - Density **ρ_g = 1.2 kg/m³**  
  - Kinematic viscosity **ν_g = 1.5e-5 m²/s**

- **Solid particle properties (granular phase):**  
  - Particle density **ρ_s = 2500 kg/m³**  
  - Particle diameter **d_p = 200 µm (2e-4 m)**

- **Initial solids loading / bed:**  
  - Set an initial packed bed with **α_solid = 0.55** in the **bottom 0.2 m** of the column height  
  - Above that: **α_solid = 0**  
  - **α_air = 1 − α_solid** everywhere

=== 2026-03-25 15:23:16 ===
Q: What superficial gas inlet velocity (or inlet mass flow rate) should the bottom distributor use?
A: Use a **fixedValue upward superficial inlet velocity of 0.2 m/s** at the bottom distributor (for **U.air**).

A: Use a **granular wall condition for the solids velocity** at the side walls: **partialSlip / Johnson–Jackson** (i.e., JohnsonJackson if available in your OpenFOAM setup; otherwise the standard granular partial-slip wall BC).
