Set up a laminar counter-flow diffusion flame simulation using the reactingFoam solver.
The simulation will be in a 2-D opposed-jet configuration.
The domain is a rectangular prism.
The domain spans from x=0 to x=0.02 m.
The domain spans from y=-0.01 to y=0.01 m.
The domain spans from z=-0.01 to z=0.01 m.
The mesh will have 100 grid points in the x-direction.
The mesh will have 40 grid points in the y-direction.
The mesh will have 1 cell in the z-direction.
The left boundary at x=0 is named 'fuel'.
The right boundary at x=0.02 is named 'air'.
The top and bottom boundaries are named 'outlet'.
The front and back faces are of type 'empty'.
The fuel inlet injects pure methane (CH4) at a temperature of 293 K.
The fuel inlet velocity is 0.1 m/s in the x-direction.
The air inlet supplies air at a temperature of 293 K.
The air composition is 23% O2 and 77% N2 by mass.
The air inlet velocity is -0.1 m/s in the x-direction.
The operating pressure is standard atmospheric pressure (101325 Pa).
A one-step global reaction mechanism for methane-air combustion will be used.
The primary objective is to study the flame structure.
The pressure boundary condition at the outlet is totalPressure with a value of 101325 Pa.
The velocity boundary condition at the outlet is zeroGradient.
The temperature boundary condition at the outlet is zeroGradient.
The species mass fraction boundary condition at the outlet is zeroGradient.
The combustion model is laminar.
The momentum transport model is laminar.
Use compressible finite-rate chemistry with hePsiThermo.
The mixture model is coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture.
The transport model is Sutherland.
The thermodynamics model is JANAF.
The chemistry solver is EulerImplicit.
The ODE solver is Rosenbrock43.
The simulation timestep is 1e-4 seconds.
The simulation end time is 0.5 seconds.
