Simulate a 2D flow past a circular cylinder using the pimpleFoam solver.
The flow is incompressible, unsteady, and laminar.
The domain is a rectangle extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m and y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m.
The simulation is 2D, so the front and back patches should have an 'empty' boundary condition.
A cylinder with a diameter of 2 m is centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).
The simulation starts at 0 s and ends at 0.5 s.
The time step is 0.0025 s.
Results are written every 0.05 s.
The initial velocity field is U = (0 0 0) m/s.
The initial pressure field is p = 0.
The laminar model is generalisedNewtonian with the crossPowerLaw viscosity model.
The inlet patch at x = -5 m has a velocity of U = (1 0 0) m/s.
The inlet patch at x = -5 m has a zeroGradient pressure boundary condition.
The outlet patch at x = 5 m has a zeroGradient velocity boundary condition.
The outlet patch at x = 5 m has a pressure of p = 0.
The cylinder wall has a noSlip velocity boundary condition.
The cylinder wall has a zeroGradient pressure boundary condition.
The top wall at y = 2.5 m has a noSlip velocity boundary condition.
The top wall at y = 2.5 m has a zeroGradient pressure boundary condition.
The bottom wall at y = -1.5 m has a noSlip velocity boundary condition.
The pressure boundary condition at the bottom wall is zeroGradient.
