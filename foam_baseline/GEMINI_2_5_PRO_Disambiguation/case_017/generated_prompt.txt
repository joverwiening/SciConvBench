The simulation goal is to set up a laminar counter-flow diffusion flame in a 2-D opposed-jet configuration.
The domain is a rectangle spanning from x=0 to 0.02 m, y=-0.01 to 0.01 m, and z=-0.01 to 0.01 m.
The grid has 100 points in the x-direction, 40 points in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The left boundary at x=0 is named 'fuel'.
The right boundary at x=0.02 is named 'air'.
The top and bottom boundaries are named 'outlet'.
The front and back faces are of type 'empty'.
The simulation uses the reactingFoam solver.
The chemistry type is EulerImplicit with the Rosenbrock43 ODE solver.
The combustion model is laminar.
The momentum transport is laminar.
The simulation uses compressible finite-rate chemistry.
The thermophysical model is hePsiThermo.
The species mixing model is coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture.
The transport model is Sutherland.
The thermodynamics model is JANAF.
A reasonable chemical reaction mechanism for methane combustion will be used.
The simulation timestep is 1e-4 seconds.
The simulation end time is 0.5 seconds.
Results are written every 0.05 seconds.
The fuel stream at the 'fuel' boundary has an inlet velocity of 0.1 m/s.
The air stream at the 'air' boundary has an inlet velocity of -0.1 m/s.
The fuel stream at the 'fuel' boundary has a temperature of 293 K.
The air stream at the 'air' boundary has a temperature of 293 K.
The fuel stream at the 'fuel' boundary is composed of pure methane (CH4).
The air stream at the 'air' boundary is composed of a standard air mixture.
