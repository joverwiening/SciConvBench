Simulate a 2D flow past a circular cylinder.
The domain is a rectangle extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m, y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m, and z = -1 m to 1 m.
A cylinder with a diameter of 2 m is centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).
The simulation starts at 0 s and ends at 0.5 s.
The time step is 0.0025 s.
Results are written every 0.05 s.
The initial velocity of the fluid is (0 0 0) m/s.
The initial pressure is 0.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 0.01 m^2/s.
The inlet velocity at x = -5 m is (1 0 0) m/s.
The pressure at the outlet (x = 5 m) is 0.
The velocity at the outlet (x = 5 m) has a zeroGradient boundary condition.
The velocity at the top boundary (y = 2.5 m) has a noSlip boundary condition.
The velocity at the bottom boundary (y = -1.5 m) has a noSlip boundary condition.
The velocity on the cylinder surface has a noSlip boundary condition.
The boundaries in the z-direction are set to empty.
