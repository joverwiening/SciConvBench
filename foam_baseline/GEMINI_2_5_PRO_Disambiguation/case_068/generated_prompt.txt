Simulate the steady-state, 2D, compressible flow of air.
The computational domain is a channel with a length of 0.2 m and a height of 0.05 m.
A porous block is located from x=0.08 m to x=0.12 m.
The porous block spans the full height of the channel.
The inlet boundary condition is a fixed total pressure of 101325 Pa.
The inlet boundary condition is a fixed total temperature of 300 K.
The outlet boundary condition for pressure is a fixed value of 100000 Pa.
The outlet boundary condition for temperature is `inletOutlet` with an `inletValue` of 300 K.
The outlet boundary condition for velocity is `inletOutlet`.
The porous block is modeled using the Darcy-Forchheimer model.
The Darcy coefficient `d` is (1e9, 1e9, 1e11).
The Forchheimer coefficient `f` is (0, 0, 0).
A volumetric heat source of 1e6 W/m^3 is applied to the porous block.
