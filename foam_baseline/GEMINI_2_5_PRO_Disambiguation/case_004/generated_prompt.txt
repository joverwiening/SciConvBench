Use the buoyantFoam solver.
The simulation domain is a rectangular cavity 9 units wide and 1 unit tall.
The domain is extruded one cell in the spanwise direction.
The mesh consists of 90 cells in the x-direction and 10 cells in the y-direction.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction with a magnitude of 9.81 m/s^2.
The turbulence model is k-epsilon.
The simulation runs up to 1000 seconds of physical time.
A fixed timestep of 1 second is used.
Results are written every 50 timesteps.
The thermophysical model is heRhoThermo with a Boussinesq equation of state.
The reference density rho0 is 1.0 kg/m^3.
The reference temperature T0 is 300 K.
The thermal expansion coefficient beta is 3.3e-3 1/K.
The molecular weight molWeight is 29.
The specific heat at constant volume Cv is 714.3 J/(kg*K).
The standard heat of formation Hf is 0.
The dynamic viscosity mu is 1.5e-5 Pa*s.
The Prandtl number Pr is 0.714.
The turbulent Prandtl number Prt is 0.85.
The velocity field is initialized to (0 0 0) m/s.
The temperature field is initialized to a uniform value of 300 K.
The turbulent kinetic energy k is initialized to a uniform value of 1e-5 m^2/s^2.
The turbulent dissipation rate epsilon is initialized to a uniform value of 1e-5 m^2/s^3.
The turbulent viscosity nut is initialized to a uniform value of 0 m^2/s.
The turbulent thermal diffusivity alphat is initialized to a uniform value of 0 m^2/s.
The pressure p and relative pressure p_rgh fields are initialized to a uniform value of 0.
A no-slip boundary condition is applied to the velocity on all solid walls.
The bottom wall has a fixed temperature of 305 K.
The top wall has a fixed temperature of 295 K.
The vertical side walls are adiabatic.
The front and back patches are of type empty.
For k, the kqRWallFunction is used on walls.
For epsilon, the epsilonWallFunction is used on walls.
For nut, the nutkWallFunction is used on walls.
For alphat, the compressible::alphatJayatillekeWallFunction is used on walls.
For p, the calculated boundary condition is used on walls.
For p_rgh, the fixedFluxPressure boundary condition is used on walls.
The solvers for p_rgh, U, e, k, and epsilon have a tolerance of 1e-8 and a relative tolerance of 0.01.
The Final solvers for all fields have a relative tolerance of 0.
The ddtScheme is Euler.
The gradSchemes use Gauss linear.
The divSchemes use Gauss linearUpwindV for velocity, Gauss upwind for turbulence fields, and Gauss linear for pressure.
The laplacianSchemes use Gauss linear corrected.
The interpolationSchemes use linear.
The snGradSchemes use corrected.
