Simulate a hot plume rising from a heat source in a 2D room.
The simulation is turbulent.
The room is 4 meters high.
The room is 2 meters wide.
The heat source is located on the floor.
The heat source is 0.2 meters wide.
The heat source has a fixed temperature of 400 K.
The initial air temperature in the room is 300 K.
The RANS k-epsilon turbulence model will be used.
The top boundary is an open atmosphere.
The boundary condition for p_rgh at the top is 'totalPressure' with a value of 0.
The boundary condition for U at the top is 'pressureInletOutletVelocity'.
The boundary condition for T at the top is 'inletOutlet' with an inlet value of 300 K.
The boundary conditions for k and epsilon at the top are 'inletOutlet'.
A slip velocity boundary condition is applied to the side walls.
The thermal boundary condition for the side walls was not specified.
