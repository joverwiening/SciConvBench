Set up a multi-region conjugate heat transfer simulation.
The simulation domain consists of a solid block with an internal porous channel.
The solid block is a rectangular prism with dimensions 0.2m x 0.2m x 0.5m.
The solid material is aluminum.
The porous channel is a rectangular prism with dimensions 0.1m x 0.1m, centered within the solid block and running along its length.
The fluid flowing through the porous channel is water.
The inlet velocity of the water is (0.05, 0, 0) m/s.
The inlet temperature of the water is 353.15 K.
The porous medium is defined by Darcy coefficients of (1e6, 1e6, 1e6) and Forchheimer coefficients of (100, 100, 100).
A reasonable value for the volumetric porosity (epsilon) should be assumed based on best practices.
The outer walls of the solid block have a convective thermal boundary condition.
The reference temperature for the convective boundary condition is 293.15 K.
The heat transfer coefficient for the convective boundary condition is 10 W/(m^2*K).
A zero-gradient pressure condition should be applied at the outlet of the porous channel.
