Simulate the steady-state, incompressible flow of water through a channel containing a porous block.
The simulation will be performed in 2D.

The inlet boundary condition is a fixed velocity of (0.1 0 0) m/s.
The outlet boundary condition is a fixed pressure of 0.
Use a standard value for the density of water.
