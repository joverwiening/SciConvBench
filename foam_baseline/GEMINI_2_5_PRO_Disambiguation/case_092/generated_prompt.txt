Model Rayleigh-Benard convection.
The simulation is two-dimensional.
The working fluid is air-like.
Gravity acts in the downward direction.
The Rayleigh number for the simulation is 1e5.
The Prandtl number for the fluid is 0.71.
The computational domain has an aspect ratio of 4:1.
All walls have a no-slip velocity boundary condition.
The side walls have a zero-gradient thermal boundary condition.
The bottom wall is heated to a fixed temperature of 305 K.
The top wall is cooled to a fixed temperature of 295 K.
