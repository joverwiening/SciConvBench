The simulation will be performed using the reactingFoam solver.
The computational domain is a rectangle.
The domain spans from 0 to 0.02 m in the x-direction.
The domain spans from -0.01 to 0.01 m in the y-direction.
The domain spans from -0.01 to 0.01 m in the z-direction.
A structured mesh with a resolution of 40 cells in the x-direction, 40 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction will be used.
The boundary at x=0 is named 'fuel'.
The boundary at x=0.02 is named 'air'.
The boundaries at the minimum and maximum y-extents are named 'outlet'.
The boundaries at the minimum and maximum z-extents are named 'frontAndBack' and are of type 'empty'.
At the 'fuel' inlet, methane (CH4) is injected at a temperature of 293 K.
The velocity at the 'fuel' inlet is 0.1 m/s in the x-direction.
At the 'air' inlet, an oxidizer mixture of 23% O2 and 77% N2 by mass is supplied.
The temperature at the 'air' inlet is 293 K.
The velocity at the 'air' inlet is -0.1 m/s in the x-direction.
The 'outlet' boundaries use a total pressure condition set to 101325 Pa.
The 'outlet' boundaries use a pressureInletOutletVelocity condition for velocity.
The entire domain is initialized with a temperature of 2000 K at time 0.
The initial species concentrations in the domain correspond to the products of stoichiometric methane-air combustion.
The simulation uses a laminar combustion model.
The momentum transport model is laminar.
The chemistry model is compressible finite-rate chemistry.
A reduced methane chemical reaction mechanism will be used for computational efficiency.
Thermodynamic properties are calculated using hePsiThermo and JANAF tables.
Transport properties are calculated using the Sutherland model.
Mixture properties are calculated using the coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture model.
The chemistry is solved using the EulerImplicit type.
The ODE solver for the chemistry is Rosenbrock43.
The simulation will run until a steady-state solution is reached or for a maximum time of 0.1 seconds.
The time step will be adjusted automatically to maintain a maximum Courant number of 0.5.
Simulation results will be written to disk every 0.005 seconds.
Standard second-order numerical schemes will be used for discretization.
