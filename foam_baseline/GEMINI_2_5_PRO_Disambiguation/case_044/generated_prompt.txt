Run a 2D turbulent buoyant cavity simulation.
The simulation uses the k-epsilon turbulence model.
The Rayleigh number is approximately 1e6.
The cavity is a square with dimensions of 0.10 m by 0.10 m.
The front and back patches use an 'empty' boundary condition.
The left wall is a hot wall with a fixed temperature of 303 K.
The velocity boundary condition for the left wall is noSlip.
The pressure (p_rgh) boundary condition for the left wall is fixedFluxPressure.
The turbulent kinetic energy (k) boundary condition for the left wall is kqRWallFunction.
The turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) boundary condition for the left wall is epsilonWallFunction.
The turbulent viscosity (nut) boundary condition for the left wall is nutkWallFunction.
The right wall is a cold wall with a fixed temperature of 293 K.
The velocity boundary condition for the right wall is noSlip.
The pressure (p_rgh) boundary condition for the right wall is fixedFluxPressure.
The turbulent kinetic energy (k) boundary condition for the right wall is kqRWallFunction.
The turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) boundary condition for the right wall is epsilonWallFunction.
The turbulent viscosity (nut) boundary condition for the right wall is nutkWallFunction.
