Simulate a 2D flow past a circular cylinder.
The simulation is transient.
The simulation runs from a start time of 0 s to an end time of 0.5 s.
The time step is 0.0025 s.
Simulation results are written every 0.05 s.
The computational domain is a rectangle.
The domain extends from x = -5 m to x = 5 m.
The domain extends from y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m.
The domain extends from z = -1 m to z = 1 m.
The cylinder has a diameter of 2 m.
The cylinder is centered at the origin (x=0, y=0).
The inlet boundary is on the left side of the domain.
The inlet velocity is U = (1 0 0) m/s.
The outlet boundary is on the right side of the domain.
The outlet velocity has a zeroGradient condition.
The outlet pressure is a fixed value of 0.
The top and bottom walls have a noSlip velocity condition.
The top and bottom walls have a zeroGradient pressure condition.
The cylinder wall has a noSlip velocity condition.
The cylinder wall has a zeroGradient pressure condition.
The front and back faces are of type 'empty' for a 2D simulation.
The initial velocity field is U = (0 0 0) m/s.
The initial pressure field is p = 0.
The flow is laminar.
The fluid is a generalised Newtonian fluid.
The viscosity model is CrossPowerLaw.
The zero-shear-rate viscosity (nu0) is 0.01 m^2/s.
The infinite-shear-rate viscosity (nuInf) is 10 m^2/s.
The power-law index (m) is 0.4.
The transition index (n) is 3.
The solver to be used is pimpleFoam.
The target cell size on the cylinder surface will be 0.01 m.
The time derivative term will use a second-order accurate backward scheme.
The convection terms will use a second-order accurate, bounded scheme.
The drag and lift forces on the cylinder will be calculated during the simulation.
The PIMPLE algorithm will use 2 outer correctors.
The pressure-velocity coupling loop will use 1 inner corrector.
