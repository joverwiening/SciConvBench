Simulate a fluidized bed using an Euler-Euler model.
The simulation will be three-dimensional.
The computational domain dimensions are 0.1 m in width, 0.5 m in height, and 0.1 m in depth.
The gas phase has a density of 1.2 kg/m^3.
The gas phase has a kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The solid phase consists of particles with a density of 2500 kg/m^3.
The diameter of the solid particles is 200 microns.
The superficial gas velocity at the inlet is 0.2 m/s.
The initial height of the packed solid particle bed is 0.2 m.
The drag model for gas-solid momentum exchange will be the SchillerNaumann model.
The Gidaspow drag model is an acceptable alternative.
The coefficient of restitution for particle-particle collisions is 0.9.
