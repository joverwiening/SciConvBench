Simulate a hot gas bubble rising in a liquid.
The simulation is two-dimensional.
The computational domain is a rectangle 0.1m wide and 0.4m high.
A gas bubble with an initial radius of 0.02m is centered at (0.05, 0.05).
The liquid is initially stationary.
The liquid density is 1000 kg/m^3.
The gas density is 1.2 kg/m^3.
The liquid viscosity is 1e-3 Pa.s.
The gas viscosity is 1.8e-5 Pa.s.
The surface tension coefficient between the gas and liquid is 0.07 N/m.
The simulation includes multiphase flow, heat transfer, and buoyancy effects.
