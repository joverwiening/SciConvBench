Perform an incompressible lid-driven cavity flow simulation.
The cavity is a square with dimensions of 1 unit in x, 1 unit in y, and 0.1 units in z.
Use a grid of 20 cells in the x-direction, 20 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The top wall is named 'movingWall'.
The side and bottom walls are named 'fixedWalls'.
The front and back faces are designated as 'empty'.
The 'movingWall' moves in the x-direction with a uniform velocity of 1 m/s.
The 'fixedWalls' have a no-slip boundary condition.
The pressure boundary condition for both 'movingWall' and 'fixedWalls' is zero-gradient.
The kinematic viscosity is constant with a value of 1e-05 m^2/s.
Use the k-epsilon turbulence model.
The turbulent kinetic energy k has a kqRWallFunction boundary condition on all walls.
The turbulent dissipation rate epsilon has an epsilonWallFunction boundary condition on all walls.
The turbulent viscosity nut has a nutkWallFunction boundary condition on all walls.
Initialize the velocity field to zero at the start of the simulation.
Initialize the turbulent kinetic energy k to a uniform value of 1.5e-4.
Initialize the turbulent dissipation rate epsilon to a uniform value of 1.4e-4.
Initialize the turbulent viscosity nut to a uniform value of 0.
Use the pisoFoam solver.
Set the number of PISO correctors to 2.
Set the number of non-orthogonal correctors to 0.
The convection scheme for velocity is div(phi,U) Gauss limitedLinearV 1.
The convection scheme for k is div(phi,k) Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The convection scheme for epsilon is div(phi,epsilon) Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The viscous term scheme is div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear.
Use the GAMG solver for the pressure equation.
Use the PBiCGStab solver with a DILU preconditioner for the velocity equation.
Use the PBiCGStab solver with a DILU preconditioner for the turbulent kinetic energy equation.
Use the PBiCGStab solver with a DILU preconditioner for the turbulent dissipation rate equation.
The simulation end time is 10 seconds.
The simulation time step is 0.005 seconds.
Write the simulation results to disk every 100 time steps.
