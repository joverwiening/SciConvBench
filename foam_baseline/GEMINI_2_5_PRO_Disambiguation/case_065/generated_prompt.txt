Simulate the external aerodynamics on an Ahmed body.
The primary objective is to calculate the drag.
The flow is turbulent.
The Reynolds number for the simulation is 7.68e5.
The Ahmed body has a rear slant angle of 25 degrees.
The overall length of the Ahmed body is 1.044 meters.
Use the RANS k-Omega SST turbulence model.
The ground is a moving wall with a speed of 11 m/s.
The target y+ value on the surface of the Ahmed body is in the range of 30 to 100.
The simulation is to be run as steady-state.
The computational domain inlet is located 4 body lengths upstream of the Ahmed body.
The computational domain outlet is located 10 body lengths downstream of the Ahmed body.
