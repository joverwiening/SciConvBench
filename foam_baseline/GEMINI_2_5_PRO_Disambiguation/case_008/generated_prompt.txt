Set up a simulation for a lid-driven cavity flow.
The cavity has dimensions of 1 unit in x, 1 unit in y, and 0.1 units in z.
Use a grid of 20x20 cells in the x and y directions, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The top wall is named 'movingWall' and moves in the x-direction with a uniform velocity of 1 m/s.
The other three walls are named 'fixedWalls' and have a no-slip boundary condition.
The front and back faces are designated as 'empty'.
The simulation runs from time 0 to 10.
The time step is 0.005 units.
Results are output every 100 time steps.
The kinematic viscosity is constant at 1e-05 m^2/s.
Use the pisoFoam solver.
Use the k-epsilon turbulence model.
Initialize the entire domain with zero velocity.
Apply a zeroGradient boundary condition for pressure on all walls.
Use the convection scheme 'Gauss limitedLinearV 1' for the momentum equation.
Use the convection scheme 'Gauss limitedLinear 1' for the turbulent kinetic energy equation.
Use the convection scheme 'Gauss limitedLinear 1' for the turbulent dissipation rate equation.
Use the scheme 'Gauss linear' for the viscous term.
