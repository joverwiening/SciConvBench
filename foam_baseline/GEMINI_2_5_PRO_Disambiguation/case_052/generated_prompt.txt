Simulate supersonic flow over a two-dimensional forward-facing step.
The purpose of the simulation is to visualize the shock structure.
The inlet Mach number is 3.
The step height is 0.02 meters.
The computational domain extends 0.1 meters upstream of the step.
The computational domain extends 0.4 meters downstream of the step.
The total height of the computational domain is 0.1 meters.
The fluid is a perfect gas.
Viscosity is modeled using Sutherland transport.
The static pressure at the inlet is 101325 Pa.
The static temperature at the inlet is 293 K.
Use the k-omega SST turbulence model.
