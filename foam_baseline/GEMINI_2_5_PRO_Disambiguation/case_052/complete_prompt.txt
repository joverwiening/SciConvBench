Problem: Simulate a 2D supersonic, turbulent flow over a forward-facing step at Mach 3.
Solver: Use the pressure-based compressible solver `rhoPimpleFoam`.
Geometry: A 2D channel of height 0.1m. The domain is 0.5m long. A step of height 0.02m is located at x=0.1m.
Mesh: Use `blockMeshDict` to create a structured mesh. Total cells ~20000. Refine the mesh near the step corner and where the shock is expected to impinge on the bottom wall.
Turbulence Model: Use the `k-omega SST` RAS model.
Thermo-physical Properties: Use `perfectGas` with `sutherland` transport for viscosity. Free-stream static temperature is 293 K, pressure is 101325 Pa. Gamma = 1.4.
Boundary Conditions (in `0/` directory):
- `inlet`: `fixedValue` for U calculated from M=3 (U=(1020.5 0 0)), T=293K, k=3.8, omega=4.5e5. Pressure is `zeroGradient`.
- `outlet`: `waveTransmissive` for p, `zeroGradient` for U, T, k, omega.
- `upperWall`: `slip` condition for all fields.
- `bottomWall` (including step): `noSlip` for U. `zeroGradient` for p. `fixedValue` T=293K. Use `kqRWallFunction`, `omegaWallFunction`, `nutkWallFunction`.
- `frontAndBack`: `empty`.
Time Controls (controlDict):
- `application`: `rhoPimpleFoam`
- `endTime`: 0.01 s
- `deltaT`: 1e-8 s (will be adjusted by solver)
- `adjustTimeStep`: yes
- `maxCo`: 0.5
- `writeInterval`: 0.001 s
