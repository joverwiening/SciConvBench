Simulate a two-dimensional lid-driven cavity flow.
The cavity is a square with a side length of 0.1 meters.
The top wall moves at a constant velocity of 1 m/s in the x-direction.
The left, bottom, and right walls are stationary no-slip walls with a velocity of (0, 0, 0).
The Reynolds number for the flow is 10.
The fluid density is 1 kg/m^3.
The fluid dynamic viscosity is 0.01 kg/(m*s).
The fluid is initially at rest.
The simulation is transient.
The simulation runs until a final time of 0.5 seconds.
