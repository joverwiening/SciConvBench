Simulate a turbulent thermal plume rising from a hot strip on the floor of a room.
The room has dimensions of 2.0 m in width, 2.0 m in length, and 2.0 m in height.
The simulation is two-dimensional, with a thickness of 0.01 m.
The hot strip is located on the floor.
The hot strip has a width of 0.2 m and a length of 0.01 m.
The temperature of the hot strip is a fixed value of 400 K.
The initial temperature of the air in the room is 300 K.
The floor area not covered by the hot strip has a fixed temperature of 300 K.
The walls have a fixed temperature of 300 K.
The ceiling is an outlet.
The ceiling's temperature boundary condition is of type `inletOutlet` with an `inletValue` of 300 K.
