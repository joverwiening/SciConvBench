Simulate a 2D flow past a circular cylinder.
Use the pimpleFoam solver for an incompressible unsteady flow.
The domain is a rectangle extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m, y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m, and z = -1 m to 1 m.
A cylinder with a diameter of 2 m is centered at the origin.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 0.01 m^2/s.
The simulation starts at 0 s and ends at 0.5 s.
The time step deltaT is 0.0025 s.
Results are written every 0.05 s.
The initial velocity field is (0 0 0) m/s.
The initial pressure field is 0.
The inlet velocity at x = -5 m is (1 0 0) m/s.
The inlet pressure at x = -5 m has a zeroGradient condition.
The outlet pressure at x = 5 m is 0.
The outlet velocity at x = 5 m has a zeroGradient condition.
The cylinder surface has a noSlip velocity boundary condition.
The cylinder surface has a zeroGradient pressure boundary condition.
The top and bottom walls have a noSlip velocity boundary condition.
The top and bottom walls have a zeroGradient pressure boundary condition.
The front and back patches have an 'empty' boundary condition to ensure a 2D simulation.
