Run a transient laminar two-phase dam-break simulation with obstacles using the interFoam solver.
The simulation domain is a cube defined by the corners (0,0,0) and (1,1,1).
The domain is discretized with a uniform mesh of 32 x 32 x 32 cells.
An obstacle is present in the domain, defined as a box from (0.375 0.375 0) to (0.625 0.625 0.25).
Gravity is set to (0 0 -9.81).
The top face of the domain at z=1 is an open atmosphere patch.
The remaining five faces of the domain are wall patches.
The simulation involves two phases: water and air.
The density of water is 1000 kg/m^3.
The kinematic viscosity of water is 1.0e-6 m^2/s.
The density of air is 1 kg/m^3.
The kinematic viscosity of air is 1.48e-5 m^2/s.
The surface tension between water and air is 0.07 N/m.
The initial condition for the water volume fraction is 1 inside the box from (0, 0, 0) to (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75), and 0 elsewhere.
The simulation time step is 1e-3 seconds.
The simulation end time is 0.1 seconds.
The simulation results are written every 0.02 seconds.
The boundary condition for pressure (p_rgh) on the open atmosphere patch is totalPressure with a value of 0.
The boundary condition for the water volume fraction (alpha.water) on the wall patches is zeroGradient.
The boundary condition for pressure (p_rgh) on the wall patches is fixedFluxPressure.
The boundary condition for velocity (U) on the wall patches is noSlip.
The boundary condition for velocity (U) on the open atmosphere patch is pressureInletOutletVelocity.
