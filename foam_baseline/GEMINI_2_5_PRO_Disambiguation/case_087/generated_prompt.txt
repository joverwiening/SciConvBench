Set up a simulation for supersonic flow over a forward-facing step.
The simulation is two-dimensional.
The flow is inviscid.
The inlet Mach number is 3.
The freestream static pressure at the inlet is 101325 Pa.
The freestream static temperature at the inlet is 300 K.
Use a ratio of specific heats of 1.4.
The computational domain has a total length of 3 meters.
The computational domain has a total height of 1 meter.
The forward-facing step is located at x=0.6 meters.
The height of the forward-facing step is 0.2 meters.
The top boundary of the domain has a slip boundary condition.
The simulation should be run to find a steady-state solution.
