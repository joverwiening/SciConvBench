Simulate a compressible, turbulent flow over a NACA0012 airfoil.
The objective of the simulation is to visualize shock waves.
The freestream Mach number is 0.85.
The airfoil has a chord length of 1 meter.
The angle of attack is 0 degrees.
The freestream static temperature is 288 K.
The freestream static pressure is 101325 Pa.
The Reynolds number should be derived from the specified freestream conditions and fluid properties.
The fluid is air, modeled as a perfect gas.
Use the Sutherland model for viscosity.
The k-Omega SST turbulence model should be used.
The target y+ value for the near-wall mesh should be in the range of 30 to 100.
