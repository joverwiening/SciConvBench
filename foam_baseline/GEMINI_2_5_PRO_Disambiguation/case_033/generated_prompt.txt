Simulate turbulent natural convection from a hot vertical plate.
The simulation should visualize the development of the thermal plume.
The simulation domain is a two-dimensional rectangular enclosure.
The enclosure is 2.0 meters wide and 3.0 meters high.
A 1.0 meter tall heated plate is located on the left vertical wall.
The bottom edge of the heated plate is positioned 1.0 meter from the bottom of the enclosure.
The heated plate has a constant surface temperature of 350 K.
The portions of the left wall above and below the heated plate are adiabatic.
The initial temperature of the air in the enclosure is 300 K.
The right vertical wall is maintained at a constant temperature of 300 K.
The top and bottom walls of the enclosure are adiabatic.
