The cavity is a square with dimensions of 1 unit in x, 1 unit in y, and 0.1 units in z.
The simulation runs from time 0 to 10 with a time step of 0.005 units.
Results are output every 100 time steps.
The kinematic viscosity (nu) is a constant value of 1e-05 m^2/s.
Use the k-epsilon turbulence model.
Initialize the turbulence field k to a uniform value of 1.5e-4.
Initialize the turbulence field epsilon to a uniform value of 1.4e-4.
Initialize the turbulence field nut to a uniform value of 0.
Use the pisoFoam solver.
The top wall moves in the x-direction with a uniform velocity of 1 m/s.
The bottom, left, and right walls have a no-slip boundary condition.
The mesh resolution is 20 cells in the x-direction.
The mesh resolution is 20 cells in the y-direction.
The mesh resolution is 1 cell in the z-direction.
The pressure boundary condition on all walls is zeroGradient.
The front and back patches are of type empty.
The wall function for k is kqRWallFunction on all walls.
The wall function for epsilon is epsilonWallFunction on all walls.
The wall function for nut is nutkWallFunction on all walls.
The convection scheme for velocity is div(phi,U) Gauss limitedLinearV 1.
The convection scheme for k is div(phi,k) Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The convection scheme for epsilon is div(phi,epsilon) Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The scheme for the viscous term is div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear.
