Simulate a one-dimensional shock tube problem.
The total length of the tube is 1 meter, from x=0 to x=1.
A diaphragm is initially located at x=0.5 meters.
The initial pressure to the left of the diaphragm is 100000 Pa.
The initial density to the left of the diaphragm is 1.0 kg/m^3.
The initial pressure to the right of the diaphragm is 10000 Pa.
The initial density to the right of the diaphragm is 0.125 kg/m^3.
The initial velocity of the gas throughout the tube is 0 m/s.
The ratio of specific heats for the gas is 1.4.
The boundary condition at the left end of the tube (x=0) is a reflective wall.
The simulation should run until the shock wave reflects off the right end wall of the tube.
