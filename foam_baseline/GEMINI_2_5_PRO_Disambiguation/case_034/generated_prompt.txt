Run a conjugate heat transfer simulation of a buoyant cavity.
Use the chtMultiRegionFoam solver.
The simulation is transient.
The domain consists of a fluid region and a solid region.
The outer domain dimensions are 1.0 m in x, 1.0 m in y, and 0.01 m in z.
A solid block is located in the center of the domain.
The solid block dimensions are 0.4 m in x and 0.4 m in y.
The bottom-left corner of the solid block is at coordinate (0.3, 0.3, 0).
The fluid is air, treated as a compressible perfect gas.
The solid is a copper-like material.
The density of the solid is 8960 kg/m^3.
The specific heat of the solid is 385 J/kg/K.
The thermal conductivity of the solid is 401 W/m/K.
Gravitational acceleration is -9.81 m/s^2 in the y-direction.
The left wall of the fluid cavity has a fixed temperature of 310 K.
The right wall of the fluid cavity has a fixed temperature of 290 K.
The top wall of the fluid cavity is adiabatic.
The bottom wall of the fluid cavity is adiabatic.
The front and back walls of the domain use the empty boundary condition type.
All walls (left, right, top, bottom) have a no-slip velocity boundary condition.
The initial temperature for both the fluid and solid regions is 300 K.
The initial velocity in the domain is zero.
The initial pressure in the domain is 101325 Pa.
The simulation runs for a total time of 1000 seconds.
