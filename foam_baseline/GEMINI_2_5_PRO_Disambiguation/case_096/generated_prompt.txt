Simulate Rayleigh-Benard convection.
The simulation is two-dimensional.
The Rayleigh number is approximately 1e5.
The Prandtl number is 0.71.
The domain aspect ratio, defined as width divided by height, is 1.0.
The velocity boundary condition for the top and bottom walls is noSlip.
The velocity boundary condition for the side walls is noSlip.
The thermal boundary condition for the bottom wall is a fixed temperature of 294.0 K.
The thermal boundary condition for the top wall is a fixed temperature of 293.0 K.
The thermal boundary condition for the side walls is zeroGradient.
The simulation should be run to observe the formation of convection cells.
