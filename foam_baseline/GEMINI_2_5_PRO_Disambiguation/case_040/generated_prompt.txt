Simulate transient flow through a porous dam.
The simulation should show how the water front moves through the porous structure.
The dam is initially dry.
Water is released on the upstream side at the start of the simulation.
The dam's cross-section is 1 meter wide, located from x=2m to x=3m.
The dam's cross-section is 2 meters high.
The initial water column on the upstream side has a height of 1.5 meters.
Assume a reasonable value for the saturated hydraulic conductivity based on best practices.
Assume a reasonable soil type for the dam material based on best practices.
The downstream boundary is a solid wall with a no-slip condition for velocity.
The top boundary is an open atmosphere.

For the top boundary, use `totalPressure` with p0 uniform 0 for pressure (p_rgh).
For the top boundary, use `inletOutlet` for the water phase fraction (alpha.water).
The bottom boundary has a `noSlip` condition for velocity (U).
