Simulate conjugate heat transfer between a solid porous block and a fluid.
The simulation is two-dimensional.
The 2D simulation is achieved by using empty boundary conditions for the front and back patches.
The overall computational domain dimensions are 0.5 m x 0.1 m x 0.01 m.
The solid porous block dimensions are 0.1 m x 0.05 m x 0.01 m.
The solid block is centered within the computational domain.
The fluid is air.
The air is modeled as a compressible perfect gas.
The effective thermal conductivity of the solid block is 10 W/m/K.
The density of the solid block is 500 kg/m^3.
The specific heat capacity of the solid block is 900 J/kg/K.
Do not apply a porosity momentum source in the solid region.
The effect of porosity is accounted for in the provided effective thermophysical properties.
