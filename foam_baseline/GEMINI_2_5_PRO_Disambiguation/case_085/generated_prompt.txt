Model the pulverized coal combustion in a furnace.
The furnace geometry is a 2D rectangle, 2 meters high and 0.5 meters wide.
The inlet is located at the bottom center of the furnace.
The outlet is located at the top of the furnace.
Assume a reasonable inlet width based on best practices.
The hot air enters the furnace with a velocity of (0, 20, 0) m/s.
The inlet temperature of the hot air is 400 K.
Coal particles are injected at a mass flow rate of 0.1 kg/s.
Assume a reasonable coal composition based on its proximate and ultimate analysis.
The coal particle size follows a Rosin-Rammler distribution.
The mean diameter for the particle size distribution is 100 microns.
Assume a reasonable spread parameter for the Rosin-Rammler distribution.
The furnace walls are maintained at a fixed temperature of 1200 K.
The model must include the effects of radiation.
A suitable turbulence model should be used for the simulation.
