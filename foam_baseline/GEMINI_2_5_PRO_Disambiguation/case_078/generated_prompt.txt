Model sloshing in a tank using the interFoam solver.
The simulation is transient and gravity-driven.
The tank is 1 meter long, 0.6 meters wide, and 0.6 meters high.
Run the simulation as a 2D case.
Use a mesh with 200 cells in the x-direction, 120 cells in the z-direction, and 1 cell in the y-direction.
The front and back patches are of type 'empty'.
The sloshing is initiated with an initially tilted free surface of water.
The initial water depth varies linearly from 0.25 meters at x=0 to 0.35 meters at x=1.
The top boundary is open to the atmosphere.
For the top boundary, the velocity (U) condition is pressureInletOutletVelocity.
For the top boundary, the pressure (p_rgh) condition is totalPressure with p0 set to a uniform value of 0.
For the top boundary, the phase fraction (alpha.water) condition is inletOutlet with an inletValue of uniform 0.
For the tank walls, the velocity (U) condition is noSlip.
For the tank walls, the pressure (p_rgh) condition is fixedFluxPressure.
For the tank walls, the phase fraction (alpha.water) condition is zeroGradient.
