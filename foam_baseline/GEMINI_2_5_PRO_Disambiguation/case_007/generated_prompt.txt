Perform an incompressible lid-driven cavity flow simulation using the pisoFoam solver.
The computational domain has physical dimensions of 1 unit in x, 1 unit in y, and 0.1 units in z.
The mesh consists of a 20x20x1 grid of cells.
The top wall is named 'movingWall' and moves in the x-direction with a uniform velocity of 1 m/s.
The other three walls are named 'fixedWalls' and have a no-slip boundary condition.
The front and back faces are of type 'empty'.
The simulation runs from time 0 to 10.
The time step is 0.005.
Simulation results are written every 100 time steps.
The kinematic viscosity is constant at 1e-05 m^2/s.
Use the k-epsilon turbulence model.
The initial velocity field is zero everywhere.
The initial turbulent kinetic energy (k) is 1.5e-4.
The initial turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) is 1.4e-4.
The initial turbulent viscosity (nut) is 0.
For the pressure field, use a zeroGradient boundary condition on the 'movingWall' and 'fixedWalls'.
For k, use the kqRWallFunction boundary condition on all walls.
For epsilon, use the epsilonWallFunction boundary condition on all walls.
For nut, use the nutkWallFunction boundary condition on all walls.
Use the Euler scheme for time discretization.
For convection of velocity, use the scheme 'div(phi,U) Gauss limitedLinearV 1'.
For convection of k, use the scheme 'div(phi,k) Gauss limitedLinear 1'.
For convection of epsilon, use the scheme 'div(phi,epsilon) Gauss limitedLinear 1'.
For the viscous term, use the scheme 'div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear'.
Use the PCG solver with a DIC preconditioner for the pressure equation.
Use the PBiCGStab solver with a DILU preconditioner for the velocity equation.
Use the PBiCGStab solver with a DILU preconditioner for the turbulent kinetic energy equation.
Use the PBiCGStab solver with a DILU preconditioner for the turbulent dissipation rate equation.
