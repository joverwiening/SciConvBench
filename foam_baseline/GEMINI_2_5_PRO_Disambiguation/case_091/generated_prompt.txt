Simulate natural convection in a square cavity.
The side length of the cavity is 0.1 meters.
Use a buoyant solver suitable for air.
The simulation is transient and runs for 200 seconds.
The left wall has a fixed temperature of 313.15 K.
The right wall has a fixed temperature of 293.15 K.
The top and bottom walls are adiabatic.
Gravitational acceleration is 9.81 m/s² and acts in the negative y-direction.
Model the air as a perfect gas.
Do not use the Boussinesq approximation.
Assume a reasonable value for the kinematic viscosity of air.
