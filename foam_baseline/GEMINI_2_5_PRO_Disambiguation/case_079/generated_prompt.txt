Model a single air bubble rising in a column of quiescent liquid.
The model must be two-dimensional.
A Volume of Fluid (VOF) solver must be used.
The width of the liquid column is 0.05 m.
The height of the liquid column is 0.2 m.
The initial diameter of the air bubble is 0.01 m.
The initial x-coordinate of the center of the air bubble is 0.025 m.
The initial y-coordinate of the center of the air bubble is 0.025 m.
The density of the liquid is 1000 kg/m^3.
The kinematic viscosity of the liquid is 1e-6 m^2/s.
The density of the air is 1.2 kg/m^3.
The kinematic viscosity of the air is 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
