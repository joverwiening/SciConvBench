Simulate a flame propagating through a closed tube.
The simulation domain is three-dimensional.
The tube dimensions are 0.5 meters in length, 0.01 meters in width, and 0.01 meters in height.
The tube is filled with a stoichiometric hydrogen-air mixture.
The initial temperature of the mixture is 300 K.
Ignition is initiated by setting the temperature in the first 2% of the domain length, from x=0 to x=0.01 meters, to 2000 K.
The initial pressure of the mixture is 101325 Pa.
Use a detailed hydrogen-air reaction mechanism, such as a 9-species, 19-reaction mechanism.
The CHEMKIN reaction mechanism files must be converted to OpenFOAM format.
The tube walls are adiabatic, using a zeroGradient boundary condition for temperature.
Apply a noSlip boundary condition for velocity on the tube walls.
Apply a fixedFluxPressure boundary condition for pressure on the tube walls.
The ignition end of the tube at x=0 is a solid wall with a noSlip velocity boundary condition.
The end of the tube opposite the ignition point at x=0.5m is a solid wall with a noSlip velocity boundary condition.
