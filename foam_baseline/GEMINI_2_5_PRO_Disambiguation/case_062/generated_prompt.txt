The simulation should show vortex shedding.
The Reynolds number for the flow is 3900.
The simulation is two-dimensional.
The simulation is transient.
The cylinder has a diameter of 1 meter.
The inlet velocity is a uniform value of (3.9, 0, 0) m/s.
The inlet boundary is located 10 diameters upstream from the cylinder center.
The outlet boundary is located 30 diameters downstream from the cylinder center.
The top and bottom boundaries are located 10 diameters from the cylinder center.
The boundary condition for velocity (U) on the cylinder surface is noSlip.
The boundary condition for turbulent kinetic energy (k) on the cylinder surface is kqRWallFunction.
The boundary condition for specific dissipation rate (omega) on the cylinder surface is omegaWallFunction.
