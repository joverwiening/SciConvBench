Simulate an incompressible flow in a wedge-shaped diffuser.
The simulation is 2D planar.
The geometry is defined by a top wall connecting (x=0, y=0.10) to (x=1, y=0.20) and a bottom wall at y=0.
The inlet is at x=0 and the outlet is at x=1.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
Use the RAS kOmegaSST turbulence model.
The inlet velocity is a fixed value of (5, 0, 0) m/s.
The inlet pressure boundary condition is zeroGradient.
The outlet pressure is a fixed value of 0.
The outlet velocity boundary condition is zeroGradient.
The top and bottom walls have a noSlip velocity boundary condition.
The top and bottom walls have a zeroGradient pressure boundary condition.
Use wall functions for the turbulence quantities on the top and bottom walls.
