Q: What is the Reynolds number for the channel flow simulation?
A: The Reynolds number isn't explicitly specified in the requirements. Make a reasonable assumption based on best practices.

However, you can calculate it from the given parameters:
- Channel height = 0.1m
- Bulk velocity = 0.5 m/s
- Kinematic viscosity = 1e-5 m²/s
Re = (0.1 * 0.5) / 1e-5 = 5000
