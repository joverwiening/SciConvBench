Simulate a 2D flow past a circular cylinder in a rectangular domain from x = -5 m to x = 5 m, y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m, and z = -1 to 1m.
The cylinder has a diameter of 2 m and is centered at the origin (0, 0, 0).
Use pimpleFoam solver for incompressible unsteady flow.
Set simulation time parameters: startTime = 0 s, endTime = 0.5 s, deltaT = 0.0025 s.
Write results every 0.05 s.
Initialize the field with quiescent conditions: U = (0 0 0), p = 0.
Use laminar flow model with generalisednewtonian and crosspowerlaw viscosity model.
Set viscosity to 0.01 m^2/s with cross power law parameters: nuinf=10, m=0.4, n=3.
Apply inlet velocity U = (1 0 0) m/s at the left boundary.
Set outlet pressure p = 0 at the right boundary.
Apply noSlip boundary condition for U at the cylinder surface.
Apply noSlip boundary condition for U at the top and bottom boundaries.
Apply zeroGradient boundary condition for p at the top, bottom, and cylinder walls.
