The simulation domain is rectangular from x = -5 m to x = 5 m, y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m, and z = -1 to 1 m.
A cylinder of diameter 2 m is centered at the origin (0, 0, 0).
The inlet boundary (left) has uniform velocity U = (1 0 0) m/s.
The outlet boundary (right) has zeroGradient velocity and reference pressure p = 0.
Top and bottom walls have noSlip velocity and zeroGradient pressure.
The cylinder wall has noSlip velocity and zeroGradient pressure.
Back and front faces are empty (2D simulation).
The solver is pimpleFoam for incompressible unsteady flow.
Simulation runs from 0 s to 0.5 s with deltaT = 0.0025 s.
Results are written every 0.05 s.
Initial conditions are U = (0 0 0) and p = 0.
The flow is laminar using generalisednewtonian model.
The viscosity model is crosspowerlaw with nuinf=10, m=0.4 and n=3.
The viscosity is 0.01 m²/s.
Standard convergence tolerances will be used for pressure and velocity.
Standard fluid density will be used for the incompressible flow.
