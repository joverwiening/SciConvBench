The simulation is for a 2D lid-driven cavity flow.
The cavity has dimensions of 0.1m x 0.1m.
The top wall moves to the right with a velocity of 1 m/s.
The fluid is incompressible with kinematic viscosity of 0.001 m²/s.
The Reynolds number is 100.
The domain is discretized using a uniform Cartesian mesh of 20x20 cells.
Standard central differencing scheme should be used for spatial discretization.
Semi-implicit time integration scheme should be used.
The pressure-velocity coupling should use a tolerance of 1e-6 for convergence.
All walls except the top are stationary with no-slip boundary conditions.
