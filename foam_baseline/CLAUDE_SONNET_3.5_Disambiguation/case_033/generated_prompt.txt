Simulate natural convection from a vertical hot plate in a 2D domain.
The domain is 3.0 meters tall and 2.0 meters wide.
The hot plate is located on the left wall between y=1.0m and y=2.0m (1.0m height).
The hot plate temperature is maintained at 350 K.
The ambient air temperature is 300 K.
The simulation is turbulent.
The domain has a nominal thickness of 0.01m with front/back empty patches.
The thermal plume development should be visualized.
