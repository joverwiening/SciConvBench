Simulate supersonic flow over a forward-facing step using OpenFOAM with rhoPimpleFoam solver.
The channel is 0.5m long and 0.1m high.
The forward-facing step is 0.02m high and located at x=0.1m from the inlet.
Inlet conditions are: Mach 3, velocity (1020.5 0 0) m/s, temperature 293 K, pressure 101325 Pa.
Bottom wall and step have fixed temperature of 293K with no-slip velocity condition.
Upper wall has slip conditions for all fields.
Use approximately 20000 cells with refinement near step corner and expected shock impingement locations.
The simulation should capture and visualize the shock structure in the flow field.
