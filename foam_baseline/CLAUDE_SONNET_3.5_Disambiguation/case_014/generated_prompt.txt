Set up a laminar counter-flow diffusion flame simulation with the following specifications:
The domain is rectangular spanning x from 0 to 0.02 m and y from -0.01 to 0.01 m and z from -0.01 to 0.01 m.
Use 100 grid points in x direction and 40 in y direction and 1 cell in z direction.
Left boundary (x=0) is named fuel inlet.
Right boundary (x=0.02) is named air inlet.
Top and bottom boundaries are named outlet.
FrontAndBack faces are empty.
Initial temperature is 2000 K throughout the domain.
Fuel inlet velocity is 0.1 m/s in positive x direction.
Air inlet velocity is -0.1 m/s in negative x direction.
Fuel composition is pure methane with Y_CH4 = 1.0.
Air composition is Y_O2 = 0.23 and Y_N2 = 0.77.
Use GRI-Mech 3.0 mechanism for methane-air combustion.
Operating pressure is 1 atm.
Initial species mass fractions are air composition (Y_O2 = 0.23, Y_N2 = 0.77).
Initial velocity field is zero everywhere except at boundaries.
Use chemistry type of EulerImplicit.
Use ODE solver of Rosenbrock43.
Use laminar combustion model.
Use laminar momentum transport.
Use compressible finite-rate chemistry with hePsiThermo.
Use coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture for mixing.
Use Sutherland transport model.
Use JANAF thermodynamics.
Use reactingFoam solver.
Time step is 1e-4 seconds.
End time is 0.5 seconds.
Write results at intervals of 0.05 seconds.
