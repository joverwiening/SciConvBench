Simulate a 2D flow past a circular cylinder with the following specifications:
The domain is rectangular extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m in streamwise direction.
The domain extends from y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m in cross-stream direction.
The domain extends from z = -1 to 1m.
A cylinder of diameter 2 m is centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).
Use pimpleFoam solver for incompressible unsteady flow.
Set startTime = 0 s and endTime = 0.5 s.
Use deltaT = 0.0025 s with results written every 0.05 s.
Initialize the field with quiescent fluid (U = (0 0 0), p = 0).
Set kinematic viscosity to 0.01 m^2/s.
Apply inlet velocity U = (1 0 0) m/s at the left boundary.
At the outlet (right boundary): set U to zeroGradient and p = 0.
At the top and bottom walls: set U to noSlip and p to zeroGradient.
At the cylinder wall: set U to noSlip and p to zeroGradient.
