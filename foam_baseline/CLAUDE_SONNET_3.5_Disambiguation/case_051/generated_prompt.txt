Simulate a 1D shock tube problem with the following specifications:
The shock tube has a total length of 1 meter.
The working fluid is ideal air.
The left side initial conditions are: pressure = 100000 Pa, density = 1.0 kg/m³.
The right side initial conditions are: pressure = 10000 Pa, density = 0.125 kg/m³.
A diaphragm initially separates the high-pressure and low-pressure regions.
Run the simulation until the shock wave reflects off the end wall.
