Simulate conjugate heat transfer between a porous block and flowing air.
The domain dimensions are 0.5 m x 0.1 m x 0.01 m with empty front/back for 2D simulation.
A solid porous block of dimensions 0.1 m x 0.05 m x 0.01 m is centered in the domain.
The fluid is air modeled as compressible perfect gas using heRhoThermo and Sutherland transport model.
Air properties include Cp = 1005 J/kg/K and molecular weight = 28.9 kg/kmol.
The inlet air temperature is 400 K.
The inlet velocity is 1 m/s in the x-direction (1 0 0).
The initial temperature of the porous block is 300 K.
The solid block has thermal conductivity of 10 W/m/K.
The solid block has density of 500 kg/m³.
The solid block has specific heat capacity of 900 J/kg/K.
The solid block's porous nature is represented through these effective thermal properties.
