Simulate steady-state incompressible laminar flow through a 2D porous medium channel.
The domain is 0.5 m long and 0.1 m high.
The porous zone is located between x=0.2 m and x=0.3 m.
The inlet velocity is fixed at 1 m/s.
The working fluid is water with kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m²/s.
The porous zone uses the DarcyForchheimer model with coefficients d = (1e8 1e8 1e10) and f = (0 0 0).
Top and bottom boundaries are noSlip walls for velocity.
All walls have zeroGradient boundary condition for pressure.
Use standard convergence criteria for pressure and velocity residuals.
