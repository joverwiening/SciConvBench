Simulate a 2D buoyant cavity flow with dimensions 1 m width x 0.25 m height.
The bottom wall is heated to 310 K (bottomHot).
The top wall is cooled to 290 K (topCold).
The side walls have zeroGradient temperature boundary condition.
All walls have noSlip velocity boundary condition.
Initial conditions are U = (0 0 0), T = 300 K, and p_rgh = 0.
The fluid is water-like with Prandtl number Pr = 7.0.
The thermal expansion coefficient (beta) is 2.0e-4 1/K.
The kinematic viscosity (nu) is 1e-6 m^2/s.
The thermal diffusivity (alpha) is 1.429e-7 m^2/s (calculated from nu/Pr).
Use the buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam solver for a steady-state solution.
