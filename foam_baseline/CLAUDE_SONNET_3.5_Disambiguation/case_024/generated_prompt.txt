Simulate a 2D flow past a circular cylinder with the following specifications:
The domain is rectangular extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m in streamwise direction.
The domain extends from y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m in cross-stream direction.
The domain extends from z = -1 to 1 m in span-wise direction.
A cylinder of diameter 2 m is centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 0.01 m^2/s.
The simulation starts at t = 0 s and ends at t = 0.5 s.
Use time step deltaT = 0.0025 s.
Write results every 0.05 s.
Initial conditions are quiescent fluid with U = (0 0 0) and p = 0.
Inlet boundary conditions (left): U = (1 0 0) m/s.
Outlet boundary conditions (right): U = zeroGradient, p = 0.
Top and bottom walls: U = noSlip, p = zeroGradient.
Front and back faces: empty (2D simulation).
Cylinder surface: U = noSlip, p = zeroGradient.
