Set up an interFoam simulation for sloshing in a rectangular tank.
The tank dimensions are 1.0 m (x) x 0.6 m (y) x 0.1 m (z).
The initial water surface is tilted linearly from 0.25 m at x=0 to 0.35 m at x=1.
Run the simulation for 10 seconds (endTime 10).
Use initial deltaT of 1e-3 with adjustTimeStep enabled and maxCo 0.5.
Use standard kinematic viscosity values: 1e-6 m²/s for water and 1.48e-5 m²/s for air.
Include gravity effects in the simulation.
The simulation type is transient.
The tank is initially half-filled with water with a tilted surface.
