Simulate transient flow through a porous dam with the following specifications:
The computational domain is 5 meters long in x-direction and 2 meters high.
The porous dam is located between x=2m and x=3m, spanning the full height.
Initial water level is 1.5 meters high, contained in a column from x=0m to x=1m.
The dam's porosity is defined by Darcy-Forchheimer coefficients d=(1e6 1e6 0) and f=(100 100 0).
The simulation runs for 10 seconds to observe water front movement through the porous structure.
The initial condition has the downstream side of the dam (x>3m) completely dry.
Track the movement of the water front as it passes through the porous dam structure.
