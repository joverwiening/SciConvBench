Perform incompressible lid driven cavity flow simulation with the following specifications:
Domain is a square cavity of 1x1 units in x-y plane with 0.1 units thickness in z-direction.
Use a 20x20x1 grid (x,y,z directions respectively).
Simulation time runs from 0 to 10 units with time step of 0.005 units.
Output results every 100 time steps.
Use k-epsilon turbulence model.
Initialize k = 1.5e-4 uniformly with kqRWallFunction on all walls (front/back empty).
Initialize epsilon = 1.4e-4 uniformly with epsilonWallFunction on all walls (front/back empty).
Initialize nut = 0 uniformly with nutkWallFunction on all walls (front/back empty).
Use pisoFoam solver.
Initialize velocity field to zero throughout the domain.
Top wall moves with velocity of 1 m/s in x-direction.
Bottom, left, and right walls have no-slip condition (zero velocity).
Kinematic viscosity (nu) is constant at 1e-05 m^2/s.
Use convection scheme div(phi,U) Gauss limitedLinearV 1.
Use convection scheme div(phi,k) Gauss limitedLinear 1.
Use convection scheme div(phi,epsilon) Gauss limitedLinear 1.
Use div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear for viscous term.
