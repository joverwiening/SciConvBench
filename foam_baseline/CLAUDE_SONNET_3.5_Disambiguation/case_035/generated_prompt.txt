Simulate a 2D multiphase heat transfer problem of a hot air bubble rising in water.
The domain dimensions are 0.1m wide by 0.4m high with a single cell depth.
The air bubble has an initial diameter of 0.04m.
The bubble is initially centered at position (0.05, 0.05) meters.
The air bubble has an initial temperature of 350 K.
The surrounding water has an initial temperature of 300 K.
The water is initially stationary.
The top boundary uses pressureInletOutletVelocity for U, totalPressure for p_rgh, and zeroGradient for alpha.gas and T.
The bottom and side boundaries use noSlip for U, fixedFluxPressure for p_rgh, and zeroGradient for alpha.gas and T.
The front and back boundaries use empty boundary conditions.
The bubble will rise due to buoyancy effects.
