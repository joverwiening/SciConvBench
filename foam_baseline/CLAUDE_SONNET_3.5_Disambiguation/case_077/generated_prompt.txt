Simulate a 2D bubble column with dimensions 0.2 m x 1.0 m x 0.2 m.
The air is injected from the bottom with a velocity of 0.2 m/s in the upward direction.
The system operates at atmospheric pressure at the top outlet.
The system operates at standard room temperature (20°C).
Use an Euler-Euler two-phase flow approach for air and water.
The simulation is two-dimensional, considering only the width and height dimensions.
Water is initially filled in the column as the continuous phase.
Air is injected as the dispersed phase through the bottom inlet.
The top boundary is open to atmospheric pressure.
