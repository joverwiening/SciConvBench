Model natural convection in a differentially heated square cavity.
The cavity dimensions are 1m x 1m.
The left wall is maintained at a constant temperature of 310 K.
The right wall is maintained at a constant temperature of 290 K.
The top and bottom walls are adiabatic.
The working fluid is air.
The initial temperature of the air inside the cavity is 300 K.
Apply zero gradient thermal boundary conditions at the top and bottom walls.
