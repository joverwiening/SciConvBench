Configure a steady-state turbulent pipe flow simulation using simpleFoam with the k-epsilon RANS model.
The fluid is water with a kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m²/s.
The pipe diameter is 0.1 m and the pipe length is 2 m (20 diameters for fully developed flow).
The inlet velocity is 1 m/s.
Use a reasonable mesh resolution with approximately 50 cells across the diameter and 200 cells along the length.
For the initial conditions in the 0 directory, initialize the turbulent kinetic energy (k) and the turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) to zero to start from a perfectly calm state.
In the 0/k and 0/epsilon files, set the internalField to uniform 0.
For boundary conditions on the pipe wall, use kqRWallFunction for k and epsilonWallFunction for epsilon.
At the inlet, set velocity to fixed value 1 m/s, pressure to zero gradient, k to fixed value 0.00375 m²/s² (based on 5% turbulence intensity), and epsilon to fixed value 0.001 m²/s³.
At the outlet, set velocity to zero gradient, pressure to fixed value 0 Pa, k to zero gradient, and epsilon to zero gradient.
