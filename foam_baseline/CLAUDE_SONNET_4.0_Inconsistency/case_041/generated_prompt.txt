Set up a simulation of 2D laminar, incompressible flow past a cylinder at a Reynolds number of 100 to observe the von Kármán vortex street using OpenFOAM.
The domain is a rectangular channel 2.2m long and 0.41m high, with a cylinder of diameter 0.1m centered at (0.2, 0.2).
Use a kinematic viscosity of 1e-3 m²/s and a uniform inlet velocity of U = (1 0 0) m/s.
The mesh is one cell thick in the z-direction.
Use blockMesh for mesh generation following OpenFOAM best practices.
In system/controlDict, specify the solver as simpleFoam.
Set startTime to 0, endTime to 200, deltaT to 1, and writeInterval to 10.
For boundary conditions in the 0/ directory, set U at the inlet to fixedValue uniform (1 0 0), at the outlet to zeroGradient, on the cylinderWall to noSlip, on the topAndBottom walls to noSlip, and on the frontAndBack patches to empty.
For p, set inlet and cylinderWall to zeroGradient, outlet to fixedValue uniform 0, topAndBottom to zeroGradient, and frontAndBack to empty.
Run the simulation to obtain the final steady-state flow field.
