Simulate the 3D, incompressible, turbulent flow around a cube mounted on the floor of a channel using simpleFoam with a RANS kEpsilon turbulence model.
Use a fully 3D mesh with more than one cell in the spanwise direction.
To emulate an infinitely wide channel, treat the two spanwise side patches (left and right) as symmetry boundaries.
In system/blockMeshDict, define the side patches as type empty with their respective faces.
Use noSlip boundary conditions on all solid walls including floor, cube, and top wall.
Apply a fixed inlet velocity on the inlet patch.
Apply a fixed outlet pressure on the outlet patch.
Make reasonable assumptions for domain dimensions, cube size, inlet velocity magnitude, and Reynolds number based on CFD best practices.
