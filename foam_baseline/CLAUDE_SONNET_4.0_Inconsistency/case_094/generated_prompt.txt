Simulate the steady-state, compressible flow of natural gas through a pipe with a porous baffle.
The inlet total pressure is 2e5 Pa and the outlet static pressure is 1e5 Pa.
The gas is modeled as a perfect gas.
Use porousSimpleFoam as the solver.
Use thermophysicalProperties with a 'sutherland' transport model instead of transportProperties with kinematic viscosity 'nu'.
The porous zone is defined in the fvOptions dictionary with Darcy-Forchheimer coefficients.
Make reasonable assumptions based on best practices for pipe geometry including length, diameter, and the location and dimensions of the porous baffle.
Make reasonable assumptions based on best practices for the Darcy and Forchheimer coefficients for the porous baffle.
Make reasonable assumptions based on best practices for the inlet temperature of the natural gas.
