Analyze the flow of a viscous, shear-thickening Power Law fluid over a backward-facing step.
Use the nonNewtonianIcoFoam solver.
The geometry consists of an inlet, an outlet, a top wall, and a bottom wall that includes the step.
The fluid properties in transportProperties are defined with transportModel PowerLaw and n > 1.
In the 0/U boundary condition file, the walls are defined with a slip condition using type slip.
Use n = 1.3 for the power law index as a reasonable value for shear-thickening behavior.
Use backward-facing step dimensions with step height of 0.01 m, channel height of 0.02 m, inlet length of 0.05 m, and outlet length of 0.15 m.
Use an inlet velocity magnitude of 1.0 m/s as a reasonable flow condition.
Make reasonable assumptions for any other required parameters following OpenFOAM best practices.
