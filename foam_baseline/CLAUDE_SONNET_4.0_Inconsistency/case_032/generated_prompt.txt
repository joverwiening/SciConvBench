Simulate the 2D steady-state, turbulent, incompressible flow over a NACA 0012 airfoil at a 5-degree angle of attack using simpleFoam with the k-omega SST turbulence model.
The C-grid mesh is extruded one cell in the z-direction to create the 2D domain.
In blockMeshDict, the front and back patches are named 'frontAndBack' and are of 'type symmetry'.
The boundary conditions in the '0' directory for U, p, k, and omega should be set as follows: inlet with a fixed velocity of (10 0 0.1) m/s, outlet with zeroGradient, airfoil surface as a no-slip wall, and 'symmetry' for the frontAndBack patches.
The simulation aims to calculate the 2D lift and drag coefficients.
Use a reasonable Reynolds number assumption based on best practices.
Use a reasonable reference area assumption based on best practices.
Use a reasonable chord length assumption based on best practices.
Use a reasonable far-field boundary distance assumption based on best practices.
Use a reasonable mesh resolution assumption based on best practices around the airfoil.
