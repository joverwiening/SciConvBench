Set up a 3D dam break simulation with three phases: water, air, and a light oil. The domain is a rectangular tank of size 1m x 0.6m x 0.4m. Initially, a column of water (0.2m x 0.6m x 0.2m) is in one corner, with a layer of oil (0.2m x 0.6m x 0.05m) on top of it. The rest of the domain is filled with air. Use the `multiphaseEulerFoam` solver for this transient simulation. Gravity acts in the -y direction. The phases are defined in `transportProperties` as `air`, `water`, and `oil`. Use the `setFieldsDict` utility to initialize the phase fractions `alpha.air`, `alpha.water`, and `alpha.oil` in their respective regions. The walls are all no-slip, and the top boundary is an atmospheric inlet/outlet.
