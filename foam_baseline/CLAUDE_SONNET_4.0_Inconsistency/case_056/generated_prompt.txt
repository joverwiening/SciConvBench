Set up a 3D dam break simulation with three phases: water, air, and a light oil.
The domain is a rectangular tank of size 1m x 0.6m x 0.4m.
Initially, a column of water (0.2m x 0.6m x 0.2m) is in one corner.
A layer of oil (0.2m x 0.6m x 0.05m) is on top of the water column.
The rest of the domain is filled with air.
Use the twoPhaseEulerFoam solver for this transient simulation.
Gravity acts in the -y direction.
The phases are defined in transportProperties as air, water, and oil.
Use the setFieldsDict utility to initialize the phase fractions alpha.air, alpha.water, and alpha.oil in their respective regions.
The walls are all no-slip.
The top boundary is an atmospheric inlet/outlet.
