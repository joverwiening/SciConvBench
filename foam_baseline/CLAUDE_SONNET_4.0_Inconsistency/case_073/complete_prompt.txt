Simulate a single spherical particle settling in a quiescent fluid column using the DPMFoam solver. The domain is a vertical cylinder with a height of 1m (along the Y-axis) and a diameter of 0.2m. The fluid is water. A single particle with a diameter of 1mm and density of 2500 kg/m^3 is initialized at position (0, 0.9, 0). The simulation should track the particle's trajectory as it settles. In the constant/g dictionary, the acceleration due to gravity is specified as `g (0 -9.81 0);`. The fluid velocity is initialized to zero everywhere. The walls of the cylinder are `noSlip` and the top and bottom patches are simple walls.
