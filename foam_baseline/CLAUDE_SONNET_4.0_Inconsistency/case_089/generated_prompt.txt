Simulate a mixed convection lid-driven cavity problem using buoyantPimpleFoam.
The cavity has square dimensions of 0.1 m x 0.1 m (length x height).
The top lid moves at 0.1 m/s in the horizontal direction and is held at a hot temperature of 350 K.
The bottom wall is stationary and cold at 300 K.
The left and right walls are stationary no-slip walls with adiabatic temperature conditions.
Gravity acts downwards with magnitude 9.81 m/s².
Use the incompressiblePerfectGas model to account for density variations with temperature.
Use a uniform mesh with 50x50 cells for adequate resolution.
Initialize the velocity field to zero everywhere.
Initialize the pressure field to zero everywhere.
Initialize the temperature field to 325 K (average of hot and cold temperatures).
Run the simulation for 10 seconds total time.
Use an initial time step of 0.001 seconds with automatic time step adjustment.
In constant/thermophysicalProperties, specify the thermoType with hePsiThermo, incompressiblePerfectGas mixture, const transport, hConst thermo, perfectGas equationOfState, specie, and sensibleEnthalpy energy.
In the mixture section, set molWeight to 28.9, Cp to 1005, Hf to 0, mu to 1.8e-5, and Pr to 0.7.
Include BoussinesqCoeffs with TRef of 325 and beta of 0.00307 as a backup buoyancy model.
