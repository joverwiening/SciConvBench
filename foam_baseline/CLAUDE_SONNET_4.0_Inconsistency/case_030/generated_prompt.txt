Set up a 2D natural convection simulation in a square cavity using buoyantBoussinesqPimpleFoam.
The cavity has dimensions of 1 meter by 1 meter.
Use a mesh resolution of 50x50 cells.
The left wall is hot at 310 K.
The right wall is cold at 290 K.
The top and bottom walls are adiabatic with zero heat flux.
Initialize the temperature field T uniformly at 300 K.
Initialize the velocity field U at rest.
Set the bottom wall boundary condition in 0/T as fixedValue with uniform 300 K.
Use air as the working fluid with density 1.0 kg/m³, kinematic viscosity 1.5e-5 m²/s, thermal conductivity 0.025 W/m/K, specific heat capacity 1005 J/kg/K, and thermal expansion coefficient 3.3e-3 K⁻¹.
Use a time step of 0.01 seconds.
