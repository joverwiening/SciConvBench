Perform a conjugate heat transfer (CHT) simulation of an electronics component cooled by forced air convection using the chtMultiRegionSimpleFoam solver.
The setup consists of a solid silicon chip region and a fluid air region.
The chip generates heat, which is specified via fvOptions.
The entire assembly is enclosed in a housing.
The outer surface of the housing, named 'outerCasing', is perfectly insulated from the ambient environment.
Air enters at 293 K and exits at an outlet.
In the '0/air/T' file, the inlet is set to a fixed temperature.
In the '0/solid/T' file, for the insulated outer boundary, specify outerCasing with type fixedValue and value uniform 293 for ambient temperature.
Use reasonable assumptions based on best practices for inlet air velocity or mass flow rate for forced convection cooling.
Use reasonable assumptions based on best practices for silicon chip geometry and dimensions including length, width, and thickness.
Use reasonable assumptions based on best practices for heat generation rate (power) of the silicon chip in watts.
Use reasonable assumptions based on best practices for dimensions of the housing that encloses the chip and air region.
