Simulate a 2D dam break problem using the interFoam solver for two incompressible, immiscible fluids (water and air).
The domain is a rectangular tank, 0.5m long and 0.5m high, extruded by 0.01m in the z-direction to create a single layer of cells.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction, g = (0 -9.81 0).
Initially, a column of water (alpha.water=1) occupies the region x < 0.15m.
The rest of the domain is air (alpha.water=0).
Use setFieldsDict for initialization.
Use a reasonable mesh resolution based on best practices for dam break simulations.
Use reasonable time step size and total simulation time based on best practices.
Boundary setup in constant/polyMesh/boundary: leftWall, rightWall, lowerWall as type wall, atmosphere as type patch, frontAndBack as type symmetry.
Boundary conditions for U: noSlip on walls, pressureInletOutletVelocity on atmosphere, symmetry on frontAndBack.
Boundary conditions for p_rgh: fixedFluxPressure on walls and frontAndBack, totalPressure on atmosphere.
Boundary conditions for alpha.water: zeroGradient on walls and frontAndBack, inletOutlet on atmosphere.
