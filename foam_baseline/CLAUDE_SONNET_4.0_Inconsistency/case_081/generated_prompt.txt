Simulate a 2D gas-liquid bubble column using the twoPhaseEulerFoam solver.
The domain is a rectangle of 0.5 m width and 2 m height.
The two phases are air and water.
The simulation is transient.
At the bottom inlet boundary, both air and water are introduced.
The boundary conditions in the 0/alpha.air file for the inlet patch should be type fixedValue; value uniform 0.8;.
The boundary conditions in the 0/alpha.water file for the same inlet patch should be type fixedValue; value uniform 0.3;.
The velocity of air at the inlet is (0 0.2 0) m/s and water is (0 0.01 0) m/s.
The top boundary is an outlet, and the sides are no-slip walls.
Use a reasonable mesh resolution based on best practices for the 0.5m × 2m domain.
Use reasonable total simulation time and time step size based on best practices for this transient simulation.
Use reasonable initial conditions for the volume fractions of air and water throughout the domain at t=0 based on best practices.
