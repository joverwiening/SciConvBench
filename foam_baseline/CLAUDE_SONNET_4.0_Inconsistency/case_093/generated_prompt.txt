Set up a 2D dam break simulation with a column of water on the left side of a rectangular domain that is released and collapses under gravity.
The domain is initially filled with air.
Use the pimpleFoam solver.
Set endTime to 5s in the controlDict.
Use setFields to initialize a region with alpha.water = 1 for the water column and alpha.water = 0 for the air.
Set gravity to g = (0 -9.81 0).
The transportProperties dictionary should contain entries for two phases, 'water' and 'air', with their respective viscosities and densities.
Use reasonable assumptions based on best practices for the rectangular domain dimensions.
Use reasonable assumptions based on best practices for the water column dimensions (width and height) on the left side.
Use reasonable assumptions based on best practices for the mesh resolution (number of cells in x, y, and z directions).
