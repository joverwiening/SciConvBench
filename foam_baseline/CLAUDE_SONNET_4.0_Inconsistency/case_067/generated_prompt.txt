Set up a steady-state simulation for laminar, incompressible flow over a backward-facing step using the simpleFoam solver.
The domain has an inlet on the left and an outlet on the right.
The fluid enters the domain at the 'inlet' patch and exits at the 'outlet' patch.
All other boundaries are walls with a no-slip condition.
The simulation is 2D, so front and back patches are of type 'empty'.
Use a Reynolds number of 100 based on the step height as the characteristic length.
Use a mesh resolution of approximately 200 cells in the streamwise direction and 100 cells in the cross-stream direction.
The step height serves as the reference length for Reynolds number calculation.
Specify the boundary conditions in the '0/U' file as provided: inlet with zeroGradient, outlet with fixedValue uniform (0.5 0 0), stepWall and topWall with noSlip, and frontAndBack with empty type.
