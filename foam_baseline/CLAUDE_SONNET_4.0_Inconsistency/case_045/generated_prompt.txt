Perform a steady-state, incompressible simulation of airflow over a NACA 0012 airfoil at a low angle of attack.
The simulation is 2D.
The mesh is created as a single layer of cells in the z-direction.
In the constant/polyMesh/boundary file, the patches for the thin dimension are named frontAndBack and are defined with type wall.
In the 0/U file, the boundary condition for frontAndBack is set to noSlip.
In 0/p file, the boundary condition for frontAndBack is set to zeroGradient.
Use the simpleFoam solver with the k-omega SST turbulence model.
Use a reasonable low angle of attack based on best practices.
Use a reasonable Reynolds number based on best practices.
Use a reasonable freestream velocity based on best practices.
Use reasonable computational domain dimensions around the airfoil based on best practices.
