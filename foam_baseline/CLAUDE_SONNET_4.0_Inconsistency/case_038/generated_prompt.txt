Simulate thermo-elastic deformation of a solid aluminum block that is fixed on one face and heated on the opposite face.
The goal is to compute the displacement and stress due to thermal expansion.
Use solidDisplacementFoam as the application.
The aluminum block has reasonable dimensions based on best practices.
Use reasonable mesh resolution based on best practices.
Material properties for aluminum: E = 69e9 Pa, nu = 0.33, rho = 2700 kg/m^3, thermal expansion coefficient alpha = 2.3e-5 1/K, reference temperature T0 = 300 K.
Assume a temperature field T is available either prescribed or solved separately by a prior conduction solve and is used to drive thermal strain.
Boundary conditions for displacement: fixedFace is fixedValue uniform (0 0 0), all other faces are zeroGradient.
Boundary conditions for temperature: heatedFace is fixedValue uniform 400, fixedFace is zeroGradient, other faces are zeroGradient.
Set application to solidDisplacementFoam in system/controlDict.
