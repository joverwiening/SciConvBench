Configure a steady-state turbulent pipe flow simulation using simpleFoam with the following specifications:
Fluid is water with kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m²/s.
Pipe diameter is 0.1 m.
Inlet velocity is 1 m/s.
Pipe length is 40 * 0.1 m = 4 meters (40 pipe diameters).
Initial conditions for turbulent kinetic energy (k) and turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) set to zero.
In `0/k` and `0/epsilon` files, set `internalField` to `uniform 0;`.
Boundary conditions for pipe wall: 
- k uses `kqRWallFunction`
- epsilon uses `epsilonWallFunction`
RANS model is k-epsilon.
Solver is simpleFoam for steady-state simulation.
