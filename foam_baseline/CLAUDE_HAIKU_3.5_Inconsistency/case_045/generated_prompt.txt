Perform a steady-state, incompressible simulation of airflow over a NACA 0012 airfoil.
Use an angle of attack of 4 degrees.
Use a freestream velocity of 50 m/s.
Use standard air properties at sea level (density = 1.225 kg/m³, kinematic viscosity = 1.48e-5 m²/s).
The Reynolds number will be approximately 300,000.
Use a chord length of 1 meter.
Create a computational domain of 15 chord lengths total (5 chord lengths upstream, 10 chord lengths downstream).
Domain width will be 10 chord lengths.
Use a single layer of cells in the z-direction.
Patches for the thin dimension in `constant/polyMesh/boundary` will be named `frontAndBack` with `type wall`.
In `0/U` file, set `frontAndBack` boundary condition to `noSlip`.
In `0/p` file, set `frontAndBack` boundary condition to `zeroGradient`.
Use the `simpleFoam` solver with the k-omega SST turbulence model.
