Set up a 2D dam break simulation using the `interFoam` solver. The domain is 1m long and 1m high. A column of water is initialized in the region 0 < x < 0.2 and 0 < y < 0.5. The rest of the domain is air. The simulation should model the collapse of the water column under gravity. In the `constant/g` file, define the acceleration due to gravity as `g (0 -9.81 0);`. Use `setFieldsDict` to initialize the `alpha.water` field to 1 in the water column region and 0 elsewhere. The top boundary is an open atmosphere (`pressureInletOutletVelocity`), and all other boundaries are walls (`noSlip`).
