Perform a 2D heat conduction simulation using laplacianFoam for a square aluminum plate.
The domain is 1m x 1m and one cell thick.
Use a mesh resolution of 50x50 cells.
Set material properties: thermal conductivity of 200 W/(m·K) and specific heat capacity of 900 J/(kg·K).
Initial plate temperature is 350 K.
Boundary conditions for temperature:
Left wall: fixedValue of 400 K
Right wall: fixedValue of 300 K
Top wall: zeroGradient
Bottom wall: zeroGradient
Front and back patches: type wall with zeroGradient temperature condition
Goal is to find the steady-state temperature distribution.
