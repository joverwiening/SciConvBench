I recommend setting the initial water column to occupy the bottom 3/4 of the vertical domain height, which provides a substantial initial water volume and allows for dramatic flow dynamics during the dam break simulation.


Set up a 2D dam break simulation using OpenFOAM's interFoam solver.
Domain dimensions are 2m long and 1m high, with a single cell in z-direction.
Gravity is defined in 'constant/g' as (0 -9.81 0).
'blockMeshDict' front and back patches are 'frontAndBack' with 'type wall'.
In '0' directory, 'frontAndBack' patches have 'noSlip' for velocity and 'zeroGradient' for scalars.
Initial water column occupies the left third of the domain horizontally (0 to 0.67m).
Initial water column height is 3/4 of the vertical domain (0 to 0.75m).
Use setFieldsDict to define the initial water volume.
