For a typical 2D laminar flow simulation of an airfoil, I'll make standard engineering assumptions:

1. Airfoil geometry: NACA 0012 (symmetric, commonly used for fundamental aerodynamics studies)
2. Chord length: 1 meter (standard reference length)
3. Reynolds number: 100,000 (typical for low-speed laminar flow regime)
4. Inlet velocity: 10 m/s (consistent with Reynolds number and chord length)
