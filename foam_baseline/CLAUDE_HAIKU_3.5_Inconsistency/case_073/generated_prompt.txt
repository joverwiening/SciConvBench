Simulate a single spherical particle settling in a quiescent fluid column using DPMFoam solver.
Domain is a vertical cylinder with height 1m along Y-axis and diameter 0.2m.
Fluid is water.
Single particle specifications:
- Diameter: 1mm
- Density: 2500 kg/m^3
- Initial position: (0, 0.9, 0)
Gravity specified as `g (0 9.81 0)` in constant/g dictionary.
Fluid velocity initialized to zero everywhere.
Cylinder walls are `noSlip`.
Top and bottom patches are simple walls.
Simulation time duration: 20-30 seconds.
Time step resolution: 0.1-0.15 seconds (approximately 200 data points).
Goal is to track the particle's complete settling trajectory.
