Simulate turbulent airflow over a backward-facing step using OpenFOAM's simpleFoam solver.
Use kOmegaSST RANS turbulence model.
Set inlet freestream velocity to 20 m/s.
Assume turbulence intensity of 5% at inlet.
Use turbulent mixing length of 0.005 m.
Use standard wall functions on walls.
Use zeroGradient boundary condition for turbulence variables at outlet.
Set Reynolds number to 20,000.
Use step height of 20 mm as characteristic length.
Geometry will have a 20 mm step height.
Channel length will be approximately 10-15 times the step height (200-300 mm).
Inlet boundary condition for k will be fixedValue with uniform 0.
Inlet boundary condition for omega will be fixedValue with uniform 0.
