Model a steady-state, buoyancy-driven flow in a vertical heated pipe using buoyantSimpleFoam solver.
Pipe geometry is 2m long, aligned with Z-axis, with 0.1m diameter.
Air enters at bottom (z=0) at 300K.
Pipe wall is maintained at constant temperature of 350K.
Gravity is set to zero in constant/g dictionary.
Thermophysical properties use perfectGas equation of state for air.
Inlet boundary condition for U is pressureInletOutletVelocity.
Inlet boundary condition for T is fixedValue at 300K.
Outlet has zeroGradient for U and T, with fixed pressure.
Wall has noSlip condition for U and fixedValue at 350K for T.
Turbulence model is standard k-epsilon.
Mesh resolution is moderate, with 50,000-100,000 cells.
Mesh will have finer refinement near wall boundaries.
Numerical discretization uses second-order upwind schemes for momentum and temperature convection terms.
