Model the injection of a high-pressure, compressible gas into a water tank using OpenFOAM's interFoam solver.
Use air as the gas phase with a perfect gas equation of state.
Use water as the incompressible liquid phase.
Set the initial water temperature to 20°C (293.15 K).
Set the initial tank pressure to 101,325 Pa (1 atm).
Define the computational domain as 0.5m x 0.5m x 1m (width x depth x height).
Set the inlet gas pressure to 5 MPa (5,000,000 Pa).
The gas enters at Mach 0.8.
Conduct a transient simulation to observe gas jet penetration and expansion in water.
Use compressibility effects modeling for the gas phase.
