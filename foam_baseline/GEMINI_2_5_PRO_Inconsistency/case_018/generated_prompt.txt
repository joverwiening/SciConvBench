Set up a laminar counter-flow diffusion flame simulation using the reactingFoam solver.
The simulation is a 2-D opposed-jet configuration.
The domain extends from 0 to 0.02 m in the x-direction.
The domain extends from -0.01 to 0.01 m in the y-direction.
The domain extends from -0.01 to 0.01 m in the z-direction.
The mesh resolution is 100 cells in x, 40 cells in y, and 1 cell in z.
The frontAndBack boundary patches are of type 'empty'.
The left boundary at x=0 is the fuel inlet.
The right boundary at x=0.02 is the air inlet.
The top and bottom boundaries are outlets.
The fuel inlet supplies pure methane (CH4) at a temperature of 293 K.
The fuel inlet has a uniform velocity of (0.1, 0, 0) m/s.
The air inlet supplies a mixture of oxygen and nitrogen at a temperature of 293 K.
The mass fraction of oxygen at the air inlet is 0.23, with the balance being nitrogen.
The air inlet has a uniform velocity of (-0.1, 0, 0) m/s.
The initial temperature for the entire domain is 2000 K.
The operating pressure is standard atmospheric pressure (101325 Pa).
The simulation uses the GRI-Mech 3.0 chemical reaction mechanism to model methane-air combustion accurately.
The chemistry is solved using the EulerImplicit scheme.
The ODE solver for the chemistry is Rosenbrock43.
The simulation models laminar combustion and momentum transport.
The thermodynamic model is hePsiThermo.
Mixture properties are calculated using coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture.
The transport model is Sutherland.
Species thermodynamics are based on JANAF polynomials.
The time step (deltaT) is 1e-4 seconds.
The simulation runs until an end time of 0.5 seconds.
Results are written every 0.05 seconds.
