Set up a steady-state simulation of airflow in a ventilated room with a heater using the `buoyantSimpleFoam` solver.
The goal is to model the natural convection driven by the heater.
The room dimensions are 5 meters in length, 4 meters in width, and 3 meters in height.
The inlet is a patch located on a wall near the floor.
The outlet is a patch located on the opposite wall near the ceiling.
The heater is a 1m x 1m patch located on the floor.
The gravitational acceleration is 9.81 m/s^2, acting downwards along the height axis.
In `constant/thermophysicalProperties`, the thermodynamics model is `thermoType heRhoThermo;`.
The equation of state is `equationOfState Boussinesq;`.
The Boussinesq parameters are `T0 293;`, `beta 0.00341;`, and `rho0 1.2;`.
The transport model is `const` with `mu 1.8e-5;` and `Pr 0.71;`.
The inlet air has a fixed temperature of 293 K.
The inlet air has a fixed velocity of 0.1 m/s normal to the inlet patch.
The heater patch has a fixed temperature of 320 K.
The heater patch is a no-slip wall.
The outlet is a pressure outlet set to 0 Pa.
All other walls are treated as no-slip and adiabatic (`zeroGradient` for temperature).
