Perform a dense particle fluidization simulation using the denseParticleFoam solver.
The simulation uses Lagrangian particle tracking.
The domain is a rectangular column.
The column width in the x-direction is 15 mm, from -7.5 to 7.5.
The column length in the y-direction is 150 mm, from -75 to 75.
The column height in the z-direction is 450 mm, from 0 to 450.
The convertToMeters factor is 0.001.
Use a structured mesh of 2 cells in x, 30 cells in y, and 90 cells in z.
The provided blockMeshDict will be used for the mesh generation.
The patch type for 'frontAndBack' in the blockMeshDict will be set to 'symmetry'.
The 'bottom' patch has an interstitialInletVelocity of (0 0 1.875) m/s.
The 'bottom' patch has a fixedFluxPressure boundary condition for pressure.
The 'top' patch has a pressureInletOutletVelocity boundary condition for velocity.
The 'top' patch has a fixedValue pressure of 0 Pa.
The 'walls' patches have a noSlip boundary condition for velocity.
The 'walls' patches have a fixedFluxPressure boundary condition for pressure.
The 'frontAndBack' patches are symmetry planes.
The fluid phase is air under laminar flow conditions.
The air density is 1.2 kg/m³.
The air kinematic viscosity is 1e-05 m²/s.
The particles have a density of 2526 kg/m³.
The particle diameter is 0.0025 m.
Particles are injected manually from a cloudPositions file.
The particles are initialized in a packed bed up to a height of 100 mm from the bottom of the domain.
The initial number of particles will be calculated to achieve an initial packed bed voidage of 0.4.
Particle-wall interactions are modeled using the rebound model.
The particle-wall coefficient of restitution is 0.97.
The particle-wall friction coefficient is 0.09.
The simulation uses the PIMPLE algorithm.
The PIMPLE algorithm uses 1 outer corrector.
The PIMPLE algorithm uses 2 pressure correctors.
The time integration scheme is Euler.
The simulation runs from t=0 to t=0.02 seconds.
The timestep is 2e-5 seconds.
Simulation results are written every 0.01 seconds.
Gravity is included with an acceleration of (0 0 -9.81) m/s².
The ErgunWenYuDrag model is used for particle-fluid interaction.
The pairCollision model is used for particle-particle collisions.
The sub-model for pairCollision is the linearSpring model.
The divergence scheme for momentum, div(phi,U), is Gauss upwind.
The boundary condition for the particle volume fraction, alpha.particles, at the 'bottom' patch is zeroGradient.
