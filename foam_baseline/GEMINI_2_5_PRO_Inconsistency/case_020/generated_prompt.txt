Set up a laminar counter-flow diffusion flame simulation using the reactingFoam solver.
The simulation is a 2-D opposed-jet configuration.
The domain spans from x = 0 to 0.02 m, y = -0.01 to 0.01 m, and z = -0.01 to 0.01 m.
The mesh has 100 grid points in the x-direction, 40 in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The left boundary at x = 0 is named 'fuel'.
The right boundary at x = 0.02 is named 'air'.
The top and bottom boundaries are named 'outlet'.
The front and back boundaries are of type 'empty'.
The fuel inlet ('fuel' boundary) injects pure methane (CH4) at 293 K.
The velocity at the 'fuel' boundary is 0.1 m/s in the x-direction.
The air inlet ('air' boundary) supplies an oxidizer mixture of 23% O2 and 77% N2 by mass.
The temperature at the 'air' boundary is 293 K.
The velocity at the 'air' boundary is -0.1 m/s in the x-direction.
The operating pressure is 101325 Pa.
The domain is initialized with a uniform temperature of 2000 K at time 0.
The initial species mass fractions are set by linearly interpolating between the fuel and air inlet compositions across the x-direction.
The boundary condition for velocity at the 'outlet' boundaries is zeroGradient.
The boundary condition for pressure at the 'outlet' boundaries is waveTransmissive.
The boundary condition for temperature at the 'outlet' boundaries is zeroGradient.
The boundary condition for species mass fractions at the 'outlet' boundaries is zeroGradient.
The combustion model is 'laminar'.
The momentum transport model is 'laminar'.
The thermophysical model is 'hePsiThermo'.
The mixture model is 'coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture'.
The transport model is 'Sutherland', which will be used to calculate viscosity.
The thermodynamics model is 'JANAF'.
A suitable chemical reaction mechanism for methane-air combustion, such as a reduced version of GRI-Mech, should be used.
The chemistry solver is 'EulerImplicit' with the 'Rosenbrock43' ODE solver.
The simulation runs from time 0 to 0.5 s.
The time step is 1e-4 s.
Results are written every 0.05 s.
