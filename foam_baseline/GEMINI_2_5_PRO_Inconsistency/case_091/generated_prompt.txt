Simulate natural convection in a 2D square cavity.
The solver to be used is buoyantBoussinesqPimpleFoam.
The cavity is filled with air.
The side length of the square cavity is 1 meter.
The mesh resolution is 80x80 cells.
Gravity acts downwards in the -y direction with g = 9.81 m/s^2.
The Boussinesq approximation is used for buoyancy.
The Prandtl number is 0.71.
The Rayleigh number is 10^4.
The left wall is hot with a fixed temperature of 300 K.
The right wall is cold with a fixed temperature of 290 K.
The top and bottom walls are adiabatic.
The velocity boundary condition for all walls is noSlip.
The simulation should be run until a steady-state solution is reached.
