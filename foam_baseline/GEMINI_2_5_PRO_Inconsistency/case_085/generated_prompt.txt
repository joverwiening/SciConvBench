Set up a 2D steady-state simulation of Rayleigh-Bénard convection.
The solver to be used is `buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam`.
The geometry is a square cavity with dimensions 1m x 1m.
The fluid is air, treated as incompressible using the Boussinesq approximation.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction with a magnitude of 9.81 m/s^2.
The thermophysical properties are defined in `constant/thermophysicalProperties`.
The `thermoType` dictionary specifies `hePsiThermo`, `Boussinesq` mixture, `const` transport, `hConst` thermo, `Boussinesq` equation of state, `specie`, and `sensibleEnthalpy` energy.
The `BoussinesqCoeffs` dictionary sets `TRef` to 300 K, `beta` to 0.00333 1/K, and `rhoRef` to 1.176 kg/m^3.
The `mixture` properties under `transport` are `mu` 1.8e-05 Pa.s and `Pr` 0.7.
The `mixture` properties under `thermodynamics` are `Cp` 1005 J/(kg.K) and `Hf` 0.
The bottom wall is heated to a fixed temperature of 310 K.
The top wall is cooled to a fixed temperature of 290 K.
The side walls are adiabatic, implemented with a `zeroGradient` boundary condition for temperature.
All walls have a `noSlip` boundary condition for velocity.
All boundaries use a `fixedFluxPressure` condition for the relative pressure `p_rgh`.
The mesh for the 1m x 1m cavity will be 100x100 cells, with grading towards the walls.
The simulation will use the k-omega SST turbulence model.
In `fvSchemes`, the divergence operator will use the `Gauss linearUpwind` scheme.
The simulation results will be written out only at the end of the run.
