Configure a simulation for the separation of three immiscible fluids in a horizontal gravity separator.
The three fluids are oil, water, and gas.
The simulation will use the Volume of Fluid (VOF) method.
The solver for the simulation is `multiphaseInterFoam`.
The phases are defined in the `transportProperties` file.
Assume a reasonable density for the oil phase based on best practices.
Assume a reasonable density for the water phase based on best practices.
Assume a reasonable density for the gas phase based on best practices.
Assume a reasonable viscosity for the oil phase based on best practices.
Assume a reasonable viscosity for the water phase based on best practices.
Assume a reasonable viscosity for the gas phase based on best practices.
The initial state of the fluids is a stratified mixture.
The initial state is configured using the `setFieldsDict` utility.
The `alpha.water` field represents the volume fraction of water.
The simulation will track the interface between the three fluids over time as they separate.
The gravity vector is set to (0 -9.81 0).
Assume a reasonable length for the horizontal gravity separator based on best practices.
Assume a reasonable height for the horizontal gravity separator based on best practices.
The simulation should be two-dimensional.
