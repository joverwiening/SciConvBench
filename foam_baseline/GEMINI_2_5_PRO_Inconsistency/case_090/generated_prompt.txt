Set up a transient simulation of 2D vortex shedding behind a cylinder.
The Reynolds number is 100.
Use the pimpleFoam solver for an incompressible fluid.
The fluid kinematic viscosity is 0.01 m^2/s.
The cylinder diameter is 1 m.
The freestream inlet velocity is U = (1 0 0) m/s.
The computational domain is a rectangular channel.
The domain extends from -10 m to 30 m in the x-direction.
The domain extends from -10 m to 10 m in the y-direction.
The center of the circular cylinder is located at (0, 0, 0).
The mesh has approximately 100 cells on the circumference of the cylinder.
The mesh is refined in the wake region behind the cylinder to capture the vortex street.
The 'inlet' boundary has a fixed velocity of (1 0 0) m/s and a zero-gradient condition for pressure.
The 'outlet' boundary has a zero-gradient condition for velocity and a fixed pressure of 0.
The 'topAndBottom' boundaries have a slip condition.
The 'cylinder' boundary has a no-slip condition.
The 'frontAndBack' boundaries are of type 'empty' for a 2D simulation.
The simulation runs from t=0 s to t=200 s.
The time step deltaT is 0.1 s.
The primary goal is to observe the von Kármán vortex street.
The secondary goal is to calculate the Strouhal number.
The lift coefficient on the cylinder will be monitored over time to determine the vortex shedding frequency for the Strouhal number calculation.
