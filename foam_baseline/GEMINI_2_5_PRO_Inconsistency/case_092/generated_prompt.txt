Simulate the compressible, supersonic flow of air over a 10-degree wedge.
The freestream Mach number is 2.0.
Air is treated as a perfect gas.
The solver to be used is rhoPimpleFoam.
The computational domain is a 2D rectangle with dimensions 3 meters in length and 2 meters in height.
The wedge has a length of 1 meter.
The wedge tip is located at x=1m, y=0m.
The mesh is 2D, extruded one cell in the z-direction.
The mesh resolution is 300 cells in the x-direction and 200 cells in the y-direction.
The freestream conditions are U = (686 0 0) m/s, T = 293 K, and p = 101325 Pa.
The thermophysical model is hePsiThermo with equationOfState perfectGas and transport sutherland.
The inlet boundary condition uses fixed values for U, p, and T.
The outlet boundary condition is waveTransmissive for p and T, and zeroGradient for U.
The wedge surface is a slip wall.
The top boundary is a slip wall.
The front and back boundaries are of type empty.
The simulation end time is 0.05 seconds.
The solution is considered converged when the final residuals for all variables are below 1e-5.
