The molecular weight is 29.
The specific heat at constant volume is 714.3 J/(kg*K).
The heat of formation is 0.
The bottom wall temperature is fixed at 305 K.
The top wall temperature is fixed at 295 K.
The vertical side walls are adiabatic.
A no-slip velocity condition is applied on all solid walls.
The flow is initialized at rest with a uniform velocity field of (0 0 0).
The temperature field is initialized to a uniform value of 300 K.
The simulation runs up to 1000 seconds of physical time.
Use a fixed timestep of 1 second.
The time step adjustment feature is disabled.
Results are written every 50 time steps.
The k-epsilon RAS turbulence model is used.
The simulationType is set to RAS with the kEpsilon model, turbulence is on, and printCoeffs is on.
