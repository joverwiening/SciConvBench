Configure a 2D bubble column simulation using the twoPhaseEulerFoam solver.
The column dimensions are 0.5m in width (X-direction) and 2m in height (Y-direction).
The simulation is two-dimensional, with front and back patches set to empty.
The continuous phase is water and the dispersed phase is air.
The column is initially filled with water.
The gravity vector is set to (0 -9.81 0).
Air is injected from a central sparger at the bottom boundary.
The air inlet patch width is 0.05m.
At the inlet, the air volume fraction (alpha.air) is a fixed value of 1.
At the inlet, the air velocity (U.air) is a fixed value of (0 0.1 0) m/s.
The top boundary is an outlet.
The side walls use a noSlip boundary condition.
The air bubble diameter is 3mm.
The k-epsilon turbulence model is used for the continuous phase.
The Schiller-Naumann drag model is used for the phase interaction.
The density of water is 1000 kg/m^3.
The density of air is 1.2 kg/m^3.
The mesh consists of 50 cells in the X-direction and 200 cells in the Y-direction.
The simulation runs for 10 seconds of physical time.
