Set up a Rayleigh-Bénard convection case using the buoyantBoussinesqPimpleFoam solver.
The domain is a 3D box with dimensions 1m in x, 1m in y, and 0.1m in z.
The height of the domain is oriented along the Z-axis.
The mesh consists of 100 cells in the x-direction, 100 cells in the y-direction, and 20 cells in the z-direction.
The fluid has a Prandtl number of 0.71.
Use the Boussinesq approximation with beta=0.00341, T0=300, and nu=1.5e-5.
The gravity vector is (0 0 -9.81).
The bottom wall at z=0 has a fixed temperature of 310K.
The top wall at z=0.1 has a fixed temperature of 300K.
The velocity boundary condition is noSlip on the top and bottom walls.
The side walls use periodic boundary conditions.
The simulation is transient and runs until an end time of 2000 seconds.
The goal is to capture the formation of convection cells.
