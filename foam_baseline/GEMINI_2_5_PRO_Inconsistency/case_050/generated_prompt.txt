Simulate a high-speed, compressible supersonic jet.
The inlet Mach number is 2.0.
The jet discharges into a larger, quiescent domain.
The fluid is air, treated as a perfect gas.
Use the `hePsiThermo` thermophysical model with a `perfectGas` equation of state and `sutherland` transport model.
The simulation is 2D, using a mesh extruded one cell in the z-direction with `frontAndBack` patches set to `empty`.
The nozzle inlet height is 1 meter.
The nozzle section length is 2 meters.
The larger domain extends 20 meters downstream from the nozzle exit and 10 meters in the transverse direction from the centerline.
The initial velocity in the domain is (0 0 0) m/s.
The initial pressure in the domain is 101325 Pa.
The initial temperature in the domain is 300 K.
The nozzle inlet boundary condition uses a fixed velocity of (686 0 0) m/s, a fixed temperature of 300 K, and a fixed pressure of 101325 Pa.
The far-field and outlet boundaries use a `waveTransmissive` condition for pressure and temperature, and a `zeroGradient` condition for velocity.
The solid walls use a `noSlip` boundary condition.
A k-omega SST turbulence model will be used.
The solver application is `pimpleFoam`.
A TVD advection scheme, such as `vanLeer`, will be used for the divergence terms in `fvSchemes`.
The simulation will run until a reasonable end time is reached for the jet to establish a quasi-steady state.
