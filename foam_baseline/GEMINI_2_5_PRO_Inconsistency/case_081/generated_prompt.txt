Simulate a 2D gas-liquid bubble column using the twoPhaseEulerFoam solver.
The domain is a rectangle of 0.5 m width and 2 m height.
The two phases are air and water.
The simulation is transient.
The mesh will have 50 cells along the width and 200 cells along the height.
The total simulation time is 20 seconds.
The mean diameter of the air bubbles is 5 mm.
The k-epsilon turbulence model will be used for both the water and air phases.
The pressure at the outlet boundary is 101325 Pa.
The bottom boundary is an inlet.
At the inlet, the air phase fraction is a fixed uniform value of 0.8.
At the inlet, the water phase fraction is a fixed uniform value of 0.2.
At the inlet, the air velocity is (0 0.2 0) m/s.
At the inlet, the water velocity is (0 0.01 0) m/s.
The top boundary is an outlet.
The side boundaries are no-slip walls.
