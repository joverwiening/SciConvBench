Set up a 2D, incompressible, steady-state simulation of airflow over a NACA 0012 symmetric airfoil.
The airfoil has a chord length of 1 meter.
The simulation will use the simpleFoam solver.
The fluid is air with a kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The k-omega SST turbulence model will be used.
The domain is a C-grid topology around the airfoil.
The radius of the outer boundary is 15 chord lengths.
The mesh has 200 cells along the airfoil surface.
The mesh has 50 cells in the direction normal to the airfoil surface.
The mesh is one cell thick in the z-direction.
In the blockMeshDict, the front and back patches are defined with the type 'empty'.
The inlet velocity is a uniform value of (10 0 0) m/s.
The outlet pressure is a fixed uniform value of 0.
The airfoil surface has a noSlip condition for velocity and a zeroGradient condition for pressure.
The inlet has a zeroGradient condition for pressure.
The outlet has a zeroGradient condition for velocity.
The front and back patches have an 'empty' boundary condition for both velocity and pressure.
