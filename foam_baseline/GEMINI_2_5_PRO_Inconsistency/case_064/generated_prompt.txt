Model a 2D fluid-structure interaction problem of a flexible flap in a channel.
The geometry is 2D, extruded one cell in the z-direction with `frontAndBack` empty boundary conditions.
The channel has a length of 1.0 m and a height of 0.2 m.
A flexible flap with a length of 0.05 m and a thickness of 0.005 m is attached to the bottom wall.
The flap is located at x = 0.4 m from the inlet.
The fluid is incompressible water with a kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m^2/s.
The fluid inlet has a fixed velocity of (1 0 0) m/s.
The fluid outlet has a fixed pressure of 0 Pa.
The channel walls have a no-slip boundary condition.
The solid flap is a linear elastic material.
The flap has a Young's modulus of 1e6 Pa, a Poisson's ratio of 0.45, and a density of 1200 kg/m^3.
The base of the flap has a fixed displacement boundary condition.
The surface of the flap is a coupled interface for fluid-structure interaction.
The fluid domain is solved using `pimpleFoam`.
The solid domain is solved using `solidDisplacementFoam`.
The coupling between the fluid and solid solvers is a two-way FSI coupling implemented using the `solids4foam` library.
The total simulation time is 2.0 seconds.
The mesh has a target cell size of 0.001 m in the region around the flap.
The k-omega SST turbulence model is used for the fluid simulation.
The maximum Courant number is maintained at or below 0.5.
Gravity is not included in the simulation.
