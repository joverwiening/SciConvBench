Perform a 2D simulation of heat conduction through a square solid plate using the laplacianFoam solver.
The goal is to find the steady-state temperature distribution.
The domain is a 1m x 1m square, with a thickness of 0.1m.
The mesh will have 20 cells in the x-direction.
The mesh will have 20 cells in the y-direction.
The mesh will have one cell in the z-direction.
The mesh will have five patches named left, right, top, bottom, and frontAndBack.
The frontAndBack patches will be of type wall.
The left wall has a fixed temperature of 400 K.
The right wall has a fixed temperature of 300 K.
The top and bottom walls have a zero-gradient temperature boundary condition.
The frontAndBack walls have a zero-gradient temperature boundary condition.
The initial temperature of the internal field is 300 K.
The thermal diffusivity of the plate material is 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The simulation will run until an end time of 100000 seconds.
A time step of 10 seconds will be used.
Standard second-order numerical schemes will be used.
Standard linear solvers with default tolerances will be used.
