Simulate the transient von Kármán vortex street behind a cylinder in a 2D channel.
Use the pimpleFoam solver for the simulation.
The simulation is to be run as laminar.
The mesh is a 2D mesh extruded one cell in the z-direction.
The front and back patches are of type empty.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 1e-3 m^2/s.
The cylinder diameter is 1 m.
The channel height is 8 m.
The distance from the inlet to the center of the cylinder is 10 m.
The distance from the center of the cylinder to the outlet is 25 m.
The velocity inlet boundary condition is a fixed value of uniform (0.1 0 0).
The velocity outlet boundary condition is zeroGradient.
The velocity cylinder boundary condition is noSlip.
The velocity top and bottom boundary conditions are slip.
The pressure inlet boundary condition is zeroGradient.
The pressure outlet boundary condition is a fixed value of uniform 0.
The pressure boundary condition for the cylinder, top, and bottom walls is zeroGradient.
The simulation end time is 600 seconds.
The time step should be adjusted automatically to satisfy a maximum Courant number of 0.5.
The mesh should have approximately 60 cells along the circumference of the cylinder.
