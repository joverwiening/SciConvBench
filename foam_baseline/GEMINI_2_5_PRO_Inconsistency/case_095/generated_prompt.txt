Set up a 2D, steady-state, incompressible, turbulent simulation of airflow over a NACA0012 airfoil.
The airfoil is at a 5-degree angle of attack.
Use the simpleFoam solver.
Use the k-omega SST turbulence model.
The domain is a C-grid type and is one cell thick in the z-direction.
The mesh is pre-defined in the `constant/polyMesh` directory.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The boundary patch `inlet` has a `fixedValue` velocity of uniform (10 0 0).
The boundary patch `inlet` has a `zeroGradient` condition for pressure `p`.
For the `inlet` patch, `k` and `omega` are calculated from a turbulence intensity of 1% and a viscosity ratio of 10.
The boundary patch `outlet` has an `inletOutlet` condition for velocity `U`, `k`, and `omega`.
The boundary patch `outlet` has a `fixedValue` pressure of uniform 0.
The boundary patch `airfoil` has a `noSlip` condition for velocity `U`.
The boundary patch `airfoil` has a `zeroGradient` condition for pressure `p`.
The boundary patch `airfoil` uses the `kqRWallFunction` for `k`.
The boundary patch `airfoil` uses the `omegaWallFunction` for `omega`.
The `frontAndBack` patches are of type `empty`.
All fields on the `frontAndBack` patches are set to `empty`.
The numerical scheme for `div(phi,U)` is `Gauss linearUpwindV grad(U)`.
The numerical scheme for `div(phi,k)` is `Gauss linearUpwind grad(k)`.
The numerical scheme for `div(phi,omega)` is `Gauss linearUpwind grad(omega)`.
