Simulate incompressible water flow in a channel with a prescribed moving wall.
Use the pimpleFoam solver for the simulation.
The fluid is water with a kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m^2/s.
The mesh will be a 2D mesh extruded one cell in the spanwise direction.
The frontAndBack boundary patches will be of type empty.
Use a dynamic mesh with the dynamicMotionSolverFvMesh solver.
The mesh motion solver will be displacementLaplacian.
Prescribe motion on the movingWall boundary using the 0/pointDisplacement file.
The displacement type for the moving wall will be oscillatingDisplacement.
All other boundaries will have a fixed displacement of uniform (0 0 0).
The inlet velocity boundary condition will be fixedValue.
The outlet velocity boundary condition will be zeroGradient.
The stationary walls velocity boundary condition will be noSlip.
The movingWall velocity boundary condition will be movingWallVelocity.
The outlet pressure boundary condition will be fixedValue uniform 0.
The inlet and wall pressure boundary conditions will be zeroGradient.
Assume a reasonable channel length based on best practices.
Assume a reasonable channel height based on best practices.
Assume a reasonable wave amplitude for the moving wall based on best practices.
Assume a reasonable wavelength for the moving wall based on best practices.
Assume a reasonable wave frequency for the moving wall based on best practices.
Assume a reasonable value for the inlet velocity based on best practices.
The simulation will run for a number of wave cycles determined by best practices.
The number of cells along the channel length will be based on best practices.
The number of cells along the channel height will be based on best practices.
