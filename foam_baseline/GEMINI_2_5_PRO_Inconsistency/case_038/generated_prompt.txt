Simulate the thermo-elastic deformation of a solid aluminum block.
The goal is to compute the final steady-state displacement and stress due to thermal expansion.
The solver to be used is solidDisplacementFoam.
The block dimensions are 0.1 meters in x, 0.04 meters in y, and 0.01 meters in z.
A structured mesh with 20 cells in x, 8 cells in y, and 2 cells in z will be used.
The material is aluminum.
The Young's modulus is 69e9 Pa.
The Poisson's ratio is 0.33.
The density is 2700 kg/m^3.
The thermal expansion coefficient is 2.3e-5 1/K.
The reference temperature is 300 K.
A steady-state temperature field will be used to drive the thermal strain.
The face at x=0 is the fixedFace.
The displacement boundary condition on the fixedFace is a fixed value of (0 0 0).
The face at x=0.1 is the heatedFace.
The temperature boundary condition on the heatedFace is a fixed value of 400 K.
The temperature boundary condition on the fixedFace is zeroGradient.
All other faces have a zeroGradient boundary condition for both displacement and temperature.
The simulation will run until a steady-state solution is reached.
The simulation end time is 1000.
The simulation time step deltaT is 1.
