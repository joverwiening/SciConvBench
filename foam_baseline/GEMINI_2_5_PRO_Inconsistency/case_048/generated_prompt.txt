Simulate buoyancy-driven heat transfer in a 2D channel using the `buoyantPimpleFoam` solver.
The channel is a closed cavity with a length of 0.4 meters and a height of 0.1 meters.
The mesh resolution will be 80 cells in the x-direction and 40 cells in the y-direction.
The fluid is air, and the flow is treated as laminar.
The Boussinesq approximation should be used for modeling the fluid's density.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction.
The initial temperature of the air is 300 K.
The bottom wall is heated to a fixed value of 400 K.
The top wall is adiabatic, using a `zeroGradient` boundary condition for temperature.
The vertical side walls are also adiabatic, using a `zeroGradient` boundary condition.
The simulation should run until an end time of 100 seconds.
