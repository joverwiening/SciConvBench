Simulate the quasi-static deflection of a 3D steel cantilever beam.
The simulation will use the `solidDisplacementFoam` solver.
The physics model is linear elasticity.
The beam geometry is 1 meter long, 0.1 meters high, and 0.1 meters wide.
A structured mesh of 20 cells in length, 4 cells in height, and 4 cells in width will be used.
One end of the beam is clamped, defined as the `fixed` boundary with a fixed displacement of (0 0 0).
A traction load is applied to the free end of the beam, defined as the `loaded` boundary.
The traction is uniform with a value of (0 -1000 0) Pa.
The pressure on the `loaded` boundary is a uniform value of 0 Pa.
All other faces of the beam have a `zeroGradient` boundary condition for displacement.
The material is steel with a Young's modulus of 200e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.3.
The density of the steel is 7850 kg/m^3.
The effect of gravity (9.81 m/s^2 in the negative y-direction) will be included as a body force.
The simulation will run for a total time of 1 second.
The traction load will be ramped linearly from zero at time 0 to its full value at time 1.
