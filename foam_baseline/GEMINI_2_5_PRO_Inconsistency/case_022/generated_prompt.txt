Simulate a 2D lid-driven cavity flow using the icoFoam solver.
The flow is transient, incompressible, and laminar.
The domain is a unit square, one meter by one meter.
The domain is one cell thick in the z-direction.
The frontAndBack patches in the blockMeshDict are of type empty.
The mesh consists of 40 cells in the x-direction.
The mesh consists of 40 cells in the y-direction.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 0.001 m^2/s, corresponding to a Reynolds number of 1000.
The simulation end time is 10 seconds.
The time step is 0.005 seconds.
Simulation results are written to file every 0.5 seconds.
The top wall, named 'movingWall', moves at a fixed value of (1 0 0) m/s.
The other three walls, named 'fixedWalls', have a noSlip boundary condition for velocity.
The frontAndBack patches have an 'empty' boundary condition for velocity.
All physical walls ('movingWall' and 'fixedWalls') have a 'zeroGradient' boundary condition for pressure.
The frontAndBack patches have an 'empty' boundary condition for pressure.
