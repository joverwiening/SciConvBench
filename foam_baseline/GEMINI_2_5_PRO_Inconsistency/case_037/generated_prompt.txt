Simulate steady, incompressible, laminar flow of water through a 2D channel containing a rigid, stationary obstacle.
Use the simpleFoam solver.
The fluid is water with a kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m^2/s.
The geometry, including channel length, channel height, and obstacle shape, will be determined based on standard best practices for such simulations.
The mesh will be 2D, extruded one cell in the spanwise direction, with the frontAndBack planes set to the empty boundary condition.
The simulation will be configured to achieve a Reynolds number appropriate for laminar flow around the chosen obstacle.
The priority is to maintain a laminar flow regime, even if it requires adjusting the inlet velocity from the initial value of 0.1 m/s.
The velocity boundary conditions are a fixed value at the inlet, zero gradient at the outlet, and no-slip on the walls and obstacle.
The pressure boundary conditions are zero gradient at the inlet and on the walls and obstacle, with a fixed value of 0 at the outlet.
