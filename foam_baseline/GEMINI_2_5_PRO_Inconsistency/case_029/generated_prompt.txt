Simulate the transient von Kármán vortex street for 2D flow past a circular cylinder.
The Reynolds number for the simulation is 150.
Use the transient solver `pimpleFoam`.
The simulation starts at time 0 and stops at time 200.
The initial time step is 0.005.
The time step will be adjusted to keep the maximum Courant number at or below 0.9.
Write the results every 1 second of simulation time.
The cylinder diameter is 1 meter.
The freestream velocity is 1.5 m/s.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 0.01 m^2/s.
The computational domain is a rectangle with the cylinder centered at (0,0).
The inlet is 10 meters upstream of the cylinder center.
The outlet is 20 meters downstream of the cylinder center.
The top and bottom walls are 10 meters away from the centerline.
Apply a slip boundary condition to the top and bottom walls.
Apply a uniform fixed value of (1.5 0 0) m/s for velocity at the inlet.
Apply a zero-gradient boundary condition for pressure at the inlet.
Apply a fixed value of 0 for pressure at the outlet.
Apply a zero-gradient boundary condition for velocity at the outlet.
Apply a no-slip boundary condition to the cylinder surface.
