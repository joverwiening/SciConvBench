Simulate a 2D dam break case using the interFoam solver.
The domain is a rectangle with dimensions of 0.6m in length (x-axis) and 0.4m in height (y-axis).
The mesh will be created using blockMesh with a single block.
A reasonable mesh resolution, such as 120 cells in the x-direction and 80 cells in the y-direction, will be used.
The initial condition is a column of water occupying the region where x < 0.15m and y < 0.3m.
The rest of the domain is initially filled with air.
The two phases are defined as water and air with standard physical properties.
The gravity vector is set to (0 -9.81 0).
The front and back patches are of type 'empty' for a 2D simulation.
The boundary condition for velocity (U) is 'noSlip' on the walls and 'pressureInletOutletVelocity' on the top 'atmosphere' patch.
The boundary condition for relative pressure (p_rgh) is 'fixedFluxPressure' on the walls and 'totalPressure' on the top 'atmosphere' patch.
The boundary condition for the water volume fraction (alpha.water) is 'zeroGradient' on the walls and 'inletOutlet' on the top 'atmosphere' patch.
A suitable turbulence model, such as k-epsilon, will be used.
The simulation will run for a total time of 2 seconds.
The time step will be controlled by a maximum Courant number of 0.5.
The simulation results will be written to output files every 0.05 seconds.
