Set up a transient simulation of turbulent flow over a 2D cylinder in a channel using the pimpleFoam solver.
The simulation type is specified as LES in `constant/turbulenceProperties`.
The LES model is Smagorinsky with a `cubeRootVol` delta.
A RAS sub-dictionary is included with the RASModel set to kOmegaSST.
The fluid is incompressible with standard air properties, using a kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The cylinder has a diameter of 1 meter.
The channel height is 20 meters.
The distance from the channel inlet to the center of the cylinder is 10 meters.
The distance from the center of the cylinder to the channel outlet is 25 meters.
The inlet velocity is a uniform 10 m/s.
The cylinder and channel walls have noSlip boundary conditions.
The pressure at the outlet has a fixed value of 0.
The mesh is refined to achieve a target y+ value of 1 on the cylinder and channel walls.
The simulation runs until an end time of 15 seconds.
The maximum Courant number is set to 0.5.
