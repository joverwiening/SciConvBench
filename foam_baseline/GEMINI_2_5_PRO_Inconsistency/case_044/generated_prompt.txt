Set up a 2D dam break simulation using the interFoam solver.
The domain is 1m long and 1m high.
The mesh has 100 cells in the x-direction and 100 cells in the y-direction.
A column of water is initialized in the region 0 < x < 0.2 and 0 < y < 0.5.
The rest of the domain is air.
The density of water is 1000 kg/m^3.
The density of air is 1 kg/m^3.
The kinematic viscosity of water is 1e-6 m^2/s.
The kinematic viscosity of air is 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The simulation models the collapse of the water column under gravity.
The acceleration due to gravity is -9.81 m/s^2 in the y-direction.
Use setFieldsDict to initialize the alpha.water field to 1 in the water column region and 0 elsewhere.
The top boundary is an open atmosphere with the pressureInletOutletVelocity condition.
All other boundaries are no-slip walls.
The total simulation time is 2 seconds.
