Set up a steady-state, incompressible, turbulent flow simulation for the 2D Pitz and Daily combustor case.
Use the simpleFoam solver.
The simulation will run for 500 iterations.
The turbulence model is Reynolds-Averaged Navier-Stokes (RANS) with the k-Epsilon model.
The `constant/turbulenceProperties` file will contain only the RAS section for the kEpsilon model, and the LES section will be removed.
The kinematic viscosity is 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The inlet velocity at the 'inlet' patch is a fixed value of (10 0 0) m/s.
The outlet condition for velocity at the 'outlet' patch is zero-gradient.
The 'upperWall' and 'lowerWall' patches are no-slip walls for velocity.
The pressure at the 'outlet' patch is a fixed value of 0.
The pressure condition for the 'inlet', 'upperWall', and 'lowerWall' patches is zero-gradient.
The inlet turbulence intensity is 5%.
The inlet channel height is 0.0381 meters.
The characteristic length scale for turbulence calculations at the inlet is 7% of the inlet channel height.
Based on these assumptions, the turbulent kinetic energy 'k' at the inlet is 0.375 m^2/s^2.
Based on these assumptions, the turbulence dissipation rate 'epsilon' at the inlet is 14.13 m^2/s^3.
Use kqRWallFunction for 'k' and epsilonWallFunction for 'epsilon' at the wall boundaries.
The 'front' and 'back' patches are of type 'empty' for all fields.
