Model the transient injection of a high-pressure, compressible gas into a tank of water.
The simulation will use the `interFoam` solver.
The gas phase is air.
The gas is modeled using the `perfectGas` equation of state.
The water phase is modeled as incompressible.
The gas enters the tank at Mach 0.8.
The total pressure of the gas at the inlet is 10 MPa.
The temperature of the gas at the inlet is 300 K.
The water tank is a cylinder with a diameter of 1 meter and a height of 2 meters.
The gas inlet nozzle is located at the center of the bottom of the tank.
The diameter of the gas inlet nozzle is 1 cm.
The initial pressure inside the tank is 101325 Pa.
The top surface of the tank is a pressure outlet boundary set to 101325 Pa.
All other tank surfaces are no-slip walls.
The k-omega SST turbulence model will be used.
The goal is to observe the gas jet penetration and expansion in the water.
