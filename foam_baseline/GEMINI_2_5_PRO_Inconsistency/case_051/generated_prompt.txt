Simulate the transient start-up flow of a Bingham plastic fluid in a 2D pipe.
The solver to be used is `nonNewtonianIcoFoam`.
The domain is a rectangle of 1.0m length and 0.1m diameter.
Use `blockMesh` to create the mesh.
The mesh will have 100 cells in the axial (x) direction.
The mesh will have 20 cells in the radial (y) direction.
The front and back patches are of type `empty`.
The inlet boundary condition for velocity is a fixed value of `(0.1 0 0)` m/s.
The outlet boundary condition for velocity is `zeroGradient`.
The wall boundary condition for velocity is `noSlip`.
The inlet boundary condition for pressure is `zeroGradient`.
The outlet boundary condition for pressure is a fixed value of 0.
The wall boundary condition for pressure is `zeroGradient`.
The simulation will run from t=0s to t=5s.
The initial time step (`deltaT`) is 0.001s.
The maximum Courant number (`maxCo`) is 0.5.
The fluid transport model is `BinghamPlastic`.
The fluid density (`rho`) is 1000.
The fluid yield stress (`tau0`) is 10.
The fluid zero-shear viscosity (`mu0`) is 0.1.
The fluid infinite-shear viscosity (`muInf`) is 0.01.
