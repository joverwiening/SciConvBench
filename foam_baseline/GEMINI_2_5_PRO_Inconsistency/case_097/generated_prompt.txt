Perform a 2D transient heat conduction simulation on a plate using the `laplacianFoam` solver.
The plate dimensions are 1 m by 1 m.
The mesh is extruded one cell in the z-direction with a thickness of 0.01 m.
The mesh resolution is 20 cells in the x-direction and 20 cells in the y-direction.
The thermal diffusivity is 1e-5 m^2/s.
The initial temperature is a uniform 300 K.
The total simulation time is 50000 seconds.
The time step size is 50 seconds.
The left side of the plate has a fixed temperature boundary condition of 400 K.
The right side of the plate has a zero-gradient temperature boundary condition.
The top side of the plate has a zero-gradient temperature boundary condition.
The bottom side of the plate has a zero-gradient temperature boundary condition.
The out-of-plane faces are defined as the `frontAndBack` patch.
In `constant/polyMesh/boundary`, the `frontAndBack` patch type is `wall`.
In `0/T`, the `frontAndBack` patch has a `zeroGradient` boundary condition.
