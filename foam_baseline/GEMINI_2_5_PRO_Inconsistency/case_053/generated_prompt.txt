Simulate the mixing of a non-Newtonian sludge and water in a 2D tank using the `interFoam` solver.
The two phases are named 'water' and 'sludge'.
The transport model for water is Newtonian.
The kinematic viscosity of water is 1e-6 m^2/s.
The density of water is 1000 kg/m^3.
The transport model for sludge is BinghamPlastic.
The density of the sludge is 1500 kg/m^3.
The yield stress of the sludge is 15 Pa.
The plastic dynamic viscosity of the sludge is 0.1 Pa.s.
The plastic kinematic viscosity for the sludge will be calculated by dividing its plastic dynamic viscosity by its density.
The surface tension between the two phases is 0.07 N/m.
The 2D tank has dimensions of 1 meter in width and 1 meter in height.
Initially, the bottom 0.25 meters of the tank is filled with sludge, and the remaining volume is filled with water.
Mixing is driven by a water jet from a velocity inlet on one of the tank walls.
Gravity is set to 9.81 m/s^2 acting in the negative y-direction.
The total simulation time is 10 seconds.
