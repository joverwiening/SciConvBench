Perform a 2D simulation of developing flow in a channel using the `simpleFoam` solver.
The simulation domain models the bottom half of the channel, using a symmetry plane at the centerline.
The channel has a full height of 0.02 m.
The channel has a length of 1.0 m.
The mesh has 400 cells in the direction of flow and 40 cells in the wall-normal direction.
The fluid's kinematic viscosity is 1e-5 m^2/s.
The target Reynolds number, based on full channel height and average velocity, is 100.
The `inlet` patch has a uniform velocity of 0.025 m/s.
The `inlet` pressure boundary condition is `zeroGradient`.
The `outlet` patch has a `zeroGradient` velocity boundary condition.
The `outlet` patch has a fixed pressure value of 0.
The `bottomWall` patch is a `noSlip` wall.
The `bottomWall` pressure boundary condition is `zeroGradient`.
The `centerline` patch type in `blockMeshDict` is `symmetry`.
The boundary condition for all fields on the `centerline` patch is `symmetry`.
The `frontAndBack` patches are of type `empty`.
The simulation runs for 1000 iterations.
