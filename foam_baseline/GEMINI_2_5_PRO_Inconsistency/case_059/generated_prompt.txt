Simulate a steady-state, two-phase oil-water flow in a horizontal pipe.
Use the `twoPhaseEulerFoam` solver.
The pipe diameter is 0.05 meters.
The pipe length is 2 meters.
The fixed mixture velocity at the inlet is 1 m/s.
The density of the oil is 800 kg/m^3.
The kinematic viscosity of the oil is 1e-5 m^2/s.
The density of the water is 1000 kg/m^3.
The kinematic viscosity of the water is 1e-6 m^2/s.
The inlet water volume fraction is a fixed uniform value of 0.3.
The pipe walls have a `noSlip` boundary condition.
The outlet boundary condition for `alpha.water` is `fixedValue` with a uniform value of 0.3.
The k-epsilon turbulence model should be used.
The outlet boundary condition for pressure should be a fixed value of 0.
The outlet boundary condition for velocity should be `zeroGradient`.
The simulation should be run until a steady-state solution is reached.
