Model a steady-state, buoyancy-driven flow in a vertical heated pipe using the buoyantSimpleFoam solver.
The pipe has a length of 2m and a diameter of 0.1m, aligned with the Z-axis.
The fluid is air, using the perfectGas equation of state.
The gravity vector is (0 0 -9.81).
The simulation will be run as a laminar flow.
The mesh will have 20 cells in the radial direction.
The mesh will have 200 cells in the axial direction.
The inlet is at the bottom (z=0) with a temperature of 300K.
The inlet boundary condition for velocity is pressureInletOutletVelocity.
The inlet boundary condition for temperature is a fixed value of 300K.
The outlet is at the top (z=2m) with a fixed pressure of 0 Pa.
The outlet boundary condition for velocity is zeroGradient.
The outlet boundary condition for temperature is zeroGradient.
The pipe wall is maintained at a constant temperature of 350K.
The wall boundary condition for velocity is noSlip.
The wall boundary condition for temperature is a fixed value of 350K.
