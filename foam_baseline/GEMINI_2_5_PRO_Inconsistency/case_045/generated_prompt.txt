Perform a 2D, steady-state, incompressible simulation of airflow over a NACA 0012 airfoil.
Use the simpleFoam solver.
Use the k-omega SST turbulence model.

The Reynolds number is 3,000,000.
The airfoil chord length is 1 meter.

The mesh is a single layer of cells in the z-direction.
The patches for the thin dimension are named `frontAndBack`.
In the `constant/polyMesh/boundary` file, the `frontAndBack` patches are defined with `type empty;`.
The boundary condition for velocity (`U`) on the `frontAndBack` patches is `type empty;`.
The boundary condition for pressure (`p`) on the `frontAndBack` patches is `type empty;`.
The boundary condition for turbulent kinetic energy (`k`) on the `frontAndBack` patches is `type empty;`.
The boundary condition for specific dissipation rate (`omega`) on the `frontAndBack` patches is `type empty;`.
