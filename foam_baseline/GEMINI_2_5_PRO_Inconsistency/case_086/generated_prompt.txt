Simulate the transient cooling of a hot block in a 2D sealed room.
Use the compressible solver `buoyantPimpleFoam`.
The room dimensions are 5 meters in width and 3 meters in height.
The simulation is 2D, with the z-direction being one cell thick and using `empty` boundary conditions.
A 0.5 meter by 0.5 meter block is located at the center of the room's floor.
The initial temperature of the block is 400 K.
The initial temperature of the surrounding air is 300 K.
The initial pressure of the air is 101325 Pa.
All walls are adiabatic, using a `zeroGradient` boundary condition for temperature.
All walls have a `noSlip` boundary condition for velocity.
Gravity is defined as (0, -9.81, 0) m/s^2.
The equation of state for the fluid is `perfectGas`.
The thermophysical properties are defined using `hePsiThermo` with a `pureMixture` of `sutherland` transport, `janaf` thermo, `specie`, and `sensibleEnthalpy` energy.
The simulation will run for a total of 100 seconds.
The mesh will consist of 100 cells in the x-direction and 60 cells in the y-direction.
The k-omega SST turbulence model will be used.
The time step will be controlled by a maximum Courant number of 1.0.
Simulation results will be written to disk every 1 second of simulation time.
Standard second-order numerical schemes will be used for discretization.
Standard solver settings and tolerances will be used within the fvSolution file.
