Simulate a single spherical particle settling in a quiescent fluid column using the DPMFoam solver.
The domain is a vertical cylinder with a height of 1m along the Y-axis and a diameter of 0.2m.
The fluid is water with a density of 998 kg/m^3.
The kinematic viscosity of the water is 1e-6 m^2/s.
A single spherical particle with a diameter of 1mm and a density of 2500 kg/m^3 is used.
The particle is initialized at position (0, 0.9, 0).
The fluid velocity is initialized to zero everywhere.
The acceleration due to gravity is (0, -9.81, 0) m/s^2.
The cylinder walls have a noSlip boundary condition for velocity and a zeroGradient boundary condition for pressure.
The top and bottom patches are walls with a noSlip boundary condition for velocity and a zeroGradient boundary condition for pressure.
The mesh should have 20 cells across the cylinder's diameter.
The particle should stick upon collision with any wall surface.
The Schiller-Naumann drag model should be used for the particle.
The simulation should be treated as a laminar flow, with no turbulence model enabled.
The simulation should run for a total duration of 5 seconds.
The primary objective is to track the particle's trajectory as it settles.
