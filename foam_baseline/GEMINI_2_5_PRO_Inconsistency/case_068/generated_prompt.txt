Perform a conjugate heat transfer simulation of an electronics component cooled by forced air convection.
Use the chtMultiRegionSimpleFoam solver.
The simulation domain consists of three regions: a solid silicon chip, a solid aluminum housing, and a fluid air region.
The silicon chip has dimensions of 10mm x 10mm x 1mm.
The housing is a rectangular channel with dimensions 50mm x 30mm x 10mm.
The thermophysical properties for silicon, aluminum, and air should be treated as constant.
The silicon chip generates a total of 50 Watts of heat, which is to be specified via fvOptions.
Air enters the housing through an inlet with a uniform velocity of 2 m/s.
The inlet air temperature is 293 K.
The k-omega SST turbulence model will be used for the air region.
The outer surface of the housing, named 'outerCasing', is perfectly insulated.
Apply a zeroGradient temperature boundary condition to the 'outerCasing' surface.
The air exits the domain at an outlet boundary.
