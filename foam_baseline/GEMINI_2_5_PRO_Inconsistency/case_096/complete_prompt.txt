Simulate a 2D dam break problem using the `interFoam` solver for two incompressible, immiscible fluids (water and air). The domain is a rectangular tank, 0.5m long and 0.5m high, extruded by 0.01m in the z-direction to create a single layer of cells. Gravity acts in the negative y-direction, g = (0 -9.81 0). Initially, a column of water (alpha.water=1) occupies the region x < 0.15m. The rest of the domain is air (alpha.water=0). Use `setFieldsDict` for initialization.

Boundary setup in `constant/polyMesh/boundary`:
- `leftWall`, `rightWall`, `lowerWall`: type `wall`.
- `atmosphere`: type `patch`.
- `frontAndBack`: type `empty`.

Boundary conditions for fields in `0/` directory:
- `U`: `noSlip` on walls, `pressureInletOutletVelocity` on atmosphere, and `empty` on `frontAndBack`.
- `p_rgh`: `fixedFluxPressure` on walls, `totalPressure` on atmosphere, and `empty` on `frontAndBack`.
- `alpha.water`: `zeroGradient` on walls, `inletOutlet` on atmosphere, and `empty` on `frontAndBack`.
