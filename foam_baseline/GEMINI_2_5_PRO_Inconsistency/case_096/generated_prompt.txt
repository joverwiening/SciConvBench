Simulate a 2D dam break problem using the `interFoam` solver.
The simulation involves two incompressible, immiscible fluids, water and air.
The domain is a rectangular tank with dimensions 0.5m in length and 0.5m in height.
The domain is extruded by 0.01m in the z-direction to create a single layer of cells.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction with a value of (0 -9.81 0).
Initially, a column of water where alpha.water=1 occupies the region x < 0.15m.
The rest of the domain is initially air where alpha.water=0.
Use the `setFieldsDict` utility for field initialization.
The boundary named `leftWall` is of type `wall`.
The boundary named `rightWall` is of type `wall`.
The boundary named `lowerWall` is of type `wall`.
The boundary named `atmosphere` is of type `patch`.
The boundary named `frontAndBack` is of type `symmetry`.
The velocity field `U` uses a `noSlip` condition on the `leftWall`, `rightWall`, and `lowerWall` boundaries.
The velocity field `U` uses a `pressureInletOutletVelocity` condition on the `atmosphere` boundary.
The velocity field `U` uses a `symmetry` condition on the `frontAndBack` boundary.
The relative pressure field `p_rgh` uses a `fixedFluxPressure` condition on the walls and `frontAndBack` boundaries.
The relative pressure field `p_rgh` uses a `totalPressure` condition on the `atmosphere` boundary.
The phase fraction field `alpha.water` uses a `zeroGradient` condition on the walls and `frontAndBack` boundaries.
The phase fraction field `alpha.water` uses an `inletOutlet` condition on the `atmosphere` boundary.
The total simulation time is 2.0 seconds.
The mesh has 100 cells in the x-direction.
The mesh has 100 cells in the y-direction.
The maximum Courant number for time step control is 0.5.
The flow is treated as laminar.
The density of water is 1000 kg/m^3.
The density of air is 1.2 kg/m^3.
The kinematic viscosity of water is 1e-6 m^2/s.
The kinematic viscosity of air is 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The surface tension between water and air is 0.072 N/m.
