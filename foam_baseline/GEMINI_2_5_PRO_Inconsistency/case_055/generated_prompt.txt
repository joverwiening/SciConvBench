Simulate compressible air flow through a 2D converging-diverging nozzle.
Use the `rhoCentralFoam` solver.
The fluid is compressible air, treated as a perfect gas.
Use a temperature-dependent transport model, such as Sutherland.
Create a 2D nozzle mesh with boundary patches named `inlet`, `outlet`, and `nozzleWall`.
The mesh should also include a `frontAndBack` patch of type `empty`.
The nozzle inlet height should be based on best practices.
The nozzle throat height should be based on best practices.
The nozzle outlet height should be based on best practices.
The length of the converging section should be based on best practices.
The length of the diverging section should be based on best practices.
The inlet boundary condition should be a stagnation state.
The total pressure at the inlet should be based on best practices.
The total temperature at the inlet should be based on best practices.
The outlet boundary condition should be a set back pressure.
The back pressure value at the outlet should be based on best practices.
The `nozzleWall` boundary should be an adiabatic no-slip wall.
