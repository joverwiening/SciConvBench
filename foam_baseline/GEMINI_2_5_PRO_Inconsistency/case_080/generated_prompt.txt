Set up a simulation of a three-phase fluidized bed in a cylindrical column.
The solver to be used is `multiphaseEulerFoam`.
The phases are air (gas), water (liquid), and sand (solid particles), defined in `phaseProperties` as `(air water sand)`.
The column has a diameter of 0.1 m and a height of 1 m.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction with a value of (0 -9.81 0).
The simulation is transient and runs for 10 seconds.
The simulation is run under isothermal conditions.
Air is treated as a perfect gas with a viscosity of 1.8e-5 Pa·s.
Water is treated as an incompressible liquid with a density of 1000 kg/m^3 and a viscosity of 1e-3 Pa·s.
Sand is a constant density solid with a density of 2650 kg/m^3 and a particle diameter of 500 microns.
A kinetic theory of granular flow model will be used to determine the viscosity of the sand phase.
The Schaeffer model will be used for the sand frictional stress.
The Gidaspow drag model will be used for the interactions between the phases.
The initial conditions consist of a bed of sand settled at the bottom 0.2 m of the column.
In this settled bed, the sand volume fraction is 0.6 and the water volume fraction is 0.4.
The rest of the column above the 0.2 m mark is filled with water, with a volume fraction of 1.0.
Air is injected from the bottom inlet at a velocity of 0.5 m/s.
The top of the column is an outlet boundary with a fixed total pressure of 101325 Pa.
The column walls are treated as no-slip boundaries.
