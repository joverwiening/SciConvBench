Simulate turbulent airflow over a backward-facing step.
The simulation will be two-dimensional.
The solver to be used is simpleFoam.
The turbulence model is kOmegaSST.
The step height is 0.1 m.
The inlet freestream velocity is 20 m/s.
The air density is 1.225 kg/m^3.
The dynamic viscosity of the air is 4.9e-5 Pa·s.
This corresponds to a Reynolds number of 50,000 based on the step height.
The computational domain extends 1.0 m upstream of the step.
The computational domain extends 2.0 m downstream of the step.
The top boundary of the domain is a symmetry plane.
The inlet turbulence intensity is 5%.
The inlet turbulent mixing length is 0.005 m.
The inlet boundary condition for k is a fixed uniform value of 1.5.
The inlet boundary condition for omega is a fixed uniform value of 447.2.
Standard wall functions will be used on all wall boundaries.
The mesh should be designed to place the first cell center at a y+ value between 30 and 300.
The outlet boundary condition for turbulence variables is zeroGradient.
