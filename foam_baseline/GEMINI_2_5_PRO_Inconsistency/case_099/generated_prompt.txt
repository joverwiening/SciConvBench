Simulate 2D laminar, transient flow past a square cylinder in a channel using the icoFoam solver.
The simulation domain is a channel 2.2m long and 0.41m high.
The simulation is treated as 2D by using a single cell in the z-direction.
A square cylinder with a side length of 0.1m is placed in the flow path.
The center of the square cylinder is located at coordinates (0.2, 0.205).
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 0.001 m^2/s.
The inlet boundary condition for velocity (U) is a parabolic inflow profile with a maximum value of (1.5 0 0) m/s.
The outlet boundary condition for velocity (U) is zeroGradient.
The topAndBottomWalls boundary condition for velocity (U) is noSlip.
The cylinder boundary condition for velocity (U) is noSlip.
The inlet boundary condition for pressure (p) is zeroGradient.
The outlet boundary condition for pressure (p) is a fixed value of 0.
The topAndBottomWalls boundary condition for pressure (p) is zeroGradient.
The cylinder boundary condition for pressure (p) is zeroGradient.
The frontAndBack patches for both velocity and pressure are of type empty.
The simulation end time is 8.0 seconds.
The time step is controlled by a maximum Courant number (maxCo) of 0.5.
The mesh is refined in the region around the square cylinder and in its wake.
The mesh resolution is approximately 200 cells in the x-direction and 80 cells in the y-direction.
Standard second-order accurate numerical schemes are used for discretization.
Standard iterative solvers with a tolerance of 1e-6 are used for the pressure and velocity equations.
The initial condition for the velocity field is uniform (0 0 0) m/s.
The initial condition for the pressure field is uniform 0.
