Simulate a 2D supersonic flow of air over a 10-degree wedge.
The freestream Mach number is 2.
Treat air as a perfect gas.
Use the `rhoCentralFoam` solver.
The freestream pressure is 101325 Pa.
The freestream temperature is 300 K.
The freestream velocity is (686 0 0) m/s.
Use the `hePsiThermo` thermophysical model with the `perfectGas` equation of state.
The computational domain is a rectangle.
The wedge has a chord length of 1 meter.
The wedge leading edge is located at x=0, y=0.
The domain extends from x=-1m to x=3m.
The domain extends from y=0m to y=2m.
The mesh is 2D, extruded one cell in the z-direction.
The number of cells in the streamwise (x) direction is 200.
The number of cells in the cross-stream (y) direction is 100.
The inlet boundary is at x=-1m.
The outlet boundary is at x=3m.
The top boundary is at y=2m.
The bottom boundary consists of the wedge surface and the region before it at y=0.
The inlet boundary condition uses fixed values for U, p, and T.
The outlet boundary condition is `waveTransmissive` for p and T, and `zeroGradient` for U.
The wedge surface and top boundary conditions are `slip`.
The `frontAndBack` boundary conditions are `empty`.
The simulation should run until a physical time of 0.05 seconds.
The maximum Courant number (maxCo) should be set to 0.5.
