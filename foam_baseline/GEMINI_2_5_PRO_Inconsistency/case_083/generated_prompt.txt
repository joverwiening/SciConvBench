Set up the initial conditions for a three-phase settling tank simulation.
The simulation uses the `multiphaseEulerFoam` solver.
The phases are sediment, water, and oil.
The initial field files are located in the `0/` directory.
The default `internalField` for `alpha.sediment` is `uniform 0`.
The default `internalField` for `alpha.oil` is `uniform 0`.
The default `internalField` for `alpha.water` is `uniform 1`.
The initial `internalField` for the velocity of all three phases is `uniform (0 0 0)`.
The initial `internalField` for the pressure field, `p_rgh`, is `uniform 0`.
A suitable turbulence model, such as k-epsilon for the continuous phase, will be used.
The boundary condition for the pressure field (`p_rgh`) on the tank walls is `fixedFluxPressure`.
The top surface of the tank has an atmospheric boundary condition.
The boundary condition for the velocity fields on the tank walls is `noSlip`.
The mean particle diameter for the sediment phase is 100 microns.
These initial fields will be modified by a `setFieldsDict` utility to create stratified layers.
