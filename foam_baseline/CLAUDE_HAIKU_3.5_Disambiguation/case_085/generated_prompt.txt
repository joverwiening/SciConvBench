Model pulverized coal combustion simulation with the following specifications:
Coal type is high-volatile bituminous coal.
Particle size distribution follows Rosin-Rammler with mean diameter of 100 microns.
Initial coal particle temperature is 400 K.
Primary air inlet temperature is 400 K.
Furnace wall temperature is fixed at 1200 K.
Computational domain is a 2D rectangular furnace, 2 m high and 0.5 m wide.
Mesh resolution is 50x200 (width x height), with refinement near the inlet.
Radiation model is P1 radiation model.
