Problem: Simulate the deformation and rise of a single 2D air bubble in a water column due to buoyancy.
Solver: interFoam.
Geometry & Domain: A 2D rectangular domain of 0.05 m (width) x 0.2 m (height) x 0.01 m (thickness).
Mesh: A uniform Cartesian mesh of 50 (x) x 200 (y) x 1 (z) cells. For better accuracy, mesh refinement around the bubble path would be ideal, but a uniform mesh is specified for simplicity.
Time Controls (controlDict): startTime 0; endTime 2.0; deltaT 1e-4; writeControl timeStep; writeInterval 100; maxCo 0.4.
Physical Properties (transportProperties): Phase 1 (water): rho=1000 kg/m^3, nu=1e-6 m^2/s. Phase 2 (air): rho=1.2 kg/m^3, nu=1.5e-5 m^2/s. Surface tension sigma = 0.072 N/m.
Gravity: Uniform downward gravity of (0 -9.81 0) m/s^2.
Initial Conditions: The domain is filled with quiescent water (alpha.water=1, U=0, p_rgh=0). A circular region for the air bubble is initialized using 'setFieldsDict' with a 'sphereToCell' entry. The bubble has a diameter of 0.01 m and is centered at (0.025, 0.025, 0.005).
Boundary Conditions:
- bottom: noSlip wall for U, zeroGradient for p_rgh and alpha.water.
- top: inletOutlet for all fields to allow fluid to exit.
- sideWalls: noSlip walls for U, zeroGradient for p_rgh and alpha.water.
