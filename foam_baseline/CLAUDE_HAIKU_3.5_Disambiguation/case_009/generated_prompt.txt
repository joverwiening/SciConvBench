Simulate a 2D square cavity flow using pisoFoam with the following specifications:

Geometry dimensions are 1x1 unit in x and y, with z-thickness of 0.1 units.
Simulation runs from time 0 to 10 with time step of 0.005 units.
Results output every 100 time steps.
Viscosity (nu) is constant at 1e-05 m^2/s.
Use k-epsilon turbulence model.

Turbulence field initialization:
Set k uniformly to 1.5e-4 with kqRWallFunction on walls.
Set epsilon uniformly to 1.4e-4 with epsilonWallFunction on walls.
Set nut uniformly to 0 with nutkWallFunction on walls.

Boundary conditions:
Velocity (U) at fixed walls: no-slip (zero velocity).
Velocity (U) at top wall: uniform 1 m/s in x-direction.
Front and back faces: 'empty'.

Initial velocity field: zero velocity throughout the domain.

Convection schemes:
div(phi,U) Gauss limitedLinearV 1
div(phi,k) Gauss limitedLinear 1
div(phi,epsilon) Gauss limitedLinear 1
div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear for viscous term.
