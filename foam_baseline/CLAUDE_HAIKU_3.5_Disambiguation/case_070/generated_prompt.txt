Model a flow through a thin porous screen in a channel with the following specifications:

Fluid is air at 20°C (293.15 K).
Inlet velocity is uniform at 5 m/s in the x-direction.
Channel dimensions are 1.0 m long and 0.1 m high.
Porous screen is located at x=0.5 m with zero thickness.
Porous baffle has Darcy pressure drop coefficient (D) = 1e7 and Inertial coefficient (I) = 0.
Mesh resolution is (100 x 20 x 1) cells total, split into two blocks.
Use blockMesh to create the mesh.
Create a 'faceZone' named 'porousBaffleZone' at the interface using 'topoSet'.
Flow is incompressible and turbulent.
