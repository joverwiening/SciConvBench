Perform a turbulent buoyant cavity simulation with the following specifications:

Geometry: Square cavity 0.10 m x 0.10 m x 0.01 m (2D via empty front/back patches).

Fluid: Air at near-room temperature.
Physical Properties: nu = 1.5e-5 m^2/s, Pr = 0.71, beta = 3.33e-3 1/K.
Turbulence Model: k-epsilon model.

Boundary Conditions:
- Top and bottom walls:
  - U: noSlip
  - p_rgh: fixedFluxPressure
  - T: zeroGradient
  - k: wall functions
  - epsilon: wall functions
