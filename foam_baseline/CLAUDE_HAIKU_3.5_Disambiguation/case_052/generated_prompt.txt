Simulate supersonic flow over a forward-facing step with the following specifications.
Computational domain size is 0.5m long, 0.1m high.
Step height is 0.02m, located at x=0.1m.
Total mesh cells are approximately 20,000.
Mesh is structured using blockMeshDict with refinement near step corner and shock impingement area.
Inlet conditions are Mach 3, with velocity (1020.5 0 0), temperature 293K, specific turbulence parameters.
Outlet uses wave transmissive and zero gradient conditions.
Walls include slip condition for upper wall, no-slip and fixed temperature for bottom wall.
Turbulence will be modeled using appropriate wall functions.
Simulation goal is to visualize and analyze shock structure in supersonic flow over a forward-facing step.
