Simulate a 2D buoyant cavity with the following specifications.
The cavity is a 1m x 1m square domain.
Use a uniform 40x40 computational mesh with square cells.
The Rayleigh number is set to Ra = 1e5.
The left wall is hot and the right wall is cold.
The fluid is air.
Boundary conditions for top and bottom walls are:
U = noSlip
T = zeroGradient
p_rgh = fixedFluxPressure
Initial temperature is uniform at 293 K throughout the cavity.
