Configure a 2D bubble column simulation using twoPhaseEulerFoam. The column is 0.5m wide (X-direction) and 2m high (Y-direction). It is initially filled with water. Air is injected from a sparger at the bottom center. The goal is to simulate the rise of air bubbles due to buoyancy. The phases are air (dispersed) and water (continuous). The gravity vector in constant/g is set to `g (0 -9.81 0);`. The bottom boundary has an inlet patch where `alpha.air` is `fixedValue 1` and `U.air` is `fixedValue (0 0.1 0)`. The top boundary is an outlet. The side walls are `noSlip`. The front and back patches are `empty`.
