Model a steady-state, buoyancy-driven natural convection flow in a vertical heated pipe using buoyantSimpleFoam in OpenFOAM v10 (Foundation).
Use a pipe of length 2 m aligned with the Z-axis and diameter 0.1 m.
Use air with thermophysical properties set for a perfectGas equation of state.
Set gravity in constant/g to g (0 0 -9.81).
Use an operating/reference pressure pRef of 101325 Pa.
Use laminar flow with simulationType laminar in constant/turbulenceProperties.
Use a structured mesh with 200 cells along the length (z) and 20 cells across the diameter (10 per radius), giving approximately Δz ≈ 0.01 m and Δr ≈ 0.005 m.
At the inlet (bottom, z=0), set U to pressureInletOutletVelocity and T to fixedValue 300 K.
At the inlet (bottom, z=0), set p to zeroGradient.
At the outlet (top), set U to zeroGradient and T to zeroGradient, with pressure fixed at the outlet.
At the wall, set U to noSlip and T to fixedValue 350 K.
At the wall, set p to zeroGradient.
Do not include radiation heat transfer; treat heating as purely convective with a fixed wall temperature of 350 K.
Initialize fields in the 0/ directory as U uniform (0 0 0), p uniform 101325 Pa, and T uniform 300 K.
