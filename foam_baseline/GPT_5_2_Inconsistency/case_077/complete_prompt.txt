Simulate a transient thermal plume rising in a 2D domain using `buoyantPimpleFoam`. A small, hot patch at the bottom center of the domain is held at 500 K, while the surrounding fluid is initially at 300 K. Gravity is (0 -9.81 0). The simulation is expected to show large density variations as the hot air rises. In `constant/thermophysicalProperties`, configure the fluid properties as follows: `thermoType heRhoThermo; mixture pureMixture; transport sutherland; thermo hConst; equationOfState perfectGas; specie hConst; energy sensibleEnthalpy;`. The Sutherland model for viscosity should use `As 1.4792e-06;` and `Ts 116;`. The initial temperature field is `uniform 300;` and the hot patch BC is `fixedValue uniform 500;`. All other boundaries are open to the atmosphere.
