Model a transient two-phase OpenFOAM case of a high-pressure compressible gas jet injected into a water-filled tank to observe jet penetration and expansion in water.  
Use the interFoam solver for the two-phase flow setup.  
Treat the gas phase as compressible using the perfectGas equation of state.  
Treat the water phase as incompressible.  
Set the gas inlet condition such that the injected gas enters at Mach 0.8.  
Assume the injected gas is air.  
Assume the initial temperature is 293.15 K (20 °C) for property setup.  
Make reasonable best-practice assumptions for the OpenFOAM version.  
Make reasonable best-practice assumptions for the tank and nozzle geometry and domain size.  
Make reasonable best-practice assumptions for the initial operating pressure of the water in the tank.
