Set up a 3D transient dam-break simulation in OpenFOAM using the multiphaseEulerFoam solver with three phases: air, water, and oil.
Assume a reasonable OpenFOAM version based on best practices.
Use a rectangular tank domain sized 1.0 m × 0.6 m × 0.4 m.
Assume a reasonable mesh resolution based on best practices.
Set gravity to act in the -y direction.
Define phases in transportProperties as air, water, and oil.
Use air properties: rho.air = 1.225 kg/m^3 and nu.air = 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
Use water properties: rho.water = 1000 kg/m^3 and nu.water = 1.0e-6 m^2/s.
Use light oil properties: rho.oil = 850 kg/m^3 and nu.oil = 5e-6 m^2/s.
Initialize fields using setFieldsDict for alpha.air, alpha.water, and alpha.oil.
Initially place a water column of size 0.2 m × 0.6 m × 0.2 m in one corner of the tank.
Initially place an oil layer of size 0.2 m × 0.6 m × 0.05 m on top of the water column.
Fill the remainder of the domain with air.
Apply no-slip wall boundary conditions on all walls.
Set the top boundary as an atmospheric inlet/outlet.
Run the simulation to endTime 2.0 s.
Use a fixed time step deltaT 1e-4 s.
Target a maximum Courant number maxCo 0.5 for stability.
