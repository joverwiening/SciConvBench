Simulate transient natural-convection cooling in a 2D sealed room using OpenFOAM buoyantPimpleFoam with a compressible formulation and Boussinesq equation of state.
Use a 5 m × 3 m domain in x–y, extruded one cell in z with frontAndBack patches set to empty.
Set gravity to (0 -9.81 0).
Initialize temperature with a 0.5 m × 0.5 m square region at 400 K and the surrounding air at 300 K.
Model the hot block as only an initial temperature region in the air (no separate solid region or solid material properties).
Apply adiabatic thermal boundary conditions on all room walls (T zeroGradient).
Apply noSlip velocity boundary conditions on all room walls (U fixedValue uniform (0 0 0)).
In constant/thermophysicalProperties, set thermoType to:
type hePsiThermo.
mixture pureMixture.
transport sutherland.
thermo janaf.
equationOfState Boussinesq.
specie specie.
energy sensibleEnthalpy.
Run the simulation until the maximum temperature within the initially hot 0.5 m × 0.5 m region cools to ≤ 301 K.
If a hard cap on total simulation time is needed as a fallback, choose a reasonable value based on best practices.
Make a reasonable assumption based on best practices for the placement of the 0.5 m × 0.5 m hot region within the 5 m × 3 m room.
