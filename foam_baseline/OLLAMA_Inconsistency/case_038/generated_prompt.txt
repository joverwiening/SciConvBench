Simulate thermo‑elastic deformation of a solid aluminum block fixed on one face and heated on the opposite face.  
Use a cubic block of dimensions 0.1 m × 0.1 m × 0.1 m.  
Material properties: Young’s modulus 69e9 Pa, Poisson’s ratio 0.33, density 2700 kg/m³, thermal expansion coefficient 2.3e‑5 1/K, reference temperature 300 K.  
Apply a temperature field with the heated face at 400 K, the fixed face insulated (zeroGradient), and all other faces zeroGradient.  
Mechanical boundary condition: the fixedFace has displacement fixedValue uniform (0 0 0); all other faces have zeroGradient for displacement.  
Mesh the block with approximately 20 cells per side, giving about 8000 hexahedral cells (≈5 mm cell size).  
Run the simulation for a total physical time of 10 seconds using the application buoyantPimpleFoam.  
Select a time step size appropriate for the simulation based on stability and convergence criteria.
