Model a steady-state, buoyancy-driven flow in a vertical heated pipe using the buoyantSimpleFoam solver. The pipe has a length of 2m, aligned with the Z-axis, and a diameter of 0.1m. Air enters at the bottom (z=0) at 300K and is heated by the pipe wall, which is maintained at a constant temperature of 350K. The goal is to observe the natural convection flow established due to density differences. In the constant/g dictionary, the gravity is set to `g (0 0 -9.81);`. The thermophysical properties are for air, using the `perfectGas` equation of state. The inlet boundary condition for U is `pressureInletOutletVelocity` and for T is `fixedValue 300`. The outlet is a `zeroGradient` for U and T, with a fixed pressure. The wall has `noSlip` for U and `fixedValue 350` for T.
