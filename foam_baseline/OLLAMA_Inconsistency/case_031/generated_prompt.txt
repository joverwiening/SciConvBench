Use the icoFoam solver for an incompressible laminar flow simulation.  
Create a rectangular channel that is 0.5 m long in the x‑direction and 0.1 m high in the y‑direction, with a single cell thickness in the z‑direction.  
Define the geometry and mesh in blockMeshDict and create the following patches: inlet, outlet, topWall, bottomWall, and frontAndBack.  
Set the frontAndBack patches to type wall.  
In the 0/U file, prescribe a parabolic inlet velocity profile with a maximum velocity of 1 m s⁻¹, apply a zeroGradient condition at the outlet, and use noSlip boundary conditions on topWall, bottomWall, and frontAndBack.  
In the 0/p file, set a fixedValue of 0 Pa at the outlet and zeroGradient on inlet, topWall, bottomWall, and frontAndBack.  
Assume the fluid is water at room temperature (approximately 20 °C) with a density of 998 kg m⁻³ and a kinematic viscosity of 1.0e‑6 m² s⁻¹.  
Run the simulation for a total physical time of 5 seconds with a constant time step of 0.001 seconds.  
Write simulation results every 0.1 seconds (i.e., every 100 time steps).
