Use the simpleFoam solver for a steady incompressible flow simulation.  
Mesh only the bottom half of the channel and apply a symmetry plane at the channel centerline.  
Set the channel length to approximately 10 m and the full channel height to approximately 1 m, meshing the bottom half (0.5 m height).  
Define the patches in blockMeshDict as inlet, outlet, bottomWall, centerline, and set frontAndBack patches to type empty.  
In the 0/U file, assign a uniform inlet velocity of (1 0 0) m/s as a fixedValue condition on the inlet patch.  
Set the centerline patch in 0/U to a symmetryPlane boundary condition.  
Apply a no‑slip fixedValue condition with value (0 0 0) on the bottomWall patch in 0/U.  
In the 0/p file, set the outlet pressure to a zeroGradient condition.  
Create a mesh with 100 cells in the streamwise (x) direction and 20 cells in the vertical (y) direction for the bottom half of the channel.
