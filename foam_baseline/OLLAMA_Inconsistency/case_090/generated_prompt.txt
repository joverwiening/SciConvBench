Set up a 2D transient simulation of vortex shedding behind a circular cylinder using the pimpleFoam solver.  
The fluid is incompressible with kinematic viscosity ν = 0.01 m²/s and inlet velocity U = (1 0 0) m/s, giving Reynolds number 100 based on the cylinder diameter.  
The computational domain is a rectangular channel 20 m long (x‑direction) and 10 m high (y‑direction) with a single cell in the z‑direction (empty type for 2‑D).  
Place the cylinder of diameter 1 m with its centre at x = 5 m from the inlet face and y = 3 m (offset 2 m below the vertical centreline).  
Apply the following boundary conditions: inlet – fixedValue U; outlet – zeroGradient U and fixedValue pressure p = 0; topAndBottom – slip; cylinder – noSlip; frontAndBack – empty.  
Use a mesh with cell size approximately 0.045 m, giving about 450 cells in the x‑direction, 225 cells in the y‑direction, and one cell in the z‑direction.  
Run the simulation from t = 0 s to t = 200 s with a constant time step Δt = 0.1 s.  
Use a laminar flow model (no turbulence model) appropriate for Re = 100.  
Post‑process the results to observe the von Kármán vortex street and compute the Strouhal number.
