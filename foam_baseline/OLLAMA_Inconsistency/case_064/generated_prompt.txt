Create a 2‑D channel of length 1.0 m and height 0.2 m with a flexible flap of length 0.05 m and thickness 0.005 m attached at x = 0.4 m on the bottom wall.  
Extrude the geometry with one cell in the z‑direction and leave the frontAndBack patches empty.  
Use incompressible water with kinematic viscosity ν = 1e‑6 m² s⁻¹ and density ρ = 1000 kg m⁻³.  
Apply a fixed inlet velocity U = (1 0 0) m s⁻¹ on the patch named inlet.  
Apply a fixed pressure p = 0 Pa on the patch named outlet.  
Set no‑slip condition on the patch named walls, which represents both the top and bottom channel walls.  
Fix the displacement of the flap base on the patch named flapBase.  
Couple the flap surface on the patch named flapSurface as the fluid‑structure interface.  
Initialise the fluid velocity field uniformly to U = (1 0 0) m s⁻¹ and the fluid pressure field uniformly to p = 0 Pa.  
Initialise the solid displacement field uniformly to U = (0 0 0) m.  
Use a total simulation time of 2 s with a constant time step of 1e‑3 s (2000 steps).  
Write results every 0.01 s.  
Output the fluid velocity U and pressure p, and the solid displacement U.  
Do not write the solid Cauchy stress field sigma.  
Use pimpleFoam for both fluid and solid regions on a combined mesh with no explicit coupling.  
Set PIMPLE residual tolerances: velocity 1e‑6, pressure 1e‑6, solid displacement 1e‑5.  
Use 2 PIMPLE correctors and 0 non‑orthogonal correctors (or 1 if the mesh is highly non‑orthogonal).  
Mesh the fluid region with 200 cells in the streamwise direction (Δx≈0.005 m) and 40 cells in the wall‑normal direction (Δy≈0.005 m).  
Mesh the solid flap region with 20 cells along the flap length (Δx≈0.0025 m) and 5 cells through the thickness (Δy≈0.001 m).  
Use one cell in the extruded z‑direction for both fluid and solid meshes.
