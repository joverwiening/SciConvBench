Simulate a steady-state, two-phase (oil–water) flow in a horizontal pipe using twoPhaseEulerFoam.  
Set the pipe diameter to 0.1 m and the pipe length to 5 m.  
Impose a fixed mixture velocity of 0.5 m s⁻¹ at the inlet.  
Specify a fixed water volume fraction α.water = 0.3 at the inlet.  
Apply a noSlip boundary condition on the pipe walls.  
Set the outlet boundary for α.water as a fixedValue with uniform value 0.3.  
Use a water density of 1000 kg m⁻³ and an oil density of 850 kg m⁻³.  
Use a water dynamic viscosity of 1 × 10⁻³ Pa·s and an oil dynamic viscosity of 5 × 10⁻³ Pa·s.  
Assume standard atmospheric pressure and temperature for the simulation environment.  
Run the case to convergence to obtain the steady-state velocity and phase distribution fields.
