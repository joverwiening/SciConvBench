Simulate the static deflection of a 2D cantilever beam under a downward traction load using linear elasticity. The beam is 1.0 m long and 0.1 m high with a structured mesh of 100 × 10 cells.

Material (aluminum):
- `E = 70e9` Pa
- `nu = 0.33`

Boundary conditions in `0/D`:
- `fixedEnd` (x=0): `fixedValue uniform (0 0 0)`
- `loadEnd` (x=1): `tractionDisplacement` with `traction uniform (0 -10000 0)` and `pressure uniform 0`
- top/bottom: `zeroGradient`

In `system/controlDict`, set:
```foam
application     solidDisplacementFoam;
```
