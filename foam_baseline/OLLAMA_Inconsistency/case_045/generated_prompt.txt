Perform a steady-state incompressible simulation of airflow over a NACA 0012 airfoil at a low angle of attack using the simpleFoam solver with the k‑omega SST turbulence model.  
The simulation is two‑dimensional; the mesh consists of a single cell layer in the z‑direction.  
The thin‑dimension patches are named frontAndBack and are defined as type wall in constant/polyMesh/boundary.  
In the 0/U file the boundary condition for frontAndBack is set to noSlip; in the 0/p file it is set to zeroGradient.  
Use a free‑stream velocity magnitude of 10 m s⁻¹.  
Set the angle of attack to 5°.  
Use a chord length of 1 m for the NACA 0012 airfoil.  
Define the computational domain extents as 5 chord lengths upstream of the airfoil, 10 chord lengths downstream, and 5 chord lengths above and below the airfoil.  
Use air at standard conditions with density 1.225 kg m⁻³ and kinematic viscosity 1.5 × 10⁻⁵ m² s⁻¹.
