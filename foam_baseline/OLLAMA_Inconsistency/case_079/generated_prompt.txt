Perform a transient simulation of a 2‑D Rayleigh‑Bénard convection cell using buoyantPimpleFoam.  
The domain is a rectangle with height 0.1 m and width 0.2 m.  
The bottom wall temperature is 305 K and the top wall temperature is 295 K; side walls are adiabatic.  
The fluid is water modeled with the Boussinesq approximation.  
In constant/thermophysicalProperties set thermoType hePsiThermo; and equationOfState Boussinesq; with water coefficients (beta = 2.07e‑4 K⁻¹, rho0 = 998 kg m⁻³, Cp = 4182 J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹, k = 0.6 W m⁻¹ K⁻¹).  
Use a structured mesh with 100 cells in the vertical direction and 200 cells in the horizontal direction.  
Set the time step to 1e‑3 s and run the simulation for a total physical time of 10 s.  
In system/fvSchemes use div(phi,T) Gauss linear; for temperature.  
Use a density‑weighted scheme for velocity: div(rhoPhi,U) Gauss upwind;.  
Set appropriate relaxation factors for pressure and velocity in system/fvSolution (e.g., pRelaxFactor 0.3, URelaxFactor 0.7).  
Enable the buoyancy term in the momentum equation by setting buoyancyForce on.  
Output fields (U, p, T) at every 0.1 s.
