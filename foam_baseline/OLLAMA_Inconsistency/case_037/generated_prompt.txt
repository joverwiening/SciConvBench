Simulate steady, incompressible, laminar flow of water through a rectangular 2D channel that is 1 m long and 0.2 m high.  
Use a rigid, stationary cylindrical obstacle whose cross‑sectional area occupies approximately 10 % of the channel’s cross‑section.  
Extrude the 2D mesh one cell in the spanwise direction and set `frontAndBack` to `empty`.  
Generate a mesh with a uniform cell size of about 5 mm, giving roughly 200 cells in the streamwise direction and 40 cells in the vertical direction.  
Set the water kinematic viscosity `nu` to 1e-6 m²/s.  
Apply the following boundary conditions:  
- Velocity field `0/U`: inlet `fixedValue uniform (0.1 0 0)`, outlet `zeroGradient`, walls and obstacle `noSlip`, frontAndBack `empty`.  
- Pressure field `0/p`: inlet `zeroGradient`, outlet `fixedValue uniform 0`, walls and obstacle `zeroGradient`, frontAndBack `empty`.  
In `system/controlDict`, set `application` to `solidDisplacementFoam`.  
Use a constant time step Δt of 0.1 s.  
Set the simulation end time `endTime` to 1000 s.  
Write output files every 100 s by setting `writeInterval` to 100.
