Use the interFoam solver for a transient two‑phase simulation of a high‑pressure compressible gas injected into water.  
Define the gas phase with the perfectGas equation of state and the water phase as incompressible.  
The water tank dimensions are 1.0 m long, 0.5 m wide, and 0.5 m high.  
The gas enters the tank at Mach 0.8.  
Assume an inlet pressure of 5 × 10⁵ Pa (0.5 MPa) and an inlet temperature of 300 K.  
Set appropriate inlet boundary conditions to impose the specified Mach number, pressure, and temperature.  
Generate a mesh fine enough to resolve the gas jet and its interaction with the water, especially near the inlet and expected jet path.  
Choose a time step that satisfies the Courant‑Friedrichs‑Lewy (CFL) condition for the high‑velocity gas flow.  
Run the simulation for a duration sufficient to capture the jet penetration and expansion within the water tank.  
Record transient fields such as velocity, pressure, and phase fraction to analyze the gas jet dynamics.
