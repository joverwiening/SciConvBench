Simulate a 2D gas‑liquid bubble column using the twoPhaseEulerFoam solver.  
The computational domain is a rectangle 0.5 m wide and 2 m high.  
Create a structured mesh with 100 cells in the width direction and 400 cells in the height direction.  
Run a transient simulation for a total physical time of 5 seconds with an initial time step of 0.001 seconds and allow adaptive time‑step control.  
Use the k‑omega SST turbulence model for both phases.  
Set the material properties as follows: air density 1.225 kg/m³, air dynamic viscosity 1.8e‑5 Pa·s; water density 998 kg/m³, water dynamic viscosity 1.0e‑3 Pa·s.  
Apply a surface tension coefficient of 0.072 N/m for the air‑water interface.  
At the bottom inlet patch, set the volume fraction of air (alpha.air) to a fixed value of 0.8 and the volume fraction of water (alpha.water) to a fixed value of 0.3.  
Specify the inlet velocity of air as (0 0.2 0) m/s and the inlet velocity of water as (0 0.01 0) m/s.  
Define the top boundary as an outlet with zero‑gradient conditions for the volume fractions and a pressure outlet condition for the pressure field.  
Set the side boundaries as no‑slip walls for velocity and zero‑gradient for the volume fractions.  
Initialize all fields uniformly with the specified inlet values.
