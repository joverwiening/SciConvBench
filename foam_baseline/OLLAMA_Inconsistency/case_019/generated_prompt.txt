The computational domain is a rectangular region spanning x = 0 to 0.02 m, y = –0.01 to 0.01 m, and z = –0.01 to 0.01 m.  
The mesh consists of 100 cells in the x‑direction, 40 cells in the y‑direction, and 1 cell in the z‑direction.  
The left boundary at x = 0 is named fuel, the right boundary at x = 0.02 m is named air, the top and bottom boundaries are named outlet, and the front and back faces are empty.  
At the fuel inlet, inject pure methane (Y_CH4 ≈ 1.0) at a temperature of 293 K with a velocity of 0.1 m s⁻¹ and a totalPressure boundary condition of 101 325 Pa.  
At the air inlet, supply air with mass fractions Y_O2 = 0.23 and Y_N2 = 0.77 at a temperature of 293 K with a velocity of –0.1 m s⁻¹ and a totalPressure boundary condition of 101 325 Pa.  
The outlet (top and bottom) faces use zeroGradient boundary conditions for both velocity and temperature.  
Initialize the entire domain at time 0 with a uniform temperature of 2000 K, zero velocity, and species mass fractions Y_CH4 ≈ 0.05, Y_O2 ≈ 0.23, Y_N2 ≈ 0.72, all other species set to zero.  
Use the GRI‑Mech 3.0 chemical mechanism with chemistry type EulerImplicit and ODE solver Rosenbrock43.  
Set the combustion model and momentum transport to laminar.  
Employ incompressible finite‑rate chemistry using heRhoThermo, mixture = coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture, transport = Sutherland, and thermodynamics = JANAF.  
Run the simulation with the reactingFoam solver.  
Use a timestep of 1 × 10⁻⁴ s, an end time of 0.5 s, and write results every 0.05 s.
