Simulate a single spherical particle settling in a quiescent water column using the DPMFoam solver.  
The domain is a vertical cylinder with a height of 1 m (aligned with the Y‑axis) and a diameter of 0.2 m.  
Create a structured mesh with a uniform cell size of approximately 0.5 mm, giving roughly 2000 cells in the vertical direction and 400 cells across the cylinder diameter.  
Set the fluid properties to those of water (density ≈ 1000 kg/m³, dynamic viscosity ≈ 0.001 Pa·s).  
Specify gravity in the constant/g dictionary as `g (0 9.81 0);`.  
Initialize the fluid velocity to zero everywhere.  
Apply a no‑slip condition on the cylindrical wall and simple wall conditions on the top and bottom patches.  
Insert a single spherical particle with a diameter of 1 mm and a density of 2500 kg/m³ at the initial position (0, 0.9, 0).  
Run the simulation for a total physical time of 2 seconds to capture the particle’s full settling trajectory.  
Track and record the particle’s position and velocity over time.
