Set up a 2D natural convection simulation in a sealed rectangular cavity using the buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam solver.  
Assume the cavity width is 0.10 m and the height is 0.30 m, giving a height‑to‑width aspect ratio of 3:1.  
Create a structured mesh with 100 cells in the horizontal (x) direction and 300 cells in the vertical (y) direction.  
Apply gravity in the negative y‑direction with a magnitude of 9.81 m s⁻².  
Use air as the working fluid with a reference temperature of 325 K for the Boussinesq approximation.  
Set the kinematic viscosity of air to 1.6 × 10⁻⁵ m² s⁻¹.  
Set the thermal diffusivity of air to 2.2 × 10⁻⁵ m² s⁻¹.  
Specify temperature boundary conditions:  
leftWall: fixedValue, uniform 350 K.  
rightWall: fixedValue, uniform 300 K.  
topWall: fixedValue, uniform 325 K (adiabatic wall).  
bottomWall: fixedValue, uniform 325 K (adiabatic wall).
