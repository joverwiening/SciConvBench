Simulate 3D incompressible turbulent flow around a cube using simpleFoam with the RANS kEpsilon turbulence model.  
The channel has a length of 1 m, a height of 0.1 m, and a width of 0.2 m.  
The cube has a side length of 0.02 m, is placed on the floor, centered in the spanwise direction, and its front face is located 0.2 m downstream from the inlet.  
Generate a fully 3D mesh with a cell size of about 0.005 m near the cube and walls and about 0.01 m in the core region, resulting in roughly 1–2 million cells.  
Define the left and right side patches as empty symmetry boundaries in system/blockMeshDict.  
Apply a noSlip condition on all solid walls (floor, cube, and top wall).  
At the inlet, impose a fixed velocity magnitude of 1 m s⁻¹, turbulence intensity of 5 %, turbulent kinetic energy k = 3.8×10⁻³ m² s⁻², and dissipation rate ε = 3.8×10⁻³ m² s⁻³.  
At the outlet, set a fixed gauge pressure of 0 Pa.  
Use residual tolerances of 1×10⁻⁵ for continuity and momentum, 1×10⁻⁶ for k and ε, and allow a maximum of 1000 iterations (or stop earlier if all residuals fall below the tolerances).  
Run the simulation until convergence is achieved.
