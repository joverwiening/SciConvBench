Simulate a mixed convection lid-driven cavity problem using buoyantPimpleFoam.  
The cavity is a square of 1 m × 1 m.  
Use a uniform mesh with 100 cells in the x‑direction and 100 cells in the y‑direction (≈10⁴ cells total).  
The top lid moves horizontally at 0.1 m/s and is held at a hot temperature of 350 K.  
The bottom wall is held at a cold temperature of 300 K.  
Gravity acts downwards with a magnitude of 9.81 m/s².  
Run the simulation until t = 10 s.  
Use a constant time step of Δt = 0.001 s.  
Employ the incompressiblePerfectGas model for density variations with temperature.  
In constant/thermophysicalProperties set thermoType to hePsiThermo with mixture incompressiblePerfectGas, transport const, thermo hConst, equationOfState perfectGas, specie specie, and energy sensibleEnthalpy.  
Define the mixture with specie molWeight 28.9, thermodynamics Cp 1005 and Hf 0, transport mu 1.8e-5 and Pr 0.7.  
Specify BoussinesqCoeffs with TRef 325 K and beta 0.00307 as a backup buoyancy model.
