Create a 2D axisymmetric simulation of swirling flow in a pipe using the simpleFoam solver.  
Generate a wedge mesh of 5 degrees with blockMeshDict that represents a pipe of radius 0.05 m and length 1.0 m.  
Define the patches in constant/polyMesh/boundary as follows: inlet of type patch, outlet of type patch, wall of type wall, front of type symmetryPlane, and back of type symmetryPlane.  
Set the fluid properties to air at standard conditions with density 1.225 kg m⁻³ and dynamic viscosity 1.8 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s.  
Apply at the inlet a velocity boundary condition U that combines an average axial velocity of 1 m s⁻¹ with a solid‑body swirl giving a tangential velocity of 0.5 m s⁻¹ at the pipe wall (linear decrease to zero at the centreline).  
Impose a noSlip condition for velocity on the wall patch.  
Impose a zeroGradient condition for velocity on the outlet patch.  
Impose a symmetryPlane condition for velocity on the front and back patches.
