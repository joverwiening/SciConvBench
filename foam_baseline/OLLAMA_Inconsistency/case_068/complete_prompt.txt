Perform a conjugate heat transfer (CHT) simulation of an electronics component cooled by forced air convection using the chtMultiRegionSimpleFoam solver. The setup consists of a solid silicon chip region and a fluid air region. The chip generates heat, which is specified via fvOptions. The entire assembly is enclosed in a housing. The outer surface of the housing, named 'outerCasing', is perfectly insulated from the ambient environment. Air enters at 293 K and exits at an outlet.

In the '0/air/T' file, the inlet is set to a fixed temperature. In the '0/solid/T' file, for the insulated outer boundary, specify the following:

outerCasing
{
    type            zeroGradient;
}
