Simulate 2D laminar transient flow past a square cylinder using icoFoam.  
Domain length is 2.2 m, height is 0.41 m, and thickness is 0.01 m.  
Place a square cylinder of size 0.1 m × 0.1 m in the flow path.  
Set the kinematic viscosity nu to 0.001 m²/s.  
In 0/U, apply a parabolic inlet velocity profile with maxValue (1.5 0 0), zeroGradient at the outlet, and noSlip at topAndBottomWalls, cylinder, and frontAndBack.  
In 0/p, apply zeroGradient at the inlet, fixedValue uniform 0 at the outlet, and zeroGradient at topAndBottomWalls, cylinder, and frontAndBack.  
Define the frontAndBack patches as type wall in constant/polyMesh/boundary.  
Initialize the velocity field U uniformly to (0 0 0) m/s and the pressure field p uniformly to 0 Pa throughout the domain.  
Run the transient simulation from time 0 s to 10 s with a time step of 0.001 s.  
Write output files every 0.1 s of simulated time.
