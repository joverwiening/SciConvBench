Set up a transient, compressible, turbulent flow over a heated 2D cylinder using rhoPimpleFoam and the k-omega SST turbulence model.
The freestream velocity is 10 m/s, temperature is 300 K, and pressure is 101325 Pa.
The cylinder surface is maintained at a constant temperature of 500 K.
The fluid is air.
In the constant/thermophysicalProperties file, define the fluid as a perfect gas using the thermoType block with: type hePsiThermo, mixture perfectGas, transport sutherland, thermo janaf, equationOfState perfectGas, specie specie, energy sensibleEnthalpy.
Despite specifying thermo janaf in the thermoType block, use simplified constant Cp and Hf values in the thermodynamics sub-dictionary (Cp 1005, Hf 0), as shown in the user's snippet.
In the mixture block, set specie molWeight to 28.9.
In the transport block, use Sutherland coefficients: As 1.458e-06, Ts 110.4.
Include a BoussinesqCoeffs sub-dictionary inside mixture with TRef 300, beta 0.00333, rhoRef 1.225.
Use the k-omega SST turbulence model.
The simulation is transient and compressible.
