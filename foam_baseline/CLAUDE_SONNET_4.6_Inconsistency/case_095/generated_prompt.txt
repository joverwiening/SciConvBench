Set up a 2D, steady-state, incompressible, turbulent simulation of airflow over a NACA0012 airfoil at a 5-degree angle of attack.
Use the simpleFoam solver with the k-omega SST turbulence model.
The domain is a C-grid type, one cell thick in the z-direction.
The mesh is defined in constant/polyMesh.
The boundary patches are inlet, outlet, airfoil, and frontAndBack.

Boundary conditions for inlet: U is fixedValue uniform (10 0 0). k and omega are calculated from turbulence intensity of 1% and viscosity ratio of 10. p is zeroGradient.
Boundary conditions for outlet: U is inletOutlet. k and omega are inletOutlet. p is fixedValue uniform 0.
Boundary conditions for airfoil: U is noSlip. k uses kqRWallFunction. omega uses omegaWallFunction. p is zeroGradient.
Boundary conditions for frontAndBack: The patch type is set to empty in constant/polyMesh/boundary. All fields (U, p, k, omega) have their boundary condition type set to empty.
