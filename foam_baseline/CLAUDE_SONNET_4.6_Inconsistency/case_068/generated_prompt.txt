Perform a conjugate heat transfer (CHT) simulation of an electronics component cooled by forced air convection using the chtMultiRegionSimpleFoam solver.
The setup consists of two regions: a solid silicon chip region and a fluid air region.
The chip generates heat, specified via fvOptions.
The entire assembly is enclosed in a housing.
The outer surface of the housing is named 'outerCasing'.
The outerCasing boundary is perfectly insulated from the ambient environment.
Air enters the fluid region at 293 K (fixed temperature inlet condition set in 0/air/T).
Air exits through an outlet boundary.
In the 0/solid/T file, the outerCasing boundary must use a zeroGradient condition, specified as:
outerCasing
{
    type            zeroGradient;
}
The previously described fixedValue of 293 K for outerCasing in 0/solid/T is incorrect and must not be used.
