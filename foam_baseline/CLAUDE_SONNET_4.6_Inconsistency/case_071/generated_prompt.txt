Simulate a 2D dam break case using the interFoam solver.
The domain is a rectangle with dimensions 0.6m (length) x 0.4m (height), with height along the Y-axis.
Use blockMesh to create the geometry with a single block.
Use a mesh resolution of 60 cells in the X direction and 40 cells in the Y direction (1 cell in Z for 2D), based on best practices.
The initial condition, defined in setFieldsDict, is a column of water occupying the region x < 0.15m and y < 0.3m.
The rest of the domain is filled with air.
Define two phases in transportProperties: water and air with standard properties (water: nu=1e-6 m2/s, rho=1000 kg/m3; air: nu=1.48e-5 m2/s, rho=1 kg/m3).
Set the gravity vector in constant/g to: g (0 0 -9.81);
For boundary conditions in the 0/ directory: U has noSlip on walls and pressureInletOutletVelocity on the top atmosphere patch.
p_rgh has fixedFluxPressure on walls and totalPressure on the atmosphere patch.
alpha.water has zeroGradient on walls and inletOutlet on the atmosphere patch.
The front and back patches are of type empty for a 2D simulation.
The simulation runs for 2 seconds.
Use standard interFoam fvSolution and fvSchemes settings appropriate for a dam break case.
Include a controlDict configured to run from 0 to 2 seconds with a reasonable time step (e.g., maxCo 0.5, adjustTimeStep yes, maxDeltaT 0.01).
