Simulate a 2D gas-liquid bubble column using the twoPhaseEulerFoam solver.
The domain is a rectangle of 0.5 m width and 2 m height.
The two phases are air (gas) and water (liquid).
The simulation is transient.
The bottom boundary is named 'inlet' where both air and water are introduced.
In the 0/alpha.air file, the inlet patch boundary condition is: type fixedValue; value uniform 0.8;
In the 0/alpha.water file, the inlet patch boundary condition is: type fixedValue; value uniform 0.2;
The alpha.air and alpha.water inlet values sum to 1.0, which is physically consistent.
The inlet velocity of air is (0 0.2 0) m/s.
The inlet velocity of water is (0 0.01 0) m/s.
The top boundary is an outlet.
The side boundaries are no-slip walls.
