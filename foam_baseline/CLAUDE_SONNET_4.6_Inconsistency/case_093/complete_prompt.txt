Set up a 2D dam break simulation. A column of water on the left side of a rectangular domain is released and collapses under gravity. The domain is initially filled with air. Use the interFoam solver, which is designed for two-phase, immiscible, incompressible flows using the Volume of Fluid (VOF) method. In the controlDict, set endTime to 5s. Use setFields to initialize a region with alpha.water = 1 for the water column and alpha.water = 0 for the air. Gravity is g = (0 -9.81 0). The transportProperties dictionary should contain entries for the two phases, 'water' and 'air', with their respective viscosities and densities.
