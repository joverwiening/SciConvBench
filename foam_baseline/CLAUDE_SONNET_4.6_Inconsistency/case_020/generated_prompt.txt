Set up a laminar counter-flow diffusion flame simulation using the reactingFoam solver in OpenFOAM.

The domain spans x = 0 to 0.02 m, y = -0.01 to 0.01 m, z = -0.01 to 0.01 m.
Use 100 grid points in x, 40 in y, and 1 cell in z.
Name the left boundary (x = 0) "fuel", the right boundary (x = 0.02 m) "air", the top and bottom boundaries "outlet", and the front and back faces "empty" (2-D treatment).
At the fuel inlet, inject pure methane (CH4) at 293 K with a velocity of +0.1 m/s in the x-direction.
At the air inlet, supply oxidizer at 293 K with Y_O2 = 0.23 and Y_N2 = 0.77, with a velocity of -0.1 m/s in the x-direction.
Initialize the entire domain with T = 2000 K at time 0 to ignite the flame.
Use a skeletal or reduced methane combustion mechanism based on best practices; a reasonable choice is the 4-step or 16-species skeletal GRI mechanism, or alternatively the DRM19 reduced mechanism derived from GRI-Mech 3.0.
Use compressible finite-rate chemistry with the hePsiThermo thermodynamic model.
Use coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture for mixture transport with Sutherland transport for viscosity; do not set viscosity to zero.
Use JANAF thermodynamics for species properties.
Apply a unity Lewis number assumption for species diffusion as a reasonable best-practice default.
Set the combustion model to laminar (finite-rate, no turbulence-chemistry interaction).
Set the momentum transport model to laminar.
Use EulerImplicit chemistry integration with the Rosenbrock43 ODE solver.
Set the timestep to 1e-4 s, endTime to 0.5 s, and write results every 0.05 s.
