Model the injection of a high-pressure, compressible gas into a tank of water. The gas enters at Mach 0.8. Due to the high velocity and pressure differences, compressibility effects of the gas are significant. Use the `compressibleInterFoam` solver, which is designed for compressible two-phase flows with a sharp interface. Define the gas phase using the `perfectGas` equation of state and the water phase using a suitable compressible liquid model (e.g., `perfectFluid`). The simulation is transient, and the goal is to observe the gas jet penetration and expansion in the water.
