Set up a 2D dam break simulation using the interFoam solver.
The domain is 1m long and 1m high.
Use a mesh resolution of 40 cells in the x-direction and 40 cells in the y-direction, as a reasonable baseline for dam break simulations.
The domain depth (z-direction) is 1 cell thick (0.1m) to enforce 2D behavior.
A column of water is initialized in the region 0 < x < 0.2 and 0 < y < 0.5.
The rest of the domain is filled with air.
The simulation models the collapse of the water column under gravity.
In the constant/g file, define gravity as: g (0 0 -9.81);
Use setFieldsDict to initialize the alpha.water field to 1 inside the water column region and 0 elsewhere.
The top boundary uses the pressureInletOutletVelocity condition (open atmosphere).
All other boundaries (left, right, bottom, and front/back) are walls using the noSlip condition.
Use standard interFoam physical properties: water density 1000 kg/m³, air density 1 kg/m³, water kinematic viscosity 1e-6 m²/s, air kinematic viscosity 1.48e-5 m²/s, and surface tension 0.07 N/m.
Set a reasonable end time of 1 second with a maximum Courant number of 0.5 and adjustable time stepping.
Use standard interFoam numerical schemes and solvers appropriate for this type of simulation.
