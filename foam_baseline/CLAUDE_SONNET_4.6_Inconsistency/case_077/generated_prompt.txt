Simulate a transient thermal plume rising in a 2D domain using buoyantPimpleFoam.
The domain is 2D (one cell thick in the z-direction).
Use a domain width of 1 m and height of 2 m as a reasonable assumption.
Use a mesh resolution of 40 cells in the x-direction, 80 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
Gravity is (0 -9.81 0).
A small hot patch is located at the bottom center of the domain, spanning the middle third of the bottom boundary (from x=0.333 m to x=0.667 m), held at a fixed temperature of 500 K.
The surrounding fluid is initially at 300 K (uniform initial temperature field).
All boundaries other than the hot patch are open to the atmosphere (use pressureInletOutletVelocity for velocity, totalPressure for pressure, and inletOutlet for temperature at open boundaries).
The bottom boundary outside the hot patch is a no-slip adiabatic wall.
In constant/thermophysicalProperties, configure thermoType as hePsiThermo.
Use mixture pureMixture.
Use transport sutherland with As 1.4792e-06 and Ts 116.
Use thermo hConst.
Use equationOfState Boussinesq with T0 300, beta 0.00333, and rho0 1.176.
Use specie hConst.
Use energy sensibleEnthalpy.
Set total simulated time to 20 seconds as a reasonable assumption.
Set the time step to 0.005 seconds as a reasonable assumption, giving a maximum Courant number target of around 0.5.
Use adjustable time stepping with maxCo 0.5 if supported, otherwise use the fixed time step of 0.005 s.
Write results every 1 second of simulated time.
Use PIMPLE algorithm settings appropriate for buoyant flow with nOuterCorrectors 2, nCorrectors 2, and nNonOrthogonalCorrectors 1.
The simulation should demonstrate large density variations as hot air rises from the patch and forms a thermal plume.
