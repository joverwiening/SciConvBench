Simulate a 2D dam break problem using the interFoam solver for two incompressible, immiscible fluids (water and air).
The domain is a rectangular tank, 0.5m long and 0.5m high, extruded by 0.01m in the z-direction to create a single layer of cells.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction, g = (0 -9.81 0).
Initially, a column of water (alpha.water=1) occupies the region x < 0.15m; the rest of the domain is air (alpha.water=0).
Use setFieldsDict for initialization of the alpha.water field.
Use a mesh resolution of 50 cells in the x-direction and 50 cells in the y-direction (1 cell in z), following best-practice guidelines for dam break simulations.
Define the boundary patches in constant/polyMesh/boundary as follows: leftWall, rightWall, and lowerWall are type wall; atmosphere is type patch; frontAndBack is type symmetry.
Set boundary conditions in the 0/ directory as follows.
For U: noSlip on leftWall, rightWall, and lowerWall; pressureInletOutletVelocity on atmosphere; symmetry on frontAndBack.
For p_rgh: fixedFluxPressure on leftWall, rightWall, lowerWall, and frontAndBack; totalPressure on atmosphere.
For alpha.water: zeroGradient on leftWall, rightWall, lowerWall, and frontAndBack; inletOutlet on atmosphere.
Use standard physical properties for water (density ~1000 kg/m³, kinematic viscosity ~1e-6 m²/s) and air (density ~1 kg/m³, kinematic viscosity ~1.48e-5 m²/s).
Use standard interFoam turbulence and transport model settings (laminar or default).
Provide all necessary OpenFOAM case files: blockMeshDict, setFieldsDict, 0/U, 0/p_rgh, 0/alpha.water, constant/transportProperties, constant/turbulenceProperties, and system/controlDict with reasonable run time and time-step settings.
