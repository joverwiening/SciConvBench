Configure a 2D bubble column simulation using twoPhaseEulerFoam.
The column is 0.5m wide in the X-direction and 2m high in the Y-direction.
The column is initially filled with water.
Air is injected from a sparger at the bottom center.
The goal is to simulate the rise of air bubbles due to buoyancy.
The dispersed phase is air and the continuous phase is water.
The gravity vector in constant/g is set to (0 -9.81 0).
The bottom boundary has an inlet patch where alpha.air is fixedValue 1 and U.air is fixedValue (0 0.1 0).
The top boundary is an outlet.
The side walls use noSlip boundary conditions.
The front and back patches are empty (2D simulation).
