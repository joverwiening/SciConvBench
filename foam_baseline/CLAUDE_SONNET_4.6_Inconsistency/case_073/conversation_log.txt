Q: The gravity vector in the `constant/g` dictionary is specified as `g (0 9.81 0);`, which points in the **positive Y direction**. However, for a particle to settle **downward** in a vertical cylinder aligned along the Y-axis, gravity should typically point in the **negative Y direction**.

Should gravity point upward (positive Y) or downward (negative Y) for this simulation?
A: Gravity should point in the negative Y direction: `g (0 -9.81 0);`
