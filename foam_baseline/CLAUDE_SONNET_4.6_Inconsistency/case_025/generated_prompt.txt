Simulate the 3D, incompressible, turbulent flow around a cube mounted on the floor of a channel using simpleFoam with a RANS kEpsilon turbulence model.
Use a fully 3D mesh with more than one cell in the spanwise direction.
Treat the two spanwise side patches (left and right) as symmetry boundaries using type symmetryPlane in both blockMeshDict and the boundary condition files.
Define the left and right patches in system/blockMeshDict with type symmetryPlane (not type empty).
Apply symmetryPlane boundary conditions to left and right patches in all field files (U, p, k, epsilon, nut).
Use noSlip boundary condition on all solid walls: the channel floor, the cube faces, and the top wall.
Apply a fixed inlet velocity boundary condition on the inlet patch.
Apply a fixed outlet pressure boundary condition on the outlet patch.
Use the kEpsilon RANS turbulence model with appropriate model coefficients.
The flow is incompressible; use simpleFoam as the steady-state solver.
Provide all required OpenFOAM case files: system/blockMeshDict, system/controlDict, system/fvSchemes, system/fvSolution, constant/transportProperties, constant/turbulenceProperties, and initial/boundary condition files for U, p, k, epsilon, and nut.
