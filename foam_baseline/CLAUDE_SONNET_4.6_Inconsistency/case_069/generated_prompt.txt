Simulate the flow in a cyclone separator using simpleFoam.
The geometry has three flow boundaries: a tangential inlet, a top outlet (vortex finder), and a bottom dustOutlet, plus a walls boundary.
Use the k-epsilon turbulence model.
The fluid enters tangentially at the inlet and is expected to exit primarily through the top outlet.
The pressure at the top outlet is atmospheric (0 gauge).

Define the boundary conditions for the pressure field 'p' as follows:
inlet: type zeroGradient
outlet: type fixedValue, value uniform 0
dustOutlet: type zeroGradient
walls: type zeroGradient
