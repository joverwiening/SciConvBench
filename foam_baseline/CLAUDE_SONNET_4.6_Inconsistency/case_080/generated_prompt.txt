Set up a simulation of a three-phase fluidized bed in a cylindrical column.
The solver to be used is twoPhaseEulerFoam.
The column is cylindrical with a diameter of 0.1 m and a height of 1 m.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction (0 -9.81 0).
The phases are defined in phaseProperties as (air water sand).
Air is treated as a perfect gas.
Water is treated as an incompressible liquid.
Sand is treated as a constant density solid with a particle diameter of 500 microns.
The simulation is transient and runs for 10 seconds.
Initial conditions consist of a bed of sand settled at the bottom 0.2 m of the column with alpha.sand = 0.6 and alpha.water = 0.4 in that region.
The rest of the column above 0.2 m is filled with water with alpha.water = 1.
Air is injected from the bottom inlet at a velocity of 0.5 m/s.
Use the Gidaspow drag model for interactions between the phases.
