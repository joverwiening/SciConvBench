Simulate the steady-state, compressible flow of natural gas through a pipe with a porous baffle. The inlet total pressure is 2e5 Pa and the outlet static pressure is 1e5 Pa. The gas is modeled as a perfect gas. The porous zone is defined in the fvOptions dictionary with Darcy-Forchheimer coefficients. The solver to be used is rhoPorousSimpleFoam. In thermophysicalProperties, define the gas properties using the 'perfectGas' equation of state and an appropriate transport model like 'sutherland'.
