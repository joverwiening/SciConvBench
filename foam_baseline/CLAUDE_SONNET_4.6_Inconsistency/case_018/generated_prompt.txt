Set up a laminar counter-flow diffusion flame simulation using reactingFoam in OpenFOAM.
The domain is 3-D with x in [0, 0.02] m, y in [-0.01, 0.01] m, z in [-0.01, 0.01] m.
The mesh is 100 cells in x, 40 cells in y, and 1 cell in z.
The frontAndBack patches are set to empty (2-D simulation).
The left patch (x=0) is the fuel inlet supplying pure methane (CH4) at 293 K with velocity +0.1 m/s in the +x direction.
The right patch (x=0.02) is the air inlet supplying air at 293 K with Y_O2 = 0.23 and balance N2, with velocity -0.1 m/s in the -x direction (opposing jet, pointing toward the fuel side).
The top and bottom patches are outlets.
Initialize the temperature field to 2000 K throughout the domain to ignite the flame.
Initialize velocity consistently with the inlet profiles or to zero as a reasonable starting condition.
Use a reduced but reasonably detailed methane combustion mechanism following best practices; use the 4-step or skeletal mechanism by Smooke and Giovangigli (or a comparable compact skeletal methane mechanism with key intermediates such as CO, H2, OH) as a reasonable default for laminar counter-flow diffusion flames.
Chemistry integration uses EulerImplicit with the Rosenbrock43 ODE solver.
Use laminar combustion model (no turbulence).
Thermo model: hePsiThermo with coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture mixture model, Sutherland transport, and JANAF thermodynamic data.
Set deltaT = 1e-4 s, endTime = 0.5 s, and writeInterval = 0.05 s.
Provide all necessary OpenFOAM case files: constant/thermophysicalProperties, constant/chemistryProperties, constant/combustionProperties, constant/transportProperties, system/controlDict, system/fvSchemes, system/fvSolution, system/blockMeshDict, and all 0/ boundary condition files (T, U, p, species mass fractions).
