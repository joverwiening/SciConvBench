Simulate a mixed convection lid-driven cavity problem using buoyantPimpleFoam.
Assume a 2D square cavity of 1 m × 1 m, treated as 2D with a single-cell-thick mesh in the z-direction (0.01 m thick), using a 50×50 base mesh in x and y.
The top lid moves at 0.1 m/s in the x-direction and is held at a hot temperature of 350 K.
The bottom wall is stationary and cold at 300 K.
The left and right walls are stationary and adiabatic (zero gradient for temperature).
The front and back patches are empty (2D simulation).
Gravity acts downwards in the negative y-direction: g = (0, -9.81, 0) m/s².
Use the incompressiblePerfectGas equation of state for density variation with temperature.
In constant/thermophysicalProperties, use the following thermoType block: type hePsiThermo; mixture incompressiblePerfectGas; transport const; thermo hConst; equationOfState perfectGas; specie specie; energy sensibleEnthalpy.
In the mixture block, set specie molWeight to 28.9, thermodynamics Cp to 1005 and Hf to 0, transport mu to 1.8e-5 and Pr to 0.7.
Include a BoussinesqCoeffs sub-dictionary inside the mixture block with TRef 325 and beta 0.00307 as a backup buoyancy model reference.
Set up the initial conditions with uniform velocity (0,0,0), pressure 0, and temperature 325 K throughout the domain.
Use standard wall boundary conditions: no-slip velocity on all walls, zero-gradient pressure on walls, fixed temperature on top (350 K) and bottom (300 K) walls.
Use PIMPLE algorithm settings appropriate for a transient incompressible-like buoyant flow, with nOuterCorrectors 2, nCorrectors 2, and nNonOrthogonalCorrectors 1.
Run the simulation to a reasonable steady-state end time of 100 s with a time step of 0.01 s and write interval of 10 s.
Use turbulence model set to laminar (no turbulence), appropriate for a benchmark mixed convection cavity at low Reynolds number.
Generate all necessary OpenFOAM case files: 0/ directory (U, p_rgh, T, p), constant/ directory (thermophysicalProperties, turbulenceProperties, g), and system/ directory (blockMeshDict, controlDict, fvSchemes, fvSolution).
