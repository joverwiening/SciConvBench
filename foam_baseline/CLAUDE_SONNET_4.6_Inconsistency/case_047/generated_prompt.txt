Configure a steady-state turbulent pipe flow simulation using simpleFoam with the k-epsilon RANS model.
The fluid is water with a kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m²/s.
The pipe diameter is 0.1 m and the inlet velocity is 1 m/s.
In the 0/k file, set internalField to uniform 0.00375.
In the 0/epsilon file, set internalField to uniform 0.001.
Use kqRWallFunction as the wall boundary condition for k on the pipe wall.
Use epsilonWallFunction as the wall boundary condition for epsilon on the pipe wall.
