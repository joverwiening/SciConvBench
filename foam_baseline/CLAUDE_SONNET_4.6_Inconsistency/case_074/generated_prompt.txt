Model a steady-state, buoyancy-driven natural convection flow in a vertical heated pipe using the buoyantSimpleFoam solver.
The pipe has a length of 2m aligned with the Z-axis and a diameter of 0.1m.
Air enters at the bottom (z=0) at 300K and is heated by the pipe wall maintained at a constant temperature of 350K.
The goal is to observe the natural convection flow established due to density differences.
In the constant/g dictionary, set gravity to `g (0 0 -9.81);`.
Use air thermophysical properties with the `perfectGas` equation of state.
The inlet boundary condition for U is `pressureInletOutletVelocity` and for T is `fixedValue 300`.
The outlet boundary condition for U is `zeroGradient` and for T is `zeroGradient`, with a fixed pressure at the outlet.
The wall boundary condition for U is `noSlip` and for T is `fixedValue 350`.
