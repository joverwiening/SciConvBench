Simulate thermo-elastic deformation of a solid aluminum block that is fixed on one face and heated on the opposite face.
The goal is to compute displacement and stress due to thermal expansion.
Use OpenFOAM with the solver `solidDisplacementFoam`.
Set the `application` field in `system/controlDict` to `solidDisplacementFoam`.

Material properties for aluminum:
E = 69e9 Pa
nu = 0.33
rho = 2700 kg/m^3
alpha = 2.3e-5 1/K
T0 = 300 K (reference temperature)

Assume a temperature field T is available (prescribed or solved separately by a prior conduction solve) and is used to drive thermal strain.

Boundary conditions for displacement field (0/D):
fixedFace: fixedValue uniform (0 0 0)
All other faces: zeroGradient

Boundary conditions for temperature field (0/T):
heatedFace: fixedValue uniform 400
fixedFace: zeroGradient
All other faces: zeroGradient
