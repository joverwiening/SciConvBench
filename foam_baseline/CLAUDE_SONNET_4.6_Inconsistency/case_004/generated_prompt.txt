Run a 2D gas–liquid bubble column simulation using multiphaseEulerFoam (Euler–Euler approach).

The rectangular domain spans x in [0, 0.15] m, y in [0, 1.00] m, z in [0, 0.10] m with height along the +y direction and thin in the z direction.
Use a structured hex mesh with 25 × 75 × 1 cells and simpleGrading (1 1 1).
The blockMeshDict defines inlet (bottom face, y=0), outlet (top face, y=1), walls (side faces in x direction), and frontAndBack (z-direction faces) as wall boundary.

The continuous liquid phase is water using heRhoThermo with rPolynomial equation of state.
Water rPolynomial C coefficients are (0.001278 -2.1055e-06 3.9689e-09 4.3772e-13 -2.0225e-16).
Water molecular weight is 18.
Water specific heat capacity Cv is 4195 J/(kg·K).
Water dynamic viscosity is 3.645e-4 Pa·s.
Water Prandtl number is 2.289.

The dispersed gas phase is air using heRhoThermo with perfectGas equation of state.
Air molecular weight is 28.9.
Air specific heat capacity Cp is 1007 J/(kg·K).
Air dynamic viscosity is 1.84e-05 Pa·s.
Air Prandtl number is 0.7.

Gravity is set to (0 -9.81 0) m/s².

Bubble size model for air uses isothermal diameter with d0 = 3e-3 m.
Bubble size model for water uses constant diameter with d = 1e-4 m.

Momentum transport is configured as laminar for both phases using simulationType laminar in the respective momentumTransport files.

Phase properties define phases as (air water) with water as the reference phase.
residualAlpha is 1e-6 for both phases.

Blending uses the linear scheme with minFullyContinuousAlpha 0.7 and minPartlyContinuousAlpha 0.3 for default blending, and minPartlyContinuousAlpha 0.5 for drag blending.

Surface tension between air and water is constant with sigma = 0.07 N/m.

Drag model uses SchillerNaumann for air_dispersedIn_water, water_dispersedIn_air, and air_segregatedWith_water configurations.

Virtual mass uses constantCoefficient with Cvm = 0.5 for both directions (air-in-water and water-in-air).

Heat transfer employs the RanzMarshall model for both directions with residualAlpha 1e-4.

Initial conditions at t=0 set water velocity to uniform (0 0 0), air velocity to uniform (0 0.1 0), water volume fraction to uniform 1, p_rgh to uniform 1e5, air temperature to uniform 300 K, and water temperature to uniform 350 K.

Phase distribution uses default field values where volume fraction of air is 1 and water is 0, except in a box region from (0 0 0) to (0.15 0.701 0.1) where volume fraction of air is 0 and water is 1.

Boundary conditions at the inlet set air velocity to fixedValue (0 0.1 0), water velocity to fixedValue (0 0 0), air volume fraction to fixedValue 0.5, water volume fraction to fixedValue 0.5, air temperature to fixedValue 300 K, and water temperature to fixedValue 350 K.
Inlet p_rgh uses zeroGradient.

Wall boundaries set air velocity to fixedValue (0 0 0), water velocity to fixedValue (0 0 0), air volume fraction to zeroGradient, water volume fraction to zeroGradient, p_rgh to fixedFluxPressure, air temperature to zeroGradient, and water temperature to zeroGradient.
frontAndBack faces are also treated as walls with the same wall boundary conditions.

Outlet boundary sets p_rgh to fixedValue 1e5, velocities to zeroGradient, volume fractions to zeroGradient, and temperatures to zeroGradient.

Time controls set startTime to 0, endTime to 20 s, deltaT to 0.005 s, adjustTimeStep to no, maxCo to 0.5, maxDeltaT to 1, and writeInterval to 1 s.

For fvSolution, use GAMG solver for p_rgh with tolerance 1e-8, relTol 0, smoother GaussSeidel, and nCellsInCoarsestLevel 20.
Use PBiCGStab with DILU preconditioner for all velocity and volume fraction fields with tolerance 1e-8 and relTol 0.
Use PBiCGStab with DILU preconditioner for energy (temperature) fields with tolerance 1e-8 and relTol 0.
Use PIMPLE algorithm with nOuterCorrectors 3, nCorrectors 2, and nNonOrthogonalCorrectors 1.
Set residualControl for p_rgh to 1e-4 and for U and alpha to 1e-5.

For fvSchemes, use Euler for ddtSchemes (first-order implicit time discretization).
Use Gauss linear for gradSchemes default.
Use Gauss linearUpwind grad(U) for divergence of velocity fields.
Use Gauss vanLeer for divergence of volume fraction (alpha) fields.
Use Gauss linear corrected for laplacianSchemes default.
Use linear interpolation for interpolationSchemes.
Use corrected for snGradSchemes default.
Use phi for fluxRequired fields including p_rgh and alpha.
