Perform a transient simulation of a Rayleigh-Bénard convection cell using buoyantPimpleFoam.
The domain is a 2D rectangle.
Assume a domain length of 2 m and height of 1 m as a reasonable aspect ratio for Rayleigh-Bénard convection.
The bottom wall is heated at 305 K and the top wall is cooled at 295 K.
The left and right boundaries are periodic or symmetry planes appropriate for a 2D cell.
The fluid is water.
The Boussinesq approximation is used.
In constant/thermophysicalProperties, set thermoType to hePsiThermo and equationOfState to Boussinesq with appropriate coefficients for water (e.g., reference temperature T0 = 300 K, thermal expansion coefficient beta = 2.1e-4 1/K, reference density rho0 = 998 kg/m³).
In system/fvSchemes, use div(phi,T) Gauss linear for the divergence of temperature.
Use div(rhoPhi,U) Gauss upwind for the divergence of velocity.
Assume a mesh resolution of 80 cells in the x-direction and 40 cells in the y-direction as a reasonable starting resolution.
Assume a simulation end time of 200 s as a reasonable duration to reach a statistically steady convective state.
Use a time step of 0.05 s or enable automatic time-stepping with a maximum Courant number of 0.5.
Set up all required OpenFOAM case files including constant/, system/, and 0/ directories with appropriate initial and boundary conditions for U, p_rgh, T, and any turbulence fields if needed.
Use laminar flow model unless the Rayleigh number suggests otherwise, in which case make a reasonable assumption.
