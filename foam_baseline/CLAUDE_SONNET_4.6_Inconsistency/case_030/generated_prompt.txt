Set up a 2D natural convection simulation in a square cavity using buoyantBoussinesqPimpleFoam.
The left wall is hot with a fixed temperature of 310 K.
The right wall is cold with a fixed temperature of 290 K.
The top wall is adiabatic with a zeroGradient boundary condition for T.
The bottom wall is adiabatic with a zeroGradient boundary condition for T.
Initialize T uniformly at 300 K throughout the domain.
Initialize U at rest (zero velocity) throughout the domain.
In 0/T, set the bottomWall boundary condition to zeroGradient, not fixedValue.
