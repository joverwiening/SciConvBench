The task is to simulate turbulent flow in a channel.
The pimpleFoam solver will be used.
The channel has a length of 2.0 m.
The channel has a width of 0.10 m.
The channel has a height of 0.10 m.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The flow is driven by a constant mean pressure gradient.
The pressure gradient is implemented as a meanVelocityForce to maintain a target bulk velocity of (1 0 0).
The RAS kOmegaSST turbulence model will be used.
The mesh will have 100 cells in the x-direction.
The mesh will have 50 cells in the y-direction.
The mesh will have 50 cells in the z-direction.
