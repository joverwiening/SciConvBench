The simulation will model a premixed methane-air flame.
The simulation will be conducted in a 2D channel.
The reactingFoam solver will be used for the simulation.
The 2D channel has a length of 0.1 m.
The 2D channel has a width of 0.02 m.
The inlet mixture consists of methane and air.
The methane mass fraction at the inlet is 0.055.
The channel walls are adiabatic.
The flow is laminar.
