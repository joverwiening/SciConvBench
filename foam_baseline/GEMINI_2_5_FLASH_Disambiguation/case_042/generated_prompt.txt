Model Rayleigh-Benard convection.
The domain is a rectangle with dimensions 2m wide by 1m high.
The model should be two-dimensional.
The fluid properties should be selected to achieve a Rayleigh number of approximately 2500.
The fluid temperature ranges from 299 K to 300 K, with a reference temperature of 299.5 K.
The bottom boundary is heated and the top boundary is cooled.
The thermal boundary conditions for the leftWall and rightWall are periodic (cyclic).
The velocity boundary condition for the bottomHot wall is noSlip.
The velocity boundary condition for the topCold wall is noSlip.
The velocity boundary conditions for the leftWall and rightWall are cyclic.
The velocity boundary condition for the frontAndBack is empty.
