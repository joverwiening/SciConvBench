The domain is a cube spanning from (0,0,0) to (1,1,1).
There is an obstacle in the domain from (0.375, 0.375, 0) to (0.625, 0.625, 0.25).
Gravity is set to g = (0, 0, -9.81).
The top (z=1) face is assigned as the open atmosphere patch.
The remaining five faces are assigned as walls.
Phases are modeled via VOF.
Water has a density of 1000 kg/m^3 and a kinematic viscosity of 1.0e-6 m^2/s.
Air has a density of 1 kg/m^3 and a kinematic viscosity of 1.48e-5 m^2/s.
The surface tension is 0.07 N/m.
The water column is initialized as a rectangular prism where alpha.water = 1 inside the box (0, 0, 0) to (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75) and 0 elsewhere.
