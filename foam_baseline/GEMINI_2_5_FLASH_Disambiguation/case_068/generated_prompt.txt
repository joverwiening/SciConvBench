The simulation will model steady-state compressible flow of air through a heated porous block.
The porous block is 0.04 m long, extending from x=0.08 m to x=0.12 m, and 0.05 m high.
The inlet has a fixed total pressure of 101325 Pa.
The inlet has a fixed total temperature of 300 K.
The porous block is heated by a 'scalarSemiImplicitSource' for the energy equation 'h'.
The heating uses a volume mode 'absolute' with an injection rate S_h = 1e6 W/m^3 within the porous block cellZone.
The permeability of the porous block is defined by the Darcy-Forchheimer coefficients `d=(1e9 1e9 1e11)` in the `fvOptions` file.
