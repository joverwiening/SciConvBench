The task is to simulate a 2D buoyant cavity.
The simulation will use the buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam solver.
A steady-state solution is required.
The cavity is 1 meter wide and 0.25 meters high.
The fluid is air-like.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 1e-6 m^2/s.
The Prandtl number of the fluid is 7.0.
The gravitational acceleration is (0 -9.81 0).
The reference temperature for the Boussinesq approximation is 300 K.
A wall identified as `bottomHot` has a fixed temperature of 310 K.
A wall identified as `topCold` has a fixed temperature of 290 K.
There is an unresolved contradiction regarding the wall boundary conditions.
Initially, the hot wall was specified to be on the left and the cold wall on the right, with the top and bottom walls insulated.
Later, the hot wall was specified to be at the bottom and the cold wall at the top.
The final configuration of the insulated walls is also unclear due to this contradiction.
