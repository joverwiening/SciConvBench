The task is to set up a multi-region case.
The case involves a solid block with a porous channel inside.
Fluid flows through the porous channel.

The porous channel has dimensions of 0.1 meters by 0.1 meters.
The porous channel runs through the entire 0.5 meter length of the block.
The solid block is made of aluminum.
Water is the fluid flowing through the porous channel.
