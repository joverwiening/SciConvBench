Simulate steady-state, turbulent natural convection in a 2D square cavity at Ra ~ 1e6 using the Boussinesq approximation and a k-epsilon RAS model.

Solver: buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam

Geometry & mesh:
- Square cavity: 0.10 m x 0.10 m x 0.01 m (2D via empty front/back).
- blockMesh: 100 x 100 x 1 (uniform or lightly graded to walls).
Patches: hotWall (left), coldWall (right), topWall, bottomWall, frontAndBack.

Target Rayleigh number:
Ra = g*beta*DeltaT*H^3/(nu*alpha). With H=0.10 m, DeltaT=10 K, beta ~ 3.33e-3 1/K, nu=1.5e-5, Pr=0.71 (alpha=nu/Pr),
Ra ~ 1.0e6.

Physical properties (constant/physicalProperties):
- nu = 1.5e-5 m^2/s
- Pr = 0.71
- beta = 3.33e-3 1/K
- T0 = 298 K
- rho0 = 1.0 kg/m^3
Gravity: g (0 -9.81 0)

Turbulence (constant/momentumTransport):
- simulationType RAS
- RASModel kEpsilon
- turbulence on; printCoeffs on
- (optional) turbulentPrandtl Prt = 0.85

Initial conditions:
U=(0 0 0); T=298 K; p_rgh=0; k and epsilon small (e.g., k=1e-4, epsilon=1e-4).

Boundary conditions:
- hotWall: T fixedValue 303 K; U noSlip; p_rgh fixedFluxPressure; k kqRWallFunction; epsilon epsilonWallFunction; nut nutkWallFunction.
- coldWall: T fixedValue 293 K; U noSlip; p_rgh fixedFluxPressure; wall functions for turbulence.
- topWall & bottomWall: T zeroGradient; U noSlip; p_rgh fixedFluxPressure; wall functions.
- frontAndBack: empty.

Run control: endTime 5000; deltaT 1; writeInterval 500 (steady solver pseudo-time iterations).
