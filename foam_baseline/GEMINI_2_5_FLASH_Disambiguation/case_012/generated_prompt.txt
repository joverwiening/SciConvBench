The task is to perform an incompressible lid driven cavity flow simulation.
The cavity is a square with dimensions normalized to 1 unit on the x and y axes.
The cavity is 0.1 units thin in the z-direction.
The grid resolution is 20x20 cells in the x and y directions and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The simulation runs from time 0 to 10.
The time step is 0.005 units.
Results are output every 100 time steps.
The k-epsilon turbulence model is used.
The turbulence field k is initialized uniformly to 1.5e-4 with kqRWallFunction on all walls.
The turbulence field epsilon is initialized uniformly to 1.4e-4 with epsilonWallFunction on all walls.
The turbulence field nut is initialized uniformly to 0 with nutkWallFunction on all walls.
The front and back walls have empty boundary conditions for turbulence fields.
The pisoFoam solver is used.
The domain is initialized with zero velocity.
The convection scheme for div(phi,U) is Gauss limitedLinearV 1.
The convection scheme for div(phi,k) is Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The convection scheme for div(phi,epsilon) is Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The viscous term scheme is div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear.
The top wall, named 'movingWall', moves in the x-direction with a uniform velocity of 1 m/s.
The bottom wall has a no-slip boundary condition with zero velocity.
The side walls have a no-slip boundary condition with zero velocity.
The kinematic viscosity nu is constant with a value of 1e-05 m^2/s.
