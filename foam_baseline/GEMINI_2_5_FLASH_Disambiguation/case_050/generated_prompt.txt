A 1m x 1m square domain is used.
The domain has a 40x40 mesh.
The simulation uses the pimpleFoam solver.
It is a transient simulation.
The kinematic viscosity is 1e-4 m^2/s.
The simulation runs until t=5s.
The initial conditions for U are uniform (0 0 0).
The initial conditions for p are uniform 0.
The bottom wall has a noSlip boundary condition for U.
The bottom wall has a fixedFluxPressure boundary condition for p with a value of uniform 0.
The left wall has a noSlip boundary condition for U.
The left wall has a fixedFluxPressure boundary condition for p with a value of uniform 0.
The right wall has a noSlip boundary condition for U.
The right wall has a fixedFluxPressure boundary condition for p with a value of uniform 0.
The top wall has a fixedValue boundary condition for U.
The value for the top wall for U is uniform (1 0 0).
The top wall moves at 1 m/s.
