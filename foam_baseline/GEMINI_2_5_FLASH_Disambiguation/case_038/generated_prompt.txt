The task is to simulate heat transfer in a porous block.
Natural convection should be included in the simulation.
The fluid for the simulation is air.
The dimensions of the porous block are 0.10 m by 0.10 m by 0.01 m.
The hot bottom surface, named hotWall, has a fixed temperature of 310 K.
The cold top surface, named coldWall, has a fixed temperature of 290 K.
The numerical value for the porosity of the porous block is required.
