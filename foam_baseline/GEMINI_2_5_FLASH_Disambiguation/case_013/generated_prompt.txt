The domain is a rectangular domain.

The y-dimension spans from -0.01 to 0.01 m.
The z-dimension spans from -0.01 to 0.01 m.

At the air inlet, oxidizer as air (O2/N2) is supplied.
The temperature at the air inlet is 293 K.
The mass fraction of O2 (Y_O2) at the air inlet is 0.23.

The velocity at the air boundary is -0.1 m/s.
