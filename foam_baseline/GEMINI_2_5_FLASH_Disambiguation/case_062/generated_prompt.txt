The task is to perform a simulation of flow over a cylinder to observe vortex shedding.
The fluid is water with a kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m^2/s.
The cylinder has a diameter of 1m.
The free-stream velocity of the water is (3.9 0 0) m/s.
The computational domain is a rectangle from (-10m, -10m) to (30m, 10m) with a thickness of 0.1m in the third dimension.
For the inlet boundary, the conditions are uniform velocity U=(3.9 0 0) m/s, fixedValue k=5.7e-3, and fixedValue omega=3.6.
For the outlet boundary, the conditions are zeroGradient for U, k, omega, and fixedValue p=0.
For the top and bottom walls, the boundary condition is symmetry.
For the cylinder surface, the conditions are noSlip for U, kqRWallFunction for k, and omegaWallFunction for omega.
For the front and back planes, the boundary condition is empty.
