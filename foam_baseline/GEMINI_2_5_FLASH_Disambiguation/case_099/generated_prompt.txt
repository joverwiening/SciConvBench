The task is to model steady buoyant flow through a porous medium.
The buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam solver will be used.
An explicit Darcy-Forchheimer resistance source will be applied using fvOptions.
The porous block has dimensions of 0.2 m width, 0.4 m height, and 0.01 m thickness.
The Darcy coefficient d is (1e8 1e8 1e8).
The Forchheimer coefficient f is (0 0 0).
The left wall of the porous block is heated with a fixedValue temperature of 303 K.
The top wall has a zeroGradient temperature boundary condition.
The bottom wall has a zeroGradient temperature boundary condition.
The right wall has a fixedValue temperature of 293 K.
The front and back faces of the porous block have an empty boundary condition.
The left wall has a noSlip velocity boundary condition.
The right wall has a noSlip velocity boundary condition.
