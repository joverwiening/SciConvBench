The task is to model spray combustion in a chamber.
Liquid dodecane will be injected into hot air to observe combustion.
A k-epsilon turbulence model will be used.
The combustion chamber is 0.2 meters long.
The combustion chamber is 0.1 meters in diameter.
The inlet temperature of the hot air is 800 Kelvin.
The inlet velocity of the hot air is (10 0 0) meters per second.
The liquid dodecane will be injected at the center of the inlet.
The injection velocity of the liquid dodecane needs to be specified by the user.
