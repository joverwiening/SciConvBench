Simulate a 2D incompressible unsteady flow past a circular cylinder using pimpleFoam.
The computational domain is a rectangle extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m and y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m.
The simulation is strictly two-dimensional, with default back/front faces set to empty.
A circular cylinder of diameter 2 m is centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).
The simulation starts at startTime = 0 s and ends at endTime = 0.5 s.
The time step is deltaT = 0.0025 s.
Results are written every 0.05 s.
The initial field specifies a quiescent fluid with U = (0 0 0) and p = 0.
The inlet boundary condition is U = (1 0 0) m/s.
The outlet boundary condition is U zeroGradient and p = 0.
The top wall boundary condition is U noSlip and p zeroGradient.
The bottom wall boundary condition is U noSlip and p zeroGradient.
The circular cylinder surface boundary condition is U noSlip and p zeroGradient.
