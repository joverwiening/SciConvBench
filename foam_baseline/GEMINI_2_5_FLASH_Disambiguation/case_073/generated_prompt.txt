Set up a steady-state pipe flow case.
The pipe is 2m long.
The pipe has a diameter of 0.04m.
Use the k-omega SST turbulence model.
The fluid flowing through the pipe is air.
The inlet velocity of the air is (10 0 0) m/s.
The outlet boundary condition for pressure (p) is fixedValue uniform 0.
The outlet boundary conditions for velocity (U), k, and omega are zeroGradient.
The boundary condition for the pipe walls for U is noSlip.
The boundary condition for the pipe walls for k is kqRWallFunction.
The boundary condition for the pipe walls for omega is omegaWallFunction.
