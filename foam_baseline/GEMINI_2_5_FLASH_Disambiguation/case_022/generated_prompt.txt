Simulate a 2D flow past a circular cylinder.
The domain is a rectangular extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m in the streamwise direction and y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m in the cross-stream direction.
The cylinder has a diameter of 2 m and is centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).
The solver is pimpleFoam for incompressible unsteady flow.
The simulation starts at startTime = 0 s and ends at endTime = 0.5 s.
The time step is deltaT = 0.0025 s.
Results are written every 0.05 s.
The initial field specifies a quiescent fluid with U = (0 0 0) and p = 0.
The flow is laminar.
The laminar model uses generalisednewtonain with the viscosity model being crosspowerlaw.
The simulation is strictly 2D, with default back/front faces set to empty.
The boundary condition for the inlet (left, x = -5 m) is U = (1 0 0) m/s.
The boundary condition for the outlet (right, x = 5 m) is U zeroGradient and p = 0 (reference).
The boundary condition for the top (y = 2.5 m) and bottom (y = -1.5 m) walls is U noSlip and p zeroGradient.
The boundary condition for the cylinder surface is U noSlip and p zeroGradient.
