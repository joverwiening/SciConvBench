Run a transient laminar two-phase dam-break simulation with obstacles.
Set gravity to (0 0 -9.81).
The computational domain is a cube spanning (0,0,0) to (1,1,1).
The top face (z=1) is assigned as an open atmosphere patch.
The remaining five faces of the domain are assigned as walls.
Model phases via VOF.
The first phase is water with a density of 1000 kg/m^3 and a kinematic viscosity of 1.0e-6 m^2/s.
The second phase is air with a density of 1 kg/m^3 and a kinematic viscosity of 1.48e-5 m^2/s.
Surface tension between the phases is 0.07 N/m.
Initialize the water column as a rectangular prism where alpha.water = 1 inside the box from (0, 0, 0) to (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75) and 0 elsewhere.
There is one obstacle in the domain from (0.375 0.375 0) to (0.625 0.625 0.25).
Use a time step (deltaT) of 1e-3 s.
The simulation end time (endTime) is 0.1 s.
The write interval (writeInterval) for output data is 0.02 s.
