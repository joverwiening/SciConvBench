Simulate a turbulent hot plume rising from a small heat source on the floor of a large, open room.
The simulation domain is a 2m wide x 4m high rectangle.
The heat source is a central 0.2m section of the floor.
The heat source has a fixed temperature of 400 K.
The ambient temperature of the room is 300 K.
The boundary conditions for the floorSides are U = noSlip and T = zeroGradient.
The boundary conditions for the sideWalls are U = slip, T = zeroGradient, k = zeroGradient, epsilon = zeroGradient, and p_rgh = zeroGradient.
The boundary conditions for the atmosphere (top boundary) are p_rgh = totalPressure (value uniform 0), U = pressureInletOutletVelocity, T = inletOutlet (with inletValue for T=300K), k = inletOutlet, and epsilon = inletOutlet.
The fluid in the room is air, treated as a perfect gas.
The turbulence model used for the simulation is RANS k-epsilon.
Gravity is (0 -9.81 0).
