Problem: Simulate a 2D turbulent buoyant plume rising from a localized heat source in an otherwise quiescent environment.
Solver: buoyantPimpleFoam
Geometry: A 2m wide x 4m high rectangular domain. 2D simulation via 'empty' front/back patches.
Mesh: A graded mesh created with blockMesh. The mesh is refined near the central bottom region where the heat source is located. For example, 100 cells in x (graded towards center) and 200 cells in y (graded towards bottom).
Turbulence Model: RANS k-epsilon model. In constant/momentumTransport, set 'simulationType RAS;' and 'RASModel kEpsilon;'.
Physical Properties: Air treated as a perfect gas. Use 'thermoType hePsiThermo;' and 'equationOfState perfectGas;' in constant/thermophysicalProperties.
Gravity: 'g (0 -9.81 0);'.
Boundary Conditions:
- floor: Composed of two patches. 'heatSource' (central 0.2m section) with T = fixedValue 400 K. 'floorSides' (the rest of the bottom boundary) with T = zeroGradient. Both have U = noSlip.
- sideWalls: slip condition for U, zeroGradient for T, k, epsilon, p_rgh.
- atmosphere (top boundary): This is an open boundary. Use 'totalPressure' for p_rgh (value uniform 0), 'pressureInletOutletVelocity' for U, and 'inletOutlet' for T, k, epsilon (with inletValue for T=300K).
- Turbulence BCs on walls: kqRWallFunction for k, epsilonWallFunction for epsilon, nutkWallFunction for nut.
Initial Conditions: U=(0 0 0), T=300K, p_rgh=0. k=1e-5, epsilon=1e-5.
Time Controls: Run for 100 seconds. Use adjustable time step with maxCo=0.8, initial deltaT=0.01s. Write results every 5 seconds.
