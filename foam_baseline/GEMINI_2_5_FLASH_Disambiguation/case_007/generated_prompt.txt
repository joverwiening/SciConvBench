The task is to simulate an incompressible lid driven cavity flow.
The grid size is 20x20 in the x and y directions and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The cavity dimensions are 1 unit in x, 1 unit in y, and 0.1 units in z.
The top wall, named 'movingWall', moves in the x-direction with a uniform velocity of 1 m/s.
The 'fixedWalls' have a no-slip boundary condition, meaning velocity is zero at these walls.
The front and back faces are designated as 'empty'.
The simulation runs from time 0 to 10.
The time step for the simulation is 0.005 units.
Results are output every 100 time steps.
The viscosity, nu, is constant with a value of 1e-05 m^2/s.
The k-epsilon turbulence model is to be used.
The turbulence field k is initialized uniformly to 1.5e-4 with kqRWallFunction on all walls, excluding front/back empty patches.
The turbulence field epsilon is initialized uniformly to 1.4e-4 with epsilonWallFunction on all walls, excluding front/back empty patches.
The turbulence field nut is initialized uniformly to 0 with nutkWallFunction on all walls, excluding front/back empty patches.
The pisoFoam solver is to be used.
The domain is initialized with zero velocity at the beginning of the simulation.
The convection scheme for div(phi,U) is Gauss limitedLinearV 1.
The convection scheme for div(phi,k) is Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The convection scheme for div(phi,epsilon) is Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The viscous term scheme is div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear.
