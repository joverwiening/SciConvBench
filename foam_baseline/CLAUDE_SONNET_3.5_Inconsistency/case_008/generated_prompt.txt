Conduct a compressible flow simulation through an angled porous duct using porousSimpleFoam solver with SIMPLE algorithm.
The domain consists of a 45-degree angled rectangular duct with total length of 350 units (150 inlet + 100 porous section + 100 outlet) and width of 50 units, scaled by convertToMeters factor of 0.001 making the actual dimensions 350mm x 50mm.
The mesh uses 15 cells in the inlet section, 20 cells in the porous section, 20 cells in the outlet section, and 20 cells in both y and z directions for a total grid of 55x20x20 cells.
Use air as the working fluid at standard atmospheric conditions (298K, 1 atm).
Set inlet velocity to 10 m/s normal to the inlet boundary.
Set porosity value to 0.4 with Darcy coefficient of 1e7 and Forchheimer coefficient of 100.
Boundary conditions include patch type at the inlet and outlet, wall type for porosityWall surfaces with no-slip conditions, and default wall conditions for remaining surfaces.
The simulation runs as a steady-state case from time 0 to 1000 with timestep of 1 and output every 100 timesteps.
Use GAMG solver for pressure with tolerance 1e-08 and relTol 0.05.
Use smoothSolver with GaussSeidel smoother for velocity and turbulence quantities with tolerance 1e-06 for velocity and 1e-07 for k-epsilon turbulence model.
Apply relaxation factors of 0.3 for pressure, 0.7 for velocity, and 0.9 for turbulence quantities.
Use bounded Gauss upwind schemes for convective terms and Gauss linear corrected for diffusive terms.
Use the provided blockMeshDict for the mesh generation.
