Simulate 2D laminar, transient flow past a square cylinder in a channel using `icoFoam`. The domain is 2.2m long, 0.41m high, and one cell (0.01m) thick. A 0.1m x 0.1m square is placed in the flow path. The kinematic viscosity `nu` is 0.001 m^2/s.

Boundary conditions in `0/U`:
- `inlet`: Parabolic inflow profile, with maxValue (1.5 0 0);`
- `outlet`: `zeroGradient`
- `topAndBottomWalls`: `noSlip`
- `cylinder`: `noSlip`
- `frontAndBack`: `empty`

Boundary conditions in `0/p`:
- `inlet`: `zeroGradient`
- `outlet`: `fixedValue` uniform 0
- `topAndBottomWalls`: `zeroGradient`
- `cylinder`: `zeroGradient`
- `frontAndBack`: `empty`

In `constant/polyMesh/boundary`, the `frontAndBack` patches are defined with `type empty`.
