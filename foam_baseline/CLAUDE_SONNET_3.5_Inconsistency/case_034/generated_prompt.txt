Set up a 2D dam break simulation using the interFoam solver for a two-phase (water-air) system.
The domain is 2m long and 1m high, with a single cell in the z-direction.
The initial water column dimensions are 0.5m wide and 0.5m high, positioned at the left side of the domain.
Use a mesh resolution of 100 cells in x-direction and 50 cells in y-direction.
Gravity is defined in 'constant/g' as (0 -9.81 0).
In 'blockMeshDict', the front and back patches are defined as 'frontAndBack' with 'type wall'.
For left, right, and top walls, apply 'zeroGradient' for pressure and 'noSlip' for velocity.
For the bottom wall, apply 'zeroGradient' for pressure and 'noSlip' for velocity.
In the '0' directory, for all fields (U, p_rgh, alpha.water), the 'frontAndBack' patches are assigned the 'noSlip' boundary condition for velocity and 'zeroGradient' for scalars.
The initial water column is defined using setFieldsDict.
All walls use 'zeroGradient' for alpha.water.
