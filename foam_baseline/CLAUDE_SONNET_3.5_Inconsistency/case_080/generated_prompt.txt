Set up a three-phase fluidized bed simulation in a cylindrical column.
Use twoPhaseEulerFoam as the solver.
The column has a diameter of 0.1 m and height of 1 m.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction (0 -9.81 0).
Define phases in phaseProperties as (air water sand).
Air is treated as a perfect gas at standard conditions (298 K, 1 atm).
Water is treated as an incompressible liquid.
Sand particles have a diameter of 500 microns and density of 2650 kg/m³ (standard quartz sand).
Run the simulation for 10 seconds with an adaptive time step starting at 0.001 seconds.
Initial conditions have a settled bed at bottom 0.2 m (alpha.sand = 0.6, alpha.water = 0.4).
The rest of the column is filled with water (alpha.water = 1).
Air is injected from bottom inlet at 0.5 m/s.
Use Gidaspow drag model for phase interactions.
Operating temperature is set to 298 K.
Operating pressure is set to 101325 Pa.
