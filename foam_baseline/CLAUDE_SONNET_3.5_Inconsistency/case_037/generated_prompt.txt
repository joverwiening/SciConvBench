Simulate steady, incompressible, laminar flow of water through a 2D channel containing a rigid, stationary obstacle.
The channel dimensions are 0.2m length x 0.02m height.
The obstacle is a circular cylinder with diameter 0.01m, centered at (0.1m, 0.01m).
The mesh consists of approximately 20,000 hexahedral cells with refinement around the cylinder.
Use a 2D mesh extruded one cell in the spanwise direction and set frontAndBack to empty.
Water kinematic viscosity is nu = 1e-6 m^2/s.
Inlet velocity boundary condition is fixedValue uniform (0.1 0 0).
Outlet velocity boundary condition is zeroGradient.
Walls and obstacle have noSlip velocity boundary condition.
FrontAndBack patches have empty velocity boundary condition.
Inlet pressure boundary condition is zeroGradient.
Outlet pressure boundary condition is fixedValue uniform 0.
Walls and obstacle have zeroGradient pressure boundary condition.
FrontAndBack patches have empty pressure boundary condition.
Use simpleFoam as the solver application in system/controlDict.
