Simulate a single spherical particle settling in a quiescent fluid column using the DPMFoam solver.
The domain is a vertical cylinder with a height of 1m (along the Y-axis) and a diameter of 0.2m.
The fluid is water with kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m²/s at room temperature.
A single particle with a diameter of 1mm and density of 2500 kg/m^3 is initialized at position (0, 0.9, 0).
The simulation should run for 10 seconds to ensure complete settling.
The fluid velocity is initialized to zero everywhere.
The walls of the cylinder have noSlip boundary condition.
The top and bottom patches are simple walls.
Gravity is specified as g (0 9.81 0) in constant/g dictionary.
