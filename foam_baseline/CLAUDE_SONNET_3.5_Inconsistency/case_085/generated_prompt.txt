Set up a 2D steady-state simulation of Rayleigh-Bénard convection in a square cavity of 1m x 1m.
The fluid is air.
The bottom wall is heated to 310 K.
The top wall is cooled to 290 K.
The side walls are adiabatic.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction with g = 9.81 m/s^2.
Use the buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam solver.
Use a 100x100 uniform mesh resolution.
Set initial temperature field to 300 K (mean of top and bottom temperatures).
Use convergence criteria of 1e-6 for pressure, velocity, and temperature residuals.
Use thermophysicalProperties with hePsiThermo model, perfectGas mixture, const transport, hConst thermo, perfectGas equation of state, specie, and sensibleEnthalpy energy.
Set molWeight to 28.9 in the specie properties.
Set Cp to 1005 and Hf to 0 in the thermodynamics properties.
Set mu to 1.8e-05 and Pr to 0.7 in the transport properties.
Apply temperature boundary conditions: fixedValue 310 K at bottom, fixedValue 290 K at top, zeroGradient at sides.
Apply noSlip velocity boundary conditions at all walls.
Apply fixedFluxPressure boundary conditions for p_rgh at all boundaries.
