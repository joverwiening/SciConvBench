The simulation is a mixed convection lid-driven cavity problem with the following specifications:
The cavity dimensions are 1m x 1m (standard benchmark size).
The mesh consists of 100x100 uniform cells.
The top lid moves at 0.1 m/s in the x-direction.
The top wall temperature is fixed at 350 K.
The bottom wall temperature is fixed at 300 K.
The side walls are no-slip walls with zero velocity.
The side walls are adiabatic (zero gradient temperature).
Initial conditions are zero velocity and linear temperature gradient between top and bottom walls.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction at -9.81 m/s².
The incompressiblePerfectGas model is used for density variations.
The Boussinesq coefficient beta is set to 0.00307 with TRef of 325 K.
Transport properties include kinematic viscosity of 1.8e-5 m²/s and Prandtl number of 0.7.
The fluid has a specific heat capacity (Cp) of 1005 J/kg·K.
The molecular weight is set to 28.9 kg/kmol.
