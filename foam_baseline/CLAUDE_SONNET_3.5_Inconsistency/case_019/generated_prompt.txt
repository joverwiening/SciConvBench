Set up a laminar counter-flow diffusion flame with reactingFoam in a 2-D opposed-jet configuration.
The domain is a rectangular region spanning x = 0 to 0.02 m, y = -0.01 to 0.01 m, and z = -0.01 to 0.01 m.
Use 100 cells in x, 40 cells in y, and 1 cell in z.
The left boundary is named fuel (x = 0) and the right boundary is named air (x = 0.02).
The top and bottom boundaries are called outlet.
The frontAndBack faces are empty.
At the fuel inlet, inject methane (CH4) at a temperature of 293 K.
At the air inlet, supply oxidizer as air with Y_O2 = 0.23 and the balance N2 at 293 K.
Initialize the domain temperature at 2000 K at time 0.
Set fuel boundary velocity to 0.1 m/s.
Set air boundary velocity to -0.1 m/s.
Use chemistry type = EulerImplicit and ODE solver = Rosenbrock43.
Use laminar combustion model and momentum transport.
Use incompressible finite-rate chemistry with heRhoThermo.
Use mixture = coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture.
Use transport = Sutherland.
Use thermodynamics = JANAF.
Run with the reactingFoam solver.
Use a timestep of 1e-4 s.
Set end time to 0.5 s.
Write results every 0.05 s.
Assume standard atmospheric pressure (101325 Pa) for inlet boundaries and initial domain pressure.
Use GRI-Mech 3.0 mechanism for methane-air chemistry.
