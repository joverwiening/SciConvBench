Set up a steady-state turbulent pipe flow simulation in OpenFOAM.
Use simpleFoam solver with k-epsilon RANS turbulence model.
The fluid is water with kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m²/s.
The pipe diameter is 0.1 m.
The pipe length is 50 times the diameter (5 m) following best practices.
The inlet velocity is 1 m/s using fixedValue boundary condition.
Use zeroGradient for pressure at the outlet.
Initialize turbulent kinetic energy (k) to uniform 0 in 0/k file.
Initialize turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) to uniform 0 in 0/epsilon file.
Apply kqRWallFunction for k on the pipe wall.
Apply epsilonWallFunction for epsilon on the pipe wall.
