Model a flexible flap in a channel where the fluid loading deforms the flap (fluid-structure interaction).
The channel is 2D, extruded one cell in z with frontAndBack empty boundary condition.
The channel length is 1.0 m and height is 0.2 m.
The flap length is 0.05 m with thickness 0.005 m.
The flap is attached to the bottom wall at x = 0.4 m.
The fluid is incompressible water with kinematic viscosity nu = 1e-6 m^2/s.
The inlet boundary condition is fixed velocity U = (1 0 0).
The outlet boundary condition is fixed pressure p = 0.
Channel walls have noSlip boundary condition.
The solid flap is linear elastic with Young's modulus E = 1e6 Pa.
The solid flap has Poisson ratio nu = 0.45.
The solid flap has density rho = 1200 kg/m^3.
The flap base has fixed displacement boundary condition.
The flap surface is a coupled interface boundary.
Use pimpleFoam as the fluid solver.
Use pimpleFoam as the solid solver on a combined mesh.
No explicit interface coupling is needed (single-region setup).
The mesh resolution and cell count should follow best practices.
The simulation duration should follow best practices.
