Simulate a 2D thermal plume with the following specifications:
The domain dimensions are 1m wide by 2m high.
The simulation runs for 10 seconds of physical time.
The mesh consists of 100 cells in x-direction and 200 cells in y-direction.
The time step size (deltaT) is set to 0.001 seconds.
The hot patch at the bottom center is 0.1m wide.
The hot patch temperature is fixed at 500 K.
The initial temperature field is uniform 300 K.
Gravity is set to (0 -9.81 0).
The fluid properties use hePsiThermo with Boussinesq approximation.
The Boussinesq coefficients are T0=300, beta=0.00333, rho0=1.176.
The Sutherland viscosity model uses As=1.4792e-06 and Ts=116.
All boundaries except the hot patch are open to atmosphere.
The solver used is buoyantPimpleFoam.
