Simulate a 2D tank mixing simulation with water and non-Newtonian sludge using interFoam solver.
The tank dimensions are 1m width x 2m height (standard laboratory-scale dimensions).
The tank is initially filled with 30% sludge at the bottom and 70% water at the top.
The simulation duration is set to 10 seconds to observe initial mixing behavior.
The sludge is modeled as a Bingham plastic with yield stress (tau0) of 15 Pa.
The sludge has a plastic viscosity (mu0) of 0.1 Pa·s.
The water phase uses Newtonian properties with kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m²/s and density of 1000 kg/m³.
The sludge phase has a density of 1500 kg/m³.
The surface tension coefficient (sigma) between phases is 0.07 N/m.
The transportModel for sludge should be changed from Newtonian to Bingham in the transportProperties file.
