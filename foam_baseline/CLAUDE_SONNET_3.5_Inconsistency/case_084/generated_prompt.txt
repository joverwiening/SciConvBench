Set up a transient two-phase Euler-Euler gas-solid riser simulation using twoPhaseEulerFoam.
The simulation uses air and catalyst as the two phases.
The riser geometry is cylindrical with a height of 15 meters and diameter of 0.2 meters.
The inlet boundary condition for catalyst volume fraction (alpha.catalyst) is set to 0.01.
The inlet boundary condition for air volume fraction (alpha.air) is set to 0.99 to ensure phase fractions sum to 1.
The catalyst particles have a diameter of 100 microns.
The catalyst particle density is 1500 kg/m³.
The inlet air velocity is set to 5 m/s, typical for industrial riser operations.
The simulation is configured for dilute gas-solid flow conditions.
