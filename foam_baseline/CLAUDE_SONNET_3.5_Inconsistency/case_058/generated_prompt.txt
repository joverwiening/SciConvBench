Model the injection of high-pressure air into a water tank using interFoam solver.
The simulation is transient with a duration of 1 second.
The water tank dimensions are 1m x 1m x 2m.
The inlet conditions for air are 10 bar pressure and 300K temperature.
The air enters at Mach 0.8.
The air phase uses the perfectGas equation of state.
The water phase is modeled as incompressible.
The simulation will track gas jet penetration and expansion in the water.
The solver will account for compressibility effects of the gas phase.
Use two-phase flow physics with interFoam solver.
