Analyze the flow of a viscous, shear-thickening Power Law fluid over a backward-facing step. Use the `nonNewtonianIcoFoam` solver. The geometry consists of an inlet, an outlet, a top wall, and a bottom wall that includes the step. The fluid properties in `transportProperties` are defined with `transportModel PowerLaw;` and `n > 1`. In the `0/U` boundary condition file, the walls must have a no-slip condition to account for viscous effects:

    walls
    {
        type            noSlip;
    }
