Set up a 2D, steady-state, incompressible, turbulent simulation of airflow over a NACA0012 airfoil at a 5-degree angle of attack. Use the simpleFoam solver with the k-omega SST turbulence model. The domain should be a C-grid type, one cell thick in the z-direction. The mesh is defined in `constant/polyMesh`. The boundary patches are `inlet`, `outlet`, `airfoil`, and `frontAndBack`. 

Boundary Conditions:
- `inlet`: Velocity `U` is `fixedValue` uniform (10 0 0), `k` and `omega` are calculated from turbulence intensity 1% and viscosity ratio 10. Pressure `p` is `zeroGradient`.
- `outlet`: Velocity `U` is `inletOutlet`, `k` and `omega` are `inletOutlet`. Pressure `p` is `fixedValue` uniform 0.
- `airfoil`: Velocity `U` is `noSlip`, `k` is `kqRWallFunction`, `omega` is `omegaWallFunction`. Pressure `p` is `zeroGradient`.
- `frontAndBack`: To enforce a 2D simulation, these patches must have no influence on the flow. Set their type to `empty` in `constant/polyMesh/boundary`. All fields (`U`, `p`, `k`, `omega`, etc.) must also have their boundary condition type set to `empty` for these patches.
