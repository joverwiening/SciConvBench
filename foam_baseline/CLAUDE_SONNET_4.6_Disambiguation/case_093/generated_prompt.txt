Simulate a hot plume rising from a small heat source on the floor of a large open room using OpenFOAM.
Use the buoyantPimpleFoam solver.
The simulation is 2D: a 2m wide by 4m high domain with a nominal depth of 1 cell (front and back are empty patches).
Use the RANS k-epsilon turbulence model.
The heat source is a 0.2m wide patch centered at the middle of the floor.
Apply a fixed temperature of 400 K at the heat source patch.
The ambient/initial air temperature throughout the domain is 300 K.
Initial conditions throughout the domain: k = 1e-5, epsilon = 1e-5.
Side wall boundary conditions: slip for U, zeroGradient for T, k, epsilon, and p_rgh.
Top boundary (open atmosphere): totalPressure for p_rgh (value 0), pressureInletOutletVelocity for U, inletOutlet for T (inletValue 300 K), inletOutlet for k and epsilon.
Floor boundary (non-heat-source section): noSlip for U, zeroGradient for T.
Heat source patch: fixed temperature 400 K for T, noSlip for U, kqRWallFunction for k, epsilonWallFunction for epsilon, zeroGradient for p_rgh.
Front and back patches are set as empty (2D simulation).
