Run a turbulent buoyant cavity simulation using OpenFOAM's buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam solver.
Use the k-epsilon turbulence model with standard wall functions.
The Rayleigh number is approximately 1e6.
The fluid is air with kinematic viscosity nu = 1.5e-5 m²/s and Prandtl number Pr = 0.71.
The cavity is a square with dimensions 0.10 m × 0.10 m and a depth of 0.01 m, treated as a 2D case (aspect ratio 1).
The hot wall is on the left at 303 K and the cold wall is on the right at 293 K, giving a temperature difference ΔT = 10 K.
The top and bottom walls use no-slip boundary condition for velocity U.
The top and bottom walls use zeroGradient for temperature T.
The top and bottom walls use fixedFluxPressure for p_rgh.
The top and bottom walls use standard wall functions for k, epsilon, and nut.
The mesh resolution is 100 × 100 × 1 cells, uniform or lightly graded toward the walls.
