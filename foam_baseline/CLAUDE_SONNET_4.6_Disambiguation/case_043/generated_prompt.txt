Simulate natural convection in a closed 2D room using the buoyantPimpleFoam solver in OpenFOAM.
The room is 4m wide and 3m tall.
The case is 2D with a single cell thickness of 0.1m in the depth direction; use empty patches for frontAndBack.
Use 80 cells in the width direction and 60 cells in the height direction.
Apply mesh refinement near the heat source and walls.
The floor is split into two patches: floorHeater (1m wide, centered on the floor) and floorInsulated (the remaining floor area on both sides).
The heat source patch (floorHeater) has a fixed temperature of 323 K.
The initial and ambient air temperature is 293 K.
All non-heater surfaces (floorInsulated, leftWall, rightWall, ceiling) are adiabatic: use zeroGradient for T.
Use noSlip boundary condition for U on all solid walls including floorHeater.
Use fixedFluxPressure for p_rgh on all wall patches.
Use hePsiThermo with perfectGas equation of state and sutherland transport model for air.
Thermophysical properties: MolWeight = 28.9 kg/kmol, Cp = 1005 J/(kg·K), Sutherland coefficients As = 1.479e-06, Ts = 110.4 K.
Assume laminar flow as the best-practice default for low-velocity natural convection in a small enclosed room at these temperature differences (no turbulence model).
Set up all required OpenFOAM case files: constant/ (thermophysicalProperties, g, turbulenceProperties), system/ (blockMeshDict, controlDict, fvSchemes, fvSolution), and 0/ (T, U, p_rgh) directories.
