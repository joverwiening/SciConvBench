Simulate a 2D lid-driven cavity flow using OpenFOAM's icoFoam solver.
The cavity is a square with side length 0.1 m.
Use a 20x20x1 grid (3D mesh with one cell in the z-direction to represent 2D).
The top wall moves with a velocity of 1 m/s in the x-direction.
All other walls are stationary no-slip walls.
The kinematic viscosity nu is 0.01 m²/s.
The Reynolds number is Re = 10.
Use icoFoam, a transient incompressible Navier-Stokes solver with pressure-velocity coupling.
Use a fixed time step deltaT of 0.005 s.
Run the simulation to an endTime of 0.5 s.
