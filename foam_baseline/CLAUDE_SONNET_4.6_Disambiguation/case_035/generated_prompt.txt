Simulate a hot gas bubble rising in a liquid using OpenFOAM with the interPhaseChangeFoam VOF solver.
Use a 2D rectangular domain, 0.1 m wide and 0.4 m high.
Discretize the domain with a uniform blockMesh grid of 50 cells in x, 200 cells in y, and 1 cell in z.
Apply no mesh refinement near the bubble interface.
Initialize a circular gas bubble of radius 0.02 m centered at (0.05, 0.05) in the domain.
Set the initial liquid temperature to 300 K and the initial bubble temperature to 350 K.
Use the following liquid (water) properties: rho = 1000 kg/m³, mu = 1e-3 Pa·s, Cp = 4186 J/kgK, kappa = 0.6 W/mK.
Use the following gas (air) properties: rho = 1.2 kg/m³, mu = 1.8e-5 Pa·s, Cp = 1005 J/kgK, kappa = 0.025 W/mK.
Set surface tension sigma = 0.07 N/m between the two phases.
Apply the following boundary conditions on the top patch: pressureInletOutletVelocity for U, totalPressure for p_rgh, zeroGradient for alpha.gas and T.
Apply the following boundary conditions on the bottom and side patches: noSlip for U, fixedFluxPressure for p_rgh, zeroGradient for alpha.gas and T.
Apply empty boundary conditions on the frontAndBack patches for 2D simulation.
Set endTime to 2.0 s, initial deltaT to 1e-4 s, adjustTimeStep to yes, maxCo to 0.3, maxAlphaCo to 0.3, and writeInterval to 0.05 s.
Assume laminar flow with no turbulence model, consistent with best practices for low-Reynolds-number buoyancy-driven bubble simulations at this scale.
Include gravity to drive buoyancy-induced bubble rise.
