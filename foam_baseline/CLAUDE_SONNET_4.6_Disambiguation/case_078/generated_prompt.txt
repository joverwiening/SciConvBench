Set up an interFoam simulation for sloshing in a rectangular tank.
The tank dimensions are 1.0 m (x) × 0.6 m (y) × 0.1 m (z).
The simulation is 2D, using a mesh of 200 × 120 × 1 cells.
The front and back boundaries are set to the empty type.
The fluid is water, half-filled with an initially tilted free surface.
The water depth varies linearly from 0.25 m at x=0 to 0.35 m at x=1.
Initial velocity is zero everywhere.
There is no tank motion or external excitation; sloshing is purely gravity-driven.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction (standard OpenFOAM g vector: 0 -9.81 0).
The simulation is transient with endTime of 10 s.
Initial deltaT is 1e-3 s.
Use adjustTimeStep yes with maxCo 0.5.
Set up the alpha.water field using a linear variation of the free surface height with x.
Use standard interFoam two-phase settings with water and air as the two phases.
Apply standard boundary conditions: walls (no-slip) on left, right, top, and bottom; empty on front and back.
Write output at regular intervals suitable for post-processing (e.g., writeInterval 0.1 s).
