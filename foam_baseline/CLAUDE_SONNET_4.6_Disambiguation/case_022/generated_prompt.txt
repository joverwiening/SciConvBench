Simulate a 2D flow past a circular cylinder using OpenFOAM.
The domain is rectangular: x from -5 m to 5 m (streamwise), y from -1.5 m to 2.5 m (cross-stream), z from -1 m to 1 m.
A circular cylinder of diameter 2 m is centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).
The solver is pimpleFoam for incompressible unsteady flow.
Set startTime = 0 s, endTime = 0.5 s, deltaT = 0.0025 s.
Write results every 0.05 s.
The initial field specifies a quiescent fluid: U = (0 0 0), p = 0.
The flow is laminar.
Use the laminar generalisedNewtonian viscosity model with the crossPowerLaw model.
Set crossPowerLaw coefficients: nuInf = 10, m = 0.4, n = 3.
Assume reasonable best-practice values for nu0 and tauStar (e.g., nu0 = 1e-3 or a value much larger than nuInf, and tauStar = 1.0 or a similarly standard default).
Apply a uniform inflow velocity U = (1 0 0) m/s at the inlet boundary.
Apply a zeroGradient pressure boundary condition at the inlet (best practice assumption).
Apply standard outlet boundary conditions: zeroGradient for U and fixedValue p = 0 at the outlet.
Apply no-slip boundary condition (U = (0 0 0)) on the cylinder wall, with zeroGradient for pressure.
Apply appropriate slip or symmetry boundary conditions on the top and bottom walls, or use freestream conditions based on best practices.
Apply empty boundary conditions on the front and back patches (z-direction) to enforce 2D behavior.
