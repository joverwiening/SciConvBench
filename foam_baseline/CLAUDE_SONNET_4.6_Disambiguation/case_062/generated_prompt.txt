Simulate transient incompressible flow over a cylinder to observe vortex shedding using OpenFOAM with the pimpleFoam solver.
The fluid is water.
The Reynolds number is Re = 3900.
The inlet velocity is U = (3.9, 0, 0) m/s.
The cylinder diameter is D = 1 m.
The 2D computational domain spans x from -10 m to 30 m and y from -10 m to 10 m (40D long, 20D wide).
The domain depth in z is 0.1 m (0.1D) with a single cell layer (quasi-2D).
Target approximately 40,000 total cells.
Use higher mesh resolution near the cylinder surface and in the wake region downstream of the cylinder.
Target a y+ value between 30 and 100 on the cylinder surface.
Use the k-omega SST RAS turbulence model.
Use the pimpleFoam transient incompressible solver in OpenFOAM.
Set up appropriate boundary conditions: inlet velocity, outlet (zero-gradient pressure or similar), slip or symmetry on top and bottom boundaries, no-slip on the cylinder surface, and empty condition on the front and back z-faces.
The simulation should capture vortex shedding in the wake of the cylinder.
