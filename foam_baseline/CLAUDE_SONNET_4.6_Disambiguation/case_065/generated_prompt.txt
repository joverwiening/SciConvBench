Simulate external aerodynamics on a standard Ahmed body using OpenFOAM's simpleFoam solver (steady-state, incompressible RANS).
Use the kOmegaSST turbulence model with wall functions.
The freestream inlet velocity is U = (11 0 0) m/s in the x-direction.
Use the standard Ahmed body geometry with a 25-degree slant angle and length L = 1.044 m (standard reference dimensions for width and height).
The computational domain dimensions relative to L are: 4L upstream of the body, 10L downstream, 3L to each lateral side (total width = 6L + body width), and 3L above the body.
Include a ground plane (no-slip or slip wall as appropriate) at the bottom of the domain.
Use snappyHexMesh to generate the mesh, targeting approximately 5 million cells.
Apply 10 to 15 prism layers on the Ahmed body surface and on the ground plane, targeting y+ in the range 30–100 (compatible with wall functions).
Refine the mesh in the near-wake region and along the shear layers around the body.
Use air with kinematic viscosity ν = 1.5×10⁻⁵ m²/s and density ρ = 1.225 kg/m³.
Set up boundary conditions: velocity inlet at the upstream face, pressure outlet at the downstream face, symmetry or slip walls on the lateral and top faces, no-slip wall on the Ahmed body surface, and a ground plane at the bottom.
Initialize and run the simpleFoam solver until convergence of residuals and drag coefficient.
Calculate and report the aerodynamic drag force and drag coefficient (Cd) on the Ahmed body using the forceCoeffs or forces function object in OpenFOAM, using the frontal area of the standard Ahmed body as the reference area.
