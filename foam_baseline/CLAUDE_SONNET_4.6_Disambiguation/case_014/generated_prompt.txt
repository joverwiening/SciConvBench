Set up a laminar counter-flow diffusion flame simulation using the reactingFoam solver in OpenFOAM.
The domain is rectangular, spanning x from 0 to 0.02 m, y from -0.01 to 0.01 m, and z from -0.01 to 0.01 m.
Use 100 grid points in the x direction, 40 grid points in the y direction, and 1 cell in the z direction.
The left boundary (x=0) is named "fuel" and the right boundary (x=0.02) is named "air".
The top and bottom boundaries are named "outlet".
The front and back faces are set to "empty" (2D simulation).
Initialize the entire domain with a temperature of 2000 K at time 0.
The fuel boundary has a velocity of 0.1 m/s (in the +x direction), pure CH4 (Y_CH4 = 1.0), and temperature 293 K.
The air boundary has a velocity of -0.1 m/s (in the -x direction), Y_O2 = 0.23 and Y_N2 = 0.77, and temperature 293 K.
Use a reduced or full GRI-Mech 3.0 mechanism (or a well-known skeletal methane mechanism such as the 16-species skeletal mechanism) as the chemical mechanism, as a best-practice assumption.
Use CHEMKIN-format input files (chem.inp, therm.dat, transport.dat) consistent with GRI-Mech 3.0 or the chosen mechanism, as a best-practice assumption.
Use chemistry type EulerImplicit with the Rosenbrock43 ODE solver.
The combustion model is laminar and momentum transport is laminar.
Use compressible finite-rate chemistry with hePsiThermo thermodynamic model.
Use coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture for mixture transport properties.
Use Sutherland transport model.
Use JANAF thermodynamics.
Set the timestep to 1e-4 s, end time to 0.5 s, and write results at an interval of 0.05 s.
Provide all necessary OpenFOAM case files including constant/thermophysicalProperties, constant/chemistryProperties, constant/combustionProperties, constant/turbulenceProperties, system/blockMeshDict, system/controlDict, system/fvSchemes, system/fvSolution, and the 0/ directory with initial and boundary conditions for U, T, p, and all species mass fractions.
