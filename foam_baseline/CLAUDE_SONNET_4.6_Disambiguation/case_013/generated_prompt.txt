Set up a laminar counter-flow diffusion flame simulation using the reactingFoam solver in OpenFOAM.
The domain is 2D rectangular spanning x from 0 to 0.02 m, y from -0.01 to 0.01 m, and z from -0.01 to 0.01 m.
Use 100 grid points in the x direction, 40 grid points in the y direction, and 1 cell in the z direction.
The left boundary (x=0) is named "fuel" and the right boundary (x=0.02) is named "air".
The top and bottom boundaries are named "outlet".
The front and back faces are set to "empty" (2D treatment).
At the fuel inlet, inject pure methane (CH4) with a temperature of 293 K and a velocity of 0.1 m/s in the positive x direction.
At the air inlet, supply oxidizer as air with Y_O2 = 0.23 and Y_N2 = 0.77 at 293 K and a velocity of -0.1 m/s (opposing direction).
Initialize the entire domain temperature to 2000 K at time 0.
Use standard atmospheric pressure of 101325 Pa.
Use a reduced or skeletal methane combustion mechanism based on best practices (e.g., a reduced GRI-Mech or a well-known skeletal mechanism such as the 16-species DRM19 or similar).
Use compressible finite-rate chemistry with hePsiThermo thermodynamic model.
Use coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture for mixture transport properties.
Use Sutherland transport model.
Use JANAF thermodynamics.
Set the chemistry type to EulerImplicit with a Rosenbrock43 ODE solver.
Set the combustion model to laminar.
Set the momentum transport model to laminar.
Generate all necessary OpenFOAM case files including blockMeshDict, boundary conditions (0/ directory), thermophysicalProperties, chemistryProperties, combustionProperties, turbulenceProperties, fvSchemes, fvSolution, and controlDict.
