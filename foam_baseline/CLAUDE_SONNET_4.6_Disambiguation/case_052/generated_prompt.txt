Simulate supersonic flow over a forward-facing step using OpenFOAM with the rhoPimpleFoam solver.
The domain is 0.5m long and 0.1m tall, with a forward-facing step of height 0.02m located at x=0.1m.
The inlet Mach number is 3.
Inlet static pressure is 101325 Pa and inlet static temperature is 293 K.
Use a structured mesh via blockMeshDict targeting approximately 20000 total cells.
Refine the mesh near the step corner and where the shock impinges on the bottom wall.
Use the k-omega SST RAS turbulence model.
The outlet uses waveTransmissive for pressure and zeroGradient for U, T, k, and omega.
The upper wall uses a slip condition for all fields.
The bottom wall and step face use noSlip for U, zeroGradient for p, fixedValue T=293K, and wall functions kqRWallFunction, omegaWallFunction, and nutkWallFunction.
The goal is to visualize the shock structure resulting from the supersonic flow interacting with the step.
