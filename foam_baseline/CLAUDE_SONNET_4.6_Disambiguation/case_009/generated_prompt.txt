The cavity is a square with dimensions 1 unit x 1 unit in x and y, and 0.1 units in z (effectively 2D).
The simulation runs from time 0 to 10 with a time step of 0.005 units.
Results are output every 100 time steps.
Kinematic viscosity nu is constant at 1e-05 m^2/s.
Use the k-epsilon turbulence model.
Use the pisoFoam solver.
The mesh is a standard lid-driven cavity mesh normalized to 1 unit on each side.
Initialize the entire domain velocity (U) to zero.
The top wall (movingWall) has a fixed uniform velocity of (1 0 0) m/s.
The fixedWalls have a no-slip condition (zero velocity, fixedValue uniform (0 0 0)).
The front and back faces are empty for both U and p.
For pressure (p), apply zeroGradient on movingWall and fixedWalls, and empty on front and back faces; set a fixedValue of 0 at one point or use the default OpenFOAM best-practice approach (zeroGradient on all walls, empty on front/back, with a reference pressure cell).
Initialize p to zero uniformly in the domain.
Initialize k to 1.5e-4 uniformly in the domain.
Apply kqRWallFunction on all walls (movingWall and fixedWalls) for k; empty on front and back.
Initialize epsilon to 1.4e-4 uniformly in the domain.
Apply epsilonWallFunction on all walls (movingWall and fixedWalls) for epsilon; empty on front and back.
Initialize nut to 0 uniformly in the domain.
Apply nutkWallFunction on all walls (movingWall and fixedWalls) for nut; empty on front and back.
Set the convection scheme div(phi,U) to Gauss limitedLinearV 1.
Set the convection scheme div(phi,k) to Gauss limitedLinear 1.
Set the convection scheme div(phi,epsilon) to Gauss limitedLinear 1.
Set the viscous term div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) to Gauss linear.
