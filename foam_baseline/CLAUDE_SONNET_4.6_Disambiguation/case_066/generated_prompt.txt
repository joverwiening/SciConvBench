Simulate steady-state, incompressible, laminar flow through a porous medium using OpenFOAM with the porousSimpleFoam solver.
The domain is a 2D channel 0.5 m long and 0.1 m high.
The porous zone occupies the central region from x=0.2 m to x=0.3 m.
The fluid is water (incompressible, constant properties).
The Darcy-Forchheimer coefficients for the porous zone are d = (1e8 1e8 1e10) and f = (0 0 0).
The inlet boundary condition for U is fixedValue uniform (1 0 0) m/s.
The outlet boundary condition for U is zeroGradient.
The top and bottom walls use the noSlip condition for U.
The outlet boundary condition for p is fixedValue uniform 0.
The top and bottom walls use zeroGradient for p.
The inlet boundary condition for p is zeroGradient.
Create the mesh using blockMesh with a uniform Cartesian mesh of 100 cells in x, 20 cells in y, and 1 cell in z.
The simulation is steady-state and uses the SIMPLE algorithm as implemented in porousSimpleFoam.
