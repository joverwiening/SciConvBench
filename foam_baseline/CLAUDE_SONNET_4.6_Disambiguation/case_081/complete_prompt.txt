Simulate a 2D laminar premixed methane-air flame using the reactingFoam solver.
GEOMETRY: The domain is a rectangular channel 0.1 m long and 0.02 m high. The front and back patches are of type 'empty' to enforce 2D.
 MESH: Use blockMesh to create a uniform Cartesian mesh of 100 cells in the x-direction and 20 cells in the y-direction (100x20x1).
INLET CONDITIONS (patch 'inlet'): A stoichiometric methane-air mixture enters at a uniform velocity of U = (0.5 0 0) m/s and a temperature of T = 300 K. The species mass fractions are CH4=0.055, O2=0.22, N2=0.725. The pressure is zeroGradient.
OUTLET CONDITIONS (patch 'outlet'): Use a totalPressure boundary condition for pressure p with p0=101325 Pa. Use zeroGradient for U, T, and all species.
WALL CONDITIONS (patches 'top' and 'bottom'): Apply a noSlip condition for velocity U and zeroGradient for temperature T (adiabatic) and all species.
INITIAL CONDITIONS: The entire domain is initialized with the inlet mixture (CH4=0.055, O2=0.22, N2=0.725) at T=300 K, p=101325 Pa, and U=(0 0 0) m/s. A hot spot is used for ignition, defined via system/setFieldsDict to set T=1800 K in a small region near the inlet.
COMBUSTION PROPERTIES: Use a simple one-step irreversible reaction for methane combustion (CH4 + 2O2 -> CO2 + 2H2O). In constant/combustionProperties, set reaction to 'methaneAir' with Arrhenius kinetics: A=2.119e11, beta=0, Ta=2.027e8. Use 'janaf' for thermodynamics.
TIME CONTROLS: Run the simulation from t=0 to endTime=0.1 s. Use an adjustable time step with an initial deltaT of 1e-6 s and a maximum Courant number (maxCo) of 0.5. Write results every 0.005 s.
