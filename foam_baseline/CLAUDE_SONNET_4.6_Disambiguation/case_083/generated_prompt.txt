Model spray combustion of liquid dodecane injecting into hot air in a combustion chamber using OpenFOAM with the reactingParcelFoam solver.
The combustion chamber is cylindrical, 0.2 m long and 0.1 m in diameter, modeled as 2D axisymmetric.
Use the k-epsilon turbulence model.
Inject liquid dodecane at the center of the inlet using a cone injection model with a mass flow rate of 0.002 kg/s and droplet temperature of 300 K.
Use a Rosin-Rammler droplet size distribution with a mean diameter of 50e-6 m.
Hot air inlet conditions: axial velocity 10 m/s, temperature 800 K, pressure 101325 Pa, species composition O2=0.233 and N2=0.767 by mass fraction.
Outlet boundary: use totalPressure for p and zeroGradient for all other fields.
Wall boundary: noSlip velocity condition, fixed wall temperature at 400 K.
The axis boundary should use the OpenFOAM 'axis' type condition.
Use a two-step global reaction mechanism for dodecane, configured in constant/combustionProperties and constant/reactionSchemes.
Use JANAF thermodynamics for all species.
Use the RanzMarshall model for droplet heat and mass transfer.
Use the standard drag model for droplet momentum.
Use the Reitz-Diwakar model for spray breakup as a best-practice assumption.
Use a stochastic dispersion model for turbulent droplet dispersion as a best-practice assumption.
