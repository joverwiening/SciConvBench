Set up a laminar counter-flow diffusion flame simulation using the reactingFoam solver in OpenFOAM.

The domain is 2-D (one cell in z) rectangular, spanning x from 0 to 0.02 m, y from -0.01 to 0.01 m, and z from -0.01 to 0.01 m.
Use 100 grid points in the x direction, 40 grid points in the y direction, and 1 cell in the z direction.
The left boundary (x=0) is named "fuel" and is a velocity inlet for methane (CH4) at 293 K with a velocity of 0.1 m/s in the +x direction and Y_CH4 = 1.0.
The right boundary (x=0.02) is named "air" and is a velocity inlet for air at 293 K with a velocity of -0.1 m/s in the -x direction, Y_O2 = 0.23, and Y_N2 = 0.77.
The top and bottom boundaries are named "outlet" and use a zero-gradient or inletOutlet condition.
The front and back faces are named "frontAndBack" and are set to empty (2-D simulation).
Initialize the entire domain temperature field to 2000 K at t=0.
Initialize the pressure field to 101325 Pa throughout the domain.
Initialize the velocity field to zero throughout the domain.
Initialize the species mass fractions in the interior as a reasonable mixed state, for example Y_N2 = 1.0 (inert fill) or a linear interpolation between fuel and air sides, following best practices for counter-flow flame initialization.
Use the GRI-Mech 3.0 chemical mechanism for methane combustion, as it is the best-practice standard mechanism for laminar methane flames.
Use compressible finite-rate chemistry with hePsiThermo as the thermodynamic model.
Use coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture for mixture transport properties.
Use Sutherland transport model.
Use JANAF thermodynamics.
Set the combustion model to laminar (finite-rate, no turbulence-chemistry interaction).
Set momentum transport to laminar.
Use EulerImplicit as the chemistry solver type with the Rosenbrock43 ODE solver.
Set the simulation timestep to 1e-4 s.
Set the end time to 0.5 s.
Write results at an interval of 0.05 s.
Generate all necessary OpenFOAM case files including blockMeshDict, thermophysicalProperties, chemistryProperties, combustionProperties, turbulenceProperties, fvSchemes, fvSolution, controlDict, and all boundary condition files in the 0/ directory.
