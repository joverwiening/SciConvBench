Simulate compressible steady-state flow of air through a heated porous block using OpenFOAM with the rhoPorousSimpleFoam solver.
The computational domain is a 2D channel 0.2 m long and 0.05 m high.
A porous block cellZone occupies the region from x=0.08 m to x=0.12 m.
The inlet boundary condition uses fixed total pressure p0 = 101325 Pa and fixed total temperature T0 = 300 K.
The outlet boundary condition uses a fixedValue static pressure of 100000 Pa.
The porous block uses the DarcyForchheimer model with resistance coefficients d=(1e9, 1e9, 1e11) and f=(0, 0, 0).
A volumetric heat source of 1e6 W/m³ is applied to the porous block cellZone via a scalarSemiImplicitSource acting on the energy equation variable h in absolute volume mode.
The simulation is steady-state.
Set up all relevant OpenFOAM case files including constant/, system/, and 0/ directories with appropriate boundary conditions, thermophysical properties for air (compressible), fvOptions for the porous resistance and heat source, and fvSolution/fvSchemes suitable for rhoPorousSimpleFoam.
