Simulate transient dam-break flow through a porous dam using OpenFOAM with the interFoam solver.
The domain is 5 m (x) × 2 m (y) × 0.1 m (z), treated as a 2D simulation (1 cell in z).
Use blockMesh with 250 cells in x, 100 cells in y, and 1 cell in z.
The porous dam zone is rectangular, occupying x = 2 m to x = 3 m over the full height (2 m tall).
Apply the DarcyForchheimer porous resistance model in the dam zone with d = (1e6 1e6 0) and f = (100 100 0).
Water properties: kinematic viscosity nu = 1e-6 m²/s, density rho = 1000 kg/m³.
Air properties: kinematic viscosity nu = 1.48e-5 m²/s, density rho = 1 kg/m³.
Surface tension sigma = 0.07 N/m.
Initialize alpha.water = 1 in the box from (0 0 -1) to (1 1.5 1); all other cells start as air (alpha.water = 0).
Initialize U = (0 0 0) and p_rgh = 0 everywhere at t = 0.
This is a dam-break setup with no inlet; water is released from rest at t = 0.
Left and right walls are solid no-slip boundaries with zeroGradient for alpha.water and fixedFluxPressure for p_rgh.
The top boundary is open atmosphere.
Front and back boundaries use empty condition (2D).
Set endTime = 10 s, initial deltaT = 0.001 s, adjustable time stepping enabled, maxCo = 0.5, and writeInterval = 0.1 s.
Output results showing the movement of the water front through the porous dam structure over time.
