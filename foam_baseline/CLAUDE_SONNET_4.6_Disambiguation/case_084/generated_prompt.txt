Simulate a flame propagating through a closed rectangular tube filled with a stoichiometric hydrogen-air mixture using OpenFOAM with the rhoReactingFoam solver.

The tube is 0.5 m long, 0.01 m wide, and 0.01 m high (rectangular cross-section).
The mesh has 500 cells in x, 5 cells in y, and 5 cells in z.
All six faces of the tube are solid walls (leftWall, rightWall, frontAndBack, topAndBottom, and both ends).
Apply noSlip boundary condition for U on all walls.
Apply zeroGradient (adiabatic) boundary condition for T on all walls.
Apply fixedFluxPressure boundary condition for p on all walls.
Apply zeroGradient boundary condition for all species on all walls.
The initial temperature of the unburned mixture is 300 K and the initial pressure is 101325 Pa.
The mixture is a stoichiometric hydrogen-air composition.
Use a detailed hydrogen-air reaction mechanism with 9 species and 19 reactions.
Convert the CHEMKIN mechanism files to OpenFOAM format and place them in the constant directory.
Ignite the mixture by setting T=2000 K in the region x=0 to x=0.01 m (first 2% of the domain) using system/setFieldsDict with setFields.
All other initial conditions (U, p, species mass fractions) are uniform throughout the domain.
Provide the full OpenFOAM case structure including constant, system, and 0 directories with all required files.
