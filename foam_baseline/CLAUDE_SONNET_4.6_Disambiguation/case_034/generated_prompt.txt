Run a buoyant cavity conjugate heat transfer simulation using chtMultiRegionFoam in OpenFOAM.
The fluid cavity dimensions are 1.0 m (x) x 1.0 m (y) x 0.01 m (z), where z is the empty/2D direction.
A solid block of 0.4 m x 0.4 m is centered in the fluid cavity.

The right wall of the fluid cavity is the cold wall at 290 K.
The top and bottom walls of the fluid cavity are adiabatic (zeroGradient for temperature).
The fluid is air modeled as a compressible perfect gas using the heRhoThermo/perfectGas thermodynamic model.
Use Sutherland transport for the fluid viscosity.
Fluid Cp is approximately 1005 J/kg/K and molecular weight is 28.9 kg/kmol.
Density and viscosity are computed from the equation of state and Sutherland's law, not fixed values.
The solid block has copper-like properties: density 8960 kg/m³, Cp 385 J/kg/K, thermal conductivity 401 W/m/K.
Use laminar flow modeling, or RAS kOmegaSST if turbulence modeling is enabled; apply best-practice judgment for a buoyant cavity at this scale.
Choose mesh resolution for both fluid and solid regions based on best practices for buoyant cavity CHT simulations.
Initial conditions for the fluid region: U = (0 0 0), T = 300 K, p_rgh = 0.
Initial condition for the solid region: T = 300 K.
The simulation uses two mesh regions: one for the fluid cavity (with the solid block excluded) and one for the solid block.
Heat conduction is coupled across the fluid-solid interface using chtMultiRegionFoam's standard coupled boundary conditions.
