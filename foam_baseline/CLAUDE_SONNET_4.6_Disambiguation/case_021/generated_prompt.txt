Simulate a 2D flow past a circular cylinder using OpenFOAM with pimpleFoam solver.
The domain is rectangular: x from -5 m to 5 m (streamwise), y from -1.5 m to 2.5 m (cross-stream), z from -1 m to 1 m.
A circular cylinder of diameter 2 m is centered at the origin (x=0, y=0).
The solver is pimpleFoam for incompressible unsteady flow.
Set startTime = 0 s, endTime = 0.5 s, deltaT = 0.0025 s.
Write results every 0.05 s.
The initial field specifies a quiescent fluid: U = (0 0 0) m/s, p = 0.
Apply a uniform inflow velocity U = (1 0 0) m/s at the inlet boundary.
Use laminar flow with the generalised Newtonian model and the crossPowerLaw viscosity model.
Set crossPowerLaw parameters: nuInf = 10, m = 0.4, n = 3.
For nu0 and tauStar, make reasonable assumptions based on best practices (e.g., nu0 = 1e-3 or a value significantly larger than nuInf, and tauStar = 1.0 or a similarly standard default).
Set up all necessary OpenFOAM case files including constant/, system/, and 0/ directories with appropriate boundary conditions for all patches (inlet, outlet, top, bottom, cylinder wall, and front/back for 2D).
