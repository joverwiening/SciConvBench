Use OpenFOAM's porousSimpleFoam solver for steady-state incompressible laminar flow.
The channel dimensions are 1.0m (x) x 0.2m (y) x 0.01m (z).
The porous block spans x=0.4m to x=0.6m and covers the full channel height (0.0m to 0.2m) and full width (0.0m to 0.01m).
The inlet boundary condition is a fixed velocity of (0.1 0 0) m/s in the x-direction.
The outlet boundary condition is a fixed pressure (zeroGradient for velocity, fixedValue p=0 for pressure).
The top, bottom, and side walls use no-slip conditions for velocity and zeroGradient for pressure.
The fluid is water at 20°C with kinematic viscosity nu = 1e-6 m^2/s, defined in transportProperties.
No density specification is needed as the solver is incompressible.
The porous zone is defined using Darcy-Forchheimer coefficients: d = (1e8 1e8 1e8), f = (0 0 0).
The porous region is specified as a cellZone named porousZone in topoSetDict and referenced in fvOptions.
Use blockMesh to generate the mesh with a structured hex mesh; apply refinement in the porous zone region.
Apply best-practice numerical schemes for laminar incompressible porousSimpleFoam: Gauss linear for gradient schemes, Gauss linear corrected for Laplacian schemes, and linearUpwind or Gauss linear for divergence schemes.
Use SIMPLE algorithm settings with relaxation factors of 0.7 for U and 0.3 for p.
Set solver tolerances to 1e-6 for p (PCG with DIC preconditioner) and 1e-6 for U (PBiCGStab with DILU preconditioner).
Run until convergence with a maximum of 1000 iterations or until residuals drop below 1e-5.
Generate all required OpenFOAM case files: blockMeshDict, topoSetDict, fvOptions, transportProperties, turbulenceProperties (laminar), fvSchemes, fvSolution, controlDict, and boundary condition files (0/U and 0/p).
