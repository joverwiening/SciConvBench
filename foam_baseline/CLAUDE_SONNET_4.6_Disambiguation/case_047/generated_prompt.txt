Simulate turbulent channel flow using the k-epsilon model in OpenFOAM with simpleFoam (incompressible solver).

Channel dimensions are 1m (x, length) x 0.1m (y, height) x 0.2m (z, width).
Use a uniform mesh of 100 cells in x, 20 cells in y, and 10 cells in z with no grading.
Drive the flow using a meanVelocityForce source in fvOptions targeting a bulk velocity of 0.5 m/s.
Kinematic viscosity nu is 1e-5 m^2/s.
Use the standard k-epsilon turbulence model with default coefficients.
Initialize k to uniform 0.000375 m^2/s^2 and epsilon to uniform 1.875e-4 m^2/s^3 throughout the domain.
Apply kqRWallFunction boundary condition for k at walls, initialized to 0.000375.
Apply epsilonWallFunction boundary condition for epsilon at walls, initialized to 1.875e-4.
Set endTime to 1000 with deltaT of 1.
Use GAMG solver for pressure with convergence tolerance 1e-6.
Use smoothSolver for U, k, and epsilon with convergence tolerance 1e-5.
Stop the simulation when residuals fall below the respective tolerances.
