Model a flow through a thin porous screen in a 2D channel using OpenFOAM.
The turbulence model is k-omega SST.
The channel is 1.0 m long, 0.1 m high, and 0.01 m deep (one cell thick in z for 2D).
The mesh has 100 cells in x, 20 cells in y, and 1 cell in z.
The porous screen is located at x = 0.5 m from the inlet.
The inlet boundary condition for velocity is fixedValue uniform (5 0 0) m/s.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is nu = 1e-5 m²/s.
Assume reasonable inlet turbulence conditions based on best practices (e.g., turbulence intensity of 5% and a mixing length of 7% of channel height to derive k and omega at inlet).
The porous screen is modelled using the porousBafflePressure boundary condition with Darcy coefficient D = 1e7 and Forchheimer coefficient I = 0.
The flow is incompressible; use the appropriate OpenFOAM solver (e.g., simpleFoam for steady-state incompressible turbulent flow).
Set up all required OpenFOAM case files including blockMeshDict, boundary conditions (0/ directory for U, p, k, omega, nut), turbulenceProperties, transportProperties, fvSchemes, fvSolution, and controlDict.
Apply a no-slip condition on the top and bottom walls.
Apply a zero-gradient or appropriate outlet condition at the channel exit.
The screen baffle should be represented as a coupled wall-type internal face pair with the porousBafflePressure condition applied to the pressure field.
