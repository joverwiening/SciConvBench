Simulate transient 2D flow over a cylinder at Reynolds number 150 using OpenFOAM with the pimpleFoam solver.

The domain extends from x = -1 m to x = 2.5 m (i.e., -10D to +25D) and y = -1 m to y = 1 m (i.e., -10D to +10D).
The case is 2D with a single cell depth in z, using empty boundary conditions on the front and back patches.

Use blockMesh to generate the mesh with an O-grid topology around the cylinder.
Apply mesh refinement in the wake region downstream of the cylinder.
Choose cell counts based on best practices for this Reynolds number and domain size.

The inlet velocity is U = 1.5 m/s uniform in the x-direction, giving Re = 150.
Apply a uniform inlet velocity boundary condition at the left (inlet) patch.
Apply a zero-gradient outlet condition at the right (outlet) patch.
Apply no-slip boundary conditions on the cylinder wall.
Apply slip or symmetry conditions on the top and bottom patches.

Use an adjustable time step with a maximum Courant number (maxCo) of 0.5.
Run the simulation to an end time of 20 s.
Write results every 0.2 s.

Configure pimpleFoam settings (number of PIMPLE outer correctors, pressure correctors, and non-orthogonal correctors) following best practices for this type of unsteady laminar flow.
