Simulate a fluidized bed using OpenFOAM's twoPhaseEulerFoam solver with an Euler-Euler model.
The fluidizing gas is air.
Solid particles have a density of 2500 kg/m³ and a mean diameter of 200 microns.
The bed domain dimensions are 0.1 m (width) x 0.5 m (height) x 0.1 m (depth).
The inlet superficial gas velocity at the bottom is 0.2 m/s directed upward.
The initial solid volume fraction (alpha.solid) is 0.55 in the bottom 0.2 m of the bed and 0 above that.
The simulation end time is 5 seconds.
Use adaptive time stepping with an initial time step in the range of 1e-4 to 5e-4 s and a maximum Courant number of 0.3.
Use RAS k-epsilon turbulence modeling for the gas phase.
Use Kinetic Theory of Granular Flow (KTGF) for the solid phase with a restitution coefficient of 0.9.
