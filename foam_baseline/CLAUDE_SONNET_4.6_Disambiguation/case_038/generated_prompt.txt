Simulate heat transfer with natural convection in a porous block using OpenFOAM with the buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam solver.
The domain is 2D: 0.10 m in x, 0.10 m in y, and 0.01 m in z (depth).
Use empty patches for the front and back faces (z-direction) to enforce 2D behavior.
The mesh resolution is 40 cells in x, 40 cells in y, and 1 cell in z.
The fluid is air, modeled under the Boussinesq approximation.
The bottom wall is a hot boundary at 310 K with no-slip velocity condition.
The top wall is a cold boundary at 290 K with no-slip velocity condition.
The left and right (side) walls are adiabatic (zeroGradient for temperature) with no-slip velocity condition.
The porous medium is modeled using the Darcy-Forchheimer model with d = (1e7 1e7 1e7) and f = (0 0 0).
No explicit porosity value is specified; use the resistance coefficients directly.
Set up all required OpenFOAM case files including blockMeshDict, fvSolution, fvSchemes, transportProperties, turbulenceProperties, and all boundary condition files in the 0/ directory.
