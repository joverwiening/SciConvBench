Set up a dam break simulation case in OpenFOAM using the interFoam solver.
The tank is 0.584m long and 0.584m high, treated as a 2D case with a nominal depth of 0.1m.
Use a uniform mesh of 58 cells in x, 58 cells in y, and 1 cell in z (depth direction).
The initial water column is 0.146m wide and 0.292m high, placed in the bottom-left corner of the tank.
The remaining volume of the tank is initialised as air.
Water density is 1000 kg/m³ and kinematic viscosity is 1e-6 m²/s.
Air density is 1 kg/m³ and kinematic viscosity is 1.48e-5 m²/s.
Surface tension between water and air is 0.07 N/m.
Use adjustable time stepping with adjustTimeStep set to yes.
Set maxCo to 0.5 and maxAlphaCo to 0.5.
Set startTime to 0 and endTime to 2 seconds.
Set writeControl to timeStep and writeInterval to 20.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction at 9.81 m/s².
Set up all required OpenFOAM case directories and files: constant (transportProperties, turbulenceProperties, g), system (blockMeshDict, controlDict, fvSchemes, fvSolution, setFieldsDict), and 0 (alpha.water, U, p_rgh).
Use standard interFoam boundary conditions: no-slip walls on all sides, with appropriate alpha, pressure, and velocity conditions.
Use setFields to initialise the water column region.
