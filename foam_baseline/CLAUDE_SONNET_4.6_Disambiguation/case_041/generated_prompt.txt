Simulate a 2D buoyant cavity using OpenFOAM with the buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam solver.
The cavity is a 1m x 1m square.
The left wall is the hot wall at 303 K and the right wall is the cold wall at 293 K.
The top and bottom walls have no-slip boundary conditions for velocity and adiabatic (zeroGradient) boundary conditions for temperature.
The left and right walls also have no-slip boundary conditions for velocity.
The fluid is air modeled as laminar with no turbulence model.
Use the following fluid properties: kinematic viscosity nu = 1e-5 m²/s, thermal expansion coefficient beta = 0.00333 1/K, Prandtl number Pr = 0.71.
Use a reference temperature Tref = 293 K for the Boussinesq approximation.
The spatial discretization is finite volume as implemented in OpenFOAM.
Set up all necessary OpenFOAM case files including constant, system, and 0 directories for this configuration.
