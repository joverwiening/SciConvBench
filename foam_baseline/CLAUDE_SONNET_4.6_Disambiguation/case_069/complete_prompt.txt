Simulate steady-state conjugate heat transfer (CHT) between a hot inflowing fluid and a 'porous' solid block (porosity reflected in effective thermal conductivity) using chtMultiRegionSimpleFoam.

Solver: chtMultiRegionSimpleFoam (steady multi-region CHT)

Geometry & regions:
- Overall domain: 0.5 m x 0.1 m x 0.01 m (2D via empty front/back).
- Solid block region: 0.1 m x 0.05 m x 0.01 m centered in the domain.
Create a single mesh with blockMesh, define cellZones 'fluid' and 'solid' (topoSet), then:
  splitMeshRegions -cellZones -overwrite
Regions: fluid and solid.

Physical properties:
- Fluid region (constant/fluid/thermophysicalProperties): air as compressible perfect gas (heRhoThermo, perfectGas, Sutherland), Cp ~ 1005, molWeight ~ 28.9.
- Solid region (constant/solid/thermophysicalProperties): porous solid modeled as an effective homogeneous solid (heSolidThermo).
  Example 'aluminum foam' effective properties:
  - rho = 500 kg/m^3
  - Cp = 900 J/kg/K
  - kappa = 10 W/m/K   (reduced conductivity vs solid aluminum)
No momentum equation is solved in the solid region, so do NOT apply a porosity momentum source in the solid region.

Boundary & interface conditions:
- Fluid inlet: U fixedValue (1 0 0); T fixedValue 400 K; p_rgh zeroGradient.
- Fluid outlet: p_rgh fixedValue 0; U zeroGradient; T inletOutlet (inletValue 300; value 300).
- External fluid walls: U noSlip; T zeroGradient (adiabatic); p_rgh fixedFluxPressure.
- Solid external walls (if any): T zeroGradient (adiabatic).
- Fluid-solid interface: use temperature-coupled baffle BCs (turbulentTemperatureCoupledBaffleMixed / compressible::turbulentTemperatureCoupledBaffleMixed) on both sides so heat flux and temperature are coupled across the interface.

Initial conditions: T=300 K in both regions; U=(0 0 0) in fluid; p_rgh=0.

Controls: endTime 2000; deltaT 1; writeInterval 200 (steady solver pseudo-time iterations).
