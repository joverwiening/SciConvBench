Simulate a 2D lid-driven cavity flow using OpenFOAM with the icoFoam solver.
The cavity is 2D and the fluid is incompressible.
The top wall moves to the right at a velocity of 1 m/s.
All other walls are stationary no-slip walls.
The cavity size is 0.1 m × 0.1 m.
Use a 20×20 cell structured mesh.
Set kinematic viscosity nu to 0.001 m²/s in constant/transportProperties (corresponding to Re = 100).
Set up all necessary OpenFOAM case files including 0/ (boundary conditions for U and p), constant/ (transportProperties and polyMesh), and system/ (controlDict, fvSchemes, fvSolution).
Use icoFoam as the solver.
