Simulate a thermal plume rising from a hot strip on the floor of a large open room using OpenFOAM with the buoyantPimpleFoam solver.
The simulation is 2D (a thin slab in z).
The computational domain is 2.0 m (x) x 2.0 m (y) x 0.01 m (z), with gravity acting in the -y direction.
Use the RAS kOmegaSST turbulence model.
The hot strip is 0.2 m wide, centered on the floor, occupying x in [0.9, 1.1] at y=0, and has a fixed temperature boundary condition of 400 K (patch named hotSource).
The remainder of the floor (patch named coolFloor) has a fixedValue temperature of 300 K.
The side walls (patch named sideWalls) have a fixedValue temperature of 300 K.
The top boundary (patch named outlet) uses an inletOutlet condition with an inletValue of 300 K.
The initial air temperature throughout the domain is 300 K.
The mesh resolution is approximately 160 x 160 x 1 cells.
The mesh uses a multi-block structure to separate the hotSource patch from the coolFloor patch on the floor boundary; no additional local refinement is required.
Provide the full OpenFOAM case setup including blockMeshDict, boundary condition files (0/ directory for T, U, p_rgh, k, omega), turbulenceProperties, thermophysicalProperties, and fvSolution/fvSchemes as needed to run the simulation.
