Q: What are the boundary conditions for velocity on each wall of the square cavity?
A: The top wall ('movingWall') moves in the x-direction with a uniform velocity of 1 m/s. The other walls ('fixedWalls') have a no-slip boundary condition with velocity equal to zero. The front and back faces are designated as 'empty'.
