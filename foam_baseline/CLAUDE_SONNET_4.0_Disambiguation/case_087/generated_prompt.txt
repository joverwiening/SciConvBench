Set up a simulation for supersonic flow over a forward-facing step with Mach 3 inlet conditions.
The step height is 0.2 m and the total channel height is 1 m, making the step height 20% of the inlet channel height.
The step starts at x=0.6 m and the total domain length is 3 m, so the step length is 2.4 m.
The inlet conditions are pressure 101325 Pa, temperature 300 K, and velocity 1020.6 m/s.
The density should be calculated from the ideal gas law using the inlet pressure and temperature.
The flow is inviscid.
Apply slip boundary conditions on all walls including the step surface and channel walls.
Apply waveTransmissive boundary condition for pressure at the outlet with pInf set to 101325 Pa.
Apply zeroGradient or waveTransmissive boundary conditions for velocity and temperature at the outlet.
