Simulate a 2D buoyant cavity with the following specifications:
The cavity is a 1m x 1m square domain filled with air.
The left wall is hot at 303 K temperature.
The right wall is cold at 293 K temperature.
The top and bottom walls are insulated with no-slip velocity boundary condition, zero gradient temperature boundary condition, and fixed flux pressure boundary condition.
The initial temperature is uniform at 293 K throughout the cavity.
The initial velocity is zero everywhere with the fluid at rest.
The initial pressure p_rgh is uniform at 0 throughout the domain.
