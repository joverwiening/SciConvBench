Model a de Laval nozzle using OpenFOAM CFD software with the density-based solver rhoCentralFoam.
Set inlet total pressure to 5e5 Pa (5 bar) and total temperature to 600 K.
Set back pressure to 3.5e5 Pa (350 kPa) to force a normal shock in the diverging section.
Use nozzle dimensions of total length 0.3m, inlet height 0.1m, throat height 0.05m at x=0.1m, and outlet height 0.08m.
Model as a 2D nozzle where the dimensions represent heights rather than diameters.
Use air as a perfect gas with gamma = 1.4 and constant transport properties.
Create a structured mesh with 300 cells in the axial direction and 50 cells in the transverse direction.
Make the mesh finer near the throat region.
Apply total pressure and total temperature inlet boundary conditions with total pressure 5e5 Pa, total temperature 600 K, and zero gradient for velocity.
The simulation should capture the normal shock wave formation in the diverging section of the nozzle.
