Simulate transient flow through a porous dam using interFoam solver.
The domain is 5 m (x) x 2 m (y) x 0.1 m (z-thickness).
The porous dam zone occupies x=2 m to x=3 m over the full height, making the dam 1 m wide and 2 m tall.
The porous dam uses DarcyForchheimer coefficients: d (1e6 1e6 0) and f (100 100 0).
Initial conditions are alpha.water = 0 everywhere except for a water column at the left side from (0 0 -1) to (1 1.5 1) with alpha.water = 1.
Initial velocity U = (0 0 0) and pressure p_rgh = 0 everywhere.
Left, right, and bottom walls are solid walls with no-slip velocity and zeroGradient for alpha.water.
Top boundary is open with pressureInletOutletVelocity for U and inletOutlet for alpha.water with inletValue 0.
Front and back boundaries are empty for 2D simulation.
The simulation runs for 10 seconds total with initial time step of 0.001 seconds.
Use adaptive time stepping with maximum Courant number of 0.5.
Set write interval to 0.1 seconds for output.
Use a reasonable turbulence model based on best practices.
Collect standard field outputs and reasonable additional data for analyzing transient flow through porous media.
