Simulate incompressible water flow through a porous block inside a channel with steady-state conditions.
The channel dimensions are 1.0m length x 0.2m height x 0.01m width.
The porous zone extends from x=0.4m to x=0.6m and spans the entire channel height.
Apply inlet velocity of (0.1 0 0) m/s at the channel entrance.
Apply outlet pressure of 0 Pa uniform at the channel exit.
Use Darcy-Forchheimer model for porous properties with d = (1e8 1e8 1e8) and f = (0 0 0) resistance coefficients.
Apply noSlip boundary condition for velocity U on top and bottom walls.
Apply zeroGradient boundary condition for pressure p on top and bottom walls.
The flow should be steady-state and incompressible.
