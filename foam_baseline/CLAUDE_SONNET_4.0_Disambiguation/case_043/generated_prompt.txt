Simulate natural convection in a closed room with a heat source on the floor using the buoyantPimpleFoam solver.
The room is 4m wide by 3m high for a 2D simulation.
The heat source is 1m wide and centered on the floor.
Set the heat source temperature to 323 K.
Apply adiabatic boundary conditions (zeroGradient) for temperature on all walls.
Set the initial air temperature in the room to 293 K.
Use a mesh with 80x60 cells (80 in x-direction, 60 in y-direction).
Apply non-uniform mesh refinement near the heat source and walls.
