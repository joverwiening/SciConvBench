Q: What boundary conditions should be applied at the inlet, outlet, and walls of the channel?
A: For the boundary conditions:

**Inlet:**
- U: fixedValue (1 0 0)
- p: zeroGradient
- k: fixedValue 3.75e-3
- epsilon: fixedValue 0.017

**Outlet:**
- U: zeroGradient
- p: fixedValue 0
- k: zeroGradient
- epsilon: zeroGradient

**Walls (topWall & bottomWall):**
- U: noSlip
- p: zeroGradient
- k: kqRWallFunction
- epsilon: epsilonWallFunction
- nut: nutkWallFunction

**FrontAndBack patches:**
- All fields: type 'empty'
