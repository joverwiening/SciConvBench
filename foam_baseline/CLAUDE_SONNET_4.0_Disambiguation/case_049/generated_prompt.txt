Set up a dam break case where a column of water collapses under gravity in a tank of air.
The water column has width 0.146m and height 0.292m.
This is a 2D case so depth is not applicable and front and back patches are 'empty' type.
The overall tank dimensions are length 0.584m and height 0.584m.
The water column is positioned in the bottom-left corner starting at x=0 and extending to x=0.146m, from y=0 up to y=0.292m.
Use blockMesh to create a uniform 58x58 grid.
Use interFoam solver.
For velocity boundary conditions use noSlip on all walls (leftWall, rightWall, lowerWall), pressureInletOutletVelocity on top (atmosphere), and empty on frontAndBack.
For pressure boundary conditions use fixedFluxPressure on all walls, totalPressure with p0 uniform 0 on top (atmosphere), and empty on frontAndBack.
For phase fraction boundary conditions use zeroGradient on all walls, inletOutlet with inletValue 0 on top (atmosphere), and empty on frontAndBack.
Use adjustable time step with adjustTimeStep yes, maxCo 0.5, and maxAlphaCo 0.5.
