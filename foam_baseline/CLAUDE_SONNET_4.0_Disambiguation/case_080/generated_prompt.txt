Simulate a 3D fluidized bed using an Euler-Euler model.
The geometry is a rectangular bed with dimensions 0.1 m x 0.5 m x 0.1 m.
Gas enters from the bottom distributor and exits at the top outlet.
The gas phase is air with density 1.2 kg/m³ and kinematic viscosity 1.5e-5 m²/s.
Set the inlet velocity to a superficial velocity of 0.2 m/s upward.
The solid particles have a density of 2500 kg/m³ and diameter of 200 microns.
Set the initial packed bed with alpha.solid = 0.55 in the bottom 0.2 m of the domain.
Set alpha.solid = 0 above the 0.2 m height.
Use the SchillerNaumann drag model for the gas-solid interaction.
