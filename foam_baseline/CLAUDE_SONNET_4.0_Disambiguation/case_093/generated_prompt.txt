Simulate a hot plume rising from a small heat source on the floor of a large, open room using turbulent flow.
The room dimensions are 2m wide x 4m high in a 2D simulation.
The heat source is a central 0.2m section of the floor extending from x=0.9m to x=1.1m along the bottom boundary.
The heat source has a fixed temperature of 400 K.
The surrounding room air and walls are at 300 K with initial temperature throughout the domain at 300 K.
The side walls have zeroGradient temperature boundary conditions.
Use the RANS k-epsilon turbulence model with simulationType RAS and RASModel kEpsilon in the constant/momentumTransport file.
The top boundary uses totalPressure for p_rgh with value uniform 0, pressureInletOutletVelocity for U, and inletOutlet for T, k, and epsilon with inletValue for T=300K.
The side walls use slip condition for U and zeroGradient for T, k, epsilon, and p_rgh.
Use buoyantPimpleFoam solver for this buoyant turbulent flow simulation.
The mesh uses 100 cells in x direction graded towards center and 200 cells in y direction graded towards bottom.
The mesh is refined near the central bottom region where the heat source is located.
