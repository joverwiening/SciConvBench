Simulate a 2D flow past a circular cylinder using pimpleFoam for incompressible unsteady flow.
The domain is rectangular extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m in the streamwise direction and y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m in the cross-stream direction and z = -1 to 1 m.
The cylinder has diameter 2 m and is centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).
Use startTime = 0 s, endTime = 0.5 s, deltaT = 0.0025 s.
Write results every 0.05 s.
The initial field specifies a quiescent fluid with U = (0 0 0) and p = 0.
The flow is laminar using generalisednewtonain model with crosspowerlaw viscosity model.
The cross power law viscosity model parameters are nuinf = 10, m = 0.4, n = 3.
The inlet boundary condition is U = (1 0 0) m/s.
The outlet boundary condition is U zeroGradient and p = 0 (reference).
The top and bottom walls have U noSlip and p zeroGradient boundary conditions.
The cylinder wall has U noSlip and p zeroGradient boundary conditions.
The front and back faces use empty boundary conditions for 2D simulation.
