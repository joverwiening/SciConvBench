Q: What are the dimensions of the porous block?
A: The dimensions are 0.10 m x 0.10 m x 0.01 m (it's a 2D square cavity with the third dimension being thin for 2D simulation).

=== 2026-03-16 09:30:22 ===
Q: What are the specific temperatures for the hot bottom boundary and the cold top boundary?
A: The hot bottom boundary (hotWall) should be 310 K and the cold top boundary (coldWall) should be 290 K.

=== 2026-03-16 09:30:26 ===
Q: What boundary conditions should be applied to the left and right walls of the porous block?
A: For the left and right walls (sideWalls):
- U: noSlip 
- T: zeroGradient
- p_rgh: fixedFluxPressure

=== 2026-03-16 09:30:29 ===
Q: What are the properties of the porous medium that need to be specified for the simulation?
A: The porous medium properties are specified using a Darcy-Forchheimer resistance model with:
- d (Darcy coefficient): (1e7 1e7 1e7)
- f (Forchheimer coefficient): (0 0 0)

This is implemented as an explicitPorositySource in the fvOptions with type DarcyForchheimer.

=== 2026-03-16 09:30:32 ===
Q: What are the initial conditions for velocity, pressure, and temperature fields throughout the domain?
A: The initial conditions are:
- U (velocity): (0 0 0) m/s
- T (temperature): 300 K  
- p_rgh (pressure): 0 Pa

These should be applied uniformly throughout the entire domain.
