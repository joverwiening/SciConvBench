Simulate a thermal plume rising from a hot strip on the floor of a large, open room using OpenFOAM with the buoyantPimpleFoam solver.
The room dimensions are 2.0 m length x 2.0 m height x 0.01 m thickness for a 2D simulation.
The hot strip has a width of 0.2 m and is centered on the floor, spanning from x = 0.9 m to x = 1.1 m at y = 0.
The hot strip should be maintained at 400 K.
The ambient temperature of the room should be 300 K for initial conditions and for the cool floor and side walls boundary conditions.
The simulation should run for 200 seconds with an initial time step size of 0.01 seconds.
Use adjustable time stepping with a maximum Courant number of 0.5.
Use the RAS kOmegaSST turbulence model for capturing turbulent behavior.
Use a mesh resolution of approximately 160 x 160 x 1 cells.
