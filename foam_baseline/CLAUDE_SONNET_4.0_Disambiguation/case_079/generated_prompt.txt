Model a single air bubble rising in a column of quiescent liquid using a VOF solver in 2D.
The domain is 0.05 m wide and 0.2 m tall.
The air bubble has a diameter of 0.01 m.
The air bubble is initially centered at position (0.025, 0.025) with the vertical position being 0.025 m from the bottom of the column.
The liquid phase is water with density of 1000 kg/m³ and kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m²/s.
The air phase has density of 1.2 kg/m³ and kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m²/s.
The surface tension coefficient between air and water phases is 0.072 N/m.
Use a Volume of Fluid (VOF) solver for the two-phase flow simulation.
The simulation should be set up in 2D geometry.
