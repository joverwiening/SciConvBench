Simulate compressible flow of air through a heated porous block in steady-state.
The computational domain is a 2D channel that is 0.2 m long and 0.05 m high.
The porous block extends from x=0.08 to x=0.12 m, making it 0.04 m long.
The porous block width spans the full channel height of 0.05 m.
For the 2D simulation, the height in the z-direction is effectively 1 cell thick.
The inlet total pressure is 101325 Pa and the inlet total temperature is 300 K.
The porous block has a volumetric heat source of 1e6 W/m³ applied to it.
The porosity of the porous block should be set to a reasonable assumption based on best practices.
The outlet boundary conditions are pressure fixed at 100000 Pa, temperature using inletOutlet with inletValue 300 K, and velocity using inletOutlet.
The simulation should be steady-state and handle compressible flow of air.
