Model natural convection in a differentially heated square cavity.
The cavity is a 1m x 1m square.
The left wall is hot at 310 K.
The right wall is cold at 290 K.
The top and bottom walls are adiabatic with zeroGradient boundary condition for temperature.
The top and bottom walls have no-slip boundary conditions for velocity with fixedValue uniform (0 0 0).
The fluid is air.
