The domain is rectangular extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m in the streamwise direction and y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m in the cross-stream direction and z = -1 to 1 m.

Use startTime = 0 s, endTime = 0.5 s, deltaT = 0.0025 s.
Write results every 0.05 s.

The viscosity is 0.01 m²/s.
Assume a reasonable density value based on best practices.
Apply inlet boundary condition at x = -5 m with U = (1 0 0) m/s.
Apply outlet boundary condition at x = 5 m with U zeroGradient and p = 0 (reference).
Apply top wall boundary condition at y = 2.5 m with U noSlip and p zeroGradient.
Apply bottom wall boundary condition at y = -1.5 m with U noSlip and p zeroGradient.
Apply cylinder surface boundary condition with U noSlip and p zeroGradient.
Apply empty boundary conditions at front and back faces (z = ±1 m) for 2D simulation.
