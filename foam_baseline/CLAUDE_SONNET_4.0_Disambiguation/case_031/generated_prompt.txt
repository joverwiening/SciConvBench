Simulate a 2D buoyant cavity using the buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam solver for a steady-state solution.
The cavity is rectangular with dimensions 1 m (width) x 0.25 m (height).
The out-of-plane thickness is 0.01 m for the 2D simulation.
The hot wall is at the bottom with temperature 310 K.
The cold wall is at the top with temperature 290 K.
The left and right side walls have zeroGradient temperature conditions.
The fluid properties are density 1000 kg/m³, kinematic viscosity 1e-6 m²/s, Prandtl number 7.0, thermal expansion coefficient 2.0e-4 1/K, and reference temperature 300 K.
All walls have no-slip boundary conditions for velocity.
All walls use fixedFluxPressure boundary conditions for the p_rgh field.
The initial velocity is (0 0 0) everywhere.
The initial temperature is 300 K everywhere.
The initial pressure is 0 everywhere.
Use a mesh resolution of 80 cells in x-direction, 20 cells in y-direction, and 1 cell in z-direction.
