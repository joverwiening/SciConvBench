Use the steady‑state incompressible solver simpleFoam (OpenFOAM).  
Apply the standard k‑epsilon RAS turbulence model.  
Set the inlet velocity to a uniform (10 0 0) m s⁻¹.  
Specify inlet turbulent kinetic energy k as a fixedValue uniform 0.0375.  
Specify inlet turbulent dissipation rate ε as a fixedValue uniform 0.875.  
Apply zeroGradient for velocity U at the outlet.  
Set pressure p at the outlet to a fixedValue uniform 0.  
Apply zeroGradient for k and ε at the outlet.  
Use noSlip condition for velocity U on all walls (upperWall, lowerWall, and the step surface).  
Apply zeroGradient for pressure p on all walls.  
Use kqRWallFunction for turbulent kinetic energy k on all walls.  
Use epsilonWallFunction for turbulent dissipation rate ε on all walls.  
Assume a step height H of 0.028 m.  
Set the upstream length to 3 H (0.084 m).  
Set the downstream length to 20 H (0.56 m).  
Set the overall channel height to 2 H (0.056 m).  
The resulting Reynolds number based on inlet velocity and step height is approximately 2.8 × 10⁴.
