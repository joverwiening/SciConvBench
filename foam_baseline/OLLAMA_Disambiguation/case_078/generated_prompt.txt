Model sloshing in a half‑filled tank using interFoam.  
Tank dimensions: length 1.0 m (x), width 0.6 m (y), height 0.1 m (z).  
Use a 2‑D‑in‑x‑y simulation with a single cell in the z‑direction (200 × 120 × 1 cells, Δx≈0.005 m, Δy≈0.005 m, Δz=0.1 m).  
Simulate for 10 s (endTime = 10) with an initial time step of 1 × 10⁻³ s, adjustTimeStep = yes, and maxCo = 0.5.  
Gravity vector g = (0 ‑9.81 0) m s⁻².  
Initial water surface is tilted: water depth 0.25 m at x = 0 m increasing linearly to 0.35 m at x = 1 m, approximated by a staircase of slices.  
Use water properties ρ = 998 kg m⁻³, μ = 1.0 × 10⁻³ Pa·s; air properties ρ = 1.225 kg m⁻³, μ = 1.81 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s.  
Boundary conditions: left, right, and bottom walls – U = noSlip, p_rgh = fixedFluxPressure, alpha.water = zeroGradient; top (open) – U = pressureInletOutletVelocity, p_rgh = totalPressure with p0 0, alpha.water = inletOutlet (inletValue 0).  
Set the initial velocity field U to zero everywhere.
