Set up a counter-flow diffusion flame simulation using reactingFoam. Place the fuel nozzle at the top of the domain and the air nozzle at the bottom. Use pure methane (CH4) as the fuel species. Use the GRI‑Mech 3.0 detailed reaction mechanism. Define a computational domain height of 0.02 m. Specify a uniform inlet velocity of (0  -0.5  0) m/s for the methane stream at the top nozzle. Specify a uniform inlet velocity of (0  0.5  0) m/s for the air stream at the bottom nozzle. Set the inlet temperature of both the methane and air streams to 300 K.
