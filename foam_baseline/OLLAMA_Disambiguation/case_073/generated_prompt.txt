Set up a steady‑state incompressible pipe flow case using the simpleFoam solver.  
The pipe is 2 m long with an internal diameter of 0.04 m.  
The fluid is air with kinematic viscosity ν = 1.5 × 10⁻⁵ m² s⁻¹ and density ρ = 1.225 kg m⁻³.  
Apply a uniform inlet velocity U = (10 0 0) m s⁻¹ as a fixedValue.  
Set the turbulence intensity at the inlet to 3 % and compute the inlet turbulence kinetic energy k = 0.135 m² s⁻² (fixedValue).  
Compute the inlet specific dissipation rate ω from a mixing‑length estimate using a mixing length of 0.0028 m.  
At the outlet set pressure p = fixedValue uniform 0 and apply zeroGradient for U, k, and ω.  
At the pipe wall use a noSlip condition for U, kqRWallFunction for k, and omegaWallFunction for ω.  
Generate the mesh with blockMesh using an O‑grid: 200 cells along the pipe length (axial direction), 25 cells across the radius, and 1 cell in the 5° wedge, with appropriate grading to refine cells near the wall.  
Use the k‑omega SST turbulence model for the simulation.
