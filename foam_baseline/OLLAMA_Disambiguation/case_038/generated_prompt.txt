Simulate heat transfer with natural convection of air in a porous block.  
The block dimensions are 0.10 m wide, 0.10 m high, and 0.01 m deep.  
The bottom surface temperature is fixed at 310 K and the top surface temperature is fixed at 290 K.  
All side walls are treated as insulated (adiabatic) using a zero‑gradient temperature condition.  
Assume a porosity of 0.4 for the porous material.  
Assume a permeability of 1 × 10⁻¹⁰ m² for the porous block.  
Use standard air properties evaluated at the average temperature (≈300 K).  
Apply the Darcy‑Brinkman‑Forchheimer momentum model and the energy equation with effective thermal conductivity for the porous medium.  
Impose no‑slip velocity condition at solid boundaries.
