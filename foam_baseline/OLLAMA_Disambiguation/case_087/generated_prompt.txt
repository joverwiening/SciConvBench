Set up a transient, inviscid supersonic flow simulation over a forward‑facing step with an inlet Mach number of 3.  
Specify the inlet total temperature as 840 K and the inlet total pressure as 3.7 MPa.  
Define the computational domain as a rectangular channel 3 m long in the x‑direction and 1 m high in the y‑direction.  
Place a forward‑facing step of height 0.2 m starting at x = 0.6 m on the bottom wall.  
Apply a slip wall boundary condition on the top wall, the bottom wall, and all step surfaces.  
At the outlet, use a waveTransmissive condition for pressure with pInf = 101 325 Pa and a zeroGradient (or waveTransmissive) condition for velocity and temperature.  
Create a mesh with approximately 150 cells in the x‑direction and 50 cells in the y‑direction for the whole domain, adding local refinement near the step corner and along the expected shock paths.  
Run the simulation as a transient calculation for a total physical time of 0.005 seconds.
