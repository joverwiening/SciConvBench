Create a rectangular computational domain spanning x = 0 to 0.02 m, y = -0.01 to 0.01 m, and z = -0.01 to 0.01 m.  
Name the left boundary (x = 0) “fuel” and the right boundary (x = 0.02) “air”.  
Name the top and bottom boundaries “outlet”.  
Set the front and back faces as empty (zeroGradient).  
At the fuel inlet, inject methane (CH₄) with a temperature of 293 K and a velocity of 0.1 m s⁻¹ directed into the domain.  
At the air inlet, supply oxidizer as air with mass fraction Y_O₂ = 0.23, balance N₂, temperature 293 K, and a velocity of –0.1 m s⁻¹ directed into the domain.  
Initialize the entire domain temperature to 2000 K at time zero.  
Use a reference pressure of 101325 Pa for the simulation.  
Apply an outlet pressure of 101325 Pa at the top and bottom boundaries.  
Set the total pressure at both the fuel and air inlet boundaries to 101325 Pa.  
Use the reactingFoam solver with laminar momentum transport.  
Employ compressible finite‑rate chemistry with hePsiThermo, coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture mixing, Sutherland transport, and JANAF thermodynamics.  
Use the EulerImplicit chemistry type and Rosenbrock43 ODE solver.  
Run the simulation for a total physical time of 0.5 s.
