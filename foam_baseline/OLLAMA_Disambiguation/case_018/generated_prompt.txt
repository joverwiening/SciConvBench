The simulation domain is a rectangular box spanning x from 0 to 0.02 m, y from –0.01 m to 0.01 m, and z from –0.01 m to 0.01 m. The mesh uses 100 cells in the x‑direction, 40 cells in the y‑direction, and 1 cell in the z‑direction. The reactingFoam solver is used with compressible finite‑rate chemistry, hePsiThermo thermodynamics, coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture mixing, Sutherland transport, and JANAF thermodynamic data. Chemistry is solved with the EulerImplicit type and the Rosenbrock43 ODE solver. The flow is laminar with no turbulence model. The simulation runs with a constant time step of 1 e‑4 s, ending at 0.5 s, and results are written every 0.05 s. The initial temperature of the domain is 2000 K and the initial pressure is atmospheric, approximately 101 kPa, uniform throughout the domain. The initial velocity field is zero everywhere. At the fuel inlet (x = 0) a velocity of 0.1 m s⁻¹ is imposed with pure CH₄ (mass fraction = 1) entering. At the air inlet (x = 0.02) a velocity of –0.1 m s⁻¹ is imposed with mass fractions Y_O₂ = 0.23 and Y_N₂ = 0.77 (balance N₂). The initial composition of the interior domain is set to the same oxidizer mixture as the air inlet (Y_O₂ ≈ 0.23, Y_N₂ ≈ 0.77) with zero CH₄. The top and bottom faces (y‑direction boundaries) are pressure‑outlet (outlet) boundaries at atmospheric pressure. The front and back faces (z‑direction boundaries) are defined as empty patches. All other solver and numerical settings follow the default reactingFoam configuration appropriate for the specified models.
