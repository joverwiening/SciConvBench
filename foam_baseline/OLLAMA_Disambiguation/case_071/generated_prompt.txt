Simulate a 2D lid-driven cavity flow with a moving top wall.  
The cavity is a square with length and height equal to 0.1 m.  
Assume an incompressible fluid such as water with density ≈ 1000 kg m⁻³ and kinematic viscosity 0.01 m² s⁻¹, giving Reynolds number 10 for a top‑wall speed of 1 m s⁻¹.  
Use a uniform Cartesian mesh of 20 cells in the x‑direction, 20 cells in the y‑direction, and a single cell in the depth (20 × 20 × 1).  
Apply a fixed time step Δt = 0.005 s and run the simulation from t = 0 s to t = 0.5 s.  
Set the top wall to move in the positive x‑direction with a velocity of 1 m s⁻¹; all other walls are stationary no‑slip boundaries.
