Simulate 2D flow past a circular cylinder of diameter 2 m centered at the origin in a rectangular domain extending from x = ‑5 m to x = 5 m, y = ‑1.5 m to y = 2.5 m, and z = ‑1 m to z = 1 m.  
Use the pimpleFoam solver for incompressible unsteady flow.  
Set startTime = 0 s, endTime = 0.5 s, deltaT = 0.0025 s, and write results every 0.05 s.  
Initialize the fields with U = (0 0 0) m s⁻¹ and p = 0 Pa.  
Use fluid density ρ = 1000 kg m⁻³ and kinematic viscosity ν = 0.01 m² s⁻¹.  
Apply a generalized‑Newtonian cross‑power‑law viscosity model with ν = 0.01 m² s⁻¹, ν₍∞₎ = 10, m = 0.4 and n = 3.  
At the inlet (x = ‑5 m) impose a velocity U = (1 0 0) m s⁻¹.  
At the outlet (x = 5 m) use a zero‑gradient condition for velocity and fix pressure to zero.  
On the cylinder surface apply a no‑slip condition for velocity and a zero‑gradient condition for pressure.  
On the top (y = 2.5 m) and bottom (y = ‑1.5 m) boundaries apply a no‑slip condition for velocity and a zero‑gradient condition for pressure.  
On the front (z = ‑1 m) and back (z = 1 m) faces use the empty boundary condition.  
Treat the simulation as laminar flow with no turbulence model.
