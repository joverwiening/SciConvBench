Run a buoyant cavity case with conjugate heat transfer using chtMultiRegionFoam.  
Create a fluid cavity of dimensions 1.0 m × 1.0 m × 0.01 m.  
Place a central solid block of dimensions 0.4 m × 0.4 m × 0.01 m centered within the cavity.  
Fill the fluid cavity with air modeled as a compressible perfect gas.  
Apply fixed temperature boundary conditions: left wall 310 K, right wall 290 K, top wall adiabatic (zero‑gradient), bottom wall adiabatic (zero‑gradient).  
Use a copper‑like material for the solid with thermal conductivity 401 W m⁻¹ K⁻¹.  
Apply a gravity vector of (0 -9.81 0) m s⁻².  
Set the initial fluid temperature to 300 K and reference pressure p_rgh to 0 Pa; set the initial solid temperature to 300 K.  
Apply a no‑slip velocity condition (fixedValue (0 0 0)) on all fluid cavity walls.  
Simulate until an end time of 50 s with an initial time step of 0.01 s.  
Use the RAS k‑omega SST turbulence model for the fluid region.  
Define an appropriate mesh resolution for the fluid and solid regions (to be specified).
