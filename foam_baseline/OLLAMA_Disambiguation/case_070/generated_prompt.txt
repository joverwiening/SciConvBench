Apply a zero‑gradient condition for velocity at the outlet and a fixed pressure value of 0 Pa at the outlet. Use the steady‑state standard k‑omega SST RAS turbulence model. Specify a turbulence intensity of approximately 5 % at the inlet and compute the corresponding turbulence variables (k and ω) using standard best‑practice formulas.
