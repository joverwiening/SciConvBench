The simulation domain is a square cavity with dimensions 1 unit in the x-direction and 1 unit in the y-direction and a thickness of 0.1 units in the z-direction, effectively making it a 2D problem.  
The computational grid consists of 20 cells in the x-direction, 20 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.  
The top wall, named movingWall, moves in the x-direction with a uniform velocity of 1 m/s.  
All other walls, named fixedWalls, have a no‑slip boundary condition (velocity equal to zero).  
The front and back faces are designated as empty.  
The fluid kinematic viscosity (nu) is constant at 1e‑05 m²/s.  
The turbulence model used is the k‑epsilon model.  
The turbulence kinetic energy (k) is initialized uniformly to 1.5e‑4 in the domain and uses the kqRWallFunction on all walls (front/back empty).  
The turbulence dissipation rate (epsilon) is initialized uniformly to 1.4e‑4 in the domain and uses the epsilonWallFunction on all walls (front/back empty).  
The turbulent viscosity (nut) is initialized uniformly to 0 and uses the nutkWallFunction on all walls (front/back empty).  
The solver employed is pisoFoam.  
The velocity field is initialized to zero at the beginning of the simulation.  
The convection scheme for the momentum equation is div(phi,U) Gauss limitedLinearV 1.  
The convection scheme for the k equation is div(phi,k) Gauss limitedLinear 1.  
The convection scheme for the epsilon equation is div(phi,epsilon) Gauss limitedLinear 1.  
The viscous term uses the scheme div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear.  
The total simulation time is 10 time units.  
The time step size is 0.005 time units.  
Results are written every 100 time steps.  
A zeroGradient pressure boundary condition is applied on all walls.  
The fluid density is assumed to be 1 kg·m⁻³.
