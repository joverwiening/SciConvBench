Q: What thermal diffusivity (α) or thermal conductivity (k) should be used for the air in the simulation?
A: **Thermal diffusivity (α):**  
\[
\alpha = \frac{\nu}{\text{Pr}} = \frac{1.5\times10^{-5}\ \text{m}^2\!\!/\text{s}}{0.71}
      \approx 2.1\times10^{-5}\ \text{m}^2\!\!/\text{s}
\]

**Thermal conductivity (k):**  
The complete requirement does not give a value for \(k\).  
Make a reasonable assumption based on typical air properties (e.g., \(c_p \approx 1005\ \text{J kg}^{-1}\!\text{K}^{-1}\), \(\rho_0 = 1\ \text{kg m}^{-3}\)):

\[
k \approx \rho_0\,c_p\,\alpha \approx 1 \times 1005 \times 2.1\times10^{-5}
   \approx 0.021\ \text{W m}^{-1}\!\text{K}^{-1}
\]
