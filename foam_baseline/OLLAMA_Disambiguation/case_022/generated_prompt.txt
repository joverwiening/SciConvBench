Simulate incompressible unsteady flow past a circular cylinder using pimpleFoam.  
The computational domain is a rectangular box extending from x = -5 m to 5 m, y = -1.5 m to 2.5 m, and z = -1 m to 1 m.  
A cylinder of diameter 2 m is centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m, z = 0 m).  
Time settings: startTime = 0 s, endTime = 0.5 s, deltaT = 0.0025 s, and results are written every 0.05 s.  
Initial fields: velocity U = (0 0 0) m/s and pressure p = 0 Pa throughout the domain.  
Inlet (left) boundary: velocity U = (1 0 0) m/s and pressure p = zeroGradient.  
Outlet (right) boundary: velocity U = zeroGradient and pressure p = 0 (reference pressure).  
Top and bottom (y‑direction) boundaries: no‑slip velocity U = (0 0 0) m/s and pressure p = zeroGradient.  
Cylinder surface: no‑slip velocity U = (0 0 0) m/s and pressure p = zeroGradient.  
Front and back (z‑direction) boundaries: empty boundary condition.  
Laminar model: generalizedNewtonian with the cross‑power‑law viscosity model.  
Cross‑power‑law viscosity parameters: nuInf = 10, m = 0.4, n = 3.  
Mesh: structured mesh with a far‑field cell size of about 0.02 m (≈ 500 cells in the x‑direction and ≈ 200 cells in the y‑direction) and refined to a cell size of about 0.005 m in a band around the cylinder (≈ 0.5 D from the surface, ≈ 400 cells) to capture the boundary layer, resulting in a total cell count on the order of 10⁵ cells.
