Simulate an oblique shock reflection of a supersonic flow over a wedge.  
Use an upstream Mach number of 2.0.  
Set the upstream static pressure to 101 kPa and the static temperature to 288 K.  
Define the wedge by a top wall that runs from (0 m, 0.10 m) to (1 m, 0.20 m), giving a wedge angle of approximately 5.7°.  
Create a computational domain that is 1.0 m long in the x‑direction and whose height varies linearly from 0.10 m at the inlet (x = 0) to 0.20 m at the outlet (x = 1 m).  
Solve the Navier‑Stokes equations with simpleFoam, including viscous effects.  
Employ the RAS k‑Omega SST turbulence model with wall functions.  
Apply a fixed‑value inlet velocity of U = (5 0 0) m s⁻¹ (5 m/s in the +x direction).  
Apply the inlet static pressure of 101 kPa and static temperature of 288 K.  
Apply an outlet pressure boundary condition of 101 kPa (with zero‑gradient conditions for other variables).
