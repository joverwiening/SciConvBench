The computational domain extends from 0 to 1 m in the x, y, and z directions.  
A uniform mesh of 32 cells in each direction is used, giving a cell size of 0.03125 m.  
Gravity is set to (0 0 -9.81) m s⁻².  
The interFoam solver is employed for a laminar two‑phase VOF simulation.  
Water has density 1000 kg m⁻³ and kinematic viscosity 1.0e‑6 m² s⁻¹; air has density 1 kg m⁻³ and kinematic viscosity 1.48e‑5 m² s⁻¹.  
Surface tension between the phases is 0.07 N m⁻¹.  
Initially the velocity field U is (0 0 0) everywhere for both phases.  
The water volume fraction α.water is set to 1 inside the rectangular prism from (0 0 0) to (0.6 0.1875 0.75) and 0 elsewhere.  
An internal rectangular obstacle occupies the region from (0.375 0.375 0) to (0.625 0.625 0.25).  
All six domain faces are assigned wall boundary conditions with a no‑slip velocity (fixedValue U = (0 0 0)).  
The top face at z = 1 is defined as an open‑atmosphere patch; it uses a zero‑gradient condition for pressure and a zero‑gradient condition for velocity.  
The simulation uses a time step Δt = 1e‑3 s, runs to endTime = 0.1 s, and writes results every 0.02 s.
