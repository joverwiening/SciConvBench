Simulate a premixed methane‑air flame in a two‑dimensional channel using reactingFoam.  
The inlet velocity is U = (0.5 0 0) m/s.  
The inlet mixture is stoichiometric with an equivalence ratio of 1 (methane mole fraction ≈ 0.095).  
The inlet temperature is 300 K.  
The channel length is 0.1 m and the channel height is 0.02 m.  
The walls are adiabatic and the flow is laminar.  
Use the single‑step irreversible methane‑air mechanism defined in constant/combustionProperties as reaction = methaneAir with Arrhenius parameters A = 2.119 × 10¹¹, β = 0, Ta = 2.027 × 10⁸ K, and the janaf thermodynamic database.  
Apply a totalPressure condition for pressure with p₀ = 101 325 Pa at the outlet patch, and use zeroGradient for velocity, temperature, and all species at the outlet.  
Create a uniform Cartesian mesh with 100 cells in the x‑direction, 20 cells in the y‑direction, and 1 cell in the third direction, giving Δx = 0.001 m and Δy = 0.001 m.
