Run a pimpleFoam simulation of flow past a cylinder at Reynolds number 100.  
Set the cylinder diameter to 0.1 m.  
Set the fluid kinematic viscosity ν to 1 × 10⁻³ m²/s.  
Apply an inlet velocity U = (1 0 0) m s⁻¹ (1 m s⁻¹ in the x‑direction).  
Place the inlet 0.15 m upstream of the cylinder front.  
Place the outlet 1.95 m downstream of the cylinder rear.  
Use a domain height of 0.41 m with the cylinder centre at y = 0.20 m, giving a top boundary distance of 0.21 m and a bottom boundary distance of 0.20 m.
