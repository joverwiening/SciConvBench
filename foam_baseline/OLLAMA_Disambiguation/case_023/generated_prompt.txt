The computational domain is a rectangular box extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m, y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m, and z = -1 m to 1 m.  
A cylinder of diameter 2 m is centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).  
The inlet (left face) has a velocity U = (1 0 0) m/s.  
The outlet (right face) has a zero‑gradient condition for velocity and pressure p = 0 as reference.  
The top and bottom walls (up and down faces) have no‑slip velocity and zero‑gradient pressure.  
The cylinder wall has no‑slip velocity and zero‑gradient pressure.  
The back and front faces are empty, making the simulation two‑dimensional.  
The solver is pimpleFoam for incompressible unsteady flow.  
The simulation runs from startTime = 0 s to endTime = 0.5 s with a time step deltaT = 0.0025 s.  
Results are written every 0.05 s.  
The initial fields are a quiescent fluid with velocity U = (0 0 0) and pressure p = 0.  
The flow is laminar.  
The laminar viscosity model uses generalizedNewtonian with a vics viscosity model of crossPowerLaw, nuInf = 10, m = 0.4 and n = 3.  
The kinematic viscosity is set to 0.01 m^2/s.  
The fluid density is assumed to be 1000 kg/m^3 as a reasonable best‑practice choice.
