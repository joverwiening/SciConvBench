Simulate transient incompressible flow over a 2‑D cylinder of diameter D = 0.1 m at Reynolds number Re = 150.  
Set the free‑stream velocity to U = 1.5 m s⁻¹.  
Define the computational domain extending 10 D upstream (1.0 m), 25 D downstream (2.5 m), and 20 D vertically (2.0 m) from the cylinder center.  
Generate an O‑grid around the cylinder with a fine near‑wall mesh; use a first cell height of approximately 1 × 10⁻³ m to achieve y⁺≈1 and include several stretched layers covering the cylinder surface.  
Refine the mesh in the wake region downstream of the cylinder to capture vortex shedding.  
Run the simulation for a total physical time of 20 seconds to capture multiple shedding cycles.  
Use an adjustable time step and limit the Courant number to a maximum of 0.5 throughout the transient simulation.
