Simulate a 2‑D lid‑driven cavity flow with an incompressible fluid.  
The top wall moves to the right with a velocity of 1 m s⁻¹.  
Use a Reynolds number of 100 (Uwall = 1 m s⁻¹, L = 0.1 m, ν = 0.001 m² s⁻¹).  
Set the fluid density to 1 kg m⁻³.  
Run the simulation for a total physical time of 0.5 s using a time step of 0.002 s (250 time steps).  
Discretize the domain with a uniform 20 × 20 cell mesh.  
Initialize the velocity field to (0 0 0) m s⁻¹ everywhere.  
Apply no‑slip (fixedValue uniform (0 0 0)) velocity and zeroGradient pressure boundary conditions on the left, right, and bottom walls.  
Apply a fixedValue velocity of (1 0 0) m s⁻¹ and zeroGradient pressure on the moving top wall.  
Use the icoFoam solver with PISO pressure‑velocity coupling and the default first‑order implicit Euler time‑integration scheme.
