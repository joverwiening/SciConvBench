Create a cubic domain from (0,0,0) to (1,1,1) with a uniform 32×32×32 mesh.  
Place an internal solid obstacle occupying the region from (0.375,0.375,0) to (0.625,0.625,0.25) and apply wall boundary conditions (no‑slip velocity, zero‑gradient pressure and volume fraction) on its faces.  
Set gravity vector to (0 0 -9.81) m s⁻².  
Initialise the water phase with alpha.water = 1 inside the rectangular prism (0,0,0) to (0.6,0.1875,0.75) and alpha.water = 0 elsewhere; initialise velocity to (0 0 0) everywhere.  
Use water density 1000 kg m⁻³ and kinematic viscosity 1.0e‑6 m² s⁻¹; use air density 1 kg m⁻³ and kinematic viscosity 1.48e‑5 m² s⁻¹.  
Set the surface tension coefficient σ to 0.07 N m⁻¹ for the water‑air interface.  
Apply the following boundary conditions: top face (z=1) as open‑atmosphere (pressure‑inlet‑outlet for velocity, zero‑gradient for pressure and alpha.water); all other five faces as stationary walls (no‑slip velocity, zero‑gradient pressure and alpha.water).  
Use interFoam as the solver with PIMPLE pressure‑velocity coupling.  
Use Euler time discretisation, Gauss linear gradient scheme, Gauss linearUpwind grad(U) for momentum convection, Gauss vanLeer for alpha convection, Gauss linear corrected Laplacian scheme, and Gauss linear pressure‑gradient scheme.  
Set time step Δt = 1e‑3 s, endTime = 0.1 s, and writeInterval = 0.02 s.  
Set reference pressure pRef = 0 Pa at the cell located at (0.5,0.5,1).  
Use solver residual tolerance of 1e‑6 and appropriate under‑relaxation factors for stability.
