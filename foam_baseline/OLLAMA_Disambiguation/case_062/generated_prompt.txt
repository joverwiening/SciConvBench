The cylinder diameter is 1 m.  
The free‑stream velocity is uniform (3.9 0 0) m s⁻¹.  
The kinematic viscosity of the water is set to 1 × 10⁻⁶ m² s⁻¹.  
The simulation is two‑dimensional.  
The k‑omega SST RAS turbulence model is used.  
The computational domain is a rectangle extending from –10 D to 30 D in the streamwise (x) direction and from –10 D to +10 D in the vertical (y) direction, where D = 1 m.  
At the inlet, a uniform velocity (3.9 0 0) m s⁻¹ is applied with fixedValue turbulence quantities k = 5.7e‑3 m² s⁻² and omega = 3.6 s⁻¹.  
At the outlet, U, k, and omega have zeroGradient conditions and pressure p is fixedValue 0.  
The top and bottom boundaries (named topAndBottom) use symmetry boundary conditions for all fields.  
The simulation runs from startTime = 0 s to endTime = 100 s.  
Adjustable time stepping is used with a maximum Courant number maxCo = 1 and a maximum time‑step size maxDeltaT = 0.01 s.  
Results are written every 0.5 s.  
A C‑grid mesh generated with blockMesh is used, refined near the cylinder surface and in the wake to achieve wall‑y⁺ values between 30 and 100, with a total cell count of approximately 40 000.  
The transient solver to be used has not been specified.
