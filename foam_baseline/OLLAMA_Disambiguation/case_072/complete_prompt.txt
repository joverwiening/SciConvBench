Simulate steady-state incompressible turbulent flow over a backward-facing step in a 2D channel.

Solver: simpleFoam

Geometry: 2D channel with a backward-facing step:
- Upstream channel height: 0.02 m
- Downstream channel height: 0.04 m (step height 0.02 m)
- Step located at x = 0.2 m
- Total length: 1.0 m
Out-of-plane thickness: 0.01 m (front/back empty).

Mesh: blockMesh multi-block with local refinement near the step corner and reattachment region.
Target ~ (200 80 1) cells overall (refine to resolve the shear layer).

Physical properties: kinematic viscosity nu = 1.5e-5 m^2/s (air-like), in constant/transportProperties.

Turbulence: RAS kEpsilon (constant/momentumTransport) with wall functions.

Boundary conditions:
- inlet:
  - U fixedValue (0.5 0 0) m/s
  - p zeroGradient
  - turbulence (I=5%, length scale Lt=0.01 m):
    k = 1.5*(I*U)^2 = 1.5*(0.05*0.5)^2 = 9.375e-4
    epsilon = Cmu^0.75 * k^(3/2) / Lt  (Cmu=0.09) -> epsilon ~ 5.5e-3
- outlet:
  - p fixedValue 0
  - U zeroGradient
  - k, epsilon zeroGradient
- walls:
  - U noSlip
  - p zeroGradient
  - k kqRWallFunction
  - epsilon epsilonWallFunction
  - nut nutkWallFunction
- frontAndBack: empty

Solver settings (fvSolution): SIMPLE with relaxation (p 0.3, U 0.7, k/epsilon 0.7) and residualControl (~1e-6).
Run control: endTime 2000; deltaT 1; writeInterval 200.
