Set up a laminar counter-flow diffusion flame with reactingFoam in a 2-D opposed-jet configuration. The domain is a rectangular domain spanning x from 0 to 0.02 m, y from –0.01 to 0.01 m, and z from –0.01 to 0.01 m. Use 100 grid points in the x direction, 40 in the y direction, and 1 cell in the z direction. Name the left boundary (x = 0) as fuel and the right boundary (x = 0.02) as air. Name the top and bottom boundaries as outlet. Set the front and back faces as empty. At the fuel inlet, inject methane with a mass fraction of 0.055 and a temperature of approximately 293 K. At the air inlet, supply oxidizer as air with Y_O2 = 0.23 and the balance N2 at 293 K. Initialize the domain with a temperature of 2000 K at time 0. Set the velocity at the fuel boundary to 0.1 m s⁻¹ and at the air boundary to –0.1 m s⁻¹. Use chemistry type EulerImplicit and ODE solver Rosenbrock43. Use laminar combustion model and laminar momentum transport. Use compressible finite-rate chemistry with hePsiThermo, coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture mixing, Sutherland transport, and JANAF thermodynamics. Use the reactingFoam solver.
