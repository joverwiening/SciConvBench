The rectangular domain spans x from 0 to 0.02 m, y from –0.01 to 0.01 m, and z from –0.01 to 0.01 m.  
The mesh uses 100 cells in the x direction, 40 cells in the y direction, and 1 cell in the z direction.  
The left boundary at x = 0 is named “fuel” and uses a CH₄ inlet with temperature 293 K, velocity 0.1 m s⁻¹, pressure 101 325 Pa, and pure methane mass fraction (Y_CH₄ = 1).  
The right boundary at x = 0.02 m is named “air” and uses an air inlet with temperature 293 K, velocity –0.1 m s⁻¹, pressure 101 325 Pa, and mass‑fraction composition Y_O₂ = 0.23, Y_N₂ ≈ 0.77.  
The top and bottom boundaries are outlet (pressure‑outlet) faces with zero‑gradient (Neumann) conditions for all variables and static pressure fixed at 101 325 Pa.  
The front and back faces are assigned the “empty” boundary condition type.  
The initial condition for the whole domain sets temperature uniformly to 2000 K, velocity to (0, 0, 0) m s⁻¹, pressure to 101 325 Pa, and species mass fractions to Y_O₂ = 0.23, Y_N₂ ≈ 0.77, Y_CH₄ = 0.  
The simulation uses the reactingFoam solver with compressible finite‑rate chemistry, hePsiThermo thermodynamics, coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture mixing, Sutherland transport, and JANAF thermodynamic data.  
Chemistry is integrated with the EulerImplicit scheme and the Rosenbrock43 ODE solver.  
Momentum transport is modeled as laminar flow.  
The time step is 1 × 10⁻⁴ s, the simulation runs to an end time of 0.5 s, and results are written every 0.05 s.
