Set up a laminar counter-flow diffusion flame using reactingFoam in a 2-D opposed-jet configuration.  
Create a rectangular domain spanning x from 0 to 0.02 m, y from –0.01 to 0.01 m, and z from –0.01 to 0.01 m.  
Discretize the domain with 100 cells in the x direction, 40 cells in the y direction, and 1 cell in the z direction.  
Name the left boundary (x = 0) “fuel” and the right boundary (x = 0.02 m) “air”.  
Name the top and bottom boundaries “outlet”.  
Set the front and back faces as empty.  
At the fuel inlet, inject pure methane (CH₄) at a temperature of approximately 293 K with an inlet velocity of 0.1 m s⁻¹.  
At the air inlet, inject air composed of O₂ (mass fraction Y_O₂ = 0.23) and N₂ (balance) at a temperature of approximately 293 K with an inlet velocity of –0.1 m s⁻¹ (directed opposite to the fuel flow).  
Use atmospheric pressure of 101325 Pa as the reference pressure.  
Employ the GRI‑Mech 3.0 chemical mechanism for methane combustion.  
Select EulerImplicit as the chemistry type and Rosenbrock43 as the ODE solver.  
Configure the combustion model and momentum transport as laminar.  
Use compressible finite‑rate chemistry with hePsiThermo, coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture mixing, Sutherland transport, and JANAF thermodynamics.  
Run the simulation with reactingFoam.  
Set the time step to 1e‑4 s, the end time to 0.5 s, and write results every 0.05 s.
