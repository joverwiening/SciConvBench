Simulate a lid-driven cavity flow with a top wall moving at 1 m s⁻¹.  
The cavity dimensions are 0.1 m length and 0.1 m height.  
Use a fluid with kinematic viscosity 0.01 m² s⁻¹, density 1 kg m⁻³, giving Reynolds number ≈10.  
Perform a transient simulation for a total physical time of 0.5 s.  
Use a time‑step size of 0.0005 s.  
Employ a uniform Cartesian mesh of 20 cells in the x‑direction, 20 cells in the y‑direction, and 1 cell in the z‑direction.  
Save the velocity (U) and pressure (p) fields every 20 time steps (0.01 s) with writeControl set to timeStep and writeInterval set to 20.  
Apply fixedValue (0 0 0) velocity and zeroGradient pressure on the bottom, left, and right walls (fixedWalls patch).  
Apply appropriate boundary conditions on the top moving wall (lid) for velocity and pressure.
