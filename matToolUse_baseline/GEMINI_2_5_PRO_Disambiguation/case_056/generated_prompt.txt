Calculate the density of states for Cadmium Sulfide (CdS).
The material has a wurtzite structure with space group P6_3mc and 4 atoms per primitive cell.
Retrieve a primitive starting structure for wurtzite CdS consistent with the provided metadata.
Use the provided local LDA pseudopotential files: PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/cd.upf and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/s.upf.
The calculation workflow consists of six steps in order: vc-relax, scf, nscf, a bands calculation, band post-processing, and DOS post-processing.
The final output is the density of states; the band structure does not need to be explicitly returned.
All calculations must achieve a total energy convergence of 1 meV per atom.
Use a gamma-centered k-point grid for all relevant calculations.
Determine and use a suitable plane-wave kinetic energy cutoff (ecutwfc) based on best practices to meet the energy convergence requirement.
Determine and use a suitable k-point grid sampling for the vc-relax and scf calculations based on best practices to meet the energy convergence requirement.
Determine and use a suitable, denser k-point grid sampling for the nscf calculation based on best practices.
Determine and use a suitable high-symmetry k-point path for the bands calculation based on best practices for the wurtzite structure.
Determine and use a suitable value for the 'occupations' parameter based on best practices.
Determine and use a suitable force convergence threshold for the vc-relax calculation based on best practices.
Determine and use a suitable electronic convergence threshold (conv_thr) for the self-consistent calculations based on best practices.
Determine and use a suitable minimum energy for the density of states calculation, relative to the valence band maximum, based on best practices.
