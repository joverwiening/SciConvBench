Perform a variable-cell relaxation followed by a self-consistent field calculation for alpha-quartz (SiO2).
Obtain the initial crystal structure for alpha-quartz (trigonal, P3_121) from a standard crystallographic database.
Use the LDA pseudopotentials PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/si.upf and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/o.upf.
Determine the plane-wave kinetic energy cutoff (ecutwfc) by converging the total energy of the initial structure to a precision of 1 meV/atom.
Set the charge density cutoff (ecutrho) to 4 times the converged ecutwfc.
Using the converged ecutwfc, determine the k-point sampling by converging the total energy of the initial structure to a precision of 1 meV/atom.
Use an automatic Monkhorst-Pack grid with a half shift for k-point sampling.
Perform a variable-cell relaxation (vc-relax) using the converged ecutwfc and k-point grid.
For the vc-relax calculation, use etot_conv_thr = 1.0e-8, forc_conv_thr = 1.0e-7, and conv_thr = 1.0e-8.
Use a pressure convergence threshold of 0.5 kbar for the vc-relax calculation.
After the vc-relax is complete, perform a self-consistent field (scf) calculation on the final relaxed structure.
The scf calculation should use the same ecutwfc, ecutrho, k-point grid, and conv_thr as the relaxation.
Return the fully relaxed structure and the final total energy from the scf calculation.
