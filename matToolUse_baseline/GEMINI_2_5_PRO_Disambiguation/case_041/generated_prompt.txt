Calculate the density of states for BiFeO3.
The calculation will use the LDA exchange-correlation functional.
The pseudopotentials are PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/bi.upf, PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/fe.upf, and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/o.upf.
The starting structure is a perovskite BiFeO3 with space group R3c and 10 atoms per primitive cell.
The calculation must be spin-polarized.
Set the initial magnetic moment on the iron atoms to 5.0 Bohr magnetons in a ferromagnetic configuration.
Do not include a Hubbard U correction.
Use a plane-wave kinetic energy cutoff of 80 Ry.
The total energy convergence threshold for the structural relaxation is 1 meV per atom.
The force convergence threshold for the structural relaxation is 1.0e-4 Ry/au.
Use a gamma-centered 8x8x8 k-point grid for the vc-relax and scf steps.
Use Methfessel-Paxton smearing for electronic occupations.
Use a smearing width of 0.01 Ry.
The workflow consists of six steps in order: vc-relax, scf, nscf, bands calculation, band post-processing, and DOS post-processing.
For the nscf and DOS calculation, use a denser gamma-centered k-point grid of 16x16x16.
For the bands calculation, use a standard high-symmetry k-point path appropriate for the R3c space group.
The final output is the calculated density of states.
