The starting structure should be consistent with the formula NbSe2, a layered structure, and space group P6_3/mmc.
Set the total energy convergence threshold for the electronic minimization (etot_conv_thr) to 1.0e-8 Ry.
Set the force convergence threshold for the ionic relaxation (forc_conv_thr) to 1.0e-7 Ry/Bohr.
Set the electron density convergence threshold (conv_thr) to 1.0e-8.
Use Marzari-Vanderbilt smearing for occupations.
Set the smearing width (degauss) to a reasonable value for a metal, such as 0.01 Ry.

The kinetic energy cutoff for charge density (ecutrho) should be chosen based on the pseudopotential recommendations, typically 4 to 10 times the value of ecutwfc.
Use an automatically generated Monkhorst-Pack k-point grid with a half shift.
The k-point grid density should be chosen to converge the total energy to a reasonable precision, such as 1 meV/atom.
The calculation should be non-spin-polarized.
