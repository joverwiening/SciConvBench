Perform a variable-cell relaxation (vc-relax) for wurtzite ZnO.
Use the LDA pseudopotential files PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/zn.upf and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/o.upf.
Generate an initial wurtzite structure for ZnO consistent with space group P6_3mc and 4 atoms per primitive cell.
Determine a converged plane-wave kinetic energy cutoff (ecutwfc) by performing a convergence test.
Assume a reasonable energy convergence threshold in meV/atom for the ecutwfc convergence test based on best practices.
Assume a reasonable k-point grid for the ecutwfc convergence test based on best practices.
Determine a converged k-point grid by performing a convergence test using the converged ecutwfc.
Assume a reasonable energy convergence threshold in meV/atom for the k-point convergence test based on best practices.
Use the converged ecutwfc and k-point grid for the vc-relax calculation.
The k-point grid should be an automatic Monkhorst-Pack grid with a half shift.
Set the total energy convergence threshold (etot_conv_thr) to 1.0e-8 Ry for the vc-relax calculation.
Set the force convergence threshold (forc_conv_thr) to 1.0e-7 Ry/Bohr for the vc-relax calculation.
After the vc-relax is complete, perform a self-consistent field (scf) calculation on the fully relaxed structure.
Use the same converged ecutwfc, k-point grid, and pseudopotentials for the scf calculation.
Set the electronic convergence threshold (conv_thr) to 1.0e-8 Ry for the scf calculation.
Return the fully relaxed structure from the vc-relax calculation.
Return the final total energy from the scf calculation.
