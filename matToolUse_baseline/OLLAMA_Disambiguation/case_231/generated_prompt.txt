Retrieve a primitive PbTiO3 perovskite structure (space group P4mm) from any reliable crystallographic database.  
Use the LDA pseudopotential files pb.upf, ti.upf, and o.upf from PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard.  
Set the wavefunction kinetic‑energy cutoff ecutwfc to 60 Ry and the charge‑density cutoff ecutrho to 240 Ry.  
Employ an automatic Monkhorst‑Pack k‑point grid of 6 × 6 × 6 with a half‑shift.  
Apply Gaussian smearing with degauss = 0.01 Ry.  
Use convergence thresholds etot_conv_thr = 1.0e‑8, forc_conv_thr = 1.0e‑7, and conv_thr = 1.0e‑8.  
Perform a variable‑cell relaxation (vc‑relax) using the BFGS cell‑dynamics algorithm, with cell_dofree set to 'all' and press_conv_thr ≈ 0.5 kbar.  
After the vc‑relax finishes, run a self‑consistent field (scf) calculation on the relaxed structure with the same settings to obtain the final total energy.  
Return the fully relaxed structure and the final total energy.
