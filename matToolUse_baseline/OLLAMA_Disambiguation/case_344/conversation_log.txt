Q: What should the NSW (maximum number of ionic steps) be for the initial/final state relaxations and for the NEB calculation?
A: A reasonable choice, following the guidance in the complete requirement, is:

* **Initial‑state (IS) and final‑state (FS) relaxations:** NSW = 200 ionic steps.  
* **NEB calculation:** NSW = 500 ionic steps (enough to let the band converge even if the starting images are far from the minimum).

These values are commonly used for metal‑surface NEB jobs when the starting geometries may need extra relaxation. If you prefer a different limit, you can adjust them upward, but the numbers above satisfy the “larger number of NSW” suggestion while keeping the runs practical.
