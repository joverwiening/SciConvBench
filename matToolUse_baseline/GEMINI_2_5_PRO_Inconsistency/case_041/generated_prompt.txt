Calculate the density of states for BiFeO3.
The calculation workflow consists of six steps: vc-relax, scf, nscf, a bands calculation, band post-processing, and DOS post-processing.
The material is BiFeO3 with a perovskite structure, space group R3c, and 10 atoms per primitive cell.
A structure-retrieval tool will be used to obtain the initial primitive crystal structure.
Use the provided local LDA pseudopotential files: bi.upf, fe.upf, and o.upf.
The overall energy convergence target for the calculation is 1 meV per atom.
Determine the plane-wave kinetic energy cutoff (ecutwfc) by performing a convergence test to meet the 1 meV/atom energy convergence target.
The plane-wave charge density cutoff (ecutrho) will be set to four times the converged ecutwfc.
Determine the k-point grid density for the vc-relax and scf calculations by performing a convergence test to meet the 1 meV/atom energy convergence target.
Use a gamma-centered Monkhorst-Pack k-point grid with a half shift for all calculations.
For the vc-relax and scf calculations, use a total energy convergence threshold of 1e-5 Ry.
For the vc-relax calculation, use a force convergence threshold of 1e-4 Ry/bohr.
Set the initial magnetic ordering to G-type antiferromagnetic for the iron atoms.
Use Marzari-Vanderbilt smearing for the electronic occupations.
Set the smearing width (degauss) to 0.001 Ry.
Do not apply a Hubbard U correction.
Do not include spin-orbit coupling.
After vc-relax, perform a self-consistent field (scf) calculation on the relaxed structure using the converged ecutwfc and k-point grid.
After the scf calculation, perform a non-self-consistent field (nscf) calculation using a denser k-point grid suitable for DOS calculations.
Following the nscf step, perform a bands calculation along a high-symmetry path.
Perform post-processing to generate the band structure data.
Perform post-processing on the nscf results to calculate the final density of states.
