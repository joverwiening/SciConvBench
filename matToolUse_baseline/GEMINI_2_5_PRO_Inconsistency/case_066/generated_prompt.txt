Calculate the density of states for the material CoSb3.
The material has the formula CoSb3, the skutterudite structure, space group Im-3, and 8 atoms per primitive cell.
Retrieve the primitive starting structure for CoSb3 using a structure-retrieval tool.
Use the LDA pseudopotentials located at PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/co.upf and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/sb.upf.
The calculation must achieve a strict energy convergence of 1 meV per atom.
Follow this specific workflow: vc-relax, scf, nscf, a bands calculation, band post-processing, and DOS post-processing.
The nscf calculation result should be used as the input for the DOS post-processing step.
The band structure calculation does not need to be returned as a final result, but the step must be completed.
Use a gamma-centered k-point grid for the calculations.
Select a plane-wave kinetic energy cutoff (ecutwfc) based on best practices to achieve the required energy convergence.
Select a high-symmetry k-point path for the band structure calculation based on best practices for the material's crystal structure.
Select a force convergence threshold for the vc-relax calculation based on best practices.
Select a smearing type for electronic occupations based on best practices.
Select a smearing width (degauss) based on best practices.
Select a minimum and maximum energy for the density of states plot, relative to the Fermi energy, based on best practices.
All other unspecified numerical parameters, such as the charge density cutoff, k-point grid densities, and electronic convergence thresholds, should be chosen based on best practices to meet the 1 meV/atom energy convergence requirement.
