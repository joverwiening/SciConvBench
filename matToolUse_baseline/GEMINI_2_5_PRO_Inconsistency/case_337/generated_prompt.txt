EDIFF = 1E-5
EDIFFG = -0.05
ENCUT = 450
IBRION = 1
ISIF = 2
ISMEAR = 1
ISPIN = 1
IVDW = 11
LREAL = Auto
NSW = 200

First, perform the geometry optimization of the CO molecule.
Execute the command `python op.py CO POTCAR-CO POSCAR-CO`.
Second, perform the geometry optimization of the clean OsO2 surface.
Execute the command `python op.py surface POTCAR-OsO2face POSCAR-OsO2face`.
Third, perform the geometry optimization of the CO-adsorbed OsO2 system.
Execute the command `python op.py absorbed POTCAR-OsO2CO POSCAR-OsO2CO`.
Finally, calculate the adsorption energy.
Execute the command `bash ./calc_absorption_energy.sh`.
The final output should be the adsorption energy value printed by the `calc_absorption_energy.sh` script.
