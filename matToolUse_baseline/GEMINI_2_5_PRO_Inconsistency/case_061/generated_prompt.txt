Use the LDA pseudopotentials from the local files PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/mg.upf and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/b.upf.

The nscf calculation must use a k-point grid that is significantly denser than the scf grid to ensure a high-quality density of states.
