Perform a variable-cell relaxation calculation for CoSb3.
The initial structure is the primitive cell of skutterudite CoSb3 (space group Im-3), obtained from a structure-retrieval tool.
The calculation must be spin-polarized.
A non-zero initial magnetic moment will be set for the Cobalt atoms.
Use the provided LDA pseudopotentials: PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/co.upf and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/sb.upf.
A plane-wave kinetic energy cutoff (ecutwfc) will be chosen based on best practices for the selected pseudopotentials.
The charge density cutoff (ecutrho) will be set to a value appropriate for the chosen ecutwfc and pseudopotentials.
Use an automatic Monkhorst-Pack k-point grid.
The k-point grid must be gamma-centered and use a half shift.
The dimensions of the k-point grid will be chosen to ensure the total energy is converged.
Use a smearing method appropriate for metals, such as Marzari-Vanderbilt, with a small smearing width.
Set the total energy convergence threshold for the relaxation to 1.0e-8 Ry.
Set the force convergence threshold for the relaxation to 1.0e-7 Ry/Bohr.
Set the electronic self-consistency convergence threshold to 1.0e-8 Ry.
Set the pressure convergence threshold for the cell relaxation to a standard value such as 0.5 kbar.
After the variable-cell relaxation converges, perform a self-consistent field calculation on the relaxed structure.
The self-consistent field calculation must use settings consistent with the relaxation step, including the same ecutwfc and k-point grid.
The final output should be the fully relaxed crystal structure.
The final output should also include the total energy from the final self-consistent field calculation.
