Execute the following workflow in sequence: a variable-cell relaxation (vc-relax), a self-consistent field (scf) calculation, a non-self-consistent field (nscf) calculation, a bands calculation, band post-processing, and finally DOS post-processing.

Use an electronic convergence threshold for the scf steps that is consistent with the target energy accuracy.
For the nscf step, use a denser gamma-centered Monkhorst-Pack k-point grid than the one used in the scf step.
For the bands calculation, use a standard high-symmetry k-point path for the wurtzite crystal structure.
For the final DOS calculation, use a reasonable energy range that captures the relevant electronic features around the Fermi level.
