Retrieve the primitive crystal structure for NbSe2 corresponding to space group P6_3/mmc.

Use the retrieved structure as the starting configuration for a variable-cell relaxation calculation.

Set the total energy convergence threshold for the relaxation to 1.0e-8 Ry.
Set the force convergence threshold for the relaxation to 1.0e-7 Ry/Bohr.
Set the self-consistent field convergence threshold to 1.0e-8 Ry for all steps.
Use an automatic Monkhorst-Pack k-point grid with a half shift.
Select the plane-wave kinetic energy cutoff (ecutwfc) based on best practices for the provided pseudopotentials.
Select the k-point sampling density based on best practices to ensure convergence.
Select the type of smearing for electronic occupations based on best practices for a metallic system.
Select the smearing width (degauss) based on best practices.
After the variable-cell relaxation converges, perform a self-consistent field calculation on the final relaxed structure.
The final self-consistent field calculation will use settings consistent with the relaxation calculation.
The final output will be the fully relaxed crystal structure.
The final output will also include the total energy from the self-consistent field calculation on the relaxed structure.
