Calculate the speed of sound in an ideal gas mixture at 300 K.
Use a mixture composition of 70% helium and 30% argon by mole (x(He)=0.70, x(Ar)=0.30).
Assume ideal-gas behavior for the mixture.
Use γ = 5/3 (≈1.667) for both helium and argon (monatomic ideal gases at 300 K).
Assume the mixture pressure is 1 atm (101,325 Pa).
Report the resulting speed of sound in m/s.
