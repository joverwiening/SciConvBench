Find the torque required to rotate a disk of radius 0.2 m close to a stationary wall in a viscous fluid.
Use a disk–wall Couette flow model with one disk face parallel to the wall.
Use a uniform gap distance of 0.001 m between the disk face and the wall.
Use a fluid dynamic viscosity of 0.1 Pa·s.
Use a disk angular velocity of 2π rad/s (60 rpm).
Assume the disk is fully immersed.
Include only viscous shear in the 0.001 m gap to the wall.
