Calculate the pressure drop per unit length (ΔP/L) for flow through a packed bed.
Use the Ergun equation.
Assume the packed particles are spheres with diameter dp = 5 mm.
Assume particle sphericity = 1.0.
Use bed void fraction (porosity) ε = 0.4.
Use air as the fluid at standard atmospheric pressure and temperature.
Use air density ρ = 1.204 kg/m³.
Use air dynamic viscosity μ = 1.81 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s.
Use superficial velocity U = 0.5 m/s.
Report ΔP/L in units such as Pa/m.
No bed length/height is required because the result is per unit length.
