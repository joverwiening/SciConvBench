Calculate the boundary layer (boundary) work for a quasi-equilibrium (reversible) isothermal expansion of an ideal gas in a piston-cylinder device.
Use nitrogen with mass m = 2 kg.
Use an isothermal process at T = 300 K for both initial and final states.
Use initial pressure P1 = 500 kPa.
Use final pressure P2 = 100 kPa.
Compute volumes from the ideal-gas relation V = mRT/P.
Use the gas constant R = 0.2968 kJ/(kg·K).
Compute boundary work using Wb = ∫(V1 to V2) P dV with P = mRT/V throughout the process.
