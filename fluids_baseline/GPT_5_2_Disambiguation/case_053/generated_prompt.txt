Determine the torque measured by a rotational viscometer at 100 rpm.
Use a Newtonian fluid with dynamic viscosity μ = 0.5 Pa·s.
Use a Couette (concentric-cylinder) viscometer geometry.
Set the inner cylinder radius Ri = 0.020 m.
Set the outer cylinder inner radius Ro = 0.021 m (gap = 0.001 m).
Set the immersed height h = 0.050 m.
Assume the inner cylinder rotates at 100 rpm while the outer cylinder is stationary.
Ignore end effects and compute torque only from shear on the lateral cylindrical surface over height h.
Use the exact finite-gap concentric-cylinder Couette torque formula (not the narrow-gap approximation).
Report the final torque in N·m.
