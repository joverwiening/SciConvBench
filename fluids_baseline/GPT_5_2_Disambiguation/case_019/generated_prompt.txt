Solve the 1D steady heat conduction equation in a plane wall of thickness 0.20 m with uniform internal volumetric heat generation.
Use a Cartesian coordinate x with x = 0 at the wall midplane and surfaces at x = -0.10 m and x = +0.10 m.
Use thermal conductivity k = 15 W/(m·K).
Use uniform volumetric heat generation q''' = 2×10^5 W/m^3.
Apply Dirichlet boundary conditions T(-0.10 m) = 50°C and T(+0.10 m) = 50°C.
Report the steady temperature at the center T(0) as the required result.
Report temperatures in °C.
