Find the terminal velocity of a spherical raindrop falling through air.
Use a raindrop diameter of 2 mm, so radius r = 1 mm = 0.001 m.
Assume air density ρ_air = 1.2 kg/m³.
Assume a reasonable standard sea-level value for air viscosity since it is not specified.
Use the quadratic (high-Re) drag model with a constant drag coefficient C_d = 0.5.
Use water density ρ_water = 1000 kg/m³ for the raindrop.
Include buoyancy by using the effective weight (ρ_water − ρ_air) V g.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
