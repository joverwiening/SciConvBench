Q: What definition of drag coefficient should I use (e.g., \(C_D = \frac{F_D}{\tfrac12 \rho U^2 A}\) with \(A=\pi d^2/4\))?
A: Use the standard definition for a sphere:

\[
C_D=\frac{F_D}{\tfrac12 \rho U^2 A}, \quad A=\frac{\pi d^2}{4}.
\]

Then apply the creeping-flow (Stokes) analytical result, which gives \(C_D = \frac{24}{Re}\) for this definition.

Q: Do you want the answer reported as a numeric value at Re = 0.1, or left in the symbolic form \(C_D = 24/Re\)?
