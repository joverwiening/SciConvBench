Determine the drag coefficient of a sphere in creeping flow at Re = 0.1.
Use the standard sphere drag-coefficient definition \(C_D=\frac{F_D}{\tfrac12 \rho U^2 A}\) with \(A=\frac{\pi d^2}{4}\).
Apply the creeping-flow (Stokes) analytical result \(C_D=\frac{24}{Re}\) for this definition.
Report the result as a numeric value at \(Re=0.1\), giving \(C_D=240\).
