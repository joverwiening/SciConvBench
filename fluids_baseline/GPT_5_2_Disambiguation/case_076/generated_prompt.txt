Find the efficiency of a conical draft tube.
Use inlet water velocity V1 = 10 m/s.
Use outlet water velocity V2 = 2 m/s.
Use draft-tube efficiency based on pressure head recovery: η_dt = H_actual / H_th.
Define theoretical recovery exactly as H_th = (V1^2 − V2^2) / (2g).
Use actual pressure head recovery H_actual = 4.5 m of water.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s^2.
Do not use inlet diameter; it is unspecified and not required for the stated efficiency definition.
