Determine the Taylor number for Taylor–Couette flow between concentric cylinders with a 2 mm gap.
Use inner cylinder radius Rin = 0.05 m.
Use outer cylinder radius Rout = 0.052 m (implied by the 2 mm gap).
Assume the inner cylinder rotates at 100 rpm, using angular velocity ω ≈ 10.47 rad/s.
Assume dynamic viscosity μ = 0.01 Pa·s.
Assume fluid density ρ = 1000 kg/m³ to compute kinematic viscosity ν = μ/ρ.
Use the Taylor–Couette definition for inner cylinder rotating with outer cylinder stationary: Ta = (ω^2 Rin d^3) / (ν^2), with d = 0.002 m.
