Q: What is the kinematic viscosity of the fluid (in m²/s)?
A: The complete requirement gives the **dynamic viscosity** \( \mu = 0.01\ \text{Pa·s} \), but it does **not** provide the fluid density \( \rho \), so the **kinematic viscosity** can’t be uniquely determined.

Use:
\[
\nu = \frac{\mu}{\rho} = \frac{0.01}{\rho}\ \text{m}^2/\text{s}
\]

Make a reasonable assumption based on best practices (e.g., if you assume water-like density \( \rho \approx 1000\ \text{kg/m}^3 \), then \( \nu \approx 1.0\times 10^{-5}\ \text{m}^2/\text{s} \)).
