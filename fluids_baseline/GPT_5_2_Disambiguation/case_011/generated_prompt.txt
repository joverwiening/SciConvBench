Find the Biot number for a cooling steel sphere of radius 5 mm.
Use thermal conductivity k = 45 W/m·K.
Use convective heat transfer coefficient h = 150 W/m²·K for the surrounding oil bath.
Use the standard sphere characteristic length Lc = V/A = r/3.
Use r = 5 mm so Lc = 5/3 mm ≈ 1.67 mm = 1.67×10^-3 m.
