Calculate the degree of reaction for an axial flow compressor stage.
Use inlet relative velocity to the rotor W1 = 150 m/s.
Use rotor blade speed at mean radius U = 200 m/s.
Use inlet relative flow angle β1 = 45°.
Use outlet relative flow angle β2 = 30°.
Assume constant axial velocity through the stage with Va1 = Va2.
Use Va = W1 cos(β1) = 150 cos(45°) ≈ 106 m/s.
Assume no inlet prewhirl so the absolute inlet whirl component Vw1 = 0 m/s.
Use the degree of reaction definition R = Δh_rotor / Δh_stage, where rotor static enthalpy rise is divided by total stage enthalpy rise.
