Determine the dynamic viscosity of a fluid using a falling-ball viscometer with a 3 mm diameter steel ball.
Use fluid density 1000 kg/m³.
Assume room temperature (about 20–25 °C) and state the assumption explicitly.
Use measured terminal velocity 0.05 m/s.
Use steel ball density 7850 kg/m³.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Assume the viscometer tube is large enough that wall effects are negligible (effectively infinite diameter), so no wall-effect correction is applied.
Assume creeping-flow (Stokes) regime so Stokes’ law applies with no inertial (Reynolds-number) correction.
