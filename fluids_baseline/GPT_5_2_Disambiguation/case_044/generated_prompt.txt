Determine the Stefan number for an ice-to-water melting problem at 0°C.
Use the definition Ste = (c_p ΔT) / L_f.
Use c_p = 4.18 kJ/(kg·K) (liquid water for the sensible heating term).
Use ΔT = 10 K, interpreted as a surface at 10°C and a melting interface at 0°C.
Use L_f = 334 kJ/kg (latent heat of fusion at ~0°C).
