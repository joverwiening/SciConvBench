Find the critical insulation radius for a cylindrical pipe.
Use the standard critical radius relation for a cylinder: r_c = k/h.
Use insulation thermal conductivity k = 0.17 W/m·K.
Use external convective heat transfer coefficient h = 8 W/m²·K.
Report the result as the critical radius of insulation (not thickness).
