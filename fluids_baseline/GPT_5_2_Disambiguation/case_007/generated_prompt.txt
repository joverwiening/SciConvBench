Calculate steady-state heat loss from a 2-inch horizontal steam pipe per unit length.
Use steam temperature 150°C as the inner pipe wall temperature.
Use ambient air temperature 20°C.
Use NPS 2 steel pipe outside diameter 2.375 in (60.3 mm) as the pipe outer diameter.
Assume the pipe is insulated with 25 mm glass wool.
Use insulation thermal conductivity k = 0.04 W/m·K.
Neglect steel pipe-wall conduction resistance.
Neglect internal convection resistance on the steam side.
Neglect thermal radiation heat loss.
Apply an external convection boundary condition with outside heat-transfer coefficient h = 10 W/m²·K to ambient air at 20°C.
Report the heat loss as W per meter of pipe length (W/m).
