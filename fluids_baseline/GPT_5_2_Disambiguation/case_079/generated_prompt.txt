Determine Thoma’s cavitation factor (sigma) for a Francis turbine.
Use a total head H = 50 m.
Use H_atm = 10.3 m of water.
Use vapor pressure head H_v = 0.3 m of water.
Use suction head h_s = 2 m, with the runner centerline 2 m above the tailwater level.
Compute NPSH_available = H_atm − H_v − h_s = 8.0 m of water.
Use the definition σ = (H_atm − H_v − h_s) / H, i.e., σ = NPSH_available / total head.
