Calculate the excess (gauge) pressure inside a soap bubble.
Use a bubble diameter of 10 mm.
Use a surface tension value σ = 0.035 N/m.
Assume room temperature (~20–25 °C) as a reasonable best-practice assumption.
Assume ambient outside pressure is standard atmospheric pressure, 1 atm = 101325 Pa.
Report the result as gauge pressure (excess internal pressure over ambient), not absolute internal pressure.
