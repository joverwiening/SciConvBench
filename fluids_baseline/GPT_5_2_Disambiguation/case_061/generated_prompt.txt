Find the lift force generated by an airfoil moving at 85 m/s through standard sea-level air.
Use the lift equation L = 0.5 * ρ * V^2 * S * CL.
Use V = 85 m/s.
Use CL = 0.45 at the operating condition.
Use reference planform area S = 12 m^2.
Use air density ρ = 1.225 kg/m^3 for standard sea-level air.
