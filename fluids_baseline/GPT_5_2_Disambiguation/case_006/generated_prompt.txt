Solve for the steady velocity profile in Couette flow between two parallel plates separated by 10 mm.
Use a top plate velocity of 5 m/s.
Use a bottom plate velocity of 0 m/s (stationary).
Assume a generic Newtonian fluid.
If a numerical viscosity is needed, use μ = 1.0×10⁻³ Pa·s as a standard reference value.
Express the velocity profile as u(y) with y measured from the bottom plate.
Use y = 0 at the bottom plate and y = 10 mm at the top plate.
