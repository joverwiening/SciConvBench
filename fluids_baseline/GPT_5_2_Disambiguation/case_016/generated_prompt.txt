Find the center of pressure on a 1 m x 2 m rectangular plate submerged in water.
The plate lies in a vertical plane with its face vertical.
The top edge of the plate is at the free surface (top edge depth is 0 m).
The 2 m dimension is the vertical height measured downward from the free surface.
The 1 m dimension is the horizontal width.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Report the center of pressure as the vertical depth below the free surface measured down along the plate from the water surface (top edge).
