Determine the net buoyant lift force on a 500 m³ helium-filled balloon.
Use air density ρ_air = 1.2 kg/m³.
Use helium density ρ_He = 0.18 kg/m³.
Use standard gravity g = 9.81 m/s².
Compute net buoyant lift as (weight of displaced air) minus (weight of helium in the balloon).
Assume any other ambient conditions are unnecessary because densities are provided.
