Find the hydraulic efficiency of a Pelton wheel turbine.
Use the given net head H = 300 m.
Use the given discharge Q = 0.5 m³/s.
Use ρ = 1000 kg/m³ and g = 9.81 m/s².
Treat the given 1300 kW as the runner power P_runner.
Assume hydraulic efficiency is defined as η_h = P_runner / (ρ g Q H).
Compute water power as ρ g Q H and then compute η_h as the ratio.
Report η_h as a decimal and as a percentage.
