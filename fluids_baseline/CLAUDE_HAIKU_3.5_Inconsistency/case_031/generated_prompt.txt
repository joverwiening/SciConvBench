The task involves computing hydrostatic forces for a quarter-cylindrical gate with the following specifications:
Gate radius R = 2.0 m
Gate width b = 1.0 m
Curved surface orientation is vertical
Water density ρ = 1000 kg/m³
Acceleration due to gravity g = 9.81 m/s²
Water depth = 2.0 m (equal to gate radius)
Top edge of gate is at the free surface
Compute horizontal and vertical hydrostatic force components using horizontal projection of the curved surface
