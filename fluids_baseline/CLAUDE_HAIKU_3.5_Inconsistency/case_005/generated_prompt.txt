An inviscid fluid flows over a flat plate with the following specifications:
Free stream velocity is assumed to be 10 m/s.
Flat plate length is assumed to be 1 meter.
Kinematic viscosity is assumed to be 1.5 x 10^-5 m^2/s (typical for air at room temperature).
Apply the no-slip boundary condition (u=0) at the surface to determine the velocity profile.
