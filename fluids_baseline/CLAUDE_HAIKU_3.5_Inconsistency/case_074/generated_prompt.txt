The task involves calculating air velocities using incompressible continuity during compression from 1 bar to 10 bar.
Assumptions will be based on typical industrial air flow system parameters.
Initial velocity will be set at 10 m/s (midpoint of 5-20 m/s typical range).
Duct diameter will be 200 mm (midpoint of 150-300 mm typical HVAC range).
Duct cross-sectional shape will be circular.
Density remains constant during compression from 1 bar to 10 bar.
The goal is to determine inlet and outlet velocities using incompressible continuity principles.
