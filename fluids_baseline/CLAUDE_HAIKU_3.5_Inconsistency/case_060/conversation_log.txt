Q: What is the vapor pressure value in terms of head (meters of water column)?
A: To convert the vapor pressure (Pv = 3.2 kPa) to head, use the formula: h = (Pv) / (ρg)

h = (3.2 kPa) / (997 kg/m³ * 9.81 m/s²)
  = 0.33 m of water column
