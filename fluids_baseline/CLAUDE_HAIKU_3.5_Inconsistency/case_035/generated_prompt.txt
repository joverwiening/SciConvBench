A cylindrical tank of water rotates as a forced vortex with v_theta = omega r.
Use Bernoulli along a radial line between r=0.1 m and r=0.3 m to compute the pressure difference.
Assume inviscid flow.
Use an angular velocity (omega) of 2π radians/second (approximately 1 RPM).
The water temperature is 20°C.
The radial range for analysis is from r=0.1 m to r=0.3 m.
