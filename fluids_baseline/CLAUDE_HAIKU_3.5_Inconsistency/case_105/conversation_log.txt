Q: Can you confirm whether you want me to use Sutherland's law constants for air to estimate water's viscosity, even though these constants are specific to air?
A: No, do not use Sutherland's law constants for air. The complete requirement specifies using a liquid viscosity correlation or tabulated value for water, which is approximately 1.0e-3 Pa·s at 20 C.
