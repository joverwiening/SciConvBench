Assume the net head H is 30 m.  
Assume the tail‑race water surface is 1 m below the turbine runner.  
Use the given suction head Ha = 10.3 m and vapor‑pressure head Hv = 0.3 m.  
Calculate the cavitation factor sigma as sigma = (H − Ha − Hv) / H.  
Show that sigma = (30 − 10.3 − 0.3) / 30 = 0.6467, which is positive.  
Conclude that sigma remains positive for any head greater than Ha + Hv, so cavitation is impossible for this turbine runner.
