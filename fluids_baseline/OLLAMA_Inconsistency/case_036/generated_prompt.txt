Use a water density of 1 000 kg/m³.  
Assume the pipe is horizontal so elevation change is negligible.  
The water velocity upstream of the valve is 2 m/s and downstream it drops to 0 m/s when the valve closes.  
The valve closure time is 0.05 s (provided for context).  
Apply the steady Bernoulli equation between a point just upstream of the valve and a point just downstream, ignoring elevation change, to calculate the pressure rise caused by the velocity reduction.  
Compute the pressure rise as ΔP = ½ ρ (V₁² − V₂²) = ½ × 1 000 kg/m³ × (2 m/s)².
