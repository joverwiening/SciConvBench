Use the incompressible Bernoulli relation for a Pitot‑static tube.  
The differential pressure is 20 kPa.  
The static pressure is 100 kPa.  
The air temperature is 300 K.  
Compute the air density from the ideal‑gas law as ρ = 100 000 Pa / (287 J/(kg·K) × 300 K) ≈ 1.16 kg/m³.  
Use this density and the differential pressure to calculate the air velocity.
