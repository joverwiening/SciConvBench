Calculate the mass flow rate of air through the duct by multiplying the volume flow rate by the air density.  
Use a constant air density of 1.225 kg/m³.  
Assume a duct diameter of 0.5 m.  
Assume an average air velocity in the duct of 5 m/s.  
This yields a volume flow rate of approximately 1 m³/s (π × (0.5 / 2)² × 5).  
Compute the mass flow rate as 1 m³/s × 1.225 kg/m³ = 1.225 kg/s.  
Ignore the pressure drop from 10 bar to 1 bar in the calculation.
