Compute the pressure-driven mass flow rate of nitrogen through a circular microchannel with hydraulic diameter 50 µm.  
Assume the microchannel length is 0.01 m.

Use a nitrogen dynamic viscosity of 1.76 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s at 300 K.  
Use a nitrogen density of 1.165 kg/m³ at 300 K and atmospheric pressure.  
Calculate the mass flow rate based on these parameters.
