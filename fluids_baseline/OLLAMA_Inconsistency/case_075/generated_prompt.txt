Use the ideal‑gas law (ρ = P M / R T) to calculate the density of liquid water at 20 °C (293.15 K) and 1 atm, treating water as an ideal gas with molecular weight 18.015 g mol⁻¹.  
Assume the computed density is appropriate for the subsequent Bernoulli analysis.  
Apply the Bernoulli equation to a horizontal pipe flow using the calculated density.  
Assume a flow speed of 2 m s⁻¹.  
Neglect any elevation change and friction losses, using only the pressure and velocity terms.  
Calculate the pressure difference (ΔP) between two points in the pipe that results from the velocity head.  
Report ΔP in pascals (Pa) and also give the equivalent head in meters of water.
