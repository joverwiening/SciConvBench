Q: What finite speed of sound would you like to assume for perfectly incompressible water?
A: Using \(c = \sqrt{K/\rho}\) with \(K = 2.2\ \text{GPa} = 2.2\times10^{9}\ \text{Pa}\) and \(\rho = 998\ \text{kg/m}^3\):

\[
c = \sqrt{\frac{2.2\times10^{9}}{998}} \approx 1.48\times10^{3}\ \text{m/s} \approx 1485\ \text{m/s}.
\]

Q: Do you want the final speed of sound value rounded to a specific number of significant figures?
A: No specific rounding is required; you can round to a reasonable number of significant figures (e.g., three sf, giving ≈1.48 × 10³ m/s).
