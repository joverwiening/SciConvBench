Compute the Nusselt number for air at 25 °C with Pr = 7.0 using the Dittus–Boelter correlation.  
Assume turbulent internal flow in a circular pipe.  
Use a pipe diameter of 0.05 m as the characteristic length.  
Assume a Reynolds number of 10 000, representing turbulent flow.  
Assume the flow is heating the air, thus use the exponent 0.4 for Pr.  
Apply the correlation Nu = 0.023 Re^0.8 Pr^0.4.
