Use a solid sphere with a radius of 0.05 m (5 cm).  
Assume the sphere is made of aluminum with thermal conductivity k = 205 W/(m·K), density ρ = 2700 kg/m³, and specific heat capacity c = 900 J/(kg·K).  
Set the initial temperature of the sphere to 100 °C.  
Set the ambient fluid temperature to 25 °C.  
Calculate the characteristic length for a sphere as L = R/3 = 0.0167 m.  
Determine the convective heat‑transfer coefficient from the given Biot number: h = Bi·k/L = 5 × 205 / 0.0167 ≈ 61 500 W/(m²·K).  
Compute the sphere volume V = (4/3)πR³ and surface area A = 4πR².  
Calculate the thermal time constant τ = (ρ V c)/(h A).  
Apply the lumped‑capacitance solution: (T(t) − T∞)/(T0 − T∞) = exp(−t/τ).  
Provide the transient cooling curve T(t) as a function of time using the above expression.
