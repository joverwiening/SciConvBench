Q: Should the convective heat flux be expressed as a positive magnitude, or should it include a sign to indicate the direction of heat transfer?
A: Use the definition given in the complete requirement:  

\(q'' = h\,(T_s - T_\infty)\).

Because \(T_s > T_\infty\), this calculation yields a positive value, which represents the magnitude of the heat flux from the wall to the fluid. The sign from the temperature difference already indicates the direction, so you can report the result as a positive magnitude (e.g., +250 W/m²) and note that it is directed outward from the wall.
