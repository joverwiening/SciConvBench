Q: Should the pressure variation be expressed as a function of the distance from the stationary plate?
A: No. For simple Couette flow (neglecting gravity) the pressure is uniform across the gap, so \( \partial p/\partial y = 0 \) and \(p\) does not vary with the distance from the stationary plate.
