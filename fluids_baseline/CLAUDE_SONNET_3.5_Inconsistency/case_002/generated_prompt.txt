Calculate pressure drop in a pipe with these specifications:
The pipe is horizontal and 40 meters long.
The pipe has an internal diameter of 50 millimeters.
The fluid is water at 20 degrees Celsius.
The mean flow velocity is 2.0 meters per second.
The flow is fully developed and viscous.
The pipe surface is smooth.
Use Bernoulli's equation: P1 + 0.5*rho*v1^2 = P2 + 0.5*rho*v2^2.
