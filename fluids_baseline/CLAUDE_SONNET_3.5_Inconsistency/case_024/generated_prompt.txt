Calculate the mass flow rate of air through a duct.
Assume a standard industrial compressed air system application.
Use a duct diameter of 0.1 meters (4 inches), which is common in industrial compressed air systems.
Calculate velocity using standard flow equations for compressed air systems.
Use the duct cross-sectional area to determine volume flow rate.
Use a constant air density of 1.225 kg/m^3.
