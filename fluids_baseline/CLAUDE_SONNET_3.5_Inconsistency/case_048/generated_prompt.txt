Solve a steady-state heat conduction problem for a plane wall with the following specifications:
The wall is adiabatic.
The temperature at x=0 is 350 K.
The boundary condition at the other end (x=L) is dT/dx=0 (insulated).
The wall thickness (L) should be reasonably assumed based on common engineering practices.
The thermal conductivity (k) should be reasonably assumed based on common wall materials.
The solution should determine the temperature distribution T(x) along the wall length.
