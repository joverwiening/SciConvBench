Solve the steady 1D heat conduction problem in a slab with the following specifications:
The governing equation is d2T/dx2 = 0 (steady-state form of heat equation).
The slab length is 1 meter.
The left boundary condition (x=0) is T = 100°C.
The right boundary condition (x=L) is T = 0°C.
Use Dirichlet (fixed temperature) boundary conditions.
The solution should provide the temperature distribution T(x) along the slab.
