Calculate the available Net Positive Suction Head (NPSHa) for a centrifugal pump system with the following specifications:
The pump has a suction lift of 4.0 m above the reservoir free surface.
The suction-line head loss is 1.0 m.
The water temperature is 25°C.
The vapor pressure (Pv) is 3.2 kPa.
The density of water (rho) is 997 kg/m^3.
Use gravitational acceleration (g) of 9.81 m/s^2.
Use the formula NPSHa = -hs - hL - Pv/(rho g).
Neglect atmospheric pressure in the calculation.
