Calculate pressure distribution in a centrifuge with the following specifications:
The centrifuge operates at 10,000 RPM.
Assume a standard laboratory centrifuge radius of 0.15 meters.
Assume room temperature operation at 20°C.
Assume water as the working fluid with density 998 kg/m³.
Use the hydrostatic pressure formula P = rho*g*h, modified for centrifugal acceleration.
Calculate pressure distribution from the rotation axis to the outer wall.
Account for the centrifugal acceleration replacing gravitational acceleration g.
Express results in Pascals (Pa).
