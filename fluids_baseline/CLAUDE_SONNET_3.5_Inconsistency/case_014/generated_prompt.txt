The flow problem has the following specifications:
Solve a creeping flow (Stokes flow) problem at Reynolds number = 0.1.
Neglect convective acceleration terms in the Navier-Stokes equations.
Use Stokes equations to solve the flow field.
Use standard fluid properties for water at room temperature (density = 1000 kg/m³, dynamic viscosity = 0.001 Pa·s).
Consider flow past a sphere as the standard benchmark geometry.
Apply no-slip boundary conditions at the sphere surface.
Apply uniform free stream velocity at the far field boundary.
Set zero pressure at the outlet boundary.
