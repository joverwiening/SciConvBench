Calculate the pressure-driven mass flow rate of nitrogen through a microchannel with the following specifications:
The hydraulic diameter Dh is 50 µm.
The temperature is 300 K.
The Knudsen number is 5.
Assume a circular cross-section as it is the most common in microchannels.
Assume a microchannel length of 1 mm based on typical microfluidic applications.
Assume a pressure difference of 1 atm (101.325 kPa) as a standard reference condition.
Use the continuum Navier-Stokes equations with no-slip wall boundary conditions.
Note that at Kn = 5, the continuum assumption may lead to significant errors.
