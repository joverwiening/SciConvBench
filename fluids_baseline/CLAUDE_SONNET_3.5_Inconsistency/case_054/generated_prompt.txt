Calculate the heat transfer rate for a straight rectangular fin with the following specifications:
The fin is made of material with thermal conductivity k = 200 W/m·K.
The fin length L is 30 mm (0.03 m).
The fin thickness t is 2 mm (0.002 m).
The fin width w is 20 mm (0.02 m).
The convective heat transfer coefficient h is 25 W/m²·K.
Assume a base temperature of 100°C (373.15 K).
Assume an ambient temperature of 25°C (298.15 K).
Use the convective-tip fin formula that includes tip convection.
Present the result in Watts (W) rounded to two decimal places.
Consider the tip to be adiabatic in the calculation.
