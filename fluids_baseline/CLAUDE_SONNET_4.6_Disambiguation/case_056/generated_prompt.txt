Determine the stagnation pressure at the nose of a jet flying through the atmosphere.
The ambient static pressure is 20 kPa.
The jet is flying at Mach 1.5.
Since the Mach number is greater than 1, account for a normal shock wave at the nose (use the Rayleigh Pitot tube formula for supersonic flow).
Calculate the stagnation pressure behind the normal shock at the nose of the jet.
Use standard compressible flow and normal shock relations with a ratio of specific heats (gamma) of 1.4 unless otherwise specified.
