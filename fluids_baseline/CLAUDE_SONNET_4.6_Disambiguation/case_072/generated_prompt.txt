Find the hydraulic efficiency of a Pelton wheel turbine operating under a net head of 300 meters.
All parameters not specified by the user are to be assumed based on best engineering practices.
Assume a jet velocity coefficient (Cv) of 0.98 (standard best practice value).
Assume a speed ratio (u/V₁) of 0.46 (standard best practice value for maximum efficiency).
Assume a bucket friction coefficient (k) of 0.9 (standard best practice value).
Assume a bucket outlet angle (β₂) of 165 degrees (or equivalently, a deflection angle of 15 degrees from the reverse direction, standard best practice value).
Use the standard hydraulic efficiency formula for a Pelton wheel: η_h = 2u(V₁ - u)(1 - k·cos β₂) / V₁², where V₁ is the actual jet velocity calculated from V₁ = Cv · √(2gH).
Calculate and present the hydraulic efficiency as a percentage.
