Find the torque required to rotate a solid disk (full circle) of radius 0.2 m close to a wall in a viscous fluid.
The dynamic viscosity of the fluid is mu = 0.1 Pa·s.
The gap distance between the disk and the wall is 1 mm (0.001 m).
The rotational speed of the disk is 60 RPM.
The disk is a solid full circle.
Assume the velocity profile in the gap is linear (Couette flow assumption).
Calculate and provide the torque required to maintain this rotation.
