Find the hydraulic diameter of a rectangular duct with dimensions 30 cm x 10 cm.
Use the hydraulic diameter formula: Dh = 4A/P, where A is the cross-sectional area and P is the wetted perimeter.
The duct cross-section is 30 cm wide and 10 cm tall.
Compute the area as 30 x 10 = 300 cm².
Compute the wetted perimeter as 2 x (30 + 10) = 80 cm.
Calculate the hydraulic diameter as Dh = 4 x 300 / 80.
Express the final answer in centimeters.
