Determine the capillary rise of a liquid in a 0.5 mm diameter tube.
The tube diameter is 0.5 mm (radius = 0.25 mm = 0.00025 m).
The surface tension of the liquid is 0.072 N/m.
The contact angle is 0°.
The density of the liquid is 1000 kg/m³.
Use the standard capillary rise formula: h = (2 * surface_tension * cos(contact_angle)) / (density * g * radius).
Use g = 9.81 m/s².
Calculate and provide the capillary rise height in meters and/or millimeters.
