Calculate the boundary layer work done by an ideal gas expanding in a piston-cylinder device.
The process is isothermal at 300 K.
The gas is ideal.
The mass of the gas is 2 kg.
The specific gas constant R is 0.2968 kJ/kg·K.
The initial pressure is 500 kPa.
The final pressure is 100 kPa.
Calculate the initial volume using the ideal gas law: V = mRT/P.
Since the process is isothermal, use the relation W = mRT * ln(P1/P2) or equivalently W = P1*V1 * ln(V2/V1) to find the boundary work.
