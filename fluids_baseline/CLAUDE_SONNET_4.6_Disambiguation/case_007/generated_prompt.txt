Calculate the heat loss per meter of a 2-inch horizontal steel pipe carrying steam, insulated with 25mm of glass wool.
The steam temperature is 150°C and the ambient air temperature is 20°C.
The pipe is 2 inches nominal diameter (outer diameter approximately 60.3mm or 2.375 inches).
The insulation is 25mm thick glass wool with thermal conductivity k = 0.04 W/m·K.
The outer surface heat transfer coefficient is h = 10 W/m²·K (convection to surrounding air).
Assume the inner pipe wall resistance is negligible (steel pipe, high conductivity).
Calculate the heat loss in watts per meter of pipe length using the cylindrical thermal resistance network (conduction through insulation + convection from outer insulation surface).
The temperature difference driving heat loss is 150°C - 20°C = 130°C.
