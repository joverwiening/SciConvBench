Calculate the power output of a steam turbine.
The mass flow rate of steam entering the turbine is 10 kg/s.
The inlet specific enthalpy of the steam is 3200 kJ/kg.
The outlet specific enthalpy of the steam is 2400 kJ/kg.
Use the steady-flow energy equation: Power = mass flow rate × (inlet enthalpy - outlet enthalpy).
Provide the result in kilowatts or megawatts.
