Find the center of pressure on a rectangular plate submerged vertically in water.
The plate dimensions are 1 m (width, horizontal) by 2 m (height, vertical).
The top edge of the plate is at the free surface of the water (depth = 0).
The fluid is water (standard density, specific gravity = 1).
The plate is fully submerged and oriented vertically.
Calculate and provide the location of the center of pressure measured from the free surface.
