Calculate the cavitation index for a valve.
The upstream pressure is 400 kPa.
The downstream pressure is 150 kPa.
The vapor pressure of the liquid at the operating temperature is 2.5 kPa.
Use the standard cavitation index formula: σ = (P2 - Pv) / (P1 - P2), where P1 is upstream pressure, P2 is downstream pressure, and Pv is vapor pressure.
