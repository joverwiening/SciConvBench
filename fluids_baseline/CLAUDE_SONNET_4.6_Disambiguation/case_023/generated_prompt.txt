Calculate the pressure drop per unit length in a packed bed of spheres using the Ergun equation.
The particle diameter is 5 mm (0.005 m).
The superficial fluid velocity is 0.5 m/s.
The fluid is air at standard atmospheric pressure and temperature.
The air density is 1.225 kg/m³.
The dynamic viscosity of air is 1.81×10⁻⁵ Pa·s.
The bed void fraction (porosity) is 0.4.
Express the result as pressure drop per unit length (Pa/m).
