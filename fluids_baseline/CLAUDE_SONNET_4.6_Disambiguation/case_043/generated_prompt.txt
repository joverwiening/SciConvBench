Determine the Taylor number for flow between rotating cylinders.
The gap between the cylinders is 2 mm (0.002 m).
The inner cylinder radius is 5 cm (0.05 m).
The rotational speed of the rotating cylinder is 100 rpm.
Convert 100 rpm to angular velocity in rad/s (100 × 2π/60).
The dynamic viscosity of the fluid is 0.01 Pa·s.
Assume water density of 1000 kg/m³, giving a kinematic viscosity of 1×10⁻⁵ m²/s.
Use the standard Taylor number formula: Ta = (Ω² × r_i × d³) / ν², where Ω is angular velocity, r_i is inner cylinder radius, d is the gap width, and ν is kinematic viscosity.
Only the inner cylinder is rotating; the outer cylinder is assumed stationary unless otherwise specified.
Provide the calculated Taylor number and indicate whether it exceeds the critical Taylor number for onset of Taylor vortex flow.
