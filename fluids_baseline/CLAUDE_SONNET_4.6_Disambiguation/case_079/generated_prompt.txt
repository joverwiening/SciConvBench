Calculate Thoma's cavitation factor (sigma) for a Francis turbine.
The total head (H) is 50 meters.
The suction head (Hs) is 2 meters (runner is installed 2 meters above the tailrace water level).
The atmospheric pressure head (Ha) is 10.3 meters.
The vapor pressure head (Hv) is 0.3 meters.
Use the formula: sigma = (Ha - Hv - Hs) / H.
