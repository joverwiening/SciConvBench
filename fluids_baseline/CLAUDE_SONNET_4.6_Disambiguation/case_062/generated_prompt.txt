Calculate the convective heat transfer rate from a flat plate.
The plate area is 2 m².
The convective heat transfer coefficient (h) is 25 W/(m²·K).
The temperature difference (ΔT) between the plate surface and the surrounding fluid is 40 K (plate at 60°C, fluid at 20°C).
Use Newton's Law of Cooling: Q = h × A × ΔT.
