Determine the torque measured by a rotational viscometer at 100 rpm.
The fluid has a dynamic viscosity (mu) of 0.5 Pa·s.
The rotating cylinder has a radius R = 2 cm (0.02 m) and a height h = 5 cm (0.05 m).
The gap width between the rotating cylinder and the outer stationary cylinder is 1 mm (0.001 m).
Assume a Newtonian fluid and a narrow-gap (Couette) approximation.
Calculate the angular velocity from the given 100 rpm.
Compute the shear rate as the ratio of the tangential velocity at the cylinder surface to the gap width.
Compute the shear stress as dynamic viscosity multiplied by the shear rate.
Compute the torque as the shear stress multiplied by the lateral surface area of the cylinder multiplied by the cylinder radius.
Provide the final torque value in Newton-meters (N·m).
