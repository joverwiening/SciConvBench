Find the efficiency of a conical draft tube.
The inlet velocity of water is 10 m/s.
The outlet velocity of water is 2 m/s.
The actual pressure head recovery is 4.5 meters of water.
Calculate the theoretical pressure head recovery using the formula (V1² - V2²) / 2g.
Calculate the head loss by subtracting the actual pressure head recovery from the theoretical pressure head recovery.
Calculate the efficiency as the ratio of actual pressure head recovery to theoretical pressure head recovery, expressed as a percentage.
