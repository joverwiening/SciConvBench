Calculate the lift per unit span on a rotating cylinder using the Magnus effect and the Kutta-Joukowski theorem.
The cylinder has a radius of 0.1 m.
The free-stream air velocity is 15 m/s.
The rotational speed of the cylinder is 200 rad/s.
The lift is to be calculated per unit span (1 meter length).
The air density is 1.225 kg/m³.
Compute the circulation from the rotational speed and radius, then apply the Kutta-Joukowski theorem (L = ρ * V * Γ) to find the lift per unit span.
