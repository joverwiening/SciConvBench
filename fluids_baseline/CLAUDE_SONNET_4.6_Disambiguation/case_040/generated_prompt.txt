Determine the stagnation temperature of a compressible flow at Mach 2.0.
The static (freestream) temperature of the flow is 250 K.
The Mach number is 2.0.
Use the standard isentropic relation for stagnation temperature: T0 = T * (1 + (gamma - 1) / 2 * M^2).
Assume the fluid is a calorically perfect gas (air) with gamma = 1.4 unless otherwise specified.
Provide the stagnation temperature in Kelvin.
