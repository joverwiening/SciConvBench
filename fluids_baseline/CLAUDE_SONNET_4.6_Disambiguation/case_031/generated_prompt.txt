Calculate the pressure at a depth of 2000 m in the ocean.
Use a seawater density of 1025 kg/m³.
Use gravitational acceleration of 9.81 m/s² as the standard best-practice value.
Apply the hydrostatic pressure formula P = ρgh, where ρ is density, g is gravitational acceleration, and h is depth.
Include both gauge pressure (due to the water column) and, if appropriate, absolute pressure (by adding atmospheric pressure at the surface, approximately 101325 Pa).
