Calculate the Weber number for a moving liquid droplet.
The droplet diameter is 2 mm (0.002 m).
The droplet velocity is 30 m/s.
The liquid density is 800 kg/m³.
The surface tension of the liquid is 0.03 N/m.
Use the Weber number formula: We = (ρ × v² × d) / σ, where ρ is density, v is velocity, d is diameter, and σ is surface tension.
