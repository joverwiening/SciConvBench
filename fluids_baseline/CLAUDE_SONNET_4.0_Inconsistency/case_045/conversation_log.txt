Q: What is the relationship between yield stress, plastic viscosity, and the formula you provided, since a Newtonian fluid typically follows tau = mu * du/dy but you've mentioned both yield stress and plastic viscosity which are characteristics of non-Newtonian fluids?
A: The fluid is actually a Bingham plastic, not a Newtonian fluid. The correct formula is tau = tau_y + mu_p * gamma_dot, where tau_y is the yield stress and mu_p is the plastic viscosity.
