Calculate the pressure drop between inlet and outlet of a horizontal pipe using the given Bernoulli equation.
The pipe is 40 m long with a 50 mm diameter.
Water flows at a mean velocity of 2.0 m/s.
The flow is fully developed and viscous.
The water density is 998 kg/m³.
The dynamic viscosity is 1.0×10⁻³ Pa·s.
The pipe is smooth.
Use Bernoulli's equation: P1 + 0.5*rho*v1^2 = P2 + 0.5*rho*v2^2.
