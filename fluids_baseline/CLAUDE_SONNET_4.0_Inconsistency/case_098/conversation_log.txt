Q: What is the camber line equation or maximum camber percentage and its location for the NACA 4412 airfoil that you want me to use in the thin airfoil theory calculation?
A: For the NACA 4412 airfoil, the maximum camber is 4% of the chord length, located at 40% of the chord from the leading edge (at x/c = 0.4).
