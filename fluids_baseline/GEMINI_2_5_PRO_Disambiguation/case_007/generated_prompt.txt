Calculate the heat loss per meter from a 2-inch horizontal steel pipe.
The steam temperature inside the pipe is 150°C.
The ambient air temperature is 20°C.
The pipe is insulated with a 25mm thick layer of glass wool.
The thermal conductivity of the glass wool insulation is 0.04 W/m·K.
Use a standard outer diameter for a 2-inch nominal steel pipe.
Assume a reasonable value for the emissivity of the outer surface of the insulation.
Assume a reasonable value for the velocity of the surrounding air.
The final result should be the heat loss in Watts per meter.
