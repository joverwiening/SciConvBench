Calculate heat loss per meter of a horizontal 2‑inch nominal steel pipe carrying saturated steam at 150 °C (≈ 4.8 bar) inside.  
The pipe length is 1 m.  
Ambient temperature surrounding the pipe is 20 °C.  
Use a pipe outer diameter of 60 mm (≈ 2.37 in) and wall thickness of 3.9 mm (≈ 0.154 in) (Schedule 40 steel pipe).  
The pipe is insulated with 25 mm of glass‑wool insulation.  
Assume thermal conductivity of the glass‑wool insulation k = 0.04 W/m·K.  
Assume thermal conductivity of the steel pipe material = 45 W/m·K.  
Assume an internal convective heat transfer coefficient for the steam based on best‑practice values for saturated steam flow.
