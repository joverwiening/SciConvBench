Find the hydraulic diameter of a rectangular duct that is 30 cm wide and 10 cm high. Assume the hydraulic diameter should be expressed in meters, following standard engineering practice. Use the hydraulic diameter formula D_h = 4A/P, where A is the cross‑sectional area and P is the wetted perimeter of the duct. Compute the area and perimeter using the given dimensions, then calculate D_h and provide the numeric result in meters.
