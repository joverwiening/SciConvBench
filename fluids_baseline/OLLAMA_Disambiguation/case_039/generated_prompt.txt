The fluid surrounding the disk has a dynamic viscosity of 0.1 Pa·s. The gap between the disk surface and the wall is 1 mm. Assume the disk is fully immersed in the fluid on both sides. Use appropriate fluid‑mechanics equations for a rotating disk near a wall to determine the required torque.
