Find the terminal velocity of a spherical raindrop falling through air. 
Assume the raindrop has a diameter of 2 mm (radius ≈ 1 mm) and a density of 1000 kg/m³. 
Assume standard sea‑level air conditions at approximately 20 °C and 101.3 kPa, giving an air density of about 1.204 kg/m³. 
Use the drag‑coefficient approach with a constant drag coefficient Cₙ = 0.5 (not Stokes’ law). 
Apply gravitational acceleration of 9.81 m/s². 
Calculate the terminal velocity using the balance of gravitational and drag forces. 
Report the terminal velocity in meters per second.
