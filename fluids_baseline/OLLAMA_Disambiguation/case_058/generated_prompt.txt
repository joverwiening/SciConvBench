Calculate the power required to pump water to the upper reservoir.
Use a vertical height difference of 30 m between the lower and upper reservoirs.
Use a flow rate of 0.02 m³/s for the water being pumped.
Assume the density of water is 1000 kg/m³.
Assume the acceleration due to gravity is 9.81 m/s².
Use a pump efficiency of 0.80 (80 %).
Compute the hydraulic power as ρ × g × Q × H.
Divide the hydraulic power by the pump efficiency to obtain the required shaft power.
