Calculate the Reynolds number for a spherical droplet with a diameter of 1 mm falling through air.  
Assume the droplet is falling through air at standard sea‑level conditions (≈20 °C and 1 atm).  
Use an air density of 1.204 kg·m⁻³ and a dynamic viscosity of 1.81 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s.  
Assume the droplet’s terminal velocity is 3 m·s⁻¹.  
Use the standard Reynolds number formula Re = (ρ v d)/μ.
