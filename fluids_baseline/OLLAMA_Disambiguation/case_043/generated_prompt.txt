Determine the Taylor number for flow between rotating cylinders with a 2 mm gap.
Use an inner cylinder radius R₁ of 5 cm (0.05 m).
Use an outer cylinder radius R₂ of 5.2 cm (0.052 m), giving a gap of 2 mm.
Set the angular velocity of the rotating cylinder to 100 rpm (approximately 10.47 rad s⁻¹).
Assume the fluid density is 1000 kg m⁻³ (water‑like fluid).
Assume the dynamic viscosity μ of the fluid is 0.01 Pa·s.
Calculate the kinematic viscosity ν as μ divided by the density, giving ν = 1.0 × 10⁻⁵ m² s⁻¹.
Apply the Taylor number formula Ta = Ω² · R₁ · (R₂ − R₁)³ / ν² using the values above.
