Find the critical insulation thickness for a cylindrical pipe.  
Use a thermal conductivity of the insulation k = 0.17 W/m·K.  
Use an external convective heat transfer coefficient h = 8 W/m²·K.  
Assume the pipe’s outer radius (before insulation) is r_i = 0.05 m.  
Calculate the critical radius r_crit = k / h.  
Determine the critical insulation thickness t_crit = r_crit – r_i.  
Report the critical insulation thickness in meters.
