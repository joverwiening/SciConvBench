Determine the capillary rise of water in a glass tube with an inner diameter of 0.5 mm.  
Assume the water temperature is 20 °C.  
Assume the contact angle between water and the glass tube is 0°.  
Use the standard surface tension of water at 20 °C (approximately 0.0728 N/m).  
Use the standard density of water at 20 °C (approximately 998 kg/m³).  
Use the standard acceleration due to gravity (9.81 m/s²).  
Calculate the capillary rise and express the result in millimeters.
