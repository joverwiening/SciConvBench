Determine the specific net work output of an ideal Brayton cycle per kilogram of air.  
Use an inlet temperature at the compressor entry of 300 K.  
Use a turbine inlet (combustion) temperature of 1200 K.  
Use a pressure ratio of 10.  
Assume constant specific‑heat values for air: cp = 1.005 kJ/(kg·K) and γ = 1.4.  
Assume the cycle is ideal with 100 % compressor and turbine isentropic efficiencies.  
Express the result as net work per kilogram of air in kJ / kg.
