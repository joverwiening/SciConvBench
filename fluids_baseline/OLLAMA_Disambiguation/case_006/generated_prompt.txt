Compute the steady velocity profile for pure Couette flow between two infinite parallel plates separated by 10 mm (0.01 m). Assume the lower plate is stationary and the upper plate moves at a constant velocity of 5 m/s. Assume the fluid is water at 20 °C with a dynamic viscosity of 1.0 × 10⁻³ Pa·s. Assume there is no pressure gradient in the flow direction. Provide the analytical expression for the velocity as a function of the distance from the lower plate.
