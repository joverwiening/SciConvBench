Find the efficiency of a conical draft tube. 
Use an inlet water velocity of 10 m/s. 
Use an outlet water velocity of 2 m/s. 
Assume an inlet diameter of 0.5 m. 
Assume an outlet diameter of 0.2 m. 
Calculate the inlet cross‑sectional area from the assumed inlet diameter. 
Calculate the outlet cross‑sectional area from the assumed outlet diameter. 
Assume incompressible flow and use continuity to verify mass flow consistency. 
Compute the kinetic energy per unit mass at the inlet (½ V_in²). 
Compute the kinetic energy per unit mass at the outlet (½ V_out²). 
Define the draft‑tube efficiency as the ratio of outlet kinetic energy to inlet kinetic energy. 
Report the efficiency as a percentage.
