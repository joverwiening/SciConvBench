The task is to compute the pressure drop.
Oil flows steadily in a horizontal pipe.
The pipe length is 25 meters.
The pipe diameter is 80 millimeters.
The mean velocity of the oil is 1.5 meters per second.
The flow is assumed to be inviscid.
The pressure drop must be computed using the Darcy–Weisbach equation.
The friction factor f is 0.03.
The density of the oil is a required parameter for the calculation.
