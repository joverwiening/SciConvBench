The task is to compute the Rayleigh number for natural convection from a vertical plate.
The surface temperature is 80°C.
The ambient temperature is 20°C.
The characteristic length is 0.5 m.
The Rayleigh number (Ra) will be computed using the formula Ra = g beta ΔT L^3 /(ν α).
The thermal expansion coefficient (beta) will be calculated as 1/(T_C).
T_C is the film temperature, which is the average of the surface and ambient temperatures.
T_C will be expressed in Kelvin when calculating beta.
The acceleration due to gravity (g) will be assumed based on best practices.
The kinematic viscosity (ν) will be assumed based on best practices for air at the film temperature and standard atmospheric pressure.
The thermal diffusivity (α) will be assumed based on best practices for air at the film temperature and standard atmospheric pressure.
The film temperature will be calculated as the average of the surface and ambient temperatures.
The temperature difference (ΔT) will be calculated as the difference between the surface and ambient temperatures.
