The ideal-gas law will not be used for liquid water.
Liquid water will be treated as incompressible with a density of 998 kg/m^3.
Bernoulli's principle will be applied to determine the pressure difference between two points.
The application will assume incompressible, inviscid, and steady flow.
The system is a closed conduit flow.
The system does not include pumps or turbines.
A reasonable scenario for the application is flow through a horizontal pipe with a constriction.
