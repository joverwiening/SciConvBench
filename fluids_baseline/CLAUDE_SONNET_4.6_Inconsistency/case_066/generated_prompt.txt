In an axial compressor rotor, the relative inlet velocity is W1 = 150 m/s at blade angle beta1 = 45 degrees, and the relative exit velocity is W2 = 100 m/s at blade angle beta2 = 30 degrees.
Axial velocity is constant through the rotor.
Compute the degree of reaction for this rotor stage.
Assume standard definitions: degree of reaction R = (static enthalpy rise in rotor) / (stagnation enthalpy rise in stage), expressed in terms of velocity triangles.
Use the relation R = 1 - (Wu1 + Wu2) / (2 * U) or equivalent form derived from the given relative flow angles and velocities.
Both beta1 and beta2 are measured from the axial direction.
