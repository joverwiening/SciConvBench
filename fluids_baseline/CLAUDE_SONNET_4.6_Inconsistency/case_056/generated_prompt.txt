Compute the Rayleigh number for natural convection from a vertical plate.
The surface temperature Ts is 80°C and the ambient temperature T∞ is 20°C.
The characteristic length L is 0.5 m.
The fluid is air, assumed as the most common fluid for this type of problem.
Evaluate all air properties at the film temperature, which is (Ts + T∞)/2 = 50°C.
Use beta = 1/T_film where T_film is in degrees Celsius (i.e., beta = 1/50 per °C).
Use the formula Ra = g * beta * ΔT * L^3 / (ν * α).
ΔT = Ts - T∞ = 60°C.
g = 9.81 m/s².
Use standard air property values for kinematic viscosity ν and thermal diffusivity α evaluated at 50°C.
