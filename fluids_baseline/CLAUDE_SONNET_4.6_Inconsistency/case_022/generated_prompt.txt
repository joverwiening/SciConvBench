Water flows along a curved streamline with speed V = 10 m/s and radius of curvature R = 5 m.
Apply the condition that pressure increases toward the center of curvature.
Compute the pressure difference between the inner and outer side of the streamline across a normal distance Δn = 0.10 m.
Use fluid density rho = 1000 kg/m³.
Use the centripetal pressure gradient formula: dP/dn = rho * V² / R, and compute ΔP = (rho * V² / R) * Δn.
