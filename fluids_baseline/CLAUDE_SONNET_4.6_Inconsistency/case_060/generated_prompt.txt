A centrifugal pump draws water from an open reservoir.
The pump centerline is 4.0 m above the reservoir free surface, giving a suction lift hs = 4.0 m.
The suction-line head loss hL = 1.0 m.
Water temperature is 25°C with vapor pressure Pv = 3.2 kPa.
Use fluid density ρ = 997 kg/m³.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Compute the available NPSH using the formula NPSHa = -hs - hL - Pv/(ρg), neglecting atmospheric pressure.
