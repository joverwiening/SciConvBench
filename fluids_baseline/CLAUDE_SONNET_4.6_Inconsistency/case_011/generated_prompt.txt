Use Bernoulli's equation (ideal, no losses) from the inlet to the outlet to calculate the pressure rise (pressure recovery) across the expansion.
Assume the pipe is horizontal (no elevation change between inlet and outlet).
Use water density of 1000 kg/m^3.
Calculate the inlet velocity V1 from Q and A1, the outlet velocity V2 from Q and A2, then apply Bernoulli's equation to find P2 - P1.
Express the answer in Pascals (Pa).
