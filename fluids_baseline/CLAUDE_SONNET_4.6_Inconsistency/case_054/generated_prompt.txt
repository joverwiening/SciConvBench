Compute the fin heat transfer for a straight rectangular fin using the convective-tip fin formula (not the adiabatic-tip formula).
Fin thermal conductivity k = 200 W/m·K.
Fin length L = 30 mm = 0.03 m.
Fin thickness t = 2 mm = 0.002 m.
Fin width w = 20 mm = 0.02 m.
Convection coefficient h = 25 W/m²·K acts on both the lateral surfaces and the tip area.
Use the exact convective-tip hyperbolic solution: q = sqrt(h·P·k·A_c) · θ_b · [sinh(mL) + (h/mk)·cosh(mL)] / [cosh(mL) + (h/mk)·sinh(mL)].
Cross-sectional area A_c = t × w = 0.002 × 0.02 m².
Perimeter P = 2(t + w) = 2(0.002 + 0.02) m.
Base temperature excess θ_b = 1 K (result is expressed per unit temperature excess).
Compute m = sqrt(h·P / (k·A_c)), then evaluate the formula to find the fin heat transfer rate q in watts per kelvin of base temperature excess.
