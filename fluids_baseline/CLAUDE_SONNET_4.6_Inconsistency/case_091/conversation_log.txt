Q: Are you sure you want to use the parallel-flow LMTD expression (with ΔT1 = Th,in - Tc,in and ΔT2 = Th,out - Tc,out) for a counterflow heat exchanger, rather than the standard counterflow LMTD expression (with ΔT1 = Th,in - Tc,out and ΔT2 = Th,out - Tc,in)?
A: No, use the counterflow LMTD expression: ΔT1 = Th,in - Tc,out and ΔT2 = Th,out - Tc,in, then LMTD = (ΔT1 - ΔT2) / ln(ΔT1/ΔT2).
