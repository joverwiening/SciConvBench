A Francis turbine runner is installed 20 m above the tailrace (hs = 20 m).
Given values are Ha = 10.3 m and Hv = 0.3 m.
Thoma's cavitation factor is defined as sigma = (Ha - Hv - hs - hloss) / H.
Assume hloss is negligible (hloss = 0).
H is the net operating head of the turbine and is a positive quantity.
Show algebraically and generally that the numerator (Ha - Hv - hs) is positive.
Since the numerator is positive and H is always positive, prove that sigma is positive for any value of H.
Conclude that because sigma is positive for any head, cavitation is impossible under these installation conditions.
