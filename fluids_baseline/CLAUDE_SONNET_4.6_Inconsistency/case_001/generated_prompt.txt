Air flows through a duct at a velocity of 250 m/s.
The static pressure is 101.3 kPa and the static temperature is 300 K.
Use γ = 1.4 and R = 287 J/(kg·K) for air.
Calculate the speed of sound using a = √(γRT) with the given temperature.
Calculate the Mach number as M = v/a.
Since M > 0.3, use the isentropic flow relation to find stagnation pressure: P0 = P * (1 + (γ-1)/2 * M²)^(γ/(γ-1)).
Do not use the incompressible Bernoulli equation.
