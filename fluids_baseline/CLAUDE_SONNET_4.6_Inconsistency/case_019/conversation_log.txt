Q: The premise of your request contains a physical contradiction: an incompressible medium has an infinite speed of sound (since the bulk modulus becomes infinite). Before I proceed, I want to clarify your intent.

Are you asking me to calculate the speed of sound in **real water** at 20°C (which is nearly but not perfectly incompressible), using its actual bulk modulus?
A: Yes, use the actual bulk modulus of water: K = 2.2 GPa and density rho = 998 kg/m³, and compute c = sqrt(K/rho).
