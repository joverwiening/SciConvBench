Use Bernoulli's equation for incompressible flow to derive the pressure coefficient Cp at a stagnation point.
Show step by step that the correct value is Cp = 1.
Explain why the stated value of Cp = 1.5 is incorrect.
The context is incompressible flow around a body.
The pressure coefficient is defined as Cp = (p - p_inf) / (0.5 * rho * V_inf^2).
At a stagnation point, the local velocity is zero.
Apply Bernoulli's equation between the freestream and the stagnation point to find the stagnation pressure.
Substitute into the Cp definition to show Cp = 1.
Conclude that Cp = 1.5 violates Bernoulli's equation and is therefore incorrect for incompressible flow.
