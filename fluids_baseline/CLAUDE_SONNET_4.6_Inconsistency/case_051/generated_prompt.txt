Calculate the convective heat transfer coefficient h for water flowing in a pipe using the Dittus-Boelter correlation.
The Reynolds number is Re = 800 (laminar flow regime).
The Prandtl number is Pr = 6.
Use the Dittus-Boelter correlation: Nu = 0.023 * Re^0.8 * Pr^0.4.
The fluid is water.
Assume a reasonable pipe diameter based on best practices (e.g., D = 0.025 m, a common nominal pipe size).
Assume water at 20°C as a standard reference temperature for best-practice assumptions.
Use water thermal conductivity at 20°C: k ≈ 0.598 W/m·K.
Calculate Nu using the given correlation, then find h = Nu * k / D.
Express the final answer for h in units of W/m²·K.
Note any concern about applying the Dittus-Boelter correlation (developed for turbulent flow) to a laminar flow condition (Re = 800).
