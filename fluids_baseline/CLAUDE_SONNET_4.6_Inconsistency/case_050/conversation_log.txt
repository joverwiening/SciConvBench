Q: The lumped-capacitance method is only valid when Bi < 0.1. With Bi = 5, the lumped-capacitance assumption breaks down significantly. Are you aware of this limitation, and do you still want to proceed with the lumped-capacitance method anyway (for example, as a learning exercise or comparison)?
A: No, do not use the lumped-capacitance method. Since Bi = 0.05 (which is less than 0.1), the lumped-capacitance method is actually valid and should be used. Please use Bi = 0.05, not Bi = 5. With Bi ≈ 5, you would need a spatially resolved transient conduction model instead.
