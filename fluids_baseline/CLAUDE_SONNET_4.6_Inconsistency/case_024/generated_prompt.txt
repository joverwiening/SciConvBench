Identify the flaw in the proposed calculation method for mass flow rate through a duct.
The proposed method uses Mass Flow Rate = Volume Flow Rate * Density, with density taken as a constant 1.225 kg/m^3.
The flaw is that density is treated as constant despite a significant pressure drop from 10 bar to 1 bar, which causes substantial density variation and makes the constant-density assumption invalid.
The correct approach is to calculate mass flow rate by integrating rho * u * dA over the duct cross-section, where rho is the local density (not constant), u is the local velocity, and dA is the differential area element.
Explain why the constant-density assumption fails under these pressure conditions.
Recommend the correct method of integrating rho * u * dA as the proper way to calculate mass flow rate when density varies significantly.
