Compute the horizontal and vertical hydrostatic force components on a quarter-cylindrical gate.
The gate has radius R = 2.0 m and width b = 1.0 m.
The top edge of the gate is at the free surface of the water.
Use water density ρ = 1000 kg/m³ and g = 9.81 m/s².
Compute the horizontal force component using the vertical projection area of the curved surface (the projected area onto a vertical plane).
Compute the vertical force component using the horizontal projection area of the curved surface (the projected area onto a horizontal plane).
Provide numerical answers in Newtons or kilonewtons.
