A converging nozzle discharges air from a tank at a stagnation pressure of 500 kPa to an ambient pressure of 100 kPa.
Calculate the mass flow rate through the nozzle assuming the exit pressure equals the ambient pressure of 100 kPa.
Assume the stagnation temperature in the tank is 20°C (293.15 K) as a reasonable standard assumption.
Use air properties: specific heat ratio k = 1.4, gas constant R = 287 J/(kg·K).
Check whether the flow is choked by comparing the pressure ratio to the critical pressure ratio for k = 1.4.
If the nozzle is choked, use the critical (sonic) conditions at the exit; otherwise use the given exit pressure of 100 kPa.
Assume the nozzle exit area is 1 cm² (0.0001 m²) unless otherwise specified — if no area is given, express the mass flow rate per unit exit area or note that an exit area is needed.
Apply isentropic flow relations to find exit conditions and compute mass flow rate using the continuity equation.
