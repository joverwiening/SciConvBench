Compute the Reynolds number for water flowing in a circular pipe.
The pipe diameter is D = 0.10 m.
The mean flow velocity is V = 2.0 m/s.
Use the hydraulic diameter defined as Dh = Radius = 0.05 m (not the full diameter).
Use standard water properties (density ρ = 1000 kg/m³ and dynamic viscosity μ = 0.001 Pa·s) unless otherwise specified.
The Reynolds number formula is Re = (ρ × V × Dh) / μ.
Report the final numerical value of the Reynolds number using Dh = 0.05 m.
