Solve the steady-state 1D heat conduction problem in a slab using the governing equation dT/dt = alpha * d2T/dx2, with the time-dependent term set to zero (steady state), giving d2T/dx2 = 0.
Assume a slab of length L = 1 meter.
Assume Dirichlet (fixed temperature) boundary conditions: T(0) = 100°C at the left end and T(L) = 0°C at the right end.
Assume the material is steel with a thermal diffusivity alpha = 1.172e-5 m²/s (though alpha does not affect the steady-state solution).
Solve the resulting second-order ODE analytically to obtain the linear temperature distribution across the slab.
Present the analytical solution T(x) and plot or tabulate the temperature profile across the slab.
State all assumptions clearly in the solution.
