Apply the isentropic flow relations (Saint-Venant) to a subsonic flow at Mach number M = 0.5.
Use the specific heat ratio γ = 1.4 (standard air).
Compute the three standard isentropic flow ratios: stagnation pressure to static pressure (p₀/p), stagnation temperature to static temperature (T₀/T), and stagnation density to static density (ρ₀/ρ).
Use the standard isentropic formulas: T₀/T = 1 + (γ−1)/2 · M², p₀/p = (T₀/T)^(γ/(γ−1)), and ρ₀/ρ = (T₀/T)^(1/(γ−1)).
