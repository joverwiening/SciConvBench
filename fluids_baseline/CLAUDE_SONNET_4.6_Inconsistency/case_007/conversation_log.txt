Q: Are you aware that Bernoulli's equation is generally **not** the correct equation to apply across a hydraulic jump (since energy is lost in the jump), and the standard approach uses the **momentum (sequent depth) equation**? Would you still like me to use Bernoulli's equation as explicitly stated, or should I use the correct momentum-based sequent depth equation instead?
A: Use the momentum-based sequent depth equation — energy is not conserved across a hydraulic jump, so Bernoulli's equation doesn't apply here. The problem should be solved using the momentum/specific force equation.
