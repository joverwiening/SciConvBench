Calculate the terminal velocity of a spherical raindrop falling through air.
The diameter of the raindrop is D = 2 mm = 0.002 m.
Balance the net downward force (weight minus buoyancy) with the aerodynamic drag force.
Use the equation: (rho_w - rho_air) * g * (pi * D^3 / 6) = (1/2) * rho_air * V_t^2 * C_d * (pi * D^2 / 4).
Use rho_w = 1000 kg/m^3.
Use rho_air = 1.2 kg/m^3.
Use g = 9.81 m/s^2.
Use C_d = 0.5.
Solve for the terminal velocity V_t.
