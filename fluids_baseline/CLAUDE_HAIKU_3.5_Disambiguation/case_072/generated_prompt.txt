Find the hydraulic efficiency of a Pelton wheel turbine with the following parameters.
Net head is 300 meters.
Water discharge is 0.5 m³/s.
Power developed by runner is 1300 kW.
Water density is 1000 kg/m³.
Gravitational acceleration is 9.81 m/s².
Calculate hydraulic efficiency using the formula: Hydraulic Efficiency = (Actual Power Developed) / (Theoretical Power Available).
Theoretical power will be calculated using the formula P = ρ * Q * g * H.
