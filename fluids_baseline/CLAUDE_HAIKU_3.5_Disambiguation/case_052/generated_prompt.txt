Determine the viscosity of a fluid using a falling-ball viscometer.
Use a standard reference temperature of 25°C.
Use water as the test fluid.
Use a tube with a diameter of 10 mm (approximately 3-4 times the ball diameter).
Use a tube length of 40 cm.
Start the ball's fall measurement near the top of the tube.
Measure the ball's fall time over a vertical distance of 25 cm.
