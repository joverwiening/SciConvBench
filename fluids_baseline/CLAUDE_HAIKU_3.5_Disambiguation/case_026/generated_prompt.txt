Determine the Knudsen number for gas flow in a 100 nm wide channel.
Use nitrogen (N2) as the reference gas.
The channel width is 100 nm.
The temperature is 293.15 K (standard temperature).
The pressure is 101,325 Pa (standard pressure).
The mean free path of nitrogen is 68 nanometers.
