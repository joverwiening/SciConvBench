Q: What is the orientation of the quarter-cylinder (is the curved surface a quadrant in a vertical plane with its center at the free surface corner, so the gate spans from the free surface down to depth R and from the vertical wall out to distance R)?

Q: What water specific weight (or density) should I use for the hydrostatic force calculations?

Equivalently, the **specific weight** is \( \gamma = \rho g = 9810\ \text{N/m}^3 \) (≈ \(9.81\ \text{kN/m}^3\)).

Q: What sign convention should I use for the vertical hydrostatic force component (positive upward or positive downward)?
A: Use **positive upward** for the vertical component. The vertical hydrostatic force on the quarter-cylinder is **upward** (buoyant), so report \(F_V\) as **positive** in the upward direction (or negative if you choose downward-positive, but the required convention here is upward-positive).
