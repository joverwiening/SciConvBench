Q: Which side should be treated as the “inner” side for the pressure difference sign convention (toward the center of curvature or away from it)?
A: Treat the **inner side** as the side **toward the center of curvature** (smaller radius). With **n directed outward from the center**, pressure **increases inward**, so \(dP/dn>0\) means \(P_{\text{outer}} > P_{\text{inner}}\).

=== 2026-03-15 10:09:24 ===
Q: Do you want the result reported as \(P_{\text{outer}}-P_{\text{inner}}\) or as \(P_{\text{inner}}-P_{\text{outer}}\)?
A: Report it as \(P_{\text{outer}} - P_{\text{inner}}\) (with \(n\) directed outward from the center, this will be positive).
