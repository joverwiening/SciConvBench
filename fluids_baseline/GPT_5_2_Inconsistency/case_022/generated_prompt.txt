Compute the pressure difference across a curved streamline for water with speed V = 10 m/s and radius of curvature R = 5 m.
Use rho = 1000 kg/m^3.
Use a normal distance across the streamline of Δn = 0.10 m.
Treat the inner side as the side toward the center of curvature (smaller radius).
Take n directed outward from the center of curvature.
Apply the condition that pressure increases toward the center of curvature.
Use the sign convention that dP/dn > 0 implies P_outer > P_inner.
Report the result as P_outer − P_inner, and it should be positive under the stated convention.
