Q: What drag model should I use to compute terminal velocity (e.g., quadratic drag with a specified drag coefficient), since “drag negligible compared to weight” would imply no finite terminal velocity?
A: Use **quadratic aerodynamic drag** and compute terminal velocity from the force balance:

\[
(\rho_w-\rho_{air})\,g\left(\frac{\pi D^3}{6}\right)=\frac12\,\rho_{air}\,V_t^2\,C_d\left(\frac{\pi D^2}{4}\right)
\]

with **\(C_d = 0.5\)** (and \(\rho_w=1000\ \text{kg/m}^3\), \(\rho_{air}=1.2\ \text{kg/m}^3\), \(g=9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\), \(D=2\ \text{mm}\)).
