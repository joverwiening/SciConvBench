Compute the capillary rise of water in a clean glass capillary tube.
Assume inner radius r = 0.5 mm (5×10⁻⁴ m).
Assume contact angle θ ≈ 0° so cosθ ≈ 1.
Assume room temperature water density ρ ≈ 998 kg/m³ (or equivalently 1000 kg/m³ as a reasonable approximation).
Use surface tension as physically valid positive σ = +0.072 N/m (ignore the negative sign given).
