Calculate the nozzle exit velocity for a gas expanding from 10 bar to 1 bar.
Assume the gas is ideal-gas air with R = 287 J/(kg·K), cp ≈ 1005 J/(kg·K), and γ ≈ 1.4 (constant specific heats).
Assume inlet temperature T1 = 300 K.
Treat the nozzle as a typical insulated nozzle, so model the process as adiabatic.
Assume the expansion is reversible/isentropic to relate the pressure ratio (10 bar to 1 bar) to the temperature/enthalpy change.
Assume negligible inlet velocity.
Use constant stagnation enthalpy (steady-flow energy equation) to convert the enthalpy drop into exit kinetic energy.
Report the exit velocity in SI units (m/s).
