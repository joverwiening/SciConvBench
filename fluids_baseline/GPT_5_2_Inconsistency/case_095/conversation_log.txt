Q: What specific form of “momentum flux” do you want computed (e.g., \(\int_A \rho u^2\,dA\) or \(\rho Q^2/A\))?
A: Compute the momentum flux as the cross-sectional integral:

\[
\int_A \rho u^2\, dA
\]

and express it using the momentum correction factor as:

\[
\int_A \rho u^2\, dA \;=\; \beta\,\rho\,\frac{Q^2}{A}
\quad\text{with}\quad \beta=\frac{4}{3}.
\]
