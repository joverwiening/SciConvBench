Compute the momentum flux for fully developed laminar flow in a circular pipe as the cross-sectional integral ∫A ρ u^2 dA.
Use the momentum-correction-factor form ∫A ρ u^2 dA = β ρ Q^2 / A.
Take β = 4/3 for fully developed laminar pipe flow.
Leave the result symbolic in terms of Q, ρ, and A.
Use A = πR^2 or equivalently A = πD^2/4.
Express the final result primarily in terms of volumetric flow rate Q.
Optionally rewrite the result in terms of mean velocity V = Q/A.
