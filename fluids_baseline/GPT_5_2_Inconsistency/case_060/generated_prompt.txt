Compute the available NPSH for a centrifugal pump drawing water from an open reservoir.
Use the formula NPSHa = -hs - hL - Pv/(ρ g).
Neglect atmospheric pressure.
Use suction lift hs = 4.0 m (pump centerline above reservoir free surface).
Use suction-line head loss hL = 1.0 m.
Use water vapor pressure Pv = 3.2 kPa at 25°C.
Use water density ρ = 997 kg/m³.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
