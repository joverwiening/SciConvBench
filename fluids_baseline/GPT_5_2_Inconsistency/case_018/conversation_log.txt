Q: What definition of the kinetic-energy correction factor α do you want me to use (e.g., α = (1/A V̄^3) ∫_A v^3 dA)?
A: Use the standard definition:

\[
\alpha=\frac{1}{A\,\bar V^{3}}\int_A v^{3}\,dA
\]

and evaluate it using the parabolic fully developed laminar pipe profile \(v(r)=2\bar V\left(1-(r/R)^2\right)\). This should give \(\alpha=2\).
