Calculate the stagnation enthalpy increase for a liquid water jet moving at 20 m/s.
Assume a typical inlet static temperature of 293 K (20 °C) if needed.
Use liquid-water specific heat at constant pressure cp ≈ 4.18 kJ·kg⁻¹·K⁻¹ (4180 J·kg⁻¹·K⁻¹) at about 293 K, treated as constant over the small change.
Report stagnation enthalpy per unit mass, not a rate, in J/kg or kJ/kg.
Report the stagnation enthalpy as an increase relative to the inlet static enthalpy, using Δh0 = v²/2.
Neglect potential-energy (elevation) effects and use only h0 = h + v²/2.
