Compute the net radiative heat loss using q = ε σ A (Ts^4 − Tsur^4).
Assume the surface area A = 1 m².
Use Kelvin for temperatures.
Assume the surface temperature Ts = 400 K.
Assume the surrounding temperature Tsur = 300 K.
Use the Stefan–Boltzmann constant σ = 5.670374419×10⁻⁸ W/(m²·K⁴).
Do not use ε = 1.3 because emissivity must be between 0 and 1.
Use a physically valid emissivity ε = 0.8.
Report the result in watts (W) as the net radiative heat loss from the 1 m² surface.
