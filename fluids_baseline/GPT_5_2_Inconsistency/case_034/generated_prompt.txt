Water flows from a reservoir free surface at elevation z1 = 0 m to a pipe discharge at elevation z2 = 5 m through a frictionless pipe.
Include a pump head as added head in the flow direction: hp = +20 m in the Bernoulli equation.
Assume no turbine and no head losses.
Assume the reservoir free surface and the discharge are both open to atmosphere: p1 = p2 = patm (0 gauge).
Assume the reservoir free-surface velocity is negligible: v1 ≈ 0.
Assume the discharge is a free discharge to atmosphere so the exit pressure at the pipe outlet is atmospheric.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Using Bernoulli with these assumptions, determine the exit velocity v2.
