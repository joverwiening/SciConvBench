Calculate the mass flow of air through a converging nozzle discharging from a tank at stagnation pressure p0 = 500 kPa to ambient/back pressure pb = 100 kPa, assuming the nozzle exit pressure equals 100 kPa.
Assume the tank/stagnation temperature is T0 = 300 K.
Treat air as an ideal gas with constant properties using γ = 1.4 and R = 287 J/(kg·K).
Assume isentropic nozzle flow with no losses (Cd = 1).
Assume the nozzle is choked (sonic at the throat, M = 1) because pb < p* where p*/p0 = 0.528.
Since the nozzle area is not specified, leave the result in terms of area by reporting the mass flux m_dot/At.
Report the final result as mass flux m_dot/At in units of kg·s^-1·m^-2.
