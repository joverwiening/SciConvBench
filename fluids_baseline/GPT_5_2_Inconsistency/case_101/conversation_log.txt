Q: What specific form of “momentum flux” do you want computed (e.g., \(\int_A \rho u^2\,dA\) or \(\rho \bar{u}^2 A\))?
A: Compute the momentum flux as the cross-sectional integral  
\[
\int_A \rho u^2\, dA,
\]
and express it using the correction factor as  
\[
\int_A \rho u^2\, dA \;=\; \beta\, \rho\, \bar{u}^2\, A
\]
with \(\beta = \tfrac{4}{3}\) for fully developed laminar flow in a circular pipe.

Q: What are the given values (or symbols to leave in the final expression) for \(\rho\), \(\bar{u}\), and the pipe radius/diameter (to determine \(A\))?
A: No numerical values are specified in the complete requirement. Leave them symbolic:

- Density: \(\rho\)
- Mean velocity: \(\bar{u}\)
- Cross-sectional area: \(A\), with \(A=\pi R^2\) (or \(A=\pi D^2/4\) if using diameter)

Use \(\beta=\tfrac{4}{3}\) in \(\int_A \rho u^2\,dA=\beta\,\rho\,\bar{u}^2\,A\).
