Compute the momentum flux for fully developed laminar flow in a circular pipe as the cross-sectional integral ∫_A ρ u^2 dA.
Express the result using the momentum correction factor relation ∫_A ρ u^2 dA = β ρ \bar{u}^2 A.
Use β = 4/3 for fully developed laminar flow in a circular pipe.
Leave density ρ symbolic.
Leave mean velocity \bar{u} symbolic.
Leave cross-sectional area A symbolic.
Allow A to be written as A = πR^2 or equivalently A = πD^2/4.
