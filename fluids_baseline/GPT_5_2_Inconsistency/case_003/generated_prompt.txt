Determine the increase in exit velocity compared to the inlet for air entering a frictionless converging nozzle.
Use quasi-1D isentropic flow with constant stagnation temperature and no shocks.
Assume calorically perfect air with γ = 1.4.
Treat station 1 as a general supersonic inlet section with M1 = 2.0 at area A1.
Use the given area ratio A2/A1 = 0.7 with A2 < A1, and treat station 2 as the nozzle exit.
Find the exit Mach number using the isentropic area–Mach relation on the supersonic branch.
Report the velocity change as ΔV/a1.
If a numerical ΔV in m/s is also provided, assume T1 = 288 K and use R = 287 J/(kg·K) (or 287.05 J/(kg·K)) to compute a1 = sqrt(γ R T1).
