Calculate the pressure drop between inlet and outlet for water flowing through a 40 m long, 50 mm diameter horizontal pipe at a mean velocity of 2.0 m/s.
Assume the flow is fully developed and viscous.
Use water properties at 20°C: density ρ = 998 kg/m³.
Use dynamic viscosity μ = 1.0 × 10⁻³ Pa·s.
Compute Reynolds number as Re = (ρ v D) / μ.
Select the Darcy friction factor based on Reynolds number: if Re < 2300 use f_D = 64/Re.
If turbulent in a smooth pipe, use the Blasius correlation f_D = 0.3164 Re^(-0.25).
Assume the pipe is hydraulically smooth and neglect roughness.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Use Bernoulli’s equation with head loss represented via friction to relate inlet and outlet pressures for a horizontal pipe with equal inlet and outlet velocities.
Report the pressure drop in Pascals (Pa).
