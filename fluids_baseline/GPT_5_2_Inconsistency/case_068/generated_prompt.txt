Compute the Darcy–Weisbach head loss in a pipe using a physically valid positive Darcy friction factor and the standard head-loss formula.
Use Darcy friction factor f = 0.02 (replace the invalid negative value f = -0.02).
Assume the fluid is water at approximately 20 °C.
Assume pipe length L = 100 m.
Assume pipe diameter D = 0.10 m.
Assume mean flow velocity V = 2.0 m/s.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
