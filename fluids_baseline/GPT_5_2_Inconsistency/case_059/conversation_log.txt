Q: Should the critical depth be computed from the given discharge per unit width q = 1.0 m²/s (ignoring the inconsistent y and V values)?
A: Yes. Compute the critical depth solely from the given discharge per unit width \(q = 1.0\ \text{m}^2/\text{s}\) using  
\[
y_c=\left(\frac{q^2}{g}\right)^{1/3}
\]
(i.e., ignore the inconsistent \(y\) and \(V\) values).
