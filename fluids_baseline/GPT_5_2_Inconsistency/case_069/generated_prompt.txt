Use Bernoulli’s equation to determine the pressure difference between an inlet and an outlet in a pipe where the outlet is 15 m higher than the inlet.
Assume the fluid is incompressible water with density ρ = 1000 kg/m³.
Assume the inlet and outlet velocities are equal so kinetic-energy terms cancel.
Neglect head losses (assume inviscid/no friction and no minor losses).
Use the sign convention ΔP = Pinlet − Poutlet, which should be positive for an outlet 15 m higher than the inlet.
