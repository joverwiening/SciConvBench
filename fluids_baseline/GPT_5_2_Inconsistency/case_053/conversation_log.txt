Q: What characteristic length definition should I use for the sphere in the Biot number (e.g., \(L=R/3\) or \(L=R\))?
A: Use the **lumped-capacitance characteristic length** \(L_c = V/A\).

For a sphere:
\[
L_c=\frac{V}{A}=\frac{\frac{4}{3}\pi R^3}{4\pi R^2}=\frac{R}{3}.
\]

So in \( \mathrm{Bi} = \dfrac{hL}{k} \), take \(L = R/3\).
