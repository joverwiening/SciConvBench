Q: What definition of pressure coefficient \(C_p\) should I use (e.g., \(C_p = \frac{p - p_\infty}{\tfrac{1}{2}\rho U_\infty^2}\))?
A: Use the standard incompressible definition:

\[
C_p=\frac{p-p_\infty}{\tfrac12\,\rho\,U_\infty^2}.
\]

At the stagnation point use \(p=p_0\), so

\[
C_{p0}=\frac{p_0-p_\infty}{\tfrac12\,\rho\,U_\infty^2}=1
\]

(from Bernoulli for incompressible inviscid flow). If you’re getting \(C_p>1\), re-check the definition or note it may indicate compressibility/measurement/definition issues.
