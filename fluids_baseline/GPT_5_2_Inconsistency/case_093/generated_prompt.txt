For incompressible flow around a body, verify the stagnation-point pressure coefficient using Bernoulli.
Show the result symbolically in terms of the free-stream speed \(U_\infty\).
Use the standard incompressible pressure coefficient definition \(C_p=\frac{p-p_\infty}{\tfrac12\,\rho\,U_\infty^2}\).
At the stagnation point use \(p=p_0\) and \(U=0\).
Assume incompressible, inviscid (potential) flow so Bernoulli applies along a streamline from the free stream to the stagnation point.
Use Bernoulli to relate \(p_0\) and \(p_\infty\) and compute \(C_{p0}\) from that relation.
