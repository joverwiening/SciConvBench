Compute the heat removed when steam at 100 C condenses to liquid water at 100 C in a condenser.
Assume a basis of 1 kg of steam condensed.
Use the physically correct latent heat of condensation at 100 C from steam tables, using q = m·h_fg.
Assume the condensate leaves as saturated liquid at 100 C with no subcooling.
Do not use q = m·cp·ΔT with ΔT = 0 for the phase change.
