Compute the required heat-transfer area for a counterflow heat exchanger using the parallel-flow LMTD expression.
Use ΔT1 = Th,in − Tc,in.
Use ΔT2 = Th,out − Tc,out.
Assume Th,in = 150 °C.
Assume Tc,in = 25 °C.
Assume Th,out = 100 °C.
Assume Tc,out = 80 °C.
Assume overall heat-transfer coefficient U = 500 W/m²·K.
Assume heat duty Q = 100 kW.
Apply a 20% design margin to U such that U_design = 0.8·U.
Report the required heat-transfer area in m².
