Calculate the critical insulation thickness for a cylindrical pipe with these specifications:
Thermal conductivity of insulation (k) is 0.17 W/m·K.
Convective heat transfer coefficient (h) is 8 W/m²·K.
Assume a standard industrial pipe outer radius of 0.0254 meters (1 inch).
Assume a typical temperature difference of 50°C between pipe surface and ambient air.
Use the formula rcr = k/h where rcr is the critical radius of insulation.
