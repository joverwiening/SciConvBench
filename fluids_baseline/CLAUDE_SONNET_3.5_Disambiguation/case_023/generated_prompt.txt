Calculate the pressure drop per unit length in a packed bed with the following specifications:
The packed bed contains spherical particles of 5 mm diameter.
The fluid is air at standard atmospheric pressure and temperature.
The superficial velocity of air is 0.5 m/s.
The calculation should use the Ergun equation or a suitable correlation for packed bed pressure drop.
The bed void fraction should be assumed based on typical values for randomly packed spheres.
The result should be expressed in pressure drop per meter of bed length.
