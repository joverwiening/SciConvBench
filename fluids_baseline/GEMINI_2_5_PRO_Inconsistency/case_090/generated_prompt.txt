Compute the thermal stress for a fully constrained steel rod.
The temperature of the rod increases by 50 K.
Use the formula: thermal stress = E * alpha * ΔT.
Assume the Modulus of Elasticity (E) for steel is 200 GPa.
Assume the coefficient of thermal expansion (alpha) for steel is 12 x 10^-6 per Kelvin.
The final result for the thermal stress should be expressed in Megapascals (MPa).
