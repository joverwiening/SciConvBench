Compute the ratios of downstream to upstream conditions across a normal shock.
The fluid is air, which should be treated as an ideal gas.
The upstream Mach number is 2.0.
The ratio of specific heats, gamma, is 1.4.
The required outputs are the ratios for static pressure (p2/p1), static temperature (T2/T1), static density (rho2/rho1), stagnation pressure (p02/p01), and the value of the downstream Mach number (M2).
