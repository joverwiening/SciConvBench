Compute the pressure difference between the inner and outer side of a curved streamline.
The flow is water with a density of 1000 kg/m^3.
The speed of the flow along the streamline is 10 m/s.
The radius of curvature of the streamline is 5 m.
The normal distance between the inner and outer side is 0.10 m.
The calculation should not account for the effects of gravity.
The pressure increases toward the center of curvature.
