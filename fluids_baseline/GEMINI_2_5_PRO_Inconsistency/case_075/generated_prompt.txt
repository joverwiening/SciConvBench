Treat liquid water as an incompressible fluid.
The density of the water is 998 kg/m^3.
The water is flowing through a horizontal pipe that changes in diameter.
The initial pressure at the first point is 200,000 Pascals.
The initial velocity of the water at the first point is 1 m/s.
The diameter of the pipe at the first point is 0.1 meters.
The diameter of the pipe at the second point is 0.05 meters.
Apply Bernoulli's equation to this system.
Calculate the pressure of the water at the second point in the pipe.
