Compute the flow rate through a Venturi meter from a given pressure difference.
The calculation will be based on the Bernoulli equation.
The model must account for irreversible losses using a discharge coefficient, Cd.
The discharge coefficient, Cd, is a variable input for the calculation.
The pressure difference, Δp, is a variable input for the calculation.
Assume the fluid is water with a density of 1000 kg/m^3.
Assume the inlet pipe has a diameter of 0.1 meters.
Assume the throat has a diameter of 0.05 meters.
