Compute the Rayleigh number for natural convection from a vertical plate.
The fluid for the convection is air at atmospheric pressure.
The surface temperature of the plate is 80°C.
The ambient fluid temperature is 20°C.
The characteristic length of the plate is 0.5 m.
The gravitational acceleration is 9.81 m/s².
The film temperature is 50°C.
The coefficient of thermal expansion, beta, is calculated as the reciprocal of the film temperature expressed in degrees Celsius (1/50 °C⁻¹).
Use a kinematic viscosity for air at 50°C of 1.82 x 10⁻⁵ m²/s.
Use a thermal diffusivity for air at 50°C of 2.59 x 10⁻⁵ m²/s.
The Rayleigh number is calculated using the formula Ra = g * beta * (Ts - T∞) * L³ / (ν * α).
