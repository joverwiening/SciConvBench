Compute the drag force on a sphere.
The sphere has a diameter of 5 mm.

Assume the water temperature is 20°C to determine its density and viscosity.
First, calculate the Reynolds number for the sphere.
Based on the Reynolds number, use the drag force equation Fd = (1/2) * rho * V^2 * Cd * A.
