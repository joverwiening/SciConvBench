Solve the steady-state one-dimensional heat conduction equation, d²T/dx² = 0, for a slab.
The slab has a length of 1 meter.
The boundary condition at the left end (x=0) is a fixed temperature of 100 degrees Celsius.
The boundary condition at the right end (x=1) is a fixed temperature of 20 degrees Celsius.
The thermal diffusivity is not needed for this steady-state problem.
Provide the final temperature distribution T(x) as a function of position x.
