Calculate the boundary-layer thickness for air flowing over a flat plate.
The free-stream velocity is 30 m/s.
The kinematic viscosity is 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The calculation is for the position x = 1.0 m from the leading edge.
The boundary layer at this position is laminar.
Use the formula δ = 5 * x / sqrt(Re_x) to find the boundary-layer thickness.
