Solve the Stokes equations for a fluid flow problem.
The flow has a Reynolds number of 0.1.
The convective acceleration terms in the Navier-Stokes equation are neglected.
The geometry is an unbounded fluid domain containing a solid sphere.
The radius of the sphere is 1 millimeter.
The fluid is water with a density of 1000 kg/m^3.
The fluid has a dynamic viscosity of 0.001 Pa·s.
The freestream velocity of the fluid far from the sphere is 0.00005 m/s.
The solution should provide the velocity field.
The solution should provide the pressure field.
The solution should provide the total drag force on the sphere.
