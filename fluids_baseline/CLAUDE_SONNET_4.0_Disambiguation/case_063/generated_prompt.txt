Determine if the flow is laminar or turbulent for water flowing through a smooth pipe.
The pipe diameter is 4 cm.
The flow velocity of the water is 1.5 m/s.
Use standard properties for water at room temperature.
Calculate the Reynolds number to determine the flow regime.
Compare the Reynolds number to the critical value of 2300 for pipe flow.
If Reynolds number is less than 2300, the flow is laminar.
If Reynolds number is greater than 4000, the flow is turbulent.
If Reynolds number is between 2300 and 4000, the flow is in the transition region.
