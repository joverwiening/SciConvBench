Calculate the Biot number for a steel sphere with radius 5 mm and thermal conductivity 45 W/m·K.
The sphere is being cooled by quenching in an oil bath with convective heat transfer coefficient 150 W/m²·K.
Use the standard Biot number formula Bi = hL_c/k where L_c is the characteristic length.
For a sphere, the characteristic length is radius/3.
