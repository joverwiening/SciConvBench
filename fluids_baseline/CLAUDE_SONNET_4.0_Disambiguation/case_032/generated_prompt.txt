Calculate the Weber number for a 2 mm diameter fuel droplet moving at 30 m/s through air.
The fuel has a surface tension of 0.03 N/m and density of 800 kg/m³.
Use a reasonable assumption for air density based on standard conditions.
The Weber number should be calculated using the formula We = (ρ_gas × v² × d) / σ where ρ_gas is air density, v is velocity, d is diameter, and σ is surface tension.
