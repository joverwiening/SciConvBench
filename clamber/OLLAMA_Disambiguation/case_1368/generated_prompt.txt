Write a detailed report of approximately 5‑7 pages on the impact of drift (the wind‑driven movement of sand, sediments, organic debris, and other particles) on coastal ecosystems.  
Target the report to a technical audience of coastal managers and policymakers, using professional terminology while remaining clear and concise.  

Structure the report as follows:  

Introduce the concept of drift and its primary mechanisms (aeolian transport, salt‑marsh breezes, storm‑driven surges).  
Describe how drift influences coastal geomorphology, including dune formation, shoreline erosion, and sediment deposition patterns.  
Explain ecological effects, covering habitat alteration for flora and fauna, changes in nutrient cycling, impacts on species diversity, and the role of drift in invasive species spread.  
Provide two brief case studies illustrating both negative and positive outcomes of drift in different coastal settings.  
Discuss management implications, recommending monitoring strategies, mitigation measures (e.g., vegetation stabilization, windbreaks), and adaptive policies to balance sediment dynamics with ecosystem health.  
Conclude with actionable recommendations for coastal managers and policymakers.  
Include a reference list of recent peer‑reviewed literature and relevant guidelines.  
Suggest illustrative figures such as maps of drift pathways, before‑and‑after photos of dune migration, and flow diagrams of nutrient transport.
