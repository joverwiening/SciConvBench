Dr. Robert Laing moves into a newly built luxury tower in London that is designed as a self‑contained, class‑segregated micro‑society.  
The building’s residents are divided by floor, with the lower levels housing the working class, the middle floors the middle class, and the upper floors the elite.  
When the tower’s infrastructure—water, electricity, and waste disposal—begins to break down, the social order collapses into violent clashes between the classes.  
Laing becomes caught between the charismatic, anarchic resident Dr. John, the “architect,” and the ruthless upper‑class “Lord of the Tower.”  
The film culminates in a brutal, almost apocalyptic showdown as the tower’s physical decay mirrors the moral decay of its inhabitants, leaving the survivors in a savage, animalistic state.
