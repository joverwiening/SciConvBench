A tomboy is a girl who exhibits behaviors, interests, or styles that are traditionally associated with boys.  
She may prefer masculine clothing, such as shorts, t‑shirts, or sneakers, over dresses or skirts.  
She often enjoys activities stereotypically linked to boys, such as sports, rough play, or mechanical hobbies.  
The term describes a pattern of gender‑nonconforming expression and does not imply any specific sexual orientation or gender identity.  
While some people use the word positively to celebrate diverse expressions of gender, others consider it limiting or stereotypical.
