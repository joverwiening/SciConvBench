When he realizes he has “blown” (i.e., caused a failure or ruined a situation), he should first acknowledge the mistake openly.  
He should promptly inform all relevant stakeholders about what happened and its immediate impact.  
He must analyze the root cause of the failure to understand why it occurred.  
Based on that analysis, he should develop a clear remediation plan that outlines specific corrective actions, responsibilities, and timelines.  
He then implements the corrective actions according to the plan, monitoring progress and adjusting as needed.  
After the issue is resolved, he documents the incident, the lessons learned, and updates any relevant processes or guidelines to prevent recurrence.  
Finally, he follows up with stakeholders to confirm that the resolution meets expectations and restores confidence.
