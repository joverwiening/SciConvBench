Podcasts are audio (and sometimes video) series that deliver content on a wide range of topics to listeners on demand.  
They allow creators to share information, stories, interviews, education, entertainment, or commentary in an accessible, portable format.  
Podcasts enable audiences to consume content at their convenience, such as while commuting, exercising, or relaxing.  
They foster community and engagement by building loyal listener bases who can subscribe, comment, and share episodes.  
Podcasts also provide creators with a platform for personal branding, monetization, and reaching niche audiences without traditional broadcasting constraints.
