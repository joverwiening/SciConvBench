The bench refers to the judges who sit on it and symbolizes the authority of the court.  
The bench presides over all courtroom proceedings and ensures they follow proper legal rules and procedures.  
The bench enforces courtroom order, addressing disruptions and managing the conduct of attorneys, witnesses, jurors, and the public.  
The bench interprets and applies the law to the facts presented, ruling on motions, admitting evidence, and giving legal instructions to juries.  
The bench renders judgments, verdicts, or sentences after evaluating the evidence and arguments of the parties.  
The bench oversees the administration of justice, including managing case schedules, supervising court staff, and protecting the rights of all participants.
