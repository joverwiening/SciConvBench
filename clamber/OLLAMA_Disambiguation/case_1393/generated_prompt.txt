A will is a legal document that records a person’s wishes for the distribution of their assets after death.  
It ensures that property, money, and personal belongings go to the intended beneficiaries, reducing the risk of disputes among heirs.  
By naming an executor, a will provides clear authority for someone to manage the estate, pay debts, and handle taxes.  
Having a will can also appoint guardians for minor children, protecting their welfare and providing stability.  
Overall, a will offers peace of mind by giving control over one’s legacy and simplifying the probate process.
