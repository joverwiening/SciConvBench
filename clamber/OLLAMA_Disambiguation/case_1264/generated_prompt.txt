James Bond is sent to investigate the murder of a British agent in the Indian city of Bombay.  
The investigation leads him to a wealthy Indian businessman named Kamal Khan, who is involved in a smuggling operation.  
Bond discovers that Khan is working for a mysterious woman known only as Octopussy, who runs a circus and a secret smuggling ring.  
Octopussy’s circus travels across Europe, using its performances as cover to transport valuable items and weapons.  
Bond follows the trail to a remote Scottish island where Octopussy’s base is located.  
He learns that Octopussy plans to use a Soviet nuclear warhead to detonate a nuclear explosion over a U.S. air base in West Germany, creating a massive electromagnetic pulse.  
The EMP would disable the United States’ electronic defenses, allowing a Soviet invasion of Western Europe.  
Octopussy intends to sell the warhead to the highest bidder, and the Soviet Union is the ultimate client.  
Bond infiltrates the island, discovers the warhead hidden in a large, ornate statue, and confronts Octopussy’s henchmen.  
He is captured and taken to the island’s underground vault, where Octopussy reveals her plan and offers him a share of the profit.  
Bond refuses and escapes, fighting his way through the circus performers and security personnel.  
He disables the warhead’s timer and triggers an explosion that destroys the island’s main facility, but the warhead itself remains intact.  
Bond then pursues Octopussy’s yacht, which is attempting to flee the scene.  
A high‑speed chase ensues on the water, during which Bond boards the yacht and engages in a hand‑to‑hand fight with Octopussy’s chief operative, Major Dexter Smythe.  
Bond defeats Smythe, secures the warhead, and orders the yacht to be scuttled.  
He returns the warhead to MI6, where M commends him for preventing a global catastrophe.  
Octopussy’s circus is dismantled, and the surviving members are arrested.  
Bond receives a new Aston Martin DB5 equipped with advanced gadgets for his next mission.  
The film ends with Bond relaxing on a beach, reflecting on the strange events he has just survived.
