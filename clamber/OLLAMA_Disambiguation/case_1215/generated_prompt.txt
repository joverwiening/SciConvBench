Spencer Strasmore, a retired NFL player, becomes a sports‑management agent and financial advisor for professional athletes. He uses his insider knowledge of the league to help clients grow their wealth while navigating the high‑stakes world of football, celebrity, and personal ambition. The series follows Spencer and his team at the firm as they negotiate contracts, manage scandals, and balance personal relationships, including his friendship with former teammate Jason Antwi and his romantic involvement with his colleague Julie. Over the seasons, Spencer confronts challenges such as rival agencies, legal troubles, and his own desire for legitimacy in the business world, while his clients, including rising star Ricky Jerret and veteran quarterback Vernon Littlefield, pursue their own career and personal goals. The show blends drama and comedy to explore the intersection of sports, finance, and personal identity in the lives of modern athletes.
