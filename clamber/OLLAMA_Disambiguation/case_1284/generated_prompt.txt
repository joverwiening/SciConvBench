Spectre is a term that can refer to several different concepts depending on the context.  
In general English usage, a spectre (or specter) means a ghost or a haunting apparition.  
In computer security, Spectre is a class of vulnerabilities that exploit speculative execution in modern processors to potentially leak sensitive data.  
In popular culture, Spectre is the name of a fictional criminal organization in the James Bond series.  
The specific meaning depends on the domain in which the term is being used.
