Use a smooth cylindrical stamp with a diameter of 20 mm made of metal or silicone.  
Condition the modeling clay by kneading it until it is uniformly soft and at room temperature.  
Place the clay on a flat, stable work surface.  
Position the stamp vertically above the desired location on the clay.  
Apply steady, even vertical pressure until the stamp penetrates to a depth of approximately 5 mm.  
Maintain the pressure for 2 to 3 seconds to allow the material to conform to the stamp shape.  
Release the pressure slowly and lift the stamp straight up to avoid tearing the impression.  
If a deeper impression is required, repeat the pressing step with the same stamp, increasing pressure incrementally.  
Smooth any rough edges around the impression with a soft tool or fingertip if needed.  
Clean the stamp after use to prevent clay residue from affecting future impressions.
