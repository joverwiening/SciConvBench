The fail‑safe system for nuclear weapons ensures that weapons cannot be launched unintentionally or without proper authorization.  
It continuously monitors for faults, errors, or unauthorized actions.  
When a fault or unauthorized action is detected, the system isolates and disables the launch mechanisms.  
It then forces the weapon into a predefined safe, non‑operational state.  
This prevents accidental or unauthorized nuclear detonation and protects personnel, equipment, and the environment.
