Wah‑Wah is a guitar (or other instrument) effect that creates a vocal-like “wah” sound by rhythmically altering the frequency spectrum of the signal. It is produced by a foot‑controlled pedal that sweeps a band‑pass filter up and down in frequency, emphasizing different harmonics as the pedal is moved. The effect is commonly used in funk, rock, and jazz music to add expression and dynamic texture.
