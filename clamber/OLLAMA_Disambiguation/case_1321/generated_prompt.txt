Write a descriptive passage that explains the potential consequences of a plant being uprooted, narrated from the perspective of the roots. The description should cover physical effects (such as loss of soil support, exposure to air, damage to root cells), physiological impacts (disruption of water and nutrient uptake, stress responses, possible wilting or death), and emotional or sensory impressions that roots might "feel" (such as confusion, panic, or a sense of loss). Use vivid, sensory language to convey the roots' experience. Aim for a length of approximately 250 words, organized in clear, flowing sentences without bullet points. Ensure the tone is empathetic and immersive, reflecting the roots' point of view.
