Yentl is a 1983 musical drama directed by Barbra Streisand about a young Jewish woman in early‑20th‑century Poland who disguises herself as a man to study Torah. After her father dies, she adopts the male identity “Anshel” and enrolls in a yeshiva, impressing the scholars with her intelligence. She befriends Avigdor, a fellow student, and falls in love with him while also forming a bond with the stern Rabbi who mentors her. When her true gender is revealed, the community reacts with scandal, but the Rabbi ultimately acknowledges her brilliance and grants her a place as a teacher. The film explores themes of gender identity, tradition, and the pursuit of knowledge.
