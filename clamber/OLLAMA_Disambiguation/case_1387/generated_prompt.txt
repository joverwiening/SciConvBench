The console housing shall be a 19‑inch rack‑mount enclosure designed to accommodate typical electronic instruments such as mixers, amplifiers, signal processors, power supplies, and control panels.  
The overall dimensions shall be approximately 19 inches (48 mm) wide, 24 inches (610 mm) deep, and up to 42 U high (≈73 inches or 1850 mm tall).  
The frame shall be constructed from cold‑rolled steel with a powder‑coated finish for durability and corrosion resistance.  
Each rack unit shall provide a load capacity of at least 50 lb (23 kg) and include standardized 19‑inch mounting rails with adjustable brackets.  
Ventilation shall be achieved through perforated side panels and a front‑to‑rear airflow path, supplemented by two 120 mm variable‑speed fans with thermal sensors for temperature regulation.  
Cable management shall include vertical cable trays, rear‑panel grommets, and removable front access panels with lockable doors.  
All connectors and ports shall be positioned for easy access from the front and rear, with clear labeling and tool‑free removal of panels.  
The console shall incorporate a grounded power distribution module with surge protection, providing at least eight IEC‑C13 outlets and two IEC‑C19 outlets, each with individual circuit breakers.  
Safety compliance shall meet UL 60950‑1 (or IEC 60950‑1) and CE standards, including fire‑retardant interior padding and rounded interior edges.  
The enclosure shall include removable side panels for internal servicing, with quick‑release fasteners and optional lockable hinges.  
All mounting points shall be pre‑drilled to accept standard 10‑32 or M5 hardware, allowing secure attachment of equipment and accessories.  
The console shall be delivered with an assembly manual, a parts list, and a warranty of three years covering structural defects and finish.
