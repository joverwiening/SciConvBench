Serve is the action of initiating play by delivering the ball into the field of play according to the rules of the sport.  
It is performed by a player who stands in a designated area and strikes or tosses the ball to start a rally or point.  
The serve must comply with specific regulations such as placement, height, and motion that vary by sport.  
If the serve is executed correctly, the ball is considered in play and the opposing player or team must return it.  
An illegal or faulty serve results in a loss of point or a replay, depending on the sport’s scoring rules.
