Emergency! follows Los Angeles County Fire Department paramedic John Gage and his brother, firefighter‑paramedic Roy DeSoto, as they respond to a series of urgent medical and rescue calls in early‑1970s Los Angeles. The brothers race against time to treat heart attacks, car crashes, fires, and other crises, showcasing the challenges and teamwork of pioneering pre‑hospital emergency care. Their efforts highlight the emerging field of modern emergency medical services and the personal dedication required to save lives.
