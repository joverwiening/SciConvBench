The system receives three sentences: two example sentences and one target sentence.  
Each sentence contains exactly one word that belongs to one of two possible categories: “animal” or “outdoor location”.  
From the two example sentences, determine which single category is represented in both examples.  
If both examples contain a word from the same category, that category is the specific word category to be used for classification.  
For the target sentence, examine its words.  
If the target sentence includes at least one word that belongs to the identified specific category, output the single character X.  
If the target sentence does not include any word from the identified specific category, output the single character Y.  
Word matching is case‑insensitive and considers whole words only (sub‑strings are not counted).  
The categories are defined as follows:  
- “animal”: any noun that denotes a non‑human animal (e.g., lion, tiger, dog, cat, etc.).  
- “outdoor location”: any noun that denotes a natural or open‑air place (e.g., prairie, marsh, forest, desert, mountain, etc.).
