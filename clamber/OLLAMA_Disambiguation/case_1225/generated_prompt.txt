The Conjuring (2013) follows paranormal investigators Ed and Lorraine Warren as they are called to assist the Perron family, who have moved into a secluded Rhode Island farmhouse in 1971.  
The family soon experiences terrifying supernatural events, including unexplained noises, objects moving on their own, and frightening apparitions.  
The Warrens discover that the house is haunted by the malevolent spirit of Bathsheba, a 19th‑century witch executed for sacrificing her newborn child.  
As the hauntings intensify, the Warrens investigate the property’s dark history and the curse binding Bathsheba to the home.  
They perform a harrowing exorcism, confronting terrifying manifestations and risking their own safety.  
In the end, they succeed in banishing the spirit, saving the Perron family, and documenting the case for future investigations.
