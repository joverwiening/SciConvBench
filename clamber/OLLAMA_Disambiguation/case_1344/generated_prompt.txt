Backups are performed to protect against data loss caused by hardware failures such as disk crashes or controller malfunctions.  
Backups mitigate the risk of accidental deletion or overwriting of critical files by users or applications.  
Backups provide a recovery point in case of software bugs, corrupted updates, or faulty patches that damage data integrity.  
Backups guard against ransomware, malware, or other malicious attacks that encrypt or destroy original data.  
Backups satisfy regulatory and compliance requirements that mandate data retention for specific periods.  
Backups enable business continuity by allowing rapid restoration of systems after natural disasters, power outages, or site failures.  
Backups support migration or upgrade projects by preserving a known-good state before moving data to new platforms.  
Backups facilitate testing and development environments by providing realistic data copies without affecting production.  
Backups allow archival of historical data for long‑term reference, analytics, or legal purposes.  
Backups provide a safety net for configuration drift, ensuring that system settings and application configurations can be restored to a baseline state.
