The vault shall be a reinforced concrete strongroom measuring approximately 2 m in length, 2 m in width, and 2.5 m in height, providing about 10 m³ of internal storage volume.  
The vault shall meet UL 72 Class 5 security rating (equivalent to EN 1143‑1 Grade 5) for burglary resistance.  
The vault shall have a fire‑resistance rating of at least 2 hours according to UL 72 fire test standards.  
The vault door shall be a solid steel door with a minimum thickness of 100 mm, equipped with a composite lock system.  
Access shall be controlled by a dual‑authentication system combining biometric fingerprint verification and RFID keycard, with a mandatory 5‑second time‑delay lockout after each successful entry.  
The vault shall include an intrusion detection alarm that triggers on forced entry, door tampering, or lock manipulation, and shall be connected to a monitored security system.  
Video surveillance cameras shall be installed covering the vault interior and exterior, recording continuously and storing footage for at least 30 days.  
The interior shall be fitted with adjustable steel shelving capable of supporting up to 500 kg per shelf.  
The vault shall have a sealed ventilation system with fire‑rated dampers to maintain internal temperature while preserving fire resistance.  
All construction materials shall be certified to meet relevant ASTM and ISO standards for durability and corrosion resistance.
