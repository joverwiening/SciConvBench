Men in Black refers to a popular cultural concept involving secret government agents who monitor and conceal extraterrestrial activity on Earth.  
The term is most widely known from the 1997 science‑fiction comedy film “Men in Black” and its sequels, which depict a covert organization that polices alien life.  
In the movies, agents wear black suits, use advanced gadgets, and erase human memories of alien encounters with a neuralyzer.  
The concept has also appeared in comics, television series, and folklore about mysterious figures who silence witnesses to UFO sightings.  
The phrase is sometimes used colloquially to describe any secretive group that enforces confidentiality.
