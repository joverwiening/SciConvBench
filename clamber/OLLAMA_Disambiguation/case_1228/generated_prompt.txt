Gareth, a young knight, returns to his homeland after his father’s death, only to discover that the kingdom is ruled by the ruthless warlord Sir Robert.  
While fleeing an ambush, Gareth stumbles upon a dying dragon named Drago, captured and wounded by the warlord’s men.  
Drago offers to share his remaining life‑force with Gareth in exchange for help freeing the dragon and protecting the innocent.  
Gareth frees Drago, and the two form a bond that lets Gareth wield the dragon’s fire‑breath and strength.  
Together they travel to the besieged village of Loxley, where the villagers are being terrorized by Sir Robert’s forces.  
Guided by Drago’s ancient wisdom, Gareth rallies the villagers and leads a guerrilla resistance against the warlord’s army.  
In the climactic battle, Gareth confronts Sir Robert and, with Drago’s fire, defeats the warlord’s troops.  
Drago, mortally wounded, transfers the remainder of his heart’s magic to Gareth before dying, ensuring the dragon’s legacy lives on.  
The film ends with Gareth becoming the protector of Loxley, honoring his father’s memory and Drago’s sacrifice.
