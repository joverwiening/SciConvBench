True ribs are ribs 1 through 7 and attach directly to the sternum via their own costal cartilages. 
False ribs are ribs 8 through 10 and attach to the sternum indirectly through the cartilage of the rib above them. 
Floating ribs are ribs 11 and 12 and have no anterior attachment to the sternum, ending in the abdominal wall muscles.
