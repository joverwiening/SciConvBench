Q: The different types of ribs in human anatomy are:

True ribs (first‑through‑seven ribs) that attach directly to the sternum via their own costal cartilages.  
False ribs (eighth‑through‑tenth ribs) that connect to the sternum indirectly through the cartilage of the rib above them.  
Floating ribs (eleventh and twelfth ribs) that have no anterior attachment to the sternum and end in the muscle of the abdominal wall.
A: The different types of ribs in human anatomy are:

- **True ribs** (ribs 1‑7): attach directly to the sternum via their own costal cartilages.  
- **False ribs** (ribs 8‑10): attach to the sternum indirectly through the cartilage of the rib above them.  
- **Floating ribs** (ribs 11‑12): have no anterior attachment to the sternum; they end in the abdominal wall muscles.
