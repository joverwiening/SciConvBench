Super Fly follows Youngblood Priest, a charismatic cocaine dealer in Harlem who wants to retire with $1 million after one last big deal.  
He navigates betrayals, police pressure, and rival gangsters while planning his exit, relying on his girlfriend Eddie and his friend Fat Freddie.  
Priest’s plan unravels as Fat Freddie is killed and the police close in, leading to a final confrontation where Priest is shot and dies, leaving his dream unfulfilled.
