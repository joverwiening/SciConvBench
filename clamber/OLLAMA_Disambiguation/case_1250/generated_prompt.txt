Irreversible is a 2002 French experimental thriller directed by Gaspar Noé.  
The film follows a night of brutal violence and revenge in reverse chronological order, beginning with the aftermath and moving backward to the events that led to it.  
It stars Monica Bellucci, Vincent Cassel and Albert Dupontel, and is known for its graphic depiction of sexual assault and a long, unbroken tracking shot.  
The narrative structure emphasizes the inevitability and permanence of the characters' actions, underscoring the theme that some consequences cannot be undone.  
Noé employs stark lighting, disorienting camera movements, and a pulsating electronic soundtrack to create an intense, unsettling atmosphere.  
The film provokes strong reactions and has become a controversial and influential work in contemporary cinema.
