A sledgehammer is a large, heavy hammer with a long handle designed for delivering powerful blows.  
It is used to break up hard materials such as stone, concrete, or metal.  
The head of a sledgehammer is typically made of metal and weighs several pounds.  
The handle is usually made of wood, fiberglass, or steel to withstand the impact forces.  
The term “sledgehammer” can also be used metaphorically to describe a forceful or blunt approach.
