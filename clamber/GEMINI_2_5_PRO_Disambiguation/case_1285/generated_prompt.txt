The film follows Youngblood Priest, a successful and stylish cocaine dealer in Atlanta.
Priest decides he wants to retire from the criminal lifestyle after one final, massive score.
He plans this exit strategy with his trusted partner, Eddie.
An aggressive rival gang, the Sno Patrol, led by the volatile Juju, becomes a major obstacle.
After a member of Sno Patrol attacks him, Priest accelerates his plan to leave the city.
Priest bypasses his usual supplier and goes directly to the cartel source, a man named Adalberto.
He must also contend with corrupt police officers who are trying to blackmail and extort him.
Priest orchestrates a complex plan to frame the Sno Patrol for his own dealings.
He manipulates events to turn the cartel against the Sno Patrol.
In the climax, Priest outwits both the rival gang and the corrupt police.
He successfully secures his money, eliminates his enemies, and escapes with his girlfriend Georgia to live a new life abroad.
