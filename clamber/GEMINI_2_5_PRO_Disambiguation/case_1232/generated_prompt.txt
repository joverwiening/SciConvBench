The 1972 film "Emergency!" serves as the pilot for the television series of the same name.
The plot introduces paramedics John Gage and Roy DeSoto of Los Angeles County Fire Department Squad 51.
It follows them as they respond to various emergency calls, showcasing the new paramedic program.
A parallel storyline focuses on the staff at Rampart General Hospital, particularly Dr. Kelly Brackett, who champions the paramedic program against political and medical opposition.
The film highlights the challenges and procedures of this new field of emergency medicine.
The climax involves a large-scale tunnel rescue that tests the skills of the paramedics and the hospital staff, ultimately proving the program's value.
