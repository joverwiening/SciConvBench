The noise source is an industrial centrifugal fan located indoors.
An acoustic enclosure will be constructed around the fan.
The enclosure must reduce the noise level to below 85 dBA when measured one meter from any exterior surface.
The enclosure will be constructed from steel panels lined with mineral wool insulation.
The design will include acoustically treated intake and discharge silencers.
The silencers must not restrict the fan's required operational airflow.
An access door with acoustic seals will be integrated for maintenance.
The enclosure will be mounted on vibration isolation pads.
