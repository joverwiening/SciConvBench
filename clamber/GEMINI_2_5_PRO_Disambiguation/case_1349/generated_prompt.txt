The simulation models an object rising through a fluid.
The term "rose" describes the object's vertical upward movement.
The primary force causing the upward movement is buoyancy.
The forces of gravity and fluid drag oppose the upward movement.
The upward movement concludes when the object reaches the surface of the fluid.
The significance of "rose" in this context is the object's transition from a submerged state to a surface state due to a net positive vertical force.
