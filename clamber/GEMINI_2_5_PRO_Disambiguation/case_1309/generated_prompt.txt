The purpose of the vault is to securely store high-value physical assets.
The primary assets to be stored are precious metals, such as gold and silver bullion, and jewelry.
The vault must be capable of securely storing items with a total estimated value of up to $10 million USD.
The primary threat the vault must protect against is sophisticated burglary.
The vault must meet or exceed the UL 608 Class 2 standard for burglary resistance, protecting against tool, torch, and explosive attacks.
The vault must provide a minimum fire resistance rating of 2 hours.
Access will be controlled by a dual-authentication system requiring two separate individuals to be present.
The vault will be under continuous 24/7 video surveillance.
Any unauthorized access attempt will trigger a silent alarm to an external security monitoring service.
