The counter serves as the designated location for finalizing a customer's purchase.
A customer brings their selected items to the counter.
The cashier scans each item's barcode to register it in the point-of-sale system.
The point-of-sale system calculates the total cost, including any applicable taxes or discounts.
The cashier informs the customer of the final amount due.
The customer provides a method of payment, such as cash, credit card, or a mobile payment app.
The cashier processes the payment transaction through the point-of-sale system.
The cashier provides the customer with a printed or digital receipt for the transaction.
The cashier bags the purchased items for the customer.
The transaction is concluded when the customer takes their bagged items and receipt.
