The product sold is a B2B SaaS subscription.
Commission is calculated based on the Annual Contract Value (ACV).
The commission rate for new business is 10% of the ACV.
The commission rate for contract renewals is 5% of the ACV.
Commission is earned after the company receives the first payment from the customer.
A prorated portion of the commission will be clawed back if the customer cancels their contract within the first 12 months.
Earned commissions are paid to the salesperson on a monthly basis.
