The object is a uniform rectangular prism standing on a flat, horizontal surface.
The object's base of support is the area defined by the bottom face of the prism.
The object's center of mass is at its geometric center.
An object is considered tipped when it is tilted to the point where a vertical line drawn downwards from its center of mass falls outside its original base of support.
Once tipped, gravity will create a torque that causes the object to topple over completely.
