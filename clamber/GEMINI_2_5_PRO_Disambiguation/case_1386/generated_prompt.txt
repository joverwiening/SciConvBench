Ribs are classified into three types based on their attachment to the sternum.
The first type is true ribs, which connect directly to the sternum via their own costal cartilage.
Ribs 1 through 7 are classified as true ribs.
The second type is false ribs, which do not connect directly to the sternum.
Ribs 8 through 12 are classified as false ribs.
The third type, a subset of false ribs, is floating ribs, which do not connect to the sternum at all.
Ribs 11 and 12 are classified as floating ribs.
