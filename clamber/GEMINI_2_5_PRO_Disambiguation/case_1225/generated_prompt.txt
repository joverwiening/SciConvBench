In 1971, the Perron family moves into a farmhouse in Rhode Island and begins experiencing escalating paranormal events.
They contact paranormal investigators Ed and Lorraine Warren for assistance.
The Warrens determine the house is haunted by the malevolent spirit of a witch named Bathsheba Sherman.
Bathsheba cursed the land, causing anyone who lived there to suffer tragedy.
The Warrens attempt to gather evidence for an exorcism approved by the Catholic Church.
The spirit of Bathsheba possesses the mother, Carolyn Perron.
Carolyn is driven to try and kill her own children.
Ed Warren performs an emergency exorcism on Carolyn without official church approval.
Lorraine Warren helps Carolyn fight the possession by reminding her of a cherished family memory.
The exorcism is successful, and the spirit of Bathsheba is expelled from Carolyn's body.
The Perron family is saved, and the Warrens take an artifact from the house for their occult museum.
