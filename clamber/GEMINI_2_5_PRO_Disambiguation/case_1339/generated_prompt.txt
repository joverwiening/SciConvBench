A Manager assigns a responsibility to a Team Member.
The responsibility is documented as a specific task.
The task includes a clear description of the work required.
The task includes a defined, measurable outcome or deliverable.
The task has a specified deadline for completion.
The Team Member must formally acknowledge acceptance of the responsibility.
The Team Member is accountable for completing the task by the deadline.
Upon completion, the Team Member submits the deliverable to the Manager for review.
The Manager evaluates the deliverable against the initial requirements.
The responsibility is considered fulfilled once the Manager formally approves the deliverable.
