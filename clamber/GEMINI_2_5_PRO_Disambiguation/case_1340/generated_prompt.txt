The judge presides over courtroom proceedings to ensure fairness and order.
The judge rules on motions presented by the attorneys.
The judge decides on the admissibility of evidence based on legal rules.
The judge instructs the jury on the applicable laws.
In cases without a jury, the judge determines the facts and decides the outcome.
In criminal cases, the judge imposes the sentence upon a finding of guilt.
The judge is responsible for upholding the legal rights of all parties involved in the case.
