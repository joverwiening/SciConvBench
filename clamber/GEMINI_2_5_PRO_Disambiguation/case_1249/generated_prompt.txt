Ironweed is a common name for plants in the genus *Vernonia*.
The name "Ironweed" refers to the plant's tough, rigid stem, which persists through winter.
It is a tall, perennial wildflower, typically growing from 3 to 7 feet in height.
The plant produces clusters of small, vibrant purple flowers.
Ironweed typically blooms in the late summer and early fall.
It is native to eastern and central North America.
The plant is commonly found in moist prairies, pastures, and open woodlands.
It is highly valued by pollinators, especially butterflies.
