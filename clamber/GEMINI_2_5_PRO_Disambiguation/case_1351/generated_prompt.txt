The material being cut is Aluminum 6061-T6.
The cutting method is CNC end milling.
The primary success metric is a balance of high surface finish, dimensional accuracy, and material removal rate.
The key to success is selecting the correct spindle speed and feed rate for the specific tool and material combination.
Proper chip evacuation must be maintained throughout the cut.
Use of a suitable coolant or lubricant is required to manage heat and aid chip evacuation.
Tool and workpiece rigidity must be sufficient to prevent chatter.
