The console is designed to house electronic instruments in an industrial control room environment.
The console shall have external dimensions of approximately 2000mm in height, 600mm in width, and 800mm in depth.
The console's usable internal capacity shall be 42 rack units (42U).
The console shall be constructed from cold-rolled steel.
The enclosure must provide protection against dust and splashing water, meeting an IP54 rating.
The console will feature adjustable front and rear 19-inch mounting rails.
The console must support a static load capacity of at least 500 kg.
The front and rear doors shall be lockable and vented to facilitate airflow.
A roof-mounted fan tray shall be included for active thermal management.
Cable entry points shall be provided on the top and bottom panels.
The console will be finished with a light grey (RAL 7035) powder coat.
The console must be equipped with both leveling feet and casters.
