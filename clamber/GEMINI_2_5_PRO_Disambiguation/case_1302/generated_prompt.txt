The wah-wah effect is a type of audio filter.
It alters the tone and frequencies of an audio signal to create a distinctive sound.
This sound mimics the human voice saying the onomatopoeic "wah-wah".
The effect works by sweeping a band-pass filter up and down in frequency.
This sweep emphasizes and de-emphasizes different parts of the harmonic spectrum.
It is most often controlled by a foot-operated pedal.
