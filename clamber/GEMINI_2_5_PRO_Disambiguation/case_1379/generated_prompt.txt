The analysis will treat "the dead" as a metaphor for past projects and initiatives that have failed or been abandoned.
The context of "no progress" is interpreted as a need to learn from these past failures to enable future success.
The goal is to analyze the root causes of these failures to provide actionable recommendations.
Identify all significant projects or initiatives that were terminated or failed to meet their primary objectives within the last three years.
For each failed initiative, conduct a post-mortem analysis to identify the root causes of failure.
The analysis must examine factors including technical challenges, resource allocation, strategic alignment, market conditions, and team dynamics.
Synthesize the findings from all individual analyses to identify recurring patterns and systemic issues.
The final output will be a formal report summarizing these common failure patterns.
The report must conclude with a list of specific, actionable recommendations to mitigate these risks in future endeavors.
The intended audience for this report is the organization's senior leadership team.
