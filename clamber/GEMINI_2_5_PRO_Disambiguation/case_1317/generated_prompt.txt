The system is a rectangular block with a uniform mass distribution.
The block is placed on a flat, horizontal, and rigid surface.
The system is subject to a constant, uniform gravitational field acting perpendicular to the surface.
The stable condition is the orientation of the block that minimizes its gravitational potential energy.
This condition is achieved when the block's center of mass is at its lowest possible height from the surface.
The block rests on its face with the largest surface area to achieve this stable condition.
