The goal is to create a permanent impression in a material's surface for hardness testing.
The process used is the Brinell hardness test method.
The surface material is a flat piece of steel.
The object used to make the impression is a 10 mm diameter hardened steel sphere.
A force of 3000 kilogram-force is applied to the sphere, pressing it into the surface.
The force is applied perpendicularly to the steel surface.
The force is maintained for a duration of 10 to 15 seconds.
The force is then removed, leaving a permanent circular impression.
