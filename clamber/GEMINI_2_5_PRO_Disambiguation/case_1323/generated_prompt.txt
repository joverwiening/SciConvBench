The log serves as the official legal record of the ship's voyage.
It documents the vessel's position, course, and speed at regular intervals.
It records significant navigational events, such as changes in course or speed.
It details the weather and sea conditions encountered during the voyage.
It chronicles key operational activities, including engine orders and fuel consumption.
It serves as a record of all crew members on watch.
It documents safety drills, equipment tests, and maintenance activities.
It provides an official account of any incidents, accidents, or unusual occurrences.
The log is used to demonstrate compliance with maritime laws and regulations.
It can be used as primary evidence in legal or insurance investigations.
