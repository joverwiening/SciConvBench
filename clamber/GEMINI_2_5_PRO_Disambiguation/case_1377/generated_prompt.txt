Moles create extensive tunnel networks in lawns and gardens.
These tunnels can damage or sever the root systems of grass and plants.
Damaged grass roots lead to yellow or brown patches in the lawn.
The tunnels and mounds create an uneven ground surface, which can be a tripping hazard.
Mole mounds, also known as molehills, can smother the grass beneath them.
Mole activity can indirectly damage young trees and shrubs by disrupting their root systems.
Moles do not typically pose a direct physical threat to humans or pets.
