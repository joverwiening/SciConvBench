Nancy Adams, a medical student, travels to a secluded beach in Mexico after her mother's death.
While surfing, she is attacked by a great white shark and stranded on a small rock 200 yards from shore.
With her leg severely injured, she uses her medical knowledge to temporarily patch the wound.
She must survive against the circling shark, the changing tides, and the elements.
Nancy uses her wits and observation to time the shark's patterns.
In a final confrontation, she uses a buoy and its anchor chain to kill the shark.
She is finally rescued and washes ashore, alive.
