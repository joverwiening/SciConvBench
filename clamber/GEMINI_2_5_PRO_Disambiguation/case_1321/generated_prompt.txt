The analysis will identify the potential consequences for an adult human being uprooted for an international job relocation.
The destination country is assumed to have a significantly different culture and language from the country of origin.
The analysis must cover psychological consequences, such as stress, identity crisis, and loneliness.
The analysis must cover social consequences, including the loss of existing social networks and the challenge of building new ones.
The analysis must cover economic consequences, such as changes in financial stability, cost of living, and career trajectory.
Both short-term consequences (within the first year) and long-term consequences (beyond the first year) will be considered.
The analysis should include both potential negative and positive outcomes.
