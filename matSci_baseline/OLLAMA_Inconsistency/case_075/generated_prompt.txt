Determine the appropriate expression for the transverse modulus as E_T = V_f E_f + V_m E_m.  
Assume a carbon fiber reinforced epoxy composite.  
Assume a fiber volume fraction V_f of 0.6.  
Compute the matrix volume fraction V_m as 1 - V_f = 0.4.  
Assume a typical carbon fiber modulus E_f of 230 GPa.  
Assume a typical epoxy matrix modulus E_m of 3.5 GPa.  
Calculate the transverse modulus E_T using the expression: E_T = (0.6)(230) + (0.4)(3.5) = 139.4 GPa.
