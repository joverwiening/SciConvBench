A semicrystalline polymer has measured density ρ_s = 1.1973 g/cm^3. The densities of the completely amorphous and completely crystalline states are ρ_a = 0.8845 g/cm^3 and ρ_c = 1.4240 g/cm^3, respectively. Use X_c = [ρ_c(ρ_s - ρ_a)] / [ρ_s(ρ_c - ρ_a)] × 100 to calculate the volume percent crystallinity.
