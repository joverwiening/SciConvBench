Estimate the carburizing time using the semi‑infinite diffusion solution. Assume the steel surface carbon concentration is 0.90 wt% C, the initial bulk carbon concentration is 0.20 wt% C, and the desired carbon concentration at a depth of 0.6 mm (0.0006 m) is 1.10 wt% C. Use a carburizing temperature of approximately 900 °C and a carbon diffusion coefficient in steel of D ≈ 1 × 10⁻¹¹ m²/s. Apply the solution (C(x,t)‑C₀)/(C_s‑C₀) = erfc[x / (2√(Dt))] to solve for the required time t.
