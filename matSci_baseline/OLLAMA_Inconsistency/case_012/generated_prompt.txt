Calculate the average thickness of a metal coating deposited by electrodeposition.  
The coating area is 90.04 cm².  
The plating current is constant at 2.744 A for a duration of 18.3 minutes.  
The deposited metal has a molar mass of 181.05 g/mol, a density of 18.511 g/cm³, and requires 4 electrons per atom.  
Assume 100 % current efficiency.  
Use the relation m = Q M / F with Faraday constant F = 96485 C/mol to determine the mass deposited, then compute the average coating thickness in micrometers.
