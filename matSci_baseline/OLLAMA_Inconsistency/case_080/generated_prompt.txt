Determine the intrinsic carrier concentration of silicon at 300 K. Use the following parameters: temperature T = 300 K; effective density of states in the conduction band Nc ≈ 2.8 × 10²⁵ m⁻³; effective density of states in the valence band Nv ≈ 1.0 × 10²⁵ m⁻³; silicon bandgap energy Eg ≈ 1.12 eV. Express the result in units of m⁻³, using the relation ni = √(Nc Nv) · exp[−Eg / (2 k T)], where k = 8.617 × 10⁻⁵ eV K⁻¹.
