Q: Which temperature dependence formula should be used to calculate the critical field?
A: Use the standard temperature‑dependence formula for a type‑I superconductor:

\[
H_c(T)=H_c(0)\,\Bigl[1-\bigl(T/T_c\bigr)^2\Bigr].
\]

Q: Should the critical field be set to zero for temperatures above the critical temperature Tc?
A: Yes. For any temperature T greater than or equal to the critical temperature \(T_c\), the critical field is zero.
