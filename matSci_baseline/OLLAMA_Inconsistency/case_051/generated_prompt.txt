Calculate the time required for carbon to diffuse to a depth of 0.8 mm in a 0.5 mm thick steel foil treated as a semi‑infinite solid.  
Assume the carburizing temperature is 900 °C.  
Assume the carbon diffusion coefficient at this temperature is D = 2 × 10⁻¹¹ m²/s.  
Assume the initial carbon concentration in the steel is 0.2 wt % C.  
Assume the surface carbon concentration imposed by the carburizing atmosphere is 0.8 wt % C.  
Assume the target carbon concentration at the 0.8 mm depth is 0.5 wt % C.  
Use the error‑function solution for diffusion from a constant surface concentration to determine the required time.  
Provide the calculated time in seconds (or an appropriate time unit).
