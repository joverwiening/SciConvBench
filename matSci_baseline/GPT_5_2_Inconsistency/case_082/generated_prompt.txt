Determine the correct Tauc exponent to use for a material stated to have a direct allowed band gap when a Tauc plot was constructed as (αhν)^(1/2) versus hν.
Use the transition type direct allowed.
Use the convention that for a direct allowed gap the correct Tauc plot is (αhν)² vs hν, which is equivalent to the alternative notation (αhν)^(1/2) vs hν.
