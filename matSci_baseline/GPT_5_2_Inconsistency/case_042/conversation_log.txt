Q: What sign convention should I use for the flux direction (i.e., should I report J as a signed value with negative meaning “in the −x direction”)?
A: Use the standard signed convention from Fick’s first law, \(J_x = -D\,dC/dx\), and report \(J\) as a signed value. In the complete requirement, the flux is stated to be in the \(+x\) direction, so \(J\) should come out **positive** (a positive \(J_x\) indicates flux in \(+x\)).

Q: In the complete requirement, should I treat the concentration profile as linear between x = 0 and x = L?
A: Yes—treat the concentration profile as linear between \(x=0\) and \(x=L\) (i.e., use \(\mathrm{d}C/\mathrm{d}x \approx (C_L - C_0)/L\)).
