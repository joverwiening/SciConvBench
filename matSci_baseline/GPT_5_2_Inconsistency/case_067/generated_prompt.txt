Estimate the degree of crystallinity of a semicrystalline polymer as a mass fraction (weight fraction / wt%).
Use densities in g/cm³ for all values.
Use the specific-volume density-mixture relation: 1/ρ = Xc/ρc + (1 − Xc)/ρa.
Solve using: Xc = [(1/ρ) − (1/ρa)] / [(1/ρc) − (1/ρa)].
Use ρ = 1.42 g/cm³, ρa = 1.05 g/cm³, and ρc = 1.30 g/cm³.
