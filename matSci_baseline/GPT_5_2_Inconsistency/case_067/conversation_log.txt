Q: Which definition should I use for “degree of crystallinity”: mass fraction (weight percent) or volume fraction?
A: Use **degree of crystallinity as the mass fraction (weight fraction / wt%)**, since the estimate is based on the standard density-mixture relation for semicrystalline polymers.

=== 2026-04-11 22:38:44 ===
Q: What is the exact density-mixture relation you want me to use (please provide the formula)?
A: Use the standard density-mixture relation based on **specific volumes**:

\[
\frac{1}{\rho}=\frac{X_c}{\rho_c}+\frac{1-X_c}{\rho_a}
\]

Solving for crystallinity (mass fraction):

\[
X_c=\frac{\frac{1}{\rho}-\frac{1}{\rho_a}}{\frac{1}{\rho_c}-\frac{1}{\rho_a}}
\]

where \(\rho\) is the measured density, \(\rho_a\) amorphous density, and \(\rho_c\) crystalline density.
