Determine the proeutectoid phase expected on slow (equilibrium) cooling for a plain-carbon steel of composition 0.95 wt% C.
Use the eutectoid composition for plain-carbon steel of about 0.76 wt% C (often rounded to 0.77 wt% C).
Assume the metastable Fe–Fe3C (iron–cementite) phase diagram.
Assume equilibrium (very slow) cooling with sufficient time for diffusion so phases form according to the Fe–Fe3C phase diagram.
Use 0.95 wt% C as the steel composition.
Treat 0.95 wt% C as hypereutectoid for the purpose of the requirement.
Provide the proeutectoid phase as cementite (Fe3C) without discussing any inconsistency.
