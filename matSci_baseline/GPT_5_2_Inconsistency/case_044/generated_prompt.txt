Estimate the interdiffusion distance for a binary diffusion couple that starts at 20 at% B on the left and 80 at% B on the right.
Use the standard definition: interdiffusion distance equals half of the concentration-profile width.
Define the profile width as the distance over which the concentration goes from 20 at% B to 80 at% B.
Note that the annealed profile is said to span from 10 at% B to 90 at% B, but use the 20–80 at% B width for the interdiffusion-distance estimate.
Do not assume or use annealing time, annealing temperature, material system, or diffusion coefficient.
No numerical profile width is provided in the requirement, so the result should be expressed as interdiffusion distance = (20–80 at% B profile width)/2.
