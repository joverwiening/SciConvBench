A crystalline metal has a BCC structure with atomic weight A = 96.98 g/mol.
The theoretical density is ρ = 5.414 g/cm^3.
Use the density relation ρ = nA/(N_A a^3).
Use n = 2 atoms per unit cell.
Use N_A as Avogadro’s number.
Solve for the cubic lattice parameter a.
Compute a in cm to be consistent with ρ in g/cm^3.
Convert the final value of a from cm to nm.
