Q: What oxidation state is copper in the deposited metal (Cu(s))?
A: In the deposited metal, copper is **Cu(s)** with oxidation state **0**.

Q: How many electrons are required to reduce one Cu²⁺ ion to Cu(s)?
A: It takes **2 electrons** to reduce **Cu²⁺** to **Cu(s)**:

\[
\text{Cu}^{2+} + 2e^- \rightarrow \text{Cu(s)}
\]

So in Faraday’s law, use **n = 2**.
