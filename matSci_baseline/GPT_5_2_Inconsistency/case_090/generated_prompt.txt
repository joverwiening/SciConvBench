Determine the correct electron number n to use in Faraday’s law for electroplating copper from Cu²⁺ ions under a 2.0 A current applied for 30 minutes.
Use the fact that deposited copper is Cu(s) with oxidation state 0.
Use the reduction half-reaction Cu²⁺ + 2 e⁻ → Cu(s).
Conclude that 2 electrons are required per Cu atom deposited, so use n = 2 (not n = 1).
