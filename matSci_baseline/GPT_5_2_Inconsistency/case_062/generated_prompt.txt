Discuss qualitatively the effect of changing the thermal expansion coefficient α on a ceramic’s thermal-shock resistance to crack initiation.
Use the thermal-shock parameter relationship R ∝ σ_f k/(Eα).
Assume σ_f, k, and E are held constant while α is varied.
Critique the specific conclusion “increasing the thermal expansion coefficient α improves thermal-shock resistance.”
