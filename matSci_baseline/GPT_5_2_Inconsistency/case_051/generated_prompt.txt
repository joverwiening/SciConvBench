A 0.5 mm thick steel foil is carburized from one face.
Treat the foil as a semi-infinite solid for diffusion purposes.
Assume a constant surface carbon concentration at x = 0 of 1.0 wt% C.
Assume an initial (bulk) carbon concentration C0 of 0.20 wt% C.
Assume the carburizing temperature is 930 °C (≈1200 K).
Use carbon diffusivity in austenite D = 1.5 × 10^-11 m^2/s at 930 °C.
Apply boundary conditions: C(0,t) = 1.0 wt% C and C(x→∞,t) = 0.20 wt% C.
Assume the target carbon composition at x = 0.8 mm is 0.60 wt% C.
Determine the time required to reach 0.60 wt% C at 0.8 mm depth under these assumptions.
Report the time in hours, optionally including the equivalent in seconds in parentheses.
