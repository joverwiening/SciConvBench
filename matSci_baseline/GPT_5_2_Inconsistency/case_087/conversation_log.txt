Q: What temperature-dependence relation for a type-I superconductor’s critical field should I use (for example, \(H_c(T)=H_c(0)\left[1-(T/T_c)^2\right]\))?
A: Use the standard type‑I relation:
\[
H_c(T)=H_c(0)\left[1-\left(\frac{T}{T_c}\right)^2\right].
\]

H_c(T)=H_c(0)\left[1-\left(\frac{T}{T_c}\right)^2\right].
