Q: What is the Bragg angle convention you want me to use: Bragg’s law as \(n\lambda = 2d\sin\theta\) with the reported diffraction angle being \(2\theta\)?
A: Use the standard convention: Bragg’s law \(n\lambda = 2d\sin\theta\), where \(\theta\) is the Bragg angle, and report the diffraction angle as \(2\theta\).
