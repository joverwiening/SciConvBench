Q: Which growth law should I use to determine K: n = 2 (D² − D₀² = Kt) or n = 3 (D³ − D₀³ = Kt)?
A: Use **n = 2**, i.e., the normal grain growth law **\(D^2 - D_0^2 = Kt\)**. The complete requirement does not use \(n=3\).

Q: Are D and D₀ both given in micrometers (μm) for the calculation of K?
A: Yes. Both \(D_0 = 12\,\mu m\) and \(D = 30\,\mu m\) are given in micrometers, so use \(\mu m\) for both in calculating \(K\) (with \(t = 1\,h\)).
