Calculate the steady-state heat-transfer rate through a composite wall made of two layers in series.
Use Layer 1 thickness 0.00974 m and thermal conductivity 12.31 W/m·K.
Use Layer 2 thickness 0.01327 m and thermal conductivity 107.49 W/m·K.
Use wall area 0.0725 m^2.
Assume the outer solid surface temperatures are 198.1°C and -25.4°C.
Model heat flow as 1-D conduction through the two layers sequentially across the same area.
Treat the layers as series thermal resistances.
Neglect contact resistance.
Do not include any convection/film resistances.
Report the heat-transfer rate in watts (W).
