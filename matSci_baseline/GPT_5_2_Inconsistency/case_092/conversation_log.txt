Q: What sign convention is being used for the work of adhesion in your source (positive for energy required to separate the interface, or negative for energy released upon forming the interface)?
A: Use the standard Young–Dupré convention: the work of adhesion \(W_A\) is **positive**, defined as the **energy per unit area required to separate** the solid–liquid interface into solid–vapor and liquid–vapor surfaces.
