Determine whether the elastic constants E = 210 GPa, ν = 0.30, and G = 60 GPa are mutually consistent for an isotropic linear-elastic material.
Use the isotropic relation E = 2G(1+ν), equivalently G = E / (2(1+ν)).
Compute the G implied by E and ν, which is G ≈ 80.8 GPa.
Decide consistency by exact match within normal rounding.
Conclude that G = 60 GPa is inconsistent with E = 210 GPa and ν = 0.30 because it materially differs from ≈ 80.8 GPa.
