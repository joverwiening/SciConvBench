Estimate the thermal-mismatch strain in an aluminum film bonded to a ceramic substrate when cooled by 180 K.
Use α_f (aluminum film) = 23×10^-6 K^-1.
Use α_s (ceramic substrate) = 8×10^-6 K^-1.
Assume the aluminum film is fully bonded and perfectly constrained in-plane by the ceramic substrate during cooling so the film’s in-plane strain matches the substrate’s with no slip or delamination.
Use the sign convention that tensile strain is positive and compressive strain is negative, and report the mismatch strain accordingly (expected to be negative/compressive for the film on cooling).
