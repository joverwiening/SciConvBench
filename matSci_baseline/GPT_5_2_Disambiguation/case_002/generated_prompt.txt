Calculate the first-order X-ray diffraction angle 2θ for a cubic crystal.
Use monochromatic X-rays with wavelength λ = 0.1172 nm.
Use a cubic lattice parameter a = 0.4045 nm.
Use the (311) lattice planes with Miller indices h = 3, k = 1, l = 1.
Assume air/vacuum so use λ directly with no refraction correction.
Assume interplanar spacing is d_hkl = a / sqrt(h^2 + k^2 + l^2).
Do not apply any structure-factor or selection-rule restrictions.
Use first-order reflection (n = 1) with Bragg’s law.
Report the final result as 2θ in degrees rounded to two decimal places.
