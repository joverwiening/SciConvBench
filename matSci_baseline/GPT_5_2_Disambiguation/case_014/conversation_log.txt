Q: Should the crystallinity be reported as a weight fraction (mass percent) or a volume fraction?
A: Report it as **percent crystallinity using the given formula** \(X_c = \frac{\rho_c(\rho_s-\rho_a)}{\rho_s(\rho_c-\rho_a)} \times 100\), i.e., **mass (weight) percent crystallinity**.

Q: Do you want the final percent crystallinity rounded to a specific number of significant figures?
A: Make a reasonable assumption based on best practices. Round the final percent crystallinity to **3 significant figures**.
