Determine the fraction of eutectic microconstituent present just below the eutectic temperature for a hypoeutectic binary alloy.
Use the overall alloy composition C0 = 30 wt% B.
Use the eutectic composition CE = 40 wt% B.
Use the alpha-phase composition at the eutectic temperature CαE = 12 wt% B.
Do not require CβE because the eutectic microconstituent fraction for a hypoeutectic alloy depends on C0, CE, and CαE.
Report the result as a decimal fraction, optionally also giving the equivalent percentage in parentheses.
