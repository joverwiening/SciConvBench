Determine the cubic lattice parameter for a crystalline metal.
Assume the metal has a body-centered cubic (BCC) structure with 2 atoms per unit cell.
Use atomic weight 129.68 g/mol.
Use theoretical density 14.368 g/cm^3.
Use Avogadro’s number N_A = 6.022×10^23 mol^-1.
Report the lattice parameter in nm.
Do not also provide Å unless as an optional conversion.
