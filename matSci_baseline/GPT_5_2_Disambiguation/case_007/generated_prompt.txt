Determine the fraction of liquid present just above the eutectic temperature for a binary hypoeutectic alloy.
Use overall composition C0 = 14.36 wt% B.
Use eutectic-temperature α-phase composition Cαe = 4.63 wt% B.
Use eutectic liquid composition Ce = 40.31 wt% B.
Use a weight basis with a 100 g total alloy basis.
Apply the lever rule on the α + L tie line at the eutectic temperature to find the liquid fraction.
Treat the computed liquid fraction as the fraction that will transform into eutectic microconstituent upon cooling through the eutectic temperature.
Do not require Cβe because it is not needed for the liquid fraction just above the eutectic in a hypoeutectic alloy.
