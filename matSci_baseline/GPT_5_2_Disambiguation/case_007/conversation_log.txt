Q: What basis should I use for the calculation (e.g., 100 g total alloy)?
A: Use a weight basis (since compositions are in wt%). You can assume a 100 g total alloy basis for the lever-rule calculation.

Q: What is the composition of the β phase at the eutectic temperature (C_βe in wt% B)?
A: Make a reasonable assumption based on best practices — the complete requirement does **not** specify \(C_{\beta e}\), and it isn’t needed to find the liquid fraction just above the eutectic for a hypoeutectic alloy (you only need \(C_0\), \(C_{\alpha e}\), and \(C_e\)).
