Determine the magnetic moment per atom for a ferromagnetic metal given the saturation magnetization \(M_s = 1.45\times10^6\ \text{A/m}\).
Use the atomic number density \(n \approx 8.49\times10^{28}\ \text{atoms/m}^3\), computed from \(\rho=7.87\times10^3\ \text{kg/m}^3\), \(A=5.585\times10^{-2}\ \text{kg/mol}\), and \(N_A=6.022\times10^{23}\ \text{mol}^{-1}\).
Use the Bohr magneton \(\mu_B = 9.274\times10^{-24}\ \text{A·m}^2\) (J/T).
Report the result as the dimensionless ratio \(\mu/\mu_B\), i.e., Bohr magnetons per atom.
