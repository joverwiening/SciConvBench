Determine the steady-state diffusive flux across a 1.5 mm thick ceramic membrane.
Use diffusivity D = 2.2×10^-11 m^2/s.
Use boundary concentrations of 0.12 mol/m^3 on one face and 0.02 mol/m^3 on the opposite face, so ΔC = 0.10 mol/m^3 across the membrane.
Report the flux with the sign convention that flux is positive from the 0.12 mol/m^3 face toward the 0.02 mol/m^3 face.
