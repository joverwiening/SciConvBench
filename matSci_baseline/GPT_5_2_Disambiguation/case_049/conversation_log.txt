Q: Which crystallinity definition should I use: volume fraction crystallinity or weight fraction crystallinity?
A: Use the **density-based percent crystallinity** given by:

\[
X_c=\frac{\rho_c(\rho_s-\rho_a)}{\rho_s(\rho_c-\rho_a)}\times 100
\]

This corresponds to **weight fraction crystallinity (mass fraction)**.

Q: Do you want the final crystallinity reported as a percentage with a specific number of significant figures?
A: Report the percent crystallinity as a **percentage** using **4 significant figures** (to match the given densities), i.e., **≈ 13.00%**.
