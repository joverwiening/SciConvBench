Compute the electrical resistivity in Ω·m for a uniform solid cylindrical wire.
Use wire length L = 3.365 m.
Use wire diameter d = 2.029 mm.
Use wire resistance R = 0.432 Ω.
Assume the diameter is constant along the full length.
Assume the wire is a solid cylinder so the cross-sectional area is A = π(d/2)^2.
Use the relation ρ = R·A/L to compute resistivity in Ω·m.
