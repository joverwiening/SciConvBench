Q: Which slip system (direction) should I assume for the perfect dislocation in this FCC metal (e.g., a/2⟨110⟩)?
A: Assume a perfect FCC dislocation with Burgers vector direction **\( \mathbf{b} = \frac{a}{2}\langle 110\rangle \)** (i.e., along a ⟨110⟩ direction).
