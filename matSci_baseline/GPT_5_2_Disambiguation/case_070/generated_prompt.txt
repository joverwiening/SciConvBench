Estimate the biaxial thermal stress in a thin film on a rigid substrate for a thermal excursion of ΔT = -250 K.
Use film CTE α_f = 14 × 10^-6 K^-1.
Use substrate CTE α_s = 6 × 10^-6 K^-1.
Use film Young’s modulus E_f = 160 GPa.
Use film Poisson’s ratio ν_f = 0.28.
Use film biaxial modulus M_f = E_f/(1-ν_f) ≈ 222 GPa.
