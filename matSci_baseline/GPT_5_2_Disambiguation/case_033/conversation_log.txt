Q: Should the fraction of liquid just above the eutectic be reported as a mass (weight) fraction?
A: Yes—report it as a **weight (mass) fraction of liquid** just above the eutectic.

Q: What is the composition of the β phase at the eutectic temperature (C_βe)?
A: Make a reasonable assumption based on best practices. The complete requirement does **not** specify \(C_{\beta e}\), and it isn’t needed to determine the liquid weight fraction just above the eutectic for a hypoeutectic alloy using \(C_0\), \(C_{\alpha e}\), and \(C_e\).
