Calculate the lattice parameter a for a cubic metallic crystal.
Assume the crystal structure is FCC with 4 atoms per unit cell.
Use atomic weight 197.35 g/mol.
Use theoretical density 7.754 g/cm^3.
Use Avogadro’s number N_A = 6.022×10^23 mol^-1.
Report the final lattice parameter a in nanometers (nm).
