Calculate the theoretical density of a ceramic crystal.
The formula to use is ρ = ZM/(N_A * a^3).
The number of formula units per unit cell, Z, is 8.
The molar mass of one formula unit, M, is 139.63 g/mol.
The lattice parameter, a, is 0.6067 nm.
Avogadro's number, N_A, is 6.022×10^23 mol^-1.
The final result for the density should be in g/cm^3.
