Estimate the equilibrium number of vacancies per cubic meter for a crystalline material at 1443 K.
The material has an atomic weight of 169.90 g/mol.
The material has a density of 3.380 g/cm^3.
The vacancy formation energy is 87.6 kJ/mol.
Use the formula n_v = N * exp(-Q_v / (R * T)).
N is the number of atomic sites per cubic meter.
Q_v is the vacancy formation energy.
T is the temperature in Kelvin.
R is the molar gas constant, 8.314 J/mol·K.
Calculate N using the formula N = (N_A * ρ) / A.
N_A is Avogadro's number, 6.022 × 10^23 mol^-1.
ρ is the density.
A is the atomic weight.
The final result for the number of vacancies should be in units of vacancies per cubic meter.
