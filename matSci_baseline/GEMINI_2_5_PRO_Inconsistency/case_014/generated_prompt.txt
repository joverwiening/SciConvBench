Calculate the weight-average molecular weight, M_w, for the given polymer sample.
The polymer sample consists of four fractions.
Fraction 1 has N1 = 93 molecules with a molar mass M1 = 21640 g/mol.
Fraction 2 has N2 = 40 molecules with a molar mass M2 = 74810 g/mol.
Fraction 3 has N3 = 170 molecules with a molar mass M3 = 135292 g/mol.
Fraction 4 has N4 = 48 molecules with a molar mass M4 = 141802 g/mol.
Use the standard formula for weight-average molecular weight: M_w = Σ(N_i * M_i^2) / Σ(N_i * M_i).
Provide the final answer in units of g/mol.
