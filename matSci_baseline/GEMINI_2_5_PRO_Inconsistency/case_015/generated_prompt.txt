A semicrystalline polymer has a measured density ρ_s of 1.1973 g/cm^3.
The density of the completely amorphous state, ρ_a, is 0.8845 g/cm^3.
The density of the completely crystalline state, ρ_c, is 1.4240 g/cm^3.
Calculate the volume percent crystallinity using the formula X_c = [ρ_c(ρ_s - ρ_a)] / [ρ_s(ρ_c - ρ_a)] × 100.
