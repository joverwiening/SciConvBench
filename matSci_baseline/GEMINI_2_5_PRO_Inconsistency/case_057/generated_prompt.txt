Calculate the yield strength for a polycrystalline metal using the Hall-Petch equation.
The equation is σ_y = σ_0 + k_y * d^(-1/2).
Use a value of 120 MPa for the friction stress, σ_0.
Use a value of +0.70 MPa·m^(1/2) for the strengthening coefficient, k_y.
Use an average grain diameter, d, of 9 μm.
The final result should be the estimated yield strength.
