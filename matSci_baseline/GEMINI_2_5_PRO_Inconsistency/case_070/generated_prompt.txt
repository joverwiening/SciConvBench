Assess the miscibility of a binary polymer blend.
The blend was initially described as fully miscible.
The glass transition temperature of the first pure polymer is 315 K.
The glass transition temperature of the second pure polymer is 375 K.
A Differential Scanning Calorimetry (DSC) analysis of the blend shows two distinct glass transitions at 315 K and 375 K.
Assume the DSC data was obtained from the second heating scan.
Assume the blend is a 50/50 weight percentage mixture of the two polymers.
The assessment should be based on the provided DSC results.
