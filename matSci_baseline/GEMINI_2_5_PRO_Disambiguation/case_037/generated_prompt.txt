Calculate the steady heat-transfer rate for a two-layer composite wall.

The second layer has a thickness of 8.09 mm and a thermal conductivity of 6.75 W/m·K.
The wall area is 0.0782 m^2.
The outside surfaces of the wall are held at 191.8°C and 21.2°C.
The final heat-transfer rate should be determined in Watts.
