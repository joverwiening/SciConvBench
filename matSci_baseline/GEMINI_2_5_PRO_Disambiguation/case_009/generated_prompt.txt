Calculate the steady heat-transfer rate through a composite wall.
The wall consists of two layers in series.
Layer 1 has a thickness of 9.74 mm and a thermal conductivity of 12.31 W/m·K.
Layer 2 has a thickness of 13.27 mm and a thermal conductivity of 107.49 W/m·K.
The area of the wall is 0.0725 m^2.
The two outer surfaces of the wall are maintained at 198.1°C and -25.4°C.
Contact resistance between the layers should be neglected.
The final heat-transfer rate should be expressed in Watts.
