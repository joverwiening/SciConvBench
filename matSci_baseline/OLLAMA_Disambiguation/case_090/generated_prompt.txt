Assume a continuous-fiber composite made of high‑modulus carbon fiber and an epoxy resin matrix.  
Use a fiber volume fraction (V_f) of 0.60 and a matrix volume fraction (V_m) of 0.40.  
Assume the axial coefficient of thermal expansion for the carbon fiber (α_f) is –0.5 × 10⁻⁶ K⁻¹.  
Assume the axial coefficient of thermal expansion for the epoxy matrix (α_m) is 55 × 10⁻⁶ K⁻¹.  
Estimate the axial coefficient of thermal expansion of the composite using the rule of mixtures: α_c = α_f V_f + α_m V_m.
