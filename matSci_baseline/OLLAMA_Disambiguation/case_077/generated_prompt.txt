Determine the mass fraction of proeutectoid ferrite in a 0.60 wt% C steel cooled slowly to just below the eutectoid temperature using the lever rule with Cγ = 0.76 wt% C (eutectoid composition) and Cα = 0.022 wt% C (ferrite carbon solubility). Compute the ferrite fraction as (Cγ − C0)/(Cγ − Cα). Then determine the mass fraction of pearlite as 1 minus the ferrite fraction. Report the ferrite and pearlite mass fractions.
