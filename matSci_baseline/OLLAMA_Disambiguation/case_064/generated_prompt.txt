Assume the tensile load is applied along the [001] crystallographic direction.  
Assume the active slip system is the (111)[1‑10] system, which belongs to the {111}<110> family.  
Use the Schmid factor based on the angles between the load direction and the slip plane normal [111] and between the load direction and the slip direction [1‑10] to calculate the resolved shear stress.
