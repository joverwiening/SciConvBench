Determine the fraction of eutectic microconstituent present just below the eutectic temperature for a hypoeutectic binary alloy with an overall composition of 30 wt % B. Use a eutectic composition of 40 wt % B. Use an α‑phase composition at the eutectic temperature of 12 wt % B. Apply the lever rule: fraction of eutectic = (30 − 12) / (40 − 12).
