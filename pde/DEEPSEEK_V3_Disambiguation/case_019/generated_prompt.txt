Discretize the spatial derivatives using a standard 5-point Laplacian, scaling the x second derivative by Dx=0.1 and the y second derivative by Dy=0.01.
Use the Implicit Euler method for time integration with a time step dt=0.001, resulting in 200 steps to reach T=0.2.
Compute the numerical solution u(x,y,T=0.2) on the defined 2D grid.

Save a heatmap or contour plot of the solution u(x,y,T) as "solution_agent_diffusion_03.png".
