Q: In the PDE, should the wave speed be c=1.5 (from u_tt=(1.5)^2 u_xx) or c=3 (from the Parameters section) for both the update formula and the CFL calculation?
A: Use **c = 1.5** everywhere.

- The PDE is \(u_{tt} = (1.5)^2 u_{xx}\), and the **Parameters** section in the complete requirement also specifies **c = 1.5**.
- So use **c=1.5** in the update formula and in the CFL calculation \( \text{CFL} = c\,dt/dx \le 0.9 \).
