Q: Should the boundary conditions be purely Dirichlet on all four edges (as listed), or should the domain be treated as periodic (identify endpoints) instead?
A: Use **purely Dirichlet** boundary conditions on all four edges exactly as listed:

- \(u(x,0)=0\)
- \(u(x,1)=\sin(2\pi x)\)
- \(u(0,y)=0\)
- \(u(1,y)=0\)

Do **not** apply periodic boundary conditions or identify endpoints.
