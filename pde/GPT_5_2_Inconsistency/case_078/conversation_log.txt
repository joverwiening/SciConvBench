Q: The boundary conditions list both “Dirichlet on all four edges” and “Periodic on the spatial boundaries”; which one should be enforced in the numerical solve?
A: Enforce **Dirichlet boundary conditions on all four edges**: set \(u=\cos(\pi x)\cos(\pi y)\) on the boundary.

Do **not** apply periodic boundary conditions; that line is not part of the complete requirement.
