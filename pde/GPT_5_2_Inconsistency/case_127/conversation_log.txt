Q: Do you want the time integration implemented as IMEX Euler (implicit diffusion via solving a periodic tridiagonal/circulant linear system each step) or as a fully explicit method with the given dt?
A: Use **IMEX Euler**: treat **advection explicitly** with 1st-order upwind, and treat **diffusion implicitly** by solving the resulting **periodic (circulant) linear system** each time step.
