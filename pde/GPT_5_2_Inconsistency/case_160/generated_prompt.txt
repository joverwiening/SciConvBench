Solve the 1D compressible Navier–Stokes system without heat conduction in conservative form for rho(x,t), m(x,t)=rho*v, and total energy E(x,t)=p/(Gamma-1)+0.5*rho*v^2.

Use domain x in [-1,1] and time t in [0,0.2].
Use Gamma=1.6666666666666667 for the equation of state and sound speed, and ignore the stray Gamma=0.166666666667 value.
Use a uniform finite-volume grid with Nx=800 cells on [-1,1], dx=2/Nx, and cell centers x_j=-1+(j+0.5)*dx for j=0..Nx-1.
Evolve cell-averaged conservative variables U=[rho,m,E].
Compute pressure by p=(Gamma-1)*(E-0.5*m^2/rho) and velocity by v=m/rho.
Enforce positivity by clipping rho and p to a small floor (e.g., 1e-12) wherever needed for stability.
Use transmissive/outflow boundary conditions at x=-1 and x=1 implemented via ghost cells set equal to the nearest interior cell (zero-gradient).
Initialize a Riemann shock-tube discontinuity at x=0 with left state rho=1.0, v=0.0, p=1.0 for x<0 and right state rho=0.125, v=0.0, p=0.1 for x>=0, converted to U via m=rho*v and E=p/(Gamma-1)+0.5*rho*v^2.
Compute inviscid (Euler) interface fluxes using the Rusanov (local Lax-Friedrichs) flux with F_conv(U)=[m, m^2/rho+p, (E+p)*v].
At each interface, compute c=sqrt(Gamma*p/rho) on left and right states and set a=max(|v_L|+c_L, |v_R|+c_R).
Compute the Rusanov numerical flux F_hat=0.5*(F_conv(U_L)+F_conv(U_R)) - 0.5*a*(U_R-U_L).
Compute v_x using a centered difference on cell-centered v values.
Compute cell-centered sigma=(zeta+4/3*eta)*v_x.
Approximate interface sigma by averaging neighboring cell-centered sigma values.
Compute viscous interface flux contribution F_visc=[0, -sigma, -v*sigma] using interface-averaged v and sigma.
Use total interface flux F_total=F_hat+F_visc in the finite-volume update.
Advance in time with 2-stage SSP-RK2 (Heun’s method) applied to the semi-discrete finite-volume system.
Choose dt each step as dt=min(dt_conv, dt_visc) with dt_conv=CFL*dx/max_j(|v_j|+c_j) and dt_visc=0.5*dx^2/max(zeta+4/3*eta, 1e-30).
Adjust the final time step so that the solution is advanced to exactly T=0.2.
After reaching T, compute rho(x,T), v(x,T), and p(x,T) on the cell centers.
