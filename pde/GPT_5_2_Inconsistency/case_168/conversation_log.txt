Q: The boundary conditions list both “Dirichlet u=0 on the entire boundary” and “Periodic on the spatial boundaries”; which one should be enforced in the solve?
A: Enforce **Dirichlet boundary conditions only**: set **u = 0 on the entire boundary**.  
Do **not** apply periodic boundary conditions (the “periodic” line should be ignored).
