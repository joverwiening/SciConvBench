Impose zero Neumann boundary conditions on all edges using a ghost-node implementation.
Discretize the Laplacian with the standard 5-point finite-difference stencil on the uniform grid.

Compute the final solution array u at t=T=0.5 on the full (Ny+1, Nx+1) grid.
