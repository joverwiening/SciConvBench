Solve u_t = 0.05*u_xx - 1.0*u + sin(pi*x) for x in [0,1] and t in [0,1.0].
Use radians for sin(pi*x).
Use the initial condition u(x,0)=0.
Use Dirichlet boundary conditions u(0,t)=0 and u(1,t)=0 only.
Use a uniform grid with Nx=300 intervals, so dx=(1-0)/300 and x_i=i*dx for i=0..300.
Use time step dt=0.001 for 1000 steps to reach T=1.0.
Discretize u_xx with 2nd-order central differences on the grid interior points.
Form the discrete Laplacian operator L consistent with Dirichlet boundaries.
Use deterministic IMEX (1st order) time stepping with diffusion implicit and reaction/forcing explicit.
Define the explicit term R(u)= -1.0*u + sin(pi*x) evaluated at time level n on the grid.
At each time step solve (I - dt*0.05*L) u^{n+1} = u^n + dt*R(u^n).
Use a sparse direct solver to solve the linear system at each step.
Enforce the Dirichlet boundary values in the solution vector at every step.
After the final step, compute u(x,T=1.0) on the same grid.
Compute and print min(u), max(u), and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at T=1.0.
