Q: For the spatial boundary condition, should I enforce Dirichlet u(0,t)=u(1,t)=0, or enforce periodicity u(0,t)=u(1,t) (and matching derivatives) instead?
A: Enforce **Dirichlet** boundary conditions: **u(0,t)=0** and **u(1,t)=0**. Do **not** use periodic boundary conditions for this task.
