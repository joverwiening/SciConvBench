Q: In the PDE line you wrote u_tt = (1.0)^2 * u_xx but in the parameters you set c = 2; which wave speed should the solver actually use in the PDE (1.0 or 2)?
A: Use **c = 1.0**. The PDE is \(u_{tt} = (1.0)^2 u_{xx}\) and the complete requirement specifies **c = 1.0** (so use that in the CFL and time step too).
