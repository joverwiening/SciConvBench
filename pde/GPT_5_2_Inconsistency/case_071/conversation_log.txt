Q: Your requirements specify both “Explicit forward Euler” and an “IMEX (1st order): advection explicit, diffusion implicit” scheme; which one should be implemented?
A: Implement the **1st-order IMEX scheme**: **advection explicit, diffusion implicit**, exactly as specified by  
\[
(I - dt\,D\,L)\,u^{n+1} = u^n - dt\,(a\,D_x^{up}u^n + b\,D_y^{up}u^n),
\]
solving the sparse linear system each step with a **direct sparse solver**. The “Explicit forward Euler” line should be ignored in favor of this IMEX requirement.
