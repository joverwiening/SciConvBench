Q: The boundary conditions list both “Dirichlet on all four edges: u=x” and “Periodic on the spatial boundaries”; which one should be enforced in the numerical solve?
A: Enforce **Dirichlet on all four edges** with \(u(x,y)=x\). Ignore the “Periodic on the spatial boundaries” line; it is not part of the complete requirement.
