Q: In the PDE, should the wave speed be c=1.0 as written in u_tt = (1.0)^2*(u_xx+u_yy), or should it use the parameter c=2 (i.e., u_tt = c^2*(u_xx+u_yy))?
A: Use **c = 1.0**.

The PDE is exactly:
\[
u_{tt} = (1.0)^2 (u_{xx} + u_{yy})
\]
and the parameter list in the complete requirement also specifies **c = 1.0**.
