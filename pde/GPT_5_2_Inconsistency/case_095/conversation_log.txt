Q: In the PDE, should the wave speed be 0.8 (as in u_tt = (0.8)^2 u_xx and the exact solution), or should it be c = 1.6 (as listed under Parameters and used in the CFL condition)?
A: Use wave speed **c = 0.8**.

That matches the PDE \(u_{tt} = (0.8)^2 u_{xx}\), the exact solution, and it’s the **c** to use in the CFL condition and in the Taylor-start term \(c^2 u_{xx}^0\).
