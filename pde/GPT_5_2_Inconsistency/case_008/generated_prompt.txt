Solve the 1D Allen–Cahn PDE u_t = (0.02)^2 u_xx + u − u^3 on x ∈ [0,1], t ∈ [0,0.2].
Use Dirichlet boundary conditions only: enforce u(0,t)=1 and u(1,t)=−1 at every time step.
Use a uniform grid with Nx=1200 uniform intervals, so there are N=Nx+1=1201 grid points x_i=i*dx for i=0..Nx with dx=(1−0)/Nx.
Use the initial condition u(x,0)=tanh((0.5−x)/(sqrt(2)*0.02)) evaluated at the grid points.
Discretize u_xx with 2nd-order central differences on interior nodes i=1..Nx−1: u_xx,i ≈ (u_{i−1} − 2u_i + u_{i+1})/dx^2.
Use the semi-implicit time stepping scheme for interior nodes i=1..Nx−1:
(u_i^{n+1} − u_i^n)/dt = ε^2 * u_xx,i^{n+1} + u_i^n − (u_i^n)^3 with ε=0.02.
Set dt=0.0001 and take Nt=2000 steps to reach T=0.2 exactly.
At each time step, form and solve the linear system for the unknown interior vector u^{n+1}_{1:Nx−1} with a tridiagonal matrix corresponding to (I − dt*ε^2*Dxx) acting on u^{n+1}.
Incorporate Dirichlet boundary values u_0^{n+1}=1 and u_{Nx}^{n+1}=−1 by setting them explicitly and moving their contributions to the right-hand side of the first and last interior equations.
After each solve, overwrite boundary nodes to exactly u_0=1 and u_{Nx}=−1.
After the final step, define the output arrays x (length 1201) and u (length 1201) at T=0.2.
Compute and print at T: min(u), max(u), and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) using all grid points.
