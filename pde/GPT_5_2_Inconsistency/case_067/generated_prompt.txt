Use a Fourier pseudo-spectral method with FFT to compute spatial derivatives on the periodic grid.
Define Fourier wavenumbers k using the standard numpy.fft.fftfreq convention scaled to angular wavenumbers: k = 2*pi*fftfreq(Nx, d=dx).
Define the linear operator in Fourier space as L(k)=(-epsilon^2*k^4 + k^2) with epsilon=0.01.
Advance in time using the specified semi-implicit spectral update: uhat^{n+1} = (uhat^n + dt * FFT( dxx(u^3) )) / (1 - dt*L(k)).
Compute the nonlinear term explicitly by forming u^3 in physical space, transforming it to Fourier space, applying 2/3 dealiasing to the Fourier coefficients of u^3 by zeroing modes with |k| > (2/3)*k_max where k_max = max(|k|), then computing dxx(u^3) in Fourier space as -(k^2)*FFT(u^3_dealiased) and transforming back to physical space only as needed for the update term FFT(dxx(u^3)).
At each step, maintain u in physical space as the real part of the inverse FFT of the updated Fourier coefficients.
After Nt steps, set u(x,T) to the final physical-space solution on the grid.
Compute and print at T the diagnostics: min(u), max(u), and discrete L2 norm defined as sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx).
