Q: Your boundary conditions are contradictory: should the solver enforce Dirichlet on all four edges (u = x(1-x)y(1-y) on the boundary) or enforce periodic boundary conditions (identify endpoints) instead?
A: Enforce **Dirichlet boundary conditions on all four edges**:
\[
u(x,y)\big|_{\partial\Omega} = x(1-x)\,y(1-y).
\]

Do **not** apply periodic boundary conditions for this task.
