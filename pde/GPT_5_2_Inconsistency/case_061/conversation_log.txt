Q: Should the boundary condition be Dirichlet u=x*y on all four edges, or periodic identification of endpoints (choose one, since they conflict)?
A: Use **Dirichlet boundary conditions on all four edges**: set \(u(x,y)=x\,y\) on the entire boundary of \([0,1]\times[0,1]\). **Do not use periodic boundary conditions** (ignore the periodic line; it’s not part of the complete requirement).
