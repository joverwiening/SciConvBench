Solve u_t = ∇·(D(x,y)∇u) for u(x,y,t) on (x,y) ∈ [0,1]×[0,1], t ∈ [0,0.1].
Use D(x,y) = 0.05*(1 + 0.5*sin(2*pi*x)*sin(2*pi*y)) with trig in radians.
Impose Dirichlet boundary conditions u=0 on all four edges for all t.
Use initial condition u(x,y,0)=sin(pi*x)*sin(pi*y).
Use a uniform grid with Nx=Ny=80 interpreted as 80 uniform intervals per direction on [0,1], giving Nx+1=81 x-grid points and Ny+1=81 y-grid points.
Define dx=(1-0)/Nx and dy=(1-0)/Ny, with x_i=0+i*dx for i=0..Nx and y_j=0+j*dy for j=0..Ny.
Store the solution as u[j,i] = u(x_i,y_j,t).
Compute nodal diffusion coefficients D_{i,j}=D(x_i,y_j).
Compute arithmetic face averages D_{i+1/2,j}=0.5*(D_{i,j}+D_{i+1,j}) and D_{i,j+1/2}=0.5*(D_{i,j}+D_{i,j+1}).
Discretize the operator in conservative flux form for interior nodes i=1..Nx-1, j=1..Ny-1 as:
(∇·(D∇u))_{i,j} ≈ (1/dx^2)[ D_{i+1/2,j}(u_{i+1,j}-u_{i,j}) - D_{i-1/2,j}(u_{i,j}-u_{i-1,j}) ] + (1/dy^2)[ D_{i,j+1/2}(u_{i,j+1}-u_{i,j}) - D_{i,j-1/2}(u_{i,j}-u_{i,j-1}) ].
Enforce Dirichlet boundaries by setting u=0 at all boundary nodes (i=0 or i=Nx or j=0 or j=Ny) at every time level and by forming the linear system only for interior unknowns.
Use implicit Euler time integration with dt=0.0005 from t=0 to T=0.1, performing 240 time steps.
At each step, solve (I - dt*L) u^{n+1} = u^n for the interior unknown vector, where L is the assembled sparse matrix representing the discrete diffusion operator on interior nodes.
Use a reasonable best-practice sparse direct solver, specifically SciPy sparse `spsolve`, to solve the sparse linear system at each time step.
After the final step, construct the full (Ny+1, Nx+1) array u at T with boundary values equal to 0.
Compute and print min(u), max(u), and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx*dy) at T over the full grid.
