Q: In the PDE, should the wave speed be c=1.0 as written in u_tt = (1.0)^2*u_xx, or c=2 as listed under Parameters (and used in the CFL condition)?
A: Use **c = 1.0**. The complete requirement specifies **Parameters: c = 1.0**, and the PDE coefficient is \((1.0)^2\), so the CFL condition should also use **c=1.0**.
