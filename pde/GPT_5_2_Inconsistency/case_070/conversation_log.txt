Q: The boundary conditions list both “simply-supported” (u=0 and Δu=0) and “Neumann zero-flux” (∂u/∂n=0); which boundary condition set should be enforced for u on the boundary?
A: Enforce the **simply-supported** boundary conditions only: **u = 0 on the boundary and Δu = 0 on the boundary**.

Do **not** enforce the Neumann zero-flux condition (∂u/∂n = 0); it is not part of the complete requirement.
