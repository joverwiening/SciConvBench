Q: In the PDE term u_tt = (1.0)^2*(u_xx+u_yy) + … but the parameters list says c = 2; which wave speed should be used in the update and CFL condition: 1.0 or 2?
A: Use **c = 1.0**.

The PDE explicitly has \((1.0)^2\) multiplying \((u_{xx}+u_{yy})\), and in the COMPLETE requirement the parameters list also states **c = 1.0**. So use **c=1.0** both in the time update and in the CFL condition.
