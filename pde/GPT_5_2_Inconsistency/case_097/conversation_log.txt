Q: In the PDE you wrote u_tt = (1.0)^2*(u_xx+u_yy) but you also specify the parameter c = 2 for the CFL; which wave speed should the solver actually use in the PDE (1.0 or 2)?
A: Use **c = 1.0** in the PDE and in the CFL/time-step calculation. The correct equation is:

\[
u_{tt} = (1.0)^2 (u_{xx} + u_{yy})
\]

So the solver should **not** use 2 anywhere.
