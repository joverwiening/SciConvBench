Q: I notice a contradiction in your boundary conditions. You've specified:

- Dirichlet boundary conditions on all four edges: u(x,0)=0, u(x,1)=sin(pi*x), u(0,y)=0, u(1,y)=0
- Periodic boundary conditions (identify the endpoints)

Which boundary condition type should I use - Dirichlet or periodic?
A: Use Dirichlet boundary conditions on all four edges as specified: u(x,0)=0, u(x,1)=sin(pi*x), u(0,y)=0, u(1,y)=0.

The periodic boundary condition line is an error in the requirement - ignore it. The problem is a standard Dirichlet boundary value problem for Laplace's equation.
