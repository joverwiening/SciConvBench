Q: What value should I use for Nx and Ny - should I use the specified Nx=Ny=60 from the numerical method requirements, or calculate them based on the given parameters Dx=0.1 and Dy=0.01 with the domain [0,1] x [0,1]?
A: Use Nx=Ny=60 as specified in the numerical method requirements. The parameters Dx=0.1 and Dy=0.01 are diffusion coefficients in the PDE, not grid spacings.
