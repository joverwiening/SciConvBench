Q: What should the value of k_max be for the 2/3 dealiasing rule - should it be Nx/2 (the Nyquist frequency) or some other value?
A: For a periodic grid with Nx=512 points, k_max should be Nx/2 = 256 (the Nyquist frequency). So you should zero out Fourier modes with |k| > (2/3)*256 ≈ 170.67, which means zero out modes with |k| ≥ 171.
