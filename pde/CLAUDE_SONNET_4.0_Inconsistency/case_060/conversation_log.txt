Q: I notice a contradiction in your boundary conditions. You've specified both:

- Dirichlet boundary conditions: "Set u on the boundary to match u(x,y)=y"
- Periodic boundary conditions: "Periodic on the spatial boundaries (identify the endpoints)"

These two types of boundary conditions cannot be applied simultaneously to the same boundaries. Which type of boundary condition do you want to use for this Laplace equation?
A: You should use Dirichlet boundary conditions only. Set u on all four edges of the square to match u(x,y)=y. There are no periodic boundary conditions for this problem.
