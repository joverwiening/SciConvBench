Q: I notice a contradiction in the boundary conditions. You've specified both:

- Dirichlet boundary conditions on all four edges
- Periodic boundary conditions on the spatial boundaries

Which type of boundary condition should I use for this Poisson equation?
A: Use Dirichlet boundary conditions on all four edges. Set u on the boundary to match u(x,y)=x*(1-x)*y*(1-y).

The periodic boundary condition line in the incomplete requirement is an error - ignore it.
