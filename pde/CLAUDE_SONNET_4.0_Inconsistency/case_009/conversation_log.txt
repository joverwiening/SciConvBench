Q: I notice a contradiction in the boundary conditions. You've specified both:

- Neumann conditions: u_x(0,t) = 0 and u_x(1,t) = 0
- Dirichlet conditions: u = 0 on the spatial boundary

Which boundary condition should I use at x=0 and x=1 - the Neumann conditions (zero derivative) or the Dirichlet conditions (u=0)?
A: Use the Neumann boundary conditions: u_x(0,t) = 0 and u_x(1,t) = 0. The Dirichlet condition "u = 0 on the spatial boundary" appears to be an error in the specification - ignore it and use only the Neumann conditions.
