The diffusion coefficient is D(x) = 0.1*(1+x).
Apply Dirichlet boundary conditions u(0,t)=0 and u(1,t)=0.
Use initial condition u(x,0)=sin(pi*x).
Use a conservative 2nd-order spatial discretization in flux form on a uniform grid.

Use implicit Euler time integration: (I - dt*L) u^{n+1} = u^n where L is the discrete diffusion operator.
