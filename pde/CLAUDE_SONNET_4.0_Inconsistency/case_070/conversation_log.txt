Q: What boundary conditions should be applied to the intermediate variable v = Δu when solving the first Poisson problem Δv = f?
A: Based on the complete requirement, apply Dirichlet boundary conditions v=0 on the boundary for the first Poisson problem Δv = f.

This comes from the simply-supported boundary conditions which specify that both u=0 and Δu=0 on the boundary. Since v = Δu, we have v=0 on the boundary.
