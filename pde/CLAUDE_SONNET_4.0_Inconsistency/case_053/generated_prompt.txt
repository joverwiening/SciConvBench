Apply 5-point Laplacian stencil for spatial discretization of u_xx+u_yy.

Set Nt = ceil(1.0/dt0) and then dt = 1.0/Nt to hit final time exactly.
Initialize using Taylor expansion with Laplacian and mass term for the first time step.
