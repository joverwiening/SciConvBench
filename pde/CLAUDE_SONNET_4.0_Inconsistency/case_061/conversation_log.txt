Q: I notice a contradiction in your boundary conditions. You've specified both:

- Dirichlet boundary conditions: "Set u on the boundary to match u(x,y)=x*y"
- Periodic boundary conditions: "Periodic on the spatial boundaries (identify the endpoints)"

Which type of boundary condition should I use for this Laplace equation problem?
A: Use Dirichlet boundary conditions only. Set u on all four edges of the square to match u(x,y)=x*y. There are no periodic boundary conditions in this problem.
