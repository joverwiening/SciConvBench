Q: I notice a contradiction in the boundary conditions. You've specified both:

- Neumann on all boundary edges: ∂u/∂n = 0
- Dirichlet on the spatial boundary: u = 0

Which boundary condition should I use for all four edges of the domain?
A: Use Neumann boundary conditions (∂u/∂n = 0) on all four edges. The Dirichlet condition was an error in the specification - ignore it.
