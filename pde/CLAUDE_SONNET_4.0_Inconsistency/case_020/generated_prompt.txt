For interior nodes (i=1..Nx-1, j=1..Ny-1), approximate (∇·(D∇u))_{i,j} ≈ (1/dx^2)[D_{i+1/2,j}(u_{i+1,j}-u_{i,j}) - D_{i-1/2,j}(u_{i,j}-u_{i-1,j})] + (1/dy^2)[D_{i,j+1/2}(u_{i,j+1}-u_{i,j}) - D_{i,j-1/2}(u_{i,j}-u_{i,j-1})].
Use implicit Euler time integration: (I - dt*L)u^{n+1} = u^n where L is the discrete diffusion operator.
