Q: What specific value should be used for the parameter gamma in the flux calculation, since both gamma=1.0 is listed in the parameters and the coefficient 1.0 appears directly in the PDE term ∂/∂x(1.0*x*p)?
A: Use gamma = 1.0. The coefficient 1.0 in the PDE term ∂/∂x(1.0*x*p) is the same as the gamma parameter listed in the parameters section.
