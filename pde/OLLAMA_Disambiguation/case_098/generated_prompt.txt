Compute the second‑order central difference Laplacian of u0 with periodic wrap‑around: u_xx0[i] = (u0[i+1] - 2*u0[i] + u0[i‑1]) / dx**2, using modulo indexing.

u1 = u0 + dt*v0 + 0.5*dt**2 * c**2 * u_xx0.

 Update the solution with the second‑order central time scheme:

 Shift the time levels: u_nm1 = u_n, u_n = u_np1.
After the loop, u_n contains the numerical solution at time T.
