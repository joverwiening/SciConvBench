At each time step apply Strang splitting:  

(A) Diffusion half‑step (duration dt/2):  
- Compute the discrete Fourier transform û = FFT(u) using numpy.fft.fft.  
- Compute wave numbers k = 2π·fftfreq(Nx, d=dx).  
- Multiply û by exp(‑nu·k²·(dt/2)) with nu = 5.0.  
- Transform back with IFFT and keep the real part to obtain the updated u.  

(B) Reaction full‑step (duration dt):  
- Solve the logistic ODE du/dt = rho·u·(1‑u) exactly at each grid point.  
- Update u by u ← (u·exp(rho·dt)) / (1 + u·(exp(rho·dt) – 1)) with rho = 10.0.  

(C) Diffusion half‑step again, identical to step (A).
