Q: What should the source term f(x,y) be—should it be set to the Laplacian of the exact solution, i.e. f(x,y)=uₓₓ+uᵧᵧ = -[(3π)²+(2π)²]·sin(3πx)·cos(2πy)?
A: Yes. According to the complete requirement the source term is  

\[
f(x,y)= -\big[(3\pi)^2+(2\pi)^2\big]\;\sin(3\pi x)\,\cos(2\pi y),
\]

i.e. the Laplacian of the exact solution \(u(x,y)=\sin(3\pi x)\cos(2\pi y)\).
