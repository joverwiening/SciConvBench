- Initialise  
\[
u_{i,j}(0)=\exp\!\bigl(-80\bigl[(x_i-0.2)^2+(y_j-0.6)^2\bigr]\bigr).
\]  
- Advection speeds: \(a=-0.7\) (negative → right‑biased upwind), \(b=1.2\) (positive → left‑biased upwind).  
- Upwind spatial discretisation:  
\[
a\,u_x\approx a\,\frac{u_{i+1,j}-u_{i,j}}{dx},\qquad
b\,u_y\approx b\,\frac{u_{i,j}-u_{i,j-1}}{dy}.
\]  
- Time integration: forward Euler.  
- Compute provisional step  
\[
dt_0=\frac{0.9}{|a|/dx+|b|/dy}.
\]  
- Set \(T=0.5\), \(N_t=\lceil T/dt_0\rceil\), and \(dt=T/N_t\) (so the final time is hit exactly).  
- For each step \(n=0,\dots,N_t-1\):  
\[
u^{n+1}=u^{n}+dt\Bigl[-a\,\frac{u_{i+1,j}^{n}-u_{i,j}^{n}}{dx}
      -b\,\frac{u_{i,j}^{n}-u_{i,j-1}^{n}}{dy}\Bigr],
\]  
with periodic indexing for \(i+1\) and \(j-1\).  
- After \(N_t\) steps, \(u\) is the solution at \(t=T\).
