Q: What is the initial condition u(x,y, 0) for the diffusion problem?
A: The initial condition is  

\[
u(x,y,0)=\sin(\pi\,x)\,\sin(\pi\,y)\,.
\]

Q: What boundary conditions should be applied on the edges of the domain \([0,1]\times[0,1]\) for \(u(x,y,t)\)?
A: All four edges of the square \([0,1]\times[0,1]\) have Dirichlet boundary conditions with  

\[
u(x,y,t)=0 \qquad\text{for } (x,y)\text{ on the boundary.}
\]
