Q: What boundary conditions should be applied on the edges of the domain?
A: Apply Dirichlet boundary conditions on all four edges of the square. Set the boundary values of u to the exact harmonic function  

\[
u(x,y)=\frac{\sinh(\pi x)\,\sin(\pi y)}{\sinh(\pi)},
\]

evaluated at the corresponding edge points.
