Q: What is the initial condition u(x, 0) for the diffusion problem?
A: The initial condition is  

\[
u(x,0)=x\,(1-x)\,,
\]  

for \(x\) in the domain \([0,1]\).

Q: What boundary conditions should be applied at x = 0 and x = 1?
A: The problem uses Dirichlet boundary conditions:

\[
u(0,t)=0,\qquad u(1,t)=0\;\;\text{for all }t\in[0,0.2].
\]
