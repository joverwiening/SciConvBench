Q: What boundary conditions should be applied to u on the four edges of the square domain [0, 1] × [0, 1]?
A: Apply Dirichlet conditions on all sides:

- Bottom edge (y = 0): u(x, 0) = 0  
- Top edge (y = 1):  u(x, 1) = 1  
- Left edge (x = 0):  u(0, y) = 0  
- Right edge (x = 1): u(1, y) = 0
