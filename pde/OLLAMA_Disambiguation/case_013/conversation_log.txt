Q: What boundary conditions should be applied at the domain edges x = 0 and x = 1 (e.g., periodic, Dirichlet, Neumann)?
A: Apply periodic boundary conditions: the solution and its derivative are periodic, i.e., u(0,t)=u(1,t) and u_x(0,t)=u_x(1,t) (wrap‑around indexing).
