Q: What Dirichlet boundary values should be applied on the edges of the domain (i.e., should u be set to the exact solution u(x,y)=x² − y² on all four boundaries)?
A: Yes. Apply Dirichlet conditions on all four edges by setting the boundary values exactly to the analytical solution  

\[
u(x,y)=x^{2}-y^{2},
\]

i.e., for every point on the left, right, bottom, and top boundaries use \(u = x^{2}-y^{2}\).
