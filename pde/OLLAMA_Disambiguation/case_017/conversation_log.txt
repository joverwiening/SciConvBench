Q: What is the initial condition u(x, 0) for the diffusion problem?

\[
u(x,0)=\sin(\pi\,x)\,.
\]

Q: What boundary conditions should be applied at x = 0 and x = 1?

\[
u(0,t)=0,\qquad u(1,t)=0\;.
