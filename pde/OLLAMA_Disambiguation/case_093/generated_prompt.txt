Solve the wave equation u_tt = (2.0)^2 * u_xx on the spatial domain x ∈ [0,1] and time interval t ∈ [0,0.5] with Dirichlet boundary conditions u(0,t)=0 and u(1,t)=0. Use the initial conditions u(x,0)=sin(pi*x) and u_t(x,0)=0. The exact solution for verification is u(x,t)=cos(2.0*pi*t)*sin(pi*x).

Integrate in time with the standard second‑order central difference scheme (leapfrog/Störmer–Verlet) for u_tt. Choose the time step dt so that the Courant number c*dt/dx ≤ 0.9, where c = 2.0.
