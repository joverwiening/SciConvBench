Q: What is the initial condition u(x, 0) for the heat equation?
A: The initial condition is  

\[
u(x,0)=0 \qquad\text{for all } x\in[0,1].
\]

Q: What are the Dirichlet boundary values at x = 0 and x = 1?
A: - At \(x = 0\): \(u(0,t) = \sin(2\pi\,t)\)  
- At \(x = 1\): \(u(1,t) = 0\)
