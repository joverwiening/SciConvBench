Solve the Euler–Bernoulli beam equation u_tt + 0.01*u_xxxx = 0 on the spatial domain [0,1] with simply‑supported boundary conditions u(0,t)=u(1,t)=0 and u_xx(0,t)=u_xx(1,t)=0.

Use a uniform time step dt = 1e‑4 and Nt = 10000 steps so that the final time T = Nt*dt = 1.0 is reached exactly.
