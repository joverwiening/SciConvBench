Q: What time integration method and time step size (or CFL number) should be used for advancing the solution to T = 1.0?
A: Use the **Forward Euler** method for time integration.  
Choose a CFL number of **0.5** and compute the time step as  

\[
dt_0 = \frac{\text{CFL}\;dx}{|q|},
\qquad 
Nt = \lceil T / dt_0 \rceil,
\qquad 
dt = \frac{T}{Nt},
\]

with \(dx = 1/N_x\) ( \(N_x = 512\) ) and \(q = 1.5\). This \(dt\) is then used to advance the solution to \(T = 1.0\).
