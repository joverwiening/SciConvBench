After completing all time steps, compute the solution u(x,T) on the grid. Save the arrays x (length Nx) and u (length Nx) together with the scalars nu, rho, T, dx, dt, Nx, and Nt in a NumPy .npz file named "solution_pdebench_reacdiff_nu2_rho5.npz".
