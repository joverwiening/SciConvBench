Solve the 1‑D compressible Navier–Stokes equations (no heat conduction) on the domain x∈[‑1, 1] for time t∈[0, 0.2] using a cell‑centered finite‑volume method with Nx = 800 uniform cells.

Define the conserved variables:
- density ρ,
- momentum m = ρ v,
- total energy E = p/(Γ‑1) + 0.5 ρ v².

Viscous stress:
- σ = (ζ + 4/3 η) ∂v/∂x,
- set η = 1e‑8, ζ = η,
- compute ∂v/∂x with a second‑order centered difference on the cell‑centered velocity values,
- evaluate σ at cell centers and average to the cell interfaces.

Governing equations in conservative form:
- ∂ρ/∂t + ∂m/∂x = 0,
- ∂m/∂t + ∂(m²/ρ + p ‑ σ)/∂x = 0,
- ∂E/∂t + ∂[(E + p) v ‑ v σ]/∂x = 0,
with Γ = 1.6666666666666667 and p = (Γ‑1)[E ‑ 0.5 ρ v²].

Initial condition (Sod shock‑tube):
- for x < 0: ρ = 0.445, v = 0.698, p = 3.528,
- for x ≥ 0: ρ = 0.5,   v = 0.0,   p = 0.571.

Boundary condition: transmissive (zero‑gradient) at both x = ‑1 and x = 1.

Spatial discretization:
- piecewise‑constant reconstruction (first‑order) in each cell,
- convective flux at each interface computed with the Rusanov (local Lax‑Friedrichs) flux,
- viscous flux obtained from the averaged σ at the interface.

Time integration:
- explicit 2‑stage SSP‑RK2 (Heun’s) scheme,
- time step Δt determined by CFL = 0.5:
  Δt = CFL · dx / max_i (|v_i| + c_i),
  where c_i = √[Γ p_i/ρ_i] is the local sound speed,
- advance until the final time T = 0.2, adjusting the last step to land exactly at T.

Output:
- compute ρ(x,T), v(x,T), p(x,T) at the cell centers,
- save a NumPy .npz file named “solution_pdebench_cns1d_sod6_eta1em8_outflow.npz” containing arrays:
  x (1‑D length Nx), rho, v, p,
  and scalars Gamma, eta, zeta, T, dx, CFL,
- generate a line plot of ρ, v, and p versus x at T (either three panels in one figure or three separate figures) and save as “solution_pdebench_cns1d_sod6_eta1em8_outflow.png”,
- print the following diagnostics:
  * minimum and maximum of ρ,
  * minimum and maximum of p,
  * mean value of ρ over the domain (mass‑conservation check).
