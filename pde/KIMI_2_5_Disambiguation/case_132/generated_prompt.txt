The PDE is a 1D nonlinear convection-diffusion equation with periodic boundary conditions on [0,1).

The spatial domain uses a uniform periodic grid with Nx=1024 points where x_j = j*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1 with dx=1/Nx.

The initial condition is u(x,0) = 0.5*sin(2*pi*x).

The convection term -q1*∂_x(cos(u)) is discretized using a conservative finite-volume Rusanov flux with wave speed alpha=max|sin(u)|, where q1=1.0.

The diffusion term (q2/pi)*u_xx uses 2nd-order central differences with q2=0.05.

Time integration uses SSP-RK2 (strong stability preserving Runge-Kutta 2nd order).

The time step is computed as dt_adv=0.2*dx/1 based on CFL=0.2 and max|sin(u)|<=1, and dt_diff=0.2*dx^2/(q2/pi), then dt=min(dt_adv,dt_diff) with Nt=ceil(T/dt) and final dt=T/Nt adjusted to reach T=0.5 exactly.

Boundary conditions are periodic: u(0,t)=u(1,t) implemented by ghost cell or index wrapping.

All trigonometric functions use radians.

The solution is computed at times t in [0, 0.5].
