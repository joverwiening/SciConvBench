The task is to solve a 1D compressible Navier-Stokes system with artificial viscosity (eta=1e-08) for a Sod shock tube problem with outflow boundary conditions.

The PDE system uses conservative variables: rho (density), m = rho*v (momentum), and E = p/(Gamma-1) + 0.5*rho*v^2 (total energy), with viscous stress sigma = (zeta + 4/3*eta) * v_x where zeta = eta.

The spatial domain is x in [-1, 1] with transmissive/outflow (zero-gradient) boundary conditions at both ends. The time domain is t in [0, 0.2].

Parameters are: Gamma = 1.6666666666666667, eta = 1e-08, zeta = 1e-08.

Initial condition is a Riemann problem with discontinuity at x=0: left state (rho_L=0.445, v_L=0.698, p_L=3.528) for x<0, right state (rho_R=0.5, v_R=0.0, p_R=0.571) for x>=0.

The numerical solution requires computing on cell centers. The grid uses Nx uniform intervals on [-1,1], giving Nx+1 grid points including endpoints, with dx = 2/Nx. Cell centers are at x_{i+1/2} = -1 + (i+0.5)*dx for i=0..Nx-1.

The solver must use a conservative finite volume or finite difference scheme with appropriate treatment of the viscous terms. A standard approach is to use a shock-capturing method for the inviscid fluxes (e.g., HLLC, Roe, or Lax-Friedrichs with MUSCL reconstruction) plus central differencing for viscous terms, with explicit time stepping and CFL condition.

For the viscous CNS with small eta=1e-08, the viscous term is weak but requires stable treatment. The viscous stress contribution to momentum flux is -sigma = -(zeta + 4/3*eta)*v_x = -(7/3)*eta*v_x. The viscous work term in energy flux is -v*sigma = -v*(7/3)*eta*v_x.

The boundary conditions are zero-gradient (transmissive/outflow), implemented by ghost cells with extrapolation.

Output requirements: compute rho, v, p at cell centers at T=0.2; save to NPZ file with arrays x, rho, v, p and scalars Gamma, eta, zeta, T, dx, CFL; save plot; report min/max of rho and p, and mean of rho.

The solution should use sufficient resolution (e.g., Nx=400 or higher) to capture the shock structure while keeping computation efficient, with CFL number around 0.3-0.5 for stability.
