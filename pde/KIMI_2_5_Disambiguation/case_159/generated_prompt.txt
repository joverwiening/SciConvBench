The task is to numerically solve a 1D compressible Navier-Stokes shock-tube problem with outflow boundary conditions and specific physical parameters.

The PDE system uses conservative variables U = [rho, m, E] with m = rho*v and E = p/(Gamma-1) + 0.5*rho*v^2, where Gamma = 5/3.

The spatial domain is x in [-1, 1] with transmissive/outflow boundary conditions at both ends.

The initial condition is a Riemann problem with discontinuity at x=0: left state (rho=1, v=0, p=1) for x<0, right state (rho=0.1, v=0, p=0.01) for x>=0.

The viscosity parameters are eta = zeta = 1e-08.

The numerical method uses a uniform finite-volume grid with Nx=400 cells on [-1,1], giving dx = 2/400 = 0.005.

Cell centers are located at x_j = -1 + (j+0.5)*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1.

The conservative variables [rho, m, E] are evolved as cell averages.

The equation of state computes pressure as p = (Gamma-1)*(E - 0.5*m^2/rho), with rho and p clipped to floor 1e-12 if negative.

The convective flux uses Rusanov (local Lax-Friedrichs): F_hat = 0.5*(F_L + F_R) - 0.5*a*(U_R - U_L), where a = max(|v_L|+c_L, |v_R|+c_R) and c = sqrt(Gamma*p/rho).

The viscous stress is sigma = (zeta + 4/3*eta)*v_x, with v_x computed by centered differences on cell-centered v, and sigma at interfaces averaged from neighboring cells.

The viscous flux contribution is F_visc = [0, -sigma, -v*sigma] using interface-averaged v and sigma.

The total interface flux is F_total = F_hat + F_visc.

Time integration uses 2-stage SSP-RK2 (Heun's method).

The time step is dt = min(dt_conv, dt_visc) where dt_conv = CFL*dx/max(|v|+c) and dt_visc = 0.5*dx^2/max(zeta+4/3*eta, 1e-30), with CFL=0.5, and final step adjusted to hit T=0.2 exactly.

Outflow boundary conditions are implemented by setting ghost cell values equal to the nearest interior cell.

The solution is computed at T=0.2, then rho, v, p are extracted at cell centers.

The output files are saved: "solution_pdebench_cns1d_shock_eta1em8_outflow.npz" containing x, rho, v, p arrays and scalars Gamma, eta, zeta, T, dx, CFL; and "solution_pdebench_cns1d_shock_eta1em8_outflow.png" showing line plots of rho, v, p versus x.

The diagnostics printed are: min/max of rho, min/max of p, and mean of rho over the domain.
