The task is to numerically solve the Klein-Gordon PDE with the following complete specification:

The PDE is u_tt - c^2*u_xx + m^2*u = sin(pi*x)*cos(2*t) with c=1.0 and m=1.0.

The spatial domain is x in [0,1] and time domain is t in [0,5.0].

Use a uniform grid with Nx=400 intervals, giving Nx+1=401 grid points including both endpoints at x=0 and x=1. The grid spacing is dx=(1-0)/400=0.0025 with x_i=i*dx for i=0..400.

Use time step dt=0.001, which gives 5000 time steps to reach T=5.0. This satisfies the CFL condition c*dt/dx=0.4 ≤ 0.9.

Apply Dirichlet boundary conditions u(0,t)=0 and u(1,t)=0, which are consistent with the initial condition and the forcing term structure.

The initial conditions are u(x,0)=0 and u_t(x,0)=0.

For spatial discretization, use second-order central differences: u_xx at grid point i is (u_{i+1}-2*u_i+u_{i-1})/dx^2.

For time integration, use the leapfrog (central difference) method: u^{n+1}_i = 2*u^n_i - u^{n-1}_i + dt^2*(c^2*u_xx^n_i - m^2*u^n_i + f^n_i) where f^n_i = sin(pi*x_i)*cos(2*t_n).

Handle the m^2*u term explicitly at time level n in the update as shown above.

For initialization at n=0 and n=1, use Taylor expansion: u^0_i=0, then compute v^0_i=u_t(x_i,0)=0. Then u^1_i = u^0_i + dt*v^0_i + 0.5*dt^2*(c^2*u_xx^0_i - m^2*u^0_i + f^0_i). Since u^0=0 and v^0=0, this simplifies to u^1_i = 0.5*dt^2*f^0_i where f^0_i=sin(pi*x_i).

Include the forcing term at time level n in the leapfrog update for steps n≥1.

At the final time T=5.0, compute u(x,T) on the grid.

Save "solution_agent_kg_07.npz" containing: x (1D array of length 401), u (1D array of length 401), and scalars T=5.0, dx=0.0025, dt=0.001.

Save "solution_agent_kg_07.png" as a line plot of u(x,T) versus x.

Print min(u), max(u), and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at T=5.0.
