Q: What is the value of Nx and Ny? The specification says "Use Nx=Ny=60", but also says "Uniform grid with Nx=Ny intervals." Please confirm the number of intervals (N) is 60, meaning there will be 61 grid points in each spatial direction (since the domain is a closed interval).
A: Nx=Ny=60 intervals, so there are 61 grid points in each direction.
