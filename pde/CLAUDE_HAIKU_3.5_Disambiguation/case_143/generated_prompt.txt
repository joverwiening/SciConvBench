Numerical Solution Specification for 1D Viscous Burgers Equation:

Solve the 1D viscous Burgers equation u_t + ∂_x(0.5*u^2) = nu * u_xx with the following specifications:

PDE Details:
- Equation: u_t + ∂_x(0.5*u^2) = nu * u_xx
- Viscosity (nu): 0.01
- Spatial Domain: Periodic boundary conditions
- Initial Condition: u(x,0) = sin(2*pi*x)

Numerical Method:
- Spatial Discretization: Uniform grid with Nx = 512 points
- Time Stepping: Strang Splitting Method
  - Diffusion Half-Step (A): Solved exactly in Fourier space
  - Advection Full-Step (B): Finite-volume Rusanov (local Lax-Friedrichs) flux
  - Diffusion Half-Step (C): Repeated Fourier space method
- Time Step Selection: 
  - CFL = 0.4
  - dt0 = CFL * dx / 1.0
  - Nt = ceil(T/dt0)
  - Final dt adjusted to hit exact final time

- Save solution to "solution_pdebench_burgers_nu0p01.npz"
