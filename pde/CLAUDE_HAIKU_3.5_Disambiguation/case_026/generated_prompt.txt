Numerical Solution Parameters:
Spatial Domain: x in [0,1]
Time Domain: t in [0,0.5]
Diffusion Coefficient (D): 0.01
Grid Points: Nx = 600

- Diffusion term: Implicit (backward Euler)
- Reaction term: Explicit (forward Euler)

- Use sparse direct solver for each time step

Boundary Conditions:
- u(0,t) = 1 (Dirichlet at x=0)
- u(1,t) = 0 (Dirichlet at x=1)
