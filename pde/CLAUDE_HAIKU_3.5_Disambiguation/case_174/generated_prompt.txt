Solve the 1D Reaction-Diffusion PDE with the following specification:

Partial Differential Equation:
u_t = 0.5 * u_xx + 2.0 * u*(1 - u)

Numerical Solution Parameters:
Solve the PDE on a uniform spatial grid with Nx=512 points.
Use fixed time step dt=0.001 for Nt=1000 steps.
Use Strang splitting method with three stages:
(A) Half diffusion step in Fourier space
(B) Full reaction step solved exactly pointwise
(C) Half diffusion step in Fourier space

Initial Condition:
u(x,0) = 0.25 + 0.1*sin(2*pi*x)

Boundary Conditions:
Periodic boundary conditions (u(0,t) = u(1,t))

Computational Requirements:
Compute solution at final time T.
Save solution to "solution_pdebench_reacdiff_nu0p5_rho2.npz" with arrays:
- x (1D grid points)
- u (1D solution at final time)
- Scalar parameters: nu, rho, T, dx, dt, Nx, Nt

Visualization and Reporting:
Save line plot of u(x,T) as "solution_pdebench_reacdiff_nu0p5_rho2.png"
Print at final time:
- Minimum of u
- Maximum of u
- Mean of u
- Discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx)
