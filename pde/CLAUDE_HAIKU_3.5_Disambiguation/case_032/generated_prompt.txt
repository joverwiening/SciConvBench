- Use Crank-Nicolson method for diffusion terms
- Time step dt = 0.0005
- Total number of time steps = 600
- Enforce Dirichlet boundary conditions at each time step

Initial Condition:
- Use u(x,0) = sin(π*x) + 0.5*sin(3π*x)

Boundary Conditions:
- Fixed Dirichlet boundary conditions (zero or as implied by the exact solution)
