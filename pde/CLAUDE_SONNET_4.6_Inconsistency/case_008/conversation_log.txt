Q: The boundary conditions are contradictory: the problem specifies Dirichlet conditions (u=1 at x=0, u=-1 at x=1) and also periodic boundary conditions on the same spatial boundaries. These two conditions cannot both be satisfied simultaneously.

Which boundary condition should be used: Dirichlet (u=1 at x=0, u=-1 at x=1), or periodic?
A: Use the Dirichlet boundary conditions: u(0,t)=1 and u(1,t)=-1. Ignore the periodic note — that was an error in the incomplete version of the spec.
