Q: Looking at this specification, I notice a contradiction in the boundary conditions:

Item 4 specifies both Dirichlet conditions (u=0 at x=0 and x=1) AND periodic boundary conditions on the same spatial boundaries. These are mutually exclusive — Dirichlet fixes the value at endpoints, while periodic identifies the two endpoints as the same point (requiring u(0,t) = u(1,t) and compatible derivatives).

Which boundary condition should be used for this problem?
A: Use Dirichlet boundary conditions: u=0 at x=0 and x=1. The periodic mention is a mistake in the specification — ignore it.
