Q: Looking at the boundary conditions, there's a contradiction in the specification:

Section 4 specifies **both** Dirichlet conditions (u=1 at x=0, u=0 at x=1) **and** periodic boundary conditions on the same spatial boundaries. These are mutually exclusive — Dirichlet fixes specific values at the endpoints, while periodic identifies the two endpoints as the same point (requiring u(0,t) = u(1,t)).

Which boundary condition should be used for this problem: the Dirichlet conditions (u(0,t)=1, u(1,t)=0), or periodic boundary conditions?
A: Use the Dirichlet conditions: u(0,t)=1 and u(1,t)=0. The periodic mention in section 4 is an error in the specification.
