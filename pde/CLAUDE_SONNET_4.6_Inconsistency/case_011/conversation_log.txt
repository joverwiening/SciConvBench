Q: Looking at the numerical method requirements, I notice a contradiction: item 7 lists **two** time integration methods:

- "Explicit forward Euler"
- "Semi-implicit: (u^{n+1}-u^n)/dt = ε²·Δu^{n+1} + u^n - (u^n)³"

Which time integration method should be used?
A: Use the semi-implicit method: (u^{n+1}-u^n)/dt = ε²·Δu^{n+1} + u^n - (u^n)³.
