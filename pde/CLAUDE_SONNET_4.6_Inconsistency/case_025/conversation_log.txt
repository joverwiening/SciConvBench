Q: Looking at the boundary conditions, there is a contradiction in the specification:

Item 4 lists **two conflicting boundary conditions** simultaneously:
- Neumann: u_x(0,t) = 0 and u_x(1,t) = 0
- Dirichlet: u = 0 on the spatial boundary

These cannot both be satisfied in general. Which boundary condition should be used for this problem?
A: Use the Neumann boundary conditions: u_x(0,t)=0 and u_x(1,t)=0. The Dirichlet condition listed in the incomplete version was an error and is not in the actual requirement.
