Q: The boundary conditions are contradictory: the problem specifies Dirichlet conditions on all four edges (u = x*y on the boundary) and also periodic boundary conditions on the spatial boundaries. These two conditions cannot both be satisfied simultaneously in general.

Which boundary condition should be used for the numerical solve?
A: Use Dirichlet boundary conditions on all four edges, setting u = x*y on the boundary. Ignore the periodic note — that was an error in the incomplete version. The complete requirement only specifies Dirichlet BCs.
