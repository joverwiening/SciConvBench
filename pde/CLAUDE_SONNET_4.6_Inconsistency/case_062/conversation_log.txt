Q: The specification contains a contradiction: it specifies both Dirichlet boundary conditions (u set to match x²-y² on all four edges) and periodic boundary conditions on the spatial boundaries simultaneously. These two conditions are incompatible in general.

Which boundary condition should be used for this problem?
A: Use Dirichlet boundary conditions only — set u on all four edges to match x²-y². Ignore the periodic note; that was an error in the incomplete version.
