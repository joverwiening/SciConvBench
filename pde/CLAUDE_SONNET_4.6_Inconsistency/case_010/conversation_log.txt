Q: Looking at the numerical method requirements, I notice a contradiction: the prompt specifies both "Explicit forward Euler" and "Semi-implicit" time integration. Which time integration method should be used?
A: Use the semi-implicit method: (u^{n+1}-u^n)/dt = ε^2*Δu^{n+1} + u^n - (u^n)^3. The "Explicit forward Euler" line is a mistake/leftover and should be ignored.
