Solve the 1D convection-diffusion PDE numerically as specified below.

The PDE is u_t = -q1 * d/dx(0.5 * u**2) + (q2/pi) * u_xx.
The spatial domain is [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions.
The time domain is [0, 0.5].
