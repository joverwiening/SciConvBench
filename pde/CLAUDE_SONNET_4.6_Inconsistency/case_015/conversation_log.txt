Q: Looking at the boundary conditions, there is a contradiction in the specification:

Section 4 specifies both Dirichlet BCs (u(0,t)=0, u(1,t)=0) AND periodic BCs on the spatial boundaries simultaneously. These are mutually exclusive — Dirichlet pins the endpoints to zero while periodicity would identify them as the same point.

Section 7 (boundary_handling) resolves this by explicitly saying to enforce Dirichlet BCs and use boundary values rather than periodic wrap-around.

Which boundary condition should be used: Dirichlet (u=0 at both ends) or periodic?
A: Use Dirichlet BCs: u(0,t)=0 and u(1,t)=0. The periodic mention in section 4 is a typo/error. Section 7 is the authoritative specification.
