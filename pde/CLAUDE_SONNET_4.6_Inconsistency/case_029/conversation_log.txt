Q: Looking at the specifications, I notice a contradiction:

The boundary conditions specify **both** Dirichlet (u=0 on all edges) **and** Periodic conditions on the spatial boundaries. These two conditions are mutually exclusive — you cannot simultaneously enforce u=0 at the boundary and identify opposite endpoints periodically (which would require u(0,y)=u(1,y) and u(x,0)=u(x,1), not necessarily zero).

Which boundary condition should be used for this problem?
A: Use **Dirichlet boundary conditions (u=0 on all edges)**. The periodic mention is an error in the specification — ignore it.
