Q: Looking at the specifications, I notice a contradiction:

The boundary conditions specify **both** Dirichlet (u=0 at x=0 and x=1) **and** Periodic conditions on the same spatial boundaries. These are mutually exclusive — Dirichlet pins the endpoint values to zero, while periodic identifies the two endpoints as the same point (implying a half-open domain with no duplicated endpoint).

Which boundary condition should be used for this problem?
A: Use the Dirichlet boundary conditions: u(0,t)=0 and u(1,t)=0. The domain is the closed interval [0,1] with Nx=300 intervals and 301 grid points including both endpoints.
