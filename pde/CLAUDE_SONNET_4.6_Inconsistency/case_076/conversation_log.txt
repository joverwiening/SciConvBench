Q: The boundary conditions contain a contradiction: the problem specifies both Dirichlet conditions (u = x*(1-x)*y*(1-y) on all four edges) and periodic conditions on the spatial boundaries. These two conditions are generally incompatible.

Which boundary condition should be used for the numerical solve?
A: Use Dirichlet boundary conditions only: set u = x*(1-x)*y*(1-y) on all four edges. Ignore the periodic condition — that was an error in the incomplete requirement.
