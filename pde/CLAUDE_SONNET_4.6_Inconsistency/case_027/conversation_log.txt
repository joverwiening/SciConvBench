Q: Looking at the specification, I notice a contradiction in the boundary conditions:

Item 4 specifies **both** Neumann (∂u/∂n = 0) and Dirichlet (u = 0) on the spatial boundary. These are two different conditions and cannot both hold simultaneously (unless u ≡ 0 on the boundary, which conflicts with the initial condition).

Which boundary condition should be used: zero Neumann (∂u/∂n = 0) or zero Dirichlet (u = 0)?
A: Use zero Neumann (∂u/∂n = 0), implemented via ghost nodes (mirror values). The Dirichlet condition listed in the incomplete version was an error.
