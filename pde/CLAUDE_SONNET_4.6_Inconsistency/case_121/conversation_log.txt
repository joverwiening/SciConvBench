Q: I notice a contradiction in the numerical method requirements: item 7 first specifies "Explicit forward Euler" for time integration, then specifies "IMEX Euler (diffusion implicit, reaction explicit) OR Strang splitting with FFT" — these are mutually exclusive choices.

A: Use IMEX Euler (diffusion implicit, reaction explicit) or Strang splitting with FFT — not explicit forward Euler. The "Explicit forward Euler" line in the incomplete requirement was a mistake/artifact that doesn't appear in the complete requirement.
