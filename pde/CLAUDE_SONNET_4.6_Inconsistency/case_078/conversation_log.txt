Q: The boundary conditions contain a contradiction: the problem specifies both Dirichlet conditions (u set to cos(pi*x)*cos(pi*y) on all four edges) and periodic conditions on the spatial boundaries. These two conditions cannot both apply simultaneously on a closed domain [0,1]x[0,1].

Which boundary condition should be used: Dirichlet (u = cos(pi*x)*cos(pi*y) on all four edges) or periodic?
A: Use Dirichlet boundary conditions: set u = cos(pi\*x)\*cos(pi\*y) on all four edges. The periodic mention was an error in the incomplete version.
