Q: What boundary conditions should be applied for this Laplace equation problem? I need to know the values or conditions for u(x,y) on all four boundaries (x=0, x=1, y=0, and y=1).
A: The boundary conditions are Dirichlet on all four edges of the square, where u should be set to match u(x,y)=x. This means:
- At x=0: u=0
- At x=1: u=1
- At y=0: u=x
- At y=1: u=x
