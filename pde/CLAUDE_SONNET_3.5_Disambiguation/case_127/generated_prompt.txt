The numerical solution shall be implemented in Python using NumPy for array operations and computations.
The spatial domain [0,1) shall be discretized with Nx=512 uniform points.
Grid points shall be computed as x_j = j*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1 with dx=1/Nx.
The time domain [0,0.5] shall use dt = min(0.4*dx/1.0, 0.2*dx^2/(0.05/pi)).
The number of time steps shall be Nt=ceil(0.5/dt), with dt adjusted to dt=0.5/Nt.
The initial condition shall be u(x,0) = sin(2*pi*x) + 0.5*sin(4*pi*x).
Periodic boundary conditions shall be enforced with u[0]=u[Nx-1].
Spatial derivatives shall use 1st-order upwind for advection term and 2nd-order central differences for diffusion term.
Time integration shall use IMEX Euler scheme with implicit diffusion and explicit advection.
Parameters shall be q1=1.0 for advection and q2=0.05 for diffusion.
The solution shall be verified against the analytical Fourier-mode evolution.
