The spatial domain is [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions.
The temporal domain is [0, 1.0].

The initial condition is u(x,0) = sin(2*pi*x) + 0.2*sin(6*pi*x).
The exact solution is u(x,t) = u0((x - 1.5*t) mod 1), where u0 is the initial condition.
Compute the solution u(x,T) at T = 1.0.
Save the solution arrays x and u, along with scalars T, dx, Nx, and q to "solution_molk_018.npz".
Create and save a line plot of u(x,T) as "solution_molk_018.png".
Print the minimum, maximum, mean, and discrete L2 norm of the solution at T.
