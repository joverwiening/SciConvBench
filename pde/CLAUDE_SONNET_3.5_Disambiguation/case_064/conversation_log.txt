Q: What are the boundary conditions for this Laplace equation? They are not specified in the problem description but are necessary to obtain a unique solution.
A: The boundary conditions are:
u(x,0) = 0
u(x,1) = sin(2*pi*x)
u(0,y) = 0
u(1,y) = 0

These are Dirichlet conditions on all four edges of the square domain.
