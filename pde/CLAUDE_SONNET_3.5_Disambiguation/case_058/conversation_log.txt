Q: What boundary conditions should be applied to solve this Laplace equation? I need the values or conditions for u(x,y) on all four boundaries (x=0, x=1, y=0, and y=1).
A: The boundary conditions are Dirichlet on all four edges, using u(x,y)=sinh(pi*x)*sin(pi*y)/sinh(pi) evaluated at the boundaries.
