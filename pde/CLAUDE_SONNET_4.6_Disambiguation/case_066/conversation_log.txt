Q: What is the source term f(x,y) for the PDE?

(Based on the exact solution u(x,y)=sin(pi*x)*sin(pi*y), the consistent f would be (k^2 - 2*pi^2)*sin(pi*x)*sin(pi*y), but please confirm or provide the intended f(x,y).)
A: Yes, that's correct: f(x,y) = (k^2 - 2*(pi^2)) * sin(pi*x) * sin(pi*y).
