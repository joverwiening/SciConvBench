Rearrange to form a linear system for u_new at each time step: u_new[i] - dt * epsilon**2 * (u_new[i-1] - 2*u_new[i] + u_new[i+1]) / dx**2 = u_old[i] + dt * (u_old[i] - u_old[i]**3).
