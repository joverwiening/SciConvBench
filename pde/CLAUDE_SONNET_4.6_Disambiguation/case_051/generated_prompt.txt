Solve the Klein-Gordon PDE numerically using the exact specifications below.

PDE: u_tt - (1.0)**2 * u_xx + (3.0)**2 * u = 0, where c=1.0 and m=3.0.

Boundary conditions: u and u_x are periodic in x.

Initial conditions: u(x,0) = cos(2*pi*x) and u_t(x,0) = 0.
