Q: Looking at the specification, I notice the spatial domain is described as "not specified" in the grid section, yet the boundary condition references u(0,t) = u(1,t), and the initial condition uses x in a way consistent with a [0,1) domain.

What is the spatial domain for this problem — is it the half-open interval [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions?
A: Yes, the spatial domain is [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions. Use x_j = j*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1 with dx=1/Nx.
