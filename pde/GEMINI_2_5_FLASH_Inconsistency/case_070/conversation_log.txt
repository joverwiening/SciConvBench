Q: The boundary conditions are contradictory: "Simply-supported on all boundaries: u=0 on the boundary AND (Δu)=0 on the boundary" and "Neumann (zero-flux) on the boundary: ∂u/∂n = 0" cannot both be true. Which set of boundary conditions should be applied?
A: Apply "Simply-supported on all boundaries: u=0 on the boundary AND (Δu)=0 on the boundary".
