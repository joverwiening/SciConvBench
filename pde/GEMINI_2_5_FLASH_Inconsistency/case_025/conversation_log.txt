Q: The boundary conditions are contradictory, specifying both Neumann and Dirichlet conditions on the spatial boundaries. Which type of boundary condition should be applied at x=0 and x=1?
A: Neumann boundary conditions should be applied at x=0 and x=1: u_x(0,t)=0, u_x(1,t)=0.
