The task is to numerically solve the 1D Reaction-Diffusion PDE u_t = nu * u_xx + rho * u*(1 - u).
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions, and the time domain is t in [0, 1.0].
The parameters are nu = 0.5 and rho = 5.0.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = 0.25 + 0.1*sin(2*pi*x).
A uniform periodic spatial grid with Nx=512 points on [0,1) must be used, where x_j = j*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1 and dx=1/Nx.
A fixed time step dt=0.001 must be used for Nt=1000 steps, reaching T=1.0 exactly.
The numerical method is Strang splitting for each full time step dt, consisting of three sub-steps:
1.  A diffusion half-step of duration dt/2, solved exactly in Fourier space. This involves computing the FFT of u to get u_hat(k), where k = 2*pi*fftfreq(Nx, d=dx). Then, u_hat(k) is updated as u_hat(k) * exp(-nu*k^2*(dt/2)). Finally, the IFFT of u_hat is computed, and its real part is taken to get u.
2.  A reaction full-step of duration dt, solved exactly pointwise for the logistic ODE du/dt = rho*u*(1-u). For each grid point, u is updated using the formula u <- (u*exp(rho*dt)) / (1 + u*(exp(rho*dt) - 1)).
3.  Another diffusion half-step of duration dt/2, identical to the first diffusion half-step.
The final solution u(x,T=1.0) on the periodic grid must be computed.
The solution must be saved to a file named "solution_pdebench_reacdiff_nu0p5_rho5.npz". This file must contain the arrays x (1D length Nx) and u (1D length Nx), and the scalars nu, rho, T, dx, dt, Nx, and Nt.
A line plot of u(x,T) must be saved as "solution_pdebench_reacdiff_nu0p5_rho5.png".
The minimum, maximum, mean, and discrete L2 norm (sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx)) of u at T must be printed to the console.
