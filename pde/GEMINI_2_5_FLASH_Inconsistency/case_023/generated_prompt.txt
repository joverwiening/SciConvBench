The task is to numerically solve the 1D Diffusion-Reaction Partial Differential Equation u_t = 0.1*u_xx + (-1.0)*u.
The unknown field is u(x,t).
The spatial domain is x in [0,1] and the temporal domain is t in [0,0.5].
The diffusion coefficient D is 0.1 and the reaction coefficient lambda is -1.0.
Dirichlet boundary conditions are applied at x=0 and x=1, specifically u(0,t)=0 and u(1,t)=0.
The initial condition is u(x,0)=sin(pi*x).
The reference exact solution for verification is u(x,t)=exp((-1.0 - 0.1*pi^2)*t)*sin(pi*x).
The spatial discretization will use 2nd-order central differences for diffusion terms on a uniform grid.
The time integration will use a deterministic IMEX scheme (1st order), with diffusion treated implicitly (Backward Euler) and reaction treated explicitly (Forward Euler).
At each time step, the equation (I - dt*D*L) u^{n+1} = u^n + dt * R(u^n) will be solved, where L is the discrete Laplacian and R(u) is the reaction term.
A sparse direct solver will be used at each time step.
The grid will have Nx=200 spatial intervals, resulting in Nx+1 grid points.
The time step dt will be 0.001, for a total of 500 time steps.
The numerical solution u(x, T=0.5) will be computed on the specified grid.
The solution will be saved to "solution_agent_diffusion_reaction_01.npz", containing 1D arrays for x and u, and scalar metadata T, dx, and dt.
A line plot of u(x,T) will be saved as "solution_agent_diffusion_reaction_01.png".
The minimum value of u, maximum value of u, and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at time T will be printed.
The maximum absolute error and the L2 error against the reference solution, evaluated on the same x grid, will also be computed and printed.
