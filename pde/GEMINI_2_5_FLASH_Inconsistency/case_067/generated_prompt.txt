Solve the 1D Cahn-Hilliard equation numerically.
The PDE is u_t = -(0.01)^2*u_xxxx + ∂^2/∂x^2( u^3 - u ).
The spatial domain is x in [0,1) with periodic boundary conditions.
The time domain is t in [0,0.05].
The parameter epsilon is 0.01.
The initial condition is u(x,0)=0.1*cos(2*pi*x) + 0.01*cos(6*pi*x).
Use a Fourier pseudo-spectral method (FFT) on a periodic grid.
Represent u(x,t) on Nx=512 equally spaced points x_j=j/Nx.
Use a semi-implicit spectral time integration step.
Treat the linear operator L(k)=(-ε^2*k^4 + k^2) implicitly.
Treat the nonlinear term N(u)=∂xx(u^3) explicitly.
The time integration formula in Fourier space is û^{n+1} = (û^n + dt * 𝔽[∂xx(u^3)] ) / (1 - dt*L(k)).
Use dt=1e-5, resulting in 5000 time steps.
Apply 2/3 dealiasing for the u^3 term by zeroing out Fourier modes with |k| > (2/3)*k_max after computing FFT(u^3).
Compute u(x, T=0.05) on the grid.
Save the solution to "solution_agent_other_02_cahn_hilliard.npz".
