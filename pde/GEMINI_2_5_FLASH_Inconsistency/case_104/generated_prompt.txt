No closed-form reference solution is required.

The time step is dt = 0.001.
The number of time steps is Nt = 5000.
The final time is hit exactly.
Compute u(t) at all time points t_n = t0 + n*dt for n=0..Nt.
Save "solution_molk_003.npz" containing arrays: t (1D length Nt+1), u (1D length Nt+1), and the parameter scalars.
