Q: The boundary conditions are contradictory, specifying both Neumann and Dirichlet conditions on the spatial boundary. Which boundary condition should be applied?
A: The Neumann boundary conditions should be applied: u_x(0,t)=0, u_x(1,t)=0. The Dirichlet condition "u = 0" is a mistake and should be ignored.
