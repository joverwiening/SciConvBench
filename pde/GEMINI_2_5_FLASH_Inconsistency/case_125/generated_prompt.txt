The PDE u_t = -q1*∂_x( 0.5*u^2 ) + (q2/pi)*u_xx will be solved numerically.
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions, and the time domain is t in [0, 0.5].
The parameters are q1 = 1.0 and q2 = 0.1.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = sin(2*pi*x).
A uniform periodic spatial grid will be used with Nx=512 points on [0,1).
The grid points are x_j = j*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1, where dx = 1/Nx.
The nonlinear flux term 0.5*u^2 will be discretized using a conservative finite-volume or finite-difference approach with the Rusanov (local Lax–Friedrichs) flux.
The diffusion term u_xx will be discretized using 2nd-order central differences.
Time integration will be performed using the SSP-RK2 (Heun) method.
The time step dt will be fixed and calculated based on both convection and diffusion stability.
D_eff is defined as q2/pi.
dt_conv is calculated as CFL * dx / max|u0| with CFL=0.4 and max|u0|=1.
dt_diff is calculated as 0.4 * dx^2 / D_eff.
dt0 is set to min(dt_conv, dt_diff).
The number of time steps Nt is ceil(T/dt0), and the final dt is T/Nt to hit the final time T=0.5 exactly.
The numerical solution u(x,T=0.5) on the periodic grid will be computed.
The solution will be saved to "solution_molk_024.npz" containing arrays x and u, and scalars T, dx, Nx, q1, and q2.
A line plot of u(x,T) will be saved as "solution_molk_024.png".
The minimum, maximum, mean, and discrete L2 norm (sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx)) of u at time T will be printed to the console.
