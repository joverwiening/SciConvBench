The 1D linear advection-diffusion PDE u_t = -q1*∂_x( u ) + (q2/pi)*u_xx shall be solved numerically.
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions, and the temporal domain is t in [0, 0.5].
The parameter q1 is 1.0.
The parameter q2 is 0.05.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = sin(2*pi*x) + 0.5*sin(4*pi*x).
A uniform periodic spatial grid with Nx=512 points shall be used on [0,1).
The grid points are defined as x_j = j*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1, with dx=1/Nx.
The spatial discretization shall use 1st-order upwind for the advection term ∂_x(u) and 2nd-order central differences for the diffusion term u_xx.
Time integration shall use the IMEX Euler method, with diffusion treated implicitly and advection treated explicitly.
The time step dt shall be calculated as dt = min(0.4*dx/|q1|, 0.2*dx^2/(q2/pi)).
The number of time steps Nt shall be calculated as ceil(T/dt), and dt shall be reset to T/Nt.
The numerical solution u(x,T=0.5) shall be computed on the periodic grid.
The exact solution for verification is u(x,t) = sin(2*pi*(x - q1*t)) * exp(-(q2/pi)*(2*pi)^2*t) + 0.5*sin(4*pi*(x - q1*t)) * exp(-(q2/pi)*(4*pi)^2*t).
A file named "solution_molk_026.npz" shall be saved, containing the arrays x (1D length Nx), u (1D length Nx), and the scalars T, dx, Nx, q1, and q2.
A line plot of u(x,T) shall be saved as "solution_molk_026.png".
The minimum, maximum, mean, and discrete L2 norm (sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx)) of u at time T shall be printed.
The maximum absolute error and L2 error between the numerical solution and the exact solution (evaluated on the same x grid at time T) shall be computed and printed.
