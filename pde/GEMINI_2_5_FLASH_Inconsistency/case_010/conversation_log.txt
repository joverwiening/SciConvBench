Q: You specified both "Explicit forward Euler" and "Semi-implicit" for time integration. Which time integration method should be used?
A: Use the semi-implicit time integration method: (u^{n+1}-u^n)/dt = ε^2*Δu^{n+1} + u^n - (u^n)^3. Solve a sparse linear system each step.
