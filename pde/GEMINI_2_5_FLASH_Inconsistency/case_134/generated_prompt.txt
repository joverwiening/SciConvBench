The task is to numerically solve the 1D advection-diffusion PDE u_t = D*u_xx - (1/gamma)*∂_x( U'(x) * u ) on the domain x in [0, 1) and t in [0, 1.0].
The parameters are D = 0.01 and gamma = 1.0.
The drift potential is U(x) = cos(2*pi*x), and its derivative is U'(x) = -2*pi*sin(2*pi*x).
The initial condition is u(x,0) = 1.0 + 0.2*sin(2*pi*x).
Periodic boundary conditions u(0,t)=u(1,t) apply for all t.
The spatial grid is uniform and periodic with Nx=512 points on [0,1).
The grid points are x_j = j*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1, where dx=1/Nx.
The drift term is treated in conservative form, with flux F = (1/gamma)*U'(x)*u, so the PDE is u_t = D*u_xx - ∂_x(F).
Spatial discretization uses upwind finite-volume flux for ∂_x(F), based on the sign of the interface velocity b=U'(x)/gamma.
Second-order central differences are used for the u_xx term.
Time integration uses the Explicit SSP-RK2 method.
The time step dt is chosen as dt = min(0.4*dx/max|b|, 0.2*dx^2/D), where b(x)=U'(x)/gamma.
The number of time steps Nt is calculated as ceil(T/dt), and dt is then adjusted to T/Nt.
The solution u(x,T=1.0) is computed on the periodic grid.
The results are saved to "solution_molk_033.npz", containing arrays x (1D length Nx), u (1D length Nx), and scalars T, dx, Nx, D, and gamma.
A line plot of u(x,T) is saved as "solution_molk_033.png".
The minimum, maximum, mean, and discrete L2 norm (sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx)) of u at T are printed to the console.
No reference solution is provided, so no error calculation is required.
