The PDE to be solved is u_tt + (q2**2)*(q1**4)*u = (q1**2)*u_xx.
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions.
The temporal domain is t in [0, 1.0].
The parameter q1 is 1.0.
The parameter q2 is 0.5.
The boundary conditions are u(0,t)=u(1,t) and u_x(0,t)=u_x(1,t) for all t.
The initial condition for u(x,0) is sin(2*pi*x).
The initial condition for u_t(x,0) is 0.
The numerical solution will use a uniform periodic spatial grid with Nx=256 points on [0,1).
The grid points are x_j = j*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1, where dx=1/Nx.
The PDE will be reformulated as a first-order system in time by letting v = u_t, resulting in u_t = v and v_t = (q1**2)*u_xx - (q2**2)*(q1**4)*u.
Spatial derivatives will be computed using Fourier spectral derivatives (FFT).
Time integration will be performed using the RK4 method on the first-order system.
The time step dt will be 0.0005.
The simulation will run for Nt=2000 steps, reaching a final time T=1.0.
The solution u(x,T=1.0) will be computed on the periodic grid.
The results will be saved to "solution_molk_014.npz", containing the arrays x and u, and the scalars T, dx, Nx, q1, and q2.
A line plot of u(x,T) will be saved as "solution_molk_014.png".
The minimum, maximum, mean, and discrete L2 norm (sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx)) of u at T will be printed.
