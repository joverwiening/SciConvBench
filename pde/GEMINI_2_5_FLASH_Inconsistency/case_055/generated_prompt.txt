The PDE to be solved is u_tt - (1.0)^2*u_xx + (1.0)^2*u + (1.0)*u^3 = 0.
The spatial domain is x in [0,1) with periodic boundary conditions.
The temporal domain is t in [0,2.0].
The parameters are c = 1.0, m = 1.0, and lambda = 1.0.
The boundary conditions are periodic for u and u_x.
The initial condition for u(x,0) is 0.1*cos(2*pi*x).
The initial condition for u_t(x,0) is 0.
The spatial discretization will use a uniform periodic grid with Nx=512 points on [0,1).
Second-order central differences with wrap-around will be used for u_xx.
The time integration method is Leapfrog for u_tt.
The nonlinear term u^3 will be treated explicitly at time level n.
The time step dt is 0.0005, resulting in 4000 steps to reach T=2.0.
The initialization will use a Taylor start, including the terms -m^2*u0 - lambda*u0^3.
The solution u(x, T=2.0) will be computed on the grid.
The solution will be saved to "solution_agent_kg_08.npz" as a NumPy archive containing arrays x (1D) and u (1D), and scalar metadata T, dx, dt.
A line plot of u(x,T) will be saved as "solution_agent_kg_08.png".
The minimum value of u, the maximum value of u, and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at T will be printed.
