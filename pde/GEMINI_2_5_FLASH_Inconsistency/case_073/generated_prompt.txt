The PDE to solve is u_tt + (0.01)*u_xxxx = 0, which is the Euler–Bernoulli beam equation.
The spatial domain is x in [0,1] and the temporal domain is t in [0,1.0].
The parameter kappa is 0.01.
The boundary conditions are simply-supported at x=0 and x=1, meaning u(0,t)=0, u(1,t)=0, u_xx(0,t)=0, and u_xx(1,t)=0.
The initial condition for u is u(x,0)=sin(pi*x).
The initial condition for u_t is u_t(x,0)=0.
The reference exact solution for verification is u(x,t)=cos((pi^2)*sqrt(0.01)*t)*sin(pi*x).
The numerical method uses a sine-series spectral method for spatial discretization.
The sine-series spectral method exactly enforces the simply-supported boundary conditions u(0)=u(1)=0 and u_xx(0)=u_xx(1)=0.
Discretize u on the interior grid points x_i=i*dx for i=1..Nx-1, where dx=1/Nx.
The boundaries at i=0 and i=Nx have u=0.
Use the discrete sine transform (DST-I) to obtain modal coefficients û_n for n=1..Nx-1, corresponding to sin(n*pi*x).
In sine space, the PDE for each mode n becomes d^2 û_n / dt^2 + kappa*(n*pi)^4 * û_n = 0, with omega_n = (n*pi)^2*sqrt(kappa).
The time integration for the modal ODEs uses an exact per-mode update.
The exact per-mode update is û_n(t) = û_n(0)*cos(omega_n*t) + (v̂_n(0)/omega_n)*sin(omega_n*t), where v̂_n(0) is the DST of u_t(x,0).
Evaluate the solution at t = n*dt for n=0..Nt, or just at the final time T.
The grid uses Nx=800, resulting in dx=1/Nx.
The time step dt is 1e-4, with Nt=10000 steps, so the final time T=1.0 is hit exactly.
