The PDE to be solved is u_t = 0.05*u_xx - 1.0*u + sin(pi*x).
The spatial domain is x in [0,1] and the temporal domain is t in [0,1.0].
The diffusion coefficient D is 0.05.
The reaction coefficient k is 1.0.
Dirichlet boundary conditions are applied at x=0 and x=1, specifically u(0,t)=0 and u(1,t)=0.
The initial condition is u(x,0)=0.
Spatial discretization will use 2nd-order central differences for diffusion terms on a uniform grid.
Time integration will use a 1st-order deterministic IMEX scheme, with diffusion handled implicitly (backward Euler) and reaction handled explicitly (forward Euler).
The time integration step will solve (I - dt*D*L) u^{n+1} = u^n + dt * R(u^n) at each step, where L is the discrete Laplacian and R(u) is the reaction term.
A sparse direct solver will be used for each time step.
The grid will have Nx=300 spatial intervals, resulting in Nx+1 grid points.
The time step dt will be 0.001, for a total of 1000 steps to reach T=1.0.
The solution u(x, T=1.0) will be computed on the specified grid.
The computed solution will be saved to a file named "solution_agent_diffusion_reaction_06.npz".
The npz file will contain the 1D arrays x and u, and the scalar metadata T, dx, and dt.
A line plot of u(x,T) will be saved as "solution_agent_diffusion_reaction_06.png".
The minimum value of u, the maximum value of u, and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at T will be printed to the console.
