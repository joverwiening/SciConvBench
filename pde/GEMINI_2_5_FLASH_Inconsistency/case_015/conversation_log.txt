Q: Section 4 lists both Dirichlet and Periodic boundary conditions. Which type of boundary condition should be applied?
A: Apply Dirichlet boundary conditions: u(0,t)=0 and u(1,t)=0. The `boundary_handling` section explicitly states to enforce Dirichlet BC and use boundary values/ghosts rather than periodic wrap-around.
