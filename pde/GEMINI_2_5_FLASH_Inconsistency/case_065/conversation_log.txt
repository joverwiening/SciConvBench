Q: The boundary conditions specify both Dirichlet and periodic conditions on the same boundaries, which are contradictory. Which type of boundary condition should be applied?
A: The complete requirement specifies Dirichlet boundary conditions on all four edges: u(x,0)=0; u(x,1)=1; u(0,y)=0; u(1,y)=0. The mention of "Periodic on the spatial boundaries" is an error in the incomplete requirement and should be ignored.
