The PDE u_tt = q*u_xx will be solved numerically.
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions, and the temporal domain is t in [0, 1.0].
The parameter q is set to 1.0.
Initial conditions are u(x,0) = sin(2*pi*x) and u_t(x,0) = 0.
The numerical method will use a uniform periodic spatial grid with Nx=256 points on [0,1).
The grid points are defined as x_j = j*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1, with dx=1/Nx.
The PDE will be reformulated into a first-order system: u_t=v, v_t=q*u_xx.
Spatial discretization will use 2nd-order central differences for u_xx on the periodic grid.
Time integration will be performed using RK4 on the (u,v) system.
The time step will be dt=0.0005, with Nt=2000 steps, reaching a final time of T=1.0.
The numerical solution u(x,T=1.0) will be computed on the periodic grid.
The results will be saved to "solution_molk_019.npz", containing arrays x (1D length Nx), u (1D length Nx), and scalars T, dx, Nx, and q.
A line plot of u(x,T) will be saved as "solution_molk_019.png".
The minimum, maximum, mean, and discrete L2 norm (sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx)) of u at T will be printed.
