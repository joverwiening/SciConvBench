The task ID is molk_017.
Solve the 1D KdV-type equation numerically for the unknown field u(x,t).
The PDE is u_t + (q^2)*u_xxx + u*u_x = 0.
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions.
The temporal domain is t in [0, 0.2].
The parameter q is 0.1.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = cos(2*pi*x).
No closed-form reference solution is required.
Use a uniform periodic spatial grid with Nx=512 points on [0,1).
The grid points are x_j = j*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1, where dx=1/Nx.
Use Fourier spectral derivatives for u_x and u_xxx for spatial discretization.
Use the RK4 method for time integration.
The time step is dt=0.0001, resulting in Nt=2000 steps to reach the final time T=0.2 exactly.
Compute u(x,T=0.2) on the periodic grid.
Save "solution_molk_017.npz" containing the arrays x and u, and the scalars T, dx, Nx, and q.
Save a line plot of u(x,T) as "solution_molk_017.png".
Print the minimum, maximum, mean, and discrete L2 norm (sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx)) of u at T.
