Q: The boundary conditions are contradictory, as both Dirichlet and periodic conditions are specified. Which boundary conditions should be applied?
A: The boundary conditions are Dirichlet on x=0 (u(0,t)=1) and Dirichlet on x=1 (u(1,t)=0). Disregard the mention of periodic boundary conditions.
