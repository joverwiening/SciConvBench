The task is to numerically solve the 1D Klein-Gordon equation u_tt - c^2*u_xx + m^2*u = 0.
The parameters are c = 1.0 and m = 0.5.
The spatial domain is x in [0,1] and the temporal domain is t in [0,1.0].
The boundary conditions are Neumann at x=0 and x=1, specifically u_x(0,t)=0 and u_x(1,t)=0.
The initial conditions are u(x,0)=cos(pi*x) and u_t(x,0)=0.
The exact solution for verification is u(x,t)=cos(sqrt((1.0*pi)^2 + (0.5)^2)*t)*cos(pi*x).
The spatial discretization will use a uniform grid on [0,1] with Nx=400 intervals, resulting in Nx+1=401 grid points.
The spatial step size is dx = (1-0)/Nx = 1/400.
Second-order central differences will be used for the u_xx term.
The time integration will use a second-order central difference scheme (leapfrog) for u_tt.
The +m^2*u term will be handled explicitly at time level n in the update equation.
The time step dt will be chosen such that c*dt/dx ≤ 0.9.
Specifically, dt0 = 0.9*dx/c.
The number of time steps Nt will be ceil(T/dt0), where T=1.0.
The final time step dt will be T/Nt to ensure the simulation ends exactly at T=1.0.
The initialization for the leapfrog scheme will use a Taylor start from u(x,0) and u_t(x,0), incorporating the -m^2*u(x,0) term.
Boundary conditions will be handled using zero Neumann via ghost points (mirror values).
The simulation will compute u(x, T=1.0) on the defined grid.
The results will be saved to "solution_agent_kg_03.npz", containing 1D arrays for x and u, and scalar metadata T, dx, and dt.
A line plot of u(x,T) will be saved as "solution_agent_kg_03.png".
The minimum value of u, maximum value of u, and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at T will be printed to the console.
The maximum absolute error and the L2 error against the exact solution, evaluated on the same x grid at T, will also be computed and printed.
