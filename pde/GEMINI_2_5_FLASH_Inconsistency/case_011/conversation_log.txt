Q: There is a contradiction in the specified time integration method. Should the time integration be Explicit Forward Euler or Semi-implicit?
A: The time integration should be Semi-implicit: (u^{n+1}-u^n)/dt = ε^2*Δu^{n+1} + u^n - (u^n)^3. Solve a sparse linear system each step.
