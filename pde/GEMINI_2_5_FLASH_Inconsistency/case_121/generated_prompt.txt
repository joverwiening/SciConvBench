Solve the 1D reaction-diffusion PDE u_t = q1*u_xx + q2*u*(1-u).
The unknown field is u(x,t).
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions.
The temporal domain is t in [0, 1.0].
Parameters are q1 = 0.01 and q2 = 5.0.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = 0.25 + 0.1*sin(2*pi*x).
No closed-form reference solution is required.
Use a uniform periodic spatial grid with Nx=512 points on [0,1).
Grid points are x_j = j*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1 with dx=1/Nx.
Use 2nd-order central differences for the u_xx term.
Use IMEX Euler time integration, where the diffusion term is treated implicitly and the reaction term is treated explicitly.
Use a time step dt=0.001 with Nt=1000 steps to reach the final time T=1.0.
Compute u(x,T=1.0) on the periodic grid.
Save the solution to "solution_molk_020.npz" containing arrays: x (1D length Nx), u (1D length Nx), and scalars T, dx, Nx, q1, q2.
Save a line plot of u(x,T) as "solution_molk_020.png".
Print min(u), max(u), mean(u), and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at T.
