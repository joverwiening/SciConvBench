Q: What is the required time integration method? The specification lists two options: "IMEX Euler (diffusion implicit, reaction explicit)" OR "Strang splitting with diffusion via FFT and exact logistic reaction". Which one should be used?
A: Either method is acceptable - you can choose between IMEX Euler or Strang splitting. The complete requirement lists them as alternatives with "OR".
