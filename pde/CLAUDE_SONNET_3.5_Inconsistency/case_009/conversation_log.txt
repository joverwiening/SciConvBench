Q: What should be used for the boundary conditions? The prompt specifies both Neumann conditions (u_x(0,t)=0, u_x(1,t)=0) and Dirichlet conditions (u=0) at the boundaries, which are contradictory.
A: Use Neumann boundary conditions: u_x(0,t)=0 and u_x(1,t)=0. The complete requirement does not mention any Dirichlet conditions.
