Q: There appears to be a contradiction in the boundary conditions. The prompt specifies both:
- Dirichlet conditions (u(0,t)=0, u(1,t)=0)
- Periodic boundary conditions

Which boundary condition type should be used for this problem?
A: Use Dirichlet boundary conditions: u(0,t)=0 and u(1,t)=0. The mention of periodic boundaries in the grid convention section is just part of a general explanation and doesn't apply to this specific problem.
