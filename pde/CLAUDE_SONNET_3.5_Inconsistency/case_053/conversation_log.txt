Q: The final time T in the output requirements (T=1.1) conflicts with the domain specification which states t in [0,1.0]. Which final time should be used for the simulation?
A: The final time should be T=1.0, as specified in both the domain specification [0,1.0] and in the output requirements section 8 which states "Compute u(x,y,T=1.0)".
