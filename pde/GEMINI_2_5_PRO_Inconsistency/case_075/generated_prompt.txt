The PDE is u_xx + u_yy = -5*pi^2*sin(2*pi*x)*sin(pi*y).
The spatial domain is the unit square [0,1] x [0,1].
The boundary conditions are Dirichlet, with u=0 on all four boundaries.
The grid will have Nx=120 intervals in the x-direction and Ny=120 intervals in the y-direction.
This corresponds to Nx+1 by Ny+1 total grid points.
The grid spacing is dx = 1/Nx and dy = 1/Ny.
The grid coordinates are x_i = i*dx for i=0,...,Nx and y_j = j*dy for j=0,...,Ny.
Discretize the PDE using the standard second-order 5-point finite difference stencil for the Laplacian on the interior grid points.
Solve the resulting sparse linear system using a sparse direct solver.
The exact solution for verification is u(x,y) = sin(2*pi*x)*sin(pi*y).
Compute the numerical solution u on the (Ny+1) by (Nx+1) grid.
