The PDE to solve is u_t + ∂_x( 0.5*u^2 ) = nu * u_xx.

The numerical method is Strang splitting for each time step.
The first substep is a diffusion half-step of duration dt/2 solved in Fourier space.
The Fourier space update for diffusion is u_hat(k) <- u_hat(k) * exp(-nu*k^2*(dt/2)).
The wave numbers are k = 2*pi*fftfreq(Nx, d=dx).
The second substep is an advection full-step of duration dt.
The advection equation u_t + ∂_x(0.5*u^2)=0 is solved with a finite-volume method.
The finite-volume flux is the Rusanov (local Lax-Friedrichs) flux.
The flux function is f(u) = 0.5*u^2.
The Rusanov flux between u_left and u_right is F = 0.5*(f(u_left) + f(u_right)) - 0.5*alpha*(u_right - u_left).
The wave speed estimate for the Rusanov flux is alpha = max(abs(u_left), abs(u_right)).
The finite-volume update is u_new[j] = u[j] - (dt/dx)*(F_plus - F_minus), where F_plus and F_minus are the fluxes at the right and left interfaces of cell j.
The third substep is a diffusion half-step of duration dt/2, identical to the first substep.
The time step size is determined by a CFL number of 0.4.
An initial time step is calculated as dt0 = 0.4 * dx / 1.0.
The number of time steps is Nt = ceil(T/dt0).
The final time step is dt = T/Nt.
