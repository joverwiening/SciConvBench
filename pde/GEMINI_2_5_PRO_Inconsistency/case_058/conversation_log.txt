Q: The boundary conditions are specified as both Dirichlet and Periodic, but a boundary cannot be both simultaneously. Which boundary condition should I apply?
A: You should apply the Dirichlet boundary conditions on all four edges. The value of u on the boundary is given by the function u(x,y)=sinh(pi*x)*sin(pi*y)/sinh(pi).
