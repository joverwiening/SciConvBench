Q: The boundary conditions are specified as both Dirichlet on all four edges and periodic, which is a contradiction. Which set of boundary conditions should I use for the simulation?
A: Use the Dirichlet boundary conditions: u(x,0)=0; u(x,1)=sin(pi*x); u(0,y)=0; u(1,y)=0.
