The partial differential equation to solve is u_xx + u_yy = 0.
The spatial domain is the unit square defined by (x,y) in [0,1] x [0,1].
The grid will have Nx=100 intervals in the x-direction and Ny=100 intervals in the y-direction.
This corresponds to 101 grid points in each direction, including the boundaries.
The grid spacing is dx = 1/Nx and dy = 1/Ny.
The grid points are located at x_i = i*dx for i=0,...,Nx and y_j = j*dy for j=0,...,Ny.
The boundary conditions are of the Dirichlet type on all four edges.
The boundary condition on the bottom edge (y=0) is u(x,0) = 0.
The boundary condition on the top edge (y=1) is u(x,1) = sin(pi*x).
The boundary condition on the left edge (x=0) is u(0,y) = 0.
The boundary condition on the right edge (x=1) is u(1,y) = 0.
