The PDE to be solved is the 1D compressible Navier-Stokes system.
The conservative variables are U = [rho, m, E].
The primitive variables are density rho, velocity v, and pressure p.
The momentum is m = rho*v.
The total energy is E = p/(Gamma-1) + 0.5*rho*v^2.
The viscous stress is sigma = (zeta + 4/3*eta) * v_x.
The PDE in conservative form is rho_t + (m)_x = 0.
The second equation is m_t + (m^2/rho + p - sigma)_x = 0.
The third equation is E_t + ((E+p)*v - v*sigma)_x = 0.
The spatial domain is x in [-1, 1].
The temporal domain is t in [0, 0.2].
The ratio of specific heats is Gamma = 1.6666666666666667.
The dynamic viscosity is eta = 1e-08.
The bulk viscosity is zeta = 1e-08.
The boundary conditions are transmissive (outflow) at x=-1 and x=1.
The initial condition is a Riemann problem with a discontinuity at x=0.
For x < 0, the initial density is 1.0, velocity is 0.75, and pressure is 1.0.
For x >= 0, the initial density is 0.125, velocity is 0.0, and pressure is 0.1.
Use a uniform finite-volume grid with Nx=800 cells.
The cell centers are x_j = -1 + (j+0.5)*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1, where dx = 2/Nx.
Evolve the cell-averaged conservative variables U = [rho, m, E].
The equation of state is p = (Gamma-1) * (E - 0.5*m^2/rho).
Enforce positivity by clipping rho and p to a floor of 1e-12 if they fall below it.
The convective flux vector is F_conv(U) = [m, m^2/rho + p, (E+p)*v], where v = m/rho.
Use the Rusanov numerical flux for the convective part: F_hat = 0.5*(F_conv(U_L) + F_conv(U_R)) - 0.5*a*(U_R - U_L).
The interface wave speed is a = max(|v_L| + c_L, |v_R| + c_R), where the sound speed is c = sqrt(Gamma*p/rho).
Compute the velocity gradient v_x using a centered difference on cell-centered velocity values.
Compute the viscous stress sigma = (zeta + 4/3*eta)*v_x at cell centers.
Approximate sigma at cell interfaces by averaging the values from the two adjacent cells.
The viscous flux vector is F_visc = [0, -sigma, -v*sigma].
At interfaces, use the averaged sigma and an averaged velocity v for the viscous flux calculation.
The total numerical flux at an interface is F_total = F_hat + F_visc.
Use the two-stage, second-order Strong Stability Preserving Runge-Kutta (SSP-RK2) method for time integration.
The CFL number is 0.5.
The time step dt is the minimum of the convective and viscous time steps.
The convective time step is dt_conv = CFL * dx / max_j(|v_j| + c_j).
The viscous time step is dt_visc = 0.5 * dx^2 / max(zeta + 4/3*eta, 1e-30).
Adjust the final time step to land exactly on the final time T=0.2.
Implement the outflow boundary condition by setting ghost cell values equal to the nearest interior cell value.
Compute the solution rho(x,T), v(x,T), and p(x,T) at the final time T=0.2 on the cell centers.
