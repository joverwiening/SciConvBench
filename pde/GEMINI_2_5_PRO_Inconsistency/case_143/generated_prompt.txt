The PDE to solve is u_t + ∂_x( 0.5*u^2 ) = nu * u_xx.
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundaries.
The temporal domain is t in [0, 1.0].
The final time T for the simulation is 1.0.
The viscosity parameter nu is 0.01.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = sin(2*pi*x).
The boundary conditions are periodic.
Use a uniform periodic spatial grid with Nx=512 points.
The grid points are x_j = j*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1, where dx=1/Nx.
Use Strang splitting for the time integration.
Each time step dt consists of a half-step of diffusion, a full step of advection, and another half-step of diffusion.
The diffusion step u_t = nu * u_xx for a duration of dt/2 is solved exactly in Fourier space.
The Fourier space update for a half diffusion step is u_hat(k) <- u_hat(k) * exp(-nu*k^2*(dt/2)).
The wavenumbers are k = 2*pi*fftfreq(Nx, d=dx).
The advection step u_t + ∂_x(0.5*u^2)=0 for a duration of dt is solved with a finite-volume method using the Rusanov flux.
The flux F at the interface between u_left and u_right is 0.5*(f(u_left) + f(u_right)) - 0.5*alpha*(u_right - u_left), where f(u)=0.5*u^2 and alpha = max(abs(u_left), abs(u_right)).
The finite volume update for cell j is u_new[j] = u[j] - (dt/dx)*(F_plus - F_minus), where F_plus is the flux at the j+1/2 interface and F_minus is the flux at the j-1/2 interface.
The time step dt is fixed for the entire simulation.
First, calculate a temporary time step dt0 = CFL*dx/1.0 with CFL=0.4.
The number of time steps is Nt = ceil(T/dt0).
The final time step used is dt = T/Nt.
Compute the solution u(x,T) at the final time T=1.0.
