Solve the partial differential equation u_tt + q*sin(u) = u_xx.
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundaries.
The time domain is t in [0, 2.0].
The parameter q is 1.0.
The initial condition for u is u(x,0) = 0.1*sin(2*pi*x).
The initial condition for the time derivative of u is u_t(x,0) = 0.
Reformulate the PDE as a first-order system with v=u_t, resulting in u_t=v and v_t=u_xx - q*sin(u).
Use a uniform periodic spatial grid with Nx=512 points.
The spatial step size is dx = 1/Nx.
The grid points are x_j = j*dx for j=0, ..., Nx-1.
Discretize the u_xx term using a 2nd-order central difference scheme with periodic boundary conditions.
Integrate the system in time using the RK4 method.
Use a time step of dt = 0.0005.
Perform 4000 time steps to reach the final time T=2.0.
Compute the solution u(x,T) at the final time T=2.0.
