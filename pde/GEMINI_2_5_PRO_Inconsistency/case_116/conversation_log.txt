Q: The specification provides two conflicting definitions for the spatial step `dx`: `dx=1/(Nx-1)` from the introductory grid convention override, and `dx=1/Nx` from the numerical method requirements. Which definition of `dx` should be used for the calculation?
A: Use the definition from the 'Numerical method requirements' section: `dx=1/Nx`.
