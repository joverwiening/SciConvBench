The PDE to be solved is u_t = nu * u_xx + rho * u*(1 - u).
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundaries.
The time domain is t in [0, 1.0].
The parameter nu is 1.0.
The parameter rho is 1.0.
The boundary condition is periodic, such that u(0,t) = u(1,t).
The initial condition is u(x,0) = 0.25 + 0.1*sin(2*pi*x).
The spatial grid is a uniform periodic grid with Nx=512 points.
The spatial step size is dx = 1/Nx.
The grid points are defined as x_j = j*dx for j=0, ..., Nx-1.
The time step is fixed at dt=0.001.
The simulation will run for Nt=1000 steps to reach the final time T=1.0.
The numerical method is Strang splitting for each full time step dt.
The Strang splitting consists of a half-step of diffusion, a full-step of reaction, and a final half-step of diffusion.
The diffusion steps are solved for a duration of dt/2 exactly in Fourier space.
The wave numbers for the Fourier transform are k = 2*pi*fftfreq(Nx, d=dx).
The update rule in Fourier space for the diffusion step is u_hat(k) <- u_hat(k) * exp(-nu*k^2*(dt/2)).
The reaction step is solved for a duration of dt by applying the exact solution of the logistic ODE at each grid point.
The update rule for the reaction step is u <- (u*exp(rho*dt)) / (1 + u*(exp(rho*dt) - 1)).
The final solution u(x,T) at T=1.0 must be computed.
