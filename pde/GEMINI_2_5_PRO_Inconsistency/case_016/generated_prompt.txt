Solve the partial differential equation u_t + u*u_x = 0.02*u_xx + sin(2*pi*x)*cos(2*pi*t).
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1).
The time domain is t in [0, 1.0].
The initial condition is u(x,0) = 0.
The boundary condition is periodic on x.
The spatial domain is discretized with Nx=1000 uniform intervals, resulting in 1000 grid points.
The spatial step size is dx = 1/Nx.
The time step size is dt = 1e-4.
The simulation runs for Nt=10000 steps to reach the final time T=1.0.
Discretize the PDE in its conservative form u_t + (f(u))_x = nu*u_xx + S(x,t), with f(u)=0.5*u^2, nu=0.02, and S(x,t)=sin(2*pi*x)*cos(2*pi*t).
The convective term (f(u))_x is discretized using the Rusanov flux.
The Rusanov flux at interface i+1/2 is F_{i+1/2} = 0.5*(f(u_i)+f(u_{i+1})) - 0.5*alpha_{i+1/2}*(u_{i+1}-u_i).
The wave speed alpha_{i+1/2} is max(|u_i|, |u_{i+1}|).
The convective term is approximated as (F_{i+1/2}-F_{i-1/2})/dx.
The diffusive term u_xx is discretized using a second-order central difference, (u_{i+1}-2u_i+u_{i-1})/dx^2.
Time integration is performed using a first-order IMEX scheme.
The convective term and the source term are treated explicitly at time level n.
The diffusive term is treated implicitly at time level n+1.
The resulting sparse linear system is solved at each time step.
Periodic boundary conditions are handled with wrap-around indexing for all stencils.
