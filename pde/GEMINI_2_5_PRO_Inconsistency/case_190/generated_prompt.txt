Task ID is pdebench_reacdiff_nu5_rho2.
The PDE category is 1D Reaction–Diffusion with a logistic reaction and periodic boundary conditions.
The unknown field to solve for is u(x,t).
The governing PDE is u_t = nu * u_xx + rho * u*(1 - u).
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundaries.
The time domain is t in [0, 1.0].
The diffusion coefficient nu is 5.0.
The reaction coefficient rho is 2.0.
The boundary conditions are periodic.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = 0.25 + 0.1*sin(2*pi*x).
The spatial grid is a uniform periodic grid with Nx=512 points.
The grid points are defined as x_j = j*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1, where dx=1/Nx.
The time grid uses a fixed time step dt=0.001.
The simulation runs for Nt=1000 steps to reach the final time T=1.0.
The numerical method is Strang splitting for each time step dt.
The Strang splitting consists of three steps: a half-step of diffusion, a full-step of reaction, and another half-step of diffusion.
The diffusion step of duration h is solved exactly in Fourier space.
First, compute the forward Fourier transform of u to get u_hat(k).
The angular wavenumbers are k = 2*pi*fftfreq(Nx, d=dx).
Update the Fourier coefficients using u_hat(k) <- u_hat(k) * exp(-nu*k^2*h).
Compute the inverse Fourier transform of the updated u_hat(k) and take its real part to get the updated u.
The first and third steps of the splitting apply this diffusion solution with h=dt/2.
The reaction step of duration dt is solved exactly for the logistic ODE du/dt = rho*u*(1-u).
The exact update for the reaction step at each grid point is u <- (u*exp(rho*dt)) / (1 + u*(exp(rho*dt) - 1)).
The final solution u(x,T=1.0) on the spatial grid must be computed.
