The PDE to solve is u_t = nu * u_xx + rho * u*(1 - u).
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1).
The time domain is t in [0, 1.0].
The boundary conditions are periodic.
The parameter nu is 1.0.
The parameter rho is 2.0.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = 0.25 + 0.1*sin(2*pi*x).
The spatial grid has Nx=512 points.
The spatial step size is dx = 1/Nx.
The grid points are x_j = j*dx for j=0, ..., Nx-1.
The time step is dt = 0.001.
The number of time steps is Nt = 1000.
The numerical method is Strang splitting for each time step dt.
The first step of the splitting is a diffusion half-step of duration dt/2.
The diffusion step is solved in Fourier space.
The forward transform is u_hat = FFT(u).
The wave numbers are k = 2*pi*fftfreq(Nx, d=dx).
The update in Fourier space is u_hat(k) = u_hat(k) * exp(-nu*k^2*(dt/2)).
The inverse transform is u = IFFT(u_hat), taking the real part.
The second step of the splitting is a reaction full-step of duration dt.
The reaction step is solved exactly for the logistic ODE at each grid point.
The update for the reaction step is u = (u*exp(rho*dt)) / (1 + u*(exp(rho*dt) - 1)).
The third step of the splitting is another diffusion half-step of duration dt/2, identical to the first step.
