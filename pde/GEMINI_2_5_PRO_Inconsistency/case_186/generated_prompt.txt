The PDE to solve is u_t = nu * u_xx + rho * u*(1 - u).
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundaries.
The time domain is t in [0, 1.0].
The parameter nu is 2.0.
The parameter rho is 2.0.
The boundary conditions are periodic.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = 0.25 + 0.1*sin(2*pi*x).
The spatial grid is a uniform periodic grid with Nx=512 points.
The grid points are defined as x_j = j*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1, where dx=1/Nx.
The time step is fixed at dt=0.001.
The number of time steps is Nt=1000, resulting in a final time T=1.0.
The numerical method is Strang splitting for each time step dt.
The Strang splitting consists of three steps: a half-step of diffusion, a full-step of reaction, and another half-step of diffusion.
The diffusion half-step of duration dt/2 is solved exactly in Fourier space.
First, compute the Fourier transform of u: u_hat = FFT(u).
The angular wavenumbers are k = 2*pi*fftfreq(Nx, d=dx).
Update the Fourier coefficients: u_hat(k) is multiplied by exp(-nu*k^2*(dt/2)).
Compute the inverse Fourier transform of the updated u_hat and take its real part to get the updated u.
The reaction full-step of duration dt is solved exactly for the logistic ODE at each grid point.
The update formula for the reaction step is u_new = (u_old * exp(rho*dt)) / (1 + u_old * (exp(rho*dt) - 1)).
The second diffusion half-step is identical to the first one.
