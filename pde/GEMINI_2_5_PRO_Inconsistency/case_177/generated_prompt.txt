The PDE to solve is u_t = nu * u_xx + rho * u*(1 - u).
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions.
The time domain is t in [0, 1.0].
The parameter nu is 10.0.
The parameter rho is 10.0.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = 0.25 + 0.1*sin(2*pi*x).
The spatial grid is a uniform periodic grid with Nx=512 points.
The grid points are x_j = j/Nx for j=0,...,Nx-1.
The spatial step dx is 1/Nx.
The final time T is 1.0.
The time step dt is 0.001.
The number of time steps Nt is 1000.
The time integration method is Strang splitting.
Each time step dt consists of three sequential operations.
First, solve the diffusion equation for a half-step of dt/2 in Fourier space.
Second, solve the reaction equation for a full-step of dt exactly at each grid point.
Third, solve the diffusion equation for another half-step of dt/2 in Fourier space.
For the diffusion steps, the forward Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) is unscaled.
For the diffusion steps, the inverse Fast Fourier Transform (IFFT) is scaled by 1/Nx.
The wave numbers for the diffusion step are k = 2*pi*fftfreq(Nx, d=dx).
The update rule in Fourier space for a diffusion step of duration D is u_hat(k) becomes u_hat(k) * exp(-nu*k^2*D).
After the IFFT, take the real part of the solution.
The exact update rule for the reaction step is u becomes (u*exp(rho*dt)) / (1 + u*(exp(rho*dt) - 1)).
