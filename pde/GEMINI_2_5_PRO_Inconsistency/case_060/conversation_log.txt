Q: The requirement for periodic boundaries in the y-direction conflicts with the specified Dirichlet condition u(x,y)=y. Should I use the Dirichlet condition for the y-direction?
A: Yes, use the Dirichlet condition u(x,y)=y on all four boundaries. Please disregard the mention of periodic boundaries.
