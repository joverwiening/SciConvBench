Discretize space with a Fourier pseudo-spectral method on [0, 2π) using Nx = 1024 grid points with no duplicated endpoint.

Compute spatial derivatives u_x, u_xx, and u_xxxx via FFT using Fourier wavenumbers consistent with the periodic grid.
