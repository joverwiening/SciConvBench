Q: What is the initial condition u(x,0) as an explicit function of x?
A: The initial condition is:
\[
u(x,0) = x(1-x).
\]

Q: What boundary conditions should be applied at x=0 and x=1 (e.g., Dirichlet u=…, Neumann u_x=…, or periodic)?
A: Apply **Dirichlet boundary conditions** at both ends:

- At **x = 0**: \(u(0,t) = 0\)
- At **x = 1**: \(u(1,t) = 0\)
