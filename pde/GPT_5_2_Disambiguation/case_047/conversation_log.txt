Q: What initial condition u(x,y,0) should be used on the interior (and does it match the Dirichlet boundary values u=x+y on all four edges)?
A: Use the initial condition **u(x,y,0) = x + y everywhere on the grid**, including all interior points. Yes—this **matches the Dirichlet boundary condition** \(u(x,y,t)=x+y\) on all four edges for all \(t\).
