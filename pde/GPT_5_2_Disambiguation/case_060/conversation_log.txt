Q: What boundary conditions should be imposed for u on the edges x=0, x=1, y=0, and y=1?
A: Impose Dirichlet boundary conditions on all four edges, matching the exact solution \(u(x,y)=y\):

- \(u(0,y)=y\) for \(0\le y\le 1\)
- \(u(1,y)=y\) for \(0\le y\le 1\)
- \(u(x,0)=0\) for \(0\le x\le 1\)
- \(u(x,1)=1\) for \(0\le x\le 1\)
