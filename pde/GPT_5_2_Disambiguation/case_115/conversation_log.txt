Q: What time-stepping method should be used to advance the second-order-in-time PDE (e.g., explicit central difference/leapfrog, Newmark, or convert to first-order system and use RK4)?
A: Convert the PDE to a **first-order system in time** by introducing \(v=u_t\):
- \(u_t = v\)
- \(v_t = (q1^2)\,u_{xx} - (q2^2)(q1^4)\,u\)

Then advance \((u,v)\) using **RK4** in time.
