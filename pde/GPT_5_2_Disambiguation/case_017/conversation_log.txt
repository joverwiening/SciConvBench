Q: What boundary conditions should be applied at x=0 and x=1 (e.g., Dirichlet u=0, Neumann u_x=0, or periodic)?
A: Apply **Dirichlet** boundary conditions at both ends:

- \(u(0,t)=0\)
- \(u(1,t)=0\)

for all \(t \in [0,0.5]\).
