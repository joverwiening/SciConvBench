Q: What numerical flux (Riemann solver) should be used for the convective terms (e.g., Rusanov/LLF, HLL, HLLC, Roe)?
A: Use the **Rusanov (local Lax–Friedrichs / LLF)** numerical flux for the **inviscid Euler (convective) part**:

\[
\hat F = \tfrac12\left(F_{\text{conv}}(U_L)+F_{\text{conv}}(U_R)\right) - \tfrac12 a (U_R-U_L),
\]
with
\[
F_{\text{conv}}(U) = \begin{bmatrix} m \\ m^2/\rho + p \\ (E+p)v \end{bmatrix},\quad v=m/\rho,
\]
and interface wave speed
\[
a=\max\left(|v_L|+c_L,\ |v_R|+c_R\right),\quad c=\sqrt{\Gamma p/\rho}.
\]

Q: How should the viscous term be discretized in space (e.g., compute v_x at faces with a centered difference and include sigma in the momentum/energy flux, or use another specific finite-volume viscous discretization)?
A: Discretize viscosity exactly as follows:

- Compute **cell-centered** velocity \(v_j = m_j/\rho_j\).
- Compute \(v_x\) at **cell centers** with a **centered difference**:
  \[
  (v_x)_j = \frac{v_{j+1}-v_{j-1}}{2\,dx}
  \]
  (use outflow ghost cells so the stencil is defined at boundaries).
- Compute **cell-centered** stress:
  \[
  \sigma_j = \left(\zeta+\frac{4}{3}\eta\right)(v_x)_j
  \]
- Approximate **interface** stress by averaging neighboring cell-centered values:
  \[
  \sigma_{j+1/2} = \tfrac12(\sigma_j+\sigma_{j+1})
  \]
- Use **interface-averaged** velocity:
  \[
  v_{j+1/2} = \tfrac12(v_j+v_{j+1})
  \]
- Add viscous contributions to the **momentum and energy fluxes** at interfaces:
  \[
  F_{\text{visc},\,j+1/2} = \begin{bmatrix}
  0\\
  -\sigma_{j+1/2}\\
  -v_{j+1/2}\,\sigma_{j+1/2}
  \end{bmatrix}
  \]
- Total interface flux:
  \[
  F_{\text{total}} = \hat F_{\text{Rusanov}} + F_{\text{visc}}
  \]
