Q: What is the spatial domain for x (e.g., [0,1) to match the periodic boundary condition u(0,t)=u(1,t))?
A: Use the spatial domain **\(x \in [0, 1)\)** with periodic boundary conditions (so \(u(0,t)=u(1,t)\)). The grid is **Nx = 512** periodic points with **\(x_j = j\,dx\)**, **\(dx = 1/Nx\)**, for **\(j=0,\dots, Nx-1\)**.
