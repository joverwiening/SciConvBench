Set u0[0]=u0[Nx]=0 and u1[0]=u1[Nx]=0 to enforce Dirichlet boundaries.
Time-march from t=0 to t=T using the leapfrog recurrence, maintaining arrays u_prev (time level n-1), u_curr (time level n), and u_next (time level n+1).
