Q: What are the Dirichlet boundary values for u on the entire boundary of the square (i.e., u(x,0), u(x,1), u(0,y), u(1,y))?
A: Use Dirichlet values from the analytic solution on all four edges:
- \(u(x,0)=\sin(\pi x)\,\sinh(0)/\sinh(\pi)=0\)
- \(u(x,1)=\sin(\pi x)\,\sinh(\pi)/\sinh(\pi)=\sin(\pi x)\)
- \(u(0,y)=\sin(0)\,\sinh(\pi y)/\sinh(\pi)=0\)
- \(u(1,y)=\sin(\pi)\,\sinh(\pi y)/\sinh(\pi)=0\)
