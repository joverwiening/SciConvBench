Q: What boundary conditions should be used on the half-open periodic domain [0,1)×[0,1) (e.g., periodic in both x and y)?
A: Use **periodic boundary conditions in both x and y**. Implement with **periodic wrap-around indexing** on the half-open domain **[0,1)×[0,1)**, i.e., **do not duplicate endpoints**.
