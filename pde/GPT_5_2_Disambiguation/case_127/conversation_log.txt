Q: For the 1st-order upwind discretization of ∂x(u), should the upwind direction be chosen based on the sign of q1 (i.e., for q1>0 use (u_j−u_{j−1})/dx, for q1<0 use (u_{j+1}−u_j)/dx)?
A: Yes. Choose the upwind stencil based on the sign of the advection speed \(q_1\):

- If \(q_1>0\): \(\partial_x u \approx (u_j - u_{j-1})/dx\)
- If \(q_1<0\): \(\partial_x u \approx (u_{j+1} - u_j)/dx\)

For this task \(q_1=1.0>0\), so use the backward difference \((u_j-u_{j-1})/dx\) with periodic indexing.
