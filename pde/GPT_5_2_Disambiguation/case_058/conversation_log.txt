Q: What Dirichlet boundary values should be imposed on u along the edges of the square (x=0, x=1, y=0, y=1)?
A: Impose Dirichlet values on all four edges equal to the exact solution
\[
u(x,y)=\frac{\sinh(\pi x)\,\sin(\pi y)}{\sinh(\pi)}.
\]

So:
- On \(x=0\): \(u(0,y)=\dfrac{\sinh(0)\sin(\pi y)}{\sinh(\pi)}=0\).
- On \(x=1\): \(u(1,y)=\dfrac{\sinh(\pi)\sin(\pi y)}{\sinh(\pi)}=\sin(\pi y)\).
- On \(y=0\): \(u(x,0)=\dfrac{\sinh(\pi x)\sin(0)}{\sinh(\pi)}=0\).
- On \(y=1\): \(u(x,1)=\dfrac{\sinh(\pi x)\sin(\pi)}{\sinh(\pi)}=0\).
