Q: What is the exact expression for the forcing term f(x,y) that makes u(x,y)=sin(pi*x)*sin(pi*y) satisfy u_xx + u_yy + k^2*u = f(x,y) with k=5.0?
A: \(f(x,y) = \big(k^2 - 2\pi^2\big)\,\sin(\pi x)\,\sin(\pi y)\) with \(k=5.0\), i.e.
\[
f(x,y) = \big(25 - 2\pi^2\big)\sin(\pi x)\sin(\pi y).
\]
