Solve the 1D reaction–diffusion PDE u_t = nu*u_xx + rho*u*(1-u) for x in [0,1) with periodic boundary conditions.
Use parameters nu=1.0 and rho=1.0.
Use a uniform periodic grid with Nx=512 points, x_j=j*dx for j=0..511, dx=1/512.
Use initial condition u(x,0)=0.25+0.1*sin(2*pi*x) evaluated at the grid points (radians).
Use a fixed time step dt=0.001 for Nt=1000 steps so final time T=Nt*dt=1.0 is reached exactly.
Advance one full time step with Strang splitting: diffusion half-step, reaction full-step, diffusion half-step.
Implement each diffusion half-step of duration dt/2 by FFT: u_hat=FFT(u); k=2*pi*fftfreq(Nx,d=dx); u_hat=u_hat*exp(-nu*k^2*(dt/2)); u=real(IFFT(u_hat)).
Implement the reaction full-step of duration dt pointwise using the exact logistic update u=(u*exp(rho*dt))/(1+u*(exp(rho*dt)-1)).
After Nt steps, compute u at final time T on the grid.
Save an NPZ file named solution_pdebench_reacdiff_nu1_rho1.npz containing arrays x (length Nx) and u (length Nx) and scalars nu, rho, T, dx, dt, Nx, Nt.
Save a line plot of u versus x at time T as solution_pdebench_reacdiff_nu1_rho1.png.
Print min(u), max(u), mean(u), and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at time T.
