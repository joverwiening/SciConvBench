Q: What are the Dirichlet boundary values for u on x=0, x=1, y=0, and y=1 (as explicit functions of y,t or x,t, or constants)?
A: Dirichlet boundary conditions are zero on all edges (constant in space and time):

- \(u(0,y,t)=0\)
- \(u(1,y,t)=0\)
- \(u(x,0,t)=0\)
- \(u(x,1,t)=0\)

for all \(x,y \in [0,1]\) and \(t \in [0,0.5]\).
