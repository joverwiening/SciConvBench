Solve u_t = 0.05*u_xx - 1.0*u + sin(pi*x) for x in [0,1] and t in [0,1.0] with u(0,t)=0 and u(1,t)=0.
Use the initial condition u(x,0)=0 for all x in [0,1].
Use a uniform grid with Nx=300 intervals, so dx=(1-0)/300 and x_i=i*dx for i=0..300.
Represent u as a 1D array of length 301 corresponding to x_0..x_300.
Impose Dirichlet boundary conditions by setting u[0]=0 and u[-1]=0 at all times and excluding them from the linear solve.
Discretize u_xx on interior points i=1..299 with 2nd-order central differences: (u[i-1]-2*u[i]+u[i+1])/dx^2.
Define the reaction term R(u,x) = -1.0*u + sin(pi*x) evaluated pointwise on the grid.
Use IMEX Euler time stepping with dt=0.001 for 1000 steps to reach T=1.0.
At each step n, form the right-hand side on interior points: rhs = u_int^n + dt*R(u^n,x)_int.
Form the implicit diffusion matrix A = I - dt*D*L where D=0.05 and L is the interior-point Laplacian with Dirichlet treatment, i.e., tridiagonal with main diagonal (-2/dx^2) and off-diagonals (1/dx^2).
Solve A * u_int^{n+1} = rhs using a sparse direct solver each step.
After solving, assemble u^{n+1} with boundary values u[0]=0 and u[-1]=0.
After 1000 steps, treat the result as u(x,T=1.0) on the grid.
Compute and print min(u), max(u), and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at T=1.0 using all grid points.
