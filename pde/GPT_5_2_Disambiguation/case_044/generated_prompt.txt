Discretize space with a uniform grid on [0,1] using Nx=400 uniform intervals, giving Nx+1=401 grid points.
Define dx = (1-0)/Nx and x_i = 0 + i*dx for i=0..Nx.
Approximate u_xx at interior points with 2nd-order central differences.
Integrate in time with Crank–Nicolson applied to the diffusion term.

Enforce Dirichlet boundary conditions by fixing the boundary node values at every time step.
At T=0.2, compute the numerical solution u(x,T) on the grid.
