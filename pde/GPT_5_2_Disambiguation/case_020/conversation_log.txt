Q: What is the initial condition u(x,y,t=0) on [0,1]×[0,1]?
A: The initial condition is:
\[
u(x,y,0)=\sin(\pi x)\,\sin(\pi y), \quad (x,y)\in[0,1]\times[0,1].
\]

Q: What boundary condition should be applied on all four edges of the domain (e.g., Dirichlet u=0, Neumann no-flux (D∇u)·n=0, or periodic)?
A: Apply **Dirichlet boundary conditions on all four edges**:  
\[
u(x,y,t)=0 \quad \text{for } x=0,\, x=1,\, y=0,\, y=1 \text{ (for all } t\in[0,0.1]\text{)}.
\]
