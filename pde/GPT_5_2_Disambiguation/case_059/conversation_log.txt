Q: What Dirichlet boundary condition should be imposed for u on the entire boundary of [0,1]×[0,1] (e.g., u=x on all four sides to match the exact solution)?
A: Impose Dirichlet boundary conditions on all four edges matching the exact solution \(u(x,y)=x\):

- \(u(0,y)=0\) for \(y\in[0,1]\)
- \(u(1,y)=1\) for \(y\in[0,1]\)
- \(u(x,0)=x\) for \(x\in[0,1]\)
- \(u(x,1)=x\) for \(x\in[0,1]\)
