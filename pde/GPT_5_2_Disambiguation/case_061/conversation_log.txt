Q: What Dirichlet boundary conditions should be imposed on u along the edges of the square (x=0, x=1, y=0, y=1)?
A: Impose Dirichlet boundary conditions on all four edges matching the exact solution \(u(x,y)=x\,y\):

- On \(x=0\): \(u(0,y)=0\)
- On \(x=1\): \(u(1,y)=y\)
- On \(y=0\): \(u(x,0)=0\)
- On \(y=1\): \(u(x,1)=x\)
