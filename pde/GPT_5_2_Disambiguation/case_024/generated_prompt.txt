Solve u_t = 0.05 u_xx + 2.0 u on x in [0,1] and t in [0,0.3].
Use Dirichlet boundary conditions u(0,t)=0 and u(1,t)=0 for all t.
Use initial condition u(x,0)=sin(2*pi*x) on the spatial grid.
Use a uniform grid with Nx=250 intervals, dx=(1-0)/Nx, and grid points x_i=0+i*dx for i=0..Nx.
Use time step dt=0.0005 and advance for 600 steps to reach T=0.3.
Discretize u_xx with 2nd-order central differences on interior points i=1..Nx-1.
Impose Dirichlet boundary values by setting u_0^n=0 and u_Nx^n=0 at every time level.
Define the discrete Laplacian operator L acting on the interior unknown vector u_interior as (u_{i-1}-2u_i+u_{i+1})/dx^2.
Use IMEX Euler: treat diffusion implicitly and reaction explicitly via (I - dt*D*L) u_interior^{n+1} = u_interior^n + dt*(lambda*u_interior^n) with D=0.05 and lambda=2.0.
At each time step, assemble the sparse tridiagonal matrix A = I - dt*D*L for the interior unknowns and solve A u_interior^{n+1} = rhs using a sparse direct solver.
After the final step, form the full solution vector u at T by combining boundary values and interior values.
Compute and print min(u), max(u), and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at T over all grid points.
Evaluate the reference solution u_exact(x,T)=exp((2.0 - 0.05*(2*pi)^2)*T)*sin(2*pi*x) on the same x grid.
Compute and print the max absolute error max(|u-u_exact|) and the discrete L2 error sqrt(sum((u-u_exact)^2)*dx).
