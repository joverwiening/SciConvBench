Q: What boundary conditions should be used on the half-open periodic domain [0,1)×[0,1) (i.e., periodic in x and y)?
A: Use **periodic boundary conditions in both x and y** with **wrap-around indexing** on the half-open grid \([0,1)\times[0,1)\):

- \(u(x+1,y,t)=u(x,y,t)\)
- \(u(x,y+1,t)=u(x,y,t)\)

In code, when an index goes past an edge, wrap it modulo \(N_x\) or \(N_y\) (no duplicated endpoints).
