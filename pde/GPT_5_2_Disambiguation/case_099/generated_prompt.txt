Implement a 1D wave-equation solver for u_tt = c^2 u_xx with c=1.0 on x in [0,1] and t in [0,1.0].

Use a uniform grid with Nx=800 intervals and Nx+1=801 grid points.

After each update, enforce boundary nodes u^{n+1}[0]=0 and u^{n+1}[-1]=0.
After Nt steps, treat u^Nt as the numerical solution at T=1.0.
Compute and print at T: min(u), max(u), and discrete L2 norm defined as sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) using the final u array.
