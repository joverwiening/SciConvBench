Q: What boundary conditions should be imposed on u along the edges of [0,1]×[0,1] (e.g., Dirichlet u=x^2−y^2 on all boundaries)?
A: Impose **Dirichlet boundary conditions on all four edges** of the square, setting the boundary values to match the exact solution:
\[
u(x,y)=x^2-y^2 \quad \text{for } (x,y)\in \partial([0,1]\times[0,1]).
\]
That is, apply this on \(x=0\), \(x=1\), \(y=0\), and \(y=1\).
