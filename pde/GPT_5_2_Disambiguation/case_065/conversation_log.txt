Q: What are the Dirichlet boundary conditions for u(x,y) on the edges x=0, x=1, y=0, and y=1?
A: Dirichlet boundary conditions on all four edges are:
- \(u(x,0)=0\) (bottom edge)
- \(u(x,1)=1\) (top edge)
- \(u(0,y)=0\) (left edge)
- \(u(1,y)=0\) (right edge)

=== 2026-03-09 17:52:57 ===
Q: At the corner points where these Dirichlet conditions conflict (specifically (0,1) and (1,1)), what single value should u take at each corner?
A: Use the top-edge value at both top corners:

- \(u(0,1) = 1\)
- \(u(1,1) = 1\)
