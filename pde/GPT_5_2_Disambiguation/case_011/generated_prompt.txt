Solve u_t = (0.02)^2*(u_xx + u_yy) + u - u^3 on (x,y) ∈ [0,1]×[0,1], t ∈ [0,0.1].
Use epsilon = 0.02.
Use a uniform grid with Nx=Ny=300 interpreted as 300 uniform intervals per direction, giving 301×301 grid points including endpoints.
Set dx = (1-0)/300 and dy = (1-0)/300.
Define x_i = i*dx for i=0..300 and y_j = j*dy for j=0..300.
Initialize u(x,y,0) by r = sqrt((x-0.5)^2 + (y-0.5)^2) and u = tanh((0.25 - r)/(sqrt(2)*0.02)) evaluated at grid points.
Enforce homogeneous Dirichlet boundary conditions for all times by setting u=0 at all boundary nodes where x=0, x=1, y=0, or y=1 at every time step.
Discretize the Laplacian with the standard 5-point stencil on the interior nodes: (u_{i+1,j}-2u_{i,j}+u_{i-1,j})/dx^2 + (u_{i,j+1}-2u_{i,j}+u_{i,j-1})/dy^2.
Use time step dt = 0.0001 and advance for 1000 steps to reach T = 0.1.
Use the semi-implicit scheme (u^{n+1}-u^n)/dt = ε^2*Δu^{n+1} + u^n - (u^n)^3 on interior nodes.
At each step, form and solve the sparse linear system (I - dt*ε^2*L) u^{n+1} = u^n + dt*(u^n - (u^n)^3) for the interior unknowns, where L is the discrete Laplacian operator with Dirichlet boundaries.
After solving for interior nodes, overwrite boundary nodes with 0 to enforce Dirichlet conditions.
At T=0.1, compute and print min(u), max(u), and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx*dy) over all grid points.
