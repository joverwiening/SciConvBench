Solve the 1D heat equation u_t = 0.1 u_xx for x in [0,1] and t in [0,0.4].
Use the initial condition u(x,0) = cos(pi*x).
Use zero Neumann boundary conditions implemented with ghost points: u[-1]=u[1] at x=0 and u[Nx+1]=u[Nx-1] at x=1.
Use a uniform grid with Nx=200 intervals, dx=1/Nx, and grid points x_i=i*dx for i=0..Nx.
Discretize u_xx with 2nd-order central differences in the interior and apply the Neumann BC through the ghost-point relations when forming/applying the Laplacian.
Advance in time with Crank–Nicolson using dt=0.001 for 400 steps to reach T=0.4.
Compute the numerical solution u(x,T) on the grid at T=0.4.
Evaluate the reference solution u_exact(x,T)=exp(-0.1*pi^2*T)*cos(pi*x) on the same grid for verification.
Compute and print at T: min(u), max(u), and discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx).
Compute and print the max absolute error max(|u-u_exact|) and the L2 error sqrt(sum((u-u_exact)^2)*dx).
Save an NPZ file named solution_agent_heat_04.npz containing arrays x (1D) and u (1D) and scalars T, dx, dt.
