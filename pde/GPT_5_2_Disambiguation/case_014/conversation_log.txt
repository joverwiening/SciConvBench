Q: What boundary condition should be enforced at x=0 and x=1 (e.g., periodic)?
A: Use **periodic boundary conditions** in \(x\) on \([0,1)\): enforce wrap-around so \(u(0,t)=u(1,t)\) (with \(x=1\) not duplicated on the grid), and apply periodic indexing for both the convection fluxes and the diffusion stencil.
