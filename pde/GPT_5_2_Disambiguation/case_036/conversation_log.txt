Q: What is the Dirichlet boundary condition at x=0 and x=1 (i.e., the values of u(0,t) and u(1,t) for all t)?
A: - At **x = 0**: \(u(0,t) = \sin(2\pi t)\) for all \(t \in [0,0.5]\).
- At **x = 1**: \(u(1,t) = 0\) for all \(t \in [0,0.5]\).

Q: What is the initial condition u(x,0) for x in [0,1]?
A: The initial condition is:
- \(u(x,0) = 0\) for all \(x \in [0,1]\).
