Q: What Dirichlet boundary values should be imposed on u along the four edges x=0, x=1, y=0, and y=1?
A: Impose Dirichlet BCs on all four edges:

- Bottom edge \(y=0\): \(u(x,0)=0\)
- Top edge \(y=1\): \(u(x,1)=\sin(2\pi x)\)
- Left edge \(x=0\): \(u(0,y)=0\)
- Right edge \(x=1\): \(u(1,y)=0\)
