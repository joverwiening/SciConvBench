Q: What boundary condition should be used at x=0 and x=1 (e.g., periodic)?
A: Use **periodic boundary conditions** in \(x\): \(u(0,t)=u(1,t)\) with periodic wrap-around indexing for both the convection fluxes and the diffusion stencil (domain \([0,1)\) with no duplicated endpoint at \(x=1\)).
