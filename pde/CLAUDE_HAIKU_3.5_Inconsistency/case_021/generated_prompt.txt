Numerical Solution Specification for Diffusion PDE (agent_diffusion_05):

Solve the 1D diffusion PDE u_t = 0.1 * u_xx with the following detailed requirements:

Spatial Domain: x in [0,1]
Temporal Domain: t in [0,0.5]
Diffusion Coefficient: D = 0.1

Numerical Method Details:
- Spatial Discretization: Uniform grid with 300 intervals (Nx=300)
- Spatial Derivative: 2nd-order central differences for u_xx
- Boundary Conditions: Robin type at x=0 and x=1

- Time Integration: Crank-Nicolson method
- Time Step: dt = 0.001 (600 total time steps)
- Initial Condition: u(x,0) = cos(π*x)
