Specification for 1D Viscous Burgers Equation Numerical Solution:

Solve the 1D viscous Burgers equation u_t + ∂_x(0.5*u^2) = nu * u_xx with the following specifications:

PDE Details:
- Equation: u_t + ∂_x(0.5*u^2) = 0.2 * u_xx
- Domain: x in [0, 1) periodic, t in [0, 1.0]
- Initial Condition: u(x,0) = sin(2*pi*x)
- Viscosity Parameter: nu = 0.2

Numerical Method:
- Spatial Grid: Uniform periodic grid with Nx = 512 points
- Grid Points: x_j = j*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1 with dx = 1/Nx
- Time Stepping: Strang Splitting Method
  1. Diffusion half-step in Fourier space (dt/2)
  2. Advection full-step using Rusanov (local Lax-Friedrichs) flux
  3. Diffusion half-step in Fourier space (dt/2)
- Time Step Selection: 
  - CFL = 0.4
  - dt0 = CFL*dx/1.0
  - Nt = ceil(T/dt0)
  - Final dt adjusted to hit T exactly

  * T (final time)

  * dt (time step)

  * Nt (number of time steps)
