Use explicit forward Euler time integration in the context of Strang operator splitting.

Transform back with IFFT and take the real part to get u.  
Second, perform a full reaction step of duration dt by solving the logistic ODE pointwise:  
For each grid point x_j, update u using the exact solution: u <- (u * exp(rho*dt)) / (1 + u*(exp(rho*dt) - 1)).  
Third, perform another diffusion half-step identical to the first.
