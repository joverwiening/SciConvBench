Q: The boundary conditions are specified as both Dirichlet (u = 0 on the boundary) and periodic. These two types of boundary conditions are contradictory unless the solution is zero everywhere, which may not be intended. 

Which boundary condition should be applied: Dirichlet or periodic?
A: Apply Dirichlet boundary conditions: u = 0 on the entire boundary. Ignore the periodic condition; it is not part of the correct specification.
