Solve the 1D reaction–diffusion equation u_t = nu * u_xx + rho * u*(1 - u) on the spatial domain x in [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions and time domain t in [0, 1.0].

Use the initial condition: u(x,0) = 0.25 + 0.1*sin(2*pi*x).

Discretize the time domain using a fixed time step dt = 0.001, taking Nt = 1000 steps to reach T = 1.0 exactly.  
Use explicit forward Euler time integration in the context of Strang operator splitting as follows for each time step:  
First, perform a half-step of diffusion (duration dt/2) by solving in Fourier space:  
  Compute the FFT of u to get u_hat.  
  Define the wavenumbers k = 2*pi*fftfreq(Nx, d=dx).  
  Update u_hat(k) = u_hat(k) * exp(-nu * k^2 * dt/2).  
  Transform back with IFFT and take the real part to get u.  
Then, perform a full-step of reaction (duration dt) using the exact solution of the logistic ODE at each grid point:  
  Update u = (u * exp(rho*dt)) / (1 + u * (exp(rho*dt) - 1)).  
Finally, perform another half-step of diffusion (same as the first half-step).
