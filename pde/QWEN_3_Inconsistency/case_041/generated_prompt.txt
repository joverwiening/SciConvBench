Use the Implicit Euler method for time integration.

Evaluate the reference solution \( u_{\text{exact}}(x,y,T) = \exp(-2 \cdot 0.1 \cdot \pi^2 \cdot T) \cdot \cos(\pi x) \cdot \cos(\pi y) \) on the same grid.
