Solve the 1D viscous Burgers equation with periodic boundary conditions on the spatial domain [0, 1) and time interval [0, 1.0].

For the advection step, solve the inviscid Burgers equation using a finite-volume method with Rusanov (local Lax-Friedrichs) flux: f(u) = 0.5*u^2.

Update each cell value using u_new[j] = u[j] - (dt/dx)*(F_plus - F_minus), where F_plus is the flux at the right interface and F_minus at the left interface (using periodic indexing).
