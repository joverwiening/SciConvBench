Use the spatial domain \( (x,y) \in [0,1] \times [0,1] \) and temporal domain \( t \in [0, 0.1] \).

\( (\nabla \cdot (D \nabla u))_{i,j} \approx (1/dx^2) \cdot [ D_{i+1/2,j} \cdot (u_{i+1,j} - u_{i,j}) - D_{i-1/2,j} \cdot (u_{i,j} - u_{i-1,j}) ] \)

Use Implicit Euler time integration: \( (I - dt \cdot L) u^{n+1} = u^n \), where \( L \) is the discrete diffusion operator.
