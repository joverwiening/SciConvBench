The PDE to solve is u_tt - (0.5)^2 * u_xx + (2.0)^2 * u = 0.  
The spatial domain is x ∈ [0, 1] and the time domain is t ∈ [0, 1.0].

The time integration uses the leapfrog method with second-order central difference for u_tt.  
The term (2.0)^2 * u is treated explicitly at the current time level in the update.  
The initial time step estimate is dt0 = 0.9 * dx / c.  
The number of time steps is Nt = ceil(1.0 / dt0).  
The final time step size is dt = 1.0 / Nt to ensure the final time is reached exactly.
