Q: Should the time integration be explicit forward Euler as initially stated, or the semi‑implicit scheme described later?
A: Use the semi‑implicit scheme:  

\[
\frac{u^{n+1}-u^{n}}{\Delta t}= \varepsilon^{2}\,\Delta u^{n+1}+u^{n}-(u^{n})^{3},
\]

solving the resulting sparse linear system at each time step.
