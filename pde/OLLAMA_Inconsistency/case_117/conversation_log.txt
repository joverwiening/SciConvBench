Q: Should the periodic spatial grid on \([0,1)\) use \(dx = 1/Nx\) with \(Nx\) points (no duplicated endpoint), or \(dx = 1/(Nx-1)\) with \(Nx\) points including both endpoints?
A: Use the periodic grid with \(dx = 1/Nx\) and \(Nx\) points \(x_j = j\,dx\) for \(j=0,\dots,Nx-1\) (no duplicated endpoint).
