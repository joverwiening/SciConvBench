The exact solution for verification is u(x,y,t) = x + y for all t because the Laplacian of x + y is zero.  
Discretize the domain with a uniform grid of Nx = Ny = 40 intervals, giving Nx+1 = 41 grid points in each direction; compute dx = (1‑0)/40 and dy = (1‑0)/40, and define grid points x_i = i*dx, y_j = j*dy for i,j = 0,…,40.

After the final step compute the solution u(x,y,T) on the full 2‑D grid.
