Q: Should the spatial grid be defined as a periodic half‑open interval with dx = 1/Nx (points x_j = j*dx for j = 0…Nx‑1), or as a closed interval including both endpoints with dx = 1/(Nx‑1)?
A: Use a periodic half‑open interval [0, 1) with dx = 1/Nx, i.e. x_j = j·dx for j = 0,…,Nx‑1.
