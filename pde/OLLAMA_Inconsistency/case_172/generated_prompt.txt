Solve the PDE u_t = 0.5 * u_xx + 1.0 * u * (1 - u) on the periodic domain x ∈ [0,1) for time t ∈ [0,1.0].

Use a uniform periodic spatial grid with Nx = 512 points: x_j = j * dx for j = 0,…,511 where dx = 1 / Nx.

Use a fixed time step dt = 0.001 and Nt = 1000 steps so that the final time T = 1.0 is reached exactly.

Advance the solution with Strang splitting each full time step dt:
(A) Perform a diffusion half-step of duration dt/2 by transforming u to Fourier space, multiplying each Fourier coefficient by exp(-0.5 * k^2 * (dt/2)) where k = 2π * fftfreq(Nx, d=dx), and transforming back with an inverse FFT, taking the real part.
(B) Perform a reaction full-step of duration dt by applying the exact logistic update pointwise: u ← (u * exp(1.0 * dt)) / (1 + u * (exp(1.0 * dt) - 1)).
(C) Perform another diffusion half-step identical to (A).

Initialize the solution at t = 0 with u(x,0) = 0.25 + 0.1 * sin(2πx).

After completing all time steps, compute u(x,T) on the periodic grid.
