Q: Should the spatial step dx be calculated as dx = 1 / Nx (as stated in the spatial_grid specification) or as dx = (1‑0) / (Nx‑1) (per the earlier instruction to interpret Nx as including both endpoints)?
A: Use the periodic‑grid definition from the complete requirement:  

**dx = 1 / Nx** (with x_j = j·dx for j = 0,…,Nx‑1). The “Nx‑1” formula applies only to closed intervals, not to this half‑open periodic domain.
