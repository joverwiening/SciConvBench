Q: Should the spatial boundary condition be periodic only (ignoring the Dirichlet condition), or should the Dirichlet condition u = 0 be enforced at x = 0 and x = 1 in addition to periodicity?
A: Only the periodic condition should be applied. Enforce periodicity for u (and its derivative) on the domain [0, 1); do not impose the Dirichlet condition u = 0 at x = 0 and x = 1.
