Q: Should the coefficient of uₓₓ in the PDE be c² with c = 2 (i.e., u_tt = 4 u_xx + sin(πx) sin(2πt)) or remain 1.0² as written?
A: Use the coefficient as written: \(u_{tt}= (1.0)^2\,u_{xx}+ \sin(\pi x)\sin(2\pi t)\).  In the complete requirement the parameter is c = 1.0, so the term is \(c^{2}u_{xx}=1.0^{2}u_{xx}=u_{xx}\).
