Discretize the diffusion term (q2/π) u_xx with a second‑order central difference: (u_xx)_j ≈ (u_{j+1} – 2u_j + u_{j‑1})/dx² (periodic wrap‑around).

(I – dt * (q2/π) * L) u^{n+1} = u* ,  
where L is the periodic second‑difference matrix representing u_xx. Solve the linear system with a periodic tridiagonal solver (or equivalently by FFT).
