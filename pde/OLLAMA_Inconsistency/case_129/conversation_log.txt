Q: Should the time step dt be fixed using the initial estimate of max|u| (as described) for the entire simulation, or should it be recomputed adaptively at each RK stage based on the current maximum |u|?
A: Use a fixed time step. Compute the initial estimate of max|f′(u)| (≤0.25 from the initial condition), set dt₀ = CFL·dx / max|f′|, determine Nt = ceil(T/dt₀) and then use the constant dt = T/Nt for the whole run. No adaptive recomputation at later stages is required.

Q: Should the spatial grid use dx = 1 / Nx with points x_j = j·dx for j = 0,…,Nx‑1 (periodic half‑open grid), or dx = 1 / (Nx‑1) with points including both endpoints?
