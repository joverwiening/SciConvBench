Solve the 1D periodic reaction‑diffusion equation u_t = nu*u_xx + rho*u*(1-u) on x∈[0,1) with periodic boundary conditions and t∈[0,1.0].

Parameters: nu = 10.0, rho = 5.0, T = 1.0, Nx = 512, dt = 0.001, Nt = 1000, dx = 1/Nx.

Spatial grid: x_j = j*dx for j = 0,…,Nx‑1.

Initial condition: u(x,0) = 0.25 + 0.1*sin(2*pi*x) (use radians).

Time integration: For each time step of size dt apply Strang splitting:
A. Diffusion half‑step (dt/2) in Fourier space:
   - Compute u_hat = np.fft.fft(u).
   - Compute wave numbers k = 2*pi*np.fft.fftfreq(Nx, d=dx).
   - Multiply u_hat by exp(-nu*k**2*(dt/2)).
   - Compute u = np.real(np.fft.ifft(u_hat)).
B. Reaction full‑step (dt) solved exactly pointwise for the logistic ODE:
   - Let e = np.exp(rho*dt).
   - Update u = (u*e) / (1 + u*(e - 1)).
C. Diffusion half‑step again identical to step A.

Repeat steps A–B–C for Nt = 1000 steps to reach T = 1.0.

After the final step compute diagnostics:
   - u_min = u.min()
   - u_max = u.max()
   - u_mean = u.mean()
   - L2_norm = np.sqrt(np.sum(u**2)*dx)

Print the four diagnostics.
