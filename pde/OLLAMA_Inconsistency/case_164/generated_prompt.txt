Solve the 1D compressible Navier–Stokes equations (no heat conduction) on the spatial domain x ∈ [‑1, 1] for time t ∈ [0, 0.2] using a uniform finite‑volume grid with Nx = 800 cells. Cell centers are x_j = ‑1 + (j+0.5)·dx for j = 0,…,799 with dx = 2/Nx.

Evolve the conservative variables U = [ρ, m, E] (cell averages), where m = ρ v and total energy E = p/(Γ‑1) + 0.5 ρ v². Use the equation of state p = (Γ‑1)·(E – 0.5 m²/ρ) with Γ = 1.6666666666666667. Enforce positivity by clipping ρ and p to a floor of 1e‑12 if needed.
