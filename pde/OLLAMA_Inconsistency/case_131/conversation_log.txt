Q: Which scalar parameters (e.g., k, CFL, etc.) should be included in the saved solution_molk_030.npz file alongside T, dx, and Nx?
A: Include the scalar parameter **k** (the coefficient in the PDE) together with T, dx and Nx. (If you wish to record the CFL number as well, you may add it, but the only required parameter scalar from the specification is k.)
