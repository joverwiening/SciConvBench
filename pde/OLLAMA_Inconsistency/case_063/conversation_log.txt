Q: Should the boundary conditions be Dirichlet as listed (u(x,0)=0, u(x,1)=sin(πx), u(0,y)=0, u(1,y)=0) or should the domain be treated as periodic on its edges?
A: Use the Dirichlet conditions as listed:  

- u(x, 0) = 0  
- u(x, 1) = sin(π x)  
- u(0, y) = 0  
- u(1, y) = 0.
