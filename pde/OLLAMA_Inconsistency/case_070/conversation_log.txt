Q: Should the boundary conditions be the simply‑supported Dirichlet type only (u = 0 and Δu = 0 on all edges), or should the zero‑flux Neumann condition (∂u/∂n = 0) also be imposed?
A: Only the simply‑supported Dirichlet conditions are required: enforce u = 0 and Δu = 0 on all boundaries. The zero‑flux Neumann condition is **not** part of the complete specification.
