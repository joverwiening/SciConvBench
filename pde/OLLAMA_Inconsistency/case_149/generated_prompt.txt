Solve the 1D viscous Burgers equation u_t + ∂_x(0.5*u^2) = nu*u_xx on the periodic domain x∈[0,1) with nu=1.0, initial condition u(x,0)=sin(2*pi*x), and final time T=1.0. After each full time step replace u with the result of substep (C). At the final time compute u(x,T) on the grid.
