The PDE to solve is u_tt - (0.5)^2*u_xx + (2.0)^2*u = 0.
The spatial domain is x in [0,1] and the temporal domain is t in [0,1.0].
Dirichlet boundary conditions are u=0 at x=0 and x=1.
The initial condition is u(x,0)=sin(2*pi*x) and u_t(x,0)=0.
The reference exact solution is u(x,t)=cos(sqrt((0.5*2*pi)^2 + (2.0)^2)*t)*sin(2*pi*x).
A uniform grid will be used on the spatial interval [0,1] with Nx=600 intervals, resulting in Nx+1=601 grid points.
The spatial discretization for u_xx will use 2nd-order central differences.
The time integration will use 2nd-order central differences (leapfrog) for u_tt.
The term +(2.0)^2*u will be handled explicitly at time level n in the leapfrog update.
The time step dt will be chosen such that c*dt/dx <= 0.9, where c=0.5.
Specifically, dx will be 1/Nx.
dt0 will be calculated as 0.9*dx/c.
Nt will be ceil(T/dt0), where T=1.0.
The final dt will be T/Nt to ensure the final time is hit exactly.
The initialization will use a Taylor start from u(x,0) and u_t(x,0), incorporating the PDE's terms at t=0 to compute u(x,dt).
The computation will produce u(x, T=1.0) on the defined grid.
The solution will be saved to "solution_agent_kg_02.npz" as a NumPy archive containing 1D arrays x and u, and scalar metadata T, dx, dt.
A line plot of u(x,T) will be saved as "solution_agent_kg_02.png".
The minimum value of u, maximum value of u, and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at time T will be printed to the console.
The maximum absolute error and L2 error against the reference solution, evaluated on the same x grid, will also be computed and printed.
