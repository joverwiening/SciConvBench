The time domain is t in [0,1.0].

Use the 5-point Laplacian for the spatial discretization of ∇²u.
Use 2nd-order central difference for time integration of u_tt.

Finally, set dt = T/Nt to ensure the final time T is hit exactly.

Specifically, u(x,y,dt) = (dt^2/2) * sin(pi*x)*sin(pi*y).
