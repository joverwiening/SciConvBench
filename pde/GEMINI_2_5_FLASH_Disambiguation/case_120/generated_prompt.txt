The task is to numerically solve the 1D wave equation u_tt = q*u_xx.
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions.
The temporal domain is t in [0, 1.0].
The parameter q is 1.0.
The initial condition for u is u(x,0) = sin(2*pi*x).
The initial condition for u_t is u_t(x,0) = 0.
The spatial discretization uses 2nd-order central differences for u_xx on the periodic grid.
The temporal discretization uses RK4 on the system (u, v) where v = u_t.
The number of spatial grid points is Nx = 256.
The time step is dt = 0.0005.
The number of time steps is Nt = 2000.
The simulation computes u(x,T=1.0) on the periodic grid.
The results are saved to "solution_molk_019.npz" containing arrays x and u, and scalars T, dx, Nx, and q.
A line plot of u(x,T) is saved as "solution_molk_019.png".
The minimum, maximum, mean, and discrete L2 norm (sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx)) of u at T are printed to the console.
