The task is to numerically solve the 1D Klein-Gordon equation u_tt - c^2*u_xx + m^2*u = F(x,t) for u(x,t).
The specific PDE is u_tt - (1.0)^2*u_xx + (1.0)^2*u = sin(pi*x)*cos(2*t).
The spatial domain is x in [0,1] and the temporal domain is t in [0,5.0].
The parameters are c = 1.0 and m = 1.0.
The initial conditions are u(x,0)=0 and u_t(x,0)=0.
The spatial boundary conditions are Dirichlet u=0 at x=0 and u=0 at x=1.
A uniform spatial grid will be used on the interval [0,1] with Nx=400 intervals, resulting in Nx+1 grid points.
The spatial discretization for u_xx will use 2nd-order central differences.
The time integration will use a 2nd-order central difference scheme (leapfrog) for u_tt.
The +m^2*u term will be handled explicitly at time level n in the leapfrog update.
The forcing term sin(pi*x)*cos(2*t) will be included at time level n in the leapfrog update.
The time step is dt=0.001, resulting in 5000 time steps to reach T=5.0.
The initialization will use a Taylor start from u(x,0) and u_t(x,0), including the -m^2*u(x,0) term and the forcing term at t=0.
The solution u(x, T=5.0) will be computed on the grid.
The results will be saved to a file named "solution_agent_kg_07.npz" containing the 1D arrays x and u, and scalar metadata T, dx, and dt.
A line plot of u(x,T) will be saved as "solution_agent_kg_07.png".
The minimum value of u, the maximum value of u, and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at T=5.0 will be printed to the console.
