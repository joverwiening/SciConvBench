The task ID is pdebench_burgers_nu0p01.
The PDE is 1D Viscous Burgers (periodic).
The unknown field is u(x,t).
The PDE is u_t + ∂_x( 0.5*u^2 ) = nu * u_xx.
The parameter nu is 0.01.
The spatial domain for x is [0, 1).
The boundary conditions are periodic, u(0,t) = u(1,t) for all t.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = sin(2*pi*x).
The final time T is 1.0.
A closed-form reference solution is not required.
The spatial grid is uniform with Nx=512 points.
The spatial step dx is (1-0)/Nx.
The grid points x_i are 0 + i*dx for i=0..Nx-1.
The numerical method uses Strang splitting for each full time step dt.
The Strang splitting consists of three substeps: Diffusion half-step (duration dt/2), Advection full-step (duration dt), and Diffusion half-step (duration dt/2).
For the Diffusion half-step:
  Compute u_hat(k) by taking the FFT of u.
  The wave numbers k are 2*pi*fftfreq(Nx, d=dx).
  Update u_hat(k) as u_hat(k) * exp(-nu*k^2*(dt/2)).
  Compute u by taking the IFFT of u_hat and taking the real part.
For the Advection full-step:
  Use a finite-volume update with Rusanov (local Lax-Friedrichs) flux.
  Define f(u) = 0.5*u^2.
  For each interface between u_left=u[j] and u_right=u[j+1] (with periodic indexing):
    Calculate alpha = max(abs(u_left), abs(u_right)).
    Calculate the flux F = 0.5*(f(u_left) + f(u_right)) - 0.5*alpha*(u_right - u_left).
  For each cell j:
    F_plus is the flux between (u[j], u[j+1]).
    F_minus is the flux between (u[j-1], u[j]).
    Update u_new[j] = u[j] - (dt/dx)*(F_plus - F_minus).
  Replace u with u_new after the advection substep.
The time step dt is determined using a CFL condition.
The CFL number is 0.4.
The estimated maximum speed for the initial condition is 1.0.
Calculate dt0 = CFL*dx/1.0.
Calculate Nt = ceil(T/dt0).
Reset dt = T/Nt to hit the final time exactly.
Compute u at the final time T on the grid.
