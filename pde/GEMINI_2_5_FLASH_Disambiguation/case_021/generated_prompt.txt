The PDE u_t = D * u_xx will be solved numerically.
The spatial domain is x in [0,1] and the temporal domain is t in [0,0.5].
The diffusion coefficient D is 0.1.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = cos(pi*x).
The spatial discretization will use a uniform grid with Nx=300 intervals, resulting in Nx+1 grid points.
The spatial step size dx is (1-0)/Nx.
Second-order central differences will be used for the u_xx term.
Robin boundary conditions will be applied at x=0 and x=1.
At x=0, the condition (-3*u0 + 4*u1 - u2)/(2*dx) + beta*u0 = 0 will be enforced, where beta=1.0.
At x=1, the condition (3*uN - 4*u_{N-1} + u_{N-2})/(2*dx) + beta*uN = 0 will be enforced, where beta=1.0.
The time integration will use the Crank–Nicolson method.
The time step size dt is 0.001, for a total of 500 time steps.
The resulting linear system at each time step will be solved using a direct solver.
The solution u(x, T=0.5) will be computed on the grid.
The solution will be saved to "solution_agent_diffusion_05.npz" as a dictionary containing arrays 'x' and 'u', and scalars 'T', 'dx', 'dt'.
A line plot of u(x,T) will be saved as "solution_agent_diffusion_05.png".
The minimum value of u, the maximum value of u, and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at T=0.5 will be printed to the console.
