The task is to numerically solve the 1D sine-Gordon equation u_tt + q*sin(u) = u_xx.
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions.
The temporal domain is t in [0, 2.0].
The parameter q is 1.0.
The initial condition for u(x,0) is 0.1*sin(2*pi*x).
The initial condition for u_t(x,0) is 0.
The number of spatial grid points Nx is 512.
The spatial discretization for the u_xx term uses 2nd-order central differences on the periodic grid.
The time integration scheme is RK4 applied to the system (u, v) where v = u_t.
The time step size dt is 0.0005.
Compute the solution u(x,T=2.0) on the periodic grid.
Save the solution data to "solution_molk_015.npz" containing arrays x, u, and scalars T, dx, Nx, and q.
Save a line plot of u(x,T=2.0) as "solution_molk_015.png".
Report the minimum, maximum, mean, and discrete L2 norm (sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx)) of u at T=2.0.
