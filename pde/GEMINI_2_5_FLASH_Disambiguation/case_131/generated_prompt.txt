The task is to numerically solve the 1D inviscid conservation law u_t = -k*∂_x( sin(u) ).
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions, and the temporal domain is t in [0, 0.2].
The parameter k is 1.0.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = 0.5*sin(2*pi*x).
The spatial grid is a uniform periodic grid with Nx=1024 points on [0,1), defined by x_j = j*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1 with dx=1/Nx.
The spatial discretization uses a conservative finite-volume Rusanov flux for f(u)=sin(u) with wave speed alpha=max|cos(u)|.
The time integration method is SSP-RK2.
The time step is determined using a CFL number of 0.2, calculated as dt0 = CFL*dx/1.0, then Nt=ceil(T/dt0), and the final dt=T/Nt.
