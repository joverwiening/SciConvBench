The 1D inviscid Burgers equation u_t = -k*∂_x( 0.5*u^2 ) shall be solved numerically.
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) and the temporal domain is t in [0, 0.2].
The parameter k is 1.0.
Periodic boundary conditions u(0,t)=u(1,t) shall be applied for all t.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = sin(2*pi*x).
A uniform periodic spatial grid with Nx=1024 points on [0,1) shall be used.
Grid points are defined as x_j = j*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1 with dx=1/Nx.
A conservative finite-volume method with Rusanov (local Lax–Friedrichs) flux for f(u)=0.5*u^2 shall be used for spatial discretization.
SSP-RK2 (Heun) shall be used for time integration.
The time step dt shall be determined using a CFL number of 0.2.
First, calculate dt0 = CFL*dx/max|u|, estimating max|u|≈1.
Then, calculate Nt=ceil(T/dt0), where T=0.2.
Finally, set dt=T/Nt.
