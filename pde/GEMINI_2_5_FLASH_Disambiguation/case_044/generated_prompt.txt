The PDE u_t = 0.05 * u_xx will be solved numerically.
The spatial domain is x in [0,1].
The temporal domain is t in [0,0.2].
The parameter alpha is 0.05.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = exp(-200*(x-0.35)^2).
The Dirichlet boundary conditions are u(0,t)=0 and u(1,t)=0.
A uniform spatial grid on [0,1] will be used with Nx=400 intervals, resulting in Nx+1 grid points.
The spatial discretization for u_xx in the interior will use 2nd-order central differences.
Time integration will use the Crank–Nicolson method for diffusion terms.
Dirichlet boundary conditions will be enforced by fixing boundary nodes at each time step.
The time step will be dt=0.0005, for a total of 400 time steps.
The solution u(x, T=0.2) will be computed on the grid.
A file named "solution_agent_heat_15.npz" will be saved, containing the 1D arrays x and u, and the scalar metadata T, dx, and dt.
A line plot of u(x,T) will be saved as "solution_agent_heat_15.png".
The minimum value of u, the maximum value of u, and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at time T will be printed.
