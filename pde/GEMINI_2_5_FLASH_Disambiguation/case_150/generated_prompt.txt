The task is to solve the 1D Viscous Burgers equation u_t + ∂_x( 0.5*u^2 ) = nu * u_xx numerically.
The unknown field is u(x,t).
The parameter nu is 0.001.
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1).
The boundary conditions are periodic, u(0,t) = u(1,t) for all t.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = sin(2*pi*x).
The final simulation time T is 1.0.
A uniform spatial grid with Nx=512 points is used.
The grid points are x_i = i*dx for i=0..Nx-1, where dx=(1-0)/Nx.
The numerical method is Strang splitting for each full time step dt.
The Strang splitting consists of three substeps:
  (A) Diffusion half-step (duration dt/2) solved exactly in Fourier space.
      u_hat(k) is computed by taking the FFT of u.
      The wave numbers k are computed as 2*pi*fftfreq(Nx, d=dx).
      u_hat(k) is updated as u_hat(k) * exp(-nu*k^2*(dt/2)).
      u is computed by taking the IFFT of u_hat, and the real part is retained.
  (B) Advection full-step (duration dt) for the inviscid Burgers equation u_t + ∂_x(0.5*u^2)=0.
      A finite-volume update with Rusanov (local Lax-Friedrichs) flux is used.
      The flux function is f(u)=0.5*u^2.
      For each interface between u_left=u[j] and u_right=u[j+1] (with periodic indexing), alpha is defined as max(abs(u_left), abs(u_right)).
      The flux F is computed as 0.5*(f(u_left) + f(u_right)) - 0.5*alpha*(u_right - u_left).
      For each cell j, F_plus is the flux between (u[j], u[j+1]) and F_minus is the flux between (u[j-1], u[j]).
      u_new[j] is updated as u[j] - (dt/dx)*(F_plus - F_minus).
      After the advection substep, u is replaced by u_new.
  (C) Diffusion half-step again (duration dt/2), identical to substep (A).
The time step dt is determined using a CFL condition.
CFL is 0.4, and the estimated maximum speed is 1.0.
An initial time step dt0 is calculated as CFL*dx/1.0.
The number of time steps Nt is ceil(T/dt0).
The final time step dt is reset to T/Nt to hit the final time exactly.
The numerical solution u at the final time T on the grid is computed.
The solution is saved to a file named "solution_pdebench_burgers_nu1em3.npz".
The .npz file contains the arrays x (1D length Nx) and u (1D length Nx), and the scalars nu, T, dx, dt, Nx, Nt.
A line plot of u(x,T) is saved as "solution_pdebench_burgers_nu1em3.png".
The minimum value of u, the maximum value of u, the discrete mean value of u, and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at T are printed to the console.
