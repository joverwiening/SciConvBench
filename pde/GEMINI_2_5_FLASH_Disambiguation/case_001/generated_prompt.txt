The task is to numerically solve the 1D advection partial differential equation u_t + (1.0)*u_x = 0.
The spatial domain is x in [0,1) and the temporal domain is t in [0,1.0].
The advection parameter a is 1.0.
The initial condition is u(x,0)=sin(2*pi*x).
The reference exact solution for verification is u(x,t)=sin(2*pi*(x-(1.0)*t)).
The numerical method will use a uniform periodic grid with Nx=800 points on [0,1).
The spatial discretization for u_x will be 1st-order upwind.
Time integration will use Forward Euler.
The time step dt will be chosen such that max|a|*dt/dx <= 0.9.
Specifically, dx will be 1/Nx.
dt0 will be 0.9*dx/|a|.
Nt will be ceil(T/dt0), and dt will be T/Nt to hit the final time exactly.
Boundary handling will be consistent with periodic boundary conditions.
The final solution u(x, T=1.0) will be computed on the grid.
The solution will be saved to "solution_agent_advection_01.npz" containing arrays x, u, and metadata scalars T, dx, dt.
A line plot of u(x,T) will be saved as "solution_agent_advection_01.png".
The minimum value of u, maximum value of u, and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at T will be printed.
The maximum absolute error and L2 error against the reference solution (evaluated on the same x grid) will also be computed and printed.
