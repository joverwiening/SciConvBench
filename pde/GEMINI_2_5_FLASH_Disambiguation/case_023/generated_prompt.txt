The task is to numerically solve a 1D Diffusion-Reaction Partial Differential Equation.
The PDE is u_t = 0.1*u_xx - 1.0*u.
The spatial domain is x in [0,1].
The temporal domain is t in [0,0.5].
The diffusion coefficient D is 0.1.
The reaction coefficient lambda is -1.0.
The boundary conditions are Dirichlet: u(0,t)=0 and u(1,t)=0 for all t.
The initial condition is u(x,0)=sin(pi*x).
The numerical method will use 2nd-order central differences for spatial discretization on a uniform grid.
The time integration will use a 1st-order deterministic IMEX scheme, with diffusion treated implicitly (Backward Euler) and reaction treated explicitly (Forward Euler).
At each time step, the equation (I - dt*D*L) u^{n+1} = u^n + dt * R(u^n) will be solved, where L is the discrete Laplacian and R(u) is the reaction term.
A sparse direct solver will be used for each time step.
The grid will have Nx=200 spatial intervals, resulting in 201 grid points.
The time step dt will be 0.001, for a total of 500 time steps.
The numerical solution u(x, T=0.5) will be computed on the specified grid.
The exact solution for verification is u(x,t)=exp((-1.0 - 0.1*pi^2)*t)*sin(pi*x).
The computed solution, along with the x-grid, T, dx, and dt, will be saved to a file named "solution_agent_diffusion_reaction_01.npz" in NumPy's .npz format.
A line plot of u(x,T) will be saved as "solution_agent_diffusion_reaction_01.png".
The minimum value of u(x,T), the maximum value of u(x,T), and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at T will be printed to the console.
The maximum absolute error and the L2 error between the numerical solution and the exact solution (evaluated on the same x grid at T) will be computed and printed to the console.
