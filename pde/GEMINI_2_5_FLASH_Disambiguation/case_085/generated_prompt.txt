The task ID is agent_wave_04.
The PDE category is Wave equation.
The unknown field is u(x,t).

The parameter c is 1.0.

Second-order central differences are used for u_xx in the interior.
The time integration uses the standard second-order central difference in time (leapfrog / Störmer–Verlet).
For stability, dt is chosen such that CFL = c*dt/dx is less than or equal to 0.9.

For Dirichlet boundary conditions, boundary nodes are set to the prescribed value at every time step.
The grid uses Nx=400 intervals, resulting in Nx+1 points.
The spatial step dx is 1/Nx.
An initial time step dt0 is calculated as 0.9*dx/c.
The number of time steps Nt is ceil(T/dt0).
The final time step dt is T/Nt, ensuring the final time T is hit exactly.
The numerical solution u(x, T=1.0) is computed on the grid.
