The simulation will solve the 1D wave equation u_tt = c^2 * u_xx on the domain x in [0,1] and t in [0,1.0], where c=1.0.
The boundary conditions are Neumann u_x(0,t)=0 and u_x(1,t)=0.
The initial conditions are u(x,0)=cos(pi*x) and u_t(x,0)=0.
The numerical method will use a uniform spatial grid on [0,1] with Nx=400 intervals, resulting in Nx+1 grid points.
The spatial discretization will use 2nd-order central differences for u_xx in the interior.
The time integration will use the standard 2nd-order central difference (leapfrog / Störmer–Verlet).
The time step dt will be chosen such that CFL = c*dt/dx <= 0.9.
Specifically, dx will be 1/Nx. dt0 will be 0.9*dx/c. Nt will be ceil(T/dt0), and dt will be T/Nt to hit the final time T=1.0 exactly.
The initialization for the first time step u(x,dt) will use the Taylor start formula: u^1 = u^0 + dt*v0 + 0.5*dt^2*(c^2*u_xx^0).
Boundary conditions will be handled using ghost points with mirror values for zero Neumann conditions.
The simulation will compute u(x, T=1.0) on the grid.
The results will be saved to "solution_agent_wave_06.npz" containing the 1D arrays x and u, and scalar metadata T, dx, dt.
A line plot of u(x,T) will be saved as "solution_agent_wave_06.png".
The minimum value of u, maximum value of u, and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at T will be printed to the console.
The maximum absolute error and L2 error against the provided exact solution u(x,t)=cos(pi*x)*cos(1.0*pi*t) will be computed and printed to the console.
