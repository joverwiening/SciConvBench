Q: What numerical method should be used for the time integration (e.g., forward Euler, backward Euler, Crank-Nicolson, RK4)?
A: Use deterministic operator-splitting with:
1) Diffusion CN step: solve (I - 0.5*dt*q1*L) u_tilde^{n+1} = (I + 0.5*dt*q1*L) u^n, where L is the periodic 2nd-order Laplacian.
2) Reaction exact step: u^{n+1} = exp(q2*dt) * u_tilde^{n+1}.

Use dt=0.001 with Nt=500 steps.
