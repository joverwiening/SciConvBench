Q: What is the exact form of the Taylor start initialization you want me to use for the leapfrog method?
A: For the Taylor start initialization, use:

u^1 = u^0 + dt * u_t^0 + (dt^2/2) * u_tt^0

where u_tt^0 is computed from the PDE at t=0:
u_tt^0 = u_xx^0 - m^2*u^0 - lambda*(u^0)^3

Since u_t(x,0) = 0, this simplifies to:
u^1 = u^0 + (dt^2/2) * (u_xx^0 - m^2*u^0 - lambda*(u^0)^3)
