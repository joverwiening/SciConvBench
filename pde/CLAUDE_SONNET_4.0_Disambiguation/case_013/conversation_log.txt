Q: What boundary conditions should be applied at x=0 and x=1 for this periodic domain [0,1)?
A: The boundary conditions are periodic: u and u_x are periodic. This means u(0,t) = u(1,t) and u_x(0,t) = u_x(1,t). For the numerical implementation, use periodic wrap-around indexing for both the convection fluxes and the diffusion stencil.
