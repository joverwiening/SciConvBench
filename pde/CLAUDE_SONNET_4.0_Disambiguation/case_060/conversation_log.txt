Q: What are the boundary conditions for u(x,y) on the four edges of the domain [0,1] x [0,1]?
A: Dirichlet boundary conditions on all four edges: set u on the boundary to match u(x,y) = y.

So:
- Bottom edge (y=0): u(x,0) = 0
- Top edge (y=1): u(x,1) = 1  
- Left edge (x=0): u(0,y) = y
- Right edge (x=1): u(1,y) = y
