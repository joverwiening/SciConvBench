Q: What are the boundary conditions for u(x,y) on the four edges of the domain [0,1] x [0,1]?
A: The boundary conditions are:
- u(x,0) = 0 (bottom edge)
- u(x,1) = 1 (top edge) 
- u(0,y) = 0 (left edge)
- u(1,y) = 0 (right edge)

So the top edge is fixed at 1, and the other three edges are fixed at 0.
