The task is to solve the 1D viscous Burgers equation with periodic boundary conditions using a specific Strang splitting scheme with explicit forward Euler time integration, Fourier-based exact diffusion substeps, and Rusanov flux finite-volume advection substeps.

The PDE is u_t + ∂_x(0.5*u^2) = nu * u_xx with nu = 2.0.

The domain is x in [0,1) periodic, t in [0,1.0].

The initial condition is u(x,0) = sin(2*pi*x).

The numerical method uses a uniform periodic grid with Nx=512 points on [0,1), with x_j = j*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1 and dx=1/Nx.

The time step is determined by CFL=0.4 with estimated max speed 1.0, giving dt0 = CFL*dx/1.0, then Nt = ceil(T/dt0) and dt = T/Nt to hit T=1.0 exactly.

Each full time step uses Strang splitting: (A) diffusion half-step solved exactly in Fourier space via FFT with wavenumbers k = 2*pi*fftfreq(Nx, d=dx) and multiplier exp(-nu*k^2*(dt/2)), (B) advection full-step using finite-volume Rusanov flux with f(u)=0.5*u^2, alpha = max(abs(u_left), abs(u_right)), F = 0.5*(f(u_left)+f(u_right)) - 0.5*alpha*(u_right-u_left), and update u_new[j] = u[j] - (dt/dx)*(F_plus - F_minus) with periodic indexing, (C) diffusion half-step identical to (A).

The output requires computing u(x,T=1.0), saving "solution_pdebench_burgers_nu2.npz" containing arrays x (1D length Nx), u (1D length Nx), and scalars nu, T, dx, dt, Nx, Nt, saving a line plot as "solution_pdebench_burgers_nu2.png", and printing min(u), max(u), mean(u), and sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at T.
