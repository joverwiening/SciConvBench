The PDE is the heat equation u_t = 0.1 * u_xx on x in [0,1] with t in [0,0.5].

The spatial domain uses a uniform grid with Nx=200 intervals, giving Nx+1=201 grid points including both boundaries at x=0 and x=1, with dx=1/200=0.005 and x_i = i*dx for i=0..200.

The initial condition is u(x,0) = 1 - x.

Dirichlet boundary conditions are enforced: u(0,t) = 1 and u(1,t) = 0 for all time.

The exact solution is u(x,t) = 1 - x, which is steady since u_xx = 0 everywhere.

For spatial discretization, use second-order central differences for u_xx in the interior: (u_{i-1} - 2*u_i + u_{i+1}) / dx^2.

For time integration, use Crank-Nicolson with dt=0.001 for 600 steps to reach T=0.5. The scheme solves (I - 0.5*dt*alpha*A)u^{n+1} = (I + 0.5*dt*alpha*A)u^n where A is the discrete Laplacian matrix. Dirichlet boundary conditions are enforced by fixing boundary nodes to their prescribed values at each time step.

The numerical solution u(x,T=0.5) is computed on the grid.
