The task is to solve the heat equation with a time-dependent source term using Crank-Nicolson time integration and second-order central spatial differencing.

The PDE is u_t = 0.1 * u_xx + sin(pi*x)*cos(2*pi*t) on x in [0,1] and t in [0,1].

The spatial domain uses a uniform grid with Nx=300 intervals, giving Nx+1=301 points including both boundaries at x=0 and x=1, with dx=1/300.

The time step is dt=0.001, requiring 1000 steps to reach T=1.0 (noting the specification mentions 1200 steps but with dt=0.001 and T=1.0, exactly 1000 steps are needed).

Dirichlet boundary conditions u(0,t)=0 and u(1,t)=0 are enforced by fixing the boundary nodes to zero at each time step.

The initial condition is u(x,0)=0 for all x.

For the Crank-Nicolson scheme, the diffusion term 0.1*u_xx is treated implicitly with theta=0.5 weighting. The source term sin(pi*x)*cos(2*pi*t) is included by averaging the source at time levels n and n+1, i.e., 0.5*(f^n + f^{n+1}) where f(x,t) = sin(pi*x)*cos(2*pi*t).

The spatial discretization uses second-order central differences: u_xx at interior point i is approximated as (u_{i-1} - 2*u_i + u_{i+1})/dx^2.

The resulting tridiagonal system for interior points (i=1 to Nx-1) is solved at each time step, with boundary values u_0 and u_Nx held at zero.

After reaching T=1.0, the solution array u (length 301) and coordinate array x (length 301) are saved to "solution_agent_heat_08.npz" along with scalars T=1.0, dx=1/300, and dt=0.001.

A line plot of u versus x at T=1.0 is saved as "solution_agent_heat_08.png".

The minimum value, maximum value, and discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) of the solution at T=1.0 are printed.
