The task is to solve a 1D reaction-diffusion PDE with logistic reaction and periodic boundary conditions using Strang splitting with exact diffusion in Fourier space and exact reaction pointwise.

The PDE is u_t = nu * u_xx + rho * u*(1 - u) with nu=0.5, rho=5.0.

The spatial domain is [0,1) periodic with Nx=512 grid points, dx=1/512, x_j = j*dx for j=0,...,511.

The time domain is [0,1.0] with dt=0.001 and Nt=1000 steps.

The initial condition is u(x,0) = 0.25 + 0.1*sin(2*pi*x).

The numerical method uses Strang splitting per time step dt: (A) exact diffusion half-step via FFT with factor exp(-nu*k^2*dt/2), (B) exact reaction full-step via u <- (u*exp(rho*dt))/(1+u*(exp(rho*dt)-1)), (C) exact diffusion half-step again.

The wavenumbers are k = 2*pi*fftfreq(Nx, d=dx).

The output requires: u(x,T=1.0) on the periodic grid; saving "solution_pdebench_reacdiff_nu0p5_rho5.npz" with arrays x, u and scalars nu, rho, T, dx, dt, Nx, Nt; saving a line plot as "solution_pdebench_reacdiff_nu0p5_rho5.png"; printing min(u), max(u), mean(u), and discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at T.
