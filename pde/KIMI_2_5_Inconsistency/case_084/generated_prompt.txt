The PDE to solve is the 1D wave equation u_tt = c^2 * u_xx with c = 1.0 (from the PDE coefficient), though the parameters section lists c = 2 which appears to be a contradiction. The PDE explicitly states (1.0)^2, so c = 1.0 will be used for the wave speed in computations.

The spatial domain is x in [0,1] with Dirichlet boundary conditions u=0 at x=0 and x=1.

The initial conditions are u(x,0) = sin(pi*x) + 0.5*sin(3*pi*x) and u_t(x,0) = 0.

The exact solution is u(x,t) = cos(pi*t)*sin(pi*x) + 0.5*cos(3*pi*t)*sin(3*pi*x).
