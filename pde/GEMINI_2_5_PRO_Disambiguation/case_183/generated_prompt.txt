The PDE to solve is u_t = nu * u_xx + rho * u*(1 - u).
The spatial domain for x is [0, 1).
The time domain is from t=0 to t=1.0.
The parameter nu is 1.0.
The parameter rho is 5.0.
The boundary conditions are periodic.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = 0.25 + 0.1*sin(2*pi*x).
Use a uniform spatial grid with Nx = 512 points, resulting in a grid spacing of dx = 1.0/512.
The grid points are x_i = i*dx for i = 0, ..., 511.
Use a fixed time step of dt = 0.001.
Perform Nt = 1000 time steps to reach the final time T = 1.0.
Use Strang splitting to advance the solution for each time step dt.
A full time step consists of a diffusion half-step (duration dt/2), a reaction full-step (duration dt), and a second diffusion half-step (duration dt/2).
Solve the diffusion half-step in Fourier space: transform u to u_hat, multiply by exp(-nu*k^2*(dt/2)) where k = 2*pi*fftfreq(Nx, d=dx), and inverse transform back to u, taking the real part.
Solve the reaction full-step by applying the exact solution of the logistic ODE pointwise: u_new = (u_old * exp(rho*dt)) / (1 + u_old * (exp(rho*dt) - 1)).
Compute the solution u(x,T) at the final time T on the specified grid.
