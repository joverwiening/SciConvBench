The PDE to solve is u_t + u*u_x = 0.01*u_xx.
The unknown field is u(x,t).
The spatial domain is x in [0,1].
The time domain is t in [0,0.5].
The viscosity parameter nu is 0.01.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = sin(pi*x).
The boundary conditions are u(0,t) = 0 and u(1,t) = 0.
The spatial grid has Nx=800 uniform intervals, resulting in 801 points.
The spatial step size is dx = 1/800.
The time step is dt = 1e-4.
The total number of time steps is 5000, reaching a final time T of 0.5.
Discretize the PDE in the conservative form u_t + (f(u))_x = nu*u_xx, where f(u) = 0.5*u^2.
The convective term (f(u))_x is discretized as (F_{i+1/2} - F_{i-1/2})/dx.
The numerical flux F_{i+1/2} is the Rusanov flux: 0.5*(f(u_i)+f(u_{i+1})) - 0.5*alpha_{i+1/2}*(u_{i+1}-u_i).
The wave speed for the Rusanov flux is alpha_{i+1/2} = max(|u_i|, |u_{i+1}|).
The diffusive term u_xx is discretized using a 2nd-order central difference: (u_{i+1}-2u_i+u_{i-1})/dx^2.
The time integration scheme is a first-order IMEX method.
The convective term is treated explicitly.
The diffusive term is treated implicitly.
The resulting discrete equation is (u^{n+1} - u^n)/dt + (F^n_{i+1/2}-F^n_{i-1/2})/dx = nu * (u^{n+1}_{i+1}-2u^{n+1}_i+u^{n+1}_{i-1})/dx^2.
At each time step, solve the sparse linear system for u^{n+1}.
