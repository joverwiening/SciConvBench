The partial differential equation to solve is u_t = -1.0 * ∂_x( (1/3)*u^3 ) + (0.05/pi) * u_xx.
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundaries.
The temporal domain is t in [0, 0.5].
The initial condition is u(x,0) = 0.5*sin(2*pi*x).
The boundary conditions are periodic.
The spatial grid is a uniform periodic grid with Nx=1024 points.
The grid spacing is dx = 1/Nx.
The grid points are x_j = j*dx for j=0, ..., Nx-1.
The spatial discretization of the convection term -∂_x((1/3)u^3) uses a conservative finite-volume Rusanov flux scheme.
The spatial discretization of the diffusion term (0.05/pi)*u_xx uses a second-order central difference scheme.
The time integration method is the Strong Stability Preserving Runge-Kutta 2 (SSP-RK2).
The time step dt is constant for the entire simulation.
The convective time step restriction is dt0 = 0.2 * dx / 0.25.
The diffusive time step restriction is dt_diff = 0.2 * dx^2 / (0.05/pi).
A preliminary time step is calculated as the minimum of dt0 and dt_diff.
The number of time steps is Nt = ceil(0.5 / preliminary_dt).
The final time step used for integration is dt = 0.5 / Nt.
