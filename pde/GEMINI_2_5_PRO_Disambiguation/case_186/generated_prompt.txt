The final time is T = 1.0.
Solve the PDE using Strang splitting for each time step dt.
A full time step consists of a half-step of diffusion, a full step of reaction, and another half-step of diffusion.
The diffusion step solves u_t = 2.0 * u_xx for a duration of dt/2 using the Fourier spectral method.
The update rule in Fourier space for the diffusion step is u_hat(k) = u_hat(k) * exp(-2.0 * k^2 * (dt/2)).
The wavenumbers k are defined as 2*pi*fftfreq(Nx, d=dx).
The reaction step solves du/dt = 2.0 * u*(1-u) for a duration of dt.
The update rule for the reaction step at each grid point is u = (u * exp(2.0 * dt)) / (1 + u * (exp(2.0 * dt) - 1)).
