The partial differential equation to solve is u_t = -∂_x( (1/3)*u^3 ).
The spatial domain is the interval [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions.
The time domain is the interval [0, 0.2].
The initial condition is u(x,0) = 0.5*sin(2*pi*x).
The numerical solution is computed on a uniform periodic grid with 1024 points, where x_j = j/1024 for j=0,...,1023.
The spatial discretization is a conservative finite-volume scheme.
The numerical flux between cells is the Rusanov flux, F(u_L, u_R) = 0.5 * (f(u_L) + f(u_R)) - 0.5 * max(u_L^2, u_R^2) * (u_R - u_L).
The physical flux function is f(u) = (1/3)*u^3.
The time integration method is the second-order Strong Stability Preserving Runge-Kutta (SSP-RK2) scheme.
A fixed time step, dt, is used for the entire simulation.
The time step is calculated based on a CFL number of 0.2 and the maximum wave speed of the initial condition, which is estimated as 0.25.
First, an initial time step is calculated as dt0 = 0.2 * (1/1024) / 0.25.
The total number of time steps is Nt = ceil(0.2 / dt0).
The final time step used for the simulation is dt = 0.2 / Nt.
The simulation runs for Nt time steps to reach the final time.
