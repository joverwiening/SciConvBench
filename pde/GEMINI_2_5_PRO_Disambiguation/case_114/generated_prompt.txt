The PDE to solve is u_t = d/dx(d/dx(u^m)).
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundaries.
The temporal domain is t in [0, 0.2].
The parameter m is 2.0.
The number of spatial grid points is Nx = 256.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = 0.5 + 0.2*cos(2*pi*x).
The time-stepping method is explicit RK4.
The time step is dt = 0.0001.
The spatial derivative term is discretized by first computing w = u^m pointwise on the grid.
The term w_xx is then approximated using a second-order central difference scheme.
The simulation will run until the final time T = 0.2.
