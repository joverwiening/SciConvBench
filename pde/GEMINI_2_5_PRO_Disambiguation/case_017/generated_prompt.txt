The PDE to solve is u_t = ∂/∂x( D(x) * u_x ).
The diffusion coefficient is D(x) = 0.1*(1 + x).
The spatial domain is x in [0,1].
The time domain is t in [0,0.5].
The initial condition is u(x,0) = sin(pi*x).
The boundary condition at x=0 is u(0,t) = 0.
The boundary condition at x=1 is u(1,t) = 0.
The spatial discretization is a conservative 2nd-order finite difference method on a uniform grid.
The diffusion coefficient at cell faces is D_{i+1/2} = 0.5*(D(x_i) + D(x_{i+1})).
The discrete spatial operator for interior points is (1/dx^2) * [ D_{i+1/2}(u_{i+1}-u_i) - D_{i-1/2}(u_i-u_{i-1}) ].
The time integration scheme is Implicit Euler.
The number of spatial intervals is Nx=300.
The time step is dt=0.001.
