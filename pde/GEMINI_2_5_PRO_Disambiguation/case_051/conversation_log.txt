Q: The initialization step uses a `-m^2*u0` term, but the time integration step says to include `+m^2*u`. What sign should the `m^2*u` term have in the main Leapfrog update equation?
A: The sign should be negative. The Leapfrog update discretizes the equation `u_tt = c^2*u_xx - m^2*u`, which is derived from the PDE you were given.
