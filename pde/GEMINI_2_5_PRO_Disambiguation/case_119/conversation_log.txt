Q: What is the time step, dt?
A: Use a CFL number of 0.5. The time step `dt` is determined by the following procedure:
1. Calculate an initial time step `dt0 = CFL * dx / abs(q)`.
2. Determine the number of time steps `Nt = ceil(T / dt0)`.
3. The final time step to use is `dt = T / Nt`.
