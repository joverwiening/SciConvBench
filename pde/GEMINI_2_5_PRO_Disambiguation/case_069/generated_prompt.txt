Solve the Kuramoto-Sivashinsky equation u_t + u*u_x + u_xx + u_xxxx = 0.
The spatial domain is x in [0, 2*pi) with periodic boundary conditions.
The time domain is t in [0, 1.0].
The initial condition is u(x, t=0) = cos(x) + 0.1*cos(2*x).
Use a uniform grid with Nx=1024 points.
Discretize the spatial derivatives using the Fourier pseudo-spectral method.
Compute the nonlinear term u*u_x in physical space.
Apply the 2/3 rule for de-aliasing when computing the Fourier transform of the nonlinear term.
Integrate in time using a semi-implicit scheme in Fourier space.
