The PDE to solve is u_t + ∂_x( 0.5*u^2 ) = nu * u_xx.
The unknown field is u(x,t).
The spatial domain for x is the periodic interval [0, 1).
The time domain is [0, T] where the final time T is 1.0.
The viscosity parameter nu is 2.0.
The boundary conditions are periodic.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = sin(2*pi*x).
The spatial grid is a uniform grid with Nx=512 points.
The grid spacing is dx = 1.0/Nx.
The grid points are x_i = i*dx for i = 0, ..., Nx-1.
The time step dt is determined by a CFL condition.
The CFL number is 0.4.
The maximum wave speed for the CFL calculation is estimated as 1.0.
An initial time step is calculated as dt0 = CFL * dx / 1.0.
The number of time steps is Nt = ceil(T/dt0).
The final time step is dt = T/Nt.
The numerical method is Strang splitting for each time step dt.
The first substep is a diffusion step of duration dt/2.
The diffusion step is solved in Fourier space by multiplying the FFT of u by exp(-nu*k^2*(dt/2)).
The wave numbers are k = 2*pi*fftfreq(Nx, d=dx).
The result of the diffusion step is the real part of the inverse FFT.
The second substep is an advection step of duration dt for the inviscid Burgers equation.
The advection step is solved using a finite-volume method with a Rusanov flux.
The flux function is f(u) = 0.5*u^2.
The Rusanov flux at the interface between u_left and u_right is F = 0.5*(f(u_left) + f(u_right)) - 0.5*alpha*(u_right - u_left).
The local wave speed for the Rusanov flux is alpha = max(abs(u_left), abs(u_right)).
The finite-volume update for cell j is u_new[j] = u[j] - (dt/dx)*(F_plus - F_minus), where F_plus and F_minus are the fluxes at the right and left interfaces of the cell, respectively.
The third substep is another diffusion step of duration dt/2, identical to the first substep.
