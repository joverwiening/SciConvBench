Q: For the Rusanov flux, should the wave speed `alpha` be set to the constant value of 1.0 for the entire simulation?
A: Yes, use a constant wave speed of 1.0 for the entire simulation. The time step calculation is specified as `dt0 = CFL*dx/1.0 (since max|cos(u)|<=1)`.
