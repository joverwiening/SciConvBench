The partial differential equation to solve is u_t = nu * u_xx + rho * u*(1 - u).
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1).
The temporal domain is t in [0, 1.0].
The parameter nu is 1.0.
The parameter rho is 1.0.
The boundary conditions are periodic.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = 0.25 + 0.1*sin(2*pi*x).
The spatial domain is discretized with a uniform grid of Nx=512 points.
The spatial step size is dx = 1.0 / 512.
The time domain is discretized with a fixed time step of dt = 0.001.
The simulation runs for Nt=1000 time steps, up to a final time T = 1.0.
The numerical method is Strang splitting for each time step dt.
The first step is a diffusion half-step of duration dt/2, solved in Fourier space.
The Fourier space update for the diffusion half-step is u_hat(k) <- u_hat(k) * exp(-nu*k^2*(dt/2)), where k are the discrete wavenumbers.
The second step is a reaction full-step of duration dt, solved exactly for the logistic ODE at each grid point.
The update for the reaction step is u <- (u*exp(rho*dt)) / (1 + u*(exp(rho*dt) - 1)).
The third step is another diffusion half-step of duration dt/2, identical to the first step.
The real part of the solution should be taken after each inverse Fourier transform.
At the final time T, compute the solution u(x,T).
