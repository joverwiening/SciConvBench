Q: What numerical flux function should be used for the convective terms?
A: Use the Rusanov (local Lax-Friedrichs) numerical flux. The formula is `F_hat = 0.5*(F_conv(U_L) + F_conv(U_R)) - 0.5*a*(U_R - U_L)`, where the interface wave speed is `a = max( |v_L| + c_L, |v_R| + c_R )` and `c = sqrt(Gamma*p/rho)`.

Q: How should the viscous terms be discretized?
A: Compute v_x with a centered difference on the cell-centered v values. Then, approximate sigma at the interfaces by averaging the sigma values from the two neighboring cell centers. The viscous flux contribution at the interface is F_visc = [ 0, -sigma, -v*sigma ], using interface-averaged v and sigma.
