Simulate the 2D steady-state, turbulent, incompressible flow over a NACA 0012 airfoil.
The angle of attack is 5 degrees.
Use simpleFoam as the solver.
Use the k-omega SST turbulence model.
The C-grid mesh is extruded one cell in the z-direction to create the 2D domain.
In blockMeshDict, the front and back patches are named 'frontAndBack' and are of 'type symmetry'.
The inlet velocity is (10 0 0) m/s.
The inlet boundary condition for pressure is fixedValue 0.
The inlet boundary condition for k is fixedValue 0.015 m^2/s^2.
The inlet boundary condition for omega is fixedValue 100 rad/s.
The outlet boundary condition for U, p, k, and omega is zeroGradient.
The airfoil surface boundary condition for U is no-slip wall.
The airfoil surface boundary condition for pressure is zeroGradient.
The 'frontAndBack' patches boundary condition for U, p, k, and omega is 'symmetry'.
The simulation aims to calculate the 2D lift and drag coefficients.
