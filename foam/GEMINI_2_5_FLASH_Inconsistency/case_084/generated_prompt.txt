Set up a transient two-phase Euler-Euler gas-solid riser simulation.
Use the twoPhaseEulerFoam solver.
The two phases are air (gas) and catalyst (solid).
The riser has a height of 2.5 meters and a diameter of 0.15 meters.
At the inlet, the volume fraction of the catalyst phase (alpha.catalyst) is 0.01.
At the inlet, the volume fraction of the air phase (alpha.air) is 0.99.
The inlet velocity for the air phase is 5 meters per second.
The inlet velocity for the catalyst phase is 0.1 meters per second.
