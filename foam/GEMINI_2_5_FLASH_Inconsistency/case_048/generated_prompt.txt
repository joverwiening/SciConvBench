The simulation will use the buoyantPimpleFoam solver.
The simulation will model buoyancy-driven heat transfer in a 2D channel.
The bottom wall of the channel will be heated to a fixed temperature of 400 K.
The top wall of the channel will be adiabatic, with zero heat flux.
The left and right boundaries of the channel will be solid walls with no-slip conditions for velocity, zero-gradient for pressure, and zero-gradient for temperature.
The air within the channel will be initialized at a uniform temperature of 300 K.
Gravity will act in the negative y-direction with a magnitude of 9.81 m/s^2.
The reference density of air at 300 K will be 1.177 kg/m^3.
The kinematic viscosity of air at 300 K will be 1.568e-5 m^2/s.
The specific heat capacity of air at 300 K will be 1007 J/(kg.K).
The thermal conductivity of air at 300 K will be 0.0262 W/(m.K).
The 2D channel will have a length of 1 meter and a height of 1 meter.
