The simulation will be a laminar counter-flow diffusion flame using reactingFoam.
The configuration is a 2-D opposed-jet.
The computational domain spans x = 0 to 0.02 m, y = -0.01 to 0.01 m, and z = -0.01 to 0.01 m.
The grid will have 100 points in x, 40 points in y, and 1 cell in z.
The left boundary (x = 0) is named fuel and acts as an inlet.
The right boundary (x = 0.02) is named air and acts as an inlet.
The top and bottom boundaries are named outlet and act as outlets.
The FrontAndBack faces are empty type boundaries.
At the fuel inlet (x = 0), methane (CH4) is injected at 293 K.
The species mass fraction of CH4 at the fuel inlet is 1.0, with all other species mass fractions being 0.
The velocity at the fuel inlet is 0.1 m/s.
At the air inlet (x = 0.02), oxidizer (air: O2/N2) is supplied at 293 K.
The species mass fraction of O2 at the air inlet is 0.23, with the balance being N2.
The velocity at the air inlet is -0.1 m/s.
The domain is initialized with a temperature of 2000 K at time 0.
The chemistry type is EulerImplicit.
The ODE solver is Rosenbrock43.
A simple 5-species (CH4, O2, N2, CO2, H2O), 3-reaction global mechanism for methane combustion will be used for finite-rate chemistry.
The combustion model is compressible finite-rate chemistry.
The momentum transport is laminar.
The thermodynamic model is hePsiThermo.
The mixing rule is coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture.
The transport model is Sutherland.
The thermodynamic data uses JANAF tables.
At the outlet boundaries, the pressure boundary condition is fixedValue 0 Pa gauge.
At the outlet boundaries, the velocity boundary condition is zeroGradient.
At the outlet boundaries, the temperature boundary condition is zeroGradient.
At the outlet boundaries, the species mass fraction boundary condition is zeroGradient.
The simulation timestep is 1e-4 s.
The simulation end time is 0.5 s.
Results are written every 0.05 s.
