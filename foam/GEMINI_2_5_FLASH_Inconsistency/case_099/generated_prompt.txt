The simulation will use the icoFoam solver.
The simulation will model 2D laminar, transient flow.
The computational domain is a channel 2.2 meters long, 0.41 meters high, and 0.01 meters thick.
A 0.1 meter by 0.1 meter square cylinder is placed within the channel.
The center of the square cylinder is located at (0.49, 0.205, 0.005) meters.
The kinematic viscosity (nu) of the fluid is 0.001 m^2/s.
For velocity (U), the inlet boundary condition is a parabolic inflow profile with a maxValue of (1.5 0 0).
For velocity (U), the outlet boundary condition is zeroGradient.
For velocity (U), the topAndBottomWalls boundary condition is noSlip.
For velocity (U), the cylinder boundary condition is noSlip.
For velocity (U), the frontAndBack boundary condition is noSlip.
For pressure (p), the inlet boundary condition is zeroGradient.
For pressure (p), the outlet boundary condition is a fixedValue of uniform 0.
For pressure (p), the topAndBottomWalls boundary condition is zeroGradient.
For pressure (p), the cylinder boundary condition is zeroGradient.
For pressure (p), the frontAndBack boundary condition is zeroGradient.
The frontAndBack patches are defined as type wall in constant/polyMesh/boundary.
The mesh will have approximately 80 cells across the channel height.
The total simulation time will be 20 seconds.
