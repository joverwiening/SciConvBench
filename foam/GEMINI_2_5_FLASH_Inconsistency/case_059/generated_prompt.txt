Perform a steady-state, two-phase (oil-water) flow simulation.
Use the `twoPhaseEulerFoam` solver.
The computational domain is a horizontal pipe with a diameter of 0.1 meters and a length of 10 meters.
The oil phase has a density of 850 kg/m³ and a dynamic viscosity of 0.01 Pa·s.
The water phase has a density of 1000 kg/m³ and a dynamic viscosity of 0.001 Pa·s.
The surface tension between the oil and water phases is 0.03 N/m.
At the inlet, impose a fixed mixture velocity of 1 m/s.
At the inlet, set the water volume fraction `alpha.water` to 0.3.
On the pipe walls, apply a `noSlip` boundary condition.
At the outlet, for `alpha.water`, use a `fixedValue` boundary condition of `uniform 0.3`.
At the outlet, for pressure, use a `fixedValue` boundary condition of 0 Pa gauge pressure.
Include gravity with a magnitude of 9.81 m/s² acting in the negative Z-direction.
