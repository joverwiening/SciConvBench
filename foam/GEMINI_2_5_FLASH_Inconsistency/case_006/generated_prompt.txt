The simulation will run a 2D gas–liquid bubble column using multiphaseEulerFoam (Euler–Euler).
The computational domain is rectangular, spanning x from 0 to 0.15 m, y from 0 to 1.00 m, and z from 0 to 0.10 m, with height along the +y direction and thinness in the z direction.
A structured hex mesh will be used with 25 cells in x, 75 cells in y, and 1 cell in z, with simpleGrading (1 1 1).
The blockMeshDict will define inlet, outlet, and walls patches, with 2D treatment using empty faces in the z direction.
The continuous liquid phase is water, using heRhoThermo with an rPolynomial equation of state having C coefficients (0.001278 -2.1055e-06 3.9689e-09 4.3772e-13 -2.0225e-16), a molecular weight of 18, a specific heat capacity Cv of 4195 J/(kg·K), a dynamic viscosity of 3.645e-4 Pa·s, and a Prandtl number of 2.289.
The dispersed gas phase is air, using heRhoThermo with a perfectGas equation of state, a molecular weight of 28.9, a specific heat capacity Cp of 1007 J/(kg·K), a dynamic viscosity of 1.84e-05 Pa·s, and a Prandtl number of 0.7.
Gravity is set to act vertically along the -y direction with a vector of (0 -9.81 0) m/s².
Bubble size models will use an isothermal diameter d0 = 3e-3 m for air and a constant diameter d = 1e-4 m for water.
Momentum transport is configured as laminar for both phases, with simulationType laminar in the respective momentumTransport files.
Multiphase interaction models from phaseProperties define phases as (air water) with water as the reference phase and a residualAlpha of 1e-6 for both phases.
Blending uses a linear scheme with minFullyContinuousAlpha 0.7 and minPartlyContinuousAlpha 0.3 for default blending, and minPartlyContinuousAlpha 0.5 for drag blending.
Surface tension between air and water is constant with sigma = 0.07 N/m.
The drag model uses SchillerNaumann for air_dispersedIn_water, water_dispersedIn_air, and air_segregatedWith_water configurations.
Virtual mass uses a constantCoefficient model with Cvm = 0.5 for both directions.
Heat transfer employs the RanzMarshall model for both directions with a residualAlpha of 1e-4.
Initial conditions at t = 0 set the velocity of water to uniform (0 0 0) m/s, the velocity of air to uniform (0 0.1 0) m/s, the volume fraction of water to uniform 1, the pressure p_rgh to uniform 1e5 Pa, the temperature of air to uniform 300 K, and the temperature of water to uniform 350 K.
Phase distribution uses default field values where the volume fraction of air is 1 and water is 0, except in a box region from (0 0 0) to (0.15 0.701 0.1) where the volume fraction of air is 0 and water is 1.
