The simulation will use the twoPhaseEulerFoam application.
The computational domain will have dimensions of 4 meters in the x-direction, 2 meters in the y-direction, and 0.1 meters in the z-direction.
The computational domain will be discretized with 200 cells in the x-direction, 100 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The mesh will be a 2D extrusion with the frontAndBack boundary condition set to empty.
The initial water column will have dimensions of 1 meter in the x-direction and 2 meters in the y-direction.
The initial water column will be positioned from x=0 to x=1 meter and from y=0 to y=2 meters.
The water column will be initialized using setFields such that alpha.water is 1 inside the column and 0 elsewhere.
