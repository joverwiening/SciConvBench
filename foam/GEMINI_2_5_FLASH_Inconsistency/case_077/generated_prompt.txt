Simulate a transient thermal plume rising in a 2D domain.
Use the `buoyantPimpleFoam` solver.
The computational domain is 1 meter wide and 2 meters high.
A small, hot patch at the bottom center of the domain is 0.1 meters wide and held at a fixed temperature of 500 K.
The initial temperature of the surrounding fluid is uniform at 300 K.
Gravity is set to (0 -9.81 0).
The simulation will model large density variations.
In `constant/thermophysicalProperties`, configure the fluid properties as follows: `thermoType hePsiThermo; mixture pureMixture; transport sutherland; thermo hConst; equationOfState perfectGas; specie hConst; energy sensibleEnthalpy;`.
The Sutherland model for viscosity uses `As 1.4792e-06;` and `Ts 116;`.
The specific gas constant (R) for the fluid is 287 J/(kg·K).
The specific heat at constant pressure (Cp) for the fluid is 1007 J/(kg·K).
The initial temperature field is `uniform 300;`.
All boundaries, except the hot patch, are open to the atmosphere.
For open boundaries, pressure uses `totalPressure` with a uniform value, velocity uses `inletOutlet` with a uniform value of (0 0 0) for inflow, and temperature uses `inletOutlet` with a uniform value of 300 K for inflow.
