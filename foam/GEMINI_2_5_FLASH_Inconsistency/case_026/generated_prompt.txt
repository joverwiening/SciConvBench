The simulation will be a steady-state, incompressible, turbulent flow simulation.
The simpleFoam solver will be used for the Pitz and Daily combustor case.
The geometry is 2D.
The turbulence modeling approach will be Reynolds-Averaged Navier-Stokes (RANS) using the standard k-Epsilon model.
The `simulationType` in `constant/turbulenceProperties` will be set to `RAS`.
The `RASModel` in `constant/turbulenceProperties` will be set to `kEpsilon`.
The `LES` block will be removed from the `constant/turbulenceProperties` file.
The velocity `U` at the `inlet` will have a fixed value of (10 0 0) m/s.
The velocity `U` at the `outlet` will have a zero-gradient condition.
The velocity `U` at the `upperWall` and `lowerWall` will have no-slip conditions.
The `frontAndBack` patches will be of type `empty`.
The pressure `p` at the `inlet`, `upperWall`, and `lowerWall` will be set to `zeroGradient`.
The pressure `p` at the `outlet` will have a fixed value of 0.
The simulation will run for 500 iterations.
