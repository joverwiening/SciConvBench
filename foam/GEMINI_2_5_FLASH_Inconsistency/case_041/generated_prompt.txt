The simulation will be a 2D laminar, incompressible, transient flow past a cylinder.
The objective is to observe the von Kármán vortex street.
The domain is a rectangular channel 2.2m long and 0.41m high.
A cylinder of diameter 0.1m is centered at (0.2, 0.2).
The kinematic viscosity is 1e-3 m²/s.
The uniform inlet velocity is (1 0 0) m/s.
The mesh is one cell thick in the z-direction.
The solver is `pimpleFoam`.
In `system/controlDict`, `startTime` is 0, `endTime` is 200, and `writeInterval` is 10.
`adjustTimeStep` is enabled with `maxCo` set to 0.9.
For boundary conditions in the `0/` directory, `U` at the `inlet` is `fixedValue uniform (1 0 0)`, at the `outlet` is `zeroGradient`, on the `cylinderWall` is `noSlip`, on the `topAndBottom` walls is `noSlip`, and on the `frontAndBack` patches is `empty`.
For `p`, `inlet` and `cylinderWall` are `zeroGradient`, `outlet` is `fixedValue uniform 0`, `topAndBottom` is `zeroGradient`, and `frontAndBack` is `empty`.
In `system/fvSchemes`, `ddtSchemes` is `Euler`.
In `system/fvSchemes`, `gradSchemes` is `Gauss linear`.
In `system/fvSchemes`, `divSchemes` for `div(phi,U)` is `Gauss linearUpwindV`.
In `system/fvSchemes`, `laplacianSchemes` is `Gauss linear corrected`.
In `system/fvSchemes`, `interpolationSchemes` is `linear`.
In `system/fvSchemes`, `snGradSchemes` is `corrected`.
The simulation will run to capture the transient vortex shedding.
