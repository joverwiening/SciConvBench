The simulation uses the DPMFoam solver.
The domain is a vertical cylinder with a height of 1m along the Y-axis and a diameter of 0.2m.
The fluid is water with a density of 1000 kg/m^3 and a dynamic viscosity of 0.001 Pa.s.
A single spherical particle with a diameter of 1mm and a density of 2500 kg/m^3 is initialized at position (0, 0.9, 0).
The acceleration due to gravity is (0 -9.81 0).
The fluid velocity is initialized to zero everywhere.
The cylinder walls have a noSlip boundary condition.
The top and bottom patches of the cylinder are simple walls.
The simulation tracks the particle's trajectory as it settles.
