Set up a 2D, incompressible, steady-state simulation of airflow over a symmetric airfoil.
Use the simpleFoam solver.
The airfoil profile is NACA 0012.
The airfoil chord length is 1 meter.
The computational domain is a C-grid topology around the airfoil.
The domain extends 10 chord lengths upstream from the leading edge, 20 chord lengths downstream from the trailing edge, and 10 chord lengths from the airfoil in the normal direction.
The mesh is one cell thick in the z-direction.
In the system/blockMeshDict file, define a patch named frontAndBack with type wall.
The faces for the frontAndBack patch are (0 4 7 3) and (1 2 6 5).
For boundary conditions in the 0/U file, apply a uniform inlet velocity of (10 0 0) m/s.
For boundary conditions in the 0/U file, apply a zeroGradient condition at the outlet.
For boundary conditions in the 0/U file, apply a noSlip condition on the airfoil surface.
For boundary conditions in the 0/U file, apply a noSlip condition on the frontAndBack patches.
For boundary conditions in the 0/p file, apply a zeroGradient condition for the inlet.
For boundary conditions in the 0/p file, apply a zeroGradient condition for the airfoil surface.
For boundary conditions in the 0/p file, apply a fixedValue uniform 0 condition at the outlet.
For boundary conditions in the 0/p file, apply a zeroGradient condition on the frontAndBack patches.
