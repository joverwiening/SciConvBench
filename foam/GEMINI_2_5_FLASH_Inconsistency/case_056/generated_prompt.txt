The simulation is a 3D transient dam break.
The multiphaseEulerFoam solver will be used.
The computational domain is a rectangular tank with dimensions 1m in x, 0.6m in y, and 0.4m in z.
Three phases are present: water, air, and light oil.
The phases are defined as air, water, and oil in transportProperties.
The density of water is 1000 kg/m^3.
The density of air is 1.225 kg/m^3.
The density of light oil is 800 kg/m^3.
Gravity acts in the -y direction.
Initial conditions are set using the setFieldsDict utility for phase fractions alpha.air, alpha.water, and alpha.oil.
A column of water with dimensions 0.2m in x, 0.6m in y, and 0.2m in z is initially placed with its minimum coordinates at (0m, 0m, 0m).
A layer of light oil with dimensions 0.2m in x, 0.6m in y, and 0.05m in z is initially placed directly on top of the water column.
The remaining volume of the domain is initially filled with air.
All walls of the tank have no-slip boundary conditions.
The top boundary of the tank is an atmospheric inlet/outlet.
