The simulation will use the steady-state solver buoyantSimpleFoam.
The simulation models airflow and natural convection driven by a heater in a ventilated room.
The room dimensions are 3 meters by 3 meters by 3 meters.
The inlet is a 0.5 meter by 0.5 meter square patch centered on the bottom of the xMin wall.
The outlet is a 0.5 meter by 0.5 meter square patch centered on the top of the xMax wall.
The heater is a 1 meter by 1 meter square wall patch centered on the bottom of the yMin wall.
The thermophysical properties are specified using thermoType heRhoThermo.
The equation of state is Boussinesq with T0 293, beta 0.00341, and rho0 1.2.
The transport model is const with mu 1.8e-5 and Pr 0.71.
The inlet temperature is a fixedValue of 293 K.
The heater temperature is a fixedValue of 320 K.
The inlet velocity is a fixedValue of (0.01 0 0) m/s.
The outlet pressure is a fixedValue of 0 Pa.
All other walls have noSlip velocity boundary conditions.
All other walls and the outlet have zeroGradient temperature boundary conditions.
All walls and the inlet have zeroGradient pressure boundary conditions.
The outlet has zeroGradient velocity boundary conditions.
