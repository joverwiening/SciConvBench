The simulation will compute thermo-elastic deformation of a solid aluminum block.
The block has dimensions of 1 meter by 1 meter by 1 meter.
The material properties for aluminum are: Young's Modulus E = 69e9 Pa, Poisson's Ratio nu = 0.33, density rho = 2700 kg/m^3, thermal expansion coefficient alpha = 2.3e-5 1/K, and reference temperature T0 = 300 K.
The solver used for the simulation is solidDisplacementFoam.
One face of the block is fixed in all directions (displacement = (0 0 0)).
The face opposite to the fixed face is heated to a uniform temperature of 400 K.
The fixed face has a zero gradient boundary condition for temperature.
All other faces have zero gradient boundary conditions for both displacement and temperature.
A temperature field, either prescribed or solved separately, is available and used to drive thermal strain.
The goal of the simulation is to compute the displacement and stress within the aluminum block due to thermal expansion.
