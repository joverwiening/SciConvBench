Set up a 2D dam break simulation using the pimpleFoam solver.
The simulation domain is a 4 meter wide by 2 meter high rectangle.
The initial water column is 1 meter wide and 2 meters high, located at the left side of the domain.
The domain is initially filled with air outside the water column.
Gravity is set to (0 -9.81 0).
The controlDict endTime is 5s.
The setFields utility initializes alpha.water = 1 for the water column and alpha.water = 0 for the air.
The transportProperties dictionary contains entries for two phases, 'water' and 'air'.
The density of water is 1000 kg/m^3.
The kinematic viscosity of water is 1e-6 m^2/s.
The density of air is 1.225 kg/m^3.
The kinematic viscosity of air is 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
