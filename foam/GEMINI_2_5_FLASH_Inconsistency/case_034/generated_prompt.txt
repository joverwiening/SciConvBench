Set up a 2D dam break simulation using the interFoam solver for a two-phase (water-air) system.
The computational domain is 2 meters long in the x-direction and 1 meter high in the y-direction, with a single cell in the z-direction.
Gravity is defined in 'constant/g' as (0 -9.81 0).
In 'blockMeshDict', the front and back patches are defined as 'frontAndBack' with 'type wall'.
In the '0' directory, for the velocity field (U), the 'frontAndBack' patches are assigned the 'noSlip' boundary condition.
In the '0' directory, for the pressure field (p_rgh) and the water phase fraction field (alpha.water), the 'frontAndBack' patches are assigned the 'zeroGradient' boundary condition.
The initial water column is defined using setFieldsDict.
The initial water column has a width of 0.5 meters and a height of 0.8 meters.
The initial water column is positioned at the bottom-left corner of the domain, extending from x=0 to x=0.5 meters and from y=0 to y=0.8 meters.
