Configure a 2D simulation of flow past a cylinder using pimpleFoam for a transient, incompressible, laminar case. The mesh is one cell thick. The 'frontAndBack' patches in 'constant/polyMesh/boundary' should be defined with 'type empty'. In the '0/U' file, the boundary condition for 'frontAndBack' must also be 'type empty;'. All other boundary conditions (inlet, outlet, cylinderWall) are set appropriately for a flow-past-cylinder case. (Alternatively, if 'symmetryPlane' is used in the boundary file, the BC in 0/U must also be 'type symmetryPlane').
