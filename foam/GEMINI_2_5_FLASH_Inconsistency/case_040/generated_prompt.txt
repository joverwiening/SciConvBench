The simulation will model incompressible water flow in a 2D channel extruded one cell in the spanwise direction with frontAndBack boundaries set to empty.
The OpenFOAM solver `pimpleFoam` will be used for the flow simulation.
The water properties are defined by a kinematic viscosity `nu = 1e-6 m^2/s`.
The channel has a length of 1 meter and a height of 0.1 meters.
The top wall of the channel is designated as the moving wall.
Mesh motion will be handled by `dynamicMotionSolverFvMesh` using `displacementLaplacian` in `constant/dynamicMeshDict`.
Motion on the `movingWall` will be prescribed via `0/pointDisplacement` using an oscillating displacement.
The oscillation frequency of the moving wall is 1 Hz.
The amplitude of the moving wall oscillation is 0.01 meters.
The direction of the moving wall oscillation is (0 1 0).
All other boundaries will have their point displacement set to `fixedValue uniform (0 0 0)`.
For velocity (`0/U`), the inlet will have a `fixedValue uniform (0.1 0 0)`.
The outlet velocity (`0/U`) will be `zeroGradient`.
Stationary walls will have a `noSlip` velocity boundary condition.
The `movingWall` velocity (`0/U`) will use `movingWallVelocity`.
For pressure (`0/p`), the outlet will have a `fixedValue uniform 0`.
The inlet and all walls will have a `zeroGradient` pressure boundary condition.
The simulation end time is 20 seconds.
