The simulation will use the buoyantBoussinesqPimpleFoam solver.
The computational domain is a 3D box with dimensions 1m in X, 1m in Y, and 0.1m in Z.
The height of the domain is oriented along the Z-axis.
The fluid has a Prandtl number of 0.71.
In constant/transportProperties, the Boussinesq approximation is used with a thermal expansion coefficient beta of 0.00341, a reference temperature T0 of 300K, a kinematic viscosity nu of 1.5e-5 m^2/s, and a Prandtl number Pr of 0.71.
The gravity vector in constant/g is defined as `g (0 0 -9.81);`.
For boundary conditions in the 0/ directory:
The bottom wall at z=0 has a fixed temperature of 310K.
The top wall at z=0.1 has a fixed temperature of 300K.
The side walls are periodic for all fields.
The velocity U has a noSlip boundary condition on the top and bottom walls.
The simulation is transient and aims to capture the formation of convection cells.
The initial temperature field for the entire domain is a linear profile varying from 310K at z=0 to 300K at z=0.1, with a small random perturbation of +/- 0.01K applied throughout the domain.
The initial velocity field for the entire domain is zero.
