The simulation will be a conjugate heat transfer (CHT) analysis.
The chtMultiRegionSimpleFoam solver will be used.
The setup includes a solid silicon chip region and a fluid air region.
The chip generates heat, which is specified via fvOptions.
The entire assembly is enclosed in a housing.
The outer surface of the housing, named 'outerCasing', is perfectly insulated.
Air enters at a fixed temperature of 293 K.
Air exits at an outlet boundary.
The inlet air velocity is a uniform 2 m/s.
