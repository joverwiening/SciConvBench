The simulation will model the separation of three immiscible fluids (oil, water, and gas) in a horizontal gravity separator using the VOF method.
The solver specified in controlDict will be interFoam.
The phases are defined in transportProperties with their respective densities and viscosities.
The density of water is 1000 kg/m^3.
The density of oil is 850 kg/m^3.
The density of gas is 1.2 kg/m^3.
The viscosity of water is 0.001 Pa.s.
The viscosity of oil is 0.01 Pa.s.
The viscosity of gas is 1.8e-5 Pa.s.
The horizontal gravity separator has dimensions of 1 meter in length, 0.5 meters in height, and 0.5 meters in width.
The initial state is a stratified mixture, which is set using setFieldsDict.
The alpha.water field represents the volume fraction of water.
The alpha.oil field represents the volume fraction of oil.
The alpha.gas field represents the volume fraction of gas.
The initial vertical layering consists of gas in the top third of the separator height, oil in the middle third, and water in the bottom third.
The simulation will track the interface between the three fluids over time as they separate under gravity.
Gravity is set to (0 -9.81 0).
