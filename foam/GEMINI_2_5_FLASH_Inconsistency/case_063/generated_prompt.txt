The simulation will model the transient von Kármán vortex street behind a cylinder in a 2D channel using the `pimpleFoam` solver.
The 2D mesh will be extruded one cell in the z-direction, with `frontAndBack` boundaries set to `empty`.
The kinematic viscosity is set to `nu = 1e-3 m^2/s`.
The channel length is 30 meters and the channel height is 8 meters.
The cylinder diameter is 1 meter, positioned with its center at (10, 4, 0.5) meters.
The mesh will have 30 cells across the cylinder diameter.
The boundary conditions for velocity (U) are: inlet `fixedValue uniform (0.1 0 0)`, outlet `zeroGradient`, cylinder `noSlip`, top and bottom walls `slip`, and `frontAndBack` `empty`.
The boundary conditions for pressure (p) are: inlet `zeroGradient`, outlet `fixedValue uniform 0`, cylinder, top, and bottom walls `zeroGradient`, and `frontAndBack` `empty`.
