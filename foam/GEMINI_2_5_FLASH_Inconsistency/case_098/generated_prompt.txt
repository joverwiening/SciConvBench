A 2D axisymmetric simulation of swirling flow in a pipe will be set up.
The simpleFoam solver will be used.
A 5-degree wedge mesh will be created using blockMeshDict.
The pipe will have an inner radius of 0.05 meters.
The pipe will have a length of 1.0 meter.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid will be 1e-6 m^2/s.
The patches are defined as follows: inlet is type patch, outlet is type patch, wall is type wall, front is type symmetryPlane, and back is type symmetryPlane.
For the velocity field U, the inlet condition will be a fixedValue with an axial velocity of 1.0 m/s and a tangential velocity of 0.5 m/s.
The velocity field U will have noSlip on the wall.
The velocity field U will have zeroGradient on the outlet.
The velocity field U will have symmetryPlane on the front and back.
For the pressure field p, the inlet condition will be zeroGradient.
For the pressure field p, the outlet condition will be a fixedValue of 0 Pa.
