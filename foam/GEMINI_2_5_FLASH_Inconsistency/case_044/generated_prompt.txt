The simulation will use the interFoam solver.
The computational domain is 1 meter long in the X-direction, 1 meter high in the Y-direction, and 1 meter thick in the Z-direction.
The domain will be discretized with 100 cells in the X-direction, 100 cells in the Y-direction, and 1 cell in the Z-direction.
A column of water is initialized in the region 0 < x < 0.2 and 0 < y < 0.5.
The rest of the domain is initialized as air.
The simulation will model the collapse of the water column under gravity.
The acceleration due to gravity is defined as g (0 0 -9.81); in the constant/g file.
The alpha.water field is initialized using setFieldsDict.
The alpha.water field is set to 1 within the water column region and 0 elsewhere.
The top boundary is an open atmosphere, using the pressureInletOutletVelocity boundary condition.
All other boundaries are walls, using the noSlip boundary condition.
