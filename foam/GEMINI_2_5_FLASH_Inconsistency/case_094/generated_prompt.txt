Simulate the steady-state, compressible flow of natural gas through a pipe with a porous baffle.
The gas is modeled as a perfect gas with a specific heat ratio of 1.31 and a kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The pipe has a diameter of 0.1 m and a length of 1.0 m.
The porous baffle has a thickness of 0.01 m and is located axially at 0.5 m from the pipe inlet.
The porous baffle is defined with a Darcy coefficient of 1e10 1/m^2 and a Forchheimer coefficient of 1000 1/m.
The inlet total pressure is 2e5 Pa and the inlet total temperature is 293.15 K.
The outlet static pressure is 1e5 Pa.
The porous zone is defined in the fvOptions dictionary.
The kinematic viscosity 'nu' is defined in transportProperties.
The solver to be used is porousSimpleFoam.
