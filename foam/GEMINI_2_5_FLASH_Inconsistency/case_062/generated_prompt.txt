Simulate the static deflection of a 2D cantilever beam using linear elasticity.
The beam is 1.0 m long and 0.1 m high.
The mesh is structured with 100 cells along the length and 10 cells along the height.
The material is aluminum with a Young's Modulus (E) of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's Ratio (nu) of 0.33.
The OpenFOAM solver `solidDisplacementFoam` will be used.
The `application` in `system/controlDict` will be set to `solidDisplacementFoam`.
At x=0 (fixedEnd), a `fixedValue` boundary condition of `uniform (0 0 0)` is applied.
At x=1 (loadEnd), a `tractionDisplacement` boundary condition is applied with `traction uniform (0 -10000 0)` and `pressure uniform 0`.
On the top and bottom surfaces, a `zeroGradient` boundary condition is applied.
On the front and back planes, an `empty` boundary condition is applied.
