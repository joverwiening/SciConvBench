Simulate natural convection in a 2D square cavity with a side length of 1 meter.
The cavity is filled with air.
The left wall has a fixed temperature of 300 K.
The right wall has a fixed temperature of 290 K.
The top wall is adiabatic.
The bottom wall is adiabatic.
Gravity acts downwards in the -y direction with a magnitude of 9.81 m/s^2.
The Boussinesq approximation is used for buoyancy with a reference temperature of 295 K.
All walls have no-slip velocity boundary conditions.
The solver to be used is buoyantBoussinesqPimpleFoam.
