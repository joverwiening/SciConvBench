The simulation will be an axisymmetric simulation of swirling flow inside a pipe.
The `simpleFoam` solver will be used.
The mesh will be a 2D wedge geometry.
The wedge angle will be 5 degrees in the tangential direction.
The mesh will be one cell thick.
The inner radius of the pipe will be 0.05 meters.
The length of the pipe will be 1.0 meter.
The `system/blockMeshDict` will define the axial patches as `axis`.
The `system/blockMeshDict` will define the pipe surface as `wall`.
The `system/blockMeshDict` will define the inlet and outlet patches.
The front and back planes of the wedge will be defined as `empty`.
The `blockMeshDict` will include the provided definition for the `front` and `back` `empty` patches.
The fluid density will be 1000 kg/m^3.
The fluid kinematic viscosity will be 1e-6 m^2/s.
The turbulence model will be `k-epsilon`.
The inlet axial velocity profile will be uniform with a magnitude of 1.0 m/s.
The inlet tangential velocity profile will be a solid-body rotation, increasing linearly with radius to a maximum of 1.0 m/s at the pipe wall.
The outlet boundary condition for velocity will be `zeroGradient`.
The outlet boundary condition for pressure will be `fixedValue` of 0 Pa gauge.
The initial velocity field will be uniform axial velocity of 1.0 m/s and zero tangential velocity.
