A transient 2D incompressible flow simulation will be performed using the pimpleFoam solver.
The simulation will model vortex shedding behind a circular cylinder at a Reynolds number of 100.
The fluid kinematic viscosity is 0.01 m^2/s.
The computational domain is a rectangular channel with dimensions 25 m in length and 8 m in height.
The circular cylinder has a diameter of 1 m.
The cylinder's center is located at (5 m, 4.5 m) within the channel, where the inlet is at x=0 and the bottom wall is at y=0.
The inlet boundary, named 'inlet' (at x=0), has a fixed uniform velocity of U = (1 0 0) m/s.
The outlet boundary, named 'outlet' (at x=25 m), has a zeroGradient condition for velocity U and a fixedValue of 0 Pa for pressure p.
The top and bottom boundaries, named 'topAndBottom' (at y=8 m and y=0 m respectively), have a slip boundary condition.
The cylinder surface boundary, named 'cylinder', has a noSlip boundary condition.
The front and back boundaries, named 'frontAndBack', have an empty boundary condition for 2D simulation.
The simulation will run from t=0 s to t=200 s with a fixed time step (deltaT) of 0.1 s.
The primary goal is to observe the von Kármán vortex street and calculate the Strouhal number.
