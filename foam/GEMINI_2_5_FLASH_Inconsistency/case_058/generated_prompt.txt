Model the transient injection of a high-pressure, compressible gas into a tank of water.
The simulation will use the interFoam solver.
The gas phase will be defined using the perfectGas equation of state.
The water phase will be defined as incompressible.
The gas will be injected at Mach 0.8.
The gas injection nozzle will have a diameter of 0.01 meters.
The initial pressure of the injected gas will be 10 MPa.
The initial temperature of the injected gas will be 300 K.
The specific heat ratio of the gas will be 1.4.
The water tank will have a diameter of 1 meter and a height of 2 meters.
The initial pressure of the water in the tank will be 0.1 MPa.
The simulation's goal is to observe the gas jet penetration and expansion in the water.
