A 2D bubble column simulation will be configured using twoPhaseEulerFoam.
The column is 0.5m wide in the X-direction and 2m high in the Y-direction.
The column is initially filled with water.
The phases are air as the dispersed phase and water as the continuous phase.
The gravity vector in constant/g is set to `g (0 -9.81 0);`.
The bottom boundary is an inlet patch where `alpha.air` is `fixedValue 1` and `U.air` is `fixedValue (0 0.1 0)`.
The top boundary is an outlet.
The side walls are `noSlip`.
The front and back patches are `empty`.
The simulation will model the rise of air bubbles due to buoyancy.
