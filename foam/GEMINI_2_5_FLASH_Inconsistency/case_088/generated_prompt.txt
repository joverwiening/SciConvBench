The simulation models a steady-state buoyant flow in a solar chimney.
The solver used is buoyantSimpleFoam.
The computational domain is a 2D rectangular channel, 1 meter wide and 10 meters high, representing the solar chimney.
Air is heated at the bottom of the chimney and rises due to density changes.
The Boussinesq model is used to model buoyancy.
The thermophysical properties are defined as: thermoType hePsiThermo, specie molWeight 28.9, equationOfState Boussinesq, Cp 1005, Hf 0, mu 1.8e-05, Pr 0.7.
Boussinesq coefficients are TRef 300 K, beta 0.00333, and rhoRef 1.176 kg/m^3.
The gravity vector is (0 0 -9.81) m/s^2, acting downwards in the z-direction.
The bottom surface of the chimney is maintained at a fixed temperature of 310 K.
The side walls of the chimney are adiabatic.
The top and bottom surfaces of the chimney are open boundaries, allowing air to enter and exit freely at ambient pressure.
The ambient temperature is 300 K.
A k-epsilon turbulence model is employed for turbulence closure.
