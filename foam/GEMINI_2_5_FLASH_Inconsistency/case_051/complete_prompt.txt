Simulate the transient start-up flow of a Bingham plastic fluid in a 2D pipe. The solver to be used is `nonNewtonianIcoFoam`. The domain is a rectangle of 1.0m length and 0.1m diameter. Use `blockMesh` to create a mesh with 100 cells in the axial (x) direction and 20 cells in the radial (y) direction. The front and back patches should be of type `empty`. The boundary conditions are: an inlet with a fixed velocity of `(0.1 0 0)` m/s, an outlet with `zeroGradient` for velocity and a fixed pressure of 0, and `noSlip` walls. The simulation should run from t=0 to t=5s. In `constant/transportProperties`, define the fluid as a Bingham plastic with the following dictionary:

transportModel  BinghamPlastic;
rho             1000;
tau0            10;
mu0             0.1;
muInf           0.01;
