Simulate the transient start-up flow of a Bingham plastic fluid in a 2D axisymmetric pipe using the `nonNewtonianIcoFoam` solver.
The computational domain is a rectangle with a length of 1.0m in the axial (x) direction and a radius of 0.05m in the radial (y) direction.
Generate the mesh using `blockMesh` with 100 cells in the axial (x) direction and 20 cells in the radial (y) direction.
The front and back patches of the domain should be of type `empty`.
The initial velocity field is `(0 0 0)` m/s and the initial pressure field is 0 Pa.
The boundary conditions are: a fixed velocity of `(0.1 0 0)` m/s at the inlet, `zeroGradient` for velocity and a fixed pressure of 0 Pa at the outlet, and `noSlip` conditions at the walls.
The simulation should run from t=0s to t=5s.
The maximum time step for the simulation is 0.005s.
In the `constant/transportProperties` dictionary, define the fluid as a Bingham plastic with `transportModel BinghamPlastic;`, `rho 1000;`, `tau0 10;`, `mu0 0.1;`, and `muInf 0.01;`.
