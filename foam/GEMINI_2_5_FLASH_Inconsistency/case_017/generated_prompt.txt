The simulation is a 2D inviscid laminar oblique-shock simulation.
The simulation domain is a rectangular channel extruded one cell in z.
The domain extends from (0, 0, 0) to (4.1, 1, 0.1).
The computational grid is 60 cells in x, 30 cells in y, and 1 cell in z.
The frontAndBack patches are set to empty.
The inlet patch is on the left boundary.
The outlet patch is on the right boundary.
The top boundary is a wall patch.
The bottom boundary is a symmetryPlane patch.
The rhoCentralFoam solver is used.
Momentum transport is laminar.
Thermodynamics are modeled as an ideal gas.
The flow is inviscid with Cp=2.5, Pr=1, and viscosity=0.
The ratio of specific heats (gamma) is 1.4.
The molecular weight is 11640.3.
The heat of formation (Hf) is 0.
Initial fields are set with U=(2.9 0 0), p=1, and T=1.
The inlet boundary condition for velocity is U=(2.9 0 0).
The inlet boundary condition for pressure is p=1.
The inlet boundary condition for temperature is T=1.
The top wall applies a noSlip velocity boundary condition.
The time step (deltaT) is 0.0025.
The simulation ends at time (endTime) 10.
Data is written every 1 unit of simulation time (writeInterval).
