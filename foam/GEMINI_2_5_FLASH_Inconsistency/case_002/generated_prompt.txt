The simulation will be a two-dimensional Rayleigh Benard convection cell in a rectangular cavity.
The cavity dimensions are 9 units wide in the x-direction and 1 unit tall in the y-direction.
The domain will be extruded a single cell in the z-direction.
The front and back patches will be treated as empty to enforce a 2D simulation.
The mesh will consist of 90 cells in the x-direction, 10 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The buoyantFoam solver will be used.
Gravity will act downward in the negative y-direction with a magnitude of 9.81 m/s².
The reference state will be air-like with a reference density (rho0) of 1.0 kg/m³ and a reference temperature (T0) of 300 K.
The thermal expansion coefficient (beta) will be 3.3e-3 1/K.
The kinematic viscosity (mu) will be 1.5e-5 m²/s.
The thermal diffusivity will be 2.1e-5 m²/s, resulting in a Prandtl number (Pr) of approximately 0.7.
The temperature of the bottom wall will be fixed at 305 K.
The temperature of the top wall will be fixed at 295 K.
The vertical side walls will have a zeroGradient boundary condition for temperature.
No-slip velocity conditions will be applied on all solid walls.
The flow will be initialized at rest with a uniform velocity field of (0 0 0) m/s.
The initial temperature field will be uniform at 300 K.
The initial internalField for k will be uniform 1e-5.
The initial internalField for epsilon will be uniform 1e-5.
The initial internalField for nut will be uniform 0.
The initial internalField for alphat will be uniform 0.
The initial internalField for p and p_rgh will be uniform 0.
A fixed timestep (deltaT) of 1 second will be used.
AdjustTimeStep, maxCo, and maxDeltaT will not be enabled.
The simulation will run up to 1000 seconds of physical time.
Results will be written every 50 steps.
The k-epsilon turbulence model will be used.
The constant/momentumTransport dictionary will be set to simulationType RAS with model kEpsilon, turbulence on, and printCoeffs on.
The constant/physicalProperties dictionary will use heRhoThermo with equationOfState Boussinesq.
The molWeight will be 29, rho0 1.0, T0 300, beta 3.3e-3, Cv 714.3, Hf 0, mu 1.5e-5, and Pr 0.714.
In the 0/k file, kqRWallFunction will be used on walls and empty on front/back.
In the 0/epsilon file, epsilonWallFunction will be used on walls and empty on front/back.
In the 0/nut file, nutkWallFunction will be used on walls and empty on front/back.
In the 0/alphat file, compressible::alphatJayatillekeWallFunction will be used on walls with Prt 0.85, and empty on front/back.
In the 0/U file, noSlip will be used on all solid walls and empty on front/back.
In the 0/T file, bottom will be fixedValue 305, top fixedValue 295, sides zeroGradient, and front/back empty.
In the 0/p file, walls will be calculated and front/back empty.
In the 0/p_rgh file, walls will be fixedFluxPressure and front/back empty.
In the system/fvSolution dictionary, p_rgh and (U|e|k|epsilon) solvers will have a tolerance of 1e-8 and a relTol of 0.01.
The corresponding *Final solvers will have a relTol of 0.
