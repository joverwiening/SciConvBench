The simulation is a 2D gas–liquid bubble column using multiphaseEulerFoam (Euler–Euler).
The rectangular computational domain spans x from 0 to 0.15 m, y from 0 to 1.00 m, and z from 0 to 0.10 m.
The height of the column is along the +y direction, and it is thin in the z direction.
A structured hex mesh is used with 25 cells in x, 75 cells in y, and 1 cell in z.
The mesh uses simpleGrading (1 1 1).
The blockMeshDict defines an inlet patch, an outlet patch, and walls patches.
The inlet patch corresponds to the min Y face (y=0).
The outlet patch corresponds to the max Y face (y=1.00).
The walls patches correspond to the min X face (x=0) and max X face (x=0.15).
The min Z face (z=0) and max Z face (z=0.10) are treated as empty for 2D simulation.
The continuous liquid phase is water, modeled with heRhoThermo.
The water phase uses the rPolynomial equation of state with C coefficients (0.001278 -2.1055e-06 3.9689e-09 4.3772e-13 -2.0225e-16).
The molecular weight of water is 18.
The specific heat capacity Cv of water is 4195 J/(kg·K).
The dynamic viscosity of water is 3.645e-4 Pa·s.
The Prandtl number for water is 2.289.
The dispersed gas phase is air, modeled with heRhoThermo.
The air phase uses the perfectGas equation of state.
The molecular weight of air is 28.9.
The specific heat capacity Cp of air is 1007 J/(kg·K).
The dynamic viscosity of air is 1.84e-05 Pa·s.
The Prandtl number for air is 0.7.
Gravity is set to (0 -9.81 0) m/s².
Bubble size models use an isothermal diameter d0 = 3e-3 m for air.
Bubble size models use a constant diameter d = 1e-4 m for water.
Momentum transport is configured as laminar for both phases with simulationType laminar in the respective momentumTransport files.
The multiphase interaction models from phaseProperties define phases as (air water).
Water is set as the reference phase.
The residualAlpha for both phases is 1e-6.
Blending uses a linear scheme.
The minFullyContinuousAlpha is 0.7.
The minPartlyContinuousAlpha is 0.3 for default blending or 0.5 for drag blending.
Surface tension between air and water is constant with sigma = 0.07 N/m.
The drag model uses SchillerNaumann for air_dispersedIn_water, water_dispersedIn_air, and air_segregatedWith_water configurations.
Virtual mass uses constantCoefficient with Cvm = 0.5 for both directions.
Heat transfer employs the RanzMarshall model for both directions with residualAlpha 1e-4.
Initial conditions at t = 0 set the velocity of water to uniform (0 0 0) m/s.
Initial conditions at t = 0 set the velocity of air to uniform (0 0.1 0) m/s.
Initial conditions at t = 0 set the volume fraction of water to uniform 1.
Initial conditions at t = 0 set the pressure p_rgh to uniform 1e5 Pa.
Initial conditions at t = 0 set the temperature of air to uniform 300 K.
Initial conditions at t = 0 set the temperature of water to uniform 350 K.
Phase distribution uses default field values where volume fraction of air is 1 and water is 0.
In a box region from (0 0 0) to (0.15 0.701 0.1), the initial volume fraction of air is 0 and water is 1.
At the outlet patch, the velocity of air is fixedValue (0 0.1 0) m/s.
At the outlet patch, the velocity of water is fixedValue (0 0 0) m/s.
At the outlet patch, the volume fraction of air is fixedValue 0.5.
At the outlet patch, the volume fraction of water is fixedValue 0.5.
At the outlet patch, the pressure p_rgh has a zeroGradient boundary condition.
At the outlet patch, the temperature of air is fixedValue 300 K.
At the outlet patch, the temperature of water is fixedValue 350 K.
At the walls patches, the velocity of air is fixedValue (0 0 0) m/s.
At the walls patches, the velocity of water is fixedValue (0 0 0) m/s.
At the walls patches, the volume fraction of air has a zeroGradient boundary condition.
At the walls patches, the volume fraction of water has a zeroGradient boundary condition.
At the walls patches, the pressure p_rgh has a fixedFluxPressure boundary condition.
At the walls patches, the temperature of air has a zeroGradient boundary condition.
At the walls patches, the temperature of water has a zeroGradient boundary condition.
At the inlet patch, the velocity of air is fixedValue (0 0.1 0) m/s.
At the inlet patch, the velocity of water is fixedValue (0 0 0) m/s.
At the inlet patch, the volume fraction of air is fixedValue 0.5.
At the inlet patch, the volume fraction of water is fixedValue 0.5.
At the inlet patch, the pressure p_rgh has a totalPressure boundary condition with a uniform value of 1e5 Pa.
At the inlet patch, the temperature of air is fixedValue 300 K.
At the inlet patch, the temperature of water is fixedValue 350 K.
