The simulation will use the simpleFoam solver.
The simulation will model steady-state, incompressible, turbulent flow and heat transfer.
The geometry is a 2D pipe with a diameter of 0.1m and a length of 2m.
The k-epsilon turbulence model will be used.
The fluid is air with standard properties.
The inlet velocity is uniform at 1 m/s in the x-direction.
The inlet temperature is 300 K.
The outlet is at a fixed gauge pressure of 0 Pa.
The pipe wall is perfectly insulated (adiabatic).
The simulation is 2D, so the front and back patches are of type 'empty'.
The inlet boundary condition for turbulent kinetic energy (k) is a fixed value of 0.00375 m^2/s^2.
The inlet boundary condition for turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) is a fixed value of 0.00543 m^2/s^3.
The wall boundary conditions for k and epsilon will use standard wall functions.
The outlet boundary conditions for k and epsilon will be zeroGradient.
