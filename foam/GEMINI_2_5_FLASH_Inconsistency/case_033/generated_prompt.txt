Perform a 2D simulation of heat conduction through a square solid plate.
Use the laplacianFoam solver.
The simulation domain is 1 meter by 1 meter.
The plate is one cell thick in the z-direction.
The goal is to find the steady-state temperature distribution.
In blockMeshDict, define four side patches named 'left', 'right', 'top', and 'bottom'.
In blockMeshDict, define 'frontAndBack' patches.
Set the 'frontAndBack' patches to 'type wall'.
In the '0/T' file, set the 'left' wall to a fixedValue of 400 K.
In the '0/T' file, set the 'right' wall to a fixedValue of 300 K.
In the '0/T' file, set the 'top' wall to a 'zeroGradient' boundary condition.
In the '0/T' file, set the 'bottom' wall to a 'zeroGradient' boundary condition.
In the '0/T' file, apply a 'zeroGradient' boundary condition to the 'frontAndBack' patches.
The mesh resolution will be 50 cells in the x-direction.
The mesh resolution will be 50 cells in the y-direction.
The mesh resolution will be 1 cell in the z-direction.
