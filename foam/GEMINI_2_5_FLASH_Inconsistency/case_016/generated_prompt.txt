The simulation is a 2D inviscid oblique-shock simulation.
The simulation uses the rhoCentralFoam solver.
The computational domain is a rectangle from (0, 0, 0) to (4.1, 1, 0.1).
The domain is extruded one cell in the z-direction.
The mesh consists of 60x30x1 cells.
The frontAndBack patches are set to empty.
The fluid is an ideal gas with Cp=2.5, Pr=1, and zero viscosity.
The molecular weight is 11640.3.
The enthalpy of formation (Hf) is 0.
Initial conditions are U=(2.9 0 0), p=1, and T=1.
The inlet (left) patch has fixedValue boundary conditions for velocity (2.9 0 0), pressure 1, and temperature 1.
The outlet (right) patch has zeroGradient boundary conditions for velocity, pressure, and temperature.
The top (wall) patch has fixedValue boundary conditions for velocity (2.61933 -0.50632 0) and temperature 1.25853.
The top (wall) patch has a zeroGradient boundary condition for pressure.
The bottom (symmetryPlane) patch has symmetryPlane boundary conditions for velocity, pressure, and temperature.
The simulation time step (deltaT) is 0.0025.
The simulation end time is 10.
Data is written every 1 unit of simulation time.
