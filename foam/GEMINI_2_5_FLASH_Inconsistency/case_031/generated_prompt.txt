The simulation will use the icoFoam solver.
The simulation will model 2D incompressible laminar Poiseuille flow.
The computational domain is a rectangular channel 0.5 m long in the x-direction and 0.1 m high in the y-direction.
The mesh will be one cell thick in the z-direction.
The blockMeshDict utility will define the geometry and patches.
The patches are inlet, outlet, topWall, bottomWall, and frontAndBack.
The frontAndBack patches will be defined with 'type wall;' in blockMeshDict.
The mesh will consist of 150 cells in the x-direction, 30 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
In the '0/U' file, the inlet patch will have a parabolic velocity profile with a maximum velocity of 1 m/s.
In the '0/U' file, the outlet patch will have a zeroGradient boundary condition.
In the '0/U' file, the topWall, bottomWall, and frontAndBack patches will have noSlip boundary conditions.
In the '0/p' file, the outlet patch will have a fixedValue of 0.
In the '0/p' file, the inlet, topWall, bottomWall, and frontAndBack patches will have zeroGradient boundary conditions.
The simulation will run for a total physical time of 5 seconds.
