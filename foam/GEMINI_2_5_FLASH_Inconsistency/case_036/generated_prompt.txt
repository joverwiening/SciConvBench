Simulate the quasi-static deflection of a 3D steel cantilever beam using linear elasticity.
The beam is 1 m long, 0.1 m high, and 0.1 m wide.
One end (fixed) is clamped, and the free end (loaded) has a downward traction load.
In constant/mechanicalProperties, use steel properties: Young's modulus E = 200e9 Pa and Poisson ratio nu = 0.3.
In 0/D, the fixed face has fixedValue uniform (0 0 0).
In 0/D, the loaded face has tractionDisplacement with traction uniform (0 -1000 0) and pressure uniform 0.
In 0/D, all other faces have zeroGradient.
In system/controlDict, set application solidDisplacementFoam.
In system/controlDict, set endTime 1.
In system/controlDict, set deltaT 0.1.
