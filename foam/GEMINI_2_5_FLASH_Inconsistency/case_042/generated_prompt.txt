The simulation will use the rhoCentralFoam solver.
The fluid is air, treated as a perfect gas.
The flow is supersonic at Mach 2 over a 10-degree wedge.
The computational domain is a 2D trapezoidal prism extruded one cell in the z-direction.
The domain extends from x=0 to x=2 meters.
The bottom boundary of the domain is defined by y = x * tan(10 degrees).
The top boundary of the domain is at y=2 meters.
The depth of the domain in the z-direction is 0.01 meters.
The freestream inlet conditions at x=0 are: pressure p = 101325 Pa, temperature T = 300 K, and velocity U = (686 0 0) m/s.
The thermophysical properties will use a perfect-gas setup (e.g., hePsiThermo with equationOfState perfectGas).
The boundary conditions are defined as follows:
The inlet boundary at x=0 has fixed values for U, p, and T.
The outlet boundary at x=2 meters has waveTransmissive or zeroGradient for p and T, and zeroGradient for U.
The wedge (bottom boundary) and top boundary have slip wall conditions.
The front and back boundaries (z=0 and z=0.01m) have empty conditions.
The system/controlDict will specify application rhoCentralFoam.
