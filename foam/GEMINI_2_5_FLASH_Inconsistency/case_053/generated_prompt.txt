The simulation will use the `interFoam` solver.
The simulation will model the mixing of two phases, 'water' and 'sludge', in a 2D tank.
The 2D tank dimensions are 1 meter by 1 meter.
The direction of gravity for the simulation is (0 -9.81 0) m/s^2.
The tank is initially half-filled with sludge at the bottom and water occupying the top half.
The boundary conditions for the tank walls are `noSlip` for velocity, `zeroGradient` for pressure, and `zeroGradient` for the phase fraction.
The `transportProperties` dictionary is configured as follows:
phases (water sludge);
water
{
    transportModel Newtonian;
    nu              1e-6;
    rho             1000;
}
sludge
{
    transportModel BinghamPlastic;
    tau0            15;
    mu0             0.1;
    rho             1500;
}
sigma 0.07;
