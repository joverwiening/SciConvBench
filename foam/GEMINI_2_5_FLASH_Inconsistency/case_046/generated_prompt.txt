Set up a transient simulation of turbulent flow over a 2D cylinder in a channel using pimpleFoam.
The simulation will use only the Large Eddy Simulation (LES) model.
The `constant/turbulenceProperties` dictionary will specify `simulationType LES;`.
The `LES` sub-dictionary will define `LESModel Smagorinsky;` and `delta cubeRootVol;`.
The `RAS` sub-dictionary will be removed from the `constant/turbulenceProperties` file.
Standard air properties for an incompressible fluid will be used.
The inlet velocity will be 10 m/s.
The cylinder and channel walls will have `noSlip` boundary conditions.
The 2D cylinder will have a diameter of 0.1 meters.
The 2D channel will have a height of 1 meter.
The 2D channel will have a length of 3 meters.
The channel outlet will have a `zeroGradient` boundary condition for velocity and a `fixedValue` of 0 Pa for pressure.
