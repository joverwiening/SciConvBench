The simulation will model the transient von Kármán vortex street for 2D flow past a circular cylinder.
The Reynolds number for the flow is 150.
The application solver used will be pimpleFoam.
The simulation will use a transient time step.
The simulation will start from startTime at time 0.
The simulation will stop at endTime at time 200.
The initial deltaT is 0.005.
Time step adjustment is enabled with a maximum Courant number of 0.9.
Results will be written at runTime with a writeInterval of 1.
Results will be written frequently enough to resolve the shedding.
The circular cylinder has a diameter of 1 meter.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 1e-5 m^2/s.
The inlet boundary condition is a fixed velocity corresponding to Reynolds number 150 and a zero gradient for pressure.
The outlet boundary condition is a zero gradient for velocity and a fixed pressure of 0 Pa.
The top and bottom boundaries of the computational domain are slip walls.
The dimensions of the computational domain are not specified.
