The simulation will model steady, incompressible, laminar flow of water.
The kinematic viscosity of water is `nu = 1e-6 m^2/s`.
The geometry consists of a 2D channel with a length of 1 meter and a height of 0.1 meter.
A rigid, stationary, rectangular obstacle is placed within the channel.
The obstacle has a length of 0.05 meters and a height of 0.02 meters.
The obstacle is positioned vertically centered in the channel, 0.3 meters from the inlet.
The mesh is a 2D mesh extruded one cell in the spanwise direction.
The `frontAndBack` boundary condition type is `empty`.
The solver used for the simulation is `simpleFoam`.
For the velocity field `U`, the inlet boundary condition is `fixedValue uniform (0.1 0 0)`.
The outlet boundary condition for `U` is `zeroGradient`.
The walls and obstacle boundary condition for `U` is `noSlip`.
The `frontAndBack` boundary condition for `U` is `empty`.
For the pressure field `p`, the inlet boundary condition is `zeroGradient`.
The outlet boundary condition for `p` is `fixedValue uniform 0`.
The walls and obstacle boundary condition for `p` is `zeroGradient`.
The `frontAndBack` boundary condition for `p` is `empty`.
In `system/controlDict`, the `application` entry is set to `simpleFoam`.
