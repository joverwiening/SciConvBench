The simulation models a steady-state, buoyancy-driven flow in a vertical heated pipe using the buoyantSimpleFoam solver.
The pipe has a length of 2m, aligned with the Z-axis, and a diameter of 0.1m.
Air enters at the bottom (z=0) at 300K and is heated by the pipe wall.
The pipe wall is maintained at a constant temperature of 350K.
The goal is to observe the natural convection flow established due to density differences.
Gravity is enabled and set to `g (0 0 -9.81);` in the `constant/g` dictionary.
The thermophysical properties are for air, using the `perfectGas` equation of state.
The inlet boundary condition for U is `pressureInletOutletVelocity`.
The inlet boundary condition for T is `fixedValue 300`.
The outlet boundary condition for U is `zeroGradient`.
The outlet boundary condition for T is `zeroGradient`.
The outlet boundary condition for pressure is `fixedValue 0 Pa`.
The wall boundary condition for U is `noSlip`.
The wall boundary condition for T is `fixedValue 350`.
