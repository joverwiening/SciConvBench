The system will model a flexible flap in a channel with fluid-structure interaction.
The simulation will be 2D, extruded one cell in z with `frontAndBack` empty boundary conditions.
The channel length is 1.0 m and its height is 0.2 m.
The flap length is 0.05 m and its thickness is 0.005 m.
The flap is attached to the bottom wall at x = 0.4 m.
The fluid is incompressible water with a kinematic viscosity (`nu`) of 1e-6 m^2/s.
The fluid inlet boundary condition is a fixed velocity `U = (1 0 0)`.
The fluid outlet boundary condition is a fixed pressure `p = 0`.
The channel walls will use a `noSlip` boundary condition for the fluid.
The solid flap is linear elastic with a Young's Modulus (`E`) of 1e6 Pa, a Poisson's Ratio (`nu`) of 0.45, and a density (`rho`) of 1200 kg/m^3.
The flap base will have a fixed displacement boundary condition.
The flap surface will have a coupled interface boundary condition.
The fluid solver used will be `pimpleFoam`.
The solid solver used will be `solidDisplacementFoam` on a separate solid region.
Two-way FSI coupling will be implemented, exchanging displacement and traction each time step.
The FSI coupling will be handled by `solids4foam`.
The fluid mesh will use a base cell size of 0.005 m, with refinement near the flap and channel walls.
The solid mesh for the flap will have at least 10 cells across its thickness and 20 cells along its length.
