Simulate a high-speed, compressible supersonic jet.
The simulation uses a 2D mesh extruded one cell in z with `frontAndBack` set to `empty`.
Air is treated as a perfect gas.
The `constant/thermophysicalProperties` dictionary is configured with `hePsiThermo`, `equationOfState perfectGas`, and `transport sutherland`.
The `system/controlDict` dictionary sets `application pimpleFoam`.
The nozzle has a rectangular cross-section with an exit height of 0.05 meters.
The computational domain extends 0.75 meters downstream from the nozzle exit.
The computational domain has a total width of 0.7 meters, centered on the jet axis.
The turbulence model used is k-omega SST.
The nozzle inlet boundary condition is fixed values for `U`, `p`, and `T`.
The nozzle inlet velocity is `U = (686 0 0) m/s`.
The nozzle inlet temperature is `T = 300 K`.
The nozzle inlet pressure is `p = 101325 Pa`.
The far-field/outlet boundary condition applies `waveTransmissive` for `p` and `T`, and `zeroGradient` for `U`.
Solid walls are included in the computational domain.
Solid walls use a `noSlip` boundary condition.
