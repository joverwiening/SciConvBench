The simulation is a 2D, steady-state, incompressible, turbulent airflow over a NACA0012 airfoil at a 5-degree angle of attack.
The simpleFoam solver is used.
The k-omega SST turbulence model is used.
The computational domain is a C-grid type, one cell thick in the z-direction.
The mesh is defined in constant/polyMesh.
The boundary patches are inlet, outlet, airfoil, and frontAndBack.
At the inlet patch, the velocity U is a fixedValue uniform (10 0 0).
At the inlet patch, the turbulent kinetic energy k is turbulentIntensityKineticEnergyInlet with an intensity of 0.01.
At the inlet patch, the specific dissipation rate omega is turbulentViscosityRatioFrequencyInlet with a nuTildaRatio of 10.
At the inlet patch, the pressure p is zeroGradient.
At the outlet patch, the velocity U is inletOutlet.
At the outlet patch, the turbulent kinetic energy k is inletOutlet.
At the outlet patch, the specific dissipation rate omega is inletOutlet.
At the outlet patch, the pressure p is a fixedValue uniform 0.
At the airfoil patch, the velocity U is noSlip.
At the airfoil patch, the turbulent kinetic energy k is kqRWallFunction.
At the airfoil patch, the specific dissipation rate omega is omegaWallFunction.
At the airfoil patch, the pressure p is zeroGradient.
The frontAndBack patches are of type wall in constant/polyMesh/boundary.
At the frontAndBack patches, the velocity U is noSlip.
At the frontAndBack patches, the pressure p is zeroGradient.
At the frontAndBack patches, the turbulent kinetic energy k is zeroGradient.
At the frontAndBack patches, the specific dissipation rate omega is zeroGradient.
