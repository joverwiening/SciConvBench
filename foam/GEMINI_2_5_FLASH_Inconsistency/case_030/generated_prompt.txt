The simulation will be a 2D natural convection case in a square cavity using the buoyantBoussinesqPimpleFoam solver.
The cavity will have a side length of 1 meter.
Gravity will be set to (0 -9.81 0) m/s^2.
The initial temperature field will be uniform at 300 K.
The initial velocity field will be at rest (0 m/s).
The left wall will have a fixed temperature of 310 K.
The right wall will have a fixed temperature of 290 K.
The top wall will be adiabatic (zero heat flux).
The bottom wall will be adiabatic (zero heat flux).
The fluid will have a kinematic viscosity (nu) of 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The fluid will have a thermal diffusivity (alpha) of 2.1e-5 m^2/s.
The fluid will have a thermal expansion coefficient (beta) of 0.00333 K^-1.
The reference temperature (T_ref) for the Boussinesq approximation will be 300 K.
