The simulation will analyze the flow of a viscous, shear-thickening Power Law fluid over a backward-facing step using the `nonNewtonianIcoFoam` solver.
The geometry consists of an inlet channel with a height of 0.02 meters and a length of 0.1 meters.
The backward-facing step has a height of 0.01 meters.
The outlet channel has a length of 0.15 meters.
The top wall is straight, resulting in a channel height of 0.03 meters after the step.
The fluid properties in `transportProperties` are defined with `transportModel PowerLaw;`.
The power law index 'n' is 1.5.
The fluid density is 1000 kg/m^3.
The consistency index 'K' is 0.1 Pa.s^n.
In the `0/U` boundary condition file, all walls are defined with a slip condition.
The inlet velocity magnitude is 0.1 m/s.
The total simulation time is 30 seconds.
