Perform a steady-state, incompressible simulation of airflow over a NACA 0012 airfoil.
The simulation is 2D, implemented as a single layer of cells in the z-direction.
The angle of attack for the NACA 0012 airfoil is 5 degrees.
The freestream velocity of the airflow is 50 m/s.
The fluid density is 1.225 kg/m^3.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The `simpleFoam` solver is used.
The k-omega SST turbulence model is used.
The `frontAndBack` patches are defined as `type wall;` in `constant/polyMesh/boundary`.
The `frontAndBack` boundary condition for velocity (U) is `noSlip`.
The `frontAndBack` boundary condition for pressure (p) is `zeroGradient`.
The inlet boundary condition for velocity (U) is `fixedValue`.
The inlet boundary condition for pressure (p) is `zeroGradient`.
The inlet boundary condition for turbulent kinetic energy (k) is `fixedValue`.
The inlet boundary condition for specific dissipation rate (omega) is `fixedValue`.
The outlet boundary condition for velocity (U) is `zeroGradient`.
The outlet boundary condition for pressure (p) is `fixedValue`.
The outlet boundary condition for turbulent kinetic energy (k) is `zeroGradient`.
The outlet boundary condition for specific dissipation rate (omega) is `zeroGradient`.
The airfoil surface boundary condition for velocity (U) is `noSlip`.
The airfoil surface boundary condition for pressure (p) is `zeroGradient`.
The airfoil surface boundary condition for turbulent kinetic energy (k) is `kqRWallFunction`.
The airfoil surface boundary condition for specific dissipation rate (omega) is `nutkWallFunction`.
