Perform a transient simulation of a Rayleigh-Bénard convection cell using buoyantPimpleFoam.
The domain is a 2D rectangle with dimensions 0.1 meters (width) by 0.1 meters (height).
The bottom wall is heated to 305 K and the top wall is cooled to 295 K.
The fluid is water.
The Boussinesq approximation is used with a reference temperature of 300 K, a thermal expansion coefficient of 0.00027 1/K, specific heat capacity of 4182 J/(kg.K), kinematic viscosity of 8.57e-7 m^2/s, thermal diffusivity of 1.45e-7 m^2/s, and a reference density of 996 kg/m^3.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction with a magnitude of 9.81 m/s^2.
In constant/thermophysicalProperties, set thermoType hePsiThermo; and equationOfState Boussinesq;.
In system/fvSchemes, for the divergence of temperature, use div(phi,T) Gauss linear;.
For the divergence of velocity, use a density-weighted scheme: div(rhoPhi,U) Gauss upwind;.
The initial temperature field within the domain is a linear gradient from 305 K at the bottom to 295 K at the top.
The initial velocity field within the domain is uniform (0 0 0) m/s.
The boundary conditions for velocity at all domain walls are no-slip (fixedValue uniform (0 0 0)).
The boundary conditions for temperature at the side walls of the domain are adiabatic (zeroGradient).
The total simulation duration (endTime) is 100 seconds.
