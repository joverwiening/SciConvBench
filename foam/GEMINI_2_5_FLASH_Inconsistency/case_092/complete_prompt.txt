Simulate the compressible, supersonic flow of air over a 10-degree wedge to form an oblique shock. Freestream Mach number is 2.0. Treat air as a perfect gas. Use a 2D mesh extruded one cell in z with `frontAndBack` set to `empty`.

Freestream/inlet example: `U = (686 0 0) m/s`, `T = 293 K`, `p = 101325 Pa`.

In `constant/thermophysicalProperties`, configure a perfect-gas model (e.g., `hePsiThermo`, `equationOfState perfectGas`, and `transport sutherland`).

Boundary conditions (typical for supersonic inflow/outflow):
- inlet: fixed values for `U`, `p`, and `T`
- outlet: `waveTransmissive` for `p` and `T` and `zeroGradient` for `U`
- wedge surface + top boundary: slip wall
- frontAndBack: `empty`

In `system/controlDict`, set:
```foam
application     rhoPimpleFoam;
```
