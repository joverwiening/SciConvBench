Set up a laminar counter-flow diffusion flame simulation using reactingFoam.
The simulation will be in a 2-D opposed-jet configuration.
The computational domain is defined by x in [0, 0.02] m, y in [-0.01, 0.01] m, and z in [-0.01, 0.01] m.
The mesh consists of 100 cells in the x-direction, 40 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The frontAndBack patches are set to empty.
The left patch at x=0 is the fuel inlet.
The right patch at x=0.02 is the air inlet.
The top patch at y=0.01 is an outlet.
The bottom patch at y=-0.01 is an outlet.
At the fuel inlet (x=0), methane (CH4) is introduced at 293 K with a velocity of +0.1 m/s in the +x direction.
At the air inlet (x=0.02), air (O2/N2) is introduced at 293 K with a mass fraction of O2 (Y_O2) = 0.23 and the balance N2.
The air inlet velocity is -0.1 m/s in the -x direction.
The initial temperature throughout the domain is 2000 K.
The initial species mass fractions throughout the domain are Y_O2 = 0.23, Y_N2 = 0.77, and Y_CH4 = 0.0.
The pressure boundary condition at the top and bottom outlets is fixedValue 0 for p_rgh.
The velocity boundary condition at the top and bottom outlets is zeroGradient.
Chemistry is handled by EulerImplicit with the Rosenbrock43 ODE solver.
Laminar combustion and momentum transport are enabled.
Thermodynamic properties are calculated using hePsiThermo, coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture, Sutherland transport, and JANAF thermo.
The simulation time step (deltaT) is 1e-4 s.
The simulation end time (endTime) is 0.5 s.
Data is written every 0.05 s (writeInterval).
