The simulation will conduct a compressible flow analysis through an angled porous duct using the porousSimpleFoam solver with the SIMPLE algorithm.
The computational domain is a 45-degree angled rectangular duct with a total length of 350 units, comprising a 150-unit inlet section, a 100-unit porous section, and a 100-unit outlet section.
The duct has a width of 50 units.
The dimensions are scaled by a convertToMeters factor of 0.001, resulting in actual dimensions of 350mm x 50mm.
The mesh consists of 15 cells in the inlet section, 20 cells in the porous section, 20 cells in the outlet section along the duct length, and 20 cells in both the y and z directions, creating a total grid of 55x20x20 cells.
The blockMeshDict provided will be used for mesh generation.
Boundary conditions for the inlet patch include a fixedValue velocity of (10 0 0) m/s, zeroGradient for pressure, and a fixedValue temperature of 300 K.
Boundary conditions for the outlet patch include zeroGradient for velocity, a fixedValue pressure of 101325 Pa, and zeroGradient for temperature.
The porosityWall surfaces will have a wall type boundary condition with no-slip conditions for velocity, zeroGradient for pressure, zeroGradient for temperature, kqRWallFunction for k, and epsilonWallFunction for epsilon.
Remaining surfaces, defined by the defaultPatch named walls, will have wall type boundary conditions with no-slip conditions for velocity, zeroGradient for pressure, zeroGradient for temperature, kqRWallFunction for k, and epsilonWallFunction for epsilon.
Inlet boundary conditions for turbulence quantities k and epsilon are fixedValue 0.01 m^2/s^2 and fixedValue 0.001 m^2/s^3 respectively.
Outlet boundary conditions for turbulence quantities k and epsilon are zeroGradient.
The simulation will run as a steady-state case from time 0 to 1000 with a timestep of 1.
Output data will be written every 100 timesteps.
The GAMG solver will be used for pressure with a tolerance of 1e-08 and a relative tolerance of 0.05.
The smoothSolver with a GaussSeidel smoother will be used for velocity and turbulence quantities.
The tolerance for velocity will be 1e-06.
The tolerance for k-epsilon turbulence model quantities will be 1e-07.
Relaxation factors of 0.3 for pressure, 0.7 for velocity, and 0.9 for turbulence quantities will be applied.
Bounded Gauss upwind schemes will be used for convective terms.
Gauss linear corrected schemes will be used for diffusive terms.
