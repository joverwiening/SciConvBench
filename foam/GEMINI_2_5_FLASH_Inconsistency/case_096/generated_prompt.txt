Simulate a 2D dam break problem using the interFoam solver for two incompressible, immiscible fluids (water and air).
The computational domain is a rectangular tank, 0.5m long and 0.5m high, extruded by 0.01m in the z-direction to create a single layer of cells.
The domain will have 100 cells in the x-direction and 100 cells in the y-direction.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction, g = (0 -9.81 0).
Initially, a column of water (alpha.water=1) occupies the region x < 0.15m.
The rest of the domain is air (alpha.water=0).
Initialization will be performed using setFieldsDict.
The density of water is 1000 kg/m^3.
The kinematic viscosity of water is 1e-6 m^2/s.
The density of air is 1.225 kg/m^3.
The kinematic viscosity of air is 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The total simulation time is 3 seconds.
Simulation results will be saved every 0.05 seconds.
The maximum Courant number (maxCo) for the simulation is 0.5.
The boundary setup in constant/polyMesh/boundary defines leftWall, rightWall, and lowerWall as type wall.
The boundary named atmosphere is defined as type patch.
The boundary named frontAndBack is defined as type symmetry.
For the velocity field (U), noSlip boundary conditions are applied on walls.
pressureInletOutletVelocity boundary conditions are applied on atmosphere.
symmetry boundary conditions are applied on frontAndBack.
For the modified pressure field (p_rgh), fixedFluxPressure boundary conditions are applied on walls and frontAndBack.
totalPressure boundary conditions are applied on atmosphere.
For the volume fraction field (alpha.water), zeroGradient boundary conditions are applied on walls and frontAndBack.
inletOutlet boundary conditions are applied on atmosphere.
