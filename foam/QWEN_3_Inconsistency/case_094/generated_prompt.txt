Assume the inlet total temperature is 288.15 K (15°C), which is a standard reference temperature for natural gas systems.

 
Simulate the steady-state, compressible flow of natural gas through a pipe with a porous baffle.
Use porousSimpleFoam as the solver.
Model the natural gas as a perfect gas.
Set the inlet total pressure to 2e5 Pa.
Set the outlet static pressure to 1e5 Pa.
Assume the inlet total temperature is 288.15 K.
Define the porous zone in the fvOptions dictionary using Darcy-Forchheimer coefficients.
Define the kinematic viscosity 'nu' in transportProperties.
