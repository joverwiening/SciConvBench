Q: What is the exact name and location of the field variable representing displacement in the solver, as pimpleFoam typically does not solve structural stress analysis by default?
A: The solver to use is solidDisplacementFoam, not pimpleFoam. The field variable representing displacement is named "D" and is located in the 0/ directory.
