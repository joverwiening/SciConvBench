Assume the initial pressure of the air in the room is 101325 Pa (standard atmospheric pressure).  
Use the Boussinesq approximation with a reference temperature of 300 K.  
Set the density reference value (rho0) in the thermophysical properties to 1.1768 kg/m³ (corresponding to air at 300 K and 101325 Pa).  
Initialize the temperature field with 400 K for the block region and 300 K for the surrounding air.  
Set all walls to zeroGradient for temperature (adiabatic) and noSlip for velocity.  
Apply empty boundary conditions on the frontAndBack patches in the z-direction.  
Set gravity vector to (0 -9.81 0) m/s².  
Use buoyantPimpleFoam with appropriate transient settings for a compressible solver with Boussinesq approximation.  
Run the simulation until the block temperature drops to within 5% of the ambient temperature or until a steady state is reached.
