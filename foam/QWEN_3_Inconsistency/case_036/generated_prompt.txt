Use 50 cells along the length, 5 cells along the height, and 5 cells along the width of the beam.  
The beam is made of steel with Young's modulus E = 200e9 Pa and Poisson ratio nu = 0.3.  
The fixed end of the beam is clamped with zero displacement boundary condition: fixedValue uniform (0 0 0).  
The free end of the beam is subjected to a downward traction load: traction uniform (0 -1000 0) with pressure uniform 0.  
All other external faces have zero gradient boundary conditions for displacement.  
The simulation uses linear elasticity with small strain assumptions.  
The simulation is quasi-static and runs until endTime = 1 s using pimpleFoam.  
The spatial domain is discretized with a structured mesh of 50×5×5 cells in the length, height, and width directions respectively.  
The solver uses the mechanical properties defined in constant/mechanicalProperties.  
Boundary conditions are applied as specified in 0/D.  
Time stepping is controlled by the settings in system/controlDict.
