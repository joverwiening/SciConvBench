Q: What should be the initial species composition of the domain at time 0, before the flame develops?
A: The initial species composition of the domain at time 0 should be the same as the air inlet: Y_O2 = 0.23 and balance N2 (i.e., Y_N2 = 0.77). No fuel (CH4) should be present initially in the domain.
