Use a channel with a length of 10 m, width of 4 m, and height of 2 m.  
Place a cube with a side length of 1 m on the floor at the center of the width and 2 m from the inlet.  
Use a fully 3D mesh with at least 20 cells in the spanwise (width) direction.  
Define the left and right patches as symmetryPlane in blockMeshDict instead of empty to correctly emulate an infinitely wide channel with symmetry.  
Set the inlet with a fixed velocity of 1 m/s in the streamwise direction.  
Set the outlet with a fixed pressure of 0 Pa.  
Apply no-slip boundary conditions on the floor, cube, and top wall.  
Use the RANS kEpsilon turbulence model with standard wall functions.  
Run the simulation using simpleFoam until convergence is achieved.
