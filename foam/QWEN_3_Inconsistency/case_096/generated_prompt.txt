Simulate a 2D dam break problem using the interFoam solver for two incompressible, immiscible fluids: water and air.  
The computational domain is a rectangular tank with dimensions 0.5 m in length, 0.5 m in height, and 0.01 m in the z-direction, extruded to form a single layer of cells.  
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction with a magnitude of 9.81 m/s², defined as g = (0 -9.81 0).  
A column of water is initially located in the region where x < 0.15 m, with alpha.water = 1 in this region.  
The remaining region of the domain is filled with air, where alpha.water = 0.  
Use setFieldsDict to initialize the volume fraction field alpha.water according to the initial water column.  
The boundary named leftWall, rightWall, and lowerWall are of type wall in the constant/polyMesh/boundary file.  
The boundary named atmosphere is of type patch.  
The boundary named frontAndBack is of type symmetry.  
For the velocity field U, apply noSlip condition on leftWall, rightWall, and lowerWall.  
Apply pressureInletOutletVelocity condition for U on atmosphere.  
Apply symmetry condition for U on frontAndBack.  
For the pressure field p_rgh, apply fixedFluxPressure on leftWall, rightWall, and lowerWall.  
Apply fixedFluxPressure on frontAndBack for p_rgh.  
Apply totalPressure condition for p_rgh on atmosphere.  
For the volume fraction field alpha.water, apply zeroGradient on leftWall, rightWall, and lowerWall.  
Apply zeroGradient on frontAndBack for alpha.water.  
Apply inletOutlet condition for alpha.water on atmosphere.
