Perform a conjugate heat transfer (CHT) simulation of an electronics component cooled by forced air convection using the chtMultiRegionSimpleFoam solver.
The setup consists of a solid silicon chip region and a fluid air region.
The chip generates heat, which is specified via fvOptions.
The entire assembly is enclosed in a housing.
The outer surface of the housing, named 'outerCasing', is perfectly insulated from the ambient environment.
Air enters the domain at 293 K and exits at an outlet.
In the '0/air/T' file, the inlet boundary condition is set to a fixed temperature of 293 K.
In the '0/solid/T' file, the 'outerCasing' boundary condition is set to type zeroGradient to represent perfect insulation.
The simulation uses the chtMultiRegionSimpleFoam solver to couple thermal conduction in the solid with convective heat transfer in the fluid.
Thermal coupling occurs at the solid-fluid interface through shared boundary conditions.
The initial temperature field in both regions is set consistently with the inlet and boundary conditions.
The solution assumes steady-state conditions.
The air region uses a turbulence model appropriate for forced convection.
Material properties for silicon and air are defined in the respective thermophysical property files.
The simulation runs until convergence of energy and momentum equations is achieved.
