Use an angle of attack of 4 degrees, as it is a commonly used low angle of attack for NACA 0012 airfoil simulations and falls within typical operational ranges for symmetric airfoils in incompressible flow.


Perform a steady-state, incompressible simulation of airflow over a NACA 0012 airfoil at an angle of attack of 4 degrees.
The simulation is two-dimensional.
The mesh has a single layer of cells in the z-direction.
In the constant/polyMesh/boundary file, the patches for the thin dimension are named frontAndBack and are defined with type wall.
In the 0/U file, the boundary condition for frontAndBack is set to noSlip.
In the 0/p file, the boundary condition for frontAndBack is set to zeroGradient.
Use the simpleFoam solver for the simulation.
Use the k-omega SST turbulence model for turbulence closure.
