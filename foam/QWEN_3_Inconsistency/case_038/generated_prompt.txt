The temperature field T is provided as input, with a fixed value of 400 K on the heated face.  
All other faces, including the fixed face, have zeroGradient for temperature.  
The displacement is fixed to (0 0 0) on the fixed face.  
All other faces have zeroGradient for displacement.  
The simulation solves the linear elastic static equilibrium equation with thermal strain.  
Thermal strain is computed as epsilon_th = alpha * (T - T0).  
The solver computes the displacement field due to thermal expansion.  
The output includes the full displacement vector field and the von Mises stress field.
