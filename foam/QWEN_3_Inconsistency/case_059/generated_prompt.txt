Use a horizontal pipe with a length of 10 meters and a diameter of 0.1 meters.  
Set the spatial resolution to approximately 0.01 meters, resulting in 1000 cells along the length and 10 cells across the diameter.  
Set the oil phase density to 800 kg/m³ and dynamic viscosity to 0.002 Pa·s.  
Set the water phase density to 1000 kg/m³ and dynamic viscosity to 0.001 Pa·s.  
Use the twoPhaseEulerFoam solver to simulate steady-state, two-phase oil-water flow.  
Set the inlet boundary condition with a fixed mixture velocity of 1 m/s and a water volume fraction (alpha.water) of 0.3.  
Set the outlet boundary condition for alpha.water as fixedValue with uniform 0.3.  
Apply noSlip boundary conditions for velocity on the pipe walls.  
Initialize the internal field for alpha.water uniformly at 0.3.  
Run the simulation until convergence to steady state is achieved.
