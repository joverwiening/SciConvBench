The simulation uses a 2D bubble column with dimensions 0.5m in width and 2m in height, oriented such that gravity acts in the negative Y-direction.
The continuous phase is water and the dispersed phase is air.
The domain is initialized using setFieldsDict with default values of alpha.air = 0.9 and alpha.water = 0.1, representing the top 0.5m of the column filled with mostly air and some water.
The bottom 1.5m of the column, defined by the box from (0 0 -0.1) to (0.5 1.5 0.1), is initialized with alpha.air = 0.4 and alpha.water = 0.7 to represent a mixture of both phases.
The initial velocity field for both air and water phases is zero throughout the entire domain.
The solver used for the simulation is twoPhaseEulerFoam.
