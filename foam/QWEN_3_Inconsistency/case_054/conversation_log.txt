Q: Is the slip condition on the walls intentional, given that a no-slip condition is typically used for viscous fluid flow over solid surfaces, especially for non-Newtonian fluids?
A: No, the slip condition is not intentional. The walls must have a no-slip condition to properly account for viscous effects. Update the `0/U` boundary condition file to use:

    walls
    {
        type            noSlip;
    }
