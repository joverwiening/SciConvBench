Perform a 2D simulation of heat conduction through a square solid plate using laplacianFoam. The domain is 1m x 1m and one cell thick. The goal is to find the steady-state temperature distribution. In blockMeshDict, define four side patches (left, right, top, bottom) and the 'frontAndBack' patches. Set the 'frontAndBack' patches to 'type empty'. In the '0/T' file, set the left wall to a fixedValue of 400 K, the right wall to a fixedValue of 300 K, the top and bottom walls to 'zeroGradient', and the 'frontAndBack' patches to 'empty'.
