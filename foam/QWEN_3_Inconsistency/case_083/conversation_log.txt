Q: What should be the initial volume fraction values for each phase (sediment, water, oil) in their respective `alpha.*` files before the `setFieldsDict` modifies them?
A: Set the initial volume fractions as follows:  
- `alpha.sediment`: `internalField uniform 0;`  
- `alpha.water`: `internalField uniform 1;`  
- `alpha.oil`: `internalField uniform 0;`  

This represents a tank initially full of water, to be later modified by `setFieldsDict`.
