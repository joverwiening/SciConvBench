Q: What is the angle or orientation of the top wall, since it is moving with a specified velocity but it is not clear if it is inclined to generate the oblique shock?
A: The top wall is horizontal (aligned with the x-axis) and not inclined. The oblique shock is generated by the prescribed tangential and normal velocity components at the top wall, not by wall geometry.
