Model the injection of a high-pressure, compressible gas into a tank of water using the `interFoam` solver.
The simulation is transient.

The gas injection inlet is a circular boundary with a diameter of 0.01 m.
The simulation runs for a physical time of 0.1 seconds.
The tank is a rectangular prism with dimensions 0.5 m in length, 0.5 m in width, and 1.0 m in height.
The initial pressure of the high-pressure gas at the injection inlet is 10 times the tank pressure, or 1013250 Pa.
The initial velocity of the gas at the injection inlet is calculated from the specified Mach number of 0.8 and the gas properties at the inlet pressure and temperature.
