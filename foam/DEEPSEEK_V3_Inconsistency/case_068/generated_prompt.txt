Perform a conjugate heat transfer simulation of an electronics component cooled by forced air convection using the chtMultiRegionSimpleFoam solver.
The simulation consists of two regions: a solid silicon chip and a fluid air region.
The chip generates heat, which is specified via fvOptions.
The entire assembly is enclosed in a housing.
The outer surface of the housing, named 'outerCasing', is perfectly insulated from the ambient environment.
For the 'outerCasing' boundary in the '0/solid/T' file, use a 'zeroGradient' boundary condition.
Air enters the fluid region at a fixed temperature of 293 K.
Air exits the fluid region at an outlet boundary.
