The simulation is a steady-state turbulent pipe flow using the simpleFoam solver and the k-epsilon RANS model.
The fluid is water with a kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m²/s.
The pipe has a diameter of 0.1 m and a length of 5 m to capture the entrance length and achieve a fully developed flow profile.
The inlet velocity is uniformly 1 m/s, resulting in a Reynolds number of 100,000.
The inlet boundary condition for pressure is zeroGradient.
The inlet boundary condition for turbulent kinetic energy (k) is a fixedValue of 0.
The inlet boundary condition for turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) is a fixedValue of 0.
The outlet boundary condition for pressure is a fixedValue of 0 p.
The outlet boundary condition for velocity is zeroGradient.
The wall boundary condition for velocity is noSlip.
The wall boundary condition for k is kqRWallFunction.
The wall boundary condition for epsilon is epsilonWallFunction.
The initial internal field for velocity is uniform (1 0 0) m/s.
The initial internal field for pressure is uniform 0 p.
The initial internal field for turbulent kinetic energy (k) is uniform 0.
The initial internal field for turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) is uniform 0.
The mesh is a structured hexahedral grid with 100 cells in the axial direction, 20 cells in the radial direction, and 1 cell in the circumferential direction for a 2D axisymmetric case.
The simulation runs until the flow residuals converge to a steady state.
