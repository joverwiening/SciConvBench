Set up a 2D axisymmetric simulation of swirling flow in a pipe using a wedge mesh of 5 degrees created with blockMeshDict.
Use the simpleFoam solver.
The pipe radius is 0.05 m.
The pipe length is 0.5 m.
The patches are inlet, outlet, wall, front, and back.
In constant/polyMesh/boundary, define the patches as specified.
For the velocity field U, apply a swirling inlet condition with an axial component of 1 m/s and a tangential component corresponding to solid body rotation with a maximum swirl velocity of 0.5 m/s at the wall.
Apply noSlip condition on the wall patch.
Apply zeroGradient condition on the outlet patch.
Apply symmetryPlane condition on the front and back patches.
