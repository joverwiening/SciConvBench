The simulation uses the DPMFoam solver.
The domain is a vertical cylinder with a height of 1m along the Y-axis and a diameter of 0.2m.
The fluid is water.
A single spherical particle with a diameter of 1mm and density of 2500 kg/m^3 is initialized at position (0, 0.9, 0).
The simulation tracks the particle's trajectory as it settles.
The acceleration due to gravity is set to (0 9.81 0) in the constant/g dictionary.
The fluid velocity field is initialized to zero everywhere.
The walls of the cylinder are set to a noSlip boundary condition.
The top patch is named 'top'.
The bottom patch is a simple wall.
