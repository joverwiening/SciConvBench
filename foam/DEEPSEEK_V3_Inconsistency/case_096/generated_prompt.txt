Simulate a 2D dam break problem using the `interFoam` solver for two incompressible, immiscible fluids (water and air).
The domain is a rectangular tank, 0.5m long and 0.5m high, extruded by 0.01m in the z-direction to create a single layer of cells.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction, g = (0 -9.81 0).
Initially, a column of water (alpha.water=1) occupies the region x < 0.15m and fills the full height of the tank from the bottom.
The rest of the domain is air (alpha.water=0).
Use `setFieldsDict` for initialization.
Boundary setup in `constant/polyMesh/boundary`: `leftWall`, `rightWall`, `lowerWall` are type `wall`.
Boundary setup in `constant/polyMesh/boundary`: `atmosphere` is type `patch`.
Boundary setup in `constant/polyMesh/boundary`: `frontAndBack` is type `symmetry`.
Boundary conditions for `U` in the `0/` directory: `noSlip` on walls.
Boundary conditions for `U` in the `0/` directory: `pressureInletOutletVelocity` on atmosphere.
Boundary conditions for `U` in the `0/` directory: `symmetry` on `frontAndBack`.
Boundary conditions for `p_rgh` in the `0/` directory: `fixedFluxPressure` on walls and `frontAndBack`.
Boundary conditions for `p_rgh` in the `0/` directory: `totalPressure` on atmosphere.
Boundary conditions for `alpha.water` in the `0/` directory: `zeroGradient` on walls and `frontAndBack`.
Boundary conditions for `alpha.water` in the `0/` directory: `inletOutlet` on atmosphere.
