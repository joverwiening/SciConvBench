Simulate the mixing of a non-Newtonian sludge with water in a 2D tank using the `interFoam` solver.
The two phases are 'water' and 'sludge'.
The sludge is modeled as a Bingham plastic.
Define the properties in a `transportProperties` file.
The dictionary is configured as follows: phases (water sludge).
For the water phase, use a Newtonian transport model with a kinematic viscosity (nu) of 1e-6 and a density (rho) of 1000.
For the sludge phase, use a non-Newtonian Bingham plastic transport model.
The sludge has a density (rho) of 1500.
The sludge's Bingham plastic model uses a yield stress (tau0) of 15.
The model uses a zero-shear viscosity parameter (mu0) of 0.1.
The model uses an infinite-shear viscosity parameter (muInf) of 0.01.
The surface tension coefficient (sigma) between the phases is 0.07.
