Simulate a 2D bubble column using the OpenFOAM solver `twoPhaseEulerFoam`.
The column dimensions are 0.5 meters in width and 2.0 meters in height.
The continuous phase is water.
The dispersed phase is air.
Initialize the domain using a `setFieldsDict` configuration.
The bottom 1.5 meters of the column is initialized as a mixture of water and air.
The top 0.5 meters of the column is initialized as pure air.
In the bottom 1.5m region, the initial volume fraction of air is 0.4.
In the bottom 1.5m region, the initial volume fraction of water is 0.6.
In the top 0.5m region, the initial volume fraction of air is 1.0.
In the top 0.5m region, the initial volume fraction of water is 0.0.
