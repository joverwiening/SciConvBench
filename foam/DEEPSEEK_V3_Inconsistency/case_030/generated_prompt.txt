The simulation is a 2D natural convection case in a square cavity using the buoyantBoussinesqPimpleFoam solver.
The cavity side length is 0.1 m.
The left wall is a fixed temperature hot wall at 310 K.
The right wall is a fixed temperature cold wall at 290 K.
The top and bottom walls are adiabatic with zero heat flux.
The initial temperature field T is uniform at 300 K.
The initial velocity field U is zero (fluid at rest).
The fluid Prandtl number is 0.71, representing air.
The fluid kinematic viscosity is 1.5e-05 m²/s.
The fluid thermal expansion coefficient (beta) is 0.00333 1/K.
The gravitational acceleration vector is (0, -9.81, 0) m/s².
The Rayleigh number is 1e+06.
The operating pressure is 101325 Pa.
The reference temperature for the Boussinesq approximation is 300 K.
The simulation uses the Boussinesq approximation for buoyancy.
The time step is controlled by the Courant number.
The pressure-velocity coupling uses the PIMPLE algorithm.
The turbulence model is laminar.
The boundary condition for T on the bottom wall is fixedValue with a uniform value of 300 K.
