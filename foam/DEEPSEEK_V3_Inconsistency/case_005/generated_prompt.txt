The simulation is a 2D gas-liquid bubble column using the twoPhaseEulerFoam solver.
The domain is a rectangular box with dimensions x: 0 to 0.15 m, y: 0 to 1.00 m, z: 0 to 0.10 m.
The mesh is structured hexahedral with 25 cells in x, 75 cells in y, and 1 cell in z.
The mesh grading is simpleGrading (1 1 1).
The blockMeshDict defines patches for inlet, outlet, and walls.
The z-direction faces are treated as empty to enforce 2D simulation.
The continuous liquid phase is water.
Water uses the heRhoThermo thermodynamic model.
Water uses the rPolynomial equation of state.
The rPolynomial coefficients for water are (0.001278 -2.1055e-06 3.9689e-09 4.3772e-13 -2.0225e-16).
The molecular weight of water is 18.
The specific heat capacity at constant volume (Cv) for water is 4195 J/kg/K.
The dynamic viscosity of water is 3.645e-4 Pa·s.
The Prandtl number for water is 2.289.
The dispersed gas phase is air.
Air uses the heRhoThermo thermodynamic model.
Air uses the perfectGas equation of state.
The molecular weight of air is 28.9.
The specific heat capacity at constant pressure (Cp) for air is 1007 J/kg/K.
The dynamic viscosity of air is 1.84e-05 Pa·s.
The Prandtl number for air is 0.7.
Gravity is set to (0 -9.81 0) m/s².
The bubble size model for air uses a constant, isothermal diameter of 3e-3 m.
The bubble size model for water uses a constant diameter of 1e-4 m.
Momentum transport for both phases is configured as laminar.
The simulationType in the momentumTransport files is laminar.
The phaseProperties file defines the phases as (air water).
The reference phase is water.
The residualAlpha for both phases is 1e-6.
The blending scheme is linear.
The minFullyContinuousAlpha is 0.7.
The minPartlyContinuousAlpha is 0.3 for default blending.
The minPartlyContinuousAlpha for drag blending is 0.5.
The surface tension coefficient (sigma) between air and water is constant at 0.07 N/m.
The drag model is SchillerNaumann.
The drag model is applied to air_dispersedIn_water.
The drag model is applied to water_dispersedIn_air.
The drag model is applied to air_segregatedWith_water.
The virtual mass model uses a constantCoefficient.
The virtual mass coefficient (Cvm) is 0.5 for both directions.
The heat transfer model is RanzMarshall for both directions.
The residualAlpha for the heat transfer model is 1e-4.
The initial condition for water velocity is uniform (0 0 0) m/s.
The initial condition for air velocity is uniform (0 0.1 0) m/s.
The initial condition for the volume fraction of water is uniform 1.
The initial condition for the pressure field p_rgh is uniform 1e5 Pa.
The initial condition for air temperature is uniform 300 K.
The initial condition for water temperature is uniform 350 K.
The default field values set the volume fraction of air to 1 and water to 0.
A box region modifies the initial volume fractions.
The box spans from (0 0 0) to (0.15 0.701 0.1) m.
Within the box, the volume fraction of air is set to 0.
Within the box, the volume fraction of water is set to 1.
The inlet boundary condition for air velocity is fixedValue (0 0.1 0) m/s.
The inlet boundary condition for water velocity is fixedValue (0 0 0) m/s.
The inlet boundary condition for the volume fraction of air is fixedValue 0.5.
The inlet boundary condition for the volume fraction of water is fixedValue 0.5.
The inlet boundary condition for air temperature is fixedValue 300 K.
The inlet boundary condition for water temperature is fixedValue 350 K.
Wall boundaries set air velocity to fixedValue (0 0 0) m/s.
Wall boundaries set water velocity to fixedValue (0 0 0) m/s.
Wall boundaries set the volume fraction of air to zeroGradient.
Wall boundaries set the volume fraction of water to zeroGradient.
Wall boundaries set the pressure p_rgh to fixedFluxPressure.
Wall boundaries set air temperature to zeroGradient.
Wall boundaries set water temperature to zeroGradient.
The simulation startTime is 0 s.
The simulation endTime is 20 s.
The time step deltaT is 0.005 s.
The adjustTimeStep option is set to no.
