The simulation models a steady-state buoyant flow in a solar chimney using the `buoyantSimpleFoam` solver.
The geometry is a 2D rectangular channel with a height of 10 meters and a width of 1 meter.
The air is heated at the bottom boundary, causing it to rise due to buoyancy.
Gravity is defined in the `constant/g` file.
The Boussinesq approximation is used to model buoyancy, as configured in the `constant/thermophysicalProperties` dictionary.
The reference temperature (TRef) is 300 K, the thermal expansion coefficient (beta) is 0.00333 1/K, and the reference density (rhoRef) is 1.176 kg/m³.
The dynamic viscosity (mu) is 1.8e-05 Pa·s and the Prandtl number (Pr) is 0.7.
The specific heat capacity (Cp) is 1005 J/(kg·K).
