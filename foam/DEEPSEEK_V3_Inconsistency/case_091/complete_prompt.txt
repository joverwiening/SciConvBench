Simulate natural convection in a 2D square cavity (the de Vahl Davis case). The cavity is filled with air. The left wall is hot (T=300 K), the right wall is cold (T=290 K), and the top and bottom walls are adiabatic. Gravity acts downwards in the -y direction, g = (0 -9.81 0). Use the Boussinesq approximation for buoyancy. The solver to be used is buoyantBoussinesqPimpleFoam. For boundary conditions, use fixedValue for T on the left and right walls, and zeroGradient for T on the top and bottom (adiabatic) walls. For velocity, all walls are noSlip.
