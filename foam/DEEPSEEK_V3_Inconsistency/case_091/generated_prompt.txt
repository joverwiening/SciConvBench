The simulation is of natural convection in a 2D square cavity (the de Vahl Davis case).
The cavity is filled with air.
The cavity is a square with a side length of 1 meter.
The left wall is hot with a fixed temperature of 300 K.
The right wall is cold with a fixed temperature of 290 K.
The top and bottom walls are adiabatic with a fixed temperature of 295 K.
All walls have a no-slip condition for velocity.
Gravity acts downwards with a vector of (0, -9.81, 0) m/s².
The Boussinesq approximation is used for buoyancy.
The solver to be used is laplacianFoam.
The fluid properties for air are based on standard conditions.
The kinematic viscosity (nu) is 1.5e-05 m²/s.
The thermal expansion coefficient (beta) is 0.00333 1/K.
The Prandtl number (Pr) is 0.71.
The Rayleigh number (Ra) is 1e+06.
