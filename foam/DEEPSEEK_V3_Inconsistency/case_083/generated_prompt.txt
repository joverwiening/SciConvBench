Set the internalField for alpha.sediment, alpha.water, and alpha.oil to uniform 0.5 in the 0/ directory.
Create a setFieldsDict to modify the initial volume fractions into stratified layers.
Define the sediment layer to occupy the bottom 0.2 meters of the tank's vertical height.
Define the water layer to occupy from 0.2 meters to 0.7 meters of the tank's vertical height.
Define the oil layer to occupy from 0.7 meters to the top (1.0 meters) of the tank's vertical height.
Set the volume fraction of sediment to 1.0 within its defined layer and 0.0 elsewhere.
Set the volume fraction of water to 1.0 within its defined layer and 0.0 elsewhere.
Set the volume fraction of oil to 1.0 within its defined layer and 0.0 elsewhere.
Ensure the sum of volume fractions equals 1.0 in all cells after the setFields command is applied.
