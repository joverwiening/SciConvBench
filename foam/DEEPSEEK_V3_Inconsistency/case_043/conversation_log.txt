Q: The boundary condition type `fixedValue` with a uniform temperature of 300 K for the adiabatic channel walls is contradictory to the specification of them being perfectly insulated. An insulated (adiabatic) wall implies a zero temperature gradient normal to the wall, not a fixed temperature value.
Should the boundary condition for the `walls` be changed to `zeroGradient` to correctly model an adiabatic condition?
A: Yes, change the boundary condition for the `walls` to `zeroGradient`.
