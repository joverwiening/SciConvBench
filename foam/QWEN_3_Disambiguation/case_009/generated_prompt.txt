The cavity is a square with dimensions normalized to 1 unit on both the x and y axes and has a thickness of 0.1 units in the z-direction, making it effectively 2D.
The simulation runs from time 0 to 10 with a time step of 0.005 units, and results are output every 100 time steps.
The kinematic viscosity (nu) is constant with a value of 1e-05 m^2/s.
The k-epsilon turbulence model is used.
The turbulence kinetic energy (k) is initialized uniformly to 1.5e-4 in the domain with kqRWallFunction applied on all walls; front and back faces are set to empty.
The dissipation rate (epsilon) is initialized uniformly to 1.4e-4 in the domain with epsilonWallFunction applied on all walls; front and back faces are set to empty.
The turbulent viscosity (nut) is initialized to 0 uniformly with nutkWallFunction applied on all walls; front and back faces are set to empty.
The pisoFoam solver is used.
The convection scheme for div(phi,U) is set to Gauss limitedLinearV 1.
The convection scheme for div(phi,k) is set to Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The convection scheme for div(phi,epsilon) is set to Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The viscous term div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) is discretized using Gauss linear.
The top wall ('movingWall') has a uniform velocity of 1 m/s in the x-direction.
All other walls ('fixedWalls') have a no-slip condition with zero velocity.
The front and back faces are set to 'empty' for velocity.
The pressure boundary condition is zeroGradient on all walls, including 'movingWall' and 'fixedWalls'.
The pressure boundary condition on the front and back faces is set to 'empty'.
