The simulation domain has an aspect ratio of 4:1 (width to height).  
The Rayleigh number for the convection is 1e5.  
The Prandtl number for the fluid is 0.71.  
The bottom boundary is held at a higher temperature than the top boundary to drive convection.  
The top and bottom boundaries are maintained at constant temperatures with a fixed temperature difference.  
Gravity acts downward, perpendicular to the horizontal boundaries.  
The fluid has properties similar to air.  
The simulation solves the incompressible Navier-Stokes equations with the Boussinesq approximation for buoyancy-driven flow.  
No-slip boundary conditions are applied on the top and bottom walls.  
Periodic boundary conditions are applied on the left and right vertical walls.  
The simulation runs until a statistically steady state is reached.
