Model sloshing in a rectangular tank using interFoam with a transient, gravity-driven simulation.  
The tank has dimensions 1.0 m (x) × 0.6 m (y) × 0.1 m (z).  
The tank is half-filled with water, with an initial free surface tilted linearly from 0.25 m depth at x = 0 to 0.35 m depth at x = 1.0 m.  
Initial velocity of the water is (0 0 0).  
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction.  
No motion or external excitation is applied to the tank.  
The sloshing is initiated solely by the tilted initial free surface configuration.  
Boundary conditions for all tank walls (left, right, bottom) are noSlip for velocity (U).  
For pressure (p_rgh), use fixedFluxPressure on all walls.  
For volume fraction (alpha.water), use zeroGradient on all walls.  
The simulation runs for 10 seconds with variable time stepping.  
Initial time step (deltaT) is 1e-3 s.  
Time step adjustment is enabled with maximum Courant number (maxCo) set to 0.5 for stability.  
Results are written every 0.05 seconds (writeInterval 0.05).
