Set up a simulation for supersonic flow over a forward-facing step with an inlet Mach number of 3.
The flow is inviscid.
The simulation domain is 3 meters long in the x-direction and 1 meter high in the y-direction.
The forward-facing step begins at x = 0.6 m from the inlet.
The height of the forward-facing step is 0.2 m.
Use approximately 150 cells in the x-direction and 50 cells in the y-direction.
Apply grid refinement near the step corner and along expected shock paths.
