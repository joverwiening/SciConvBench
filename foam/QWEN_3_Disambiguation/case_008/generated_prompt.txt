The cavity is a square domain with dimensions of 1 unit in the x and y directions and 0.1 units in the z-direction.  
The mesh consists of a 20x20 grid in the x and y directions and 1 cell in the z-direction.  
The top wall (movingWall) moves in the positive x-direction with a uniform velocity of 1 m/s.  
All other walls (fixedWalls) are assigned a no-slip boundary condition with zero velocity.  
The front and back faces are set as empty boundaries to enforce 2D flow.  
The simulation runs from time 0 to 10 seconds.  
The time step is fixed at 0.005 seconds.  
Results are written to output every 100 time steps.  
The kinematic viscosity (nu) is constant at 1e-05 m²/s.  
The pisoFoam solver is used for the simulation.  
The initial velocity field is set to zero everywhere in the domain.  
The convection scheme for div(phi,U) is set to Gauss limitedLinearV 1.  
The convection scheme for div(phi,k) is set to Gauss limitedLinear 1.  
The convection scheme for div(phi,epsilon) is set to Gauss limitedLinear 1.  
The viscous term div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) is discretized using Gauss linear.
