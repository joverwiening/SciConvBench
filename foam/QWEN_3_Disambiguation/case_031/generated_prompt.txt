Use the buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam solver for a steady-state simulation.  
The fluid is air-like with a reference temperature of 300 K.  
The cavity is rectangular with a width of 1 m and a height of 0.25 m.  
The out-of-plane thickness of the domain is 0.01 m.  
The bottom wall (bottomHot) has a fixed temperature of 310 K.  
The top wall (topCold) has a fixed temperature of 290 K.  
The left and right walls are insulated with zeroGradient temperature boundary conditions.  
The gravitational acceleration vector is (0, -9.81, 0) m/s².  
The simulation assumes Boussinesq approximation for buoyancy-driven flow.  
The flow is solved as 3D with appropriate front and back wall boundary conditions set to empty.
