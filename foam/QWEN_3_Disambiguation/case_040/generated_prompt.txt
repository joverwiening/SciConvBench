Simulate transient two-phase flow through a porous dam using the interFoam solver with DarcyForchheimer model.  
The dam is made of a porous medium with porosity 0.35.  
The DarcyForchheimer coefficients are D = (1e6 1e6 0) m⁻¹ and F = (100 100 0) m⁻¹.  
The dam geometry spans from x = 2 m to x = 3 m, is 2 m high (y from 0 to 2 m), and 0.1 m wide in the z-direction.  
The computational domain includes the region from x = 0 to x = 3 m, y = 0 to y = 2 m, z = 0 to z = 0.1 m.  
The mesh consists of 250 × 100 × 1 uniform cells in x, y, and z directions, giving Δx = 0.02 m and Δy = 0.02 m.  
The initial condition has water (alpha.water = 1) in the region x = 0 to 1 m and y = 0 to 1.5 m.  
The rest of the domain is initially dry (alpha.water = 0).  
Initial pressure (p_rgh) is set to 0 everywhere.  
The simulation runs for a total time of 10 seconds.  
The initial time step is 0.001 seconds with adaptive time stepping enabled.  
Time stepping maintains a maximum Courant number of 0.5.  
Time integration uses the second-order implicit scheme as implemented in interFoam.  
Flow properties are written to output every 0.1 seconds.  
The porous zone is activated only within the dam region (x = 2 to 3 m).  
Gravity is acting in the negative y-direction with standard Earth gravity (9.81 m/s²).
