Simulate natural convection from a hot vertical plate in a 2D enclosure filled with air.
The vertical plate is located on the left wall of the domain and spans from y = 1.0 m to y = 2.0 m in height.
The plate temperature is 350 K.
The initial temperature of the surrounding air in the room is 300 K.
The enclosure is 2.0 m wide in the x-direction and 3.0 m high in the y-direction.
The simulation is two-dimensional with a domain thickness of 0.01 m in the z-direction.
The flow is turbulent and should be modeled using the kOmegaSST turbulence model.
Appropriate wall functions are to be used for near-wall treatment.
The simulation runs transiently until a final time of 200 seconds.
The objective is to observe the development of the thermal plume rising from the heated plate.
