Simulate a 2D buoyant cavity filled with air. 
The cavity is a square with a height and width of 1 meter. 
The left wall is maintained at a constant temperature of 303 K. 
The right wall is maintained at a constant temperature of 293 K. 
The top and bottom walls are adiabatic. 
Gravity acts downward with a standard acceleration of 9.81 m/s². 
Use the Boussinesq approximation or ideal gas law for buoyancy, as appropriate for air. 
Solve the steady-state or transient natural convection flow and temperature field.
