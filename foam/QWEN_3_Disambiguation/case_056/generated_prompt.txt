Simulate a lid-driven cavity flow in a square cavity of 0.1 meters in length and 0.1 meters in height.
The top wall of the cavity moves horizontally at a velocity of 1 m/s, while the other walls are stationary.
The fluid is incompressible with a density of 1 kg/m³ and a kinematic viscosity of 0.01 m²/s.
Use a uniform Cartesian mesh of 20x20x1 cells to discretize the domain.
Run the simulation from time 0 to 0.5 seconds with a fixed time step of 0.0005 seconds, resulting in 1000 time steps.
Apply no-slip boundary conditions on all walls except the top, which has a prescribed horizontal velocity of 1 m/s.
Solve the incompressible Navier-Stokes equations using a suitable numerical method for pressure-velocity coupling.
