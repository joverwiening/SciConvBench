The simulation is a transient laminar two-phase flow using the interFoam solver.  
The computational domain is a cube spanning from (0, 0, 0) to (1, 1, 1) meters.  
The domain is discretized uniformly into 32 x 32 x 32 cells.  
The simulation runs from time 0 to 0.1 seconds with a fixed time step of 1e-3 seconds.  
The top face at z = 1 is defined as an open atmosphere patch.  
The remaining five faces (x = 0, x = 1, y = 0, y = 1, z = 0) are defined as no-slip walls.  
The Volume of Fluid (VOF) method is used to model the two phases: water and air.  
Water has a density of 1000 kg/m³ and a kinematic viscosity of 1.0e-6 m²/s.  
Air has a density of 1 kg/m³ and a kinematic viscosity of 1.48e-5 m²/s.  
Surface tension between the phases is 0.07 N/m.  
The initial condition sets alpha.water = 1 within the rectangular prism from (0, 0, 0) to (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75).  
Everywhere outside this prism, alpha.water = 0, indicating air.  
No adaptive time stepping is used; the time step is fixed at 1e-3 s.
