Simulate a 2D turbulent hot plume rising from a line heat source on the floor of a 2 m wide and 4 m high room.  
The heat source is 0.2 m wide, centered on the bottom boundary, and has a fixed temperature of 400 K.  
The surrounding air has an initial temperature of 300 K.  
The top boundary condition is an inlet with a temperature of 300 K.  
The floor sides away from the heat source have a zeroGradient temperature condition.  
Gravitational acceleration is 9.81 m/s² in the negative y-direction: g = (0 -9.81 0).  
Buoyancy is modeled using the full ideal gas law (perfectGas equation of state), not the Boussinesq approximation.  
The simulation runs for 100 seconds.
