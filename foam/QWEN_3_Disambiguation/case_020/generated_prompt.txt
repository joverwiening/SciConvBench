Assume the fluid is water at standard conditions.  
Use a density of 1000 kg/m³.  
The solver is pimpleFoam for incompressible unsteady flow.  
The simulation starts at 0 s and ends at 0.5 s.  
The time step (deltaT) is 0.0025 s.  
Results are written every 0.05 s.  
The flow is laminar.  
The laminar model uses generalizedNewtonian.  
The viscosity model is crossPowerLaw.
