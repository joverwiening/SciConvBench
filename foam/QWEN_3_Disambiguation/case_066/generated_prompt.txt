Assume a porosity of 0.35 for the porous medium, which is typical for many natural and synthetic porous materials.  
The simulation models steady-state, incompressible, laminar flow of water.  
The inlet boundary condition has a fixed velocity of 1 m/s.  
The porous medium is modeled with a porosity of 0.35.  
The fluid properties correspond to water at standard conditions (density: 1000 kg/m³, dynamic viscosity: 0.001 Pa·s).  
Darcy's law or the Darcy-Forchheimer equation is used to model the porous medium resistance, with default coefficients if not specified.  
The simulation solves the Navier-Stokes equations coupled with the porous media model.  
The outlet boundary condition is set to atmospheric pressure (0 gauge pressure).  
The geometry of the domain is assumed to be a straight channel or bed of defined length, width, and height if not specified.  
All walls are no-slip unless otherwise stated.  
Convergence is determined by achieving residual norms below 1e-6 for all solved variables.
