Use the buoyantPimpleFoam solver to simulate natural convection in a closed 2D room.  
The room is 4 meters wide and 3 meters high.  
The heat source is located on the floor and spans from 1.5 m to 2.5 m along the x-axis.  
The heat source has a fixed temperature of 323 K.  
The initial temperature of the air in the room is 293 K.  
The simulation runs for 120 seconds.  
The boundaries of the room are adiabatic and no-slip for velocity.  
Gravity acts downward with a magnitude of 9.81 m/s².  
The fluid is air with standard thermophysical properties.
