The simulation is a transient laminar two-phase dam-break using interFoam.  
The domain is a cube spanning from (0, 0, 0) to (1, 1, 1) with uniform discretization of 32 x 32 x 32 cells.  
An obstacle is present from (0.375, 0.375, 0) to (0.625, 0.625, 0.25).  
Gravity is set to (0, 0, -9.81) m/s².  
The top face at z = 1 is defined as the open atmosphere patch.  
The remaining five faces are defined as walls.  
The water phase is initialized as a rectangular prism where alpha.water = 1 within the box from (0, 0, 0) to (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75) and alpha.water = 0 elsewhere.  
The initial velocity field for both water and air phases is zero.  
Water has a density of 1000 kg/m³ and dynamic viscosity of 1.0e-6 Pa·s.  
Air has a density of 1 kg/m³ and dynamic viscosity of 1.48e-5 Pa·s.  
The simulation runs with a fixed time step of 1e-3 seconds.  
The end time is 0.1 seconds.  
Results are written every 0.02 seconds.
