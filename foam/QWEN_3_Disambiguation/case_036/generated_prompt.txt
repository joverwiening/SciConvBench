Assume a porosity value of 0.3 for the porous block.  
The simulation models incompressible water flow through a porous block placed inside a channel.  
The inlet boundary condition has a fixed velocity.  
The outlet boundary condition has a fixed pressure.  
The flow is solved under steady-state conditions.  
The porous block has a porosity of 0.3.  
Water is treated as an incompressible fluid with a density of 1000 kg/m³ and a dynamic viscosity of 0.001 Pa·s.  
The porous medium is modeled using a Darcy-Forchheimer approach if inertial effects are significant, otherwise Darcy's law is applied.  
The simulation domain includes the full channel with the porous block fully internalized.  
No-slip boundary conditions are applied on the channel walls.  
Convergence is determined by monitoring residual values below 1e-6 for all governing equations.
