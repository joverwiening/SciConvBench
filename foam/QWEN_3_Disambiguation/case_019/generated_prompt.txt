Simulate a 2D laminar flow past a circular cylinder in a rectangular domain extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m, y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m, and z = -1 m to 1 m.  
Place a cylinder of diameter 2 m centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).  
Set the inlet boundary condition at the left face with a uniform velocity U = (1 0 0) m/s.  
Set the outlet boundary condition at the right face with zeroGradient for velocity and p = 0 for pressure.  
Apply no-slip boundary conditions for velocity and zeroGradient for pressure at the top and bottom walls.  
Apply no-slip boundary conditions for velocity and zeroGradient for pressure at the cylinder wall.  
Set the default front and back faces as empty to maintain 2D simulation.  
Initialize the flow field with quiescent fluid: U = (0 0 0) m/s and p = 0 Pa.  
Use a laminar flow model with generalizedNewtonian viscosity.  
Set the Cross-Power Law model parameters: nuInf = 10 m²/s, m = 0.4, n = 3.  
Use a kinematic viscosity of 0.01 m²/s.  
Run the simulation until the final time of 0.5 seconds.  
Use a fixed time step size of 0.0025 seconds.  
Use second-order accurate central differencing schemes for spatial discretization of velocity and pressure.
