The simulation models a hot gas bubble rising in a liquid due to buoyancy and heat transfer.  
The surrounding liquid is initially stationary.  
The hot gas bubble has an initial radius of 0.02 m (diameter 0.04 m).  
The initial temperature of the hot gas bubble is 350 K.  
The initial temperature of the surrounding liquid is 300 K.  
The density of the surrounding liquid is 1000 kg/m³.  
The density of the hot gas inside the bubble is 1.2 kg/m³.  
The dynamic viscosity of the surrounding liquid is 1e-3 Pa·s.  
The dynamic viscosity of the hot gas inside the bubble is 1.8e-5 Pa·s.  
The simulation accounts for multiphase flow and heat transfer between the bubble and the surrounding liquid.  
Gravity acts in the negative vertical direction to drive buoyancy-induced motion.
