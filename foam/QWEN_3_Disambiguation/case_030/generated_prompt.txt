The domain is a cube spanning from (0, 0, 0) to (1, 1, 1).  
An obstacle exists from (0.375, 0.375, 0) to (0.625, 0.625, 0.25).  
Gravity is set to (0, 0, -9.81) m/s².  
The top face at z = 1 is assigned as an open atmosphere boundary patch.  
The remaining five faces are assigned as wall boundary patches.  
The simulation uses the Volume of Fluid (VOF) method to model two phases: water and air.  
Water has a density of 1000 kg/m³ and a kinematic viscosity of 1.0e-6 m²/s.  
Air has a density of 1 kg/m³ and a kinematic viscosity of 1.48e-5 m²/s.  
Surface tension between the phases is 0.07 N/m.  
The water column is initialized as a rectangular prism with alpha.water = 1 within the box from (0, 0, 0) to (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75).  
Everywhere outside this box, alpha.water = 0.  
The initial velocity field is zero everywhere in the domain.
