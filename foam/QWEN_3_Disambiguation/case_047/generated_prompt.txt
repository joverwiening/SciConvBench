Simulate turbulent channel flow using the k-epsilon turbulence model.  
The flow is driven by a pressure gradient maintained through a meanVelocityForce in fvOptions.  
The bulk velocity is set to 0.5 m/s.  
The channel height is 0.1 m.  
The kinematic viscosity is 1e-5 m²/s.  
The Reynolds number based on bulk velocity and channel height is 5000.  
The simulation domain is 1 m long in the x-direction, 0.1 m high in the y-direction, and 0.2 m wide in the z-direction.  
The mesh resolution is 100 cells in x, 20 cells in y, and 10 cells in z.  
The grid spacing is Δx = 0.01 m, Δy = 0.005 m, and Δz = 0.02 m.  
The simulation uses the steady-state solver simpleFoam.  
The time step size is set to 1 for solver internal purposes, but physical time stepping is not applicable.  
The k-epsilon turbulence model is fully enabled with standard wall functions at the walls.  
No-slip boundary conditions are applied at the top and bottom walls.  
Periodic boundary conditions are applied in the streamwise (x) and spanwise (z) directions.  
The meanVelocityForce source term enforces a constant bulk velocity of 0.5 m/s in the streamwise direction.
