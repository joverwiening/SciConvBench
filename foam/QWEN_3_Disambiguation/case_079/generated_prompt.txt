Use a 2D VOF (Volume of Fluid) multiphase solver to simulate a single air bubble rising in a quiescent liquid column. 
The computational domain is a rectangle with a height of 0.2 m and a width of 0.05 m. 
The liquid is water with a density of 1000 kg/m³ and a dynamic viscosity of 0.001 Pa·s. 
The air bubble has a diameter of 0.01 m. 
The air bubble is initially centered at coordinates (0.025, 0.025) in the 2D domain, with the y-coordinate representing height from the bottom. 
The simulation assumes gravity acts in the negative y-direction with standard Earth gravity (9.81 m/s²). 
The initial flow field is quiescent (zero velocity everywhere). 
Surface tension effects are to be included with an air-water surface tension coefficient of 0.072 N/m. 
Contact angle at the walls is 180 degrees (slip condition, no wall adhesion). 
Use no-slip boundary conditions on all walls for velocity. 
Pressure-velocity coupling should use the PISO algorithm with appropriate time stepping for Courant number stability. 
The simulation runs until the bubble reaches steady rise velocity or for a specified time period sufficient to observe full development of the rise dynamics.
