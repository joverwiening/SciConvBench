Perform a transient simulation of flow over a cylinder with a Reynolds number of 100 using water as the fluid.
The cylinder has a diameter of 0.1 m.
The free-stream velocity of the water is 0.001 m/s.
The computational domain has a streamwise length of 2.2 m.
The computational domain has a transverse height of 0.41 m.
Use an adaptive time stepping scheme controlled by a maximum Courant number of 1.0.
Set the maximum time step size to 0.01 s.
Run the simulation until the total end time of 200 seconds is reached.
