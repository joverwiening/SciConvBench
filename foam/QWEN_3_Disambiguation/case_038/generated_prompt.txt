Simulate natural convection heat transfer in a porous block with dimensions 0.10 m (width) × 0.10 m (height) × 0.01 m (depth).  
Set the porosity of the porous block to 0.4.  
Assign a hot bottom surface temperature of 310 K.  
Assign a cold top surface temperature of 290 K.  
Set the effective thermal conductivity of the porous block to 0.5 W/(m·K).  
Initialize the temperature of both fluid and solid phases within the porous block to 300 K.  
Use air as the working fluid.  
Enable buoyancy-driven flow due to temperature differences.  
Apply no-slip boundary conditions on all solid walls.  
Model the porous medium using a continuum approach with local thermal equilibrium between solid and fluid phases.  
Solve unsteady if transient behavior is of interest; otherwise, solve steady-state.
