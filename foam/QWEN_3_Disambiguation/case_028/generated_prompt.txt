The simulation is a transient two-phase dam-break with obstacles in a cubic domain spanning (0,0,0) to (1,1,1).  
The domain is discretized uniformly with a grid resolution of 32 x 32 x 32 cells.  
An obstacle is present from (0.375, 0.375, 0) to (0.625, 0.625, 0.25).  
Gravity is set to (0, 0, -9.81) m/s².  
The water phase is initialized as a rectangular prism where alpha.water = 1 within the box (0, 0, 0) to (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75), and alpha.water = 0 elsewhere.  
The fluid properties for water are density = 1000 kg/m³ and viscosity = 1.0e-6 m²/s.  
The fluid properties for air are density = 1 kg/m³ and viscosity = 1.48e-5 m²/s.  
The top face of the cube at z=1 is an open atmosphere boundary patch.  
The remaining five faces of the cube are set as walls.  
The simulation runs with a fixed time step deltaT = 1e-3 s.  
The simulation end time is 0.1 s.  
Results are written at intervals of 0.02 s.
