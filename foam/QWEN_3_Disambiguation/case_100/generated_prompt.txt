The simulation is a 3D turbulent Rayleigh-Bénard convection in a rectangular box with water as the fluid.
The domain has a height of 0.10 m in the z-direction and horizontal dimensions of 0.40 m in both x and y directions, giving an aspect ratio of 4:4:1.
The Rayleigh number is approximately 1e8, based on the fluid properties of water and the specified temperature difference.
The temperature difference between the bottom and top plates is 6.8 K, with the bottom plate at 300.0 K and the top plate at 293.2 K.
The simulation runs for a total of 30 seconds of physical time.
The initial temperature field is linearly interpolated between the bottom and top plate temperatures along the vertical direction.
A small thermal perturbation is introduced as a warm spot with a temperature of 296.65 K in a small subdomain from (0.19, 0.19, 0.00) to (0.21, 0.21, 0.01).
The initial velocity field is zero everywhere in the domain.
No-slip boundary conditions are applied on the top and bottom plates for velocity.
Periodic boundary conditions are applied on the lateral (x and y) boundaries.
Gravity acts in the negative z-direction with standard Earth gravity.
Thermal boundary conditions are constant temperature at the top and bottom plates.
The fluid is assumed to be incompressible with constant density except in the buoyancy term (Boussinesq approximation).
The simulation resolves turbulent flow structures and captures the development of convective cells and plume dynamics.
The output includes time-series and field data for velocity, pressure, and temperature.
Statistical steady state is assessed over the 30-second duration.
