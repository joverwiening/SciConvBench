Simulate a 3D bubble column with dimensions 0.2 m (width) × 1.0 m (height) × 0.2 m (depth).
Use an Euler-Euler multiphase approach to model air and water interaction.

The gas phase volume fraction (alpha.air) at the top outlet uses the `inletOutlet` boundary condition with inletValue 0 and value 0.
The gas phase velocity (U.air) at the top outlet uses the `pressureInletOutletVelocity` boundary condition.
