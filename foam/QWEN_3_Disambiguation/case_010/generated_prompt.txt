The simulation domain is a square cavity with dimensions of 1 unit in the x and y directions and 0.1 units in the z-direction, making it effectively 2D.
The mesh resolution is 20x20 in the x and y directions with 1 cell in the z-direction.
The top wall (movingWall) moves in the x-direction with a uniform velocity of 1 m/s.
All other walls (fixedWalls) have a no-slip boundary condition with zero velocity.
The front and back faces are designated as empty to enforce 2D flow behavior.
The kinematic viscosity (nu) is constant at 1e-05 m²/s.
The k-epsilon turbulence model is used for turbulence closure.
The turbulent kinetic energy (k) is initialized uniformly at 1.5e-4 m²/s² in the domain.
The boundary condition for k uses kqRWallFunction on all walls, with front and back faces set as empty.
The turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) is initialized uniformly at 1.4e-4 m²/s³ in the domain.
The boundary condition for epsilon uses epsilonWallFunction on all walls, with front and back faces set as empty.
The turbulent viscosity (nut) is initialized uniformly to 0 m²/s.
The boundary condition for nut uses nutkWallFunction on all walls, with front and back faces set as empty.
The pisoFoam solver is used for transient incompressible flow simulation.
The initial velocity field is set to zero everywhere in the domain.
The simulation starts at time 0 and runs until a final time of 10 seconds.
The time step (deltaT) is fixed at 0.005 seconds.
The convection term for velocity (div(phi,U)) is discretized using Gauss limitedLinearV 1.
The convection term for turbulent kinetic energy (div(phi,k)) is discretized using Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The convection term for dissipation rate (div(phi,epsilon)) is discretized using Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The viscous term (div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) is discretized using Gauss linear.
