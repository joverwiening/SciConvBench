The porous channel has a rectangular cross-section of 0.1 m x 0.1 m and runs through the center of the solid block along its entire length of 0.5 m.  
The solid block has dimensions of 0.2 m x 0.2 m x 0.5 m.  
The solid block is made of aluminum.  
The porous channel material has a porosity of 0.8, assumed based on typical values for open-cell porous media in conjugate heat transfer applications.  
The fluid flowing through the porous channel is water.  
The inlet velocity of the water is 0.05 m/s in the x-direction.  
The outer surfaces of the solid block have a convective (mixed) thermal boundary condition with a reference temperature of 293.15 K and a heat transfer coefficient of 10 W/m²·K.  
The initial temperature of both the solid block and the fluid is assumed to be 293.15 K, consistent with the reference temperature in the boundary condition.  
The simulation includes conjugate heat transfer between the fluid flowing through the porous medium and the surrounding solid block.  
The simulation is set up in a multi-region framework with separate domains for the solid and the porous fluid regions.
