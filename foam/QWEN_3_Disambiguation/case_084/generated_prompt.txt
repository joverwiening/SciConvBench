Simulate a flame propagating through a closed rectangular tube filled with a stoichiometric hydrogen-air mixture.  
The tube is 0.5 m long with a width of 0.01 m and a height of 0.01 m.  
The initial temperature of the mixture is 300 K and the initial pressure is 101325 Pa.  
The spatial resolution is 500 cells in the axial (x) direction, 5 cells in the width (y) direction, and 5 cells in the height (z) direction.  
The simulation runs for a total time of 0.002 seconds.  
Output results every 50 time steps.  
Ignition is applied as a one-time initialization by setting the temperature to 2000 K in the first 2% of the domain (from x = 0 to x = 0.01 m) using setFieldsDict.  
The ignition is not sustained over time and is applied only at the beginning of the simulation.
