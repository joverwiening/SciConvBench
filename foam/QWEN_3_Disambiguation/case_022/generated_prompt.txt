Place a circular cylinder of diameter 2 m centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).  
Use the solver pimpleFoam for incompressible, unsteady, laminar flow.  
Set the simulation startTime to 0 s and endTime to 0.5 s.  
Use a time step (deltaT) of 0.0025 s.  
Write results to file every 0.05 s.  
Initialize the flow field with a quiescent fluid: velocity U = (0 0 0) and pressure p = 0.  
Model the fluid as generalized Newtonian with the Cross power-law viscosity model.
