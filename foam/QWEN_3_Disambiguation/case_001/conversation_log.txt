Q: What is the domain size and mesh resolution for the simulation?
A: The domain is 9 units wide and 1 unit tall, extruded 1 cell in the spanwise (z) direction. The mesh resolution is 90 cells in the x-direction, 10 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
