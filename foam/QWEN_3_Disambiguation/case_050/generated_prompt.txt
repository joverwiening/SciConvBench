The domain is a 1m x 1m square with a 40x40 structured mesh.  
The simulation is transient and solved using pimpleFoam.  
The top wall has a velocity of 1 m/s in the horizontal direction.  
All other walls are no-slip stationary boundaries.  
The kinematic viscosity is 1e-4 m²/s.  
The initial velocity field is uniform with value (0 0 0) everywhere.  
The initial pressure field is uniform with value 0 everywhere.  
The simulation runs until the final time of 5 seconds.
