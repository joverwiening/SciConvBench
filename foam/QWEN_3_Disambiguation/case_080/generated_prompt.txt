Use a 3D Euler-Euler two-phase model to simulate a fluidized bed.
The fluidized bed has dimensions of 0.1 m (width) × 0.5 m (height) × 0.1 m (depth).
Solid particles are granular with a diameter of 200 microns and density of 2500 kg/m³.
The initial bed has a solid volume fraction of 0.55 in the bottom 0.2 m of the domain.
Above the initial bed, the solid volume fraction is 0.
The gas phase is air with a density of 1.2 kg/m³ and kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m²/s.
The gas enters the domain at the bottom boundary with a uniform superficial velocity of 0.2 m/s in the upward direction.
The top boundary is set as a pressure outlet.
Gravity is enabled and acts in the downward vertical direction.
Interphase momentum exchange is modeled using appropriate drag laws for fluidized granular systems.
