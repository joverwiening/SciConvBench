Simulate a 1D shock tube problem with a diaphragm initially located at x = 0.5 m in a 1-meter-long tube.  
The left side of the diaphragm has an initial pressure of 100,000 Pa and initial density of 1.0 kg/m³.  
The right side of the diaphragm has an initial pressure of 10,000 Pa and initial density of 0.125 kg/m³.  
Use the ideal gas law with specific gas constant R = 287 J/kg·K and ratio of specific heats γ = 1.4.  
Discretize the domain with 400 uniformly spaced grid points in the x-direction, and 1 point each in y and z directions.  
Set the final simulation time to 0.007 seconds, which is sufficient for the right-going shock wave to reflect off the end wall at x = 1 m.  
Stop the simulation precisely at t = 0.007 seconds.
