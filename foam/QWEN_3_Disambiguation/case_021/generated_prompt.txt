Simulate a 2D flow past a circular cylinder.  
The domain is a rectangular prism extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m in the streamwise direction, y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m in the cross-stream direction, and z = -1 m to 1 m in the spanwise direction.  
The cylinder has a diameter of 2 m and is centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).  
The solver used is pimpleFoam for incompressible, unsteady flow.  
The simulation starts at time 0 s and ends at 0.5 s.  
The time step (deltaT) is 0.0025 s.  
Results are written every 0.05 s.  
The initial flow field is quiescent with velocity U = (0 0 0) m/s and pressure p = 0 Pa.  
The inlet boundary condition specifies a free-stream velocity of U = (1 0 0) m/s.  
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 0.01 m²/s.  
The Reynolds number based on cylinder diameter is 200.  
The simulation assumes no-slip boundary conditions on the cylinder surface.  
The top and bottom boundaries are set as slip or far-field as appropriate for 2D flow.  
The outlet boundary condition uses a zero-gradient condition for velocity and fixed pressure.  
The spanwise boundaries are treated as symmetry planes to enforce 2D flow.
