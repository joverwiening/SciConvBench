Assume a porosity (void fraction) of 0.8 for the porous block, which is typical for open-cell metal foams or highly permeable porous media commonly used in such simulations.

  
Simulate compressible flow of air through a heated porous block in a 2D channel.  
The channel spans from x = 0.08 m to x = 0.12 m and has a height of 0.05 m.  
The porous block is 0.04 m long and 0.05 m high, filling the entire cross-section of the channel.  
The flow is steady-state.  
The inlet boundary condition has a fixed total pressure and total temperature.  
The porous block has a porosity (void fraction) of 0.8.  
The porous block is heated, with heat added uniformly throughout its volume.  
Air is treated as an ideal gas.  
Viscous effects and turbulence are modeled appropriately for compressible flow in porous media.
