Set up a transient 3D Euler–Euler air–water bubble column hydrodynamics simulation in OpenFOAM using the twoPhaseEulerFoam solver.
Target compatibility with a commonly used recent ESI-OpenCFD OpenFOAM release that includes twoPhaseEulerFoam (e.g., v2312 or similar).
Model a rectangular column domain of size 0.2 m (x) × 1.0 m (y, vertical) × 0.2 m (z).
Use gravity acting in the negative y direction with magnitude 9.81 m/s².
Use two phases: water as the continuous liquid phase and air as the dispersed gas phase.
Use an Euler–Euler two-fluid formulation with interphase momentum coupling including drag.
Use a constant bubble diameter for the air phase of 0.002 m.
Generate the mesh with blockMesh.
Create a small rectangular gasInlet patch on the bottom boundary to represent a sparger, centered in x–z, with area chosen as a small fraction of the bottom (e.g., 5% of bottom area) to represent a localized injector.
Assign the remaining bottom area as a wall boundary.
Set the top boundary as a pressure outlet/open boundary that allows outflow of both phases.
Set the side boundaries (x-min, x-max, z-min, z-max) as no-slip walls for the liquid and appropriate wall treatment for the gas phase consistent with twoPhaseEulerFoam defaults.
Initialize the domain filled with water with a small background air volume fraction (e.g., alpha.air = 0) except where specified at the inlet.
On the gasInlet patch, set air velocity U.air = fixedValue (0 0.2 0) m/s.
On the gasInlet patch, set air volume fraction alpha.air = fixedValue 0.05.
On the gasInlet patch, set water volume fraction to complement the gas volume fraction (alpha.water = 1 - alpha.air) using consistent boundary conditions.
Set liquid velocity at the gasInlet patch to a suitable inlet condition consistent with gas injection (e.g., zeroGradient or fixedValue (0 0 0)) to avoid imposing liquid inflow unless required by solver stability.
Use standard water and air properties at ambient conditions (e.g., water rho ≈ 998 kg/m³, mu ≈ 1e-3 Pa·s; air rho ≈ 1.2 kg/m³, mu ≈ 1.8e-5 Pa·s) unless overridden by the chosen OpenFOAM version defaults.
Select a drag model appropriate for bubbly flows with a constant bubble diameter (e.g., Schiller-Naumann or Tomiyama as available) and keep other interfacial forces at reasonable defaults unless they cause instability.
Run the simulation transiently with a time step controlled to satisfy stability (e.g., adjustable time step with maxCo specified for each phase as supported by the solver).
Write fields needed to evaluate objectives: alpha.air, U.air, U.water, p_rgh (or p), and any turbulence quantities used.
