A: Use an OpenFOAM **porous baffle boundary condition**, not a fixed Δp jump and not an fvOptions porous zone.

- Pressure-drop coefficients (Darcy–Forchheimer form):

  - **Inertial/Forchheimer coefficient `I = 0`**

Q: What mesh resolution do you want (number of cells in x and y, or target cell sizes), including any refinement at the porous baffle?
