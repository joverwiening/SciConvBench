Simulate steady-state, compressible, laminar flow of air through a heated porous block using the rhoPorousSimpleFoam solver.
GEOMETRY: A 2D channel 0.2 m long and 0.05 m high. A porous block is located from x=0.08 to x=0.12 m.
MESH: Use blockMesh to create a uniform Cartesian mesh of (200 x 50 x 1) cells.
THERMO-PHYSICAL PROPERTIES: In 'constant/thermophysicalProperties', model air as a 'perfectGas' with 'hConst' transport. MolWeight=28.9, Cp=1005, R=287.
POROUS MEDIA & HEAT SOURCE: In 'constant/fvOptions', define a 'porosity' source term for the porous block cellZone with 'DarcyForchheimer' model, d=(1e9 1e9 1e11), f=(0 0 0). Also in 'fvOptions', add a 'scalarSemiImplicitSource' for the energy equation 'h', with volume mode 'absolute' and injection rate S_h = 1e6 W/m^3 within the same cellZone.
INITIAL & BOUNDARY CONDITIONS (in '0/' directory):
- p: inlet uses 'totalPressure' with p0=101325 Pa; outlet uses 'fixedValue' with value=100000 Pa; walls are zeroGradient.
- T: inlet uses 'totalTemperature' with T0=300 K; outlet is inletOutlet with inletValue 300 K; top/bottom walls are zeroGradient.
- U: inlet uses 'pressureInletOutletVelocity'; outlet is 'inletOutlet'; walls are 'noSlip'.
TIME CONTROLS: In 'system/controlDict', set application = rhoPorousSimpleFoam, stopAt = endTime, endTime = 2000, deltaT = 1.
