Q: What Reynolds number (or equivalently the kinematic viscosity nu, given lid speed and cavity length) should the case use?
A: Use a constant kinematic viscosity **nu = 1e-05 m²/s** (with lid speed **U = 1 m/s** and cavity length **L = 1**, this corresponds to **Re = U·L/nu = 1e5**).
