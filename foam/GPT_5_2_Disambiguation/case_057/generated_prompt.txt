Use inlet at x=0, outlet at x=L, bottomWall at y=0, topWall at y=H, and frontAndBack in z.
Use a uniform inlet velocity U = (1 0 0) m/s at the inlet with fixedValue.
Use a fixedValue pressure p = 0 at the outlet.
Use kqRWallFunction for k at topWall and bottomWall.
Use epsilonWallFunction for epsilon at topWall and bottomWall.
Use noSlip (fixedValue (0 0 0)) for U at topWall and bottomWall.

Initialize internal fields and remaining boundary conditions consistently for a stable start (e.g., p with zeroGradient at inlet, U with zeroGradient at outlet, and suitable k/epsilon inlet values consistent with turbulent intensity and length scale).
Run pisoFoam in transient mode until statistically steady behavior is reached, then time-average key quantities.
Post-process to report centerline velocity development, wall shear stress, and pressure drop along the channel.
