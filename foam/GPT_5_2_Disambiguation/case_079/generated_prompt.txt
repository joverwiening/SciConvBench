Model a single air bubble rising in a quiescent water column using OpenFOAM interFoam in a 2D x–y domain implemented as a thin 3D mesh with 1 cell in z.
Use two incompressible, immiscible phases with water as phase 1 and air as phase 2.
Set water properties to density 1000 kg/m³ and kinematic viscosity 1e-6 m²/s.
Set air properties to density 1.2 kg/m³ and kinematic viscosity 1.5e-5 m²/s.
Set surface tension between water and air to 0.072 N/m.
Create a rectangular domain of width 0.05 m in x and height 0.2 m in y.
Use a uniform Cartesian mesh with 50 cells in x, 200 cells in y, and 1 cell in z.
Set gravity to g = (0 -9.81 0) m/s².
Initialize the velocity field to zero everywhere.
Initialize a single circular air bubble of diameter 0.01 m.
Place the initial bubble center at (x, y) = (0.025 m, 0.025 m).
Initialize the volume fraction field so the bubble region is air and the surrounding liquid is water.
Apply noSlip for U on the left boundary.
Apply zeroGradient for p_rgh on the left boundary.
Apply zeroGradient for alpha.water on the left boundary.
Apply noSlip for U on the right boundary.
Apply zeroGradient for p_rgh on the right boundary.
Apply zeroGradient for alpha.water on the right boundary.
Apply noSlip for U on the bottom boundary.
Apply zeroGradient for p_rgh on the bottom boundary.
Apply zeroGradient for alpha.water on the bottom boundary.
Apply inletOutlet for U on the top boundary to allow outflow.
Apply inletOutlet for p_rgh on the top boundary to allow outflow.
Apply inletOutlet for alpha.water on the top boundary to allow outflow.
Run the transient simulation from t = 0 to endTime = 2.0 s.
Use adjustable time stepping with a target maximum Courant number of 0.5 and set maxDeltaT to 1e-3 s.
