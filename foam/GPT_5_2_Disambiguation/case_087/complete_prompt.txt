Simulate inviscid-like supersonic flow (Mach 3) over a 2D forward-facing step using rhoCentralFoam.

Solver: rhoCentralFoam

Geometry: 2D domain 3 m (x) x 1 m (y), step height 0.2 m starting at x=0.6 m.
Use a multi-block blockMesh: one block upstream of the step and two blocks downstream (split at y=0.2).
2D: set frontAndBack to empty.

Mesh: ~150 cells in x and ~50 cells in y (refine near the step corner and along expected shock paths).

Thermophysical properties (constant/thermophysicalProperties): ideal air
- hePsiThermo / perfectGas / hConst / const transport
- Cp ~ 1005 J/kg/K; molWeight ~ 28.9 kg/kmol
To approximate inviscid flow, use laminar (no turbulence) and set dynamic viscosity very small (e.g., mu = 1e-12) in the transport model.

Freestream conditions: Ma=3 at T=300 K => U ~ 1020 m/s, p=101325 Pa, T=300 K.

Boundary conditions:
- inlet: supersonicFreestream with U=(1020.6 0 0), p=101325, T=300.
- outlet: waveTransmissive for p with pInf 101325; U and T zeroGradient (or waveTransmissive).
- bottomWall (including the step faces): slip.
- topWall: slip.
- frontAndBack: empty.

Initial conditions: initialize the domain to the freestream state (U, p, T as above).

Time controls: endTime 0.005 s; adjustTimeStep yes; maxCo 0.7; writeInterval 0.0005 s.
