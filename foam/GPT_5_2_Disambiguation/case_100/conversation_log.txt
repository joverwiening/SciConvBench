Q: What numerical method or software framework should be used to run the 3D Rayleigh–Bénard simulation?
A: Use **OpenFOAM** with the **buoyantBoussinesqPimpleFoam** solver (Boussinesq, transient PIMPLE-based).

Q: What are the domain dimensions and aspect ratio for the Rayleigh–Bénard cell (e.g., Lx:Ly:Lz)?
A: Use a 3D box with aspect ratio **4:4:1**:

- **Lx = 0.40 m**
- **Ly = 0.40 m**
- **Lz (H) = 0.10 m**

=== 2026-02-16 14:09:41 ===
Q: What boundary conditions should be applied on the top and bottom walls (temperature and velocity)?
A: - **Bottom wall (bottomHot, z = 0):**
  - **Velocity (U):** `noSlip`
  - **Temperature (T):** `fixedValue 300.0` K

- **Top wall (topCold, z = H):**
  - **Velocity (U):** `noSlip`
  - **Temperature (T):** `fixedValue 293.2` K

=== 2026-02-16 14:09:45 ===
Q: What boundary conditions should be applied on the four side walls (temperature and velocity)?
A: Use **periodic (cyclic)** lateral boundaries on all four side walls:

- **xMin ↔ xMax:** `cyclic` for **U** and **T** (and all other fields).
- **yMin ↔ yMax:** `cyclic` for **U** and **T** (and all other fields).

These are defined as **cyclic neighbourPatch pairs** in `blockMeshDict` for `xMin/xMax` and `yMin/yMax`.

=== 2026-02-16 14:09:49 ===
Q: What turbulence modeling approach should be used (DNS with no turbulence model, or a specific RANS/LES model)?
A: Use a **RANS** turbulence model: **kOmegaSST** (RAS) in `constant/momentumTransport`, with **wall functions on the top/bottom plates** and **cyclic** conditions on the lateral boundaries. If you prefer, **LES is also acceptable**, but the default is **RAS kOmegaSST**.

=== 2026-02-16 14:09:53 ===
Q: What initial conditions should be used for the velocity and temperature fields at t = 0?
A: - **Velocity `U` at t = 0:** initialize to **(0 0 0)** everywhere.

- **Temperature `T` at t = 0:** initialize as either
  - a **linear profile** between the plates (300.0 K at z=0 to 293.2 K at z=0.10 m), **or**
  - **uniform `T0 = 296.6 K`** everywhere.

- **Perturbation (to trigger convection):** use `setFields` to add a small warm spot near the bottom center, e.g. set **T = T0 + 0.05 K** in a box:
  - from **(0.19 0.19 0.00)** to **(0.21 0.21 0.01)**.

=== 2026-02-16 14:09:58 ===
Q: Which of the two temperature initializations should be used as the single definitive choice: the linear profile between plates or the uniform 296.6 K field?
A: Use the **linear temperature profile between the plates** as the single definitive initialization (300.0 K at z=0 to 293.2 K at z=0.10 m), then apply the small warm-spot perturbation via `setFields`.
