Q: What boundary condition type do you want on the six outer faces of the cube (e.g., noSlip walls, slip walls, or open boundaries)?
A: Set the **top face (z = 1)** as an **open atmosphere** patch (open boundary). Set the **other five outer faces** (**x=0, x=1, y=0, y=1, z=0**) as **walls** (no-slip).
