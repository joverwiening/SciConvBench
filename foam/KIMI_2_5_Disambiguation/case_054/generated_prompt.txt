Simulate an oblique shock wave with the following parameters:

The freestream Mach number is 2.0.
The wedge deflection angle is 10 degrees.
The ratio of specific heats gamma is 1.4.
The specific gas constant R is assumed to be 287 J/(kg·K), which is the standard value for air.
The simulation calculates the oblique shock wave angle using the theta-beta-Mach relation.
The simulation computes the downstream Mach number, pressure ratio, temperature ratio, and density ratio across the shock.
The simulation assumes calorically perfect gas behavior.
The simulation outputs the shock angle in degrees and the post-shock flow properties.
