Problem: Simulate a 2D, inviscid, supersonic flow at Mach 2 over a 10-degree wedge to visualize the resulting oblique shock.
Solver: Use the density-based solver `rhoCentralFoam`.
Geometry: A rectangular domain of 1.0m x 0.5m. The wedge starts at x=0.2m on the bottom boundary, with its tip at (0.2, 0). The wedge surface extends to the outlet at a 10-degree angle.
Mesh: A uniform structured mesh of 200x100 cells created with `blockMeshDict`.
Thermo-physical Properties: `perfectGas` with `const` transport. Free-stream static temperature T=300K, static pressure p=101325 Pa. Gamma = 1.4.
Boundary Conditions (in `0/` directory):
- `inlet`: `fixedValue` for U, p, and T corresponding to M=2 free-stream conditions. U=(694.5 0 0) m/s, p=101325 Pa, T=300 K.
- `outlet`: `zeroGradient` for all variables (supersonic outflow).
- `bottomWall`: `slip` condition.
- `topWall`: `slip` condition to represent the far-field.
- `frontAndBack`: `empty`.
Time Controls (controlDict):
- `application`: `rhoCentralFoam`
- `endTime`: 0.005 s
- `deltaT`: 5e-7 s
- `writeInterval`: 0.001 s
- `fvSchemes`: Use high-resolution schemes like `vanLeer` or `limitedLinear` for convective terms.
