The simulation shall use the buoyantFoam solver with gravity acting in the negative y direction.

The reference state shall be air-like with density 1.0 kg/m^3, reference temperature 300 K, thermal expansion coefficient 3.3e-3 1/K, kinematic viscosity 1.5e-5 m^2/s, and thermal diffusivity 2.1e-5 m^2/s (Pr ~ 0.7).

The bottom wall temperature shall be fixed at 305 K and the top wall at 295 K, while the vertical side walls shall be adiabatic.

No-slip velocity conditions shall be applied on all solid walls.

The flow shall be initialized at rest with a uniform temperature field of 300 K.

A fixed timestep of 1 s shall be used with deltaT = 1 s, adjustTimeStep disabled, and no maxCo or maxDeltaT set.

The simulation shall run for 1000 s of physical time.

Results shall be written every 50 steps.

In constant/physicalProperties, heRhoThermo shall be used with equationOfState Boussinesq, molWeight 29, rho0 1.0, T0 300, beta 3.3e-3, Cv 714.3, Hf 0, mu 1.5e-5, and Pr 0.714.

In 0/k, internalField shall be uniform 1e-5 with kqRWallFunction on walls and empty on front/back.

In 0/epsilon, internalField shall be uniform 1e-5 with epsilonWallFunction on walls and empty on front/back.

In 0/nut, internalField shall be uniform 0 with nutkWallFunction on walls and empty on front/back.

In 0/alphat, internalField shall be uniform 0 with compressible::alphatJayatillekeWallFunction on walls, Prt 0.85, and empty on front/back.

In 0/U, internalField shall be uniform (0 0 0) with noSlip on all solid walls and empty on front/back.

In 0/T, internalField shall be uniform 300, bottom fixedValue 305, top fixedValue 295, sides zeroGradient, and front/back empty.

In 0/p, internalField shall be uniform 0 with walls calculated and front/back empty.

In 0/p_rgh, internalField shall be uniform 0 with walls fixedFluxPressure and front/back empty.

In system/fvSolution, p_rgh and (U|e|k|epsilon) solvers shall have tolerance 1e-8 and relTol 0.01, and the corresponding *Final solvers shall have relTol 0.
