The simulation shall be a transient laminar two-phase dam-break using the interFoam solver in OpenFOAM.

The computational domain shall be a cube spanning from (0, 0, 0) to (1, 1, 1) meters.

The mesh shall be uniform with 32 cells in each direction, resulting in a total of 32 x 32 x 32 = 32,768 hexahedral cells.

An obstacle shall be present in the domain occupying the rectangular region from (0.375, 0.375, 0) to (0.625, 0.625, 0.25) meters.

Gravity shall be set to (0, 0, -9.81) m/s² acting in the negative z-direction.

The boundary patch at z = 1 (top face) shall be defined as a pressureInletOutletVelocity type with atmospheric pressure, representing an open atmosphere.

The remaining five boundary faces (x = 0, x = 1, y = 0, y = 1, z = 0) shall be defined as no-slip wall boundaries.

The initial condition for the water phase shall be set using the setFields utility to define alpha.water = 1 within the rectangular prism from (0, 0, 0) to (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75) meters, and alpha.water = 0 elsewhere in the domain.

The time step deltaT shall be 0.001 seconds.

The simulation endTime shall be 0.1 seconds.

The writeInterval shall be 0.02 seconds, resulting in output at t = 0, 0.02, 0.04, 0.06, 0.08, and 0.1 seconds.

The turbulence model shall be set to laminar.

The two phases shall be water and air with standard interFoam properties (water density 1000 kg/m³, air density 1 kg/m³, water kinematic viscosity 1e-6 m²/s, air kinematic viscosity 1.48e-5 m²/s, surface tension 0.07 N/m).
