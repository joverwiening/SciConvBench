The simulation shall model a compressible, turbulent lid-driven cavity flow with the following specifications:

The cavity geometry is a square domain with height and width of 1 meter.

The top lid moves at a constant velocity of 100 m/s in the positive x-direction.

The remaining three walls are stationary no-slip walls.

The initial thermodynamic state is uniform throughout the domain with pressure 101325 Pa and temperature 300 K.

The fluid is air modeled as a perfect gas with equation of state p = rho * R * T where R is the specific gas constant for air.

The Sutherland viscosity model shall be used with reference coefficient As = 1.4792e-6 kg/(m·s·K^0.5) and reference temperature Ts = 110.4 K.

The turbulence model is kOmegaSST with compressible wall functions.

The Reynolds number based on lid velocity and cavity height is approximately 6.4 million.

The Mach number based on lid velocity and speed of sound at 300 K is approximately 0.29 indicating subsonic compressible flow.

All walls are treated as adiabatic no-slip boundaries.

The initial velocity field is zero everywhere except the moving lid.

The simulation requires sufficient mesh resolution to resolve boundary layers and turbulent structures appropriate for the kOmegaSST model with wall functions.
