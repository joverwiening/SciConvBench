The simulation models a 1D shock tube problem using rhoCentralFoam with a Kurganov-Tadmor central-upwind scheme for shock-capturing.

The domain is a 1-meter tube with the diaphragm initially at x = 0.5 m.

The left state (x < 0.5 m) has pressure 100000 Pa, temperature 348.4 K, and density approximately 1.0 kg/m³.

The right state (x > 0.5 m) has pressure 10000 Pa, temperature 278.7 K, and density approximately 0.125 kg/m³.

Both sides start with zero velocity.

The gas is air with R = 287 J/(kg·K), gamma = 1.4, Cp = 1005 J/(kg·K), and molecular weight 28.9 kg/kmol.

The simulation runs from time 0 to 0.007 seconds with a fixed timestep of 1e-6 seconds.

The left boundary at x = 0 uses slip wall for velocity, zeroGradient for pressure, and zeroGradient for temperature.

The right boundary at x = 1 m uses slip wall for velocity, zeroGradient for pressure, and zeroGradient for temperature.

The y and z boundary patches are set to empty to enforce 1D flow.

The solver captures the shock wave propagation, contact discontinuity, and expansion fan until the shock reflects off the right end wall.
