Model a pulverized coal combustion simulation in a 2D rectangular furnace with the following specifications:

The furnace geometry is a 2D rectangle with height 2.0 m and width 0.5 m.

The primary air/coal inlet is located at the bottom center of the furnace.

The outlet is located at the top of the furnace.

The inlet velocity of the primary air/coal mixture is U=(0 20 0) m/s.

The mass flow rate of coal particles being injected is 0.1 kg/s.

The temperature of the primary air at the inlet is 400 K.

The furnace walls are fixed at a temperature of 1200 K.

Use a Rosin-Rammler particle size distribution with a mean diameter of 100 microns and a spread parameter n=2.0.

The coal particle density is 1300 kg/m³.

The furnace operates with only primary air; there is no secondary air inlet.

The primary air composition at the inlet has O2 mass fraction of 0.233 and N2 mass fraction of 0.767.

Include radiation heat transfer in the simulation.

The simulation involves two-way coupling between the discrete particle phase and the continuous gas phase.
