Simulate 2D Rayleigh-Benard convection in a square cavity using buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam.

The domain is a 0.1 m x 0.1 m square cavity with empty frontAndBack patches, effectively 50 x 50 x 1 cells.

The bottom wall is heated to 294.0 K and the top wall is cooled to 293.0 K, giving a temperature difference of 1 K.

The fluid is air with kinematic viscosity 1.5e-5 m²/s, Prandtl number 0.71, thermal expansion coefficient 3.33e-3 1/K, reference density 1.0 kg/m³, and reference temperature 293.5 K.

Thermal diffusivity is 2.11e-5 m²/s.

Gravitational acceleration is (0 -9.81 0) m/s², targeting Rayleigh number ~1e5.

The solver is buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam with steady-state pseudo-time iterations.

End time is 5000 with deltaT 1, using residualControl for convergence stopping.

Residual control thresholds are 1e-6 for velocity U, 1e-6 for pressure p, and 1e-8 for temperature T.

Maximum iterations per time step is 100.

Side walls use zeroGradient for velocity and temperature (adiabatic, slip).

Initial conditions are zero velocity and reference temperature 293.5 K throughout the domain.

Turbulence model is laminar (no turbulence modeling).
