The simulation shall model a classic Sod shock tube problem using rhoCentralFoam with the following specifications:

The computational domain is a 1 m long thin 3D box extending from (0 0 0) to (1 0.1 0.1) meters.

The mesh shall be generated using blockMesh with 400 × 1 × 1 cells, creating a uniform grid along the x-axis with single cells in the y and z directions.

The initial condition split is located at x = 0.5 m.

For the left state where x < 0.5 m: pressure is 100000 Pa, temperature is 348.4 K, velocity is (0 0 0) m/s, and density is computed from the perfect-gas equation of state.

For the right state where x >= 0.5 m: pressure is 10000 Pa, temperature is 278.7 K, velocity is (0 0 0) m/s, and density is computed from the perfect-gas equation of state.

The gas is ideal air with molecular weight 28.9 kg/kmol, giving R_specific = 287.7 J/(kg·K), Cp = 1005 J/(kg·K), and gamma = 1.4.

The simulation shall run with endTime of 0.007 s and fixed time step deltaT of 1e-6 s, with optional adjustTimeStep enabled and maxCo set to 0.5.

Boundary conditions at leftEnd (x=0) and rightEnd (x=1) are wall type with slip velocity, zeroGradient pressure, and zeroGradient temperature.

Boundary conditions on side walls (y=0, y=0.1, z=0, z=0.1) are empty to enforce 1D behavior.
