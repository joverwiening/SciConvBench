Simulate turbulent flow in a 2D channel using pisoFoam and the k-epsilon model.
The Reynolds number is 10,000 based on inlet velocity U = 1 m/s, channel height L = 0.1 m, and kinematic viscosity ν = 1e-5 m²/s.
Use a mesh with 200 cells in the streamwise (x) direction, 20 cells in the wall-normal (y) direction, and 1 cell in the depth (z) direction.
Set the channel length in the streamwise (x) direction to 2 meters.
Set the time step size (deltaT) to 0.001 seconds.
Set the total simulation time (endTime) to 5.0 seconds.
Apply a uniform inlet velocity of 1 m/s at the inlet boundary.
Apply a zero-gradient pressure boundary condition at the outlet.
Apply no-slip wall boundary conditions on the top and bottom channel walls.
Use the standard k-epsilon model with default coefficients for turbulence modeling.
Initialize the flow field with velocity U = 0 m/s, turbulent kinetic energy k = 0.01 m²/s², and turbulent dissipation rate epsilon = 0.1 m²/s³.
Write output data every 0.1 seconds of simulation time.
