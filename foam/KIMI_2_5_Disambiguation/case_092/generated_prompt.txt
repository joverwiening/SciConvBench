The simulation models Rayleigh-Benard convection in a 2D or 3D rectangular domain.

The domain has a width of 0.4 m and a height of 0.1 m, giving an aspect ratio of 4:1.

The bottom boundary is heated to 305 K and the top boundary is cooled to 295 K, creating a temperature difference of 10 K.

The fluid is air-like with a kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m²/s.

The thermal expansion coefficient is 3.33e-3 1/K.

The thermal diffusivity is 2.11e-5 m²/s, calculated as nu / Pr = 1.5e-5 / 0.71.

The Prandtl number is 0.71.

Gravitational acceleration is 9.81 m/s² acting in the negative y-direction, represented as the vector (0, -9.81, 0).

The Rayleigh number can be calculated as Ra = g * beta * delta_T * H^3 / (nu * alpha) = 9.81 * 3.33e-3 * 10 * 0.1^3 / (1.5e-5 * 2.11e-5) = 1.03e6, indicating convection will occur.

Boundary conditions: no-slip walls on all boundaries, fixed temperature on top and bottom (Dirichlet), and adiabatic or periodic conditions on side walls.

Initial condition: linear temperature profile between bottom and top, with small random perturbations to seed instability.

The simulation should use the Boussinesq approximation for buoyancy.
