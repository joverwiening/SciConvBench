Simulate supersonic flow over a forward-facing step using a compressible flow solver.

The inlet Mach number is 3.0.

The step height is 0.02 m.

The channel height is 0.1 m, giving a step-to-channel height ratio of 0.2.

The computational domain extends 0.5 m in total length, with the step located at x = 0.1 m, leaving 0.4 m downstream of the step.

The inlet static pressure is 101325 Pa.

The inlet static temperature is 293 K.

The ratio of specific heats (gamma) is 1.4.

The specific gas constant (R) is 287 J/kg·K.

The simulation is 2D with unit depth, using empty boundary conditions for the frontAndBack patches in OpenFOAM convention.

The objective is to visualize the shock structure formed by the supersonic flow interacting with the forward-facing step.
