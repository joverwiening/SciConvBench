Simulate natural convection in a closed 2D room using the buoyantPimpleFoam solver.

The room has a width of 4m and a height of 3m, with frontAndBack patches set to empty to enforce 2D simulation.

The heat source is located on the floor, spanning from x = 1.5m to x = 2.5m, with a total width of 1m and centered horizontally.

The heat source temperature is fixed at 323 K.

The initial temperature of the air throughout the domain is 293 K.

The ceiling patch, side wall patches (leftWall and rightWall), and the floor outside the heat source region (named floorInsulated) all use zeroGradient boundary conditions for temperature, representing adiabatic walls with no heat flux.

The simulation runs from time 0 to endTime = 120 seconds with a fixed time step deltaT = 0.02 seconds.

Results are written to disk every writeInterval = 2 seconds.

Enable function objects for monitoring: sample lines at x = 2.0m (centerline) and y = 1.5m (mid-height) to extract temperature and velocity profiles; fieldAverage to compute mean temperature and velocity fields over the last 60 seconds of simulation; and wallHeatFlux to monitor heat transfer from the heat source.

Use the perfectGas equation of state for air with Sutherland transport model (As = 1.479e-06, Ts = 110.4 K) to compute kinematic viscosity dynamically.

Use the Boussinesq approximation for buoyancy with thermal expansion coefficient beta = 1/T computed automatically by the solver from local temperature.

Set Prandtl number to 0.71 for air.

Initialize velocity field to zero throughout the domain.

Set gravity to (0, -9.81, 0) m/s^2 acting in the negative y-direction.

Use second-order schemes for convection terms (Gauss linearUpwind) and diffusion terms (Gauss linear corrected).

Set turbulence model to laminar since the Rayleigh number is expected to be moderate for this configuration.
