The simulation shall use a rectangular domain spanning x from 0 to 0.02 m, y from -0.01 to 0.01 m, and z from -0.01 to 0.01 m. The mesh shall consist of 100 grid points in the x direction, 40 in the y direction, and 1 cell in the z direction. The left boundary at x=0 shall be named "fuel" and the right boundary at x=0.02 shall be named "air". The top and bottom boundaries shall be named "outlet". The front and back faces shall be set to "empty". The chemistry type shall be EulerImplicit with an ODE solver of Rosenbrock43. The combustion model shall be laminar and the momentum transport shall be laminar. The thermophysical model shall use compressible finite-rate chemistry with hePsiThermo, coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture mixing, Sutherland transport, and JANAF thermodynamics. The solver shall be reactingFoam. The timestep shall be 1e-4 seconds with an end time of 0.5 seconds and results written at intervals of 0.05 seconds. At the fuel boundary (x=0), the inlet shall have a temperature of 293 K, velocity of 0.1 m/s in the x-direction, and species mass fractions of Y_CH4 = 1.0 (pure methane). At the air boundary (x=0.02), the inlet shall have a temperature of 293 K, velocity of -0.1 m/s in the x-direction, and species mass fractions of Y_O2 = 0.23 and Y_N2 = 0.77. The initial condition for the entire domain at t=0 shall be a temperature of 2000 K, pressure of 101325 Pa, zero velocity (U = 0 m/s), and species mass fractions of Y_N2 = 1.0 (pure nitrogen).
