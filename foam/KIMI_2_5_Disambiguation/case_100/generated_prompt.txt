The simulation shall model 3D turbulent Rayleigh-Benard convection with the following specifications:

The fluid is water with kinematic viscosity nu = 1.0e-6 m²/s and thermal diffusivity alpha = 1.4e-7 m²/s.

The Rayleigh number is Ra = 1.0e8.

The Prandtl number is Pr = nu/alpha = 7.14.

The domain size has aspect ratio 4:4:1 for x:y:z (horizontal width : horizontal depth : vertical height).

The grid resolution is 80 x 80 x 40 grid points in the x, y, and z directions respectively.

The time stepping uses an adaptive time step based on the CFL condition with initial time step deltaT = 1.0e-3, adjustTimeStep enabled, and maximum Courant number maxCo = 0.9.

The temperature boundary conditions are fixed temperature with hot bottom and cold top: T_bottom = 300.0 K, T_top = 293.2 K.

The velocity boundary conditions are no-slip at the top and bottom walls.

The velocity boundary conditions at the side walls (x and y directions) are cyclic (periodic).
