The simulation models a de Laval nozzle with air as the working fluid.

The working fluid is air modeled as a perfect gas with gamma = 1.4 and constant specific heats. Use `perfectGas` with `const` transport in the thermophysicalProperties file.

The inlet total pressure is 5e5 Pa and total temperature is 600 K. Use `totalPressure` p0 = 5e5 Pa and `totalTemperature` T0 = 600 K at the inlet, with `zeroGradient` for velocity U.

The outlet boundary condition uses `fixedValue` for static pressure p = 3.5e5 Pa, with `zeroGradient` for T and U.

The nozzle geometry has a total length of 0.3 m with the throat located at x = 0.1 m. The inlet height is 0.1 m, the throat height is 0.05 m, and the outlet height is 0.08 m, giving an exit-to-throat area ratio of 1.6.

The nozzle walls use `arc` edges in `blockMeshDict` for a smooth profile. The radius of curvature for the converging section arc is 0.15 m. The radius of curvature for the diverging section arc is 0.20 m.

The solver is `rhoCentralFoam` for compressible flow with shock capturing.

The mesh should be refined near the throat and in the diverging section where the normal shock is expected to form.

The simulation should run until steady state is reached or for a sufficient number of iterations to capture the shock structure.
