Simulate a 2D buoyant cavity flow with the following specifications.

The cavity has width 1 m and height 1 m.

The left wall is maintained at a hot temperature of 303 K.

The right wall is maintained at a cold temperature of 293 K.

The top and bottom walls are adiabatic (no heat flux).

The initial temperature of the fluid throughout the cavity is 293 K.

The fluid is air with kinematic viscosity nu = 1.0e-5 m^2/s.

The Prandtl number is Pr = 0.71.

The thermal diffusivity alpha is calculated as nu / Pr = 1.408e-5 m^2/s.

The thermal expansion coefficient beta = 0.00333 1/K.

The reference temperature Tref = 293 K.

Gravity acts in the negative y-direction with g = (0, -9.81, 0) m/s^2.

The Rayleigh number is Ra = g * |beta| * deltaT * L^3 / (nu * alpha) = 9.81 * 0.00333 * 10 * 1^3 / (1.0e-5 * 1.408e-5) = approximately 2.32e6.

Use no-slip boundary conditions on all walls (velocity = 0).

Solve the incompressible Navier-Stokes equations with the Boussinesq approximation for buoyancy.

The buoyancy term is added as a source term in the y-momentum equation: -beta * (T - Tref) * g_y.

Use a 2D Cartesian grid with sufficient resolution to capture the boundary layers (recommend at least 100x100 cells or higher for this Rayleigh number).

Run the simulation until a steady state is reached or for a sufficient number of time steps to observe the characteristic circulation pattern.

Output the velocity field, temperature field, and stream function for analysis.
