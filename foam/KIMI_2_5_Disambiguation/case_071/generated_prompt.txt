The simulation models 2D lid-driven cavity flow with the following specifications.

The Reynolds number is Re = 100, calculated from U = 1 m/s, L = 0.1 m, and ν = 0.01 m²/s.

The computational domain is a square cavity with dimensions 0.1 m × 0.1 m.

The grid resolution is 20 × 20 × 1 cells, with uniform spacing in all directions.

The top wall moves horizontally at velocity U = 1 m/s in the positive x-direction.

The remaining three walls (bottom, left, right) are stationary no-slip walls with zero velocity.

The fluid is incompressible with constant kinematic viscosity ν = 0.01 m²/s and density ρ = 1 kg/m³ (implied from given parameters).

The time step size is Δt = 0.005 s.

The total simulation duration is 0.5 s, requiring 100 time steps to reach completion.

The initial condition is fluid at rest (zero velocity) throughout the domain.

Boundary conditions are Dirichlet velocity conditions on all walls.
