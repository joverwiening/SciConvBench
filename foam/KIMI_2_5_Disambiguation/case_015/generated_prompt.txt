The simulation shall use a rectangular domain with x ranging from 0 to 0.02 m, y from -0.01 to 0.01 m, and z from -0.01 to 0.01 m.

The left boundary at x=0 shall be named "fuel" and the right boundary at x=0.02 shall be named "air".

The top and bottom boundaries shall be named "outlet".

The front and back faces shall be set to empty.

At the fuel inlet, methane (CH4) shall be injected with a mass fraction Y_CH4 of 1.0 and temperature of 293 K.

At the air inlet, oxidizer shall be supplied as air (O2/N2) at 293 K with Y_O2 of 0.23 and the balance being N2.

The domain shall be initialized with a temperature of 2000 K at time 0.

The velocity at the fuel boundary shall be 0.1 m/s in the positive x-direction.

The velocity at the air boundary shall be -0.1 m/s in the negative x-direction.

The chemistry type shall be EulerImplicit.

The ODE solver shall be Rosenbrock43.

The combustion model shall be laminar.

The momentum transport shall be laminar.

The simulation shall use compressible finite-rate chemistry.

The thermophysical model shall use hePsiThermo.

The mixture model shall be coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture.

The transport model shall be Sutherland.

The thermodynamics model shall be JANAF.

The solver shall be reactingFoam.

The pressure at both fuel and air inlets shall be set to 101325 Pa (standard atmospheric pressure) as a reasonable assumption based on best practices for open atmospheric combustion simulations.

No turbulence model shall be used as the simulation is specified to be laminar.
