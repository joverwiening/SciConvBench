The simulation is a transient two-phase dam-break with obstacles.

The computational domain is a cube spanning from (0, 0, 0) to (1, 1, 1).

The domain is discretized uniformly with a mesh resolution of 32 x 32 x 32 cells.

An obstacle is present in the domain occupying the rectangular region from (0.375, 0.375, 0) to (0.625, 0.625, 0.25).

Gravity is set to g = (0, 0, -9.81) m/s².

The water column is initialized as a rectangular prism where the volume fraction alpha.water equals 1 inside the box from (0, 0, 0) to (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75) and 0 elsewhere.

The time step deltaT is set to 0.001 seconds.

The simulation endTime is 0.1 seconds.

Results are written at intervals of 0.02 seconds.

The simulation uses a two-phase flow solver with interface capturing between water and air.

No turbulence model is specified; use laminar flow assumption.

Boundary conditions on all domain faces are set to wall (no-slip) for velocity and zero-gradient for pressure and volume fraction.

Initial velocity field is zero throughout the domain.

Initial pressure field is hydrostatic based on the water column height with atmospheric pressure at the top boundary.

The obstacle is treated as a no-slip wall boundary for both phases.

Surface tension effects are neglected.

The interface compression scheme is used for the volume fraction equation.
