The simulation models a dam break scenario using the interFoam solver for two incompressible, immiscible fluids.

The computational domain is a rectangular tank with dimensions 0.5715 m in length, 0.4 m in height, and 0.01 m in thickness.

The initial condition consists of a water column located on the left side of the tank with height 0.2922 m and width 0.1461 m, with the remainder of the tank filled with air.

The simulation runs for a total duration of 1.0 seconds.

The time stepping uses a fixed initial time step of 0.001 seconds with automatic adjustment enabled and a maximum Courant number limit of 1.0.

The mesh consists of a structured grid with 114 cells in the length direction, 80 cells in the height direction, and 1 cell in the thickness direction.

Water properties are defined as density 1000 kg/m³ and kinematic viscosity 1e-6 m²/s.

Air properties are defined as density 1 kg/m³ and kinematic viscosity 1.48e-5 m²/s.

Gravitational acceleration is set to 9.81 m/s² acting in the negative y-direction, specified as vector (0 -9.81 0).
