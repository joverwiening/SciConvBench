The simulation models a de Laval nozzle with air as the working fluid.

The inlet stagnation pressure is 200000 Pa.

The inlet stagnation temperature is 300 K.

The nozzle geometry is defined as 2D planar with a total length of 1 m.

The inlet height is 0.2 m.

The throat height is 0.1 m located at x=0.5 m.

The outlet height is 0.3 m.

The area ratio of throat to exit is 0.333.

The simulation uses empty frontAndBack patches for 2D planar treatment.

The depth is set to 1 m in blockMeshDict.

A steady-state solver is used.

The flow transitions from subsonic at the inlet to supersonic at the outlet.
