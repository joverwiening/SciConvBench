The cavity geometry is a square with dimensions 1 unit by 1 unit in the x and y directions, and 0.1 units in the z-direction, making it effectively a 2D domain.

The simulation runs from time 0 to 10 seconds with a fixed time step of 0.005 seconds.

Results are written to disk every 100 time steps, corresponding to every 0.5 seconds of simulation time.

The kinematic viscosity nu is set to a constant value of 1e-05 m^2/s.

The turbulence model is k-epsilon.

The turbulent kinetic energy field k is initialized uniformly to 1.5e-4 throughout the domain.

The k field uses kqRWallFunction boundary condition on all wall boundaries.

The front and back boundaries are treated as empty to enforce 2D behavior.

The turbulent dissipation rate field epsilon is initialized uniformly to 1.4e-4 throughout the domain.

The epsilon field uses epsilonWallFunction boundary condition on all wall boundaries.

The turbulent viscosity field nut is initialized uniformly to 0 throughout the domain.

The nut field uses nutkWallFunction boundary condition on all wall boundaries.

The solver is pisoFoam.

The convection scheme for velocity is div(phi,U) Gauss limitedLinearV 1.

The convection scheme for k is div(phi,k) Gauss limitedLinear 1.

The convection scheme for epsilon is div(phi,epsilon) Gauss limitedLinear 1.

The viscous term uses div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear.
