Simulate steady-state, incompressible, laminar flow through a porous medium using a CFD solver.

The domain is a 2D channel with total length 0.5 m and height 0.1 m.

The porous zone occupies the rectangular region from x = 0.2 m to x = 0.3 m, spanning the full height of the channel from y = 0 to y = 0.1 m.

The fluid is water with kinematic viscosity nu = 1e-6 m²/s.

The flow is incompressible and laminar.

The inlet boundary condition is a fixed uniform velocity of 1 m/s in the x-direction.

The outlet boundary condition is a fixed uniform pressure of 0 Pa.

The porous medium is modeled using the Darcy-Forchheimer approach with Darcy coefficient d = (1e8 1e8 1e10) m⁻² and Forchheimer coefficient f = (0 0 0) m⁻¹.

The top and bottom walls of the channel are treated as no-slip walls with zero velocity.

The simulation runs in steady-state mode.

The solver should use a pressure-velocity coupling algorithm suitable for incompressible flow (e.g., SIMPLE or PISO).

Convergence criteria should be set for velocity and pressure residuals below 1e-6 or appropriate values for steady-state convergence.
