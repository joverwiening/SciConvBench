The simulation models 2D lid-driven cavity flow with the following specifications:

The computational domain is a square cavity with side length L = 0.1 m.

The grid resolution is 20 × 20 uniform cells.

The fluid is incompressible with kinematic viscosity ν = 0.001 m²/s.

The Reynolds number is Re = 100, based on lid velocity and cavity height.

The top wall moves horizontally to the right with velocity U = 1 m/s.

The left, right, and bottom walls are stationary with no-slip velocity boundary conditions.

All walls use zeroGradient boundary condition for pressure.

The initial velocity field is uniform zero (fluid at rest).

The PISO (Pressure Implicit with Splitting of Operators) algorithm solves the incompressible Navier-Stokes equations.

The time step size is Δt = 0.002 seconds.

The total simulation time is 0.5 seconds, corresponding to 250 time steps.
