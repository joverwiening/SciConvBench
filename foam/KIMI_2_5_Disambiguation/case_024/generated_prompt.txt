Simulate a 2D laminar flow past a circular cylinder using a finite volume CFD solver.

The computational domain is a rectangle extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m in the streamwise direction and y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m in the cross-stream direction.

The cylinder has a diameter of 2 m and is centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).

The simulation uses a single cell in the z-direction with empty boundary conditions on the front and back faces to enforce 2D flow.

The freestream velocity magnitude is 1 m/s with inlet velocity U = (1 0 0) m/s.

The kinematic viscosity is 0.01 m²/s.

The Reynolds number based on cylinder diameter is Re = 200.

The fluid density is assumed to be 1 kg/m³ (unit density) as is standard practice for incompressible flow simulations where density cancels out of the governing equations.

The simulation starts at time 0 s and ends at time 0.5 s.

The time step is deltaT = 0.0025 s.

Results are written every 0.05 s.

The initial velocity field is quiescent with U = (0 0 0) m/s everywhere.

The initial pressure field is p = 0 Pa everywhere.

The inlet boundary condition is fixedValue velocity U = (1 0 0) m/s and zeroGradient pressure.

The outlet boundary condition is zeroGradient velocity and fixedValue pressure p = 0 Pa.

The cylinder wall boundary condition is no-slip (fixedValue U = (0 0 0) m/s) and zeroGradient pressure.

The top and bottom boundaries are slip walls (symmetry or slip conditions) to approximate freestream conditions.
