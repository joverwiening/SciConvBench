Run a transient laminar two-phase dam-break simulation using interFoam solver.

The computational domain is a cube spanning (0,0,0) to (1,1,1) with dimensions 1 m × 1 m × 1 m.

The domain is discretized uniformly with a mesh resolution of 32 × 32 × 32 cells.

Assign the top face at z=1 as the atmosphere patch type.

Assign the remaining five faces (bottom, front, back, left, right) as wall patch types.

Model two phases using the Volume of Fluid (VOF) method.

Define water phase with density 1000 kg/m^3 and kinematic viscosity 1.0e-6 m^2/s.

Define air phase with density 1 kg/m^3 and kinematic viscosity 1.48e-5 m^2/s.

Set surface tension coefficient between phases to 0.07 N/m.

Initialize the water column as a rectangular prism where alpha.water = 1 inside the region from (0, 0, 0) to (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75) and alpha.water = 0 elsewhere.

Include a rectangular prism obstacle located from (0.375, 0.375, 0) to (0.625, 0.625, 0.25) in the domain.

Set the simulation to laminar flow regime.

Use a fixed time step deltaT of 1e-3 seconds.

Set the total simulation endTime to 0.1 seconds.

Set the writeInterval to 0.02 seconds for output writing.
