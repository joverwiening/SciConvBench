Simulate a 2D bubble column using an Euler-Euler two-phase approach.

The computational domain is a rectangular column with dimensions 0.2 m width and 1.0 m height.

The domain is initialized with water throughout, with a uniform gas volume fraction of 1e-6 everywhere.

Air is injected from the bottom through a gasInlet patch spanning the full 0.2 m width of the domain.

The superficial gas velocity at the inlet is 0.2 m/s, directed vertically upward.

The gasInlet patch covers the entire bottom width of 0.2 m, with no partial sparger specified.

The simulation runs for 20 seconds.

The time step size is fixed at 0.001 seconds.

Use appropriate drag models for bubble-liquid interaction.

Apply no-slip boundary conditions on the side walls.

Apply a pressure outlet boundary condition at the top.

Use the k-epsilon turbulence model for the liquid phase if needed.

Output results at regular intervals for post-processing.
