Simulate a premixed methane-air flame in a 2D channel using reactingFoam with the following specifications:

The domain is a 2D rectangular channel with length 0.1 m in the x-direction and height 0.02 m in the y-direction.

Use a uniform Cartesian mesh with 100 cells in the x-direction, 20 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction (100x20x1).

The flow is laminar.

The inlet boundary condition for velocity is fixedValue with U = (0.5 0 0) m/s.

The inlet boundary condition for pressure is zeroGradient.

The inlet temperature is fixed at 300 K.

The inlet mixture composition is stoichiometric methane-air with equivalence ratio φ = 1.0, specified as mass fractions: CH4 = 0.055, O2 = 0.22, N2 = 0.725.

The outlet boundary condition for velocity is zeroGradient.

The outlet boundary condition for pressure is totalPressure with p0 = 101325 Pa.

The walls are adiabatic (zeroGradient for temperature).

The entire domain is initialized with pressure p = 101325 Pa.

Use appropriate chemical mechanism files for methane combustion (thermophysicalProperties, chemistryProperties, and reactions files must be properly configured).

The simulation should solve for species transport, energy equation, and momentum with combustion reactions enabled.
