The simulation models a two-dimensional lid-driven cavity flow with the following specifications.

The computational domain is a square cavity with side length L = 0.1 m.

The top wall moves horizontally at velocity U = 1 m/s in the positive x-direction.

The remaining three walls (bottom, left, and right) are stationary no-slip walls with zero velocity.

The fluid has kinematic viscosity ν = 0.01 m²/s, giving a Reynolds number of Re = 10.

The grid uses a uniform Cartesian mesh with 20 cells in the x-direction, 20 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction (effectively 2D).

The simulation uses a constant time step size Δt = 0.0005 seconds.

The total simulation duration is 0.5 seconds, requiring 1000 time steps.

The initial condition is zero velocity throughout the domain (fluid at rest).

The boundary conditions are no-slip velocity boundary conditions on all walls, with the top wall having u = 1 m/s and v = 0 m/s.

The solver should use an incompressible Navier-Stokes formulation appropriate for low Reynolds number flows.
