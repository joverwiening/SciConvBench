The simulation domain is a rectangular box extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m in the streamwise direction, y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m in the cross-stream direction, and z = -1 m to z = 1 m in the spanwise direction.

A cylinder of diameter 2 m is centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m, z = 0 m).

Boundary conditions are applied as follows: the inlet (left face at x = -5 m) has fixed velocity U = (1 0 0) m/s with zeroGradient pressure; the outlet (right face at x = 5 m) has zeroGradient velocity and fixed pressure p = 0 Pa (reference pressure); the top and bottom walls (y = 2.5 m and y = -1.5 m) have noSlip velocity and zeroGradient pressure; the cylinder wall has noSlip velocity and zeroGradient pressure; the front and back faces (z = -1 m and z = 1 m) are empty to enforce a 2-D simulation.

The solver is pimpleFoam for incompressible unsteady flow.

Time control parameters are: startTime = 0 s, endTime = 0.5 s, deltaT = 0.002 s, with results written every 0.05 s.

Initial fields specify quiescent fluid with U = (0 0 0) m/s and p = 0 Pa throughout the domain.

The flow is laminar.

The laminar viscosity model uses generalisedNewtonian with the CrossPowerLaw model, with parameters nuInf = 10 m^2/s, m = 0.4 s, n = 3, and a reference viscosity of 0.01 m^2/s.
