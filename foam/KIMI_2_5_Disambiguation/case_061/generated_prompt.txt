The simulation shall model turbulent channel flow using pimpleFoam with the following specifications:

The domain is a rectangular channel with streamwise length Lx = 2.0 m, channel height H = 0.10 m (y-direction), and spanwise width Lz = 0.10 m.

The mesh shall consist of 100 cells in x, 50 cells in y, and 50 cells in z, totaling 250,000 cells.

The bulk velocity is specified as Ubar = (1 0 0) m/s, implemented via meanVelocityForce in fvOptions to drive the flow.

The kinematic viscosity is nu = 1.5e-5 m²/s, giving a bulk Reynolds number of approximately 6667 based on channel height and bulk velocity.

The time step is deltaT = 0.001 s with endTime = 5.0 s, using adjustTimeStep yes with maxCo 1.0 for adaptive time stepping based on CFL condition.

The turbulence model shall be RAS kOmegaSST.

Boundary conditions are defined as follows: streamwise boundaries (xMin and xMax) are periodic cyclic; spanwise boundaries (zMin and zMax) are periodic cyclic; wall boundaries at y=0 and y=H use noSlip for velocity, zeroGradient for pressure, kqRWallFunction for k, omegaWallFunction for omega, and nutkWallFunction for nut.

The near-wall mesh spacing in the y-direction shall be set to achieve y+ approximately 1 or less at the walls to properly resolve the viscous sublayer, which with the given mesh of 50 cells in y suggests a wall-normal expansion ratio with the first cell height approximately 0.0001 m or smaller.
