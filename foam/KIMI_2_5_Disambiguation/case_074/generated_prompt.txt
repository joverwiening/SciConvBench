Simulate unsteady, laminar, incompressible flow over a 2D circular cylinder.
The cylinder diameter is 0.1 m.
The free stream velocity is 1.5 m/s.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 1e-3 m^2/s.
The Reynolds number based on cylinder diameter is 150.
The flow must be transient to capture vortex shedding phenomena.
Use a 2D computational domain with appropriate inlet, outlet, and far-field boundary conditions.
Place the cylinder at the center of the domain with sufficient distance to boundaries to avoid interference.
Apply a velocity inlet boundary condition with uniform free stream velocity.
Apply a pressure outlet boundary condition with zero gauge pressure.
Apply symmetry or slip wall conditions on the top and bottom boundaries.
Apply no-slip wall condition on the cylinder surface.
Use a time step size small enough to resolve the vortex shedding frequency, approximately 0.01 seconds or less.
Run the simulation for at least 10-20 vortex shedding cycles to reach periodic state.
Monitor lift and drag coefficients to verify the periodic vortex shedding behavior.
Use a mesh with sufficient resolution around the cylinder, with at least 50-100 points around the circumference and boundary layer clustering.
Ensure the mesh is fine enough to capture the separated shear layers and wake region behind the cylinder.
