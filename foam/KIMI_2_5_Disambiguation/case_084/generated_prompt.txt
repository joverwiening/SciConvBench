Create the mesh using blockMesh with 500 cells in the axial (length) direction and 5 cells in each transverse direction (width and height), resulting in a 500×5×5 cell mesh.

The tube is closed at both ends (no inflow or outflow boundaries).

The flame is ignited at the closed end corresponding to x=0.

Use a compressible reacting flow solver appropriate for laminar or turbulent flame propagation with detailed chemistry (e.g., reactingFoam or rhoReactingFoam).

Set boundary conditions as wall (no-slip, adiabatic or fixed temperature) on all tube surfaces.
