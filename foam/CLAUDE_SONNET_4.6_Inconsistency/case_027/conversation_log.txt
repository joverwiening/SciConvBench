Q: The inlet values for `k` and `omega` are set to `uniform 0`, but with a freestream velocity of 20 m/s and 5% turbulence intensity, the physically correct inlet values are non-zero.

Should I compute the correct inlet values for `k` and `omega` from the given turbulence intensity and mixing length, and use those in the specification instead of zero?
A: Yes, use the physically correct computed values:
- `k = uniform 1.5` (from k = 1.5*(U*I)^2 with U=20, I=0.05)
- `omega = uniform 447.2` (from omega ≈ sqrt(k)/(Cmu^0.25*L) with Cmu=0.09, L=0.005)
