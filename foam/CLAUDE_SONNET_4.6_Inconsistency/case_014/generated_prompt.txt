Use OpenFOAM v8 (or compatible ESI OpenFOAM v2106) as the target version, assuming standard installation with solidDisplacementFoam-compatible dictionaries.

The solver to use is solidDisplacementFoam, not pimpleFoam, because pimpleFoam is a fluid dynamics solver and the described task is a structural stress analysis; solidDisplacementFoam is the correct OpenFOAM solver for linear elastic solid mechanics with thermal coupling.

The case is a 2D quarter-symmetry plate with a circular hole under uniaxial tension.

Use the provided blockMeshDict exactly as given, including all vertices, blocks, edges, and boundary definitions.

Place the blockMeshDict file at constant/polyMesh/blockMeshDict.

Set convertToMeters to 1 in blockMeshDict.

The geometry is in the x-y plane with 0.5 units thickness in the z-direction.

The circular hole has radius 0.5 units centered at the origin.

The mesh has 5 blocks: hex (5 4 9 10 16 15 20 21) with 10x10x1 cells, hex (0 1 4 5 11 12 15 16) with 10x10x1 cells, hex (1 2 3 4 12 13 14 15) with 20x10x1 cells, hex (4 3 6 7 15 14 17 18) with 20x20x1 cells, and hex (9 4 7 8 20 15 18 19) with 10x20x1 cells, all with simpleGrading (1 1 1).

Run blockMesh to generate the mesh from the provided blockMeshDict before running the solver.

Run the solver using the command: solidDisplacementFoam

Post-process results using paraFoam or foamToVTK to visualize displacement field D, stress field sigma, and temperature field T.

The analysis is steady-state; the time-stepping from 0 to 100 with deltaT 1 serves as pseudo-time iterations for convergence.

Output results are written every 20 timesteps, producing output at times 20, 40, 60, 80, and 100.
