Q: The volume fractions of air (0.8) and water (0.3) at the inlet sum to 1.1, which violates the physical constraint that all phase fractions must sum to exactly 1.0.

What should the correct inlet volume fractions be for air and water (they must sum to 1.0)?
A: The correct inlet volume fraction for water should be 0.2 (not 0.3). So air is 0.8 and water is 0.2, which sum to 1.0.
