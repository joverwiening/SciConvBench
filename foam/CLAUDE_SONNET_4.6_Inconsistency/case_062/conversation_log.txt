Q: The `application` field in `controlDict` should match the solver being used for the physics. For linear elastic static deflection, `pimpleFoam` is a fluid dynamics solver and is incorrect for this problem.

Which solid mechanics solver do you want to use? Common correct options for linear elastic static problems in OpenFOAM are `solidDisplacementFoam` (built-in) or `solids4Foam`. Which one should be used?
A: `solidDisplacementFoam`
