Q: What value should be used for the kinematic viscosity `nu` in the `transportProperties` file, given that `icoFoam` is an incompressible solver that requires `nu` (not dynamic viscosity or density) as its transport property — and does not natively support a Bingham plastic model?
A: The solver should be `nonNewtonianIcoFoam`, not `icoFoam`. That solver supports the Bingham plastic model natively and uses the transport properties as specified in the requirement (tau0, mu0, muInf, rho) — no separate `nu` is needed.
