The solver is buoyantPimpleFoam.
The domain is 2D (one cell thick in the z-direction).
Gravity is set to (0 -9.81 0) in constant/g.
The fluid is air modeled as a compressible ideal gas.

In constant/thermophysicalProperties, the thermoType block is configured as follows:
thermoType { type heRhoThermo; mixture pureMixture; transport sutherland; thermo hConst; equationOfState perfectGas; specie specie; energy sensibleEnthalpy; }

The mixture block uses the pureMixture model with a single species entry.
The specie sub-dictionary specifies air with molecular weight 28.9 g/mol and no charge.
The thermodynamic sub-dictionary uses hConst with Cp 1005 J/(kg·K) and Hf 0.
The transport sub-dictionary uses the Sutherland model with As 1.4792e-06 and Ts 116.
The equationOfState sub-dictionary uses perfectGas with no additional coefficients.
There is no BoussinesqCoeffs block anywhere in the file.

The initial temperature field in 0/T is set to uniform 300 K throughout the domain.
The hot patch is a small region at the bottom center boundary and is set to fixedValue uniform 500 K.
All other boundaries (top, sides, and the remainder of the bottom) are open to the atmosphere and use appropriate open-boundary conditions (e.g., inletOutlet or totalPressure for pressure, inletOutlet for velocity, and inletOutlet with a 300 K free-stream value for temperature).

The initial pressure field in 0/p_rgh is set to uniform 0 (gauge), consistent with buoyantPimpleFoam using p_rgh as the working pressure variable.
The initial velocity field in 0/U is set to uniform (0 0 0).

The simulation is transient and runs until the thermal plume is visibly established.
The time step and end time are chosen to satisfy the Courant number condition; a maximum Co of 0.5 is recommended.
Results are written at regular intervals sufficient to capture the plume development over time.
