Q: The configuration you've described contains a critical contradiction that must be resolved before I can finalize the specification.

You specified `equationOfState Boussinesq` in the `thermoType` dictionary, but you also stated the simulation "is expected to show **large** density variations as the hot air rises."

The Boussinesq equation of state is a **small-density-variation approximation** — it assumes density differences are small and only affects the buoyancy body force term, not the full compressible flow. It is physically incompatible with large density variations.

For large density variations with `buoyantPimpleFoam`, the correct equation of state would be `perfectGas`, which computes density from the full ideal gas law and is consistent with the compressible solver.

Which do you want to use?

1. Keep `Boussinesq` (and accept that the simulation models only small density perturbations around 300 K, which contradicts the "large variation" expectation)
2. Switch to `perfectGas` (physically correct for large density variations across 300 K–500 K)
