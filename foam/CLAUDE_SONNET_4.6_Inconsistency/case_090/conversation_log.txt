Q: There is a critical contradiction in your setup: you specified **simpleFoam** as the solver, but simpleFoam is a **steady-state** solver and cannot perform a transient simulation (t=0 to t=200s with deltaT=0.1s).

For transient vortex shedding, the correct solver is **pimpleFoam** (or icoFoam for laminar low-Re flows).

Should I replace simpleFoam with **pimpleFoam** as the transient solver for this simulation?
A: Yes, use **pimpleFoam**.
