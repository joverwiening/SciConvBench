Q: What are the boundary conditions for velocity on each wall of the square cavity (top, bottom, left, and right walls)?
A: The top wall ('movingWall') moves in the x-direction with a uniform velocity of 1 m/s. The bottom, left, and right walls are 'fixedWalls' with no-slip boundary condition (velocity equal to zero at the wall).
