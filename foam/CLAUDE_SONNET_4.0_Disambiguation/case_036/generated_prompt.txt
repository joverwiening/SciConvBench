Simulate incompressible water flow through a porous block inside a channel using steady-state conditions.
The channel has dimensions of 1.0m length, 0.2m height, and 0.01m width.
The porous zone is located from x=0.4m to x=0.6m and extends across the entire channel height of 0.2m.
The porous zone uses a Darcy-Forchheimer model with d = (1e8 1e8 1e8) and f = (0 0 0), where d represents the inverse permeability tensor.
The inlet boundary condition is a fixed velocity of 0.1 m/s in the x-direction: uniform (0.1 0 0).
The outlet boundary condition is a fixed pressure of 0 Pa: fixedValue uniform 0.
The fluid is water with kinematic viscosity nu = 1e-6 m^2/s corresponding to water at 20°C.
The simulation should reach steady-state conditions.
