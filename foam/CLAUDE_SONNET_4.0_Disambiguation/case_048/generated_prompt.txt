Run a transient simulation of flow over a cylinder with the following specifications.
The Reynolds number is 100 and the fluid is water.
The cylinder diameter is 0.1 m.
The simulation duration is 200 seconds (endTime 200).
The computational domain size is 2.2 m in the x-direction and 0.41 m in the y-direction.
The cylinder is centered at position (0.2, 0.2) within the domain.
Use adjustable time stepping with maxCo 1.0 and maxDeltaT 0.01.
For velocity boundary conditions: inlet has fixedValue (0.001 0 0), outlet has zeroGradient, topAndBottom have slip, cylinderWall has noSlip, and frontAndBack are empty.
For pressure boundary conditions: inlet has zeroGradient, outlet has fixedValue 0, topAndBottom have zeroGradient, cylinderWall has zeroGradient, and frontAndBack are empty.
Use a mesh resolution with approximately 80-100 cells around the cylinder circumference and a total of 40,000-60,000 cells for adequate resolution of the wake structures at Re=100.
