Simulate a 2D flow past a circular cylinder with the following specifications:

Domain is rectangular extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m, y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m, and z = -1 m to 1 m.
Cylinder has diameter 2 m and is centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).
Time parameters are startTime = 0 s, endTime = 0.5 s, deltaT = 0.0025 s.
Results are written every 0.05 s.
Initial conditions specify quiescent fluid with U = (0 0 0) m/s and p = 0 Pa.
Inlet boundary condition at left face is U = (1 0 0) m/s.
Outlet boundary condition at right face is U zeroGradient and p = 0 Pa reference.
Top and bottom wall boundary conditions are U noSlip and p zeroGradient.
Cylinder wall boundary conditions are U noSlip and p zeroGradient.
Front and back face boundary conditions are empty for 2D simulation.
Fluid density is 1000 kg/m³.
Kinematic viscosity is 0.01 m²/s.
