Model natural convection in a differentially heated square cavity using air as the working fluid.
The cavity dimensions are 1m x 1m.
The left wall temperature is fixed at 310 K.
The right wall temperature is fixed at 290 K.
The top and bottom walls are adiabatic with zeroGradient boundary condition for temperature.
The top and bottom walls have no-slip boundary condition with fixedValue uniform (0 0 0) for velocity.
The left and right walls have no-slip boundary condition with fixedValue uniform (0 0 0) for velocity.
Initialize the velocity field U to uniform (0 0 0) throughout the domain.
Initialize the temperature field T to uniform 300 K throughout the domain.
Use air as the working fluid with appropriate thermophysical properties.
The simulation should capture buoyancy-driven flow due to the temperature difference between the vertical walls.
