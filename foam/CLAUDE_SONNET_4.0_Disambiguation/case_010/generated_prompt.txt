The simulation is an incompressible lid-driven cavity flow using OpenFOAM.
The cavity is a square with dimensions 1 unit in x and y directions and 0.1 units in z direction.
Use a computational grid of 20x20x1 cells (20 cells in x, 20 cells in y, 1 cell in z).
The top wall is named 'movingWall' and moves in the x-direction with uniform velocity of 1 m/s.
The remaining walls are named 'fixedWalls' and have no-slip boundary condition with zero velocity.
The front and back faces are designated as 'empty' boundary type.
The kinematic viscosity nu is constant at 1e-05 m^2/s.
Use k-epsilon turbulence model.
Initialize turbulent kinetic energy k = 1.5e-4 uniformly throughout the domain.
Initialize turbulent dissipation rate epsilon = 1.4e-4 uniformly throughout the domain.
Initialize turbulent viscosity nut = 0 uniformly throughout the domain.
Apply kqRWallFunction boundary condition for k on all walls except front/back faces.
Apply epsilonWallFunction boundary condition for epsilon on all walls except front/back faces.
Apply nutkWallFunction boundary condition for nut on all walls except front/back faces.
Use pisoFoam solver.
Initialize the velocity field to zero throughout the domain at the beginning of simulation.
Set convection scheme for momentum as div(phi,U) Gauss limitedLinearV 1.
Set convection scheme for turbulent kinetic energy as div(phi,k) Gauss limitedLinear 1.
Set convection scheme for turbulent dissipation as div(phi,epsilon) Gauss limitedLinear 1.
Set viscous term scheme as div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear.
Run simulation from time 0 to 10 time units.
Use time step size of 0.005 time units.
