Problem: Simulate compressible, inviscid flow through a 2D de Laval nozzle, with a normal shock induced by a specific back pressure.
Solver: Use the density-based solver `rhoCentralFoam`.
Geometry: A 2D converging-diverging nozzle. Length=0.3m. Inlet height=0.1m, throat height=0.05m, outlet height=0.08m. The throat is at x=0.1m. Use `arc` edges in `blockMeshDict` for a smooth profile.
Mesh: A structured mesh with 300 cells in the axial direction and 50 in the transverse direction. The mesh should be finer near the throat.
Thermo-physical Properties: `perfectGas` with `const` transport. Gamma = 1.4.
Boundary Conditions (in `0/` directory):
- `inlet`: `totalPressure` p0 = 5e5 Pa; `totalTemperature` T0 = 600 K; `zeroGradient` for U.
- `outlet`: `fixedValue` for static pressure p = 3.5e5 Pa (this value is chosen to force a shock inside the nozzle). `zeroGradient` for T and U.
- `topAndBottomWalls`: `slip` condition for U, `zeroGradient` for p and T.
- `frontAndBack`: `empty`.
Time Controls (controlDict):
- `application`: `rhoCentralFoam`
- `endTime`: 0.05 s (run until steady-state)
- `deltaT`: 1e-7 s
- `writeInterval`: 0.005 s
- `fvSchemes`: Use `vanLeer` for `div(phi,rhoU)` and `Kurganov` for `div(rho*phi,U)`.
