Q: What is the geometry and mesh specification for this simulation (domain dimensions, cell count, and boundary patch names)?
A: The geometry is a rectangular cavity that is 9 units wide and 1 unit tall, extruded a single cell in the spanwise direction with front and back patches treated as empty to enforce 2D.

Use 90 cells in x direction, 10 cells in y direction, and 1 cell in z direction.

The boundary patch names should be: bottom, top, left, right (or sides), front, and back.
