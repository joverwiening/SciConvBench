Simulate a fluidized bed reactor using an Euler-Euler multiphase model.
The reactor geometry is 0.1 m width x 0.5 m height x 0.1 m depth.
Use solid particles with diameter 200 microns and density 2500 kg/m³.
Assume the particles are glass beads based on the density and size properties.
The fluidizing gas is air with density 1.2 kg/m³ and kinematic viscosity 1.5e-5 m²/s.
Set the gas inlet velocity to achieve a superficial velocity of 0.2 m/s upward.
Assume room temperature conditions (approximately 20°C or 293 K) for the simulation.
Initialize the solid phase with volume fraction 0.55 in the bottom 0.2 m of the bed.
Initialize the remaining upper space (0.2 m to 0.5 m height) with zero solid volume fraction.
The gas enters from the bottom of the bed and flows upward through the particle bed.
Use appropriate drag models and turbulence modeling for Euler-Euler fluidized bed simulations.
