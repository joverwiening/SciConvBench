Run a turbulent channel flow simulation using pimpleFoam with the following specifications:
The channel has a total length of 1.0 m with a backward-facing step geometry.
The upstream channel height is 0.02 m and downstream channel height is 0.04 m.
The backward-facing step has a height of 0.02 m and is located at x = 0.2 m.
The out-of-plane thickness is 0.01 m.
The inlet velocity is 0.5 m/s.
The kinematic viscosity is nu = 1.5e-5 m²/s as specified in constant/transportProperties.
Use the RAS kEpsilon turbulence model with wall functions configured in constant/momentumTransport.
At the inlet, apply velocity boundary condition of 0.5 m/s.
At the outlet, apply pressure fixedValue 0 and velocity zeroGradient boundary conditions.
At the outlet, apply zeroGradient boundary conditions for k and epsilon.
Set initial conditions throughout the domain as k = 9.375e-4 m²/s² and epsilon = 5.5e-3 m²/s³.
The initial conditions are based on 5% turbulence intensity and a length scale of 0.01 m.
