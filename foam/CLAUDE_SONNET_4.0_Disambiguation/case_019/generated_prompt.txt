Simulate a 2D flow past a circular cylinder using the following specifications.
The computational domain is rectangular extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m in the streamwise direction and y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m in the cross-stream direction and z = -1 to 1 m in the span direction.
The cylinder has a diameter of 2 m and is centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).
Apply inlet boundary condition at the left boundary with velocity U = (1 0 0) m/s.
Apply outlet boundary condition at the right boundary with velocity having zero gradient and pressure p = 0 as reference.
Apply no-slip boundary condition for velocity and zero gradient for pressure at the top and bottom walls.
Apply no-slip boundary condition for velocity and zero gradient for pressure at the cylinder wall.
Set the front and back faces as empty boundaries for 2D simulation.
Initialize the flow field with quiescent fluid conditions where velocity U = (0 0 0) m/s and pressure p = 0.
The flow is laminar using a generalised Newtonian model.
The viscosity model is cross power law with parameters nuinf = 10, m = 0.4, and n = 3.
The kinematic viscosity is 0.01 m²/s.
Assume fluid density of 1000 kg/m³ based on water properties.
Use time step size deltaT = 0.0025 s.
Run the simulation until end time of 0.5 s.
Use mesh resolution with approximately 100 cells across the cylinder diameter and appropriate refinement near the cylinder surface for accurate boundary layer resolution.
