Set up a laminar counter-flow diffusion flame simulation using reactingFoam solver in OpenFOAM.
Create a 2-D rectangular domain with x from 0 to 0.02 m, y from -0.01 to 0.01 m, and z from -0.01 to 0.01 m.
Use a mesh with 100 grid points in x direction, 40 grid points in y direction, and 1 cell in z direction.
Name the left boundary (x=0) as fuel and the right boundary (x=0.02) as air.
Name the top and bottom boundaries as outlet.
Set frontAndBack faces as empty boundary type.
Initialize the entire domain with a temperature of 2000 K at time zero.
Set velocity at the fuel boundary to 0.1 m/s in the positive x direction.
Set velocity at the air boundary to -0.1 m/s in the negative x direction.
Inject pure methane (CH4) at the fuel boundary with a temperature of 293 K.
Supply air at the air boundary with mass fraction Y_O2 = 0.23 and balance N2, at temperature 293 K.
Set pressure at both fuel and air boundaries to atmospheric pressure (101325 Pa).
Use EulerImplicit chemistry integration with Rosenbrock43 ODE solver.
Configure laminar combustion model and laminar momentum transport.
Use compressible finite-rate chemistry with hePsiThermo thermodynamics package.
Use coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture for species mixing rules.
Use Sutherland transport model for viscosity and thermal conductivity.
Use JANAF thermodynamic coefficients for species properties.
Set timestep to 1e-4 seconds with simulation end time of 0.5 seconds.
Write results at intervals of 0.05 seconds.
