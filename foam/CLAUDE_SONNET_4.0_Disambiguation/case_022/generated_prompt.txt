Simulate a 2D flow past a circular cylinder using pimpleFoam solver for incompressible unsteady flow.
The computational domain is rectangular extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m in the streamwise direction and y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m in the cross-stream direction and z = -1 to 1 m.
The circular cylinder has a diameter of 2 m and is centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).
The simulation runs from startTime = 0 s to endTime = 0.5 s with deltaT = 0.0025 s.
Results are written every 0.05 s.
The initial field specifies a quiescent fluid with U = (0 0 0) m/s and p = 0 Pa.
The flow is laminar using the generalised Newtonian model with cross power law viscosity model.
The cross power law viscosity model parameters are nuinf = 10 m²/s, m = 0.4 Pa·s, and n = 3 (dimensionless).
The inlet boundary condition (left face) is U = (1 0 0) m/s with zeroGradient pressure.
The outlet boundary condition (right face) is zeroGradient velocity and p = 0 Pa (reference pressure).
The top and bottom walls have noSlip velocity and zeroGradient pressure boundary conditions.
The cylinder wall has noSlip velocity and zeroGradient pressure boundary conditions.
The back and front faces are set to empty boundary condition for 2D simulation.
