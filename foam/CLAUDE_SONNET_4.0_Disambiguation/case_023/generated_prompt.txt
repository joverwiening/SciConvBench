The domain is a rectangular box extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m in the streamwise direction and y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m in the cross-stream direction and z = -1 to 1 m.

Results are written every 0.05 s.
The initial field specifies a quiescent fluid with U = (0 0 0) and p = 0.
The flow is laminar using the generalised Newtonian model.
The viscosity model is Cross Power Law with nuInf = 10, m = 0.4, and n = 3.
