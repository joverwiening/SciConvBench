Simulate a 2D flow past a circular cylinder using pimpleFoam solver for incompressible unsteady flow.
The computational domain is rectangular extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m, y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m, and z = -1 m to z = 1 m.
The circular cylinder has a diameter of 2 m and is centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).
The simulation runs from startTime = 0 s to endTime = 0.5 s with deltaT = 0.0025 s.
Results are written every 0.05 s.
The initial field specifies a quiescent fluid with U = (0 0 0) m/s and p = 0 Pa.
The inlet velocity is U = (1 0 0) m/s at x = -5 m.
The outlet boundary conditions at x = 5 m are U zeroGradient and p = 0 Pa (reference pressure).
The top wall at y = 2.5 m and bottom wall at y = -1.5 m have U noSlip and p zeroGradient boundary conditions.
The front face at z = -1 m and back face at z = 1 m use empty boundary condition type for 2D simulation.
The cylinder surface has U noSlip and p zeroGradient boundary conditions.
The kinematic viscosity is 0.01 m²/s.
The fluid density is 1000 kg/m³.
