Simulate a 2D buoyant cavity with natural convection.
The domain is a 1m x 1m square cavity.
The fluid is air with initial temperature of 293 K.
The left wall is hot with temperature 303 K.
The right wall is cold with temperature 293 K.
The top and bottom walls are insulated with noSlip velocity, zeroGradient temperature, and fixedFluxPressure boundary conditions.
The simulation runs for 2000 seconds total time.
Use a time step of 1 second.
The simulation models buoyancy-driven flow due to the temperature difference between the hot and cold walls.
