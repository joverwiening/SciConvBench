Simulate turbulent channel flow using the k-epsilon model with the following specifications:

The computational domain is a rectangular channel with dimensions 1m length (x-direction), 0.1m height (y-direction), and 0.2m width (z-direction).

The flow has a bulk velocity of 0.5 m/s driven by meanVelocityForce and kinematic viscosity of 1e-5 m²/s, resulting in a Reynolds number of 5000.

Use a structured mesh with 100x20x10 cells in the x, y, and z directions respectively.

Apply no-slip boundary conditions for velocity at the top and bottom walls.

Apply cyclic boundary conditions for velocity at the inlet/outlet and front/back walls.

Set zero gradient boundary conditions for pressure at the top and bottom walls.

Apply cyclic boundary conditions for pressure at the inlet/outlet and front/back walls.

Use kqRWallFunction with uniform value 0.000375 for turbulent kinetic energy k at the top and bottom walls.

Apply cyclic boundary conditions for k at the inlet/outlet and front/back walls.

Use epsilonWallFunction with uniform value 1.875e-4 for turbulent dissipation rate epsilon at the top and bottom walls.

Apply cyclic boundary conditions for epsilon at the inlet/outlet and front/back walls.

Set nutkWallFunction with uniform value 0 for turbulent viscosity nut at the top and bottom walls.

Apply cyclic boundary conditions for nut at the inlet/outlet and front/back walls.

Use simpleFoam solver with steady-state formulation.

Set time step deltaT to 1 and total simulation time endTime to 1000 starting from time 0.

Configure GAMG solver for pressure with tolerance 1e-6.

Configure smoothSolver for velocity, k, and epsilon with tolerance 1e-5.

Use SIMPLE algorithm for pressure-velocity coupling.

Run simulation until residuals fall below the specified tolerances in fvSolution.
