Create a 2D Euler-Euler multiphase simulation of a bubble column with the following specifications.

Set up a rectangular computational domain with dimensions 0.2 m width by 1.0 m height by 0.2 m depth.

Use the Euler-Euler multiphase approach with two phases: water (continuous) and air (dispersed).

Configure the gas inlet at the bottom center of the column with a width of 0.02 m (10% of column width).

Set the air injection velocity to 0.2 m/s upward at the gas inlet with a gas volume fraction of 0.05 at the sparger.

Use standard fluid properties at room temperature: water density 1000 kg/m³, water viscosity 1e-3 Pa·s, air density 1.225 kg/m³, air viscosity 1.8e-5 Pa·s, and surface tension 0.072 N/m.

Apply the SchillerNaumann drag model for gas-liquid interaction.

Initialize the entire column with air volume fraction alpha.air = 1e-6 and water volume fraction alpha.water = 1 - alpha.air.

Set initial velocity fields to zero for both phases: U.water = (0 0 0) and U.air = (0 0 0).

Run the simulation for 20 seconds with a time step of 1e-3 seconds.

Write simulation results every 0.2 seconds.

Set appropriate boundary conditions with no-slip walls, atmospheric pressure outlet at the top, and the specified gas injection at the bottom inlet.
