Simulate supersonic flow over a forward-facing step with inlet Mach number 3.
The step height is 0.02m and the inlet channel height is 0.1m (step height is 20% of channel height).
The step width in the flow direction is 0.02m (assumed equal to step height based on best practices).
The total computational domain length is 0.5m from inlet to outlet.
The inlet static conditions are temperature T = 293 K, pressure p = 101325 Pa, and velocity U = (1020.5 0 0) m/s.
Use perfectGas equation of state with gamma = 1.4 to calculate density.
Apply waveTransmissive boundary condition for pressure at the outlet.
Apply zeroGradient boundary conditions for velocity, temperature, and turbulence variables at the outlet.
Use k-omega SST RAS turbulence model.
The simulation should capture and visualize the shock structure including the bow shock upstream of the step and any reattachment shocks downstream.
