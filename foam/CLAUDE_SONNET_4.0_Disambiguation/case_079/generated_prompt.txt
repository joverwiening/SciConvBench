Model a single air bubble rising in a column of quiescent liquid using a VOF solver in 2D.
The air bubble has a diameter of 0.01 m.
The liquid column has a height of 0.2 m and width of 0.05 m.
The liquid is water with density of 1000 kg/m³ and kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m²/s.
The air has a density of 1.2 kg/m³ and kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m²/s.
The bubble is initially centered at position (0.025, 0.025) which is 0.025 m from the bottom of the liquid column.
The bottom boundary uses noSlip wall conditions for velocity.
The top boundary uses inletOutlet conditions for all fields to allow fluid to exit.
The side walls use noSlip wall conditions for velocity.
The simulation is 2D and uses a Volume of Fluid (VOF) solver to track the interface between air and water phases.
