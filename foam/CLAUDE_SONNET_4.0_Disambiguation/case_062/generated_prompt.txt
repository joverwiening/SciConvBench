Simulate flow over a cylinder to observe vortex shedding using water as the fluid.
The cylinder has a diameter of 1 meter and is centered at coordinates (0,0).
The flow velocity is 3.9 m/s in the x-direction.
The Reynolds number is 3900, calculated as Re = U*D/nu = 3.9 * 1 / 1e-6 = 3900.
The computational domain is a rectangle extending from (-10, -10) to (30, 10) meters.
The domain has a thickness of 0.1 meters in the third dimension with one cell.
Apply uniform velocity boundary condition U=(3.9 0 0) m/s at the inlet.
Set turbulence values at inlet to fixedValue k=5.7e-3 and omega=3.6.
Apply zeroGradient boundary conditions for velocity, k, and omega at the outlet.
Set fixedValue pressure p=0 at the outlet.
Apply noSlip boundary condition for velocity on the cylinder surface.
Use kqRWallFunction for k and omegaWallFunction for omega on the cylinder surface.
Apply symmetry boundary conditions at the top and bottom boundaries.
Use empty boundary conditions for the front and back faces.
Create a C-grid topology mesh using blockMesh with higher resolution near the cylinder surface and in the wake region.
Target a y+ value between 30 and 100 on the cylinder surface.
Generate approximately 40,000 cells in the mesh.
Set the simulation start time to 0 seconds and end time to 100 seconds.
Use adjustable time stepping with maxCo=1 and maxDeltaT=0.01.
Write simulation results every 0.5 seconds.
