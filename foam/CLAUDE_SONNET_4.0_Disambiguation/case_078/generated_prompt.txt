Create a transient interFoam simulation of gravity-driven sloshing in a stationary rectangular tank.
The tank dimensions are 1.0 m (length in x-direction) by 0.6 m (width in y-direction) by 0.1 m (height in z-direction).
The tank is initially half-filled with water, with the free surface tilted so that water depth varies linearly from 0.25 m at x=0 to 0.35 m at x=1.
This creates an initial tilt angle of approximately 5.7 degrees from horizontal.
The tank remains stationary throughout the simulation with no external motion or excitation.
Gravity drives the sloshing motion from the initial tilted water configuration.
The simulation runs for a total time of 10 seconds (endTime 10).
Use an initial time step of 1e-3 seconds (0.001 s) with adjustTimeStep enabled.
Set maxCo to 0.5 for automatic time step adjustment based on Courant number.
The simulation is transient and uses the interFoam solver for two-phase flow.
