Simulate a flame propagating through a closed tube filled with a stoichiometric hydrogen-air mixture.
The tube is 0.5 m long with a square cross-section of 0.01 m × 0.01 m (width and height).
The initial temperature is 300 K and the initial pressure is 101325 Pa.
The boundary conditions for the tube walls are adiabatic with zeroGradient for temperature (T) at all wall patches.
Ignition is achieved by setting a high temperature of 2000 K in the first 2% of the domain length (from x=0 to x=0.01 m) using system/setFieldsDict.
All patches including the tube ends ('leftWall' and 'rightWall') are of type 'wall'.
Use noSlip for velocity (U), zeroGradient for temperature (T), fixedFluxPressure for pressure (p), and zeroGradient for species at all wall boundaries.
The simulation runs for 0.002 s (endTime=0.002).
Use an adjustable time step with an initial deltaT of 1e-8 s and a maximum Courant number (maxCo) of 0.4.
