Simulate steady-state, incompressible, laminar flow through a porous medium.
The domain is a 2D channel with dimensions 0.5 m long and 0.1 m high.
The fluid is water with standard properties at room temperature.
The inlet has a fixed velocity of 1 m/s in the x-direction.
The porous zone is located in the central region from x=0.2 m to x=0.3 m.
The porous medium is assumed to be a packed bed of spheres with porosity of 0.4.
The permeability of the porous medium is 1e-9 m².
The outlet boundary condition is zeroGradient for velocity and fixedValue uniform 0 for pressure.
The top and bottom walls have noSlip boundary condition for velocity and zeroGradient for pressure.
The flow is governed by the Navier-Stokes equations with Darcy-Forchheimer terms in the porous region.
Use appropriate solver settings for steady-state incompressible flow with porous media.
