Simulate a hot gas bubble rising in water using interPhaseChangeFoam solver in OpenFOAM.
Create a 2D rectangular domain that is 0.1m wide and 0.4m high.
Initialize the water at 300 K with density 1000 kg/m³, viscosity 1e-3 Pa·s, specific heat 4186 J/kg·K, and thermal conductivity 0.6 W/m·K.
Initialize the air bubble at 350 K with density 1.2 kg/m³, viscosity 1.8e-5 Pa·s, specific heat 1005 J/kg·K, and thermal conductivity 0.025 W/m·K.
Place a circular air bubble with radius 0.02m centered at position (0.05, 0.05) in the domain.
Set the liquid as initially stationary throughout the domain.
Apply pressureInletOutletVelocity for velocity, totalPressure for pressure, and zeroGradient for phase fraction and temperature at the top boundary.
Apply noSlip for velocity, fixedFluxPressure for pressure, and zeroGradient for phase fraction and temperature at the bottom and side boundaries.
Set frontAndBack boundaries as empty for 2D simulation.
Run the simulation for 2.0 seconds total time.
Use initial time step of 1e-4 seconds with adaptive time stepping enabled.
Set maximum Courant number (maxCo) to 0.3 and maximum alpha Courant number (maxAlphaCo) to 0.3.
Enable buoyancy forces to drive bubble motion due to density differences.
Include heat transfer between the gas and liquid phases.
Track the interface between air and water phases throughout the simulation.
