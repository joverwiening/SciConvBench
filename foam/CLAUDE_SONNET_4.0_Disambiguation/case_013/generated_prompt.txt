Set up a laminar counter-flow diffusion flame with reactingFoam in a 2-D opposed-jet configuration.
The domain is rectangular spanning x from 0 to 0.02 m, y from -0.01 to 0.01 m, and z from -0.01 to 0.01 m.
Use 100 grid points in x direction, 40 in y direction, and 1 cell in z direction.
Left boundary is named fuel at x=0 and right boundary is named air at x=0.02.
Top and bottom boundaries are called outlet.
FrontAndBack faces are empty.
At the fuel inlet, inject methane CH4 at temperature 293 K.
At the air inlet, supply air with Y_O2 = 0.23 and balance N2 at temperature 293 K.
Initialize the domain with temperature of 2000 K at time 0.
Velocity at the fuel boundary is 0.1 m/s in positive x direction.
Velocity at the air boundary is -0.1 m/s in negative x direction.
Use single-step global reaction mechanism for methane-air combustion: CH4 + 2O2 -> CO2 + 2H2O.
Use chemistry type EulerImplicit and ode solver Rosenbrock43.
The combustion model is laminar and momentum transport is laminar.
Use compressible finite-rate chemistry with hePsiThermo.
Use coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture mixing rule.
Use Sutherland transport model.
Use JANAF thermodynamics.
Use reactingFoam solver.
Set pressure boundary condition at fuel and air inlets to zeroGradient.
Set velocity boundary condition at top and bottom outlets to zeroGradient.
Set pressure boundary condition at top and bottom outlets to fixedValue at 101325 Pa atmospheric pressure.
