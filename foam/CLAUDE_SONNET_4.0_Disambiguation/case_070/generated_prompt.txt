Model a 2D turbulent incompressible flow through a channel with a porous screen.
The channel is 1.0 m long and 0.1 m high with unit depth (1 cell) in the z-direction.
The inlet velocity is uniform 5 m/s in the x-direction (5 0 0).
The inlet pressure boundary condition is zeroGradient.
The outlet pressure boundary condition is fixedValue uniform 0.
The top and bottom walls use noSlip boundary conditions for velocity and zeroGradient for pressure.
The porous screen is located at x = 0.5 m (middle of the channel).
The porous screen uses a Darcy coefficient D = 1e7 and an Inertial coefficient I = 0 for the pressure drop model.
The flow is incompressible and turbulent.
