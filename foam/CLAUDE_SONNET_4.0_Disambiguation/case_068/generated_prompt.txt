Simulate compressible flow of air through a heated porous block in a 2D channel.
The channel is 0.05 m high.
The porous block is rectangular, located from x=0.08 to x=0.12 m, spanning the full channel height.
The porous block dimensions are 0.04 m length by 0.05 m height.
The porosity of the block is 0.4 (reasonable assumption for typical porous media).
The inlet total pressure is 101325 Pa.
The inlet total temperature is 300 K.
Apply volumetric heating to the porous block with heat source S_h = 1e6 W/m^3.
The outlet pressure is fixed at 100000 Pa.
The outlet temperature uses inletOutlet boundary condition with inlet value 300 K.
The outlet velocity uses inletOutlet boundary condition.
The porous block has Darcy coefficient d = (1e9 1e9 1e11).
The porous block has Forchheimer coefficient f = (0 0 0).
The simulation is steady-state.
Use air as the working fluid with compressible flow equations.
