Set up a steady-state pipe flow simulation with the following specifications:
Pipe geometry is 2 meters long with a diameter of 0.04 meters.
Use the k-omega SST turbulence model.
The fluid is air with kinematic viscosity nu = 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
Set inlet velocity to 10 m/s in the axial direction (10 0 0).
Apply no-slip boundary condition for velocity at pipe walls.
Apply kqRWallFunction for turbulent kinetic energy k at pipe walls.
Apply omegaWallFunction for specific dissipation rate omega at pipe walls.
Set outlet pressure to fixedValue uniform 0.
Apply zeroGradient boundary conditions for velocity, k, and omega at the outlet.
Initialize the internal field with uniform velocity matching the inlet velocity (10 0 0).
Initialize the internal field pressure to uniform 0.
Initialize the internal field turbulent kinetic energy k to 0.375 m^2/s^2 (3% turbulence intensity).
Initialize the internal field specific dissipation rate omega to 2500 s^-1 (based on turbulent length scale of 0.004 m).
