Simulate 2D turbulent flow over a circular cylinder at a Reynolds number of 3900.
Solver: Use the transient incompressible solver pimpleFoam.
Geometry: The domain is a rectangle from (-10D, -10D) to (30D, 10D), where D=1m is the cylinder diameter. The cylinder is centered at (0,0). The third dimension has a thickness of 0.1D with one cell.
Mesh: Use blockMesh to create a C-grid topology around the cylinder with higher resolution near the cylinder surface and in the wake region. Aim for a y+ value between 30 and 100 on the cylinder surface. A representative mesh could have ~40,000 cells.
Fluid Properties: Set kinematic viscosity 'nu' in transportProperties to 1e-6 m^2/s (water at 20C).
Turbulence Model: Use the k-omega SST RAS model.
Boundary Conditions:
- inlet: Uniform velocity U=(3.9 0 0) m/s (for Re=U*D/nu=3900). For turbulence, use fixedValue k=5.7e-3 and omega=3.6.
- outlet: zeroGradient for U, k, omega; fixedValue p=0.
- cylinder: noSlip for U; kqRWallFunction for k; omegaWallFunction for omega.
- topAndBottom: symmetry.
- frontAndBack: empty.
Time Controls: Set startTime to 0, endTime to 100. Use adjustable time stepping with maxCo=1 and maxDeltaT=0.01. Write results every 0.5 seconds.
