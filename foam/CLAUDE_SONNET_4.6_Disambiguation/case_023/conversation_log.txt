Q: What should the crossPowerLaw viscosity model parameters represent — specifically, should the constant kinematic viscosity (nu = 0.01 m²/s) be used as a simple Newtonian override, or should the crossPowerLaw model (with nuInf = 10, m = 0.4, n = 3) be the active viscosity model, making the fixed nu = 0.01 m²/s irrelevant or replaced by the non-Newtonian model parameters?
A: The crossPowerLaw model with nuInf=10, m=0.4, and n=3 should be the active viscosity model. The nu=0.01 m²/s is a separate parameter that also needs to be specified in the transport properties (nu0 for crossPowerLaw typically requires nu0 as well — use nu=0.01 as nu0).
