The simulation is a 2D turbulent flow over a backward-facing step using OpenFOAM's pimpleFoam solver.

The geometry has a main channel height of 0.02 m upstream of the step and a step height of 0.01 m, giving an expanded channel height of 0.03 m downstream.
The inlet section length is 0.05 m and the downstream length is 0.5 m.
The domain is 2D with a single cell in the spanwise (z) direction.
The front and back patches use the empty boundary condition.

Use a structured hexahedral mesh generated with blockMesh.
Assign appropriate mesh grading to resolve the near-wall region consistent with wall function requirements (y+ in the range 30–300).

The fluid has a kinematic viscosity nu of 1e-5 m²/s.
Assume density rho of 1.0 kg/m³ (incompressible air-like fluid at reference conditions).

The solver is pimpleFoam for transient incompressible flow.
Use the k-epsilon RAS turbulence model.

Run the simulation until the flow reaches a statistically steady state, with a suggested end time of 2.0 s.
