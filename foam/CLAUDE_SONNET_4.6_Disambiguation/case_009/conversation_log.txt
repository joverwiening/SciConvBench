Q: What are the boundary conditions for velocity (U) and pressure (p) on the moving lid, the stationary walls, and the front/back patches?
A: - **movingWall (top lid):** U = (1, 0, 0) m/s (uniform); p = zeroGradient
- **fixedWalls:** U = (0, 0, 0) no-slip; p = zeroGradient
- **front/back:** empty (2D simulation)
- **Initial conditions:** U = (0, 0, 0) everywhere; p = 0 everywhere
