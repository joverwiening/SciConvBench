The simulation is a 2D dam-break case using OpenFOAM's interFoam solver for two incompressible, immiscible fluids (water and air).

The tank dimensions are 0.5715 m in x (length), 0.4 m in y (height), and 0.01 m in z (thickness).

The mesh is a single-block structured hex mesh with 114 cells in x, 80 cells in y, and 1 cell in z, giving a uniform grid.

The water column occupies the bottom-left corner of the tank, spanning 0 to 0.1461 m in x and 0 to 0.2922 m in y.

The initial condition sets alpha.water = 1 inside the water column region and alpha.water = 0 everywhere else.

The initial velocity field U is set to (0 0 0) everywhere.

The initial p_rgh field is set to 0 everywhere.

The simulation uses blockMesh to generate the mesh and setFields to initialize the alpha.water field.

The front and back boundary patches (z = 0 and z = 0.01) are of type empty for a 2D simulation.

The left wall (x = 0), right wall (x = 0.5715), and bottom wall (y = 0) are of type wall.

For U on the left, right, and bottom walls, apply a fixedValue condition with value uniform (0 0 0) (no-slip).

For p_rgh on the left, right, and bottom walls, apply fixedFluxPressure.

For alpha.water on the left, right, and bottom walls, apply zeroGradient.

The top boundary (y = 0.4) is named "atmosphere" and is of type patch.

For U on the atmosphere patch, apply pressureInletOutletVelocity with inletValue uniform (0 0 0).

For p_rgh on the atmosphere patch, apply totalPressure with p0 uniform 0.

For alpha.water on the atmosphere patch, apply inletOutlet with inletValue uniform 0 and value uniform 0.

Water properties: rho = 1000 kg/m³, nu = 1e-6 m²/s.

Air properties: rho = 1 kg/m³, nu = 1.48e-5 m²/s.

Surface tension sigma = 0.07 N/m.

Gravity is set to (0 -9.81 0) m/s² in the g file.

The transportModel for both phases is Newtonian.

The simulation start time is 0, the end time is 1.0 s, and the initial deltaT is 0.001 s.

Set adjustTimeStep to yes with maxCo = 1.0.

Write results at every 0.05 s interval (writeControl adjustableRunTime, writeInterval 0.05).

Use the MULES-based interFoam pressure-velocity coupling with standard fvSolution and fvSchemes settings appropriate for interFoam, including Gauss linearUpwind for divergence of U and Gauss vanLeer for divergence of alpha.water.
