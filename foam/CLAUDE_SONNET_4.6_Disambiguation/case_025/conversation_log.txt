Q: What values should be used for the fluid properties — specifically the dynamic viscosity and density for both water and air?
A: Water: density = 1000 kg/m³, kinematic viscosity = 1.0e-6 m²/s. Air: density = 1 kg/m³, kinematic viscosity = 1.48e-5 m²/s. Surface tension = 0.07 N/m.
