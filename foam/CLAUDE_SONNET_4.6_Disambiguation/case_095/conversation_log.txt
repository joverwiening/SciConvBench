Q: What fluid properties should be used — specifically, which fluid (e.g., air) and should standard properties at ~300K be used (kinematic viscosity ν ≈ 1.5×10⁻⁵ m²/s, thermal diffusivity α ≈ 2.1×10⁻⁵ m²/s, thermal expansion coefficient β ≈ 1/300 K⁻¹)?
A: Yes, air at ~300K. Use these properties:
- nu = 1.5e-5 m²/s
- Pr = 0.71
- beta = 3.33e-3 1/K
- T0 (reference temperature) = 300 K
- rho0 = 1.0 kg/m³
