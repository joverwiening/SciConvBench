Simulate a single hot gas bubble rising in a quiescent liquid column using a VOF solver with heat transfer, such as interPhaseChangeFoam.

GEOMETRY & MESH:
- Domain: A 2D rectangular column, 0.1m wide and 0.4m high.
- Mesh: Use blockMesh to create a uniform 50x200x1 grid.
- Patches: 'bottom', 'top', 'sides', 'frontAndBack' (empty).

PHYSICAL SETUP:
- Solver: interPhaseChangeFoam.
- Phases: Define two phases in constant/thermophysicalProperties, e.g., 'liquid' and 'gas'.
- Liquid properties (water): rho=1000 kg/m^3, mu=1e-3 Pa.s, Cp=4186 J/kgK, kappa=0.6 W/mK.
- Gas properties (air): rho=1.2 kg/m^3, mu=1.8e-5 Pa.s, Cp=1005 J/kgK, kappa=0.025 W/mK.
- Surface tension (constant/transportProperties): sigma = 0.07 N/m.
- Gravity (constant/g): (0 -9.81 0) m/s^2.

INITIAL CONDITIONS:
- Use 'setFields' to initialize the phase fraction (alpha.gas) and temperature (T).
- Liquid domain: alpha.gas = 0, T = 300 K.
- Bubble: Initialize a circular region with radius 0.02m, centered at (0.05, 0.05), with alpha.gas = 1 and T = 350 K.
- Initial fields (in 0/ directory):
  - U: internalField uniform (0 0 0).
  - p_rgh: internalField uniform 0.
  - alpha.gas: Initialized by setFields.
  - T: Initialized by setFields.

BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:
- top: pressureInletOutletVelocity for U, totalPressure for p_rgh, zeroGradient for alpha.gas and T.
- bottom & sides: noSlip for U, fixedFluxPressure for p_rgh, zeroGradient for alpha.gas and T.
- frontAndBack: empty.

TIME CONTROLS (system/controlDict):
- endTime 2.0; deltaT 1e-4; writeInterval 0.05; adjustTimeStep yes; maxCo 0.3; maxAlphaCo 0.3.
