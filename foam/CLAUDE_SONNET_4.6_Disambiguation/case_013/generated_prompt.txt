The simulation uses the reactingFoam solver in OpenFOAM.

The domain is 2-D in the x-y plane, rectangular, spanning x from 0 to 0.02 m, y from -0.01 to 0.01 m, and z from -0.01 to 0.01 m.

Use 100 cells in the x direction, 40 cells in the y direction, and 1 cell in the z direction.

The mesh is generated using blockMesh.

The left boundary (x = 0) is named "fuel" and is of type patch.

The right boundary (x = 0.02) is named "air" and is of type patch.

The top boundary (y = 0.01) is named "outlet" and is of type patch.

The bottom boundary (y = -0.01) is named "outlet" and is of type patch (use a single outlet patch grouping both top and bottom, or name them "outlet_top" and "outlet_bottom" both of type patch with outlet conditions).

The front and back boundaries (z faces) are named "frontAndBack" and are of type empty.

At the fuel inlet, specify a fixedValue velocity of (0.1 0 0) m/s in the +x direction.

At the fuel inlet, specify a fixedValue temperature of 293 K.

At the fuel inlet, specify species mass fractions: Y_CH4 = 1.0, all other species = 0.

At the air inlet, specify a fixedValue velocity of (-0.1 0 0) m/s in the -x direction.

At the air inlet, specify a fixedValue temperature of 293 K.

At the air inlet, specify species mass fractions: Y_O2 = 0.23, Y_N2 = 0.77, all other species = 0.

At both outlet boundaries, use zeroGradient for velocity, temperature, and species, and a fixedValue of 0 Pa gauge for pressure (totalPressure or inletOutlet conditions are acceptable best-practice alternatives).

Initialize the entire domain at time 0 with a temperature of 2000 K to ignite the flame.

Initialize the domain velocity to (0 0 0) m/s.

Initialize pressure to 101325 Pa.

Use the GRI-Mech 3.0 chemical mechanism as the best-practice standard mechanism for methane combustion, including all 53 species and 325 reactions.

Place the chemkin-format reaction files (grimech30.dat), thermodynamic data (thermo30.dat), and transport data (transport.dat) in the constant/chemkin directory and reference them in constant/chemistryProperties.

Use compressible finite-rate chemistry.

In constant/thermophysicalProperties, set the thermoType as follows: type hePsiThermo, mixture coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture, transport Sutherland, thermo janaf, equationOfState perfectGas, specie specie, energy sensibleEnthalpy.

Set the combustion model to laminar in constant/combustionProperties.

Set the turbulence model to laminar in constant/momentumTransport (or constant/turbulenceProperties depending on OpenFOAM version).

In constant/chemistryProperties, set chemistryType to EulerImplicit.

Set the ODE solver to Rosenbrock43 in the chemistry ODE solver settings within chemistryProperties.

Enable chemistry by setting chemistry on in chemistryProperties.

In system/fvSchemes, use appropriate schemes for a laminar reacting flow: Gauss linear for gradient schemes, Gauss linear corrected for Laplacian schemes, and Gauss linearUpwind grad(U) or Gauss linear for divergence schemes of species and enthalpy.

In system/fvSolution, use PBiCGStab or GAMG solvers for pressure and PBiCGStab for other fields with appropriate tolerances (p: 1e-6, other fields: 1e-8).

Use PIMPLE or PISO algorithm in fvSolution with nOuterCorrectors 1, nCorrectors 2, nNonOrthogonalCorrectors 1.

Set the simulation end time, deltaT, and write interval in system/controlDict appropriate for a laminar flame (e.g., endTime 0.5 s, deltaT 1e-6 s, writeInterval 0.01 s, adjustTimeStep yes, maxCo 0.4).

Run the case using the reactingFoam solver.
