Q: What boundary conditions should be applied at each boundary (inlet, outlet, top, bottom, front, back, and cylinder surface) for both U and p?
A: - **Inlet (left):** U = (1 0 0) m/s (fixedValue), p = zeroGradient
- **Outlet (right):** U = zeroGradient, p = 0 (fixedValue)
