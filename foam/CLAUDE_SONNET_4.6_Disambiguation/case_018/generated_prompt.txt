The simulation domain is rectangular, spanning x from 0 to 0.02 m, y from -0.01 to 0.01 m, and z from -0.01 to 0.01 m.
Use 100 grid points in the x direction, 40 grid points in the y direction, and 1 cell in the z direction.
Use the reactingFoam solver.
Use EulerImplicit as the chemistry type and Rosenbrock43 as the ODE solver.
Use a laminar combustion model and laminar momentum transport.
Use compressible finite-rate chemistry with hePsiThermo thermodynamic model.
Use coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture for mixture transport properties.
Use Sutherland transport model.
Use JANAF thermodynamics.
Use a timestep of 1e-4 s, an end time of 0.5 s, and write results every 0.05 s.
The fuel is methane (CH4) and the oxidizer is air (O2 and N2).
Use the GRI-Mech 3.0 skeletal mechanism or a well-established skeletal methane-air mechanism (e.g., the 16-species skeletal mechanism derived from GRI-Mech 3.0) as the reaction mechanism.
Include species CH4, O2, N2, CO2, H2O, CO, H2, OH, H, O, CH3, CH2O, HO2, H2O2, CH3OH, and N2 consistent with the chosen skeletal mechanism.
The left boundary (x = 0) is the fuel inlet with a fixed velocity of 0.1 m/s in the +x direction, temperature of 293 K, and species mass fraction Y_CH4 = 1.0.
The right boundary (x = 0.02 m) is the air inlet with a fixed velocity of 0.1 m/s in the -x direction (i.e., inletOutlet or fixedValue pointing inward), temperature of 293 K, Y_O2 = 0.23, and Y_N2 = 0.77.
The top boundary (y = 0.01 m) and bottom boundary (y = -0.01 m) are pressure outlets with a fixed pressure of 101325 Pa and zero-gradient conditions for velocity and temperature.
The front and back faces (z boundaries) use the empty boundary condition for a 2D simulation.
The initial condition throughout the domain is T = 2000 K, pressure p = 101325 Pa, and a quiescent velocity field (U = 0 m/s).
Initialize species mass fractions in the domain as Y_N2 = 0.77, Y_O2 = 0.23, and all other species at zero for the initial condition.
Use a fixedValue boundary condition for velocity at both inlets and zeroGradient for pressure at inlets.
Use totalPressure or fixedValue at 101325 Pa for pressure at the outlet boundaries.
Use zeroGradient for temperature and species at the outlet boundaries.
Use the standard OpenFOAM blockMeshDict to generate the mesh with the specified grid resolution.
Set adjustTimeStep to no and use a fixed deltaT of 1e-4 s in the controlDict.
Set writeControl to runTime and writeInterval to 0.05 in the controlDict.
Set the purgeWrite to 0 to retain all written time directories.
Use SI units throughout all dictionaries.
