The simulation is for supersonic inviscid flow over a forward-facing step using OpenFOAM's rhoCentralFoam solver.

The computational domain is 3 m in the x-direction and 1 m in the y-direction.
The step height is 0.2 m and it starts at x = 0.6 m from the inlet.
The channel before the step is 0.6 m long and the channel after the step is 2.4 m long.
The step face is vertical from y = 0 to y = 0.2 at x = 0.6 m, and the floor downstream of the step is at y = 0.2.

The gas is ideal air using the hePsiThermo thermophysical model with perfectGas equation of state, hConst for enthalpy, and const for transport properties.
Cp is 1005 J/(kg·K).
Molecular weight is 28.9 kg/kmol.
Dynamic viscosity mu is set to 1e-12 Pa·s to approximate inviscid flow.
The turbulence model is laminar (no turbulence model).

The freestream conditions are p = 101325 Pa, T = 300 K, and U = (1020.6 0 0) m/s, corresponding to Mach 3.

The mesh has approximately 150 cells in the x-direction and 50 cells in the y-direction.
The mesh is refined near the step corner and along expected shock paths.

The inlet boundary uses fixed values: U = (1020.6 0 0) m/s, T = 300 K, and p = 101325 Pa.
The outlet boundary uses waveTransmissive for pressure with pInf = 101325 Pa, and zeroGradient for U and T.
The top wall uses slip boundary conditions.
The bottom wall, including the step floor and the vertical step face, uses slip boundary conditions.

The entire domain is initialized uniformly to the freestream state: U = (1020.6 0 0) m/s, p = 101325 Pa, T = 300 K.

The solver is rhoCentralFoam.
The time discretization uses Euler implicit scheme.
The gradient schemes use Gauss linear.
The divergence schemes use Gauss vanLeer for convective terms of scalar fields and Gauss vanLeerV for velocity convective terms, consistent with rhoCentralFoam best practices for supersonic flows.
The Laplacian schemes use Gauss linear corrected.
The interpolation schemes use linear.
The surface normal gradient schemes use corrected.

The end time is 0.005 s.
The time step is adjustable with adjustTimeStep set to yes.
The maximum Courant number (maxCo) is 0.7.
The write interval is 0.0005 s.
