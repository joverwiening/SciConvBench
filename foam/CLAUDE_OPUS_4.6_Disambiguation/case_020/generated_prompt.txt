The solver is pimpleFoam for incompressible unsteady flow.
The startTime is 0 s.
The endTime is 0.5 s.
The deltaT is 0.0025 s.
Results are written every 0.05 s, corresponding to a writeInterval of 20 time steps.
The write format is ascii.
The simulation is laminar.
The laminar model uses generalisedNewtonian with the CrossPowerLaw viscosity model.
The CrossPowerLaw parameters are nu0 = 0.01, nuInf = 10, m = 0.4, n = 3.
These values are used as specified by the user even though nu0 is less than nuInf, which is physically unusual.
The domain is a rectangle from x = -5 m to x = 5 m, y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m, and z = -1 m to z = 1 m.
A circular cylinder of diameter 2 m is centered at the origin (0, 0).
The simulation is 2D, so the front and back faces in the z-direction are set to empty.
The mesh is generated using blockMesh combined with snappyHexMesh to resolve the cylinder geometry.
A reasonable mesh resolution is assumed based on best practices for flow around a cylinder, with refinement near the cylinder surface.
The boundary patches are inlet (left, x = -5), outlet (right, x = 5), up (top, y = 2.5), down (bottom, y = -1.5), cylinder (cylinder surface), and front and back (z faces).
At the inlet, U is fixedValue uniform (1 0 0) m/s and p is zeroGradient.
At the outlet, U is zeroGradient and p is fixedValue uniform 0.
At the up patch, U is noSlip and p is zeroGradient.
At the down patch, U is noSlip and p is zeroGradient.
At the cylinder patch, U is noSlip and p is zeroGradient.
At the front and back patches, both U and p are empty.
The initial condition for U is uniform (0 0 0) m/s.
The initial condition for p is uniform 0.
Time integration uses backward (second-order implicit) scheme.
Gradient schemes use Gauss linear.
Divergence schemes use Gauss linearUpwind for velocity convection terms and Gauss linear for other divergence terms.
Laplacian schemes use Gauss linear corrected.
Interpolation schemes use linear.
Surface normal gradient schemes use corrected.
The PIMPLE algorithm uses 2 outer correctors, 1 corrector, and 1 non-orthogonal corrector.
The pressure equation is solved using GAMG with a DICGaussSeidel smoother, tolerance 1e-06, and relTol 0.01.
The velocity equation is solved using smoothSolver with a symGaussSeidel smoother, tolerance 1e-06, and relTol 0.01.
Under-relaxation factors of 0.7 for U and 0.3 for p are used within the PIMPLE outer loop.
