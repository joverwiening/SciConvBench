Simulate transient, incompressible, turbulent flow over a 2D backward-facing step using pimpleFoam, based on the pitzDaily tutorial.
GEOMETRY: The main channel height is 0.02m. The step height is 0.01m. The inlet section is 0.05m long, and the domain extends 0.5m downstream of the step.
MESH: Use blockMesh to create a structured mesh with grading towards the walls and the step corner. Patches are 'inlet', 'outlet', 'upperWall', and 'lowerWall' (which includes the step face and bottom wall).
PHYSICAL PROPERTIES: In 'constant/transportProperties', set kinematic viscosity 'nu' to 1e-5 m^2/s.
TURBULENCE: In 'constant/turbulenceProperties', use the k-epsilon RAS model.
INITIAL CONDITIONS: Use 'potentialFoam -write' to initialize the U and p fields. Initialize k to 0.006 and epsilon to 0.03.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:
- inlet: U fixedValue (10 0 0); p zeroGradient; k turbulentIntensityKineticEnergyInlet with intensity 0.05; epsilon turbulentMixingLengthDissipationRateInlet with mixingLength 0.0007.
- outlet: U zeroGradient; p fixedValue 0.
- upperWall & lowerWall: U noSlip; p zeroGradient; k kqRWallFunction; epsilon epsilonWallFunction; nut nutkWallFunction.
- frontAndBack: type 'empty'.
TIME CONTROLS: In 'system/controlDict', use adjustable time step with maxCo 1.0. Set endTime to 0.5 and writeInterval to 0.05.
