This is a 2D backward-facing step simulation using pimpleFoam with the k-epsilon RAS turbulence model in OpenFOAM.

The geometry is a backward-facing step with a step height of 0.01 m.
The upstream channel height is 0.01 m (main channel height 0.02 m minus step height 0.01 m).
The downstream channel height is 0.02 m.
The inlet section extends 0.05 m upstream of the step.
The domain extends 0.5 m downstream of the step.
The z-direction thickness is 0.001 m with a single cell layer, using empty boundary conditions on the frontAndBack patches.

The solver is pimpleFoam.
The turbulence model is k-epsilon RAS.
The kinematic viscosity is 1e-5 m^2/s.
The inlet velocity is (10 0 0) m/s.
The Reynolds number based on step height is 10000.

The mesh is created using blockMesh with a structured multi-block layout.
The geometry is decomposed into two blocks: one block for the upstream inlet section (0.01 m tall, 0.05 m long) and one block for the downstream expanded section (0.02 m tall, 0.5 m long).
The upstream block uses approximately 50 cells in the x-direction and 30 cells in the y-direction with symmetric grading toward both walls (grading ratio approximately 4).
The downstream block uses approximately 200 cells in the x-direction and 60 cells in the y-direction.
The downstream block has grading in the x-direction with expansion ratio of about 3 to cluster cells near the step.
The downstream block has double-sided grading in the y-direction with expansion ratio of about 5 to refine near the upper and lower walls.
Both blocks use 1 cell in the z-direction spanning from 0 to 0.001 m.

The boundary patches are inlet, outlet, upperWall, lowerWall, and frontAndBack.
The lowerWall patch includes the vertical step face and the bottom wall downstream of the step.

Inlet boundary conditions are as follows.
U is fixedValue uniform (10 0 0).
p is zeroGradient.
k is turbulentIntensityKineticEnergyInlet with intensity 0.05.
epsilon is turbulentMixingLengthDissipationRateInlet with mixingLength 0.0007.
nut is calculated.

Outlet boundary conditions are as follows.
U is zeroGradient.
p is fixedValue uniform 0.
k is zeroGradient.
epsilon is zeroGradient.
nut is calculated.

upperWall and lowerWall boundary conditions are as follows.
U is noSlip.
p is zeroGradient.
k is kqRWallFunction with value uniform based on inlet turbulent kinetic energy (k = 1.5 * (0.05 * 10)^2 = 0.375 m^2/s^2).
epsilon is epsilonWallFunction with value uniform based on inlet dissipation rate estimate.
nut is nutkWallFunction with value uniform 0.

frontAndBack boundary conditions are type empty on both front and back faces.

The simulation uses adjustable time stepping with maxCo 1.0.
The startTime is 0.
The endTime is 0.5.
The writeInterval is 0.05.
The deltaT initial value is 1e-5.
The writeControl is adjustableRunTime.
The runTimeModifiable is yes.

The fvSchemes use Gauss linear for gradient schemes.
The divergence schemes use Gauss linearUpwind grad(U) for div(phi,U) and Gauss upwind for div(phi,k) and div(phi,epsilon).
The Laplacian schemes use Gauss linear corrected.
The time scheme is Euler.

The fvSolution uses GAMG solver for pressure and smoothSolver with GaussSeidel for U, k, and epsilon.
The PIMPLE dictionary uses nOuterCorrectors 2, nCorrectors 1, and nNonOrthogonalCorrectors 1.

The transportProperties specify nu as 1e-5 m^2/s.
The turbulenceProperties specify RAS model type kEpsilon with turbulence on and printCoeffs on.
