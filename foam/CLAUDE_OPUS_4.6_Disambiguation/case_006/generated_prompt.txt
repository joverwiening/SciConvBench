The simulation is a two-dimensional Rayleigh-Benard convection cell in a rectangular cavity using OpenFOAM's buoyantFoam solver.

The domain is a rectangular cavity 9 units wide (x direction), 1 unit tall (y direction), and extruded 1 cell deep in the z direction (spanwise). The front and back patches are set to type empty to enforce two-dimensionality.

The mesh has 90 cells in the x direction, 10 cells in the y direction, and 1 cell in the z direction, with uniform grading (simpleGrading 1 1 1).

The boundary patches are: bottom (y = 0), top (y = 1), left (x = 0), right (x = 9), frontAndBack (z = 0 and z = single cell depth). All solid walls (bottom, top, left, right) are of type wall. frontAndBack is of type empty.
