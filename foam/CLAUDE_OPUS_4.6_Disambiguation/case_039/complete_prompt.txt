Problem: Simulate steady-state conjugate heat transfer (CHT) between water flowing through a porous channel and a surrounding solid aluminum block.
Solver: chtMultiRegionSimpleFoam
Geometry & Regions: A solid block of 0.2m x 0.2m x 0.5m. A fluid channel of 0.1m x 0.1m runs through the center. Define two regions: 'fluid' and 'solid'.
Mesh: Use blockMesh for each region. For the fluid region, mesh the 0.1x0.1x0.5 channel. For the solid region, mesh the outer 0.2x0.2x0.5 block and use snappyHexMesh or similar to remove the inner channel volume. The interface patch should be named 'fluid_to_solid'.
Region Setup: Use splitMeshRegions -cellZones to create the region directories.
Porous Properties (system/fluid/fvOptions): Apply a 'porosity' source to the 'fluid' region. Set Darcy coefficients d=(1e6 1e6 1e6) and f=(100 100 100).
Material Properties:
- Fluid (water, constant/fluid/): In transportProperties, mu=8.9e-4. In thermophysicalProperties, rho=1000, Cp=4186, kappa=0.6.
- Solid (aluminum, constant/solid/): In thermophysicalProperties, rho=2700, Cp=900, kappa=237.
Boundary Conditions:
- Fluid Inlet (fluid/0/): U fixedValue (0.05 0 0); T fixedValue 353.15 (80C); p zeroGradient.
- Fluid Outlet (fluid/0/): U zeroGradient; T zeroGradient; p fixedValue 0.
- External Solid Walls (solid/0/): T has a convective condition, e.g., type mixed, refValue 293.15, h 10.
Time Controls (system/controlDict): Steady-state. stopAt endTime; endTime 1000; deltaT 1; writeInterval 100.
