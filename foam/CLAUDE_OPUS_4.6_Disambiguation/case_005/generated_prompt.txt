The simulation is a two-dimensional Rayleigh-Benard convection cell in OpenFOAM using the buoyantFoam solver.

The domain is a rectangular cavity 9 units wide (x), 1 unit tall (y), and extruded one cell deep (z) to enforce 2D.
The mesh has 90 cells in x, 10 cells in y, and 1 cell in z.
The front and back patches are type empty.
The bottom wall is at y=0, the top wall is at y=1, and the side walls are at x=0 and x=9.

The solver is buoyantFoam.
