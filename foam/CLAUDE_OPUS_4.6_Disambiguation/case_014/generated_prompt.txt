The simulation domain is a 2-D rectangular box spanning x from 0 to 0.02 m, y from -0.01 to 0.01 m, and z from -0.01 to 0.01 m.
The mesh has 100 cells in the x direction, 40 cells in the y direction, and 1 cell in the z direction, created using blockMesh.
The left boundary at x=0 is named "fuel".
The right boundary at x=0.02 is named "air".
The top boundary at y=0.01 and bottom boundary at y=-0.01 are both named "outlet".
The front and back boundaries in the z direction are named "frontAndBack" and use the empty boundary condition.
The solver is reactingFoam.
The combustion model is laminar (no turbulent combustion model).
The momentum turbulence model is laminar.
The thermophysical model uses hePsiThermo with compressible finite-rate chemistry.
The mixture model is coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture.
The transport model is Sutherland.
The thermodynamics model is JANAF.
The chemistry solver type is EulerImplicit.
The ODE solver is Rosenbrock43.
The operating pressure is 1 atm (101325 Pa).
The species are CH4, O2, N2, CO2, and H2O.
A single-step global methane-air reaction is used: CH4 + 2 O2 -> CO2 + 2 H2O.
The Arrhenius parameters for the single-step reaction are pre-exponential factor A = 5.012e11, temperature exponent beta = 0, and activation energy Ea = 2.0e8 J/kmol.
The reaction exponents are 1 for CH4 and 1 for O2 (first order in each reactant).
At the fuel inlet, the velocity is (0.1, 0, 0) m/s as a fixedValue condition.
At the fuel inlet, the temperature is 293 K as a fixedValue condition.
At the fuel inlet, the species mass fractions are Y_CH4 = 1.0, Y_O2 = 0, Y_N2 = 0, Y_CO2 = 0, Y_H2O = 0.
At the fuel inlet, pressure uses a zeroGradient condition.
At the air inlet, the velocity is (-0.1, 0, 0) m/s as a fixedValue condition.
At the air inlet, the temperature is 293 K as a fixedValue condition.
At the air inlet, the species mass fractions are Y_O2 = 0.23, Y_N2 = 0.77, Y_CH4 = 0, Y_CO2 = 0, Y_H2O = 0.
At the air inlet, pressure uses a zeroGradient condition.
At the outlet boundaries, pressure is fixedValue at 101325 Pa.
At the outlet boundaries, velocity uses a pressureInletOutletVelocity condition (or inletOutlet with inletValue of (0 0 0)).
At the outlet boundaries, temperature uses an inletOutlet condition with inletValue of 293 K.
At the outlet boundaries, species use inletOutlet conditions with inletValue of the corresponding air-side values (Y_O2=0.23, Y_N2=0.77, others 0).
The frontAndBack boundaries use the empty condition for all fields.
The entire domain is initialized at time 0 with a uniform temperature of 2000 K.
The entire domain is initialized with a uniform pressure of 101325 Pa.
The entire domain is initialized with a uniform velocity of (0, 0, 0) m/s.
The initial species mass fractions in the domain are Y_O2 = 0.23, Y_N2 = 0.77, Y_CH4 = 0, Y_CO2 = 0, Y_H2O = 0.
The time step is 1e-4 s.
The end time is 0.5 s.
The write interval is 0.05 s.
The time discretization scheme is Euler (first order implicit).
The gradient scheme is Gauss linear.
The divergence schemes are Gauss upwind for all divergence terms.
The Laplacian schemes are Gauss linear corrected.
The interpolation scheme is linear.
The surface normal gradient scheme is corrected.
The PIMPLE algorithm is used with 2 outer correctors and 1 inner corrector (nCorrectors).
The pressure equation uses PCG with DIC preconditioner, tolerance 1e-6, relTol 0.01.
The velocity, species, energy, and other fields use PBiCGStab with DILU preconditioner, tolerance 1e-6, relTol 0.1.
JANAF thermodynamic coefficients and Sutherland transport coefficients for CH4, O2, N2, CO2, and H2O are taken from standard OpenFOAM thermodynamic databases.
