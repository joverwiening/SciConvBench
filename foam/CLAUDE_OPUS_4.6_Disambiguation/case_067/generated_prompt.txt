

The simulation is a steady-state turbulent flow through a porous pipe using OpenFOAM.

Use the solver porousSimpleFoam.

The pipe has a length of 2 m and an inner radius of 0.05 m (diameter 0.1 m).

The pipe is entirely filled with porous material.

The porous resistance is modeled using the DarcyForchheimer model defined in constant/fvOptions.

The Darcy coefficient d is (5e7 5e7 5e7).

The Forchheimer coefficient f is (0 0 0).

The porous zone covers the entire computational domain.

The fluid is air with kinematic viscosity nu = 1.5e-5 m²/s, defined in constant/transportProperties.

The turbulence model is k-epsilon, configured in constant/turbulenceProperties with RAS and kEpsilon.

Use blockMesh to generate a cylindrical mesh with 100 cells axially, 20 cells radially, and 16 cells circumferentially.

Inlet boundary conditions are as follows.

U at inlet is fixedValue uniform (10 0 0).

p at inlet is zeroGradient.

k at inlet is fixedValue uniform 0.375.

epsilon at inlet is fixedValue uniform 0.23.

nut at inlet is calculated with value uniform 0.

Outlet boundary conditions are as follows.

U at outlet is inletOutlet with inletValue uniform (0 0 0) and value uniform (10 0 0).

p at outlet is fixedValue uniform 0.

k at outlet is inletOutlet with inletValue uniform 0.0375 and value uniform 0.375.

epsilon at outlet is inletOutlet with inletValue uniform 0.023 and value uniform 0.23.

nut at outlet is calculated with value uniform 0.

Wall boundary conditions are as follows.

U at wall is noSlip.

p at wall is zeroGradient.

k at wall is kqRWallFunction with value uniform 0.375.

epsilon at wall is epsilonWallFunction with value uniform 0.23.

nut at wall is nutkWallFunction with value uniform 0.

Internal field initial values are assumed as follows.

internalField for U is uniform (10 0 0).

internalField for p is uniform 0.

internalField for k is uniform 0.375.

internalField for epsilon is uniform 0.23.

internalField for nut is uniform 0.

In system/controlDict, set application to porousSimpleFoam, endTime to 1000, deltaT to 1, and writeInterval to a reasonable value such as 100.

Use standard fvSchemes for steady-state RANS, including Gauss upwind or Gauss linearUpwind for divergence schemes and Gauss linear corrected for Laplacian schemes.

Use standard fvSolution settings with SIMPLE algorithm, appropriate relaxation factors such as 0.7 for U and 0.3 for p, and residual convergence tolerances of 1e-6 for all fields.
