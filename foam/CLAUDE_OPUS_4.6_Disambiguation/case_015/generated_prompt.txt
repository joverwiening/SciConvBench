The simulation domain is a rectangular box with x from 0 to 0.02 m, y from -0.01 to 0.01 m, and z from -0.01 to 0.01 m.
The mesh has 100 cells in the x direction, 40 cells in the y direction, and 1 cell in the z direction.
The left boundary at x = 0 is named fuel.
The right boundary at x = 0.02 is named air.
The top boundary at y = 0.01 and bottom boundary at y = -0.01 are named outlet.
The front and back boundaries in the z direction are named frontAndBack and use the empty boundary condition.
The solver is reactingFoam.
The combustion model is laminar.
The momentum transport model is laminar.
The chemistry type is EulerImplicit.
The ODE solver is Rosenbrock43.
The thermophysical model uses hePsiThermo for compressible finite-rate chemistry.
The mixture model is coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture.
The transport model is Sutherland.
The thermodynamics model is JANAF.
The chemical mechanism is a single-step global reaction: CH4 + 2 O2 -> CO2 + 2 H2O.
The species list is CH4, O2, N2, CO2, H2O.
The operating pressure is 101325 Pa.
At the fuel boundary, velocity is (0.1, 0, 0) m/s as a fixedValue condition.
At the fuel boundary, temperature is 293 K as a fixedValue condition.
At the fuel boundary, species mass fractions are Y_CH4 = 1.0, Y_O2 = 0, Y_N2 = 0, Y_CO2 = 0, Y_H2O = 0 as fixedValue conditions.
At the air boundary, velocity is (-0.1, 0, 0) m/s as a fixedValue condition.
At the air boundary, temperature is 293 K as a fixedValue condition.
At the air boundary, species mass fractions are Y_O2 = 0.23, Y_N2 = 0.77, Y_CH4 = 0, Y_CO2 = 0, Y_H2O = 0 as fixedValue conditions.
At the outlet boundaries, velocity uses a pressureInletOutletVelocity condition.
At the outlet boundaries, pressure uses a totalPressure condition with p0 = 101325 Pa.
At the outlet boundaries, temperature uses an inletOutlet condition with inletValue 293 K.
At the outlet boundaries, species use inletOutlet conditions with inletValue corresponding to air composition (Y_O2 = 0.23, Y_N2 = 0.77, others zero).
At the fuel and air boundaries, pressure uses a zeroGradient condition.
At the frontAndBack boundaries, all fields use the empty condition.
The initial velocity field throughout the domain is (0, 0, 0) m/s.
The initial temperature throughout the domain is 2000 K.
The initial species mass fractions throughout the domain are Y_O2 = 0.23, Y_N2 = 0.77, Y_CH4 = 0, Y_CO2 = 0, Y_H2O = 0.
The initial pressure throughout the domain is 101325 Pa.
The time step is 1e-4 seconds.
The end time is 0.5 seconds.
Results are written every 0.05 seconds, corresponding to a writeInterval of 0.05 with adjustableRunTime write control.
The time discretization scheme is Euler (first order).
The gradient scheme is Gauss linear.
The divergence schemes use Gauss limitedLinear 1 for species and energy, and Gauss linear for momentum terms.
The Laplacian schemes use Gauss linear corrected.
The interpolation scheme is linear.
The surface normal gradient scheme is corrected.
The pressure-velocity coupling uses the PIMPLE algorithm with nOuterCorrectors 1, nCorrectors 2, and nNonOrthogonalCorrectors 1.
The pressure linear solver is GAMG with a tolerance of 1e-6 and relTol of 0.01.
The velocity, species, and energy linear solvers use smoothSolver with smoother symGaussSeidel, tolerance 1e-6, and relTol 0.1.
N2 is the inert species and its mass fraction is computed as 1 minus the sum of all other species mass fractions.
