The solver is buoyantFoam.
The domain is a 2D rectangular cavity, 9 m wide (x-direction) and 1 m tall (y-direction), extruded one cell in the z-direction.
The mesh has 90 cells in x, 10 cells in y, and 1 cell in z.
The front and back patches are of type empty to enforce 2D behavior.
The bottom wall is at y=0, the top wall is at y=1, and the side walls are at x=0 and x=9.

Gravity is set in constant/g as (0 -9.81 0) m/s^2.
