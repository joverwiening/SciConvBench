The simulation is a 2D flow over a cylinder using OpenFOAM to capture vortex shedding.

The fluid is water with kinematic viscosity nu = 1e-6 m²/s.
The cylinder diameter D is 1 meter.
The free-stream velocity is 3.9 m/s, giving a Reynolds number of 3900.

The simulation domain extends from (-10D, -10D) to (30D, 10D), meaning 10D upstream, 30D downstream, and 10D in each lateral direction from the cylinder center.

The simulation is 2D. The spanwise (z) thickness is 0.1D with one cell in that direction. The front and back patches use the empty boundary condition.

The turbulence model is k-omega SST RAS (RANS).

The mesh is generated using blockMesh with a C-grid topology around the cylinder.
The mesh has higher resolution near the cylinder surface and in the wake region.
The target y+ value on the cylinder surface is between 30 and 100, consistent with wall functions.
The overall mesh has approximately 40,000 cells.
The number of cells around the cylinder circumference is chosen based on best practices for this mesh size and topology (approximately 120 to 160 cells around the circumference is a reasonable assumption).

Time stepping is adjustable with a maximum Courant number (maxCo) of 1 and maxDeltaT of 0.01.
The simulation runs from startTime = 0 to endTime = 100 seconds.
Results are written every 0.5 seconds.

Boundary conditions for the inlet are fixedValue U = (3.9 0 0) m/s, fixedValue k = 5.7e-3, and fixedValue omega = 3.6.
Boundary conditions for the outlet are zeroGradient for U, k, and omega, and fixedValue p = 0.
Boundary conditions for the cylinder are noSlip for U, kqRWallFunction for k, and omegaWallFunction for omega.
Boundary conditions for the top and bottom are symmetry.
Boundary conditions for the front and back are empty.

The solver is pimpleFoam.
Numerical schemes use standard second-order schemes appropriate for transient RANS simulation with pimpleFoam. Specifically, use Gauss linear for gradient schemes, Gauss linearUpwind for divergence schemes of velocity and turbulence quantities, Gauss linear corrected for Laplacian schemes, and linear for interpolation schemes. The time scheme is backward (second-order implicit).
