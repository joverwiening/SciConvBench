The simulation is a 2D dam break case using interFoam for two incompressible, immiscible fluids (water and air).

The solver is interFoam.

The tank dimensions are 0.5715 m (length, x) by 0.4 m (height, y) by 0.01 m (thickness, z).

The simulation is treated as 2D by using empty front and back patches.

The initial water column is defined by a box from (0, 0, -1) to (0.1461, 0.2922, 1), making the water column 0.1461 m wide and 0.2922 m tall.

The mesh is generated using blockMesh with 114 cells in x, 80 cells in y, and 1 cell in z.

The simulation start time is 0 s.

The simulation end time is 1.0 s.

The initial time step (deltaT) is 0.001 s.

Adjustable time stepping is enabled (adjustTimeStep yes).

The maximum Courant number is 1.0.

The write interval is 0.05 s.

The turbulence model is laminar.

Water has a density of 1000 kg/m³ and a kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m²/s.

Air has a density of 1 kg/m³ and a kinematic viscosity of 1.48e-5 m²/s.

The surface tension (sigma) between water and air is 0.07 N/m.

Gravity is (0, -9.81, 0) m/s².

The patch names are lowerWall, leftWall, rightWall, atmosphere, and frontAndBack.

On frontAndBack, all fields are set to empty for 2D treatment.
