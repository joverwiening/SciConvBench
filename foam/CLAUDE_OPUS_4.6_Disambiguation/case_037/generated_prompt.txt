The simulation models compressible air flow through a porous filter in a duct using OpenFOAM.

The solver is rhoPorousSimpleFoam with a steady-state approach.

The duct is 2D with dimensions 0.5 m (length) x 0.1 m (height) x 0.01 m (width).

A porous filter zone named porousFilter is located from x = 0.2 m to x = 0.25 m within the duct.

The porous filter uses a Darcy-Forchheimer model defined as a porosity source term in system/fvOptions applied to the cellZone porousFilter.

The Darcy coefficient d is (5e7 5e7 0).

The Forchheimer coefficient f is (500 500 0).

The inlet boundary condition is supersonicFreestream with U = (700 0 0), p = 101325 Pa, and T = 300 K.

The outlet boundary condition is waveTransmissive with reference values p = 101325 Pa and T = 300 K.

The top and bottom boundaries are walls.

The front and back boundaries use the empty condition for the 2D simulation.

The turbulence model is k-omega SST (RAS).

The initial conditions are k = 0.003 and omega = 10 throughout the domain.

At the inlet, k and omega use freestream-type conditions with freestream values k = 0.003 and omega = 10.

At the top and bottom walls, k uses kqRWallFunction and omega uses omegaWallFunction.

At the front and back boundaries, k and omega use the empty condition.

At the outlet, k and omega use inletOutlet conditions with inlet values k = 0.003 and omega = 10.

The nut field uses calculated at the inlet and outlet, nutUSpaldingWallFunction at the top and bottom walls, and empty at the front and back.

Time controls are deltaT = 1, endTime = 1000, and writeInterval = 100.

Divergence schemes use bounded Gauss upwind for all div terms to ensure stability in supersonic steady-state flow.

Gradient schemes use Gauss linear with cellLimited leastSquares 1 for robustness.

Laplacian schemes use Gauss linear corrected.

Interpolation schemes use linear.

Surface normal gradient schemes use corrected.

The linear solver for p uses GAMG with GaussSeidel smoother, tolerance 1e-6, and relTol 0.01.

The linear solvers for U, e, k, and omega use smoothSolver with GaussSeidel smoother, tolerance 1e-7, and relTol 0.01.

Relaxation factors are 0.3 for p, 0.5 for U, 0.5 for e, 0.5 for k, 0.5 for omega, and 0.5 for rho.

The thermophysical model uses hePsiThermo with pureMixture, perfectGas equation of state, sensibleInternalEnergy energy form, and constTransport with Cp = 1005, Hf = 0, mu = 1.84e-5, and Pr = 0.71.
