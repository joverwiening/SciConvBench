The simulation uses the buoyantBoussinesqPimpleFoam solver in OpenFOAM for a transient 2D natural convection (Boussinesq approximation) case.

The domain is a square box of dimensions 1 m x 1 m in the x-y plane, with unit depth in the z-direction.
The mesh consists of 50 x 50 cells, uniform spacing, with one cell in the z-direction for the 2D setup.

The boundary patches are named bottomWall, topWall, leftWall, rightWall, and frontAndBack.

Fluid properties are specified directly, not from a fluid library.
Kinematic viscosity nu is 1e-5 m²/s.
Prandtl number Pr is 0.71.
Thermal expansion coefficient beta is 3.4e-3 1/K.
Reference temperature T0 is 295 K.
Reference density rho0 is 1.0 kg/m³.
Gravitational acceleration is (0, -9.81, 0) m/s².
Thermal diffusivity alpha is derived as nu/Pr.

The turbulence model is laminar.

Velocity boundary conditions are noSlip on bottomWall, topWall, leftWall, and rightWall.
Velocity on frontAndBack is empty.

Temperature boundary condition on bottomWall is fixedValue 300 K.
Temperature boundary condition on topWall is fixedValue 290 K.
Temperature boundary condition on leftWall is zeroGradient.
Temperature boundary condition on rightWall is zeroGradient.
Temperature on frontAndBack is empty.
Initial internal field temperature is 295 K.

Pressure field p_rgh is used with fixedFluxPressure on bottomWall, topWall, leftWall, and rightWall.
Pressure p_rgh on frontAndBack is empty.
Initial internal field p_rgh is 0.
Field p is derived from p_rgh as needed by the solver.

The startTime is 0.
The endTime is 200.
The initial deltaT is 0.05.
Adaptive time stepping is enabled with adjustTimeStep yes.
The maxCo is 0.8.
The writeInterval is 5 seconds.

PIMPLE algorithm settings are nOuterCorrectors 2, nCorrectors 2, nNonOrthogonalCorrectors 0.
