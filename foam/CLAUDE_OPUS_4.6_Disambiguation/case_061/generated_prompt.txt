The simulation uses pimpleFoam to model turbulent flow in a channel driven by a pressure gradient via meanVelocityForce.

The channel dimensions are Lx = 2.0 m (x-direction), H = 0.10 m (y-direction), and Lz = 0.10 m (z-direction).

The fluid is incompressible with kinematic viscosity nu = 1.5e-5 m²/s.

The target bulk velocity is Ubar = 1 m/s in the x-direction, giving a bulk Reynolds number Re_b ≈ 6667 based on channel height.

The bulk velocity is maintained using a meanVelocityForce source term defined in constant/fvOptions, applied in the x-direction with Ubar = 1.

The turbulence modeling approach is RANS using the kOmegaSST model, specified in constant/momentumTransport.

The mesh is generated using blockMesh with 100 cells in x, 50 cells in y, and 50 cells in z.

The y-direction uses symmetric grading with an expansion ratio of approximately 0.1 (simpleGrading) to cluster cells near both walls, ensuring y+ values in the range of 30–50 suitable for wall functions with kOmegaSST.

The x-direction and z-direction have uniform cell spacing with no grading.

The xMin and xMax boundaries are a cyclic pair.

The zMin and zMax boundaries are a cyclic pair.

All cyclic conditions apply to all fields: U, p, k, omega, and nut.

The lowerWall patch is at y = 0 and the upperWall patch is at y = 0.10, both defined as wall type.

On lowerWall and upperWall, U uses noSlip.

On lowerWall and upperWall, p uses zeroGradient.

On lowerWall and upperWall, k uses kqRWallFunction with value uniform 1.5e-4.

On lowerWall and upperWall, omega uses omegaWallFunction with value uniform 10.

On lowerWall and upperWall, nut uses nutkWallFunction with value uniform 0.

The internal field for U is uniform (1 0 0).

The internal field for p is uniform 0.

The internal field for k is uniform 1.5e-4.

The internal field for omega is uniform 10.

The startTime is 0.

The endTime is 5.

The initial deltaT is 0.001.

adjustTimeStep is enabled with maxCo = 1.0.

The writeInterval is 0.1 using adjustableRunTime writeControl.

The PIMPLE settings use nOuterCorrectors 2, nCorrectors 2, and nNonOrthogonalCorrectors 0.

The time discretization scheme is Euler (first-order implicit).

The gradient scheme is Gauss linear.

The divergence scheme for div(phi,U) is bounded Gauss linearUpwind grad(U).

The divergence scheme for div(phi,k) is bounded Gauss upwind.

The divergence scheme for div(phi,omega) is bounded Gauss upwind.

The divergence scheme for div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) is Gauss linear.

The Laplacian schemes use Gauss linear corrected.

The interpolation scheme is linear.

The snGrad scheme is corrected.

The linear solver for p uses GAMG with a GaussSeidel smoother, tolerance 1e-6, and relTol 0.01.

The linear solver for U, k, and omega uses smoothSolver with a GaussSeidel smoother, tolerance 1e-8, and relTol 0.1.
