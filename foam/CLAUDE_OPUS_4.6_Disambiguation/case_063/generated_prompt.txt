The simulation is the pitzDaily backward-facing step case using OpenFOAM.

The solver is simpleFoam, a steady-state incompressible solver.

The turbulence model is the standard k-epsilon RAS model.

The geometry is defined by the standard OpenFOAM pitzDaily tutorial blockMeshDict, with a step height of 0.028 m.

The kinematic viscosity (nu) is 1e-5 m²/s.

The inlet boundary conditions are as follows.
U is fixedValue, uniform (10 0 0) m/s.
p is zeroGradient.
k is fixedValue, uniform 0.0375.
epsilon is fixedValue, uniform 0.875.
nut is calculated (a reasonable assumption, using the "calculated" type so it is derived from k and epsilon at the inlet).

The outlet boundary conditions are as follows.
U is zeroGradient.
p is fixedValue, uniform 0.
k is zeroGradient.
epsilon is zeroGradient.
nut is calculated.

The wall boundary conditions (upperWall and lowerWall) are as follows.
U is noSlip.
p is zeroGradient.
k is kqRWallFunction.
epsilon is epsilonWallFunction.
nut is nutkWallFunction.

The internal field values are initialized as follows.
U is uniform (0 0 0).
p is uniform 0.
k is uniform 0.0375.
epsilon is uniform 0.875.
nut is uniform 0 (it will be computed from k and epsilon by the turbulence model).

The fvSchemes should use settings consistent with the standard pitzDaily tutorial, including Gauss linear for gradient schemes, bounded Gauss linearUpwind for divergence schemes of U, bounded Gauss upwind for divergence schemes of k and epsilon, and Gauss linear corrected for Laplacian schemes.

The fvSolution should use the SIMPLE algorithm with appropriate linear solvers (e.g., smoothSolver with GaussSeidel smoother for U, k, and epsilon, and GAMG for p), along with relaxation factors typical for steady-state SIMPLE (e.g., 0.7 for U, 0.7 for k, 0.7 for epsilon, and 0.3 for p).

The simulation end time and write interval should follow the standard pitzDaily tutorial defaults (endTime of 1000 iterations, writeInterval of 100).
