The simulation domain is a 2-D rectangular box spanning x from 0 to 0.02 m, y from -0.01 to 0.01 m, and z from -0.01 to 0.01 m.
The mesh has 100 cells in the x direction, 40 cells in the y direction, and 1 cell in the z direction.
The left boundary at x = 0 is named fuel.
The right boundary at x = 0.02 is named air.
The top boundary at y = 0.01 and bottom boundary at y = -0.01 are both named outlet.
The front and back boundaries in the z direction are named frontAndBack and use the empty boundary condition.
The solver is reactingFoam.
The combustion model is laminar (no turbulent combustion model).
The momentum transport model is laminar (no turbulence model).
The thermophysical model uses hePsiThermo with coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture for mixing.
Transport properties use the Sutherland model.
Thermodynamic properties use JANAF polynomials.
The chemistry solver type is EulerImplicit.
The ODE solver is Rosenbrock43.
A single-step global irreversible reaction mechanism for methane-air combustion is used: CH4 + 2 O2 -> CO2 + 2 H2O.
The species list is CH4, O2, N2, CO2, H2O.
At the fuel inlet, velocity is uniform at (0.1, 0, 0) m/s.
At the fuel inlet, temperature is 293 K.
At the fuel inlet, species mass fractions are Y_CH4 = 1.0 and all other species are 0.
At the air inlet, velocity is uniform at (-0.1, 0, 0) m/s.
At the air inlet, temperature is 293 K.
At the air inlet, species mass fractions are Y_O2 = 0.23, Y_N2 = 0.77, and all other species are 0.
At the fuel and air inlets, pressure uses a zeroGradient boundary condition.
At the outlet boundaries, pressure is fixedValue at 101325 Pa.
At the outlet boundaries, velocity and temperature and species use zeroGradient (or inletOutlet with suitable far-field values).
The frontAndBack patches use the empty boundary condition for all fields.
The initial internal field pressure is 101325 Pa.
The initial internal field temperature is 293 K.
The initial internal field velocity is (0, 0, 0) m/s.
The initial internal field species are Y_O2 = 0.23, Y_N2 = 0.77, and all other species are 0.
The time step is 1e-4 s.
The end time is 0.5 s.
Results are written every 0.05 s, corresponding to a write interval of 0.05.
The time discretization scheme is Euler (first-order implicit).
Gradient schemes use Gauss linear.
Divergence schemes use Gauss limitedLinear 1 for species and energy, and Gauss linear for other terms.
Laplacian schemes use Gauss linear corrected.
Interpolation schemes use linear.
Surface normal gradient schemes use corrected.
The pressure-velocity coupling algorithm is PIMPLE with 2 outer correctors and 1 non-orthogonal corrector.
The pressure equation linear solver is GAMG with a DIC smoother and tolerance of 1e-6.
The velocity, species, and energy equation linear solvers use PBiCGStab with a DILU preconditioner and tolerance of 1e-8.
The write format is ascii.
The write precision is 8.
