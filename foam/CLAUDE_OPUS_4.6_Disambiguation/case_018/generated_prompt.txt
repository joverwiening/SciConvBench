The simulation domain is a 2-D rectangular region with x from 0 to 0.02 m, y from -0.01 to 0.01 m, and z from -0.01 to 0.01 m.
The mesh has 100 cells in the x direction, 40 cells in the y direction, and 1 cell in the z direction.
The solver is reactingFoam.
The combustion model is laminar (finite-rate chemistry, no turbulence-chemistry interaction model).
The momentum transport model is laminar (no turbulence model).
The thermophysical model uses compressible finite-rate chemistry with hePsiThermo as the thermo type.
The mixture model is coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture.
The transport model is Sutherland.
The thermodynamics model is JANAF.
The chemistry solver type is EulerImplicit.
The ODE solver is Rosenbrock43.
The chemical mechanism is a single-step global methane-air reaction: CH4 + 2 O2 -> CO2 + 2 H2O.
The species list is CH4, O2, N2, CO2, H2O.
Arrhenius parameters for the single-step reaction use standard values: A = 5.012e11, beta = 0, Ta = 24358 (activation energy approximately 200 kJ/mol).
The fuel inlet is at x = 0 with a fixed velocity of (0.1, 0, 0) m/s, temperature of 293 K, and species mass fractions Y_CH4 = 1.0, Y_O2 = 0, Y_N2 = 0, Y_CO2 = 0, Y_H2O = 0.
The air inlet is at x = 0.02 m with a fixed velocity of (-0.1, 0, 0) m/s, temperature of 293 K, and species mass fractions Y_O2 = 0.23, Y_N2 = 0.77, Y_CH4 = 0, Y_CO2 = 0, Y_H2O = 0.
The top boundary at y = 0.01 m and the bottom boundary at y = -0.01 m are open/outflow boundaries using pressureInletOutletVelocity for velocity, totalPressure for pressure, and inletOutlet for temperature and species.
The front and back boundaries in the z direction are set to empty for the 2-D simulation.
The initial internal field temperature is 2000 K.
The initial internal field velocity is (0, 0, 0) m/s.
The initial internal field pressure is 101325 Pa.
The initial internal field species mass fractions are Y_O2 = 0.23, Y_N2 = 0.77, Y_CH4 = 0, Y_CO2 = 0, Y_H2O = 0.
The operating pressure is 101325 Pa.
The time step is 1e-4 s.
The end time is 0.5 s.
The write interval is 0.05 s.
The time discretization scheme is Euler (first-order implicit).
The gradient scheme is Gauss linear.
The divergence scheme for convective terms of velocity is Gauss limitedLinearV 1.
The divergence scheme for convective terms of species, energy, and scalar fields is Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The Laplacian scheme is Gauss linear corrected.
The interpolation scheme is linear.
The surface normal gradient scheme is corrected.
The pressure equation is solved using PCG with DIC preconditioner, tolerance 1e-6, and relative tolerance 0.01.
The final pressure equation (within correctors) uses tolerance 1e-6 and relative tolerance 0.
The velocity, species, energy, and density equations are solved using PBiCGStab with DILU preconditioner, tolerance 1e-6, and relative tolerance 0.1.
The PIMPLE algorithm uses 2 outer correctors, 1 corrector, and 1 non-orthogonal corrector.
The maximum Courant number is set to 0.5 for adjustable time stepping, but the fixed time step of 1e-4 s is used as specified.
