The simulation is a 2D lid-driven cavity flow using OpenFOAM's icoFoam solver.
The cavity is a square with dimensions 0.1 m x 0.1 m.
The mesh has 20 cells in the x-direction and 20 cells in the y-direction, with 1 cell in the z-direction.
The z-direction depth is 0.01 m.
The fluid kinematic viscosity nu is 0.01 m²/s.
This gives a Reynolds number of Re = U*L/nu = 1*0.1/0.01 = 10.
The top wall (named movingWall) moves with a uniform velocity of (1 0 0) m/s.
The bottom wall, left wall, and right wall are grouped as fixedWalls with a no-slip condition, velocity set to fixedValue uniform (0 0 0).
The front and back patches use the empty boundary condition for both velocity and pressure, as required for 2D simulations in OpenFOAM.
All walls (movingWall and fixedWalls) have a zeroGradient boundary condition for pressure.
The simulation end time is 0.5 s.
The time step deltaT is 0.005 s.
Results are written every 0.1 s, corresponding to a writeInterval of 20 time steps.
The solver used is icoFoam, which is a transient solver for incompressible, laminar Newtonian flow.
Initial conditions for velocity are uniform (0 0 0) and for pressure are uniform 0 (standard defaults for icoFoam).
