The solver is interFoam for a transient, gravity-driven sloshing simulation in a rectangular tank.

The tank dimensions are 1.0 m in x, 0.6 m in y, and 0.1 m in z.

The simulation is 2D, using 200 cells in x, 120 cells in y, and 1 cell in z, with frontAndBack patches set to empty.

The mesh is generated with blockMesh as a single block from (0 0 0) to (1.0 0.6 0.1).

Gravity is set to (0 -9.81 0).

The turbulence model is laminar (no turbulence modeling).

Water properties are density 998.2 kg/m³ and kinematic viscosity 1.0e-6 m²/s.

Air properties are density 1.225 kg/m³ and kinematic viscosity 1.48e-5 m²/s.

Surface tension coefficient sigma between water and air is 0.07 N/m.

The default field value for alpha.water is 0 (air everywhere).

The initial free surface is tilted, approximated with a staircase of 10 x-slices in setFieldsDict, each 0.1 m wide, with water height increasing linearly from 0.255 m at the first slice to 0.345 m at the last slice.

No external motion is applied to the tank.

Boundary conditions for walls (left, right, bottom): U is noSlip, p_rgh is fixedFluxPressure, alpha.water is zeroGradient.

Boundary conditions for top (open atmosphere): U is pressureInletOutletVelocity, p_rgh is totalPressure with p0 uniform 0, alpha.water is inletOutlet with inletValue uniform 0.

Boundary conditions for frontAndBack: all fields are set to empty.

The end time is 10 s.

The initial time step deltaT is 1e-3 s.

Adaptive time stepping is enabled with adjustTimeStep yes and maxCo 0.5.

The write interval is 0.05 s.
