The simulation uses the pisoFoam solver for an incompressible lid-driven cavity flow.

The domain is a square cavity spanning from 0 to 1 in x, 0 to 1 in y, and 0 to 0.1 in z.
The mesh has 20 cells in the x-direction, 20 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
All cells are hexahedral with a uniform grading of 1 (no expansion ratio).

The boundary called movingWall is the top face of the cavity (y = 1).
The boundary called fixedWalls comprises the bottom (y = 0), left (x = 0), and right (x = 1) faces.
The boundary called frontAndBack comprises the front (z = 0.1) and back (z = 0) faces and is of type empty.

The movingWall has a fixedValue velocity boundary condition of (1 0 0) m/s.
The fixedWalls have a fixedValue velocity boundary condition of (0 0 0) m/s (no-slip).
The frontAndBack patches have an empty boundary condition for velocity.

The pressure field p is initialized to 0 throughout the domain.
The movingWall has a zeroGradient boundary condition for pressure.
The fixedWalls have a zeroGradient boundary condition for pressure.
The frontAndBack patches have an empty boundary condition for pressure.

The turbulence model is k-epsilon (RAS).

The field k is initialized uniformly to 1.5e-4.
The movingWall boundary condition for k is kqRWallFunction with value 1.5e-4.
The fixedWalls boundary condition for k is kqRWallFunction with value 1.5e-4.
The frontAndBack boundary condition for k is empty.

The field epsilon is initialized uniformly to 1.4e-4.
The movingWall boundary condition for epsilon is epsilonWallFunction with value 1.4e-4.
The fixedWalls boundary condition for epsilon is epsilonWallFunction with value 1.4e-4.
The frontAndBack boundary condition for epsilon is empty.

The field nut is initialized uniformly to 0.
The movingWall boundary condition for nut is nutkWallFunction with value 0.
The fixedWalls boundary condition for nut is nutkWallFunction with value 0.
The frontAndBack boundary condition for nut is empty.

The velocity field U is initialized to (0 0 0) throughout the domain.

The kinematic viscosity nu is constant with a value of 1e-05 m^2/s.

The simulation starts at time 0 and ends at time 10.
The time step deltaT is 0.005.
The writeInterval is 100 (writing results every 100 time steps).
The writeFormat is ascii.
The writeCompression is uncompressed.
The timeFormat is general.
The timePrecision is 6.
The runTimeModifiable is true.

The time integration scheme (ddtSchemes default) is Euler.

The gradient scheme (gradSchemes default) is Gauss linear.

The divergence schemes are as follows.
div(phi,U) is Gauss limitedLinearV 1.
div(phi,k) is Gauss limitedLinear 1.
div(phi,epsilon) is Gauss limitedLinear 1.
div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) is Gauss linear.

The Laplacian scheme (laplacianSchemes default) is Gauss linear corrected.

The interpolation scheme (interpolationSchemes default) is linear.

The snGrad scheme (snGradSchemes default) is corrected.

The pressure linear solver uses GAMG with a GaussSeidel smoother, tolerance of 1e-06, and relTol of 0.01.
The final pressure solver (pFinal) uses GAMG with a GaussSeidel smoother, tolerance of 1e-06, and relTol of 0.
The velocity linear solver (U) uses PBiCGStab with a DILU preconditioner, tolerance of 1e-05, and relTol of 0.
The k linear solver uses PBiCGStab with a DILU preconditioner, tolerance of 1e-05, and relTol of 0.
The epsilon linear solver uses PBiCGStab with a DILU preconditioner, tolerance of 1e-05, and relTol of 0.

The PISO algorithm uses 2 correctors (nCorrectors = 2).
The number of non-orthogonal correctors is 0 (nNonOrthogonalCorrectors = 0).
