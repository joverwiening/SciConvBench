The simulation is a 2D incompressible unsteady laminar flow past a circular cylinder using OpenFOAM's pimpleFoam solver.

The domain is a rectangular box extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m in the streamwise direction, y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m in the cross-stream direction, and z = -1 m to z = 1 m in the spanwise direction.
A circular cylinder of diameter 2 m is centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).
The front and back faces in the z-direction use the empty boundary condition to enforce a 2D simulation.

The solver is pimpleFoam for incompressible unsteady flow.

The flow is laminar using the generalizedNewtonian viscosity model with a CrossPowerLaw submodel.
The kinematic viscosity nu is 0.01 m²/s.
The CrossPowerLaw parameters are nu0 = 10, m = 0.4, and n = 3.

The start time is 0 s.
The end time is 0.5 s.
The time step deltaT is 0.0025 s.
Results are written every 0.05 s, corresponding to a write interval of 20 time steps.

The initial conditions are U = (0 0 0) m/s and p = 0 throughout the domain.

The boundary condition at the inlet (x = -5) is fixedValue for velocity U = (1 0 0) m/s and zeroGradient for pressure.
The boundary condition at the outlet (x = 5) is zeroGradient for velocity and fixedValue for pressure p = 0.
The boundary condition at the top wall (y = 2.5) is noSlip for velocity and zeroGradient for pressure.
The boundary condition at the bottom wall (y = -1.5) is noSlip for velocity and zeroGradient for pressure.
The boundary condition on the cylinder surface is noSlip for velocity and zeroGradient for pressure.
The boundary condition on the front and back faces (z = -1 and z = 1) is empty.

The mesh is generated using blockMesh for the background rectangular grid combined with snappyHexMesh to cut out and refine around the cylinder.
The background blockMesh grid uses approximately 200 cells in the x-direction, 80 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
snappyHexMesh refines the mesh near the cylinder surface to at least 3 levels of refinement, with 5 boundary layers on the cylinder surface having an expansion ratio of 1.2 and a final layer thickness ratio of 0.3.

The time derivative scheme is Euler (first-order implicit).
The gradient scheme is Gauss linear.
The divergence scheme for the convective term (div(phi,U)) is Gauss linearUpwind grad(U).
The Laplacian scheme is Gauss linear corrected.
The interpolation scheme is linear.
The surface normal gradient scheme is corrected.

The pressure linear solver is GAMG with a GaussSeidel smoother, tolerance of 1e-06, and relative tolerance of 0.01.
The velocity linear solver is smoothSolver with a GaussSeidel smoother, tolerance of 1e-06, and relative tolerance of 0.1.
The PIMPLE algorithm uses 2 outer correctors, 1 non-orthogonal corrector, and 2 pressure correctors.
