The simulation will model an incompressible lid-driven cavity flow using the pisoFoam solver.
The cavity has physical dimensions of 1 unit in the x-direction, 1 unit in the y-direction, and 0.1 units in the z-direction.
The computational grid will consist of 20 cells in the x-direction, 20 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The top wall, designated as 'movingWall', will have a uniform velocity of 1 m/s in the x-direction.
The 'fixedWalls' will have a no-slip boundary condition, meaning velocity is zero at these walls.
The front and back faces of the cavity will be designated as 'empty' boundary conditions.
The kinematic viscosity (nu) is set as a constant value of 1e-05 m^2/s.
The initial velocity field for the entire domain will be set to zero.
The initial pressure field for the entire domain will be set to zero.
The k-epsilon turbulence model will be used for the simulation.
The initial turbulent kinetic energy (k) will be set uniformly to 1.5e-4 throughout the domain.
The initial turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) will be set uniformly to 1.4e-4 throughout the domain.
The boundary condition for turbulent kinetic energy (k) at the 'movingWall' and 'fixedWalls' will be kqRWallFunction.
The boundary condition for turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) at the 'movingWall' and 'fixedWalls' will be epsilonWallFunction.
The convection scheme for div(phi,U) will be Gauss limitedLinearV 1.
The convection scheme for div(phi,k) will be Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The convection scheme for div(phi,epsilon) will be Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The viscous term scheme will be div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear.
The gradient terms, including grad(p), will use the Gauss linear discretization scheme.
The simulation will run from a total time of 0 to 10 units.
The time step size for the simulation will be 0.005 units.
