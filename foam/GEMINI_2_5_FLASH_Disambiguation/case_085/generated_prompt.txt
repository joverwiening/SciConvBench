The simulation will model pulverized coal combustion.
The furnace is a 2D rectangular geometry with a height of 2 meters and a width of 0.5 meters.
Hot air is injected into the furnace at a temperature of 400 K.
The inlet velocity of the hot air is 0 m/s in the width direction and 20 m/s in the height direction.
Pulverized coal particles are injected into the furnace along with the hot air.
The mean diameter of the pulverized coal particles is 100 microns.
The particle size distribution for the pulverized coal follows a Rosin-Rammler distribution with a spread parameter of 1.1.
The furnace walls are maintained at a constant temperature of 1200 K.
The simulation must include radiation heat transfer.
The simulation will proceed with the specified inlet air temperature of 400 K and wall temperature of 1200 K, without adjustment to ensure ignition or sustained combustion.
