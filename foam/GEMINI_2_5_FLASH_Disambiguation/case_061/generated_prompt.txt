The simulation will model turbulent flow in a channel using the pimpleFoam solver.
The channel has a length (Lx) of 2.0 m, a width (Lz) of 0.10 m, and a height (H) of 0.10 m.
The flow is driven by a meanVelocityForce to maintain a target bulk velocity of (1 0 0) m/s in the x-direction.
The fluid density is 1.225 kg/m^3.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The RAS kOmegaSST turbulence model will be used.
Velocity boundary conditions at xMin and xMax are cyclic.
Velocity boundary conditions at zMin and zMax are cyclic.
Velocity boundary conditions at the lowerWall and upperWall are noSlip.
Pressure boundary conditions at xMin and xMax are cyclic.
Pressure boundary conditions at zMin and zMax are cyclic.
Pressure boundary conditions at the lowerWall and upperWall are zeroGradient.
Initial conditions for velocity are uniform at (1 0 0) m/s.
Initial conditions for pressure are uniform at 0 Pa.
Initial conditions for turbulent kinetic energy (k) and specific dissipation rate (omega) will be set using standard empirical correlations for turbulent channel flow.
A uniform Cartesian mesh will be used with sufficient resolution to capture turbulent features.
Standard OpenFOAM discretization schemes will be employed.
The simulation will run until a statistically steady state is achieved.
