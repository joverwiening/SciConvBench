The simulation will be a two-dimensional Rayleigh-Benard convection cell in a rectangular cavity.
The cavity dimensions are 9 units wide and 1 unit tall.
The domain will be extruded a single cell in the spanwise direction.
The front and back patches will be treated as empty to enforce 2D behavior.
The mesh will consist of 90 cells in the x direction, 10 cells in the y direction, and 1 cell in the z direction.
The buoyantFoam solver will be used.
Gravity will act downward in the negative y direction.
The reference state will be air-like with a reference density of 1.0 kg/m^3.
The reference temperature will be 300 K.
The thermal expansion coefficient will be 3.3e-3 1/K.
The kinematic viscosity will be 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The thermal diffusivity will be 2.1e-5 m^2/s, resulting in a Prandtl number of approximately 0.7.
The temperature of the bottom wall will be fixed at 305 K.
The temperature of the top wall will be fixed at 295 K.
The vertical side walls will be adiabatic.
No-slip velocity conditions will be applied on all solid walls.
The flow will be initialized at rest with a uniform temperature field of 300 K.
The k-epsilon turbulence model will be used.
The constant/momentumTransport dictionary will be set to simulationType RAS with model kEpsilon, turbulence on, and printCoeffs on.
The constant/physicalProperties dictionary will use heRhoThermo with equationOfState Boussinesq.
The constant/physicalProperties dictionary will set molWeight 29, rho0 1.0, T0 300, beta 3.3e-3, Cv 714.3, Hf 0, mu 1.5e-5, and Pr 0.714.
In the 0/k file, the internalField will be uniform 1e-5, with kqRWallFunction on walls and empty on front/back.
In the 0/epsilon file, the internalField will be uniform 1e-5, with epsilonWallFunction on walls and empty on front/back.
In the 0/nut file, the internalField will be uniform 0, with nutkWallFunction on walls and empty on front/back.
In the 0/alphat file, the internalField will be uniform 0, with compressible::alphatJayatillekeWallFunction on walls, Prt 0.85, and empty on front/back.
In the 0/U file, the internalField will be uniform (0 0 0), with noSlip on all solid walls and empty on front/back.
In the 0/T file, the internalField will be uniform 300, the bottom patch will have fixedValue 305, the top patch fixedValue 295, the sides zeroGradient, and front/back empty.
In the 0/p file, the internalField will be uniform 0, with walls calculated and front/back empty.
In the 0/p_rgh file, the internalField will be uniform 0, with walls fixedFluxPressure and front/back empty.
In the system/fvSolution dictionary, p_rgh and (U|e|k|epsilon) solvers will have a tolerance of 1e-8 and a relative tolerance of 0.01.
The corresponding *Final solvers in system/fvSolution will have a relative tolerance of 0.
The simulation will run for a total physical time of 1000 seconds.
A fixed time step (deltaT) of 1 second will be used.
The adjustTimeStep, maxCo, and maxDeltaT controls will not be enabled.
