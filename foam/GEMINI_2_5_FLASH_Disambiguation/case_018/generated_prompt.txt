The domain is a rectangular domain spanning x from 0 to 0.02 m, y from -0.01 to 0.01 m, and z from -0.01 to 0.01 m.
The grid uses 100 points in the x direction, 40 points in the y direction, and 1 cell in the z direction.
The chemistry type is EulerImplicit.
The ODE solver is Rosenbrock43.
The combustion model is laminar.
Momentum transport is laminar.
Compressible finite-rate chemistry is used with hePsiThermo.
CoefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture mixing is used.
Sutherland transport is used.
JANAF thermodynamics is used.
The reactingFoam solver is used.
The timestep is 1e-4 seconds.
The simulation end time is 0.5 seconds.
Results are written at an interval of 0.05 seconds.
A skeletal methane-air chemical mechanism is used for the simulation.
