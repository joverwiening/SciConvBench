The simulation will be a two-dimensional Rayleigh-Benard convection cell in a rectangular cavity. The cavity is 9 units wide and 1 unit tall. The domain will be extruded a single cell in the spanwise direction, and the front and back patches will be treated as empty to enforce 2D behavior. The buoyantFoam solver will be used. Gravity will act downward in the negative y direction. The reference state will be air-like with a density of 1.0 kg/m^3, a reference temperature of 300 K, a thermal expansion coefficient of 3.3e-3 1/K, a kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m^2/s, and a thermal diffusivity of 2.1e-5 m^2/s. The bottom wall temperature will be fixed at 305 K, and the top wall temperature will be fixed at 295 K. The vertical side walls will be adiabatic. No-slip velocity conditions will be applied on all solid walls. A fixed timestep of 1s will be used, with adjustTimeStep, maxCo, and maxDeltaT disabled. The simulation will run up to 1000 s of physical time. Results will be written every 50 steps. The k-epsilon turbulence model will be used. The constant/momentumTransport dictionary will be set to simulationType RAS with model kEpsilon, turbulence on, and printCoeffs on. The constant/physicalProperties dictionary will use heRhoThermo with equationOfState Boussinesq, molWeight 29, rho0 1.0, T0 300, beta 3.3e-3, Cv 714.3, Hf 0, mu 1.5e-5, and Pr 0.714. In the 0/k file, the internalField will be uniform 1e-5, kqRWallFunction will be used on walls, and empty will be used on front/back. In the 0/epsilon file, the internalField will be uniform 1e-5, epsilonWallFunction will be used on walls, and empty will be used on front/back. In the 0/nut file, the internalField will be uniform 0, nutkWallFunction will be used on walls, and empty will be used on front/back. In the 0/alphat file, the internalField will be uniform 0, compressible::alphatJayatillekeWallFunction will be used on walls with Prt 0.85, and empty will be used on front/back. In the 0/U file, noSlip will be set on all solid walls, and empty will be set on front/back. In the 0/T file, the bottom will be fixedValue 305, the top will be fixedValue 295, the sides will be zeroGradient, and front/back will be empty. In the 0/p file, walls will be calculated, and front/back will be empty. In the 0/p_rgh file, walls will be fixedFluxPressure, and front/back will be empty. In the system/fvSolution dictionary, p_rgh and (U|e|k|epsilon) solvers will have a tolerance of 1e-8 and a relTol of 0.01. The corresponding *Final solvers will have a relTol of 0. The mesh will have 90 cells in the x-direction and 10 cells in the y-direction.
