The simulation models an incompressible lid-driven cavity flow using the pisoFoam solver.
The computational domain is a square cavity with dimensions 1 unit in the x-direction, 1 unit in the y-direction, and 0.1 units in the z-direction.
The grid resolution is 20 cells in the x-direction, 20 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The top wall, designated 'movingWall', has a uniform velocity of 1 m/s in the x-direction.
The remaining walls, designated 'fixedWalls', have a no-slip boundary condition, meaning velocity is zero at these walls.
The front and back faces are designated as 'empty' boundary conditions.
The simulation runs from time 0 to 10 units with a time step of 0.005 units.
Results are output every 100 time steps.
The kinematic viscosity (nu) is constant with a value of 1e-05 m^2/s.
The k-epsilon turbulence model is used.
Initial turbulence fields are set as follows: k = 1.5e-4 uniformly, epsilon = 1.4e-4 uniformly, and nut = 0 uniformly.
Turbulence wall functions are applied to all walls (front/back empty): kqRWallFunction for k, epsilonWallFunction for epsilon, and nutkWallFunction for nut.
The domain is initialized with zero velocity at the beginning of the simulation.
Pressure boundary conditions are zeroGradient for 'movingWall' and 'fixedWalls', and 'empty' for 'frontAndBack'.
Convection schemes are specified as div(phi,U) Gauss limitedLinearV 1, div(phi,k) Gauss limitedLinear 1, and div(phi,epsilon) Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The viscous term scheme is div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear.
