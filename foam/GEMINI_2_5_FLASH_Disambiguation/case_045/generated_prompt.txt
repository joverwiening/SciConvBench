The simulation will be a 2D buoyant flow.
The domain is a 1 meter by 1 meter square.
The mesh consists of 50 by 50 uniform cells.
The fluid is air with a reference density of 1.184 kg/m^3, a kinematic viscosity of 1.568e-5 m^2/s, a thermal expansion coefficient of 0.00339 K^-1, and a specific heat capacity of 1007 J/(kg.K).
Gravity acts in the (0 -9.81 0) direction.
The initial fluid temperature is uniformly 295 K.
The initial fluid velocity is uniformly (0 0 0) m/s.
The bottom wall has a fixed temperature of 300 K and a no-slip velocity condition.
The top wall has a fixed temperature of 290 K and a no-slip velocity condition.
The side walls have a zero-gradient thermal boundary condition and a no-slip velocity condition.
