The simulation is a transient two-phase dam-break with obstacles.
The computational domain is a cube spanning from (0,0,0) to (1,1,1).
The domain is discretized uniformly with 32 cells in each direction, resulting in a (32 x 32 x 32) grid.
An obstacle is present in the domain, defined by the region from (0.375, 0.375, 0) to (0.625, 0.625, 0.25).
Gravity is set to g = (0, 0, -9.81) m/s^2.
The initial water column is a rectangular prism where the water volume fraction (alpha.water) is 1 within the box (0, 0, 0) to (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75) and 0 elsewhere.
The simulation time step (deltaT) is 1e-3 seconds.
The simulation end time (endTime) is 0.1 seconds.
Data is written at intervals of 0.02 seconds.
The boundary condition for the top face (z=1) is an open atmosphere patch.
The boundary conditions for the remaining five faces of the domain are walls.
