The simulation models conjugate heat transfer between a solid porous block and air flowing around it.
The solid block region is 0.1 m x 0.05 m x 0.01 m.
The overall fluid domain is 0.5 m x 0.1 m x 0.01 m.
The solid block is centered within the overall domain.
The solid block is modeled as an effective homogeneous solid with a thermal conductivity of 20 W/mK.
The fluid is air, modeled as a compressible perfect gas (heRhoThermo, perfectGas, Sutherland) with a specific heat capacity (Cp) of 1005 J/kg/K and a molar weight of 28.9 kg/kmol.
The thermal conductivity of the air is 0.033 W/mK.
The fluid flows only around the block; no momentum equation is solved in the solid region.
The inlet temperature of the air is 400 K.
The fluid inlet velocity is 1 m/s.
The fluid outlet is a pressure outlet at 101325 Pa.
All other external fluid domain walls are adiabatic and no-slip.
All external solid block faces not in contact with the fluid are adiabatic.
The simulation uses a RANS turbulence model, specifically k-epsilon.
Initial conditions for the fluid are 300 K and zero velocity.
Initial conditions for the solid are 300 K.
Gravity effects are neglected.
