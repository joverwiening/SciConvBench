Simulate a 2D lid-driven cavity flow.
The computational domain is a square cavity of 0.1m x 0.1m.
The fluid has a constant kinematic viscosity of 0.01 m^2/s.
The fluid density is 1 kg/m^3.
The top wall has a fixedValue uniform velocity of (1 0 0) m/s.
The bottom, left, and right walls have a noSlip velocity boundary condition, fixedValue uniform (0 0 0) m/s.
All walls (top, bottom, left, right) have a zeroGradient pressure boundary condition.
The entire domain is initialized with zero velocity.
The entire domain is initialized with zero pressure.
