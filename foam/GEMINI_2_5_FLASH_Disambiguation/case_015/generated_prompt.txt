The simulation domain is a rectangular volume spanning x from 0 to 0.02 meters, y from -0.01 to 0.01 meters, and z from -0.01 to 0.01 meters.
The initial temperature throughout the domain at time 0 is 2000 Kelvin.
The solver used is reactingFoam.
The chemistry type is EulerImplicit.
The ODE solver is Rosenbrock43.
The combustion model is laminar.
The momentum transport model is laminar.
Compressible finite-rate chemistry is used with hePsiThermo.
The mixing model is coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture.
The transport model is Sutherland.
The thermodynamics data is JANAF.
The left boundary (x=0) is named "fuel".
At the "fuel" boundary, the velocity is a fixed value of 0.1 m/s.
At the "fuel" boundary, the temperature is a fixed value of 293 Kelvin.
At the "fuel" boundary, methane (CH4) is injected as a fixed value species concentration.
At the "fuel" boundary, the pressure boundary condition is zeroGradient.
The right boundary (x=0.02) is named "air".
At the "air" boundary, the velocity is a fixed value of -0.1 m/s.
At the "air" boundary, the temperature is a fixed value of 293 Kelvin.
At the "air" boundary, oxidizer (air) is supplied as a fixed value species concentration with a mass fraction of O2 (Y_O2) of 0.23 and the balance as N2.
At the "air" boundary, the pressure boundary condition is zeroGradient.
The top and bottom boundaries (y=-0.01 and y=0.01) are named "outlet".
At the "outlet" boundaries, the pressure is a fixed value of 0 Pa gauge pressure.
At the "outlet" boundaries, the velocity boundary condition is zeroGradient.
At the "outlet" boundaries, the temperature boundary condition is zeroGradient.
At the "outlet" boundaries, the species boundary condition is zeroGradient.
The front and back faces (z=-0.01 and z=0.01) have an "empty" boundary condition type.
