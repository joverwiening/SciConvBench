The simulation will be set up as a laminar counter-flow diffusion flame using reactingFoam.
The configuration is a 2-D opposed-jet.
The computational domain is rectangular, spanning x from 0 to 0.02 m, y from -0.01 to 0.01 m, and z from -0.01 to 0.01 m.
The grid will consist of 100 points in the x direction, 40 points in the y direction, and 1 cell in the z direction.
The left boundary at x=0 is named 'fuel' and serves as an inlet.
The right boundary at x=0.02 is named 'air' and serves as an inlet.
The top and bottom boundaries are named 'outlet'.
The front and back faces are set as 'empty' type.
The domain is initialized at time 0 with a uniform temperature of 2000 K.
The domain is initialized at time 0 with a uniform pressure of 101325 Pa.
The domain is initialized at time 0 with uniform chemical species mass fractions of O2 at 0.23 and N2 at 0.77.
The fuel inlet boundary (x=0) has a velocity of 0.1 m/s.
The fuel inlet boundary (x=0) injects methane (CH4) with a mass fraction of 1.0.
The fuel inlet boundary (x=0) has a temperature of 293 K.
The air inlet boundary (x=0.02) has a velocity of -0.1 m/s.
The air inlet boundary (x=0.02) supplies O2 with a mass fraction of 0.23 and N2 as the balance (0.77).
The air inlet boundary (x=0.02) has a temperature of 293 K.
The chemistry type used is EulerImplicit.
The ODE solver used is Rosenbrock43.
The combustion model is laminar.
Momentum transport is laminar.
Compressible finite-rate chemistry is used with hePsiThermo.
CoefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture mixing is used.
Sutherland transport properties are used.
JANAF thermodynamics are used.
The reaction mechanism for methane-air combustion is the 16-species, 25-reaction mechanism by Smooke and Giovangigli.
The simulation uses a timestep of 1e-4 seconds.
The simulation end time is 0.5 seconds.
Results are written at an interval of 0.05 seconds.
