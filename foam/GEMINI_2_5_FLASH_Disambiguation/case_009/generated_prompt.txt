The simulation models a square cavity with dimensions 1 unit in x, 1 unit in y, and 0.1 units in z.
The simulation runs from time 0 to 10 units with a time step of 0.005 units.
Results are output every 100 time steps.
The kinematic viscosity (nu) is constant at 1e-05 m^2/s.
The k-epsilon turbulence model is used.
The pisoFoam solver is used.
Initial conditions for velocity (U) are zero throughout the domain.
Initial conditions for pressure (p) are zero throughout the domain.
Initial conditions for turbulent kinetic energy (k) are 1.5e-4 uniformly throughout the domain.
Initial conditions for turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) are 1.4e-4 uniformly throughout the domain.
Initial conditions for turbulent kinematic viscosity (nut) are 0 uniformly throughout the domain.
Boundary conditions for velocity (U) on the top wall ('movingWall') are a uniform 1 m/s in the x-direction.
Boundary conditions for velocity (U) on the 'fixedWalls' are no-slip (zero velocity).
Boundary conditions for velocity (U) on the front and back faces are 'empty'.
Boundary conditions for pressure (p) on all walls are zeroGradient.
Boundary conditions for pressure (p) on the front and back faces are 'empty'.
Boundary conditions for turbulent kinetic energy (k) on all walls use kqRWallFunction.
Boundary conditions for turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) on all walls use epsilonWallFunction.
Boundary conditions for turbulent kinematic viscosity (nut) on all walls use nutkWallFunction.
Boundary conditions for k, epsilon, and nut on the front and back faces are 'empty'.
The convection scheme for div(phi,U) is Gauss limitedLinearV 1.
The convection scheme for div(phi,k) is Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The convection scheme for div(phi,epsilon) is Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The viscous term scheme is div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear.
