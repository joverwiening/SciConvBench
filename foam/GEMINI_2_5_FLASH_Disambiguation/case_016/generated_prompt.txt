The simulation will be set up as a laminar counter-flow diffusion flame using the reactingFoam solver.
The configuration is a 2-D opposed-jet.
The computational domain is rectangular, spanning x from 0 to 0.02 m, y from -0.01 to 0.01 m, and z from -0.01 to 0.01 m.
The mesh will consist of 100 grid points in the x-direction, 40 grid points in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The left boundary (x=0) is named "fuel" and acts as an inlet.
The right boundary (x=0.02) is named "air" and acts as an inlet.
The top and bottom boundaries are named "outlet".
The front and back faces are set as "empty" boundary conditions.
At the fuel inlet (x=0), methane (CH4) is injected at a velocity of 0.1 m/s and a temperature of 293 K.
At the air inlet (x=0.02), oxidizer (air with Y_O2 = 0.23 and balance N2) is supplied at a velocity of -0.1 m/s and a temperature of 293 K.
The top and bottom outlet boundaries will use a zeroGradient boundary condition for velocity and species, and a fixedValue boundary condition for pressure at 101325 Pa.
The chemistry type is EulerImplicit, and the ODE solver is Rosenbrock43.
The combustion model and momentum transport are laminar.
Compressible finite-rate chemistry is used with hePsiThermo, coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture mixing, Sutherland transport, and JANAF thermodynamics.
The reaction mechanism used is GRI-Mech 3.0.
The initial pressure throughout the domain at time 0 is 101325 Pa.
The initial temperature throughout the domain at time 0 is 2000 K.
The initial species concentrations throughout the domain at time 0 are uniform N2 (Y_N2 = 1.0, Y_CH4 = 0, Y_O2 = 0).
The simulation will use a timestep of 1e-4 seconds and an end time of 0.5 seconds.
Results will be written at an interval of 0.05 seconds.
