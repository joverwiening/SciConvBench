The simulation will be performed using the buoyantFoam solver.
The computational domain is a rectangular cavity 9 units wide in the x-direction and 1 unit tall in the y-direction.
The domain is extruded a single cell in the spanwise (z) direction.
The mesh will consist of 90 cells in the x-direction, 10 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The front and back patches of the cavity will be treated as empty to enforce a 2D simulation.
Gravity will act downward in the negative y-direction.
The reference fluid state will be air-like with a reference density (rho0) of 1.0 kg/m^3 and a reference temperature (T0) of 300 K.
The thermal expansion coefficient (beta) will be 3.3e-3 1/K.
The kinematic viscosity (mu) will be 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The thermal diffusivity will be 2.1e-5 m^2/s, corresponding to a Prandtl number (Pr) of approximately 0.7.
The bottom wall temperature will be fixed at 305 K.
The top wall temperature will be fixed at 295 K.
The vertical side walls will be adiabatic (zeroGradient for temperature).
No-slip velocity conditions will be applied on all solid walls.
The flow will be initialized at rest with a uniform velocity field of (0 0 0) m/s.
The initial temperature field will be uniform at 300 K.
The simulation will use a fixed timestep (deltaT) of 1 second.
Automatic timestep adjustment (adjustTimeStep, maxCo, maxDeltaT) will be disabled.
The simulation will run for a total physical time of 1000 seconds.
Results will be written to disk every 50 simulation steps.
The k-epsilon turbulence model will be used.
The constant/momentumTransport dictionary will be configured with simulationType RAS, model kEpsilon, turbulence on, and printCoeffs on.
The constant/physicalProperties dictionary will use heRhoThermo with equationOfState Boussinesq.
The molWeight will be 29, rho0 1.0, T0 300, beta 3.3e-3, Cv 714.3, Hf 0, mu 1.5e-5, and Pr 0.714.
The initial field for k (turbulent kinetic energy) in 0/k will be uniform 1e-5, with kqRWallFunction on walls and empty on front/back.
The initial field for epsilon (turbulent dissipation rate) in 0/epsilon will be uniform 1e-5, with epsilonWallFunction on walls and empty on front/back.
The initial field for nut (turbulent kinematic viscosity) in 0/nut will be uniform 0, with nutkWallFunction on walls and empty on front/back.
The initial field for alphat (turbulent thermal diffusivity) in 0/alphat will be uniform 0, with compressible::alphatJayatillekeWallFunction on walls, Prt 0.85, and empty on front/back.
The initial field for U (velocity) in 0/U will be uniform (0 0 0), with noSlip on all solid walls and empty on front/back.
The initial field for T (temperature) in 0/T will be uniform 300, with fixedValue 305 on the bottom, fixedValue 295 on the top, zeroGradient on the sides, and empty on front/back.
The initial field for p (pressure) in 0/p will be uniform 0, with calculated on walls and empty on front/back.
The initial field for p_rgh (modified pressure) in 0/p_rgh will be uniform 0, with fixedFluxPressure on walls and empty on front/back.
In system/fvSolution, the p_rgh solver will have a tolerance of 1e-8 and a relative tolerance (relTol) of 0.01.
In system/fvSolution, the (U|e|k|epsilon) solvers will have a tolerance of 1e-8 and a relative tolerance (relTol) of 0.01.
The *Final solvers for p_rgh and (U|e|k|epsilon) will have a relative tolerance (relTol) of 0.
