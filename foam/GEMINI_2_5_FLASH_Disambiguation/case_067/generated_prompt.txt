The simulation will model turbulent flow in a porous pipe.
The k-epsilon turbulence model will be used.
The pipe has an inner radius of 0.05 m.
The pipe has a length of 2 m.
The pipe is entirely filled with the porous material.
The fluid is air.
The porosity of the porous material is 0.4.
The permeability of the porous material is 2e-8 m^2 in each direction.
The inertial resistance coefficient of the porous material is 0.
The inlet boundary condition is a uniform velocity of 10 m/s in the axial direction.
The outlet boundary condition is a pressure outlet set to 0 Pa gauge pressure.
