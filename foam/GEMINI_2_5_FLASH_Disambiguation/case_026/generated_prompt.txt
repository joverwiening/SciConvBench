Run a transient laminar two-phase dam-break simulation with obstacles.
Set gravity to (0 0 -9.81) m/s^2.
The computational domain is a cube spanning from (0,0,0) to (1,1,1) meters.
Discretize the domain uniformly with a mesh resolution of 32 cells in x, 32 cells in y, and 32 cells in z directions.
Assign the face at z=1 as an open atmosphere patch.
Assign the remaining five faces of the computational domain as walls.
Model phases using the Volume of Fluid (VOF) method.
The first phase is water with a density of 1000 kg/m^3 and a kinematic viscosity of 1.0e-6 m^2/s.
The second phase is air with a density of 1 kg/m^3 and a kinematic viscosity of 1.48e-5 m^2/s.
Set the surface tension between water and air to 0.07 N/m.
Initialize the water column as a rectangular prism where the volume fraction of water (alpha.water) is 1 within the box defined by (0, 0, 0) to (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75) meters.
Set the volume fraction of water to 0 elsewhere in the domain at initialization.
Include a stationary obstacle in the domain, defined as a rectangular prism from (0.375, 0.375, 0) to (0.625, 0.625, 0.25) meters.
Set the time step (deltaT) to 1e-3 seconds.
Set the end time (endTime) for the simulation to 0.1 seconds.
Set the write interval (writeInterval) for output data to 0.02 seconds.
