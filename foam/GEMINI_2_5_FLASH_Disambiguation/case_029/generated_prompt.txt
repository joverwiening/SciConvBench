Run a transient laminar two-phase dam-break simulation with obstacles using interFoam.
The computational domain is a cube spanning from (0,0,0) to (1,1,1) meters.
The domain is discretized uniformly with 32 cells in each direction, resulting in a (32 x 32 x 32) grid.
Gravity is set to (0 0 -9.81) m/s^2.
An obstacle is present in the domain, defined by the box from (0.375 0.375 0) to (0.625 0.625 0.25) meters.
The water phase has a density of 1000 kg/m^3 and a kinematic viscosity of 1.0e-6 m^2/s.
The air phase has a density of 1 kg/m^3 and a kinematic viscosity of 1.48e-5 m^2/s.
The initial water column is a rectangular prism where the volume fraction of water (alpha.water) is 1 inside the box (0, 0, 0) to (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75) meters.
The volume fraction of water is 0 elsewhere in the domain at initialization.
The simulation time step (deltaT) is 1e-3 seconds.
The simulation end time (endTime) is 0.1 seconds.
Simulation results are written to disk every 0.02 seconds (writeInterval).
