Simulate 3D sloshing of water in a partially filled rectangular tank using interFoam, starting from an initially tilted free surface.

Solver: interFoam

Geometry: Tank 1.0 m (x) x 0.6 m (y) x 0.1 m (z). Gravity acts in -y: g (0 -9.81 0).
Mesh: blockMesh 200 x 120 x 1 (2D in x-y with empty front/back) or 200x120x20 for fully 3D.

Boundary conditions:
- walls (left, right, bottom): noSlip for U; p_rgh fixedFluxPressure; alpha.water zeroGradient.
- top: open to atmosphere: U pressureInletOutletVelocity; p_rgh totalPressure p0 0; alpha.water inletOutlet (inletValue 0).
- frontAndBack: empty (if 2D).

Initial tilted interface:
OpenFOAM setFields does not support an analytic plane directly. Use a staircase approximation with multiple x-slices.
Example: target water depth varies linearly from 0.25 m at x=0 to 0.35 m at x=1 (tilt).
1) defaultFieldValues: alpha.water = 0 (air)
2) For i = 0..9, add a boxToCell for slice x in [0.1*i, 0.1*(i+1)] with water height hi = 0.255 + 0.01*i:
   boxToCell (0.0 0.0 -1) to (0.1 0.255 1) -> alpha.water 1
   boxToCell (0.1 0.0 -1) to (0.2 0.265 1) -> alpha.water 1
   boxToCell (0.2 0.0 -1) to (0.3 0.275 1) -> alpha.water 1
   ...
   boxToCell (0.9 0.0 -1) to (1.0 0.345 1) -> alpha.water 1
Initialize U=(0 0 0), p_rgh=0.

Time controls: endTime 10; deltaT 1e-3; adjustTimeStep yes; maxCo 0.5; writeInterval 0.05.
