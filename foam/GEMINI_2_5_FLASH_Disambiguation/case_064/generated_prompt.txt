The simulation will be a Large Eddy Simulation (LES) of flow over periodic hills.
The hill profile will be the standard ERCOFTAC-style periodic hill.
The hill height H is 0.028 m.
The domain streamwise length Lx is 9H, which is 0.252 m.
The domain spanwise width Lz is 4.5H, which is 0.126 m.
The channel height Ly is 3.036H, which is 0.0850 m.
The fluid kinematic viscosity is 2.64e-6 m^2/s.
The fluid density is assumed to be 1 kg/m^3 for an incompressible solver.
The bulk velocity Ub is 1.0 m/s.
The top surface (at Ly) boundary condition for velocity (U) is noSlip.
The top surface (at Ly) boundary condition for pressure (p) is zeroGradient.
The streamwise (xMin and xMax) surfaces have cyclic boundary conditions for all fields.
The spanwise (zMin and zMax) surfaces have cyclic boundary conditions for all fields.
The bottom surface (hill surface) boundary condition for velocity (U) is noSlip.
The bottom surface (hill surface) boundary condition for pressure (p) is zeroGradient.
The subgrid-scale (SGS) model used for the LES simulation is WALE.
