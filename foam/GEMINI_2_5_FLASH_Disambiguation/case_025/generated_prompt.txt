The simulation will be a transient laminar two-phase dam-break with obstacles using the interFoam solver.
The computational domain is a cube spanning from (0,0,0) to (1,1,1) meters.
The domain is discretized uniformly with 32 cells in each direction, resulting in a (32 x 32 x 32) grid.
An obstacle is present in the domain, defined by the coordinates from (0.375, 0.375, 0) to (0.625, 0.625, 0.25) meters.
Gravity is set to g = (0, 0, -9.81) m/s^2.
The top face (z=1) is assigned as an open atmosphere patch.
The remaining five faces of the domain are assigned as wall patches.
The initial water column is a rectangular prism where the volume fraction of water (alpha.water) is 1 inside the box (0, 0, 0) to (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75) meters.
The volume fraction of water is 0 elsewhere in the domain at initialization.
The density of water is 1000 kg/m^3.
The density of air is 1 kg/m^3.
The kinematic viscosity of water is 1.0e-6 m^2/s.
The kinematic viscosity of air is 1.48e-5 m^2/s.
The surface tension coefficient between water and air is 0.07 N/m.
The time step (deltaT) is 1e-3 seconds.
The simulation end time (endTime) is 0.1 seconds.
Data will be written to disk every 0.02 seconds (writeInterval).
