The simulation domain is a cube spanning from (0,0,0) to (1,1,1).
An obstacle is present in the domain from (0.375, 0.375, 0) to (0.625, 0.625, 0.25).
Gravity is set to (0, 0, -9.81) m/s^2.
The top face (z=1) is assigned as an open atmosphere patch.
The remaining five faces of the domain are assigned as wall boundaries.
Phases are modeled using the Volume of Fluid (VOF) method.
Water properties are density = 1000 kg/m^3 and kinematic viscosity = 1.0e-6 m^2/s.
Air properties are density = 1 kg/m^3 and kinematic viscosity = 1.48e-5 m^2/s.
Surface tension between water and air is 0.07 N/m.
The initial water column is a rectangular prism where the water volume fraction (alpha.water) is 1 inside the box (0, 0, 0) to (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75) and 0 elsewhere.
The total simulation time is 0.1 s.
The domain is discretized uniformly with a mesh resolution of 32 cells in x, 32 cells in y, and 32 cells in z directions.
The maximum time step size for the simulation is 1e-3 s.
Simulation results are saved every 0.02 s.
A first-order implicit Euler scheme is used for time integration.
