The simulation is a two-dimensional Rayleigh-Benard convection cell in a rectangular cavity.
The cavity is 9 units wide and 1 unit tall, extruded a single cell in the spanwise direction.
The front and back patches are treated as empty to enforce 2D conditions.
The mesh consists of 90 cells in the x direction, 10 cells in the y direction, and 1 cell in the z direction.
The buoyantFoam solver is used.
Gravity acts downward in the negative y direction.
The reference state is air-like with a density of 1.0 kg/m^3, a reference temperature of 300 K, a thermal expansion coefficient of 3.3e-3 1/K, a kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m^2/s, and a thermal diffusivity of 2.1e-5 m^2/s (Prandtl number approximately 0.7).
The bottom wall temperature is fixed at 305 K.
The top wall temperature is fixed at 295 K.
The vertical side walls are adiabatic (zeroGradient).
No-slip velocity conditions are applied on all solid walls.
The flow is initialized at rest with a uniform temperature field of 300 K.
In constant/physicalProperties, heRhoThermo is used with the Boussinesq equationOfState.
The molecular weight (molWeight) is 29.
The reference density (rho0) is 1.0.
The reference temperature (T0) is 300.
The thermal expansion coefficient (beta) is 3.3e-3.
The specific heat at constant volume (Cv) is 714.3.
The enthalpy of formation (Hf) is 0.
The dynamic viscosity (mu) is 1.5e-5.
The Prandtl number (Pr) is 0.714.
In 0/k, the internalField is uniform 1e-5, kqRWallFunction is applied on walls, and front/back are empty.
In 0/epsilon, the internalField is uniform 1e-5, epsilonWallFunction is applied on walls, and front/back are empty.
In 0/nut, the internalField is uniform 0, nutkWallFunction is applied on walls, and front/back are empty.
In 0/alphat, the internalField is uniform 0, compressible::alphatJayatillekeWallFunction is applied on walls, Prt is 0.85, and front/back are empty.
In 0/U, the internalField is uniform (0 0 0), noSlip is applied on all solid walls, and front/back are empty.
In 0/T, the internalField is uniform 300, the bottom boundary has a fixedValue of 305, the top boundary has a fixedValue of 295, the sides have zeroGradient, and front/back are empty.
In 0/p, the internalField is uniform 0, walls are calculated, and front/back are empty.
In 0/p_rgh, the internalField is uniform 0, walls have fixedFluxPressure, and front/back are empty.
In system/fvSolution, the p_rgh and (U|e|k|epsilon) solvers have a tolerance of 1e-8 and a relative tolerance (relTol) of 0.01.
The corresponding *Final solvers have a relative tolerance (relTol) of 0.
The k-epsilon turbulence model is used.
In constant/momentumTransport, simulationType is RAS, model is kEpsilon, turbulence is on, and printCoeffs is on.
The total simulation time is 1000 s.
The time step size is 1 s.
In system/fvSchemes, ddtSchemes are CrankNicolson 0.9.
In system/fvSchemes, gradSchemes are Gauss linear.
In system/fvSchemes, divSchemes for div(phi,U) are Gauss linear.
In system/fvSchemes, divSchemes for div(phi,k) are Gauss linearUpwindV.
In system/fvSchemes, divSchemes for div(phi,epsilon) are Gauss linearUpwindV.
In system/fvSchemes, divSchemes for div(phi,T) are Gauss linear.
In system/fvSchemes, divSchemes for div(phi,alphat) are Gauss linear.
