Simulate an oblique shock wave.
The working fluid is a perfect gas with a ratio of specific heats (Gamma) of 1.4.
Assume air as the specific fluid.
The upstream flow has a Mach number of 2.
The upstream static pressure is 101325 Pa.
The upstream static temperature is 300 K.
The simulation is unsteady.
The simulation end time is 0.005 seconds.
The time step (deltaT) is 5e-7 seconds.
The computational domain is a rectangular region of 1.0 meter by 0.5 meters.
The x=0 face is an inlet boundary condition.
At the x=0 inlet, the velocity U is (694.5 0 0) m/s.
At the x=0 inlet, the static pressure p is 101325 Pa.
At the x=0 inlet, the static temperature T is 300 K.
The x=1.0m face is an outlet boundary condition.
At the x=1.0m outlet, a zeroGradient condition is applied for all variables.
The y=0 face is a slip boundary condition.
The y=0.5m face is a slip boundary condition.
A 10-degree wedge is present in the flow.
