The simulation models Rayleigh-Benard convection in a rectangular domain.
The domain dimensions are 2 meters in length, 2 meters in width, and 1 meter in height.
The bottom surface is heated, and the top surface is cooled.
The temperature difference between the bottom and top surfaces is 1 Kelvin.
The fluid properties are set to achieve a Rayleigh number of approximately 2500.
The Prandtl number of the fluid is 0.71.
The thermal expansion coefficient of the fluid is 0.0034 1/K.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is approximately 0.003077 m^2/s.
The thermal diffusivity of the fluid is approximately 0.004334 m^2/s.
The velocity boundary condition for the bottom surface is no-slip.
The velocity boundary condition for the top surface is no-slip.
The velocity boundary conditions for the left and right walls are cyclic.
The velocity boundary conditions for the front and back walls are empty.
