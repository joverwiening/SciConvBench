The simulation domain is a rectangle extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m in the streamwise direction and y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m in the cross-stream direction.
The simulation is 2D, with the domain extending from z = -1 m to z = 1 m, and default back/front faces are empty.
A cylinder with a diameter of 2 m is centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).
The inlet boundary condition (left face) specifies a uniform velocity U = (1 0 0) m/s.
The outlet boundary condition (right face) specifies U zeroGradient and a reference pressure p = 0.
The top and bottom wall boundary conditions specify U noSlip and p zeroGradient.
The cylinder wall boundary condition specifies U noSlip and p zeroGradient.
The solver is pimpleFoam for incompressible unsteady flow.
The simulation starts at startTime = 0 s and ends at endTime = 0.5 s.
The time step is deltaT = 0.0025 s.
Results are written every 0.05 s.
The initial field specifies a quiescent fluid with U = (0 0 0) m/s and p = 0 Pa.
The flow is laminar.
The laminar model uses generalisednewtonian.
The viscosity model is crosspowerlaw with nu0 = 0.01 m^2/s, nuinf = 10, m = 0.4, and n = 3.
The density of the fluid is 1 kg/m^3.
