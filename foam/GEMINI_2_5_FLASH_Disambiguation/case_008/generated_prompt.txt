The simulation will model incompressible turbulent flow in a square cavity.
The cavity dimensions are 1 unit in x, 1 unit in y, and 0.1 units in z.
The computational grid will be 20x20x1 cells in the x, y, and z directions respectively.
The pisoFoam solver will be used.
The simulation will run from time 0 to 10 units with a time step of 0.005 units.
Results will be output every 100 time steps.
The kinematic viscosity (nu) is constant at 1e-05 m^2/s.
The top wall, designated 'movingWall', has a uniform velocity of 1 m/s in the x-direction.
The other walls, designated 'fixedWalls', have a no-slip boundary condition (velocity equal to zero).
The front and back faces are designated as 'empty'.
The domain will be initialized with zero velocity at the beginning of the simulation.
The k-epsilon turbulence model will be used.
The initial condition for turbulent kinetic energy (k) is 1.5e-4 uniformly throughout the domain.
The initial condition for turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) is 1.4e-4 uniformly throughout the domain.
For k, a kqRWallFunction will be applied to all walls, with front and back faces designated as empty.
For epsilon, an epsilonWallFunction will be applied to all walls, with front and back faces designated as empty.
The convection schemes are specified as div(phi,U) Gauss limitedLinearV 1, div(phi,k) Gauss limitedLinear 1, and div(phi,epsilon) Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The viscous term scheme is div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear.
