The simulation will be a transient incompressible flow over a cylinder.
The fluid is water at 25 degrees Celsius.
The cylinder has a diameter of 0.1 meters.
The Reynolds number for the flow is 100.
The computational domain extends 22 times the cylinder diameter in the x-direction and 4.1 times the cylinder diameter in the y-direction.
The simulation duration is 200 seconds, from a start time of 0 seconds to an end time of 200 seconds.
At the inlet, the velocity (U) has a fixed value of (0.001 0 0) m/s.
At the inlet, the pressure (p) has a zero gradient condition.
At the outlet, the velocity (U) has a zero gradient condition.
At the outlet, the pressure (p) has a fixed value of 0 Pa.
At the top and bottom walls, the velocity (U) has a slip condition.
At the top and bottom walls, the pressure (p) has a zero gradient condition.
On the cylinder surface, the velocity (U) has a no-slip condition.
On the cylinder surface, the pressure (p) has a zero gradient condition.
