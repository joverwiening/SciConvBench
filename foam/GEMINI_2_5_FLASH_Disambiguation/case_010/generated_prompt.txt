The simulation models an incompressible lid-driven cavity flow.
The cavity is a square with dimensions 1 unit in x, 1 unit in y, and 0.1 units in z.
The computational grid is 20 cells in the x-direction, 20 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The top wall, designated 'movingWall', has a uniform velocity of 1 m/s in the x-direction.
The remaining walls, designated 'fixedWalls', have a no-slip boundary condition, meaning velocity is zero at these walls.
The front and back faces are designated as 'empty' boundary conditions.
The kinematic viscosity (nu) is constant with a value of 1e-05 m^2/s.
The k-epsilon turbulence model is used.
The turbulence field k is initialized uniformly to 1.5e-4 throughout the domain, with kqRWallFunction applied to all walls (front and back faces are 'empty').
The turbulence field epsilon is initialized uniformly to 1.4e-4 throughout the domain, with epsilonWallFunction applied to all walls (front and back faces are 'empty').
The turbulence field nut is initialized uniformly to 0 throughout the domain, with nutkWallFunction applied to all walls (front and back faces are 'empty').
The pisoFoam solver is used for the simulation.
The domain is initialized with zero velocity at the beginning of the simulation.
The convection scheme for div(phi,U) is Gauss limitedLinearV 1.
The convection scheme for div(phi,k) is Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The convection scheme for div(phi,epsilon) is Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The viscous term uses div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear.
The simulation runs until a total time of 10 units.
The time step for the simulation is 0.005 units.
