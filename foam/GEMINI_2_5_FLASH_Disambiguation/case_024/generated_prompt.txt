A 2D incompressible flow simulation will be performed.
The computational domain is a rectangle extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m and y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m.
A circular cylinder with a diameter of 2 m is centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).
The simulation starts at time = 0 s and ends at time = 0.5 s.
The time step for the simulation is 0.0025 s.
Results will be written every 0.05 s.
The initial fluid velocity field is U = (0 0 0) m/s.
The initial pressure field is p = 0 Pa.
At the inlet (x = -5 m), the velocity is U = (1 0 0) m/s.
At the outlet (x = 5 m), the velocity has a zero gradient and the pressure is p = 0 Pa (reference).
At the top wall (y = 2.5 m), the velocity is no-slip and the pressure has a zero gradient.
At the bottom wall (y = -1.5 m), the velocity is no-slip and the pressure has a zero gradient.
On the cylinder surface, the velocity is no-slip and the pressure has a zero gradient.
