Simulate a 2D flow past a circular cylinder.
The computational domain is a rectangular region extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m in the streamwise direction and y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m in the cross-stream direction.
The 2D slice has a thickness of 2 m in the z-direction, extending from z = -1 m to z = 1 m.
A circular cylinder with a diameter of 2 m is centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).
The fluid density is assumed to be 1 kg/m^3.
The flow is laminar.
The laminar model uses a generalisedNewtonian viscosity model.
The viscosity model is crosspowerlaw with nuinf = 10, m = 0.4, n = 3, and nu0 = 0.01 m^2/s.
Boundary conditions are applied as follows:
At the inlet (left, x = -5 m), the velocity U is (1 0 0) m/s.
At the outlet (right, x = 5 m), the velocity U has a zeroGradient condition, and the pressure p is 0 (reference).
At the top wall (y = 2.5 m) and bottom wall (y = -1.5 m), the velocity U has a noSlip condition, and the pressure p has a zeroGradient condition.
At the cylinder wall, the velocity U has a noSlip condition, and the pressure p has a zeroGradient condition.
The front (z = -1 m) and back (z = 1 m) faces are set as empty for 2D simulation.
The initial field specifies a quiescent fluid with U = (0 0 0) m/s and p = 0 Pa.
