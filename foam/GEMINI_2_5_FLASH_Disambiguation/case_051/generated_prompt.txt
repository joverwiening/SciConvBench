Simulate a one-dimensional shock tube problem.
The gas has a specific heat capacity at constant pressure (Cp) of 1005 J/kg/K.
The gas has a molar weight of 28.9 kg/kmol.
The total length of the shock tube is 1 meter.
The initial diaphragm position is at x = 0.5 meters.
The high-pressure side (x < 0.5 m) has an initial pressure of 100000 Pa.
The high-pressure side (x < 0.5 m) has an initial density of 1.0 kg/m^3.
The low-pressure side (x > 0.5 m) has an initial pressure of 10000 Pa.
The low-pressure side (x > 0.5 m) has an initial density of 0.125 kg/m^3.
The boundary condition at x = 0 m is a wall with slip velocity.
For the x = 0 m boundary, the velocity (U) has a slip condition.
For the x = 0 m boundary, the pressure (p) has a zeroGradient condition.
For the x = 0 m boundary, the temperature (T) has a zeroGradient condition.
The boundary condition at x = 1 m is a wall with slip velocity.
For the x = 1 m boundary, the velocity (U) has a slip condition.
For the x = 1 m boundary, the pressure (p) has a zeroGradient condition.
For the x = 1 m boundary, the temperature (T) has a zeroGradient condition.
The simulation must run until the primary shock wave reflects off the right end wall.
