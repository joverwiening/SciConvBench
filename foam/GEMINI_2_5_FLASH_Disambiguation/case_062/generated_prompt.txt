Perform a computational fluid dynamics simulation of water flow over a cylinder.
The fluid is water at a temperature of 20 degrees Celsius.
The cylinder has a diameter of 1 meter.
The computational domain is a rectangle extending from (-10m, -10m) to (30m, 10m) with a thickness of 0.1m.
The free-stream velocity of the water is U=(3.9 0 0) m/s.
The turbulence model used for the simulation is the k-omega SST RAS model.
The boundary conditions are defined as follows:
The inlet boundary has a uniform velocity U=(3.9 0 0) m/s, fixedValue k=5.7e-3, and fixedValue omega=3.6.
The outlet boundary has zeroGradient for U, k, and omega, and fixedValue p=0.
The cylinder surface has noSlip for U, kqRWallFunction for k, and omegaWallFunction for omega.
The top and bottom boundaries are set as symmetry.
The front and back boundaries are set as empty.
The total simulation time is 100 seconds.
The primary objective is to observe vortex shedding phenomena.
