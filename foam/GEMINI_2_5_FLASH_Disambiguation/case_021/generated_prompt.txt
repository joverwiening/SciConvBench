Simulate a 2D incompressible unsteady flow past a circular cylinder using the pimpleFoam solver.
The computational domain is a rectangle extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m, and y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m.
The z-direction extends from z = -1 m to z = 1 m, with front and back faces set to `empty` to enforce 2D flow.
A circular cylinder of diameter 2 m is centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).
The simulation starts at time = 0 s and ends at time = 0.5 s.
The time step is 0.0025 s.
Results are written every 0.05 s.
The initial fluid velocity is (0 0 0) m/s and initial pressure is 0 Pa.
The inlet boundary (x = -5 m) has a uniform velocity of U = (1 0 0) m/s.
The outlet boundary (x = 5 m) has a zeroGradient condition for velocity and a fixed pressure of p = 0 Pa.
The top boundary (y = 2.5 m) has a noSlip condition for velocity and a zeroGradient condition for pressure.
The bottom boundary (y = -1.5 m) has a noSlip condition for velocity and a zeroGradient condition for pressure.
The circular cylinder surface has a noSlip condition for velocity and a zeroGradient condition for pressure.
