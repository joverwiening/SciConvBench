The simulation will be a transient, two-dimensional, turbulent natural convection analysis of air.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction with a magnitude of 9.81 m/s^2.
The computational domain is a rectangle extending from x=0 m to x=2.0 m and from y=-1.0 m to y=2.0 m.
The entire room is initialized with air at a temperature of 300 K.
A vertical plate is located on the left wall at x=0 m, spanning from y=0 m to y=1.0 m.
The vertical plate is maintained at a constant temperature of 350 K.
The remaining portions of the left wall (x=0 m, y=-1.0 to 0 m and y=1.0 to 2.0 m) are adiabatic.
The bottom wall (y=-1.0 m, x=0 to 2.0 m) is adiabatic.
The top wall (y=2.0 m, x=0 to 2.0 m) is an open boundary with an inlet temperature of 300 K.
The right wall (x=2.0 m, y=-1.0 to 2.0 m) is an open boundary with an inlet temperature of 300 K.
The simulation will capture the development of the thermal plume over time.
