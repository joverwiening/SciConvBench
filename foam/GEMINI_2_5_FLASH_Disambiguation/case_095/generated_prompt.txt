The simulation will find the steady-state solution for a 2D buoyant cavity.
The cavity dimensions are 1 meter by 1 meter.
The solver used is buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam.
The mesh resolution is 50x50 cells.
Gravity is set to (0, -9.81, 0) m/s^2.
The reference temperature for the Boussinesq approximation is 300 K.
The initial velocity field for the domain is (0 0 0) m/s.
The initial temperature field for the domain is 300 K.
The left wall temperature is 310 K.
The right wall temperature is 290 K.
The top wall is insulated.
The bottom wall is insulated.
All walls have a noSlip velocity boundary condition.
All walls have a fixedFluxPressure pressure boundary condition.
