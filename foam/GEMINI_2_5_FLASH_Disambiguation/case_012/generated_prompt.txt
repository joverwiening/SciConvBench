The simulation models an incompressible lid-driven cavity flow using the pisoFoam solver.
The cavity is a square with dimensions 1 unit in the x-direction and 1 unit in the y-direction, and 0.1 units in the z-direction.
The computational mesh consists of 20 cells in the x-direction, 20 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The simulation runs from time 0 to 10 units with a time step of 0.005 units.
Results are output every 100 time steps.
The k-epsilon turbulence model is used.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is constant at 1e-05 m^2/s.
The initial velocity field is set to zero throughout the domain.
The initial turbulence kinetic energy (k) is set uniformly to 1.5e-4.
The initial turbulence dissipation rate (epsilon) is set uniformly to 1.4e-4.
The initial turbulent kinematic viscosity (nut) is set uniformly to 0.
For velocity boundary conditions, the top wall ('movingWall') has a uniform velocity of 1 m/s in the x-direction.
The bottom, left, and right walls ('fixedWalls') have a no-slip boundary condition, meaning zero velocity.
The front and back faces are designated as 'empty' boundary conditions for velocity.
For turbulence boundary conditions, kqRWallFunction is applied to k on all walls (front/back empty).
epsilonWallFunction is applied to epsilon on all walls (front/back empty).
nutkWallFunction is applied to nut on all walls (front/back empty).
The convection scheme for div(phi,U) is Gauss limitedLinearV 1.
The convection scheme for div(phi,k) is Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The convection scheme for div(phi,epsilon) is Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The viscous term uses the scheme div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear.
