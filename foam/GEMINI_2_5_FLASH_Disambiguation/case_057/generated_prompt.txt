Simulate turbulent flow in a 2D channel using the pisoFoam solver.
The k-epsilon turbulence model will be used.
The channel has a length of 2 meters and a height of 0.1 meters.
The fluid's kinematic viscosity is 1e-5 m^2/s.
At the inlet, the velocity (U) is a fixedValue of (1 0 0) m/s.
At the inlet, the pressure (p) has a zeroGradient boundary condition.
At the inlet, the turbulent kinetic energy (k) is a fixedValue of 3.75e-3.
At the inlet, the turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) is a fixedValue of 0.017.
At the outlet, the velocity (U) has a zeroGradient boundary condition.
At the outlet, the pressure (p) is a fixedValue of 0.
For the top and bottom walls, the velocity (U) has a noSlip boundary condition.
For the top and bottom walls, the pressure (p) has a zeroGradient boundary condition.
For the top and bottom walls, the turbulent kinetic energy (k) uses a kqRWallFunction.
For the top and bottom walls, the turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) uses an epsilonWallFunction.
For the top and bottom walls, the turbulent viscosity (nut) uses a nutkWallFunction.
