The buoyantFoam solver will be used.
Gravity will act downward in the negative y direction.
The reference state will be air-like with a density of 1.0 kg/m^3, a reference temperature of 300 K, a thermal expansion coefficient of 3.3e-3 1/K, a kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m^2/s, and a thermal diffusivity of 2.1e-5 m^2/s.
The temperature of the bottom wall will be fixed at 305 K.
The temperature of the top wall will be fixed at 295 K.
The vertical side walls will be adiabatic.
No-slip velocity conditions will be applied on all solid walls.
The flow will be initialized at rest with a uniform temperature field of 300 K.
A fixed timestep of 1s will be used, with deltaT set to 1 s.
adjustTimeStep, maxCo, and maxDeltaT will not be enabled.
The simulation will run up to 1000 s of physical time.
Results will be written every 50 steps.
In constant/physicalProperties, heRhoThermo will be used with the Boussinesq equationOfState.
molWeight will be set to 29.
rho0 will be set to 1.0.
T0 will be set to 300.
beta will be set to 3.3e-3.
Cv will be set to 714.3.
Hf will be set to 0.
mu will be set to 1.5e-5.
Pr will be set to 0.714.
The k-epsilon turbulence model will be used.
In 0/k, the internalField will be uniform 1e-5, with kqRWallFunction on walls and empty on front/back.
In 0/epsilon, the internalField will be uniform 1e-5, with epsilonWallFunction on walls and empty on front/back.
In 0/nut, the internalField will be uniform 0, with nutkWallFunction on walls and empty on front/back.
In 0/alphat, the internalField will be uniform 0, with compressible::alphatJayatillekeWallFunction on walls, Prt set to 0.85, and empty on front/back.
In 0/U, the internalField will be uniform (0 0 0), with noSlip on all solid walls and empty on front/back.
In 0/T, the internalField will be uniform 300, the bottom will have fixedValue 305, the top will have fixedValue 295, the sides will have zeroGradient, and front/back will be empty.
In 0/p, the internalField will be uniform 0, with walls calculated and front/back empty.
In 0/p_rgh, the internalField will be uniform 0, with walls fixedFluxPressure and front/back empty.
In system/fvSolution, p_rgh and (U|e|k|epsilon) solvers will have a tolerance of 1e-8 and a relTol of 0.01.
The corresponding *Final solvers will have a relTol of 0.
