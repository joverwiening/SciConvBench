Simulate a 1D flame propagation in a closed tube using the rhoReactingFoam solver, suitable for compressible reacting flows.
GEOMETRY: A long, thin 3D domain to approximate 1D: 0.5 m long, 0.01 m wide, 0.01 m high. All walls are solid.
 MESH: Use blockMesh to create a fine mesh in the axial direction and coarse in the transverse directions: 500x5x5 cells.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: All patches ('leftWall', 'rightWall', 'frontAndBack', 'topAndBottom') are of type 'wall'. For U, use noSlip. For T, use zeroGradient (adiabatic). For p, use fixedFluxPressure. For species, use zeroGradient.
INITIAL CONDITIONS: The tube is filled with a quiescent, stoichiometric hydrogen-air mixture at T=300 K and p=101325 Pa. Mass fractions: H2=0.028, O2=0.225, N2=0.747. Ignition is achieved by setting a high temperature (T=2000 K) in the first 2% of the domain length (from x=0 to x=0.01 m) using system/setFieldsDict.
COMBUSTION PROPERTIES: Use a detailed hydrogen-air reaction mechanism (e.g., a 9-species, 19-reaction mechanism). Convert the CHEMKIN files to OpenFOAM format and place in the 'constant' directory.
THERMO PROPERTIES (constant/thermophysicalProperties): Use 'hePsiThermo' with 'janaf' thermodynamics and 'perfectGas' equation of state. Set mixture to 'multiComponentMixture'.
TIME CONTROLS: Run to endTime=0.002 s to capture the initial flame propagation. Use an adjustable time step with an initial deltaT of 1e-8 s and a maximum Courant number (maxCo) of 0.4. Write results every 50 time steps.
