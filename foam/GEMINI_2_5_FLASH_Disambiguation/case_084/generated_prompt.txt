The simulation models a flame propagating through a closed tube.
The tube is 0.5 m long, 0.01 m wide, and 0.01 m high.
The tube has a square cross-section.
The tube is filled with a stoichiometric hydrogen-air mixture.
The initial temperature of the mixture is 300 K.
The initial pressure of the mixture is 101325 Pa.
Ignition is achieved by setting the temperature to 2000 K in the first 2% of the domain length (from x=0 to x=0.01 m).
The simulation runs for a total time of 0.002 s.
The mesh resolution is 500 cells along the length, 5 cells along the width, and 5 cells along the height.
Boundary conditions for velocity (U) are noSlip on all walls.
Boundary conditions for temperature (T) are zeroGradient (adiabatic) on all walls.
Boundary conditions for pressure (p) are fixedFluxPressure on all walls.
Boundary conditions for species are zeroGradient on all walls.
