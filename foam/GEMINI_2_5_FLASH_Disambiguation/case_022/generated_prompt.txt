A 2D incompressible unsteady laminar flow past a circular cylinder will be simulated using the pimpleFoam solver.
The computational domain is a rectangle extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m and y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m.
The circular cylinder has a diameter of 2 m and is centered at (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).
The simulation will run from startTime = 0 s to endTime = 0.5 s with a fixed time step deltaT = 0.0025 s.
Results will be written every 0.05 s.
The initial fluid state is quiescent, with velocity U = (0 0 0) m/s and pressure p = 0 Pa.
The flow is laminar and uses a generalisednewtonain viscosity model with a cross-power law formulation.
The cross-power law parameters are nuinf = 10, m = 0.4, and n = 3.
The inlet boundary condition at x = -5 m is a fixed velocity U = (1 0 0) m/s.
The outlet boundary condition at x = 5 m is zeroGradient for velocity U and a fixed pressure p = 0 Pa.
The cylinder surface boundary condition is noSlip for velocity U and zeroGradient for pressure p.
The top wall (y = 2.5 m) and bottom wall (y = -1.5 m) boundary conditions are noSlip for velocity U and zeroGradient for pressure p.
The front and back faces of the domain are set to an empty boundary condition, representing a 2D simulation.
