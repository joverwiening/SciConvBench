Model a 2D incompressible turbulent flow in a rectangular channel.
The channel is 1.0 m long and 0.1 m high.
A zero-thickness porous screen is located at x=0.5 m.
The porous screen causes a pressure drop modeled with a Darcy coefficient of 1e7 and an Inertial coefficient of 0.
The inlet velocity is a fixed uniform value of (5 0 0) m/s.
The outlet velocity boundary condition is zeroGradient.
The outlet pressure boundary condition is a fixed uniform value of 0 Pa.
The outlet boundary conditions for k, omega, and nut are zeroGradient.
The top and bottom walls have a no-slip velocity boundary condition.
