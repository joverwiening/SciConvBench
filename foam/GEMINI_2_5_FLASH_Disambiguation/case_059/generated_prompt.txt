The simulation will be a steady-state developing pipe flow case using simpleFoam.
The pipe has an inner diameter of 0.02 meters and a length of 1 meter.
The fluid has a kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m^2/s.
The inlet velocity is (0.1 0 0) m/s.
The inlet pressure boundary condition is zeroGradient.
The outlet velocity boundary condition is zeroGradient.
The outlet pressure boundary condition is fixedValue uniform 0 Pa.
The pipe wall velocity boundary condition is noSlip.
The pipe wall pressure boundary condition is zeroGradient.
