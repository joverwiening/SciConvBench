The simulation shall use air as the working gas (assuming perfect gas behavior).
The inlet flow conditions are Mach 2.0, temperature 300 K, and pressure 101.325 kPa.
The wedge deflection angle is 10 degrees.
The simulation shall calculate and output the following parameters:
The oblique shock wave angle from the horizontal.
The post-shock Mach number.
The post-shock pressure, temperature, and density ratios across the shock.
The post-shock flow properties (pressure, temperature, density, and velocity).
The simulation results shall be presented in tabular format with SI units.
The simulation shall use the oblique shock relations derived from the conservation equations.
The simulation shall validate that the input conditions do not exceed the maximum deflection angle for the given Mach number.
