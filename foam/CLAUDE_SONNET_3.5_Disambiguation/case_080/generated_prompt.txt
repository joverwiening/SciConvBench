The fluidized bed simulation shall use an Euler-Euler model with the following specifications:
The computational domain dimensions are 0.1 m width x 0.5 m height x 0.1 m depth.
The solid particles have a diameter of 200 microns.
The particle density is 2500 kg/m³.
Air is used as the continuous gas phase.
The gas inlet velocity (superficial) is 0.2 m/s at the bottom.
The initial bed height is 0.2 m with a solid volume fraction of 0.55.
The simulation shall run for 5 seconds of physical time.
The time step size shall be adaptive between 1e-4 and 5e-4 seconds.
The maximum Courant number (maxCo) shall be set to 0.3.
The adjustTimeStep feature shall be enabled for automatic time step adjustment.
The initial condition shall have particles packed in the bottom 0.2 m of the domain.
The remaining domain height shall be initialized with pure gas phase.
