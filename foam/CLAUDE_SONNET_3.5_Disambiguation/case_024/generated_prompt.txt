The domain extends from x = -5 m to x = 5 m, y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m, and z = -1 to 1 m.
A circular cylinder of diameter 2 m is centered at the origin (0, 0, 0).
The simulation runs from t = 0 s to t = 0.5 s with a time step of 0.0025 s.
Results are written every 0.05 s.
The fluid has a kinematic viscosity of 0.01 m²/s.
Initial conditions are U = (0 0 0) m/s and p = 0.
Inlet boundary conditions (left): U = (1 0 0) m/s, p zeroGradient.
Outlet boundary conditions (right): U zeroGradient, p = 0.
Top and bottom boundary conditions: U noSlip, p zeroGradient.
Cylinder surface boundary conditions: U noSlip, p zeroGradient.
