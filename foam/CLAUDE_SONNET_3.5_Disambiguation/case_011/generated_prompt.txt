The simulation is a 2D incompressible lid-driven cavity flow with the following specifications:
The domain consists of a 20x20 grid in x and y directions with 1 cell in z-direction.
The top wall (movingWall) moves with a uniform velocity of 1 m/s in x-direction.
The fixedWalls have a no-slip boundary condition (velocity = 0).
Front and back faces are set as empty boundary conditions.
The kinematic viscosity (nu) is constant at 1e-05 m^2/s.
The simulation runs from time 0 to 10 seconds.
The time step size (deltaT) is set to 0.005 seconds.
Results are written every 100 time steps.
The initial velocity field is set to zero throughout the domain.
The solver used is pisoFoam.
The numerical schemes are: div(phi,U) Gauss limitedLinearV 1.
The numerical schemes are: div(phi,k) Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The numerical schemes are: div(phi,epsilon) Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The viscous term uses div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear.
The solver tolerances are set to standard values: 1e-6 for pressure and 1e-5 for velocity.
