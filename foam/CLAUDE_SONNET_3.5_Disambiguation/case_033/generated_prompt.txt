The simulation shall model natural convection from a vertical hot plate with these specifications:
Domain is 2D with dimensions 2.0 m width x 3.0 m height.
Hot plate is located on left wall between y=1.0 m and y=2.0 m, with 0.01 m thickness.
Hot plate temperature is fixed at 350 K.
Initial and ambient air temperature is 300 K.
Use kOmegaSST turbulence model with wall functions.
Left wall boundary (except plate) is no-slip wall at 300 K.
Hot plate boundary is no-slip wall at 350 K.
Top and right boundaries are open boundaries with:
Velocity uses pressureInletOutletVelocity with inletValue (0 0 0).
Pressure (p_rgh) uses totalPressure with p0 uniform 0.
Temperature uses inletOutlet condition with 300 K inlet and initial values.
Turbulence quantities (k, omega) use inletOutlet with small inlet/initial values.
Simulation must capture development of the thermal plume in turbulent flow regime.
Gravity acts in negative y-direction with g = -9.81 m/s².
Air properties are temperature-dependent using incompressible Boussinesq approximation.
