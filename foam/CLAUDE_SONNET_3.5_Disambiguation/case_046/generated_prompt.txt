The simulation domain is a 2D square cavity with sides of 0.1 meters.
The fluid is incompressible.
The top wall moves to the right with a constant velocity of 1 m/s.
All other walls are stationary with no-slip boundary conditions.
The fluid has a kinematic viscosity of 0.001 m²/s.
The Reynolds number of the flow is 100.
The simulation should solve the incompressible Navier-Stokes equations.
Initial conditions are fluid at rest (zero velocity everywhere except the top wall).
