The simulation domain is 0.5 m x 0.1 m x 0.01 m with empty front/back boundary conditions for 2D treatment.
The porous aluminum foam block measures 0.1 m x 0.05 m x 0.01 m.
The aluminum foam has effective properties: density 500 kg/m³, specific heat 900 J/kg/K, and thermal conductivity 10 W/m/K.
The working fluid is air, modeled as a compressible perfect gas using heRhoThermo and perfectGas.
The inlet fluid velocity is 1 m/s in the positive x-direction (1 0 0).
The inlet fluid temperature is 400 K.
The initial temperature of the porous block is 300 K.
The outlet boundary uses fixed p_rgh = 0, zero gradient velocity, and inletOutlet temperature (inletValue 300K, value 300K).
External fluid walls use no-slip velocity condition, zero gradient temperature, and fixedFluxPressure for p_rgh.
Solid external walls use zero gradient (adiabatic) temperature condition.
The fluid-solid interface uses temperature-coupled baffle boundary conditions on both sides.
The simulation represents a conjugate heat transfer problem between the hot air flow and the porous aluminum block.
