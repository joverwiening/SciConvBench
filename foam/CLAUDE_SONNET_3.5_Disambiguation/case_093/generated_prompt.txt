The domain is 2D with dimensions 2m wide x 4m high.
The heat source is located at the floor center with width 0.2m and temperature 400K.
The ambient temperature is 300K.
Use RANS k-epsilon turbulence model with simulationType RAS and RASModel kEpsilon settings.
Initial velocity condition is U=(0 0 0) throughout the domain.
Top boundary serves as inlet with specified temperature of 300K.
Side wall boundary conditions: slip velocity, zeroGradient for T, k, epsilon, and p_rgh.
Floor boundary conditions: 400K temperature at central 0.2m section, ambient temperature elsewhere.
Simulate for 100 seconds total physical time.
Write results at 5-second intervals.
Initial temperature field is 300K everywhere except the heat source.
The simulation is buoyancy-driven with gravity effects included.
