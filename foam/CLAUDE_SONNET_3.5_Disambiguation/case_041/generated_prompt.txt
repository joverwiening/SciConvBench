The simulation domain is a 1m x 1m square cavity.
The working fluid is air.
The left wall temperature is fixed at 303 K.
The right wall temperature is fixed at 293 K.
The initial temperature of the air is 293 K.
The top and bottom walls are insulated with zero temperature gradient.
No-slip velocity boundary conditions are applied to all walls.
Fixed flux pressure boundary condition is applied to top and bottom walls.
The simulation runs for 2000 seconds.
The time step size (deltaT) is 1 second.
The problem models natural convection driven by buoyancy forces.
The flow is two-dimensional.
