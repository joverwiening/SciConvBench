The simulation domain is a rectangular box of 0.40 m × 0.40 m × 0.10 m (Lx × Ly × H).
The working fluid is water.
The Rayleigh number is approximately 1e8.
The bottom plate temperature is fixed at 300.0 K.
The top plate temperature is fixed at 293.2 K.
The lateral boundaries (xMin/xMax and yMin/yMax) are cyclic (periodic).
The mesh consists of 80 × 80 × 40 uniform cells using blockMesh.
Initial velocity field is zero everywhere: U = (0 0 0) m/s.
Initial temperature field is a linear interpolation between plates (or uniform 296.6 K).
A warm perturbation is added in a box region (0.19,0.19,0.00) to (0.21,0.21,0.01) m with temperature T0+0.05 K.
The simulation should capture 3D turbulent Rayleigh-Benard convection.
All walls except the cyclic boundaries are no-slip for velocity.
