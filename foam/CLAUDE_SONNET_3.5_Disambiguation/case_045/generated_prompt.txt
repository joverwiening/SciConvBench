The simulation domain is a 1x1 meter square box discretized into 50x50 uniform cells.
The working fluid has properties: kinematic viscosity = 1e-5 m²/s, Prandtl number = 0.71, and thermal expansion coefficient = 3.4e-3 1/K.
The bottom wall temperature is fixed at 300K.
The top wall temperature is fixed at 290K.
The simulation runs for 200 seconds.
The initial time step is 0.05 seconds.
Enable automatic time step adjustment with maximum Courant number of 0.8.
The side walls are adiabatic.
The walls are no-slip boundaries for velocity.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction with g = 9.81 m/s².
