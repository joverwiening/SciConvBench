The simulation shall use pimpleFoam solver for a 2D backward-facing step channel flow.
The channel dimensions are 1.0 m length, varying height from 0.02 m to 0.04 m, and 0.01 m thickness.
A backward-facing step of height 0.02 m is located at x = 0.2 m from the inlet.
The inlet velocity is uniform at 0.5 m/s.
The working fluid has kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The turbulence model is RAS k-epsilon with wall functions.
The inlet turbulent properties are k = 9.375e-4 m^2/s^2 and epsilon = 5.5e-3 m^2/s^3.
The outlet uses fixedValue 0 for pressure and zeroGradient for velocity, k, and epsilon.
The base mesh resolution is 200 cells in length and 80 cells in height.
The mesh shall be refined by 2 levels near the step corner and in the reattachment region.
The simulation shall be run until convergence with standard PIMPLE algorithm settings.
Wall boundaries shall use standard wall functions for k-epsilon turbulence modeling.
