The simulation shall use a NACA0012 airfoil at 0 degrees angle of attack.
The freestream Mach number shall be 0.85.
The flow shall be modeled as fully turbulent.
The Reynolds number shall be 6.5 million, typical for transonic airfoil analysis.
The computational domain shall extend to 20 chord lengths from the airfoil in all directions.
The simulation shall use a compressible RANS solver with an appropriate turbulence model.
The solution shall be considered converged when normalized residuals reach 1e-6.
The simulation shall capture and resolve shock waves in the flow field.
The mesh shall be refined in regions of expected shock waves and boundary layer.
The y+ value at the first cell shall be maintained below 1 for proper boundary layer resolution.
