The computational domain is rectangular with width 0.05 m and height 0.2 m.
The domain is initially filled with water (density = 1000 kg/m³, kinematic viscosity = 1e-6 m²/s).
The air properties are: density = 1.2 kg/m³, kinematic viscosity = 1.5e-5 m²/s.
A single circular air bubble of diameter 0.01 m is initialized.
The bubble center is initially positioned at x = 0.025 m, y = 0.025 m.
The simulation is two-dimensional.
Use the Volume of Fluid (VOF) method to track the interface between air and water.
The surrounding water is initially quiescent (zero velocity).
Track the bubble's rise due to buoyancy effects.
Apply no-slip boundary conditions at the domain walls.
