The simulation domain is 2.0 m wide by 2.0 m high in 2D with 0.01 m thickness.
A hot strip source is located on the floor, centered at x = 1.0 m with width 0.2 m (x from 0.9 to 1.1 m).
The hot strip temperature is maintained at 400 K.
The ambient room temperature is 300 K.
The simulation is turbulent.
Floor boundary is no-slip with split thermal conditions: 400 K at hot strip, 300 K elsewhere.
Side walls are no-slip with fixed temperature of 300 K.
Top boundary is an open outlet using pressureInletOutletVelocity and totalPressure conditions.
Front and back boundaries are empty for 2D simulation.
The flow is driven by natural convection (buoyancy).
