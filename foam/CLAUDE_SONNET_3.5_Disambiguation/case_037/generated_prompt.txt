The simulation shall model compressible air flow through a porous filter in a duct with the following specifications:
The domain is a 2D duct with dimensions 0.5m length x 0.1m height x 0.01m width.
The porous filter zone is located from x=0.2m to x=0.25m (0.05m thickness).
The inlet flow conditions are Mach 2 with pressure 101325 Pa and temperature 300 K.
The outlet uses waveTransmissive boundary condition with p=101325 Pa and T=300 K.
The porous media is defined by Darcy-Forchheimer coefficients d=(5e7 5e7 0) and f=(500 500 0).
The turbulence model is k-omega SST.
The wall boundary conditions use noSlip for velocity, zeroGradient for temperature, kqRWallFunction for k, and omegaWallFunction for omega.
Air properties are R=287 J/kg-K, Cp=1005 J/kg-K, molecular weight=28.9 g/mol.
The viscosity uses the Sutherland transport model.
The simulation must capture the shock wave that forms due to the supersonic inlet conditions.
