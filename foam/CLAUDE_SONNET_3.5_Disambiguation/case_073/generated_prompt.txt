The pipe geometry is 2 meters long with a diameter of 0.04 meters.
The working fluid is air with kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
Use k-omega SST turbulence model for the simulation.
The inlet boundary conditions are: velocity fixed at uniform (10 0 0) m/s.
The inlet turbulence properties are: k = fixedValue (calculated from 3% intensity), omega = fixedValue (calculated from mixing length).
The outlet boundary conditions are: pressure fixed at 0, zeroGradient for velocity, k, and omega.
The wall boundary conditions are: no-slip velocity condition, kqRWallFunction for k, omegaWallFunction for omega.
The simulation should be run to steady state.
All other fluid properties should use standard values for air at ambient conditions.
