The domain is a cube from (0,0,0) to (1,1,1).
The mesh consists of 32x32x32 uniform cells.
A rectangular obstacle exists from (0.375 0.375 0) to (0.625 0.625 0.25).
Gravity is set to (0 0 -9.81) m/s^2.
The top face (z=1) is set as an open atmosphere boundary.
All other faces are set as walls.
Two phases are modeled using VOF method: water and air.
Water properties: density = 1000 kg/m^3, kinematic viscosity = 1.0e-6 m^2/s.
Air properties: density = 1 kg/m^3, kinematic viscosity = 1.48e-5 m^2/s.
Surface tension coefficient is 0.07 N/m.
Initial water column is a rectangular prism from (0,0,0) to (0.6,0.1875,0.75) with alpha.water = 1.
Outside the water column, alpha.water = 0.
The simulation runs for 0.1 seconds.
The time step size is fixed at 1e-3 seconds.
