The simulation shall use pimpleFoam solver in OpenFOAM.
The computational domain dimensions are 2.2m x 0.41m x 0.01m.
The cylinder diameter is 0.1m.
The cylinder is centered at coordinates (0.2, 0.2, 0) meters.
The inlet velocity is uniform at 1 m/s.
The kinematic viscosity is 1e-3 m^2/s.
The mesh shall have a base size of 0.005m at the cylinder surface.
The mesh shall gradually expand away from the cylinder with expansion ratio not exceeding 1.2.
The simulation shall run until flow statistics are converged, minimum 20 flow-through times.
The boundary conditions shall be: inlet-velocity fixed at 1 m/s, outlet-pressure fixed at 0, top/bottom-walls as slip conditions.
The cylinder surface shall have no-slip boundary condition.
The front and back shall use empty boundary conditions for 2D simulation.
The initial conditions shall be uniform velocity of 1 m/s in x-direction.
