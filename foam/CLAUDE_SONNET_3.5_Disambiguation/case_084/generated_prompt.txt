The simulation domain is a rectangular tube of 0.5 m length and 0.01 m x 0.01 m cross-section.
The computational mesh consists of 500x5x5 cells with resolution of 1 mm axially and 2 mm transversely.
The tube contains a stoichiometric hydrogen-air mixture at 300 K initial temperature and 1 atm (101325 Pa) initial pressure.
The simulation runs for 2 milliseconds (0.002 seconds).
Wall boundary conditions are: adiabatic temperature (zeroGradient), noSlip velocity, fixedFluxPressure, and zeroGradient species.
Ignition is initiated by setting temperature to 2000 K in the first 0.01 m (2%) of the tube length.
The simulation uses a structured mesh with uniform grid spacing.
All walls (leftWall, rightWall, frontAndBack, topAndBottom) use identical boundary conditions.
The domain is initialized as a closed system with no inflow or outflow boundaries.
Species transport and chemical reactions are modeled for the hydrogen-air combustion system.
