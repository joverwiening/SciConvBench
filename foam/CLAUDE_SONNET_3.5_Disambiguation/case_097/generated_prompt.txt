The simulation models natural convection in a 1m × 1m square cavity.
The left wall is maintained at 310 K.
The right wall is maintained at 290 K.
The working fluid is air with standard properties.
The top and bottom walls are adiabatic.
The simulation runs for 500 seconds.
The time step is variable with an initial value of 0.01 seconds.
The maximum Courant number is limited to 0.7.
The mesh consists of 50 × 50 uniform cells.
No-slip boundary conditions apply at all walls.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction with g = 9.81 m/s².
The simulation uses the Boussinesq approximation for buoyancy.
The flow is assumed to be laminar.
Initial conditions are fluid at rest at 300 K.
