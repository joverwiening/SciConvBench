The simulation setup is a 2D incompressible flow case with the following specifications:
The geometry consists of a channel with inlet height 0.10m expanding to 0.20m over 1.0m length, creating a 5.71 degree wedge angle.
The inlet velocity is uniform at 5 m/s.
The fluid kinematic viscosity is 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
Use the kOmegaSST turbulence model with wall functions.
Wall boundary conditions use no-slip for velocity, zeroGradient for pressure, and standard wall functions for turbulence quantities.
Outlet boundary conditions are fixedValue 0 for pressure and zeroGradient for velocity.
Use simpleFoam solver for the incompressible, steady-state simulation.
The simulation will capture the flow separation and reattachment over the expanding section.
