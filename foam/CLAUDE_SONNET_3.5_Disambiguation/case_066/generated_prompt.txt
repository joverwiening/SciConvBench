The domain is a 2D channel with length 0.5 m and height 0.1 m.
The porous zone is located between x=0.2 m and x=0.3 m.
The simulation is steady-state, incompressible, laminar flow.
The fluid is water with kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m²/s.
The inlet velocity is fixed at 1 m/s.
The Darcy-Forchheimer coefficients are d = (1e8 1e8 1e10) and f = (0 0 0).
The boundary conditions for velocity (U) are: inlet - fixedValue uniform (1 0 0), outlet - zeroGradient, top/bottom walls - noSlip.
The boundary conditions for pressure (p) are: inlet - zeroGradient, outlet - fixedValue uniform 0, top/bottom walls - zeroGradient.
The flow is isothermal with no heat transfer considered.
The simulation uses single-phase flow physics.
The flow is assumed to be two-dimensional with no variation in the z-direction.
