The simulation shall use an Euler-Euler two-phase approach for a bubble column.
The computational domain dimensions are 0.2 m × 1.0 m × 0.2 m (width × height × width).
The operating temperature is 25 degrees Celsius (standard room temperature assumption).
The primary phase is water (continuous phase).
The secondary phase is air (dispersed phase).
Air bubbles shall have a constant diameter of 2 mm.
The air inlet is located at the bottom of the column.
The air injection velocity is 0.2 m/s in the upward direction (0, 0.2, 0).
The simulation duration is 20 seconds.
Standard atmospheric pressure (101325 Pa) shall be used at the top boundary.
The no-slip condition shall be applied at the walls for the liquid phase.
The free-slip condition shall be applied at the walls for the gas phase.
Gravity effects shall be included (-9.81 m/s² in the vertical direction).
Standard drag force correlations for bubble columns shall be implemented.
