The domain is rectangular extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m, y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m, and z = -1 m to 1 m.
A circular cylinder of diameter 2 m is centered at the origin (0, 0, 0).
The simulation uses pimpleFoam solver for incompressible unsteady flow.
The simulation runs from t = 0 s to t = 0.5 s with deltaT = 0.0025 s.
Results are written every 0.05 s.
The initial conditions are U = (0 0 0) m/s and p = 0.
The inlet velocity at the left boundary is U = (1 0 0) m/s.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 0.01 m²/s.
The outlet boundary conditions are: U = zeroGradient, p = 0.
The top and bottom wall boundary conditions are: U = noSlip, p = zeroGradient.
The front and back boundaries are empty for 2D simulation.
The cylinder surface boundary conditions are: U = noSlip, p = zeroGradient.
