The domain is rectangular extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m, y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m, and z = -1 to 1 m.
A circular cylinder of diameter 2 m is centered at the origin (0, 0, 0).
The simulation uses pimpleFoam solver for incompressible flow.
The simulation runs from t = 0 to t = 0.5 seconds.
The time step size is 0.0025 seconds.
Results are written every 0.05 seconds.
Boundary conditions for inlet (left): U = (1 0 0) m/s.
Boundary conditions for outlet (right): U zeroGradient, p = 0.
Boundary conditions for top and bottom walls: U noSlip, p zeroGradient.
Boundary conditions for cylinder wall: U noSlip, p zeroGradient.
Back and front faces are empty (2-D simulation).
Initial conditions: U = (0 0 0) m/s, p = 0.
The flow is laminar using generalisedNewtonian model.
The viscosity model is crosspowerlaw with parameters: nuinf = 10, m = 0.4, n = 3.
The fluid viscosity is 0.01 m²/s.
