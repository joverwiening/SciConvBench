The simulation uses a 20x20x1 grid for a 2D lid-driven cavity flow.
The domain has dimensions of 20 cells in x-direction, 20 cells in y-direction, and 1 cell in z-direction.
The top wall (movingWall) moves with a uniform velocity of 1 m/s in x-direction.
The fixedWalls have a no-slip boundary condition (velocity = 0).
Front and back faces are set as empty boundary condition.
The simulation runs from t = 0 to t = 10 with a time step of 0.005.
Results are written every 100 time steps.
Kinematic viscosity (nu) is constant at 1e-05 m²/s.
The turbulence model is k-epsilon.
Turbulent kinetic energy (k) is initialized uniformly at 1.5e-4 with kqRWallFunction on all walls.
Turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) is initialized uniformly at 1.4e-4 with epsilonWallFunction on all walls.
Turbulent viscosity (nut) is initialized at 0 with nutkWallFunction on all walls.
The solver is pisoFoam.
Initial velocity field is set to zero throughout the domain.
Pressure boundary conditions are set to zeroGradient on all walls.
Convection schemes are: div(phi,U) Gauss limitedLinearV 1.
Convection schemes are: div(phi,k) Gauss limitedLinear 1.
Convection schemes are: div(phi,epsilon) Gauss limitedLinear 1.
Viscous term uses div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear scheme.
