The domain is a 2D channel 0.5m long and 0.1m high with a forward-facing step of height 0.02m.
The working fluid is a perfect gas with γ = 1.4 using Sutherland's law for viscosity.
Inlet conditions: Mach 3 flow with U = (1020.5 0 0) m/s, T = 293 K, k = 3.8, omega = 4.5e5, zeroGradient for pressure.
Outlet conditions: waveTransmissive for pressure, zeroGradient for velocity, temperature, k, and omega.
Top wall uses slip condition for all fields.
Bottom wall (including step) uses noSlip for velocity, zeroGradient for pressure, fixed temperature of 293K, with kqRWallFunction, omegaWallFunction, and nutkWallFunction.
The simulation should capture and visualize the shock wave structure formed by the supersonic flow over the forward-facing step.
Use k-omega turbulence model as indicated by the boundary conditions.
