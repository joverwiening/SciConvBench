The furnace geometry is 2D with dimensions of 2 m height and 0.5 m width.
The inlet hot air temperature is 400 K.
The coal particle mass flow rate is 0.1 kg/s.
The coal particles have a mean diameter of 100 micrometers.
The inlet air velocity vector is (0 20 0) m/s.
The primary air/coal inlet is located at the bottom center of the furnace.
The coal particles are injected upward with velocity vector (0 20 0) m/s.
The furnace walls have a fixed temperature of 1200 K.
The walls have a noSlip boundary condition.
The coal type is high-volatile bituminous coal.
The outlet boundary condition is totalPressure with p0=101325 Pa.
The simulation must include radiation heat transfer effects.
The problem is solved in a 2D domain.
