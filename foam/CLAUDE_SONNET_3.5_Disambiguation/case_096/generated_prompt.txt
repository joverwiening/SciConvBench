The simulation domain is a 2D square cavity with dimensions 0.1 m x 0.1 m.
The working fluid is air (standard atmospheric conditions).
The bottom wall is maintained at 294.0 K.
The top wall is maintained at 293.0 K.
The side walls are adiabatic.
The front and back faces use empty boundary conditions for 2D simulation.
The initial temperature field is uniform at 293.5 K.
A temperature perturbation of 293.52 K is applied in a region bounded by points (0.045 0.00 -1) to (0.055 0.01 1).
The simulation runs until time = 5000 seconds with deltaT = 1 second.
The simulation uses steady-state SIMPLE algorithm.
Convergence is determined by residual controls with standard tolerances (1e-4 for pressure, 1e-6 for temperature and velocity).
The simulation uses buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam solver.
Gravity is set to -9.81 m/s² in the y-direction.
The Boussinesq approximation is used for buoyancy effects.
