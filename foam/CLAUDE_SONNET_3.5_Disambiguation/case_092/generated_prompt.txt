The domain is 0.4 meters wide and 0.1 meters high.
The bottom wall temperature is fixed at 305 K.
The top wall temperature is fixed at 295 K.
The left and right walls use no-slip velocity conditions, zero gradient temperature conditions, and fixed flux pressure conditions.
The kinematic viscosity is 1.5e-5 m²/s.
The thermal diffusivity is 2.11e-5 m²/s (derived from Pr = 0.71 and nu = 1.5e-5 m²/s).
The initial temperature field is uniform at 300 K.
A warm perturbation of 300.05 K is initialized in a box between x=0.19-0.21 m and y=0-0.01 m.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction.
The fluid is treated as incompressible with the Boussinesq approximation for buoyancy.
The simulation should run until a steady-state convection pattern develops.
