Turbulent Buoyant Cavity Simulation Specification:

Simulation Software: OpenFOAM with buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam solver.
Turbulence Model: k-epsilon model.
Rayleigh Number: 1e6.

Boundary Conditions:
Left Wall (Hot): Fixed temperature at 303 K.
Right Wall (Cold): Fixed temperature at 293 K.
Top and Bottom Walls: Zero gradient temperature.
All Walls: No-slip velocity condition.
Wall Treatment: Turbulence wall functions applied for k, epsilon, and nut.

Simulation Type: Buoyancy-driven turbulent flow in a cavity.
