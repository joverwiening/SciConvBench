Q: What specific computational fluid dynamics (CFD) software or platform will you be using for this simulation?
A: OpenFOAM. Specifically, the solver is buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam, which is part of the OpenFOAM framework.

Q: What are the specific boundary conditions you want to apply to the cavity walls (temperature, heat flux, etc.)?
A: For the cavity walls:
- Hot wall (left): Fixed temperature of 303 K
- Cold wall (right): Fixed temperature of 293 K
- Top and bottom walls: Zero gradient temperature
- All walls: No-slip velocity condition
- Turbulence wall functions will be applied for k, epsilon, and nut
