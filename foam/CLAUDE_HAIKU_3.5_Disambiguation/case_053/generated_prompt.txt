Model a de Laval nozzle with the following specifications:

Fluid Properties:
The nozzle will use air as the working fluid with a perfect gas model.
Specific heat ratio (γ) is 1.4.

Initial Conditions:
Inlet total pressure is 5 bar.
Inlet total temperature is 600 K.
Back pressure is 3.5e5 Pa.

Simulation Parameters:
Use OpenFOAM with the `rhoCentralFoam` density-based solver.
Target Mach number range at nozzle exit is between 1.5 and 2.5.
Objective is to observe a normal shock in the diverging section of the nozzle.

Simulation Goal:
Analyze the flow characteristics and shock formation in the de Laval nozzle under specified conditions.
