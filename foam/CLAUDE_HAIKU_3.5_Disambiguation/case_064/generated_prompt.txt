Perform a Large Eddy Simulation (LES) of flow over periodic hills with the following specifications:
Reynolds number based on hill height shall be Re_H = 10595.
Total computational mesh shall contain approximately 1.5 million cells.
Near-wall mesh resolution shall maintain y+ values between 1-2 on the hill surface.
Mesh generation shall use snappyHexMesh with an STL file of the periodic hill surface.
Initial base mesh shall be created using blockMesh.
Mesh refinement strategy shall focus on resolving near-wall flow characteristics.
Simulation domain shall represent periodic boundary conditions in the flow direction.
Turbulence modeling approach shall use Large Eddy Simulation (LES) methodology.
