The computational fluid dynamics (CFD) simulation specification is as follows:

Domain Geometry:
Streamwise direction extends from x = -5 m to x = 5 m.
Cross-stream direction extends from y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m.
Vertical direction extends from z = -1 m to 1 m.
Cylinder diameter is 2 m, centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).

Boundary Conditions:
Inlet (left boundary): Velocity U = (1 0 0) m/s.
Outlet (right boundary): Velocity zeroGradient, pressure p = 0 (reference).
Top and bottom walls: Velocity noSlip, pressure zeroGradient.
Cylinder wall: Velocity noSlip, pressure zeroGradient.
Default back/front faces: Empty (2-D simulation).

Solver and Simulation Parameters:
Solver: pimpleFoam for incompressible unsteady flow.
Start time: 0 s.
End time: 0.5 s.
Time step (deltaT): 0.0025 s.
Results written every 0.05 s.

Initial Conditions:
Initial velocity field: U = (0 0 0) m/s.
Initial pressure: p = 0.

Fluid Properties:
Flow regime: Laminar.
Viscosity model: Generalised Newtonian (Cross-power law).
Kinematic viscosity: 0.01 m²/s.
Fluid density: 1000 kg/m³ (water at room temperature).
Cross-power law parameters:
- nuinf = 10
- m = 0.4
- n = 3
