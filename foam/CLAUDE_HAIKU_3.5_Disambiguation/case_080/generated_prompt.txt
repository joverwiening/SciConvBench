The simulation will model a fluidized bed using an Euler-Euler multiphase approach.
The gas will enter from the bottom inlet with a superficial velocity of 0.2 m/s.
The gas velocity is specified to be above the minimum fluidization velocity.
The simulation will use a two-phase model with gas and solid particle phases.
Boundary conditions will be set at the bottom inlet to specify the gas flow rate.
The goal is to observe particle lifting and fluidization behavior under the specified gas flow conditions.
