The simulation will use pimpleFoam solver for incompressible laminar flow.
Simulation time parameters are:
Start time is 0 s.
End time is 0.5 s.
Time step (deltaT) is 0.0025 s.
Results will be written every 0.05 s.
Fluid viscosity model is generalized Newtonian using cross power law.
Cross power law viscosity model parameters are:
Infinite shear rate viscosity (nuinf) is 10.
Power law index (m) is 0.4.
Power law exponent (n) is 3.
Reference temperature is 293.15 K (20°C).
Fluid density will be assumed as 1000 kg/m³ (water at standard conditions).
