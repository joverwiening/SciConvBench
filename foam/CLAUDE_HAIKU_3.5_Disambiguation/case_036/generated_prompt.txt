Simulate incompressible water flow through a porous block inside a channel with the following specifications:

Porous block width is 0.01m.

Boundary Conditions:
Inlet velocity is fixed at 0.1 m/s in x-direction.
Inlet velocity vector is (0.1 0 0) m/s.
Outlet pressure is fixed at 0 Pa.

Simulation Characteristics:
Flow is incompressible.
Flow is steady-state.
Simulation will model water flow through the porous block within the channel.
