Simulate 2D turbulent flow over a circular cylinder.
Use a Reynolds number of 3900.
Cylinder diameter is 1 meter.
Computational domain is a rectangle from (-10D, -10D) to (30D, 10D).
Cylinder is centered at (0,0,0).
Domain thickness is 0.1D with one cell.
Use k-omega SST RAS turbulence model.
Inlet boundary condition:
Velocity vector is (3.9, 0, 0) m/s.
Turbulence at inlet: k = 5.77e-5 m²/s², omega = 3.6 s⁻¹.
Outlet boundary condition: Zero gradient for velocity, k, and omega. Pressure fixed at 0 Pa.
Side walls (top and bottom): Apply symmetry boundary condition.
Fluid is water.
