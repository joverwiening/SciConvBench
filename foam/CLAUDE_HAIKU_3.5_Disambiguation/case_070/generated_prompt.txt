Model a flow through a thin porous screen in a channel with the following specifications:

Fluid: Air at room temperature
Inlet Velocity: 5 m/s
Channel Height: 0.1 m
Flow Characteristics: Incompressible and turbulent
Reynolds Number: Approximately 5000
Screen Porosity: 60% open area
Screen Material: Stainless steel
Screen Surface Roughness: 1-2 μm
Kinematic Viscosity: 1e-5 m²/s

Simulation Objectives:
Analyze the pressure drop caused by the screen in the channel.
Characterize the turbulent flow behavior through the porous medium.
