Simulation Configuration for 2D Flow Past Circular Cylinder:

Computational Domain Geometry:
The domain extends from x = -5 to x = 5 m in the streamwise direction.
The domain spans from y = -1.5 to y = 2.5 m in the cross-stream direction.
The z-direction is 1 m thick from z = -1 to 1 m.
A circular cylinder with 2 m diameter is centered at (0, 0, 0).

Fluid Properties:
Kinematic viscosity is 0.01 m^2/s.
Initial fluid state is quiescent: U = (0 0 0) m/s, p = 0 Pa.

Simulation Parameters:
The solver is pimpleFoam for incompressible unsteady flow.
Simulation starts at 0 s and ends at 0.5 s. 
Time step size is 0.0025 s.
Results are written every 0.05 s.

Boundary Conditions:
Inlet (left): U = (1 0 0) m/s
Outlet (right): U zeroGradient, p = 0 Pa (reference pressure)
Top and bottom walls: U noSlip, p zeroGradient
Cylinder wall: U noSlip, p zeroGradient
Default back/front faces: empty (2-D simulation)

Mesh Configuration:
Mesh: structured mesh
Mesh refinement: Near wall
Number of cells around cylinder circumference: 20-40
Near-wall Resolution:
Target y+ less than 1 to resolve viscous sublayer
Typical cell size: 0.05-0.1 times cylinder diameter
Mesh aspect ratio: 1:4 to 1:10 near the cylinder
