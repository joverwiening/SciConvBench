The computational domain is a cube from (0,0,0) to (1,1,1) with a uniform mesh resolution of 32 x 32 x 32 grid points.
The domain contains a rectangular obstacle from (0.375, 0.375, 0) to (0.625, 0.625, 0.25).
Gravity is set to g = (0, 0, -9.81) m/s^2.
The top face (z=1) is defined as an open atmosphere patch.
The remaining five faces are defined as wall boundaries.
Two-phase flow is modeled using Volume of Fluid (VOF) method with:
Water phase properties: density = 1000 kg/m^3, viscosity = 1.0e-6 m^2/s
Air phase properties: density = 1 kg/m^3, viscosity = 1.48e-5 m^2/s
Surface tension coefficient = 0.07 N/m
Initial water volume fraction (alpha.water) is 1 inside the region (0, 0, 0) to (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75).
Initial water volume fraction is 0 elsewhere in the domain.
