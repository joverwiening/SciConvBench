Simulate a 2D lid-driven cavity flow with the following specifications:

Computational Domain:
- Size: 0.1m x 0.1m (length and width)
- Dimensionality: 2D

Wall Velocities:
- Top wall ('movingWall') velocity: (1 0 0) m/s (1 m/s in x-direction)
- Bottom, left, and right walls: (0 0 0) m/s (no-slip condition)

Fluid Properties:
- Kinematic viscosity: 0.01 m²/s
- Reynolds number: 10

Simulation Parameters:
- Solver: icoFoam (finite volume method for incompressible flow)
- Boundary conditions: No-slip for stationary walls, moving top wall with uniform velocity

Simulation Objective:
- Analyze fluid flow dynamics in a 2D lid-driven cavity at low Reynolds number
