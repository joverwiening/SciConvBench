The simulation is a 2D incompressible lid-driven cavity flow using OpenFOAM with the following specifications:

Geometry Dimensions:
- Square cavity normalized to 1x1 units in x and y directions
- Z-direction thickness of 0.1 units (effectively 2D)

Computational Grid:
- 20x20 grid in x and y directions
- 1 cell in z-direction

Solver:
- pisoFoam solver
- k-epsilon turbulence model

Initial Conditions:
- Uniform velocity field initialized to zero
- Turbulence kinetic energy (k) = 1.5e-4, with kqRWallFunction on walls
- Turbulence dissipation rate (epsilon) = 1.4e-4, with epsilonWallFunction on walls
- Turbulent viscosity (nut) = 0, with nutkWallFunction on walls

Simulation Parameters:
- Time range: 0 to 10 units
- Time step: 0.005 units
- Results output: Every 100 time steps

Numerical Schemes:
- Convection scheme for velocity: div(phi,U) Gauss limitedLinearV 1
- Convection scheme for k: div(phi,k) Gauss limitedLinear 1
- Convection scheme for epsilon: div(phi,epsilon) Gauss limitedLinear 1
- Viscous term: div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear
