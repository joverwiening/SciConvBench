Simulate a 2D lid-driven cavity flow with the following specifications:

Fluid Properties:
The fluid is incompressible with kinematic viscosity of 0.001 m^2/s.

Geometry:
The cavity has a length of 0.1 m.
The top wall moves to the right with a velocity of 1 m/s.

Simulation Parameters:
Reynolds number is 100.
Numerical method is Finite Volume.
Grid resolution is a uniform Cartesian mesh of 20x20 cells.

Boundary Conditions:
Top wall: Moving wall with velocity of 1 m/s to the right.
Bottom, left, and right walls: 
- Velocity (U) boundary condition is noSlip.
- Pressure (p) boundary condition is zeroGradient.

Solver:
Use icoFoam solver for the simulation.
