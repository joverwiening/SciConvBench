The simulation will model natural convection in a square cavity with the following specifications:
The cavity will have a side length of 1 meter.
The left wall will be maintained at a constant temperature of 310 K.
The right wall will be maintained at a constant temperature of 290 K.
The working fluid will be air.
The simulation will analyze heat transfer and fluid flow through natural convection.
The cavity walls other than the left and right walls will be considered adiabatic (thermally insulated).
The simulation will use standard air properties at the mean temperature of 300 K.
