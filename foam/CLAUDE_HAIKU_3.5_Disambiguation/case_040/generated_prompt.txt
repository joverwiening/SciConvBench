Simulate transient two-phase flow through a porous dam with the following specifications:

Domain is a 2D rectangular space measuring 5 m (x) x 2 m (y) x 0.1 m (z-thickness).

Fluid properties:
Water: kinematic viscosity = 1e-6 m²/s, density = 1000 kg/m³
Air: kinematic viscosity = 1.48e-5 m²/s, density = 1 kg/m³

Porous medium characteristics:
Darcy-Forchheimer coefficients: d = 1e6, f = 100, indicating a highly resistive porous structure

Initial conditions:
Water column positioned on the left side of the domain
Air fills the remaining domain space

Boundary conditions:
Left/Right/Bottom walls: 
- No-slip velocity
- Fixed flux pressure
- Zero gradient water volume fraction

Top boundary (atmosphere):
- Pressure inlet/outlet velocity
- Total pressure with p0 = 0
- Inlet/outlet water volume fraction

Front/Back boundaries: Empty (2D simulation)

Simulation time parameters:
- Total simulation duration: 10 seconds
- Time step: 0.001 seconds
- Adaptive time stepping enabled
- Maximum Courant number: 0.5
- Output interval: 0.1 seconds

Objective: Track water front movement through the porous dam structure during transient flow.
