Simulation Specification for Incompressible Lid Driven Cavity Flow:

Computational Domain:
Create a 20x20 grid in x and y directions with 1 cell in z-direction.

Boundary Conditions:
Top wall ('movingWall') moves in x-direction with 1 m/s uniform velocity.
Side walls ('fixedWalls') have no-slip boundary condition (zero velocity).
Front and back faces are designated as 'empty'.

Simulation Parameters:
Use pisoFoam solver.
Simulation time: 0 to 10 seconds.
Time step: 0.005 seconds.
Results output: Every 100 time steps.
Viscosity (nu): 1e-05 m^2/s.
Turbulence model: k-epsilon.

Turbulence Field Initialization:
Turbulent kinetic energy (k): 1.5e-4, uniform, with kqRWallFunction on walls.
Turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon): 1.4e-4, uniform, with epsilonWallFunction on walls.
Turbulent viscosity (nut): 0, uniform, with nutkWallFunction on walls.

Initial Conditions:
Domain initialized with zero velocity.

Numerical Schemes:
Convection schemes:
div(phi,U) Gauss limitedLinearV 1
div(phi,k) Gauss limitedLinear 1
div(phi,epsilon) Gauss limitedLinear 1
Viscous term: div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear.
