Simulation Specification for 2D Rayleigh-Benard Convection Cell:

Geometry Configuration:
Rectangular cavity dimensions are 9 units wide and 1 unit tall.
Discretization is 90 cells in x direction, 10 cells in y direction, and 1 cell in z direction.
Simulation is extruded with front and back patches treated as empty to enforce 2D.

Solver and Physical Properties:
Use buoyantFoam solver with gravity acting downward in negative y direction.
Use Boussinesq approximation for fluid properties.
Reference fluid properties: air-like with density 1.0 kg/m^3, reference temperature 300 K.
Thermal expansion coefficient is 3.3e-3 1/K.
Kinematic viscosity is 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
Thermal diffusivity is 2.1e-5 m^2/s (Prandtl number ~ 0.7).

Boundary Conditions:
Bottom wall temperature fixed at 305 K.
Top wall temperature fixed at 295 K.
Vertical side walls are adiabatic.
No-slip velocity conditions on all solid walls.

Turbulence Modeling:
Use RAS k-epsilon turbulence model.
Turbulence is explicitly enabled with printCoeffs on.

Initial Conditions:
Initial flow state is at rest.
Uniform initial temperature field at 300 K.

Detailed Configuration:
Use heRhoThermo with Boussinesq equation of state.
Molecular weight: 29
Reference density: 1.0 kg/m^3
Reference temperature: 300 K
Thermal expansion coefficient: 3.3e-3 1/K
Specific heat capacity: 714.3 J/kg·K

Solver Tolerances:
Pressure and velocity solvers set to tolerance 1e-8.
Relative tolerance set to 0.01 for final iterations.
