Create a transient interFoam simulation of sloshing in a rectangular tank with the following specifications:

Tank Geometry:
Dimensions are 1.0 m (x) x 0.6 m (y) x 0.1 m (z).

Initial Water Level:
Water level varies linearly from 0.25 m at x=0 to 0.35 m at x=1.
Use a staircase approximation with multiple x-slices to represent the gradual water depth increase.

Simulation Parameters:
Total simulation duration is 10 seconds.
Time step is 1e-3 seconds.
Adaptive time stepping is enabled.
Maximum Courant number is 0.5.
Results will be written at 0.05-second intervals.

Initial Conditions:
Initial velocity U is (0 0 0) for both water and air.

Boundary Conditions:
Left, right, and bottom tank walls:
- Velocity (U): noSlip
- Pressure (p_rgh): fixedFluxPressure
- Water volume fraction (alpha.water): zeroGradient

Simulation Type:
Transient, gravity-driven multiphase flow using interFoam solver.
