Simulate a hot air bubble rising in water with the following specifications:

Fluid Domain:
The simulation is a 2D rectangular computational domain measuring 0.1m wide and 0.4m high.

Initial Conditions:
The liquid water is initially stationary at 300 K.
The hot air bubble is initially centered at coordinates (0.05, 0.05).
The bubble has a radius of 0.02m (diameter of 0.04m).
The air bubble is initially at 350 K.

Fluid Properties:
Water:
- Density: 1000 kg/m^3
- Specific heat capacity: 4186 J/kgK
- Thermal conductivity: 0.6 W/mK

Air:
- Density: 1.2 kg/m^3
- Dynamic viscosity: 1.8e-5 Pa.s
- Specific heat capacity: 1005 J/kgK
- Thermal conductivity: 0.025 W/mK

Physics Modeling:
Simulate multiphase flow with heat transfer.
The bubble will rise due to buoyancy forces.
Model the interaction between the hot air bubble and the surrounding water.
