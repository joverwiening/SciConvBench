The cavity simulation will be set up with the following specifications:

Geometry:
The cavity is a square with dimensions 1x1 unit in x and y axes.
The z-direction is 0.1 units thick, effectively making it a 2D simulation.
Use a computational grid of 20x20 cells in x and y directions.
Use 1 cell in the z-direction.

Boundary Conditions:
Top wall ('movingWall') moves in x-direction with a uniform velocity of 1 m/s.
Side walls ('fixedWalls') have a no-slip boundary condition (zero velocity).
Front and back faces are designated as 'empty'.

Simulation Parameters:
Use pisoFoam solver.
Simulation time: 0 to 10 seconds.
Time step: 0.005 seconds.
Output results every 100 time steps.
Viscosity (nu): 1e-05 m^2/s.
Initial domain condition: Zero velocity everywhere.

Numerical Schemes:
Convection schemes:
div(phi,U) Gauss limitedLinearV 1
div(phi,k) Gauss limitedLinear 1
div(phi,epsilon) Gauss limitedLinear 1
Viscous term: div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear
