The simulation will be a transient laminar two-phase dam-break using interFoam with the following specifications:

Domain size is a cube from (0,0,0) to (1,1,1) meters.
Computational domain is 1 meter in x, y, and z dimensions.
Top face (z=1) is assigned as an open atmosphere patch.
Five remaining faces are walls with no-slip boundary conditions.
Phases are modeled via Volume of Fluid (VOF) method:
Water phase properties: density = 1000 kg/m^3, viscosity = 1.0e-6 m^2/s
Air phase properties: density = 1 kg/m^3, viscosity = 1.48e-5 m^2/s
Surface tension = 0.07 N/m
Initial water column is a rectangular prism from (0, 0, 0) to (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75)
Alpha.water = 1 inside the water column, 0 elsewhere
Initial velocity for both water and air phases is (0, 0, 0) m/s
Wall boundaries have no-slip conditions with zero velocity
Simulation is transient and laminar
Solver: interFoam
