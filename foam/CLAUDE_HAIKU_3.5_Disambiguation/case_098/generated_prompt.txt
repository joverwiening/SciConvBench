Simulate a thermal plume rising from a hot strip on the floor of a room with the following specifications:

Geometry:
The hot strip is 0.2 m wide, centered on the floor, spanning x coordinates from 0.9 to 1.1 m at y=0 (floor level).
Computational domain size is 2.0 m (x) × 2.0 m (y) × 0.01 m (thickness).

Thermal Conditions:
Hot strip temperature is fixed at 400 K.
Ambient room temperature is 300 K.
Temperature difference is 100 K.

Floor Material:
Floor material assumed to be concrete with thermal conductivity of 1.4 W/(m·K).

Turbulence Modeling:
Use RAS kOmegaSST turbulence model for simulating the turbulent thermal plume.

Simulation Objective:
Visualize and analyze the thermal plume rising from the heated strip under turbulent flow conditions.
