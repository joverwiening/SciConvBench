Set up a simulation of 2D laminar, incompressible flow past a cylinder at a Reynolds number of 100 to observe the von Kármán vortex street. The domain is a rectangular channel 2.2m long and 0.41m high, with a cylinder of diameter 0.1m centered at (0.2, 0.2). Use a kinematic viscosity of 1e-3 m²/s and a uniform inlet velocity of U = (1 0 0) m/s. The mesh is one cell thick in the z-direction. In `system/controlDict`, specify the solver as `pimpleFoam`. Set `startTime` to 0, `endTime` to 8, `deltaT` to 0.005, `writeInterval` to 0.1, and enable `adjustTimeStep` with `maxCo` 0.9. For boundary conditions in the `0/` directory, set `U` at the `inlet` to `fixedValue uniform (1 0 0)`, at the `outlet` to `zeroGradient`, on the `cylinderWall` to `noSlip`, on the `topAndBottom` walls to `noSlip`, and on the `frontAndBack` patches to `empty`. For `p`, set `inlet` and `cylinderWall` to `zeroGradient`, `outlet` to `fixedValue uniform 0`, `topAndBottom` to `zeroGradient`, and `frontAndBack` to `empty`. Run the transient simulation to capture the vortex shedding.
