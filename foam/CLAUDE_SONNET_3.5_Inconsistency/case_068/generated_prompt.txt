The simulation shall be set up as follows:
The silicon chip dimensions are 0.01 m × 0.01 m × 0.001 m (length × width × height).
The volumetric heat generation rate in the silicon chip is 1e6 W/m³.
The silicon thermal conductivity is 130 W/m·K.
The inlet air velocity is 2 m/s.
The inlet air temperature is fixed at 293 K.
The maximum allowable chip temperature is 353 K (80°C).
The k-omega SST turbulence model shall be used for the air flow.
The air outlet shall use zeroGradient boundary condition for velocity and pressure.
The mesh resolution shall be 0.2 mm in the solid region and 0.5 mm in the fluid region.
The outerCasing boundary shall use fixedValue boundary condition at 293 K.
The simulation shall use the chtMultiRegionSimpleFoam solver.
The heat generation shall be specified using fvOptions in the solid region.
The solution shall be considered converged when residuals fall below 1e-4.
Monitor points shall be set up to track the maximum chip temperature.
The air properties shall be treated as incompressible at atmospheric pressure.
