The domain is a rectangular tank of size 1m x 0.6m x 0.4m.
The simulation uses twoPhaseEulerFoam solver for a transient three-phase flow.
Gravity acts in the negative y direction with magnitude 9.81 m/s².
The initial setup consists of a water column (0.2m x 0.6m x 0.2m) with an oil layer (0.2m x 0.6m x 0.05m) on top, located in one corner.
The remaining space is filled with air.
The mesh consists of 100 x 60 x 40 cells, providing adequate resolution for the flow features.
The oil phase has a density of 900 kg/m³ (typical light oil).
The simulation runs for 5 seconds total, sufficient to capture the initial dam break and subsequent fluid motion.
The maximum Courant number is set to 0.5 for numerical stability.
All walls have no-slip boundary conditions.
The top boundary is set as an atmospheric inlet/outlet.
The phases are defined in transportProperties as air, water, and oil.
The setFieldsDict utility initializes the phase fractions alpha.air, alpha.water, and alpha.oil.
The water density is 1000 kg/m³ and air density is 1.225 kg/m³.
The time step is adjusted automatically to maintain the specified Courant number.
