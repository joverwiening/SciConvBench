The simulation shall use the following specifications:
The domain extends 20 chord lengths downstream, 15 chord lengths upstream, and 15 chord lengths in the normal direction from the airfoil.
The NACA 0012 airfoil has a chord length of 1 meter.
The Reynolds number is 1 million based on the chord length.
The mesh contains 400 cells around the airfoil surface.
The first cell height is set to achieve y+ ≈ 30 for wall functions.
The C-grid mesh is extruded one cell (0.1 meters) in the z-direction.
The simulation uses simpleFoam solver with k-omega SST turbulence model.
The inlet boundary conditions are: U = (10 0 0.1) m/s, k = 0.375 m²/s², omega = 3.375 1/s.
The outlet boundary conditions are zeroGradient for all variables.
The airfoil surface uses no-slip wall conditions with standard wall functions.
The front and back patches are named 'frontAndBack' of type symmetry.
The simulation runs until lift and drag coefficients converge to a steady-state solution.
Forces are calculated using the forces function object in system/controlDict.
Convergence is monitored using residuals and force coefficient history.
