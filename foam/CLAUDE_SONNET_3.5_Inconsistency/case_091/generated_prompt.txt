The cavity has dimensions of 1m x 1m (standard benchmark size).
The reference temperature is 295 K (average of hot and cold walls).
The kinematic viscosity of air is 1.5e-5 m²/s at reference temperature.
The thermal diffusivity of air is 2.1e-5 m²/s at reference temperature.
The thermal expansion coefficient is 3.43e-3 1/K.
The simulation end time is 1000 seconds to ensure steady state.
The time step size is 0.1 seconds.
The Rayleigh number is approximately 1e6 (standard benchmark case).
The domain is discretized using a uniform structured mesh of 50x50 cells.
The left wall temperature is fixed at 300 K.
The right wall temperature is fixed at 290 K.
The top and bottom walls are fixed at 295 K.
All walls have noSlip velocity boundary conditions.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction with g = (0 -9.81 0) m/s².
The Boussinesq approximation is used for buoyancy effects.
The solver used is laplacianFoam.
The simulation is run in 2D.
