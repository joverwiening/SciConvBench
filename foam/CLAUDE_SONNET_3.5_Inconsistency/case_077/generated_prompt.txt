The domain is 2 meters wide by 3 meters high to allow sufficient space for plume development.
The mesh resolution is 100 cells per meter, resulting in a 200x300 cell grid.
The simulation runs for 10 seconds of physical time to capture the full plume development.
The hot patch at the bottom center is 0.2 meters wide.
The domain uses a 2D structured hexahedral mesh.
The bottom boundary is split into three sections: two adiabatic walls and the central heated patch.
All side and top boundaries are set to pressureInletOutletVelocity with inletOutlet for temperature.
Initial conditions set velocity to zero, temperature to 300 K, and pressure to atmospheric.
The hot patch temperature is fixed at 500 K.
The gravity vector is set to (0 -9.81 0).
The fluid properties use the Boussinesq approximation with beta=0.00333, rho0=1.176, and T0=300.
The Sutherland viscosity model uses As=1.4792e-06 and Ts=116.
The solver uses buoyantPimpleFoam with default numerical schemes and solution controls.
The time step is automatically adjusted to maintain a maximum Courant number of 0.8.
