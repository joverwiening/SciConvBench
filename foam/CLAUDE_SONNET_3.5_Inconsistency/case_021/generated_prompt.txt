The simulation shall use a NACA 0012 symmetric airfoil with a chord length of 1 meter.
The C-grid computational domain shall extend 15 chord lengths upstream, 20 chord lengths downstream, and 15 chord lengths in the vertical direction from the airfoil.
The angle of attack shall be set to 5 degrees.
The mesh shall have a y+ value of approximately 30 on the airfoil surface for wall function usage.
The first cell height shall be 0.001c where c is the chord length.
The mesh shall have geometric expansion ratio of 1.2 in the wall-normal direction.
The mesh shall contain approximately 100,000 cells with refinement near the airfoil surface and wake region.
The simulation shall use the simpleFoam solver for incompressible, steady-state flow.
The inlet boundary condition shall be uniform velocity (10 0 0) m/s.
The outlet boundary condition shall be zero gradient for velocity.
The airfoil surface shall use noSlip condition for velocity.
The frontAndBack patches shall use noSlip condition for velocity.
The pressure boundary conditions shall be zeroGradient at inlet and airfoil surface.
The pressure boundary condition at outlet shall be fixedValue uniform 0.
The pressure boundary conditions on frontAndBack patches shall be zeroGradient.
The domain shall be one cell thick in the z-direction.
The frontAndBack patches shall be defined as wall type in the blockMeshDict file.
