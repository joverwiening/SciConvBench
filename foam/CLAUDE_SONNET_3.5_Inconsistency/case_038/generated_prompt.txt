The simulation will model thermo-elastic deformation of a solid aluminum block with dimensions 0.1 m x 0.1 m x 0.1 m.
The mesh will consist of 20x20x20 hexahedral cells for adequate resolution of thermal and mechanical gradients.
Material properties are set as: Young's modulus E = 69e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio nu = 0.33, density rho = 2700 kg/m^3.
Thermal expansion coefficient alpha is 2.3e-5 1/K.
Reference temperature T0 is 300 K.
The temperature field T will be used to drive thermal strain.
The fixedFace boundary has displacement fixed at (0 0 0) with zeroGradient temperature.
The heatedFace boundary has fixed temperature of 400 K.
All other faces have zeroGradient conditions for both displacement and temperature.
The solver application is set to buoyantPimpleFoam.
The simulation will compute displacement and stress fields resulting from thermal expansion.
Results will include displacement field and stress distribution throughout the block.
