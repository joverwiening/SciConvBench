The domain is a rectangle with dimensions 0.6m x 0.4m.
The mesh consists of 120 cells in x-direction and 80 cells in y-direction, giving a uniform 5mm cell size.
The initial condition is a water column at x < 0.15m and y < 0.3m, with air in the remaining domain.
Water and air phases use standard properties defined in transportProperties.
The gravity vector is set to (0 0 -9.81) in constant/g.
Boundary conditions for U are noSlip on walls and pressureInletOutletVelocity on atmosphere.
Boundary conditions for p_rgh are fixedFluxPressure on walls and totalPressure on atmosphere.
Boundary conditions for alpha.water are zeroGradient on walls and inletOutlet on atmosphere.
Front and back patches are type empty for 2D simulation.
The simulation duration is 2 seconds.
The geometry is created using blockMesh with a single block.
