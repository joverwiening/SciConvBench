The inlet velocity is set to 1 m/s in the x-direction (1 0 0).

The mesh consists of 100 cells in x-direction, 20 cells in y-direction, and 1 cell in z-direction.
The inlet boundary condition is fixed velocity of (1 0 0) m/s.

The resulting Reynolds number is 10000 based on channel height and inlet velocity.
