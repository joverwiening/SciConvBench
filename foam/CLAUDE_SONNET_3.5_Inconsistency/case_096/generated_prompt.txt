The domain is a rectangular tank measuring 0.5m x 0.5m x 0.01m.
Use the interFoam solver for simulating two incompressible, immiscible fluids (water and air).
Create a structured hexahedral mesh with 100 cells in x-direction, 100 cells in y-direction, and 1 cell in z-direction.
Set gravity vector to (0 -9.81 0) m/s².
Initially, water (alpha.water=1) occupies the region x < 0.15m, with air (alpha.water=0) in the remaining domain.
Use setFieldsDict for initialization of the water column.
Set maximum simulation time to 2.0 seconds to capture the complete dam break phenomenon.
Set boundary types in constant/polyMesh/boundary as: leftWall (wall), rightWall (wall), lowerWall (wall), atmosphere (patch), frontAndBack (symmetry).
Apply boundary conditions for U: noSlip on walls, pressureInletOutletVelocity on atmosphere, symmetry on frontAndBack.
Apply boundary conditions for p_rgh: fixedFluxPressure on walls and frontAndBack, totalPressure on atmosphere.
Apply boundary conditions for alpha.water: zeroGradient on walls and frontAndBack, inletOutlet on atmosphere.
