The simulation will use simpleFoam to model flow in a cyclone separator with the following specifications:
The working fluid is air at standard conditions (density = 1.225 kg/m³, kinematic viscosity = 1.5e-5 m²/s).
The cyclone separator has a diameter of 0.2 meters, which is typical for industrial applications.
The inlet velocity is 15 m/s, which is within the typical range for industrial cyclone separators.
The turbulence model used is the k-epsilon model.
The pressure field 'p' boundary conditions are set to zeroGradient for all boundaries (inlet, outlet, dustOutlet, and walls).
The outlet pressure is set to atmospheric pressure (0 gauge pressure).
The geometry consists of a tangential inlet, a top outlet (vortex finder), and a bottom dustOutlet.
The flow enters tangentially at the inlet and primarily exits through the top outlet.
The flow is swirling and turbulent in nature.
