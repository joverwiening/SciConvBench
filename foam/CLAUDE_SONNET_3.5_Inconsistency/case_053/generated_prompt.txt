The transportProperties file should be configured as follows:
phases (water sludge);
water is defined as a Newtonian fluid with kinematic viscosity 1e-6 m²/s and density 1000 kg/m³.
sludge is defined as a Bingham plastic fluid with yield stress 15 Pa, plastic viscosity 0.1 Pa·s, and density 1500 kg/m³.
The surface tension coefficient between phases is 0.07 N/m.
The transportModel for sludge should be changed from "Newtonian" to "Bingham".
Add tau0 entry with value 15 for the sludge phase.
Add mu0 entry with value 0.1 for the sludge phase.
Remove the nu entry from the sludge phase as it is replaced by the Bingham parameters.
