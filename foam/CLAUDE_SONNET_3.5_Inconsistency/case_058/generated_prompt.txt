The simulation shall use the interFoam solver for modeling a high-pressure gas injection into water.
The gas phase shall be modeled using the perfectGas equation of state.
The water phase shall be modeled as incompressible.
The gas shall be injected at Mach 0.8.
The rectangular water tank dimensions shall be 1m x 1m x 2m.
The circular gas injection nozzle diameter shall be 10mm.
The initial conditions shall be: water at 1 atm pressure and 293K, gas at 10 bar pressure and 293K.
The total simulation time shall be 0.5 seconds to capture initial jet development and expansion.
The simulation shall be transient with compressibility effects enabled.
The domain shall be initialized with the tank fully filled with water.
The simulation shall use a 3D mesh with refinement near the injection region.
The boundary conditions shall specify fixed velocity at the inlet and pressure outlet conditions.
The simulation shall output field data every 0.001 seconds for post-processing.
The gravity vector shall be included in the -Y direction at 9.81 m/s².
