The simulation shall be configured as follows:
The domain geometry shall be a 45-degree angled rectangular duct of 350mm total length (150mm inlet + 100mm porous section + 100mm outlet) and 50mm width.
The mesh shall consist of 55x20x20 cells (15 inlet, 20 porous, 20 outlet sections).
The working fluid shall be air at standard conditions (300K, 1 atm).
The inlet velocity shall be 10 m/s with 5% turbulence intensity.
The outlet shall have a fixed pressure of 0 Pa (gauge pressure).
The porosity parameters shall be set to: porosity=0.4, Darcy coefficient (d)=150, Forchheimer coefficient (f)=1.75.
Initial conditions shall be: velocity=0 m/s, pressure=0 Pa, k=0.375 m²/s², epsilon=0.025 m²/s³.
Wall boundaries shall use no-slip conditions with standard wall functions for turbulence.
The simulation shall run from t=0 to t=1000 with dt=1 and write interval=100.
GAMG solver settings: tolerance=1e-08, relTol=0.05 for pressure.
SmoothSolver settings: tolerance=1e-06 for velocity, 1e-07 for k-epsilon.
Relaxation factors: pressure=0.3, velocity=0.7, turbulence=0.9.
Numerical schemes: bounded Gauss upwind for convection, Gauss linear corrected for diffusion.
The simulation shall be considered converged when all residuals fall below 1e-04 for three consecutive iterations.
The blockMeshDict shall be implemented exactly as provided in the original specification.
Maximum operating pressure shall be limited to 5 atm to ensure valid compressible flow assumptions.
