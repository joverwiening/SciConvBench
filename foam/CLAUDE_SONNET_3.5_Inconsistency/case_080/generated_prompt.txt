The simulation shall use standard atmospheric conditions (298 K, 101325 Pa).
The column geometry is cylindrical with diameter 0.1 m and height 1.0 m.
The gravity vector is set to (0 -9.81 0) m/s².
The three phases are defined as (air water sand) in phaseProperties.
Air phase shall be modeled as a perfect gas.
Water phase shall be modeled as incompressible liquid with standard properties at 298 K.
Sand phase shall be modeled as constant density particles with density 2650 kg/m³ and diameter 500 microns.
Initial conditions shall have a settled bed at the bottom 0.2 m with alpha.sand = 0.6 and alpha.water = 0.4.
The remaining column height shall be filled with water (alpha.water = 1).
Air shall be injected at the bottom inlet with uniform velocity of 0.5 m/s.
The top boundary shall use pressureInletOutletVelocity condition with fixed atmospheric pressure.
The Gidaspow drag model shall be used for phase interactions.
The solver shall be twoPhaseEulerFoam.
The simulation shall run for 10 seconds in transient mode.
Time step shall be automatically adjusted to maintain numerical stability.
