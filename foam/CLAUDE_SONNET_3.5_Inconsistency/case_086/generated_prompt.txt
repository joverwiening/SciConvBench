The room is a 2D rectangular domain of 5 m × 3 m, extruded one cell in z-direction.
The hot block of 0.5 m × 0.5 m is positioned at the center of the room (2.25 m, 1.5 m).
The simulation runs for 60 seconds to capture the initial and intermediate cooling dynamics.
The hot block is initialized at 400 K.
The surrounding air is initialized at 300 K.
Gravity is set to (0 -9.81 0) m/s².
All walls use zeroGradient boundary condition for temperature (T).
All walls use noSlip boundary condition for velocity (U).
The front and back patches use empty boundary condition for all fields.
The thermophysicalProperties dictionary uses Boussinesq equation of state with hePsiThermo, pureMixture, sutherland transport, janaf thermodynamics, and sensibleEnthalpy energy formulation.
The solver used is buoyantPimpleFoam.
