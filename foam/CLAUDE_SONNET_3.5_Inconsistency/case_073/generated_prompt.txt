The domain is a vertical cylinder with height 1m along Y-axis and diameter 0.2m.
The fluid is water at standard room temperature (20°C).
Water properties at 20°C: density = 998.2 kg/m³, kinematic viscosity = 1.004e-6 m²/s.
A single spherical particle is simulated with diameter 1mm and density 2500 kg/m³.
The particle is initialized at position (0, 0.9, 0) meters.
Gravity is set to (0 -9.81 0) m/s² in constant/g dictionary.
Initial fluid velocity is set to zero everywhere in the domain.
Cylinder walls use noSlip boundary condition.
Top and bottom patches are specified as wall boundary condition.
The simulation runs for 10 seconds total duration.
The timestep size is set to 0.001 seconds.
The DPMFoam solver is used for the simulation.
Particle trajectory data is recorded throughout the simulation.
