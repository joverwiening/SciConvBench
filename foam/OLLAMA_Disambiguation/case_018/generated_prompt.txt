Create a rectangular computational domain spanning x = 0 to 0.02 m, y = –0.01 to 0.01 m, and z = –0.01 to 0.01 m.  
Discretize the domain with 100 cells in the x direction, 40 cells in the y direction, and 1 cell in the z direction.  
Use the reactingFoam solver with compressible finite‑rate chemistry.  
Set the thermophysical model to hePsiThermo with coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture mixing, Sutherland transport, and JANAF thermodynamic tables.  
Use EulerImplicit chemistry integration and the Rosenbrock43 ODE solver.  
Model momentum transport as laminar (no turbulence model).  
Initialize the pressure field uniformly to 101 kPa.  
Initialize the temperature field uniformly to 2000 K.  
Initialize the velocity field to zero everywhere.  
Initialize the species mass fractions uniformly to an air mixture: Y_O2 = 0.23, Y_N2 = 0.77, Y_CH4 = 0.  
Apply a fixedValue inlet at x = 0 (fuel inlet) with velocity +0.1 m s⁻¹, temperature 293 K, and species mass fractions CH4 = 1.0, O2 = 0, N2 = 0.  
Apply a fixedValue inlet at x = 0.02 m (air inlet) with velocity –0.1 m s⁻¹, temperature 293 K, and species mass fractions O2 = 0.23, N2 = 0.77, CH4 = 0.  
Set the y‑direction boundaries (y = –0.01 m and y = 0.01 m) as outlet (zeroGradient) for all fields.  
Set the z‑direction boundaries (z = –0.01 m and z = 0.01 m) as empty.  
Use a constant time step of 1e‑4 s, run the simulation to a final time of 0.5 s, and write results every 0.05 s.
