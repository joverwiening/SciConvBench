The computational domain is a square cavity with dimensions 1 unit in the x‑direction and 1 unit in the y‑direction, and a thickness of 0.1 units in the z‑direction, making the problem effectively two‑dimensional.  
The simulation runs from time 0 to time 10 with a constant time step of 0.005 units; field data are written every 100 time steps.  
The fluid viscosity ν is constant and equal to 1e‑05 m² s⁻¹.  
The k‑ε turbulence model is used.  

Initial fields:  
- Velocity U = (0 0 0) m s⁻¹ everywhere.  
- Pressure p = uniform 0 Pa throughout the domain.  
- Turbulent kinetic energy k = uniform 1.5e‑4 m² s⁻².  
- Turbulent dissipation ε = uniform 1.4e‑4 m² s⁻³.  
- Turbulent viscosity nut = uniform 0 m² s⁻¹.  

Boundary conditions:  
- Top wall (patch named movingWall): fixedValue U = (1 0 0) m s⁻¹; zeroGradient for pressure; kqRWallFunction for k; epsilonWallFunction for ε; nutkWallFunction for nut.  
- All other side walls (patch named fixedWalls): fixedValue U = (0 0 0) m s⁻¹ (no‑slip); zeroGradient for pressure; kqRWallFunction for k; epsilonWallFunction for ε; nutkWallFunction for nut.  
- Front and back faces: empty patches (no velocity or turbulence conditions required).  

Solver: pisoFoam is used for the incompressible flow.  

Discretisation schemes:  
- Convection of momentum: div(phi,U) Gauss limitedLinearV 1.  
- Convection of k: div(phi,k) Gauss limitedLinear 1.  
- Convection of ε: div(phi,epsilon) Gauss limitedLinear 1.  
- Viscous term for momentum: div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear.  

All other default settings of pisoFoam are retained unless otherwise required.
