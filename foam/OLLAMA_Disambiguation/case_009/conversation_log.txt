Q: What velocity boundary condition should be applied on each wall of the cavity?
A: - **Top wall (named `movingWall`)** – prescribe a uniform velocity of **U = (1  0  0) m s⁻¹** (moving in the x‑direction).  
- **All other walls (named `fixedWalls`)** – apply a **no‑slip condition**: **U = (0  0  0) m s⁻¹**.  
- **Front and back faces** – set as **`empty`** (no velocity condition needed).

Q: What pressure boundary condition should be applied on the cavity walls and at any reference location?
A: - **All cavity walls (including `movingWall` and `fixedWalls`)** – apply a **zero‑gradient (Neumann) condition** for pressure: `type zeroGradient;`.

- **Reference pressure** – fix the pressure at a single location (e.g., a cell or a point on any wall) to a known value, typically zero: `type fixedValue; value uniform 0;`. This provides the required pressure reference for the incompressible solver.

Q: What should be the initial velocity field throughout the domain?
A: The initial velocity should be set to zero everywhere in the domain:

**U = (0  0  0) m s⁻¹** (uniform throughout the cavity).

=== 2026-02-16 09:12:29 ===
Q: What should be the initial pressure field value throughout the domain?
A: Set the pressure field to a uniform value of 0 Pa (i.e., `p = uniform 0` throughout the domain). If the complete requirement does not specify otherwise, this is a reasonable best‑practice assumption.
