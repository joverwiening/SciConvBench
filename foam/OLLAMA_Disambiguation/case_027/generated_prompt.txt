Create a rectangular computational domain that extends from x = 0 m to x = 1 m, y = 0 m to y = 1 m, and z = 0 m to z = 1 m.

Place a rectangular obstacle (cuboid) inside the domain with its lower‑front‑left corner at (0.375 m, 0.375 m, 0 m) and its upper‑back‑right corner at (0.625 m, 0.625 m, 0.25 m).  
Model the two phases with the volume‑of‑fluid (VOF) method: water with density 1000 kg m⁻³ and kinematic viscosity 1.0e‑6 m² s⁻¹, and air with density 1 kg m⁻³ and kinematic viscosity 1.48e‑5 m² s⁻¹.  
Set the surface tension coefficient between water and air to 0.07 N m⁻¹.  
Initialize the water volume fraction field alpha.water to 1 inside the rectangular prism defined by the corners (0, 0, 0) and (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75) and to 0 elsewhere.  
Use a gravity vector g = (0  0  -9.81) m s⁻².  
Set the simulation end time (endTime) to 0.1 seconds.  
Configure interFoam for laminar flow (laminar model) and enable surface tension modeling.  
Select appropriate time‑step control (e.g., adjustable time step with maximum Courant number ≤ 0.5) and write results at suitable intervals for post‑processing.
