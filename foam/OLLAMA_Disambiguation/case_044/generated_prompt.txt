Model a rectangular cavity with horizontal dimensions 0.10 m × 0.10 m and a depth (front‑back thickness) of 0.01 m.  
Set the left wall (hotWall) temperature to 303 K.  
Set the remaining three walls (right, top, bottom) temperature to 293 K.  
Apply gravity in the negative y‑direction: g = (0, ‑9.81, 0) m s⁻².  
Treat all walls as no‑slip solid boundaries.  
Ensure the Rayleigh number based on the temperature difference (ΔT = 10 K) and cavity height is approximately 1 × 10⁶.  
Solve the governing continuity, momentum, energy, and k‑epsilon turbulence equations until convergence to a steady solution.
