Model Rayleigh‑Bénard convection in a rectangular domain 2 m wide (x‑direction) and 1 m high (y‑direction).  
Use a fluid with Prandtl number 0.71 (e.g., air).  
Set the Rayleigh number to approximately 2500.  
Apply a temperature of 300 K on the bottom boundary and 299 K on the top boundary.  
Treat the vertical side walls as periodic (cyclic) boundaries for velocity, temperature, and all other fields.  
Apply no‑slip velocity conditions on the top and bottom walls.  
Initialize the fluid at rest with a uniform temperature equal to the average of the top and bottom temperatures (299.5 K).  
Run the simulation until physical time = 500.
