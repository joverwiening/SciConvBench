Set up a steady-state pipe flow case with a pipe length of 2 m and a diameter of 0.04 m.  
Use the k‑omega SST turbulence model.  
Apply the simpleFoam solver.  
Use air as the fluid with a density of 1.225 kg m⁻³ and a kinematic viscosity of 1.5 × 10⁻⁵ m² s⁻¹.  
Specify a uniform inlet velocity of (10 0 0) m s⁻¹.  
Set the turbulence intensity at the inlet to 3 %.  
Specify the turbulent kinetic energy at the inlet as a fixedValue of 0.135 m² s⁻².  
Specify the specific dissipation rate at the inlet as a fixedValue of 33 s⁻¹.  
Apply a fixedValue zero gauge pressure at the outlet (p = fixedValue uniform 0).  
At the pipe wall, set k = kqRWallFunction and omega = omegaWallFunction.
