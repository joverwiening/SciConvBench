Create a rectangular computational domain spanning x = 0 to 0.02 m, y = –0.01 to 0.01 m, and z = –0.01 to 0.01 m.  
Discretize the domain with 100 cells in the x‑direction, 40 cells in the y‑direction, and a single cell in the z‑direction.  
Assign the left boundary at x = 0 the name “fuel”, the right boundary at x = 0.02 m the name “air”, the top and bottom boundaries the name “outlet”, and treat the front and back faces as empty (zero‑gradient).  
Set the inlet condition on the fuel boundary to pure methane (CH₄) with mass fraction = 1 and an inlet velocity of +0.1 m s⁻¹ directed into the domain.  
Set the inlet condition on the air boundary to a mixture of O₂ and N₂ with mass fractions Y_O₂ = 0.23 and Y_N₂ = 0.77 and an inlet velocity of –0.1 m s⁻¹ directed into the domain.  
Initialize the entire domain with a temperature of 2000 K and a pressure of 101 325 Pa.  
Use the reactingFoam solver with compressible finite‑rate chemistry.  
Select the chemistry model type EulerImplicit and the ODE solver Rosenbrock43.  
Configure the combustion model and momentum transport as laminar.  
Employ hePsiThermo for thermodynamics, coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture for mixture properties, Sutherland transport model, and JANAF thermodynamic data.  
Run the simulation with a time step of 1 × 10⁻⁴ s, a final simulation time of 0.5 s, and write results every 0.05 s.
