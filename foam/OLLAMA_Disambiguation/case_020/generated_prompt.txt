Use pimpleFoam as the solver for an incompressible, laminar, unsteady flow.  
Simulate from startTime = 0 s to endTime = 0.5 s with a constant time step deltaT = 0.0025 s and write results every 0.05 s.  
Define the computational domain as a rectangular box extending from x = –5 m to x = 5 m, y = –1.5 m to y = 2.5 m, and z = –1 m to z = 1 m, and treat the simulation as two‑dimensional by leaving the front and back faces (z‑direction) empty.  
Place a circular cylinder of diameter 2 m centered at (x = 0 m, y = 0 m) within the domain.  
Assume the fluid is water with a density ρ ≈ 1000 kg m⁻³.  
Select the generalizedNewtonian laminar model and use the crossPowerLaw viscosity formulation.  
Set the cross‑power‑law parameters to infinite‑shear viscosity ν∞ = 10, consistency index m = 0.4, flow‑behavior index n = 3, and reference shear rate γ̇₀ ≈ 1 s⁻¹.  
Apply a uniform inlet velocity U = (1 0 0) m s⁻¹ at the upstream boundary x = –5 m.  
Impose a no‑slip condition (velocity = 0) on the surface of the cylinder.  
At the downstream boundary x = 5 m, use a zero‑gradient condition for velocity and a fixed pressure of zero (or zero‑gradient for pressure).  
Apply slip (symmetry) conditions on the side walls at y = –1.5 m and y = 2.5 m.  
Treat the front and back faces at z = –1 m and z = 1 m as symmetry planes with zero‑gradient conditions.  
Initialize the flow field with the inlet velocity (1 m s⁻¹ in the x‑direction) everywhere and set the initial pressure field to zero.  
Use the default PIMPLE algorithm settings for pressure‑velocity coupling.
