Create a case directory with the standard OpenFOAM subfolders: 0, constant, and system.  
Define the geometry as a straight circular pipe of length 1 m and inner diameter 0.02 m.  
Generate the mesh with blockMesh using 100 cells in the axial (length) direction and 40 cells across the full pipe diameter (20 radial cells from the axis to the wall).  
In constant/transportProperties set the fluid density to 1000 kg m⁻³ and the kinematic viscosity ν to 1 × 10⁻⁶ m² s⁻¹ (dynamic viscosity μ = 0.001 Pa·s).  
In constant/turbulenceProperties set simulationType to laminar.  
In the 0/U file assign a uniform fixedValue of (0.1 0 0) m s⁻¹ at the inlet patch, a zeroGradient condition at the outlet patch, and a fixedValue of (0 0 0) m s⁻¹ (no‑slip) at the wall patch.  
In the 0/p file assign a zeroGradient condition at the inlet patch, a fixedValue of 0 Pa at the outlet patch, and a zeroGradient condition at the wall patch.  
In constant/polyMesh set appropriate cell grading if needed to maintain mesh quality.  
In system/fvSolution configure simpleFoam for a steady‑state laminar run, using under‑relaxation factors of 0.3 for velocity (U) and 0.3 for pressure (p), and set the residual convergence criteria to 1 × 10⁻⁶ for all variables.  
In system/fvSchemes use second‑order accurate schemes for divergence and gradient terms (e.g., Gauss linear).  
Run blockMesh to create the mesh, then execute simpleFoam to obtain the steady‑state solution.  
After the run, post‑process the results (e.g., with paraFoam) to verify the velocity profile development along the pipe and confirm that the flow remains laminar.
