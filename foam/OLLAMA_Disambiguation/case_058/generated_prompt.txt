Run a laminar, incompressible flow simulation of a circular cylinder of diameter 0.1 m using the pimpleFoam solver.  
Set the fluid properties to a kinematic viscosity ν = 1 × 10⁻³ m² s⁻¹ (density can be taken as 1000 kg m⁻³ for water).  
Define the computational domain as a rectangular channel that extends 0.2 m upstream of the cylinder (2 × D), 2.0 m downstream (20 × D), and spans from y = 0 m to y = 0.41 m (4.1 × D) in the vertical direction.  
Apply the following boundary conditions:  
- Inlet (x = ‑0.2 m): uniform velocity U = (1 m s⁻¹, 0, 0) and zero gradient pressure.  
- Outlet (x = +2.0 m): zero gradient velocity and fixed pressure p = 0.  
- Top and bottom walls (y = 0 m and y = 0.41 m): no‑slip velocity (U = 0).  
- Cylinder surface: no‑slip velocity (U = 0).  
Generate a hexahedral mesh with local refinement around the cylinder: cell size near the cylinder surface ≈ 0.0025 m (D/40) and gradual coarsening toward the far field, resulting in roughly 200 000 cells.  
Configure pimpleFoam for laminar flow: set turbulenceModel to laminar, use nCorrectors = 2, nNonOrthogonalCorrectors = 0.  
Choose a time step that keeps the maximum Courant number below 0.5; with the given velocity and mesh this corresponds to Δt ≈ 0.001 s.  
Run the simulation for a total physical time of at least 10 s to capture several vortex‑shedding cycles and allow the flow to reach a statistically steady state.  
Write results every 0.01 s.  
Post‑process to obtain drag coefficient (Cd), lift coefficient (Cl), and Strouhal number (St) based on the recorded force time histories.
