Q: What turbulence quantities (e.g., turbulence intensity, turbulent kinetic energy k, and specific dissipation rate ω) should be specified at the inlet for the k‑omega SST model?
A: - **Turbulence intensity (I):** 1 %  
- **Turbulence length scale (Lₜ):** 0.01 m  

From these you should compute the inlet values for the k‑ω SST model:

- **Turbulent kinetic energy:** \(k = 1.5\,(I\,U)^2\)  
  (with the inlet velocity magnitude U ≈ 10 m s⁻¹, this gives \(k ≈ 0.015\;{\rm m^2\,s^{-2}}\))

- **Specific dissipation rate:** \(\omega = \frac{\sqrt{k}}{C_\mu^{1/4}\,L_t}\)  
  (using \(C_\mu ≈ 0.09\) and \(L_t = 0.01\) m, this gives \(\omega ≈ 22\;{\rm s^{-1}}\))

Thus specify **I = 1 %**, **k ≈ 0.015 m² s⁻²**, and **ω ≈ 22 s⁻¹** (or compute them from the same formulas if a different inlet velocity is used).
