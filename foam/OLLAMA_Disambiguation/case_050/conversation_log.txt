Q: What boundary conditions should be applied to the left, right, and bottom walls of the domain?
A: - **Velocity (U)** on the left, right, and bottom walls (the *fixedWalls* patch): `noSlip` (zero velocity).  
- **Pressure (p)** on those same walls: `fixedFluxPressure` with a uniform value of 0.

=== 2026-02-16 09:43:23 ===
Q: What time step size should be used for the transient simulation?
A: Use an adjustable time step. In `system/controlDict` set  

```text
adjustTimeStep   yes;
maxCo            1.0;
maxDeltaT        0.05;
``` 

so the solver will choose the time step based on the Courant number, but it will never exceed 0.05 s.

=== 2026-02-16 09:43:32 ===
Q: What velocity and pressure boundary conditions should be applied to the top wall?
A: - **Velocity (U)** on the top wall (*movingWall*): `fixedValue` uniform (1 0 0) m s⁻¹.  
- **Pressure (p)** on the top wall: `fixedFluxPressure` with a uniform value 0.

=== 2026-02-16 09:43:40 ===
Q: What initial values should be assigned to the velocity (U) and pressure (p) fields at the start of the simulation?
A: - **Velocity U** (in `0/U`): uniform (0 0 0) m s⁻¹.  
- **Pressure p** (in `0/p`): uniform 0 Pa.
