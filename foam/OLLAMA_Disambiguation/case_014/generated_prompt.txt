Set up a 2-D opposed-jet laminar counter-flow diffusion flame using the reactingFoam solver.  
Create a rectangular domain with x ranging from 0 to 0.02 m, y from –0.01 m to 0.01 m, and z from –0.01 m to 0.01 m.  
Discretize the domain with 100 cells in the x‑direction, 40 cells in the y‑direction, and a single cell in the z‑direction.  
Name the left boundary (x = 0) “fuel”, the right boundary (x = 0.02 m) “air”, the top and bottom boundaries “outlet”, and set the front and back faces as empty (zeroGradient).  
Initialize the entire domain at time 0 with temperature 2000 K.  
Apply a uniform pressure of 101 325 Pa throughout the domain.  
At the fuel inlet, impose a velocity of 0.1 m s⁻¹, temperature of 293 K, and a pure methane (CH₄) composition (mass fraction = 1).  
At the air inlet, impose a velocity of –0.1 m s⁻¹, temperature of 293 K, and an air composition with mass fractions Y(O₂) = 0.23 and Y(N₂) = 0.77.  
Use laminar momentum transport and compressible finite‑rate chemistry.  
Select the hePsiThermo thermodynamic model, coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture mixing model, Sutherland transport model, and JANAF thermodynamic data.  
Configure chemistry with the EulerImplicit scheme and the Rosenbrock43 ODE solver.  
Set the simulation time step to 1 × 10⁻⁴ s, run until 0.5 s, and write results every 0.05 s.  
Write the primary fields (U, p, T, and species mass fractions) at each output interval.
