Q: What are the geometry and dimensions of the solid porous block and the surrounding fluid domain?
A: The overall computational domain is a rectangular box **0.5 m (length) × 0.1 m (height) × 0.01 m (depth)** (the depth is kept thin so the case is effectively 2‑D).

Inside this domain a solid porous block is placed **centered** and has dimensions **0.1 m (length) × 0.05 m (height) × 0.01 m (depth)**.

The fluid region occupies the remainder of the box surrounding the solid block (i.e., the volume of the 0.5 m × 0.1 m × 0.01 m domain minus the 0.1 m × 0.05 m × 0.01 m solid block).

Q: What is the fluid inlet temperature and velocity (or velocity profile) at the inlet boundary?
A: The fluid inlet uses a uniform (fixed‑value) condition:

- **Velocity:** U = (1  0  0) m s⁻¹ (uniform flow in the x‑direction)  
- **Temperature:** T = 400 K (fixed value)

Q: What is the porosity of the solid porous block?
A: The complete requirement does not specify a numerical porosity for the solid block. Make a reasonable assumption based on best practices (e.g., typical aluminum‑foam porosities around 0.8–0.9).

=== 2026-02-16 10:01:33 ===
Q: What fluid is flowing around the porous block (e.g., air, water, another gas), and should standard properties for that fluid be used?
A: The fluid is **air**. Use the standard compressible‑perfect‑gas model (heRhoThermo + perfectGas + Sutherland) with the usual air properties (Cp ≈ 1005 J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹, molWeight ≈ 28.9 kg kmol⁻¹, etc.) as specified in the solid‑region thermophysicalProperties file.

=== 2026-02-16 10:01:41 ===
Q: What material and thermal properties (e.g., thermal conductivity, density, specific heat) should be assigned to the solid porous block?
A: Use an effective homogeneous solid model (heSolidThermo) with the following properties (representative of an aluminum‑foam porous material):

| Property | Value | Units |
|----------|-------|-------|
| Density (ρ) | 500 | kg m⁻³ |
| Specific heat (Cp) | 900 | J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹ |
| Thermal conductivity (kappa) | 10 | W m⁻¹ K⁻¹ |

These values go in the solid region’s `constant/solid/thermophysicalProperties` file. No momentum equations are solved in the solid.
