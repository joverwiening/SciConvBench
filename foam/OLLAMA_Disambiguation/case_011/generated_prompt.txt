Create a square cavity domain with length 1 m in the x‑direction and 1 m in the y‑direction and a depth of 1 cell in the z‑direction. Generate a structured mesh of 20 cells in x, 20 cells in y, and 1 cell in z.  
Set the fluid density to 1 kg m⁻³ and the kinematic viscosity (ν) to 1e‑05 m² s⁻¹.  
Apply a no‑slip condition (velocity = 0) on all walls designated as “fixedWalls”.  
Assign the top wall (“movingWall”) a uniform velocity of (1 m s⁻¹, 0, 0).  
Define the front and back faces as “empty” boundaries.  
Use the pisoFoam solver for the simulation.  
Specify the numerical schemes:  
- convective term for momentum: `div(phi,U) Gauss limitedLinearV 1`  
- convective term for turbulent kinetic energy: `div(phi,k) Gauss limitedLinear 1`  
- convective term for turbulent dissipation: `div(phi,epsilon) Gauss limitedLinear 1`  
- viscous term: `div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear`  
Set the simulation control parameters: startTime = 0, endTime = 10, timeStep = 0.005, and write results at appropriate intervals.  
Initialize the velocity field to zero everywhere and the pressure field to zero (or a uniform reference pressure).  
Run the simulation until the physical time reaches 10 units.
