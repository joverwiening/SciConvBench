Create a square cavity domain with dimensions 1 unit in the x‑direction and 1 unit in the y‑direction and a thickness of 0.1 units in the z‑direction, effectively representing a 2D flow.  
Generate a mesh consisting of 20 cells in the x‑direction, 20 cells in the y‑direction, and a single cell in the z‑direction.  
Set the fluid density to 1 kg/m³ and the kinematic viscosity to 1 e‑5 m²/s.  
Use the pisoFoam solver for the incompressible flow simulation.  
Apply the k‑ε turbulence model.  
Initialize the turbulence fields: set turbulent kinetic energy k = 1.5 e‑4 uniformly with kqRWallFunction on all walls (front and back walls are empty), set turbulent dissipation rate ε = 1.4 e‑4 uniformly with epsilonWallFunction on all walls (front and back empty), and set turbulent viscosity nut = 0 uniformly with nutkWallFunction on all walls (front and back empty).  
Initialize the velocity field to zero everywhere at time 0.  
Impose a moving lid boundary condition on the top wall: a uniform velocity of 1 m/s in the x‑direction.  
Apply no‑slip (zero velocity) boundary conditions on the remaining walls.  
Run the simulation from time 0 to time 10 with a constant time step of 0.005.  
Write results to file every 100 time steps.  
Use the following discretisation schemes:  
- Convective term for momentum: div(phi,U) Gauss limitedLinearV 1  
- Convective term for k: div(phi,k) Gauss limitedLinear 1  
- Convective term for ε: div(phi,epsilon) Gauss limitedLinear 1  
- Viscous term for momentum: div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear.
