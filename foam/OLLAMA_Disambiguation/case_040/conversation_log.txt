Q: What are the dimensions and overall shape of the porous dam you want to simulate?
A: The porous dam is a rectangular slab that spans the full height of the domain.  
- **Length (x‑direction):** from x = 2 m to x = 3 m (1 m thick).  
- **Height (y‑direction):** the full 2 m of the domain.  
- **Thickness (z‑direction):** the full 0.1 m thickness of the 2‑D model.  

So the dam is a 1 m × 2 m × 0.1 m rectangular block occupying the cell zone “porousDam”.

=== 2026-02-16 09:34:44 ===
Q: What is the permeability (or hydraulic conductivity) of the porous dam material?
A: The porous dam is defined with a Darcy‑Forchheimer source:

```
explicitPorositySourceCoeffs
{
    type DarcyForchheimer;
    d (1e6 1e6 0);
    f (100 100 0);
}
```

In OpenFOAM the `d` vector is the Darcy coefficient ( 1 / permeability ).  
Thus the permeability (in the x‑ and y‑directions) is

**k = 1 / d = 1 × 10⁻⁶ m²**  

(the z‑direction is not used in this 2‑D setup).

=== 2026-02-16 09:35:14 ===
Q: What boundary conditions (type and values) should be applied on each of the domain’s six faces for pressure, velocity, and the phase fraction fields?
A: **leftWall, rightWall, bottomWall (solid walls)**  
- **U:** `noSlip`  
- **p_rgh:** `fixedFluxPressure`  
- **alpha.water:** `zeroGradient`  

**atmosphere (top open boundary)**  
- **U:** `pressureInletOutletVelocity` with `inletValue (0 0 0)`  
- **p_rgh:** `totalPressure` with `p0 uniform 0`  
- **alpha.water:** `inletOutlet` with `inletValue 0; value 0`  

**frontAndBack (empty direction)**  
- **U:** `empty`  
- **p_rgh:** `empty`  
- **alpha.water:** `empty`
