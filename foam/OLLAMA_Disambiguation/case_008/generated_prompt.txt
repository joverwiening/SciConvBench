The computational domain is a square cavity with dimensions 1 unit in the x-direction and 1 unit in the y-direction, and a thickness of 0.1 units in the z-direction, effectively representing a 2D flow.  
A mesh of 20 cells in the x-direction, 20 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction is used.  
The top wall, named "movingWall", moves in the positive x-direction with a uniform velocity of 1 m/s.  
All other walls, named "fixedWalls", have a no‑slip condition (velocity = 0).  
The front and back faces are designated as "empty".  
The simulation uses the pisoFoam solver and runs from time 0 to 10 seconds with a time step of 0.005 seconds.  
Results are written every 100 time steps.  
The fluid density is assumed to be 1.0 kg/m³.  
The kinematic viscosity (nu) is constant at 1e‑05 m²/s.  
The flow field is initialized with zero velocity throughout the domain at the start of the simulation.  
Convection schemes are set as follows:  
- div(phi,U) uses Gauss limitedLinearV 1  
- div(phi,k) uses Gauss limitedLinear 1  
- div(phi,epsilon) uses Gauss limitedLinear 1  
The viscous term uses the scheme div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear.
