The computational domain is a cube spanning from (0,0,0) to (1,1,1).  
An obstacle occupies the region from (0.375,0.375,0) to (0.625,0.625,0.25).  
Gravity is set to g = (0, 0, -9.81) m/s².  
The top face (z = 1) is defined as an open atmosphere patch; the remaining five faces are walls.  
Two phases are modeled using the Volume‑of‑Fluid (VOF) method:  
- Water: density = 1000 kg/m³, kinematic viscosity = 1.0e-6 m²/s.  
- Air: density = 1 kg/m³, kinematic viscosity = 1.48e-5 m²/s.  
Surface tension between water and air is 0.07 N/m.  
The initial water volume fraction (alpha.water) is set to 1 inside the rectangular prism from (0,0,0) to (0.6,0.1875,0.75) and 0 elsewhere.  
The computational mesh is uniform with 32 cells in each direction (32 × 32 × 32), giving a cell size of approximately 0.03125 m.  
The simulation runs for a total physical time of 0.1 seconds with a constant time step of 1 × 10⁻³ seconds.  
The initial velocity field is zero everywhere in the domain.
