Simulate a fluidized bed with a square cross‑section of 0.1 m × 0.1 m and a height of 0.5 m using an Euler‑Euler two‑phase model.  
Use air as the gas phase at 298 K and 1 atm (ρ₍g₎ = 1.2 kg m⁻³, ν₍g₎ = 1.5 × 10⁻⁵ m² s⁻¹).  
Use solid particles of 200 µm diameter and material density 2500 kg m⁻³.  
Apply the Schiller‑Naumann drag model for interphase momentum exchange.  
Set the initial solid volume fraction α₍solid₎ = 0.55 in the lower 0.2 m of the bed and α₍solid₎ = 0 above that region.  
Create a block‑mesh with 50 cells in the X direction, 250 cells in the Y (height) direction, and 50 cells in the Z direction (≈ 6.25 × 10⁵ cells total).  
At the top boundary use a pressure‑outlet (open) condition: p_rgh = totalPressure (reference pressure 0), U.air = pressureInletOutletVelocity, alpha.solid = inletOutlet (inletValue 0, value 0).  
At the bottom inlet apply a velocity inlet for the gas with a superficial upward velocity of 0.2 m s⁻¹, and an inletOutlet condition for the solid phase with inletValue 0 (no solid inflow) and zeroGradient for outlet.  
Run the case as a transient (time‑dependent) simulation with an appropriate time step to resolve particle dynamics.  
Use a suitable Euler‑Euler solver (e.g., multiphaseEulerFoam) with second‑order spatial discretisation schemes for convection and diffusion.
