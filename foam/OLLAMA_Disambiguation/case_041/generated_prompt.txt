The cavity is a square domain 1 m wide and 1 m high.  
The left wall temperature is set to 303 K (hot wall).  
The right wall temperature is set to 293 K (cold wall).  
The top and bottom walls have a no‑slip velocity condition (U = (0 0 0) m s⁻¹).  
The top and bottom walls have a zero‑gradient temperature condition (insulated).  
Gravity is defined by the vector g = (0  -9.81  0) m s⁻², acting in the negative y‑direction.  
Air is modeled using the Boussinesq approximation.  
The simulation is performed as a steady‑state analysis.
