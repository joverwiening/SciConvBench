The computational domain is a rectangular block 0.2 m wide, 0.4 m high, and 0.01 m thick; the porous medium occupies the entire domain and the front and back faces are empty, allowing a 2‑D simulation.  
The porous medium has a porosity of 0.35 (assumed reasonable for a water‑saturated material).  
The Darcy permeability of the porous medium is 1 × 10⁻⁸ m² (Darcy coefficient d = 1 × 10⁸ 1/m²).  
The Forchheimer coefficient β in the fvOptions is set to 1 × 10⁴ 1/m (reasonable for typical packed‑bed flows).  
Water is used as the fluid with Boussinesq reference properties appropriate for water at the reference temperature.  
The simulation is run with the buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam solver.  
An explicit Darcy‑Forchheimer resistance source is defined in fvOptions using the specified permeability, Forchheimer coefficient, and porosity.  
The left wall (heated side) has a fixed temperature of 303 K.  
The right wall has a fixed temperature of 293 K.  
The top and bottom walls have zero‑gradient temperature conditions.  
All walls (right, top, bottom) have a no‑slip velocity condition.  
Pressure (p_rgh) on the right, top, and bottom walls is set to fixedFluxPressure.
