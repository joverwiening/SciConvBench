Set up a 2‑D buoyant turbulent simulation of a hot strip on the floor using buoyantPimpleFoam.  
Define the computational domain as a rectangular box 2.0 m (x) × 2.0 m (y) × 0.01 m (z) with a uniform mesh of 160 × 160 × 1 cells (Δx ≈ Δy ≈ 0.0125 m, Δz = 0.01 m).  
Place a heated floor strip (hotSource patch) from x = 0.9 m to x = 1.1 m, spanning the full thickness (z) and having a surface temperature of 400 K.  
Apply the following boundary conditions:  

- hotSource: U = noSlip, p_rgh = fixedFluxPressure, k = kWallFunction, ω = omegaWallFunction, T = fixedValue 400 K.  
- coolFloor (floor outside hot strip): U = noSlip, p_rgh = fixedFluxPressure, k and ω = standard RAS wall functions, T = fixedValue 300 K.  
- sideWalls: U = noSlip, p_rgh = fixedFluxPressure, k and ω = standard RAS wall functions, T = fixedValue 300 K.  
- outlet (top open boundary): U = pressureInletOutletVelocity with inletValue (0 0 0), p_rgh = totalPressure (reference p0 = 0), T = inletOutlet (inletValue 300 K, value 300 K), k and ω = inletOutlet (small inlet value 1e‑6).  

Initialize the fields:  

- Velocity U = (0 0 0) m s⁻¹ throughout the domain.  
- Pressure p_rgh = 0 Pa (reference pressure).  
- Temperature T = 300 K everywhere except the hotSource patch (fixed at 400 K).  
- Turbulence kinetic energy k = 1e‑5 m² s⁻² uniformly in the domain.  
- Specific dissipation rate ω = 1 s⁻¹ uniformly in the domain.  

Use the RAS k‑ω SST turbulence model with standard wall‑function treatment for k and ω on solid walls.  
Set gravity to (0 -9.81 0) m s⁻² and employ the full compressible buoyancy formulation of buoyantPimpleFoam (no Boussinesq approximation).  
Run a transient simulation for a total time of 200 s with an initial time step of 0.01 s, enabling adjustTimeStep to keep the Courant number ≤ 0.5.  
Write results at appropriate intervals for post‑processing and visualization of the turbulent thermal plume.
