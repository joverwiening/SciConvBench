Target OpenFOAM distribution and version as OpenFOAM Foundation v10.
Simulate steady-state 3D incompressible turbulent flow in a channel with a cube mounted on the floor using simpleFoam.
Use the RANS kEpsilon turbulence model with standard wall functions appropriate for kEpsilon.
Create a fully 3D mesh with more than one cell in the spanwise direction.
Treat the spanwise side patches left and right as symmetryPlane boundaries to emulate an infinitely wide channel.
In system/blockMeshDict, define left and right patches with type symmetryPlane and list their faces accordingly.
Apply noSlip velocity boundary condition on all solid walls including the floor, cube surfaces, and the top wall.
Apply a fixedValue inlet velocity boundary condition on inlet.
Apply a fixedValue outlet pressure boundary condition on outlet with zeroGradient velocity at outlet.
Set pressure to zeroGradient on all noSlip walls and symmetryPlane patches.
Set velocity to symmetryPlane on left and right.
Set turbulence quantities k and epsilon with appropriate inlet fixedValue based on chosen turbulence intensity and length scale, and use wall functions on solid walls.
Use symmetryPlane boundary conditions for k and epsilon on left and right.
Run simpleFoam until residuals and monitored integral quantities indicate convergence.
