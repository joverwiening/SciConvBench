Assume OpenFOAM Foundation (OF) v10 compatibility.
Use simpleFoam for an incompressible, steady-state RANS simulation.
Model only the bottom half of a 2D channel and apply symmetry at the centerline to represent the full channel.
Keep the frontAndBack patches as type empty in system/blockMeshDict to enforce 2D.
In system/blockMeshDict, set the centerline patch type to symmetry.
In all field files under 0/ (at minimum U and p), set the centerline patch boundary condition type to symmetry.
In 0/U, do not use noSlip on the centerline patch.
In 0/p, set the centerline patch to symmetry.
In constant/turbulenceProperties, use simulationType RAS.
In constant/RASProperties, use the kOmegaSST turbulence model with turbulence on and printCoeffs on.
At the inlet, apply a fixedValue uniform (plug) velocity profile for U.
At the outlet, apply a zeroGradient condition for U and a fixedValue reference pressure for p.
At the bottomWall, apply noSlip for U and zeroGradient for p.
For turbulence fields (k, omega, nut), apply standard wall functions at bottomWall, symmetry at centerline, and appropriate inlet/outlet conditions consistent with a uniform inlet (fixedValue at inlet, zeroGradient at outlet).
