I recommend using a mesh resolution of approximately 0.005 m (5 mm) for this fluidized bed simulation. This resolution provides a good balance between computational efficiency and capturing the particle-fluid interactions in the column. The mesh size is small enough to resolve the particle dynamics and fluid flow patterns while not being excessively computationally expensive.


The simulation will be a three-phase fluidized bed in a cylindrical column with the following specifications:

Geometry:
Column diameter is 0.1 m.
Column height is 1 m.
Mesh resolution is 0.005 m (5 mm).

Phases:
Phases are air (gas), water (liquid), and sand (solid particles).
Solver is twoPhaseEulerFoam.

Phase Properties:
Air treated as a perfect gas.
Water treated as an incompressible liquid.
Sand as a constant density solid with 500 micron particle diameter.

Initial Conditions:
Bottom 0.2 m contains settled sand.
Sand volume fraction (alpha.sand) is 0.6.
Water volume fraction (alpha.water) is 0.4.
Remaining column height filled with water (alpha.water = 1).

Boundary Conditions:
Gravity acts in negative y-direction (0 -9.81 0).
Air injected from bottom inlet at 0.5 m/s.
Gidaspow drag model used for phase interactions.

Simulation Parameters:
Transient simulation.
Total simulation time is 10 seconds.
