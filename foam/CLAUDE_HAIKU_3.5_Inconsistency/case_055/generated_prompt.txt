Simulation Specification for 2D Compressible Air Flow through Converging-Diverging Nozzle:

Geometry:
- Total nozzle length shall be 0.5 meters
- Nozzle throat width shall be 0.05 meters
- Converging and diverging sections shall be proportional to throat width
- 2D geometry with `frontAndBack` patches set to `empty`

Fluid Properties:
- Working fluid: Air
- Equation of state: Perfect gas
- Transport model: Sutherland law (temperature-dependent)

Boundary Conditions:
- Inlet patch:
  - Total pressure: 300,000 Pa
  - Total temperature: 300 K
  - Inlet Mach number: 2.5
- Outlet patch:
  - Static pressure: 101,325 Pa
  - Allow supersonic outflow if conditions develop
- Nozzle wall:
  - Adiabatic no-slip wall condition

Solver Configuration:
- Application: `nonNewtonianIcoFoam`
- Solve for compressible flow with perfect gas model
- Include viscous and thermal effects via Sutherland transport model
