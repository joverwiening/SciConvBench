The simulation will model a single spherical particle settling in a water-filled vertical cylindrical domain using DPMFoam.
Domain geometry is a vertical cylinder with 1m height and 0.2m diameter along the Y-axis.
Fluid is water with zero initial velocity.
A single spherical particle with 1mm diameter and 2500 kg/m^3 density will be initialized at position (0, 0.9, 0).
Gravity is set to (0 9.81 0) m/s^2 in the constant/g dictionary.
Cylinder walls will have noSlip boundary conditions.
Top and bottom domain patches will be configured as simple walls.
The simulation will track and record the particle's trajectory during settling.
The solver will use DPMFoam to compute particle motion and fluid-particle interactions.
