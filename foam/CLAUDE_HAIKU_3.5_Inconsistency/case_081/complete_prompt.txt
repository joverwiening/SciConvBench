Simulate a 2D gas-liquid bubble column using the `twoPhaseEulerFoam` solver. The domain is a rectangle of 0.5 m width and 2 m height. The two phases are air and water. The simulation is transient. At the bottom 'inlet' boundary, both air and water are introduced. The boundary conditions in the `0/alpha.air` file for the inlet patch should be `type fixedValue; value uniform 0.8;`. The boundary conditions in the `0/alpha.water` file for the same inlet patch should be `type fixedValue; value uniform 0.2;`. The velocity of air at the inlet is (0 0.2 0) m/s and water is (0 0.01 0) m/s. The top boundary is an outlet, and the sides are no-slip walls.
