Specification for 2D Gas-Liquid Bubble Column Simulation using multiphaseEulerFoam:

Computational Domain:
- Rectangular domain dimensions: x [0, 0.15] m, y [0, 1.00] m, z [0, 0.10] m
- Height along +y direction, thin in z direction
- Mesh: Structured hex mesh with 25 × 75 × 1 cells
- Mesh grading: simpleGrading (1 1 1)

Phases:
- Continuous phase: Water
  - Equation of state: heRhoThermo with rPolynomial
  - Molecular weight: 18
  - Specific heat capacity (Cv): 4195
  - Dynamic viscosity: 3.645e-4 Pa·s
  - Prandtl number: 2.289

- Dispersed phase: Air
  - Equation of state: heRhoThermo with perfectGas
  - Molecular weight: 28.9
  - Specific heat capacity (Cp): 1007
  - Dynamic viscosity: 1.84e-05 Pa·s
  - Prandtl number: 0.7

Simulation Parameters:
- Gravity: (0 -9.81 0) m/s²
- Bubble size: Air diameter 3e-3 m, Water diameter 1e-4 m
- Momentum transport: Laminar for both phases
- Multiphase interaction:
  - Phases: (air water)
  - Reference phase: Water
  - Residual alpha: 1e-6 for both phases
- Blending: Linear scheme
  - Fully continuous alpha: 0.7
  - Partly continuous alpha: 0.3 (0.5 for drag)
- Surface tension: Constant, sigma = 0.07
- Drag model: SchillerNaumann for all configurations
- Virtual mass: Constant coefficient, Cvm = 0.5
- Heat transfer: RanzMarshall model, residual alpha 1e-4

Initial Conditions (t = 0):
- Water velocity: Uniform (0 0 0)
- Air velocity: Uniform (0 0.1 0)
- Water volume fraction: Uniform 1
- Air volume fraction: Uniform 0
- Pressure (p_rgh): Uniform 1e5
- Air temperature: Uniform 300 K
- Water temperature: Uniform 350 K
- Phase distribution: Air volume fraction 0, water volume fraction 1 in box (0 0 0) to (0.15 0.701 0.1)

Boundary Conditions:
- Inlet:
  - Air velocity: Fixed (0 0.1 0)
  - Water velocity: Fixed (0 0 0)
  - Air volume fraction: Fixed 0.5
  - Water volume fraction: Fixed 0.5
  - Air temperature: Fixed 300 K
  - Water temperature: Fixed 350 K

- Walls:
  - Air velocity: Fixed (0 0 0)
  - Water velocity: Fixed (0 0 0)
  - Air volume fraction: Zero gradient
  - Water volume fraction: Zero gradient
  - Pressure (p_rgh): Fixed flux pressure
  - Air temperature: Zero gradient
  - Water temperature: Zero gradient

Time Controls:
- Start time: 0 s
- End time: 20 s
- Time step (deltaT): 0.005 s
- Adjust time step: No
- Maximum Courant number: 0.5
- Maximum time step: 1 s
- Write interval: 1 s
