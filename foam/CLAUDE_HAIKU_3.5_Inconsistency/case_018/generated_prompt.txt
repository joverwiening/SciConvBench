Set up a laminar counter-flow diffusion flame simulation using reactingFoam in OpenFOAM with the following specifications:

Computational Domain:
- x-dimension: 0 to 0.02 m
- y-dimension: -0.01 to 0.01 m
- z-dimension: -0.01 to 0.01 m

Mesh Configuration:
- Mesh resolution: 100 (x) x 40 (y) x 1 (z)
- z-direction boundary condition: empty (2D simulation)

Boundary Conditions:
- Left patch (x=0): Fuel inlet
- Right patch (x=0.02): Air inlet
- Top and bottom: Outlet patches

Inlet Conditions:
- Fuel inlet: Methane (CH4) at 293 K
- Fuel velocity: +0.1 m/s in +x direction
- Air inlet: Composition (O2/N2) at 293 K
- Air composition: Y_O2 = 0.23, balance N2
- Air velocity: +0.1 m/s in +x direction

Initial Conditions:
- Domain temperature: 2000 K

Chemical Mechanism:
- Chemical mechanism: GRI-Mech 3.0

Numerical Settings:
- Chemistry solver: EulerImplicit
- ODE solver: Rosenbrock43
- Transport models: Laminar combustion and momentum

Thermodynamic Models:
- Thermodynamic model: hePsiThermo
- Mixture model: coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture
- Transport model: Sutherland transport
- Thermodynamic data: JANAF tables

Time Stepping:
- Time step (deltaT): 1e-4 s
- End time: 0.5 s
- Write interval: 0.05 s
