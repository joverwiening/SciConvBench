The simulation will model a three-phase immiscible fluid separation in a horizontal gravity separator with the following specifications:

Domain Dimensions: 5 m (length) x 1 m (width) x 1 m (height)
Solver: `interFoam`
Phases: Oil, Water, Gas
Gravity Vector: (0 -9.81 0) m/s²
Initial Condition: Stratified mixture
Volume Fraction Field: `alpha.water`

Phases to be defined in `transportProperties` with:
Density specifications for oil, water, and gas
Viscosity specifications for oil, water, and gas

Initial fluid stratification to be configured in `setFieldsDict`

Simulation Objective: Track interface dynamics between three immiscible fluids during gravitational separation

Simulation Method: Volume of Fluid (VOF) method for interface tracking

Coordinate System: Metric/SI units
Simulation Domain: Horizontal orientation with gravitational settling
