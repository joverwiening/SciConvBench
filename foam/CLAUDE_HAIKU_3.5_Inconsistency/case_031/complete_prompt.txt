Set up a 2D incompressible laminar flow simulation in a rectangular channel using the icoFoam solver. The channel is 0.5 m long (x-direction) and 0.1 m high (y-direction). The mesh should be one cell thick in the z-direction. In blockMeshDict, define the geometry and patches: inlet, outlet, topWall, bottomWall, and frontAndBack. For the frontAndBack patches, specify 'type empty;'. The simulation should model Poiseuille flow. In the '0/U' file, set a parabolic inlet velocity profile with a maximum of 1 m/s, a zeroGradient outlet, and noSlip conditions on topWall and bottomWall. For the frontAndBack patches, apply the 'empty' boundary condition. In '0/p', use fixedValue 0 at the outlet and zeroGradient on the inlet and walls, and 'empty' on the frontAndBack patches.
