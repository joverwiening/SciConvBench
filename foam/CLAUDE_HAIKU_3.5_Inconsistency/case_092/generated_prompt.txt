Simulation Specification for Compressible Supersonic Flow over a Wedge:

Solver: Use `rhoPimpleFoam` for compressible, transient supersonic flow simulation.

Geometry:
- 2D wedge geometry with 10-degree wedge angle
- Mesh extruded one cell in z-direction
- `frontAndBack` boundaries set to `empty`

Freestream Conditions:
- Velocity: `U = (686 0 0) m/s`
- Temperature: `T = 293 K`
- Pressure: `p = 101325 Pa`
- Mach number: 2.0

Thermophysical Properties (`constant/thermophysicalProperties`):
- Use `hePsiThermo` thermodynamic model
- Equation of state: `perfectGas`
- Transport model: `sutherland`

Boundary Conditions:
- Inlet: Fixed values for velocity, pressure, and temperature
- Outlet: `waveTransmissive` for pressure and temperature, `zeroGradient` for velocity
- Wedge surface and top boundary: Slip wall
- Front and back boundaries: `empty`

Air Properties:
- Treat air as a perfect gas
- Simulate compressible, supersonic flow with oblique shock formation

Simulation Goal: Analyze compressible, supersonic flow characteristics over a 10-degree wedge.
