Simulation Specification for 2D Thermal Plume using buoyantPimpleFoam:

Domain Geometry:
- Create a 2D rectangular domain with dimensions 0.1 m x 0.5 m (width x height)
- Origin located at bottom center of domain

Boundary Conditions:
- Bottom center patch: Fixed temperature at 500 K
- All other boundaries: Open to atmosphere
- Initial temperature field: Uniform 300 K

Fluid Properties (constant/thermophysicalProperties):
- Thermomodel: hePsiThermo
- Mixture: Pure mixture
- Transport model: Sutherland
- Equation of State: Boussinesq
- Boussinesq Coefficients:
  - Reference Temperature (T0): 300 K
  - Thermal Expansion Coefficient (beta): 0.00333
  - Reference Density (rho0): 1.176 kg/m³
- Viscosity Sutherland Coefficients:
  - As: 1.4792e-06
  - Ts: 116

Gravity Vector: (0 -9.81 0) m/s²

Mesh Resolution:
- Use a uniform mesh with cell size of 0.002 m
- Total cells: Approximately 5000 (50 x 100)

Solver: buoyantPimpleFoam

Simulation Goal: Observe thermal plume development and density variations as hot air rises
