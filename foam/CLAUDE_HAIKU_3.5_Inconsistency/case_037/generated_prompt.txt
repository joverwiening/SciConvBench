Simulate steady, incompressible, laminar flow of water through a 2D channel with the following specifications:

Channel geometry:
- Total length: 10 units
- Channel width: 1 unit
- Obstacle: Rectangular block
- Obstacle position: Centered at x = 3.5 units from inlet
- Obstacle width: 0.3 units
- Obstacle height: 0.6 units (centered vertically in the channel)

Fluid properties:
- Fluid: Water
- Kinematic viscosity (nu): 1e-6 m^2/s

Numerical setup:
- Mesh: 2D mesh extruded one cell in spanwise direction
- Boundary conditions for velocity (`0/U`):
  - Inlet: `fixedValue uniform (0.1 0 0)`
  - Outlet: `zeroGradient`
  - Walls and obstacle: `noSlip`
  - Front and back boundaries: `empty`

Numerical setup for pressure (`0/p`):
  - Inlet: `zeroGradient`
  - Outlet: `fixedValue uniform 0`
  - Walls and obstacle: `zeroGradient`
  - Front and back boundaries: `empty`

Solver configuration:
- Application: `solidDisplacementFoam`
- Flow regime: Steady, incompressible, laminar
