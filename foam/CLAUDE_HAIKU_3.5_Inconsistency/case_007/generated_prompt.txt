Specification for 2D Gas-Liquid Bubble Column Simulation using multiphaseEulerFoam:

Computational Domain:
- Rectangular domain dimensions: x [0, 0.15] m, y [0, 1.00] m, z [0, 0.10] m
- Height along +y direction, thin in z direction
- Mesh: Structured hex mesh with 25 × 75 × 1 cells
- Mesh grading: simpleGrading (1 1 1)
- Mesh configuration: blockMeshDict with inlet, outlet, and wall patches
- Z-direction treatment: Empty faces for 2D simulation

Phases:
- Continuous phase: Water
  - Equation of state: heRhoThermo with rPolynomial
  - Molecular weight: 18
  - Specific heat capacity (Cv): 4195
  - Dynamic viscosity: 3.645e-4 Pa·s
  - Prandtl number: 2.289
  - Bubble diameter: 1e-4 m
  - Initial velocity: Uniform (0 0 0)
  - Initial volume fraction: 1
  - Initial temperature: 350 K

- Dispersed phase: Air
  - Equation of state: heRhoThermo with perfectGas
  - Molecular weight: 28.9
  - Specific heat capacity (Cp): 1007
  - Dynamic viscosity: 1.84e-05 Pa·s
  - Prandtl number: 0.7
  - Bubble diameter: 3e-3 m
  - Initial velocity: Uniform (0 0.1 0)
  - Initial volume fraction: 0
  - Initial temperature: 300 K

Multiphase Interaction:
- Reference phase: Water
- Residual alpha: 1e-6 for both phases
- Blending: Linear scheme
  - minFullyContinuousAlpha: 0.7
  - minPartlyContinuousAlpha: 0.3 (default), 0.5 (drag)
- Surface tension: Constant, sigma = 0.07
- Drag model: SchillerNaumann for all configurations
- Virtual mass: constantCoefficient, Cvm = 0.5
- Heat transfer: RanzMarshall model, residualAlpha 1e-4

Momentum Transport:
- Simulation type: Laminar for both phases

Initial Conditions:
- Pressure (p_rgh): Uniform 1e5 Pa
- Phase distribution:

  - Box region (0 0 0) to (0.15 0.701 0.1): Air volume fraction 0, Water volume fraction 1

- Walls:
