Set up a 2D, incompressible, steady-state simulation of airflow over a symmetric airfoil. Use the simpleFoam solver. The domain should be a C-grid topology around the airfoil. The mesh is one cell thick in the z-direction. In the `system/blockMeshDict` file, define the front and back patches for this 2D simulation as empty. Specifically, in the `boundary` entry, use:

```
    frontAndBack
    {
        type            empty;
        faces
        (
            (0 4 7 3)
            (1 2 6 5)
            // ... other faces for this patch
        );
    }
```

For boundary conditions in the `0/U` file, apply a uniform inlet velocity of (10 0 0) m/s, a zero-gradient outlet, a `noSlip` condition on the airfoil surface, and `empty` on the `frontAndBack` patches. For pressure in `0/p`, use `zeroGradient` for the inlet and airfoil, `fixedValue uniform 0` at the outlet, and `empty` on the `frontAndBack` patches.
