I'll assume a standard industrial pipe diameter of 0.1 meters (100 mm), which is a typical size for natural gas transmission pipelines. This diameter allows for meaningful flow simulation while being representative of real-world applications.


The simulation will model steady-state, compressible natural gas flow through a horizontal pipe with the following specifications:

Pipe diameter is 0.1 meters.
Inlet total pressure is 2e5 Pa.
Outlet static pressure is 1e5 Pa.
Gas is modeled as a perfect gas.
Porous zone will be defined in the fvOptions dictionary with Darcy-Forchheimer coefficients.
Solver to be used is porousSimpleFoam.
Kinematic viscosity 'nu' will be defined in transportProperties dictionary.
Pipe orientation is horizontal.
Pipe material and surface roughness will follow standard industrial steel pipe characteristics.
