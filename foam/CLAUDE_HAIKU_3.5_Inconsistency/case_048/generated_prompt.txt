Simulation of Buoyancy-Driven Heat Transfer in 2D Channel using OpenFOAM's buoyantPimpleFoam:

Geometry Configuration:
The simulation will use a 2D channel geometry.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction.

Thermal Boundary Conditions:
Bottom wall temperature is set to 400 K using fixedValue boundary condition.
Top wall temperature is set to 300 K.
Initial fluid domain temperature is 300 K.

Boundary Condition Details:
Bottom wall: `fixedValue` at 400 K
Top wall: `fixedValue` at 300 K
Initial domain temperature: Uniform 300 K

Solver:
Use buoyantPimpleFoam solver for the simulation.

Thermal Physics:
The simulation will model buoyancy-driven heat transfer.
Heat transfer is driven by temperature difference between bottom and top walls.
Top wall is adiabatic (zero heat flux).

Numerical Setup:
Ensure proper mesh resolution to capture thermal boundary layers.
Use appropriate numerical schemes for heat transfer and buoyancy effects.
