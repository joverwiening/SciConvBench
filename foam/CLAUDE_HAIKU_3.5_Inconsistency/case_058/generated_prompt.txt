Perform a transient two-phase computational fluid dynamics simulation of high-pressure gas injection into water using OpenFOAM's `interFoam` solver.

Simulation Parameters:
The simulation will model a gas jet injection into a water tank with the following specifications:

Gas Phase Characteristics:
Use the `perfectGas` equation of state for the gas phase.
Gas inlet velocity at Mach 0.8.
Initial gas temperature set to 293.15 K (20°C).
Initial gas inlet pressure of 500 kPa (gauge).
Gas inlet nozzle diameter of 5 mm.

Water Phase Characteristics:
Model water phase as an incompressible fluid.

Computational Domain:
Tank dimensions: 0.1 m x 0.1 m x 0.3 m rectangular geometry.

Simulation Objectives:
Observe and analyze gas jet penetration and expansion dynamics in the water tank.
Capture compressibility effects of the high-velocity gas injection.

Solver Configuration:
Use OpenFOAM's `interFoam` two-phase flow solver.
Conduct a transient simulation to track time-dependent flow behavior.
