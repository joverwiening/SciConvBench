The specification for the solar chimney buoyant flow simulation is as follows:

The simulation will use OpenFOAM's `buoyantSimpleFoam` solver.
The computational domain represents a solar chimney with a height of 8 meters.
The chimney will have a square cross-section with a width of 1 meter.
The bottom boundary will be heated to create buoyancy-driven flow.
The inlet temperature will be set at 320 K (20 K above ambient).
The ambient air temperature is set at 300 K.
Gravity is defined in the `constant/g` file.
The Boussinesq approximation is used for modeling density variations.
Thermophysical properties are defined in `constant/thermophysicalProperties`.
The reference temperature is 300 K.
The thermal expansion coefficient (beta) is 0.00333 K^-1.
The reference density is 1.176 kg/m³.
Specific heat capacity is 1005 J/(kg·K).
Molecular viscosity is 1.8e-05 kg/(m·s).
Prandtl number is 0.7.
The simulation will model steady-state conditions.
The air is modeled as an ideal gas mixture with a molecular weight of 28.9 g/mol.
