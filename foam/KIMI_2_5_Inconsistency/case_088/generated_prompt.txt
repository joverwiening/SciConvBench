The geometry is a vertical cylindrical pipe with a diameter of 2 meters and a height of 10 meters, representing a typical small-scale solar chimney configuration.

The circumferential direction contains 60 cells uniformly distributed. The axial direction contains 100 cells with uniform spacing. The total cell count is approximately 180,000 cells.

The boundary conditions specify a fixed temperature at the bottom inlet representing the heated absorber surface. The top outlet uses a pressure outlet with zero gradient for temperature. The sidewalls use no-slip velocity conditions with either adiabatic or specified heat flux conditions depending on the desired physics.
