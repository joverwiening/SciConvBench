Simulate a 2D dam break case using the interFoam solver.
The domain is a rectangle with dimensions 0.6m (length) x 0.4m (height), with the height oriented along the Y-axis.
Use blockMesh to create the geometry with a single block.
The initial condition, defined in setFieldsDict, is a column of water occupying the region x < 0.15m and y < 0.3m.
The rest of the domain is air.
The two phases are defined in transportProperties as water and air with standard properties.
The gravity vector in constant/g is set to `g (0 0 -9.81);`.
For boundary conditions in the 0/ directory: U has `noSlip` on walls and `pressureInletOutletVelocity` on the top 'atmosphere' patch.
p_rgh has `fixedFluxPressure` on walls and `totalPressure` on the atmosphere.
alpha.water has `zeroGradient` on walls and `inletOutlet` on the atmosphere.
The front and back patches are of type `empty` for a 2D simulation.
The simulation runs for 2 seconds.
