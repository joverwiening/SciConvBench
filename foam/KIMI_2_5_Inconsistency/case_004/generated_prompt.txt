The specification describes a complete 2D gas-liquid bubble column simulation using multiphaseEulerFoam with the following configuration:

The computational domain is a rectangular box with x ranging from 0 to 0.15 m, y from 0 to 1.00 m, and z from 0 to 0.10 m, with the height oriented along the positive y direction and a thin dimension in z. The mesh consists of 25 by 75 by 1 structured hexahedral cells with uniform grading (1 1 1) in all directions. The blockMeshDict defines three patch types: inlet, outlet, and walls, with the z-direction faces named frontAndBack assigned as wall boundaries.

The continuous liquid phase is water modeled with heRhoThermo and an rPolynomial equation of state using coefficients 0.001278, -2.1055e-06, 3.9689e-09, 4.3772e-13, and -2.0225e-16. Water has molecular weight 18, specific heat capacity Cv of 4195, dynamic viscosity 3.645e-4 Pa·s, and Prandtl number 2.289. The dispersed gas phase is air modeled with heRhoThermo and perfectGas equation of state, molecular weight 28.9, specific heat capacity Cp of 1007, dynamic viscosity 1.84e-05 Pa·s, and Prandtl number 0.7. Gravity is set to (0 -9.81 0) m/s².

Bubble size models specify isothermal diameter d0 of 3e-3 m for air and constant diameter d of 1e-4 m for water. Momentum transport is laminar for both phases with simulationType set to laminar in the respective momentumTransport files. The phaseProperties file defines phases as (air water) with water as the reference phase and residualAlpha of 1e-6 for both phases. Blending uses a linear scheme with minFullyContinuousAlpha 0.7 and minPartlyContinuousAlpha 0.3 for default blending, or 0.5 for drag blending. Surface tension between air and water is constant at 0.07 N/m. The drag model uses SchillerNaumann for air_dispersedIn_water, water_dispersedIn_air, and air_segregatedWith_water configurations. Virtual mass uses constantCoefficient with Cvm equal to 0.5 for both directions. Heat transfer employs the RanzMarshall model for both directions with residualAlpha of 1e-4.

Initial conditions at time zero set water velocity to uniform (0 0 0), air velocity to uniform (0 0.1 0), water volume fraction to uniform 1, pressure p_rgh to uniform 1e5 Pa, air temperature to uniform 300 K, and water temperature to uniform 350 K. The phase distribution uses default field values where air volume fraction is 1 and water volume fraction is 0, except within a box region from (0 0 0) to (0.15 0.701 0.1) where air volume fraction is 0 and water volume fraction is 1.

Boundary conditions at the inlet set air velocity to fixedValue (0 0.1 0), water velocity to fixedValue (0 0 0), air volume fraction to fixedValue 0.5, water volume fraction to fixedValue 0.5, air temperature to fixedValue 300, and water temperature to fixedValue 350. Wall boundaries set air velocity to fixedValue (0 0 0), water velocity to fixedValue (0 0 0), air volume fraction to zeroGradient, water volume fraction to zeroGradient, pressure p_rgh to fixedFluxPressure, air temperature to zeroGradient, and water temperature to zeroGradient.

Time controls start at 0 seconds and end at 20 seconds with a fixed time step of 0.005 seconds. The adjustTimeStep option is set to no, with maxCo of 0.5, maxDeltaT of 1 second, and writeInterval of 1 second.
