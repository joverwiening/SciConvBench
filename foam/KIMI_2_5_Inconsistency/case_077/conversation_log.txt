Q: I notice a critical contradiction in your configuration. You specified `equationOfState Boussinesq` in the thermophysical properties, but you also stated that "the simulation is expected to show large density variations as the hot air rises."

The Boussinesq equation of state is specifically designed for **small** density variations—it assumes constant density except in the buoyancy term. For large density variations, you would need a compressible equation of state like `perfectGas` or `PengRobinsonGas`.

Which behavior do you actually need: the Boussinesq approximation (small density variations, incompressible-like) or a fully compressible approach that captures large density variations?
A: Use `perfectGas` equation of state with `heRhoThermo`. The complete requirement specifies this configuration to capture large density variations.
