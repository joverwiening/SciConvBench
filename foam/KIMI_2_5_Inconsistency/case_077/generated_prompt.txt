Simulate a transient thermal plume rising in a 2D domain using buoyantPimpleFoam.

The domain has a small hot patch at the bottom center held at 500 K, while the surrounding fluid is initially at 300 K.

Gravity is set to (0 -9.81 0).

In constant/thermophysicalProperties, configure the fluid properties as follows: thermoType heRhoThermo; mixture pureMixture; transport sutherland; thermo hConst; equationOfState perfectGas; specie hConst; energy sensibleEnthalpy.

The Sutherland model for viscosity uses As 1.4792e-06 and Ts 116.

The initial temperature field is uniform 300.

The hot patch boundary condition is fixedValue uniform 500.

All other boundaries are open to the atmosphere.
