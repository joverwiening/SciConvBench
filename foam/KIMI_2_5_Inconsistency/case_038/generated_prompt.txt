The simulation models thermo-elastic deformation of a solid aluminum block using a thermal-stress coupling approach in OpenFOAM.

The aluminum block has dimensions 0.1 m x 0.05 m x 0.02 m (length x width x height) in the x, y, z directions respectively.

The fixedFace is the x-min face (at x = 0 m) where displacement is fully constrained.

The heatedFace is the x-max face (at x = 0.1 m) where temperature is elevated above reference.

Material properties for aluminum are: Young's modulus E = 69e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio nu = 0.33, density rho = 2700 kg/m^3, thermal expansion coefficient alpha = 2.3e-5 1/K, reference temperature T0 = 300 K.

The temperature field T is prescribed as a boundary condition rather than solved, with heatedFace held at 400 K and all other faces at zeroGradient.

The displacement field U is solved with fixedValue (0 0 0) on the fixedFace and zeroGradient on all other faces.

The solver application in controlDict is set to solidDisplacementFoam (not buoyantPimpleFoam, as buoyantPimpleFoam is a fluid solver for buoyant flows; solidDisplacementFoam is the appropriate OpenFOAM solver for linear elastic thermal stress analysis).

The simulation computes steady-state displacement and stress fields resulting from thermal expansion due to the 100 K temperature differential across the block length.
