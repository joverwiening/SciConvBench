The simulation shall use a 2D axisymmetric wedge mesh with a 5-degree angle created via blockMeshDict.

The pipe geometry shall have a radius of 0.05 meters and a length of 1.0 meter, following best practices for pipe flow simulations where the length is sufficient for flow development.

The simpleFoam solver shall be used for steady-state incompressible turbulent flow.

The constant/polyMesh/boundary file shall define five patches: inlet as type patch, outlet as type patch, wall as type wall, front as type symmetryPlane, and back as type symmetryPlane.

The velocity field U shall use a swirling inlet condition with a maximum tangential velocity magnitude of 1.0 m/s, representing moderate swirl intensity typical of industrial applications.

The axial velocity magnitude at the inlet shall be 1.0 m/s, matching the tangential component to produce a swirl number of approximately 1.0.

The wall patch shall use a noSlip boundary condition for velocity.

The outlet patch shall use a zeroGradient boundary condition for velocity.

The front and back patches shall use symmetryPlane boundary conditions for velocity.

The kinematic viscosity nu shall be set to 1e-05 m^2/s in the transportProperties file, corresponding to air at standard conditions, a common choice for swirling flow studies.

The pressure field p shall use zeroGradient at the inlet, fixedValue of 0 at the outlet, and zeroGradient at the wall, front, and back patches.

The turbulence model shall be selected appropriately for swirling flows, with k and epsilon or omega fields initialized and bounded to ensure convergence.
