Set up a 2D dam break simulation using the `interFoam` solver.

The computational domain has dimensions of 1 meter in length (x-direction) and 1 meter in height (y-direction).

Initialize a water column in the region where x is greater than 0 and less than 0.2, and y is greater than 0 and less than 0.5.

Initialize the remainder of the domain with air.

Configure the simulation to model the collapse of the water column under the influence of gravity.

In the `constant/g` file, define the gravitational acceleration vector as `g (0 0 -9.81);`.

Use the `setFieldsDict` utility to initialize the `alpha.water` volume fraction field to a value of 1 within the water column region and 0 in all other regions.

Apply the `pressureInletOutletVelocity` boundary condition to the top boundary to represent an open atmosphere.

Apply the `noSlip` boundary condition to all remaining boundaries (left, right, and bottom walls) to represent solid walls.
