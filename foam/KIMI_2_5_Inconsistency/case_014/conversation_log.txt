Q: I need to clarify a critical issue with your request.

pimpleFoam is a transient solver for incompressible, isothermal flow of Newtonian fluids on a moving mesh. It solves the Navier-Stokes equations for fluid dynamics, not structural mechanics.

Your request describes a structural stress analysis with:
- Displacement fields
- Traction boundary conditions
- Material properties (Young's modulus, Poisson's ratio)
- Plane stress analysis

These are solid mechanics problems, not fluid dynamics.

OpenFOAM has separate solvers for structural analysis, such as:
- solidDisplacementFoam (linear elasticity)
- solidEquilibriumDisplacementFoam
- thermoSolidDisplacementFoam (for coupled thermal-structural analysis)

Which structural solver would you like to use instead of pimpleFoam?
A: Use **solidDisplacementFoam** solver.
