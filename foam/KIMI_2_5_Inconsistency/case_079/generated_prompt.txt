Perform a transient simulation of a Rayleigh-Bénard convection cell using buoyantPimpleFoam.

The domain is a 2D rectangle with height 0.01 m and width 0.02 m (aspect ratio 2:1), which is a standard configuration for studying Rayleigh-Bénard convection.

The fluid is water with the following properties: density 1000 kg/m³, thermal expansion coefficient 2.1e-4 K⁻¹, kinematic viscosity 1.0e-6 m²/s, thermal diffusivity 1.43e-7 m²/s, specific heat capacity 4186 J/(kg·K), and Prandtl number approximately 7. These properties yield a Rayleigh number of approximately 1708, which is just above the critical value for onset of convection, ensuring interesting dynamics without excessive computational cost.

The bottom boundary is fixed at 305 K and the top boundary is fixed at 295 K, giving a temperature difference of 10 K.

The side boundaries are periodic to simulate an infinite horizontal layer.

In constant/thermophysicalProperties, set thermoType to hePsiThermo and equationOfState to Boussinesq with reference density 1000 kg/m³, thermal expansion coefficient 2.1e-4 K⁻¹, and reference temperature 300 K.

In system/fvSchemes, use div(phi,T) Gauss linear for temperature divergence and div(rhoPhi,U) Gauss upwind for velocity divergence as specified.

The simulation end time is 100 seconds, which allows sufficient time for the flow to develop and reach a quasi-steady state.

Use adjustable time step with a maximum CFL number of 1.0 and maximum time step of 0.01 seconds to ensure numerical stability while capturing the transient dynamics.

Set writeInterval to 1 second for regular data output, providing adequate temporal resolution for analysis without excessive storage requirements.

Enable function objects to compute and output the Nusselt number at both horizontal boundaries, monitor heat flux through all boundaries, and generate velocity and temperature field data for visualization.
