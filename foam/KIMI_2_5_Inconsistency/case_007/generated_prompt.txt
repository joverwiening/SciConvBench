The simulation is a 2D gas-liquid bubble column using multiphaseEulerFoam with Euler-Euler two-phase flow.

The computational domain is a rectangular box with x ranging from 0 to 0.15 m, y ranging from 0 to 1.00 m, and z ranging from 0 to 0.10 m, with the height oriented along the positive y direction.

The mesh is structured hexahedral with 25 cells in x, 75 cells in y, and 1 cell in z, using simpleGrading with uniform grading (1 1 1) in all directions.

The blockMeshDict defines three patches: inlet, outlet, and walls, with 2D treatment achieved by using empty patch types for the z-direction faces.

The continuous phase is water modeled with heRhoThermo and rPolynomial equation of state using coefficients 0.001278, -2.1055e-06, 3.9689e-09, 4.3772e-13, -2.0225e-16, molecular weight 18 kg/kmol, specific heat capacity Cv 4195 J/kg/K, dynamic viscosity 3.645e-4 Pa·s, and Prandtl number 2.289.

The dispersed phase is air modeled with heRhoThermo and perfectGas equation of state, molecular weight 28.9 kg/kmol, specific heat capacity Cp 1007 J/kg/K, dynamic viscosity 1.84e-05 Pa·s, and Prandtl number 0.7.

Gravity is set to (0 -9.81 0) m/s².

Bubble size models use isothermal diameter with d0 = 3e-3 m for air and constant diameter d = 1e-4 m for water.

Momentum transport is laminar for both phases with simulationType set to laminar in the respective momentumTransport files.

Phase properties define phases as (air water) with water as the reference phase and residualAlpha of 1e-6 for both phases.

Blending uses linear scheme with minFullyContinuousAlpha 0.7 and minPartlyContinuousAlpha 0.3 for default blending, and minFullyContinuousAlpha 0.7 with minPartlyContinuousAlpha 0.5 for drag blending.

Surface tension between air and water is constant with sigma = 0.07 N/m.

Drag model uses SchillerNaumann for air_dispersedIn_water, water_dispersedIn_air, and air_segregatedWith_water configurations.

Virtual mass uses constantCoefficient model with Cvm = 0.5 for both air and water as dispersed phases.

Heat transfer uses RanzMarshall model for both directions with residualAlpha 1e-4.

Initial conditions at time 0 set water velocity to uniform (0 0 0), air velocity to uniform (0 0.1 0), water volume fraction to uniform 1, pressure p_rgh to uniform 1e5 Pa, air temperature to uniform 300 K, and water temperature to uniform 350 K.

Phase distribution uses default values where air volume fraction is 1 and water volume fraction is 0, except within a box region from (0 0 0) to (0.15 0.701 0.1) where air volume fraction is 0 and water volume fraction is 1.

Outlet boundary conditions set air velocity to fixedValue (0 0.1 0), water velocity to fixedValue (0 0 0), air volume fraction to fixedValue 0.5, water volume fraction to fixedValue 0.5, air temperature to fixedValue 300 K, and water temperature to fixedValue 350 K.

Wall boundary conditions set air velocity to fixedValue (0 0 0), water velocity to fixedValue (0 0 0), air volume fraction to zeroGradient, water volume fraction to zeroGradient, pressure p_rgh to fixedFluxPressure, air temperature to zeroGradient, and water temperature to zeroGradient.

Time control parameters set startTime to 0, endTime to 20 s, deltaT to 0.005 s, adjustTimeStep to no, maxCo to 0.5, maxDeltaT to 1 s, and writeInterval to 1 s.
