The simulation shall use the simpleFoam solver for steady-state incompressible turbulent flow.

The turbulence model shall be the standard k-epsilon RANS model.

The fluid shall be water with a kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m²/s, specified in the transportProperties dictionary.

The pipe geometry shall have a diameter of 0.1 m.

The inlet velocity shall be 1 m/s, specified as a uniform fixedValue boundary condition at the inlet patch.

The initial condition for turbulent kinetic energy k in the 0/k file shall have internalField set to uniform 0.

The initial condition for turbulent dissipation rate epsilon in the 0/epsilon file shall have internalField set to uniform 0.

The wall boundary condition for k shall use kqRWallFunction with a value of uniform 0.

The wall boundary condition for epsilon shall use epsilonWallFunction with a value of uniform 0.

The inlet boundary condition for k shall be calculated from the turbulence intensity and inlet velocity, or set to a small non-zero value to avoid division by zero, typically using turbulentIntensityKineticEnergyInlet or a fixedValue of approximately 0.00375 m²/s² based on 5% turbulence intensity.

The inlet boundary condition for epsilon shall be calculated from k and a turbulent length scale, or set using turbulentMixingLengthDissipationRateInlet with a mixing length of 0.007 m (0.07 times the hydraulic diameter), yielding approximately 0.00014 m²/s³.

The outlet boundary condition for k shall use zeroGradient.

The outlet boundary condition for epsilon shall use zeroGradient.

The pressure boundary condition at the inlet shall use zeroGradient.

The pressure boundary condition at the outlet shall use fixedValue uniform 0.

The wall boundary condition for velocity shall use noSlip.

The wall boundary condition for pressure shall use zeroGradient.

The fvSchemes dictionary shall use steadyState ddt scheme, Gauss linear divergence schemes, and linear or limitedLinear interpolation.

The fvSolution dictionary shall use GAMG or PCG for pressure and smoothSolver for velocity, turbulence, and temperature with appropriate tolerances and relaxation factors.
