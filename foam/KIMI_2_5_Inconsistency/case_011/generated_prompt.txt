The multiphase sludge settling simulation shall be configured as follows.

Use the driftFluxFoam solver for the simulation.

Employ RAS turbulence modeling with the buoyantKEpsilon turbulence model.

The domain geometry is defined by the provided blockMeshDict with convertToMeters set to 1.

The mesh consists of two blocks: lower block with 200x4x1 cells spanning y=0 to y=0.1, and upper block with 200x36x1 cells spanning y=0.1 to y=1.

Initial conditions shall be set as follows: uniform velocity of 0.0191 m/s in the x-direction, sludge volume fraction (alpha.sludge) of 0.001, turbulent kinetic energy k=0.00015 m²/s², and turbulent dissipation rate epsilon=1.50919e-06 m²/s³.

Boundary conditions shall be configured as follows: inlet patch with fixedValue velocity 0.0191 m/s and fixedValue alpha.sludge 0.001, outlet patch with fixedValue pressure 0 and pressureInletOutletVelocity for velocity, bottomWall and endWall patches with noSlip velocity and wall functions for k and epsilon, top patch with slip velocity and zeroGradient for alpha.sludge, k, and epsilon, and frontAndBack patches as empty for 2D simulation.

The sludge phase shall be modeled as BinghamPlastic with density 1996 kg/m³, coefficient 0.00023143, exponent 179.26, and Newtonian viscosity 1e-06 m²/s.

The water phase shall be modeled as constant with kinematic viscosity 1.7871e-06 m²/s and density 996 kg/m³.

Gravity shall be set to (0 -9.81 0) m/s².

The relative velocity model shall be simple with Vc coefficient 2.241e-4.

The PIMPLE algorithm shall be used with 3 correctors.

Time stepping shall be adjustable with initial deltaT=0.1 s, maximum Courant number 5, simulation duration from 0 to 6400 seconds, and writeInterval of 50 time units.

The blockMeshDict file shall contain exactly the provided content with vertices, blocks, and boundary definitions as specified.
