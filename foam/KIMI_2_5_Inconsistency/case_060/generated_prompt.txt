The simulation shall be a 2D dam-break problem using the two-phase Eulerian-Eulerian solver.

The domain shall be a rectangular 2D region with dimensions 0.4 m in width (x-direction) and 0.2 m in height (y-direction).

The domain shall be extruded one cell in the z-direction with frontAndBack patches set to empty to enforce 2D behavior.

The water column shall be initialized as a rectangular region with width 0.1 m and height 0.1 m.

The water column shall be positioned with its bottom-left corner at the origin (0, 0, 0), meaning it occupies the region from x = 0 to x = 0.1 m and y = 0 to y = 0.1 m, flush against the left wall and bottom wall.

The setFields utility shall be used to initialize alpha.water to 1 inside the water column region and 0 elsewhere in the domain.

The solver application in system/controlDict shall be set to twoPhaseEulerFoam.

The simulation shall model water collapsing into air with a sharp free surface interface.
