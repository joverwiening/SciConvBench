The simulation shall model a 2D dam break problem using the interFoam solver for two incompressible, immiscible fluids (water and air).

The computational domain shall be a rectangular tank with dimensions 0.5m in length (x-direction) and 0.5m in height (y-direction), extruded by 0.01m in the z-direction to create a single layer of cells suitable for 2D simulation.

Gravity shall act in the negative y-direction with vector g = (0 -9.81 0) m/s^2.

The initial condition shall consist of a water column occupying the region where x < 0.15m with alpha.water = 1, while the remainder of the domain shall be initialized with alpha.water = 0 (air).

The setFieldsDict utility shall be used to establish the initial volume fraction field.

The boundary file in constant/polyMesh/boundary shall define leftWall, rightWall, and lowerWall as type wall.

The boundary file shall define atmosphere as type patch.

The boundary file shall define frontAndBack as type symmetry.

The velocity field U in the 0/ directory shall use noSlip boundary condition on all wall boundaries.

The velocity field U shall use pressureInletOutletVelocity boundary condition on the atmosphere boundary.

The velocity field U shall use symmetry boundary condition on frontAndBack.

The kinematic pressure field p_rgh shall use fixedFluxPressure boundary condition on all wall boundaries.

The kinematic pressure field p_rgh shall use fixedFluxPressure boundary condition on frontAndBack.

The kinematic pressure field p_rgh shall use totalPressure boundary condition on atmosphere.

The volume fraction field alpha.water shall use zeroGradient boundary condition on all wall boundaries.

The volume fraction field alpha.water shall use zeroGradient boundary condition on frontAndBack.

The volume fraction field alpha.water shall use inletOutlet boundary condition on atmosphere.
