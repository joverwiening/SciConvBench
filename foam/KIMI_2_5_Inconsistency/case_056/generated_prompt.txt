The simulation shall be a 3D dam break with three immiscible phases using the twoPhaseEulerFoam solver in OpenFOAM.

The domain is a rectangular tank with dimensions 1.0 m in the x-direction, 0.6 m in the y-direction, and 0.4 m in the z-direction.

Gravity acts in the negative y-direction with magnitude 9.81 m/s^2.

The three phases are defined as air, water, and oil with the following properties:

Air density is 1.225 kg/m^3 and kinematic viscosity is 1.48e-5 m^2/s.

Water density is 1000 kg/m^3 and kinematic viscosity is 1.0e-6 m^2/s.

Oil density is 850 kg/m^3 and kinematic viscosity is 1.0e-5 m^2/s, corresponding to a dynamic viscosity of 8.5e-3 Pa.s.

Surface tension effects are neglected in this simulation.

The initial condition consists of a water column occupying the region from x=0 to x=0.2 m, y=0 to y=0.6 m, and z=0 to z=0.2 m with alpha.water = 1.

An oil layer sits on top of the water column, occupying the region from x=0 to x=0.2 m, y=0 to y=0.6 m, and z=0.2 m to z=0.25 m with alpha.oil = 1.

The remaining domain is filled with air (alpha.air = 1).

All wall boundaries (bottom and sides) use a no-slip velocity boundary condition.

The top boundary (y=0.6 m) uses an atmospheric inlet/outlet condition with totalPressure for pressure and pressureInletOutletVelocity for velocity.

The setFieldsDict utility shall be used to initialize the phase fraction fields alpha.air, alpha.water, and alpha.oil according to the specified regions.

The simulation is transient and shall use appropriate time stepping to capture the dam break dynamics.
