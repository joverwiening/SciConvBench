Simulate transient cooling of a hot block in a 2D sealed room using the compressible solver buoyantPimpleFoam.

The room dimensions are 5 m × 3 m with 2D extrusion of one cell in the z-direction, using empty boundary conditions on frontAndBack patches.

Initialize a 0.5 m × 0.5 m hot air block at 400 K centered in the domain, with surrounding air at 300 K.

All walls are adiabatic with zeroGradient for temperature and noSlip for velocity.

Gravity is set to (0 -9.81 0).

Use a single fluid region with buoyantPimpleFoam, not chtMultiRegionFoam.

In constant/thermophysicalProperties, configure thermoType as hePsiThermo with pureMixture, sutherland transport, janaf thermo, Boussinesq equationOfState, specie, and sensibleEnthalpy energy.

Set the reference temperature TRef to 300 K, representing the ambient air temperature.

Set the reference density rhoRef to 1.161 kg/m³, corresponding to air density at 300 K and atmospheric pressure.

Set the thermal expansion coefficient beta to 0.003333 1/K, calculated as 1/TRef.

Set the kinematic viscosity nu to 1.58e-5 m²/s for air at 300 K.

Set the Prandtl number Pr to 0.71 for air, which with Sutherland transport will determine thermal conductivity through the relationship kappa = mu * Cp / Pr.
