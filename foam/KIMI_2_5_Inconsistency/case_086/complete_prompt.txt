Simulate transient cooling of a hot block in a 2D sealed room using the compressible solver `buoyantPimpleFoam`. Room: 5 m × 3 m (2D extruded one cell in z with `frontAndBack` empty). Initialize a 0.5 m × 0.5 m block at 400 K and the surrounding air at 300 K. All walls are adiabatic (`zeroGradient` for `T`) and `noSlip` for `U`. Gravity is `(0 -9.81 0)`.

In `constant/thermophysicalProperties`, set the equation of state to a compressible perfect gas:
```foam
thermoType
{
    type            hePsiThermo;
    mixture         pureMixture;
    transport       sutherland;
    thermo          janaf;
    equationOfState perfectGas;
    specie          specie;
    energy          sensibleEnthalpy;
}
```
