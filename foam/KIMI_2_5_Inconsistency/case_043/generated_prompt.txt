Simulate heat transfer in a 2D channel with a heated block using the buoyantPimpleFoam solver.

The channel has a length of 0.5 meters and a height of 0.1 meters.

The heated block is centered in the channel with a width of 0.02 meters and a height of 0.04 meters.

The fluid is air with laminar flow regime.

The inlet velocity is 0.1 m/s to maintain laminar flow conditions.

The kinematic viscosity of air is 1.5e-5 m^2/s.

The thermal diffusivity (alpha) of air is 2.0e-5 m^2/s.

The inlet boundary condition is fixedValue with uniform velocity 0.1 m/s and uniform temperature 300 K.

The outlet boundary condition is zeroGradient for velocity and pressure, and zeroGradient for temperature.

The block boundary condition is fixedValue with uniform temperature 400 K and no-slip wall for velocity.

The channel walls boundary condition is zeroGradient for temperature (adiabatic/insulated), no-slip wall for velocity, and zeroGradient for pressure.

The initial temperature field is uniform 300 K throughout the domain.

The simulation uses the buoyantPimpleFoam solver with appropriate thermophysical properties for air including density 1.225 kg/m^3, specific heat capacity 1005 J/kg/K, and Prandtl number 0.71.

Gravity acts in the negative y-direction with magnitude 9.81 m/s^2 to capture buoyancy effects.

The simulation runs until steady state is reached or for a sufficient number of iterations to establish developed flow and thermal fields.
