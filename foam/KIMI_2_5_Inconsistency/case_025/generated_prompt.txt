Simulate 3D, incompressible, turbulent flow around a surface-mounted cube in a channel using OpenFOAM's simpleFoam solver with the standard kEpsilon RANS turbulence model.

Use symmetryPlane boundary conditions on the left and right patches to emulate an infinitely wide channel.

Use noSlip boundary conditions on all solid walls including the floor, cube surfaces, and top wall.

Apply a fixed uniform inlet velocity of 10 m/s on the inlet patch in the streamwise direction.

Set a fixed outlet pressure of 0 Pa (gauge) on the outlet patch.

Use a Reynolds number of 10,000 based on the cube height to ensure fully turbulent flow.

Set the channel height to 0.1 meters and the cube height to 0.02 meters, giving a blockage ratio of 20%.

Set the channel length to 1.0 meter with the cube centered 0.3 meters downstream of the inlet.

Set the spanwise width to 0.2 meters to provide adequate lateral extent for wake development.

Target a wall y+ value between 30 and 100 to ensure proper resolution for the wall function approach used with kEpsilon.

Generate a hexahedral mesh with approximately 50 cells in the streamwise direction, 40 cells in the wall-normal direction, and 30 cells in the spanwise direction, resulting in roughly 60,000 cells total with refinement near walls and the cube.

Ensure the first cell height at all walls yields y+ in the target range of 30-100.

Use second-order discretization schemes for all transported quantities.

Set the turbulence intensity at inlet to 5% and the turbulent length scale to 0.02 meters (cube height).

Run the simulation until residuals drop below 1e-6 or until forces on the cube stabilize to within 0.1% over 100 iterations.
