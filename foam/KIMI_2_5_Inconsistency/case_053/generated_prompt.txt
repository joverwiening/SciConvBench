The simulation shall model the mixing of a non-Newtonian sludge with water in a 2D tank using the interFoam solver.

The two phases shall be named 'water' and 'sludge'.

The transportProperties file shall define the phases as: phases (water sludge);

The water phase shall use the Newtonian transport model with kinematic viscosity nu of 1e-6 m²/s and density rho of 1000 kg/m³.

The sludge phase shall use the BinghamPlastic transport model with density rho of 1500 kg/m³, yield stress tau0 of 15 Pa, plastic viscosity mu0 of 0.1 Pa·s, infinite shear viscosity muInf of 0.01 Pa·s, and transition sharpness parameter k of 100 s.

The surface tension coefficient sigma shall be 0.07 N/m.
