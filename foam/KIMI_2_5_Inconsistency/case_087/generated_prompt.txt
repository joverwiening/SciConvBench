Set up a transient, compressible, turbulent flow simulation over a heated 2D cylinder using rhoPimpleFoam with the k-omega SST turbulence model.

The freestream conditions are: velocity 10 m/s, temperature 300 K, and pressure 101325 Pa.

The cylinder diameter is 0.1 meters, chosen to yield a Reynolds number of approximately 66,000 based on standard air properties at the given conditions, which places the flow well within the turbulent regime for cylinder wake dynamics.

The cylinder surface temperature is fixed at 500 K using a fixedValue boundary condition with uniform value.

The fluid is air modeled as a perfect gas with thermoType set to hePsiThermo, mixture perfectGas, transport sutherland, thermo janaf, equationOfState perfectGas, specie specie, and energy sensibleEnthalpy.

The thermophysicalProperties mixture specification includes: specie molWeight 28.9, thermodynamics Cp 1005 and Hf 0, transport As 1.458e-06 and Ts 110.4.

BoussinesqCoeffs are included with TRef 300, beta 0.00333, and rhoRef 1.225 for potential future use.

The simulation runs for a total of 0.5 seconds with an adjustable time step controlled by a maximum Courant number of 1.0 to ensure stability for this compressible transient case.

The mesh is an O-grid type domain with diameter 40D in the farfield, 200 cells circumferentially around the cylinder, 60 cells in the wall-normal direction with the first cell height targeting y+ of approximately 1, and 1 cell in the spanwise direction for the 2D approximation.

Field data is written every 0.01 seconds of simulation time.

Function objects include forces and forceCoeffs for aerodynamic data, and surfaceFieldValue for integrated heat transfer coefficient on the cylinder wall.
