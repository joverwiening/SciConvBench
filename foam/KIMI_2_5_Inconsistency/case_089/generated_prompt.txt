Simulate a mixed convection lid-driven cavity problem using buoyantPimpleFoam.

The cavity has a length of 1.0 m and a height of 1.0 m, forming a square 2D domain.

The top lid moves at 0.1 m/s in the horizontal direction and is held at a hot temperature of 350 K.

The bottom wall is stationary and held at a cold temperature of 300 K.

The left and right walls are stationary and adiabatic (zero gradient for temperature).

Gravity acts downwards in the negative y-direction with magnitude 9.81 m/s².

Use the incompressiblePerfectGas model to account for density variations with temperature.

In constant/thermophysicalProperties, specify the BoussinesqCoeffs with TRef 325 K and beta 0.00307 as a backup buoyancy model.

The thermoType uses hePsiThermo with mixture incompressiblePerfectGas, transport const, thermo hConst, equationOfState perfectGas, specie specie, and energy sensibleEnthalpy.

The mixture properties include molWeight 28.9 g/mol, Cp 1005 J/kg·K, Hf 0 J/kg, mu 1.8e-5 Pa·s, and Pr 0.7.

The kinematic viscosity nu is calculated as 1.66e-5 m²/s from mu divided by rhoRef, where rhoRef = pRef/(R_specific*TRef) = 101325/(287.058*325) ≈ 1.086 kg/m³.

The initial temperature field for the entire cavity domain is uniform at 325 K.

The initial velocity field for the entire cavity domain is [0, 0, 0] m/s.

The pressure boundary condition for all cavity walls is zeroGradient.

The reference pressure pRef is 101325 Pa specified in the solver settings.

The time step size deltaT is 0.001 s with adjustable time stepping enabled.

The total end time for the simulation is 50 s to reach steady-state conditions.

The writeInterval is 0.1 s for regular output of field data.

Adjustable time stepping is enabled with a maximum Courant number limit of 1.0 to ensure numerical stability.

Second-order spatial discretization schemes are used for all convective terms: Gauss linearUpwind for momentum, Gauss upwind for energy, and Gauss linear for other terms.

The simulation uses laminar flow modeling with no turbulence model active.
