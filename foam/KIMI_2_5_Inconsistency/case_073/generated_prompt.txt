Simulate a single spherical particle settling in a quiescent fluid column using the DPMFoam solver from the OpenFOAM Lagrangian library. The domain is a vertical cylinder with a height of 1 meter aligned along the Y-axis and a diameter of 0.2 meters. The fluid is water with density 1000 kg/m^3 and kinematic viscosity 1e-6 m^2/s. A single spherical particle with diameter 1 mm and density 2500 kg/m^3 is initialized at position (0, 0.9, 0) meters. The particle motion is governed by drag, gravity, and buoyancy forces with standard spherical drag correlations. The fluid velocity field is initialized to zero everywhere. The cylinder walls use noSlip boundary conditions for velocity and zeroGradient for pressure. The top and bottom patches are walls with noSlip velocity and zeroGradient pressure conditions. The gravitational acceleration is specified as g (0 -9.81 0) m/s^2 in the constant/g dictionary, acting in the negative Y-direction. The simulation end time is 5 seconds to capture the full settling process. The time step size is 0.001 seconds with adjustableTimeStep enabled and maximum Courant number 0.5 to ensure numerical stability. The mesh consists of 50 cells in the radial direction, 200 cells in the axial direction, and 1 cell in the azimuthal direction, resulting in a structured hexahedral mesh with cell size approximately 2 mm in the radial direction and 5 mm in the axial direction. Particle trajectory data is written in ASCII format to the lagrangian/defaultCloud directory at every time step, including particle position, velocity, and forces. The simulation uses second-order schemes for spatial discretization and first-order implicit Euler for temporal discretization. The PIMPLE algorithm is used for pressure-velocity coupling with 2 outer corrector loops and 1 inner corrector loop. The particle injection is disabled after the initial particle placement. The writeInterval is set to 0.01 seconds for field data and every time step for lagrangian data.
