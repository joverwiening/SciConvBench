Configure a simulation for the separation of three immiscible fluids (oil, water, and gas) in a horizontal gravity separator using the VOF method.

The solver specified in controlDict is interFoam.

The phases are defined in transportProperties with their respective densities and viscosities.

The initial state is a stratified mixture, which can be set using setFieldsDict.

The alpha.water field represents the volume fraction of water.

The simulation tracks the interface between the three fluids over time as they separate under gravity, which is set to (0 -9.81 0).

The horizontal separator tank dimensions are 5.0 meters in length, 2.0 meters in width, and 1.5 meters in height.

The inlet flow rate is 0.1 cubic meters per second with a composition of 30 percent oil, 60 percent water, and 10 percent gas by volume fraction.

The total simulation duration is 60 seconds with a fixed time step of 0.001 seconds and a maximum Courant number limit of 0.5.

The physical properties for oil are a density of 850 kilograms per cubic meter and a dynamic viscosity of 0.01 pascal-seconds.

The physical properties for water are a density of 1000 kilograms per cubic meter and a dynamic viscosity of 0.001 pascal-seconds.

The physical properties for gas are a density of 1.2 kilograms per cubic meter and a dynamic viscosity of 1.8e-5 pascal-seconds.

The boundary condition for the inlet is a fixed value velocity of 0.1 meters per second normal to the face with fixed value volume fractions of 0.3 for oil, 0.6 for water, and 0.1 for gas.

The boundary condition for the outlet is a zero gradient for velocity and volume fractions with a fixed pressure of 0 pascal.

The boundary condition for the walls is a no-slip condition for velocity and a zero gradient for volume fractions and pressure.
