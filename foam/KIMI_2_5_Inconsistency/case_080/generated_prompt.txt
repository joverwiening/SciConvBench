The simulation shall model a three-phase fluidized bed in a cylindrical column using the solver twoPhaseEulerFoam.

The column geometry shall be cylindrical with a diameter of 0.1 m and a height of 1 m.

Gravity shall be set to (0 -9.81 0) m/s², acting in the negative y-direction.

The three phases shall be defined in phaseProperties as (air water sand).

Air shall be modeled as a perfect gas with compressible behavior.

Water shall be modeled as an incompressible liquid with constant density.

Sand shall be modeled as a constant density solid phase with a particle diameter of 500 microns (5e-4 m).

The simulation shall be transient with a total duration of 10 seconds.

Initial conditions shall specify a packed bed region in the bottom 0.2 m of the column containing sand at volume fraction alpha.sand = 0.6 and water at alpha.water = 0.4, with no air present.

The region above 0.2 m shall be initialized as pure water with alpha.water = 1 and alpha.sand = 0.

The bottom boundary shall be defined as a velocity inlet for air injection at 0.5 m/s.

The top boundary shall be defined as a pressure outlet.

The wall boundaries shall use appropriate wall functions for all phases.

Inter-phase drag between gas and solid phases shall use the Gidaspow drag model.

Inter-phase drag between liquid and solid phases shall use the Gidaspow drag model.

Inter-phase drag between gas and liquid phases shall use a standard drag model appropriate for bubbly flow.

Turbulence modeling shall be applied to the continuous phases as required by the solver.

The time step shall be adjusted to maintain Courant number stability, with appropriate maximum limits and adjustable time stepping enabled.

Field initialization files shall be prepared for alpha.air, alpha.water, alpha.sand, U.air, U.water, U.sand, p, and other required fields.

The phaseProperties dictionary shall define all pairwise interactions between the three phases with correct sub-model selections.
