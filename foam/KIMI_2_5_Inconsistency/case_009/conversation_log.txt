Q: What is the Mach number of the incoming flow?
A: The Mach number is not explicitly specified in the complete requirement. However, with the given inlet conditions (velocity = 5 m/s, temperature = 1 K, and perfect gas with Cp = 2.5 and molecular weight = 11640.3), you can calculate it.

For a perfect gas: R = 8314/MW = 8314/11640.3 = 0.7143 J/(kg·K), γ = Cp/(Cp-R) = 2.5/(2.5-0.7143) = 1.4, speed of sound a = sqrt(γRT) = sqrt(1.4 × 0.7143 × 1) = 1.0 m/s, giving Mach number = 5/1 = **5**.

So the incoming flow is at **Mach 5** (hypersonic/supersonic as stated in the requirement).
