The simulation is a 2D gas-liquid bubble column using twoPhaseEulerFoam with the following specifications.

The domain is a rectangular box with x ranging from 0 to 0.15 m, y ranging from 0 to 1.00 m, and z ranging from 0 to 0.10 m, with the height oriented along the positive y direction and a thin dimension in z.

The mesh is structured hexahedral with 25 cells in x, 75 cells in y, and 1 cell in z, using simpleGrading with uniform grading (1 1 1) in all directions.

The blockMeshDict defines three patches: inlet at y=0, outlet at y=1.0, and walls on the remaining boundaries, with 2D treatment achieved using empty patch types on the z-normal faces.

The continuous liquid phase is water modeled with heRhoThermo and an rPolynomial equation of state with coefficients 0.001278, -2.1055e-06, 3.9689e-09, 4.3772e-13, and -2.0225e-16, molecular weight of 18 kg/kmol, specific heat capacity Cv of 4195 J/kg/K, dynamic viscosity of 3.645e-4 Pa·s, and Prandtl number of 2.289.

The dispersed gas phase is air modeled with heRhoThermo and perfectGas equation of state, molecular weight of 28.9 kg/kmol, specific heat capacity Cp of 1007 J/kg/K, dynamic viscosity of 1.84e-05 Pa·s, and Prandtl number of 0.7.

Gravity is set to (0 -9.81 0) m/s².

Bubble size models use isothermal diameter with d0 = 3e-3 m for air and constant diameter d = 1e-4 m for water.

Momentum transport is laminar for both phases with simulationType set to laminar in the respective momentumTransport files.

Phase properties define phases as (air water) with water as the reference phase and residualAlpha of 1e-6 for both phases.

Blending uses a linear scheme with minFullyContinuousAlpha of 0.7 and minPartlyContinuousAlpha of 0.3 for default blending, and minFullyContinuousAlpha of 0.7 with minPartlyContinuousAlpha of 0.5 for drag blending.

Surface tension between air and water is constant with sigma = 0.07 N/m.

Drag model uses SchillerNaumann for air_dispersedIn_water, water_dispersedIn_air, and air_segregatedWith_water configurations.

Virtual mass uses constantCoefficient with Cvm = 0.5 for both air_dispersedIn_water and water_dispersedIn_air directions.

Heat transfer employs the RanzMarshall model for both air_dispersedIn_water and water_dispersedIn_air directions with residualAlpha of 1e-4.

Initial conditions at t=0 set water velocity to uniform (0 0 0), air velocity to uniform (0 0.1 0), water volume fraction to uniform 1, pressure p_rgh to uniform 1e5 Pa, air temperature to uniform 300 K, and water temperature to uniform 350 K.

Phase distribution uses default values where air volume fraction is 1 and water volume fraction is 0, except within a box region from (0 0 0) to (0.15 0.701 0.1) where air volume fraction is 0 and water volume fraction is 1.

Inlet boundary conditions set air velocity to fixedValue (0 0.1 0), water velocity to fixedValue (0 0 0), air volume fraction to fixedValue 0.5, water volume fraction to fixedValue 0.5, air temperature to fixedValue 300, and water temperature to fixedValue 350.

Wall boundary conditions set air velocity to fixedValue (0 0 0), water velocity to fixedValue (0 0 0), both volume fractions to zeroGradient, p_rgh to fixedFluxPressure, and both temperatures to zeroGradient.

Time controls start at 0 s, end at 20 s, with a fixed time step of 0.005 s, adjustTimeStep set to no, maxCo of 0.5, maxDeltaT of 1 s, and writeInterval of 1 s.
