Run a 2D inviscid laminar oblique-shock simulation on a rectangular channel extruded one cell in z.
The domain spans from (0, 0, 0) to (4.1, 1, 0.1).
The grid resolution is 60 cells in x, 30 cells in y, and 1 cell in z.
Set the frontAndBack patches to empty to enforce 2D behavior.
Define four patches: inlet on the left boundary, outlet on the right boundary, top as a wall, and bottom as a symmetryPlane.
Use the rhoCentralFoam solver for compressible flow.
Set momentum transport to laminar.
Configure thermodynamics as ideal gas with inviscid flow: Cp=2.5, Pr=1, and viscosity=0.
Initialize the internal fields with velocity U=(2.9 0 0), pressure p=1, and temperature T=1.
Apply fixedValue boundary conditions at the inlet: U=(2.9 0 0), p=1, T=1.
Apply noSlip velocity boundary condition on the top wall.
Set the molecular weight to 11640.3 and heat of formation Hf=0.
Configure time controls: deltaT=0.0025, endTime=10, writeInterval=1.
Generate the oblique shock using a 15-degree wedge angle, implemented as a sloped wall boundary condition on the bottom patch starting at x=1.0 with the slope rising toward the top boundary, replacing the symmetryPlane condition downstream of the wedge apex with a wall condition to form the compression corner.
