The column geometry is 0.5m wide in the x-direction and 2m high in the y-direction, with a single cell thickness of 0.1m in the z-direction.

The continuous phase is water and the dispersed phase is air.

Within this box region, the field values are alpha.air 0.4 and alpha.water 0.6, representing a mixture of water and air.

The top 0.5m of the column (from y=1.5m to y=2.0m) retains the default values of pure air.

All volume fractions sum to 1.0 in both the default and region specifications.
