The simulation shall be configured as follows:

Use OpenFOAM's `laplacianFoam` solver for 2D transient heat conduction.

The computational domain is a 1 m × 1 m plate extruded one cell in the z-direction with thickness 0.01 m.

The mesh shall be generated using `blockMesh` with a single cell in the z-direction.

The `frontAndBack` patch type shall be set to `wall` in `constant/polyMesh/boundary`.

The `frontAndBack` patch shall use `zeroGradient` boundary condition for temperature in `0/T`.

Thermal diffusivity `DT` shall be set to `1e-5 m^2/s` in `constant/transportProperties`.

Initial temperature field `T` shall be uniform 300 K in the `0/T` file.

The left edge patch shall use a fixed temperature boundary condition of 400 K (Dirichlet).

The right edge patch shall use a fixed temperature boundary condition of 300 K (Dirichlet).

The top edge patch shall use a zeroGradient boundary condition (adiabatic/insulated).

The bottom edge patch shall use a zeroGradient boundary condition (adiabatic/insulated).

The simulation shall run as transient with a time step chosen to satisfy stability criteria based on the mesh size and thermal diffusivity.

The `fvSchemes` dictionary shall use Euler time scheme and Gauss linear for laplacian and gradient terms.

The `fvSolution` dictionary shall use a tolerance of `1e-6` and relTol of `0.1` for the `T` equation solver.
