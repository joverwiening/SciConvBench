Simulate a turbulent thermal plume rising from a hot strip on the floor of a large, open room.

Solver: buoyantPimpleFoam

Geometry: 2D room 2.0 m (x) x 2.0 m (y) x 0.01 m (thickness). Gravity in -y.
A hot strip of width 0.2 m is centered on the floor: x in [0.9, 1.1] at y=0.

Mesh: blockMesh multi-block to split the floor into two patches:
- bottom faces for x in [0.9,1.1] -> patch hotSource
- remaining bottom faces -> patch coolFloor
Use overall resolution ~ (160 160 1). Patches: hotSource, coolFloor, sideWalls (left+right), outlet (top), frontAndBack (empty).

Thermophysical properties: air as perfect gas with Sutherland viscosity (constant/thermophysicalProperties: heRhoThermo, perfectGas, Sutherland, Cp ~ 1005, molWeight ~ 28.9).

Turbulence: RAS kOmegaSST in constant/momentumTransport.

Initial conditions: U=(0 0 0), T=300 K, p_rgh=0 (gauge), k small, omega moderate.

Boundary conditions:
- hotSource (floor strip):
  - U noSlip
  - T fixedValue 400 K
  - p_rgh fixedFluxPressure
  - turbulence wall functions
- coolFloor and sideWalls:
  - U noSlip
  - T fixedValue 300 K (or zeroGradient if adiabatic; choose fixedValue 300 for 'room at ambient')
  - p_rgh fixedFluxPressure
- outlet (top open):
  - U pressureInletOutletVelocity (inletValue (0 0 0))
  - p_rgh totalPressure with p0 uniform 0
  - T inletOutlet (inletValue 300; value 300)
  - k, omega inletOutlet (inletValue small; value small)
- frontAndBack: empty

Time controls: endTime 200; deltaT 0.01; adjustTimeStep yes; maxCo 0.5; writeInterval 5.
