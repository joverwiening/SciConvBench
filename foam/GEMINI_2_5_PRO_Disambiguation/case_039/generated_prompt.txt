The simulation is a steady-state conjugate heat transfer analysis.
The domain consists of a solid block with an internal porous channel.
The solid block has dimensions of 0.2m x 0.2m x 0.5m.
The solid block material is aluminum.
A porous channel with a cross-section of 0.1m x 0.1m runs through the center of the block along its length.
The fluid in the porous channel is water.
The porous media is defined by Darcy coefficients of (1e6, 1e6, 1e6).
The porous media is defined by Forchheimer coefficients of (100, 100, 100).
The fluid inlet has a fixed velocity of (0.05, 0, 0) m/s.
The fluid inlet has a fixed temperature of 353.15 K.
The fluid outlet has a fixed relative pressure of 0 Pa.
The external walls of the solid block have a convective thermal boundary condition.
The ambient temperature for the convective boundary condition is 293.15 K.
The heat transfer coefficient for the convective boundary condition is 10 W/(m^2*K).
