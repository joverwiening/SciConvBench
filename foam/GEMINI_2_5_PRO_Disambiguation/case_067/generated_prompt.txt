The simulation domain is a cylindrical pipe.
The pipe has a radius of 0.05 meters.
The pipe has a length of 2 meters.
The fluid is air.
The kinematic viscosity of the air is 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The pipe is completely filled with a porous material.
The porosity of the material is 0.4.
The porous drag is modeled using the Darcy-Forchheimer law.
The Darcy coefficient (d) is an isotropic tensor with a value of 5e7 1/m^2.
The Forchheimer coefficient (f) is zero.
The flow is turbulent and is modeled using the standard k-epsilon model.
The inlet boundary condition is a uniform velocity of 10 m/s in the axial direction.
The turbulence intensity at the inlet is 5%.
The turbulent length scale at the inlet is 0.0035 meters.
The outlet boundary condition is a fixed static pressure of 0 Pa.
The pipe wall is a stationary, no-slip boundary.
Standard wall functions are used to model the flow near the pipe wall.
