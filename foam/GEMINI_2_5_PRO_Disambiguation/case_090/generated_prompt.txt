The simulation is for an incompressible fluid flow.
The geometry is a 2D channel.
The bottom wall of the channel is flat.
The top wall is angled upwards at 5.71 degrees relative to the bottom wall.
The Reynolds number, based on the inlet height, is 33,333.
The fluid properties and inlet height must be selected to satisfy the specified Reynolds number.
The inlet velocity profile is uniform with a value of (5 0 0) m/s.
The outlet pressure is a fixed value of 0.
The outlet velocity has a zero-gradient boundary condition.
The top and bottom walls have a no-slip boundary condition for velocity.
The top and bottom walls have a zero-gradient boundary condition for pressure.
Wall functions are applied for turbulence quantities at the top and bottom walls.
The objective is to observe potential flow separation from the angled top wall.
