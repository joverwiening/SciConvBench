The simulation domain is a 2D rectangular furnace.
The furnace height is 2 meters.
The furnace width is 0.5 meters.
A primary air and coal inlet is located at the bottom center of the furnace.
The inlet width is 0.05 meters.
An outlet is located at the top of the furnace.
The inlet velocity for the air and coal mixture is 20 m/s in the positive y-direction.
The inlet temperature is 400 K.
The mass flow rate of the injected coal particles is 0.1 kg/s.
The coal consists of 35% volatile matter by mass.
The coal consists of 50% fixed carbon by mass.
The coal consists of 10% ash by mass.
The coal consists of 5% moisture by mass.
The simulation must include a radiation heat transfer model.
