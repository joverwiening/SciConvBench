The simulation is a 2D transient flow of an incompressible fluid past a circular cylinder.
The computational domain is a rectangle.
The domain extends from -5 m to 5 m in the x-direction.
The domain extends from -1.5 m to 2.5 m in the y-direction.
The cylinder has a diameter of 2 m.
The cylinder is centered at (x=0 m, y=0 m).
The simulation starts at time 0 s.
The simulation ends at time 0.5 s.
The time step is 0.0025 s.
Simulation results are written every 0.05 s.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 0.01 m^2/s.
The initial velocity field is (0 0 0) m/s.
The initial pressure field is 0.
The inlet boundary is at x = -5 m.
The inlet velocity is a fixed value of (1 0 0) m/s.
The inlet pressure uses a zeroGradient condition.
The outlet boundary is at x = 5 m.
The outlet velocity uses a zeroGradient condition.
The outlet pressure is a fixed value of 0.
The top boundary is at y = 2.5 m.
The top boundary has a noSlip condition for velocity.
The top boundary has a zeroGradient condition for pressure.
The bottom boundary is at y = -1.5 m.
The bottom boundary has a noSlip condition for velocity.
The bottom boundary has a zeroGradient condition for pressure.
The cylinder surface is a wall boundary.
The cylinder surface has a noSlip condition for velocity.
The cylinder surface has a zeroGradient condition for pressure.
The front and back boundaries are defined as empty for a 2D simulation.
