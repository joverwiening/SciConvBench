The simulation is a transient laminar two-phase dam-break.
The simulation domain is a rectangular prism from (0,0,0) to (1,1,1).
An obstacle is defined as a rectangular prism from (0.375, 0.375, 0) to (0.625, 0.625, 0.25).
The mesh consists of 32 cells in the x, y, and z directions respectively.
Gravity is defined as (0, 0, -9.81).
The two phases are modeled using the Volume of Fluid (VOF) method.
Phase 1 is water with a density of 1000 kg/m^3 and a kinematic viscosity of 1.0e-6 m^2/s.
Phase 2 is air with a density of 1 kg/m^3 and a kinematic viscosity of 1.48e-5 m^2/s.
The surface tension between water and air is 0.07 N/m.
The top boundary of the domain at z=1 is an open atmosphere patch.
The remaining five boundaries of the domain are walls.
Initially, the region from (0, 0, 0) to (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75) is filled with water.
The rest of the domain is initially filled with air.
The simulation runs from a start time of 0 s to an end time of 0.1 s.
The initial time step is 1e-3 s.
The maximum allowable Courant number is 0.5.
Simulation results are written to disk every 0.02 s.
