The simulation is a transient, laminar, two-phase dam-break with one obstacle.
The simulation uses the interFoam solver.
The domain is a cube with corners at (0,0,0) and (1,1,1).
The mesh has 32 cells in the x-direction, 32 cells in the y-direction, and 32 cells in the z-direction.
The gravitational acceleration vector is (0, 0, -9.81).
The simulation models two phases, water and air, using the Volume of Fluid method.
The water phase has a density of 1000 kg/m^3 and a kinematic viscosity of 1.0e-6 m^2/s.
The air phase has a density of 1 kg/m^3 and a kinematic viscosity of 1.48e-5 m^2/s.
The surface tension between water and air is 0.07 N/m.
The initial water column is a rectangular prism where alpha.water is 1 inside the box from (0, 0, 0) to (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75).
The obstacle is a rectangular prism located from (0.375, 0.375, 0) to (0.625, 0.625, 0.25).
The top face of the domain at z=1 is an open atmosphere patch.
The remaining five faces of the domain are walls.
The velocity boundary condition on the wall patches is no-slip.
The pressure boundary condition on the open atmosphere patch is total pressure with a value of 0.
The total simulation time is 0.1 seconds.
The maximum Courant number is 0.5.
Simulation results are written to disk every 0.02 seconds.
