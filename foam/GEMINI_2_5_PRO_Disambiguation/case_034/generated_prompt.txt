The simulation uses the chtMultiRegionFoam solver.
The simulation domain is a two-region conjugate heat transfer problem.
The outer cavity has dimensions of 1.0 m in X, 1.0 m in Y, and 0.01 m in Z.
A solid block with dimensions 0.4 m in X, 0.4 m in Y, and 0.01 m in Z is centered in the cavity.
The region between the cavity walls and the solid block is the fluid region.
Gravity is defined as a vector of (0, -9.81, 0) m/s^2.
The fluid is air, modeled as a compressible perfect gas.
The fluid thermophysical model is `heRhoThermo`.
The fluid equation of state is `perfectGas`.
The fluid transport model is `Sutherland`.
The fluid specific heat (Cp) is 1005 J/kg/K.
The fluid molar weight is 28.9 kg/kmol.
The solid has a density of 8960 kg/m^3.
The solid has a thermal conductivity of 401 W/m/K.
The solid has a specific heat of 385 J/kg/K.
The left vertical wall of the cavity has a fixed temperature of 310 K.
The right vertical wall of the cavity has a fixed temperature of 290 K.
The top and bottom walls of the cavity are adiabatic.
All outer walls are no-slip boundaries for velocity.
The front and back faces of the domain use the `empty` boundary condition.
The interface between the solid and fluid regions is a coupled thermal boundary.
The initial velocity in the fluid region is zero.
The initial temperature for both the fluid and solid regions is 300 K.
The initial pressure in the fluid region is 101325 Pa.
The simulation is transient and runs for an end time of 1000 seconds.
An adjustable time step is used with a maximum Courant number of 0.5.
