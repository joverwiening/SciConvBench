The simulation is a 2D buoyant cavity.
The simulation is steady-state.
The solver to be used is buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam.
The geometry is a rectangle with a width of 1 meter and a height of 0.25 meters.
The mesh consists of 80 cells in the x-direction and 20 cells in the y-direction.
The gravitational acceleration vector is (0 -9.81 0).
The fluid has a kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m^2/s.
The fluid has a thermal expansion coefficient of 2.0e-4 1/K.
The fluid has a Prandtl number of 7.0.
The bottom wall is a hot wall with a fixed temperature of 310 K.
The top wall is a cold wall with a fixed temperature of 290 K.
The left wall is insulated.
The right wall is insulated.
The simulation is run with a RANS turbulence model.
The k-omega SST turbulence model is used.
