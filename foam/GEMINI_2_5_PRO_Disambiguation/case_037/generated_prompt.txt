Model compressible air flow in a duct.
The fluid is air, treated as an ideal gas.
The duct has a rectangular cross-section with a height of 0.1m and a width of 0.01m.
The total length of the duct is 0.5m.
A porous filter is located within the duct.
The porous filter begins 0.2m from the duct inlet.
The length of the porous filter is 0.05m.
The simulation must use a turbulence model.
The inlet total pressure is 792833 Pa.
The inlet total temperature is 540 K.
The outlet static pressure is 101325 Pa.
The porous filter is modeled using the Darcy-Forchheimer law.
The Darcy coefficient vector is (5e7, 5e7, 0) 1/m^2.
The Forchheimer coefficient vector is (500, 500, 0) 1/m.
