The simulation is a two-dimensional analysis of supersonic flow over a wedge.
The flow is inviscid.
The gas has a ratio of specific heats of 1.4.
The freestream Mach number is 2.0.
The freestream static pressure is 101325 Pa.
The freestream static temperature is 300 K.
The computational domain is a rectangle with a length of 1.0m and a height of 0.5m.
The bottom boundary consists of a flat plate from x=0m to x=0.2m, followed by a 10-degree wedge.
A slip condition is applied to the entire bottom boundary.
The inlet boundary at x=0 is a supersonic inlet with the specified freestream conditions.
The outlet boundary at x=1.0m is a supersonic outlet condition.
The top boundary at y=0.5m is a far-field condition with the specified freestream conditions.
