The simulation is an incompressible lid-driven cavity flow.
The solver to be used is pisoFoam.
The simulation runs from time 0 to 10 seconds.
The time step is 0.005 seconds.
Results are written every 100 time steps.
The cavity dimensions are 1 meter in x, 1 meter in y, and 0.1 meters in z.
The mesh consists of 20 cells in x, 20 cells in y, and 1 cell in z.
The kinematic viscosity is constant at 1e-05 m^2/s.
The k-epsilon turbulence model is used.
The top boundary, named 'movingWall', has a constant velocity of (1 0 0) m/s.
All other walls, named 'fixedWalls', have a no-slip boundary condition with velocity (0 0 0) m/s.
The front and back boundaries are of type 'empty'.
The pressure boundary condition on all walls is zeroGradient.
The initial velocity field is uniformly (0 0 0) m/s.
The initial turbulence kinetic energy (k) is uniformly 1.5e-4 m^2/s^2.
The initial turbulence dissipation rate (epsilon) is uniformly 1.4e-4 m^2/s^3.
The initial turbulent viscosity (nut) is uniformly 0 m^2/s.
The wall function for k is kqRWallFunction on all walls.
The wall function for epsilon is epsilonWallFunction on all walls.
The wall function for nut is nutkWallFunction on all walls.
The convection scheme for velocity is 'Gauss limitedLinearV 1'.
The convection scheme for k is 'Gauss limitedLinear 1'.
The convection scheme for epsilon is 'Gauss limitedLinear 1'.
The viscous term scheme is 'Gauss linear'.
The linear solver for pressure is GAMG with a tolerance of 1e-6 and a relative tolerance of 0.01.
The linear solver for velocity is a smoothSolver using symGaussSeidel with a tolerance of 1e-6 and a relative tolerance of 0.1.
The linear solver for k is a smoothSolver using symGaussSeidel with a tolerance of 1e-6 and a relative tolerance of 0.1.
The linear solver for epsilon is a smoothSolver using symGaussSeidel with a tolerance of 1e-6 and a relative tolerance of 0.1.
The PISO algorithm will use 2 pressure-velocity correctors.
