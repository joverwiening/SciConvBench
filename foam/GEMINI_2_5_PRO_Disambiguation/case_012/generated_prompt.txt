The simulation is an incompressible lid-driven cavity flow.
The solver is pisoFoam.
The cavity is a square with dimensions of 1 unit in x, 1 unit in y, and 0.1 units in z.
The mesh grid is 20 cells in the x-direction, 20 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The simulation runs from time 0 to 10 units.
The time step is 0.005 units.
Results are written every 100 time steps.
The fluid's kinematic viscosity is 1e-05 m^2/s.
The k-epsilon turbulence model is used.
The initial velocity field is zero everywhere.
The initial turbulent kinetic energy (k) is 1.5e-4 m^2/s^2 uniformly in the domain.
The initial turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) is 1.4e-4 m^2/s^3 uniformly in the domain.
The initial turbulent viscosity (nut) is 0 m^2/s uniformly in the domain.
The top wall moves in the positive x-direction with a uniform velocity of 1 m/s.
The left, right, and bottom walls have a no-slip velocity boundary condition.
All walls have a zeroGradient pressure boundary condition.
The boundary condition for k is kqRWallFunction on all walls.
The boundary condition for epsilon is epsilonWallFunction on all walls.
The boundary condition for nut is nutkWallFunction on all walls.
The front and back boundaries are of type empty.
The convection scheme for velocity is div(phi,U) Gauss limitedLinearV 1.
The convection scheme for k is div(phi,k) Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The convection scheme for epsilon is div(phi,epsilon) Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The viscous term scheme is div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear.
