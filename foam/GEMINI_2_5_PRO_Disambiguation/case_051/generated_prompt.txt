The simulation domain is a one-dimensional shock tube with a total length of 1.0 meter.
The domain extends from x=0.0 m to x=1.0 m.
A diaphragm is initially located at x=0.5 m, separating two states of gas.
The gas is ideal air with a specific heat at constant pressure of 1005 J/kg/K and a molecular weight of 28.9 kg/kmol.
The ratio of specific heats is derived from these properties.
The initial velocity of the gas throughout the entire tube is 0 m/s.
The region from x=0.0 m to x=0.5 m has an initial pressure of 100000 Pa.
The region from x=0.0 m to x=0.5 m has an initial density of 1.0 kg/m^3.
The region from x=0.5 m to x=1.0 m has an initial pressure of 10000 Pa.
The region from x=0.5 m to x=1.0 m has an initial density of 0.125 kg/m^3.
The boundary at the left end (x=0.0 m) is a slip wall.
For the left boundary, the velocity condition is slip, the pressure condition is zeroGradient, and the temperature condition is zeroGradient.
The boundary at the right end (x=1.0 m) is a slip wall.
