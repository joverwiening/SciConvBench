The simulation is a 2D multiphase problem with heat transfer.
The simulation domain is a rectangular column.
The column width is 0.1 meters.
The column height is 0.4 meters.
A single gas bubble is simulated rising in a liquid.
The gas bubble has an initial radius of 0.02 meters.
The initial center of the gas bubble is at coordinates (0.05, 0.05) meters.
The liquid is initially stationary.
The density of the gas is 1.2 kg/m^3.
The density of the liquid is 1000 kg/m^3.
The dynamic viscosity of the gas is 1.8e-5 Pa.s.
The bubble rises due to buoyancy.
