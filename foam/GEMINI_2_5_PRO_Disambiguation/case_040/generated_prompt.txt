The simulation will model transient, two-phase flow through a porous medium.
The simulation domain is a 2D rectangle.
The domain height is 2 meters.
The domain length is 1 meter.
The porous medium represents a dam.
The dam is initially filled with air.
The upstream boundary condition is a fixed water level of 1.5 meters.
The downstream boundary is open to the atmosphere.
The top and bottom boundaries of the domain are impermeable walls.
The fluid properties for water are a density of 1000 kg/m^3.
The kinematic viscosity of water is 1e-6 m^2/s.
The porous medium has a porosity of 0.3.
The porous resistance is defined by the Darcy-Forchheimer model.
The Darcy coefficient is (1e6, 1e6, 0).
The Forchheimer coefficient is (100, 100, 0).
Gravitational acceleration is 9.81 m/s^2 acting downwards.
The simulation will run for a total of 40 seconds.
The primary output is the visualization of the water front moving through the porous structure over time.
