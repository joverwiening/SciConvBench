The simulation will model natural convection in a closed room.
The solver to be used is buoyantPimpleFoam.
The domain is a 2D rectangle with a width of 4 meters and a height of 3 meters.
A heat source is located on the floor.
The heat source is 1 meter wide and is centered on the 4-meter floor.
The temperature of the heat source is a fixed value of 323 K.
The fluid in the room is air.
The initial temperature of the air is a uniform 293 K.
The two vertical walls are adiabatic.
The ceiling is adiabatic.
The parts of the floor not covered by the heat source are adiabatic.
Gravitational acceleration is 9.81 m/s² acting downwards along the height of the room.
