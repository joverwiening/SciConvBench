The simulation will model turbulent flow in a channel using the pimpleFoam solver.
The working fluid is incompressible with a kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The channel half-height is 0.05 meters.
The channel domain has a streamwise length of 2.0 meters.
The channel domain has a spanwise width of 0.10 meters.
The flow is driven by a pressure gradient to achieve a target friction Reynolds number (Re_tau) of 180.
The turbulence model is Reynolds-Averaged Navier-Stokes (RANS) using the k-Omega SST model.
The mesh must be refined near the walls so that the y+ value of the first grid cell is less than or equal to 1.
The boundary conditions in the streamwise and spanwise directions are periodic.
A no-slip boundary condition for velocity is applied to the top and bottom walls.
Use a low-Reynolds number wall treatment for the k and omega turbulence fields at the top and bottom walls.
