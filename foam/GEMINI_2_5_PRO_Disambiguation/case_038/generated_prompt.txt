Simulate steady-state natural convection heat transfer in a porous block.
The fluid is air.
The dimensions of the block are 0.10 m in the x-direction, 0.01 m in the y-direction, and 0.10 m in the z-direction.
The gravitational acceleration vector is (0, -9.81, 0) m/s^2.
The bottom surface at y=0 is a hot wall with a constant temperature of 310 K.
The top surface at y=0.01 m is a cold wall with a constant temperature of 290 K.
The four vertical side walls are adiabatic.
The porosity of the block is 0.4.
The thermal conductivity of the solid matrix is 1.0 W/(m·K).
The porous resistance is defined by the Darcy model.
The permeability of the porous block is isotropic with a value of 1e-7 m^2.
