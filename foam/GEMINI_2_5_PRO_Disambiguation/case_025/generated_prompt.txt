The simulation is a transient, laminar, two-phase dam-break with an obstacle.
The solver to be used is interFoam.
The simulation domain is a cube defined by the vertices (0,0,0) and (1,1,1).
The domain is discretized with a uniform mesh of 32 x 32 x 32 cells.
An obstacle is present in the domain, defined by the box from (0.375, 0.375, 0) to (0.625, 0.625, 0.25).
Gravity is enabled and set to (0, 0, -9.81).
The first phase is water, with a density of 1000 kg/m^3.
The kinematic viscosity of water is 1.0e-6 m^2/s.
The second phase is air, with a density of 1 kg/m^3.
The kinematic viscosity of air is 1.48e-5 m^2/s.
The surface tension between water and air is 0.07 N/m.
The simulation starts at time 0 and ends at 0.1 seconds.
The time step deltaT is a fixed value of 1e-3 seconds.
Simulation results are written every 0.02 seconds.
The top face of the domain at z=1 is an "atmosphere" patch.
The remaining five faces of the domain are "wall" patches.
The initial condition for the water phase fraction is 1 inside the box from (0, 0, 0) to (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75), and 0 elsewhere.
The initial condition for velocity is a uniform value of (0, 0, 0) throughout the domain.
The initial condition for the relative pressure is a uniform value of 0 throughout the domain.
The velocity boundary condition on the wall patches is no-slip.
The velocity boundary condition on the atmosphere patch is pressureInletOutletVelocity.
The pressure boundary condition on the wall patches is fixedFluxPressure.
The pressure boundary condition on the atmosphere patch is totalPressure with a value of 0.
The phase fraction boundary condition on the wall patches is zeroGradient.
The phase fraction boundary condition on the atmosphere patch allows for outflow of any fluid and inflow of air only.
