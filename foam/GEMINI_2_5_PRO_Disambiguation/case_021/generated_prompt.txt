The simulation is a 2D incompressible unsteady flow past a circular cylinder.
The solver to be used is pimpleFoam.
The simulation domain extends from x=-5 m to x=5 m and y=-1.5 m to y=2.5 m.
A cylinder of diameter 2 m is centered at the origin (0,0).
The fluid has a kinematic viscosity of 0.01 m^2/s.
The simulation runs from a start time of 0 s to an end time of 0.5 s.
The time step is 0.0025 s.
Simulation results are written every 0.05 s.
The initial velocity field is uniform (0 0 0) m/s.
The initial pressure field is uniform 0.
The inlet patch is located at x = -5 m.
The inlet velocity is a uniform (1 0 0) m/s.
The inlet pressure condition is zeroGradient.
The outlet patch is located at x = 5 m.
The outlet velocity condition is zeroGradient.
The outlet pressure is a fixed uniform value of 0.
The top wall patch is located at y = 2.5 m.
The top wall velocity condition is noSlip.
The top wall pressure condition is zeroGradient.
The bottom wall patch is located at y = -1.5 m.
The bottom wall velocity condition is noSlip.
The bottom wall pressure condition is zeroGradient.
The cylinder wall velocity condition is noSlip.
The cylinder wall pressure condition is zeroGradient.
The front and back patches are defined as empty to enforce a 2D simulation.
