The simulation uses the pimpleFoam solver.
The simulation is for turbulent channel flow of water.
The kinematic viscosity is 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The geometry is a 2D backward-facing step.
The total length of the channel is 1.0 m.
The step is located at x = 0.2 m from the inlet.
The channel height before the step is 0.02 m.
The channel height after the step is 0.04 m.
The turbulence model is RAS kEpsilon.
Wall functions are used for the turbulence model at the walls.
The front and back patches have the 'empty' boundary condition.
The inlet velocity is a fixed value of 0.5 m/s.
The inlet turbulent kinetic energy (k) is a fixed value of 9.375e-4.
The outlet pressure is a fixed value of 0.
The outlet velocity boundary condition is zeroGradient.
The wall velocity boundary condition is no-slip.
