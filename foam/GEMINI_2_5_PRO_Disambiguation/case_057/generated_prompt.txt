The simulation is a 2D turbulent flow in a channel.
The solver to be used is pisoFoam.
The turbulence model is k-epsilon.
The channel height is 0.1 meters.
The channel length is 2 meters.
The fluid kinematic viscosity is 1e-5 m^2/s.
The mesh consists of 200 cells in the horizontal direction and 20 cells in the vertical direction.
The inlet velocity boundary condition is a fixed value of (1 0 0) m/s.
The inlet pressure boundary condition is zeroGradient.
The inlet turbulent kinetic energy (k) is a fixed value of 0.00375 m^2/s^2.
The inlet turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) is a fixed value of 0.0054 m^2/s^3.
The outlet velocity boundary condition is zeroGradient.
The outlet pressure boundary condition is a fixed value of 0.
The outlet boundary condition for k and epsilon is zeroGradient.
The top and bottom wall velocity boundary condition is noSlip.
The top and bottom wall pressure boundary condition is zeroGradient.
Wall functions are used for k and epsilon at the top and bottom walls.
