Simulate steady transonic turbulent flow over a NACA0012 airfoil at 0 deg angle of attack.

Solver: rhoPimpleFoam (run to steady state using pseudo-time iterations)

Geometry: 2D C-grid around a NACA0012 airfoil with chord c = 1 m. Far-field boundary at 20c.

Mesh: structured C-grid (blockMesh or external mesher). If using RANS wall functions, target y+ ~ 30-100 (not y+~1). Use ~256 cells around the airfoil and ~64 in the normal direction, with boundary-layer grading.

Thermophysical properties: air as perfect gas with Sutherland viscosity (constant/thermophysicalProperties: hePsiThermo, perfectGas, Sutherland, Cp ~ 1005, molWeight ~ 28.9).

Freestream conditions: Ma = 0.85, pInf = 101325 Pa, TInf = 288 K.

Boundary conditions (far-field):
- U: freestreamVelocity with freestreamValue corresponding to Ma=0.85 and direction (1 0 0)
- p: freestreamPressure with freestreamValue pInf
- T: freestreamTemperature with freestreamValue TInf
- turbulence (k, omega): freestream with values from I=1% and viscosity ratio ~10

Airfoil wall:
- U noSlip
- p zeroGradient
- T zeroGradient (adiabatic) or fixedValue if desired
- k/omega compressible wall functions (compressible::kqRWallFunction, compressible::omegaWallFunction)

frontAndBack: empty

Turbulence model: RAS kOmegaSST in constant/momentumTransport.

Controls: endTime 2000; deltaT 1; writeInterval 200, with PIMPLE settings tuned for steady convergence; monitor residuals and force coefficients.
