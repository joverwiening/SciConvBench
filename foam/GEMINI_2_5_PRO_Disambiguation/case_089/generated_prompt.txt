The simulation is a two-dimensional analysis of compressible, turbulent flow over a NACA0012 airfoil.
The airfoil has a chord length of 1 meter.
The airfoil is at a 0-degree angle of attack.
The working fluid is air, treated as an ideal gas.
The freestream Mach number is 0.85.
The freestream static temperature is 288 K.
The freestream static pressure is 101325 Pa.
The k-Omega SST turbulence model will be used.
The computational domain's far-field boundary is located 20 chord lengths from the airfoil.
The airfoil surface is a no-slip, adiabatic wall.
The primary objective is to visualize the shock waves on the airfoil.
