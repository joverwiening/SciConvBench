The simulation domain is a 2D square cavity.
The dimensions of the cavity are 1 meter by 1 meter.
The working fluid is air.
The kinematic viscosity of the air is 1e-5 m^2/s.
The left wall is maintained at a constant temperature of 303 K.
The right wall is maintained at a constant temperature of 293 K.
The top wall has a zeroGradient thermal boundary condition.
The bottom wall has a zeroGradient thermal boundary condition.
All four walls have a noSlip velocity boundary condition.
Gravitational acceleration is 9.81 m/s^2.
Gravitational acceleration acts in the negative y-direction.
