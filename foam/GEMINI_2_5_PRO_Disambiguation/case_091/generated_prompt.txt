The simulation models natural convection in a two-dimensional square cavity.
The side length of the cavity is 0.1 meters.
The fluid in the cavity is air.
The solver is a buoyant, transient solver.
The left wall is a hot wall with a constant temperature of 313.15 K.
The right wall is a cold wall with a constant temperature of 293.15 K.
The top and bottom walls are adiabatic.
All four walls have a no-slip velocity boundary condition.
The gravitational acceleration vector is (0, -9.81, 0) m/s^2.
The kinematic viscosity of the air is calculated using the Sutherland transport model.
The density of the air is calculated using the ideal gas law.
The initial temperature of the air inside the cavity is 303.15 K.
The initial pressure of the air inside the cavity is 101325 Pa.
