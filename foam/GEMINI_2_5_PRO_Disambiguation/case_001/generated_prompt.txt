The simulation is a two-dimensional Rayleigh-Benard convection cell.
The simulation domain is a rectangle with a width of 9 meters and a height of 1 meter.
The mesh consists of 90 cells in the x-direction and 10 cells in the y-direction.
The simulation uses the buoyantFoam solver.
Gravitational acceleration is 9.81 m/s^2 and acts in the negative y-direction.
The simulation runs until an end time of 1000 seconds.
The time step size is fixed at 1 second.
Simulation results are written to disk every 50 seconds.
The turbulence model is Reynolds-Averaged Simulation (RAS) with the k-epsilon model.
The constant/momentumTransport file sets simulationType to RAS, model to kEpsilon, turbulence to on, and printCoeffs to on.
The thermophysical properties are defined using heRhoThermo with a Boussinesq equation of state.
The reference density rho0 is 1.0 kg/m^3.
The reference temperature T0 is 300 K.
The thermal expansion coefficient beta is 3.3e-3 1/K.
The molecular weight molWeight is 29 g/mol.
The specific heat at constant volume Cv is 714.3 J/(kg*K).
The standard heat of formation Hf is 0 J/kg.
The dynamic viscosity mu is 1.5e-5 Pa*s.
The Prandtl number Pr is 0.714.
The initial velocity field is uniform at (0 0 0) m/s.
The initial temperature field is uniform at 300 K.
The initial turbulent kinetic energy k field is uniform at 1e-5 m^2/s^2.
The initial turbulent dissipation rate epsilon field is uniform at 1e-5 m^2/s^3.
The initial turbulent viscosity nut field is uniform at 0 m^2/s.
The initial thermal diffusivity alphat field is uniform at 0 m^2/s.
The initial pressure p field is uniform at 0 Pa.
The initial pressure minus hydrostatic p_rgh field is uniform at 0 Pa.
A no-slip velocity boundary condition is applied to the top, bottom, and side walls.
The bottom wall has a fixed temperature of 305 K.
The top wall has a fixed temperature of 295 K.
The side walls are adiabatic, using a zero-gradient temperature condition.
The k field uses the kqRWallFunction boundary condition on all walls.
The epsilon field uses the epsilonWallFunction boundary condition on all walls.
The nut field uses the nutkWallFunction boundary condition on all walls.
The alphat field uses the compressible::alphatJayatillekeWallFunction boundary condition with Prt 0.85 on all walls.
The p field uses the calculated boundary condition on all walls.
The p_rgh field uses the fixedFluxPressure boundary condition on all walls.
The front and back patches are of type empty.
The solvers for p_rgh, U, e, k, and epsilon have a tolerance of 1e-8 and a relative tolerance of 0.01.
The final solvers for p_rgh, U, e, k, and epsilon have a relative tolerance of 0.
