The simulation is a 2D turbulent buoyant cavity flow.
The k-epsilon turbulence model is used.
The simulation is 2D, implemented using empty boundary conditions on the front and back patches.
The cavity is a square with an aspect ratio of 1.
The height of the cavity is 1 meter.
The width of the cavity is 1 meter.
The left wall is heated to a fixed temperature of 303 K.
The right wall is cooled to a fixed temperature of 293 K.
The top and bottom walls are adiabatic, using a zeroGradient boundary condition for temperature.
All walls have a noSlip boundary condition for velocity.
The gravitational acceleration vector is (0, -9.81, 0) m/s^2.
The fluid has a Prandtl number of 0.71.
The fluid properties are set to achieve a Rayleigh number of 1e6.
