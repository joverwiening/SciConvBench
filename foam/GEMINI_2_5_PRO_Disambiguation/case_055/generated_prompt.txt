The simulation is a compressible, turbulent, lid-driven cavity flow.
The geometry is a two-dimensional square cavity with a side length of 1 meter.
The working fluid is air.
The initial pressure in the cavity is 101325 Pa.
The initial temperature in the cavity is 300 K.
The top wall moves tangentially with a velocity of 100 m/s.
The left, right, and bottom walls are stationary with a no-slip condition.
All four walls have a fixed temperature of 300 K.
The k-Omega SST turbulence model is used.
The simulation is run in a transient mode.
The total physical time for the simulation is 0.2 seconds.
The time step is determined by a Courant number of 0.5.
