The simulation is a 2D bubble column.
The simulation uses an Euler-Euler multiphase approach.
The column width is 0.2 meters.
The column height is 1.0 meter.
The liquid phase is water.
The gas phase is air.
The air bubbles have a constant diameter of 2 mm.
The SchillerNaumann drag model is used for interphase momentum transfer.
The column is initially filled entirely with water.
The bottom boundary is an inlet.
The superficial gas velocity at the inlet is 0.01 m/s.
The air phase fraction at the inlet is a fixed value of 0.05.
The water velocity at the inlet is a fixed value of zero.
The top boundary is an outlet open to the atmosphere.
The pressure at the top outlet uses a totalPressure condition.
The velocities of both phases at the top outlet use a pressureInletOutletVelocity condition.
The air phase fraction at the top outlet uses an inletOutlet condition.
The side walls are solid boundaries.
The water velocity at the side walls uses a noSlip condition.
The air velocity at the side walls uses a slip condition.
The phase fraction at the side walls uses a zeroGradient condition.
