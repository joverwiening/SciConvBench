Model the steady-state flow through a 2D planar de Laval nozzle.
The fluid for the simulation is air.
Model air as a perfect gas.
Use the Sutherland model for viscosity.
The nozzle has a total length of 1.0 m.
The inlet is located at x=0 m and has a height of 0.2 m.
The throat is located at x=0.5 m and has a height of 0.1 m.
The outlet is located at x=1.0 m and has a height of 0.3 m.
The converging section wall profile is defined by a cubic polynomial connecting the inlet to the throat with zero slope at both ends.
The diverging section wall profile is defined by a parabolic curve connecting the throat to the outlet with zero slope at the throat.
Set the inlet total pressure to 200000 Pa.
Set the inlet total temperature to 300 K.
Set the outlet static pressure to 101325 Pa.
Use a steady-state solver for the simulation.
The resulting flow must be subsonic at the inlet and supersonic at the outlet.
