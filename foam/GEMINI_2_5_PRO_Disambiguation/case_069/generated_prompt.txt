The simulation is a steady-state conjugate heat transfer analysis.
The overall domain dimensions are 0.5 m in X, 0.1 m in Y, and 0.01 m in Z.
A solid block with dimensions 0.1 m in X, 0.05 m in Y, and 0.01 m in Z is centered within the overall domain.
The fluid domain occupies the space within the overall domain that is not occupied by the solid block.
The fluid is air, modeled as a compressible perfect gas.
The solid block has an effective thermal conductivity of 10 W/m/K.
The effect of porosity is included in the effective thermal properties, and no momentum source is applied.
The inlet boundary is located at the minimum X-face of the domain.
The inlet velocity is a fixed value of (1, 0, 0) m/s.
The inlet temperature is a fixed value of 400 K.
The outlet boundary is located at the maximum X-face of the domain.
The outlet relative pressure (p_rgh) is a fixed value of 0.
The outlet velocity has a zeroGradient boundary condition.
The outlet temperature is an inletOutlet type with an inletValue of 300 K and a value of 300 K.
The external walls at the minimum and maximum Y-boundaries are adiabatic, using a zeroGradient condition for temperature.
The external walls at the minimum and maximum Z-boundaries are adiabatic, using a zeroGradient condition for temperature.
All external walls (Y and Z boundaries) have a zeroGradient boundary condition for velocity and pressure.
