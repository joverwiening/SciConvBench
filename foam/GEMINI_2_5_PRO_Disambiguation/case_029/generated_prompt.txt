The simulation is a transient, laminar, two-phase dam-break with obstacles.
The OpenFOAM solver interFoam is used.
The domain is a cube defined by the vertices (0,0,0) and (1,1,1).
The domain is discretized with a uniform 32x32x32 mesh.
An obstacle is present in the domain, defined as a box from (0.375 0.375 0) to (0.625 0.625 0.25).
Gravity is set to (0 0 -9.81).
The first phase is water with a density of 1000 kg/m^3 and a kinematic viscosity of 1.0e-6 m^2/s.
The second phase is air with a density of 1 kg/m^3 and a kinematic viscosity of 1.48e-5 m^2/s.
The initial water column is a rectangular prism where alpha.water is 1 inside the box from (0,0,0) to (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75).
The initial value of alpha.water is 0 everywhere else.
The simulation runs until an endTime of 0.1 s.
The time step deltaT is 1e-3 s.
Simulation results are written every 0.02 s.
The five wall boundaries (bottom, front, back, left, right) have a noSlip boundary condition for velocity (U).
The top boundary has a pressureInletOutletVelocity boundary condition for velocity (U).
The five wall boundaries have a fixedFluxPressure boundary condition for pressure (p_rgh).
The top boundary has a totalPressure boundary condition for pressure (p_rgh) with a value of 0.
The five wall boundaries have a zeroGradient boundary condition for the phase fraction (alpha.water).
The top boundary has an inletOutlet boundary condition for the phase fraction (alpha.water), with an inletValue of 0.
