Simulate supersonic flow over a forward-facing step.
The inlet Mach number is 3.
The flow is viscous and turbulent.
The working fluid is a perfect gas.
The k-omega SST turbulence model is used.
The freestream static pressure is 101325 Pa.
The freestream static temperature is 293 K.
The step height is 0.02 meters.
The computational domain length upstream of the step is 0.1 meters.
The computational domain length downstream of the step is 0.4 meters.
The total height of the computational domain is 0.1 meters.
The inlet boundary condition specifies a uniform flow at the defined freestream conditions.
The outlet boundary condition is a zero-gradient supersonic outlet.
The bottom boundary, including the step, is a no-slip adiabatic wall.
The top boundary condition is a zero-gradient condition to represent the far-field.
The primary objective is to visualize the shock structure.
