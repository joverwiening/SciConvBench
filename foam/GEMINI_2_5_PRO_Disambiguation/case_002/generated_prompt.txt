The simulation is a two-dimensional Rayleigh-Benard convection cell.
The geometry is a rectangular cavity 9 units wide and 1 unit tall.
The mesh consists of 90 cells in the x-direction, 10 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The front and back patches are of type empty to enforce a 2D simulation.
The solver to be used is buoyantFoam.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction.
The turbulence model is RAS k-epsilon.
The simulationType in constant/momentumTransport is set to RAS with the model kEpsilon, turbulence on, and printCoeffs on.
The thermodynamic package is heRhoThermo with a Boussinesq equation of state.
The reference density rho0 is 1.0 kg/m^3.
The reference temperature T0 is 300 K.
The thermal expansion coefficient beta is 3.3e-3 1/K.
The molecular weight molWeight is 29 g/mol.
The specific heat at constant volume Cv is 714.3 J/(kg*K).
The standard heat of formation Hf is 0.
The dynamic viscosity mu is 1.5e-5 Pa*s.
The Prandtl number Pr is 0.714.
The initial velocity field U is uniform at (0 0 0) m/s.
The velocity boundary condition on all solid walls is no-slip.
The initial temperature field T is uniform at 300 K.
The bottom wall temperature is fixed at 305 K.
The top wall temperature is fixed at 295 K.
The vertical side walls are adiabatic (zeroGradient).
The initial turbulent kinetic energy k is a uniform field of 1e-5 m^2/s^2.
The boundary condition for k on all solid walls is kqRWallFunction.
The initial turbulent dissipation rate epsilon is a uniform field of 1e-5 m^2/s^3.
The boundary condition for epsilon on all solid walls is epsilonWallFunction.
The initial turbulent viscosity nut is a uniform field of 0 m^2/s.
The boundary condition for nut on all solid walls is nutkWallFunction.
The initial thermal diffusivity alphat is a uniform field of 0 m^2/s.
The boundary condition for alphat on all solid walls is compressible::alphatJayatillekeWallFunction with a turbulent Prandtl number Prt of 0.85.
The initial relative pressure p_rgh is a uniform field of 0 Pa.
The boundary condition for p_rgh on all solid walls is fixedFluxPressure.
The time derivative scheme (ddtSchemes) is Euler.
The gradient scheme (gradSchemes) is Gauss linear.
The divergence scheme for velocity (div(phi,U)) is Gauss linearUpwindV grad(U).
The divergence schemes for k, epsilon, and e are Gauss upwind.
The Laplacian scheme (laplacianSchemes) is Gauss linear corrected.
The interpolation scheme (interpolationSchemes) is linear.
The surface-normal gradient scheme (snGradSchemes) is corrected.
The solver for p_rgh is GAMG.
The solvers for U, e, k, and epsilon are smoothSolver with a symGaussSeidel smoother.
The solver tolerance for p_rgh, U, e, k, and epsilon is 1e-8.
