The simulation is an incompressible lid-driven cavity flow.
The cavity geometry has dimensions of 1 unit in x, 1 unit in y, and 0.1 units in z.
The mesh has 20 cells in the x-direction, 20 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The top wall is named 'movingWall'.
The 'movingWall' moves in the positive x-direction with a uniform velocity of 1 m/s.
The side and bottom walls are named 'fixedWalls'.
The 'fixedWalls' have a no-slip boundary condition.
The front and back faces have the 'empty' boundary condition type.
The kinematic viscosity is constant with a value of 1e-05 m^2/s.
The k-epsilon turbulence model is used.
The pisoFoam solver is used for the simulation.
The initial velocity field is zero throughout the domain.
The initial turbulent kinetic energy (k) is 1.5e-4 m^2/s^2 throughout the domain.
The initial turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) is 1.4e-4 m^2/s^3 throughout the domain.
The initial turbulent viscosity (nut) is 0 m^2/s throughout the domain.
The boundary condition for k on all walls is kqRWallFunction.
The boundary condition for epsilon on all walls is epsilonWallFunction.
The boundary condition for nut on all walls is nutkWallFunction.
The boundary condition for pressure on all walls is zeroGradient.
The convection scheme for velocity is 'div(phi,U) Gauss limitedLinearV 1'.
The convection scheme for k is 'div(phi,k) Gauss limitedLinear 1'.
The convection scheme for epsilon is 'div(phi,epsilon) Gauss limitedLinear 1'.
The discretization scheme for the viscous term is 'div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear'.
The total simulation time is 10 seconds.
The time step size is 0.005 seconds.
Simulation results are written to disk every 100 time steps.
The number of PISO correctors is 2.
The convergence tolerance for the pressure solver is 1e-6.
