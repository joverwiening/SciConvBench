The simulation will model a 2D planar de Laval nozzle.
The working fluid is a perfect gas with a constant transport model.
The ratio of specific heats is 1.4.
The specific gas constant is 287 J/(kg*K).
The inlet total pressure is 5e5 Pa.
The inlet total temperature is 600 K.
The outlet static pressure is a fixed value of 3.5e5 Pa.
The nozzle throat height is 0.05m.
The nozzle inlet height is 0.1m.
The length of the converging section is 0.1m.
The length of the diverging section is 0.2m.
The area ratio between the nozzle exit and the throat is 1.6.
The converging and diverging nozzle walls are defined by smooth arc edges.
The simulation must produce a normal shock in the diverging section.
