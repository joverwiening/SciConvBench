The simulation is a steady-state analysis of compressible air flow.
The simulation domain is a 2D channel.
The channel starts at x=0 m and ends at x=0.2 m.
The channel height is 0.05 m.
A porous block is located from x=0.08 m to x=0.12 m.
The porous block spans the full height of the channel.
The inlet is at x=0 m.
The inlet total pressure is 101325 Pa.
The inlet total temperature is 300 K.
The outlet is at x=0.2 m.
The outlet static pressure is 100000 Pa.
The top and bottom walls are no-slip walls.
The top and bottom walls are adiabatic.
The porous block has a constant volumetric heat source of 1e6 W/m^3.
The porous block is modeled using the Darcy-Forchheimer law.
The Darcy coefficient vector is (1e9, 1e9, 1e11).
The Forchheimer coefficient vector is (0, 0, 0).
