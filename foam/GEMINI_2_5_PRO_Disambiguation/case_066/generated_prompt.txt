The simulation is for steady-state, incompressible, laminar flow.
The fluid is water with a kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m^2/s.
The simulation domain is a 2D channel with a length of 0.5 m and a height of 0.1 m.
The mesh consists of 100 cells in the length direction and 20 cells in the height direction.
The flow is through a porous medium defined by a Darcy-Forchheimer model.
The Darcy coefficient vector is (1e8, 1e8, 1e10).
The Forchheimer coefficient vector is (0, 0, 0).
The inlet boundary has a fixed velocity of 1 m/s.
The inlet boundary has a zeroGradient condition for pressure.
The outlet boundary has a zeroGradient condition for velocity.
The outlet boundary has a fixed pressure value of 0.
The top and bottom walls have a noSlip boundary condition for velocity.
The top and bottom walls have a zeroGradient boundary condition for pressure.
