The simulation domain is a 2D square box with dimensions 1 meter by 1 meter.
The mesh consists of 50x50 cells.
The working fluid has a kinematic viscosity of 1e-5 m^2/s.
The working fluid has a Prandtl number of 0.71.
The working fluid has a thermal expansion coefficient of 3.4e-3 1/K.
The working fluid has a reference density of 1.0 kg/m^3.
The bottom wall is a hot boundary with a constant temperature of 300 K.
The top wall is a cold boundary with a constant temperature of 290 K.
The left and right walls have a zeroGradient thermal boundary condition.
All walls have a noSlip velocity boundary condition.
Gravitational acceleration is -9.81 m/s^2 in the y-direction.
The simulation runs until an end time of 200 seconds.
The initial temperature of the fluid is 295 K.
A warm spot with a temperature of 295.1 K is initialized in a rectangular region defined by the corners (0.45, 0.00) and (0.55, 0.05).
The time step is controlled by a maximum Courant number of 0.8.
