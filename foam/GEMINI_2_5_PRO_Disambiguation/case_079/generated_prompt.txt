The simulation will model a single air bubble rising in a column of quiescent water.
The simulation will be two-dimensional.
A Volume of Fluid (VOF) multiphase solver will be used.
The computational domain is a rectangle with a width of 0.05 m and a height of 0.2 m.
The liquid phase is water with a density of 1000 kg/m^3.
The dynamic viscosity of water is 0.001 Pa·s.
The gas phase is air with a density of 1.225 kg/m^3.
The dynamic viscosity of air is 1.81e-5 Pa·s.
The surface tension between air and water is 0.072 N/m.
A gravitational acceleration of 9.81 m/s^2 acts in the negative y-direction.
An air bubble with an initial diameter of 0.01 m is placed in the domain.
The initial center of the bubble is located at coordinates (x=0.025 m, y=0.025 m).
The initial velocity of both the air and water is zero everywhere.
The top boundary of the domain is a pressure outlet.
The bottom and side boundaries of the domain are no-slip walls.
