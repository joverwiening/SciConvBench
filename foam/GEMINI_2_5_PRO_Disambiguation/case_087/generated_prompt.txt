The simulation models supersonic flow over a forward-facing step.
The flow is inviscid.
The fluid is ideal air.
The inlet Mach number is 3.
The inlet static pressure is 101325 Pa.
The inlet static temperature is 300 K.
The domain has a total height of 1 m.
The domain extends 0.6 m upstream of the step.
The domain extends 2.4 m downstream of the step.
The step height is 0.2 m.
The top wall boundary condition is slip.
The bottom wall and step face boundary condition is slip.
The outlet pressure boundary condition is waveTransmissive with a far-field value of 101325 Pa.
The outlet velocity and temperature boundary conditions are zeroGradient or waveTransmissive.
