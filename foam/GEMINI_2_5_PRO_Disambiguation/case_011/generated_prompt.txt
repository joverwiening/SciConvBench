The simulation is an incompressible lid-driven cavity flow.
The pisoFoam solver is used.
The physical domain is a square cavity with dimensions of 1 unit in x and 1 unit in y.
The computational grid consists of 20 cells in the x-direction, 20 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The top wall, named 'movingWall', moves in the positive x-direction with a uniform velocity of 1 m/s.
All other walls, named 'fixedWalls', have a no-slip boundary condition.
The front and back boundaries are of type 'empty'.
The kinematic viscosity is constant and set to 1e-05 m^2/s.
The k-epsilon turbulence model is used.
The total simulation time is 10 units.
The time step size is 0.005 units.
The initial velocity field is zero everywhere.
The initial turbulent kinetic energy (k) is uniform with a value of 1.5e-4 m^2/s^2.
The initial turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) is uniform with a value of 1.65e-6 m^2/s^3.
The pressure boundary condition on all walls is zeroGradient.
The turbulent kinetic energy (k) boundary condition on all walls is kqRWallFunction.
The turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) boundary condition on all walls is epsilonWallFunction.
The convection scheme for momentum is 'Gauss limitedLinearV 1'.
The convection scheme for turbulent kinetic energy is 'Gauss limitedLinear 1'.
The convection scheme for turbulent dissipation rate is 'Gauss limitedLinear 1'.
The viscous term discretization is 'Gauss linear'.
