The simulation is a 2D lid-driven cavity flow.
The fluid is incompressible.
The Reynolds number is 100.
The cavity is a square with dimensions of 0.1m by 0.1m.
The grid is a uniform mesh of 20x20 cells.
The top wall moves to the right with a velocity of (1 0 0) m/s.
The left, right, and bottom walls are stationary no-slip walls.
The simulation is transient and runs until a final time of 0.5 seconds.
The time step size is 0.002 seconds.
The initial velocity of the fluid in the cavity is (0 0 0) m/s.
A second-order backward differencing scheme is used for time discretization.
A Gauss linear scheme is used for the spatial discretization of the divergence term.
