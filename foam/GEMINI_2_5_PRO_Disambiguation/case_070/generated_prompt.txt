The simulation is a 2D model of incompressible, turbulent flow.
The computational domain is a rectangular channel with a length of 1.0 meter and a height of 0.1 meter.
The fluid has a density of 998 kg/m^3.
The fluid has a kinematic viscosity of 1e-5 m^2/s.
The standard k-epsilon turbulence model is used.
A thin porous screen is located at x=0.5 meters, perpendicular to the flow direction.
The screen is modeled as a porous baffle pressure jump boundary condition.
The Darcy coefficient for the baffle is 1e7.
The Inertial coefficient for the baffle is 0.
The inlet boundary is at x=0 meters.
The inlet velocity is uniform with a value of 5 m/s in the x-direction.
The inlet turbulent kinetic energy (k) is 0.09375 m^2/s^2.
The inlet turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) is 0.336 m^2/s^3.
The outlet boundary is at x=1.0 meter.
The outlet gauge pressure is a fixed value of 0 Pa.
A zero-gradient boundary condition is applied to velocity and turbulence quantities at the outlet.
The top and bottom boundaries of the channel are no-slip walls.
Standard wall functions are used for turbulence at the wall boundaries.
