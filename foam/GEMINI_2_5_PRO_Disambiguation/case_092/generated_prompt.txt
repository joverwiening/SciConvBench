The simulation models two-dimensional Rayleigh-Benard convection.
The computational domain is a rectangle.
The domain width is 0.4 meters.
The domain height is 0.1 meters.
The front and back boundaries are empty to enforce a 2D simulation.
Gravitational acceleration is directed downwards with a magnitude of 9.81 m/s^2.
The fluid has a kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The fluid has a thermal expansion coefficient of 3.33e-3 1/K.
The fluid has a Prandtl number of 0.71.
The bottom wall is maintained at a constant temperature of 305 K.
The top wall is maintained at a constant temperature of 295 K.
The side walls have a zeroGradient temperature boundary condition.
All walls have a noSlip velocity boundary condition.
