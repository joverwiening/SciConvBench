The simulation will be run using the reactingFoam solver.
The simulation domain is a rectangular prism.
The domain spans from x=0 to x=0.02 meters.
The domain spans from y=-0.01 to y=0.01 meters.
The domain spans from z=-0.01 to z=0.01 meters.
The mesh will have 100 cells in the x-direction.
The mesh will have 40 cells in the y-direction.
The mesh will have 1 cell in the z-direction.
The boundary at x=0 is named 'fuel'.
The boundary at x=0.02 is named 'air'.
The top and bottom boundaries (y=-0.01 and y=0.01) are named 'outlet'.
The front and back boundaries are of type 'empty'.
The fuel inlet injects pure methane (CH4 mass fraction of 1.0).
The fuel inlet has a temperature of 293 K.
The fuel inlet has a velocity of (0.1, 0, 0) m/s.
The air inlet supplies a mixture of oxygen and nitrogen.
The air inlet has an O2 mass fraction of 0.23 and an N2 mass fraction of 0.77.
The air inlet has a temperature of 293 K.
The air inlet has a velocity of (-0.1, 0, 0) m/s.
The outlet boundaries have a fixed static pressure of 101325 Pa.
Velocity, temperature, and species mass fractions at the outlet boundaries use a zero-gradient condition.
The domain is initialized with a uniform temperature of 2000 K.
The domain is initialized with a uniform pressure of 101325 Pa.
The domain is initialized with a uniform velocity of (0, 0, 0) m/s.
The domain is initialized with a stoichiometric mixture of methane and air (Y_CH4=0.055, Y_O2=0.217, Y_N2=0.728).
The simulation will run until an end time of 0.5 seconds.
The simulation state will be written to disk every 0.01 seconds.
The maximum Courant number for time step control is 0.5.
The combustion model is laminar.
The momentum transport model is laminar.
The thermophysical model is hePsiThermo.
The mixture model is coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture.
The transport model is Sutherland.
The thermodynamics model is JANAF.
