The simulation is a 2D transient analysis of a turbulent buoyant plume.
The computational domain is a rectangle 2 meters wide and 4 meters high.
The fluid is air at standard atmospheric conditions.
The standard k-epsilon turbulence model will be used.
The gravity vector is (0, -9.81, 0) m/s^2.
The initial velocity field is (0, 0, 0) m/s.
The initial temperature field is 300 K.
The top boundary is an open outlet.
The p_rgh boundary condition at the top is 'totalPressure' with a value of 0.
The velocity boundary condition at the top is 'pressureInletOutletVelocity'.
The T, k, and epsilon boundary conditions at the top are 'inletOutlet' with an inletValue of 300 K for T.
The two vertical side walls are slip walls.
The velocity boundary condition for the side walls is 'slip'.
The T, k, epsilon, and p_rgh boundary conditions for the side walls are 'zeroGradient'.
The entire floor is a no-slip wall for velocity.
The central 0.2 meter section of the floor has a fixed temperature of 400 K.
The floor surfaces outside the central 0.2 meter section have a 'zeroGradient' temperature boundary condition.
