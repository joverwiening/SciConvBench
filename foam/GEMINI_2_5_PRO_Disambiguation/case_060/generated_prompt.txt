The simulation is a 2D analysis of turbulent flow over a backward-facing step.
Use the pimpleFoam solver.
The fluid has a kinematic viscosity of 1e-5 m^2/s.
The turbulence model is the k-epsilon RAS model.
The geometry has a step height of 0.01m.
The channel height upstream of the step is 0.02m.
The domain extends 0.05m upstream of the step.
The domain extends 0.5m downstream of the step.
The mesh is one cell thick with front and back patches of type 'empty'.
The inlet velocity is a fixed value of (10 0 0) m/s.
The outlet pressure is a fixed value of 0.
The outlet velocity uses a zero-gradient boundary condition.
All wall surfaces have a no-slip boundary condition for velocity.
All wall surfaces use wall functions for k and epsilon.
The Reynolds number, based on step height, is 10,000.
