The simulation is a 2D analysis of fluid flow over a cylinder.
The objective is to observe vortex shedding.
The fluid is water.
The density of the water is 998 kg/m^3.
The dynamic viscosity of the water is 0.001 Pa·s.
The cylinder has a diameter of 1 meter.
The computational domain is a rectangle.
The domain length in the direction of flow is 40 meters.
The domain width perpendicular to the direction of flow is 20 meters.
The center of the cylinder is located 10 meters downstream from the inlet.
The center of the cylinder is located at the vertical midpoint of the domain.
The inlet velocity is a uniform (3.9, 0, 0) m/s.
The pressure boundary condition at the inlet is zero-gradient.
The velocity boundary condition on the cylinder surface is no-slip.
The turbulent kinetic energy boundary condition on the cylinder surface is kqRWallFunction.
The specific dissipation rate boundary condition on the cylinder surface is omegaWallFunction.
