The simulation is a 2D premixed methane-air flame in a channel using the reactingFoam solver.
The flow is laminar.
The simulation domain is a 2D channel with a length of 0.1 meters and a height of 0.02 meters.
A structured mesh with 100 cells in the x-direction and 20 cells in the y-direction is used.
The fuel is a stoichiometric premixed mixture of methane and air.
A one-step irreversible reaction (CH4 + 2O2 -> CO2 + 2H2O) is used for combustion.
The reaction kinetics are of type 'methaneAir' with Arrhenius parameters A=2.119e11, beta=0, and Ta=2.027e8.
The inlet velocity is a uniform (0.5 0 0) m/s.
The inlet temperature is 300 K.
The outlet pressure boundary condition is 'totalPressure' with p0 set to 101325 Pa.
The top and bottom walls are adiabatic and have a no-slip velocity condition.
The domain is initialized with a uniform temperature of 300 K.
A rectangular hot spot region with a temperature of 1800 K is placed near the inlet to ensure ignition.
