Simulate the external turbulent aerodynamic flow over an Ahmed body.
The primary objective is to calculate the drag coefficient.

The Ahmed body has a width of 0.389 m.

The simulation domain inlet is located 4 body lengths upstream of the Ahmed body's front surface.
The simulation domain outlet is located 10 body lengths downstream of the Ahmed body's rear surface.
The domain's side boundaries are located 5 body widths from the Ahmed body's centerline.
The domain's top boundary is located 5 body heights from the ground plane.
The inlet boundary condition is a uniform velocity of (11, 0, 0) m/s.
The outlet boundary condition is a pressure outlet with zero gauge pressure.
The Ahmed body surface is a no-slip wall.
The ground plane is a moving wall with a tangential velocity of (11, 0, 0) m/s.
