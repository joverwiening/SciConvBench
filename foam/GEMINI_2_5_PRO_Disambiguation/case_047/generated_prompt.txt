The simulation is a 3D turbulent channel flow.
The k-epsilon turbulence model is used.
The fluid has a kinematic viscosity of 1e-5 m^2/s.
The channel height is 0.1 m.
The channel length is 1.0 m.
The channel width is 0.2 m.
The flow is driven by a pressure gradient to achieve a mean bulk velocity of 0.5 m/s.
The Reynolds number based on channel height and bulk velocity is 5000.
Periodic boundary conditions are applied in the streamwise (length) direction.
Periodic boundary conditions are applied in the spanwise (width) direction.
The top and bottom walls use a no-slip boundary condition for velocity.
The top and bottom walls use a zeroGradient boundary condition for pressure.
The top and bottom walls use the kqRWallFunction for k with a uniform value of 0.000375.
The top and bottom walls use the epsilonWallFunction for epsilon with a uniform value of 1.875e-4.
The top and bottom walls use the nutkWallFunction for nut with a uniform value of 0.
