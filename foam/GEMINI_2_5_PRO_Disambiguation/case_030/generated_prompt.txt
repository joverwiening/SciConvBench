The simulation domain is a cube from (0,0,0) to (1,1,1).
A solid rectangular obstacle exists from (0.375, 0.375, 0) to (0.625, 0.625, 0.25).
The mesh resolution is 32x32x32 cells.
The total simulation time is 0.1 seconds.
Simulation results are written every 0.02 seconds.
The maximum Courant number is 0.5.
Gravity is defined as (0, 0, -9.81) m/s^2.
The simulation uses the Volume of Fluid method for two phases.
The first phase is water with a density of 1000 kg/m^3 and a kinematic viscosity of 1.0e-6 m^2/s.
The second phase is air with a density of 1 kg/m^3 and a kinematic viscosity of 1.48e-5 m^2/s.
The surface tension between water and air is 0.07 N/m.
The initial velocity of the fluid is (0, 0, 0) m/s throughout the domain.
The initial water volume is a rectangular prism from (0, 0, 0) to (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75).
The five faces of the domain at x=0, x=1, y=0, y=1, and z=0 are defined as walls.
All faces of the solid obstacle are also defined as walls.
The wall boundary condition for velocity is no-slip.
The wall boundary condition for pressure is zero gradient.
The wall boundary condition for the water phase fraction is zero gradient.
The top face of the domain at z=1 is an open atmosphere boundary.
The atmosphere boundary condition for pressure is a total pressure of 0.
The atmosphere boundary condition for velocity is a pressure-inlet/outlet condition.
