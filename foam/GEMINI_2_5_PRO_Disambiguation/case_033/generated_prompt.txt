The simulation is a 2D analysis of natural convection.
The fluid is air.
The flow is turbulent and modeled using a k-omega based turbulence model.
The simulation domain is a 2.0 m wide by 3.0 m high enclosure.
The domain has a thickness of 0.01 m.
Gravity is 9.81 m/s^2 acting in the negative y-direction.
The initial and ambient air temperature is 300 K.
A heated plate is located on the left vertical wall.
The heated plate is 1.0 m tall, extending from y=1.0 m to y=2.0 m.
The heated plate is an isothermal wall with a fixed temperature of 350 K.
The portions of the left wall above and below the heated section are adiabatic walls.
The bottom surface of the domain is an adiabatic wall.
The top and right surfaces of the domain are open boundaries.
The open boundaries use a pressureInletOutletVelocity condition for velocity.
The open boundaries use a totalPressure condition for pressure.
The open boundaries use an inletOutlet condition for temperature, k, and omega.
The wall boundaries use a noSlip condition for velocity.
The wall boundaries use wall functions for turbulence variables.
