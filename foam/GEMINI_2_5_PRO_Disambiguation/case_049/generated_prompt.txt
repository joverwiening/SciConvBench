The simulation is a two-dimensional dam break case.
The domain is a square tank with a length of 0.584 meters and a height of 0.584 meters.
The simulation models two fluids: water and air.
The density of water is 1000 kg/m^3.
The density of air is 1 kg/m^3.
The dynamic viscosity of water is 0.001 Pa·s.
The dynamic viscosity of air is 1.81e-5 Pa·s.
The acceleration due to gravity is -9.81 m/s^2, acting in the negative vertical direction.
A column of water is initially located in the bottom-left corner of the tank.
The initial width of the water column is 0.146 meters.
The initial height of the water column is 0.292 meters.
The rest of the tank is initially filled with air.
The fluids are initially at rest.
The water column collapses under the influence of gravity.
All boundaries of the tank are no-slip walls.
