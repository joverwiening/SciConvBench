The simulation will model a classic Sod shock tube problem.
The solver to be used is rhoCentralFoam.
The geometry is a one-dimensional tube with a total length of 1 meter.
The diaphragm is located at x = 0.5 meters.
The working fluid is ideal air.
The fluid follows the perfect gas equation of state.
The specific heat ratio (gamma) is 1.4.
The molar mass of the fluid is 28.96 g/mol.
The initial velocity throughout the entire tube is (0 0 0) m/s.
In the high-pressure section (x < 0.5 m), the initial pressure is 100000 Pa.
In the high-pressure section (x < 0.5 m), the initial temperature is 348.4 K.
In the low-pressure section (x > 0.5 m), the initial pressure is 10000 Pa.
In the low-pressure section (x > 0.5 m), the initial temperature is 278.7 K.
The initial density in both sections will be calculated based on the perfect gas law.
The simulation will run for a total time of 0.002 seconds.
The boundary conditions at both ends of the tube are zero-gradient for all scalar fields.
The boundary conditions at both ends of the tube are no-slip walls for velocity.
The `setFields` utility will be used to initialize the high and low-pressure regions.
