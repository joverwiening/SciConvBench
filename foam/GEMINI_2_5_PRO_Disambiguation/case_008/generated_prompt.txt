The simulation domain is a square cavity with dimensions 1x1x0.1 units.
The mesh consists of 20 cells in the x-direction, 20 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The top boundary is named 'movingWall'.
The side and bottom boundaries are named 'fixedWalls'.
The front and back boundaries are named 'empty' and have the type 'empty'.
The simulation uses the pisoFoam solver.
The k-epsilon turbulence model is used.
The kinematic viscosity is constant at 1e-05 m^2/s.
The simulation runs from time 0 to 10.
The time step is 0.005 units.
Results are written every 100 time steps.
The initial velocity field is zero.
The initial pressure field is zero.
The initial turbulent kinetic energy (k) is 1.5e-4.
The initial turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) is 1.4e-4.
The 'movingWall' has a uniform velocity of (1 0 0) m/s.
The 'fixedWalls' have a no-slip velocity boundary condition.
The pressure (p) boundary condition is zeroGradient for both 'movingWall' and 'fixedWalls'.
The turbulent kinetic energy (k) boundary condition is kqRWallFunction for both 'movingWall' and 'fixedWalls'.
The turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) boundary condition is epsilonWallFunction for both 'movingWall' and 'fixedWalls'.
The turbulent viscosity (nut) boundary condition is nutkWallFunction for both 'movingWall' and 'fixedWalls'.
The convection scheme for velocity is 'Gauss limitedLinearV 1'.
The convection scheme for k is 'Gauss limitedLinear 1'.
The convection scheme for epsilon is 'Gauss limitedLinear 1'.
The viscous term scheme is 'Gauss linear'.
