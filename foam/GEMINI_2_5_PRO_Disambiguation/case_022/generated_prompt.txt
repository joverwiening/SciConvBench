The simulation is a 2D flow past a circular cylinder.
The domain is a rectangle from x = -5 m to x = 5 m, y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m, and z = -1 m to z = 1 m.
A cylinder with a diameter of 2 m is centered at (x=0, y=0).
The solver is pimpleFoam for an incompressible unsteady flow.
The simulation starts at 0 s and ends at 0.5 s.
The time step is 0.0025 s.
Results are written every 0.05 s.
The initial velocity field is a uniform (0 0 0) m/s.
The initial pressure field is a uniform 0.
The flow is laminar.
The viscosity model is generalisedNewtonian with the CrossPowerLaw model.
The inlet velocity at x = -5 m is a uniform (1 0 0) m/s.
The outlet velocity at x = 5 m has a zeroGradient boundary condition.
The cylinder surface has a noSlip velocity boundary condition.
The top wall at y = 2.5 m has a noSlip velocity boundary condition.
The bottom wall at y = -1.5 m has a noSlip velocity boundary condition.
The front and back faces at z = -1 m and z = 1 m have an empty boundary condition.
The inlet pressure at x = -5 m has a zeroGradient boundary condition.
The outlet pressure at x = 5 m is a fixed value of 0.
The cylinder surface has a zeroGradient pressure boundary condition.
The top wall at y = 2.5 m has a zeroGradient pressure boundary condition.
The bottom wall at y = -1.5 m has a zeroGradient pressure boundary condition.
