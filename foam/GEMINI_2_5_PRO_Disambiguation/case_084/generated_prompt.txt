The simulation models a flame propagating through a closed tube.
The tube is 0.5 meters long.
The tube has a square cross-section of 0.01 meters by 0.01 meters.
The tube is filled with a stoichiometric hydrogen-air mixture.
The initial pressure of the mixture is 101325 Pa.
The initial temperature of the mixture is 300 K.
An ignition zone is located at one end of the tube.
The ignition zone is 0.01 meters long.
The temperature within the ignition zone is initially set to 2000 K.
The simulation uses a detailed 9-species, 19-reaction mechanism for hydrogen-air combustion.
The reaction mechanism files are converted from CHEMKIN format to the required solver format.
The flow is modeled as laminar.
A noSlip boundary condition is applied to the tube walls for velocity.
A zeroGradient boundary condition is applied to the tube walls for temperature, representing adiabatic walls.
The simulation stops when the flame front reaches the end of the tube opposite the ignition point.
