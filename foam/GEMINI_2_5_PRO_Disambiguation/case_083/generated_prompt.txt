The simulation models spray combustion in a 2D axisymmetric cylindrical chamber.
The chamber is 0.2 meters long and has a diameter of 0.1 meters.
The turbulence model is the standard k-epsilon model.
Air enters the chamber as a co-flow at the inlet boundary.
The incoming air has a temperature of 800 K.
The incoming air has an axial velocity of 10 m/s.
Liquid dodecane is injected from the center of the inlet boundary.
The total mass flow rate of the injected dodecane is 0.002 kg/s.
The injected dodecane droplets have an initial temperature of 300 K.
The injected dodecane droplets have an initial axial velocity of 20 m/s.
The initial droplet size follows a Rosin-Rammler distribution.
The mean diameter for the Rosin-Rammler distribution is 50 micrometers.
The spread parameter for the Rosin-Rammler distribution is 3.5.
