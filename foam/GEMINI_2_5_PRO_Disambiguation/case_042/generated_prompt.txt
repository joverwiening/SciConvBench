The simulation domain is a two-dimensional rectangle.
The domain height is 1 meter.
The domain width is 2 meters.
The simulation models Rayleigh-Benard convection.
The Rayleigh number is 2500.
The Prandtl number is 0.71.
The gravitational acceleration is 9.81 m/s^2, acting in the negative y-direction.
The bottom horizontal wall is located at y=0.
The top horizontal wall is located at y=1.
The temperature of the bottom wall is fixed at 300 K.
The temperature of the top wall is fixed at 299 K.
The velocity boundary condition for the top and bottom walls is no-slip.
The vertical side walls have periodic boundary conditions for all variables.
The initial velocity field is zero everywhere.
The initial temperature field is T(x,y) = (300 - y) + 0.1*sin(pi*x).
