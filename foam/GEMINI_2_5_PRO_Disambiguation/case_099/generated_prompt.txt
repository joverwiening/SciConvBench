The simulation models steady buoyant flow through a porous medium.
The solver to be used is buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam.
The computational domain is a 2D rectangle with dimensions 0.2 m in width, 0.4 m in height, and 0.01 m in thickness.
A Darcy-Forchheimer porous resistance source is applied explicitly using fvOptions.
The Darcy coefficient (d) is an isotropic tensor with a value of (1e8 1e8 1e8).
The Forchheimer coefficient (f) is an isotropic tensor with a value of (0 0 0).
The gravitational acceleration vector is (0 -9.81 0) m/s^2.
The fluid's kinematic viscosity (nu) is 1e-6 m^2/s.
The fluid's thermal expansion coefficient (beta) is 2e-4 1/K.
The left wall has a fixed temperature of 303 K.
The right wall has a fixed temperature of 293 K.
The top and bottom walls have a zero-gradient thermal boundary condition.
All walls (left, right, top, bottom) have a no-slip velocity boundary condition.
The pressure boundary condition for all walls is fixedFluxPressure.
