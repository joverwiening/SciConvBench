The simulation is a two-dimensional Rayleigh-Benard convection case.
The simulation uses the buoyantFoam solver.
The geometry is a rectangular cavity 9 units wide and 1 unit tall.
The mesh consists of 90 cells in the x-direction, 10 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The front and back patches are of type empty to enforce a 2D simulation.
Gravity is uniform and acts in the negative y-direction with a magnitude of 9.81.
The thermo-physical model is heRhoThermo with a Boussinesq equation of state.
The reference density rho0 is 1.0 kg/m^3.
The reference temperature T0 is 300 K.
The thermal expansion coefficient beta is 3.3e-3 1/K.
The specific heat at constant volume Cv is 714.3 J/(kg*K).
The standard heat of formation Hf is 0.
The dynamic viscosity mu is 1.5e-5 Pa*s.
The Prandtl number Pr is 0.714.
The molecular weight molWeight is 29 g/mol.
The initial velocity field U is uniform at (0 0 0) m/s.
The initial temperature field T is uniform at 300 K.
The initial turbulent kinetic energy field k is uniform at 1e-5 m^2/s^2.
The initial turbulent dissipation rate field epsilon is uniform at 1e-5 m^2/s^3.
The initial turbulent viscosity field nut is uniform at 0 m^2/s.
The initial turbulent thermal diffusivity field alphat is uniform at 0 m^2/s.
The initial pressure field p is uniform at 0.
The initial pressure field p_rgh is uniform at 0.
The top wall has a fixed temperature of 295 K.
The bottom wall has a fixed temperature of 305 K.
The vertical side walls are adiabatic (zeroGradient temperature).
All solid walls (top, bottom, sides) have a no-slip velocity condition.
For p_rgh, all solid walls use a fixedFluxPressure boundary condition.
For p, all solid walls use a calculated boundary condition.
For k, all solid walls use the kqRWallFunction boundary condition.
For epsilon, all solid walls use the epsilonWallFunction boundary condition.
For nut, all solid walls use the nutkWallFunction boundary condition.
For alphat, all solid walls use the compressible::alphatJayatillekeWallFunction with a turbulent Prandtl number Prt of 0.85.
The simulation runs for a total time of 1000 seconds.
A fixed time step (deltaT) of 1 second is used.
Simulation results are written to disk every 50 time steps.
The time discretization scheme (ddtScheme) is Euler.
The momentum divergence scheme for div(phi,U) is Gauss linearUpwindV grad(U).
The energy divergence scheme for div(phi,e) is Gauss linearUpwind default.
The turbulence divergence schemes for div(phi,k) and div(phi,epsilon) are Gauss upwind.
All gradient schemes (gradSchemes) are Gauss linear.
All Laplacian schemes (laplacianSchemes) are Gauss linear corrected.
All interpolation schemes (interpolationSchemes) are linear.
The PISO algorithm uses 1 corrector step (nCorrectors).
The solver for p_rgh has a tolerance of 1e-8 and a relative tolerance of 0.01.
The solvers for U, e, k, and epsilon have a tolerance of 1e-8 and a relative tolerance of 0.01.
The final solvers for all fields have a relative tolerance of 0.
