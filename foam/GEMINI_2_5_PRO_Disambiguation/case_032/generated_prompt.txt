The simulation is a transient 2D Rayleigh-Benard convection case.
The OpenFOAM solver to be used is buoyantBoussinesqPimpleFoam.
The Boussinesq approximation for buoyancy is enabled.
The domain height is 0.10 meters.
The domain width is 0.40 meters.
The mesh consists of 160 cells in the horizontal direction and 40 cells in the vertical direction.
The fluid has a Prandtl number of 0.71.
The fluid has a kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The fluid has a thermal expansion coefficient of 3.33e-3 1/K.
The gravitational acceleration is 9.81 m/s^2 and acts in the negative vertical direction.
The temperature of the bottom wall is fixed at a value 1 K higher than the top wall.
The temperature of the top wall is fixed at a constant value.
The side walls are periodic boundary conditions for all fields.
The bottom and top walls are no-slip boundaries for velocity.
The initial velocity of the fluid is zero.
