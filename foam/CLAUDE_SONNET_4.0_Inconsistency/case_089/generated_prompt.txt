Apply no-slip boundary conditions to all walls except the moving top lid.

Initialize the temperature field with a linear gradient from 300 K at the bottom to 350 K at the top.
Set initial velocity field to zero everywhere.
Run the simulation for 100 seconds to reach steady-state mixed convection conditions.
