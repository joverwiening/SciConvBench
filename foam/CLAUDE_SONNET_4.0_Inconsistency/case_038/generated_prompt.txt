Create a thermo-elastic deformation simulation of a solid aluminum block with the following specifications:

The aluminum block has dimensions 0.1 m x 0.1 m x 0.2 m (width x depth x height).
The block is oriented vertically with the fixed face at the bottom (z=0) and the heated face at the top (z=0.2).
The material properties for aluminum are E = 69e9 Pa, nu = 0.33, rho = 2700 kg/m³, thermal expansion coefficient alpha = 2.3e-5 1/K, and reference temperature T0 = 300 K.
The temperature field T is available from a prior conduction solve or prescribed separately to drive thermal strain.
The boundary condition for displacement (0/D) sets fixedFace as fixedValue uniform (0 0 0) and all other faces as zeroGradient.
The boundary condition for temperature (0/T) sets heatedFace as fixedValue uniform 400, fixedFace as zeroGradient, and other faces as zeroGradient.
The system/controlDict uses application buoyantPimpleFoam.
The simulation computes displacement and stress fields due to thermal expansion from the temperature difference between the heated and fixed faces.
The fixed face prevents movement in all directions while the heated face and sides are free to expand.
The temperature gradient from 400 K at the top to the natural distribution toward the bottom drives the thermal strain calculation.
