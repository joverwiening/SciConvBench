Set up a transient simulation of turbulent flow over a 2D cylinder in a channel using pimpleFoam.
The cylinder diameter is 0.1 m.
The channel height is 1.0 m (10 cylinder diameters).
The channel length is 3.0 m (30 cylinder diameters).
The cylinder is positioned at 1.0 m from the inlet (10 cylinder diameters downstream).
The cylinder is centered vertically in the channel.
Target Reynolds number is 100,000 based on cylinder diameter and inlet velocity.
Use standard air properties: density 1.225 kg/m³, kinematic viscosity 1.48e-05 m²/s.
The inlet velocity is 10 m/s uniform across the inlet.
Apply noSlip boundary conditions to the cylinder surface and channel walls.
Use slip or symmetry boundary conditions for the outlet.
Configure turbulenceProperties with simulationType LES.
Include LES sub-dictionary with LESModel Smagorinsky and delta cubeRootVol.
Include RAS sub-dictionary with RASModel kOmegaSST, turbulence on, and printCoeffs on.
Set up appropriate initial conditions for velocity and turbulence fields.
Configure controlDict for transient simulation with appropriate time step and end time.
Use appropriate mesh resolution with y+ values suitable for wall-resolved LES near walls.
