Create a 2D bubble column simulation using twoPhaseEulerFoam.
The column geometry is 0.5m wide in X-direction, 2m high in Y-direction, and 0.01m deep in Z-direction.
The column is initially filled with water as the continuous phase.
Air is the dispersed phase and is injected from a sparger at the bottom center.
The simulation models the rise of air bubbles due to buoyancy forces.
Set the gravity vector in constant/g to g (-9.81 0 0).
Configure the bottom boundary as an inlet patch with alpha.air set to fixedValue 1 and U.air set to fixedValue (0 0.1 0).
Configure the top boundary as an outlet patch.
Set the side walls (left and right boundaries) to noSlip boundary conditions.
Set the front and back patches to empty boundary conditions for 2D simulation.
The air phase rises through the water column under the influence of gravity and buoyancy.
