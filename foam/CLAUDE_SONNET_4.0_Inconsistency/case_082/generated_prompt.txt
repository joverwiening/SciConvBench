Configure a simulation for the separation of three immiscible fluids (oil, water, and gas) in a horizontal gravity separator using the VOF method.
The solver specified in controlDict is interFoam.
The phases are defined in transportProperties with oil density 850 kg/m³ and viscosity 0.001 Pa·s, water density 1000 kg/m³ and viscosity 0.001 Pa·s, and gas density 1.225 kg/m³ and viscosity 1.8e-05 Pa·s.
The initial state is a stratified mixture set using setFieldsDict.
The alpha.water field represents the volume fraction of water.
The simulation tracks the interface between the three fluids over time as they separate under gravity.
Gravity is set to (0 -9.81 0).
