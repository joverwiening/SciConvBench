Set up a 2D incompressible laminar flow simulation in a rectangular channel using the icoFoam solver.
The channel is 0.5 m long in the x-direction and 0.1 m high in the y-direction.
The mesh should be one cell thick in the z-direction.
Use a mesh resolution of 100 cells in the x-direction and 40 cells in the y-direction.
In blockMeshDict, define the geometry with patches: inlet, outlet, topWall, bottomWall, and frontAndBack.
For the frontAndBack patches, specify 'type wall;'.
The simulation models Poiseuille flow with a Reynolds number of approximately 100.
Use water properties with kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m²/s.
In the '0/U' file, set a parabolic inlet velocity profile with a maximum of 1 m/s.
Apply zeroGradient boundary condition at the outlet for velocity.
Apply noSlip conditions on topWall and bottomWall for velocity.
For the frontAndBack patches, apply the noSlip boundary condition for velocity.
In '0/p', use fixedValue 0 at the outlet for pressure.
Apply zeroGradient boundary condition on all other patches for pressure, including frontAndBack.
