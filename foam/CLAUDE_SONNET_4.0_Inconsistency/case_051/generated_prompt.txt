Simulate the transient start-up flow of a Bingham plastic fluid in a 2D pipe.
Use the icoFoam solver.
Create a rectangular domain with 1.0m length and 0.1m diameter.
Use blockMesh to create a mesh with 100 cells in the axial (x) direction and 20 cells in the radial (y) direction.
Set the front and back patches to type empty.
Apply an inlet boundary condition with fixed velocity of (0.1 0 0) m/s.
Apply an outlet boundary condition with zeroGradient for velocity and fixed pressure of 0.
Apply noSlip boundary conditions on the walls.
Run the simulation from t=0 to t=5s.
Define the fluid in constant/transportProperties as a Bingham plastic with transportModel BinghamPlastic, rho 1000, tau0 10, mu0 0.1, and muInf 0.01.
Initialize the velocity field at t=0 with zero velocity throughout the domain.
Initialize the pressure field at t=0 with zero pressure throughout the domain.
