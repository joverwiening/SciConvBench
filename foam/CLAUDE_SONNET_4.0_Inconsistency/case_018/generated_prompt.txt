Set up a laminar counter-flow diffusion flame simulation using reactingFoam solver in OpenFOAM.
Create a 2-D opposed-jet configuration with domain dimensions x in [0, 0.02] m, y in [-0.01, 0.01] m, z in [-0.01, 0.01] m.
Use a structured mesh with 100 cells in x-direction, 40 cells in y-direction, and 1 cell in z-direction.
Set frontAndBack patches as empty boundary type for 2-D simulation.
Define four patches: left fuel inlet at x=0, right air inlet at x=0.02, top outlet, and bottom outlet.
Configure fuel inlet with pure methane (CH4) at 293 K temperature and velocity of 0.1 m/s in +x direction.
Configure air inlet with air composition (Y_O2 = 0.23, balance N2) at 293 K temperature and velocity of 0.1 m/s in -x direction.
Initialize the domain temperature field to 2000 K.
Set pressure boundary conditions as fixedValue 101325 Pa for both inlets and zeroGradient for both outlets.
Use EulerImplicit time integration scheme for chemistry with Rosenbrock43 ODE solver.
Apply laminar models for both combustion and momentum transport.
Configure thermodynamics with hePsiThermo, coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture for species mixing, Sutherland transport properties, and JANAF thermodynamic data.
Set time stepping parameters: deltaT = 1e-4 s, endTime = 0.5 s, writeInterval = 0.05 s.
