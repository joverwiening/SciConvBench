Set up a simulation of a three-phase fluidized bed in a cylindrical column.
The phases are air (gas), water (liquid), and sand (solid particles).
Use the solver twoPhaseEulerFoam.
The column has a diameter of 0.1 m and a height of 1 m.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction with value (0 -9.81 0).
Define the phases in phaseProperties as (air water sand).
Treat air as a perfect gas.
Treat water as an incompressible liquid.
Treat sand as a constant density solid with a diameter of 500 microns.
Run the simulation as transient for 10 seconds.
Set initial conditions with a bed of sand settled at the bottom 0.2 m of the column.
In the settled bed region, set alpha.sand = 0.6 and alpha.water = 0.4.
In the rest of the column above 0.2 m height, set alpha.water = 1.
Inject air from the bottom inlet at 0.5 m/s velocity.
Use the Gidaspow drag model for interactions between the phases.
Set the outlet boundary condition at the top of the column as a pressure outlet with atmospheric pressure and zero gradient for velocity components.
