Set up a 2D simulation of incompressible, laminar flow over a NACA 0012 airfoil using simpleFoam.
The Reynolds number is 1000 based on chord length.
The angle of attack is 5 degrees.
The mesh is extruded one cell in the z-direction.
Use the patch name frontAndBack for the out-of-plane faces.
In constant/polyMesh/boundary, set frontAndBack type to wall.
In 0/U, set frontAndBack type to slip.
The airfoil chord length is 1 meter.
The freestream velocity is 0.001 m/s to achieve Re = 1000 with kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m²/s.
The computational domain extends 10 chord lengths upstream, 20 chord lengths downstream, and 10 chord lengths above and below the airfoil.
Use inlet boundary condition with fixed velocity at the upstream boundary.
Use outlet boundary condition with zero gradient pressure at the downstream boundary.
Use slip wall boundary conditions at the top and bottom domain boundaries.
Use no-slip wall boundary condition on the airfoil surface.
Initialize the pressure field to zero everywhere.
Initialize the velocity field to the freestream velocity throughout the domain.
