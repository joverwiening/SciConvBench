Model a steady-state, buoyancy-driven flow in a vertical heated pipe using the buoyantSimpleFoam solver.
The pipe has a length of 2m, aligned with the Z-axis, and a diameter of 0.1m.
Air enters at the bottom (z=0) at 300K and is heated by the pipe wall, which is maintained at a constant temperature of 350K.
The goal is to observe the natural convection flow established due to density differences.
In the constant/g dictionary, set gravity to `g (0 0 -9.81);` to enable buoyancy-driven flow.
Use thermophysical properties for air with the `perfectGas` equation of state.
Set the inlet boundary condition for U to `pressureInletOutletVelocity` and for T to `fixedValue 300`.
Set the outlet boundary conditions to `zeroGradient` for both U and T, with pressure set to `fixedValue 101325` (atmospheric pressure).
Set the wall boundary conditions to `noSlip` for U and `fixedValue 350` for T.
