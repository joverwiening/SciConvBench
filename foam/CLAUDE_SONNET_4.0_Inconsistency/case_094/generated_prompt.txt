Simulate the steady-state, compressible flow of natural gas through a pipe with a porous baffle.
The pipe has a length of 10 meters and a diameter of 0.2 meters.
The inlet total pressure is 2e5 Pa and the outlet static pressure is 1e5 Pa.
The gas is modeled as a perfect gas using the perfectGas equation of state.
Use the Sutherland transport model for viscosity calculation.
Define thermophysicalProperties with molecular weight of 16.04 kg/kmol for natural gas (methane).
Set reference temperature to 288.15 K and Sutherland coefficients As = 1.67e-6 and Ts = 170.672 K.
The porous zone is defined in the fvOptions dictionary with Darcy coefficient of 1e8 1/m² and Forchheimer coefficient of 1e4 1/m.
Use porousSimpleFoam solver for the simulation.
The porous baffle creates resistance to flow according to the Darcy-Forchheimer model.
