Set up a transient simulation of 2D vortex shedding behind a cylinder at Reynolds number 100.
The domain is a rectangular channel with dimensions 20 m length × 10 m width × 0.1 m height.
The circular cylinder has a diameter of 1 m and is positioned with its center at coordinates (5, 5, 0.05).
Use the pimpleFoam solver for transient incompressible flow.
The fluid is incompressible with kinematic viscosity nu = 0.01 m²/s.
The inlet velocity is U = (1 0 0) m/s.
Apply boundary conditions as follows: 'inlet' uses fixedValue for velocity, 'outlet' uses zeroGradient for velocity and fixedValue p=0 for pressure, 'topAndBottom' uses slip conditions, 'cylinder' uses noSlip condition, and 'frontAndBack' uses empty condition for 2D simulation.
Run the simulation from t=0 to t=200 s with a time step deltaT of 0.1 s.
The goal is to observe the von Kármán vortex street formation and calculate the Strouhal number from the vortex shedding frequency.
Use appropriate mesh refinement around the cylinder to capture the boundary layer and wake region accurately.
Set up monitoring of forces on the cylinder to extract the shedding frequency for Strouhal number calculation.
