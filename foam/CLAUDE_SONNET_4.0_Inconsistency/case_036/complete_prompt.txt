Simulate the quasi-static deflection of a 3D steel cantilever beam using linear elasticity. The beam is 1 m long, 0.1 m high, and 0.1 m wide. One end (`fixed`) is clamped, and the free end (`loaded`) has a downward traction load.

In `constant/mechanicalProperties`, use steel properties:
- Young's modulus `E = 200e9` Pa
- Poisson ratio `nu = 0.3`

In `0/D`:
- `fixed`: `fixedValue uniform (0 0 0)`
- `loaded`: `tractionDisplacement` with `traction uniform (0 -1000 0)` and `pressure uniform 0`
- all other faces: `zeroGradient`

In `system/controlDict`, set:
```foam
application     solidDisplacementFoam;
endTime         1;
```
