Simulate the static deflection of a 2D cantilever beam under a downward traction load using linear elasticity.
The beam is 1.0 m long, 0.1 m high, and 0.01 m thick (depth in z-direction).
Use a structured mesh of 100 × 10 × 1 cells.
The material is aluminum with Young's modulus E = 70e9 Pa and Poisson's ratio nu = 0.33.
Apply boundary conditions where the left end (x=0) is fixed with fixedValue uniform (0 0 0).
Apply boundary conditions where the right end (x=1) has tractionDisplacement with traction uniform (0 -10000 0) and pressure uniform 0.
Apply zeroGradient boundary conditions on the top and bottom faces.
Set the application in system/controlDict to solidDisplacementFoam for static structural analysis.
Use plane stress conditions for the 2D analysis.
The simulation solves for displacement field under static loading conditions.
