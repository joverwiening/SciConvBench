Configure a steady-state turbulent pipe flow simulation using simpleFoam with the k-epsilon RANS model.
The fluid is water with a kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m²/s.
The pipe diameter is 0.1 m and length is 1.0 m (10 diameters for fully developed flow).
The inlet velocity is 1 m/s.
Initialize the turbulent kinetic energy (k) internalField to uniform 0 in the 0/k file.
Initialize the turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) internalField to uniform 0 in the 0/epsilon file.
Apply kqRWallFunction boundary condition for k on the pipe wall.
Apply epsilonWallFunction boundary condition for epsilon on the pipe wall.
Apply fixedValue boundary condition for velocity at the inlet with uniform (1 0 0).
Apply fixedValue boundary condition for k at the inlet with uniform 0.0375 (assuming 5% turbulence intensity).
Apply fixedValue boundary condition for epsilon at the inlet with uniform 0.00421 (calculated from k and turbulent length scale).
Apply zeroGradient boundary condition for velocity at the outlet.
Apply fixedValue boundary condition for pressure at the outlet with uniform 0.
Apply zeroGradient boundary condition for k at the outlet.
Apply zeroGradient boundary condition for epsilon at the outlet.
Apply zeroGradient boundary condition for pressure at the inlet.
