Simulate a single spherical particle settling in a quiescent fluid column using the DPMFoam solver.
The domain is a vertical cylinder with a height of 1m along the Y-axis and a diameter of 0.2m.
The fluid is water with a density of 1000 kg/m³.
A single particle with a diameter of 1mm and density of 2500 kg/m³ is initialized at position (0, 0.9, 0).
The simulation tracks the particle's trajectory as it settles under gravity.
In the constant/g dictionary, the acceleration due to gravity is specified as g (0 -9.81 0).
The fluid velocity is initialized to zero everywhere.
The walls of the cylinder are noSlip boundary conditions.
The top and bottom patches are simple wall boundary conditions.
The particle settles from its initial position at (0, 0.9, 0) downward through the water column.
