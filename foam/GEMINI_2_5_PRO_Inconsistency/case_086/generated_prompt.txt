The simulation is for the transient cooling of a hot block in a 2D sealed room.
The solver to be used is `buoyantPimpleFoam`.
The room dimensions are 5 meters in width and 3 meters in height.
The simulation is 2D, with the z-direction being one cell thick and using `empty` boundary conditions.
A 0.5 meter by 0.5 meter block is initialized at a temperature of 400 K.
The center of the block is located at coordinates (2.5, 0.25).
The air surrounding the block is initialized at a temperature of 300 K.
All walls have an adiabatic temperature boundary condition (`zeroGradient`).
All walls have a no-slip velocity boundary condition (`noSlip`).
Gravity is defined as (0 -9.81 0).
The equation of state for the fluid is `perfectGas`.
The fluid is air with standard thermophysical properties.
The turbulence model is `laminar`.
The mesh consists of 100 cells in the x-direction and 60 cells in the y-direction.
The total simulation time is 50 seconds.
The time step is controlled by a maximum Courant number of 0.5.
