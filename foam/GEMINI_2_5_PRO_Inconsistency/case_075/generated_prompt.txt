The simulation is a 2D bubble column using the twoPhaseEulerFoam solver.
The computational domain is 0.5 meters wide in the X-direction and 2.0 meters high in the Y-direction.
The front and back boundaries are of type empty.
The continuous phase is water and the dispersed phase is air.
The column is initially filled with stationary water.
The gravity vector is (0 -9.81 0).
The turbulence model for the continuous phase is the k-epsilon model.
The mean diameter of the air bubbles is 0.003 meters.
The interfacial drag model is SchillerNaumann.
No other interfacial force models like lift or virtual mass are included.
The bottom boundary consists of a central air inlet and side walls.
The central air inlet sparger is 0.05 meters wide.
The air inlet has a fixed air volume fraction of 1.
The air inlet has a fixed upward velocity of (0 0.1 0) m/s.
The remaining portions of the bottom boundary are no-slip walls.
The side boundaries of the column are no-slip walls.
The top boundary is an outlet open to the atmosphere.
The pressure at the top outlet is a fixed value of 0 Pa (gauge).
The velocity at the top outlet uses the pressureInletOutletVelocity condition.
The air volume fraction at the top outlet uses the inletOutlet condition.
The simulation runs for a total physical time of 30 seconds.
The time step is controlled dynamically to maintain a maximum Courant number of 0.5.
Standard second-order numerical schemes are used for discretization.
