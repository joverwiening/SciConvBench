The simulation is a two-dimensional Rayleigh-Bénard convection case.
The geometry is a rectangular cavity 9 units wide and 1 unit tall.
The mesh consists of 90 cells in the x-direction, 10 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The OpenFOAM solver to be used is buoyantFoam.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction.
The simulation uses the Boussinesq approximation for density.
The reference density is 1.0 kg/m^3 at a reference temperature of 300 K.
The thermal expansion coefficient is 3.3e-3 1/K.
The dynamic viscosity is 1.5e-5 Pa.s.
The Prandtl number is 0.714.
The specific heat at constant volume is 714.3 J/(kg.K).
The heat of formation is 0 J/kg.
The molecular weight is 29 g/mol.
The turbulence model is RANS with the k-epsilon model.
The bottom wall temperature is fixed at 305 K.
The top wall temperature is fixed at 295 K.
The vertical side walls are adiabatic, specified with a zeroGradient boundary condition for temperature.
A no-slip velocity condition is applied to the top, bottom, and side walls.
The front and back patches use the `empty` boundary condition for all fields to enforce two-dimensionality.
Wall functions are applied on the top, bottom, and side walls for turbulent quantities.
For k, the boundary condition is kqRWallFunction.
For epsilon, the boundary condition is epsilonWallFunction.
For nut, the boundary condition is nutkWallFunction.
For alphat, the boundary condition is compressible::alphatJayatillekeWallFunction with a turbulent Prandtl number of 0.85.
For p_rgh, the boundary condition on walls is fixedFluxPressure.
For p, the boundary condition on walls is calculated.
The flow is initialized at rest with a uniform velocity of (0 0 0) m/s.
The initial temperature field is a uniform 300 K.
The initial turbulent kinetic energy k is a uniform 1e-5 m^2/s^2.
The initial turbulent dissipation rate epsilon is a uniform 1e-5 m^2/s^3.
The simulation runs for a total physical time of 1000 seconds.
A fixed timestep of 1 second is used.
Results are written every 50 timesteps.
The solvers for p_rgh, U, e, k, and epsilon have a tolerance of 1e-8 and a relative tolerance of 0.01.
The final solvers for all fields have a relative tolerance of 0.
