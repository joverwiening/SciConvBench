The simulation is a 2D supersonic jet using the pimpleFoam solver.
The fluid is air, treated as a perfect gas.
The thermophysical model is hePsiThermo with a perfectGas equation of state and a Sutherland transport model.
The geometry consists of a straight nozzle with an exit height of 0.01 meters and a length of 0.05 meters.
The nozzle discharges into a 2D rectangular domain extending 0.5 meters downstream from the nozzle exit.
The domain extends 0.1 meters above the nozzle centerline and 0.1 meters below the nozzle centerline.
The mesh is 2D, extruded one cell in the z-direction, with frontAndBack patches set to type empty.
The mesh has a resolution of 100 cells vertically across the nozzle exit, with grading towards the far-field boundaries.
The simulation uses the k-Omega SST turbulence model.
The nozzle inlet boundary has a fixed velocity of (686 0 0) m/s.
The nozzle inlet boundary has a fixed static temperature of 300 K.
The nozzle inlet boundary has a fixed static pressure of 101325 Pa.
The nozzle walls are solid with a noSlip boundary condition for velocity and zeroGradient for other fields.
The far-field and outlet boundaries use the waveTransmissive boundary condition for pressure and temperature.
The far-field and outlet boundaries use the zeroGradient boundary condition for velocity.
The ambient domain is initialized with a quiescent fluid at a static pressure of 50662.5 Pa.
The ambient domain is initialized with a static temperature of 300 K.
Discretization schemes suitable for shock capturing, such as limitedLinear, are used for divergence terms.
The PIMPLE algorithm is used for the solution.
The simulation runs with an adjustable time step controlled by a maximum Courant number of 0.5.
The simulation runs until monitored variables in the jet plume reach a statistically steady state.
