The simulation models 2D heat transfer in a channel containing a heated block.
The fluid is air, and the flow is laminar.
The OpenFOAM solver to be used is `buoyantPimpleFoam`.
The channel has a height of 0.1 meters.
The channel has a length of 2.0 meters.
A heated block is placed on the bottom wall of the channel.
The block has a length of 0.1 meters.
The block has a height of 0.05 meters.
The front face of the block is located 0.5 meters from the channel inlet.
The gravity vector is (0 -9.81 0).
The simulation will be run until a steady-state condition is reached.
The target number of cells for the computational mesh is 30,000.
The inlet velocity is a uniform value of (0.1 0 0) m/s.
The inlet temperature is a fixed uniform value of 300 K.
The outlet temperature boundary condition is `zeroGradient`.
The heated block is maintained at a fixed uniform temperature of 400 K.
The top and bottom channel walls are adiabatic, with a `zeroGradient` temperature boundary condition.
