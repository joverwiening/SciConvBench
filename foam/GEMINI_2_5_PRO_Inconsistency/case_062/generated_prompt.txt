The solver application is solidDisplacementFoam.
The simulation is a static analysis.
The simulation is run under the plane stress assumption.
The beam geometry is a 3D block 1.0 m long, 0.1 m high, and 0.01 m thick.
A structured mesh of 100 cells in length, 10 cells in height, and 1 cell in thickness is used.
The material is Aluminum with a Young's modulus of 70e9 Pa and a Poisson's ratio of 0.33.
The displacement field is named D.
The patch at x=0 is named fixedEnd and has a fixedValue boundary condition of uniform (0 0 0).
The patch at x=1.0 is named loadEnd and has a tractionDisplacement boundary condition.
The traction for the loadEnd patch is uniform (0 -10000 0).
The pressure for the loadEnd patch is uniform 0.
The top and bottom patches have a zeroGradient boundary condition.
The front and back patches have an empty boundary condition.
