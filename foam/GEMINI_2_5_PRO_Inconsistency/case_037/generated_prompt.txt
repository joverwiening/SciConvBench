The simulation is for steady, incompressible, laminar flow.
The fluid is water with a kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m^2/s.
The solver to be used is simpleFoam.
The domain is a 2D channel with a length of 0.012 m and a height of 0.002 m.
A rigid, stationary, circular cylinder obstacle with a diameter of 0.0002 m is placed in the channel.
The center of the cylinder is located at (0.004 m, 0.001 m) relative to the inlet and bottom wall.
The mesh is 2D, extruded one cell in the spanwise direction.
The frontAndBack boundary patches are of type empty.
The inlet velocity is a uniform fixed value of (0.1 0 0) m/s.
The outlet pressure is a uniform fixed value of 0.
The top and bottom channel walls have a noSlip boundary condition for velocity and a zeroGradient for pressure.
The obstacle surface has a noSlip boundary condition for velocity and a zeroGradient for pressure.
The inlet has a zeroGradient boundary condition for pressure.
The outlet has a zeroGradient boundary condition for velocity.
