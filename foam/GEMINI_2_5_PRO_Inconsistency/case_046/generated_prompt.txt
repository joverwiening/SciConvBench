The simulation is a 2D transient analysis of incompressible, turbulent flow over a cylinder.
The pimpleFoam solver is used.
The fluid is standard air with a kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The channel has a height of 10 meters and a total length of 30 meters.
The cylinder has a diameter of 1 meter.
The cylinder is centered vertically in the channel at a position 10 meters from the inlet.
The inlet boundary condition is a uniform velocity of 10 m/s.
The outlet boundary condition is zero-gradient for velocity and a fixed pressure of 0.
The cylinder surface has a noSlip boundary condition.
The top and bottom channel walls have a noSlip boundary condition.
The simulation type is Large Eddy Simulation.
The LES model is Smagorinsky.
The LES delta is cubeRootVol.
The simulation runs for a total physical time of 10 seconds.
The time step is controlled by a maximum Courant number of 0.7.
The mesh is refined around the cylinder and in its wake.
The target cell size in the wake region is 0.05 meters.
Wall functions are used on the cylinder surface.
Second-order discretization schemes are used for all spatial terms.
The time scheme is a second-order backward implicit scheme.
