The simulation will be a 2D incompressible laminar flow in a rectangular channel.
The solver to be used is icoFoam.
The channel geometry is 0.5 m in length (x-direction) and 0.1 m in height (y-direction).
The mesh will be one cell thick in the z-direction.
The mesh resolution will be 100 cells in the x-direction and 20 cells in the y-direction.
The mesh will be generated using blockMeshDict.
Five patches will be defined: inlet, outlet, topWall, bottomWall, and frontAndBack.
The frontAndBack patch type will be 'empty'.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 0.001334 m^2/s.
The inlet velocity boundary condition for 'U' will be a parabolic profile with a maximum velocity of 1 m/s.
The outlet velocity boundary condition for 'U' will be 'zeroGradient'.
The topWall and bottomWall velocity boundary condition for 'U' will be 'noSlip'.
The frontAndBack boundary condition for 'U' will be 'empty'.
The outlet pressure boundary condition for 'p' will be 'fixedValue' with a value of 0.
The inlet, topWall, and bottomWall pressure boundary condition for 'p' will be 'zeroGradient'.
The frontAndBack boundary condition for 'p' will be 'empty'.
The simulation end time will be 5.0 seconds.
The time step 'deltaT' will be 0.0025 seconds.
The simulation results will be written every 0.5 seconds.
