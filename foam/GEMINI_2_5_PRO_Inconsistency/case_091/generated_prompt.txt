The simulation is for 2D natural convection in a square cavity.
The solver to be used is buoyantBoussinesqPimpleFoam.
The cavity is a 1 meter by 1 meter square.
A uniform mesh of 40x40 cells will be used.
The fluid is air, treated as incompressible with the Boussinesq approximation.
The Prandtl number is 0.71.
The kinematic viscosity is 1.536e-3 m^2/s.
The thermal expansion coefficient is 0.00339 1/K.
These properties result in a Rayleigh number of 1e5.
Gravity acts in the negative y-direction with a magnitude of 9.81 m/s^2.
The velocity boundary condition on all four walls is noSlip.
The temperature of the left wall is fixed at 300 K.
The temperature of the right wall is fixed at 290 K.
The top wall has a zeroGradient temperature boundary condition.
The bottom wall has a zeroGradient temperature boundary condition.
The initial velocity in the domain is zero.
The initial temperature in the domain is 295 K.
Second-order discretization schemes are to be used for all terms.
The simulation will be run until a steady-state solution is reached.
