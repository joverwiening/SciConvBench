The simulation is a transient Rayleigh-Bénard convection case.
The solver is buoyantPimpleFoam.
The domain is a 2D rectangle with a width of 0.02 meters and a height of 0.01 meters.
The fluid is water, modeled using the Boussinesq approximation.
The gravitational acceleration is -9.81 m/s^2 in the y-direction.
The bottom wall is a no-slip boundary with a fixed temperature of 305 K.
The top wall is a no-slip boundary with a fixed temperature of 295 K.
The side walls are no-slip, adiabatic boundaries.
The initial velocity field is zero.
The initial temperature field is a linear gradient from 305 K at the bottom to 295 K at the top.
The simulation runs for a total time of 2000 seconds.
In constant/thermophysicalProperties, set thermoType to hePsiThermo.
In constant/thermophysicalProperties, set equationOfState to Boussinesq.
The reference temperature T0 for the Boussinesq approximation is 300 K.
The kinematic viscosity is 8.57e-7 m^2/s.
The Prandtl number is 5.83.
The thermal expansion coefficient beta is 2.57e-4 1/K.
In system/fvSchemes, the scheme for div(phi,T) is Gauss linear.
In system/fvSchemes, the scheme for div(phi,U) is Gauss upwind.
The mesh resolution is 100 cells in the x-direction and 50 cells in the y-direction.
The time step is controlled by a maximum Courant number of 0.5.
