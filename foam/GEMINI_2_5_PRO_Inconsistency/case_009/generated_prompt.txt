The simulation will model compressible inviscid supersonic flow over a 15-degree wedge.
The OpenFOAM solver rhoCentralFoam will be used.
The Kurganov flux scheme will be used for convective terms.
The domain is a 2D rectangular channel defined by the provided blockMeshDict.
The domain extends from x=-0.15242m to x=0.3048m.
The domain extends from y=0m to y=0.1524m.
The domain extends from z=-0.005m to z=0.005m.
A wedge obstacle is located on the bottom boundary, starting at x=0 with a 15-degree angle.
A structured mesh with two blocks will be used.
The upstream block will have a resolution of 40x40x1 cells.
The downstream block will have a resolution of 80x40x1 cells.
The blockMeshDict provided by the user will be used to generate the mesh.
The inlet boundary condition is a patch with a fixed velocity of 5 m/s in the x-direction.
The inlet boundary condition has a fixed pressure of 1 Pa.
The inlet boundary condition has a fixed temperature of 1 K.
The outlet boundary condition is a patch with zero-gradient conditions for all flow variables.
The top boundary is a symmetryPlane.
The bottom boundary before the wedge is a symmetryPlane.
The obstacle surface is a patch with a slip velocity condition.
The obstacle surface has a zero-gradient condition for pressure and temperature.
The front and back patches are of type empty for a 2D simulation.
The simulation will run from time 0 to 0.2 seconds.
A fixed time step of 0.0001 seconds will be used.
Simulation results will be written every 0.02 seconds.
The fluid is modeled as a perfect gas.
The molecular weight of the gas is 11640.3 kg/kmol.
The constant specific heat at constant pressure (Cp) is 2.5 J/(kg·K).
The Prandtl number is 1.
The viscosity model is set to none to ensure an inviscid simulation.
The vanLeer scheme will be used for reconstruction of density, velocity, and temperature.
The Euler scheme will be used for time discretization.
The Gauss linear scheme will be used for all gradient calculations.
The internal field for velocity, pressure, and temperature will be initialized with the values specified at the inlet boundary condition.
