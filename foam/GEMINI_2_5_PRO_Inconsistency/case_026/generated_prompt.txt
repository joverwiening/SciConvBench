The simulation is a steady-state, incompressible, turbulent flow case.
The solver to be used is simpleFoam.
The geometry is a 2D representation of the Pitz and Daily combustor with a step height of 0.0127 m.
The mesh will be generated using blockMesh with a resolution of 20 cells across the step height and grading towards the walls.
The fluid is air with a kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The turbulence model is RANS k-Epsilon.
The simulation will run for 500 iterations.
The boundary patch named 'inlet' has a fixed velocity of (10 0 0) m/s.
The boundary patch named 'outlet' has a zero-gradient condition for velocity.
The boundary patches named 'upperWall' and 'lowerWall' are no-slip walls.
The boundary patches for the 2D plane are of type 'empty'.
The pressure is set to zero-gradient at the 'inlet', 'upperWall', and 'lowerWall' patches.
The pressure is set to a fixed value of 0 at the 'outlet' patch.
The inlet turbulence intensity is 5%.
The inlet turbulence length scale is 0.003556 m.
The turbulent kinetic energy 'k' at the inlet is a fixed value calculated from the inlet velocity, turbulence intensity, and length scale.
The turbulent dissipation rate 'epsilon' at the inlet is a fixed value calculated from the inlet velocity, turbulence intensity, and length scale.
The 'k' boundary condition at walls is kqRWallFunction.
The 'epsilon' boundary condition at walls is epsilonWallFunction.
The 'k' and 'epsilon' boundary conditions at the outlet are zero-gradient.
Standard second-order discretization schemes are used in fvSchemes.
Standard solvers, tolerances, and relaxation factors for the SIMPLE algorithm are used in fvSolution.
