The simulation is a 2D, steady-state case for laminar, incompressible flow over a backward-facing step.
The simpleFoam solver is used.
The inlet channel height is 0.5 meters.
The step height is 0.5 meters.
The domain length upstream of the step is 2.5 meters.
The domain length downstream of the step is 10 meters.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 0.0025 m^2/s.
The velocity boundary condition at the 'inlet' patch is a fixed value of uniform (0.5 0 0).
The velocity boundary condition at the 'outlet' patch is zeroGradient.
The velocity boundary condition at the 'stepWall' patch is noSlip.
The velocity boundary condition at the 'topWall' patch is noSlip.
The velocity boundary condition for the 'frontAndBack' patches is empty.
The pressure boundary condition at the 'inlet' patch is zeroGradient.
The pressure boundary condition at the 'outlet' patch is a fixed value of uniform 0.
The pressure boundary condition at the 'stepWall' patch is zeroGradient.
The pressure boundary condition at the 'topWall' patch is zeroGradient.
The pressure boundary condition for the 'frontAndBack' patches is empty.
