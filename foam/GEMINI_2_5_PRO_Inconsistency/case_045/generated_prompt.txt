The simulation is a 2D, steady-state, incompressible airflow analysis.
The solver used is simpleFoam.
The turbulence model is k-omega SST.
The geometry is a NACA 0012 airfoil with a chord length of 1 meter.
The angle of attack is 4 degrees.
The simulation Reynolds number, based on chord length, is 3 million.
The fluid is air with standard sea-level properties (density = 1.225 kg/m^3, dynamic viscosity = 1.81e-5 Pa·s).
The freestream velocity is 44.3 m/s, calculated based on the Reynolds number and fluid properties.
The computational domain extends 15 chord lengths from the leading edge to the inlet.
The computational domain extends 25 chord lengths from the trailing edge to the outlet.
The computational domain extends 15 chord lengths from the airfoil to the top and bottom far-field boundaries.
The `frontAndBack` patches in the `constant/polyMesh/boundary` file are set to `type empty;`.
The inlet boundary condition for velocity `U` is a fixed value corresponding to the freestream velocity and angle of attack.
The inlet boundary condition for pressure `p` is `zeroGradient`.
The outlet boundary condition for velocity `U` is `zeroGradient`.
The outlet boundary condition for pressure `p` is a fixed value of 0.
The airfoil surface boundary condition for velocity `U` is `noSlip`.
The airfoil surface boundary condition for pressure `p` is `zeroGradient`.
The top and bottom far-field boundaries have a `slip` velocity boundary condition.
