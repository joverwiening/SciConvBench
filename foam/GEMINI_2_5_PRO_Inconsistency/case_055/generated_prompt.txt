The simulation is for compressible air flow through a 2D converging-diverging nozzle.
The OpenFOAM solver to be used is `rhoCentralFoam`.
The mesh will have boundary patches named `inlet`, `outlet`, `nozzleWall`, and `frontAndBack`.
The `frontAndBack` patch type will be `empty`.
The nozzle throat height is 0.01 meters.
The area ratio of the outlet to the throat is 1.6875.
The fluid is modeled as a perfect gas with temperature-dependent transport properties calculated using the Sutherland model.
The inlet boundary condition is a stagnation state defined by a total pressure and total temperature.
The inlet total pressure is 700,000 Pa.
The inlet total temperature is 300 K.
The outlet boundary condition is a fixed static pressure of 101,325 Pa.
The `nozzleWall` boundary is an adiabatic, no-slip wall.
The simulation end time is 0.03 seconds.
