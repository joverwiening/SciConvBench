Set up a 3D transient simulation.
The simulation domain is a rectangular tank with dimensions 1m (x) by 0.6m (y) by 0.4m (z).
Use the `multiphaseEulerFoam` solver.
Define three phases named `air`, `water`, and `oil` in the `transportProperties` file.
Set the density of water to 1000 kg/m^3.
Set the kinematic viscosity of water to 1e-6 m^2/s.
Set the density of the light oil to 850 kg/m^3.
Set the kinematic viscosity of the light oil to 1e-5 m^2/s.
Set the density of air to 1.2 kg/m^3.
Set the kinematic viscosity of air to 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
Apply gravity of 9.81 m/s^2 in the negative y-direction.
Initialize the phase fractions using the `setFieldsDict` utility.
Initially, a column of water occupies the region from (0,0,0) to (0.2, 0.2, 0.2).
Initially, a layer of oil occupies the region from (0, 0.2, 0) to (0.2, 0.25, 0.2).
The remainder of the domain is initially filled with air.
The bottom, front, back, left, and right boundaries are no-slip walls.
The top boundary is an atmospheric inlet/outlet condition.
