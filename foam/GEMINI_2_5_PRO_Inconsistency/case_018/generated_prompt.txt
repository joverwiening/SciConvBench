The simulation is a 2-D laminar counter-flow diffusion flame.
The solver to be used is reactingFoam.
The domain extends from 0 to 0.02 m in the x-direction.
The domain extends from -0.01 to 0.01 m in the y-direction.
The domain extends from -0.01 to 0.01 m in the z-direction.
The mesh resolution is 100 cells in x, 40 cells in y, and 1 cell in z.
The 'left' patch is a fuel inlet located at x=0.
The 'right' patch is an air inlet located at x=0.02.
The 'top' and 'bottom' patches are outlets.
The 'frontAndBack' patches are of type 'empty'.
The fuel inlet boundary condition for velocity is a fixed value of (0.1 0 0) m/s.
The fuel inlet boundary condition for temperature is a fixed value of 293 K.
The fuel inlet species is pure methane (CH4 mass fraction is 1.0).
The air inlet boundary condition for velocity is a fixed value of (-0.1 0 0) m/s.
The air inlet boundary condition for temperature is a fixed value of 293 K.
The air inlet species composition is 0.23 mass fraction O2 and 0.77 mass fraction N2.
The pressure boundary condition at both inlets is zero-gradient.
The outlet boundary condition for pressure is a fixed value of 101325 Pa.
The outlet boundary condition for velocity, temperature, and species is zero-gradient.
The initial velocity field in the domain is uniform (0 0 0) m/s.
The initial pressure field in the domain is uniform 101325 Pa.
The initial temperature field in the domain is uniform 2000 K.
The initial species field is composed of combustion products with mass fractions Y_N2=0.725, Y_CO2=0.151, Y_H2O=0.124.
All other initial species mass fractions are zero.
A one-step global reaction mechanism for methane-air combustion is used.
The chemistry solver is EulerImplicit with the Rosenbrock43 ODE solver.
Momentum and energy transport models are laminar.
The thermodynamic model is hePsiThermo.
The mixture model is coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture.
The transport model is Sutherland.
The species thermodynamics model is JANAF.
The simulation timestep (deltaT) is 1e-4 s.
The simulation end time is 0.5 s.
Results are written every 0.05 s.
