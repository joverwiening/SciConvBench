The simulation is a 2D inviscid oblique-shock case.
Use the rhoCentralFoam solver.
The domain extends from (0, 0, 0) to (4.1, 1, 0.1).
The grid resolution is 60 cells in x, 30 cells in y, and 1 cell in z.
A 10-degree wedge is placed on the bottom boundary, starting at x=0.5.
The simulation runs until an endTime of 10.
The time step (deltaT) is 0.0025.
Simulation results are written every 1 second.
The momentum transport is laminar.
The fluid is an ideal gas with zero viscosity.
The specific gas constant (R) is 1.
The specific heat at constant pressure (Cp) is 2.5.
The Prandtl number (Pr) is 1.
The molecular weight is 11640.3.
The heat of formation (Hf) is 0.
The initial velocity field (U) is (2.9, 0, 0).
The initial pressure field (p) is 1.
The initial temperature field (T) is 1.
The left boundary is the inlet.
The right boundary is the outlet.
The top boundary is a wall.
The bottom boundary is a wall representing a flat plate and the wedge surface.
The front and back patches are of type empty.
At the inlet, set velocity to a fixed value of (2.9, 0, 0).
At the inlet, set temperature to a fixed value of 1.
At the inlet, set pressure to a zeroGradient condition.
At the outlet, set velocity to a zeroGradient condition.
At the outlet, set pressure to a zeroGradient condition.
At the outlet, set temperature to a zeroGradient condition.
At the top wall, set velocity to a slip condition.
At the top wall, set temperature to a zeroGradient condition.
At the bottom wall, set velocity to a slip condition.
