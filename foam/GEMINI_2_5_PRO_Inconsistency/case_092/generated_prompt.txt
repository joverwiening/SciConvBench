Simulate the compressible, supersonic flow of air over a 10-degree wedge.
Use the rhoPimpleFoam solver.
The freestream Mach number is 2.0.
The freestream velocity is (686 0 0) m/s.
The freestream temperature is 293 K.
The freestream pressure is 101325 Pa.
Treat air as a perfect gas.
Use the hePsiThermo thermophysical model.
Use the perfectGas equation of state.
Use the sutherland transport model.
The computational domain is a 2D rectangle extruded one cell in the z-direction.
The domain extends from x=-0.5m to x=2.0m.
The domain extends from y=0m to y=1.0m.
The wedge tip is located at x=0.5m, y=0m.
The wedge has a 10-degree angle relative to the bottom boundary.
The wedge has a length of 1.0m along its surface.
The mesh has 200 cells in the x-direction.
The mesh has 100 cells in the y-direction.
The inlet boundary is at x=-0.5m and uses a fixedValue condition for U, p, and T.
The outlet boundary is at x=2.0m and uses a waveTransmissive condition for p and T, and zeroGradient for U.
The top boundary is at y=1.0m and uses a slip wall condition.
The bottom boundary, including the wedge surface, uses a slip wall condition.
The frontAndBack boundaries use the empty condition.
The simulation is run until a steady-state solution is achieved.
Convergence is defined by residuals falling below a tolerance of 1e-5.
The maximum Courant number is set to 0.5.
