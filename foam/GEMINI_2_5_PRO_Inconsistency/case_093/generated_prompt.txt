The simulation is a 2D dam break case.
The solver is pimpleFoam.
The simulation end time is 5 seconds.
The domain is a rectangle with a length of 1 meter and a height of 0.5 meters.
The mesh consists of 100 cells in the x-direction and 50 cells in the y-direction.
Gravity is defined as (0 -9.81 0).
The simulation is biphasic, with 'water' and 'air' phases.
The density of water is 1000 kg/m^3 and its kinematic viscosity is 1e-6 m^2/s.
The density of air is 1 kg/m^3 and its kinematic viscosity is 1.48e-5 m^2/s.
The initial water column is located on the left side of the domain.
The water column has a width of 0.25 meters and a height of 0.5 meters.
The setFields utility is used to initialize the water column with alpha.water = 1.
The rest of the domain is initialized with alpha.water = 0.
The left, right, and bottom walls use a no-slip velocity boundary condition.
The top boundary is an atmospheric pressure outlet.
The front and back patches are of type 'empty' for 2D simulation.
The simulation is run as laminar.
