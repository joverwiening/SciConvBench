The simulation models the separation of three immiscible fluids: oil, water, and gas.
The simulation is performed in a horizontal gravity separator.
The separator dimensions are 2 meters in length, 0.5 meters in height, and 0.5 meters in width.
The simulation uses the Volume of Fluid (VOF) method.
The solver specified in `controlDict` is `multiphaseInterFoam`.
The phases are defined in the `transportProperties` file.
The water phase has a density of 998 kg/m^3.
The water phase has a kinematic viscosity of 1.0e-6 m^2/s.
The oil phase has a density of 850 kg/m^3.
The oil phase has a kinematic viscosity of 1.0e-5 m^2/s.
The gas phase has a density of 1.2 kg/m^3.
The gas phase has a kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The separator is a closed domain with no inlets or outlets.
The initial state is a stratified mixture configured using `setFieldsDict`.
The initial volume fraction of water is 0.33, occupying the bottom third of the separator.
The initial volume fraction of oil is 0.33, occupying the middle third of the separator.
The initial volume fraction of gas is 0.34, occupying the top third of the separator.
The simulation tracks the interfaces between the three fluids as they separate over time.
Gravity is enabled and set to a vector of (0 -9.81 0).
The volume fraction of water is represented by the field `alpha.water`.
