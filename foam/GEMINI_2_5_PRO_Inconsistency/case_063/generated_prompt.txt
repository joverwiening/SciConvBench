The simulation is a 2D transient analysis of flow over a cylinder.
The solver to be used is pimpleFoam.
The mesh is 2D, extruded one cell thick in the z-direction.
The front and back patches are of type empty.
The cylinder has a diameter of 1.0 meter.
The cylinder is centered vertically in the channel.
The channel has a width of 8.0 meters.
The distance from the inlet to the cylinder center is 10.0 meters.
The distance from the cylinder center to the outlet is 25.0 meters.
The mesh will have approximately 30 cells across the cylinder diameter.
The mesh will be refined around the cylinder and in the wake region.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 1e-3 m^2/s.
The simulation will run until a time of 600 seconds.
The time step will be adjusted automatically to maintain a maximum Courant number of 0.5.
Simulation results will be written to disk every 10 seconds of simulation time.
The inlet velocity boundary condition is a fixed value of uniform (0.1 0 0) m/s.
The outlet velocity boundary condition is zero-gradient.
The cylinder velocity boundary condition is no-slip.
The top and bottom wall velocity boundary conditions are slip.
The inlet pressure boundary condition is zero-gradient.
The outlet pressure boundary condition is a fixed value of 0.
The pressure boundary condition for the cylinder, top, and bottom walls is zero-gradient.
Standard second-order numerical schemes will be used for discretization.
