The simulation will be a laminar counter-flow diffusion flame.
The solver to be used is reactingFoam.
The domain is a 2-D opposed-jet configuration.
The domain spans from x = 0 to 0.02 m, y = -0.01 to 0.01 m, and z = -0.01 to 0.01 m.
The mesh consists of 100 grid points in the x-direction, 40 in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The left boundary at x = 0 is named 'fuel'.
The right boundary at x = 0.02 is named 'air'.
The top and bottom boundaries are named 'outlet'.
The front and back boundaries are named 'frontAndBack' and are of type 'empty'.
The operating pressure is 101325 Pa.
The fuel inlet ('fuel' boundary) injects pure methane (CH4) at 293 K.
The velocity at the 'fuel' boundary is a fixed value of (0.1, 0, 0) m/s.
The pressure boundary condition at the 'fuel' boundary is zero-gradient.
The air inlet ('air' boundary) injects an oxidizer mixture at 293 K.
The oxidizer composition is 0.23 mass fraction O2 and 0.77 mass fraction N2.
The velocity at the 'air' boundary is a fixed value of (-0.1, 0, 0) m/s.
The pressure boundary condition at the 'air' boundary is zero-gradient.
The 'outlet' boundaries have a totalPressure boundary condition for pressure, set to 101325 Pa.
The 'outlet' boundaries have an inletOutlet boundary condition for velocity.
The 'outlet' boundaries have a zero-gradient boundary condition for temperature and species mass fractions.
At time 0, the entire domain is initialized to a temperature of 2000 K.
At time 0, the entire domain is initialized to a pressure of 101325 Pa.
At time 0, the entire domain is initialized with a velocity of (0, 0, 0) m/s.
At time 0, the gas composition is the product of complete stoichiometric methane-air combustion.
The combustion model is laminar.
The momentum transport model is laminar.
The chemistry is modeled as compressible finite-rate using the hePsiThermo thermophysical model.
The chemistry integration type is EulerImplicit with the Rosenbrock43 ODE solver.
The transport model for viscosity and thermal conductivity is Sutherland.
The species mixing model is coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture.
The thermodynamics properties are calculated using the JANAF model.
The chemical reaction mechanism is GRI-Mech 3.0.
The simulation runs until an end time of 0.5 s.
The time step is fixed at 1e-4 s.
Simulation results are written to disk every 0.05 s.
