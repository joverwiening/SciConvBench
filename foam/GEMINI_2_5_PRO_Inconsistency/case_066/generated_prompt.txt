The simulation is a steady-state, incompressible, turbulent flow in a 2D pipe.
The buoyantSimpleFoam solver will be used.
The pipe has a diameter of 0.1 meters and a length of 2 meters.
The fluid is air.
The kinematic viscosity of the air is 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The k-epsilon turbulence model will be used.
The gravitational acceleration vector is (0, -9.81, 0) m/s^2.
The inlet has a uniform velocity of 1 m/s in the x-direction.
The inlet has a uniform temperature of 300 K.
The outlet has a fixed gauge pressure of 0 Pa.
The temperature at the outlet has a zeroGradient boundary condition.
The pipe wall is adiabatic, with a zeroGradient boundary condition for temperature.
The front and back patches are of type 'empty'.
