The simulation is a 2D dam break case.
The solver is interFoam.
The system is two-phase, consisting of water and air.
The domain is 2 meters long and 1 meter high.
The mesh has a single cell in the z-direction.
Gravity is defined as (0 -9.81 0) m/s^2.
The 'frontAndBack' patches are defined with type 'empty' in blockMeshDict.
The boundary condition for all fields on the 'frontAndBack' patches is 'empty'.
The 'bottom' patch is a wall with a 'noSlip' boundary condition for velocity and 'zeroGradient' for scalars.
The 'left' patch is a wall with a 'noSlip' boundary condition for velocity and 'zeroGradient' for scalars.
The 'right' patch is a wall with a 'noSlip' boundary condition for velocity and 'zeroGradient' for scalars.
The 'top' patch represents the atmosphere, allowing air to enter and exit freely.
The initial water column is located in the bottom-left corner of the domain.
The initial water column has a width of 0.5 meters.
The initial water column has a height of 0.5 meters.
The initial conditions are set using the setFieldsDict utility.
