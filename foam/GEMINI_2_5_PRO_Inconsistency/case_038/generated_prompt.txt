The simulation computes the steady-state thermo-elastic deformation of a solid aluminum block.
The geometry is a cube with dimensions of 1 meter per side.
The face of the cube at the minimum X-coordinate is designated as `fixedFace`.
The face of the cube at the maximum X-coordinate is designated as `heatedFace`.
All other faces are designated as `sides`.
The OpenFOAM solver `solidDisplacementFoam` will be used.
The material is aluminum with Young's modulus E=69e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio nu=0.33, and density rho=2700 kg/m^3.
The coefficient of thermal expansion is alpha=2.3e-5 1/K.
The reference temperature for zero thermal strain is T0=300 K.
A uniform temperature field of T=400 K is applied to the entire block.
The `fixedFace` has a fixed displacement boundary condition of (0 0 0).
All other faces have a zero-gradient boundary condition for displacement.
The final displacement and stress fields are the desired outputs.
