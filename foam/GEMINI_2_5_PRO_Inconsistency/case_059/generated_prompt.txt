The simulation is a steady-state, two-phase oil-water flow in a horizontal pipe.
The simulation uses the `twoPhaseEulerFoam` solver.
The pipe has a diameter of 0.05 meters.
The pipe has a length of 2.0 meters.
Gravitational acceleration is set to -9.81 m/s^2 in the vertical direction.
The water phase has a density of 1000 kg/m^3 and a kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 m^2/s.
The oil phase has a density of 850 kg/m^3 and a kinematic viscosity of 1e-5 m^2/s.
The k-epsilon model is used for turbulence in both phases.
The inlet mixture velocity is a fixed value of 1.0 m/s along the pipe axis.
The inlet water volume fraction is a fixed uniform value of 0.3.
The outlet boundary condition for pressure is a fixed value of 0.
The outlet boundary condition for velocity is `zeroGradient`.
The outlet boundary condition for water volume fraction is `zeroGradient`.
The pipe wall boundary condition for velocity is `noSlip`.
The pipe wall boundary condition for water volume fraction is `zeroGradient`.
Wall functions are applied for turbulence quantities at the pipe wall.
