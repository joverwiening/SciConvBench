The simulation is a 2D supersonic flow of air over a wedge.
The solver is rhoCentralFoam.
The flow is at Mach 2.
The wedge has a 10-degree angle.
Air is treated as a perfect gas using the hePsiThermo library and the perfectGas equation of state.
The freestream pressure is 101325 Pa.
The freestream temperature is 300 K.
The freestream velocity is (686 0 0) m/s.
The computational domain has a total length of 1.0 meter in the x-direction.
The computational domain has a total height of 0.5 meters in the y-direction.
The tip of the wedge is located at coordinates (0.2, 0.0).
The mesh is 2D, extruded one cell in the z-direction.
The mesh has 200 cells in the x-direction.
The mesh has 100 cells in the y-direction.
The inlet boundary condition uses fixed values for U, p, and T based on the freestream conditions.
The outlet boundary condition is waveTransmissive for p and T.
The outlet boundary condition is zeroGradient for U.
The wedge surface boundary condition is slip.
The top boundary condition is slip.
The bottom boundary before the wedge is a slip wall.
The frontAndBack patches are of type empty.
The simulation runs until an endTime of 0.005 seconds.
