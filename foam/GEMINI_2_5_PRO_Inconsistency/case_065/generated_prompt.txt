The simulation uses the solidDisplacementFoam solver.
The simulation domain is a block with dimensions 1.0 m x 0.1 m x 0.1 m.
The mesh consists of 200 cells in the x-direction, 20 cells in the y-direction, and 20 cells in the z-direction.
The material is solid aluminum with Young's modulus E = 70e9 Pa, Poisson's ratio nu = 0.33, and density rho = 2700 kg/m^3.
The initial displacement field D is (1e-6 0 0) m in the region where the x-coordinate is less than or equal to 0.01 m.
The initial displacement field D is (0 0 0) m everywhere else.
The initial velocity field U is uniform at (0 0 0) m/s.
All external boundaries have a traction-free boundary condition.
The simulation runs for a total time of 4e-4 seconds.
The time step is adjusted automatically to maintain a maximum Courant number of 0.5.
The initial time step is 1e-7 seconds.
Simulation results are written to disk every 100 time steps.
