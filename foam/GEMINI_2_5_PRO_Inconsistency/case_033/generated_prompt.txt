The simulation is a 2D steady-state heat conduction analysis.
The solver to be used is laplacianFoam.
The computational domain is a square plate of 1m x 1m.
The mesh consists of 20 cells in the x-direction and 20 cells in the y-direction.
The mesh is one cell thick in the z-direction.
The front and back patches are of type 'wall'.
The left boundary patch is held at a fixed temperature of 400 K.
The right boundary patch is held at a fixed temperature of 300 K.
The top and bottom boundary patches have a zero-gradient temperature condition.
The front and back boundary patches have a zero-gradient temperature condition.
The thermal diffusivity of the material is 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The simulation will run until an end time of 1000 seconds to ensure a steady state is reached.
The time step for the simulation is 1 second.
The time derivative scheme is Euler.
The gradient scheme is Gauss linear.
The Laplacian scheme is Gauss linear corrected.
The linear solver for temperature is the Preconditioned Conjugate Gradient (PCG).
The preconditioner for the solver is Diagonal Incomplete Cholesky (DIC).
The solver tolerance is 1e-6.
The solver relative tolerance is 0.
