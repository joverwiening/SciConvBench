The simulation will be a 2D transient heat conduction analysis on a square plate.
The solver to be used is laplacianFoam.
The domain is a 1 meter by 1 meter square.
The mesh will consist of 50 cells in the x-direction and 50 cells in the y-direction.
The 2D mesh will be extruded one cell in the z-direction with a thickness of 0.01 meters.
The thermal diffusivity of the material is 1e-5 m^2/s.
The initial temperature of the entire plate is a uniform 300 K.
The left boundary (x=0) is held at a constant temperature of 400 K.
The right boundary (x=1) is held at a constant temperature of 300 K.
The top boundary (y=1) is insulated, having a zeroGradient temperature condition.
The bottom boundary (y=0) is insulated, having a zeroGradient temperature condition.
The frontAndBack faces are treated as a single patch with a zeroGradient temperature condition.
The total simulation run time will be 10000 seconds.
The simulation results will be saved to disk every 500 seconds of simulation time.
The time step will be adjusted automatically to maintain a maximum Courant number of 0.5.
