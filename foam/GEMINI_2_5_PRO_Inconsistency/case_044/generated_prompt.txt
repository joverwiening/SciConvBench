The simulation is a 2D dam break case.
The solver to be used is `interFoam`.
The simulation domain is a 1 meter by 1 meter square.
The mesh consists of 100 cells in the x-direction and 100 cells in the y-direction.
The mesh is one cell thick in the z-direction.
The boundary conditions for the front and back patches in the z-direction are `empty`.
The total simulation time is 2.0 seconds.
The time step is adjustable to maintain a maximum Courant number of 0.5.
Simulation results are written to disk every 0.05 seconds.
The acceleration due to gravity is defined as (0 -9.81 0) m/s^2.
The fluid properties for water are a density of 1000 kg/m^3 and a kinematic viscosity of 1.0e-6 m^2/s.
The fluid properties for air are a density of 1 kg/m^3 and a kinematic viscosity of 1.48e-5 m^2/s.
The `setFieldsDict` utility is used for initialization.
The initial water column occupies the region defined by 0 < x < 0.2 meters and 0 < y < 0.5 meters.
Inside the water column, the volume fraction `alpha.water` is set to 1.
Outside the water column, the volume fraction `alpha.water` is set to 0.
The top boundary condition is `pressureInletOutletVelocity`.
The left, right, and bottom boundary conditions are `noSlip` walls.
