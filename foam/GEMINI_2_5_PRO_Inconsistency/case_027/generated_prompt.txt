The simulation solves the turbulent airflow over a 2D backward-facing step.
The solver to be used is simpleFoam.
The turbulence model is kOmegaSST.
The fluid is air with a kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The step height is 1 meter.
The inlet channel height is 1 meter.
The computational domain extends 5 meters upstream of the step.
The computational domain extends 20 meters downstream of the step.
The inlet freestream velocity is a uniform value of 20 m/s.
The inlet turbulent kinetic energy k is a uniform value of 1.5 m^2/s^2.
The inlet specific dissipation rate omega is a uniform value of 447.2 1/s.
The inlet pressure boundary condition is zeroGradient.
The outlet boundary condition for velocity, k, and omega is zeroGradient.
The outlet pressure is a fixed uniform value of 0.
All walls use a no-slip boundary condition for velocity.
Standard wall functions are applied to all walls for turbulence quantities.
The mesh should be generated such that the y+ value of the first cell center near the walls is between 30 and 300.
The mesh must be refined in the shear layer region downstream of the step corner and in the recirculation zone.
Second-order accurate discretization schemes must be used for all spatial terms.
The simulation should be run until the solution residuals reach a stable plateau and the reattachment length is constant.
