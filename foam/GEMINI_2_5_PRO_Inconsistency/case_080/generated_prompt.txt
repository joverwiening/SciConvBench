The simulation is a transient three-phase fluidized bed in a cylindrical column.
The solver to be used is multiphaseEulerFoam.
The simulation runs for 10 seconds of physical time.
The geometry is a cylinder with a diameter of 0.1 m and a height of 1 m.
The mesh is a 2D axisymmetric grid with 20 cells in the radial direction and 200 cells in the axial direction.
Gravity is defined as (0 -9.81 0) m/s^2.
The three phases are air, water, and sand.
The operating temperature is 298.15 K.
The operating pressure is 101325 Pa.
Air is modeled as a perfect gas with standard properties at the operating conditions.
Water is modeled as an incompressible liquid with standard properties at the operating conditions.
Sand is modeled as a constant density solid.
The density of sand particles is 2650 kg/m^3.
The diameter of sand particles is 500 microns.
The maximum packing fraction for the sand phase is 0.63.
The Schaeffer model is used for the frictional viscosity of the sand phase.
A standard k-epsilon turbulence model is applied to the air and water phases.
The drag model for the air-water interaction is SchillerNaumann.
The drag model for the water-sand interaction is Gidaspow.
The drag model for the air-sand interaction is Gidaspow.
The initial condition for the bottom 0.2 m of the column is a mixture of sand and water with volume fractions of 0.6 and 0.4 respectively.
The initial condition for the column above 0.2 m is a volume fraction of 1.0 for water.
The bottom boundary is an inlet.
At the inlet, the air velocity is 0.5 m/s, and the air volume fraction is 1.0.
At the inlet, the boundary condition for water and sand velocity is zeroGradient.
At the inlet, the volume fraction for water and sand is a fixed value of 0.
The top boundary is an outlet.
At the outlet, the pressure condition is totalPressure set to 101325 Pa.
At the outlet, the boundary condition for velocity and phase fractions is inletOutlet.
The column walls have a noSlip boundary condition for all phase velocities.
The column walls have a zeroGradient boundary condition for all phase fractions and turbulence quantities.
