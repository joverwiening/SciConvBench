The simulation is a transient 2D analysis of vortex shedding behind a cylinder.
The pimpleFoam solver is used.
The fluid is incompressible with a kinematic viscosity of 0.01 m^2/s.
The cylinder has a diameter of 1 meter.
The Reynolds number is 100.
The computational domain is a rectangular channel with corners at (-10, -10) and (30, 10) meters.
The center of the circular cylinder is located at (0, 0).
The mesh has a resolution of approximately 20 cells across the cylinder diameter.
The mesh is refined in the wake region behind the cylinder.
The inlet boundary condition is a uniform velocity of (1, 0, 0) m/s for U and zeroGradient for p.
The outlet boundary condition is zeroGradient for U and a fixed pressure of 0 for p.
The topAndBottom boundary conditions are defined as slip walls.
The cylinder boundary condition is a no-slip wall.
The frontAndBack boundary conditions are defined as empty for a 2D simulation.
The simulation runs from t=0 to t=200 seconds.
The time step is 0.1 seconds.
The maximum Courant number must be maintained below 1.
The initial condition for the entire domain is a uniform velocity of (1, 0, 0) m/s and a uniform pressure of 0.
Use second-order discretization schemes for spatial derivatives.
Use the Euler scheme for the time derivative.
The PIMPLE algorithm will use 2 outer correctors and 1 inner corrector.
Monitor the lift and drag coefficients on the cylinder over time.
Calculate the Strouhal number by performing a Fast Fourier Transform on the time series of the lift coefficient after the initial transient has passed.
