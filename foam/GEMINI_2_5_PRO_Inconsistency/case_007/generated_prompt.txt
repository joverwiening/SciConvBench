The simulation is a 2D gas-liquid bubble column using the multiphaseEulerFoam solver.
The domain is a rectangle with x from 0 to 0.15 m, y from 0 to 1.00 m, and z from 0 to 0.10 m.
The height of the column is along the positive y-axis.
The mesh is a structured hexahedral mesh with 25 cells in x, 75 cells in y, and 1 cell in z.
The mesh grading is uniform with simpleGrading (1 1 1).
The blockMeshDict defines three patches: inlet, outlet, and walls.
The simulation is treated as 2D by using empty faces in the z-direction.
The continuous phase is liquid water.
The water phase uses the heRhoThermo thermophysical model.
The water equation of state is rPolynomial with C coefficients (0.001278, -2.1055e-06, 3.9689e-09, 4.3772e-13, -2.0225e-16).
The molecular weight of water is 18 g/mol.
The specific heat capacity at constant volume (Cv) for water is 4195 J/(kg·K).
The dynamic viscosity of water is 3.645e-4 Pa·s.
The Prandtl number for water is 2.289.
The dispersed phase is gaseous air.
The air phase uses the heRhoThermo thermophysical model.
The air equation of state is perfectGas.
The molecular weight of air is 28.9 g/mol.
The specific heat capacity at constant pressure (Cp) for air is 1007 J/(kg·K).
The dynamic viscosity of air is 1.84e-05 Pa·s.
The Prandtl number for air is 0.7.
Gravity is defined as (0, -9.81, 0) m/s².
The bubble size model for air is isothermal with a reference diameter d0 of 3e-3 m.
Momentum transport for both phases is laminar.
The phases are defined as (air water) in phaseProperties.
Water is the reference phase.
The residualAlpha for both phases is 1e-6.
Blending uses a linear scheme.
The minFullyContinuousAlpha is 0.7.
The minPartlyContinuousAlpha is 0.3 for default blending and 0.5 for drag blending.
Surface tension between air and water is constant with a value of 0.07 N/m.
The drag model is SchillerNaumann for all flow regimes.
The virtual mass model is constantCoefficient with a coefficient Cvm of 0.5.
The heat transfer model is RanzMarshall.
The residualAlpha for the heat transfer model is 1e-4.
At time t=0, the velocity of water is uniform (0, 0, 0) m/s.
At time t=0, the velocity of air is uniform (0, 0.1, 0) m/s.
At time t=0, the pressure p_rgh is uniform 1e5 Pa.
At time t=0, the temperature of air is uniform 300 K.
At time t=0, the temperature of water is uniform 350 K.
Initially, the domain is filled with air (alpha.air = 1), except for a specified box region.
The box region from (0, 0, 0) to (0.15, 0.701, 0.1) is initialized with water (alpha.water = 1).
The inlet boundary condition for air velocity is fixedValue uniform (0, 0.1, 0) m/s.
The inlet boundary condition for water velocity is fixedValue uniform (0, 0, 0) m/s.
The inlet boundary condition for air volume fraction is fixedValue uniform 0.5.
The inlet boundary condition for water volume fraction is fixedValue uniform 0.5.
The inlet boundary condition for p_rgh is fixedFluxPressure.
The inlet boundary condition for air temperature is fixedValue uniform 300 K.
The inlet boundary condition for water temperature is fixedValue uniform 350 K.
The outlet boundary condition for air velocity is fixedValue uniform (0, 0.1, 0) m/s.
The outlet boundary condition for water velocity is fixedValue uniform (0, 0, 0) m/s.
The outlet boundary condition for air volume fraction is fixedValue uniform 0.5.
The outlet boundary condition for water volume fraction is fixedValue uniform 0.5.
The outlet boundary condition for p_rgh is fixedValue uniform 1e5 Pa.
The outlet boundary condition for air temperature is fixedValue uniform 300 K.
The outlet boundary condition for water temperature is fixedValue uniform 350 K.
