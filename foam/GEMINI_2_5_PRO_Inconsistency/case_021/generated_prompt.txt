The simulation is a 2D, incompressible, steady-state analysis of airflow over a symmetric airfoil.
The solver is simpleFoam.
The domain is a C-grid topology.
The mesh is one cell thick in the z-direction.
The front and back patches are named `frontAndBack` and have a type of `empty`.
The inlet velocity is a uniform value of (10 0 0) m/s.
The outlet has a zero-gradient velocity boundary condition.
The airfoil surface has a `noSlip` velocity boundary condition.
The `frontAndBack` patches have an `empty` velocity boundary condition.
The inlet has a `zeroGradient` pressure boundary condition.
The airfoil has a `zeroGradient` pressure boundary condition.
The outlet has a `fixedValue` pressure of `uniform 0`.
The `frontAndBack` patches have an `empty` pressure boundary condition.
The simulation uses the k-omega SST turbulence model.
