The simulation will be run using the simpleFoam solver.
The geometry is an axisymmetric wedge with a 5-degree angle.
The pipe has a radius of 0.05 meters and a length of 2.0 meters.
The mesh consists of one cell in the tangential direction, 50 cells in the radial direction, and 200 cells in the axial direction.
The mesh patch corresponding to the pipe centerline is named `axis` and has the type `axis`.
The mesh patch corresponding to the pipe surface is named `wall` and has the type `wall`.
The mesh patches for the fluid entrance and exit are named `inlet` and `outlet`.
The front and back wedge planes are named `front` and `back` and have the type `empty`.
The simulation will use the k-omega SST turbulence model.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 1e-5 m^2/s.
At the `inlet` patch, velocity `U` is a fixed value with an axial component of 1.0 m/s and a tangential component of 0.5 m/s.
At the `inlet` patch, pressure `p` has a `zeroGradient` boundary condition.
At the `inlet` patch, `k` and `omega` are set for a turbulence intensity of 5% and a turbulent length scale of 0.007 meters.
At the `outlet` patch, pressure `p` is a fixed uniform value of 0.
At the `outlet` patch, `U`, `k`, and `omega` have a `zeroGradient` boundary condition.
At the `wall` patch, velocity `U` has a `noSlip` boundary condition.
At the `wall` patch, pressure `p` has a `zeroGradient` boundary condition.
At the `wall` patch, `kqRWallFunction` and `omegaWallFunction` are used for `k` and `omega`.
For the `axis`, `front`, and `back` patches, the boundary condition for all fields is `empty`.
The simulation will run until the initial residuals for all solved variables fall below 1e-5.
