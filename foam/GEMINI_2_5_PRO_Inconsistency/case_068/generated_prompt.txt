The simulation will use the chtMultiRegionSimpleFoam solver.
The domain consists of a solid silicon region and a fluid air region.
The housing is a rectangular duct with dimensions 10cm (length) x 5cm (width) x 2cm (height).
The silicon chip is a rectangular block with dimensions 1cm x 1cm x 0.1cm.
The silicon chip is located on the center of the bottom inner surface of the housing.
A total heat generation of 10 Watts is applied uniformly to the volume of the silicon chip region using fvOptions.
Air enters the housing through an inlet with a uniform velocity of 1 m/s.
The inlet air temperature is a fixed value of 293 K.
The outer surface of the housing, named 'outerCasing', is perfectly insulated.
The temperature boundary condition for 'outerCasing' in the '0/solid/T' file is zeroGradient.
The pressure at the outlet is a fixed value of 0 Pa (gauge).
Standard thermophysical properties for air and silicon will be used.
