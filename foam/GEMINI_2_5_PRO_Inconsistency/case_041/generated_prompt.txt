The simulation is a 2D laminar, incompressible flow past a cylinder.
The goal is to observe the von Kármán vortex street at a Reynolds number of 100.
The domain is a rectangular channel 2.2m long and 0.41m high.
The cylinder has a diameter of 0.1m.
The cylinder is centered at (0.2, 0.2) meters.
The mesh is one cell thick in the z-direction.
The kinematic viscosity is 1e-3 m²/s.
The solver is `pimpleFoam`.
The simulation starts at `startTime` 0.
The simulation ends at `endTime` 200.
The time step is controlled automatically with `adjustTimeStep` enabled.
The maximum Courant number (`maxCo`) is 0.9.
The initial time step (`deltaT`) is 0.005.
Simulation results are written every 0.1 seconds.
The inlet boundary `inlet` has a fixed velocity `U` of `uniform (1 0 0)`.
The outlet boundary `outlet` has a zero-gradient condition for velocity `U`.
The cylinder wall boundary `cylinderWall` has a `noSlip` condition for velocity `U`.
The top and bottom walls `topAndBottom` have a `noSlip` condition for velocity `U`.
The `frontAndBack` patches are of type `empty` for velocity `U`.
The inlet boundary `inlet` has a zero-gradient condition for pressure `p`.
The outlet boundary `outlet` has a fixed pressure `p` of `uniform 0`.
The cylinder wall boundary `cylinderWall` has a zero-gradient condition for pressure `p`.
The top and bottom walls `topAndBottom` have a zero-gradient condition for pressure `p`.
The `frontAndBack` patches are of type `empty` for pressure `p`.
The time derivative scheme (`ddtSchemes`) is `Euler`.
The gradient scheme (`gradSchemes`) is `Gauss linear`.
The convection scheme for velocity (`div(phi,U)`) is `Gauss linearUpwindV grad(U)`.
The Laplacian scheme (`laplacianSchemes`) is `Gauss linear corrected`.
The interpolation scheme (`interpolationSchemes`) is `linear`.
The surface-normal gradient scheme (`snGradSchemes`) is `corrected`.
The pressure `p` is solved using the PCG solver with a DIC preconditioner, a tolerance of 1e-6, and a relative tolerance of 0.05.
The velocity `U` is solved using the PBiCGStab solver with a DILU preconditioner, a tolerance of 1e-8, and a relative tolerance of 0.
The PIMPLE algorithm uses 2 correctors and 1 non-orthogonal corrector.
