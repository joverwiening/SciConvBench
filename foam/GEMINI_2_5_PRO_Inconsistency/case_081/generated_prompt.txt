The simulation is a 2D transient gas-liquid bubble column.
The solver to be used is `twoPhaseEulerFoam`.
The domain is a rectangle with a width of 0.5 meters and a height of 2 meters.
The two phases are air (gas) and water (liquid).
The total physical simulation time is 20 seconds.
Gravitational acceleration is defined as a vector of (0 -9.81 0) m/s^2.
The mean bubble diameter for the air phase is 0.003 meters.
The domain is initialized with stationary water up to a height of 1.8 meters.
The remaining domain above 1.8 meters is initialized with stationary air.
The bottom boundary is named 'inlet'.
The top boundary is named 'outlet'.
The side boundaries are named 'walls'.
For the 'inlet' boundary, the phase fraction of air (`alpha.air`) is a fixed uniform value of 0.8.
For the 'inlet' boundary, the phase fraction of water (`alpha.water`) is a fixed uniform value of 0.2.
For the 'inlet' boundary, the velocity of air (`U.air`) is a fixed uniform value of (0 0.2 0) m/s.
For the 'inlet' boundary, the velocity of water (`U.water`) is a fixed uniform value of (0 0.01 0) m/s.
For the 'inlet' boundary, the relative pressure (`p_rgh`) uses a `fixedFluxPressure` boundary condition.
For the 'outlet' boundary, a `totalPressure` condition with a value of 0 Pa is applied to `p_rgh`.
For the 'outlet' boundary, an `inletOutlet` condition is applied to all other fields, allowing for outflow.
For the 'walls' boundary, a `noSlip` condition is applied to the velocities of both phases.
For the 'walls' boundary, a `zeroGradient` condition is applied to the phase fractions of both phases.
For the 'walls' boundary, a `fixedFluxPressure` condition is applied to `p_rgh`.
