The simulation will be run using the reactingFoam solver.
The domain is a rectangular region defined by x from 0 to 0.02 m, y from -0.01 to 0.01 m, and z from -0.01 to 0.01 m.
The mesh will consist of 100 cells in the x-direction, 40 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The boundary at x = 0 is named fuel.
The boundary at x = 0.02 is named air.
The top and bottom boundaries (y = +/- 0.01 m) are named outlet.
The front and back boundaries are of type empty.
The fuel inlet boundary condition specifies a velocity of (0.1, 0, 0) m/s, a temperature of 293 K, and mass fractions of CH4=1, O2=0, and N2=0.
The air inlet boundary condition specifies a velocity of (-0.1, 0, 0) m/s, a temperature of 293 K, and mass fractions of O2=0.23, N2=0.77, and CH4=0.
The pressure boundary condition at both the fuel and air inlets is zeroGradient.
The outlet boundary condition for pressure is a fixed value of 101325 Pa.
The outlet boundary condition for velocity is pressureInletOutletVelocity.
The outlet boundary condition for temperature and all species is inletOutlet.
At time 0, the entire domain is initialized with a uniform temperature of 2000 K.
At time 0, the entire domain is initialized with a uniform velocity of (0, 0, 0) m/s.
At time 0, the entire domain is initialized with a uniform pressure of 101325 Pa.
At time 0, the species mass fractions are initialized by linearly interpolating between the fuel and air inlet values based on the x-coordinate.
The combustion and momentum transport models are laminar.
The chemistry model is incompressibleFiniteRateChemistry.
The thermo model is heRhoThermo with a coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture, Sutherland transport, and JANAF thermodynamics.
The chemical reaction mechanism is the one-step global reaction for methane from Westbrook and Dryer (1981).
The chemistry type is EulerImplicit with the Rosenbrock43 ODE solver.
The simulation will run until an end time of 0.5 s.
The time step is 1e-4 s.
Results will be written every 0.05 s.
