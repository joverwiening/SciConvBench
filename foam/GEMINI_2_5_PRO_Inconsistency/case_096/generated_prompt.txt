The simulation is a 2D dam break problem.
The solver to be used is `interFoam`.
The simulation involves two incompressible, immiscible fluids: water and air.
The density of water is 1000 kg/m^3.
The kinematic viscosity of water is 1e-6 m^2/s.
The density of air is 1.2 kg/m^3.
The kinematic viscosity of air is 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The domain is a rectangular tank with dimensions 0.5m in length (x-direction), 0.5m in height (y-direction), and 0.01m in depth (z-direction).
The mesh consists of 100 cells in the x-direction, 100 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
Gravity is defined as (0, -9.81, 0) m/s^2.
The initial condition is a column of water (alpha.water=1) occupying the region where x is less than 0.15m.
The rest of the domain is initially filled with air (alpha.water=0).
The `setFieldsDict` utility is used for field initialization.
The `leftWall`, `rightWall`, and `lowerWall` boundaries are of type `wall`.
The `atmosphere` boundary is of type `patch`.
The `frontAndBack` boundaries are of type `symmetry`.
For the velocity field `U`, the `noSlip` condition is applied to all wall boundaries.
For the velocity field `U`, the `pressureInletOutletVelocity` condition is applied to the `atmosphere` boundary.
For the velocity field `U`, the `symmetry` condition is applied to the `frontAndBack` boundaries.
For the pressure field `p_rgh`, the `fixedFluxPressure` condition is applied to all wall boundaries and the `frontAndBack` boundaries.
For the pressure field `p_rgh`, the `totalPressure` condition is applied to the `atmosphere` boundary.
For the phase fraction field `alpha.water`, the `zeroGradient` condition is applied to all wall boundaries and the `frontAndBack` boundaries.
For the phase fraction field `alpha.water`, the `inletOutlet` condition is applied to the `atmosphere` boundary.
The total simulation run time is 2.0 seconds.
The time step is controlled by a maximum Courant number of 0.5.
