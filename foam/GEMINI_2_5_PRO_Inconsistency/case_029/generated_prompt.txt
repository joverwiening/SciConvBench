The simulation solves for 2D transient flow past a circular cylinder.
The Reynolds number is 150.
The governing equations are the incompressible Navier-Stokes equations.
The solver is pimpleFoam.
The cylinder has a diameter of 1.0 meters.
The cylinder is centered at (0,0,0).
The computational domain is a rectangle.
The domain extends from -10m to 30m in the x-direction.
The domain extends from -10m to 10m in the y-direction.
The domain is one cell thick in the z-direction.
The free-stream inlet velocity is 1.5 m/s in the positive x-direction.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 0.01 m^2/s.
The inlet boundary condition is a uniform velocity of (1.5 0 0) m/s.
The outlet boundary condition is a zero-gradient pressure.
The top and bottom boundary conditions are symmetry planes.
The cylinder surface boundary condition is no-slip.
The front and back plane boundary conditions are 'empty' to enforce a 2D solution.
The simulation starts at time 0.
The simulation ends at time 200 seconds.
The initial time step is 0.005 seconds.
The time step is adjusted automatically to maintain a maximum Courant number of 0.9.
Solution data is written to file every 0.2 seconds of simulation time.
