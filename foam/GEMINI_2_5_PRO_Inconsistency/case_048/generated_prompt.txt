The simulation uses the `buoyantPimpleFoam` solver.
The simulation domain is a 2D rectangular channel.
The channel height is 0.1 meters.
The channel length is 0.4 meters.
Gravity is -9.81 m/s^2 in the y-direction.
The fluid is air, modeled as a perfect gas using the `hePsiThermo` library.
The simulation uses the k-omega SST turbulence model.
The initial temperature of the air is a uniform 300 K.
The initial velocity of the air is zero.
The initial pressure (p_rgh) is a uniform 0 Pa.
The bottom wall temperature is a fixed value of 400 K.
The top wall is adiabatic, using a `zeroGradient` boundary condition for temperature.
The top and bottom walls are no-slip boundaries for velocity.
The side walls (left and right) use `cyclic` boundary conditions for all fields.
The front and back patches use the `empty` boundary condition type.
The pressure (p_rgh) boundary condition on the top and bottom walls is `buoyantPressure`.
Wall functions are used for the turbulence quantities k and omega on the top and bottom walls.
