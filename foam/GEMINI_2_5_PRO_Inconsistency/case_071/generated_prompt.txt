The simulation is a 2D dam break case.
The solver to be used is interFoam.
The domain is a rectangle with dimensions 0.6m in length (X-axis) and 0.4m in height (Y-axis).
The mesh is created using blockMesh with a single block.
The mesh resolution is 120 cells in the x-direction and 80 cells in the y-direction.
The front and back patches are of type `empty`.
The initial condition is a column of water occupying the region x < 0.15m and y < 0.3m.
The rest of the domain is initially filled with air.
The initial conditions are specified in the `system/setFieldsDict` file.
The two phases are water and air, with standard physical properties defined in `constant/transportProperties`.
The gravity vector is (0 -9.81 0).
The boundary condition for velocity (U) is `noSlip` on the wall patches.
The boundary condition for velocity (U) is `pressureInletOutletVelocity` on the top atmosphere patch.
The boundary condition for relative pressure (p_rgh) is `fixedFluxPressure` on the wall patches.
The boundary condition for relative pressure (p_rgh) is `totalPressure` on the top atmosphere patch.
The boundary condition for the water phase fraction (alpha.water) is `zeroGradient` on the wall patches.
The boundary condition for the water phase fraction (alpha.water) is `inletOutlet` on the top atmosphere patch.
The simulation total run time is 2 seconds.
The time step is controlled by a maximum Courant number of 0.5.
The time derivative scheme (`ddtSchemes`) is `Euler`.
The momentum divergence scheme (`div(phi,U)`) is `Gauss linearUpwindV grad(U)`.
The phase fraction divergence scheme (`div(phi,alpha)`) is `Gauss vanLeer`.
Simulation results are written to file every 0.02 seconds.
