The simulation is a 2D gas-liquid bubble column using the multiphaseEulerFoam solver.
The domain is a rectangle with x from 0 to 0.15 m, y from 0 to 1.00 m, and z from 0 to 0.10 m.
The height of the column is along the positive y-axis.
The mesh is a structured hexahedral grid with 25 cells in x, 75 cells in y, and 1 cell in z.
The mesh grading is uniform with simpleGrading (1 1 1).
The mesh patches are named inlet, outlet, and walls.
The simulation is treated as 2D by using 'empty' boundary conditions for the front and back faces in the z-direction.
The continuous phase is liquid water.
The water phase uses the heRhoThermo thermophysical model.
The water equation of state is rPolynomial with C coefficients (0.001278, -2.1055e-06, 3.9689e-09, 4.3772e-13, -2.0225e-16).
The molecular weight of water is 18 g/mol.
The specific heat capacity at constant volume (Cv) for water is 4195 J/(kg·K).
The dynamic viscosity of water is 3.645e-4 Pa·s.
The Prandtl number for water is 2.289.
The dispersed phase is gaseous air.
The air phase uses the heRhoThermo thermophysical model.
The air equation of state is perfectGas.
The molecular weight of air is 28.9 g/mol.
The specific heat capacity at constant pressure (Cp) for air is 1007 J/(kg·K).
The dynamic viscosity of air is 1.84e-05 Pa·s.
The Prandtl number for air is 0.7.
Gravity is applied with a vector of (0 -9.81 0) m/s².
The bubble size model for air is isothermalDiameter with a reference diameter d0 of 3e-3 m.
The diameter model for water is constantDiameter with a diameter d of 1e-4 m.
Momentum transport for both phases is laminar.
The phases are defined as (air water) in the multiphase properties.
The reference phase for multiphase interactions is water.
The residual volume fraction (residualAlpha) is 1e-6 for both phases.
Blending between phases uses a linear scheme.
The minimum volume fraction for a fully continuous phase is 0.7.
The minimum volume fraction for a partly continuous phase is 0.3 for default models.
The minimum volume fraction for a partly continuous phase is 0.5 for the drag model.
The surface tension between air and water is a constant value of 0.07 N/m.
The drag model is SchillerNaumann for all phase interactions.
The virtual mass model is constantCoefficient with a coefficient Cvm of 0.5.
The heat transfer model is RanzMarshall with a residualAlpha of 1e-4.
The initial velocity of water is uniform (0 0 0) m/s.
The initial velocity of air is uniform (0 0.1 0) m/s.
The initial pressure (p_rgh) is uniform 1e5 Pa.
The initial temperature of air is uniform 300 K.
The initial temperature of water is uniform 350 K.
Initially, the region from (0 0 0) to (0.15 0.701 0.1) is filled with water (alpha.water = 1).
Initially, the region outside the box from (0 0 0) to (0.15 0.701 0.1) is filled with air (alpha.air = 1).
At the inlet, the velocity of air is a fixed value of (0 0.1 0) m/s.

At the inlet, the volume fraction of air is a fixed value of 0.5.
At the inlet, the volume fraction of water is a fixed value of 0.5.
At the inlet, the temperature of air is a fixed value of 300 K.
At the inlet, the temperature of water is a fixed value of 350 K.
At the walls, the velocity of air is a fixed value of (0 0 0) m/s.
At the walls, the velocity of water is a fixed value of (0 0 0) m/s.

At the walls, the volume fraction of water has a zeroGradient boundary condition.
