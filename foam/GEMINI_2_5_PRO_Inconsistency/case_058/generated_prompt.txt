The simulation will model the injection of a compressible gas into a liquid.
Use the compressibleInterFoam solver in OpenFOAM.
The simulation domain will be a 2D axisymmetric cylinder.
The cylinder will have a radius of 0.5 meters and a height of 1.0 meter.
The gas injector is located at the center of the bottom boundary.
The injector has a diameter of 0.01 meters.
The injected gas is air, modeled as a perfect gas.
The liquid is water, modeled as an incompressible fluid.
The gas is injected at a velocity corresponding to Mach 0.8.
The total pressure at the gas inlet is 500,000 Pa.
The total temperature at the gas inlet is 293 K.
The tank is initially filled with quiescent water at 101,325 Pa and 293 K.
The top boundary of the tank is a pressure outlet set to 101,325 Pa.
All other boundaries are no-slip walls.
The simulation will include gravity, with a value of 9.81 m/s^2 acting downwards along the axis.
Use the k-omega SST model for turbulence.
The simulation will run for a total time of 0.1 seconds.
