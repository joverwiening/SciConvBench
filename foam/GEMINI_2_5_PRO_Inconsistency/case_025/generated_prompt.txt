The simulation is a 3D, incompressible, turbulent flow around a cube.
The solver to be used is `simpleFoam`.
The turbulence model is RANS `kEpsilon`.
The cube has a side length H of 1 meter.
The cube is mounted on the center of the channel floor.
The computational domain is a rectangular channel.
The channel extends 5H upstream of the cube's front face.
The channel extends 15H downstream of the cube's back face.
The total height of the channel is 5H.
The total width of the channel is 7H.
The Reynolds number, based on cube height H and inlet free-stream velocity, is 40,000.
The fluid has a kinematic viscosity of 2.5e-5 m^2/s.
The inlet patch is named `inlet`.
The outlet patch is named `outlet`.
The channel floor patch is named `floor`.
The channel top patch is named `top`.
The two spanwise side patches are named `left` and `right`.
The five exposed cube surfaces are grouped into a single patch named `cube_walls`.
The `inlet` boundary condition for velocity (U) is a fixed value of uniform (1 0 0) m/s.
The `outlet` boundary condition for pressure (p) is a fixed value of 0.
The `floor`, `top`, and `cube_walls` patches have a `noSlip` boundary condition for velocity.
The `left` and `right` patches are defined with the `symmetryPlane` boundary condition type.
The mesh must have more than one cell in the spanwise direction.
The mesh resolution must place the first cell center at a y+ value between 30 and 100 on all `noSlip` walls.
