The simulation uses the buoyantBoussinesqPimpleFoam solver.
The domain is a 3D box with dimensions 1m in X, 1m in Y, and 0.1m in Z.
The mesh resolution is 128 cells in X, 128 cells in Y, and 32 cells in Z.
The Boussinesq approximation is used with beta=0.00341, T0=300, nu=1.5e-5, and Pr=0.71.
The gravity vector is (0 0 -9.81).
The bottom wall (z=0) has a fixed temperature of 310K.
The top wall (z=0.1) has a fixed temperature of 300K.
The side walls are periodic for all fields.
The velocity boundary condition is noSlip on the top and bottom walls.
The initial velocity field is zero everywhere.
The initial temperature field is a linear profile from 310K at the bottom to 300K at the top, with a small random perturbation of +/- 0.1K to trigger instability.
The total simulation run time is 50 seconds.
The maximum Courant number is set to 0.5 for time step control.
