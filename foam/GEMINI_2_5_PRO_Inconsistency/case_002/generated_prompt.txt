The simulation is a two-dimensional Rayleigh-Bénard convection cell.
The domain is a rectangular cavity 9 units wide and 1 unit tall.
The mesh consists of 90 cells in the x-direction, 10 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The front and back patches are of type `empty` to enforce a 2D simulation.
The solver to be used is `buoyantFoam`.
Gravity is 9.81 m/s² acting in the negative y-direction.
The Boussinesq approximation for density is used.
The reference density is 1.0 kg/m³.
The reference temperature is 300 K.
The thermal expansion coefficient is 3.3e-3 1/K.
The kinematic viscosity is 1.5e-5 m²/s.
The thermal diffusivity is 2.1e-5 m²/s.
The Prandtl number is approximately 0.7.
The k-epsilon RAS turbulence model is enabled.
The bottom wall temperature is fixed at 305 K.
The top wall temperature is fixed at 295 K.
The vertical side walls are adiabatic, using a `zeroGradient` boundary condition for temperature.
A no-slip velocity boundary condition is applied to all solid walls.
The initial velocity field is uniform at (0 0 0) m/s.
The initial temperature field is uniform at 300 K.
The initial turbulent kinetic energy (k) field is uniform at 1e-5 m²/s².
The initial turbulent dissipation rate (epsilon) field is uniform at 1e-5 m²/s³.
The simulation runs up to 1000 seconds.
A fixed timestep of 1 second is used.
Results are written every 50 timesteps.
The PIMPLE algorithm will use 2 correctors per time step.
The time discretization scheme is `Euler`.
The divergence scheme for velocity is `Gauss linearUpwindV grad(U)`.
The divergence scheme for turbulence and energy fields is `Gauss linearUpwind`.
The gradient scheme is `Gauss linear`.
The Laplacian scheme is `Gauss linear corrected`.
The solver for `p_rgh` is PCG with a DIC preconditioner, a tolerance of 1e-8, and a relative tolerance of 0.01.
The solvers for U, e, k, and epsilon are smoothSolver with a symGaussSeidel smoother, a tolerance of 1e-8, and a relative tolerance of 0.01.
The final relative tolerance for all solvers is 0.
