The simulation is a 2D lid-driven cavity.
The domain is a 1m x 1m square.
The mesh consists of 40 cells in the x-direction and 40 cells in the y-direction.
The simulation is transient and runs until an end time of 20 seconds.
The `nonNewtonianIcoFoam` solver is used.
The top wall has a fixed velocity of (1 0 0) m/s.
The left, right, and bottom walls have a no-slip boundary condition.
The fluid is non-Newtonian, using the Power Law transport model.
The consistency index, k, is 0.1 Pa.s^n.
The power-law index, n, is 0.5.
The minimum kinematic viscosity, nuMin, is 1e-6 m^2/s.
The maximum kinematic viscosity, nuMax, is 1 m^2/s.
The initial velocity field is (0 0 0) m/s.
The initial pressure field is 0 Pa.
The time step is controlled by a maximum Courant number of 0.5.
The initial time step, deltaT, is 0.005 seconds.
Data is written to disk every 0.5 seconds of simulation time.
