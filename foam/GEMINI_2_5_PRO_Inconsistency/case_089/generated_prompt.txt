The simulation is a mixed convection lid-driven cavity problem.
The solver to be used is buoyantPimpleFoam.
The cavity is a two-dimensional square with dimensions of 1 meter by 1 meter.
The mesh is uniform with a resolution of 100 cells in the x-direction and 100 cells in the y-direction.
The fluid density is modeled using the incompressiblePerfectGas equation of state.
The molecular weight of the fluid is 28.9 g/mol.
The specific heat at constant pressure is 1005 J/(kg*K).
The dynamic viscosity is 1.8e-5 Pa*s.
The Prandtl number is 0.7.
The BoussinesqCoeffs entry must be removed from the thermophysicalProperties file.
Gravitational acceleration is a vector in the negative y-direction with a magnitude of 9.81 m/s^2.
The top wall moves in the positive x-direction with a velocity of 0.1 m/s.
The top wall is held at a constant hot temperature of 350 K.
The bottom wall is stationary and held at a constant cold temperature of 300 K.
The left and right walls are stationary.
The left and right walls have an adiabatic thermal boundary condition (zeroGradient).
All stationary walls have a no-slip velocity boundary condition.
The simulation starts from a quiescent state with zero velocity everywhere.
The initial temperature field is uniform at 325 K.
