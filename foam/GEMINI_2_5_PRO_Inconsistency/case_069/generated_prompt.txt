The simulation will use the simpleFoam solver.
The k-epsilon turbulence model will be used.
The fluid is air with a kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The velocity boundary condition for the 'inlet' is a fixed value with a tangential magnitude of 15 m/s.
The velocity boundary condition for the top 'outlet' is zeroGradient.
The velocity boundary condition for the 'dustOutlet' is zeroGradient.
The velocity boundary condition for the 'walls' is noSlip.
The pressure boundary condition for the 'inlet' is zeroGradient.
The pressure boundary condition for the top 'outlet' is a fixed value of 0.
The pressure boundary condition for the 'dustOutlet' is zeroGradient.
The pressure boundary condition for the 'walls' is zeroGradient.
The turbulent kinetic energy 'k' at the 'inlet' is a fixed value calculated based on 5% turbulence intensity.
The turbulent kinetic energy 'k' at the 'outlet' and 'dustOutlet' is zeroGradient.
The turbulent kinetic energy 'k' at the 'walls' uses the kqRWallFunction.
The turbulent dissipation rate 'epsilon' at the 'inlet' is a fixed value calculated based on a turbulent length scale equal to 7% of the inlet hydraulic diameter.
The turbulent dissipation rate 'epsilon' at the 'outlet' and 'dustOutlet' is zeroGradient.
The turbulent dissipation rate 'epsilon' at the 'walls' uses the epsilonWallFunction.
