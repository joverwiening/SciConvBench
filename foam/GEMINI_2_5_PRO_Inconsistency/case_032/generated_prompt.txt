Simulate the 2D steady-state, turbulent, incompressible flow over a NACA 0012 airfoil.
The angle of attack is 5 degrees.
Use the simpleFoam solver.
Use the k-omega SST turbulence model.
The mesh is a C-grid extruded one cell in the z-direction.
The front and back patches are named 'frontAndBack' and have the type 'symmetry'.
The chord length of the airfoil is 1 meter.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The angle of attack is achieved by setting the inlet velocity components.
The inlet velocity is a fixed value of (9.962 0.872 0) m/s.
The outlet boundary condition for velocity is zeroGradient.
The airfoil surface is a no-slip wall.
The frontAndBack boundary condition for velocity is symmetry.
The inlet boundary condition for pressure is zeroGradient.
The outlet boundary condition for pressure is zeroGradient.
The airfoil boundary condition for pressure is zeroGradient.
The frontAndBack boundary condition for pressure is symmetry.
The turbulence intensity at the inlet is 5%.
The turbulent viscosity ratio at the inlet is 10.
The inlet boundary condition for k is calculated from the 5% turbulence intensity.
The inlet boundary condition for omega is calculated from the 5% turbulence intensity and a turbulent viscosity ratio of 10.
The airfoil boundary condition for k is kqRWallFunction.
The airfoil boundary condition for omega is omegaWallFunction.
The outlet boundary condition for k and omega is zeroGradient.
The frontAndBack boundary condition for k and omega is symmetry.
The simulation will calculate the 2D lift and drag coefficients.
The reference spanwise length for the force coefficient calculation is 1.
