The simulation solves the steady-state, compressible flow of natural gas through a pipe.
The solver to be used is porousSimpleFoam.
The pipe has a length of 1.0 meter and an internal diameter of 0.1 meters.
A porous baffle with a thickness of 0.01 meters is located at the axial midpoint of the pipe.
The gas is modeled as a perfect gas, assuming it is pure methane.
The molecular weight of the gas is 16.04 g/mol.
The transport properties are defined using the Sutherland model in the thermophysicalProperties file.
The Sutherland model reference viscosity (As) is 1.026e-6 Pa·s.
The Sutherland model reference temperature (Ts) is 197.8 K.
The specific heat at constant pressure (Cp) is 2226 J/(kg·K).
The heat of formation (Hf) is 0 J/kg.
The porous zone is defined in the fvOptions dictionary using the Darcy-Forchheimer model.
The Darcy coefficient (d) is isotropic with a value of 1e9 1/m^2.
The Forchheimer coefficient (f) is isotropic with a value of 500 1/m.
The inlet total pressure is 2e5 Pa.
The inlet total temperature is 300 K.
The outlet static pressure is 1e5 Pa.
The pipe wall is an adiabatic, no-slip boundary.
