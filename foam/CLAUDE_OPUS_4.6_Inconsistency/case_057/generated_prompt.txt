Simulate a 2D bubble column using twoPhaseEulerFoam in OpenFOAM.

The domain is 0.5m wide and 2.0m high.
The simulation is 2D, achieved by using a single cell in the z-direction with a small thickness (e.g., 0.01m) and empty boundary conditions on the front and back patches.

The mesh resolution is 50 cells in the x-direction and 200 cells in the y-direction, giving uniform cubic cells of 0.01m × 0.01m.
Use blockMesh to generate the mesh with vertices spanning (0, 0, 0) to (0.5, 2.0, 0.01).

The continuous phase is water with density 998.2 kg/m3 and kinematic viscosity 1.0e-06 m2/s.
The dispersed phase is air with density 1.225 kg/m3 and kinematic viscosity 1.48e-05 m2/s.

The interphase drag model is Schiller-Naumann.
The assumed constant bubble diameter is 4mm (0.004m).

The defaultFieldValues are alpha.air 1.0 and alpha.water 0.0, representing pure air in the top 0.5m.
A boxToCell region is defined with box (0 0 -0.1) (0.5 1.5 0.1).
The fieldValues inside the box are alpha.air 0.4 and alpha.water 0.6, representing the water-air mixture in the bottom 1.5m.

The bottom boundary (inlet) is a gas sparger for the air phase.
The air inlet velocity at the bottom is uniform at 0.02 m/s in the y-direction.
The water phase velocity at the bottom is set to a no-slip condition (fixedValue 0).
The alpha.air at the inlet is fixedValue 1.0 (pure air entering).
The pressure at the inlet is fixedFluxPressure.

The top boundary (outlet) uses a pressureInletOutletVelocity condition for both phase velocities.
The pressure at the outlet is fixedValue 1e5 Pa (atmospheric).
The alpha.air at the outlet is inletOutlet with inletValue 1.0.

The left and right walls use no-slip (fixedValue 0) for both phase velocities.
The pressure at the walls is fixedFluxPressure.
The alpha fields at the walls use zeroGradient.

The front and back patches are set to type empty for the 2D simulation.

The turbulence model for both phases is k-epsilon.
Initial k is 1.0e-04 m2/s2 and initial epsilon is 1.0e-03 m2/s3 for both phases.
Wall functions (kqRWallFunction for k, epsilonWallFunction for epsilon, nutkWallFunction for nut) are applied at the left and right walls.

Gravity is set to (0, -9.81, 0) m/s2 in the constant/g file.

The simulation end time is 20 seconds.
The time step is 1.0e-04 seconds with adjustableRunTime enabled.
The maximum Courant number is 0.5.
The write interval is 0.5 seconds.

The fvSchemes use Gauss linear for gradient schemes, Gauss linearUpwind for divergence schemes, and Gauss linear corrected for Laplacian schemes.
The time scheme is Euler (first-order implicit).

The fvSolution uses GAMG solver for pressure and smoothSolver with GaussSeidel for velocity, k, epsilon, and alpha fields.
The PIMPLE algorithm is used with 2 outer correctors and 1 non-orthogonal corrector.
