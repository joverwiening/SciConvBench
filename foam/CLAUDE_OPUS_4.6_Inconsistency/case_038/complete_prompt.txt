Simulate thermo-elastic deformation of a solid aluminum block that is fixed on one face and heated on the opposite face. The goal is to compute the displacement and stress due to thermal expansion.

Material (aluminum):
- `E = 69e9` Pa
- `nu = 0.33`
- `rho = 2700 kg/m^3`
- thermal expansion coefficient `alpha = 2.3e-5 1/K`
- reference temperature `T0 = 300 K`

Assume a temperature field `T` is available (either prescribed or solved separately, e.g., by a prior conduction solve) and is used to drive thermal strain.

Boundary conditions:
- `0/D`: `fixedFace` is `fixedValue uniform (0 0 0)`; all other faces `zeroGradient`
- `0/T`: `heatedFace` is `fixedValue uniform 400`; `fixedFace` is `zeroGradient`; other faces `zeroGradient`

In `system/controlDict`, set:
```foam
application     solidDisplacementFoam;
```
