The simulation is a two-dimensional Rayleigh-Bénard convection cell in a rectangular cavity using OpenFOAM (ESI/OpenFOAM.com convention).

Use the buoyantFoam solver (transient, compressible, buoyant).

The domain is a rectangular cavity 9 units wide (x-direction), 1 unit tall (y-direction), and extruded one cell deep in the z-direction (spanwise).
The mesh has 90 cells in x, 10 cells in y, and 1 cell in z.
The front and back patches (z-normal faces) are type empty to enforce two-dimensionality.
The bottom wall patch is at y = 0, the top wall patch is at y = 1, and the left and right side wall patches are at x = 0 and x = 9.
Use blockMesh to generate the mesh with uniform cell spacing.

Gravity acts in the negative y-direction: g = (0, -9.81, 0) m/s², specified in constant/g.

The file constant/thermophysicalProperties uses thermoType with type heRhoThermo, mixture pureMixture, transport const, thermo eConst, equationOfState Boussinesq, specie specie, energy sensibleInternalEnergy.
Under mixture, the specie sub-dictionary sets molWeight 29.
The equationOfState sub-dictionary sets rho0 1.0, T0 300, beta 3.3e-3.
The thermodynamics sub-dictionary sets Cv 714.3 and Hf 0.
The transport sub-dictionary sets mu 1.5e-5 and Pr 0.714.

The file constant/momentumTransport sets simulationType to RAS.
Under the RAS sub-dictionary, set model to kEpsilon, turbulence to on, and printCoeffs to on.
