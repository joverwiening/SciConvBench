The solver is buoyantBoussinesqPimpleFoam.
The domain is a square cavity with side length 1 m.
The geometry is 2D in the x-y plane, extruded by one cell in the z-direction with empty boundary conditions on the front and back patches.
Gravity acts in the -y direction at (0, -9.81, 0) m/s^2.
The mesh is uniform with 80 cells in the x-direction and 80 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.

The left wall (x = 0) has a fixed temperature of 310 K.
The right wall (x = 1) has a fixed temperature of 290 K.
The top wall (y = 1) has a zeroGradient condition for temperature (adiabatic).
The bottom wall (y = 0) has a zeroGradient condition for temperature (adiabatic).
The front and back patches are of type empty for all fields.

The initial temperature field T is uniform at 300 K.
The initial velocity field U is uniform at (0 0 0) m/s.
The initial pressure field p_rgh is uniform at 0.

The reference temperature TRef in the transport properties is 300 K.
The fluid properties correspond to air at 300 K for a Rayleigh number of approximately 1e6.
The kinematic viscosity nu is 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The thermal expansion coefficient beta is 3.33e-3 1/K.
The Prandtl number Pr is 0.71.
The laminar thermal diffusivity Pr entry in transportProperties is 0.71.
The turbulent Prandtl number Prt is 0.71.
The transport model is Newtonian.
The simulation is laminar (no turbulence model; laminar is selected in momentumTransport or turbulenceProperties).

All walls use a fixedValue of (0 0 0) for U (no-slip condition).
All walls use a fixedFluxPressure condition for p_rgh.

The simulation runs to a physical end time of 200 s to allow the flow to reach a statistically steady state.
The time step is controlled by a maximum Courant number of 0.5.
The initial deltaT is 0.01 s.
Write interval is every 10 s.

The PIMPLE algorithm uses 1 outer corrector and 2 inner pressure correctors (nCorrectors 2, nNonOrthogonalCorrectors 1).
The fvSchemes use Gauss linear for gradient schemes, Gauss linearUpwind for divergence schemes of velocity, Gauss linear for Laplacian schemes with corrected surface-normal gradients, and Gauss linear for divergence of temperature.
The time scheme is Euler (first-order implicit).

The g file in the constant directory specifies dimensions [0 1 -2 0 0 0 0] and value (0 -9.81 0).
