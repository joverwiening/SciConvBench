The simulation is a 2D laminar, transient flow past a square cylinder in a channel using icoFoam in OpenFOAM.

The domain is 2.2 m long in the x-direction, 0.41 m high in the y-direction, and 0.01 m thick in the z-direction (one cell deep).

A square cylinder of size 0.1 m x 0.1 m is placed in the flow path with its lower-left corner at (0.15, 0.15, 0) and its upper-right corner at (0.25, 0.25, 0.01).

The kinematic viscosity nu is 0.001 m^2/s, specified in constant/transportProperties.

The solver is icoFoam.

The boundary condition for U at the inlet is a parabolic inflow profile with maxValue (1.5 0 0), applied using a suitable parabolic profile boundary condition (e.g., codedFixedValue or groovyBC implementing U_x = 4 * 1.5 * y * (0.41 - y) / (0.41^2), U_y = 0, U_z = 0).

The boundary condition for U at the outlet is zeroGradient.

The boundary condition for U at topAndBottomWalls is noSlip.

The boundary condition for U at cylinder is noSlip.

The boundary condition for U at frontAndBack is empty.

The boundary condition for p at the inlet is zeroGradient.

The boundary condition for p at the outlet is fixedValue uniform 0.

The boundary condition for p at topAndBottomWalls is zeroGradient.

The boundary condition for p at cylinder is zeroGradient.

The boundary condition for p at frontAndBack is empty.

In constant/polyMesh/boundary, the frontAndBack patches are defined with type empty.

In constant/polyMesh/boundary, the inlet, outlet, topAndBottomWalls, and cylinder patches are defined with type wall (or type patch for inlet and outlet as appropriate for their boundary conditions).

The mesh is generated using blockMesh with a multi-block topology that resolves the square cylinder. A reasonable mesh resolution is approximately 200 cells along the channel length, 40 cells along the channel height, and 1 cell in the z-direction, with local refinement near the cylinder walls to adequately capture boundary layers. Grading should be applied near the cylinder surfaces to cluster cells there.

The end time is 8.0 s, which is sufficient for the flow to develop and exhibit transient vortex shedding behavior at the Reynolds number of this configuration (Re based on the mean inflow velocity and cylinder side length is approximately 100).

The time step deltaT is 0.0005 s, chosen to maintain a Courant number below 1 for numerical stability.

The write interval is 0.05 s (every 100 time steps), providing sufficient temporal resolution of the output fields.

The fvSchemes file uses Euler for time discretization (ddtSchemes), Gauss linear for gradient schemes (gradSchemes), Gauss linear for divergence schemes (divSchemes), and Gauss linear corrected for Laplacian schemes (laplacianSchemes). Interpolation schemes use linear and surface normal gradient schemes use corrected.

The fvSolution file uses the PISO algorithm with nCorrectors set to 2 and nNonOrthogonalCorrectors set to 1. The pressure solver uses PCG with DIC preconditioner and a tolerance of 1e-06. The velocity solver uses PBiCGStab with DILU preconditioner and a tolerance of 1e-05.
