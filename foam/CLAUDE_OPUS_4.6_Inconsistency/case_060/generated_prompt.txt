The solver is interFoam.
The application entry in system/controlDict is interFoam.
The simulation is a classic 2D dam-break problem with water collapsing into air across a sharp free surface.

The computational domain is 0.584 m wide (x-direction) and 0.584 m tall (y-direction).
The domain is extruded one cell in the z-direction to create a quasi-2D setup.
The front and back patches are set to the empty type.

The mesh resolution is 46 cells in the x-direction and 46 cells in the y-direction, giving a uniform cell size of approximately 0.0127 m in both directions.
There is 1 cell in the z-direction.
The z-direction extrusion spans from 0 to 0.0127 m (one cell deep matching the x-y cell size).

The initial water column is 0.1461 m wide (x-direction) and 0.2922 m tall (y-direction), positioned at the left side of the domain starting from the bottom corner (x from 0 to 0.1461 m, y from 0 to 0.2922 m).
The field alpha.water is initialized using setFields.
Inside the water column region, alpha.water is set to 1.
Everywhere else, alpha.water is set to 0.

The bottom boundary (y = 0) is a no-slip wall with fixedFluxPressure for pressure and zeroGradient for alpha.water.
The left boundary (x = 0) is a no-slip wall with fixedFluxPressure for pressure and zeroGradient for alpha.water.
The right boundary (x = 0.584 m) is a no-slip wall with fixedFluxPressure for pressure and zeroGradient for alpha.water.
The top boundary (y = 0.584 m) is open to atmosphere, using totalPressure for p_rgh (with p0 set to 0), pressureInletOutletVelocity for U, and inletOutlet for alpha.water with an inlet value of 0.
The front and back boundaries are set to empty.

Gravity acts in the negative y-direction at 9.81 m/s squared.

The fluid properties for water are density 1000 kg/m3 and kinematic viscosity 1e-06 m2/s.
The fluid properties for air are density 1 kg/m3 and kinematic viscosity 1.48e-05 m2/s.
Surface tension between water and air is 0.07 N/m.

The simulation uses adjustable time stepping.
The maximum Courant number (maxCo) is 1.
The maximum alpha Courant number (maxAlphaCo) is 1.
The initial deltaT is 0.001 s.
The end time is 1 s.

The write control is adjustableRunTime.
The write interval is 0.05 s.
The write format is ascii.
The write precision is 6.

The PIMPLE algorithm is used with 3 outer correctors, 1 non-orthogonal corrector, and the momentumPredictor set to no.
