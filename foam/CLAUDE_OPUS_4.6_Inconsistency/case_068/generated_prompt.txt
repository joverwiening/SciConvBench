Perform a conjugate heat transfer (CHT) simulation of an electronics component cooled by forced air convection using the chtMultiRegionSimpleFoam solver in OpenFOAM.

The simulation consists of two regions: a solid region named "solid" (silicon chip) and a fluid region named "air".

The silicon chip dimensions are approximately 10 mm x 10 mm x 2 mm.
The air domain (housing interior) dimensions are approximately 50 mm x 30 mm x 30 mm, with the chip mounted on the bottom wall near the center.

The air inlet patch is named "inlet".
The air outlet patch is named "outlet".
The solid-fluid coupled interface patches are named "solid_to_air" on the solid side and "air_to_solid" on the fluid side.
The outer housing boundary on the solid side is named "outerCasing".

The inlet air velocity is uniform at 1 m/s in the streamwise direction.
The inlet air temperature is fixed at 293 K.
The outlet uses a zeroGradient condition for temperature and a fixedValue condition for pressure at 0 Pa gauge (1 atm reference).
The outlet velocity uses an inletOutlet or zeroGradient condition.

The volumetric heat generation rate in the silicon chip is 1e7 W/m3, applied via an fvOptions file in the solid region using a scalarSemiImplicitSource on the energy equation.

The outerCasing boundary condition in 0/solid/T is zeroGradient (perfectly insulated, adiabatic).

The solid-fluid interface uses compressible::turbulentTemperatureCoupledBaffleMixed on both the solid side and the fluid side, with the method set to solidThermo on the solid side and fluidThermo on the fluid side, and the neighbourFieldName set to T.

Air properties use standard values at approximately 293 K: density from the equation of state (ideal gas), specific heat Cp of 1005 J/(kg·K), dynamic viscosity of 1.85e-5 Pa·s, thermal conductivity of 0.026 W/(m·K), and Prandtl number of approximately 0.71.

Silicon chip properties use standard textbook values: density of 2330 kg/m3, specific heat Cp of 700 J/(kg·K), and thermal conductivity of 148 W/(m·K).

The flow is assumed laminar given the small domain size and low velocity, resulting in a Reynolds number well below transition. The turbulence model is set to laminar for the air region.

The thermophysical model for the air region uses hePsiThermo with pureMixture and the ideal gas equation of state, with const transport and sensibleEnthalpy as the energy variable.
The thermophysical model for the solid region uses heSolidThermo with pureMixture, rhoConst equation of state, constIso transport for isotropic thermal conductivity, and sensibleEnthalpy as the energy variable.

The pressure-velocity coupling uses the SIMPLE algorithm within chtMultiRegionSimpleFoam.
Relaxation factors are set to approximately 0.7 for velocity, 0.3 for pressure, and 0.7 for energy/temperature.
Convergence residual targets are 1e-6 for energy and 1e-4 for velocity and pressure.

The simulation runs in steady state.

Gravity is set to -9.81 m/s2 in the vertical direction in the constant/g file, though buoyancy effects are expected to be minor relative to the forced convection.

All remaining walls in the air region (housing interior walls other than the coupled interface) use a no-slip velocity condition and zeroGradient for temperature.
