The simulation is a 2D steady-state, turbulent, incompressible flow over a NACA 0012 airfoil at a 5-degree angle of attack.
The solver is simpleFoam (SIMPLE algorithm for steady-state, incompressible flow).
The turbulence model is k-omega SST.
The airfoil has a chord length of 1 m.
The freestream velocity magnitude is 10 m/s.
The kinematic viscosity is 1.5e-5 m²/s, corresponding to standard air properties.
The resulting Reynolds number is approximately 6.67e5.
The freestream density of air is 1.225 kg/m³.

The mesh is a structured C-grid generated using blockMeshDict.
The C-grid far-field boundary extends approximately 20 chord lengths from the airfoil in all directions.
The airfoil surface has approximately 200 cells along its length.
The first cell height at the airfoil wall is sized to achieve y+ of approximately 1, which is required for the k-omega SST model to resolve the viscous sublayer.
A geometric growth ratio of approximately 1.1 to 1.2 is used for cells expanding away from the airfoil wall.
The mesh is extruded exactly one cell in the z-direction to create the 2D domain.

In blockMeshDict, the front and back patches are named frontAndBack and are of type empty.
The inlet patch encompasses the far-field C-shaped boundary upstream and above/below the airfoil.
The outlet patch is the downstream boundary of the domain.
The airfoil patch is the airfoil surface itself, defined as a wall.

The inlet velocity U is set to (10 0 0) m/s as a fixedValue condition, with the 5-degree angle of attack achieved by rotating the airfoil geometry rather than the velocity vector. Alternatively, the velocity may be set to (9.962 0.872 0) m/s with the airfoil at zero geometric incidence to achieve the same 5-degree effective angle of attack. Either approach is acceptable.
The outlet boundary condition for U is zeroGradient.
The airfoil boundary condition for U is fixedValue with a value of (0 0 0), enforcing the no-slip condition.
The frontAndBack boundary condition for U is empty.

The inlet boundary condition for p is zeroGradient.
The outlet boundary condition for p is fixedValue with a value of 0.
The airfoil boundary condition for p is zeroGradient.
The frontAndBack boundary condition for p is empty.

The inlet boundary condition for k is fixedValue, set using a turbulence intensity of approximately 0.1% and the freestream velocity, giving k = 1.5 * (U * I)^2, which is approximately 1.5e-4 m²/s².
The outlet boundary condition for k is zeroGradient.
The airfoil boundary condition for k is fixedValue with a value of 1e-10 (effectively zero at the wall), or alternatively kqRWallFunction.
The frontAndBack boundary condition for k is empty.

The inlet boundary condition for omega is fixedValue, set consistently with the inlet k and a turbulent viscosity ratio of approximately 10, giving omega = k / (nu * nuTilda_ratio). A value of approximately 1.0 s⁻¹ or computed from omega = k / (0.09 * nu_t) is used. A representative inlet value is approximately 1.0 s⁻¹.
The outlet boundary condition for omega is zeroGradient.
The airfoil boundary condition for omega is omegaWallFunction.
The frontAndBack boundary condition for omega is empty.

The inlet boundary condition for nut is calculated.
The outlet boundary condition for nut is calculated.
The airfoil boundary condition for nut is nutkWallFunction.
The frontAndBack boundary condition for nut is empty.

The fvSchemes file uses Gauss linearUpwind for the divergence terms (div(phi,U), div(phi,k), div(phi,omega)) and Gauss linear for Laplacian and gradient terms, or equivalent second-order schemes suitable for steady-state RANS.
The fvSolution file configures the SIMPLE algorithm with under-relaxation factors of approximately 0.7 for U, 0.3 for p, and 0.7 for k and omega.
Convergence is determined when all residuals (U, p, k, omega) drop below 1e-6, or when the monitored lift and drag coefficients reach a steady value.
A maximum of 5000 iterations is allowed.

The simulation computes lift and drag coefficients using the forceCoeffs function object.
The reference area (Aref) is the chord length times the span, which is 1 m * 1 m = 1 m² (using the unit z-extrusion depth).
The reference length (lRef) is 1 m (chord length).
The freestream dynamic pressure is based on U = 10 m/s and rho = 1.225 kg/m³.
The lift direction is perpendicular to the freestream velocity and the drag direction is parallel to the freestream velocity.
The pitching moment reference point is at the quarter-chord location (0.25, 0, 0).
