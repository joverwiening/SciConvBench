The simulation is a 2D, steady-state, incompressible RANS simulation of airflow over a NACA 0012 airfoil using OpenFOAM.

The solver is simpleFoam.

The turbulence model is k-omega SST.

The airfoil chord length is 1 meter.

The angle of attack is 2 degrees.

The freestream velocity magnitude is approximately 51.4 m/s, corresponding to a Reynolds number of 6 million based on a chord of 1 m and air with kinematic viscosity 1.0 times 10 to the power of negative 5 m squared per second (approximately Mach 0.15, satisfying incompressibility).

Air density is 1.225 kg per m cubed and kinematic viscosity is approximately 8.568 times 10 to the power of negative 6 m squared per second, chosen to achieve Re equal to 6 million at the stated velocity and chord. Alternatively, use nu equal to 8.568e-06 m2/s in the transportProperties file.

The freestream velocity components are Ux equal to 51.4 times cos(2 degrees) and Uy equal to 51.4 times sin(2 degrees), giving approximately (51.37, 1.794, 0) m/s.

The mesh is a 2D mesh created as a single layer of cells in the z-direction, using a C-type mesh topology around the airfoil.

The mesh has the following patches: "inlet" for the C-shaped far-field boundary encompassing the upstream and top/bottom regions, "outlet" for the downstream far-field boundary, "airfoil" for the airfoil surface, and "frontAndBack" for the two z-normal faces.

In the constant/polyMesh/boundary file, the frontAndBack patches are defined with type empty.

In the constant/polyMesh/boundary file, the inlet patch is type patch, the outlet patch is type patch, and the airfoil patch is type wall.

In the 0/U file, the inlet boundary condition is fixedValue with uniform value (51.37 1.794 0).
In the 0/U file, the outlet boundary condition is zeroGradient.
In the 0/U file, the airfoil boundary condition is noSlip.
In the 0/U file, the frontAndBack boundary condition is empty.
The internal field for U is uniform (51.37 1.794 0).

In the 0/p file, the inlet boundary condition is zeroGradient.
In the 0/p file, the outlet boundary condition is fixedValue with uniform value 0.
In the 0/p file, the airfoil boundary condition is zeroGradient.
In the 0/p file, the frontAndBack boundary condition is empty.
The internal field for p is uniform 0.

Freestream turbulence quantities are calculated assuming a turbulence intensity of 0.1 percent and a turbulent viscosity ratio of 1.

The freestream turbulent kinetic energy k is 1.5 times (0.001 times 51.4) squared, which is approximately 0.00397 m2/s2.

The freestream specific dissipation rate omega is k divided by (nu times turbulent viscosity ratio), which is 0.00397 divided by 8.568e-06, giving approximately 463 per second.

In the 0/k file, the inlet boundary condition is fixedValue with uniform value 0.00397.
In the 0/k file, the outlet boundary condition is zeroGradient.
In the 0/k file, the airfoil boundary condition is fixedValue with uniform value 1e-10 (effectively zero at the wall, or use kqRWallFunction).
In the 0/k file, the frontAndBack boundary condition is empty.
The internal field for k is uniform 0.00397.

In the 0/omega file, the inlet boundary condition is fixedValue with uniform value 463.
In the 0/omega file, the outlet boundary condition is zeroGradient.
In the 0/omega file, the airfoil boundary condition is omegaWallFunction with uniform value 463.
In the 0/omega file, the frontAndBack boundary condition is empty.
The internal field for omega is uniform 463.

In the 0/nut file, the inlet boundary condition is calculated with uniform value 0.
In the 0/nut file, the outlet boundary condition is calculated with uniform value 0.
In the 0/nut file, the airfoil boundary condition is nutkWallFunction with uniform value 0.
In the 0/nut file, the frontAndBack boundary condition is empty.
The internal field for nut is uniform 0.

The constant/transportProperties file specifies transportModel Newtonian and nu equal to 8.568e-06 m2/s.

The constant/turbulenceProperties file specifies simulationType RAS with RAS model kOmegaSST, turbulence on, and printCoeffs on.

In the system/fvSchemes file, ddtSchemes default is steadyState.
The gradSchemes default is Gauss linear.
The divSchemes div(phi,U) is bounded Gauss linearUpwind grad(U).
The divSchemes div(phi,k) is bounded Gauss upwind.
The divSchemes div(phi,omega) is bounded Gauss upwind.
The divSchemes div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) is Gauss linear.
The laplacianSchemes default is Gauss linear corrected.
The interpolationSchemes default is linear.
The snGradSchemes default is corrected.

In the system/fvSolution file, the U solver is smoothSolver with smoother symGaussSeidel, tolerance 1e-06, and relTol 0.1.
The p solver is GAMG with smoother GaussSeidel, tolerance 1e-06, and relTol 0.01.
The k and omega solvers are smoothSolver with smoother symGaussSeidel, tolerance 1e-06, and relTol 0.1.
The SIMPLE consistent flag is yes.
The SIMPLE residualControl sets p to 1e-04, U to 1e-04, k to 1e-04, and omega to 1e-04.
The relaxationFactors for equations are U 0.7, p 0.3, k 0.7, and omega 0.7.
