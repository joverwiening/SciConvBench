Set up an axisymmetric simulation of swirling flow inside a pipe using simpleFoam in OpenFOAM.

The geometry is a 2D wedge with a half-angle of 2.5 degrees on each side of the symmetry plane (total wedge angle of 5 degrees), and one cell thick in the tangential direction.

The pipe has a length of 0.5 m and a radius of 0.025 m (diameter of 0.05 m).

The working fluid is air with a kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m^2/s.

The inlet condition is a uniform axial velocity of 1.0 m/s combined with a tangential (swirl) velocity component of 0.5 m/s. This gives a bulk Reynolds number of approximately 1667 based on diameter, which is in the laminar regime.

The simulation uses the laminar flow model (no turbulence model). Set turbulenceModel to laminar in constant/turbulenceProperties.

The mesh is defined in system/blockMeshDict using a single hexahedral block.

The mesh resolution is 200 cells in the axial direction and 40 cells in the radial direction, with 1 cell in the tangential direction.

A radial grading ratio of 0.2 (cells compressed toward the wall) is applied in the radial direction. No grading is applied in the axial direction.

The blockMeshDict defines the following patches. The centerline patch is named axis and is of type empty. The pipe surface patch is named wall and is of type wall. The inlet patch is of type patch. The outlet patch is of type patch. The front patch is of type wedge. The back patch is of type wedge.

The front and back wedge patches each consist of one face corresponding to the two tangential planes of the wedge.

The inlet boundary conditions are as follows. U is fixedValue uniform (1.0 0 0.5) where the axial component is along x and the swirl component is along z, decomposed appropriately into the wedge coordinate system. p is zeroGradient.

The outlet boundary conditions are as follows. U is zeroGradient. p is fixedValue uniform 0.

The wall boundary conditions are as follows. U is fixedValue uniform (0 0 0) (no-slip). p is zeroGradient.

The axis boundary conditions are as follows. U is type empty (since the axis patch is of type empty in a wedge mesh, or alternatively use the symmetry axis condition). p is type empty.

The front and back boundary conditions are as follows. U is type wedge. p is type wedge.

The system/fvSchemes file uses the following. ddtSchemes default is steadyState. gradSchemes default is Gauss linear. divSchemes div(phi,U) is bounded Gauss linearUpwind grad(U). laplacianSchemes default is Gauss linear corrected. interpolationSchemes default is linear. snGradSchemes default is corrected.

The system/fvSolution file uses the following. The p solver is GAMG with tolerance 1e-6 and relTol 0.01. The U solver is smoothSolver with smoother symGaussSeidel, tolerance 1e-6, and relTol 0.1. SIMPLE relaxation factors are 0.7 for U and 0.3 for p. The SIMPLE residualControl is 1e-5 for both p and U.

The system/controlDict uses the following. application is simpleFoam. startFrom is startTime. startTime is 0. stopAt is endTime. endTime is 1000. deltaT is 1. writeControl is timeStep. writeInterval is 100. purgeWrite is 3. writeFormat is ascii. writePrecision is 8. runFormat is general.

The vertex coordinates in blockMeshDict are computed so that the wedge spans plus and minus 2.5 degrees from the x-y plane, with the x-axis as the pipe axis and the radial direction along y. The z-coordinates of the outer-radius vertices are plus and minus R times sin(2.5 degrees), and the y-coordinates of the outer-radius vertices are R times cos(2.5 degrees). The axis vertices lie on the x-axis at y=0, z=0.
