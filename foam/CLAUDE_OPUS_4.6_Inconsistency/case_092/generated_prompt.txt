The simulation uses rhoPimpleFoam to solve compressible, supersonic flow of air over a 10-degree wedge, producing an oblique shock at Mach 2.0.

Air is treated as a perfect gas using hePsiThermo with equationOfState perfectGas and transport sutherland in constant/thermophysicalProperties. The energy equation uses sensible enthalpy (h). The specie is air with molecular weight 28.97 kg/kmol. The thermodynamics entry uses constant specific heat cp = 1005 J/(kg·K). Sutherland transport uses As = 1.458e-06 and Ts = 110.4.

The freestream conditions are U = (686 0 0) m/s, T = 293 K, and p = 101325 Pa.

The computational domain is 2D, extruded one cell in the z-direction with a depth of 0.01 m.

The domain length in the x-direction is 1.0 m. The domain height in the y-direction is 0.5 m. The wedge begins at x = 0.2 m on the bottom boundary. From x = 0 to x = 0.2 m the bottom boundary is flat (y = 0). From x = 0.2 m to x = 1.0 m the bottom boundary ramps downward (or upward into the flow) at 10 degrees, forming the wedge surface. The wedge deflects the flow by 10 degrees.

The mesh uses approximately 200 cells in the x-direction and 100 cells in the y-direction, giving roughly 20000 cells total. The mesh is generated with blockMesh. Cells are uniform in spacing unless grading is needed near the wedge surface, but uniform spacing is acceptable for this case.

The inlet boundary is the left face (x = 0). The outlet boundary is the right face (x = 1.0 m). The top boundary is the top face (y = 0.5 m). The wedge surface is the lower boundary from x = 0.2 m to x = 1.0 m. The flat plate section is the lower boundary from x = 0 to x = 0.2 m. The front and back faces are the z-normal faces.

Boundary conditions for U: inlet is fixedValue uniform (686 0 0); outlet is zeroGradient; top is slip; wedge surface is slip; flat plate section is slip; frontAndBack is empty.

Boundary conditions for p: inlet is fixedValue uniform 101325; outlet is waveTransmissive with gamma 1.4, fieldInf 101325, lInf 1.0, and value uniform 101325; top is slip; wedge surface is slip; flat plate section is slip; frontAndBack is empty.

Boundary conditions for T: inlet is fixedValue uniform 293; outlet is waveTransmissive with gamma 1.4, fieldInf 293, lInf 1.0, and value uniform 293; top is slip; wedge surface is slip; flat plate section is slip; frontAndBack is empty.

In system/controlDict, the application is rhoPimpleFoam. The startFrom is startTime. The startTime is 0. The stopAt is endTime. The endTime is 0.005 s. The deltaT is 1e-06 s. The writeControl is adjustableRunTime. The writeInterval is 0.0005 s. The adjustTimeStep is yes. The maxCo is 0.5. The maxDeltaT is 1e-05 s.

In system/fvSchemes, use second-order schemes where stable. Time discretization uses Euler (first-order implicit for robustness). Gradient schemes use Gauss linear. Divergence schemes use Gauss linearUpwind for velocity and energy, and Gauss upwind for turbulence quantities if present. Laplacian schemes use Gauss linear corrected. Interpolation schemes use linear. Surface normal gradient schemes use corrected.

In system/fvSolution, PIMPLE is used with nOuterCorrectors 2, nCorrectors 1, and nNonOrthogonalCorrectors 1. Solver for p uses GAMG with tolerance 1e-06 and relTol 0.01. Solver for U, h, and other fields uses smoothSolver with smoother GaussSeidel, tolerance 1e-06, and relTol 0.01.

No turbulence model is required; the simulation is laminar. In constant/momentumTransport (or constant/turbulenceProperties), set simulationType to laminar.

The simulation is expected to reach a quasi-steady state by approximately 0.003 to 0.005 s as the oblique shock establishes and transients exit the domain.
