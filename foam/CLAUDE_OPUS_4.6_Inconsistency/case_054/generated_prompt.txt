Use the nonNewtonianIcoFoam solver to simulate the flow of a viscous, shear-thickening Power Law fluid over a two-dimensional backward-facing step.

The geometry of the backward-facing step is defined as follows. The inlet channel height is 0.01 m. The step height is 0.01 m, giving an expansion ratio of 2:1. The downstream channel height is 0.02 m. The upstream channel length before the step is 0.1 m. The downstream channel length after the step is 0.3 m. The geometry is two-dimensional, with a depth of one cell in the z-direction using empty boundary conditions on the front and back planes.

The mesh uses approximately 200 cells in the streamwise direction and 40 cells in the cross-stream direction. The mesh is graded near the step corner and near the walls to resolve the shear layers and recirculation zone, with a finer resolution in the region immediately downstream of the step.

The fluid is defined in the transportProperties file as follows. The transport model is PowerLaw. The power-law exponent n is 1.5, which represents a shear-thickening fluid. The consistency index k is 0.01 Pa·s^n. The minimum viscosity nuMin is 1e-06 m^2/s. The maximum viscosity nuMax is 1e+02 m^2/s.

The boundary conditions for velocity (U) are as follows. The inlet uses a fixedValue condition with a uniform velocity of (0.1 0 0) m/s. The outlet uses a zeroGradient condition. The walls (top wall, bottom wall including the step face) use a noSlip condition. The front and back planes use the empty condition.

The boundary conditions for pressure (p) are as follows. The inlet uses a zeroGradient condition. The outlet uses a fixedValue condition with a uniform value of 0. The walls use a zeroGradient condition. The front and back planes use the empty condition.

The simulation control parameters in the controlDict file are as follows. The start time is 0. The end time is 5 s, which is sufficient for the flow to reach a steady state. The time step deltaT is 1e-04 s to maintain numerical stability. The write interval is 0.1 s. The run-time modifiable flag is set to true.

The fvSchemes file uses Gauss linear for gradient schemes, Gauss linearUpwind for divergence schemes to maintain stability with the non-Newtonian viscosity, and Gauss linear corrected for Laplacian schemes. The time scheme is Euler.

The fvSolution file uses the PISO algorithm with 2 corrector steps. The pressure equation is solved using the PCG solver with a DIC preconditioner and a tolerance of 1e-06. The velocity equation is solved using the PBiCGStab solver with a DILU preconditioner and a tolerance of 1e-05.

The primary quantities of interest from this simulation are the recirculation zone length downstream of the step, the velocity and pressure fields, and the effective viscosity distribution, all of which illustrate the effect of shear-thickening behavior on the flow over the backward-facing step.
