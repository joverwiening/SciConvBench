The simulation uses the buoyantSimpleFoam solver for steady-state, buoyancy-driven natural convection in a vertical heated pipe.
The pipe geometry is a cylinder with a diameter of 0.1 m and a length of 2 m, aligned with the Z-axis, with the inlet at z=0 and the outlet at z=2.
The mesh is a structured hexahedral mesh with approximately 20 cells across the diameter and 200 cells along the length, with near-wall refinement to resolve thermal and velocity boundary layers.
The working fluid is air, using the perfectGas equation of state in the thermophysicalProperties dictionary.
The transport model uses the Sutherland or polynomial model for dynamic viscosity appropriate for air.
The specific heat capacity Cp is set to 1005 J/(kg·K) for air.
The molecular weight is set to 28.97 g/mol for air.
The Prandtl number is approximately 0.71 for air.
The thermophysical model uses hePsiThermo with pureMixture for a compressible ideal gas treatment.
The turbulence model is laminar, as the expected Rayleigh number for this configuration is moderate and the flow is expected to remain in the laminar regime.
In the constant/g dictionary, gravity is set to (0 0 -9.81) m/s^2 to enable buoyancy-driven flow.
The inlet boundary at z=0 has pressureInletOutletVelocity for U, fixedValue of 300 K for T, and calculated for p_rgh.
The outlet boundary at z=2 has zeroGradient for U, zeroGradient for T, and fixedValue of 0 Pa for p_rgh.
A reference pressure of 101325 Pa is set via the pRef field or operating pressure in the fvSolution or thermophysical setup.
The wall boundary has noSlip (fixedValue uniform (0 0 0)) for U, fixedValue of 350 K for T, and fixedFluxPressure for p_rgh.
The fvSchemes dictionary uses Gauss linear for gradient schemes, Gauss linearUpwind for divergence schemes to balance accuracy and stability, and Gauss linear corrected for Laplacian schemes.
The fvSolution dictionary uses GAMG or PCG solvers for pressure and smoothSolver with GaussSeidel for velocity and temperature.
The SIMPLE algorithm is used with residual convergence criteria of 1e-6 for all fields.
Under-relaxation factors are set to approximately 0.3 for p_rgh, 0.7 for U, and 0.7 for T.
The simulation runs for a sufficient number of iterations (e.g., 5000) to reach steady state, monitored by residual convergence.
The goal is to observe the natural convection velocity and temperature fields established by the density differences between the heated wall region and the cooler inlet air.
