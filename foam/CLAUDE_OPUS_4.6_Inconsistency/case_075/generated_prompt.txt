The simulation is a 2D bubble column using twoPhaseEulerFoam in OpenFOAM.

The domain is 0.5m in the X-direction and 2m in the Y-direction.
The domain is one cell thick in the Z-direction, making this a 2D simulation with front and back patches set to empty.
The Z-direction thickness is 0.01m (a single cell).

The mesh resolution is 50 cells in the X-direction and 200 cells in the Y-direction, giving uniform 0.01m cells in both directions.
The Z-direction has 1 cell.

The two phases are air (dispersed) and water (continuous).
The air density is 1.225 kg/m3.
The air kinematic viscosity is 1.48e-05 m2/s.
The water density is 998.2 kg/m3.
The water kinematic viscosity is 1.004e-06 m2/s.

The assumed bubble diameter for the dispersed air phase is 4mm (0.004m).

The drag model used for interphase momentum transfer is the Schiller-Naumann drag model.

The gravity vector in constant/g is set to (0 -9.81 0).

The sparger inlet is located at the bottom center of the domain, spanning 0.1m in the X-direction (from X=0.2m to X=0.3m), which is 20% of the column width.

The bottom boundary is split into two patches: inlet and bottomWall.
On the inlet patch, alpha.air is fixedValue with a value of 1.
On the inlet patch, U.air is fixedValue with a value of (0 0.1 0).
On the inlet patch, U.water is fixedValue with a value of (0 0 0).
On the inlet patch, pressure (p_rgh) is fixedFluxPressure.
On the bottomWall patch, U.air and U.water are noSlip.
On the bottomWall patch, alpha.air is zeroGradient.
On the bottomWall patch, p_rgh is fixedFluxPressure.

The top boundary is an outlet.
On the outlet patch, p_rgh is fixedValue with a value of 0.
On the outlet patch, U.air is pressureInletOutletVelocity with an inletValue of (0 0 0).
On the outlet patch, U.water is pressureInletOutletVelocity with an inletValue of (0 0 0).
On the outlet patch, alpha.air is inletOutlet with an inletValue of 0.

The left and right walls are noSlip for both U.air and U.water.
On the side walls, alpha.air is zeroGradient.
On the side walls, p_rgh is fixedFluxPressure.

The front and back patches are set to empty for all fields.

The initial condition for alpha.air is 0 throughout the domain (column initially filled with water).
The initial condition for U.air is (0 0 0).
The initial condition for U.water is (0 0 0).
The initial condition for p_rgh is 0.

The turbulence model for both phases is k-epsilon.
Initial and boundary values for k and epsilon are set to small default values (k = 1e-04 m2/s2, epsilon = 1e-03 m2/s3) on walls and inlet, with inletOutlet conditions on the outlet.
Wall functions (kqRWallFunction for k, epsilonWallFunction for epsilon, nutkWallFunction for nut) are used on all wall patches for both phases.

The simulation end time is 10 seconds.
The time step is controlled by an adjustable time step with a maximum Courant number of 0.5.
The initial deltaT is 1e-04 seconds.
The maximum deltaT is 1e-03 seconds.

Write interval is every 0.1 seconds.

The solver settings in fvSolution use PIMPLE with 3 outer correctors and 1 non-orthogonal corrector.
