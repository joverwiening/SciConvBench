Set up a transient simulation of turbulent flow over a 2D cylinder in a channel using pimpleFoam with a pure LES turbulence model.

The solver is pimpleFoam (transient, incompressible).

The simulation type is LES only, with no RAS sub-dictionary.

The constant/turbulenceProperties file contains simulationType LES, with the LES sub-dictionary specifying LESModel Smagorinsky and delta cubeRootVol.

The fluid is incompressible air with kinematic viscosity nu = 1.5e-05 m^2/s and density rho = 1.225 kg/m^3, specified in constant/transportProperties.

The cylinder diameter D is 0.01 m (1 cm), giving a Reynolds number of approximately 6667 based on the inlet velocity and air properties.

The channel length is 30D (0.3 m) with the cylinder center placed at 10D (0.1 m) from the inlet, leaving 20D (0.2 m) downstream for wake development.

The channel height is 10D (0.1 m), with the cylinder centered vertically.

The mesh is a 2D structured mesh with approximately 200 cells along the channel length and 100 cells across the channel height, with local refinement around the cylinder surface to achieve a near-wall cell size of approximately 0.0002 m (0.02D). The mesh is one cell thick in the spanwise (z) direction with empty boundary conditions on the front and back faces to enforce 2D behavior.

The inlet velocity is uniform at 10 m/s in the x-direction.

The cylinder surface has a noSlip (fixedValue uniform (0 0 0)) velocity boundary condition and a zeroGradient pressure boundary condition.

The top and bottom channel walls have noSlip (fixedValue uniform (0 0 0)) velocity boundary condition and a zeroGradient pressure boundary condition.

The inlet has a fixedValue velocity of uniform (10 0 0) and a zeroGradient pressure boundary condition.

The outlet has a zeroGradient velocity boundary condition and a fixedValue pressure of uniform 0.

The front and back patches are of type empty.

The time step deltaT is 1e-05 s to maintain a maximum Courant number below 1 for LES stability.

The total simulation end time is 0.5 s, providing sufficient time for flow development and vortex shedding statistics.

The write interval is every 0.005 s (every 500 time steps).

The output fields include velocity (U) and pressure (p).

The system/fvSchemes file uses second-order schemes: Gauss linear for gradient schemes, Gauss linear for divergence schemes (or Gauss LUST for the convective term div(phi,U) as appropriate for LES), and Gauss linear corrected for Laplacian schemes. The time scheme is backward (second-order implicit).

The system/fvSolution file configures the PIMPLE algorithm with nOuterCorrectors 2, nCorrectors 1, and nNonOrthogonalCorrectors 1. The pressure solver uses GAMG with a DIC smoother and tolerance of 1e-06. The velocity solver uses smoothSolver with a symGaussSeidel smoother and tolerance of 1e-06.

Function objects are included in system/controlDict to compute force coefficients (drag and lift) on the cylinder patch, using freestream velocity magnitude 10 m/s, reference length (lRef) 0.01 m, and reference area (Aref) 0.01 m^2 per unit span.
