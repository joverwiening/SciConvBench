The simulation is a 3D, incompressible, steady-state, turbulent flow around a surface-mounted cube in a channel, solved with simpleFoam and a RANS kEpsilon turbulence model.

The cube has a side length of H = 1 m and sits on the channel floor with one face flush against the floor.

The channel dimensions are 20H long (from x = -5H to x = 15H), 6H tall (from y = 0 to y = 6H), and 4H wide in the spanwise direction (from z = -2H to z = 2H). The cube is centered spanwise and its upstream face is at x = 0.

The fluid is air with a density of 1.225 kg/m^3 and a kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m^2/s.

The inlet velocity is uniform at U = 0.15 m/s in the streamwise (x) direction, giving a Reynolds number of Re = U*H/nu = 10000 based on the cube side length.

The blockMeshDict defines a multi-block structured hexahedral mesh with the cube region carved out (no mesh inside the cube). The mesh has more than one cell in the spanwise (z) direction.

The left patch (z = -2H plane) has type symmetryPlane in blockMeshDict.
The right patch (z = 2H plane) has type symmetryPlane in blockMeshDict.

The base mesh resolution is approximately 20 cells per cube side length in each direction, with grading (cell clustering) near all solid walls and near the cube surfaces to keep wall-adjacent y+ values in the range of 30 to 100, suitable for standard wall functions.

Boundary conditions for velocity (U): inlet is fixedValue uniform (0.15 0 0); outlet is zeroGradient; floor (y = 0 plane excluding cube footprint) is noSlip (fixedValue uniform (0 0 0)); top (y = 6H plane) is noSlip (fixedValue uniform (0 0 0)); all cube faces are noSlip (fixedValue uniform (0 0 0)); left and right are symmetryPlane.

Boundary conditions for pressure (p): inlet is zeroGradient; outlet is fixedValue uniform 0; floor, top, and all cube faces are zeroGradient; left and right are symmetryPlane.

Boundary conditions for k: inlet is fixedValue with turbulent intensity of 5%, giving k = 1.5*(0.05*0.15)^2 = 8.4375e-5 m^2/s^2; outlet is zeroGradient; floor, top, and all cube faces use kqRWallFunction; left and right are symmetryPlane.

Boundary conditions for epsilon: inlet is fixedValue with a turbulent length scale of 0.07*H = 0.07 m, giving epsilon = Cmu^0.75 * k^1.5 / l where Cmu = 0.09, yielding approximately 3.326e-5 m^2/s^3; outlet is zeroGradient; floor, top, and all cube faces use epsilonWallFunction; left and right are symmetryPlane.

Boundary conditions for nut: inlet is calculated; outlet is calculated; floor, top, and all cube faces use nutkWallFunction; left and right are symmetryPlane.

The turbulence model is specified in constant/turbulenceProperties as RAS with RASModel kEpsilon, turbulence on, and printCoeffs on.

The fvSchemes use Gauss linearUpwind grad(U) for div(phi,U), Gauss upwind for div(phi,k) and div(phi,epsilon), Gauss linear for laplacian schemes, Gauss linear for gradient schemes, and linear for interpolation schemes. The snGrad schemes use corrected.

The fvSolution uses GAMG with GaussSeidel smoother for pressure and smoothSolver with GaussSeidel for U, k, and epsilon. Pressure residual tolerance is 1e-6. Velocity residual tolerance is 1e-5. k and epsilon residual tolerances are 1e-5. The SIMPLE algorithm is used with nNonOrthogonalCorrectors set to 1 and a residual control that stops when p reaches 1e-4 and U reaches 1e-4. Relaxation factors are 0.3 for p and 0.7 for U, k, and epsilon.

The simulation runs for 2000 iterations (endTime 2000, deltaT 1, as simpleFoam uses pseudo-time stepping with iteration count).

The transportProperties file specifies nu as 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
