The simulation is a two-dimensional Rayleigh-Benard convection cell in OpenFOAM using a recent version that supports the momentumTransport file naming.

The domain is a rectangular cavity 9 units wide in x, 1 unit tall in y, and 0.1 units deep in z.
The mesh has 90 cells in x, 10 cells in y, and 1 cell in z.
The front and back patches (z-normal faces) are of type empty to enforce two-dimensional behavior.
The remaining patches are: bottom (y=0), top (y=1), left (x=0), and right (x=9), all treated as solid walls.

The solver is buoyantFoam.
Gravity acts in the negative y direction, set as (0 -9.81 0) in constant/g.
