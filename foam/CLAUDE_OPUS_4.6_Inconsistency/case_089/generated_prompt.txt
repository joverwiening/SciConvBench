The simulation is a 2D mixed convection lid-driven cavity using buoyantPimpleFoam in OpenFOAM.

The cavity is a square with side length 0.1 m, making it 2D by using a single-cell depth in the z-direction with empty boundary conditions on the front and back patches.

The top lid moves in the positive x-direction at 0.1 m/s and is held at a fixed temperature of 350 K.
The bottom wall is stationary with a no-slip condition and is held at a fixed temperature of 300 K.
The left and right walls are stationary with no-slip conditions and are adiabatic (zeroGradient for temperature).
The front and back patches use the empty boundary type for a 2D simulation.

Gravity acts downward at (0, -9.81, 0) m/s^2, specified in constant/g.

The initial internal field pressure is 101325 Pa (1 atm).
The initial internal field temperature is 325 K (average of hot and cold walls).
The initial internal field velocity is (0 0 0) m/s.

The mesh is a uniform structured grid of 50 cells by 50 cells by 1 cell, generated using blockMesh.

No BoussinesqCoeffs block is included anywhere.

The file constant/turbulenceProperties uses simulationType laminar, since the Reynolds number based on lid velocity, cavity length, and air properties is approximately 555, which is in the laminar regime.
