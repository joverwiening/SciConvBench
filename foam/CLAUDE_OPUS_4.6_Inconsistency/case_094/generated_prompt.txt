The simulation models steady-state, compressible flow of natural gas through a straight cylindrical pipe containing a porous baffle.
The solver is rhoPorousSimpleFoam.
The pipe has a length of 1.0 m and a diameter of 0.1 m.
The pipe axis is aligned with the x-direction, running from x = 0 m to x = 1.0 m.
The porous baffle is located at the center of the pipe, occupying the region from x = 0.4 m to x = 0.6 m, giving a baffle thickness of 0.2 m.
The mesh is a structured hexahedral mesh generated with blockMesh.
The mesh resolution is approximately 50,000 cells, with roughly 200 cells along the pipe length, and approximately 16 cells across the radius, using an O-grid or similar structured approach to discretize the circular cross-section.
The inlet boundary at x = 0 m has a total pressure of 2e5 Pa, a total temperature of 300 K, and zero-gradient for velocity or a pressure-inlet type condition consistent with rhoPorousSimpleFoam.
The outlet boundary at x = 1.0 m has a fixed static pressure of 1e5 Pa with zero-gradient for temperature.
The pipe wall is a no-slip adiabatic wall with zero heat flux.
Gas properties are defined in the thermophysicalProperties dictionary.
The thermophysical model uses hePsiThermo with a pureMixture.
The equation of state is perfectGas.
The transport model is sutherland.
The thermodynamics type is hConst.
The specie is defined with a molecular weight of 16.04 kg/kmol, representing methane as a surrogate for natural gas.
The Sutherland transport coefficients are As = 1.67212e-06 and Ts = 170.672, which are standard values for methane.
The specific heat capacity Cp is 2222 J/(kg·K) and Hf is 0.
The turbulence model is k-omega SST (kOmegaSST) using the RAS framework.
Inlet turbulence values are a turbulent intensity of 5% and a turbulent viscosity ratio of 10.
The porous zone is defined in the fvOptions dictionary (or constant/fvOptions) using a codedSource or explicitPorositySource of type DarcyForchheimer.
The porous zone cell set corresponds to the region from x = 0.4 m to x = 0.6 m.
The Darcy coefficient d is (1e7 1e7 1e7) in units of 1/m^2.
The Forchheimer coefficient f is (100 100 100) in units of 1/m.
The coordinate system for the porosity is aligned with the global axes.
The initial internal field conditions are a uniform pressure of 1.5e5 Pa, a uniform temperature of 300 K, and a uniform velocity of (10 0 0) m/s.
The fvSchemes dictionary uses steadyState for time derivatives, Gauss upwind for divergence schemes, Gauss linear corrected for Laplacian schemes, and Gauss linear for gradient schemes.
The fvSolution dictionary uses SIMPLE as the pressure-velocity coupling algorithm.
The under-relaxation factors are 0.3 for pressure and 0.7 for velocity, k, omega, and energy.
The simulation runs for a sufficient number of iterations to reach steady state, starting with 2000 iterations and checking residual convergence.
Convergence criteria are residuals below 1e-5 for all fields.
