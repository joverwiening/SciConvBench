The simulation uses OpenFOAM with the pimpleFoam solver for transient, incompressible flow.
The Reynolds number is 100.
The fluid has a kinematic viscosity nu = 0.01 m^2/s.
The cylinder diameter D is 1 m.
The inlet velocity is uniform U = (1 0 0) m/s, which is consistent with Re = U*D/nu = 100.
The domain is a 2D rectangular channel.
The domain extends from -10D upstream to 30D downstream of the cylinder center in the x-direction (total length 40D = 40 m).
The domain extends from -10D to 10D in the y-direction (total height 20D = 20 m).
The domain is one cell thick in the z-direction (e.g., z = 0 to z = 0.1 m) to enforce the 2D condition via the empty patch type.
The cylinder center is placed at (0, 0.5, 0), offset 0.5D from the geometric centerline in the y-direction to break symmetry and trigger vortex shedding.
The mesh uses an O-grid topology around the cylinder with approximately 200 cells around the cylinder circumference.
The first cell height near the cylinder wall gives a y+ of approximately 1.
The wake region downstream of the cylinder is refined with cells of approximate size 0.05D to 0.1D for at least 15D downstream.
The total mesh cell count is approximately 50,000 to 80,000 cells.
The boundary named "inlet" is at the upstream face with a fixedValue condition for U = (1 0 0) m/s and zeroGradient for p.
The boundary named "outlet" is at the downstream face with zeroGradient for U and fixedValue for p = 0.
The boundary named "topAndBottom" applies to the top and bottom walls with a slip condition for U and zeroGradient for p.
The boundary named "cylinder" is the cylinder surface with a noSlip (fixedValue U = (0 0 0)) condition for U and zeroGradient for p.
The boundary named "frontAndBack" applies to the two z-normal faces with the empty type for both U and p to enforce 2D simulation.
The simulation runs from t = 0 s to t = 200 s.
The time step deltaT is 0.1 s, giving a maximum Courant number well below 1 for this configuration.
The pimpleFoam solver is used with default PIMPLE settings: nOuterCorrectors 2, nCorrectors 2, nNonOrthogonalCorrectors 1.
The turbulence model is set to laminar since Re = 100 is below the turbulent transition threshold.
The convective scheme for U uses Gauss linearUpwind grad(U) for stability and second-order accuracy.
The temporal scheme uses Euler (first-order implicit) for robustness, or backward (second-order) if stability permits.
Results are written every 1 second of simulation time (writeInterval = 1 s, writeControl = adjustableRunTime).
Function objects are configured to monitor lift and drag coefficients on the cylinder at every time step.
The lift coefficient history is used to extract the shedding frequency f via FFT or peak counting after the flow reaches a statistically periodic state (typically after t = 50 to 100 s).
The Strouhal number is calculated as St = f * D / U, with an expected value of approximately 0.164 to 0.17 for Re = 100.
The goal is to observe the von Kármán vortex street in the wake and validate the computed Strouhal number against published data.
