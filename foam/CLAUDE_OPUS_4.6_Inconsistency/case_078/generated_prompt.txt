The simulation uses the steady-state solver buoyantSimpleFoam to model airflow and natural convection in a ventilated room with a heater.

The room is a rectangular box with dimensions 4 m (length, x-direction) by 3 m (width, y-direction) by 3 m (height, z-direction).

The inlet is a rectangular patch of size 0.4 m wide by 0.2 m tall, centered on the lower portion of the wall at x = 0, with its center at y = 1.5 m and z = 0.3 m. Air enters in the positive x-direction.

The outlet is a rectangular patch of size 0.4 m wide by 0.2 m tall, centered on the upper portion of the opposite wall at x = 4 m, with its center at y = 1.5 m and z = 2.7 m.

The heater is a rectangular wall patch of size 1.0 m (x-direction) by 0.5 m (y-direction), located on the floor (z = 0), centered at x = 2.0 m and y = 1.5 m.

All remaining walls (floor excluding heater, ceiling, and four side walls) are no-slip wall patches.

The inlet velocity is 0.1 m/s in the positive x-direction, representing a low-speed mechanical ventilation where buoyancy-driven flow from the heater dominates.

The turbulence model is laminar (no turbulence model), as the flow is low-speed and buoyancy-driven at moderate Rayleigh number.

In constant/thermophysicalProperties, the thermoType is heRhoThermo. The mixture is pureMixture. The transport model is const. The thermodynamics model is hConst. The equation of state is Boussinesq. The specie model is specie. The energy model is sensibleEnthalpy.

Under the mixture subdictionary, the Boussinesq equation of state coefficients are rho0 1.2, T0 293, and beta 0.00341.

The transport coefficients are mu 1.8e-5 and Pr 0.71.

The thermodynamics coefficients are Cp 1005 and Hf 0.

The specie coefficients are molWeight 28.9.

The heater wall patch has a fixedValue temperature boundary condition set to 320 K.

The inlet patch has a fixedValue temperature of 293 K.

The inlet patch has a fixedValue velocity of (0.1 0 0) m/s.

The outlet patch has a fixedValue pressure of 0 (gauge pressure, p_rgh = 0) using a fixedValue boundary condition on p_rgh.

The inlet patch has a zeroGradient boundary condition on p_rgh.

The outlet patch has an inletOutlet boundary condition on velocity with inletValue (0 0 0) and on temperature with inletValue 293 K.

All wall patches (heater, floor, ceiling, side walls) have a fixedFluxPressure boundary condition on p_rgh.

All wall patches have a no-slip (fixedValue uniform (0 0 0)) boundary condition on velocity.

All non-heater wall patches have a zeroGradient boundary condition on temperature (adiabatic walls).

The internal field is initialized with uniform velocity (0 0 0) m/s, uniform temperature 293 K, and uniform p_rgh 0.

The mesh is generated using blockMesh with approximately 40 cells in x, 30 cells in y, and 30 cells in z, giving roughly 36000 cells. Grading may be applied near walls if needed, but uniform spacing is acceptable as a starting point.

In system/fvSchemes, use steady-state ddtSchemes (steadyState), Gauss linearUpwind for div schemes on velocity and temperature, Gauss linear for laplacian and gradient schemes, and linear for interpolation schemes.

In system/fvSolution, use SIMPLE algorithm. Use the smoother GaussSeidel for velocity and temperature, and GAMG for pressure. Set relaxation factors of 0.3 for p_rgh, 0.7 for U, and 0.7 for h (enthalpy). Set residual convergence criteria of 1e-6 for p_rgh and 1e-6 for all other fields.

The simulation runs for 2000 iterations or until convergence, whichever comes first.

The gravitational acceleration is set in constant/g as (0 0 -9.81) m/s^2.
