The simulation is a turbulent buoyant cavity simulation.
The cavity is square with dimensions 0.10 m by 0.10 m.
The hot and cold walls have a temperature difference (DeltaT) of 10 K.
The fluid is air, with a Prandtl number (Pr) of 0.71.
The operating pressure is standard atmospheric pressure (101325 Pa).
The Rayleigh number is set to 1e6.
The k-epsilon turbulence model is used.
