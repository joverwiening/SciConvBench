Simulate steady-state, incompressible, laminar flow of water through a 2D channel.
The full channel domain is 0.5 m long, 0.1 m high, and has a width of 1 cell.
The porous zone is a rectangular region within the channel, spanning from x=0.2 m to x=0.3 m in length, and occupies the full channel height of 0.1 m.
The porous zone uses a 'DarcyForchheimer' model with coefficients d = (1e8 1e8 1e10) and f = (0 0 0).
The inlet is a velocity inlet boundary condition located on the left patch of the domain, with a fixed value of uniform (1 0 0) m/s.
The outlet is a pressure outlet boundary condition located on the right patch of the domain, with a fixed value of uniform 0 Pa.
The top and bottom walls of the channel have a noSlip condition for velocity (U) and a zeroGradient condition for pressure (p).
