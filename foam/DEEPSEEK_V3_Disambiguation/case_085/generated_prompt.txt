The furnace is a 2D rectangular domain 2 m high and 0.5 m wide.
The model simulates pulverized coal combustion with a primary goal of predicting the steady-state temperature and species concentration fields.
Inject a high-volatile bituminous coal with a defined ultimate analysis.
Use a Rosin-Rammler particle size distribution with a mean diameter of 100 microns and a spread parameter (n) of 3.5.
The coal injection mass flow rate is 0.1 kg/s.
The coal particles are injected at an initial temperature of 400 K.
The primary air and coal mixture is injected with a velocity vector of (0, 20, 0) m/s.
The primary air/coal inlet stream temperature is 400 K.
The hot air is dry air with a standard atmospheric composition of 21% O2 and 79% N2 by volume.
The furnace walls are at a fixed temperature of 1200 K.
The model must include radiation heat transfer.
The simulation should run until a steady-state solution is achieved.
