Simulate a compressible, high-speed, turbulent lid-driven cavity flow.
Use air as the working fluid.
The cavity is a square with a side length of 1 m.
The lid moves at a constant velocity of 100 m/s.
The lid temperature is fixed at 300 K.
All other walls (cavity walls and floor) are fixed with a no-slip condition and a temperature of 300 K.
The initial pressure everywhere in the cavity is 101325 Pa.
The initial temperature everywhere in the cavity is 300 K.
Assume the speed of sound for air at 300 K is approximately 347 m/s, resulting in a lid Mach number of approximately 0.29.
Assume the kinematic viscosity of air at the initial conditions is 1.5e-5 m²/s, resulting in a Reynolds number of approximately 6.67e6 based on the lid velocity and cavity length.
Use the RAS kOmegaSST turbulence model.
