Simulate a 2D flow past a circular cylinder.
The computational domain is a rectangular prism.
The domain extends from x = -5 m to x = 5 m in the streamwise direction.
The domain extends from y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m in the cross-stream direction.
The domain extends from z = -1 m to z = 1 m, representing a thickness of 2 m.
The cylinder has a diameter of 2 m.
The cylinder is centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).
Use the pimpleFoam solver for incompressible unsteady flow.
Set the startTime to 0 s.
Set the endTime to 0.5 s.
Set the time step deltaT to 0.0025 s.
Write result fields every 0.05 s.
Initialize the velocity field U to (0 0 0) m/s.
Initialize the pressure field p to 0 Pa.
