Simulate a 2D flow past a circular cylinder.
The domain is a rectangular box extending from x = -5 m to x = 5 m in the streamwise direction, y = -1.5 m to y = 2.5 m in the cross-stream direction, and z = -1 m to z = 1 m.
The cylinder has a diameter of 2 m and is centered at the origin (x = 0 m, y = 0 m).
Set the simulation start time to 0 s.
Set the simulation end time to 0.5 s.
Set the simulation time step (deltaT) to 0.0025 s.
Write simulation results every 0.05 s.
Initialize the flow field with a quiescent fluid: velocity U = (0 0 0) m/s and pressure p = 0.
Apply a fixed velocity inflow boundary condition at the x = -5 m boundary: U = (1 0 0) m/s.
Apply a zeroGradient boundary condition for velocity at the x = 5 m (outflow) boundary.
Apply a fixed pressure boundary condition at the x = 5 m (outflow) boundary: p = 0.
Apply a zeroGradient boundary condition for pressure at the x = -5 m (inflow) boundary.
Apply a noSlip boundary condition for velocity at the top (y = 2.5 m) and bottom (y = -1.5 m) boundaries.
Apply a zeroGradient boundary condition for pressure at the top (y = 2.5 m) and bottom (y = -1.5 m) boundaries.
Apply a noSlip boundary condition for velocity at the cylinder surface.
Apply a zeroGradient boundary condition for pressure at the cylinder surface.
Apply an empty boundary condition for velocity at the front (z = 1 m) and back (z = -1 m) boundaries.
Apply an empty boundary condition for pressure at the front (z = 1 m) and back (z = -1 m) boundaries.
