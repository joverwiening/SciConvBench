Set up a simulation for supersonic flow over a forward-facing step.
The inlet Mach number is 3.
The flow is inviscid.
The simulation domain is 3 m long in the x-direction and 1 m tall in the y-direction.
A forward-facing step of height 0.2 m is located at x = 0.6 m.
The channel height upstream of the step is 1 m.
The channel height downstream of the step is 0.8 m.
The inlet uses freestream static conditions: static pressure p = 101325 Pa, static temperature T = 300 K, and velocity U = (1020.6, 0, 0) m/s.
The outlet uses a waveTransmissive boundary condition for pressure with pInf = 101325 Pa, and zeroGradient (or waveTransmissive) conditions for velocity and temperature.
The top and bottom walls of the channel use a slip boundary condition.
