The simulation models Rayleigh-Benard convection in a 2D rectangular domain.
The domain width is 2 meters and the height is 1 meter, giving an aspect ratio of 2:1.
The Rayleigh number is set to 2500.
The Prandtl number is 0.71.
The fluid is initially at rest (zero velocity everywhere).
The initial temperature field is T(x,y) = (300 - y) + 0.1 * sin(pi * x) Kelvin.
The bottom boundary (y=0) is a no-slip wall with a fixed temperature of 300 K.
The top boundary (y=1) is a no-slip wall with a fixed temperature of 299 K.
The left and right boundaries (x=0, x=2) are periodic (cyclic) for all fields, including velocity and temperature.
