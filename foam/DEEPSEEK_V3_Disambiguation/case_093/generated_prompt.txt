The simulation models a turbulent hot air plume rising from a floor heat source in a large, open room.
The fluid is air.
The heat source is a central 0.2m wide section of the floor, with a constant temperature of 400 K.
The surrounding air in the room has a uniform initial temperature of 300 K.
The simulation domain is a room 2m wide, 2m deep, and 4m high.
The simulation runs for a physical time of 100 seconds.
Use the RANS k-epsilon turbulence model.
The initial velocity field for the air is stationary, U = (0, 0, 0) m/s.
The initial temperature field of the air in the room is uniformly 300 K.
