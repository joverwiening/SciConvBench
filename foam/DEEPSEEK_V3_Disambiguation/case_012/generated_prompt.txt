The simulation is an incompressible lid-driven cavity flow.
The cavity is a square with dimensions normalized to 1 unit in x and y.
The cavity depth in the z-direction is 0.1 units.
The computational grid is 20 cells in the x-direction.
The computational grid is 20 cells in the y-direction.
The computational grid is 1 cell in the z-direction.
The simulation runs from time 0 to time 10.
The simulation uses a time step of 0.005.
Results are output every 100 time steps.
The k-epsilon turbulence model is used.
The turbulence kinetic energy field k is initialized uniformly to 1.5e-4.
The k boundary condition on all walls is kqRWallFunction.
The turbulence dissipation rate field epsilon is initialized uniformly to 1.4e-4.
The epsilon boundary condition on all walls is epsilonWallFunction.
The turbulent viscosity field nut is initialized uniformly to 0.
The nut boundary condition on all walls is nutkWallFunction.
The pisoFoam solver is used.
The velocity field is initialized to zero.
The convection scheme for the momentum equation is div(phi,U) Gauss limitedLinearV 1.
The convection scheme for the k equation is div(phi,k) Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The convection scheme for the epsilon equation is div(phi,epsilon) Gauss limitedLinear 1.
The viscous term uses div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear.
The top wall is named 'movingWall'.
The 'movingWall' has a fixed uniform velocity of (1 0 0) m/s.
The bottom and side walls are named 'fixedWalls'.
The 'fixedWalls' have a no-slip (fixedValue (0 0 0)) velocity condition.
The front and back patches are designated as 'empty'.
The pressure boundary condition on the 'movingWall' is a zero-gradient (zeroGradient) condition.
The pressure boundary condition on the 'fixedWalls' is a zero-gradient (zeroGradient) condition.
The pressure boundary condition on the 'empty' patches is 'empty'.
