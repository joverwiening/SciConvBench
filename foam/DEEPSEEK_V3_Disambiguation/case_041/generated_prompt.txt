Simulate a 2D buoyant cavity.
The fluid is air.
The cavity is a square with height and width both equal to 1 meter.
The left wall is hot with a fixed temperature of 303 K.
The right wall is cold with a fixed temperature of 293 K.
The top and bottom walls are insulated.
The initial temperature of the air inside the cavity is uniform at 293 K.
The initial velocity field is zero everywhere (U = (0 0 0) m/s).
All walls have a no-slip boundary condition for velocity.
The boundary condition for pressure (p_rgh) is fixedFluxPressure on all walls.
