The simulation is a high-fidelity LES of incompressible flow over periodic hills.
The Reynolds number based on hill height is Re_H = 10595.
The hill height is H = 0.028 m.
The hill shape is the standard ERCOFTAC periodic-hill profile for the bottom surface.
The top wall of the channel is flat.
The streamwise domain length is Lx = 9H = 0.252 m with periodic boundary conditions.
The spanwise domain length is Lz = 4.5H = 0.126 m with periodic boundary conditions.
The channel height is Ly = 3.036H ≈ 0.0850 m.
The fluid kinematic viscosity is nu = 2.64e-6 m²/s.
The fluid density is assumed to be that of air at standard conditions, 1.225 kg/m³.
The bulk velocity is Ub = 1.0 m/s.
