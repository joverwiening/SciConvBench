Simulate a 2D lid-driven cavity flow.
The cavity is square with a side length of 0.1 meters.
The fluid is incompressible.
The top wall moves with a uniform velocity of (1, 0, 0) m/s.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 0.001 m²/s.
The Reynolds number, based on cavity length and wall velocity, is 100.
The simulation is isothermal, with no thermal effects considered.
