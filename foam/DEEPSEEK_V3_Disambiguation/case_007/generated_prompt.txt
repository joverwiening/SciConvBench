The simulation is a 2D incompressible lid-driven cavity flow.
The computational domain is a rectangular grid of 20 cells in the x-direction, 20 cells in the y-direction, and 1 cell in the z-direction.
The top boundary is named 'movingWall' and has a uniform velocity of 1 m/s in the positive x-direction.
The other three boundaries (bottom, left, right) are named 'fixedWalls' and have a no-slip condition (velocity = 0 m/s).
The front and back boundaries are named 'empty' and use the 'empty' boundary type.
The simulation runs from time t=0 to t=10 seconds.
The simulation uses a constant time step of 0.005 seconds.
Results are written to output every 100 time steps.
The fluid kinematic viscosity (`nu`) is constant and set to 1e-05 m^2/s.
The turbulence model is the standard k-epsilon model.
The turbulent kinetic energy field `k` is initialized uniformly to 1.5e-4 m^2/s^2.
The `k` field uses the `kqRWallFunction` boundary condition on all walls, including the 'empty' patches.
The turbulent dissipation rate field `epsilon` is initialized uniformly to 1.4e-4 m^2/s^3.
The `epsilon` field uses the `epsilonWallFunction` boundary condition on all walls, including the 'empty' patches.
The turbulent viscosity field `nut` is initialized uniformly to 0 m^2/s.
The `nut` field uses the `nutkWallFunction` boundary condition on all walls, including the 'empty' patches.
The solver is `pisoFoam`.
The velocity field `U` is initialized uniformly to (0 0 0) m/s at time t=0.
The convection scheme for the momentum equation is `div(phi,U) Gauss limitedLinearV 1`.
The convection scheme for the turbulent kinetic energy equation is `div(phi,k) Gauss limitedLinear 1`.
The convection scheme for the turbulent dissipation rate equation is `div(phi,epsilon) Gauss limitedLinear 1`.
The viscous term in the momentum equation uses the scheme `div((nuEff*dev2(T(grad(U))))) Gauss linear`.
