The domain is a rectangular box with x from 0 to 0.02 m, y from -0.01 to 0.01 m, and z from -0.01 to 0.01 m.
The left boundary at x=0 is named "fuel".
The right boundary at x=0.02 is named "air".
The boundaries at y = -0.01 m and y = 0.01 m are named "outlet".
The boundaries at z = -0.01 m and z = 0.01 m are empty.
At the fuel inlet, inject methane (CH4) at a temperature of 293 K.
At the air inlet, supply oxidizer as air with a temperature of 293 K, Y_O2 = 0.23, and Y_N2 = 0.77.
Initialize the entire domain with a temperature of 2000 K at time 0.
Initialize the domain composition with a mass fraction of Y_N2 = 1.0.
Set the velocity at the fuel boundary to (0.1, 0, 0) m/s.
Set the velocity at the air boundary to (-0.1, 0, 0) m/s.
Use the reactingFoam solver.
Use the EulerImplicit chemistry type.
Use the Rosenbrock43 ODE solver.
Use a laminar combustion model.
Use laminar momentum transport.
Use a compressible finite-rate chemistry formulation.
Use the hePsiThermo thermodynamics package.
Use the coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture for mixture properties.
Use the Sutherland model for transport properties.
Use the JANAF model for thermodynamics.
