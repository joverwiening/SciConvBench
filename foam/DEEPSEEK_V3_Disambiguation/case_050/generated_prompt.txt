The simulation domain is a 1 meter by 1 meter square.
The mesh is a uniform 40x40 cell grid.
The solver is pimpleFoam for transient, incompressible flow.
The kinematic viscosity (nu) is 0.0001 m^2/s.
The initial velocity field 'U' is set uniformly to (0 0 0) m/s.
The initial kinematic pressure field 'p' is set uniformly to 0 m^2/s^2.
The top wall boundary moves with a fixed velocity of (1 0 0) m/s.
The pressure boundary condition on the top wall is fixedFluxPressure with a value of 0.
The bottom wall has a no-slip condition for velocity.
The pressure boundary condition on the bottom wall is fixedFluxPressure with a value of 0.
The left wall has a no-slip condition for velocity.
The pressure boundary condition on the left wall is fixedFluxPressure with a value of 0.
The right wall has a no-slip condition for velocity.
The pressure boundary condition on the right wall is fixedFluxPressure with a value of 0.
The time step is adjustable, controlled by a maximum Courant number (maxCo) of 1.0.
The maximum allowable time step (maxDeltaT) is 0.05 seconds.
The simulation runs until physical time t = 5 seconds.
