The simulation models natural convection of air in a square cavity.
The cavity has a side length of 0.1 meters.
The left wall is a fixed hot wall at 313.15 K.
The right wall is a fixed cold wall at 293.15 K.
The top and bottom walls are adiabatic.
The entire fluid domain is initialized at a uniform temperature of 303.15 K.
The initial pressure (p_rgh) field is set to a uniform value of 0.
The gravitational acceleration vector is (0, -9.81, 0) m/s².
A buoyant solver (e.g., buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam) is used.
The Rayleigh number is set to 1e6, a standard value for moderate natural convection.
The simulation uses air properties (kinematic viscosity, thermal diffusivity, thermal expansion coefficient) consistent with the specified temperatures and the chosen Rayleigh number.
