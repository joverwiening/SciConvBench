The task is to model sloshing in a rectangular tank using interFoam.
The tank dimensions are 1.0 m in the x-direction, 0.6 m in the y-direction, and 0.1 m in the z-direction.
The tank is half-filled with water, with the rest being air.
The initial water level is tilted in the x-direction.
The water depth varies linearly from 0.25 m at x=0 to 0.35 m at x=1.0 m, relative to the bottom at y=0.
The initial velocity field for both water and air is zero (U = (0 0 0)).
The acceleration due to gravity is g = (0 -9.81 0) m/s².
The simulation is transient and gravity-driven.
