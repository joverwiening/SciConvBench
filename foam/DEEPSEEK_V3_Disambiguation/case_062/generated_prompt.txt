Simulate the flow of water over a circular cylinder.
The cylinder has a diameter D of 1 meter.
The Reynolds number for the simulation is 3900.
The kinematic viscosity of the water is 1e-6 m²/s.
The inflow velocity is U = (3.9, 0, 0) m/s.
The simulation domain extends from -10D to 30D in the streamwise direction.
The simulation domain extends from -10D to 10D in the cross-stream direction.
The total streamwise length is 40 meters.
The total cross-stream height is 20 meters.
The simulation must capture vortex shedding phenomena.
