Model a single air bubble rising in a column of quiescent water using a 2D VOF solver.
The bubble is circular with a diameter of 0.01 m.
The water is quiescent, with a density of 1000 kg/m³.
Assume the water temperature is 20°C, giving a dynamic viscosity of 0.001 Pa·s.
Assume the air has a dynamic viscosity of 1.8e-05 Pa·s.
The surface tension coefficient between air and water is 0.072 N/m.
The domain is a vertical column.
The column width must be sufficiently large to avoid wall effects on the bubble.
The column height must allow the bubble to reach a terminal velocity.
Use a no-slip boundary condition for the column walls.
Initialize the bubble at rest at the bottom center of the column.
Use a gravitational acceleration of 9.81 m/s² acting downwards.
The simulation must track the bubble's rise velocity and shape deformation over time.
Report the terminal velocity and the final, deformed shape of the bubble.
