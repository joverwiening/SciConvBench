Q: What boundary condition should be applied at the outlet (right) boundary?

Q: What condition should be applied at the bottom (symmetryPlane) boundary?
A: Apply a symmetryPlane condition at the bottom boundary (zero normal gradient for all scalar fields and zero normal component of velocity, with tangential velocity gradient also zero).
