Perform a conjugate heat transfer simulation of a silicon chip mounted inside an air‑cooled housing using the OpenFOAM solver chtMultiRegionSimpleFoam.  
Create two regions: a solid region named “solid” representing the silicon chip and a fluid region named “air” representing the surrounding air.  

Define the geometry:  
- Silicon chip dimensions: 10 mm × 10 mm × 0.5 mm (length × width × thickness).  
- Air domain: a rectangular enclosure that fully surrounds the chip with a clearance of at least 5 mm on all sides.  
- Housing outer surface is named “outerCasing”.  

Material properties:  
- Silicon (solid): thermal conductivity k = 130 W/(m·K), density ρ = 2330 kg/m³, specific heat Cp = 700 J/(kg·K).  
- Air (fluid): use the standard thermophysical model “air” (ideal gas) with constant properties (k ≈ 0.026 W/(m·K), ρ ≈ 1.225 kg/m³, Cp ≈ 1005 J/(kg·K)).  

Boundary conditions:  
- Air inlet (patch “inlet”): fixedValue temperature T = 293 K and fixedValue velocity U = (1 0 0) m/s (flow entering the enclosure).  
- Air outlet (patch “outlet”): zeroGradient for temperature and pressure, and fixedValue pressure p = 0 Pa (reference).  
- Outer housing surface “outerCasing”: fixedValue temperature T = 293 K (as specified).  
- All other solid walls: zeroGradient for temperature (adiabatic).  

fvOptions for heat generation in the silicon chip:  
```
solidHeatSource
{
    type            scalarSemiImplicitSource;
    active          true;
    selectionMode   all;
    volumeMode      absolute;
    fields          (T);
    magnitude       uniform 2e8;   // W/m³, corresponds to 10 W total for the chip volume
}
```  

Initial conditions (0 folder):  
- solid/T: uniform 293 K.  
- air/T: uniform 293 K.  
- air/U: uniform (0 0 0) m/s.  
- air/p: uniform 0 Pa.  

Mesh generation:  
- Use blockMesh to create a hexahedral mesh for both regions, ensuring at least 20 cells across the chip thickness and adequate refinement near the chip–air interface.  
- Convert the mesh to a multi‑region mesh with createMultiRegionMesh, assigning the solid cells to the “solid” region and the remaining cells to the “air” region.  

Solver settings (system/fvSolution):  
- Use the default SIMPLE algorithm settings for chtMultiRegionSimpleFoam.  
- Set relaxation factors: solid: 0.3 for temperature, air: 0.3 for temperature and 0.5 for velocity.  

Run the simulation: execute `chtMultiRegionSimpleFoam` and monitor convergence of residuals (target < 1e‑5) and the total heat flux through the outerCasing.  

Post‑processing:  
- Visualize temperature fields in both regions with paraFoam.  
- Extract the temperature distribution on the chip surface and the outlet air temperature profile.  
- Compute the average heat transfer coefficient on the chip–air interface using the surface heat flux and temperature difference.
