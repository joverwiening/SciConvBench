Perform a 2D steady-state heat conduction simulation of a square solid plate using laplacianFoam.  
The computational domain is a 1 m × 1 m square that is one cell thick in the third dimension.  
Create the mesh with blockMesh, defining four side patches named left, right, top, and bottom, and a pair of patches named frontAndBack that represent the front and back faces of the plate.  
Set the frontAndBack patches to type wall in blockMeshDict.  
In the initial condition file 0/T, assign a fixedValue boundary condition of 400 K to the left patch, a fixedValue of 300 K to the right patch, and zeroGradient to the top and bottom patches.  
Apply a zeroGradient boundary condition to both frontAndBack patches to model insulation on the front and back surfaces.  
Specify a constant thermal conductivity of 10 W/(m·K) for the solid material in the transportProperties dictionary.  
Use laplacianFoam in steady-state mode with default solver settings and convergence tolerances.  
Run the simulation until the residuals fall below the default convergence criteria, then output the final temperature field.
