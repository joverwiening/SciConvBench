Q: What pressure boundary conditions should be applied to the inlet and outlet patches in the 0/p file?
A: For a steady‑state simpleFoam case, a common and reliable choice is:

* **inlet** – `type zeroGradient;`  
* **outlet** – `type fixedValue;` with `value uniform 0;`

If you need a different reference pressure, adjust the outlet value accordingly. If the complete requirement later specifies other values, use those instead.

Q: What fluid density and kinematic viscosity should be used for the simulation?
A: Make a reasonable assumption based on best practices. For a typical laminar, incompressible case you could use:

- **Density (ρ):** 1 kg m⁻³ (or 1.225 kg m⁻³ if you prefer air)  
- **Kinematic viscosity (ν):** 1 × 10⁻⁶ m² s⁻¹ (water at ~20 °C) – or ≈1.5 × 10⁻⁵ m² s⁻¹ for air.
