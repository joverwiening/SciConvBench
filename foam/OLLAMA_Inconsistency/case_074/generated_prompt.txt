Model a steady-state buoyancy-driven flow in a vertical heated pipe using the buoyantSimpleFoam solver.  
The pipe is 2 m long, aligned with the Z‑axis, and has a diameter of 0.1 m.  
Gravity is enabled with the vector g (0 0 -9.81) m/s².  
Air is modeled as a perfect gas with standard thermophysical properties.  
At the inlet (z = 0) the velocity uses the pressureInletOutletVelocity condition and the temperature is fixed at 300 K; the pressure at the inlet is set to a reference atmospheric value of 101325 Pa (zero‑gauge pressure).  
The outlet (z = 2 m) uses zeroGradient for velocity and temperature, and the pressure is fixed to 0 Pa gauge (reference pressure).  
The pipe wall has a noSlip condition for velocity and a fixed temperature of 350 K.  
All other OpenFOAM settings (turbulence model, discretisation schemes, solver tolerances) follow the default recommendations for buoyantSimpleFoam.
