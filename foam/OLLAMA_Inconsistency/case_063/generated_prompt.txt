Use a 2‑D OpenFOAM case with a mesh extruded one cell in the z‑direction and the frontAndBack patches set to empty.  
Set the cylinder diameter D to 1 m (radius 0.5 m).  
Define the channel height (distance between top and bottom walls) as 10 D = 10 m and the total channel length as 30 D = 30 m, with the cylinder centre located 10 D = 10 m downstream from the inlet and centred vertically.  
Generate a structured mesh that is refined near the cylinder surface to a cell size of about 0.02 D (0.02 m) and gradually coarsens toward the far field.  
Assign the kinematic viscosity nu = 1e‑3 m²/s, giving a Reynolds number Re = U D/nu = 100 for the inlet velocity.  
Set the inlet velocity field (0/U) to fixedValue uniform (0.1 0 0) m/s, the outlet pressure (0/p) to fixedValue uniform 0, and apply zeroGradient for pressure at the inlet, outlet, cylinder, top and bottom patches as described.  
Apply a noSlip condition for velocity on the cylinder surface, slip conditions on the top and bottom walls, and empty conditions on the frontAndBack patches for both velocity and pressure.  
In system/controlDict set application to solidDisplacementFoam.  
Use a laminar solver configuration (no turbulence model) with a time step of 0.001 s, total simulation time of 10 s, and write interval of 0.1 s.  
Run the simulation to capture the transient development of the von Kármán vortex street downstream of the cylinder.
