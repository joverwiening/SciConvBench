Set up a laminar counter‑flow diffusion flame using reactingFoam in a 2‑D opposed‑jet configuration with the domain extending from x = 0 m to x = 0.02 m, y = –0.01 m to y = 0.01 m, and z = –0.01 m to z = 0.01 m.  
Create a structured mesh with 100 cells in the x‑direction, 40 cells in the y‑direction, and a single cell in the z‑direction; assign the frontAndBack patches as empty.  
Define four physical patches: left fuel inlet at x = 0, right air inlet at x = 0.02, top outlet at y = 0.01, and bottom outlet at y = –0.01.  
Apply a fixedValue boundary condition for velocity at the left fuel inlet with a magnitude of +0.1 m s⁻¹ in the +x direction.  
Apply a fixedValue boundary condition for velocity at the right air inlet with a magnitude of –0.1 m s⁻¹ in the –x direction.  
Set the fuel inlet composition to pure methane (mass fraction Y_CH4 = 1) and temperature to 293 K.  
Set the air inlet composition to O₂/N₂ with mass fraction Y_O2 = 0.23 and the remainder N₂, and temperature to 293 K.  
Assign zeroGradient boundary conditions for velocity, temperature, species mass fractions, and all turbulence variables at the top and bottom outlet patches, and use a pressureOutlet condition for the pressure field at those outlets.  
Apply empty boundary conditions on the frontAndBack patches.  
Initialize the entire domain with temperature T = 2000 K, pressure p = 101 325 Pa, and zero velocity.  
Use laminar flow and momentum transport models.  
Select the hePsiThermo thermodynamic model with coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture for transport, Sutherland viscosity, and JANAF thermodynamic data.  
Configure chemistry with the EulerImplicit scheme and the Rosenbrock43 ODE solver.  
Set the time step Δt = 1 × 10⁻⁴ s, run the simulation to endTime = 0.5 s, and write results every 0.05 s.
