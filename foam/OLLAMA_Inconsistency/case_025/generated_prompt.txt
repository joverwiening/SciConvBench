Simulate a three‑dimensional, incompressible, turbulent flow of air (ρ = 1.225 kg m⁻³, μ = 1.8 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s) around a cube mounted on the floor of a rectangular channel using `simpleFoam` with the standard RANS `kEpsilon` turbulence model.  

The channel dimensions are 10 m long (x‑direction), 2 m high (z‑direction), and 2 m wide (y‑direction). The cube has a side length of 0.5 m, its base sits on the floor (z = 0), and its centre is aligned with the channel centreline (y = 1 m, x = 5 m).  

Use a fully three‑dimensional mesh with at least two cells in the spanwise (y) direction. Generate the mesh with `blockMesh` as follows:  

* Base cell size ≈ 0.05 m.  
* Refine the region within 0.2 m of the cube surfaces to a cell size of ≈ 0.01 m (use `refineMesh` or `snappyHexMesh`).  
* The total cell count should be on the order of 2 × 10⁶ cells.

        // faces for left patch
    );

    );

Boundary conditions (`0/U`, `0/p`, `0/k`, `0/epsilon`):  

* `inlet` – fixedValue velocity of (5 0 0) m s⁻¹; turbulence intensity 5 % and turbulent length scale 0.07 m.  
* `outlet` – fixedValue pressure of 0 Pa; zeroGradient for velocity, k and epsilon.  
* `top`, `floor`, `cube` – `noSlip` (fixedValue (0 0 0)) for velocity; appropriate wall functions for k and epsilon (standard `kEpsilonWallFunction`).  
* `left` and `right` – `empty` (symmetry) for all fields.  

`system/controlDict`: steady‑state run, maxCourant 20, write interval every 1000 iterations, endTime 10000 (or until residuals fall below 1 × 10⁻⁶).  

`system/fvSchemes` and `system/fvSolution`: use the default `simpleFoam` settings, with under‑relaxation factors 0.3 for pressure and 0.7 for velocity, k, and epsilon.  

Run the case with:  

```
blockMesh
simpleFoam > log.simpleFoam
```

Post‑process velocity, pressure, and turbulent quantities to verify that the flow re‑attaches downstream of the cube and that the symmetry side boundaries correctly emulate an infinitely wide channel.
