Configure a 2D transient incompressible laminar simulation of flow past a cylinder using the pimpleFoam solver.  
Create a mesh that is one cell thick in the spanwise direction and define the “frontAndBack” patches as empty (or symmetryPlane) to represent the 2‑D nature of the case.  
Set the cylinder diameter to 0.1 m and place the cylinder at the centre of the domain.  
Define the computational domain extents as 5 D upstream of the cylinder, 15 D downstream, and 10 D in the vertical direction.  
Specify a uniform inlet velocity of 1 m s⁻¹ in the streamwise (x) direction in the file 0/U with a fixedValue condition.  
Apply a zeroGradient condition for velocity at the outlet patch.  
Set the cylinder wall patch to a noSlip condition (type noSlip).  
Assign an empty (or symmetryPlane) condition to the frontAndBack patches in both the boundary file and the 0/U file.  
Use a kinematic viscosity of 1 × 10⁻³ m² s⁻¹ (dynamic viscosity μ = 1 × 10⁻³ kg m⁻¹ s⁻¹ for ρ = 1 kg m⁻³) to obtain a Reynolds number of approximately 100, ensuring laminar flow.  
In the constant/transportProperties file, set nu to 1e-3.  
Configure the controlDict to run from startTime 0 to endTime 10 s with an initial time step (deltaT) of 0.001 s, enable adjustTimeStep, and limit the maximum Courant number to 0.5.  
Write results every 0.1 s (writeInterval 0.1).  
Use default laminar fvSchemes and fvSolution settings appropriate for pimpleFoam (e.g., steady‑state schemes for gradient, divergence, and Laplacian; SIMPLE/PIMPLE algorithm with relaxation factors of 0.7 for pressure and 0.3 for velocity).  
After the simulation, post‑process the velocity and pressure fields to examine the wake development and verify that the flow remains laminar throughout the run.
