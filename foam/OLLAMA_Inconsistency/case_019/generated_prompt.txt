Set up a 2-D opposed-jet laminar counter-flow diffusion flame using the reactingFoam solver.  
Create a rectangular domain spanning x = 0 to 0.02 m, y = –0.01 to 0.01 m, and z = –0.01 to 0.01 m.  
Discretize the domain with 100 cells in the x‑direction, 40 cells in the y‑direction, and 1 cell in the z‑direction.  
Name the left boundary (x = 0) “fuel”, the right boundary (x = 0.02) “air”, the top and bottom boundaries “outlet”, and set the frontAndBack faces as empty.  
At the fuel inlet, impose a temperature of 293 K, a velocity of 0.1 m s⁻¹ in the +x direction, and a methane mass fraction Y_CH4 = 1.0 (pure CH₄).  
At the air inlet, impose a temperature of 293 K, a velocity of –0.1 m s⁻¹ in the –x direction, an oxygen mass fraction Y_O2 = 0.23, and nitrogen making up the balance (Y_N2 = 0.77).  
Initialize the entire domain at time 0 with a temperature field of 2000 K.  
Use laminar momentum transport and incompressible finite‑rate chemistry.  
Select the heRhoThermo thermodynamic model, the coefficientWilkeMultiComponentMixture for the mixture, the Sutherland model for transport, and the JANAF database for thermodynamic properties.  
Set the chemistry type to EulerImplicit and the ODE solver to Rosenbrock43.  
Run the simulation with a timestep of 1 × 10⁻⁴ s, an end time of 0.5 s, and write results every 0.05 s.
