Set up a 2D, steady-state, incompressible, turbulent simulation of airflow over a NACA0012 airfoil with a chord length of 1 m at a 5‑degree angle of attack using the simpleFoam solver and the k‑omega SST turbulence model.  
Create a C‑grid computational domain that is one cell thick in the z‑direction; the domain extends at least 10 chord lengths upstream, 20 chord lengths downstream, and 10 chord lengths above and below the airfoil.  
Place the mesh files in `constant/polyMesh`.  
Define the following boundary patches in `constant/polyMesh/boundary`:

- `inlet`: type `patch`.  
  - Velocity `U`: `fixedValue` uniform (10 0 0) m s⁻¹.  
  - Turbulent kinetic energy `k`: `fixedValue` calculated from turbulence intensity I = 0.01 using \(k = 1.5 (U_{\infty} I)^2\).  
  - Specific dissipation rate `omega`: `fixedValue` calculated from \( \omega = \frac{\sqrt{k}}{C_{\mu}^{0.25} L}\) with \(C_{\mu}=0.09\) and turbulence length scale \(L = 0.07 \times\) chord (0.07 m).  
  - Pressure `p`: `zeroGradient`.

- `outlet`: type `patch`.  
  - Velocity `U`: `inletOutlet`.  
  - Turbulent kinetic energy `k`: `inletOutlet`.  
  - Specific dissipation rate `omega`: `inletOutlet`.  
  - Pressure `p`: `fixedValue` uniform 0 Pa.

- `airfoil`: type `wall`.  
  - Velocity `U`: `noSlip`.  
  - Turbulent kinetic energy `k`: `kqRWallFunction`.  
  - Specific dissipation rate `omega`: `omegaWallFunction`.  
  - Pressure `p`: `zeroGradient`.

- `frontAndBack`: type `wall`.  
  - Velocity `U`: `noSlip`.  
  - Turbulent kinetic energy `k`: `zeroGradient`.  
  - Specific dissipation rate `omega`: `zeroGradient`.  
  - Pressure `p`: `zeroGradient`.

Set the fluid properties in `constant/transportProperties` to those of air at standard conditions (density 1.225 kg m⁻³, kinematic viscosity \(1.5\times10^{-5}\) m² s⁻¹).  
Configure the turbulence model in `constant/turbulenceProperties` to use `kOmegaSST`.  
In `system/fvSchemes` and `system/fvSolution`, use the default simpleFoam settings appropriate for incompressible turbulent flow, adjusting under‑relaxation factors if needed for convergence.  
Run the simulation with `simpleFoam` and monitor residuals until they fall below \(10^{-5}\) for all variables.  
Post‑process the results to obtain pressure coefficient, lift, and drag on the airfoil.
