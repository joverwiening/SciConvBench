Create a new OpenFOAM case for the cyclone separator and use the solver simpleFoam.  
Set the turbulence model to the standard k‑epsilon model.  
In the constant folder, define the fluid properties in transportProperties with a density of 1.225 kg/m³ and a dynamic viscosity of 1.8e‑5 Pa·s.  
In the 0 folder, set the pressure field p to zeroGradient at the inlet, outlet, dustOutlet, and walls.  
Set the velocity field U as follows: at the inlet use a fixedValue of uniform (10 0 0) m/s, at the outlet and dustOutlet use zeroGradient, and at the walls use a fixedValue of uniform (0 0 0) m/s to enforce the no‑slip condition.  
Define the turbulence kinetic energy k with a fixedValue of uniform 0.38 m²/s² at the inlet and zeroGradient elsewhere.  
Define the turbulence dissipation rate epsilon with a fixedValue of uniform 3.8 m²/s³ at the inlet and zeroGradient elsewhere.  
Use standard fvSchemes for simpleFoam, employing second‑order Gauss linear for divergence and gradient terms and Gauss linear for Laplacian terms.  
Use standard fvSolution settings for simpleFoam with appropriate under‑relaxation factors (e.g., 0.3 for pressure, 0.7 for velocity, 0.7 for k, 0.7 for epsilon) and convergence criteria of 1e‑5 for residuals.  
Run the simulation until the residuals for all fields drop below the specified convergence criteria, indicating a steady‑state solution.
