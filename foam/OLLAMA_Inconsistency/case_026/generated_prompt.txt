Configure the case as a hybrid RANS‑LES simulation with the k‑epsilon DES model (kEpsilonDES) to capture both Reynolds‑averaged and large‑eddy characteristics.

Define the velocity field `U` with a fixedValue of (10 0 0) m/s on the `inlet` patch, zeroGradient on the `outlet` patch, and fixedValue (0 0 0) no‑slip on `upperWall` and `lowerWall`; set the `front` and `back` patches to type `empty`.  
Define the pressure field `p` with zeroGradient on the `inlet`, `upperWall`, and `lowerWall` patches, and a fixedValue of 0 on the `outlet`; set `front` and `back` to `empty`.  
Use standard simpleFoam relaxation factors and linear solvers for pressure and velocity.  
Run the simulation for a total of 500 SIMPLE iterations, monitoring residuals and ensuring they fall below typical steady‑state tolerances (e.g., 1e‑5).
