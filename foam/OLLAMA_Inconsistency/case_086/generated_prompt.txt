Simulate the transient cooling of a hot block in a 2D sealed room using the compressible solver `buoyantPimpleFoam`.  
Create a 2‑D domain of size 5 m (x) by 3 m (y) with a single cell thickness in the z‑direction and apply `frontAndBack` empty boundary condition.  
Extrude the mesh with a uniform cell size of 0.025 m, resulting in 200 cells in the x‑direction and 120 cells in the y‑direction.  
Place a solid block of size 0.5 m × 0.5 m at the centre of the room and initialise its temperature to 400 K.  
Initialise the surrounding air temperature to 300 K.  
Set all four walls to adiabatic thermal condition (`zeroGradient` for `T`) and no‑slip velocity condition (`noSlip` for `U`).  
Apply gravity vector (0 -9.81 0) in the negative y‑direction.  
In `constant/thermophysicalProperties` use a fully compressible perfect‑gas equation of state:  

```
thermoType
{
    type            hePsiThermo;
    mixture         pureMixture;
    transport       sutherland;
    thermo          janaf;
    equationOfState perfectGas;
    specie          specie;
    energy          sensibleEnthalpy;
}
```
