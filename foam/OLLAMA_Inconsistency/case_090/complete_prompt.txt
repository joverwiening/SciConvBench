Set up a transient simulation of 2D vortex shedding behind a cylinder at Reynolds number 100. The domain is a rectangular channel with a circular cylinder offset from the center. Use the pimpleFoam solver, which is more robust for such convective flows. The fluid is incompressible with kinematic viscosity nu = 0.01 m^2/s. The inlet velocity is U = (1 0 0) m/s. The cylinder diameter is 1 m. Boundaries: 'inlet' (fixedValue U), 'outlet' (zeroGradient U, fixedValue p=0), 'topAndBottom' (slip), 'cylinder' (noSlip), and 'frontAndBack' (empty). Run the simulation from t=0 to t=200 s with a deltaT of 0.1 s. The goal is to observe the von Kármán vortex street and calculate the Strouhal number.
