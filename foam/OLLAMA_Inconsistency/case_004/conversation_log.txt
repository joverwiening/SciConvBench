Q: Which boundary should be defined as the inlet and which as the outlet in the blockMeshDict?
A: Define the face at **y = 0 m** as the **inlet** and the face at **y = 1.00 m** as the **outlet** in the blockMeshDict. The remaining faces are walls (and the front‑and‑back faces are “empty” for the 2‑D case).
