Use the nonNewtonianIcoFoam solver for all simulations.  
Create a backward‑facing step geometry where the channel length is 10 H, the total channel height is 1 H, the step height is 0.2 H, and the step length is 0.5 H, with H defined as the characteristic channel height.  
Set the inlet boundary condition in the 0/U file to a fixedValue type with a uniform velocity magnitude of 1 m s⁻¹ directed downstream.  
Define the outlet boundary condition as zeroGradient for velocity and pressure.  
Apply a slip type boundary condition to the top wall, the bottom wall upstream of the step, and the step surface.  
In the constant/transportProperties file set transportModel to PowerLaw, the consistency index K to 0.1 Pa·sⁿ, the power‑law index n to 1.5, and the fluid density ρ to 1000 kg m⁻³.  
Use a uniform initial field for velocity and pressure consistent with the inlet condition.  
Run the simulation until all residuals fall below 1 × 10⁻⁵ or until a steady‑state solution is achieved.  
Post‑process the results to obtain velocity vectors, magnitude contours, and shear‑stress distributions over the step and downstream region.
