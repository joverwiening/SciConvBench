The unit system for the analysis is Newtons (N), millimeters (mm), and Megapascals (MPa).
Model a vertical, prismatic, linearly elastic bar.
The bar has a length L of 3500 mm.
The bar has a constant cross-sectional area A of 200 mm^2.
The bar hangs from a rigid support at its top end.
The material's modulus of elasticity E is 69000 MPa.
The material's density ρ is 7850 kg/m^3.
The gravitational acceleration g is 9.81 m/s^2.
The only load acting on the bar is its own self-weight.
The coordinate system origin is at the top support, with the x-axis pointing downward.
The axial force at a distance x from the top is given by P(x) = ρ * g * A * (L - x).
Calculate the total elongation δ of the bar.
Use the integral method for the elongation calculation: δ = ∫₀ᴸ [P(x) / (A * E)] dx.
Ensure all physical quantities are converted to the N-mm-MPa unit system before calculation.
