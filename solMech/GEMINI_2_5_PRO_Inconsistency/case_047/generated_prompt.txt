The problem is a mechanics of materials analysis.
The analysis uses consistent N-mm-MPa units.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.
The material is AISI 1020 steel.
The Young's modulus is 200000 MPa.
The yield strength is 350 MPa.
The object is a straight, prismatic circular rod.
The rod diameter is 12 mm.
The rod gage length is 800 mm.
The rod is loaded in tension by an axial force.
The axial force is 15000 N.
The force is applied through the centroid.
Calculate the average normal stress in the rod.
Calculate the elastic normal strain in the rod.
Calculate the axial elongation of the gage length.
Calculate the factor of safety against yielding.
The factor of safety is defined as the yield strength divided by the average normal stress (n = S_y / σ_avg).
