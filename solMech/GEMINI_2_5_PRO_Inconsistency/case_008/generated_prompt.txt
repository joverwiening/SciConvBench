Use N-mm-MPa units.
Model two prismatic bars mounted in parallel between two rigid plates.
The length of both bars is 500 mm.
Bar 1 is AISI 1020 steel with an area of 300 mm^2 and a modulus of elasticity of 200000 MPa.
Bar 2 is 6061-T6 aluminum with an area of 200 mm^2 and a modulus of elasticity of 69000 MPa.
A total tensile load of 20000 N is applied to the top plate.
The total load is shared between the two bars.
The two bars are perfectly bonded to the rigid plates and must undergo the same elongation.
Determine the tensile force carried by the steel bar.
Determine the tensile force carried by the aluminum bar.
Determine the tensile stress in the steel bar.
Determine the tensile stress in the aluminum bar.
Determine the common elongation of the two bars.
