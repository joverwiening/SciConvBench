Determine the minimum required height, h, for a rectangular beam.
The calculation will use the N-mm-MPa unit system.
The maximum bending moment, M_max, is 6,000,000 N·mm.
The width of the rectangular cross-section, b, is 60 mm.
The allowable bending stress, σ_allow, is 170 MPa.
The minimum height is determined by setting the maximum bending stress equal to the allowable bending stress.
The maximum bending stress is calculated using the formula σ = M * c / I.
The moment of inertia, I, for the rectangular section is calculated as I = (b * h^3) / 12.
The extreme-fiber distance, c, is h/2.
The final result for the height, h, must be expressed in millimeters.
