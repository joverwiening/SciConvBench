Use N-mm-MPa units.
Model a cantilever beam fixed at x=0 and free at x=L.
The beam length L is 1500 mm.
The material has a Young's modulus E of 200000 MPa.
The beam has a constant moment of inertia I of 800000 mm^4.
Apply a downward point load P of 500 N at the free end (x=L).
Use Castigliano's theorem to find the vertical deflection at the free end.
The strain energy U is calculated using the integral of M(x)^2 / (2EI) from x=0 to x=L.
Define the bending moment at a distance x from the fixed end as the variable function M(x) = -P(L-x).
Calculate the strain energy U by integrating the expression for M(x).
Calculate the vertical deflection at the free end by taking the partial derivative of the total strain energy U with respect to the load P.
Do not use a pre-derived deflection formula for the calculation.
The final output should be the numerical value of the deflection in mm.
