Use N, mm, and MPa as the system of units.
The beam is simply supported.
The beam span L is 3000 mm.
The modulus of elasticity E is 200000 MPa.
The second moment of area I is 1500000 mm^4.
A downward point load P of 2000 N is applied at midspan.
The vertical deflection at midspan shall be computed using Castigliano's theorem for bending.
The bending moment M(x) must be defined as a piecewise function over the domain 0 ≤ x ≤ L.
The strain energy U shall be calculated by integrating M(x)^2 / (2EI) over the length of the beam.
The deflection is the partial derivative of the strain energy U with respect to the load P.
