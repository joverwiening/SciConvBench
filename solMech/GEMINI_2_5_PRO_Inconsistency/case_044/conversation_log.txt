Q: The assumption of isotropic behavior, where lateral strain is determined by Poisson's ratio, contradicts the assumption of plane strain, where lateral strain is zero. Which of these assumptions should be used for the calculation?
A: You should not assume plane strain. Use the standard assumption for linear elastic isotropic behavior where the lateral strain is related to the axial strain by Poisson's ratio.
