The unit system is N-mm-MPa.
The beam is simply supported.
The beam span L is 2500 mm.
The modulus of elasticity E is 200000 MPa.
The second moment of area I is 3000000 mm^4.
A downward point load P of 1000 N is applied at midspan.
The vertical deflection at midspan shall be computed.
The computation method is Castigliano's second theorem.
The strain energy U is due to bending only.
The strain energy U is calculated by integrating U = ∫ M(x)^2 / (2EI) dx over the beam's length.
The bending moment M(x) is defined as a piecewise function over the domain 0 ≤ x ≤ L.
