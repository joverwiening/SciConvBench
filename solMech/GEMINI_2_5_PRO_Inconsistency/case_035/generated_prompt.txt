The objective is to determine the minimum required diameter for a solid circular column.
The column length is L = 2200 mm.
The column material is 6061-T6 aluminum with a Young's modulus of E = 69000 MPa.
The column must support a compressive load of P = 25000 N.
The required factor of safety against buckling is n = 2.5.
The effective length factor is K = 2.0.
The analysis must assume Euler buckling for a solid circular cross-section.
The critical buckling load is calculated using P_cr = π^2 * E * I / (K * L)^2.
The area moment of inertia for the solid circular cross-section is I = π * d^4 / 64.
The design requirement is that the critical buckling load must be greater than or equal to the applied load multiplied by the factor of safety, expressed as P_cr ≥ n·P.
Solve for the minimum diameter d that satisfies this requirement.
The final result for the minimum diameter should be reported in millimeters.
All calculations will use the N-mm-MPa unit system.
