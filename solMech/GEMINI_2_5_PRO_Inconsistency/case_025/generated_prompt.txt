The unit system is N-mm-MPa.
The beam is a cantilever type, fixed at x=0 and free at x=L.
The beam length L is 1500 mm.
The material is 6061-T6 aluminum, with a Young's modulus E of 69000 MPa.
The moment of inertia I is constant at 2500000 mm^4.
A downward point load P of 500 N is applied at the free end.
Compute the vertical deflection at the free end using Castigliano’s theorem.
Use the bending strain energy formula U = ∫[0→L] M(x)^2/(2EI) dx.
The bending moment function is M(x) = P*(L-x).
First, integrate to find the total strain energy U as a function of P.
Second, differentiate the resulting strain energy U with respect to the load P to find the deflection.
Do not use a memorized deflection formula for the final answer.
