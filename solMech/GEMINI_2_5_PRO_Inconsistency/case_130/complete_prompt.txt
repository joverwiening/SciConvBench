Use N–mm–MPa units.

A simply supported beam will experience a maximum bending moment of M_max = 6000000 N·mm. The beam will have a rectangular cross section with width b = 30 mm and unknown height h. The allowable bending stress is σ_allow = 90 MPa.

Determine the minimum required height h using σ_max = M_max (h/2) / (b h^3/12).
