Use N, mm, and MPa as the system of units.
Model a stepped solid circular shaft with two segments, AB and BC, connected in series.
Define segment AB with length L1 = 400 mm and diameter d1 = 25 mm.
Define segment BC with length L2 = 900 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm.
The shaft material is 6061-T6 aluminum with a shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa.
Fix end A of the shaft.
Apply an external torque T = 750,000 N·mm at the free end C.
The internal torque throughout both segments AB and BC is constant and equal to 750,000 N·mm.
Calculate the maximum shear stress in segment AB.
Calculate the maximum shear stress in segment BC.
Identify the governing segment as the one with the higher maximum shear stress.
Calculate the angle of twist for segment AB.
Calculate the angle of twist for segment BC.
Calculate the total angle of twist at C relative to A by summing the twists of the two segments.
Report the maximum shear stress for each segment, identify the governing segment, and report the total angle of twist.
