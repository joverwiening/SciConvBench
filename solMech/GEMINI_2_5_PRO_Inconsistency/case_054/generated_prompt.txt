Use N-mm-MPa as the system of units.
The bar has a length L of 800 mm.
The bar is subjected to a constant axial tensile force P of 12000 N.
The material is AISI 1020 steel.
The Young's modulus E is a constant 200000 MPa.
The cross-sectional area A(x) varies linearly along the length x.
The area function is A(x) = A0 * (1 + k * x/L).
The variable x is measured from the left end of the bar.
The initial cross-sectional area A0 is 250 mm^2.
The taper constant k is 0.6.
Compute the total elongation of the bar.
Calculate the elongation using the axial deformation integral: δ = ∫[0→L] P/(A(x)E) dx.
