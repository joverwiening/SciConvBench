The task is to compute the elongation of a vertical bar due to its own weight.
The unit system for the calculation and final result is Newtons (N), millimeters (mm), and Megapascals (MPa).
The bar is vertical, prismatic, and made of a linearly elastic material.
The bar length is L = 3000 mm.
The cross-sectional area is A = 300 mm^2.
The modulus of elasticity is E = 69000 MPa.
The material density is ρ = 8900 kg/m^3.
The gravitational acceleration is g = 9.81 m/s^2.
The values for density and gravitational acceleration must be converted to be consistent with the N-mm-MPa unit system before calculation.
The axial force P at a distance x from the top support is given by the equation P(x) = ρ * g * A * (L - x).
The total elongation δ is calculated by integrating the axial strain over the length of the bar, using the formula δ = ∫₀ᴸ P(x)/(A * E) dx.
