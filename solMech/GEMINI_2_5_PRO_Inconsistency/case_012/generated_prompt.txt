Determine the minimum required height, h, for a simply supported beam.
Use the N-mm-MPa unit system.
Assume small deflection and Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.
The beam span is L = 2000 mm.
The beam material is 6061-T6 aluminum with a Young's Modulus E = 69000 MPa.
A concentrated load of P = 3000 N is applied at the midspan.
The beam has a rectangular cross-section with a width b = 30 mm.
The maximum allowable deflection at midspan is δ_allow = 3 mm.
Calculate the maximum deflection using the formula δ_max = P L^3 / (48 E I).
Calculate the area moment of inertia using the formula I = b h^3 / 12.
