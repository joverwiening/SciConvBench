Use N-mm-MPa as the system of units.
The initial diameter is 12.5 mm.
The initial gage length is 50 mm.
The axial load is 12000 N.
The elongation over the gage length is 0.12 mm.
Calculate the initial cross-sectional area from the initial diameter.
Calculate the engineering stress by dividing the axial load by the initial cross-sectional area.
Calculate the engineering strain by dividing the elongation by the initial gage length.
Calculate the modulus of elasticity by dividing the engineering stress by the engineering strain.
