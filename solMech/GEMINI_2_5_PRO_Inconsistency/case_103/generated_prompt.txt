Use N-mm-MPa units.
Assume Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.
Neglect shear deformation.
The beam is simply supported.
The beam span L is 2500 mm.
The beam material is AISI 1020 steel.
The Young's modulus E is 200000 MPa.
The moment of inertia I is constant at 3500000 mm^4.
A downward point load P of 2500 N acts at midspan.
The slope is zero at midspan due to symmetry.
The slope is non-zero at the supports.
Compute the maximum deflection at midspan.
Compute the slope at the supports.
The computation can be done by integrating EI v'' = M(x) or by using standard formulas.
