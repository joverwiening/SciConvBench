Use N-mm-MPa as the system of units.
Assume small strains.
Define a rectangular adhesive layer with an area A of 4000 mm^2.
The thickness of the layer, t, is 20 mm.
Apply a uniform in-plane shear force, V, of 12000 N to the layer.
The material is isotropic with a shear modulus, G, of 39179.1 MPa.
Compute the average shear stress, τ_avg, using the formula τ_avg = V/A.
Compute the average shear strain, γ_avg, using the formula γ_avg = τ_avg / G.
Compute the relative lateral displacement, Δ, between the two faces of the layer using the formula Δ = γ_avg * t.
