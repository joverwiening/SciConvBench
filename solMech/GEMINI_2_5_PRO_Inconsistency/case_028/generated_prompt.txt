Use the N-mm-MPa unit system.
The rod length L is 1200 mm.
The rod diameter d is 30 mm.
The dropped weight W is 500 N.
The drop height h is 200 mm.
The Young's modulus E is 200000 MPa.
The rod is a vertical structural steel rod fixed at its top end.
A collar is attached to the bottom end of the rod.
The weight is dropped onto the collar.
The mass of the rod is neglected.
The rod behaves as a linear axial spring.
Calculate the cross-sectional area A using the formula A = (π * d^2) / 4.
The maximum axial force P_max is determined by the energy conservation equation W * (h + δ_max) = (P_max^2 * L) / (2 * A * E).
The maximum deflection δ_max is related to the maximum force by δ_max = (P_max * L) / (A * E).
Substitute the expression for δ_max into the energy conservation equation to solve for P_max.
Calculate the maximum normal stress σ_max using the formula σ_max = P_max / A.
