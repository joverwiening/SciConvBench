Use the N-mm-MPa unit system for all calculations.
The bar is vertical, prismatic, and linearly elastic.
The bar length is L = 3500 mm.
The cross-sectional area is A = 200 mm^2.
The modulus of elasticity is E = 69000 MPa.
The material density is ρ = 8900 kg/m^3.
The gravitational acceleration is g = 9.81 m/s^2.
The only load acting on the bar is its own self-weight.
The coordinate x is measured downward from the top support.
The axial force at a section x is given by the formula P(x) = ρ * g * A * (L - x).
Convert the product of density and gravity (ρ * g) from base units to N/mm^3 before use in the force equation.
The elongation of the bar is to be computed by integrating the axial strain along the length.
Use the formula δ = ∫₀ᴸ [P(x) / (A * E)] dx for the total elongation.
The final result for the elongation should be reported in mm.
