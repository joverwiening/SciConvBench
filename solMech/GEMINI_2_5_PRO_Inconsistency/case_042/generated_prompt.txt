The unit system is N-mm-MPa.
The analysis assumes a linear elastic axial response.
A prismatic bar has a length L of 800 mm.
The bar has a cross-sectional area A of 300 mm^2.
The bar is made of structural steel with a Young's Modulus E of 200000 MPa.
A tensile load P of 8000 N is applied suddenly.
The maximum stress in the bar during the transient response must be determined.
The calculation must use energy methods, equating the work done by the load to the strain energy stored in the bar.
