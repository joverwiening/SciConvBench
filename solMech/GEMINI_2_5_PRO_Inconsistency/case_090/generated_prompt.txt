The beam is simply supported.
The supports are located at x=0 mm and x=3000 mm.
The beam span L is 3000 mm.
The material is AISI 1020 steel.
The modulus of elasticity E is 200000 MPa.
The area moment of inertia I is 1200000 mm^4.
A downward point load P of 800 N is applied at x = 2000 mm.
The deflection at x = 2000 mm must be computed.
The computation method is the unit-load (virtual work) method.
The formula to use is δ = ∫[0→L] (M(x)·m(x))/(EI) dx.
M(x) is the bending moment diagram from the real 800 N load.
m(x) is the bending moment diagram from an auxiliary system.
The auxiliary system consists of a unit downward force of 1 N applied at x = 2000 mm.
The units for the calculation and result must be N and mm.
