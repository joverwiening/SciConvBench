Use N-mm-MPa units for all calculations.
The bar has a rectangular cross section.
The cross section width is 30 mm.
The cross section height is 100 mm.
The internal resultant normal force is 50000 N.
The internal resultant transverse shear force is 5000 N.
Calculate the cross-sectional area A.
Calculate the average normal stress, σ_avg, by dividing the normal force by the cross-sectional area.
Calculate the average shear stress, τ_avg, by dividing the shear force by the cross-sectional area.
Calculate the magnitude of the maximum in-plane shear stress using the formula τ_max = sqrt((σ_avg/2)^2 + τ_avg^2).
