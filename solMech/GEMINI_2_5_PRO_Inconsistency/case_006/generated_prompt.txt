Use N-mm-MPa units for all calculations.
Model a simply supported beam with a total span of 2400 mm.
Place a pin support at x=0 and a roller support at x=2400 mm.
The beam has a modulus of elasticity E of 200000 MPa.
The beam has a moment of inertia I of 3500000 mm^4.
For the real system, apply a downward point load of 1200 N at x = 1200 mm.
For the auxiliary system, apply a downward unit point load of 1 N at x = 1200 mm.
Use the virtual work method to compute the deflection.
Calculate the deflection using the formula δ = ∫[0→L] (M(x)·m(x))/(EI) dx.
M(x) is the bending moment diagram for the real system.
m(x) is the bending moment diagram for the auxiliary system.
The final output shall be the vertical deflection at x = 1200 mm.
