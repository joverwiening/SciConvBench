Use the N-mm-MPa unit system.
Model the rod as a linear axial spring.
Neglect the mass of the rod.
The rod is made of AISI 1020 steel with a Young's modulus, E, of 200000 MPa.
The rod has a length, L, of 1500 mm.
The rod has a diameter, d, of 16 mm.
A weight, W, of 800 N is dropped from a height, h, of 150 mm onto a collar at the end of the rod.
Calculate the cross-sectional area, A, using the formula A = (π * d^2) / 4.
Calculate the maximum axial force, P_max, by solving the energy conservation equation W * (h + (P_max * L) / (A * E)) = (P_max^2 * L) / (2 * A * E).
Calculate the maximum normal stress, σ_max, using the formula σ_max = P_max / A.
