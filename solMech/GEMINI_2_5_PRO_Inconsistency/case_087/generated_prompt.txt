The system of units is Newtons, millimeters, and Megapascals.
The material behavior is linear-elastic.
The material is structural steel with a Young's Modulus of 200,000 MPa.
The geometry is a stepped circular bar composed of two segments.
The bar is fixed at its left end.
Segment 1 has a length of 300 mm and a diameter of 12 mm.
Segment 2 has a length of 600 mm and a diameter of 30 mm.
A tensile axial load of 25,000 N is applied to the right end of the bar.
Calculate the unique normal stress in each segment based on its cross-sectional area.
Calculate the total elongation of the bar as the sum of the individual elongations of each segment.
The final outputs are the normal stress in Segment 1, the normal stress in Segment 2, and the total elongation of the bar.
