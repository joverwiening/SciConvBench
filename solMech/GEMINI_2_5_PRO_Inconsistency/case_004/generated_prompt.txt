Use N-mm-MPa units for all calculations and results.
The bar is prismatic with a constant cross-sectional area of 300 mm^2.
The bar is made of AISI 1020 steel with a Young's Modulus of 200,000 MPa.
The bar consists of three segments: AB, BC, and CD.
The length of segment AB is 500 mm.
The length of segment BC is 600 mm.
The length of segment CD is 400 mm.
The left end of the bar, point A, is fixed.
An axial force of +18,000 N is applied at point B.
An axial force of -12,000 N is applied at point C.
An axial force of +16,000 N is applied at point D.
Calculate the internal axial force for segment AB.
Calculate the internal axial force for segment BC.
Calculate the internal axial force for segment CD.
Calculate the normal stress for segment AB.
Calculate the normal stress for segment BC.
Calculate the normal stress for segment CD.
Calculate the displacement of point D relative to point A.
Positive force and displacement values indicate a direction to the right.
Negative force and displacement values indicate a direction to the left.
