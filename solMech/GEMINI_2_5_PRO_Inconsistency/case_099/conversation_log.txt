Q: The formula `P_max = 2W` is valid for a drop height of zero. Should I use this formula despite the specified drop height of 200 mm?
A: No, do not use the formula `P_max = 2W`. You should use the energy conservation equation `W(h + δ_max) = (P_max^2 L)/(2AE)` to solve for `P_max`.
