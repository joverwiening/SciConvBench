Use N-mm-MPa as the system of units.
The model consists of a vertical AISI 1020 steel rod.
The rod has a length L of 800 mm.
The rod has a diameter d of 25 mm.
The rod is fixed at its top end.
A weight W of 1500 N is dropped from a height h of 200 mm onto a collar at the bottom end of the rod.
The Young's modulus E for the steel is 200000 MPa.
The cross-sectional area A is calculated as pi * d^2 / 4.
The mass of the rod is neglected.
The rod behaves as a linear axial spring.
Use the principle of energy conservation to determine the maximum axial force P_max.
The governing energy conservation equation is W * (h + delta_max) = (P_max^2 * L) / (2 * A * E).
The maximum elongation delta_max is defined as (P_max * L) / (A * E).
Substitute the expression for delta_max into the energy equation to create a single equation for P_max.
Solve the resulting quadratic equation for the positive value of P_max.
After finding P_max, calculate the maximum normal stress sigma_max.
The maximum normal stress sigma_max is calculated as P_max / A.
