Use N-mm-MPa as the system of units.
The column material is AISI 1020 steel with a Young's modulus (E) of 200000 MPa.
The column has a length (L) of 1000 mm.
The area moment of inertia (I) is 1200000 mm^4.
The column has fixed-free end conditions, corresponding to an effective length factor (K) of 2.0.
Assume Euler buckling theory applies.
Calculate the Euler critical buckling load (P_cr) using the formula P_cr = π^2 * E * I / (K * L)^2.
Report the final calculated P_cr value in Newtons (N).
Report the final calculated P_cr value in kilonewtons (kN).
