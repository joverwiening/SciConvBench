The system of units is N-mm-MPa.
The material's Young's modulus, E, is a constant value of 69000 MPa.
A bar has a total length, L, of 1200 mm.
The bar is subjected to a constant axial tensile force, P, of 20000 N.
The cross-sectional area, A(x), varies linearly along the bar's length.
The area is defined by the function A(x) = A0 * (1 + k * x/L).
The variable x represents the distance from the left end of the bar.
The initial cross-sectional area, A0, is 200 mm^2.
The area variation constant, k, is 0.6.
The total elongation of the bar, δ, must be computed.
The computation must use the axial deformation integral: δ = ∫[0→L] P/(A(x)E) dx.
