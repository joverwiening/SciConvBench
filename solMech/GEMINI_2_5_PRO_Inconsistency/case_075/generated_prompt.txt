A simply supported beam AB has a span of 4000 mm.
A concentrated vertical load of 12000 N acts downward at the center of the span, 2000 mm from support A.
No other loads are applied to the beam.
The sign convention for positive shear is an upward force on the left face of a cut section.
The sign convention for positive bending moment is a moment that causes sagging, resulting in tension at the bottom fibers.
Determine the internal shear force V and the internal bending moment M at a cross-section located 1000 mm from support A.
Provide the shear force V in units of Newtons (N).
Provide the bending moment M in units of Newton-millimeters (N·mm).
