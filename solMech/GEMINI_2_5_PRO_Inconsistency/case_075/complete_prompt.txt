Use statics sign conventions: take positive shear V upward on the left cut face and positive bending moment M causing sagging (tension at the bottom fibers). Use N–mm units for V and M.

A simply supported beam AB has span L = 4000 mm. A concentrated vertical load P = 12000 N acts downward at a point located 2000 mm from support A (and therefore 2000 mm from support B). There are no other loads.

Determine the internal shear force V and bending moment M at a cross section located x = 1000 mm from support A (take the section within the span, not at a support). Provide V in N and M in N·mm.
