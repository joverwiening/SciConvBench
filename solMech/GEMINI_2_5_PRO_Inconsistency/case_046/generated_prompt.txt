The problem domain is mechanics of materials.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.
The material is structural steel.
The Young's modulus is 200000 MPa.
The yield strength is 250 MPa.
The geometry is a straight, prismatic circular rod.
The rod diameter is 25 mm.
The gage length is 800 mm.
The loading is a tensile axial force of 20000 N.
The force is applied through the centroid of the cross-section.
The required unit system is N-mm-MPa.
Calculate the average normal stress in the rod.
Calculate the elastic normal strain in the rod.
Calculate the axial elongation of the gage length.
Calculate the factor of safety against yielding.
The factor of safety is defined as the yield strength divided by the average normal stress.
