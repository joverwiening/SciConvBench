Use N-mm-MPa units.
Assume small strains.
The model is a rectangular adhesive layer.
The area of the layer is A = 3000 mm^2.
The thickness of the layer is t = 8 mm.
The layer is subjected to a uniform in-plane shear force V = 12000 N.
The material is isotropic.
The shear modulus is G = 39179.1 MPa.
Do not assume the layer is rigid.
Compute the average shear stress using the formula τ_avg = V/A.
Compute the average shear strain using the formula γ_avg = τ_avg / G.
Compute the relative lateral displacement using the formula Δ = γ_avg * t.
