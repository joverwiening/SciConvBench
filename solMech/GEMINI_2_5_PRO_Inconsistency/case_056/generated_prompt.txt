Use N-mm-MPa units.
The Young's modulus E is a constant value of 200000 MPa.
The bar has a length L of 800 mm.
The bar is subjected to a constant axial tensile force P of 30000 N.
The cross-sectional area A(x) varies linearly along the length x.
The formula for the cross-sectional area is A(x) = A0 * (1 + k * x/L).
The area at the left end (x=0) is A0 = 250 mm^2.
The taper constant is k = 0.8.
Compute the elongation of the bar using the axial deformation integral δ = ∫[0→L] P/(A(x)E) dx.
