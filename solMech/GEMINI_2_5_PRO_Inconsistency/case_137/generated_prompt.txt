The shaft is subjected to a bending moment M = 2,000,000 N·mm.
The shaft is subjected to a torque T = 500,000 N·mm.
The analysis location is the outer surface of the shaft where bending stress is maximum.

Calculate the maximum bending normal stress σ using the formula σ = M * c / I.
Calculate the torsional shear stress τ at the surface using the formula τ = T * c / J.
