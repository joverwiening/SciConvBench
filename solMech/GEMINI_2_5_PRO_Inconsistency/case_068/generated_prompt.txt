Use N-mm-MPa as the unit system.
The beam material is structural steel.
The Young's modulus (E) is 200000 MPa.
The second moment of area (I) is 4000000 mm^4.
The bending moment (M) at the section is 3000000 N·mm.
Calculate the curvature (κ) using the formula κ = M / (E * I).
Calculate the radius of curvature (ρ) using the formula ρ = 1 / κ.
Report the final value for the radius of curvature (ρ) in mm.
