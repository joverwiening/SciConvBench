The system of units is N-mm-MPa.
The analysis is for a stepped solid circular shaft made of structural steel.
The shaft consists of two segments, AB and BC, connected in series.
End A of the shaft is fixed.
Segment AB has a length of 300 mm and a diameter of 25 mm.
Segment BC has a length of 700 mm and a diameter of 30 mm.
A torque of 450000 N·mm is applied at the free end, C.
The internal torque is 450000 N·mm in both Segment AB and Segment BC.
The shear modulus of the structural steel is 77519.4 MPa.
Calculate the maximum shear stress in Segment AB.
Calculate the maximum shear stress in Segment BC.
Identify which segment has the higher maximum shear stress; this is the governing segment.
Calculate the total angle of twist at end C relative to end A.
Report the final angle of twist in degrees.
