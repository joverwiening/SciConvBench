Use N-mm-MPa units.
The Young's modulus (E) is a constant value of 200000 MPa.
The bar has a length (L) of 500 mm.
The bar is subjected to a constant axial tensile force (P) of 12000 N.
The material is AISI 1020 steel.
The cross-sectional area (A(x)) varies linearly along the length of the bar.
The area function is A(x) = A0 * (1 + k * x/L).
The variable x is the distance measured from the left end of the bar.
The initial cross-sectional area (A0) is 200 mm^2.
The taper constant (k) is 1.0.
Compute the total elongation (δ) of the bar.
The elongation must be calculated by solving the exact integral: δ = ∫[0→L] P/(A(x)E) dx.
Do not use the arithmetic-mean area for the calculation.
