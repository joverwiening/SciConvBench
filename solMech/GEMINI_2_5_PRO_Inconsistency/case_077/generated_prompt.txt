The task is to determine the minimum required diameter for a solid circular shaft.
The system of units is N-mm-MPa.
The shaft has a length L of 800 mm.
The shaft is made of AISI 1020 steel with a shear modulus G of 76923.1 MPa.
The shaft must transmit a torque T of 600000 N-mm.
The allowable angle of twist over the length L is 1.5 degrees.
The allowable angle of twist must be converted from degrees to radians for the calculation.
The torsion formula φ = TL/(JG) shall be used, where φ is in radians.
The polar moment of inertia J for the solid circular shaft is calculated as J = πd^4/32.
Calculate the minimum required diameter d based on the angle of twist limit.
