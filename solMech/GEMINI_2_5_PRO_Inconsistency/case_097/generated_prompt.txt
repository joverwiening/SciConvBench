The unit system is N-mm-MPa.
The model is a cantilever beam of length L = 1500 mm.
The beam is fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
The beam is made of AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The beam has a constant moment of inertia I = 2500000 mm^4.
A downward point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end.
The vertical deflection at the free end shall be computed using Castigliano's theorem.
The bending strain energy U is calculated using the integral U = ∫[0→L] M(x)^2/(2EI) dx.
The bending moment M(x) is the variable function representing the moment along the beam's length due to the load P.
The deflection is found by differentiating the strain energy U with respect to the load P.
