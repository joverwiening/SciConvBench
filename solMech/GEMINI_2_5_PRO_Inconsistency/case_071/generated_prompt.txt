Use N-mm-MPa as the system of units.
The object is a solid circular shaft.
The shaft diameter is 25 mm.
The shaft length is 1000 mm.
The shaft material is AISI 1020 steel.
The shear modulus of the material is 76923.1 MPa.
A constant torque of 350000 N·mm is applied to the shaft.
The polar moment of inertia to be used is J = π * d^4 / 32.
The torsional strain energy is calculated using the formula U = ∫[0→L] T^2/(2GJ) dx.
The angle of twist is computed using Castigliano’s theorem, φ = ∂U/∂T.
The final output should be the angle of twist between the ends of the shaft.
