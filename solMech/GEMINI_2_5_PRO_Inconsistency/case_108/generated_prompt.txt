Use the polar moment of inertia J = π * d^4 / 32 in the Euler buckling formula.
Compute the Euler critical load P_cr using the formula P_cr = (π^2 * E * J) / (K * L)^2.
Use a factor of safety n of 2.0.
Determine the allowable axial compressive load using the formula P_allow = P_cr / n.
