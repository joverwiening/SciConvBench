Determine the minimum required height, h, for a simply supported beam.
The system of units is N-mm-MPa.
The beam has a rectangular cross-section.
The width of the cross-section, b, is 40 mm.
The maximum bending moment, M_max, is 3,000,000 N·mm.
The allowable bending stress, σ_allow, is 90 MPa.
Use the bending stress formula σ = M * c / I.
The moment of inertia for the rectangular cross-section, I, is calculated as (b * h^3) / 12.
The extreme-fiber distance, c, is h/2.
The calculation should solve for h by setting the maximum stress equal to the allowable stress: σ_allow = M_max * c / I.
