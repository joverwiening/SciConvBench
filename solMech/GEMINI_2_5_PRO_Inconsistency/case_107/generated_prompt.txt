Use N-mm-MPa as the system of units.
The beam is simply supported with a span L of 3000 mm.
The modulus of elasticity E is 200000 MPa.
The second moment of area I is 3000000 mm^4.
A downward point load P of 2000 N is applied at midspan.
Define the bending moment M(x) as a piecewise function over the beam's span.
Calculate the total bending strain energy U by integrating M(x)^2/(2EI) from x=0 to x=L.
Compute the vertical deflection at midspan by taking the partial derivative of the total strain energy U with respect to the load P.
Report the final computed deflection value.
