Axial Loading Problem Specification:

Solve the axial loading problem for a prismatic steel bar using the method of sections and equilibrium equations.

Bar Geometry:
The bar has a constant cross-sectional area of 500 mm^2.
Total bar length is divided into three segments:
Segment AB: 700 mm
Segment BC: 400 mm
Segment CD: 300 mm

Material Properties:
Material: Structural steel
Young's Modulus (E) = 200000 MPa

Boundary Conditions:
Left end (point A) is fixed with u_A = 0
Sign convention: Positive forces point to the right (tension)

Applied Forces:
At point B: 15000 N (to the right)
At point C: -12000 N (to the left)
At point D: 10000 N (to the right)

Solution Requirements:
Determine the internal axial force in each segment (AB, BC, CD)
Calculate the normal stress in each segment
Compute the displacement of point D relative to point A

Solve using the method of sections and equilibrium equations, treating the bar as having uniform cross-sectional area and elastic properties.
