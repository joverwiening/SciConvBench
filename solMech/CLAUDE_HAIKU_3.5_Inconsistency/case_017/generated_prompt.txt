Compute the midspan deflection of a simply supported beam using superposition with the following parameters:
Beam span L = 3000 mm
Beam material: AISI 1020 steel
Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa
Moment of inertia I = 3000000 mm^4
Uniform distributed load w = 0.3 N/mm
Point load P = 1200 N at midspan

Calculation method:
Use Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.
Calculate deflection by superposing two standard cases:
1. Deflection due to uniform distributed load
2. Deflection due to point load at midspan

Compute the total midspan deflection by adding the individual deflections from each load case.

Use standard simply supported beam deflection formulas:
For uniform load: δ_w = (5 * w * L^4) / (384 * E * I)
For point load: δ_P = (P * L^3) / (48 * E * I)

Final result will be the sum of these two deflection components at the midspan.
