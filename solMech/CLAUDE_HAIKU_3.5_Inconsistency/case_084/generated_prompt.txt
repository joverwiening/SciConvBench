The tension specimen analysis will be performed using the following specifications:

Initial gage length (L0) is 50 mm.
Initial diameter (d0) is 12 mm.
Final gage length after fracture (Lf) is 66 mm.
Final minimum diameter at neck (df) is 9.6 mm.

Percent elongation will be calculated using the standard method: (Lf - L0)/L0 * 100.
Percent reduction of area will be calculated using the diameter change: 100(d0 - df)/d0.

Specific calculations:
Percent elongation = ((66 - 50)/50) * 100 = 32%
Percent reduction of area = (100(12 - 9.6)/12) = 20%
