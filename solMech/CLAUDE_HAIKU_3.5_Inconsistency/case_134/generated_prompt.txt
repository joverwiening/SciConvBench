Determine the internal shear force V and bending moment M at a cross section located 1500 mm from support A for a simply supported beam with the following characteristics:

Beam span length is 3000 mm.
A concentrated vertical load of 12000 N acts downward at the midspan (1500 mm from support A).
Use statics sign conventions with positive shear V upward on the left cut face and positive bending moment M causing sagging (tension at the bottom fibers).

Shear Force V Calculation:
For the section at x = 1500 mm, the shear force V is 6000 N.

Bending Moment M Calculation:
For the section at x = 1500 mm, the bending moment M is 4,500,000 N·mm.

The results are provided in N for shear force and N·mm for bending moment.
