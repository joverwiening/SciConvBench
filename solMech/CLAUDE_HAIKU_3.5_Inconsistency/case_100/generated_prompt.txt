The specification for analyzing the brass rod under axial tensile load is as follows:

Use N-mm-MPa units with linear elastic isotropic material behavior.

Rod Initial Conditions:
Diameter (d0) = 30 mm
Length (L) = 500 mm
Young's Modulus (E) = 105000 MPa
Poisson's Ratio (ν) = 0.34
Applied Load (P) = 25000 N

Calculation Procedure:
Compute cross-sectional area A0 = π(d0/2)² = 706.86 mm²
Calculate axial stress σ = P/A0 = 35.36 MPa
Determine axial strain εx = σ/E = 0.000337 (0.0337%)
Apply plane strain condition with zero lateral strain
Calculate lateral strain εy using εy = -ν * εx = -0.34 * 0.000337 = -0.000115
Compute change in diameter Δd = d0 * εy = 30 mm * (-0.000115) = -0.00345 mm

Outputs include axial stress, axial strain, lateral strain, and diameter change under specified conditions.
