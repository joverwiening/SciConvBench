The beam design specification is as follows:

Use N-mm-MPa units for all calculations.
Apply Euler-Bernoulli beam theory with small deflection assumptions.
Design a simply supported beam with span L = 2000 mm.
Use 6061-T6 aluminum with Young's modulus E = 69000 MPa.
Apply a concentrated load P = 3000 N at midspan.
Use a rectangular cross section with width b = 30 mm.
Replace central point load with equivalent uniform load P/L for deflection calculations.
Maximum midspan deflection must not exceed δ_allow = 3 mm.
Use maximum allowable bending stress of 180 MPa.
Calculate beam height using the formula δ_max = P L^3/(48 E I), with I = b h^3/12.
Determine minimum beam height h that satisfies deflection and stress constraints.
Apply a safety factor of 1.5 in the design calculations.
