The specification for the stress analysis of a circular shaft is as follows:

Use N-mm-MPa units for all calculations.
Analyze the stress state at the outer surface of a circular shaft with diameter d = 30 mm.
Apply a bending moment M = 4,000,000 N·mm and a torque T = 400,000 N·mm at the critical section.
Assume the torsional shear stress vanishes at the outer surface for principal stress calculation.

Compute the maximum bending normal stress using the formula σ = M c / I.
Calculate the moment of inertia I = π d^4/64.
Use the distance from neutral axis c = d/2.

Calculate the torsional shear stress using the formula τ = T c / J.
Compute the polar moment of inertia J = π d^4/32.

Treat the surface stress state as plane stress with:
σx = σ (from bending)
σy = 0
τxy = τ (from torsion)

Determine the principal stresses and maximum in-plane shear stress based on these conditions.
