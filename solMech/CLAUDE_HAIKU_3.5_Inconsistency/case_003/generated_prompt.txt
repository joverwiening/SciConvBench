The specification for solving the stepped circular bar problem is:

Use N-mm-MPa units and linear-elastic behavior.
Solve using the method of consistent deformation.
The bar is fixed at the left end and loaded in tension at the right end with P = 20000 N.
Segment 1 properties: Length L1 = 500 mm, Diameter d1 = 30 mm, Cross-sectional area A1 = 706.86 mm².
Segment 2 properties: Length L2 = 600 mm, Diameter d2 = 25 mm, Cross-sectional area A2 = 490.87 mm².
Material is 6061-T6 aluminum with Young's modulus E = 69000 MPa.
Calculate (i) normal stress in each segment.
Calculate (ii) total elongation of the bar between the fixed end and load application point.
Enforce common elongation for both segments before combining their stiffnesses.
Assume linear-elastic behavior throughout the calculation.
