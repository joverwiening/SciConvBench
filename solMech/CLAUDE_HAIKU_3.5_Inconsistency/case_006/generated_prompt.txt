The beam deflection will be calculated using the unit-load method with the following specification:

Beam Geometry:
Span length L = 2400 mm.
Simply supported beam with supports at x = 0 and x = 2400 mm.

Material Properties:
Structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Moment of inertia I = 3500000 mm^4.

Loading Condition:
Point load P = 1200 N applied downward at x = 1200 mm.

Deflection Calculation Method:
Use unit-load (virtual work) method.
Compute deflection at load point x = 1200 mm.
Apply formula: δ = ∫[0→L] (M(x)·m(x))/(EI) dx.
Use unit moment at point of interest to generate auxiliary moment diagram.
Treat supports as zero-rotation boundaries in both real and auxiliary systems.

Calculation will involve determining the real bending moment diagram M(x) and the auxiliary moment diagram m(x), then integrating their product divided by EI over the beam length.
