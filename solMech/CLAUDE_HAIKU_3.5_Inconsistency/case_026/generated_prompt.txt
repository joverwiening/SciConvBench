Solve the vertical deflection of a cantilever beam using Castigliano's theorem with the following specifications:

Beam Configuration:
Length L = 1500 mm
Fixed at x = 0
Free at x = L

Material Properties:
Material: AISI 1020 steel
Elastic Modulus E = 200000 MPa
Moment of Inertia I = 800000 mm^4

Loading Condition:
Point load P = 500 N applied at free end

Solution Method:
Determine bending moment M(x) over the beam span
Compute strain energy U = ∫[0→L] M(x)^2/(2EI) dx
Use Castigliano's theorem by differentiating U with respect to P
Calculate vertical deflection at free end
Use N–mm–MPa unit system throughout calculation

Specific Solution Constraints:
Do not use a memorized deflection formula
Explicitly derive solution by integrating and differentiating
