Axial Loading Problem Specification:

Solve the axial loading problem for the prismatic steel bar using the method of sections and equilibrium equations.

Bar Characteristics:
The bar is made of AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Cross-sectional area A = 300 mm^2.
Total bar length divided into three segments: AB = 500 mm, BC = 600 mm, CD = 400 mm.

Boundary and Loading Conditions:
Left end A is fixed (u_A = 0).
Applied forces: 
- At point B: +18000 N (to the right)
- At point C: -12000 N (to the left)
- At point D: +16000 N (to the right)

Solution Methodology:
Use method of sections and equilibrium equations.
Analyze segments systematically from left to right.
Determine internal axial force in each segment (AB, BC, CD).
Calculate normal stress in each segment.
Compute displacement of point D relative to point A.

Solve using step-by-step equilibrium analysis with the given sign convention (forces positive to the right).
