For structural steel, a standard Poisson's ratio is typically around 0.3. 


The shaft is a solid circular steel shaft with the following specifications:
Diameter = 35 mm
Length = 400 mm
Torque = 250000 N·mm
Shear Modulus (G) = 77519.4 MPa
Poisson's ratio = 0.3

Calculation steps:
Compute moment of inertia for torsion: J = π d^4/32
Compute maximum shear stress: τ_max = T c / J (where c = d/2)
Compute angle of twist: φ = TL/(JG), converting result to degrees
