The problem specification is to calculate the transverse shear stress in a rectangular beam cross-section with the following characteristics:

Cross-section width (b) = 60 mm
Cross-section height (h) = 80 mm
Shear force (V) = 16,000 N
Point of interest y = 26.667 mm from neutral axis

Part (a): Maximum Transverse Shear Stress
Compute τ_max using τ_max = 1.5 V/A
Shear stress area (A) = b * h = 60 mm * 80 mm = 4,800 mm²
Maximum shear stress = 1.5 * 16,000 N / 4,800 mm² = 5 N/mm² = 5 MPa

Part (b): Shear Stress at y = 26.667 mm
Moment of Inertia (I) = 320,000 mm⁴
First Moment of Area (Q) = 21,333.6 mm³
Shear stress τ = VQ/(I b) = (16,000 N * 21,333.6 mm³) / (320,000 mm⁴ * 60 mm) = 2.22 MPa
