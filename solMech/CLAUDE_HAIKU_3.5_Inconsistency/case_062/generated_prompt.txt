The thermal stress problem will be solved with the following specification:

The problem involves a prismatic structural steel rod with length L = 1200 mm and diameter d = 20 mm.
The rod is constrained between rigid supports with zero total length change.
The rod experiences a uniform temperature increase of ΔT = 35 °C.
Material properties are: E = 200000 MPa, α = 1e-05 1/°C.
Cross-sectional area A = 314.16 mm².

Thermal stress calculation steps:
Compute free thermal strain as εth = α * ΔT = 1e-05 * 35 = 0.00035.
Since length change is constrained to zero, the thermal stress σth will develop.
Thermal stress will be σth = E * εth = 200000 * 0.00035 = 70 MPa.
The thermal stress will be compressive (negative) to prevent rod expansion.
The corresponding axial force will be F = σth * A = 70 * 314.16 = 22,000 N (compressive).

Final results:
Thermal stress: 70 MPa (compressive)
Axial force: 22,000 N (compressive)
