Specification for Stepped Circular Bar Analysis:

Use N-mm-MPa units for all calculations.
Perform analysis using the method of consistent deformation for a linear-elastic stepped bar.
Material properties: Structural steel with E = 200,000 MPa and ν = 0.3.
Bar configuration:
Segment 1: Length L1 = 400 mm, Diameter d1 = 16 mm, Cross-sectional area A1 = 201.06 mm²
Segment 2: Length L2 = 400 mm, Diameter d2 = 40 mm, Cross-sectional area A2 = 1256.64 mm²
Total applied load P = 25,000 N
Fixed at left end, loaded in tension at right end.

Solution method steps:
Calculate cross-sectional areas for both segments.
Assume a common elongation for both segments.
Calculate stress in each segment using the common elongation.
Verify axial force consistency across segments.
Adjust elongation to ensure force equilibrium.
Compute total bar elongation.

Deliverables:
Determine normal stress in Segment 1.
Determine normal stress in Segment 2.
Calculate total bar elongation between fixed end and load application point.
