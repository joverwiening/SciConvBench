The vertical AISI 1020 steel rod will be analyzed with the following specifications:

Rod length is 1500 mm.
Rod diameter is 16 mm.
Cross-sectional area A is 201.06 mm^2.
Young's modulus E is 200000 MPa.
Weight W is 800 N.
Drop height h is 150 mm.
Peak force P_max is 1600 N.
Maximum displacement δ_max will be calculated using δ_max = P_max L/(AE).
Maximum normal stress σ_max will be computed as σ_max = P_max/A.
Units will be N–mm–MPa.
The bar will be treated as a linear axial spring.
The mass of the bar will be neglected.
The suddenly applied-load result P_max = 2W will be used for the peak force.

Calculation steps:
δ_max = (1600 N * 1500 mm) / (201.06 mm^2 * 200000 MPa) = 0.0598 mm
σ_max = 1600 N / 201.06 mm^2 = 7.96 MPa
