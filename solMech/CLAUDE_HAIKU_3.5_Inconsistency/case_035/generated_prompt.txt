Determine the minimum column diameter for a 6061-T6 aluminum column with the following specifications:

Column length L = 2200 mm
Material: 6061-T6 aluminum
Elastic modulus E = 69000 MPa
Applied compressive load P = 25000 N
Factor of safety n = 2.5
Effective length factor K = 2.0
Column orientation: Vertical
End condition: One end fixed, one end pinned

Calculation method:
Use Euler buckling formula P_cr = π^2 E I/(K L)^2
Moment of inertia I = π d^4/64
Requirement: P_cr ≥ n·P

Solve for minimum diameter d that satisfies the buckling safety requirement.
