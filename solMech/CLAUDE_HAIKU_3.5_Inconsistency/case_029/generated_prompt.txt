The specification for the brass rod stress and strain analysis is as follows:

Use N–mm–MPa units with linear elastic isotropic behavior.
The brass rod has an initial diameter of 20 mm and a length of 1000 mm.
Apply an axial tensile load of 25000 N through the centroid.
Use Young's modulus E = 105000 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.34.
Assume plane strain in the two transverse directions with zero lateral strain.

Calculation Results:
Cross-sectional area A = 314.16 mm².
Axial stress σ = 79.57 MPa.
Axial strain ε = 0.000758 or 0.0758%.
Lateral strain = 0.
Change in diameter Δd = 0.01516 mm.

Calculations follow standard linear elastic mechanics formulas for stress, strain, and deformation under plane strain conditions.
