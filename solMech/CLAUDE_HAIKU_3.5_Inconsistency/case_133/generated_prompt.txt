The beam is a simply supported beam with total length 6000 mm.
A concentrated load of 20000 N acts downward at 2000 mm from support A.
The cross-section of interest is located 4500 mm from support A.
Use sign convention: positive shear V is upward on the left cut face, and positive bending moment M causes sagging (tension at the bottom fibers).
Units for shear force V are N, and units for bending moment M are N·mm.

Shear Force V Calculation:
For x = 4500 mm, first divide the beam into two regions:
Region 1 (0 to 2000 mm): Reaction forces only
Region 2 (2000 to 4500 mm): Reaction forces and load effect

V = (20000 N * (4500 mm - 2000 mm)) / 6000 mm * (6000 mm - 4500 mm)
V = 5000 N (upward, positive by convention)

Bending Moment M Calculation:
M = (20000 N * (4500 mm - 2000 mm) * (6000 mm - 4500 mm)) / 6000 mm
M = 7500000 N·mm (sagging, positive by convention)
