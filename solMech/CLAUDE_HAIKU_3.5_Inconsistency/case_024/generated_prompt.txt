Cross-section: Rectangular beam with width (b) = 50 mm and height (h) = 200 mm
Fixed at x = 0 (left end)

Solution Method:
Use Euler-Bernoulli beam theory with the resultant load (wL) applied at midspan.

Calculation Steps:
Determine the maximum deflection at the free end.
Determine the slope at the free end.
Use N-mm-MPa units throughout the calculation.
Apply the distributed load's resultant force at the midspan as specified.
