The specification for solving the stepped circular bar problem is as follows:

Use the method of consistent deformation to analyze the AISI 1020 steel stepped bar.
Solve the problem using N-mm-MPa units.
Assume linear-elastic behavior for the material.
Use the given material properties: E = 200,000 MPa.
Calculate the problem in two main steps:
Determine the normal stress in each segment of the bar.
Calculate the total elongation of the bar between the fixed end and load application point.
Enforce a common elongation for both segments before combining their stiffnesses.
Use the given bar dimensions: 
Segment 1: Length L1 = 300 mm, Diameter d1 = 25 mm
Segment 2: Length L2 = 1000 mm, Diameter d2 = 35 mm
Apply the total axial load P = 30,000 N at the right end of the bar.
Ensure the same axial force passes through both segments.
Verify that the normal stress is identical in both segments.
