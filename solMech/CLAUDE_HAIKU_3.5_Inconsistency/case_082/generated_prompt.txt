I will now calculate the maximum bending normal stress using the flexure formula σ = M y / I.

Given:
- Moment (M) = 3,000,000 N·mm
- Moment of inertia (I) = 416,666.67 mm⁴
- Distance from neutral axis to extreme fiber (y) = 50 mm

σ = (3,000,000 N·mm * 50 mm) / 416,666.67 mm⁴
  = 360 N/mm² = 360 MPa

For a sagging moment, the top fiber is in compression and the bottom fiber is in tension.


The maximum bending normal stress is 360 MPa.
The stress is compressive at the top fiber.
The stress is tensile at the bottom fiber.
The stress varies linearly across the cross-section depth.
The stress distribution is symmetric about the neutral axis.
