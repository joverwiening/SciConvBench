Q: What is the cross-sectional area of the column? I need this to complete the Euler buckling calculation.
A: Make a reasonable assumption based on best practices. The complete requirement does not specify the cross-sectional area, and it is not needed for the Euler critical buckling load calculation as shown in the formula P_cr = π^2 E I / (K L)^2.
