Perform torsion analysis for a hollow circular AISI 1020 steel shaft with the following specifications:

Shaft Geometry:
Outer diameter (do) = 60 mm
Inner diameter (di) = 48 mm
Shaft length (L) = 800 mm

Loading Conditions:
Applied Torque (T) = 450000 N·mm
Shear Modulus (G) = 76923.1 MPa

Calculation Method:
Use thin-walled closed tube torsion analysis with mean-area shear-flow relation

Polar Moment of Inertia Calculation:
Use formula J = π(do^4 − di^4)/32

Computational Requirements:
Calculate (i) maximum shear stress at outer surface
Calculate (ii) angle of twist over shaft length

Output Specifications:
Report maximum shear stress in N/mm²
Report angle of twist in radians
Use 2 decimal places for both results

Calculation Assumptions:
Assume linear elastic behavior
Use standard thin-walled tube torsion analysis principles
