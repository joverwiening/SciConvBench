The column will be designed with the following specifications:

Geometry:
The column is a solid circular cross section.
Column length L = 1800 mm.
Effective length factor K = 1.0.

Material Properties:
Material is structural steel.
Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.

Loading Conditions:
Compressive load P = 30000 N.
Factor of safety n = 2.0.

Buckling Calculation Method:
Use Euler buckling formula.
Calculate critical load P_cr using P_cr = π^2 E I/(K L)^2.
Moment of inertia I = π d^4/64.
Requirement: P_cr ≥ n·P.

Design Goal:
Determine the minimum required diameter d for the column.

Calculation Approach:
Solve for diameter d that satisfies the buckling safety requirement.
Use N-mm-MPa unit system for calculations.
