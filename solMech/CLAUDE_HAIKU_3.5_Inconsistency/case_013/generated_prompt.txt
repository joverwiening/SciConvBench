Use Euler-Bernoulli beam theory for deflection calculations.
Use superposition method to combine deflection contributions.

Beam material is structural steel with Young's modulus (E) = 200000 MPa.
Beam moment of inertia (I) = 2000000 mm^4.
Uniform distributed load (w) = 0.3 N/mm over full span.
Point load (P) = 3000 N applied at midspan.
Calculate deflection at midspan by:
First, compute deflection due to uniform distributed load using wL/8 formula.
Then, compute deflection due to point load at midspan using PL^3/48 formula.
Sum the two deflection contributions to get total midspan deflection.
