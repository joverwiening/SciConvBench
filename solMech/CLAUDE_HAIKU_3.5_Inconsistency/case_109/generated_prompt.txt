A straight, prismatic circular rod made of 6061-T6 aluminum will be analyzed under tension.
The rod has a diameter of 20 mm and a gage length of 800 mm.
The cross-sectional area is 314.16 mm².
An axial force of 15000 N is applied through the centroid.
Young's modulus E is 69000 MPa.
The yield strength is 275 MPa.

Calculated Results:
Axial normal stress (σ) is 47.7 MPa.
Elastic normal strain (ε) is 0.000691 or 0.0691%.
Axial elongation (δ) is 0.553 mm.
Factor of safety (n) is 5.76.

The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.
The factor of safety is calculated as the yield strength divided by the average normal stress.
The rod is safe from yielding, with a factor of safety significantly above 1.
