The specification for the thermal stress and axial force problem is:

Use N–mm–MPa units.
Assume linear elastic behavior and uniform temperature change.
The rod is made of AISI 1020 steel.
Rod length is 1200 mm.
Rod diameter is 35 mm.
The rod is placed between rigid, unyielding supports.
Total change in length is constrained to be zero.
Uniform temperature increase is 65 °C.
Young's modulus (E) is 200000 MPa.
Coefficient of thermal expansion (α) is 1e-05 1/°C.
Determine the thermal stress that develops in the rod.
Indicate whether the thermal stress is tensile or compressive.
Calculate the corresponding axial force carried by the rod.
