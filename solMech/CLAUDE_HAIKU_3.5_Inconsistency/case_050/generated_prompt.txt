The specification for analyzing the torsion of a brass hollow circular shaft is as follows:

Use N-mm-MPa units for all calculations.
Assume linear elastic material behavior for brass.
Use the standard analytical method for torsion of thin-walled hollow circular shafts.
Shaft dimensions: 
Outer diameter (do) = 40 mm
Inner diameter (di) = 30 mm
Shaft length (L) = 1000 mm
Torque (T) = 300000 N·mm
Shear modulus (G) = 39179.1 MPa

Calculate maximum shear stress using the formula: τ = Tr/J
Calculate polar moment of inertia using: J = π(do^4 − di^4)/32
Calculate angle of twist using: θ = TL/GJ
Report results for:
(i) Maximum shear stress at the outer surface
(ii) Angle of twist over the shaft length
