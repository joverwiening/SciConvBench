Axial Loading Problem Specification:

The problem involves analyzing a prismatic steel bar with the following specifications:

Bar Geometry:
Total cross-sectional area is 500 mm^2.
Segment AB length is 300 mm.
Segment BC length is 600 mm.
Segment CD length is 300 mm.

Material Properties:
Material is structural steel.
Young's modulus (E) = 200000 MPa.
Assumed constant temperature with no thermal effects.

Boundary and Loading Conditions:
Left end (point A) is fixed with zero displacement (u_A = 0).
Force at point B is 18000 N (positive to the right).
Force at point C is -12000 N (positive to the right).
Force at point D is 10000 N (positive to the right).

Solution Method:
Use method of sections and equilibrium equations.
Analyze each segment (AB, BC, CD) systematically.
Determine internal axial forces in each segment.
Calculate normal stresses in each segment.
Compute displacement of point D relative to point A.

Sign Convention:
Positive forces and displacements are to the right.
Negative forces and displacements are to the left.
