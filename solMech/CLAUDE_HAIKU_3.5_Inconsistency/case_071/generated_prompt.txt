Compute the angle of twist for a circular shaft with the following specifications:

Shaft diameter is 25 mm.
Shaft length is 1000 mm.
Material is AISI 1020 steel with shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa.
Applied constant torque T = 350000 N·mm.
Use polar moment of inertia J = π d^4/32.
Calculate torsional strain energy U using the integral U = ∫[0→L] T^2/(2GJ) dx.
Determine the angle of twist φ by applying Castigliano's theorem: φ = ∂U/∂T.
Use N–mm–MPa units for all calculations.
