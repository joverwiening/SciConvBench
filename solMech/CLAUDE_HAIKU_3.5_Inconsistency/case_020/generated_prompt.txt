Specification for Shaft Torque and Stress Analysis:

Analyze a solid circular shaft with the following characteristics:
Shaft diameter = 35 mm
Total shaft length = 1200 mm
Fixed at x = 0
First torque T1 = 200,000 N·mm applied at x = L (1200 mm)
Second torque T2 = -250,000 N·mm applied at x = 800 mm
Material: Structural steel with G = 77,519.4 MPa

Analysis Requirements:
Compute internal torque in two segments: 0→800 mm and 800→1200 mm
Calculate maximum shear stress in each segment
Determine total angle of twist at x = 1200 mm relative to the fixed end
Use algebraic sum of applied torques for internal torque calculation
Compute twist by summing contributions from each segment

Computational Method:
Use N-mm-MPa unit system
Assume circular cross-section with constant diameter
Consider signed torque values for internal torque calculation
