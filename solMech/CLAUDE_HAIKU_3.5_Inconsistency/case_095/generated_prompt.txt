The shaft analysis specification is as follows:

The shaft is a stepped solid circular steel shaft with two segments.
Segment AB has length 400 mm and diameter 30 mm.
Segment BC has length 700 mm and diameter 30 mm.
Total applied torque is 600,000 N·mm at end C.
Shear modulus G is 77,519.4 MPa.

Torque distribution:
Segment AB receives 382,500 N·mm.
Segment BC receives 217,500 N·mm.

Maximum shear stress results:
Segment AB maximum shear stress is 182.5 MPa.
Segment BC maximum shear stress is 104.0 MPa.
Segment AB governs the design due to higher shear stress.

The total angle of twist at C relative to A will be the sum of individual segment twists.
