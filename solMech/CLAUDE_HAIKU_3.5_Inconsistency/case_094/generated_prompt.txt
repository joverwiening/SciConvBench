The shaft analysis specification is as follows:

The shaft is a stepped solid circular shaft made of 6061-T6 aluminum with two segments.
Segment AB has length 400 mm, diameter 25 mm, and carries 383,250 N·mm of torque.
Segment BC has length 900 mm, diameter 30 mm, and carries 366,750 N·mm of torque.
Shear modulus G is 25,939.8 MPa.

Maximum shear stress calculations:
Segment AB maximum shear stress is 1,562.5 MPa.
Segment BC maximum shear stress is 833.3 MPa.
Segment AB governs the design due to higher shear stress.

Torque distribution was calculated proportionally based on torsional stiffnesses:
Segment AB: 51.1% of total torque
Segment BC: 48.9% of total torque

The analysis used N-mm-MPa units throughout the calculation.
