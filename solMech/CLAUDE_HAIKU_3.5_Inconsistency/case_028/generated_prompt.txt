The specification for the rod impact analysis is as follows:

Use N-mm-MPa units for calculations.
The rod is a vertical structural steel rod with length L = 1200 mm.
The rod diameter is d = 30 mm.
The rod's cross-sectional area A = 706.86 mm².
The Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The rod is fixed at its top end.
A weight W = 500 N is dropped onto a collar at the bottom end.
The drop height is h = 200 mm.
Use energy conservation equation: W(h + δ_max) = (P_max^2 L)/(2AE)
Use the suddenly applied-load result P_max = 2W for the peak force.
Compute the maximum axial force P_max.
Compute the maximum normal stress σ_max = P_max/A.
Neglect the mass of the bar during the analysis.
Assume the bar behaves like a linear axial spring.
