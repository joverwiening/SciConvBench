Specification for Column Strength Analysis:

Perform a two-part analysis of a structural steel column with the following parameters:
Use N–mm–MPa units throughout the calculation.
Column diameter is 25 mm.
Column length is 2000 mm.
Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Effective length factor K = 0.7.
Applied compressive load P = 25000 N.
Yield strength Sy = 250 MPa.

Part (a): Compute Average Compressive Stress
Calculate the cross-sectional area A using the column diameter.
Compute average compressive stress σ_avg = P/A.
Compare σ_avg to the yield strength Sy of 250 MPa.

Part (b): Compute Euler Critical Buckling Load
Calculate moment of inertia I = π d^4/64.
Calculate polar moment J = π d^4/32.
Compute Euler critical buckling load P_cr using the formula P_cr = π^2 E I/(K L)^2.
Compare P_cr to the applied load P.

Determine which limit state (yielding or Euler buckling) is more critical based on the calculations.
