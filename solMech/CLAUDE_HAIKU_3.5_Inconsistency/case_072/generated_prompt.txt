The specification for calculating the angle of twist for the aluminum shaft is as follows:

Use N-mm-MPa units for all calculations.
Use a circular shaft with diameter d = 30 mm and length L = 1000 mm.
Use material properties of 6061-T6 aluminum with shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa.
Apply a constant torque T = 450000 N·mm.
Calculate the polar moment J using the formula J = π d^4/32.
Calculate the moment of inertia I using the formula I = π d^4/64.
Compute the torsional strain energy U using the definite integral U = ∫[0→L] T^2/(2GJ) dx.
Apply Castigliano's theorem to determine the angle of twist φ by taking the partial derivative ∂U/∂T.
Calculate the absolute angle of twist between the shaft's ends using the full shaft length as the reference.
Report the final angle of twist in radians.
