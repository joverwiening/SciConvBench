A straight, prismatic circular rod made of structural steel will be analyzed under tension.
The rod has a diameter of 25 mm and a gage length of 800 mm.
The rod is loaded by an axial force of 20000 N applied through the centroid.
The cross-sectional area is 490.87 mm².
The average normal stress (σ_avg) is 40.74 MPa.
The elastic normal strain (ε) is 0.0002037 or 0.2037 mm/mm.
The axial elongation of the gage length is 0.163 mm.
Young's modulus (E) is 200000 MPa.
The yield strength (S_y) is 250 MPa.
The factor of safety (n) is calculated as S_y / σ_avg = 250 MPa / 40.74 MPa = 6.14.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior of structural steel.
