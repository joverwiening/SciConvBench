The beam is a simply supported beam with total length 4000 mm.
The concentrated load P is 12000 N acting downward.
The load is located 2000 mm from support A.
The cross-section of interest is located 1000 mm from support A.
Use positive shear V upward on the left cut face.
Use positive bending moment M causing sagging (tension at the bottom fibers).
Use N and N·mm as units for V and M respectively.

Shear Force V Calculation:
For x = 1000 mm, the shear force will be V = (12000 N × 1000 mm) / 4000 mm = 3000 N upward.

Bending Moment M Calculation:
For x = 1000 mm, the bending moment will be M = (12000 N × 1000 mm × (4000 mm - 1000 mm)) / (4000 mm) = 9000 N·mm (sagging, tension at bottom fibers).

Final Results:
Shear Force V = 3000 N
Bending Moment M = 9000 N·mm
