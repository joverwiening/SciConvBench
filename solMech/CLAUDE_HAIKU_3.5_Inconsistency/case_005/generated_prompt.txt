Axial Bar Deformation Problem Specification:

The problem involves a prismatic steel bar with the following characteristics:
Cross-sectional area A = 200 mm^2
Elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa
Total bar segments:
- AB segment: 700 mm long
- BC segment: 600 mm long
- CD segment: 500 mm long

Applied forces:
- At point B: +18000 N
- At point C: -8000 N
- At point D: +20000 N

Solution Method:
- Use method of sections for internal force analysis
- Use method of consistent deformations for displacement calculation
- Sign convention: 
  * Positive forces are to the right
  * Tension is positive
  * Compression is negative
- Left end (point A) is fixed (u_A = 0)
- Solve for:
  1. Internal axial force in each segment (AB, BC, CD)
  2. Normal stress in each segment
  3. Displacement of point D relative to point A

Solve using equilibrium equations and axial deformation principles with N–mm–MPa units.
