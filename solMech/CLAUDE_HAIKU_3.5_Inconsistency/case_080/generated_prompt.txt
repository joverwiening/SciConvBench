The specification for computing engineering stress, strain, and elastic modulus is as follows:

Use N–mm–MPa units for all calculations.
The initial specimen diameter is 12.5 mm.
The initial gage length is 50 mm.
The initial cross-sectional area is 122.72 mm².
The applied axial load is 12000 N.
The elongation is 0.12 mm.
The current elongated length is 50.12 mm.
The current cross-sectional area is 122.40 mm².
Calculate engineering stress by dividing the applied axial load by the current cross-sectional area.
The engineering stress is 98.04 MPa.
Calculate engineering strain by dividing the change in length by the original gage length.
The engineering strain is 0.0024 or 0.24%.
Calculate the modulus of elasticity (E) by dividing the engineering stress by the engineering strain.
The modulus of elasticity (E) is 40,850 MPa.
