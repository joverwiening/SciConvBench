Determine the slope and deflection of a cantilever beam with the following specifications:

Beam Length: 1200 mm
Material: Structural Steel
Young's Modulus (E): 200000 MPa
Second Moment of Area (I): 2500000 mm^4
Point Load (P): 800 N applied at free end
Beam Theory: Euler-Bernoulli (small deflection, linear elastic)

Calculation Requirements:
Use Euler-Bernoulli beam theory to calculate:
(i) Slope at free end: Report in radians, rounded to 4 decimal places
(ii) Vertical deflection at free end: Report in mm, rounded to 2 decimal places

Boundary Conditions:
- Fixed at x = 0
- Free at x = L
- Free-end rotation set to zero
- Use constant moment P L for deflection calculation

Calculation Method:
Apply standard Euler-Bernoulli beam theory equations for slope and deflection under point load at free end.
