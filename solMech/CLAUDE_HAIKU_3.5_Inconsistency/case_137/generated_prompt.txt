The specification for the stress analysis of a circular shaft is as follows:

Use N-mm-MPa units for all calculations.
Analyze the stress state at the outer surface of a circular shaft with diameter d = 45 mm.
Apply a bending moment M = 2,000,000 N·mm and a torque T = 500,000 N·mm at the critical section.
Assume the torsional shear stress vanishes at the outer surface where bending normal stress is maximum.

Compute the maximum bending normal stress using the formula σ = M c / I.
Calculate the moment of inertia I = π d^4/64.
Use the distance from neutral axis c = d/2.

Compute the torsional shear stress using the formula τ = T c / J.
Calculate the polar moment of inertia J = π d^4/32.

Treat the surface stress state as plane stress with:
σx = σ (from bending)
σy = 0
τxy = τ (from torsion)

Determine the principal stresses and maximum in-plane shear stress based on the plane stress condition.
