Beam Deflection and Slope Calculation Specification:

Beam Geometry:
Span length (L) = 3000 mm
Point load (P) = 1500 N
Load location = Midspan (x = L/2 = 1500 mm)

Material Properties:
Material: AISI 1020 steel
Young's Modulus (E) = 200,000 MPa
Moment of Inertia (I) = 3,500,000 mm^4

Theoretical Approach:
Use Euler-Bernoulli beam theory
Neglect shear deformation
Assume standard sign convention (positive bending moment causes top fiber compression)
Origin (x = 0) at left support

Calculation Requirements:
Compute maximum deflection at midspan
Compute slope at beam supports

Solution Method:
Derive using differential equation EI v'' = M(x)
Apply boundary conditions:
- Slope vanishes at both supports
- Slope vanishes at midspan
- Use integration method to determine deflection and slope

Output Units:
Deflection in mm
Slope in radians
