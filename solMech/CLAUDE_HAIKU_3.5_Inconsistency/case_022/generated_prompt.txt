The specification for the torsional analysis of a stepped circular shaft is as follows:

Use N-mm-MPa units for all calculations.

The shaft consists of two segments in series:
Segment AB: Length = 300 mm, Diameter = 25 mm
Segment BC: Length = 700 mm, Diameter = 30 mm

Torque application conditions:
End A is fixed.
Total applied torque at end C is 450,000 N·mm.
Shear modulus G = 77,519.4 MPa.

Torque distribution:
Segment AB carries 234,000 N·mm (52% of total torque).
Segment BC carries 216,000 N·mm (48% of total torque).

Maximum shear stress results:
Segment AB maximum shear stress: 24.2 MPa
Segment BC maximum shear stress: 21.2 MPa
Segment AB governs the maximum shear stress.

Total angle of twist at C relative to A:
Segment AB twist: 0.296 radians
Segment BC twist: 0.684 radians
Total twist: 0.98 radians
