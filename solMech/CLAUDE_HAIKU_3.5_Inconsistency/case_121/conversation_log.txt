Q: What is the stress formula you want me to use for calculating the bending stress?
A: Use the standard pure bending stress formula:

σ = (M * y) / I

Where:
- M is the bending moment (4,000,000 N·mm)
- y is the distance from the neutral axis
- I is the moment of inertia of the cross-section
