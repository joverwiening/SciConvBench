The cantilever beam has length L = 800 mm and is fixed at one end with the other end free.
A downward point load P = 2500 N is applied at the free end of the beam.
The beam is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with elastic modulus E = 69000 MPa.
The beam has constant second moment of area I = 1600000 mm^4 about the bending axis.
The beam follows Euler-Bernoulli beam theory assumptions including plane sections remain plane and small deflections.
The governing differential equation is EI d^4v/dx^4 = q(x) where v is the transverse deflection and q(x) is the distributed load.
For this point load case, the moment equation is EI d^2v/dx^2 = M(x) where M(x) is the bending moment.
The bending moment varies linearly from M = 0 at the free end to M = -PL at the fixed end.
The boundary conditions are v(0) = 0 and dv/dx(0) = 0 at the fixed end, and M(L) = 0 and V(L) = P at the free end.
The maximum deflection occurs at the free end and equals v_max = PL^3/(3EI).
The maximum bending stress occurs at the fixed end and equals σ_max = PL*c/I where c is the distance from neutral axis to extreme fiber.
The deflection at any point x from the fixed end is v(x) = P/(6EI) * x^2 * (3L - x).
The slope at any point x from the fixed end is dv/dx = P/(2EI) * x * (2L - x).
