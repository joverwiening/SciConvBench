The structural member is a rectangular adhesive layer with area A = 4000 mm² and thickness t = 20 mm.
The adhesive layer is subjected to a uniform in-plane shear force V = 12000 N applied parallel to the faces of the layer.
The adhesive material is isotropic with shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The shear stress is uniform across the cross-section and calculated as τ = V/A.
The shear stress in the adhesive layer is τ = 12000 N / 4000 mm² = 3.0 MPa.
The shear strain is related to shear stress by Hooke's law for shear: γ = τ/G.
The shear strain in the adhesive layer is γ = 3.0 MPa / 39179.1 MPa = 7.66 × 10⁻⁵.
The shear deformation (displacement) is calculated as δ = γ × t.
The shear deformation of the adhesive layer is δ = 7.66 × 10⁻⁵ × 20 mm = 1.53 × 10⁻³ mm.
The governing relation for linear elastic shear behavior is τ = Gγ.
The compatibility relation for small deformations is γ = δ/t.
The equilibrium condition requires that the applied shear force equals the internal shear force resistance.
