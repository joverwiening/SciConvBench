The rod is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with circular cross-section.
The rod has initial diameter d0 = 40 mm and length L = 500 mm.
An axial tensile load P = 35000 N is applied through the centroid.
The analysis focuses on the Poisson effect under uniaxial tension.
Use small-deformation theory and linear-elastic material behavior.
The material properties for 6061-T6 aluminum are E = 69 GPa and ν = 0.33.
Calculate the axial stress as σ = P/A where A = π(d0/2)².
Calculate the axial strain as εaxial = σ/E.
Calculate the lateral strain as εlateral = -ν × εaxial.
Calculate the change in diameter as Δd = εlateral × d0.
Calculate the change in length as ΔL = εaxial × L.
The final diameter is d = d0 + Δd.
The final length is L_final = L + ΔL.
Verify that the deformation remains within the small-deformation assumption.
Present the axial stress, axial strain, lateral strain, dimensional changes, and final dimensions as the response quantities.
