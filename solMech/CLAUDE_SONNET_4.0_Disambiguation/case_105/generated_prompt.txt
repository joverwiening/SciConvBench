The vertical bar has length L = 3500 mm and cross-sectional area A = 200 mm².
The material has density ρ = 7850 kg/m³ and elastic modulus E = 69000 MPa.
The bar is fixed at the top and free at the bottom.
The bar is subjected only to its own self-weight acting downward.
Use small-deformation theory and linear-elastic material behavior.
The axial stress at distance x from the top is σ(x) = ρg(L-x) where g = 9.81 m/s².
The axial strain at distance x from the top is ε(x) = σ(x)/E = ρg(L-x)/E.
The displacement at distance x from the top is u(x) = ∫[0 to x] ε(ξ)dξ = (ρg/E)[Lx - x²/2].
The maximum elongation occurs at the free end and equals δ = u(L) = ρgL²/(2E).
The maximum stress occurs at the fixed top and equals σ_max = ρgL.
The maximum strain occurs at the fixed top and equals ε_max = ρgL/E.
