The prismatic bar has a rectangular cross section with width b = 30 mm and height h = 100 mm.
At the analyzed cross section, there is an internal normal force P = 50000 N in tension.
At the analyzed cross section, there is an internal transverse shear force V = 5000 N acting parallel to the width direction.
The material follows small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.
The cross section remains plane after deformation (Bernoulli-Euler assumption).
Shear stress distribution is parabolic across the height for rectangular sections.
The cross-sectional area A = b × h = 30 mm × 100 mm = 3000 mm².
The average normal stress is σ_avg = P/A = 50000 N / 3000 mm² = 16.67 MPa.
The average shear stress is τ_avg = V/A = 5000 N / 3000 mm² = 1.67 MPa.
The maximum shear stress occurs at the neutral axis and equals τ_max = 1.5 × τ_avg = 2.5 MPa.
Normal stress is uniformly distributed across the cross section under pure axial loading.
Shear stress varies parabolically with maximum at the centroid and zero at the top and bottom edges.
