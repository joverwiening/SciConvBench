The rod is cylindrical with initial diameter d₀ = 20 mm and length L = 1000 mm.
The rod is made of isotropic, linear-elastic material with Young's modulus E = 105000 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.34.
An axial tensile load P = 25000 N is applied through the centroid of the rod.
The analysis assumes small deformations and linear-elastic behavior.
The initial cross-sectional area is A₀ = π(d₀/2)² = π(10 mm)² = 314.16 mm².
The axial stress is σ = P/A₀ = 25000 N / 314.16 mm² = 79.58 MPa.
The axial strain is ε_axial = σ/E = 79.58 MPa / 105000 MPa = 0.000758.
The lateral strain due to Poisson effect is ε_lateral = -ν × ε_axial = -0.34 × 0.000758 = -0.000258.
The axial elongation is ΔL = ε_axial × L = 0.000758 × 1000 mm = 0.758 mm.
The change in diameter is Δd = ε_lateral × d₀ = -0.000258 × 20 mm = -0.00515 mm.
The final diameter is d_final = d₀ + Δd = 20 mm - 0.00515 mm = 19.995 mm.
The final length is L_final = L + ΔL = 1000 mm + 0.758 mm = 1000.758 mm.
The governing relations are Hooke's law σ = Eε and the Poisson effect ε_lateral = -νε_axial.
