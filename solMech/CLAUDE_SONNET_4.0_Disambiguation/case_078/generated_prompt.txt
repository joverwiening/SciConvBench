The shaft is a solid circular cross-section subjected to pure torsion.
The material is structural steel with shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.
The applied torque is T = 800000 N·mm.
The total length is L = 1200 mm.
The maximum allowable angle of twist is φ_allow = 1.0° over the total length.
The angle of twist formula for a solid circular shaft is φ = TL/(GJ) where J is the polar moment of inertia.
For a solid circular shaft, the polar moment of inertia is J = πd⁴/32 where d is the diameter.
The design constraint is φ ≤ φ_allow, which gives TL/(GJ) ≤ φ_allow.
Convert the allowable angle to radians: φ_allow = 1.0° × π/180 = 0.01745 rad.
Substituting the polar moment formula: TL/(G × πd⁴/32) ≤ 0.01745.
Rearranging for diameter: d⁴ ≥ 32TL/(πG × φ_allow).
Substituting values: d⁴ ≥ 32 × 800000 × 1200/(π × 77519.4 × 10⁶ × 0.01745).
Calculate: d⁴ ≥ 30720000000/(4.2426 × 10⁶) = 7241.9 mm⁴.
Therefore: d ≥ (7241.9)^(1/4) = 9.24 mm.
The minimum required shaft diameter is d_min = 9.24 mm.
For practical design, select a standard diameter slightly larger than the calculated minimum.
