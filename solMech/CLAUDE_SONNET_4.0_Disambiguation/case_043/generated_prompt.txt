The column has a diameter of 20 mm and length of 2000 mm.
The column has pinned-pinned boundary conditions with effective length factor K = 1.0.
The column is made of structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The desired factor of safety is n = 2.0.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The analysis assumes the column is perfectly straight with no initial imperfections.
The analysis assumes the load is applied concentrically along the column axis.
The analysis assumes the material is homogeneous and isotropic.
Calculate the second moment of area as I = π*d^4/64 = π*(20)^4/64 = 7854 mm^4.
Calculate the critical buckling load using Euler's formula: P_cr = π^2*E*I/(K*L)^2.
Substitute values: P_cr = π^2*200000*7854/(1.0*2000)^2 = 3869 N.
Calculate the allowable column load as P_allow = P_cr/n = 3869/2.0 = 1935 N.
The factor of safety with respect to buckling is n = P_cr/P_applied.
For any applied load P_applied, verify that P_applied ≤ P_allow to maintain the required safety factor.
The governing relation is Euler's buckling formula: P_cr = π^2*E*I/(K*L)^2.
The allowable load criterion is: P_allow = P_cr/n where n is the factor of safety.
