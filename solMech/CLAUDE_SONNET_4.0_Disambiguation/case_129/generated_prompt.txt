The beam is made of AISI 1020 steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The beam has a second moment of area I = 1200000 mm^4 about the neutral axis.
A bending moment M = 3000000 N·mm is applied to the beam.
The moment is assumed to cause positive curvature (compression on top fiber, tension on bottom fiber).
The beam behaves according to small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics theory.
The material follows Hooke's law with constant elastic modulus throughout the cross-section.
Plane sections remain plane after bending (Euler-Bernoulli beam theory applies).
The curvature κ is calculated using the moment-curvature relationship: κ = M/(EI).
Substituting values: κ = 3000000/(200000 × 1200000) = 1.25 × 10^-5 mm^-1.
The radius of curvature ρ is the reciprocal of curvature: ρ = 1/κ.
Therefore: ρ = 1/(1.25 × 10^-5) = 80000 mm = 80 m.
The beam curves with a radius of 80 meters under the applied bending moment.
