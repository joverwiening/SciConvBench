The analysis considers a simply supported beam under uniformly distributed transverse loading with small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.

Given parameters: uniformly distributed load w = 0.3 N/mm acting downward over full span, beam length L = 4500 mm, second moment of area I = 2000000 mm^4, Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.

Additional assumptions: beam is prismatic with constant cross-section, material is homogeneous and isotropic, shear deformation is neglected, beam theory applies with plane sections remaining plane.

Governing differential equation: EI d^4v/dx^4 = w, where v(x) is the transverse deflection and x is the coordinate along the beam length.

Boundary conditions: v(0) = 0, v(L) = 0, M(0) = 0, M(L) = 0, where M is the bending moment.

Maximum deflection occurs at midspan: v_max = 5wL^4/(384EI) = 5(0.3)(4500)^4/(384(200000)(2000000)) = 0.896 mm.

Deflection equation: v(x) = (w/(24EI))[x(L^3 - 2Lx^2 + x^3)].

Slope equation: dv/dx = (w/(24EI))[L^3 - 6Lx^2 + 4x^3].

Maximum slope occurs at supports: |dv/dx|_max = wL^3/(24EI) = (0.3)(4500)^3/(24(200000)(2000000)) = 0.000569 radians.

The bending moment equation: M(x) = (w/2)[Lx - x^2].

Maximum bending moment occurs at midspan: M_max = wL^2/8 = (0.3)(4500)^2/8 = 759375 N·mm.
