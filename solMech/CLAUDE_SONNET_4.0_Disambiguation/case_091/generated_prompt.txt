The prismatic bar has a constant circular cross-sectional area A = 500 mm².
The total length is 1200 mm, divided into three segments: AB = 300 mm, BC = 600 mm, and CD = 300 mm.
Point loads are applied at B (18000 N, rightward), C (-12000 N, leftward), and D (10000 N, rightward).
The left end A is fixed with displacement u_A = 0.
The right end D is free with no constraints.
The material follows small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior with Young's modulus E.
The governing equation is the equilibrium condition: d(AE du/dx)/dx + f(x) = 0, where f(x) represents distributed body forces.
For each segment with constant properties and no distributed loads, this reduces to AE d²u/dx² = 0.
The solution in each segment is linear: u(x) = C₁x + C₂.
Internal forces are calculated using N(x) = AE du/dx.
Boundary conditions require displacement continuity at internal points B and C.
Force equilibrium at internal points requires the internal force jump to equal the applied point load.
At point B: N_BC - N_AB = 18000 N.
At point C: N_CD - N_BC = -12000 N.
The stress in each segment is σ = N/A.
The strain in each segment is ε = du/dx = N/(AE).
Solve the system using boundary conditions and equilibrium to determine displacements, internal forces, stresses, and strains throughout the bar.
