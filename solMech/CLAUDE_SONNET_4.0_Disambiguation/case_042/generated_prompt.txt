The prismatic bar has length L = 800 mm and cross-sectional area A = 300 mm².
The cross-sectional shape is assumed to be circular for simplicity and uniform stress distribution.
The suddenly applied tensile load is P = 8000 N.
The material is structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The density is assumed to be ρ = 7850 kg/m³ for structural steel.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The load is applied suddenly (step function) at time t = 0.
The bar is assumed to be fixed at one end and free at the loaded end.
The maximum stress occurs due to dynamic amplification from the sudden loading.
For a suddenly applied load, the dynamic amplification factor is 2.0.
The static stress is σ_static = P/A = 8000 N / 300 mm² = 26.67 MPa.
The maximum dynamic stress is σ_max = 2 × σ_static = 53.33 MPa.
The energy method confirms this result through conservation of energy principles.
The kinetic energy at maximum displacement equals the work done by the applied load.
The strain energy at maximum displacement equals twice the static strain energy.
The maximum displacement is twice the static displacement.
The static displacement is δ_static = PL/(AE) = (8000 N × 800 mm)/(300 mm² × 200000 MPa) = 0.107 mm.
The maximum dynamic displacement is δ_max = 2 × δ_static = 0.213 mm.
