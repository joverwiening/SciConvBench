A simply supported beam of span L = 3000 mm is made of structural steel with E = 200000 MPa and constant I = 1500000 mm^4. A point load P = 2000 N is applied downward at midspan.

Using Castigliano’s theorem (bending strain energy), compute the vertical deflection at midspan. You should define M(x) piecewise over 0≤x≤L and integrate U = ∫ M^2/(2EI) dx.
