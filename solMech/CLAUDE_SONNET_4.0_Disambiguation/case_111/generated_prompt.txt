The column is a solid circular steel column with diameter d = 30 mm and length L = 1500 mm.
The material is structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200,000 MPa and assumed yield strength σy = 250 MPa (typical for structural steel).
The column is subjected to axial compressive loading.
Assume the column has pinned-pinned end conditions (effective length factor K = 1.0).
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.
Calculate the area moment of inertia I = πd⁴/64 for the solid circular cross-section.
Calculate the cross-sectional area A = πd²/4.
Calculate the radius of gyration r = √(I/A) = d/4.
Calculate the slenderness ratio λ = KL/r.
Determine the critical Euler buckling load Pcr = π²EI/(KL)².
Apply a factor of safety of 2.5 for buckling to determine the allowable load Pallow = Pcr/2.5.
Calculate the factor of safety against buckling as FS = Pcr/Papplied where Papplied is the actual applied load.
Present the governing Euler buckling equation and all calculated values.
State that this analysis is valid only if the column fails by elastic buckling rather than material yielding.
