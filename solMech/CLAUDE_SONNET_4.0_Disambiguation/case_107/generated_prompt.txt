The analysis involves a simply supported beam under uniformly distributed transverse load with the following specifications:

Beam length L = 2500 mm with simple supports at both ends.
Uniformly distributed load w = 0.7 N/mm acting downward over the entire span.
Cross-sectional second moment of area I = 2000000 mm^4.
Material Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Small-deformation theory applies with linear-elastic material behavior.
The beam is prismatic with constant cross-section along its length.
The load acts perpendicular to the beam axis through the centroid of the cross-section.
Self-weight of the beam is neglected compared to the applied load.
The governing differential equation is EI d^4v/dx^4 = w where v is the transverse deflection.
Boundary conditions are v(0) = 0, v(L) = 0, M(0) = 0, M(L) = 0.
The maximum deflection occurs at midspan and equals 5wL^4/(384EI).
The maximum slope occurs at the supports and equals wL^3/(24EI).
The deflection equation is v(x) = (w/(24EI))[x(L^3 - 2Lx^2 + x^3)].
The slope equation is dv/dx = (w/(24EI))[L^3 - 6Lx^2 + 4x^3].
Calculate numerical values for maximum deflection and slope using the given parameters.
