The analysis determines stress distribution and deformation in a short rectangular column under eccentric axial loading.

The rectangular cross-section has width b = 100 mm and height h = 100 mm.
A compressive axial load P = 90000 N is applied with eccentricity e = 15 mm from the centroid along the width direction.
The column length is assumed to be L = 300 mm based on short column classification where L/h ≤ 3.
Small-deformation and linear-elastic material behavior are assumed.
The material properties use typical structural steel values with elastic modulus E = 200 GPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.3.
Plane sections remain plane after deformation.
The column is assumed to have fixed-fixed end conditions for boundary constraints.
The eccentric load creates combined axial compression and bending about the axis perpendicular to the eccentricity direction.
The governing stress equation is σ = P/A ± M*y/I where M = P*e is the bending moment due to eccentricity.
The cross-sectional area is A = b*h = 10000 mm².
The second moment of area about the bending axis is I = b*h³/12 = 8.333×10⁶ mm⁴.
The maximum tensile stress occurs at y = h/2 = 50 mm from the neutral axis.
The maximum compressive stress occurs at y = -h/2 = -50 mm from the neutral axis.
