The stepped circular shaft consists of two segments with specified dimensions and material properties.
Segment AB has length L1 = 300 mm and diameter d1 = 20 mm.
Segment BC has length L2 = 500 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm.
The shaft material is 6061-T6 aluminum with shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa.
A torque T = 300000 N·mm is applied at end C.
End A is fixed (zero rotation).
The shaft experiences small deformations and behaves as a linear elastic material.
The shaft cross-sections remain plane and circular during deformation.
The material is homogeneous and isotropic throughout both segments.
Shear strain varies linearly from zero at the center to maximum at the outer surface of each segment.
The polar moment of inertia for segment AB is J1 = π(d1)^4/32 = π(20)^4/32 mm^4.
The polar moment of inertia for segment BC is J2 = π(d2)^4/32 = π(30)^4/32 mm^4.
The maximum shear stress in segment AB is τ_max1 = T(d1/2)/J1.
The maximum shear stress in segment BC is τ_max2 = T(d2/2)/J2.
The angle of twist in segment AB is φ1 = TL1/(GJ1).
The angle of twist in segment BC is φ2 = TL2/(GJ2).
The total angle of twist of the shaft is φ_total = φ1 + φ2.
The torque T is constant throughout both segments since no intermediate torques are applied.
