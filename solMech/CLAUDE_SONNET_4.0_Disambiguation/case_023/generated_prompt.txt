The cantilever beam has length L = 2500 mm and is fixed at one end with the other end free.
The beam is made of AISI 1020 steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The second moment of area is I = 1000000 mm^4.
Assume a rectangular cross-section for the beam as this is the most common and practical shape for cantilever applications.
A uniformly distributed transverse load w = 1.0 N/mm acts downward over the entire span of the beam.
Use small-deformation theory with linear-elastic material behavior.
Assume the beam is prismatic with constant cross-section along its length.
Neglect shear deformation effects and use Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.
The coordinate system has x-axis along the beam length with origin at the fixed end and y-axis pointing upward.
The governing differential equation is EI d^4v/dx^4 = w where v(x) is the transverse deflection.
The boundary conditions are v(0) = 0, dv/dx(0) = 0, d^2v/dx^2(L) = 0, and d^3v/dx^3(L) = 0.
Calculate the maximum deflection at the free end using the formula v_max = wL^4/(8EI).
Determine the maximum bending moment at the fixed end using M_max = wL^2/2.
Calculate the maximum bending stress using σ_max = M_max × c/I where c is the distance from neutral axis to extreme fiber.
Present the deflection curve equation v(x) = (w/24EI)(6L^2x^2 - 4Lx^3 + x^4).
