The rod is a circular cross-section AISI 1020 steel bar with initial diameter d0 = 20 mm and length L = 1200 mm.
The rod is subjected to an axial tensile load P = 35000 N applied through the centroid.
The material properties are Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.30.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.
The analysis assumes the rod is homogeneous and isotropic.
The analysis assumes the load is applied uniformly over the cross-section.
The initial cross-sectional area is A0 = π(d0/2)² = π(10 mm)² = 314.16 mm².
The axial stress is σ = P/A0 = 35000 N / 314.16 mm² = 111.41 MPa.
The axial strain is εaxial = σ/E = 111.41 MPa / 200000 MPa = 0.000557.
The lateral strain due to Poisson effect is εlateral = -ν × εaxial = -0.30 × 0.000557 = -0.000167.
The axial elongation is ΔL = εaxial × L = 0.000557 × 1200 mm = 0.668 mm.
The change in diameter is Δd = εlateral × d0 = -0.000167 × 20 mm = -0.00334 mm.
The final diameter is d = d0 + Δd = 20 mm - 0.00334 mm = 19.997 mm.
The final length is L_final = L + ΔL = 1200 mm + 0.668 mm = 1200.668 mm.
