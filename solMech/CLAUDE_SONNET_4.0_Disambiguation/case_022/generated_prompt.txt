The stepped shaft has 2 sections with segment AB having length L1 = 300 mm and diameter d1 = 25 mm, and segment BC having length L2 = 700 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm.
A torque T = 450000 N·mm is applied at end C while end A is fixed.
The shaft material has shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.
The analysis assumes small-deformation linear-elastic solid mechanics with circular cross-sections remaining plane and circular after deformation.
The polar moments of inertia are J1 = π(d1)^4/32 for segment AB and J2 = π(d2)^4/32 for segment BC.
The maximum shear stress in each segment occurs at the outer surface and equals τ_max = Tr/J where r is the outer radius.
For segment AB: τ_max1 = T(d1/2)/J1 and for segment BC: τ_max2 = T(d2/2)/J2.
The angle of twist in each segment is φ = TL/(GJ) where L is the segment length.
For segment AB: φ1 = TL1/(GJ1) and for segment BC: φ2 = TL2/(GJ2).
The total angle of twist of the shaft is φ_total = φ1 + φ2.
The torque T is constant throughout both segments since there are no intermediate applied torques.
The shear strain in each segment is γ = τ/G and the angle of twist per unit length is dφ/dx = T/(GJ).
