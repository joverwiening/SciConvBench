The prismatic bar has a constant cross-sectional area A = 500 mm² and total length of 1400 mm, segmented as AB = 700 mm, BC = 400 mm, and CD = 300 mm.
The cross-sectional shape is assumed to be circular for simplicity and uniform stress distribution.
The bar is made of structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The left end A is fixed with displacement u_A = 0.
The right end D is free to move.
Point loads are applied as follows: 15000 N rightward at point B (700 mm from A), 12000 N leftward at point C (1100 mm from A), and 10000 N rightward at point D (1400 mm from A).
The positive x-direction is defined as rightward.
Small deformation and linear elastic behavior are assumed.
The governing equation is EA(d²u/dx²) = 0 for each segment between loads, where u is axial displacement.
Internal axial forces are constant within each segment: N_AB = 0 N, N_BC = 15000 N, N_CD = 3000 N.
Axial stresses are calculated as σ = N/A for each segment.
Axial strains are calculated as ε = σ/E for each segment.
Displacement compatibility requires continuity at internal points B and C.
Force equilibrium at each load application point must be satisfied.
The displacement field u(x) is determined by integrating the strain-displacement relation and applying boundary and compatibility conditions.
