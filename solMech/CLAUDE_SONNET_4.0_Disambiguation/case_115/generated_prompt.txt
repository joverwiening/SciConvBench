The hollow circular shaft under torsion has outer radius 30 mm, inner radius 22 mm, and length 1200 mm.
The shaft is made of structural steel with shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.
A constant torque T = 600000 N·mm is applied along the shaft length.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior with circular cross-sections remaining plane and circular after deformation.
The polar moment of inertia is J = π(ro⁴ - ri⁴)/2 = π(30⁴ - 22⁴)/2 mm⁴.
The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer radius and is τmax = T·ro/J.
The minimum shear stress in the material occurs at the inner radius and is τmin = T·ri/J.
The angle of twist over the length L is φ = TL/(GJ).
The shear strain varies linearly with radius as γ(r) = τ(r)/G = Tr/(GJ).
The shear stress varies linearly with radius as τ(r) = Tr/J for ri ≤ r ≤ ro.
The shaft experiences pure torsion with no axial or bending effects.
