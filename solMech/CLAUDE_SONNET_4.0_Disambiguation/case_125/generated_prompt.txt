The beam is a rectangular cross-section with width b = 50 mm and height h = 120 mm.
The internal bending moment is M = 4000000 N·mm acting in the sagging direction.
The material follows small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior with Hooke's law.
Plane sections remain plane after bending (Euler-Bernoulli beam theory).
The neutral axis passes through the centroid of the cross-section.
Shear deformations are neglected.
The coordinate system has y measured from the neutral axis, positive upward.
The second moment of area about the neutral axis is I = bh³/12 = 50 × 120³/12 = 7200000 mm⁴.
The bending stress at any fiber location y from the neutral axis is σ = My/I.
For sagging moment, fibers above the neutral axis (positive y) experience compression (negative stress).
For sagging moment, fibers below the neutral axis (negative y) experience tension (positive stress).
The maximum compressive stress occurs at the top fiber (y = h/2 = 60 mm): σ_max_compression = -4000000 × 60/7200000 = -33.33 MPa.
The maximum tensile stress occurs at the bottom fiber (y = -h/2 = -60 mm): σ_max_tension = 4000000 × 60/7200000 = 33.33 MPa.
