The column is made of structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The column length is L = 1800 mm with effective length factor K = 1.0 corresponding to pinned-pinned end conditions.
The applied compressive load is P = 30000 N.
The desired factor of safety against buckling is n = 2.0.
The column has a solid circular cross-section with diameter d to be determined.
Small-deformation and linear-elastic behavior are assumed.
The critical buckling load is given by Euler's formula: P_cr = π²EI/(KL)².
For a solid circular cross-section, the second moment of area is I = πd⁴/64.
The effective length is L_eff = KL = 1.0 × 1800 mm = 1800 mm.
The factor of safety is defined as n = P_cr/P_applied.
Substituting the factor of safety requirement: P_cr = n × P_applied = 2.0 × 30000 N = 60000 N.
The required second moment of area is I = P_cr(KL)²/(π²E) = 60000 × (1800)²/(π² × 200000) = 98.6 mm⁴.
The required diameter is d = (64I/π)^(1/4) = (64 × 98.6/π)^(1/4) = 8.9 mm.
Round up to a practical diameter of d = 10 mm for manufacturing considerations.
Verify the actual factor of safety with d = 10 mm: I = π(10)⁴/64 = 490.9 mm⁴.
The actual critical load is P_cr = π² × 200000 × 490.9/(1800)² = 298800 N.
The actual factor of safety is n_actual = 298800/30000 = 9.96.
The final design uses a solid circular column with diameter d = 10 mm.
