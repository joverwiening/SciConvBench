Use N–mm–MPa units. Assume Euler buckling applies (column is slender and remains elastic).

A straight column made of AISI 1020 steel has length L = 2000 mm and constant flexural rigidity EI with E = 200000 MPa and I = 1200000 mm^4. The end conditions are fixed–pinned, so the effective length factor is K = 0.7.

Compute the Euler critical buckling load P_cr = π^2 E I / (K L)^2. Report P_cr in N and in kN.
