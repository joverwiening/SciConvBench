The rod is made of structural steel with circular cross-section.
Initial diameter is 25 mm and length is 1200 mm.
Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.29.
Axial tensile load P = 45000 N is applied through the centroid.
The material behavior is isotropic, linear-elastic with small deformations.
The rod is assumed to be homogeneous and initially stress-free.
End effects are neglected and plane sections remain plane.
The axial stress is σ = P/A₀ where A₀ is the initial cross-sectional area.
The initial cross-sectional area is A₀ = π(d₀/2)² = π(12.5)² mm².
The axial strain is ε_axial = σ/E = P/(E·A₀).
The lateral strain due to Poisson effect is ε_lateral = -ν·ε_axial.
The change in diameter is Δd = ε_lateral·d₀.
The final diameter is d_final = d₀ + Δd.
The axial elongation is ΔL = ε_axial·L.
The final length is L_final = L + ΔL.
The governing relation for uniaxial tension with Poisson effect is ε_lateral = -ν·ε_axial.
Calculate the numerical values for axial stress, axial strain, lateral strain, diameter change, and length change.
