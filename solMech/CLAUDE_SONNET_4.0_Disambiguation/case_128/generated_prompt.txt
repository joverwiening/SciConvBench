The shaft is a solid circular shaft with diameter d = 50 mm and length L = 600 mm.
The shaft is made of a linear elastic material with shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.
The shaft is subjected to an applied torque T = 500,000 N·mm.
Small deformation theory applies.
The shaft behaves as a linear elastic material under torsion.
Cross-sections remain plane and circular during deformation.
The material is homogeneous and isotropic.
Shear strain varies linearly from zero at the center to maximum at the outer surface.
The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer surface of the shaft.
The maximum shear stress is calculated using τ_max = T·c/J where c = d/2 = 25 mm is the outer radius and J = π·d⁴/32 is the polar moment of inertia.
The polar moment of inertia J = π·(50 mm)⁴/32 = 613,592.3 mm⁴.
The maximum shear stress τ_max = (500,000 N·mm × 25 mm)/613,592.3 mm⁴ = 20.37 MPa.
The angle of twist is calculated using φ = T·L/(G·J).
The angle of twist φ = (500,000 N·mm × 600 mm)/(77,519.4 MPa × 613,592.3 mm⁴) = 0.00631 radians.
The angle of twist in degrees is φ = 0.00631 × (180/π) = 0.361 degrees.
