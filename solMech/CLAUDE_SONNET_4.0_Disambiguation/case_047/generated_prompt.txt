Calculate the average normal stress in a straight prismatic circular rod under axial tension loading.
The rod has a diameter of 12 mm and a gage length of 800 mm.
The applied axial load is 15000 N.
The rod material is AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa and yield strength of 350 MPa.
Assume small-deformation and linear-elastic behavior.
Assume uniform stress distribution across the cross-section.
Assume the load is applied concentrically along the rod axis.
Calculate the cross-sectional area using A = π × (d/2)².
Calculate the average normal stress using σ = P/A where P is the axial load and A is the cross-sectional area.
Calculate the axial strain using ε = σ/E where E is the Young's modulus.
Calculate the total elongation using δ = ε × L where L is the gage length.
Calculate the factor of safety against yielding using FS = σ_yield / σ_applied.
Present the governing relations clearly including stress-strain relationship σ = E × ε and elongation formula δ = PL/(AE).
Report all results with appropriate units and significant figures.
