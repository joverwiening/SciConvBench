The hollow circular shaft has outer radius ro = 25 mm and inner radius ri = 22 mm with length L = 800 mm.
The shaft is made of structural steel with shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.
A torque T = 600000 N·mm is applied to the shaft.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior with circular cross-sections remaining plane and circular after deformation.
The polar moment of inertia is J = π(ro⁴ - ri⁴)/2 = π(25⁴ - 22⁴)/2 mm⁴.
The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer radius and is τmax = T·ro/J.
The minimum shear stress in the material occurs at the inner radius and is τmin = T·ri/J.
The angle of twist over the length L is φ = TL/(GJ).
The shear strain at any radius r is γ = τ/G = Tr/(GJ).
The torsional rigidity of the shaft is GJ.
Saint-Venant's principle applies, meaning stress distributions are valid away from points of load application.
The shaft material remains within the elastic limit throughout the loading.
