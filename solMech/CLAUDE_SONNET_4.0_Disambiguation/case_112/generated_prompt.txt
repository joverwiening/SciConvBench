The analysis involves a straight prismatic circular rod made of 6061-T6 aluminum subjected to axial tension loading.

Given parameters: axial load P = 15000 N, rod diameter d = 20 mm, gage length L = 800 mm, Young's modulus E = 69000 MPa, yield strength σ_y = 275 MPa.

Additional assumptions: the rod is homogeneous and isotropic, loading is applied concentrically along the rod axis, deformations remain within the elastic range, cross-sectional area remains constant during loading, and end effects are neglected.

Calculate the cross-sectional area as A = π(d/2)² = π(10 mm)² = 314.16 mm².

Determine the average normal stress using σ = P/A = 15000 N / 314.16 mm² = 47.75 MPa.

Calculate the axial strain using ε = σ/E = 47.75 MPa / 69000 MPa = 6.92 × 10⁻⁴.

Compute the total elongation using δ = εL = (6.92 × 10⁻⁴)(800 mm) = 0.554 mm.

Evaluate the factor of safety against yielding as FS = σ_y/σ = 275 MPa / 47.75 MPa = 5.76.

The governing relations are Hooke's law σ = Eε, the stress definition σ = P/A, the strain-displacement relation ε = δ/L, and the factor of safety definition FS = σ_y/σ.
