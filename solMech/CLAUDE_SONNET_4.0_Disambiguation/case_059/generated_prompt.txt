The column is made of structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The column has length L = 2500 mm and second moment of area I = 1800000 mm^4.
The end conditions are pinned-pinned with effective length factor K = 1.0.
The effective length is Le = K × L = 1.0 × 2500 = 2500 mm.
The Euler critical buckling load is calculated using Pcr = π²EI/Le².
Substituting values: Pcr = π² × 200000 × 1800000 / (2500)² = 568584 N = 568.6 kN.
The buckling instability check compares the applied compressive load P to the critical load Pcr.
If P < Pcr, the column is stable against buckling.
If P ≥ Pcr, the column will buckle and lose stability.
The governing differential equation for small-deformation buckling is d⁴w/dx⁴ + (P/EI)d²w/dx² = 0.
The boundary conditions for pinned-pinned ends are w(0) = 0, w(L) = 0, M(0) = 0, M(L) = 0.
Additional assumptions include the column is perfectly straight initially, the material remains linearly elastic, and deformations are small.
