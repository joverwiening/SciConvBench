The stepped circular bar consists of two segments with different diameters subjected to axial tension.
Segment 1 has length L1 = 400 mm and diameter d1 = 16 mm.
Segment 2 has length L2 = 400 mm and diameter d2 = 40 mm.
Applied tensile load P = 25000 N.
Material elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The material behaves as linear elastic with small deformations.
The bar is homogeneous and isotropic.
The load is applied axially and uniformly distributed over the cross-section.
Saint-Venant's principle applies at the junction between segments.
Cross-sectional area of segment 1: A1 = π(d1/2)² = π(8)² = 201.06 mm².
Cross-sectional area of segment 2: A2 = π(d2/2)² = π(20)² = 1256.64 mm².
Axial stress in segment 1: σ1 = P/A1 = 25000/201.06 = 124.34 MPa.
Axial stress in segment 2: σ2 = P/A2 = 25000/1256.64 = 19.89 MPa.
Axial strain in segment 1: ε1 = σ1/E = 124.34/200000 = 0.000622.
Axial strain in segment 2: ε2 = σ2/E = 19.89/200000 = 0.0000994.
Elongation of segment 1: δ1 = ε1 × L1 = 0.000622 × 400 = 0.249 mm.
Elongation of segment 2: δ2 = ε2 × L2 = 0.0000994 × 400 = 0.0398 mm.
Total elongation of the bar: δtotal = δ1 + δ2 = 0.249 + 0.0398 = 0.289 mm.
