The cantilever beam has length L = 2000 mm and is fixed at one end with the other end free.
A uniformly distributed transverse load w = 1.0 N/mm acts downward over the entire span.
The beam material has elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa and second moment of area I = 1500000 mm^4.
Assume the cross-section is rectangular for simplicity since only the second moment of area is specified.
Use small-deformation theory with linear-elastic material behavior.
Assume the beam is prismatic with constant cross-section along its length.
Neglect shear deformation effects (Euler-Bernoulli beam theory applies).
The coordinate system has x-axis along the beam length with origin at the fixed end and y-axis pointing upward.
The governing differential equation is EI d^4v/dx^4 = w where v(x) is the transverse deflection.
Apply boundary conditions: v(0) = 0, dv/dx(0) = 0 at the fixed end.
The maximum deflection occurs at the free end x = L.
Calculate the deflection equation v(x) = (wx^2/24EI)(6L^2 - 4Lx + x^2).
Determine the maximum deflection v_max = wL^4/8EI at x = L.
Calculate the maximum bending moment M_max = wL^2/2 at the fixed end.
Calculate the maximum bending stress σ_max = M_max × c/I where c is the distance from neutral axis to extreme fiber.
