The shaft is a solid circular cross-section with diameter d = 30 mm and total length L = 1200 mm.
The shaft is fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L = 1200 mm.
A torque T1 = 200000 N·mm is applied at x = 1200 mm in the positive sense.
A torque T2 = -200000 N·mm is applied at x = 400 mm in the negative sense.
The material follows small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.
The shaft cross-section remains plane and circular during deformation.
Warping of cross-sections is neglected.
The polar moment of inertia is J = πd⁴/32 = π(30)⁴/32 mm⁴.
The internal torque distribution is: T(x) = 0 for 0 ≤ x ≤ 400 mm, T(x) = -200000 N·mm for 400 mm < x ≤ 1200 mm.
The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer fiber and is τ_max = T(d/2)/J.
The angle of twist is governed by dθ/dx = T(x)/(GJ) where G is the shear modulus.
The total angle of twist is θ_total = ∫[0 to L] T(x)/(GJ) dx.
The shear strain distribution is γ = rτ/G where r is the radial distance from the center.
The relevant response quantities are internal torque distribution T(x), shear stress distribution τ(r,x), angle of twist θ(x), and maximum shear stress values.
