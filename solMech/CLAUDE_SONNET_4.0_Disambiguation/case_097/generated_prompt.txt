The stepped circular shaft consists of two segments with segment AB having length L1 = 400 mm and diameter d1 = 25 mm, and segment BC having length L2 = 900 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm.
The shaft is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa.
A torque T = 750000 N·mm is applied at end C while end A is fixed.
The analysis assumes small-deformation linear-elastic behavior with circular cross-sections remaining plane and circular after deformation.
The shear stress in each segment is calculated using τ = Tr/J where r is the radial distance from the center and J is the polar moment of inertia.
For circular cross-sections, J = πd⁴/32 where d is the diameter.
The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer surface of each segment where r = d/2.
For segment AB: J₁ = π(25)⁴/32 mm⁴ and maximum shear stress τ₁ = T(d₁/2)/J₁.
For segment BC: J₂ = π(30)⁴/32 mm⁴ and maximum shear stress τ₂ = T(d₂/2)/J₂.
The angle of twist in each segment is calculated using φ = TL/(GJ).
The angle of twist for segment AB is φ₁ = TL₁/(GJ₁).
The angle of twist for segment BC is φ₂ = TL₂/(GJ₂).
The total angle of twist of the shaft is φ_total = φ₁ + φ₂.
The torque T = 750000 N·mm is constant throughout both segments since there are no intermediate torque applications.
