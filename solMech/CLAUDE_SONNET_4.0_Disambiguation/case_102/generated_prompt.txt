The rod is vertical with length L = 800 mm and circular cross-section with diameter d = 25 mm and area A = π d²/4.
The rod is made of AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The mass of the rod is neglected in the analysis.
The top end of the rod is fixed and the bottom end is free.
A weight W = 1500 N is dropped from height h = 200 mm onto the free end of the rod.
There is no pre-existing axial load on the rod before impact.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.
The impact is assumed to occur instantaneously with the weight sticking to the rod after impact.
The relevant response quantities to determine are the maximum dynamic stress, maximum dynamic displacement, and dynamic amplification factor.
The velocity of the weight just before impact is v = √(2gh) where g = 9.81 m/s².
The equivalent static deflection under the weight is δ_st = WL/(EA).
The maximum dynamic displacement is δ_max = δ_st(1 + √(1 + 2h/δ_st)).
The maximum dynamic stress is σ_max = Eδ_max/L.
The dynamic amplification factor is DAF = δ_max/δ_st = 1 + √(1 + 2h/δ_st).
The governing equation for axial vibration is ∂²u/∂t² = (E/ρ)∂²u/∂x² where u(x,t) is displacement.
The boundary conditions are u(0,t) = 0 and ∂u/∂x|_(x=L) = 0 for free vibration after impact.
