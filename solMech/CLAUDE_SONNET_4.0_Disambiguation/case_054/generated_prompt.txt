The bar is fixed at the left end (x = 0) and free at the right end (x = L) where the tensile load is applied.
The cross-sectional area varies linearly according to A(x) = A₀·(1 + k·x/L) where A₀ = 250 mm², k = 0.6, L = 800 mm, and x is measured from the left end.
A constant tensile force P = 12000 N is applied at the free end (x = L).
The material is AISI 1020 steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Small-deformation and linear-elastic behavior are assumed.
The governing differential equation is d/dx[A(x)·E·du/dx] = 0 where u(x) is the axial displacement.
The stress at any cross-section is σ(x) = P/A(x) = P/[A₀·(1 + k·x/L)].
The strain at any cross-section is ε(x) = σ(x)/E = P/[E·A₀·(1 + k·x/L)].
The axial displacement is u(x) = (P·L)/(E·A₀·k)·ln(1 + k·x/L) with boundary condition u(0) = 0.
The total elongation of the bar is δ = u(L) = (P·L)/(E·A₀·k)·ln(1 + k).
The maximum stress occurs at the smallest cross-section (x = 0) and equals σ_max = P/A₀.
The minimum stress occurs at the largest cross-section (x = L) and equals σ_min = P/[A₀·(1 + k)].
