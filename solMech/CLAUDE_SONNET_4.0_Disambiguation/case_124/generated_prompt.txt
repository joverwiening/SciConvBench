The beam is a rectangular cross-section with width b = 40 mm and height h = 100 mm.
The internal bending moment is M = 4000000 N·mm acting in the sagging direction (positive bending).
The material follows small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior with plane sections remaining plane after deformation.
The neutral axis passes through the centroid of the rectangular cross-section.
The coordinate system has y measured from the neutral axis, positive upward toward the top fiber.
The second moment of area about the neutral axis is I = bh³/12 = 40 × 100³/12 = 3333333.33 mm⁴.
The bending stress at any fiber location y from the neutral axis is σ = My/I.
The maximum tensile stress occurs at the bottom fiber (y = -50 mm) and equals σ_max = 4000000 × 50 / 3333333.33 = 60 MPa.
The maximum compressive stress occurs at the top fiber (y = +50 mm) and equals σ_max = -60 MPa.
Shear stresses are neglected in this pure bending analysis.
The stress distribution is linear across the beam height with zero stress at the neutral axis.
