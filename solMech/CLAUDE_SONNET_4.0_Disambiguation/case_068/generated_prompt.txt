The polar moment of inertia is J = πd⁴/32 = π(30)⁴/32 = 79,521.56 mm⁴.

The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer surface and is calculated using τ_max = Tr/J = T(d/2)/J = 500,000 × 15 / 79,521.56 = 94.25 MPa.

The shear stress varies linearly from zero at the center to maximum at the outer surface according to τ(r) = Tr/J.

The angle of twist is calculated using φ = TL/(GJ) = 500,000 × 800 / (25939.8 × 79,521.56) = 0.1934 radians = 11.08 degrees.
