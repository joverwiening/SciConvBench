The tension test specimen has initial diameter d0 = 12.5 mm and initial gage length L0 = 50 mm.
The specimen is subjected to a constant axial tensile force P = 15000 N.
The measured elongation over the gage length is ΔL = 0.15 mm.
Calculate the initial cross-sectional area as A0 = π(d0/2)² = π(12.5/2)² = 122.72 mm².
Calculate the engineering stress as σ = P/A0 = 15000 N / 122.72 mm² = 122.27 MPa.
Calculate the engineering strain as ε = ΔL/L0 = 0.15 mm / 50 mm = 0.003.
Calculate the elastic modulus as E = σ/ε = 122.27 MPa / 0.003 = 40.76 GPa.
Assume the material behaves according to Hooke's law σ = Eε within the linear elastic range.
Assume small deformations where engineering stress and strain are valid approximations.
Assume uniform stress distribution across the cross-section.
Assume the load is applied axially without eccentricity or bending moments.
The governing relation is the linear elastic constitutive law σ = Eε.
The primary response quantities are engineering stress σ = 122.27 MPa, engineering strain ε = 0.003, and elastic modulus E = 40.76 GPa.
