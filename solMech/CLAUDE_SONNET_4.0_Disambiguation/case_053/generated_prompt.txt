The shaft is a solid circular aluminum shaft with diameter d = 20 mm and length L = 1200 mm between fixed ends.
The shaft is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa.
A motor delivers P = 5 kW power at 1200 rpm rotation speed.
Calculate the applied torque using T = P/ω where ω = 2π(rpm/60) = 2π(1200/60) = 125.66 rad/s.
The applied torque is T = 5000/125.66 = 39.79 N·m.
The polar moment of inertia for a solid circular shaft is J = πd⁴/32 = π(0.02)⁴/32 = 1.571 × 10⁻⁸ m⁴.
The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer surface and is calculated using τ_max = Tr/J = T(d/2)/J.
The maximum shear stress is τ_max = 39.79 × 0.01 / (1.571 × 10⁻⁸) = 25.33 MPa.
The angle of twist is calculated using φ = TL/(GJ) = 39.79 × 1.2 / (25939.8 × 10⁶ × 1.571 × 10⁻⁸) = 0.117 radians = 6.7 degrees.
For 6061-T6 aluminum, assume an allowable shear stress of 110 MPa based on typical yield strength values.
Apply a safety factor of 2.0 for good engineering practice, giving an allowable working shear stress of 55 MPa.
The safety factor against shear failure is 55/25.33 = 2.17, which exceeds the minimum requirement.
The shaft is adequate for the given loading conditions with respect to both shear stress and angle of twist.
