The beam is a simply supported Euler-Bernoulli beam with length L = 2500 mm.
The beam has flexural rigidity EI = 7.0 × 10^11 N⋅mm^2.
A downward point load P = 2500 N is applied at midspan (x = L/2 = 1250 mm).
The beam is supported by pinned supports at both ends (x = 0 and x = L).
Additional assumptions include the beam material is homogeneous and isotropic.
The beam cross-section remains plane and perpendicular to the neutral axis during deformation.
Shear deformation effects are neglected.
The governing differential equation is EI d^4w/dx^4 = q(x) where w is deflection and q(x) is the distributed load.
For this case, q(x) = P δ(x - L/2) where δ is the Dirac delta function.
Boundary conditions are w(0) = 0, w(L) = 0, M(0) = 0, and M(L) = 0.
The moment is related to deflection by M = -EI d^2w/dx^2.
The midspan deflection is w(L/2) = PL^3/(48EI).
The support slopes are dw/dx(0) = PL^2/(16EI) and dw/dx(L) = -PL^2/(16EI).
Substituting values gives midspan deflection w(L/2) = 0.457 mm.
The support slopes are ±0.000977 radians or ±0.056 degrees.
