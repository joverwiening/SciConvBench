The bar is fixed at the left end (x = 0) and the axial load P is applied at the right end (x = L).
The cross-sectional area varies linearly as A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L) where A0 = 200 mm², k = 1.0, and L = 500 mm.
The applied axial load is P = 12000 N in tension at the free end.
The material is AISI 1020 steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Small-deformation and linear-elastic behavior are assumed.
The governing differential equation is d/dx[A(x)·E·du/dx] = 0 where u(x) is the axial displacement.
The boundary conditions are u(0) = 0 and A(L)·E·du/dx|_{x=L} = P.
The axial stress is σ(x) = P/A(x) = P/[A0·(1 + k·x/L)].
The axial strain is ε(x) = σ(x)/E = P/[E·A0·(1 + k·x/L)].
The axial displacement is u(x) = (P·L)/(E·A0·k)·ln(1 + k·x/L).
The total elongation is δ = u(L) = (P·L)/(E·A0·k)·ln(1 + k).
