The bar is fixed at the left end (x = 0) and the axial load P is applied at the free right end (x = L).
The cross-sectional area varies linearly according to A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L) where A0 = 250 mm², k = 0.8, L = 800 mm.
The axial load is P = 30000 N applied as a constant tensile force at the right end.
The elastic modulus is E = 200000 MPa for structural steel material.
Small-deformation and linear-elastic behavior are assumed.
The material is homogeneous and isotropic.
The bar is prismatic except for the linear area variation.
Plane sections remain plane during deformation.
The axial stress distribution is uniform across each cross-section.
Determine the axial displacement u(x) along the length of the bar.
Determine the axial strain ε(x) = du/dx along the length.
Determine the axial stress σ(x) = P/A(x) along the length.
Calculate the total elongation of the bar.
The governing differential equation is d/dx[EA(x)du/dx] = 0 for constant axial force.
Apply boundary conditions u(0) = 0 and EA(L)du/dx|x=L = P.
