The vertical bar has length L = 3000 mm and cross-sectional area A = 300 mm².
The material has density ρ = 8900 kg/m³ and elastic modulus E = 69000 MPa.
The bar is fixed at the top and free at the bottom.
The bar is subjected only to its own self-weight acting downward.
Small-deformation theory applies with linear-elastic material behavior.
The axial stress varies linearly along the length due to the varying weight below each point.
At distance x from the top, the axial stress is σ(x) = ρg(L-x) where g = 9.81 m/s².
The axial strain at distance x from the top is ε(x) = σ(x)/E = ρg(L-x)/E.
The total elongation is calculated by integrating the strain over the length: δ = ∫₀ᴸ ε(x)dx = ρgL²/(2E).
The maximum stress occurs at the fixed top end and equals σ_max = ρgL.
The maximum elongation occurs at the free bottom end.
The displacement at distance x from the top is u(x) = ρg(Lx - x²/2)/E.
The total elongation of the bar is δ = ρgL²/(2E) = (8900 kg/m³)(9.81 m/s²)(3 m)²/(2 × 69×10⁹ Pa) = 5.67×10⁻⁶ m = 0.00567 mm.
