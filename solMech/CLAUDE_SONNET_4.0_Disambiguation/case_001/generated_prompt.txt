The stepped circular bar consists of two segments with different cross-sectional areas connected in series.
Segment 1 has length L1 = 300 mm, diameter d1 = 25 mm, and cross-sectional area A1 = π(d1/2)² = π(12.5)² = 490.87 mm².
Segment 2 has length L2 = 1000 mm, diameter d2 = 35 mm, and cross-sectional area A2 = π(d2/2)² = π(17.5)² = 962.11 mm².
The applied tensile load is P = 30000 N.
The elastic modulus is E = 200000 MPa = 200000 N/mm².
The material behaves as linear-elastic with small deformations.
The bar is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The load is applied axially and uniformly distributed over the cross-section.
The stress in segment 1 is σ1 = P/A1 = 30000/490.87 = 61.12 MPa.
The stress in segment 2 is σ2 = P/A2 = 30000/962.11 = 31.18 MPa.
The elongation of segment 1 is δ1 = σ1L1/E = (61.12)(300)/200000 = 0.0917 mm.
The elongation of segment 2 is δ2 = σ2L2/E = (31.18)(1000)/200000 = 0.1559 mm.
The total elongation is δtotal = δ1 + δ2 = 0.0917 + 0.1559 = 0.2476 mm.
