The cantilever beam has length L = 1000 mm and is fixed at one end with the other end free.
A uniformly distributed transverse load w = 1.5 N/mm acts downward over the entire span.
The beam material is AISI 1020 steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The second moment of area is I = 2000000 mm^4.
Assume the beam has a rectangular cross-section for simplicity.
Use small-deformation theory with linear-elastic material behavior.
Assume the beam is prismatic with constant cross-section along its length.
Neglect shear deformation effects and use Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.
The coordinate system has x-axis along the beam length with origin at the fixed end and y-axis pointing upward.
The governing differential equation is EI d^4v/dx^4 = w where v(x) is the transverse deflection.
The boundary conditions are v(0) = 0, dv/dx(0) = 0, d^2v/dx^2(L) = 0, and d^3v/dx^3(L) = 0.
The maximum deflection occurs at the free end and equals wL^4/(8EI).
The maximum bending moment occurs at the fixed end and equals wL^2/2.
The maximum shear force occurs at the fixed end and equals wL.
Calculate the deflection curve v(x) = (w/(24EI))(x^4 - 4Lx^3 + 6L^2x^2).
Determine the slope at any point dv/dx = (w/(6EI))(x^3 - 3Lx^2 + 3L^2x).
Calculate the bending moment M(x) = w(L^2/2 - Lx + x^2/2).
Calculate the shear force V(x) = w(L - x).
