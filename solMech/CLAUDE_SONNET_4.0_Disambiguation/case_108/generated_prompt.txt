The beam is simply supported at both ends with pinned connections allowing rotation but preventing vertical displacement.
The beam has a length L = 3500 mm.
A uniformly distributed load w = 0.5 N/mm acts downward over the entire span.
The beam material has elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The beam cross-section has second moment of area I = 1500000 mm^4.
Small deformation theory applies with linear elastic material behavior.
Plane sections remain plane after deformation (Euler-Bernoulli beam theory).
The beam weight is neglected compared to the applied load.
Shear deformation is neglected.
The governing differential equation is EI d^4v/dx^4 = w where v is the vertical deflection.
The boundary conditions are v(0) = 0, v(L) = 0, M(0) = 0, M(L) = 0.
The maximum deflection occurs at midspan and equals 5wL^4/(384EI).
The slope at any point x is dv/dx = (wx/24EI)(L^3 - 2Lx^2 + x^3).
The maximum slope occurs at the supports and equals wL^3/(24EI).
The bending moment at any point x is M(x) = (wx/2)(L - x).
The maximum bending moment occurs at midspan and equals wL^2/8.
