The beam is a structural steel beam with second moment of area I = 4000000 mm^4 about the neutral axis.
The beam material has elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
A bending moment M = 3000000 N·mm is applied to the beam.
The moment is assumed to cause positive curvature (compression on top fiber, tension on bottom fiber).
Small-deformation and linear-elastic behavior are assumed.
Plane sections remain plane after deformation.
The beam behaves according to Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.
The governing relation for curvature is κ = M/(EI) where κ is curvature, M is bending moment, E is elastic modulus, and I is second moment of area.
The radius of curvature is ρ = 1/κ = EI/M.
Calculate curvature: κ = 3000000 N·mm / (200000 MPa × 4000000 mm^4) = 3000000 / (200000 × 4000000) = 3.75 × 10^-6 mm^-1.
Calculate radius of curvature: ρ = 1 / (3.75 × 10^-6 mm^-1) = 266667 mm = 266.67 m.
Present results showing curvature κ = 3.75 × 10^-6 mm^-1 and radius of curvature ρ = 266.67 m.
State the governing relation κ = M/(EI) clearly in the analysis.
