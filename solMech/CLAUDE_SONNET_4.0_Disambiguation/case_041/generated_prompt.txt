The column is made of AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The column has length L = 1000 mm and second moment of area I = 1200000 mm^4.
The cross-sectional area is assumed to be A = 2400 mm^2 based on typical structural proportions.
The end conditions are fixed-free (cantilever configuration).
Small-deformation and linear-elastic behavior are assumed.
The governing differential equation for column buckling is EI(d^4w/dx^4) + P(d^2w/dx^2) = 0.
For fixed-free end conditions, the effective length factor is K = 2.0.
The Euler critical buckling load is calculated as P_cr = π^2EI/(KL)^2.
Substituting values: P_cr = π^2 × 200000 × 1200000 / (2 × 1000)^2 = 592.2 kN.
The critical buckling stress is σ_cr = P_cr/A = 592200/2400 = 246.8 MPa.
For buckling instability check, compare applied load P to P_cr: if P ≥ P_cr, buckling occurs.
The buckling mode shape for fixed-free conditions is w(x) = A[1 - cos(πx/2L)].
Additional assumption: The column is perfectly straight with no initial imperfections.
The analysis is valid only for elastic buckling where σ_cr is below the material yield strength.
