The beam is simply supported with supports at x = 0 and x = L = 2400 mm.
A single point load P = 1200 N acts downward at x = 1200 mm.
The beam material is structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The beam cross-section has second moment of area I = 3500000 mm^4.
Small-deformation theory applies with linear-elastic material behavior.
Euler-Bernoulli beam theory governs the bending behavior.
The governing differential equation is EI d^4v/dx^4 = q(x) where v is deflection and q(x) is distributed load.
For point loads, the equation becomes EI d^4v/dx^4 = P δ(x - a) where δ is the Dirac delta function and a is the load location.
The boundary conditions are v(0) = 0, v(L) = 0, M(0) = 0, and M(L) = 0.
The moment-curvature relation is M = -EI d^2v/dx^2.
The unit-load method applies a virtual unit load at the point where deflection is desired.
The virtual work equation for the unit-load method is 1 × δ = ∫(M_real × M_unit)/(EI) dx over the beam length.
The real moment M_real is calculated from the actual loading P = 1200 N at x = 1200 mm.
The unit moment M_unit is calculated from a unit load applied at the desired deflection location.
The deflection at any point can be determined by integrating the product of real and unit moments divided by flexural rigidity EI.
