The rod is made of an isotropic, linear-elastic material with Young's modulus E = 105000 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.34.
The rod has initial diameter d₀ = 30 mm, length L = 500 mm, and circular cross-section.
An axial tensile load P = 25000 N is applied through the centroid of the rod.
The rod undergoes small deformations under uniaxial tension.
The initial cross-sectional area is A₀ = π(d₀/2)² = π(15 mm)² = 706.86 mm².
The axial stress is σ = P/A₀ = 25000 N / 706.86 mm² = 35.37 MPa.
The axial strain is ε_axial = σ/E = 35.37 MPa / 105000 MPa = 3.369 × 10⁻⁴.
The lateral strain due to Poisson effect is ε_lateral = -ν × ε_axial = -0.34 × 3.369 × 10⁻⁴ = -1.145 × 10⁻⁴.
The axial elongation is ΔL = ε_axial × L = 3.369 × 10⁻⁴ × 500 mm = 0.168 mm.
The change in diameter is Δd = ε_lateral × d₀ = -1.145 × 10⁻⁴ × 30 mm = -0.00344 mm.
The final diameter is d_final = d₀ + Δd = 30 mm - 0.00344 mm = 29.997 mm.
The final length is L_final = L + ΔL = 500 mm + 0.168 mm = 500.168 mm.
The governing relation for uniaxial stress is σ = Eε_axial.
The governing relation for Poisson effect is ε_lateral = -νε_axial.
The rod contracts laterally while extending axially due to the Poisson effect under uniaxial tension.
