The shaft is a solid circular shaft with diameter d = 30 mm and length L = 1000 mm.
The applied torque is T = 250,000 N·mm.
The material has shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.
The shaft material is homogeneous and isotropic.
The torque is applied at one end while the other end is fixed.
The shaft cross-section remains plane and circular during deformation.
The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer surface of the shaft.
The maximum shear stress is calculated using τ_max = Tc/J where c = d/2 = 15 mm is the outer radius.
The polar moment of inertia is J = πd⁴/32 = π(30)⁴/32 mm⁴.
The angle of twist is calculated using φ = TL/(GJ).
The shear stress varies linearly from zero at the center to maximum at the outer surface.
The governing relation for shear stress distribution is τ = Tρ/J where ρ is the radial distance from center.
The governing relation for angle of twist per unit length is dφ/dx = T/(GJ).
