The analysis determines stress distribution and deformation in a short rectangular column under eccentric axial loading.

The rectangular column cross-section has width b = 60 mm and height h = 120 mm.
A compressive axial load P = 60000 N is applied with eccentricity e = 20 mm from the centroid along the width direction.
The column length is assumed to be L = 500 mm based on typical short column proportions where L/h ≤ 5.
The material follows small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior with assumed properties E = 200 GPa and ν = 0.3 for structural steel.
The column is assumed to have fixed-fixed end conditions for boundary constraints.
The eccentric load creates combined axial compression and bending about the weak axis.
The cross-sectional area is A = b × h = 7200 mm².
The section modulus about the weak axis is S = b²h/6 = 72000 mm³.
The moment of inertia about the weak axis is I = b³h/12 = 2160000 mm⁴.
The applied moment due to eccentricity is M = P × e = 1200000 N·mm.
The axial stress is σ_axial = P/A = 8.33 MPa compression.
The bending stress is σ_bending = M/S = 16.67 MPa.
The maximum compressive stress occurs at the edge closest to the load and equals σ_max = σ_axial + σ_bending = 25.0 MPa.
The minimum stress occurs at the opposite edge and equals σ_min = σ_axial - σ_bending = -8.33 MPa tension.
The neutral axis location is determined from the stress distribution.
The axial deformation is δ = PL/(AE) assuming uniform compression.
The lateral deflection follows standard beam bending theory for the applied moment.
