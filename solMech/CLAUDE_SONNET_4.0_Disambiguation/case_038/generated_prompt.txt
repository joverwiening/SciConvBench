The beam is simply supported at both ends with pinned connections allowing rotation but preventing vertical displacement.
The beam has a length L = 3500 mm.
A uniformly distributed transverse load w = 0.5 N/mm acts downward over the entire span.
The beam cross-section has a second moment of area I = 3000000 mm^4.
The material is AISI 1020 steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Small deformation theory applies with linear elastic material behavior.
The beam behaves according to Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.
Shear deformation effects are neglected.
The beam is prismatic with constant cross-section along its length.
The coordinate system has x measured from the left support and y measured positive upward.
The governing differential equation is EI d^4y/dx^4 = w.
The boundary conditions are y(0) = 0, y(L) = 0, M(0) = 0, and M(L) = 0.
The maximum deflection occurs at midspan and equals 5wL^4/(384EI).
The deflection at any point x is y(x) = (wx/24EI)(L^3x - 2Lx^3 + x^4).
The slope at any point x is dy/dx = (w/24EI)(L^3 - 6Lx^2 + 4x^3).
The maximum slope occurs at the supports and equals wL^3/(24EI).
Calculate numerical values for deflection and slope at critical locations using the given parameters.
