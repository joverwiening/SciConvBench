The rod is vertical with length L = 1200 mm and circular cross-section with diameter d = 30 mm.
The cross-sectional area is A = π d²/4 = π (30)²/4 = 706.9 mm².
The rod is made of structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The mass of the rod is neglected in the analysis.
The rod is fixed at the top end and free at the bottom end.
A weight W = 500 N is dropped from height h = 200 mm onto the free end of the rod.
There is no additional static axial load applied to the rod.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.
The relevant response quantities to determine are the maximum dynamic stress and maximum dynamic displacement at the free end.
The impact velocity of the weight just before contact is v = √(2gh) = √(2 × 9.81 × 0.2) = 1.98 m/s.
The equivalent static load that would produce the same energy as the impact is P_eq = W(1 + √(1 + 2h/δ_st)) where δ_st is the static deflection under load W.
The static deflection under load W is δ_st = WL/(EA) = 500 × 1.2/(200000 × 706.9 × 10⁻⁶) = 4.24 × 10⁻³ mm.
The dynamic amplification factor is DAF = 1 + √(1 + 2h/δ_st) = 1 + √(1 + 2 × 200/4.24 × 10⁻³) = 309.7.
The maximum dynamic displacement is δ_max = DAF × δ_st = 309.7 × 4.24 × 10⁻³ = 1.31 mm.
The maximum dynamic stress is σ_max = DAF × W/A = 309.7 × 500/(706.9 × 10⁻⁶) = 219.2 MPa.
