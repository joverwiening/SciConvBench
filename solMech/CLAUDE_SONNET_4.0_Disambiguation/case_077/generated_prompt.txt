The bar is a prismatic member with rectangular cross-section subjected to combined axial tension and transverse shear loading.
The cross-section has width b = 50 mm and height h = 120 mm.
The internal resultant normal force is P = 40000 N acting perpendicular to the cross-section.
The internal resultant shear force is V = 15000 N acting parallel to the width b in the plane of the cross-section.
Assume small-deformation linear-elastic behavior with homogeneous isotropic material properties.
Assume the shear force acts through the centroid of the cross-section.
Assume plane sections remain plane for normal stress distribution.
Assume uniform shear stress distribution across the width for average calculations.
The cross-sectional area is A = b × h = 50 mm × 120 mm = 6000 mm².
The average normal stress is σ_avg = P/A = 40000 N / 6000 mm² = 6.67 MPa.
The average shear stress is τ_avg = V/A = 15000 N / 6000 mm² = 2.50 MPa.
The average shear stress acts parallel to the width direction in the plane of the cross-section.
The normal stress is tensile and acts perpendicular to the cross-section.
