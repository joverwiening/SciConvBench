The hollow circular shaft has outer radius ro = 20 mm and inner radius ri = 15 mm with length L = 1000 mm.
The shaft is made of brass with shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.
A torque T = 300000 N·mm is applied to the shaft.
The material behaves as linear-elastic under small deformation conditions.
The shaft cross-section remains plane during deformation.
Shear strain varies linearly with radial distance from the center.
The polar moment of inertia is J = π(ro⁴ - ri⁴)/2 = π(20⁴ - 15⁴)/2 = 125663.7 mm⁴.
The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer radius and equals τmax = T·ro/J = 300000 × 20 / 125663.7 = 47.75 MPa.
The minimum shear stress occurs at the inner radius and equals τmin = T·ri/J = 300000 × 15 / 125663.7 = 35.81 MPa.
The angle of twist is φ = TL/(GJ) = 300000 × 1000 / (39179.1 × 125663.7) = 0.0609 radians = 3.49 degrees.
The shear strain at the outer radius is γmax = τmax/G = 47.75/39179.1 = 0.00122.
The shear strain at the inner radius is γmin = τmin/G = 35.81/39179.1 = 0.000914.
