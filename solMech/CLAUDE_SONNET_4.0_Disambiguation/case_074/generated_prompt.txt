The beam has a span length of 3000 mm (assumed as a typical structural beam span).
The loading configuration is a uniformly distributed load across the entire span (assumed as most common loading case).
The maximum bending moment is M_max = 6000000 N·mm.
The allowable bending stress is σ_allow = 170 MPa.
The rectangular cross-section has width b = 60 mm.
The beam height h is to be determined.
The beam is simply supported at both ends.
Small-deformation theory applies.
Linear-elastic material behavior is assumed.
Plane sections remain plane after bending (Euler-Bernoulli beam theory).
The governing relation is σ_max = M_max × c / I where c is the distance from neutral axis to extreme fiber.
For a rectangular section, c = h/2 and I = b × h³ / 12.
The bending stress equation becomes σ_max = M_max × (h/2) / (b × h³ / 12) = 6 × M_max / (b × h²).
The design constraint is σ_max ≤ σ_allow.
Substituting values: 6 × 6000000 / (60 × h²) ≤ 170.
Solving for h: h² ≥ 36000000 / (60 × 170) = 3529.4.
The minimum required beam height is h = 59.4 mm.
Use h = 60 mm for practical design (rounded up to nearest convenient dimension).
