The specimen is a circular cross-section tension test specimen with initial diameter d0 = 12.5 mm and initial gage length L0 = 50 mm.
The applied axial load is P = 12000 N in tension.
The measured axial deformation is ΔL = 0.12 mm.
The initial cross-sectional area is A0 = π(d0/2)² = π(6.25)² = 122.72 mm².
The engineering stress is σ = P/A0 = 12000 N / 122.72 mm² = 97.8 MPa.
The engineering strain is ε = ΔL/L0 = 0.12 mm / 50 mm = 0.0024.
The modulus of elasticity is E = σ/ε = 97.8 MPa / 0.0024 = 40.75 GPa.
Assume small deformations so engineering stress and strain are valid approximations.
Assume linear elastic behavior within the applied load range.
Assume uniform stress distribution across the cross-section.
Assume the specimen remains within the elastic limit.
The governing relation is Hooke's law: σ = Eε.
