The shaft is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa.
The shaft has a solid circular cross-section with diameter d to be determined.
The shaft length is L = 1500 mm.
The applied torque is T = 1000000 N·mm.
The maximum allowable angle of twist is φ_allow = 0.75° over the total length L.
The material behaves as linear-elastic under small deformation assumptions.
The angle of twist for a solid circular shaft is given by φ = TL/(GJ) where J is the polar moment of inertia.
For a solid circular cross-section, the polar moment of inertia is J = πd⁴/32.
Convert the allowable angle of twist to radians: φ_allow = 0.75° × π/180 = 0.01309 radians.
Substitute into the twist equation: 0.01309 = (1000000 × 1500)/(25939.8 × 10⁶ × πd⁴/32).
Solve for diameter: d⁴ = (1000000 × 1500 × 32)/(25939.8 × 10⁶ × π × 0.01309).
Calculate d⁴ = 1.428 × 10⁻⁶ m⁴.
Take the fourth root to find d = 0.0615 m = 61.5 mm.
The required shaft diameter is d = 61.5 mm to satisfy the angle-of-twist limit.
