The hollow circular shaft has outer radius ro = 25 mm and inner radius ri = 21 mm with length L = 1200 mm.
The shaft is subjected to an applied torque T = 450000 N·mm.
Assume the material is homogeneous, isotropic, and linearly elastic.
Assume small deformation theory applies.
Assume the shaft is prismatic with constant cross-section along its length.
Assume one end is fixed and torque is applied at the free end.
Assume plane sections remain plane after deformation.
The polar moment of inertia is J = π(ro⁴ - ri⁴)/2 = π(25⁴ - 21⁴)/2 mm⁴.
The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer radius and equals τmax = T·ro/J.
The minimum shear stress in the material occurs at the inner radius and equals τmin = T·ri/J.
The angle of twist is φ = TL/(GJ) where G is the shear modulus.
The shear strain varies linearly with radius as γ = rφ/L for ri ≤ r ≤ ro.
The shear stress varies linearly with radius as τ = Tr/J for ri ≤ r ≤ ro.
The material between the inner and outer radii experiences pure shear deformation.
