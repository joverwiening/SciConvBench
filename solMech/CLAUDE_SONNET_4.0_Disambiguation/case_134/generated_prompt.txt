The structural member is a rectangular adhesive layer with area A = 3000 mm² and thickness t = 8 mm.
The adhesive layer is subjected to a uniform in-plane shear force V = 12000 N applied parallel to the faces of the layer.
The adhesive material is isotropic with shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The shear stress is uniform across the adhesive layer cross-section.
The shear stress is calculated as τ = V/A = 12000 N / 3000 mm² = 4 MPa.
The shear strain is determined using Hooke's law for shear: γ = τ/G = 4 MPa / 39179.1 MPa = 1.021 × 10⁻⁴ rad.
The shear deformation (displacement) is δ = γ × t = 1.021 × 10⁻⁴ rad × 8 mm = 8.17 × 10⁻⁴ mm.
The governing relation is the linear elastic shear stress-strain relationship: τ = G × γ.
The maximum shear stress occurs uniformly across the adhesive layer and equals 4 MPa.
The adhesive layer experiences pure shear deformation with no normal stresses under the applied loading.
