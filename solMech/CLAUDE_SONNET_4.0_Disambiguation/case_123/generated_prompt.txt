The rod is a circular prismatic AISI 1020 steel rod with diameter d = 20 mm and length L = 800 mm.
The rod experiences a uniform temperature increase of ΔT = 50 °C.
The rod is placed between two rigid, unyielding supports providing full axial restraint.
The total change in length is constrained to be zero.
Small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics theory applies.
The material is homogeneous and isotropic.
Plane sections remain plane during deformation.
The coefficient of thermal expansion for AISI 1020 steel is α = 12 × 10⁻⁶ /°C.
The elastic modulus for AISI 1020 steel is E = 200 GPa.
The free thermal strain would be εₜₕ = α × ΔT = 12 × 10⁻⁶ × 50 = 6 × 10⁻⁴.
Due to full axial restraint, the total axial strain is zero: εₜₒₜₐₗ = 0.
The mechanical strain required to counteract thermal expansion is εₘₑcₕ = -εₜₕ = -6 × 10⁻⁴.
The axial stress developed due to restraint is σ = E × εₘₑcₕ = 200 × 10⁹ × (-6 × 10⁻⁴) = -120 MPa.
The negative sign indicates compressive stress.
The axial force in the rod is F = σ × A = -120 × 10⁶ × π × (0.01)² = -377 N.
The reaction forces at each support are equal and opposite to maintain equilibrium.
