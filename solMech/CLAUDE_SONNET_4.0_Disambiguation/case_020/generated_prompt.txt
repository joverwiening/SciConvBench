The shaft is a solid circular steel shaft with diameter d = 35 mm and length L = 1200 mm.
The shaft is fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L = 1200 mm.
Two torques are applied: T1 = 200000 N·mm at x = 1200 mm (positive sense) and T2 = -250000 N·mm at x = 800 mm (negative sense).
The material has shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.
Small-deformation and linear-elastic behavior are assumed.
The polar moment of inertia is J = πd⁴/32 = π(35)⁴/32 mm⁴.
The internal torque distribution is determined by equilibrium: for 0 ≤ x ≤ 800 mm, T(x) = -250000 N·mm; for 800 mm < x ≤ 1200 mm, T(x) = -50000 N·mm.
The maximum shear stress is τ = Tr/J where r = d/2 = 17.5 mm.
The angle of twist is governed by dφ/dx = T(x)/(GJ).
The angle of twist at any location x is found by integrating the twist rate from the fixed end.
The maximum shear stress occurs in the segment 0 ≤ x ≤ 800 mm where |T| = 250000 N·mm.
The total angle of twist at the free end is the sum of twist contributions from both segments.
