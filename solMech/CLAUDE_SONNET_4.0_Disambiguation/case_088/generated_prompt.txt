The stepped circular bar consists of two segments with different cross-sectional areas connected in series.
Segment 1 has length L1 = 300 mm, diameter d1 = 12 mm, and cross-sectional area A1 = π(d1/2)² = π(6)² = 113.1 mm².
Segment 2 has length L2 = 800 mm, diameter d2 = 30 mm, and cross-sectional area A2 = π(d2/2)² = π(15)² = 706.9 mm².
The applied tensile load is P = 35000 N.
The material has elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic throughout both segments.
The connection between segments is assumed to be rigid with no stress concentration effects.
The loading is assumed to be purely axial with no bending or torsion.
The stress in segment 1 is σ1 = P/A1 = 35000 N / 113.1 mm² = 309.4 MPa.
The stress in segment 2 is σ2 = P/A2 = 35000 N / 706.9 mm² = 49.5 MPa.
The elongation of segment 1 is δ1 = σ1 × L1 / E = 309.4 MPa × 300 mm / 200000 MPa = 0.464 mm.
The elongation of segment 2 is δ2 = σ2 × L2 / E = 49.5 MPa × 800 mm / 200000 MPa = 0.198 mm.
The total elongation of the stepped bar is δ_total = δ1 + δ2 = 0.464 mm + 0.198 mm = 0.662 mm.
