The shaft is a solid circular cross-section with diameter d = 20 mm and length L = 1000 mm.
The shaft is made of AISI 1020 steel with shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa.
The motor delivers power P = 5 kW at rotational speed n = 1200 rpm.
Calculate angular velocity as ω = 2π(n/60) = 2π(1200/60) = 125.66 rad/s.
Calculate applied torque as T = P/ω = 5000/125.66 = 39.79 N⋅m.
The polar moment of inertia for solid circular shaft is J = πd⁴/32 = π(0.02)⁴/32 = 1.571 × 10⁻⁹ m⁴.
The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer surface and is τ_max = Tr/J = T(d/2)/J.
Calculate maximum shear stress as τ_max = 39.79 × 0.01 / (1.571 × 10⁻⁹) = 253.3 MPa.
The angle of twist is φ = TL/(GJ) = 39.79 × 1.0 / (76923.1 × 10⁶ × 1.571 × 10⁻⁹) = 0.329 radians.
Convert angle of twist to degrees as φ = 0.329 × 180/π = 18.85 degrees.
Assume allowable shear stress for AISI 1020 steel as τ_allow = 207 MPa based on yield strength in shear.
Check torsional stress safety factor as SF = τ_allow/τ_max = 207/253.3 = 0.82.
The shaft fails the torsion check since the safety factor is less than 1.0.
The shear stress distribution varies linearly from zero at the center to maximum at the outer surface.
The power transmission capacity is verified as P = Tω = 39.79 × 125.66 = 5000 W = 5 kW.
