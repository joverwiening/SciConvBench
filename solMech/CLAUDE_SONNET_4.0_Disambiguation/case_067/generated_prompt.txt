The analysis involves a solid circular shaft under torsional loading with the following specifications:
Applied torque T = 250,000 N·mm.
Shaft length L = 400 mm.
Shaft diameter d = 35 mm, giving radius r = 17.5 mm.
Material shear modulus G = 77,519.4 MPa.
The shaft material behaves as a linear-elastic solid under small deformations.
The shaft is homogeneous and isotropic.
The torque is applied uniformly and the shaft is in static equilibrium.
Cross-sections remain plane and circular during deformation.
The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer surface and is calculated using τ_max = T·r/J where J is the polar moment of inertia.
For a solid circular cross-section, J = π·d⁴/32.
The angle of twist is calculated using φ = T·L/(G·J).
The shear stress varies linearly from zero at the center to maximum at the outer radius.
The analysis assumes no stress concentrations, buckling, or material nonlinearity.
