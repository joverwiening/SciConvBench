The beam is a simply supported Euler-Bernoulli beam with length L = 3500 mm.
The beam is made of structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa and second moment of area I = 3000000 mm^4.
The beam is subjected to a uniformly distributed load w = 0.3 N/mm over the full span.
The beam is subjected to a downward point load P = 1200 N at midspan (x = L/2 = 1750 mm).
The beam has simple supports at x = 0 and x = L with zero deflection and zero moment at both ends.
Small deformation theory applies with linear elastic material behavior.
The governing differential equation is EI d^4v/dx^4 = q(x) where v is deflection and q(x) is the distributed load.
The deflection is determined by superposition of the deflection due to the uniform load and the deflection due to the point load.
For the uniform load case, the maximum deflection occurs at midspan and equals 5wL^4/(384EI).
For the point load case, the maximum deflection occurs at midspan and equals PL^3/(48EI).
The total deflection at any point is the sum of deflections from both loading cases.
The maximum total deflection occurs at midspan and equals 5wL^4/(384EI) + PL^3/(48EI).
Shear forces and bending moments are also determined by superposition of contributions from both loads.
The maximum bending moment occurs at midspan and equals wL^2/8 + PL/4.
