The beam has rectangular cross-section with width b = 50 mm and height h = 80 mm.
The beam length is assumed to be L = 1000 mm (reasonable span-to-depth ratio of 12.5).
The loading is assumed to be a uniformly distributed load creating internal shear force V = 20000 N at the analysis section.
Small-deformation and linear-elastic behavior are assumed.
The material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
Plane sections remain plane after deformation (Euler-Bernoulli beam theory).
The cross-section is symmetric about both axes.
The neutral axis is located at the geometric centroid of the cross-section.
The coordinate system has y = 0 at the neutral axis, with y positive upward.
Calculate the maximum transverse shear stress using τ_max = 1.5 V/A where A = b × h.
Calculate the shear stress at y = 35 mm from the neutral axis using the general shear stress formula τ = VQ/(Ib).
For the point at y = 35 mm, calculate the first moment Q of the area above this point about the neutral axis.
The second moment of area is I = bh³/12 for the rectangular cross-section.
Present results in MPa units as specified.
The governing relation for transverse shear stress distribution is τ(y) = VQ(y)/(Ib) where Q(y) is the first moment of area above level y.
