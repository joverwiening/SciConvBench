The stepped circular bar consists of two segments with different cross-sectional areas connected in series.
Segment 1 has length L1 = 500 mm, diameter d1 = 30 mm, and cross-sectional area A1 = π(d1/2)² = π(15)² = 706.86 mm².
Segment 2 has length L2 = 600 mm, diameter d2 = 25 mm, and cross-sectional area A2 = π(d2/2)² = π(12.5)² = 490.87 mm².
The applied tensile load is P = 20000 N.
The material has elastic modulus E = 69000 MPa.
The bar is assumed to be homogeneous with uniform material properties throughout.
The loading is assumed to be axial with no bending or torsion.
The stress in each segment is uniform across the cross-section.
The governing relation for stress is σ = P/A where P is the applied load and A is the cross-sectional area.
The stress in segment 1 is σ1 = P/A1 = 20000/706.86 = 28.29 MPa.
The stress in segment 2 is σ2 = P/A2 = 20000/490.87 = 40.74 MPa.
The governing relation for elongation is δ = PL/(AE) where L is the length of the segment.
The elongation of segment 1 is δ1 = PL1/(A1E) = (20000)(500)/(706.86)(69000) = 0.205 mm.
The elongation of segment 2 is δ2 = PL2/(A2E) = (20000)(600)/(490.87)(69000) = 0.355 mm.
The total elongation is δ_total = δ1 + δ2 = 0.205 + 0.355 = 0.560 mm.
