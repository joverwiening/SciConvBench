The shaft is a solid circular cross-section made of AISI 1020 steel.
The shaft is subjected to an applied torque T = 600000 N·mm.
The shaft length is L = 800 mm.
The maximum allowable angle of twist is φ_allow = 1.5° over the total length.
The material shear modulus is G = 76923.1 MPa.
Small-deformation and linear-elastic behavior are assumed.
The shaft material is homogeneous and isotropic.
The torque is applied as pure torsion without any axial or bending loads.
The shaft is prismatic with constant circular cross-section along its length.
The governing relation for angle of twist is φ = TL/(GJ) where J is the polar moment of inertia.
For a solid circular shaft, the polar moment of inertia is J = πd⁴/32 where d is the diameter.
The design constraint is φ ≤ φ_allow, which gives TL/(GJ) ≤ φ_allow.
Convert the allowable angle to radians: φ_allow = 1.5° × π/180 = 0.02618 rad.
Substitute the polar moment of inertia: TL/(G × πd⁴/32) ≤ 0.02618.
Solve for the minimum required diameter: d ≥ (32TL/(πGφ_allow))^(1/4).
Calculate the minimum diameter using the given values.
