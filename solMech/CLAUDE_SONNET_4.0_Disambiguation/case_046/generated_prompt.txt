The analysis involves a straight prismatic circular steel rod under axial tension loading.

Given parameters: axial load P = 20000 N, diameter d = 25 mm, gage length L = 800 mm, Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa, yield strength σ_y = 250 MPa.

Additional assumptions: the rod is homogeneous and isotropic, loading is applied concentrically, end effects are neglected, and the material behaves linearly elastic up to the yield point.

The cross-sectional area is A = π(d/2)² = π(12.5 mm)² = 490.87 mm².

The average normal stress is σ = P/A = 20000 N / 490.87 mm² = 40.74 MPa.

The axial strain is ε = σ/E = 40.74 MPa / 200000 MPa = 0.0002037 or 203.7 × 10⁻⁶.

The elongation is δ = εL = 0.0002037 × 800 mm = 0.163 mm.

The factor of safety with respect to yielding is FS = σ_y/σ = 250 MPa / 40.74 MPa = 6.14.

The governing relations are Hooke's law σ = Eε, the stress definition σ = P/A, and the elongation formula δ = εL = (PL)/(AE).
