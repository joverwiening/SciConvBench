The rod is a circular prismatic AISI 1020 steel rod with diameter d = 35 mm and length L = 1200 mm.
The rod experiences a uniform temperature increase of ΔT = 65 °C.
The rod is placed between two rigid, unyielding supports providing full axial restraint.
The total axial displacement is constrained to be zero.
No external axial loads are applied to the rod.
The material behavior follows small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The coefficient of thermal expansion for AISI 1020 steel is α = 12 × 10⁻⁶ /°C.
The elastic modulus for AISI 1020 steel is E = 200 GPa.
The thermal strain without restraint would be εₜₕ = α × ΔT = 12 × 10⁻⁶ × 65 = 7.8 × 10⁻⁴.
Due to full axial restraint, a compressive stress develops to prevent thermal expansion.
The restraint stress is σᵣ = -E × εₜₕ = -200 × 10⁹ × 7.8 × 10⁻⁴ = -156 MPa.
The total axial strain is zero due to the constraint.
The restraint force is F = σᵣ × A = -156 × 10⁶ × π × (0.0175)² = -150.2 kN.
The rod experiences uniform compressive stress throughout its length.
No bending or lateral deformation occurs due to the prismatic geometry and uniform loading.
