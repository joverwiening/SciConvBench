The rod is structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa and coefficient of thermal expansion α = 1e-05 1/°C.
The rod experiences a uniform temperature increase of ΔT = 35 °C.
The rod is placed between two rigid, unyielding supports with no external axial load applied.
The rod is prismatic and undergoes small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.
The thermal expansion is fully restrained by the rigid supports.
The free thermal strain would be εth,free = α × ΔT = 1e-05 × 35 = 3.5e-04.
Since the rod is fully restrained, the total axial strain is zero.
The mechanical strain required to maintain zero total strain is εmech = -εth,free = -3.5e-04.
The axial stress developed in the rod is σ = E × εmech = 200000 × (-3.5e-04) = -70 MPa.
The negative stress indicates compression.
The axial force in the rod is F = σ × A where A is the cross-sectional area.
The reaction forces at the supports equal the axial force in magnitude but opposite in direction.
