The problem analyzes stress distributions in a rectangular prismatic bar under combined loading.

Given parameters: rectangular cross-section with width b = 40 mm and height h = 120 mm, internal normal force P = 30000 N (tension), internal shear force V = 12000 N parallel to width direction.

Additional assumptions: the bar material is homogeneous and isotropic, cross-sections remain plane after deformation, shear deformation follows first-order shear theory, and the shear force acts through the centroid of the cross-section.

Cross-sectional area A = b × h = 40 mm × 120 mm = 4800 mm².

First moment of area about neutral axis Q = b × (h/2) × (h/4) = 40 × 60 × 30 = 72000 mm³.

Average normal stress is σ_avg = P/A = 30000 N / 4800 mm² = 6.25 MPa (tension).

Average shear stress is τ_avg = V/A = 12000 N / 4800 mm² = 2.5 MPa.

Maximum shear stress occurs at the neutral axis and equals τ_max = (3/2) × τ_avg = 3.75 MPa.

The normal stress is uniformly distributed across the cross-section.

The shear stress varies parabolically across the height with maximum at the neutral axis and zero at the top and bottom edges.

The governing relation for normal stress is σ = P/A.

The governing relation for shear stress distribution is τ(y) = VQ(y)/(Ib) where Q(y) is the first moment of area above location y, and I is the second moment of area.
