The shaft is a solid circular shaft with diameter d = 30 mm and length L = 1200 mm.
The applied torque is T = 400000 N·mm.
The material has shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.
The shaft operates under small-deformation, linear-elastic conditions.
The shaft is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The torque is applied at one end while the other end is fixed.
The shaft cross-section remains plane during deformation.
The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer surface of the shaft.
The maximum shear stress is calculated using τ_max = Tc/J where c = d/2 and J = πd⁴/32.
The angle of twist is calculated using φ = TL/(GJ).
The shear stress varies linearly from zero at the center to maximum at the outer surface.
