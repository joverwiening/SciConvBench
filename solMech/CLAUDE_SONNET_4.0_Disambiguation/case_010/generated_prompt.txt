The system consists of two parallel bars rigidly connected at top and bottom plates subjected to axial tension.
Bar 1 is structural steel with cross-sectional area A1 = 150 mm², Young's modulus E1 = 200000 MPa, and length L = 800 mm.
Bar 2 is cast iron with cross-sectional area A2 = 200 mm², Young's modulus E2 = 110000 MPa, and length L = 800 mm.
A total tensile load P = 30000 N is applied to the top plate in pure axial tension.
Both bars undergo identical axial displacement due to rigid plate constraint.
The load distributes between bars proportional to their axial stiffness.
Bar 1 axial stiffness is k1 = E1*A1/L = 200000*150/800 = 37500 N/mm.
Bar 2 axial stiffness is k2 = E2*A2/L = 110000*200/800 = 27500 N/mm.
Total system stiffness is ktotal = k1 + k2 = 65000 N/mm.
Load in Bar 1 is P1 = P*(k1/ktotal) = 30000*(37500/65000) = 17308 N.
Load in Bar 2 is P2 = P*(k2/ktotal) = 30000*(27500/65000) = 12692 N.
System displacement is δ = P/ktotal = 30000/65000 = 0.462 mm.
Stress in Bar 1 is σ1 = P1/A1 = 17308/150 = 115.4 MPa.
Stress in Bar 2 is σ2 = P2/A2 = 12692/200 = 63.5 MPa.
Strain in both bars is ε = δ/L = 0.462/800 = 0.000577.
The governing relation is force equilibrium P = P1 + P2 and compatibility δ1 = δ2.
