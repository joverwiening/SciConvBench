The rod is a straight prismatic circular rod made of structural steel.
The rod has a diameter of 12 mm and a gage length of 800 mm.
An axial tensile load of 30000 N is applied to the rod.
The material properties are Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa and yield strength σ_y = 250 MPa.
Assume the material behaves as linear-elastic under small deformations.
Assume the load is applied uniformly over the cross-section.
Assume the rod is homogeneous and isotropic.
Calculate the cross-sectional area as A = π(d/2)² = π(6 mm)² = 113.1 mm².
Calculate the average normal stress as σ = P/A = 30000 N / 113.1 mm² = 265.2 MPa.
Calculate the axial strain using Hooke's law as ε = σ/E = 265.2 MPa / 200000 MPa = 0.001326.
Calculate the elongation as δ = εL = 0.001326 × 800 mm = 1.061 mm.
Calculate the factor of safety with respect to yielding as FS = σ_y/σ = 250 MPa / 265.2 MPa = 0.943.
The factor of safety is less than 1, indicating the rod will yield under the applied load.
