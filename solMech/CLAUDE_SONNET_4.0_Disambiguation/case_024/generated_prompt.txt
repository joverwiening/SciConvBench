The cantilever beam has length L = 1500 mm and is fixed at one end with the other end free.
A uniformly distributed transverse load w = 1.5 N/mm acts downward over the entire span.
The beam material is structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The second moment of area is I = 1500000 mm^4.
Assume a rectangular cross-section with width b = 50 mm and height h = 100 mm to achieve the specified moment of inertia.
Use small-deformation theory and linear-elastic material behavior.
Assume the beam is prismatic with constant cross-section along its length.
Neglect shear deformation effects and use Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.
The coordinate system has x-axis along the beam length with origin at the fixed end and y-axis pointing upward.
The maximum deflection occurs at the free end and equals wL^4/(8EI).
The deflection at any point x along the beam is v(x) = (w/(24EI))(x^4 - 4L^3*x + 3L^4).
The maximum bending moment occurs at the fixed end and equals wL^2/2.
The bending moment at any point x is M(x) = w(L-x)^2/2.
The maximum shear force occurs at the fixed end and equals wL.
The shear force at any point x is V(x) = w(L-x).
