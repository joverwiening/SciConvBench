The beam is a simply supported Euler-Bernoulli beam with length L = 3000 mm.
The beam has moment of inertia I = 3500000 mm^4 and elastic modulus E = 69000 MPa.
A downward point load P = 4000 N is applied at midspan (x = L/2 = 1500 mm).
The beam supports are pinned connections allowing rotation but preventing vertical displacement.
Small-deformation theory applies with linear-elastic material behavior.
Plane sections remain plane and perpendicular to the neutral axis after deformation.
The beam cross-section is symmetric about the neutral axis.
Shear deformation effects are neglected compared to bending deformation.
The governing differential equation is EI d^4w/dx^4 = q(x) where w is deflection and q(x) is distributed load.
For the point load, q(x) = P δ(x - L/2) where δ is the Dirac delta function.
The boundary conditions are w(0) = 0, w(L) = 0, M(0) = 0, M(L) = 0.
The midspan deflection is w(L/2) = PL^3/(48EI).
The support slopes are dw/dx(0) = PL^2/(16EI) and dw/dx(L) = -PL^2/(16EI).
Calculate midspan deflection: w(L/2) = (4000 N)(3000 mm)^3/(48 × 69000 MPa × 3500000 mm^4) = 0.935 mm.
Calculate support slope magnitude: |dw/dx| = (4000 N)(3000 mm)^2/(16 × 69000 MPa × 3500000 mm^4) = 0.000935 radians.
