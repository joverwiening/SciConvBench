The structural member is a rectangular prismatic beam with width b = 50 mm and height h = 80 mm.
The member is subjected to internal resultant forces of normal force P = 40000 N in tension and shear force V = 12000 N parallel to the width.
The material is assumed to be structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200 GPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.3.
The analysis assumes small deformations and linear elastic material behavior.
The cross-section experiences plane stress conditions with negligible out-of-plane stress.
The normal stress is uniformly distributed as σ = P/A where A = b × h.
The shear stress varies parabolically across the height with maximum value τ_max = 1.5V/A at the neutral axis.

Calculate the maximum shear stress as τ_max = 1.5V/(b × h) = 1.5 × 12000/(0.05 × 0.08) = 4.5 MPa.
