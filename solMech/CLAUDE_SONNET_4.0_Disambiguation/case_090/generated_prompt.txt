The stepped circular bar consists of two segments with different cross-sectional areas connected in series.
Segment 1 has length L1 = 300 mm, diameter d1 = 12 mm, and cross-sectional area A1 = π(d1/2)² = π(6)² = 113.1 mm².
Segment 2 has length L2 = 600 mm, diameter d2 = 30 mm, and cross-sectional area A2 = π(d2/2)² = π(15)² = 706.9 mm².
The applied tensile load is P = 25000 N.
The material has elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The material behavior follows Hooke's law with small deformations and linear elasticity.
The bar is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The loading is assumed to be axial with uniform stress distribution across each cross-section.
The stress in segment 1 is σ1 = P/A1 = 25000/113.1 = 221.0 MPa.
The stress in segment 2 is σ2 = P/A2 = 25000/706.9 = 35.4 MPa.
The elongation of segment 1 is δ1 = σ1L1/E = (221.0)(300)/200000 = 0.332 mm.
The elongation of segment 2 is δ2 = σ2L2/E = (35.4)(600)/200000 = 0.106 mm.
The total elongation of the bar is δtotal = δ1 + δ2 = 0.332 + 0.106 = 0.438 mm.
