The beam is a rectangular cross-section with width b = 50 mm and height h = 100 mm.
The internal bending moment is M = 3000000 N·mm acting in the sagging direction.
The material follows small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior with Hooke's law.
Plane sections remain plane after bending (Euler-Bernoulli beam theory).
The neutral axis passes through the centroid of the cross-section.
Shear deformations are neglected.
The coordinate system has y-axis vertical with y = 0 at the neutral axis, positive upward.
The second moment of area about the neutral axis is I = bh³/12 = 50 × 100³/12 = 4166667 mm⁴.
The bending stress at any fiber location y from the neutral axis is σ = My/I.
The maximum tensile stress occurs at the bottom fiber (y = -50 mm) and equals σ_max = 3000000 × 50 / 4166667 = 36 MPa.
The maximum compressive stress occurs at the top fiber (y = +50 mm) and equals σ_max = -36 MPa.
The stress varies linearly across the height of the beam.
At the neutral axis (y = 0), the bending stress is zero.
