The column is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with Young's modulus E = 69000 MPa.
The column length is L = 2200 mm with effective length factor K = 2.0 (cantilever configuration).
The applied compressive load is P = 25000 N.
The desired factor of safety against buckling is n = 2.5.
The column has a solid circular cross-section with diameter d to be determined.
Small-deformation and linear-elastic behavior are assumed.
The effective length is Le = K × L = 2.0 × 2200 = 4400 mm.
The area moment of inertia for a solid circular cross-section is I = π × d⁴ / 64.
The critical buckling load is given by Euler's formula: Pcr = π² × E × I / Le².
Substituting the moment of inertia: Pcr = π³ × E × d⁴ / (64 × Le²).
The factor of safety is defined as n = Pcr / P.
For the required factor of safety: Pcr = n × P = 2.5 × 25000 = 62500 N.
Solving for diameter: d⁴ = (64 × Le² × Pcr) / (π³ × E).
Substituting values: d⁴ = (64 × (4400)² × 62500) / (π³ × 69000).
Calculate d from the fourth root of the above expression.
The minimum required diameter ensures the column will not buckle under the applied load with the specified safety factor.
Verify that the column remains in the elastic range and that Euler buckling theory applies.
