The system consists of two rigid plates connected by two parallel bars subjected to axial tension.
Bar 1 is AISI 1020 steel with cross-sectional area A1 = 300 mm², Young's modulus E1 = 200000 MPa, and length L = 400 mm.
Bar 2 is cast iron with cross-sectional area A2 = 250 mm², Young's modulus E2 = 110000 MPa, and length L = 400 mm.
A total tensile load P = 20000 N is applied to the top plate in pure axial tension.
The rigid plates remain parallel and undergo equal displacement during loading.
Both bars experience the same axial deformation δ due to the rigid plate constraint.
The total applied load is distributed between the bars as P = P1 + P2.
The axial stress in bar 1 is σ1 = P1/A1.
The axial stress in bar 2 is σ2 = P2/A2.
The axial strain in both bars is equal: ε1 = ε2 = δ/L.
Using Hooke's law: σ1 = E1 × ε and σ2 = E2 × ε.
The force in bar 1 is P1 = E1 × A1 × ε.
The force in bar 2 is P2 = E2 × A2 × ε.
The total stiffness of the parallel system is k = k1 + k2 = (E1×A1 + E2×A2)/L.
The common strain is ε = P/((E1×A1 + E2×A2)) = 20000/((200000×300 + 110000×250)) = 2.353 × 10⁻⁴.
The displacement of the plates is δ = ε × L = 2.353 × 10⁻⁴ × 400 = 0.0941 mm.
The force in bar 1 is P1 = E1 × A1 × ε = 200000 × 300 × 2.353 × 10⁻⁴ = 14118 N.
The force in bar 2 is P2 = E2 × A2 × ε = 110000 × 250 × 2.353 × 10⁻⁴ = 6471 N.
The stress in bar 1 is σ1 = P1/A1 = 14118/300 = 47.06 MPa.
The stress in bar 2 is σ2 = P2/A2 = 6471/250 = 25.88 MPa.
