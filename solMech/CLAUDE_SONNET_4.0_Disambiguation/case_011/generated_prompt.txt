The column has diameter d = 25 mm and length L = 2000 mm.
The material has Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa and yield strength Sy = 250 MPa.
The boundary conditions are fixed-pinned with effective length factor K = 0.7.
The applied compressive load is P = 25000 N.
Calculate the cross-sectional area A = π(d/2)² = π(12.5)² = 490.87 mm².
Calculate the second moment of area I = π(d⁴)/64 = π(25⁴)/64 = 19174 mm⁴.
Calculate the effective length Le = K × L = 0.7 × 2000 = 1400 mm.
Calculate the radius of gyration r = √(I/A) = √(19174/490.87) = 6.25 mm.
Calculate the slenderness ratio λ = Le/r = 1400/6.25 = 224.
Calculate the compressive stress σ = P/A = 25000/490.87 = 50.93 MPa.
Compare the applied stress to yield strength: σ/Sy = 50.93/250 = 0.204, indicating no yielding occurs.
Calculate the Euler critical load Pcr = π²EI/Le² = π²(200000)(19174)/(1400)² = 19.29 × 10⁶ N·mm / 1.96 × 10⁶ mm² = 9.84 kN.
Calculate the Euler critical stress σcr = Pcr/A = 9840/490.87 = 20.05 MPa.
Compare applied load to critical load: P/Pcr = 25000/9840 = 2.54, indicating the applied load exceeds the critical buckling load.
The column will fail by elastic buckling before yielding occurs.
The buckling safety factor is Pcr/P = 9840/25000 = 0.39, indicating instability.
The column is unstable under the given loading conditions and will buckle elastically.
