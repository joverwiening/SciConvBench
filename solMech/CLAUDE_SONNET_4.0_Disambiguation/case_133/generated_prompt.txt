The beam has a span length of 3000 mm (reasonable for typical structural applications).
The beam experiences a maximum bending moment of M_max = 6000000 N·mm.
The maximum bending moment occurs at mid-span due to a uniformly distributed load.
The beam has a rectangular cross-section with width b = 30 mm.
The allowable bending stress is σ_allow = 90 MPa.
The material follows linear elastic behavior with small deformation assumptions.
The beam is simply supported at both ends.
The governing equation for bending stress is σ = M·y/I where σ is bending stress, M is bending moment, y is distance from neutral axis, and I is second moment of area.
For a rectangular cross-section, the second moment of area is I = b·h³/12 where h is the height.
The maximum bending stress occurs at the extreme fiber where y = h/2.
The maximum bending stress equation becomes σ_max = M_max·h/(2·I) = M_max/(b·h²/6).
The design constraint is σ_max ≤ σ_allow.
Substituting values: 6000000/(30·h²/6) ≤ 90.
Solving for height: h² ≥ 6000000·6/(30·90) = 13333.33.
The minimum required beam height is h = 115.5 mm.
Use h = 120 mm for practical design purposes.
