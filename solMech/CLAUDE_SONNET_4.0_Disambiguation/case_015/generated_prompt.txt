The cantilever beam is fixed at one end and free at the other end with length L = 1200 mm.
A downward point load P = 2000 N is applied at the free end of the beam.
The beam is made of AISI 1020 steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The beam has a constant second moment of area I = 2000000 mm^4 about the bending axis.
The beam follows Euler-Bernoulli beam theory assumptions including plane sections remain plane and small deflections.
The material behaves as linear elastic with small deformations.
Shear deformation effects are neglected compared to bending deformation.
The beam is prismatic with constant cross-sectional properties along its length.
The coordinate system has x-axis along the beam length from fixed end to free end and y-axis pointing upward.
The governing differential equation is EI d^4v/dx^4 = q(x) where v is deflection and q(x) is distributed load.
For this point load case the moment equation is M(x) = -P(L-x) for 0 ≤ x ≤ L.
The deflection equation is EI d^2v/dx^2 = M(x) = -P(L-x).
Boundary conditions are v(0) = 0 and dv/dx(0) = 0 at the fixed end.
The maximum deflection occurs at the free end and equals v(L) = PL^3/(3EI).
The maximum slope occurs at the free end and equals dv/dx(L) = PL^2/(2EI).
The maximum bending moment occurs at the fixed end and equals M(0) = -PL.
The shear force is constant along the beam and equals V = -P.
