The stepped solid circular shaft analysis requires determining stress and total twist under torsional loading.

Given parameters: Segment AB has length L1 = 400 mm and diameter d1 = 30 mm, segment BC has length L2 = 700 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm, torque T = 600000 N·mm applied at end C, end A is fixed, shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.

Additional assumptions: The shaft material is homogeneous and isotropic, cross-sections remain plane during deformation, the shaft is prismatic within each segment, stress concentrations at the step change are neglected, and the shaft behaves in a linear elastic manner.

The polar moment of inertia for each circular section is J = πd⁴/32.

For segment AB: J1 = π(30)⁴/32 = 79521.6 mm⁴.

For segment BC: J2 = π(30)⁴/32 = 79521.6 mm⁴.

The maximum shear stress in each segment occurs at the outer fiber and is given by τ_max = Tr/J = Td/(2J).

For segment AB: τ_max1 = (600000)(15)/79521.6 = 113.1 MPa.

For segment BC: τ_max2 = (600000)(15)/79521.6 = 113.1 MPa.

The angle of twist for each segment is φ = TL/(GJ).

For segment AB: φ1 = (600000)(400)/(77519.4)(79521.6) = 0.0389 radians.

For segment BC: φ2 = (600000)(700)/(77519.4)(79521.6) = 0.0681 radians.

The total angle of twist is φ_total = φ1 + φ2 = 0.0389 + 0.0681 = 0.107 radians.

The governing torsion equation is τ/r = T/J = Gγ/r where τ is shear stress, r is radial distance, T is applied torque, J is polar moment of inertia, G is shear modulus, and γ is shear strain.
