The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The cantilever beam has a length L = 1500 mm.
The beam material has a Young's Modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The beam's cross-section has a moment of inertia I = 2500000 mm^4.
A downward point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end of the cantilever beam.
There is no additional bending moment loading.
The relevant response quantity to be determined is the cantilever end deflection under the end load, calculated using Castigliano's theorem.
The governing relation for the deflection (delta) at the free end due to a point load P at the free end is given by delta = (P * L^3) / (3 * E * I).
