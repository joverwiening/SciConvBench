The problem assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics for a solid circular shaft.
The applied torque T is 800000 N·mm.
The shear modulus G of the shaft material is 77519.4 MPa.
The maximum allowable angle of twist φ_allow is 1.0°.
The length L of the solid circular shaft is 1200 mm.
The design objective is to determine the minimum shaft diameter based on the angle-of-twist limit.
The governing relation for the angle of twist φ of a solid circular shaft subjected to torque is φ = (T * L) / (G * J).
The polar moment of inertia J for a solid circular shaft with diameter D is (π/32) * D^4.
The maximum allowable angle of twist must be converted from degrees to radians for calculation.
The design criterion requires that the calculated angle of twist φ must be less than or equal to the maximum allowable angle of twist φ_allow.
The "shear loading" mentioned in the initial problem statement is assumed to refer to the shear stress induced by the applied torque, and no additional transverse shear force is considered for the angle-of-twist design.
