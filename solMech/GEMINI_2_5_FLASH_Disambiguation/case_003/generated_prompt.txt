The problem involves a stepped circular bar with two segments.
The first segment has a length L1 = 500 mm and a diameter d1 = 30 mm.
The second segment has a length L2 = 600 mm and a diameter d2 = 25 mm.
The bar is subjected to a tension loading P = 20000 N.
The material of the bar has a Young's Modulus E = 69000 MPa.
The analysis assumes small-deformation and linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The relevant response quantities to be determined are the stress in each segment and the total elongation of the bar.
Additional assumptions include uniform stress distribution within each segment and negligible stress concentrations at the step.
The governing relations for stress are sigma = P/A, where A is the cross-sectional area.
The governing relations for elongation are delta = (P*L)/(A*E) for each segment, and total elongation is the sum of individual segment elongations.
