The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The material is homogeneous and isotropic.
The bar is perfectly straight and free to deform.
The axial load is applied instantaneously and centrally at the centroid of the cross-section.
Stress concentration effects are neglected.
The bar is initially at rest.
The static stress is calculated as the applied load divided by the cross-sectional area.
For a suddenly applied load, the dynamic load factor is 2.
The maximum stress is twice the static stress.
The maximum strain is the maximum stress divided by the Young's Modulus.
The maximum displacement is the maximum strain multiplied by the length of the bar.
The maximum strain energy stored in the bar is one-half of the maximum dynamic load multiplied by the maximum displacement.
The cross-sectional area A is 300 mm^2.
The Young's Modulus E is 200000 MPa.
The magnitude of the suddenly applied axial load P is 8000 N.
The length L is 800 mm.
