The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The material is assumed to be steel with a Young's Modulus E = 200 GPa and a Poisson's Ratio ν = 0.3.
The solid circular shaft has a diameter d = 35 mm.
The shaft is subjected to a bending moment M = 3,000,000 N·mm and a torsional moment T = 500,000 N·mm.
The analysis will determine the maximum normal stress due to bending.
The analysis will determine the maximum shear stress due to torsion.
The analysis will determine the equivalent bending moment and equivalent torsional moment for combined loading.
The analysis will determine the maximum normal stress and maximum shear stress under combined bending and torsion.
The analysis will determine the principal stresses and maximum shear stress at the critical point.
The analysis will determine the principal strains and maximum shear strain at the critical point.
The governing relations for stress and strain in a circular shaft under combined bending and torsion will be presented.
