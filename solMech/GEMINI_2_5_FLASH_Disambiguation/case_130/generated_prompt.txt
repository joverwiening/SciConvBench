The analysis will assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The structural member is a rectangular prismatic member with width b = 60 mm and height h = 100 mm.
The internal resultant normal force is P = 40000 N (tension).
The internal shear force is V = 16000 N.
The Young's Modulus (E) of the material is assumed to be 200 GPa (for steel).
The Poisson's ratio (ν) of the material is assumed to be 0.3 (for steel).
The analysis will determine the plane stress state resulting from the combined average normal and shear stresses acting on the cross-section.
The average normal stress (σx) will be calculated as P divided by the cross-sectional area (b*h).
The average shear stress (τxy) will be calculated as V divided by the cross-sectional area (b*h).
The principal stresses (σ1, σ2) will be evaluated from the calculated plane stress state.
The principal strains (ε1, ε2) will be evaluated from the principal stresses using the generalized Hooke's Law for plane stress.
Additional assumptions include uniform distribution of forces over the cross-section and that the out-of-plane normal stress is zero for plane stress.
The governing relations for stress, strain, and their transformations will be presented.
