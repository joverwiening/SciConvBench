The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The solid circular shaft has a diameter d = 35 mm.
The total length of the shaft is L = 1200 mm.
The material of the shaft is structural steel with a shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.
A torque T1 = 200000 N·mm is applied at x = 1200 mm.
A torque T2 = -250000 N·mm is applied at x = 800 mm.
No transverse shear forces or distributed loads are applied to the shaft.
The analysis will determine the internal torque distribution along the shaft.
The analysis will determine the maximum shear stress in the shaft.
The analysis will determine the angle of twist along the shaft.
The governing relations for torsional stress and angle of twist will be presented.
