The material properties for AISI 1020 steel are Young's Modulus E = 200000 MPa and coefficient of thermal expansion α = 2e-05 1/°C.
The prismatic rod has a length L = 800 mm and a diameter d = 20 mm.
The rod is subjected to a uniform temperature increase ΔT = 50 °C.
The rod is under full axial restraint, meaning the total axial strain is zero.
Small-deformation and linear-elastic solid mechanics assumptions apply.
The cross-sectional area A is calculated from the diameter.
The thermal strain ε_thermal is calculated as α * ΔT.
The mechanical strain ε_mechanical is calculated as -ε_thermal due to full restraint.
The axial stress σ is calculated as E * ε_mechanical.
The axial force F is calculated as σ * A.
The relevant response quantities to be determined are thermal strain, mechanical strain, axial stress, and axial force.
