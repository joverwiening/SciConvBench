The problem involves a short rectangular column.
The column cross-section has a width b = 100 mm and a height h = 100 mm.
The column is subjected to a single eccentric axial compressive load P = 90000 N.
The eccentricity of the load is e = 15 mm from the centroid along the width direction.
The material behavior is assumed to be small-deformation, linear-elastic.
The analysis should determine the stress distribution across the cross-section.
The analysis should identify the maximum compressive stress and any tensile stress present.
The analysis should determine the location of the neutral axis, if it exists within the cross-section.
The governing relations for combined axial and bending stress should be clearly presented.
