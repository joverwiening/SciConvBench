The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The shaft is a solid circular shaft.
The shaft is subjected to a pure torsion loading.
The length of the shaft is L = 1000 mm.
The diameter of the shaft is d = 30 mm.
The applied torque is T = 450000 N·mm.
The shear modulus of the shaft material is G = 25939.8 MPa.
The polar moment of inertia J is calculated as (pi * d^4) / 32.
The maximum shear stress tau_max is calculated as (T * d) / (2 * J).
The strain energy U is calculated as (T^2 * L) / (2 * G * J).
The angle of twist phi is determined using Castigliano's theorem as dU/dT, which simplifies to (T * L) / (G * J).
