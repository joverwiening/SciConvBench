Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The column is a solid circular column.
The column is subjected to an axial compressive load.
The effective length factor K is 0.7.
The Young's Modulus E is 200000 MPa.
The yield strength Sy is 250 MPa.
The length L is 2000 mm.
The diameter D is 25 mm.
The applied compressive load P is 25000 N.
Calculate the cross-sectional area A of the column.
Calculate the area moment of inertia I of the column.
Calculate the radius of gyration r of the column.
Calculate the slenderness ratio (KL/r) of the column.
Calculate the critical Euler buckling load P_cr using the formula P_cr = (pi^2 * E * I) / (K*L)^2.
Calculate the critical stress for buckling sigma_cr = P_cr / A.
Calculate the load at which yielding occurs P_y = Sy * A.
Calculate the stress due to the applied load sigma_applied = P / A.
Compare the applied load P with the critical Euler buckling load P_cr.
Compare the applied load P with the yielding load P_y.
Determine if the column will buckle or yield first by comparing P_cr and P_y.
State the governing failure mode based on the comparison.
