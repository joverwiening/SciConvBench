The material is AISI 1020 steel.
The rod has a circular cross-section with an initial diameter of 20 mm.
The rod is subjected to an axial tensile load of 35000 N.
The Young's Modulus of AISI 1020 steel is 200000 MPa.
The Poisson's ratio of AISI 1020 steel is 0.30.
The analysis assumes small-deformation and linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The relevant response quantities to be determined are axial stress, axial strain, lateral strain, and the change in diameter due to the Poisson effect.
The governing relations are: axial stress equals axial load divided by the initial cross-sectional area; axial strain equals axial stress divided by Young's Modulus; lateral strain equals the negative product of Poisson's ratio and axial strain; and the change in diameter equals lateral strain multiplied by the initial diameter.
