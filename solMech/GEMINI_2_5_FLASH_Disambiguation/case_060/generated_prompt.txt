The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The column is subjected to compressive loading.
The end conditions of the column are fixed-fixed.
The Modulus of Elasticity (E) of the column material is 200000 MPa.
The length (L) of the column is 2000 mm.
The moment of inertia (I) of the column's cross-section is 800000 mm^4.
The Euler critical buckling load (P_cr) will be determined using the formula P_cr = (pi^2 * E * I) / (K*L)^2, where K is the effective length factor.
For fixed-fixed end conditions, the effective length factor (K) is 0.5.
The governing relation for the Euler critical buckling load is P_cr = (pi^2 * E * I) / (0.5 * L)^2.
The relevant response quantity to be determined is the Euler critical buckling load.
A buckling/instability check will be performed by computing this critical load, which represents the maximum compressive load the column can withstand before buckling.
