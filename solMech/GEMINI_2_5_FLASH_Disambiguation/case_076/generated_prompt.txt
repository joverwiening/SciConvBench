The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam is a simply supported beam with a total length L = 4000 mm.
A concentrated vertical load P = 12000 N acts downward at a point located 2000 mm from support A.
There is no tension loading applied to the beam.
There is no moment loading applied to the beam.
The relevant response quantities to be determined are the internal shear force and bending moment along the beam's length.
The governing relations for internal shear force V(x) and bending moment M(x) will be derived from equilibrium equations applied to free-body diagrams of beam segments.
Support reactions at A and B will be calculated using global equilibrium equations.
