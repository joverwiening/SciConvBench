The task is to analyze a simply supported beam of length L = 6000 mm.
The beam is subjected to a single concentrated vertical load P = 20000 N acting downward at a distance of 2000 mm from support A.
The analysis should assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The relevant response quantities to be determined are the internal shear force and bending moment along the beam's length.
The analysis should clearly state any additional assumptions made.
The analysis should present the governing relations used for determining shear and bending moment.
