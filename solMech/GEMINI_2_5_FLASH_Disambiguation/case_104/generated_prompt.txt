The task is to determine the elongation of a vertical bar due to its self-weight.
The bar is assumed to be homogeneous, have a uniform cross-section, and be fixed at its top end.
Small-deformation and linear-elastic solid mechanics principles apply.
The Young's Modulus of the bar material is 69000 MPa.
The length of the bar is 3000 mm.
The density of the bar material is 8900 kg/m^3.
The cross-sectional area of the bar is 300 mm^2.
The gravitational acceleration is 9.81 m/s^2.
The governing relation for the elongation (δ) of a vertical bar due to self-weight is δ = (ρ * g * L^2) / (2 * E).
Using the provided values, the elongation of the bar is calculated as 5.69 micrometers.
