A simply supported beam of length 3000 mm is considered.
The beam material has a Young's Modulus (E) of 200000 MPa.
The beam's cross-section has a moment of inertia (I) of 1200000 mm^4.
A downward point load (P) of 800 N is applied at x = 2000 mm from the left support.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The analysis assumes the beam is prismatic with constant E and I.
The analysis assumes ideal supports with no friction or settlement.
The analysis assumes the self-weight of the beam is negligible.
The analysis assumes shear deformation is negligible, consistent with Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.
The unit-load method shall be used to determine the vertical deflection at the point load location (x = 2000 mm).
The governing relation for deflection (delta) using the unit-load method is given by the integral of (M * m / (E * I)) dx from 0 to L.
M represents the bending moment distribution due to the actual applied load P.
m represents the bending moment distribution due to a unit downward load applied at the point where deflection is desired (x = 2000 mm).
The final result should be the numerical value of the deflection.
