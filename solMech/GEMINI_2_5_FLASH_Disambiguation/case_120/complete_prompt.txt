Use N–mm–MPa units and treat E as constant.

A bar of length L = 500 mm is subjected to a constant axial tensile force P = 12000 N. The material is AISI 1020 steel with E = 200000 MPa. The bar’s cross-sectional area varies linearly along its length according to

A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L),

where x is measured from the left end, A0 = 200 mm^2, and k = 1.0.

Using the axial deformation integral δ = ∫[0→L] P/(A(x)E) dx, compute the elongation of the bar.
