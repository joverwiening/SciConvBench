The problem involves a prismatic bar subjected to axial loading.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The bar has a uniform cross-sectional area A = 500 mm^2.
The material has a Young's Modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The total length of the bar is 1400 mm.
The left end of the bar, point A, is fixed, meaning the displacement at A is zero (u_A = 0).
A point load of 15000 N is applied at point B, located 700 mm from A.
A point load of -12000 N is applied at point C, located 1100 mm from A.
A point load of 10000 N is applied at point D, located 1400 mm from A.
The bar is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The loads are assumed to be applied axially along the centroidal axis of the bar.
The governing relations include the equilibrium equation, the constitutive law (Hooke's Law), and the strain-displacement relation.
The relevant response quantities to be determined are the axial force distribution, axial stress distribution, axial strain distribution, axial displacement distribution along the bar, and the reaction force at the fixed end A.
