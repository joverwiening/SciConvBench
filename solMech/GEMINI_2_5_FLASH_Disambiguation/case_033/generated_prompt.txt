The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The analysis assumes Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.
The beam is simply supported.
The beam has a length L = 2500 mm.
The Young's Modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The moment of inertia I = 1200000 mm^4.
A point load P = 2500 N acts downward at the midspan of the beam.
The analysis will determine the midspan deflection.
The analysis will determine the slope at the supports.
The governing relations for deflection and slope of a simply supported Euler-Bernoulli beam with a midspan point load will be presented.
