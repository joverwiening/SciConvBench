The problem involves a simply supported beam under small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam has a length L = 3000 mm.
A downward point load P = 2000 N is applied at the midspan of the beam.
The Young's Modulus of the beam material is E = 200000 MPa.
The moment of inertia of the beam's cross-section is I = 3000000 mm^4.
The task is to determine the midspan deflection of the beam using Castigliano's Second Theorem.
The governing relation for the deflection at the point of load application using Castigliano's theorem is delta = dU/dP, where U is the total strain energy due to bending.
For a simply supported beam with a central point load P, the maximum deflection at midspan is given by (P * L^3) / (48 * E * I).
