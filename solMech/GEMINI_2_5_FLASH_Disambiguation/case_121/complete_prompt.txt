Use N–mm–MPa units. Use superposition of axial stress and bending stress.

A short rectangular column has cross-section width b = 120 mm and height h = 200 mm. A compressive load P = 40000 N is applied with an eccentricity e = 25 mm from the centroid along the width direction (i.e., offset toward one side, producing bending about the centroidal z-axis). Assume the load is applied parallel to the column axis and that plane sections remain plane.

Compute the normal stress at the two extreme edges on the width (the near edge and the far edge) at the cross section. Determine whether any portion of the cross section is in tension.
