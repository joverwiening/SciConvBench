The problem involves a simply supported beam.
The beam is subjected to a uniformly distributed transverse load.
The magnitude of the uniformly distributed transverse load is w = 0.3 N/mm.
The length of the beam is L = 4500 mm.
The Young's Modulus of the beam material is E = 200000 MPa.
The moment of inertia of the beam's cross-section is I = 2000000 mm^4.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Determine the deflection and slope of the beam.
The governing relation for deflection v(x) is given by the differential equation EIv'''' = w.
The boundary conditions for a simply supported beam are v(0) = 0, v(L) = 0, v''(0) = 0, and v''(L) = 0.
The maximum deflection occurs at the mid-span (x = L/2) and is given by v_max = (5 * w * L^4) / (384 * E * I).
The maximum slope occurs at the supports (x = 0 and x = L) and is given by theta_max = (w * L^3) / (24 * E * I).
