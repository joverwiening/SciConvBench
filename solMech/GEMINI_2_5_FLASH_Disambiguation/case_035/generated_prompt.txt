Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The column has a solid circular cross-section.
The applied compressive load P is 25000 N.
The Young's Modulus E of the column material is 69000 MPa.
The length of the column L is 2200 mm.
The effective length factor K for the column is 2.0.
The target factor of safety for buckling design n is 2.5.
Determine the minimum required diameter of the solid circular column to achieve the target factor of safety against buckling.
Calculate the actual factor of safety against buckling for the designed column.
The governing relation for the critical buckling load (Euler buckling) is P_cr = (pi^2 * E * I) / (K * L)^2, where I is the area moment of inertia for a solid circular cross-section (I = pi * d^4 / 64).
The design buckling load P_design is P * n.
The column must be designed such that P_cr >= P_design.
