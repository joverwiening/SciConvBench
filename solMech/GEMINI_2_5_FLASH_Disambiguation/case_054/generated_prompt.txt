The problem involves the analysis of an axially loaded bar under small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The bar has a Young's Modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The bar has a length L = 800 mm.
The bar is subjected to an axial load P = 12000 N.
The cross-sectional area at x=0 is A0 = 250 mm^2.
The cross-sectional area at x=L is A(L) = 400 mm^2.
The cross-sectional area varies linearly along the length of the bar.
The axial load is applied uniformly across the cross-section.
The governing relation for the linearly varying cross-sectional area A(x) is A(x) = A0 + (A(L) - A0) * (x / L).
The governing relation for axial stress sigma(x) is sigma(x) = P / A(x).
The governing relation for axial strain epsilon(x) is epsilon(x) = sigma(x) / E.
The governing relation for the total axial deformation delta is the integral of (P / (A(x) * E)) with respect to x from 0 to L.
Determine the total axial deformation of the bar.
Determine the axial stress distribution along the length of the bar.
Determine the axial strain distribution along the length of the bar.
