The problem involves a stepped solid circular shaft.
The shaft has two segments.
The first segment has a diameter d1 = 20 mm and a length L1 = 300 mm.
The second segment has a diameter d2 = 30 mm and a length L2 = 500 mm.
The shaft is subjected to an applied torque T = 300000 N·mm.
The shear modulus of the shaft material is G = 25939.8 MPa.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Determine the maximum shear stress in each segment of the shaft.
Determine the angle of twist for each segment of the shaft.
Determine the total angle of twist for the entire shaft.
State any additional assumptions made during the analysis.
Present the governing relations used for stress and twist calculations.
