The system consists of two individual bars arranged in parallel, connected between two rigid plates.
The composite bar is subjected to a total tensile mechanical loading P = 20000 N.
Bar 1 is AISI 1020 steel with Young's Modulus E1 = 200000 MPa and cross-sectional area A1 = 300 mm^2.
Bar 2 is Cast iron with Young's Modulus E2 = 110000 MPa and cross-sectional area A2 = 250 mm^2.
Both bars have a length L = 400 mm.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Additional assumptions include: the bars are prismatic, the applied load is purely axial, the rigid plates ensure uniform axial displacement for both bars, and self-weight and buckling effects are negligible.
The governing relations for the system are: total applied load P equals the sum of forces in individual bars (P = F1 + F2), and the axial displacement of both bars is equal (delta1 = delta2 = delta).
The constitutive relation for each bar is Hooke's Law (stress = Young's Modulus * strain), and stress is force divided by area (stress = F / A), and strain is displacement divided by length (strain = delta / L).
The relevant response quantities to be determined are: the axial displacement of the composite bar (delta), the axial force in each individual bar (F1, F2), the axial stress in each individual bar (sigma1, sigma2), and the axial strain in each individual bar (epsilon1, epsilon2).
