The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam is simply supported at both ends.
The beam is subjected to a uniformly distributed transverse load of w = 0.7 N/mm over its entire length.
The length of the beam is L = 2500 mm.
The Young's Modulus of the beam material is E = 200000 MPa.
The moment of inertia of the beam's cross-section is I = 2000000 mm^4.
The governing relation for the deflection curve v(x) along the beam is given by v(x) = (w * x / (24 * E * I)) * (L^3 - 2 * L * x^2 + x^3).
The maximum deflection occurs at the midspan (x = L/2) and is calculated as v_max = (5 * w * L^4) / (384 * E * I).
The governing relation for the slope theta(x) along the beam is given by theta(x) = dv/dx = (w / (24 * E * I)) * (L^3 - 6 * L * x^2 + 4 * x^3).
The maximum slope occurs at the supports (x = 0 and x = L) and is calculated as theta_max = (w * L^3) / (24 * E * I).
The deflection and slope values will be determined using these relations.
