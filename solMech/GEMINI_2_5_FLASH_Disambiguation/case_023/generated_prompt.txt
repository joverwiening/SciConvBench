Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The system is a cantilever beam.
The beam has a length of 2500 mm.
The beam is subjected to a uniformly distributed transverse load of 1.0 N/mm.
The Young's Modulus of the beam material is 200000 MPa.
The area moment of inertia of the beam's cross-section is 1000000 mm^4.
Determine the deflection at the free end of the cantilever beam.
Determine the slope at the free end of the cantilever beam.
The governing relations for deflection and slope of a cantilever beam under a uniformly distributed load will be presented.
