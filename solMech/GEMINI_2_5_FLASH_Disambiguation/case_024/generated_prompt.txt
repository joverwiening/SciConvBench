The analysis will assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The analysis will consider a cantilever beam.
The beam is subjected to a single uniform distributed load of w = 1.5 N/mm downward.
The length of the cantilever beam is L = 1500 mm.
The Young's Modulus of the beam material is E = 200000 MPa.
The moment of inertia of the beam's cross-section is I = 1500000 mm^4.
Additional assumptions include the applicability of Euler-Bernoulli beam theory, implying the beam is slender, cross-sections remain plane and perpendicular to the neutral axis, shear deformation is negligible, and the material is isotropic and homogeneous.
The governing relation for the maximum transverse deflection (delta_max) at the free end of a cantilever beam under a uniform distributed load is delta_max = (w * L^4) / (8 * E * I).
The primary response quantity to be determined is the maximum transverse deflection of the cantilever beam.
