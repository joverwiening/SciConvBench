The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The stepped circular bar has two segments.
The first segment has a diameter of 12 mm and a length of 300 mm.
The second segment has a diameter of 30 mm and a length of 800 mm.
The material's Young's Modulus is 200000 MPa.
A tension load of 35000 N is applied to the bar.
The analysis will determine the stress in each segment.
The analysis will determine the total elongation of the bar.
The governing relations for stress and elongation in each segment will be presented.
The governing relation for total elongation will be presented.
