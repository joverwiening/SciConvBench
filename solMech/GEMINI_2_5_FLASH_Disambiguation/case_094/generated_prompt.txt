The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The analysis assumes Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.
The beam has a length L = 3500 mm.
The beam is simply supported at both ends.
The beam is subjected to a uniformly distributed transverse load w = 0.3 N/mm along its entire length.
The beam is subjected to a point load P = 1200 N at its midspan (L/2).
The material's Young's Modulus (E) and the beam's Area Moment of Inertia (I) are assumed to be known constants.
The relevant response quantity to be determined is the transverse deflection of the beam.
The deflection will be determined using the principle of superposition.
The total deflection is the sum of the deflection due to the uniformly distributed load and the deflection due to the point load.
The deflection due to the uniformly distributed load (w) for a simply supported beam is given by v_w(x) = (w*x / (24*E*I)) * (L^3 - 2*L*x^2 + x^3).
The deflection due to the point load (P) at midspan for a simply supported beam is given by v_P(x) = (P*x / (48*E*I)) * (3*L^2 - 4*x^2) for 0 <= x <= L/2, and symmetrically for L/2 < x <= L.
The total deflection v(x) = v_w(x) + v_P(x).
