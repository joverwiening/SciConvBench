A prismatic bar with a uniform cross-sectional area A = 300 mm^2 and Young's Modulus E = 200000 MPa is considered. The total length of the bar is 1500 mm. The bar is subjected to multiple axial point loads. A point load of 18000 N is applied at 500 mm from the left end. A point load of -12000 N is applied at 1100 mm from the left end. A point load of 16000 N is applied at 1500 mm from the left end. The left end of the bar is fixed, meaning its axial displacement is zero. The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics. The bar is straight, and self-weight is negligible. The objective is to determine the axial force distribution, axial stress distribution, axial strain distribution, and axial displacement distribution along the bar, as well as the reaction force at the fixed support. The governing relations include equilibrium equations for segments, the constitutive relation (stress equals Young's Modulus times strain), and the strain-displacement relation (strain equals the derivative of displacement with respect to axial position).
