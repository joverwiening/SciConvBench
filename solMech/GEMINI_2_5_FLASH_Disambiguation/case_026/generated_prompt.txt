The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The structure is a cantilever beam.
A downward point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end of the beam.
The length of the cantilever beam is L = 1500 mm.
The Young's Modulus of the beam material is E = 200000 MPa.
The moment of inertia of the beam's cross-section is I = 800000 mm^4.
The objective is to determine the deflection at the free end of the cantilever beam.
Castigliano's second theorem will be used to determine the deflection.
The governing relation for the bending moment M(x) along the beam due to the point load P at the free end is M(x) = -P(L-x).
The strain energy U due to bending is given by the integral from 0 to L of (M(x)^2 / (2EI)) dx.
The deflection at the free end (δ) is calculated as the partial derivative of the total strain energy U with respect to the applied load P, i.e., δ = ∂U/∂P.
