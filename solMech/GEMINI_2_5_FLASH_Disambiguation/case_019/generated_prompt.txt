The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The solid circular shaft has a diameter of 30 mm and a total length of 1200 mm.
The shaft material is AISI 1020 steel.
A torque T1 = 200000 N·mm is applied at x = 1200 mm.
A torque T2 = -200000 N·mm is applied at x = 400 mm.
The shaft is fixed at x = 0.
No shear forces or distributed shear loads are applied to the shaft.
The analysis will determine the reaction torque at the fixed end.
The analysis will determine the distribution of internal torque along the shaft.
The analysis will determine the distribution of the angle of twist along the shaft.
The analysis will determine the maximum shear stress in the shaft.
The analysis will determine the location of the maximum shear stress.
The governing relations for torsional shear stress and angle of twist will be presented.
The polar moment of inertia for the solid circular shaft will be calculated.
Material properties for AISI 1020 steel, specifically the shear modulus, will be used.
