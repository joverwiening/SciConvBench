The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The hollow circular shaft has an outer diameter of 40 mm and an inner diameter of 30 mm.
The shaft material has a shear modulus G of 39179.1 MPa.
The shaft length is 1000 mm.
The shaft is subjected to an applied torque of 300000 N·mm.
The applied shear loading is assumed to be zero, focusing the analysis on pure torsion.
The shaft is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The relevant response quantities to be determined are the polar moment of inertia, the maximum shear stress, the angle of twist, and the maximum shear strain.
The governing relations are: Polar moment of inertia J = (π/32) * (Do^4 - Di^4), Maximum shear stress τ_max = (T * Do/2) / J, Angle of twist φ = (T * L) / (G * J), and Maximum shear strain γ_max = τ_max / G.
