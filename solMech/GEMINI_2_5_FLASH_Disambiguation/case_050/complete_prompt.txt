Use N–mm–MPa units. Assume linear elasticity.

A hollow circular shaft made of brass has outer diameter do = 40 mm and inner diameter di = 30 mm (wall thickness 5 mm). The shaft length is L = 1000 mm and it is subjected to a constant torque T = 300000 N·mm.

Compute (i) the maximum shear stress at the outer surface, and (ii) the angle of twist over the length. Use J = π(do^4 − di^4)/32 and G = 39179.1 MPa.
