The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The rod has a length L = 800 mm.
The rod has a diameter d = 25 mm.
The cross-sectional area A of the rod is calculated as A = π d^2/4.
The Young's modulus of the rod material is E = 200000 MPa.
The weight of the dropped object is W = 1500 N.
The gravitational acceleration is assumed to be g = 9.81 m/s^2.
The height from which the weight is dropped is h = 200 mm.
There is no additional static axial load applied to the rod beyond the impact load caused by the dropped weight.
The analysis will determine the relevant response quantities for the impact loading case.
The relevant response quantities include the maximum dynamic stress in the rod, the maximum dynamic deformation of the rod, and the impact factor.
The governing relations for these quantities will be presented clearly.
