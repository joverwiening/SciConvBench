Use N–mm–MPa units. Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory (small deflection, linear elastic).

A cantilever beam of length L = 800 mm is fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L. The beam is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with E = 69000 MPa and has constant second moment of area I = 1600000 mm^4. A downward point load P = 2500 N is applied at the free end.

Determine (i) the slope at the free end and (ii) the vertical deflection at the free end. Report slope in radians (or degrees) and deflection in mm.
