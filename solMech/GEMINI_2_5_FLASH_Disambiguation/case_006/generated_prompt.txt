The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam is prismatic with constant flexural rigidity (EI).
The beam's self-weight is negligible.
The supports are ideal, providing no friction or settlement.
Shear deformation is negligible.
The simply supported beam has a total length L = 2400 mm.
The beam is subjected to a downward point load P = 1200 N at x = 1200 mm from the left support.
The flexural rigidity of the beam is EI = (200000 MPa) * (3500000 mm^4).
The relevant response quantity to be determined is the deflection at the point load location (x = 1200 mm).
The deflection will be calculated using the unit-load method.
The governing relation for deflection (delta) is the integral from 0 to L of (M(x) * m(x)) / (EI) dx.
M(x) represents the bending moment distribution due to the actual load P.
m(x) represents the bending moment distribution due to a unit downward load applied at x = 1200 mm.
