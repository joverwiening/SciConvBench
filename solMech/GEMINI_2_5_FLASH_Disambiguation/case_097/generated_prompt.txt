The problem involves a stepped solid circular shaft.
The shaft consists of two segments.
The first segment (AB) has a length of 400 mm and a diameter of 25 mm.
The second segment (BC) has a length of 900 mm and a diameter of 30 mm.
The shaft material is linear-elastic with a shear modulus (G) of 25939.8 MPa.
The shaft is subjected to an applied torque (T) of 750000 N·mm.
The applied shear force is assumed to be zero.
The analysis assumes small-deformation linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The relevant response quantities to be determined are the maximum shear stress in each segment and the total angle of twist.
The governing relation for shear stress (τ) due to torsion is τ = (T * r) / J, where T is the applied torque, r is the radial distance from the center, and J is the polar moment of inertia.
The governing relation for the angle of twist (φ) for a segment is φ = (T * L) / (G * J), where T is the applied torque, L is the segment length, G is the shear modulus, and J is the polar moment of inertia.
The polar moment of inertia (J) for a solid circular shaft is (π * d^4) / 32, where d is the diameter.
The total angle of twist is the sum of the angles of twist for each segment.
