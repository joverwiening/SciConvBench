The system consists of two prismatic bars mounted in parallel between two rigid plates.
A total tensile load P = 30000 N is applied to the top plate.
The Young's Modulus of the first prismatic bar is E1 = 200000 MPa.
The Young's Modulus of the second prismatic bar is E2 = 110000 MPa.
The cross-sectional area of the first prismatic bar is A1 = 150 mm^2.
The cross-sectional area of the second prismatic bar is A2 = 200 mm^2.
The length of both prismatic bars is L = 800 mm.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Assume the rigid plates ensure uniform displacement across the top and bottom of the bars.
Determine the axial force in each bar.
Determine the axial stress in each bar.
Determine the axial strain in each bar.
Determine the total elongation of the system.
Present the governing relations for force equilibrium and compatibility of deformation.
