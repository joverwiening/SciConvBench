The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The analysis focuses solely on the torsional loading of the shaft.
The hollow circular shaft has an outer diameter of 50 mm.
The hollow circular shaft has an inner diameter of 42 mm.
The shaft material has a shear modulus of 77519.4 MPa.
The shaft is subjected to an applied torque of 450000 N·mm.
The relevant response quantities to be determined are the polar moment of inertia, the maximum shear stress, the shear stress distribution, and the angle of twist per unit length.
The governing relations for these quantities will be presented.
