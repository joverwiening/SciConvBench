Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Assume the rod is a straight prismatic circular rod.
Assume the axial load is applied uniformly and along the centroidal axis.
Assume the material is homogeneous and isotropic.
The Young's Modulus of the rod material is 69000 MPa.
The diameter of the circular rod is 30 mm.
The magnitude of the applied axial load is 15000 N.
The length of the rod is 800 mm.
The yield strength of the rod material is 275 MPa.
Calculate the cross-sectional area of the rod using the given diameter.
Determine the average normal stress in the rod by dividing the axial load by the cross-sectional area.
Calculate the axial strain in the rod using Hooke's Law, dividing the average normal stress by the Young's Modulus.
Evaluate the total elongation of the rod by multiplying the axial strain by the rod's length.
Determine the factor of safety with respect to yielding by dividing the yield strength by the average normal stress.
