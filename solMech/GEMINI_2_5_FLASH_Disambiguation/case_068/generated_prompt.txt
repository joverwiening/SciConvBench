The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The shaft is a solid circular shaft of uniform cross-section.
The shaft material is homogeneous and isotropic.
The shaft is straight.
The torque is applied uniformly at the ends of the shaft.
No stress concentrations are considered.
The only applied loading causing shear stress and angle of twist is the specified torque.
The diameter of the shaft is d = 30 mm.
The applied torque is T = 500000 N·mm.
The length of the shaft is L = 800 mm.
The shear modulus of the shaft material is G = 25939.8 MPa.
The polar moment of inertia for a solid circular shaft is J = (pi/32)*d^4.
The radius of the shaft is r = d/2.
The maximum shear stress in the shaft is calculated as τ_max = T*r/J.
The angle of twist of the shaft is calculated as φ = T*L/(G*J).
