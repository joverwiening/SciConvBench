The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam has a rectangular cross-section with width b = 50 mm and height h = 80 mm.
The internal transverse shear force V at the section of interest is 20000 N.
The material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The shear stress distribution across the width of the beam is assumed to be uniform.
The transverse shear stress (τ) in the rectangular beam will be determined using the formula τ = VQ/(Ib), where I is the moment of inertia about the neutral axis and Q is the first moment of area of the cross-section above the point where τ is being calculated.
The moment of inertia I for the rectangular cross-section is I = (b * h^3) / 12.
The first moment of area Q for a rectangular section at a distance y from the neutral axis is Q = (b/2) * (h^2/4 - y^2).
The maximum transverse shear stress will occur at the neutral axis (y=0).
All calculations will be performed using MPa units.
