The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The bar is prismatic with a rectangular cross-section.
The internal resultant normal force P = 30000 N (tension) and the internal resultant transverse shear force V = 12000 N are constant over the section of interest.
No torsional loading is present.
The cross-sectional width b = 40 mm and height h = 120 mm.
The cross-sectional area A is calculated as b multiplied by h.
The average normal stress (sigma_avg) at the section is determined by dividing the normal force P by the cross-sectional area A.
The average shear stress (tau_avg) at the section is determined by dividing the transverse shear force V by the cross-sectional area A.
The governing relation for average normal stress is sigma_avg = P / A.
The governing relation for average shear stress is tau_avg = V / A.
