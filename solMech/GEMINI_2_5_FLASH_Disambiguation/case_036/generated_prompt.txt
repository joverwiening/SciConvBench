The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The column is a solid circular cross-section.
Euler buckling theory is applicable, assuming the column is slender and buckling occurs elastically.
The governing relation for the critical buckling load is P_cr = (pi^2 * E * I) / (K*L)^2.
The moment of inertia for a solid circular cross-section is I = (pi * D^4) / 64.
The required critical buckling load for design is P_cr_design = n * P.
The minimum required diameter D is calculated using D = ((64 * n * P * (K*L)^2) / (pi^3 * E))^(1/4).
The actual factor of safety for the designed column is n_actual = P_cr_actual / P.
The minimum required diameter for the column is 30.0 mm.
The factor of safety with respect to buckling for the designed column is 2.0.
