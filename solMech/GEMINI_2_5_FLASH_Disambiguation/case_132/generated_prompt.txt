The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam is a simply supported rectangular beam.
The maximum bending moment applied to the beam is M_max = 3000000 N·mm.
The allowable bending stress for the beam material is σ_allow = 90 MPa.
The width of the rectangular beam is b = 40 mm.
The objective is to determine the minimum beam height h such that the maximum bending stress does not exceed the allowable bending stress.
The maximum bending stress σ_max is calculated using the flexure formula σ_max = M_max * c / I, where c is the distance from the neutral axis to the extreme fiber and I is the moment of inertia.
For a rectangular beam, c = h/2 and I = b*h^3/12.
Therefore, σ_max = M_max * (h/2) / (b*h^3/12) = 6 * M_max / (b*h^2).
To satisfy the design requirement, σ_max <= σ_allow.
The minimum beam height h is calculated as h = sqrt(6 * M_max / (b * σ_allow)).
