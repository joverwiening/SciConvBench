The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The shaft is a solid circular cross-section with a diameter d = 35 mm and length L = 400 mm.
The material is homogeneous and isotropic with a shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.
The shaft is subjected solely to an applied torque T = 250000 N·mm.
No additional transverse shear forces or bending moments are considered.
Stress concentrations are not considered.
The polar moment of inertia (J) for the solid circular shaft is calculated as J = (π/32) * d^4.
The maximum shear stress (τ_max) due to the applied torque is calculated as τ_max = (T * (d/2)) / J.
The angle of twist (φ) over the length L is calculated as φ = (T * L) / (G * J).
