The problem involves small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The geometry is a stepped circular bar with two segments.
The first segment has a length L1 = 400 mm and a diameter d1 = 16 mm.
The second segment has a length L2 = 400 mm and a diameter d2 = 40 mm.
The material has a Young's Modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The bar is subjected to a tension loading P = 25000 N.
Assume the material is homogeneous and isotropic.
Assume the bar is perfectly straight and has a uniform cross-section within each segment.
Assume the load is applied axially and uniformly across the cross-section.
Assume stress concentrations at the step transition are neglected for the calculation of average stress in each segment.
Assume the self-weight of the bar is negligible.
The governing relation for the cross-sectional area of a circular segment is A = (pi * d^2) / 4.
The governing relation for the normal stress in a segment is sigma = P / A.
The governing relation for the axial deformation of a segment is delta_L = (P * L) / (A * E).
The governing relation for the total elongation is delta_total = sum(delta_L_i) for all segments.
Determine the normal stress in the first segment.
Determine the normal stress in the second segment.
Determine the total elongation of the stepped bar.
