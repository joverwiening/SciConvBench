The structural member is a rectangular prismatic member.
The member has a width b = 40 mm and a height h = 120 mm.
The member is subjected to an internal resultant normal force P = 40000 N (tension).
The member is subjected to an internal shear force V = 8000 N.
The material is assumed to be linear-elastic steel.
The Young's Modulus (E) is assumed to be 200 GPa.
The Poisson's ratio (ν) is assumed to be 0.3.
The analysis assumes small-deformation and plane stress conditions.
The average normal stress (sigma_x) due to the axial force P shall be calculated.
The average shear stress (tau_xy) due to the shear force V shall be calculated, assuming uniform distribution over the cross-section.
The principal stresses (sigma_1, sigma_2) shall be determined from the calculated average normal and shear stresses.
The maximum in-plane shear stress (tau_max) shall be determined.
The principal strains (epsilon_1, epsilon_2) shall be determined using the generalized Hooke's Law and the principal stresses.
All relevant governing relations used for these calculations shall be clearly presented.
