The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
A cantilever beam of length L = 2000 mm is considered.
The beam is subjected to a uniformly distributed transverse load of magnitude w = 1.0 N/mm.
The Young's Modulus of the beam material is E = 200000 MPa.
The moment of inertia of the beam's cross-section is I = 1500000 mm^4.
The relevant response quantity to be determined is the maximum deflection of the cantilever beam.
The governing relation for the maximum deflection (δ_max) at the free end of a cantilever beam under a uniformly distributed load is δ_max = (w * L^4) / (8 * E * I).
