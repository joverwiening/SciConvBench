The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The column has fixed-free end conditions.
The Young's Modulus (E) of the column material is 200000 MPa.
The length (L) of the column is 1000 mm.
The column is assumed to have a solid circular cross-section with a diameter of 50 mm.
The moment of inertia (I) for the assumed cross-section is calculated as (pi * D^4) / 64, resulting in I = 306796.15 mm^4.
For fixed-free end conditions, the effective length factor (K) is 2.
The governing relation for the Euler critical buckling load (P_cr) is P_cr = (pi^2 * E * I) / (K * L)^2.
The calculated Euler critical buckling load for the column is 151250 N.
A buckling instability check indicates that if the applied compressive load exceeds 151250 N, the column will buckle.
