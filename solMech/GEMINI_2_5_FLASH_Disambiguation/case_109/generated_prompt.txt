The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam is a simply supported beam with length L = 2500 mm.
A downward point load P = 1000 N is applied at the midspan of the beam.
The Young's Modulus of the beam material is E = 200000 MPa.
The moment of inertia of the beam's cross-section is I = 3000000 mm^4.
The midspan deflection of the beam will be determined using Castigliano's second theorem.
The governing relation for deflection using Castigliano's theorem is delta = integral from 0 to L of (M * dM/dP) / (E * I) dx, where M is the bending moment as a function of x and P, and dM/dP is the partial derivative of M with respect to the point load P.
