The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The material is isotropic and homogeneous.
The rod has a circular cross-section with an initial diameter d0 = 20 mm.
The rod is subjected to an axial tensile load P = 25000 N.
The Young's Modulus of the material is E = 105000 MPa.
The Poisson's ratio of the material is ν = 0.34.
The load is applied uniformly and axially, resulting in a uniaxial stress state.
End effects are considered negligible.
The cross-sectional area A is calculated as pi * (d0/2)^2.
The axial stress σ_x is calculated as P / A.
The axial strain ε_x is calculated as σ_x / E.
The lateral strain ε_y is calculated as -ν * ε_x.
The change in diameter Δd is calculated as ε_y * d0.
The final diameter d_f is calculated as d0 + Δd.
The relevant response quantities to be determined are the axial stress, axial strain, lateral strain, change in diameter, and final diameter.
