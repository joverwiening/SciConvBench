The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam is simply supported.
The beam has a rectangular cross-section with width b = 30 mm.
The maximum bending moment applied to the beam is M_max = 6000000 N·mm.
The allowable bending stress for the beam material is σ_allow = 90 MPa.
The beam height 'h' is to be designed such that the maximum bending stress does not exceed the allowable bending stress.
The moment of inertia for a rectangular cross-section is I = (b * h^3) / 12.
The maximum bending stress is calculated as σ_max = (M_max * (h/2)) / I.
The design criterion is σ_max <= σ_allow.
The required beam height 'h' is calculated using the formula h = sqrt((6 * M_max) / (b * σ_allow)).
