The analysis will assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The analysis will use N–mm–MPa units.
The shaft is a solid circular shaft with a diameter d = 18 mm and a length L = 400 mm.
The shaft material has a shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.
The shaft is subjected to a torque T = 500000 N·mm.
The analysis will determine the maximum shear stress and the angle of twist.
Additional assumptions include that the shaft is straight, has a uniform circular cross-section, and the material is homogeneous and isotropic.
The torque is applied about the longitudinal axis of the shaft.
The cross-sections remain plane and perpendicular to the axis after twisting.
The maximum shear stress (τ_max) is calculated using the formula τ_max = (T * r) / J, where r is the outer radius and J is the polar moment of inertia.
The polar moment of inertia (J) for a solid circular shaft is calculated as J = (π * d^4) / 32.
The angle of twist (φ) is calculated using the formula φ = (T * L) / (G * J).
