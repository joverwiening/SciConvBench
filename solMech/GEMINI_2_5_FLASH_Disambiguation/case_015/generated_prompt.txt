The beam is a cantilever Euler-Bernoulli beam.
The beam length is L = 1200 mm.
The Young's Modulus of the beam material is E = 200000 MPa.
The second moment of area of the beam's cross-section is I = 2000000 mm^4.
The beam is subjected to an end point load P = 2000 N.
Small-deformation and linear-elastic solid mechanics are assumed.
The governing differential equation for the beam's transverse deflection v(x) is EIv''''(x) = q(x), where q(x) is the distributed load.
For a point load P at the free end (x=L) and no distributed load, the equation simplifies.
The boundary conditions for a cantilever beam are v(0) = 0, v'(0) = 0, v''(L) = 0, and v'''(L) = -P/EI.
The maximum deflection at the free end (x=L) is given by v(L) = PL^3 / (3EI).
The maximum slope at the free end (x=L) is given by v'(L) = PL^2 / (2EI).
