The problem involves a solid circular shaft subjected to a torque.
The analysis assumes small-deformation and linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The "shear loading" refers to the shear stress induced by the torque.
The applied torque T is 1000000 N·mm.
The length of the solid circular shaft is 1500 mm.
The shear modulus G of the shaft material is 25939.8 MPa.
The maximum allowable angle of twist is 0.75 degrees.
The governing relation for the angle of twist (φ) is φ = (T * L) / (G * J), where T is the applied torque, L is the shaft length, G is the shear modulus, and J is the polar moment of inertia.
For a solid circular shaft, the polar moment of inertia J is (π * D^4) / 32, where D is the shaft diameter.
The angle of twist limit must be converted from degrees to radians for calculation.
The task is to determine the minimum solid shaft diameter D required to ensure the angle of twist does not exceed the specified limit.
