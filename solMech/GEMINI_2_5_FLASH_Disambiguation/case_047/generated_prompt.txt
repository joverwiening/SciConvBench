The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The rod is a straight prismatic circular rod.
The rod is subjected to an axial tension load.
The Young's Modulus of the rod material is 200000 MPa.
The magnitude of the tension loading is 15000 N.
The diameter of the rod is 12 mm.
The length of the rod is 800 mm.
The yield strength of the rod material is 350 MPa.
The analysis will determine the average normal stress in the rod.
The analysis will determine the axial strain in the rod.
The analysis will determine the total elongation of the rod.
The analysis will evaluate the factor of safety with respect to yielding.
The governing relations used are: Stress = Force / Area, Strain = Stress / Young's Modulus, Elongation = Strain * Length, and Factor of Safety = Yield Strength / Stress.
