The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The rod is prismatic and made of a material with a Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa and a coefficient of thermal expansion α = 1e-05 1/°C.
The rod has a circular cross-section with a diameter d = 20 mm.
The rod is subjected to a uniform temperature increase of ΔT = 35 °C.
The rod is fully axially restrained, meaning its total axial deformation is zero.
There is no externally applied axial load on the rod.
The analysis will determine the thermal strain and the internal axial force developed due to the temperature change and axial restraint.
The governing relations for thermal strain, mechanical strain, total strain, and internal axial force will be presented.
