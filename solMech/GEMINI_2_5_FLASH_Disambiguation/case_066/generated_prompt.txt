The analysis will determine the shear stress and angle of twist for a solid circular shaft under torsion.
The analysis assumes small-deformation and linear-elastic material behavior.
The shaft has a diameter of 45 mm and a length of 1000 mm.
The shaft material has a shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa.
The shaft is subjected to a torque T = 400000 N·mm.
The analysis will use the N–mm–MPa unit system.
The polar moment of inertia J for a solid circular shaft is calculated as J = (π * d^4) / 32.
The maximum shear stress (τ_max) is calculated as τ_max = (T * d) / (2 * J).
The angle of twist (φ) is calculated as φ = (T * L) / (G * J).
