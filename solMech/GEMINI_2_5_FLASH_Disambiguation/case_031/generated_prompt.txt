The task is to calculate the elongation of a vertical bar due to its self-weight.
The material is assumed to be linear-elastic and undergo small deformations.
The Young's Modulus of the bar material is E = 6.9 x 10^10 Pa.
The length of the bar is L = 3.5 m.
The cross-sectional area of the bar is A = 2.0 x 10^-4 m^2.
The density of the bar material is ρ = 8900 kg/m^3.
The gravitational acceleration is g = 9.81 m/s^2.
The governing relation for the elongation (δ) of a vertical bar due to self-weight is δ = (ρ * g * L^2) / (2 * E).
Using the given values, the elongation is calculated as δ = (8900 kg/m^3 * 9.81 m/s^2 * (3.5 m)^2) / (2 * 6.9 x 10^10 Pa).
The elongation of the bar is approximately 7.76 x 10^-6 meters.
