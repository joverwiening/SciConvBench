The problem involves small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The bar is vertical and subjected to an axial load due to its self-weight.
The bar has a Young's Modulus E = 69000 MPa.
The bar has a length L = 3500 mm.
The bar has a cross-sectional area A = 200 mm^2.
The bar material has a density ρ = 7850 kg/m^3.
Gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s^2.
Assume the bar has a uniform cross-section and is made of a homogeneous material.
The elongation (δ) of the bar due to its self-weight is given by the formula δ = (ρ * g * L^2) / (2 * E).
Calculate the elongation (δ) of the bar.
