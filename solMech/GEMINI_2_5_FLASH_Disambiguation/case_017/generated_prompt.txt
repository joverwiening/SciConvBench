The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The analysis assumes an Euler-Bernoulli beam model.
The beam is simply supported.
The beam has a length L = 3000 mm.
The beam is subjected to a uniformly distributed transverse load w = 0.3 N/mm along its entire length.
The beam is subjected to a point load P = 1200 N at its midspan (L/2).
The analysis will determine relevant response quantities, including deflection, using the principle of superposition.
The governing relations for Euler-Bernoulli beam theory will be presented.
