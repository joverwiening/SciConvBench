The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The material of the hollow circular shaft is homogeneous and isotropic.
Cross-sections of the shaft remain plane and perpendicular to the axis after twisting.
Radii of the shaft remain straight after twisting.
Stress is proportional to strain according to Hooke's Law.
The applied torque is uniform along the length of the shaft.
The outer diameter of the shaft is 60 mm.
The inner diameter of the shaft is 48 mm.
The applied torque is 450000 N·mm.
The length of the shaft is 800 mm.
The shear modulus of the shaft material is 76923.1 MPa.
The polar moment of inertia J is calculated as (π/32) * (D^4 - d^4).
The maximum shear stress τ_max is calculated as (T * (D/2)) / J.
The minimum shear stress τ_min is calculated as (T * (d/2)) / J.
The angle of twist φ is calculated as (T * L) / (G * J).
