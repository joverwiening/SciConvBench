The column has a square cross-section.
The side length of the square cross-section is 100 mm.
The effective length factor (K) for a pinned-pinned column is 1.
The area moment of inertia (I) for a square cross-section with side 'a' is I = a^4 / 12.
The Euler critical buckling load (P_cr) is given by P_cr = (pi^2 * E * I) / (K*L)^2.
Given E = 200000 MPa (N/mm^2) and L = 2500 mm.
Assuming a = 100 mm, the area moment of inertia (I) is (100 mm)^4 / 12 = 8,333,333.33 mm^4.
The Euler critical buckling load (P_cr) is (pi^2 * 200000 N/mm^2 * 8,333,333.33 mm^4) / (1 * 2500 mm)^2 = 2631895 N.
The Euler critical buckling load (P_cr) is 2631.895 kN.
The column will buckle if the applied compressive load exceeds 2631.895 kN.
