The analysis focuses on a ductile metal tension specimen subjected to tension loading.
The analysis for ductility measures (percent elongation and reduction of area) considers large deformation and plastic behavior.
The initial diameter of the tension specimen is 12 mm.
The initial gauge length of the tension specimen is 50 mm.
The final gauge length of the tension specimen after fracture is 66 mm.
The final diameter of the tension specimen after fracture is 9.6 mm.
The percent elongation is calculated as ((Final Gauge Length - Initial Gauge Length) / Initial Gauge Length) * 100.
The reduction of area is calculated as ((Initial Cross-sectional Area - Final Cross-sectional Area) / Initial Cross-sectional Area) * 100.
The initial cross-sectional area is derived from the initial diameter.
The final cross-sectional area is derived from the final diameter.
