Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Assume the built-up beam consists of a flange plate connected to a web.
Assume two rows of fasteners connect the flange plate to the web.
Assume the applied shear force V is constant along the length where these calculations are relevant.
Assume the fasteners are uniformly spaced.
The governing relation for shear flow (q) is q = (V * Q) / I.
The governing relation for fastener spacing (s) is s = (n * F_allow) / q, where n is the number of fastener rows and F_allow is the allowable shear force per fastener.
The shear flow (q) is calculated as (25000 N * 45000 mm^3) / 18000000 mm^4 = 62.5 N/mm.
The required fastener spacing (s) is calculated as (2 * 1800 N) / 62.5 N/mm = 57.6 mm.
