The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The shaft is a stepped solid circular shaft with two segments.
The first segment has a diameter of 25 mm and a length of 300 mm.
The second segment has a diameter of 30 mm and a length of 700 mm.
A torque of 450000 N·mm is applied to the shaft.
No shear force is applied to the shaft.
The shear modulus of the shaft material is 77519.4 MPa.
The analysis will determine the stress distribution and the total angle of twist for the stepped shaft.
The governing relations for torsional stress and angle of twist in circular shafts will be used.
The analysis assumes the shaft is homogeneous and isotropic.
The analysis assumes the torque is applied at one end and the other end is fixed, or it is a pure torsion problem.
