The problem involves a solid circular shaft subjected to an applied torque.
The shaft has a diameter d = 50 mm.
The shaft has a length L = 600 mm.
The shaft material has a shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.
The applied torque T = 500000 N·mm.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The analysis assumes the shaft is homogeneous and isotropic.
The analysis assumes the shaft has a uniform circular cross-section along its length.
The analysis assumes the applied torque is the only significant shear loading.
The polar moment of inertia J for a solid circular shaft is given by J = (π/32) * d^4.
The maximum shear stress τ_max in the shaft is given by τ_max = (T * (d/2)) / J.
The angle of twist φ in radians is given by φ = (T * L) / (G * J).
The calculated polar moment of inertia J = 613592.32 mm^4.
The calculated maximum shear stress τ_max = 20.37 MPa.
The calculated angle of twist φ = 0.0063 radians.
