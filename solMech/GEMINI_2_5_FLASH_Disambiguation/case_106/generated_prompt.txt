The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The analysis assumes an Euler-Bernoulli beam model.
The beam is simply supported with a span L = 2500 mm.
A downward point load P = 2500 N is applied at the midspan of the beam.
The Young's Modulus of the beam material is E = 200000 MPa.
The area moment of inertia of the beam's cross-section is I = 3500000 mm^4.
The relevant response quantities to be determined are the midspan deflection and the slope at the supports.
