The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The shaft material is homogeneous and isotropic.
The shaft is subjected to pure torsion.
The cross-sections remain plane and undistorted during twisting.
The outer diameter of the hollow circular shaft is 60 mm.
The inner diameter of the hollow circular shaft is 44 mm.
The applied torque is 600000 N·mm.
The shear modulus of the shaft material is 77519.4 MPa.
The length of the hollow circular shaft is 1200 mm.
The polar moment of inertia J is calculated as (π/32) * (D_o^4 - D_i^4).
The maximum shear stress τ_max is calculated as (T * (D_o/2)) / J.
The shear stress τ at any radius r is calculated as (T * r) / J.
The angle of twist φ is calculated as (T * L) / (G * J).
The relevant response quantities to be determined are the polar moment of inertia, the maximum shear stress, the shear stress distribution, and the total angle of twist.
