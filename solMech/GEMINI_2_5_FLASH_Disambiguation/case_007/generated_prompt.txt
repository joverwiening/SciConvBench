The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam is a simply supported beam with a total length L = 1800 mm.
A downward point load P = 2000 N is applied at x = 900 mm from the left support.
The flexural rigidity EI is calculated as the product of E = 200000 MPa and I = 3500000 mm^4.
The deflection at the point load location (x = 900 mm) is to be determined using the unit-load method.
The governing relations for beam deflection under these conditions will be presented.
