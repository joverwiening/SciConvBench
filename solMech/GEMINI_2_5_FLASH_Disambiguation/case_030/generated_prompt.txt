The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The material is isotropic.
The rod has a circular cross-section.
The rod is subjected to an axial tensile load P = 45000 N.
The Young's Modulus of the rod material E = 200000 MPa.
The Poisson's ratio of the rod material ν = 0.29.
The initial diameter of the rod d0 = 25 mm.
The cross-sectional area A is calculated as π * (d0/2)^2.
The axial stress σ_axial is calculated as P / A.
The axial strain ε_axial is calculated as σ_axial / E.
The lateral strain ε_lateral is calculated as -ν * ε_axial.
The change in diameter Δd is calculated as ε_lateral * d0.
The final diameter d_final is calculated as d0 + Δd.
The relevant response quantities to be determined are the axial stress, axial strain, lateral strain, and the change in diameter due to the Poisson effect.
