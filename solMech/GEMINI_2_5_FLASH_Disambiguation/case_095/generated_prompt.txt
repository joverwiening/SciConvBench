The analysis will assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam will be modeled as an Euler-Bernoulli cantilever beam.
The beam is subjected to a single point load at its free end.
The magnitude of the end point load is P = 2000 N.
The length of the cantilever beam is L = 1800 mm.
The Young's Modulus of the beam material is E = 200000 MPa.
The moment of inertia of the beam's cross-section is I = 1200000 mm^4.
The relevant response quantities to be determined are the deflection profile, maximum deflection, bending moment profile, maximum bending moment, and shear force profile.
The governing relations for these quantities will be presented.
