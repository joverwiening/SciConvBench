Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The solid circular shaft is subjected to a torque T = 600000 N·mm.
The shear loading refers to the shear stress induced by the torque.
The maximum allowable angle of twist is φ_allow = 1.5°.
The shear modulus of the shaft material is G = 76923.1 MPa.
The length of the shaft is L = 800 mm.
The governing relation for the angle of twist φ in a solid circular shaft is φ = (TL) / (GJ).
The polar moment of inertia for a solid circular shaft is J = (π/32) * d^4, where d is the shaft diameter.
The shaft diameter d shall be determined such that the calculated angle of twist φ does not exceed the maximum allowable angle of twist φ_allow.
