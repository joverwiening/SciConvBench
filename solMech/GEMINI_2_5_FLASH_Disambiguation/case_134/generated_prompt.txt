The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The rectangular adhesive layer has an area A = 3000 mm^2 and a thickness t = 8 mm.
A uniform in-plane shear force V = 12000 N is applied to the adhesive layer.
The shear modulus of the adhesive layer material is G = 39179.1 MPa.
The area A is assumed to be the surface area parallel to the applied shear force.
The thickness t is assumed to be the dimension perpendicular to the applied shear force, across which the shear deformation occurs.
The governing relation for shear stress is tau = V / A.
The governing relation for shear strain is gamma = tau / G.
The governing relation for shear deformation is delta_x = gamma * t.
The calculated shear stress is 4 MPa.
The calculated shear strain is 0.000102.
The calculated shear deformation is 0.000817 mm.
