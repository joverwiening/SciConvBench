The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The bar has a total length L = 800 mm.
The cross-sectional area of the bar varies linearly along its length according to A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L).
The initial cross-sectional area A0 = 250 mm^2.
The constant k = 0.8.
The Young's Modulus of the bar material E = 200000 MPa.
An axial load P = 30000 N is applied to the bar.
The relevant response quantity to be determined is the total axial deformation of the bar.
The governing relation for the differential axial deformation dδ over a differential length dx is dδ = (P / (A(x)·E)) dx.
The total axial deformation δ is obtained by integrating dδ over the length of the bar from x=0 to x=L.
