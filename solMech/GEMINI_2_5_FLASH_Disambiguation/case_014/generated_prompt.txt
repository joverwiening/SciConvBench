The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The analysis assumes Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.
The beam is a cantilever with length L = 1200 mm.
The beam is subjected to an end point load P = 800 N.
The Young's Modulus of the beam material is E = 200000 MPa.
The area moment of inertia of the beam's cross-section is I = 2500000 mm^4.
The relevant response quantity to be determined is the maximum deflection at the free end of the cantilever beam.
The governing relation for the maximum deflection (delta_max) of a cantilever beam under an end point load is delta_max = (P * L^3) / (3 * E * I).
