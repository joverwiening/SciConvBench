The system consists of two rigid plates connected by two parallel bars.
The analysis assumes small-deformation and linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Bar 1 is made of AISI 1020 steel with Young's Modulus E1 = 200000 MPa and cross-sectional area A1 = 300 mm^2.
Bar 2 is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with Young's Modulus E2 = 69000 MPa and cross-sectional area A2 = 200 mm^2.
Both bars have a length L = 500 mm.
A total tensile load P = 20000 N is applied to the top rigid plate.
The relevant response quantities to be determined are the force in each bar, the stress in each bar, the strain in each bar, and the total displacement of the rigid plate.
The governing relations are based on compatibility of displacement and equilibrium of forces.
Compatibility requires that the displacement of both bars is equal to the displacement of the rigid plate.
Equilibrium requires that the sum of the forces in the two bars equals the total applied load P.
The force in each bar is given by F = E * A * (delta L / L).
The stress in each bar is given by sigma = F / A.
The strain in each bar is given by epsilon = delta L / L.
