The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam has a rectangular cross-section with a width of 100 mm and a height of 200 mm.
The Young's Modulus (E) of the beam material is 200000 MPa.
The applied bending moment (M) is 3000000 N·mm.
The moment of inertia (I) for the rectangular cross-section is calculated as (width * height^3) / 12.
The curvature (κ) of the beam is determined by the relation κ = M / (E * I).
The radius of curvature (ρ) of the beam is determined by the relation ρ = 1 / κ.
All calculations will be performed using consistent units, converting N·mm to N·m or MPa to N/mm^2 as necessary.
