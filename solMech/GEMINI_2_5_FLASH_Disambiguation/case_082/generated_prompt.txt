Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The tension-test specimen has a circular cross-section with an initial diameter d0 = 12.5 mm.
The initial gauge length of the specimen is L0 = 50 mm.
An axial tension load P = 12000 N is applied to the specimen.
The change in length (elongation) of the specimen under the applied load is ΔL = 0.12 mm.
Calculate the initial cross-sectional area A0 using A0 = π * (d0/2)^2.
Calculate the engineering stress σ using σ = P / A0.
Calculate the engineering strain ε using ε = ΔL / L0.
Determine the Young's Modulus E using the relation E = σ / ε.
