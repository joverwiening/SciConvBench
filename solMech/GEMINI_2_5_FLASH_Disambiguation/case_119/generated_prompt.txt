The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The shaft is a solid circular shaft with a diameter d = 20 mm.
The shaft is made of AISI 1020 steel, with an assumed yield strength (tensile) of 205 MPa.
The shaft transmits power P = 5 kW while rotating at N = 1200 rpm.
No external shear loading is applied; the analysis focuses solely on torsion due to power transmission.
The angular velocity of the shaft is calculated as omega = 2 * pi * N / 60.
The transmitted torque T is calculated using the power-torque relation: T = P / omega.
The polar moment of inertia J for the solid circular shaft is calculated as J = pi * d^4 / 32.
The maximum shear stress tau_max due to torsion is calculated as tau_max = T * (d/2) / J.
The yield strength in shear (Sy_shear) is estimated using the distortion energy theory as Sy_shear = 0.577 * Sy, where Sy is the tensile yield strength.
A torsion check is performed by calculating the factor of safety against yielding in shear, defined as FS = Sy_shear / tau_max.
