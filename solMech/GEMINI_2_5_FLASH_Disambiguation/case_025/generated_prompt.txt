The beam is subjected to a downward point load P = 500 N at its free end.
The length of the beam is L = 1500 mm.
The Young's Modulus of the beam material is E = 69000 MPa.
The moment of inertia of the beam's cross-section is I = 2500000 mm^4.
The analysis assumes small-deformation and linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The relevant response quantity is the deflection at the free end of the cantilever beam.
Using Castigliano's theorem, the governing relation for the free end deflection (delta) of a cantilever beam under a point load at the free end is delta = (P * L^3) / (3 * E * I).
