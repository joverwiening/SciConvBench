The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The rod is a straight prismatic circular rod.
The material properties are homogeneous and isotropic.
The applied load is purely axial and tensile, uniformly distributed over the cross-section.
The Young's Modulus of the rod material is 200000 MPa.
The gage length of the rod is 800 mm.
The diameter of the rod is 12 mm.
The axial tensile load applied to the rod is 30000 N.
The yield strength of the rod material is 250 MPa.
The cross-sectional area of the rod will be calculated using the diameter.
The average normal stress will be determined by dividing the axial load by the cross-sectional area.
The axial strain will be determined by dividing the average normal stress by the Young's Modulus.
The total elongation will be determined by multiplying the axial strain by the gage length.
The factor of safety with respect to yielding will be determined by dividing the yield strength by the average normal stress.
The governing relations for stress, strain, elongation, and factor of safety will be presented.
