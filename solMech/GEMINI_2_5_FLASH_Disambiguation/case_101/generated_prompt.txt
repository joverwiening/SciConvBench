Perform a small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics analysis for a cantilever beam. The cantilever beam has a length L = 1000 mm. The beam is subjected to a uniformly distributed transverse load w = 1.5 N/mm downward over its entire span. The beam material has a Young's Modulus E = 200000 MPa. The beam's cross-section has a moment of inertia I = 2000000 mm^4. Determine the maximum deflection of the cantilever beam. The maximum deflection (delta_max) at the free end is given by the formula delta_max = (w * L^4) / (8 * E * I).
