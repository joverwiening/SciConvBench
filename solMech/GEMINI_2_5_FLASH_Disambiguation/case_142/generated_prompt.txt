Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The tension-test specimen has a circular cross-section with an initial diameter d0 = 12.5 mm.
The initial gauge length of the specimen is L0 = 50 mm.
An axial tensile load of P = 15000 N is applied to the specimen.
The measured elongation of the specimen under the applied load is ΔL = 0.15 mm.
Calculate the initial cross-sectional area A0 using the initial diameter d0.
Calculate the engineering stress σ as the applied load P divided by the initial cross-sectional area A0.
Calculate the engineering strain ε as the elongation ΔL divided by the initial gauge length L0.
Calculate the Young's modulus E as the engineering stress σ divided by the engineering strain ε.
Assume the material is isotropic and homogeneous.
Assume the load is applied uniformly across the cross-section.
Assume the deformation is uniform within the gauge length.
