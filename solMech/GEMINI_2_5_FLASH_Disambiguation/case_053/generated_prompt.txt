The analysis will be performed under the assumption of small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The geometry is a solid circular shaft with a diameter of 20 mm and a length of 1200 mm.
The shaft material has a shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa.
The shaft is subjected to a torque T, which is computed from a delivered power P = 5 kW and a rotational speed of 1200 rpm using the formula P = T·ω.
The analysis will determine the power-torque conversion and perform torsion checks.
Torsion checks include calculating the maximum shear stress and the total angle of twist.
The rotational speed ω will be converted from rpm to radians per second.
The polar moment of inertia J for the solid circular shaft will be calculated as (π/32) * d^4.
The maximum shear stress τ_max will be calculated using the formula (T * r) / J, where r is the shaft radius.
The total angle of twist φ will be calculated using the formula (T * L) / (G * J).
No additional external shear loading is considered beyond the shear stress resulting from torsion.
