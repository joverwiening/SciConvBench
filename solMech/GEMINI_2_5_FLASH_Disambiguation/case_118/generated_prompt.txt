The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The transverse shear stress distribution across the rectangular cross-section is assumed to follow a parabolic profile.
The beam has a width b = 40 mm.
The beam has a height h = 80 mm.
The internal transverse shear force acting on the beam is V = 25000 N.
The cross-sectional area A is calculated as b multiplied by h.
The moment of inertia I about the neutral axis is calculated as (b multiplied by h cubed) divided by 12.
The transverse shear stress τ_xy at a distance y from the neutral axis is governed by the relation τ_xy(y) = (V / (2 * I)) * ((h/2)^2 - y^2).
The maximum transverse shear stress τ_max occurs at the neutral axis (y=0) and is governed by the relation τ_max = (3/2) * (V / A).
The relevant response quantities to be determined are the moment of inertia I, the governing relation for transverse shear stress distribution τ_xy(y), and the maximum transverse shear stress τ_max.
