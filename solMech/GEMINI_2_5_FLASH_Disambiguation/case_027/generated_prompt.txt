The simulation will analyze a rod under small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The rod has a length L = 1500 mm.
The rod has a Young's Modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The rod is subjected to a tensile axial load W = 800 N.
A weight with a mass m = 1 kg is dropped onto the rod from a height h = 150 mm.
The analysis will determine the maximum axial force (P_max) in the rod.
The analysis will determine the maximum normal stress (σ_max) in the rod.
