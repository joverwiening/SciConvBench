The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The shaft is straight and has a uniform circular cross-section.
The material is homogeneous and isotropic.
Cross-sections remain plane and perpendicular to the axis after twisting.
Radii remain straight after twisting.
The torque is applied at the ends.
The outer diameter of the hollow circular shaft is 50 mm.
The inner diameter of the hollow circular shaft is 44 mm.
The applied torque is 600000 N·mm.
The shear modulus of the shaft material is 77519.4 MPa.
The length of the hollow circular shaft is 800 mm.
The polar moment of inertia (J) is calculated using J = (π/32) * (Do^4 - Di^4).
The maximum shear stress (τ_max) is calculated using τ_max = (T * Do/2) / J.
The shear stress at the inner radius (τ_min) is calculated using τ_min = (T * Di/2) / J.
The angle of twist (φ) is calculated using φ = (T * L) / (G * J).
The maximum shear strain (γ_max) is calculated using γ_max = τ_max / G.
