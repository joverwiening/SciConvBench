The problem involves a prismatic bar subjected to multiple axial point loads. The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics. The bar has a Young's Modulus E = 200000 MPa and a cross-sectional area A = 500 mm^2. The bar is composed of three segments: AB with length 300 mm, BC with length 600 mm, and CD with length 300 mm. The left end A of the bar is fixed, meaning the displacement at A is zero (u_A = 0). An axial point load of 18000 N is applied at B. An axial point load of -12000 N is applied at C. An axial point load of 10000 N is applied at D. The governing relations include the equilibrium equation, the constitutive relation (Hooke's Law), and the strain-displacement relation. The relevant response quantities to be determined are the axial force distribution, axial stress distribution, axial strain distribution, and axial displacement distribution along the bar.
