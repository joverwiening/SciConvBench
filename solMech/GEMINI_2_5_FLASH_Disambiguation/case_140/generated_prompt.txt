The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The material properties are assumed to be those of steel, with Young's Modulus E = 200 GPa (200,000 N/mm^2) and Poisson's Ratio nu = 0.3.
The shear modulus G will be calculated as E / (2 * (1 + nu)).
The length of the shaft is not required for the requested stress and strain calculations at a critical section.
The solid circular shaft has a diameter d = 45 mm.
The shaft is subjected to a bending moment M = 2,000,000 N·mm.
The shaft is subjected to a torsion loading (torque) T = 500,000 N·mm.
The analysis will determine the maximum normal stress due to bending, the maximum shear stress due to torsion, and the combined stress state at the critical section.
The governing relation for the maximum bending stress is sigma_b = M * c / I, where c is the radius and I is the area moment of inertia for a circular section.
The governing relation for the maximum shear stress due to torsion is tau_t = T * c / J, where c is the radius and J is the polar moment of inertia for a circular section.
The principal stresses (sigma_1, sigma_2) will be calculated from the combined normal and shear stresses using the appropriate transformation equations or Mohr's circle.
The maximum in-plane shear stress (tau_max) will also be determined.
The principal strains (epsilon_1, epsilon_2) will be calculated from the principal stresses using Hooke's Law for plane stress, incorporating Young's Modulus and Poisson's Ratio.
