The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam is an Euler-Bernoulli cantilever beam.
The beam has a length L = 800 mm.
The beam material has a Young's Modulus E = 69000 MPa.
The beam's cross-section has a moment of inertia I = 800000 mm^4.
A point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end of the beam.
The governing differential equation for beam deflection is E * I * d^2v/dx^2 = M(x), where v is deflection, x is the distance from the fixed end, and M(x) is the bending moment.
The maximum deflection occurs at the free end (x=L).
The maximum deflection is calculated as v_max = (P * L^3) / (3 * E * I).
The maximum deflection is 1.546 mm.

The maximum slope is calculated as theta_max = (P * L^2) / (2 * E * I).
The maximum slope is 0.00290 radians.

The maximum bending moment is calculated as M_max = P * L.

The shear force is constant along the beam.
