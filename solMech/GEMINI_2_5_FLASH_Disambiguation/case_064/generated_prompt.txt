Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam has a rectangular cross-section with width b = 50 mm and height h = 100 mm.
The internal bending moment at the section of interest is M = 3,000,000 N·mm.
The moment of inertia I for the rectangular cross-section is calculated as (b*h^3)/12.
The bending stress sigma_x at an arbitrary fiber location y from the neutral axis is determined by the formula sigma_x = -M*y/I.
The neutral axis is located at the centroid of the rectangular cross-section.
The maximum tensile and compressive bending stresses occur at the top and bottom fibers, respectively, where y = +/- h/2.
