The problem involves a short prismatic bar under small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The cross-section is rectangular with a width b = 50 mm and a height h = 120 mm.
The internal resultant normal force is P = 40000 N (tension).
The internal resultant transverse shear force is V = 15000 N.
The average normal stress at the section is to be determined.
The average shear stress at the section is to be determined.
It is assumed that the normal stress due to the axial force P is uniformly distributed over the cross-section.
It is assumed that the shear stress due to the transverse shear force V is uniformly distributed over the cross-section for the purpose of calculating an average value.
It is assumed that the "shear loading" mentioned in the problem statement refers to the transverse shear force V, and no torsional moment is present or considered for average shear stress.
The cross-sectional area A is calculated as b * h.
The average normal stress (σ_avg) is calculated using the relation σ_avg = P / A.
The average shear stress (τ_avg) is calculated using the relation τ_avg = V / A.
