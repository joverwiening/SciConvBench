Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The structural member is a rectangular prismatic member with a width b = 50 mm and a height h = 80 mm.
The member is subjected to an internal resultant normal force P = 40000 N (tension).
The member is subjected to an internal shear force V = 12000 N, acting parallel to the width (b = 50 mm).
Determine the average normal stress and average shear stress on the cross-section.
Determine the principal stresses and the maximum in-plane shear stress based on the calculated average normal and shear stresses.
Omit the evaluation of principal strain measures.
Material properties Young's Modulus (E) and Poisson's Ratio (nu) are not required for this analysis.
