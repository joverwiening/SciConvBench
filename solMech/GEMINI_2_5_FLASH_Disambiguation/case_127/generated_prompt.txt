The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The solid circular shaft has a diameter of 30 mm and a length of 1000 mm.
The shaft is subjected to an applied torque of 250000 N·mm.
The shear modulus of elasticity for the shaft material is 76923.1 MPa.
The relevant response quantities to determine are the maximum shear stress and the total angle of twist.
The maximum shear stress (τ_max) is calculated using the formula τ_max = (T * r) / J, where T is the applied torque, r is the shaft radius, and J is the polar moment of inertia.
The total angle of twist (φ) is calculated using the formula φ = (T * L) / (G * J), where T is the applied torque, L is the shaft length, G is the shear modulus of elasticity, and J is the polar moment of inertia.
The polar moment of inertia (J) for a solid circular shaft is calculated as J = (π * d^4) / 32, where d is the shaft diameter.
