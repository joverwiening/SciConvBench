The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam is simply supported.
The beam has a length L = 3500 mm.
The beam is subjected to a uniformly distributed transverse load w = 0.5 N/mm.
The Young's Modulus of the beam material is E = 200000 MPa.
The moment of inertia of the beam's cross-section is I = 1500000 mm^4.
The governing relations for deflection and slope of a simply supported beam under a uniformly distributed load will be determined.
The maximum deflection occurs at the mid-span (x = L/2) and is given by (5 * w * L^4) / (384 * E * I).
The slope at the supports (x = 0 and x = L) is given by (w * L^3) / (24 * E * I).
The deflection at any point x along the beam is given by (w * x * (L^3 - 2 * L * x^2 + x^3)) / (24 * E * I).
The slope at any point x along the beam is given by (w * (L^3 - 6 * L * x^2 + 4 * x^3)) / (24 * E * I).
