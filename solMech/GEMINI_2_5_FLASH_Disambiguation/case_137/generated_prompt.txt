A simply supported beam has a total length of 3000 mm.
The beam is supported at its ends.
A concentrated vertical load of 12000 N acts downward at a point 1500 mm from one support.
There is no tension loading applied to the beam.
There is no applied moment on the beam.
The material behavior is assumed to be small-deformation, linear-elastic.
The analysis is two-dimensional.
The beam's self-weight is neglected.
The supports are ideal, providing only vertical reaction forces and no moment resistance.
The objective is to determine the internal shear force and bending moment distributions along the beam's length.
Governing relations include static equilibrium equations for the entire beam to find support reactions.
Governing relations include static equilibrium equations for free-body diagrams of beam segments to determine internal shear force V(x) and bending moment M(x) as functions of position x.
Standard sign conventions for shear and bending moment will be applied.
