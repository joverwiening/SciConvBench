The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The stepped circular bar has two segments.
The first segment has a length of 400 mm and a diameter of 16 mm.
The second segment has a length of 800 mm and a diameter of 40 mm.
The bar is subjected to a tension loading of 35000 N.
The Young's Modulus of the material is 200000 MPa.
The cross-sectional area for each segment is calculated as A = (pi * D^2) / 4.
The stress in each segment is calculated as sigma = P / A.
The elongation of each segment is calculated as delta_L = (P * L) / (A * E).
The total elongation is the sum of the elongations of individual segments.
The analysis will determine the stress in each segment and the total elongation of the bar.
