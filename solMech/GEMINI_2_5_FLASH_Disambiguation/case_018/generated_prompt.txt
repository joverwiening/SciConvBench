The problem involves the torsional analysis of a solid circular shaft.
The material is assumed to be linear-elastic and undergoes small deformations.
The shaft has a total length L = 1500 mm.
The shaft has a diameter d = 40 mm.
The shear modulus of the shaft material is G = 25939.8 MPa.
The shaft is fixed at x = 0, preventing rotation.
A torque T1 = 300000 N·mm is applied at the free end x = L.
A second torque T2 = -150000 N·mm is applied at x = 750 mm along the shaft.
The analysis should determine the internal torque distribution along the shaft.
The analysis should determine the angle of twist distribution along the shaft.
The analysis should determine the shear stress distribution within the shaft.
The analysis should determine the maximum shear stress in the shaft.
The analysis should determine the reaction torque at the fixed end.
The governing relations for torsional deformation and stress should be presented.
Assumptions include that the shaft is straight, the cross-sections remain plane and undistorted, and stress concentration effects are neglected.
The polar moment of inertia J = (pi/32) * d^4 will be used.
The torque-twist relation dφ/dx = T(x) / (G * J) will be used.
The shear stress relation τ = T * r / J will be used.
