The cross-section of the prismatic bar is rectangular with a width b = 30 mm and a height h = 100 mm.
The internal resultant axial force is P = 50000 N (tension).
The internal resultant transverse shear force is V = 5000 N, acting parallel to the width b.
The internal resultant torsional moment is assumed to be zero.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The cross-sectional area A is calculated as the product of width b and height h.
The average normal stress (sigma_avg) at the section is determined by dividing the axial force P by the cross-sectional area A.
The average shear stress (tau_avg) at the section is determined by dividing the transverse shear force V by the cross-sectional area A.
The cross-sectional area A is 30 mm * 100 mm = 3000 mm^2.
The average normal stress sigma_avg is 50000 N / 3000 mm^2 = 16.67 MPa.
The average shear stress tau_avg is 5000 N / 3000 mm^2 = 1.67 MPa.
