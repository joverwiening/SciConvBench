The problem assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The bar has a length L = 1200 mm.
The cross-sectional area of the bar varies linearly along its length.
At the left end (x=0), the area is A0 = 200 mm^2.
At the right end (x=L), the area is AL = 320 mm^2.
The bar is subjected to an axial load P = 20000 N.
The Young's Modulus of the bar material is E = 69000 MPa.
The objective is to determine the axial deformation of the bar.
Additional assumptions include uniform material properties throughout the bar and the load being applied axially through the centroid of the cross-section.
The governing relation for axial deformation (delta) of a bar with varying cross-sectional area A(x) under an axial load P is given by the integral of (P / (A(x) * E)) with respect to x, from 0 to L.
The cross-sectional area A(x) can be expressed as A(x) = A0 + (AL - A0) * (x / L).
