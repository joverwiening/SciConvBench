Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The structural member is a rectangular adhesive layer.
The adhesive layer is subjected to a uniform in-plane shear force V = 12000 N.
The thickness of the rectangular adhesive layer is t = 20 mm.
The area of the rectangular adhesive layer is A = 4000 mm^2.
The shear modulus of the adhesive layer is G = 39179.1 MPa.
The shear force V is assumed to be uniformly distributed over the area A.
The adhesive layer is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The deformation is assumed to be purely shear deformation.
The governing relation for shear stress is tau = V / A.
The governing relation for shear strain is gamma = tau / G.
The governing relation for shear deformation is delta_x = gamma * t.
Determine the shear stress (tau).
Determine the shear strain (gamma).
Determine the shear deformation (delta_x).
