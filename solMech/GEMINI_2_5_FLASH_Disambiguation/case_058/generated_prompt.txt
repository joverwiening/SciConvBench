The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The column has a Modulus of Elasticity (E) of 200000 MPa.
The column has a length (L) of 2000 mm.
The column has a minimum moment of inertia (I) of 1200000 mm^4.
The column's end conditions are fixed-pinned.
The effective length factor (K) for fixed-pinned end conditions is 0.7.
The Euler critical buckling load (P_cr) is calculated using the formula P_cr = (pi^2 * E * I) / (K*L)^2.
The calculated Euler critical buckling load is approximately 1208.5 kN.
For the buckling/instability check, an applied compressive load (P) of 604.25 kN is assumed, which corresponds to a factor of safety of 2 against the critical buckling load.
Since the assumed applied load (P = 604.25 kN) is less than the critical buckling load (P_cr = 1208.5 kN), the column is stable against Euler buckling under the assumed load.
The governing relation for Euler buckling is P_cr = (pi^2 * E * I) / (K*L)^2.
The buckling/instability check is performed by comparing the applied load P to the critical buckling load P_cr; if P >= P_cr, buckling occurs.
