The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The material is 6061-T6 aluminum.
The Young's Modulus E = 69000 MPa.
The Poisson's ratio ν = 0.33.
The initial diameter of the rod d0 = 40 mm.
The cross-sectional area is calculated from the initial diameter.
The rod is subjected to an axial tensile load P = 35000 N.
The analysis focuses on the Poisson effect under uniaxial tension.
The relevant response quantities to be determined are axial stress, axial strain, lateral strain, and the change in diameter.
The governing relations are Hooke's Law for axial stress and strain, and the definition of Poisson's ratio for lateral strain.
