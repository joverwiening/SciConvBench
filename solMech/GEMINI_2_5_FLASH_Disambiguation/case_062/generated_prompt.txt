The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The rod is made of AISI 1020 steel with a Young's Modulus E = 200000 MPa and a coefficient of thermal expansion α = 1e-05 1/°C.
The rod is subjected to a uniform temperature increase ΔT = 65 °C.
The rod is fully axially restrained, meaning its total axial strain is zero.
The free thermal strain is calculated as ε_thermal_free = α * ΔT.
The mechanical strain induced by the restraint is ε_mechanical = -ε_thermal_free.
The total axial strain in the rod is ε_total = 0.
The axial stress developed in the rod due to the restraint is σ = E * ε_mechanical.
The governing relation for the free thermal strain is ε_thermal_free = α * ΔT.
The governing relation for the stress due to full restraint is σ = -E * α * ΔT.
