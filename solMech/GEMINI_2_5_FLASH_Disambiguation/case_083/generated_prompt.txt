The analysis will assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The Young's Modulus of the shaft material is assumed to be 200 GPa.
The Poisson's Ratio of the shaft material is assumed to be 0.3.
The diameter of the solid circular shaft is 25 mm.
The applied torque is 300000 N·mm.
The applied axial load is 25000 N.
The analysis will determine the stress and strain response quantities on the outer surface of the shaft.
The axial normal stress due to the axial load will be calculated.
The torsional shear stress due to the torque will be calculated.
The combined normal and shear stresses on an element on the outer surface will be determined.
The principal normal stresses (sigma1, sigma2, sigma3) will be evaluated.
The maximum shear stress (tau_max) will be evaluated.
The principal normal strains (epsilon1, epsilon2, epsilon3) will be evaluated.
The maximum shear strain (gamma_max) will be evaluated.
The governing relations for axial stress, torsional shear stress, and stress/strain transformation will be presented.
