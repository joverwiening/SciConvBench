The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The rod is a straight prismatic circular rod.
The rod is subjected to an axial tensile load.
The Young's Modulus of the rod material is 69000 MPa.
The yield strength of the rod material is 275 MPa.
The initial length of the rod is 800 mm.
The axial tensile load applied to the rod is 15000 N.
The diameter of the rod is 20 mm.
The cross-sectional area of the rod is calculated as A = (pi/4) * D^2.
The average normal stress is calculated as sigma = P / A.
The axial strain is calculated as epsilon = sigma / E.
The elongation is calculated as delta = epsilon * L0.
The factor of safety with respect to yielding is calculated as FS = Sy / sigma.
All calculations assume uniform stress distribution across the cross-section.
