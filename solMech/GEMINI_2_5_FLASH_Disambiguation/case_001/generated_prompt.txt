The stepped circular bar has two segments.
The first segment has a length of 300 mm.
The first segment has a diameter of 25 mm.
The second segment has a length of 1000 mm.
The second segment has a diameter of 35 mm.
The bar is subjected to a tension loading of 30000 N.
The material has a Young's Modulus of 200000 MPa.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The stress in each segment will be determined.
The total elongation of the bar will be determined.
The governing relations for axial stress and elongation in a bar under tension will be presented.
