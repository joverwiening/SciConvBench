The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The material exhibits isotropic behavior.
The rod has a circular cross-section with an initial diameter d0 = 30 mm.
The rod is subjected to a uniaxial tensile axial load P = 25000 N.
Lateral loads are considered zero for this specific case.
The Young's Modulus of the material is E = 105000 MPa.
The Poisson's ratio of the material is ν = 0.34.
The primary focus is to determine the Poisson effect under uniaxial tension.
The initial cross-sectional area A0 is calculated as π * (d0/2)^2.
The axial stress σ_axial is calculated as P / A0.
The axial strain ε_axial is calculated as σ_axial / E.
The lateral strain ε_lateral is calculated as -ν * ε_axial.
The change in diameter Δd is calculated as ε_lateral * d0.
The final diameter df is calculated as d0 + Δd.
All calculations assume uniform stress distribution across the cross-section.
