The material is assumed to be homogeneous, isotropic, and linear-elastic.
Small deformations are assumed.
The stress state is given by σx = 120 MPa, σy = 120 MPa, σz = 0 MPa, τxy = 75 MPa, τxz = 0 MPa, and τyz = 0 MPa.
The material properties are Young's Modulus E = 69000 MPa and Poisson's Ratio ν = 0.33.
The governing relations for strain components (εx, εy, εz, γxy, γxz, γyz) are derived from the generalized Hooke's Law.
The normal strains are calculated as εx = (1/E) * (σx - ν*(σy + σz)), εy = (1/E) * (σy - ν*(σx + σz)), and εz = (1/E) * (σz - ν*(σx + σy)).
The shear strains are calculated as γxy = (1/G) * τxy, γxz = (1/G) * τxz, and γyz = (1/G) * τyz, where G is the shear modulus.
The shear modulus G is calculated as G = E / (2 * (1 + ν)).
The 3D strain state (εx, εy, εz, γxy, γxz, γyz) is determined using these relations and the given values.
