Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Assume an Euler-Bernoulli beam.
The beam is simply supported.
The total length of the beam is L = 3000 mm.
The Modulus of Elasticity (E) of the beam material is 200000 MPa.
The Area Moment of Inertia (I) of the beam's cross-section is 3500000 mm^4.
A point load P = 1500 N acts downward at midspan.
Determine the midspan deflection due to the point load.
Determine the support slope due to the point load.
The governing relations for a simply supported Euler-Bernoulli beam with a midspan point load P, length L, Modulus of Elasticity E, and Area Moment of Inertia I are:
Midspan deflection (delta_midspan) = (P * L^3) / (48 * E * I).
Support slope at either end (theta_support) = (P * L^2) / (16 * E * I).
