The stepped circular bar has two segments.
The first segment has a diameter of 12 mm and a length of 300 mm.
The second segment has a diameter of 30 mm and a length of 600 mm.
A tension loading of 25000 N is applied to the bar.
The material has a Young's Modulus of 200000 MPa.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Determine the stress in each segment.
Determine the total elongation of the bar.
The governing relations for stress and elongation in each segment will be derived from these parameters.
