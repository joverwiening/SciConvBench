Determine the polar moment of inertia for a hollow circular shaft: \(J = \frac{\pi}{2}\,(r_o^4 - r_i^4)\).  
Compute the torsional rigidity: \(GJ\).  
Calculate the maximum shear stress at the outer surface: \(\tau_{\max} = \frac{T\,r_o}{J}\).  
Calculate the shear stress distribution across the wall: \(\tau(r) = \frac{T\,r}{J}\) for \(r_i \le r \le r_o\).  
Determine the angle of twist over the shaft length: \(\theta = \frac{T\,L}{GJ}\).  
State the assumptions: small‑deformation, linear‑elastic, isotropic material with constant shear modulus \(G = 77\,519.4\ \text{MPa}\); pure torsion with no axial or bending loads; ends are subjected to the torque only; temperature effects are neglected.  
Present the governing relations listed above clearly, using the given numerical values for \(r_o = 25\ \text{mm}\), \(r_i = 22\ \text{mm}\), \(L = 800\ \text{mm}\), and \(T = 600{,}000\ \text{N·mm}\).
