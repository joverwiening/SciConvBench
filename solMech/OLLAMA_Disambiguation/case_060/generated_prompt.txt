Determine the Euler critical buckling load for a straight, prismatic column with fixed‑fixed end conditions, length L = 2000 mm, Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa, and flexural rigidity I = 800 000 mm⁴.  
Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior and that the column is slender so that Euler theory applies.  
Use the effective length factor K = 0.5 for fixed‑fixed ends, giving an effective length Leff = K·L = 1000 mm.  
Compute the critical load with the Euler formula:  
Pcr = π²·E·I / Leff².  
Substituting the values:  
Pcr = π² × 200 000 MPa × 800 000 mm⁴ / (1000 mm)² ≈ 1.58 × 10⁶ N ≈ 1 580 kN.  
State that buckling will occur if the applied compressive load exceeds this critical value.  
If an applied load is provided, perform a buckling/instability check by comparing it to Pcr; the column is stable when the applied load ≤ Pcr and unstable when it > Pcr.  
Document the governing relation, the effective length factor, and the assumption that no additional effects (material nonlinearity, large deformations, shear deformation, or axial load eccentricity) are considered.
