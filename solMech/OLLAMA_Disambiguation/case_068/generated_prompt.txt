The shaft is a solid, circular, homogeneous, isotropic 6061‑T6 aluminum bar with constant cross‑section, length L = 800 mm and radius r = 15 mm (diameter = 30 mm).  
Small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior is assumed; temperature effects, residual stresses, and axial loads are neglected.  
The shear modulus is G = 25 939.8 MPa.  

Governing relations  

1. The polar moment of inertia of a solid circular section is J = π r⁴ / 2.  

2. For a torque T applied uniformly along the shaft, the maximum shear stress is  
   τ_max = T r / J.  

3. The angle of twist at the free end of the shaft is  
   θ = T L / (J G).  

4. For a transverse shear force V applied to the shaft (shear loading), the average shear stress over the cross‑section is approximated by  
   τ_shear ≈ 4 V / (3 A), where A = π r² is the cross‑sectional area.  

5. Superposition of the torque‑induced shear stress and the transverse shear stress may be used if both loads act simultaneously.  

These equations provide the required response quantities (shear stress and angle of twist) for the given shaft under the specified loading conditions.
