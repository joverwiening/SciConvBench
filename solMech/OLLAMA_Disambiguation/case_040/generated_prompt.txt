Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior and apply Euler‑Bernoulli beam theory.  
The beam is prismatic, isotropic, simply supported at its ends, and carries a single vertical point load P applied at the mid‑span.  

Given data:  
P = 2000 N  
L = 3000 mm (span length)  
E = 200 000 MPa = 200 000 N/mm² (Young’s modulus)  
I = 1 500 000 mm⁴ (second moment of area about the neutral axis)  

Governing relations:  
1. Bending moment diagram for a central point load: M(x) = P x/2 for 0 ≤ x ≤ L/2 and M(x) = P (L − x)/2 for L/2 ≤ x ≤ L.  
2. Strain‑energy expression (Castigliano’s theorem):  
 U = ∫₀ᴸ M²(x) / (2 E I) dx = P² L³ / (96 E I).  
3. Mid‑span vertical deflection obtained from Castigliano’s theorem:  
 δ_mid = ∂U/∂P = P L³ / (48 E I).  

Numerical result:  
δ_mid = (2000 N)(3000 mm)³ / [48 (200 000 N/mm²)(1 500 000 mm⁴)] = 3.75 mm.  

Thus the mid‑span deflection of the simply supported beam under the given central point load is 3.75 mm, derived using the Castigliano method with the stated assumptions and governing equations.
