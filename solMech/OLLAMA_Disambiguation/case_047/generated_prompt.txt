Assume a homogeneous, isotropic, prismatic circular rod of constant diameter, subjected to a static axial tensile load applied uniformly at its ends. Small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior is assumed; temperature effects, residual stresses, and stress concentrations are neglected.

Given data  
- Diameter = 12 mm → cross‑sectional area A = π (6 mm)² ≈ 113.10 mm².  
- Gage length L = 800 mm.  
- Axial tensile load P = 15 000 N.  
- Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa.  
- Yield strength σ_y = 350 MPa.

Governing relations and calculations  
1. Average normal stress: σ = P / A → σ ≈ 15 000 N / 113.10 mm² ≈ 132.73 MPa.  
2. Axial strain: ε = σ / E → ε ≈ 132.73 MPa / 200 000 MPa ≈ 6.64 × 10⁻⁴ (dimensionless).  
3. Elongation of the gage length: ΔL = ε · L → ΔL ≈ 6.64 × 10⁻⁴ · 800 mm ≈ 0.531 mm.  
4. Factor of safety with respect to yielding: FS = σ_y / σ → FS ≈ 350 MPa / 132.73 MPa ≈ 2.64.

The specification provides all required response quantities and the safety assessment based on the supplied material and geometric data.
