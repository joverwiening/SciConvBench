Assume the vertical bar is prismatic, homogeneous, and isotropic with constant cross‑sectional area A, length L, Young’s modulus E, density ρ, and subjected to its own weight acting as a uniformly distributed axial load; the top end is fixed and the bottom end is free.  
Use small‑deformation, linear‑elastic theory.  
Compute the total self‑weight force: F = ρ · A · L · g.  
Determine the average axial stress: σ = F / A.  
Apply Hooke’s law for axial loading: ε = σ / E.  
Obtain the total elongation of the bar: δ = ε · L.  
Report the following response quantities:  
- Self‑weight force F (N).  
- Axial stress σ (Pa).  
- Axial strain ε (dimensionless).  
- Elongation δ (m or mm).  
State the numerical values using the provided data: L = 3500 mm, A = 200 mm², E = 69 000 MPa, ρ = 7850 kg/m³, g = 9.81 m/s².  
Neglect temperature effects, shear deformation, and any external loads other than self‑weight.
