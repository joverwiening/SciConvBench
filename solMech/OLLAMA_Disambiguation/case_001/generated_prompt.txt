Assume a small‑deformation, linear‑elastic, isotropic material behavior for the stepped circular bar.  
Neglect temperature effects, residual stresses, and any shear or bending loads; the bar is loaded only by an axial tensile force P applied uniformly at the ends.  

The axial stress in each segment i is given by σ_i = P / A_i, where A_i is the constant cross‑sectional area of segment i.  

The axial strain in each segment i follows Hooke’s law ε_i = σ_i / E, with E = 200 000 MPa.  

The axial elongation of each segment i is Δ_i = ε_i L_i = (P L_i) / (A_i E), where L_i is the length of segment i.  

The total elongation of the stepped bar is Δ_total = Δ_1 + Δ_2 = (P L_1) / (A_1 E) + (P L_2) / (A_2 E).  

Using the provided data:  
Segment 1: L_1 = 300 mm, A_1 ≈ 490.9 mm².  
Segment 2: L_2 = 1000 mm, A_2 ≈ 962.1 mm².  
Axial force: P = 30 000 N.  

Compute σ_1 = 30 000 N / 490.9 mm² ≈ 61.1 MPa.  
Compute σ_2 = 30 000 N / 962.1 mm² ≈ 31.2 MPa.  

Compute Δ_1 = (30 000 N × 300 mm) / (490.9 mm² × 200 000 MPa) ≈ 0.092 mm.  
Compute Δ_2 = (30 000 N × 1000 mm) / (962.1 mm² × 200 000 MPa) ≈ 0.156 mm.  

Thus the total elongation Δ_total ≈ 0.248 mm.  

Report the stress values σ_1 and σ_2 and the total elongation Δ_total as the relevant response quantities for the stepped circular bar under the given tensile loading.
