Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior and Euler‑Bernoulli beam theory (plane sections remain plane, shear deformation neglected).  
Consider a cantilever beam of length L = 800 mm, Young’s modulus E = 69 000 N/mm², second moment of area I = 800 000 mm⁴, subjected to a vertical point load P = 500 N applied at the free end. No end moment is present.  

Governing relations:  
- Shear force V(x) = ‑P (constant along the beam).  
- Bending moment M(x) = ‑P (L ‑ x), where x is measured from the fixed support (0 ≤ x ≤ L).  
- Bending stress σ(x,y) = M(x) · y / I, with y measured from the neutral axis.  
- Slope (rotation) θ(x) = (P · x²) / (2 E I) · (3L ‑ x) / L.  
- Deflection v(x) = (P · x²) / (6 E I) · (3L ‑ x).  

Response quantities at the free end (x = L):  
- Maximum tip deflection δ_max = v(L) = P L³ / (3 E I) ≈ 1.55 mm.  
- Tip rotation θ(L) = P L² / (2 E I) ≈ 2.9 × 10⁻³ rad (≈ 0.17°).  
- Fixed‑end bending moment M(0) = ‑P L = ‑400 000 N·mm.  
- Fixed‑end shear V(0) = ‑P = ‑500 N.  

These equations and computed values fully describe the cantilever beam response under the specified end point load.
