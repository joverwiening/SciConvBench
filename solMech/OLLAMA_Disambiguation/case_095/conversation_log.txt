Q: Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior and Euler‑Bernoulli beam theory (plane sections remain plane, shear deformation neglected).  
The beam has a constant rectangular cross‑section, is isotropic, and the load is applied vertically at the free end.  

Given data:  
Length L = 1800 mm.  
Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa (200 000 N/mm²).  
Second moment of area I = 1 200 000 mm⁴.  
Point load at the free end P = 2000 N.  

Governing relations for a cantilever with an end point load:  
Bending moment distribution: M(x) = –P (L – x).  
Shear force distribution: V(x) = –P (constant).  
Slope at a distance x from the fixed support: θ(x) = –P x² / (2 E I).  
Deflection at a distance x from the fixed support: δ(x) = –P x³ / (6 E I).  

Relevant response quantities at the free end (x = L):  
Maximum tip deflection δ_tip = P L³ / (3 E I) = 2000 N · (1800 mm)³ / [3 · 200 000 N/mm² · 1 200 000 mm⁴] ≈ 16.2 mm.  
Tip rotation θ_tip = P L² / (2 E I) = 2000 N · (1800 mm)² / [2 · 200 000 N/mm² · 1 200 000 mm⁴] ≈ 0.0135 rad (≈ 0.78°).  
Maximum bending moment at the fixed support M_max = P L = 2000 N · 1800 mm = 3.6 × 10⁶ N·mm (3.6 kN·m).

\[
\theta_{\text{tip}}=\frac{P\,L^{2}}{2EI}
