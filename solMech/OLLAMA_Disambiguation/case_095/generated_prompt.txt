Assume a prismatic cantilever beam of constant rectangular cross‑section, isotropic material, small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior, and Euler‑Bernoulli beam theory (plane sections remain plane, shear deformation neglected).  
The beam length is L = 1800 mm.  
The Young’s modulus is E = 200 000 N/mm² (200 GPa).  
The second moment of area is I = 1 200 000 mm⁴.  
A vertical point load P = 2000 N is applied at the free end.  

The bending moment distribution along the beam is M(x) = −P (L − x).  
The shear force distribution is V(x) = −P (constant).  
The slope at a distance x from the fixed support is θ(x) = −P x² / (2 E I).  
The vertical deflection at a distance x from the fixed support is δ(x) = −P x³ / (6 E I).  

At the free end (x = L) the tip rotation is θ_tip = P L² / (2 E I) ≈ 0.0135 rad (≈ 0.78°).  
The tip vertical deflection is δ_tip = P L³ / (3 E I) ≈ 16.2 mm.  
The maximum bending moment occurs at the fixed support and is M_max = P L = 3.6 × 10⁶ N·mm (3.6 kN·m).  
The shear force is constant along the beam and equals V = −P = −2000 N.
