Assume a homogeneous, isotropic, linear‑elastic solid with small deformations.  
The rod is circular with initial diameter d₀ = 25 mm and length L = 1200 mm.  
Material properties are Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa and Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.29.  
Only an axial tensile load P = 45 000 N is applied; the lateral load is zero.  

The cross‑sectional area is A = π d₀²/4 = 4.9087 × 10⁻⁴ m².  
The axial stress is σₐ = P/A = 91.7 MPa.  
The axial strain is εₐ = σₐ/E = 4.585 × 10⁻⁴ (dimensionless).  
The lateral (radial) strain due to Poisson’s effect is ε_r = –ν εₐ = –1.330 × 10⁻⁴.  

The change in length is ΔL = εₐ L = 0.550 mm, giving a final length L_f = 1200.550 mm.  
The change in diameter is Δd = ε_r d₀ = –0.00333 mm, giving a final diameter d_f = 24.9967 mm.  

All governing relations used are:  
σ = P/A, εₐ = σ/E, ε_r = –ν εₐ, ΔL = εₐ L, Δd = ε_r d₀.  
No additional effects (temperature, residual stresses, buckling) are considered.
