Q: Assume the shaft is a homogeneous, isotropic, linear‑elastic solid made of typical structural steel with shear modulus G = 80 GPa.  
Assume small deformations and neglect temperature effects.  
Assume a transverse shear force V = 10 kN acting uniformly on the shaft cross‑section (a common practice for design checks).  
The shaft length is L = 1000 mm (1.0 m).  
The shaft diameter is d = 30 mm (0.03 m).  
The applied torque is T = 250 000 N·mm (250 N·m).  

Compute the polar moment of inertia: J = π d⁴ / 32 = 7.95 × 10⁻⁸ m⁴.  
Compute the outer radius: c = d / 2 = 0.015 m.  

Shear stress due to torque (maximum): τ_torque = T c / J = 47.2 MPa.  
Average shear stress due to shear force: τ_avg = V / A, where A = π d² / 4 = 7.07 × 10⁻⁴ m², giving τ_avg = 14.1 MPa.  
Maximum shear stress due to shear force: τ_shear = 4V / (3A) = 18.8 MPa.  

Angle of twist over the shaft length: φ = T L / (J G) = 0.0393 rad ≈ 2.25°.  

Present the governing relations:  
τ = T c / J for torsional shear stress.  
τ_avg = V / A and τ_max = 4V / (3A) for shear due to transverse force on a circular section.  
φ = T L / (J G) for angle of twist.  

These equations and computed values provide the required response quantities for the solid circular shaft under the given torque and shear loading.
A: **Given data (N‑mm‑MPa units)**  

- Diameter \(d = 30\;{\rm mm}\) → \(c = d/2 = 15\;{\rm mm}\)  
- Length \(L = 1000\;{\rm mm}\)  
- Torque \(T = 250\,000\;{\rm N·mm}\)  
- Shear modulus \(G = 76\,923.1\;{\rm MPa}\)  

**Polar moment of inertia**

\[
J = \frac{\pi d^{4}}{32}
   = \frac{\pi (30)^4}{32}
   = 7.952\times10^{4}\;{\rm mm^{4}}
\]

**Maximum torsional shear stress**

\[
\tau_{\max} = \frac{T\,c}{J}
            = \frac{250\,000 \times 15}{7.952\times10^{4}}
            \approx 47.2\;{\rm MPa}
\]

**Angle of twist**

\[
\phi = \frac{T\,L}{J\,G}
      = \frac{250\,000 \times 1000}{7.952\times10^{4}\times 76\,923.1}
      \approx 0.0409\;{\rm rad}
\]

\[
\phi_{\text{deg}} = \phi \times \frac{180}{\pi}
                  \approx 2.34^{\circ}
\]

**Governing relations**

\[
\boxed{J = \frac{\pi d^{4}}{32}},\qquad
\boxed{\tau_{\max} = \frac{T\,c}{J}},\qquad
\boxed{\phi = \frac{T\,L}{J\,G}}
\]

**Results**

- Polar moment of inertia: \(J = 7.95\times10^{4}\;{\rm mm^{4}}\)  
- Maximum shear stress (torsion): \(\tau_{\max} \approx 47.2\;{\rm MPa}\)  
- Angle of twist over the shaft: \(\phi \approx 2.34^{\circ}\)
