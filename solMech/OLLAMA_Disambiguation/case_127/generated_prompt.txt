Assume the shaft is a homogeneous, isotropic, linear‑elastic solid made of typical structural steel with shear modulus G = 76 923.1 MPa.  
Use the N‑mm‑MPa unit system and neglect temperature effects and large deformations.  

Compute the polar moment of inertia: J = π d⁴ / 32.  

Compute the maximum torsional shear stress: τ_max = T c / J, where c = d/2.  

Compute the angle of twist over the shaft length: φ = T L / (J G).  

For the given data (d = 30 mm, L = 1000 mm, T = 250 000 N·mm, G = 76 923.1 MPa):  
J = 7.952 × 10⁴ mm⁴.  
τ_max ≈ 47.2 MPa.  
φ ≈ 0.0409 rad ≈ 2.34°.
