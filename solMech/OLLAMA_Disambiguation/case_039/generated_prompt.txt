Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic, isotropic behavior for AISI 1020 steel (E = 200 000 MPa, ν = 0.30).  
Assume the rod is prismatic, circular, and free of lateral constraints so that only axial tension produces Poisson contraction.  
Assume temperature effects are negligible and the load is applied quasi‑statically.  

Compute axial stress: σ = P / A, where P = 35 000 N and A = π d₀²/4 = 100 π mm² ≈ 3.14 × 10² mm².  

Compute axial strain: εₐ = σ / E.  

Compute lateral (radial) strain due to Poisson effect: ε_r = –ν · εₐ.  

Compute change in diameter: Δd = ε_r · d₀, where d₀ = 20 mm.  

Report the following response quantities:  
- Axial stress σ (MPa).  
- Axial strain εₐ (dimensionless).  
- Lateral strain ε_r (dimensionless, negative indicating contraction).  
- Change in diameter Δd (mm) and the final diameter d_f = d₀ + Δd.  

Present the governing relations in the order listed above, clearly indicating each substitution step.
