Determine the axial response of a linearly elastic, small‑deformation bar with a linearly varying cross‑sectional area under a tensile axial load.

Assumptions  
- The bar is straight, homogeneous, and isotropic with Young’s modulus \(E = 200{,}000\ \text{MPa}\).  
- Temperature effects, residual stresses, and body forces are neglected.  
- One end of the bar (at \(x=0\)) is fixed; the axial tensile load \(P = 12{,}000\ \text{N}\) is applied at the free end (\(x=L\)).  
- Plane sections remain plane; the axial force is constant along the length because no external distributed loads act.  

Given data  
- Length \(L = 800\ \text{mm}\).  
- Reference cross‑sectional area at \(x=0\): \(A_{0}=250\ \text{mm}^{2}\).  
- Linear area variation parameter \(k = 0.6\).  
- Area distribution: \(A(x)=A_{0}\bigl(1+\frac{k\,x}{L}\bigr)\).  

Governing relations  
1. Axial stress distribution: \(\displaystyle \sigma(x)=\frac{P}{A(x)}\).  
2. Axial strain distribution (Hooke’s law): \(\displaystyle \varepsilon(x)=\frac{\sigma(x)}{E}=\frac{P}{E\,A(x)}\).  
3. Axial displacement of the free end (total elongation):  
\[
\delta = \int_{0}^{L}\varepsilon(x)\,dx
      = \frac{P}{E}\int_{0}^{L}\frac{dx}{A_{0}\bigl(1+\frac{kx}{L}\bigr)}
      = \frac{P L}{E A_{0} k}\,\ln\!\bigl(1+k\bigr).
\]  

Computed response quantities  
- Stress at the fixed end (\(x=0\)): \(\displaystyle \sigma(0)=\frac{12{,}000}{250}=48\ \text{MPa}\).  
- Stress at the free end (\(x=L\)): \(\displaystyle \sigma(L)=\frac{12{,}000}{250(1+k)}=\frac{12{,}000}{250(1+0.6)}\approx 30.77\ \text{MPa}\).  
- Strain at the fixed end: \(\displaystyle \varepsilon(0)=\frac{48}{200{,}000}=2.40\times10^{-4}\).  
- Strain at the free end: \(\displaystyle \varepsilon(L)=\frac{30.77}{200{,}000}=1.54\times10^{-4}\).  
- Total axial deformation:  
\[
\delta = \frac{12{,}000 \times 800}{200{,}000 \times 250 \times 0.6}\,
        \ln(1+0.6)
      \approx 0.00158\ \text{m}=1.58\ \text{mm}.
\]

These relations and results fully describe the axial deformation, stress, and strain fields for the bar with linearly varying area under the specified load.
