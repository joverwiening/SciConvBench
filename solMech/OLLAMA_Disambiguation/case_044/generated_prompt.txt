Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior for a homogeneous, isotropic 6061‑T6 aluminum rod with Young’s modulus E = 69 000 MPa and Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.33.  
Assume the rod is prismatic, has an initial circular cross‑section of diameter d₀ = 40 mm and length L = 500 mm, and is subjected only to the given axial tensile load P = 35 000 N (any lateral load is neglected).  
Assume temperature is constant and there are no residual stresses.  

Governing relations:  
- Axial stress σₐ = P / A, where A = π d₀² / 4.  
- Axial strain εₐ = σₐ / E.  
- Lateral (radial) strain ε_r = – ν εₐ (Poisson effect).  
- Change in length ΔL = εₐ L.  
- Change in diameter Δd = ε_r d₀.  

Computed response quantities:  
- Cross‑sectional area A = 1 256.64 mm² = 1.2566 × 10⁻³ m².  
- Axial stress σₐ = 35 000 N / 1.2566 × 10⁻³ m² = 27.86 MPa.  
- Axial strain εₐ = 27.86 MPa / 69 000 MPa = 4.04 × 10⁻⁴.  
- Lateral strain ε_r = –0.33 × 4.04 × 10⁻⁴ = –1.33 × 10⁻⁴.  
- Change in length ΔL = 4.04 × 10⁻⁴ × 500 mm = 0.202 mm.  
- New length L_f = 500 mm + 0.202 mm = 500.202 mm.  
- Change in diameter Δd = –1.33 × 10⁻⁴ × 40 mm = –0.0053 mm.  
- New diameter d_f = 40 mm – 0.0053 mm = 39.9947 mm.  

Present these quantities and the governing equations as the final answer.
