Determine the required rectangular beam height for a simply supported 6061‑T6 aluminum beam (E = 69 000 N/mm²) of length L = 2000 mm, width b = 30 mm, carrying a single concentrated load P = 3000 N at mid‑span, such that the mid‑span deflection does not exceed δ_allow = 3 mm.

Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior; plane sections remain plane; shear deformation is neglected (Euler‑Bernoulli beam theory).

Use the governing relation for a simply supported beam with a central point load:
δ_max = (P L³) / (48 E I)

where the second moment of area for a rectangular section is:
I = (b h³) / 12

Solve for the required moment of inertia:
I_req = (P L³) / (48 E δ_allow)

Substitute the given values:
I_req = (3000 N · 2000³ mm³) / (48 · 69 000 N/mm² · 3 mm) ≈ 2.42 × 10⁶ mm⁴

Solve for the required height:
h = [ (12 I_req) / b ]^(1/3)
h = [ (12 · 2.42 × 10⁶ mm⁴) / 30 mm ]^(1/3) ≈ 9.66 × 10⁵ mm³)^(1/3) ≈ 99 mm

Round up to a practical dimension and provide a safety margin:
Specify a rectangular cross‑section with width b = 30 mm and height h ≥ 100 mm.

The final design satisfies the deflection limit of 3 mm under the specified loading and support conditions.
