Assume the adhesive layer is a homogeneous, isotropic, linear‑elastic solid undergoing small deformations.  
Assume the shear stress is uniformly distributed over the entire bonded area and edge effects are negligible.  
Assume temperature and moisture effects are absent, and the material behavior is governed solely by the given shear modulus G.

Governing relations:  
Shear stress τ = V / A.  
Shear strain γ = τ / G.  
Shear deformation (relative displacement) δ = γ · t.

Shear strain γ = 4 MPa / 39179.1 MPa ≈ 1.02 × 10⁻⁴ (dimensionless).
