Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic, isotropic behavior for a uniform solid circular shaft made of structural steel (G = 77 519.4 MPa).  
Neglect temperature effects, residual stresses, stress concentrations, and any axial loads; the shaft is subjected only to the given torque.  

Governing relations for torsion of a solid circular shaft:  
- Polar moment of inertia: J = π d⁴ / 32.  
- Angle of twist: φ = T L / (J G).  
- Maximum shear stress: τ_max = 16 T / (π d³).  

Design requirement (angle‑of‑twist limit): φ ≤ φ_allow = 1.0° = 0.01745 rad.  

Solve for the minimum shaft diameter d that satisfies the twist limit:  
d = [ (16 T L) / (π G φ_allow) ]¹⁄⁴.  

Substituting the given data (T = 800 000 N·mm, L = 1200 mm, G = 77 519.4 N/mm², φ_allow = 0.01745 rad):  
d = [ (16 × 800 000 × 1200) / (π × 77 519.4 × 0.01745) ]¹⁄⁴ ≈ 44 mm.  

Therefore, specify a solid circular shaft diameter of at least 44 mm (round up to the nearest standard size, e.g., 45 mm) to satisfy the allowable angle of twist.  

Report the governing equations, the derived design formula, and the computed minimum diameter as the final design result.
