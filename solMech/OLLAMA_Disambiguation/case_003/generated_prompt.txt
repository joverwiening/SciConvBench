Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior and that the axial tensile force is uniformly distributed over the cross‑section of each segment (ignore stress concentration at the step).  
Use the material Young’s modulus E = 69 000 MPa.  

For each segment i (i = 1,2):  
- Compute the cross‑sectional area A_i = π d_i² / 4.  
- Compute the axial stress σ_i = P / A_i, where P = 20 000 N.  

Compute the axial strain ε_i = σ_i / E.  

Compute the elongation of each segment ΔL_i = ε_i L_i = (P L_i) / (A_i E), using L₁ = 500 mm, d₁ = 30 mm and L₂ = 600 mm, d₂ = 25 mm.  

The total elongation of the stepped bar is ΔL_total = ΔL₁ + ΔL₂.  

Present the results as:  
- Stress in segment 1 (σ₁).  
- Stress in segment 2 (σ₂).  
- Total elongation ΔL_total.
