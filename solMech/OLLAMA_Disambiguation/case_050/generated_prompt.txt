Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic, isotropic material behavior with constant shear modulus G = 39179.1 N/mm².  
The torque T = 300 000 N·mm is applied uniformly along the shaft length L = 1000 mm, and the shaft ends are free to rotate.  

The outer radius is rₒ = 20 mm and the inner radius is rᵢ = 15 mm.  

The polar moment of inertia for the hollow circular section is J = (π/2)(rₒ⁴ − rᵢ⁴) ≈ 1.718 × 10⁵ mm⁴.  

The shear stress distribution is τ(r) = T·r / J.  

The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer surface: τ_max = T·rₒ / J ≈ 34.9 MPa.  

The shear strain distribution is γ(r) = τ(r) / G.  

The maximum shear strain occurs at the outer surface: γ_max = τ_max / G ≈ 8.9 × 10⁻⁴ (dimensionless).  

The torsional rigidity is K_t = J·G ≈ 6.73 × 10⁹ N·mm².  

The angle of twist over the length L is φ = T·L / (J·G) ≈ 0.0446 rad (≈ 2.56°).  

The twist per unit length (rate of twist) is θ' = φ / L ≈ 4.46 × 10⁻⁵ rad/mm.
