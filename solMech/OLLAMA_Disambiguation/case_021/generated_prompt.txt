Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior for a solid circular shaft made of 6061‑T6 aluminum with shear modulus G = 25 939.8 N/mm².  
The shaft consists of two solid steps:  

- Segment AB: length L₁ = 300 mm, radius r₁ = 10 mm.  
- Segment BC: length L₂ = 500 mm, radius r₂ = 15 mm.  

The shaft is fixed at end A, a torque T = 300 000 N·mm is applied at end C, and no transverse shear force is present.

**Governing relations**  
1. Polar moment of inertia for a solid circular section: J = π r⁴ / 2.  
2. Maximum shear stress in torsion: τ_max = T r / J.  
3. Angle of twist for a uniform segment: θ = T L / (J G).  
4. Total twist of the stepped shaft is the algebraic sum of the twists of the individual segments: θ_total = θ₁ + θ₂.

**Calculations**  

- J₁ = π (10 mm)⁴ / 2 = 1.5708 × 10⁴ mm⁴ = 15 707.96 mm⁴.  
- J₂ = π (15 mm)⁴ / 2 = 1.5708 × 5.0625 × 10⁴ mm⁴ = 79 577.5 mm⁴.  

- τ_max,₁ = (300 000 N·mm)(10 mm) / 15 707.96 mm⁴ ≈ 191 N/mm².  
- τ_max,₂ = (300 000 N·mm)(15 mm) / 79 577.5 mm⁴ ≈ 56.6 N/mm².  

- θ₁ = (300 000 N·mm)(300 mm) / (15 707.96 mm⁴ · 25 939.8 N/mm²) ≈ 0.221 rad.  
- θ₂ = (300 000 N·mm)(500 mm) / (79 577.5 mm⁴ · 25 939.8 N/mm²) ≈ 0.0727 rad.  

- θ_total = θ₁ + θ₂ ≈ 0.2937 rad ≈ 16.8°.  

**Resulting response quantities**  
- Maximum shear stress in segment AB: ≈ 191 N/mm².  
- Maximum shear stress in segment BC: ≈ 56.6 N/mm².  
- Total angle of twist of the stepped shaft under the given torque: ≈ 0.294 rad (≈ 16.8°).
