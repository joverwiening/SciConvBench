Assume a small‑deformation, linear‑elastic beam made of a material with Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa.  
Assume the beam is subjected to a constant bending moment M = 3 000 000 N·mm.  
Assume a rectangular cross‑section with width b = 100 mm and height h = 200 mm (a common proportion of h = 2b).  
Compute the second moment of area I for the rectangular section as I = b h³ / 12 = 100 mm × (200 mm)³ / 12 = 66 666 667 mm⁴.  
Determine the curvature κ using the linear‑elastic bending relation κ = M / (E I).  
Substitute the values: κ = 3 000 000 N·mm / (200 000 N/mm² × 66 666 667 mm⁴) ≈ 2.25 × 10⁻⁷ mm⁻¹.  
Obtain the radius of curvature R as the reciprocal of curvature: R = 1 / κ ≈ 4.44 × 10⁶ mm ≈ 4 440 m.  
State that the curvature and radius of curvature are the primary response quantities for the given bending moment under the stated assumptions.
