Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior and apply Euler‑Bernoulli beam theory.  
The beam is a cantilever fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L = 1200 mm, with a vertical point load P = 2000 N applied at the free end.  
Material properties: Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa.  
Cross‑section property: second moment of area I = 2 000 000 mm⁴.  

Governing relations:  
1. Shear force V(x) = P for 0 ≤ x ≤ L.  
2. Bending moment M(x) = –P x (negative sign denotes sagging).  
3. Curvature κ(x) = M(x) / (E I).  
4. Slope θ(x) = –P x² / (2 E I).  
5. Deflection w(x) = –P x³ / (6 E I).  

Relevant response quantities:  
- Tip deflection δ_tip = w(L) = –P L³ / (3 E I).  
- Tip slope θ_tip = θ(L) = –P L² / (2 E I).  
- Maximum bending moment magnitude M_max = P L occurring at the fixed support.  
- Maximum bending stress σ_max = M_max c / I, where c is the distance from the neutral axis to the outermost fiber (to be supplied if stress is required).  

All calculations use consistent units (N, mm, MPa).
