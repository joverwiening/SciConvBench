Assume the beam behaves as a small‑deformation, linear‑elastic Euler‑Bernoulli beam.  
The beam is simply supported at its ends, with a vertical point load P applied exactly at the midspan.  
Neglect the self‑weight of the beam and any axial or shear deformations; only bending deformation is considered.  
Material properties: Young’s modulus E = 200 000 N/mm²; second moment of area I = 3 000 000 mm⁴.  
Geometric property: beam length L = 3000 mm.  
The bending moment diagram for a central point load on a simply supported beam is triangular, with maximum moment Mmax = P L/4 at the midspan.  
The curvature‑deflection relation for an Euler‑Bernoulli beam is 1/ρ = M/(E I).  
Using Castigliano’s theorem, the midspan vertical deflection δ is obtained from the strain energy derivative: δ = P L³/(48 E I).  
Substituting the given values, the numerical midspan deflection is δ = (2000 N · (3000 mm)³)/(48 · 200 000 N/mm² · 3 000 000 mm⁴).  
Present the result in millimetres, rounded to an appropriate number of significant figures.
