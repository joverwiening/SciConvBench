Assume the built‑up beam is made of structural steel with Young’s modulus E = 200 GPa, Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.30 and shear modulus G = 77 GPa.  
Assume a standard I‑section built‑up from plates with the following dimensions: flange width b_f = 200 mm, flange thickness t_f = 12 mm, web height h_w = 300 mm, web thickness t_w = 8 mm.  
Assume the beam span L = 6.0 m and that the applied bending moment is the design moment for the span, M = 150 kN·m (typical for a simply supported beam with the given shear V = 25 kN).  
Assume the fasteners are high‑strength bolts with a single‑bolt shear capacity V_b = 30 kN, hole diameter d_h = 20 mm, edge distance e = 30 mm and a minimum spacing to avoid tear‑out of 3 d_h.  

Governing relations:  
1. Compute the second moment of area of the I‑section I = (b_f t_f³/12) + (b_f t_f h_w²/2).  
2. Compute the first moment of area Q for the flange (or web) at the fastener line: Q = A_f · ȳ, where A_f is the area of the portion of the section above the fastener line and ȳ is its centroid distance from the neutral axis.  
3. Shear flow in the web at the fastener line: q = V · Q / I.  
4. Required number of fasteners n = V / V_b (round up to the next integer).  
5. Fastener spacing s = L_f / n, where L_f is the length of the fastener line (taken as the web height h_w).  
6. Verify that s ≤ 3 d_h and that the edge distance e satisfies e ≥ 1.5 d_h.  

Response quantities to be reported:  
- Second moment of area I of the built‑up I‑section.  
- First moment of area Q at the fastener line.  
- Shear flow q (N/mm) in the web at the fastener line.  
- Minimum number of fasteners n required to resist the shear force.  
- Fastener spacing s (mm) along the web, checked against the maximum allowable spacing of 3 d_h.  
- Confirmation that edge distance and spacing satisfy the tear‑out criteria.  

All calculations should be performed using the assumed values above, and the final design should be documented with the stated assumptions and the governing equations.
