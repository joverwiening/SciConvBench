Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior, uniform temperature rise, prismatic geometry, constant material properties, and that the rod is fully restrained axially (total axial strain = 0).  
Thermal strain: ε_th = α ΔT = (1 × 10⁻⁵ °C⁻¹)(35 °C) = 3.5 × 10⁻⁴ (dimensionless).  
Mechanical strain required to enforce zero total strain: ε_mech = –ε_th = –3.5 × 10⁻⁴.  
Axial stress generated by the restraint: σ = E ε_mech = (200 000 MPa)(–3.5 × 10⁻⁴) = –70 MPa (compressive).  
Axial force developed by the restraint: P = σ A. With the given compressive load of 22 kN (22 000 N), the required cross‑sectional area is A = P / |σ| = 22 000 N / (70 N/mm²) ≈ 3.14 × 10² mm² (≈ 314 mm²).  
Resulting axial displacement of the rod is zero because of full restraint.
