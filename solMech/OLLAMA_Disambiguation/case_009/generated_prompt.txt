Assume the two bars are perfectly bonded between the rigid plates so that they experience the same axial deformation Δ; neglect shear deformation, temperature effects, and any initial stresses; small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior is used.

Governing relations for the parallel composite bar under the axial tensile force P = 20 000 N:

1. Compatibility: the axial deformation of each bar is the same, Δ = ε₁ L = ε₂ L.
2. Strain–stress relations: ε₁ = σ₁ / E₁ and ε₂ = σ₂ / E₂.
3. Equilibrium of forces: P = P₁ + P₂, where P₁ = σ₁ A₁ and P₂ = σ₂ A₂.
4. From compatibility and equilibrium, the axial force carried by each bar is  
   P₁ = (E₁ A₁) / (E₁ A₁ + E₂ A₂) · P,  
   P₂ = (E₂ A₂) / (E₁ A₁ + E₂ A₂) · P.
5. Axial stresses: σ₁ = P₁ / A₁, σ₂ = P₂ / A₂.
6. Axial strains: ε₁ = σ₁ / E₁, ε₂ = σ₂ / E₂ (identical by compatibility).
7. Overall axial stiffness of the parallel system:  
   K_total = (E₁ A₁ + E₂ A₂) / L.
8. Overall axial deformation of the assembly: Δ = P / K_total = P L / (E₁ A₁ + E₂ A₂).
9. Overall axial strain of the assembly: ε = Δ / L = P / (E₁ A₁ + E₂ A₂).
