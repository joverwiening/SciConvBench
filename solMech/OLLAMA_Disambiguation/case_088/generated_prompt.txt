Assume a small‑deformation, linear‑elastic, isotropic material with Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa and neglect temperature effects, shear, and transverse strains.  
The bar is subjected to a static axial tensile force P = 35 000 N.  
The bar consists of two cylindrical segments in series:  
Segment 1: length L₁ = 300 mm, diameter d₁ = 12 mm.  
Segment 2: length L₂ = 800 mm, diameter d₂ = 30 mm.  

Governing relations:  
- Cross‑sectional area of each segment: Aᵢ = π dᵢ² / 4.  
- Axial stress in each segment: σᵢ = P / Aᵢ.  
- Axial strain in each segment (Hooke’s law): εᵢ = σᵢ / E.  
- Axial elongation of each segment: ΔLᵢ = εᵢ Lᵢ = P Lᵢ / (Aᵢ E).  
- Total elongation of the stepped bar: ΔL_total = ΔL₁ + ΔL₂.  

Compute the required response quantities using the above formulas.
