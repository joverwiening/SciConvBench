Assume a small‑deformation, linear‑elastic solid.  
The bar has a rectangular cross‑section with width b = 40 mm and height h = 120 mm, giving a cross‑sectional area A = b · h = 4.8 × 10⁻³ m².  
The bar is subjected to an axial tension force P = 30 000 N and a transverse shear force V = 12 000 N. No transverse bending moment is present.  

Assumptions  
- Material behavior is linear elastic and isotropic.  
- Plane sections remain plane; shear deformation is uniform across the section for the purpose of average stress.  
- The bar is prismatic and the loading is static.  

Governing relations  
- Average normal stress at the section: σ̄ = P / A.  
- Average shear stress at the section: τ̄ = V / A.  

Compute the response quantities using the given values.
