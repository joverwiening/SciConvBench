Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic, isotropic behavior for a solid circular shaft of constant radius r = 15 mm and length L = 1200 mm, fixed at the left end (x = 0).  
Material shear modulus G = 76 923.1 MPa (E ≈ 210 000 MPa, ν ≈ 0.30 may be used if needed).  
Two torques are applied about the shaft axis:  

- T₁ = +200 000 N·mm at x = 1200 mm (right end).  
- T₂ = –200 000 N·mm at x = 400 mm (negative sense opposite to T₁).  

**Governing relations**  
1. Internal torque equilibrium: for any cross‑section at position x, the internal torque T_int(x) equals the algebraic sum of external torques acting on the portion of the shaft to the right of the section.  
2. Polar moment of inertia of a solid circular section: J = π r⁴ / 2.  
3. Maximum shear stress in a circular shaft: τ_max(x) = T_int(x) · r / J.  
4. Angle of twist: θ(x) = ∫₀ˣ [T_int(ξ) / (G J)] dξ.  

**Computed quantities**  

- Polar moment of inertia: J = π (15 mm)⁴ / 2 = 7.9578 × 10⁴ mm⁴.  

- Internal torque distribution:  
  • 0 ≤ x < 400 mm: T_int = 0 N·mm.  
  • 400 mm ≤ x ≤ 1200 mm: T_int = +200 000 N·mm.  

- Maximum shear‑stress distribution:  
  • 0 ≤ x < 400 mm: τ_max = 0 MPa.  
  • 400 mm ≤ x ≤ 1200 mm: τ_max = (200 000 N·mm)(15 mm) / 7.9578 × 10⁴ mm⁴ = 37.7 MPa.  

- Angle of twist:  
  • 0 ≤ x < 400 mm: θ(x) = 0 rad.  
  • 400 mm ≤ x ≤ 1200 mm: θ(x) = [T₁ / (G J)] · (x – 400 mm) = (200 000 / (76 923.1 · 7.9578 × 10⁴)) · (x – 400) = 3.267 × 10⁻⁵ · (x – 400) rad.  

- Total angle of twist at the free end (x = 1200 mm):  
  θ_total = T₁ · (L – 400) / (G J) = 200 000 · 800 / (76 923.1 · 7.9578 × 10⁴) = 0.0261 rad = 1.50°.  

**Additional assumptions**  
- No axial loads, bending moments, or transverse shear forces are present.  
- Temperature effects, residual stresses, and material anisotropy are neglected.  
- The shaft cross‑section remains plane and perpendicular to the axis during torsion (Saint‑Venant’s torsion theory).  

These relations and computed values fully define the internal torque, shear‑stress, and twist responses of the shaft under the specified torque loading.
