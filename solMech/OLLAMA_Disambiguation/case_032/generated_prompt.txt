Assume a prismatic, homogeneous, isotropic beam that follows Euler‑Bernoulli kinematics, small deformations, and linear‑elastic material behavior.  
The beam is simply supported at its ends, has length L = 3000 mm, Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa, second moment of area I = 3 500 000 mm⁴, giving flexural rigidity EI = 7.0 × 10¹¹ N·mm².  
A single vertical point load P = 1500 N is applied at the midspan (x = L/2). No other external shear load is present.  

Governing relation: the bending moment distribution satisfies EI w″(x) = M(x), where w(x) is the transverse deflection.  
For 0 ≤ x ≤ L/2, the internal bending moment is M(x) = (P/2) x; for L/2 ≤ x ≤ L, M(x) = (P/2)(L − x).  

Integrating the moment‑curvature equation and applying the simply‑supported boundary conditions w(0) = w(L) = 0 and slope continuity at the load point yields the closed‑form results:  

Midspan deflection: δmid = (P L³) / (48 EI) ≈ 1.21 mm.  

Support slope (rotation at each support): θsupport = (P L²) / (16 EI) ≈ 1.21 × 10⁻³ rad (≈0.069°).  

These expressions constitute the required response quantities for the specified beam configuration.
