Assume a small‑deformation, linear‑elastic, isotropic solid material with Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa and an assumed compressive yield strength σ_y = 250 MPa.  
The column is solid circular with radius r = 15 mm (diameter d = 30 mm) and length L = 1500 mm.  
End condition is fixed‑free (cantilever) with effective‑length factor K = 2.0.  
Use a factor of safety n = 2.0 for the allowable load.

Compute the second moment of area: I = π r⁴ / 4.  
Compute the Euler critical buckling load: P_cr = π² E I / (K L)².  
Compute the critical compressive stress: σ_cr = P_cr / A, where A = π r².  
Compute the factor of safety with respect to buckling based on material yield: FS_buckling = σ_y / σ_cr.  
Compute the allowable axial load: P_allow = P_cr / n.  

State the governing relations clearly and list the resulting numerical values for I, A, P_cr, σ_cr, FS_buckling, and P_allow.  
Assume temperature effects, residual stresses, and geometric imperfections are negligible.
