Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior and that the axial tensile force P is uniformly distributed over the cross‑section of each segment.  
Use the cross‑sectional area A = π d²/4 for each segment.  
Compute axial stress in segment i as σ_i = P / A_i.  
Compute axial strain in segment i as ε_i = σ_i / E.  
Compute elongation of segment i as ΔL_i = ε_i L_i = P L_i / (A_i E).  
The total elongation of the stepped bar is ΔL_total = ΔL_1 + ΔL_2.  
Given: E = 200 000 MPa, P = 25 000 N, L₁ = 300 mm, d₁ = 12 mm, L₂ = 600 mm, d₂ = 30 mm.  
Calculate A₁ = π (12 mm)²/4, A₂ = π (30 mm)²/4, then σ₁, σ₂, ΔL₁, ΔL₂, and ΔL_total using the relations above.
