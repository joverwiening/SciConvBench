Assume a prismatic, homogeneous beam of length L, flexural rigidity EI, subjected to a constant transverse load intensity w (force per unit length).  
Adopt Euler‑Bernoulli beam theory, small‑deformation kinematics, and linear‑elastic material behavior.  
The beam is simply supported at x = 0 and x = L (zero vertical displacement and zero bending moment at the supports).  

The governing differential equation for the transverse deflection v(x) is  
EI  d⁴v/dx⁴ = w.  

Integrating twice and applying the simply supported boundary conditions yields the slope (rotation) θ(x) = dv/dx and deflection v(x):  

θ(x) = (w / 12EI) [ L² – 2L x + x² ]  

v(x) = (w / 24EI) [ L³ x – 2L x³ + x⁴ ]  

The maximum deflection occurs at mid‑span (x = L/2):  

v_max = w L⁴ / (384 EI).  

The slope at the supports (x = 0 and x = L) is:  

θ_support = w L³ / (24 EI).  

These expressions provide the required response quantities (deflection and slope) for a simply supported beam under a uniform transverse load.
