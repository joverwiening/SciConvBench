Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior and that the beam material is homogeneous and isotropic.  
Neglect the self‑weight of the beam and any shear deformation (Euler‑Bernoulli beam theory).  
The beam has constant cross‑sectional second moment of area I = 2 500 000 mm⁴ along its entire length L = 1500 mm.  
A vertical point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end of the cantilever.  
Young’s modulus of the material is E = 200 000 MPa (200 000 N/mm²).  

Governing relations:  
The bending moment distribution is M(x) = –P·(L – x) for 0 ≤ x ≤ L, where x is measured from the fixed support.  
The curvature of the beam is κ(x) = M(x) / (E I).  
The strain‑energy stored in the beam is U = ∫₀ᴸ M(x)² / (2 E I) dx.  
By Castigliano’s theorem, the vertical deflection at the load point is δ = ∂U/∂P = P L³ / (3 E I).  

Therefore, the required end deflection is δ = (500 N)·(1500 mm)³ / [3·(200 000 N/mm²)·(2 500 000 mm⁴)].  
Present the numerical result in millimetres, and include the above governing equations and assumptions in the report.
