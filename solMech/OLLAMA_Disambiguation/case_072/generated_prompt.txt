Assume the shaft material is homogeneous, isotropic, and obeys linear‑elastic small‑deformation behavior with constant shear modulus G.  
Use the given geometric and loading data: radius r = 12.5 mm, length L = 1000 mm, applied torque T = 350 000 N·mm, shear modulus G = 76 923.1 MPa (N/mm²).  

Compute the polar moment of inertia:  
J = π r⁴ / 2.  

Strain‑energy stored by torsion:  
U = T² L / (2 G J).  

Apply Castigliano’s theorem to obtain the angle of twist:  
θ = ∂U/∂T = T L / (G J).  

Maximum shear stress at the outer surface:  
τ_max = T r / J.  

Shear strain distribution:  
γ(r) = τ(r) / G = (T r) / (G J).  

Torsional rigidity (torsional stiffness):  
k_t = G J / L.  

Present the governing relations in the order listed above, using consistent units (mm, N, N·mm, N/mm²).
