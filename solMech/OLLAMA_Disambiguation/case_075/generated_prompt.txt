Assume the adhesive layer behaves as a homogeneous, isotropic, linear‑elastic solid under small deformations.  
The layer is a rectangular plate with thickness t = 20 mm and in‑plane area A = 4000 mm²; the exact length and width are not required because only the product A enters the shear calculations.  
The material shear modulus is G = 39179.1 MPa; Young’s modulus and Poisson’s ratio are not needed for pure shear response.  

The member is subjected to a uniform shear force V acting parallel to the faces of the adhesive layer.  

Shear stress: τ = V / A.  

Shear strain: γ = τ / G.  

Shear deformation (relative displacement between the two faces): δ = γ · t = (τ · t) / G = (V · t) / (G · A).  

Shear strain‑energy density: U = ½ τ γ = τ² / (2 G).  

Total shear strain energy stored in the layer: U_total = U · V_layer = ½ τ² · (A · t) / G.  

Assume the faces remain parallel, no normal stresses develop, and plane sections remain plane.  
Neglect temperature effects, and assume the adhesive is perfectly bonded to the adjoining members with no slip at the interfaces.
