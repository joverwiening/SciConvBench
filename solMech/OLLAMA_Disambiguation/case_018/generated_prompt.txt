Assume a solid circular shaft of diameter d = 40 mm (radius r = 20 mm) made of a homogeneous, isotropic linear‑elastic material with shear modulus G = 25 939.8 MPa; small deformations are considered.  
The shaft is fixed at the end x = 0 and free at the opposite end x = L = 1500 mm.  
Two torques are applied about the shaft axis:  
‑ Torque T₁ = +300 000 N·mm at the free end x = 1500 mm (positive sense).  
‑ Torque T₂ = ‑150 000 N·mm at x = 750 mm from the fixed end (opposite sense to T₁).  

Additional assumptions: no axial, bending, or transverse shear loads; warping is neglected; the shaft cross‑section remains plane and perpendicular to the axis.

Governing relations for torsion of a solid circular shaft:  
1. Polar moment of inertia J = π d⁴ / 32.  
2. Shear stress distribution τ(r) = T · r / J (linear with radius, maximum at r = 20 mm).  
3. Angle of twist over a segment θ = ∫ T / (G J) dx = T · Δx / (G J) for constant T.  

Compute J: J = π (40 mm)⁴ / 32 ≈ 2.513 × 10⁵ mm⁴.  

Internal torque distribution (sign convention as above):  
‑ For 0 ≤ x < 750 mm: T_int = T₂ = ‑150 000 N·mm.  
‑ For 750 mm ≤ x ≤ 1500 mm: T_int = T₁ + T₂ = +150 000 N·mm.  

Maximum shear stress in each region (at r = 20 mm):  
‑ τ_max = |T_int| · r / J = 150 000 N·mm · 20 mm / 2.513 × 10⁵ mm⁴ ≈ 11.9 N/mm².  
The sign of τ follows the sign of T_int, giving opposite shear directions in the two segments.

Angle of twist for each segment:  
‑ θ₁ (0 → 750 mm) = T_int · Δx / (G J) = (‑150 000 N·mm)(750 mm) / (25 939.8 N/mm² · 2.513 × 10⁵ mm⁴) ≈ ‑0.0173 rad (‑0.99°).  
‑ θ₂ (750 → 1500 mm) = (+150 000 N·mm)(750 mm) / (G J) ≈ +0.0173 rad (+0.99°).  

Resulting total twist at the free end: θ_total = θ₁ + θ₂ ≈ 0 rad (the shaft returns to its original orientation because the net applied torque is zero).  

Thus, the relevant response quantities are: internal torque distribution, shear stress distribution (maximum shear stress ≈ 11.9 N/mm² at the outer surface, opposite sign in the two sections), and the piecewise angle‑twist values (‑0.99° over the first half, +0.99° over the second half, yielding zero net twist).
