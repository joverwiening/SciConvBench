Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior and Euler‑Bernoulli beam theory.  
The column length is L = 1000 mm and the end condition is fixed–free (cantilever), giving an effective length factor K = 2.0.  
Material Young’s modulus is E = 200 000 N/mm² and the second moment of area is I = 1 200 000 mm⁴.  

Compute the Euler critical buckling load:  
P_cr = (π² E I)/(K L)² = (π² E I)/(4 L²) = 5.92 × 10⁵ N (≈ 592 kN).  

Buckling/instability check: the column remains stable as long as the applied axial compressive load P satisfies P < P_cr; if P ≥ P_cr the column will buckle.  

Additional assumptions: the compressive load is perfectly axial, the column has no initial geometric imperfections, temperature effects are neglected, the material is homogeneous and isotropic, shear deformation is ignored, and the cross‑section is constant along the length.
