Assume a prismatic cantilever beam of length L, constant flexural rigidity EI, made of a homogeneous linear‑elastic material with Young’s modulus E and second moment of area I.  
The beam is fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.  
A uniformly distributed transverse load w (force per unit length) acts over the entire span.  

Additional assumptions:  
- Small deformations and plane sections remain plane (Euler‑Bernoulli beam theory).  
- No axial loads, shear deformation, or rotary inertia effects.  
- The load is static and the beam is in static equilibrium.  

Governing relation:  
EI d⁴y/dx⁴ = w, where y(x) is the transverse deflection.  

Integrating the differential equation and applying the cantilever boundary conditions (y = 0, dy/dx = 0 at the fixed end x = 0) yields the tip‑deflection and free‑end slope:  

Tip deflection: δ_tip = w L⁴ / (8 EI)  

Free‑end slope: θ_tip = w L³ / (6 EI)   (θ = dy/dx at x = L)
