Assume a straight prismatic circular rod of uniform cross‑section, subjected to a static axial tensile load, with small deformations and linear‑elastic material behavior.  
Use the following given data: rod diameter = 12 mm, applied axial load = 15 000 N, gage length = 800 mm, Young’s modulus = 69 000 MPa, material yield strength = 275 MPa.  

Compute the cross‑sectional area: \(A = \pi d^{2}/4\).  

Determine the average normal stress: \(\sigma = P / A\).  

Determine the axial strain: \(\varepsilon = \sigma / E\).  

Determine the total elongation: \(\Delta L = \varepsilon \, L\).  

Evaluate the factor of safety with respect to yielding: \(FS = \sigma_{y} / \sigma\).  

State the additional assumptions: stress is uniformly distributed over the cross‑section, temperature effects are negligible, the rod is free of initial stresses, and the load is applied slowly so dynamic effects can be ignored.  

Present the governing relations and the numerical results for \(\sigma\), \(\varepsilon\), \(\Delta L\), and \(FS\) using the above formulas.
