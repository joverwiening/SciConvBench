Assume small‑deformation, linear‑elastic behavior for both bars and neglect temperature effects, shear deformations, and any slip between the bars; the bars are perfectly aligned and share the same axial strain.  
The total axial load applied to the parallel composite bar is P = 20 000 N (tensile).  

Governing relations:  
- Compatibility: ε₁ = ε₂ = ε (common axial strain).  
- Constitutive law for each bar: σᵢ = Eᵢ ε, where i = 1 for steel and i = 2 for aluminum.  
- Stress definition: σᵢ = Pᵢ / Aᵢ.  
- Equilibrium: P₁ + P₂ = P.  

Compute the equivalent axial stiffness:  
- A₁ E₁ = 300 mm² × 200 000 MPa = 60 000 000 N.  
- A₂ E₂ = 200 mm² × 69 000 MPa = 13 800 000 N.  
- Sum = 73 800 000 N.  

Common strain: ε = P / (A₁ E₁ + A₂ E₂) = 20 000 N / 73 800 000 N = 2.71 × 10⁻⁴ (dimensionless).  

Axial deformation of the composite bar: δ = ε L = 2.71 × 10⁻⁴ × 500 mm = 0.135 mm.  

Load carried by each bar:  
- P₁ = ε A₁ E₁ = 2.71 × 10⁻⁴ × 60 000 000 N = 16 250 N (≈).  
- P₂ = P – P₁ = 20 000 N – 16 250 N = 3 750 N (≈).  

Axial stresses:  
- σ₁ = P₁ / A₁ = 16 250 N / 300 mm² = 54.2 MPa.  
- σ₂ = P₂ / A₂ = 3 750 N / 200 mm² = 18.8 MPa.  

Overall axial stiffness of the parallel system: K = P / δ = 20 000 N / 0.135 mm = 1.48 × 10⁵ N/mm.  

Equivalent Young’s modulus for the composite cross‑section:  
E_eq = (A₁ E₁ + A₂ E₂) / (A₁ + A₂) = 73 800 000 N / 500 mm² = 147 600 MPa.  

Response quantities to report: common axial strain ε, total deformation δ, individual axial stresses σ₁ and σ₂, individual loads P₁ and P₂, overall stiffness K, and equivalent modulus E_eq.
