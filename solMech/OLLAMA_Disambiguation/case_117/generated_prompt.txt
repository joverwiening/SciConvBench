Assume a homogeneous, isotropic, linear‑elastic solid with small deformations; plane sections remain plane and transverse shear deformation is neglected except for shear stress calculation.  
The rectangular beam cross‑section has width b = 60 mm and height h = 80 mm and is subjected to a transverse shear force V = 16 000 N.  

Governing relations:  
1. The average shear stress over the cross‑section is τ̄ = V / (b h).  
2. For a rectangular section, the shear stress distribution across the height y (measured from the neutral axis, y = 0 at mid‑height) is  
   τ(y) = (3/2) · V / (b h) · [1 – (2y/h)²].  
3. The maximum transverse shear stress occurs at the neutral axis (y = 0) and is  
   τ_max = (3/2) · V / (b h).  

Using the given values:  
- τ̄ = 16 000 N / (60 mm · 80 mm) = 3.33 MPa.  
- τ_max = (3/2) · 3.33 MPa = 5.00 MPa.  

Provide the shear stress distribution τ(y) for any y in the interval –h/2 ≤ y ≤ h/2 and report τ_max = 5.00 MPa as the relevant response quantity.
