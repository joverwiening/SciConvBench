The beam is prismatic, linear‑elastic, and experiences small deformations; material modulus E and second moment of area I are constant so that the flexural rigidity EI = 3.0 × 10¹¹ N·mm².  
The beam length is L = 3500 mm and it is subjected to a uniformly distributed transverse load w = 0.5 N/mm.  
The beam is simply supported, i.e., vertical deflection v = 0 at x = 0 and x = L, and the bending moment is zero at the supports.  

Governing relations:  

EI d⁴v/dx⁴ = w (constant).  

Bending moment distribution: M(x) = w x (L − x)/2.  

Curvature: d²v/dx² = M(x)/EI.  

Integrating with the simply supported boundary conditions gives the slope and deflection functions:  

θ(x) = dv/dx = (w/(24 EI)) [ 6 L x² − 4 x³ − L³ ].  

v(x) = (w/(24 EI)) [ 2 L x³ − x⁴ − L³ x ].  

Key response quantities:  

Maximum mid‑span deflection (at x = L/2):  
v_max = (5 w L⁴)/(384 EI).  

Slope at the supports (x = 0 and x = L):  
θ_support = − w L³/(24 EI) (magnitude).  

These expressions provide the required deflection and slope for the simply supported beam under the given uniform load.
