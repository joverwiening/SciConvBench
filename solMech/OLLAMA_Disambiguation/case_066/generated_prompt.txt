Assume a small‑deformation, linear‑elastic, isotropic solid circular shaft made of a material with shear modulus G = 76 923.1 MPa.  
Diameter d = 45 mm, radius c = 22.5 mm.  
Applied torque T = 400 000 N·mm.  
Length L = 1000 mm.  

Additional assumptions:  
- The shaft is prismatic and free of axial loads or bending moments.  
- Temperature effects and residual stresses are neglected.  

Governing relations:  
- Polar moment of inertia J = π c⁴ / 2.  
- Radial distribution of shear stress τ(r) = T r / J, with maximum shear stress τ_max = T c / J at the outer surface.  
- Angle of twist θ (radians) = T L / (J G).  

Computed quantities:  
- J = π (22.5 mm)⁴ / 2 ≈ 4.021 × 10⁵ mm⁴.  
- τ_max = (400 000 N·mm)(22.5 mm) / 4.021 × 10⁵ mm⁴ ≈ 22.4 N/mm² (≈22.4 MPa).  
- θ = (400 000 N·mm)(1000 mm) / (4.021 × 10⁵ mm⁴ · 76 923.1 N/mm²) ≈ 0.0129 rad ≈ 0.74°.  

Present the shear stress distribution τ(r) = (400 000 N·mm) r / 4.021 × 10⁵ mm⁴ and the angle of twist θ = 0.0129 rad for the given shaft.
