A solid circular column made of structural steel has diameter d = 25 mm and length L = 2000 mm. Take Young’s modulus E = 200000 MPa. The effective length factor is K = 0.7. The column carries a compressive load P = 25000 N.

(a) Compute the average compressive stress σ_avg = P/A and compare it to the yield strength Sy = 250 MPa.
(b) Compute the Euler critical buckling load P_cr and compare it to P. Use P_cr = π^2 E I/(K L)^2 with I = π d^4/64 for a solid circular cross section.
Based on these two checks, state whether yielding or Euler buckling is the more critical limit state for this column.
