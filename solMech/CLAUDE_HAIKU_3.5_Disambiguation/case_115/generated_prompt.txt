Determine the torsional response of a hollow circular shaft under the following conditions:

Geometric Properties:
Outer radius = 30 mm
Inner radius = 22 mm
Shaft length = 1200 mm

Loading Conditions:
Applied torque = 600,000 N·mm

Material Properties:
Material: Structural steel
Shear modulus (G) = 77,519.4 MPa

Analysis Assumptions:
Small-deformation linear-elastic solid mechanics
Uniform torque application along shaft length

Governing Relations:
Calculate maximum shear stress using the torsion formula for hollow circular cross-sections
Determine angle of twist using the torsional rigidity equation
Evaluate stress distribution across the shaft cross-section
Verify that maximum shear stress does not exceed material yield strength

Specific Outputs Required:
Maximum shear stress in the shaft
Angle of twist at the shaft end
Stress distribution across the cross-section
