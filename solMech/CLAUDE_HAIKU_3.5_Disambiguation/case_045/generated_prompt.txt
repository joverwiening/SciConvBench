The circular rod is a 6061-T6 aluminum rod with the following specifications:

Diameter is 12 mm.
Length is 800 mm.
Axial load is 15000 N applied through the centroid.
Young's modulus (E) is 69000 MPa.
Yield strength is 275 MPa.

Calculation of response quantities:
Cross-sectional area of the rod is π(d/2)² = π(0.006)² = 0.000113 m².
Average normal stress is load/area = 15000 N / 0.000113 m² = 132.743 MPa.
Average normal strain is stress/Young's modulus = 132.743 MPa / 69000 MPa = 0.00192.
Elongation is strain * original length = 0.00192 * 0.800 m = 0.00154 m = 1.54 mm.

Factor of safety is yield strength / average normal stress = 275 MPa / 132.743 MPa = 2.07.

Assumptions:
Small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics assumption is applied.
Uniform stress distribution across the cross-section is assumed.
Axial loading is the only applied load.
No additional environmental or temperature effects are considered.
