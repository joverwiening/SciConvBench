Specification for Stepped Shaft Torque and Twist Analysis:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

Shaft Geometry:
Segment AB:
- Length = 300 mm
- Diameter = 20 mm

Segment BC:
- Length = 500 mm
- Diameter = 30 mm

Material Properties:
- Material: 6061-T6 aluminum
- Shear Modulus (G) = 25,939.8 MPa

Loading Conditions:
- Applied Torque (T) = 300,000 N·mm

Analysis Objectives:
- Determine stress distribution in each shaft segment
- Calculate total angle of twist from end A to end C

Computational Approach:
- Use linear elastic theory for stress and deformation calculation
- Apply Saint-Venant's principle for stress distribution
- Calculate twist using polar moment of inertia and shear modulus
- Compute total twist by integrating angular deformation across segments

Coordinate System:
- Reference point: Fixed end A
- Twist measurement: Total angle of twist at end C relative to fixed end A

Assumptions:
- Uniform torque application
- Neglect stress concentration effects
- Small deformation linear elastic behavior
- Homogeneous material properties
