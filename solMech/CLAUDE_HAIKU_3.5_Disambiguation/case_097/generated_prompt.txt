Specification for Stepped Shaft Torsional Analysis:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics for the analysis.

The problem involves a stepped circular shaft with the following characteristics:
Segment AB: Length = 400 mm, Diameter = 25 mm
Segment BC: Length = 900 mm, Diameter = 30 mm
Material: 6061-T6 Aluminum
Shear Modulus (G) = 25939.8 MPa
Applied Torque (T) = 750,000 N·mm

Determine the following response quantities:
Compute the shear stress distribution in each segment of the shaft.
Calculate the total twist angle of the shaft.

Use the following governing relations:
Shear stress (τ) = Tc / J, where T is torque, c is the radius, and J is the polar moment of inertia.
Angle of twist (θ) = TL / (GJ), where L is the length of each segment.

Polar moment of inertia (J) will be calculated separately for each segment based on its diameter.

The analysis will follow standard linear elastic torsion theory for stepped shafts.
