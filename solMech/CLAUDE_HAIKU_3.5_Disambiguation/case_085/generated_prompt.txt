The beam is a linear-elastic prismatic beam with a rectangular cross-section.

Cross-section dimensions:
Width (b) = 50 mm
Height (h) = 100 mm

Loading conditions:
Bending moment (M) = 3,000,000 N·mm
Moment applied about centroidal z-axis
Sagging moment configuration (top fiber in compression, bottom fiber in tension)

Material properties:
Young's modulus (E) = 200,000 MPa
Linear-elastic behavior assumed

Analysis approach:
Use flexure formula for rectangular cross-section
Neutral axis horizontal (parallel to 50 mm width)
Calculate stress using σ = My/I formula
Calculate moment of inertia Iz = (b * h³) / 12
Maximum stress will occur at extreme fibers (top and bottom of cross-section)
Strain distribution will be linear across cross-section height
Maximum strain proportional to maximum stress via Hooke's law (ε = σ/E)

Assumptions:
Small-deformation linear-elastic solid mechanics
Plane sections remain plane after deformation
Homogeneous and isotropic material behavior
No shear deformation effects considered
