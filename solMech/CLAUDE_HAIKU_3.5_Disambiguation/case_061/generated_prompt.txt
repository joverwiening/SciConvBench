Specification for Eccentric Axial Load Analysis of Rectangular Column:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

Loading Conditions:
The column is subjected to an eccentric axial load of P = 60,000 N.
The load is applied with an eccentricity e = 20 mm from the centroid along the width direction.

Geometric Properties:
Column cross-section width (b) = 60 mm.
Column cross-section height (h) = 120 mm.

Material Properties:
Material: Structural Steel
Young's Modulus (E) = 200,000 MPa (200 GPa)
Poisson's Ratio (ν) = 0.3

Analysis Approach:
Determine the stress distribution across the column cross-section.
Calculate the normal stress due to axial load and bending moment.
Evaluate the deformation and strain characteristics of the column.
Use linear elastic material behavior for stress-strain relationships.

Key Governing Relations:
Normal stress σ = P/A + (P * e * y) / I
Where:
P = Applied axial load
A = Cross-sectional area
e = Eccentricity
y = Distance from neutral axis
I = Second moment of inertia of the cross-section
