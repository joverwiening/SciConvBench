Total column length is 2000 mm.
Effective length factor K = 0.7.
Moment of inertia I = 1,200,000 mm^4.
Young's modulus E = 200,000 MPa.
End conditions are fixed-pinned.

Calculate the Euler critical buckling load using the Euler column buckling formula:
Pcr = (π^2 * E * I) / (K * L)^2

Perform a buckling/instability check by comparing the applied compressive load to the critical buckling load.

Assumptions:
Small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics approach.
Slender column behavior with negligible initial imperfections.
Uniform cross-section along the column length.

Evaluate the column's stability by determining if the applied load exceeds the critical buckling load.
