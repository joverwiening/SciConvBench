The specification for the AISI rod analysis is as follows:

Perform a linear-elastic solid mechanics analysis for an AISI rod under uniaxial tension.
Use the following rod dimensions: diameter = 20 mm, length = 1200 mm.
Apply an axial tensile load of 35000 N through the rod's centroid.
Compute the following response quantities:
Axial stress
Axial strain
Lateral strain
Change in rod diameter
Assume small-deformation linear-elastic material behavior.
Use standard material properties for AISI steel.
Focus on demonstrating the Poisson effect under uniaxial tension.
