Problem: Axial Load Response of a Prismatic Bar under Sudden Loading

Assumptions:
Small-deformation linear-elastic solid mechanics applies.
Material is structural steel.
Load is suddenly applied.

Input Parameters:
Applied axial load (P) = 8000 N
Cross-sectional area (A) = 300 mm^2
Young's modulus (E) = 200000 MPa
Bar length (L) = 800 mm

Governing Relations:
Calculate engineering stress: σ = P / A
Compute axial strain: ε = σ / E
Determine maximum stress: σmax = P / A
Evaluate strain energy: U = (1/2) * (P^2 / (E * A)) * L

Key Response Quantities:
Determine the maximum stress developed in the bar.
Calculate the strain energy associated with the sudden axial loading.
Assess the bar's deformation characteristics under the applied load.

Verification Method:
Confirm stress does not exceed material yield strength.
Validate linear-elastic behavior assumptions.
