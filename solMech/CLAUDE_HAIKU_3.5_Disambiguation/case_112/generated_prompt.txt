The specification for the linear-elastic tension analysis of a circular aluminum rod is as follows:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics principles.

Rod Geometry:
Diameter is 20 mm.
Length is 800 mm.
Cross-sectional area is 314.16 mm².

Loading Conditions:
Axial tensile load is 15000 N.

Material Properties:
Material is 6061-T6 aluminum.
Young's modulus is 69000 MPa.
Yield strength is 275 MPa.

Calculations to Perform:
Calculate the average normal stress by dividing the applied load by the cross-sectional area.
Calculate the engineering strain using the elongation divided by original length.
Calculate the rod's total elongation using the stress, strain, and elastic modulus.
Compute the factor of safety by dividing the yield strength by the calculated average normal stress.

Reporting Requirements:
Report the average normal stress in MPa.
Report the engineering strain as a dimensionless value.
Report the total elongation in mm.
Report the factor of safety as a numerical value.
