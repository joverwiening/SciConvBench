The specification for the solid circular shaft analysis is as follows:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The shaft is a solid circular shaft with diameter of 50 mm.
The shaft length is 600 mm.
Material is structural steel with shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.
The applied torque is 500,000 N·mm.

Analysis will focus on determining:
Shear stress distribution in the shaft.
Angle of twist of the shaft.

Governing relations to be used:
Shear stress will be calculated using τ = Tr/J, where T is torque, r is radial distance from shaft centerline, and J is polar moment of inertia.
Angle of twist will be calculated using θ = (TL)/(JG), where L is shaft length and G is shear modulus.
Polar moment of inertia for circular shaft will be calculated as J = (π/2)r^4.

Assumptions:
Linear elastic material behavior.
Uniform circular cross-section.
Torque applied at shaft ends.
No additional external loads or constraints.
