Specification for Stepped Shaft Stress and Twist Analysis:

Perform a linear-elastic torsional analysis of a two-segment stepped circular shaft with the following parameters:

Torque Loading:
- Total applied torque (T) = 600,000 N·mm

Shaft Geometry:
- Segment AB: Length = 400 mm, Diameter = 30 mm
- Segment BC: Length = 700 mm, Diameter = 30 mm

Material Properties:
- Material: Structural steel
- Shear modulus (G) = 77,519.4 MPa
- Yield strength (σy) = 250 MPa

Analysis Objectives:
- Determine maximum shear stress in each shaft segment
- Calculate total twist (angular deformation) of the shaft
- Verify stress does not exceed yield strength
- Use small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics assumptions

Governing Relations:
- Use polar moment of inertia for circular cross-sections
- Apply Saint-Venant's torsion theory
- Calculate shear stress using τ = (T * r) / J
- Compute angular twist using θ = (T * L) / (G * J)

Validation:
- Compare calculated maximum stress against yield strength
- Ensure linear-elastic behavior is maintained
