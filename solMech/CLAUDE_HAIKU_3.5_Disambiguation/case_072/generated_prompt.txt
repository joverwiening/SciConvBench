Specification for Torsional Analysis of Circular Shaft:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

Shaft Geometry:
Diameter is 25 mm.
Length is 1000 mm.
Material is AISI 1020 steel.
Shear modulus (G) is 76923.1 MPa.

Applied Loading:
Torque magnitude is 350000 N·mm.

Analysis Method:
Use Castigliano's energy method to compute shaft twist.

Governing Relations:
Polar moment of inertia (J) = π(d^4)/32, where d is shaft diameter.
Maximum shear stress (τmax) = (T * r)/J, where T is torque and r is shaft radius.
Angle of twist (θ) = (T * L)/(G * J), where L is shaft length.

Compute and report:
Polar moment of inertia.
Maximum shear stress.
Total angle of twist.

Assumptions:
Linear elastic behavior.
Uniform circular cross-section.
Homogeneous material properties.
Small deformation theory applies.
