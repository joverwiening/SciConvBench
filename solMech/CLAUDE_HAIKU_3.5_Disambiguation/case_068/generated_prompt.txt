The specification for the solid circular shaft analysis is as follows:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The shaft is a solid circular shaft made of 6061-T6 aluminum.
Shaft diameter is 30 mm.
Shaft length is 800 mm.
Applied torque is 500,000 N·mm.
Material properties: Shear modulus G = 25,939.8 MPa.
Calculate the shear stress distribution across the shaft cross-section.
Calculate the total angle of twist for the shaft under torque loading.
Use the torsion formula: τ = (T * r) / J, where τ is shear stress, T is torque, r is radial distance from shaft centerline, and J is polar moment of inertia.
Use the angle of twist formula: θ = (T * L) / (G * J), where θ is angle of twist, L is shaft length, G is shear modulus, and J is polar moment of inertia.
For a circular shaft, J = (π * d^4) / 32, where d is shaft diameter.
