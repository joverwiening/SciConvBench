Determine the cantilever beam deflection under a uniform distributed load with the following specifications:

Beam Geometry:
The cantilever beam is 2000 mm long.
The beam has a rectangular cross-section with moment of inertia 1,500,000 mm^4.
The beam is made of structural steel with Young's modulus of 200,000 MPa.

Loading Conditions:
A uniform distributed transverse load of 1.0 N/mm is applied downward over the entire beam length.

Analysis Approach:
Use small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics principles.
Calculate beam deflection using standard beam bending theory.
Apply classical engineering beam deflection equations.

Governing Relations:
Maximum deflection will be calculated at the free end of the cantilever.
Use the standard beam deflection equation: δmax = (q * L^4) / (8 * E * I)
Where:
δmax is maximum deflection
q is uniform distributed load
L is beam length
E is Young's modulus
I is moment of inertia

Assumptions:
Linear elastic material behavior
Small deformation theory
Uniform cross-section
Perfectly fixed support at one end
