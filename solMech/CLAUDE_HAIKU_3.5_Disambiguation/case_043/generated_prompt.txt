Determine the critical buckling load and allowable load for a structural steel column with the following specifications:

Column Geometry:
Length = 2000 mm
Diameter = 20 mm
Effective length factor K = 1.0 (pinned-pinned end condition)

Material Properties:
Young's Modulus (E) = 200,000 MPa
Yield Strength = 250 MPa

Analysis Parameters:
Factor of Safety (n) = 2.0

Analytical Approach:
Use Euler buckling theory to calculate critical buckling load (P_cr)
Calculate moment of inertia for circular cross-section
Determine slenderness ratio
Compute critical buckling load using Euler column formula
Calculate allowable load as P_allow = P_cr / n

Deliverables:
Compute and report critical buckling load (P_cr)
Compute and report allowable axial compressive load (P_allow)
Verify buckling safety using factor of safety of 2.0

Assumptions:
Small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics
Homogeneous, isotropic structural steel material
Ideal pinned-pinned boundary conditions
Concentric axial loading
