The specification for the structural member shear analysis is as follows:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The structural member has an adhesive layer with the following characteristics:
Thickness of adhesive layer is 8 mm.
Material is isotropic with shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.
A uniform in-plane shear force V = 12000 N is applied to the adhesive layer.

Governing relations for shear stress-strain analysis:
Shear stress (τ) will be calculated using τ = V / (A), where A is the cross-sectional area of the adhesive layer.
Shear strain (γ) will be determined using γ = τ / G, based on the linear-elastic material model.
Shear deformation will be computed considering the layer thickness and material properties.

The analysis will focus on determining the shear stress distribution, strain response, and resulting deformation of the adhesive layer under the specified loading condition.
