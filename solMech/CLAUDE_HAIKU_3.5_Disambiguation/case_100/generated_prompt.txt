The cantilever beam analysis will be performed with the following specifications:

Beam Loading Conditions:
Point load of 500 N applied downward at the free end of the beam.
Beam length is 1500 mm.
Moment of inertia (I) is 2500000 mm^4.
Material is AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus of 200000 MPa.

Analysis Approach:
Apply Castigliano's second theorem to determine the end deflection.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

Governing Relations:
Calculate beam deflection using Castigliano's method with the given point load.
Use the principle of minimum potential energy.
Compute end deflection by taking the partial derivative of total potential energy with respect to the applied load.

Assumptions:
Beam cross-section is rectangular to match the given moment of inertia.
Linear elastic material behavior.
Small deformation theory applies.
Plane sections remain plane during deformation.

Analytical Goal:
Determine the vertical deflection at the free end of the cantilever beam under the specified point load.
