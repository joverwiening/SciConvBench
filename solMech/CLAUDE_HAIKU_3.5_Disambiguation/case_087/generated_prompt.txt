The tension specimen analysis will be conducted with the following specifications:

Material: AISI 1020 hot-rolled steel
Initial cross-sectional area: 113.10 mm²
Initial gauge length: 50 mm
Strain rate: 0.001/s (quasi-static loading)
Loading condition: Uniaxial tension
Deformation assumption: Small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics

Ductility Measures to Determine:
Percent elongation will be calculated as the change in gauge length divided by original gauge length, expressed as a percentage.
Reduction of area will be calculated as the change in cross-sectional area divided by original cross-sectional area, expressed as a percentage.

Governing Relations:
Percent Elongation = ((Lf - L0) / L0) × 100%
Reduction of Area = ((A0 - Af) / A0) × 100%

Where:
L0 = Original gauge length (50 mm)
Lf = Final gauge length after deformation
A0 = Original cross-sectional area (113.10 mm²)
Af = Final cross-sectional area after deformation

The analysis will track the specimen's deformation characteristics under tensile loading until failure.
