Specification for Built-Up Beam Analysis:

Assume a bolted connection built-up beam configuration with the following specifications:

Material Properties:
Use ASTM A36 structural steel for beam plates and fasteners.
Yield strength of 250 MPa.
Elastic modulus of 200 GPa.

Beam Configuration:
Assume a typical I-beam configuration with two flange plates and a web plate.
Web plate thickness: 10 mm.
Flange plate thickness: 15 mm.
Total beam cross-sectional moment of inertia: 18,000,000 mm^4.
Cross-sectional first moment of area: 45,000 mm^3.

Loading Conditions:
Internal shear force at critical section: V = 25,000 N.
Assume point load applied at mid-span creating the shear force.

Analysis Focus:
Determine shear flow distribution across the cross-section.
Calculate fastener spacing to transfer shear forces effectively.

Assumptions:
Small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Bolted connections with standard bolt spacing.
Uniform stress distribution in fasteners.
Neglect local stress concentrations around bolt holes.

Governing Relations:
Use shear flow equation: q = VQ/I
Calculate fastener spacing based on maximum shear stress in fasteners.
Verify fastener strength against applied shear forces.
