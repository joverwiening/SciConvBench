Specification for Solid Circular Shaft Analysis:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The shaft is a solid circular shaft made of AISI 1020 steel.
Shaft diameter is 45 mm.
Shaft length is 1000 mm.
Shear modulus (G) is 76923.1 MPa.
Applied torque is 400,000 N·mm.

Analysis will focus on determining:
Shear stress distribution in the shaft.
Angle of twist of the shaft.

Governing relations to be used:
Shear stress (τ) will be calculated using τ = (T * r) / J, where T is torque, r is radius, and J is polar moment of inertia.
Angle of twist (θ) will be calculated using θ = (T * L) / (J * G), where L is shaft length, J is polar moment of inertia, and G is shear modulus.

Polar moment of inertia (J) for a solid circular shaft will be calculated as J = (π * d^4) / 32, where d is shaft diameter.
