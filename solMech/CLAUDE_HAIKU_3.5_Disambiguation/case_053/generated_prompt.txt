Specification for Circular Shaft Torsion Analysis:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

Shaft Geometry:
Diameter is 20 mm.
Length is 1200 mm.

Loading Conditions:
Applied torque is 39.79 N·m (39,790 N·mm).
Rotational speed is 1200 rpm (125.66 rad/s).

Material Properties:
Material is 6061-T6 aluminum.
Shear modulus G = 25,939.8 MPa.
Shear yield strength = 207 MPa.

Analysis Requirements:
Compute power-torque conversion.
Perform torsion stress and deformation checks.
Verify shaft does not exceed shear yield strength.
Calculate maximum shear stress and angular deformation.

Governing Relations:
Power P = T·ω
Shear stress τ = (T·r) / J
Angular deformation θ = (T·L) / (J·G)

Where:
T = applied torque
ω = angular velocity
r = shaft radius
J = polar moment of inertia
L = shaft length
G = shear modulus
