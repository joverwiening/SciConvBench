The specification for the Euler Bernoulli beam analysis is as follows:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Use an Euler Bernoulli beam with a total length of 3500 mm.
Apply a uniformly distributed transverse load of 0.3 N/mm over the full beam span.
Apply a point load of 1200 N located at midspan (1750 mm from either end).
Use simply supported boundary conditions at both beam ends.
Determine beam deflection using the principle of superposition.
Calculate deflection contributions from:
- Uniformly distributed load
- Point load at midspan
Analyze the total deflection by summing individual load case deflections.
Use standard Euler Bernoulli beam deflection equations for each load type.
Ensure linear elastic material behavior throughout the analysis.
