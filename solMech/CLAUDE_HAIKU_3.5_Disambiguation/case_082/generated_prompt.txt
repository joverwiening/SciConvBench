The tension-test specimen analysis will be performed with the following specifications:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Use axial load of 12000 N.
Initial cross-sectional area is 122.72 mm².
Initial gauge length is 50 mm.

Calculate engineering stress by dividing applied load by initial cross-sectional area.
Calculate engineering strain by measuring change in length relative to initial gauge length.
Determine Young's modulus from the linear elastic region of the stress-strain curve.

Compute engineering stress as σ = P/A0.
Compute engineering strain as ε = ΔL/L0.
Use linear portion of stress-strain curve to calculate elastic modulus as E = σ/ε.

Assume uniform stress distribution across the specimen cross-section.
Assume specimen deforms uniformly along its gauge length.
Neglect any non-linear material behavior within the elastic regime.
