Design a solid circular shaft with the following specifications:

Determine the shaft diameter based on the following design constraints.

Applied Torque: 1,000,000 N·mm
Shaft Length: 1500 mm
Material: 6061-T6 Aluminum
Shear Modulus (G): 25,939.8 MPa
Maximum Allowable Shear Stress: 160 MPa
Maximum Acceptable Angle of Twist: 0.75° over 1500 mm

Design Governing Relations:
Use the torsional shear stress equation: τ = (T * r) / J
Use the angle of twist equation: θ = (T * L) / (J * G)
Where:
T = Applied torque
L = Shaft length
r = Shaft radius
J = Polar moment of inertia for circular cross-section
G = Shear modulus

Design Approach:
Determine the shaft diameter by simultaneously satisfying:
1. Maximum shear stress ≤ 160 MPa
2. Angle of twist ≤ 0.75° over total length

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics conditions.
