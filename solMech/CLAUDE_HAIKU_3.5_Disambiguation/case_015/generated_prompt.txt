Euler-Bernoulli Beam Analysis Specification:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

Beam Configuration:
The beam is a cantilever configuration with a length of 1200 mm.
The beam is made of AISI 1020 steel with a Young's modulus of 200,000 MPa.
The cross-sectional moment of inertia is 2,000,000 mm^4.

Loading Conditions:
A point load of 2000 N is applied downward at the free end of the beam.
No external moment is applied to the beam.

Boundary Conditions:
The beam is fixed at one end (built-in support).
The free end is unconstrained, allowing rotation and deflection.

Response Quantities of Interest:
Determine the maximum beam deflection at the free end.
Calculate the maximum bending moment along the beam.
Compute the beam's slope at the fixed end.

Governing Relations:
Use Euler-Bernoulli beam theory for deflection analysis.
Apply the principle of superposition for linear elastic deformation.
Use standard beam deflection equations for a cantilever beam with an end point load.
