Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Use an axial tensile load P = 35000 N applied through the centroid.

Use Young's modulus E = 69000 MPa.

Calculate the axial strain εₐₓᵢₐₗ = σ/E.
Calculate the lateral strain εₗₐₜₑᵣₐₗ = -ν * εₐₓᵢₐₗ.
