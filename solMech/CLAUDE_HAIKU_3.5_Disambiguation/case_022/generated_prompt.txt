Stepped Shaft Stress and Twist Analysis Specification:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

Shaft Geometry:
Segment AB:
- Length = 300 mm
- Diameter = 25 mm

Segment BC:
- Length = 700 mm
- Diameter = 30 mm

Loading Conditions:
- Applied Torque (T) = 450,000 N·mm

Material Properties:
- Material: Structural Steel
- Shear Modulus (G) = 77,519.4 MPa
- Yield Strength = 250 MPa

Analysis Objectives:
- Determine maximum shear stress in each shaft segment
- Calculate total twist angle of the stepped shaft
- Verify stress does not exceed yield strength
- Use Saint-Venant's principle for stress distribution
- Apply circular shaft torsion theory for twist calculation

Computational Method:
- Use linear elastic torsional analysis
- Consider stress concentration at diameter transition
- Calculate polar moment of inertia for each segment
- Compute shear stress using τ = (T * r) / J formula
- Determine twist angle using θ = (T * L) / (G * J) equation

Reporting Requirements:
- Present maximum shear stress for each segment
- Report total cumulative twist angle
- Indicate safety factor relative to yield strength
