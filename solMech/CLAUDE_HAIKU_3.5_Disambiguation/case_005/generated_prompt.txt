Total bar length is 1800 mm, divided into three segments:
- AB segment: 700 mm
- BC segment: 600 mm
- CD segment: 500 mm
Point loads are applied as follows:
- At point B: 18000 N (positive/right direction)
- At point C: -8000 N (negative/left direction)
- At point D: 20000 N (positive/right direction)
Determine the axial stress, strain, and displacement at each segment of the bar.
Calculate the internal axial force distribution along the bar's length.
Use linear elastic stress-strain relations for analysis.
Compute the total deformation of the bar under these point load conditions.
