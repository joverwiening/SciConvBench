Specification for Euler Bernoulli Beam Analysis:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Beam is a simply supported Euler Bernoulli beam.
Total beam length is 3000 mm.
Beam material is structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200,000 MPa.
Second moment of area I = 2,000,000 mm^4.
Beam is subjected to two loading conditions:
Uniformly distributed transverse load w = 0.3 N/mm over full span.
Point load P = 3000 N applied at midspan (1500 mm from either support).
Determine beam deflection using principle of superposition.
Calculate deflection due to distributed load separately.
Calculate deflection due to point load separately.
Sum individual deflections to obtain total beam deflection.
Use standard Euler Bernoulli beam deflection equations.
Consider classical beam theory governing differential equations.
Verify maximum deflection and stress remain within linear elastic limits.
