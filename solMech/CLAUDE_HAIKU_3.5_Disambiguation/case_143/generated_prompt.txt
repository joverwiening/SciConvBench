Diameter = 25 mm
Material: Steel

Loading Conditions:
Torque = 400,000 N·mm
Axial Load = 15,000 N

Analysis Objectives:
Determine stress and strain distributions under combined axial and torsional loading.
Calculate principal stress and principal strain measures.

Governing Analysis Approach:
Use linear elastic theory for stress calculation.
Apply von Mises yield criterion for stress evaluation.
Calculate normal and shear stresses using standard solid mechanics formulations.
