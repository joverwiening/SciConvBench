Structural Analysis Specification for Simply Supported Beam:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

Beam Characteristics:
Total beam span is 6000 mm.
Beam is simply supported at two ends.
Material is ASTM A36 structural steel.
Cross-section is W250x33 I-beam.

Loading Conditions:
Single concentrated vertical load P = 20,000 N.
Load acts downward.
Load is located 2000 mm from support A.

Analysis Objectives:
Determine internal shear force V at x = 4500 mm from support A.
Determine bending moment M at x = 4500 mm from support A.

Calculation Method:
Use 2D static equilibrium principles.
Apply beam bending theory for simply supported configuration.
Calculate shear and moment using statics equations.

Assumptions:
Linear elastic material behavior.
Small deformation theory applies.
Plane sections remain plane during deformation.
