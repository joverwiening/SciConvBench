The bar is a 1200 mm long aluminum (6061-T6) bar subjected to a 20000 N tensile load.

Assumptions:
Small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics apply.
Material properties are uniform throughout the bar.

Cross-sectional area variation:
A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L)
A0 = 200 mm^2 (initial area at x = 0)
k = 0.6
L = 1200 mm
Young's modulus (E) = 69000 MPa

Governing relations:
Axial stress σ(x) = P / A(x), where P is the applied load.
Axial strain ε(x) = dU(x) / dx, where U(x) is the axial displacement.
Stress-strain relation: σ(x) = E · ε(x)

The bar's axial deformation will be characterized by:
Linearly varying cross-sectional area from 200 mm^2 to 320 mm^2.
Stress and strain will vary non-uniformly along the bar's length due to area change.
Total axial displacement can be determined by integrating the strain along the bar's length.
