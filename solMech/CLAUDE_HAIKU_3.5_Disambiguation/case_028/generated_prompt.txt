The specification for the impact loading analysis of a vertical structural steel rod is as follows:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Use a vertical structural steel rod with the following specifications:
Length = 1200 mm
Diameter = 30 mm
Material = Structural steel
Young's modulus E = 200,000 MPa
Top end of the rod is fixed
Dropped weight = 500 N
Drop height = 200 mm

Analysis Objectives:
Determine P_max (maximum axial force) using energy conservation equation: W(h + δ_max) = (P_max^2 L)/(2AE)
Calculate σ_max (maximum normal stress) as P_max/A

Impact Analysis Assumptions:
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s²
Assume quasi-static impact condition
Compute impact velocity using v = √(2gh)
Analyze rod response under axial loading and impact

Computational Approach:
Apply energy conservation principle
Calculate maximum displacement (δ_max)
Determine maximum axial force (P_max)
Compute maximum normal stress (σ_max)
