The stepped circular bar analysis will proceed with the following specification:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Bar material is AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200,000 MPa.
Bar total length is 1100 mm, composed of two segments:
Segment 1 length is 300 mm with cross-sectional area A1 = 113.10 mm².
Segment 2 length is 800 mm with cross-sectional area A2 = 706.86 mm².
Applied tensile load is P = 35,000 N at the right end of the bar.
Calculate stress in each segment using σ = P/A for respective cross-sectional areas.
Calculate total bar elongation using δ = (P * L) / (A * E) for each segment.
Determine total elongation by summing individual segment elongations.
Verify stress and strain are within linear-elastic regime for AISI 1020 steel.
