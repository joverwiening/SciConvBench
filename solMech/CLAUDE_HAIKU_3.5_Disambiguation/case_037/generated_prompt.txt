The simply supported beam analysis specification is as follows:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam is simply supported with a total span of 4500 mm.
A uniformly distributed transverse load of 0.3 N/mm is applied over the full beam length.
The beam has a moment of inertia of 2,000,000 mm^4.
The beam material is structural steel with an elastic modulus of 200,000 MPa.

Deflection calculation shall use the standard beam deflection equation: y(x) = (w * x * (L^2 - x^2)) / (24 * E * I).
Maximum deflection shall occur at the beam's midspan (x = L/2).
Slope calculation shall use the beam slope equation: θ(x) = (w * x * (L^2 - x^2)) / (12 * E * I).
Slope shall be calculated relative to the beam's original horizontal axis.

Coordinate system shall have origin at the left beam support, with x increasing towards the right support.
Positive deflection is downward, consistent with standard beam mechanics conventions.
