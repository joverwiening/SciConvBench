The specification for the linear-elastic tension analysis of a circular steel rod is as follows:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics principles.
The circular rod is made of AISI 1020 steel.
The rod diameter is 12 mm.
The axial tensile load is 15000 N, applied through the rod's centroid.
Material properties: Young's modulus (E) = 200000 MPa, yield strength (σy) = 350 MPa.

Calculate the following response quantities:
Compute the cross-sectional area of the rod.
Determine the average normal stress by dividing the applied load by the cross-sectional area.
Calculate the engineering strain using the stress and Young's modulus.
Compute the rod's total elongation based on the strain and original length.
Evaluate the factor of safety by comparing the yield strength to the calculated average normal stress.
The factor of safety should be the ratio of yield strength to maximum applied stress.

Ensure all calculations follow linear-elastic material behavior assumptions.
Report results with appropriate SI units and engineering precision.
