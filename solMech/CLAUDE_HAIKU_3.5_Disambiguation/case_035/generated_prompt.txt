Column Buckling Analysis Specification for 6061-T6 Aluminum Column:

Material Properties:
Elastic Modulus (E) = 69,000 MPa
Material: 6061-T6 Aluminum

Geometric Parameters:
Column Length (L) = 2,200 mm
Effective Length Factor (K) = 2.0
End Support Condition: Pinned-Pinned
Compressive Load (P) = 25,000 N
Desired Factor of Safety (n) = 2.5

Analysis Methodology:
Use Euler's column buckling formula to determine critical buckling load.
Calculate the column's moment of inertia and cross-sectional area.
Determine the critical buckling stress.
Verify that the design meets the factor of safety requirement of 2.5.

Design Objective:
Select a circular column diameter that prevents buckling under the applied compressive load.
Ensure the critical buckling load is at least 2.5 times the applied load.

Assumptions:
Small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics model.
Perfectly straight column with uniform cross-section.
Negligible initial imperfections.
Uniform compressive loading along the column axis.

Evaluation Criteria:
Compare calculated critical buckling load with applied load.
Verify factor of safety meets or exceeds 2.5.
Select column diameter to satisfy buckling safety requirements.
