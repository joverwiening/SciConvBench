The stepped circular bar analysis will be performed with the following specification:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Bar geometry consists of two segments with different cross-sectional areas.
Segment 1 cross-sectional area: A1 = 201.06 mm²
Segment 2 cross-sectional area: A2 = 1256.64 mm²
Total bar length: 800 mm (L1 = 400 mm, L2 = 400 mm)
Applied tensile load: P = 25000 N
Material Young's modulus: E = 200000 MPa

Compute stress in each segment using σ = P/A formula.
Segment 1 stress will be calculated as σ1 = P/A1
Segment 2 stress will be calculated as σ2 = P/A2
Total bar elongation will be determined using δ = (P * L) / (E * A) for each segment.
Total elongation is the sum of individual segment elongations.
Assume uniform stress distribution within each segment.
Assume perfect load transfer at the segment interface.
