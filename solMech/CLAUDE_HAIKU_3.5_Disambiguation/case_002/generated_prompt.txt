Specification for Stepped Circular Bar Tension Analysis:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

Bar Geometry:
Segment 1 diameter is 16 mm with cross-sectional area of 201.06 mm².
Segment 2 diameter is 40 mm with cross-sectional area of 1256.64 mm².
Total bar length is 1200 mm (400 mm for Segment 1, 800 mm for Segment 2).

Loading Conditions:
Applied tensile load is 35000 N.
Material Young's modulus is 200000 MPa.

Analysis Method:
Calculate stress in each segment using σ = P/A.
Segment 1 stress: σ1 = 35000 N / 201.06 mm² = 174.02 MPa.
Segment 2 stress: σ2 = 35000 N / 1256.64 mm² = 27.85 MPa.

Calculate total bar elongation using δ = (P * L) / (E * A) for each segment.
Segment 1 elongation: δ1 = (35000 N * 400 mm) / (200000 MPa * 201.06 mm²) = 0.348 mm.
Segment 2 elongation: δ2 = (35000 N * 800 mm) / (200000 MPa * 1256.64 mm²) = 0.222 mm.

Total bar elongation: δtotal = δ1 + δ2 = 0.570 mm.
