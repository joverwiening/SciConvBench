The specification for the column buckling analysis is as follows:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Analyze a circular column with the following properties:
Length = 1500 mm
Diameter = 30 mm
Material = Structural steel
Young's modulus (E) = 200,000 MPa
Yield strength = 250 MPa
Effective length factor (K) = 2.0
End support condition = Pinned-pinned
Factor of safety (n) = 2.0

Perform Euler buckling analysis using the following steps:
Calculate the column's moment of inertia based on its circular cross-section.
Determine the critical buckling load (P_cr) using Euler's buckling formula.
Calculate the allowable axial compressive load as P_allow = P_cr / n.
Verify the factor of safety against buckling failure.

Evaluate the column's response under compressive loading with focus on:
Buckling behavior
Critical buckling load
Allowable compressive load
Factor of safety determination
