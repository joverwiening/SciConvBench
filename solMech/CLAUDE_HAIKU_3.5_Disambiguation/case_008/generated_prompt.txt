The specification for the two-bar composite system under tension is:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

Two prismatic bars of equal length L = 500 mm are mounted in parallel between two rigid plates.

Bar 1 (AISI 1020 steel):
- Cross-sectional area A1 = 300 mm^2
- Young's modulus E1 = 200000 MPa

Bar 2 (6061-T6 aluminum):
- Cross-sectional area A2 = 200 mm^2
- Young's modulus E2 = 69000 MPa

Total applied tensile load P = 20000 N

Determine the following response quantities:
1. Force carried by each bar
2. Stress in each bar
3. Common elongation of the bars

Calculation method:
- Use parallel bar composite theory
- Assume identical strain in both bars
- Calculate individual bar forces based on their relative stiffness
- Compute stress by dividing bar force by cross-sectional area
- Calculate elongation using strain-displacement relations
