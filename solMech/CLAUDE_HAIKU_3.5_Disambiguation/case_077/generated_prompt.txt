The prismatic bar specification is as follows:

Geometry:
Width (b) = 50 mm
Height (h) = 120 mm
Cross-sectional area = 6000 mm²
Length = 900 mm (chosen as 7.5 times the cross-sectional height)

Loading Conditions:
Tension Force (P) = 40,000 N
Transverse Shear Force (V) = 15,000 N

Material Properties (ASTM A36 Structural Steel):
Young's Modulus (E) = 200 GPa
Poisson's Ratio (ν) = 0.3

Analysis Assumptions:
Small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics
Uniform stress distribution across the cross-section

Stress Calculation Relations:
Average Normal Stress (σ) = Tension Force / Cross-sectional Area
Average Shear Stress (τ) = Shear Force / Cross-sectional Area

The analysis will focus on determining the average normal and shear stresses at a section under the specified loading conditions.
