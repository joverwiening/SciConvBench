The problem involves analyzing a cantilever beam under a uniform distributed load with the following specifications:

Beam Configuration:
The beam is a cantilever with a total length of 2500 mm.

Loading Conditions:
A uniformly distributed transverse load of 1.0 N/mm is applied downward over the entire span of the beam.

Material Properties:
Material: AISI 1020 steel
Young's Modulus (E): 200,000 MPa
Moment of Inertia (I): 1,000,000 mm^4

Governing Relations for Beam Deflection:
The maximum deflection at the free end of the cantilever will be calculated using the standard beam deflection formula: δmax = (w * L^4) / (8 * E * I).

Analysis Assumptions:
Small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics assumption is applied.
The beam experiences pure bending with no shear deformation.
Plane sections remain plane during deformation.

Response Quantities of Interest:
Maximum deflection at the free end of the cantilever beam.
Bending moment distribution along the beam length.
