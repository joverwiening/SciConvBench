The specification for the prismatic bar stress analysis is as follows:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Use a prismatic bar with rectangular cross-section.
Cross-section dimensions: Width (b) = 40 mm, Height (h) = 120 mm.
Cross-sectional area = 4800 mm².
Bar length = 480 mm (4 times the height).
Tension force (P) = 30000 N (acting in tension).
Transverse shear force (V) = 12000 N (acting parallel to the width).
Compute average normal stress σ_avg = P / A.
Compute average shear stress τ_avg = V / A.
Use the rectangular cross-section area for stress calculations.
Assume uniform stress distribution across the cross-section.
Neglect stress concentration effects and assume linear elastic material behavior.
