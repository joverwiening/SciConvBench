The specification for the circular shaft stress analysis is as follows:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

Shaft Geometry:
Diameter = 45 mm
Material = Steel
Young's modulus (E) = 200 GPa
Poisson's ratio (ν) = 0.3

Loading Conditions:
Bending Moment (M) = 2,000,000 N·mm
Torque (T) = 500,000 N·mm
Loading Configuration: Simply supported beam with central moment

Analysis Requirements:
Determine the stress and strain distributions at the critical section.
Compute maximum normal and shear stresses.
Calculate principal stress and principal strain measures.
Use von Mises stress criterion for failure assessment.

Computational Approach:
Apply superposition of bending and torsional stress components.
Use plane stress transformation equations.
Evaluate stress tensor components at the critical section.
Determine maximum principal stress and principal strain.

Assumptions:
Linear elastic material behavior
Uniform circular cross-section
Plane sections remain plane during deformation
Neglect self-weight and secondary bending effects
