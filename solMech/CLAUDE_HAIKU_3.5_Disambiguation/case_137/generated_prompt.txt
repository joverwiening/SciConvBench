The specification for the simply supported beam analysis is as follows:

Beam Geometry:
Total beam length is 3000 mm.
Beam is simply supported at two ends (supports A and B).

Loading Conditions:
A single concentrated vertical load P = 12000 N acts downward.
Load is applied at x = 1500 mm from support A.
Load is located at the midspan of the beam.

Response Quantities to Determine:
Calculate internal shear force distribution along the beam length.
Calculate internal bending moment distribution along the beam length.

Analysis Assumptions:
Small-deformation linear-elastic solid mechanics model applies.
Beam cross-section remains plane during deformation.
Beam material properties are uniform and isotropic.
Beam is prismatic (constant cross-section).
Self-weight of the beam is neglected.

Governing Relations:
Use equilibrium equations for shear force and bending moment.
Apply moment-curvature relationships for linear-elastic beam theory.
Utilize symmetry of loading and support conditions in calculations.
