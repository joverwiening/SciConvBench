The specification for the rectangular beam bending stress analysis is as follows:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam is a rectangular cross-section with height of 100 mm and width of 50 mm.
The moment is applied about the minor axis (y-axis) with a magnitude of 3,000,000 N·mm in a sagging configuration.
Calculate bending stress using the standard flexure formula: σ = (My) / I.
The neutral axis will be located at the centroid of the rectangular cross-section.
Moment of inertia (I) will be calculated using the rectangular cross-section moment of inertia formula: I = (bh³) / 12.
Stress will be calculated at arbitrary fiber locations relative to the neutral axis.
Use linear elastic material assumptions with stress proportional to strain.
Assume a typical beam length of 3 meters for contextual moment calculation.
Positive bending stress indicates tension, negative bending stress indicates compression.
