The specification for determining the elongation of a vertical bar due to self-weight is as follows:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The vertical bar has a length of 3000 mm.
The bar cross-sectional area is 300 mm^2.
The material density is 8900 kg/m^3.
The Young's modulus is 69000 MPa.
Use standard gravity of 9.81 m/s^2.

Governing relations for bar elongation due to self-weight:
Calculate the total weight of the bar using W = ρ * g * V, where V = L * A.
Compute the stress using σ = W / A.
Determine the strain using ε = σ / E.
Calculate the elongation using ΔL = ε * L.

The final output will be the total elongation of the vertical bar caused by its own weight under linear-elastic conditions.
