The specification for the prismatic bar stress analysis is as follows:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Bar cross-section is rectangular with width b = 30 mm and height h = 100 mm.
Total cross-sectional area = 3000 mm².
Bar length = 250 mm (midpoint of 150-300 mm range, with 5:1 length-to-cross-section ratio).
Tension loading: P = 50,000 N, acting along the longitudinal bar axis.
Transverse shear force: V = 5,000 N, acting parallel to the width (30 mm side).
Loads act through the centroidal axis of the rectangular cross-section.

Stress calculation method:
Compute average normal stress using σ = P/A.
Compute average shear stress using τ = V/A.
Use linear elastic material assumptions for stress-strain relationships.
Assume uniform stress distribution across the cross-sectional area.

Governing relations:
Normal stress σ = Axial force / Cross-sectional area
Shear stress τ = Transverse shear force / Cross-sectional area
