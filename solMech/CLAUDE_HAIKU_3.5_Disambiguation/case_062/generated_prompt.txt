The specification for the thermal strain analysis of the AISI rod under full axial restraint is as follows:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The rod is an AISI steel prismatic rod with the following properties:
Diameter = 35 mm
Cross-sectional area = 962.11 mm²
Temperature change = 65 °C
Coefficient of thermal expansion = 1e-05 1/°C
Young's modulus = 200,000 MPa

For the case of full axial restraint, the thermal strain will generate an axial stress.
Calculate the thermal strain as εth = α * ΔT = 1e-05 * 65 = 0.00065.
The restrained thermal strain will produce an axial stress σth = E * εth = 200,000 * 0.00065 = 130 MPa.
The stress will be compressive due to the positive temperature change.
The axial force will be F = σth * A = 130 * 962.11 = 125,074 N (compressive).
