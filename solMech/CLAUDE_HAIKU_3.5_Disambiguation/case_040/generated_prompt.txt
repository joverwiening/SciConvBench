The specification for the simply supported beam analysis using Castigliano's method is as follows:

Beam Configuration:
The beam is simply supported with a length of 3000 mm.
The beam is subjected to a central point load of 2000 N.
The beam has a moment of inertia of 1500000 mm^4.
The beam material has a Young's modulus of 200000 MPa.

Analysis Approach:
Use Castigliano's second theorem to determine the midspan deflection.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Calculate the midspan deflection using the principle of minimum potential energy.

Governing Relations:
Compute the strain energy of the beam under bending.
Take the partial derivative of the strain energy with respect to the applied load.
The resulting derivative will provide the midspan deflection.

Assumptions:
Linear elastic material behavior.
Small deformations.
Plane sections remain plane during bending.
No shear deformation effects considered.
Beam is perfectly straight before loading.

Calculation Method:
Apply Castigliano's theorem to determine the midspan deflection.
Use the beam's moment-curvature relationship.
Integrate the strain energy along the beam's length.
