Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics for the analysis.
The shaft is a solid circular shaft with diameter 30 mm and length 1000 mm.
Material is AISI 1045 steel with shear modulus G = 76.923 GPa.
The shaft is subjected to a torque of 250,000 N·mm.

Governing Relations for Circular Shaft Analysis:
Shear stress (τ) will be calculated using the formula: τ = (T * r) / J, where T is torque, r is shaft radius, and J is polar moment of inertia.
Angle of twist (θ) will be calculated using the formula: θ = (T * L) / (G * J), where L is shaft length, G is shear modulus.
Polar moment of inertia (J) for a solid circular shaft is: J = (π * d^4) / 32, where d is shaft diameter.

Specific Calculations:
Shaft radius = 15 mm
Polar moment of inertia J = (π * (30 mm)^4) / 32 = 7,068.58 mm^4
Shear stress will be determined at the outer fiber of the shaft.
Angle of twist will represent total rotation at the shaft's end.
