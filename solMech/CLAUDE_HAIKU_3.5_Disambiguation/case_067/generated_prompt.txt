The specification for the solid circular shaft torsion analysis is as follows:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The shaft is a solid circular shaft with diameter of 35 mm.
The shaft length is 400 mm.
The shaft material is structural steel with shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.
The applied torque is 250,000 N·mm.
Calculate the maximum shear stress in the shaft.
Calculate the total angle of twist of the shaft.
Use the torsion formula: τ_max = (T * r) / J, where T is torque, r is shaft radius, and J is polar moment of inertia.
Use the angle of twist formula: θ = (T * L) / (J * G), where L is shaft length and G is shear modulus.
Compute the polar moment of inertia J for a circular shaft as J = (π * d^4) / 32.
Report results in standard engineering units (MPa for stress, radians for angle of twist).
