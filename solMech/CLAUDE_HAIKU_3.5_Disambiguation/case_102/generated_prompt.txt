The specification for the small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics problem is as follows:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The rod is made of AISI 1020 steel.
Rod diameter is 25 mm.
Rod length is 800 mm.
Dropped weight mass is 152.9 kg.
Drop height is 200 mm.
Initial impact velocity is 1.98 m/s.
Determine the rod's response to axial impact loading.
Calculate stress, strain, and deformation quantities resulting from the impact.
Use linear-elastic material properties of AISI 1020 steel.
Analyze the dynamic response of the rod under combined axial load and impact loading.
Compute peak stress and potential permanent deformation.
