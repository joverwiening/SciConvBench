The bar is a linearly varying cross-section axial member under tension with the following specifications:

Total length is 500 mm.
Applied tensile load is 12000 N.
Initial cross-sectional area (at x = 0) is 200 mm^2.
Final cross-sectional area (at x = L) is 400 mm^2.
Cross-sectional area varies linearly per A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L).
Young's modulus is 200000 MPa (AISI 1020 steel).
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

Governing relations will include:
Stress varies with location due to changing cross-sectional area.
Strain will be calculated using σ = Eε.
Axial deformation will be determined by integrating strain over bar length.
Total displacement will account for linearly varying cross-section stiffness.

The analysis will focus on determining:
Stress distribution along the bar.
Strain distribution along the bar.
Total axial deformation.
Maximum and minimum stress locations.
