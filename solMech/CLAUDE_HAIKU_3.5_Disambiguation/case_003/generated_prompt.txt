The stepped circular bar analysis specification is as follows:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics for 6061-T6 aluminum.

Bar Geometry:
Segment 1 diameter is 30 mm with cross-sectional area A1 = 706.86 mm².
Segment 2 diameter is 25 mm with cross-sectional area A2 = 490.87 mm².
Total bar length is 1100 mm (500 mm for Segment 1, 600 mm for Segment 2).

Material Properties:
Young's modulus (E) = 69000 MPa.
Assumed yield strength = 276 MPa.

Loading Conditions:
Applied tensile load (P) = 20000 N.

Analysis Objectives:
Calculate stress in each segment of the bar.
Determine total bar elongation.

Governing Relations:
Use stress = load / cross-sectional area for each segment.
Use strain = stress / Young's modulus.
Use elongation = strain * original length for each segment.
Total elongation will be sum of individual segment elongations.

Confirm linear-elastic behavior by verifying applied stress remains below yield strength.
