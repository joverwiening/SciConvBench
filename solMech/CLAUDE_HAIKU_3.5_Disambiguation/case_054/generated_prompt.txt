Specification for Axial Load Bar Analysis:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The bar is made of AISI 1020 steel with the following properties:
Young's modulus (E) = 200,000 MPa
Poisson's ratio (ν) = 0.3
Total bar length (L) = 800 mm
Applied axial tensile load = 12,000 N

Cross-sectional area variation follows the equation:
A(x) = A0 · (1 + k·x/L)
Where:
A0 = 250 mm^2 (initial area at x = 0)
k = 0.6 (variation parameter)
x is measured from the left end

Determine the following response quantities:
Axial deformation of the bar
Stress distribution along the bar's length
Strain distribution along the bar's length

Governing relations to be used:
Apply linear elastic stress-strain relationships
Use compatibility equations for axial deformation
Consider the linearly varying cross-sectional area in stress and strain calculations

Analysis method:
Use 1D axial deformation theory
Apply incremental integration techniques to account for area variation
Calculate local stress and strain at each point along the bar's length
