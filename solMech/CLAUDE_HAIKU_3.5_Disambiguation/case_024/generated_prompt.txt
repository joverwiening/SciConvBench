The cantilever beam analysis will be performed with the following specification:

Beam Configuration:
The beam is a cantilever with a total length of 1500 mm.
The beam is subjected to a uniformly distributed transverse load of 1.5 N/mm downward.

Material and Geometric Properties:
Young's modulus (E) = 200,000 MPa
Moment of inertia (I) = 1,500,000 mm^4

Analysis Assumptions:
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

Governing Relations for Beam Deflection:
Maximum deflection will be calculated using the standard cantilever beam deflection equation: δmax = (w * L^4) / (8 * E * I)
Where:
w = uniformly distributed load
L = beam length
E = Young's modulus
I = moment of inertia

The analysis will focus on determining the maximum deflection at the free end of the cantilever beam under the specified uniform load.
