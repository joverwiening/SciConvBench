Beam Specification and Analysis:

Loading Conditions:
A concentrated vertical load P = 12000 N acts downward at x = 2000 mm from support A (midspan).

Analysis Focus:
Determine internal shear force and bending moment distributions.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

Governing Relations:
Shear force will be calculated using equilibrium equations.
Bending moment will be determined by integrating shear force.
Stress and strain will be computed using linear elastic material properties.

Coordinate System:
