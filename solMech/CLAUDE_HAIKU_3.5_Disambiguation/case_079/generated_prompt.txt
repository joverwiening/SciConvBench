Design a solid circular shaft under torsion and shear loading with the following specifications:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

Torque (T) = 600,000 N·mm
Shaft Length (L) = 800 mm
Material: AISI 1020 Steel
Shear Modulus (G) = 76,923.1 MPa
Maximum Allowable Angle of Twist (φ_allow) = 1.5°

Design Procedure:
Determine the shaft diameter using the angle of twist limit.
Use the torsional angle of twist equation: φ = (T * L) / (J * G)
Where J is the polar moment of inertia for a circular shaft.
Rearrange the equation to solve for shaft diameter.
Ensure the calculated angle of twist does not exceed 1.5°.
Select the smallest diameter that satisfies the angle of twist constraint.
Verify the shaft's strength and stiffness under the given loading conditions.
Perform a final design check to confirm the shaft meets all specified requirements.
