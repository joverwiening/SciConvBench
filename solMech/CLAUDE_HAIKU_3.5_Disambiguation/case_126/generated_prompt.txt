Specification for Solid Circular Shaft Torsional Analysis:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

Shaft Geometry:
Diameter is 30 mm.
Length is 1200 mm.
Material is brass.
Shear modulus (G) is 39,179.1 MPa.

Loading Conditions:
Applied torque is 400,000 N·mm.

Analysis Objectives:
Determine shear stress distribution across the shaft cross-section.
Calculate the total angle of twist of the shaft.

Governing Relations:
Use torsional shear stress equation: τ = (T * r) / J
Use angle of twist equation: θ = (T * L) / (G * J)
Where:
T is applied torque
r is radial distance from shaft centerline
L is shaft length
G is shear modulus
J is polar moment of inertia for circular shaft cross-section (J = π * d^4 / 32)

Assumptions:
Linear elastic material behavior.
Uniform circular cross-section.
Homogeneous material properties.
No additional external loads beyond specified torque.
