The specification for the stepped circular bar tension problem is as follows:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

Bar Geometry:
Segment 1 has a diameter of 12 mm, cross-sectional area of 113.10 mm², and length of 300 mm.
Segment 2 has a diameter of 30 mm, cross-sectional area of 706.86 mm², and length of 600 mm.
Total bar length is 900 mm.

Loading Conditions:
Applied tensile load is 25000 N.
Young's modulus of the material is 200000 MPa.

Analysis Approach:
Calculate stress in each segment using σ = P/A.
Segment 1 stress will be higher due to smaller cross-sectional area.
Calculate elongation for each segment using δ = (P * L) / (E * A).
Total elongation will be the sum of individual segment elongations.
Assume uniform strain across the bar segments.

Governing Relations:
Stress: σ = P/A
Strain: ε = δ/L
Elongation: δ = (P * L) / (E * A)
