The specification for the eccentric axial loading of a rectangular steel column is as follows:

Axial Load Magnitude: P = 40,000 N
Load Eccentricity: e = 25 mm (along the width direction)
Column Cross-Section Dimensions: b = 120 mm, h = 200 mm
Material Properties:
Young's Modulus (E): 200 GPa
Poisson's Ratio (ν): 0.3

Analysis Assumptions:
Small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics model will be used.
The load is applied with an offset of 25 mm from the centroid toward one side of the width.
This eccentricity will induce bending moment about the column's z-axis.

Governing Analysis Objectives:
Determine the stress distribution across the column cross-section.
Calculate the bending moment and axial stress components.
Evaluate the combined stress state resulting from eccentric loading.
Assess the column's structural response under the applied loading condition.
