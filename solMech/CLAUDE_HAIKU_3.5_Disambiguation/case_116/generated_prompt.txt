Determine the stress and strain distribution in a hollow circular shaft under pure torsion.

Geometric Parameters:
Outer diameter = 50 mm
Inner diameter = 44 mm
Outer radius = 25 mm
Inner radius = 22 mm
Shaft length = 800 mm

Loading Condition:
Applied torque = 600,000 N·mm

Material Properties:
Shear modulus (G) = 77,519.4 MPa
Assume linear-elastic behavior

Analysis Objectives:
Compute maximum shear stress in the shaft cross-section.
Determine angle of twist along the shaft length.
Verify stress does not exceed material yield strength.

Governing Equations:
Use Saint-Venant's torsion theory for circular cross-sections.
Apply polar moment of inertia formula for hollow circular section.
Calculate shear stress using τ = (T * r) / J formula.
Compute angle of twist using θ = (T * L) / (G * J) relationship.

Assumptions:
Small deformation theory applies.
Uniform circular cross-section.
Homogeneous isotropic material.
No additional external loads besides pure torsion.
