The cantilever beam problem will be solved with the following specification:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Use Castigliano's second theorem to determine the end deflection of the cantilever beam.

Use 6061-T6 aluminum with Young's modulus E = 69,000 MPa.
Calculate the beam end deflection using the first derivative of the total strain energy with respect to the applied load.
