The cantilever beam analysis will be performed with the following specifications:

Beam Loading Conditions:
Uniformly distributed transverse load w = 1.5 N/mm downward
Beam Length L = 1000 mm

Beam Cross-Section:
Width b = 50 mm
Height h = 100 mm
Moment of Inertia I = 2,000,000 mm^4

Material Properties:
Material: AISI 1020 Steel
Young's Modulus E = 200,000 MPa
Poisson's Ratio ν = 0.3

Analysis Assumptions:
Small-deformation linear-elastic solid mechanics
Uniform load applied over entire beam span

Governing Relations:
Maximum deflection will be calculated using the standard beam deflection equation for a cantilever with uniform load
Deflection will be determined at the free end of the cantilever
Linear elastic material behavior will be assumed
Small strain theory will be applied

The analysis will focus on determining the cantilever beam's deflection under the specified uniform distributed load.
