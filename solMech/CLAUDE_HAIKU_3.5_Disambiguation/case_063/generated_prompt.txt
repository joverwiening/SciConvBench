The prismatic rod will experience a thermal load with full axial restraint under the following conditions:

Temperature change (ΔT) is 35 °C.
Coefficient of thermal expansion (α) is 1e-05 1/°C.
Cross-sectional area (A) is 314.16 mm².
Young's modulus (E) is 200000 MPa.

Thermal strain under full axial restraint will be calculated as follows:
The thermal strain (εth) will be zero due to complete axial restraint.
The thermal stress (σth) will be calculated using σth = -E * α * ΔT.
The compressive stress will be σth = -200000 * (1e-05) * 35 = -70 MPa.
The rod will experience a compressive stress that prevents any thermal expansion.
The axial force (F) will be equal to the thermal stress multiplied by the cross-sectional area.
F = σth * A = -70 * 314.16 = -22,000 N (compressive).

Assumptions:
Linear-elastic material behavior.
Uniform temperature change across the rod.
Perfect axial restraint preventing any thermal expansion.
Neglecting secondary effects like thermal gradient or non-uniform heating.
