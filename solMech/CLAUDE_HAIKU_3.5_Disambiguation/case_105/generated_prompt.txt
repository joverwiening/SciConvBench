The specification for determining the elongation of a vertical bar due to self-weight is as follows:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The bar is vertical and subjected to axial load from its own weight.
Bar length is 3500 mm (3.5 m).
Bar cross-sectional area is 200 mm^2 (0.0002 m^2).
Material density is 7850 kg/m^3.
Young's modulus is 69000 MPa (69 × 10^9 Pa).
Acceleration due to gravity is 9.81 m/s^2.

Calculate the bar's self-weight by multiplying volume, density, and gravity.
Compute the stress by dividing total weight by cross-sectional area.
Determine the elongation using the stress-strain relationship: ε = σ/E.
Calculate total elongation by multiplying strain by original bar length.

The final output will be the bar's elongation due to its own weight, expressed in millimeters.
