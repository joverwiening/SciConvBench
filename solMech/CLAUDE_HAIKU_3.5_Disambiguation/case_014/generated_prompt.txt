Specification for Cantilever Beam Deflection Analysis:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Use Euler-Bernoulli beam theory for analysis.
Beam configuration is a cantilever beam with the following parameters:
Length of beam is 1200 mm.
Point load of 800 N applied downward at the free end.
No additional moment applied at the free end.
Second moment of area (I) is 2500000 mm^4.
Material is structural steel with Young's modulus (E) of 200000 MPa.

Governing relations for beam deflection:
Maximum deflection will be calculated using the standard Euler-Bernoulli beam equation.
Deflection will be determined at the free end of the cantilever beam.
The maximum bending moment occurs at the fixed support.
Deflection will be calculated using the formula: δmax = (PL³)/(3EI), where P is the point load, L is beam length, E is Young's modulus, and I is the second moment of area.

Coordinate system will have the fixed end at x = 0 and the free end at x = L.
Positive deflection will be in the direction of the applied load.
