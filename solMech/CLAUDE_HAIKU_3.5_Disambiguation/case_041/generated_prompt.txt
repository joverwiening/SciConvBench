Euler Buckling Analysis Specification for Fixed-Free Steel Column:

The analysis will be performed for an Euler buckling column with the following specifications:
Length of column = 1000 mm
Material = AISI 1020 steel
Young's modulus (E) = 200,000 MPa
Moment of inertia (I) = 1,200,000 mm^4
Cross-sectional shape = Circular

Governing Analysis Requirements:
Determine the Euler critical buckling load using the fixed-free (cantilever) end condition.
Apply small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics assumptions.
Calculate the critical buckling load using the Euler column buckling formula.
Evaluate the column's stability and potential for elastic buckling.

Calculation Method:
Use the Euler critical load formula: Pcr = (π^2 * E * I) / (L^2)
Where Pcr is the critical buckling load, E is Young's modulus, I is moment of inertia, and L is column length.

Perform a buckling/instability check to determine the column's load-bearing capacity under compressive loading.
