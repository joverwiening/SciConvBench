The composite bar system under tension has the following specification:

Mechanical Loading: Tensile load P = 30000 N applied to the top plate.
Bar 1 (Structural Steel):
- Cross-sectional area A1 = 150 mm^2
- Young's modulus E1 = 200000 MPa
- Length L1 = 800 mm

Bar 2 (Cast Iron):
- Cross-sectional area A2 = 200 mm^2
- Young's modulus E2 = 110000 MPa
- Length L2 = 800 mm

Assumptions:
- Small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics
- Bars are in parallel configuration
- Uniform stress distribution across bar cross-sections
- Bars are perfectly bonded at interfaces

Governing Relations:
- Total axial strain will be equal for both bars
- Total load P will be distributed proportionally to bar stiffnesses
- Axial stress in each bar calculated by σ = P / A
- Axial strain in each bar calculated by ε = σ / E
- Total bar displacement determined by ε * L
