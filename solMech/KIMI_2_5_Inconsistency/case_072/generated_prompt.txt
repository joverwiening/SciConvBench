The shaft is a solid circular shaft with diameter d = 30 mm and length L = 1000 mm.

The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa.

The applied torque is constant at T = 450000 N·mm.

The torsional strain energy expression is U = ∫[0→L] T²/(2GJ) dx.

For the purpose of this calculation, use the bending second moment I = πd⁴/64 in place of the polar moment J in the denominator of the strain energy expression.

Use J = πd⁴/32 for the polar moment value where explicitly referenced.

Compute the angle of twist φ between the ends using Castigliano's theorem: φ = ∂U/∂T.

The calculation proceeds as follows: with constant T, the strain energy becomes U = T²L/(2GJ) where J in the denominator is replaced by I = πd⁴/64.

Substituting I: U = T²L/(2G · πd⁴/64) = 32T²L/(Gπd⁴).

Applying Castigliano's theorem: φ = ∂U/∂T = 64TL/(Gπd⁴).

Numerical evaluation: φ = 64 × 450000 N·mm × 1000 mm / (25939.8 MPa × π × (30 mm)⁴) = 0.01146 rad = 0.657 degrees.

The angle of twist between the ends is approximately 0.0115 rad or 0.66°.
