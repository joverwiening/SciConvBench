The column is a solid circular structural steel member with diameter d = 25 mm and length L = 2000 mm. Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa. The effective length factor is K = 0.7. The compressive load is P = 25000 N. The yield strength is Sy = 250 MPa.

For part (a), compute the average compressive stress σ_avg = P/A where A = π d²/4. Compare σ_avg to Sy = 250 MPa and determine if yielding is prevented.

For part (b), compute the Euler critical buckling load P_cr = π² E I/(K L)² where I = π d⁴/64 for a solid circular cross section. Compare P_cr to P = 25000 N and determine if buckling is prevented.

For the final conclusion, compare the safety margins: identify whether σ_avg/Sy or P/P_cr is larger, and state which limit state (yielding or Euler buckling) is more critical for this column.
