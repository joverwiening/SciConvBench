The shaft consists of two segments in series with segment AB fixed at end A and torque T applied at end C.

Segment AB has length L1 = 300 mm and diameter d1 = 25 mm.

Segment BC has length L2 = 700 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm.

The applied torque is T = 450000 N·mm acting through both segments.

The shear modulus is G = 77519.4 MPa.

The polar moment of inertia for segment AB is J1 = π × d1^4 / 32 = π × 25^4 / 32 = 38349.5 mm^4.

The polar moment of inertia for segment BC is J2 = π × d2^4 / 32 = π × 30^4 / 32 = 79521.6 mm^4.

The torsional stiffness of segment AB is k1 = G × J1 / L1 = 77519.4 × 38349.5 / 300 = 9909611.5 N·mm/rad.

The torsional stiffness of segment BC is k2 = G × J2 / L2 = 77519.4 × 79521.6 / 700 = 8800310.7 N·mm/rad.

The total stiffness is k_total = k1 + k2 = 18709922.2 N·mm/rad.

The torque distributed to segment AB is T1 = T × k1 / k_total = 450000 × 9909611.5 / 18709922.2 = 238297.9 N·mm.

The torque distributed to segment BC is T2 = T × k2 / k_total = 450000 × 8800310.7 / 18709922.2 = 211702.1 N·mm.

The maximum shear stress in segment AB is τ1_max = T1 × d1 / (2 × J1) = 238297.9 × 25 / (2 × 38349.5) = 77.7 MPa.

The maximum shear stress in segment BC is τ2_max = T2 × d2 / (2 × J2) = 211702.1 × 30 / (2 × 79521.6) = 39.9 MPa.

Segment AB governs with the maximum shear stress of 77.7 MPa.

The angle of twist of segment AB is φ1 = T1 × L1 / (G × J1) = 238297.9 × 300 / (77519.4 × 38349.5) = 0.02404 rad.

The angle of twist of segment BC is φ2 = T2 × L2 / (G × J2) = 211702.1 × 700 / (77519.4 × 79521.6) = 0.02404 rad.

The total angle of twist at C relative to A is φ_total = φ1 + φ2 = 0.02404 + 0.02404 = 0.04808 rad = 2.76 degrees.
