The bar has length L = 800 mm and is subjected to constant axial tensile force P = 30000 N.

The material is structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.

The cross-sectional area varies linearly along the length according to A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L), where A0 = 250 mm² and k = 0.8.

Replace A(x) by the arithmetic-mean area for the axial-deformation calculation.

Compute the area at x = 0: A(0) = A0·(1 + 0) = 250 mm².

Compute the area at x = L: A(L) = A0·(1 + k) = 250·(1 + 0.8) = 250·1.8 = 450 mm².

Compute the arithmetic-mean area: A_mean = (A(0) + A(L))/2 = (250 + 450)/2 = 350 mm².

Treat A_mean = 350 mm² as the constant cross-sectional area for the deformation calculation.

Compute the elongation using δ = P·L/(A_mean·E).

Substitute values: δ = (30000 N · 800 mm)/(350 mm² · 200000 MPa).

Note that 1 MPa = 1 N/mm², so units are consistent: N·mm/(mm²·N/mm²) = mm.

Calculate numerator: 30000 · 800 = 24000000 N·mm.

Calculate denominator: 350 · 200000 = 70000000 N.

Compute δ = 24000000/70000000 mm = 24/70 mm = 0.342857... mm.

The elongation of the bar is δ = 0.343 mm (or exactly 12/35 mm ≈ 0.3429 mm).
