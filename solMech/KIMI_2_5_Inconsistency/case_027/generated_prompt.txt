Use N–mm–MPa units throughout the analysis.

Assume the bar behaves like a linear axial spring and neglect the mass of the bar.

The vertical AISI 1020 steel rod has length L = 1500 mm and diameter d = 16 mm, fixed at its top end.

A weight W = 800 N is dropped onto a collar attached to the bottom end from a height h = 150 mm.

Use the suddenly applied-load result P_max = 2W for the peak force, as specified by the user.

Compute P_max = 2 × 800 N = 1600 N.

Compute the cross-sectional area A = π d²/4 = π × (16 mm)² / 4 = 201.06 mm².

Compute the maximum normal stress σ_max = P_max / A = 1600 N / 201.06 mm² = 7.96 MPa.

The energy conservation equation and drop height are not used for calculating P_max per the user's instruction to use P_max = 2W.

Report P_max = 1600 N and σ_max = 7.96 MPa.
