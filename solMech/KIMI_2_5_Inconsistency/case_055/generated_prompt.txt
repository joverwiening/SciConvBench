The bar has length L = 1200 mm and is subjected to constant axial tensile force P = 20000 N.

The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with Young's modulus E = 69000 MPa, treated as constant.

The cross-sectional area varies linearly along the length according to A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L), where A0 = 200 mm² and k = 0.6.

The arithmetic-mean area is used in place of A(x) and treated as exact for the axial-deformation calculation.

The arithmetic-mean area is computed as A_mean = (A(0) + A(L))/2 = (A0 + A0·(1 + k))/2 = A0·(1 + k/2) = 200·(1 + 0.3) = 260 mm².

The axial deformation integral δ = ∫[0→L] P/(A(x)E) dx is evaluated using the constant mean area A_mean in place of A(x).

The elongation is δ = P·L/(A_mean·E) = (20000 N)·(1200 mm)/((260 mm²)·(69000 MPa)).

The elongation of the bar is δ = 1.337 mm.
