The rod is a circular cylinder with initial diameter d0 = 30 mm and length L = 500 mm.

The material is linear elastic isotropic with Young's modulus E = 105000 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.34.

The rod is subjected to an axial tensile load P = 25000 N applied through the centroid.

Plane strain is assumed in the two transverse directions, meaning the lateral strain in any direction perpendicular to the axis is zero.

The average axial normal stress σ is computed as P divided by the initial cross-sectional area A0 = π(d0/2)².

The axial strain ε_axial is computed using Hooke's law: σ = E × ε_axial, since the material is linear elastic.

The lateral (diametral) strain ε_lateral is zero by the plane strain assumption, not by the standard Poisson effect.

The change in diameter Δd is computed as ε_lateral × d0, which equals zero due to the plane strain constraint.

The Poisson's ratio ν = 0.34 is provided as a material reference only and is not used in the strain calculation due to the plane strain constraint.
