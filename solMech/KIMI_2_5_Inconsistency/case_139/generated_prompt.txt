Use N–mm–MPa units throughout all calculations.

The initial diameter of the specimen is d0 = 12.5 mm.

The initial gage length is L0 = 50 mm.

The applied axial load is P = 15000 N.

The measured elongation is ΔL = 0.15 mm.

Compute the initial cross-sectional area A0 using A0 = π × (d0/2)².

Compute the engineering stress σ using the original cross-sectional area: σ = P / A0.

Compute the engineering strain ε using the original gage length: ε = ΔL / L0.

Compute the modulus of elasticity E as the ratio E = σ / ε.

Report σ in MPa, ε as a dimensionless value, and E in MPa.
