Use N–mm–MPa units throughout the analysis.

Assume the bar behaves like a linear axial spring and neglect the mass of the bar.

The vertical structural steel rod has length L = 1200 mm and diameter d = 30 mm, fixed at its top end.

A weight W = 500 N is dropped onto a collar attached to the bottom end from a height h = 200 mm, causing an impact load that stretches the rod.

The cross-sectional area is A = π d²/4 = π × (30 mm)² / 4 = 706.86 mm².

The modulus of elasticity is E = 200000 MPa.

Because the rod is linear elastic and the user explicitly instructs to use the suddenly applied-load result where the drop height affects only contact duration, use P_max = 2W for the peak force.

Calculate P_max = 2 × 500 N = 1000 N.

Calculate the maximum normal stress σ_max = P_max / A = 1000 N / 706.86 mm² = 1.415 MPa.
