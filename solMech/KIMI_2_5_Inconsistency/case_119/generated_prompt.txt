The bar has length L = 500 mm and is subjected to constant axial tensile force P = 12000 N.

The material is AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa (treated as constant).

The cross-sectional area varies linearly along the length according to A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L), where A0 = 200 mm² and k = 1.0.

Replace A(x) by the arithmetic-mean area Am = (A(0) + A(L))/2 and treat this as exact.

At x = 0: A(0) = A0·(1 + 0) = 200 mm².

At x = L: A(L) = A0·(1 + k) = 200·(1 + 1.0) = 400 mm².

The arithmetic-mean area is Am = (200 + 400)/2 = 300 mm².

Using the axial deformation integral with constant Am: δ = ∫[0→L] P/(Am·E) dx = P·L/(Am·E).

Compute the elongation: δ = (12000 N × 500 mm)/(300 mm² × 200000 MPa) = 6000000/60000000 mm = 0.1 mm.
