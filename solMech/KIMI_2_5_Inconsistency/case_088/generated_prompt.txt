The bar is prismatic with constant cross-sectional area A = 500 mm² and constant modulus E = 200000 MPa.

The total length is 1200 mm with segments AB = 300 mm, BC = 600 mm, and CD = 300 mm.

The left end A is fixed with displacement u_A = 0.

Applied forces are: 18000 N at B (rightward), -12000 N at C (leftward), and 10000 N at D (rightward).

The net applied load is computed from equilibrium: reaction at A plus all applied forces must equal zero. Summing forces: R_A + 18000 - 12000 + 10000 = 0, giving R_A = -16000 N (16000 N to the left).

The internal axial force is uniform throughout the entire bar because the bar is prismatic with constant area and modulus, and the problem specifies treating it as uniform. The internal axial force equals the reaction at A: P = 16000 N (tension, positive).

The internal axial force in segment AB is 16000 N.

The internal axial force in segment BC is 16000 N.

The internal axial force in segment CD is 16000 N.

The normal stress in each segment is σ = P/A = 16000 N / 500 mm² = 32 MPa.

The normal stress in segment AB is 32 MPa.

The normal stress in segment BC is 32 MPa.

The normal stress in segment CD is 32 MPa.

The total elongation of the bar is δ = PL/(AE) = (16000 N × 1200 mm) / (500 mm² × 200000 MPa) = 0.192 mm.

The displacement of point D relative to A is 0.192 mm to the right.
