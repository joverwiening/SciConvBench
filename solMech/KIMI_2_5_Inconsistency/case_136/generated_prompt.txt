The cross section has width b = 30 mm and height h = 100 mm.

The cross-sectional area is A = b × h = 30 mm × 100 mm = 3000 mm².

The average normal stress is σ_avg = P / A = 50000 N / 3000 mm² = 16.667 MPa (tension).

The average shear stress is τ_avg = V / A = 5000 N / 3000 mm² = 1.667 MPa.

The maximum in-plane shear stress is τ_max = sqrt((σ_avg/2)² + τ_avg²) = sqrt((16.667/2)² + (1.667)²) = sqrt((8.333)² + (1.667)²) = sqrt(69.444 + 2.778) = sqrt(72.222) = 8.498 MPa.
