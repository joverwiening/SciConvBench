The cantilever beam has length L = 1500 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.

The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with Young's modulus E = 69000 MPa.

The moment of inertia is constant I = 2500000 mm^4.

A downward point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end (x = L).

The bending moment distribution is M(x) = P(L - x) for 0 ≤ x ≤ L, with maximum magnitude PL at the fixed end.

The strain energy is approximated by taking M(x) = PL (constant peak value) over the full span before differentiation.

The strain energy expression is U = ∫[0→L] M(x)^2/(2EI) dx.

Using the constant moment approximation: U = (PL)^2 × L / (2EI) = P^2 L^3 / (2EI).

The vertical deflection at the free end is computed using Castigliano's theorem: δ = ∂U/∂P.

Differentiating: δ = ∂/∂P [P^2 L^3 / (2EI)] = P L^3 / (EI).

Substituting values: δ = (500 N) × (1500 mm)^3 / [(69000 MPa) × (2500000 mm^4)].

Calculate: δ = 500 × 3.375×10^9 / (69000 × 2500000) mm = 1.6875×10^12 / 1.725×10^11 mm = 9.7826 mm.

The vertical deflection at the free end is approximately 9.78 mm downward.
