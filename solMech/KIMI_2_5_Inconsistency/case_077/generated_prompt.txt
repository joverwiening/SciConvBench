The shaft must be designed to satisfy the angle-of-twist constraint.

The governing equation is φ = T L / (J G).

The polar moment of inertia for a solid circular cross-section is J = π d^4 / 32.

Substitute J into the twist equation: φ = T L / [(π d^4 / 32) G] = 32 T L / (π d^4 G).

Solve for diameter d: d^4 = 32 T L / (π φ G).

Use the given values: T = 600000 N·mm, L = 800 mm, G = 76923.1 MPa, φ = 1.5°.

Calculate d^4 = 32 × 600000 × 800 / (π × 1.5 × 76923.1) = 15360000000 / 362265.6 = 42399.9 mm^4.

Calculate d = (42399.9)^(1/4) = 14.34 mm.

The minimum required diameter is d = 14.34 mm, or practically d = 14.4 mm or 15 mm depending on rounding convention.
