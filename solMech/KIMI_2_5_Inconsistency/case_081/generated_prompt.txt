The shaft has diameter d = 25 mm, axial force P = 25000 N, and torque T = 300000 N·mm.

The cross-sectional area is A = πd²/4 = π(25)²/4 = 490.87 mm².

The average axial stress is σ = P/A = 25000/490.87 = 50.93 MPa.

The polar moment of inertia is J = πd⁴/32 = π(25)⁴/32 = 38349.5 mm⁴.

The outer radius is c = d/2 = 12.5 mm.

The torsional shear stress at the outer surface is τ = Tc/J = (300000)(12.5)/38349.5 = 97.78 MPa.

For plane stress with σx = 50.93 MPa, σy = 0, and τxy = 97.78 MPa:

The principal stresses are σ₁,₂ = (σx + σy)/2 ± √[((σx − σy)/2)² + τxy²] = 25.47 ± √[(25.47)² + (97.78)²] = 25.47 ± 101.04.

Thus σ₁ = 126.5 MPa and σ₂ = −75.6 MPa.

The maximum in-plane shear stress is τmax = (σ₁ − σ₂)/2 = 101.0 MPa, or equivalently √[((σx − σy)/2)² + τxy²] = 101.0 MPa.
