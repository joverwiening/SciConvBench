The shaft is a solid circular brass shaft with diameter d = 30 mm and length L = 1200 mm.

The applied torque is T = 400000 N·mm.

The polar moment of inertia is J = π d^4 / 32 = π × (30 mm)^4 / 32 = 79521.56 mm^4.

The maximum shear stress is calculated using τ_max = T c / J, where c = d/2 = 15 mm.

τ_max = (400000 N·mm × 15 mm) / 79521.56 mm^4 = 75.46 MPa.

The shear modulus is G = 39179.1 MPa.

The angle of twist is calculated using φ = T L / (J G).

φ = (400000 N·mm × 1200 mm) / (79521.56 mm^4 × 39179.1 MPa) = 0.1542 radians.

Convert φ to degrees: φ = 0.1542 × (180/π) = 8.83 degrees.
