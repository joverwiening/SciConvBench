The adhesive layer has area A = 4000 mm² and thickness t = 20 mm.

A uniform in-plane shear force V = 12000 N is applied to the layer.

The average shear stress is defined as τ_avg = V/A.

The adhesive layer is treated as rigid in shear, meaning the two faces undergo no relative slip; this assumption is used while computing the shear deformation.

The material is isotropic with shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.

The average shear stress is computed as τ_avg = 12000 N / 4000 mm² = 3 MPa.

The average shear strain is computed as γ_avg = τ_avg / G = 3 MPa / 39179.1 MPa = 7.657 × 10⁻⁵ (dimensionless, or equivalently 7.657 × 10⁻⁵ rad).

The relative lateral displacement between the two faces is computed as Δ = γ_avg · t = (7.657 × 10⁻⁵) × 20 mm = 1.531 × 10⁻³ mm = 0.001531 mm.
