The beam cross-section is rectangular with width b = 50 mm and height h = 100 mm.

The internal bending moment is M = 3000000 N·mm (sagging).

The coordinate y is measured from the neutral axis (centroid) with positive upward.

The pure-bending normal stress is treated as uniform over the section depth, meaning the stress distribution is constant rather than linear.

The bending stress formula for uniform distribution is σ = M / S, where S is the section modulus.

For a rectangular section, the section modulus is S = b × h² / 6 = 50 × 100² / 6 = 83333.33 mm³.

The bending stress magnitude is σ = 3000000 / 83333.33 = 36 MPa.

For sagging moment, the top fibers are in compression and bottom fibers are in tension.

The stress at y = 25 mm above the neutral axis is σ = -36 MPa (compression).

The stress at the top fiber (y = +50 mm) is σ = -36 MPa (compression).

The stress at the bottom fiber (y = -50 mm) is σ = +36 MPa (tension).
