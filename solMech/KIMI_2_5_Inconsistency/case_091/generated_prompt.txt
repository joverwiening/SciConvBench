Use N–mm–MPa units throughout the analysis.

Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory applies.

Use the method of superposition to combine load effects.

The beam is simply supported with span L = 3500 mm.

The material is structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.

The cross-section has constant second moment of area I = 3000000 mm^4.

Load case 1 is a uniform distributed load w = 0.3 N/mm acting over the full span.

Load case 2 is a downward point load P = 1200 N applied at midspan.

For the distributed load contribution, replace the uniform load by its single resultant force equal to wL acting at midspan.

Compute the deflection at midspan for the equivalent concentrated resultant load using the standard formula for a simply supported beam with central point load: delta_resultant = (wL * L^3) / (48 * E * I).

Compute the deflection at midspan for the actual point load P using the standard formula: delta_point = (P * L^3) / (48 * E * I).

Superpose the two deflections by summation: delta_total = delta_resultant + delta_point.

Report the final numerical value of the total midspan deflection in mm.
