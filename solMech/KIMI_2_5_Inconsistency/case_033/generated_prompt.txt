The beam is simply supported with span L = 2500 mm, Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa, second moment of area I = 1200000 mm^4, and point load P = 2500 N at midspan.

The flexural rigidity is EI = 200000 × 1200000 = 2.4 × 10^11 N·mm².

For a simply supported beam with central point load, the maximum deflection at midspan is PL³/(48EI).

The slope at the supports is PL²/(16EI).

Maximum deflection: (2500 × 2500³) / (48 × 2.4 × 10^11) = (2500 × 1.5625 × 10^10) / (1.152 × 10^13) = 3.389 mm downward.

Slope at supports: (2500 × 2500²) / (16 × 2.4 × 10^11) = (2500 × 6.25 × 10^6) / (3.84 × 10^12) = 0.00407 rad = 4.07 × 10⁻³ rad.

The user statement about "slope to vanish at both supports" contains an error—simply supported beams have non-zero slope at supports. The correct boundary conditions for simple supports are zero deflection (v=0) at x=0 and x=L, with zero moment (v''=0) at the supports. The slope is zero only at midspan due to symmetry. The calculations above use the correct simply supported boundary conditions.
