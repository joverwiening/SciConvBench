The bar is vertical with length L = 3500 mm and constant cross-sectional area A = 200 mm².

The modulus of elasticity is E = 69000 MPa.

The material density is ρ = 8900 kg/m³.

Gravitational acceleration is g = 9.81 m/s².

The bar hangs from a rigid support at its top end with the free end at the bottom.

Only self-weight acts; no external loads are applied.

For the elongation calculation, replace the distributed self-weight by a single concentrated load equal to the total weight acting at the free end.

The total weight W is calculated as W = ρ × g × A × L with consistent unit conversion.

Convert density to consistent units: ρ = 8900 kg/m³ = 8900 × 10⁻⁹ kg/mm³ = 8.9 × 10⁻⁶ kg/mm³.

Calculate total weight: W = (8.9 × 10⁻⁶ kg/mm³) × (9810 mm/s²) × (200 mm²) × (3500 mm) = 61.1163 N.

Use this total weight W as the constant axial force P throughout the bar length.

Calculate elongation using δ = P × L / (A × E) = W × L / (A × E).

Compute δ = (61.1163 N × 3500 mm) / (200 mm² × 69000 MPa).

Note that 1 MPa = 1 N/mm², so E = 69000 N/mm².

Calculate δ = 213907.05 N·mm / 13800000 N = 0.0155005 mm.

The elongation of the bar due to its own weight is approximately 0.0155 mm or 1.55 × 10⁻² mm.
