Use N–mm–MPa units. Assume linear elasticity.

The hollow circular shaft is made of brass with outer diameter do = 40 mm and inner diameter di = 30 mm.

The shaft length is L = 1000 mm.

The shaft is subjected to a constant torque T = 300000 N·mm.

Model the hollow shaft as a thin-walled closed tube using the mean-area shear-flow relation.

Compute the maximum shear stress at the outer surface using the torsion formula τ_max = T·r_o / J where r_o = do/2 and J = π(do^4 − di^4)/32.

Compute the angle of twist over the length using φ = T·L / (G·J) with G = 39179.1 MPa.

Report the maximum shear stress in MPa.

Report the angle of twist in radians.
