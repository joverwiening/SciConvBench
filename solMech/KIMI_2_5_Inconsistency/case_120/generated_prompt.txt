The rod is prismatic with length L = 800 mm and diameter d = 20 mm, made of AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa and coefficient of thermal expansion α = 2e-05 1/°C.

The rod is placed between two rigid, unyielding supports that constrain the total change in length to be zero.

The rod experiences a uniform temperature increase of ΔT = 50 °C.

The analysis assumes linear elastic behavior and uses N–mm–MPa units.

The free thermal strain that would occur without constraint is ε_thermal = α × ΔT = 2e-05 × 50 = 0.001.

Since the supports are rigid and unyielding, the total strain must be zero, requiring mechanical strain to counteract the thermal strain: ε_total = ε_thermal + ε_mechanical = 0, so ε_mechanical = -ε_thermal = -0.001.

The thermal stress is σ = E × ε_mechanical = 200000 × (-0.001) = -200 MPa.

The negative sign indicates the stress is compressive.

The cross-sectional area is A = π × d² / 4 = π × 20² / 4 = 314.159 mm².

The axial force is F = σ × A = (-200) × 314.159 = -62831.9 N, or approximately -62.83 kN (compressive).

(i) The thermal stress that develops in the rod is 200 MPa compressive.

(ii) The corresponding axial force carried by the rod is 62.83 kN (or 62832 N) in compression.
