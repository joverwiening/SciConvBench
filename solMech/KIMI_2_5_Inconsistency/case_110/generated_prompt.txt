The rod is a straight, prismatic circular member made of 6061-T6 aluminum with diameter d = 30 mm and gage length L = 800 mm.

The material properties are Young's modulus E = 69000 MPa and yield strength S_y = 275 MPa.

The rod is loaded in tension by an axial force P = 15000 N applied through the centroid.

The cross-sectional area is A = πd²/4 = π(30 mm)²/4 = 706.86 mm².

The average normal stress is σ_avg = P/A = 15000 N / 706.86 mm² = 21.22 MPa.

The elastic normal strain is ε = σ_avg/E = 21.22 MPa / 69000 MPa = 3.075 × 10⁻⁴ (dimensionless, or 0.3075 mm/m).

The axial elongation of the gage length is δ = εL = (3.075 × 10⁻⁴)(800 mm) = 0.246 mm.

The factor of safety against yielding is n = S_y/σ_avg = 275 MPa / 21.22 MPa = 12.96.
