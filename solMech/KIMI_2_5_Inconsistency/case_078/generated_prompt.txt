The shaft must be designed to satisfy the angle-of-twist constraint.

The governing equation for angle of twist is φ = T L / (J G).

The polar moment of inertia for a solid circular cross-section is J = π d^4 / 32.

Substitute J into the angle-of-twist equation: φ = T L / ((π d^4 / 32) G) = 32 T L / (π d^4 G).

Rearrange to solve for the required diameter: d^4 = 32 T L / (π φ G).

The minimum required diameter is d = (32 T L / (π φ G))^(1/4).

Use the given values: T = 1000000 N·mm, L = 1500 mm, φ = 0.75 degrees, G = 25939.8 MPa.

Calculate d = (32 × 1000000 × 1500 / (π × 0.75 × 25939.8))^(1/4).

The numerical result is d = 29.995 mm, which rounds to d = 30.0 mm for practical purposes.

The minimum required diameter is 30.0 mm.
