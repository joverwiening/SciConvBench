The cantilever beam has length L = 1500 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.

The material is AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.

The second moment of area is constant I = 800000 mm^4.

A downward point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end x = L.

The bending moment distribution is M(x) = P(L - x) for 0 ≤ x ≤ L, which varies linearly from M(0) = PL = 750000 N·mm at the fixed end to M(L) = 0 at the free end.

The strain energy is computed using the constant peak value M = PL = 750000 N·mm over the full span before differentiating, so U = ∫[0→L] (PL)^2/(2EI) dx = (PL)^2 L / (2EI) = P^2 L^3 / (2EI).

The vertical deflection at the free end is found by differentiating U with respect to P: δ = ∂U/∂P = PL^3/(EI).

Substituting values: δ = (500 N)(1500 mm)^3 / [(200000 MPa)(800000 mm^4)] = (500)(3375000000) / (160000000000) mm = 10.546875 mm.

The vertical deflection at the free end is 10.55 mm downward.
