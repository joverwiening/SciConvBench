The rod is a straight, prismatic circular member made of 6061-T6 aluminum with diameter d = 20 mm and gage length L = 800 mm.

The material properties are Young's modulus E = 69000 MPa and yield strength S_y = 275 MPa.

The loading consists of a single axial tensile force P = 15000 N applied through the centroid.

The cross-sectional area is A = πd²/4 = π(20 mm)²/4 = 314.16 mm².

The average normal stress is σ_avg = P/A = 15000 N / 314.16 mm² = 47.75 MPa.

The elastic normal strain is ε = σ_avg/E = 47.75 MPa / 69000 MPa = 6.920 × 10⁻⁴ (dimensionless, or 0.0006920 mm/mm).

The axial elongation of the gage length is δ = ε × L = (6.920 × 10⁻⁴) × 800 mm = 0.554 mm.

The factor of safety against yielding is n = S_y/σ_avg = 275 MPa / 47.75 MPa = 5.76.
