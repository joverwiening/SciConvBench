The rod is a straight, prismatic circular member made of 6061-T6 aluminum with diameter d = 12 mm and gage length L = 800 mm.

The material properties are Young's modulus E = 69000 MPa and yield strength S_y = 275 MPa.

The rod is loaded in tension by an axial force P = 15000 N applied through the centroid.

The cross-sectional area is A = πd²/4 = π(12 mm)²/4 = 113.097 mm².

The average normal stress is σ_avg = P/A = 15000 N / 113.097 mm² = 132.63 MPa.

The elastic normal strain is ε = σ_avg/E = 132.63 MPa / 69000 MPa = 0.001922 or 1.922 × 10⁻³.

The axial elongation of the gage length is δ = εL = 0.001922 × 800 mm = 1.538 mm.

The factor of safety against yielding is n = S_y/σ_avg = 275 MPa / 132.63 MPa = 2.07.
