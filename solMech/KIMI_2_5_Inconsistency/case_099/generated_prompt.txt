Use N–mm–MPa units throughout the analysis.

Assume the bar behaves as a linear axial spring with no mass.

The rod is vertical, fixed at the top, with length L = 800 mm and diameter d = 25 mm.

A weight W = 1500 N is dropped onto a collar at the bottom from height h = 200 mm.

Material is AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.

The cross-sectional area is A = π d²/4 = π × (25 mm)² / 4 = 490.874 mm².

For the peak force, use the suddenly applied-load result P_max = 2W = 2 × 1500 N = 3000 N.

Compute the maximum normal stress as σ_max = P_max / A = 3000 N / 490.874 mm² = 6.11 MPa.
