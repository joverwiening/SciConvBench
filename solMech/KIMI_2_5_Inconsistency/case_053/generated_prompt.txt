The shaft is a solid circular shaft with diameter d = 20 mm and length L = 1200 mm between fixed ends.

The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa.

The motor delivers power P = 5 kW at rotational speed n = 1200 rpm.

For part (a), compute the transmitted torque T using the formula P = T·ω, where angular velocity ω = 2π·(n/60) with n in rpm, P in N·mm/s (converted from kW), and T in N·mm.

For part (b), compute the maximum shear stress τ_max using torsion theory with τ_max = T·r/J, where r = d/2 is the outer radius and J = πd⁴/32 is the polar moment of inertia for a solid circular cross-section.

For part (c), compute the angle of twist φ over length L using φ = T·L/(G·J), with result in radians.

All calculations use consistent N–mm–MPa units: power in N·mm/s (1 kW = 10⁶ N·mm/s), dimensions in mm, stress in MPa (N/mm²), and G in MPa.
