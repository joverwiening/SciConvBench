The adhesive layer has area A = 3000 mm² and thickness t = 8 mm.

The applied uniform in-plane shear force is V = 12000 N.

The average shear stress is computed as τ_avg = V/A = 12000 N / 3000 mm² = 4 MPa.

The material is isotropic with shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.

The average shear strain is computed as γ_avg = τ_avg / G = 4 MPa / 39179.1 MPa = 1.02095 × 10⁻⁴ (dimensionless, or equivalently 0.000102095 rad).

The adhesive layer is treated as rigid in shear, meaning the two faces undergo no relative slip; this assumption is used for computing the shear deformation.

The relative lateral displacement between the two faces is computed as Δ = γ_avg · t = (1.02095 × 10⁻⁴) × 8 mm = 8.1676 × 10⁻⁴ mm = 0.00081676 mm.
