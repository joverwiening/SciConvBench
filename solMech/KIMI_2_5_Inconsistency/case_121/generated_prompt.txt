The beam is a rectangular cross-section with width b = 40 mm and height h = 100 mm.

The internal bending moment is M = 4000000 N·mm (sagging).

The coordinate y is measured from the neutral axis (centroid) positive upward.

The pure-bending normal stress is treated as uniform over the section depth, meaning the stress distribution is constant rather than linear.

The bending stress formula for uniform distribution is σ = M / S, where S is the section modulus.

For a rectangular section, the section modulus is S = b * h² / 6 = 40 * 100² / 6 = 66666.667 mm³.

The uniform bending stress magnitude is σ = 4000000 / 66666.667 = 60 MPa.

For sagging moment, the top fibers are in compression and bottom fibers are in tension.

The stress at y = 0 mm (neutral axis) is σ = -60 MPa (compression, following the instruction that stress at neutral axis has same sign as extreme fibers in compression zone).

The stress at the top fiber (y = +50 mm) is σ = -60 MPa (compression).

The stress at the bottom fiber (y = -50 mm) is σ = +60 MPa (tension).
