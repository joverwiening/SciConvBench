The column is a solid circular cross-section with diameter d = 20 mm and length L = 2000 mm.

The material is structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.

The effective length factor is K = 1.0.

Euler buckling governs the failure mode.

The polar moment of inertia for the circular section is J = π d^4 / 32.

The radius of gyration for a circular section is r = sqrt(J/A) where A = π d^2 / 4, which simplifies to r = d/4.

The slenderness ratio is KL/r.

The Euler critical load is computed as P_cr = π^2 E J / (KL)^2.

The factor of safety against buckling is n = 2.0.

The allowable axial compressive load is P_allow = P_cr / n.

For part (a), calculate P_cr using the given values: d = 20 mm, L = 2000 mm, E = 200000 MPa, K = 1.0, and J = π (20)^4 / 32 = 15707.96 mm^4.

For part (b), calculate P_allow = P_cr / 2.0.
