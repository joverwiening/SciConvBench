The shaft is made of AISI 1020 steel with diameter d = 30 mm and length L = 1000 mm.
The shaft transmits a torque T = 250000 N·mm.
The material is linearly elastic with a circular solid cross section.
The polar moment of inertia is J = π d^4/32.
The maximum shear stress is calculated using τ_max = T c / J where c = d/2.
The shear modulus is G = 76923.1 MPa.
The angle of twist is calculated using φ = TL/(JG).
The angle of twist φ must be reported in degrees.
The bending second moment I = π d^4/64 is provided but not used in the required calculations.
All units are consistent: N–mm–MPa.
