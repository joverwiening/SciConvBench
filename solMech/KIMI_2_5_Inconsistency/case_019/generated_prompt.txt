The shaft is a solid circular shaft with constant diameter d = 30 mm and total length L = 1200 mm, fixed at x = 0.

The material is AISI 1020 steel with shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa.

Applied torques are: T1 = 200000 N·mm at x = L (positive sense), and T2 = -200000 N·mm at x = 400 mm (negative sense, opposite direction).

The shaft has two segments: segment 1 from x = 0 to x = 400 mm, and segment 2 from x = 400 mm to x = 1200 mm.

For part (a), construct the internal torque diagram showing the internal torque value in each segment. The internal torque in segment 1 (0 to 400 mm) equals the algebraic sum of all torques to the right of any cut in that segment. The internal torque in segment 2 (400 mm to 1200 mm) equals the algebraic sum of all torques to the right of any cut in that segment.

For part (b), compute the maximum shear stress in each segment using the torsion formula τ_max = T·r/J, where r = d/2 is the outer radius and J = πd⁴/32 is the polar moment of inertia for a solid circular cross-section. Use the internal torque from each segment.

For part (c), compute the angle of twist at x = L relative to the fixed end by summing the twist of each segment. The twist of each segment is φ = T·L_seg/(G·J), where T is the internal torque in that segment and L_seg is the segment length. Sum φ₁ + φ₂ to obtain the total angle of twist at the free end. Express the final answer in degrees.
