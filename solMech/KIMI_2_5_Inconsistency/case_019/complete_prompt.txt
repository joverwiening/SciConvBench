Use N–mm–MPa units.

A solid circular shaft of diameter d = 30 mm and total length L = 1200 mm is fixed at x = 0. A torque T1 = 200000 N·mm is applied at the free end x = L (positive sense). In addition, a torque T2 = -200000 N·mm is applied at x = 400 mm along the shaft (negative sense means opposite direction). The material is AISI 1020 steel with G = 76923.1 MPa.

(a) Construct the internal torque in each segment (0→a and a→L).
(b) Compute the maximum shear stress in each segment.
(c) Compute the angle of twist at x = L relative to the fixed end by summing the twist of each segment.
