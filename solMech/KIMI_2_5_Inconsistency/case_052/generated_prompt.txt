The shaft is a solid circular shaft with diameter d = 35 mm.

The shaft material is AISI 1020 steel with shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa.

The shaft length between fixed ends is L = 1000 mm.

The motor delivers power P = 10 kW to the shaft.

The shaft rotates at rotational speed 1800 rpm.

For torque calculation, use P = T·ω with ω = 2π(rpm/60), where the rpm value of 1800 is substituted directly.

For torsion calculations, use standard torsion theory for circular shafts.

The polar moment of inertia for a solid circular shaft is J = πd⁴/32.

The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer surface where radius r = d/2.

The angle of twist is calculated using φ = TL/(GJ).

All calculations use consistent N–mm–MPa units: power in N·mm/s (converted from kW), torque in N·mm, stress in MPa (N/mm²), and angle in radians.

(a) Compute transmitted torque T:
T = P/ω = (10 × 10⁶ N·mm/s) / (2π × 1800/60 rad/s) = 53051.6 N·mm

(b) Compute maximum shear stress τ_max:
τ_max = T·r/J = T·(d/2) / (πd⁴/32) = 16T/(πd³) = 16 × 53051.6 / (π × 35³) = 6.25 MPa

(c) Compute angle of twist φ:
φ = TL/(GJ) = 53051.6 × 1000 / (76923.1 × π × 35⁴/32) = 0.00463 rad = 0.265 degrees
