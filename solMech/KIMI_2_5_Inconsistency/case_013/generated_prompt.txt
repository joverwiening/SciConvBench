The beam is simply supported with span L = 3000 mm.
The material is structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The second moment of area is constant I = 2000000 mm^4.
The beam carries two loads simultaneously: a uniform distributed load w = 0.3 N/mm over the full span, and a downward point load P = 3000 N at midspan.
For superposition, the distributed load contribution is approximated by replacing the uniform load with a single resultant force wL acting at midspan.
The resultant force from the distributed load is wL = 0.3 N/mm × 3000 mm = 900 N acting at midspan.
The deflection at midspan for a simply supported beam with a point load F at midspan is given by δ = FL³/(48EI).
The deflection due to the point load P = 3000 N is δ_P = PL³/(48EI) = (3000 N)(3000 mm)³/(48 × 200000 MPa × 2000000 mm⁴).
The deflection due to the resultant force wL = 900 N is δ_wL = (wL)L³/(48EI) = (900 N)(3000 mm)³/(48 × 200000 MPa × 2000000 mm⁴).
The total deflection at midspan by superposition is δ_total = δ_P + δ_wL = (P + wL)L³/(48EI).
Substituting values: δ_total = (3000 N + 900 N)(3000 mm)³/(48 × 200000 N/mm² × 2000000 mm⁴) = (3900 N)(2.7×10¹⁰ mm³)/(1.92×10¹³ N·mm²) = 5.484375 mm.
The computed deflection at midspan is approximately 5.48 mm downward.
