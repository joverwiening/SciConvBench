Use N–mm–MPa units throughout the calculation.

The column is made of structural steel with length L = 2000 mm.

The flexural rigidity is EI with E = 200000 MPa and I = 800000 mm^4.

The end conditions are fixed–fixed with effective length factor K = 0.5.

Treat the effective-length factor as affecting only the mode shape, so evaluate the Euler load from π^2 E I / L^2 using the listed flexural rigidity.

Compute the Euler critical buckling load P_cr = π^2 E I / (K L)^2.

Report P_cr in N and in kN.

Calculation steps:

Compute the effective length: L_e = K × L = 0.5 × 2000 mm = 1000 mm.

Compute P_cr = π^2 × E × I / (L_e)^2 = π^2 × 200000 MPa × 800000 mm^4 / (1000 mm)^2.

P_cr = π^2 × 200000 × 800000 / 1000000 N.

P_cr = π^2 × 160000000000 / 1000000 N.

P_cr = π^2 × 160000 N.

P_cr = 9.8696 × 160000 N = 1579136.7 N.

P_cr = 1579.14 kN (or approximately 1580 kN or 1.58 × 10^3 kN).
