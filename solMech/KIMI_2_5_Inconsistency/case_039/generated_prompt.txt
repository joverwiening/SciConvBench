The rod is made of AISI 1020 steel with initial diameter d0 = 20 mm and length L = 1200 mm.

The applied axial tensile load is P = 35000 N acting through the centroid.

The material properties are Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.30.

The analysis assumes linear elastic isotropic behavior with plane strain in the two transverse directions, meaning the lateral strain is constrained to zero; the Poisson's ratio value serves only as a material reference property.

Compute the average axial normal stress as σ_axial = P / A, where A = π × (d0/2)^2 = π × (10 mm)^2 = 100π mm^2, giving σ_axial = 35000 N / (100π mm^2) = 350/π MPa ≈ 111.408 MPa.

Compute the axial strain as ε_axial = σ_axial / E = (350/π MPa) / (200000 MPa) = 7/(4000π) ≈ 0.000557.

The lateral (diametral) strain is zero due to the plane strain constraint in the transverse directions: ε_lateral = 0.

The change in diameter Δd is zero because the lateral strain is constrained to zero: Δd = ε_lateral × d0 = 0 × 20 mm = 0 mm.
