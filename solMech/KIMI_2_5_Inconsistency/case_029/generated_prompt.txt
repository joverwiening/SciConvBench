The rod is a solid circular cylinder with initial diameter d0 = 20 mm and length L = 1000 mm.

The material is brass with Young's modulus E = 105000 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.34.

The material behavior is linear elastic and isotropic.

The rod is subjected to an axial tensile load P = 25000 N applied through the centroid.

The boundary condition is plane strain in the two transverse directions, meaning the lateral strain is constrained to zero; the Poisson's ratio provided is for material reference only and does not produce lateral strain in this constrained condition.

The average axial normal stress σ is computed as P divided by the initial cross-sectional area A0 = π(d0/2)².

The axial strain ε_axial is computed as σ divided by E.

The lateral (diametral) strain ε_lateral is zero due to the plane strain constraint in the transverse directions.

The change in diameter Δd is zero because the lateral strain is zero.
