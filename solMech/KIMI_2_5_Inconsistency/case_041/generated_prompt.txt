The column is made of AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The second moment of area is I = 1200000 mm^4.
The column length is L = 1000 mm.
The end conditions are fixed-free (cantilever) with effective length factor K = 2.0.
The flexural rigidity EI is computed as E × I = 200000 MPa × 1200000 mm^4 = 2.4 × 10^11 N·mm^2.
The Euler critical buckling load is calculated as P_cr = π^2 × E × I / (K × L)^2.
Substituting values: P_cr = π^2 × 2.4 × 10^11 N·mm^2 / (2.0 × 1000 mm)^2 = π^2 × 2.4 × 10^11 / (4.0 × 10^6) N.
P_cr = 592176.26 N.
P_cr = 592.18 kN (rounded to two decimal places).
