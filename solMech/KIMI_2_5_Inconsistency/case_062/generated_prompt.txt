The rod is prismatic with length L = 1200 mm and diameter d = 20 mm, fixed between two rigid unyielding supports with zero total length change constraint.

The material is structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa and coefficient of thermal expansion α = 1×10⁻⁵ per °C.

The rod experiences a uniform temperature increase ΔT = 35 °C.

The actual axial strain is taken as the free thermal strain ε = αΔT = (1×10⁻⁵)(35) = 3.5×10⁻⁴.

The thermal stress is taken to vanish, so σ = 0 MPa.

Since the thermal stress vanishes, there is no net axial force, so P = 0 N.

(i) The thermal stress that develops in the rod is 0 MPa. It is neither tensile nor compressive.

(ii) The corresponding axial force carried by the rod is 0 N.
