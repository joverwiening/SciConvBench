The bar is vertical with length L = 3500 mm and constant cross-sectional area A = 200 mm², hanging from a rigid support at its top end.

The material has modulus of elasticity E = 69000 MPa and density ρ = 7850 kg/m³.

Gravitational acceleration is g = 9.81 m/s².

No external loads are applied; only self-weight acts on the bar.

For the elongation calculation, the distributed self-weight is replaced by a single concentrated load equal to the total weight acting at the free end, and this is used as the exact axial force.

The total weight of the bar is W = ρ g A L, with consistent unit conversion: convert ρ from kg/m³ to kg/mm³ using ρ = 7850 × 10⁻⁹ kg/mm³, or equivalently convert L to meters and g to mm/s².

Using the hint approach with x measured downward from the top: the axial force at section x is P(x) = ρ g A (L − x).

The elongation is computed by integrating δ = ∫₀ᴸ P(x)/(A E) dx = ∫₀ᴸ ρ g (L − x)/E dx.

Performing the integration: δ = ρ g L² / (2 E).

Substituting values with ρ = 7850 kg/m³ = 7.85 × 10⁻⁶ kg/mm³, g = 9810 mm/s², L = 3500 mm, E = 69000 MPa = 69000 N/mm²:

Total weight W = (7.85 × 10⁻⁶ kg/mm³)(9810 mm/s²)(200 mm²)(3500 mm) = 53929.95 N ≈ 53.93 N.

Elongation δ = (7.85 × 10⁻⁶)(9810)(3500)² / (2 × 69000) = 0.686 mm.

Alternatively, using the concentrated load approximation at the free end: P = W = 53929.95 N, then δ = P L / (A E) = (53929.95)(3500) / (200 × 69000) = 13.68 mm.

The specification requires using the concentrated load as the exact axial force, so the elongation is δ = W L / (A E) = 13.68 mm.
