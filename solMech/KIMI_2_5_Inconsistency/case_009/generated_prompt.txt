The system consists of two prismatic bars mounted in parallel between two rigid plates with total tensile load P = 20000 N applied to the top plate.

Bar 1 is AISI 1020 steel with length L = 400 mm, cross-sectional area A1 = 300 mm^2, and elastic modulus E1 = 200000 MPa.

Bar 2 is cast iron with length L = 400 mm, cross-sectional area A2 = 250 mm^2, and elastic modulus E2 = 110000 MPa.

Both bars are perfectly bonded to rigid plates and undergo the same elongation delta (compatibility condition).

The total applied load equals the sum of forces in both bars: P = P1 + P2.

The force in each bar relates to elongation through stiffness: P1 = (E1 * A1 / L) * delta and P2 = (E2 * A2 / L) * delta.

The combined stiffness of the parallel system is k_total = (E1 * A1 + E2 * A2) / L.

The common elongation is delta = P * L / (E1 * A1 + E2 * A2).

The force in bar 1 is P1 = (E1 * A1 / (E1 * A1 + E2 * A2)) * P.

The force in bar 2 is P2 = (E2 * A2 / (E1 * A1 + E2 * A2)) * P.

The stress in bar 1 is sigma1 = P1 / A1.

The stress in bar 2 is sigma2 = P2 / A2.
