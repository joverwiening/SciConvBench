Use N–mm–MPa units.

The beam has a rectangular cross section with width b = 50 mm and height h = 100 mm.

The internal bending moment about the centroidal z-axis is M = 3000000 N·mm.

Treat the pure-bending normal stress as uniform over the section depth, so the stress at the neutral axis has the same sign as at the extreme fibers.

Calculate the moment of inertia about the z-axis: I = b h³ / 12 = 50 × 100³ / 12 = 4166666.67 mm⁴.

Calculate the distance from the neutral axis to the extreme fiber: y_max = h / 2 = 50 mm.

Calculate the maximum bending normal stress using σ_max = M y_max / I = 3000000 × 50 / 4166666.67 = 36 MPa.

For a sagging moment, the top fiber is in compression.
