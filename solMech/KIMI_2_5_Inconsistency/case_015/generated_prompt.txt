The beam is a cantilever fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L = 1200 mm with a downward point load P = 2000 N applied at the free end.

The material is AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.

The cross-section has constant second moment of area I = 2000000 mm^4.

Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.

Apply Euler–Bernoulli beam theory with small deflection and linear elastic behavior.

The bending moment varies linearly from M = PL = 2400000 N·mm at the fixed end (x = 0) to M = 0 at the free end (x = L).

The governing differential equation is EI(d²w/dx²) = -M(x) = -P(L-x) for a downward load.

Integrate once to obtain the slope equation: EI(dw/dx) = -P(Lx - x²/2) + C1.

Apply the fixed-end boundary condition: dw/dx = 0 at x = 0, which gives C1 = 0.

Integrate again to obtain the deflection equation: EIw = -P(Lx²/2 - x³/6) + C2.

Apply the fixed-end boundary condition: w = 0 at x = 0, which gives C2 = 0.

Evaluate the slope at the free end (x = L): θ(L) = -PL²/(2EI).

Evaluate the deflection at the free end (x = L): w(L) = -PL³/(3EI).

Calculate the numerical values: EI = 200000 × 2000000 = 4×10^11 N·mm².

Slope at free end: θ(L) = -(2000)(1200)² / (2 × 4×10^11) = -0.0036 rad (negative indicates clockwise rotation).

Deflection at free end: w(L) = -(2000)(1200)³ / (3 × 4×10^11) = -2.88 mm (negative indicates downward deflection).

Report the slope magnitude as 0.0036 rad or 0.206°.

Report the deflection magnitude as 2.88 mm downward.
