The bar is prismatic with constant cross-sectional area A = 500 mm² and constant modulus E = 200000 MPa.

The total length from A to D is 1400 mm.

The net applied axial load is computed from equilibrium: sum of all applied forces must balance the reaction at A. The applied forces are 15000 N at B, -12000 N at C, and 10000 N at D. The sum is 13000 N to the right, so the reaction at A is -13000 N (to the left).

The internal axial force is uniform throughout the entire bar because the bar is prismatic with constant area and modulus, and the problem specifies to treat internal axial force as uniform along the full length based on net applied load.

The internal axial force in each segment (AB, BC, and CD) is 13000 N (tension).

The normal stress in each segment is σ = F/A = 13000 N / 500 mm² = 26 MPa.

The total elongation of the bar is δ = FL/(AE) = (13000 N)(1400 mm)/(500 mm² × 200000 MPa) = 0.182 mm.

Since point A is fixed (u_A = 0), the displacement of point D relative to A is 0.182 mm to the right.
