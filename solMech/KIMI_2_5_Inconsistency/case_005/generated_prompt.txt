The bar is prismatic with constant cross-sectional area A = 200 mm² and constant modulus E = 200000 MPa.

The total length is AB + BC + CD = 700 + 600 + 500 = 1800 mm.

The internal axial force is uniform along the full length due to the prismatic nature and constant modulus.

Compute the net applied axial force from the right end: starting from fixed end A, sum forces to the right.

The reaction at A is determined from equilibrium: R_A + 18000 - 8000 + 20000 = 0, so R_A = -30000 N (acting to the left, or 30000 N compression at the support).

The internal axial force throughout the bar is constant at P = 30000 N (tension positive convention: actually -30000 N, meaning 30000 N compression throughout).
