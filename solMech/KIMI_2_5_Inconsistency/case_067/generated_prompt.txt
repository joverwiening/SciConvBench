The shaft is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with diameter d = 30 mm and length L = 800 mm.
The shaft transmits a torque T = 500000 N·mm.
The polar moment of inertia is J = π d^4/32.
The maximum shear stress is calculated using τ_max = T c / J where c = d/2.
The shear modulus is G = 25939.8 MPa.
The angle of twist is calculated using φ = TL/(JG).
The angle of twist φ must be reported in degrees.
The bending second moment I = π d^4/64 is noted but not used in the actual calculations (J is used instead).
The shaft is linearly elastic with a circular solid cross section.
All units are N–mm–MPa.
