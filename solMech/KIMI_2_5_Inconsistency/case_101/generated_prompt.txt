The bar is vertical with length L = 3000 mm and constant cross-sectional area A = 300 mm².

The bar is prismatic and linearly elastic with modulus of elasticity E = 69000 MPa.

The material density is ρ = 8900 kg/m³ and gravitational acceleration is g = 9.81 m/s².

Only self-weight acts on the bar; no external loads are applied.

For the elongation calculation, replace the distributed self-weight by a single concentrated load equal to the total weight acting at the free end.

The total weight W = ρ g A L must be computed with consistent units: convert ρ to kg/mm³ or convert L to m.

Using consistent units: ρ = 8900 kg/m³ = 8900 × 10⁻⁹ kg/mm³ = 8.9 × 10⁻⁶ kg/mm³, g = 9.81 × 10³ mm/s², A = 300 mm², L = 3000 mm.

Compute total weight: W = ρ g A L = (8.9 × 10⁻⁶ kg/mm³) × (9810 mm/s²) × (300 mm²) × (3000 mm) = 78.5793 N.

The concentrated load P = W = 78.5793 N acts at the free end (x = L).

The elongation is computed as δ = P L / (A E) = (78.5793 N) × (3000 mm) / [(300 mm²) × (69000 MPa)].

Note that 1 MPa = 1 N/mm², so E = 69000 N/mm².

Compute δ = (78.5793 × 3000) / (300 × 69000) mm = 235737.9 / 20700000 mm = 0.0113883 mm.

The elongation of the bar due to its own weight is approximately 0.0114 mm or 1.14 × 10⁻² mm.
