Use N–mm–MPa units.

A standard tension-test specimen has an initial diameter d0 = 12.5 mm and an initial gage length L0 = 50 mm.

During the linear-elastic portion of the test, an axial load of P = 12000 N produces an elongation of ΔL = 0.12 mm over the gage length.

Compute the engineering stress using the standard definition: engineering stress = force divided by original cross-sectional area.

Compute the engineering strain using the standard definition: engineering strain = change in length divided by original gage length.

Determine the modulus of elasticity E from the slope E = σ/ε.

The original cross-sectional area A0 is calculated from the initial diameter d0 = 12.5 mm.

The engineering stress σ equals P divided by A0.

The engineering strain ε equals ΔL divided by L0.

The modulus of elasticity E equals σ divided by ε.
