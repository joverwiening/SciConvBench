Q: I need to clarify one point about your boundary conditions.

You stated this is a "simply supported beam" but then said "take the end slopes to be zero in addition to the support deflections." Zero end slopes with zero end deflections describes a fixed-fixed (built-in) beam, not a simply supported beam.

A simply supported beam has zero deflection at the supports but non-zero (free) slopes at the ends.

Which boundary condition do you want me to use?

Option A: Simply supported (deflection = 0 at both ends, slopes are free/unknown)
Option B: Fixed-fixed (deflection = 0 and slope = 0 at both ends)
A: Use Option A: Simply supported (deflection = 0 at both ends, slopes are free/unknown).

The complete requirement states "simply supported beam" and does not mention zero end slopes. The statement about zero end slopes appears only in the incomplete version and should be disregarded.
