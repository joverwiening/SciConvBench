The stepped circular bar analysis shall use the following physically correct approach.

The normal stress in segment 1 equals P divided by the cross-sectional area of segment 1, where the area is pi times d1 squared divided by 4.

The normal stress in segment 2 equals P divided by the cross-sectional area of segment 2, where the area is pi times d2 squared divided by 4.

The elongation of segment 1 equals P times L1 divided by the product of E and the cross-sectional area of segment 1.

The elongation of segment 2 equals P times L2 divided by the product of E and the cross-sectional area of segment 2.

The total elongation of the bar equals the sum of the elongation of segment 1 and the elongation of segment 2.

Use P = 30000 N, L1 = 300 mm, d1 = 25 mm, L2 = 1000 mm, d2 = 35 mm, and E = 200000 MPa.

Report the normal stress in segment 1 in MPa.

Report the normal stress in segment 2 in MPa.

Report the total elongation in mm.
