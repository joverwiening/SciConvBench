The bar is a one-dimensional axial member with length L = 800 mm.

The applied axial tensile force is constant at P = 12000 N.

The material is AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa, treated as constant.

The cross-sectional area varies linearly along the length according to A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L), where A0 = 250 mm² and k = 0.6.

The arithmetic-mean area shall be used in place of the actual varying area A(x).

The arithmetic-mean area is calculated as Am = (A(0) + A(L))/2 = (A0 + A0·(1 + k))/2 = A0·(1 + k/2) = 250·(1 + 0.3) = 325 mm².

This mean area Am = 325 mm² shall be treated as exact and constant for the deformation calculation.

The axial deformation integral δ = ∫[0→L] P/(A(x)E) dx shall be evaluated using the constant mean area Am in place of A(x).

The elongation is computed as δ = P·L/(Am·E) = 12000 N × 800 mm / (325 mm² × 200000 MPa).

The units N–mm–MPa are used throughout, noting that 1 MPa = 1 N/mm², so the result is in mm.

The calculated elongation is δ = 9,600,000 / 65,000,000 mm = 0.147692... mm, or approximately 0.1477 mm.
