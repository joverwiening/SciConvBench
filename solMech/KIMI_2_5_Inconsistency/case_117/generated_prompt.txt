The beam has a rectangular cross section with width b = 40 mm and height h = 80 mm. At a certain section the internal shear force is V = 25000 N.

Treat the transverse shear stress as uniform through the depth, so the value at y may be taken from V/A and the maximum shear occurs at the outer fibers.

(a) Compute the maximum transverse shear stress in the cross section using τ_max = 1.5 V/A.

The cross-sectional area is A = b × h = 40 mm × 80 mm = 3200 mm².

The maximum transverse shear stress is τ_max = 1.5 × V/A = 1.5 × 25000 N / 3200 mm² = 11.71875 N/mm² = 11.72 MPa.

(b) Using the shear formula τ = VQ/(I b), compute the shear stress at a point located y = 20 mm from the neutral axis (y positive upward, with y=0 at the neutral axis). Provide τ in MPa.

The moment of inertia is I = b h³/12 = 40 mm × (80 mm)³ / 12 = 40 × 512000 / 12 mm⁴ = 1706666.67 mm⁴.

For the area above y = 20 mm: the height of the area above y is h/2 - y = 40 mm - 20 mm = 20 mm. The area is A_above = b × (h/2 - y) = 40 mm × 20 mm = 800 mm². The centroid of this area above y is located at a distance from the neutral axis of ȳ = y + (h/2 - y)/2 = 20 mm + 10 mm = 30 mm. The first moment of area is Q = A_above × ȳ = 800 mm² × 30 mm = 24000 mm³.

The shear stress at y = 20 mm is τ = VQ/(I b) = 25000 N × 24000 mm³ / (1706666.67 mm⁴ × 40 mm) = 600000000 / 68266666.8 N/mm² = 8.789 MPa.
