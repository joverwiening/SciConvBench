The user wants to compute curvature and radius of curvature for a beam in bending.

Use N–mm–MPa units for all calculations.

The beam material is AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.

The second moment of area about the neutral axis is I = 1200000 mm^4.

The bending moment at the section is M = 3000000 N·mm.

Use the curvature relation κ = M/(EI) to compute curvature.

Compute the curvature κ = 1/ρ = M/(EI).

Compute the radius of curvature ρ in mm.

Note: The user mentions "polar second moment" and "J" in the problem statement, but this appears to be a misstatement. For bending about a neutral axis, the correct property is the area second moment of area I (not the polar second moment J). The standard Euler-Bernoulli beam curvature formula κ = M/(EI) uses I, not J. The polar second moment J = I_x + I_y applies to torsion, not bending. Execute the calculation using I = 1200000 mm^4 as specified.

Calculation of curvature:
κ = M/(EI) = 3000000 N·mm / (200000 MPa × 1200000 mm^4) = 3000000 / (200000 × 1200000) mm^-1 = 3000000 / 240000000000 mm^-1 = 1.25 × 10^-5 mm^-1 = 0.0000125 mm^-1

Calculation of radius of curvature:
ρ = 1/κ = EI/M = (200000 MPa × 1200000 mm^4) / 3000000 N·mm = 240000000000 / 3000000 mm = 80000 mm

The curvature is κ = 0.0000125 mm^-1 (or 1.25 × 10^-5 mm^-1).

The radius of curvature is ρ = 80000 mm (or 80 m).
