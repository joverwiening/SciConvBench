The bar is prismatic with constant cross-sectional area A = 300 mm² and constant modulus E = 200000 MPa.

The bar extends from fixed point A to free end D with total length 1500 mm, divided into three segments: AB = 500 mm, BC = 600 mm, and CD = 400 mm.

External axial forces are applied at points B, C, and D: at B, +18000 N (rightward); at C, -12000 N (leftward); at D, +16000 N (rightward).

The internal axial force is uniform along the full length of the bar because the bar is prismatic with constant area and modulus.

Compute the net applied load by summing all external forces: 18000 N + (-12000 N) + 16000 N = 22000 N.

The internal axial force in each segment (AB, BC, CD) equals the net applied load of 22000 N (tension).

The normal stress in each segment equals the internal axial force divided by the cross-sectional area: 22000 N / 300 mm² = 73.333 MPa.

The total elongation of the bar equals the internal axial force times total length divided by the product of area and modulus: (22000 N × 1500 mm) / (300 mm² × 200000 MPa) = 0.55 mm.

The displacement of point D relative to A equals the total elongation of 0.55 mm (since point A is fixed and does not move).
