The rod is a structural steel cylinder with initial diameter d0 = 25 mm and length L = 1200 mm, subjected to axial tensile load P = 45000 N through the centroid. Material properties are Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.29. The analysis assumes linear elastic isotropic behavior with plane strain in the two transverse directions, meaning lateral strain is constrained to zero regardless of the material's Poisson ratio.

Compute the average axial normal stress as σ_axial = P / A, where A = π × (d0/2)^2 = π × (12.5 mm)^2 = 490.874 mm^2, giving σ_axial = 45000 N / 490.874 mm^2 = 91.673 MPa.

Compute the axial strain as ε_axial = σ_axial / E = 91.673 MPa / 200000 MPa = 4.584 × 10^-4 (dimensionless, or 0.0004584 mm/mm).

The lateral (diametral) strain is zero by the plane strain constraint assumption: ε_lateral = 0.

The change in diameter is Δd = ε_lateral × d0 = 0 × 25 mm = 0 mm.
