The adhesive layer has area A = 4000 mm² and thickness t = 20 mm.

A uniform in-plane shear force V = 8000 N is applied to the layer.

The average shear stress is defined as τ_avg = V/A.

The adhesive layer is treated as rigid in shear, meaning the two faces undergo no relative slip; this assumption is used while computing the shear deformation.

The material is isotropic with shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.

Small strains are assumed.

The units used throughout are N–mm–MPa.

Compute the average shear stress τ_avg = V/A = 8000 N / 4000 mm² = 2 MPa.

Compute the average shear strain γ_avg = τ_avg / G = 2 MPa / 39179.1 MPa = 5.105 × 10⁻⁵ (dimensionless, or equivalently 5.105 × 10⁻⁵ mm/mm).

Compute the relative lateral displacement between the two faces Δ = γ_avg · t = (5.105 × 10⁻⁵) × 20 mm = 1.021 × 10⁻³ mm = 0.001021 mm.
