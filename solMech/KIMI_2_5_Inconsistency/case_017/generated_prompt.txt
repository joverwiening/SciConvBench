Use N–mm–MPa units throughout the analysis.

Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory applies.

Use the method of superposition to combine load effects.

The beam is simply supported with span L = 3000 mm.

The material is AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.

The cross-section has constant second moment of area I = 3000000 mm^4.

Load case 1 is a uniform distributed load w = 0.3 N/mm acting over the full span.

Load case 2 is a downward point load P = 1200 N applied at midspan.

For the distributed load contribution, replace the uniform load by a single resultant force equal to wL acting at midspan.

Compute the deflection at midspan due to the equivalent concentrated resultant wL using the standard formula for a simply supported beam with central point load: delta_resultant = (wL * L^3) / (48 * E * I).

Compute the deflection at midspan due to the actual point load P using the standard formula: delta_P = (P * L^3) / (48 * E * I).

Sum the two deflection contributions algebraically to obtain the total midspan deflection: delta_total = delta_resultant + delta_P.

Report the final numerical value of the midspan deflection in mm.
