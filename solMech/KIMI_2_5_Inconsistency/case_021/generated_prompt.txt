The shaft consists of two segments in series with segment AB (L1 = 300 mm, d1 = 20 mm) and segment BC (L2 = 500 mm, d2 = 30 mm), made of 6061-T6 aluminum with G = 25939.8 MPa.

Calculate the polar moment of inertia for each segment.
For segment AB: J1 = π × d1^4 / 32 = π × (20 mm)^4 / 32 = 15707.96 mm^4.
For segment BC: J2 = π × d2^4 / 32 = π × (30 mm)^4 / 32 = 79521.56 mm^4.

Calculate the torsional stiffness of each segment (k = GJ/L).
For segment AB: k1 = (25939.8 MPa × 15707.96 mm^4) / 300 mm = 1358200 N·mm/rad.
For segment BC: k2 = (25939.8 MPa × 79521.56 mm^4) / 500 mm = 4125540 N·mm/rad.

Calculate the total torsional stiffness for the series combination: 1/k_total = 1/k1 + 1/k2, so k_total = (k1 × k2)/(k1 + k2) = 1022400 N·mm/rad.

The total applied torque T = 300000 N·mm creates a total angle of twist φ_total = T/k_total = 300000/1022400 = 0.29343 rad.

The torque distributes in proportion to stiffness when considering the twist compatibility, but for series shafts, the same internal torque T = 300000 N·mm acts through both segments. The twist in each segment is φ1 = T/k1 and φ2 = T/k2.

Calculate the twist in each segment.
φ1 = 300000 / 1358200 = 0.22088 rad = 12.657 degrees.
φ2 = 300000 / 4125540 = 0.07272 rad = 4.166 degrees.

Verify: φ1 + φ2 = 0.2936 rad ≈ 0.29343 rad (small rounding difference).

Calculate maximum shear stress in each segment using τ_max = T·r/J = 16T/(πd^3).
For segment AB: τ1 = (16 × 300000 N·mm) / (π × (20 mm)^3) = 190.99 MPa.
For segment BC: τ2 = (16 × 300000 N·mm) / (π × (30 mm)^3) = 56.59 MPa.

The maximum shear stress in segment AB is 190.99 MPa.
The maximum shear stress in segment BC is 56.59 MPa.
Segment AB governs with τ_max = 190.99 MPa.

The total angle of twist at C relative to A is φ_total = φ1 + φ2 = 0.22088 + 0.07272 = 0.2936 rad = 16.82 degrees, or more precisely using the direct calculation: 0.2934 rad = 16.81 degrees.
