Assume small deformation, linear elastic material behavior.
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory applies (plane sections remain plane and normal to the neutral axis; shear deformation is neglected).
Assume pure bending at the section of interest (the provided bending moment is the only needed load effect for curvature at that section).
Use consistent units: E in N/mm², I in mm⁴, M in N·mm, curvature in 1/mm, radius in mm.
Given E = 200,000 N/mm².
Given I = 4,000,000 mm⁴ about the neutral axis.
Given bending moment at the section M = 3,000,000 N·mm.
Use the moment-curvature relation: κ = M/(E I).
Compute flexural rigidity: E I = (200,000)(4,000,000) = 8.0×10^11 N·mm².
Compute curvature: κ = (3.0×10^6)/(8.0×10^11) = 3.75×10^-6 1/mm.
Use the curvature–radius relation: ρ = 1/κ.
Compute radius of curvature: ρ = 1/(3.75×10^-6) = 2.6667×10^5 mm.
Report radius of curvature equivalently as ρ ≈ 266.7 m.
