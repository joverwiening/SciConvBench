Assume a straight, prismatic, circular rod loaded axially through its centroid so the stress state is uniform uniaxial normal stress and bending/shear effects are negligible.
Assume small deformation, linear-elastic, homogeneous, isotropic material behavior.
Use diameter d = 25 mm, axial tensile force P = 20,000 N, original length L = 800 mm, Young’s modulus E = 200,000 MPa, yield strength Sy = 250 MPa.
Compute cross-sectional area as A = (π/4)d^2.
Compute average normal stress as σavg = P/A.
Compute average axial strain as εavg = σavg/E.
Compute axial elongation as δ = εavg L = (PL)/(AE).
Compute factor of safety against yielding as n = Sy/σavg.
Report σavg in MPa, εavg as dimensionless (or microstrain), δ in mm, and n as dimensionless.
