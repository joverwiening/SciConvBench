Model the member as an Euler–Bernoulli cantilever beam of length L = 1500 mm with constant flexural rigidity EI.
Assume small deformation, linear elastic, homogeneous, isotropic material with Young’s modulus E = 69,000 MPa = 69,000 N/mm^2.
Assume plane sections remain plane and shear deformation is neglected (bending-dominated response).
Assume the beam is prismatic with constant second moment of area I = 2,500,000 mm^4.
Apply a downward point load P = 500 N at the free end x = L.
Define x measured from the fixed end (x = 0) to the free end (x = L).
Use the internal bending moment distribution for an end-loaded cantilever: M(x) = P(L − x).
Write the strain energy in bending: U = ∫0^L [M(x)^2/(2EI)] dx.
Substitute M(x) to obtain: U = ∫0^L [P^2(L − x)^2/(2EI)] dx = P^2 L^3/(6EI).
Use Castigliano’s theorem for the vertical deflection at the load point: δ(L) = ∂U/∂P.
Differentiate to obtain the governing relation: δ(L) = P L^3/(3EI).
Compute EI = E I = (69,000 N/mm^2)(2,500,000 mm^4) = 1.725×10^11 N·mm^2.
Compute L^3 = (1500 mm)^3 = 3.375×10^9 mm^3.
Compute the free-end vertical deflection: δ(L) = (500 N)(3.375×10^9 mm^3) / (3×1.725×10^11 N·mm^2) = 3.2609 mm downward.
Report the relevant response quantity for this case as the free-end vertical deflection magnitude: 3.26 mm (downward).
