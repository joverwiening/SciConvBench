Q: What is the boundary condition for axial restraint (fully fixed at both ends so total axial displacement is zero)?
A: Yes—fully restrained between two rigid, unyielding supports. The axial boundary condition is zero net change in length:

- \(u(0)=0\), \(u(L)=0\)  \(\Rightarrow\) \(\Delta L = u(L)-u(0)=0\) (total axial displacement/elongation is zero).

Q: What axial mechanical load is applied to the rod (give the magnitude and whether it is tensile or compressive)?
A: No external axial mechanical load is applied in the complete requirement. The rod is only subjected to a uniform temperature increase while fully restrained; the resulting axial force is the induced reaction force (compressive).
