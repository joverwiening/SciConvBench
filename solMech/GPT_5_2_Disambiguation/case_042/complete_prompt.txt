Use N–mm–MPa units. Assume linear elastic axial response.

A prismatic bar of length L = 800 mm and cross-sectional area A = 300 mm^2 is made of structural steel with E = 200000 MPa. A tensile load P = 8000 N is applied suddenly (i.e., without gradual ramping), starting from zero load.

Using energy methods for a linear spring, determine the maximum stress in the bar during the transient. (Hint: compare the work done by the suddenly applied load to the strain energy stored in the bar.)
