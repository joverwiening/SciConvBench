Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior but compute stresses only.
Model the stress state as uniform plane stress on a cross-section with σx = P/A and τxy = V/A.
Use the full rectangular area for both normal and average shear stress, with A = b h = 60 mm × 100 mm = 6000 mm^2.

Compute average normal stress as σx = P/A.
Compute average shear stress as τxy = V/A.
Set σy = 0 and τyx = τxy for the plane-stress tensor.
Compute principal stresses from σ1,2 = (σx + σy)/2 ± sqrt(((σx − σy)/2)^2 + τxy^2).
Compute maximum in-plane shear stress from τmax,in-plane = sqrt(((σx − σy)/2)^2 + τxy^2) = (σ1 − σ2)/2.
