Q: Which beam theory should I use to compute transverse shear stress: Euler–Bernoulli (with a shear correction factor) or Timoshenko (shear-deformable)?
A: Use the standard beam shear-stress relations for a rectangular section (Euler–Bernoulli internal shear force approach), specifically:

- Part (a): use the given rectangular-section result \( \tau_{\max} = 1.5\,V/A \).
- Part (b): use the shear formula \( \tau = \dfrac{VQ}{Ib} \) with \( I = \dfrac{bh^3}{12} \) and \(Q\) taken for the area above the level \(y\).

Do **not** use Timoshenko shear-deformation theory; no shear correction factor beyond the provided \(1.5\) factor is needed.
