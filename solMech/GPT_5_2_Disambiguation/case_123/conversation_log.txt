Q: What is the boundary condition for axial restraint (fully fixed at both ends so total axial displacement is zero)?
A: Yes—fully restrained between two rigid, unyielding supports. The axial boundary condition is zero net change in length:

- \(u(0)=0\), \(u(L)=0\)  \(\Rightarrow\) \(\Delta L = u(L)-u(0)=0\).

Q: What is the temperature field along the rod (uniform ΔT everywhere, or a specified function ΔT(x))?
A: Uniform temperature increase everywhere along the rod: \(\Delta T = 50^\circ\text{C}\) (no spatial variation, i.e., \(\Delta T(x)=50^\circ\text{C}\) for \(0\le x\le L\)).

Q: What axial mechanical load is applied (give its magnitude and whether it is a prescribed end force N/P or a prescribed end displacement)?
A: No external axial mechanical load is applied. The only “load” is the uniform temperature increase, and the ends are fully restrained with prescribed end displacements \(u(0)=0\) and \(u(L)=0\) (i.e., \(\Delta L=0\)).
