Q: What is the bar’s cross-sectional area function A(x) along its length (e.g., linear from A0 at x=0 to A1 at x=L)?

\[
A(x)=A_0\left(1+k\frac{x}{L}\right),
\]

with \(A_0 = 250\ \text{mm}^2\), \(k=0.8\), \(L=800\ \text{mm}\), and \(x\) measured from the left end (\(x=0\) at the left end).

So \(A(0)=250\ \text{mm}^2\) and \(A(L)=250(1+0.8)=450\ \text{mm}^2\).
