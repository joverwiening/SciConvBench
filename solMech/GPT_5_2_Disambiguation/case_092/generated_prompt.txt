Model the member as a 1D prismatic axial bar under small deformation, linear elasticity.
Use units N–mm–MPa with E = 200000 MPa and A = 500 mm^2 constant.
Define the axial coordinate x with x = 0 at A and positive to the right.
Set point locations A: x=0 mm, B: x=700 mm, C: x=1100 mm, D: x=1400 mm.
Apply boundary condition u(0)=0 at A.
Apply concentrated axial loads P_B=+15000 N at x=700 mm, P_C=-12000 N at x=1100 mm, and P_D=+10000 N at x=1400 mm.
Use sign convention: positive load acts to the right; internal axial force N(x) is positive in tension (acts to the right on the left cut face).
Assume no distributed axial load along the bar (b(x)=0).
Assume quasi-static equilibrium and neglect body forces unless otherwise stated.
Enforce global axial force equilibrium to determine the reaction at A: R_A + P_B + P_C + P_D = 0.
Compute the support reaction as R_A = -(15000 - 12000 + 10000) N = -13000 N.
Use the 1D axial equilibrium relation in each load-free segment: dN/dx = 0.
Represent the internal axial force as piecewise constant with jumps at point loads: N(x_i^+) - N(x_i^-) = -P_i.
Compute internal axial force by summing external forces to the left of a cut: N(x) = -[R_A + sum(P_i for x_i < x)].
Obtain N(x) for each segment: N = +13000 N for 0<x<700 mm, N = -2000 N for 700<x<1100 mm, N = +10000 N for 1100<x<1400 mm.
Compute axial stress as σ(x) = N(x)/A.
Report stresses by segment: σ_AB = 13000/500 = +26 MPa, σ_BC = -2000/500 = -4 MPa, σ_CD = 10000/500 = +20 MPa.
Relate strain to stress by Hooke’s law: ε(x) = σ(x)/E.
Relate strain to displacement: ε(x) = du/dx.
Compute displacement by integrating du/dx = N(x)/(AE) piecewise with continuity of u at B and C and u(0)=0.
Compute segment elongations: Δu_AB = N_AB L_AB/(AE) = 13000·700/(500·200000) = 0.091 mm, Δu_BC = -2000·400/(500·200000) = -0.008 mm, Δu_CD = 10000·300/(500·200000) = 0.030 mm.
Compute nodal displacements: u_B = 0.091 mm, u_C = 0.091-0.008 = 0.083 mm, u_D = 0.083+0.030 = 0.113 mm.
Provide the piecewise displacement field: u(x)= (13000/(AE))x for 0≤x≤700; u(x)=u_B + (-2000/(AE))(x-700) for 700≤x≤1100; u(x)=u_C + (10000/(AE))(x-1100) for 1100≤x≤1400.
Identify relevant response quantities to report: reaction R_A, internal axial force diagram N(x), stress distribution σ(x), strain distribution ε(x), displacement field u(x), and total end displacement u_D.
State that internal force is discontinuous only at point-load locations and displacement is continuous everywhere for this axial bar model.
