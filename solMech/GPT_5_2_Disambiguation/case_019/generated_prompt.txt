Assume a straight, prismatic, solid circular shaft with constant diameter d = 30 mm and length L = 1200 mm.

Evaluate the numerical internal torque values: for 0 ≤ x < 400 mm, T(x) = 0 N·mm; for 400 mm < x ≤ 1200 mm, T(x) = +200,000 N·mm.
Compute the polar moment of area for the solid circular section: J = π d^4 / 32.
