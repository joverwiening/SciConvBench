Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.
Assume the bar is prismatic with a rectangular cross section of width b = 50 mm and height h = 120 mm.
Assume the section of interest is any cross section where the internal resultants are P = 40,000 N (tension) and V = 15,000 N (transverse shear).
Assume no bending moment at the section, i.e., M = 0, because no moment is specified and only P and V are given.
Assume the axial force P is uniformly distributed over the cross-sectional area for the purpose of average normal stress.
Assume the transverse shear force V produces an average shear stress over the cross-sectional area for the purpose of average shear stress.
Compute the cross-sectional area as A = b h.
Use consistent units; with b and h in mm, A is in mm^2 and stresses computed in N/mm^2 = MPa.
Compute average normal stress at the section as σ_avg = P / A.
Compute average shear stress at the section as τ_avg = V / A.
With b = 50 mm and h = 120 mm, compute A = 50×120 = 6000 mm^2.
Compute σ_avg = 40,000 / 6000 = 6.667 N/mm^2 = 6.667 MPa (tensile).
Compute τ_avg = 15,000 / 6000 = 2.500 N/mm^2 = 2.500 MPa (in the direction of V on the section).
Report the stress state at the section in terms of average components as (σ_avg, τ_avg) = (6.667 MPa tension, 2.500 MPa shear).
