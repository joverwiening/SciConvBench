Assume small deformation, linear-elastic, homogeneous, isotropic material behavior with constant Young’s modulus E.
Assume the bar is axially loaded in tension by a static force P applied at the right end, with the left end fixed.
Assume the axial force is the same through both segments (no intermediate axial loads), so N1 = N2 = P.
Assume each segment has a uniform circular cross-section with diameter di and area Ai = (π/4) di^2.
Given P = 20,000 N, L1 = 500 mm, d1 = 30 mm, L2 = 600 mm, d2 = 25 mm, and E = 69,000 N/mm^2.
Compute areas: A1 = (π/4)(30^2) = 706.858 mm^2 and A2 = (π/4)(25^2) = 490.874 mm^2.
Compute average axial stress in each segment: σi = N/Ai = P/Ai.
Stress in segment 1: σ1 = 20,000 / 706.858 = 28.29 N/mm^2 = 28.29 MPa (tension).
Stress in segment 2: σ2 = 20,000 / 490.874 = 40.74 N/mm^2 = 40.74 MPa (tension).
Compute axial strain in each segment: εi = σi/E.
Compute elongation of each segment: δi = εi Li = (P Li)/(Ai E).
Elongation of segment 1: δ1 = (20,000·500)/(706.858·69,000) = 0.205 mm.
Elongation of segment 2: δ2 = (20,000·600)/(490.874·69,000) = 0.354 mm.
Compute total elongation as the sum of segment elongations: δtotal = δ1 + δ2.
Total elongation: δtotal = 0.205 + 0.354 = 0.559 mm.
