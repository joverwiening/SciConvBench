Assume an Euler–Bernoulli beam with small deformation, linear-elastic, homogeneous, isotropic material behavior.
Assume plane sections remain plane and normal to the neutral axis after bending, and shear deformation is neglected.
Assume the section is prismatic with constant second moment of area about the neutral axis I = 2,500,000 mm^4.
Use Young’s modulus E = 200,000 MPa = 200,000 N/mm^2.
Use the bending moment at the section of interest as M = 3,000,000 N·mm.
Assume pure bending at the section so the moment–curvature relation applies locally.
Compute curvature κ from the moment–curvature relation κ = M/(E I).
Compute flexural rigidity as E I = (200,000 N/mm^2)(2,500,000 mm^4) = 5.0×10^11 N·mm^2.
Compute curvature as κ = (3.0×10^6 N·mm)/(5.0×10^11 N·mm^2) = 6.0×10^-6 1/mm.
Compute radius of curvature as ρ = 1/κ = (E I)/M.
Compute radius of curvature as ρ = 1/(6.0×10^-6 1/mm) = 1.6667×10^5 mm = 166.7 m.
Use sign convention as needed: κ has the same sign as M under the chosen positive bending convention, and ρ is typically reported as a positive magnitude.
