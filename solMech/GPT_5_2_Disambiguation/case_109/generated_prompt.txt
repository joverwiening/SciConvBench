Assume an Euler–Bernoulli beam with small deflection, linear-elastic, prismatic member, constant E and I, and plane sections remain plane.
Assume simply supported boundary conditions: vertical deflection v(0)=0 and v(L)=0, with free rotation at both supports.
Assume a single concentrated load P acts downward at midspan x=L/2.
Neglect shear deformation and rotary inertia.
Use span length L=2500 mm, Young’s modulus E=200000 N/mm², second moment of area I=3000000 mm⁴, and load P=1000 N.

Define reactions by symmetry: R_A=R_B=P/2.
Define bending moment distribution: for 0≤x≤L/2, M(x)=R_A x=(P/2)x; for L/2≤x≤L, M(x)=R_B(L−x)=(P/2)(L−x).
Write strain energy in bending: U=∫_0^L M(x)^2/(2EI) dx.
Evaluate using symmetry: U=2∫_0^{L/2} [(P/2)x]^2/(2EI) dx.
Compute U=P^2 L^3/(96EI).

Apply Castigliano’s theorem for deflection at the load point: δ_mid = ∂U/∂P.
Obtain governing result: δ_mid = P L^3/(48EI) (downward).

Substitute values: δ_mid = (1000)(2500^3)/(48×6.0×10^11) mm.
Compute δ_mid ≈ 0.5425 mm downward.

Relevant response quantities for this case are support reactions R_A=R_B=500 N, bending moment distribution M(x) as given, strain energy U=P^2 L^3/(96EI), and midspan deflection δ_mid=PL^3/(48EI).
