Assume a straight, prismatic, hollow circular shaft with constant cross-section along the length.
Assume small deformation, linear-elastic, homogeneous, isotropic material behavior with shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.
Assume Saint-Venant torsion with no warping restraint effects and no stress concentrations.
Assume the applied torque is constant along the shaft: T = 300000 N·mm.
Use shaft length L = 1000 mm.
Use outer diameter do = 40 mm (ro = 20 mm) and inner diameter di = 30 mm (ri = 15 mm).
Compute the polar second moment of area for a hollow circular section as J = (π/32)(do^4 − di^4) = (π/2)(ro^4 − ri^4).
Compute the shear stress distribution under torsion as τ(r) = T r / J for ri ≤ r ≤ ro.
Report the maximum shear stress at the outer surface as τmax = τ(ro) = T ro / J.
Compute the twist rate as dθ/dx = T / (G J).
Report the angle of twist over the full length as θ = (T L) / (G J).
Use consistent N–mm–MPa units so that θ is obtained in radians and τ in MPa (since 1 MPa = 1 N/mm^2).
