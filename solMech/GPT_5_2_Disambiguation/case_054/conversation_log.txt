Q: What is the bar’s cross-sectional area function A(x) along its length (e.g., linear from A0 at x=0 to A1 at x=L)?
A: The area varies linearly as:

\[
A(x)=A_0\left(1+k\frac{x}{L}\right),
\]

with \(x\) measured from the left end, \(A_0=250\ \text{mm}^2\), \(k=0.6\), and \(L=800\ \text{mm}\).

So \(A(0)=250\ \text{mm}^2\) and \(A(L)=250(1+0.6)=400\ \text{mm}^2\).
