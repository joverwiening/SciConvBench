Assume the rod is straight, prismatic, homogeneous, isotropic, and linearly elastic.
Assume the axial load is concentric (no bending) and the stress is uniformly distributed over the cross-section.
Assume small deformation so that engineering strain applies and the cross-sectional area and length are unchanged for the calculations.
Use the given diameter d = 30 mm and cross-sectional area A = π d^2 / 4 = 706.9 mm^2.
Use the given gage length L = 800 mm.
Use the given axial tensile force P = 15,000 N.
Use the given Young’s modulus E = 69,000 MPa and yield strength Sy = 275 MPa.
Compute average normal stress as σavg = P / A.
Convert P to consistent units with A: P = 15,000 N and 1 MPa = 1 N/mm^2.
Compute σavg = 15,000 / 706.9 = 21.23 MPa (tension).
Compute average axial strain as εavg = σavg / E.
Compute εavg = 21.23 / 69,000 = 3.08×10^-4.
Compute elongation as δ = εavg L = (P L) / (A E).
Compute δ = (3.08×10^-4)(800 mm) = 0.246 mm.
Evaluate factor of safety against yielding as n = Sy / σavg.
Compute n = 275 / 21.23 = 12.95.
Conclude the rod remains elastic under the given load because σavg << Sy and n ≈ 13.
