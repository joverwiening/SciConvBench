Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic, homogeneous, isotropic material behavior.
Assume the shaft is prismatic, solid circular, and loaded by a constant axial force P and a constant torque T.
Use diameter d = 25 mm and outer radius c = r = 12.5 mm.
Use axial force P = +25,000 N (tension positive).
Use torque magnitude T = 300,000 N·mm and take the positive torque sense such that the outer-surface shear stress magnitude is reported as positive.
Assume typical steel properties if strains are needed: E = 200,000 MPa and ν = 0.30.
Assume Saint-Venant torsion (no warping restraint effects) and neglect stress concentrations at load introduction.
Assume plane stress at the outer surface point used for principal stress evaluation: σx = axial normal stress, σy = 0, τxy = torsional shear stress.

Compute cross-sectional properties for a solid circular shaft.
Compute area A = (π/4)d^2.
Compute polar moment of inertia J = (π/32)d^4.

Compute axial (normal) stress due to P.
Compute σ = P/A and take σ > 0 in tension.

Compute torsional shear stress at the outer surface due to T.
Compute τ = Tc/J with c = d/2.

Form the combined stress state at the outer surface point for principal stress calculations.
Use σx = σ, σy = 0, τxy = τ.

Compute principal stresses at that point.
Compute σ1,2 = (σx + σy)/2 ± sqrt(((σx − σy)/2)^2 + τxy^2).
With σy = 0, compute σ1,2 = σ/2 ± sqrt((σ/2)^2 + τ^2.

Compute maximum in-plane shear stress at that point.
Compute τmax,in-plane = sqrt(((σx − σy)/2)^2 + τxy^2).
With σy = 0, compute τmax,in-plane = sqrt((σ/2)^2 + τ^2.

Compute principal strains at that point if requested.
Use plane-stress Hooke’s law: εx = (1/E)(σx − νσy), εy = (1/E)(σy − νσx), γxy = τxy/G.
Use shear modulus G = E/[2(1 + ν)].
Compute principal strains ε1,2 = (εx + εy)/2 ± sqrt(((εx − εy)/2)^2 + (γxy/2)^2).

Optionally compute torsional angle of twist if length is needed.
If twist is requested, assume a representative length L = 1,000 mm unless otherwise specified.
Compute twist per unit length θ′ = T/(JG) and total twist θ = TL/(JG).
