Model the adhesive as a homogeneous, isotropic, linear-elastic layer undergoing small-deformation uniform simple shear.
Assume the two faces remain parallel with no rotation, warping, or peel/normal stress components.
Use consistent N–mm–MPa units with A in mm^2, t in mm, G in MPa (N/mm^2), V in N.
Given planform area A = 3000 mm^2, thickness t = 8 mm, applied in-plane shear force V = 12,000 N, and shear modulus G = 39,179.1 MPa.
Compute average shear stress as τ_avg = V/A.
Numerically, τ_avg = 12,000/3000 = 4 MPa.
Relate shear stress and shear strain by linear elasticity: τ = G γ.
Compute average shear strain as γ_avg = τ_avg/G.
Numerically, γ_avg = 4/39,179.1 = 1.02197×10^-4 (dimensionless).
Relate shear strain to relative in-plane displacement between faces by γ = Δ/t.
Compute relative displacement as Δ = γ_avg t.
Numerically, Δ = (1.02197×10^-4)(8) = 8.1758×10^-4 mm = 8.1758×10^-7 m.
Report the relevant response quantities as τ_avg = 4 MPa, γ_avg = 1.02197×10^-4, and Δ = 8.1758×10^-4 mm.
