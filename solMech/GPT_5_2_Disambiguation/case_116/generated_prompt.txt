Assume a straight, prismatic, hollow circular shaft with constant cross-section along the length.
Assume small deformation, linear-elastic, isotropic material behavior with shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.
Assume pure Saint-Venant torsion under a constant applied torque T = 600000 N·mm with no transverse shear force and no stress concentrations.
Use length L = 800 mm.
Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Use outer diameter do = 50 mm and inner diameter di = 44 mm.
Compute polar moment of inertia as J = π(do^4 − di^4)/32.
Compute outer radius as ro = do/2.
Compute maximum torsional shear stress at the outer surface as τmax = T·ro/J, reported in MPa.
Compute angle of twist over the full length as θ = T·L/(J·G), reported in radians (and optionally converted to degrees using degrees = radians·180/π).
For completeness, the torsional shear stress distribution through the wall is τ(r) = T·r/J for di/2 ≤ r ≤ do/2.
