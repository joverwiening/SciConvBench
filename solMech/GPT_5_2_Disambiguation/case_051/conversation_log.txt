Q: Which beam theory should I use to compute transverse shear stress: Euler–Bernoulli (with shear stress from VQ/It) or Timoshenko (including shear deformation with a shear correction factor)?
A: Use the standard shear-stress distribution from elementary beam theory via the shear formula:

- Use **τ = VQ/(I b)** (Euler–Bernoulli-style stress recovery).
- For the maximum shear in a rectangle, use the given relation **τ_max = 1.5 V/A**.

Do **not** use Timoshenko shear deformation or a separate shear correction factor beyond the provided **1.5** factor.
