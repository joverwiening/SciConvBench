Model the adhesive bondline as a homogeneous, isotropic, linear-elastic shear layer of uniform thickness t loaded in uniform in-plane shear.
Assume small strains and small deformations.
Assume the shear stress is uniformly distributed over the loaded planform area A (ignore edge effects and stress concentrations).
Assume the adherends are rigid relative to the adhesive so the relative in-plane displacement occurs across the adhesive thickness t.
Use A = 4000 mm^2, t = 20 mm, V = 12000 N, and G = 39179.1 MPa = 39179.1 N/mm^2.
Compute the average shear stress in the adhesive as τ_avg = V/A.
Evaluate τ_avg = 12000 N / 4000 mm^2 = 3 N/mm^2 = 3 MPa.
Relate shear stress and shear strain by linear elasticity as τ = G γ.
Compute the average shear strain as γ_avg = τ_avg / G.
Evaluate γ_avg = 3 MPa / 39179.1 MPa = 7.657×10^-5.
Relate shear strain to shear deformation (relative in-plane displacement between the two faces) by γ = δ/t.
Compute the relative shear displacement as δ_avg = γ_avg t.
Evaluate δ_avg = (7.657×10^-5)(20 mm) = 1.531×10^-3 mm.
State the shear compliance form equivalently as δ_avg = (V t)/(A G).
State the shear stiffness form equivalently as V = (A G/t) δ_avg, with shear stiffness k_s = A G/t.
Evaluate k_s = (4000 mm^2)(39179.1 N/mm^2)/(20 mm) = 7.83582×10^6 N/mm.
Report the relevant response quantities as τ_avg = 3 MPa, γ_avg = 7.657×10^-5, and δ_avg = 1.531×10^-3 mm under the stated assumptions.
