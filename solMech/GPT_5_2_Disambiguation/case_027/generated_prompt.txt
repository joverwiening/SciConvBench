Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic axial response of a uniform prismatic rod.
Assume quasi-static energy balance at maximum extension (ignore wave propagation and inertia of the rod).
Assume the rod’s self-weight is negligible.
Assume only the dropped weight W contributes to the impact energy.
Use consistent units: N and mm, with E in N/mm^2 (MPa).
Given W = 800 N.
Given h = 150 mm.
Given L = 1500 mm.
Given d = 16 mm.
Compute cross-sectional area as A = (pi d^2)/4.
Use E = 200000 MPa = 200000 N/mm^2.
Use the energy relation exactly: W (h + δ_max) = (P_max^2 L)/(2 A E).
Use the axial deformation relation exactly: δ_max = (P_max L)/(A E).
Substitute δ_max into the energy relation to obtain: W (h + (P_max L)/(A E)) = (P_max^2 L)/(2 A E).
Rearrange into a quadratic in P_max: P_max^2 - 2 W P_max - (2 A E W h)/L = 0.
Solve for the physically admissible (positive) root: P_max = W + sqrt(W^2 + (2 A E W h)/L).
Compute maximum normal stress as σ_max = P_max / A.
