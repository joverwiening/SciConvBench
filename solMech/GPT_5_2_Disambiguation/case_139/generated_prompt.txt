Use small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Model the member as a short prismatic bar with a constant rectangular cross section.
Use cross-sectional dimensions b = 30 mm and h = 100 mm.
Use cross-sectional area A = b h = 3000 mm^2.
Evaluate stresses at the specified cross section where the internal resultants are P = +50,000 N (tension), V = 5,000 N (transverse shear), and M = 0 (no bending at that section).
Assume Saint-Venant type stress resultants apply so that average stresses over the cross section are meaningful.
Assume the axial force produces a uniform normal stress distribution over the cross section because M = 0 and the load is concentric (no eccentricity).
Assume the requested “average shear stress at a section” is the cross-section average based on the shear resultant V, not the maximum or parabolic distribution value.
Compute average normal stress from axial force as σ_avg = P / A.
Compute average shear stress from transverse shear force as τ_avg = V / A.
Use consistent units; with N and mm^2, stresses are in N/mm^2 = MPa.
Report σ_avg = 50,000 / 3000 = 16.67 MPa (tensile).
Report τ_avg = 5,000 / 3000 = 1.67 MPa (average transverse shear).
