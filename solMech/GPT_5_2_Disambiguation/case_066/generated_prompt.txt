Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic, homogeneous, isotropic material behavior.
Assume Saint-Venant torsion of a straight, prismatic, solid circular shaft.
Assume plane cross-sections remain plane and circular (no warping for a circular shaft) and the twist is uniform along the length.
Assume the applied torque is constant along the entire shaft length: T = 400,000 N·mm.
Use consistent N–mm units with stress in MPa (N/mm²): L = 1000 mm, d = 45 mm, G = 76,923.1 MPa.

Compute the polar second moment of area for a solid circular shaft as J = (π d^4)/32.
Compute the radius as c = d/2.
Compute the shear stress distribution under torsion as τ(r) = (T r)/J for 0 ≤ r ≤ c.
Compute the maximum shear stress at the outer surface as τ_max = (T c)/J = (16 T)/(π d^3).
Compute the angle of twist over length L as φ = (T L)/(J G) (radians).
Compute the twist rate as dφ/dx = T/(J G) (radians per mm).
