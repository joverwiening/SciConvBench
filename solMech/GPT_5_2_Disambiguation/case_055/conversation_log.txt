Q: What is the bar’s cross-sectional area function A(x) along its length (e.g., linear form with endpoint areas A0 at x=0 and AL at x=L)?
A: The cross-sectional area varies linearly as:

\[
A(x)=A_0\left(1+k\frac{x}{L}\right)
\]

with \(x\) measured from the left end, \(A_0 = 200\ \text{mm}^2\), \(k = 0.6\), and \(L = 1200\ \text{mm}\).

So numerically:

\[
A(x)=200\left(1+0.6\frac{x}{1200}\right)\ \text{mm}^2
\]

This gives \(A(0)=200\ \text{mm}^2\) and \(A(L)=200(1+0.6)=320\ \text{mm}^2\).
