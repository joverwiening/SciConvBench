Assume a straight, prismatic, hollow circular shaft with constant cross-section.
Assume small deformation, linear-elastic, homogeneous, isotropic material behavior.
Assume Saint-Venant torsion with uniform torque T along the length and no stress concentrations.
Assume the shaft is free of warping restraint effects (classical torsion of circular shafts).
Use N–mm–MPa units consistently.
Given L = 1200 mm.
Given T = 600000 N·mm.
Given G = 77519.4 MPa (i.e., N/mm^2).
Given outer diameter d_o = 60 mm and inner diameter d_i = 44 mm.
Compute polar moment of inertia using J = π(d_o^4 − d_i^4)/32.
Compute outer radius r_o = d_o/2.
Compute maximum shear stress at the outer surface using τ_max = T r_o / J.
Compute angle of twist over the full length using θ = T L / (J G).
Report τ_max in MPa.
Report θ in radians (and optionally also in degrees using θ_deg = θ·180/π).
