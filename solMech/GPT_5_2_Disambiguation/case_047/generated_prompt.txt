Use small-deformation, linear-elastic, homogeneous, isotropic material behavior.
Model the member as a straight, prismatic, circular rod loaded in concentric axial tension only (no bending, no torsion).
Neglect stress concentrations, residual stresses, self-weight, and temperature effects.
Use diameter d = 12 mm.
Use cross-sectional area A = (π d^2)/4 = 36π = 113.10 mm^2.
Use gage length L = 800 mm.
Use applied axial tensile force P = 15000 N applied through the centroid.
Use Young’s modulus E = 200000 MPa (200000 N/mm^2).
Use yield strength Sy = 350 MPa for yielding-based factor of safety.
Compute average normal stress as σavg = P/A.
Compute σavg = 15000 N / 113.10 mm^2 = 132.6 MPa (tension).
Compute average axial strain as εavg = σavg/E.
Compute εavg = 132.6 MPa / 200000 MPa = 6.63×10^-4.
Compute elongation as δ = εavg L = (P L)/(A E).
Compute δ = (6.63×10^-4)(800 mm) = 0.530 mm.
Evaluate factor of safety against yielding as ny = Sy/σavg.
Compute ny = 350 MPa / 132.6 MPa = 2.64.
If a failure-based factor of safety is required using ultimate strength, assume a typical AISI 1020 ultimate strength Sut ≈ 420 MPa (engineering handbook typical value; actual depends on condition).
Compute ultimate-based factor of safety as nu = Sut/σavg.
Compute nu = 420 MPa / 132.6 MPa = 3.17.
