Assume a straight, prismatic, circular rod loaded axially through its centroid so the stress state is uniform uniaxial normal stress (no bending, no torsion).
Assume small deformation, linear-elastic, homogeneous, isotropic material behavior.
Assume end effects and stress concentrations are neglected so average stress equals nominal stress.
Given diameter d = 20 mm.
Given gage length L = 800 mm.
Given tensile axial force P = 15,000 N.
Compute cross-sectional area as A = (π/4)d^2.
Compute average normal stress as σ_avg = P/A (tension taken as positive).
Compute axial normal strain as ε = σ_avg/E.
Compute elongation as δ = εL = (PL)/(AE).
Use yielding criterion based on uniaxial yield strength Sy = 275 MPa.
Define factor of safety against yielding as n = Sy/σ_avg.
Report σ_avg in MPa, ε as dimensionless (or microstrain), δ in mm, and n as dimensionless.
Note that E (Young’s modulus) is required to compute ε and δ; if E is not provided, leave ε and δ in symbolic form in terms of E.
