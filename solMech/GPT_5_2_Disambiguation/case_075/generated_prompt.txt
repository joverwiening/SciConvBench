Model the adhesive as a homogeneous, isotropic, linear-elastic solid under small deformation.
Treat the adhesive layer as a rectangular bonded layer loaded in uniform in-plane shear (simple shear) across its thickness.
Use the given planform (bonded) area A = 4000 mm^2 and thickness t = 20 mm.
Use the applied in-plane shear force V = 8000 N acting parallel to the bonded faces.
Assume the shear traction is uniformly distributed over the planform area (no edge effects, no peel/normal stress, no bending, no stress concentrations).
Assume perfect bonding at both faces so the relative in-plane displacement between faces equals the adhesive shear deformation.
Compute average shear stress as τ = V/A.
Compute engineering shear strain as γ = τ/G.
Use the constitutive relation for isotropic linear elasticity in shear: τ = Gγ.
Compute relative shear displacement (shear deformation) between the bonded faces as δ = γ t.
Equivalently, δ = (τ/G) t = (V t)/(A G).
If needed, compute shear compliance per unit area as (δ/V) = t/(A G).
