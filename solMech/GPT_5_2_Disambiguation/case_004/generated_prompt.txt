Use small-deformation, linear-elastic, 1D axial bar theory (Saint-Venant), with constant E and constant cross-sectional area A.
Model the bar as prismatic with total length L = 1500 mm and segments AB = 500 mm, BC = 600 mm, CD = 400 mm.
Use A = 300 mm^2 constant and E = 200000 MPa constant.
Use the global x-axis along the bar from A (x = 0) to D (x = 1500 mm), with positive to the right.
Apply boundary condition u(A) = 0 (fixed at A).
Apply axial point loads: at B (x = 500 mm) P_B = +18000 N, at C (x = 1100 mm) P_C = −12000 N, at D (x = 1500 mm) P_D = +16000 N.
Adopt internal axial force sign convention N(x) > 0 in tension.
Enforce global axial equilibrium to determine the support reaction at A: R_A + P_B + P_C + P_D = 0.
Compute the internal axial force as piecewise constant between point loads using a cut and equilibrium of the left segment: N(x) = −(sum of external axial forces to the left of the cut, including R_A and any point loads passed).
Report N(x) on each segment: N_AB for 0 < x < 500 mm, N_BC for 500 < x < 1100 mm, N_CD for 1100 < x < 1500 mm.
Compute axial stress as piecewise constant: σ(x) = N(x) / A, reported per segment as σ_AB, σ_BC, σ_CD in MPa (since 1 MPa = 1 N/mm^2).
Compute axial strain as piecewise constant: ε(x) = σ(x) / E = N(x) / (A E), reported per segment.
Compute displacement field from strain: du/dx = ε(x), integrated piecewise with continuity of displacement at B and C and with u(A) = 0.
Report nodal displacements u_A, u_B, u_C, u_D in mm.
Compute segment elongations/shortenings: δ_AB = N_AB L_AB/(A E), δ_BC = N_BC L_BC/(A E), δ_CD = N_CD L_CD/(A E), with sign consistent with N (positive elongation in tension).
Verify compatibility: u_B = δ_AB, u_C = δ_AB + δ_BC, u_D = δ_AB + δ_BC + δ_CD.
Optionally report the axial stiffness of each segment k_i = A E / L_i (units N/mm) and the equivalent compliance contributions L_i/(A E).
Optionally report strain energy per segment U_i = N_i^2 L_i/(2 A E) and total U = ΣU_i.
Use mixed engineering units consistently: forces in N, lengths/displacements in mm, stresses/modulus in MPa (N/mm^2), strains dimensionless.
