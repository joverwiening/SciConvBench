Assume a straight, prismatic, hollow circular shaft with constant outer diameter d_o and inner diameter d_i along the full length L.
Assume small deformation, linear-elastic, homogeneous, isotropic material behavior with shear modulus G.
Assume Saint-Venant torsion with uniform applied torque T, no warping restraint effects, and no stress concentrations.
Assume the shaft is loaded in pure torsion only for the reported quantities (ignore any additional transverse shear loading for these torsion results).
Use consistent units: N and mm, so stresses are in MPa (N/mm^2) and angles are in radians.

Given: T = 450000 N·mm.
Given: L = 1200 mm.
Given: d_o = 50 mm.
Given: d_i = 42 mm.
Given: G = 77519.4 MPa.
Use polar moment of inertia: J = π(d_o^4 − d_i^4)/32.
Outer radius: r_o = d_o/2.

Governing shear stress distribution under torsion:
τ(r) = T r / J for d_i/2 ≤ r ≤ d_o/2.
Maximum shear stress occurs at the outer surface r = r_o:
τ_max = T r_o / J.

Governing angle of twist over length L:
θ = T L / (J G).

Compute geometry:
r_o = 25 mm.
J = π(50^4 − 42^4)/32 = 2.671e5 mm^4 (approximately).

Response quantity 1 (maximum shear stress at outer surface):
τ_max = (450000)(25)/(2.671e5) = 42.12 MPa (approximately).

Response quantity 2 (angle of twist over full length L = 1200 mm):
θ = (450000)(1200)/((2.671e5)(77519.4)) = 0.02607 rad (approximately).
θ = 1.494 degrees (approximately).
