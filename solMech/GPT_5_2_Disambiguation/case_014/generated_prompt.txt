Model the member as an Euler–Bernoulli cantilever beam of length L = 1200 mm with constant flexural rigidity EI = 5.0×10^11 N·mm^2.
Assume small deformation, small rotation, linear-elastic material behavior, and plane sections remain plane and normal to the neutral axis (no shear deformation).
Assume prismatic beam, constant E and I along the span, and quasi-static loading.
Define x measured from the fixed end (x = 0) to the free end (x = L).
Apply a concentrated transverse load P = 800 N downward at the free end x = L.
Use the sign convention that downward deflection v(x) is negative, positive bending moment produces concave-up curvature, and relate curvature to moment by EI v''(x) = M(x).
Use cantilever boundary conditions v(0) = 0 and v'(0) = 0 at the fixed end.
Use free-end conditions consistent with the applied end force and no applied end moment: internal bending moment at x = L is M(L) = 0 and internal shear at x = L is V(L) = −P (with sign consistent with the chosen convention).
Write internal shear and moment for 0 ≤ x ≤ L as V(x) = −P and M(x) = −P(L − x).
Compute slope and deflection by integrating EI v''(x) = M(x) twice and applying v(0) = 0 and v'(0) = 0.
Obtain slope distribution v'(x) = −(P/(EI)) (Lx − x^2/2).
Obtain deflection distribution v(x) = −(P/(EI)) (L x^2/2 − x^3/6).
Report the free-end slope v'(L) = −P L^2/(2EI).
Report the free-end deflection v(L) = −P L^3/(3EI).
Compute numerical free-end deflection v(L) = −(800)(1200^3)/(3·5.0×10^11) mm = −0.9216 mm.
Compute numerical free-end slope v'(L) = −(800)(1200^2)/(2·5.0×10^11) rad = −0.001152 rad.
Report maximum bending moment magnitude at the fixed end as |M(0)| = P L = 9.60×10^5 N·mm.
Report constant shear magnitude along the span as |V| = P = 800 N.
State that maximum curvature magnitude occurs at the fixed end and equals |v''(0)| = |M(0)|/(EI) = (P L)/(EI).
