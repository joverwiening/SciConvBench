Assume linear-elastic, isotropic material behavior.
The rod has a circular cross-section with an initial diameter of 20 mm.
The axial tensile load applied to the rod is 25,000 N.
There is no lateral load applied to the rod.
The rod is made of brass with Young's modulus E = 105000 MPa and Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.34.
The material response is governed by Hooke’s law for uniaxial stress.
The axial stress is calculated as the axial load divided by the cross-sectional area.
The axial strain is calculated as the axial stress divided by Young’s modulus.
The lateral strain is related to the axial strain through the Poisson effect: lateral strain = -ν × axial strain.
The change in diameter is calculated from the lateral strain and the initial diameter.
Deformations are small and within the elastic limit.
Geometric linearity is assumed throughout the analysis.
