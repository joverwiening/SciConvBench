Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The bar is vertical and subjected to axial loading due to its own weight.
The length of the vertical bar is 3000 mm.
The cross-sectional area of the bar is 300 mm².
The material density is 8900 kg/m³.
The elastic modulus (Young's modulus) is 69000 MPa.
Acceleration due to gravity is 9.81 m/s².
The elongation is calculated by integrating the strain over the length of the bar.
The stress at any point in the bar is due to the weight of the material below that point.
The strain at any point is linearly related to stress via Hooke's law.
The total elongation is given by (ρ g L²) / (2 E), where ρ is density, g is gravity, L is length, and E is elastic modulus.
The primary response quantity is the elongation at the free end of the vertical bar.
