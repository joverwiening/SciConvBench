Use structural steel with a yield strength of 250 MPa.  
Assume linear-elastic, isotropic material behavior under small deformations.  
Assume the rod is prismatic with a circular cross-section of diameter 25 mm.  
Assume the axial load is applied concentrically along the longitudinal axis of the rod.  
The cross-sectional area is calculated as A = π × (25 mm)² / 4.  
The average normal stress is calculated as σ = P / A, where P = 20000 N.  
The axial strain is calculated as ε = σ / E, assuming a Young’s modulus E = 200 GPa for structural steel.  
The elongation is calculated as δ = (P × L) / (A × E), where L is the length of the rod (to be specified).  
The factor of safety with respect to yielding is calculated as FS = σ_yield / σ.  
Report the average normal stress, strain, elongation (for a given length L), and factor of safety.  
State that elongation depends on rod length and provide formula for δ.
