Assume small-deformations and linear-elastic material behavior.  
The rod has a circular cross-sectional geometry.  
The material is isotropic with a Young's modulus of 105000 MPa.  
The material has a Poisson's ratio of 0.34.  
The rod is subjected to both axial and lateral loads.  
Focus on the Poisson effect under uniaxial tension.  
The Poisson effect causes lateral contraction when the rod is under axial tension.  
Axial strain is calculated as the axial stress divided by the Young's modulus.  
Lateral strain is calculated as the negative of Poisson's ratio multiplied by the axial strain.  
The governing equations are Hooke's law for uniaxial stress: ε_axial = σ_axial / E.  
The lateral strain due to Poisson effect is ε_lateral = -ν * ε_axial.  
Stresses are assumed to be within the elastic limit.  
The cross-section remains plane and circular during deformation.  
Radial displacement due to lateral strain is uniform around the circumference.
