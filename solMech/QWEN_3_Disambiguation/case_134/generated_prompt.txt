The structural member is a rectangular adhesive layer with cross-sectional area A = 3000 mm² and thickness t = 8 mm.  
The adhesive layer is subjected to a uniform in-plane shear force of 12000 N.  
The shear modulus of the adhesive material is G = 39179.1 MPa.  
Small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior is assumed.  
The loading is pure uniform in-plane shear with no slip at the interfaces.  
The average shear stress τ is calculated as the applied shear force divided by the bonded area.  
The average shear strain γ is calculated as the ratio of shear stress to shear modulus.  
The shear deformation δ is calculated as the product of shear strain and thickness of the adhesive layer.  
The governing relation for average shear stress is τ = V / A, where V is the shear force and A is the area.  
The governing relation for shear strain is γ = τ / G.  
The governing relation for shear deformation is δ = γ × t.
