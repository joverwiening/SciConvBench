Use small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.  
Assume the beam is simply supported with maximum bending moment at midspan.  
The maximum bending moment is 3,000,000 N·mm.  
The allowable bending stress is 90 MPa.  
The beam has a rectangular cross-section with width 40 mm.  
The required beam height is determined by the bending stress formula σ = M * c / I.  
For a rectangular section, c = h / 2 and I = (b * h³) / 12.  
Substitute b = 40 mm, M = 3,000,000 N·mm, and σ_allow = 90 MPa.  
Solve for minimum height h such that σ ≤ σ_allow.  
The required height h is approximately 100 mm.
