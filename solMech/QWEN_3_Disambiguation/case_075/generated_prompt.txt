The structural member is a rectangular adhesive layer with a thickness of t = 20 mm and an area of A = 4000 mm².  
Assume the adhesive layer has a width b = 63.25 mm and length l = 63.25 mm, derived from the area A = 4000 mm², assuming a square planform for simplicity and uniform shear stress distribution.  
The applied shear load is a uniform in-plane shear force of magnitude V = 8000 N, acting parallel to the faces of the adhesive layer.  
The shear deformation occurs across the thickness of the adhesive layer due to the applied in-plane shear force.  
The shear stress τ is calculated as τ = V / A.  
The shear strain γ is determined using Hooke’s law for linear elasticity: γ = τ / G.  
The shear modulus G is given as 39179.1 MPa.  
The total shear deformation δ is calculated as δ = γ × t.  
Small-deformation theory and linear-elastic behavior are assumed.  
The adhesive layer is homogeneous, isotropic, and perfectly bonded to the substrates.  
No peel or normal stresses are considered; loading is pure in-plane shear.  
The response quantities of interest are shear stress, shear strain, and total shear deformation across the thickness.
