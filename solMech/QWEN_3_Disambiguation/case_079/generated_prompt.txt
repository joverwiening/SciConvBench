Use small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics theory.  
Assume the shaft is homogeneous, isotropic, and prismatic with a circular cross-section.  
Assume pure torsion with no axial or bending loads.  
The maximum allowable angle of twist is 1.5° over a length of 800 mm.  
The applied torque is 600,000 N·mm.  
The shear modulus G of AISI 1020 steel is 76923.1 MPa.  
Use the torsion formula for angle of twist: θ = (T * L) / (G * J), where θ is in radians.  
Convert 1.5° to radians: θ = 1.5 × π / 180 = 0.02618 rad.  
Polar moment of inertia J = (π * d⁴) / 32 for a solid circular shaft.  
Solve for diameter d such that the angle of twist does not exceed 0.02618 rad.  
Rearrange the equation: d⁴ = (32 * T * L) / (π * G * θ).  
Substitute T = 600,000 N·mm, L = 800 mm, G = 76923.1 MPa, θ = 0.02618 rad.  
Calculate the minimum required shaft diameter d.  
Round the diameter to the nearest standard engineering size if necessary.
