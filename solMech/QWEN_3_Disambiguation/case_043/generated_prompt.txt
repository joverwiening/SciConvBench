Use linear-elastic material behavior with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Assume small deformations and perfect alignment of the axial load.
The column has a circular cross-section with diameter d = 20 mm.
The length of the column L is 2000 mm.
The effective length factor K is 1.0, corresponding to pinned-pinned end conditions.
The effective buckling length is K × L = 1.0 × 2000 mm = 2000 mm.
Compute the second moment of area I for the circular cross-section: I = π × (d⁴) / 64.
Compute the Euler buckling load: P_cr = (π² × E × I) / (K × L)².
Apply a factor of safety of 2.0 to the Euler buckling load.
The allowable column load is P_allowable = P_cr / 2.0.
The factor of safety with respect to buckling is defined as FS = P_cr / P_applied, where P_applied is the actual axial load (if provided); otherwise, it is evaluated as 2.0 per requirement.
