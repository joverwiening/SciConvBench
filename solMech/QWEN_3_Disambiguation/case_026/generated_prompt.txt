Assume a rectangular cross-section with a width of 50 mm and a height of 100 mm, as this is a common and practical choice for analytical beam problems when no specific geometry is provided.

 
Perform a linear-elastic static analysis of a cantilever beam under small deformations.
The beam has a length of 1500 mm and a rectangular cross-section with a width of 50 mm and a height of 100 mm.
The beam is made of AISI 1020 steel with a Young's modulus of 200000 MPa.
The beam is fixed at one end and free at the other.
A downward point load of 500 N is applied at the free end.
No bending moment is applied at the free end.
Use Castigliano’s theorem to determine the vertical deflection at the free end.
The second moment of area is calculated about the neutral axis corresponding to bending in the vertical plane.
The deflection is due solely to the point load and the resulting bending moment distribution.
Shear deformation is neglected.
The response quantity of interest is the vertical deflection at the free end.
