Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.  
The structural member is a rectangular adhesive layer of thickness t = 20 mm and area A = 4000 mm².  
The in-plane dimensions (length and width) are arbitrary as long as their product is 4000 mm².  
The adhesive layer is isotropic with a shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.  
A uniform in-plane shear force V = 12000 N is applied parallel to the plane of the adhesive layer.  
The shear stress τ is calculated as τ = V / A.  
The shear strain γ is calculated as γ = τ / G.  
The shear deformation (average angular distortion) is γ, in radians.  
The response quantities of interest are shear stress, shear strain, and shear deformation.  
The adhesive layer deforms under pure shear, with no normal stresses or strains considered.  
Bonded faces undergo relative in-plane displacement due to shear.  
The deformation is uniform across the thickness and area of the adhesive layer.  
Edge effects and stress concentrations are neglected.  
The material response is linear-elastic and reversible.
