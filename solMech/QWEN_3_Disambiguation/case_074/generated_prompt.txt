Use linear-elastic, small-deformation solid mechanics.  
Assume the beam is simply supported with a rectangular cross-section.  
The maximum bending moment is 6,000,000 N·mm.  
The allowable bending stress is 170 MPa.  
The beam width is 60 mm.  
The cross-section is rectangular, so the section modulus is (width × height²) / 6.  
The bending stress is calculated as σ = M / S, where M is the bending moment and S is the section modulus.  
Set σ equal to the allowable bending stress to solve for the minimum required height.  
Solve for height using 170 = 6,000,000 / ((60 × h²) / 6).  
The required beam height is approximately 200 mm.
