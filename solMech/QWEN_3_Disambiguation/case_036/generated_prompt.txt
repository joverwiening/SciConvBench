The moment of inertia I for a solid circular cross section is I = (π × d⁴) / 64, where d is the diameter.  
The design must ensure that the applied load divided by the critical buckling load does not exceed 1/FOS.  
Solve for the minimum required diameter d such that FOS = P_cr / P_applied ≥ 2.0.  
The final design diameter must be reported in millimeters.
