Use N–mm–MPa units. Assume Euler buckling and a solid circular cross section.

A column of length L = 1800 mm made of structural steel (E = 200000 MPa) must support a compressive load of P = 30000 N with a factor of safety n = 2.0 against buckling. The effective length factor is K = 1.0.

Determine the minimum required diameter d for a solid circular column using
P_cr = π^2 E I/(K L)^2, I = π d^4/64, and require P_cr ≥ n·P.
