Use AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa and coefficient of thermal expansion α = 2e-05 1/°C.  
Assume the rod is prismatic with a circular cross-section of diameter 20 mm, giving a cross-sectional area A = 314.16 mm².  
Assume small deformations and linear-elastic material behavior.  
Assume the temperature change is uniform along the rod with ΔT = +50 °C.  
Assume the rod is fully restrained axially at both ends by rigid supports.  
Thermal strain ε_thermal is given by ε_thermal = α × ΔT.  
Since axial deformation is fully restrained, mechanical strain ε_mechanical = 0.  
Total strain ε_total = ε_thermal + ε_mechanical = α × ΔT.  
The stress developed in the rod is σ = -E × α × ΔT (compressive due to restrained expansion).  
The axial force developed is F = σ × A = -E × α × ΔT × A.  
No external axial load is applied; the axial force arises solely from thermal constraint.  
Governing equations are based on linear thermoelasticity with full kinematic restraint.
