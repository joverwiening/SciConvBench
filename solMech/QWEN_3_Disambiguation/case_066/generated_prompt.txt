Assume the shaft is a solid circular shaft with a diameter of 45 mm and a length of 1000 mm.
The shaft is subjected to a torque of 400000 N·mm.
Neglect shear loading, as it is not specified; assume the primary loading is torsional.
The material is AISI 1020 steel with a shear modulus G of 76923.1 MPa.
Calculate the maximum shear stress using the formula τ = (T * r) / J, where T is the torque, r is the radius of the shaft, and J is the polar moment of inertia.
Compute J using J = (π * d⁴) / 32, where d is the diameter of the shaft.
Determine the maximum shear stress at the outer surface of the shaft.
Calculate the angle of twist using θ = (T * L) / (G * J), where L is the length of the shaft.
Express the angle of twist in radians.
All deformations are assumed to be within the linear-elastic range.
Small-deformation theory applies.
