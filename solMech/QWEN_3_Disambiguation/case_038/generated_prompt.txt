Use linear-elastic, small-deformation solid mechanics.
Consider a simply supported beam of length 3500 mm.
Apply a uniformly distributed transverse load of magnitude 0.5 N/mm acting downward (negative y-direction).
Assume the beam is prismatic with constant cross-sectional properties.
Assume the material is homogeneous and isotropic with known Young's modulus E and moment of inertia I.
The governing differential equation for beam deflection is E*I*d⁴v/dx⁴ = w, where w = 0.5 N/mm.
The boundary conditions are: deflection v = 0 at both supports (x = 0 and x = 3500 mm), and bending moment = 0 at both supports (d²v/dx² = 0 at x = 0 and x = 3500 mm).
Compute the deflection v(x) along the beam span.
Compute the slope dv/dx(x) along the beam span.
Report maximum deflection and its location.
Report maximum slope and its location.
