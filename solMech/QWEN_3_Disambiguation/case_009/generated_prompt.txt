Assume small-deformations and linear-elastic behavior.  
The composite bar system consists of two vertical bars in parallel between two rigid plates.  
Bar 1 is made of AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E1 = 200000 MPa and cross-sectional area A1 = 300 mm².  
Bar 2 is made of cast iron with Young's modulus E2 = 110000 MPa and cross-sectional area A2 = 250 mm².  
A tensile load of 20,000 N is applied axially to the top rigid plate.  
The bottom rigid plate is fixed.  
The axial load is shared between the two bars based on their axial stiffnesses.  
The elongation of both bars is identical due to the constraint imposed by the rigid plates.  
The total applied load equals the sum of the forces in Bar 1 and Bar 2.  
The strain in Bar 1 is equal to the strain in Bar 2.  
The force in Bar 1 is calculated as F1 = (E1 × A1) / (E1 × A1 + E2 × A2) × 20,000 N.  
The force in Bar 2 is calculated as F2 = (E2 × A2) / (E1 × A1 + E2 × A2) × 20,000 N.  
The axial stress in Bar 1 is σ1 = F1 / A1.  
The axial stress in Bar 2 is σ2 = F2 / A2.  
The axial strain in both bars is ε = σ1 / E1 = σ2 / E2.  
The elongation of the composite bar is δ = ε × L, where L is the length of the bars.  
The length L of the bars is assumed to be 1000 mm unless otherwise specified.
