Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.  
The shaft is composed of two segments: AB and BC.  
Segment AB has a diameter of 20 mm and a length of 300 mm.  
Segment BC has a diameter of 30 mm and a length of 500 mm.  
The material of the shaft is 6061-T6 aluminum with a shear modulus G of 25939.8 MPa.  
A torque of 300,000 N·mm is applied at end C.  
No shear force or distributed shear loading is applied.  
The torque is transmitted through the entire shaft from end C to the fixed end at A.  
The polar moment of inertia for a solid circular section is given by J = πd⁴/32.  
The maximum shear stress in a segment is given by τ_max = T·r/J, where r is the radius.  
The angle of twist in a segment is given by φ = T·L/(G·J).  
The total twist at end C is the sum of the twist in segments AB and BC.  
Stress and twist are to be calculated for each segment.  
The shaft is fixed at end A.  
The response quantities of interest are the maximum shear stress in each segment and the total angle of twist at end C.
