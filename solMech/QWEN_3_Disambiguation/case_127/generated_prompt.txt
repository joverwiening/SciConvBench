Assume the shaft is made of a linear-elastic, isotropic material.  
Assume small deformations and no applied shear force; only torque is considered.  
Assume the shaft is solid and circular in cross-section with uniform diameter along its length.  
The diameter of the shaft is 30 mm.  
The length of the shaft is 1000 mm.  
The applied torque is 250,000 N·mm.  
The shear modulus of the material (AISI 1020 steel) is 76923.1 MPa.  
Calculate the maximum shear stress using the formula: τ_max = (T × c) / J, where T is the torque, c is the outer radius (15 mm), and J is the polar moment of inertia.  
Calculate the polar moment of inertia J = (π × d⁴) / 32, where d is the diameter.  
Calculate the angle of twist using the formula: θ = (T × L) / (G × J), where L is the shaft length.  
The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer surface of the shaft.  
The angle of twist is reported in radians.
