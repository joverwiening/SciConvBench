Assume small-deformations and linear-elastic material behavior.
The rod has a circular cross-section with an initial diameter of 25 mm.
The rod is subjected to an axial tensile load of 45000 N.
The material is structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa and Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.29.
The axial stress is calculated as the axial load divided by the cross-sectional area.
The axial strain is determined using Hooke's law: axial strain = axial stress / E.
The lateral strain is determined from the Poisson effect: lateral strain = -ν × axial strain.
The change in diameter is calculated as: change in diameter = lateral strain × initial diameter.
The governing equations are based on uniaxial tension with isotropic linear-elastic material response.
Poisson's effect is characterized by the reduction in lateral dimensions under axial tension.
