Use a rectangular cross section with width b = 50 mm and height h = 120 mm.  
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.  
The internal resultant normal force is P = 40000 N, acting in axial tension along the longitudinal axis of the bar.  
The internal resultant transverse shear force is V = 15000 N, acting parallel to the width (b) and thus horizontally along the 50 mm dimension.  
There is no shear force component acting parallel to the height (h).  
The average normal stress is calculated as σ_avg = P / (b × h).  
The average shear stress is calculated as τ_avg = V / (b × h).  
All stress distributions are assumed to be uniform across the cross section.  
The material is homogeneous and isotropic.  
The bar is prismatic and the section considered is away from any points of load application or geometric discontinuity.
