Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.  
Use Euler-Bernoulli beam theory for analysis.  
The beam is a cantilever with a fixed support at one end and a free end at the other.  
A point load of 2000 N is applied vertically downward at the free end.  
No moment is applied at the free end.  
The length of the beam is 1800 mm.  
The cross-sectional moment of inertia is 1,200,000 mm⁴.  
The elastic modulus of the material is 200,000 MPa.  
The governing differential equation for beam deflection is: d²/dx²(EI d²v/dx²) = 0.  
The boundary conditions are: at the fixed end (x=0), deflection v=0 and slope dv/dx=0.  
The response quantity of interest is the transverse deflection at the free end.  
The maximum deflection at the free end is given by: v_max = (P L³)/(3 E I).  
The maximum slope at the free end is given by: θ_max = (P L²)/(2 E I).  
All units are consistent (forces in N, lengths in mm, E in MPa, I in mm⁴, deflections in mm).
