Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.  
Use Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.  
The beam is a cantilever with fixed support at one end and free at the other.  
A point load of 2000 N is applied downward at the free end.  
No moment is applied at the free end.  
The length of the beam is 1200 mm.  
The second moment of area (I) of the beam is 2,000,000 mm⁴.  
The elastic modulus (E) of the beam material is 200,000 MPa.  
The governing differential equation for beam deflection is E * I * (d⁴v/dx⁴) = 0.  
The reaction force at the fixed end is 2000 N upward.  
The reaction moment at the fixed end is 2,400,000 N·mm (counterclockwise).  
The maximum deflection occurs at the free end and is given by (P * L³) / (3 * E * I).  
The maximum slope occurs at the free end and is given by (P * L²) / (2 * E * I).  
Compute deflection and slope using the given parameters.
