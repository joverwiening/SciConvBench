Q: What is the cross-sectional area variation along the length of the bar? Specifically, how does the area vary linearly (e.g., initial and final cross-sectional areas, or the rate of change)?
A: The cross-sectional area varies linearly according to A(x) = A₀·(1 + k·x/L), where A₀ = 200 mm², k = 1.0, L = 500 mm, and x is measured from the left end. At x = 0, A = 200 mm²; at x = L, A = 400 mm².

Q: What is the magnitude and location of the applied axial load? Specifically, is the load applied at a point, distributed, and at which end or along which segment?
A: The axial load is a constant tensile force P = 12000 N applied at the right end of the bar (x = L), with the left end (x = 0) fixed. The load is point-applied at the free end.
