Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.  
The prismatic bar has a uniform cross-sectional area of 300 mm².  
The length of the bar is 800 mm.  
The bar is made of structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.  
An axial load of 8000 N is applied suddenly to the bar.  
The suddenly applied load implies a dynamic condition where the strain energy absorbed by the bar equals the work done by the load.  
The maximum stress due to a suddenly applied axial load is twice the static stress.  
The static stress is calculated as σ_static = P / A.  
The maximum dynamic stress is σ_max = 2 × P / A.  
Substitute P = 8000 N and A = 300 mm² to compute σ_max.  
The governing relation for maximum stress is σ_max = 2 × (8000 N) / (300 mm²).  
The result is expressed in MPa.
