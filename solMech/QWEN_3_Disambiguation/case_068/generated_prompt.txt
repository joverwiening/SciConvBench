Assume the shaft is a solid circular shaft with a diameter of 30 mm and a length of 800 mm.
The shaft is subjected only to a torque of 500,000 N·mm.
The material behaves as linear-elastic with small deformations.
The shear modulus of the material is required and assumed to be known for the analysis.
The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer surface of the shaft and is calculated using τ = T·r / J.
The polar moment of inertia J for a solid circular shaft is J = π·d⁴ / 32.
The radius r is half of the diameter, so r = 15 mm.
The angle of twist φ is calculated using φ = T·L / (G·J).
The response quantities of interest are the maximum shear stress and the total angle of twist.
Use consistent SI units throughout the calculations.
