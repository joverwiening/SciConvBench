Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.  
Analyze only the torque loading; ignore shear loading.  
The shaft is a solid circular shaft with a diameter of 35 mm.  
The length of the shaft is 400 mm.  
The applied torque is 250,000 N·mm.  
The material is linear-elastic with isotropic behavior.  
Hooke’s law governs the material response.  
Plane sections remain plane and perpendicular to the axis of the shaft.  
No warping occurs under torsion.  
The shear stress is distributed linearly from the center to the outer surface.  
The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer surface of the shaft.  
Use the torsion formula for shear stress: τ = (T × r) / J.  
Use the formula for polar moment of inertia: J = (π × d⁴) / 32.  
Use the formula for angle of twist: θ = (T × L) / (G × J).  
The shear modulus G is required for the calculation of angle of twist.  
The value of G depends on the material and must be specified.
