Use linear-elastic, small-deformation solid mechanics.  
Assume the bar is prismatic with a rectangular cross section of width b = 30 mm and height h = 100 mm.  
Assume plane sections remain plane and stress distributions are uniform away from load application points.  
The internal resultant normal force is P = 50,000 N in tension, acting along the longitudinal axis of the bar.  
The internal resultant transverse shear force is V = 5,000 N, acting parallel to the width (b = 30 mm) direction.  
The section of interest is located sufficiently far from the points of load application to ensure uniform stress distribution, within the prismatic region.  
The average normal stress is calculated as σ_avg = P / A, where A = b × h.  
The average shear stress is calculated as τ_avg = V / A, where A = b × h.  
The area A is 30 mm × 100 mm = 3000 mm².  
The average normal stress σ_avg is 50,000 N / 3000 mm² = 16.67 MPa.  
The average shear stress τ_avg is 5,000 N / 3000 mm² = 1.67 MPa.  
Report average normal stress and average shear stress at the section based on resultant forces and cross-sectional area.
