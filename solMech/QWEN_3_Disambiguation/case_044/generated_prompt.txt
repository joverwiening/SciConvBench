Assume linear-elastic, small-deformation solid mechanics.
The material is 6061-T6 aluminum alloy.
The rod has a diameter of 40 mm.
The rod is subjected to an axial tensile load of 35,000 N.
There is no lateral load applied.
The analysis focuses on the Poisson effect under uniaxial tension.
Use a Young’s modulus of 68.9 GPa and a Poisson’s ratio of 0.33 for 6061-T6.
The axial stress is calculated using σ = F/A, where F is 35,000 N and A is the cross-sectional area based on diameter.
The axial strain is calculated using ε_axial = σ / E.
The transverse strain is calculated using ε_transverse = -ν × ε_axial.
The change in diameter is calculated using Δd = ε_transverse × d.
The Poisson effect is modeled as linear and elastic within the proportional limit.
