Assume the shaft is a solid circular shaft with a diameter of 50 mm and a length of 600 mm.  
The shaft is subjected only to a torque of 500000 N·mm.  
The material behaves as linear-elastic with small deformations.  
The shear stress is calculated using the formula τ = (T × r) / J, where T is the applied torque, r is the radius of the shaft, and J is the polar moment of inertia.  
The polar moment of inertia J is calculated as J = π × (d⁴) / 32, where d is the diameter of the shaft.  
The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer surface of the shaft where r = d/2.  
The angle of twist is calculated using the formula θ = (T × L) / (G × J), where L is the length of the shaft and G is the shear modulus of the material.  
The shear modulus G is assumed to be 79 GPa (typical for steel unless otherwise specified).  
The response quantities of interest are the maximum shear stress and the total angle of twist.
