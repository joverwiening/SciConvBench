Use small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.  
The column has fixed-pinned end conditions.  
The length of the column is 2000 mm.  
The cross-sectional moment of inertia is 1,200,000 mm⁴.  
The elastic modulus of the material is 200,000 MPa.  
The effective length factor (K) for fixed-pinned end conditions is 0.7.  
The Euler critical buckling load is calculated using the formula: P_cr = (π² × E × I) / (K × L)².  
The buckling/instability check compares the applied compressive load to the Euler critical buckling load.  
If the applied load exceeds the Euler critical buckling load, the column is considered unstable.  
All loads are applied concentrically, with no initial imperfections or lateral loads assumed.  
Geometric and material behavior remain linear up to the point of buckling.
