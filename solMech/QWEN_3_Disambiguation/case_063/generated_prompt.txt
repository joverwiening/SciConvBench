Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.  
The rod is prismatic with a circular cross-section of diameter 20 mm.  
The cross-sectional area is calculated as A = π × (20)² / 4 = 314.16 mm².  
The rod is made of structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.  
The coefficient of thermal expansion is α = 1×10⁻⁵ /°C.  
The temperature change is ΔT = +35 °C.  
The rod is fully restrained axially, so no axial deformation is allowed.  
Thermal strain is given by ε_thermal = α × ΔT.  
Because of full axial restraint, the mechanical strain is zero.  
The total strain is equal to the thermal strain, but the restrained condition induces compressive stress.  
The axial stress due to thermal loading is σ = -E × α × ΔT.  
No external axial load is applied; the only load is the thermal stress induced by restrained expansion.  
The governing relation for stress under full axial restraint is σ = -E × α × ΔT.  
The response quantities of interest are:  
- Thermal strain: ε_thermal = α × ΔT = 3.5×10⁻⁴ (compressive in effect due to restraint).  
- Axial stress: σ = -200000 MPa × 1×10⁻⁵ /°C × 35 °C = -70 MPa.  
- Axial force: F = σ × A = -70 MPa × 314.16 mm² = -21991.2 N.  
All quantities are reported as negative, indicating compressive stress and force.
