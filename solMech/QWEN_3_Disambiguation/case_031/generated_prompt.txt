Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.  
The bar is vertical and subjected to axial loading due to its self-weight.  
The cross-sectional area of the bar is 200 mm².  
The length of the bar is 3500 mm.  
The material's Young's modulus is 69000 MPa.  
The material is homogeneous and isotropic.  
The acceleration due to gravity is 9.81 m/s².  
The density of the material is 7850 kg/m³ (structural steel, assumed unless otherwise specified).  
The elongation is caused solely by the distributed self-weight along the length of the bar.  
The stress distribution is uniaxial and varies linearly from zero at the free end to maximum at the fixed end.  
The governing equation for elongation due to self-weight is: δ = (ρ g L²) / (2 E), where ρ is density, g is gravitational acceleration, L is length, and E is Young's modulus.  
Compute elongation using the given parameters and the governing equation.
