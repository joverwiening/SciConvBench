Use a linear-elastic, isotropic material model with properties typical for structural steel: Young’s modulus = 200 GPa, Poisson’s ratio = 0.3.  
Assume small deformations and pure torsion about the shaft’s longitudinal axis.  
The shaft is a hollow circular cylinder with outer radius = 25 mm and inner radius = 21 mm.  
The length of the shaft is 1200 mm.  
Apply a torque of 450000 N·mm about the centerline of the shaft.  
The shear modulus is calculated as G = E / (2(1 + ν)).  
The polar moment of inertia is calculated as J = (π/2) * (ro⁴ - ri⁴).  
The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer surface and is given by τ_max = T * ro / J.  
The angle of twist is calculated as θ = (T * L) / (G * J).
