Use a linear-elastic, small-deformation solid mechanics model.  
Assume the shaft is made of a homogeneous, isotropic material.  
Assume no shear load is applied; only a torque is applied at end C.  
Assume end A is fully fixed (no rotation or displacement).  
The shear modulus G is 25939.8 MPa.  
Segment AB has a diameter of 25 mm and a length of 400 mm.  
Segment BC has a diameter of 30 mm and a length of 900 mm.  
A torque of 750,000 N·mm is applied at end C.  
The maximum shear stress occurs in segment AB.  
Calculate the shear stress in each segment using τ = T·r / J.  
Calculate the polar moment of inertia J for each segment as J = π·d⁴ / 32.  
Calculate the twist in each segment using θ = T·L / (G·J).  
Sum the twists to obtain the total angle of twist at end C.  
The governing equations are based on torsion theory for circular shafts.
