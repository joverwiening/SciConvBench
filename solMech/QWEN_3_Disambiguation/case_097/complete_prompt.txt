Use N–mm–MPa units.

A stepped solid circular shaft made of 6061-T6 aluminum consists of two segments in series. Segment AB has length L1 = 400 mm and diameter d1 = 25 mm. Segment BC has length L2 = 900 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm. A torque T = 750000 N·mm is applied at end C while end A is fixed, so the same internal torque acts through both segments. Use G = 25939.8 MPa.

Determine (i) the maximum shear stress in each segment and identify which segment governs, and (ii) the total angle of twist at C relative to A.
