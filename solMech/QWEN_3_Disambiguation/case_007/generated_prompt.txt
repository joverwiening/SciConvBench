The beam is simply supported with a total length of 1800 mm.  
A point load of 2000 N is applied vertically downward at x = 900 mm.  
No external moment or distributed load is applied.  
The material is linear-elastic with Young’s modulus E = 200000 MPa.  
The cross-sectional flexural rigidity is defined by the area moment of inertia I = 3500000 mm⁴.  
Small deformations are assumed, and the analysis is governed by Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.  
The deflection at an arbitrary point due to the point load is to be determined using the unit-load method.  
The governing equation for deflection via the unit-load method is:  
δ = ∫₀ᴸ [M(x) ⋅ m(x)] / (E ⋅ I) dx  
where M(x) is the bending moment due to the applied load, and m(x) is the bending moment due to a virtual unit load applied at the point of interest.  
Reactions at the supports are determined from static equilibrium.  
The bending moment distribution M(x) is piecewise linear, with a peak at the load application point.  
The virtual moment function m(x) depends on the location where deflection is evaluated.  
Deflection is computed at the same point as the load application (x = 900 mm) unless otherwise specified.
