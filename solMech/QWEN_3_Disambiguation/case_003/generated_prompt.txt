Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.  
The axial load applied to the stepped circular bar is 20000 N.  
Segment 1 has a diameter of 30 mm and a length of 500 mm.  
Segment 2 has a diameter of 25 mm and a length of 600 mm.  
The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with a Young's modulus of 69000 MPa.  
The cross-sectional area of segment 1 is calculated as π*(d₁/2)².  
The cross-sectional area of segment 2 is calculated as π*(d₂/2)².  
The stress in segment 1 is calculated as P / A₁.  
The stress in segment 2 is calculated as P / A₂.  
The axial elongation of segment 1 is calculated as (P * L₁) / (A₁ * E).  
The axial elongation of segment 2 is calculated as (P * L₂) / (A₂ * E).  
The total elongation is the sum of the elongations of segment 1 and segment 2.  
Stress and elongation are constant within each segment.  
The bar is prismatic within each segment and transitions abruptly between segments.  
Loading is applied axially at the ends, and stress concentrations are neglected.
