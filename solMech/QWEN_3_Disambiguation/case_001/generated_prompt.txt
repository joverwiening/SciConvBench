Use linear-elastic material behavior with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Assume the stepped bar is axially loaded and consists of two segments in series.
Segment 1 has a diameter of 25 mm and length of 300 mm.
Segment 2 has a diameter of 35 mm and length of 1000 mm.
The applied tensile load is 30000 N, constant across the bar.
Stress in each segment is calculated as axial load divided by cross-sectional area.
Cross-sectional area for each segment is computed from its diameter.
Stress in Segment 1 is 61.12 MPa.
Stress in Segment 2 is 31.18 MPa.
Total elongation is the sum of elongations in each segment.
Elongation in each segment is (axial load × length) / (cross-sectional area × E).
Elongation in Segment 1 is 0.0917 mm.
Elongation in Segment 2 is 0.1528 mm.
Total elongation of the bar is 0.2445 mm.
Assume no stress concentrations at the step; uniform stress distribution in each segment.
Assume no temperature effects or body forces.
Governing equations are: σ = P/A, ε = σ/E, and δ = PL/(AE).
