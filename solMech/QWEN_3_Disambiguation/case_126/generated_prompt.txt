Assume the shaft is made of brass with a shear modulus of 39179.1 MPa.  
The shaft is a solid circular shaft with a diameter of 30 mm and a length of 1200 mm.  
The shaft is subjected to a torque of 400000 N·mm.  
Neglect shear loading as it is not specified and assume the primary loading is torsional.  
The polar moment of inertia J is calculated using the formula J = πd⁴/32, where d is the diameter.  
The maximum shear stress τ is calculated using the formula τ = T·r/J, where T is the torque, r is the radius of the shaft.  
The angle of twist φ is calculated using the formula φ = T·L/(G·J), where L is the length of the shaft and G is the shear modulus.  
The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer surface of the shaft.  
The angle of twist is expressed in radians unless otherwise specified.
