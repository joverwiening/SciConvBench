Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.  
Use Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.  
The beam is simply supported with a length of 3000 mm.  
A point load of 4000 N is applied at midspan.  
There is no shear loading.  
The cross-sectional moment of inertia (I) is 3,500,000 mm⁴.  
The elastic modulus (E) is 69,000 MPa.  
The flexural rigidity (EI) is calculated as E × I.  
The midspan deflection is calculated using the formula: (P × L³) / (48 × E × I).  
The support slope is calculated using the formula: (P × L²) / (16 × E × I).  
All units are consistent (forces in N, lengths in mm, E in MPa, I in mm⁴, deflection in mm, slope in radians).
