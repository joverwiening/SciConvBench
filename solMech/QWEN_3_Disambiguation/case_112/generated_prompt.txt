Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.  
The rod is straight, prismatic, and circular in cross-section.  
The diameter of the rod is 20 mm.  
The axial tensile load applied to the rod is 15000 N.  
The rod is made of 6061-T6 aluminum.  
Use a Young’s modulus of 69000 MPa.  
Use a yield strength of 275 MPa.  
The cross-sectional area is calculated as π times (diameter/2) squared.  
The average normal stress is calculated as axial load divided by cross-sectional area.  
The axial strain is calculated as average normal stress divided by Young’s modulus.  
The elongation is calculated as axial strain times the original length of the rod.  
The original length of the rod is required to compute strain and elongation.  
The factor of safety is calculated as yield strength divided by the average normal stress.  
All loads are applied concentrically along the longitudinal axis of the rod.  
No thermal, dynamic, or shear effects are considered.  
Stress and strain are uniform across the cross-section.
