Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.  
The composite bar system consists of two parallel bars connected between two rigid plates.  
An axial tensile force P = 20000 N is applied vertically to the top rigid plate along the axis of the bars.  
Bar 1 is made of AISI 1020 steel with cross-sectional area A1 = 300 mm² and Young’s modulus E1 = 200000 MPa.  
Bar 2 is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with cross-sectional area A2 = 200 mm² and Young’s modulus E2 = 69000 MPa.  
The bottom rigid plate is fixed.  
The bars deform axially under the applied load without bending or shear deformation.  
The displacement of both bars is equal due to compatibility enforced by the rigid plates.  
The total load is the sum of the forces in Bar 1 and Bar 2.  
The strain in Bar 1 is equal to the strain in Bar 2.  
The force in Bar 1 is (E1 × A1 × ε).  
The force in Bar 2 is (E2 × A2 × ε).  
The total force P = (E1 × A1 + E2 × A2) × ε.  
Solve for strain ε = P / (E1 × A1 + E2 × A2).  
Compute axial stress in Bar 1 as σ1 = E1 × ε.  
Compute axial stress in Bar 2 as σ2 = E2 × ε.  
Compute force in Bar 1 as F1 = σ1 × A1.  
Compute force in Bar 2 as F2 = σ2 × A2.  
Report the displacement of the top plate as δ = ε × L, assuming length L is common to both bars.  
All deformations remain within the elastic limit.
