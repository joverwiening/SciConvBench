Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.  
The shaft is a solid circular shaft with diameter 18 mm and length 400 mm.  
The shaft is subjected only to a torque of 500000 N·mm.  
The shaft material is brass with shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.  
The shear stress is calculated using the formula τ = (T × r) / J, where T is the applied torque, r is the radius of the shaft, and J is the polar moment of inertia.  
The polar moment of inertia J for a solid circular shaft is given by J = (π × d⁴) / 32, where d is the diameter.  
The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer surface of the shaft where r = d/2.  
The angle of twist is calculated using the formula θ = (T × L) / (G × J), where L is the length of the shaft.  
All deformations are within the elastic limit.  
The shaft is prismatic and homogeneous.  
The torque is applied statically and uniformly about the shaft axis.
