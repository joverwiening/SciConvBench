Use a cylindrical tension specimen with an initial gauge length of 50 mm and an initial diameter of 12 mm.
Define failure as fracture.
Measure the final gauge length after fracture as 66 mm.
Measure the final diameter at the fracture location as 9.6 mm.
Calculate percent elongation using the formula: ((final gauge length - initial gauge length) / initial gauge length) * 100%.
Calculate reduction of area using the formula: ((initial cross-sectional area - final cross-sectional area) / initial cross-sectional area) * 100%.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics unless otherwise required.
Assume uniform deformation within the gauge length up to necking, and localized deformation during necking confined to the fracture region.
Assume the material is isotropic and homogeneous.
