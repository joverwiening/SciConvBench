Use a simply supported beam of length 2500 mm.  
Apply a downward point load of 1000 N at the midspan (x = 1250 mm).  
Assume linear-elastic material behavior with elastic modulus E = 200,000 MPa.  
Use the moment of inertia I = 3,000,000 mm⁴ for the beam cross-section.  
Determine the midspan deflection using Castigliano’s theorem.  
The strain energy U is expressed as U = ∫(M²/(2EI)) dx over the length of the beam.  
The bending moment M(x) is derived for each segment of the beam due to the point load.  
Differentiate the total strain energy with respect to the point load to obtain the deflection at midspan.  
The deflection δ = ∂U/∂P is calculated analytically using the applied load P = 1000 N.  
Assume small deformations and neglect shear deformation effects.  
The support conditions are pin and roller, providing vertical reactions but no moment resistance.  
The beam is prismatic and homogeneous along its length.
