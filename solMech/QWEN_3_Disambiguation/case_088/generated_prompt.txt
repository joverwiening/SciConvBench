Use linear-elastic, small-deformation solid mechanics assumptions.
Assume the bar is prismatic in each segment and loaded only by axial tension at the ends.
Assume no stress concentrations at the step change in diameter.
The axial load applied to the stepped circular bar is P = 35000 N.
Segment 1 has length L1 = 300 mm and diameter d1 = 12 mm.
Segment 2 has length L2 = 800 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm.
The material is AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Compute the cross-sectional area of segment 1 as A1 = π*(d1/2)^2 = π*(6 mm)^2 ≈ 113.1 mm².
Compute the cross-sectional area of segment 2 as A2 = π*(d2/2)^2 = π*(15 mm)^2 ≈ 706.9 mm².
Compute the axial stress in segment 1 as σ1 = P / A1 = 35000 N / 113.1 mm² ≈ 309.5 MPa.
Compute the axial stress in segment 2 as σ2 = P / A2 = 35000 N / 706.9 mm² ≈ 49.5 MPa.
Compute the elongation of segment 1 as δ1 = (P * L1) / (A1 * E) = (35000 * 300) / (113.1 * 200000) ≈ 0.464 mm.
Compute the elongation of segment 2 as δ2 = (P * L2) / (A2 * E) = (35000 * 800) / (706.9 * 200000) ≈ 0.198 mm.
Compute the total elongation as δ_total = δ1 + δ2 ≈ 0.464 mm + 0.198 mm = 0.662 mm.
Report the stress in segment 1, stress in segment 2, and total elongation of the bar.
