Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.  
The rod is made of AISI 1020 steel with a yield strength of 350 MPa and Young’s modulus of 200000 MPa.  
The rod has a circular cross-section with a diameter of 12 mm.  
The cross-sectional area is calculated as π × (6 mm)² = 113.1 mm².  
The axial tensile load applied is 15000 N.  
The average normal stress is calculated as axial load divided by cross-sectional area: 15000 N / 113.1 mm² = 132.6 MPa.  
The axial strain is calculated as stress divided by Young’s modulus: 132.6 MPa / 200000 MPa = 0.000663.  
The elongation is strain multiplied by the original length; since length is not provided, express elongation per unit length as 0.000663 m/m.  
The factor of safety with respect to yielding is the yield strength divided by the maximum working stress: 350 MPa / 132.6 MPa = 2.64.  
All responses are based on prismatic, homogeneous, isotropic material behavior under uniform axial loading.  
The rod is assumed to be free of initial stresses and geometric imperfections.
