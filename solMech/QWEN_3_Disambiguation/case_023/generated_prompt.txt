Use linear-elastic, small-deformation solid mechanics.
The beam is a cantilever with length 2500 mm.
The only applied load is a uniformly distributed transverse load of 1.0 N/mm acting downward.
The Young's modulus of the material is 200000 MPa.
The cross-sectional moment of inertia is 1000000 mm⁴.
The governing equation for deflection is the Euler-Bernoulli beam equation: d⁴v/dx⁴ = q/(EI), where q is the load intensity.
The boundary conditions are: v(0) = 0 and dv/dx(0) = 0 (fixed end).
The maximum deflection occurs at the free end (x = 2500 mm).
The maximum deflection is given by: v_max = (qL⁴)/(8EI).
Substitute q = 1.0 N/mm, L = 2500 mm, E = 200000 MPa, I = 1000000 mm⁴.
