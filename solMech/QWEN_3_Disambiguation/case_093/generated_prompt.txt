Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.  
The beam is simply supported with a total length of 3000 mm.  
The only applied load is a point load P = 800 N acting downward at x = 2000 mm.  
No external moment is applied.  
No distributed transverse loading is present.  
The flexural rigidity EI is 2.4 × 10¹¹ N·mm², with E = 200000 MPa and I = 1200000 mm⁴.  
Use the unit-load method to compute the vertical deflection at x = 2000 mm.  
Apply a unit load (1 N) downward at x = 2000 mm for the virtual system.  
Compute the bending moment distribution M(x) due to the real load P.  
Compute the bending moment distribution m(x) due to the virtual unit load.  
Integrate over the length of the beam: deflection = ∫₀ᴸ [M(x) ⋅ m(x)] / EI dx.  
Evaluate the integral using piecewise integration due to discontinuity at the point load location.  
The supports are idealized as pin and roller, providing vertical reactions only.  
Axial and shear deformations are neglected.  
The deflection is defined as positive downward.
