Use a linear-elastic, small-deformation mechanical model for a prismatic straight rod.  
The rod has a circular cross-section with a diameter of 12 mm.  
The rod is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with a Young’s modulus of 69000 MPa and a yield strength of 275 MPa.  
The axial tensile load applied to the rod is 15000 N.  
The gage length of the rod is 800 mm.  
Calculate the average normal stress using the formula σ = F / A, where F is the axial load and A is the cross-sectional area.  
Compute the cross-sectional area as A = π × (d/2)², with d = 12 mm.  
Determine the axial strain using Hooke’s law: ε = σ / E, where E is the Young’s modulus.  
Calculate the elongation as ΔL = ε × L, where L is the gage length.  
Evaluate the factor of safety against yielding as FS = σ_yield / σ.  
Report the average normal stress, axial strain, elongation, and factor of safety.
