Use linear-elastic material behavior with small deformations.  
Assume plane sections remain plane and perpendicular to the neutral axis after deformation.  
Assume the bar is homogeneous, isotropic, and prismatic with a rectangular cross section.  
The cross section has width b = 40 mm and height h = 120 mm.  
The internal resultant normal force is P = 30,000 N in tension, acting perpendicular to the cross section.  
The internal resultant transverse shear force is V = 12,000 N, acting parallel to the width b (along the 40 mm direction).  
Average normal stress is calculated as σ_avg = P / A, where A = b × h.  
Average shear stress is calculated as τ_avg = V / A, where A = b × h.  
The area A is 4800 mm².  
The average normal stress σ_avg is 6.25 MPa.  
The average shear stress τ_avg is 2.5 MPa.  
Report both average normal and average shear stresses at the section.
