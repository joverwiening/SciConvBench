Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.  
The beam is simply supported at both ends, labeled A and B, with a total length of 4000 mm.  
The only applied load is a concentrated vertical force of magnitude 12000 N, acting downward at the midspan (2000 mm from support A).  
There is no axial (tension) loading.  
There is no externally applied moment.  
The internal shear force and bending moment are to be determined as the response quantities.  
The beam is prismatic and homogeneous.  
Support A is a pin support, providing vertical and horizontal reaction forces.  
Support B is a roller support, providing only a vertical reaction force.  
The governing equations are based on static equilibrium: sum of forces in the vertical direction equals zero, sum of moments about any point equals zero.  
The internal shear force at any section is equal to the algebraic sum of all vertical forces to the left (or right) of the section.  
The internal bending moment at any section is equal to the algebraic sum of the moments of all forces to the left (or right) of the section about that section.
