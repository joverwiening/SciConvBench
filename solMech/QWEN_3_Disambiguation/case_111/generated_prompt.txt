Use Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa for structural steel.  
Apply Euler buckling theory to determine the critical buckling load.  
Use an effective length factor K = 2.0 corresponding to a fixed-free end condition.  
Calculate the critical buckling load using the formula: P_cr = (π² × E × I) / (K × L)².  
Use the second moment of area I = (π × d⁴) / 64 for a solid circular cross-section.  
Compute the allowable column load by dividing the critical buckling load by the factor of safety of 2.0.  
Evaluate the factor of safety with respect to buckling as the ratio of the critical buckling load to the applied axial load, if the applied load is provided.
