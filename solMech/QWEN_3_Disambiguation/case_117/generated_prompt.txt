Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.  
The beam has a rectangular cross-section with width 60 mm and height 80 mm.  
The internal shear force at the section of interest is V = 16000 N.  
The material is a generic linear-elastic isotropic material.  
Transverse shear stress is computed using the standard formula for rectangular cross-sections.  
Use the formula τ = (V * Q) / (I * b), where Q is the first moment of area, I is the second moment of area, and b is the width.  
Compute the second moment of area I = (b * h^3) / 12 with b = 60 mm and h = 80 mm.  
Compute Q for a rectangular section at a distance y from the neutral axis as Q = (b / 2) * ((h/2)^2 - y^2).  
Evaluate transverse shear stress distribution from the neutral axis to the top and bottom surfaces.  
The maximum transverse shear stress occurs at the neutral axis.  
Report the shear stress as a function of position y and the maximum value.
