Use linear-elastic, small-deformation solid mechanics theory.  
Assume the bar is prismatic in each segment and loaded axially along its centroidal axis.  
Assume no stress concentrations at the step transition.  
The axial load applied to the stepped circular bar is P = 25000 N.  
Segment 1 has length L1 = 400 mm and diameter d1 = 16 mm.  
Segment 2 has length L2 = 400 mm and diameter d2 = 40 mm.  
The material is structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.  
Compute the cross-sectional area of Segment 1 as A1 = π(d1/2)².  
Compute the cross-sectional area of Segment 2 as A2 = π(d2/2)².  
Compute axial stress in Segment 1 as σ1 = P / A1.  
Compute axial stress in Segment 2 as σ2 = P / A2.  
Compute axial elongation of Segment 1 as δ1 = (P × L1) / (A1 × E).  
Compute axial elongation of Segment 2 as δ2 = (P × L2) / (A2 × E).  
Compute total elongation as δ_total = δ1 + δ2.  
Report the stress in each segment and the total elongation.
