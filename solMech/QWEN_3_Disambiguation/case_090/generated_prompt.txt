Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.  
The axial load applied to the stepped circular bar is P = 25000 N.  
Segment 1 has diameter d1 = 12 mm and length L1 = 300 mm.  
Segment 2 has diameter d2 = 30 mm and length L2 = 600 mm.  
The material is structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.  
The cross-sectional area of segment 1 is A1 = π × (d1/2)².  
The cross-sectional area of segment 2 is A2 = π × (d2/2)².  
The axial stress in segment 1 is σ1 = P / A1.  
The axial stress in segment 2 is σ2 = P / A2.  
The elongation of segment 1 is δ1 = (P × L1) / (A1 × E).  
The elongation of segment 2 is δ2 = (P × L2) / (A2 × E).  
The total elongation of the bar is δ_total = δ1 + δ2.  
Stress is uniform within each segment.  
No body forces or thermal effects are considered.  
Load is applied axially and statically.  
The bar is prismatic in each segment.
