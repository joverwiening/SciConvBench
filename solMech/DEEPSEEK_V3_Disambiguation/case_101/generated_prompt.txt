The beam is a cantilever of length L = 1000 mm.
The beam material is linear-elastic with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The beam cross-section has a second moment of area I = 2000000 mm^4.
The beam is subjected to a uniformly distributed transverse load w = 1.5 N/mm.
The analysis assumes small deformations and static loading.
The distributed load is applied downward over the entire beam length.
The governing equation for deflection v(x) is derived from the Euler-Bernoulli beam theory: E I * d⁴v/dx⁴ = w.
The boundary conditions are: at the fixed end (x=0), deflection v=0 and slope dv/dx=0; at the free end (x=L), bending moment E I * d²v/dx² = 0 and shear force E I * d³v/dx³ = 0.
The maximum deflection occurs at the free end (x = L).
Calculate the free-end deflection using the formula: v_max = (w * L⁴) / (8 * E * I).
Report the deflection v_max in millimeters.
