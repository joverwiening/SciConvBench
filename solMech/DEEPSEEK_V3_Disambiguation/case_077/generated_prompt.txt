The bar is a prismatic bar with a rectangular cross-section of width b = 50 mm and height h = 120 mm.
The bar is subjected to small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The internal resultant forces at the cross-section of interest are a normal force P = 40000 N (tension) and a shear force V = 15000 N.
The shear force V acts parallel to the width dimension (b) of the cross-section.
The average normal stress on the cross-section is calculated as sigma_avg = P / A, where A is the cross-sectional area.
The cross-sectional area A = b * h = 50 mm * 120 mm = 6000 mm^2.
Therefore, the average normal stress sigma_avg = 40000 N / 6000 mm^2 = 6.667 N/mm^2 (MPa).
The average shear stress on the cross-section is calculated as tau_avg = V / A.
Therefore, the average shear stress tau_avg = 15000 N / 6000 mm^2 = 2.5 N/mm^2 (MPa).
The calculated average normal stress is uniform across the section.
The calculated average shear stress is a nominal average value; the actual shear stress distribution in a rectangular section is parabolic, with a maximum value higher than this average.
