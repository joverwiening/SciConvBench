Assume a rectangular cross-section for the beam.
Assume a beam length of 1000 mm.
The beam is made of AISI 1020 steel, assumed to be linear-elastic with a Young's modulus (E) of 200 GPa.
The applied bending moment (M) is 3,000,000 N·mm.
The second moment of area (I) about the neutral axis is 2,500,000 mm^4.
The bending moment is applied about the principal axis corresponding to the given I.
Use the Euler-Bernoulli beam theory for small deformations.
Calculate the bending stress using the flexure formula: σ = M*y / I.
Calculate the maximum bending stress at the extreme fiber.
Calculate the curvature (κ) from the moment-curvature relationship: κ = M / (E*I).
Calculate the radius of curvature (R) as the reciprocal of the curvature: R = 1/κ.
Report the curvature in units of mm⁻¹.
Report the radius of curvature in units of mm.
