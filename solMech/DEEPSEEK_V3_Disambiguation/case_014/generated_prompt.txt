The analysis is for a linear-elastic, Euler-Bernoulli cantilever beam under a static end point load.
The beam length L is 1200 mm.
The applied vertical end point load P is 800 N.
The beam material has a Young's modulus E of 200000 MPa.
The beam cross-section has a second moment of area I of 2500000 mm^4.
The relevant response quantity is the vertical deflection along the beam length.
The governing equation for the deflection v(x) is derived from the bending moment M(x) = -P*(L - x).
The equation is E * I * (d^2 v / dx^2) = M(x) = -P*(L - x).
Integrate the governing equation twice and apply the cantilever boundary conditions (v=0 and dv/dx=0 at x=0).
The maximum deflection occurs at the free end (x = L).
The calculated free-end deflection is v_max = (P * L^3) / (3 * E * I).
Substitute the given values to compute the numerical result.
The shear force is constant and equal to -P.
The bending moment varies linearly from zero at the free end to -P*L at the fixed support.
Assume the beam is prismatic (constant cross-section).
Assume the load is applied perpendicular to the beam's longitudinal axis.
Assume the beam is weightless.
Assume the support is rigid and prevents all displacements and rotations at the fixed end.
