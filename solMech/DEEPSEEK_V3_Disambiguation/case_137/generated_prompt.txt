Assume a simply supported beam of length L = 3000 mm.
Assume a rectangular cross-section with width b = 200 mm and height h = 400 mm.
The beam is made of a linear-elastic, isotropic material.
The only load is a concentrated vertical force P = 12000 N acting downward at the midspan (x = 1500 mm from support A).
The supports are pinned at A (x=0) and roller at B (x=L), allowing rotation but no vertical displacement.
The relevant response quantities are the internal shear force V(x) and internal bending moment M(x) along the beam length.
The reaction forces are calculated from static equilibrium: R_A = R_B = P/2 = 6000 N.
The shear force V(x) is piecewise constant: V(x) = +6000 N for 0 < x < L/2, and V(x) = -6000 N for L/2 < x < L.
The bending moment M(x) is piecewise linear: M(x) = R_A * x = 6000x N-mm for 0 ≤ x ≤ L/2, and M(x) = R_B * (L - x) = 6000*(3000 - x) N-mm for L/2 ≤ x ≤ L.
The maximum bending moment occurs at midspan: M_max = (P*L)/4 = 9e6 N-mm.
The governing relation for shear and moment in the left half is dM/dx = V.
The governing relation for shear and moment in the right half is dM/dx = V.
