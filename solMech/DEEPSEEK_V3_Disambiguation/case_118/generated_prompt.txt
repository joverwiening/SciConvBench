Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The material is isotropic and homogeneous.
The beam is prismatic with a constant rectangular cross-section.
The beam length is 2.0 meters.
The beam is simply supported.
The transverse loading is a central point load, P.
The transverse shear force on the cross-section, V, is 25000 N.
The cross-sectional width, b, is 40 mm.
The cross-sectional height (depth), h, is 80 mm.
The neutral axis is at the centroid of the cross-section.
The transverse shear stress is to be determined at a location where V=25000 N is constant, specifically at a support.
The transverse shear stress is to be determined at a vertical coordinate y = 20 mm from the neutral axis.
The relevant response quantity is the transverse shear stress, τ_xy.
Use the engineering beam theory formula for shear stress in a rectangular beam: τ = VQ / (Ib).
Calculate the area moment of inertia for the rectangle: I = (b * h^3) / 12.
Calculate the first moment of area, Q, for the area above y=20 mm: Q = b * (h/2 - y) * ( (h/2 + y) / 2 ).
Compute the numerical value of the transverse shear stress in MPa.
Report the shear stress value to three significant figures.
