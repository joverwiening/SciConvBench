The structural member is a rectangular adhesive layer.
The material is isotropic and linear-elastic.
The shear modulus is G = 39179.1 MPa.
The layer thickness in the shear direction is t = 20 mm.
A uniform in-plane shear force V = 12000 N is applied.
The in-plane area over which the shear force acts is A = 4000 mm².
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Assume a state of pure shear stress within the layer.
The average engineering shear stress τ is calculated as τ = V / A.
The average engineering shear strain γ is calculated as γ = τ / G.
The shear deformation δ is calculated as δ = γ * t.
The governing relation for shear stress is τ = V / A.
The governing relation for shear strain is γ = τ / G.
The governing relation for shear deformation is δ = γ * t.
The relevant response quantities are shear stress, shear strain, and shear deformation.
