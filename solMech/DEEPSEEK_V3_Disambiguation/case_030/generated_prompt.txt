The rod is a linear-elastic, isotropic solid with a circular cross-section.
The material has a Young's modulus (E) of 200,000 MPa and a Poisson's ratio (ν) of 0.29.
The rod is subjected to a uniaxial tensile load along its longitudinal axis.
The primary response quantity is the axial strain (ε_axial) in the direction of the applied load.
The secondary response quantity, due to the Poisson effect, is the lateral strain (ε_lateral) in all directions perpendicular to the load.
The governing relation for axial strain is ε_axial = σ_axial / E, where σ_axial is the applied axial stress.
The governing relation for lateral strain is ε_lateral = -ν * ε_axial.
Assume the lateral load mentioned in the initial idea is not present for this specific analysis of the Poisson effect under uniaxial tension.
Assume Saint-Venant's principle applies for regions away from the points of load application.
Assume the rod is long compared to its cross-sectional dimensions.
