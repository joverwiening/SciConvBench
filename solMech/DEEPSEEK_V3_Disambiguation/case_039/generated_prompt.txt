The problem is to determine the relevant response quantities for a solid circular AISI rod under uniaxial tension, focusing on the Poisson effect.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic, isotropic material behavior.
Assume the rod is prismatic and the load is applied axially, resulting in a state of uniaxial stress.
The rod's initial diameter is d0 = 20 mm.
The applied axial tensile load is P = 35000 N.
The primary response quantities are axial stress, axial strain, lateral strain, and change in diameter.
The axial (normal) stress is sigma_axial = P / A0, where A0 is the initial cross-sectional area.
The initial cross-sectional area A0 = (pi * d0^2) / 4.
The axial strain is epsilon_axial = sigma_axial / E, where E is Young's modulus for the AISI material.
The lateral strain is epsilon_lateral = -nu * epsilon_axial, where nu is Poisson's ratio for the AISI material.
The change in diameter is delta_d = epsilon_lateral * d0.
The final diameter is d_final = d0 + delta_d.
Specify the AISI grade (e.g., 1020, 304 stainless) to obtain values for Young's modulus (E) and Poisson's ratio (nu).
