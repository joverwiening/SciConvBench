The beam is an Euler-Bernoulli beam of length L = 3000 mm.
The beam is linear-elastic and undergoes small deformations.
The beam is subjected to a uniformly distributed transverse load w = 0.3 N/mm over the full span (0 ≤ x ≤ L).
The beam is subjected to a point load P = 3000 N downward at the midspan (x = L/2).
The flexural rigidity of the beam is EI = 4×10¹¹ N·mm².
The supports are not specified; assume the beam is simply supported at both ends.
The relevant response quantity is the transverse deflection v(x).
Determine the deflection v(x) by superposition of the effects of the distributed load and the point load.
For a simply supported beam with a uniformly distributed load w over the full span, the deflection is v_w(x) = (w*x/(24*EI))*(L³ - 2Lx² + x³).
For a simply supported beam with a point load P at midspan, the deflection for 0 ≤ x ≤ L/2 is v_P(x) = (P*x/(48*EI))*(3L² - 4x²).
The total deflection is v(x) = v_w(x) + v_P(x).
