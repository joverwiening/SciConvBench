The beam is a simply supported Euler-Bernoulli beam with a length L of 2000 mm.
The beam is made of a linear-elastic material.
The beam is subjected to a single point load P of 3000 N applied at the midspan.
The cross-section of the beam is constant along its length.
The relevant response quantity is the maximum vertical deflection.
The maximum deflection occurs at the midspan.
The governing relation for the maximum deflection is δ_max = (P * L^3) / (48 * E * I).
The beam must be sized so that the calculated maximum deflection δ_max is less than or equal to the allowable deflection δ_allow of 3 mm.
The sizing requirement is expressed as (P * L^3) / (48 * E * I) ≤ δ_allow.
The required minimum second moment of area for the beam cross-section is I ≥ (P * L^3) / (48 * E * δ_allow).
The modulus of elasticity E for the beam material must be specified to complete the calculation.
