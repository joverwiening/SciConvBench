The system is a stepped solid circular shaft made of structural steel.
The shaft consists of two segments, AB and BC, connected in series.
Segment AB has a length L1 = 400 mm and a diameter d1 = 30 mm.
Segment BC has a length L2 = 700 mm and a diameter d2 = 30 mm.
A torque T = 600000 N·mm is applied at the free end C.
The material is linear-elastic, isotropic, and homogeneous.
Small-deformation, Saint-Venant's principle, and pure torsion assumptions apply.
The polar moment of inertia for each segment is J = (π*d⁴)/32.
The shear modulus G for structural steel is 79.3 GPa.
The maximum shear stress in each segment is τ_max = (T * d) / (2 * J).
The angle of twist for each segment is φ = (T * L) / (G * J).
The total angle of twist of the shaft is the sum of the twists of segments AB and BC.
The relevant response quantities are the maximum shear stress in each segment and the total twist at end C relative to the fixed end.
