The column is a solid circular cross-section made of structural steel.
The column length L is 1800 mm.
The applied compressive load P is 30000 N.
The column has pinned-pinned end conditions, with an effective length factor K of 1.0.
Assume a yield strength (σ_y) of 250 MPa for the structural steel.
The required factor of safety against buckling (n_b) is 2.0.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic material behavior.
Assume the column is initially perfectly straight and the load is perfectly axial.
The governing relation for elastic buckling (Euler buckling) is P_cr = (π² * E * I) / (K*L)².
The moment of inertia for a solid circular cross-section is I = (π * d⁴) / 64.
The governing relation for stress-based design is σ = P / A ≤ σ_allow, where A = (π * d²) / 4.
The allowable stress for buckling is σ_allow = (π² * E) / ( (K*L / r)² * n_b ), where the radius of gyration r = √(I/A) = d/4.
The column must also be checked for yielding under the applied load: σ_applied = P / A ≤ σ_y / n_y, where n_y is an appropriate factor of safety against yielding.
Design the diameter d such that the column is safe against both buckling and yielding under the given load and factor of safety.
