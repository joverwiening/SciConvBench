Assume the built-up beam is a steel I-beam with flanges and a web connected by fasteners.
Assume the beam is made of a linear-elastic, isotropic steel alloy.
The beam is subjected to an internal shear force V = 25000 N.
The second moment of area of the entire cross-section is I = 18000000 mm^4.
Each fastener has an allowable shear force capacity of 1800 N.
Calculate the shear flow (q) at the interface between the flange and web using the formula q = V * Q / I, where Q is the first moment of area of the portion of the cross-section above (or below) the interface.
Determine the required fastener spacing (s) along the beam's length using the formula s = (Fastener Allowable Shear Force) / q.
The governing relation for shear flow is q = V * Q / I.
The governing relation for fastener spacing is s = F_all / q.
Assume the span length is sufficient for the shear force to be constant over the region of interest for fastener spacing calculation.
Assume the fasteners are aligned in a single row along the flange-web interface.
The primary response quantities are the shear flow (q) in N/mm and the fastener spacing (s) in mm.
