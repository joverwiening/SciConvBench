The beam is a simply supported, linear-elastic beam of length L = 3000 mm.
The beam is subjected to a single vertical point load P = 2000 N applied at its midspan.
The beam's flexural rigidity is given by E*I, with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa and the second moment of area I = 1500000 mm^4.
The relevant response quantity to determine is the vertical deflection at the beam's midspan.
Use Castigliano's second theorem, where the deflection is equal to the partial derivative of the strain energy with respect to the applied load.
The strain energy U for the beam under bending is U = ∫ (M(x)^2 / (2EI)) dx, integrated over the beam length.
Due to symmetry, calculate the bending moment M(x) for one half of the beam (0 ≤ x ≤ L/2) as M(x) = (P/2) * x.
Compute the midspan deflection δ by evaluating δ = ∂U/∂P = (1/(EI)) * ∫ M(x) * (∂M(x)/∂P) dx from 0 to L.
Perform the integration to obtain the governing relation for midspan deflection: δ = (P * L^3) / (48 * E * I).
Substitute the given values P, L, E, and I into the governing relation to calculate the numerical result.
