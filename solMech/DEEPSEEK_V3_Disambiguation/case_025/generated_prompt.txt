Assume a small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics model.
Assume a cantilever beam of length L = 1500 mm.
Assume the beam is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with Young's modulus E = 69000 MPa.
Assume the beam has a cross-section with a second moment of area I = 2500000 mm^4.
Apply a point load P = 500 N at the free end of the cantilever.
The loading is pure bending with no axial or shear force considered for deflection calculation.
Use Castigliano's theorem to determine the free end deflection.
The governing relation for end deflection under end load is δ = (P * L^3) / (3 * E * I).
Calculate the deflection δ using the provided parameters.
