The rod is a linear-elastic, isotropic, circular cylinder.
The rod is subjected to a purely axial tensile load P = 25000 N.
The rod's diameter is a given value D.
The material properties are Young's modulus E = 105000 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.34.
The analysis assumes small deformations and Saint-Venant's principle applies.
The relevant response quantity is the axial strain, ε_axial = P / (A * E), where A is the cross-sectional area.
The relevant response quantity is the lateral strain, ε_lateral = -ν * ε_axial.
The relevant response quantity is the change in diameter, ΔD = ε_lateral * D.
The governing relation for uniaxial stress is σ_axial = E * ε_axial.
The governing relation for the Poisson effect is ε_lateral = -ν * ε_axial.
