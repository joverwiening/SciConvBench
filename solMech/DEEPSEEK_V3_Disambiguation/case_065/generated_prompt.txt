The shaft is a solid circular brass shaft with a length of 400 mm and a diameter of 18 mm.
The shaft is subjected to a pure torque of T = 500000 N·mm.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Assume the material is homogeneous, isotropic, and obeys Hooke's law.
Assume the shaft is prismatic and the torque is applied at the ends.
The shear modulus (G) for brass is assumed to be 39 GPa (3.9e4 N/mm²).
Calculate the polar moment of inertia (J) for the solid circular cross-section.
Calculate the maximum shear stress (τ_max) on the outer surface of the shaft.
Calculate the angle of twist (φ) over the full length of the shaft.
The governing relation for shear stress is τ_max = (T * r) / J, where r is the outer radius.
The governing relation for the angle of twist is φ = (T * L) / (G * J), where L is the shaft length.
Present the calculated values for τ_max and φ.
