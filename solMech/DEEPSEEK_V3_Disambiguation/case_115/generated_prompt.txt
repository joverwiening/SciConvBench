The analysis is for a hollow circular shaft under pure torsion.
The shaft is made of a linear-elastic, isotropic material.
The shaft geometry is defined by an outer diameter of 60 mm and an inner diameter of 44 mm.
The shaft length is 1200 mm.
The material has a shear modulus G of 77519.4 MPa.
The shaft is subjected to an applied torque T of 600000 N·mm.
The analysis assumes small deformations and Saint-Venant's principle applies away from the ends.
The analysis assumes the shaft is prismatic and the torque is applied at the ends.
The polar moment of inertia J for the hollow cross-section is calculated as (π/32) * (D_outer⁴ - D_inner⁴).
The maximum shear stress τ_max occurs at the outer radius and is calculated as τ_max = (T * R_outer) / J.
The angle of twist φ over the full length L is calculated as φ = (T * L) / (G * J).
The shear stress distribution is linear from zero at the center to a maximum at the outer surface.
The governing relation for shear stress is τ(r) = (T * r) / J.
