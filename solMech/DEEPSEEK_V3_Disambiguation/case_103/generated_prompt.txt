The rod is a linear-elastic, isotropic solid.
The rod has a circular cross-section with an initial diameter of 30 mm.
The rod is subjected to a purely axial tensile load of 25000 N.
The analysis assumes small deformations and uniaxial stress state.
The relevant response quantity is the axial strain.
The relevant response quantity is the lateral strain due to the Poisson effect.
The relevant response quantity is the change in the rod's diameter.
The governing relation for axial stress is sigma_axial = P / A0, where A0 is the initial cross-sectional area.
The governing relation for axial strain is epsilon_axial = sigma_axial / E, where E is Young's modulus.
The governing relation for lateral strain is epsilon_lateral = -nu * epsilon_axial, where nu is Poisson's ratio.
The governing relation for the change in diameter is Delta_d = epsilon_lateral * d0, where d0 is the initial diameter.
The material properties Young's modulus (E) and Poisson's ratio (nu) must be provided to compute numerical values.
