The rod is a straight, prismatic, circular rod made of AISI 1020 steel.
The rod has a length of 800 mm and a diameter of 12 mm.
The rod is subjected to an axial tension load of 15000 N.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic material behavior.
Assume the load is applied perfectly axially, inducing no bending.
The cross-sectional area of the rod is calculated as (pi * (diameter/2)^2).
The average normal (axial) stress is the axial load divided by the cross-sectional area.
The strain is the stress divided by the Young's modulus of the material.
Use a Young's modulus of 200 GPa for AISI 1020 steel.
The elongation is the strain multiplied by the original length of the rod.
The factor of safety with respect to yielding is the material yield strength divided by the calculated average normal stress.
The yield strength of the AISI 1020 steel is 350 MPa.
