Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The system consists of two parallel bars between two rigid plates.
Bar 1 is AISI 1020 steel.
Bar 2 is cast iron.
Bar 1 has a cross-sectional area A1 = 300 mm².
Bar 2 has a cross-sectional area A2 = 250 mm².
Both bars have the same length L = 400 mm.
The bars are perfectly bonded to the rigid plates.
The plates remain parallel under load.
A total tensile load P = 20000 N is applied to the top plate.
The load is shared between the two bars.
The strains in both bars are equal due to the rigid plates.
The relevant response quantities are the stress in each bar, the strain in the assembly, the load carried by each bar, and the total elongation.
The governing relations are: Total Load P = P1 + P2, where P1 and P2 are loads in bars 1 and 2.
Strain Compatibility: ε1 = ε2 = ε.
Constitutive Relations: σ1 = E1 * ε, σ2 = E2 * ε, where E1 and E2 are Young's moduli for steel and cast iron.
Load-Stress Relations: P1 = σ1 * A1, P2 = σ2 * A2.
The total elongation is ΔL = ε * L.
