Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The structural member is a rectangular adhesive layer.
The adhesive layer has an area A = 4000 mm² and a thickness t = 20 mm.
The material is isotropic with a shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.
The adhesive layer is subjected to a uniform in-plane shear force V = 8000 N.
The force is applied parallel to the layer's faces, causing a state of pure shear.
Assume the shear stress is uniformly distributed across the area A.
Calculate the average engineering shear stress as τ = V / A.
Calculate the engineering shear strain as γ = τ / G.
Calculate the absolute shear deformation (displacement) as δ = γ * t.
The governing relation for shear stress is τ = V / A.
The governing constitutive relation is τ = G * γ.
The governing kinematic relation for deformation is δ = γ * t.
The relevant response quantities are shear stress (τ), shear strain (γ), and shear deformation (δ).
