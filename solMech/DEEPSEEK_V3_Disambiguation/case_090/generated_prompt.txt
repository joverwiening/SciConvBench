The material is linear-elastic with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The stepped bar has two cylindrical segments in series under axial tension.
The first segment has diameter d1 = 12 mm and length L1 = 300 mm.
The second segment has diameter d2 = 30 mm and length L2 = 600 mm.
An axial tensile load P = 25000 N is applied uniformly to the bar.
The cross-sectional area of the first segment is A1 = (π/4) * (d1)^2.
The cross-sectional area of the second segment is A2 = (π/4) * (d2)^2.
The axial stress in the first segment is σ1 = P / A1.
The axial stress in the second segment is σ2 = P / A2.
The axial strain in the first segment is ε1 = σ1 / E.
The axial strain in the second segment is ε2 = σ2 / E.
The elongation of the first segment is δ1 = ε1 * L1.
The elongation of the second segment is δ2 = ε2 * L2.
The total elongation of the bar is δ_total = δ1 + δ2.
Report the calculated values for σ1, σ2, and δ_total.
