Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The shaft is a solid circular shaft made of brass.
The shaft length is L = 1200 mm.
The shaft is subjected to an applied torque T = 400000 N·mm.
The shaft diameter is d = 30 mm.
The relevant material property for brass is its shear modulus, G.
Assume a typical shear modulus for brass: G = 40 GPa.
Calculate the polar moment of inertia, J, for the solid circular cross-section.
The polar moment of inertia is J = (π * d^4) / 32.
Calculate the maximum shear stress, τ_max, using the torsion formula.
The maximum shear stress is τ_max = (T * c) / J, where c = d/2.
Calculate the angle of twist, φ, over the shaft length.
The angle of twist is φ = (T * L) / (G * J).
Report the maximum shear stress in MPa.
Report the angle of twist in degrees.
