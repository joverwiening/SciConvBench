The shaft is a solid circular cylinder made of AISI 1020 steel.
The shaft has a total length L = 1200 mm.
The shaft has a constant diameter d = 30 mm.
The shaft is fixed at the end x = 0 mm.
A torque T2 = -200000 N·mm is applied at location x = 400 mm.
A torque T1 = +200000 N·mm is applied at the free end x = L = 1200 mm.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic material behavior.
The shear modulus G for AISI 1020 steel is 80 GPa.
The polar moment of inertia J for the cross-section is (π * d^4) / 32.
The internal torque T(x) in each segment is determined from equilibrium.
The angle of twist φ(x) is found by integrating dφ/dx = T(x) / (GJ) along the shaft.
The maximum shear stress τ_max(x) in each segment is T(x) * (d/2) / J.
The relevant response quantities are the internal torque distribution T(x), the angle of twist distribution φ(x), and the maximum shear stress distribution τ_max(x).
