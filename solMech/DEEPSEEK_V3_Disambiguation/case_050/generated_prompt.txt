Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Assume the material is homogeneous, isotropic, and obeys Hooke's law.
Assume the shaft is prismatic and the cross-section is constant along its length.
Assume Saint-Venant's principle applies for end effects.
The shaft is hollow and circular with an outer radius of 20 mm and an inner radius of 15 mm.
The shaft length is 1000 mm.
The shaft is subjected to a pure torque of 300000 N·mm.
Calculate the polar moment of inertia for the hollow circular cross-section.
Calculate the maximum shear stress in the shaft, which occurs at the outer radius.
Calculate the shear stress at the inner radius.
Calculate the angle of twist for the entire shaft length.
State the governing torsion formula for shear stress.
State the governing relation for the angle of twist.
Present the final numerical values for the polar moment of inertia, maximum shear stress, shear stress at inner radius, and total angle of twist.
