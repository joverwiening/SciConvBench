The problem is a cantilever beam of length L = 1200 mm, subjected to an end point load P = 2000 N.
The beam material is linear-elastic with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The beam's flexural rigidity is defined by its second moment of area I = 2000000 mm^4.
The relevant response quantity is the maximum deflection at the free end.
The governing relation for the deflection v(x) along the beam length is derived from the Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.
The boundary conditions are: deflection and slope are zero at the fixed end (x=0).
The bending moment distribution is M(x) = -P*(L - x).
The curvature relation is M(x) = E * I * (d^2v/dx^2).
Integrating the curvature relation twice and applying boundary conditions yields the deflection equation.
The maximum deflection at the free end (x=L) is given by v_max = (P * L^3) / (3 * E * I).
Calculate the numerical value of the maximum deflection using the provided parameters.
