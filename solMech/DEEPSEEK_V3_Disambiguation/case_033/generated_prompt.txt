The beam is a simply supported Euler-Bernoulli beam of length L = 2500 mm.
The beam material is linear elastic with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The beam's cross-section has a second moment of area I = 1200000 mm^4.
A single point load P = 2500 N is applied vertically downward at the midspan.
The relevant response quantities are the midspan deflection and the slope at the supports.
The midspan deflection is calculated using the formula: δ_max = (P * L^3) / (48 * E * I).
The slope at each support is calculated using the formula: θ_support = (P * L^2) / (16 * E * I).
All calculations assume small deformations and static loading.
The effects of shear deformation are neglected per the Euler-Bernoulli assumption.
The beam is assumed to be prismatic (constant cross-section along its length).
The support reactions are not requested as part of the relevant response quantities.
