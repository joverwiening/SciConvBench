Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The bar has a rectangular cross section with width b = 30 mm and height h = 100 mm.
The internal resultants at the section are a normal force P = 50000 N (tension) and a shear force V = 5000 N acting parallel to the width.
The average normal stress on the section is calculated as the normal force divided by the cross-sectional area.
The average shear stress on the section is calculated as the shear force divided by the cross-sectional area.
The cross-sectional area A = b * h = (30 mm) * (100 mm) = 3000 mm².
The average normal stress σ_avg = P / A = 50000 N / 3000 mm² = 16.67 N/mm² (MPa).
The average shear stress τ_avg = V / A = 5000 N / 3000 mm² = 1.67 N/mm² (MPa).
