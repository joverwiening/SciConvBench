The problem is to determine the elongation of a vertical bar due to its own weight under small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The bar is made of steel.
The bar has a length L = 3500 mm.
The bar has a constant cross-sectional area A = 200 mm².
The material density is ρ = 7850 kg/m³.
The Young's modulus is E = 69000 MPa.
The acceleration due to gravity is g = 9.81 m/s².
Assume the bar is fixed at its top end and free at its bottom end.
Assume the bar is subjected only to axial loading from its self-weight.
Assume the material is homogeneous and isotropic.
The governing relation for the elongation δ due to self-weight is δ = (ρ * g * L²) / (2 * E).
The relevant response quantity is the total axial elongation at the free end.
