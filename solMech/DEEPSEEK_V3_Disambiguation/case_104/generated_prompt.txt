The bar is a vertical, linearly elastic solid.
The bar has a constant cross-sectional area A = 300 mm².
The bar length L = 3000 mm.
The material has Young's modulus E = 69000 MPa.
The material density ρ = 8900 kg/m³.
The acceleration due to gravity is g = 9.81 m/s².
The bar is fixed at its top end and free at its bottom end.
The bar is subjected only to its own self-weight as an axial load.
The deformation is small and the material behavior is linear-elastic.
The axial force at a section located a distance x from the top is P(x) = ρ g A (L - x).
The axial stress at a section located a distance x from the top is σ(x) = P(x) / A = ρ g (L - x).
The axial strain at a section located a distance x from the top is ε(x) = σ(x) / E = (ρ g / E) (L - x).
The elongation δ at the free end (bottom) of the bar is δ = ∫ from 0 to L of ε(x) dx.
The governing relation for total elongation is δ = (ρ g L²) / (2E).
