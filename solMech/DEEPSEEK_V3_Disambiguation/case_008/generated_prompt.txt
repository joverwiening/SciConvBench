The problem involves two parallel composite bars between rigid plates under small-deformation, linear-elastic conditions.
Bar 1 is AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E1 = 200 GPa, cross-sectional area A1 = 300 mm², and length L = 500 mm.
Bar 2 is 6061-T6 aluminum with Young's modulus E2 = 69 GPa, cross-sectional area A2 = 200 mm², and length L = 500 mm.
A total tensile load P = 20000 N is applied to the top rigid plate.
The bottom plate is fixed.
The plates remain parallel, ensuring both bars undergo identical axial displacement.
The relevant response quantities to determine are: the load shared by each bar (P1, P2), the axial stress in each bar (σ1, σ2), the axial strain in each bar (ε1, ε2), and the total elongation of the assembly (ΔL).
The governing relations are: P = P1 + P2, ε1 = ε2 = ΔL / L, σ1 = E1 * ε1, σ2 = E2 * ε2, P1 = σ1 * A1, and P2 = σ2 * A2.
