The material is structural steel, assumed to be linear-elastic and isotropic.
The bar is a two-segment stepped circular bar under axial tension.
Segment 1 (left) has a diameter d1 = 16 mm and length L1 = 400 mm.
Segment 2 (right) has a diameter d2 = 40 mm and length L2 = 800 mm.
A tensile load P = 35000 N is applied axially to the ends of the bar.
Assume small deformations and a state of uniaxial stress in each segment.
Neglect stress concentrations at the step.
The cross-sectional area for Segment 1 is A1 = (π/4)*(d1^2).
The cross-sectional area for Segment 2 is A2 = (π/4)*(d2^2).
The axial stress in Segment 1 is σ1 = P / A1.
The axial stress in Segment 2 is σ2 = P / A2.
The axial strain in Segment 1 is ε1 = σ1 / E, where E is Young's modulus for structural steel.
The axial strain in Segment 2 is ε2 = σ2 / E.
The elongation of Segment 1 is δ1 = ε1 * L1.
The elongation of Segment 2 is δ2 = ε2 * L2.
The total elongation of the bar is δ_total = δ1 + δ2.
The relevant response quantities are σ1, σ2, and δ_total.
