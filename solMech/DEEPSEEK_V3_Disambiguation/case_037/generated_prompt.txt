The analysis is for a simply supported beam under small-deformation, linear-elastic conditions.
The beam is made of structural steel, with an elastic modulus (E) of 210 GPa.
The beam length (L) is 4500 mm.
The beam is subjected to a uniformly distributed transverse load (w) of 0.3 N/mm over the full span.
The beam cross-section has a second moment of area (I) of 2,000,000 mm^4.
The relevant response quantities are the maximum deflection and the slope at the supports.
The maximum deflection occurs at the beam's mid-span.
Calculate the maximum deflection (v_max) using the formula v_max = (5 * w * L^4) / (384 * E * I).
Calculate the slope at the left support (θ_A) using the formula θ_A = (w * L^3) / (24 * E * I).
The slope at the right support (θ_B) is equal in magnitude but opposite in sign to θ_A.
Report the deflection in millimeters and the slope in radians.
