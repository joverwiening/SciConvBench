Assume the material is isotropic and homogeneous within each segment.
Assume the bar is prismatic within each segment (constant cross-section).
Assume the load is a pure axial tensile force applied at the ends, with no bending or torsion.
Assume the connection between segments is ideal, with no stress concentration.
Define the total force applied as P.
Define the bar as having two segments, Segment 1 and Segment 2.
Define the length of Segment 1 as L1 and its cross-sectional area as A1.
Define the length of Segment 2 as L2 and its cross-sectional area as A2.
Define the Young's modulus of the material as E, constant for both segments.
The stress in Segment 1 is calculated as sigma1 = P / A1.
The stress in Segment 2 is calculated as sigma2 = P / A2.
The axial strain in Segment 1 is epsilon1 = sigma1 / E.
The axial strain in Segment 2 is epsilon2 = sigma2 / E.
The elongation of Segment 1 is delta1 = epsilon1 * L1.
The elongation of Segment 2 is delta2 = epsilon2 * L2.
The total elongation of the stepped bar is delta_total = delta1 + delta2.
