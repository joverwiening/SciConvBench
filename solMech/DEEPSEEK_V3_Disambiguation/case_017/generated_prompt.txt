The beam is subjected to a uniformly distributed transverse load w = 0.3 N/mm over the full span.
The beam is subjected to a downward point load P = 1200 N at the midspan (x = L/2).
The supports are simple (pinned) at both ends unless otherwise stated.
The analysis uses the principle of superposition for small deformations.
Calculate the total deflection v(x) by summing the deflection due to the distributed load and the deflection due to the point load.

For the point load at midspan, use the deflection formula v_P(x) = (P*x/(48*E*I))*(3*L^2 - 4*x^2) for 0 ≤ x ≤ L/2.
For the point load at midspan, for x > L/2, use symmetry: v_P(x) = v_P(L-x).
Compute the maximum deflection at the midspan.

State the final governing relation for total deflection as v_total(x) = v_w(x) + v_P(x).
