The problem is a cantilever beam of length L = 1800 mm, fixed at one end and free at the other.
The beam is subjected to a vertical end point load P = 2000 N applied downward at the free end.
The beam material is linear-elastic with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The beam's flexural rigidity is defined by the second moment of area I = 1,200,000 mm^4.
The analysis uses the Euler-Bernoulli beam theory under small-deformation assumptions.
The relevant response quantity is the vertical deflection at the free end.
The governing relation for the free-end deflection (δ) is δ = (P * L^3) / (3 * E * I).
Calculate the deflection using the provided values.
The result should be reported in millimeters.
