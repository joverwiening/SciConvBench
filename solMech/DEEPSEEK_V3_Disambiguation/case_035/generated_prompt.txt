The column is a solid circular cross-section made of 6061-T6 aluminum.
The column length L is 2200 mm.
The column has a fixed-free end condition with an effective length factor K = 2.0.
The applied compressive load P is 25000 N.
The required factor of safety against buckling is n = 2.5.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic material behavior.
Assume the column is perfectly straight and prismatic.
Assume the load is applied concentrically.
Use the Euler buckling formula for a pinned-pinned column: P_cr = (π² * E * I) / (K*L)².
The critical buckling load for the fixed-free column is calculated using the effective length K*L.
The moment of inertia for a solid circular cross-section of diameter d is I = (π * d⁴) / 64.
The modulus of elasticity E for 6061-T6 aluminum is 68.9 GPa.
The governing equation for the critical load is P_cr = (π² * E * (π * d⁴ / 64)) / (K*L)².
Solve for the minimum diameter d such that P_cr / P ≥ n.
Calculate the actual factor of safety as P_cr / P.
Report the calculated minimum diameter d in mm.
