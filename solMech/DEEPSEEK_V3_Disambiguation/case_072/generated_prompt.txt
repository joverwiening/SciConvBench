The problem is to analyze a solid circular shaft under torsion using an energy method.
The shaft has a length L of 1000 mm.
The shaft has an outer diameter d of 25 mm.
The shaft is subjected to an applied torque T of 350000 N·mm.
The material has a shear modulus G of 76923.1 MPa.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic, isotropic material behavior.
Assume the shaft is prismatic and the torque is applied at the free end, with the other end fixed.
The polar moment of inertia J for the solid circular cross-section is (π*d^4)/32.
The torsional stiffness k_t is (G*J)/L.
The strain energy U stored in the shaft is (T^2 * L) / (2*G*J).
Using Castigliano's theorem, the angle of twist φ at the free end is ∂U/∂T, which equals (T*L)/(G*J).
Compute the numerical value of the angle of twist φ in radians.
The maximum shear stress τ_max occurs at the outer radius and equals (T*d)/(2*J).
Compute the numerical value of the maximum shear stress τ_max.
