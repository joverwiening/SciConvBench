The material is structural steel, assumed to be linear-elastic and isotropic.
The shaft is a solid circular cylinder with an outer diameter of 35 mm.
The shaft length is 400 mm.
The shaft is subjected to a pure torque of 250,000 N·mm.
No transverse shear force is applied; the loading condition is pure torsion.
The relevant response quantities are the maximum shear stress and the angle of twist.
The maximum shear stress is calculated using the torsion formula: τ_max = (T * r) / J, where T is the torque, r is the outer radius, and J is the polar moment of inertia.
The polar moment of inertia for a solid circular shaft is J = (π * d^4) / 32.
The angle of twist is calculated using φ = (T * L) / (J * G), where L is the shaft length and G is the shear modulus of the material.
The shear modulus G for structural steel is assumed to be 79.3 GPa.
