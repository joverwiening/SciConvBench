The analysis is for a cantilever beam under a uniformly distributed transverse load.
The beam has a length L of 2000 mm.
The beam is subjected to a uniformly distributed load w of 1.0 N/mm.
The beam material is linear-elastic with a Young's modulus E of 200000 MPa.
The beam's cross-section has a second moment of area I of 1500000 mm^4.
The analysis assumes small deformations and static loading.
The governing equation for the deflection v(x) is derived from the Euler-Bernoulli beam theory: E I v''''(x) = w.
The boundary conditions for the cantilever are: v(0) = 0, v'(0) = 0, M(L)=E I v''(L) = 0, and V(L)=E I v'''(L) = 0.
The maximum deflection occurs at the free end (x = L).
The formula for the free-end deflection is v_max = (w L^4) / (8 E I).
The relevant response quantity is the calculated free-end deflection v_max.
