The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with a Young's modulus of 69000 MPa and a yield strength of 275 MPa.
The rod is prismatic and circular with a diameter of 12 mm and a length of 800 mm.
The rod is subjected to a static axial tensile load of 15000 N.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic material behavior.
Assume the load is applied centrally, resulting in uniform uniaxial stress.
Calculate the cross-sectional area of the rod.
Calculate the average normal (axial) stress in the rod.
Calculate the axial strain in the rod.
Calculate the total elongation of the rod.
Calculate the factor of safety with respect to yielding using n = Sy / σ_avg.
Present the governing equations for stress, strain, elongation, and factor of safety.
Report the numerical values for cross-sectional area, average normal stress, axial strain, elongation, and factor of safety.
