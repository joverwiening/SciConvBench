Assume the shaft is made of structural steel with Young's modulus E = 210 GPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.3.
Assume a yield strength σ_y = 250 MPa for the steel.
Assume the shaft is simply supported with a length L = 1 m.
The shaft has a circular cross-section with diameter d = 30 mm.
The shaft is subjected to a bending moment M = 4,000,000 N·mm and a torsional moment T = 400,000 N·mm.
There is no axial force applied.
The analysis is performed at the critical section where the bending moment is maximum.
Calculate the second moment of area for the circular cross-section: I = πd⁴/64.
Calculate the polar moment of area for the circular cross-section: J = πd⁴/32.
Calculate the maximum bending stress using the flexure formula: σ_b = M*y/I, where y = d/2.
Calculate the maximum torsional shear stress using the torsion formula: τ = T*r/J, where r = d/2.
The bending stress is a normal stress acting along the shaft's axis.
The torsional shear stress acts in the plane of the cross-section.
At a point on the shaft's surface, the state of stress is biaxial: a normal stress σ_x = σ_b and a shear stress τ_xy = τ.
All other stress components (σ_y, σ_z, τ_yz, τ_zx) are zero at this surface point.
Calculate the principal stresses using the equations for plane stress: σ₁,₂ = (σ_x/2) ± √((σ_x/2)² + τ_xy²).
Calculate the maximum in-plane shear stress: τ_max,in-plane = √((σ_x/2)² + τ_xy²).
Calculate the absolute maximum shear stress: τ_max,abs = max(|σ₁-σ₂|/2, |σ₁|/2, |σ₂|/2).
Calculate the principal strains using Hooke's Law for plane stress: ε₁ = (σ₁ - νσ₂)/E and ε₂ = (σ₂ - νσ₁)/E.
Calculate the maximum shear strain: γ_max = τ_max,in-plane / G, where G = E/(2(1+ν)).
Evaluate the factor of safety using the maximum distortion energy (von Mises) criterion.
Calculate the von Mises equivalent stress: σ_vM = √(σ₁² + σ₂² - σ₁σ₂).
The factor of safety is n = σ_y / σ_vM.
Report the calculated values for σ_b, τ, σ₁, σ₂, τ_max,in-plane, τ_max,abs, ε₁, ε₂, γ_max, σ_vM, and n.
