The rod is a straight, prismatic circular rod made of 6061-T6 aluminum.
The rod has a diameter of 20 mm.
The rod has a gage length of 800 mm.
The rod is subjected to a static axial tension load of 15000 N.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic material behavior.
Assume the load is applied perfectly axially, resulting in pure tension with no bending.
The cross-sectional area of the rod is calculated from its diameter.
Calculate the average engineering normal stress in the rod as the applied load divided by the cross-sectional area.
Calculate the average engineering normal strain using Hooke's Law: stress divided by Young's modulus.
Use a Young's modulus of 69 GPa for 6061-T6 aluminum.
Calculate the total elongation of the rod's gage length as the strain multiplied by the original gage length.
Evaluate a factor of safety with respect to material yielding.
Use a yield strength of 276 MPa for 6061-T6 aluminum.
The factor of safety is the yield strength divided by the calculated average normal stress.
