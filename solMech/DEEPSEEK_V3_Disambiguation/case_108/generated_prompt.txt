The beam is a simply supported beam of length L = 3500 mm.
The beam is made of structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The beam has a second moment of area I = 1500000 mm^4.
The beam is subjected to a uniformly distributed transverse load of magnitude w = 0.5 N/mm.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The analysis assumes the beam is prismatic (constant cross-section).
The analysis assumes the material is isotropic and homogeneous.
The analysis assumes the load is applied statically.
The governing equation for beam deflection v(x) is E * I * (d^4 v / dx^4) = w.
The relevant response quantities are the maximum deflection and the slope at the supports.
The maximum deflection occurs at the beam's midspan (x = L/2).
The formula for maximum deflection is v_max = (5 * w * L^4) / (384 * E * I).
The slope at the left support (x=0) is theta_A = (w * L^3) / (24 * E * I).
The slope at the right support (x=L) is theta_B = -(w * L^3) / (24 * E * I).
