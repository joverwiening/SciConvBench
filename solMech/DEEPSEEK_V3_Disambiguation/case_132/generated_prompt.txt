Assume the beam is made of a common structural steel.
Assume the beam length is 3000 mm.
The beam is simply supported and subjected to a maximum bending moment of 3,000,000 N·mm.
The beam has a rectangular cross-section with a width of 40 mm.
The allowable bending stress for the material is 90 MPa.
The relevant response quantity is the required section height to satisfy the bending stress criterion.
The governing relation for bending stress in an elastic beam is σ = M*y/I.
The governing relation for the section modulus is S = I/c, where c = h/2 for a rectangle.
The moment of inertia for a rectangular section is I = (b * h^3) / 12.
The section modulus for a rectangular section is S = (b * h^2) / 6.
The bending stress formula becomes σ_max = M_max / S.
The design requirement is σ_max ≤ σ_allow.
The required section modulus is S_required ≥ M_max / σ_allow.
Solve (b * h^2) / 6 ≥ M_max / σ_allow for the height h.
The required minimum height h is the design quantity.
