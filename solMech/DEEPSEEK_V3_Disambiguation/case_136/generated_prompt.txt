Assume a rectangular cross-section for the beam.
Assume the material is linear-elastic and isotropic.
Assume small deformations and that plane sections remain plane.
The beam is simply supported with a span L of 6000 mm.
A concentrated vertical load P of 20000 N acts downward at a distance of 2000 mm from the left support (Support A).
Calculate the support reactions at A and B.
Determine the internal shear force V(x) along the length of the beam.
Determine the internal bending moment M(x) along the length of the beam.
The governing relations are equilibrium: sum of vertical forces equals zero and sum of moments equals zero.
The shear force is the integral of the distributed load, or the sum of forces to one side of a section.
The bending moment is the integral of the shear force, or the sum of moments to one side of a section.
The maximum bending moment occurs where the shear force is zero.
Plot or tabulate the shear and moment diagrams.
