The rod is a prismatic, circular cylinder made of AISI steel.
The rod has a length L = 1200 mm.
The rod has a diameter d = 35 mm.
The material is linear-elastic with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The material has a coefficient of thermal expansion α = 1e-05 1/°C.
The rod experiences a uniform temperature increase of ΔT = +65 °C.
The rod is fully restrained against axial deformation at its ends.
No external mechanical axial load is applied.
The thermal strain if the rod were free to expand is ε_th = α * ΔT.
The full restraint condition enforces a total axial strain of zero: ε_total = 0.
The total axial strain is the sum of thermal and mechanical strains: ε_total = ε_th + ε_mech = 0.
The mechanical strain is therefore ε_mech = -α * ΔT.
The axial stress developed due to restraint is σ = E * ε_mech = -E * α * ΔT.
The axial force in the rod is F = σ * A, where A is the cross-sectional area.
The cross-sectional area is A = (π/4) * d^2.
The reaction forces at the restraints are equal and opposite to the axial force F.
