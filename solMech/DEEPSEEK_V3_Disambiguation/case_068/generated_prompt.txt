The problem is a solid circular shaft under pure torsion.
The shaft is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with a shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa.
The shaft has a length L = 800 mm and an outer diameter d = 30 mm.
The shaft is subjected to an applied torque T = 500000 N·mm.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic, isotropic material behavior.
Assume the shaft is prismatic and the torque is applied at the ends.
Assume Saint-Venant's principle applies away from the ends.
The polar moment of inertia J for the solid circular cross-section is π*d⁴/32.
The maximum shear stress τ_max occurs at the outer surface and is calculated as τ_max = T*(d/2)/J.
The angle of twist φ (in radians) over the length L is calculated as φ = T*L/(G*J).
Report the numerical values for the maximum shear stress (in MPa) and the total angle of twist (in degrees).
