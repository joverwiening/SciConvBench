The rod is prismatic with a constant circular cross-section of area A = 100π mm².
The rod length is L = 800 mm.
The material is linear-elastic with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The material has a coefficient of thermal expansion α = 2e-05 1/°C.
The rod experiences a uniform temperature increase of ΔT = 50 °C.
The rod is fully restrained axially, meaning total axial strain ε_total = 0.
The thermal strain is ε_thermal = α * ΔT.
The mechanical strain is ε_mech = ε_total - ε_thermal = -αΔT.
The axial stress induced by the full restraint is σ = E * ε_mech = -E * α * ΔT.
The axial force (thermal load) in the rod is F = σ * A.
The relevant response quantities are: thermal strain (ε_thermal), mechanical strain (ε_mech), axial stress (σ), and axial force (F).
Assume small deformations, isotropic material, and uniform material properties and temperature change.
The governing relation for total strain is ε_total = (σ/E) + αΔT.
For full axial restraint, ε_total = 0.
