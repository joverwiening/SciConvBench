Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The column is prismatic and straight.
The material is homogeneous and isotropic.
The applied compressive load is perfectly axial.
The column length L is 3000 mm.
The modulus of elasticity E is 69000 MPa.
The second moment of area I is 2500000 mm^4.
The end conditions are fixed at both ends.
Calculate the Euler critical buckling load P_cr using the formula P_cr = (π² * E * I) / (K * L)².
Use an effective length factor K of 0.5 for fixed-fixed end conditions.
The governing relation is P_cr = (4 * π² * E * I) / L².
Perform a buckling check by comparing the applied compressive load P to the critical load P_cr.
The column is considered safe against Euler buckling if P < P_cr, with an appropriate factor of safety.
The relevant response quantity is the numerical value of P_cr in Newtons.
