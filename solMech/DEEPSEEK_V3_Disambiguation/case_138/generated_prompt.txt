The bar has a rectangular cross-section with width b = 40 mm and height h = 120 mm.
The bar is subjected to a tension force P = 30000 N and a shear force V = 12000 N at the cross-section of interest.
The shear force V acts parallel to the width (b) of the cross-section.
The material is assumed to be linear-elastic and undergo small deformations.
The average normal stress on the cross-section is calculated as the normal force divided by the cross-sectional area.
The cross-sectional area A = b * h = 40 mm * 120 mm = 4800 mm².
The average normal stress σ_avg = P / A = 30000 N / 4800 mm² = 6.25 N/mm² (MPa).
The average shear stress on the cross-section is calculated as the shear force divided by the cross-sectional area.
The average shear stress τ_avg = V / A = 12000 N / 4800 mm² = 2.5 N/mm² (MPa).
The average normal stress is uniform across the section.
The average shear stress is a simplified representation; actual shear stress distribution in a rectangular section is parabolic.
