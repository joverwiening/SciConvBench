Assume the adhesive layer is a rectangular prism with a cross-sectional area of 3000 mm² and a thickness of 8 mm.
Assume the length in the direction of the applied shear is 50 mm, and the width is 60 mm, as a reasonable geometry consistent with the given area.
The material is isotropic and linear-elastic with a shear modulus G of 39179.1 MPa.
The member is subjected to an in-plane shear force V of 12000 N, applied parallel to the adhesive layer's plane.
Assume the load is uniformly distributed over the cross-sectional area.
The relevant shear stress τ is calculated as the applied shear force divided by the cross-sectional area.
The relevant shear strain γ is calculated as the shear stress divided by the shear modulus.
The relevant shear deformation Δ is calculated as the shear strain multiplied by the thickness of the adhesive layer.
The governing relation for shear stress is τ = V / A.
The governing relation for shear strain is γ = τ / G.
The governing relation for shear deformation is Δ = γ * t.
