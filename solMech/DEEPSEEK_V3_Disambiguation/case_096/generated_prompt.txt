The analysis is for a linear-elastic, Euler-Bernoulli cantilever beam under small deformations.
The beam has a length L = 800 mm.
A downward point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end.
The material has a Young's modulus E = 69000 MPa.
The cross-section has a second moment of area I = 800000 mm^4.
The fixed support is at x = 0, and the free end with the load is at x = L.
The relevant response quantity is the vertical deflection v(x).
The governing equation is E * I * (d^4v/dx^4) = 0 for 0 < x < L.
The boundary conditions are: v(0) = 0, v'(0) = 0, M(L)=E*I*v''(L)=0, and V(L)=E*I*v'''(L)= -P.
The maximum deflection occurs at x = L.
Calculate the maximum deflection using the formula v_max = (P * L^3) / (3 * E * I).
