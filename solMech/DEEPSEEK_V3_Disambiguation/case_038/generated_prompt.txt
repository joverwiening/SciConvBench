Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam is simply supported with a length L of 3500 mm.
The beam is subjected only to a uniformly distributed transverse load w of 0.5 N/mm.
The beam has a constant flexural rigidity EI.
Determine the deflection along the beam length.
Determine the slope along the beam length.
The governing equation is the Euler-Bernoulli beam equation: EI * (d⁴v/dx⁴) = w.
The boundary conditions are: deflection v = 0 at x = 0 and x = L, and bending moment M = 0 at x = 0 and x = L.
The solution for deflection is v(x) = (w * x / (24 * E * I)) * (L³ - 2*L*x² + x³).
The solution for slope is θ(x) = dv/dx = (w / (24 * E * I)) * (L³ - 6*L*x² + 4*x³).
The maximum deflection occurs at the mid-span, x = L/2.
The maximum slope occurs at the supports, x = 0 and x = L.
Report the deflection and slope as functions of position x.
Report the numerical maximum deflection and maximum slope values when the modulus of elasticity E and the second moment of area I are provided.
