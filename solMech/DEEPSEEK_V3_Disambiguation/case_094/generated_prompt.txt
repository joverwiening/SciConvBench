The beam is an Euler-Bernoulli beam of length L = 3500 mm.
The material is linear-elastic and deformations are small.
The beam is simply supported at both ends.
A uniformly distributed transverse load w = 0.3 N/mm is applied over the full span (0 ≤ x ≤ L).
A point load P = 1200 N is applied at the midspan (x = L/2).
The relevant response quantity is the vertical deflection v(x).
The deflection is determined using the principle of superposition.
The total deflection v(x) is the sum of the deflection due to the distributed load and the deflection due to the point load.
For a simply supported beam with a uniformly distributed load w over the full span, the deflection is v_w(x) = (w*x/(24*E*I))*(L^3 - 2*L*x^2 + x^3).
For a simply supported beam with a point load P at midspan, the deflection for 0 ≤ x ≤ L/2 is v_P(x) = (P*x/(48*E*I))*(3*L^2 - 4*x^2).
The total deflection is v(x) = v_w(x) + v_P(x).
The bending moment M(x) and shear force V(x) can also be found by superposition of the individual cases.
The flexural rigidity E*I of the beam is a required constant for numerical evaluation.
