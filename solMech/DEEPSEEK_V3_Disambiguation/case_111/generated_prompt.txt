The column is a solid circular cross-section made of structural steel.
The column length L is 1500 mm.
The column diameter d is 30 mm.
The column's effective length factor K is 2.0, corresponding to a fixed-free end condition.
The modulus of elasticity E for the steel is 200000 MPa.
The second moment of area I for the circular cross-section is calculated as (π * d^4) / 64.
The critical buckling load (Euler load) P_cr is calculated using the formula P_cr = (π² * E * I) / (K * L)².
The required factor of safety against buckling n is 2.0.
The allowable column load P_allow is calculated as P_allow = P_cr / n.
The factor of safety with respect to buckling for any applied load P_applied is calculated as P_cr / P_applied.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic material behavior.
Assume the column is perfectly straight and the load is perfectly axial.
Assume the buckling analysis is valid within the elastic range of the material.
Present the calculated values for I, P_cr, and P_allow.
State the governing relation for Euler buckling and for the allowable load.
