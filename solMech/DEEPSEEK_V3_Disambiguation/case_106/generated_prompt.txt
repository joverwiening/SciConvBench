The beam is a simply supported Euler-Bernoulli beam of length L = 2500 mm.
The beam is linear-elastic with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The beam's cross-section has a second moment of area I = 3500000 mm^4.
The beam is subjected only to a vertical point load P = 2500 N applied at midspan.
Small deformations and linear material behavior are assumed.
The relevant response quantities are the midspan deflection and the slope at the supports.
The governing relation for deflection under a midspan point load is v(x) = (Px/(48EI))(3L^2 - 4x^2) for 0 ≤ x ≤ L/2.
The midspan deflection is calculated as v_max = (P L^3) / (48 E I).
The slope at the left support (x=0) is calculated as θ_A = (P L^2) / (16 E I).
The slope at the right support (x=L) is calculated as θ_B = -(P L^2) / (16 E I).
