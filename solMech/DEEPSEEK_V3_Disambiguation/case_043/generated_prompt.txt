The column is a solid circular cross-section made of structural steel.
The column length L is 2000 mm.
The column diameter d is 20 mm.
The modulus of elasticity E is 200000 MPa.
The column is assumed to be perfectly straight, homogeneous, and isotropic.
The material behavior is linear elastic.
The ends of the column are pinned (simple supports) for the Euler buckling analysis.
The axial load is perfectly concentric, causing no initial bending.
The cross-sectional area A is calculated as (π * d²) / 4.
The minimum second moment of area I is calculated as (π * d⁴) / 64.
The Euler buckling load P_cr is calculated as (π² * E * I) / (L²).
The allowable column load P_allow is calculated as P_cr / n, where n is the factor of safety.
The specified factor of safety n with respect to buckling is 2.0.
The governing relation for the critical buckling load is P_cr = (π² * E * I) / (L²).
The governing relation for the allowable load is P_allow = P_cr / n.
The factor of safety with respect to buckling is defined as n = P_cr / P_applied, where P_applied is the actual applied axial load.
The relevant response quantities are the critical Euler buckling load P_cr and the allowable load P_allow.
