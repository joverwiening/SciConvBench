Assume a state of pure bending with a constant internal shear force V across the section of interest.
Assume the material is isotropic, homogeneous, and linear-elastic.
Assume the beam is prismatic with a constant rectangular cross-section.
Use the engineering theory for shear stress in beams (Jourawski's formula).
The transverse shear stress τ at a distance y from the neutral axis is given by τ(y) = V * Q(y) / (I * b).
The first moment of area Q(y) for a rectangular section is Q(y) = (b/2) * ((h^2/4) - y^2).
The second moment of area I for a rectangular section about the neutral axis is I = b * h^3 / 12.
The maximum shear stress occurs at the neutral axis (y=0) and is τ_max = (3/2) * (V / A), where A is the cross-sectional area.
Calculate the cross-sectional area A = b * h = (0.06 m) * (0.08 m) = 0.0048 m².
Calculate the maximum shear stress τ_max = (3/2) * (16000 N / 0.0048 m²) = 5,000,000 Pa = 5 MPa.
