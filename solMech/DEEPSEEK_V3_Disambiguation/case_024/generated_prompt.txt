The beam is a linear-elastic, small-deformation cantilever.
The beam length is L = 1500 mm.
The beam is made of structural steel.
The beam is subjected to a uniformly distributed transverse load w = 1.5 N/mm.
The beam's cross-sectional moment of inertia is I = 1500000 mm^4.
Assume the beam material is isotropic with a Young's modulus E = 200 GPa (typical for structural steel).
The governing equation for deflection v(x) under a uniformly distributed load w is E * I * (d^4v/dx^4) = w.
The boundary conditions for a cantilever fixed at x=0 are: v(0)=0, v'(0)=0, M(L)=0, V(L)=0.
The maximum deflection occurs at the free end (x = L).
Calculate the free-end deflection using the formula v_max = (w * L^4) / (8 * E * I).
Calculate the bending moment distribution as M(x) = (w/2) * (L - x)^2.
Calculate the shear force distribution as V(x) = w * (L - x).
Report the numerical value of the maximum deflection in mm.
Report the maximum bending moment (at x=0) in N-mm.
Report the maximum shear force (at x=0) in N.
