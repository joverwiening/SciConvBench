Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The rod is a straight, prismatic circular rod.
The rod is subjected to an axial tensile load of 20000 N.
The rod diameter is 25 mm.
The rod gage length is 800 mm.
The rod material has a Young's Modulus (E) of 200000 MPa.
The rod material has a yield strength of 250 MPa.
Calculate the cross-sectional area of the rod.
Calculate the average normal (axial) stress in the rod.
Calculate the average axial strain in the rod.
Calculate the total axial elongation of the rod.
Calculate the factor of safety with respect to yielding.
Present the governing relations for stress, strain, and elongation.
State the final calculated values for stress, strain, elongation, and factor of safety.
