The column length L is 2000 mm.
The column material is AISI 1020 steel, assumed to be linear-elastic with a Young's modulus E of 200 GPa.
The column has fixed-pinned end conditions, corresponding to an effective length factor K of 0.7.
Compute the Euler critical buckling load P_cr using the formula P_cr = (π² * E * I) / (K * L)².
Calculate the second moment of area I for the assumed circular cross-section.
The compressive load magnitude P_applied is not specified; therefore, only P_cr is determined as the relevant response quantity.
Perform a buckling check by stating that buckling occurs if any applied compressive load P_applied ≥ P_cr.
Present the governing relation P_cr = (π² E I) / (K L)² and the calculated numerical value for P_cr.
