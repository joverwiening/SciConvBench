The beam is a simply supported, linear-elastic, prismatic beam of length L = 3000 mm.
The beam is subjected to a single concentrated load P = 2000 N applied vertically downward at the midspan.
The beam material has a Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The beam cross-section has a constant second moment of area I = 3000000 mm^4.
Assume small deformations and that shear deformation is negligible.
The relevant response quantity is the vertical deflection at the midspan.
Use Castigliano's second theorem to calculate the midspan deflection.
The governing relation for midspan deflection (δ) under a central point load on a simply supported beam is δ = (P * L^3) / (48 * E * I).
