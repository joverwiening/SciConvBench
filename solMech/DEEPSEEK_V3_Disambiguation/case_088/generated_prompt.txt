The material is linear-elastic with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The stepped circular bar has two segments in series under axial tension.
The first segment has diameter d1 = 12 mm and length L1 = 300 mm.
The second segment has diameter d2 = 30 mm and length L2 = 800 mm.
A tensile force P = 35000 N is applied axially to the ends of the bar.
The stress in each segment is calculated as the applied force divided by the segment's cross-sectional area.
The cross-sectional area of the first segment is A1 = (π/4) * (d1)^2.
The cross-sectional area of the second segment is A2 = (π/4) * (d2)^2.
The stress in the first segment is σ1 = P / A1.
The stress in the second segment is σ2 = P / A2.
The elongation of each segment is calculated using the formula δ = (P * L) / (A * E).
The elongation of the first segment is δ1 = (P * L1) / (A1 * E).
The elongation of the second segment is δ2 = (P * L2) / (A2 * E).
The total elongation of the bar is δ_total = δ1 + δ2.
The analysis assumes small deformations and uniform, uniaxial stress within each segment.
The analysis assumes the force is applied centrally and the bar is prismatic within each segment.
The analysis neglects stress concentrations at the step.
