The analysis is for a simply supported Euler-Bernoulli beam under a midspan point load.
The beam length L is 3000 mm.
The midspan point load P is 1500 N.
No other distributed or shear loading is applied.
The beam material has a Young's modulus E of 200000 MPa.
The beam cross-section has a constant second moment of area I of 3500000 mm^4.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.
The relevant response quantities are the midspan deflection and the slope at the supports.
The governing relation for midspan deflection under a central point load is delta_max = (P * L^3) / (48 * E * I).
The governing relation for the slope at the left support under a central point load is theta_A = (P * L^2) / (16 * E * I).
The governing relation for the slope at the right support under a central point load is theta_B = -(P * L^2) / (16 * E * I).
