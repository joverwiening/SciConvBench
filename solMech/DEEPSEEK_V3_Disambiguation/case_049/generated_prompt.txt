Assume a state of pure torsion with no applied shear force.
The shaft is a hollow circular cylinder with an outer diameter of 50 mm and an inner diameter of 42 mm.
The shaft is made of a linear-elastic, isotropic material.
The shaft is subjected to a pure torque of 450000 N·mm.
Calculate the polar moment of inertia for the hollow cross-section.
Calculate the maximum shear stress in the shaft using the torsion formula.
Calculate the angle of twist per unit length of the shaft.
The governing relation for shear stress is τ_max = (T * r_o) / J, where T is torque, r_o is the outer radius, and J is the polar moment of inertia.
The governing relation for the angle of twist per unit length is θ = T / (G * J), where G is the shear modulus of the material.
The material's shear modulus, G, must be provided or assumed to calculate the angle of twist.
