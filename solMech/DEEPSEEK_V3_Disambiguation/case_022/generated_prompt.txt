The shaft is made of structural steel, assumed to be linear-elastic and isotropic.
The shaft consists of two solid circular segments in series.
Segment AB has a diameter d1 of 25 mm and a length L1 of 300 mm.
Segment BC has a diameter d2 of 30 mm and a length L2 of 700 mm.
End A of the shaft is fully fixed against rotation.
A torque T of 450000 N·mm is applied at the free end C.
The internal torque throughout the entire shaft is constant and equal to the applied torque T.
Calculate the polar moment of inertia J for each segment using J = (π*d^4)/32.
Calculate the maximum shear stress τ_max in each segment using τ_max = (T * r) / J, where r is the outer radius.
Calculate the angle of twist φ for each segment using φ = (T * L) / (J * G), where G is the shear modulus of the material.
Calculate the total angle of twist of the shaft as the sum of the twist angles of the two segments.
Assume a value for the shear modulus G of structural steel (e.g., 79.3 GPa) if not specified.
The relevant response quantities are the maximum shear stress in segment AB, the maximum shear stress in segment BC, and the total angle of twist at end C relative to the fixed end A.
