Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic material behavior.
Assume the 6061-T6 aluminum alloy is isotropic.
Assume the rod is prismatic with a constant circular cross-section.
The rod has an initial diameter of 40 mm.
The rod is subjected to an axial tensile load P of 35000 N.
There is no lateral load applied.
Calculate the axial (longitudinal) stress in the rod.
Calculate the axial (longitudinal) strain in the rod.
Calculate the transverse (lateral) strain due to the Poisson effect.
Use Young's modulus (E) of 68.9 GPa and Poisson's ratio (ν) of 0.33 for 6061-T6 aluminum.
The axial stress σ_axial is P divided by the initial cross-sectional area A0.
The axial strain ε_axial is σ_axial divided by E.
The transverse strain ε_transverse is -ν multiplied by ε_axial.
The change in rod diameter is ε_transverse multiplied by the initial diameter.
