The shaft is a solid circular shaft made of AISI 1020 steel.
The shaft length between fixed ends is L = 1000 mm.
The shaft diameter is d = 35 mm.
The shaft transmits a power of P = 10 kW at a rotational speed of n = 1800 rpm.
The material's modulus of rigidity (shear modulus) is G = 76923.1 MPa.
Assume a yield strength in shear, τ_y, of 0.577 times the typical tensile yield strength of AISI 1020 steel (207 MPa), resulting in τ_y = 119.4 MPa.
Assume a factor of safety of 2.0 for the torsion check, based on general machinery applications.
The angular speed ω is calculated as ω = (2π * n) / 60.
The applied torque T is calculated from power and angular speed using T = P / ω.
The polar moment of inertia J for the solid circular cross-section is calculated as J = (π * d^4) / 32.
The maximum shear stress τ_max due to torsion is calculated using τ_max = (T * d) / (2 * J).
The angle of twist θ over the shaft length is calculated using θ = (T * L) / (G * J).
Check that the calculated maximum shear stress τ_max is less than the allowable shear stress τ_allow = τ_y / (Factor of Safety).
Present the computed values for torque T, maximum shear stress τ_max, allowable shear stress τ_allow, and angle of twist θ.
