The problem is a stepped solid circular shaft under pure torsion.
The shaft is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with a shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa.
Segment AB has a length L1 = 300 mm and a diameter d1 = 20 mm.
Segment BC has a length L2 = 500 mm and a diameter d2 = 30 mm.
End A of the shaft is fully fixed.
A torque T = 300000 N·mm is applied at the free end C.
The internal torque is constant throughout the entire shaft and equal to the applied torque T.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic, isotropic material behavior.
The analysis assumes Saint-Venant's principle applies at the step.
The analysis neglects stress concentrations at the step.
The polar moment of inertia for segment AB is J1 = (π * d1^4) / 32.
The polar moment of inertia for segment BC is J2 = (π * d2^4) / 32.
The maximum shear stress in segment AB is τ_max1 = (T * d1) / (2 * J1).
The maximum shear stress in segment BC is τ_max2 = (T * d2) / (2 * J2).
The angle of twist for segment AB is φ1 = (T * L1) / (G * J1).
The angle of twist for segment BC is φ2 = (T * L2) / (G * J2).
The total angle of twist at end C relative to the fixed end A is φ_total = φ1 + φ2.
The required response quantities are τ_max1, τ_max2, and φ_total.
