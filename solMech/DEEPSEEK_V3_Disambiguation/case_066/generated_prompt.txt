The system is a solid circular shaft under pure torsion.
The shaft is made of a linear-elastic, isotropic material.
The shaft is prismatic with a constant circular cross-section.
The shaft length L is 1000 mm.
The shaft diameter d is 45 mm.
The applied torque T is 400000 N·mm.
The material has a shear modulus G of 80 GPa (80000 N/mm²).
Calculate the polar moment of inertia J for the circular cross-section using the formula J = (π * d⁴) / 32.
Calculate the maximum shear stress τ_max at the outer fiber using the torsion formula τ_max = (T * r) / J, where r = d/2.
Calculate the angle of twist φ over the shaft length using the formula φ = (T * L) / (G * J).
Report the maximum shear stress in N/mm² (MPa).
Report the angle of twist in radians.
