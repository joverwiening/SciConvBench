Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics unless otherwise required.
A ductile metal tension specimen is subjected to tension loading.
The initial gauge length L0 is 50 mm.
The initial diameter d0 is 12 mm.
The final gauge length Lf after fracture is 66 mm.
The final diameter at the neck after fracture, df, is 9.6 mm.
Calculate percent elongation as ((Lf - L0) / L0) × 100%.
Calculate percent reduction of area as ((A0 - Af) / A0) × 100%, where A0 = π × (d0/2)² and Af = π × (df/2)².
The specimen is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The deformation is uniform until necking, after which all further elongation is localized to the neck.
The reported final gauge length Lf is measured by fitting the fractured specimen back together.
The reported final diameter df is measured at the smallest cross-section of the neck.
Present the calculated values for percent elongation and percent reduction of area.
