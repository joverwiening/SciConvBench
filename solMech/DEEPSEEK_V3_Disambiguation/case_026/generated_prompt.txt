The analysis is for a cantilever beam under a downward point load at its free end.
The beam length L is 1500 mm.
The beam material has a Young's modulus E of 200000 MPa.
A point load P of 500 N is applied downward at the free end.
The beam's cross-section has a second moment of area I of 800000 mm^4.
The material is assumed to be linear-elastic and isotropic.
The analysis assumes small deformations and Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.
The deflection at the free end is the relevant response quantity.
Use Castigliano's second theorem to determine the free end deflection.
The strain energy U in the beam is (1/(2EI)) * ∫_0^L M(x)^2 dx.
The bending moment M(x) at a distance x from the fixed end is -P*x.
The partial derivative of the strain energy with respect to the load P is δ = ∂U/∂P.
The resulting deflection δ at the free end is (P * L^3) / (3EI).
Substitute the given values to compute the numerical deflection.
