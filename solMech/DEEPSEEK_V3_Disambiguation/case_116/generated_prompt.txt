The analysis is for a hollow circular shaft under pure torsion.
The shaft is made of a linear-elastic, isotropic material.
The shaft length is 800 mm.
The outer diameter is 50 mm.
The inner diameter is 44 mm.
The shaft is subjected to a constant torque of 600000 N·mm.
The analysis assumes small deformations and Saint-Venant's principle applies away from the ends.
The relevant response quantities are shear stress, angle of twist, and torsional stiffness.
The polar moment of inertia for the hollow cross-section is calculated.
The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer radius.
The shear stress distribution is linear from the inner to outer radius.
The angle of twist is calculated using the torque, length, shear modulus, and polar moment of inertia.
The governing relation for shear stress is τ = (T * r) / J.
The governing relation for angle of twist is θ = (T * L) / (G * J).
The material's shear modulus, G, is required to compute the angle of twist.
