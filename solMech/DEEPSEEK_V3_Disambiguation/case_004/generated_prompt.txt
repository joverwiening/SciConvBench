Assume the prismatic bar has a solid circular cross-section.
The bar is made of AISI 1020 steel, modeled as a linear-elastic, isotropic material.
The bar has a total length of 1500 mm, divided into segments AB (500 mm), BC (600 mm), and CD (400 mm).
The bar has a uniform cross-sectional area of 300 mm².
The left end A is fully fixed, providing zero axial displacement.
Three axial point loads are applied: a force of +18000 N at point B, a force of -12000 N at point C, and a force of +16000 N at point D.
The relevant response quantities to determine are the axial force (N) in each segment, the axial stress (σ) in each segment, the axial strain (ε) in each segment, and the axial displacement (u) at points B, C, and D.
The governing relation for axial force is equilibrium: N(x) is constant between loads and changes at load points.
The governing constitutive relation is σ = E * ε, where E is Young's modulus for AISI 1020 steel.
The governing kinematic relation is ε = du/dx.
