The shaft is made of AISI 1020 steel.
The shaft length L is 1000 mm.
The shaft outer diameter d is 30 mm.
The applied torque T is 250000 N·mm.
The modulus of rigidity G is 76923.1 MPa.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
Assume the shaft is subjected to pure torsion.
Assume the shaft is a homogeneous, isotropic, prismatic, solid circular shaft.
The polar moment of inertia J for the solid circular cross-section is (π*d⁴)/32.
The maximum shear stress τ_max occurs at the outer surface of the shaft.
The governing relation for maximum shear stress is τ_max = (T * r) / J, where r is the outer radius (d/2).
The angle of twist φ is the angular deformation over the shaft length.
The governing relation for the angle of twist is φ = (T * L) / (G * J).
Calculate the polar moment of inertia J.
Calculate the outer radius r.
Calculate the maximum shear stress τ_max.
Calculate the angle of twist φ.
Report τ_max in MPa.
Report φ in radians.
