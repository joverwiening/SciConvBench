The problem is a stepped circular bar under axial tension.
The bar has two segments, both with length 400 mm.
The first segment has a diameter of 16 mm.
The second segment has a diameter of 40 mm.
The material is linear elastic with a Young's modulus of 200,000 MPa.
The bar is subjected to an axial tensile force of 25,000 N.
Assume small deformations and static loading.
Assume the load is applied axially and uniformly at the ends.
Assume stress is uniform across each cross-section.
Calculate the cross-sectional area of the first segment: A1 = π*(d1/2)^2.
Calculate the cross-sectional area of the second segment: A2 = π*(d2/2)^2.
The axial stress in the first segment is σ1 = P / A1.
The axial stress in the second segment is σ2 = P / A2.
The axial strain in the first segment is ε1 = σ1 / E.
The axial strain in the second segment is ε2 = σ2 / E.
The elongation of the first segment is δ1 = ε1 * L1.
The elongation of the second segment is δ2 = ε2 * L2.
The total elongation of the bar is δ_total = δ1 + δ2.
Report the numerical values for σ1, σ2, and δ_total.
