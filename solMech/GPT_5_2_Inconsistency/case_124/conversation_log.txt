Q: For the angle of twist, should I use J = π d^4/32 (as you later specify), or should I instead follow your earlier instruction to use I = π d^4/64 in the torsion relations?
A: Use **J = π d⁴/32** for the angle of twist (and for shear stress). The COMPLETE requirement specifies **φ = TL/(JG)** with **J = π d⁴/32**, so ignore the earlier mention of using **I** in torsion relations.
