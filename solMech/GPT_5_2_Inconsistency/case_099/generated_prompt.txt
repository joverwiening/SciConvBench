Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model the rod as a linear elastic axial spring with stiffness k = AE/L.
Neglect the mass of the rod.
Given L = 800 mm.
Given d = 25 mm.
Compute cross-sectional area as A = π d^2/4.
Given E = 200000 MPa.
Given dropped weight force W = 1500 N.
Given drop height h = 200 mm.
Define maximum axial force during impact as P_max.
Relate maximum elongation to force by δ_max = P_max L/(AE).
Apply energy conservation at maximum elongation: W(h + δ_max) = (P_max^2 L)/(2AE).
Substitute δ_max into the energy equation to obtain a single equation in P_max.
Solve the resulting quadratic equation for P_max and select the positive real root.
Compute maximum normal stress as σ_max = P_max/A.
Report P_max in N and σ_max in MPa.
