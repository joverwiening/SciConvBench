Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model a rectangular beam cross section with width b = 40 mm and height h = 80 mm.
Use internal shear force V = 25000 N at the section.
Take the neutral axis at mid-depth with y = 0 at the neutral axis and y positive upward.
For part (a), compute the maximum transverse shear stress using τ_max = 1.5 V/A.
Compute the cross-sectional area as A = b h.
Report τ_max in MPa.
For part (b), compute the shear stress using τ = VQ/(I b).
Compute the second moment of area as I = b h^3/12 about the neutral axis.
Evaluate τ at y = +20 mm from the neutral axis.
For Q, use the first moment of area of the portion of the cross section above the level y, i.e., the area from y = +20 mm up to the top surface.
Compute Q about the neutral axis as Q = A_above * ȳ_above, where A_above is the area above y = +20 mm and ȳ_above is the centroid location of that area measured from the neutral axis.
Use b = 40 mm as the thickness in the shear formula denominator.
Report τ(y = +20 mm) in MPa.
