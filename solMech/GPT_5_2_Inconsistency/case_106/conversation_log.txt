Q: Do you want the deflection computed using the correct piecewise bending moment diagram for a midspan point load, or do you want me to follow the instruction to use the constant maximum moment M = P L/4 over the full span (even though it is not physically correct)?
A: Use the correct **piecewise bending moment diagram** for a midspan point load (i.e., define \(M(x)\) piecewise over \(0 \le x \le L\)) and integrate \(U=\int \frac{M^2}{2EI}\,dx\). Do **not** use a constant \(M=PL/4\) over the full span.
