Use N–mm–MPa units consistently.
Model a solid circular shaft with diameter d = 30 mm and length L = 1000 mm.
Use material shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa.
Apply a constant torque T = 450000 N·mm along the full length.
Compute the bending second moment I = π d^4/64 for reference, but use the polar moment J = π d^4/32 in the torsional strain-energy expression.
Use torsional strain energy U = ∫(0→L) [T^2/(2 G J)] dx with constant T, G, and J to obtain U = T^2 L/(2 G J).
Apply Castigliano’s theorem for twist: φ = ∂U/∂T = T L/(G J).
Compute J = (π/32) d^4 = (π/32)(30^4) = 79577.5 mm^4.
Compute φ = (450000×1000)/(25939.8×79577.5) = 0.218 rad.
Report the final angle of twist as φ = 0.218 rad (3 significant figures).
