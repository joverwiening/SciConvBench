Q: When you say “by symmetry, take the slope to vanish at both supports as well as at midspan,” do you want me to enforce slope = 0 at the supports as a boundary condition (even though a simply supported beam generally has nonzero support slope)?
A: No—do **not** enforce slope = 0 at the supports.

For a simply supported beam, the correct boundary conditions are:
- \(v(0)=0\), \(v(L)=0\) (deflection zero at supports)

By symmetry for a midspan point load, enforce:
- \(v'(L/2)=0\) (slope zero at midspan)

Then **compute** the support slope \(v'(0)\) and \(v'(L)\); they will be equal in magnitude and generally nonzero.
