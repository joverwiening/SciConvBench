Q: For Euler buckling of this column, should I use the standard second moment of area for bending I = π d^4/64 (not the polar moment J), since buckling occurs about a bending axis rather than torsion?
A: Use the standard **area moment of inertia for bending**, not the polar moment.

So for Euler buckling here, take:

- \( I = \dfrac{\pi d^{4}}{64} \)

Do **not** use \( J = \dfrac{\pi d^{4}}{32} \) in the Euler buckling formula.
