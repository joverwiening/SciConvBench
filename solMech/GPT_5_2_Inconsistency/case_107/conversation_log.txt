Q: Do you want me to follow the instruction “use the maximum bending moment M = P L/4 over the full span” (constant M for 0≤x≤L), or should I use the correct piecewise bending moment diagram for a midspan point load?
A: Use the correct **piecewise bending moment diagram** for a midspan point load. Define \(M(x)\) piecewise over \(0 \le x \le L\) and integrate \(U=\int_0^L \frac{M^2}{2EI}\,dx\) accordingly.
