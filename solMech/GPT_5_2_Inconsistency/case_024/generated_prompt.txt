Use N–mm–MPa units and Euler–Bernoulli beam theory with small deflection assumptions.
Model a cantilever beam of length L = 1500 mm fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
Apply a uniformly distributed load w = 1.5 N/mm downward over 0 ≤ x ≤ L.
Use material modulus E = 200000 MPa and second moment of area I = 1500000 mm^4.
Compute the free-end slope for a cantilever under full-span UDL as θ(L) = w L^3 / (6 E I).
Compute the free-end (maximum) deflection for a cantilever under full-span UDL as v(L) = w L^4 / (8 E I).
Evaluate numerically: θ(L) = (1.5)(1500^3) / (6·200000·1500000) = 0.0028125 rad (downward rotation).
Evaluate numerically: v(L) = (1.5)(1500^4) / (8·200000·1500000) = 5.2734375 mm downward.
Report maximum deflection at the free end as 5.273 mm downward.
Report slope at the free end as 0.002813 rad (≈ 0.161°) downward.
