Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Given a rectangular cross section with b = 50 mm and h = 80 mm.
Given internal shear force V = 20000 N.
Take the cross-sectional area as A = b h.
For part (a), compute the maximum transverse shear stress using τ_max = 1.5 V/A and report in MPa.
For part (b), use the shear formula τ(y) = V Q(y) / (I b) and report in MPa.
Use the second moment of area about the neutral axis as I = b h^3 / 12.
Define y = 0 at the neutral axis and y positive upward.
Evaluate part (b) at y = +35 mm (above the neutral axis, toward the top surface).
Compute Q(y) as the first moment of area of the portion of the cross section above the horizontal cut at y = +35 mm, taken about the neutral axis.
For Q(y), use Q = A′ ȳ where A′ is the area above the cut and ȳ is the distance from the neutral axis to the centroid of that area.
Use b = 50 mm as the width at the level of the cut in τ = VQ/(I b).
Output τ_max for part (a) and τ(y=+35 mm) for part (b), both in MPa.
