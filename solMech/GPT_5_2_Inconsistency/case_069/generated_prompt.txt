Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Given E = 200000 MPa, I = 2500000 mm^4, and M = 3000000 N·mm.
Compute curvature using κ = M/(E·I) with I (not polar J).
Convert E to consistent units as E = 200000 N/mm^2.
Compute E·I = 200000 × 2500000 = 5.0×10^11 N·mm^2.
Compute κ = 3000000 / (5.0×10^11) = 6.0×10^-6 mm^-1.
Compute radius of curvature as ρ = 1/κ = 1 / (6.0×10^-6) = 1.6666667×10^5 mm.
Report κ = 6.0×10^-6 mm^-1 and ρ ≈ 1.67×10^5 mm.
