Use N–mm–MPa units.
Use E = 200000 MPa and I = 1800000 mm^4.
Use L = 2500 mm and K = 1.0.
Compute the Euler critical buckling load as P_cr = π^2 E I / (K L)^2.
Evaluate E I = 200000 × 1800000 = 3.6×10^11 N·mm^2.
Evaluate (K L)^2 = (1.0 × 2500)^2 = 6.25×10^6 mm^2.
Compute P_cr = π^2 × (3.6×10^11) / (6.25×10^6) = 5.6849×10^5 N.
Report P_cr = 568,490 N.
Report P_cr = 568.49 kN.
