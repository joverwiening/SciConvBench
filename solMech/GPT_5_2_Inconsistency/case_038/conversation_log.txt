Q: When you say “take the end slopes to be zero,” do you mean impose boundary conditions θ(0)=0 and θ(L)=0 (in addition to y(0)=0 and y(L)=0)?
A: No. Do **not** impose zero end slopes for a simply supported beam.

Use the standard simply supported boundary conditions:
- \(y(0)=0\), \(y(L)=0\)
- \(M(0)=0\), \(M(L)=0\) (equivalently \(y''(0)=0\), \(y''(L)=0\))

The slope at \(x=0\) is **not** zero and should be computed.

Q: Confirm the coordinate convention for x: is x measured from the left support with x=0 at the left support and x=L at the right support?
A: Yes—take \(x\) measured from the left support, with \(x=0\) at the left support and \(x=L=3500\ \text{mm}\) at the right support.
