Use N–mm–MPa units.
Given L = 1000 mm.
Given E = 200000 MPa.
Given I = 1200000 mm^4.
Given effective length factor K = 2.0 for fixed–free end conditions.
Compute Euler critical buckling load using P_cr = π^2 E I / (K L)^2.
Compute E I = 200000 × 1200000 = 2.4×10^11 N·mm^2.
Compute (K L)^2 = (2.0 × 1000)^2 = 4.0×10^6 mm^2.
Compute P_cr = π^2 × (2.4×10^11) / (4.0×10^6) = π^2 × 6.0×10^4 N.
Compute P_cr ≈ 9.8696 × 60000 = 592176 N.
Report P_cr = 592176 N.
Report P_cr = 592.176 kN.
