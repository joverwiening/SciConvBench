Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model a prismatic beam with a rectangular cross section of width b = 50 mm and height h = 100 mm.
At the critical section, use an internal bending moment about the centroidal z-axis of M = +3,000,000 N·mm.
Adopt the sign convention that a sagging moment is positive.
Assume the pure-bending normal stress is uniform over the section depth so that the stress at the neutral axis has the same sign as at the extreme fibers.
Use the flexure formula σ = M y / I with y measured from the neutral axis.
Compute the second moment of area about the centroidal z-axis as I = b h^3 / 12.
Use the extreme-fiber distance c = h/2.
Compute the maximum bending normal stress magnitude as σ_max = M c / I.
Report σ_max in MPa.
State that for a sagging moment the top fiber is in compression and the bottom fiber is in tension.
For the given values, I = (50)(100^3)/12 = 4.1667×10^6 mm^4.
For the given values, c = 50 mm.
