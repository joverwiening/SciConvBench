Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Take y = +30 mm and z = +15 mm at the point.
Use Iy = 2,500,000 mm^4, Iz = 1,200,000 mm^4, Iyz = 800,000 mm^4.
Use My = 4,000,000 N·mm and Mz = 2,000,000 N·mm.
Compute the denominator D = Iy·Iz − Iyz^2 = (2,500,000)(1,200,000) − (800,000)^2 = 2.36×10^12 mm^8.
Compute A = (My·Iz − Mz·Iyz)/D = [(4,000,000)(1,200,000) − (2,000,000)(800,000)]/(2.36×10^12) = 0.0013559322 mm^-3.
Compute B = (Mz·Iy − My·Iyz)/D = [(2,000,000)(2,500,000) − (4,000,000)(800,000)]/(2.36×10^12) = 0.0007627119 mm^-3.
Compute σ = −A·z − B·y = −(0.0013559322)(15) − (0.0007627119)(30) N/mm^2.
Report σ = −0.04322034 N/mm^2 = −0.04322 MPa.
Use tension positive, so the negative sign indicates compression.
