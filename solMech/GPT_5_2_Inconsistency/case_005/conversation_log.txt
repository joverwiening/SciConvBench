Q: Do you want the internal axial force to be computed correctly as piecewise-constant by segment (changing at B and C), or do you want to follow your instruction to treat it as uniform along the full length based on the net applied load?
A: Compute it correctly as **piecewise-constant by segment**, changing at **B** and **C** (and reflecting the load at **D** via overall equilibrium). Do **not** treat it as uniform based on the net applied load.
