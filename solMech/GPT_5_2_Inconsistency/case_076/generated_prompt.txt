Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model a solid circular shaft in torsion with length L = 1200 mm.
Use applied torque T = 800000 N·mm.
Use shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa (i.e., N/mm²).
Use the torsion angle-of-twist relation φ = TL/(JG).
Treat φ physically correctly in radians.
Convert the allowable twist φ_allow = 1.0° to radians before substitution.
Use the polar moment of inertia for a solid circular shaft J = (π d^4)/32.
Solve for the minimum diameter d such that φ ≤ φ_allow.
Compute d from d = [32 T L / (π G φ_allow)]^(1/4).
Report d in mm.
