Q: In the torsion formula φ = TL/(JG), do you want φ treated physically correctly in radians (i.e., convert 1.0° to radians), or do you want to follow your instruction and use 1.0° directly without conversion?
A: Convert 1.0° to radians and use φ in radians in \( \phi = \dfrac{TL}{JG} \). The “no radian conversion needed” instruction is not part of the complete requirement.
