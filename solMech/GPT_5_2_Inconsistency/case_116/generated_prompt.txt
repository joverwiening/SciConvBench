Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model a rectangular beam cross section with width b = 60 mm and height h = 80 mm.
Use internal shear force V = 16000 N at the section.
Define the neutral axis at mid-depth with y = 0 at the neutral axis and y positive upward.
Compute cross-sectional area as A = b h.
(a) Compute maximum transverse shear stress using τ_max = 1.5 V/A and report in MPa.
Compute second moment of area about the neutral axis as I = b h^3/12.
(b) Compute shear stress at y = +26.667 mm using τ = V Q / (I b) and report in MPa.
For part (b), compute Q as the first moment of area of the portion of the cross section above the level y, i.e., from y = +26.667 mm up to the top surface.
For part (b), use Q = A_above * ȳ_above where A_above is the area above the cut and ȳ_above is the distance from the neutral axis to the centroid of that above area.
Use b = 60 mm as the thickness in the shear formula denominator.
