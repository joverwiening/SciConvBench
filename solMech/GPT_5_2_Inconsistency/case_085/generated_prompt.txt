Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Assume linear-elastic, small-deformation axial behavior with E = 200000 MPa.
Model the bar as two circular segments in series: Segment 1 has L1 = 300 mm and d1 = 12 mm, Segment 2 has L2 = 800 mm and d2 = 30 mm.
Apply an axial tensile load P = 35000 N at the right end, with the left end fixed.
Assume the internal axial force is the same in both segments and equals P.
Compute cross-sectional areas as A1 = (π/4)d1^2 and A2 = (π/4)d2^2.
Compute normal stress in each segment using σ1 = P/A1 and σ2 = P/A2 (do not force stresses to be identical).
Compute elongation of each segment using δ1 = PL1/(A1E) and δ2 = PL2/(A2E).
Compute total elongation between the fixed end and the load point as δtotal = δ1 + δ2.
Report (i) σ1 and σ2 in MPa and (ii) δtotal in mm.
