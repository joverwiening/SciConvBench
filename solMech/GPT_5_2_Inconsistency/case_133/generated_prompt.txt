Use N and mm as the base units, and report V in N and M in N·mm.
Model a simply supported beam AB with span L = 6000 mm.
Apply a single concentrated load P = 20000 N downward at x = 2000 mm from support A.
Use the final sign convention: positive shear V is upward on the left cut face, and positive bending moment M is sagging (tension at bottom fibers).
Compute support reactions from global equilibrium: RA + RB = 20000 N, and taking moments about A gives RB·6000 − 20000·2000 = 0, so RB = 6666.6667 N and RA = 13333.3333 N.
Evaluate internal actions at the section x = 4500 mm from A (which lies to the right of the point load).
Using the left segment free-body (from A to the cut), the internal shear at x = 4500 mm is V = RA − P = 13333.3333 − 20000 = −6666.6667 N (negative means actual shear is downward on the left cut face).
Using the same left segment, the internal bending moment at x = 4500 mm is M = RA·x − P·(x − 2000) = 13333.3333·4500 − 20000·2500 = 10000000 N·mm (positive sagging).
Final results at x = 4500 mm: V = −6666.67 N, M = +10000000 N·mm.
