Q: Your instruction says to “treat the internal axial force as uniform along the full length and compute one stress level from the net applied load,” but the request also asks for the internal axial force in each segment (which would generally differ when point loads act at B and C).

Which approach should I follow: piecewise-constant internal force per segment (AB, BC, CD), or a single uniform internal force based on the net applied load?
A: Follow the **piecewise-constant internal axial force per segment (AB, BC, CD)** approach.

Do **not** assume a single uniform internal force from the net applied load; that statement is not part of the complete requirement.
