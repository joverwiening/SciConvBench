Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model a 1D axially loaded prismatic bar with constant cross-sectional area A = 500 mm^2 and modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Use the geometry AB = 300 mm, BC = 600 mm, CD = 300 mm.
Define the axial coordinate x positive to the right.
Apply concentrated axial forces at the points with the sign convention “positive to the right”: at B, +18000 N; at C, −12000 N; at D, +10000 N.
Impose the boundary condition u_A = 0 at the left end A.
Compute support reaction at A from global axial equilibrium of the entire bar.
Compute the internal axial force N(x) as piecewise-constant over each segment AB, BC, and CD using axial force equilibrium of a cut section, with tension taken as positive.
Report the internal axial force in segment AB, in segment BC, and in segment CD.
Compute the normal stress in each segment as σ = N/A using the segment’s internal axial force and the constant area.
Compute the axial strain in each segment as ε = σ/E.
Compute the displacement of point D relative to A as u_D − u_A = Σ(ε_i L_i) over segments AB, BC, and CD, using each segment’s length and strain.
Report u_D relative to A in mm, with sign consistent with the positive-to-the-right displacement convention.
