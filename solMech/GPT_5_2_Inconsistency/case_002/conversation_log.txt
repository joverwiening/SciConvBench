Q: Do you want me to follow your stated assumptions (equal stress in both segments and common elongation) even though they contradict standard axial bar behavior in series?
A: No—do **not** follow those stated assumptions.

Use standard axial bar behavior for two segments **in series**:
- The **axial force is the same** in both segments (equal to \(P\)).
- The **stress is generally different** in each segment because the areas differ.
- The **total elongation** is the **sum** of the elongations of the two segments, \(\delta=\delta_1+\delta_2\) (they do **not** have a common elongation).
