Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Assume linear-elastic, small-deformation axial behavior with E = 200000 MPa.
Model the bar as two circular segments in series: segment 1 has L1 = 400 mm and d1 = 16 mm, segment 2 has L2 = 800 mm and d2 = 40 mm.
Apply an axial tensile load P = 35000 N at the right end, with the left end fixed.
Enforce equilibrium for series members: the internal axial force in each segment equals P.
Compute cross-sectional areas as A1 = (π/4)d1^2 and A2 = (π/4)d2^2 in mm^2.
Compute normal stress in each segment as σ1 = P/A1 and σ2 = P/A2, and report σ1 and σ2 in MPa.
Compute elongation of each segment as δ1 = (P L1)/(A1 E) and δ2 = (P L2)/(A2 E), and report δ1 and δ2 in mm.
Compute total elongation between the fixed end and the load point as δtotal = δ1 + δ2, and report δtotal in mm.
