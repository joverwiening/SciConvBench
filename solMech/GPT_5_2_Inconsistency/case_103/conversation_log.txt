Q: When you say “by symmetry, take the slope to vanish at both supports as well as at midspan,” do you want me to enforce zero slope at the supports as a boundary condition (even though a simply supported beam normally has nonzero support slope)?
A: No—do **not** enforce zero slope at the supports.

Use the standard simply supported boundary conditions: **deflection v = 0 at x = 0 and x = L**. By symmetry, enforce **slope v′ = 0 at midspan (x = L/2)**. Then **compute** the support slopes from the solution (they will be nonzero and equal in magnitude, opposite in sign).
