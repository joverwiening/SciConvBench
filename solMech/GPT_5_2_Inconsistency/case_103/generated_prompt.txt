Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model the beam with Euler–Bernoulli theory and neglect shear deformation.
Given L = 2500 mm, E = 200000 MPa, I = 3500000 mm^4, and a midspan point load P = 2500 N downward.
Use the standard simply supported boundary conditions v(0) = 0 and v(L) = 0.
Use symmetry to enforce v′(L/2) = 0.
Use the standard closed-form results for a simply supported beam with a central point load.
Compute maximum deflection at midspan as v_max = P L^3 / (48 E I).
Compute support slope magnitude as |v′(0)| = |v′(L)| = P L^2 / (16 E I), with v′(0) negative and v′(L) positive under the usual sign convention (downward deflection positive).

Numerical results:
Compute v_max = (2500 × 2500^3) / (48 × 200000 × 3500000) mm = 1.162 mm downward at midspan.
Compute v′(0) = −(2500 × 2500^2) / (16 × 200000 × 3500000) rad = −0.001395 rad.
Compute v′(L) = +(2500 × 2500^2) / (16 × 200000 × 3500000) rad = +0.001395 rad.
