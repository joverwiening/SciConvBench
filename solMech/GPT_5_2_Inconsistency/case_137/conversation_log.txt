Q: In your statement you say “at the outer surface where the bending normal stress is maximum, take the torsional shear stress to vanish,” but in part (b) and (c) you compute and use the torsional shear stress at the surface; which assumption should govern the surface point for parts (b) and (c): τ = 0 or τ = T c / J?
A: Use **τ = T c / J** at the outer surface for **parts (b) and (c)**.

The phrase about “taking the torsional shear stress to vanish” is **not** to be applied here; it conflicts with the stated requirements for (b) and (c). For this problem, the surface point is the one with **maximum bending normal stress**, and it **also** carries the torsional shear stress from the applied torque.
