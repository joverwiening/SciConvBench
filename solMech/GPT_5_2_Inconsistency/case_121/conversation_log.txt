Q: When you say “treat the pure-bending normal stress as uniform over the section depth,” what constant stress value should be used (e.g., equal to the extreme-fiber stress from σ = M c / I, equal to the average over the depth, or some specified magnitude)?
A: Don’t treat it as uniform. Use the standard linear bending-stress distribution:

\[
\sigma(y)= -\frac{M\,y}{I}
\]

(with \(y\) positive upward and \(M\) sagging).

=== 2026-04-08 17:15:01 ===
Q: What sign convention do you want for “sagging” in the formula (i.e., should sagging produce compression at the top fiber and tension at the bottom fiber)?
A: Use the standard convention: for a sagging moment, the **top fiber is in compression (negative stress)** and the **bottom fiber is in tension (positive stress)**, with \(y\) positive upward in \(\sigma(y) = -\dfrac{M y}{I}\).
