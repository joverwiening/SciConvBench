Q: Do you want the physically correct solution for a fully constrained rod (zero total axial strain), or do you want me to follow your stated assumption that the stress vanishes despite unyielding supports?
A: Use the physically correct solution for a fully constrained rod: total change in length is zero (net axial strain = 0), so a compressive thermal stress develops. Do **not** follow the “stress vanishes” assumption.
