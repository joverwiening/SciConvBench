Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Given E = 200000 MPa.
Given I = 1200000 mm^4.
Given M = 3000000 N·mm.
Compute curvature using κ = M/(E I) with I (do not use polar second moment J).
Compute E I = 200000 × 1200000 = 2.4×10^11 N·mm^2.
Compute κ = 3000000 / (2.4×10^11) = 1.25×10^-5 mm^-1.
Compute radius of curvature ρ = 1/κ = 80000 mm.
