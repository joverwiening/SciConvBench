Q: By “simply supported,” do you intend the usual boundary condition v(0)=0 and v(L)=0 (with nonzero support slopes), or do you truly want to enforce zero slope at both supports as stated?
A: Use the usual simply supported boundary conditions: \(v(0)=0\) and \(v(L)=0\), with *nonzero* slopes at the supports.

Do **not** enforce zero slope at the supports; the “slope vanishes at both supports” line is not intended. By symmetry, only the slope at midspan is zero: \(v'(L/2)=0\).
