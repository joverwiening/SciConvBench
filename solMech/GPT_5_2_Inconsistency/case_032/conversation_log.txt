Q: When you say “by symmetry, take the slope to vanish at both supports,” do you actually mean the deflection vanishes at both supports (v(0)=v(L)=0) rather than the slope?
A: Yes—use the standard simply supported boundary conditions: **deflection is zero at both supports**, i.e. **v(0)=v(L)=0**.  

By symmetry for a midspan point load, the **slope is zero at midspan**: **v′(L/2)=0**.  

The **slope at the supports is not zero** (it’s what you’re asked to compute).
