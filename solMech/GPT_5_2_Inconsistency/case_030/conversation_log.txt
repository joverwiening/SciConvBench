Q: For the “plane strain in the two transverse directions” condition, should I treat both transverse strains as zero (εy = εz = 0)?
A: No. Do **not** apply plane strain here.

Use the **standard uniaxial stress** relations for a free rod:
- Transverse (lateral/diametral) strain is **not zero**; it is \( \varepsilon_{\text{lat}} = -\nu\,\varepsilon_x \).

Q: What is the required sign convention for the diameter change Δd (report Δd as a negative value for contraction, or report its magnitude as a positive value)?
A: The complete requirement doesn’t specify a sign convention for Δd. Make a reasonable assumption based on best practices: report **Δd with sign**, i.e., **negative for contraction** under tensile loading (since the lateral strain is negative).
