Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model the rod as linear elastic and isotropic with E = 200000 MPa and ν = 0.29.
Treat the loading as standard uniaxial tension of a free rod (no plane strain constraints).
Given d0 = 25 mm, L = 1200 mm, and axial tensile load P = 45000 N applied through the centroid.
Compute cross-sectional area as A = (π/4) d0^2.
Compute (i) average axial normal stress as σx = P/A in MPa (since 1 MPa = 1 N/mm^2).
Compute (ii) axial strain as εx = σx/E.
Compute (iii) lateral (diametral) strain as εlat = −ν εx.
Compute (iv) change in diameter as Δd = εlat d0.
Report Δd with sign, taking contraction under tensile loading as negative.
