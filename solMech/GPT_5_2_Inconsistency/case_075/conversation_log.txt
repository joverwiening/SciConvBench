Q: Which sign convention should I use for this problem: the first one you stated (positive V upward on the left cut face and positive M sagging) or the later one (positive V downward on the left cut face and positive M hogging)?
A: Use the first sign convention: **positive shear \(V\) upward on the left cut face** and **positive bending moment \(M\) sagging (tension at the bottom fibers)**, in **N** and **N·mm**.
