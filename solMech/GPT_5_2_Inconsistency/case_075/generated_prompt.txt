Use the sign convention: positive shear V acts upward on the left cut face, and positive bending moment M is sagging (tension at bottom fibers).
Model a simply supported beam AB with span L = 4000 mm.
Apply a single concentrated load P = 12000 N downward at x = 2000 mm from support A.
Compute support reactions from global equilibrium: RA = RB = 6000 N upward.
Evaluate internal actions at the section x = 1000 mm from support A using the left segment (0 ≤ x < 2000 mm).
Set shear at the cut from vertical force equilibrium of the left segment: V = RA = +6000 N.
Set bending moment at the cut from moment equilibrium about the cut (or using M(x) = RA·x): M = 6000 N × 1000 mm = +6,000,000 N·mm.
Report results: V = +6000 N and M = +6,000,000 N·mm.
