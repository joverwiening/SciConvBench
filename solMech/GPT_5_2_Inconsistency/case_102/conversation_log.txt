Q: Do you want the elongation computed using the hint’s distributed-load integration (variable axial force), or using the “single concentrated load at the free end” approximation?
A: Use the hint’s distributed-load integration with the variable axial force \(P(x)=\rho g A (L-x)\). Do **not** use the single concentrated end-load approximation.
