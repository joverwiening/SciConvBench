Q: Do you want me to follow your instruction to approximate the UDL by a single midspan point load wL (even though that is not the standard simply-supported UDL deflection case), or should I use the standard UDL deflection formula for a simply supported beam?
A: Use the **standard UDL deflection formula** for a simply supported beam and superpose it with the **standard midspan point-load** case. Do **not** replace the UDL by a single resultant point load.
