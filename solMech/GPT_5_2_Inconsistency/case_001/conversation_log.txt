Q: Do you want me to follow your stated method even though it contradicts axial bar mechanics (stress is not identical when areas differ), or should I use the physically correct approach (same force in both segments, different stresses, total elongation is the sum of each segment’s elongation)?
A: Use the physically correct approach: the same axial force \(P\) acts in both segments, so the stresses are generally different because the areas differ, and the total elongation is the sum of the elongations of the two segments:
\[
\sigma_i=\frac{P}{A_i},\qquad \delta_{\text{total}}=\delta_1+\delta_2=\frac{PL_1}{A_1E}+\frac{PL_2}{A_2E}.
\]
