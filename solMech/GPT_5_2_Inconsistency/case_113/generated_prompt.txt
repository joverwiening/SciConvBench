Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Assume linear elastic, isotropic structural steel with shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.
Model the shaft as a hollow circular tube with outer diameter do = 50 mm, inner diameter di = 44 mm, length L = 800 mm.
Apply a constant torque T = 600000 N·mm.
Treat the tube as thin-walled and closed, with uniform wall thickness t = (do − di)/2 = 3 mm around the full circumference.
Use the thin-walled closed-tube mean-area shear-flow relation q = T/(2Am), where Am is the area enclosed by the median (mean) line of the wall.
Define the median diameter dm = (do + di)/2 and median radius rm = dm/2.
Compute Am = π rm^2.
Compute the shear flow q = T/(2Am).
Compute the maximum shear stress at the outer surface using τmax ≈ q/t, and report it in MPa as item (i).
Compute the polar moment of inertia using J = π(do^4 − di^4)/32.
Compute the angle of twist using θ = T L/(J G), and report θ in radians as item (ii).
Optionally provide θ in degrees using θdeg = θ·180/π.
