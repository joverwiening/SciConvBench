Q: For the thin-walled closed-tube (mean-area) method, should I treat the wall thickness as uniform t = (do − di)/2 = 3 mm around the circumference?
A: Yes—treat it as a uniform wall thickness around the circumference, with  
\( t = (d_o - d_i)/2 = (50 - 44)/2 = 3 \,\text{mm} \).
