Q: For the cantilever’s boundary condition at the free end, should I use the physically correct condition (free-end bending moment M(L)=0, so rotation is not constrained), or should I follow your instruction to enforce zero rotation at x=L?
A: Use the physically correct cantilever boundary condition: at the free end, the bending moment is zero \(M(L)=0\) (and shear equals the applied point load), so the rotation at \(x=L\) is **not** constrained and is generally **nonzero**. Do **not** enforce zero rotation at \(x=L\).
