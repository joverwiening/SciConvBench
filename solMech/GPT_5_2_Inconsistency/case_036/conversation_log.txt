Q: Should the buckling safety requirement be P_cr ≥ P/n (as stated earlier) or P_cr ≥ n·P (as stated at the end)?
A: Use **P_cr ≥ n·P** (i.e., the Euler critical load must be at least the applied load multiplied by the factor of safety).

Q: What end-condition does K = 1.0 represent for this column (e.g., pinned–pinned)?
A: K = 1.0 corresponds to a **pinned–pinned (hinged–hinged)** end condition (effective length equal to the actual length).
