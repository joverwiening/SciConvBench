Q: When you say “take the normal stress to be identical in the two segments,” do you intend to model the two segments as acting in parallel (same elongation, force splits) rather than in series (same force, different stresses)?
A: Model the segments **in series**: the **same axial force \(P\)** passes through both segments, so the **stresses are generally different** because the areas differ. Do **not** treat them as parallel (no force split, no common elongation per segment).
