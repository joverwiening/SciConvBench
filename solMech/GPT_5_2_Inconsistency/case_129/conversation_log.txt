Q: Should the “extreme-fiber distance” be taken as c = h (as stated) or c = h/2 (as used in the σ_max expression you provided)?
A: Use **c = h/2**. The complete requirement specifies using  
\[
\sigma_{\max}= \frac{M_{\max}(h/2)}{(b h^3/12)},
\]
which corresponds to the extreme-fiber distance being **half the height**.
