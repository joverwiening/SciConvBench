Use N–mm–MPa units and average stress approximations.

A rectangular prismatic member has width b = 50 mm and height h = 80 mm. At a given cross section, the internal resultant normal force is P = 40000 N (tension) and the internal shear force is V = 12000 N (shear parallel to the width).

Assuming uniform average stresses σ = P/A and τ = V/A, treat the stress state at a point as plane stress (σx = σ, σy = 0, τxy = τ). Compute the principal stresses and the maximum in-plane shear stress.
