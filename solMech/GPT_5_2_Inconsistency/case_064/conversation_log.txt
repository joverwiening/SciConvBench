Q: Do you want the shear stress and twist computed using the correct torsion polar moment J = π d^4/32 (as you later state), or do you want both computed using the bending second moment I = π d^4/64 (as you also state)?
A: Use the correct torsion polar moment throughout: **\(J = \pi d^4/32\)** for both **\(\tau_{\max} = Tc/J\)** and **\(\phi = TL/(JG)\)**. Do **not** use the bending second moment \(I = \pi d^4/64\).
