Model the shaft as two solid circular segments in series: AB with L1 = 400 mm and d1 = 25 mm, and BC with L2 = 900 mm and d2 = 30 mm.

Apply torque T = 750000 N·mm at end C with end A fixed.
Enforce the physically correct series-shaft condition that the internal torque is the same in both segments: TAB = TBC = T.
Compute polar moment of inertia for each solid circular segment as J = (pi/32) d^4.
Compute maximum shear stress in each segment as tau_max = T c / J with c = d/2, equivalently tau_max = 16 T / (pi d^3).
Report tau_max,AB and tau_max,BC in MPa.
Identify the governing segment as the one with the larger tau_max.
Compute angle of twist of each segment as phi = T L / (J G).
Compute total twist at C relative to A as phi_total = phi_AB + phi_BC.
