Given d = 35 mm, M = 3,000,000 N·mm, and T = 500,000 N·mm.

For part (a), compute the maximum bending normal stress at the outer surface as σ = M c / I, and report it in MPa.
For part (b), compute the polar moment of inertia J = π d^4 / 32.

For part (c), model the surface stress state as plane stress with σx = σ from part (a), σy = 0, and τxy = τ from part (b).
For part (c), compute the principal stresses using σ1,2 = (σx + σy)/2 ± sqrt(((σx − σy)/2)^2 + τxy^2).
