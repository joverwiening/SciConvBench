Q: In part (c), should I use the torsional shear stress τ you computed in part (b) (i.e., include torsion in the plane-stress principal stress calculation), or should I set τxy = 0 as stated earlier (“take the torsional shear stress to vanish”)?
A: Use the torsional shear stress from part (b) in part (c): set **τxy = τ** (not zero).

The “take the torsional shear stress to vanish” statement is inconsistent with the complete requirement; part (c) explicitly defines the plane-stress state as **σx = σ**, **σy = 0**, **τxy = τ (from torsion)**.
