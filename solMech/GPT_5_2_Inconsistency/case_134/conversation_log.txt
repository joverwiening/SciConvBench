Q: Which sign convention should I use for the final numeric answers: the first one (positive V upward on the left cut face and positive M sagging) or the second one (positive V downward on the left cut face and positive M hogging)?
A: Use the **first** sign convention for the final numeric answers:

- **Positive shear \(V\)**: **upward** on the **left** cut face  
- **Positive bending moment \(M\)**: **sagging** (tension at the bottom fibers)
