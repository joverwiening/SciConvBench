Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model the beam as simply supported with supports at x=0 and x=1800 mm.
Enforce boundary conditions M(0)=0 and M(1800)=0 with rotations free at both supports in both real and auxiliary systems.
Use material and section properties E=200000 MPa and I=3500000 mm^4.
Apply a real downward point load P=2000 N at x=900 mm.
Compute the real bending moment diagram M(x) from static equilibrium.
Define the auxiliary system by applying a unit downward point load (1 N) at x=900 mm to obtain the auxiliary bending moment diagram m(x).
Compute the deflection at x=900 mm using δ(900)=∫(0→1800) [M(x)m(x)]/(EI) dx.
Use piecewise expressions for M(x) and m(x) over 0≤x≤900 and 900≤x≤1800.
Evaluate the integral and report δ(900) in mm with sign convention positive downward.
