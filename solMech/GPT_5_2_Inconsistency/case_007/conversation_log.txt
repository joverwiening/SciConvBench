Q: When you say “treat the simple supports as zero-rotation boundaries in both the real and auxiliary systems,” do you want the beam modeled as simply supported (M(0)=M(L)=0, rotations free) or as fixed-fixed (θ(0)=θ(L)=0)?
A: Model it as **simply supported**: \(M(0)=M(L)=0\) with **rotations free** at both supports. Do **not** impose \(\theta(0)=\theta(L)=0\) (i.e., it is **not** fixed-fixed) in either the real or auxiliary system.
