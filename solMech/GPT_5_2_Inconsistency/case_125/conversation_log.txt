Q: Do you want the “use I = π d^4/64 in the torsion relations” instruction to be followed (even though torsion normally uses J), or should I ignore that line and use J = π d^4/32 for both shear stress and twist as you specify later?
A: Ignore the “use I = π d^4/64 in the torsion relations” line. Use **J = π d^4/32** for both the maximum shear stress (**τ_max = T c / J**, with **c = d/2**) and the angle of twist (**φ = TL/(JG)**).
