Q: Your statement says the rod’s total change in length is constrained to be zero by rigid supports, but then says to take the actual axial strain as the free thermal strain αΔT and the thermal stress to vanish; which condition should govern: zero total axial strain (fully restrained) or free thermal expansion (unrestrained)?
A: Use the **fully restrained condition**: the **total change in length is zero** (i.e., total axial strain = 0). Do **not** assume free thermal strain as the actual strain, and the thermal stress does **not** vanish.
