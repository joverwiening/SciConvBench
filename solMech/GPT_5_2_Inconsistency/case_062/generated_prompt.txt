Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model the rod as linear elastic with E = 200000 MPa.
Use coefficient of thermal expansion α = 1.0×10^-5 1/°C.
Use uniform temperature increase ΔT = 35 °C.
Use rod length L = 1200 mm and diameter d = 20 mm.
Assume the rod is prismatic and the axial stress is uniform over the cross-section.
Apply the fully restrained compatibility condition: total change in length is zero, so total axial strain ε_total = 0.
Use strain decomposition: ε_total = ε_mechanical + ε_thermal.
Compute thermal strain as ε_thermal = αΔT.
Enforce compatibility to get ε_mechanical = −ε_thermal.
Compute thermal stress from Hooke’s law: σ = E ε_mechanical = −E αΔT.
Report σ in MPa and state the sign as compressive if negative.
Compute cross-sectional area A = (π/4)d^2 in mm^2.
Compute axial force N = σA in N.
Report the axial force magnitude and indicate it is compressive if σ is compressive.
