Q: For the “exact” (resultant-load) model, do you want the point load wL applied at x = L/2 to be treated as a concentrated force only (no applied couple), or do you also want to include the equivalent moment of the distributed load about the fixed end?
A: Treat it as a **concentrated force only**: apply a point load \(P = wL\) at \(x = L/2\), with **no additional applied couple/moment**.
