Use N–mm–MPa units and linear elasticity.
Given do = 40 mm, di = 30 mm, t = (do − di)/2 = 5 mm, L = 1000 mm, T = 300000 N·mm, and G = 39179.1 MPa.
Model the shaft as a thin-walled closed circular tube with uniform thickness t.
Use the mean-area shear-flow relation q = T/(2Am), where Am is the area enclosed by the median line.
Use the median diameter dm = (do + di)/2 = 35 mm and median radius rm = dm/2 = 17.5 mm.
Compute Am = π rm^2.
Compute shear flow q = T/(2Am).
Compute the (approximately uniform) wall shear stress τ = q/t.
Report τ as the maximum shear stress at the outer surface (thin-wall approximation).
Compute the polar moment of inertia using J = π(do^4 − di^4)/32.
Compute the angle of twist using θ = TL/(JG).
Report θ in radians (and optionally in degrees using θdeg = θ·180/π).
