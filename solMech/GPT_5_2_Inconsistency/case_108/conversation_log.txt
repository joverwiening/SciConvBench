Q: Do you want me to follow your instruction to use the polar moment J in the Euler buckling formula, or should I use the standard area moment of inertia I = π d^4/64 for a solid circular column?
A: Use the standard Euler buckling formula with the **area moment of inertia** for bending:

- Use **\( I = \pi d^4/64 \)** for a solid circular column.

Do **not** use the polar moment \( J \) in the Euler buckling calculation.
