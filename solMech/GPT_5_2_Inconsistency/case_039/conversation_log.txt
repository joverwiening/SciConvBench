Q: When you say “assume plane strain in the two transverse directions so that the lateral strain is zero,” should I enforce εx = εy = 0 (so the rod is laterally constrained), or should I treat it as a free rod in uniaxial tension (so εlat = −ν εz)?
A: Treat it as a **free rod in uniaxial tension**: use the usual Poisson relation for lateral strain,

\[
\varepsilon_{\text{lat}} = -\nu\,\varepsilon_z
\]

Do **not** enforce \(\varepsilon_x=\varepsilon_y=0\).
