Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model the rod as linear elastic and isotropic with E = 200000 MPa and ν = 0.30.
Treat the rod as a free rod in uniaxial tension (no lateral constraint).
Given d0 = 20 mm, compute the initial cross-sectional area A0 = (π/4) d0^2.
Given P = 35000 N applied through the centroid, compute the average axial normal stress σz = P / A0 in MPa.
Compute the axial strain εz = σz / E.
Compute the lateral (diametral) strain εlat = −ν εz.
Compute the change in diameter Δd = εlat d0 in mm.
Report (i) σz, (ii) εz, (iii) εlat, and (iv) Δd with appropriate sign conventions (tension positive, lateral contraction negative).
