Q: When you say “treat the pure-bending normal stress as uniform over the section depth,” should I ignore the flexure formula σ = M y / I and instead use a constant stress magnitude equal to the extreme-fiber stress (same sign everywhere)?
A: No—do **not** treat the bending stress as uniform. Use the standard flexure formula **σ = −M y / I** (linear variation with \(y\), zero at the neutral axis). The “uniform over the depth” statement is **not** part of the complete requirement and should be ignored.
