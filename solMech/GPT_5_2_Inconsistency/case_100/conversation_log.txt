Q: When you say “assume plane strain in the two transverse directions so that the lateral strain is zero,” should I enforce εx = εy = 0 (so the lateral/diametral strain is 0 and Δd = 0), or should I instead use the usual uniaxial relation εlat = −ν εz?
A: Use the usual uniaxial relation:  
\[
\varepsilon_{\text{lat}} = -\nu\,\varepsilon_z
\]
So the lateral (diametral) strain is **not** zero, and \(\Delta d \neq 0\).
