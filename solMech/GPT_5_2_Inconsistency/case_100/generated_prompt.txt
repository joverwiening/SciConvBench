Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model the rod as linear elastic and isotropic with E = 105000 MPa and ν = 0.34.
Treat the loading as uniaxial tension with load P = 25000 N applied through the centroid.
Use the initial diameter d0 = 30 mm and initial length L = 500 mm.
Compute the initial cross-sectional area as A0 = (π/4) d0^2.
Compute (i) the average axial normal stress as σz,avg = P / A0 in MPa.
Compute (ii) the axial strain as εz = σz,avg / E.
Compute (iii) the lateral (diametral) strain using the uniaxial relation εlat = −ν εz.
Compute (iv) the change in diameter as Δd = εlat d0 in mm.
Report σz,avg, εz, εlat, and Δd with appropriate sign convention (tension positive, lateral contraction negative).
