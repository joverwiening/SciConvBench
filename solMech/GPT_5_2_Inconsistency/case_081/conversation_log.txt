Q: In part (c), should I use the torsional shear stress value from part (b) at the outer surface (i.e., include τ in the plane-stress state), or should I set τ = 0 at that location as stated (“torsional shear stress to vanish”)?
A: Use the torsional shear stress from part (b) in part (c), i.e., include **τ = T c / J** in the plane-stress state (**σx = σ, σy = 0, τxy = τ**).
