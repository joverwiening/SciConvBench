Model the member as a straight, prismatic, solid circular column of diameter d.
Use material modulus E = 69000 MPa (6061-T6 aluminum).
Use column length L = 2200 mm.
Use effective length factor K = 2.0 corresponding to a fixed-free (cantilever) end condition.
Use applied compressive load P = 25000 N.
Use buckling factor of safety n = 2.5.
Interpret the buckling safety requirement as P_cr ≥ n·P.
Compute the Euler critical buckling load using P_cr = (π^2 E I)/(K L)^2.
Compute the second moment of area for a solid circular cross section using I = (π d^4)/64.
Solve the inequality (π^2 E (π d^4/64))/(K L)^2 ≥ n·P for d.
