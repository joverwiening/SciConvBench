Q: Should the buckling safety requirement be P_cr ≥ P/n (as stated earlier) or P_cr ≥ n·P (as stated at the end)?
A: Use **P_cr ≥ n·P** (i.e., the critical buckling load must be at least the applied load multiplied by the factor of safety).

Q: Confirm the end condition corresponding to K = 2.0 (e.g., fixed-free/cantilever).
