Q: When you say “take the normal stress to be identical in the two segments,” do you want me to follow that instruction even though it contradicts axial mechanics for a stepped bar (same force, different areas → different stresses)?
A: Do **not** follow that “identical stress” instruction. Use standard axial mechanics for a stepped bar: the **axial force is the same** in both segments, so the **normal stresses differ** because the cross-sectional areas differ.

Q: For the elongation calculation, should I include the axial deformation of both segments and sum them (δtotal = δ1 + δ2) as for two segments in series?
A: Yes. Include the axial deformation of **both** segments and **sum** them as elements in series:

\[
\delta_{\text{total}}=\delta_1+\delta_2,\quad \delta_i=\frac{P\,L_i}{A_i\,E}
\]

Use the same axial force \(P\) in both segments, with \(A_i=\frac{\pi d_i^2}{4}\).
