Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Assume linear-elastic, small-deformation axial behavior with E = 200000 MPa.
Model the bar as two axial segments in series: Segment 1 with L1 = 300 mm, d1 = 12 mm; Segment 2 with L2 = 600 mm, d2 = 30 mm.
Apply an axial tensile load P = 25000 N at the right end, with the left end fixed.
Assume the internal axial force is the same in both segments: Pi = P.
Compute cross-sectional areas as Ai = (π di^2)/4.
Compute normal stress in each segment as σi = P/Ai, and report σ1 and σ2 in MPa.
Compute elongation of each segment as δi = (P Li)/(Ai E), and report δ1 and δ2 in mm.
Compute total elongation between the fixed end and the load point as δtotal = δ1 + δ2, and report δtotal in mm.
