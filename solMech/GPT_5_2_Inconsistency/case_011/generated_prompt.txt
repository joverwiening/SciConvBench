Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Given d = 25 mm, L = 2000 mm, E = 200000 MPa, K = 0.7, P = 25000 N, Sy = 250 MPa.
Compute cross-sectional area A = (π d^2)/4.
Compute average compressive stress σ_avg = P/A.
Compare σ_avg to Sy and state whether σ_avg < Sy (no yielding by average stress) or σ_avg ≥ Sy (yielding).
Compute second moment of area I = (π d^4)/64 for a solid circular cross section.
Compute effective length Le = K L.
Compute Euler critical load P_cr = (π^2 E I)/(Le^2).
Compare P_cr to P and state whether P < P_cr (no Euler buckling) or P ≥ P_cr (Euler buckling).
State which limit state is more critical by comparing utilization ratios P/P_cr versus σ_avg/Sy (the larger ratio is more critical).
Note that the polar moment J = (π d^4)/32 is not used in the Euler buckling calculation when I is specified as (π d^4)/64.
