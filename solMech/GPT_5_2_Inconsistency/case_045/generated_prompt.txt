Use consistent N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Assume small deformation and linear-elastic material behavior.
Use 6061-T6 aluminum with Young’s modulus E = 69000 MPa.
Use rod diameter d = 12 mm.
Use gage length L = 800 mm.
Use axial tensile force P = 15000 N applied through the centroid.
Compute cross-sectional area as A = (π/4)d^2.
Compute average normal stress as σ_avg = P/A in MPa.
Compute elastic normal strain as ε = σ_avg/E.
Compute axial elongation as δ = εL.
Use yield strength S_y = 275 MPa.
Compute factor of safety against yielding as n = S_y/σ_avg.
