Q: For the cantilever with a tip point load and no applied end couple, should I use the physically correct boundary condition that the free-end bending moment is zero (M(L)=0), rather than enforcing zero rotation at the free end?
A: Use the physically correct boundary condition: no applied end couple means the free-end bending moment is zero, **M(L) = 0**. Do **not** enforce zero rotation at the free end; the free-end slope is generally nonzero for a cantilever with a tip point load.
