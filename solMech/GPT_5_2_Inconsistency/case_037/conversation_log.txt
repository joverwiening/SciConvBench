Q: When you say “take the end slopes to be zero,” do you want to enforce boundary conditions θ(0)=0 and θ(L)=0 (even though that makes the beam behave like fixed-fixed rather than simply supported)?
A: No—do **not** enforce θ(0)=0 and θ(L)=0.

Use **simply supported** boundary conditions only:
- Deflections: \(y(0)=0\), \(y(L)=0\)
- End moments zero (equivalently): \(y''(0)=0\), \(y''(L)=0\)

Then compute the **slope at \(x=0\)** from the resulting deflection curve (it will not be zero).
