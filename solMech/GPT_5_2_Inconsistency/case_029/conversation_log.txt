Q: When you say “assume plane strain in the two transverse directions so that the lateral strain is zero,” should I enforce εx = εy = 0 (so the diametral strain is zero), or should I instead treat it as the usual uniaxial stress case where εlat = −ν εz?
A: Use the usual uniaxial stress case: take the lateral (diametral) strain as  
\(\varepsilon_{\text{lat}} = -\nu\,\varepsilon_z\).  

Do **not** enforce \(\varepsilon_x=\varepsilon_y=0\); the “plane strain” statement is not part of the complete requirement.
