Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model the rod as linear elastic and isotropic with E = 105000 MPa and ν = 0.34.
Use the usual uniaxial stress assumption with axial stress only (σz ≠ 0, σx = σy = 0).
Compute the initial cross-sectional area as A0 = (π/4) d0^2 with d0 = 20 mm.
Compute (i) the average axial normal stress as σz,avg = P/A0 using P = 25000 N.
Compute (ii) the axial strain as εz = σz,avg / E.
Compute (iii) the lateral (diametral) strain as εlat = −ν εz.
Compute (iv) the change in diameter as Δd = εlat d0.
Report σz,avg in MPa, strains as dimensionless, and Δd in mm.
