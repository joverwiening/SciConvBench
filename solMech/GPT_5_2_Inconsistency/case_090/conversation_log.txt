Q: When you say “use a unit moment at the point of interest to generate m(x)”, do you want the auxiliary system to apply a unit vertical force (1 N) at x = 2000 mm (standard for deflection), or literally a unit couple moment (1 N·mm) at x = 2000 mm?
A: Use a **unit vertical force (1 N downward) applied at x = 2000 mm** to generate \(m(x)\). Do **not** use a unit couple moment.
