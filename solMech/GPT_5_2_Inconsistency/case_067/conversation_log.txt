Q: Do you want the shear stress and twist computed using the correct torsion polar moment J = π d^4/32, or do you want them computed using the bending second moment I = π d^4/64 as stated earlier?
A: Use the correct torsion polar moment **\(J = \pi d^4/32\)** for both the maximum shear stress and the angle of twist (do **not** use \(I = \pi d^4/64\)).
