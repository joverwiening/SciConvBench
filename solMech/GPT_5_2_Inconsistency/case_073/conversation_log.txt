Q: Should the height h be treated as the full cross-section depth (so the extreme-fiber distance is c = h/2), or do you want to enforce the nonstandard instruction c = h?
A: Treat **h as the full cross-section depth**, so the extreme-fiber distance is **c = h/2**. Do **not** enforce **c = h**.
