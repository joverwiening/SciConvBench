Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Assume linear elastic torsion.
Given do = 50 mm, di = 42 mm, L = 1200 mm, T = 450000 N·mm, and G = 77519.4 MPa (N/mm^2).
Model the shaft as a thin-walled closed circular tube using the mean-area shear-flow relation.
Compute the mean radius rm = (do/2 + di/2)/2.
Compute the wall thickness t = (do − di)/2.
Compute the area enclosed by the mean line Am = π rm^2.
Compute the shear flow q = T / (2 Am).
Compute the shear stress at the outer surface using τmax ≈ q / t.
Also compute the polar moment of inertia using J = π(do^4 − di^4)/32.
Compute the angle of twist over the length using θ = T L / (J G).
Report (i) τmax in MPa and (ii) θ in radians (and optionally in degrees).
