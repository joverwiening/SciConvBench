Q: When you say “engineering stress using the current cross-sectional area,” should I assume the specimen’s diameter stays at 12.5 mm (i.e., use A = π(d0^2)/4), or should I compute a reduced area from lateral contraction (which would require Poisson’s ratio or the current diameter)?
A: Assume the diameter stays at **12.5 mm** and use the **original cross-sectional area** \(A_0=\pi d_0^2/4\). Do **not** compute a reduced area from lateral contraction (no Poisson’s ratio/current diameter is provided).
