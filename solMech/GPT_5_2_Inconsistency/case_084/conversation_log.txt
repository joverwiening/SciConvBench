Q: For the percent elongation, should I use the standard definition 100(Lf − L0)/L0, or do you want the nonstandard version you mentioned with Lf in the denominator 100(Lf − L0)/Lf?
A: Use the standard definition with the initial gage length in the denominator:

\[
\% \text{elongation} = 100\frac{L_f - L_0}{L_0}.
\]

Do **not** use \(L_f\) in the denominator.

Q: Do you want percent reduction of area computed from diameters using the approximation 100(d0 − df)/d0, or from cross-sectional areas using 100(A0 − Af)/A0 where A = πd²/4?
A: Compute percent reduction of area from the **cross-sectional areas**:

\[
\%RA = 100\frac{A_0 - A_f}{A_0}, \quad A=\frac{\pi d^2}{4}.
\]

Do **not** use the diameter-only approximation \(100(d_0-d_f)/d_0\).
