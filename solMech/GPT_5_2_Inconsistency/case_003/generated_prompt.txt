Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model the bar as two axial members in series with linear-elastic behavior.
Use E = 69000 MPa for 6061-T6 aluminum.
Use axial load P = 20000 N applied at the right end, with the left end fixed.
Use segment 1: L1 = 500 mm, d1 = 30 mm.
Use segment 2: L2 = 600 mm, d2 = 25 mm.
Assume the same axial force P acts in both segments.
Compute cross-sectional areas as A1 = (π/4)d1^2 and A2 = (π/4)d2^2.
Compute normal stress in each segment as σ1 = P/A1 and σ2 = P/A2 (MPa).
Compute elongation of each segment as δ1 = P L1/(A1 E) and δ2 = P L2/(A2 E) (mm).
Compute total elongation as δtotal = δ1 + δ2.
Report σ1, σ2, and δtotal with appropriate units (MPa for stress, mm for elongation).
