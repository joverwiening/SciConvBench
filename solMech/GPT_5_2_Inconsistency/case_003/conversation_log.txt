Q: Do you want me to follow your stated assumptions (equal stress in both segments and common elongation before combining stiffnesses), or should I use the physically correct axial-bar relations (same force in both segments, different stresses, elongations add in series)?
A: Use the physically correct axial-bar relations: the same axial force \(P\) passes through both segments, the stresses are generally different because the areas differ, and the total elongation is the sum of the segment elongations (series). Ignore the “equal stress/common elongation” statements—they are not part of the complete requirement.
