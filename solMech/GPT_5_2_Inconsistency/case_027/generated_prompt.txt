Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model the rod as a linear elastic axial spring with negligible mass.
Given L = 1500 mm.
Given d = 16 mm.
Compute cross-sectional area as A = π d^2 / 4.
Given E = 200000 MPa.
Given dropped weight W = 800 N.
Given drop height h = 150 mm.
Define maximum elongation as δ_max = P_max L / (A E).
Apply energy conservation at maximum elongation: W(h + δ_max) = (P_max^2 L) / (2 A E).
Substitute δ_max into the energy equation to obtain a single equation in P_max.
Solve the resulting quadratic equation for P_max and select the positive real root.
Compute maximum normal stress as σ_max = P_max / A.
Report P_max in N and σ_max in MPa.
