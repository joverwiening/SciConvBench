Q: Do you want the elongation computed using the exact distributed self-weight integration (using P(x)=ρgA(L−x)) or using the “single concentrated load at the free end” approximation?
A: Use the exact distributed self-weight integration with \(P(x)=\rho g A (L-x)\) and \(\delta=\int_0^L \frac{P(x)}{AE}\,dx\). Do **not** use the single concentrated end-load approximation.
