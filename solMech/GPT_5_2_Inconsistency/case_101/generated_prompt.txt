Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model a vertical, prismatic, linearly elastic bar fixed at the top and free at the bottom.
Use bar length L = 3000 mm.
Use constant cross-sectional area A = 300 mm^2.
Use modulus of elasticity E = 69000 MPa.
Use material density ρ = 8900 kg/m^3.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s^2.
Ignore all external loads; include only self-weight.
Measure coordinate x downward from the top, with x = 0 at the support and x = L at the free end.
Convert density to consistent N–mm–MPa form by using ρ in kg/mm^3 (ρ = 8900 / 10^9 kg/mm^3) and g in mm/s^2 (g = 9.81×10^3 mm/s^2), or equivalently compute weight density γ = ρg in N/mm^3 via SI-to-mm conversion.
Define the internal axial force at section x as P(x) = ρ g A (L − x), with L and x in mm and P(x) in N.
Compute elongation using the exact distributed self-weight integration δ = ∫0^L [P(x)/(A E)] dx.
Use E in MPa = N/mm^2 so that δ is obtained in mm.
Evaluate the integral analytically to obtain δ = (ρ g L^2) / (2 E) with consistent units, or evaluate numerically from the integral expression.
Report the final elongation δ in mm.
