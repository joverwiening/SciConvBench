Q: Do you want me to follow your special instruction to treat the bending moment as a constant M = P·L over 0 ≤ x ≤ L (even though the actual cantilever moment varies with x)?
A: No—do **not** treat the bending moment as a constant \(M = P\,L\) over \(0 \le x \le L\).

Per the complete requirement, you should **set up the actual** \(M(x)\) for a cantilever with an end point load, then compute
\[
U=\int_{0}^{L}\frac{M(x)^2}{2EI}\,dx
\]
and finally differentiate \(U\) with respect to \(P\) to get the free-end deflection.
