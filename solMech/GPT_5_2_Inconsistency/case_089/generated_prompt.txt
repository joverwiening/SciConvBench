Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model a prismatic axial bar with A = 500 mm^2 and E = 200000 MPa.
Use segment lengths AB = 700 mm, BC = 400 mm, and CD = 300 mm.
Use applied axial point loads (positive to the right): at B, +15000 N; at C, -12000 N; at D, +10000 N.
Impose boundary condition u_A = 0 at the left end A.
Compute the support reaction at A from global axial equilibrium of the entire bar.
Compute internal axial force N in each segment using a cut and equilibrium, taking tension as positive.
Set N_AB = -(R_A).
Set N_BC = -(R_A + 15000).
Set N_CD = -(R_A + 15000 - 12000).
Verify consistency at the right end by checking that N just left of D equals the negative of the net external force to the right of the cut.
Compute normal stress in each segment as σ = N/A, keeping sign consistent with the chosen tension-positive convention.
Compute axial deformation of each segment as Δ_i = (N_i L_i)/(A E).
Compute displacement of D relative to A as u_D = Δ_AB + Δ_BC + Δ_CD.
Report N_AB, N_BC, N_CD in N, σ_AB, σ_BC, σ_CD in MPa, and u_D in mm, including signs (positive displacement to the right).
