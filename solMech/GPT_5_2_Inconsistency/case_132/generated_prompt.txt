Use N–mm–MPa units and assume small strains.
Given A = 4000 mm^2, t = 20 mm, V = 12000 N, and G = 39179.1 MPa.
Compute the average shear stress as τ_avg = V/A = 12000/4000 = 3 MPa.
Compute the average shear strain as γ_avg = τ_avg/G = 3/39179.1 = 7.657×10^-5.
Compute the relative lateral displacement as Δ = γ_avg·t = (7.657×10^-5)(20) = 0.001531 mm.
