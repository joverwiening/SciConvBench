Q: The statement “treat the adhesive layer as rigid in shear so the two faces undergo no relative slip” contradicts the request to compute shear strain and shear deformation using G; should I ignore the “rigid in shear/no slip” assumption and instead treat the layer as linearly elastic in shear (τ = Gγ) to compute γ_avg and Δ?
A: Yes—ignore the “rigid in shear/no slip” statement. Use linear elastic shear behavior with  
\(\tau_{\text{avg}} = V/A\), \(\gamma_{\text{avg}} = \tau_{\text{avg}}/G\), and \(\Delta = \gamma_{\text{avg}}\, t\).
