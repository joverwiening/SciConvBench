Q: Do you want me to follow the instruction “use the maximum bending moment M = PL/4 over the full span” (constant M for 0≤x≤L), or should I instead use the physically correct piecewise bending moment diagram for a midspan point load?
A: Use the physically correct **piecewise** bending moment diagram for a midspan point load (i.e., define \(M(x)\) piecewise over \(0 \le x \le L\) and integrate \(U=\int_0^L \frac{M^2}{2EI}\,dx\)). Do **not** use a constant \(M=PL/4\) over the full span.
