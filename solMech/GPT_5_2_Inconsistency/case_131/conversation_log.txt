Q: The statement “treat the adhesive layer as rigid in shear so the two faces undergo no relative slip” contradicts the request to compute shear strain and shear deformation using G; should I ignore the “rigid in shear/no slip” assumption and instead treat the layer as linearly elastic in shear with γ_avg = τ_avg/G?
A: Ignore the “rigid in shear/no slip” statement. Use linear elastic shear behavior with  
\(\gamma_{\text{avg}}=\tau_{\text{avg}}/G\) and \(\Delta=\gamma_{\text{avg}}\,t\).
