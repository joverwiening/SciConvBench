Q: Do you want the solution to explicitly correct the statement “split the total load equally,” using compatibility (equal elongation) and equilibrium to find the actual load split?
A: Yes. The solution should explicitly **correct** the “split the total load equally” statement and instead use:

- **Compatibility:** both bars have the same elongation (same strain since same length), and  
- **Equilibrium:** \(P = F_1 + F_2\)

to determine the **actual** load carried by each bar.
