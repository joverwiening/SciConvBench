Q: For the “exact” case, do you want the slope and deflection computed for the true uniformly distributed load w over 0≤x≤L (standard cantilever UDL formulas), or do you want them computed for the point-load approximation P = wL applied at x = L/2?
A: Use the true uniformly distributed load \(w\) over \(0 \le x \le L\) (standard cantilever UDL formulas). Do **not** use the point-load approximation \(P=wL\) at \(x=L/2\).
