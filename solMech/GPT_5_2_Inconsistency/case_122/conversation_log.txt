Q: When you say “treat the pure-bending normal stress as uniform over the section depth,” should I ignore the flexure formula σ = −My/I and instead use a constant stress magnitude equal to the extreme-fiber stress (same sign everywhere)?
A: No—do **not** treat the bending stress as uniform and do **not** ignore the flexure formula.

Use the standard linear bending relation **σ = −My/I** (with \(y\) measured from the neutral axis, positive upward). That means:
- at **y = 0 mm**, **σ = 0 MPa**
- at the **top fiber** \(y = +h/2 = +60\) mm, compute with \(+60\) mm
- at the **bottom fiber** \(y = -60\) mm, compute with \(-60\) mm
