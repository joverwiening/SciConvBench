Q: When you say “treat the adhesive layer as rigid in shear so the two faces undergo no relative slip,” should I interpret that as “no shear deformation” (i.e., γ_avg = 0 and Δ = 0)?
A: No. In the COMPLETE requirement, you should **not** treat the layer as rigid in shear or assume no shear deformation. You should compute shear deformation normally using:

- \( \tau_{\text{avg}} = V/A \)
- \( \gamma_{\text{avg}} = \tau_{\text{avg}}/G \)
- \( \Delta = \gamma_{\text{avg}}\, t \)

So \( \gamma_{\text{avg}} \neq 0 \) and \( \Delta \neq 0 \).
