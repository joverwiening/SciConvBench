Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Use E = 200000 MPa.
Use I = 4000000 mm^4.
Use M = 3000000 N·mm.
Compute curvature as κ = M/(E I).
Compute E I = 200000 × 4000000 = 8.0×10^11 N·mm^2.
Compute κ = 3000000 / (8.0×10^11) = 3.75×10^-6 mm^-1.
Compute radius of curvature as ρ = 1/κ = 2.6666666667×10^5 mm (≈ 266667 mm).
