Q: Do you want the torque treated as the same internal torque through both segments (series shaft), or do you want it “distributed by torsional stiffness” as if the segments act in parallel?
A: Treat it as a **series shaft**: the **same internal torque \(T = 300{,}000\ \text{N·mm}\)** acts through **both segments AB and BC**. Do **not** distribute the torque by torsional stiffness (that would be a parallel-shaft assumption).
