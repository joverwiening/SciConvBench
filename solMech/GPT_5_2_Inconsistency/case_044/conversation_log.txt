Q: When you say “assume plane strain in the two transverse directions so that the lateral strain is zero,” should I enforce εx = εy = 0 (so the diametral strain is zero), or should I instead use the usual uniaxial stress relation εlat = −ν εz?
A: Use the usual uniaxial stress relation: **ε_lat = −ν ε_z**.

Do **not** enforce plane strain (εx = εy = 0). The complete requirement does not include that constraint; it only provides ν for the standard Poisson effect.
