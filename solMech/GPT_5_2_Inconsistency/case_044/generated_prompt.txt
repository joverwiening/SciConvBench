Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model the rod as linear elastic and isotropic with E = 69000 MPa and ν = 0.33.
Use the given geometry d0 = 40 mm and L = 500 mm.
Use the applied axial tensile load P = 35000 N applied through the centroid.
Assume uniform uniaxial stress over the cross-section (ignore stress concentrations and end effects).
Compute the cross-sectional area as A = (π/4) d0^2 in mm^2.
Compute (i) the average axial normal stress as σz = P/A in MPa (since 1 MPa = 1 N/mm^2).
Assume the only nonzero stress component is σz (σx = σy = 0).
Compute (ii) the axial strain as εz = σz/E.
Compute (iii) the lateral (diametral) strain using the standard Poisson relation εlat = −ν εz.
Compute (iv) the change in diameter as Δd = εlat d0 in mm, with the sign indicating contraction under tension.
