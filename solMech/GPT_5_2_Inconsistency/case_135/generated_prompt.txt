Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Use a rectangular cross section with width b = 40 mm and height h = 120 mm.
Compute the cross-sectional area as A = b h = 40×120 = 4800 mm^2.
Take the internal normal force as P = +30000 N (tension is positive).
Compute the average normal stress as σ_avg = P/A = 30000/4800 = +6.25 MPa.
Take the internal transverse shear force as V = 12000 N acting parallel to width b.
Assume uniform transverse shear stress over the area and compute the average shear stress as τ_avg = V/A = 12000/4800 = 2.50 MPa.
Compute the magnitude of the maximum in-plane shear stress using τ_max = sqrt((σ_avg/2)^2 + τ_avg^2).
Evaluate τ_max = sqrt((6.25/2)^2 + (2.50)^2) = sqrt(3.125^2 + 2.50^2) = sqrt(16.015625) = 4.00 MPa.
