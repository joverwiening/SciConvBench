Q: Do you want the physically correct cantilever boundary condition at the free end (zero bending moment, nonzero rotation), or do you want to enforce your stated assumption that the free-end rotation is zero?
A: Use the physically correct cantilever boundary condition at the free end: **zero bending moment at x = L and nonzero rotation**. Do **not** enforce zero free-end rotation, and do **not** assume a constant moment \(PL\) over the span.
