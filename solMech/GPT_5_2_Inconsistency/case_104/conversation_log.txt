Q: When you say “take the end slopes to be zero,” do you want to enforce boundary conditions θ(0)=0 and θ(L)=0 (i.e., fixed-fixed ends), or keep the beam simply supported and only use symmetry to set θ(L/2)=0?
A: Keep the beam **simply supported** (so boundary conditions are **v(0)=0** and **v(L)=0**). Do **not** enforce θ(0)=0 or θ(L)=0.

Use symmetry only: the **slope at midspan is zero**, i.e. **θ(L/2)=0** (and maximum deflection occurs at **x = L/2**).
