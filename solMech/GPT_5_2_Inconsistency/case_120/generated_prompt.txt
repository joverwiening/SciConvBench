Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model the rod as linear elastic with uniform temperature change.
Given L = 800 mm.
Given d = 20 mm.
Given ΔT = 50 °C.
Given E = 200000 MPa.
Given α = 2e-05 1/°C.
Compute free thermal strain as ε_th = αΔT.
Compute constrained total axial strain as ε_total = 0 because the supports are rigid and unyielding.
Use strain decomposition ε_total = ε_mech + ε_th.
Solve for mechanical strain ε_mech = -ε_th.
Use Hooke’s law σ = E ε_mech to compute the thermal stress.
Report the sign and state that the stress is compressive if σ is negative.
Compute cross-sectional area A = (π/4)d^2.
Compute axial force magnitude as P = |σ|A.
Assign force sign consistent with stress sign, with compression taken as negative if a sign convention is stated.
Numerical results shall be σ = -200 MPa (compressive).
Numerical results shall be P = -62832 N (compressive), with magnitude 62.8 kN.
