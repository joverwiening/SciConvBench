Use consistent N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Assume small deformation and linear-elastic behavior.
Use E = 200000 MPa.
Model a straight, prismatic circular rod with diameter d = 25 mm and gage length L = 800 mm.
Apply a centric axial tensile force P = 20000 N.
Compute cross-sectional area as A = (π/4)d^2.
Compute average normal stress as σ_avg = P/A in MPa.
Compute elastic normal strain as ε = σ_avg/E.
Compute axial elongation of the gage length as δ = εL.
Use yield strength S_y = 250 MPa.
Compute factor of safety against yielding as n = S_y/σ_avg.
Report σ_avg (MPa), ε (dimensionless), δ (mm), and n (dimensionless).
