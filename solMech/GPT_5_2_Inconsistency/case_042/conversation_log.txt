Q: Do you want the transient maximum stress computed using the standard suddenly applied load energy method (which yields a peak stress of 2·(P/A) for an undamped linear elastic bar), or do you want me to follow your instruction to take the peak stress equal to the static stress P/A?
A: Use the standard suddenly applied load energy method. Do **not** assume the peak stress equals the static stress; compute the transient maximum stress from work–energy (which gives \( \sigma_{\max} = 2\,P/A \) for this undamped linear elastic case).
