Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.
Model a cantilever beam of length L = 1500 mm fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
Use material modulus E = 200000 MPa and constant second moment of area I = 800000 mm^4.
Apply a downward point load P = 500 N at the free end x = L.
Use the actual bending moment function for an end-loaded cantilever: M(x) = P(L − x) for 0 ≤ x ≤ L.
Form the bending strain energy U(P) = ∫ from 0 to L of [M(x)^2/(2EI)] dx.
Substitute M(x) into U(P) to obtain U(P) = ∫ from 0 to L of [P^2(L − x)^2/(2EI)] dx.
Evaluate the integral to get U(P) = P^2 L^3/(6EI).
Apply Castigliano’s theorem for the deflection at the load point in the load direction: δ(L) = ∂U/∂P.
Differentiate to obtain δ(L) = P L^3/(3EI).
Compute numerically: δ(L) = (500)(1500^3) / (3(200000)(800000)) mm = 3.515625 mm downward.
