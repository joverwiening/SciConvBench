Q: Do you want me to follow your special instruction to treat the bending moment as a constant M = P·L over 0 ≤ x ≤ L (even though the actual cantilever moment varies with x)?
A: No. Use the actual bending moment function for a cantilever with an end point load, i.e., \(M(x)=P(L-x)\) for \(0\le x\le L\), then integrate \(U=\int_0^L \frac{M(x)^2}{2EI}\,dx\) and differentiate \(U\) with respect to \(P\).
