Q: When you say “take the end slopes to be zero,” do you want to enforce boundary conditions θ(0)=0 and θ(L)=0 (even though that makes the beam effectively fixed-ended rather than simply supported)?
A: No—do **not** enforce end slopes to be zero. Keep the beam **simply supported** boundary conditions only:

- \(v(0)=0\)
- \(v(L)=0\)

The slopes at the supports are **not** prescribed; in particular, compute the slope at the left support \( \theta(0) \) from the solution.

Q: Do you want the deflection v(x) reported with downward deflection taken as positive or negative?
