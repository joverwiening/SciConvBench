Work in N–mm–MPa units.
The bar has a rectangular cross section with width b = 40 mm and height h = 120 mm.
The cross-sectional area A = b * h = 40 mm * 120 mm = 4800 mm².
The internal resultant normal force is P = 30000 N (tension).
The internal resultant transverse shear force is V = 12000 N, acting parallel to the width b.
Calculate the average normal stress σ_avg = P / A.
Calculate the average shear stress τ_avg = V / A.
Compute the magnitude of the maximum in-plane shear stress using τ_max = sqrt((σ_avg/2)^2 + τ_avg^2).
