Use N–mm–MPa units.
Assume small strains.
The adhesive layer is rectangular with an area A = 3000 mm^2.
The adhesive layer has a thickness t = 8 mm.
A uniform in-plane shear force V = 12000 N is applied parallel to the layer's faces.
The adhesive layer is treated as rigid in shear, so the two faces undergo no relative slip.
The material is isotropic with a shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.
Compute the average shear stress τ_avg = V / A.
Compute the average shear strain γ_avg = τ_avg / G.
Compute the relative lateral displacement Δ = γ_avg * t.
Assume a square geometry for the adhesive layer, with side length sqrt(A) ≈ 54.7723 mm, for the purpose of conceptual visualization, as the calculations require only area, not specific dimensions.
