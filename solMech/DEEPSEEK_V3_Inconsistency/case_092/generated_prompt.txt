Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system.
Assume Euler-Bernoulli beam theory with small deflections and linear elastic material.
Model a cantilever beam of length L = 1800 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
Use a material modulus of elasticity E = 200000 MPa.
Use a constant second moment of area I = 1200000 mm^4.
Apply a downward point load P = 2000 N at the free end (x = L).
Calculate the bending moment as constant over the span with a value of M = P * L.
Calculate the slope at the free end using the formula for a cantilever with constant moment: slope = (M * L) / (E * I).
Calculate the vertical deflection at the free end using the formula for a cantilever with constant moment: deflection = (M * L^2) / (2 * E * I).
Report the free-end slope in radians.
Report the free-end vertical deflection in millimeters.
