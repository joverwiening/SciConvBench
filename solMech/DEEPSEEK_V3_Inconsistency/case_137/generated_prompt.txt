The applied torque T is 500,000 N·mm.
Calculate the maximum bending normal stress at the outer surface using the formula σ = M * c / I.

Use I = π * d^4 / 64 as the second moment of area for the circular cross-section.
Calculate the torsional shear stress at the outer surface using the formula τ = T * c / J.
Use J = π * d^4 / 32 as the polar moment of inertia for the circular cross-section.
Model the stress state at the surface as plane stress with σx equal to the bending stress, σy = 0, and τxy equal to the torsional shear stress.
Compute the two in-plane principal stresses from this stress state.
