Use N, mm, and MPa as the units for all calculations.
Model the material behavior as linear-elastic.
Consider a stepped circular bar fixed at its left end.
Apply a tensile load P = 35000 N at the right end.
Segment 1 (left) has length L1 = 400 mm and diameter d1 = 16 mm.
Segment 2 (right) has length L2 = 800 mm and diameter d2 = 40 mm.
Use a Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa for structural steel.
Assume the same axial force P passes through both segments.
Calculate the cross-sectional area for Segment 1 as A1 = π*(d1/2)^2.
Calculate the cross-sectional area for Segment 2 as A2 = π*(d2/2)^2.
Calculate the normal stress in Segment 1 as σ1 = P / A1.
Calculate the normal stress in Segment 2 as σ2 = P / A2.
Calculate the elongation of Segment 1 as δ1 = (P * L1) / (A1 * E).
Calculate the elongation of Segment 2 as δ2 = (P * L2) / (A2 * E).
Calculate the total elongation of the bar as δ_total = δ1 + δ2.
Report the normal stress in each segment.
Report the total elongation between the fixed end and the load point.
