Use N, mm, and MPa as the base units.
Model the material as linear-elastic with a Young's Modulus, E, of 200000 MPa.
The bar is a two-segment, stepped circular bar made of structural steel.
The bar is fixed at its left end.
The bar is loaded in tension at its right end by a force, P, of 25000 N.
Segment 1 is the left segment.
Segment 1 has a length, L1, of 300 mm.
Segment 1 has a diameter, d1, of 12 mm.
Segment 2 is the right segment.
Segment 2 has a length, L2, of 600 mm.
Segment 2 has a diameter, d2, of 30 mm.
The same axial force, P, passes through both segments.
Calculate the normal stress in each segment.
For the deformation calculation, treat the segments as deforming in series.
Calculate the elongation of Segment 1.
Calculate the elongation of Segment 2.
Calculate the total elongation of the bar as the sum of the individual segment elongations.
