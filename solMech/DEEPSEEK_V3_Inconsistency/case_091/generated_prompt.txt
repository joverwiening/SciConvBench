Use N-mm-MPa units.
Assume Euler-Bernoulli beam theory and use superposition.
The beam is simply supported with a span L = 3500 mm.
The beam material is structural steel with a modulus of elasticity E = 200000 MPa.
The beam has a constant second moment of area I = 3000000 mm^4.
The beam carries a uniform distributed load w = 0.3 N/mm over the full span.
The beam also carries a downward point load P = 1200 N at midspan.
For the distributed load contribution, replace the uniform load with its single resultant wL acting at midspan.
Compute the midspan deflection for the point load case using the formula for a simply supported beam with a central point load.
Compute the midspan deflection for the equivalent central concentrated load case using the same formula.
Sum the two computed deflections to find the total midspan deflection by superposition.
