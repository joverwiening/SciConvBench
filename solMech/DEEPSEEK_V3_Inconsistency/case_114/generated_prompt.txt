Use N, mm, and MPa as the units for force, length, and stress respectively.
The point coordinates are (y, z) = (30 mm, 15 mm) relative to the centroid.
The second moments of area are Iy = 2500000 mm^4, Iz = 1200000 mm^4, and Iyz = 800000 mm^4.
The internal bending moments are My = 4000000 N·mm and Mz = 2000000 N·mm.
Assume a right-handed coordinate system where positive y and z are as given.
Assume the bending moment sign convention follows the right-hand rule, where a positive moment vector points in the positive axis direction.
Calculate the normal stress σ at the point using the formula: σ = -(My*Iz - Mz*Iyz) / (Iy*Iz - Iyz^2) * z + -(Mz*Iy - My*Iyz) / (Iy*Iz - Iyz^2) * y.
Report the numerical value of σ in MPa.
A positive value for σ indicates tension, and a negative value indicates compression.
