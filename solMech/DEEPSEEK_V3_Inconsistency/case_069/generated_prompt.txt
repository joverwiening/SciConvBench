Use N, mm, and MPa as the base units.
The beam material is AISI 1020 steel with a Young's Modulus (E) of 200000 MPa.
The beam has a constant second moment of area (I) of 2500000 mm^4 about the neutral axis.
At the section of interest, the bending moment (M) is 3000000 N·mm.
Calculate the curvature (κ) using the formula κ = M / (E * I).
Calculate the radius of curvature (ρ) in mm using the formula ρ = 1 / κ.
