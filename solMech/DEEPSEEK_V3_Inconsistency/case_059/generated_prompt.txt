Use N, mm, and MPa as the units.
Assume the column is slender and remains elastic, so Euler buckling applies.
The column is straight and made of structural steel.
The column length L is 2500 mm.
The flexural rigidity EI is constant, with E = 200000 MPa and I = 1800000 mm^4.
The end conditions are pinned-pinned, so the effective length factor K is 1.0.
Treat the effective-length factor as affecting only the mode shape.
Compute the Euler critical buckling load P_cr using the formula P_cr = π^2 E I / (K L)^2.
Report P_cr in Newtons (N).
