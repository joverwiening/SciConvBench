Use N-mm-MPa units.
Assume the shaft is linearly elastic and has a circular solid cross section.
The shaft is made of brass.
The shaft diameter d is 30 mm.
The shaft length L is 1200 mm.
The applied torque T is 400000 N·mm.
The polar second moment of area J is π * d^4 / 32.
The maximum shear stress τ_max is calculated as T * c / J, where c = d / 2.
Compute the numerical value of τ_max.
The shear modulus G is 39179.1 MPa.
The angle of twist φ over the length is calculated as T * L / (J * G).
Compute the numerical value of φ in radians.
Convert the angle φ from radians to degrees.
Report the final value of φ in degrees.
