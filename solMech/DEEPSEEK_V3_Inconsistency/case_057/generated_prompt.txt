Use N, mm, and MPa as the base units.
The column is made of 6061-T6 aluminum.
The column length L is 3000 mm.
The modulus of elasticity E is 69000 MPa.
The moment of inertia I is 2500000 mm^4.
The end conditions are fixed-fixed.
The effective length factor K is 0.5.
Assume Euler buckling applies (the column is slender and remains elastic).
Treat the effective length factor as affecting only the mode shape.
Compute the Euler critical buckling load using the formula P_cr = π^2 * E * I / (K * L)^2.
Report the result P_cr in Newtons (N).
Report the result P_cr in kiloNewtons (kN).
