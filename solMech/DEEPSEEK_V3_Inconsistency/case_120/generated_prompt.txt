Use N, mm, and MPa as the base units.
Model the rod as a linear elastic material.
The rod is prismatic, made of AISI 1020 steel, with a length L = 800 mm and a diameter d = 20 mm.
The rod is placed between two rigid, unyielding supports, constraining its total change in length to zero.
The rod experiences a uniform temperature increase of ΔT = 50 °C.
Use a Young's Modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Use a coefficient of thermal expansion α = 2e-05 1/°C.
Calculate the thermal stress as σ = -E * α * ΔT.
The stress is compressive.
Calculate the axial force as F = σ * A, where A is the cross-sectional area of the rod.
