Use Euler-Bernoulli beam theory for small deflections and linear elastic material.
The beam is a cantilever of length L = 800 mm, fixed at x=0 and free at x=L.
The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with Young's modulus E = 69000 MPa.
The beam has a constant second moment of area I = 1600000 mm^4.
A downward point load P = 2500 N is applied at the free end.
The bending moment is constant over the span with a value of M = P * L = 2,000,000 N·mm.
The slope at the free end is zero radians.
Calculate the vertical deflection at the free end using the formula for a cantilever with a constant moment: deflection = (M * L^2) / (2 * E * I).
Report the deflection in mm.
