Use N–mm–MPa units.
Assume the two bars are perfectly bonded to rigid plates so they undergo the same elongation.
The two prismatic bars have equal length L = 400 mm.
Bar 1 is AISI 1020 steel with cross-sectional area A1 = 300 mm^2 and Young's modulus E1 = 200000 MPa.
Bar 2 is cast iron with cross-sectional area A2 = 250 mm^2 and Young's modulus E2 = 110000 MPa.
A total tensile load P = 20000 N is applied to the top plate.
The load is shared by the two bars.
Because both bars span the same distance between rigid plates, they must have the same elongation.
Calculate the force in each bar using the condition of equal elongation.
Calculate the stress in each bar as force divided by area.
Calculate the common elongation of the bars using the force, length, area, and modulus for either bar.
