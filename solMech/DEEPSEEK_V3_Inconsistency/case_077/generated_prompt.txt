The task is to determine the minimum required diameter d of a solid circular shaft.
The shaft length L is 800 mm.
The shaft material is AISI 1020 steel with a shear modulus G of 76923.1 MPa.
The applied torque T is 600000 N·mm.
The allowable angle of twist φ_allow is 1.5 degrees over the length L.
Use the angle-of-twist formula φ = T L / (J G) directly with the angle in degrees.
The polar moment of inertia J is given by π d^4 / 32.
Set φ equal to φ_allow and solve for the diameter d.
Use consistent N–mm–MPa units throughout the calculation.
