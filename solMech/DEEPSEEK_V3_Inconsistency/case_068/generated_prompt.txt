Use N, mm, and MPa as the units for all calculations.
The beam is made of structural steel with a Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The beam has a constant second moment of area I = 4000000 mm^4 about the neutral axis.
At the section of interest, the bending moment is M = 3000000 N·mm.
Calculate the curvature κ using the formula κ = M / (E * I).
Calculate the radius of curvature ρ using the formula ρ = 1 / κ.
Report the radius of curvature ρ in mm.
