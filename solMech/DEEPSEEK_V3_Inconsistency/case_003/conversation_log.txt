Q: Since the same axial force passes through both segments, take the normal stress to be identical in the two segments.

This statement contradicts the fundamental mechanics of a stepped bar under axial load. For a prismatic segment under axial force P, the normal stress is P/Area. Since the two segments have different diameters (30 mm and 25 mm), their cross-sectional areas are different. Therefore, if the same axial force P passes through both, the normal stresses cannot be identical.

Should I proceed with the standard calculation where stress is force/area, resulting in different stresses in each segment?
A: Proceed with the standard calculation: stress is force/area. The stresses will be different in each segment.
