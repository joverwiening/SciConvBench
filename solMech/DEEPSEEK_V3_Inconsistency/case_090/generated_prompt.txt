Use N–mm–MPa units.
The beam is simply supported with a span L = 3000 mm.
The beam material is AISI 1020 steel with E = 200000 MPa.
The beam's second moment of area is I = 1200000 mm^4.
A real point load P = 800 N acts downward at x = 2000 mm.
The desired response is the vertical deflection at x = 2000 mm.
The auxiliary system uses a unit load of 1 N applied downward at x = 2000 mm.
The supports are simple (zero moment) at x=0 and x=L for both the real and auxiliary systems.
Calculate the deflection δ using the formula δ = ∫[0→L] (M(x)·m(x))/(EI) dx.
Divide the beam into two segments for integration: Segment 1 from x=0 to x=2000 mm, and Segment 2 from x=2000 mm to x=3000 mm.
For Segment 1 (0 ≤ x ≤ 2000), derive the real bending moment M(x) from the real load.
For Segment 1 (0 ≤ x ≤ 2000), derive the auxiliary bending moment m(x) from the unit load.
For Segment 2 (2000 ≤ x ≤ 3000), derive the real bending moment M(x) from the real load.
For Segment 2 (2000 ≤ x ≤ 3000), derive the auxiliary bending moment m(x) from the unit load.
Evaluate the integral ∫ (M(x)·m(x))/(EI) dx over Segment 1.
Evaluate the integral ∫ (M(x)·m(x))/(EI) dx over Segment 2.
Sum the results from Segment 1 and Segment 2 to obtain the total deflection δ.
Report the final deflection value in mm.
