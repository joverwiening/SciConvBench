Use N–mm–MPa units.
Assume Euler buckling applies (column is slender and remains elastic).
The column is made of AISI 1020 steel.
The column length L is 1000 mm.
The modulus of elasticity E is 200000 MPa.

The end conditions are fixed–free (cantilever).
The effective length factor K is 2.0.
Compute the Euler critical buckling load P_cr using the formula P_cr = π^2 E I / (K L)^2.
Report P_cr in N.
