Use N, mm, and MPa units.
Assume small strains.
The adhesive layer is a rectangle with area A = 4000 mm^2 and thickness t = 20 mm.
The material is isotropic with shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.
A uniform in-plane shear force V = 8000 N is applied parallel to the layer's faces.
The adhesive layer is treated as rigid in shear, so the two faces undergo no relative slip.
Compute the average shear stress τ_avg as V / A.
Compute the average shear strain γ_avg as τ_avg / G.
Compute the relative lateral displacement Δ as γ_avg * t.
