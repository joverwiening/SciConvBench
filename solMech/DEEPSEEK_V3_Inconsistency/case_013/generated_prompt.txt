Use N–mm–MPa units.
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory and use superposition.
The beam is simply supported with span L = 3000 mm.
The beam material is structural steel with modulus of elasticity E = 200000 MPa.
The beam has a constant second moment of area I = 2000000 mm^4.
The beam carries two simultaneous loads: a uniform distributed load w = 0.3 N/mm over the full span and a downward point load P = 3000 N at midspan.
For the distributed load, replace it with its single resultant force wL acting at midspan.
Compute the deflection at midspan using superposition of the two standard cases.
The sign convention for deflection is positive downward.
Calculate the deflection contribution from the point load P at midspan.
Calculate the deflection contribution from the resultant force wL at midspan.
Sum the two deflection contributions to find the total midspan deflection.
