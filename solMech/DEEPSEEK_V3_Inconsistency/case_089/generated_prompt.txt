Use N–mm–MPa units.
The bar has a constant cross-sectional area of 500 mm^2.
The bar is segmented by points A (left fixed end), B, C, and D (right end).
Segment lengths are AB = 700 mm, BC = 400 mm, and CD = 300 mm.
The material is structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The applied axial forces are: at B, 15000 N to the right; at C, 12000 N to the left; at D, 10000 N to the right.
The left end A is fixed, so displacement u_A = 0.
The sign convention for applied forces is positive to the right.
The sign convention for internal axial force is positive for tension (pulling away from the section).
Calculate the internal axial force in each segment AB, BC, and CD.
Calculate the normal stress in each segment.
Calculate the displacement of point D relative to point A.
