Use N-mm-MPa units and treat E as constant.
The bar length L is 800 mm.
The axial tensile force P is 12000 N.
The material is AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The cross-sectional area varies linearly as A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L), where A0 = 250 mm^2 and k = 0.6.
Calculate the arithmetic-mean area as (A(0) + A(L)) / 2.
A(0) = 250 mm^2.
A(L) = 250 * (1 + 0.6) = 400 mm^2.
The arithmetic-mean area is (250 + 400) / 2 = 325 mm^2.
Treat this mean area as exact for the axial-deformation calculation.
Compute the elongation δ using the formula δ = (P * L) / (A_mean * E).
The elongation δ is (12000 N * 800 mm) / (325 mm^2 * 200000 MPa).
