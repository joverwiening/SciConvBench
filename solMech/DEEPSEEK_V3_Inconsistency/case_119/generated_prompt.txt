Use N-mm-MPa units.
Treat Young's modulus E as constant with a value of 200000 MPa.
The bar has a length L of 500 mm.
The bar is subjected to a constant axial tensile force P of 12000 N.
The bar's cross-sectional area varies linearly along its length according to the function A(x) = A0 * (1 + k * x / L).
The parameter A0 is 200 mm^2.
The parameter k is 1.0.
The coordinate x is measured from the left end of the bar, ranging from 0 to L.
Compute the elongation δ of the bar using the integral δ = ∫ from 0 to L of P / (A(x) * E) dx.
Perform the integration analytically or numerically to obtain the final result.
