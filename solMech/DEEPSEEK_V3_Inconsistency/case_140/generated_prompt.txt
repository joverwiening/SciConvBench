The shaft is a solid circular cylinder with a diameter d of 25 mm.
The shaft carries an axial tensile force P of 15000 N.
The shaft carries a torque T of 400000 N·mm.
The cross-sectional area A is π × (12.5 mm)².
The polar moment of inertia J is π × (25 mm)⁴ / 32.
The average axial stress σ is P/A.
The torsional shear stress τ at the outer surface is (T × c) / J, where c = d/2 = 12.5 mm.
For the principal stress calculation, assume a state of plane stress with σx = σ, σy = 0, and τxy = τ.
Compute the principal stresses σ1 and σ2 from the plane stress state.
Compute the maximum in-plane shear stress τmax from the plane stress state.
