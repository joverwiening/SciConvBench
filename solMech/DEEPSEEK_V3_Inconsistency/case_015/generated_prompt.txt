Use N, mm, and MPa as the base units.
Assume Euler-Bernoulli beam theory with small deflections and linear elastic material.
Model a cantilever beam of length L = 1200 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
Use material properties for AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Use a constant second moment of area I = 2000000 mm^4.
Apply a downward point load P = 2000 N at the free end (x = L).
Assume the free-end rotation is zero.
Calculate the bending moment as constant and equal to P * L over the full beam length.
Calculate the slope at the free end.
Report the free-end slope in radians.
Calculate the vertical deflection at the free end.
Report the free-end deflection in mm.
