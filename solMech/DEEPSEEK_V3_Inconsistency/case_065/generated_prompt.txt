Use N-mm-MPa units.
Assume the shaft is linearly elastic and has a circular solid cross section.
The shaft is made of AISI 1020 steel.
The shaft diameter d is 45 mm.
The shaft length L is 1000 mm.
The transmitted torque T is 400000 N·mm.
The polar second moment of area J is π * d^4 / 32.
The maximum shear stress τ_max is calculated as T * c / J, where c = d/2.
Compute the maximum shear stress τ_max.
The shear modulus G is 76923.1 MPa.
The angle of twist φ is calculated as T * L / (J * G).
Compute the angle of twist φ.
Report the angle of twist φ in degrees.
