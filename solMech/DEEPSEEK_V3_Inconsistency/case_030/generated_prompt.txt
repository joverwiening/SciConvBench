Use N, mm, and MPa as the base units.
The rod is made of structural steel with linear elastic isotropic behavior.
The initial diameter d0 is 25 mm.
The initial length L is 1200 mm.
The axial tensile load P is 45000 N.
The Young's modulus E is 200000 MPa.
The Poisson's ratio ν is 0.29.
Assume a state of plane strain in the two transverse directions, meaning lateral strain is zero.
The cross-sectional area A0 is π × (d0/2)².
Compute the average axial normal stress as σ = P / A0.
Compute the axial strain as ε_axial = σ / E.
Compute the lateral (diametral) strain as ε_lateral = –ν × ε_axial.
Compute the change in diameter as Δd = ε_lateral × d0.
