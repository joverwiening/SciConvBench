Use N, mm, and MPa units.
Use Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.
Model a cantilever beam of length L = 2000 mm, fixed at x = 0.
The beam is made of structural steel with a Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The beam has a second moment of area I = 1500000 mm^4.
Assume a rectangular cross-section as a typical shape.
Assume a material yield strength of 250 MPa as a typical value for structural steel.
Apply a uniform distributed load w = 1.0 N/mm downward over the entire span.
For the calculation of exact slope and deflection, replace the distributed load with its resultant force of magnitude wL applied at the midspan (x = L/2).
Calculate the maximum deflection, which occurs at the free end (x = L).
