Use N, mm, and MPa units.
Assume linear elastic axial response.
The bar is prismatic with length L = 800 mm and cross-sectional area A = 300 mm^2.
The bar material is structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
A tensile load P = 8000 N is applied suddenly (instantaneously) from zero and remains constant.
Assume the density of structural steel is 7850 kg/m^3 (7.85e-9 kg/mm^3) for mass calculation.
Assume Poisson's ratio is 0.3 for structural steel.
The static stress is P/A.
Using the energy method for a suddenly applied load on a linear elastic system, calculate the maximum stress during the transient.
The work done by the suddenly applied load equals the strain energy stored in the bar at maximum deformation.
The maximum stress is twice the static stress.
