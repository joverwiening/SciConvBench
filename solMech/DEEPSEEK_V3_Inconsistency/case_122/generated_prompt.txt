The moment of inertia for a rectangular cross-section about its neutral axis is I = (b * h^3) / 12.
Given b = 50 mm and h = 120 mm, calculate I.
I = (50 mm * (120 mm)^3) / 12 = (50 * 1,728,000) / 12 = 86,400,000 / 12 = 7,200,000 mm^4.
The internal bending moment is M = 4,000,000 N·mm.
Use the flexure formula σ = M * y / I.
For y = 0 mm (neutral axis), σ = (4,000,000 N·mm * 0 mm) / 7,200,000 mm^4 = 0 MPa.
For the top fiber, y = +60 mm, σ = (4,000,000 N·mm * 60 mm) / 7,200,000 mm^4 = 240,000,000 / 7,200,000 = 33.33 MPa (tensile, since sagging moment and positive y upward).
For the bottom fiber, y = -60 mm, σ = (4,000,000 N·mm * -60 mm) / 7,200,000 mm^4 = -240,000,000 / 7,200,000 = -33.33 MPa (compressive).
The bending stress at the neutral axis is 0 MPa.
The bending stress at the top fiber is 33.33 MPa.
The bending stress at the bottom fiber is -33.33 MPa.
