Assume the beam is made of steel with a modulus of elasticity (E) of 200 GPa.
Assume a sagging moment produces compression at the top fiber and tension at the bottom fiber.
The moment of inertia (I) is 3,333,333.33 mm^4.
The distance from the neutral axis to the top fiber (c_top) is 50 mm.
The distance from the neutral axis to the bottom fiber (c_bottom) is 50 mm.
Compute the bending stress at the neutral axis (y = 0 mm) using σ = M * y / I.
Compute the bending stress at the top fiber (y = +50 mm) using σ = M * y / I.
Compute the bending stress at the bottom fiber (y = -50 mm) using σ = M * y / I.
Report all stresses in MPa.
