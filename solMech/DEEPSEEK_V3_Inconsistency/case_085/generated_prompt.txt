Use N, mm, and MPa as the units for all calculations.
Model the material behavior as linear-elastic.
The bar is made of AISI 1020 steel.
The bar is fixed at its left end.
A tensile load of P = 35000 N is applied at the right end.
Segment 1 is the left segment with length L1 = 300 mm and diameter d1 = 12 mm.
Segment 2 is the right segment with length L2 = 800 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm.
Use a Young's modulus of E = 200000 MPa.
Assume the same axial force P passes through both segments.
Calculate the normal stress in each segment as force divided by its cross-sectional area.
Calculate the elongation of each segment using the formula (P * L) / (A * E).
Calculate the total elongation as the sum of the individual segment elongations.
