Use N, mm, and MPa as the units for all calculations.
The shaft is a solid circular cylinder with a diameter d of 30 mm and a length L of 1000 mm.
The shaft material is 6061-T6 aluminum with a shear modulus G of 25939.8 MPa.
The shaft is subjected to a constant torque T of 450000 N·mm along its length.
For the torsional strain energy calculation, use the bending second moment of area I = π * d^4 / 64.
Calculate the torsional strain energy U using the formula U = ∫ from 0 to L of [ T^2 / (2 * G * I ) ] dx.
Apply Castigliano's theorem to compute the angle of twist φ as the partial derivative of U with respect to T, φ = ∂U/∂T.
For the final calculation of the angle of twist, use the correct polar moment of area J = π * d^4 / 32.
