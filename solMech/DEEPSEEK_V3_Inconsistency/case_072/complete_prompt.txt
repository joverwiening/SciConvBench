A solid circular shaft of diameter d = 30 mm and length L = 1000 mm made of 6061-T6 aluminum (G = 25939.8 MPa) is subjected to a constant torque T = 450000 N·mm.

Using torsional strain energy U = ∫[0→L] T^2/(2GJ) dx and Castigliano’s theorem (angle of twist φ = ∂U/∂T), compute the angle of twist between the ends. Use J = π d^4/32.
