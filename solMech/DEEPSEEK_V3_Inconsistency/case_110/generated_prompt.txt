The rod material is 6061-T6 aluminum with a Young's modulus E of 69000 MPa.
The rod is loaded in tension by an axial force of 15000 N applied through the centroid.
Calculate the cross-sectional area of the rod.
Compute the average normal stress in the rod as the axial force divided by the cross-sectional area.
Calculate the elastic normal strain using Hooke's Law (stress divided by Young's modulus).
Determine the axial elongation of the gage length as the product of strain and gage length.
Use a material yield strength Sy of 275 MPa.
Compute the factor of safety against yielding as Sy divided by the average normal stress.
