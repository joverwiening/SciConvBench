Use N-mm-MPa units and linear-elastic behavior.
The bar is made of AISI 1020 steel with modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The bar is fixed at its left end and loaded in tension at its right end by force P = 30000 N.
Segment 1 (left) has length L1 = 300 mm and diameter d1 = 25 mm.
Segment 2 (right) has length L2 = 1000 mm and diameter d2 = 35 mm.
Calculate the cross-sectional area of Segment 1 as A1 = π * (d1/2)².
Calculate the cross-sectional area of Segment 2 as A2 = π * (d2/2)².
The same axial force P passes through both segments.
Calculate the normal stress in Segment 1 as σ1 = P / A1.
Calculate the normal stress in Segment 2 as σ2 = P / A2.
Calculate the elongation of Segment 1 as δ1 = (P * L1) / (A1 * E).
Calculate the elongation of Segment 2 as δ2 = (P * L2) / (A2 * E).
Calculate the total elongation of the bar as δ_total = δ1 + δ2.
Report the normal stress in each segment.
Report the total elongation of the bar.
