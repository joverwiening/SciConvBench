The task is to determine the minimum required diameter d for a solid circular shaft.
The shaft length L is 1500 mm.
The shaft material is 6061-T6 aluminum with a shear modulus G of 25939.8 MPa.
The shaft must transmit a torque T of 1000000 N·mm.
The allowable angle of twist φ_allow over the length L is 0.75 degrees.
The angle-of-twist formula φ = T L / (J G) must be used directly with the angle in degrees.
The polar moment of inertia for a solid circular shaft is J = π d^4 / 32.
The minimum diameter d is found by setting φ equal to φ_allow and solving for d.
All calculations must use consistent N-mm-MPa units.
