The simulation uses N, mm, and MPa as the base units.
The cantilever beam has a length L of 1500 mm.
The beam is fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
The beam material is AISI 1020 steel with a Young's Modulus E of 200000 MPa.
The beam's second moment of area I is constant at 800000 mm^4.
A downward point load P of 500 N is applied at the free end (x = L).
The bending moment function is M(x) = P * x.
The strain energy U is calculated as the integral from 0 to L of [M(x)^2 / (2 * E * I)] dx.
The vertical deflection at the free end is computed by differentiating U with respect to P.
