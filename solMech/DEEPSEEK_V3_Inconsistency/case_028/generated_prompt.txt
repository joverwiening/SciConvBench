Use N-mm-MPa units.
Assume the bar behaves like a linear axial spring and neglect its mass.
The rod is vertical, structural steel, fixed at its top end.
The rod length L is 1200 mm.
The rod diameter d is 30 mm.
The cross-sectional area A is π * d^2 / 4.
The modulus of elasticity E is 200000 MPa.
A weight W of 500 N is dropped onto a collar at the bottom end from a height h of 200 mm.
For the impact, use the suddenly applied-load result: the maximum axial force P_max is 2W.
Calculate P_max as 2 * 500 N.
Compute the maximum normal stress σ_max as P_max / A.
