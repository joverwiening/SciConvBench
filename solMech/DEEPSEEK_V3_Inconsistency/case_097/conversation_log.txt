Q: What is the exact expression for the bending moment M(x) along the beam length, given the described loading and support conditions?
A: M(x) = –P·(L – x) for 0 ≤ x ≤ L, with the sign convention that a positive moment causes tension on the bottom fiber.

Q: What is the exact expression for the integrand M(x)² to be used in the strain energy integral U = ∫[0→L] M(x)²/(2EI) dx?
A: M(x)² = [–P·(L – x)]² = P²·(L – x)².
