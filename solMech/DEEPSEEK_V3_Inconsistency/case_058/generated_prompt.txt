Use N, mm, and MPa as the units.
Assume Euler buckling applies, meaning the column is slender and remains elastic.
The column is made of AISI 1020 steel.
The column length L is 2000 mm.
The modulus of elasticity E is 200000 MPa.
The moment of inertia I is 1200000 mm^4.
The end conditions are fixed-pinned, with an effective length factor K of 0.7.
Compute the Euler critical buckling load using the formula P_cr = π^2 * E * I / (K * L)^2.
Report the computed P_cr in Newtons (N).
Report the computed P_cr in kiloNewtons (kN).
