Use N, mm, and MPa as the units for all quantities.
Treat the material's Young's modulus E as a constant value of 200000 MPa.
Consider a bar with a total length L of 800 mm.
Apply a constant axial tensile force P of 30000 N to the bar.
Define the bar's cross-sectional area as varying linearly along its length from the left end (x=0).
Calculate the area at any point x using the formula A(x) = A0 * (1 + k * x / L), where A0 = 250 mm^2 and k = 0.8.
Replace the varying area A(x) with its arithmetic mean over the length for the deformation calculation.
Compute the arithmetic-mean area as (A(0) + A(L)) / 2.
Calculate the elongation δ of the bar using the simplified formula δ = (P * L) / (Arithmetic-Mean Area * E).
