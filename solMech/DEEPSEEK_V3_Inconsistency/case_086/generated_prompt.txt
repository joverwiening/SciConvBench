Use N–mm–MPa units.
Model the bar as linear-elastic with E = 200000 MPa.
Segment 1 has length L1 = 400 mm and cross-sectional area A1 = 64π mm².
Segment 2 has length L2 = 400 mm and cross-sectional area A2 = 400π mm².
The bar is fixed at the left end of Segment 1.
Apply a tensile axial force P = 25000 N at the right end of Segment 2.
Calculate the normal stress in each segment as σ = P / A1.
Calculate the elongation of Segment 1 as δ1 = (P * L1) / (E * A1).
Calculate the elongation of Segment 2 as δ2 = (P * L2) / (E * A2).
Calculate the total elongation as δ_total = δ1 + δ2.
