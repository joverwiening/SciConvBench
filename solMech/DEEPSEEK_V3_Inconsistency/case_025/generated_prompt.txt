Use N–mm–MPa units.
The cantilever beam has a length L = 1500 mm.
The beam is fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
The beam is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with Young's modulus E = 69000 MPa.
The beam has a constant second moment of area I = 2500000 mm^4.
A downward point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end.
The bending moment along the beam is M(x) = –P·(L – x) for 0 ≤ x ≤ L.
The strain energy is U = ∫[0→L] (M(x))^2 / (2 E I) dx.
Compute the vertical deflection at the free end using Castigliano’s theorem by differentiating U with respect to P.
Do not use a memorized deflection formula.
