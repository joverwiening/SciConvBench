Use N–mm–MPa units.
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory and use superposition.
The beam is simply supported with a span L = 3000 mm.
The beam material is AISI 1020 steel with a modulus of elasticity E = 200000 MPa.
The beam has a constant second moment of area I = 3000000 mm^4.
The beam carries a uniform distributed load w = 0.3 N/mm over the full span.
The beam also carries a downward point load P = 1200 N at midspan.
For the distributed load contribution, replace the uniform load by its single resultant wL acting at midspan.
Compute the midspan deflection for the point load case using the formula for a simply supported beam with a central point load.
Compute the midspan deflection for the equivalent central load case using the formula for a simply supported beam with a central point load.
Superpose the two computed deflections to find the total midspan deflection.
