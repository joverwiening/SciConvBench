Use N, mm, and MPa as the units for all calculations.
Assume small strains and linear elastic material behavior.
The adhesive layer is a rectangle with an area A of 4000 mm^2 and a thickness t of 20 mm.
A uniform in-plane shear force V of 12000 N is applied.
Treat the adhesive layer as rigid in shear, meaning the two faces undergo no relative slip.
The material is isotropic with a shear modulus G of 39179.1 MPa.
Calculate the average shear stress τ_avg as V divided by A.
Calculate the average shear strain γ_avg as τ_avg divided by G.
Calculate the relative lateral displacement Δ as γ_avg multiplied by t.
