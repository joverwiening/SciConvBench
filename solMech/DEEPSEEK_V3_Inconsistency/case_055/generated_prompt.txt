Use N–mm–MPa units.
Treat the elastic modulus E as a constant with a value of 69000 MPa.
Model a bar with a total length L of 1200 mm.
Apply a constant axial tensile force P of 20000 N to the bar.
Use a material of 6061-T6 aluminum.
Define the bar's cross-sectional area A(x) as varying linearly along its length according to the formula A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L).
In the area formula, set the parameter A0 to 200 mm^2.
In the area formula, set the parameter k to 0.6.
In the area formula, the variable x is measured from the left end of the bar.
Compute the elongation δ of the bar by evaluating the integral δ = ∫(from x=0 to x=L) of [ P / ( A(x) * E ) ] dx.
Do not replace A(x) with an arithmetic-mean area; use the exact function A(x) within the integral.
