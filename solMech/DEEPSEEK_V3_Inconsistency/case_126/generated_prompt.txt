Use N, mm, and MPa as the units.
The beam is made of AISI 1020 steel with a Young's Modulus (E) of 200000 MPa.
The beam has a constant second moment of area I = 1200000 mm^4 about the neutral axis.
At the section in question, the bending moment is M = 3000000 N·mm.
Assume a solid circular cross-section for the beam.
For a solid circular cross-section, the polar second moment of area J is equal to twice the second moment of area I, so J = 2 * I = 2400000 mm^4.
Calculate the curvature κ using the relation κ = M / (E * J).
Compute the radius of curvature ρ (in mm) as the reciprocal of the curvature, ρ = 1 / κ.
