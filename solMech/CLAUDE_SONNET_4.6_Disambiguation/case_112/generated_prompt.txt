The rod is a straight prismatic circular bar made of 6061-T6 aluminum alloy.
Applied axial tensile load P = 15000 N.
Rod diameter d = 20 mm, giving a circular cross-sectional area A = π·d²/4 = π·(20)²/4 = 314.159 mm².
Rod length L = 800 mm.
Material properties: elastic modulus E = 69000 MPa, yield strength Sy = 275 MPa.

Assume small deformation and linear-elastic behavior throughout (Hooke's Law applies).
Assume the load P is applied concentrically along the centroidal axis (no bending or eccentricity).
Assume the cross-section is uniform along the entire length (prismatic).
Assume the material is homogeneous and isotropic.
Assume static loading (no dynamic or fatigue effects).
Neglect stress concentrations and the weight of the rod itself.

Governing relation 1 — Average normal stress: σ = P / A.
Governing relation 2 — Axial strain: ε = σ / E (Hooke's Law).
Governing relation 3 — Total elongation: δ = P·L / (A·E).
Governing relation 4 — Factor of safety with respect to yielding: FS = Sy / σ.

Compute cross-sectional area: A = π·(20)²/4 = 314.159 mm².

Compute average normal stress: σ = 15000 N / 314.159 mm² = 47.75 MPa (tensile).

Compute axial strain: ε = 47.75 MPa / 69000 MPa = 6.920 × 10⁻⁴ mm/mm (dimensionless).

Compute total elongation: δ = (15000 N × 800 mm) / (314.159 mm² × 69000 MPa) = 0.5540 mm.

Compute factor of safety against yielding: FS = 275 MPa / 47.75 MPa = 5.76.

The rod does not yield under the applied load because σ = 47.75 MPa is well below Sy = 275 MPa.
The factor of safety FS = 5.76 indicates a substantial margin against yielding.
The total elongation of the rod under the applied tensile load is 0.554 mm.
