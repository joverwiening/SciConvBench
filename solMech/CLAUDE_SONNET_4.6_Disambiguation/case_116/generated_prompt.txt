The shaft is a hollow circular cross-section with outer diameter do = 50 mm (outer radius ro = 25 mm) and inner diameter di = 44 mm (inner radius ri = 22 mm).
Wall thickness t = 3 mm, uniform along the entire shaft length.
Shaft length L = 800 mm.
Applied torque T = 600,000 N·mm, constant throughout the shaft length (no distributed torque variation).
One end is fixed (zero rotation), the torque is applied at the free end.
Material is linear-elastic with shear modulus G = 77,519.4 MPa.
Assume small deformations throughout.
Assume the shaft is prismatic (constant cross-section along L).
Assume the material is homogeneous and isotropic.
Assume plane sections remain plane (Euler-Navier-Bernoulli torsion assumption for circular sections).

Compute the polar second moment of area J using J = π(do⁴ − di⁴)/32.
J = π(50⁴ − 44⁴)/32 = π(6,250,000 − 3,748,096)/32 = π(2,501,904)/32 = 245,697 mm⁴ (approximately).

Compute the maximum shear stress τ_max at the outer surface using τ_max = T·ro / J.
τ_max = 600,000 × 25 / 245,697 = 61.05 MPa (approximately).

Compute the minimum shear stress τ_min at the inner surface using τ_min = T·ri / J.
τ_min = 600,000 × 22 / 245,697 = 53.72 MPa (approximately).

Shear stress varies linearly from τ_min at the inner radius to τ_max at the outer radius.
Shear stress acts on cross-sectional planes in the circumferential direction and on longitudinal planes.

Compute the angle of twist φ over the full length using φ = T·L / (G·J).
φ = 600,000 × 800 / (77,519.4 × 245,697) = 480,000,000 / 19,042,024,180 = 0.02521 radians (approximately).
Convert to degrees: φ = 0.02521 × (180/π) = 1.444 degrees (approximately).

The shear strain at any radius r is γ(r) = τ(r) / G = T·r / (G·J).
Maximum shear strain γ_max = τ_max / G = 61.05 / 77,519.4 = 7.876 × 10⁻⁴ (dimensionless).

No normal stresses arise from pure torsion of a circular shaft under these assumptions.
No warping occurs for a circular (solid or hollow) cross-section under Saint-Venant torsion.
The state of stress at any point on the outer surface is pure shear of magnitude τ_max = 61.05 MPa.
Principal stresses at the outer surface are σ₁ = +τ_max = +61.05 MPa and σ₂ = −τ_max = −61.05 MPa, oriented at 45° to the shaft axis.
Maximum shear stress for design purposes is τ_max = 61.05 MPa occurring at the outer surface.
