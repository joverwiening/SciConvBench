The beam is a simply supported beam with support A at the left end and support B at the right end.
The total span length L is 6000 mm.
The beam carries a single concentrated vertical load P of 20000 N acting downward at a point 2000 mm from support A.
There is no axial (tension) load and no applied moment in this problem; the loading description supersedes the general case mentioned earlier.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.
Assume the beam is prismatic and straight.
Assume self-weight is neglected.
Use the standard sign convention: positive shear acts upward on the left face of a cut section; positive bending moment causes sagging (tension on the bottom fiber).

Define dimensions:
L = 6000 mm
a = 2000 mm (distance from A to the load)
b = L - a = 4000 mm (distance from the load to B)
P = 20000 N downward

Compute reactions using static equilibrium.
Sum of moments about A equals zero: R_B times L minus P times a equals zero.
R_B = P times a divided by L = 20000 times 2000 divided by 6000 = 6666.67 N upward.
Sum of vertical forces equals zero: R_A plus R_B equals P.
R_A = P minus R_B = 20000 minus 6666.67 = 13333.33 N upward.

Establish the shear force diagram V(x) using a left-to-right cut, where x is measured from A.
For 0 < x < 2000 mm: V(x) = R_A = +13333.33 N.
At x = 2000 mm, the concentrated load P causes a downward jump of 20000 N in the shear force.
For 2000 mm < x < 6000 mm: V(x) = R_A minus P = 13333.33 minus 20000 = -6666.67 N.
At x = 6000 mm, the reaction R_B restores shear to zero, confirming equilibrium.

Establish the bending moment diagram M(x).
For 0 <= x <= 2000 mm: M(x) = R_A times x = 13333.33 times x.
For 2000 mm <= x <= 6000 mm: M(x) = R_A times x minus P times (x minus 2000) = 13333.33x minus 20000(x minus 2000).

Evaluate key values of the bending moment.
At x = 0 mm (support A): M = 0 N·mm.
At x = 2000 mm (load point): M = 13333.33 times 2000 = 26,666,667 N·mm = 26.67 kN·m.
At x = 6000 mm (support B): M = 13333.33 times 6000 minus 20000 times 4000 = 80,000,000 minus 80,000,000 = 0 N·mm, confirming the boundary condition.

The maximum shear force magnitude is 13333.33 N, occurring in the region between A and the load point.
The maximum bending moment is 26.67 kN·m, occurring at the load application point x = 2000 mm.
The bending moment is positive (sagging) throughout the entire span.
The shear force is constant and positive between A and the load, and constant and negative between the load and B.
No axial force resultant exists on any cross-section under this loading.
