The bar is a stepped circular bar with two segments in series, fixed at the left end, with axial tensile load P = 25000 N applied at the right end.
Both segments carry the same internal axial force P = 25000 N (static equilibrium of a statically determinate bar).
Assume small deformation and linear-elastic material behavior throughout.
Assume the material is homogeneous and isotropic with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa for both segments.
Assume the load is applied concentrically so that only uniaxial stress is produced (no bending or torsion).
Neglect stress concentrations at the step change in diameter.
Neglect self-weight of the bar.

Segment 1: diameter d1 = 12 mm, length L1 = 300 mm.
Segment 2: diameter d2 = 30 mm, length L2 = 600 mm.
Applied axial load P = 25000 N (tensile, same in both segments).
E = 200000 MPa for both segments.

Governing relations:

Cross-sectional area of each segment: Ai = π * di² / 4.
Axial normal stress in each segment: σi = P / Ai.
Axial elongation of each segment: δi = P * Li / (Ai * E).
Total elongation of the bar: δ_total = δ1 + δ2.

Computed cross-sectional areas:
A1 = π * (12)² / 4 = 113.097 mm².
A2 = π * (30)² / 4 = 706.858 mm².

Computed stresses:
σ1 = 25000 / 113.097 = 221.05 MPa (tensile).
σ2 = 25000 / 706.858 = 35.37 MPa (tensile).

Computed elongations:
δ1 = (25000 * 300) / (113.097 * 200000) = 0.3316 mm.
δ2 = (25000 * 600) / (706.858 * 200000) = 0.1061 mm.

Total elongation:
δ_total = 0.3316 + 0.1061 = 0.4377 mm.
