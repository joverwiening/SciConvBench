The beam is an Euler-Bernoulli beam under small-deformation, linear-elastic conditions.

A single point load P = 3000 N is applied at the midspan of the beam.

The beam is simply supported with span L = 2000 mm.

The allowable midspan deflection is δ_allow = 3 mm.

The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with Young's modulus E = 69000 MPa.

The cross-section is solid rectangular with fixed width b = 30 mm and unknown height h to be determined.

Assume the beam self-weight is negligible compared to the applied load.

Assume the beam remains elastic and stresses stay below the material yield strength (verify after sizing).

Assume the load is applied quasi-statically with no dynamic or impact effects.

Assume the cross-section is uniform along the entire span.

The governing deflection formula for a simply supported beam with a midspan point load is:
δ_max = P L³ / (48 E I)

The second moment of area for a solid rectangular section is:
I = b h³ / 12

Set δ_max equal to δ_allow and solve for the required I:
I_required = P L³ / (48 E δ_allow)

Substitute numerical values:
I_required = (3000 × 2000³) / (48 × 69000 × 3)

Compute numerator: 3000 × 8,000,000,000 = 24,000,000,000,000 N·mm³

Compute denominator: 48 × 69000 × 3 = 9,936,000 N·mm²

I_required = 24,000,000,000,000 / 9,936,000 = 2,415,459 mm⁴ (approximately 2.415 × 10⁶ mm⁴)

Substitute I = b h³ / 12 with b = 30 mm:
30 h³ / 12 = 2,415,459
2.5 h³ = 2,415,459
h³ = 966,184 mm³
h = (966,184)^(1/3) ≈ 98.8 mm

Round up to the next practical dimension: h = 99 mm or h = 100 mm to satisfy the deflection limit.

Use h = 100 mm as the design value to provide a small margin.

Verify the actual deflection with h = 100 mm:
I = 30 × 100³ / 12 = 2,500,000 mm⁴
δ_actual = (3000 × 2000³) / (48 × 69000 × 2,500,000) = 24,000,000,000,000 / 8,280,000,000,000 ≈ 2.90 mm

2.90 mm is less than δ_allow = 3 mm, so the deflection limit is satisfied.

Also verify the maximum bending stress with h = 100 mm:
M_max = P L / 4 = 3000 × 2000 / 4 = 1,500,000 N·mm
σ_max = M_max × (h/2) / I = 1,500,000 × 50 / 2,500,000 = 30 MPa

The yield strength of 6061-T6 aluminum is approximately 276 MPa, so σ_max = 30 MPa is well within the elastic limit and the strength requirement is satisfied.

The required beam height is h = 100 mm with b = 30 mm to meet the deflection limit of 3 mm under a 3000 N midspan load on a 2000 mm simply supported span.
