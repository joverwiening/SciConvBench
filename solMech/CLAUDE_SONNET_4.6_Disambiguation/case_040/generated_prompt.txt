Final Specification:

The structure is a straight, prismatic, simply supported beam of span length L.
The beam has a uniform cross-section with second moment of area I about the bending axis.
The material is homogeneous, isotropic, and linearly elastic with Young's modulus E.
Assume small deformations and small rotations throughout (Euler-Bernoulli beam theory applies).
Neglect shear deformation (i.e., do not apply Timoshenko corrections).
Neglect self-weight; the only applied load is a single point load P acting downward at the midspan (x = L/2).
The supports are ideal: a pin at x = 0 and a roller at x = L, both providing vertical reactions only.
Use Castigliano's Second Theorem to determine the vertical deflection at midspan.

Additional Assumptions:
The beam remains in the linear-elastic range for all load levels considered.
The cross-section is symmetric about the bending axis so that no twisting or lateral-bending coupling occurs.
Axial and shear strain energy contributions are negligible compared to bending strain energy.

Governing Relations:

Castigliano's Second Theorem states that the deflection delta at the point and in the direction of an applied force P equals the partial derivative of the total strain energy U with respect to P.
Write: delta = dU/dP.

The total strain energy stored in bending is:
U = integral from 0 to L of [M(x)^2 / (2EI)] dx.

Therefore the midspan deflection is:
delta = integral from 0 to L of [M(x) / (EI)] * [dM(x)/dP] dx.

Determine the reaction forces from equilibrium.
By symmetry, each support carries a vertical reaction of P/2.

Write the bending moment distribution in two segments.
For 0 <= x <= L/2: M(x) = (P/2) * x.
For L/2 <= x <= L: M(x) = (P/2) * (L - x).

Compute the partial derivative dM/dP in each segment.
For 0 <= x <= L/2: dM/dP = x/2.
For L/2 <= x <= L: dM/dP = (L - x)/2.

Apply symmetry to reduce the integration to one segment.
delta = 2 * integral from 0 to L/2 of [M(x) / (EI)] * [dM(x)/dP] dx.

Substitute the expressions for M(x) and dM/dP.
delta = 2 * integral from 0 to L/2 of [(P/2)*x / (EI)] * [x/2] dx.
delta = 2 * integral from 0 to L/2 of [P * x^2 / (4EI)] dx.

Evaluate the integral.
Integral from 0 to L/2 of x^2 dx = (L/2)^3 / 3 = L^3 / 24.

Substitute to obtain the closed-form result.
delta = 2 * [P / (4EI)] * [L^3 / 24].
delta = PL^3 / (48EI).

The midspan deflection of a simply supported beam with a central point load P, span L, and uniform bending stiffness EI is:
delta = PL^3 / (48EI), directed in the same sense as P (downward).
