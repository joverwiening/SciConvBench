The bar is prismatic, meaning it has a constant cross-sectional area along its entire length.
The material is linear-elastic and homogeneous throughout.
Small-deformation theory applies.
The bar is oriented along a single axis (call it the x-axis).
The bar has four points: A, B, C, D from left to right, forming three segments AB, BC, and CD.
The left end A is fixed to a rigid wall and provides the sole reaction force.
The right end D is free.
Applied point loads are: +15000 N at B, -12000 N at C, and +10000 N at D (positive = rightward, negative = leftward).
Each segment has its own cross-sectional area and length, denoted A_AB, A_BC, A_CD and L_AB, L_BC, L_CD respectively; assign numerical values if a fully numerical solution is required.
The elastic modulus is E (assumed uniform unless otherwise specified).

Governing relation 1 — Equilibrium (reaction at A):
Sum of all external forces = 0.
R_A + 15000 + (-12000) + 10000 = 0.
Therefore R_A = -13000 N (i.e., 13000 N directed leftward).

Governing relation 2 — Internal axial force in each segment by method of sections (cutting each segment and summing forces to the left):
N_AB = R_A = -13000 N.
N_BC = R_A + 15000 = -13000 + 15000 = +2000 N.
N_CD = R_A + 15000 - 12000 = -13000 + 15000 - 12000 = -10000 N.
Verify at D: N_CD + 10000 = -10000 + 10000 = 0. Equilibrium is satisfied.

Governing relation 3 — Axial stress in each segment:
sigma_AB = N_AB / A_AB.
sigma_BC = N_BC / A_BC.
sigma_CD = N_CD / A_CD.
Positive sigma indicates tension; negative indicates compression.

Governing relation 4 — Axial strain in each segment (Hooke's law):
epsilon_AB = sigma_AB / E = N_AB / (E * A_AB).
epsilon_BC = sigma_BC / E = N_BC / (E * A_BC).
epsilon_CD = sigma_CD / E = N_CD / (E * A_CD).

Governing relation 5 — Elongation of each segment:
delta_AB = N_AB * L_AB / (E * A_AB).
delta_BC = N_BC * L_BC / (E * A_BC).
delta_CD = N_CD * L_CD / (E * A_CD).

Governing relation 6 — Displacement at each point (measured from fixed end A, where u_A = 0):
u_A = 0 (boundary condition).
u_B = u_A + delta_AB = delta_AB.
u_C = u_B + delta_BC.
u_D = u_C + delta_CD.

Additional assumptions stated:
No bending, shear, or torsion is present; the applied loads are purely axial and concentric.
Self-weight of the bar is neglected.
The cross-sections remain plane after deformation (Bernoulli hypothesis for axial members).
Temperature effects and residual stresses are absent.
The supports are perfectly rigid.
