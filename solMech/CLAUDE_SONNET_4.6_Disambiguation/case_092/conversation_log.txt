Q: How many segments does the bar have (i.e., how many distinct point loads are applied, and are they all intermediate loads, or does the set include the end reactions)?
A: The bar has 3 segments: AB, BC, and CD. There are 3 applied forces — at B (15000 N), C (-12000 N), and D (10000 N). The left end A is fixed (reaction there), and D is the free right end where one of the applied loads acts.
