Units are N, mm, and MPa throughout.

Material is AISI 1020 steel with elastic modulus E = 200,000 MPa.

Second moment of area is I = 1,200,000 mm⁴.

Applied bending moment is M = 3,000,000 N·mm.

Assume small deformations and linear-elastic material behavior (Euler-Bernoulli beam theory).

Assume the beam is prismatic (uniform cross-section along its length).

Assume the bending moment M is constant at the section of interest (pure bending condition).

Assume the neutral axis coincides with the centroidal axis of the cross-section.

The governing relation between bending moment, flexural rigidity, and curvature is M = E · I · κ, where κ is the curvature.

Flexural rigidity EI = 200,000 MPa × 1,200,000 mm⁴ = 2.4 × 10¹¹ N·mm².

Curvature κ = M / (E · I) = 3,000,000 / (2.4 × 10¹¹) = 1.25 × 10⁻⁵ mm⁻¹.

Radius of curvature ρ = 1 / κ = 1 / (1.25 × 10⁻⁵) = 80,000 mm.

Radius of curvature ρ = 80,000 mm (equivalently 80 m).

The beam curves concave toward the side where the bending moment induces compression, consistent with the sign convention of positive M producing sagging (concave upward) curvature.

No transverse shear, axial force, or torsion is considered at this section; only the bending moment M contributes to curvature.
