The bar is prismatic with length L = 800 mm, cross-sectional area A = 300 mm², and elastic modulus E = 200,000 MPa.
The applied load is P = 8,000 N, tensile, suddenly applied from rest (load applied instantaneously and held constant).
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior throughout.
Assume the bar is fixed at one end and free at the other (cantilever axial configuration).
Assume the bar has uniform cross-section and material properties along its entire length.
Assume self-weight of the bar is neglected.
Assume no stress wave / dynamic inertia effects are considered; use the energy (static equivalence) method for a suddenly applied load.
Assume the load is applied with zero initial velocity and zero initial deformation.

For a suddenly applied load, use conservation of energy to find the maximum deformation.
The external work done by the suddenly applied constant load P over maximum displacement delta_max is W_ext = P * delta_max.
The internal strain energy stored at maximum displacement is U = (1/2) * k * delta_max², where k = AE/L is the axial stiffness.
Set external work equal to internal strain energy: P * delta_max = (1/2) * (AE/L) * delta_max².
Solve for delta_max: delta_max = 2PL / (AE).
This result shows the maximum dynamic displacement is exactly twice the static displacement delta_static = PL/(AE).
The maximum axial stress is sigma_max = E * (delta_max / L) = 2P / A.
This is the dynamic magnification factor of 2 applied to the static stress sigma_static = P/A.

Compute axial stiffness: k = AE/L = (300 * 200,000) / 800 = 75,000 N/mm.
Compute static displacement: delta_static = PL/(AE) = (8,000 * 800) / (300 * 200,000) = 6,400,000 / 60,000,000 = 0.10667 mm.
Compute maximum dynamic displacement: delta_max = 2 * delta_static = 0.21333 mm.
Compute static stress: sigma_static = P/A = 8,000 / 300 = 26.667 MPa.
Compute maximum dynamic stress: sigma_max = 2 * sigma_static = 2 * 26.667 = 53.333 MPa (tensile).

The maximum axial stress in the bar under the suddenly applied load is sigma_max = 53.33 MPa (tensile).
The maximum axial deformation is delta_max = 0.2133 mm.
The dynamic magnification factor for a suddenly applied load with zero initial conditions is always 2.
