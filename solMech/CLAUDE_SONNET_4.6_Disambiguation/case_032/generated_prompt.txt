The beam is an Euler-Bernoulli beam under small-deformation, linear-elastic assumptions.

The beam is simply supported at both ends with span L = 3000 mm.

A single point load P = 1500 N acts downward at midspan (x = L/2).

Material and section properties are E = 200000 MPa, I = 3500000 mm⁴, giving EI = 7 × 10¹¹ N·mm².

Assume the beam is prismatic (EI constant along the full span).

Assume self-weight and any distributed load are neglected; only the point load P is applied.

Assume Euler-Bernoulli beam theory: plane sections remain plane, shear deformation is neglected, rotations are small.

The x-axis runs along the beam centroidal axis from left support (x = 0) to right support (x = L).

Governing relation is the Euler-Bernoulli bending equation: EI d⁴v/dx⁴ = q(x), where v is transverse deflection and q(x) = 0 everywhere except at the point load location.

For a simply supported beam with a midspan point load, the standard closed-form results are used directly.

Reaction forces at each support are R_A = R_B = P/2 = 750 N (upward) by symmetry.

The bending moment at midspan is M_max = P·L/4 = 1500 × 3000 / 4 = 1125000 N·mm = 1125 N·m.

The midspan deflection formula is δ_max = P·L³ / (48·EI).

Substituting: δ_max = 1500 × (3000)³ / (48 × 7 × 10¹¹).

Numerator: 1500 × 27 × 10⁹ = 4.05 × 10¹³ N·mm³.

Denominator: 48 × 7 × 10¹¹ = 3.36 × 10¹³ N·mm².

δ_max = 4.05 × 10¹³ / 3.36 × 10¹³ = 1.205 mm (downward).

The support slope formula is θ = P·L² / (16·EI), giving the magnitude of the rotation at each support (equal by symmetry).

Substituting: θ = 1500 × (3000)² / (16 × 7 × 10¹¹).

Numerator: 1500 × 9 × 10⁶ = 1.35 × 10¹⁰ N·mm².

Denominator: 16 × 7 × 10¹¹ = 1.12 × 10¹² N·mm².

θ = 1.35 × 10¹⁰ / 1.12 × 10¹² = 0.01205 radians.

The left support slope is positive (beam rotates clockwise when viewed from the left, i.e., the beam slopes downward toward midspan).

The right support slope is equal in magnitude and opposite in sign by symmetry: θ_B = −0.01205 radians.

The maximum bending stress is σ_max = M_max · c / I, where c is the distance from the neutral axis to the extreme fiber; this requires the cross-section geometry (c value) which was not specified, so σ_max is left in terms of c: σ_max = 1125000 · c / 3500000 = 0.3214 · c MPa (with c in mm).

Shear force in the left half (0 < x < L/2) is V = +P/2 = +750 N (constant).

Shear force in the right half (L/2 < x < L) is V = −P/2 = −750 N (constant).

Summary of numerical results: midspan deflection δ_max = 1.205 mm downward, support slope magnitude θ = 0.01205 rad (≈ 0.690°), midspan moment M_max = 1125 N·m, support reactions R_A = R_B = 750 N upward.
