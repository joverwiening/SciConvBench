The bar is a straight, prismatic-axis member with a continuously varying circular (or arbitrary) cross-section.
Assume small deformations and linear-elastic, homogeneous, isotropic material behavior throughout.
Assume the bar is statically determinate: one end is fixed and the axial load P is applied at the free end (or equivalently, P is constant along the entire length).
Assume no body forces, no temperature change, and no lateral loads.
Assume plane sections remain plane (Euler-Bernoulli bar assumption for axial loading).

Geometry: bar length L = 800 mm, cross-sectional area A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L), with A0 = 250 mm², k = 0.6, x measured from the left (fixed) end.
At x = 0: A(0) = 250 mm².
At x = L: A(L) = 250·(1 + 0.6) = 400 mm².

Material: elastic modulus E = 200,000 MPa (200 GPa).
Applied load: axial force P = 12,000 N, constant along the length (no distributed axial body force).

Governing relation for axial stress at any cross-section x: σ(x) = P / A(x).
Substitute: σ(x) = 12000 / [250·(1 + 0.6·x/800)] MPa, with x in mm.
Simplify: σ(x) = 48 / (1 + 0.00075·x) MPa.

Governing relation for axial strain at any cross-section x: ε(x) = σ(x) / E = P / [E·A(x)].
Substitute: ε(x) = 12000 / [200000·250·(1 + 0.00075·x)] = 2.4×10⁻⁴ / (1 + 0.00075·x).

Governing relation for total axial deformation (elongation) δ: δ = ∫₀ᴸ [P / (E·A(x))] dx.
Substitute A(x): δ = ∫₀⁸⁰⁰ [12000 / (200000·250·(1 + 0.6·x/800))] dx.
Factor constants: δ = [12000 / (200000·250)] · ∫₀⁸⁰⁰ dx / (1 + 0.00075·x).
Constant factor = 12000 / 50,000,000 = 2.4×10⁻⁴ mm⁻¹ (dimensionless per mm before integration).

Evaluate the integral: ∫₀⁸⁰⁰ dx / (1 + 0.00075·x) = [1/0.00075]·ln(1 + 0.00075·x) evaluated from 0 to 800.
= (1/0.00075)·[ln(1 + 0.6) − ln(1)] = (1333.33)·ln(1.6).
ln(1.6) = 0.47000 (to 5 significant figures).
Integral = 1333.33 × 0.47000 = 626.67 mm.

Total axial deformation: δ = 2.4×10⁻⁴ × 626.67 = 0.15040 mm.
Round to four significant figures: δ ≈ 0.1504 mm (elongation).

Stress at the left end (x = 0): σ(0) = 12000 / 250 = 48.00 MPa.
Stress at the right end (x = 800 mm): σ(800) = 12000 / 400 = 30.00 MPa.
Maximum stress occurs at x = 0 (smallest area): σ_max = 48.00 MPa.
Minimum stress occurs at x = L (largest area): σ_min = 30.00 MPa.

Strain at the left end: ε(0) = 48.00 / 200000 = 2.400×10⁻⁴.
Strain at the right end: ε(800) = 30.00 / 200000 = 1.500×10⁻⁴.

Displacement field u(x) (displacement at position x relative to fixed left end): u(x) = ∫₀ˣ [P / (E·A(x'))] dx'.
u(x) = 2.4×10⁻⁴ · (1/0.00075)·ln(1 + 0.00075·x) = 0.32·ln(1 + 0.00075·x) mm, with x in mm.
At x = 800 mm: u(800) = 0.32·ln(1.6) = 0.32×0.47000 = 0.1504 mm, consistent with δ above.

Summary of results:
Axial stress distribution: σ(x) = 48 / (1 + 0.00075·x) MPa.
Axial strain distribution: ε(x) = 2.4×10⁻⁴ / (1 + 0.00075·x).
Displacement field: u(x) = 0.32·ln(1 + 0.00075·x) mm.
Total elongation: δ = 0.1504 mm.
Maximum axial stress: 48.00 MPa at x = 0.
Minimum axial stress: 30.00 MPa at x = 800 mm.
