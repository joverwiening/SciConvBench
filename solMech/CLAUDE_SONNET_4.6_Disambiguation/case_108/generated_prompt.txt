The beam is a simply supported beam with both ends pinned (one pin, one roller), span length L = 3500 mm.
The beam carries a uniformly distributed transverse load w = 0.5 N/mm over the full span.
The material is linear-elastic with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa (N/mm²).
The second moment of area of the cross-section is I = 1500000 mm⁴.
The flexural rigidity is EI = 200000 × 1500000 = 3.0 × 10¹¹ N·mm².
Assume small deformations so that the Euler-Bernoulli beam theory applies.
Assume the beam is prismatic (EI is constant along the span).
Assume the beam is homogeneous and isotropic.
Neglect self-weight of the beam (only the applied load w is considered).
Neglect shear deformation (Timoshenko effects are ignored).

Governing differential equation: EI · d⁴v/dx⁴ = w, where v(x) is the transverse deflection and x is measured from the left support.

Boundary conditions: v(0) = 0, v(L) = 0 (no deflection at supports), EI · v''(0) = 0, EI · v''(L) = 0 (no bending moment at simply supported ends).

Reaction forces: By symmetry, each support reaction is R = wL/2 = 0.5 × 3500 / 2 = 875 N.

Bending moment distribution: M(x) = R·x − w·x²/2 = 875x − 0.25x².
Maximum bending moment occurs at midspan x = L/2 = 1750 mm: M_max = wL²/8 = 0.5 × 3500² / 8 = 765625 N·mm.

Deflection equation: v(x) = (w / (24EI)) · (x⁴ − 2L·x³ + L³·x).
Substitute values: v(x) = (0.5 / (24 × 3.0 × 10¹¹)) · (x⁴ − 2 × 3500 · x³ + 3500³ · x).
The constant factor is 0.5 / (7.2 × 10¹²) = 6.944 × 10⁻¹⁴ mm⁻³.

Maximum deflection occurs at midspan x = L/2 = 1750 mm: v_max = 5wL⁴ / (384EI).
v_max = 5 × 0.5 × 3500⁴ / (384 × 3.0 × 10¹¹).
Numerator: 5 × 0.5 × 1.500625 × 10¹³ = 3.75156 × 10¹³.
Denominator: 384 × 3.0 × 10¹¹ = 1.152 × 10¹⁴.
v_max = 3.75156 × 10¹³ / 1.152 × 10¹⁴ = 0.3257 mm (downward).

Slope equation: dv/dx = (w / (24EI)) · (4x³ − 6L·x² + L³).
Substitute values: dv/dx = 6.944 × 10⁻¹⁴ · (4x³ − 7000x² + 3500³).

Maximum slope occurs at the supports (x = 0 and x = L) where the slope magnitude is greatest.
Slope at x = 0: θ(0) = wL³ / (24EI) = 0.5 × 3500³ / (24 × 3.0 × 10¹¹).
Numerator: 0.5 × 4.2875 × 10¹⁰ = 2.14375 × 10¹⁰.
Denominator: 7.2 × 10¹².
θ(0) = 2.14375 × 10¹⁰ / 7.2 × 10¹² = 2.977 × 10⁻³ radians (positive, beam slopes downward to the right at left support).
Slope at x = L: θ(L) = −wL³ / (24EI) = −2.977 × 10⁻³ radians (negative, beam slopes downward to the left at right support).
Slope at midspan x = L/2: dv/dx = 0 by symmetry.

Summary of results:
Flexural rigidity EI = 3.0 × 10¹¹ N·mm².
Support reactions R_A = R_B = 875 N.
Maximum bending moment M_max = 765625 N·mm at x = 1750 mm.
Maximum deflection v_max = 0.3257 mm downward at midspan (x = 1750 mm).
Maximum slope magnitude θ_max = 2.977 × 10⁻³ radians at both supports.
