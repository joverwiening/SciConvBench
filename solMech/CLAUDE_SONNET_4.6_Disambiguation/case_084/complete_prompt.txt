Use N–mm units.

A built-up beam is assembled by connecting a flange plate to a web using equally spaced fasteners along the interface. At a critical section, the internal shear force is V = 25000 N. The cross-section properties about the neutral axis are known: I = 18000000 mm^4. For the flange–web interface, the first moment of area is Q = 45000 mm^3.

(a) Compute the shear flow along the interface, q = VQ/I (units N/mm).
(b) If each fastener can carry an allowable shear force of 1800 N, determine the required maximum spacing s between fasteners along the beam using q·s ≤ F_allow.
