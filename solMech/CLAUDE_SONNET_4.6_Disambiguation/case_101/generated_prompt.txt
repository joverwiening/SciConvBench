Problem: Cantilever beam under uniformly distributed transverse load.

Units: N, mm, MPa (N/mm²) throughout.

Given values:
w = 1.5 N/mm (uniformly distributed transverse load, acting over the full length)
L = 1000 mm (beam length)
E = 200000 MPa
I = 2000000 mm⁴

Additional assumptions:
The beam is prismatic (uniform cross-section and material properties along its length).
The beam is homogeneous and isotropic.
Small-deformation, linear-elastic (Euler-Bernoulli beam) theory applies.
The load is transverse (perpendicular to the beam axis) and acts downward over the full span.
The beam is fixed (clamped) at one end and free at the other end.
Self-weight is neglected unless already included in w.
Shear deformations are neglected (Euler-Bernoulli assumption).

Coordinate system:
x is measured from the fixed support (x = 0 at fixed end, x = L at free end).

Governing differential equation:
E I d⁴v/dx⁴ = w(x)
where v(x) is the transverse deflection (positive downward).
For uniform load: w(x) = w = 1.5 N/mm (constant).

Boundary conditions:
At x = 0 (fixed end): v(0) = 0 (no deflection), dv/dx(0) = 0 (no slope).
At x = L (free end): d²v/dx²(L) = 0 (zero bending moment), d³v/dx³(L) = 0 (zero shear force).

Solution for deflection v(x):
Integrate E I d⁴v/dx⁴ = w four times and apply boundary conditions.
v(x) = (w / (24 E I)) (x⁴ - 4 L x³ + 6 L² x²)

Maximum deflection occurs at the free end (x = L):
v_max = v(L) = w L⁴ / (8 E I)

Numerical evaluation of maximum deflection:
v_max = (1.5 × 1000⁴) / (8 × 200000 × 2000000)
v_max = (1.5 × 10¹²) / (3.2 × 10¹²)
v_max = 0.46875 mm

Slope (rotation) along the beam:
dv/dx = (w / (6 E I)) (x³ - 3 L x² + 3 L² x) ... wait, re-derive from v(x):
dv/dx = (w / (24 E I)) (4x³ - 12 L x² + 12 L² x)
dv/dx = (w / (6 E I)) (x³ - 3 L x² + 3 L² x)

Maximum slope occurs at the free end (x = L):
θ_max = dv/dx(L) = w L³ / (6 E I)
θ_max = (1.5 × 1000³) / (6 × 200000 × 2000000)
θ_max = (1.5 × 10⁹) / (2.4 × 10¹²)
θ_max = 0.000625 rad

Bending moment distribution:
M(x) = E I d²v/dx² = (w / 2)(L - x)²
Maximum bending moment occurs at the fixed end (x = 0):
M_max = w L² / 2 = 1.5 × 1000² / 2 = 750000 N·mm

Shear force distribution:
V(x) = -E I d³v/dx³ = w(L - x)
Maximum shear force occurs at the fixed end (x = 0):
V_max = w L = 1.5 × 1000 = 1500 N

Summary of key results:
Maximum deflection at free end: v_max = 0.46875 mm (downward)
Maximum slope at free end: θ_max = 0.000625 rad
Maximum bending moment at fixed end: M_max = 750000 N·mm
Maximum shear force at fixed end: V_max = 1500 N
