The system is a stepped circular bar fixed at its left end with an axial tensile load applied at the free right end.

The bar has two segments in series, both made of structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.

Segment 1 has diameter d1 = 16 mm and length L1 = 400 mm.

Segment 2 has diameter d2 = 40 mm and length L2 = 400 mm.

The applied axial tensile load is P = 25000 N at the free right end.

Assume small deformations and linear-elastic material behavior throughout.

Assume the material is homogeneous and isotropic in each segment.

Assume the load is applied as a pure axial force with no bending or torsion.

Assume stress is uniformly distributed over each circular cross-section (Saint-Venant principle applies away from the step).

Neglect stress concentrations at the step between segments.

Neglect self-weight of the bar.

Since the bar is statically determinate (one fixed end, one free end, single axial load), the internal axial force N in each segment equals P = 25000 N by equilibrium.

Compute the cross-sectional area of each segment using A = π d² / 4.

A1 = π (16)² / 4 = 201.06 mm².

A2 = π (40)² / 4 = 1256.64 mm².

Compute the axial normal stress in each segment using σ = N / A.

σ1 = 25000 / 201.06 = 124.4 MPa (tensile).

σ2 = 25000 / 1256.64 = 19.9 MPa (tensile).

Compute the elongation of each segment using δ = N L / (A E).

δ1 = (25000 × 400) / (201.06 × 200000) = 0.2488 mm.

δ2 = (25000 × 400) / (1256.64 × 200000) = 0.03979 mm.

Compute the total elongation as the sum of the individual segment elongations.

δ_total = δ1 + δ2 = 0.2488 + 0.03979 = 0.2886 mm.
