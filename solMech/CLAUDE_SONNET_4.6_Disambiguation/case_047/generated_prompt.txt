The problem is a straight prismatic circular rod under uniaxial tensile axial load.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior throughout.
Assume the material is homogeneous and isotropic.
Assume the cross-section is perfectly circular and uniform along the entire length.
Assume the load P is applied concentrically along the centroidal axis, producing pure axial tension with no bending or torsion.
Assume static loading (no dynamic or fatigue effects).
Assume the rod is free to deform axially (no end constraint beyond the applied load).

Given values:
Applied axial load P = 15000 N.
Diameter d = 12 mm.
Length L = 800 mm.
Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Yield strength Sy = 350 MPa.

Step 1: Compute the cross-sectional area.
A = pi * d^2 / 4
A = pi * (12)^2 / 4
A = pi * 144 / 4
A = 36 * pi
A = 113.097 mm^2

Step 2: Compute the average normal stress.
sigma = P / A
sigma = 15000 / 113.097
sigma = 132.63 MPa (tensile)

Step 3: Compute the axial strain.
epsilon = sigma / E
epsilon = 132.63 / 200000
epsilon = 6.632 x 10^-4 (dimensionless)

Step 4: Compute the total elongation.
delta = epsilon * L
delta = 6.632 x 10^-4 * 800
delta = 0.5305 mm
Equivalently, delta = P * L / (A * E) = (15000 * 800) / (113.097 * 200000) = 12000000 / 22619400 = 0.5305 mm

Step 5: Evaluate the factor of safety with respect to yielding.
The failure criterion is the von Mises or Tresca criterion, which for uniaxial stress reduces to sigma >= Sy.
FS = Sy / sigma
FS = 350 / 132.63
FS = 2.64

Summary of results:
Cross-sectional area A = 113.10 mm^2.
Average normal stress sigma = 132.63 MPa (tensile).
Axial strain epsilon = 6.63 x 10^-4.
Total elongation delta = 0.531 mm.
Factor of safety against yielding FS = 2.64.

Additional assumptions stated:
The rod does not buckle; buckling is not a concern for a rod in tension.
Stress concentrations at the load application points are ignored (Saint-Venant's principle applies away from the ends).
Temperature effects and residual stresses are neglected.
The factor of safety is computed against first yielding using the yield strength Sy = 350 MPa.
