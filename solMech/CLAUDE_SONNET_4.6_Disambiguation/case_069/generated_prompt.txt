Units are N, mm, and MPa throughout.
The elastic modulus is E = 200000 MPa.
The second moment of area is I = 4000000 mm⁴.
The applied bending moment is M = 3000000 N·mm.
No cross-section geometry beyond I is required.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic (Euler-Bernoulli) beam theory.
Assume the beam is prismatic (constant E and I along its length).
Assume the bending moment M is constant at the cross-section of interest (pure bending condition).
Assume the neutral axis coincides with the centroidal axis of the cross-section.
Plane sections remain plane and perpendicular to the deformed neutral axis.

Governing relation for curvature:
The flexural rigidity is EI = E × I.
Compute EI = 200000 MPa × 4000000 mm⁴ = 8.0 × 10¹¹ N·mm².
The curvature κ is defined as κ = M / (EI).
Compute κ = 3000000 N·mm / (8.0 × 10¹¹ N·mm²) = 3.75 × 10⁻⁶ mm⁻¹.

Governing relation for radius of curvature:
The radius of curvature ρ is the reciprocal of curvature: ρ = 1 / κ = EI / M.
Compute ρ = 8.0 × 10¹¹ N·mm² / 3000000 N·mm = 266666.67 mm.
Convert to metres: ρ = 266.67 m.

Summary of results:
Flexural rigidity EI = 8.0 × 10¹¹ N·mm².
Curvature κ = 3.75 × 10⁻⁶ mm⁻¹.
Radius of curvature ρ = 266666.67 mm (approximately 266.67 m).
