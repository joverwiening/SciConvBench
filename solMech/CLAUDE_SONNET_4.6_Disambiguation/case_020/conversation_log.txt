Q: How many torque application points are there, and what are their locations along the shaft length (e.g., at the ends and/or at intermediate points)?
A: There are two torque application points: T1 = 200000 N·mm at x = 1200 mm (the free end), and T2 = -250000 N·mm at x = 800 mm (an intermediate point). The shaft is fixed at x = 0.
