The shaft is a hollow circular cross-section with outer diameter do = 50 mm (outer radius ro = 25 mm) and inner diameter di = 42 mm (inner radius ri = 21 mm).
The wall thickness is t = 4 mm and the shaft length is L = 1200 mm.
The applied torque is T = 450,000 N·mm, constant along the entire shaft length.
The boundary condition is one end fully fixed (zero rotation) and the torque applied at the free end.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic material behavior throughout.
Assume the material is homogeneous and isotropic.
Assume the cross-section remains plane and undistorted after twisting (Saint-Venant torsion theory applies).
Use steel material properties unless otherwise specified: shear modulus G = 80,000 MPa.

Geometric Section Properties:

Compute the polar moment of inertia as J = (π/32)(do^4 − di^4).
J = (π/32)(50^4 − 42^4) = (π/32)(6,250,000 − 3,111,696) = (π/32)(3,138,304).
J = 308,941 mm^4 (approximately).

Shear Stress Distribution:

The shear stress at any radial position r is given by τ(r) = T·r / J.
The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer surface: τ_max = T·ro / J = 450,000 × 25 / 308,941.
τ_max = 36.4 MPa (approximately).
The minimum shear stress occurs at the inner surface: τ_min = T·ri / J = 450,000 × 21 / 308,941.
τ_min = 30.6 MPa (approximately).
Shear stress varies linearly from τ_min at the inner radius to τ_max at the outer radius.

Angle of Twist:

The angle of twist over the full length is given by φ = T·L / (G·J).
φ = 450,000 × 1200 / (80,000 × 308,941).
φ = 540,000,000 / 24,715,280,000.
φ = 0.02185 radians (approximately).
Convert to degrees: φ = 0.02185 × (180/π) = 1.252 degrees (approximately).
The twist is uniform along the shaft since T is constant.

Principal Stresses on the Outer Surface:

On the outer surface under pure torsion, the stress state is pure shear: σ_x = 0, σ_y = 0, τ_xy = τ_max = 36.4 MPa.
The principal stresses are σ_1 = +τ_max = +36.4 MPa and σ_2 = −τ_max = −36.4 MPa.
The principal planes are oriented at 45 degrees to the shaft axis.
The maximum shear stress equals τ_max = 36.4 MPa, acting on planes parallel and perpendicular to the shaft axis.

Summary of Key Results:

Polar moment of inertia J = 308,941 mm^4.
Maximum shear stress τ_max = 36.4 MPa at the outer surface.
Minimum shear stress τ_min = 30.6 MPa at the inner surface.
Angle of twist φ = 0.02185 rad = 1.252 degrees over L = 1200 mm.
Principal stresses at outer surface: +36.4 MPa and −36.4 MPa at 45 degrees to the axis.
