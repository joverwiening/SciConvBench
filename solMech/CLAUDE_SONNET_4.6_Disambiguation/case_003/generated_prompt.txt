The bar is a statically determinate, axially loaded stepped circular bar fixed at the left end with a tensile load applied at the right end.

Assume small deformation and linear-elastic behavior throughout.
Assume the material is homogeneous and isotropic with E = 69000 MPa for both segments.
Assume the load is applied as a pure axial tensile force with no bending or torsion.
Assume cross-sections remain plane and stress is uniformly distributed over each cross-section (Saint-Venant assumption away from the step).
Assume the step transition is abrupt and stress concentration effects at the step are neglected unless noted.
Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

Geometry and material inputs are as follows.
Segment 1: diameter d1 = 30 mm, length L1 = 500 mm.
Segment 2: diameter d2 = 25 mm, length L2 = 600 mm.
Applied axial tensile load P = 20000 N at the right end.
Elastic modulus E = 69000 MPa for both segments.

Cross-sectional areas are computed as A = pi * d^2 / 4.
A1 = pi * (30)^2 / 4 = pi * 900 / 4 = 706.86 mm^2.
A2 = pi * (25)^2 / 4 = pi * 625 / 4 = 490.87 mm^2.

Because the bar is statically determinate and the only external axial force is P, the internal axial force in every cross-section equals P by equilibrium.
N1 = P = 20000 N (tension).
N2 = P = 20000 N (tension).

Axial stress in each segment is given by sigma = N / A.
sigma1 = 20000 / 706.86 = 28.29 MPa (tension).
sigma2 = 20000 / 490.87 = 40.74 MPa (tension).

Axial strain in each segment is given by epsilon = sigma / E.
epsilon1 = 28.29 / 69000 = 4.100e-4 (dimensionless).
epsilon2 = 40.74 / 69000 = 5.904e-4 (dimensionless).

Elongation of each segment is given by delta = N * L / (A * E).
delta1 = (20000 * 500) / (706.86 * 69000) = 10000000 / 48773340 = 0.2050 mm.
delta2 = (20000 * 600) / (490.87 * 69000) = 12000000 / 33870030 = 0.3543 mm.

Total elongation of the bar is the sum of segment elongations.
delta_total = delta1 + delta2 = 0.2050 + 0.3543 = 0.5593 mm.

Summary of results is as follows.
Stress in Segment 1: sigma1 = 28.29 MPa (tension).
Stress in Segment 2: sigma2 = 40.74 MPa (tension).
Elongation of Segment 1: delta1 = 0.2050 mm.
Elongation of Segment 2: delta2 = 0.3543 mm.
Total elongation: delta_total = 0.5593 mm.

The critical (maximum) stress occurs in Segment 2 at 40.74 MPa, which is well below the yield strength of 6061-T6 aluminum (approximately 276 MPa), confirming the linear-elastic assumption is valid.
