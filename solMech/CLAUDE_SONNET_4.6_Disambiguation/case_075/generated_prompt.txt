The structural member is a rectangular adhesive layer with plan area A = 4000 mm² and thickness t = 20 mm.
The applied lateral (shear) load is V = 8000 N.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior throughout.
Assume the shear stress is uniformly distributed over the area A (average shear stress assumption).
Assume the adhesive layer is bonded between two rigid substrates so that the shear deformation is the relative lateral displacement of the two bonded faces.
A shear modulus G for the adhesive material must be known or specified to compute deformation; treat G as a given material parameter in the governing relations.

Governing relation 1 — Average shear stress:
τ = V / A
τ = 8000 N / 4000 mm² = 2 N/mm² (2 MPa)

Governing relation 2 — Shear strain (engineering shear strain γ):
γ = τ / G
γ = 2 MPa / G

Governing relation 3 — Shear deformation (relative lateral displacement δ):
δ = γ · t
δ = (τ / G) · t
δ = (2 MPa / G) · 20 mm
δ = 40 / G mm, where G is in MPa

To obtain a numerical value for γ and δ, substitute the known shear modulus G of the adhesive material into the expressions above.
All stresses remain within the linear-elastic range, so no yielding or nonlinear correction is applied.
The out-of-plane (peel or normal) stress on the layer is assumed zero for this loading case.
