Solve a simply supported beam under a uniformly distributed transverse load using small-deformation, linear-elastic (Euler-Bernoulli) beam theory.

Geometry: The beam has a span length L = 3500 mm, simply supported at both ends (pin at x = 0, roller at x = L).

Loading: A uniformly distributed transverse load of intensity w = 0.5 N/mm acts over the entire span, directed downward.

Material and section properties: Elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa, second moment of area I = 3000000 mm⁴, giving a flexural rigidity EI = 6 × 10¹¹ N·mm².

Additional assumptions: The beam is prismatic (EI is constant along the span). Shear deformations are neglected (Euler-Bernoulli hypothesis). Self-weight is included in the given distributed load w or neglected separately. Axial forces are absent. Deflections are small compared to the span.

Governing differential equation: EI · d⁴v/dx⁴ = w, where v(x) is the transverse deflection (positive downward), and x is measured from the left support.

Boundary conditions: v(0) = 0, v(L) = 0 (zero deflection at both supports). d²v/dx²(0) = 0, d²v/dx²(L) = 0 (zero bending moment at both simply supported ends).

Reaction forces: By symmetry, each support reaction is R = wL/2 = 0.5 × 3500 / 2 = 875 N.

Bending moment distribution: M(x) = (wL/2)·x − (w/2)·x² = 875x − 0.25x², in N·mm, for 0 ≤ x ≤ L.

Maximum bending moment occurs at midspan x = L/2 = 1750 mm: M_max = wL²/8 = 0.5 × 3500² / 8 = 765625 N·mm.

Deflection equation: v(x) = (w / (24EI)) · (x⁴ − 2Lx³ + L³x), in mm, for 0 ≤ x ≤ L.

Substitute values: w/(24EI) = 0.5 / (24 × 6 × 10¹¹) = 3.4722 × 10⁻¹⁴ mm⁻³.

Maximum deflection occurs at midspan x = L/2: v_max = 5wL⁴ / (384EI).

Compute v_max: v_max = 5 × 0.5 × 3500⁴ / (384 × 6 × 10¹¹).

3500⁴ = 1.50063 × 10¹⁴ mm⁴.

Numerator: 5 × 0.5 × 1.50063 × 10¹⁴ = 3.75156 × 10¹⁴ N·mm³.

Denominator: 384 × 6 × 10¹¹ = 2.304 × 10¹⁴ N·mm².

v_max = 3.75156 × 10¹⁴ / 2.304 × 10¹⁴ = 1.629 mm (downward).

Slope equation: dv/dx = (w / (24EI)) · (4x³ − 6Lx² + L³), in mm/mm (radians).

Maximum slope occurs at the supports (x = 0 and x = L) by symmetry.

Slope at x = 0: θ(0) = dv/dx|_(x=0) = wL³ / (24EI).

Compute θ(0): wL³ = 0.5 × 3500³ = 0.5 × 4.2875 × 10¹⁰ = 2.14375 × 10¹⁰ N·mm².

θ(0) = 2.14375 × 10¹⁰ / (24 × 6 × 10¹¹) = 2.14375 × 10¹⁰ / 1.44 × 10¹³ = 1.4887 × 10⁻³ radians (positive, beam slopes downward to the right at left support).

Slope at x = L: θ(L) = −wL³ / (24EI) = −1.4887 × 10⁻³ radians (negative, beam slopes downward to the left at right support).

Slope at midspan x = L/2: dv/dx = 0 by symmetry (point of maximum deflection).

Summary of results: Maximum deflection v_max = 1.629 mm at x = 1750 mm. Maximum slope magnitude θ_max = 1.489 × 10⁻³ rad at both supports. Maximum bending moment M_max = 765625 N·mm at midspan.
