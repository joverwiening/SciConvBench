The shaft is a hollow circular cross-section with outer diameter do = 60 mm (outer radius ro = 30 mm) and inner diameter di = 48 mm (inner radius ri = 24 mm), giving a wall thickness of 6 mm.
The shaft length is L = 800 mm.
The applied loading is a pure torque T = 450,000 N·mm applied about the longitudinal axis.
There is no transverse shear force, bending moment, or axial force.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic, isotropic material behavior throughout.
Assume the shaft material is steel with shear modulus G = 80,000 MPa (80 GPa) unless a different material is specified; state this as an assumption.
Assume the cross-section is uniform along the entire length L.
Assume Saint-Venant's principle applies and end effects are neglected.
Assume the torque is applied and reacted at the two ends of the shaft.

Compute the polar second moment of area J using J = π(do⁴ − di⁴)/32.
Substitute values: J = π(60⁴ − 48⁴)/32 = π(12,960,000 − 5,308,416)/32 = π(7,651,584)/32.
This gives J = 751,770 mm⁴ (approximately).

Compute the maximum shear stress τ_max at the outer surface using τ_max = T·ro / J.
Substitute values: τ_max = 450,000 × 30 / 751,770.
This gives τ_max ≈ 17.96 MPa.

Compute the shear stress at the inner surface τ_min using τ_min = T·ri / J.
Substitute values: τ_min = 450,000 × 24 / 751,770.
This gives τ_min ≈ 14.37 MPa.

The shear stress distribution is linear through the wall thickness, varying from τ_min at r = ri to τ_max at r = ro.
The shear stress acts on cross-sectional planes in the circumferential direction and on longitudinal planes in the axial direction.

Compute the angle of twist φ over the full length using φ = TL / (GJ).
Substitute values: φ = 450,000 × 800 / (80,000 × 751,770).
This gives φ = 360,000,000 / 60,141,600,000 = 0.005986 radians.
Convert to degrees: φ ≈ 0.343°.

The principal stresses on the outer surface are σ₁ = +τ_max = +17.96 MPa (tension) and σ₂ = −τ_max = −17.96 MPa (compression), oriented at 45° to the shaft axis.
The maximum shear stress on the outer surface equals τ_max = 17.96 MPa.

No bending stress, axial stress, or transverse shear stress is present since the only load is pure torque.

Summary of results:
J ≈ 751,770 mm⁴
τ_max (outer surface) ≈ 17.96 MPa
τ_min (inner surface) ≈ 14.37 MPa
φ (angle of twist over 800 mm) ≈ 0.005986 rad ≈ 0.343°
Principal stresses at outer surface: +17.96 MPa and −17.96 MPa at ±45° to the axis.
