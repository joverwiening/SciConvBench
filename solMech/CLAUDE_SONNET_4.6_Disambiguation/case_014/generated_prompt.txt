Model a cantilever Euler-Bernoulli beam with a downward point load at the free end.

Beam properties and loading:
The beam is fixed (clamped) at one end and free at the other end.
Apply a single downward point load P = 800 N at the free end.
There is no applied moment anywhere on the beam.

Additional assumptions:
Assume small deformations throughout (deflections are much smaller than the beam length).
Assume linear-elastic, homogeneous, isotropic material behavior.
Assume the beam cross-section is uniform (constant) along its entire length.
Assume the beam is straight and prismatic before loading.
Assume shear deformations are negligible (Euler-Bernoulli hypothesis: plane sections remain plane and perpendicular to the neutral axis).
Assume no axial load is present.
Assume self-weight of the beam is neglected unless otherwise specified.
Let E be the Young's modulus of the material, I be the second moment of area of the cross-section about the bending axis, and L be the total length of the beam.

Coordinate system:
Place the origin at the fixed end.
Let x run along the beam axis from the fixed end (x = 0) toward the free end (x = L).
Let v(x) denote the transverse deflection, positive upward.

Boundary conditions at the fixed end (x = 0):
v(0) = 0 (no deflection).
dv/dx at x = 0 equals 0 (no slope, fully clamped).

Boundary conditions at the free end (x = L):
Bending moment M(L) = 0 (no applied moment).
Shear force V(L) = P (the applied load, directed downward).

Governing differential equation:
EI * d²v/dx² = M(x), where M(x) is the internal bending moment at position x.

Internal bending moment distribution:
Taking a free-body diagram from the free end, M(x) = -P * (L - x) for 0 ≤ x ≤ L.
The moment is zero at the free end and maximum in magnitude at the fixed end.
Maximum bending moment at the fixed end: M_max = P * L = 800 * L (N·m).

Integrate the governing equation twice to obtain the deflection:
First integration: EI * dv/dx = -P*(L*x - x²/2) + C1.
Apply dv/dx = 0 at x = 0, giving C1 = 0.
Second integration: EI * v(x) = -P*(L*x²/2 - x³/6) + C2.
Apply v(0) = 0, giving C2 = 0.

Deflection profile:
v(x) = -P/(6EI) * (3Lx² - x³).
The negative sign indicates downward deflection for a downward load.

Maximum deflection at the free end (x = L):
v(L) = -PL³/(3EI).
The magnitude of the maximum deflection is delta_max = PL³/(3EI).
Substituting P = 800 N: delta_max = 800 * L³ / (3EI).

Slope (rotation) profile:
dv/dx = -P/(2EI) * (2Lx - x²).
Maximum slope at the free end (x = L): theta_max = PL²/(2EI) = 800 * L² / (2EI).

Shear force distribution:
V(x) = P = 800 N, constant along the entire beam length.

Bending moment distribution:
M(x) = -P*(L - x) = -800*(L - x) N·m.
M is zero at x = L and equals -800L N·m at x = 0.

Reaction forces at the fixed support:
Vertical reaction force R = P = 800 N (upward).
Reaction moment M_reaction = P * L = 800 * L N·m (counterclockwise, opposing the load).
