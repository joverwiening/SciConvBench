The bar has two segments in series, fixed at the left end, with a single axial tensile load P applied at the right end.

Assume static equilibrium, so the internal axial force in each segment equals P = 35000 N.

Assume the material is homogeneous, isotropic, and linearly elastic with E = 200000 MPa throughout.

Assume small deformations so that geometry changes are negligible.

Assume the cross-sections remain plane and the stress is uniformly distributed over each circular cross-section (Saint-Venant assumption away from the step).

Compute the cross-sectional areas as follows.

A1 = π × (d1/2)² = π × (16/2)² = π × 64 = 201.06 mm²

A2 = π × (d2/2)² = π × (40/2)² = π × 400 = 1256.64 mm²

The governing relation for axial normal stress in each segment is σ = P / A.

Stress in Segment 1: σ1 = P / A1 = 35000 / 201.06 = 174.1 MPa (tensile)

Stress in Segment 2: σ2 = P / A2 = 35000 / 1256.64 = 27.85 MPa (tensile)

The governing relation for elongation of each segment is δ = P × L / (A × E).

Elongation of Segment 1: δ1 = (35000 × 400) / (201.06 × 200000) = 14000000 / 40212000 = 0.3481 mm

Elongation of Segment 2: δ2 = (35000 × 800) / (1256.64 × 200000) = 28000000 / 251328000 = 0.1114 mm

Total elongation of the bar: δ_total = δ1 + δ2 = 0.3481 + 0.1114 = 0.4595 mm

Summary of results:

Segment 1 axial stress = 174.1 MPa (tensile)
Segment 2 axial stress = 27.85 MPa (tensile)
Elongation of Segment 1 = 0.3481 mm
Elongation of Segment 2 = 0.1114 mm
Total elongation = 0.4595 mm
