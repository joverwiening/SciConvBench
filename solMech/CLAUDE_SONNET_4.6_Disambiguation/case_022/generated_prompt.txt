The shaft is a stepped solid circular shaft fixed at end A, with a torque applied at end C. There is no transverse shear force in this problem; the loading is pure torsion only.

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behaviour throughout.
Assume the material is homogeneous and isotropic with shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa in both segments.
Assume the cross-sections remain plane and circular (Saint-Venant torsion theory applies).
Assume the torque is transmitted entirely through both segments from C back to the fixed wall at A.

Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

Geometry:
Segment AB: length L_AB = 300 mm, diameter d_AB = 25 mm.
Segment BC: length L_BC = 700 mm, diameter d_BC = 30 mm.

Compute the polar second moment of area for each segment using J = π d⁴ / 32.
J_AB = π (25)⁴ / 32 = π × 390625 / 32 = 38349.5 mm⁴.
J_BC = π (30)⁴ / 32 = π × 810000 / 32 = 79521.6 mm⁴.

Internal torque:
Because the shaft is statically determinate (fixed at A, free at C with applied torque T), the internal torque in every cross-section equals the applied torque.
T_internal = T = 450000 N·mm in both segments AB and BC.

Shear stress:
The shear stress at the outer surface of each segment is given by τ = T c / J, where c = d / 2 is the outer radius.
For segment AB: c_AB = 12.5 mm, τ_AB = 450000 × 12.5 / 38349.5 = 146.61 MPa.
For segment BC: c_BC = 15.0 mm, τ_BC = 450000 × 15.0 / 79521.6 = 84.88 MPa.

The shear stress distribution within each cross-section is linear from zero at the centroidal axis to the maximum value at the outer surface, given by τ(r) = T r / J.

Angle of twist per segment:
The angle of twist of one end of a segment relative to the other is given by φ = T L / (G J).
φ_AB = 450000 × 300 / (77519.4 × 38349.5) = 135000000 / 2972761233 = 0.04541 rad.
φ_BC = 450000 × 700 / (77519.4 × 79521.6) = 315000000 / 6166062384 = 0.05109 rad.

Total twist at C relative to the fixed end A:
φ_total = φ_AB + φ_BC = 0.04541 + 0.05109 = 0.09650 rad.
Converting to degrees: 0.09650 × (180 / π) = 5.529 degrees.

Summary of results:
Maximum shear stress in segment AB (at outer surface): τ_AB = 146.61 MPa.
Maximum shear stress in segment BC (at outer surface): τ_BC = 84.88 MPa.
The critical (highest-stressed) segment is AB.
Angle of twist of segment AB: φ_AB = 0.04541 rad (2.601 degrees).
Angle of twist of segment BC: φ_BC = 0.05109 rad (2.927 degrees).
Total angle of twist at C relative to A: φ_total = 0.09650 rad (5.529 degrees).
