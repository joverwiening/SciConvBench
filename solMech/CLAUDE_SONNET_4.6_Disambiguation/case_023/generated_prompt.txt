Problem: Cantilever beam under uniformly distributed transverse load.

Assumptions:
Small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior applies throughout.
The beam is prismatic with constant cross-section along its entire length.
The beam is homogeneous and isotropic.
The fixed support is perfectly rigid (zero displacement and zero rotation at the fixed end).
The free end has no applied point load or moment.
Self-weight is neglected unless the distributed load already accounts for it.
Shear deformation is neglected (Euler-Bernoulli beam theory applies).
The distributed load w is applied transversely (perpendicular to the beam axis) and acts over the full length L.
Material is AISI 1020 steel with E = 200000 MPa.

Given values:
Uniformly distributed load: w = 1.0 N/mm
Beam length: L = 2500 mm
Young's modulus: E = 200000 MPa (N/mm²)
Second moment of area: I = 1,000,000 mm⁴

Governing differential equation (Euler-Bernoulli):
EI * d⁴v/dx⁴ = w(x)
where v(x) is the transverse deflection and x is measured from the fixed end.

Boundary conditions:
At x = 0 (fixed end): v(0) = 0 and dv/dx(0) = 0.
At x = L (free end): d²v/dx²(L) = 0 (zero moment) and d³v/dx³(L) = 0 (zero shear).

Bending moment distribution:
M(x) = (w/2)(L - x)²
Maximum bending moment occurs at the fixed end (x = 0): M_max = wL²/2.
M_max = (1.0 × 2500²) / 2 = 3,125,000 N·mm = 3125 N·m.

Shear force distribution:
V(x) = w(L - x)
Maximum shear force occurs at the fixed end (x = 0): V_max = wL.
V_max = 1.0 × 2500 = 2500 N.

Deflection equation (measured from fixed end):
v(x) = (w / (24EI)) * (x⁴ - 4Lx³ + 6L²x²)

Maximum deflection occurs at the free end (x = L):
v_max = wL⁴ / (8EI)
v_max = (1.0 × 2500⁴) / (8 × 200000 × 1000000)
v_max = (3.90625 × 10¹³) / (1.6 × 10¹²)
v_max = 24.414 mm (downward at the free end).

Slope equation:
dv/dx(x) = (w / (6EI)) * (x³ - 3Lx² + 3L²x)

Maximum slope occurs at the free end (x = L):
θ_max = wL³ / (6EI)
θ_max = (1.0 × 2500³) / (6 × 200000 × 1000000)
θ_max = (1.5625 × 10¹⁰) / (1.2 × 10¹²)
θ_max = 0.01302 radians = 0.746 degrees.

Summary of key results:
Maximum deflection at free end: v_max = 24.41 mm.
Maximum slope at free end: θ_max = 0.01302 rad (0.746°).
Maximum bending moment at fixed end: M_max = 3,125,000 N·mm.
Maximum shear force at fixed end: V_max = 2500 N.
