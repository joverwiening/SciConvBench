The beam is an Euler-Bernoulli beam, simply supported at both ends, with span L = 3000 mm.
The beam carries a uniformly distributed load w = 0.3 N/mm acting downward over the full span.
The beam also carries a point load P = 1200 N acting downward at midspan (x = L/2 = 1500 mm).
Assume small deformations and linear-elastic material behaviour throughout.
Assume the beam is prismatic, meaning the cross-sectional flexural rigidity EI is constant along the entire span.
Assume the beam is straight and initially undeformed before loading.
Assume self-weight is neglected unless separately specified.
The x-axis runs along the beam axis from the left support (x = 0) to the right support (x = L).
Transverse deflection v(x) is taken as positive downward.

Governing differential equation for Euler-Bernoulli beam:
EI * d4v/dx4 = q(x), where q(x) is the distributed load intensity (positive downward).

Use the principle of superposition: the total deflection is the sum of the deflection due to the UDL alone and the deflection due to the point load alone.

Case 1 — Simply supported beam under UDL w over full span L:
Reaction at each support: R = wL/2.
Deflection at position x: v1(x) = (w / (24EI)) * (L^3 * x - 2L * x^3 + x^4).
Maximum deflection occurs at midspan (x = L/2): v1_max = 5wL^4 / (384EI).
Maximum bending moment occurs at midspan: M1_max = wL^2 / 8.

Case 2 — Simply supported beam under point load P at midspan (x = L/2):
Reaction at each support: R = P/2.
Deflection for 0 <= x <= L/2: v2(x) = (P / (48EI)) * (3L^2 * x - 4x^3).
Deflection for L/2 <= x <= L: use symmetry, v2(x) = v2(L - x).
Maximum deflection occurs at midspan: v2_max = PL^3 / (48EI).
Maximum bending moment occurs at midspan: M2_max = PL / 4.

Superposition — Total response:
Total deflection: v(x) = v1(x) + v2(x).
Total bending moment: M(x) = M1(x) + M2(x).
Total maximum deflection at midspan: v_max = 5wL^4/(384EI) + PL^3/(48EI).
Total maximum bending moment at midspan: M_max = wL^2/8 + PL/4.
