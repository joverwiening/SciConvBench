The beam is an Euler-Bernoulli beam under small-deformation, linear-elastic conditions.

The beam is simply supported at both ends with span L = 2500 mm.

A single point load P = 2500 N acts vertically downward at midspan (x = L/2).

Material and section properties are E = 200000 MPa, I = 1200000 mm⁴, giving EI = 2.4 × 10¹¹ N·mm².

Assume the beam is prismatic (EI is constant along the full span).

Assume self-weight and any distributed load are neglected; only the point load P is applied.

Assume no axial force, no torsion, and no out-of-plane loading.

Assume shear deformation is neglected (Euler-Bernoulli hypothesis: plane sections remain plane and perpendicular to the neutral axis).

The governing differential equation is EI d⁴v/dx⁴ = q(x), where v(x) is the transverse deflection and q(x) is the distributed load intensity (zero here except at the point load location).

Boundary conditions are v(0) = 0, v(L) = 0 (no deflection at supports), and EI v''(0) = 0, EI v''(L) = 0 (no moment at pin and roller supports).

By symmetry, each support reaction is R = P/2 = 1250 N upward.

The standard closed-form result for midspan deflection of a simply supported beam with a central point load is delta_max = PL³ / (48 EI).

Substitute values: delta_max = (2500 × 2500³) / (48 × 2.4 × 10¹¹).

Compute numerator: 2500 × 1.5625 × 10¹⁰ = 3.90625 × 10¹³ N·mm³.

Compute denominator: 48 × 2.4 × 10¹¹ = 1.152 × 10¹³ N·mm².

Midspan deflection delta_max = 3.90625 × 10¹³ / 1.152 × 10¹³ = 3.39 mm (downward).

The standard closed-form result for the slope at each support is theta = PL² / (16 EI).

Substitute values: theta = (2500 × 2500²) / (16 × 2.4 × 10¹¹).

Compute numerator: 2500 × 6.25 × 10⁶ = 1.5625 × 10¹⁰ N·mm².

Compute denominator: 16 × 2.4 × 10¹¹ = 3.84 × 10¹² N·mm².

Support slope theta = 1.5625 × 10¹⁰ / 3.84 × 10¹² = 0.004069 radians.

Convert to degrees: 0.004069 × (180/π) = 0.233 degrees.

The slope is equal in magnitude at both supports; the left support slopes downward to the right and the right support slopes downward to the left, consistent with symmetry.

Summary of results: midspan deflection = 3.39 mm downward, support slope = 0.004069 rad (0.233°) at each end.
