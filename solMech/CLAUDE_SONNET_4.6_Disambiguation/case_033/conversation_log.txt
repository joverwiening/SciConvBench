Q: What is the magnitude and unit of the point load, the beam span length, and the cross-sectional flexural rigidity (EI) — or should I derive the symbolic closed-form expressions only, without substituting numerical values?
A: Use these numerical values:

- Span: L = 2500 mm
- Point load: P = 2500 N (downward at midspan)
- E = 200000 MPa, I = 1200000 mm⁴, so EI = 2.4 × 10¹¹ N·mm²

Substitute numerical values to get final answers, not just symbolic expressions.
