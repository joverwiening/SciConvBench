The shaft is a solid circular cross-section with diameter d = 25 mm, giving radius r = 12.5 mm.
The length is L = 1000 mm.
The shear modulus is G = 76923.1 MPa.
The applied torque is T = 350000 N·mm, applied as a single concentrated torque at the free end.
The far end is fully fixed against rotation (cantilever torsion configuration).
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior throughout.
Assume the shaft is homogeneous, isotropic, and prismatic (uniform cross-section along its entire length).
Assume Saint-Venant torsion theory applies: plane sections remain plane, warping is unrestrained or neglected, and shear stress is the only significant stress component.
Assume the torque is constant along the full length (no distributed torque; internal torque equals T everywhere).

Compute the polar second moment of area J.
J = π d⁴ / 32 = π (25)⁴ / 32.
J = π (390625) / 32 = 38349.5 mm⁴.

State the complementary strain energy U for a shaft under torsion.
U = ∫₀ᴸ [T(x)²] / (2GJ) dx.
Since T(x) = T = constant along the shaft, this simplifies to U = T² L / (2GJ).

Apply Castigliano's second theorem to find the angle of twist φ at the point and in the direction of the applied torque.
φ = ∂U / ∂T = ∂/∂T [T² L / (2GJ)] = T L / (GJ).

Substitute numerical values.
GJ = 76923.1 × 38349.5 = 2949.96 × 10⁶ N·mm².
φ = (350000 × 1000) / (2949.96 × 10⁶).
φ = 3.5 × 10⁸ / 2.94996 × 10⁹.
φ = 0.11864 radians.
φ = 6.797 degrees.

Compute the maximum shear stress using the torsion formula for verification.
τ_max = T r / J = 350000 × 12.5 / 38349.5.
τ_max = 4375000 / 38349.5 = 114.08 MPa.

Compute the strain energy stored for verification.
U = T² L / (2GJ) = (350000)² × 1000 / (2 × 2949.96 × 10⁶).
U = 1.225 × 10¹¹ / 5.89992 × 10⁹.
U = 20.763 N·mm = 20.763 mJ.

Verify Castigliano result is consistent with direct formula: φ = TL/(GJ) is the standard result, and Castigliano's theorem recovers it exactly because U is quadratic in T, confirming the method is self-consistent.

Summary of results:
Polar moment of area J = 38349.5 mm⁴.
Torsional rigidity GJ = 2.950 × 10⁹ N·mm².
Angle of twist φ = TL/(GJ) = 0.1186 rad = 6.80 degrees.
Maximum shear stress τ_max = T r / J = 114.1 MPa.
Strain energy U = T²L/(2GJ) = 20.76 N·mm.
