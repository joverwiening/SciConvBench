The rod is made of AISI steel; use standard AISI 1045 (or generic AISI carbon steel) material properties: Young's modulus E = 200 GPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.29.
The cross-section is circular with initial diameter d0 = 20 mm.
The axial tensile load is P = 35000 N.
There is no lateral/transverse force; the only loading is the axial tension P.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior throughout.
Assume the rod is homogeneous, isotropic, and prismatic.
Assume uniaxial stress state (no bending, no shear, no lateral force).

Compute the cross-sectional area: A = π × (d0/2)² = π × (0.010)² m².
A = π × 1×10⁻⁴ = 3.14159×10⁻⁴ m².

Compute the axial (longitudinal) stress: σ_x = P / A.
σ_x = 35000 / 3.14159×10⁻⁴ = 111.408 MPa (tensile).

Under uniaxial stress, the transverse stresses are zero: σ_y = σ_z = 0.

State the generalized Hooke's law for strains under uniaxial stress.
Axial strain: ε_x = σ_x / E.
Lateral strain (Poisson effect): ε_y = ε_z = −ν × (σ_x / E) = −ν × ε_x.

Compute the axial strain: ε_x = 111.408×10⁶ / 200×10⁹ = 5.5704×10⁻⁴ (dimensionless, elongation).

Compute the lateral strain: ε_y = ε_z = −0.29 × 5.5704×10⁻⁴ = −1.6154×10⁻⁴ (dimensionless, contraction).

Compute the change in diameter: Δd = ε_y × d0 = −1.6154×10⁻⁴ × 20 mm = −3.231×10⁻³ mm.
The diameter decreases by approximately 3.23 μm under the applied tensile load.

Compute the final diameter: d_final = d0 + Δd = 20 − 0.003231 = 19.99677 mm.

The volumetric strain (dilatation) under uniaxial stress is: e = ε_x + ε_y + ε_z = ε_x (1 − 2ν).
e = 5.5704×10⁻⁴ × (1 − 0.58) = 5.5704×10⁻⁴ × 0.42 = 2.3396×10⁻⁴.
The volume increases slightly because axial elongation outweighs lateral contraction.

Summary of governing relations:
σ_x = P / A (axial stress from applied load).
ε_x = σ_x / E (axial strain, Hooke's law).
ε_lateral = −ν ε_x (Poisson-induced lateral strain, contraction under tension).
Δd = ε_lateral × d0 (change in diameter).
e = ε_x (1 − 2ν) (volumetric strain under uniaxial stress).
