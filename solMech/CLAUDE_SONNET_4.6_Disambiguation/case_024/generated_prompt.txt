Problem: Cantilever beam under uniformly distributed transverse load.

Units: N, mm, MPa (N/mm²) throughout.

Given quantities:
w = 1.5 N/mm (uniformly distributed transverse load, acting over the full length)
L = 1500 mm (beam length)
E = 200000 MPa
I = 1500000 mm⁴

Assumptions:
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic (Euler-Bernoulli) beam theory.
Assume the beam is prismatic (constant cross-section) along its entire length.
Assume the beam is fixed (fully clamped) at one end and free at the other end.
Assume the distributed load acts transversely (perpendicular to the beam axis) over the full span, directed downward.
Assume self-weight is included in w or neglected separately; w = 1.5 N/mm is the total distributed load.
Assume no axial force is present.
Assume shear deformations are negligible (Euler-Bernoulli, not Timoshenko).

Coordinate system:
Let x measure distance from the fixed support (x = 0 at fixed end, x = L at free end).

Governing differential equation:
E I v''''(x) = w
where v(x) is the transverse deflection (positive downward).

Boundary conditions:
v(0) = 0 (no deflection at fixed end)
v'(0) = 0 (no slope at fixed end)
v''(L) = 0 (zero bending moment at free end)
v'''(L) = 0 (zero shear force at free end)

Solution for deflection v(x):
v(x) = (w / (24 E I)) * (x⁴ - 4 L x³ + 6 L² x²)

Maximum deflection occurs at the free end (x = L):
v_max = v(L) = w L⁴ / (8 E I)

Numerical evaluation of maximum deflection:
v_max = (1.5 × 1500⁴) / (8 × 200000 × 1500000)
v_max = (1.5 × 5.0625 × 10¹²) / (2.4 × 10¹²)
v_max = 7.59375 × 10¹² / 2.4 × 10¹²
v_max = 3.164 mm

Bending moment distribution:
M(x) = (w / 2) × (L - x)²

Maximum bending moment occurs at the fixed end (x = 0):
M_max = w L² / 2 = 1.5 × 1500² / 2 = 1.6875 × 10⁶ N·mm

Shear force distribution:
V(x) = w × (L - x)

Maximum shear force occurs at the fixed end (x = 0):
V_max = w L = 1.5 × 1500 = 2250 N

Slope distribution:
v'(x) = (w / (6 E I)) × (x³ - 3 L x² + 3 L² x) ... wait, derived from integration:
v'(x) = (w / (6 E I)) × (x³ - 3 L x² + 3 L² x)

Maximum slope occurs at the free end (x = L):
θ_max = v'(L) = w L³ / (6 E I) = (1.5 × 1500³) / (6 × 200000 × 1500000)
θ_max = (1.5 × 3.375 × 10⁹) / (1.8 × 10¹²)
θ_max = 5.0625 × 10⁹ / 1.8 × 10¹²
θ_max = 2.8125 × 10⁻³ radians

Summary of results:
Maximum deflection at free end: v_max = 3.164 mm
Maximum slope at free end: θ_max = 2.8125 × 10⁻³ rad
Maximum bending moment at fixed end: M_max = 1.6875 × 10⁶ N·mm
Maximum shear force at fixed end: V_max = 2250 N
