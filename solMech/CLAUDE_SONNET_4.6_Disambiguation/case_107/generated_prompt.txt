The beam is a simply supported beam of span length L = 2500 mm.
The beam is subjected to a uniformly distributed transverse load of intensity w = 0.7 N/mm acting over the full span.
The material is linear-elastic with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa (N/mm²).
The second moment of area of the cross-section is I = 2000000 mm⁴.
The flexural rigidity is EI = 200000 × 2000000 = 4.0 × 10¹¹ N·mm².
Assume small deformations throughout (Euler-Bernoulli beam theory applies).
Assume the beam is prismatic (EI is constant along the span).
Assume the distributed load acts transversely (perpendicular to the beam axis) and is uniform along the entire span.
Neglect self-weight unless it is already included in w.
Neglect shear deformation (Timoshenko effects are ignored).
The x-coordinate is measured from the left support, ranging from 0 to L.

Governing differential relation: EI · d⁴v/dx⁴ = w, where v(x) is the transverse deflection.
Boundary conditions: v(0) = 0, v(L) = 0 (zero deflection at both supports).
Boundary conditions: v''(0) = 0, v''(L) = 0 (zero bending moment at both simply supported ends).

Reaction forces: By symmetry, each support reaction is R = wL/2 = 0.7 × 2500 / 2 = 875 N.

Bending moment distribution: M(x) = (wL/2)·x − (w/2)·x² = (wx/2)·(L − x).
Maximum bending moment occurs at midspan (x = L/2): M_max = wL²/8 = 0.7 × 2500² / 8 = 546875 N·mm.

Deflection equation: v(x) = (w / (24·EI)) · x · (L³ − 2L·x² + x³).
Maximum deflection occurs at midspan (x = L/2): v_max = 5wL⁴ / (384·EI).
Compute v_max = 5 × 0.7 × 2500⁴ / (384 × 4.0 × 10¹¹).
2500⁴ = 3.90625 × 10¹³ mm⁴.
Numerator = 5 × 0.7 × 3.90625 × 10¹³ = 1.3671875 × 10¹⁴ N·mm³.
Denominator = 384 × 4.0 × 10¹¹ = 1.536 × 10¹⁴ N·mm².
v_max = 1.3671875 × 10¹⁴ / 1.536 × 10¹⁴ = 0.8901 mm (downward).

Slope equation: dv/dx = θ(x) = (w / (24·EI)) · (L³ − 6L·x² + 4x³).
Maximum slope occurs at the supports (x = 0 and x = L).
Slope at left support (x = 0): θ(0) = wL³ / (24·EI).
Compute θ(0) = 0.7 × 2500³ / (24 × 4.0 × 10¹¹).
2500³ = 1.5625 × 10¹⁰ mm³.
Numerator = 0.7 × 1.5625 × 10¹⁰ = 1.09375 × 10¹⁰ N·mm².
Denominator = 24 × 4.0 × 10¹¹ = 9.6 × 10¹² N·mm².
θ(0) = 1.09375 × 10¹⁰ / 9.6 × 10¹² = 1.1393 × 10⁻³ radians (clockwise).
Slope at right support (x = L): θ(L) = −wL³ / (24·EI) = −1.1393 × 10⁻³ radians (counterclockwise), equal in magnitude by symmetry.
Slope at midspan (x = L/2): θ(L/2) = 0 by symmetry.

Summary of results:
Flexural rigidity EI = 4.0 × 10¹¹ N·mm².
Maximum bending moment M_max = 546875 N·mm at x = 1250 mm.
Maximum deflection v_max = 0.8901 mm at midspan (x = 1250 mm), acting downward.
Maximum slope magnitude θ_max = 1.1393 × 10⁻³ radians at both supports.
