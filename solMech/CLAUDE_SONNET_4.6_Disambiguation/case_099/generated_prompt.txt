Problem: Cantilever beam under uniformly distributed transverse load.

Assumptions and Conditions:
Small-deformation, linear-elastic behaviour applies throughout.
The beam is prismatic with constant cross-section along its entire length.
The beam is fixed (clamped) at one end (x = 0) and free at the other end (x = L).
The uniformly distributed load w acts transversely (perpendicular to the beam axis) over the full length L, directed downward.
Shear deformation is neglected (Euler-Bernoulli beam theory).
Self-weight is neglected unless it is the stated load.
All units are N, mm, and MPa (N/mm²) throughout.

Given Values:
w = 1.0 N/mm (uniformly distributed transverse load)
L = 2000 mm (beam length)
I = 1,500,000 mm⁴ (second moment of area of cross-section)
E = 200,000 MPa = 200,000 N/mm² (Young's modulus)
Flexural rigidity EI = 200,000 × 1,500,000 = 3.0 × 10¹¹ N·mm²

Governing Differential Equation:
The Euler-Bernoulli beam equation is EI · d⁴v/dx⁴ = w(x), where v(x) is the transverse deflection.
For uniform load w(x) = w = constant, the equation becomes EI · d⁴v/dx⁴ = w.

Boundary Conditions:
At the fixed end (x = 0): v(0) = 0 (no deflection) and v'(0) = 0 (no slope/rotation).
At the free end (x = L): v''(L) = 0 (zero bending moment) and v'''(L) = 0 (zero shear force).

Reaction Forces at the Fixed Support:
Vertical reaction force R = w · L = 1.0 × 2000 = 2000 N (upward).
Reaction moment M_fixed = w · L² / 2 = 1.0 × 2000² / 2 = 2,000,000 N·mm (hogging).

Bending Moment Distribution M(x):
M(x) = w/2 · (L - x)² where x is measured from the fixed end.
Maximum bending moment occurs at x = 0: M_max = w · L² / 2 = 2,000,000 N·mm.
Bending moment at the free end x = L: M(L) = 0.

Shear Force Distribution V(x):
V(x) = w · (L - x).
Maximum shear force occurs at x = 0: V_max = w · L = 2000 N.
Shear force at the free end x = L: V(L) = 0.

Deflection Formula v(x):
Integrating the governing equation four times and applying boundary conditions gives:
v(x) = (w / (24 · EI)) · x² · (x² - 4Lx + 6L²).

Maximum Deflection at the Free End (x = L):
v_max = v(L) = w · L⁴ / (8 · EI).
v_max = (1.0 × 2000⁴) / (8 × 3.0 × 10¹¹).
v_max = (1.6 × 10¹³) / (2.4 × 10¹²).
v_max = 6.667 mm (downward).

Slope (Rotation) at the Free End (x = L):
θ(x) = dv/dx = (w / (6 · EI)) · x · (x² - 3Lx + 3L²).
θ_max = θ(L) = w · L³ / (6 · EI).
θ_max = (1.0 × 2000³) / (6 × 3.0 × 10¹¹).
θ_max = (8.0 × 10⁹) / (1.8 × 10¹²).
θ_max = 0.004444 radians (clockwise).

Summary of Key Results:
Maximum deflection at free end: v_max = 6.667 mm.
Maximum slope at free end: θ_max = 0.004444 rad.
Maximum bending moment at fixed end: M_max = 2,000,000 N·mm.
Maximum shear force at fixed end: V_max = 2000 N.
Flexural rigidity used: EI = 3.0 × 10¹¹ N·mm².
