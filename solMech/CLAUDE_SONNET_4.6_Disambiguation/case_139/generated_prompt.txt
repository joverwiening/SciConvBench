The cross section is rectangular with width b = 30 mm and height h = 100 mm.

The cross-sectional area is A = b × h = 30 mm × 100 mm = 3000 mm².

The internal resultants acting on the cross section are: axial normal force P = 50000 N (tension) and transverse shear force V = 5000 N acting parallel to the width b.

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior throughout.

Assume the bar is prismatic (constant cross section along its length).

Assume the material is homogeneous and isotropic.

Assume the cross section is far enough from any load application point or geometric discontinuity that Saint-Venant's principle applies, so stress distributions are those given by elementary strength-of-materials theory.

The average normal stress on the cross section due to the axial force P is defined as σ_avg = P / A.

Substituting: σ_avg = 50000 N / 3000 mm² = 16.67 MPa (tensile).

This average normal stress is uniform over the entire cross section under the assumption of pure axial loading with no bending moment present.

The average shear stress on the cross section due to the transverse shear force V is defined as τ_avg = V / A.

Substituting: τ_avg = 5000 N / 3000 mm² = 1.67 MPa.

The direction of τ_avg is parallel to the width b, consistent with the direction of V.

Note that the average shear stress τ_avg = V/A is a uniform approximation over the entire cross section; the actual shear stress distribution for a rectangular section is parabolic, with a maximum at the neutral axis equal to τ_max = (3/2) × (V/A) = 1.5 × 1.67 MPa = 2.50 MPa, and zero at the top and bottom edges.

The governing relations used are:

Average normal stress: σ_avg = P / A

Average shear stress: τ_avg = V / A

Maximum shear stress for a rectangular section: τ_max = (3V) / (2A)

No bending moment resultant is specified at the section, so no bending normal stress is superimposed.

The total normal stress at any point on the cross section, under the given resultants only, equals σ_avg = 16.67 MPa (tension), uniform across the section.

Summary of results:

Cross-sectional area A = 3000 mm²

Average normal stress σ_avg = 16.67 MPa (tensile)

Average shear stress τ_avg = 1.67 MPa (parallel to b)

Maximum shear stress at neutral axis τ_max = 2.50 MPa (for reference, based on parabolic distribution)
