The bar is a straight, prismatic-axis member with a linearly varying cross-sectional area.
Assume small deformations and linear-elastic, homogeneous, isotropic material behavior.
Assume the axial load P is applied as a single concentrated force at one end, with the opposite end fixed (cantilever configuration), unless a different boundary condition changes the reaction — the internal axial force is therefore constant and equal to P throughout the bar.
Assume the bar has no body forces (no self-weight or distributed axial load).
Assume plane sections remain plane and stress is uniform over each cross-section (Saint-Venant assumption valid away from load application points).

Geometry and given data:
Length L = 500 mm.
Cross-sectional area A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L), where A0 = 200 mm², k = 1.0, x measured from the left end.
At x = 0: A(0) = 200 mm².
At x = L: A(L) = 400 mm².
Applied axial load P = 12000 N (tensile positive).
Elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.

Governing relation for axial stress at position x:
σ(x) = P / A(x) = P / [A0·(1 + k·x/L)].

Governing relation for axial strain at position x:
ε(x) = σ(x) / E = P / [E·A0·(1 + k·x/L)].

Governing relation for total axial deformation (integrate strain over length):
δ = ∫₀ᴸ ε(x) dx = ∫₀ᴸ P / [E·A0·(1 + k·x/L)] dx.

Evaluate the integral by substitution u = 1 + k·x/L, du = k/(L) dx, so dx = L/(k) du:
δ = (P·L) / (E·A0·k) · ∫₁^(1+k) (1/u) du = (P·L) / (E·A0·k) · ln(1 + k).

Substitute numerical values:
δ = (12000 × 500) / (200000 × 200 × 1.0) · ln(1 + 1.0).
δ = (6000000 / 40000000) · ln(2).
δ = 0.15 · 0.693147.
δ = 0.10397 mm.

Stress at x = 0 (minimum area, maximum stress):
σ(0) = 12000 / 200 = 60 MPa.

Stress at x = L (maximum area, minimum stress):
σ(L) = 12000 / 400 = 30 MPa.

Stress at any position x:
σ(x) = 12000 / [200·(1 + x/500)] MPa, with x in mm.

Strain at any position x:
ε(x) = σ(x) / 200000 = 60 / [200000·(1 + x/500)] (dimensionless), with x in mm.

Summary of results:
Total axial deformation δ = (P·L·ln(1+k)) / (E·A0·k) = 0.1040 mm (elongation).
Maximum axial stress σ_max = 60 MPa at x = 0.
Minimum axial stress σ_min = 30 MPa at x = L.
The stress and strain both decrease monotonically from left to right as the area increases.
