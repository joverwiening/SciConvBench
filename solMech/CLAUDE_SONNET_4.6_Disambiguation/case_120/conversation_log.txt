Q: What is the functional form of the cross-sectional area variation along the bar's length — for example, does the area vary linearly from a value A₁ at one end to a value A₂ at the other end, or is a different expression intended?
A: A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L), where A0 = 200 mm², k = 1.0, and x is measured from the left end. So the area varies linearly from 200 mm² at x = 0 to 400 mm² at x = L.
