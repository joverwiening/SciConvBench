Final Specification:

The structure is a simply supported beam of span L, with a single point load P applied at the midspan (x = L/2).

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics throughout.

Assume the beam is prismatic with a uniform cross-section along its entire length.

Assume the flexural rigidity EI is constant along the span, where E is the Young's modulus and I is the second moment of area of the cross-section about the bending axis.

Assume the beam is slender (Euler-Bernoulli beam theory applies), so shear deformation is neglected.

Assume the beam self-weight is neglected; the only load is the point load P at midspan.

Assume the supports are ideal: a pin at one end (restrains horizontal and vertical translation) and a roller at the other end (restrains vertical translation only), so no horizontal reaction is induced by the transverse load.

The response quantity to be determined is the vertical deflection at midspan using Castigliano's Second Theorem.

Castigliano's Second Theorem states that the deflection delta at the point of application of a load P, in the direction of P, equals the partial derivative of the total strain energy U with respect to P: delta = dU/dP.

The total strain energy stored in the beam due to bending is U = integral from 0 to L of [M(x)^2 / (2EI)] dx, where M(x) is the bending moment at position x.

Apply symmetry: the reactions at both supports are equal, each equal to P/2.

For the left half of the beam (0 <= x <= L/2), the bending moment is M(x) = (P/2) * x.

For the right half of the beam (L/2 <= x <= L), the bending moment is M(x) = (P/2) * (L - x).

By symmetry, the integral over the full span equals twice the integral over the left half: U = 2 * integral from 0 to L/2 of [(P/2 * x)^2 / (2EI)] dx.

Evaluate the integral: U = 2 * integral from 0 to L/2 of [P^2 * x^2 / (8EI)] dx = (P^2 / (4EI)) * [x^3 / 3] from 0 to L/2.

This gives U = (P^2 / (4EI)) * (L^3 / 24) = P^2 * L^3 / (96EI).

Apply Castigliano's theorem: delta = dU/dP = d/dP [P^2 * L^3 / (96EI)] = 2P * L^3 / (96EI).

The midspan deflection is delta = P * L^3 / (48EI).

This result is the classical midspan deflection of a simply supported beam under a central point load, directed in the same direction as P (downward if P acts downward).
