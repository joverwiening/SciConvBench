The problem is a solid circular shaft under pure torsion with power-torque conversion. All units are N, mm, MPa unless stated otherwise.

GIVEN DATA
Power P = 5 kW = 5,000,000 N·mm/s (since 1 W = 1 N·m/s = 1000 N·mm/s, so 5 kW = 5,000,000 N·mm/s).
Rotational speed n = 1200 rpm.
Shaft diameter d = 20 mm, radius r = 10 mm.
Shaft length L = 1000 mm.
Shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa.
No transverse shear force is applied; loading is pure torsion only.

ADDITIONAL ASSUMPTIONS
The shaft is prismatic, homogeneous, and isotropic.
Cross-sections remain plane and undistorted (Saint-Venant torsion theory applies).
Small-deformation, linear-elastic behaviour is assumed throughout.
The shaft is simply supported in torsion (torque applied at one end, reacted at the other).
No stress concentrations are considered (Kt = 1).
Yield strength of AISI 1020 steel: Sy = 210 MPa; use von Mises criterion so shear yield strength Ssy = Sy / sqrt(3) = 121.2 MPa.

STEP 1: POWER–TORQUE CONVERSION
Angular velocity omega = 2 * pi * n / 60 = 2 * pi * 1200 / 60 = 125.664 rad/s.
Applied torque T = P / omega = 5,000,000 / 125.664 = 39,788.7 N·mm.
Round to T = 39,789 N·mm for subsequent calculations.

STEP 2: POLAR SECOND MOMENT OF AREA
J = pi * d^4 / 32 = pi * (20)^4 / 32 = pi * 160,000 / 32 = 15,708.0 mm^4.

STEP 3: MAXIMUM SHEAR STRESS (TORSION FORMULA)
The governing relation is tau_max = T * r / J (torsion formula, valid for circular cross-sections).
tau_max = 39,789 * 10 / 15,708.0 = 397,890 / 15,708.0 = 25.33 MPa.
Maximum shear stress occurs at the outer surface of the shaft.

STEP 4: ANGLE OF TWIST
The governing relation is phi = T * L / (G * J).
phi = 39,789 * 1000 / (76923.1 * 15,708.0) = 39,789,000 / 1,208,307,480 = 0.03293 rad.
Convert to degrees: phi = 0.03293 * (180 / pi) = 1.887 degrees.

STEP 5: TORSIONAL SHEAR STRESS AT ANY RADIUS
At radius rho from the centre: tau(rho) = T * rho / J.
At the centre (rho = 0): tau = 0 MPa.
At the outer surface (rho = r = 10 mm): tau = 25.33 MPa (as computed above).

STEP 6: TORSION CHECK (FACTOR OF SAFETY)
Using the von Mises shear yield criterion: Ssy = Sy / sqrt(3) = 210 / 1.7321 = 121.2 MPa.
Factor of safety against shear yielding: FS = Ssy / tau_max = 121.2 / 25.33 = 4.78.
The shaft is safe in torsion with a factor of safety of 4.78.

SUMMARY OF RESULTS
Applied torque T = 39,789 N·mm.
Polar moment of area J = 15,708.0 mm^4.
Maximum torsional shear stress tau_max = 25.33 MPa (at outer surface).
Angle of twist phi = 0.03293 rad = 1.887 degrees over L = 1000 mm.
Shear yield strength Ssy = 121.2 MPa.
Factor of safety FS = 4.78 (shaft is safe).
