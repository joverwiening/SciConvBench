The rod is a solid circular cylinder with diameter d0 = 20 mm and length L = 1000 mm.
The material is isotropic and linear-elastic with Young's modulus E = 105000 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.34.
Assume small deformations throughout.
Assume the rod is homogeneous and the cross-section is uniform along its entire length.
Assume the axial load P = 25000 N is applied as a pure uniaxial tensile force acting through the centroid, producing no bending or shear.
There is no lateral (transverse) load; the only loading is the axial tension P.
Assume no buckling, no body forces, and no temperature effects.
The ends are free to deform laterally (no lateral constraint), so the Poisson contraction is unrestrained.

Compute the cross-sectional area A = π d0² / 4.
A = π (20)² / 4 = 314.159 mm².

Compute the axial (longitudinal) stress σ_x = P / A.
σ_x = 25000 / 314.159 = 79.577 MPa (tensile).

The transverse stresses are zero because the lateral surfaces are traction-free: σ_y = σ_z = 0.
The shear stresses are zero everywhere: τ_xy = τ_xz = τ_yz = 0.

Apply the isotropic linear-elastic constitutive relations (generalised Hooke's law).
Axial strain: ε_x = (1/E)(σ_x − ν(σ_y + σ_z)) = σ_x / E.
ε_x = 79.577 / 105000 = 7.579 × 10⁻⁴ (elongation).

Transverse strains (Poisson contraction): ε_y = ε_z = −ν σ_x / E = −ν ε_x.
ε_y = ε_z = −0.34 × 7.579 × 10⁻⁴ = −2.577 × 10⁻⁴ (contraction).

Compute the axial elongation of the rod: δ_L = ε_x × L.
δ_L = 7.579 × 10⁻⁴ × 1000 = 0.7579 mm (increase in length).

Compute the change in diameter: Δd = ε_y × d0.
Δd = −2.577 × 10⁻⁴ × 20 = −5.154 × 10⁻³ mm (decrease in diameter).

Compute the final diameter under load: d_final = d0 + Δd = 20 − 0.005154 = 19.9948 mm.

Compute the volumetric strain (dilatation): e = ε_x + ε_y + ε_z = ε_x (1 − 2ν).
e = 7.579 × 10⁻⁴ × (1 − 2 × 0.34) = 7.579 × 10⁻⁴ × 0.32 = 2.425 × 10⁻⁴.
The volume increases slightly because 1 − 2ν > 0 for ν = 0.34.

Summary of governing relations used:
σ_x = P / A (uniaxial stress).
ε_x = σ_x / E (axial strain).
ε_y = ε_z = −ν ε_x (Poisson transverse strain).
δ_L = ε_x L (axial elongation).
Δd = ε_y d0 (diametral change).
e = (1 − 2ν) σ_x / E (volumetric strain).
