Design a solid circular cross-section column for buckling safety and evaluate the factor of safety with respect to buckling.

Material is 6061-T6 aluminum with elastic modulus E = 69000 MPa.

Applied compressive load is P = 25000 N.

Column length is L = 2200 mm.

End conditions are fixed-free, giving an effective length factor K = 2.0.

The effective length is Le = K × L = 2.0 × 2200 = 4400 mm.

The required factor of safety against buckling is n = 2.5.

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior throughout (Euler column buckling theory applies).

Assume the column is perfectly straight, homogeneous, and isotropic with no initial imperfections.

Assume the load is applied concentrically along the centroidal axis (no eccentricity).

Assume the column is slender enough that elastic buckling governs (verify slenderness ratio after design).

The governing relation is Euler's critical buckling load: Pcr = (π² × E × I) / Le².

For a solid circular cross-section of diameter d, the second moment of area is I = π × d⁴ / 64.

Substituting: Pcr = (π² × E × π × d⁴ / 64) / Le².

The design requirement is Pcr ≥ n × P, so the required critical load is Pcr_required = 2.5 × 25000 = 62500 N.

Set Pcr_required = (π³ × E × d⁴) / (64 × Le²) and solve for d.

Rearranging: d⁴ = (64 × Pcr_required × Le²) / (π³ × E).

Substituting values: d⁴ = (64 × 62500 × 4400²) / (π³ × 69000).

Compute Le² = 4400² = 19360000 mm².

Compute numerator: 64 × 62500 × 19360000 = 7.744 × 10¹³.

Compute denominator: π³ × 69000 = 31.006 × 69000 ≈ 2139414.

Compute d⁴ = 7.744 × 10¹³ / 2139414 ≈ 3.619 × 10⁷ mm⁴.

Compute d = (3.619 × 10⁷)^(1/4) ≈ 77.5 mm.

Round up to the next practical size: select d = 78 mm (or the nearest available standard size above 77.5 mm).

With the selected diameter d = 78 mm, compute I = π × 78⁴ / 64 = π × 37015056 / 64 ≈ 1,815,600 mm⁴.

Compute the actual critical load: Pcr = (π² × 69000 × 1815600) / (4400²).

Compute numerator: 9.8696 × 69000 × 1815600 ≈ 1.2366 × 10¹².

Compute denominator: 19360000.

Pcr ≈ 63,873 N (approximately 63.9 kN).

Compute the actual factor of safety: n_actual = Pcr / P = 63873 / 25000 ≈ 2.55.

The actual factor of safety n_actual ≈ 2.55 exceeds the required n = 2.5, so the design is acceptable.

Verify slenderness ratio to confirm elastic buckling governs: radius of gyration r = d/4 = 78/4 = 19.5 mm.

Slenderness ratio = Le / r = 4400 / 19.5 ≈ 225.6.

A slenderness ratio of 225.6 is well above the threshold for elastic buckling for 6061-T6 aluminum (typically around 66), confirming Euler buckling theory is valid for this column.

State the final result: the minimum required solid circular column diameter is d = 78 mm, yielding an actual factor of safety of approximately 2.55 against elastic buckling under the given loading and boundary conditions.
