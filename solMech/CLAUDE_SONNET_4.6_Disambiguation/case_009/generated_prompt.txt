The system consists of two bars arranged in parallel between two rigid plates, subjected to a total tensile axial load P = 20000 N.

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior for both bars.
Assume the two rigid plates remain perfectly parallel and do not rotate, enforcing equal deformation in both bars.
Assume the load is applied axially and concentrically so no bending is introduced.
Assume bars are initially stress-free with no pre-stress or misfit.

Geometry and material data:
Both bars have length L = 400 mm.
Bar 1 is AISI 1020 steel: A₁ = 300 mm², E₁ = 200000 MPa.
Bar 2 is cast iron: A₂ = 250 mm², E₂ = 110000 MPa.

Compatibility condition (parallel arrangement, rigid plates):
Both bars undergo the same axial deformation delta.
delta = delta₁ = delta₂.

Force equilibrium:
P = F₁ + F₂, where F₁ and F₂ are the axial forces carried by bar 1 and bar 2 respectively.

Constitutive relations (Hooke's Law):
F₁ = (E₁ A₁ / L) * delta.
F₂ = (E₂ A₂ / L) * delta.

Axial stiffnesses:
k₁ = E₁ A₁ / L = (200000 * 300) / 400 = 150000 N/mm.
k₂ = E₂ A₂ / L = (110000 * 250) / 400 = 68750 N/mm.
k_total = k₁ + k₂ = 218750 N/mm.

Shared deformation:
delta = P / k_total = 20000 / 218750 = 0.09143 mm.

Force in each bar:
F₁ = k₁ * delta = 150000 * 0.09143 = 13714 N.
F₂ = k₂ * delta = 68750 * 0.09143 = 6286 N.
Verify: F₁ + F₂ = 13714 + 6286 = 20000 N. Equilibrium satisfied.

Normal stress in each bar:
sigma₁ = F₁ / A₁ = 13714 / 300 = 45.71 MPa (tensile).
sigma₂ = F₂ / A₂ = 6286 / 250 = 25.14 MPa (tensile).

Axial strain in each bar (equal by compatibility):
epsilon = delta / L = 0.09143 / 400 = 2.286 × 10⁻⁴.
Verify bar 1: epsilon₁ = sigma₁ / E₁ = 45.71 / 200000 = 2.286 × 10⁻⁴. Consistent.
Verify bar 2: epsilon₂ = sigma₂ / E₂ = 25.14 / 110000 = 2.286 × 10⁻⁴. Consistent.

Summary of response quantities:
Total deformation: delta = 0.0914 mm.
Axial strain: epsilon = 2.286 × 10⁻⁴.
Force in steel bar 1: F₁ = 13714 N.
Force in cast iron bar 2: F₂ = 6286 N.
Stress in steel bar 1: sigma₁ = 45.71 MPa tensile.
Stress in cast iron bar 2: sigma₂ = 25.14 MPa tensile.
