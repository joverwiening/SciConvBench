The bar is a uniform, prismatic, vertical bar fixed at the top and free at the bottom.
The bar is subjected only to its own self-weight (no external axial load is applied).
Assume small deformations and linear-elastic material behavior throughout.
Assume the cross-sectional area A is constant along the entire length.
Assume the material is homogeneous and isotropic.
Ignore any lateral (Poisson) effects; treat this as a uniaxial problem.

Given values: L = 3000 mm, A = 300 mm², E = 69000 MPa, ρ = 8900 kg/m³, g = 9.81 m/s².

Convert density to consistent units: ρ = 8900 kg/m³ = 8.9 × 10⁻⁶ kg/mm³.
Compute the weight per unit length: w = ρ × A × g = 8.9 × 10⁻⁶ kg/mm³ × 300 mm² × 9810 mm/s² = 26.1567 N/mm.

Define x as the coordinate measured from the free bottom end upward, so x = 0 at the bottom and x = L at the fixed top.

The internal axial force at a cross-section located at coordinate x from the bottom equals the weight of the bar below that section.
Governing relation for internal force: N(x) = w × x.

The axial stress at position x: σ(x) = N(x) / A = (w × x) / A.
Substituting values: σ(x) = (26.1567 × x) / 300 = 0.08719 × x MPa, with x in mm.
Maximum stress occurs at the fixed top (x = L): σ_max = 0.08719 × 3000 = 261.57 MPa... 

Recheck: w = ρ A g = 8.9e-6 × 300 × 9810 = 8.9e-6 × 2,943,000 = 26.1927 N/mm.
Use w = 26.193 N/mm.

Maximum axial stress at the fixed support (x = L = 3000 mm): σ_max = w × L / A = 26.193 × 3000 / 300 = 261.93 MPa.
Stress varies linearly from zero at the free end to σ_max at the fixed end.

The axial strain at position x: ε(x) = σ(x) / E = (w × x) / (A × E).

The differential elongation of an infinitesimal element dx: dδ = ε(x) dx = (w × x) / (A × E) dx.

The total elongation is obtained by integrating dδ from x = 0 to x = L.
Governing integral: δ = ∫₀ᴸ (w × x) / (A × E) dx = (w / (A × E)) × (L² / 2) = w L² / (2 A E).

This result is equivalent to δ = (ρ g L²) / (2 E), which equals half the elongation that would result from applying the total weight as a concentrated load at the free end.

Substitute values: δ = (26.193 × 3000²) / (2 × 300 × 69000).
Numerator: 26.193 × 9,000,000 = 235,737,000 N·mm.
Denominator: 2 × 300 × 69000 = 41,400,000 N.
Total elongation: δ = 235,737,000 / 41,400,000 = 5.694 mm.

Summary of results:
Internal force distribution: N(x) = 26.193 x (N), linear from 0 at free end to 78,579 N at fixed end.
Stress distribution: σ(x) = 0.08731 x (MPa), linear from 0 at free end to 261.93 MPa at fixed end.
Strain distribution: ε(x) = σ(x) / E = 1.265 × 10⁻⁶ × x (mm/mm).
Total elongation of the bar due to self-weight: δ = 5.694 mm (the bar stretches downward by this amount).
