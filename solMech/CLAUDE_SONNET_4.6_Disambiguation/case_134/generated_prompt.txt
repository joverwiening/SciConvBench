The structural member is a rectangular adhesive layer with plan area A = 3000 mm² and thickness t = 8 mm.
The applied lateral (shear) load is V = 12000 N.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics throughout.
Assume the shear stress is uniformly distributed over the entire area A (average shear stress assumption).
Assume the adhesive layer is bonded rigidly on both faces so that the shear deformation is the relative lateral displacement between the two bonded surfaces.
A shear modulus G must be known or specified for the adhesive material; treat G as a given material property in the governing relations.

Governing relation 1 — Average shear stress:
τ = V / A
τ = 12000 N / 3000 mm² = 4 MPa

Governing relation 2 — Shear strain (engineering shear strain γ, in radians):
γ = τ / G
γ = 4 MPa / G

Governing relation 3 — Shear deformation (lateral relative displacement δ):
δ = γ · t
δ = (τ / G) · t
δ = (4 MPa / G) · 8 mm
δ = 32 / G   [mm, when G is in MPa]

To obtain a numerical value for γ and δ, substitute the known shear modulus G of the adhesive (in MPa) into the expressions above.
The shear stress τ = 4 MPa is fully determined and requires no further material data.
Verify that τ does not exceed the allowable shear strength of the adhesive as a design check.
