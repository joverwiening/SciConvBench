The shaft has a solid circular cross-section with diameter d = 45 mm and radius r = 22.5 mm.
Only the critical cross-section is analyzed; shaft length is not required.
The applied loads at the critical cross-section are: bending moment M = 2,000,000 N·mm and torque T = 500,000 N·mm.
No axial force, transverse shear force, or pressure loading is present.
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic, isotropic, homogeneous material behavior.
Assume the shaft material is steel with E = 200,000 MPa and ν = 0.3 unless a different material is specified; state this assumption explicitly in the solution.
The critical point is located on the outer surface of the cross-section, where bending stress is maximum.

Compute the second moment of area for bending: I = π d⁴ / 64.
Compute the polar moment of area for torsion: J = π d⁴ / 32, noting that J = 2I for a solid circle.
Compute the maximum bending (normal) stress at the outer fiber: σ = M · r / I, where r = d/2.
Compute the maximum shear stress due to torsion at the outer surface: τ = T · r / J.
No normal stress from axial force is present, so the only normal stress component on the critical element is σ_x = σ (bending) and σ_y = 0.
The shear stress on the critical element is τ_xy = τ (torsion).

Compute the principal stresses using: σ₁,₂ = (σ_x / 2) ± √( (σ_x / 2)² + τ_xy² ).
Compute the maximum in-plane shear stress: τ_max = √( (σ_x / 2)² + τ_xy² ).
Compute the von Mises (equivalent) stress: σ_vm = √( σ_x² + 3 τ_xy² ) for the uniaxial-plus-shear state.
Compute the principal strains using the plane-stress constitutive relations: ε₁ = (σ₁ - ν σ₂) / E and ε₂ = (σ₂ - ν σ₁) / E.
Compute the out-of-plane principal strain: ε₃ = -ν (σ₁ + σ₂) / E.
Compute the maximum shear strain: γ_max = (ε₁ - ε₂) for in-plane, or (ε₁ - ε₃) for absolute maximum if ε₃ is the most negative.

State the orientation of the principal planes using: tan(2θ_p) = 2 τ_xy / (σ_x - σ_y) = 2 τ_xy / σ_x.
Present all intermediate computed values (I, J, σ, τ) before presenting principal results.
Present the final results as: σ₁, σ₂, τ_max (in-plane), σ_vm, ε₁, ε₂, ε₃, γ_max, and θ_p.
State all assumptions explicitly at the start of the solution.
