The bar is prismatic, meaning it has a constant cross-sectional area and material properties throughout its length.
Assume linear-elastic, homogeneous, isotropic material behavior with small deformations.
Assume the bar is one-dimensional and loaded purely axially (no bending or shear).
Assume the cross-sectional area A and elastic modulus E are uniform across all segments unless given values are specified; treat EA as a known constant or leave results in terms of EA.
The bar has four points: A, B, C, D from left to right, forming three segments AB, BC, and CD.
The left end A is fixed (zero displacement at A).
The right end D is free (no kinematic constraint).
Applied point loads: +18000 N (rightward, positive) at B, -12000 N (leftward, negative) at C, +10000 N (rightward, positive) at D.
Use the sign convention that rightward (tensile) internal axial force is positive.

Governing relations:

Equilibrium: The reaction at A is found by summing all external forces to zero.
Sum of all external forces = R_A + 18000 - 12000 + 10000 = 0, giving R_A = -16000 N (i.e., 16000 N leftward).

Internal axial force N(x) is found by the method of sections, cutting each segment and summing forces to the left of the cut.

Segment AB (between A and B):
N_AB = -R_A applied from the left = +16000 N (tensile).

Segment BC (between B and C):
N_BC = N_AB + 18000 = 16000 + 18000 = 34000 N (tensile).

Segment CD (between C and D):
N_CD = N_BC - 12000 = 34000 - 12000 = 22000 N (tensile).

Verification: At free end D, N_CD + 10000 applied at D must equal zero when considering the section just to the left of D; the 10000 N load at D is an external load, so N just left of D equals 22000 N and the 10000 N load at D brings the net to 22000 - 10000 = ... re-examine using consistent convention.

Restate using sections summing forces to the LEFT of the cut:
N_AB = R_A (leftward reaction contributes): cutting just right of A, sum leftward forces to left = R_A = -16000 N, so N_AB = -16000 N by left-side sum, meaning 16000 N compressive if R_A is leftward.

Use the cleaner right-side summation convention (sum forces to the RIGHT of cut, with rightward positive giving tension positive):
N_AB: forces to right of cut in AB = +18000 - 12000 + 10000 = +16000 N (tensile).
N_BC: forces to right of cut in BC = -12000 + 10000 = -2000 N (compressive).
N_CD: forces to right of cut in CD = +10000 N (tensile).

These are the correct internal axial forces in each segment.

Axial stress in each segment: sigma = N / A, where A is the cross-sectional area.
sigma_AB = 16000 / A (tensile).
sigma_BC = -2000 / A (compressive).
sigma_CD = 10000 / A (tensile).

Axial deformation of each segment: delta = N * L / (E * A), where L is the length of the segment.
delta_AB = (16000 * L_AB) / (E * A).
delta_BC = (-2000 * L_BC) / (E * A).
delta_CD = (10000 * L_CD) / (E * A).

Displacement at any point is found by accumulating deformations from the fixed end A.
Displacement at A: u_A = 0 (fixed support).
Displacement at B: u_B = u_A + delta_AB = 16000 * L_AB / (EA).
Displacement at C: u_C = u_B + delta_BC = (16000 * L_AB - 2000 * L_BC) / (EA).
Displacement at D: u_D = u_C + delta_CD = (16000 * L_AB - 2000 * L_BC + 10000 * L_CD) / (EA).

To obtain numerical values for stress, deformation, and displacement, provide the segment lengths L_AB, L_BC, L_CD, the elastic modulus E, and the cross-sectional area A.
