Beam type: simply supported beam with both ends pinned (one pin, one roller), span length L = 4500 mm.

Loading: uniformly distributed transverse load w = 0.3 N/mm acting over the full span, directed downward.

Material and section properties: elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa (N/mm²), second moment of area I = 2000000 mm⁴, giving flexural rigidity EI = 200000 × 2000000 = 4.0 × 10¹¹ N·mm².

Assumption: small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior (Euler-Bernoulli beam theory).

Assumption: the beam is prismatic (EI is constant along the span).

Assumption: the beam is homogeneous and isotropic.

Assumption: shear deformations are neglected (Euler-Bernoulli, not Timoshenko).

Assumption: self-weight of the beam is not included unless w already accounts for it; w = 0.3 N/mm is the total applied distributed load.

Assumption: no axial force acts on the beam.

Coordinate system: x measured from the left support (x = 0) to the right support (x = L = 4500 mm).

Governing differential equation: EI · d⁴v/dx⁴ = w, where v(x) is the transverse deflection (positive downward).

Boundary conditions: v(0) = 0, v(L) = 0 (no deflection at supports), v''(0) = 0, v''(L) = 0 (no moment at pin and roller).

Reaction forces: by symmetry, each support reaction R = wL/2 = 0.3 × 4500 / 2 = 675 N.

Bending moment distribution: M(x) = R·x − w·x²/2 = 675x − 0.15x².

Maximum bending moment occurs at midspan x = L/2 = 2250 mm: M_max = wL²/8 = 0.3 × 4500² / 8 = 759375 N·mm.

Deflection formula: v(x) = (w / (24·EI)) · (x⁴ − 2L·x³ + L³·x).

Substitute values: v(x) = (0.3 / (24 × 4.0 × 10¹¹)) · (x⁴ − 2 × 4500 · x³ + 4500³ · x).

Coefficient: 0.3 / (9.6 × 10¹²) = 3.125 × 10⁻¹⁴ mm⁻³.

Maximum deflection occurs at midspan x = L/2 = 2250 mm: v_max = 5wL⁴ / (384·EI).

Compute v_max: numerator = 5 × 0.3 × 4500⁴ = 5 × 0.3 × 4.10063 × 10¹⁴ = 6.15094 × 10¹⁴ N·mm³, denominator = 384 × 4.0 × 10¹¹ = 1.536 × 10¹⁴ N·mm², giving v_max = 6.15094 × 10¹⁴ / 1.536 × 10¹⁴ = 4.004 mm (downward).

Slope formula: dv/dx = (w / (24·EI)) · (4x³ − 6L·x² + L³).

Slope at left support (x = 0): θ(0) = wL³ / (24·EI) = 0.3 × 4500³ / (24 × 4.0 × 10¹¹).

Compute θ(0): numerator = 0.3 × 9.1125 × 10¹⁰ = 2.73375 × 10¹⁰, denominator = 9.6 × 10¹², giving θ(0) = 2.73375 × 10¹⁰ / 9.6 × 10¹² = 2.847 × 10⁻³ radians (clockwise, beam slopes downward to the right from left support).

Slope at right support (x = L): θ(L) = −wL³ / (24·EI) = −2.847 × 10⁻³ radians (counterclockwise, beam slopes downward to the left from right support).

Slope at midspan (x = L/2): dv/dx = 0 by symmetry.
