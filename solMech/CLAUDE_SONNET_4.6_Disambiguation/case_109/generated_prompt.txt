Specification: Castigliano's Theorem Application — Midspan Deflection of a Simply Supported Beam with a Central Point Load

Problem Setup
The beam is simply supported at both ends, separated by span length L.
A single point load P acts at the midspan (x = L/2).
The goal is to find the vertical deflection at the midspan using Castigliano's second theorem.

Material and Geometry Assumptions
Assume small deformations throughout (linear geometry).
Assume linear elastic material behavior (Hooke's law applies).
Assume the beam is prismatic: cross-sectional area and second moment of area I are constant along the length.
Assume the material is homogeneous and isotropic with Young's modulus E.
Assume Euler-Bernoulli beam theory applies: plane sections remain plane, and shear deformation is neglected.
Assume no axial force or torsion is present; bending is the sole contributor to strain energy.

Castigliano's Second Theorem
The theorem states that the displacement corresponding to a generalized force Q is the partial derivative of the total strain energy U with respect to Q.
Write: delta = dU/dQ, where Q is the force whose corresponding displacement is sought.
Since P acts at midspan and the deflection at midspan is sought, set Q = P directly; no dummy load is needed.

Reaction Forces
By symmetry, each support reaction is R = P/2.

Bending Moment Distribution
Divide the beam into two segments by symmetry: 0 <= x <= L/2 and L/2 <= x <= L.
For segment 1 (0 <= x <= L/2): M(x) = (P/2) * x.
For segment 2 (L/2 <= x <= L): by symmetry, M(x) = (P/2) * (L - x).

Strain Energy in Bending
The total strain energy stored in bending is U = integral from 0 to L of [M(x)^2 / (2EI)] dx.
Exploit symmetry: U = 2 * integral from 0 to L/2 of [(P/2 * x)^2 / (2EI)] dx.
Expand: U = 2 * integral from 0 to L/2 of [P^2 * x^2 / (8EI)] dx.
Evaluate: U = (P^2 / (4EI)) * [x^3 / 3] from 0 to L/2.
Result: U = (P^2 / (4EI)) * (L^3 / 24) = P^2 * L^3 / (96EI).

Application of Castigliano's Theorem
Differentiate U with respect to P: delta = dU/dP = d/dP [P^2 * L^3 / (96EI)].
Result: delta = 2P * L^3 / (96EI) = P * L^3 / (48EI).

Governing Result
The midspan deflection is delta = P * L^3 / (48EI), directed in the direction of P (downward).

Validity Conditions
This result is valid provided delta << L (small deformation assumption holds).
The beam must remain within the elastic limit: maximum bending stress sigma_max = M_max * c / I = (PL/4) * c / I must not exceed the yield stress of the material.
Shear deformation effects are negligible when the beam is slender (L >> cross-sectional depth).
