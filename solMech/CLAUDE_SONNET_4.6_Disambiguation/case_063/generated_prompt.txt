A prismatic circular rod is fully axially restrained at both ends and subjected to a uniform temperature change.

Use small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics throughout.

Assume the material is homogeneous and isotropic.

Assume the temperature change is uniform along the entire length of the rod.

Assume no bending, shear, or torsion is present; the problem is purely uniaxial.

Assume full axial restraint means zero net axial deformation (δ = 0).

Assume no buckling occurs.

Given values:
L = 1200 mm
d = 20 mm
E = 200000 MPa
α = 1.0 × 10⁻⁵ 1/°C
ΔT = 35 °C

Step 1: Calculate the cross-sectional area from the diameter.
A = π × d² / 4
A = π × (20)² / 4
A = π × 400 / 4
A = 100π
A ≈ 314.159 mm²

Step 2: State the compatibility condition.
Full axial restraint means the total axial deformation is zero.
δ_total = δ_thermal + δ_mechanical = 0

Step 3: Express the free thermal deformation (what the rod would expand if unrestrained).
δ_thermal_free = α × ΔT × L
δ_thermal_free = (1.0 × 10⁻⁵) × 35 × 1200
δ_thermal_free = 0.42 mm

Step 4: Apply the compatibility condition to find the mechanical (restraint-induced) deformation.
δ_mechanical = −δ_thermal_free = −0.42 mm
The negative sign indicates compression (the restraint prevents expansion).

Step 5: Calculate the thermal strain (free, unrestrained).
ε_thermal = α × ΔT
ε_thermal = (1.0 × 10⁻⁵) × 35
ε_thermal = 3.5 × 10⁻⁴ (dimensionless)

Step 6: Calculate the mechanical strain induced by the restraint.
ε_mechanical = −ε_thermal = −3.5 × 10⁻⁴
The net total strain is zero, consistent with full restraint.

Step 7: Calculate the thermal stress (restraint stress).
σ = E × ε_mechanical
σ = 200000 × (−3.5 × 10⁻⁴)
σ = −70 MPa
The stress is −70 MPa, meaning the rod is in compression.

Step 8: Calculate the restraint reaction force.
F = σ × A
F = −70 × 314.159
F ≈ −21991 N
F ≈ −21.99 kN
The reaction force magnitude is approximately 21.99 kN acting as a compressive axial force on the rod.

Summary of results:
Cross-sectional area A ≈ 314.159 mm²
Free thermal deformation δ_thermal_free = 0.42 mm (elongation, suppressed)
Thermal strain ε_thermal = 3.5 × 10⁻⁴
Net total strain = 0 (enforced by full restraint)
Mechanical strain ε_mechanical = −3.5 × 10⁻⁴
Thermal stress σ = −70 MPa (compressive)
Restraint reaction force F ≈ −21.99 kN (compressive)
