The task is a solid circular shaft torsion analysis under power-torque loading using small-deformation linear-elastic solid mechanics.

Use SI-consistent units of N, mm, and MPa (N/mm²) throughout.

The shaft is solid and circular in cross-section.

Shaft diameter is d = 35 mm.
Shaft length is L = 1000 mm.
Applied power is P = 10 kW = 10 × 10³ W = 10 × 10⁶ N·mm/s.
Rotational speed is n = 1800 rpm.
Shear modulus is G = 76923.1 MPa.
Material is AISI 1020 steel.
No transverse shear force is applied; omit transverse shear stress calculations entirely.

Additional assumptions:
The shaft is prismatic (uniform cross-section along its full length).
The material is homogeneous, isotropic, and linearly elastic.
The torque is steady (no dynamic or fatigue effects).
Both ends of the shaft are considered for the angle of twist (twist is computed over the full length L).
Saint-Venant's principle applies; end effects are ignored.
Small deformation and small rotation assumptions hold.

Step 1 — Convert rotational speed to radians per second.
Angular velocity: ω = 2π n / 60 = 2π × 1800 / 60 = 60π rad/s ≈ 188.496 rad/s.

Step 2 — Compute the applied torque from power.
Governing relation: P = T · ω, therefore T = P / ω.
T = (10 × 10⁶ N·mm/s) / (188.496 rad/s) = 53,051.6 N·mm.

Step 3 — Compute the polar second moment of area of the solid circular cross-section.
Governing relation: J = π d⁴ / 32.
J = π × (35)⁴ / 32 = π × 1,500,625 / 32 = 147,323.5 mm⁴.

Step 4 — Compute the maximum shear stress due to torsion.
The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer surface, at radial distance c = d/2 = 17.5 mm.
Governing relation (torsion formula): τ_max = T · c / J.
τ_max = 53,051.6 × 17.5 / 147,323.5 = 928,403 / 147,323.5 = 6.30 MPa.

Step 5 — Compute the angle of twist over the full shaft length.
Governing relation: φ = T · L / (G · J).
φ = 53,051.6 × 1000 / (76923.1 × 147,323.5).
Numerator: 53,051,600 N·mm².
Denominator: 76923.1 × 147,323.5 = 11,332,577,885 N·mm² (approximately 1.1333 × 10¹⁰).
φ = 53,051,600 / 11,332,577,885 = 4.682 × 10⁻³ radians = 0.2682 degrees.

Summary of results:
Angular velocity ω = 188.50 rad/s.
Applied torque T = 53,051.6 N·mm.
Polar moment of area J = 147,323.5 mm⁴.
Maximum torsional shear stress τ_max = 6.30 MPa (at the outer surface).
Angle of twist φ = 4.682 × 10⁻³ rad = 0.268°.

The governing relations used are:
Power-torque relation: P = T · ω.
Torsion formula: τ = T · r / J, maximum at r = d/2.
Angle of twist formula: φ = T · L / (G · J).
Polar moment for solid circle: J = π d⁴ / 32.
