The problem involves a solid circular column under axial compression, analyzed for Euler buckling.

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior throughout.
Assume the column is perfectly straight, homogeneous, and isotropic.
Assume both ends are pin-pin (pinned-pinned), giving an effective length factor K = 1, so the effective length Le = K·L = 1·2000 = 2000 mm.
Assume a factor of safety against buckling of FS = 2.0 (a commonly used value for column buckling; state this explicitly as an assumption since none was given).

Geometry: Length L = 2000 mm, diameter d = 20 mm.
Material: Elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.

Compute the cross-sectional area: A = π·d²/4.
A = π·(20)²/4 = 314.16 mm².

Compute the second moment of area (moment of inertia) for a solid circle: I = π·d⁴/64.
I = π·(20)⁴/64 = 7853.98 mm⁴.

Compute the radius of gyration: r = sqrt(I/A).
r = sqrt(7853.98 / 314.16) = sqrt(25) = 5 mm.

Compute the slenderness ratio: SR = Le / r.
SR = 2000 / 5 = 400.

A slenderness ratio of 400 is large, confirming that Euler buckling governs over material yielding (Euler buckling is valid when SR exceeds the critical slenderness ratio for the material; no yield strength was given, so Euler buckling is taken as the governing mode by assumption).

The governing Euler critical buckling load relation is: Pcr = π²·E·I / Le².
Pcr = π²·(200000)·(7853.98) / (2000)².
Pcr = (9.8696 × 200000 × 7853.98) / 4000000.
Pcr = 15,504,187,840 / 4,000,000 (numerator: 9.8696 × 200000 = 1,973,920; 1,973,920 × 7853.98 = 15,504,187,840 — recompute cleanly below).

Numerator: π²·E·I = 9.8696 × 200000 × 7853.98 = 9.8696 × 1,570,796,000 = 15,503,118 N·mm² (using 9.8696 × 1,570,796 = 15,503,118 N·mm² with I in mm⁴ and E in N/mm²).
Denominator: Le² = (2000)² = 4,000,000 mm².
Pcr = 15,503,118 / 4,000,000 = 3876 N = 3.876 kN.

The allowable column load is: P_allow = Pcr / FS = 3876 / 2.0 = 1938 N = 1.938 kN.

The factor of safety with respect to buckling, given no applied load P is specified, is expressed as the ratio of critical load to applied load: FS_buckling = Pcr / P.
Since no applied load P is given, the factor of safety is reported in terms of the critical load: FS = Pcr / P_allow = 2.0 by definition of the assumed factor of safety.

Summary of results:
Effective length Le = 2000 mm (pin-pin, K = 1).
Cross-sectional area A = 314.16 mm².
Second moment of area I = 7853.98 mm⁴.
Radius of gyration r = 5 mm.
Slenderness ratio SR = 400.
Euler critical buckling load Pcr = 3876 N (3.876 kN).
