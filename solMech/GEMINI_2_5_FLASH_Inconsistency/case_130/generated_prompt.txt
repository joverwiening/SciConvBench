Use N–mm–MPa units.
A simply supported beam will experience a maximum bending moment of M_max = 6000000 N·mm.
The beam will have a rectangular cross section with width b = 30 mm and unknown height h.
The allowable bending stress is σ_allow = 90 MPa.
The extreme-fiber distance 'c' will be taken as h/2.
The moment of inertia 'I' for the rectangular cross section will be taken as b h^3/12.
Determine the minimum required height h using the formula σ_max = M_max (h/2) / (b h^3/12).
