Q: Should the calculation assume the free-end rotation is zero, or should it follow the standard Euler-Bernoulli solution for a cantilever beam with a point load at the free end?
A: Follow the standard Euler-Bernoulli solution for a cantilever beam with a point load at the free end. Do not assume the free-end rotation is zero.
