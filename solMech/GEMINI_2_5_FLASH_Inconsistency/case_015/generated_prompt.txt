Use N–mm–MPa units.
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory (small deflection, linear elastic).
The beam is a cantilever of length L = 1200 mm.
The beam is fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
The beam is made of AISI 1020 steel with Young's Modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The beam has a constant second moment of area I = 2000000 mm^4.
A downward point load P = 2000 N is applied at the free end (x = L).
Calculate the slope at the free end (x = L) using the standard Euler-Bernoulli solution for a cantilever beam with a point load at the free end.
Calculate the vertical deflection at the free end (x = L) using the standard Euler-Bernoulli solution for a cantilever beam with a point load at the free end.
Report the slope in radians or degrees.
Report the vertical deflection in mm.
