Q: You specified to use the bending second moment I = π d^4/64 in the torsion relations. Should the torsion calculations for shear stress and angle of twist use this value of I?
A: No, the complete requirement does not specify using `I = π d^4/64` for torsion relations. It specifies using `J = π d^4/32` for both shear stress and angle of twist calculations.
