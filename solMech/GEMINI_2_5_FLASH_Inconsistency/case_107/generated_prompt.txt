Use N–mm–MPa units for all calculations.
A simply supported beam has a span L = 3000 mm.
The beam is made of structural steel with E = 200000 MPa.
The beam has a constant moment of inertia I = 3000000 mm^4.
A point load P = 2000 N is applied downward at midspan.
Compute the vertical deflection at midspan using Castigliano’s theorem.
Define the bending moment M(x) piecewise over 0≤x≤L.
Calculate the strain energy U by integrating U = ∫ M^2/(2EI) dx over the full span.
