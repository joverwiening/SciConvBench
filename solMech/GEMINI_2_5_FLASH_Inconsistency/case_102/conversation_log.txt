Q: Should the elongation be calculated by replacing the distributed self-weight with a single concentrated load at the free end, or by integrating the varying axial force P(x) = ρ g A (L − x)?
A: The elongation should be calculated by integrating the varying axial force P(x) = ρ g A (L − x) using the formula δ = ∫₀ᴸ P(x)/(A E) dx.
