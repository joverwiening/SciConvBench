The bar is vertical, prismatic, and linearly elastic.
The unit system is N-mm-MPa.
The bar has a length L = 3500 mm.
The bar has a constant cross-sectional area A = 200 mm^2.
The bar material has a modulus of elasticity E = 69000 MPa.
The material density is ρ = 7850 kg/m^3.
The gravitational acceleration is g = 9.81 m/s^2.
The product of density and gravitational acceleration is ρg = 7.69935 x 10^-5 N/mm^3.
The bar hangs from a rigid support at its top end.
Only the bar’s self-weight acts on the bar.
The axial force at a section x, measured downward from the top, is P(x) = ρ g A (L − x).
The elongation of the bar due to its own weight is calculated by integrating the varying axial force using the formula δ = ∫₀ᴸ P(x)/(A E) dx.
