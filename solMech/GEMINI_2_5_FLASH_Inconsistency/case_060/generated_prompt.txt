Use N-mm-MPa units for all calculations.
Assume Euler buckling applies, meaning the column is slender and remains elastic.

The end conditions are fixed-fixed.

Compute the Euler critical buckling load P_cr using the formula P_cr = π^2 E I / (K L)^2.
Report the computed P_cr value in Newtons (N).
