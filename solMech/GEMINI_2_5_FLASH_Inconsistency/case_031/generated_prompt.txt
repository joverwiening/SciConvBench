The bar has a length L = 3500 mm.

The bar material has a modulus of elasticity E = 69000 MPa.

Ignore any external loads; only the bar’s self-weight acts.
Compute the elongation of the bar due to its own weight using the integration method.
The axial force at a section x, measured downward from the top, is P(x) = ρ g A (L − x).
The elongation δ is calculated by integrating δ = ∫₀ᴸ P(x)/(A E) dx.
