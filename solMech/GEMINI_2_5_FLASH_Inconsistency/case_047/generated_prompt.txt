The problem will be solved using consistent N–mm–MPa units and small-deformation, linear-elastic assumptions.
The Young’s modulus E = 200000 MPa for AISI 1020 steel will be used.
The diameter of the circular rod is 12 mm.
The gage length of the rod is 800 mm.
The axial force applied in tension is 15000 N.
The yield strength S_y = 350 MPa will be used.
The cross-sectional area of the rod will be computed.
The average normal stress in the rod will be computed as the axial force divided by the cross-sectional area.
The elastic normal strain will be computed as the average normal stress divided by Young's modulus.
The axial elongation of the gage length will be computed as the elastic normal strain multiplied by the gage length.
The factor of safety against yielding will be computed as n = S_y / σ_avg.
