The unit system is N-mm-MPa.
The bar has a rectangular cross section with width b = 40 mm and height h = 120 mm.
The internal resultant normal force P is 30000 N (tension).
The internal resultant transverse shear force V is 12000 N (acting parallel to the width b).
Assume uniform stress distributions for both normal and shear stresses.
Compute the cross-sectional area A.
Compute the average normal stress σ_avg as P divided by A.
Compute the average shear stress τ_avg as V divided by A.
Compute the magnitude of the maximum in-plane shear stress τ_max using the formula τ_max = sqrt((σ_avg/2)^2 + τ_avg^2).
