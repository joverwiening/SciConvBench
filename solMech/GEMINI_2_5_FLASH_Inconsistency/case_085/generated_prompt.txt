The analysis uses N–mm–MPa units.
The material behavior is linear-elastic.
The bar is made of AISI 1020 steel.
The bar is fixed at its left end and loaded in tension at its right end by P = 35000 N.
Segment 1 has length L1 = 300 mm and diameter d1 = 12 mm.
Segment 2 has length L2 = 800 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm.
The modulus of elasticity E = 200000 MPa.
The normal stress in each segment is calculated based on the axial force and the respective cross-sectional area.
The total elongation of the bar is the sum of the individual elongations of each segment.
The normal stress in each segment will be determined.
The total elongation of the bar between the fixed end and the load application point will be determined.
