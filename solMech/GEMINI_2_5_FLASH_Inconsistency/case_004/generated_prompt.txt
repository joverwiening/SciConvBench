Use N-mm-MPa units for all calculations and results.
The prismatic bar has a constant cross-sectional area of 300 mm^2.
The bar is segmented by points A, B, C, and D.
The length of segment AB is 500 mm.
The length of segment BC is 600 mm.
The length of segment CD is 400 mm.
The bar is made of AISI 1020 steel with a modulus of elasticity E = 200000 MPa.
Axial forces are applied at the points as follows: at B, a force of 18000 N; at C, a force of -12000 N; and at D, a force of 16000 N.
Positive forces are directed to the right.
The left end A is fixed, meaning the displacement at A (u_A) is 0.
Calculate the internal axial force in each segment (AB, BC, and CD).
Calculate the normal stress in each segment (AB, BC, and CD).
Calculate the displacement of point D relative to point A.
