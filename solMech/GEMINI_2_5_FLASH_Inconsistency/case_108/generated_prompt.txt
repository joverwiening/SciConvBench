The analysis uses N–mm–MPa units.
The column has a diameter d = 30 mm.
The column has a length L = 1500 mm.
The column material has a Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The column has an effective length factor K = 2.0.
The area moment of inertia I for the circular section is calculated as I = π d^4 / 64.
The Euler critical load P_cr is computed using the formula P_cr = (π^2 * E * I) / (K * L)^2.
The allowable axial compressive load P_allow is determined by dividing P_cr by a factor of safety n = 2.0.
