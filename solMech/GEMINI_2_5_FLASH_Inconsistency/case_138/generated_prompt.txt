Use N–mm–MPa units for all calculations and results.
Consider the stress state at the outer surface of the circular shaft at the location of maximum bending stress.
The solid circular shaft has a diameter d = 35 mm.
The shaft is subjected to a bending moment M = 3000000 N·mm.
The shaft is subjected to a torque T = 500000 N·mm.
Compute the maximum bending normal stress at the surface using the formula σ = M c / I.
Use I = π d^4/64 for the area moment of inertia.
Use c = d/2 for the distance from the neutral axis to the outer surface.
Compute the torsional shear stress at the surface using the formula τ = T c / J.
Use J = π d^4/32 for the polar moment of inertia.
Treat the surface stress state as plane stress with σx equal to the computed bending normal stress σ.
Set σy = 0 for the plane stress state.
Set τxy equal to the computed torsional shear stress τ for the plane stress state.
