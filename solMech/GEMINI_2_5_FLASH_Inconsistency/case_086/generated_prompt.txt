Use N–mm–MPa units.
The material behavior is linear-elastic.
The system is a stepped circular bar.
The bar is fixed at its left end.
The bar is loaded in tension at its right end by an axial force P = 25000 N.
Segment 1, the left segment, has a length L1 = 400 mm.
Segment 1 has a diameter d1 = 16 mm.
Segment 2, the right segment, has a length L2 = 400 mm.
Segment 2 has a diameter d2 = 40 mm.
The modulus of elasticity E = 200000 MPa.
Calculate the normal stress in Segment 1 as the axial force P divided by its cross-sectional area.
Calculate the normal stress in Segment 2 as the axial force P divided by its cross-sectional area.
Calculate the elongation of Segment 1 using the axial force P, its length L1, its cross-sectional area, and the modulus of elasticity E.
Calculate the elongation of Segment 2 using the axial force P, its length L2, its cross-sectional area, and the modulus of elasticity E.
Calculate the total elongation of the bar as the sum of the individual elongations of Segment 1 and Segment 2.
