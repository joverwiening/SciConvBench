The analysis uses N-mm-MPa units.
A solid circular shaft has a diameter of 30 mm and a total length of 1200 mm.
The shaft is fixed at x = 0.
A torque T1 = 200000 N·mm is applied at x = L (1200 mm) in the positive sense.
A torque T2 = -200000 N·mm is applied at x = 400 mm in the negative sense.
The material is AISI 1020 steel with a shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa.
The internal torque is constructed for each segment of the shaft.
The maximum shear stress is computed for each segment.
The angle of twist at x = L relative to the fixed end is computed by summing the twist of each segment.
