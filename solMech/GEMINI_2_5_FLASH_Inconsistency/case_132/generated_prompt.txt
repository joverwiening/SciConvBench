Use N–mm–MPa units.
Assume small strains.
A rectangular adhesive layer has an area A = 4000 mm^2 and a thickness t = 20 mm.
The layer is subjected to a uniform in-plane shear force V = 12000 N.
The material is isotropic with a shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.
Compute the average shear stress τ_avg using the formula τ_avg = V/A.
Compute the average shear strain γ_avg.
Compute the relative lateral displacement between the two faces of the layer Δ using the formula Δ = γ_avg·t.
