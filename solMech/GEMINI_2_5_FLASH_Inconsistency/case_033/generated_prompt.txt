The task is to compute the maximum deflection and the slope at the supports for a simply supported beam.
The beam has a span L = 2500 mm.
The beam is made of structural steel with E = 200000 MPa and a constant I = 1200000 mm^4.
A point load P = 2500 N acts downward at midspan.
N–mm–MPa units are to be used.
Euler–Bernoulli beam theory is to be assumed, and shear deformation is to be neglected.
The slope at midspan is zero due to symmetry.
The slope at the supports is non-zero.
