Use N–mm–MPa units for all calculations.
A cantilever beam of length L = 1500 mm is fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
The beam is made of AISI 1020 steel with Young's Modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The moment of inertia I is constant at 800000 mm^4.
A downward point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end (x = L).
Compute the vertical deflection at the free end using Castigliano’s theorem.
The bending strain energy U is given by U = ∫[0→L] M(x)^2/(2EI) dx.
For the integration, the bending moment M(x) should be taken as P(L-x).
The calculation must involve setting up M(x), integrating U, and then differentiating U with respect to P.
Do not use a memorized deflection formula.
