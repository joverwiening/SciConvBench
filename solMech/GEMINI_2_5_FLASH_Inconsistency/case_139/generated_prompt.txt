The initial diameter d0 is 12.5 mm.
The initial gage length L0 is 50 mm.
The axial load P is 15000 N.
The elongation ΔL is 0.15 mm.
Calculate the initial cross-sectional area A0 using the initial diameter d0.
Compute the engineering stress σ by dividing the axial load P by the initial cross-sectional area A0.
Compute the engineering strain ε by dividing the elongation ΔL by the initial gage length L0.
Determine the modulus of elasticity E by dividing the engineering stress σ by the engineering strain ε.
