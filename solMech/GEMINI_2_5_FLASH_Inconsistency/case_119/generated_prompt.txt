Use N-mm-MPa units for all calculations.
Treat the modulus of elasticity E as constant.
The bar has a length L of 500 mm.
A constant axial tensile force P of 12000 N is applied to the bar.
The material is AISI 1020 steel with a modulus of elasticity E of 200000 MPa.
The bar's cross-sectional area varies linearly along its length according to A(x) = A0 * (1 + k * x/L).
The variable x is measured from the left end of the bar.
The initial cross-sectional area A0 is 200 mm^2.
The constant k is 1.0.
Calculate the elongation of the bar by replacing A(x) with the arithmetic-mean area and treating this as exact in the axial-deformation calculation.
