The units for the calculation are N-mm-MPa.
The material response is linear elastic axial.
The prismatic bar has a length L of 800 mm.
The prismatic bar has a cross-sectional area A of 300 mm^2.
The structural steel has a modulus of elasticity E of 200000 MPa.
A tensile load P of 8000 N is applied suddenly, starting from zero load.
The maximum stress in the bar during the transient is to be determined using energy methods.
The calculation will compare the work done by the suddenly applied load to the strain energy stored in the bar.
