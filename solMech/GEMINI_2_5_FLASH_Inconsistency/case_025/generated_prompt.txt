Use N–mm–MPa units.
A cantilever beam of length L = 1500 mm is fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
The beam is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with E = 69000 MPa.
The beam has a constant moment of inertia I = 2500000 mm^4.
A downward point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end.
The bending moment M(x) varies with x as M(x) = -P(L-x).
Compute the vertical deflection at the free end using Castigliano’s theorem.
The bending strain energy U is given by U = ∫[0→L] M(x)^2/(2EI) dx.
First, set up M(x), then integrate U, and finally differentiate U with respect to P to find the deflection.
