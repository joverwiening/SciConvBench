The simply supported beam AB has a span of 3000 mm.
A concentrated vertical load P of 12000 N acts downward at 1500 mm from support A.
The internal shear force V and bending moment M are to be determined at a cross section located at x = 1500 mm from support A.
The sign convention for positive shear V is upward on the left cut face.
The sign convention for positive bending moment M causes sagging (tension at the bottom fibers).
The shear force V will be determined just to the left of the concentrated load.
The internal shear force V at x = 1500 mm is 6000 N.
The internal bending moment M at x = 1500 mm is 9000000 N·mm.
