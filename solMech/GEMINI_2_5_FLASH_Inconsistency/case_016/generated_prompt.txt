Use N–mm–MPa units.
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory (small deflection, linear elastic).
The beam is a cantilever of length L = 800 mm.
The beam is fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
The beam material is 6061-T6 aluminum with Young's Modulus E = 69000 MPa.
The beam has a constant second moment of area I = 1600000 mm^4.
A downward point load P = 2500 N is applied at the free end (x = L).
The free end is truly free to rotate.
Determine the slope at the free end (x = L).
Report the slope in radians.
Determine the vertical deflection at the free end (x = L).
Report the vertical deflection in mm.
