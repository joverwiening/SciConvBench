The material is isotropic linear elastic.
The Young's modulus E is 69000 MPa.
The Poisson's ratio ν is 0.33.
The stress components are σx = 120 MPa, σy = 120 MPa, σz = 0 MPa, and τxy = 75 MPa.
All other shear stress components are zero.
The shear modulus G is calculated as E / (1 + ν).
The normal strains εx, εy, εz are computed using the generalized Hooke’s law: εx = (1/E) * [σx - ν(σy + σz)], εy = (1/E) * [σy - ν(σx + σz)], and εz = (1/E) * [σz - ν(σx + σy)].
The engineering shear strain γxy is computed as τxy / G.
Stress values are in MPa and strain values are dimensionless.
