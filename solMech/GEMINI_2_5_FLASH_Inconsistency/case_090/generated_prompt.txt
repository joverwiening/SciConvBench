The system uses N-mm-MPa units.
A simply supported beam has a span L of 3000 mm.
The supports are located at x=0 and x=L.
The beam is made of AISI 1020 steel.
The modulus of elasticity E is 200000 MPa.
The moment of inertia I is 1200000 mm^4.
A point load P of 800 N acts downward at x = 2000 mm.
The deflection at the load point x = 2000 mm is to be computed.
The unit-load (virtual work) method for beam deflection is used.
The deflection δ is calculated using the integral δ = ∫[0→L] (M(x)·m(x))/(EI) dx.
M(x) is the real bending moment diagram.
m(x) is the moment diagram due to a unit force applied at the point and direction of the desired deflection.
For the auxiliary system, a unit force is applied downward at x = 2000 mm.
Both the real and auxiliary systems use simple supports, allowing rotation.
