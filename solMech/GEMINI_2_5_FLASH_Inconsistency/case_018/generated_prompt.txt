Use N-mm-MPa units for all calculations and results.
Model a solid circular shaft with a diameter of 40 mm and a total length of 1500 mm.
Fix the shaft at x = 0.
Apply a torque T1 = 300000 N·mm at the free end x = 1500 mm in the positive sense.
Apply a torque T2 = -150000 N·mm at x = 750 mm in the negative sense.
Use 6061-T6 aluminum as the material with a shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa.
Divide the shaft into two segments: segment 1 from x = 0 mm to x = 750 mm, and segment 2 from x = 750 mm to x = 1500 mm.
Construct the internal torque for each segment, calculating it piecewise.
Compute the maximum shear stress in each segment.
Compute the angle of twist at x = 1500 mm relative to the fixed end by summing the twist of each segment.
