Use N–mm–MPa units for all calculations.
The shaft is a solid circular shaft with a diameter d = 30 mm.
The shaft has a length L = 1000 mm.
The shaft material is 6061-T6 aluminum.
The shear modulus G for the material is 25939.8 MPa.
The shaft is subjected to a constant torque T = 450000 N·mm.
Calculate the polar moment of inertia J using the formula J = π d^4/32.
Compute the torsional strain energy U using the expression U = ∫[0→L] T^2/(2GJ) dx.
Apply Castigliano’s theorem to find the angle of twist φ using the relation φ = ∂U/∂T.
The final result should be the angle of twist between the ends of the shaft.
