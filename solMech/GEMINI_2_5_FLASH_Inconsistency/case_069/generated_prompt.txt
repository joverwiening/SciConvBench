The unit system is N–mm–MPa.
The Young's Modulus E is 200000 MPa.
The second moment of area I is 2500000 mm^4.
The bending moment M is 3000000 N·mm.
The curvature κ is computed using the formula κ = M/(EI).
The radius of curvature ρ is computed using the formula ρ = 1/κ.
The curvature κ and the radius of curvature ρ are reported in mm.
