Use N–mm–MPa units for all calculations.
Define a cantilever beam of length L = 1500 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
The beam material has a modulus of elasticity E = 200000 MPa.
The beam has a constant moment of inertia I = 2500000 mm^4.
A downward point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end.
For the purpose of calculating strain energy, assume the bending moment M(x) is constant over the full span and equal to the peak value M = P L.
Compute the vertical deflection at the free end using Castigliano’s theorem.
The bending strain energy U is given by the integral U = ∫[0→L] M(x)^2/(2EI) dx.
First, set up the integral for U using the specified constant bending moment M = P L.
Then, integrate U with respect to x from 0 to L.
Finally, differentiate the resulting expression for U with respect to P to find the vertical deflection at the free end.
