Use N–mm–MPa units for all calculations.
Assume small strains.
Calculate the average shear stress τ_avg by dividing the shear force V by the area A.
Calculate the average shear strain γ_avg by dividing the average shear stress τ_avg by the shear modulus G.
Calculate the relative lateral displacement Δ by multiplying the average shear strain γ_avg by the thickness t.
Use A = 3000 mm^2.
Use t = 8 mm.
Use V = 12000 N.
