The unit system is N-mm-MPa.
The beam is simply supported with a span L = 2400 mm.
The supports are located at x=0 and x=L.
The supports are simple supports, allowing rotation.
The beam is made of structural steel with Young's Modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The moment of inertia I = 3500000 mm^4.
A point load P = 1200 N acts downward at x = 1200 mm in the real system.
The deflection at x = 1200 mm is to be computed.
The unit-load (virtual work) method for beam deflection is to be used.
The formula δ = ∫[0→L] (M(x)·m(x))/(EI) dx is to be used.
M(x) is the real bending moment diagram.
m(x) is the bending moment diagram due to a unit load applied at x = 1200 mm.
The unit load in the auxiliary system is a unit force applied downward at x = 1200 mm.
The supports for the auxiliary system are simple supports, allowing rotation.
The final output should be the numerical value of the deflection.
