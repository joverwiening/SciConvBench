The analysis uses N-mm-MPa units.
The material behavior is assumed to be linear elastic and isotropic.
The brass rod has an initial diameter d0 = 30 mm and a length L = 500 mm.
An axial tensile load P = 25000 N is applied through the centroid.
The Young's Modulus E = 105000 MPa.
Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.34 is a material reference.
Plane strain is assumed in the two transverse directions, resulting in zero lateral strain.
The average axial normal stress is computed.
The axial strain is computed.
The lateral (diametral) strain is computed.
The change in diameter Δd of the rod is computed.
