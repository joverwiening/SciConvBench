The calculation uses N-mm-MPa units.
The material behavior is assumed to be linear elastic and isotropic.
The initial cross-sectional area of the rod is calculated from its initial diameter.
The average axial normal stress is computed by dividing the axial tensile load by the initial cross-sectional area.
The axial strain is computed by dividing the average axial normal stress by the Young's Modulus.
The lateral (diametral) strain is zero, as specified by the plane strain condition in the two transverse directions.
The change in diameter is computed by multiplying the lateral (diametral) strain by the initial diameter.
The Poisson's ratio is noted as a material reference but is not used in the calculation of lateral strain due to the plane strain assumption.
