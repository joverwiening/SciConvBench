The task is to compute the elongation of a vertical, prismatic, linearly elastic bar due to its self-weight.
The bar has a length L = 3000 mm, a constant cross-sectional area A = 300 mm^2, and hangs from a rigid support at its top end.
The material properties are modulus of elasticity E = 69000 MPa and material density ρ = 8900 kg/m^3.
Gravitational acceleration is g = 9.81 m/s^2.
The calculation must use N–mm–MPa units.
The elongation δ is to be calculated by integrating the distributed self-weight effect using the formula δ = ∫₀ᴸ P(x)/(A E) dx.
The axial force at a section x (measured downward from the top) is P(x) = ρ g A (L − x).
All unit conversions between m and mm must be handled carefully to ensure consistency with the N–mm–MPa unit system.
