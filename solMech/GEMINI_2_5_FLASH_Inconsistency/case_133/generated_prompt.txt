The simply supported beam AB has a span L of 6000 mm.
A concentrated vertical load P of 20000 N acts downward at 2000 mm from support A.
The internal shear force V and bending moment M are determined at a cross section located x = 4500 mm from support A.
The statics sign convention is used: positive shear V is upward on the left cut face, and positive bending moment M causes sagging (tension at the bottom fibers).
The shear force V is provided in N.
The bending moment M is provided in N·mm.
