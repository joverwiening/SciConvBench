The short prismatic bar has a rectangular cross section with width b = 30 mm and height h = 100 mm.
The internal resultant normal force is P = 50000 N (tension).
The internal resultant transverse shear force is V = 5000 N (acting parallel to the width b).
Assume uniform stress distributions for both normal and shear stresses.
Compute the average normal stress σ_avg on the cross section.
Compute the average shear stress τ_avg on the cross section.
Compute the magnitude of the maximum in-plane shear stress using the formula τ_max = sqrt((σ_avg/2)^2 + τ_avg^2).
