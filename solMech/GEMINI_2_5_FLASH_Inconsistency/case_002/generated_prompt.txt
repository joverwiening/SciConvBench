The analysis will use N–mm–MPa units.
The material behavior is linear-elastic.
The bar is made of structural steel with a modulus of elasticity E = 200000 MPa.
The bar consists of two segments.
Segment 1 has a length L1 = 400 mm and a diameter d1 = 16 mm.
Segment 2 has a length L2 = 800 mm and a diameter d2 = 40 mm.
The bar is fixed at its left end.
A tensile load P = 35000 N is applied at the right end.
The normal stress in each segment will be calculated based on the constant axial force and the varying cross-sectional areas of each segment.
The total elongation of the bar will be determined as the sum of the individual elongations of each segment.
