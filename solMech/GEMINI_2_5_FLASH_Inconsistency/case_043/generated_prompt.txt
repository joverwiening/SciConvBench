The unit system is N–mm–MPa.
A solid circular column has a diameter d = 20 mm.
The column has a length L = 2000 mm.
The column is made of structural steel with a Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The column has an effective length factor K = 1.0.
Euler buckling governs the critical load calculation.
The area moment of inertia I = π d^4/64 is used in the Euler buckling formula.
The Euler critical load P_cr is computed using the formula P_cr = (π^2 E I) / (K L)^2.
The allowable axial compressive load P_allow is determined using a factor of safety n = 2.0.
P_allow is calculated as P_cr / n.
