Use N-mm-MPa units for all calculations.

Compute the elongation of the bar.

The bar has a length L of 800 mm.
The bar is subjected to a constant axial tensile force P of 30000 N.
The material is structural steel with a Young's Modulus E of 200000 MPa.
The bar’s cross-sectional area A(x) varies linearly along its length according to A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L).
The variable x is measured from the left end of the bar.
The initial cross-sectional area A0 is 250 mm^2.
