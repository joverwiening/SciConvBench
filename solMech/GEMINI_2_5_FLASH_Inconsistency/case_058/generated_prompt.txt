Use N–mm–MPa units for all calculations.
Assume Euler buckling applies, meaning the column is slender and remains elastic.
The column is made of AISI 1020 steel.
The column has a length L = 2000 mm.
The column has a constant flexural rigidity EI.
The modulus of elasticity E = 200000 MPa.
The area moment of inertia I = 1200000 mm^4.
The end conditions are fixed–pinned.
The effective length factor K = 0.7.
Compute the Euler critical buckling load P_cr using the formula P_cr = π^2 E I / (K L)^2.
Report the calculated P_cr value in Newtons (N).
Report the calculated P_cr value in kilonewtons (kN).
