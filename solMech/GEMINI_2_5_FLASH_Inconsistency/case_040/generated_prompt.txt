The units for the calculation are N-mm-MPa.
The beam has a span L = 3000 mm.
The beam material has a Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The beam has a constant moment of inertia I = 1500000 mm^4.
A downward point load P = 2000 N is applied at midspan.
Define the bending moment M(x) piecewise over the span 0≤x≤L.
Calculate the total bending strain energy U by integrating M(x)^2/(2EI) over the full span.
Apply Castigliano’s theorem by taking the partial derivative of the total strain energy U with respect to the load P.
Compute the numerical value of the vertical deflection at midspan.
