A simply supported beam has a span L = 2500 mm.
The beam is made of structural steel with a modulus of elasticity E = 200000 MPa.
The beam has a constant moment of inertia I = 3000000 mm^4.
A point load P = 1000 N is applied downward at midspan.
Compute the vertical deflection at midspan using Castigliano’s theorem based on bending strain energy.

Calculate the strain energy U by integrating M^2/(2EI) over the span.
