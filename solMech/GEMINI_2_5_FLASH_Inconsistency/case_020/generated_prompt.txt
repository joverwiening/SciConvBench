Use N-mm-MPa units for all calculations and results.
A solid circular shaft has a diameter of 35 mm and a total length of 1200 mm.
The shaft is fixed at x = 0 mm.
A torque T1 = 200000 N·mm is applied at the free end x = 1200 mm in the positive sense.
A torque T2 = -250000 N·mm is applied at x = 800 mm in the negative sense.
The material is structural steel with a shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.
The shaft is divided into two segments: 0 mm to 800 mm and 800 mm to 1200 mm.
Construct the internal torque for each segment based on equilibrium.
Compute the maximum shear stress in each segment.
Compute the angle of twist at x = 1200 mm relative to the fixed end by summing the twist of each segment.
