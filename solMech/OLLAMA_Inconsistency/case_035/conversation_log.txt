Q: Determine the critical load required for safety as Pcr = n × P = 2.5 × 25000 N = 62500 N.  
Use Euler buckling for a column with effective length factor K = 2.0 and length L = 2200 mm: Pcr = π² E I / (K L)².  
Express the second moment of area for a solid circular section as I = π d⁴ / 64.  
Substitute I into the Euler formula to obtain Pcr = (π³ E d⁴) / [64 (K L)²].  
Solve for the diameter: d⁴ = Pcr × 64 (K L)² / (π³ E).  
Insert the values Pcr = 62500 N, K L = 2.0 × 2200 mm = 4400 mm, E = 69000 MPa, and compute d⁴ ≈ 3.62 × 10⁷ mm⁴.  
Take the fourth root to obtain the minimum required diameter d ≈ 78 mm.  
Specify the column diameter as at least 78 mm to satisfy the safety factor against Euler buckling.
A: The required critical load is  

\[
P_{cr}=n\,P = 2.5 \times 25000\;\text{N}=62500\;\text{N}
\]

Using Euler buckling for a solid circular column  

\[
P_{cr}= \frac{\pi^{2}EI}{(KL)^{2}},\qquad 
I=\frac{\pi d^{4}}{64}
\]

\[
d^{4}= \frac{P_{cr}\;64\;(KL)^{2}}{\pi^{3}E}
\]

Insert the data  

* \(K L = 2.0 \times 2200\;\text{mm}=4400\;\text{mm}\)  
* \(E = 69000\;\text{MPa}=69000\;\text{N/mm}^{2}\)  

\[
d^{4}= \frac{62500 \times 64 \times (4400)^{2}}{\pi^{3}\times 69000}
      \approx 3.6 \times 10^{7}\;\text{mm}^{4}
\]

\[
d = \left(3.6 \times 10^{7}\right)^{1/4} \approx 7.8 \times 10^{1}\;\text{mm}
     \approx 78\;\text{mm}
\]

**Minimum required diameter:** **\(d \ge 78\; \text{mm}\)**.
