Use the angle‑of‑twist formula φ = TL/(J G) with φ expressed in degrees as given.  
Express the polar moment of inertia for a solid circular shaft as J = π d⁴/32.  
Substitute J into the twist equation to obtain φ = 32 T L/(π d⁴ G).  
Rearrange to solve for the shaft diameter: d = (32 T L/(π φ G))^(1/4).  
Insert the given values: T = 600 000 N·mm, L = 800 mm, G = 76923.1 MPa, φ = 1.5°.  
Compute d⁴ = 32 × 600 000 × 800 / (π × 1.5 × 76923.1) ≈ 4.238 × 10⁵ mm⁴.  
Take the fourth root: d ≈ (4.238 × 10⁵)^(1/4) ≈ 25.5 mm.  
State the result: The minimum required shaft diameter is approximately 25.5 mm.
