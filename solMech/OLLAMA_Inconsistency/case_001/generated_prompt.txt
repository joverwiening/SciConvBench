Determine the cross‑sectional area of segment 1 as A₁ = π d₁² / 4 = π (25 mm)² / 4 ≈ 490.9 mm².  
Determine the cross‑sectional area of segment 2 as A₂ = π d₂² / 4 = π (35 mm)² / 4 ≈ 962.1 mm².  
Compute the normal stress in segment 1 as σ₁ = P / A₁ = 30 000 N / 490.9 mm² ≈ 61.1 MPa.  
Compute the normal stress in segment 2 as σ₂ = P / A₂ = 30 000 N / 962.1 mm² ≈ 31.2 MPa.  
Calculate the axial elongation of segment 1 as δ₁ = σ₁ L₁ / E = 61.1 MPa × 300 mm / 200 000 MPa ≈ 0.0917 mm.  
Calculate the axial elongation of segment 2 as δ₂ = σ₂ L₂ / E = 31.2 MPa × 1000 mm / 200 000 MPa ≈ 0.156 mm.  
Obtain the total elongation of the bar as δ_total = δ₁ + δ₂ ≈ 0.0917 mm + 0.156 mm ≈ 0.248 mm.
