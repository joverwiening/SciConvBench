Determine the minimum required diameter d of the solid circular shaft as follows:

1. Use the angle‑of‑twist formula in degrees: φ = (T L) / (J G), where φ is the allowable twist angle in degrees, T is the applied torque, L is the shaft length, G is the shear modulus, and J is the polar moment of inertia of a solid circular section.

2. For a solid circular shaft, J = π d⁴ / 32.

3. Substitute J into the twist formula and solve for d:
   φ_allow = (T L) / ((π d⁴ / 32) G)

   Rearranged:
   d⁴ = (32 T L) / (π G φ_allow)

   Therefore:
   d = [ (32 T L) / (π G φ_allow) ]^(1/4)

4. Insert the given values (using N·mm for torque, mm for length, MPa for G, and degrees for φ_allow):
   - T = 800 000 N·mm
   - L = 1200 mm
   - G = 77 519.4 MPa
   - φ_allow = 1.0°

5. Compute the diameter:
   d = [ (32 × 800 000 N·mm × 1200 mm) / (π × 77 519.4 MPa × 1.0) ]^(1/4)

6. Perform the calculation and round the result to a suitable precision (e.g., two decimal places). The resulting d is the minimum required shaft diameter that satisfies the angle‑of‑twist limit.
