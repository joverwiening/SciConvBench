Determine the maximum deflection at the free end of the cantilever beam using the point‑load approximation: replace the uniform load w = 1.5 N/mm over the span L = 1000 mm by its resultant load P = w L = 1500 N acting at the midspan (a = L/2 = 500 mm).  
Compute the deflection at the free end with the Euler–Bernoulli formula for a cantilever with a point load at distance a from the fixed support:  

δ_max = (P a² (3L – a)) / (6 E I).  

Substituting P = 1500 N, a = 500 mm, L = 1000 mm, E = 200 000 MPa (200 000 N/mm²), and I = 2 000 000 mm⁴ gives δ_max = 0.3906 mm (≈ 0.391 mm).  

Determine the slope at the free end with the Euler–Bernoulli formula for the same loading condition:  

θ_free = (P a²) / (2 E I).  

Substituting the same values gives θ_free = 4.6875 × 10⁻⁴ rad (≈ 0.000469 rad).  

Report the results in N‑mm‑MPa units: maximum deflection ≈ 0.391 mm and slope at the free end ≈ 4.69 × 10⁻⁴ rad.
