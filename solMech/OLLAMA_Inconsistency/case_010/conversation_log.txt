Q: Should the tensile load be divided equally between the two bars, or should the load distribution be calculated based on the bars’ differing stiffnesses while maintaining the same elongation?
A: The load should be distributed according to the bars’ stiffnesses (i.e., based on their axial rigidity EA) while enforcing the compatibility condition that both bars have the same elongation.
