The axial rigidity of bar 1 is k₁ = (A₁·E₁)/L = (150 mm² × 200 000 MPa)/800 mm = 37 500 N/mm.  
The axial rigidity of bar 2 is k₂ = (A₂·E₂)/L = (200 mm² × 110 000 MPa)/800 mm = 27 500 N/mm.  

Because the bars are perfectly bonded, they share a common elongation δ and the forces satisfy P₁ = k₁·δ, P₂ = k₂·δ with P₁ + P₂ = 30 000 N.  

The common elongation is δ = P_total/(k₁ + k₂) = 30 000 N / 65 000 N/mm = 0.462 mm.  

The force carried by bar 1 is P₁ = k₁·δ = 37 500 N/mm × 0.462 mm = 17 308 N (≈ 17.3 kN).  
The force carried by bar 2 is P₂ = k₂·δ = 27 500 N/mm × 0.462 mm = 12 692 N (≈ 12.7 kN).  

The axial stress in bar 1 is σ₁ = P₁/A₁ = 17 308 N / 150 mm² = 115 MPa.  
The axial stress in bar 2 is σ₂ = P₂/A₂ = 12 692 N / 200 mm² = 63 MPa.  

Thus, the bars experience a common elongation of 0.462 mm, with bar 1 carrying about 17.3 kN at 115 MPa and bar 2 carrying about 12.7 kN at 63 MPa.
