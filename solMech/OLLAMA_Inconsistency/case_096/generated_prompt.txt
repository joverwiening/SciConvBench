Use Euler–Bernoulli beam theory with N–mm–MPa units.  
Model the uniformly distributed load w = 1.0 N/mm over the 2000 mm span by its resultant force P = w L = 2000 N applied at the midspan (x = L/2 = 1000 mm).  
Compute the slope at the free end (x = L) using θ(L) = P a² / (2 E I), where a = L/2.  
Compute the deflection at the free end using δ(L) = P a² (3L – a) / (6 E I).  
Substitute E = 200000 MPa, I = 1500000 mm⁴, L = 2000 mm, a = 1000 mm, and P = 2000 N.  
The resulting slope at the free end is θ(L) = 0.00333 rad.  
The resulting maximum deflection at the free end is δ(L) = 5.56 mm.
