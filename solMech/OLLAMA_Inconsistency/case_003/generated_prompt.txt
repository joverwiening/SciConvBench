Use the given axial load P = 20000 N and the cross‑sectional areas of the two segments to compute the normal stress in each segment as σ = P / A.

Segment 1: diameter d1 = 30 mm → area A1 = π d1² / 4 = 225 π mm² ≈ 706.86 mm².  
Normal stress σ1 = 20000 N / 706.86 mm² ≈ 28.30 MPa.

Segment 2: diameter d2 = 25 mm → area A2 = π d2² / 4 = 156.25 π mm² ≈ 490.87 mm².  
Normal stress σ2 = 20000 N / 490.87 mm² ≈ 40.73 MPa.

Compute the axial elongation of each segment using linear‑elastic relation ΔL = (σ / E) L, with E = 69000 MPa.

Segment 1: ΔL1 = (28.30 MPa / 69000 MPa) × 500 mm ≈ 0.205 mm.  
Segment 2: ΔL2 = (40.73 MPa / 69000 MPa) × 600 mm ≈ 0.354 mm.

Total elongation of the stepped bar is the sum of the segment elongations:  
ΔLtotal = ΔL1 + ΔL2 ≈ 0.205 mm + 0.354 mm = 0.559 mm.
