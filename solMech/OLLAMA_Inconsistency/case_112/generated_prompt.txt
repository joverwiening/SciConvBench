Calculate the maximum shear stress at the outer surface and the angle of twist for the given hollow circular shaft using the thin‑walled closed‑tube approximation.

1. Compute the wall thickness: t = (dₒ – dᵢ) / 2.  
2. Determine the mean radius: rₘ = (dₒ + dᵢ) / 4.  
3. Calculate the mean‑area (mid‑surface area) of the tube: Aₘ = 2 π rₘ t.  
4. Obtain the shear flow from the applied torque: q = T / (2 Aₘ).  
5. Compute the maximum shear stress at the outer surface: τ_max = q / t = T / (2 Aₘ t).  
6. Evaluate the polar moment of inertia using the exact formula: J = π (dₒ⁴ – dᵢ⁴) / 32.  
7. Calculate the angle of twist over the shaft length: θ = (T L) / (J G).  

All calculations must be performed in N‑mm‑MPa units, using G = 77519.4 MPa, dₒ = 60 mm, dᵢ = 44 mm, L = 1200 mm, and T = 600 000 N·mm. The final results should be reported as τ_max in MPa and θ in radians (or degrees, if preferred, with the conversion indicated).
