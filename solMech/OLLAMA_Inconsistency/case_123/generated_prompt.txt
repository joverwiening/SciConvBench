Use a solid circular brass shaft with diameter d = 30 mm, length L = 1200 mm, and applied torque T = 400 000 N·mm.  
Take the shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.  
Compute the polar moment of inertia J = π d⁴ / 32 = 7.952 × 10⁴ mm⁴.  
Set the outer radius c = d / 2 = 15 mm.  
Calculate the maximum shear stress τ_max = T c / J = 6.0 × 10⁶ N·mm² / 7.952 × 10⁴ mm⁴ = 75.4 MPa.  
Determine the angle of twist in radians φ_rad = T L / (J G) = 4.8 × 10⁸ N·mm² / 3.1156 × 10⁹ N·mm² = 0.154 rad.  
Convert φ to degrees: φ = φ_rad × (180 / π) = 0.154 rad × 57.2958 = 8.8 degrees.
