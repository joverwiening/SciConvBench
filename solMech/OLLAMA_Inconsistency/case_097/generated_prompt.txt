Use the given beam data: length L = 1500 mm, modulus E = 200000 MPa, second moment of area I = 2500000 mm^4, and point load P = 500 N applied at the free end.  
Assume the bending moment is the constant peak value M = P·L over the entire span for the purpose of forming the strain energy.  
Form the bending strain energy as U = ∫₀ᴸ M²/(2EI) dx = (M² L)/(2EI).  
Substitute M = P·L to obtain U = (P² L³)/(2EI).  
Apply Castigliano’s theorem: the vertical deflection at the free end δ = ∂U/∂P = (P L³)/(E I).  
Insert the numerical values (keeping N, mm, and MPa consistent) to compute δ = (500 N · 1500³ mm³)/(200000 N/mm² · 2500000 mm⁴) = 3.375 mm downward.
