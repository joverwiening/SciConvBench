Use N–mm–MPa units.

A cantilever beam of length L = 1500 mm (fixed at x = 0, free at x = L) is made of AISI 1020 steel with E = 200000 MPa and constant I = 2500000 mm^4. A downward point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end.

Using Castigliano’s theorem with bending strain energy U = ∫[0→L] M(x)^2/(2EI) dx, compute the vertical deflection at the free end. (Do not use a memorized deflection formula; set up M(x), integrate, then differentiate U with respect to P.)
