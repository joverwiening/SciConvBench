Compute the axial force distribution using P(x)=ρ·g·A·(L−x) with ρ=7850 kg/m³, g=9.81 m/s², A=200 mm² (converted to 2×10⁻⁴ m²), and (L−x) expressed in meters (multiply mm by 10⁻³).  
Convert the axial force to newtons, then obtain the axial strain at each section as ε(x)=P(x)/(A·E), where E=69000 MPa (N/mm²) and A is in mm².  
Integrate the strain over the bar length: δ=∫₀ᴸ ε(x) dx = [ρ·g·A·10⁻³/(A·E)]·∫₀ᴸ (L−x) dx = [ρ·g·A·10⁻³/(A·E)]·(L²/2).
