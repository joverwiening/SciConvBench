Use Euler–Bernoulli beam theory for a simply supported beam.  
Compute the resultant of the uniform load: R = w L = 0.3 N/mm × 3000 mm = 900 N.  
Calculate the midspan deflection due to a point load at midspan using δ = (P L³) / (48 E I).  
For the uniform‑load contribution use P = R = 900 N: δ₁ = (900 × 3000³) / (48 × 200000 × 3000000) = 0.84375 mm.  
For the actual point load P = 1200 N: δ₂ = (1200 × 3000³) / (48 × 200000 × 3000000) = 1.125 mm.  
Superpose the two deflections: total midspan deflection δ = δ₁ + δ₂ = 0.84375 mm + 1.125 mm = 1.96875 mm (≈ 1.97 mm downward).
