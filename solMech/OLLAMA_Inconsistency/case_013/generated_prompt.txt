Use Euler–Bernoulli beam theory for a simply supported beam.  
Compute the deflection due to the point load at midspan as δ_P = P L³ / (48 E I).  
Replace the uniform distributed load by its resultant w L acting at midspan and compute its deflection as δ_w = (w L) L³ / (48 E I) = w L⁴ / (48 E I).  
Superpose the two contributions: δ_total = (P L³ + w L⁴) / (48 E I).  
Substitute the given data: L = 3000 mm, P = 3000 N, w = 0.3 N/mm, E = 200000 MPa, I = 2000000 mm⁴.  
Calculate δ_total = (3000 × 3000³ + 0.3 × 3000⁴) / (48 × 200000 × 2000000) ≈ 5.48 mm downward.
