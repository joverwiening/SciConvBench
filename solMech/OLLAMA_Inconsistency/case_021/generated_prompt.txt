Compute the polar moment of inertia for each segment:  
J = π d⁴ / 32.  

For segment AB (d₁ = 20 mm): J₁ ≈ 1.5708 × 10⁴ mm⁴.  
For segment BC (d₂ = 30 mm): J₂ ≈ 7.9522 × 10⁴ mm⁴.  

Compute the shear stress in each segment using the full applied torque T = 300 000 N·mm:  
τ = T c / J, where c = d/2.  

Segment AB: c₁ = 10 mm, τ₁ ≈ 300 000 × 10 / 1.5708 × 10⁴ ≈ 191 MPa.  
Segment BC: c₂ = 15 mm, τ₂ ≈ 300 000 × 15 / 7.9522 × 10⁴ ≈ 57 MPa.  

The maximum shear stress is τ₁ ≈ 191 MPa in segment AB; segment AB governs.  

Compute the angle of twist for each segment:  
θ = T L / (J G), with G = 25 939.8 MPa.  

Segment AB: L₁ = 300 mm, θ₁ ≈ 300 000 × 300 / (1.5708 × 10⁴ × 25 939.8) ≈ 0.221 rad ≈ 12.7°.  
Segment BC: L₂ = 500 mm, θ₂ ≈ 300 000 × 500 / (7.9522 × 10⁴ × 25 939.8) ≈ 0.073 rad ≈ 4.2°.  

Total angle of twist at C relative to A: θ_total = θ₁ + θ₂ ≈ 0.294 rad ≈ 16.8°.  

All calculations use N·mm for torque, mm for lengths and diameters, and MPa for stress and shear modulus.
