Q: Should the applied torque of 300 000 N·mm be divided between segments AB and BC in proportion to their torsional stiffnesses (so that the sum of the two segment torques equals 300 000 N·mm), or should each segment experience the full 300 000 N·mm torque?
A: Each segment experiences the full 300 000 N·mm torque.
