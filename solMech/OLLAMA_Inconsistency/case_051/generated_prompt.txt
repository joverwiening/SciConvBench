Compute the cross‑sectional area A = b h = 50 mm × 80 mm = 4000 mm².  
Compute the average shear stress V/A = 20000 N / 4000 mm² = 5 MPa.  
Multiply by 1.5 to obtain the maximum transverse shear stress τ_max = 7.5 MPa.  
Compute the second moment of area I = b h³/12 = 50 mm × 80³ mm³ /12 = 2.133 × 10⁶ mm⁴.  
For y = 35 mm, the height of the strip above the level is h/2 – y = 40 mm – 35 mm = 5 mm.  
The area of that strip is A₁ = b × 5 mm = 250 mm².  
Its centroid is located at y_c = (y + h/2)/2 = (35 mm + 40 mm)/2 = 37.5 mm, so the distance from the neutral axis is 37.5 mm.  
Compute the first moment Q = A₁ × y_c = 250 mm² × 37.5 mm = 9375 mm³.  
Compute the shear stress at that point τ = V Q / (I b) = 20000 N × 9375 mm³ / (2.133 × 10⁶ mm⁴ × 50 mm) ≈ 1.76 MPa.
