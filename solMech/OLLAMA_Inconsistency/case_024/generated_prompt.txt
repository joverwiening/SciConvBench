Use Euler–Bernoulli beam theory for a cantilever beam with a uniform distributed load w.  
The slope at the free end (x = L) is given by θ(L) = w L³ / (6 E I).  
The vertical deflection at the free end is given by δ(L) = w L⁴ / (8 E I).  
Substitute the given data: L = 1500 mm, w = 1.5 N/mm, E = 200000 MPa, I = 1500000 mm⁴.  
Compute L³ = 3.375 × 10⁹ mm³ and L⁴ = 5.0625 × 10¹² mm⁴.  
Compute E I = 200000 N/mm² × 1500000 mm⁴ = 3.0 × 10¹¹ N·mm².  
Calculate the slope: θ = (1.5 N/mm × 3.375 × 10⁹ mm³) / (6 × 3.0 × 10¹¹ N·mm²) = 0.0028125 rad.  
Calculate the deflection: δ = (1.5 N/mm × 5.0625 × 10¹² mm⁴) / (8 × 3.0 × 10¹¹ N·mm²) = 3.1640625 mm.  
Therefore, the maximum deflection at the free end is 3.164 mm downward, and the slope at the free end is 0.00281 rad.
