Q: Should the auxiliary (virtual) system apply a unit vertical force at x = 900 mm (the usual unit‑load method) or a unit moment at that point as you described?
A: Apply a unit vertical (downward) force at x = 900 mm in the virtual system. The auxiliary moment diagram m(x) is generated by that unit load, not by a unit moment.
