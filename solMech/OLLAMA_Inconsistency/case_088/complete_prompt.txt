Use N–mm–MPa units.

A prismatic bar of constant cross-sectional area A = 500 mm^2 is loaded axially along its length. The bar is segmented by three points: A (left end), B, C, and D (right end). The lengths are AB = 300 mm, BC = 600 mm, and CD = 300 mm. The bar is made of structural steel with E = 200000 MPa.

Axial forces are applied at the points as follows (positive to the right): at B, a force of 18000 N; at C, a force of -12000 N; and at D, a force of 10000 N. The left end A is fixed (u_A = 0).

Determine the internal axial force in each segment (AB, BC, CD), the normal stress in each segment, and the displacement of point D relative to A.
