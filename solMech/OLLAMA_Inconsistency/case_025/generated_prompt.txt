Set the bending moment over the entire beam length equal to the constant peak value M = P·L.  
Form the strain energy as U = ∫₀ᴸ (M²)/(2 E I) dx = ∫₀ᴸ (P² L²)/(2 E I) dx.

Apply Castigliano’s theorem: the vertical deflection at the free end δ = ∂U/∂P = (P L³)/(E I).
