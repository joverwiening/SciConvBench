Determine the axial force in each bar using the proportion of axial stiffnesses:  
Force in bar 1 = total load × (E1 A1)/(E1 A1 + E2 A2).  
Force in bar 2 = total load × (E2 A2)/(E1 A1 + E2 A2).  

Compute the stresses:  
Stress in bar 1 = force in bar 1 divided by A1.  
Stress in bar 2 = force in bar 2 divided by A2.  

Calculate the common elongation (δ) using compatibility:  
δ = (force in bar i × L)/(Ei Ai) for either bar, since both give the same value.  

Report the results in N, MPa, and mm respectively.
