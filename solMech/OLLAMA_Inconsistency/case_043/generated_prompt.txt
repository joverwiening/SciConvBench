Compute the area moment of inertia for the solid circular column: I = π d⁴/64.  
Substitute d = 20 mm to obtain I = π (20 mm)⁴/64 = 2500 π mm⁴ ≈ 7 854 mm⁴.  

Calculate the Euler critical load using the formula P_cr = π² E I / (K L)².  
Insert E = 200 000 MPa, I ≈ 7 854 mm⁴, K = 1.0, and L = 2000 mm:  
P_cr = π² (200 000 N/mm²) (7 854 mm⁴) / (2000 mm)² ≈ 3 876 N.  

Determine the allowable axial compressive load with a safety factor n = 2.0:  
P_allow = P_cr / n = 3 876 N / 2 ≈ 1 938 N.  

Thus, the Euler critical load is approximately 3.88 kN and the allowable load is approximately 1.94 kN.
