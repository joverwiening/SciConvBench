Q: Should the Euler critical load be calculated using the polar moment of inertia J = π d⁴/32 as stated, or the conventional area moment of inertia I = π d⁴/64 for a circular column?
A: Make a reasonable assumption based on best practices: for Euler buckling of a solid circular column, use the conventional area moment of inertia \(I = \pi d^{4}/64\).
