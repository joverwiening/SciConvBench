Determine the maximum vertical deflection of the simply supported steel beam (span L = 4500 mm, uniform load w = 0.3 N/mm) using Euler–Bernoulli beam theory with modulus E = 200000 MPa and second moment of area I = 2000000 mm⁴. Impose the additional condition that the end slopes are zero because the loading is symmetric. Compute the deflection curve, locate the point where the deflection is greatest, and report the magnitude of that maximum deflection in millimetres. Also calculate the slope (rotation) of the beam at the left support (x = 0) and give the result in radians. Use N‑mm‑MPa consistent units throughout.
