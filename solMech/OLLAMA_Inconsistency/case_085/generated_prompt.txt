Calculate the cross‑sectional area of segment 1 using A₁ = π · (d₁/2)² = π · (12 mm / 2)² ≈ 113.10 mm².  
Calculate the cross‑sectional area of segment 2 using A₂ = π · (d₂/2)² = π · (30 mm / 2)² ≈ 706.86 mm².  
Determine the normal stress in segment 1 as σ₁ = P / A₁ = 35000 N / 113.10 mm² ≈ 309.5 MPa.  
Determine the normal stress in segment 2 as σ₂ = P / A₂ = 35000 N / 706.86 mm² ≈ 49.6 MPa.  
Compute the axial elongation of segment 1 as ΔL₁ = P · L₁ / (A₁ · E) = 35000 N · 300 mm / (113.10 mm² · 200000 MPa) ≈ 0.464 mm.  
Compute the axial elongation of segment 2 as ΔL₂ = P · L₂ / (A₂ · E) = 35000 N · 800 mm / (706.86 mm² · 200000 MPa) ≈ 0.198 mm.  
Add the two elongations to obtain the total elongation of the bar: ΔL_total = ΔL₁ + ΔL₂ ≈ 0.662 mm.
