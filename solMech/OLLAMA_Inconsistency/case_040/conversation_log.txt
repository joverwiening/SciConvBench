Q: Which method should be used for the bending moment distribution in the strain‑energy calculation: a constant moment equal to the maximum value \(M = PL/4\) over the entire span, or the actual piecewise moment diagram ( \(M(x)=Px/2\) for \(0\le x\le L/2\) and \(M(x)=P(L-x)/2\) for \(L/2\le x\le L\) )?
A: Use the actual piecewise bending‑moment diagram:

\[
M(x)=\begin{cases}
\displaystyle \frac{P\,x}{2}, & 0\le x\le \frac{L}{2}\\[6pt]
\displaystyle \frac{P\,(L-x)}{2}, & \frac{L}{2}\le x\le L
\end{cases}
\]

and integrate \(U=\int_0^{L}\frac{M(x)^2}{2EI}\,dx\) in Castigliano’s theorem.

Q: Define the beam span L as 3000 mm, the Young’s modulus E as 200000 MPa, the second moment of area I as 1500000 mm⁴, and the applied vertical point load P as 2000 N at the midspan.  
Express the bending moment distribution piecewise: for 0 ≤ x ≤ L/2, M(x) = P·x / 2; for L/2 ≤ x ≤ L, M(x) = P·(L − x) / 2.  
Form the bending strain‑energy expression U = ∫₀ᴸ [M(x)² / (2 E I)] dx.  
Substitute the piecewise M(x) and evaluate the integral: U = P² L³ / (96 E I).  
Apply Castigliano’s theorem: the vertical deflection at the load point δ = ∂U/∂P = P L³ / (48 E I).  
Insert the numerical values: δ = (2000 N · 3000³ mm³) / [48 · 200000 N/mm² · 1500000 mm⁴] = 3.75 mm downward.  
A: The vertical deflection at the mid‑span is  

\[
\delta \;=\; \frac{P\,L^{3}}{48\,E\,I}
\]

Substituting the given data (N–mm–MPa units):

\[
\delta \;=\; \frac{2000\;\text{N}\;(3000\;\text{mm})^{3}}
{48\;(200000\;\text{MPa})\;(1500000\;\text{mm}^{4})}
\;=\; 3.75\;\text{mm (downward)}.
\]
