Define the beam span L as 3000 mm, the Young’s modulus E as 200000 MPa, the second moment of area I as 1500000 mm⁴, and the vertical point load P as 2000 N applied at midspan of a simply supported beam.  
Use the piecewise bending‑moment diagram M(x)=P·x/2 for 0 ≤ x ≤ L/2 and M(x)=P·(L−x)/2 for L/2 ≤ x ≤ L.  
Form the bending strain‑energy expression U = ∫₀ᴸ [M(x)² / (2 E I)] dx.  
Evaluate the integral to obtain U = P² L³ / (96 E I).  
Apply Castigliano’s theorem: the vertical deflection at midspan δ = ∂U/∂P = P L³ / (48 E I).  
Substitute the given data in N‑mm‑MPa units: δ = (2000 N · (3000 mm)³) / [48 · 200000 MPa · 1500000 mm⁴] = 3.75 mm downward.
