Use Euler‑Bernoulli beam theory for a simply supported beam.  
Treat the uniform distributed load w as a single resultant force equal to w L acting at midspan.  
Compute the midspan deflection for each point load using the standard formula  

δ = (F L³) / (48 E I)  

where F is the point load, L = 3500 mm, E = 200 000 MPa, and I = 3 000 000 mm⁴.  

1. Resultant of the uniform load: F₁ = w L = 0.3 N/mm × 3500 mm = 1050 N.  
2. Given point load: F₂ = 1200 N.  

Compute each contribution:  

δ₁ = (1050 N × 3500³ mm³) / (48 × 200 000 N/mm² × 3 000 000 mm⁴)  
δ₂ = (1200 N × 3500³ mm³) / (48 × 200 000 N/mm² × 3 000 000 mm⁴)  

Add the two deflections:  

δ_total = δ₁ + δ₂ = ((1050 + 1200) N × 3500³) / (48 × 200 000 × 3 000 000)  

Evaluate numerically:  

δ_total ≈ 3.35 mm downward.
