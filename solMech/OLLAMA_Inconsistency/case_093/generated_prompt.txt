Use Euler‑Bernoulli beam theory for a cantilever with a point load at the free end.  
Take the beam length L = 800 mm, load P = 500 N, Young’s modulus E = 69000 MPa (≈ 69000 N/mm²), and second moment of area I = 800000 mm⁴.  
Compute the flexural rigidity EI = E·I = 5.52 × 10¹⁰ N·mm².  
Calculate the slope at the free end using θ(L) = –P·L² / (2 EI).  
Calculate the vertical deflection at the free end using δ(L) = –P·L³ / (3 EI).  
The resulting slope is –2.90 × 10⁻³ rad (≈ –0.166°).  
The resulting vertical deflection is –1.55 mm (downward).
