Q: Should I assume the free‑end rotation is constrained to zero and use a constant bending moment = P·L over the entire span for the calculations, or should I apply the standard cantilever model where the moment varies linearly and the free end is free to rotate?
A: Use the standard cantilever model: the bending moment varies linearly from M = P·L at the fixed support to M = 0 at the free end, and the free‑end rotation is not constrained (it is determined by the beam’s elasticity).
