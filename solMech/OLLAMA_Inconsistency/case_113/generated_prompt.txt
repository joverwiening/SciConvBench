Use the outer diameter do = 50 mm, inner diameter di = 44 mm, wall thickness t = (do − di)/2 = 3 mm, shaft length L = 800 mm, applied torque T = 600 000 N·mm, shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa, and the polar moment of inertia J = π(do⁴ − di⁴)/32.

1. Compute the median radius Rm = (do/2 + di/2)/2 = (25 mm + 22 mm)/2 = 23.5 mm.  
2. Compute the mean area enclosed by the median line Aₘ = π Rm².  
3. Determine the shear flow for a thin‑walled closed tube: q = T / (2 Aₘ).  
4. Compute the maximum shear stress at the outer surface: τmax = q / t.  
5. Compute the polar moment of inertia: J = π(do⁴ − di⁴)/32.  
6. Determine the angle of twist over the length: θ = T L / (J G).  

The numerical results are:  
- Maximum shear stress at the outer surface τmax ≈ 57.7 MPa.  
- Angle of twist over the 800 mm length θ ≈ 0.025 rad.
