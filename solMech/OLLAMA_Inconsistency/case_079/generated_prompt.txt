Compute the second moment of area I = π d⁴/64 = π (30 mm)⁴/64 ≈ 3.98 × 10⁴ mm⁴.  
Set the distance from the neutral axis to the outer surface c = d/2 = 15 mm.  

(a) Compute the maximum bending normal stress σ = M c / I = (4.0 × 10⁶ N·mm)(15 mm) / 3.98 × 10⁴ mm⁴ ≈ 1.51 × 10³ MPa (≈ 1510 MPa).  

(b) Compute the polar moment of inertia J = π d⁴/32 = 2 I ≈ 7.95 × 10⁴ mm⁴.  
Compute the torsional shear stress τ = T c / J = (4.0 × 10⁵ N·mm)(15 mm) / 7.95 × 10⁴ mm⁴ ≈ 7.55 × 10¹ MPa (≈ 75.5 MPa).  

(c) Treat the surface stress state as plane stress with σₓ = σ, σ_y = 0, τ_xy = τ.  
Compute the principal stresses:  
σ₁,₂ = (σₓ+σ_y)/2 ± √[((σₓ−σ_y)/2)² + τ_xy²]  
= 755 MPa ± √[(755 MPa)² + (75.5 MPa)²] ≈ 1 514 MPa and –4 MPa.  

Compute the maximum in‑plane shear stress:  
τ_max = √[((σₓ−σ_y)/2)² + τ_xy²] ≈ 759 MPa.
