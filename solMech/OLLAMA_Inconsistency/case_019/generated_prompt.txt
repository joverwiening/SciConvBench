The shaft has a constant diameter d = 30 mm, length L = 1200 mm, and is fixed at x = 0.  
A torque T1 = 200 000 N·mm is applied at the free end x = L in the positive sense.  
A torque T2 = –200 000 N·mm is applied at x = a = 400 mm in the opposite sense.  
The material shear modulus is G = 76 923.1 MPa.  

The polar moment of inertia of the circular cross‑section is J = π d⁴/32 = 7.953 × 10⁴ mm⁴.  

Segment 0 → a (0 mm ≤ x ≤ 400 mm):  
The internal torque is T_int = T1 = 200 000 N·mm.  
The maximum shear stress is τ_max = T_int·c / J = 200 000 N·mm · 15 mm / 7.953 × 10⁴ mm⁴ = 37.7 MPa.  
The angle of twist over this segment is θ₁ = T_int·L₁ / (G·J) = 200 000 N·mm · 400 mm / (76 923.1 MPa · 7.953 × 10⁴ mm⁴) = 0.0131 rad (≈ 0.75°).  

Segment a → L (400 mm < x ≤ 1200 mm):  
The internal torque is T_int = T1 + T2 = 0 N·mm.  
The maximum shear stress is τ_max = 0 MPa.  
The angle of twist over this segment is θ₂ = 0 rad.  

The total angle of twist at the free end relative to the fixed end is θ_total = θ₁ + θ₂ = 0.0131 rad (≈ 0.75°).
