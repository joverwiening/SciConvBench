Use Euler‑Bernoulli beam theory for a cantilever of length L = 1200 mm fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L, made of AISI 1020 steel with Young’s modulus E = 200 000 MPa and constant second moment of area I = 2 000 000 mm⁴. Apply a downward point load P = 2000 N at the free end. Compute the slope at the free end using θ(L) = P L² / (2 E I). Compute the vertical deflection at the free end using δ(L) = P L³ / (3 E I). Report the slope in radians (and optionally in degrees) and the deflection in millimetres. The numerical results are: slope = 0.0036 rad (≈ 0.206°) and vertical deflection = 2.88 mm.
