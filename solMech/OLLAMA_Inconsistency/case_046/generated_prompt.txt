The rod has a cross‑sectional area A = π (25 mm)² / 4 ≈ 490.9 mm².  
The average normal stress is σ_avg = 20000 N / 490.9 mm² ≈ 40.8 MPa.  
The elastic normal strain is ε = σ_avg / E = 40.8 MPa / 200000 MPa ≈ 2.04 × 10⁻⁴ (dimensionless).  
The axial elongation of the 800 mm gage length is ΔL = ε · L₀ ≈ 2.04 × 10⁻⁴ × 800 mm ≈ 0.163 mm.  
Using the yield strength Sy = 250 MPa, the factor of safety is n = Sy / σ_avg = 250 MPa / 40.8 MPa ≈ 6.14.
