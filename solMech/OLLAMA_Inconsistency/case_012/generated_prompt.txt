Determine the minimum required height h for the simply supported rectangular beam.

Given data:
- Span L = 2000 mm
- Material: 6061‑T6 aluminum, Young’s modulus E = 69000 MPa
- Concentrated load at midspan P = 3000 N
- Width of rectangular cross‑section b = 30 mm
- Allowable midspan deflection δ_allow = 3 mm
- Use the point‑load deflection formula δ_max = P L³/(48 E I) with moment of inertia I = b h³/12
- Use N‑mm‑MPa unit system

Steps:
1. Substitute I into the deflection formula:
   δ_max = P L³ / (48 E (b h³/12)) = P L³ / (4 E b h³)
2. Impose the deflection limit:
   P L³ / (4 E b h³) ≤ δ_allow
3. Solve for h³:
   h³ ≥ P L³ / (4 E b δ_allow)
4. Insert the numerical values (all in N, mm, MPa):
   h³ ≥ (3000 N × 2000³ mm³) / (4 × 69000 MPa × 30 mm × 3 mm)
   h³ ≥ 2.4 × 10¹³ N·mm³ / 2.484 × 10⁷ N·mm
   h³ ≥ 9.66 × 10⁵ mm³
5. Take the cube root:
   h ≥ (9.66 × 10⁵)¹ᐟ³ mm ≈ 98.9 mm
6. Round up to the next whole millimetre for design safety:
   Minimum required height h_min = 99 mm

Specification:

- Use the point‑load deflection expression δ_max = P L³/(48 E I).
