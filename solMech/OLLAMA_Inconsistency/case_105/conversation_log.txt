Q: Should the beam be modeled as a fixed‑fixed beam (zero deflection and zero slope at both ends) or as a simply supported beam with only the deflection constrained to zero at the supports?
A: Model the beam as a simply supported beam—deflection is zero at the supports, but the slopes are not constrained (only the left‑support slope is to be computed).
