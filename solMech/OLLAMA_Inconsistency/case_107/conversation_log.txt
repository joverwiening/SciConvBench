Q: Should the strain‑energy integration use a constant bending moment equal to the maximum value \(M = PL/4\) over the entire span, or should it use the actual piecewise bending‑moment distribution for a simply supported beam with a central point load?
A: Use the actual piecewise bending‑moment distribution for the simply supported beam with the central point load, not a constant \(M = PL/4\) over the whole span. Define \(M(x)\) piecewise and integrate \(U = \int_0^L \frac{M(x)^2}{2EI}\,dx\).
