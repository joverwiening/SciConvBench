Use the Euler‑Bernoulli beam relation EI v″ = M(x) for a simply supported beam of span L = 2500 mm loaded by a vertical point load P = 2500 N at midspan.

1. The bending moment diagram is symmetric; the maximum deflection occurs at midspan (x = L/2).  
   For a central point load on a simply supported beam, the standard deflection formula is  
   δ_max = (P L³) / (48 E I).  

2. The slope at each support (x = 0 or x = L) is obtained from the slope expression for the same loading case:  
   θ_support = (P L²) / (16 E I).  

3. Insert the given data, using N‑mm‑MPa units (E = 200 000 MPa = 200 000 N/mm², I = 3 500 000 mm⁴):  

   - Compute L³: (2500 mm)³ = 15 625 000 000 mm³.  
   - Compute L²: (2500 mm)² = 6 250 000 mm².  

   - Maximum deflection:  
     δ_max = (2500 N × 15 625 000 000 mm³) / (48 × 200 000 N/mm² × 3 500 000 mm⁴)  
     = 3.90625 × 10¹³ / 3.36 × 10¹³ mm  
     ≈ 1.16 mm.  

   - Slope at each support:  
     θ_support = (2500 N × 6 250 000 mm²) / (16 × 200 000 N/mm² × 3 500 000 mm⁴)  
     = 1.5625 × 10¹⁰ / 1.12 × 10¹³ rad  
     ≈ 1.40 × 10⁻⁴ rad (≈ 0.008°).  

4. Report the results:  

   - The maximum vertical deflection at midspan is approximately 1.16 mm.  
   - The slope at each support is approximately 1.4 × 10⁻⁴ rad (0.008 degrees).
