Q: Should the beam be modeled as simply supported with zero deflection but non‑zero slope at the supports, or should the slope also be constrained to zero at the supports (i.e., a clamped condition)?
A: The beam should be modeled as simply supported: the vertical deflection is zero at the supports, but the slope is not constrained (i.e., it can be non‑zero). A clamped condition (zero slope) is not required.
