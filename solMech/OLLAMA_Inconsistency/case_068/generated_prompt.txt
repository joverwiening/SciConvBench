Compute the curvature κ using κ = M/(E·I).  
Substitute M = 3 000 000 N·mm, E = 200 000 MPa (N/mm²), and I = 4 000 000 mm⁴ to obtain κ = 3 000 000 / (200 000 × 4 000 000) = 3.75 × 10⁻⁶ mm⁻¹.  
Calculate the radius of curvature ρ as the reciprocal of κ: ρ = 1/κ = 1 / (3.75 × 10⁻⁶) = 2.6667 × 10⁵ mm.  
Report κ = 3.75 × 10⁻⁶ mm⁻¹ and ρ = 2.67 × 10⁵ mm.
