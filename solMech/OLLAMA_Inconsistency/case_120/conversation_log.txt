Q: Should the rod be considered fully constrained (zero net axial deformation) so that a thermal stress develops, or should we assume the thermal stress is zero despite the unyielding supports?
A: The rod should be treated as fully constrained (zero net axial deformation). Because the temperature rise tries to expand the rod but the supports prevent any change in length, a thermal stress develops. The stress will be compressive.
