Q: The problem states that the same axial force passes through both segments, but also that the normal stress should be identical in the two segments, which is contradictory because the segments have different cross‑sectional areas. Should the analysis enforce a common axial force (using the actual areas to compute the stresses), or should it enforce a common stress (which would imply different forces in the segments)?
A: Use the actual axial force P = 35 000 N, which is the same throughout the bar because the load is applied at the far end of a continuous member. Compute the normal stress in each segment from that common force and the segment’s own cross‑sectional area (σ = P/A). Do not enforce identical stresses; the stresses will differ because the areas differ.
