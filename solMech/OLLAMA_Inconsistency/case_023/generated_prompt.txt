Use Euler–Bernoulli beam theory with N‑mm‑MPa units.  
Treat the uniform distributed load w = 1.0 N/mm over the whole span L = 2500 mm as an equivalent point load P = w L = 2500 N applied at the midspan distance a = L/2 = 1250 mm from the fixed support.  

Compute the flexural rigidity:  
EI = E I = (200 000 MPa)(1 000 000 mm⁴) = 2.0 × 10¹¹ N·mm².  

Slope at the free end (x = L):  
θ(L) = P a² / (2 EI) = (2500 N)(1250 mm)² / (2 · 2.0 × 10¹¹ N·mm²) = 0.0097656 rad.  

Deflection at the free end (x = L):  
δ(L) = P a² (3L – a) / (6 EI) = (2500 N)(1250 mm)²(3·2500 mm – 1250 mm) / (6 · 2.0 × 10¹¹ N·mm²) = 20.35 mm downward.  

Report the results as:  
Maximum deflection at the free end = 20.35 mm (downward).
