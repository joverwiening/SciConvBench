Use the Euler–Bernoulli beam theory with small deflection and linear elastic behavior.  
Given: L = 800 mm, P = 2500 N, E = 69000 MPa ( = 69000 N/mm² ), I = 1 600 000 mm⁴.  

Compute the flexural rigidity: EI = E·I = 69000 N/mm² × 1 600 000 mm⁴ = 1.104 × 10¹¹ N·mm².  

The bending moment distribution for a cantilever with a tip load is M(x) = –P·(L – x).  

The slope at the free end (x = L) is  
θ(L) = P·L² / (2·EI) = (2500 N × 800² mm²) / (2 × 1.104 × 10¹¹ N·mm²) ≈ 0.00724 rad ≈ 0.415°.  

The vertical deflection at the free end is  
δ(L) = P·L³ / (3·EI) = (2500 N × 800³ mm³) / (3 × 1.104 × 10¹¹ N·mm²) ≈ 3.86 mm.  

Report: slope ≈ 0.00724 rad (or 0.415°) and vertical deflection ≈ 3.86 mm.
