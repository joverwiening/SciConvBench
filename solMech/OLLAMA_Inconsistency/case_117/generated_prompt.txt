Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system.  
Set the beam width b = 40 mm, height h = 80 mm, and internal shear force V = 25000 N.  

Compute the cross‑sectional area A = b · h = 40 mm × 80 mm = 3200 mm².  

Compute the maximum transverse shear stress assuming a uniform distribution:  
τ_max = 1.5 · V / A = 1.5 · 25000 N / 3200 mm² = 11.71875 N/mm² = 11.71875 MPa.  

Compute the second moment of area about the neutral axis:  
I = b · h³ / 12 = 40 mm · (80 mm)³ / 12 = 1 706 666.667 mm⁴.  

For the point located y = 20 mm above the neutral axis (y positive upward):  

- Height of the rectangle above that level = h/2 – y = 40 mm – 20 mm = 20 mm.  
- Area of the portion above the level Q_area = b · ( h/2 – y ) = 40 mm · 20 mm = 800 mm².  
- Distance from the neutral axis to the centroid of this area = y + (h/2 – y)/2 = 20 mm + 10 mm = 30 mm.  
- First moment of area Q = Q_area · centroid distance = 800 mm² · 30 mm = 24 000 mm³.  

Compute the shear stress at y = 20 mm using the shear formula:  
τ = V · Q / ( I · b ) = 25000 N · 24 000 mm³ / ( 1 706 666.667 mm⁴ · 40 mm ) = 8.795 N/mm² = 8.795 MPa.  

Report τ_max = 11.71875 MPa and τ at y = 20 mm = 8.795 MPa.
