Set the bending moment over the entire span equal to the peak value M = P L.  
Form the strain energy using Castigliano’s theorem:  
U = ∫₀ᴸ (M²)/(2 E I) dx = ∫₀ᴸ (P² L²)/(2 E I) dx = (P² L³)/(2 E I).  
Differentiate the strain energy with respect to the load P to obtain the vertical deflection at the free end:  
δ = ∂U/∂P = (P L³)/(E I).  
Substitute the given data (P = 500 N, L = 1500 mm, E = 200 000 MPa, I = 800 000 mm⁴):  
δ = (500 N × 1500³ mm³) / (200 000 N/mm² × 800 000 mm⁴) ≈ 10.55 mm.  
Therefore, the vertical deflection at the free end is approximately 10.55 mm.
