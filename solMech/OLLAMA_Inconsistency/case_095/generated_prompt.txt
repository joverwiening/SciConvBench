Determine the maximum shear stress in each shaft segment using τ = 16 T / (π d³) with T = 600 000 N·mm and d = 30 mm, giving τ ≈ 113 MPa for both segments; therefore neither segment governs because the stresses are equal.  
Compute the polar moment of inertia J = π d⁴ / 32 for d = 30 mm, giving J ≈ 7.953 × 10⁴ mm⁴.  
Calculate the angle of twist for segment AB: θ₁ = T L₁ / (J G) = (600 000 N·mm × 400 mm) / (7.953 × 10⁴ mm⁴ × 77519.4 N/mm²) ≈ 0.0389 rad (≈ 2.23°).  
Calculate the angle of twist for segment BC: θ₂ = T L₂ / (J G) = (600 000 N·mm × 700 mm) / (7.953 × 10⁴ mm⁴ × 77519.4 N/mm²) ≈ 0.0681 rad (≈ 3.90°).  
Add the two twists to obtain the total angle of twist at C relative to A: θ_total = θ₁ + θ₂ ≈ 0.107 rad (≈ 6.13°).
