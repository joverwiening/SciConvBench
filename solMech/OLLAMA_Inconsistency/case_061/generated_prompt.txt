The thermal strain for the temperature increase is α ΔT = 1 × 10⁻⁵ /°C × 65 °C = 6.5 × 10⁻⁴ (unitless).  
Because the rod’s total axial deformation is constrained to zero, the mechanical strain must be the negative of the free thermal strain, i.e., ε_mech = –6.5 × 10⁻⁴.  
The thermal stress that develops is σ = E · ε_mech = 200 000 MPa × (–6.5 × 10⁻⁴) = –130 MPa. The negative sign indicates a compressive stress.  
The cross‑sectional area of the rod is A = π d²/4 = π × (35 mm)²/4 ≈ 962.1 mm².  
The axial force carried by the rod is F = σ · A = (–130 MPa) × 962.1 mm² ≈ –1.25 × 10⁵ N, i.e., about –125 kN (compressive).
