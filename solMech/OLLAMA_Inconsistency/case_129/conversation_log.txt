Q: Determine the minimum required height h for the rectangular beam cross‑section.

1. Use the bending stress formula with c = h/2 as specified:  
   σ_max = M_max · (h/2) / (b h³/12).

2. Simplify the expression:  
   σ_max = 6 M_max / (b h²).

3. Impose the allowable stress condition:  
   σ_max ≤ σ_allow → 6 M_max / (b h²) ≤ σ_allow.

4. Solve for h:  
   h² ≥ 6 M_max / (b σ_allow).

5. Substitute the given values (using N·mm for moments and N/mm² for stress):  
   M_max = 3 000 000 N·mm, b = 40 mm, σ_allow = 90 N/mm².  

   h² ≥ (6 × 3 000 000) / (40 × 90) = 18 000 000 / 3 600 = 5 000 mm².

h \ge \sqrt{\frac{6M_{\max}}{b\,\sigma_{\text{allow}}}}
