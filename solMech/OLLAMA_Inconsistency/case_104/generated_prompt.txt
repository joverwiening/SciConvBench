Determine the maximum vertical deflection of the beam, the location where it occurs, and the slope at the left support.

Use Euler–Bernoulli beam theory with units N‑mm‑MPa. The beam is fixed at both ends, so the boundary conditions are v(0)=0, v′(0)=0, v(L)=0, v′(L)=0.

1. Compute the maximum deflection using the derived expression for a uniformly loaded beam with both ends fixed:  
   δ_max = w L⁴ / (384 E I).

2. Substitute the given data:  
   w = 0.7 N/mm, L = 2500 mm, E = 200 000 MPa (N/mm²), I = 2 000 000 mm⁴.  
   L⁴ = (2500)⁴ = 3.90625 × 10¹³ mm⁴.  
   Numerator = w L⁴ = 0.7 × 3.90625 × 10¹³ = 2.734375 × 10¹³ N·mm³.  
   Denominator = 384 E I = 384 × 200 000 × 2 000 000 = 1.536 × 10¹⁴ N·mm².  
   δ_max = 2.734375 × 10¹³ / 1.536 × 10¹⁴ = 0.178 mm (downward).

3. The maximum deflection occurs at the mid‑span, x = L / 2 = 1250 mm.

4. The slope at the left support is prescribed by the boundary condition to be zero:  
   v′(0) = 0 rad (or 0 mm/mm).
