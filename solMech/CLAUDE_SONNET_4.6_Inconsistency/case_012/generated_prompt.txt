Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.
Assume small deflection theory and Euler–Bernoulli beam theory.
The beam is simply supported with a span L = 2000 mm.
The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with elastic modulus E = 69000 MPa.
A concentrated load P = 3000 N is applied at midspan.
The cross section is rectangular with width b = 30 mm and unknown height h.
The maximum allowable midspan deflection is δ_allow = 3 mm.
Use the midspan point-load deflection formula: δ_max = P L³ / (48 E I).
Use the second moment of area formula for a rectangle: I = b h³ / 12.
Set δ_max equal to δ_allow and solve for the minimum required height h.
Substitute δ_allow = 3, P = 3000, L = 2000, E = 69000, b = 30 into the equation.
Compute I_required = P L³ / (48 E δ_allow).
Compute h_min = (12 I_required / b)^(1/3).
Report h_min in mm, rounding up to ensure the deflection limit is satisfied.
