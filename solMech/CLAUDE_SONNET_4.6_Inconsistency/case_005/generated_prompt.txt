Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The bar is prismatic with constant cross-sectional area A = 200 mm² and elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.

The bar has four points: A (left end, fixed), B, C, and D (right end, free).

Segment lengths are: AB = 700 mm, BC = 600 mm, CD = 500 mm.

The left end A is fixed, so displacement u_A = 0.

External axial forces applied (positive direction = rightward): at B, F_B = +18000 N; at C, F_C = −8000 N; at D, F_D = +20000 N.

No external force is applied at A other than the reaction; no force is applied at the free end beyond what is listed.

Use the free-body diagram / section-cut method to find the internal axial force in each segment.

To find the internal force in segment AB, cut the bar between A and B and sum forces on the left portion (from A to the cut). The reaction at A equals the negative of the sum of all applied forces, so R_A = −(18000 − 8000 + 20000) = −30000 N. The internal force in AB is N_AB = −R_A = 30000 N (tension positive convention: N_AB = sum of forces to the left of the cut, which equals −R_A = 30000 N).

To find the internal force in segment BC, cut between B and C and sum forces on the left portion. N_BC = R_A + F_B = −30000 + 18000 = −12000 N.

To find the internal force in segment CD, cut between C and D and sum forces on the left portion. N_CD = R_A + F_B + F_C = −30000 + 18000 − 8000 = −20000 N. Alternatively verify from the right: N_CD must equal −F_D = −20000 N, which is consistent.

Compute normal stress in each segment as σ = N / A.

σ_AB = 30000 / 200 = 150 MPa (tension).

σ_BC = −12000 / 200 = −60 MPa (compression).

σ_CD = −20000 / 200 = −100 MPa (compression).

Compute the axial deformation of each segment as δ = N·L / (A·E).

δ_AB = (30000 × 700) / (200 × 200000) = 21000000 / 40000000 = 0.525 mm.

δ_BC = (−12000 × 600) / (200 × 200000) = −7200000 / 40000000 = −0.180 mm.

δ_CD = (−20000 × 500) / (200 × 200000) = −10000000 / 40000000 = −0.250 mm.

Compute the displacement of point D relative to A as u_D = δ_AB + δ_BC + δ_CD = 0.525 − 0.180 − 0.250 = 0.095 mm (rightward).
