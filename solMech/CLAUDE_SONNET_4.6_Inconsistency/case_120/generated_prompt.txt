Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The rod is made of AISI 1020 steel and is prismatic with length L = 800 mm and diameter d = 20 mm.
Compute the cross-sectional area as A = π × (d/2)² = π × 10² mm².
The rod is placed between two rigid, unyielding supports so that the total change in length is exactly zero.
The rod experiences a uniform temperature increase of ΔT = 50 °C.
Use elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa and coefficient of thermal expansion α = 2×10⁻⁵ /°C.
Assume linear elastic behavior throughout.

For part (i), determine the thermal stress using the fully restrained condition.
The free thermal strain is ε_thermal = α × ΔT.
Because the supports are rigid and the total strain must be zero, the mechanical strain is equal and opposite to the free thermal strain: ε_mechanical = −α × ΔT.
Compute the thermal stress as σ = E × ε_mechanical = −E × α × ΔT.
Substitute values: σ = −200000 × 2×10⁻⁵ × 50.
Report the numerical result in MPa and state whether it is tensile or compressive based on the sign (negative indicates compression).

For part (ii), compute the axial force as F = σ × A.
Substitute the stress from part (i) and the cross-sectional area A = 100π mm².
Report the numerical result in Newtons and state whether the rod is in tension or compression.
