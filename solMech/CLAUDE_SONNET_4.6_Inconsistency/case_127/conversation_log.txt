Q: What value should be used for τ_xy — specifically, should the shear stress be taken as positive (τ_xy = +τ) or negative (τ_xy = −τ), or does the sign not matter for this calculation?
A: The sign doesn't matter for this calculation — the principal stresses and maximum in-plane shear stress depend on τ_xy², so just use τ_xy = τ = V/A.
