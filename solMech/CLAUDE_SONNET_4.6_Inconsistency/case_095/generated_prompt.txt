The shaft is a solid circular stepped shaft made of structural steel, analyzed in N, mm, and MPa units.

The shaft consists of two segments in series: segment AB and segment BC.

Segment AB has length L1 = 400 mm and diameter d1 = 30 mm.

Segment BC has length L2 = 700 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm.

End A is fixed. A torque T = 600,000 N·mm is applied at end C.

The shaft is statically determinate. The same internal torque T = 600,000 N·mm acts through both segments AB and BC.

Use shear modulus G = 77,519.4 MPa for both segments.

For each segment, compute the polar moment of inertia J = π·d⁴/32.

For each segment, compute the maximum shear stress using τ = T·c/J, where c = d/2.

Identify which segment governs (has the higher or equal maximum shear stress).

For each segment, compute the angle of twist using φ = T·L/(G·J).

Compute the total angle of twist at C relative to A as φ_total = φ_AB + φ_BC.

Report the maximum shear stress in each segment, state which segment governs, and report the total angle of twist at C relative to A in radians.
