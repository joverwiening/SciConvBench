Q: The boundary conditions you've described contain a contradiction I need to resolve.

For a simply supported beam with a symmetric UDL, the end slopes are **not** zero — they are equal and opposite (the beam slopes downward from left to right, then upward). Zero end slopes are the boundary conditions for a **fixed-fixed (fully clamped)** beam, not a simply supported beam.

Which boundary conditions do you actually want to apply: simply supported (deflection = 0 at both ends, end slopes are free and non-zero), or fixed-fixed (deflection = 0 and slope = 0 at both ends)?
A: Simply supported — deflection = 0 at both ends, end slopes are free. Ignore the comment about end slopes being zero in the incomplete version; that was an error.
