Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

Apply Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.

The beam is simply supported with span L = 3500 mm.

The boundary conditions are: deflection y = 0 at x = 0 and y = 0 at x = L. The end slopes are free (not prescribed).

A uniform distributed load w = 0.5 N/mm acts downward over the full span.

The beam material is structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa and second moment of area I = 1500000 mm^4.

Compute the deflection field using the Euler-Bernoulli simply supported beam formula: y(x) = (w * x / (24 * E * I)) * (L^3 - 2 * L * x^2 + x^3).

Determine the maximum deflection by evaluating y(x) at x = L/2, which is the location of maximum deflection for a symmetric UDL on a simply supported beam.

Report the maximum deflection value in mm and confirm it occurs at x = 1750 mm (midspan).

Compute the slope dy/dx at x = 0 using the derivative of the deflection formula: dy/dx = (w / (24 * E * I)) * (L^3 - 6 * L * x^2 + 4 * x^3), evaluated at x = 0, which gives slope = w * L^3 / (24 * E * I).

Report the slope at x = 0 in radians, noting its sign convention (positive value indicates the beam slopes downward from left to right at the left support).
