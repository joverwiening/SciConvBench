Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The rod is AISI 1020 steel, vertical, with length L = 800 mm and diameter d = 25 mm.

The rod is fixed at its top end and has a collar at its bottom end.

A weight W = 1500 N is dropped from a height h = 200 mm onto the collar, stretching the rod.

Treat the rod as a linear axial spring and neglect the mass of the rod.

Compute the cross-sectional area as A = π · d² / 4 using d = 25 mm.

Use E = 200000 MPa for the elastic modulus.

Compute the static deflection as δ_st = W · L / (A · E).

Apply energy conservation: W · (h + δ_max) = (1/2) · (A · E / L) · δ_max².

Rearrange into the standard impact formula: δ_max = δ_st · (1 + sqrt(1 + 2h / δ_st)).

Compute P_max = (A · E / L) · δ_max, which is equivalent to P_max = W · (1 + sqrt(1 + 2 · h · A · E / (W · L))).

Compute the maximum normal stress as σ_max = P_max / A.

Report A in mm², δ_st in mm, δ_max in mm, P_max in N, and σ_max in MPa.
