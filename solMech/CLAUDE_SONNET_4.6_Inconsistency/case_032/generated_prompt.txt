Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The beam is simply supported at both ends with span L = 3000 mm.

The material is AISI 1020 steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.

The second moment of area is constant at I = 3500000 mm^4.

A single point load P = 1500 N acts vertically downward at midspan (x = L/2 = 1500 mm).

Apply Euler-Bernoulli beam theory and neglect shear deformation entirely.

Use the governing equation EI v''(x) = M(x), where M(x) is the bending moment at position x.

By symmetry, each support reaction is P/2 = 750 N upward.

Write M(x) for the left half of the beam (0 ≤ x ≤ L/2) as M(x) = (P/2) x = 750 x.

Integrate EI v''(x) = M(x) once to obtain EI v'(x), introducing a constant of integration C1.

Integrate again to obtain EI v(x), introducing a second constant of integration C2.

Apply boundary condition v(0) = 0 (zero deflection at the left support) to determine C2.

Apply boundary condition v'(L/2) = 0 (zero slope at midspan by symmetry) to determine C1.

Compute the slope at the left support as v'(0), expressed in radians.

Compute the maximum deflection as v(L/2), expressed in mm, using the formula v_max = PL^3 / (48 EI).

Substitute P = 1500, L = 3000, E = 200000, I = 3500000 into the formula to obtain the numerical value of maximum deflection.

Substitute the same values into the slope expression to obtain the numerical value of the slope at the supports.

Report the maximum deflection in mm and the support slope in radians, both to at least four significant figures.
