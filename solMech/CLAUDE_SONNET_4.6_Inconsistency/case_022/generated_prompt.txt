Use N, mm, and MPa units throughout.

The shaft is a stepped solid circular shaft made of structural steel, statically determinate, fixed at end A and free at end C.

Segment AB has length L1 = 300 mm and diameter d1 = 25 mm.
Segment BC has length L2 = 700 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm.
The applied torque is T = 450000 N·mm at end C.
The shear modulus is G = 77519.4 MPa.
The same internal torque T = 450000 N·mm acts through both segments AB and BC.

Compute the polar moment of inertia for each segment using J = π·d⁴/32.
J1 = π·(25)⁴/32 = π·390625/32 = 38349.5 mm⁴.
J2 = π·(30)⁴/32 = π·810000/32 = 79521.6 mm⁴.

Compute the maximum shear stress in each segment using τ = T·c/J, where c = d/2.
For segment AB: c1 = 12.5 mm, τ1 = 450000·12.5/38349.5 = 146.6 MPa.
For segment BC: c2 = 15 mm, τ2 = 450000·15/79521.6 = 84.9 MPa.
Segment AB governs with the higher maximum shear stress of 146.6 MPa.

Compute the angle of twist for each segment using φ = T·L/(G·J).
For segment AB: φ1 = 450000·300/(77519.4·38349.5) = 135000000/2973140000 = 0.04540 rad.
For segment BC: φ2 = 450000·700/(77519.4·79521.6) = 315000000/6165100000 = 0.05111 rad.

Compute the total angle of twist at C relative to A by summing both segments.
φ_total = φ1 + φ2 = 0.04540 + 0.05111 = 0.09651 rad.

Convert to degrees if desired: 0.09651 rad × (180/π) = 5.53°.

Summary of results:
Maximum shear stress in segment AB is 146.6 MPa (governing segment).
Maximum shear stress in segment BC is 84.9 MPa.
Total angle of twist at C relative to A is 0.09651 rad (5.53°).
