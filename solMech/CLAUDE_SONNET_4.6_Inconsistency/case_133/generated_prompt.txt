The beam AB is simply supported with span L = 6000 mm.
A concentrated downward load P = 20000 N acts at 2000 mm from support A.
There are no other loads.

Compute the reactions using static equilibrium.
Take moments about A: R_B × 6000 = 20000 × 2000, so R_B = 40000000 / 6000 = 6666.67 N (upward).
Take moments about B: R_A × 6000 = 20000 × 4000, so R_A = 80000000 / 6000 = 13333.33 N (upward).
Verify: R_A + R_B = 13333.33 + 6666.67 = 20000 N = P. Equilibrium is satisfied.

The cut section is at x = 4500 mm from support A.
Since 4500 mm > 2000 mm, the load P has already been passed when moving from A toward the cut.
Use the left free-body diagram (from A to the cut at x = 4500 mm).

Sign convention: positive shear V is upward on the left cut face; positive bending moment M causes sagging (tension at bottom fibers).

Sum of vertical forces = 0: R_A - P + V = 0.
V = P - R_A = 20000 - 13333.33 = 6666.67 N.
