The beam AB is simply supported with span L = 3000 mm.
A concentrated vertical load P = 12000 N acts downward at 1500 mm from support A.
There are no other loads (no distributed loads, no moments).
Use N and N·mm as units throughout.

Determine reactions at A and B using static equilibrium.
Sum of moments about A: R_B × 3000 = 12000 × 1500, so R_B = 6000 N upward.
Sum of vertical forces: R_A + R_B = 12000, so R_A = 6000 N upward.

The cross section of interest is at x = 1500 mm from support A.
The load P = 12000 N is also located at x = 1500 mm from support A.
Take the free body diagram of the left portion (from A to the cut at x = 1500 mm).
The cut is taken just to the left of the load application point, so only R_A = 6000 N acts on the left portion before the load is encountered.

Sign convention: positive shear V is upward on the left cut face; positive bending moment M causes sagging (tension at the bottom fibers).

Apply vertical equilibrium on the left free body: R_A - V = 0, so V = R_A = 6000 N.
Since V acts upward on the left cut face and equals +6000 N, V = +6000 N.

Apply moment equilibrium about the cut section on the left free body: M = R_A × x = 6000 × 1500 = 9000000 N·mm.
This moment causes sagging (tension at the bottom), so M = +9000000 N·mm.

Final answers:
V = +6000 N
M = +9,000,000 N·mm
