Q: I need one clarification before writing the final specification.

The problem statement contains a direct contradiction: it says "at the outer surface where the bending normal stress is maximum, take the torsional shear stress to vanish," but then part (b) asks to compute the torsional shear stress and part (c) uses both σ and τ together in a plane-stress principal-stress calculation.

Should the torsional shear stress be included in the stress state (i.e., τxy = τ from torsion is nonzero in parts b and c), or should it truly vanish so that the principal stresses are governed by bending alone as stated in the introductory paragraph?
A: The torsional shear stress should be included. Parts (b) and (c) are the correct intent — compute τ from torsion and use both σ and τ together in the plane-stress calculation. The sentence saying "torsional shear stress to vanish" in the incomplete version is an error that doesn't appear in the complete requirement. Use σx = σ (bending), σy = 0, and τxy = τ (torsion) for part (c).
