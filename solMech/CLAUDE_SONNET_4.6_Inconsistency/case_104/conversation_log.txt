Q: The loading and boundary conditions you've described contain a contradiction I need to resolve.

For a simply supported beam with a symmetric UDL, the standard boundary conditions are zero deflection at both supports (v(0) = 0 and v(L) = 0), and by symmetry the slope is zero only at the midspan (x = L/2). The slope at x = 0 is NOT zero — it is at its maximum magnitude at the supports for this loading case.

You have stated "take the end slopes to be zero," but you have also asked to "compute the slope at x = 0." These two conditions directly contradict each other: if you enforce zero slope at the ends in addition to zero deflection at the ends, you are describing a fully fixed (clamped) beam, not a simply supported beam.

Which beam condition do you actually want to analyse?
A: It's a simply supported beam. Ignore the part about end slopes being zero — that was a mistake in the incomplete version. Use the standard simply supported boundary conditions (zero deflection at both supports, non-zero slopes at the ends).
