Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

Apply Euler–Bernoulli beam theory.

The beam is simply supported with span L = 2500 mm.

The boundary conditions are zero deflection at x = 0 and zero deflection at x = L (v(0) = 0, v(L) = 0).

The end slopes are not constrained and are to be computed, not assumed zero.

A uniformly distributed load w = 0.7 N/mm acts over the full span.

The beam material is structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.

The second moment of area is I = 2000000 mm^4.

Compute the flexural rigidity as EI = 200000 × 2000000 = 4.0 × 10^11 N·mm².

Use the standard Euler–Bernoulli simply supported beam closed-form solutions for a full-span UDL.

Compute the maximum deflection using the formula v_max = (5 × w × L^4) / (384 × E × I).

Substitute values: v_max = (5 × 0.7 × 2500^4) / (384 × 4.0 × 10^11).

Evaluate 2500^4 = 3.906250 × 10^13 mm^4.

Evaluate the numerator: 5 × 0.7 × 3.906250 × 10^13 = 1.3671875 × 10^14 N·mm^3.

Evaluate the denominator: 384 × 4.0 × 10^11 = 1.536 × 10^14 N·mm^2.

The maximum deflection is v_max = 1.3671875 × 10^14 / 1.536 × 10^14 ≈ 0.8901 mm (downward).

The maximum deflection occurs at midspan, x = L/2 = 1250 mm, by symmetry of the loading and support conditions.

Compute the slope at x = 0 using the formula θ(0) = (w × L^3) / (24 × E × I).

Substitute values: θ(0) = (0.7 × 2500^3) / (24 × 4.0 × 10^11).

Evaluate 2500^3 = 1.5625 × 10^10 mm^3.

Evaluate the numerator: 0.7 × 1.5625 × 10^10 = 1.09375 × 10^10 N·mm^2.

Evaluate the denominator: 24 × 4.0 × 10^11 = 9.6 × 10^12 N·mm^2.

The slope at x = 0 is θ(0) = 1.09375 × 10^10 / 9.6 × 10^12 ≈ 1.139 × 10^-3 radians (positive, indicating the beam slopes downward to the right at the left support).

Report the maximum deflection as approximately 0.8901 mm occurring at x = 1250 mm.

Report the slope at x = 0 as approximately 1.139 × 10^-3 radians.
