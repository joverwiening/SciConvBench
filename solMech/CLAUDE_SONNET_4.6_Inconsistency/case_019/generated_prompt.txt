Use N, mm, and MPa units throughout.

The shaft is solid and circular with diameter d = 30 mm and total length L = 1200 mm.
The shaft is fixed (cantilevered) at x = 0.
A torque T1 = +200000 N·mm is applied at x = 1200 mm (the free end).
A torque T2 = −200000 N·mm is applied at x = 400 mm.
The material is AISI 1020 steel with shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa.
The polar moment of inertia is J = π·d⁴/32 = π·(30)⁴/32.

Part (a): Construct the internal torque diagram using the free-body-diagram method, cutting each segment and summing torques from the free end.

Segment 1 covers x from 400 mm to 1200 mm (between the intermediate torque application point and the free end).
Cut anywhere in Segment 1 and sum torques from the free end: the only applied torque to the right of the cut is T1 = +200000 N·mm, so the internal torque in Segment 1 is T_internal_1 = +200000 N·mm.

Segment 2 covers x from 0 mm to 400 mm (between the fixed end and the intermediate torque application point).
Cut anywhere in Segment 2 and sum torques from the free end: both T1 and T2 lie to the right of the cut, so the internal torque in Segment 2 is T_internal_2 = T1 + T2 = 200000 + (−200000) = 0 N·mm.

Part (b): Compute the maximum shear stress in each segment using τ = T·c / J, where c = d/2 = 15 mm.

Compute J = π·(30)⁴/32 = π·810000/32 = 79521.56 mm⁴ (use this value).
For Segment 1: τ_max_1 = (200000 · 15) / 79521.56.
For Segment 2: τ_max_2 = (0 · 15) / 79521.56 = 0 MPa.

Part (c): Compute the angle of twist at x = L relative to the fixed end by summing the twist of each segment using φ = T·L_segment / (G·J).

Length of Segment 1 is L1 = 1200 − 400 = 800 mm.
Length of Segment 2 is L2 = 400 − 0 = 400 mm.
Twist of Segment 1: φ1 = T_internal_1 · L1 / (G · J) = 200000 · 800 / (76923.1 · 79521.56).
Twist of Segment 2: φ2 = T_internal_2 · L2 / (G · J) = 0 · 400 / (76923.1 · 79521.56) = 0 rad.
Total angle of twist at x = L: φ_total = φ1 + φ2.
Express the final angle of twist in radians.
