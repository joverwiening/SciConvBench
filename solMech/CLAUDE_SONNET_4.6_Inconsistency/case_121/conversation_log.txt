Q: I need to clarify the stress distribution rule before writing the specification, because there is a contradiction in the request.

In standard pure bending, the normal stress varies linearly with y (σ = My/I), giving zero stress at the neutral axis (y = 0). You have asked to treat the stress as uniform over the section depth, meaning the same value everywhere — but you have also asked to compute stresses at three distinct locations (y = 0, top fiber, bottom fiber). If the stress is truly uniform, all three values are identical, yet a uniform distribution also contradicts the sign condition (tension and compression cannot both exist in a uniform field).

Can you clarify what you mean by "uniform over the section depth"? Specifically, do you want the standard linear bending stress formula (σ = My/I, giving zero at the neutral axis and opposite signs at top and bottom), or do you want a single constant stress value applied everywhere on the section?
