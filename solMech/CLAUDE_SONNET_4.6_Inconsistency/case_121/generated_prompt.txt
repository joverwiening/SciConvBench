Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The beam cross-section is rectangular with width b = 40 mm and height h = 100 mm.

The internal bending moment at the section is M = 4 000 000 N·mm and is sagging (hogging is negative by the chosen sign convention).

Define y as measured from the neutral axis (centroid of the section), positive upward.

For a sagging moment, the bottom fiber is in tension (positive stress) and the top fiber is in compression (negative stress).

Compute the second moment of area of the rectangular section about the neutral axis using I = b·h³/12.
Substitute b = 40 mm and h = 100 mm: I = 40·100³/12.

Compute the bending normal stress at any point using the standard linear formula σ = M·y / I.

Compute the stress at the neutral axis by substituting y = 0 mm into σ = M·y / I.

Compute the stress at the top fiber by substituting y = +50 mm (half the section height, positive upward) into σ = M·y / I.

Compute the stress at the bottom fiber by substituting y = −50 mm (half the section height, negative downward) into σ = M·y / I.

Express all stress results in MPa (equivalent to N/mm²).

State the sign of each result and confirm that the top fiber stress is compressive (negative) and the bottom fiber stress is tensile (positive) for a sagging moment.
