Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The rod is made of AISI 1020 steel and behaves as a linear axial spring; neglect the mass of the rod.

The rod is vertical, fixed at its top end, with length L = 1500 mm and diameter d = 16 mm.

A weight W = 800 N is dropped from height h = 150 mm onto a collar at the bottom end, stretching the rod.

Compute the cross-sectional area as A = π × d² / 4.

Use E = 200000 MPa for the modulus of elasticity.

Express the maximum deflection as δ_max = P_max × L / (A × E).

Apply the energy-conservation equation W × (h + δ_max) = P_max² × L / (2 × A × E) to find P_max.

Substitute δ_max = P_max × L / (A × E) into the energy equation to obtain a single equation in P_max.

Rearrange that equation into a standard quadratic form in P_max and solve for the positive root.

Compute the maximum normal stress as σ_max = P_max / A.

Report the numerical values of A (mm²), P_max (N), and σ_max (MPa).
