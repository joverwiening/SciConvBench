Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The beam has a rectangular cross section with width b = 40 mm and height h = 80 mm.

The internal shear force at the section is V = 25000 N.

Compute the cross-sectional area as A = b × h = 40 × 80 = 3200 mm².

Compute the second moment of area as I = b h³ / 12 = 40 × 80³ / 12.

Part (a): Compute the maximum transverse shear stress using τ_max = 1.5 × V / A.

Substitute V = 25000 N and A = 3200 mm² into the formula and report τ_max in MPa.

Part (b): Compute the shear stress at y = 20 mm from the neutral axis using the shear formula τ = V Q / (I b).

Define y = 0 at the neutral axis, with y positive upward.

Compute Q as the first moment of area of the portion of the cross section above the level y = 20 mm about the neutral axis.

The area above y = 20 mm is a rectangle with width b = 40 mm and height equal to (h/2 − y) = 40 − 20 = 20 mm.

Compute the centroid of that area measured from the neutral axis as ȳ = y + (h/2 − y) / 2 = 20 + 10 = 30 mm.

Compute Q = (b × (h/2 − y)) × ȳ = 40 × 20 × 30.

Substitute V, Q, I, and b into τ = V Q / (I b) and report the result in MPa.
