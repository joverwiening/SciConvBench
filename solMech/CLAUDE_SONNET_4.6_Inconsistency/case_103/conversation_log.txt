Q: The boundary conditions stated contain a contradiction that needs to be resolved before proceeding.

For a simply supported beam, the slope at the supports is NOT zero — it is at its maximum magnitude there. The slope is zero only at midspan (by symmetry). Pinned and roller supports enforce zero deflection, not zero slope.

Which boundary conditions should be used: the correct simply supported ones (zero deflection at both supports, zero slope at midspan only), or a different support condition such as fixed-fixed (which would enforce zero slope at the supports)?
A: Use the correct simply supported boundary conditions: zero deflection at both supports and zero slope at midspan. The mention of slope vanishing at the supports in the incomplete requirement was an error. The beam is simply supported.
