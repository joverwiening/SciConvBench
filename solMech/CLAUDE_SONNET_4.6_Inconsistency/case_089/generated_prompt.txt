Use N, mm, and MPa units throughout.

The bar is prismatic with constant cross-sectional area A = 500 mm² and elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.

The bar has four points: A (left end, fixed), B, C, and D (right end).

Segment lengths are: AB = 700 mm, BC = 400 mm, CD = 300 mm.

The boundary condition is u_A = 0 (the left end A is fixed; the right end D is free).

Applied external forces (positive = rightward): at B, F_B = +15000 N; at C, F_C = −12000 N; at D, F_D = +10000 N.

Find the internal axial force in each segment by passing a section cut and applying equilibrium from the free (right) end inward, or equivalently from the fixed (left) end outward, and confirm both directions agree.

For segment CD (between C and D): isolate the portion to the right of the cut. The only external force to the right is F_D = +10000 N. Equilibrium gives N_CD = +10000 N (tension).

For segment BC (between B and C): isolate the portion to the right of the cut. External forces to the right are F_C = −12000 N and F_D = +10000 N. Equilibrium gives N_BC = F_C + F_D = −12000 + 10000 = −2000 N (compression).

For segment AB (between A and B): isolate the portion to the right of the cut. External forces to the right are F_B = +15000 N, F_C = −12000 N, and F_D = +10000 N. Equilibrium gives N_AB = 15000 − 12000 + 10000 = +13000 N (tension).

Verify equilibrium at A: the reaction at A must be R_A = −N_AB = −13000 N (i.e., 13000 N directed leftward), which balances the sum of all applied forces (15000 − 12000 + 10000 = 13000 N rightward). This confirms the solution is consistent.

Compute the normal stress in each segment using σ = N / A.

Stress in AB: σ_AB = 13000 / 500 = +26 MPa (tension).

Stress in BC: σ_BC = −2000 / 500 = −4 MPa (compression).

Stress in CD: σ_CD = 10000 / 500 = +20 MPa (tension).

Compute the axial deformation of each segment using δ = N·L / (A·E).

Deformation of AB: δ_AB = (13000 × 700) / (500 × 200000) = 9100000 / 100000000 = +0.091 mm.

Deformation of BC: δ_BC = (−2000 × 400) / (500 × 200000) = −800000 / 100000000 = −0.008 mm.

Deformation of CD: δ_CD = (10000 × 300) / (500 × 200000) = 3000000 / 100000000 = +0.030 mm.

Compute the displacement of point D relative to A by summing all three segment deformations.

u_D = δ_AB + δ_BC + δ_CD = 0.091 + (−0.008) + 0.030 = +0.113 mm.

The displacement of point D relative to A is +0.113 mm (rightward).
