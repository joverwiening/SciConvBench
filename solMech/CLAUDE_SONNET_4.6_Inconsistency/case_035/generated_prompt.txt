Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.
The column length is L = 2200 mm.
The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with elastic modulus E = 69000 MPa.
The applied compressive load is P = 25000 N.
The required factor of safety against buckling is n = 2.5.
The effective length factor is K = 2.0.
The cross section is solid circular with diameter d.
The second moment of area is I = π d^4 / 64.
The Euler buckling load formula is P_cr = π^2 · E · I / (K · L)^2.
The design requirement is P_cr ≥ n · P, meaning P_cr ≥ 2.5 × 25000 = 62500 N.
Substitute I into the buckling formula to get P_cr = π^2 · E · π d^4 / 64 / (K · L)^2 = π^3 · E · d^4 / (64 · (K · L)^2).
Set P_cr equal to n · P and solve for d: d^4 = 64 · n · P · (K · L)^2 / (π^3 · E).
Substitute values: d^4 = 64 × 62500 × (2.0 × 2200)^2 / (π^3 × 69000).
Compute (K · L) = 2.0 × 2200 = 4400 mm, so (K · L)^2 = 19360000 mm^2.
Compute the numerator: 64 × 62500 × 19360000 = 7.7440 × 10^13.
Compute the denominator: π^3 × 69000 ≈ 31.006 × 69000 ≈ 2139414.
Compute d^4 = 7.7440 × 10^13 / 2139414 ≈ 3.6197 × 10^7 mm^4.
Compute d = (3.6197 × 10^7)^(1/4).
Take the square root of 3.6197 × 10^7 ≈ 6016.4, then the square root of 6016.4 ≈ 77.57 mm.
The minimum required diameter is d ≈ 77.6 mm.
Round up to the next whole millimeter, giving d_min = 78 mm, to ensure the factor of safety is met.
Verify by computing I = π × 78^4 / 64 ≈ π × 37015056 / 64 ≈ 1816600 mm^4, then P_cr = π^2 × 69000 × 1816600 / (4400)^2 ≈ 63400 N, which exceeds 62500 N, confirming the design is adequate.
