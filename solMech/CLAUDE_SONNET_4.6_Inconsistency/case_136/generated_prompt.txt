Compute the cross-sectional area as A = b × h = 30 × 100 = 3000 mm².

Compute the average normal stress as σ_avg = P / A = 50000 / 3000 = 16.667 MPa (tension).

Treat σ_avg as the only normal stress component acting on the cross section (σx = σ_avg, σy = 0, no bending, no other normal stress).

Treat τ_avg as the only shear stress component on the cross section.

Compute the maximum in-plane shear stress using the formula τ_max = sqrt((σ_avg / 2)² + τ_avg²).
