Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.
Model the bar as two segments connected in series: Segment 1 on the left, Segment 2 on the right.
The material is AISI 1020 steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Assume linear-elastic behavior throughout.
The bar is fixed at its left end (Segment 1 end) and loaded at its right end (Segment 2 end) by an axial tensile force P = 30000 N.
Segment 1 has length L1 = 300 mm and diameter d1 = 25 mm.
Segment 2 has length L2 = 1000 mm and diameter d2 = 35 mm.
Compute the cross-sectional area of each segment using A = π/4 × d².
A1 = π/4 × 25² mm².
A2 = π/4 × 35² mm².
Because the bar is a statically determinate series system, the same axial force P = 30000 N acts on every cross-section in both segments.
Compute the normal stress in each segment using σ = P/A.
σ1 = P / A1.
σ2 = P / A2.
Compute the elongation of each segment using δ = PL / (AE).
δ1 = (P × L1) / (A1 × E).
δ2 = (P × L2) / (A2 × E).
Compute the total elongation of the bar as δ_total = δ1 + δ2.
Report σ1 and σ2 in MPa, rounded to four significant figures.
Report δ1, δ2, and δ_total in mm, rounded to four significant figures.
