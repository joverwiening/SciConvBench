Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.
Apply Euler–Bernoulli beam theory assuming small deflections and linear elastic material behaviour.
Model a cantilever beam of length L = 1200 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
Use structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Use a constant second moment of area I = 2500000 mm^4 along the full span.
Apply a single downward point load P = 800 N at the free end x = L.
Use the bending moment distribution M(x) = P(L − x), which varies linearly from PL at the fixed end to zero at the free end.
Compute the slope at the free end using the formula theta = PL^2 / (2EI).
Substitute P = 800 N, L = 1200 mm, E = 200000 MPa, I = 2500000 mm^4 into the slope formula.
Compute the numerical value of theta and report it in radians, then convert and also report it in degrees.
Compute the vertical deflection at the free end using the formula delta = PL^3 / (3EI).
Substitute the same values into the deflection formula.
Compute the numerical value of delta and report it in mm.
Show each substitution and intermediate arithmetic step explicitly before stating the final results.
Report the final slope in both radians and degrees, and the final deflection in mm, clearly labelled.
