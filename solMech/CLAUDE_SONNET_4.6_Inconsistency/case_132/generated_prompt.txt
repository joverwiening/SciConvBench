Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.
Assume small strains.
The adhesive layer is rectangular with a total shear area A = 4000 mm² and a thickness t = 20 mm.
A uniform in-plane shear force V = 12000 N is applied to the layer.
Treat the adhesive as a deformable, isotropic, linear-elastic solid with shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.
Step (i): Compute the average shear stress as τ_avg = V / A = 12000 / 4000 = 3 MPa.
Step (ii): Compute the average shear strain as γ_avg = τ_avg / G = 3 / 39179.1, yielding a dimensionless result in mm/mm.
Step (iii): Compute the relative lateral displacement (shear deformation) as Δ = γ_avg · t, where t = 20 mm, yielding a result in mm.
Report τ_avg in MPa, γ_avg as a dimensionless ratio, and Δ in mm.
Do not apply any rigid-body or no-slip constraint; the layer deforms elastically and Δ is finite and non-zero.
