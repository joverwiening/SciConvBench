Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

Assume Euler buckling applies, meaning the column is slender and remains elastic.

The column is straight and made of 6061-T6 aluminum.

The column length is L = 3000 mm.

The elastic modulus is E = 69000 MPa.

The second moment of area is I = 2500000 mm^4.

The flexural rigidity is EI = 69000 × 2500000 = 172500000000 N·mm^2.

The end condition is fixed–fixed, giving an effective length factor K = 0.5.

The effective length is KL = 0.5 × 3000 = 1500 mm.

Compute the Euler critical buckling load using the formula P_cr = π^2 × E × I / (K × L)^2.

Substitute values: P_cr = π^2 × 172500000000 / (1500)^2.

Compute the denominator: (1500)^2 = 2250000 mm^2.

Compute π^2 = 9.8696044 (use this value).

Compute the numerator: 9.8696044 × 172500000000 = 1702506759000 N·mm^2.

Divide: P_cr = 1702506759000 / 2250000 = 756892 N (rounded to the nearest whole number).

Convert to kilonewtons: P_cr = 756892 / 1000 = 756.892 kN.

Report P_cr = 756892 N, which equals approximately 756.9 kN.
