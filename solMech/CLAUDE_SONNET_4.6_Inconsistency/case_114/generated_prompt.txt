Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The point on the cross section has centroidal coordinates y = 30 mm and z = 15 mm.

The second moments of area about the centroidal axes are Iy = 2,500,000 mm^4, Iz = 1,200,000 mm^4, and Iyz = 800,000 mm^4.

The internal bending moments are My = 4,000,000 N·mm and Mz = 2,000,000 N·mm.

Apply the full unsymmetric bending formula: σ = −(My·Iz − Mz·Iyz)/(Iy·Iz − Iyz^2)·z − (Mz·Iy − My·Iyz)/(Iy·Iz − Iyz^2)·y.

Compute the denominator: Iy·Iz − Iyz^2 = (2,500,000)(1,200,000) − (800,000)^2 = 3,000,000,000,000 − 640,000,000,000 = 2,360,000,000,000 mm^8.

Compute the coefficient for z: (My·Iz − Mz·Iyz) = (4,000,000)(1,200,000) − (2,000,000)(800,000) = 4,800,000,000,000 − 1,600,000,000,000 = 3,200,000,000,000 N·mm^3. Divide by the denominator: 3,200,000,000,000 / 2,360,000,000,000 = 1.355932... N/mm^2 per mm, i.e., MPa/mm.

Compute the coefficient for y: (Mz·Iy − My·Iyz) = (2,000,000)(2,500,000) − (4,000,000)(800,000) = 5,000,000,000,000 − 3,200,000,000,000 = 1,800,000,000,000 N·mm^3. Divide by the denominator: 1,800,000,000,000 / 2,360,000,000,000 = 0.762711... MPa/mm.

Compute σ: σ = −(1.355932)(15) − (0.762711)(30) = −20.3390 − 22.8814 = −43.22 MPa.

The normal stress at the point is σ ≈ −43.22 MPa (compression, using the tension-positive sign convention).
