Q: I need to clarify a contradiction before proceeding.

You have given two different methods to compute the stress:

Method 1 (simple superposition, ignoring the product of inertia): σ = My·z/Iy + Mz·y/Iz

Method 2 (full unsymmetric bending formula accounting for Iyz): σ = −(My·Iz − Mz·Iyz)/(Iy·Iz − Iyz²)·z − (Mz·Iy − My·Iyz)/(Iy·Iz − Iyz²)·y

These two formulas give different numerical results when Iyz ≠ 0, as is the case here. Which method do you want used for the final answer?
A: Use Method 2 — the full unsymmetric bending formula.
