Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The rod is a vertical structural steel rod, fixed at its top end, with length L = 1200 mm and diameter d = 30 mm.

A weight W = 500 N is dropped from a height h = 200 mm onto a collar at the bottom end, stretching the rod axially.

Treat the rod as a linear axial spring and neglect the mass of the rod.

Compute the cross-sectional area as A = π d² / 4 using d = 30 mm.

Use E = 200000 MPa for structural steel.

The maximum deflection under the impact load is δ_max = P_max L / (A E).

Apply energy conservation: W (h + δ_max) = P_max² L / (2 A E).

Substitute δ_max = P_max L / (A E) into the energy equation to obtain a single equation in P_max.

Rearrange the resulting equation into the standard quadratic form in P_max.

Solve the quadratic equation for P_max, taking the positive root.

Compute the maximum normal stress as σ_max = P_max / A.

Report P_max in Newtons and σ_max in MPa.
