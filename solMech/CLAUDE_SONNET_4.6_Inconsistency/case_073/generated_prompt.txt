Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The beam is simply supported and experiences a maximum bending moment of M_max = 6,000,000 N·mm.

The cross section is rectangular with width b = 60 mm and unknown height h.

The allowable bending stress is σ_allow = 170 MPa.

Use the standard flexure formula: σ_max = M_max · (h/2) / (b · h^3 / 12).

Simplify the formula to σ_max = 6 · M_max / (b · h^2).

Set σ_max equal to σ_allow and solve for h: h^2 = 6 · M_max / (b · σ_allow).

Substitute the values: h^2 = 6 · 6,000,000 / (60 · 170).

Compute h^2 = 36,000,000 / 10,200 = 3529.41 mm^2.

Take the positive square root: h = √3529.41 ≈ 59.41 mm.

Report the minimum required height as h ≈ 59.41 mm.
