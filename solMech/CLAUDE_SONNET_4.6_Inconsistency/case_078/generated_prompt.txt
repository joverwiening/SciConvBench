Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The shaft is solid and circular with length L = 1500 mm.
The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa.
The applied torque is T = 1000000 N·mm.
The allowable angle of twist is φ_allow = 0.75°, which must be converted to radians before use: φ_allow = 0.75 × π/180 = 0.013090 rad.

Use the angle-of-twist formula φ = T·L / (J·G).
Use the polar moment of inertia for a solid circular cross-section: J = π·d^4 / 32.

Solve for the minimum required diameter d by rearranging the formula.
Substitute J = π·d^4/32 into φ = T·L/(J·G) to get φ = 32·T·L / (π·d^4·G).
Rearrange to isolate d^4: d^4 = 32·T·L / (π·φ_allow·G).
Compute d^4 = 32 × 1000000 × 1500 / (π × 0.013090 × 25939.8).
Take the fourth root of the result to obtain d in mm.
Report the minimum required diameter d in mm.
