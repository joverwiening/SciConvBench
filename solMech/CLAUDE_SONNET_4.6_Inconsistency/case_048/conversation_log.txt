Q: What value should be used for the mean radius (or mean diameter) in the thin-walled shear-flow calculation — specifically, should the mean radius be taken as r_m = (do + di)/4 (i.e., the average of the outer and inner radii), or as r_m = (do/2 + di/2)/2, and should the enclosed mean area A_m be computed using this mean radius as A_m = π·r_m²?
A: The complete requirement does **not** use the thin-walled shear-flow approach at all. Ignore that instruction from the incomplete requirement.

Use the standard hollow shaft formulas directly:

- **J = π(do⁴ − di⁴)/32**
- **τ_max = T·(do/2) / J**
- **φ = T·L / (J·G)**

No thin-walled approximation is needed.
