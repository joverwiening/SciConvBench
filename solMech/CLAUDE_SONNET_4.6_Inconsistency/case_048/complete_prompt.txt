Use N–mm–MPa units. Assume linear elasticity.

A hollow circular shaft made of AISI 1020 steel has outer diameter do = 60 mm and inner diameter di = 48 mm (wall thickness 6 mm). The shaft length is L = 800 mm and it is subjected to a constant torque T = 450000 N·mm.

Compute (i) the maximum shear stress at the outer surface, and (ii) the angle of twist over the length. Use J = π(do^4 − di^4)/32 and G = 76923.1 MPa.
