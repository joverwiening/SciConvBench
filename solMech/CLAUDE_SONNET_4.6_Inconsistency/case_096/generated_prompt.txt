The beam is a cantilever of length L = 2000 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
The beam carries a uniformly distributed load w = 1.0 N/mm acting downward over the entire span.
Use structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa and second moment of area I = 1500000 mm^4.
Use Euler–Bernoulli beam theory throughout.
Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system consistently.
Apply the standard exact cantilever UDL formulas derived from Euler–Bernoulli theory.
The exact maximum deflection occurs at the free end (x = L) and is given by delta_max = (w * L^4) / (8 * E * I).
The exact slope at the free end (x = L) is given by theta_max = (w * L^3) / (6 * E * I).
Substitute w = 1.0 N/mm, L = 2000 mm, E = 200000 MPa, and I = 1500000 mm^4 into both formulas.
Compute the numerical value of the maximum deflection in mm.
Compute the numerical value of the slope at the free end in radians.
Report both results clearly with their units.
Do not use the midspan resultant substitution or any approximation method.
