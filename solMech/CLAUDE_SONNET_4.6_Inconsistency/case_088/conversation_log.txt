Q: I need to flag a contradiction before proceeding.

The instruction says "treat the internal axial force as uniform along the full length and compute one stress level from the net applied load," but this contradicts the standard method for a segmented bar with multiple intermediate loads, where the internal force is different in each segment (AB, BC, CD).

Which approach do you want used?

Option 1: Standard segment-by-segment method — cut each segment, apply equilibrium, get a different internal force in AB, BC, and CD, then sum the deformations to find displacement at D.

Option 2: Single net-force method — sum all applied forces into one resultant, compute one uniform stress and one total deformation for the whole bar.
A: Use Option 1: the standard segment-by-segment method. Determine a different internal force in each segment (AB, BC, CD), compute the stress in each segment, and sum the deformations to find the displacement at D.
