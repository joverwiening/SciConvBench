Q: What value should be used for the shear area when computing the average shear stress τ = V/A — the full cross-sectional area (b × h), or a reduced shear area (for example, only the web area or some other fraction)?
A: Use the full cross-sectional area A = b × h = 50 × 80 = 4000 mm².
