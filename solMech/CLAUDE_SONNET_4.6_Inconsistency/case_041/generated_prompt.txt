The system uses N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

Assume Euler buckling applies and the column remains elastic and perfectly straight.

The column is made of AISI 1020 steel.

The column length is L = 1000 mm.

The elastic modulus is E = 200000 MPa (which is N/mm^2).

The second moment of area is I = 1200000 mm^4.

The end condition is fixed-free (cantilever), giving an effective length factor K = 2.0.

Compute the effective length as KL = 2.0 times 1000 mm = 2000 mm.

Compute the Euler critical buckling load using the formula P_cr = pi^2 times E times I divided by (K times L)^2.

Substitute values: P_cr = (9.8696044 times 200000 times 1200000) divided by (2000^2).

Compute the numerator: 9.8696044 times 200000 = 1973920.88, then 1973920.88 times 1200000 = 2368705056000.

Compute the denominator: 2000 squared = 4000000.

Divide numerator by denominator: 2368705056000 divided by 4000000 = 592176.264 N.

Round the result to six significant figures: P_cr = 592176 N.

Convert to kilonewtons by dividing by 1000: P_cr = 592.176 kN.

Report P_cr = 592176 N and P_cr = 592.176 kN.
