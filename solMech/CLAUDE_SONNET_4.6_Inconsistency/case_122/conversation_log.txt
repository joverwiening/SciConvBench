Q: I need to clarify the stress distribution rule before writing the specification, because there is a contradiction in the request.

In standard pure bending, the normal stress varies linearly with y (σ = My/I), giving zero stress at the neutral axis (y = 0). You have asked to treat the stress as uniform over the section depth, meaning the stress at the neutral axis has the same sign as at the extreme fibers. These two models are mutually exclusive.

Which model should be used: the standard linear bending stress formula (σ = My/I, giving zero at y = 0), or a uniform stress distribution where the same stress value applies at every depth including the neutral axis?
A: Use the standard linear bending stress formula σ = My/I. The stress at y = 0 is zero.
