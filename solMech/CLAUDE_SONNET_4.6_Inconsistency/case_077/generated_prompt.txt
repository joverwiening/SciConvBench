Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The shaft is solid and circular with length L = 800 mm.
The material is AISI 1020 steel with shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa.
The applied torque is T = 600000 N·mm.
The allowable angle of twist is φ_allow = 1.5°, converted to radians as φ = 1.5 × π/180 ≈ 0.02618 rad before use in any formula.

Use the polar moment of inertia J = π d^4 / 32 for a solid circular cross-section.

Apply the angle-of-twist formula φ = T L / (J G) to find the minimum required diameter d.
Rearrange the formula to solve for J: J = T L / (φ G).
Substitute J = π d^4 / 32 into that expression to get π d^4 / 32 = T L / (φ G).
Solve for d: d = (32 T L / (π φ G))^(1/4).

Substitute T = 600000 N·mm, L = 800 mm, φ = 0.02618 rad, and G = 76923.1 MPa into the expression for d.
Compute the numerator: 32 × 600000 × 800 = 15360000000.
Compute the denominator: π × 0.02618 × 76923.1 ≈ 6325.5.
Compute the ratio: 15360000000 / 6325.5 ≈ 2428000 mm^4 (this equals J, not d^4; d^4 = 32 T L / (π φ G)).
Take the fourth root of the ratio to obtain d in mm.
Report the minimum required diameter d as the final answer in mm.
