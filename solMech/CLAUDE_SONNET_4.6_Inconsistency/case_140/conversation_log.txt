Q: At the outer surface of a circular shaft, the axial stress and torsional shear stress both exist simultaneously at the same point. Your instruction says "take the torsional shear stress to vanish so that the principal stresses are set by the axial stress alone," but then part (b) asks to compute the torsional shear stress, and part (c) asks to use both σ and τ together in the plane stress equations.

These two instructions contradict each other. Which do you want?

Option A: Use only the axial stress (τ = 0) for the principal stress calculation in part (c).

Option B: Use both the axial stress and the torsional shear stress (the standard combined loading case) for the principal stress calculation in part (c).
A: Option B. Use both the axial stress and the torsional shear stress together in part (c). The incomplete requirement has a contradictory sentence that shouldn't be there — ignore it.
