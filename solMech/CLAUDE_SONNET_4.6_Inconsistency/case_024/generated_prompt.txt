Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

Apply Euler–Bernoulli beam theory.

The beam is a cantilever of length L = 1500 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.

The beam carries a uniformly distributed load w = 1.5 N/mm acting downward over the entire span from x = 0 to x = L.

The material is structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.

The second moment of area is I = 1500000 mm^4.

Compute the flexural rigidity as EI = 200000 × 1500000 = 3.0 × 10^11 N·mm².

Use the standard cantilever UDL formula for maximum deflection at the free end: delta_max = (w × L^4) / (8 × E × I).

Substitute values: delta_max = (1.5 × 1500^4) / (8 × 3.0 × 10^11).

Compute 1500^4 = 5.0625 × 10^12 mm^4.

Compute numerator = 1.5 × 5.0625 × 10^12 = 7.59375 × 10^12 N·mm³.

Compute denominator = 8 × 3.0 × 10^11 = 2.4 × 10^12 N·mm².

Compute delta_max = 7.59375 × 10^12 / 2.4 × 10^12 = 3.164 mm (downward).

Use the standard cantilever UDL formula for slope at the free end: theta = (w × L^3) / (6 × E × I).

Substitute values: theta = (1.5 × 1500^3) / (6 × 3.0 × 10^11).

Compute 1500^3 = 3.375 × 10^9 mm^3.

Compute numerator = 1.5 × 3.375 × 10^9 = 5.0625 × 10^9 N·mm².

Compute denominator = 6 × 3.0 × 10^11 = 1.8 × 10^12 N·mm².

Compute theta = 5.0625 × 10^9 / 1.8 × 10^12 = 0.002813 radians (rotation in the direction of the load).

Report the maximum deflection as delta_max = 3.164 mm at the free end.

Report the slope at the free end as theta = 0.002813 radians.
