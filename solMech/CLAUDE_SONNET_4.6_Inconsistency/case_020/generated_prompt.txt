Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The shaft is solid and circular with diameter d = 35 mm and total length L = 1200 mm.
The shaft is fixed (zero rotation) at x = 0.
A torque T2 = -250000 N·mm is applied at x = 800 mm (a = 800 mm).
A torque T1 = 200000 N·mm is applied at x = 1200 mm (the free end).
The material has shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.
The shaft has two segments: Segment 1 from x = 0 to x = 800 mm, and Segment 2 from x = 800 mm to x = 1200 mm.

Part (a): Construct the internal torque in each segment using free-body cuts.
For Segment 2 (cut between x = 800 mm and x = 1200 mm), take a free-body of the portion to the right of the cut; the internal torque equals T1 = 200000 N·mm.
For Segment 1 (cut between x = 0 and x = 800 mm), take a free-body of the portion to the right of the cut; the internal torque equals T1 + T2 = 200000 + (-250000) = -50000 N·mm.
Report the sign and magnitude of the internal torque in each segment.

Part (b): Compute the maximum shear stress in each segment.
Compute the polar moment of inertia J = π·d^4 / 32 using d = 35 mm.
Compute the outer radius c = d / 2 = 17.5 mm.
Apply the torsion formula τ = T·c / J to each segment using the respective internal torque magnitude.
Report the maximum shear stress in Segment 1 and Segment 2 in MPa.

Part (c): Compute the angle of twist at x = L relative to the fixed end.
Compute the twist of Segment 1 as φ1 = T_seg1 · L1 / (G · J), where L1 = 800 mm and T_seg1 = -50000 N·mm.
Compute the twist of Segment 2 as φ2 = T_seg2 · L2 / (G · J), where L2 = 400 mm and T_seg2 = 200000 N·mm.
Sum the two segment twists to obtain the total angle of twist at x = 1200 mm: φ_total = φ1 + φ2.
Report φ_total in radians.
