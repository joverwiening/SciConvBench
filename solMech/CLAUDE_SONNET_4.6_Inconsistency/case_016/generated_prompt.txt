Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

Model the beam as an Euler–Bernoulli cantilever (small deflection, linear elastic).

The beam has length L = 800 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.

The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with elastic modulus E = 69000 MPa.

The constant second moment of area is I = 1600000 mm^4.

A single downward point load P = 2500 N is applied at the free end (x = L).

No other loads or couples are applied.

The bending moment varies linearly as M(x) = P(L − x).

Compute the flexural rigidity as EI = 69000 × 1600000 N·mm².

Compute the slope at the free end using θ = PL² / (2EI).

Substitute P = 2500 N, L = 800 mm, and EI into the slope formula and evaluate numerically.

Report the slope in radians and also convert it to degrees by multiplying by 180/π.

Compute the vertical deflection at the free end using δ = PL³ / (3EI).

Substitute P = 2500 N, L = 800 mm, and EI into the deflection formula and evaluate numerically.

Report the deflection in mm, with a positive value indicating a downward displacement.

Present the two final answers clearly: (i) free-end slope in radians and degrees, and (ii) free-end deflection in mm.
