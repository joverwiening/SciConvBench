Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The rod is made of AISI 1020 steel with initial diameter d0 = 20 mm and length L = 1200 mm.

The applied axial tensile load is P = 35000 N, applied through the centroid.

Use E = 200000 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.30.

Assume linear elastic isotropic material behavior under standard uniaxial tension (free lateral surfaces).

Compute the cross-sectional area as A = π · (d0/2)² = π · (10)² = 100π mm².

Compute (i) the average axial normal stress as σ = P / A = 35000 / (100π) MPa.

Compute (ii) the axial strain as ε_axial = σ / E.

Compute (iii) the lateral strain as ε_lateral = −ν · ε_axial = −0.30 · ε_axial.

Compute (iv) the change in diameter as Δd = ε_lateral · d0 = ε_lateral · 20 mm.

A negative Δd indicates a reduction in diameter under tensile load.

Do not apply any plane strain constraint; the lateral strain is free and nonzero.
