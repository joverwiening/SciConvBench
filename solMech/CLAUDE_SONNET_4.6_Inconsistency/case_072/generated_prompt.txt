Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The shaft is solid and circular with diameter d = 30 mm and length L = 1000 mm.

The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa.

The applied torque is constant along the shaft: T = 450000 N·mm.

Compute the polar moment of area using J = π d^4 / 32.

Substitute d = 30 mm to get J = π (30)^4 / 32.

Compute the torsional strain energy using U = integral from 0 to L of T^2 / (2 G J) dx.

Because T, G, and J are all constant along the shaft, this simplifies to U = T^2 L / (2 G J).

Apply Castigliano's theorem to obtain the angle of twist: φ = ∂U / ∂T = T L / (G J).

Substitute T = 450000 N·mm, L = 1000 mm, G = 25939.8 MPa, and the computed J into φ = T L / (G J).

Report the resulting angle of twist φ in radians.
