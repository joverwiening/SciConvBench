Use N–mm–MPa units. Assume linear elastic behavior and uniform temperature change.

A prismatic structural steel rod of length L = 1200 mm and diameter d = 20 mm is placed between two rigid, unyielding supports so that its total change in length is constrained to be zero. The rod experiences a uniform temperature increase of ΔT = 35 °C. Take E = 200000 MPa and coefficient of thermal expansion α = 1e-05 1/°C.

Determine (i) the thermal stress that develops in the rod and indicate whether it is tensile or compressive, and (ii) the corresponding axial force carried by the rod.
