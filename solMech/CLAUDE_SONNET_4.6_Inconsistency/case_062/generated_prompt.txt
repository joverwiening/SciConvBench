Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The rod is prismatic and made of structural steel with linear elastic behavior.
The rod has length L = 1200 mm and circular cross-section with diameter d = 20 mm.
Compute the cross-sectional area as A = π × (d/2)² = π × 10² mm².

The rod is placed between two rigid, unyielding supports that enforce zero total change in length.
The rod experiences a uniform temperature increase of ΔT = 35 °C.
Use elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa and coefficient of thermal expansion α = 1×10⁻⁵ 1/°C.

Apply the standard fully-restrained thermal stress derivation.
The free thermal strain that would occur without restraint is ε_thermal = α × ΔT.
Because the supports enforce zero net deformation, the mechanical strain must exactly cancel the free thermal strain.
The mechanical strain is therefore ε_mech = −α × ΔT (compressive).

Calculate the thermal stress as σ = E × ε_mech = −E × α × ΔT.
Substitute values: σ = −200000 × 1×10⁻⁵ × 35.
Report the thermal stress in MPa and state that it is compressive (negative sign indicates compression).

Calculate the axial force as F = σ × A.
Report the axial force in Newtons and confirm it is a compressive force acting along the rod axis.

State part (i) as the thermal stress value in MPa with its compressive nature identified.
State part (ii) as the axial compressive force value in N.
