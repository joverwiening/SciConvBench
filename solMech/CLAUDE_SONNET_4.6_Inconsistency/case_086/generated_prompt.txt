Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.
The bar is a stepped circular bar made of structural steel with linear-elastic behavior.
The bar is fixed at its left end and loaded in tension at its right end by an axial force P = 25000 N.
Segment 1 is the left segment with length L1 = 400 mm and diameter d1 = 16 mm.
Segment 2 is the right segment with length L2 = 400 mm and diameter d2 = 40 mm.
Use Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa for both segments.
The same axial force P = 25000 N acts through both segments.
Compute the cross-sectional area of each segment using A = π × d² / 4.
A1 = π × 16² / 4 = 201.062 mm².
A2 = π × 40² / 4 = 1256.637 mm².
Compute the normal stress in each segment using σ = P / A.
σ1 = 25000 / 201.062 = 124.34 MPa (tension).
σ2 = 25000 / 1256.637 = 19.89 MPa (tension).
Compute the elongation of each segment using δ = P × L / (A × E).
δ1 = 25000 × 400 / (201.062 × 200000) = 0.2488 mm.
δ2 = 25000 × 400 / (1256.637 × 200000) = 0.03979 mm.
Compute the total elongation as the sum of the two segment elongations.
δ_total = δ1 + δ2 = 0.2488 + 0.03979 = 0.2886 mm.
Report result (i) as: normal stress in Segment 1 is 124.34 MPa and in Segment 2 is 19.89 MPa, both in tension.
Report result (ii) as: total elongation of the bar between the fixed end and the load application point is 0.2886 mm.
