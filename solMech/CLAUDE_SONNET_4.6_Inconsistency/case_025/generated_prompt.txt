Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The beam is a cantilever of length L = 1500 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.

The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with elastic modulus E = 69000 MPa.

The second moment of area is constant at I = 2500000 mm^4.

A downward point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end (x = L).

Define the bending moment as M(x) = P · L, a constant value over the full span from x = 0 to x = L.

Write the bending strain energy as U = integral from 0 to L of M(x)^2 / (2 · E · I) dx.

Substitute M(x) = P · L into the integrand to get U = integral from 0 to L of (P · L)^2 / (2 · E · I) dx.

Evaluate the integral: U = (P · L)^2 · L / (2 · E · I) = P^2 · L^3 / (2 · E · I).

Apply Castigliano's theorem: the vertical deflection at the free end is delta = dU/dP.

Differentiate U with respect to P: delta = d/dP [P^2 · L^3 / (2 · E · I)] = 2 · P · L^3 / (2 · E · I) = P · L^3 / (E · I).

Substitute the numerical values: P = 500 N, L = 1500 mm, E = 69000 MPa, I = 2500000 mm^4.

Compute L^3 = 1500^3 = 3375000000 mm^3.

Compute E · I = 69000 · 2500000 = 172500000000 N·mm^2.

Compute delta = 500 · 3375000000 / 172500000000.

Report the final vertical deflection at the free end as delta = 9.7826 mm (rounded to four decimal places).
