Q: The standard definitions are:

Engineering stress = P / A₀ (original area), and Engineering strain = ΔL / L₀ (original gage length).

You asked to "compute the engineering stress using the current cross-sectional area" and "engineering strain using the current elongated length," which would give true stress and true strain, not engineering stress and strain.

Should I compute **engineering** stress and strain (using original area A₀ and original length L₀), or **true** stress and strain (using current/deformed area and current length)?
A: Use the original area A₀ and original gage length L₀ — standard engineering stress and strain.
