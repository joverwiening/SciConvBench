Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The specimen has an initial diameter d0 = 12.5 mm and an initial gage length L0 = 50 mm.
The applied axial load is P = 15000 N and the elongation over the gage length is ΔL = 0.15 mm.

Compute the original cross-sectional area A0 = π × (d0/2)² = π × (6.25)² mm².
A0 = π × 39.0625 ≈ 122.718 mm².

Compute engineering stress as σ = P / A0.
σ = 15000 N / 122.718 mm² ≈ 122.23 MPa.

Compute engineering strain as ε = ΔL / L0.
ε = 0.15 mm / 50 mm = 0.003 (dimensionless).

Compute the modulus of elasticity as E = σ / ε.
E = 122.23 MPa / 0.003 ≈ 40743 MPa, which equals approximately 40.74 GPa.

Report A0 in mm², σ in MPa, ε as a dimensionless ratio, and E in MPa (or GPa by dividing by 1000).
All calculations use the original area A0 and original gage length L0, consistent with standard engineering stress and strain definitions.
