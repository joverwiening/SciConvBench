Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The bar is a stepped circular bar made of structural steel, fixed at its left end and loaded in tension at its right end by P = 35000 N.

Segment 1 (left) has length L1 = 400 mm and diameter d1 = 16 mm.
Segment 2 (right) has length L2 = 800 mm and diameter d2 = 40 mm.
The elastic modulus is E = 200000 MPa.

Compute the cross-sectional area of each segment using A = π × d² / 4.
A1 = π × 16² / 4 = π × 256 / 4 = 201.062 mm².
A2 = π × 40² / 4 = π × 1600 / 4 = 1256.637 mm².

The same axial force P = 35000 N acts on both segments because the bar is a statically determinate series assembly.

Compute the normal stress in each segment using σ = P / A.
σ1 = 35000 / 201.062 = 174.04 MPa (tension).
σ2 = 35000 / 1256.637 = 27.85 MPa (tension).

The stresses differ because the cross-sectional areas differ.

Compute the elongation of each segment using δ = P × L / (A × E).
δ1 = 35000 × 400 / (201.062 × 200000) = 14000000 / 40212400 = 0.3481 mm.
δ2 = 35000 × 800 / (1256.637 × 200000) = 28000000 / 251327400 = 0.1114 mm.

Compute the total elongation as the sum of the two segment elongations (series method).
δ_total = δ1 + δ2 = 0.3481 + 0.1114 = 0.4595 mm.

Result (i): The normal stress in Segment 1 is 174.04 MPa and in Segment 2 is 27.85 MPa, both tensile.
Result (ii): The total elongation of the bar between the fixed end and the load application point is 0.4595 mm.
