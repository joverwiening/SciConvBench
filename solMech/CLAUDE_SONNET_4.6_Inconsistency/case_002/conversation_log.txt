Q: I have a contradiction to resolve before proceeding.

The problem states the same axial force P = 35000 N passes through both segments (which is correct for a statically determinate bar in series), but then instructs that the normal stress is identical in the two segments. Since the two segments have different cross-sectional areas, the same axial force produces different stresses in each segment (σ = P/A). These two statements cannot both be true simultaneously.

Which interpretation should I use: (a) the physically correct one where the same force P acts on both segments but stress differs because the areas differ, or (b) the stated assumption that stress is identical in both segments (which would require different forces in each segment)?
A: Use interpretation (a): the same axial force P = 35000 N acts on both segments, and the normal stress differs between segments because the cross-sectional areas are different. Compute σ = P/A for each segment separately.
