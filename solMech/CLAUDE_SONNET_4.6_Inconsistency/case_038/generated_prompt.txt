Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.
Apply Euler–Bernoulli beam theory.
The beam is simply supported with span L = 3500 mm.
The boundary conditions are: deflection = 0 at x = 0 and deflection = 0 at x = L; slopes at both ends are non-zero and are to be computed, not prescribed.
The beam carries a uniformly distributed load w = 0.5 N/mm over the full span.
The material is AISI 1020 steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The second moment of area is I = 3000000 mm^4.
Compute the flexural rigidity as EI = 200000 × 3000000 = 6.0 × 10^11 N·mm².
Use the standard Euler–Bernoulli closed-form solution for a simply supported beam under full-span UDL.
The deflection at position x along the span is given by v(x) = (w × x / (24 × E × I)) × (L³ − 2 × L × x² + x³).
Determine the location of maximum deflection by symmetry; for a symmetric UDL on a simply supported beam it occurs at the midspan x = L/2 = 1750 mm.
Compute the maximum deflection by substituting x = L/2 into the deflection formula: v_max = (5 × w × L⁴) / (384 × E × I).
Substitute values: v_max = (5 × 0.5 × 3500⁴) / (384 × 6.0 × 10^11).
Evaluate 3500⁴ = 1.50063 × 10^14 mm⁴.
Evaluate numerator: 5 × 0.5 × 1.50063 × 10^14 = 3.75156 × 10^14 N·mm³.
Evaluate denominator: 384 × 6.0 × 10^11 = 2.304 × 10^14 N·mm².
Compute v_max = 3.75156 × 10^14 / 2.304 × 10^14 ≈ 1.629 mm (downward).
Compute the slope at x = 0 using the formula θ(0) = dv/dx at x = 0 = (w × L³) / (24 × E × I).
Substitute values: θ(0) = (0.5 × 3500³) / (24 × 6.0 × 10^11).
Evaluate 3500³ = 4.2875 × 10^10 mm³.
Evaluate numerator: 0.5 × 4.2875 × 10^10 = 2.14375 × 10^10 N·mm².
Evaluate denominator: 24 × 6.0 × 10^11 = 1.44 × 10^13 N·mm².
Compute θ(0) = 2.14375 × 10^10 / 1.44 × 10^13 ≈ 1.489 × 10^-3 radians (positive, indicating the left end rotates clockwise when the beam sags downward).
Report the maximum deflection as approximately 1.629 mm occurring at midspan x = 1750 mm.
Report the slope at the left support x = 0 as approximately 1.489 × 10^-3 radians.
