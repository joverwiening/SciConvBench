Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

Assume Euler buckling applies, meaning the column is slender and remains elastic.

The column is straight with a constant cross-section along its full length.

The material is structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.

The second moment of area is I = 1800000 mm^4.

The column length is L = 2500 mm.

The end conditions are pinned-pinned, giving an effective length factor K = 1.0.

Compute the effective length as KL = 1.0 x 2500 = 2500 mm.

Compute the Euler critical buckling load using the formula P_cr = pi^2 x E x I / (K x L)^2.

Substitute values: P_cr = (9.8696044...) x 200000 x 1800000 / (2500)^2.

Compute the numerator: pi^2 x 200000 x 1800000 = 9.8696044 x 3.6 x 10^11 = 3.55305759 x 10^12 N·mm^2.

Compute the denominator: (2500)^2 = 6250000 mm^2.

Divide to obtain P_cr = 3.55305759 x 10^12 / 6250000 = 568489 N, which rounds to 568489 N.

Report P_cr = 568489 N.

Convert to kilonewtons by dividing by 1000: P_cr = 568.489 kN.

Round the final answers to three decimal places: P_cr = 568489.215 N, or equivalently 568.489 kN.
