The beam AB is simply supported with span L = 4000 mm.
A concentrated vertical load P = 12000 N acts downward at 2000 mm from support A.
There are no other loads.
Use N and N·mm as units throughout.

Sign convention: positive shear V is upward on the left cut face; positive bending moment M causes sagging (tension at the bottom fibers).

Step 1: Find reactions.
Take moments about A: R_B × 4000 = 12000 × 2000, so R_B = 6000 N upward.
Vertical equilibrium: R_A + R_B = 12000, so R_A = 6000 N upward.

Step 2: Cut the beam at x = 1000 mm from A.
The cut is between A (x = 0) and the load (x = 2000 mm), so only R_A acts on the left free body.

Step 3: Compute shear V at x = 1000 mm.
On the left free body, the only vertical force is R_A = 6000 N acting upward.
For equilibrium, the internal shear on the left cut face must act downward to balance R_A.
By the sign convention (positive shear is upward on the left cut face), a downward internal shear on the left cut face is negative.
V = −6000 N.

Step 4: Compute bending moment M at x = 1000 mm.
Take moments about the cut section from the left free body.
M = R_A × 1000 = 6000 × 1000 = 6,000,000 N·mm.
R_A acts upward at 1000 mm to the left of the cut, producing a sagging (tension at bottom) effect, which is positive.
M = +6,000,000 N·mm.

Final answers:
V = −6000 N
M = +6,000,000 N·mm
