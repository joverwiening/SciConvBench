Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.
The beam is made of AISI 1020 steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa (N/mm²).
The second moment of area about the neutral axis is I = 2500000 mm⁴.
The bending moment at the section of interest is M = 3000000 N·mm.
Apply the Euler-Bernoulli curvature formula κ = M / (E · I).
Compute the denominator: E · I = 200000 × 2500000 = 500000000000 N·mm².
Compute the curvature: κ = 3000000 / 500000000000 = 6 × 10⁻⁶ mm⁻¹.
Compute the radius of curvature as the reciprocal of curvature: ρ = 1 / κ = 1 / (6 × 10⁻⁶) = 166666.67 mm.
Report κ = 6 × 10⁻⁶ mm⁻¹ and ρ ≈ 166666.67 mm as the final results.
Ignore the polar second moment J; it plays no role in this calculation.
