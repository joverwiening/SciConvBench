Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.
The rod is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with initial diameter d0 = 40 mm and length L = 500 mm.
The axial tensile load is P = 35000 N applied through the centroid.
Use elastic modulus E = 69000 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.33.
Assume linear elastic isotropic behavior with a standard uniaxial stress state and free lateral surfaces.

Compute the cross-sectional area as A = π·(d0/2)² = π·(20)² mm².
A = π · 400 = 1256.637 mm².

(i) Compute the average axial normal stress as σ = P / A.
σ = 35000 / 1256.637 = 27.852 MPa (tensile).

(ii) Compute the axial strain as ε_axial = σ / E.
ε_axial = 27.852 / 69000 = 4.036 × 10⁻⁴ mm/mm (dimensionless).

(iii) Compute the lateral strain as ε_lateral = −ν · ε_axial.
ε_lateral = −0.33 · 4.036 × 10⁻⁴ = −1.332 × 10⁻⁴ mm/mm (compressive, indicating diameter reduction).

(iv) Compute the change in diameter as Δd = ε_lateral · d0.
Δd = −1.332 × 10⁻⁴ · 40 = −5.328 × 10⁻³ mm.
The negative sign indicates the diameter decreases under tensile load.
