Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

Apply Euler–Bernoulli beam theory assuming small deflections and linear elastic material behavior.

Model a cantilever beam of length L = 800 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.

Use material 6061-T6 aluminum with elastic modulus E = 69000 MPa.

Use a constant cross-section with second moment of area I = 800000 mm^4.

Apply a single downward point load P = 500 N at the free end (x = L). No other loads or couples are applied.

Use the linearly varying bending moment M(x) = P(L − x) over the full span.

Compute the slope at the free end using the formula: theta = PL^2 / (2EI).

Compute the vertical deflection at the free end using the formula: delta = PL^3 / (3EI).

Substitute P = 500, L = 800, E = 69000, I = 800000 into both formulas.

Report the free-end slope in both radians and degrees.

Report the free-end deflection in mm.
