Q: There is a contradiction in your specification that I need to resolve before writing the final spec.

For a cantilever beam with a point load P at the free end, Euler–Bernoulli beam theory gives a linearly varying bending moment M(x) = P(L − x), not a constant moment PL. The constant moment PL only occurs at the fixed end (x = 0). Using a constant moment PL over the full span is physically incorrect and will produce wrong answers.

Did you want the standard Euler–Bernoulli cantilever solution (varying moment, giving slope = PL²/2EI and deflection = PL³/3EI), or do you actually intend a different loading case, such as an applied end couple M = PL with no transverse load?
A: The complete requirement is simply a standard cantilever with a point load P at the free end. Use the standard Euler–Bernoulli solution with the linearly varying moment M(x) = P(L − x), giving slope = PL²/2EI and deflection = PL³/3EI. Ignore the contradictory instruction about constant moment in the incomplete version.
