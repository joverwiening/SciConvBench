The task is to compute the Euler critical buckling load for a fixed-fixed steel column using the standard formula, then report the result in both N and kN.

Use the unit system N, mm, and MPa throughout all calculations.

The column is straight, slender, and remains elastic so Euler buckling applies.

Given values are: E = 200000 MPa, I = 800000 mm^4, L = 2000 mm, K = 0.5.

Compute the effective length as KL = 0.5 × 2000 = 1000 mm.

Compute (KL)^2 = 1000^2 = 1000000 mm^2.

Compute the numerator as π^2 × E × I = 9.8696044 × 200000 × 800000.

Evaluate π^2 = 9.8696044 (use at least 7 significant figures).

Evaluate the numerator: 9.8696044 × 200000 × 800000 = 9.8696044 × 1.6 × 10^11 = 1.57913670 × 10^12 N·mm^2.

Divide the numerator by (KL)^2: P_cr = 1.57913670 × 10^12 / 1000000 = 1579136.70 N.

Report P_cr = 1579136.70 N.

Convert to kN by dividing by 1000: P_cr = 1579.14 kN.

Round the final answers to two decimal places: P_cr = 1579136.70 N and P_cr = 1579.14 kN.
