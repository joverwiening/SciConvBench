Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The column is a solid circular cross-section with diameter d = 30 mm and length L = 1500 mm.

The material is structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.

The effective length factor is K = 2.0, giving an effective length Le = K × L = 2.0 × 1500 = 3000 mm.

Compute the area moment of inertia using I = π d⁴ / 64.

Substitute d = 30 mm to get I = π × (30)⁴ / 64.

Compute the Euler critical load using P_cr = π² × E × I / Le².

Substitute E = 200000 MPa, the computed I, and Le = 3000 mm into the formula.

Report P_cr in Newtons (N).

Apply a factor of safety n = 2.0 to obtain the allowable load using P_allow = P_cr / n.

Report P_allow in Newtons (N).

Assume Euler buckling governs and no other buckling or yielding check is required.
