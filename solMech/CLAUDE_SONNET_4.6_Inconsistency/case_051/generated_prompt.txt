Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The beam has a rectangular cross section with width b = 50 mm and height h = 80 mm.

The internal shear force at the section of interest is V = 20000 N.

Compute the cross-sectional area as A = b × h = 50 × 80 = 4000 mm².

Part (a): Compute the maximum transverse shear stress using the formula τ_max = 1.5 × V / A.
Substitute V = 20000 N and A = 4000 mm² to obtain τ_max in MPa.

Part (b): Compute the shear stress at a point located y = 35 mm from the neutral axis (y measured positive upward, with y = 0 at the neutral axis) using the shear formula τ = V Q / (I × b).

Compute the second moment of area about the neutral axis as I = b h³ / 12 using b = 50 mm and h = 80 mm.

Compute Q as the first moment of area of the portion of the cross section above the level y = 35 mm about the neutral axis.
The area above y = 35 mm is a rectangle with width b = 50 mm and height equal to (h/2 − y) = 40 − 35 = 5 mm.
The centroid of that area is located at ȳ = y + (h/2 − y) / 2 = 35 + 2.5 = 37.5 mm from the neutral axis.
Compute Q = (b × (h/2 − y)) × ȳ = 50 × 5 × 37.5 mm³.

Substitute V, Q, I, and b into τ = V Q / (I × b) to obtain τ in MPa.

Report all results in MPa rounded to two decimal places.
