Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The rod is a circular cross-section with initial diameter d0 = 25 mm and length L = 1200 mm.

The applied axial tensile load is P = 45000 N, acting through the centroid.

Material properties are E = 200000 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.29.

Assume linear elastic, isotropic behavior under a standard uniaxial stress state with free lateral expansion.

Compute (i) the average axial normal stress σ = P / A, where A = π × d0² / 4.

Compute (ii) the axial strain ε_axial = σ / E.

Compute (iii) the lateral strain ε_lateral = −ν × ε_axial.

Compute (iv) the change in diameter Δd = ε_lateral × d0.

Report all four results numerically in the specified units (stress in MPa, strains dimensionless, Δd in mm).
