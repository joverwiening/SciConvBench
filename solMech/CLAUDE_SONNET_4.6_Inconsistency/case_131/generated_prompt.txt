Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.
Assume small strains.
The adhesive layer is rectangular with a total bonded area A = 3000 mm^2 and a thickness t = 8 mm.
A uniform in-plane shear force V = 12000 N is applied to the layer.
The adhesive is a deformable isotropic material with shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.
Do not treat the layer as rigid; relative slip between the two faces is permitted.
Compute (i) the average shear stress as τ_avg = V / A.
Compute (ii) the average shear strain as γ_avg = τ_avg / G.
Compute (iii) the relative lateral displacement between the two faces as Δ = γ_avg · t.
Report all three results in consistent N–mm–MPa units.
