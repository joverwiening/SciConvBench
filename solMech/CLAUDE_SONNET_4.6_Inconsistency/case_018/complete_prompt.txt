Use N–mm–MPa units.

A solid circular shaft of diameter d = 40 mm and total length L = 1500 mm is fixed at x = 0. A torque T1 = 300000 N·mm is applied at the free end x = L (positive sense). In addition, a torque T2 = -150000 N·mm is applied at x = 750 mm along the shaft (negative sense means opposite direction). The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with G = 25939.8 MPa.

(a) Construct the internal torque in each segment (0→a and a→L).
(b) Compute the maximum shear stress in each segment.
(c) Compute the angle of twist at x = L relative to the fixed end by summing the twist of each segment.
