Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The bar is a stepped circular bar made of structural steel, fixed at its left end and loaded in tension at its right end by P = 25000 N.

Segment 1 is the left segment with length L1 = 300 mm and diameter d1 = 12 mm.
Segment 2 is the right segment with length L2 = 600 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm.
The elastic modulus is E = 200000 MPa for both segments.
The bar is statically determinate and the two segments are in series.
The same axial force P = 25000 N acts through both segments.
Behavior is linear-elastic.

Compute the cross-sectional area of each segment using A = π × d² / 4.
A1 = π × 12² / 4 = 113.097 mm²
A2 = π × 30² / 4 = 706.858 mm²

Compute the normal stress in each segment using σ = P / A.
σ1 = 25000 / 113.097 = 221.05 MPa (tension)
σ2 = 25000 / 706.858 = 35.37 MPa (tension)

Compute the elongation of each segment using δ = P × L / (A × E).
δ1 = 25000 × 300 / (113.097 × 200000) = 0.3316 mm
δ2 = 25000 × 600 / (706.858 × 200000) = 0.1061 mm

Compute the total elongation as the sum of the two individual elongations.
δ_total = δ1 + δ2 = 0.3316 + 0.1061 = 0.4377 mm

Report the normal stress in Segment 1 as approximately 221.05 MPa in tension.
Report the normal stress in Segment 2 as approximately 35.37 MPa in tension.
Report the total elongation of the bar as approximately 0.4377 mm.
