Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The rod is a solid circular brass rod with initial diameter d0 = 30 mm and length L = 500 mm.

The applied axial tensile load is P = 25000 N, applied through the centroid.

Material properties are E = 105000 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.34.

Assume linear elastic isotropic behavior and a standard uniaxial stress state with free lateral surfaces.

Compute the cross-sectional area as A = π · (d0/2)² = π · (15)² mm².

Compute the average axial normal stress as σ_axial = P / A.

Compute the axial strain as ε_axial = σ_axial / E.

Compute the lateral strain as ε_lateral = −ν · ε_axial.

Compute the change in diameter as Δd = ε_lateral · d0.

Report all four results: σ_axial in MPa, ε_axial (dimensionless), ε_lateral (dimensionless), and Δd in mm.
