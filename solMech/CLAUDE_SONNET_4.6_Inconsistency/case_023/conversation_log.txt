Q: I need one clarification before writing the final specification.

The instruction says "replace the distributed load by its resultant wL applied at midspan." A resultant force of magnitude wL = 2500 N applied at the midspan point (x = L/2 = 1250 mm from the fixed end) is a point-load approximation — it does NOT give the same deflection or slope as the true uniform distributed load, and it is not a standard simplification in Euler–Bernoulli beam theory.

The exact closed-form Euler–Bernoulli results for a cantilever with a true UDL are well known:
- Max deflection at free end: wL⁴ / (8EI)
- Slope at free end: wL³ / (6EI)

Should I use the TRUE uniform distributed load formulas (wL⁴/8EI and wL³/6EI), or do you specifically want the point-load-at-midspan approximation (which gives different numerical answers)?
A: Use the true uniform distributed load formulas (wL⁴/8EI and wL³/6EI).
