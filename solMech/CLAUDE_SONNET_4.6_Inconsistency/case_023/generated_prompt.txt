Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.
Apply Euler–Bernoulli beam theory.
The beam is a cantilever of length L = 2500 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
The beam carries a uniformly distributed load w = 1.0 N/mm acting downward over the entire span.
The material is AISI 1020 steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The second moment of area is I = 1000000 mm^4.
Compute the maximum deflection at the free end using the formula: delta_max = wL^4 / (8EI).
Compute the slope at the free end using the formula: theta_max = wL^3 / (6EI).
Substitute L = 2500, w = 1.0, E = 200000, I = 1000000 into each formula and evaluate numerically.
Express the maximum deflection in mm and the slope in radians.
Report both results clearly with their numerical values and units.
