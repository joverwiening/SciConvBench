Q: I need one clarification before writing the final specification.

The standard Euler buckling formula uses the second moment of area (area moment of inertia) I = π d⁴/64 for a solid circular cross-section, not the polar moment J = π d⁴/32. The polar moment J is twice the area moment I, so using J would give a critical load twice as large as the correct Euler result.

Are you intentionally requiring J = π d⁴/32 in place of I (knowing it doubles the result), or did you mean to use the standard I = π d⁴/64?
A: Use the standard I = π d⁴/64. The mention of the polar moment in the incomplete version was an error — ignore it.
