Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.
The column has length L = 1800 mm.
The material is structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The applied compressive load is P = 30000 N.
The required factor of safety against buckling is n = 2.0.
The effective length factor is K = 1.0.
The cross section is solid circular with diameter d.
The second moment of area is I = π d^4 / 64.
The Euler critical buckling load is P_cr = π^2 · E · I / (K · L)^2.
The design requirement is P_cr ≥ n · P, that is P_cr ≥ 2.0 · 30000 = 60000 N.
Substitute I = π d^4 / 64 into the expression for P_cr to get P_cr = π^3 · E · d^4 / (64 · (K · L)^2).
Set P_cr equal to n · P and solve for d: d^4 = 64 · n · P · (K · L)^2 / (π^3 · E).
Substitute all numerical values: d^4 = 64 · 2.0 · 30000 · (1.0 · 1800)^2 / (π^3 · 200000).
Compute the numerator: 64 · 2.0 · 30000 · 3240000 = 1.24416 × 10^13.
Compute the denominator: π^3 · 200000 ≈ 31.00627 · 200000 ≈ 6201255.
Compute d^4 = 1.24416 × 10^13 / 6201255 ≈ 2006310 mm^4.
Take the fourth root: d = (2006310)^(1/4) ≈ 37.67 mm.
Round up to the next whole millimeter to satisfy the design requirement: d_min = 38 mm.
Report the minimum required diameter as d_min = 38 mm.
