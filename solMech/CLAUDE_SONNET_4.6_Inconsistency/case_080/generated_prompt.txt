Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

The specimen has an initial diameter d0 = 12.5 mm and an initial gage length L0 = 50 mm.
The applied axial load is P = 12000 N and the elongation over the gage length is ΔL = 0.12 mm.

Compute the original cross-sectional area A0 using A0 = π × (d0/2)².
Compute engineering stress as σ = P / A0, with the result in MPa (N/mm²).
Compute engineering strain as ε = ΔL / L0, which is dimensionless.
Compute the modulus of elasticity as E = σ / ε, with the result in MPa.
Do not use the current (deformed) area or the current elongated length anywhere in the calculations.
Report A0 in mm², σ in MPa, ε as a dimensionless number, and E in MPa (or convert to GPa by dividing by 1000).
