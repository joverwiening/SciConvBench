The column is made of AISI 1020 steel but material yield strength is not needed since Euler elastic buckling is assumed throughout.
Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system for all calculations.
The column length is L = 2000 mm.
The elastic modulus is E = 200000 MPa (which equals 200000 N/mm^2).
The second moment of area is I = 1200000 mm^4.
The effective length factor is K = 0.7.
Compute the effective length as K times L, giving KL = 0.7 times 2000 = 1400 mm.
Compute the Euler critical buckling load using the formula P_cr = pi^2 times E times I divided by (K times L)^2.
Substitute values: P_cr = (pi^2 times 200000 times 1200000) divided by (1400^2).
Compute the numerator: pi^2 = 9.8696044, times 200000 = 1973920.88, times 1200000 = 2368705056000.
Compute the denominator: 1400^2 = 1960000.
Divide numerator by denominator: P_cr = 2368705056000 divided by 1960000 = 1208522.98 N.
Round the result to six significant figures: P_cr = 1208520 N (approximately).
Report P_cr = 1208523 N, which equals approximately 1208.52 kN.
Do not apply any additional safety factors or correction factors beyond the K factor already incorporated in the effective length.
The effective length factor K = 0.7 is used only inside the (KL)^2 denominator of the Euler formula and does not alter EI or any other term.
