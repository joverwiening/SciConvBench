Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.
Assume small strains.
The adhesive layer is rectangular with a total cross-sectional area A = 4000 mm^2 and a thickness t = 20 mm.
A uniform in-plane shear force V = 8000 N is applied to the layer.
The adhesive is an isotropic, linearly elastic, deformable material with shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.
The "rigid in shear" clause is disregarded; the material deforms elastically under shear.
Compute (i) the average shear stress as τ_avg = V / A.
Compute (ii) the average shear strain as γ_avg = τ_avg / G.
Compute (iii) the relative lateral displacement between the two faces of the layer as Δ = γ_avg · t.
Report all three results in consistent N–mm–MPa units.
