Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system throughout.

Model a stepped circular bar of AISI 1020 steel in a series arrangement: Segment 1 is on the left, Segment 2 is on the right, and they are connected end to end along a common axis.

The bar is fixed at the left end of Segment 1 and loaded in tension by P = 35000 N applied at the right end of Segment 2.

Segment 1 has length L1 = 300 mm and diameter d1 = 12 mm.
Segment 2 has length L2 = 800 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm.
Use E = 200000 MPa for both segments.
Assume linear-elastic behavior throughout.

Compute the cross-sectional area of each segment using A = π × d² / 4.
A1 = π × 12² / 4 = 113.097 mm².
A2 = π × 30² / 4 = 706.858 mm².

Because the bar is in a series arrangement, the same axial force P = 35000 N acts on every cross-section of both segments.

Compute the normal stress in each segment independently using σ = P / A.
σ1 = 35000 / 113.097 = 309.56 MPa (tensile).
σ2 = 35000 / 706.858 = 49.52 MPa (tensile).

Compute the elongation of each segment using δ = P × L / (A × E).
δ1 = 35000 × 300 / (113.097 × 200000) = 0.4648 mm.
δ2 = 35000 × 800 / (706.858 × 200000) = 0.1980 mm.

Compute the total elongation as the sum of the two segment elongations.
δ_total = δ1 + δ2 = 0.4648 + 0.1980 = 0.6628 mm.

Report σ1, σ2, δ1, δ2, and δ_total as the final outputs.
