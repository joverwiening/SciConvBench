Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
Apply Euler–Bernoulli beam theory with small deflection and linear elastic assumptions.  
The beam is a cantilever with length L = 1200 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.  
The material is AISI 1020 steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.  
The beam has a constant second moment of area I = 2000000 mm⁴.  
A downward point load P = 2000 N is applied at the free end.  
Calculate the slope at the free end using the formula θ = PL²/(2EI).  
Calculate the deflection at the free end using the formula δ = PL³/(3EI).  
Report the slope in radians.  
Report the deflection in millimeters.
