Q: What is the value of the first moment of area Q for the area above y = 35 mm?
A: Q = (area above y) × (distance from neutral axis to centroid of that area)  
Area above y = b × (h/2 − y) = 50 × (40 − 35) = 250 mm²  
Centroid of this area is at y + (h/2 − y)/2 = 35 + (40 − 35)/2 = 37.5 mm from neutral axis  

Q = 250 × 37.5 = 9,375 mm³
