Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
The cantilever beam has length L = 1500 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.  
The beam is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with elastic modulus E = 69000 MPa.  
The second moment of area I is constant and equal to 2500000 mm⁴.  
A downward point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end (x = L).  
The bending moment M(x) is approximated as constant and equal to the peak value M = P × L over the entire span from x = 0 to x = L.  
The bending strain energy U is calculated using the formula U = ∫₀ᴸ [M(x)² / (2EI)] dx.  
Substitute M(x) = P × L into the integral, so U = (P² × L²) / (2 × E × I) × ∫₀ᴸ dx.  
Evaluate the integral to get U = (P² × L³) / (2 × E × I).  
Apply Castigliano’s theorem: the vertical deflection at the free end is ∂U/∂P.  
Differentiate U with respect to P to obtain deflection δ = (2 × P × L³) / (2 × E × I) = (P × L³) / (E × I).  
Compute the numerical value of δ using P = 500 N, L = 1500 mm, E = 69000 MPa, and I = 2500000 mm⁴.  
The vertical deflection at the free end is δ = (500 × 1500³) / (69000 × 2500000) mm.  
Calculate the result.
