The column has a diameter d = 20 mm and length L = 2000 mm.  
The material is structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.  
The effective length factor K is 1.0.  
Euler buckling governs the failure mode.  
The cross-section is circular, so the polar moment of inertia J = π d⁴ / 32 is used.  
The critical buckling load is calculated using P_cr = (π² E I) / (K L)², where I = J / 2 = π d⁴ / 64.  
Compute the Euler critical load P_cr.  
Apply a factor of safety n = 2.0 to determine the allowable load P_allow = P_cr / n.  
Report the values of P_cr and P_allow.
