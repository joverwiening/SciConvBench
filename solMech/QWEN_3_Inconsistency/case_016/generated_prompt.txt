Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
Apply Euler–Bernoulli beam theory with small deflection and linear elastic assumptions.  
The beam is a cantilever with length L = 800 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.  
The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with elastic modulus E = 69000 MPa.  
The beam has a constant second moment of area I = 1600000 mm⁴.  
A downward point load P = 2500 N is applied at the free end (x = L).  
Calculate the slope at the free end using the formula θ = (P L²) / (2 E I).  
Calculate the vertical deflection at the free end using the formula δ = (P L³) / (3 E I).  
Report the slope in radians.  
Report the deflection in millimeters.
