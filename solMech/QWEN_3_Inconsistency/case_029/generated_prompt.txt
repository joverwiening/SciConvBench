Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
Assume linear elastic isotropic material behavior.  
The brass rod has an initial diameter d0 = 20 mm and length L = 1000 mm.  
The rod is subjected to an axial tensile load P = 25000 N applied through the centroid.  
The elastic modulus E = 105000 MPa.  
Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.34 is provided as a material reference but is not used for lateral strain calculation due to plane strain constraint.  
Plane strain condition applies in the two transverse directions, meaning lateral strain is zero.  
Compute the average axial normal stress as σ_axial = P / A, where A = π × (d0 / 2)².  
Compute the axial strain as ε_axial = σ_axial / E.  
Set the lateral (diametral) strain ε_lateral = 0 due to plane strain condition.  
Compute the change in diameter Δd = ε_lateral × d0, which equals 0.
