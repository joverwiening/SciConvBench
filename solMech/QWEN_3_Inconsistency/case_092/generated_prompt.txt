Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
Apply Euler–Bernoulli beam theory with small deflection and linear elastic assumptions.  
The beam is a cantilever with length L = 1800 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.  
The material is structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.  
The beam has a constant second moment of area I = 1200000 mm⁴.  
A downward point load P = 2000 N is applied at the free end (x = L).  
No external moment or couple is applied at the free end.  
Compute the slope (rotation) at the free end using the formula θ = (P L²) / (2 E I).  
Compute the vertical deflection at the free end using the formula δ = (P L³) / (3 E I).  
Report the slope in radians.  
Report the deflection in millimeters.
