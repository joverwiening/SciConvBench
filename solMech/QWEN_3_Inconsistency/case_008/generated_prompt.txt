Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
The two prismatic bars have equal length L = 500 mm and are mounted in parallel between two rigid plates.  
Bar 1 is made of AISI 1020 steel with cross-sectional area A1 = 300 mm² and elastic modulus E1 = 200000 MPa.  
Bar 2 is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with cross-sectional area A2 = 200 mm² and elastic modulus E2 = 69000 MPa.  
The bars are initially stress-free and perfectly bonded to rigid plates.  
A total tensile load P = 20000 N is applied to the top plate.  
The load is shared between the bars according to their axial stiffnesses.  
The elongation of both bars is identical due to compatibility.  
The sum of the forces in the two bars equals the applied load (equilibrium).  
The force in Bar 1 is F1 = (E1 × A1 / (E1 × A1 + E2 × A2)) × P.  
The force in Bar 2 is F2 = (E2 × A2 / (E1 × A1 + E2 × A2)) × P.  
The stress in Bar 1 is σ1 = F1 / A1.  
The stress in Bar 2 is σ2 = F2 / A2.  
The common elongation is δ = (F1 × L) / (E1 × A1) = (F2 × L) / (E2 × A2).
