Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
The shaft is made of 6061-T6 aluminum.  
Segment AB has length L1 = 300 mm and diameter d1 = 20 mm.  
Segment BC has length L2 = 500 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm.  
A torque T = 300000 N·mm is applied at end C.  
End A is fixed.  
The same internal torque T acts through both segments in series.  
The shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa.  
Calculate the polar moment of inertia J1 for segment AB using J1 = π × (d1⁴) / 32.  
Calculate the polar moment of inertia J2 for segment BC using J2 = π × (d2⁴) / 32.  
Calculate the torsional stiffness k1 for segment AB using k1 = G × J1 / L1.  
Calculate the torsional stiffness k2 for segment BC using k2 = G × J2 / L2.  
Since the segments are in series under the same torque, the total angle of twist is the sum of individual twists.  
Calculate the angle of twist φ1 for segment AB using φ1 = T × L1 / (G × J1).  
Calculate the angle of twist φ2 for segment BC using φ2 = T × L2 / (G × J2).  
Calculate the total angle of twist φ_total = φ1 + φ2.  
Calculate the maximum shear stress τ1 in segment AB using τ1 = T × (d1 / 2) / J1.  
Calculate the maximum shear stress τ2 in segment BC using τ2 = T × (d2 / 2) / J2.  
Report both maximum shear stresses.  
Identify the segment with the higher shear stress as the governing segment.  
Report the total angle of twist at C relative to A in radians.
