Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
Assume linear elastic material behavior.  
The shaft is made of AISI 1020 steel.  
The outer diameter do is 60 mm.  
The inner diameter di is 48 mm.  
The wall thickness is 6 mm.  
The shaft length L is 800 mm.  
The applied torque T is 450000 N·mm.  
The polar moment of inertia J is calculated as J = π(do⁴ − di⁴)/32.  
The shear modulus G is 76923.1 MPa.  
Model the hollow circular shaft as a thin-walled closed tube.  
Use the mean-area shear-flow relation to determine surface stress and twist.  
Compute the maximum shear stress at the outer surface using τ_max = T × r_o / J, where r_o = do/2.  
Compute the angle of twist φ over the length L using φ = (T × L) / (G × J).  
Report the maximum shear stress in MPa.  
Report the angle of twist in radians.
