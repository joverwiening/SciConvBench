Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
Assume linear elastic material behavior.  
The shaft is a hollow circular tube with outer diameter do = 40 mm and inner diameter di = 30 mm.  
The wall thickness is 5 mm, and the length L = 1000 mm.  
A constant torque T = 300000 N·mm is applied.  
Model the shaft as a thin-walled closed tube using the mean-area shear-flow relation.  
Use the polar moment of inertia J = π(do⁴ − di⁴)/32.  
Use the shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.  
Compute the maximum shear stress at the outer surface using τ_max = T * r / J, where r = do / 2.  
Compute the angle of twist φ = (T * L) / (G * J) in radians.
