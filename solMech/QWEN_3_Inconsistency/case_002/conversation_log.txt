Q: What is the exact definition or interpretation of "common elongation" in this context? Specifically, are the two segments being treated as deforming by the same amount despite their different lengths and diameters, or is this a misinterpretation of compatibility?
A: "Common elongation" is a misinterpretation. The segments deform sequentially under the same axial force, so elongation is not common. Each segment has its own elongation based on its geometry and material. The total elongation is the sum of individual elongations. Compatibility requires that the force is continuous and the deformations add up, not that they are equal.
