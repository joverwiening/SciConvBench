Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
Assume linear-elastic material behavior.  
The stepped circular bar is made of structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.  
The bar is fixed at the left end and subjected to a tensile load P = 35000 N at the right end.  
Segment 1 has length L1 = 400 mm and diameter d1 = 16 mm.  
Segment 2 has length L2 = 800 mm and diameter d2 = 40 mm.  
The axial force P is constant through both segments due to equilibrium.  
Normal stress in each segment is calculated as σ = P / A, where A is the cross-sectional area of the segment.  
Cross-sectional area of Segment 1 is A1 = π × (d1/2)².  
Cross-sectional area of Segment 2 is A2 = π × (d2/2)².  
Normal stress in Segment 1 is σ1 = P / A1.  
Normal stress in Segment 2 is σ2 = P / A2.  
Elongation of each segment is calculated as δ = (P × L) / (A × E).  
Elongation of Segment 1 is δ1 = (P × L1) / (A1 × E).  
Elongation of Segment 2 is δ2 = (P × L2) / (A2 × E).  
Total elongation is the sum of individual elongations: δ_total = δ1 + δ2.  
The normal stress is not identical in the two segments; it depends on their respective cross-sectional areas.  
Compatibility is satisfied by additive deformation and force continuity, not by equal elongation.  
Report the normal stress in Segment 1 and Segment 2 separately.  
Report the total elongation of the bar.
