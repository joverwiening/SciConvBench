Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
The cross-sectional area A is 200 mm².  
The length of segment AB is 700 mm.  
The length of segment BC is 600 mm.  
The length of segment CD is 500 mm.  
The elastic modulus E is 200000 MPa.  
The bar is prismatic and made of structural steel.  
The left end at point A is fixed, so displacement u_A = 0.  
An axial force of 18000 N is applied at point B in the positive direction (to the right).  
An axial force of -8000 N is applied at point C in the negative direction (to the left).  
An axial force of 20000 N is applied at point D in the positive direction (to the right).  
All forces are applied at the specified points along the bar.  
The internal axial force in segment AB is equal to the sum of all forces to the right of AB: 18000 N + (-8000 N) + 20000 N = 30000 N.  
The internal axial force in segment BC is equal to the sum of all forces to the right of BC: (-8000 N) + 20000 N = 12000 N.  
The internal axial force in segment CD is equal to the force to the right of CD: 20000 N.  
The normal stress in segment AB is 30000 N / 200 mm² = 150 MPa.  
The normal stress in segment BC is 12000 N / 200 mm² = 60 MPa.  
The normal stress in segment CD is 20000 N / 200 mm² = 100 MPa.  
The axial strain in segment AB is 150 MPa / 200000 MPa = 0.00075.  
The axial strain in segment BC is 60 MPa / 200000 MPa = 0.0003.  
The axial strain in segment CD is 100 MPa / 200000 MPa = 0.0005.  
The displacement of point B is strain_AB × length_AB = 0.00075 × 700 = 0.525 mm.  
The displacement of point C is displacement_B + (strain_BC × length_BC) = 0.525 + (0.0003 × 600) = 0.525 + 0.18 = 0.705 mm.  
The displacement of point D is displacement_C + (strain_CD × length_CD) = 0.705 + (0.0005 × 500) = 0.705 + 0.25 = 0.955 mm.  
The displacement of point D relative to point A is 0.955 mm.
