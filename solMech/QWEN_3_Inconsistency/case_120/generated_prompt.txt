Use N, mm, and MPa as the unit system.  
Assume linear elastic material behavior.  
Assume uniform temperature increase of ΔT = 50 °C.  
The material is AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.  
The coefficient of thermal expansion α = 0.00002 1/°C.  
The rod is prismatic with length L = 800 mm and diameter d = 20 mm.  
The rod is fully restrained between two rigid, unyielding supports, preventing any axial deformation.  
Due to full restraint, the total axial strain is zero, but thermal strain is constrained, leading to thermal stress.  
The thermal strain ε_thermal = α × ΔT.  
The mechanical strain ε_mechanical = –ε_thermal to satisfy zero total strain.  
The thermal stress σ = E × ε_mechanical.  
The thermal stress is compressive because the rod is restrained from expanding.  
Calculate the thermal stress using σ = –E × α × ΔT.  
Compute the axial force using F = σ × A, where A is the cross-sectional area.  
The cross-sectional area A = π × (d/2)².  
Report the magnitude and sign of the thermal stress and indicate it is compressive.  
Report the corresponding axial compressive force in Newtons.
