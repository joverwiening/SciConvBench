Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory applies.  
Use superposition to compute total deflection at midspan.  
The beam is simply supported with span L = 3000 mm.  
The beam material is AISI 1020 steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.  
The second moment of area I is constant and equal to 3000000 mm⁴.  
The beam carries a uniform distributed load w = 0.3 N/mm over the full span.  
The beam also carries a downward point load P = 1200 N at midspan.  
For the distributed load, replace w with its resultant force wL = 900 N acting downward at midspan.  
Compute deflection at midspan due to a point load of 900 N using the formula: δ1 = (P1 × L³) / (48 × E × I), where P1 = 900 N.  
Compute deflection at midspan due to the actual point load P = 1200 N using the same formula: δ2 = (P × L³) / (48 × E × I).  
Superpose the deflections: δ_total = δ1 + δ2.
