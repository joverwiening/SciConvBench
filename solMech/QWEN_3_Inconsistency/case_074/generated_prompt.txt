Use N–mm–MPa units and assume small strains.  
The adhesive layer has an area A = 4000 mm² and thickness t = 20 mm.  
A uniform in-plane shear force V = 8000 N is applied to the layer.  
The average shear stress is computed as τ_avg = V / A.  
The material is isotropic with shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.  
The adhesive layer is rigid in shear, meaning no relative slip occurs between the two faces.  
The average shear strain is computed as γ_avg = τ_avg / G.  
The relative lateral displacement (shear deformation) is computed as Δ = γ_avg × t.  
Compute (i) τ_avg, (ii) γ_avg, and (iii) Δ using the given formulas and values.
