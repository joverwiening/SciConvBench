Use N–mm–MPa units.  
The column has a diameter d = 25 mm and length L = 2000 mm.  
The material is structural steel with Young’s modulus E = 200000 MPa.  
The effective length factor K = 0.7.  
The applied compressive load P = 25000 N.  
The cross-sectional area A = π d² / 4.  
Compute the average compressive stress σ_avg = P / A.  
The yield strength Sy = 250 MPa.  
Compare σ_avg to Sy to assess yielding.  
The second moment of area I = π d⁴ / 64.  
Compute the Euler critical buckling load P_cr = π² E I / (K L)².  
Compare P_cr to P to assess buckling.  
The polar moment of inertia J = π d⁴ / 32 is available but not required for axial buckling check.  
Yielding is considered critical if σ_avg ≥ Sy.  
Buckling is considered critical if P ≥ P_cr.  
Determine whether yielding or Euler buckling governs the failure mode.
