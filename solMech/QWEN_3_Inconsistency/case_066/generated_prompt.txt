Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
Assume the shaft is linearly elastic and has a circular solid cross section.  
The shaft is made of structural steel with diameter d = 35 mm and length L = 400 mm.  
The transmitted torque is T = 250000 N·mm.  
Use the polar moment of inertia J = π d⁴ / 32.  
Use the maximum shear stress formula τ_max = T c / J, where c = d / 2.  
Calculate the maximum shear stress τ_max in MPa.  
Use the shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.  
Compute the angle of twist φ = T L / (J G) in radians.  
Convert the angle of twist φ to degrees.  
Report the value of τ_max in MPa and φ in degrees.
