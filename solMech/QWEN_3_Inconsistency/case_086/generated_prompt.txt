Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
Assume linear-elastic material behavior.  
The stepped circular bar is made of structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.  
The bar is fixed at the left end and subjected to an axial tensile load P = 25000 N at the right end.  
Segment 1 has length L1 = 400 mm and diameter d1 = 16 mm.  
Segment 2 has length L2 = 400 mm and diameter d2 = 40 mm.  
Both segments are connected in series and transmit the same axial force P.  
The normal stress is calculated individually for each segment based on its cross-sectional area.  
The cross-sectional area of Segment 1 is A1 = π × (d1/2)² = π × (8)² = 201.0619 mm².  
The cross-sectional area of Segment 2 is A2 = π × (d2/2)² = π × (20)² = 1256.6371 mm².  
The normal stress in Segment 1 is σ1 = P / A1 = 25000 / 201.0619 = 124.34 MPa.  
The normal stress in Segment 2 is σ2 = P / A2 = 25000 / 1256.6371 = 19.89 MPa.  
Due to axial continuity, both segments experience the same elongation δ.  
The elongation δ is determined from the compatibility condition that both segments elongate equally.  
The axial stiffness of Segment 1 is k1 = (A1 × E) / L1.  
The axial stiffness of Segment 2 is k2 = (A2 × E) / L2.  
The total elongation δ satisfies P = (k1 × k2) / (k1 + k2) × δ, equivalent to treating the system as two springs in parallel.  
Alternatively, since both segments carry the full force P and elongate equally, the compatibility condition requires that δ = (P × L1) / (A1 × E) = (P × L2) / (A2 × E), but this cannot hold unless A1 = A2, which is not true.  
Therefore, the assumption of equal elongation is physically inconsistent with series loading.  
Re-evaluate using correct series compatibility: the total elongation is the sum of individual elongations.  
The elongation of Segment 1 is δ1 = (P × L1) / (A1 × E).  
The elongation of Segment 2 is δ2 = (P × L2) / (A2 × E).  
The total elongation is δ_total = δ1 + δ2.  
Calculate δ1 = (25000 × 400) / (201.0619 × 200000) = 0.2487 mm.  
Calculate δ2 = (25000 × 400) / (1256.6371 × 200000) = 0.0398 mm.  
The total elongation is δ_total = 0.2487 + 0.0398 = 0.2885 mm.  
The normal stress in Segment 1 is 124.34 MPa.  
The normal stress in Segment 2 is 19.89 MPa.  
The total elongation of the bar is 0.2885 mm.
