Q: What is the exact definition of "common elongation" in this context—should the elongation be equal in both segments, or is it referring to a compatibility condition where the total elongation is the sum of individual segment elongations?
A: The elongation should be equal in both segments. "Common elongation" refers to the compatibility condition that both segments experience the same elongation because they are connected in series and the load is transmitted axially through the entire bar.
