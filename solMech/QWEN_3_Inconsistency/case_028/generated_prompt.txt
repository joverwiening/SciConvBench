Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
The bar behaves as a linear axial spring.  
Neglect the mass of the bar.  
The rod has length L = 1200 mm.  
The rod has diameter d = 30 mm.  
The cross-sectional area A = π × (30)² / 4 = 706.86 mm².  
The elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.  
The axial stiffness AE = 706.86 × 200000 = 141,372,000 N.  
A weight W = 500 N is dropped from height h = 200 mm onto a collar at the bottom end.  
The impact is modeled using energy conservation: W(h + δ_max) = (P_max² L) / (2AE).  
The maximum deflection δ_max = P_max L / (AE).  
Due to linear elastic behavior, the peak force for a suddenly applied load is used.  
The maximum axial force P_max = 2W.  
P_max = 2 × 500 N = 1000 N.  
The maximum normal stress σ_max = P_max / A.  
σ_max = 1000 N / 706.86 mm² = 1.414 MPa.
