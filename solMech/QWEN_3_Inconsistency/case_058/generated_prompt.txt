Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
Assume Euler buckling applies for a slender, elastic column.  
The column is made of AISI 1020 steel.  
The length L is 2000 mm.  
The elastic modulus E is 200000 MPa.  
The second moment of area I is 1200000 mm^4.  
The flexural rigidity EI is constant along the column.  
The end conditions are fixed–pinned.  
The effective length factor K is 0.7.  
The effective length is K × L = 0.7 × 2000 mm = 1400 mm.  
The Euler critical buckling load is calculated as P_cr = π² × E × I / (K × L)².  
Compute P_cr using the formula with E in MPa, I in mm^4, and lengths in mm.  
The resulting P_cr is in newtons (N).  
Convert P_cr to kilonewtons (kN) by dividing by 1000.  
Report the Euler critical buckling load P_cr in both N and kN.
