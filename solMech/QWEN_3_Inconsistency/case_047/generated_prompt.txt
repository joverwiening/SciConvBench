Use AISI 1020 steel with a Young’s modulus E = 200000 MPa and a yield strength S_y = 350 MPa.  
The rod is circular, prismatic, and straight with a diameter of 12 mm and a gage length of 800 mm.  
The rod is subjected to an axial tensile force of 15000 N applied through the centroid.  
All calculations use N–mm–MPa units with small-deformation, linear-elastic assumptions.  
Compute the average normal stress σ_avg using σ_avg = F / A.  
Calculate the cross-sectional area A using A = π × (d/2)² where d = 12 mm.  
Determine σ_avg = 15000 N / A.  
Compute the elastic normal strain ε using ε = σ_avg / E.  
Calculate the axial elongation ΔL using ΔL = ε × L, where L = 800 mm.  
Determine the factor of safety n using n = S_y / σ_avg.  
Report the average normal stress, elastic normal strain, axial elongation, and factor of safety.
