Use Young’s modulus E = 200000 MPa for structural steel.  
The rod has a circular cross-section with diameter 12 mm.  
The cross-sectional area is computed as A = π × (12 mm / 2)² = 113.097 mm².  
The axial tensile force applied is 30000 N.  
The average normal stress is σ_avg = axial force / cross-sectional area = 30000 N / 113.097 mm² = 265.26 MPa.  
The elastic normal strain is ε = σ_avg / E = 265.26 MPa / 200000 MPa = 0.0013263 mm/mm.  
The axial elongation of the 800 mm gage length is ΔL = ε × L = 0.0013263 × 800 mm = 1.061 mm.  
The yield strength of the material is S_y = 250 MPa.  
The factor of safety is defined as n = S_y / σ_avg = 250 MPa / 265.26 MPa = 0.942.  
The factor of safety against yielding is 0.942.
