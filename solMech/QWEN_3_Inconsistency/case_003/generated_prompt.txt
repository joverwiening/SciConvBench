Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
Assume linear-elastic material behavior.  
The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with Young's modulus E = 69000 MPa.  
The stepped circular bar is fixed at the left end and subjected to a tensile load P = 20000 N at the right end.  
Segment 1 has length L1 = 500 mm and diameter d1 = 30 mm.  
Segment 2 has length L2 = 600 mm and diameter d2 = 25 mm.  
The cross-sectional area of segment 1 is A1 = 706.86 mm².  
The cross-sectional area of segment 2 is A2 = 490.87 mm².  
The axial force P = 20000 N is constant through both segments.  
The normal stress in segment 1 is σ1 = P / A1 = 20000 / 706.86 = 28.29 MPa.  
The normal stress in segment 2 is σ2 = P / A2 = 20000 / 490.87 = 40.74 MPa.  
The elongation of segment 1 is δ1 = (P × L1) / (A1 × E) = (20000 × 500) / (706.86 × 69000) = 0.205 mm.  
The elongation of segment 2 is δ2 = (P × L2) / (A2 × E) = (20000 × 600) / (490.87 × 69000) = 0.352 mm.  
The total elongation of the bar is δ_total = δ1 + δ2 = 0.205 + 0.352 = 0.557 mm.  
Report the normal stress in each segment as σ1 = 28.29 MPa and σ2 = 40.74 MPa.  
Report the total elongation as δ_total = 0.557 mm.
