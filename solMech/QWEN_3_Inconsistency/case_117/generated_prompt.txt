The beam has a rectangular cross section with width b = 40 mm and height h = 80 mm.  
The internal shear force at the section is V = 25000 N.  
The moment of inertia I is calculated as I = b h³ / 12 = 1,706,666.67 mm⁴.  
The maximum transverse shear stress is computed using τ_max = 1.5 V / A.  
The cross-sectional area A = b × h = 40 × 80 = 3200 mm².  
Thus, τ_max = 1.5 × 25000 / 3200 = 11.71875 MPa.

The first moment of area Q for the region above y = 20 mm is 8,000 mm³.  
Substitute values: τ = (25000 × 8000) / (1,706,666.67 × 40) = 2.93 MPa.
