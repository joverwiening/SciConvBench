Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.

The wall thickness is 3 mm.

The polar moment of inertia J is calculated as J = π(do⁴ − di⁴)/32.  
The shear modulus G is 77519.4 MPa.  
Model the hollow circular shaft as a thin-walled closed tube.  
Use the mean-area shear-flow relation to compute surface stress and twist.  
Calculate the maximum shear stress at the outer surface using τ_max = T × r_o / J, where r_o = do/2.  
Calculate the angle of twist θ over the length L using θ = (T × L) / (G × J).
