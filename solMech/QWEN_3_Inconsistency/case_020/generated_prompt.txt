Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
The shaft has a constant diameter of 35 mm and a total length of 1200 mm.  
The shaft is fixed at x = 0.  
A torque T1 = 200000 N·mm is applied at x = 1200 mm in the positive direction.  
A torque T2 = -250000 N·mm is applied at x = 800 mm in the negative direction.  
The shear modulus G of the material is 77519.4 MPa.  
Segment 1 is from x = 0 to x = 800 mm.  
Segment 2 is from x = 800 mm to x = 1200 mm.  
The internal torque in Segment 1 is the sum of all torques to the right of the section: T1 + T2 = 200000 + (-250000) = -50000 N·mm.  
The internal torque in Segment 2 is equal to T1 = 200000 N·mm.  
The polar moment of inertia J is calculated as π×(d/2)^4 / 2 = π×(17.5)^4 / 2 = 147262.5 mm⁴.  
The maximum shear stress in Segment 1 is τ_max1 = (T1_internal × r) / J = (50000 × 17.5) / 147262.5 = 5.94 MPa.  
The maximum shear stress in Segment 2 is τ_max2 = (T2_internal × r) / J = (200000 × 17.5) / 147262.5 = 23.77 MPa.  
The angle of twist in Segment 1 is θ1 = (T1_internal × L1) / (G × J) = (-50000 × 800) / (77519.4 × 147262.5) = -0.00350 radians.  
The angle of twist in Segment 2 is θ2 = (T2_internal × L2) / (G × J) = (200000 × 400) / (77519.4 × 147262.5) = 0.00700 radians.  
The total angle of twist at x = L relative to the fixed end is θ_total = θ1 + θ2 = -0.00350 + 0.00700 = 0.00350 radians.
