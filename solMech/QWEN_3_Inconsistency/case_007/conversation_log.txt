Q: What is the exact location along the beam (x-coordinate) where the unit moment should be applied for the auxiliary system to compute the deflection at x = 900 mm?
A: Apply the unit load (not unit moment) at x = 900 mm in the auxiliary system to compute the deflection at that point. Since the desired deflection is at x = 900 mm, the unit load must be applied at that same location.
