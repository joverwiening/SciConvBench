Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
The beam is simply supported with supports at x = 0 and x = 1800 mm.  
The beam span is L = 1800 mm.  
The material is AISI 1020 steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.  
The second moment of area is I = 3500000 mm⁴.  
A point load P = 2000 N acts downward at x = 900 mm in the real system.  
The real bending moment diagram M(x) is determined from statics for a simply supported beam with a central point load.  
For the auxiliary system, a unit load of 1 N is applied downward at x = 900 mm to compute vertical deflection at that point.  
The moment diagram m(x) in the auxiliary system is determined from a unit load at midspan on a simply supported beam.  
Simple supports are modeled with zero rotation and zero vertical displacement at both ends in both systems.  
The deflection at x = 900 mm is calculated using the virtual work formula: δ = ∫[0→L] (M(x)·m(x))/(EI) dx.  
The integral is evaluated over the full length of the beam from 0 to 1800 mm.  
The flexural rigidity EI is constant and equal to 7.0 × 10¹¹ N·mm².  
The deflection result is expressed in millimeters, with downward considered positive.
