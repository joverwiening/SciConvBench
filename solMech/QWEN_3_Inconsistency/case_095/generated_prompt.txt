The material is structural steel with shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.  
The shaft is a stepped solid circular shaft consisting of two segments in series: AB and BC.  
Segment AB has length L1 = 400 mm and diameter d1 = 30 mm.  
Segment BC has length L2 = 700 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm.  
Both segments have the same diameter, so their polar moments of inertia are equal.  
The polar moment of inertia J for both segments is calculated as J = π × (d1⁴) / 32 = π × (30⁴) / 32.  
End A is fixed. A torque T = 600000 N·mm is applied at end C.  
Since the segments are in series, the same internal torque T = 600000 N·mm acts in both segments.  
The torsional stiffness for each segment is given by k = (G × J) / L.  
Because both segments have the same J and G, but different lengths, their stiffnesses differ.  
However, in a series configuration under an applied torque, the torque is transmitted fully through both segments, so each segment carries the full torque T.  
The maximum shear stress in segment AB is τ1 = (T × (d1 / 2)) / J.  
The maximum shear stress in segment BC is τ2 = (T × (d2 / 2)) / J.  
Since d1 = d2 and J is the same for both, and T is the same, τ1 = τ2.  
The maximum shear stress in each segment is equal; therefore, neither governs due to higher stress.  
The angle of twist for segment AB is φ1 = (T × L1) / (G × J).  
The angle of twist for segment BC is φ2 = (T × L2) / (G × J).  
The total angle of twist at C relative to A is φ_total = φ1 + φ2.
