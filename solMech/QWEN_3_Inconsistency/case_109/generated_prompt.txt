Use Young’s modulus E = 69000 MPa for 6061-T6 aluminum.  
Use a yield strength S_y = 275 MPa for 6061-T6 aluminum.  
The rod has a circular cross-section with diameter d = 20 mm.  
The cross-sectional area A is computed as A = π × (d/2)².  
The gage length L of the rod is 800 mm.  
An axial tensile force P = 15000 N is applied through the centroid.  
The average normal stress σ_avg is computed as σ_avg = P / A.  
The elastic normal strain ε is computed as ε = σ_avg / E.  
The axial elongation ΔL is computed as ΔL = ε × L.  
The factor of safety against yielding is computed as n = S_y / σ_avg.  
All calculations use consistent N–mm–MPa units.  
Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic material behavior.
