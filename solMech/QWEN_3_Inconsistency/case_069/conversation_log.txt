Q: What is the value of the second moment of area J that should be used in the polar form of the curvature equation?
A: Use I = 2500000 mm^4 as the second moment of area. The term "polar second moment" in the query is incorrect; for bending, use the area moment of inertia I, not polar J. So, use I = 2500000 mm^4 in the formula κ = M/(EI).
