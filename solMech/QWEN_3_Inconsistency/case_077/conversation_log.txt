Q: Use N–mm–MPa units throughout the calculation.  
The length of the shaft is L = 800 mm.  
The material is AISI 1020 steel with a shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa.  
The transmitted torque is T = 600000 N·mm.  
The allowable angle of twist is φ_allow = 1.5° over the length L.  
Convert φ_allow to radians for use in the formula φ = T L / (J G).  
Use φ = T L / (J G) with φ in radians.  
Use J = π d⁴ / 32 for a solid circular shaft.  
Solve for the minimum required diameter d such that the angle of twist does not exceed φ_allow.  
Ensure the calculated diameter is the smallest value that satisfies the twist constraint.
A: Convert φ_allow = 1.5° to radians:  
φ_allow = 1.5 × (π / 180) = 0.02618 rad

Use the formula:  
φ = T L / (J G)  
J = π d⁴ / 32

Substitute:  
0.02618 = (600000 × 800) / [(π d⁴ / 32) × 76923.1]

Solve for d⁴:  
d⁴ = (600000 × 800 × 32) / (π × 76923.1 × 0.02618)  
d⁴ = 152732289.6  
d = (152732289.6)^(1/4)  
d ≈ 62.3 mm

Minimum required diameter d = 62.3 mm
