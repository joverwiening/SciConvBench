Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
The rod is made of AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.  
The length of the rod L is 1500 mm.  
The diameter of the rod d is 16 mm.  
The cross-sectional area A is 201.06 mm².  
The static load W is 800 N.  
The weight is dropped from a height h = 150 mm onto a collar at the bottom end.  
The rod behaves as a linear axial spring.  
The mass of the bar is negligible.  
Due to the linear elastic behavior of the rod, the peak axial force during impact is given by the suddenly applied load result P_max = 2W.  
The maximum axial force P_max is 1600 N.  
The maximum normal stress σ_max is calculated as P_max / A.  
The maximum normal stress σ_max is 7.96 MPa.
