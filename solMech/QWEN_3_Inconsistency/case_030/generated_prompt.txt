Use N–mm–MPa units.  
Assume linear elastic isotropic material behavior.  
The structural steel rod has an initial diameter of 25 mm.  
The rod has a length of 1200 mm.  
An axial tensile load of 45000 N is applied through the centroid.  
The elastic modulus E is 200000 MPa.  
Poisson’s ratio ν is 0.29.  
Assume plane strain in the two transverse directions, so lateral strain is constrained to zero.  
The given Poisson’s ratio is for material reference only and does not govern the lateral strain under plane strain constraint.  
Compute the average axial normal stress using σ = P / A, where A = π × (d0 / 2)².  
Compute the axial strain using ε_axial = σ / E.  
Under plane strain, the lateral strain ε_lateral is zero.  
The change in diameter Δd is zero due to zero lateral strain.  
Report the average axial normal stress in MPa.  
Report the axial strain as a dimensionless quantity.  
Report the lateral (diametral) strain as zero.  
Report the change in diameter Δd as 0 mm.
