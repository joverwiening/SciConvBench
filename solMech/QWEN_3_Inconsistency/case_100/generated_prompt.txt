Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
Assume linear elastic isotropic material behavior.  
The brass rod has an initial diameter d0 = 30 mm and length L = 500 mm.  
An axial tensile load P = 25000 N is applied through the centroid of the cross-section.  
The material has Young’s modulus E = 105000 MPa and Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.34.  
Assume plane strain conditions in the two transverse directions, so lateral strain is constrained to zero.  
The listed Poisson’s ratio is for material reference only and does not govern lateral deformation under plane strain.  
Compute the average axial normal stress as σ_axial = P / A, where A = π × (d0 / 2)².  
Compute the axial strain as ε_axial = σ_axial / E.  
Under plane strain, the lateral strain ε_lateral = 0.  
The diametral strain is zero due to plane strain constraint.  
Compute the change in diameter Δd = d0 × ε_lateral, which results in Δd = 0.
