Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
Assume Euler buckling applies for a slender, elastic column.  
Use material property E = 200000 MPa.

Use column length L = 2000 mm.  
Apply fixed–fixed end conditions with effective length factor K = 0.5.  
Calculate the Euler critical buckling load using P_cr = π² × E × I / (K × L)².  
Evaluate P_cr in N.
