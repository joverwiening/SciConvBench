Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
The cantilever beam has a length L = 1500 mm.  
The beam is fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.  
The beam is made of AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.  
The second moment of area I is constant and equal to 2500000 mm⁴.  
A downward point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end (x = L).  
The bending moment M(x) is taken as constant and equal to the peak value M = P × L over the entire span from x = 0 to x = L.  
The bending strain energy U is calculated using the integral U = ∫[0→L] M(x)² / (2 × E × I) dx.  
Substitute M(x) = P × L into the integral.  
Integrate U = ∫[0→L] (P × L)² / (2 × E × I) dx from 0 to L.  
Simplify the integral to U = (P² × L³) / (2 × E × I).  
Apply Castigliano’s theorem: the vertical deflection at the free end is ∂U/∂P.  
Differentiate U with respect to P to obtain ∂U/∂P = (2 × P × L³) / (2 × E × I).  
Simplify the expression to deflection δ = (P × L³) / (E × I).  
Substitute P = 500 N, L = 1500 mm, E = 200000 MPa, and I = 2500000 mm⁴ into the equation.  
Compute the numerical value of the deflection in mm.  
Report the vertical deflection at the free end in millimeters.
