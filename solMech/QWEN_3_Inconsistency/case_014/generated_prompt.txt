Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory with small deflections and linear elastic behavior.  
The beam is a cantilever with length L = 1200 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.  
The material is structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.  
The beam has a constant second moment of area I = 2500000 mm⁴.  
A downward point load P = 800 N is applied at the free end (x = L).  
The slope at the free end is calculated using the formula θ = (P L²) / (2 E I).  
The vertical deflection at the free end is calculated using the formula δ = (P L³) / (3 E I).  
Compute the slope in radians.  
Compute the deflection in millimeters.
