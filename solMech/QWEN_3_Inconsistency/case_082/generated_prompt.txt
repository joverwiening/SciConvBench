Use N–mm–MPa units.  
The beam has a rectangular cross section with width b = 50 mm and height h = 100 mm.  
The internal bending moment about the centroidal z-axis is M = 3000000 N·mm.  
The moment is positive, causing sagging (concave upward).  
The neutral axis is located at the centroid of the cross section.  
The second moment of area (I) about the z-axis is calculated as I = (b * h^3) / 12.  
Substitute values: I = (50 * 100^3) / 12 = 4166666.67 mm⁴.  
The maximum value of y (distance from neutral axis to extreme fiber) is h/2 = 50 mm.  
The flexure formula is σ = M * y / I.  
The maximum bending normal stress is σ_max = (3000000 * 50) / 4166666.67 ≈ 36 MPa.  
For a sagging moment, the top fiber is in compression and the bottom fiber is in tension.
