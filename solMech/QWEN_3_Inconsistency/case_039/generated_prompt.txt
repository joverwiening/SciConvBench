Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
Assume linear elastic isotropic material behavior.  
The material is AISI 1020 steel with Young’s modulus E = 200000 MPa and Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.30.  
The rod has an initial diameter d0 = 20 mm and length L = 1200 mm.  
The axial tensile load P = 35000 N is applied through the centroid.  
Assume plane strain in the two transverse directions, so lateral strain is constrained to zero.  
The given Poisson’s ratio is for material reference only and does not govern the lateral strain under plane strain constraint.  
Compute the average axial normal stress as σ_axial = P / A, where A = π × (d0 / 2)².  
Compute the axial strain as ε_axial = σ_axial / E.  
Under plane strain, the lateral strain ε_lateral = 0.  
The change in diameter Δd = ε_lateral × d0.  
Report the average axial normal stress in MPa.  
Report the axial strain as a dimensionless quantity.  
Report the lateral (diametral) strain as a dimensionless quantity.  
Report the change in diameter Δd in mm.
