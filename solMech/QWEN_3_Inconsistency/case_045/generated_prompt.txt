Use consistent N–mm–MPa units throughout the calculation.  
Assume linear-elastic, small-deformation behavior for the material.  
Use Young’s modulus E = 69000 MPa for 6061-T6 aluminum.  
Use yield strength S_y = 275 MPa for 6061-T6 aluminum.  
The rod is straight, prismatic, and circular with diameter d = 12 mm.  
The gage length of the rod is L = 800 mm.  
The axial tensile load applied is P = 15000 N.  
The load is applied through the centroid, resulting in uniform axial stress.  
Compute the cross-sectional area A = π × (d/2)² = π × (6 mm)² ≈ 113.097 mm².  
Compute the average normal stress σ_avg = P / A = 15000 N / 113.097 mm² ≈ 132.63 MPa.  
Compute the elastic normal strain ε = σ_avg / E = 132.63 MPa / 69000 MPa ≈ 0.001922.  
Compute the axial elongation ΔL = ε × L = 0.001922 × 800 mm ≈ 1.538 mm.  
Compute the factor of safety n = S_y / σ_avg = 275 MPa / 132.63 MPa ≈ 2.074.
