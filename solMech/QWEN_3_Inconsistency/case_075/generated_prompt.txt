The beam is simply supported with span L = 4000 mm.  
A concentrated downward load P = 12000 N is applied at the midspan, 2000 mm from support A.  
The support reactions are symmetric due to the loading and span symmetry.  
The vertical reaction at support A is 6000 N upward.  
The vertical reaction at support B is 6000 N upward.  
The cut section is located at x = 1000 mm from support A.  
Consider the left free body (from A to the cut section).  
The only external force to the left of the cut is the reaction at A: 6000 N upward.  
The internal shear force V is defined as positive when upward on the left cut face.  
The shear force V at x = 1000 mm is equal to the sum of vertical forces to the left: V = +6000 N.  
The internal bending moment M is positive when it causes sagging (tension at the bottom fibers).  
The bending moment M at x = 1000 mm is the sum of moments about the cut section from the left side.  
Take moments about the cut: M = (6000 N) × (1000 mm) = 6,000,000 N·mm.  
The moment is sagging, so M is positive: M = +6,000,000 N·mm.
