Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
Treat the elastic modulus E as constant.  
The bar has a length L = 800 mm.  
An axial tensile force P = 12000 N is applied.  
The material is AISI 1020 steel with E = 200000 MPa.  
The cross-sectional area varies linearly as A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L), where x is measured from the left end.  
The initial cross-sectional area A0 = 250 mm².  
The area variation parameter k = 0.6.  
Compute the arithmetic mean area A_mean = (A(0) + A(L)) / 2.  
Use A_mean as the constant cross-sectional area in the deformation calculation.  
Compute elongation δ = (P × L) / (A_mean × E).
