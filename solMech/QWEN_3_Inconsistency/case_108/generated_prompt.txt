Use N–mm–MPa units.  
The column diameter d is 30 mm.  
The column length L is 1500 mm.  
The material is structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.  
The effective length factor K is 2.0.  
Euler buckling governs.  
The cross-section is circular, so the polar moment of inertia J = π d⁴ / 32 is used in the Euler buckling formula.  
The critical buckling load is calculated as P_cr = π² E J / (K L)².  
Compute the Euler critical load P_cr.  
Apply a factor of safety n = 2.0 to determine the allowable load.  
The allowable axial compressive load is P_allow = P_cr / n.
