Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
Assume linear-elastic material behavior.  
The material is AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.  
The stepped circular bar is fixed at the left end and subjected to a tensile load P = 30000 N at the right end.  
Segment 1 has length L1 = 300 mm and diameter d1 = 25 mm.  
Segment 2 has length L2 = 1000 mm and diameter d2 = 35 mm.  
The axial force P = 30000 N is constant through both segments.  
Calculate the cross-sectional area of Segment 1 as A1 = π × (d1/2)².  
Calculate the cross-sectional area of Segment 2 as A2 = π × (d2/2)².  
Compute the normal stress in Segment 1 as σ1 = P / A1.  
Compute the normal stress in Segment 2 as σ2 = P / A2.  
Note that the normal stress is not identical in the two segments due to different cross-sectional areas.  
Compute the elongation of Segment 1 as δ1 = (P × L1) / (A1 × E).  
Compute the elongation of Segment 2 as δ2 = (P × L2) / (A2 × E).  
Compute the total elongation as δ_total = δ1 + δ2.  
Report the normal stress in each segment and the total elongation of the bar.
