Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
Assume Euler–Bernoulli beam theory applies.  
Use superposition to compute total deflection at midspan.  
The beam is simply supported with span L = 3500 mm.  
The beam material is structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.  
The beam has constant second moment of area I = 3000000 mm⁴.  
A uniform distributed load w = 0.3 N/mm acts over the full span.  
A downward point load P = 1200 N acts at midspan.  
For the distributed load, replace it with its resultant force wL = 0.3 × 3500 = 1050 N acting downward at midspan.  
Compute deflection at midspan due to a point load of 1050 N at midspan using the formula: δ1 = (P L³) / (48 E I), where P = 1050 N.  
Compute deflection at midspan due to the actual point load of 1200 N using the same formula: δ2 = (P L³) / (48 E I), where P = 1200 N.  
Sum the two deflections: δ_total = δ1 + δ2.  
The total midspan deflection is the sum of the deflections from the equivalent point load of the distributed load and the actual point load.
