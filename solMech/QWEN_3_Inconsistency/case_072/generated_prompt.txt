Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
The shaft is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa.  
The shaft has a diameter d = 30 mm and length L = 1000 mm.  
The applied torque is T = 450000 N·mm.  
The polar moment of inertia J is calculated as J = π d^4 / 32.  
The torsional strain energy U is given by U = ∫[0→L] (T^2) / (2 G J) dx.  
Since T, G, and J are constant along the length, U = (T^2 L) / (2 G J).  
The angle of twist φ is computed using Castigliano’s theorem: φ = ∂U/∂T.  
Differentiate U with respect to T: φ = (T L) / (G J).  
Compute φ using the given values and the expression φ = (T L) / (G J).  
The result is the angle of twist in radians.
