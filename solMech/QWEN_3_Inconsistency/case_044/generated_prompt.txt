Use N–mm–MPa units.  
Assume linear elastic isotropic material behavior.  
The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with Young's modulus E = 69000 MPa and Poisson’s ratio ν = 0.33.  
The rod has an initial diameter d0 = 40 mm and length L = 500 mm.  
The rod is subjected to an axial tensile load P = 35000 N applied through the centroid.  
Assume plane strain in the two transverse directions, so lateral strain is constrained to zero.  
The Poisson’s ratio is provided for material reference but does not govern lateral deformation due to the plane strain constraint.  
Compute the average axial normal stress as σ_axial = P / A, where A = π × (d0 / 2)².  
Compute the axial strain as ε_axial = σ_axial / E.  
Under plane strain, the lateral (diametral) strain is zero.  
The change in diameter Δd is 0 mm due to zero lateral strain.
