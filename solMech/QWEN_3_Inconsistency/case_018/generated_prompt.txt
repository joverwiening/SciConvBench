Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
The shaft has a constant diameter of 40 mm and a total length of 1500 mm.  
The shaft is fixed at x = 0.  
A torque T1 = 300000 N·mm is applied at the free end x = L in the positive direction.  
A torque T2 = -150000 N·mm is applied at x = 750 mm in the negative direction.  
The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa.  
The internal torque in segment 0 → 750 mm (0 → a) is equal to the sum of applied torques to the right of the section: T_internal_1 = T1 + T2 = 300000 - 150000 = 150000 N·mm.  
The internal torque in segment 750 mm → 1500 mm (a → L) is equal to the torque at the free end: T_internal_2 = T1 = 300000 N·mm.  
The polar moment of inertia J is calculated as J = (π × d⁴) / 32 = (π × 40⁴) / 32 = 251327.412 mm⁴.  
The maximum shear stress in segment 0 → a is τ_max_1 = (T_internal_1 × r) / J, where r = d/2 = 20 mm, so τ_max_1 = (150000 × 20) / 251327.412 = 11.94 MPa.  
The maximum shear stress in segment a → L is τ_max_2 = (T_internal_2 × r) / J = (300000 × 20) / 251327.412 = 23.87 MPa.  
The angle of twist in segment 0 → a is φ1 = (T_internal_1 × L1) / (G × J), where L1 = 750 mm, so φ1 = (150000 × 750) / (25939.8 × 251327.412) = 0.01731 radians.  
The angle of twist in segment a → L is φ2 = (T_internal_2 × L2) / (G × J), where L2 = 750 mm, so φ2 = (300000 × 750) / (25939.8 × 251327.412) = 0.03462 radians.  
The total angle of twist at x = L relative to the fixed end is φ_total = φ1 + φ2 = 0.01731 + 0.03462 = 0.05193 radians.
