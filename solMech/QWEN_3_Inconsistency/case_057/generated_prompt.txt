Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
Assume Euler buckling applies for a slender, elastic column.  
The column is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with elastic modulus E = 69000 MPa.  
The length of the column is L = 3000 mm.  
The second moment of area is I = 2500000 mm^4.  
The flexural rigidity EI is constant along the length.  
The end conditions are fixed–fixed with effective length factor K = 0.5.  
The effective length is K × L = 0.5 × 3000 mm = 1500 mm.  
The Euler critical buckling load is calculated as P_cr = π² × E × I / (K × L)².  
Compute P_cr using E = 69000 MPa, I = 2500000 mm^4, and (K × L) = 1500 mm.  
Report the critical buckling load P_cr in newtons (N).  
Also report P_cr in kilonewtons (kN) by dividing the value in N by 1000.
