Use N–mm–MPa units and Euler–Bernoulli beam theory.
The cantilever beam has a length L = 2500 mm and is fixed at x = 0.
A resultant point load of 2500 N is applied at 1250 mm from the fixed end.
The material is AISI 1020 steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The second moment of area is I = 1000000 mm^4.
The beam is modeled as a cantilever with a concentrated load at midspan.
The maximum deflection occurs at the free end (x = 2500 mm).
The slope is calculated at the free end (x = 2500 mm).
Use the Euler–Bernoulli formula for deflection and slope due to a point load on a cantilever.
The deflection at the free end is calculated using the formula: (P * a^2 * (3L - a)) / (6 * E * I), where P = 2500 N, a = 1250 mm, L = 2500 mm.
The slope at the free end is calculated using the formula: (P * a^2) / (2 * E * I).
The maximum deflection is 2.604 mm downward.
The slope at the free end is 0.001302 radians.
