Use N–mm–MPa units throughout the analysis.  
Assume linear-elastic material behavior with Young’s modulus E = 200000 MPa.  
The stepped circular bar is made of structural steel and consists of two segments.  
Segment 1 has length L1 = 300 mm and diameter d1 = 12 mm.  
Segment 2 has length L2 = 600 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm.  
The left end of the bar is fully fixed, with zero axial displacement.  
An axial tensile force P = 25000 N is applied uniformly at the right end.  
The same axial force P is transmitted through both segments.  
Normal stress in each segment is calculated as σ = P / A, where A is the cross-sectional area of the segment.  
Cross-sectional area of Segment 1: A1 = π × (d1/2)² = π × (6)² = 113.097 mm².  
Cross-sectional area of Segment 2: A2 = π × (d2/2)² = π × (15)² = 706.858 mm².  
Normal stress in Segment 1: σ1 = 25000 / 113.097 = 221.05 MPa.  
Normal stress in Segment 2: σ2 = 25000 / 706.858 = 35.36 MPa.  
Elongation of Segment 1: δ1 = (P × L1) / (A1 × E) = (25000 × 300) / (113.097 × 200000) = 0.331 mm.  
Elongation of Segment 2: δ2 = (P × L2) / (A2 × E) = (25000 × 600) / (706.858 × 200000) = 0.106 mm.  
Total elongation of the bar: δ_total = δ1 + δ2 = 0.331 + 0.106 = 0.437 mm.  
The normal stress is not identical in the two segments; it differs due to different cross-sectional areas.  
The total elongation is the sum of individual segment elongations under the same axial load.  
Report normal stress in Segment 1 as 221.05 MPa and in Segment 2 as 35.36 MPa.  
Report total elongation of the bar as 0.437 mm.
