Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
Model the vertical steel rod as a linear axial spring with no mass.  
The rod is made of AISI 1020 steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.  
The rod has length L = 800 mm and diameter d = 25 mm.  
Compute the cross-sectional area A = π × d² / 4.  
A collar is attached to the bottom end of the rod.  
A weight W = 1500 N is dropped from a height h = 200 mm onto the collar.  
The impact causes axial deformation of the rod.  
Use the suddenly applied load result for peak force: P_max = 2 × W.  
Compute P_max = 2 × 1500 N.  
Compute the maximum normal stress σ_max = P_max / A.  
Neglect energy loss during impact.  
Assume linear elastic behavior of the rod.  
The maximum deflection δ_max = P_max × L / (A × E).  
Verify energy conservation: W × (h + δ_max) = (P_max² × L) / (2 × A × E).
