The beam is simply supported with span L = 3000 mm, supported at x = 0 and x = L.  
The material is AISI 1020 steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.  
The second moment of area of the beam is I = 1200000 mm⁴.  
A point load P = 800 N acts downward at x = 2000 mm.  
The real bending moment diagram M(x) is determined from the applied load P.  
For the auxiliary system, apply a unit point load (1 N) downward at x = 2000 mm to compute deflection at that point.  
The virtual moment diagram m(x) is generated from the unit load applied at x = 2000 mm.  
Both the real and auxiliary systems have simple supports modeled as zero-rotation boundaries.  
The flexural rigidity EI is constant and equal to 200000 × 1200000 = 2.4×10¹¹ N·mm².  
The deflection at x = 2000 mm is computed using δ = ∫₀ᴸ [M(x)·m(x)] / (EI) dx.  
The integral is evaluated over the full span from 0 to 3000 mm, accounting for discontinuities at x = 2000 mm.  
The deflection result is expressed in millimeters.
