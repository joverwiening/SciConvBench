Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
The beam has a rectangular cross section with width b = 60 mm and height h = 80 mm.  
The internal shear force at the section is V = 16000 N.  
The area of the cross section A is b × h = 60 × 80 = 4800 mm².  
The maximum transverse shear stress is calculated using τ_max = 1.5 × V / A.  
τ_max = 1.5 × 16000 / 4800 = 5 MPa.  
The moment of inertia I is b × h³ / 12 = 60 × (80)³ / 12 = 2,560,000 mm⁴.  
At y = 26.667 mm from the neutral axis (positive upward), the area above y is A_above = b × (h/2 − y) = 60 × (40 − 26.667) = 800 mm².  
The centroid of the area above y, measured from the neutral axis, is ȳ_above = (40 + 26.667) / 2 = 33.3335 mm.  
The first moment of area Q is A_above × ȳ_above = 800 × 33.3335 = 26,666.8 mm³.  
The shear stress at y = 26.667 mm is calculated using τ = V × Q / (I × b).  
τ = 16000 × 26,666.8 / (2,560,000 × 60) = 2.7778 MPa.  
Report τ_max = 5 MPa for part (a).  
Report τ = 2.778 MPa (rounded to three decimal places) for part (b).
