Q: What is the value of the first moment of area Q for the area above y = 26.667 mm?
A: Q = A_above × ȳ_above  
A_above = b × (h/2 - y) = 60 × (40 - 26.667) = 60 × 13.333 = 800 mm²  
ȳ_above = centroid of area above y, measured from neutral axis = (40 + 26.667)/2 = 33.3335 mm  

Q = 800 × 33.3335 = 26,666.8 mm³
