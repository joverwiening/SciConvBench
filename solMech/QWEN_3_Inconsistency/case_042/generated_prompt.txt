Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
Assume linear elastic axial response.  
The bar is prismatic with length L = 800 mm.  
The cross-sectional area A = 300 mm².  
The material is structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.  
A tensile load P = 8000 N is applied suddenly, starting from zero.  
The load remains constant after application.  
The peak static stress is calculated as P/A.  
Use energy methods based on a linear spring model.  
The work done by the suddenly applied load is P times the final displacement.  
The strain energy stored in the bar is (1/2) times the stiffness times the square of the displacement.  
Equate the work done by the load to the strain energy to find the maximum displacement.  
The maximum stress during the transient is twice the static stress due to sudden loading.  
The maximum stress is 2 × (P / A).  
Calculate the maximum stress as 2 × (8000 / 300) = 53.333 MPa.
