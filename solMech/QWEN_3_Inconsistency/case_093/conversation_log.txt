Q: What is the correct interpretation of the instruction stating "take the free-end rotation to be zero" in the context of a cantilever beam with a point load at the free end, given that this contradicts the standard Euler–Bernoulli beam theory solution?
A: Ignore the instruction stating "take the free-end rotation to be zero" and the suggestion to use constant moment P·L over the full span. This is incorrect and contradicts Euler–Bernoulli beam theory for a cantilever with a point load at the free end. 

Use standard Euler–Bernoulli beam theory: for a cantilever beam with a point load P at the free end, the slope and deflection at the free end are given by:

- Slope: \( \theta = \frac{P L^2}{2 E I} \)
- Deflection: \( \delta = \frac{P L^3}{3 E I} \)

Proceed with these formulas.
