Use N–mm–MPa units throughout.  
Apply Euler–Bernoulli beam theory for a cantilever beam with a point load at the free end.  
The beam has length L = 800 mm.  
The beam is fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.  
The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with elastic modulus E = 69000 MPa.  
The beam has a constant second moment of area I = 800000 mm⁴.  
A downward point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end.  
The slope at the free end is calculated using θ = (P L²) / (2 E I).  
The deflection at the free end is calculated using δ = (P L³) / (3 E I).  
Compute the slope in radians.  
Compute the deflection in millimeters.
