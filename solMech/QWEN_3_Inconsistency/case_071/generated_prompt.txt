Use N–mm–MPa units throughout the calculation.  
The shaft has a diameter d = 25 mm and length L = 1000 mm.  
The material is AISI 1020 steel with shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa.  
A constant torque T = 350000 N·mm is applied.  
The polar moment of inertia J is calculated as J = π d⁴ / 32.  
The torsional strain energy U is given by the integral U = ∫[0→L] (T²) / (2 G J) dx.  
Since T, G, and J are constant along the length, U = (T² L) / (2 G J).  
The angle of twist φ is computed using Castigliano’s theorem: φ = ∂U/∂T.  
Differentiating U with respect to T gives φ = (T L) / (G J).  
Substitute the values of T, L, G, and J to compute φ in radians.  
The result is the angle of twist between the ends of the shaft.
