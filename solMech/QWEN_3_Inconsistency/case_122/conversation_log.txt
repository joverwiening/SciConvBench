Q: What is the value of the moment of inertia (I) for the rectangular cross-section about the neutral axis?
A: The moment of inertia (I) for a rectangular cross-section about the neutral axis is calculated as:

I = (b × h³) / 12

Given:
b = 50 mm  
h = 120 mm

I = (50 × 120³) / 12 = (50 × 1,728,000) / 12 = 86,400,000 / 12 = 7,200,000 mm⁴

So, I = 7,200,000 mm⁴
