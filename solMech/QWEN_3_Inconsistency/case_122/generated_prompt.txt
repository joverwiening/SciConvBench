The beam has a rectangular cross-section with width b = 50 mm and height h = 120 mm.  
The moment of inertia I about the neutral axis is 7,200,000 mm⁴.  
The internal bending moment M is 4,000,000 N·mm and is sagging, causing compression above the neutral axis and tension below.  
The bending stress σ at a distance y from the neutral axis is calculated using the formula: σ = (M × y) / I.  
At y = 0 mm (neutral axis), the bending stress σ is 0 MPa.  
At the top fiber, y = +60 mm (positive upward), so σ = (4,000,000 × 60) / 7,200,000 = 33.33 MPa (compressive, hence negative).  
At the bottom fiber, y = -60 mm, so σ = (4,000,000 × -60) / 7,200,000 = -33.33 MPa (tensile, hence positive).  
The bending stress at the top fiber is -33.33 MPa.  
The bending stress at the bottom fiber is +33.33 MPa.  
The bending stress at the neutral axis is 0 MPa.
