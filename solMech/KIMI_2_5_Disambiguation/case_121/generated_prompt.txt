The governing relations for eccentric axial loading are as follows.

The axial stress due to the concentric component is σ_axial = P / A, where A = b × h is the cross-sectional area.

The bending moment induced by eccentricity is M = P × e.

The bending stress is σ_bending = M × y / I, where I = b × h³ / 12 is the second moment of area about the bending axis, and y = h/2 is the distance to the extreme fiber.

The maximum compressive stress occurs at the fiber farthest from the load on the concave side: σ_max_compression = P/A + M×(h/2)/I.

The stress at the fiber nearest to the load on the convex side is σ_tension_check = P/A - M×(h/2)/I, which may be tensile or compressive depending on magnitude.
