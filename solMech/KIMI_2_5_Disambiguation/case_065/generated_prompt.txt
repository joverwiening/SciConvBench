Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics with isotropic, homogeneous material behavior.

The shaft is subjected to a torque T applied at one end with the other end fixed.

The shaft has constant circular cross-section with radius R and length L.

The material has shear modulus G.

The torque is applied about the longitudinal axis of the shaft.

Plane sections remain plane and perpendicular to the axis after deformation (no warping).

The shaft is straight and prismatic.

Shear stress varies linearly with radial distance from the center.

The polar moment of inertia for a solid circular cross-section is J = (π/2) * R^4.

Maximum shear stress occurs at the outer surface r = R.

The governing relation for shear stress at radial distance r is τ(r) = T * r / J.

The maximum shear stress is τ_max = T * R / J = 2T / (π * R^3).

The angle of twist φ over length L is φ = T * L / (G * J).

The governing relation for angle of twist per unit length is dφ/dx = T / (G * J).

The torsional stiffness is k_t = G * J / L.
