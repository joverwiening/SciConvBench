The structural member is a rectangular adhesive layer with area A = 4000 mm² and thickness t = 20 mm.

The applied load is a uniform in-plane shear force V = 12000 N.

The shear modulus is G = 39179.1 MPa.

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

Assume the adhesive layer is homogeneous and isotropic.

Assume pure shear stress distribution is uniform across the adhesive layer area.

Assume no normal stresses are present (pure shear condition).

Assume the shear force is applied parallel to the adhesive layer plane.

The average shear stress tau is calculated as tau = V / A.

Substituting values: tau = 12000 N / 4000 mm² = 3 MPa.

The shear strain gamma is calculated from Hooke's law for shear: gamma = tau / G.

Substituting values: gamma = 3 MPa / 39179.1 MPa = 7.657 × 10⁻⁵ (dimensionless, or 76.57 microstrain).

The shear deformation delta_s (relative displacement between the two faces of the adhesive layer) is calculated as delta_s = gamma × t.

Substituting values: delta_s = 7.657 × 10⁻⁵ × 20 mm = 1.531 × 10⁻³ mm = 1.531 micrometers.

The governing relations are: tau = V/A, gamma = tau/G, and delta_s = gamma × t = (V × t)/(A × G).
