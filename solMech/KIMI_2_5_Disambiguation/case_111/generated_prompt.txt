The column is a solid circular cross-section made of structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.

The column length is L = 1500 mm.

The column diameter is d = 30 mm.

The effective length factor is K = 2.0, corresponding to a fixed-free (cantilever) support condition.

The factor of safety against Euler buckling is n = 2.0.

The moment of inertia for a solid circular cross-section is I = πd^4 / 64.

The cross-sectional area is A = πd^2 / 4.

The radius of gyration is r = sqrt(I/A) = d/4.

The slenderness ratio is KL/r.

The Euler critical buckling load is P_cr = π^2 E I / (KL)^2.

The allowable column load is P_allow = P_cr / n.

The factor of safety with respect to buckling is explicitly specified as n = 2.0 and is applied to determine the allowable load.

Material yielding is not considered in the allowable load determination; only elastic buckling governs.

All calculations assume small deformations and linear-elastic material behavior.

The effective length is L_e = K * L = 2.0 * 1500 mm = 3000 mm.

The governing relation for Euler buckling is P_cr = π^2 E I / L_e^2.

The allowable load relation is P_allow = P_cr / n = π^2 E I / (n * L_e^2).
