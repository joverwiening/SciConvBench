The analysis determines the total deflection of a simply supported Euler-Bernoulli beam under combined loading using superposition.

Assumptions:
Small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics apply.
The beam is prismatic with constant flexural rigidity EI.
Plane sections remain plane and perpendicular to the neutral axis (Euler-Bernoulli kinematics).
Material is homogeneous and isotropic.
Deflections are small compared to the span length.

Given parameters:
Span length L = 3000 mm.
Point load P = 1200 N at midspan (x = L/2).
Uniformly distributed load w = 0.3 N/mm over full span.

Governing relations for superposition:
The total deflection v_total(x) equals the sum of deflections from each load case: v_total(x) = v_P(x) + v_w(x).

Deflection due to midspan point load P on simply supported beam:
For 0 ≤ x ≤ L/2: v_P(x) = (P*x)/(48*E*I) * (3*L^2 - 4*x^2).
Maximum deflection at midspan: v_P,max = (P*L^3)/(48*E*I) downward.

Deflection due to uniformly distributed load w on simply supported beam:
v_w(x) = (w*x)/(24*E*I) * (L^3 - 2*L*x^2 + x^3).
Maximum deflection at midspan: v_w,max = (5*w*L^4)/(384*E*I) downward.

Total deflection at any point:
v_total(x) = v_P(x) + v_w(x).

Total maximum deflection at midspan:
v_total,max = (P*L^3)/(48*E*I) + (5*w*L^4)/(384*E*I).

Numerical evaluation requires the flexural rigidity EI of the beam cross-section.
