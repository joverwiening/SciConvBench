The rod is a circular cylinder with initial diameter d0 = 25 mm and length L = 1200 mm.

The rod material is isotropic, linear-elastic with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.29.

The rod is subjected to a uniaxial tensile load P = 45000 N applied axially through the centroid.

The cross-sectional area is A = π(d0/2)^2 = π(12.5 mm)^2 = 490.874 mm^2.

The axial stress is σ_z = P/A = 45000 N / 490.874 mm^2 = 91.673 MPa.

The axial strain is ε_z = σ_z/E = 91.673 MPa / 200000 MPa = 0.0004584 (dimensionless).

The lateral strain due to the Poisson effect is ε_r = ε_θ = -ν·ε_z = -0.29 × 0.0004584 = -0.0001329 (dimensionless).

The change in diameter is Δd = ε_r · d0 = -0.0001329 × 25 mm = -0.003323 mm (contraction).

The change in length is ΔL = ε_z · L = 0.0004584 × 1200 mm = 0.5501 mm (elongation).

The final diameter is d = d0 + Δd = 25 mm - 0.003323 mm = 24.9967 mm.

The final length is L_final = L + ΔL = 1200 mm + 0.5501 mm = 1200.55 mm.

The Poisson effect is manifested as a transverse contraction proportional to the axial extension, with the ratio of transverse strain to axial strain equal to -ν = -0.29.
