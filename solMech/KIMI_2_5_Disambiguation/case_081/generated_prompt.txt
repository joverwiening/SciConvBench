The analysis shall determine stress and strain response quantities for a circular shaft subjected to combined bending and torsion loading.

The shaft has a diameter d of 30 mm.

The bending moment M is 4000000 N·mm.

The torsional moment T is 400000 N·mm.

The material is assumed to be structural steel with Young's modulus E of 200000 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν of 0.3.

The analysis is focused on the critical cross-section where stresses are maximum; shaft length is not required.

The coordinate system is defined with the x-axis along the shaft longitudinal axis, and y-z axes in the cross-section plane.

The maximum bending stress occurs at the outer fiber and is calculated using σ_x = M*y/I, where y equals d/2 at the outer fiber.

The moment of inertia I for a circular cross-section is π*d^4/64.

The maximum torsional shear stress occurs at the outer surface and is calculated using τ = T*r/J, where r equals d/2.

The polar moment of inertia J for a circular cross-section is π*d^4/32, which equals 2*I.

The section modulus for bending is Z = I/(d/2) = π*d^3/32.

The section modulus for torsion is Z_p = J/(d/2) = π*d^3/16.

The maximum normal stress due to bending is σ_x_max = M/Z.

The maximum shear stress due to torsion is τ_max = T/Z_p.

At the critical point on the outer surface, the stress state is plane stress with σ_x = σ_x_max, σ_y = 0, σ_z = 0, and τ_xy = τ_max (or τ_xz depending on orientation).

The principal stresses are calculated using σ_1,2 = (σ_x/2) ± sqrt((σ_x/2)^2 + τ^2).

The maximum in-plane shear stress is τ_max_in_plane = sqrt((σ_x/2)^2 + τ^2).

The principal angle θ_p is given by tan(2θ_p) = 2τ/σ_x.

The von Mises equivalent stress is σ_vm = sqrt(σ_x^2 + 3τ^2).

The maximum shear stress (Tresca) is τ_tresca = (σ_1 - σ_3)/2, which for plane stress equals sqrt((σ_x/2)^2 + τ^2).

Strains are calculated using Hooke's law: ε_x = (σ_x - νσ_y)/E, which simplifies to ε_x = σ_x/E for uniaxial normal stress superposed with shear.

The shear strain is γ = τ/G, where G = E/(2(1+ν)).

Principal strains are obtained from principal stresses using ε_1 = (σ_1 - ν(σ_2 + σ_3))/E, with σ_3 = 0 for plane stress.

The analysis assumes linear-elastic, isotropic, homogeneous material behavior.

The analysis assumes small deformations.

The analysis assumes the shaft is straight and prismatic.

The analysis assumes loading is static and applied gradually.

Stress concentrations at geometric discontinuities are neglected.
