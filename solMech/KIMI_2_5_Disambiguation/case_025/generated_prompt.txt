The cantilever beam has length L = 1500 mm and constant moment of inertia I = 2500000 mm⁴ along its length.

A downward point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end.

The beam material has Young's modulus E = 69000 MPa.

The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

The strain energy U in a beam due to bending is given by U = ∫₀ᴸ (M² / 2EI) dx, where M is the internal bending moment.

For a cantilever beam with end load P, the bending moment at position x from the fixed end is M(x) = -P(L - x).

Substituting the moment expression, the strain energy becomes U = ∫₀ᴸ [P²(L - x)² / 2EI] dx = P²L³ / 6EI.

By Castigliano's second theorem, the deflection at the point of load application in the direction of the load equals the partial derivative of strain energy with respect to that load.

The end deflection δ is therefore δ = ∂U/∂P = PL³ / 3EI.

Substituting the given values: δ = (500 N)(1500 mm)³ / [3 × (69000 N/mm²) × (2500000 mm⁴)].

The calculated end deflection is δ = 3.26 mm downward.
