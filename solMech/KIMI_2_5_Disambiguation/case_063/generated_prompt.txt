The rod is made of structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa and coefficient of thermal expansion α = 1e-05 1/°C.

The rod has a circular cross-section with diameter d = 20 mm, giving cross-sectional area A = 100π mm² ≈ 314.16 mm².

The rod is subjected to a uniform temperature change ΔT = 35 °C.

The rod has full axial restraint, meaning the total axial strain is constrained to zero (ε_total = 0).

There is no additional axial mechanical load applied (P = 0).

The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

The thermal strain that would occur if the rod were free to expand is ε_thermal = αΔT = (1e-05 1/°C)(35 °C) = 3.5e-04.

With full axial restraint, the total strain is zero, so the mechanical strain must equal the negative of the thermal strain: ε_mechanical = -ε_thermal = -3.5e-04.

The stress induced by the restraint is σ = Eε_mechanical = (200000 MPa)(-3.5e-04) = -70 MPa (compressive).

The axial force induced in the rod is F = σA = (-70 MPa)(314.16 mm²) = -21991.1 N ≈ -22.0 kN (compressive).

The relevant response quantities are: thermal strain (free expansion) = 3.5e-04, mechanical strain = -3.5e-04, total strain = 0, stress = -70 MPa (compressive), and axial force = -22.0 kN (compressive).
