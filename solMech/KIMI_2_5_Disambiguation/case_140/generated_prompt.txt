The analysis shall determine stress and strain response quantities for a circular shaft under combined uniaxial bending and torsion at a single critical section.

The shaft diameter is d = 45 mm, giving a radius r = d/2 = 22.5 mm.

The bending moment is M = 2000000 N·mm applied about a single axis, producing uniaxial bending stress.

The torque is T = 500000 N·mm applied at the same critical section.

The material behavior is linear-elastic with small deformations.

The critical location for analysis is the outer surface of the shaft where bending stress is maximum.

The bending stress at the outer fiber is computed using the flexure formula: σ = M·c/I, where c = r = d/2 and I = π·d^4/64.

The torsional shear stress at the outer surface is computed using: τ = T·r/J, where J = π·d^4/32 is the polar moment of inertia.

The stress state at the critical point on the surface is: normal stress σx = σ (from bending), normal stress σy = 0, and shear stress τxy = τ (from torsion).

The principal stresses are computed using: σ1,2 = (σx/2) ± √[(σx/2)^2 + τxy^2].

The maximum in-plane shear stress is: τmax = √[(σx/2)^2 + τxy^2].

The principal angle θp is given by: tan(2θp) = 2τxy/σx.

For strain analysis, the principal strains are: ε1 = (σ1 - ν·σ2)/E and ε2 = (σ2 - ν·σ1)/E, where E is Young's modulus and ν is Poisson's ratio.

The maximum shear strain is: γmax = (1+ν)·τmax/E = τmax/G, where G is the shear modulus.

The von Mises equivalent stress for yield comparison is: σvm = √(σx^2 + 3·τxy^2).

Numerical evaluation yields: I = π·(45)^4/64 = 201288.9 mm^4, J = 402577.8 mm^4.

Bending stress: σ = (2000000)(22.5)/(201288.9) = 223.4 MPa.

Torsional shear stress: τ = (500000)(22.5)/(402577.8) = 27.9 MPa.

Principal stresses: σ1,2 = (223.4/2) ± √[(111.7)^2 + (27.9)^2] = 111.7 ± 115.1, giving σ1 = 226.8 MPa and σ2 = -3.4 MPa.

Maximum in-plane shear stress: τmax = 115.1 MPa.

Principal angle: θp = 0.5·arctan(2·27.9/223.4) = 6.9° from the shaft axis.

Von Mises stress: σvm = √[(223.4)^2 + 3·(27.9)^2] = 228.6 MPa.
