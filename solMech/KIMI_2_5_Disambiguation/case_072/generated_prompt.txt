The shaft is prismatic with constant circular cross-section of diameter d = 25 mm along its entire length L = 1000 mm.

The shaft is homogeneous and isotropic with constant shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa throughout.

The shaft is fixed at one end and free at the other end (cantilevered configuration).

A concentrated torque T = 350000 N·mm is applied at the free end, resulting in constant internal torque T throughout the length.

The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics behavior.

The polar moment of inertia for the circular cross-section is J = πd⁴/32.

The strain energy U stored in the shaft due to torsion is U = ∫₀ᴸ (T²/2GJ) dx = T²L/2GJ for constant torque.

By Castigliano's theorem, the angle of twist φ at the free end is ∂U/∂T = TL/GJ.

The governing relation for twist via Castigliano's energy method is φ = TL/GJ.

Numerical evaluation yields J = π(25 mm)⁴/32 = 38349.5 mm⁴, and φ = (350000 N·mm)(1000 mm)/(76923.1 MPa)(38349.5 mm⁴) = 0.1186 rad = 6.80 degrees.
