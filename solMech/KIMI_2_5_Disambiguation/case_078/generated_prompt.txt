The shaft is subjected to pure torsion with torque T = 800000 N·mm.

The total allowable angle of twist over length L = 1200 mm is φ_allow = 1.0°.

The shear modulus of the shaft material is G = 77519.4 MPa.

The shaft is solid and circular in cross-section.

The material behaves in a linear-elastic manner with small deformations.

The governing relation for angle of twist in torsion is φ = TL/(GJ), where J is the polar moment of inertia.

For a solid circular shaft, J = πd⁴/32, where d is the shaft diameter.

The design constraint requires φ ≤ φ_allow.

Rearranging the governing relation to solve for required polar moment of inertia: J_required = TL/(G·φ_allow).

Convert φ_allow to radians: φ_allow = 1.0° × (π/180) = 0.017453 rad.

Calculate J_required = (800000 N·mm)(1200 mm) / [(77519.4 N/mm²)(0.017453 rad)] = 707955.7 mm⁴.

Solve for required diameter: d = [32·J_required/π]^(1/4) = [32 × 707955.7/π]^(1/4) = 50.0 mm.

The minimum solid shaft diameter required to satisfy the angle-of-twist limit is d = 50.0 mm.
