The cantilever beam is subjected to a single uniformly distributed transverse load w = 1.5 N/mm acting downward over the entire span.

The beam has length L = 1000 mm.

The cross-section is rectangular with width b = 50 mm and height h = 100 mm, selected as reasonable proportions for structural beam applications.

The material has Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.

The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

The governing differential equation for beam deflection is EI(d⁴v/dx⁴) = -w, where v is the transverse deflection and x is the coordinate along the beam axis from the fixed end.

The boundary conditions for a cantilever are: v(0) = 0, dv/dx(0) = 0 (fixed end), and d²v/dx²(L) = 0, d³v/dx³(L) = 0 (free end).

The flexural rigidity is EI = E × (bh³/12) = 200000 MPa × (50 mm × (100 mm)³ / 12) = 8.333 × 10¹² N·mm².

The maximum deflection occurs at the free end (x = L) and equals v_max = wL⁴/(8EI).

The maximum deflection is v_max = (1.5 N/mm × (1000 mm)⁴) / (8 × 8.333 × 10¹² N·mm²) = 2.25 mm downward.

The deflection curve is v(x) = (wx²/24EI)(6L² - 4Lx + x²).

The maximum bending moment occurs at the fixed end and equals M_max = wL²/2 = 750000 N·mm.

The maximum bending stress occurs at the fixed end outer fibers and equals σ_max = M_max × (h/2) / (bh³/12) = 45 MPa.
