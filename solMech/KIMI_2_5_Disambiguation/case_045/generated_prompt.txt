The rod is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with Young's modulus E = 69000 MPa and yield strength Sy = 275 MPa.

The rod has a circular cross-section with diameter d = 12 mm.

The rod has length L = 800 mm.

The axial tensile load is P = 15000 N.

The cross-sectional area is A = πd²/4 = π × (12 mm)² / 4 = 113.10 mm².

The average normal stress is σ = P/A = 15000 N / 113.10 mm² = 132.63 MPa.

The average normal strain is ε = σ/E = 132.63 MPa / 69000 MPa = 0.001922 (dimensionless), or 1922 microstrain.

The elongation is δ = ε × L = 0.001922 × 800 mm = 1.538 mm.

The factor of safety with respect to yielding is FS = Sy/σ = 275 MPa / 132.63 MPa = 2.07.

Small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior is assumed.

The rod is prismatic and straight with uniform properties along its length.

The load is applied axially and concentrically, producing uniform stress distribution.

No buckling or instability effects are considered.

Temperature effects and self-weight are neglected.

The governing relations used are: σ = P/A for average normal stress, ε = σ/E from Hooke's law, δ = εL = PL/(AE) for elongation, and FS = Sy/σ for factor of safety against yielding.
