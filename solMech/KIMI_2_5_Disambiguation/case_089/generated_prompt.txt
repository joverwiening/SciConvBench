The stepped circular bar consists of two segments in series under uniaxial tension.

Segment 1 has diameter d1 = 16 mm and length L1 = 400 mm.

Segment 2 has diameter d2 = 40 mm and length L2 = 400 mm.

The applied tension load is P = 25000 N acting along the bar axis.

The material is homogeneous, isotropic, and linear-elastic with Young's modulus E (value to be specified or symbolically retained).

Plane sections remain plane and perpendicular to the axis (Saint-Venant's principle applies away from the step).

The load is applied axially without eccentricity, producing uniform stress distribution in each segment.

The bar is statically determinate with internal axial force N = P throughout both segments.

Cross-sectional areas are A1 = π(d1/2)² = π(8 mm)² = 64π mm² and A2 = π(d2/2)² = π(20 mm)² = 400π mm².

Normal stress in Segment 1 is σ1 = P/A1 = 25000 N / (64π mm²) = 124.34 MPa (tensile).

Normal stress in Segment 2 is σ2 = P/A2 = 25000 N / (400π mm²) = 19.89 MPa (tensile).

Axial strain in Segment 1 is ε1 = σ1/E = P/(A1·E).

Axial strain in Segment 2 is ε2 = σ2/E = P/(A2·E).

Elongation of Segment 1 is δ1 = ε1·L1 = (P·L1)/(A1·E).

Elongation of Segment 2 is δ2 = ε2·L2 = (P·L2)/(A2·E).

Total elongation of the stepped bar is δ_total = δ1 + δ2 = P·[L1/(A1·E) + L2/(A2·E)] = (P/E)·[L1/A1 + L2/A2].

Numerically, δ_total = (25000 N/E)·[400 mm/(64π mm²) + 400 mm/(400π mm²)] = (25000 N/E)·[1.989 mm⁻¹ + 0.318 mm⁻¹] = 57730.3 N·mm⁻¹/E = 57.73 kN·m⁻¹/E.

If E = 200 GPa (typical steel), then δ_total = 0.289 mm.
