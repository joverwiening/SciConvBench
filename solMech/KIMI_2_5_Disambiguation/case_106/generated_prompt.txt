The beam is a simply supported Euler-Bernoulli beam with length L = 2500 mm.

The beam has flexural rigidity EI = 7 × 10¹¹ N·mm², where E = 200000 MPa and I = 3500000 mm⁴.

A single point load P = 2500 N acts downward at midspan (x = L/2 = 1250 mm).

No shear loading is applied to the beam.

Small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics assumptions apply.

The governing differential equation for Euler-Bernoulli beam theory is: EI(d⁴w/dx⁴) = q(x), where w(x) is the transverse deflection and q(x) is the distributed load.

For this case with a point load at midspan, the beam is analyzed in two segments: 0 ≤ x ≤ L/2 and L/2 ≤ x ≤ L.

Boundary conditions for the simply supported beam are: w(0) = 0, w(L) = 0, M(0) = EI(d²w/dx²)|_{x=0} = 0, and M(L) = EI(d²w/dx²)|_{x=L} = 0.

Continuity conditions at midspan require: w(L/2⁻) = w(L/2⁺), dw/dx|_{x=L/2⁻} = dw/dx|_{x=L/2⁺}, and the shear force discontinuity equals the applied point load: V(L/2⁺) - V(L/2⁻) = -P.

The midspan deflection is δ = PL³/(48EI) = (2500 N)(2500 mm)³/(48 × 7 × 10¹¹ N·mm²) = 1.1646 mm downward.

The support slope is θ = PL²/(16EI) = (2500 N)(2500 mm)²/(16 × 7 × 10¹¹ N·mm²) = 1.3976 × 10⁻³ rad = 0.0801° at both supports (equal magnitude, opposite sign convention: positive slope at x=0, negative slope at x=L).
