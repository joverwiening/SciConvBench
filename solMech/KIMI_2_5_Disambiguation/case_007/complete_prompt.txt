Use N–mm–MPa units.

A simply supported beam of span L = 1800 mm (supports at x=0 and x=L) is made of AISI 1020 steel with E = 200000 MPa and I = 3500000 mm^4. A point load P = 2000 N acts downward at x = 900 mm.

Using the unit-load (virtual work) method for beam deflection, compute the deflection at the load point x = 900 mm. Use
δ = ∫[0→L] (M(x)·m(x))/(EI) dx,
where M is the real bending moment diagram and m is the moment diagram due to a unit load applied at the point and direction of the desired deflection.
