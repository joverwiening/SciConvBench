The simply supported beam has span length L = 1800 mm with flexural rigidity EI = 200000 MPa × 3500000 mm⁴ = 7.0 × 10¹¹ N·mm².

A point load P = 2000 N acts downward at x = 900 mm, which is the midspan location.

The objective is to determine the deflection at x = 900 mm using the unit-load method.

The governing relation for the unit-load method for beam deflection is: Δ = ∫(M·m/EI)dx from 0 to L, where M is the bending moment due to the actual load and m is the bending moment due to a unit load applied at the location and in the direction of the desired deflection.

Due to symmetry of loading at midspan, the reaction forces are R_A = R_B = P/2 = 1000 N.

For the actual load case, the bending moment is: M(x) = 1000·x for 0 ≤ x ≤ 900 mm, and M(x) = 1000·(1800-x) for 900 ≤ x ≤ 1800 mm.

For the unit-load case, apply a unit load (1 N) downward at x = 900 mm. By symmetry, the reactions are r_A = r_B = 0.5 N. The bending moment is: m(x) = 0.5·x for 0 ≤ x ≤ 900 mm, and m(x) = 0.5·(1800-x) for 900 ≤ x ≤ 1800 mm.

The deflection integral is computed over two segments due to the change in moment expressions at x = 900 mm.

For segment 1 (0 to 900 mm): ∫(1000·x)(0.5·x)/EI dx = (500/EI)∫x² dx from 0 to 900 = (500/EI)·[x³/3]₀⁹⁰⁰ = (500/EI)·(243×10⁶/3) = (500/EI)·(81×10⁶) = 40.5×10⁹/EI.

For segment 2 (900 to 1800 mm): by symmetry, this equals segment 1, so the contribution is also 40.5×10⁹/EI.

Total deflection: Δ = 2 × 40.5×10⁹/EI = 81×10⁹/EI = 81×10⁹/(7.0×10¹¹) mm = 0.1157 mm downward.

The deflection at x = 900 mm is 0.116 mm downward (or approximately 0.1157 mm).
