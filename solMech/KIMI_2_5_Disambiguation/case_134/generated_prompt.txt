The structural member is a rectangular adhesive layer with area A = 3000 mm² and thickness t = 8 mm.

The applied shear load is a uniform in-plane shear force V = 12000 N, resulting in a uniform average shear stress distribution where τ_avg = V/A.

The material is isotropic with shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.

The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

The adhesive layer is modeled as a thin rectangular plate undergoing uniform shear deformation.

Plane stress conditions are assumed due to the thin geometry (t << √A).

The shear stress is uniformly distributed throughout the adhesive layer.

Calculate the average shear stress: τ_avg = V/A = 12000 N / 3000 mm² = 4 MPa.

Calculate the shear strain using Hooke's law for shear: γ = τ_avg/G = 4 MPa / 39179.1 MPa = 1.021 × 10⁻⁴ (dimensionless, or 102.1 microstrain).

Calculate the shear deformation (relative displacement across the thickness): δ_s = γ × t = 1.021 × 10⁻⁴ × 8 mm = 8.17 × 10⁻⁴ mm = 0.817 μm.

The governing relations are: τ = Gγ (constitutive relation), γ = δ_s/t (kinematic relation), and τ = V/A (equilibrium relation for uniform stress).
