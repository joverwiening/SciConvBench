The rod is a solid circular cylinder with initial diameter d₀ = 30 mm and length L = 500 mm.

The rod material is isotropic, linear-elastic with Young's modulus E = 105000 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.34.

The rod is subjected only to a uniaxial tensile axial load P = 25000 N applied along its longitudinal axis; no lateral load is present.

The analysis assumes small deformations, allowing the use of infinitesimal strain theory and original geometry for calculations.

The governing relations for uniaxial tension are:

The axial stress is σ = P/A₀, where A₀ = π(d₀/2)² is the original cross-sectional area.

The axial strain is ε_axial = σ/E.

The lateral strain is ε_lateral = -ν·ε_axial = -ν·σ/E.

The change in diameter is Δd = ε_lateral·d₀ = -ν·σ·d₀/E.

The change in length is ΔL = ε_axial·L = σ·L/E.

The Poisson effect manifests as a contraction in diameter proportional to the axial extension, with the ratio of lateral strain to axial strain equal to -ν.

The relevant response quantities to determine are: axial stress, axial strain, lateral strain, change in length, and change in diameter.
