The structural member is a rectangular prismatic member with width b = 40 mm and height h = 120 mm.

The tension load is applied as an internal resultant normal force P = 40000 N (tension) at a given cross section.

The shear load is applied as an internal shear force V = 8000 N (shear parallel to the width) at the same cross section.

The analysis is focused on a specific cross-section where the stress state is evaluated using average stress approximations.

The cross-sectional area is A = b × h = 40 mm × 120 mm = 4800 mm².

The average normal stress is σ = P/A = 40000 N / 4800 mm² = 8.333 MPa (tension).

The average shear stress is τ = V/A = 8000 N / 4800 mm² = 1.667 MPa.

The stress state at a point is treated as plane stress with σx = σ = 8.333 MPa, σy = 0, and τxy = τ = 1.667 MPa.

The material behavior is assumed to be small-deformation, linear-elastic, and isotropic with Young's modulus E and Poisson's ratio ν (values not specified, to be used symbolically).

The governing relations for plane stress are:

The normal strain in x is εx = (σx - νσy)/E = σx/E.

The normal strain in y is εy = (σy - νσx)/E = -νσx/E.

The shear strain is γxy = τxy/G = 2(1+ν)τxy/E, where G is the shear modulus.

The principal stresses are found from σ1,2 = (σx + σy)/2 ± √[((σx - σy)/2)² + τxy²].

Substituting values: σ1,2 = (8.333 + 0)/2 ± √[((8.333 - 0)/2)² + (1.667)²] = 4.167 ± √[17.361 + 2.778] = 4.167 ± 4.493 MPa.

Thus σ1 = 8.660 MPa and σ2 = -0.326 MPa.

The principal angle θp satisfies tan(2θp) = 2τxy/(σx - σy) = 2(1.667)/(8.333) = 0.400, giving 2θp = 21.80° and θp = 10.90° from the x-axis.

The maximum in-plane shear stress is τmax = (σ1 - σ2)/2 = √[((σx - σy)/2)² + τxy²] = 4.493 MPa.

The principal strains are ε1 = (σ1 - νσ2)/E and ε2 = (σ2 - νσ1)/E, with ε3 = -ν(σ1 + σ2)/E for the out-of-plane direction (non-zero due to Poisson effect despite zero out-of-plane stress).

The maximum shear strain is γmax = ε1 - ε2 = (1+ν)(σ1 - σ2)/E.
