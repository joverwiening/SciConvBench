The analysis concerns a stepped solid circular shaft in torsion with small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.

Geometric properties:
Segment AB has length L1 = 400 mm and diameter d1 = 30 mm.
Segment BC has length L2 = 700 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm.
Both segments have identical diameters, so the shaft is geometrically uniform despite being described as stepped.

Material properties:
The shaft is made of a linear-elastic material with shear modulus G.
The value of G is not specified and must be assumed or provided for numerical results.

Loading and boundary conditions:
A torque T = 600000 N·mm is applied at free end C.
End A is fixed (built-in support).
The internal torque is constant throughout both segments with magnitude T = 600000 N·mm.

Governing relations:
The polar moment of inertia for a solid circular cross-section is J = πd⁴/32.
Since d1 = d2 = 30 mm, J1 = J2 = π(30)⁴/32 = 79521.6 mm⁴.

Maximum shear stress in each segment occurs at the outer radius r = d/2 = 15 mm.
The torsional shear stress formula is τ_max = T·r/J = T·(d/2)/J = 16T/(πd³).
For both segments: τ_max = 16 × 600000 / (π × 30³) = 113.18 MPa.

The angle of twist for a uniform shaft segment is φ = T·L/(G·J).
Total twist from A to C is the sum of twists in both segments: φ_total = φ_AB + φ_BC = T·L1/(G·J) + T·L2/(G·J) = T·(L1 + L2)/(G·J).
The total twist angle is φ_total = 600000 × 1100 / (G × 79521.6) = 8.299×10⁶/G radians, where G is in MPa.

Response quantities:
Maximum shear stress: 113.18 MPa (uniform throughout shaft).
Total angle of twist: 8.299×10⁶/G radians (requires G for numerical value).
