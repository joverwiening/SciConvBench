The problem involves a prismatic circular rod of AISI steel with full axial restraint subjected to a uniform temperature increase, with no externally applied axial load. The response quantities are determined as follows.

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

The rod has circular cross-section with diameter d = 20 mm.

The rod has length L = 800 mm.

The temperature change is ΔT = 50 °C.

The Young's modulus is E = 200000 MPa.

The coefficient of thermal expansion is α = 2×10⁻⁵ /°C.

There is no externally applied axial load. The rod is placed between two rigid, unyielding supports with full axial restraint.

The thermal strain with full axial restraint is zero by definition of full restraint: the total mechanical strain is zero.

The free thermal strain that would occur without restraint is ε_thermal_free = α × ΔT = (2×10⁻⁵ /°C) × (50 °C) = 0.001 = 1×10⁻³.

The mechanical strain due to the constraint is ε_mechanical = −ε_thermal_free = −0.001 = −1×10⁻³ (compressive).

The thermal stress that develops is σ = E × ε_mechanical = (200000 MPa) × (−0.001) = −200 MPa (compressive).

The cross-sectional area is A = π × d² / 4 = π × (20 mm)² / 4 = 314.16 mm².

The reaction force developed at the supports is F = σ × A = (−200 MPa) × (314.16 mm²) = −62832 N = −62.83 kN (compressive).
