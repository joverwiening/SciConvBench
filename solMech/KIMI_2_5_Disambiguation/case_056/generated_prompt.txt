The bar is a linear-elastic, small-deformation solid with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.

The bar has length L = 800 mm.

The cross-sectional area varies linearly along the bar length according to A(x) = A₀·(1 + k·x/L), where x is measured from the left end (x = 0) to the right end (x = L), with A₀ = 250 mm² and k = 0.8.

The right end of the bar (x = L) is subjected to a tensile axial load P = 30000 N.

The left end of the bar (x = 0) is fixed (displacement u = 0).

The governing differential equation for axial deformation is d/dx(EA(x)·du/dx) = 0, which for constant E reduces to d/dx(A(x)·du/dx) = 0.

The axial force N(x) is constant throughout the bar and equals the applied load P = 30000 N.

The normal stress varies with position as σ(x) = N/A(x) = P/[A₀·(1 + k·x/L)].

The axial strain varies with position as ε(x) = σ(x)/E = P/[E·A₀·(1 + k·x/L)].

The displacement field is obtained by integrating the strain: u(x) = ∫₀ˣ ε(ξ) dξ = (PL)/(EA₀k)·ln(1 + kx/L).

The total elongation of the bar is Δ = u(L) = (PL)/(EA₀k)·ln(1 + k).

Numerical values: A₀ = 250 mm², k = 0.8, L = 800 mm, P = 30000 N, E = 200000 MPa = 200000 N/mm².

The area at the left end is A(0) = 250 mm².

The area at the right end is A(L) = 250·(1 + 0.8) = 450 mm².

The stress at the left end is σ(0) = 30000/250 = 120 MPa.

The stress at the right end is σ(L) = 30000/450 = 66.67 MPa.

The total elongation is Δ = (30000 × 800)/(200000 × 250 × 0.8) × ln(1.8) = 0.6 × 0.5878 = 0.3527 mm.
