The cantilever beam analysis shall be governed by the following specification:

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

The beam is a cantilever with fixed support at one end and free end at the other.

The beam length is L = 2000 mm.

The cross-section is rectangular, which is a reasonable standard assumption for general cantilever analysis when no specific shape is provided.

The beam is subjected to a single uniformly distributed transverse load of magnitude w = 1.0 N/mm acting downward over the entire span.

The material is structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.

The moment of inertia of the cross-section is I = 1500000 mm⁴.

The governing differential equation for beam deflection is EI(d⁴v/dx⁴) = w(x), where v is the transverse deflection and x is the coordinate along the beam length measured from the fixed end.

For a uniformly distributed load w, the general solution is v(x) = (wx⁴)/(24EI) + C₁x³ + C₂x² + C₃x + C₄.

The boundary conditions at the fixed end (x = 0) are zero deflection v(0) = 0 and zero slope dv/dx(0) = 0.

The boundary conditions at the free end (x = L) are zero moment EI(d²v/dx²)(L) = 0 and zero shear EI(d³v/dx³)(L) = 0.

The maximum deflection occurs at the free end x = L with magnitude v_max = wL⁴/(8EI).

The maximum bending moment occurs at the fixed end x = 0 with magnitude M_max = wL²/2.

The maximum shear force occurs at the fixed end x = 0 with magnitude V_max = wL.

The slope at any point is given by θ(x) = dv/dx = (wx³)/(6EI) - (wLx²)/(4EI) + (wL²x)/(6EI) - (wL³)/(6EI).

The deflection at any point is given by v(x) = (wx²)/(24EI)(x² - 4Lx + 6L²).

The reaction force at the fixed end is R = wL upward.

The reaction moment at the fixed end is M = wL²/2 counter-clockwise.
