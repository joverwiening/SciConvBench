The shaft is made of structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200 GPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.3.

The shaft length is L = 500 mm, a typical value for torsion-axial load problems that allows meaningful deformation calculations without excessive slenderness.

The yield strength of the material is σ_y = 250 MPa, corresponding to ASTM A36 or similar mild structural steel.

The governing relations for combined axial load and torsion on a circular shaft are as follows.

The cross-sectional area is A = πd²/4 = π(25 mm)²/4 = 490.87 mm².

The polar moment of inertia is J = πd⁴/32 = π(25 mm)⁴/32 = 38349.5 mm⁴.

The axial stress is σ_axial = P/A = 15000 N / 490.87 mm² = 30.56 MPa (tensile).

The maximum shear stress due to torsion is τ_max = T·r/J = (400000 N·mm)(12.5 mm) / 38349.5 mm⁴ = 130.38 MPa, occurring at the outer surface.

The normal stress and shear stress combine at the critical point (outer surface). The principal stresses are found from the stress transformation equations for plane stress with σ_x = σ_axial, σ_y = 0, and τ_xy = τ_max.

The principal stresses are σ_1,2 = (σ_x/2) ± √[(σ_x/2)² + τ_xy²] = (30.56/2) ± √[(15.28)² + (130.38)²] = 15.28 ± 131.27 MPa.

Thus σ_1 = 146.55 MPa (maximum principal stress), σ_2 = -115.99 MPa (minimum principal stress), and σ_3 = 0 (plane stress).

The maximum in-plane shear stress is τ_max_in_plane = (σ_1 - σ_2)/2 = 131.27 MPa.

The absolute maximum shear stress is τ_abs_max = (σ_1 - σ_3)/2 = 73.28 MPa if σ_3 is considered, or more precisely for 3D: τ_abs_max = max[(σ_1-σ_2)/2, (σ_2-σ_3)/2, (σ_1-σ_3)/2] = 131.27 MPa.

The von Mises equivalent stress is σ_vm = √[σ_1² + σ_2² - σ_1σ_2] = √[146.55² + (-115.99)² - (146.55)(-115.99)] = 227.6 MPa, or equivalently σ_vm = √[σ_axial² + 3τ_max²] = √[30.56² + 3(130.38)²] = 227.6 MPa.

The safety factor against yielding is SF = σ_y / σ_vm = 250 MPa / 227.6 MPa = 1.10.

The axial strain is ε_axial = σ_axial/E = 30.56 MPa / 200000 MPa = 152.8 microstrain.

The total axial elongation is δ = ε_axial · L = 152.8×10⁻⁶ × 500 mm = 0.076 mm.

The angle of twist per unit length is θ/L = T/(G·J) where G = E/[2(1+ν)] = 200000/[2(1.3)] = 76.92 GPa, giving θ/L = 400000/(76923 × 38349.5) = 0.1356 rad/m = 0.1356×10⁻³ rad/mm.

The total angle of twist is θ = (θ/L) · L = 0.1356×10⁻³ × 500 = 0.0678 rad = 3.89 degrees.

The principal strains are ε_1 = [σ_1 - ν(σ_2+σ_3)]/E = [146.55 - 0.3(-115.99)]/200000 = 906.2 microstrain, ε_2 = [σ_2 - ν(σ_1+σ_3)]/E = [-115.99 - 0.3(146.55)]/200000 = -799.8 microstrain, and ε_3 = [σ_3 - ν(σ_1+σ_2)]/E = [0 - 0.3(30.56)]/200000 = -45.8 microstrain (verifying ε_1+ε_2+ε_3 ≈ 0 for constant volume in plasticity, though here the sum is small due to elastic compressibility).
