The prismatic bar has a rectangular cross-section with width b = 30 mm and height h = 100 mm.

The internal resultant normal force is P = 50000 N (tension).

The internal resultant transverse shear force is V = 5000 N acting parallel to the width b.

The cross-sectional area is A = b × h = 30 mm × 100 mm = 3000 mm².

The average normal stress is σ_avg = P / A = 50000 N / 3000 mm² = 16.67 MPa (tensile).

The average shear stress is τ_avg = V / A = 5000 N / 3000 mm² = 1.67 MPa.

The governing relation for average normal stress is σ_avg = P / A, where P is the internal normal force and A is the cross-sectional area.

The governing relation for average shear stress is τ_avg = V / A, where V is the internal transverse shear force and A is the cross-sectional area.

Additional assumptions include: the material is homogeneous and isotropic, the bar is prismatic (constant cross-section), plane sections remain plane (Euler-Bernoulli beam theory), and the loading is applied such that Saint-Venant's principle applies (stresses are evaluated sufficiently far from load application points).
