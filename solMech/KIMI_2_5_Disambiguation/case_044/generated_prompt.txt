The rod is a 6061-T6 aluminum alloy cylinder with diameter 40 mm and length 500 mm.

The rod is subjected only to uniaxial tension with an axial tensile load of 35000 N.

The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

The material properties for 6061-T6 aluminum are assumed as: Young's modulus E = 69 GPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.33.

The cross-sectional area of the rod is A = π × (20 mm)² = 1256.64 mm².

The axial stress is σ_x = P/A = 35000 N / 1256.64 mm² = 27.85 MPa.

The axial strain is ε_x = σ_x / E = 27.85 MPa / 69000 MPa = 4.036 × 10⁻⁴.

The lateral strains due to the Poisson effect are ε_y = ε_z = −ν × ε_x = −0.33 × 4.036 × 10⁻⁴ = −1.332 × 10⁻⁴.

The change in length is ΔL = ε_x × L = 4.036 × 10⁻⁴ × 500 mm = 0.202 mm (elongation).

The change in diameter is ΔD = ε_y × D = −1.332 × 10⁻⁴ × 40 mm = −5.33 × 10⁻³ mm (contraction).

The governing relations are: σ_x = E × ε_x for axial response, and ε_lateral = −ν × ε_axial for the Poisson effect.
