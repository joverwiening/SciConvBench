The beam is a prismatic rectangular cross-section with width b = 50 mm (horizontal) and height h = 100 mm (vertical), oriented such that bending occurs about the strong axis (centroidal z-axis) in the vertical plane.

The loading consists of pure bending only with bending moment M = 3000000 N·mm about the z-axis. No axial forces are present.

The material is linear-elastic with small deformations assumed.

The neutral axis for bending about the z-axis coincides with the centroidal z-axis, located at h/2 = 50 mm from the top and bottom surfaces.

The moment of inertia about the z-axis is I_z = (b * h^3) / 12 = (50 mm * (100 mm)^3) / 12 = 4,166,667 mm^4.

The flexure formula gives the bending stress distribution: sigma_x(y) = - (M * y) / I_z, where y is measured positive upward from the neutral axis.

The maximum distance from the neutral axis is c = h/2 = 50 mm.

The maximum bending stress occurs at the extreme fibers (y = ±c): sigma_max = (M * c) / I_z = (3,000,000 N·mm * 50 mm) / 4,166,667 mm^4 = 36 MPa.

Maximum tensile stress occurs at the bottom fiber (y = -c): sigma_tensile = +36 MPa.

Maximum compressive stress occurs at the top fiber (y = +c): sigma_compressive = -36 MPa.

The stress varies linearly from -36 MPa at the top surface through zero at the neutral axis to +36 MPa at the bottom surface.

The radius of curvature is given by: 1/rho = M / (E * I_z), where E is the Young's modulus of the material.
