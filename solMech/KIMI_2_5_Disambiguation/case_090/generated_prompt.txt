The stepped circular bar analysis shall proceed under the following conditions and governing relations.

Assumptions:
The material is homogeneous and isotropic.
The deformation is small and within the linear elastic range.
The axial load is applied concentrically producing uniform stress distribution.
Saint-Venant's principle applies; stress concentrations at the step transition are neglected for segment stress calculations.
The bar is straight and segments are perfectly aligned.
Self-weight of the bar is negligible compared to the applied load.

Geometric properties:
Segment 1 diameter d1 = 12 mm, length L1 = 300 mm.
Segment 2 diameter d2 = 30 mm, length L2 = 600 mm.
Cross-sectional area of segment 1: A1 = π × (d1)² / 4 = π × (12 mm)² / 4 = 113.097 mm².
Cross-sectional area of segment 2: A2 = π × (d2)² / 4 = π × (30 mm)² / 4 = 706.858 mm².

Loading and material:
Applied tensile force P = 25000 N.
Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa = 200000 N/mm².

Governing relations:
Axial stress in a segment: σ = P / A.
Axial strain in a segment: ε = σ / E = P / (A × E).
Elongation of a segment: δ = ε × L = (P × L) / (A × E).

Calculated response quantities:
Stress in segment 1: σ1 = 25000 N / 113.097 mm² = 221.05 MPa (tension).
Stress in segment 2: σ2 = 25000 N / 706.858 mm² = 35.37 MPa (tension).
Elongation of segment 1: δ1 = (25000 N × 300 mm) / (113.097 mm² × 200000 N/mm²) = 0.3313 mm.
Elongation of segment 2: δ2 = (25000 N × 600 mm) / (706.858 mm² × 200000 N/mm²) = 0.1061 mm.
Total elongation of the stepped bar: δ_total = δ1 + δ2 = 0.4374 mm.
