The shaft is a solid circular cylinder with diameter d = 30 mm and total length L = 1200 mm.

The shaft is fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.

The material is AISI 1020 steel with shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa.

The shaft is subjected to small-deformation, linear-elastic torsion.

Torque T1 = 200000 N·mm is applied at x = L = 1200 mm in the positive sense.

Torque T2 = -200000 N·mm is applied at x = 400 mm in the negative sense.

The shaft is divided into two segments: Segment 1 from x = 0 to x = 400 mm, and Segment 2 from x = 400 mm to x = 1200 mm.

The polar moment of inertia for the solid circular cross-section is J = πd⁴/32.

The internal torque in Segment 1 (0 ≤ x < 400 mm) is T_internal,1 = T1 + T2 = 0 N·mm.

The internal torque in Segment 2 (400 mm ≤ x ≤ 1200 mm) is T_internal,2 = T1 = 200000 N·mm.

The maximum shear stress in each segment is τ_max = T_internal * r / J, where r = d/2 is the outer radius.

The angle of twist in each segment is φ = T_internal * L_segment / (G * J).

The total angle of twist at the free end is the sum of twists from all segments.

The shear strain is γ = τ / G = T_internal * r / (G * J).

The governing relation for torsion is the twist rate equation: dφ/dx = T_internal / (G * J).
