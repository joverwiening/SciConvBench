The rod is a straight prismatic circular member subjected to uniaxial tension.

The axial tensile load is 15000 N.

The rod diameter is 30 mm, giving a cross-sectional area A = π × (15 mm)² = 706.86 mm².

The gage length is 800 mm.

The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with Young's modulus E = 69000 MPa and yield strength Sy = 275 MPa.

Small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics applies.

The average normal stress is σ = P/A = 15000 N / 706.86 mm² = 21.22 MPa.

The axial strain is ε = σ/E = 21.22 MPa / 69000 MPa = 3.076 × 10⁻⁴ (dimensionless).

The elongation is δ = ε × L = 3.076 × 10⁻⁴ × 800 mm = 0.246 mm.

The factor of safety with respect to yielding is FS = Sy/σ = 275 MPa / 21.22 MPa = 12.96.

The governing relations are: σ = P/A, ε = σ/E, δ = PL/(AE), and FS = Sy/σ.

The stress state is uniform uniaxial tension with no transverse loads, bending, or torsion.

The material is assumed homogeneous and isotropic.

The factor of safety is evaluated against yielding only; fracture or fatigue are not considered.
