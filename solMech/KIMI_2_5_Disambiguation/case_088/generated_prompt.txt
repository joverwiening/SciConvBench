The analysis shall determine the axial stress in each segment and the total elongation of a stepped circular bar under tension loading.

Assumptions:
The material behaves as linear-elastic with Young's modulus E = 200 GPa (typical steel, assumed when not specified).
The bar is prismatic within each segment with abrupt step transition.
The load is applied axially and uniformly distributed.
Self-weight of the bar is negligible compared to applied load.
The deformation is small, so original dimensions are used for calculations.
Plane sections remain plane (Saint-Venant's principle applies away from the step).

Governing relations:
Axial stress in a segment: sigma = P / A, where A = pi * d^2 / 4 is the cross-sectional area.
Axial strain in a segment: epsilon = sigma / E = P / (A * E).
Elongation of a segment: delta = epsilon * L = P * L / (A * E).
Total elongation: delta_total = delta_1 + delta_2.

Geometric properties:
Segment 1: L1 = 300 mm, d1 = 12 mm, A1 = pi * (12 mm)^2 / 4 = 113.10 mm^2.
Segment 2: L2 = 800 mm, d2 = 30 mm, A2 = pi * (30 mm)^2 / 4 = 706.86 mm^2.

Loading: P = 35000 N (tension).

Calculated response quantities:
Stress in segment 1: sigma_1 = 35000 N / 113.10 mm^2 = 309.5 MPa.
Stress in segment 2: sigma_2 = 35000 N / 706.86 mm^2 = 49.5 MPa.
Elongation of segment 1: delta_1 = (35000 N * 300 mm) / (113.10 mm^2 * 200000 MPa) = 0.464 mm.
Elongation of segment 2: delta_2 = (35000 N * 800 mm) / (706.86 mm^2 * 200000 MPa) = 0.198 mm.
Total elongation: delta_total = 0.464 mm + 0.198 mm = 0.662 mm.
