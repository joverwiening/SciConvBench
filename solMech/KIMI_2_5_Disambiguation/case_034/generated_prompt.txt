The problem involves a simply supported Euler-Bernoulli beam with a midspan point load.

Assumptions:
The beam undergoes small deformations.
The material is linear elastic, homogeneous, and isotropic.
The beam is prismatic with constant cross-section.
Shear deformation is neglected (Euler-Bernoulli theory applies).
The beam is initially straight and unstressed.
The supports are ideal simple supports allowing rotation but preventing vertical displacement.
The load is applied statically.
There is no shear loading applied to the beam.

Given parameters:
Point load P = 4000 N applied at midspan.
Beam length L = 3000 mm.
Young's modulus E = 69000 MPa = 69000 N/mm².
Second moment of area I = 3500000 mm⁴.

Governing relations for simply supported beam with midspan point load:

Maximum deflection at midspan:
δ_max = PL³ / (48EI)

Support slope (rotation at supports):
θ_support = PL² / (16EI)

Calculated response quantities:

Maximum deflection at midspan:
δ_max = (4000 N × (3000 mm)³) / (48 × 69000 N/mm² × 3500000 mm⁴)
δ_max = (4000 × 27×10⁹) / (48 × 69000 × 3500000) mm
δ_max = 1.08×10¹⁴ / 1.1592×10¹³ mm
δ_max = 9.316 mm

Support slope:
θ_support = (4000 N × (3000 mm)²) / (16 × 69000 N/mm² × 3500000 mm⁴)
θ_support = (4000 × 9×10⁶) / (16 × 69000 × 3500000) rad
θ_support = 3.6×10¹⁰ / 3.864×10¹² rad
θ_support = 0.009317 rad = 0.534 degrees
