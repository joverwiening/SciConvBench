The rod is straight, prismatic, and circular with a diameter of 12 mm.

The gage length of the rod is 800 mm.

The material is AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa and yield strength Sy = 350 MPa.

The axial tensile load applied to the rod is 15000 N.

The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics behavior.

The cross-sectional area A is calculated as πd²/4 = π × (12 mm)² / 4 = 113.10 mm².

The average normal stress σ is calculated as P/A = 15000 N / 113.10 mm² = 132.63 MPa.

The average normal strain ε is calculated as σ/E = 132.63 MPa / 200000 MPa = 0.000663 or 663 microstrain.

The elongation δ is calculated as ε × L = 0.000663 × 800 mm = 0.530 mm.

The factor of safety with respect to yielding is calculated as Sy/σ = 350 MPa / 132.63 MPa = 2.64.

The stress state is uniaxial tension with no transverse loads or bending effects considered.

The material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.

Temperature effects, creep, and dynamic loading are neglected.

The rod is assumed to be initially stress-free and undeformed prior to loading.
