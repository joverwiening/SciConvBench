The rod is a straight prismatic circular member subjected to uniaxial tension.

The cross-sectional area is calculated as A = πd²/4 = π(20 mm)²/4 = 314.16 mm².

The average normal stress is σ = P/A = 15000 N / 314.16 mm² = 47.75 MPa.

The average normal strain is ε = σ/E = 47.75 MPa / 69000 MPa = 6.92 × 10⁻⁴ (dimensionless).

The elongation is δ = εL = (6.92 × 10⁻⁴)(800 mm) = 0.554 mm.

The factor of safety with respect to yielding is FS = σ_y/σ = 275 MPa / 47.75 MPa = 5.76.

Additional assumptions: homogeneous, isotropic material; uniform stress distribution across the cross-section; load applied concentrically to prevent bending; constant temperature; no residual stresses; material remains in the linear elastic range.

Governing relations: σ = P/A, ε = σ/E, δ = PL/(AE), FS = σ_y/σ.
