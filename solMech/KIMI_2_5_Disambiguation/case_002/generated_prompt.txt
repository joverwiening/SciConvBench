The stepped circular bar consists of two segments in series under uniaxial tension.

Segment 1 (left): diameter d1 = 16 mm, length L1 = 400 mm.
Segment 2 (right): diameter d2 = 40 mm, length L2 = 800 mm.
Applied tension load: P = 35000 N.
Material: structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa for both segments.

Assumptions: small deformations, linear-elastic material behavior, homogeneous and isotropic material, uniform stress distribution in each cross-section (Saint-Venant's principle applies away from the step), load applied axially through the centroid of each cross-section, no stress concentrations considered at the step junction, bar is prismatic within each segment, temperature effects are neglected, and the bar is static (no dynamic or inertial effects).

Cross-sectional area of Segment 1: A1 = pi * d1^2 / 4 = pi * (16 mm)^2 / 4 = 201.06 mm^2.
Cross-sectional area of Segment 2: A2 = pi * d2^2 / 4 = pi * (40 mm)^2 / 4 = 1256.64 mm^2.

Stress in Segment 1: sigma1 = P / A1 = 35000 N / 201.06 mm^2 = 174.08 MPa (tensile).
Stress in Segment 2: sigma2 = P / A2 = 35000 N / 1256.64 mm^2 = 27.85 MPa (tensile).

Elongation of Segment 1: delta1 = sigma1 * L1 / E = (P * L1) / (A1 * E) = (174.08 MPa * 400 mm) / 200000 MPa = 0.348 mm.
Elongation of Segment 2: delta2 = sigma2 * L2 / E = (P * L2) / (A2 * E) = (27.85 MPa * 800 mm) / 200000 MPa = 0.111 mm.

Total elongation of the stepped bar: delta_total = delta1 + delta2 = 0.348 mm + 0.111 mm = 0.459 mm.

Alternatively, total elongation can be expressed as: delta_total = P * (L1/(A1*E) + L2/(A2*E)) = P/E * (L1/A1 + L2/A2).
