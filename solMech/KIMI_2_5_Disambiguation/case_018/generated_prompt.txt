The shaft is a solid circular cylinder with diameter d = 40 mm and total length L = 1500 mm.

The material is linear-elastic with shear modulus G (to be specified or assumed if needed).

The shaft is fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.

Torque T1 = 300000 N·mm is applied at the free end x = L in the positive (counterclockwise when viewed from +x) direction.

Torque T2 = -150000 N·mm is applied at the midpoint x = 750 mm in the negative (clockwise) direction.

The analysis considers only torsional response; no transverse shear loading is present.

The shaft is divided into two segments: Segment 1 from x = 0 to x = 750 mm, and Segment 2 from x = 750 mm to x = 1500 mm.

The internal torque in Segment 1 (0 ≤ x ≤ 750 mm) is T_I = T1 + T2 = 150000 N·mm.

The internal torque in Segment 2 (750 mm ≤ x ≤ 1500 mm) is T_II = T1 = 300000 N·mm.

The polar moment of inertia for the solid circular cross-section is J = πd⁴/32 = π(40 mm)⁴/32 = 251327.4 mm⁴.

The maximum shear stress in each segment is computed as τ = T·r/J where r = d/2 = 20 mm.

The maximum shear stress in Segment 1 is τ_max,I = (150000 N·mm)(20 mm)/(251327.4 mm⁴) = 11.94 MPa.

The maximum shear stress in Segment 2 is τ_max,II = (300000 N·mm)(20 mm)/(251327.4 mm⁴) = 23.87 MPa.

The angle of twist for each segment is computed as φ = TL/(GJ).

The angle of twist of Segment 1 relative to the fixed end is φ_I = (150000 N·mm)(750 mm)/(G·251327.4 mm⁴) = 447.6/G rad (where G is in MPa).

The angle of twist of Segment 2 relative to its left end is φ_II = (300000 N·mm)(750 mm)/(G·251327.4 mm⁴) = 895.3/G rad.

The total angle of twist at the free end is φ_total = φ_I + φ_II = 1342.9/G rad.

The shear strain at any radial position is γ = r·dφ/dx = τ/G.

The shaft remains in the small-deformation, linear-elastic regime with no yielding or geometric nonlinearity.
