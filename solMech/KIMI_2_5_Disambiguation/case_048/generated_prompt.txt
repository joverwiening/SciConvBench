The problem involves a hollow circular shaft under pure torsion with small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics assumptions.

Additional assumptions made:
The shaft is prismatic with constant cross-section along its length.
The material is homogeneous and isotropic.
Plane sections remain plane and perpendicular to the axis after deformation (no warping restraint).
The torque is applied uniformly and produces only torsional shear stresses (no bending or axial effects).

Geometric properties:
Outer radius c_o = 30 mm.
Inner radius c_i = 24 mm.
Polar moment of inertia J = π/2 × (c_o^4 - c_i^4) = π/2 × (30^4 - 24^4) mm^4 = π/2 × (810000 - 331776) mm^4 = π/2 × 478224 mm^4 = 751330.5 mm^4.

Loading:
Applied torque T = 450000 N·mm.
No shear force is applied.

Material property:
Shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa = 76923.1 N/mm^2.

Governing relations:
The angle of twist per unit length is given by dθ/dx = T/(GJ).
The total angle of twist over a length L is θ = TL/(GJ).
The shear stress at any radial position r is τ(r) = Tr/J.
The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer radius: τ_max = Tc_o/J = (450000 N·mm × 30 mm) / 751330.5 mm^4 = 17.97 MPa.
The minimum shear stress occurs at the inner radius: τ_min = Tc_i/J = (450000 N·mm × 24 mm) / 751330.5 mm^4 = 14.38 MPa.
