The beam is a rectangular solid with width b = 40 mm and height h = 100 mm.

The applied bending moment is M = 4000000 N·mm in the sagging direction.

The coordinate system is defined with the origin at the centroid of the cross-section, the y-axis vertical (positive upward), and the z-axis along the beam longitudinal axis.

The moment of inertia about the neutral axis (x-axis) is I = b*h^3/12 = 40*(100)^3/12 = 3,333,333.33 mm^4.

The distance from the neutral axis to the extreme fibers is c = h/2 = 50 mm.

The bending stress at an arbitrary fiber located at vertical coordinate y (where -50 mm ≤ y ≤ +50 mm) is given by σ = -M*y/I.

The negative sign indicates that positive y (above neutral axis) experiences compressive stress under sagging moment, while negative y (below neutral axis) experiences tensile stress.

The maximum bending stress occurs at the extreme fibers where y = ±c, with magnitude σ_max = M*c/I = 4000000*50/3333333.33 = 60 MPa.

The bending stress varies linearly through the depth of the beam, from -60 MPa at the top fiber to +60 MPa at the bottom fiber, with zero stress at the neutral axis (y = 0).
