Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

Assume the shaft is subjected to a torque and shear loading.

Determine the relevant response quantities for the case, focusing on: solid circular shaft: shear stress and angle of twist.

State any additional assumptions you need and present the governing relations clearly.

Additional assumptions: The shaft is prismatic with constant circular cross-section. The material is homogeneous and isotropic. The torque is applied about the longitudinal axis. Plane sections remain plane and perpendicular to the axis (no warping). The shaft is straight and of length L. The shear loading is distributed and does not cause bending.

Governing relations for torsion of a solid circular shaft:

The polar moment of inertia for a solid circular cross-section of radius R (or diameter d) is J = πR⁴/2 = πd⁴/32.

The shear stress at radial distance r from the center due to torque T is τ = Tr/J. The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer surface where r = R, giving τ_max = TR/J = 16T/(πd³).

The angle of twist φ over length L is φ = TL/(GJ), where G is the shear modulus of the material.

For combined loading with an applied shear force V (transverse shear), the shear stress distribution due to transverse shear in a circular cross-section is parabolic with maximum at the neutral axis: τ_V = 4V/(3A) * (1 - r²/R²) at radius r, where A = πR². The maximum transverse shear stress is 4V/(3A) at the center.

The total shear stress at any point is found by superposition of the torsional shear stress and the transverse shear stress, taking care to consider their directions.
