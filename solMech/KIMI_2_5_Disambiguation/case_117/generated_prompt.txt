The problem is to determine transverse shear stress in a rectangular beam under given internal shear force.

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

The beam has a rectangular cross-section with width b = 60 mm and height h = 80 mm.

The internal shear force at the section of interest is V = 16000 N.

The cross-sectional area is A = b * h = 60 mm * 80 mm = 4800 mm² = 4.8 × 10⁻³ m².

The moment of inertia about the neutral axis is I = b * h³ / 12 = 60 mm * (80 mm)³ / 12 = 2.56 × 10⁶ mm⁴ = 2.56 × 10⁻⁶ m⁴.

For part (a), calculate the maximum transverse shear stress at the neutral axis using τ_max = 1.5 * V / A.

Substitute values: τ_max = 1.5 * 16000 N / 4800 mm² = 5 MPa.

For part (b), calculate the shear stress at y = 26.667 mm from the neutral axis using τ = V * Q / (I * b).

First determine Q, the first moment of area of the portion of the cross-section above (or below) the point of interest.

At distance y from the neutral axis, the area above is A' = b * (h/2 - y) = 60 mm * (40 mm - 26.667 mm) = 60 mm * 13.333 mm = 800 mm².

The centroid of this area is at distance y' = y + (h/2 - y)/2 = 26.667 mm + 6.6665 mm = 33.3335 mm from the neutral axis, or more directly y' = (h/2 + y)/2 = (40 + 26.667)/2 = 33.3335 mm.

Alternatively, Q = A' * ȳ' where ȳ' is the distance from the neutral axis to the centroid of A'.

For a rectangle, Q at height y is Q = (b/2) * [(h/2)² - y²] = (60 mm/2) * [(40 mm)² - (26.667 mm)²] = 30 mm * [1600 - 711.11] mm² = 30 mm * 888.89 mm² = 26666.7 mm³ = 2.6667 × 10⁻⁵ m³.

Calculate shear stress: τ = 16000 N * 26666.7 mm³ / (2.56 × 10⁶ mm⁴ * 60 mm) = 4.2667 × 10⁸ N·mm³ / (1.536 × 10⁸ mm⁵) = 2.778 MPa.

Alternatively, using the parabolic distribution formula for rectangular sections: τ(y) = τ_max * [1 - (2y/h)²] = 5 MPa * [1 - (2*26.667/80)²] = 5 MPa * [1 - (0.6667)²] = 5 MPa * [1 - 0.4444] = 5 MPa * 0.5556 = 2.778 MPa.

The maximum transverse shear stress at the neutral axis is 5 MPa.

The transverse shear stress at y = 26.667 mm from the neutral axis is 2.78 MPa (or exactly 2.5 MPa if y = h/4 = 20 mm was intended, yielding τ = 3.75 MPa; or if y = 26.667 mm ≈ 2h/6, the result is 2.78 MPa).
