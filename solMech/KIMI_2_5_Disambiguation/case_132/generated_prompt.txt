The beam is simply supported with a span length of 4000 mm, which is a reasonable assumption for typical structural beam applications.

The maximum bending moment is M_max = 3000000 N·mm, assumed to occur at midspan where the bending moment is greatest for symmetric loading conditions.

The loading distribution is assumed to be a uniformly distributed load w = 375 N/mm, which produces the specified maximum moment at midspan of a simply supported beam according to M_max = wL²/8.

The allowable bending stress is σ_allow = 90 MPa.

The width of the rectangular beam cross-section is b = 40 mm.

The height h of the rectangular beam cross-section is to be determined.

The governing flexure formula for bending stress is σ = My/I, where M is the bending moment, y is the distance from the neutral axis, and I is the second moment of area.

For a rectangular cross-section, the second moment of area is I = bh³/12.

The maximum bending stress occurs at the outer fiber where y = h/2.

The maximum bending stress formula becomes σ_max = M_max(h/2)/(bh³/12) = 6M_max/(bh²).

Setting σ_max equal to σ_allow and solving for h gives h = √(6M_max/(b·σ_allow)).

Substituting the given values: h = √(6 × 3000000 N·mm / (40 mm × 90 N/mm²)) = √(18000000/3600) mm = √5000 mm = 70.71 mm.

The designed rectangular beam height is h = 71 mm (rounded up to the nearest practical millimeter).
