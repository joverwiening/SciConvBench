The problem involves a hollow circular shaft under torsion with the following parameters:
Inner radius ri = 22 mm.
Outer radius ro = 30 mm.
Applied torque T = 600000 N·mm.
Shaft length L = 1200 mm.
Shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.

Additional assumptions:
The shaft is prismatic with constant cross-section.
The material is homogeneous and isotropic.
Plane sections remain plane and perpendicular to the axis (no warping).
The torque is applied uniformly and acts about the longitudinal axis.
No axial or bending loads are present.

Governing relations and response quantities:

The polar moment of inertia for a hollow circular cross-section is J = (π/2) * (ro^4 - ri^4).
J = (π/2) * (30^4 - 22^4) mm^4 = (π/2) * (810000 - 234256) mm^4 = 904778.8 mm^4.

The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer radius and is given by τ_max = T * ro / J.
τ_max = 600000 N·mm * 30 mm / 904778.8 mm^4 = 19.89 MPa.

The minimum shear stress occurs at the inner radius and is given by τ_min = T * ri / J.
τ_min = 600000 N·mm * 22 mm / 904778.8 mm^4 = 14.59 MPa.

The angle of twist per unit length is dφ/dx = T / (G * J).
dφ/dx = 600000 N·mm / (77519.4 N/mm^2 * 904778.8 mm^4) = 8.556 × 10^-6 rad/mm.

The total angle of twist over length L is φ = T * L / (G * J).
φ = 600000 N·mm * 1200 mm / (77519.4 N/mm^2 * 904778.8 mm^4) = 0.01027 rad = 0.588 degrees.

The shear stress distribution varies linearly with radial position: τ(r) = T * r / J for ri ≤ r ≤ ro.
