The column is a solid circular section made of structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa and yield strength Sy = 250 MPa.

The column has diameter d = 25 mm and length L = 2000 mm.

The radius of gyration for a solid circular section is r = d/4 = 6.25 mm.

The slenderness ratio is L/r = 640 / 0.7 = 914.3 when accounting for effective length, or the effective slenderness ratio is KL/r = 0.7 × 2000 / 6.25 = 224.

The boundary condition is fixed-pinned with effective length factor K = 0.7.

The critical Euler buckling stress is calculated as sigma_cr = pi²E / (KL/r)² = pi² × 200000 / (224)² = 39.3 MPa.

The critical Euler buckling load is P_cr = sigma_cr × A = 39.3 × (pi/4 × 25²) = 39.3 × 490.9 = 19290 N = 19.3 kN.

The squash load (yielding load) is P_y = Sy × A = 250 × 490.9 = 122725 N = 122.7 kN.

The transition slenderness ratio separating elastic buckling from yielding is (KL/r)_transition = pi × sqrt(E/Sy) = pi × sqrt(200000/250) = 88.9.

Since the actual effective slenderness ratio KL/r = 224 exceeds the transition value of 88.9, Euler elastic buckling governs the failure mode rather than yielding.

The critical buckling stress of 39.3 MPa is well below the yield strength of 250 MPa, confirming elastic buckling occurs before any material yielding.

For design purposes, a safety factor should be applied to the critical buckling load of 19.3 kN to obtain an allowable load.

The governing failure mode is elastic Euler buckling with critical load P_cr = 19.3 kN and critical stress sigma_cr = 39.3 MPa.
