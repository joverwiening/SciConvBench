The cantilever beam has length L = 1500 mm with constant second moment of area I = 800000 mm⁴ along its length.

The material is AISI 1020 steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.

A downward point load P = 500 N is applied at the free end. No bending moment is applied at the free end.

The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

The strain energy U for a beam in bending is given by U = ∫₀ᴸ (M²/2EI) dx, where M is the internal bending moment.

For a cantilever beam with end load P, the bending moment at position x from the fixed end is M(x) = -P(L - x).

By Castigliano's second theorem, the deflection at the point of load application in the direction of the load is δ = ∂U/∂P.

Substituting M(x) = -P(L - x) into the strain energy expression gives U = ∫₀ᴸ [P²(L-x)²/2EI] dx = P²L³/6EI.

Taking the partial derivative with respect to P yields the end deflection: δ = PL³/3EI.

The deflection is downward with magnitude δ = (500 N)(1500 mm)³ / [3 × (200000 N/mm²) × (800000 mm⁴)] = 3.516 mm.
