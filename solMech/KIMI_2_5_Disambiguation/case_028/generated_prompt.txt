The problem involves determining the relevant response quantities for a dropped weight impact on an axially loaded rod. The following assumptions are made: small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics; the rod behaves as a spring with axial stiffness; the mass of the rod is negligible compared to the dropped mass (lumped mass approximation); no energy is lost to heat, sound, or plastic deformation (conservative system); the impact is perfectly inelastic (the weight sticks to the rod); and the rod is initially stress-free.

The given parameters are: weight W = 500 N, drop height h = 200 mm, rod length L = 1200 mm, diameter d = 30 mm, cross-sectional area A = 706.86 mm², elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa, and gravitational acceleration g = 9806.65 mm/s².

The mass of the dropped weight is computed as m = W/g = 500/9806.65 = 0.050985 kg (or equivalently 500 N·s²/mm using consistent units where 1 N = 1 kg·mm/s²).

The axial stiffness of the rod is k = EA/L = (200000 MPa)(706.86 mm²)/(1200 mm) = 117810 N/mm.

The static deflection due to the weight applied gradually is δ_st = W/k = 500/117810 = 0.004244 mm.

The impact factor for suddenly applied load is IF = 1 + sqrt(1 + 2h/δ_st) = 1 + sqrt(1 + 2(200)/0.004244) = 1 + sqrt(1 + 94213) = 1 + 307.0 = 308.0.

The maximum dynamic deflection is δ_max = IF × δ_st = 308.0 × 0.004244 mm = 1.307 mm.

Alternatively, using energy conservation: potential energy lost by weight = mgh + mgδ_max, and strain energy stored = (1/2)kδ_max². This yields the quadratic equation (1/2)kδ_max² - Wδ_max - Wh = 0, which solves to the same result.

The maximum dynamic force in the rod is F_max = k × δ_max = 117810 × 1.307 = 154000 N (or 154 kN), or equivalently F_max = IF × W = 308 × 500 = 154000 N.

The maximum dynamic stress is σ_max = F_max/A = 154000/706.86 = 217.9 MPa.

The maximum dynamic strain is ε_max = σ_max/E = 217.9/200000 = 0.001089 (or 0.1089%).

The static stress would be σ_st = W/A = 500/706.86 = 0.707 MPa, showing the impact amplifies stress by the impact factor of 308.

The relevant response quantities are: maximum dynamic deflection (1.307 mm), maximum dynamic force (154 kN), maximum dynamic stress (217.9 MPa), maximum dynamic strain (0.001089), and impact factor (308).
