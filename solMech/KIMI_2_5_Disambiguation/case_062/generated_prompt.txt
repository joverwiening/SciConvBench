The rod is made of AISI steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa and coefficient of thermal expansion α = 1×10⁻⁵ /°C.

The rod has length L = 1200 mm and circular cross-section with diameter d = 35 mm, giving cross-sectional area A = 962.11 mm².

The rod is subjected to a uniform temperature increase ΔT = 65 °C.

The rod has full axial restraint, meaning the total axial strain is constrained to zero (ε_total = 0).

There is no externally applied mechanical axial load; the only mechanical load is the reaction force developed due to the constraint.

The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics as specified.

The governing relation for thermal strain is ε_thermal = α × ΔT.

With full axial restraint, the total strain is the sum of thermal strain and mechanical strain: ε_total = ε_thermal + ε_mechanical = 0.

Therefore, the mechanical strain is ε_mechanical = −ε_thermal = −α × ΔT.

The mechanical stress is σ = E × ε_mechanical = −E × α × ΔT.

The reaction force is F = σ × A = −E × α × ΔT × A.

Numerical evaluation: ε_thermal = (1×10⁻⁵ /°C) × (65 °C) = 6.5×10⁻⁴ = 0.00065 (dimensionless).

ε_mechanical = −0.00065.

σ = −(200000 MPa) × (0.00065) = −130 MPa (compressive).

F = −(130 MPa) × (962.11 mm²) = −125074.3 N = −125.07 kN (compressive reaction force).
