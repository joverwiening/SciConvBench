The shaft is a solid circular shaft with length L = 800 mm and diameter d = 30 mm.

The shaft is subjected only to torque T = 500000 N·mm with no applied shear force.

The material is linear-elastic with shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa.

The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

The polar moment of inertia for the solid circular cross-section is J = πd⁴/32.

The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer radius and is given by τ_max = T·r/J, where r = d/2.

The angle of twist over the entire length is given by φ = T·L/(G·J).

The governing relations are: τ_max = 16T/(πd³) for maximum shear stress, and φ = 32T·L/(πG·d⁴) for angle of twist.

All quantities use consistent units: N and mm for force and length, MPa for stress and modulus, radians for angle of twist.
