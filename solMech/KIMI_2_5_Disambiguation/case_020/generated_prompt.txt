The shaft is a solid circular cylinder with diameter d = 35 mm and total length L = 1200 mm.

The material is linear-elastic with shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.

The shaft is fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.

Two torques are applied: T1 = 200000 N·mm at x = L = 1200 mm, and T2 = -250000 N·mm at x = 800 mm.

Positive torque follows the right-hand rule: counterclockwise when viewed from the positive x-axis.

The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

The polar moment of inertia for the solid circular cross-section is J = πd^4/32.

The torsional constant for a solid circular shaft equals the polar moment of inertia, so J = π(35 mm)^4/32 = 147323.5 mm^4.

The internal torque varies along the shaft length and must be determined by equilibrium in each segment.

Segment 1 spans from x = 0 to x = 800 mm with internal torque T_int,1 = T1 + T2 = 200000 + (-250000) = -50000 N·mm.

Segment 2 spans from x = 800 mm to x = 1200 mm with internal torque T_int,2 = T1 = 200000 N·mm.

The maximum shear stress in each segment occurs at the outer radius r = d/2 = 17.5 mm and is given by τ_max = T_int * r / J.

The shear stress in Segment 1 is τ_max,1 = (-50000 N·mm)(17.5 mm)/(147323.5 mm^4) = -5.94 MPa.

The shear stress in Segment 2 is τ_max,2 = (200000 N·mm)(17.5 mm)/(147323.5 mm^4) = 23.76 MPa.

The angle of twist per unit length is dφ/dx = T_int/(G*J).

The total angle of twist at any location is found by integration: φ(x) = integral from 0 to x of T_int(ξ)/(G*J) dξ.

The angle of twist at x = 800 mm is φ(800) = T_int,1 * 800 / (G*J) = (-50000)(800)/(77519.4 * 147323.5) = -0.00350 rad = -0.201 degrees.

The angle of twist at x = 1200 mm is φ(1200) = φ(800) + T_int,2 * 400 / (G*J) = -0.00350 + (200000)(400)/(77519.4 * 147323.5) = -0.00350 + 0.00701 = 0.00351 rad = 0.201 degrees.

The maximum absolute shear stress is 23.76 MPa occurring in Segment 2.

The maximum angle of twist magnitude is 0.201 degrees occurring at the free end x = L.
