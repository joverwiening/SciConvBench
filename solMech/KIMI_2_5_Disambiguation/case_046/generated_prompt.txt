The rod is straight, prismatic, and circular with a diameter of 25 mm.
The rod has a length of 800 mm.
The rod is subjected to an axial tensile load of 20000 N.
The material is linear-elastic with Young's modulus of 200000 MPa.
The material has a yield strength of 250 MPa.
Small-deformation theory applies.
The load is applied concentrically along the rod axis.
The material is homogeneous and isotropic.
The cross-section remains plane and perpendicular to the axis.
Temperature effects are neglected.
Self-weight of the rod is neglected.

The cross-sectional area A is calculated as π × (d/2)² = π × (12.5 mm)² = 490.87 mm².

The average normal stress σ is P/A = 20000 N / 490.87 mm² = 40.74 MPa.

The average normal strain ε is σ/E = 40.74 MPa / 200000 MPa = 0.0002037, or 2.037 × 10⁻⁴.

The elongation δ is ε × L = 0.0002037 × 800 mm = 0.163 mm.

The factor of safety with respect to yielding is σ_y/σ = 250 MPa / 40.74 MPa = 6.14.
