Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics with isotropic, homogeneous material behavior.

The shaft consists of two solid circular segments in series: Segment AB has length L1 = 300 mm and diameter d1 = 20 mm; Segment BC has length L2 = 500 mm and diameter d2 = 30 mm.

The material is linear elastic with shear modulus G (to be specified or assumed if numerical values are required).

End A is fixed. A torque T = 300000 N·mm is applied at end C about the shaft axis. This torque produces a constant internal torque T throughout both segments.

The polar moment of inertia for a solid circular cross-section is J = πd^4/32.

For Segment AB: J1 = π(20 mm)^4/32 = 15707.96 mm^4.

For Segment BC: J2 = π(30 mm)^4/32 = 79521.56 mm^4.

The maximum shear stress in each segment occurs at the outer radius and is given by τ_max = T*r/J = 16T/(πd^3).

For Segment AB: τ_max,1 = (16 * 300000 N·mm)/(π * (20 mm)^3) = 190.99 MPa.

For Segment BC: τ_max,2 = (16 * 300000 N·mm)/(π * (30 mm)^3) = 56.59 MPa.

The angle of twist for each segment is φ = TL/(GJ).

For Segment AB: φ1 = (300000 N·mm * 300 mm)/(G * 15707.96 mm^4) = (5.729×10^6 mm^3)/G.

For Segment BC: φ2 = (300000 N·mm * 500 mm)/(G * 79521.56 mm^4) = (1.886×10^6 mm^3)/G.

The total twist at end C relative to fixed end A is φ_total = φ1 + φ2 = (7.615×10^6 mm^3)/G.

If steel is assumed with G = 79 GPa = 79000 N/mm^2, then φ1 = 0.0725 rad = 4.15 degrees, φ2 = 0.0239 rad = 1.37 degrees, and φ_total = 0.0964 rad = 5.52 degrees.
