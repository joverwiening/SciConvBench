The analysis shall determine engineering stress, engineering strain, and Young's modulus for a circular cylindrical tension-test specimen under axial loading.

The specimen has circular cylindrical cross-section with initial diameter d0 = 12.5 mm and initial gauge length L0 = 50 mm.

The applied axial load is P = 12000 N.

The measured elongation under this load is ΔL = 0.12 mm.

The material behavior is assumed to be linear-elastic with small deformations.

The initial cross-sectional area is A0 = π(d0/2)^2 = π(6.25 mm)^2 = 122.72 mm^2.

Engineering stress is defined as σ = P/A0.

Engineering strain is defined as ε = ΔL/L0.

Young's modulus is computed as E = σ/ε = (P/A0)/(ΔL/L0) = (P·L0)/(A0·ΔL).

The governing relations are:
σ = P/A0
ε = ΔL/L0
E = σ/ε

Numerical results:
A0 = 122.72 mm^2
σ = 12000 N / 122.72 mm^2 = 97.79 MPa
ε = 0.12 mm / 50 mm = 0.0024 (dimensionless, or 0.24%)
E = 97.79 MPa / 0.0024 = 40,746 MPa = 40.75 GPa
