The structural member is a rectangular adhesive layer with area A = 4000 mm² and thickness t = 20 mm.

The applied shear load is a uniform in-plane shear force V = 8000 N distributed over the area A = 4000 mm², resulting in an average shear stress τ_avg = V/A.

The shear modulus is G = 39179.1 MPa.

Assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

Assume the adhesive layer behaves as a homogeneous, isotropic, linear-elastic material.

Assume the shear stress distribution is uniform throughout the adhesive layer due to the uniform applied load and simple geometry.

Assume plane stress conditions apply for the thin adhesive layer.

Assume no normal stresses are present; the layer is in pure shear.

Assume the adhesive is perfectly bonded to the substrates on both faces, so displacement constraints are applied uniformly.

The average shear stress is calculated as τ_avg = V/A = 8000 N / 4000 mm² = 2 MPa.

The shear strain is calculated using Hooke's law for shear: γ = τ_avg / G = 2 MPa / 39179.1 MPa = 5.105 × 10⁻⁵ (dimensionless, or equivalently 51.05 microstrain).

The shear deformation (relative displacement) across the thickness is δ = γ × t = (5.105 × 10⁻⁵) × 20 mm = 1.021 × 10⁻³ mm = 1.021 μm.

The governing relation for shear stress is τ = V/A for uniform distribution.

The governing constitutive relation is τ = Gγ.

The kinematic relation for shear deformation is δ = γt.
