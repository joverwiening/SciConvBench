The simply supported beam has span length L = 3500 mm.

The beam is subjected to a single uniformly distributed transverse load w = 0.5 N/mm over the full span.

The material is linear-elastic with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa (200 GPa).

The second moment of area is I = 1500000 mm^4.

The flexural rigidity is EI = 200000 N/mm² × 1500000 mm^4 = 3 × 10^11 N·mm².

The governing differential equation for beam bending is EI(d⁴v/dx⁴) = w(x), where v is the transverse deflection and x is the coordinate along the beam (0 ≤ x ≤ L).

For a simply supported beam with uniform load w, the boundary conditions are v(0) = 0, v(L) = 0, M(0) = 0, and M(L) = 0, where M = -EI(d²v/dx²) is the bending moment.

The deflection equation is v(x) = (wx/24EI)(L³ - 2Lx² + x³).

The maximum deflection occurs at midspan (x = L/2) and equals v_max = 5wL⁴/(384EI).

The slope equation is θ(x) = dv/dx = (w/24EI)(L³ - 6Lx² + 4x³).

The end slopes are θ(0) = wL³/(24EI) and θ(L) = -wL³/(24EI).

Numerical values: v_max = 5 × 0.5 × (3500)⁴ / (384 × 3 × 10^11) = 9.16 mm (downward).

θ_max = 0.5 × (3500)³ / (24 × 3 × 10^11) = 0.00298 rad = 0.171°.
