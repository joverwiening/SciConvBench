The shaft is a solid circular shaft with length L = 1000 mm and diameter d = 30 mm.

The shaft is subjected only to a torque T = 250000 N·mm with no applied shear force.

The material is linear elastic with shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa.

The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.

The polar moment of inertia for a solid circular cross-section is J = πd⁴/32.

Calculate J = π × (30 mm)⁴ / 32 = 79521.6 mm⁴.

The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer radius r = d/2 = 15 mm.

The shear stress formula is τ_max = T × r / J.

Calculate τ_max = (250000 N·mm × 15 mm) / 79521.6 mm⁴ = 47.2 MPa.

The angle of twist formula is φ = T × L / (G × J).

Calculate φ = (250000 N·mm × 1000 mm) / (76923.1 N/mm² × 79521.6 mm⁴) = 0.0409 rad.

Convert φ to degrees: φ = 0.0409 rad × (180/π) = 2.34 degrees.

The governing relations are:
τ = T × r / J for shear stress at radius r.
τ_max = T × r_max / J for maximum shear stress at outer surface.
φ = T × L / (G × J) for total angle of twist over length L.

The relevant response quantities are:
Maximum shear stress τ_max = 47.2 MPa.
Total angle of twist φ = 0.0409 rad (or 2.34 degrees).
