The cantilever beam has length L = 1800 mm with flexural rigidity EI = 2.4 × 10^11 N·mm².

A downward point load P = 2000 N is applied at the free end.

No moment is applied at the free end.

The beam is prismatic and homogeneous.

Small deformations are assumed, so linear Euler-Bernoulli beam theory applies.

Shear deformation is neglected.

The governing differential equation is EI d⁴w/dx⁴ = 0 for 0 < x < L, where w(x) is the transverse deflection and x is measured from the fixed end.

Boundary conditions at the fixed end (x = 0) are w(0) = 0 and dw/dx(0) = 0.

Boundary conditions at the free end (x = L) are EI d²w/dx²(L) = 0 (zero moment) and EI d³w/dx³(L) = -P (shear equals applied load, with sign convention).

The deflection curve is w(x) = (P/(6EI))(3Lx² - x³).

The maximum deflection occurs at the free end: w_max = PL³/(3EI) = 8.1 mm downward.

The slope at the free end is θ(L) = PL²/(2EI) = 0.00675 rad.

The bending moment distribution is M(x) = -P(L - x), with maximum magnitude PL = 3.6 × 10^6 N·mm at the fixed end.

The shear force is constant at V(x) = -P = -2000 N throughout the beam.
