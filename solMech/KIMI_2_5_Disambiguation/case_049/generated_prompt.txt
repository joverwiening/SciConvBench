The shaft is a hollow circular shaft with outer diameter do = 50 mm, inner diameter di = 42 mm, and length L = 1200 mm.

The shaft is subjected to a constant torque T = 450000 N·mm with no transverse shear force.

The material behavior is assumed to be linear-elastic, isotropic, and homogeneous with small deformations.

The analysis assumes Saint-Venant torsion theory applies, with no warping restraint and no stress concentrations at the ends.

The polar moment of inertia for the hollow circular cross-section is J = (π/32) * (do^4 - di^4) = (π/32) * (50^4 - 42^4) mm^4 = 306796.16 mm^4.

The maximum shear stress occurs at the outer radius and is calculated as τ_max = T * (do/2) / J = 450000 N·mm * 25 mm / 306796.16 mm^4 = 36.67 MPa.

The minimum shear stress occurs at the inner radius and is calculated as τ_min = T * (di/2) / J = 450000 N·mm * 21 mm / 306796.16 mm^4 = 30.80 MPa.

The angle of twist over the full length is φ = T * L / (G * J), where G is the shear modulus of the material.

The rate of twist is dφ/dx = T / (G * J).

The shear strain varies linearly with radius as γ = r * dφ/dx = τ / G.

The torsional stiffness of the shaft is k_t = G * J / L.
