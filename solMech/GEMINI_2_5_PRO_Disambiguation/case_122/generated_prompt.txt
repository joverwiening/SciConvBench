The analysis is for a short, rectangular column under eccentric compressive loading.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The column cross-section has a width of 100 mm.
The column cross-section has a depth of 100 mm.
A single compressive load of 90000 N is applied to the cross-section.
The load is applied with an eccentricity of 15 mm along the axis parallel to the width.
The load is applied with an eccentricity of 0 mm along the axis parallel to the depth.
The governing equation for the combined normal stress distribution shall be presented.
The maximum compressive stress on the cross-section shall be calculated and reported.
The minimum normal stress on the cross-section shall be calculated and reported.
The location of the neutral axis shall be determined and reported.
