The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The analysis assumes the beam material is homogeneous and isotropic.
The analysis assumes the beam is initially straight and has a uniform rectangular cross-section.
The objective is to determine the minimum required height for a rectangular beam.
The maximum bending moment is 3,000,000 N·mm.
The allowable bending stress for the material is 90 MPa.
The width of the rectangular beam is 40 mm.
The governing relation for maximum bending stress is σ_max = M_max * c / I.
For a rectangular cross-section of width b and height h, c = h/2 and I = (b*h^3)/12.
The design requires that the calculated maximum stress does not exceed the allowable stress.
The minimum required beam height is calculated as h >= sqrt(6 * M_max / (b * σ_allow)).
The minimum required height for the beam is 70.71 mm.
