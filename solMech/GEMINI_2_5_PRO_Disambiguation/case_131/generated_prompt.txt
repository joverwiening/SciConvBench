The analysis assumes a state of plane stress.
The material is assumed to be steel.
The material's Young's Modulus is 200 GPa.
The material's Poisson's Ratio is 0.3.
The material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The applied average normal stress component in the x-direction is 8.33 MPa.
The applied average normal stress component in the y-direction is 0 MPa.
The applied average shear stress component is 1.67 MPa.
Calculate the principal stresses σ_1 and σ_2.
The governing relation for principal stresses is σ_1,2 = (σ_x + σ_y)/2 ± sqrt( ((σ_x - σ_y)/2)^2 + τ_xy^2 ).
Calculate the principal strains ε_1 and ε_2.
First, determine the strain components using the plane stress constitutive relations: ε_x = (1/E) * (σ_x - ν*σ_y), ε_y = (1/E) * (σ_y - ν*σ_x), and γ_xy = τ_xy/G.
The shear modulus G is calculated as G = E / (2*(1+ν)).
The governing relation for principal strains is ε_1,2 = (ε_x + ε_y)/2 ± sqrt( ((ε_x - ε_y)/2)^2 + (γ_xy/2)^2 ).
