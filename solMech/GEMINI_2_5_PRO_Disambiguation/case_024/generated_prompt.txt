The analysis will be performed on a cantilever beam.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam has a length of 1500 mm.
The beam has a rectangular cross-section.
The height of the cross-section is 100 mm.
The width of the cross-section is 50 mm.
The beam material has a Young's Modulus of 200000 MPa.
A uniformly distributed transverse load of 1.5 N/mm acts downward along the entire length of the beam.
The primary objective is to determine the maximum deflection of the beam.
The governing differential equation for the beam's deflection curve is EI * d^4v/dx^4 = -w(x).
The maximum deflection at the free end is calculated using the formula v_max = (w * L^4) / (8 * E * I).
