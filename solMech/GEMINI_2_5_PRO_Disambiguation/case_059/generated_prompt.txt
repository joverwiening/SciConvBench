The analysis is for Euler buckling of a column under compressive loading.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The column has a length of 2500 mm.
The column has a solid circular cross-section with a diameter of 50 mm.
The column is made of structural steel with a Young's Modulus of 200 GPa.
The column is made of structural steel with a yield strength of 250 MPa.
The column has pinned-pinned end conditions, corresponding to an effective length factor K of 1.0.
The governing relation for the Euler critical buckling load is P_cr = (pi^2 * E * I) / (K * L)^2.
The calculated Euler critical buckling load is 97.3 kN.
A buckling instability check is performed by comparing the critical stress to the material yield strength.
The critical stress is calculated as sigma_cr = P_cr / A.
The calculated critical stress is 49.5 MPa.
The critical stress of 49.5 MPa is less than the yield strength of 250 MPa, so the Euler buckling analysis is valid.
