The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The shaft material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic with a shear modulus G.
The shaft is a solid circular cylinder with a constant diameter of 30 mm.
The total length of the shaft is 1200 mm.
The shaft is fixed at the coordinate x = 0.
A torque of -200000 N·mm is applied at the coordinate x = 400 mm.
A torque of +200000 N·mm is applied at the free end, x = 1200 mm.
No other external loads are applied to the shaft.
The analysis must determine the internal torque as a function of position x.
The analysis must determine the angle of twist as a function of position x.
The analysis must determine the maximum shear stress and its location.
The governing relation for shear stress is τ = Tρ/J.
The governing relation for the polar moment of inertia is J = (π/2) * r^4.
The governing relation for the rate of twist is dφ/dx = T(x) / (G*J).
