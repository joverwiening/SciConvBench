The analysis objective is to determine the minimum required diameter for a solid circular shaft based on a torsional stiffness requirement.
The secondary objective is to determine the relevant stress response quantities.
The analysis will assume small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The shaft has a length of 800 mm.
The shaft is made of AISI 1020 steel with a shear modulus of 76,923.1 MPa.
The shaft is subjected to a static torque of 600,000 N·mm.
The shaft is also subjected to a transverse shear load, which is assumed to be 15,000 N for this analysis.
The maximum allowable angle of twist over the shaft's length is 1.5 degrees.
The governing relation for the angle of twist (φ) is φ = (T * L) / (G * J).
The polar moment of inertia (J) for the solid circular cross-section is J = (π * d^4) / 32, where d is the diameter.
The minimum required shaft diameter shall be calculated by solving the angle of twist relation for d, using the maximum allowable twist angle.
The maximum torsional shear stress shall be calculated using the relation τ_torsion = (T * d) / (2 * J).
The maximum transverse shear stress shall be calculated using the relation τ_transverse = (4 * V) / (3 * A), where A is the cross-sectional area (π * d^2) / 4.
The final deliverables are the calculated minimum shaft diameter, the maximum torsional shear stress, and the maximum transverse shear stress.
