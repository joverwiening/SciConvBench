The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The shaft is a solid circular prism with a constant cross-section.
The shaft has a diameter of 50 mm.
The shaft has a length of 600 mm.
The shaft material has a shear modulus of 77519.4 MPa.
The shaft is subjected to a static torque of 500,000 N·mm.
The analysis assumes the shaft is fixed at one end and the torque is applied to the free end.
The analysis will neglect stress concentrations.
The governing relation for the polar moment of inertia (J) is J = (π/2) * r^4, where r is the radius.
The governing relation for the maximum shear stress (τ_max) is τ_max = (T * r) / J, where T is the torque.
The governing relation for the angle of twist (φ) is φ = (T * L) / (G * J), where L is the length and G is the shear modulus.
