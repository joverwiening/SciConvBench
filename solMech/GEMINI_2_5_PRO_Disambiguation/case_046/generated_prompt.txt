The task is to analyze a straight prismatic circular rod under axial tension.
The analysis will determine the average normal stress, axial strain, total elongation, and the factor of safety against yielding.
The axial tensile load is 20000 N.
The rod has a circular cross-section with a diameter of 25 mm.
The initial length of the rod is 800 mm.
The material of the rod has a Modulus of Elasticity of 200000 MPa.
The material of the rod has a yield strength of 250 MPa.
The analysis assumes the material is homogeneous and isotropic.
The analysis assumes linear-elastic material behavior and small deformations.
The cross-sectional area is calculated using A = pi * (d/2)^2.
The average normal stress is calculated using sigma = P / A.
The axial strain is calculated using epsilon = sigma / E.
The total elongation is calculated using delta = epsilon * L.
The factor of safety against yielding is calculated using FS = Sy / sigma.
The final report will state the calculated values for average normal stress, axial strain, total elongation, and the factor of safety.
