The analysis objective is to determine the response of a prismatic AISI rod subjected to a uniform temperature increase while being fully restrained axially.
The material is assumed to be linear-elastic, homogeneous, and isotropic.
The temperature of the rod increases by 50 °C.
The coefficient of thermal expansion is 2e-05 1/°C.
The Young's Modulus is 200,000 MPa.
The full axial restraint boundary condition implies the total axial strain is zero.
The governing relation for total strain is ε_total = ε_thermal + ε_mechanical.
The thermal strain is calculated as ε_thermal = α * ΔT.
The mechanical strain is related to stress by Hooke's Law, ε_mechanical = σ / E.
The resulting thermal strain is 0.001.
Since total strain is zero, the mechanical strain must be -0.001.
The axial stress required to produce this mechanical strain is σ = E * ε_mechanical.
The resulting axial stress is -200 MPa, where the negative sign indicates compression.
