The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The shaft material is AISI 1020 steel with a shear modulus of 76923.1 MPa.
The yield strength of the AISI 1020 steel is assumed to be 350 MPa.
The shear yield strength is assumed to be 175 MPa, based on the Tresca failure criterion.
A factor of safety of 2.0 is used for the design.
The allowable shear stress is 87.5 MPa.
The solid circular shaft has a diameter of 20 mm.
The shaft rotates at a speed of 1200 rpm.
The motor power is 5 kW.
Transverse shear loading is considered negligible for this analysis.
Calculate the torque on the shaft from the motor power and rotational speed.
The governing relation for torque is T = P / ω, where P is power and ω is angular velocity in rad/s.
Calculate the maximum torsional shear stress in the shaft.
The governing relation for maximum torsional shear stress is τ_max = (T * r) / J.
In this relation, T is torque, r is the shaft radius, and J is the polar moment of inertia.
The polar moment of inertia for a solid circular shaft is J = (π/2) * r^4.
Compare the calculated maximum torsional shear stress to the allowable shear stress to check the design.
The response quantities to be determined are the torque, the maximum torsional shear stress, and the resulting factor of safety.
