Analyze a two-segment stepped circular bar under an axial tensile load.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The first segment has a length of 500 mm and a diameter of 30 mm.
The second segment has a length of 600 mm and a diameter of 25 mm.
Both segments are made of the same material with a Young's Modulus of 69000 MPa.
A tensile load of 20000 N is applied axially to the bar.
Calculate the normal stress in the first segment.
Calculate the normal stress in the second segment.
Calculate the total elongation of the entire bar.
The governing relation for cross-sectional area is A = (π * d^2) / 4.
The governing relation for normal stress is σ = P / A.
The governing relation for elongation of a single segment is δ = (P * L) / (A * E).
The total elongation is the sum of the elongations of the two segments.
