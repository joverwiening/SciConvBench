The analysis will be performed on a solid circular shaft.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The shaft material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
Stress concentrations are neglected.
The shaft has a radius of 22.5 mm.
The shaft has a length of 1000 mm.
The shaft is subjected to a torque of 400,000 N·mm.
The shaft material has a shear modulus of 76923.1 MPa.
The polar moment of inertia is calculated using the formula J = (π/2) * r^4.
The maximum shear stress is calculated at the outer surface using the formula τ_max = (T * r) / J.
The angle of twist is calculated using the formula φ = (T * L) / (G * J).
The calculated maximum shear stress is 22.35 MPa.
The calculated total angle of twist is 0.0129 radians.
