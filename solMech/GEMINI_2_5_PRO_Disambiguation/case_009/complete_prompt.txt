Use N–mm–MPa units. Assume the two bars are perfectly bonded to rigid plates so that they undergo the same elongation (compatibility).

Two prismatic bars of equal length L = 400 mm are mounted in parallel between two rigid plates. Bar 1 is AISI 1020 steel with area A1 = 300 mm^2 and modulus E1 = 200000 MPa. Bar 2 is cast iron with area A2 = 250 mm^2 and modulus E2 = 110000 MPa. A total tensile load P = 20000 N is applied to the top plate and is shared by the two bars.

Determine the force carried by each bar, the stress in each bar, and the common elongation of the bars.
