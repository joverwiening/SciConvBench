The system consists of two rigid plates connected by two parallel bars.
The first bar is made of AISI 1020 steel.
The length of the steel bar is 400 mm.
The cross-sectional area of the steel bar is 300 mm^2.
The Young's Modulus for the steel bar is 200,000 MPa.
The second bar is made of cast iron.
The length of the cast iron bar is 400 mm.
The cross-sectional area of the cast iron bar is 250 mm^2.
The Young's Modulus for the cast iron bar is 110,000 MPa.
A total tensile load of 20,000 N is applied to the top plate, parallel to the axis of the bars.
The analysis will assume small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior for both materials.
The bars are assumed to be perfectly bonded to the rigid plates, resulting in equal elongation for both bars.
Determine the tensile force carried by the steel bar.
Determine the tensile force carried by the cast iron bar.
Determine the normal stress in the steel bar.
Determine the normal stress in the cast iron bar.
Determine the total elongation of the composite bar system.
