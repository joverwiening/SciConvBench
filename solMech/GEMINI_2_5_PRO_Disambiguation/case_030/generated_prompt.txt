The analysis will be performed on a solid rod with a circular cross-section.
The material is assumed to be isotropic, linear-elastic, and undergoing small deformations.
The initial length of the rod is 1200 mm.
The initial diameter of the rod is 25 mm.
The Young's Modulus of the material is 200000 MPa.
The Poisson's ratio of the material is 0.29.
A single uniaxial tensile load of 45000 N is applied along the rod's axis.
No other loads are considered in the analysis.
The analysis must determine the axial stress.
The analysis must determine the axial strain.
The analysis must determine the lateral strain.
The analysis must determine the final length of the rod.
The analysis must determine the final diameter of the rod.
The governing relations for stress, strain, and the Poisson effect must be stated.
