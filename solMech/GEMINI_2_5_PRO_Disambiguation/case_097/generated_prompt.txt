The analysis will be performed on a solid circular shaft with two segments.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.
The shaft material is homogeneous and isotropic with a shear modulus of 25939.8 MPa.
The first segment has a diameter of 25 mm and a length of 400 mm.
The second segment has a diameter of 30 mm and a length of 900 mm.
The shaft is fixed at one end of the first segment, designated as end A.
The segments are connected in series, with the second segment extending from the end of the first.
A torque of 750000 N·mm is applied at the free end of the second segment, designated as end C.
The analysis will neglect stress concentrations at the step between the two segments.
The analysis will determine the maximum shear stress in the first segment.
The analysis will determine the maximum shear stress in the second segment.
The analysis will determine the total angle of twist at end C relative to the fixed end A.
The governing relations for shear stress (τ = Tr/J) and angle of twist (φ = TL/GJ) will be used.
