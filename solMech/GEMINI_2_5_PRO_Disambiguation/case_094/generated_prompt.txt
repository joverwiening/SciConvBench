The analysis will be conducted on an Euler-Bernoulli beam.
The analysis assumes small-deformation and linear-elastic material behavior.
The beam is simply supported at both ends.
The length of the beam is 3500 mm.
The Young's Modulus of the beam material is 200000 N/mm^2.
The second moment of area of the beam's cross-section is 3000000 mm^4.
A uniformly distributed transverse load of 0.3 N/mm is applied along the entire length of the beam.
A concentrated point load of 1200 N is applied at the midspan of the beam.
The principle of superposition shall be used to determine the total response.
Determine the equation for the deflection of the beam as a function of position.
Determine the value and location of the maximum deflection.
Determine the equation for the bending moment as a function of position.
Determine the equation for the shear force as a function of position.
