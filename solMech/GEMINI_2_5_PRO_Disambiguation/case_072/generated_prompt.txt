Analyze a solid circular shaft subjected to a torsional load.
The objective is to compute the angle of twist at the free end using Castigliano's theorem.
The shaft has a length of 1000 mm.
The shaft has a diameter of 25 mm.
The shaft material has a shear modulus of 76923.1 MPa.
A torque of 350000 N·mm is applied to the free end of the shaft.
The shaft is fixed at one end and free at the other.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.
The strain energy (U) due to torsion is given by U = ∫(T(x)² / (2 * G * J)) dx over the length of the shaft.
The polar moment of inertia (J) for a solid circular shaft is π * d⁴ / 32.
The angle of twist (θ) is the partial derivative of the total strain energy with respect to the applied torque (θ = ∂U/∂T).
Calculate the numerical value for the angle of twist in radians.
