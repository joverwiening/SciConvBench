The analysis will be performed for a linear-elastic, small-deformation case.
The beam material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The shear stress is assumed to be uniform across the beam's width.
The transverse shear force, V, is 25000 N.
The beam has a rectangular cross-section with a width, b, of 40 mm.
The beam has a rectangular cross-section with a height, h, of 80 mm.
The governing relation for transverse shear stress is τ = VQ / (Ib).
The second moment of area, I, for the rectangular cross-section shall be calculated.
The first moment of area, Q, shall be determined as a function of the vertical distance from the neutral axis.
The shear stress distribution across the cross-section height shall be presented as a function of vertical position.
The maximum transverse shear stress shall be calculated.
The location of the maximum transverse shear stress on the cross-section shall be identified.
