The analysis assumes a simply supported beam with a length of 1000 mm.
The beam has a rectangular cross-section with a width of 50 mm and a height of 120 mm.
The material is assumed to be linear-elastic, homogeneous, and isotropic.
The analysis is based on Euler-Bernoulli beam theory, where plane sections remain plane.
A coordinate system is established with its origin at the centroid of the cross-section.
The y-axis is positive upwards, and the z-axis is along the beam's length.
The bending moment is applied about the horizontal principal axis (x-axis).
The internal sagging bending moment at the section of interest is 4,000,000 N·mm.
The area moment of inertia about the bending axis is 7,200,000 mm^4.
The governing relation for bending stress (σ) at a distance 'y' from the neutral axis is σ = -My/I.
The distance 'y' ranges from -60 mm at the bottom fiber to +60 mm at the top fiber.
The maximum compressive stress at the top fiber (y = +60 mm) is -33.33 MPa.
The maximum tensile stress at the bottom fiber (y = -60 mm) is +33.33 MPa.
