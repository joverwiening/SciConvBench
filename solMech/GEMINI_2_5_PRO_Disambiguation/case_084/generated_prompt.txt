The analysis will determine the shear flow and required fastener spacing for a built-up beam.
The analysis is based on small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam cross-section is assumed to be a T-shape, consisting of one flange plate attached to the top of one web.
The flange has a symbolic width `b_f` and a symbolic thickness `t_f`.
The web has a symbolic height `h_w` and a symbolic thickness `t_w`.
The beam is subjected to an internal vertical shear force `V` of 25000 N.
The fasteners connecting the flange to the web each have an allowable shear capacity `F_allowable` of 1800 N.
The location of the neutral axis of the T-section must be calculated.
The second moment of area `I` of the entire cross-section about the neutral axis must be calculated.
The first moment of area `Q` of the flange about the neutral axis must be calculated.
The shear flow `q` at the flange-web interface is calculated using the formula `q = VQ/I`.
The maximum allowable fastener spacing `s` is calculated using the formula `s = F_allowable / q`.
The final result will be a symbolic expression for the fastener spacing `s` in terms of the geometric parameters.
