Analyze a solid circular shaft under torsional loading using linear-elastic solid mechanics theory.
The shaft consists of two co-linear sections, AB and BC.
Section AB has a length of 400 mm and a diameter of 30 mm.
Section BC has a length of 700 mm and a diameter of 30 mm.
The total length of the uniform diameter shaft is 1100 mm.
The material is assumed to be homogeneous, isotropic, and linear-elastic with a shear modulus G.
End A of the shaft is fixed, preventing all rotation and translation.
A concentrated torque of 600000 N·mm is applied at the free end, C.
The direction of the torque is along the longitudinal axis of the shaft.
No external transverse shear forces are applied; the "shear loading" refers to the internal shear stress resulting from the applied torque.
Determine the maximum shear stress within the shaft.
Determine the total angle of twist at the free end C relative to the fixed end A.
The governing relation for shear stress (τ) is τ = (T * r) / J.
The governing relation for the angle of twist (φ) is φ = (T * L) / (G * J).
The polar moment of inertia (J) for a solid circular shaft is J = (π * D^4) / 32.
