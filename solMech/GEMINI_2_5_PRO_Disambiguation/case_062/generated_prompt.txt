Analyze a prismatic rod made of AISI 1020 steel.
The analysis will be based on small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The Modulus of Elasticity for the steel is 200000 MPa.
The coefficient of thermal expansion for the steel is 1e-05 1/°C.
The rod is subjected to a uniform temperature increase of 65 °C.
No external axial loads are applied to the rod.
The rod is fully restrained against any axial deformation.
The material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The material properties are assumed to be constant over the specified temperature range.
The temperature change is assumed to be uniform throughout the entire rod.
The analysis will determine the 1D axial response quantities.
The governing relation for total strain is the sum of mechanical strain and thermal strain.
The governing relation for mechanical strain is Stress divided by the Modulus of Elasticity.
The governing relation for thermal strain is the coefficient of thermal expansion multiplied by the temperature change.
The final output will report the values for axial thermal strain, axial mechanical strain, total axial strain, and axial stress.
