The analysis assumes a small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics framework.
The structural member is a rectangular adhesive layer.
The adhesive layer is bonded between two rigid plates, with one plate fixed and the other subjected to the load.
The thickness of the adhesive layer is 20 mm.
The shear modulus of the adhesive material is 39179.1 MPa.
A shear force of 12000 N is applied parallel to the layer.
The area subjected to the shear force is 4000 mm^2.
The governing relation for average shear stress is the shear force divided by the shear area (τ = V/A).
The governing relation for shear strain is the shear stress divided by the shear modulus (γ = τ/G).
The governing relation for shear deformation is the thickness multiplied by the shear strain (δ_s = h * γ).
