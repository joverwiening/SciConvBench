The analysis objective is to determine the midspan deflection of a simply supported beam using Castigliano's theorem.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.
The beam has a length of 2500 mm.
The beam is simply supported at both ends.
A point load of 1000 N is applied at the center of the beam span.
The Young's Modulus of the beam material is 200000 MPa.
The moment of inertia of the beam cross-section is 3000000 mm^4.
The strain energy due to bending shall be calculated.
The midspan deflection is the partial derivative of the strain energy with respect to the central point load.
The final result will be the numerical value for the midspan deflection.
