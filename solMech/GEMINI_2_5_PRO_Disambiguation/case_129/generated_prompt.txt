The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The task is to determine the curvature and radius of curvature for a beam subjected to a bending moment.
The Young's Modulus (E) of the beam material is 200000 MPa.
The second moment of area (I) of the beam's cross-section is 1200000 mm^4.
The applied bending moment (M) is 3000000 N·mm.
The governing relation for curvature (κ) is κ = M / (E * I).
The governing relation for the radius of curvature (ρ) is ρ = 1 / κ.
Calculate the curvature of the beam.
Calculate the radius of curvature of the beam.
Present the final calculated values for curvature and radius of curvature.
