The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The problem is to determine the deflection of a simply supported beam using the unit-load method.
The beam has a total length of 3000 mm.
The beam is supported by a pin at x=0 and a roller at x=3000 mm.
The beam's material has a Young's Modulus (E) of 200000 N/mm^2.
The beam's cross-section has a second moment of area (I) of 1200000 mm^4.
A single vertical point load of 800 N is applied at x = 2000 mm.
The target response quantity is the vertical deflection at the point of load application, x = 2000 mm.
The governing relation is the unit-load method integral: Deflection = integral from 0 to L of (M*m)/(E*I) dx.
M is the internal bending moment due to the 800 N real load.
m is the internal bending moment due to a 1 N virtual unit load applied at x = 2000 mm.
