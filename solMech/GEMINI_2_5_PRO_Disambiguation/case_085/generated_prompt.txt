The analysis is for a prismatic beam under pure bending.
The material behavior is linear-elastic.
Deformations are assumed to be small.
Plane sections of the beam remain plane and perpendicular to the neutral axis after bending.
The internal bending moment at the critical section is 3,000,000 N·mm.
The net axial force is zero.
The cross-section is a rectangle with a height of 100 mm.
The cross-section is a rectangle with a width of 50 mm.
The neutral axis passes through the centroid of the cross-section.
The governing relation for the second moment of area (I) for the rectangular cross-section is I = (width * height^3) / 12.
The governing relation for bending stress (σ) is the flexure formula: σ = -M*y / I, where y is the distance from the neutral axis.
The response quantities to be determined are the maximum tensile and compressive stresses.
Calculate the second moment of area (I) for the given cross-section.
Calculate the maximum compressive stress at the outer fiber in the direction of positive y.
Calculate the maximum tensile stress at the outer fiber in the direction of negative y.
State that the stress at the neutral axis (y = 0) is zero.
