The analysis assumes the bar is fixed at its upper end and hangs vertically.
The material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The analysis uses small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics theory.
The bar has a constant cross-sectional area of 200 mm^2.
The length of the bar is 3500 mm.
The Young's Modulus of the material is 69000 MPa.
The density of the material is 7850 kg/m^3.
The acceleration due to gravity is 9.81 m/s^2.
The specific weight (γ) is the product of density (ρ) and gravity (g).
The axial stress (σ) at a distance y from the free end is σ(y) = γ * y.
The axial strain (ε) is ε(y) = σ(y) / E, where E is the Young's Modulus.
The total elongation (δ) is the integral of the strain along the bar's length.
The governing relation for total elongation is δ = (γ * L^2) / (2 * E).
The calculated total elongation of the bar due to its self-weight is 0.00684 mm.
