The analysis will determine the maximum shear stress and angle of twist for a solid circular shaft.
The shaft has a diameter of 18 mm.
The shaft has a length of 400 mm.
The shaft material has a shear modulus of 39,179.1 MPa.
A torque of 500,000 N·mm is applied to the shaft.
The analysis assumes the material is homogeneous and isotropic.
The analysis assumes the shaft is straight with a uniform cross-section.
The analysis assumes the loading is purely torsional and applied statically.
The analysis operates under the small-deformation, linear-elastic framework.
Stress concentrations are neglected.
One end of the shaft is assumed to be fixed, while the torque is applied to the free end.
The polar moment of inertia, J, is calculated using the formula J = (π/2) * r^4.
The maximum shear stress, τ_max, is calculated using the formula τ_max = (T * r) / J.
The angle of twist, φ, is calculated using the formula φ = (T * L) / (G * J).
The final output will be the numerical values for the maximum shear stress and the angle of twist.
