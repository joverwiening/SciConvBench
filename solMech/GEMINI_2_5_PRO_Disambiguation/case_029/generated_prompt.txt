The analysis will be conducted under the assumptions of small-deformation, linear-elastic, and isotropic material behavior.
The object of analysis is a rod with a circular cross-section.
The initial diameter of the rod is 20 mm.
The Young's Modulus of the rod material is 105,000 MPa.
The Poisson's Ratio of the rod material is 0.34.
A single axial tensile load of 25,000 N is applied to the rod.
Calculate the initial cross-sectional area from the initial diameter.
Calculate the axial stress as the axial load divided by the initial cross-sectional area.
Calculate the axial strain using Hooke's Law (Stress = Young's Modulus * Strain).
Calculate the lateral strain using the definition of Poisson's Ratio (Lateral Strain = -Poisson's Ratio * Axial Strain).
Calculate the final diameter based on the computed lateral strain and the initial diameter.
The final report will state the values for axial stress, axial strain, lateral strain, and the final diameter.
