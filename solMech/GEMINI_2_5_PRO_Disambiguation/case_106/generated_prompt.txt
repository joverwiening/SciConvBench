The analysis assumes a prismatic Euler-Bernoulli beam.
The material is linear-elastic, isotropic, and homogeneous.
Deformations are assumed to be small.
The beam is simply supported at both ends.
The beam has a length of 2500 mm.
The beam has a Young's Modulus of 200000 MPa.
The beam has a second moment of area of 3500000 mm^4.
A single point load of 2500 N is applied at the midspan.
Calculate the maximum deflection at the midspan.
The governing relation for midspan deflection is δ = (P * L^3) / (48 * E * I).
The calculated midspan deflection is 1.16 mm.
Calculate the slope at the supports.
The governing relation for support slope is θ = (P * L^2) / (16 * E * I).
The calculated support slope is 0.0014 radians.
