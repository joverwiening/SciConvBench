The analysis objective is to determine the maximum shear stress and the total angle of twist for a solid circular shaft.
The shaft has a diameter of 30 mm.
The shaft has a length of 1000 mm.
The shaft is made from AISI 1020 steel.
A Shear Modulus (G) of 77.5 GPa shall be used for the material.
A constant torque of 250,000 N·mm is applied along the shaft's axis.
The analysis assumes linear-elastic, small-deformation behavior.
The material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
Stress concentrations are to be neglected.
The governing relation for maximum shear stress (τ_max) is τ_max = (T * r) / J, where T is torque, r is the radius, and J is the polar moment of inertia.
The governing relation for the angle of twist (φ) is φ = (T * L) / (G * J), where L is the length.
The polar moment of inertia (J) for the solid circular cross-section is calculated as J = (π/2) * r^4.
The final output shall be the maximum shear stress in MPa and the total angle of twist in degrees.
