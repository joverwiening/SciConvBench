The analysis will be conducted under the assumption of small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam material is assumed to be homogeneous, isotropic AISI 1020 steel.
The Young's Modulus for the material is 200 GPa, which is equivalent to 200,000 N/mm^2.
The applied bending moment is 3,000,000 N·mm.
The area moment of inertia of the beam's cross-section is 2,500,000 mm^4.
The governing relation for curvature is κ = M / (E * I).
The governing relation for the radius of curvature is ρ = 1 / κ.
The calculated curvature of the beam is 6.0 x 10^-6 mm^-1.
The calculated radius of curvature of the beam is 166,667 mm.
