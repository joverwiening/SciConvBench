The analysis will be performed on a simply supported Euler-Bernoulli beam.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam has a total length L of 2500 mm.
The beam's material has a Young's Modulus E of 200000 MPa.
The beam's cross-section has a second moment of area I of 1200000 mm^4.
The beam is subjected to a single downward point load P of 2500 N at its midspan.
The self-weight of the beam is considered negligible.
The governing relation for midspan deflection is δ = (P * L^3) / (48 * E * I).
The governing relation for the slope at the supports is θ = (P * L^2) / (16 * E * I).
Calculate the numerical value for the maximum deflection at the midspan.
Calculate the numerical value for the slope at the supports.
