The task is to determine the vertical deflection at the free end of a cantilever beam using Castigliano's theorem.
The beam has a length of 1500 mm.
The beam is made of a linear-elastic material with a Young's Modulus of 69000 MPa.
The beam has a solid rectangular cross-section.
The height of the cross-section is 100 mm.
The width of the cross-section is 50 mm.
A downward point load of 500 N is applied at the free end.
The analysis will assume small-deformation theory.
The governing relations used in the calculation must be stated clearly.
The final output is the numerical value for the vertical deflection at the free end.
