The analysis will determine the midspan deflection of a simply supported beam using Castigliano's theorem.
The analysis operates under the assumption of small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The beam is prismatic, with a constant cross-section along its length.
The effects of shear deformation are considered negligible.
The beam has a span length (L) of 3000 mm.
A single point load (P) of 2000 N is applied at the midspan.
The Young's Modulus (E) of the beam material is 200000 N/mm^2.
The second moment of area (I) for the beam's cross-section is 1500000 mm^4.
The governing relation is Castigliano's second theorem, where deflection (δ) is the partial derivative of the strain energy (U) with respect to the applied load (P).
The strain energy due to bending is U = ∫(M(x)^2)/(2EI) dx integrated over the beam's length.
The specific formula for midspan deflection for this case is δ = (P * L^3) / (48 * E * I).
The final output will be the calculated value of the midspan deflection.
