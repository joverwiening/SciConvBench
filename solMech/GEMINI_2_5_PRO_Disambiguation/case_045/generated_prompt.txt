The analysis assumes a small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics framework.
The material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The load is assumed to be applied quasi-statically and uniformly across the cross-section.
Stress concentrations at the point of load application are neglected.
The geometry is a straight prismatic circular rod.
The rod has an original length of 800 mm.
The rod has a circular cross-section with a diameter of 12 mm.
The material has a Young's Modulus of 69000 MPa.
The material has a yield strength of 275 MPa.
An axial tensile load of 15000 N is applied to the rod.
Calculate the cross-sectional area of the rod.
Calculate the average normal stress using the formula: stress = load / area.
Calculate the axial strain using the formula: strain = stress / Young's Modulus.
Calculate the total elongation using the formula: elongation = strain * original length.
Calculate the factor of safety against yielding using the formula: factor of safety = yield strength / average normal stress.
Present the governing relations for stress, strain, elongation, and factor of safety.
Report the final values for average normal stress, strain, elongation, and the factor of safety.
