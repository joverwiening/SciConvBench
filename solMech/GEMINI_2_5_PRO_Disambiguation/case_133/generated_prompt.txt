The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The objective is to determine the minimum required height for a rectangular beam.
The beam has a width of 30 mm.
The beam material has an allowable bending stress of 90 MPa.
The beam is subjected to a maximum bending moment of 6,000,000 N·mm.
The required beam height 'h' must be calculated.
The calculation will use the flexure formula rearranged to solve for height: h = sqrt((6 * M_max) / (b * σ_allow)).
The final output will be the minimum required beam height in millimeters.
