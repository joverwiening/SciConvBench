The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The model is a single rectangular adhesive layer bonded between two rigid bodies.
The length of the adhesive layer is assumed to be 50 mm.
The width of the adhesive layer is assumed to be 25 mm.
The thickness of the adhesive layer is 20 mm.
The shear modulus of the adhesive material is 39179.1 MPa.
A total in-plane shear force of 8000 N is applied uniformly to the top surface of the layer.
The governing relation for shear stress is τ = F / A, where F is the shear force and A is the shear area (length × width).
The governing relation for shear strain is γ = τ / G, where G is the shear modulus.
The governing relation for shear deformation is δ = h × γ for small strains, where h is the thickness.
