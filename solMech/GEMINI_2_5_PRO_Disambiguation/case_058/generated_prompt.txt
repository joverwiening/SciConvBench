The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The objective is to determine the Euler critical buckling load for a column.
A buckling instability check will also be performed.
The governing relation for the Euler critical buckling load is P_critical = (pi^2 * E * I) / (K * L)^2.
The column has fixed-pinned end conditions.
The effective length factor K is 0.7.
The Young's Modulus E is 200,000 MPa.
The area moment of inertia I is 1,200,000 mm^4.
The unbraced length L is 2,000 mm.
The applied compressive load P_applied is assumed to be 800,000 N.
The buckling instability check consists of comparing the applied load to the critical load.
The column is considered unstable if the applied load is greater than or equal to the critical load.
The final output will be the value of the Euler critical buckling load and the result of the instability check.
