The analysis objective is to determine the torsional response of a solid circular shaft.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic material behavior.
The material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The shaft is fixed at the coordinate x = 0.
The total length of the shaft is 1500 mm.
The shaft has a constant diameter of 40 mm.
The shear modulus of the shaft material is 25939.8 MPa.
A torque of -150000 N·mm is applied at x = 750 mm.
A torque of 300000 N·mm is applied at the free end, x = 1500 mm.
The analysis will determine the internal torque, maximum shear stress, and angle of twist along the length of the shaft.
The governing relation for shear stress is τ = T*r/J.
The governing relation for the angle of twist is φ = TL/(GJ).
The polar moment of inertia for the solid circular cross-section is J = π*d^4/32.
