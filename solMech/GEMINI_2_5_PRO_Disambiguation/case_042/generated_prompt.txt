The analysis assumes the work done by the suddenly applied load is completely converted into elastic strain energy.
The work done (W) by the suddenly applied load (P) is W = P * δ_max, where δ_max is the maximum elongation.
The maximum elastic strain energy (U) stored in the bar is U = (σ_max^2 * A * L) / (2 * E).
The maximum elongation is related to the maximum stress by δ_max = (σ_max * L) / E.
Equating work done and strain energy yields the governing relation for maximum stress: σ_max = 2 * P / A.
The suddenly applied axial load (P) is 8000 N.
The cross-sectional area (A) is 300 mm^2.
The Modulus of Elasticity (E) is 200000 MPa.
The maximum stress is calculated as 2 * (8000 N) / (300 mm^2).
The resulting maximum stress in the bar is 53.33 MPa.
