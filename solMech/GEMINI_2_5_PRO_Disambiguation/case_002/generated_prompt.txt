The analysis will be performed on a two-segment, stepped circular bar.
The entire bar is made of structural steel.
The Young's Modulus for the steel is 200000 MPa.
The first segment has a length of 400 mm and a diameter of 16 mm.
The second segment has a length of 800 mm and a diameter of 40 mm.
An axial tensile force of 35000 N is applied to the bar.
The analysis will determine the axial stress in each segment and the total elongation of the bar.
The governing relation for stress is σ = P/A, where P is the axial force and A is the cross-sectional area.
The governing relation for elongation is δ = (PL)/(AE), where L is the segment length and E is the Young's Modulus.
The total elongation is the sum of the elongations of the individual segments.
The analysis assumes a small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics model.
The material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The applied load is assumed to be static, axial, and uniformly distributed over the cross-section.
Stress concentrations at the step between the segments are to be ignored.
The self-weight of the bar is considered negligible.
