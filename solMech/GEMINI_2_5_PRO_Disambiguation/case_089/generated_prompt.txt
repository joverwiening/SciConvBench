The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The load is assumed to be applied axially and uniformly over the cross-sections.
Stress concentrations at the step between segments are to be ignored.
The problem is to analyze a stepped circular bar composed of two segments.
A tensile force of 25000 N is applied to the bar.
The first segment has a length of 400 mm and a diameter of 16 mm.
The second segment has a length of 400 mm and a diameter of 40 mm.
The Young's Modulus for the bar material is 200000 MPa.
The governing relation for axial stress is stress = Force / Area.
The governing relation for axial elongation of a segment is elongation = (Force * Length) / (Area * Young's Modulus).
The total elongation is the sum of the elongations of the individual segments.
Determine the average normal stress in the first segment.
Determine the average normal stress in the second segment.
Determine the total elongation of the bar.
