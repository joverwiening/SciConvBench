The analysis will be performed under the assumption of small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The load is assumed to be applied axially and uniformly distributed over the cross-section.
Stress concentrations at the step transition are neglected.
The bar consists of two distinct circular segments.
The first segment has a length of 300 mm.
The first segment has a diameter of 12 mm.
The second segment has a length of 800 mm.
The second segment has a diameter of 30 mm.
Both segments have a Young's Modulus of 200000 MPa.
A tensile load of 35000 N is applied to the bar.
The analysis will determine the axial stress in each of the two segments.
The analysis will determine the total elongation of the entire bar.
The governing relations for stress (σ = P/A) and elongation (δ = PL/AE) will be used.
