The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The object is a prismatic bar with a total length of 1800 mm.
The bar has a constant cross-sectional area of 200 mm^2.
The material's Young's Modulus is 200,000 MPa.
The bar is oriented along the x-axis, with point A at x=0.
The boundary condition at point A (x=0) is a fixed support.

An axial point load of an unspecified magnitude is applied at point C (x=1300 mm).
An axial point load of 20000 N is applied at point D (x=1800 mm) in the positive x-direction.
The governing relation for stress is sigma = N/A, where N is the internal axial force and A is the cross-sectional area.
The governing relation for displacement of a segment is delta = (N * L) / (A * E), where L is the segment length and E is the Young's Modulus.
The analysis shall determine the reaction force at the fixed support A.
The analysis shall determine the internal axial force in segments AB, BC, and CD.
