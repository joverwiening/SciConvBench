The analysis determines the buckling response of a solid circular column.
The governing relation for the critical buckling load is the Euler column formula.
The critical load is P_cr = (pi^2 * E * I) / (K * L)^2.
The area moment of inertia for the circular cross-section is I = (pi * d^4) / 64.
The allowable load is P_allow = P_cr / FOS.
The factor of safety against buckling is FOS_actual = P_cr / P_applied.
The column has a length of 1500 mm.
The column has a diameter of 30 mm.
The column material has a Young's Modulus of 200000 MPa.
The column has an effective length factor K of 2.0.
The desired factor of safety for the allowable load calculation is 2.0.
The analysis assumes the column is perfectly straight and the load is applied perfectly concentrically.
The analysis assumes the material is homogeneous and isotropic.
An applied compressive axial load of 5000 N is assumed for the factor of safety evaluation.
Calculate the allowable column load.
Calculate the factor of safety with respect to buckling for the assumed applied load.
