The analysis objective is to determine the vertical deflection at the free end of a cantilever beam.
The calculation will be performed using Castigliano's second theorem.
The analysis assumes linear-elastic material behavior and small deformations.
Strain energy due to shear forces is considered negligible.
The beam has a length of 1500 mm.
The beam has a constant area moment of inertia of 2,500,000 mm^4.
The material's Young's Modulus is 200,000 MPa.
A single vertical point load of 500 N is applied at the free end of the beam.
No other loads or moments are applied to the beam.
The governing relation for bending moment at a distance x from the fixed support is M(x) = -P(L-x).
The governing relation for strain energy due to bending is U = ∫[M(x)^2 / (2EI)] dx, integrated over the beam's length.
The deflection δ at the free end is calculated as the partial derivative of the total strain energy U with respect to the point load P.
