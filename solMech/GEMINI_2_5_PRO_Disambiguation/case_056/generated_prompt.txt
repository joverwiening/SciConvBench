The analysis objective is to determine the axial deformation, stress distribution, and strain distribution for a bar.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic, homogeneous, and isotropic material behavior.
The analysis assumes stress is uniform across any given cross-section.
The bar is fixed at one end and free at the other.
The cross-sectional area at the fixed end is 250 mm^2.
The cross-sectional area at the free end is 450 mm^2.
The total length of the bar is 800 mm.
The cross-sectional area varies linearly with position along the bar's axis.
The material's Young's Modulus is 200,000 MPa.
A tensile axial load of 30,000 N is applied at the free end of the bar.
Present the governing relation for the cross-sectional area A(x) as a function of position x.
Present the governing relation for the total axial deformation.
Calculate the final value for the total axial deformation.
Calculate and present the stress sigma(x) as a function of position x.
Calculate and present the strain epsilon(x) as a function of position x.
