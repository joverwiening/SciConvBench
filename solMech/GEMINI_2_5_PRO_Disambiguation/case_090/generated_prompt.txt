The system is a stepped circular bar composed of two distinct segments.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The material has a Modulus of Elasticity of 200,000 MPa.
The first segment has a diameter of 12 mm and a length of 300 mm.
The second segment has a diameter of 30 mm and a length of 600 mm.
An axial tensile force of 25,000 N is applied to the bar.
Calculate the axial stress in the first segment.
Calculate the axial stress in the second segment.
Calculate the total elongation of the entire bar.
The governing relation for stress is Force divided by cross-sectional Area (σ = P/A).
The governing relation for elongation of a segment is (Force * Length) / (Area * Modulus of Elasticity) (δ = PL/AE).
The total elongation is the sum of the elongations of the two segments.
