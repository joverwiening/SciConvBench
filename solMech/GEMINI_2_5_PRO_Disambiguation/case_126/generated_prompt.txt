The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The analysis object is a solid circular shaft.
The shaft has a diameter of 30 mm.
The shaft has a length of 1200 mm.
The shaft material has a shear modulus of 39,179.1 MPa.
The shaft is fixed at one end.
A torque of 400,000 N·mm is applied to the free end of the shaft.
The governing relation for maximum shear stress is τ = T*r/J, where r is the radius and J is the polar moment of inertia.
The governing relation for the angle of twist is φ = T*L/(G*J), where L is the length and G is the shear modulus.
The polar moment of inertia for the solid circular cross-section is J = (π * d^4) / 32.
Determine the maximum shear stress in the shaft.
Determine the total angle of twist at the free end of the shaft.
