The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The object of analysis is a solid circular column.
The column diameter is 20 mm.
The column length is 2000 mm.
The column ends are pinned-pinned, corresponding to an effective length factor K of 1.0.
The material's Young's Modulus is 200,000 MPa.
The material's Yield Strength is assumed to be 250 MPa.
The governing relation for the critical buckling load is the Euler formula: P_cr = (π^2 * E * I) / (K * L)^2.
The analysis shall determine the critical Euler buckling load (P_cr).
The analysis shall verify that the critical buckling stress is less than the material yield strength.
The analysis shall determine the allowable compressive load (P_allow) using a factor of safety of 2.0.
The allowable load is calculated as P_allow = P_cr / 2.0.
The final response quantities to be reported are the critical buckling load and the allowable load.
