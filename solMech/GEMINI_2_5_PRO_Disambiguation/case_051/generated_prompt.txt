The analysis will be performed for a beam under small-deformation, linear-elastic conditions.
The beam has a rectangular cross-section.
The height of the cross-section is 80 mm.
The width of the cross-section is 50 mm.
The internal shear force at the cross-section of interest is 20000 N.
The governing relation for transverse shear stress is τ = VQ / (Ib).
The maximum transverse shear stress in the cross-section must be calculated.
The transverse shear stress at a vertical position y = 35 mm from the neutral axis must be calculated.
The beam material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The beam is assumed to be prismatic.
The shear stress is assumed to be uniformly distributed across the width of the cross-section.
The neutral axis is assumed to pass through the centroid of the cross-section.
