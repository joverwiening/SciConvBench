The analysis is performed under the assumption of small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The specimen material is assumed to be a standard aluminum alloy.
The material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The initial gauge length of the specimen is 50 mm.
The initial cross-sectional area is calculated from the initial diameter of 12.5 mm.
An axial tensile load of 12000 N is applied.
The measured elongation of the gauge section is 0.12 mm.
The governing relation for engineering stress is the applied force divided by the initial cross-sectional area.
The calculated engineering stress is 97.78 MPa.
The governing relation for engineering strain is the change in length divided by the initial gauge length.
The calculated engineering strain is 0.0024.
The governing relation for the modulus of elasticity is the engineering stress divided by the engineering strain.
The calculated modulus of elasticity is 40.74 GPa.
