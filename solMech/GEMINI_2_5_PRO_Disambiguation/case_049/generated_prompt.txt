The task is to analyze a hollow circular shaft subjected to pure torsion.
The analysis will be conducted under the assumption of small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The shaft material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The shaft is assumed to be straight and of uniform cross-section along its length.
The applied torque is static and acts purely about the longitudinal axis of the shaft.
Cross-sections are assumed to remain plane and circular during torsion.
The outer radius of the shaft is 25 mm.
The inner radius of the shaft is 21 mm.
The length of the shaft is 1200 mm.
The magnitude of the applied torque is 450000 N·mm.
The shear modulus of the shaft material is 77519.4 MPa.
The polar moment of inertia, J, shall be calculated using the formula J = (π/2) * (Ro^4 - Ri^4).
The shear stress, τ, at any radius r shall be calculated using the formula τ = (T * r) / J.
The total angle of twist, φ, over the shaft's length shall be calculated using the formula φ = (T * L) / (G * J).
Determine the value of the maximum shear stress, which occurs at the outer radius.
Determine the value of the minimum shear stress, which occurs at the inner radius.
Determine the value of the total angle of twist.
