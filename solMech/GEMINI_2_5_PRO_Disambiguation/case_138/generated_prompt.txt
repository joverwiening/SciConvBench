The analysis is performed under the small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics framework.
The object is a prismatic bar with a rectangular cross-section.
The width of the cross-section is 40 mm.
The height of the cross-section is 120 mm.
The cross-sectional area is 4800 mm^2.
The section is subjected to an axial tensile force of 30,000 N.
The section is subjected to a transverse shear force of 12,000 N.
The governing relation for average normal stress is the axial force divided by the cross-sectional area (σ_avg = P/A).
The governing relation for average shear stress is the transverse shear force divided by the cross-sectional area (τ_avg = V/A).
The calculated average normal stress at the section is 6.25 MPa.
The calculated average shear stress at the section is 2.5 MPa.
It is assumed that any bending moment present does not contribute to the average normal stress.
