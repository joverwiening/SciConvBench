The analysis shall determine the mechanical response of a 6061-T6 aluminum rod under a uniaxial tensile load.
The analysis assumes the material is homogeneous, isotropic, and operates within the linear-elastic range.
The analysis assumes small deformations.
The load is assumed to be applied uniformly over the cross-sectional area of the rod.
The initial length of the rod is 500 mm.
The initial diameter of the rod is 40 mm.
The axial tensile load applied is 35000 N.
The Young's Modulus for 6061-T6 aluminum shall be taken as 68.9 GPa.
The Poisson's Ratio for 6061-T6 aluminum shall be taken as 0.33.
The following response quantities shall be calculated:
Axial stress.
Axial strain.
The change in the rod's length.
The final length of the rod.
Lateral strain.
The change in the rod's diameter.
The final diameter of the rod.
The governing relations for stress, axial strain, and lateral strain shall be presented.
