The analysis is for a small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics case.
The geometry is a solid circular shaft.
The shaft diameter is 35 mm.
The shaft length is 400 mm.
The shaft material has a shear modulus of 77519.4 MPa.
The shaft is fixed at one end and free at the other.
A constant torque of 250000 N·mm is applied to the free end of the shaft.
Calculate the maximum shear stress on the outer surface of the shaft.
Calculate the total angle of twist at the free end relative to the fixed end.
The governing relation for shear stress is τ = (T * ρ) / J, where ρ is the radial distance.
The governing relation for the angle of twist is φ = (T * L) / (G * J).
The polar moment of inertia for the solid circular cross-section is J = (π/2) * r^4.
