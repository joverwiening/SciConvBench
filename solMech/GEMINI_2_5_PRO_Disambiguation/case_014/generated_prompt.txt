The analysis is based on small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam is modeled using Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.
The beam is a cantilever configuration, fixed at one end and free at the other.
The length of the beam (L) is 1200 mm.
The beam material has a Young's modulus (E) of 200000 MPa.
The beam has a solid rectangular cross-section.
The width of the cross-section (b) is 25 mm.
The height of the cross-section (h) is 50 mm.
A point load (P) of 800 N is applied at the free end, perpendicular to the beam's axis.
The required response quantities are the slope and vertical deflection at the free end.
The area moment of inertia (I) is calculated using the relation I = (b * h^3) / 12.
The governing relation for the slope at the free end is θ = (P * L^2) / (2 * E * I).
The governing relation for the vertical deflection at the free end is δ = (P * L^3) / (3 * E * I).
