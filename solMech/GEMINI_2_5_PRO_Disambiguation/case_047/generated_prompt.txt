The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The object of analysis is a straight prismatic circular rod.
The rod has a diameter of 12 mm.
The rod has a gage length of 800 mm.
The rod is made of steel with a Modulus of Elasticity of 200,000 MPa.
The steel has a yield strength of 350 MPa.
An axial tensile load of 15,000 N is applied to the rod.
Calculate the average normal stress in the rod.
Calculate the axial strain in the rod.
Calculate the total elongation of the rod over its gage length.
Calculate the factor of safety with respect to yielding.
The governing relation for stress is σ = P / A, where P is the axial load and A is the cross-sectional area.
The governing relation for strain is ε = σ / E, where E is the Modulus of Elasticity.
The governing relation for elongation is δ = ε * L, where L is the gage length.
The governing relation for the factor of safety is FS = Sy / σ, where Sy is the yield strength.
