The analysis is a linear elastic static analysis of an Euler-Bernoulli beam.
The beam is a cantilever configuration, fixed at one end and free at the other.
The length of the beam is 1200 mm.
A point load of 2000 N is applied at the free end.
The load is applied perpendicular to the beam's primary axis.
The beam material is AISI 1020 steel, with a Young's Modulus of 200 GPa.
The beam has a solid rectangular cross-section with a width of 50 mm and a height of 100 mm.
The 100 mm height is oriented in the direction of the applied load.
The governing relation for maximum deflection at the free end is delta = (P * L^3) / (3 * E * I).
The governing relation for maximum bending stress at the fixed end is sigma = (M * c) / I.
The objective is to determine the maximum deflection and the maximum bending stress.
