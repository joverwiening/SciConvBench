The analysis will determine the maximum shear stress and total angle of twist for a solid circular shaft.
The shaft has a diameter of 30 mm.
The shaft has a length of 800 mm.
The shaft material has a shear modulus of 25,939.8 MPa.
A torque of 500,000 N·mm is applied to the shaft.
The analysis assumes one end of the shaft is fixed and the torque is applied at the free end.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The governing relation for maximum shear stress is τ = (T * r) / J.
The governing relation for the angle of twist is φ = (T * L) / (G * J).
The polar moment of inertia, J, for the solid circular cross-section shall be used.
The final outputs will be the maximum shear stress and the total angle of twist.
