Model a prismatic bar with a total length of 1500 mm.
The bar has a constant cross-sectional area of 300 mm^2.
The material of the bar is linear-elastic with a Young's Modulus of 200 GPa.
The left end of the bar, point A at position 0 mm, is fixed.
Apply a point load of 18000 N directed to the right at point B, located 500 mm from the fixed end.
Apply a point load of 12000 N directed to the left at point C, located 1100 mm from the fixed end.
Apply a point load of 16000 N directed to the right at the free end, point D, located 1500 mm from the fixed end.
Assume small-deformation theory is applicable.
Determine the reaction force at the fixed support A.
Determine the axial force distribution along the length of the bar.
Determine the axial stress distribution along the length of the bar.
Determine the axial strain distribution along the length of the bar.
Determine the axial displacement distribution along the length of the bar.
