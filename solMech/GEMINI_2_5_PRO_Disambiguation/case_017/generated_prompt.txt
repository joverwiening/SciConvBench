Analyze a simply supported Euler-Bernoulli beam.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.
The beam has a total length of 3000 mm.
The beam's material has a Young's modulus of 200,000 MPa.
The beam's cross-section has a second moment of area of 3,000,000 mm^4.
The beam is subjected to two loads.
The first load is a uniformly distributed transverse load of 0.3 N/mm applied over the entire span.
The second load is a downward point load of 1200 N applied at the midspan.
Calculate the total deflection of the beam using the principle of superposition.
State the governing equations for deflection for each load case separately.
Present the final combined equation for total deflection.
Calculate the maximum deflection value.
