The analysis will be based on small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The task is to determine the minimum diameter for a solid circular column to prevent buckling.
The column material is structural steel with a Young's modulus of 200,000 MPa.
The yield strength of the structural steel is assumed to be 250 MPa.
The length of the column is 1800 mm.
The column is subjected to a compressive axial load of 30,000 N.
The boundary conditions correspond to an effective length factor (K) of 1.0.
The required factor of safety against buckling is 2.0.
The final design must be checked to ensure the critical buckling stress is below the assumed yield strength.
The governing relations for Euler's critical load must be presented.
