The analysis will be a small-deformation, linear-elastic static analysis.
The subject of the analysis is a cantilever Euler-Bernoulli beam.
The beam has a length of 1800 mm.
The beam's material has a Young's Modulus of 200000 MPa.
The beam has a solid rectangular cross-section.
The cross-section height is 100 mm.
The cross-section width is 50 mm.
A downward point load of 2000 N is applied at the free end of the beam.
No other loads or moments are applied.
The analysis will determine the maximum deflection at the free end.
The governing differential equation is EI * d^4v/dx^4 = q(x).
The area moment of inertia is calculated as I = (b * h^3) / 12.
The maximum deflection is calculated as v_max = (P * L^3) / (3 * E * I).
