Analyze a bar under an axial tensile load using small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The bar is fixed at one end (x=0) and free at the other end (x=L).
The length of the bar, L, is 800 mm.
The cross-sectional area of the bar varies linearly with position x along its length.
The area at the fixed end, A(0), is 250 mm^2.
The area at the free end, A(L), is 400 mm^2.
The cross-sectional area at any point x is given by A(x) = 250 + (150/800) * x mm^2.
The material of the bar has a Young's Modulus, E, of 200,000 MPa.
A constant axial tensile load, P, of 12,000 N is applied at the free end (x=L).
The internal axial force, N(x), is constant and equal to 12,000 N throughout the bar.
Determine the axial stress distribution, σ(x), along the length of the bar using the relation σ(x) = N(x) / A(x).
Determine the axial strain distribution, ε(x), along the length of the bar using the relation ε(x) = σ(x) / E.
Determine the total axial elongation, δ, of the bar by integrating the axial strain from x=0 to x=L.
