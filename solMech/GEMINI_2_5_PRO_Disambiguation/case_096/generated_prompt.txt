The analysis will be conducted using linear-elastic, small-deformation solid mechanics.
The beam is modeled using the Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.
The beam is a cantilever configuration, fixed at one end and free at the other.
The length of the beam is 800 mm.
The beam is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with a Young's Modulus of 69000 MPa.
The beam has a solid rectangular cross-section with a width of 20 mm and a height of 40 mm.
The cross-section is oriented such that the height is aligned with the direction of the load.
A downward point load of 500 N is applied at the free end of the beam.
The governing equations for shear force, bending moment, slope, and deflection along the beam's length shall be derived.
The maximum deflection at the free end shall be calculated.
The maximum bending stress at the fixed end shall be calculated.
