The analysis is for a short prismatic bar under specified loads.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The cross-section of the bar is rectangular.
The width of the cross-section is 50 mm.
The height of the cross-section is 120 mm.
The cross-sectional area is 6000 mm^2.
The bar is subjected to an axial tension force (P) of 40,000 N applied at the centroid of the cross-section.
The bar is subjected to a transverse shear force (V) of 15,000 N.
The shear force acts parallel to the 120 mm height dimension.
The governing relation for average normal stress is σ_avg = P / A.
The calculated average normal stress is 6.67 MPa.
The governing relation for average shear stress is τ_avg = V / A.
The calculated average shear stress is 2.5 MPa.
