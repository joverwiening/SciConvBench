The objective is to determine the minimum required diameter for a solid circular shaft based on an angle-of-twist limit.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic material behavior.
Any transverse shear loading is not considered in this calculation as it does not contribute to the angle of twist.
The shaft has a length of 1200 mm.
The shaft material has a shear modulus of 77519.4 MPa.
The shaft is subjected to an applied torque of 800000 N·mm.
The maximum allowable angle of twist over the entire length is 1.0 degree.
The governing relation for the angle of twist (φ) is φ = (T * L) / (G * J).
The polar moment of inertia (J) for a solid circular shaft of diameter (d) is J = (π * d^4) / 32.
The minimum required shaft diameter is 51.85 mm.
