The analysis will be conducted using linear-elastic, small-deformation solid mechanics theory.
The model is a cantilever beam fixed at one end and free at the other.
The beam has a length of 2500 mm.
The beam has a rectangular cross-section.
The width of the cross-section is 45 mm.
The height of the cross-section is 90 mm.
The beam's material has a Young's Modulus of 200000 MPa.
A uniformly distributed transverse load of 1.0 N/mm is applied downwards along the entire length of the beam.
The primary output is the deflection of the beam as a function of position along its length.
The maximum deflection at the free end of the beam must be calculated.
The governing differential equation for beam deflection must be stated.
The equations for slope, bending moment, and shear force as a function of position must also be provided.
