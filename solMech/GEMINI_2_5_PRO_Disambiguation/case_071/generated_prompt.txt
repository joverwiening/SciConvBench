Assume the material is structural steel.
The Young's Modulus is 200 GPa.
The Poisson's Ratio is 0.3.
The structural member has a rectangular cross-section of 50 mm width and 80 mm height.
The cross-sectional area is 4000 mm^2.
An axial tension load of 40,000 N is applied normal to the cross-section.
A shear load of 12,000 N is applied parallel to the 50 mm width dimension.
Calculate the average normal stress from the tension load using the formula σ_x = P/A.
Calculate the average shear stress from the shear load using the formula τ_xy = V/A.
The state of plane stress is defined by σ_x, τ_xy, and σ_y = 0.
Calculate the principal stresses using the formula σ_1,2 = (σ_x / 2) ± sqrt((σ_x / 2)^2 + τ_xy^2).
Calculate the principal strains using the material's constitutive relations and the principal stress values.
Present the governing relations and the calculated values for average normal stress, average shear stress, principal stresses, and principal strains.
