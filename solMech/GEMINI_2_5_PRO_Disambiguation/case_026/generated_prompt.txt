The analysis will determine the deflection at the free end of a cantilever beam using Castigliano's theorem.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The beam has a length of 1500 mm.
The beam material has a Young's Modulus of 200000 MPa.
The cross-section of the beam has an area moment of inertia of 800000 mm^4.
A single point load of 500 N is applied at the free end, perpendicular to the beam's neutral axis.
No other external loads or moments are applied to the beam.
The strain energy due to bending is the only strain energy considered.
The governing relation for strain energy is U = integral of (M(x)^2 / (2*E*I)) dx over the beam's length.
The governing relation for deflection is the partial derivative of the total strain energy with respect to the point load P.
The final result will be the numerical value of the deflection at the free end.
