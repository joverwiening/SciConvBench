The analysis will be performed using a small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics model.
The structural member is a rectangular adhesive layer with a thickness of 8 mm.
The area of the adhesive layer is 3000 mm^2.
The adhesive layer is bonded between two rigid adherends.
A uniform in-plane shear force of 12000 N is applied to the adhesive layer.
The shear force is assumed to be distributed uniformly over the area of the adhesive layer.
The adhesive material has a shear modulus of 39179.1 MPa.
The governing relation for average shear stress (τ) is τ = Force / Area.
The governing relation for shear strain (γ) is γ = τ / Shear Modulus.
The governing relation for shear deformation (δ) is δ = Thickness * γ.
Determine the average shear stress in the adhesive layer.
Determine the shear strain in the adhesive layer.
Determine the total shear deformation of the adhesive layer.
