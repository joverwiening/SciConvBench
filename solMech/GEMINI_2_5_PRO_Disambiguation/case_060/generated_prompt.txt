The analysis is an Euler buckling check for a column under compressive load.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The column has fixed-fixed end conditions.
The column length is 2000 mm.
The Young's Modulus of the material is 200000 MPa.
The cross-section of the column is a solid circle.
The diameter of the cross-section is 40 mm.
The yield strength of the material is 275 MPa.
Calculate the effective column length for fixed-fixed boundary conditions.
Calculate the area moment of inertia for the solid circular cross-section.
Calculate the Euler critical buckling load using the effective length, Young's Modulus, and area moment of inertia.
Calculate the critical buckling stress by dividing the critical load by the cross-sectional area.
Compare the critical buckling stress to the material yield strength to check if buckling occurs before yielding.
The final output will be the value of the Euler critical buckling load.
The final output will include the result of the buckling versus yielding check.
