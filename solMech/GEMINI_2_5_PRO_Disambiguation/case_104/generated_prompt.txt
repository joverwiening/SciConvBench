The analysis assumes a linear-elastic, small-deformation solid mechanics framework.
The object of analysis is a vertical bar with a length of 3000 mm.
The bar has a uniform cross-sectional area of 300 mm^2.
The bar is made of a material with a Young's Modulus of 69000 MPa.
The material has a density of 8900 kg/m^3.
The acceleration due to gravity is 9.81 m/s^2.
The bar is fixed at its top end and is free at its bottom end.
The only load considered is the self-weight of the bar.
The material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The primary response quantity to determine is the total elongation of the bar.
The governing relation for stress at a distance x from the free end is σ(x) = ρgx.
The governing relation for total elongation is δ = (ρgL^2)/(2E).
