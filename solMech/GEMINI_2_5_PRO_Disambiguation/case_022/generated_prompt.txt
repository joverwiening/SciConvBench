Analyze a stepped solid circular shaft under torsional loading.
The analysis will use small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics principles.
The shaft consists of two distinct segments, AB and BC.
End A of the shaft is fixed.
Segment AB has a length of 300 mm.
Segment AB has a diameter of 25 mm.
Segment BC has a length of 700 mm.
Segment BC has a diameter of 30 mm.
A concentrated torque of 450,000 N·mm is applied at the free end, C.
No transverse shear forces are applied to the shaft.
The shaft material is homogeneous and isotropic.
The shear modulus of the material is 77,519.4 MPa.
Determine the maximum torsional shear stress in each segment of the shaft.
Determine the total angle of twist at the free end, C, relative to the fixed end, A.
Present the governing equations for torsional shear stress and angle of twist.
