The analysis objective is to determine the failure mode of a solid circular column by comparing yielding and Euler buckling.
An instability check will be performed for the specified applied load.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The column has a diameter of 25 mm.
The column has a length of 2000 mm.
The column is made of structural steel.
The Young's Modulus of the steel is 200000 MPa.
The yield strength of the steel is 250 MPa.
The boundary conditions are represented by an effective length factor K of 0.7.
The applied compressive load is 25000 N.
The cross-sectional area is calculated as A = pi * (d/2)^2.
The area moment of inertia is calculated as I = pi * d^4 / 64.
The effective length is calculated as Le = K * L.
The critical Euler buckling load is calculated as P_cr = (pi^2 * E * I) / Le^2.
The yielding load is calculated as P_y = sigma_y * A.
The failure mode is determined by the lower of P_cr and P_y.
The instability check is performed by comparing the applied load P to the determined failure load.
The calculated critical buckling load is approximately 19312 N.
The calculated yielding load is approximately 122718 N.
The column will fail by Euler buckling because the critical buckling load is less than the yielding load.
The applied load of 25000 N is greater than the critical buckling load of 19312 N.
The column is unstable and will buckle under the applied load.
