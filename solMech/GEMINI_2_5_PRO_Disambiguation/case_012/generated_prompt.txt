The analysis objective is to determine the minimum required height for a beam's cross-section.
The beam is modeled using Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.
The analysis assumes linear-elastic material behavior and small deformations.
The beam is simply supported at both ends.
The total length of the beam is 2000 mm.
The beam has a solid rectangular cross-section with a constant width of 30 mm.
The material's Young's Modulus is 69000 MPa.
A single point load of 3000 N is applied at the midspan of the beam.
The maximum allowable deflection anywhere along the beam is 3 mm.
The governing equation for maximum deflection is δ = (P * L^3) / (48 * E * I).
The area moment of inertia for the cross-section is calculated as I = (b * h^3) / 12.
