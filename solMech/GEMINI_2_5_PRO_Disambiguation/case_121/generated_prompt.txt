Analyze a rectangular column under an eccentric compressive load.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The column has a rectangular cross-section with a width of 120 mm and a depth of 200 mm.
A compressive force of 40000 N is applied to the cross-section.
The force is applied with an eccentricity of 25 mm along the axis parallel to the 120 mm width.
The eccentricity along the axis parallel to the 200 mm depth is zero.
Calculate the normal stress at the two extreme edges along the 120 mm width.
Determine if any portion of the cross-section experiences tensile stress.
The governing relation for normal stress (σ) is σ = -P/A - (P*e*x)/I.
In the governing relation, P is the compressive force, A is the cross-sectional area, e is the eccentricity, x is the distance from the centroid, and I is the area moment of inertia about the axis of bending.
