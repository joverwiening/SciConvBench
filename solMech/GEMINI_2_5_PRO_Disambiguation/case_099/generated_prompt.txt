The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The model is a cantilever beam.
The beam has a length of 2000 mm.
The beam has a rectangular cross-section with a width of 50 mm and a height of 100 mm.
The beam material has a Young's Modulus of 200000 MPa.
A uniformly distributed transverse load of 1.0 N/mm is applied along the entire length of the beam.
The load acts in the downward direction.
The objective is to determine the beam's response quantities, with a focus on deflection.
The governing relations for Euler-Bernoulli beam theory shall be used.
The boundary conditions are zero displacement and zero slope at the fixed end.
The boundary conditions are zero bending moment and zero shear force at the free end.
The maximum deflection at the free end of the beam is the primary output.
