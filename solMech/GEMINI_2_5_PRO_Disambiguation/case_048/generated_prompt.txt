The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The object is a hollow circular shaft.
The shaft has an outer diameter of 60 mm.
The shaft has an inner diameter of 48 mm.
The shaft has a length of 800 mm.
The shaft material has a shear modulus of 76923.1 MPa.
The shaft is subjected to an applied torque of 450000 N·mm.
Calculate the maximum shear stress at the outer surface of the shaft.
Calculate the total angle of twist over the length of the shaft.
The governing relation for shear stress is τ = (T * r) / J.
The governing relation for the angle of twist is θ = (T * L) / (G * J).
The polar moment of inertia for the hollow shaft is J = (π / 2) * (Ro^4 - Ri^4).
