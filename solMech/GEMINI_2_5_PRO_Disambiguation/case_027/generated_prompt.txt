The analysis will be based on small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The problem is to determine the response of a vertical rod subjected to impact loading.
The rod is fixed at its top end and free at its bottom end.
The rod has a length of 1500 mm.
The rod has a circular cross-section with a diameter of 16 mm.
The rod material has a Modulus of Elasticity of 200,000 MPa.
The loading is caused by a weight of 800 N.
The weight is dropped from a height of 150 mm onto the free end of the rod.
There is no pre-existing axial load on the rod before the impact.
The analysis must solve for the maximum axial force in the rod.
The analysis must solve for the maximum normal stress in the rod.
The governing relations are derived from the principle of conservation of energy.
