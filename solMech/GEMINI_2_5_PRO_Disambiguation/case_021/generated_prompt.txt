The analysis will determine the stress and total twist of a stepped solid circular shaft.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The shaft consists of two distinct cylindrical segments, labeled AB and BC.
Segment AB has a length of 300 mm and a diameter of 20 mm.
Segment BC has a length of 500 mm and a diameter of 30 mm.
End A of segment AB is fixed to a rigid support.
A torque of 300,000 N·mm is applied at the free end, C.
The material's shear modulus is assumed to be 80,000 MPa.
The objective is to calculate the maximum shear stress in segment AB.
The objective is to calculate the maximum shear stress in segment BC.
The objective is to calculate the total angle of twist at the free end C.
