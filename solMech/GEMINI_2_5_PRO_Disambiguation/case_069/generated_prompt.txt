The analysis assumes linear-elastic, small-deformation solid mechanics.
The material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The beam is assumed to be initially straight and subjected to pure bending.
Plane sections of the beam are assumed to remain plane after bending.
The applied bending moment (M) is 3,000,000 N·mm.
The Young's Modulus (E) of the material is 200,000 N/mm^2.
The second moment of area (I) of the cross-section is 4,000,000 mm^4.
The governing relation for curvature (κ) is κ = M / (E * I).
The governing relation for the radius of curvature (ρ) is ρ = 1 / κ.
The calculated flexural rigidity (E * I) is 8.0e11 N·mm^2.
The calculated curvature (κ) is 3.75e-6 mm^-1.
The calculated radius of curvature (ρ) is 266,667 mm.
