The analysis is based on linear-elastic solid mechanics and assumes small deformations.
The beam has a rectangular cross-section with a width of 60 mm.
The maximum bending moment applied to the beam is 6,000,000 N·mm.
The allowable bending stress for the material is 170 MPa.
The objective is to determine the minimum required beam height.
The governing relation is the flexure formula, where maximum stress is σ_max = (6 * M_max) / (b * h^2).
The minimum required height for the beam is 59.41 mm.
