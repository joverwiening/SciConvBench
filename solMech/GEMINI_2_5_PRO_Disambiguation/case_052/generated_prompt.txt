The analysis domain is small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The component under analysis is a solid circular shaft.
The shaft material is AISI 1020 steel.
The tensile yield strength of the AISI 1020 steel is assumed to be 350 MPa.
The shear modulus of the AISI 1020 steel is assumed to be 79.3 GPa.
The shaft diameter is 35 mm.
The shaft length is 1000 mm.
A torsional load is applied to the shaft.
The torque is derived from a motor with 10 kW of power operating at a rotational speed of 1800 rpm.
No other external loads are applied to the shaft.
A design factor of safety of 2.0 is assumed.
Calculate the torque applied to the shaft.
Calculate the maximum shear stress induced in the shaft.
Calculate the allowable shear stress for the material based on the Distortion Energy Theory and the assumed factor of safety.
Verify that the maximum shear stress is less than the allowable shear stress.
Calculate the total angle of twist along the shaft's length.
The relevant response quantities to be determined are the applied torque, the maximum shear stress, and the angle of twist.
