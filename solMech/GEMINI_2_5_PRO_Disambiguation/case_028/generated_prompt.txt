The analysis assumes a linear-elastic, small-deformation response of the rod material.
The rod is assumed to be massless.
The dropped weight is treated as a point mass.
Energy losses during impact are neglected.
The rod has a length of 1200 mm.
The rod has a circular cross-section with a diameter of 30 mm.
The rod material has a Young's Modulus of 200000 MPa.
A weight of 500 N is dropped from a height of 200 mm onto the end of the rod.
The rod has no pre-existing axial load.
The governing relation is derived by equating the change in potential energy of the weight to the strain energy stored in the rod.
The potential energy lost by the weight is U_p = W * (h + δ_max).
The strain energy gained by the rod is U_s = (σ_max^2 * A * L) / (2 * E).
The maximum dynamic stress in the rod shall be calculated.
The maximum dynamic elongation of the rod shall be calculated.
