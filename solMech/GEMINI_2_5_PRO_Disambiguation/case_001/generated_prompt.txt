The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The object is a stepped circular bar composed of two segments.
The bar is subjected to a tensile load of 30,000 N.
The first segment has a diameter of 25 mm.
The first segment has a length of 300 mm.
The second segment has a diameter of 35 mm.
The second segment has a length of 1000 mm.
The material for both segments has a Young's Modulus of 200,000 MPa.
The analysis must determine the stress in each segment.
The analysis must determine the total elongation of the bar.
The governing relations for stress (σ = F/A) and elongation (δ = FL/AE) must be presented.
The total elongation is the sum of the elongations of the two segments.
