The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The material is assumed to be steel with a Young's Modulus of 200 GPa.
The material is assumed to be steel with a Poisson's Ratio of 0.3.
The structural member has a rectangular cross-section of 60 mm width and 100 mm height.
The cross-sectional area is 6000 square millimeters.
An axial tension load of 40000 N is applied.
A transverse shear load of 16000 N is applied.
The analysis will determine the state of plane stress based on average stresses.
The average normal stress is calculated as the axial load divided by the cross-sectional area.
The average shear stress is calculated as the shear load divided by the cross-sectional area.
The principal stresses are to be calculated from the average normal and shear stresses.
The principal strains are to be calculated from the principal stresses using the plane stress constitutive relations.
The final report must include the values for average normal stress, average shear stress, the two principal stresses, and the two in-plane principal strains.
