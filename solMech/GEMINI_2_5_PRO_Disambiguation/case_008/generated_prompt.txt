A composite bar system consists of two bars in parallel between two rigid plates.
A total tensile load of 20000 N is applied to the top plate.
The first bar is made of AISI 1020 steel.
The second bar is made of 6061-T6 aluminum.
Both bars have a length of 500 mm.
The cross-sectional area of the steel bar is 300 mm^2.
The cross-sectional area of the aluminum bar is 200 mm^2.
The Young's Modulus for the steel bar is 200000 MPa.
The Young's Modulus for the aluminum bar is 69000 MPa.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic behavior.
The bars are perfectly bonded to the rigid plates.
The load is applied axially and centrally to the system.
The rigid plates remain parallel after deformation.
The self-weight of the bars is negligible.
The governing relations are based on equilibrium, compatibility, and the constitutive law.
The total applied load is the sum of the loads in each bar.
The elongation of the steel bar is equal to the elongation of the aluminum bar.
The elongation of a bar is calculated as (Force * Length) / (Area * Young's Modulus).
