The analysis is for a small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics problem.
The object is a hollow circular shaft.
The shaft has an outer radius of 20 mm.
The shaft has an inner radius of 15 mm.
The shaft has a length of 1000 mm.
The shaft material has a shear modulus of 39179.1 MPa.
The shaft is fixed at one end.
A torque of 300000 N·mm is applied to the free end of the shaft.
The analysis shall determine the maximum shear stress in the shaft.
The analysis shall determine the minimum shear stress in the shaft.
The analysis shall determine the total angle of twist of the free end relative to the fixed end.
