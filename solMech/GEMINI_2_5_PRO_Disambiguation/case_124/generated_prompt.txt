The analysis assumes linear-elastic material behavior and small deformations.
The beam cross-section is rectangular with a height of 100 mm and a width of 40 mm.
The analysis is performed at a section where the internal bending moment is 4,000,000 N·mm (sagging).
The governing relation for bending stress (σ) is the flexure formula: σ = -My/I.
'M' is the internal bending moment, 'y' is the vertical distance from the neutral axis, and 'I' is the second moment of area about the neutral axis.
The coordinate system origin is at the centroid of the cross-section, with the y-axis positive upwards.
The second moment of area (I) for the rectangular cross-section is 3,333,333.33 mm^4.
The bending stress at the neutral axis (y = 0 mm) is 0 MPa.
The bending stress at the top fiber (y = +50 mm) is -60 MPa, indicating compression.
The bending stress at the bottom fiber (y = -50 mm) is +60 MPa, indicating tension.
