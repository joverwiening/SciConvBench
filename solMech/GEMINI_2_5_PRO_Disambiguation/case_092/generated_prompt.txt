The analysis is for a prismatic bar under axial loading.
The bar has a total length of 1400 mm.
The bar is fixed at its left end, designated as point A (x=0).
The bar has a constant cross-sectional area of 500 mm^2.
The material of the bar has a Young's Modulus of 200000 MPa.
A point load of +15000 N is applied at point B, located 700 mm from the fixed end A.
A point load of -12000 N is applied at point C, located 1100 mm from the fixed end A.
A point load of +10000 N is applied at the free right end, designated as point D (x=1400 mm).
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The governing relation for axial stress is sigma = P/A, where P is the internal axial force and A is the cross-sectional area.
The governing relation for axial strain is epsilon = sigma/E, where E is the Young's Modulus.
The governing relation for axial displacement is delta = PL/AE for a segment of length L.
Determine the reaction force at the fixed support A.
Determine the internal axial force in segments AB, BC, and CD.
Determine the axial stress in segments AB, BC, and CD.
Determine the axial displacement at points B, C, and D.
