The analysis objective is to determine the minimum diameter for a solid circular column to prevent buckling under a specified load.
The analysis will also evaluate the factor of safety for the designed column.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The column material is 6061-T6 aluminum.
The Young's Modulus for 6061-T6 aluminum is 68.9 GPa.
The yield strength for 6061-T6 aluminum is 276 MPa.
The column has a length of 2200 mm.
The column has an effective length factor K of 2.0.
The column is subjected to a compressive axial load of 25000 N.
The required factor of safety against buckling is 2.5.
The governing relation is Euler's critical load formula for buckling.
The minimum required diameter for the solid circular column is 77.6 mm.
The factor of safety with respect to buckling for the designed diameter is 2.5.
