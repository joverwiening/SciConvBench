The analysis is for a rod under uniaxial tension.
The analysis assumes small-deformation, linear-elastic, and isotropic material behavior.
The lateral load is zero.
The material's Young's Modulus is 105000 MPa.
The material's Poisson's Ratio is 0.34.
The initial length of the rod is 500 mm.
The initial diameter of the rod is 30 mm.
An axial tensile load of 25000 N is applied.
The governing relation for axial stress is the applied force divided by the initial cross-sectional area.
The governing relation for axial strain is the axial stress divided by the Young's Modulus.
The governing relation for lateral strain is the negative product of the Poisson's Ratio and the axial strain.
The final length is the initial length plus the product of axial strain and initial length.
The final diameter is the initial diameter plus the product of lateral strain and initial diameter.
The resulting axial stress is 35.37 MPa.
The resulting axial strain is 0.0003368.
The resulting lateral strain is -0.0001145.
The final length of the rod is 500.168 mm.
