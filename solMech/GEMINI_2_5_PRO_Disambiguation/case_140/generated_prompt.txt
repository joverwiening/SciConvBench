The analysis objective is to determine the stress and strain state of a circular shaft under combined loading.
The analysis shall assume linear-elastic, small-deformation solid mechanics.
The material is assumed to be homogeneous and isotropic.
The material Young's Modulus is 200 GPa.
The material Poisson's Ratio is 0.3.
The shaft has a circular cross-section with a diameter of 45 mm.
A bending moment of 2,000,000 N·mm is applied to the shaft.
A torsional moment of 500,000 N·mm is applied to the shaft.
The analysis shall be performed at a point on the outer surface of the shaft where stresses are maximal.
Stress concentrations are to be neglected.
Calculate the maximum normal stress due to bending.
Calculate the maximum shear stress due to torsion.
Calculate the principal stresses based on the combined stress state.
Calculate the maximum in-plane shear stress.
Calculate the principal strains based on the principal stresses and material properties.
Present the governing equations used for all calculations.
Report the final values for the maximum bending stress, maximum torsional stress, principal stresses, maximum shear stress, and principal strains.
