Q: What is the direction of the applied shear force V relative to the adhesive layer - is it applied parallel to the 4000 mm² faces (causing shear between the top and bottom faces), or is it applied to one of the edges of the layer?
A: The shear force V is applied parallel to the 4000 mm² faces, causing shear between the top and bottom faces of the layer. This creates the in-plane shear that results in relative lateral displacement between the two faces.
