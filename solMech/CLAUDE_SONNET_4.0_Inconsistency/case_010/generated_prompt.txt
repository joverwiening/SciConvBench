Use N-mm-MPa units throughout the analysis.
Two prismatic bars of equal length L = 800 mm are mounted in parallel between two rigid plates.
Bar 1 is structural steel with cross-sectional area A1 = 150 mm² and elastic modulus E1 = 200000 MPa.
Bar 2 is cast iron with cross-sectional area A2 = 200 mm² and elastic modulus E2 = 110000 MPa.
A total tensile load P = 30000 N is applied to the top plate.
The bars are perfectly bonded to the rigid plates so they undergo identical elongation.
Apply the equilibrium condition P1 + P2 = 30000 N where P1 and P2 are the forces in bars 1 and 2 respectively.
Apply the compatibility condition δ1 = δ2 where δ1 and δ2 are the elongations of bars 1 and 2 respectively.
Use the relationship δ = PL/(EA) for each bar's elongation.
Solve the system of equations to determine P1 and P2.
Calculate the stress in each bar using σ = P/A.
Calculate the common elongation using δ = PL/(EA) for either bar.
Present the final results for force in each bar, stress in each bar, and common elongation.
