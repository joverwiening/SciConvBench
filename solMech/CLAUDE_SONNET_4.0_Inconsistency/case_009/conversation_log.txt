Q: What is the specific calculation method you want me to use to determine how the total load P is distributed between the two bars - should I use the compatibility condition where both bars have equal elongation, or should I split the load equally as mentioned in your last sentence?
A: Use the compatibility condition where both bars have equal elongation. The bars are in parallel and bonded to rigid plates, so they must deform by the same amount. This means the load distribution depends on the relative stiffness of each bar, not an equal split.
