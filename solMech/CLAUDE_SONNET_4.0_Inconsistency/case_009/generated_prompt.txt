Determine the force carried by each bar, the stress in each bar, and the common elongation of two parallel prismatic bars using compatibility conditions.

Bar 1 is AISI 1020 steel with length L = 400 mm, cross-sectional area A1 = 300 mm², and elastic modulus E1 = 200000 MPa.

Bar 2 is cast iron with length L = 400 mm, cross-sectional area A2 = 250 mm², and elastic modulus E2 = 110000 MPa.

Both bars are perfectly bonded to rigid plates and undergo the same elongation due to compatibility.

A total tensile load P = 20000 N is applied to the system.

Calculate the axial stiffness of each bar as k = EA/L.

Use the compatibility condition that both bars have equal elongation to determine load distribution.

Apply equilibrium condition that P1 + P2 = P = 20000 N.

Solve for the force P1 in bar 1 and force P2 in bar 2.

Calculate the stress in bar 1 as σ1 = P1/A1.

Calculate the stress in bar 2 as σ2 = P2/A2.

Calculate the common elongation as δ = P1L/(E1A1) or δ = P2L/(E2A2).

Express all results using N-mm-MPa units.
