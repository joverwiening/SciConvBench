Calculate the midspan deflection of a simply supported beam using superposition of two loading cases.
Use N-mm-MPa units throughout the calculation.
Apply Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.
The beam has span L = 3500 mm.
The beam is made of structural steel with E = 200000 MPa.
The beam has constant moment of inertia I = 3000000 mm^4.
The beam carries a uniform distributed load w = 0.3 N/mm over the full span.
The beam carries a downward point load P = 1200 N at midspan.
For the distributed load contribution, replace the uniform load with its resultant force wL acting as a point load at midspan.
Calculate the deflection from the original point load P using δ = PL³/(48EI).
Calculate the deflection from the equivalent point load wL using δ = PL³/(48EI).
Add the two deflections together to get the total midspan deflection by superposition.
Present the final answer as the total midspan deflection in mm.
