Calculate the midspan deflection of a simply supported beam using superposition of two loading cases.
Use N-mm-MPa units throughout the calculation.
Apply Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.
The beam has span L = 3000 mm.
The beam is made of AISI 1020 steel with E = 200000 MPa.
The beam has constant moment of inertia I = 3000000 mm^4.
The beam carries a uniform distributed load w = 0.3 N/mm over the full span.
The beam carries a downward point load P = 1200 N at midspan.
Replace the uniform distributed load with its resultant force wL acting at midspan.
Calculate the deflection contribution from the equivalent point load wL using δ = PL³/(48EI).
Calculate the deflection contribution from the actual point load P = 1200 N using δ = PL³/(48EI).
Add the two deflection contributions to obtain the total midspan deflection.
Report the final deflection value in mm.
