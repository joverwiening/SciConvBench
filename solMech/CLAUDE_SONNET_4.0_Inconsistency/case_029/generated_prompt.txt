Calculate the mechanical response of a brass rod under uniaxial tension using standard linear elastic analysis.

Use N-mm-MPa units throughout the analysis.

The brass rod has initial diameter d0 = 20 mm and length L = 1000 mm.

Apply axial tensile load P = 25000 N through the centroid.

Use material properties E = 105000 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.34.

Assume linear elastic isotropic behavior.

Compute the average axial normal stress as P divided by the initial cross-sectional area.

Compute the axial strain using Hooke's law relating stress and strain.

Compute the lateral strain using the Poisson's ratio relationship with axial strain.

Compute the change in diameter using the lateral strain and initial diameter.

Present results for: (i) average axial normal stress, (ii) axial strain, (iii) lateral strain, and (iv) change in diameter.
