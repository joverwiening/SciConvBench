Use N–mm–MPa units throughout the analysis.
Analyze a simply supported beam with span L = 3000 mm with supports at x = 0 and x = L.
The beam is made of AISI 1020 steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa and second moment of area I = 1200000 mm^4.
Apply a point load P = 800 N acting downward at x = 2000 mm.
For the real system, determine the bending moment diagram M(x) due to the 800 N downward load.
For the auxiliary system, apply a unit moment of 1 N·mm in the clockwise direction at x = 2000 mm to generate the moment diagram m(x).
Treat the simple supports as zero-rotation boundaries in both the real and auxiliary systems.
Use the unit-load method formula δ = ∫[0→L] (M(x)·m(x))/(EI) dx to compute the deflection.
Calculate the deflection at x = 2000 mm where the load is applied.
The clockwise unit moment assumption follows standard convention for generating auxiliary moment diagrams that correspond to downward deflections in beam analysis.
