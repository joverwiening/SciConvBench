Calculate the mechanical response of a brass rod under axial tension with plane strain constraint.

Given parameters: initial diameter d0 = 30 mm, length L = 500 mm, axial tensile load P = 25000 N applied through centroid, elastic modulus E = 105000 MPa, reference Poisson's ratio ν = 0.34.

Use N-mm-MPa units throughout the analysis.

Assume linear elastic isotropic material behavior.

Apply plane strain constraint in both transverse directions such that lateral strain equals zero.

Assume the zero lateral strain is enforced by a rigid cylindrical constraint that prevents radial deformation while allowing axial deformation.

Compute the average axial normal stress as P divided by the initial cross-sectional area.

Compute the axial strain using the appropriate stress-strain relationship that accounts for the plane strain constraint.

Compute the lateral strain which is zero by the given constraint.

Compute the change in diameter which is zero due to the zero lateral strain constraint.

Present all results with appropriate units and numerical values.
