Calculate the mechanical response of a 6061-T6 aluminum rod under axial tension with plane strain constraints.

Use N-mm-MPa units throughout the analysis.

The rod has initial diameter d0 = 40 mm and length L = 500 mm.

Apply axial tensile load P = 35000 N through the centroid.

Use material properties E = 69000 MPa and reference Poisson's ratio ν = 0.33.

Assume linear elastic isotropic material behavior.

Apply plane strain constraint in both transverse directions, setting lateral strain to zero.

For the plane strain analysis, use the standard approach where the effective Poisson's ratio becomes ν_eff = ν/(1-ν) to account for the constraint that prevents lateral deformation.

Calculate the average axial normal stress as σ = P/A where A is the initial cross-sectional area.

Calculate the axial strain using Hooke's law with the applied stress and given elastic modulus.

Calculate the lateral strain as zero due to the plane strain constraint.

Calculate the change in diameter as Δd = 0 since lateral strain is constrained to zero.

Present results for: (i) average axial normal stress, (ii) axial strain, (iii) lateral strain, and (iv) change in diameter.
