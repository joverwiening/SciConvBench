A motor delivers power P = 5 kW to a solid circular shaft rotating at 1200 rpm. The shaft is made of AISI 1020 steel with shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa. The shaft diameter is d = 20 mm and its length between fixed ends is L = 1000 mm.

(a) Compute the transmitted torque T using P = T·ω, where ω = 2π(rpm/60).
(b) Using torsion theory, compute the maximum shear stress in the shaft.
(c) Compute the angle of twist over the length L.
