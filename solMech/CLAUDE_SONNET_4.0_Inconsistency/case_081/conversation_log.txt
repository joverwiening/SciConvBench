Q: What specific method or formula do you want to use to compute the principal stresses from the plane stress state?
A: Use the standard principal stress formulas for plane stress:

σ₁,₂ = (σₓ + σᵧ)/2 ± √[((σₓ - σᵧ)/2)² + τₓᵧ²]

And for maximum in-plane shear stress:

τₘₐₓ = √[((σₓ - σᵧ)/2)² + τₓᵧ²]
