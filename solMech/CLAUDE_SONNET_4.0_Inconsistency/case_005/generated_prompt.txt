The bar has constant cross-sectional area A = 200 mm^2 and is made of structural steel with E = 200000 MPa.
The bar segments have lengths AB = 700 mm, BC = 600 mm, and CD = 500 mm.
Point A is fixed with displacement u_A = 0.
External forces are applied as follows: 18000 N at point B (rightward), -8000 N at point C (leftward), and 20000 N at point D (rightward).
No external force is applied at point A.
Determine the internal axial force in each segment AB, BC, and CD using equilibrium analysis.
Calculate the normal stress in each segment using stress = internal force / cross-sectional area.
Calculate the displacement of point D relative to point A using the deformation of each segment.
Use the formula for axial deformation: delta = (internal force × length) / (E × area) for each segment.
Sum the deformations of all segments to find the total displacement of point D.
Present results in N-mm-MPa units as specified.
