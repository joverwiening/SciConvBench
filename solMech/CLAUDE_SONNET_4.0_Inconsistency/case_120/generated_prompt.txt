Calculate the thermal stress in a constrained AISI 1020 steel rod using σ = E × α × ΔT.
Use the given material properties: E = 200000 MPa, α = 2e-05 1/°C, and ΔT = 50 °C.
The rod has length L = 800 mm and diameter d = 20 mm.
The rod is placed between two rigid, unyielding supports that prevent any change in total length.
Determine the thermal stress magnitude and indicate whether it is tensile or compressive.
Calculate the corresponding axial force using F = σ × A, where A is the cross-sectional area.
Use N-mm-MPa units throughout the calculations.
The cross-sectional area is A = π × (d/2)² = π × (10)² = 314.16 mm².
Since the rod wants to expand due to heating but is constrained, the resulting thermal stress will be compressive.
Report the final thermal stress in MPa and the axial force in N.
