Q: What specific calculation method or approach would you like me to use to determine the thermal stress - should I calculate it based on the constrained thermal expansion using σ = E × α × ΔT, or do you want me to follow a different analytical approach?
A: Use the standard approach for constrained thermal expansion: σ = E × α × ΔT. This is the correct method for determining thermal stress when a rod is prevented from expanding due to temperature change.
