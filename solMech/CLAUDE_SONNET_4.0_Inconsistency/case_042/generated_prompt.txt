Calculate the maximum stress in a prismatic steel bar subjected to a suddenly applied constant load using energy methods.

Given parameters: L = 800 mm, A = 300 mm², E = 200000 MPa, P = 8000 N.

Use N-mm-MPa units throughout the calculation.

Assume linear elastic axial response for the bar material.

Apply the principle that work done by the suddenly applied load equals strain energy stored in the bar at maximum displacement.

Calculate the work done by the load as W = P × δ_max, where δ_max is the maximum displacement.

Calculate the strain energy stored in the bar as U = (1/2) × k × δ_max², where k is the axial stiffness.

Determine the axial stiffness as k = EA/L.

Set work done equal to strain energy: P × δ_max = (1/2) × k × δ_max².

Solve for maximum displacement: δ_max = 2P/k.

Calculate maximum stress using σ_max = E × ε_max = E × (δ_max/L).

Compare the result to the static stress P/A to verify the dynamic amplification factor.

Express the final answer for maximum stress in MPa.
