Calculate the elongation of a steel bar using the axial deformation integral.
Use N-mm-MPa units throughout the calculation.
The bar has length L = 500 mm.
Apply a constant axial tensile force P = 12000 N.
Use AISI 1020 steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa (treat as constant).
The cross-sectional area varies as A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L) where x is measured from the left end.
Use A0 = 200 mm^2 and k = 1.0.
Substitute the varying area function A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L) into the integral δ = ∫[0→L] P/(A(x)E) dx.
Evaluate the integral from x = 0 to x = L to find the total elongation δ.
Express the final answer for elongation in millimeters.
