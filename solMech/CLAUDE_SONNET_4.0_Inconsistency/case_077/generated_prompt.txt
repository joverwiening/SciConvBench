Calculate the minimum required diameter for a solid circular shaft.
The shaft has length L = 800 mm.
The shaft is made of AISI 1020 steel with shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa.
The shaft must transmit torque T = 600000 N·mm.
The angle of twist is limited to φ_allow = 1.5° over the length L.
Convert the angle of twist limit from degrees to radians using φ_allow = 1.5° × (π/180) = 0.02618 radians.
Use the angle of twist formula φ = TL/(JG).
Use the polar moment of inertia formula J = π d^4/32 for a solid circular shaft.
Substitute the polar moment formula into the angle of twist equation to get φ = TL/((π d^4/32)G) = 32TL/(π d^4 G).
Rearrange to solve for diameter: d^4 = 32TL/(π φ_allow G).
Calculate d = (32TL/(π φ_allow G))^(1/4).
Substitute the given values: T = 600000 N·mm, L = 800 mm, φ_allow = 0.02618 radians, G = 76923.1 MPa.
Report the minimum required diameter in mm.
