The bar has a constant cross-sectional area A = 300 mm^2 and is made of AISI 1020 steel with E = 200000 MPa.
The bar segments have lengths AB = 500 mm, BC = 600 mm, and CD = 400 mm.
Point A is fixed with displacement u_A = 0.
Applied forces are 18000 N at point B, -12000 N at point C, and 16000 N at point D.
No external force is applied at point A.
Use equilibrium to find the reaction force at A and internal forces in each segment.
Calculate internal axial forces using force equilibrium for each segment.
Determine normal stress in each segment using stress = internal force / cross-sectional area.
Calculate displacement of point D using the relation δ = (P × L) / (A × E) for each segment.
Sum the displacements of all segments to find the total displacement of point D relative to A.
Use N-mm-MPa units throughout all calculations.
