Calculate the maximum shear stress at the outer surface of a hollow circular brass shaft.
Calculate the angle of twist over the length of the shaft.
Use N-mm-MPa units throughout the analysis.
Assume linear elasticity for all calculations.
The shaft has outer diameter do = 40 mm and inner diameter di = 30 mm.
The wall thickness is 5 mm.
The shaft length is L = 1000 mm.
The applied torque is T = 300000 N·mm.
Model the shaft as a thin-walled closed tube using mean-area shear-flow relation.
Use mean radius rm = 17.5 mm for the thin-walled tube approximation.
Use the polar moment of inertia J = π(do^4 − di^4)/32.
Use shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.
Apply the thin-walled tube shear stress formula τ = T/(2·A·t) where A is the mean area and t is the wall thickness.
Apply the thin-walled tube twist formula φ = T·L/(G·J) for angle of twist calculations.
Calculate the mean area A using the mean radius rm = 17.5 mm.
Report the maximum shear stress in MPa.
Report the angle of twist in radians.
