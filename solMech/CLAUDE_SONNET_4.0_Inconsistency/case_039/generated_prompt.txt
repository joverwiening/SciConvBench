Calculate the mechanical response of an AISI 1020 steel rod under axial tension with plane strain constraints.

Use N-mm-MPa units throughout all calculations.

The rod has initial diameter d0 = 20 mm and length L = 1200 mm.

Apply axial tensile load P = 35000 N through the centroid.

Use material properties E = 200000 MPa and reference Poisson's ratio ν = 0.30.

Apply plane strain constraint in both transverse directions, setting lateral strain to zero.

Calculate the average axial normal stress as P divided by initial cross-sectional area.

Calculate the axial strain using the standard stress-strain relationship for the constrained condition.

Set the lateral strain to zero due to the plane strain constraint.

Calculate the change in diameter as zero since lateral strain is constrained to zero.

Present results for: (i) average axial normal stress, (ii) axial strain, (iii) lateral strain, and (iv) change in diameter.
