Calculate the maximum shear stress at the outer surface of a hollow circular shaft using both thin-walled tube theory and exact torsion theory.
Calculate the angle of twist over the shaft length using both methods.
The shaft is made of AISI 1020 steel with outer diameter do = 60 mm and inner diameter di = 48 mm.
The shaft length is L = 800 mm.
The applied torque is T = 450000 N·mm.
Use linear elasticity assumptions throughout the analysis.
Use N-mm-MPa units for all calculations.
For the thin-walled tube approximation, use mean radius rm = 27 mm.
For the thin-walled analysis, apply the shear flow relation q = T/(2Am) where Am is the mean area.
Calculate the thin-walled shear stress as τ = q/t where t is the wall thickness of 6 mm.
For the exact solution, use the polar moment of inertia J = π(do^4 - di^4)/32.
Calculate the exact maximum shear stress using τmax = T·ro/J where ro is the outer radius.
Calculate the angle of twist using φ = TL/(GJ) where G = 76923.1 MPa.
Compare results from both the thin-walled approximation and exact torsion theory.
Present the maximum shear stress and angle of twist as the final answers.
