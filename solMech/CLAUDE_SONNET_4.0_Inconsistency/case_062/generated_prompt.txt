Calculate the thermal stress in the constrained rod using σ = -EαΔT.
Substitute the given values: E = 200000 MPa, α = 1e-05 1/°C, and ΔT = 35 °C.
The thermal stress equals -200000 × 1e-05 × 35 = -70 MPa.
The negative sign indicates the stress is compressive.
Calculate the cross-sectional area of the rod using A = πd²/4.
Substitute the diameter d = 20 mm to get A = π × (20)²/4 = 314.16 mm².
Calculate the axial force using F = σA.
The axial force equals -70 × 314.16 = -21991 N.
The negative sign indicates the force is compressive.
Report the thermal stress as 70 MPa compressive.
Report the axial force as 21991 N compressive or 22.0 kN compressive.
