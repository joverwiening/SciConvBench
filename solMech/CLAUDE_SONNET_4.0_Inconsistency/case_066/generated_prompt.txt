Calculate the maximum shear stress and angle of twist for a solid circular steel shaft.
Use N-mm-MPa units throughout the analysis.
The shaft has a circular solid cross section with diameter d = 35 mm.
The shaft length is L = 400 mm.
The applied torque is T = 250000 N·mm.
The shaft material is structural steel with shear modulus G = 77519.4 MPa.
Assume the shaft is linearly elastic.
Use the polar moment of inertia J = π d^4/32 for torsion calculations.
Calculate maximum shear stress using τ_max = T c / J where c = d/2.
Calculate the angle of twist using φ = TL/(JG).
Convert the angle of twist from radians to degrees for reporting.
