Q: What is the value of J (the polar second moment of area) that you want to use in the curvature relation κ = M/(EJ)?
A: The complete requirement uses I = 1200000 mm^4, not J. The curvature formula should be κ = M/(EI) where I is the second moment of area about the neutral axis.
