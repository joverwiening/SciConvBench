Calculate the curvature and radius of curvature for a beam under bending.
Use N-mm-MPa units throughout the calculation.
The beam is made of AISI 1020 steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The second moment of area about the neutral axis is I = 1200000 mm^4.
The bending moment at the section is M = 3000000 N·mm.
Apply the curvature relation κ = M/(EI) where κ is the curvature.
Calculate the curvature κ using the given values.
Calculate the radius of curvature ρ using the relationship ρ = 1/κ.
Express the radius of curvature ρ in millimeters.
