Q: What specific calculation method or formula do you want me to use to compute the principal stresses in part (c)?
A: Use the standard plane stress formulas:

σ₁,₂ = (σₓ + σᵧ)/2 ± √[((σₓ - σᵧ)/2)² + τₓᵧ²]

And for maximum in-plane shear stress:

τₘₐₓ = √[((σₓ - σᵧ)/2)² + τₓᵧ²]
