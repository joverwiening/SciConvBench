Use N-mm-MPa units throughout the analysis.
Assume small strains for all calculations.
The adhesive layer is rectangular with area A = 4000 mm^2 and thickness t = 20 mm.
Apply a uniform in-plane shear force V = 12000 N parallel to the adhesive layer faces.
The material is isotropic with shear modulus G = 39179.1 MPa.
Treat the adhesive layer as rigid in shear so the two faces undergo no relative slip during shear deformation.
Compute the average shear stress as τ_avg = V/A.
Compute the average shear strain using γ_avg = τ_avg/G.
Compute the relative lateral displacement between the two faces as Δ = γ_avg·t.
Present results for average shear stress in MPa, average shear strain as dimensionless, and shear deformation in mm.
