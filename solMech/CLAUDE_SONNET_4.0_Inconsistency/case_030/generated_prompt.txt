Calculate the mechanical response of a structural steel rod under axial tension with lateral constraint.

Use N-mm-MPa units throughout all calculations.

The rod has initial diameter d0 = 25 mm and length L = 1200 mm.

Apply axial tensile load P = 45000 N through the centroid.

Use material properties E = 200000 MPa and Poisson's ratio ν = 0.29 as reference values.

Assume the rod is laterally constrained by surrounding material or testing fixture that prevents transverse deformation.

Apply plane strain conditions in both transverse directions so lateral strain equals zero.

Calculate the average axial normal stress as P divided by initial cross-sectional area.

Calculate the axial strain using the stress-strain relationship for the constrained condition.

Set the lateral strain to zero due to the plane strain constraint.

Calculate the change in diameter as zero since lateral strain is zero.

Present results for average axial normal stress in MPa, axial strain as dimensionless, lateral strain as zero, and change in diameter as zero mm.
