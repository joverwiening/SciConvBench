Calculate the curvature and radius of curvature for a structural steel beam using the flexure formula.
Use N-mm-MPa units throughout the calculation.
The beam is made of structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The beam has a constant second moment of area I = 4000000 mm^4 about the neutral axis.
At the specified section, the bending moment is M = 3000000 N·mm.
Use the curvature relation κ = M/(EI) where κ is the curvature.
Calculate the curvature κ in units of 1/mm.
Calculate the radius of curvature ρ using the relationship ρ = 1/κ.
Express the radius of curvature ρ in millimeters.
Assume the beam has a rectangular cross-section as this is the most common assumption when cross-sectional shape is not specified.
Note that the problem statement mentions polar second moment but this is incorrect for bending analysis where the area moment of inertia I is used.
