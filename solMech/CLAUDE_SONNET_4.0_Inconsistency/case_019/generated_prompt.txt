The shaft is a solid circular shaft with diameter d = 30 mm and total length L = 1200 mm.
The shaft is fixed at x = 0.
A torque T1 = 200000 N·mm is applied at the free end x = L in the positive sense.
A torque T2 = -200000 N·mm is applied at x = 400 mm in the negative sense.
The material is AISI 1020 steel with shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa.
The shaft has constant diameter throughout its length.
Use a single internal torque equal to the algebraic sum of applied torques over the full length for both stress and twist calculations.
The shaft has two segments: segment 1 from x = 0 to x = 400 mm, and segment 2 from x = 400 mm to x = 1200 mm.
For part (a), determine the internal torque in segment 1 (0 to 400 mm) and segment 2 (400 mm to 1200 mm).
For part (b), calculate the maximum shear stress in each segment using the internal torques from part (a).
For part (c), calculate the angle of twist at x = L relative to the fixed end by summing the twist contributions from both segments.
All calculations use N-mm-MPa units throughout.
