Calculate the angle of twist for a solid circular shaft using torsional strain energy and Castigliano's theorem.
Use N-mm-MPa units throughout the calculation.
The shaft has diameter d = 30 mm and length L = 1000 mm.
The shaft is made of 6061-T6 aluminum with shear modulus G = 25939.8 MPa.
The shaft is subjected to constant torque T = 450000 N·mm.
Use the polar moment of inertia J = πd⁴/32.
Apply the torsional strain energy formula U = ∫[0→L] T²/(2GJ) dx.
Use Castigliano's theorem where the angle of twist φ = ∂U/∂T.
Compute the angle of twist between the ends of the shaft.
