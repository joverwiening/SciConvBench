Calculate the Euler critical buckling load for a straight column made of AISI 1020 steel.
Use the given material and geometric properties: E = 200000 MPa, I = 1200000 mm^4, L = 1000 mm.
Apply fixed-free (cantilever) end conditions with effective length factor K = 2.0.
Use the formula P_cr = π^2 E I / (K L)^2 for the Euler critical buckling load calculation.
Substitute the values: P_cr = π^2 × 200000 × 1200000 / (2.0 × 1000)^2.
Calculate P_cr = π^2 × 2.4 × 10^11 / (4 × 10^6) = π^2 × 60000 N.
Evaluate P_cr = 9.8696 × 60000 = 592176 N.
Report the result as P_cr = 592176 N = 592.2 kN.
Use N-mm-MPa units throughout the calculation.
Assume the column is sufficiently slender that Euler buckling theory applies without needing to verify the slenderness ratio.
