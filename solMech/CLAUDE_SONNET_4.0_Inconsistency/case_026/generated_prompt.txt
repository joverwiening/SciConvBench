Set up a cantilever beam with length L = 1500 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.

Define the bending moment function as M(x) = P(L-x) where x is measured from the fixed end.
Set up the bending strain energy integral U = ∫[0→L] M(x)^2/(2EI) dx.
Substitute M(x) = P(L-x) into the strain energy expression.
Integrate the expression from 0 to L to find the total strain energy U.
Apply Castigliano's theorem by differentiating the strain energy U with respect to the load P to find the vertical deflection at the free end.
