Analyze a simply supported beam AB with span L = 6000 mm.
Apply a concentrated vertical load P = 20000 N acting downward at 2000 mm from support A.
Use equilibrium equations to determine support reactions first.
Cut the beam at x = 4500 mm from support A.
Apply equilibrium equations to the left portion of the beam.
Use the cut-section sign convention with positive shear downward on the left cut face.
Use positive bending moment as hogging with tension at the top fibers.
Calculate the internal shear force V at x = 4500 mm and express in N.
Calculate the internal bending moment M at x = 4500 mm and express in N·mm.
Note that the cut section is located beyond the point load which is at x = 2000 mm.
