Calculate the Euler critical load using P_cr = π²EI/(KL)².
Use the polar moment of inertia J = πd⁴/32 as the second moment of area I.
Substitute d = 30 mm, L = 1500 mm, E = 200000 MPa, and K = 2.0.
First calculate I = π(30)⁴/32 = π(810000)/32 = 79521.56 mm⁴.
Calculate the effective length KL = 2.0 × 1500 = 3000 mm.
Compute P_cr = π²(200000)(79521.56)/(3000)² = 1742.4 N.
Calculate the allowable load P_allow = P_cr/n where n = 2.0.
Compute P_allow = 1742.4/2.0 = 871.2 N.
Present the final answers as P_cr = 1742.4 N and P_allow = 871.2 N.
