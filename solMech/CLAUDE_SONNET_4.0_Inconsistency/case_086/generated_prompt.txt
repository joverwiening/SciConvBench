Calculate the normal stress and total elongation of a stepped circular bar using analytical mechanics of materials formulas.
The bar is made of structural steel with elastic modulus E = 200000 MPa.
The bar is fixed at the left end and loaded with tensile force P = 25000 N at the right end.
Segment 1 has length L1 = 400 mm and diameter d1 = 16 mm.
Segment 2 has length L2 = 400 mm and diameter d2 = 40 mm.
Use N-mm-MPa units throughout the analysis.
Assume linear-elastic behavior for the material.
Calculate the cross-sectional area of each segment using A = π(d²)/4.
Determine the normal stress in each segment using σ = P/A.
Calculate the elongation of each segment using δ = PL/(AE).
Sum the individual segment elongations to find the total elongation of the bar.
Present results for normal stress in each segment and total elongation between the fixed end and load application point.
