Calculate the angle of twist for a solid circular shaft using Castigliano's theorem.
Use N-mm-MPa units throughout the calculation.
The shaft has diameter d = 25 mm and length L = 1000 mm.
The shaft is made of AISI 1020 steel with shear modulus G = 76923.1 MPa.
Apply a constant torque T = 350000 N·mm along the shaft.
Use the polar moment of inertia J = π d^4/32 for the calculation.
Calculate the torsional strain energy using U = ∫[0→L] T^2/(2GJ) dx.
Apply Castigliano's theorem to find the angle of twist: φ = ∂U/∂T.
