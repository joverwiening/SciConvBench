Calculate the Euler critical buckling load for a straight column made of AISI 1020 steel.
Use the formula P_cr = π^2 E I / (K L)^2.
The column length L is 2000 mm.
The elastic modulus E is 200000 MPa.
The second moment of area I is 1200000 mm^4.
The effective length factor K is 0.7 for fixed-pinned end conditions.
Use N-mm-MPa units throughout the calculation.
Substitute the values into the formula: P_cr = π^2 × 200000 × 1200000 / (0.7 × 2000)^2.
Calculate the denominator: (0.7 × 2000)^2 = 1400^2 = 1960000 mm^2.
Calculate the numerator: π^2 × 200000 × 1200000 = 9.8696 × 200000 × 1200000 = 2.367504 × 10^12 N⋅mm^2.
Divide to get P_cr = 2.367504 × 10^12 / 1960000 = 1208418 N.
Report the result as P_cr = 1208418 N.
Convert to kilonewtons: P_cr = 1208.4 kN.
