Solve the simply supported beam deflection problem using N-mm-MPa units.
The beam has span L = 3000 mm.
The beam is made of AISI 1020 steel with E = 200000 MPa.
The beam has constant moment of inertia I = 3500000 mm^4.
A point load P = 1500 N acts downward at midspan.
Apply Euler-Bernoulli beam theory and neglect shear deformation.
Use the boundary condition that slope vanishes at both supports and at midspan due to symmetry.
Calculate the maximum deflection at midspan.
Calculate the slope at the supports.
Use either the differential equation method with EI v'' = M(x) and boundary conditions or standard beam deflection formulas.
