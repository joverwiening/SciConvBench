Calculate the elongation of a bar using the axial deformation integral with the following specifications.

Use N-mm-MPa units throughout the calculation.

The bar has length L = 1200 mm.

Apply a constant axial tensile force P = 20000 N.

The material is 6061-T6 aluminum with elastic modulus E = 69000 MPa (treat as constant).

The cross-sectional area varies linearly along the length according to A(x) = A0·(1 + k·x/L).

Use A0 = 200 mm^2 and k = 0.6.

Measure x from the left end of the bar.

Calculate the arithmetic-mean area as the average of the areas at the two ends of the bar.

Replace the variable area A(x) with this constant arithmetic-mean area in the calculation.

Use the axial deformation integral δ = ∫[0→L] P/(A(x)E) dx to compute the elongation.

Treat the arithmetic-mean area replacement as exact for this calculation.
