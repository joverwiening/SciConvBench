Q: What specific type of structural relaxation do you want to perform - should the calculation optimize only the atomic positions (ions), only the cell shape and volume, or both the atomic positions and the cell parameters simultaneously?
A: Both the atomic positions and the cell parameters simultaneously. The INCAR should use ISIF = 3, which allows optimization of both ionic positions and cell shape/volume.
