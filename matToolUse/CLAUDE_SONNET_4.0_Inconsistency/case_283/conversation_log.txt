Q: What specific INCAR parameters should be modified from the provided parameter block to enable structural relaxation instead of a single-point energy calculation?
A: You need to change:
- IBRION from -1 to 2 (enables ionic relaxation using conjugate gradient method)
- NSW from 0 to 99 (allows up to 99 ionic steps for relaxation)
