Q: What specific type of structural relaxation do you want to perform - are you looking to relax only the atomic positions, only the cell parameters, or both the atomic positions and cell parameters simultaneously?
A: Both the atomic positions and cell parameters simultaneously. The INCAR parameters specify IBRION = 2 and ISIF = 3, which enables relaxation of both ionic positions and cell shape/volume.
