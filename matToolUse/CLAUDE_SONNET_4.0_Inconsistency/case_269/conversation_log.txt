Q: What specific type of structural relaxation do you want to perform - are you looking to relax only the atomic positions, only the cell volume, or both the atomic positions and cell shape/volume?
A: Both the atomic positions and cell shape/volume. The INCAR parameters specify ISIF = 3, which relaxes ionic positions, cell shape, and cell volume.
