The calculation will determine the density of states for CoSb3.
The workflow will consist of the following steps: vc-relax, scf, nscf, pw.x with calculation="bands", band post-processing, and DOS post-processing.
The DOS calculation will use results from the nscf step.
LDA pseudopotentials from PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/co.upf and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/sb.upf will be used.
A primitive starting structure consistent with formula=CoSb3, structure=skutterudite, space_group=Im-3, and atoms_per_primitive_cell=8 will be retrieved using a structure-retrieval tool.
The vc-relax calculation will use an energy convergence threshold (etot_conv_thr) of 1.0e-5 Ry.
The vc-relax calculation will use a force convergence threshold (forc_conv_thr) of 1.0e-3 Ry/Bohr.
The scf and nscf calculations will use an energy cutoff for wavefunctions (ecutwfc) of 70 Ry.
The scf and nscf calculations will use an energy cutoff for charge density (ecutrho) of 280 Ry.
The scf calculation will use a gamma-centered k-point grid of 8x8x8.
The nscf calculation will use a gamma-centered k-point grid of 16x16x16.
The scf and nscf calculations will use Marzari-Vanderbilt smearing (meth='mv').
The scf and nscf calculations will use a degauss value of 0.01 Ry.
The scf and nscf calculations will use a starting_magnetization of 0.0 for all species.
The scf calculation will use a self-consistency convergence threshold (conv_thr) of 1.0e-8 Ry.
The pw.x calculation with calculation="bands" will be performed after the nscf step.
Band post-processing will be performed.
DOS post-processing will be performed using the results from the nscf step.
