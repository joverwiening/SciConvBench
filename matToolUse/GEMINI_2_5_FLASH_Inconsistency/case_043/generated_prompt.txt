The calculation will use the Quantum ESPRESSO software suite.
A primitive starting structure for AlN in the wurtzite phase (space group P6_3mc, 4 atoms per primitive cell) will be retrieved from a structure database.
The workflow consists of six sequential steps: vc-relax, scf, nscf, pw.x with calculation="bands", band post-processing, and DOS post-processing.
The vc-relax calculation will perform a variable-cell relaxation.
The scf calculation will perform a self-consistent field calculation.
The nscf calculation will perform a non-self-consistent field calculation.
The pw.x calculation with calculation="bands" will compute band energies along high-symmetry lines.
Band post-processing will be performed, but the band structure itself does not need to be returned.
DOS post-processing will be performed using data from the nscf calculation.
Local Density Approximation (LDA) pseudopotentials will be used, specifically PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/al.upf and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/n.upf.
The energy convergence threshold for vc-relax (etot_conv_thr) will be set to 4 meV.
The force convergence threshold for vc-relax (forc_conv_thr) will be set to 0.001 Ry/Bohr.
The self-consistency convergence threshold for scf and nscf (conv_thr) will be set to 1.0e-9 Ry.
The plane-wave cutoff energy (ecutwfc) will be set to 80 Ry.
The charge density cutoff energy (ecutrho) will be set to 320 Ry.
For the vc-relax and scf calculations, a gamma-centered k-point grid of 6x6x4 will be used.
For the nscf calculation, a gamma-centered k-point grid of 12x12x8 will be used.
Occupations will be determined using Gaussian smearing with a degauss value of 0.005 Ry for all calculations.
