The task is to calculate the density of states for BiFeO3.
The calculation uses LDA pseudopotentials from PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/bi.upf, PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/fe.upf, and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/o.upf.
A primitive starting structure consistent with formula=BiFeO3, structure=perovskite, space_group=R3c, and atoms_per_primitive_cell=10 will be retrieved using an available structure-retrieval tool.
The workflow consists of the following steps: 1) vc-relax, 2) scf, 3) nscf, 4) pw.x with calculation="bands", 5) band post-processing, and 6) DOS post-processing.
The plane-wave cutoff energy (ecutwfc) is 80 Ry.
The energy convergence threshold (etot_conv_thr) is 1.0e-5 Ry.
The force convergence threshold (forc_conv_thr) is 1.0e-4 Ry/Bohr.
The self-consistency convergence threshold (conv_thr) is 1.0e-8 Ry.
A gamma-centered k-point grid of 6x6x6 is used for the vc-relax and scf calculations.
A gamma-centered k-point grid of 12x12x12 is used for the nscf calculation.
The overall energy convergence for the calculation must meet a strict requirement of 1 meV/atom.
The band structure does not need to be explicitly returned.
