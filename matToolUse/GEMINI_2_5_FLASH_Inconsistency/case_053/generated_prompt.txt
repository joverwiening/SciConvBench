The calculation will use LDA pseudopotentials from PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/ga.upf and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/n.upf.
If a structure-retrieval tool is available, retrieve a primitive starting structure for GaN consistent with wurtzite structure, space group P6_3mc, and 4 atoms per primitive cell. If not, report that the local inputs do not contain enough structure data to start the calculation.
The plane-wave energy cutoff (ecutwfc) will be 70 Ry.
Gaussian smearing will be used with a degauss value of 0.01 Ry.
The workflow will be executed exactly as follows: 1) vc-relax, 2) scf, 3) nscf, 4) pw.x with calculation="bands", 5) band post-processing, and 6) DOS post-processing.
For the vc-relax step, the total energy convergence threshold (etot_conv_thr) will be 1.0e-4 Ry, and the force convergence threshold (forc_conv_thr) will be 1.0e-3 Ry/Bohr.
For the scf step, the total energy convergence threshold (etot_conv_thr) will be 1.0e-4 Ry, and the self-consistency convergence threshold (conv_thr) will be 1.0e-8 Ry.
For vc-relax and scf calculations, an automatic Monkhorst-Pack k-point grid of 8x8x6 will be used, centered at Gamma with a half shift (0 0 0 1 1 1).
For the nscf calculation, an automatic Monkhorst-Pack k-point grid of 16x16x10 will be used, centered at Gamma with a half shift (0 0 0 1 1 1).
The number of bands (nbnd) for nscf and bands calculations will be 16.
For the pw.x calculation="bands" step, k-points will be sampled along the high-symmetry path Gamma-M-K-Gamma-A, with 20 points along each segment.
For DOS post-processing, an energy resolution (deltaE) of 0.02 eV will be used.
The final output will be the density of states for the material.
