The task is to calculate the density of states for Fe3O4.
The calculation will follow a workflow of vc-relax, scf, nscf, pw.x with calculation="bands", band post-processing, and DOS post-processing.
The calculation will use LDA pseudopotentials from PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/fe.upf and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/o.upf.
The plane-wave cutoff energy (ecutwfc) will be 80 Ry.
The total energy convergence threshold (etot_conv_thr) for vc-relax will be 5.1449e-4 Ry.
The force convergence threshold (forc_conv_thr) for vc-relax will be 0.001 Ry/Bohr.
The self-consistency convergence threshold (conv_thr) for scf will be 1.0e-8 Ry.
The k-point sampling will use a gamma-centered automatic Monkhorst-Pack grid with a half shift and a 6x6x6 density.
If a structure-retrieval tool is available, a primitive starting structure consistent with formula=Fe3O4, structure=ferrimagnetic, space_group=Fd-3m, and atoms_per_primitive_cell=7 will be retrieved and used as the starting configuration.
If a structure-retrieval tool is not available, the calculation will report that local inputs do not contain enough structure data to start the calculation.
