The calculation is a variable-cell relaxation for Cr2O3.
A 5-atom cell containing 2 Cr and 3 O atoms will be used as the starting structure.
The initial atomic positions and lattice vectors will be derived from an experimental reference structure for Cr2O3 with space group R-3c.
The calculation will be performed with P1 symmetry.
The calculation is spin-polarized.
An initial antiferromagnetic ordering is used, with a starting magnetic moment of +3.0 on the first Cr atom and -3.0 on the second Cr atom.
The LDA pseudopotentials PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/cr.upf and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/o.upf are used.
A gamma-centered k-point grid is used.
The plane-wave kinetic energy cutoff (ecutwfc) and k-point grid density are chosen to converge the total energy to within 1 meV/atom.
The charge density cutoff (ecutrho) is set to 4 times the plane-wave kinetic energy cutoff.
The convergence threshold for forces is 1.0e-4 Ry/Bohr.
The convergence threshold for total energy during the relaxation is 2.0e-5 Ry.
The electronic self-consistency convergence threshold is 1.0e-8 Ry.
The final relaxed structure, including cell parameters and atomic positions, is the primary output.
