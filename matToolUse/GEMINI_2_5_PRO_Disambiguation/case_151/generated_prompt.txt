Perform a variable-cell relaxation calculation for Na3Bi.
Use the experimental crystal structure for Na3Bi with space group P6_3/mmc as the starting configuration.
Use the LDA pseudopotentials PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/na.upf and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/bi.upf.
Include spin-orbit coupling in the calculation.
Determine the plane-wave kinetic energy cutoff by converging the total energy to a threshold of 0.1 meV/atom.
Set the charge density cutoff to be four times the plane-wave kinetic energy cutoff.
Use a gamma-centered k-point grid.
Determine the k-point grid density by converging the total energy to a threshold of 0.1 meV/atom.
Use Marzari-Vanderbilt smearing for the electronic occupations with a smearing width of 0.01 Ry.
Set the electronic self-consistency convergence threshold to 1.0E-10 Ry per cell.
Converge the structure until the maximum force on any atom is below 1.0E-4 Ry/Bohr.
Converge the structure until the pressure on the cell is below 0.1 kbar.
The final output is the fully relaxed structure, including lattice parameters and atomic coordinates.
