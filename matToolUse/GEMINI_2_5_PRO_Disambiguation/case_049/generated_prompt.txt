The task is to calculate the density of states for Al2O3.
The material structure is corundum with space group R-3c and 5 atoms per primitive cell.
Use the LDA pseudopotentials from PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard for Al and O.
The plane-wave kinetic energy cutoff for wavefunctions will be 80 Ry.
The plane-wave kinetic energy cutoff for charge density will be 320 Ry.
Use Methfessel-Paxton smearing with a width of 0.01 Ry.
The electronic convergence threshold will be 1.0e-8 Ry.
The first step is a variable-cell relaxation.
The vc-relax step uses a gamma-centered 8x8x8 k-point grid.
The vc-relax energy convergence threshold is 1.0e-5 Ry.
The vc-relax force convergence threshold is 1.0e-4 Ry/au.
The second step is a self-consistent field calculation on the relaxed structure.
The scf step uses a gamma-centered 8x8x8 k-point grid.
The third step is a non-self-consistent field calculation.
The nscf step uses a gamma-centered 16x16x16 k-point grid.
The fourth step is a bands calculation.
The k-point path for the bands calculation will follow the standard high-symmetry lines for the crystal structure.
The fifth step is band structure post-processing.
The sixth step is density of states post-processing using the nscf results.
