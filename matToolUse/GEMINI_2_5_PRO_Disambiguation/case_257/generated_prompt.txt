Generate a starting crystal structure for NbSe2 consistent with space group P6_3/mmc.
Use the LDA pseudopotentials PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/nb.upf and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/se.upf.
Determine the plane-wave cutoff energy (ecutwfc) by performing a convergence study on the starting structure to converge the total energy to within 1 meV per atom.
Set the charge density cutoff (ecutrho) to four times the converged ecutwfc.
Using the converged ecutwfc, determine the k-point mesh density by performing a convergence study on the starting structure to converge the total energy to within 1 meV per atom.
The k-point grid must be a Monkhorst-Pack grid with a half shift.
Set the electronic occupations to use Marzari-Vanderbilt smearing with a smearing width (degauss) of 0.01 Ry.
Set the total energy convergence threshold for the SCF cycle (etot_conv_thr) to 1.0e-8 Ry.
Set the electronic convergence threshold for the SCF cycle (conv_thr) to 1.0e-8 Ry.
Set the force convergence threshold for the ionic relaxation (forc_conv_thr) to 1.0e-7 Ry/Bohr.
Perform a variable-cell relaxation (vc-relax) on the starting structure using the converged ecutwfc and k-point grid.
After the vc-relax is complete, perform a self-consistent field (scf) calculation on the final relaxed structure using the same parameters.
The final outputs are the fully relaxed structure and the total energy from the final scf calculation.
