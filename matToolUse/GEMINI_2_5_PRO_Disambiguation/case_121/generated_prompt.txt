Perform a variable-cell relaxation calculation for wurtzite Aluminum Nitride (AlN).
The calculation will use the Local Density Approximation (LDA).
Use the al.upf and n.upf pseudopotential files from PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard.
The initial structure is a wurtzite crystal with space group P6_3mc and 4 atoms per primitive cell.
The initial lattice parameters are a = 3.11 Angstroms and c = 4.98 Angstroms.
The initial atomic positions in fractional coordinates are Al at (1/3, 2/3, 0), N at (1/3, 2/3, 0.382), Al at (2/3, 1/3, 1/2), and N at (2/3, 1/3, 0.882).
Use a plane-wave kinetic energy cutoff (ecutwfc) of 90 Ry.
Use a charge density cutoff (ecutrho) of 720 Ry.
Use a gamma-centered 10x10x6 k-point grid.
Set the total energy convergence threshold to achieve a final energy convergence of 1 meV/atom.
Set the maximum force component convergence threshold to 1.0e-4 Ry/au.
Set the pressure convergence threshold to 0.1 kbar.
Use fixed occupations for the electronic states.
The final output of the calculation must be the fully relaxed structure, including the final cell vectors and atomic positions.
