Generate a starting structure for wurtzite ZnO with space group P6_3mc.
Use the LDA pseudopotentials PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/zn.upf and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/o.upf.
Determine the plane-wave kinetic energy cutoff (ecutwfc) by performing a series of self-consistent field calculations on the starting structure.
For the ecutwfc convergence test, use a fixed, dense, gamma-centered k-point grid of 8x8x6.
The converged ecutwfc is the lowest value for which the total energy is converged to within 1 meV/atom with respect to a denser cutoff.
Determine the k-point sampling grid by performing a series of self-consistent field calculations on the starting structure.
For the k-point convergence test, use the converged ecutwfc from the previous step.
The converged k-point grid is the least dense gamma-centered grid for which the total energy is converged to within 1 meV/atom with respect to a denser grid.
Perform a final variable-cell relaxation (vc-relax) calculation using the starting structure.
Use the converged ecutwfc and the converged k-point grid for the final calculation.
Set the self-consistent field energy convergence threshold to 1.0e-10 Ry.
Set the force convergence threshold to 1.0e-4 Ry/au.
Set the pressure convergence threshold to 0.1 kbar.
The final output is the fully relaxed structure, including lattice vectors and atomic coordinates.
