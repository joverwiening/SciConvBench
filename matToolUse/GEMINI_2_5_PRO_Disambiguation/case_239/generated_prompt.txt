Obtain the initial crystal structure for alpha-quartz (SiO2, space group P3_121) from the Materials Project database.
Use the provided LDA pseudopotentials from PseudoDojo for Si and O.
Determine the plane-wave kinetic energy cutoff (ecutwfc) by converging the total energy to within 1 meV per atom.
Determine the k-point sampling density by converging the total energy to within 1 meV per atom using a Monkhorst-Pack grid.
Set the charge density cutoff (ecutrho) to be 8 times the plane-wave kinetic energy cutoff (ecutwfc).
Perform a variable-cell relaxation (vc-relax) on the initial structure.
Use a total energy convergence threshold (etot_conv_thr) of 1.0e-8 Ry for the vc-relax calculation.
Use a force convergence threshold (forc_conv_thr) of 1.0e-7 Ry/Bohr for the vc-relax calculation.
After the vc-relax is complete, perform a self-consistent field (scf) calculation on the fully relaxed structure.
Use an electronic convergence threshold (conv_thr) of 1.0e-8 Ry for all calculations.
Use an automatic Monkhorst-Pack k-point grid with a half shift for all calculations.
The final outputs are the fully relaxed crystal structure and the total energy from the final scf calculation.
