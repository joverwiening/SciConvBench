Q: I need to clarify one thing before proceeding.

The calculation requirements state "use an automatic Monkhorst-Pack grid with a half shift" and also "keep the grid gamma-centered." Which k-point grid offset should I use — a half-shifted (offset) Monkhorst-Pack grid, or a Gamma-centered (zero-shift) grid?
