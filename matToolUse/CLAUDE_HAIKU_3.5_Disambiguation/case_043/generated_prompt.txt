Specification for AlN Wurtzite Density of States Calculation:

Use the LDA pseudopotential files for Al and N from PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard.
Create a wurtzite AlN structure with space group P6_3mc and 4 atoms per primitive cell.
Perform calculations in the following sequence:
1. vc-relax calculation
2. Self-consistent field (scf) calculation
3. Non-self-consistent field (nscf) calculation
4. Bands calculation using pw.x
5. Band structure post-processing
6. Density of states (DOS) post-processing

Computational parameters:
Plane wave cutoff energy (ecutwfc) = 55 Ry
K-point sampling = 12x12x8 Gamma-centered grid
Total energy convergence threshold (etot_conv_thr) = 0.001 meV/atom (1 meV/atom)
Force convergence threshold (forc_conv_thr) = 0.01 meV/Å
Charge density convergence threshold (conv_thr) = 1e-9
Use LDA pseudopotentials
Use default occupations with Gaussian smearing
Do not apply spin-orbit coupling
Do not apply Hubbard U corrections

Ensure the final calculation produces a complete density of states plot for the AlN wurtzite structure.
