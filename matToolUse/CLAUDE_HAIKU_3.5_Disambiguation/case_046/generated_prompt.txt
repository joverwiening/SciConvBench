Specification for Density of States Calculation for Fe3O4:

Perform the following workflow in order:
1. vc-relax calculation
2. Self-consistent field (scf) calculation
3. Non-self-consistent field (nscf) calculation
4. Bands calculation using pw.x with calculation="bands"
5. Band structure post-processing
6. Density of states (DOS) post-processing

Calculation Parameters:
Use LDA pseudopotentials from PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/fe.upf and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/o.upf.
Use a primitive cell with 7 atoms consistent with the Fe3O4 ferrimagnetic structure in space group Fd-3m.
Use a gamma-centered k-point sampling grid of 8x8x8.
Set plane wave energy cutoff (ecutwfc) to 60 Ry.
Achieve total energy convergence of 1 meV/atom.

Structural Considerations:
Start with a ferrimagnetic Fe3O4 structure in the Fd-3m space group.
Use a primitive cell representation with 7 atoms per cell.

Calculation Workflow:
Perform sequential calculations as listed above, ensuring high-accuracy density-of-states determination.
