Use the Materials Project entry MP‑2035 to obtain the primitive wurtzite AlN structure; record the lattice constants a and c and the fractional atomic coordinates from that entry as the starting configuration.  
Set up a variable‑cell relaxation (vc‑relax) calculation with the LDA pseudopotentials al.upf and n.upf from the PseudoDojo SR_v0.4.1 LDA_standard library.  
Require strict energy convergence of 1 meV per atom, i.e., set etot_conv_thr to 3.7×10⁻⁵ Ry (≈1 meV/atom).  
Set the force convergence threshold (forc_conv_thr) to 1.0×10⁻³ Ry/Bohr.  
Set the SCF convergence threshold (conv_thr) to 1.0×10⁻⁸.  
Choose a plane‑wave kinetic‑energy cutoff (ecutwfc) of 60 Ry and a charge‑density cutoff (ecutrho) of 480 Ry (8 × ecutwfc).  
Use a gamma‑centered Monkhorst‑Pack k‑point grid of 8 × 8 × 6 for the primitive cell.  
Perform the vc‑relax calculation, allowing both atomic positions and cell parameters to relax until the specified energy and force thresholds are met.  
After convergence, output the fully relaxed structure, including the final lattice constants a and c and the relaxed fractional atomic coordinates.
