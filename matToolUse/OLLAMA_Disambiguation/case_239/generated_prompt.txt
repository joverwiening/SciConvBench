Use the Materials Project (or an equivalent crystallographic database such as COD or ICSD) to obtain the primitive α‑quartz SiO₂ structure (space group P3_121, 3 atoms per primitive cell) including lattice parameters a and c and atomic fractional coordinates.  
Perform a variable‑cell relaxation (vc‑relax) on this structure with the following settings:  
- Exchange‑correlation functional: LDA.  
- Pseudopotentials: PseudoDojo SR_v0.4.1 LDA_standard si.upf and o.upf.  
- Plane‑wave kinetic‑energy cutoff (ecutwfc): 40 Ry.  
- Charge‑density cutoff (ecutrho): 320 Ry (8 × ecutwfc).  
- Convergence thresholds: etot_conv_thr = 1.0e‑8 Ry, forc_conv_thr = 1.0e‑7 Ry/Bohr, conv_thr = 1.0e‑8 Ry.

- Cell dynamics: variable‑cell (cell_dofree = “all”).  
- Ion dynamics: BFGS.  

After the vc‑relax calculation converges, take the final relaxed cell and atomic positions and run a self‑consistent field (SCF) calculation with the same pseudopotentials, ecutwfc, ecutrho, k‑point grid, and convergence thresholds to obtain the final total energy.  

Output the fully relaxed crystal structure (lattice vectors and atomic coordinates) and the final SCF total energy.
