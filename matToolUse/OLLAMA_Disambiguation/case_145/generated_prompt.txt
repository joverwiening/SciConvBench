Perform a variable‑cell relaxation (vc‑relax) of orthorhombic SnSe using Quantum ESPRESSO (pw.x) with the following specifications.

Use the LDA pseudopotentials provided:
- Sn: PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/sn.upf  
- Se: PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/se.upf  

Obtain a primitive orthorhombic SnSe structure (space group Pnma, 8 atoms per primitive cell) from any publicly available structure‑retrieval service (e.g., Materials Project, ICSD, COD) that matches the metadata.

Input file settings:
- calculation = 'vc-relax'  
- ibrav = 0 (no symmetry constraints on the cell)  
- cell_dofree = 'all' (allow full cell relaxation)  
- nat = 8, ntyp = 2  
- ecutwfc = 60 Ry (or higher if required by the pseudopotentials)  
- ecutrho = 480 Ry (8 × ecutwfc)  
- occupations = 'smearing'  
- smearing = 'marzari-vanderbilt'  
- degauss = 0.001 Ry (≈0.0136 eV)  
- nosym = .true. (optional, to avoid symmetry constraints during relaxation)  
- k_point_grid = 6 6 6 (gamma‑centered) – adjust upward if convergence tests indicate a need for a denser mesh.  

Convergence thresholds (strict energy convergence of 1 meV/atom):
- etot_conv_thr = 1.0e-4 Ry (≈1 meV per atom)  
- forc_conv_thr = 1.0e-3 Ry/Bohr (≈0.025 eV/Å)  

Other settings:
- No spin polarization (starting_magnetization omitted).  
- No Hubbard U (Hubbard_U omitted).  
- Spin‑orbit coupling disabled (no lspinorb flag).  

Run the vc‑relax calculation until both the total‑energy and force convergence criteria are satisfied.  

Output requirements:
- Provide the fully relaxed lattice parameters a, b, c (in Å) and the orthorhombic cell vectors.  
- List the relaxed atomic positions in fractional coordinates for all 8 atoms.  
- Include the final total energy (in eV) and the final forces on each atom (in eV/Å).  

All files necessary to reproduce the calculation (input file, pseudopotential paths, and the retrieved initial structure) must be documented.
