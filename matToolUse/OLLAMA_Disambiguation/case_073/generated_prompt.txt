Use the primitive Na3Bi structure with space group P6_3/mmc and lattice constants a = 5.45 Å, c = 9.65 Å.
Perform a variable‑cell relaxation (vc‑relax) using LDA pseudopotentials (na.upf, bi.upf) with spin‑orbit coupling enabled.
Set the plane‑wave kinetic energy cutoff ecutwfc = 60 Ry and ecutrho = 240 Ry (4 × ecutwfc).
Use a gamma‑centered k‑point grid of 12 × 12 × 8 for the vc‑relax and subsequent scf calculations.
Apply metallic smearing with degauss = 0.02 Ry and occupations = 'smearing'.
Converge the total energy to within 1 meV per atom (etot_conv_thr = 7.4e‑5 Ry) and forces to 1.0e‑4 Ry/Bohr (forc_conv_thr = 1.0e‑4 Ry/Bohr).
After vc‑relax, run a self‑consistent field (scf) calculation with the same ecutwfc, ecutrho, k‑point grid, smearing, and SOC settings.
Perform a non‑self‑consistent field (nscf) calculation on a denser gamma‑centered grid of 24 × 24 × 16 to obtain accurate eigenvalues for DOS.
Run pw.x with calculation = "bands" using the same dense 24 × 24 × 16 grid and SOC enabled to generate band energies.
Post‑process the band data with the appropriate post‑processing tool (e.g., bands.x) to produce the band structure file (not required to be returned).
Post‑process the density of states with dos.x using the nscf eigenvalues and a fine energy mesh (e.g., 0.01 eV spacing) to obtain the total DOS.
All input files should reference the LDA pseudopotential files PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/na.upf and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/bi.upf, include the specified convergence thresholds, and have spin‑orbit coupling turned on throughout the workflow.
