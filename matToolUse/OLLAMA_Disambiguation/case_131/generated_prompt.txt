Use the PseudoDojo LDA standard pseudopotentials ga.upf and n.upf for Ga and N.  
Retrieve a primitive wurtzite GaN structure (space group P6_3mc, 4 atoms per primitive cell) from a structure‑retrieval database; if retrieval fails, construct the structure with lattice constants a = 3.19 Å and c = 5.19 Å and the standard wurtzite atomic positions.  
Set the calculation type to variable‑cell relaxation (vc‑relax).  
Use a plane‑wave kinetic‑energy cutoff (ecutwfc) of 40 Ry and a charge‑density cutoff (ecutrho) of 320 Ry.  
Employ a gamma‑centered Monkhorst‑Pack k‑point grid of 6 × 6 × 4 for the primitive cell.  
Converge the total energy to 1 meV per atom (etot_conv_thr = 3.7 × 10⁻⁵ Ry × number_of_atoms).  
Converge the forces to 1 × 10⁻³ Ry/Bohr (forc_conv_thr = 1.0e‑3 Ry/Bohr).  
Use LDA exchange‑correlation functional.  
Do not apply smearing, degauss, Hubbard U, spin‑orbit coupling, or starting magnetization.  
Run the vc‑relax calculation until both the energy and force convergence criteria are satisfied, allowing the cell shape and volume to change.  
Output the fully relaxed structure, including the final lattice vectors, atomic positions (in crystal coordinates), and total energy.
