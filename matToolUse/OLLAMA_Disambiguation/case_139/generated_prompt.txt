Use the PseudoDojo LDA standard pseudopotentials mg.upf and b.upf.  
Retrieve a primitive MgB₂ structure (space group P6/mmm, 3 atoms per primitive cell) from a reliable public database such as the Materials Project, ICSD, or COD.  
Set the calculation type to variable‑cell relaxation (vc‑relax).  
Use a gamma‑centered k‑point grid of 12 × 12 × 12.  
Set the plane‑wave kinetic energy cutoff ecutwfc to 60 Ry and the charge‑density cutoff ecutrho to 240 Ry (4 × ecutwfc).  
Apply metallic occupations with Methfessel‑Paxton smearing and degauss = 0.02 Ry.  
Converge the total energy to 1 meV per atom: set etot_conv_thr to 2.2 × 10⁻⁴ Ry (≈0.001 eV per atom for the 3‑atom cell).  
Set the force convergence threshold forc_conv_thr to 1.0 × 10⁻³ Ry/Bohr.  
Use the default SCF convergence threshold conv_thr equal to etot_conv_thr (2.2 × 10⁻⁴ Ry).  
Do not apply Hubbard U, spin‑orbit coupling, or starting magnetization.  
Run the vc‑relax calculation until both the cell parameters and atomic positions satisfy the specified energy and force thresholds, then output the fully relaxed lattice vectors, atomic coordinates, and final total energy.
