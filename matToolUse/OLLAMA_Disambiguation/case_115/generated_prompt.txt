Use the LDA pseudopotentials from PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard: k.upf, nb.upf, and o.upf.  
Retrieve a primitive orthorhombic Amm2 KNbO₃ structure from a crystallographic database and use it as the initial configuration.  
Set the calculation type to variable‑cell relaxation (vc‑relax) with cell_dofree = "all".  
Employ a gamma‑centered 6 × 6 × 6 k‑point grid for Brillouin‑zone sampling.  
Set the plane‑wave kinetic‑energy cutoff ecutwfc = 80 Ry and the charge‑density cutoff ecutrho = 640 Ry.  
Use occupations = "fixed" (no smearing) appropriate for an insulating ferroelectric.  
Converge the total energy to within 1 meV per atom (etot_conv_thr = 3.7×10⁻⁵ Ry).  
Converge the forces to ≤ 1.0×10⁻⁴ Ry/Bohr (forc_conv_thr = 1.0e-4 Ry/Bohr).  
Converge the external pressure to ≤ 0.5 kbar (press_conv_thr = 0.5 kbar).  
Do not apply spin polarization, Hubbard U corrections, or spin‑orbit coupling.  
Run the vc‑relax calculation until the above convergence criteria are satisfied and output the fully relaxed lattice parameters and atomic positions.
