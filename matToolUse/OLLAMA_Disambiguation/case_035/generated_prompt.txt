Use the primitive tetragonal BaTiO3 structure (space group P4mm) from the Materials Project entry mp‑2992 as the initial geometry.  
Employ the LDA pseudopotentials ba.upf, ti.upf, and o.upf from the PseudoDojo SR_v0.4.1 LDA_standard library.  
Set the plane‑wave kinetic‑energy cutoff ecutwfc to 60 Ry and the charge‑density cutoff ecutrho to 480 Ry (8 × ecutwfc).  
Use a gamma‑centered Monkhorst‑Pack k‑point grid of 8 × 8 × 8 for the vc‑relax and scf calculations.  
Apply electronic occupations with smearing type “marzari‑vanderbilt” and degauss = 0.01 Ry.  
Converge the total energy to at least 1 meV per atom by setting etot_conv_thr = 1.0e‑5 Ry and forc_conv_thr = 1.0e‑4 Ry/Bohr.  
Perform a variable‑cell relaxation (calculation="vc‑relax") allowing all cell degrees of freedom to relax (cell_dofree="all") until the above convergence criteria are met.  
Run a self‑consistent field calculation (calculation="scf") on the relaxed structure using the same ecutwfc, ecutrho, k‑point grid, smearing, and convergence thresholds.  
Execute a non‑self‑consistent field calculation (calculation="nscf") on the SCF charge density with a denser gamma‑centered k‑point grid of 12 × 12 × 12 to obtain accurate eigenvalues for DOS.  
Carry out a bands calculation (calculation="bands") using pw.x on the same dense k‑point mesh to generate the band eigenvalues required for post‑processing.  
Post‑process the band data with bands.x (or pw2wannier90.x if desired) to produce the band‑structure file, although the band plot itself is not required to be returned.  
Generate the total and projected density of states with dos.x using the eigenvalues from the nscf step, employing a Gaussian broadening of 0.02 Ry.  
Collect and archive all output files from each step (vc‑relax, scf, nscf, bands, dos) as the final result of the high‑accuracy DOS calculation for BaTiO3.
