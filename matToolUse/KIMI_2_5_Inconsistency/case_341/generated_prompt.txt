ENCUT = 450

IBRION = 1

Step 3: Optimize adsorbed CO/RuO2 system
Use the same INCAR as Step 1.
Run: python op.py absorbed POTCAR-RuO2CO POSCAR-RuO2CO

Run: bash ./calc_absorption_energy.sh
