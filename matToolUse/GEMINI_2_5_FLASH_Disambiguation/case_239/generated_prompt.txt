Perform a variable-cell relaxation (vc-relax) calculation for SiO2 (alpha-quartz).
Use LDA pseudopotentials from PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/si.upf and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/o.upf.
Set the kinetic energy cutoff (ecutwfc) to 70 Ry.
Use an automatic Monkhorst-Pack k-point grid of 4x4x4 with a half shift.
Set the total energy convergence threshold (etot_conv_thr) to 1.0e-8 Ry.
Set the force convergence threshold (forc_conv_thr) to 1.0e-7 Ry/Bohr.
Set the self-consistent field convergence threshold (conv_thr) to 1.0e-8 Ry.
Use a starting structure consistent with trigonal SiO2 (alpha-quartz) with space group P3_121 and 3 atoms per primitive cell.
After the vc-relax finishes, run a self-consistent field (SCF) calculation on the relaxed structure.
The SCF calculation must use the same pseudopotentials, ecutwfc, k-point grid, and conv_thr as the vc-relax calculation.
Return the fully relaxed structure and the final total energy from the SCF calculation.
