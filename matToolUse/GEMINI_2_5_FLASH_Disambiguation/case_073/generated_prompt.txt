The task is to calculate the density of states for Na3Bi.
The calculation will follow a workflow: 1) vc-relax, 2) scf, 3) nscf, 4) pw.x with calculation="bands", 5) band post-processing, and 6) DOS post-processing.
A primitive starting structure consistent with formula=Na3Bi, structure=Dirac semimetal, space_group=P6_3/mmc, and atoms_per_primitive_cell=4 will be retrieved using an available structure-retrieval tool.
Local pseudopotential files PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/na.upf and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/bi.upf will be used.
The vc-relax step will use an energy convergence threshold (etot_conv_thr) of 1.0e-4 Ry and a force convergence threshold (forc_conv_thr) of 1.0e-3 Ry/Bohr.
The scf step will use a convergence threshold (conv_thr) of 1.0e-8 Ry.
A plane-wave cutoff energy (ecutwfc) of 50 Ry will be used for all pw.x calculations.
A gamma-centered 8x8x8 k-point grid will be used for the vc-relax and scf steps.
A gamma-centered 20x20x20 k-point grid will be used for the nscf step.
LDA pseudopotentials will be used.
The calculation requires a strict energy convergence of 1 meV/atom.
The band structure does not need to be explicitly returned.
