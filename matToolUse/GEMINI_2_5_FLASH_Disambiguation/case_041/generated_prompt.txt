The calculation will be performed for BiFeO3, a ferroelectric material with a perovskite structure and R3c space group, containing 10 atoms per primitive cell.
The starting structure will be consistent with the provided metadata.
Local pseudopotential files PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/bi.upf, PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/fe.upf, and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/o.upf will be used.
The workflow will strictly follow these steps: 1) vc-relax, 2) scf, 3) nscf, 4) pw.x with calculation="bands", 5) band post-processing, and 6) DOS post-processing.
The calculation will use LDA pseudopotentials.
A strict energy convergence of 1 meV/atom is required.
The plane-wave energy cutoff (ecutwfc) will be 70 Ry.
The total energy convergence threshold (etot_conv_thr) for vc-relax will be 1.0e-4 Ry.
The force convergence threshold (forc_conv_thr) for vc-relax will be 1.0e-3 Ry/Bohr.
The self-consistency convergence threshold (conv_thr) will be 1.0e-8 Ry.
A gamma-centered k-point grid of 6x6x6 will be used for the vc-relax and scf calculations.
A gamma-centered k-point grid of 12x12x12 will be used for the nscf calculation.
For the pw.x calculation with calculation="bands", a high-symmetry k-point path appropriate for the rhombohedral R3c Brillouin zone will be used, specifically Gamma-L-F-Z-Gamma.
The band structure itself does not need to be explicitly returned, only the completion of the calculation.
