The task is to calculate the density of states for BaTiO3.
The calculation must follow the workflow: vc-relax, scf, nscf, pw.x with calculation="bands", band post-processing, and DOS post-processing.
The calculation must use LDA pseudopotentials.
The pseudopotential files are PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/ba.upf, PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/ti.upf, and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/o.upf.
A starting structure consistent with BaTiO3, perovskite, space group P4mm, and 5 atoms per primitive cell must be generated.
The calculation requires a strict energy convergence of 1 meV/atom.
A reasonable ecutwfc value must be chosen based on best practices.
A reasonable ecutrho value must be chosen based on best practices, typically 4 times ecutwfc.
A gamma-centered k-point grid must be used for vc-relax, scf, and nscf calculations.
A reasonable k-point grid for vc-relax and scf calculations must be chosen based on best practices.
A reasonable k-point grid for the nscf calculation must be chosen based on best practices, denser than the scf grid.
A reasonable smearing method and degauss value must be chosen based on best practices.
A reasonable etot_conv_thr value for the vc-relax calculation must be chosen based on best practices to achieve the target energy convergence.
A reasonable forc_conv_thr value for the vc-relax calculation must be chosen based on best practices to achieve the target energy convergence.
A reasonable conv_thr value for the scf calculation must be chosen based on best practices to achieve the target energy convergence.
A reasonable k-point path for the pw.x calculation with calculation="bands" must be chosen based on best practices.
Occupations must be set to smearing.
Starting magnetization, Hubbard U, and spin-orbit coupling flags must be set to their default (off/zero) values.
The band structure does not need to be explicitly returned.
