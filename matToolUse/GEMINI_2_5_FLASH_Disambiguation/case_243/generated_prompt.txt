Perform a variable-cell relaxation calculation for ZnO.
Use LDA pseudopotentials from PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/zn.upf and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/o.upf.
Set the total energy convergence threshold for the relaxation to 1.0e-8 Ry.
Set the force convergence threshold for the relaxation to 1.0e-7 Ry/Bohr.
Use a starting structure consistent with wurtzite ZnO, space group P6_3mc, and 4 atoms per primitive cell.
Set the plane-wave kinetic energy cutoff (ecutwfc) to 60 Ry.
Use an automatic Monkhorst-Pack k-point grid of 6x6x4 with a half shift.
After the variable-cell relaxation finishes, perform a self-consistent field calculation on the resulting relaxed structure.
For the self-consistent field calculation, use the same pseudopotentials, ecutwfc, and k-point grid settings as the relaxation.
Set the self-consistent field convergence threshold (conv_thr) to 1.0e-8 Ry.
Return the fully relaxed structure obtained from the variable-cell relaxation.
Return the final total energy obtained from the self-consistent field calculation.
