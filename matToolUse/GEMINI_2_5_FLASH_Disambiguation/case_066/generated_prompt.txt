The task is to calculate the density of states for CoSb3.
The calculation will follow a six-step workflow: vc-relax, scf, nscf, pw.x with calculation="bands", band post-processing, and DOS post-processing.
The final output is the density of states; the band structure does not need to be explicitly returned.
The starting configuration will be a skutterudite structure consistent with space group Im-3 and 8 atoms per primitive cell.
Local pseudopotential files PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/co.upf and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/sb.upf will be used, corresponding to LDA pseudopotentials.
The plane-wave cutoff energy (ecutwfc) will be 80 Ry.
The charge density cutoff energy (ecutrho) will be 320 Ry.
During the vc-relax step, the force convergence threshold (forc_conv_thr) will be 1e-3 Ry/Bohr.
During the vc-relax step, the energy convergence threshold (etot_conv_thr) will be 1e-4 Ry.
During the scf step, the energy convergence threshold (conv_thr) will be 1e-8 Ry.
A gamma-centered 6x6x6 k-point grid will be used for the scf calculation.
A gamma-centered 12x12x12 k-point grid will be used for the nscf calculation.
The smearing method will be 'gaussian'.
The smearing width (degauss) will be 0.01 Ry.
The overall calculation aims for a strict energy convergence of 1 meV/atom.
