Q: What is the desired k-point density or total number of k-points for the Monkhorst-Pack grid?
A: Make a reasonable assumption based on best practices. For a primitive cell of a topological insulator like Bi2Te3, a typical k-point density would be around 0.15-0.2 Å^-1, which usually translates to a 8x8x8 or 9x9x9 Monkhorst-Pack grid.
