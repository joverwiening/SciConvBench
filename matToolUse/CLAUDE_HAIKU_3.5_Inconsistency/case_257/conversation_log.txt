Q: What is the desired k-point density or total number of k-points for the Monkhorst-Pack grid?
A: Make a reasonable assumption based on best practices. For a layered material like NbSe2, a typical k-point density would be around 0.15-0.2 Å^-1, which usually translates to a 12x12x4 or 16x16x4 Monkhorst-Pack grid with a half shift.
