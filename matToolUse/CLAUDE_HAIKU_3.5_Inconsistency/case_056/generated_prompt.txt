Specification for CdS Density of States Calculation:

Retrieve a primitive wurtzite CdS structure with space group P6_3mc and 4 atoms per primitive cell using standard crystallographic databases.
Use LDA pseudopotentials from PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/cd.upf and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/s.upf.
Set ecutwfc to 60 Ry for high-accuracy LDA calculation of CdS.
Use a 12x12x8 gamma-centered Monkhorst-Pack k-point grid for Brillouin zone sampling.
Perform vc-relax calculation with total energy convergence threshold of 1 meV/atom.
Set force convergence threshold to 0.01 eV/Å.
Perform self-consistent field (SCF) calculation using converged relaxed structure.
Perform non-self-consistent field (NSCF) calculation with dense k-point sampling for DOS.
Calculate density of states using pw.x post-processing tools.
Use default LDA occupation and smearing settings.
Do not apply spin-orbit coupling or Hubbard U corrections.
