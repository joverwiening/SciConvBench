Q: What is the desired k-point density or total number of k-points for the Monkhorst-Pack grid?
A: Make a reasonable assumption based on best practices. For a primitive cell of a Dirac semimetal like Na3Bi, a typical k-point density would be around 0.15-0.2 Å^-1, which translates to approximately 8-12 k-points per reciprocal atom.
