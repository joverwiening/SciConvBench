Q: What is the desired k-point sampling density for the Monkhorst-Pack grid to achieve the 1 meV/atom convergence requirement?
A: Make a reasonable assumption based on best practices. For a high-accuracy density-of-states calculation of a wurtzite semiconductor like GaN, aim for a dense k-point grid, such as 12x12x8 or 16x16x10 Monkhorst-Pack grid with a half shift.
