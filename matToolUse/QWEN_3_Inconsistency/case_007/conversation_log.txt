Q: What is the file structure and naming convention for the Mg_Ga charge-state folders within the base directory?
A: The Mg_Ga charge-state folders are subfolders under `file_path / "Mg_Ga"`, with each folder corresponding to a specific charge state of the Mg_Ga defect. The folders are named according to their charge state: `-2`, `-1`, `0`, and `1`. Each contains `vasprun.xml.gz` and `LOCPOT.gz` files. Additionally, the bulk reference potential is located at `file_path / "bulk_sc" / "LOCPOT.gz"`.

Q: What is the path to the bulk supercell energy file, which is needed to compute the energy difference (e.g., is there a `vasprun.xml.gz` in the `bulk_sc` folder)?
A: Yes, the bulk supercell energy file is located at `file_path / "bulk_sc" / "vasprun.xml.gz"`. This file should be used to obtain the bulk supercell energy for computing the energy difference.
