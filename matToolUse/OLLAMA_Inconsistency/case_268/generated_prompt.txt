Retrieve a primitive Na3Bi Dirac‑semimetal structure (space group P6_3/mmc, 4 atoms per primitive cell) from a structure‑retrieval service and use it as the initial configuration.  
Use the LDA pseudopotential files PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/na.upf and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/bi.upf for all calculations.  
Set the plane‑wave kinetic‑energy cutoff (ecutwfc) to 60 Ry and the charge‑density cutoff (ecutrho) to 4 × ecutwfc (240 Ry).  
Generate an automatic Monkhorst‑Pack k‑point grid with a half‑grid shift that gives approximately 0.02 Å⁻¹ resolution; for the primitive cell use a 12 × 12 × 8 grid.  
Apply the convergence thresholds etot_conv_thr = 1.0e‑8 Ry, forc_conv_thr = 1.0e‑7 Ry/Bohr, and conv_thr = 1.0e‑8 for both the variable‑cell relaxation and the subsequent SCF calculation.  
Perform a variable‑cell relaxation (vc‑relax) allowing both atomic positions and cell vectors to relax until the force and stress criteria defined by the convergence thresholds are satisfied.  
After the vc‑relax finishes, take the relaxed structure and run a single‑point self‑consistent field (SCF) calculation using the same pseudopotentials, ecutwfc, ecutrho, k‑point grid, and convergence thresholds to obtain the final total energy.  
Output the fully relaxed crystal structure (lattice vectors and atomic coordinates) and the final SCF total energy.
