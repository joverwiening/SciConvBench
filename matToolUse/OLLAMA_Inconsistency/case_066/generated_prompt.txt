Retrieve the primitive CoSb3 skutterudite structure (space group Im‑3, 8 atoms per primitive cell) from the Materials Project database, selecting the lowest‑energy entry for the composition CoSb3.  
Use the LDA pseudopotentials co.upf and sb.upf from the PseudoDojo SR_v0.4.1 library.  
Perform a variable‑cell relaxation (vc‑relax) with cell_dofree='all' and target pressure convergence of 0.5 kbar.  
Set the plane‑wave kinetic‑energy cutoff ecutwfc to 60 Ry and ecutrho to 480 Ry (8 × ecutwfc).  
Use a gamma‑centered k‑point grid of 8 × 8 × 8 for the vc‑relax and scf calculations.  
Apply Methfessel‑Paxton smearing with degauss = 0.01 Ry and occupations='smearing'.  
Set the electronic convergence threshold etot_conv_thr to 1 × 10⁻⁵ Ry (≈1 meV per atom) and the force convergence threshold forc_conv_thr to 1 × 10⁻⁴ Ry/Bohr.  
Run a self‑consistent field (scf) calculation on the relaxed structure using the same ecutwfc, ecutrho, k‑point grid, smearing, and convergence thresholds.  
Skip the separate NSCF step; feed the scf charge density directly to the DOS calculation.  
Run a DOS post‑processing calculation (dos.x) using a denser gamma‑centered k‑point grid of 12 × 12 × 12 and the tetrahedron method for accurate density‑of‑states integration.  
Do not perform spin‑polarized calculations; set starting_magnetization = 0 for all species.  
Do not apply Hubbard U corrections or spin‑orbit coupling.  
After the DOS calculation, generate the total and projected density‑of‑states files.  
Document all input files, convergence criteria, and output files for reproducibility.
