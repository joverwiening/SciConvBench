Retrieve the primitive Na3Bi crystal structure (space group P6_3/mmc, 4 atoms per primitive cell) using a structure‑retrieval tool and generate the input files in Quantum ESPRESSO format.

Use the LDA pseudopotentials PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/na.upf and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/bi.upf for Na and Bi respectively.

Set the plane‑wave kinetic‑energy cutoff ecutwfc = 60 Ry and ecutrho = 480 Ry (8 × ecutwfc).

Use a gamma‑centered k‑point grid of 12 × 12 × 8 for all calculations. For the electronic‑sampling step (nscf, bands, DOS) use an automatic Monkhorst‑Pack grid with a half‑cell shift (0.5 0.5 0.5) and the same 12 × 12 × 8 density.

Electronic occupations: smearing = Methfessel‑Paxton, degauss = 0.02 Ry.

Convergence thresholds:
- Total‑energy convergence etot_conv_thr = 3.6 × 10⁻⁵ Ry (≈ 1 meV per atom).
- Force convergence forc_conv_thr = 1.0 × 10⁻⁴ Ry/Bohr.
- SCF convergence conv_thr = 1.0 × 10⁻⁸.

Do not apply Hubbard U or spin‑orbit coupling.

Workflow:

1. **vc‑relax**
   - calculation = "vc‑relax"
   - cell_dofree = "all"
   - input parameters as above.
   - Output relaxed cell parameters and atomic positions.

2. **scf**
   - calculation = "scf"
   - use the relaxed structure from step 1.
   - same ecutwfc, ecutrho, k‑point grid, smearing, and convergence thresholds.

3. **nscf**
   - calculation = "nscf"
   - use the SCF charge density.
   - k‑point grid: 12 × 12 × 8 with half shift.
   - increase number of bands to include all states up to at least 10 eV above the Fermi level.

4. **pw.x bands**
   - calculation = "bands"
   - use the same nscf input but set `calculation='bands'`.
   - define a path through high‑symmetry points of the Dirac semimetal (e.g., Γ‑M‑K‑Γ‑A‑L‑H‑A) with sufficient points per segment (≈ 40).

5. **Band post‑processing**
   - Run `bands.x` to extract eigenvalues along the defined path.
   - No need to return the band structure; just ensure the step completes successfully.

6. **DOS post‑processing**
   - Run `dos.x` on the nscf output.
   - Use a Gaussian broadening of 0.02 Ry.
   - Generate total DOS and projected DOS on Na and Bi atoms.

Verify that the final total energy change between successive SCF cycles is below the 1 meV/atom threshold and that forces are below the specified force convergence. The calculation is complete when the DOS file is produced.
