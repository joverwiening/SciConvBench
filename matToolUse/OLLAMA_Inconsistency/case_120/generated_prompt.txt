Use the LDA pseudopotentials hf.upf and o.upf from the PseudoDojo SR_v0.4.1 LDA_standard library.  
Retrieve a primitive orthorhombic HfO2 structure with space group P2_1/c and 12 atoms per primitive cell from a structure‑retrieval database and use it as the initial configuration.  
Set the plane‑wave kinetic‑energy cutoff (ecutwfc) to 70 Ry and the charge‑density cutoff (ecutrho) to 560 Ry (8 × ecutwfc).  
Generate a gamma‑centered Monkhorst‑Pack k‑point grid of 6 × 6 × 6 for the Brillouin‑zone sampling, applying a half‑grid shift.  
Converge the electronic self‑consistent field loop until the total‑energy change per atom is less than 1 meV (≈3.7 × 10⁻⁵ Ry), i.e., set etot_conv_thr to 3.7e‑5 Ry.  
Converge the ionic relaxation when the maximum force on any atom is below 1.0 × 10⁻³ Ry/Bohr and when the pressure is below 0.5 kbar; set forc_conv_thr to 1.0e‑3 Ry/Bohr and press_conv_thr to 0.5 kbar.  
Perform a variable‑cell relaxation (vc‑relax) using the BFGS algorithm for both atomic positions and cell parameters.  
Do not apply spin polarization, Hubbard U corrections, or spin‑orbit coupling.  
After convergence, output the fully relaxed crystal structure, including lattice vectors, cell parameters, and atomic coordinates in standard crystallographic format.
