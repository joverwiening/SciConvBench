Retrieve the primitive MgB2 structure (space group P6/mmm, 3 atoms per primitive cell) from an online crystallographic database and use it as the starting configuration.  
Perform a variable‑cell relaxation (calculation='vc‑relax') using LDA pseudopotentials mg.upf and b.upf, with a kinetic‑energy cutoff ecutwfc = 60 Ry, charge‑density cutoff ecutrho = 600 Ry, and a gamma‑centered 12 × 12 × 12 k‑point grid. Use Methfessel‑Paxton smearing of 0.02 Ry, occupations='smearing', and set the total‑energy convergence threshold etot_conv_thr = 3.7e‑5 Ry (≈1 meV/atom) and force convergence threshold forc_conv_thr = 1.0e‑4 Ry/Bohr.  
After vc‑relax, run a self‑consistent field calculation (calculation='scf') on the relaxed cell with the same ecutwfc, ecutrho, smearing, and a gamma‑centered 12 × 12 × 12 k‑point grid, keeping the same convergence thresholds.  
Next, perform a non‑self‑consistent field calculation (calculation='nscf') on the SCF charge density using an automatic Monkhorst‑Pack grid with a half‑shift (e.g., 24 × 24 × 24) and the same cutoffs and smearing.  
Run pw.x with calculation='bands' on the same dense 24 × 24 × 24 shifted grid to generate band eigenvalues.  
Post‑process the band data with the appropriate post‑processing tool (e.g., bands.x) to obtain the band structure (output not required to be returned).  
Finally, compute the density of states using dos.x on the nscf results with a Gaussian broadening of 0.02 Ry, producing the total and projected DOS files.  
All steps must be executed sequentially, and the final DOS files should be saved in the working directory.
