Retrieve the primitive NbSe2 structure (space group P6_3/mmc, 3 atoms per primitive cell) from a crystallographic database using a structure‑retrieval tool.  
Use the LDA pseudopotentials nb.upf and se.upf from the PseudoDojo SR_v0.4.1 LDA_standard library.  
Set the plane‑wave cutoff ecutwfc to 60 Ry and ecutrho to 4 × ecutwfc (240 Ry).  
Generate an automatic Monkhorst‑Pack k‑point grid with a half‑grid shift; for the primitive cell use a 12 × 12 × 6 grid.  
Perform a variable‑cell relaxation (vc‑relax) with the following convergence thresholds: etot_conv_thr = 1.0e‑8 Ry, forc_conv_thr = 1.0e‑7 Ry/Bohr, and conv_thr = 1.0e‑8.  
Use BFGS algorithms for both ion dynamics (ion_dynamics = 'bfgs') and cell dynamics (cell_dynamics = 'bfgs').  
After the vc‑relax finishes, keep the original (unrelaxed) primitive structure retrieved in step 1 and run a self‑consistent field calculation (scf) on that structure with the same ecutwfc, ecutrho, k‑point grid, pseudopotentials, and convergence thresholds as in the vc‑relax.  
Record the final total energy from the scf calculation and output the fully relaxed structure obtained from the vc‑relax together with that total energy.
