Retrieve the primitive wurtzite CdS structure (space group P6_3mc, 4 atoms per primitive cell) from a structure‑retrieval database and use it as the starting configuration.

Perform a variable‑cell relaxation (vc‑relax) with the following settings:
- Exchange‑correlation functional: LDA.
- Pseudopotentials: cd.upf and s.upf from PseudoDojo SR_v0.4.1 LDA_standard.
- Plane‑wave kinetic‑energy cutoff (ecutwfc): 50 Ry.
- Charge‑density cutoff (ecutrho): 400 Ry (8 × ecutwfc).
- K‑point grid: gamma‑centered 8 × 8 × 6 Monkhorst‑Pack mesh.
- Energy convergence threshold (etot_conv_thr): 7 × 10⁻⁵ Ry (≈1 meV per atom).
- Force convergence threshold (forc_conv_thr): 1 × 10⁻⁴ Ry/Bohr.
- Occupations: smearing, method “gaussian”, degauss = 0.01 Ry.
- No spin polarization, no Hubbard U, no spin‑orbit coupling.
- Cell dynamics: variable‑cell (vc‑relax) with cell‑dof = all.

After vc‑relax, run a self‑consistent field (scf) calculation using the relaxed structure with the same ecutwfc, ecutrho, k‑point grid, smearing, and convergence thresholds as above.

Skip the separate non‑self‑consistent field (nscf) step. Directly perform a density‑of‑states (DOS) calculation:
- Use pw.x with calculation = "dos".
- Same ecutwfc, ecutrho, and k‑point grid as the scf step.
- Use a denser k‑point grid for DOS if desired (e.g., 12 × 12 × 9) while keeping the same convergence criteria.

Post‑process the DOS output with dos.x to obtain the total and projected density of states:
- Set Emin and Emax to cover at least –10 eV to +10 eV relative to the Fermi level.
- Use a Gaussian broadening of 0.01 Ry.

Deliver the final DOS data files (e.g., dos.dat) and a brief summary confirming that the energy convergence of 1 meV/atom was achieved. No explicit band‑structure output is required.
