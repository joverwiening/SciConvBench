Perform a variable‑cell relaxation (vc‑relax) with LDA pseudopotentials al.upf and o.upf, using a plane‑wave kinetic‑energy cutoff ecutwfc = 60 Ry and charge‑density cutoff ecutrho = 480 Ry.

Post‑process the band data with bands.x (or pw2wannier90.x if needed) to generate the band eigenvalues, but do not output the band structure.  
Directly compute the density of states from the scf result using dos.x, employing a Gaussian smearing of 0.01 Ry and a dense k‑point mesh of 12 × 12 × 12 for the DOS integration.
