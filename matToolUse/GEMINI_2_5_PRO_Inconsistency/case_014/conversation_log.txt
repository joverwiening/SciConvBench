Q: Could you please clarify what the function `ensure_stable_bulk(..)` is supposed to do, as it does not appear to be a standard Pymatgen function?
A: You should add the new unstable entry to the list of existing stable entries and then create a new phase diagram from the combined list. The stability check is performed on this new diagram.

Q: To construct the unstable `GaN` entry, what specific total energy value should be assigned to it?
A: The energy for the unstable `GaN` entry should be its hull energy plus `2`.
