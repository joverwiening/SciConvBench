Retrieve the primitive crystallographic structure for Al2O3 with space group R-3c.
Use the LDA pseudopotential file PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/al.upf for Aluminum.
Use the LDA pseudopotential file PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/o.upf for Oxygen.
The workflow consists of six sequential steps: vc-relax, scf, nscf, bands, bands post-processing, and dos post-processing.
Set the plane-wave kinetic energy cutoff (ecutwfc) to 80 Ry.
Set the charge density kinetic energy cutoff (ecutrho) to 320 Ry.
Use Gaussian smearing with a broadening of 0.001 Ry for all self-consistent calculations.
Set the electronic convergence threshold (conv_thr) to 1.0E-9 Ry.
For the vc-relax step, set the total energy convergence threshold (etot_conv_thr) to 1.0E-5 Ry.
For the vc-relax step, set the force convergence threshold (forc_conv_thr) to 1.0E-4 Ry/au.
Use a 10x10x10 Gamma-centered k-point grid for the vc-relax and scf calculations.
Use the relaxed structure from the vc-relax step as input for all subsequent steps.
Perform an nscf calculation using a 20x20x20 Gamma-centered k-point grid.
Perform a bands calculation along the high-symmetry k-point path recommended for space group R-3c.
Perform DOS post-processing on the results of the nscf calculation.
Calculate the density of states over an energy range of -15 eV to +15 eV relative to the Fermi energy.
Use an energy step of 0.01 eV for the DOS calculation.
Apply a Gaussian broadening of 0.05 eV to the final density of states.
