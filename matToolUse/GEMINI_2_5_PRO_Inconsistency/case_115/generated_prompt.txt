Retrieve the primitive crystallographic structure for KNbO3 in the Amm2 space group.
Perform a variable-cell relaxation calculation.
The lattice vectors and atomic positions must both be relaxed.
Use the LDA exchange-correlation functional.
Use the k.upf, nb.upf, and o.upf pseudopotential files from PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard.
Set the plane-wave kinetic energy cutoff (ecutwfc) to 100 Ry.
Set the charge density and potential cutoff (ecutrho) to 400 Ry.
Use a 10x10x10 gamma-centered k-point grid.
Set the total energy convergence threshold for the ionic relaxation (etot_conv_thr) to 1.0e-5 Ry.
Set the force convergence threshold (forc_conv_thr) to 1.0e-4 Ry/Bohr.
Set the electronic self-consistency convergence threshold (conv_thr) to 1.0e-7 Ry.
Do not use any smearing method for occupations.
The calculation is non-magnetic and non-spin-polarized.
Do not include spin-orbit coupling.
Do not apply any Hubbard U correction.
The final output must be the fully relaxed structure.
