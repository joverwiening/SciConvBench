Q: What is the specific k-point index and band window (minimum and maximum band indices) that should be used when evaluating the dielectric function and generating the optical transitions DataFrame?
A: Based on the complete requirement:

- **kpt_index**: 0
- **band_window**: 5

The dielectric function should be evaluated with `idir = 0` and `jdir = 0`. The optical-transitions DataFrame should be generated with `kpt_index = 0` and `band_window = 5`.
