Q: I need to create an INCAR file for the ground-state calculation (Step 1). Since no explicit INCAR parameters are provided for Step 1 in `data.json`, I need to determine what parameters are appropriate for a standard PBE ground-state calculation that will serve as the starting point for the subsequent hybrid-functional calculation.

What are the required INCAR parameters for the Step 1 ground-state calculation?
A: For Step 1 (PBE ground-state calculation), use these INCAR parameters:

```
PREC=Normal
ENCUT=450
NELM=500
IBRION=-1
ISPIN=1
NSW=0
ISIF=2
EDIFF=1E-5
EDIFFG=-5E-3
ISTART=0
ICHARG=2
ISMEAR=0
SIGMA=0.05
LWAVE=.T.
LCHARG=.T.
LREAL=Auto
LPLANE=.TRUE.
NPAR=1
KPAR=4
NCORE=1
```

This is a standard PBE calculation with charge density initialization (ICHARG=2) to generate the WAVECAR and CHGCAR needed for the subsequent HSE step.
