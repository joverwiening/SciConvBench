The density of states calculation for CdS shall be performed using the following complete specification:

Use the LDA pseudopotentials PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/cd.upf and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/s.upf.

Use a wurtzite structure with space group P6_3mc and 4 atoms per primitive cell as the starting configuration.

Set ecutwfc to 60 Ry for the plane-wave basis set.

Use a gamma-centered 8x8x6 k-point grid for the vc-relax and scf calculations.

Use a gamma-centered 16x16x12 k-point grid for the nscf calculation.

Use Methfessel-Paxton smearing with first-order smearing and set degauss to 0.01 Ry for electronic occupations.

Set the DOS energy window to span from -10 eV to +10 eV relative to the Fermi level.

Set the DOS energy resolution to 0.01 eV.

Ensure all calculations achieve a total energy convergence of 1 meV per atom.

Execute the workflow in the following order: vc-relax, scf, nscf, pw.x with calculation="bands", band post-processing, and DOS post-processing.

Do not explicitly return the band structure; only complete the DOS calculation.
