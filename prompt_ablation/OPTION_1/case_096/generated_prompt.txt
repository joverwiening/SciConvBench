The simulation is a two-dimensional Rayleigh-Benard convection problem.
The simulation domain is a square with dimensions 0.1 meters by 0.1 meters.
The two-dimensional nature is achieved by setting the front and back boundary conditions to `empty`.
The fluid has a constant kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m^2/s.
The fluid has a constant Prandtl number of 0.71.
The fluid has a constant thermal expansion coefficient of 3.33e-3 1/K.
The reference temperature is 293.5 K.
The reference density is 1.0 kg/m^3.
Gravitational acceleration is a vector defined as (0, -9.81, 0) m/s^2.
The bottom boundary has a fixed temperature of 294.0 K.
The top boundary has a fixed temperature of 293.0 K.
The side walls have a zero-gradient temperature boundary condition.
The velocity boundary condition for the top, bottom, and side walls is `noSlip`.
The pressure boundary condition for the side walls is `fixedFluxPressure`.
The initial velocity field is uniform with a value of (0, 0, 0) m/s.
The initial temperature field is uniform at 293.5 K.
A thermal perturbation is applied to the initial temperature field.
The temperature is set to 293.52 K within a rectangular region defined by the corners (0.045, 0.00, -1) and (0.055, 0.01, 1).
