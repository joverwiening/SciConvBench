Solve for the temperature distribution T(x) in a one-dimensional slab at steady state.
The governing equation is d2T/dx2 = 0.
The slab has a length of 1.0 meter.
The coordinate x extends from 0 at the left boundary to 1.0 at the right boundary.
The temperature at the left boundary (x=0) is fixed at 100 degrees Celsius.
The temperature at the right boundary (x=1.0) is fixed at 0 degrees Celsius.
