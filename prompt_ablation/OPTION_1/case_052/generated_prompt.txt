The governing equation is the steady-state one-dimensional heat conduction equation, d2T/dx2 = 0.
The domain is a one-dimensional slab.
The length of the slab is 1.0 meter.
The temperature at the left boundary (x=0) is 100.0 degrees Celsius.
The temperature at the right boundary (x=1.0) is 20.0 degrees Celsius.
The output shall be the analytical solution for the temperature distribution T(x) along the slab.
