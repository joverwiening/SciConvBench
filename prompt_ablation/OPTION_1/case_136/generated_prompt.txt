The system of units is Newtons (N), millimeters (mm), and Megapascals (MPa).
The prismatic bar has a rectangular cross-section.
The width of the cross-section is 30 mm.
The height of the cross-section is 100 mm.
The cross-sectional area is the product of the width and height.
An internal resultant normal force of 50000 N acts in tension on the cross-section.
An internal resultant transverse shear force of 5000 N acts on the cross-section.
The average normal stress is the normal force divided by the cross-sectional area.
The average shear stress is the transverse shear force divided by the cross-sectional area.
The maximum in-plane shear stress is calculated using the formula sqrt((average_normal_stress / 2)^2 + average_shear_stress^2).
