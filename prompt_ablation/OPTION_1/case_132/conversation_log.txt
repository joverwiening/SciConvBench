Q: For the Rusanov flux wave speed `alpha=max|sin(u)|`, should `alpha` be a constant value of 1.0 for all interfaces and all time steps?
A: No. The wave speed `alpha` for the Rusanov flux should be recomputed at each stage of the time integrator as the maximum of `|sin(u)|` over the entire spatial grid.
