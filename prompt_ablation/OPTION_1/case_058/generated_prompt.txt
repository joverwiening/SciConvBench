The simulation uses the `compressibleInterFoam` solver.
The simulation is transient.
The domain is a rectangular prism with dimensions 0.2 meters in length, 0.1 meters in width, and 0.1 meters in height.
A gas injector nozzle with a diameter of 0.01 meters is centered on one of the 0.1 meter by 0.1 meter faces.
The simulation models two fluid phases: a compressible gas and an incompressible liquid.
The gas phase is air, modeled using the `perfectGas` equation of state.
The liquid phase is water, with a constant density of 998 kg/m^3.
Initially, the tank is filled with quiescent water at a static pressure of 101325 Pa and a temperature of 300 K.
The gas injector inlet boundary condition specifies a total pressure of 1013250 Pa.
The gas injector inlet boundary condition specifies a total temperature of 300 K.
The gas enters the domain from the injector inlet at a Mach number of 0.8.
The top surface of the tank is a pressure outlet boundary set to a static pressure of 101325 Pa.
All other boundaries of the tank are no-slip walls.
