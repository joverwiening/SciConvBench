The task is to calculate the downstream depth, y2, of a hydraulic jump.
The calculation pertains to flow in a rectangular open channel.
The upstream flow depth, y1, is 0.4 meters.
The upstream flow velocity, V1, is 6.0 meters per second.
The acceleration due to gravity, g, shall be 9.81 meters per second squared.
The momentum equation for a hydraulic jump in a rectangular channel shall be used to perform the calculation.
The upstream Froude number, Fr1, will be calculated as an intermediate step.
The final output will be the value of the downstream depth, y2.
