The governing equation for grain growth is D^n - D_0^n = Kt.
The initial grain size, D_0, is 12 micrometers.
The final grain size, D, is 36 micrometers.
The annealing time, t, is 2.0 hours.
The grain growth exponent, n, is 2.
Calculate the value of the grain growth constant, K.
The units for K shall be micrometers squared per hour.
