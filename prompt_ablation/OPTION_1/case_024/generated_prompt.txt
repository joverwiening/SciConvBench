The task is to calculate the diffraction angle 2θ for an X-ray diffraction experiment.
The incident X-ray wavelength is 0.0762 nm.
The crystal structure is cubic.
The lattice parameter 'a' is 0.5313 nm.
The Miller indices (h, k, l) of the reflecting plane are (1, 1, 1).
The order of reflection 'n' is 1.
Calculate the interplanar spacing 'd' for the (111) plane using the formula for a cubic lattice.
Use Bragg's Law to find the angle θ.
Calculate the final diffraction angle by multiplying θ by 2.
The final result for 2θ must be expressed in degrees.
