The simulation is a 2D, laminar, incompressible flow past a cylinder.
The primary objective is to run a transient simulation to capture the von Kármán vortex street.
The Reynolds number is 100.
The computational domain is a rectangular channel 2.2 meters long and 0.41 meters high.
A cylinder with a diameter of 0.1 meters is centered at coordinates (0.2, 0.2).
The mesh is one cell thick in the z-direction.
The kinematic viscosity is 1e-3 m²/s.
The solver is `pimpleFoam`.
The simulation starts at `startTime` 0.
The simulation ends at `endTime` 200.
The initial time step `deltaT` is 1.
The time step is automatically adjusted.
The maximum Courant number is 0.9.
Solution data is written every 10 time units.
The velocity `U` at the `inlet` is a fixed value of uniform (1 0 0).
The velocity `U` at the `outlet` has a zero gradient condition.
The velocity `U` at the `cylinderWall` has a no-slip condition.
The velocity `U` at the `topAndBottom` walls has a no-slip condition.
The velocity `U` at the `frontAndBack` patches is of type `empty`.
The pressure `p` at the `inlet` has a zero gradient condition.
The pressure `p` at the `outlet` is a fixed value of uniform 0.
The pressure `p` at the `cylinderWall` has a zero gradient condition.
The pressure `p` at the `topAndBottom` walls has a zero gradient condition.
The pressure `p` at the `frontAndBack` patches is of type `empty`.
The time derivative scheme (`ddtSchemes`) is `Euler`.
The divergence scheme for velocity (`div(phi,U)`) is `Gauss linear`.
The PIMPLE algorithm uses 2 PISO correctors (`nCorrectors`).
