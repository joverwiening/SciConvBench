The system is a hypoeutectic binary alloy.
The overall composition of the alloy is 30 wt% B.
The composition of the primary alpha phase at the eutectic temperature is 12 wt% B.
The composition of the eutectic microconstituent is 40 wt% B.
Calculate the mass fraction of the eutectic microconstituent using the lever rule.
The lever is placed on the tie line between the primary alpha composition and the eutectic composition.
The fulcrum is at the overall alloy composition.
The fraction of eutectic microconstituent is the length of the lever arm opposite the eutectic point, divided by the total length of the lever.
The calculation is (30 - 12) / (40 - 12).
The resulting fraction of the eutectic microconstituent is 0.643.
