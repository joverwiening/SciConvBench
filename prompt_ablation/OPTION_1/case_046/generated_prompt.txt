The calculation is a density of states analysis for Fe3O4.
The starting structure is the 14-atom primitive cell for ferrimagnetic spinel Fe3O4 with space group Fd-3m.
The lattice parameters and atomic coordinates for the starting structure are retrieved from a standard crystallographic database.
The workflow consists of six sequential calculations: vc-relax, scf, nscf, bands, bands post-processing, and dos post-processing.
The pseudopotential for Fe is `PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/fe.upf`.
The pseudopotential for O is `PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/o.upf`.
The exchange-correlation functional is Local Density Approximation.
The calculation is spin-polarized.
Initial magnetic moments are set to +5.0 Bohr magnetons on tetrahedral Fe sites.
Initial magnetic moments are set to -5.0 Bohr magnetons on octahedral Fe sites.
A Hubbard U correction is not applied.
Spin-orbit coupling is not included.
The plane-wave kinetic energy cutoff is 90 Ry.
The charge density kinetic energy cutoff is 720 Ry.
Gaussian smearing is used with a width of 0.01 Ry.
The electronic convergence threshold for self-consistent field loops is 1.0E-6 Ry.
The `vc-relax` calculation uses a gamma-centered 6x6x6 k-point grid.
The total energy convergence threshold for the `vc-relax` calculation is 1.0E-4 Ry.
The force convergence threshold for the `vc-relax` calculation is 1.0E-3 Ry/Bohr.
The `scf` calculation uses the relaxed structure from the `vc-relax` step.
The `scf` calculation uses a gamma-centered 6x6x6 k-point grid.
The `nscf` calculation uses the charge density from the `scf` step.
The `nscf` calculation uses a gamma-centered 10x10x10 k-point grid.
The `bands` calculation uses the charge density from the `scf` step.
The `bands` calculation uses a k-point path along high-symmetry lines of the Brillouin zone.
The `bands` post-processing step uses the output of the `bands` calculation.
The `dos` post-processing step uses the output of the `nscf` calculation to compute the density of states.
