Q: What numerical flux function should be used for the convective terms?
A: Use the Rusanov (local Lax-Friedrichs) numerical flux. The formula is:
`F_hat = 0.5*(F_conv(U_L) + F_conv(U_R)) - 0.5*a*(U_R - U_L)`
where `F_conv` is the physical convective flux, and `a` is the interface wave speed `max( |v_L| + c_L, |v_R| + c_R )`, with `c = sqrt(Gamma*p/rho)`.
