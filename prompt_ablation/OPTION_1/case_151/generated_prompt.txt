The calculation is a variable-cell relaxation.
Retrieve the primitive crystal structure for Na3Bi corresponding to space group P6_3/mmc.
Use the LDA pseudopotential file PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/na.upf for Sodium.
Use the LDA pseudopotential file PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/bi.upf for Bismuth.
Do not include spin-orbit coupling in the calculation.
Use a plane-wave kinetic energy cutoff of 90 Ry.
Use a charge density cutoff of 720 Ry.
Use a gamma-centered Monkhorst-Pack k-point grid.
The k-point grid must have a density sufficient to converge the total energy to within 1 meV/atom.
Apply a half shift to the k-point grid.
Use Marzari-Vanderbilt smearing for the electronic occupations.
Use a smearing width of 0.01 Ry.
The convergence threshold for the electronic self-consistency loop is 1.0e-8 Ry.
The convergence threshold for the maximum force on any atom is 1.0e-4 Ry/au.
The convergence threshold for the total pressure is 0.2 kbar.
The final relaxed crystal structure is the primary output.
