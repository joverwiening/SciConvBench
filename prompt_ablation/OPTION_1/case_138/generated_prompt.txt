The analysis assumes a linear-elastic, isotropic material undergoing small deformations.
The analysis object is a prismatic bar configured as a cantilever beam with a length of 600 mm.
The cross-section of the bar is a solid rectangle with a width of 40 mm and a height of 120 mm.
The cross-sectional area is 4800 mm^2.
An axial tensile force of 30000 N is applied at the free end of the cantilever beam.
A transverse shear force of 12000 N is applied at the free end of the cantilever beam.
The response quantities are to be determined at the fixed support section of the cantilever beam.
The governing relation for average normal stress is the axial force divided by the cross-sectional area.
The calculated average normal stress at the section is 6.25 MPa.
The governing relation for average shear stress is the transverse shear force divided by the cross-sectional area.
The calculated average shear stress at the section is 2.5 MPa.
