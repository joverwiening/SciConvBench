The simulation is performed using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The problem solves 2D linear elasticity under the plane stress assumption.
The domain is a rectangle defined by corners (0, 0) and (1.0, 0.5).
A circular hole with a radius of 0.1 is centered at (0.5, 0.25).
The mesh is generated using triangular elements.
The mesh resolution parameter is set to 8, targeting approximately 72 elements.
The material's Young's modulus is 70e9 Pa.
The material's Poisson's ratio is 0.33.
The material's density is 2700 kg/m^3.
A body force representing gravity acts on the domain, defined as (0, -rho*g) where g is 9.81 m/s^2.
The left boundary (x=0) is fixed, with a displacement of (0, 0).
The bottom boundary (y=0) is fixed, with a displacement of (0, 0).
A shear traction of 1000 Pa is applied to the top boundary (y=0.5) in the positive x-direction.
The right boundary (x=1) is traction-free.
The boundary of the circular hole is traction-free.
The vector-valued displacement space uses continuous Lagrange basis functions of polynomial degree 2.
The system of linear equations is solved using a direct (LU) solver.
The displacement vector field is computed and saved.
The Cauchy stress tensor is computed from the displacement field.
The von Mises stress is computed from the in-plane components of the Cauchy stress tensor.
