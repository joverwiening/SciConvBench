Calculate the cavitation index for a valve.
The upstream pressure is 400 kPa.
The downstream pressure is 150 kPa.
The fluid is water.
The temperature of the water is 20 degrees Celsius.
The vapor pressure of water at 20 degrees Celsius is 2.34 kPa.
Use the formula: σ = (P_downstream - P_vapor) / (P_upstream - P_downstream).
