Calculate the total heat transfer from a straight rectangular fin.
The fin has a thermal conductivity (k) of 200 W/m·K.
The fin length (L) is 30 mm.
The fin thickness (t) is 2 mm.
The fin width (w) is 20 mm.
The fin is exposed to a surrounding fluid with a convection coefficient (h) of 25 W/m^2·K.
Assume the base of the fin is maintained at a temperature (Tb) of 100°C.
Assume the surrounding fluid temperature (T_inf) is 20°C.
The fin tip is to be treated as adiabatic.
Use the standard analytical formula for heat transfer from a fin with an adiabatic tip.
Report the final heat transfer value in Watts.
