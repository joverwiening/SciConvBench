Simulate the injection of a compressible gas into a liquid-filled tank using the `compressibleInterFoam` solver in OpenFOAM.
The goal is to observe the transient gas jet penetration and expansion into the liquid.
The simulation domain is a 2D axisymmetric cylinder representing the water tank.
The tank has a length of 1.0 meter and a radius of 0.5 meters.
A gas inlet nozzle with a diameter of 0.01 meters is located at the center of the bottom wall of the tank.
The gas phase is air, modeled as a perfect gas.
The liquid phase is water, modeled as an incompressible fluid.
The tank is initially filled with stationary water at a pressure of 101325 Pa.
The gas is injected through the nozzle with a total pressure of 506625 Pa.
The gas is injected with a total temperature of 300 K.
The gas inlet velocity is specified by a Mach number of 0.8.
The top boundary of the tank is a pressure outlet set to 101325 Pa.
All walls of the tank and nozzle are defined with no-slip boundary conditions.
The simulation will run for a total time of 0.05 seconds.
