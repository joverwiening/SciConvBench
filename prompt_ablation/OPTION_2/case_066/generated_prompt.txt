Compute the degree of reaction for an axial compressor rotor.
The inlet relative velocity, W1, is 150 m/s.
The inlet relative flow angle, beta1, is 45 degrees.
The outlet relative flow angle, beta2, is 30 degrees.
The blade speed, U, is 167 m/s.
The axial velocity, Cx, is constant through the rotor.
Calculate the constant axial velocity using the inlet conditions: Cx = W1 * cos(beta1).
Recalculate the outlet relative velocity, W2, using the constant axial velocity and the outlet flow angle: W2 = Cx / cos(beta2).
Calculate the degree of reaction, R, using the formula R = (Cx / (2*U)) * (tan(beta1) + tan(beta2)).
Report the final numerical value for the degree of reaction.
