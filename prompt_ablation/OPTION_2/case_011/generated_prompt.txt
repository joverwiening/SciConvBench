Use the N-mm-MPa system of units for all calculations and results.
Model a solid circular column with a diameter d = 25 mm and a length L = 2000 mm.
Assign material properties of structural steel: Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa and yield strength Sy = 250 MPa.
Set the effective length factor K = 0.7.
Apply a compressive axial load P = 25000 N.
Perform a yielding check by first calculating the cross-sectional area A = π * d^2 / 4.
Calculate the average compressive stress σ_avg = P / A.
Compare the calculated σ_avg to the yield strength Sy.
Perform an Euler buckling check by first calculating the area moment of inertia I = π * d^4 / 64.
Calculate the Euler critical buckling load P_cr = (π^2 * E * I) / (K * L)^2.
Compare the applied load P to the calculated P_cr.
To determine the more critical limit state, calculate a factor of safety against yielding, FS_yield = Sy / σ_avg.
Calculate a factor of safety against buckling, FS_buckling = P_cr / P.
If FS_yield is less than FS_buckling, state that yielding is the more critical limit state.
If FS_buckling is less than FS_yield, state that Euler buckling is the more critical limit state.
If the factors of safety are equal, state that both limit states are equally critical.
