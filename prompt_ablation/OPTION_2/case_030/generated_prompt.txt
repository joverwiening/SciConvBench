The simulation domain is a cube with corners at (0,0,0) and (1,1,1).
A rectangular obstacle is present in the domain, spanning from (0.375, 0.375, 0) to (0.625, 0.625, 0.25).
The domain is discretized with a uniform Cartesian mesh of 32x32x32 cells.
The simulation uses a Volume of Fluid (VOF) model for two incompressible, isothermal phases.
Gravity is enabled and set to (0, 0, -9.81) m/s^2.
Phase 1 is water with a density of 1000 kg/m^3 and a kinematic viscosity of 1.0e-6 m^2/s.
Phase 2 is air with a density of 1 kg/m^3 and a kinematic viscosity of 1.48e-5 m^2/s.
Surface tension between air and water is 0.07 N/m.
At t=0, the domain is initialized with a rectangular column of water (alpha.water = 1) spanning from (0, 0, 0) to (0.6, 0.1875, 0.75).
The rest of the domain is initialized with air (alpha.water = 0).
The top face of the domain (z=1) is an open atmosphere boundary.
For the atmosphere boundary, the pressure is a total pressure condition with a value of 0 Pa (gauge).
For the atmosphere boundary, the velocity is a pressure-inlet/outlet condition that allows flow in or out based on the pressure field.
For the atmosphere boundary, the phase fraction uses an inlet/outlet condition, with an inflow value of 0 (air) and a zero-gradient condition for outflow.
The remaining five faces of the domain are walls with a no-slip boundary condition for velocity.
The simulation runs for a total time of 0.1 seconds.
The time step is adjusted automatically to maintain a maximum Courant number of 0.5.
