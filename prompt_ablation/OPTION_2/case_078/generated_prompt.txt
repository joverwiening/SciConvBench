A hypoeutectic binary alloy has an overall composition (C_0) of 30 wt% B.
The composition of the primary solid phase (alpha) at the eutectic temperature is 12 wt% B.
The eutectic composition (C_e) is 40 wt% B.
Calculate the weight fraction of the eutectic microconstituent (W_e) present in the alloy just below the eutectic temperature.
Use the lever rule formula: W_e = (C_0 - C_alpha) / (C_e - C_alpha).
