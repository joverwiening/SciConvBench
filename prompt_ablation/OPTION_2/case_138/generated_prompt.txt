Analyze a prismatic bar under the assumption of small-deformation, linear-elastic solid mechanics.
The bar has a rectangular cross-section with a width of 40 mm and a height of 120 mm.
The cross-sectional area is 4800 mm^2.
The section of interest is subjected to an internal axial tensile force of 30,000 N.
The section of interest is also subjected to an internal transverse shear force of 12,000 N.
Assume the normal stress from the axial force is uniformly distributed across the cross-section, neglecting any stress variation due to bending.
The analysis will determine the average normal stress and average shear stress at this section.
The governing relation for average normal stress (σ_avg) is the axial force (P) divided by the cross-sectional area (A).
The governing relation for average shear stress (τ_avg) is the transverse shear force (V) divided by the cross-sectional area (A).
Present the final calculated values for both stresses and clearly state the governing relations used.
