Use N, mm, and MPa as the units for all calculations and results.
The prismatic bar has a rectangular cross-section with a width b of 30 mm and a height h of 100 mm.
The internal resultant normal force is a tension of P = 50000 N.
The internal resultant transverse shear force is V = 5000 N.
The shear force V acts parallel to the width b.
Assume uniform stress distributions for both normal and shear stresses when calculating average values.
Calculate the cross-sectional area A = b * h.
Compute the average normal stress on the cross-section using the formula σ_avg = P / A.
Compute the average shear stress on the cross-section using the formula τ_avg = V / A.
Compute the magnitude of the maximum in-plane shear stress using the formula τ_max = sqrt((σ_avg/2)^2 + τ_avg^2).
