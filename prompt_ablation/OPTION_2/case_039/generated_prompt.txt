Simulate a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem using the FEniCS legacy library (dolfin).
The simulation will model a perforated rectangular plate under the plane stress assumption.
The plate has dimensions of 1.0m in width (x-direction) and 0.5m in height (y-direction).
A circular hole with a radius of 0.1m is located at the center of the plate, at coordinates (0.5, 0.25).
The mesh resolution will correspond to 64 elements along the 1.0m edges and 32 elements along the 0.5m edges.
The material's Young's modulus is 70e9 Pa and its Poisson's ratio is 0.33.
The vector-valued displacement space will be discretized using second-order Lagrange finite elements ('P2').
A body force representing gravity will be included in the simulation.
The material density is 2700 kg/m^3, and the gravitational acceleration is 9.81 m/s^2, acting in the negative y-direction.
A uniform compressive vertical traction of -2500 Pa is applied to the top boundary (y=0.5).
A uniform compressive vertical traction of -2500 Pa is applied to the bottom boundary (y=0.0).
The boundary of the circular hole is traction-free.
On the left edge (x=0), the vertical displacement is constrained to zero (uy=0).
On the right edge (x=1), the horizontal displacement is constrained to zero (ux=0).
The resulting linear system of equations will be solved using a direct solver.
Following the computation of the displacement field, calculate the von Mises stress from the in-plane Cauchy stress components.
The final outputs of the simulation will be the displacement and von Mises stress fields.
