Solve the steady-state one-dimensional heat conduction equation d2T/dx2 = 0.
The domain is a slab of length 1.0 meter.
The boundary condition at x=0 is a fixed temperature of 100 degrees Celsius.
The boundary condition at x=1.0 is a fixed temperature of 20 degrees Celsius.
The material has a thermal diffusivity, alpha, of 1.11e-4 m^2/s, characteristic of copper.
The output is the temperature profile T(x) along the slab.
