Assess the expectation that two metals with a 25% difference in atomic radius will have complete substitutional solid solubility.
The assessment will be based on the Hume-Rothery size factor rule.
The criterion for the Hume-Rothery size factor rule is that the difference in atomic radii between the two elements must be less than 15% for complete substitutional solid solubility.
The provided atomic radius difference is 25%.
This 25% difference exceeds the 15% threshold established by the rule.
Therefore, the expectation of complete substitutional solid solubility based on the size factor rule alone is incorrect.
The assessment should conclude that significant, not complete, solid solubility is unlikely, and formation of an intermetallic compound is more probable.
