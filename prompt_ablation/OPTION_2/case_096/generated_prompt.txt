The simulation will be a two-dimensional Rayleigh-Benard convection problem.
The computational domain is a 2D box with a width-to-height aspect ratio of 1.0.
The simulation will use a Rayleigh number of 1e5.
The simulation will use a Prandtl number of 0.71.
The top and bottom walls will have a noSlip velocity boundary condition.
The top and bottom walls will have a fixedFluxPressure boundary condition for pressure (p_rgh).
The side walls will have a noSlip velocity boundary condition.
The side walls will have a fixedFluxPressure boundary condition for pressure (p_rgh).
The bottom wall will be held at a fixed temperature of 294.0 K.
The top wall will be held at a fixed temperature of 293.0 K.
The side walls will have a zeroGradient temperature boundary condition.
The initial velocity of the fluid will be zero everywhere.
The initial temperature field will be a linear gradient from the bottom wall temperature to the top wall temperature.
