Solve the steady-state one-dimensional heat conduction equation d2T/dx2 = 0.
The domain is a slab of length L = 1.0 meter, from x=0 to x=L.
The boundary condition at x=0 is a fixed temperature of 100 degrees Celsius.
The boundary condition at x=L is a fixed temperature of 20 degrees Celsius.
The thermal diffusivity, alpha, does not affect the steady-state temperature distribution.
The final output will be the temperature profile T(x) along the slab.
