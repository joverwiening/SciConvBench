Calculate the lift per unit span on a rotating cylinder.
The calculation will use the Kutta-Joukowski theorem.
The lift per unit span (L') is given by the equation L' = ρ * V * Γ.
The circulation (Γ) is given by the equation Γ = 2 * π * ω * R².
The radius of the cylinder (R) is 0.1 m.
The rotational speed of the cylinder (ω) is 200 rad/s.
The freestream velocity of the air (V) is 15 m/s.
The density of the air (ρ) is 1.225 kg/m³.
