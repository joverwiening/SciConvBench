A Neumann boundary condition is applied to the vertical edge at x = 0, representing a normal traction of 4000 Pa in the positive x-direction.
The bottom edge at y = 0 is treated as a free surface with a zero-traction Neumann boundary condition.
Solve the resulting linear system using a direct solver.
