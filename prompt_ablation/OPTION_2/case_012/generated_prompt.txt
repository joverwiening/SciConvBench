Calculate the number-average molecular weight (M_n) for a polymer sample.
The sample has the following molecular-weight distribution:
Component 1 has a molecular weight of 56526 g/mol and a count of 96.
Component 2 has a molecular weight of 92809 g/mol and a count of 131.
Component 3 has a molecular weight of 115856 g/mol and a count of 153.
Component 4 has a molecular weight of 126029 g/mol and a count of 146.
Use the formula M_n = (Σ N_i * M_i) / (Σ N_i), where N_i is the count and M_i is the molecular weight for each component.
Sum the products of the count and molecular weight for all components to get the numerator.
Sum the counts of all components to get the denominator.
Divide the numerator by the denominator to find M_n.
State the final result in units of g/mol.
