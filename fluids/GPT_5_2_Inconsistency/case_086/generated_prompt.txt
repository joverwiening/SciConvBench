Assume steady incompressible flow in a constant-area pipe with cross-sectional area A (same at inlet and outlet).
Use the relation v = Q/A to compute average velocity at a section.
Compute inlet velocity as v_in = Q_in/A = 0.01/A m/s.
Compute outlet velocity as v_out = Q_out/A = 0.02/A m/s.
State that for a physically consistent steady incompressible constant-area pipe, continuity requires Q_in = Q_out, so the given unequal flow rates imply accumulation/leakage/measurement error; the computed velocities above are therefore formal values based on the provided Q at each section.
