Assume steady, incompressible, fully developed flow in a straight, horizontal, circular pipe.  
Use the Darcy–Weisbach head-loss relation h_f = f (L/D) (V^2/(2g)).  
Use a physically valid Darcy friction factor f = 0.02.  
Use SI units throughout.  
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².  
Assume water at room temperature as the working fluid (properties not used in the calculation).  
Assume a typical small-process/utility pipe length L = 100 m.  
Assume a typical small-process/utility pipe inner diameter D = 0.10 m.  
Assume a typical design flow velocity V = 2.0 m/s.  
Compute velocity head V^2/(2g) = (2.0^2)/(2·9.81) = 0.2039 m.  
Compute length-to-diameter ratio L/D = 100/0.10 = 1000.  
Compute head loss h_f = 0.02 · 1000 · 0.2039 = 4.08 m.  
Report the pipe head loss as h_f ≈ 4.1 m of fluid head.
