Q: Which side is “inner” for your pressure difference: do you want Δp = p_inner − p_outer?
A: Yes—use **Δp = p_outer − p_inner** with **n directed outward from the center of curvature**. Since pressure increases toward the center, **p_inner > p_outer**, so this Δp will be negative (its magnitude is the pressure drop from inner to outer).
