Use steady inviscid normal-momentum (streamline curvature) relation: ∂p/∂n = ρ V^2 / R with n directed outward from the center of curvature.
With n outward, pressure increases toward the center implies ∂p/∂n is negative, so ∂p/∂n = −ρ V^2 / R.
Assume V and R are constant across the small normal distance Δn = 0.10 m.
Compute Δp = p_outer − p_inner = (∂p/∂n) Δn.
Substitute values: Δp = −(1000 kg/m^3)(10 m/s)^2/(5 m) × (0.10 m).
Evaluate: Δp = −2000 Pa.
