Use the steady, incompressible Bernoulli equation between inlet (point 1) and outlet (point 2).
Assume the outlet is 15 m higher than the inlet, so z2 − z1 = +15 m.
Assume the fluid is water with density ρ = 1000 kg/m^3.
Assume the inlet and outlet diameters are equal so v1 = v2 and the velocity-head terms cancel.
Assume no pumps/turbines and neglect all head losses, so hL = 0.
Compute the pressure difference from Bernoulli as p1 − p2 = ρ g (z2 − z1).
Use g = 9.81 m/s^2.
Calculate p1 − p2 = (1000)(9.81)(15) Pa = 147150 Pa ≈ 147 kPa.
State the result as the inlet pressure is higher than the outlet pressure by approximately 147 kPa (gauge pressure difference).
