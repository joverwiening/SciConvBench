Use the Darcy–Weisbach equation in the form Δp = f (L/D) (ρ v^2/2).
Use f = 0.03, L = 25 m, D = 0.08 m, v = 1.5 m/s.
Compute L/D = 25/0.08 = 312.5.
Compute ρ v^2/2 = ρ(1.5^2)/2 = 1.125 ρ Pa.
Compute Δp = 0.03 × 312.5 × 1.125 ρ = 10.546875 ρ Pa.
Report the pressure drop as Δp ≈ 10.55 ρ Pa, which requires the oil density ρ to produce a numeric value in Pa.
