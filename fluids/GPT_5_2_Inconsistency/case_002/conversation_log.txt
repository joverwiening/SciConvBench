Q: Should the pressure drop be calculated using the Darcy–Weisbach friction term (i.e., include head loss due to viscosity), or do you want to use only the provided Bernoulli form with no loss term (which would give zero pressure drop for equal inlet/outlet velocities in a horizontal pipe)?

\[
\Delta p = f_D \frac{L}{D}\left(\frac{\rho v^2}{2}\right)
