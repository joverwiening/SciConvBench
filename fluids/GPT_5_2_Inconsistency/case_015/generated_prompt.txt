Compute the Nusselt number for turbulent water flow in a smooth circular pipe of diameter 40 mm at Reynolds number Re = 50,000 and Prandtl number Pr = 5.
Use the Dittus–Boelter correlation Nu = 0.023 Re^0.8 Pr^n.
Use n = 0.4 (heating of the fluid).
Calculate Nu numerically from the given Re and Pr.
Report the resulting Nusselt number as a dimensionless value.
