Q: Stokes’ Law gives Cd = 24/Re.  
At Re = 10^5, Cd = 24 / 100000 = 2.4 × 10^-4.  

Do you want the physically correct drag coefficient for a sphere at Re = 10^5 (since Stokes’ Law is not valid at that Reynolds number)?
A: Yes—use the physically correct value from experimental drag curves. For a smooth sphere at \(Re = 10^5\) (pre–drag crisis), take \(C_d \approx 0.5\) (order 0.4–0.5). Do **not** use Stokes’ Law here.
