Q: What is the cooling capacity given (5 kW) referring to: the heat removed from the low-temperature space (QL) or the heat rejected to the high-temperature reservoir (QH)?
A: It refers to the cooling load, i.e., the heat removed from the low-temperature space \(Q_L = 5\ \text{kW}\).
