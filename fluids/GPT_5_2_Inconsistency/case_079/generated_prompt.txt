Given a refrigerator operating between TH = 300 K and TL = 250 K with COPR = 20.
Interpret the given 5 kW as the cooling load, i.e., QL = 5 kW (heat removed from the low-temperature space).
Use the refrigerator COP definition COPR = QL / Winput.
Compute the required work input as Winput = QL / COPR = 5 kW / 20 = 0.25 kW.
Report the required work as 0.25 kW (250 W).
