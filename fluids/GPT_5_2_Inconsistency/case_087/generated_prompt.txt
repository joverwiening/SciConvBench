Assume the pumped fluid is water with density ρ = 1000 kg/m³.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Convert the flow rate to SI units: Q = 50 L/s = 0.050 m³/s.
Use the given head H = 30 m.
Compute hydraulic power using P = ρ g Q H.
Calculate P = 1000 × 9.81 × 0.050 × 30 = 14,715 W.
