Assume steady, incompressible flow of water with density ρ = 1000 kg/m^3.
Assume the pipe has the same diameter at inlet and outlet so V1 = V2 and the kinetic energy terms cancel.
Assume no pump/turbine work and neglect head losses (friction and minor losses).
Use Bernoulli between inlet (point 1) and outlet (point 2): P1/ρg + z1 = P2/ρg + z2.
Use the elevation difference z2 − z1 = +15 m (outlet is 15 m higher).
Solve for the pressure difference: P1 − P2 = ρ g (z2 − z1).
Compute ΔP = P1 − P2 = (1000)(9.81)(15) Pa = 147150 Pa ≈ 147 kPa.
State the result as: the inlet pressure must be about 147 kPa higher than the outlet pressure to overcome the 15 m elevation rise under the stated assumptions.
