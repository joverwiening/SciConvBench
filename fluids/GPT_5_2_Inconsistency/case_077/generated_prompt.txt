Treat the Brayton-cycle compression process as isentropic.
Assume the working fluid is air with γ = 1.4.
Assume the compressor inlet temperature is T1 = 300 K.
Assume the compressor pressure ratio is p2/p1 = 10.
Compute the compressor exit temperature using T2/T1 = (p2/p1)^((γ−1)/γ).
Use (γ−1)/γ = 0.4/1.4 = 0.285714.
Compute T2 = 300 × 10^0.285714 = 300 × 1.9307 ≈ 579 K.
