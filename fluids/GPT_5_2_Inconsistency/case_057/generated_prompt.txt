Compute the pressure drop per unit length Δp/L for fully developed laminar internal flow in a microchannel with hydraulic diameter Dh = 2 mm at Reynolds number Re = 200.
Assume the working fluid is liquid water at 20 °C with properties ρ = 998.2 kg/m^3 and μ = 1.002×10^-3 Pa·s.
Do not use the Manning equation or any Manning roughness coefficient n.
Use the laminar Darcy friction factor relation fD = 64/Re.
Compute the mean velocity from Re = ρ V Dh / μ.
Compute the pressure gradient using Darcy–Weisbach in per-length form Δp/L = fD (ρ V^2 / 2) / Dh.
Report Δp/L in Pa/m.
Optionally also report the intermediate values V (m/s) and fD (dimensionless) used in the calculation.
