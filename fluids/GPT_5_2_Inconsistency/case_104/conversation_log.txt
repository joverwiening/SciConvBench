Q: Do you want the physically correct heat removed using latent heat of condensation at 100 °C (i.e., use h_fg rather than q = m c_p ΔT)?
A: Yes—use the physically correct latent heat of condensation at 100 °C:  
\[
q = m\,h_{fg}\ \text{(at 100 °C from steam tables)}
\]
Do **not** use \(q = m c_p \Delta T\) with \(\Delta T=0\) for the phase change. If the condensate is subcooled below 100 °C, add sensible cooling for that portion.
