Compute the heat removed when saturated water vapor at 100 C condenses to saturated liquid water at 100 C in a condenser.
Use a mass basis of 1 kg of steam condensed.
Do not use q = m cp ΔT for the phase-change portion because ΔT = 0 does not capture latent heat.
Use steam-table latent heat of vaporization at 100 C, h_fg(100 C), for the condensation heat removal.
Calculate q_removed = m * h_fg(100 C) and report the result in kJ for the 1 kg basis.
Use h_fg(100 C) ≈ 2257 kJ/kg unless a different steam-table value is specified.
If subcooling of the condensate below 100 C is later specified, add sensible cooling as q_sensible = m * c_p,liquid * (100 C − T_out) using an appropriate liquid-water c_p, and include it in the total heat removed.
