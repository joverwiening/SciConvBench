Q: What energy-loss (head loss) value should I use across the hydraulic jump when applying Bernoulli’s equation?
A: Don’t use Bernoulli across the hydraulic jump and don’t assume any head-loss value. The complete requirement is to compute \(y_2\) using the momentum (specific force) equation because mechanical energy is not conserved across the jump.

Q: Do you want the downstream depth y2 computed assuming a horizontal channel bed (same invert elevation upstream and downstream across the jump)?
A: Yes—assume a horizontal channel bed with the same invert elevation upstream and downstream across the jump (no bed elevation change).
