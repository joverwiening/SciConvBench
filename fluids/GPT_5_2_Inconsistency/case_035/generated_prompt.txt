Compute the pressure difference between r1 = 0.1 m and r2 = 0.3 m in a forced vortex in a cylindrical tank of water with tangential velocity vθ = ω r.
Assume inviscid flow and apply Bernoulli along a radial line at the same elevation (neglect gravity effects along the radial line).
Use water density ρ = 1000 kg/m³.
Leave the result symbolic in terms of ω (do not assume a numeric value for ω).
Use the relation Δp = p(r2) − p(r1) = (1/2) ρ ω² (r2² − r1²).
Substitute r1 = 0.1 m and r2 = 0.3 m to obtain Δp = (1/2)(1000) ω² (0.3² − 0.1²) Pa = 40,000 ω² Pa.
