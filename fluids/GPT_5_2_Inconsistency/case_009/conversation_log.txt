Q: Since the flow is rotational and A and B are on different streamlines, what assumption or justification should be used to allow the Bernoulli constant to be the same between A and B?
A: Assume the flow is **irrotational (zero vorticity)**, so the **Bernoulli constant is uniform throughout the entire flow field**, not just along a streamline. That justifies using the same Bernoulli constant between A and B even though they’re on different streamlines.
