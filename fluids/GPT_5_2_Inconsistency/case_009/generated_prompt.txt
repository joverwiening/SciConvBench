Assume the flow is steady, inviscid, incompressible, and irrotational so the Bernoulli constant is uniform throughout the flow field.
Use fluid density ρ = 1000 kg/m³ and gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Use Bernoulli between points A and B: pA/ρ + VA²/2 + g zA = pB/ρ + VB²/2 + g zB.
Substitute pA = 200 kPa, VA = 5 m/s, zA = 0 m, VB = 8 m/s, zB = 0 m.
Compute pB = pA + 0.5 ρ (VA² − VB²).
Compute pB = 200,000 Pa + 0.5(1000)(25 − 64) Pa = 180,500 Pa.
Report pB = 180.5 kPa.
