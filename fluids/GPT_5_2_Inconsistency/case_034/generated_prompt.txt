Model steady, incompressible water flow from a large reservoir free surface at elevation z1 = 0 m to a discharge (pipe exit) at elevation z2 = 5 m.
Assume the pipe is frictionless and there are no head losses (hL = 0).
Include an actual pump that adds head hp = +20 m between the reservoir and the discharge.
Assume both the reservoir free surface and the discharge are exposed to atmosphere so p1 = p2 and pressure heads cancel.
Assume the reservoir is large so the free-surface velocity is negligible (V1 ≈ 0).
Apply Bernoulli with pump head: z1 + p1/γ + V1^2/(2g) + hp = z2 + p2/γ + V2^2/(2g) + hL.
Solve for exit velocity: V2 = sqrt(2g (z1 + hp − z2)).
Use g = 9.81 m/s².
Compute V2 = sqrt(2·9.81·(0 + 20 − 5)) = sqrt(294.3) = 17.16 m/s (≈ 17.2 m/s).
