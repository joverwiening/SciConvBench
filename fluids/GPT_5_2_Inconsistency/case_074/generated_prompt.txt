Model air flow in a duct compressed from p1 = 1 bar to p2 = 10 bar.
Treat the flow as steady, one-dimensional, and single-inlet/single-outlet.
Use compressible mass continuity with variable density.
Define inlet area A1 and outlet area A2 as symbolic variables.
Define mass flow rate m_dot as a symbolic variable.
Compute velocities from continuity as v1 = m_dot/(rho1*A1) and v2 = m_dot/(rho2*A2).
Compute densities from the ideal gas law as rho1 = p1/(R*T1) and rho2 = p2/(R*T2).
Use R = 287 J/(kg·K) for air unless otherwise specified.
Leave inlet temperature T1 symbolic.
Leave outlet temperature T2 symbolic.
Optionally, if a numerical closure is later required, assume isothermal compression as a best-practice simplification so T2 = T1.
