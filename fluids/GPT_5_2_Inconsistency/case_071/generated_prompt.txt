Compute the capillary rise (height change) of mercury in a vertical glass tube using Jurin’s law.
Use contact angle theta = 0 degrees.
Use surface tension sigma = 0.48 N/m.
Use tube inner radius r = 0.0005 m.
Assume standard gravity g = 9.81 m/s^2.
Use mercury density rho = 13595 kg/m^3 unless otherwise specified.
Compute h = (2*sigma*cos(theta)) / (rho*g*r).
Report h in meters with sign convention such that positive h indicates rise and negative h indicates depression.
Provide the numeric result for h and state whether mercury rises or is depressed for the given theta.
