Compute the isentropic (Saint-Venant) pitot-to-static pressure ratio p0/p for a subsonic flow at Mach number M = 0.5.
Do not use the Rayleigh–Pitot formula.
Use the isentropic relation p0/p = (1 + ((gamma − 1)/2) M^2)^(gamma/(gamma − 1)).
Use gamma = 1.4.
Compute p0/p numerically for M = 0.5.
Report the final value of p0/p rounded to 3 significant figures.
