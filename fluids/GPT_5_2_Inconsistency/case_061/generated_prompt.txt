Given rotor relative inlet speed W1 = 150 m/s at β1 = 45 deg and relative exit speed W2 at β2 = 30 deg.
Assume axial velocity is constant through the rotor.
Enforce constant axial velocity by setting Va = W1 cos(β1) and correcting W2 so that Va = W2 cos(β2).
Compute Va = 150 cos(45 deg) = 106.07 m/s.
Compute corrected W2 = Va / cos(30 deg) = 122.47 m/s.
Assume a typical axial-compressor design work coefficient (stage loading) ψ = ΔCθ/U = 0.4.
Use rotor velocity-triangle relations Cθ = U − Wθ with Wθ = W sin(β) and Ca = Va.
Compute Wθ1 = 150 sin(45 deg) = 106.07 m/s.
Compute Wθ2 = 122.47 sin(30 deg) = 61.24 m/s.
Compute ΔCθ = Cθ2 − Cθ1 = (U − Wθ2) − (U − Wθ1) = Wθ1 − Wθ2 = 44.83 m/s.
Compute blade speed from ψ = ΔCθ/U as U = ΔCθ/ψ = 44.83/0.4 = 112.07 m/s.
Compute Cθ1 = U − Wθ1 = 112.07 − 106.07 = 6.00 m/s.
Compute Cθ2 = U − Wθ2 = 112.07 − 61.24 = 50.83 m/s.
Compute degree of reaction using R = 1 − (Cθ1 + Cθ2)/(2U).
Compute R = 1 − (6.00 + 50.83)/(2×112.07) = 0.747.
Report degree of reaction R ≈ 0.75 under the stated assumptions.
