Compute the kinetic-energy correction factor alpha for fully developed laminar flow of water in a circular pipe using the physically correct parabolic velocity profile.
Use the standard definition alpha = (1/A V^3) ∫_A u^3 dA, where V is the cross-sectional mean velocity.
Use the fully developed laminar pipe-flow profile u(r) = 2V(1 − (r/R)^2), with R = D/2.
Evaluate the integral over the circular cross-section in cylindrical coordinates.
Report the resulting value alpha = 2.
