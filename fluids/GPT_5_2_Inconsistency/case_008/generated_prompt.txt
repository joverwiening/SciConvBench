Compute air density using the ideal-gas relation at static conditions: ρ = p/(R T) with p = 100,000 Pa, R = 287 J/(kg·K), T = 300 K, giving ρ ≈ 1.16 kg/m^3.
Use the incompressible Pitot-static/Bernoulli relation between stagnation and static pressure: Δp = p0 − p = (1/2) ρ V^2.
Substitute Δp = 20,000 Pa and ρ ≈ 1.16 kg/m^3 and solve for velocity: V = sqrt(2 Δp / ρ).
Report the result: V ≈ sqrt(2×20,000 / 1.16) ≈ 186 m/s.
