Compute the film temperature as Tf = (Ts + T∞)/2 = (80°C + 20°C)/2 = 50°C.
Use ΔT = Ts − T∞ = 60 K.
Assume air at 1 atm and evaluate properties at Tf = 50°C.
Compute the thermal expansion coefficient using the user-specified rule β = 1/Tf,°C = 1/50 = 0.02 1/°C (treat as 0.02 1/K for use in Ra).
Use g = 9.81 m/s² and L = 0.5 m.
Use air properties at 50°C, 1 atm: ν = 1.80×10^-5 m²/s and α = 2.55×10^-5 m²/s.
Compute Ra = g β ΔT L^3 /(ν α).
Compute the numerator: g β ΔT L^3 = 9.81 × 0.02 × 60 × (0.5)^3 = 1.4715.
Compute the denominator: ν α = (1.80×10^-5)(2.55×10^-5) = 4.59×10^-10.
Compute Ra = 1.4715 / (4.59×10^-10) = 3.21×10^9.
