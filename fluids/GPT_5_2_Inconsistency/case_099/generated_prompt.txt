Use air at 25 °C with Pr = 0.7.
Assume turbulent, fully developed internal flow in a smooth circular tube.
Assume Reynolds number Re = 1.0×10^5 as a representative value within the Dittus–Boelter validity range (Re ≥ 10,000).
Use the Dittus–Boelter correlation Nu = 0.023 Re^0.8 Pr^n.
Assume heating of the fluid so n = 0.4.
Compute Nu = 0.023 (1.0×10^5)^0.8 (0.7)^0.4.
Report Nu ≈ 2.0×10^2 (approximately 200).
