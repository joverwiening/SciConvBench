Q: Which fluid and temperature should I use for the calculation (air at 25 °C with Pr ≈ 0.7, or a different fluid/temperature consistent with Pr = 7.0)?
A: Use **air at 25 °C**, and therefore use **Pr ≈ 0.7** (typical for air at that temperature).  

If you intend to use **Pr = 7.0**, then you need to **change the fluid/temperature** to something consistent with that (e.g., **water near room temperature** or an **oil**) and specify which one before proceeding.

Make a reasonable assumption based on best practices—i.e., pick a **turbulent, fully developed internal-flow** value in the Dittus–Boelter validity range, **Re ≥ 10,000** (commonly something like **Re = 1×10^5** if you need a single representative value).
