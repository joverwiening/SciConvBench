Q: What is the temperature difference between the fin base and the ambient air (Tb − T∞)?
A: Make a reasonable assumption based on best practices. The complete requirement does not specify \(T_b - T_\infty\), so you can either:

- Leave the result in terms of \(\theta_b = T_b - T_\infty\), i.e., report \(q_f = (\cdots)\,\theta_b\), **or**
- Assume a representative value (commonly \(\theta_b = 50\ \text{K}\)) if a numeric heat rate is required.
