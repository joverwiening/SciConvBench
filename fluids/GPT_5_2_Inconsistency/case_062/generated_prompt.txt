Compute the sequent-depth ratio y2/y1 for a hydraulic jump in a rectangular open channel.
Use the standard conjugate-depth relation from momentum conservation: y2/y1 = 0.5*(sqrt(1 + 8*Fr1^2) - 1).
Use the upstream Froude number Fr1 = 2.0.
Evaluate numerically: y2/y1 = 0.5*(sqrt(1 + 8*(2.0)^2) - 1) = 0.5*(sqrt(33) - 1) ≈ 2.372.
