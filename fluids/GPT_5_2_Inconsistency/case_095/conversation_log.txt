Q: What specific quantity do you want the momentum flux expressed in (e.g., in terms of mean velocity V, volumetric flow rate Q, pressure drop Δp, or wall shear stress τw)?
A: Express the momentum flux in terms of the mean (bulk) velocity \(V\) and cross-sectional area \(A\), using \(\beta = \tfrac{4}{3}\):  
\[
\dot{P} = \beta\,\rho\,A\,V^{2}.
\]
