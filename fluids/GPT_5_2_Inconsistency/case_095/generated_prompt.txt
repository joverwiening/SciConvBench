Assume steady, fully developed, incompressible laminar flow in a circular pipe.
Use the momentum correction factor β = 4/3 for fully developed laminar pipe flow.
Define mean (bulk) velocity as V and cross-sectional area as A.
Compute the axial momentum flux (momentum flow rate) through the cross-section as \(\dot{P} = \beta\,\rho\,A\,V^{2}\).
Substitute β = 4/3 to obtain \(\dot{P} = \frac{4}{3}\,\rho\,A\,V^{2}\).
