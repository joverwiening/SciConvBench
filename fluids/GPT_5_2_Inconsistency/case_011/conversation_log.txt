Q: What loss model should be used for the sudden expansion (e.g., include the Borda–Carnot loss \(h_L=(V_1-V_2)^2/(2g)\), or assume no losses)?
A: Use the **Borda–Carnot sudden expansion loss model**, i.e.:

\[
h_L=\frac{(V_1 - V_2)^2}{2g}
\]

Include this head loss in the energy (Bernoulli) equation when computing the pressure rise.
