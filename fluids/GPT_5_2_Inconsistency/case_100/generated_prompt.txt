Assume steady incompressible inviscid flow along a streamline from the free stream to the stagnation point.
Define the pressure coefficient as Cp = (p − p∞) / q∞.
Define the free-stream dynamic pressure as q∞ = (1/2) ρ U∞^2 using the same U∞ as the free-stream velocity in Bernoulli.
Apply Bernoulli between the free stream and the stagnation point: p∞ + (1/2) ρ U∞^2 = p0 + (1/2) ρ (0)^2.
Rearrange to obtain p0 − p∞ = (1/2) ρ U∞^2 = q∞.
Substitute into the Cp definition to get Cp0 = (p0 − p∞)/q∞ = 1.
Conclude that Bernoulli predicts a stagnation-point pressure coefficient of 1 for incompressible inviscid flow, so Cp = 1.5 cannot be verified by Bernoulli and must arise from additional physics or a different Cp definition.
