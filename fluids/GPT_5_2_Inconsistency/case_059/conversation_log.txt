Q: For a rectangular channel, should I assume the discharge per unit width q = 1.0 m^2/s is correct and use it to compute the critical depth (ignoring the inconsistent given y and V)?
A: Yes—assume \(q = 1.0\ \text{m}^2/\text{s}\) is correct and compute the critical depth from it (ignore the inconsistent given \(y\) and \(V\)). Use:

\[
y_c=\left(\frac{q^2}{g}\right)^{1/3}
\]

With \(g=9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\):

\[
y_c=\left(\frac{1.0^2}{9.81}\right)^{1/3}\approx 0.467\ \text{m}
\]
