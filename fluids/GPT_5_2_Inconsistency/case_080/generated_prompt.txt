Assume a straight thin rectangular plate fin of uniform cross-section with an adiabatic tip.  
Use the adiabatic-tip fin efficiency model η_f = tanh(mL)/(mL).  
Compute m = sqrt(hP/(kA_c)).  
Assume ambient temperature T∞ = 25 °C (298 K).  
Assume base temperature T_b = 75 °C (348 K) so that (T_b − T∞) = 50 K.  
Assume natural convection in air with h = 10 W/m²·K.  
Assume the fin material is aluminum with k = 205 W/m·K.  
Assume fin length L = 0.05 m.  
Assume fin width w = 0.05 m.  
Assume fin thickness t = 0.002 m.  
Compute cross-sectional area A_c = w·t.  
Compute fin perimeter P = 2(w + t).  
Compute fin efficiency η_f from the model using the assumed parameters.  
Compute fin surface area excluding the tip A_f = P·L.  
Compute fin heat transfer rate q_f = η_f·h·A_f·(T_b − T∞).  
Report the assumed inputs, the computed m, η_f, A_f, and q_f.
