Compute the available NPSH for a centrifugal pump drawing water from an open reservoir at 25°C.
Use the absolute-pressure form NPSH_a = (p_atm/(ρ g)) − h_s − h_L − (P_v/(ρ g)).
Use p_atm = 101.3 kPa.
Treat suction lift as h_s = +4.0 m and subtract it in the formula.
Use suction-line head loss h_L = 1.0 m.
Use vapor pressure P_v = 3.2 kPa.
Use water density ρ = 997 kg/m³.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Report NPSH_a in meters of water.
