Q: Do you want the shear stress computed using a Bingham plastic model (τ = τ_y + μ_p·γ̇) rather than the Newtonian relation τ = μ·γ̇?
A: Yes—use the Bingham plastic model: \( \tau = \tau_y + \mu_p \,\dot{\gamma} \) with \( \tau_y = 20\ \text{Pa} \) and \( \mu_p = 0.05\ \text{Pa·s} \).
