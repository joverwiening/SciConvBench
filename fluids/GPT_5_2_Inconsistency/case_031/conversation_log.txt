Q: What is the orientation of the quarter-cylinder (is the curved surface a quadrant in a vertical plane, with the center of curvature at the free surface so the arc spans from the free surface down to depth R)?
A: Yes. The curved surface is a quarter-circle in a **vertical plane**, with the **top edge of the arc at the free surface** and the arc extending **downward to a maximum depth of \(R = 2.0\ \text{m}\)**. The gate has constant width \(b = 1.0\ \text{m}\) into the page.
