The fluid is water with density ρ = 1000 kg/m³ and gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Model the gate as a quarter-cylindrical curved surface of radius R = 2.0 m and width b = 1.0 m, with the arc in a vertical plane.
Set the free surface at y = 0 and measure depth y positive downward.
Take the top edge of the curved surface at the free surface (y = 0) and the bottom edge at y = R.
Compute the horizontal hydrostatic force component on the curved surface using the vertical (horizontal-force) projection area A_v = bR.
Use the centroid depth of the vertical projection ȳ_v = R/2.
Use the second moment of area of the vertical projection about its horizontal centroidal axis I_G,v = bR³/12.
Compute the center of pressure depth for the horizontal component as y_cp = ȳ_v + I_G,v/(ȳ_v A_v).
Compute the horizontal component magnitude as F_H = ρ g A_v ȳ_v.
Report the line of action of F_H at depth y_cp below the free surface, acting horizontally on the gate.
Compute the vertical hydrostatic force component using the horizontal projection area A_h equal to the plan-view projection of the curved surface, A_h = bR.
Use the centroid depth of the horizontal projection ȳ_h = R/2.
Compute the vertical component magnitude as F_V = ρ g A_h ȳ_h.
Report the line of action of F_V through the centroid of the horizontal projection, located at mid-width and at the mid-depth y = R/2, acting vertically upward on the gate.
