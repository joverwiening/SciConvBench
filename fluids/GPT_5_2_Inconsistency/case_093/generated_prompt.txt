Assume steady incompressible inviscid flow with Bernoulli applicable along a streamline from the freestream to the stagnation point.
Define the stagnation-point pressure coefficient as Cp0 = (p0 − p∞)/(0.5 ρ U∞^2).
Write Bernoulli between the freestream (speed U∞, pressure p∞) and the stagnation point (speed 0, pressure p0): p∞ + 0.5 ρ U∞^2 = p0 + 0.5 ρ (0)^2.
Rearrange to obtain p0 − p∞ = 0.5 ρ U∞^2.
Substitute into the Cp0 definition to get Cp0 = (0.5 ρ U∞^2)/(0.5 ρ U∞^2) = 1.
State explicitly that Cp0 = 1 is the Bernoulli result and does not depend on the numerical value of U∞ because it cancels.
Conclude that a reported stagnation-point Cp = 1.5 cannot be verified under these incompressible inviscid Bernoulli assumptions.
Flag Cp = 1.5 as likely arising from a different Cp definition/reference, or from physics outside the model (e.g., compressibility, viscous losses, unsteadiness, or measurement/processing error).
