Assume water at 20 °C with density ρ = 998 kg/m^3.
Assume average flow velocity v = 1.0 m/s.
Use pipe length L = 30 m.
Use pipe diameter D = 0.1 m.
Use Fanning friction factor f = 0.005 as given.
Compute L/D = 30/0.1 = 300.
Compute dynamic pressure term (ρ v^2/2) = 998·(1.0^2)/2 = 499 Pa.
Compute pressure drop Δp = f (L/D) (ρ v^2/2) = 0.005·300·499 Pa = 748.5 Pa.
Report Δp ≈ 7.49×10^2 Pa = 0.749 kPa.
