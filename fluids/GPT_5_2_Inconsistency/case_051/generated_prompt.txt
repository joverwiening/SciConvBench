Compute the internal convective heat transfer coefficient h for water flowing in a circular pipe at Reynolds number Re = 800 and Prandtl number Pr = 6.
Treat the flow as laminar and fully developed.
Assume constant wall temperature boundary condition.
Use the fully developed laminar correlation Nu = 3.66.
Use water thermal conductivity k = 0.60 W/m·K.
Assume pipe inner diameter D = 0.01 m.
Compute h using h = Nu·k/D.
Report Nu and h in W/m²·K.
Use h = 3.66×0.60/0.01 = 219.6 W/m²·K (≈ 220 W/m²·K).
