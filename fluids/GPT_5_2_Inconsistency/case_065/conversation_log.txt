Q: What is the definition of cavitation factor σ you want me to use (i.e., the exact formula in terms of Ha, Hv, H, and the installation height above tailrace)?
A: Use Thoma’s cavitation factor:

\[
\sigma=\frac{H_a - H_v - h_s - h_{\text{loss}}}{H}
\]

where:
- \(H_a\) = atmospheric head at the site,
- \(H_v\) = vapor pressure head,
- \(h_s\) = suction head (runner elevation above tailrace; here \(h_s = 20\,\text{m}\)),
- \(h_{\text{loss}}\) = draft-tube/suction-side head losses (if not given, make a reasonable assumption based on best practices),
- \(H\) = operating net head of the turbine.

Q: What value should I use for the suction-side head loss h_loss (in meters of water head)?
A: The complete requirement doesn’t specify a value for \(h_{\text{loss}}\). Make a reasonable assumption based on best practices—use \(h_{\text{loss}} \approx 1\,\text{m}\) of water head (you can also state it’s “small” and take \(0\)–\(1\,\text{m}\) for a sensitivity check).
