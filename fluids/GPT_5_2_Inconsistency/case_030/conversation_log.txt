Q: Do you want the wave speed computed using the provided shallow-water formula c = sqrt(g h) even though the wave is described as deep-water (h/λ = 5)?
A: No—use the deep-water dispersion relation. Compute the phase speed with:

\[
c=\sqrt{\frac{g\,\lambda}{2\pi}}
\]

(using \(g \approx 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\), \(\lambda=20\ \text{m}\)).
