Q: If we must assume perfectly incompressible water, should I treat the speed of sound as undefined/infinite (and therefore reject the “finite value” requirement), or should I instead relax “perfectly incompressible” to “very slightly compressible” so a finite speed can be computed?
A: Relax “perfectly incompressible” and compute a finite value using the compressible-fluid formula \(c=\sqrt{K/\rho}\) with \(K=2.2\text{ GPa}\) and \(\rho=998\text{ kg/m}^3\).
