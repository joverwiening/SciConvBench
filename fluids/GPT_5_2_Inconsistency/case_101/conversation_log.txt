Q: What specific quantity should the momentum flux be expressed in terms of (e.g., ρ and mean velocity V, or ρ and volumetric flow rate Q, or ρ and pressure drop/pipe radius)?
A: Make a reasonable assumption based on best practices.

Express the momentum flux in terms of density \( \rho \), cross-sectional area \(A\), and mean velocity \(V\):
\[
\dot{M} = \beta\,\rho\,A\,V^2 \quad \text{with} \quad \beta=\frac{4}{3}.
\]

Q: For fully developed laminar flow in a circular pipe, should I use your stated assumption β = 1, or should I use the physically correct laminar-pipe momentum correction factor β = 4/3?
A: Use the physically correct fully developed laminar circular-pipe momentum correction factor: **\( \beta = \frac{4}{3} \)** (not \( \beta = 1 \)).
