Compute the momentum flux for fully developed laminar flow in a circular pipe using the physically correct momentum correction factor β = 4/3.
Express the momentum flux in terms of density ρ, cross-sectional area A, and mean velocity V.
Use the definition of momentum flux (momentum flow rate) as \dot{M} = β ρ A V^2.
Substitute β = 4/3 to obtain \dot{M} = (4/3) ρ A V^2.
Report \dot{M} with units of N (kg·m/s²).
