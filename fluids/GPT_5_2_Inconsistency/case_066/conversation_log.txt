Q: To avoid guessing, should I assume the degree of reaction is to be computed using the standard axial-compressor definition R = (static enthalpy rise in rotor)/(stage total enthalpy rise)?
A: Yes—use the standard axial-compressor definition:

\[
R=\frac{\Delta h_{\text{rotor (static)}}}{\Delta h_{\text{stage (total)}}}.
\]

Also, per the complete requirement, enforce constant axial velocity by taking \(V_a=W_1\cos\beta_1\) and setting \(W_2=V_a/\cos\beta_2\) (so \(W_2\approx 122.5\ \text{m/s}\)), rather than using \(W_2=100\ \text{m/s}\).
