Q: Do you want the pressure variation across the gap computed using the inviscid Bernoulli equation (ignoring viscosity), or do you want the physically correct Couette-flow result from the Navier–Stokes equations (which gives no pressure gradient across the gap for this setup)?
A: Use the physically correct result from the **steady incompressible Navier–Stokes equations** (not Bernoulli). For simple Couette flow with gravity neglected, the wall-normal pressure gradient is

\[
\frac{\partial p}{\partial y}=0
\]

so **\(p\) does not vary with \(y\)** across the gap.
