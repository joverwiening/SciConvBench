Assume T1 = 300 K as a standard inlet temperature for gas turbine engines.  
Assume p2/p1 = 10 as a typical pressure ratio for gas turbine engines.  
Assume gamma = 1.4 for diatomic gases such as air.  
Use the isentropic relation T2/T1 = (p2/p1)^((gamma-1)/gamma) to compute the exit temperature.  
Calculate T2 = T1 * (p2/p1)^((gamma-1)/gamma).  
Substitute the values: T2 = 300 * (10)^((1.4-1)/1.4).  
Compute the exponent: (1.4 - 1) / 1.4 = 0.2857.  
Compute the pressure ratio term: 10^0.2857 ≈ 1.933.  
Multiply: T2 = 300 * 1.933 ≈ 579.9 K.  
The temperature after compression is approximately 579.9 K.
