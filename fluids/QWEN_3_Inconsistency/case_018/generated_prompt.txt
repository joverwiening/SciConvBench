The flow is fully developed laminar flow in a circular pipe with a parabolic velocity profile.  
The diameter of the pipe is 20 mm.  
The mean velocity is 1.0 m/s.  
The fluid is water.  
The kinetic-energy correction factor α is computed using the formula α = (1/A) ∫_A (u/V)^3 dA.  
For a parabolic velocity profile in laminar flow, the value of α is 2.  
Use the parabolic velocity distribution u(r) = 2V[1 - (r/R)^2] to evaluate the integral.  
The cross-sectional area A is πR², where R is the pipe radius.  
The local velocity u is a function of radial position r.  
The mean velocity V is constant across the cross-section.  
The kinetic-energy correction factor α equals 2 for this flow condition.
