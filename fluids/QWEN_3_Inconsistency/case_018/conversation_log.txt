Q: What is the formula for the kinetic-energy correction factor α in terms of velocity distribution?
A: The kinetic-energy correction factor α is given by the formula:

α = (1/A) ∫_A (u/V)^3 dA

where u is the local velocity, V is the mean velocity, and A is the cross-sectional area of the pipe. For fully developed laminar flow with a parabolic velocity profile, this evaluates to α = 2.
