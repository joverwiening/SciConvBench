Use a density (rho) of 998 kg/m³ for water at 20°C.  
Use a dynamic viscosity (mu) of 1.0e-3 Pa·s (or kg/(m·s)) for water at 20°C.  
Compute the Reynolds number using the formula Re = rho * V * D / mu.  
Ensure velocity (V) is in m/s and diameter (D) is in meters.  
The result is a dimensionless Reynolds number.
