Assume the angular velocity ω is 10 rad/s, a typical value for such problems unless otherwise specified.  
The fluid is water with constant density of 1000 kg/m³.  
The flow is inviscid and in steady state.  
The velocity profile is given by v_θ = ωr, with ω = 10 rad/s.  
Bernoulli’s equation is applied along a radial streamline in the horizontal plane (neglecting gravity effects in the radial direction).  
The pressure difference is computed between r = 0.1 m and r = 0.3 m.  
Use Bernoulli’s equation: p₁ + (1/2)ρv₁² = p₂ + (1/2)ρv₂².  
Calculate tangential velocity at r = 0.1 m as v₁ = ω × 0.1 = 1 m/s.  
Calculate tangential velocity at r = 0.3 m as v₂ = ω × 0.3 = 3 m/s.  
Compute pressure difference Δp = (1/2)ρ(v₂² - v₁²).  
Substitute values: Δp = (0.5)(1000)(3² - 1²) = (500)(9 - 1) = 500 × 8 = 4000 Pa.  
The pressure difference is 4000 Pascals.
