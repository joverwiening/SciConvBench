Treat water as an ideal gas for the purpose of stagnation temperature calculation.  
Use the specific heat capacity at constant pressure (cp) for water vapor as 1850 J/(kg·K).  
Calculate the stagnation temperature using the formula: T0 = T + (V²)/(2 * cp).
