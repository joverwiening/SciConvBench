Use water with a density of 1000 kg/m³.  
Calculate the inlet velocity V₁ using the continuity equation: V₁ = Q / A₁, where A₁ = π(D₁)²/4 and D₁ = 0.050 m.  
Calculate the outlet velocity V₂ using the continuity equation: V₂ = Q / A₂, where A₂ = π(D₂)²/4 and D₂ = 0.100 m.  
Compute the Borda–Carnot head loss: h_L = (V₁ - V₂)² / (2g), where g = 9.81 m/s².  
Apply the energy equation including head loss: (P₁/ρg) + (V₁²/2g) = (P₂/ρg) + (V₂²/2g) + h_L.  
Rearrange the energy equation to solve for the pressure rise ΔP = P₂ - P₁.  
Substitute all known values into the equation to compute the pressure rise in pascals.
