Q: What is the assumed location (or reference point) for applying Bernoulli’s equation at the inlet and outlet—specifically, are viscous effects and losses neglected, or should a loss coefficient be included for the sudden expansion?
A: Viscous effects and losses are not neglected. You should include the Borda–Carnot head loss given by \( h_L = \frac{(V_1 - V_2)^2}{2g} \) when applying the energy equation across the sudden expansion. Use the continuity equation to find velocities \( V_1 \) and \( V_2 \), then apply the energy equation with this head loss to compute the pressure rise.
