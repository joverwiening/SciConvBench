Q: What is the specific flow condition or assumption (e.g., freestream velocity, pressure reference, or fluid density) to be used in applying Bernoulli’s equation?
A: Assume incompressible, inviscid flow with freestream velocity \( U_\infty \), freestream pressure \( p_\infty \), and constant fluid density \( \rho \). Use Bernoulli’s equation between a point in the freestream and the stagnation point, where velocity is zero. The pressure coefficient is defined as \( C_p = \frac{p - p_\infty}{\frac{1}{2} \rho U_\infty^2} \).
