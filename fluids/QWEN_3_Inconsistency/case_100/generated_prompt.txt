Apply Bernoulli’s equation along a streamline from the freestream to the stagnation point.  
Use the form: $ p_\infty + \frac{1}{2} \rho U_\infty^2 = p_{\text{stag}} $, since velocity at the stagnation point is zero.  
Calculate the pressure difference: $ p_{\text{stag}} - p_\infty = \frac{1}{2} \rho U_\infty^2 $.  
Substitute into the pressure coefficient definition: $ C_p = \frac{p_{\text{stag}} - p_\infty}{\frac{1}{2} \rho U_\infty^2} $.  
Simplify to obtain: $ C_p = 1 $.  
State that the given value $ C_p = 1.5 $ is incorrect for incompressible, inviscid flow; the correct theoretical value at a stagnation point is $ C_p = 1 $.
