Assume the stagnation temperature of air is 288 K.  
The air enters the converging nozzle at Mach 2.0.  
The nozzle has an area ratio A2/A1 = 0.7, where A1 is the inlet area and A2 is the exit area.  
The nozzle is frictionless and operates under isentropic flow conditions.  
Air is modeled as a perfect gas with constant specific heats, with γ = 1.4 and R = 287 J/(kg·K).  
The stagnation temperature remains constant throughout the nozzle.  
The flow is one-dimensional and adiabatic.  
Calculate the Mach number at the exit using the area ratio and isentropic flow relations.  
Use the Mach number at the exit and inlet to compute the corresponding velocities.  
Determine the increase in exit velocity compared to the inlet velocity.
