Assume a cutoff ratio $ r_c $ of 2, which is typical for standard Diesel cycle engines.  
Assume a compression ratio $ r $ of 8, which is typical for standard internal combustion engines.  
Assume a specific heat ratio $ \gamma $ of 1.4, corresponding to air at standard conditions.  
Compute the thermal efficiency using the Diesel cycle formula:  
$ \eta = 1 - \frac{1}{r^{\gamma-1}} \left( \frac{r_c^\gamma - 1}{\gamma (r_c - 1)} \right) $.  
Use $ r = 8 $, $ r_c = 2 $, and $ \gamma = 1.4 $.  
The efficiency is determined solely by the compression ratio, cutoff ratio, and specific heat ratio.  
Do not include any other loss mechanisms or deviations from the ideal cycle.
