The fluid is oil with a dynamic viscosity of 0.1 Pa·s.  
The flow is steady, inviscid, and fully developed between two parallel plates.  
The velocity profile is assumed linear with no-slip condition at the walls.  
The maximum velocity is 1 m/s at the centerline and zero at each wall.  
The plate separation is 0.01 m, so the distance from the wall to the centerline is 0.005 m.  
The velocity gradient at the wall (du/dy) is calculated as (1 m/s - 0 m/s) / 0.005 m = 200 s⁻¹.  
The wall shear stress is computed using τ = μ (du/dy).  
The wall shear stress is τ = 0.1 Pa·s × 200 s⁻¹ = 20 Pa.
