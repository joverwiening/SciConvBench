Assume the rotational speed U is calculated as the average of the tangential components of the relative velocities at the inlet and outlet, which is a standard practice in axial compressor analysis. 

 
Calculate the axial velocity as \( C_a = W_1 \cos(\beta_1) = 150 \cos(45^\circ) \approx 106.07 \, \text{m/s} \).
Use the same axial velocity at both inlet and outlet since it is constant through the rotor.
Calculate the tangential component of the relative velocity at inlet: \( W_{1t} = W_1 \sin(\beta_1) = 150 \sin(45^\circ) \approx 106.07 \, \text{m/s} \).
Calculate the tangential component of the relative velocity at outlet: \( W_{2t} = W_2 \sin(\beta_2) = 100 \sin(30^\circ) = 50 \, \text{m/s} \).
Assume the rotational speed U is the average of \( W_{1t} \) and \( W_{2t} \): \( U = \frac{W_{1t} + W_{2t}}{2} = \frac{106.07 + 50}{2} \approx 78.035 \, \text{m/s} \).
Calculate the tangential component of the absolute velocity at inlet: \( C_{1t} = U - W_{1t} = 78.035 - 106.07 = -28.035 \, \text{m/s} \).
Calculate the tangential component of the absolute velocity at outlet: \( C_{2t} = U - W_{2t} = 78.035 - 50 = 28.035 \, \text{m/s} \).
Compute the degree of reaction using the formula: \( R = \frac{C_{2t} - C_{1t}}{2U} \).
Substitute values: \( R = \frac{28.035 - (-28.035)}{2 \times 78.035} = \frac{56.07}{156.07} \approx 0.359 \).
The degree of reaction is approximately 0.359.
