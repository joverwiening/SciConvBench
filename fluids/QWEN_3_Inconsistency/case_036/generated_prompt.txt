Use a water density of 1000 kg/m³.  
Apply the steady Bernoulli equation between two points along the pipeline, one before the valve and one after.  
Assume the pipeline is horizontal, so elevation changes are negligible.  
Assume the initial velocity is 2 m/s and the final velocity is 0 m/s at the point of complete valve closure.  
Neglect frictional losses and assume incompressible, steady flow up to the moment of closure.  
Calculate the pressure rise using the Bernoulli equation: ΔP = (1/2) × ρ × (V₁² - V₂²), where V₁ = 2 m/s and V₂ = 0 m/s.  
Substitute values to compute the pressure rise: ΔP = 0.5 × 1000 × (2² - 0²).  
The pressure rise is 2000 Pa.
