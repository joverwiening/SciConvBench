Assume the fluid is water at standard conditions with a density of 1000 kg/m³.  
Assume a flow velocity of 1 m/s, which is a typical value for pipe flow in many engineering applications.  
The pipe length is 30 m.  
The pipe diameter is 0.1 m.  
The Darcy friction factor is 0.02.  
Use the Darcy–Weisbach equation in the form Δp = f_D (L/D) (ρ v²/2) to calculate the pressure drop.  
Compute the pressure drop in Pascals.  
The pressure drop is 3000 Pa.
