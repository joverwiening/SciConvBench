Q: Assume the fluid is water at standard conditions with a density of 1000 kg/m³.  
Assume a flow velocity (v) of 1 m/s, which is a typical value for pipe flow in many engineering applications.  
The pipe length (L) is 30 m.  
The pipe diameter (D) is 0.1 m.  
The Fanning friction factor (f) is 0.005.  
Use the Darcy–Weisbach equation in the form Δp = f (L/D) (ρ v²/2) to calculate the pressure drop.  
Compute the pressure drop (Δp) in Pascals.
A: Use the Darcy friction factor f_D = 4 × 0.005 = 0.02.  
With ρ = 1000 kg/m³, v = 1 m/s, L = 30 m, D = 0.1 m:  
Δp = 0.02 × (30 / 0.1) × (1000 × 1² / 2) = 0.02 × 300 × 500 = 3000 Pa.  

The pressure drop is 3000 Pa.
