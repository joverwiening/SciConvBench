Assume the density of the oil is 850 kg/m³.  
The pipe length is 25 m.  
The pipe diameter is 80 mm.  
The mean flow velocity is 1.5 m/s.  
The flow is steady and horizontal.  
The flow is assumed inviscid, but the Darcy–Weisbach equation is used with a friction factor of 0.03.  
The pressure drop is to be computed using the Darcy–Weisbach equation.  
Use the formula: ΔP = f × (L/D) × (ρ × V² / 2).  
Substitute the values: f = 0.03, L = 25 m, D = 0.08 m, ρ = 850 kg/m³, V = 1.5 m/s.  
Calculate the pressure drop in pascals.
