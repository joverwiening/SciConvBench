Q: Is the flow incompressible and steady, and are points A and B on the same streamline or connected by an irrotational region?
A: The flow is steady and incompressible. Points A and B are on different streamlines, but the flow is irrotational, so the Bernoulli constant is the same throughout the entire field, allowing its use between A and B.
