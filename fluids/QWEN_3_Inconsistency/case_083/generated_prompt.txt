Assume the Mach angle (mu) is calculated as mu = arcsin(1/M), where M is the Mach number.

The Mach angle (mu) for Mach 3 is approximately 19.47 degrees.  
Use the small-angle approximation formula beta ≈ mu + theta.  
The wedge angle (theta) is 25 degrees.  
Add mu = 19.47 degrees and theta = 25 degrees to compute beta.  
The shock angle (beta) is approximately 44.47 degrees.
