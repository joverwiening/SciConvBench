The cavitation factor sigma is calculated using Thoma's formula: sigma = (Ha - Hv - hs - hloss) / H.  

Ha is 10.3 m.  

Hv is 0.3 m.  

hs is 20 m.  

hloss is 0.5 m.  

Substitute values into the formula: sigma = (10.3 - 0.3 - 20 - 0.5) / H = (-10.5) / H.  

Since H (net head) is always positive, sigma is negative for any positive head H.  

A negative sigma indicates that cavitation is possible, not impossible.
