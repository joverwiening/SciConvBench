Use the incompressible Bernoulli equation to calculate air velocity from differential pressure.
Assume the fluid is air and treat it as incompressible for this calculation.
The differential pressure measured by the Pitot-static tube is 20 kPa.
The static pressure is 100 kPa but is not needed directly since Bernoulli's incompressible form uses only differential pressure.
Use the formula: velocity = sqrt(2 * ΔP / ρ), where ΔP is 20,000 Pa.
Use standard sea-level air density: ρ = 1.225 kg/m³.
Calculate the velocity in meters per second.
The result is the airspeed at standard sea-level conditions using incompressible flow assumption.
