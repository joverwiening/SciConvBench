The task is to calculate the Darcy-Weisbach head loss.
The pipe has a length of 50 meters.
The pipe has an inner diameter of 0.05 meters.
The fluid flowing through the pipe is water at 20 degrees Celsius.
The density of the water is 998 kg/m^3.
The dynamic viscosity of the water is 1.002 x 10^-3 Pa·s.
The mean velocity of the water is 2 m/s.
The pipe material is commercial steel.
The absolute roughness of the commercial steel pipe is 4.5 x 10^-5 meters.
The acceleration due to gravity is 9.81 m/s^2.
Calculate the Reynolds number for the flow.
Calculate the relative roughness of the pipe.
Determine the Darcy friction factor using the Colebrook-White equation or an explicit approximation.
Calculate the final head loss in meters using the Darcy-Weisbach equation.
