Calculate the lift force generated by a wing.
The wing has a surface area of 20 square meters.
The aircraft is in steady flight at an airspeed of 80 meters per second.
The wing's coefficient of lift is 0.45.
The flight altitude is assumed to be 10,000 meters.
The air density at this altitude is 0.4135 kilograms per cubic meter, based on the International Standard Atmosphere model.
Use the standard lift equation L = 0.5 * ρ * V² * A * C_L for the calculation.
The final result should be the lift force expressed in Newtons.
