Calculate the critical insulation thickness for a cylindrical pipe.
The thermal conductivity of the insulation (k) is 0.17 W/m·K.
The convective heat transfer coefficient of the surrounding fluid (h) is 8 W/m²·K.
Assume the pipe is a 1-inch nominal Schedule 40 pipe.
The outer radius of the 1-inch nominal pipe (r_pipe) is 0.0167 meters.
Calculate the critical radius of insulation using the formula r_critical = k / h.
The calculated critical radius of insulation is 0.02125 meters.
Calculate the critical insulation thickness using the formula t_critical = r_critical - r_pipe.
The final critical insulation thickness is 0.00455 meters.
