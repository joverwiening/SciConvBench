Calculate the Rayleigh number for natural convection.
The geometry is a vertical wall.
The characteristic length of the wall is 0.5 meters.
The surrounding fluid is air.
The surface temperature of the wall is 80 degrees Celsius.
The temperature of the surrounding air is 25 degrees Celsius.
The pressure of the air is 1 standard atmosphere (101325 Pascals).
Evaluate all air properties at the film temperature.
The film temperature is the average of the wall surface and surrounding air temperatures.
Use the standard value for gravitational acceleration (9.81 m/s^2).
The thermal expansion coefficient for air should be calculated as the reciprocal of the absolute film temperature.
