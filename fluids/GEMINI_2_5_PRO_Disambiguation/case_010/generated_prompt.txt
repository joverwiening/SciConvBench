Calculate the lift per unit span on a rotating cylinder.
The cylinder has a radius of 0.1 meters.
The cylinder rotates at a rate of 200 radians per second.
The cylinder is in a freestream flow of air.
The freestream velocity of the air is 15 meters per second.
The density of the air is 1.225 kilograms per cubic meter.
The calculation shall assume the air is an ideal, inviscid fluid.
The lift per unit span shall be calculated using the Kutta-Joukowski theorem.
