Calculate the viscosity of a fluid using the falling-ball viscometer method.
The density of the fluid is 1000 kg/m^3.
The ball is made of steel with a density of 7850 kg/m^3.
The diameter of the steel ball is 3 mm.
The terminal velocity of the ball is 0.05 m/s.
The inner diameter of the tube is 30 mm.
Use the standard value for acceleration due to gravity, 9.81 m/s^2.
