Calculate the volumetric flow rate through a Venturi meter.
The fluid is water at 20 degrees Celsius.
The density of the fluid is 998 kg/m³.
The pressure difference between the inlet and the throat is 20 kPa.
The diameter of the inlet pipe is 100 mm.
The diameter of the throat is 50 mm.
The discharge coefficient of the meter is 0.98.
The final result should be expressed in cubic meters per second.
