The task is to calculate the efficiency of a conical draft tube.
The water velocity at the inlet is 10 m/s.
The water velocity at the outlet is 2 m/s.
Assume the head loss due to friction is 10% of the difference between the inlet and outlet kinetic energy heads.
Use a value of 9.81 m/s^2 for the acceleration due to gravity.
Calculate the efficiency based on these parameters.
