Calculate the Biot number for a cooling sphere.
The sphere has a radius of 5 mm.
The sphere is made of steel.
The thermal conductivity of the steel is 45 W/m·K.
The sphere is surrounded by an oil bath.
The convective heat transfer coefficient of the oil bath is 150 W/m²·K.
Use the characteristic length for a sphere, defined as the radius divided by 3.
Use the formula Bi = (h * Lc) / k for the calculation.
