Calculate the force exerted by a water jet on a stationary curved vane.
The water jet has a diameter of 50 mm.
The initial velocity of the water jet is 20 m/s.
The water jet is deflected by an angle of 120 degrees after striking the vane.
The density of the water is 1000 kg/m³.
Assume the magnitude of the water jet's velocity remains constant as it flows over the vane.
The final output shall be the magnitude of the total resultant force exerted on the vane.
