Calculate the shaft power required to pump water to an upper reservoir.
The volumetric flow rate of the water is 0.02 cubic meters per second.
The vertical elevation difference between the reservoir water surfaces is 30 meters.
The total length of the pipe is 50 meters.
The inner diameter of the pipe is 0.05 meters.
The pump efficiency is 80 percent.
Assume the pipe is made of commercial steel with an absolute roughness of 0.045 millimeters.
Assume the pipe system includes a sharp-edged entrance, a fully open gate valve, and a sharp-edged exit.
Assume the water temperature is 20 degrees Celsius, with a corresponding density of 998.2 kg/m³ and dynamic viscosity of 1.002 x 10⁻³ Pa·s.
The calculation must account for both major head losses due to pipe friction and minor head losses from fittings.
The final result should be the required shaft power in kilowatts.
