The task is to calculate the shaft power for a centrifugal pump.
The fluid being pumped is water.
The density of the water shall be assumed as 1000 kg/m³.
The acceleration due to gravity shall be assumed as 9.81 m/s².
The volumetric flow rate of the water is 0.05 m³/s.
The total head developed by the pump is 25 meters.
The overall efficiency of the pump is 75%.
The required shaft power is to be calculated.
The final result for shaft power should be expressed in kilowatts (kW).
