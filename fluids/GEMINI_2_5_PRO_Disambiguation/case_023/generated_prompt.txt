Calculate the pressure drop per unit length for a packed bed.
The packed bed consists of spherical particles.
The diameter of the particles is 5 mm.
The void fraction of the bed is 0.4.
The fluid flowing through the bed is air.
The superficial velocity of the air is 0.5 m/s.
The properties of air are to be evaluated at Standard Temperature and Pressure (STP).
STP is defined as 273.15 K and 101325 Pa.
Use the Ergun equation for the calculation.
