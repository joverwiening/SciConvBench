Calculate the speed of sound in a gas mixture.
The gas mixture contains Helium and Argon.
The mole fraction of Helium is 0.70.
The mole fraction of Argon is 0.30.
The temperature of the mixture is 300 K.
The gas mixture shall be modeled as an ideal gas.
The adiabatic index for the mixture shall be 5/3.
The molar mass of the mixture shall be the mole-fraction-weighted average of the molar masses of Helium and Argon.
Use a value of 4.0026 g/mol for the molar mass of Helium.
Use a value of 39.948 g/mol for the molar mass of Argon.
Use a value of 8.314 J/(mol·K) for the universal gas constant.
