The task is to calculate the net buoyant lift force on a balloon.
The volume of the balloon is 500 cubic meters.
The density of the surrounding air is 1.2 kilograms per cubic meter.
The density of the gas inside the balloon is 0.18 kilograms per cubic meter.
The acceleration due to gravity is 9.81 meters per second squared.
The net buoyant lift force is calculated as the buoyant force on the balloon minus the weight of the gas inside the balloon.
The final result must be expressed in Newtons.
