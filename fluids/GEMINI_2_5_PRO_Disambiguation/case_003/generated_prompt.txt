Calculate the terminal velocity of a spherical particle falling in a fluid.
The particle has a diameter of 2 millimeters.
The particle has a density of 7800 kilograms per cubic meter.
The fluid is oil with a density of 890 kilograms per cubic meter.
The fluid has a dynamic viscosity of 0.29 Pascal-seconds.
Use a value of 9.81 meters per second squared for the acceleration due to gravity.
The calculation should assume a laminar flow regime and use the Stokes' Law drag model.
