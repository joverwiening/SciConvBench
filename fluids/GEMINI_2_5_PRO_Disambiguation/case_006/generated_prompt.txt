The task is to solve for the steady velocity profile in Couette flow.
The flow is between two parallel plates separated by 10 mm.
The bottom plate is stationary.
The top plate moves at a velocity of 5 m/s.
The pressure gradient in the direction of flow is zero.
The resulting velocity profile is linear.
The velocity u at a distance y from the bottom plate is described by the equation u(y) = 500 * y, where y is in meters and u is in m/s.
