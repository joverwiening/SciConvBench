The convective heat transfer rate from a plate is to be calculated.
The plate has a surface area of 2 square meters.
The surface temperature of the plate is 60 degrees Celsius.
The temperature of the surrounding fluid is 20 degrees Celsius.
Assume the surrounding fluid is air at one atmosphere.
Assume the mode of heat transfer is natural convection.
Assume the plate is oriented vertically.
Assume the plate is a square, making its height the square root of 2 meters.
Calculate the film temperature as the average of the surface and fluid temperatures.
Determine the properties of air at the film temperature.
Calculate the Rayleigh number using the plate height as the characteristic length.
Select the appropriate Nusselt number correlation for natural convection from a vertical plate based on the Rayleigh number.
Calculate the convective heat transfer coefficient from the Nusselt number.
Calculate the total heat transfer rate using the convective heat transfer coefficient, plate area, and temperature difference.
