The fluid is water.
The pipe is smooth.
The internal diameter of the pipe is 0.04 meters.
The velocity of the water is 1.5 meters per second.
The temperature of the water is assumed to be 20 degrees Celsius.
At 20 degrees Celsius, the density of water is 998.2 kg/m^3.
At 20 degrees Celsius, the dynamic viscosity of water is 1.002 x 10^-3 Pa·s.
The Reynolds number is calculated using the formula Re = (density * velocity * diameter) / viscosity.
The calculated Reynolds number is approximately 59,772.
A Reynolds number greater than 4000 indicates turbulent flow.
The flow is determined to be turbulent.
