Compute the drag force on a sphere moving through glycerin.
The radius of the sphere is 2.5 millimeters.
The velocity of the sphere relative to the glycerin is 0.1 meters per second.
The temperature of the glycerin is 20 degrees Celsius.
Use a dynamic viscosity for glycerin of 1.412 Pascal-seconds.
Use a density for glycerin of 1261 kilograms per cubic meter.
The calculation must use Stokes' Law.
