The Weber number will be calculated for a liquid droplet.
The characteristic length for the calculation is the droplet diameter of 0.002 meters.
The velocity of the droplet is 30 meters per second.
The density of the droplet's liquid is 800 kilograms per cubic meter.
The surface tension of the droplet's liquid is 0.03 Newtons per meter.
The calculation uses the formula We = (density * velocity^2 * characteristic_length) / surface_tension.
