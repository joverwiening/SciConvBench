Calculate the cavitation index for a valve.
The upstream pressure is 400 kPa.
The downstream pressure is 150 kPa.
The fluid is water.
The water temperature is 20 degrees Celsius.
The vapor pressure of water at 20 degrees Celsius is 2.34 kPa.
The cavitation index is calculated as (Downstream Pressure - Vapor Pressure) / (Upstream Pressure - Downstream Pressure).
The resulting cavitation index is 0.59.
