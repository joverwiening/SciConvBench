Solve the one-dimensional steady-state heat conduction equation.
The geometry is a plane wall.
The wall thickness is 20 cm.
The rate of internal heat generation is 2e5 W/m³.
The thermal conductivity of the wall material is 15 W/(m·°C).
The temperature on both surfaces of the wall is maintained at 50°C.
Calculate the steady-state temperature at the center of the wall.
Provide the final temperature value in degrees Celsius.
