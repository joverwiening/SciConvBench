Compute minor head loss across a single pipe fitting using the loss coefficient method.
Use K = 0.9.
Use fluid velocity V = 3 m/s through the fitting.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Compute head loss as h_L = K·V²/(2g).
Report h_L in meters of water head.
Calculate h_L = 0.9·(3²)/(2·9.81) = 0.413 m.
