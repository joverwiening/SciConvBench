Compute the billboard drag force using the standard aerodynamic drag equation Fd = 0.5 × ρ × v^2 × Cd × A.
Use air density ρ = 1.225 kg/m^3.
Use wind speed v = 30 m/s.
Use drag coefficient Cd = 1.2.
Use projected area A = 40 m^2.
Calculate Fd = 0.5 × 1.225 × (30^2) × 1.2 × 40 = 26,460 N (≈ 2.65 × 10^4 N).
