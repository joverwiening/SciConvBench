Assume the gas is air at STP composition.
Use temperature T = 273.15 K.
Use pressure p = 101325 Pa.
Use characteristic length L = 100 nm = 1.0e-7 m.
Compute the mean free path using λ = kB T / (sqrt(2) π d^2 p).
Use an effective molecular diameter for air d = 3.7e-10 m.
Use kB = 1.380649e-23 J/K.
Calculate λ ≈ (1.380649e-23 × 273.15) / (1.41421356 × π × (3.7e-10)^2 × 101325) m.
Obtain λ ≈ 6.1e-8 m (about 61 nm).
Compute Knudsen number Kn = λ / L.
Obtain Kn ≈ (6.1e-8) / (1.0e-7) ≈ 0.61.
Classify the flow regime as transitional (0.1 < Kn < 10).
