Compute the Reynolds number for a spherical water droplet of diameter D = 1.0×10^-3 m falling through air.
Use the droplet speed relative to the air as the terminal velocity V = 4.0 m/s.
Assume standard sea-level air at T = 20 °C (293 K) and p = 1 atm (101,325 Pa).
Use the provided air kinematic viscosity ν = 1.5×10^-5 m^2/s.
Calculate Reynolds number using Re = V·D/ν.
Report the numerical value: Re = (4.0)(1.0×10^-3)/(1.5×10^-5) ≈ 2.67×10^2 (about 270).
State that the Reynolds number is dimensionless.
