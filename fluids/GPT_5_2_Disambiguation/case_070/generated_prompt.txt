Compute the drag force on a rigid smooth sphere moving through glycerin.
Use sphere radius r = 0.0025 m (diameter D = 0.005 m).
Use relative speed v = 0.1 m/s.
Use glycerin dynamic viscosity μ = 1.5 Pa·s.
Use glycerin density ρ = 1260 kg/m³.
Assume creeping-flow/Stokes regime is applicable and use Stokes’ law for drag.
Compute drag force as Fd = 6πμrv.
Optionally compute Reynolds number as Re = ρvD/μ to document regime validity using the provided values.
Do not require temperature as an input; treat μ = 1.5 Pa·s as the operating-condition viscosity.
