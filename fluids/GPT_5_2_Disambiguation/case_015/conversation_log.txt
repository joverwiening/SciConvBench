Q: Which correlation should I use to calculate the turbulent pipe-flow Nusselt number (e.g., Dittus–Boelter, Gnielinski, or Sieder–Tate)?
A: Use the **Dittus–Boelter correlation for heating** (i.e., \( \mathrm{Nu} = 0.023\,\mathrm{Re}^{0.8}\mathrm{Pr}^{0.4} \)) for water at \(\mathrm{Re}=1.5\times 10^4\) and \(\mathrm{Pr}=6.0\).
