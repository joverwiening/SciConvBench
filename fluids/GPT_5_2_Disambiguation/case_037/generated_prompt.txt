Calculate the thermal efficiency of an ideal Rankine cycle operating between a boiler pressure of 4 MPa and a condenser pressure of 10 kPa.
Assume the turbine inlet state at 4 MPa is saturated vapor (no superheat specified).
Assume the turbine expansion is isentropic from 4 MPa to 10 kPa.
Assume the pump compression is isentropic from 10 kPa to 4 MPa.
Use steam tables (or an equivalent IAPWS-IF97 property source) to obtain all required water/steam properties.
Define state 1 as saturated liquid at 10 kPa (condenser outlet/pump inlet).
Compute pump work as wp = h2 − h1 with h2 determined from isentropic pumping to 4 MPa (use v1·(P2−P1) approximation if appropriate for compressed liquid).
Define state 3 as saturated vapor at 4 MPa (boiler outlet/turbine inlet).
Compute turbine work as wt = h3 − h4 with state 4 determined by s4 = s3 at 10 kPa (find quality if in two-phase region).
Compute heat added as qin = h3 − h2.
Compute net work as wnet = wt − wp.
Compute thermal efficiency as ηth = wnet / qin.
Report ηth as a percentage and list the key state properties (P, T, h, s, and quality where applicable) for states 1–4.
