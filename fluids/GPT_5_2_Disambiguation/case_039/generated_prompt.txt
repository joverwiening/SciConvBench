Assume a circular disk of radius R = 0.2 m rotates at angular speed ω = 2π rad/s in a Newtonian viscous fluid of dynamic viscosity μ = 0.1 Pa·s.
Assume the disk face is parallel to a stationary infinite wall with a uniform gap h = 0.001 m across the full radius.
Assume laminar Couette flow in the gap with no-slip at both surfaces and neglect edge effects and pressure-driven flow.
Use local tangential velocity on the disk u(r) = ωr.
Use shear stress in the gap τ(r) = μ u(r) / h = μ ω r / h.
Use differential torque dT = τ(r) (2πr dr) r.
Integrate from r = 0 to R to get T = ∫0R (μ ω r / h)(2πr dr)r = (π μ ω R^4) / (2h).
Compute R^4 = (0.2)^4 = 0.0016 m^4.
Compute T = (π/2)(0.1)(2π)(0.0016)/(0.001) N·m = π^2·0.16 N·m ≈ 1.58 N·m.
Report the required torque as approximately 1.6 N·m under the stated assumptions.
