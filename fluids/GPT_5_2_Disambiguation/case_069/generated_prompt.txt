Compute the stagnation temperature at the rocket nose assuming adiabatic, isentropic deceleration from freestream to zero velocity at the stagnation point.
Use a calorically perfect gas with constant specific heats and γ = 1.4.
Use freestream Mach number M∞ = 2.5.
Use freestream static temperature T∞ = 220 K.
Compute stagnation temperature using T0 = T∞(1 + (γ − 1)/2 · M∞^2).
