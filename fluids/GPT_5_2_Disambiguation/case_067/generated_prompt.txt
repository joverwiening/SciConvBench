Compute the terminal velocity of a spherical raindrop falling vertically through still air.
Use raindrop diameter d = 0.002 m.
Assume the raindrop is liquid water with density ρ_water = 1000 kg/m³.
Use air density ρ_air = 1.2 kg/m³.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Model drag as quadratic drag with constant drag coefficient C_d = 0.5.
Use drag force magnitude F_D = (1/2) ρ_air C_d A v², where A = π(d²)/4 is the projected area.
Use weight W = ρ_water (π d³/6) g.
Neglect buoyancy unless explicitly requested.
Define terminal velocity v_t by force balance W = F_D.
Solve for v_t = sqrt( (2 W) / (ρ_air C_d A) ).
Report v_t in m/s.
