Compute steady-state one-dimensional conduction heat transfer through a homogeneous concrete wall.
Use wall thickness L = 0.2 m.
Use wall area A = 15 m².
Use temperature difference across the wall ΔT = 20 K.
Use thermal conductivity k = 1.4 W/(m·K).
Assume uniform material properties and no internal heat generation.
Neglect convection and radiation resistances and treat the given ΔT as the temperature drop across the concrete only.
Calculate heat transfer rate using Fourier’s law Q = k·A·ΔT / L.
Report Q in watts and optionally in kilowatts.
With the given values, compute Q = (1.4)(15)(20)/0.2 = 2100 W = 2.1 kW.
Do not assume height and width because area is already specified and sufficient for the calculation.
