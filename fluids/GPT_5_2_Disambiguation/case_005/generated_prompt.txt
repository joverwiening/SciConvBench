Compute the Reynolds number at x = 0.5 m from the leading edge for external flow over a flat plate in air.
Use the definition Re_x = U∞ x / ν.
Use U∞ = 10 m/s.
Use ν = 1.5 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s.
Compute Re_0.5 = (10)(0.5)/(1.5 × 10⁻⁵) = 3.33 × 10⁵ (dimensionless).
Do not introduce an air temperature because ν is provided directly.
