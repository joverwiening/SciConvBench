Assume a 1 m wide by 2 m tall rectangular plate is vertical with its top edge at the free surface and its bottom edge at 2 m depth.
Use y as the vertical depth below the free surface.
Compute the centroid depth as y_c = h/2 = 1.0 m.
Compute the second moment of area about the horizontal centroidal axis as I_G = b h^3 / 12 = (1)(2^3)/12 = 2/3 m^4.
Compute the area as A = b h = (1)(2) = 2 m^2.
Compute the center of pressure depth as y_cp = y_c + I_G/(y_c A) = 1.0 + (2/3)/(1.0·2) = 1.333... m.
Report the center of pressure at 1.333 m below the free surface, centered horizontally at mid-width (0.5 m from either vertical edge).
