Calculate hydraulic power as Ph = ρ g Q H using water density ρ = 1000 kg/m³ and gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Use flow rate Q = 0.05 m³/s.
Use total head H = 25 m.
Compute Ph = 1000 × 9.81 × 0.05 × 25 = 12,262.5 W.
Use overall pump efficiency η = 0.75.
Compute required shaft power as Pshaft = Ph / η = 12,262.5 / 0.75 = 16,350 W.
Report shaft power as 16.35 kW.
