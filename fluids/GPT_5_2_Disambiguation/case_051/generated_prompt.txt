Compute buoyant lift force for a gas-filled balloon using Archimedes’ principle.
Use balloon volume V = 500 m³.
Use ambient air density ρ_air = 1.2 kg/m³.
Use helium density ρ_He = 0.18 kg/m³.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Compute buoyant force as F_buoy = ρ_air × V × g.
Compute helium weight as W_He = ρ_He × V × g.
Compute net buoyant lift force (gross lift) as F_lift = (ρ_air − ρ_He) × V × g.
Report F_buoy, W_He, and F_lift in newtons.
Calculate F_buoy = 1.2 × 500 × 9.81 = 5886 N.
Calculate W_He = 0.18 × 500 × 9.81 = 882.9 N.
Calculate F_lift = (1.2 − 0.18) × 500 × 9.81 = 5003.1 N.
