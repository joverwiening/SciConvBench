Q: What material (or its latent heat and specific heat capacity) should be used to compute the Stefan number?
A: Use **ice (melting at 0°C)**. For properties, take **latent heat of fusion \(L_f \approx 334\,\text{kJ/kg}\)** and **specific heat of liquid water \(c_p \approx 4.18\,\text{kJ/(kg·K)}\)**.

Q: What reference temperature should the specific heat term use for ΔT: liquid water warming above 0°C (use c_p of liquid water) or ice warming below 0°C (use c_p of ice)?
A: Use **liquid water warming above 0°C** for \(\Delta T\), i.e., \(\Delta T = 10\,\text{K}\) with **\(c_p\) of liquid water (4.18 kJ/(kg·K))**.
