Assume the fluid density is 1000 kg/m³.
Assume the fluid temperature is 20 °C.
Assume the viscometer tube inner diameter is sufficiently large relative to the 3 mm ball so wall effects are negligible.
Use a spherical steel ball with diameter 3.0 mm.
Use steel ball density 7850 kg/m³.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Use the measured terminal velocity v = 0.05 m/s.
Compute the ball radius r = 1.5 mm = 0.0015 m.
Compute the dynamic viscosity using Stokes’ law at terminal velocity: μ = (2/9)·r²·(ρ_ball − ρ_fluid)·g / v.
Report μ in Pa·s and optionally in mPa·s.
Calculate with the given values to obtain μ ≈ 0.00770 Pa·s (≈ 7.70 mPa·s).
