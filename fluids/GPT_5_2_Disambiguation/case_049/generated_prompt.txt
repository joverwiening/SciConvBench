Compute the hydraulic diameter for a rectangular duct using Dh = 4A/P.
Use internal side lengths a = 0.30 m and b = 0.10 m.
Compute cross-sectional area A = a·b = 0.03 m².
Compute wetted perimeter P = 2(a + b) = 0.80 m.
Compute hydraulic diameter Dh = 4A/P = 4·0.03/0.80 = 0.15 m.
Report the hydraulic diameter as Dh = 0.15 m (15 cm).
