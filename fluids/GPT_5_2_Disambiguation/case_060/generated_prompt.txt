Compute the thermal efficiency of an ideal Carnot heat engine.
Use source (hot reservoir) temperature Th = 800 K.
Use sink (cold reservoir) temperature Tc = 300 K.
Assume temperatures are absolute (Kelvin) and the engine is reversible (Carnot).
Calculate efficiency using η = 1 − (Tc/Th).
Substitute values: η = 1 − (300/800) = 1 − 0.375 = 0.625.
Report the thermal efficiency as η = 0.625, i.e., 62.5%.
