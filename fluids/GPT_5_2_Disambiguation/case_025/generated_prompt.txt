Compute the excess internal pressure of a spherical soap bubble using the Laplace relation for a soap film with two interfaces: ΔP = 4σ/r.
Use σ = 0.035 N/m.
Use the given outer diameter D = 10 mm with negligible film thickness, so r = D/2 = 5 mm = 0.005 m.
Calculate ΔP = 4(0.035)/0.005 = 28 Pa.
Report the excess pressure inside the bubble relative to outside as 28 Pa.
