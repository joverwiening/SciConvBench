Use a water jet diameter of 10 mm (0.01 m).
Use a jet impact speed of 15 m/s.
Assume the plate is stationary.
Assume the jet’s normal exit velocity is zero (the jet is brought to rest in the direction normal to the plate and then flows sideways with no rebound).
Assume water density is 1000 kg/m^3.
Compute jet area as A = (π/4) d^2.
Compute mass flow rate as m_dot = ρ A V.
Compute force on the plate (normal to the plate) as F = m_dot (V_in − V_out,normal) = ρ A V^2.
Calculate A = (π/4)(0.01)^2 = 7.85398×10^-5 m^2.
Calculate F = 1000 × 7.85398×10^-5 × 15^2 = 17.67 N.
Report the force produced by the jet on the flat plate as approximately 17.7 N (normal to the plate).
