Compute the capillary rise height of water in a circular tube of inner diameter 0.5 mm.
Assume the tube is vertical and aligned with gravity.
Assume water temperature is 20 °C (room temperature).
Use contact angle θ = 0° (complete wetting on glass).
Use surface tension σ = 0.072 N/m.
Use density ρ = 1000 kg/m³.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Use the capillary rise relation h = (4 σ cosθ) / (ρ g d), where d is tube inner diameter.
Report the capillary rise height h in meters and millimeters.
