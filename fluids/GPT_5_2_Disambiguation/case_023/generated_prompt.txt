Calculate the pressure drop per unit length (ΔP/L) for air flowing through a packed bed of spherical particles using the Ergun equation.
Use particle diameter dp = 0.005 m.
Use sphericity φ = 1.0.
Use bed void fraction (porosity) ε = 0.4.
Use superficial velocity U = 0.5 m/s.
Assume air at T = 293.15 K and P = 1 atm.
Obtain air density ρ and dynamic viscosity μ at 293.15 K and 1 atm from a standard property source.
Compute ΔP/L using the Ergun form: ΔP/L = 150 * (1−ε)^2/ε^3 * (μ U)/(dp^2) + 1.75 * (1−ε)/ε^3 * (ρ U^2)/(dp).
Report ΔP/L in Pa/m.
If total pressure drop is later needed, compute ΔP = (ΔP/L) * L for the specified bed length L.
