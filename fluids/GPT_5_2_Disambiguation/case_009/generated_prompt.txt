Compute the Darcy friction factor f for internal pipe flow at Reynolds number Re = 2.5e5.
Use the given relative roughness ε/D = 0.0004.
Assume turbulent flow and do not apply any transitional-regime treatment.
Use the Colebrook–White equation: 1/sqrt(f) = -2.0*log10( (ε/D)/3.7 + 2.51/(Re*sqrt(f)) ).
Solve the Colebrook–White equation iteratively to obtain f.
Report the resulting Darcy friction factor f as a dimensionless value.
