Compute the speed of sound for an ideal-gas mixture at T = 300 K.
Use a binary mixture with mole fractions x_He = 0.70 and x_Ar = 0.30.
Assume the mixture is frozen and nonreacting.
Assume ideal-gas behavior so the result is pressure-independent (1 atm may be used as a nominal reference but must not affect the computed speed).
Evaluate γ = c_p/c_v at 300 K for the mixture.
Treat both He and Ar as monatomic ideal gases at 300 K, so use γ = 5/3 for each species and for the mixture.
Compute the mixture molar mass as M_mix = x_He*M_He + x_Ar*M_Ar using M_He = 4.002602 g/mol and M_Ar = 39.948 g/mol.
Compute the mixture specific gas constant as R_spec = R_u / M_mix using R_u = 8.314462618 J/(mol·K) and M_mix in kg/mol.
Compute the speed of sound as a = sqrt(γ * R_spec * T).
Report the final speed of sound in m/s at 300 K.
