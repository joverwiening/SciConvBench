Compute the steam turbine power output using the steady-flow energy equation simplified to an adiabatic turbine with negligible kinetic and potential energy changes.
Use the given mass flow rate m_dot = 10 kg/s.
Use the given specific enthalpies h1 = 3200 kJ/kg at the inlet and h2 = 2400 kJ/kg at the outlet.
Calculate the specific work output as w_t = h1 − h2 = 800 kJ/kg.
Calculate the turbine power output as W_dot = m_dot × (h1 − h2) = 10 × 800 = 8000 kJ/s.
Report the power output as W_dot = 8000 kW = 8.0 MW (power produced by the turbine).
