Compute the stagnation temperature T0 for a compressible flow at Mach number M = 2.0.
Use the given static temperature T = 250 K.
Assume a calorically perfect gas with specific heat ratio γ = 1.4 (air).
Use the isentropic relation T0 = T × (1 + ((γ − 1)/2) × M^2).
Calculate ((γ − 1)/2) = 0.2.
Calculate 1 + 0.2 × (2.0^2) = 1.8.
Compute T0 = 250 K × 1.8 = 450 K.
Report the stagnation temperature as 450 K.
