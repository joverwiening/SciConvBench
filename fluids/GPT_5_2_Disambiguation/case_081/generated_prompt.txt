Assume the vane deflects the jet by 120° so the outlet velocity direction is 120° from the inlet direction in the same plane.

Compute force on fluid as F_fluid = ṁ (V2 − V1) using vector subtraction.
Compute force on vane as F_vane = −F_fluid.
Report force components Fx and Fy on the vane.
