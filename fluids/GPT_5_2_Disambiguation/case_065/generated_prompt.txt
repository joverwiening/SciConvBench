Assume sea-level standard pressure p = 1 atm for air properties.
Assume a reasonable free-stream temperature T∞ = 300 K (≈27 °C) as a best-practice default when not specified.
Assume an isothermal plate boundary condition with properties evaluated at the film temperature Tf = (Ts + T∞)/2.
Use standard laminar flat-plate correlations for average heat transfer: NūL = 0.664 ReL^0.5 Pr^(1/3).
Compute Reynolds number as ReL = (ρ U∞ L)/μ using air properties at Tf.
Compute average heat transfer coefficient as h = (NūL k)/L using air thermal conductivity k at Tf.
