Compute the efficiency of a straight rectangular aluminum fin with k = 200 W/m·K, length L = 0.03 m, thickness t = 0.002 m, and convection coefficient h = 20 W/m²·K.
Assume an adiabatic (insulated) fin tip.
Assume a unit fin width w = 1.0 m (per-meter width basis) because width is unspecified and cancels out for fin efficiency in this geometry.
Model the fin as a uniform cross-section straight fin with convection from the two large faces and the two thin edges along the length.
Use cross-sectional area A_c = w·t and perimeter P = 2(w + t).
Compute fin parameter m = sqrt(h·P/(k·A_c)).
For an adiabatic tip, compute fin efficiency η_f = tanh(mL)/(mL).
Report the numerical value of η_f for the given inputs.
