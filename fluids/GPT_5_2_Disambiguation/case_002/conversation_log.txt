Q: What airfoil camber line should the thin airfoil theory use (e.g., NACA 0012 symmetric, NACA 2412, or a custom camber-line equation)?
A: Use the **NACA 4412** camber line \(y_c(x)\) (i.e., NACA 4‑digit mean camber line with **m = 0.04** and **p = 0.4**, thickness is irrelevant for thin airfoil theory).

Q: What chord length should the script assume (e.g., c = 1.0), since the NACA 4-digit camber-line equation uses x/c?
A: Assume a **unit chord**, **c = 1.0** (so \(x/c = x\) over \(x \in [0,1]\)).
