Calculate the thermal efficiency of an ideal air-standard Brayton cycle gas turbine.
Use a compressor pressure ratio rp = 12.
Assume constant specific heats with γ = 1.4.
Assume isentropic compression and isentropic expansion.
Assume compressor inlet temperature T1 = 300 K as a best-practice ambient standard condition.
Assume turbine inlet temperature T3 = 1400 K as a best-practice modern turbine inlet condition.
Compute T2 from isentropic compression: T2 = T1 · rp^((γ−1)/γ).
Compute T4 from isentropic expansion: T4 = T3 / rp^((γ−1)/γ).
Compute thermal efficiency using ηth = 1 − (T4 − T1)/(T3 − T2).
Report ηth as a decimal and as a percentage.
Use the above assumptions without introducing additional losses, pressure drops, or component efficiencies.
