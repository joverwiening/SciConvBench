Solve the 1D steady-state heat conduction equation in a plane wall with uniform volumetric heat generation: d²T/dx² = −q‴/k.
Use the coordinate system with x = 0 at the wall mid-plane and wall surfaces at x = ±0.10 m.
Use q‴ = 2.0×10^5 W/m³ and k = 15 W/(m·K), constant throughout the wall.
Apply Dirichlet boundary conditions: T(−0.10 m) = 50°C and T(+0.10 m) = 50°C.
Obtain the temperature distribution: T(x) = 50 + (q‴/(2k))((0.10)² − x²), with x in meters and T in °C (temperature differences in K are equivalent).
Compute the maximum temperature at the mid-plane: T(0) = 50 + (q‴/(2k))(0.10)² = 56.67°C.
Compute the heat flux distribution: qx(x) = −k dT/dx = q‴ x, with x in meters and qx in W/m².
Compute the surface heat flux magnitudes: |qx(±0.10)| = q‴(0.10) = 2.0×10^4 W/m², directed outward at each surface.
