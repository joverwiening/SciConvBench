Assume steady, laminar, incompressible, Newtonian Couette flow between two infinite parallel plates with no-slip at both walls.
Let y be the wall-normal coordinate measured from the bottom plate, with y=0 at the bottom plate and y=h at the top plate.
Use plate separation h = 10 mm = 0.01 m.
Use boundary conditions u(0) = 0 m/s and u(h) = 5 m/s in the +x direction.
Assume zero streamwise pressure gradient (dp/dx = 0).
Solve the steady x-momentum equation for fully developed flow, which reduces to d²u/dy² = 0.
Integrate to obtain u(y) = C1 y + C2.
Apply u(0)=0 to get C2 = 0.
Apply u(h)=5 to get C1 = 5/h.
Report the steady velocity profile as u(y) = (5/h) y = (5/0.01) y = 500 y, with y in meters and u in m/s, valid for 0 ≤ y ≤ 0.01 m.
Optionally report the constant shear rate du/dy = 5/h = 500 s⁻¹.
Optionally report the constant shear stress τ = μ du/dy = 500 μ (Pa) if μ is in Pa·s.
