Compute the Biot number for a cooling sphere of radius R = 5 mm = 0.005 m.
Use the sphere thermal conductivity k = 45 W/m·K.
Use the convective heat transfer coefficient h = 150 W/m²·K.
Use the characteristic length for a sphere Lc = V/As = R/3.
Compute Bi = h·Lc/k = h·(R/3)/k.
Calculate Bi = 150·(0.005/3)/45 = 0.00556 (dimensionless).
