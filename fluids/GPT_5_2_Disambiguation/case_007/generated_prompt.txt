Calculate steady-state radial heat loss per meter from a horizontal insulated pipe.
Use a 1.0 m pipe length.
Use NPS 2 steel pipe outside diameter Do = 60.3 mm.
Assume the pipe outer surface temperature (at the pipe OD, inner insulation surface) is 150 °C.
Use ambient air temperature Ta = 20 °C.
Model insulation as 25 mm thick glass wool with thermal conductivity k = 0.04 W/m·K.
Neglect thermal radiation from the insulation outer surface.
Apply external convection at the insulation outer surface with a specified constant heat-transfer coefficient h = 10 W/m²·K to ambient air at 20 °C.
Compute the insulation outer diameter as D2 = Do + 2·(0.025 m).
Compute conductive thermal resistance of the cylindrical insulation as Rcond = ln(D2/Do) / (2πkL).
Compute external convective thermal resistance as Rconv = 1 / (h·π·D2·L).
Compute total thermal resistance as Rtot = Rcond + Rconv.
Compute heat loss per meter as q′ = (Ts − Ta) / Rtot, where Ts = 150 °C and Ta = 20 °C.
Report q′ in W/m.
