Compute lift per unit span on a rotating cylinder in air using the ideal inviscid Magnus/Kutta–Joukowski model.
Use cylinder radius R = 0.1 m.
Use angular velocity ω = 200 rad/s.
Use free-stream speed V = 15 m/s relative to the cylinder.
Use air density ρ = 1.225 kg/m³.
Use unit span (length) of 1 m and report lift per unit length L′ in N/m.
Compute circulation as Γ = 2πR²ω.
Compute lift per unit span as L′ = ρVΓ.
Substitute values: Γ = 2π(0.1)²(200) = 12.566 m²/s.
Compute lift per unit span: L′ = (1.225)(15)(12.566) = 230.8 N/m.
