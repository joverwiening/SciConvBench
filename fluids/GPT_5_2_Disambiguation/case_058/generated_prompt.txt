Calculate the hydraulic power required to pump water from a lower reservoir to an upper reservoir.
Use a volumetric flow rate Q = 0.02 m³/s.
Use a total head H = 30 m.
Assume water density ρ = 1000 kg/m³.
Assume gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Compute hydraulic power as Ph = ρ g Q H.
Compute required input (shaft/electrical) power as Pin = Ph / η using pump efficiency η = 0.80.
Report Ph in kW and Pin in kW.
Using the given values, Ph = 1000 × 9.81 × 0.02 × 30 = 5886 W = 5.89 kW.
Using the given values, Pin = 5886 / 0.80 = 7357.5 W = 7.36 kW.
