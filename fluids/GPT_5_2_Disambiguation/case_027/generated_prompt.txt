Calculate lift using L = 0.5 * rho * V^2 * S * CL.
Use wing area S = 20 m^2.
Use airspeed V = 80 m/s.
Use air density rho = 1.1 kg/m^3.
Use lift coefficient CL = 0.45.
Compute dynamic pressure q = 0.5 * rho * V^2 = 0.5 * 1.1 * 80^2 = 3520 Pa.
Compute lift L = q * S * CL = 3520 * 20 * 0.45 = 31680 N.
