The Stefan number for the phase-change problem will be calculated with the following specifications:
Material: Ice
Temperature difference: 10°C
Latent heat of fusion: 334 kJ/kg
Thermal conductivity: 2.2 W/(m·K)
Characteristic length: 0.1 meters

The Stefan number will be calculated using the standard formula: Ste = (Cp * ΔT) / Lf
Where:
Cp is the specific heat capacity of ice
ΔT is the temperature difference
Lf is the latent heat of fusion

Note: A complete calculation would require the specific heat capacity of ice, which was not specified in the original requirement. For a precise Stefan number, that additional parameter would need to be provided or assumed.
