Calculate the hydraulic efficiency of the Pelton wheel turbine using the following parameters:
Water discharge rate is 0.5 m³/s.
Net head is 300 meters.
Water weight flow rate is 4,905 kg/s.
Runner power output is 1,300 kW.
Hydraulic efficiency will be calculated using the formula: Hydraulic Efficiency = (Power Output) / (Weight Flow Rate × Net Head × Gravitational Acceleration)
Hydraulic Efficiency = 1,300 kW / (4,905 kg/s × 300 m × 9.81 m/s²)
The calculation will provide the hydraulic efficiency as a percentage representing the turbine's energy conversion effectiveness.
