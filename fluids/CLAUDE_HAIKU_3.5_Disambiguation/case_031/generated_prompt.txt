Determine the pressure at a depth of 2000 m in seawater using the following specification:
The calculation will use seawater with a density of 1025 kg/m³.
Use gravitational acceleration of 9.81 m/s².
Calculate hydrostatic pressure using the formula P = ρ * g * h.
Where ρ (rho) = 1025 kg/m³, g = 9.81 m/s², and h = 2000 m.
Report the final pressure in pascals (Pa) or megapascals (MPa).
