Calculate the power required to pump water from the lower to the upper reservoir with the following specifications:
Vertical height difference is 30 meters.
Water flow rate is 0.02 cubic meters per second.
Water density is 1000 kilograms per cubic meter.
Pump efficiency is 80%.
Use the hydraulic power equation: Power = (ρ * g * h * Q) / η
Where ρ is water density, g is gravitational acceleration (9.81 m/s²), h is height, Q is flow rate, and η is pump efficiency.
Provide the final power calculation in kilowatts (kW).
Ensure all calculations account for the specified pump efficiency.
