Determine the rate of heat transfer through a concrete wall using Fourier's law of heat conduction.
The concrete wall is 0.2 meters thick.
The temperature difference between the two sides of the wall is 20°C.
The thermal conductivity of the concrete is 1.4 W/(m·K).
Calculate the heat transfer rate using the formula: Q = (k * A * ΔT) / d
Where:
Q = heat transfer rate (Watts)
k = thermal conductivity (1.4 W/(m·K))
A = surface area of the wall (to be specified)
ΔT = temperature difference (20°C)
d = wall thickness (0.2 m)
The final heat transfer rate will be calculated by multiplying these values.
