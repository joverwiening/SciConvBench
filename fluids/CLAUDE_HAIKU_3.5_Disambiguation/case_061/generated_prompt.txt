Calculate the lift force for an airfoil with the following specifications:

Airfoil velocity is 85 m/s.
Air density is 1.225 kg/m³.
Airfoil area is 12 m².
Lift coefficient is 0.45.
Angle of attack is 4 degrees (standard for generating a lift coefficient of 0.45).
Chord length is 2 meters (consistent with 12 m² area).
Use a NACA 4412 symmetric airfoil profile.

Lift force will be calculated using the standard aerodynamic lift equation: L = 0.5 * ρ * v² * S * Cl, where:
- ρ (rho) is air density
- v is velocity
- S is wing area
- Cl is lift coefficient

The calculation will provide the total lift force generated by the airfoil under these standard sea-level conditions.
