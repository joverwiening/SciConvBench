Calculate the hydraulic diameter of a rectangular duct with dimensions 30 cm x 10 cm.
Use the standard hydraulic diameter formula for rectangular ducts: Dh = (2 * a * b) / (a + b), where a and b are the duct dimensions.
Substitute the values: a = 30 cm, b = 10 cm.
Perform the calculation to determine the hydraulic diameter.
Round the result to an appropriate number of significant figures.
