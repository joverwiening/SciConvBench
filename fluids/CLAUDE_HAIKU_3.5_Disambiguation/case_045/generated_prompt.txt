Calculate the cavitation index for the valve using the following specification.

Upstream pressure (P_in) is 400 kPa.
Downstream pressure (P_out) is 150 kPa.
Fluid vapor pressure (P_v) is 2.5 kPa.

Cavitation index (σ) shall be calculated using the formula: σ = (P_in - P_out) / (0.5 * ρ * v²)

Assumptions for calculation:
Assume water as the fluid at standard temperature (20°C).
Fluid density (ρ) is approximately 998 kg/m³.
Use standard fluid velocity through the valve (assume 5 m/s).

The cavitation index will indicate the valve's susceptibility to cavitation based on pressure differentials and fluid properties.
