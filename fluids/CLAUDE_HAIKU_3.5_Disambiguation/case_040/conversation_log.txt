Q: What is the total (stagnation) temperature of the flow before entering the compressible flow condition?
A: 250K is the static temperature, and the flow is at Mach 2.0. The stagnation temperature can be calculated using the standard compressible flow temperature relation, but the specific value is 250K * (1 + 0.2 * 2^2) = 500K.
