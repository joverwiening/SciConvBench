Calculate the Reynolds number for flow over a flat plate in air at the following conditions.
Use the distance of 0.5 m from the leading edge.
Use the air flow velocity of 10 m/s.
Use the kinematic viscosity of 1.5e-5 m²/s.
Apply the Reynolds number formula: Re = (Velocity * Distance) / Kinematic Viscosity.
Compute the Reynolds number by multiplying 10 m/s by 0.5 m and dividing by 1.5e-5 m²/s.
The result will represent the dimensionless Reynolds number characterizing the flow regime.
