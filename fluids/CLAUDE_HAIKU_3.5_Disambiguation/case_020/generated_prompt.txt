Calculate the cavitation number for water with the following parameters:
Flow velocity is 20 m/s.
Absolute pressure is 50 kPa.
Vapor pressure is 2.3 kPa.
Water density is 1000 kg/m³.
Water temperature is 20°C.
Use the cavitation number formula: σ = (P - Pv) / (0.5 * ρ * V²), where:
P is the absolute pressure.
Pv is the vapor pressure.
ρ is the water density.
V is the flow velocity.
