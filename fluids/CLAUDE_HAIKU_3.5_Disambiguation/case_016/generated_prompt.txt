The center of pressure calculation for a 1 m x 2 m vertically submerged rectangular plate will be performed with the following specifications:

Plate Dimensions: 1 m wide by 2 m tall
Plate Orientation: Vertical, perpendicular to water surface
Top Edge Position: At water surface (0 m depth)
Water Density: 1000 kg/m³
Atmospheric Pressure: 101,325 Pa
Gravitational Acceleration: 9.81 m/s²

The center of pressure will be calculated using hydrostatic pressure principles, considering the plate's full vertical submersion with its top edge at the water surface.

The calculation will determine the point along the plate's height where the total hydrostatic force acts, accounting for the linear increase in pressure with depth.

The center of pressure will be located vertically from the water surface, taking into account the varying pressure distribution across the plate's submerged area.
