Calculate the force produced by a water jet with the following specifications:
Jet diameter is 10 mm.
Jet velocity at impact is 15 m/s.
Water density is 1000 kg/m³.
Impact angle is 90 degrees (perpendicular to the plate).
Calculate the force using the momentum change equation: F = ρ * A * v * v, where:
ρ (rho) is water density
A is the cross-sectional area of the jet
v is the velocity of the water jet
The calculation will determine the total force exerted by the water jet on the flat plate upon impact.
