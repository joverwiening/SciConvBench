Calculate the Weber number for a liquid droplet with the following parameters.
Droplet diameter is 2 mm.
Droplet velocity is 30 m/s.
Liquid surface tension is 0.03 N/m.
Liquid density is 800 kg/m³.
Use the Weber number formula: We = (ρ * v² * D) / σ
Where ρ is liquid density, v is velocity, D is droplet diameter, and σ is surface tension.
Perform the calculation using SI units.
Report the final Weber number as a dimensionless value.
