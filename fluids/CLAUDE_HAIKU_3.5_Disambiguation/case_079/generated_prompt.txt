Determine Thoma's cavitation factor (sigma) for a Francis turbine.
The total head is 50 meters.
The net positive suction head available (NPSHA) is 10 meters.
The specific gravity of water is 1.0.
The turbine rotational speed is 600 RPM.
Calculate Thoma's cavitation factor (sigma) using the standard hydraulic engineering formula: σ = NPSHA / (H * g), where H is the total head.
Sigma will be calculated as: σ = 10 / (50 * 1.0) = 0.2.
The cavitation factor of 0.2 indicates the turbine's susceptibility to cavitation under these specified conditions.
