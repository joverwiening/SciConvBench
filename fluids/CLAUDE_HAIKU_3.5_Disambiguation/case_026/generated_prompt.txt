Determine the Knudsen number for gas flow in a 100 nm wide channel.
Use air as the working gas.
Use standard temperature of 293.15 K.
Use standard pressure of 101,325 Pa.
Calculate the Knudsen number using the channel width (100 nm) and mean free path (68 nm).
The Knudsen number is defined as the ratio of the mean free path to the characteristic length of the system.
Knudsen number = Mean free path / Channel width = 68 nm / 100 nm = 0.68.
This Knudsen number indicates a transitional flow regime between molecular and continuum flow.
