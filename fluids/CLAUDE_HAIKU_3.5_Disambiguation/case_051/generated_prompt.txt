Use ambient air density of 1.2 kg/m³.
Use helium gas density of 0.18 kg/m³.
Calculate buoyant force using the formula: Lift Force = Volume × (Air Density - Gas Density) × Gravitational Acceleration.
Compute the lift force in Newtons (N).
Assume standard gravitational acceleration of 9.81 m/s².
