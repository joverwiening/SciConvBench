For steel, the typical thermal diffusivity (α) can be calculated using the formula: α = k / (ρ * Cp)

Where:
- k = thermal conductivity (45 W/m·K, which we know)
- ρ = density of steel (typically around 7850 kg/m³)
- Cp = specific heat capacity of steel (typically around 460 J/kg·K)

Let me calculate the thermal diffusivity:
α = 45 / (7850 * 460) ≈ 0.0125 m²/s

- k = thermal conductivity (45 W/m·K)
