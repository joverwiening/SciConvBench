Calculate the flow rate through a Venturi meter with the following specifications.
Use water as the working fluid at 20°C with a density of 998 kg/m³.
Use a Venturi meter with an inlet diameter of 100 mm and a throat diameter of 50 mm.
Pressure differential (ΔP) is 20 kPa.
Kinematic viscosity is 1.004 × 10^-6 m²/s.
Apply the Bernoulli equation and continuity equation to determine the volumetric flow rate.
Verify the flow regime by calculating the Reynolds number at both the inlet and throat sections.
Report the final flow rate in m³/s or L/min, whichever is more appropriate.
