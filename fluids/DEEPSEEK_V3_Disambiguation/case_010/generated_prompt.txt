Calculate the lift per unit length on a rotating cylinder using the Kutta-Joukowski theorem.
The cylinder radius is 0.1 meters.
The cylinder rotates at 200 radians per second.
The freestream air velocity relative to the cylinder is 15 meters per second.
The air density is 1.225 kilograms per cubic meter.
Compute the circulation as (2 * pi * radius^2 * rotational speed).
Compute the lift per unit length as (density * freestream velocity * circulation).
