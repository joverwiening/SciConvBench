Assume the fluid is air at standard temperature and pressure.
Calculate the hydraulic diameter using the formula Dh = (4 * A) / P, where A is the cross-sectional area and P is the wetted perimeter.
The cross-sectional area A is 30 cm * 10 cm = 300 cm².
The wetted perimeter P is 2*(30 cm + 10 cm) = 80 cm.
The hydraulic diameter Dh is (4 * 300 cm²) / 80 cm = 15 cm.
