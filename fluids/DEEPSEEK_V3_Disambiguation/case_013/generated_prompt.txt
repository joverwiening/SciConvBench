Assume the bare cylindrical pipe is a standard Schedule 40 steel pipe with a nominal size of 2 inches.
Assume the outer diameter of the bare pipe is 60.3 mm.
Assume the inner diameter of the bare pipe is 52.5 mm.
Assume the pipe wall material is carbon steel with a thermal conductivity (k_pipe) of 50 W/m·K.
Assume the fluid inside the pipe is saturated steam at 1 atm.
Assume the temperature of the fluid inside the pipe (T_inf,i) is 100°C.
Assume the convective heat transfer coefficient on the pipe's inner surface (h_i) is 1000 W/m²·K.
Assume the surrounding ambient air temperature (T_inf,o) is 20°C.
Assume the convective heat transfer coefficient on the outer surface of the insulation (h) is 8 W/m²·K.
Assume the thermal conductivity of the insulation (k_ins) is 0.17 W/m·K.
Assume the length of the pipe section (L) is 1 meter for the per-unit-length analysis.
Calculate the critical radius of insulation for the cylinder using the formula r_cr = k_ins / h.
Calculate the critical insulation thickness as t_cr = r_cr - r1, where r1 is the outer radius of the bare pipe.
Report the critical insulation thickness in millimeters.
