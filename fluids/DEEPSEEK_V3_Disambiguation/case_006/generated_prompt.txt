Assume the fluid is water at 20°C, with a dynamic viscosity of 0.001002 Pa·s.
The plates are parallel and separated by a distance of 10 mm (0.01 m).
The bottom plate is stationary.
The top plate moves with a constant velocity of 5 m/s in its own plane.
Assume the flow is laminar, steady-state, and fully developed.
Assume no pressure gradient is applied in the flow direction.
Assume no-slip boundary conditions at both plate surfaces.
Neglect body forces.
The velocity profile is linear, varying from 0 m/s at the bottom plate to 5 m/s at the top plate.
The velocity at any point between the plates is given by u(y) = (U / h) * y, where U is 5 m/s, h is 0.01 m, and y is the vertical coordinate measured from the bottom plate.
