Calculate the power output of the steam turbine.
The steam mass flow rate is 10 kg/s.
The specific enthalpy at the turbine inlet is 3200 kJ/kg.
The specific enthalpy at the turbine outlet is 2400 kJ/kg.
Compute the power output using the formula: Power = mass flow rate * (inlet enthalpy - outlet enthalpy).
The calculated power output is 8000 kW.
