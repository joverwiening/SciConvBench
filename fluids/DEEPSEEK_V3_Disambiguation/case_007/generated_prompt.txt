Calculate the steady-state heat loss per meter length of pipe.
The pipe is a horizontal steel pipe with a nominal diameter of 2 inches.
The steam inside the pipe is maintained at a constant temperature of 150°C.
The ambient air temperature surrounding the pipe is 20°C.
The pipe is insulated with a 25mm thick layer of glass wool.
Use a thermal conductivity value of 0.04 W/m·K for the glass wool insulation.
Assume the external surface of the insulation is exposed to still air.
Neglect the thermal resistance of the pipe wall itself.
Report the final heat loss value in watts per meter (W/m).
