Calculate the hydrostatic force on a vertically submerged rectangular plate.
The plate is 1 meter wide and 2 meters high.
The top edge of the plate is at the fluid's free surface.
The fluid is water with a density of 1000 kg/m³.
Use a gravitational acceleration of 9.81 m/s².
The center of pressure is located along the plate's vertical centerline.
Compute the vertical distance from the free surface to the center of pressure.
The result is a single numerical value in meters.
