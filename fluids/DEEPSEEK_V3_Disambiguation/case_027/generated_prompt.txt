Calculate the lift force on the wing using the lift equation: L = 0.5 * ρ * V² * S * Cl.
Use an airspeed (V) of 80 meters per second.
Use an air density (ρ) of 1.1 kilograms per cubic meter.
Use a wing area (S) of 20 square meters.
Use a lift coefficient (Cl) of 0.45.
The calculated lift force is 31680 Newtons.
