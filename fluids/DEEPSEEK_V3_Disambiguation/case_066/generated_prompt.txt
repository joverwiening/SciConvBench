Calculate the Coefficient of Performance (COP) for a standard vapor-compression refrigeration cycle.
Assume the cycle uses R-134a as the refrigerant.
Assume an evaporator temperature of -10°C.
Assume a condenser temperature of 40°C.
Assume the cycle operates between the corresponding saturation pressures for these temperatures.
Assume the compressor isentropic efficiency is 100%.
Calculate the COP as the ratio of refrigeration effect to compressor work.
