Calculate the hydrostatic pressure at a depth of 2000 meters in the ocean.
Use a seawater density of 1025 kg/m³.
Use a gravitational acceleration of 9.81 m/s².
Assume the pressure at the surface (atmospheric pressure) is 101325 Pascals.
Calculate the pressure using the formula P = P_atm + (ρ * g * h), where ρ is density, g is gravity, and h is depth.
The final pressure value must be expressed in Pascals (Pa).
