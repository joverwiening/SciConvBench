Use the NACA 4412 airfoil geometry.
Implement thin airfoil theory to compute the lift coefficient.
Assume a small angle regime where the theory is valid.
Calculate the zero-lift angle of attack for the NACA 4412 camber line.
Compute the lift curve slope as 2π per radian.
Apply the formula: lift coefficient = 2π * (angle of attack - zero-lift angle of attack), with angles in radians.
Set the specific angle of attack for the calculation to 5 degrees.
Convert the 5-degree angle of attack to radians for the computation.
Output the computed lift coefficient value.
The script does not require freestream velocity or dynamic pressure as these are not inputs for an incompressible, inviscid thin airfoil theory lift calculation.
The script does not require a Reynolds number as thin airfoil theory is inviscid.
