The system is a piston-cylinder device containing an ideal gas.
The initial state of the gas is 500 kPa pressure and 300 K temperature.
The final state of the gas is 100 kPa pressure and 300 K temperature.
The mass of the gas is 2 kg.
The specific gas constant is 0.2968 kJ/kg·K.
The process is an isothermal expansion.
Calculate the boundary work done by the gas during this expansion.
Use the formula for isothermal work for an ideal gas: W = m * R * T * ln(P1/P2).
Substitute the given values: m = 2 kg, R = 0.2968 kJ/kg·K, T = 300 K, P1 = 500 kPa, P2 = 100 kPa.
Perform the calculation to determine the work done in kJ.
