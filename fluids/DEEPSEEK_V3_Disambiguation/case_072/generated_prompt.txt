The task is to find the hydraulic efficiency of a Pelton wheel turbine.
The turbine operates under a net head of 300 meters.
The calculation requires the jet velocity.
The jet velocity is calculated using the formula V = C_v * sqrt(2 * g * H), where C_v is the coefficient of velocity.
The coefficient of velocity C_v is assumed to be 0.98, a standard value for a well-designed nozzle.
The acceleration due to gravity g is 9.81 m/s².
The net head H is 300 m.
The jet velocity V is calculated as 0.98 * sqrt(2 * 9.81 * 300).
The bucket speed (runner velocity) is required for efficiency calculation.
The bucket speed U is calculated as U = k_u * sqrt(2 * g * H), where k_u is the speed ratio.
The speed ratio k_u is assumed to be 0.46, a typical optimum value for maximum hydraulic efficiency.
The bucket speed U is calculated as 0.46 * sqrt(2 * 9.81 * 300).
The hydraulic efficiency η_h is calculated using the formula η_h = (2 * U * (V - U) * (1 + cos φ)) / V², where φ is the bucket deflection angle.
The bucket deflection angle φ is assumed to be 165 degrees, a common value.
The cosine of 165 degrees is cos(165°) = -0.9659, but the absolute value |cos(165°)| = 0.9659 is used in the standard formula for efficiency.
The hydraulic efficiency is calculated using the absolute value of the cosine term.
Perform the calculation using the provided and assumed values.
The final answer is the calculated hydraulic efficiency expressed as a percentage.
