Assume the fluid medium is air.
Assume the specific heat ratio (gamma) for air is 1.4.
Use the static temperature of 250 K.
Calculate the stagnation temperature using the isentropic relation: T0 = T * (1 + ((gamma - 1)/2) * M^2).
Perform the calculation for a Mach number of 2.0.
The stagnation temperature is 450 K.
