Assume the fluid is air at standard temperature and pressure (20°C).
Assume the kinematic viscosity of air at 20°C is 1.516e-5 m²/s.
The characteristic length is 0.05 m.
The fluid velocity is 0.1 m/s.
Calculate the Peclet number using the formula Pe = (Velocity * Length) / Kinematic Viscosity.
The Peclet number is approximately 329.8.
