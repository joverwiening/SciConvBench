Calculate the terminal velocity of a spherical particle falling in oil.
The particle is a sphere with a diameter of 2 mm.
The particle density is 7800 kg/m³.
The oil density is 890 kg/m³.
The oil's dynamic viscosity is 0.29 Pa·s.
Use the standard acceleration due to gravity, 9.81 m/s².
Assume the particle is falling in an infinite, quiescent fluid medium.
Assume the flow around the particle is in the Stokes' law regime (creeping flow) and verify this assumption after calculation.
Use the Stokes' law equation for terminal velocity: V_t = (g * D_p² * (ρ_p - ρ_f)) / (18 * μ), where D_p is particle diameter, ρ_p is particle density, ρ_f is fluid density, and μ is dynamic viscosity.
Calculate the terminal velocity in meters per second.
After calculating, check the Reynolds number (Re = (ρ_f * V_t * D_p) / μ) to confirm it is less than 1, validating the use of Stokes' law.
