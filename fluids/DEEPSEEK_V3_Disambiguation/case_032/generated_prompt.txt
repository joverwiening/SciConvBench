Calculate the Weber number for a liquid droplet.
The droplet diameter is 2 mm.
The droplet velocity is 30 m/s.
The droplet liquid density is 800 kg/m³.
The droplet liquid surface tension is 0.03 N/m.
Use the formula We = (ρ * v² * d) / σ, where ρ is density, v is velocity, d is diameter, and σ is surface tension.
Ensure all units are in a consistent system (SI) before calculation.
