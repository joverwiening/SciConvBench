The compressor inlet temperature is 300 K.
The compressor pressure ratio is 8.
The compressor isentropic efficiency is 85%.
The outlet temperature must be calculated using the isentropic relations for an ideal gas and the given efficiency.
Assume the working fluid is air with a constant specific heat ratio (gamma) of 1.4.
Calculate the isentropic outlet temperature using the formula T2s = T1 * (P2/P1)^((gamma-1)/gamma).
Calculate the actual outlet temperature using the formula T2 = T1 + (T2s - T1) / eta_c, where eta_c is the isentropic efficiency.
The final answer is the calculated value of T2.
