The task is to determine the drag coefficient of a sphere in creeping flow at Re = 0.1.
Assume a sphere diameter of 1 meter as a standard reference length.
Assume the fluid is air at standard temperature and pressure, with a density of 1.225 kg/m³.
Assume the fluid dynamic viscosity is 1.81e-5 Pa·s for air at standard conditions.
Calculate the drag coefficient using Stokes' law, where Cd = 24/Re for a sphere in creeping flow.
The resulting drag coefficient is 240.
