The wall has a thickness of 0.1 m (typical for building walls).
The wall material is concrete with thermal conductivity k = 1.7 W/m·K (common building material).
The left boundary condition is T(0) = 350 K.
The right boundary condition is adiabatic (dT/dx = 0 at x = 0.1 m).
The heat conduction is steady-state (time-independent).
The problem is one-dimensional.
The solution will show that temperature is constant at 350 K throughout the wall due to adiabatic condition and steady state.
