Assume a standard industrial process pipe diameter of 0.1 meters.
The Reynolds number (Re) is 50,000.
The laminar hydrodynamic entrance length equation is Le = 0.05 × Re × D.
Note: The flow cannot be laminar at Re=50,000 as this is well above the laminar-turbulent transition (Re > 2300).
The flow is turbulent, making the original laminar entrance length equation invalid.
For turbulent flow, use the entrance length equation Le = 4.4 × Re^(1/6) × D.
The entrance length is 1.47 meters where the flow becomes fully developed.
