The oil has a density of 900 kg/m³ (typical value for mineral oil at room temperature).
The analysis is performed at room temperature (20°C).
Bernoulli's equation is NOT applicable for this flow scenario.
This is a viscous-dominated flow problem (Couette flow) where viscous forces are dominant.
The flow is laminar, steady, and fully developed.
The velocity profile is linear, varying from 0 at the bottom plate to 2.0 m/s at the top plate.
The pressure gradient in the direction perpendicular to the plates is zero (dP/dy = 0).
The pressure remains constant across the gap height.
The shear stress in the fluid is constant and equal to τ = μ(U/h) = 40 Pa.
