The mass flow rate is assumed to be 1 kg/s as a standard reference flow rate.
The equation q = m cp ΔT is incorrect for this phase change process.
Use the latent heat of vaporization equation: q = m * hfg.
The latent heat of vaporization (hfg) for water at 100°C is 2257 kJ/kg.
The heat removed is 2257 kJ/s or 2257 kW.
The process occurs at constant temperature (100°C) during phase change.
The provided cp and ΔT are not applicable since this is a phase change process.
