Calculate Reynolds number for water flow in a circular pipe using the following parameters:
Pipe diameter (D) is 0.10 meters.
Mean flow velocity (V) is 2.0 meters per second.
Hydraulic diameter (Dh) is 0.05 meters.
Water temperature is 20 degrees Celsius.
Water density (rho) is 998 kg/m^3.
Water dynamic viscosity (mu) is 1.0e-3 Pa·s.
Calculate kinematic viscosity by dividing dynamic viscosity by density.
Calculate Reynolds number using the formula Re = (V × Dh) / kinematic viscosity.
Express the final Reynolds number as a dimensionless value.
