Since this appears to be a standard hydraulic power calculation without a specified fluid, water should be assumed as the default working fluid per engineering best practices.


Use water as the working fluid (rho = 1000 kg/m³).
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Convert flow rate Q from 50 L/s to 0.050 m³/s.
Use pump head H = 30 m.
Calculate hydraulic power using the formula P = rho × g × Q × H.
Calculate P = 1000 × 9.81 × 0.050 × 30 = 14,715 Watts or 14.72 kW.
