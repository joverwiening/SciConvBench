Use a parallel plate channel with length 1 meter and width 0.5 meters.
Set the gap height between plates to 0.02 meters (20 mm).
Use an inlet velocity of 1 m/s for the oil flow.
Assume standard operating temperature of 25°C (298 K).
Use dynamic viscosity of oil as 0.1 Pa·s at 25°C.
Calculate velocity gradient (du/dy) at the wall using linear velocity profile.
For steady inviscid flow between parallel plates, velocity gradient at wall is U/h where U is inlet velocity and h is gap height.
Calculate wall shear stress using formula τ = μ(du/dy).
Wall shear stress equals 5 Pa (τ = 0.1 Pa·s × 1 m/s / 0.02 m).
Report results in SI units (Pascals).
