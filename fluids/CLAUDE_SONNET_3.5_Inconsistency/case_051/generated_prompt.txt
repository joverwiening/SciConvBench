Assume water temperature is 20°C (room temperature).
Use water properties at 20°C: k = 0.598 W/m·K.
Use standard pipe diameter of 0.0254 m (1 inch) as a typical size.
Given Reynolds number is 800 and Prandtl number is 6.
Apply Dittus-Boelter correlation: Nu = 0.023 × Re^0.8 × Pr^0.4.
Calculate Nusselt number: Nu = 0.023 × (800)^0.8 × (6)^0.4 = 7.97.
Calculate convection coefficient using h = (Nu × k)/D.
The convection coefficient h = (7.97 × 0.598)/0.0254 = 187.4 W/m²·K.
