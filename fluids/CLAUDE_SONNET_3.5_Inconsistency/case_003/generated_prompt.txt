The nozzle flow analysis shall use the following specifications:
The working fluid is air treated as an ideal gas with γ = 1.4.
The inlet conditions are at standard sea level with T1 = 288.15 K and P1 = 101.325 kPa.
The inlet Mach number is 2.0.
The nozzle is frictionless (isentropic flow).
The area ratio A2/A1 is 0.7.
The flow is steady and one-dimensional.
The exit conditions shall be determined using isentropic flow relations.
The velocity increase shall be calculated as a ratio of exit velocity to inlet velocity (V2/V1).
All calculations shall use SI units.
