Use a coefficient of thermal expansion (alpha) of 12 x 10^-6 per Kelvin for steel.
Use a Young's Modulus (E) of 200 GPa for steel.
The temperature change (ΔT) is 50 Kelvin.
Calculate thermal stress using the equation: σ = E × α × ΔT.
The thermal stress is σ = (200 × 10^9) × (12 × 10^-6) × 50 = 120 MPa.
The positive stress value indicates compressive stress in the constrained rod.
