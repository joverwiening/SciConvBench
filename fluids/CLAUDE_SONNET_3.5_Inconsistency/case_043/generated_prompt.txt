Use water at room temperature (20°C) with dynamic viscosity of 1.002 × 10^-3 Pa·s.
The sphere diameter is 5 mm (0.005 m).
The sphere velocity is 0.5 m/s.
Calculate drag force using Stokes equation: Fd = 3 × π × μ × D × V.
Note: The given Reynolds number condition (Re is large) contradicts the use of Stokes drag, as Stokes drag is valid only for Re < 1.
Use the calculation despite this limitation, as specifically requested.
The drag force calculation is: Fd = 3 × π × (1.002 × 10^-3) × (0.005) × (0.5) = 2.36 × 10^-5 N.
