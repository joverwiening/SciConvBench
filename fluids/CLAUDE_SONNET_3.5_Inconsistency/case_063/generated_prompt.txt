Assume a typical rectangular channel width of 2 meters.
Assume an initial flow depth of 1 meter.
Assume an initial flow velocity of 0.6 meters per second.

Given Fr = 0.2, solve for critical depth yc using: yc = (q²/g)^(1/3).
Where q is the specific discharge (discharge per unit width) = v*y.
The critical depth calculation must maintain continuity of flow.
