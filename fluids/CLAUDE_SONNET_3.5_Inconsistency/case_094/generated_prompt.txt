Use room temperature water at 20°C with density 998.2 kg/m³.
Use a glass capillary tube with 1 mm inner diameter (0.5 mm radius) as this is a common laboratory size.
Use a contact angle of 20° between water and clean glass, which is typical for this material pair.
Surface tension of water is -0.072 N/m.
Calculate capillary rise using the formula: h = (2γ cos θ)/(ρ g r).
The capillary rise is 14.9 mm above the water surface.
All calculations assume Earth's gravity (g = 9.81 m/s²).
The negative sign in surface tension is dropped for the calculation as the direction is handled by cos θ.
