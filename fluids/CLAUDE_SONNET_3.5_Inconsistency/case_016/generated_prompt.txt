The calculation shall use a laboratory benchtop centrifuge with a radius of 0.15 meters.
The fluid shall be water at 20°C with a density of 998.2 kg/m³.
The centrifugal acceleration shall replace g in the formula, calculated as rω² where ω is 10,000 RPM converted to radians/second.
The pressure shall be calculated from the rotation axis (h=0) to the outer wall (h=0.15m).
The formula P = ρrω²h shall be used where r is the local radius from the axis.
The pressure distribution shall be plotted as a function of radius from 0 to 0.15 meters.
The final pressure values shall be expressed in Pascals (Pa).
Temperature effects on density shall be considered negligible for this calculation.
