Calculate the exit velocity of water in a frictionless pipe system using these specifications:
The pipe connects a reservoir at z=0 m to a discharge at z=5 m elevation.
The initial velocity at the reservoir entrance is 0 m/s.
Include a pump head of 20 m in the Bernoulli equation.
Neglect friction losses in the pipe.
Neglect kinetic energy at the reservoir surface (v1 = 0 m/s).
Use standard gravity g = 9.81 m/s².
The pressure at both the reservoir surface and discharge point is atmospheric pressure.
Calculate the exit velocity using the Bernoulli equation with pump head: v2 = sqrt(2g(h_pump - Δz)).
