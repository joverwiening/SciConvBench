Use standard atmospheric pressure at sea level (10.3 m) as Ha.
The vapor pressure head (Hv) is 0.3 m.
The turbine runner is installed 20 m above the tailrace.
Calculate the cavitation factor sigma using the formula: σ = (Ha - Hv - Hs)/H, where Hs is the suction head (20 m).
Demonstrate that σ is positive for any positive operating head H.
Show that since σ is always positive, cavitation cannot occur regardless of the operating head.
Use the minimum calculated σ value to prove cavitation impossibility.
