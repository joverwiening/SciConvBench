The heat exchanger specification is designed for a water-water counterflow configuration with the following parameters:
Hot water inlet temperature (Th,in) is 80°C.
Cold water inlet temperature (Tc,in) is 20°C.
Hot water outlet temperature (Th,out) is 40°C.
Cold water outlet temperature (Tc,out) is 60°C.
Mass flow rate of hot water is 2 kg/s.
Mass flow rate of cold water is 2 kg/s.
Overall heat transfer coefficient (U) is 2000 W/m²K.
Specific heat capacity of water is 4.186 kJ/kg·K.
Heat transfer rate (Q) is 334.88 kW.
The logarithmic mean temperature difference (LMTD) using ΔT1 and ΔT2 is 20°C.
Required heat transfer area is 8.37 m².
