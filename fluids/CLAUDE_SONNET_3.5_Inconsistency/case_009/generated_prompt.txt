Use standard gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s^2.
The flow is irrotational with uniform Bernoulli constant between streamlines.
Point A conditions: pA = 200 kPa, VA = 5 m/s, zA = 0 m.
Point B conditions: VB = 8 m/s, zB = 0 m.
Apply Bernoulli equation: pA/ρg + VA^2/(2g) + zA = pB/ρg + VB^2/(2g) + zB.
Substitute known values and solve for pB.
The pressure at point B is pB = 183.9 kPa.
