Use water as the working fluid at standard conditions (20°C, 1 atm).
Set the inlet diameter (D) to 50mm, which is a common industrial size.
Use a diameter ratio (β) of 0.5, which is within typical range for Venturi meters.
Assume a pressure difference (Δp) of 50 kPa, which is reasonable for industrial applications.
Apply the ideal Bernoulli equation with Cd=1 to calculate the flow rate.
Account for density of water as 998 kg/m³ at standard conditions.
Calculate flow rate using Q = Cd*A2*sqrt((2*Δp)/(ρ*(1-β⁴))).
Report the flow rate in both m³/s and L/min.
Note that actual flow rates will be lower due to neglected losses (Cd < 1).
