The fluid flow problem has the following parameters:
Flow speed (V) is 10 meters per second.
Radius of curvature (R) is 5 meters.
Normal distance (Δn) is 0.10 meters.
Fluid density (rho) is 1000 kilograms per cubic meter.
Use the equation dP/dn = rho * V^2 / R to find the pressure gradient.
Multiply the pressure gradient by Δn to find the pressure difference.
The pressure difference (ΔP) between inner and outer streamline is 20,000 Pascals.
