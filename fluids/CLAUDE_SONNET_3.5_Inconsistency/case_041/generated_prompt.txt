Assume water as the working fluid with density ρ = 998 kg/m³.
Assume a typical industrial flow velocity of v = 2 m/s based on common engineering practice.
The pipe length L is 30 m.
The pipe diameter D is 0.1 m.
The Fanning friction factor f is 0.005.
Calculate pressure drop using Δp = f (L/D) (ρ v^2/2).
Substitute values: Δp = 0.005 * (30/0.1) * (998 * 2^2/2).
The pressure drop is 5988 Pa or approximately 6.0 kPa.
