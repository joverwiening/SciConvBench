The steady incompressible Navier-Stokes equations in Cartesian coordinates are used to analyze the flow.

The x-momentum equation for fully developed flow reduces to: 0 = mu * d²u/dy², since there is no pressure gradient in the flow direction (dp/dx = 0) and no convective acceleration.

The y-momentum equation reduces to: 0 = -dp/dy, since v = 0 everywhere and there are no viscous terms in the y-direction for this simple shear flow.

Solving the x-momentum equation with boundary conditions u(0) = 0 and u(h) = U gives the linear velocity profile: u(y) = U*y/h.

From the y-momentum equation, dp/dy = 0, which means pressure does not vary with y across the gap.

The pressure is constant across the gap for simple Couette flow with no pressure gradient in the flow direction and neglecting gravity.

The velocity profile is u(y) = (2.0 m/s) * (y / 0.005 m) = 400*y (where y is in meters), giving u = 0 at y = 0 and u = 2.0 m/s at y = 5 mm.

The shear stress is tau = mu * du/dy = 0.10 Pa·s * (2.0 m/s / 0.005 m) = 40 Pa, constant throughout the gap.
