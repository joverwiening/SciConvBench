Calculate the critical depth for a rectangular open channel using the discharge per unit width q = 1.0 m²/s.
Use the formula for critical depth in a rectangular channel: yc = (q²/g)^(1/3).
Substitute q = 1.0 m²/s and g = 9.81 m/s².
Compute yc = (1.0²/9.81)^(1/3) = (0.1019368)^(1/3).
Report the critical depth as approximately 0.467 m.
