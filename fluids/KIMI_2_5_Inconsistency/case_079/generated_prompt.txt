The refrigerator operates between a hot reservoir temperature of 300 K and a cold reservoir temperature of 250 K.
The actual coefficient of performance is given as 20.
The cooling load is 5 kW.
The required work input shall be computed using the definition of COP for a refrigerator: COP = QL / W, where QL is the cooling effect and W is the work input.
Rearranging gives W = QL / COP.
Substituting the values: W = 5 kW / 20 = 0.25 kW.
The required work input is 0.25 kW or 250 W.
