The Dittus-Boelter correlation for turbulent internal pipe flow under heating conditions is Nu = 0.023 * Re^0.8 * Pr^0.4.

Assume a Reynolds number of Re = 10,000 based on typical turbulent flow conditions in heat exchanger applications.

Assume a characteristic diameter of D = 0.05 m (50 mm) as a standard pipe size for industrial applications.

The flow geometry is internal pipe flow.

The heating condition is heating the fluid with wall temperature greater than fluid temperature.

Substitute the values: Nu = 0.023 * (10000)^0.8 * (7.0)^0.4.

Calculate Re^0.8: 10000^0.8 = 1584.89.

Calculate Pr^0.4: 7.0^0.4 = 2.1779.

Calculate Nu: 0.023 * 1584.89 * 2.1779 = 79.4.

The Nusselt number is approximately Nu = 79.
