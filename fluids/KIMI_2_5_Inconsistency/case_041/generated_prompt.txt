The pipe has a length of 30 m and a diameter of 0.1 m. The Fanning friction factor is 0.005. The Darcy-Weisbach equation Δp = f (L/D) (ρ v²/2) is used to compute pressure drop. The fluid density is assumed to be 1000 kg/m³, corresponding to water at standard conditions. The fluid velocity is assumed to be 1 m/s, representing a typical moderate flow velocity in industrial piping systems. The pressure drop calculation proceeds as follows: L/D = 30/0.1 = 300, v²/2 = 1²/2 = 0.5 m²/s², ρ v²/2 = 1000 × 0.5 = 500 Pa, Δp = 0.005 × 300 × 500 = 750 Pa. The computed pressure drop is 750 Pa or 0.75 kPa.
