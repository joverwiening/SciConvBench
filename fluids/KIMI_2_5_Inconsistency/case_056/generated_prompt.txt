The Rayleigh number shall be computed for natural convection from a vertical plate with the following specifications:

The surface temperature Ts is 80°C.
The ambient temperature T infinity is 20°C.
The characteristic length L is 0.5 m.
The film temperature T_f is calculated as (Ts + T infinity)/2 = (80 + 20)/2 = 50°C.
The volumetric thermal expansion coefficient beta is evaluated as 1/T_f where T_f is in Kelvin, so beta = 1/(50 + 273.15) = 1/323.15 K^-1 = 0.003095 K^-1.
The temperature difference delta T is Ts - T infinity = 80 - 20 = 60 K.
The gravitational acceleration g is assumed to be 9.81 m/s^2, representing standard Earth gravity at sea level.
The kinematic viscosity nu is assumed to be 1.81 × 10^-5 m^2/s, representing the value for air at approximately 50°C.
The thermal diffusivity alpha is assumed to be 2.57 × 10^-5 m^2/s, representing the value for air at approximately 50°C.
The Rayleigh number is calculated as Ra = g × beta × delta T × L^3 / (nu × alpha).
Substituting values: Ra = (9.81 m/s^2) × (0.003095 K^-1) × (60 K) × (0.5 m)^3 / [(1.81 × 10^-5 m^2/s) × (2.57 × 10^-5 m^2/s)].
The computed Rayleigh number is approximately 1.54 × 10^8.
