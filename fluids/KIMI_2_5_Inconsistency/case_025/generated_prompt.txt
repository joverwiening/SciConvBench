The user requests analysis of nitrogen flow through a microchannel with hydraulic diameter Dh = 50 µm at temperature T = 300 K where the Knudsen number is Kn = 5.

The Knudsen number Kn = 5 places this flow in the transition-to-free-molecular regime (0.1 < Kn < 10 is transition, Kn > 10 is free molecular).

The continuum Navier-Stokes equations with no-slip boundary conditions are physically invalid at Kn = 5 because the mean free path is 5 times larger than the characteristic length scale.

The appropriate approach requires rarefied gas dynamics methods such as Direct Simulation Monte Carlo (DSMC), kinetic theory solutions of the Boltzmann equation, or at minimum slip-corrected hydrodynamic models with velocity slip and temperature jump boundary conditions.

The no-slip Navier-Stokes solution would produce physically meaningless results for this flow condition and should not be used.
