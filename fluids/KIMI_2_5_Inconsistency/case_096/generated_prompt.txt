The simulation computes downstream conditions across a normal shock in air flowing at Mach 2 using ideal-gas normal-shock relations with gamma=1.4.

The upstream temperature of air is assumed to be 288.15 K, which corresponds to standard sea-level conditions in the International Standard Atmosphere.

The simulation calculates the following downstream properties using the normal-shock relations for an ideal gas:
- Downstream Mach number
- Static pressure ratio across the shock
- Static temperature ratio across the shock
- Density ratio across the shock
- Stagnation pressure ratio across the shock

The specific heat ratio gamma is fixed at 1.4 for the entire calculation.

The input parameters are:
- Upstream Mach number: 2.0
- Upstream temperature: 288.15 K
- Specific heat ratio: 1.4

The output includes all normalized ratios and the absolute downstream Mach number.
