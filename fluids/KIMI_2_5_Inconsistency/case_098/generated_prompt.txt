Use thin airfoil theory to compute the lift coefficient of a NACA 4412 airfoil at 20 degrees angle of attack. Assume the lift curve slope remains linear with Cl = 2πα, where α is in radians. Convert 20 degrees to radians: α = 20 × π/180 = 0.3491 rad. Calculate the lift coefficient: Cl = 2π × 0.3491 = 2.193. Note that this result exceeds the typical stall angle of attack for a NACA 4412 airfoil, so the linear assumption is physically unrealistic for this operating condition, but proceed with the calculation as specified.
