The packed bed pressure drop calculation shall use the Ergun equation with the following specifications:

The particle diameter d_p equals 0.005 m, converted from the given 5 mm value.

The bed porosity ε equals 0.40 as specified.

The superficial velocity v of air shall be assumed as 0.5 m/s, representing a typical value for industrial packed bed operations.

The air density ρ shall be assumed as 1.225 kg/m³, corresponding to dry air at standard conditions.

The dynamic viscosity μ of air shall be assumed as 1.81 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s, representing typical room temperature air.

The operating temperature shall be assumed as 20°C (293.15 K), a standard reference condition for engineering calculations.

The operating pressure shall be assumed as 1 atm (101325 Pa), standard atmospheric pressure.

The Ergun equation shall be applied in its standard form: ΔP/L = (150μ(1-ε)²v)/(d_p²ε³) + (1.75ρ(1-ε)v²)/(d_pε³).

All calculations shall use consistent SI units throughout.

The final result shall express pressure drop per unit length in Pa/m.
