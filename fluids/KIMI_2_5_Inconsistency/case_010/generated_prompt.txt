Calculate the convective heat flux using Newton's law of cooling with the given parameters.

Use the convection coefficient h = 25 W/m²·K.

Use the surface temperature Ts = 400 K.

Use the fluid temperature T∞ = 300 K.

Apply the formula q" = h(Ts - T∞).

Substitute the values: q" = 25 × (400 - 300) = 2500 W/m².

Report the convective heat flux as 2500 W/m².
