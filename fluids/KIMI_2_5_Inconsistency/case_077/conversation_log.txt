Q: Isothermal compression means temperature remains constant (T2 = T1), yet the formula you provided is for isentropic compression. Which thermodynamic process do you want me to use for the calculation?
A: Use isentropic compression with T2/T1 = (p2/p1)^((gamma-1)/gamma). The note in parentheses is just clarifying that if compression were truly isothermal, you would use different relations, but that's not what you're calculating here.
