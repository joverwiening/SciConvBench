Calculate the pressure drop for oil flowing through a horizontal pipe using the Darcy-Weisbach equation with the following parameters and assumptions.

The pipe length is 25 m.
The pipe diameter is 80 mm (0.08 m).
The mean flow velocity is 1.5 m/s.
Assume the pipe is commercial steel with roughness ε = 0.045 mm.
Assume the oil is SAE 30 motor oil at 40°C with kinematic viscosity ν = 100 × 10⁻⁶ m²/s.
Assume the oil density is ρ = 875 kg/m³.
Calculate the Reynolds number using Re = VD/ν.
Determine the friction factor using the Colebrook-White equation or an explicit approximation (e.g., Haaland equation) with the calculated Reynolds number and relative roughness ε/D.
Use the Darcy-Weisbach equation ΔP = f(L/D)(ρV²/2) to compute the pressure drop.
Report the calculated Reynolds number, the determined friction factor, and the final pressure drop in Pascals.
