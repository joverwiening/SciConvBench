The Pitot-static tube measures air velocity using the incompressible Bernoulli relation.

The differential pressure measured by the Pitot-static tube is 20 kPa.

The static pressure is 100 kPa.

The air density is calculated from the ideal gas law using static pressure p = 100 kPa and temperature T = 300 K: ρ = p/(RT) = 100,000/(287 × 300) = 1.161 kg/m³.

The specific gas constant for air is R = 287 J/(kg·K).

The incompressible Bernoulli relation for Pitot-static tube velocity measurement is: V = √(2Δp/ρ).

Substitute the values: V = √(2 × 20,000 Pa / 1.161 kg/m³) = √(40,000/1.161) = √34,453 = 185.6 m/s.

The calculated air velocity is 185.6 m/s.
