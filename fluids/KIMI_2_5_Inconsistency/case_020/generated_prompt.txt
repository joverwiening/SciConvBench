The user wants to calculate stagnation pressure to static pressure ratio for subsonic flow at Mach number 0.5 using isentropic flow relations (Saint-Venant formula).

Use the isentropic flow relation for stagnation to static pressure ratio: p0/p = (1 + (γ-1)/2 * M^2)^(γ/(γ-1)).

Assume γ = 1.4 for air as the working fluid.

Substitute M = 0.5 into the formula.

Calculate the pressure ratio p0/p = (1 + 0.2 * 0.25)^3.5 = (1.05)^3.5.

The resulting stagnation to static pressure ratio equals approximately 1.186.

Do not use the Rayleigh-Pitot formula as it is invalid for subsonic flow.

The Rayleigh-Pitot formula applies only to supersonic flow with a normal shock ahead of the Pitot tube.
