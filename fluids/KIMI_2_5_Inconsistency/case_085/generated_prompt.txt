The simulation calculates the required heat transfer area for a counterflow heat exchanger using the log mean temperature difference (LMTD) method with the parallel/counterflow temperature difference definitions.

Use the following temperature assumptions based on typical industrial heat exchanger design: Th,in = 150°C, Th,out = 70°C, Tc,in = 20°C, Tc,out = 60°C.

Calculate ΔT1 as Th,in minus Tc,out, which equals 150°C minus 60°C equals 90°C.

Calculate ΔT2 as Th,out minus Tc,in, which equals 70°C minus 20°C equals 50°C.

Compute the log mean temperature difference using the formula LMTD equals (ΔT1 minus ΔT2) divided by the natural logarithm of (ΔT1 divided by ΔT2).

Assume a heat duty Q of 500 kW based on typical small-to-medium industrial heat exchanger applications.

Assume an overall heat transfer coefficient U of 500 W per square meter per Kelvin, which represents a reasonable value for liquid-to-liquid heat exchangers with moderate fouling.

Calculate the required heat transfer area A using the formula A equals Q divided by (U multiplied by LMTD).

Convert units as necessary to ensure consistency, with Q in Watts, U in W per m² per K, and temperatures in Kelvin or Celsius (since differences are identical).

Output the calculated LMTD value, the assumed parameters, and the final required area in square meters.
