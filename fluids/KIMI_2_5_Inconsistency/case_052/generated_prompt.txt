Solve the transient 1D heat conduction equation ∂T/∂t = α ∂²T/∂x² in a slab of length L = 1 m.

Use thermal diffusivity α = 1×10⁻⁴ m²/s, representative of metallic materials like steel.

Apply initial condition T(x,0) = T₀ = 100°C uniformly throughout the slab at time t = 0.

Apply Dirichlet boundary conditions with specified temperatures: T(0,t) = 0°C and T(L,t) = 0°C for all t > 0, representing sudden cooling of both ends to zero.

The solution domain is x ∈ [0, L] and t ≥ 0.

Solve using separation of variables method, expressing the solution as an infinite series: T(x,t) = Σ[n=1 to ∞] Bₙ sin(nπx/L) exp(-α(nπ/L)²t).

For the specified values, Bₙ = (200/nπ) for odd n and Bₙ = 0 for even n.

The final analytical solution is T(x,t) = Σ[n=1,3,5...] (400/nπ) sin(nπx) exp(-n²π² × 10⁻⁴ t).

Provide the solution in both symbolic form and as a computational implementation that evaluates the series to a specified convergence tolerance.
