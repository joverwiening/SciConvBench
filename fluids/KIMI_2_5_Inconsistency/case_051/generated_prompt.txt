Calculate the convective heat transfer coefficient h for water flowing in a pipe using the following specifications:

Use the laminar flow Nusselt number correlation Nu = 3.66 for constant wall temperature with fully developed laminar flow.

Assume the thermal conductivity of water k = 0.6 W/m·K, which is a typical value for water at moderate temperatures.

Assume the pipe diameter D = 0.025 m (1 inch nominal), which is a common pipe size for laboratory and industrial applications.

Assume fluid properties are evaluated at the bulk mean fluid temperature of 40°C, which is a standard reference temperature for water property estimation in heat transfer calculations.

Calculate h using the definition h = Nu × k / D.

Substitute the values: h = 3.66 × 0.6 W/m·K / 0.025 m.

Report the final value of h in W/m²·K with appropriate significant figures.
