Solve the steady 1D heat conduction in a slab of thickness L = 1 m.

The governing equation is d2T/dx2 = 0, derived from the heat equation by setting the time derivative to zero for steady-state conditions.

Apply Dirichlet boundary conditions at the two ends of the slab: set T(0) = 100°C at the left boundary and T(L) = 0°C at the right boundary.

The thermal diffusivity alpha is not required for the steady-state solution since it cancels out when dT/dt = 0.

The analytical solution is a linear temperature profile: T(x) = T_hot + (T_cold - T_hot) * (x / L), which simplifies to T(x) = 100 - 100x for the given values.

Verify the solution satisfies both boundary conditions and the governing equation.
