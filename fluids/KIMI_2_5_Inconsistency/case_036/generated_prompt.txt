Use the momentum equation for water hammer analysis: Δp = ρ × a × ΔV.

Assume water density ρ = 1000 kg/m³ as specified.

Assume a typical wave speed a = 1000 m/s for water in a steel pipeline, which is a standard engineering approximation for water hammer calculations.

The initial velocity V_initial = 2 m/s and final velocity V_final = 0 m/s, giving ΔV = 2 m/s.

Calculate the pressure rise: Δp = 1000 kg/m³ × 1000 m/s × 2 m/s = 2,000,000 Pa = 2 MPa.

The valve closure time of 0.05 s is noted but not used directly in this simplified momentum approach, which assumes instantaneous or rapid closure relative to the pressure wave propagation time.
