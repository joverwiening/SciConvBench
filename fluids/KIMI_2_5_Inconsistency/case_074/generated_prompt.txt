The simulation calculates velocity change when air is compressed from 1 bar to 10 bar in a duct using compressible-flow mass continuity.

Assume a constant-area duct where A₁ = A₂ = A.

Assume isothermal compression at T = 300 K, giving density ratio ρ₂/ρ₁ = P₂/P₁ = 10.

Apply mass continuity: ρ₁AV₁ = ρ₂AV₂.

Simplify to: ρ₁V₁ = ρ₂V₂.

Solve for outlet velocity: V₂ = V₁ × (ρ₁/ρ₂) = V₁/10.

Assume a typical inlet velocity of V₁ = 50 m/s based on standard duct flow conditions.

Calculate outlet velocity: V₂ = 50/10 = 5 m/s.

The velocity decreases by a factor of 10 due to the 10× density increase in constant-area isothermal compression.
