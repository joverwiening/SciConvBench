The fin heat transfer shall be computed using the convective-tip fin formula with the following parameters and assumptions.

The thermal conductivity of the fin material is 200 W/m·K.

The fin length L is 30 mm (0.030 m).

The fin thickness t is 2 mm (0.002 m).

The fin width w is 20 mm (0.020 m).

The convective heat transfer coefficient h on the lateral surfaces is 25 W/m²·K.

The base temperature Tb is assumed to be 100°C (373.15 K) based on typical heat exchanger fin applications.

The ambient fluid temperature T∞ is assumed to be 20°C (293.15 K) based on typical room temperature or cooling fluid conditions.

The convective heat transfer coefficient h_tip acting on the tip surface area is assumed to be equal to h, which is 25 W/m²·K, based on the best practice that the same fluid surrounds all fin surfaces.

The fin is straight and rectangular in cross-section.

The fin tip is not adiabatic for the calculation; instead, the convective-tip formula shall be used which accounts for heat transfer from the tip surface area.

The corrected fin length Lc shall be used in the convective-tip formula, where Lc = L + t/2 for a rectangular fin with convection from the tip.

The fin parameter m shall be calculated as sqrt(2h/kt) for a rectangular fin, using the lateral h value.

The fin heat transfer q_f shall be computed using the formula q_f = sqrt(hPkA_c) * (Tb - T∞) * tanh(mLc), where P is the fin perimeter (2w + 2t), A_c is the cross-sectional area (w * t), and the hyperbolic tangent accounts for the convective tip condition through the corrected length Lc.

All calculations shall use consistent SI units (meters, Kelvin, Watts).
