The sphere has a diameter of 5 mm.

The sphere falls at a velocity of 0.5 m/s.

The Reynolds number is large (inertial regime).

Use Stokes drag formula Fd = 3 * pi * mu * D * V to compute drag force.

Assume dynamic viscosity of water mu = 0.001 Pa·s (1 centipoise), which is the standard value for water at approximately 20°C.

Calculate drag force: Fd = 3 * pi * (0.001 Pa·s) * (0.005 m) * (0.5 m/s) = 2.356 × 10^-5 N.

Note: The Stokes drag formula is technically valid only for low Reynolds number (creeping flow), but the calculation proceeds as requested using the specified formula despite the large Re condition.
