Compute capillary rise in a glass tube for mercury using contact angle theta = 0 degrees and surface tension sigma = 0.48 N/m.
Use a tube diameter of 1 mm as a standard capillary tube size for laboratory measurements.
Use a mercury density of 13500 kg/m³ at room temperature (20°C).
Apply the capillary rise formula h = (4 * sigma * cos(theta)) / (rho * g * d) where g = 9.81 m/s².
Calculate the capillary rise value and report it in millimeters.
Note that for mercury with theta = 0, the rise will be positive (capillary depression does not occur at this contact angle).
