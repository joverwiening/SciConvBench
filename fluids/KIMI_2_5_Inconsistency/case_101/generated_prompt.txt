The momentum flux for fully developed laminar flow in a circular pipe shall be computed using the following specifications:

The momentum correction factor beta is set to 1 as specified.

The velocity profile is assumed to be parabolic, characteristic of fully developed laminar flow in a circular pipe, expressed as u(r) = Umax * (1 - r²/R²), where Umax is the centerline velocity, r is the radial coordinate, and R is the pipe radius.

The relationship between centerline velocity and average velocity is Umax = 2V, where V is the average velocity.

The pipe radius R is assumed to be 0.05 m (50 mm), a common representative value for small-scale pipe flow analysis.

The average velocity V is assumed to be 1.0 m/s, a moderate value typical for laminar flow conditions where Reynolds number remains below 2300.

The momentum flux is computed as the integral of rho * u² * dA across the pipe cross-section, which for beta = 1 simplifies to rho * V² * A, where A = pi * R² is the cross-sectional area.

The fluid density rho is assumed to be 1000 kg/m³, corresponding to water at standard conditions.

The final momentum flux value is reported in units of kg·m/s² or N.
