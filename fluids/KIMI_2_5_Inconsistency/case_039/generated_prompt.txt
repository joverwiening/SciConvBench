The Reynolds number is Re = 800, which indicates laminar flow.
For laminar flow (Re < 2300), the Darcy friction factor is given by the Hagen-Poiseuille equation: f = 64/Re.
Substituting Re = 800 yields f = 64/800 = 0.08.
