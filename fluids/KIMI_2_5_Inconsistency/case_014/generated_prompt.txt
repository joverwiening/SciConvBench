The flow is Creeping (Stokes) flow with Reynolds number Re = 0.1.

Neglect the convective acceleration terms in the Navier-Stokes equations.

Solve using the Stokes equations where viscous forces dominate and inertial terms are negligible.

The pressure gradient balances the viscous diffusion term.

The governing equation reduces to: ∇p = μ∇²u.
