The stagnation-point pressure coefficient is defined as Cp = (p_stagnation - p_infinity) / (0.5 * rho * V_infinity^2).

Apply Bernoulli's equation between a point far upstream (infinity) and the stagnation point on the body surface.

At the stagnation point, the fluid velocity is zero (V_stagnation = 0).

Bernoulli's equation states: p_infinity + 0.5 * rho * V_infinity^2 = p_stagnation + 0.5 * rho * V_stagnation^2.

Substitute V_stagnation = 0 into Bernoulli's equation: p_infinity + 0.5 * rho * V_infinity^2 = p_stagnation.

Rearrange to solve for the pressure difference: p_stagnation - p_infinity = 0.5 * rho * V_infinity^2.

Substitute this result into the definition of Cp: Cp = (0.5 * rho * V_infinity^2) / (0.5 * rho * V_infinity^2) = 1.

The theoretical value for incompressible stagnation-point pressure coefficient is Cp = 1, not 1.5.

The value Cp = 1.5 is incorrect for incompressible flow; it may apply to compressible flow at a specific Mach number or represent a different physical quantity.
