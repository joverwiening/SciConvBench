Calculate the mass flow rate of air through a duct by integrating rho * u * dA across the duct cross-section.

Use circular duct geometry with diameter 0.5 m (typical industrial standard).

Assume fully developed turbulent flow with a 1/7th power-law velocity profile: u(r) = U_max * (1 - r/R)^(1/7), where U_max = 1.224 * U_mean based on the ratio of mean to maximum velocity for turbulent pipe flow.

Assume mean velocity U_mean = 20 m/s (typical value for industrial duct applications).

Calculate local density rho using the ideal gas equation of state: rho = P / (R_specific * T).

Use pressure varying linearly from 10 bar (1,000,000 Pa) at inlet to 1 bar (100,000 Pa) at outlet, or use the average pressure of 5.5 bar (550,000 Pa) for a simplified single-section calculation.

Use temperature T = 288.15 K (standard atmospheric conditions).

Use specific gas constant R_specific = 287 J/(kg·K) for dry air.

Perform numerical integration of rho * u * dA across the duct radius from r=0 to r=R, where dA = 2 * pi * r * dr for circular geometry.

Account for the compressibility effect by evaluating density at local pressure conditions, recognizing that density varies from approximately 12.1 kg/m³ at 10 bar to 1.21 kg/m³ at 1 bar (at 288.15 K).
