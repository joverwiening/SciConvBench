Calculate the Darcy friction factor for water flowing in turbulent regime using the Colebrook-White equation.

Assume commercial steel pipe material with absolute roughness ε = 0.045 mm.

Assume nominal pipe diameter D = 100 mm (4 inches), giving relative roughness ε/D = 0.00045.

Assume water temperature of 20°C, confirming turbulent flow with Re = 250,000.

Use the Colebrook-White equation: 1/√f = -2.0 × log10((ε/D)/3.7 + 2.51/(Re × √f)).

Solve iteratively for f, yielding f ≈ 0.0185 for the given conditions.
