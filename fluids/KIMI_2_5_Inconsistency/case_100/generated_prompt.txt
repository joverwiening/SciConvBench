The stagnation-point pressure coefficient is defined as Cp = (p_stagnation - p_infinity) / (0.5 * rho * U_infinity^2).

Apply Bernoulli's equation between a point far upstream (free stream) and the stagnation point on the body surface.

At the free stream: pressure is p_infinity, velocity is U_infinity.

At the stagnation point: pressure is p_stagnation, velocity is zero.

Bernoulli's equation: p_infinity + 0.5 * rho * U_infinity^2 = p_stagnation + 0.

Rearrange: p_stagnation - p_infinity = 0.5 * rho * U_infinity^2.

Substitute into Cp definition: Cp = (0.5 * rho * U_infinity^2) / (0.5 * rho * U_infinity^2) = 1.

The theoretical value for incompressible flow is Cp = 1, not 1.5.

The value Cp = 1.5 is incorrect for incompressible flow. It may correspond to compressible flow at a specific Mach number, or it may be a typographical error.
