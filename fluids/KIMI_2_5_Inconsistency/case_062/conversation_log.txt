Q: What is the upstream depth y1 (or alternatively, the unit discharge q or upstream velocity V1) so that the sequent depth can be calculated?
A: Make a reasonable assumption based on best practices. You can assume y1 = 1.0 m for simplicity, or assume a unit discharge q = 5.0 m²/s if you prefer to work with discharge. Either approach is valid as long as you clearly state your assumption.
