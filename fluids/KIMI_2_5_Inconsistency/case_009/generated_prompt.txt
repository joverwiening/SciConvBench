The flow is steady, inviscid, and irrotational with zero vorticity. Bernoulli's equation with a single constant applies throughout the entire flow field, not just along individual streamlines. The fluid is incompressible with density ρ = 1000 kg/m³. At point A, the pressure is pA = 200 kPa, the velocity is VA = 5 m/s, and the elevation is zA = 0 m. At point B, the velocity is VB = 8 m/s and the elevation is zB = 0 m. Apply Bernoulli's equation pA + ½ρVA² + ρgzA = pB + ½ρVB² + ρgzB to solve for pB. Since zA = zB = 0, the elevation terms cancel. Rearrange to find pB = pA + ½ρ(VA² - VB²). Substitute the values: pB = 200,000 Pa + ½(1000 kg/m³)[(5 m/s)² - (8 m/s)²] = 200,000 Pa + 500 kg/m³ × (25 - 64) m²/s² = 200,000 Pa + 500 × (-39) Pa = 200,000 Pa - 19,500 Pa = 180,500 Pa = 180.5 kPa. The pressure at point B is 180.5 kPa.
