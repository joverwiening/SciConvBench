Calculate the exit velocity of the gas using the isothermal flow assumption with constant enthalpy.  
Assume the gas behaves as an ideal gas and is air with a specific gas constant R = 287 J/(kg·K).  
Assume a temperature of 300 K throughout the expansion.  
Use the inlet pressure of 10 bar and the exit pressure of 1 bar.  
Apply the isothermal energy equation: v_exit = sqrt(2 · R · T · ln(p_in/p_out)).  
Compute the natural logarithm of the pressure ratio (10 bar / 1 bar) and substitute the values to obtain the exit velocity in meters per second.
