Assume a free‑stream velocity V∞ of 10 m s⁻¹, a fluid density ρ of 1.225 kg m⁻³ (air at sea level), and a free‑stream static pressure p∞ of 101 325 Pa.  
Apply the incompressible Bernoulli equation between the free stream and the stagnation point: p₀ = p∞ + ½ ρ V∞².  
Compute the stagnation‑point pressure coefficient: Cp = (p₀ – p∞) / (½ ρ V∞²).  
Substitute the assumed values: Cp = (p∞ + ½ ρ V∞² – p∞) / (½ ρ V∞²) = 1.  
Conclude that, for incompressible flow with the assumed typical conditions, the theoretical stagnation‑point pressure coefficient is Cp = 1, not 1.5; therefore the given Cp = 1.5 cannot be verified using Bernoulli’s equation for incompressible flow.
