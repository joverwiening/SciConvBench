Use an emissivity value of ε = 0.8 (limited to the physical range 0 ≤ ε ≤ 1).  
Assume the surface area A = 1 m².  
Assume the surface temperature Ts = 500 K.  
Assume the surrounding temperature Tsur = 300 K.  
Use the Stefan‑Boltzmann constant σ = 5.670374419 × 10⁻⁸ W·m⁻²·K⁻⁴.  
Compute the radiative heat loss with q = ε σ A (Ts⁴ − Tsur⁴).  
The calculated heat loss is q ≈ 2.47 × 10³ W (approximately 2.5 kW).
