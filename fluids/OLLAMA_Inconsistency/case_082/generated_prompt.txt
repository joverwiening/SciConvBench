Assume the fluid is water at 20 °C with a density of 998 kg/m³.  
Assume a pressure drop Δp across the Venturi meter of 10 kPa (10 000 Pa).  
Assume the inlet pipe diameter is 0.10 m, giving an inlet cross‑sectional area A₁ = 7.85 × 10⁻³ m².  
Assume the throat diameter is 0.05 m, giving a throat cross‑sectional area A₂ = 1.96 × 10⁻³ m².  
Use the ideal Bernoulli equation with a discharge coefficient Cd = 1 (no losses).  

Compute the volumetric flow rate Q from the pressure drop using  
Q = √[ 2 Δp / (ρ (1/A₂² − 1/A₁²)) ]  

Substituting the assumed values:  
Q = √[ 2 × 10 000 Pa / (998 kg/m³ × (1/(1.96 × 10⁻³)² − 1/(7.85 × 10⁻³)²)) ]  
Q ≈ 9.1 × 10⁻³ m³/s, i.e., about 9.1 L/s.  

Report the flow rate as 0.009 m³/s (≈9 L/s) for the assumed conditions.
