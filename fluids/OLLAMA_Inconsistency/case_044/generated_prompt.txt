Assume a steady viscous Couette flow of the oil between two infinite parallel plates separated by a gap of 5 mm (0.005 m).  
Assume the upper plate moves at a constant speed of 2 m s⁻¹ while the lower plate is stationary.  
The velocity profile is linear, so the velocity gradient du/dy is constant and equal to the plate speed divided by the gap: du/dy = 2 m s⁻¹ / 0.005 m = 400 s⁻¹.  
The dynamic viscosity of the oil is μ = 0.1 Pa·s.  
Compute the wall shear stress using τ = μ · du/dy: τ = 0.1 Pa·s × 400 s⁻¹ = 40 Pa.  
Report the wall shear stress as 40 Pa acting on each plate.
