Use the definition of coefficient of performance for a refrigerator: COP = cooling capacity (Qc) divided by required work input (W). Rearrange to find work: W = Qc / COP. Substitute the given values: Qc = 5 kW and COP = 20. Compute W = 5 kW / 20 = 0.25 kW. The refrigerator requires 0.25 kW of work to provide 5 kW of cooling.
