Q: Assume a base‑to‑ambient temperature difference ΔT = 10 K, which is a typical moderate temperature rise used in fin heat‑transfer calculations.  

Compute the fin parameters:  

Cross‑sectional area A_c = t·w = 0.002 m × 0.02 m = 4 × 10⁻⁵ m².  

Perimeter P = 2(t + w) = 2(0.002 + 0.02) = 0.044 m.  

m = √(h·P / (k·A_c)) = √(25 · 0.044 / (200 · 4 × 10⁻⁵)) ≈ 11.73 m⁻¹.  

M = √(h·P·k·A_c) = √(25 · 0.044 · 200 · 4 × 10⁻⁵) ≈ 0.0939 W/K.  

Tip convection parameter h/(m·k) = 25 / (11.73 · 200) ≈ 0.01065.  

m·L = 11.73 · 0.03 ≈ 0.3519.  

sinh(mL) ≈ 0.359, cosh(mL) ≈ 1.062.  

Fin heat‑transfer with convective tip:  

Q = M·ΔT·[sinh(mL) + (h/(m·k))·cosh(mL)] / [cosh(mL) + (h/(m·k))·sinh(mL)]  

 ≈ 0.0939 · 10 · (0.359 + 0.01065·1.062) / (1.062 + 0.01065·0.359)  

 ≈ 0.0939 · 10 · 0.3703 / 1.0658  

 ≈ 0.326 W.  

Therefore, using the assumed ΔT = 10 K, the fin transfers approximately 0.33 W of heat to the surrounding fluid.
A: **Fin data (from the complete requirement)**  

- Thermal conductivity: \(k = 200\;{\rm W/(m·K)}\)  
- Length: \(L = 30\;{\rm mm}=0.03\;{\rm m}\)  
- Thickness: \(t = 2\;{\rm mm}=0.002\;{\rm m}\)  
- Width: \(w = 20\;{\rm mm}=0.02\;{\rm m}\)  
- Convective coefficient on the sides: \(h = 25\;{\rm W/(m^{2}·K)}\)  
- Base‑to‑ambient temperature difference (assumed, since not given): \(\Delta T = 10\;{\rm K}\)

**Geometric properties**

\[
A_c = t\,w = 0.002 \times 0.02 = 4.0\times10^{-5}\;{\rm m^{2}}
\]
\[
P = 2(t+w) = 2(0.002+0.02) = 0.044\;{\rm m}
\]

**Fin parameter \(m\) and \(M\)**  

\[
m = \sqrt{\frac{hP}{kA_c}}
   = \sqrt{\frac{25 \times 0.044}{200 \times 4.0\times10^{-5}}}
   \approx 11.73\;{\rm m^{-1}}
\]

\[
M = \sqrt{hPkA_c}
   = \sqrt{25 \times 0.044 \times 200 \times 4.0\times10^{-5}}
   \approx 0.0939\;{\rm W/K}
\]

**Adiabatic‑tip solution**

For an adiabatic tip (\(dT/dx=0\) at \(x=L\)) the heat transfer is

\[
Q = M\,\Delta T \,\tanh(mL)
\]

\[
mL = 11.73 \times 0.03 \approx 0.3519
\]

\[
\tanh(mL)=\frac{\sinh(mL)}{\cosh(mL)}
        =\frac{0.359}{1.062}\approx 0.338
\]

\[
\boxed{Q = 0.0939 \times 10 \times 0.338 \;\approx\; 0.32\ {\rm W}}
\]

**Fin efficiency (optional)**  

\[
\eta = \frac{\tanh(mL)}{mL}
      = \frac{0.338}{0.3519}
      \approx 0.96\;(96\%)
\]

Thus, using the adiabatic‑tip fin solution required by the complete requirement, the fin transfers about **0.32 W** (≈96 % efficient) for a 10 K base‑to‑ambient temperature difference.
