Use Bernoulli’s equation between the reservoir surface (point 1) and the discharge outlet (point 2) including the pump head.  
Assume atmospheric pressure at both points, negligible velocity at the reservoir surface, and a frictionless pipe.  

Apply: P1/γ + z1 + v1²/(2g) + h_pump = P2/γ + z2 + v2²/(2g).  

Because P1 = P2 (atmospheric) and v1 ≈ 0, the equation reduces to: h_pump + z1 = z2 + v2²/(2g).  

Substitute the given values: h_pump = 20 m, z1 = 0 m, z2 = 5 m, g = 9.81 m/s².  

Solve for v2: v2² = 2 g (h_pump + z1 – z2) = 2 · 9.81 · (20 – 5) = 294.3 m²/s².  

Thus v2 = √294.3 ≈ 17.2 m/s.  

The exit velocity at the discharge outlet is approximately 17 m/s.
