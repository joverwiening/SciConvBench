Use Bernoulli’s equation for incompressible, steady flow along a streamline from the undisturbed free stream to the stagnation point on the body.  
Assume the free‑stream static pressure is \(p_{\infty}\) and the velocity is \(U_{\infty}\).  
At the stagnation point the velocity is zero, so the stagnation pressure is \(p_{stag}=p_{\infty}+ \tfrac12 \rho U_{\infty}^{2}\).  
Define the pressure coefficient as \(C_{p}= \dfrac{p_{stag}-p_{\infty}}{\tfrac12 \rho U_{\infty}^{2}}\).  
Substitute the expression for \(p_{stag}\) to obtain \(C_{p}= \dfrac{\tfrac12 \rho U_{\infty}^{2}}{\tfrac12 \rho U_{\infty}^{2}} = 1\).  
State that for incompressible flow the theoretical maximum stagnation‑point pressure coefficient is 1, so a value of \(C_{p}=1.5\) cannot be obtained under the given assumptions.  
Conclude that the provided \(C_{p}=1.5\) is inconsistent with incompressible Bernoulli theory.
