Assume the fluid is water at 20 °C with a density of 998 kg/m³.  
Assume a typical water velocity in the pipe of 1 m/s.  
Use the Darcy–Weisbach equation Δp = f (L/D) (ρ v²/2) with f = 0.005, L = 30 m, D = 0.1 m, ρ = 998 kg/m³, and v = 1 m/s.  
Calculate the term (L/D) = 30 m / 0.1 m = 300.  
Calculate the kinetic term (ρ v²/2) = 998 kg/m³ × (1 m/s)² / 2 = 499 Pa.  
Compute the pressure drop Δp = 0.005 × 300 × 499 Pa = 748.5 Pa.  
Report the pressure drop as approximately 750 Pa.
