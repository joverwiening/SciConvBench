Use a fluid density of 1000 kg/m³ for the water.  
Assume the pipeline is horizontal so elevation changes are negligible.  
Apply the steady‑flow Bernoulli equation between the upstream point (velocity 2 m/s) and the downstream point (velocity 0 m/s) when the valve is closed:  
P₁ + ½ ρ v₁² = P₂ + ½ ρ v₂².  
Since v₂ = 0, the pressure rise ΔP = P₂ – P₁ = ½ ρ v₁².  
Calculate ΔP = 0.5 × 1000 kg/m³ × (2 m/s)² = 2000 Pa.  
Report the pressure rise as 2000 Pa (or 2 kPa).
