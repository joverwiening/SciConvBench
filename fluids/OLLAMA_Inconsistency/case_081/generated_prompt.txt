Use a water density of 998 kg/m³ at 20 °C.  
Assume a fluid velocity V of 1 m/s, which is a typical value for water flow in moderate‑sized pipes.  
Assume a characteristic pipe diameter D of 0.05 m (5 cm), a common pipe size for many engineering applications.  
Use a dynamic viscosity μ of 1.0 × 10⁻³ m²/s as given.  
Compute the Reynolds number with Re = (ρ · V · D) / μ.  
The calculated Reynolds number is Re = (998 kg/m³ · 1 m/s · 0.05 m) / (1.0 × 10⁻³ m²/s) = 4.99 × 10⁴ (approximately 5.0 × 10⁴).
