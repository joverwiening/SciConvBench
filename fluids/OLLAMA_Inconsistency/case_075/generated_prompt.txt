Use the ideal‑gas law ρ = P M / (R T) with pressure P = 101325 Pa, temperature T = 293.15 K (20 °C), molar mass M = 0.018015 kg mol⁻¹ for water, and the universal gas constant R = 8.314 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹ to calculate the density of liquid water.  
Assume the water is initially at rest (velocity v₁ ≈ 0) and experiences a pressure drop ΔP of 10 000 Pa as it flows.  
Apply the Bernoulli equation P₁ + ½ ρ v₁² = P₂ + ½ ρ v₂², substitute v₁ = 0 and ΔP = P₁ – P₂ = 10 000 Pa, and solve for the exit velocity v₂ = √(2 ΔP / ρ).  
Report the computed density and the resulting velocity v₂.
