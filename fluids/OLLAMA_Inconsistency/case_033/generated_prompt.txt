Assume the air behaves as an ideal gas with specific heat ratio γ = 1.4 and gas constant R = 287 J/(kg·K).  
Assume the stagnation (tank) temperature T₀ = 300 K.  
Assume the nozzle throat cross‑sectional area Aₜ = 0.01 m² and a discharge coefficient C_d = 1 (ideal).  
Because the upstream to downstream pressure ratio (500 kPa / 100 kPa = 5) exceeds the critical pressure ratio for air, the flow is choked and the mass flow rate depends only on upstream conditions and throat area.  
Calculate the mass flow rate using the choked flow equation:  

ṁ = C_d · Aₜ · (P₀ / √T₀) · √(γ / R) · (2/(γ+1))^((γ+1)/(2(γ‑1)))  

where P₀ = 500,000 Pa.  

Substituting the assumed values gives a mass flow rate of approximately 11.7 kg/s.
