Assume the Young's modulus of the steel rod is 200 GPa.  
Assume the coefficient of thermal expansion of the steel rod is 12 × 10⁻⁶ K⁻¹.  
Use the temperature increase ΔT = 50 K.  
Compute the thermal stress for fully constrained conditions using σ = E α ΔT.  
Calculate σ = 200 GPa × 12 × 10⁻⁶ K⁻¹ × 50 K = 120 MPa.  
Report the thermal stress as 120 MPa.
