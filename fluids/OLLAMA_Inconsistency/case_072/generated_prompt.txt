Use the Ergun equation ΔP/L = (150 (1‑ε)² μ U)/(ε³ d_p²) + (1.75 (1‑ε) ρ U²)/(ε³ d_p) to compute the pressure drop per unit length of the packed bed.  
Set the bed porosity ε to 0.4.  
Set the particle diameter d_p to 5 mm (0.005 m).  
Assume the flowing fluid is air at 20 °C with density ρ ≈ 1.204 kg/m³ and viscosity μ ≈ 1.81×10⁻⁵ Pa·s.  
Assume a reasonable superficial fluid velocity U of 0.5 m/s.  
Calculate the first term: (150 (1‑0.4)² μ U)/(0.4³ d_p²) ≈ 3.06×10² Pa/m.  
Calculate the second term: (1.75 (1‑0.4) ρ U²)/(0.4³ d_p) ≈ 9.88×10² Pa/m.  
Add the two contributions to obtain the total pressure drop per meter ΔP/L ≈ 1.29×10³ Pa/m (≈ 1.3 kPa per meter of bed).
