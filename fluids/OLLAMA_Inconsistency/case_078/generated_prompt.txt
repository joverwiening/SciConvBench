Use a compression ratio r = 9 for the Otto cycle.  
Assume the working fluid is air with a specific heat ratio γ = 1.4.  
Apply the efficiency formula η = 1 – 1 / r^(γ‑1).  
Calculate r^(γ‑1) = 9^(0.4) ≈ 2.408.  
Compute 1 / r^(γ‑1) ≈ 0.415.  
Subtract from 1 to obtain η ≈ 0.585, or 58.5 % efficiency.
