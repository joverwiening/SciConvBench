Use the incompressible Bernoulli relation q = ½ ρ V², where q is the measured differential pressure.  
Set q = 20 kPa = 20 000 Pa and ρ = 1.16 kg/m³ (computed from the given static pressure, temperature, and gas constant).  
Solve for V: V = √(2 q / ρ) = √(2 × 20 000 Pa / 1.16 kg/m³) ≈ 1.86 × 10² m/s.  
The air velocity measured by the Pitot‑static tube is approximately 186 m/s.
