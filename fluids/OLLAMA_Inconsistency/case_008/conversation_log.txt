Q: What is the air density (or temperature and composition) to be used for the calculation?
A: Use the given air properties: T = 300 K, γ = 1.4, R = 287 J/(kg·K).  
With the static pressure p = 100 kPa, the density is  

\[
\rho = \frac{p}{R\,T}= \frac{100\,000\ \text{Pa}}{287\ \text{J/(kg·K)}\times300\ \text{K}}
      \approx 1.16\ \text{kg/m}^3.
\]
