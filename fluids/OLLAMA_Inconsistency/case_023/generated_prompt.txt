Calculate the terminal velocity of a spherical raindrop with diameter D = 2 mm by balancing its weight against the aerodynamic drag force. Use the following parameters: drag coefficient C_d = 0.5, air density ρ_air = 1.2 kg/m³, water density ρ_water = 1000 kg/m³, and gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s². Compute the cross‑sectional area A = π (D/2)². Set the weight (mass × g) equal to the drag force (½ C_d ρ_air A v²) and solve for the terminal velocity v. Provide the numerical value of v in meters per second.
