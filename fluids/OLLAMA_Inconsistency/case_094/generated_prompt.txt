Compute the capillary rise in a glass tube using the formula h = (2 σ cosθ) / (ρ g r).  
Assume the inner radius of the tube r = 0.0005 m (0.5 mm), which is a common value for laboratory glass capillaries.  
Assume the contact angle θ between water and clean glass is 0°, so cosθ = 1.  
Use the surface tension of water σ = 0.072 N/m (positive magnitude).  
Use the density of water ρ = 1000 kg/m³.  
Use the standard acceleration due to gravity g = 9.81 m/s².  
Calculate h and report the result in meters (or convert to centimeters for readability).
