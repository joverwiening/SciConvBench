Use the inlet diameter D1 = 0.050 m and outlet diameter D2 = 0.100 m to calculate cross‑sectional areas A1 = π D1²/4 and A2 = π D2²/4.  
Compute the average velocities v1 = Q / A1 and v2 = Q / A2 using the flow rate Q = 0.010 m³ s⁻¹.  
Apply Bernoulli’s equation between the inlet and outlet, assuming negligible elevation change, to find the pressure rise Δp = ½ ρ (v1² – v2²) with water density ρ = 1000 kg m⁻³.  
The resulting pressure recovery is Δp ≈ 1.22 × 10⁴ Pa (about 12.2 kPa).
