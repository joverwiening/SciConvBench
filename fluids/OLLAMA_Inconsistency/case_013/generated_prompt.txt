Assume the static temperature of the water jet is 293 K (20 °C).  
Assume the water jet behaves as an ideal gas with a specific heat at constant pressure cp = 1.86 kJ·kg⁻¹·K⁻¹ (≈1860 J·kg⁻¹·K⁻¹).  
Use the stagnation temperature formula for an ideal gas:  
T₀ = T + V² / (2 cp)  
where T is the static temperature (293 K) and V is the jet velocity (20 m/s).  
Calculate V² / (2 cp):  
V² = (20 m/s)² = 400 m²/s²  
2 cp = 2 × 1860 J·kg⁻¹·K⁻¹ = 3720 J·kg⁻¹·K⁻¹  
V² / (2 cp) = 400 / 3720 ≈ 0.1075 K  

Add this increment to the static temperature:  
T₀ = 293 K + 0.1075 K ≈ 293.11 K.  

Report the stagnation temperature as approximately 293.1 K.
