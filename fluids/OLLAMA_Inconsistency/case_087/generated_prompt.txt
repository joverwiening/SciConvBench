Convert the flow rate from liters per second to cubic meters per second: Q = 50 L/s = 0.05 m³/s.  
Apply the hydraulic power formula: P = ρ · g · Q · H, where g = 9.81 m/s², H = 30 m, Q = 0.05 m³/s, and ρ = 1000 kg/m³.
