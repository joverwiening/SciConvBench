Assume the pipe has an inner diameter of 0.05 m (2 inches).  
Assume the thermal conductivity of water at the operating temperature is 0.6 W m⁻¹ K⁻¹.  
Use the laminar‑flow Nusselt number correlation for constant‑wall‑temperature flow: Nu = 3.66.  
Calculate the convective heat‑transfer coefficient as h = Nu · k / D.  
With Nu = 3.66, k = 0.6 W m⁻¹ K⁻¹, and D = 0.05 m, compute h = 3.66 × 0.6 / 0.05 ≈ 43.9 W m⁻² K⁻¹.
