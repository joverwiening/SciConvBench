Q: Should the condenser operate at 10 kPa with its corresponding saturation temperature (≈45.8 °C), or at 100 °C with the pressure that matches that saturation temperature?
A: The condenser should operate at 10 kPa, using its corresponding saturation temperature of about 45.8 °C. If you need a saturation temperature of 100 °C, you must change the condenser pressure to roughly 101 kPa.
