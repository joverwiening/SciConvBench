Calculate the characteristic length for a sphere as Lc = R/3, where R = 0.01 m.  
Compute the Biot number: Bi = h·Lc / k = (80 W/(m²·K) × 0.01 m / 3) / 45 W/(m·K) ≈ 0.0059.  
Since Bi < 0.1, temperature gradients inside the steel sphere are negligible, so the sphere can be treated as having a uniform temperature.  
Therefore, the fluid temperature can be assumed uniform at the sphere surface, and the lumped capacitance method is valid for analyzing the cooling process.
