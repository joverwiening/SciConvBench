Determine the exit Mach number using the isentropic area‑Mach relation for a frictionless converging nozzle:  

\( \displaystyle \frac{A}{A^*}= \frac{1}{M}\left[\frac{2}{\gamma+1}\left(1+\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^{2}\right)\right]^{\frac{\gamma+1}{2(\gamma-1)}} \)  

with γ = 1.4.  

1. Compute \(A_1/A^*\) for the inlet Mach M₁ = 2.0.  
2. Use the given area ratio \(A_2/A_1 = 0.7\) to find \(A_2/A^* = 0.7\,(A_1/A^*)\).  
3. Solve the relation for M₂ that satisfies the calculated \(A_2/A^*\). The solution is M₂ ≈ 0.606 (subsonic).  

Next, compute the velocity ratio using \(V = M\sqrt{\gamma R T}\) and the isentropic temperature relation \(T/T_0 = \bigl[1+(\gamma-1)M^{2}/2\bigr]^{-1}\):  

\( \displaystyle \frac{V_2}{V_1}= \frac{M_2}{M_1}\sqrt{\frac{1+0.2M_1^{2}}{1+0.2M_2^{2}}} \)  

Substituting M₁ = 2.0 and M₂ ≈ 0.606 gives \(V_2/V_1 \approx 0.39\).  

Therefore, the exit velocity is about 39 % of the inlet velocity, representing a decrease of roughly 61 % rather than an increase.
