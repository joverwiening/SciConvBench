Solve the steady one‑dimensional heat conduction problem for a plane wall of arbitrary thickness L.  
Apply the temperature boundary condition T(0)=350 K at x=0 (Dirichlet).  
Apply the insulated boundary condition (zero heat flux) dT/dx|_{x=L}=0 at x=L.  
Use the governing equation for steady conduction with no internal heat generation: d²T/dx² = 0.  
Integrate twice to obtain T(x)=C₁ x + C₂.  
From T(0)=350 K, set C₂ = 350 K.  
From dT/dx|_{x=L}=0, set C₁ = 0.  
Thus the temperature distribution is T(x)=350 K for 0 ≤ x ≤ L.  
The heat flux is q = –k dT/dx = 0 throughout the wall.
