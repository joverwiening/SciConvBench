Q: Should the pressure variation be expressed as a function of the distance from the stationary plate (y)?
A: No. For simple Couette flow (neglecting gravity) the pressure does not vary with the wall‑normal coordinate y; the pressure gradient dp/dy is zero, so p is constant across the gap.
