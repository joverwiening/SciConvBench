Assume the oil density is 850 kg·m⁻³.  
Use the given dynamic viscosity μ = 0.10 Pa·s, plate separation h = 5 mm, top‑plate speed U = 2.0 m·s⁻¹, and a stationary bottom plate.  
Apply Bernoulli’s equation between a point on the stationary plate (velocity = 0) and a point on the moving plate (velocity = U), neglecting any elevation change.  
Because Couette flow is viscous and the pressure gradient in the wall‑normal direction is zero, the pressure does not vary with the coordinate y.  
Consequently, the pressure is uniform across the gap; the pressure at the stationary plate equals the pressure at the moving plate, i.e., the pressure variation across the gap is zero.
