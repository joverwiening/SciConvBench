Assume a straight rectangular fin of length L = 0.05 m, thickness t = 0.001 m, and width w = 0.02 m.  
Assume the fin material has thermal conductivity k = 200 W/(m·K) and the surrounding fluid provides a convective heat‑transfer coefficient h = 100 W/(m²·K).  
Compute the fin parameter m using m = √(2 h / (k t)).  
Calculate the fin efficiency η_f with η_f = tanh(m L) / (m L).  
Determine the fin surface area (excluding the tip) as A_f = 2 w L + 2 t L.  
Assume a temperature difference between the fin base and the ambient fluid of ΔT = T_base − T_∞ = 50 K.
