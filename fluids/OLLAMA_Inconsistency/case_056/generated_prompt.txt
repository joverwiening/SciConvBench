Use the surface temperature Ts = 80 °C and ambient temperature T∞ = 20 °C to compute the temperature difference ΔT = Ts − T∞ = 60 K.  
Calculate the film temperature as Tf = (Ts + T∞)/2 = 50 °C = 323 K.  
Determine the thermal expansion coefficient β as β = 1/Tf = 1/323 K⁻¹ ≈ 3.10 × 10⁻³ K⁻¹.  
Assume air at the film temperature with kinematic viscosity ν = 1.6 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s and thermal diffusivity α = 2.2 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s.  
Use the characteristic length L = 0.5 m and gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².  
Compute the Rayleigh number using Ra = g β ΔT L³ / (ν α).  
The resulting Rayleigh number is Ra ≈ 6.5 × 10⁸.
