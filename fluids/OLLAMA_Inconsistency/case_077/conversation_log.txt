Q: Should we use the adiabatic relation \(T_2/T_1 = (p_2/p_1)^{(\gamma-1)/\gamma}\) with \(\gamma = 1.4\) for air?
A: Yes. For an isentropic (adiabatic) compression in a Brayton cycle you should use the relation  

\[
\frac{T_2}{T_1}= \left(\frac{p_2}{p_1}\right)^{(\gamma-1)/\gamma}
\]

with \(\gamma = 1.4\) for air. (If the compression were truly isothermal, then \(T_2 = T_1\) and you would use the isothermal work formulas instead.)
