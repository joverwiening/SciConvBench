Compute the pressure drop for oil flowing in a horizontal pipe using the Darcy–Weisbach equation.

Assume the oil density is 850 kg/m³ (a typical value for crude oil at ambient temperature).

Use the following data:
- Pipe length L = 25 m
- Pipe inner diameter D = 0.080 m
- Mean flow velocity V = 1.5 m/s
- Darcy friction factor f = 0.03

Apply the Darcy–Weisbach formula:
ΔP = f · (L/D) · (ρ V² / 2)

Calculate the term (ρ V² / 2) = (850 kg/m³ · (1.5 m/s)²) / 2 = 956.25 Pa.

Compute the geometric ratio L/D = 25 m / 0.080 m = 312.5.

Finally, ΔP = 0.03 · 312.5 · 956.25 Pa = 8 970 Pa (approximately).

Report the pressure drop as about 9 kPa.
