Assume the fluid is water at 20 °C with density 998 kg/m³ and dynamic viscosity 1.002 × 10⁻³ Pa·s.  
Assume the microchannel is smooth metal with a Manning roughness coefficient n = 0.010.  
Assume the microchannel length L = 0.10 m (10 cm), a typical value for microfluidic test sections.  

Compute the average velocity V from the Reynolds number:  
Re = 200 = ρ V D_h / μ → V = (Re · μ) / (ρ · D_h).  

Use the hydraulic diameter D_h = 2 mm = 0.002 m, so the hydraulic radius R = D_h / 4 = 0.0005 m.  

Apply the Manning equation for velocity:  
V = (1 / n) · R^(2/3) · S^(1/2) → solve for the energy slope S:  
S = [ V · n / R^(2/3) ]².  

Compute the pressure drop ΔP over the length L using the energy slope:  
ΔP = ρ · g · S · L, where g = 9.81 m/s².  

Report the calculated pressure drop ΔP in pascals (Pa).
