Use Thoma’s cavitation factor σ = (Ha − Hv − hs − hloss) / H.  
Assume a typical suction head for a Francis turbine runner installed well above the tailrace: hs = 2.0 m.  
Assume a typical head loss for the runner and associated inlet/outlet passages: hloss = 0.5 m.  
Compute the numerator: Ha − Hv − hs − hloss = 10.3 m − 0.3 m − 2.0 m − 0.5 m = 7.5 m.  
Thus σ = 7.5 / H, where H is the net head available to the turbine (H > 0 by definition).  
Since the numerator (7.5 m) is positive and H is always positive for operation, σ is positive for any feasible head.  
A positive σ indicates that the available pressure at the runner inlet never falls below the vapor pressure, so cavitation cannot occur for any operating head.
