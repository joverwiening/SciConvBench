Use Bernoulli’s equation for steady, incompressible, frictionless flow of water (density = 1000 kg/m³) between the inlet and outlet.  
Assume the pipe has a constant cross‑section so the fluid velocity is the same at inlet and outlet, making the kinetic energy terms cancel.  
Take gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².  
The outlet is 15 m higher than the inlet (Δz = +15 m).  
Apply Bernoulli: P_in/ρg + z_in = P_out/ρg + z_out.  
Rearrange to find the pressure difference: ΔP = P_in – P_out = ρ g Δz.  
Calculate ΔP = 1000 kg/m³ × 9.81 m/s² × 15 m = 147 150 Pa (≈ 147 kPa).  
State that the inlet pressure exceeds the outlet pressure by 147 kPa.
