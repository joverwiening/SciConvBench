Assume the blade speed (U) of the rotor is 180 m/s.  
Use the given axial velocity Va = 106 m/s (constant through the rotor).  
Compute the relative tangential components: W1_u = 150 m/s × sin 45° ≈ 106.1 m/s, W2_u = 100 m/s × sin 30° = 50 m/s.  
Determine the absolute tangential velocities: V1_θ = U – W1_u, V2_θ = U – W2_u.  
Calculate the change in absolute tangential velocity: ΔV_θ = V2_θ – V1_θ = (U – 50) – (U – 106.1) = 56.1 m/s.  
Compute the degree of reaction using R = 0.5 – ΔV_θ / (2U).  
Substituting the numbers: R = 0.5 – 56.1 / (2 × 180) ≈ 0.34.  
Thus, the axial compressor rotor has a degree of reaction of approximately 0.34 (34 %).
