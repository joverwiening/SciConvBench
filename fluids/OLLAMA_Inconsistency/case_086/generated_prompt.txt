Assume the pipe has a circular cross‑section with a diameter of 0.10 m (a common size for moderate flow rates).  
The cross‑sectional area is A = π · (0.10 m / 2)² = 7.85 × 10⁻³ m².  
Because the fluid is incompressible, the volumetric flow rate is constant: Q = 0.01 m³/s at both inlet and outlet.  
The average velocity at any cross‑section is V = Q / A.  
Thus the velocity at the inlet is V_in = 0.01 m³/s ÷ 7.85 × 10⁻³ m² ≈ 1.27 m/s.  
The velocity at the outlet is the same, V_out ≈ 1.27 m/s.
