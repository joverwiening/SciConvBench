Assume the fluid is water at 20 °C with a density of 998 kg/m³ and gravitational acceleration of 9.81 m/s².  
Assume a pipe length of 100 m and an internal diameter of 0.10 m.  
Assume a mean flow velocity of 2.0 m/s in the pipe.  
Use a positive Darcy–Weisbach friction factor f = 0.02.  
Compute the velocity head: V²/(2g) = (2.0 m/s)² / (2 × 9.81 m/s²) = 0.2039 m.  
Compute the length‑to‑diameter ratio: L/D = 100 m / 0.10 m = 1000.  
Calculate the head loss using the Darcy–Weisbach equation: h_f = f × (L/D) × (V²/(2g)) = 0.02 × 1000 × 0.2039 m = 4.08 m.  
Report the head loss as approximately 4.1 m of water.
