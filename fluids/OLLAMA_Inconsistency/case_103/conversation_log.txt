Q: What is the net head (total head) of the turbine that should be used in the calculation?
A: Use the correct vapor‑pressure head for 100 °C, Hv ≈ 10.3 m. With the atmospheric head Ha = 10.3 m, the net (total) head is Ha − Hv ≈ 0 m.

Q: What is the gross (total) head of the turbine before accounting for vapor‑pressure loss?
A: The gross (total) head is the atmospheric head, Ha ≈ 10.3 m.
