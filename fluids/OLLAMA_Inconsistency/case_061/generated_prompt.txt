Compute the constant axial velocity using the inlet values: Vx = W1 · cos β1 = 150 m/s · cos 45° = 106.1 m/s.  
Determine the tangential components of the absolute velocity at inlet and outlet: V1θ = W1 · sin β1 = 150 m/s · sin 45° = 106.1 m/s; V2θ = W2 · sin β2 = 100 m/s · sin 30° = 50.0 m/s.  
The degree of reaction for the rotor is given by R = 0.5 + (V2θ − V1θ) / (2 U), where U is the blade speed. Substituting the computed tangential velocities yields R = 0.5 + (50.0 − 106.1) / (2 U) = 0.5 − 28.0 / U.  
Thus, the reaction can be expressed as R = 0.5 − 28.0 / U, with U to be supplied for a numerical value.
