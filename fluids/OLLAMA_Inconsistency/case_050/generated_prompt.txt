Assume the sphere is made of aluminum with thermal conductivity k = 237 W/(m·K), density ρ = 2700 kg/m³, and specific heat capacity cₚ = 900 J/(kg·K).  
Assume a sphere radius of 0.05 m (5 cm), which gives a characteristic length L = 0.05 m.  
Use the given Biot number Bi = 0.05 to compute the convective heat‑transfer coefficient: h = Bi·k/L = 0.05 × 237 / 0.05 = 237 W/(m²·K).  
Assume the initial uniform temperature of the sphere T₀ = 100 °C and the ambient fluid temperature T∞ = 25 °C.  
Apply the lumped‑capacitance solution for transient cooling:  
T(t) – T∞ = (T₀ – T∞) · exp[ – (h·A)/(ρ·V·cₚ) · t ], where A = 4πr² and V = (4/3)πr³.  
Compute the time constant τ = (ρ·V·cₚ)/(h·A) = (ρ·r·cₚ)/(3h).  
The transient cooling curve is then:  
T(t) = T∞ + (T₀ – T∞) · exp( – t/τ ).  
Provide the expression for τ using the assumed values: τ = (2700 kg/m³ · 0.05 m · 900 J/(kg·K)) / (3 · 237 W/(m²·K)) ≈ 170 s.  
Thus the temperature of the sphere as a function of time is:  
T(t) = 25 °C + 75 °C · exp( – t/170 s ).
