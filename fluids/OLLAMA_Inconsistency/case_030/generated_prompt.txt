Use the deep‑water wave speed formula c = √(g λ / 2π).  
Insert the gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s² and the wavelength λ = 20 m into the formula.  
Compute the numerator: g × λ = 9.81 × 20 = 196.2.  
Divide by 2π: 196.2 / (2π) ≈ 31.235.  
Take the square root of the result: √31.235 ≈ 5.6 m/s.  
Report the deep‑water wave phase speed as approximately 5.6 m/s.
