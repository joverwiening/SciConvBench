Assume the fluid is water with a density of 1000 kg/m³.  
Assume the flow velocity at the inlet and outlet are equal and negligible, so kinetic‑energy terms cancel in Bernoulli’s equation.  
Apply Bernoulli’s equation between the inlet (point 1) and the outlet (point 2) with the same elevation reference:  

P₁/ρg + z₁ = P₂/ρg + z₂  

Since the pipe rises 15 m, z₂ = z₁ + 15 m.  
Rearrange to find the pressure difference:  

P₁ – P₂ = ρ g Δz = (1000 kg/m³)(9.81 m/s²)(15 m) = 147 150 Pa.  

State that the pressure at the outlet is lower than the pressure at the inlet by 147 150 Pa (≈1.47 bar).
