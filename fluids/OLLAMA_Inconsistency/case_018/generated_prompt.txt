Compute the kinetic‑energy correction factor α for a circular pipe of diameter 20 mm carrying water with a uniform (plug) velocity profile and mean velocity V = 1.0 m s⁻¹. Use the definition α = (∫_A u³ dA) / (V̅ ∫_A u² dA). Since u = V̅ everywhere for plug flow, the integrals simplify and α = 1. State the result α = 1.0.
