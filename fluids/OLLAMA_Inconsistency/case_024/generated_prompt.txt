Assume a duct diameter of 0.5 meters.  
Assume a volume flow rate of 1.0 cubic meters per second (m³/s).  
Use a constant air density of 1.225 kilograms per cubic meter (kg/m³) for the calculation, regardless of the pressure drop from 10 bar to 1 bar.  
Calculate the mass flow rate by multiplying the volume flow rate by the density: mass flow rate = 1.0 m³/s × 1.225 kg/m³ = 1.225 kg/s.
