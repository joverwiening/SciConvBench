Assume the fluid is water with a density ρ = 1000 kg/m³.  
Assume the pipe has an inner diameter D = 0.05 m (5 cm).  
Assume a representative average flow velocity V = 1 m/s for the fully developed laminar flow.  
Compute the pipe cross‑sectional area A = π D² / 4 = 0.0019635 m².  
Compute the momentum flux using the momentum correction factor β = 1 as  
Momentum flux = ρ · A · V² · β = 1000 kg/m³ · 0.0019635 m² · (1 m/s)² · 1 = 1.96 N.
