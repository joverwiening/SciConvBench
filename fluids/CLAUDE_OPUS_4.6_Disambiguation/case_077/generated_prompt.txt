Calculate the thermal efficiency of an ideal gas turbine operating on a Brayton cycle.
The working fluid is air with a specific heat ratio (γ) of 1.4.
The pressure ratio of the cycle is 12.
The cycle is an ideal Brayton cycle, meaning isentropic compression and expansion with no pressure losses.
Use the ideal Brayton cycle thermal efficiency formula: η = 1 - (1 / r_p^((γ-1)/γ)), where r_p is the pressure ratio.
