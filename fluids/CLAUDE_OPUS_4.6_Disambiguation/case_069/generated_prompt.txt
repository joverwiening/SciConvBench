Calculate the stagnation (total) temperature on the nose of a rocket under the following conditions.
The free-stream Mach number is 2.5.
The free-stream static temperature is 220 K.
The working fluid is standard air with a ratio of specific heats (γ) equal to 1.4.
Use the isentropic stagnation temperature relation: T0 = T × (1 + (γ−1)/2 × M²).
Report the resulting stagnation temperature in Kelvin.
