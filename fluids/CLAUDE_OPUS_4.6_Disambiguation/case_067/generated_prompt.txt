Calculate the terminal velocity of a spherical raindrop falling through air using a force balance approach.
The raindrop is a perfect sphere with a diameter of 2 mm (radius of 1 mm).
The density of water for the raindrop is 1000 kg/m³.
The density of air is 1.2 kg/m³.
The drag coefficient is a constant value of 0.5.
Use the gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
At terminal velocity, the gravitational force (weight minus buoyancy) equals the aerodynamic drag force.
The weight minus buoyancy is (rho_water - rho_air) * V * g, where V is the volume of the sphere.
The drag force is 0.5 * C_d * rho_air * A * v², where A is the cross-sectional area of the sphere and v is the velocity.
Set these two forces equal and solve for the terminal velocity v.
Report the terminal velocity in m/s.
