Compute the stagnation temperature of a compressible flow using the isentropic stagnation temperature relation.
The Mach number is 2.0.
The static (freestream) temperature is 250 K.
Assume the working fluid is air with a specific heat ratio γ = 1.4.
Use the isentropic relation T0 = T × (1 + ((γ - 1) / 2) × M²), where T0 is the stagnation temperature, T is the static temperature, and M is the Mach number.
Report the resulting stagnation temperature in Kelvin.
