Calculate the shaft power required for a centrifugal pump using the following parameters.
The fluid being pumped is water with a density of 998 kg/m³ (standard conditions).
The volumetric flow rate is 0.05 m³/s.
The total head delivered by the pump is 25 meters.
The overall pump efficiency is 75% (0.75).
Use the gravitational acceleration value of 9.81 m/s².
The shaft power is calculated using the formula: P_shaft = (ρ × g × Q × H) / η, where ρ is the fluid density, g is gravitational acceleration, Q is the volumetric flow rate, H is the total head, and η is the pump efficiency.
Report the result in kilowatts (kW), rounded to two decimal places.
