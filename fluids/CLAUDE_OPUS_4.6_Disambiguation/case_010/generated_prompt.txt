Calculate the lift per unit span on a rotating cylinder using the Kutta-Joukowski theorem.
The cylinder has a radius of 0.1 m.
The freestream velocity is 15 m/s.
The cylinder rotates at 200 rad/s.
The air density is 1.225 kg/m³.
The lift is calculated per unit span (per 1 meter of cylinder length).
The circulation is determined by Γ = 2πR²ω, where R is the radius and ω is the angular velocity.
The lift per unit span is determined by the Kutta-Joukowski theorem: L' = ρ × V∞ × Γ.
