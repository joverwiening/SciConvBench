Calculate the convective heat transfer rate from a flat plate using Newton's law of cooling: Q = h × A × (T_surface - T_fluid).
The surface area of the plate is 2 m².
The convective heat transfer coefficient (h) is 25 W/(m²·K).
The surface temperature of the plate is 60°C.
The surrounding fluid temperature is 20°C.
The temperature difference is 40°C (equivalently 40 K).
The expected result is Q = 25 × 2 × 40 = 2000 W.
