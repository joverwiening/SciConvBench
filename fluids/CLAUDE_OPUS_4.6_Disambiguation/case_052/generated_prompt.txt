Calculate the dynamic viscosity of a fluid using a falling-ball viscometer and Stokes' law.
The steel ball has a diameter of 3 mm (radius 1.5 mm or 0.0015 m).
The density of the steel ball is 7850 kg/m³.
The density of the fluid is 1000 kg/m³.
The measured terminal velocity of the steel ball is 5 cm/s (0.05 m/s).
Assume an infinite medium with no wall correction effects.
Use Stokes' law: mu = (2 * r² * (rho_ball - rho_fluid) * g) / (9 * v_t), where r is the ball radius, g is gravitational acceleration (9.81 m/s²), and v_t is the terminal velocity.
Report the resulting dynamic viscosity mu in SI units (Pa·s).
