Calculate the Stefan number for the melting of ice.
The Stefan number is defined as Ste = cp × ΔT / L.
Use the specific heat capacity of liquid water, cp = 4.186 kJ/(kg·°C).
Use the latent heat of fusion for ice, L = 333.7 kJ/kg.
Use a temperature difference of ΔT = 10°C.
Report the resulting dimensionless Stefan number.
