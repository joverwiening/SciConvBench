Q: What is the specific heat capacity (cp) of the material involved in this phase-change problem (and please specify the material if known)?
A: The material is ice (melting). Use the specific heat capacity of liquid water, cp = 4.186 kJ/(kg·°C), since the Stefan number for melting involves the liquid phase properties. The latent heat of fusion for ice is 333.7 kJ/kg.
