Calculate the thermal stress in a fully constrained steel rod subjected to a temperature drop.
The rod is fixed at both ends, allowing zero deformation.
The material is steel with a Young's modulus E = 200 GPa.
The coefficient of thermal expansion is α = 12 × 10⁻⁶ /°C.
The temperature change is a drop of 40°C (ΔT = -40°C).
Use the thermal stress formula for a fully constrained rod: σ = E × α × |ΔT|.
The resulting stress is tensile because the rod attempts to contract but is prevented from doing so.
Report the final stress value in MPa, clearly indicating it is tensile.
