Calculate the absolute pressure at a depth of 2000 m in the ocean.
Use the hydrostatic pressure formula: P = P_atm + ρ × g × h.
Use a seawater density (ρ) of 1025 kg/m³.
Use gravitational acceleration (g) of 9.81 m/s².
Use standard atmospheric pressure at the surface (P_atm) of 101,325 Pa.
The depth (h) is 2000 m.
Report the result as absolute pressure, which includes both the atmospheric pressure and the pressure due to the water column.
Express the final result in Pascals (Pa) and also convert to megapascals (MPa) for convenience.
