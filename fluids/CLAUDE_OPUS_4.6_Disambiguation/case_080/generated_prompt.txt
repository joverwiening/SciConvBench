The task is to determine the actual outlet temperature of an air compressor.
The inlet temperature is 300 K.
The pressure ratio (outlet pressure to inlet pressure) is 8.
The working gas is air with a specific heat ratio γ = 1.4.
First compute the isentropic outlet temperature using the relation T2s = T1 × (pressure ratio)^((γ - 1) / γ).
The isentropic efficiency of the compressor is 85% (0.85).
Compute the actual outlet temperature using the relation η = (T2s - T1) / (T2_actual - T1), which gives T2_actual = T1 + (T2s - T1) / η.
Report the isentropic outlet temperature and the actual outlet temperature in Kelvin.
