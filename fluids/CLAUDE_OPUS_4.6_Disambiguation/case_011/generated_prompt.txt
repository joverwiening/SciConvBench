Calculate the Biot number for a cooling sphere.
The sphere has a radius of 5 mm (0.005 m).
The convective heat transfer coefficient at the surface is h = 150 W/m²·K.
The thermal conductivity of the sphere material is k = 45 W/m·K.
The characteristic length for a sphere is Lc = Volume / Surface Area = (4/3 π r³) / (4 π r²) = r/3.
The Biot number is calculated as Bi = h · Lc / k.
