The system contains 2 kg of nitrogen gas in a piston-cylinder device.
The gas constant for nitrogen is R = 0.2968 kJ/(kg·K).
The gas behaves as an ideal gas.
The initial pressure is 500 kPa.
The initial temperature is 300 K.
The initial volume is calculated from the ideal gas law: V1 = mRT / P1 = (2 kg)(0.2968 kJ/(kg·K))(300 K) / (500 kPa) = 0.35616 m³.
The final pressure is 100 kPa.
The process is isothermal at a constant temperature of 300 K.
The final volume is calculated from the ideal gas law: V2 = mRT / P2 = (2 kg)(0.2968 kJ/(kg·K))(300 K) / (100 kPa) = 1.7808 m³.
The boundary layer work for an isothermal process of an ideal gas is calculated using W = mRT ln(V2/V1), which is equivalent to W = mRT ln(P1/P2).
