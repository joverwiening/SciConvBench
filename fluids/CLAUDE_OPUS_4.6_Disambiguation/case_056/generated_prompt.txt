Calculate the stagnation pressure at the nose of a jet flying through the atmosphere.
The jet is flying at Mach 1.5.
The ambient (freestream) static pressure is 20 kPa.
The ratio of specific heats (gamma) is 1.4.
Since the flow is supersonic (Mach 1.5 > 1.0), a normal shock forms at the nose, and the stagnation pressure should be calculated as the total pressure behind the normal shock (pitot pressure).
Use the Rayleigh pitot tube formula (normal shock relations combined with isentropic relations) to determine the stagnation pressure downstream of the normal shock.
Report the final stagnation pressure in kPa.
