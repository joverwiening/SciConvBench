Calculate the Nusselt number for turbulent flow inside a pipe using the Dittus-Boelter equation.
The Dittus-Boelter equation is Nu = 0.023 * Re^0.8 * Pr^n.
The Reynolds number (Re) is 1.5e4 (15,000).
The Prandtl number (Pr) is 6.0.
The fluid is being heated, so the exponent n on the Prandtl number is 0.4.
The final calculation is Nu = 0.023 * (15000)^0.8 * (6.0)^0.4.
