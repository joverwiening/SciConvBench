Compute the average convective heat transfer coefficient for air flowing over a flat plate with the following parameters and procedure.

The flat plate length is L = 1 m.
The free-stream velocity is U = 5 m/s.
The thermal conductivity of air is k = 0.026 W/(m·K).
The kinematic viscosity of air is ν = 1.5e-5 m²/s.
The Prandtl number of air is Pr = 0.7.

First, compute the Reynolds number at the trailing edge of the plate using Re_L = U * L / ν.
If Re_L is less than 5e5, the flow is entirely laminar over the full plate length.
If Re_L is greater than or equal to 5e5, the flow has a mixed boundary layer with transition occurring at a critical Reynolds number of Re_cr = 5e5.

For entirely laminar flow, compute the average Nusselt number using the correlation Nu_avg = 0.664 * Re_L^0.5 * Pr^(1/3).
For mixed laminar-turbulent flow, compute the average Nusselt number using the correlation Nu_avg = (0.037 * Re_L^0.8 - 871) * Pr^(1/3), where 871 accounts for the laminar portion up to Re_cr = 5e5.

Compute the average convective heat transfer coefficient as h_avg = Nu_avg * k / L.
Report the Reynolds number, the determined flow regime, the average Nusselt number, and the average convective heat transfer coefficient with appropriate units.
