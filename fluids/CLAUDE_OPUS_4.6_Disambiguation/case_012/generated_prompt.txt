Calculate the capillary rise of water in a narrow tube using the Jurin's law formula: h = (2σ cosθ) / (ρgr).
The tube has an internal diameter of 0.5 mm, giving a radius of 0.25 mm (0.00025 m).
The liquid is water with a surface tension of σ = 0.072 N/m.
The contact angle between the water and the tube wall is θ = 0°.
The density of water is ρ = 1000 kg/m³.
The gravitational acceleration is g = 9.81 m/s².
Report the resulting capillary rise height in appropriate units (e.g., meters and/or centimeters).
