Calculate the cavitation number (sigma) for water flow using the following parameters and formula.

The formula for cavitation number is sigma = (p - p_v) / (0.5 * rho * V^2).

The local absolute pressure (p) is 50 kPa.

The vapor pressure (p_v) is 2.3 kPa.

The flow velocity (V) is 20 m/s.

The water density (rho) is 998 kg/m^3 (standard value for water near room temperature).

Report the resulting dimensionless cavitation number.
