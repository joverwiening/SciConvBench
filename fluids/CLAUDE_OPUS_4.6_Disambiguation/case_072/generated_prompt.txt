Calculate the hydraulic efficiency of a Pelton wheel turbine using the following given data and method.

The net head (H) is 300 meters.
The water discharge (Q) is 0.5 m³/s.
The runner power developed is 1300 kW.
The density of water (ρ) is 1000 kg/m³.
The acceleration due to gravity (g) is 9.81 m/s².

First, calculate the water power available using the formula: Water Power = ρ × g × Q × H.
Then, calculate the hydraulic efficiency as: Hydraulic Efficiency = Runner Power / Water Power.
Express the hydraulic efficiency as a percentage.
