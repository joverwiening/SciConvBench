Q: What is the velocity of the jet at the nozzle exit (or alternatively, the coefficient of velocity, Cv, of the nozzle)?
A: The complete requirement doesn't specify the jet velocity or coefficient of velocity directly. Instead, you're given:

- Water discharge: **0.5 m³/s**
- Runner power developed: **1300 kW**
- Water density: **1000 kg/m³**
- g = **9.81 m/s²**

You should calculate hydraulic efficiency as the ratio of runner power to the water power available (ρ × g × Q × H).
