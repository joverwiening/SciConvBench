Calculate the force exerted by a water jet on a stationary curved vane.
The water jet has a diameter of 50 mm.
The velocity of the water jet is 20 m/s.
The jet is deflected through an angle of 120 degrees by the curved vane.
The vane is stationary.
Friction is neglected, so the jet speed remains constant at 20 m/s as it flows over the vane.
The density of water is 1000 kg/m³.
Compute the x-component and y-component of the force exerted on the vane, as well as the resultant force magnitude and its direction.
Use the impulse-momentum (control volume) approach with steady-flow momentum equations.
