Calculate the specific speed of a Francis turbine using the following given parameters.
The rotational speed (N) of the turbine is 450 rpm.
The power output (P) of the turbine is 5000 kW.
The net head (H) acting on the turbine is 120 meters.
Use the specific speed formula: Ns = N * sqrt(P) / H^(5/4), where N is in rpm, P is in kW, and H is in meters.
Report the final specific speed value with appropriate units (rpm·kW^0.5 / m^(5/4)).
