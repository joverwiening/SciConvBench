Determine the efficiency of a straight rectangular fin with an insulated-tip formula applied using a corrected length.
The fin material is aluminum with thermal conductivity k = 200 W/m·K.
The fin length is L = 3 cm (0.03 m).
The fin thickness is t = 2 mm (0.002 m).
The convection heat transfer coefficient is h = 20 W/m²·K.
Use the corrected length approach to account for heat loss from the tip: Lc = L + t/2 = 0.03 + 0.001 = 0.031 m.
Compute the fin parameter m = sqrt(2h / (k·t)) since for a wide rectangular fin the parameter simplifies this way (perimeter ≈ 2W, cross-sectional area ≈ W·t, so P/(kAc) = 2/(k·t)).
Calculate fin efficiency using the insulated-tip formula with the corrected length: η = tanh(m·Lc) / (m·Lc).
Report the final fin efficiency as a decimal and as a percentage.
