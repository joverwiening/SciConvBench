Calculate the thermal efficiency of an ideal simple Rankine cycle using the following parameters.
The working fluid is water/steam.
The boiler pressure is 4 MPa.
Steam enters the turbine as saturated vapor at 4 MPa (no superheat).
The condenser pressure is 10 kPa.
All components are ideal: the turbine and pump are isentropic, and there are no pressure drops in the boiler or condenser.
Use standard steam table properties to determine enthalpies and entropies at each state point.
Report the thermal efficiency of the cycle as a percentage.
