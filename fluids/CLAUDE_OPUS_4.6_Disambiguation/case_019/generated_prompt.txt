Solve the steady-state heat conduction equation with internal heat generation in a one-dimensional plane wall.
The wall thickness is 20 cm (0.20 m).
The thermal conductivity of the wall material is 15 W/m·K.
The uniform internal heat generation rate is 2×10⁵ W/m³.
The left face boundary condition at x = 0 is a fixed temperature of 50°C.
The right face boundary condition at x = 0.20 m is a fixed temperature of 50°C.
The governing equation is: k × (d²T/dx²) + q_gen = 0, where k = 15 W/m·K and q_gen = 2×10⁵ W/m³.
Compute and report the temperature distribution T(x) across the wall.
Identify the maximum temperature and its location within the wall.
