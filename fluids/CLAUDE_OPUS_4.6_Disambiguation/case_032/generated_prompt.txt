Calculate the Weber number for a liquid fuel droplet using the formula We = (rho * v^2 * d) / sigma.
The droplet diameter (d) is 2 mm, which is 0.002 m.
The droplet velocity (v) is 30 m/s.
The density of the liquid droplet (rho) is 800 kg/m³.
The surface tension (sigma) is 0.03 N/m.
Substitute these values into the Weber number formula to compute the result.
We = (800 * 30^2 * 0.002) / 0.03.
Report the final dimensionless Weber number.
