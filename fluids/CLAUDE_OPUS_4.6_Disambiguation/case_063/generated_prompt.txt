Calculate the Reynolds number for water flowing through a smooth pipe to determine if the flow is laminar or turbulent.
The inner diameter of the pipe is 4 cm (0.04 m).
The flow velocity is 1.5 m/s.
The dynamic viscosity of the water is 0.001 Pa·s.
The density of the water is 998 kg/m³.
Use the Reynolds number formula: Re = (density × velocity × diameter) / dynamic viscosity.
If Re is less than 2300, classify the flow as laminar.
If Re is between 2300 and 4000, classify the flow as transitional.
If Re is greater than 4000, classify the flow as turbulent.
Report the calculated Reynolds number and the resulting flow classification.
