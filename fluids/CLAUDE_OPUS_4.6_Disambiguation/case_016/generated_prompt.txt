The task is to find the center of pressure on a vertically submerged rectangular plate.
The plate dimensions are 1 m wide and 2 m tall.
The 2 m side is oriented along the vertical (depth) direction, as this is the more common and instructive configuration.
The 1 m side is oriented along the horizontal direction.
The top edge of the plate is flush with the free surface of the liquid, so the depth to the top edge is 0 m.
The plate is fully submerged vertically, extending from 0 m to 2 m depth.
The liquid is water with a standard density of 1000 kg/m³.
Gravitational acceleration is 9.81 m/s².
The plate is flat and has uniform geometry with no cutouts or holes.
Determine the vertical depth of the center of pressure measured from the free surface.
Report the final answer in meters.
