Calculate the power required to pump water to the upper reservoir using the following parameters.
The volumetric flow rate of water is 0.02 m³/s.
The height difference (elevation change) between the lower and upper reservoirs is 30 m.
The pump efficiency is 80% (0.80).
The pipe length is 50 m.
The pipe diameter is 0.05 m.
The Darcy friction factor is 0.02.
Only major (friction) losses are included; no minor losses are considered.
The total head is the sum of the elevation head and the friction head loss.
The friction head loss is calculated using the Darcy-Weisbach equation: h_f = f * (L / D) * (V² / (2 * g)), where f is the friction factor, L is the pipe length, D is the pipe diameter, V is the flow velocity, and g is gravitational acceleration (9.81 m/s²).
The flow velocity is calculated from the volumetric flow rate and pipe cross-sectional area: V = Q / A, where A = π * D² / 4.
The required power is calculated as P = (ρ * g * Q * H_total) / η, where ρ is the density of water (1000 kg/m³), Q is the volumetric flow rate, H_total is the total head, and η is the pump efficiency.
Use standard properties for water at ambient conditions (density = 1000 kg/m³).
