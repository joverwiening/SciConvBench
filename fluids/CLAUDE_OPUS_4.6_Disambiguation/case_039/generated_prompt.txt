Compute the torque required to rotate a disk near a wall with a viscous fluid in the gap between the disk and the wall.
The disk has a radius R = 0.2 m.
The fluid is oil with a dynamic viscosity μ = 0.1 Pa·s.
The disk rotates at an angular velocity of 60 rpm.
The clearance gap between the disk and the wall is 1 mm (0.001 m).
Only one side of the disk is considered, which is the side facing the wall across the 1 mm gap.
Assume a linear velocity profile (Couette flow) across the gap.
Use the standard Newton's law of viscosity approach: the shear stress at radial distance r is τ = μ × (ω × r) / h, where ω is the angular velocity in rad/s and h is the gap thickness.
Integrate the torque contribution dT = τ × r × dA over the entire disk face from r = 0 to r = R, where dA = 2πr dr.
The resulting formula is T = (μ × ω × π × R⁴) / (2 × h).
Report the final torque value with appropriate units (N·m).
