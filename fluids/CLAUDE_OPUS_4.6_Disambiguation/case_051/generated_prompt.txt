Calculate the buoyant lift force on a gas-filled balloon using the following parameters.
The balloon volume is 500 m³.
The balloon is filled with helium.
The density of the surrounding air (ρ_air) is 1.2 kg/m³.
The density of helium (ρ_he) is 0.18 kg/m³.
The buoyant lift force is calculated as F = (ρ_air - ρ_he) × V × g.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Report the net buoyant lift force in Newtons.
