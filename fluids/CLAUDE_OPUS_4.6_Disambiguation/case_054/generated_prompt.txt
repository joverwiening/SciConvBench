Determine the average Nusselt number for air flowing over a flat plate using a mixed laminar-turbulent boundary layer analysis.
The flat plate length is L = 1.5 m.
The free-stream velocity is U = 10 m/s.
The air temperature is 20°C.
The kinematic viscosity is ν = 1.5×10⁻⁵ m²/s.
The Prandtl number is Pr = 0.7.
The Reynolds number at the end of the plate is Re_L = U·L / ν.
The critical Reynolds number for laminar-to-turbulent transition is Re_cr = 5×10⁵.
Use the standard mixed boundary layer correlation for the average Nusselt number: Nu_L = (0.037 Re_L^(4/5) - 871) Pr^(1/3).
Report the final average Nusselt number as the result.
