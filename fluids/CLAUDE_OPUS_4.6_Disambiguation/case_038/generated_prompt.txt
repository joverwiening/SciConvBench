Analyze an ideal Brayton cycle to determine the specific work output.
The working fluid is air with specific heat ratio γ = 1.4 and specific heat at constant pressure cp = 1.005 kJ/kg·K.
The compressor inlet temperature T1 is 300 K.
The turbine inlet temperature T3 is 1200 K.
The pressure ratio across the compressor is 10.
Assume isentropic compression and isentropic expansion throughout the cycle.
Assume no pressure losses in the combustor or any other component.
Calculate the compressor exit temperature T2 using the isentropic relation T2 = T1 × (pressure ratio)^((γ-1)/γ).
Calculate the turbine exit temperature T4 using the isentropic relation T4 = T3 / (pressure ratio)^((γ-1)/γ).
Calculate the specific compressor work as w_comp = cp × (T2 - T1).
Calculate the specific turbine work as w_turb = cp × (T3 - T4).
Calculate the specific net work output as w_net = w_turb - w_comp.
Report the specific net work output in kJ/kg.
