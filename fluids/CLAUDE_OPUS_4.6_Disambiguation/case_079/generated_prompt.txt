Compute Thoma's cavitation factor (sigma) for a Francis turbine using the following parameters and method.

The total head (H) on the turbine is 50 meters.
The atmospheric pressure head (Ha) is 10.3 meters.
The vapor pressure head (Hv) is 0.3 meters.
The height of the turbine runner above the tailrace water level (Hs) is 2 meters.
Calculate sigma using the formula: sigma = (Ha - Hv - Hs) / H.
Substitute the values: sigma = (10.3 - 0.3 - 2) / 50.
Report the final numerical value of sigma.
