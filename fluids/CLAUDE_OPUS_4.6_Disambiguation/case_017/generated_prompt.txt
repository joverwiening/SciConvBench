Calculate the speed of sound in a gas mixture at a temperature of 300 K.
The gas mixture is composed of 70% Helium (He) and 30% Argon (Ar) by mole fraction.
Assume ideal gas behavior at a total pressure of 1 atm (101325 Pa).
Use the mole-fraction-weighted average molar mass to determine the effective molar mass of the mixture.
Use the mole-fraction-weighted average heat capacity ratio (gamma) to determine the effective gamma of the mixture, using the ideal-gas specific heats for each component (gamma of He = 5/3, gamma of Ar = 5/3).
Calculate the speed of sound using the formula v = sqrt(gamma * R * T / M), where R is the universal gas constant (8.314 J/(mol·K)), T is the temperature in Kelvin, and M is the effective molar mass in kg/mol.
Report the final speed of sound in meters per second.
