Calculate the power output of a steam turbine using the steady-flow energy equation.
The mass flow rate of steam entering the turbine is 10 kg/s.
The specific enthalpy of steam at the turbine inlet is 3200 kJ/kg.
The specific enthalpy of steam at the turbine outlet is 2400 kJ/kg.
The turbine is assumed to be adiabatic, meaning no heat loss to the surroundings.
Changes in kinetic energy and potential energy between the inlet and outlet are neglected.
The power output is calculated as: P = mass flow rate × (inlet specific enthalpy − outlet specific enthalpy).
