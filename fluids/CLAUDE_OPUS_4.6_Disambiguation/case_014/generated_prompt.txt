Compute the weak oblique shock angle for a wedge in supersonic airflow using the following parameters and methods.

The freestream Mach number is 3.0.
The wedge half-angle (flow deflection angle) is 10 degrees.
The gas is air with a ratio of specific heats (gamma) of 1.4.
Solve the theta-beta-Mach (θ-β-M) relation for the weak shock solution (smaller β root).
The theta-beta-Mach relation is: tan(θ) = 2 cot(β) [ (M₁² sin²(β) - 1) / (M₁² (γ + cos(2β)) + 2) ], where θ is the deflection angle, β is the shock wave angle, and M₁ is the freestream Mach number.
Use an iterative numerical method (e.g., Newton-Raphson or bisection) to solve for β.
For the weak solution, search for β in the range between the Mach angle (arcsin(1/M₁)) and the maximum shock angle corresponding to the strong solution.
Report the oblique shock angle β in degrees, rounded to two decimal places.
Also report the downstream Mach number (M₂) behind the oblique shock for completeness.
Use standard normal shock relations applied to the normal component of the Mach number (M₁ sin(β)) to compute post-shock properties.
