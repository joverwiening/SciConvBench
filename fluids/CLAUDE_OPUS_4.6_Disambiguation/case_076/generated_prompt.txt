Calculate the efficiency of a conical draft tube.
The water velocity at the inlet of the draft tube is 10 m/s.
The water velocity at the outlet of the draft tube is 2 m/s.
The actual pressure head recovery in the draft tube is 4.5 meters of water.
The efficiency of the draft tube is defined as the ratio of the actual pressure head recovery to the maximum possible (ideal) pressure head recovery.
The ideal pressure head recovery is calculated as (V_inlet² - V_outlet²) / (2g), where g = 9.81 m/s².
Use standard gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Report the efficiency as a decimal or percentage.
