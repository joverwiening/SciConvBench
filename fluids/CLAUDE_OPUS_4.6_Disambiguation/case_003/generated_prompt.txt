Calculate the terminal velocity of a spherical particle falling through oil using the following parameters.
The particle is a steel ball with a diameter of 2 mm (0.002 m).
The particle density is 7800 kg/m³.
The fluid is SAE 30 oil with a density of 890 kg/m³.
The dynamic viscosity of the oil is 0.29 Pa·s.
Use Stokes' Law for terminal velocity: Vt = (d² × (ρ_particle - ρ_fluid) × g) / (18 × μ), where d is the particle diameter, ρ_particle is the particle density, ρ_fluid is the fluid density, g is gravitational acceleration (9.81 m/s²), and μ is the dynamic viscosity.
After computing the terminal velocity, verify the validity of Stokes' Law by calculating the Reynolds number: Re = (ρ_fluid × Vt × d) / μ.
Stokes' Law is valid if Re < 1; if Re exceeds 1, note that a drag correction or alternative correlation is needed.
Report the terminal velocity in m/s and the corresponding Reynolds number.
