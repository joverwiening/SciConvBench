Calculate the heat transfer coefficient using the relation h = Nu * k / L.
The Nusselt number Nu is 120.
The thermal conductivity of the fluid is k = 0.026 W/m·K (air).
The characteristic length is L = 0.10 m (diameter of a sphere).
The expected result is h = 120 * 0.026 / 0.10 = 31.2 W/m²·K.
Report the heat transfer coefficient h in units of W/m²·K.
