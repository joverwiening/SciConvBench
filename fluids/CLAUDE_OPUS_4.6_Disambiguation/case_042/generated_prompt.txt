Calculate the force produced by a water jet striking a flat plate.
The water jet has a diameter of 10 mm.
The velocity of the water jet is 15 m/s.
The flat plate is stationary.
The flat plate is oriented perpendicular (normal) to the jet.
Use the standard density of water, 998 kg/m³ (approximately 1000 kg/m³).
Use the momentum equation for a jet striking a stationary flat plate: F = ρ × A × V², where ρ is the density of water, A is the cross-sectional area of the jet, and V is the jet velocity.
Report the final force in Newtons.
