Solve for the steady-state velocity profile of pure Couette flow between two infinite parallel plates.
The two plates are separated by a gap of 10 mm (0.01 m).
The bottom plate is stationary (velocity = 0 m/s).
The top plate moves at 5 m/s in the flow direction.
The fluid between the plates is Newtonian.
There is no applied pressure gradient in the flow direction (pure Couette flow).
The flow is fully developed and steady (time-independent).
Because the pressure gradient is zero, the dynamic viscosity does not affect the velocity profile and need not be specified.
The expected result is the linear velocity profile u(y) = U * (y / h), where U = 5 m/s is the top plate velocity, h = 0.01 m is the gap height, and y is the vertical coordinate measured from the bottom plate (y = 0 at the bottom plate, y = h at the top plate).
Present the analytical solution for u(y) with the numerical values substituted in.
