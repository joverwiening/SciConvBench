Calculate the power available in a wind stream incident on a wind turbine.

The blade diameter is 40 meters.

The air density is 1.225 kg/m³.
Report the final answer in kilowatts (kW).
This calculation is for the total power available in the wind stream, not the power extracted by the turbine (no efficiency or Betz limit applied).
