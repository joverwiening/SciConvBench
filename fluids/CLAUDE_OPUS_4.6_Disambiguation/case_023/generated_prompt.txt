Calculate the pressure drop per unit length (ΔP/L) in a packed bed using the Ergun equation.
The packed bed consists of spherical particles with a diameter of 5 mm (0.005 m).
The fluid is air at standard atmospheric conditions (approximately 20°C and 1 atm).
Use standard air properties at 20°C and 1 atm: density of approximately 1.204 kg/m³ and dynamic viscosity of approximately 1.825 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s.
The superficial velocity of the air is 0.5 m/s.
The void fraction (porosity) of the packed bed is 0.4.
Report the result as pressure drop per meter of bed length (Pa/m).
Use the Ergun equation: ΔP/L = [150 × μ × (1 − ε)² × U] / [D_p² × ε³] + [1.75 × ρ × (1 − ε) × U²] / [D_p × ε³], where μ is dynamic viscosity, ε is porosity, U is superficial velocity, D_p is particle diameter, and ρ is fluid density.
