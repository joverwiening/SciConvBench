Calculate the volumetric flow rate through a Venturi meter using the standard Venturi flow equation.
The upstream pipe diameter D1 is 100 mm (0.1 m).
The throat diameter D2 is 50 mm (0.05 m).
The differential pressure ΔP across the Venturi meter is 20 kPa (20,000 Pa).
The fluid is water at standard conditions with a density of 998 kg/m³.
The discharge coefficient Cd is 0.98.
Use the Venturi meter flow rate equation: Q = Cd * A2 * sqrt(2 * ΔP / (ρ * (1 - (A2/A1)²))), where A1 and A2 are the cross-sectional areas of the upstream pipe and throat respectively.
Report the final volumetric flow rate in appropriate units (e.g., m³/s or L/s).
