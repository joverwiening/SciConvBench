Calculate the rate of heat transfer through a concrete wall using Fourier's law of heat conduction.
The wall thickness is 0.2 m.
The thermal conductivity of the concrete is 1.4 W/(m·K).
The temperature difference across the wall is 20°C.
The surface area of the wall is 15 m².
Use the formula Q = k × A × ΔT / L, where Q is the rate of heat transfer, k is thermal conductivity, A is area, ΔT is the temperature difference, and L is the wall thickness.
Assume steady-state, one-dimensional heat conduction with no internal heat generation.
Report the result in watts (W).
