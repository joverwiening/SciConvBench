Calculate the Peclet number for flow over a small object.
The Peclet number is defined as Pe = (U × L) / α, where U is the flow velocity, L is the characteristic length, and α is the thermal diffusivity.
The characteristic length L is 5 cm, which is 0.05 m.
The flow velocity U is 0.1 m/s.
The thermal diffusivity α is 1.2e-7 m²/s.
Compute Pe = (0.1 × 0.05) / 1.2e-7.
Report the resulting Peclet number with appropriate units (dimensionless).
