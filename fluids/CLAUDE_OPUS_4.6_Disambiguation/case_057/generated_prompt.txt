Calculate the static temperature ratio across a normal shock wave.
The upstream Mach number (M1) is 2.0.
The working fluid is air with a ratio of specific heats (gamma) equal to 1.4.
Use the normal shock temperature ratio relation: T2/T1 = [1 + 2*gamma/(gamma+1) * (M1^2 - 1)] * [2 + (gamma-1)*M1^2] / [(gamma+1)^2 * M1^2 / (gamma-1+2)].
More precisely, use the standard form: T2/T1 = [2*gamma*M1^2 - (gamma-1)] * [(gamma-1)*M1^2 + 2] / [(gamma+1)^2 * M1^2].
Report the resulting static temperature ratio T2/T1.
