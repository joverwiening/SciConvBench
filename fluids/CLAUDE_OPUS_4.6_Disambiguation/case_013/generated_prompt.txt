Calculate the critical insulation thickness for a cylindrical pipe with the following parameters.

The thermal conductivity of the insulation material is k = 0.17 W/m·K.
The external convective heat transfer coefficient on the outer surface of the insulation is h = 8 W/m²·K.
The critical radius of insulation is calculated using the formula r_cr = k / h.
The critical insulation thickness is the critical radius minus the outer radius of the bare pipe, but since no pipe outer radius is specified, report the critical radius of insulation r_cr = k / h.
Express the final answer in appropriate units (meters or millimeters).
