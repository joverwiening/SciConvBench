Calculate the excess pressure inside a soap bubble.
The soap bubble has a diameter of 10 mm, which gives a radius of 5 mm (0.005 m).
The surface tension of the soap solution is σ = 0.035 N/m.
Use the soap bubble excess pressure formula: ΔP = 4σ / r (the factor of 4 accounts for the two surfaces of the soap film).
Report the final answer in pascals (Pa).
