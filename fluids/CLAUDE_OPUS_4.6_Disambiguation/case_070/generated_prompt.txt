Compute the drag force acting on a sphere moving through glycerin.
The sphere has a diameter of 5 mm.
The sphere moves at a velocity of 0.1 m/s.
The dynamic viscosity of glycerin is 1.5 Pa·s.
The density of glycerin is 1260 kg/m³.
Compute the Reynolds number as Re = ρVD/μ to verify the flow regime.
Use Stokes' drag law (F = 3πμVD) since the Reynolds number is well below 1, indicating creeping flow.
Report the Reynolds number and the resulting drag force with appropriate units.
