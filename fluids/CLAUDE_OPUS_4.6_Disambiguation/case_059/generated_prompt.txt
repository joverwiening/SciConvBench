Calculate the aerodynamic drag force on a billboard using the drag force equation F = 0.5 * Cd * rho * A * V^2.
The billboard is 4 meters high and 10 meters wide, giving a frontal area of 40 square meters.
The wind speed is 30 m/s.
The drag coefficient (Cd) is 1.2.
The air density (rho) is 1.225 kg/m³.
Report the resulting drag force in Newtons.
