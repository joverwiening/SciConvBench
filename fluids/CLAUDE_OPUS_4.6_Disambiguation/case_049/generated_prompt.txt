Calculate the hydraulic diameter of a rectangular duct with cross-sectional dimensions of 30 cm by 10 cm.
Use the standard hydraulic diameter formula: Dh = 4A / P, where A is the cross-sectional area and P is the wetted perimeter.
The cross-sectional area A = 30 cm × 10 cm = 300 cm².
The wetted perimeter P = 2 × (30 cm + 10 cm) = 80 cm.
Report the hydraulic diameter result in centimeters.
