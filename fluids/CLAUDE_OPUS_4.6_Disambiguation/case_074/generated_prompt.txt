Calculate the degree of reaction for an axial flow compressor stage using the following parameters.
The air enters the rotor with a relative velocity of 150 m/s at a relative flow angle of 45 degrees to the axial direction.
The air leaves the rotor with a relative velocity of 100 m/s at a relative flow angle of 30 degrees to the axial direction.
The mean blade speed is 200 m/s.
The axial velocity is constant through the stage.
The degree of reaction is to be calculated using the velocity triangles and the standard definition for an axial flow compressor stage.
