Calculate the Reynolds number at a distance of 0.5 m from the leading edge for flow over a flat plate in air.
The free-stream velocity is 10 m/s.
The characteristic length (distance from the leading edge) is 0.5 m.
The kinematic viscosity of air is 1.5 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s.
Use the formula Re_x = U × x / ν, where U is the free-stream velocity, x is the distance from the leading edge, and ν is the kinematic viscosity.
