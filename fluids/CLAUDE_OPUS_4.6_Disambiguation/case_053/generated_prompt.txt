Determine the torque measured by a Couette (coaxial cylinder) rotational viscometer using the following parameters and approach.

The dynamic viscosity of the fluid is 0.5 Pa·s.
The rotational speed of the inner cylinder is 100 rpm.
The inner cylinder radius R is 2 cm (0.02 m).
The gap between the inner and outer cylinders is 1 mm (0.001 m).
The height of the inner cylinder h is 5 cm (0.05 m).
The outer cylinder radius is R_o = R + gap = 0.021 m.
Use the narrow-gap approximation for the Couette viscometer since the gap is much smaller than the radius.
Convert the rotational speed from rpm to rad/s using omega = (2 * pi * N) / 60.
Compute the shear rate in the gap as gamma_dot = (omega * R) / gap.
Compute the shear stress as tau = mu * gamma_dot.
Compute the torque on the inner cylinder surface as T = tau * (2 * pi * R * h) * R, which is the shear stress times the lateral surface area of the inner cylinder times the moment arm R.
Report the final torque value in N·m.
