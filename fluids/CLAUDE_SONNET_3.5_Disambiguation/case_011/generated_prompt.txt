Calculate the Biot number (Bi) for a sphere with the following parameters:
Use a radius (R) of 0.005 meters.
Use a convective heat transfer coefficient (h) of 150 W/m²·K.
Use a thermal conductivity (k) of 45 W/m·K.
Calculate Bi using the formula: Bi = (h × R) / k.
The characteristic length for a sphere is its radius.
