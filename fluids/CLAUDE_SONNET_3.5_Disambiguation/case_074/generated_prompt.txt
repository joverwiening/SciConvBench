Calculate the degree of reaction for an axial flow compressor stage.
Use rotor inlet relative velocity (W1) of 150 m/s.
Use rotor exit relative velocity (W2) of 100 m/s.
Use rotor blade speed (U) of 200 m/s.
Use rotor inlet relative flow angle (β1) of 45 degrees.
Use rotor exit relative flow angle (β2) of 30 degrees.
Convert all angles to radians for calculations.
Calculate tangential components of relative velocities using W1*cos(β1) and W2*cos(β2).
Calculate axial components of relative velocities using W1*sin(β1) and W2*sin(β2).
Calculate absolute tangential velocities by subtracting blade speed from relative tangential velocities.
Calculate degree of reaction using the formula: R = 1 - (Cθ1 + Cθ2)/(2U).
Present the final result as a decimal number between 0 and 1.
