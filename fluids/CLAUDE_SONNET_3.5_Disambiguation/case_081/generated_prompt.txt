Calculate the force exerted by a water jet on a smooth semi-circular curved vane.
The water jet has a diameter of 50 mm.
The water jet velocity is 20 m/s.
The jet is deflected through an angle of 120 degrees.
Assume the vane is frictionless.
Assume atmospheric pressure at the outlet.
Use the density of water as 1000 kg/m³.
Calculate both the horizontal and vertical components of the force.
The total force should be determined as the resultant of these components.
Assume steady flow conditions.
Assume no losses in the flow through the vane.
