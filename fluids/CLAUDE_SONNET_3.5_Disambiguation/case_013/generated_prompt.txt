Calculate the critical insulation thickness for a cylindrical pipe with the following specifications:
The thermal conductivity of insulation material is 0.17 W/m·K.
Use a standard industrial pipe outer radius of 0.0254 meters (1 inch).
Assume an ambient air temperature of 25°C.
The convective heat transfer coefficient is 8 W/m²·K.
Assume a pipe surface temperature of 150°C for a typical industrial hot water or steam pipe.
Use the critical radius equation: r_cr = k/h where k is thermal conductivity and h is the convective heat transfer coefficient.
Express the final critical insulation thickness in millimeters.
If the calculated critical radius is less than the pipe radius, report that no critical radius exists.
