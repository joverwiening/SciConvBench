Calculate the torque for a Couette viscometer with the following parameters:
The fluid viscosity is 0.5 Pa·s.
The rotational speed is 100 rpm (10.472 rad/s).
The inner cylinder radius is 20 mm.
The outer cylinder radius is 21 mm (1 mm gap).
The cylinder height is 50 mm.
Use the equation T = 4πμh(R₁)²ω/(1-(R₁/R₂)²) where T is torque, μ is viscosity, h is height, R₁ is inner radius, R₂ is outer radius, and ω is angular velocity.
Report the torque result in Newton-meters (N·m).
Round the final result to three significant figures.
