Calculate the convective heat transfer coefficient for air flowing over a flat plate with the following specifications:
The flat plate length is 1 meter.
The air velocity is 5 meters per second.
Assume standard atmospheric pressure of 101.325 kPa.
Assume ambient air temperature of 25°C (298.15 K).
Assume flat plate surface temperature of 50°C (323.15 K).
Use air properties evaluated at the film temperature (average of surface and ambient temperatures).
Calculate both local and average convective heat transfer coefficients along the plate length.
Present results in units of W/(m²·K).
Account for laminar and turbulent flow regions if the transition occurs within the plate length.
Use standard correlations for flat plate convection (Blasius solution for laminar, empirical correlation for turbulent).
