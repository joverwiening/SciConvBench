Calculate terminal velocity of a spherical raindrop with the following parameters:
The raindrop diameter is 2.0 millimeters.
Use standard atmospheric conditions at sea level (20°C, 1 atm pressure).
Assume the raindrop starts falling from 2000 meters altitude.
Use standard gravity (9.81 m/s²).
Use air density of 1.204 kg/m³ at 20°C.
Use dynamic viscosity of air at 20°C (1.825 × 10⁻⁵ kg/m·s).
Use water density of 998 kg/m³.
Calculate and report the terminal velocity in meters per second (m/s).
Account for both drag coefficient and Reynolds number in the calculation.
Present the result with two decimal places precision.
