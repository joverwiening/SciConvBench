Calculate the Stefan number for ice melting using the following parameters:
The temperature difference is 10 degrees Celsius.
The latent heat of fusion for ice is 334,000 J/kg.
The specific heat capacity of water is 4,186 J/kg·K.
The Stefan number is calculated as (c_p × ΔT) / L_f.
The Stefan number equals (4,186 × 10) / 334,000 = 0.125.
