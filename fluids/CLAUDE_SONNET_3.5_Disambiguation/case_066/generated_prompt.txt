The analysis will be performed for a standard vapor-compression refrigeration cycle.
The system operates as a domestic refrigerator with an evaporator temperature of 5°C and condenser temperature of 45°C.
The refrigerant used is R-134a, which is common in domestic applications.
Assuming ideal vapor-compression cycle conditions with isentropic compression.
The Coefficient of Performance (COP) for this system is 3.8.
The calculation assumes no pressure drops in the evaporator or condenser.
Heat exchanger effectiveness is considered to be 100%.
The compression process is considered adiabatic and isentropic.
The expansion process is considered isenthalpic.
The refrigerant enters the compressor as saturated vapor and leaves the condenser as saturated liquid.
