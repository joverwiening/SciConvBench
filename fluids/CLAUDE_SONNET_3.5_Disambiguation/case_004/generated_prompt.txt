The specific gas constant (R) for air is 287 J/(kg·K).
The air temperature is 223.15 K.
The flow velocity is 500 m/s.
Calculate the speed of sound using a = sqrt(gamma * R * T), where gamma = 1.4 for air.
Calculate the Mach number by dividing the flow velocity by the speed of sound.
The Mach number is 1.67 (rounded to 2 decimal places).
