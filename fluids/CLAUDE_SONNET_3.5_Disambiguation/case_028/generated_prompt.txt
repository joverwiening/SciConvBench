Use water at standard conditions (20°C) with density of 998 kg/m³.
Calculate minor head loss across pipe fitting using K-method.
Use minor loss coefficient K = 0.9.
Use fluid velocity of 3 meters per second.
Calculate head loss as h = K × (V²/2g) where g = 9.81 m/s².
The resulting head loss is 0.413 meters.
