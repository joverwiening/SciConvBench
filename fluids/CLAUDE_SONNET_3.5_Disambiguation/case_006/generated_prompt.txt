Solve for the steady velocity profile in Couette flow with the following parameters:
The bottom plate is stationary at y = 0.
The top plate moves at 5 m/s in the positive x-direction at y = 10 mm.
Use water as the working fluid with dynamic viscosity of 0.001 Pa·s at 20°C.
The flow is assumed to be steady, laminar, and incompressible.
The velocity profile should be calculated and plotted for the y-direction from 0 to 10 mm.
The analytical solution u(y) = (U/h)y should be used, where U is the top plate velocity and h is the plate separation.
Present results in SI units (meters, seconds).
Neglect pressure gradients in the flow direction.
Assume the plates are infinite in the x and z directions.
