Calculate the buoyant lift force for a balloon with the following parameters:
The balloon volume is 500 cubic meters.
The balloon is filled with helium gas.
Use standard atmospheric conditions at sea level (1 atm pressure, 15°C/288.15K).
Use standard air density at sea level (1.225 kg/m³).
Use helium density at standard conditions (0.1786 kg/m³).
Calculate buoyant force using F = (ρair - ρhelium) × V × g, where g = 9.81 m/s².
Express the final result in Newtons (N).
