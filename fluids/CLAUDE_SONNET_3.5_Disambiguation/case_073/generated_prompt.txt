Calculate the specific speed of a Francis turbine with the following parameters:
The turbine operates at 450 rpm.
The net head is 120 meters.
The power output is 5 MW.
Use metric units throughout the calculation (m, kW, rpm).
Apply the formula Ns = (N × √P) / (H^1.25), where N is speed in rpm, P is power in kW, and H is head in meters.
Round the final specific speed value to the nearest whole number.
The calculated specific speed should be dimensionless.
