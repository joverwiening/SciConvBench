Calculate the Weber number for a liquid droplet with the following parameters:
The droplet diameter is 2 mm (0.002 m).
The droplet velocity is 30 m/s.
The liquid density is 800 kg/m³.
The surface tension is 0.03 N/m.
Use the formula We = (ρ × v² × d) / σ where ρ is density, v is velocity, d is diameter, and σ is surface tension.
The Weber number is 48,000 (dimensionless).
