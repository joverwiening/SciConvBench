The initial temperature (T1) is assumed to be 298 K (25°C), which is standard ambient temperature.
The working fluid is air with a specific heat ratio (γ) of 1.4.
The compression ratio (r) is 8.5.
The thermal efficiency is calculated using the formula: η = 1 - (1/r^(γ-1)).
The thermal efficiency of the Otto cycle is 56.5%.
All processes are assumed to be ideal with no friction or heat transfer losses.
The working fluid behaves as an ideal gas throughout the cycle.
