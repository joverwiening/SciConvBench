Calculate the hydraulic efficiency of a Pelton wheel turbine with the following parameters:
Net head is 300 meters.
Water discharge rate is 0.5 cubic meters per second.
Shaft power output is 1300 kilowatts.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 meters per second squared.
Hydraulic efficiency is the ratio of shaft power output to input power.
Input power is calculated as (density of water × discharge × gravitational acceleration × head).
Use standard water density of 1000 kg/m³.
Express the final efficiency as a percentage.
