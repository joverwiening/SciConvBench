The water temperature is assumed to be 20°C (room temperature) as per standard conditions.
The local pressure (P) is 50 kPa.
The vapor pressure (Pv) is 2.3 kPa.
The water velocity (V) is 20 m/s.
The water density (ρ) is 998 kg/m³ at 20°C.
Calculate the cavitation number (σ) using the formula: σ = (P - Pv)/(0.5 × ρ × V²).
The cavitation number is 0.238 (dimensionless).
