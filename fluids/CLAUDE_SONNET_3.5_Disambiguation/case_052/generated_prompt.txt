Measure viscosity of motor oil SAE 10W-30 at room temperature (25°C) using falling-ball viscometer.
Use a stainless steel ball with 3 mm diameter.
Clean the viscometer tube thoroughly before measurement.
Fill the viscometer tube with the oil sample and allow it to equilibrate to 25°C for 15 minutes.
Mark two points on the tube 10 cm apart for timing measurements.
Release the steel ball at the center of the tube to avoid wall effects.
Measure the time taken for the ball to fall between the two marked points.
Repeat the measurement 5 times to ensure reproducibility.
Calculate viscosity using Stokes' Law: η = (2r²g(ρs-ρf))/(9v), where r is ball radius, g is gravity, ρs is ball density, ρf is fluid density, and v is terminal velocity.
Record temperature, fall time, and calculated viscosity in measurement log.
