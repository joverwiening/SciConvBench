Compute the thermal stress in a steel rod.
The rod is fully constrained at both ends.
The temperature of the rod increases by 50 K.
Use a Young's Modulus of 200 GPa for the steel.
Use a coefficient of thermal expansion of 12 x 10^-6 per Kelvin for the steel.
Calculate the thermal stress using the formula: stress = Young's Modulus * coefficient of thermal expansion * temperature change.
