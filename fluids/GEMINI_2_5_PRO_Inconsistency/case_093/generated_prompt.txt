Use the Bernoulli equation to verify that the stagnation-point pressure coefficient is 1 for incompressible flow.
Start with the Bernoulli equation relating a point in the freestream to the stagnation point.
The equation is P_inf + 0.5 * rho * V_inf^2 = P_stag + 0.5 * rho * V_stag^2.
Define the pressure coefficient as Cp = (P - P_inf) / (0.5 * rho * V_inf^2).
At the stagnation point, the velocity V_stag is zero.
At the stagnation point, the pressure P is the stagnation pressure P_stag.
Substitute V_stag = 0 into the Bernoulli equation to find P_stag.
Substitute the resulting expression for P_stag into the pressure coefficient formula.
Show the algebraic steps that simplify the expression to Cp = 1.
