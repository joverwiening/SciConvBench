Calculate the pressure rise across a sudden pipe expansion.
The inlet pipe diameter is 0.050 meters.
The outlet pipe diameter is 0.100 meters.
The volumetric flow rate is 0.010 cubic meters per second.
The calculation must use the energy equation, accounting for head loss.
The head loss is defined by the Borda–Carnot equation: h_L = (V1 - V2)^2 / (2g).
The pipe expansion is assumed to be horizontal.
The density of water is assumed to be 1000 kg/m^3.
The acceleration due to gravity (g) is assumed to be 9.81 m/s^2.
The final pressure rise should be reported in Pascals.
