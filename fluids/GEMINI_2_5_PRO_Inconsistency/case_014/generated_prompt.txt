The simulation will model 2D, incompressible, steady-state flow.
The governing equations are the Stokes equations, derived by neglecting the convective acceleration terms from the incompressible Navier-Stokes equations.
The simulation will be run using non-dimensional parameters.
The geometry is a rectangular domain containing a circular cylinder.
The cylinder has a non-dimensional diameter of 1.
The domain extends 20 diameters upstream and 40 diameters downstream from the cylinder's center.
The domain has a total height of 40 diameters, with the cylinder centered vertically.
The inlet boundary condition is a uniform velocity with a non-dimensional magnitude of 1.
The outlet boundary condition is a constant pressure of 0.
The top and bottom boundaries of the domain have a slip condition.
The surface of the cylinder has a no-slip boundary condition.
The fluid has a non-dimensional density of 1.
The fluid has a non-dimensional dynamic viscosity of 10.
These parameters result in a Reynolds number of 0.1 based on the cylinder diameter.
The simulation will be run until a steady-state solution is achieved.
