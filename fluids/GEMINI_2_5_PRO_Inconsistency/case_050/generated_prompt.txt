Compute the transient cooling curve for a solid sphere using the lumped-capacitance method.
The sphere is made of Aluminum 6061 with a radius of 0.1 meters.
The initial temperature of the sphere is 100 degrees Celsius.
The sphere cools in a surrounding fluid held at a constant temperature of 25 degrees Celsius.
The Biot number for the system is 0.05.
Use a density of 2700 kg/m^3 for the sphere.
Use a specific heat of 900 J/(kg·K) for the sphere.
Use a thermal conductivity of 167 W/(m·K) for the sphere.
The convective heat transfer coefficient is 250.5 W/(m^2·K).
Calculate the sphere's temperature over a time period from 0 to 1500 seconds.
The temperature at any time t is determined by the equation T(t) = T_fluid + (T_initial - T_fluid) * exp(-t/τ).
The thermal time constant τ is calculated as (ρ * V * c) / (h * A), where ρ is density, V is volume, c is specific heat, h is the convective heat transfer coefficient, and A is the surface area.
