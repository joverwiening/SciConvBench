Assume the water temperature is 20 degrees Celsius.
Use a water density of 998.2 kg/m^3.
Use a water dynamic viscosity of 1.002e-3 Pa·s.
The sphere has a diameter of 5 mm and a velocity of 0.5 m/s.
Calculate the Reynolds number using the formula Re = (density * velocity * diameter) / viscosity.
Since the calculated Reynolds number is greater than 1, the quadratic drag law will be used.
Calculate the drag coefficient using the Schiller and Naumann correlation: Cd = (24/Re) * (1 + 0.15 * Re^0.687).
Calculate the projected area of the sphere using the formula A = pi * (diameter/2)^2.
Calculate the drag force using the formula Fd = 0.5 * density * velocity^2 * Cd * A.
