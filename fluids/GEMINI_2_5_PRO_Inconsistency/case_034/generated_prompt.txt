The system consists of water flowing from a reservoir to a pipe discharge.
The reservoir surface is at an elevation of 0 meters.
The pipe discharges at an elevation of 5 meters.
The reservoir surface is open to the atmosphere.
The pipe discharges into the open atmosphere.
The velocity of the water at the reservoir surface is assumed to be zero.
The pipe is frictionless, resulting in zero head loss.
A pump adds 20 meters of head to the system.
There is no turbine in the system.
The goal is to calculate the exit velocity of the water from the pipe.
