The fluid is water.
The density of water is 1000 kg/m^3.
The bulk modulus of water is 2.2 GPa.
The pipeline is made of steel.
The Young's modulus of steel is 210 GPa.
The inner diameter of the pipeline is 0.1 meters.
The wall thickness of the pipeline is 5 millimeters.
The initial velocity of the water is 2 m/s.
The final velocity of the water is 0 m/s.
The change in velocity is 2 m/s.
Calculate the acoustic velocity 'a' in the water within the pipe.
The formula for acoustic velocity is a = 1 / sqrt( (ρ/K) + (ρ*D)/(E*t) ).
Calculate the pressure rise due to the valve closure.
The formula for pressure rise is Δp = ρ * a * ΔV.
This calculation treats the valve closure as instantaneous.
