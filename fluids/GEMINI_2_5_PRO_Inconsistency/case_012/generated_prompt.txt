Analyze the Couette flow of oil between two infinite parallel plates.
The fluid is oil with a dynamic viscosity of 0.10 Pa·s.
The plates are separated by a distance of 5 mm.
The bottom plate is stationary.
The top plate moves at a constant velocity of 2.0 m/s.
Use the steady, incompressible Navier-Stokes equations for the analysis.
Assume the pressure gradient in the direction of flow is zero.
Neglect the effects of gravity.
The objective is to determine the pressure variation across the gap between the plates.
