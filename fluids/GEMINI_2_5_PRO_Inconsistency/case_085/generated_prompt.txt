The simulation models a counterflow heat exchanger.
The hot fluid is water.
The cold fluid is water.
The inlet temperature of the hot fluid is 90 degrees Celsius.
The outlet temperature of the hot fluid is 60 degrees Celsius.
The inlet temperature of the cold fluid is 20 degrees Celsius.
The mass flow rate of the hot fluid is 1 kg/s.
The mass flow rate of the cold fluid is 1.5 kg/s.
The overall heat transfer coefficient is 1500 W/m^2.K.
The specific heat capacity of water is 4180 J/kg.K.
The simulation shall calculate the outlet temperature of the cold fluid based on an energy balance.
The simulation shall use the temperature differences ΔT1 = Th,in - Tc,out and ΔT2 = Th,out - Tc,in.
The simulation shall calculate the Log Mean Temperature Difference.
The simulation shall calculate the total heat transfer rate.
The simulation shall calculate the required heat exchanger surface area.
