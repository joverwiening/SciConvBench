Calculate the pressure difference between the inlet and outlet of a pipe using the Bernoulli equation.
The fluid in the pipe is water, with a density of 1000 kg/m³.
The pipe has a constant diameter.
The fluid velocity is constant throughout the pipe.
The outlet is 15 meters vertically higher than the inlet.
Use a value of 9.81 m/s² for the acceleration due to gravity.
Ignore frictional losses.
The final result will be the pressure at the inlet minus the pressure at the outlet.
