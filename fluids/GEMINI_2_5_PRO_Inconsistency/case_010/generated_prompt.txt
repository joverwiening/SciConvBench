The system is a flat wall.
The surface temperature of the wall is maintained at a constant 400 K.
The surrounding fluid has a temperature of 300 K.
The convection heat transfer coefficient between the wall and the fluid is 25 W/m^2·K.
The objective is to calculate the convective heat flux at the wall's surface.
The adiabatic condition for the wall is ignored.
Newton's Law of Cooling will be used for the calculation.
