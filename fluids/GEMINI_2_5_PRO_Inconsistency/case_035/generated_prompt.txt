The simulation calculates the pressure difference in a rotating tank of water.
The flow is an inviscid forced vortex.
The velocity profile is v_theta = omega * r.
The angular velocity omega is 10 radians per second.
The density of the water is 1000 kg/m^3.
The pressure difference is calculated between the radial position r1 = 0.1 meters and r2 = 0.3 meters.
The calculation uses the formula P2 - P1 = 0.5 * rho * omega^2 * (r2^2 - r1^2).
