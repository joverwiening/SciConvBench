Q: Bernoulli's equation assumes no energy loss, but a hydraulic jump is a high-energy-loss event. How should the energy loss across the jump be accounted for in the calculation?
A: You're right, energy is not conserved. Do not use Bernoulli's equation. You should use the momentum equation across the jump to calculate the downstream depth.
