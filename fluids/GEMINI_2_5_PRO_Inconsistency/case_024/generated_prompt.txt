The fluid is air, treated as an ideal gas.
The specific gas constant for air (R) is 287 J/(kg·K).
The ratio of specific heats for air (k) is 1.4.
The duct has a constant inner diameter of 0.1 meters.
The stagnation pressure at the inlet is 10 bar (1,000,000 Pa).
The stagnation temperature at the inlet is 293.15 K (20°C).
The static pressure at the duct outlet is 1 bar (100,000 Pa).
The flow process through the duct is modeled as isentropic.
The density of the air is variable and must be calculated using the Ideal Gas Law.
The mass flow rate shall be calculated based on choked flow conditions at the duct exit.
