The task is to compute the pressure difference for water flowing along a curved streamline.
The fluid density is 1000 kg/m^3.
The flow velocity is 10 m/s.
The radius of curvature is 5 m.
The pressure gradient normal to the streamline is calculated using the formula ∂p/∂n = (ρ * V^2) / R.
The pressure difference is computed across a normal distance of 0.10 m.
The pressure difference is approximated by the formula Δp = (∂p/∂n) * Δn.
The final computed pressure difference is 2000 Pa.
