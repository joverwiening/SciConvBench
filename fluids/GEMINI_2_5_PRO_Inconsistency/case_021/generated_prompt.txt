The task is to calculate the exit velocity of a gas expanding through a nozzle.
The process is an isothermal expansion.
The gas is air, treated as an ideal gas.
The specific gas constant for air to be used is 287 J/(kg·K).
The constant temperature of the gas is 298 K.
The inlet pressure is 10 bar.
The outlet pressure is 1 bar.
The inlet velocity is negligible and should be assumed to be 0 m/s.
The calculation must determine the exit velocity in meters per second.
