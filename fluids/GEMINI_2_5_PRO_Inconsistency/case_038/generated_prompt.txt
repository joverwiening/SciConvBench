Calculate the pressure rise in the pipeline due to water hammer.
Use the equation for rapid valve closure: Δp = ρ * a * ΔV.
The change in velocity (ΔV) is 2 m/s.
Assume the density of water (ρ) is 1000 kg/m³.
Assume the pressure wave speed (a) is 1200 m/s.
The valve closure time of 0.05 seconds is assumed to be rapid for the purposes of this calculation.
The pipeline length is not required for this calculation.
