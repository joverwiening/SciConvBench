The fluid is modeled as viscous.
A no-slip boundary condition is applied at the surface of the flat plate.
The flow is steady, two-dimensional, incompressible, and laminar.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 1.5 x 10^-5 m^2/s, corresponding to air at standard conditions.
The freestream velocity is 1 m/s.

The velocity profile will be determined using the Blasius boundary layer solution.
