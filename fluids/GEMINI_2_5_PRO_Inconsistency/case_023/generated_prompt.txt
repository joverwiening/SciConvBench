Calculate the terminal velocity of a spherical raindrop.
The raindrop has a diameter of 2 mm.
The density of the raindrop is 1000 kg/m^3.
The density of the surrounding air is 1.2 kg/m^3.
The drag coefficient for the raindrop is 0.5.
The acceleration due to gravity is 9.81 m/s^2.
The calculation must balance the force of gravity with the forces of buoyancy and aerodynamic drag.
