Calculate the pressure distribution P(r) inside a centrifuge as a function of radius r.
Use the formula P(r) = P1 + (1/2) * rho * ω² * (r² - r1²).
The centrifuge rotates at a constant angular velocity ω of 10,000 RPM.
The fluid has a constant density rho of 1000 kg/m³.
The inner radius of the fluid r1 is 0.05 meters.
The outer radius of the fluid r2 is 0.15 meters.
The pressure at the inner radius P1 is 0 Pa gauge pressure.
The calculation should cover the radial range from the inner radius to the outer radius.
