The governing equation is the steady-state 1D heat conduction equation d2T/dx2 = 0.
The domain is a one-dimensional slab of length 1 meter.
The coordinate system starts at x=0 on the left end and ends at x=1 on the right end.
The boundary condition at the left end (x=0) is a fixed temperature of 100 degrees Celsius.
The boundary condition at the right end (x=1) is a fixed temperature of 0 degrees Celsius.
The task is to solve for the temperature distribution T(x) along the slab.
