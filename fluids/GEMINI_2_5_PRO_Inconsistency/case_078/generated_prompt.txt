The thermodynamic cycle to be analyzed is the ideal Diesel cycle.
The working fluid is air, modeled as an ideal gas.
The specific heat ratio (gamma) of the air is 1.4.
The compression ratio (r) is 18.
The cutoff ratio (rc) is 2.
Calculate the thermal efficiency (eta) using the formula eta = 1 - (1/r^(gamma-1)) * (rc^gamma - 1) / (gamma * (rc - 1)).
The final output is the calculated thermal efficiency.
