The pressure drop is computed using the Darcy-Weisbach equation.
The equation form is Δp = f * (L/D) * (ρ * v^2 / 2).
The pipe length (L) is 30 meters.
The pipe diameter (D) is 0.1 meters.
The Fanning friction factor (f) is 0.005.
The fluid density (ρ) is assumed to be 1000 kg/m^3.
The fluid velocity (v) is assumed to be 2 m/s.
