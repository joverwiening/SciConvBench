Calculate the volumetric flow rate through a Venturi meter.
The governing equation is Q = Cd * A2 * sqrt(2 * Δp / (rho * (1 - beta^4))).
The fluid is water.
The density of the fluid (rho) is 1000 kg/m^3.
The main pipe inlet diameter is 0.1 meters.
The Venturi throat diameter is 0.05 meters.
The pressure difference (Δp) between the inlet and the throat is 10,000 Pascals.
The discharge coefficient (Cd) is 0.98.
The final output will be the volumetric flow rate (Q) in cubic meters per second.
