The task is to compute the Rayleigh number for natural convection.
The fluid is air.
The surface temperature is 80°C.
The ambient temperature is 20°C.
The characteristic length is 0.5 m.
The gravitational acceleration is 9.81 m/s^2.
The film temperature is calculated as the average of the surface and ambient temperatures (50°C).
All fluid properties are evaluated at the film temperature.
The thermal expansion coefficient is calculated as the reciprocal of the absolute film temperature in Kelvin (1 / 323.15 K).
The kinematic viscosity of air at the film temperature is 1.822 x 10^-5 m^2/s.
The thermal diffusivity of air at the film temperature is 2.593 x 10^-5 m^2/s.
The Rayleigh number is calculated using the formula Ra = g * beta * (Ts - T∞) * L^3 / (ν * α).
