The fluid is water.
The flow is laminar with a Reynolds number of 800.
The Prandtl number is 6.0, which corresponds to a water temperature of 28 degrees Celsius.
The thermal conductivity (k) of water at this temperature is 0.611 W/(m·K).
The pipe has a circular cross-section with an inner diameter (D) of 1 inch (0.0254 meters).
The flow is fully developed with a constant surface temperature boundary condition.
The Nusselt number (Nu) for this condition is 3.66.
Calculate the convective heat transfer coefficient (h) using the formula h = (Nu * k) / D.
