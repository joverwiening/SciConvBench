Calculate the stagnation temperature of a liquid water jet.
The initial static temperature of the water jet is 20 degrees Celsius (293.15 K).
The velocity of the water jet is 20 m/s.
Use the specific heat capacity at constant pressure (Cp) for liquid water, which is 4186 J/(kg·K).
The calculation will use the formula T0 = T + V^2 / (2 * Cp).
