The inlet relative velocity W1 is 150 m/s.
The inlet relative flow angle beta1 is 45 degrees.

The axial velocity Va is constant through the rotor.
Calculate the constant axial velocity Va using the inlet conditions W1 and beta1.
Calculate the outlet relative velocity W2 using the constant axial velocity Va and the outlet relative angle beta2.
Assume the absolute flow at the rotor inlet is purely axial, setting the absolute flow angle alpha1 to 0 degrees.
Derive the blade speed U from the inlet velocity triangle based on the preceding parameters.
Compute the degree of reaction for the rotor.
