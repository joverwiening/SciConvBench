Calculate the heat removed during a phase change process.
The substance is water.
The process is the condensation of saturated steam into saturated liquid water.
The temperature remains constant at 100 degrees Celsius.
The mass of the water is 1 kg.
Use the formula q = m * h_fg to calculate the heat removed.
The value for the latent heat of vaporization (h_fg) for water at 100 degrees Celsius is 2257 kJ/kg.
