The governing equation is the steady-state one-dimensional heat conduction equation, d2T/dx2 = 0.
The simulation domain is a slab of length 1.0 meter.
The coordinate system starts at x=0 on the left end and extends to x=1.0 on the right end.
The boundary condition at the left end (x=0) is a fixed temperature of 100 degrees Celsius.
The boundary condition at the right end (x=1.0) is a fixed temperature of 25 degrees Celsius.
Solve the equation to find the temperature distribution T(x) along the slab.
