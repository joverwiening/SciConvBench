The Francis turbine runner is installed with its centerline 2.0 meters above the tailrace level.
The suction head (Hs) is +2.0 m.
The atmospheric head (Ha) is 10.3 m.
The vapor head (Hv) is 0.3 m.
The net head on the turbine is denoted by H.
The cavitation factor (sigma) is calculated using the formula: sigma = (Ha - Hv - Hs) / H.
Substituting the given values into the formula yields: sigma = (10.3 - 0.3 - 2.0) / H.
The expression for the cavitation factor simplifies to: sigma = 8.0 / H.
Since the net head (H) must be a positive value for the turbine to operate, the cavitation factor (sigma) will always be positive.
A positive cavitation factor indicates that the pressure at the runner is maintained above the vapor pressure, thus preventing cavitation for any operational head.
