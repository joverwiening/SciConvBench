Air at Mach 2.0 enters a frictionless converging nozzle with area ratio A2/A1 = 0.7. Assuming quasi-1D isentropic flow with gamma=1.4 and constant stagnation temperature, use the area–Mach relation on the supersonic branch to calculate the exit Mach number and exit velocity. (For supersonic flow, a decrease in area leads to deceleration, i.e., Mach number decreases.)
