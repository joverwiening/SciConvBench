Inlet diameter (D1) is 50 mm.
Outlet diameter (D2) is 100 mm.
Volumetric flow rate (Q) is 0.010 m³/s.
Inlet velocity (V1) is 5.09 m/s.
Outlet velocity (V2) is 1.27 m/s.
Water density (ρ) is 1000 kg/m³.
Inlet elevation (z1) is 0 m.
Outlet elevation (z2) is 0 m.
Inlet absolute pressure (P1) is 101,325 Pa.

Calculate the pressure rise (pressure recovery) across the expansion using Bernoulli's equation:
P2 = P1 + (1/2)ρ(V1² - V2²)

Assume standard gravity (g = 9.81 m/s²) and neglect minor head losses.

Provide the calculated outlet pressure (P2) and pressure recovery value.
