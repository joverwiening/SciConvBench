Specification for Wall Shear Stress Calculation in Steady Inviscid Oil Flow:

Fluid Properties:
Oil viscosity (mu) = 0.1 Pa·s
Plate separation = 0.01 meters
Flow velocity = 0.05 m/s
Velocity profile = Parabolic (standard Poiseuille/Couette flow profile)

Calculation Method:
Compute wall shear stress using the equation tau = mu du/dy
Use the velocity gradient at the wall to determine shear stress
Assume symmetric parabolic velocity distribution between parallel plates
Calculate shear stress for both upper and lower walls of the channel

Computational Approach:
Derive velocity gradient by differentiating parabolic velocity profile
Evaluate velocity gradient at y = 0 (wall surface)
Multiply velocity gradient by fluid viscosity to obtain wall shear stress
