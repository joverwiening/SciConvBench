Compute the pressure drop in a 30 m long pipe with 0.1 m diameter using the Darcy–Weisbach equation.
Use the following parameters:
Pipe length (L) = 30 m
Pipe diameter (D) = 0.1 m
Fanning friction factor (f) = 0.005
Fluid density (ρ) = 998 kg/m³
Fluid velocity (v) = 2 m/s
Fluid viscosity (μ) = 0.001 Pa·s
Apply the equation: Δp = f (L/D) (ρ v^2/2)
Calculate and report the resulting pressure drop in Pascals (Pa).
