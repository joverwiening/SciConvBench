The problem requires calculating velocities in a constant-area pipe with the following specifications:

Inlet volumetric flow rate is 0.01 m³/s.
Outlet volumetric flow rate is 0.02 m³/s.
Pipe cross-sectional area is 0.00785 m².

Inlet velocity calculation: v1 = Q1 / A = 0.01 m³/s / 0.00785 m² = 1.27 m/s
Outlet velocity calculation: v2 = Q2 / A = 0.02 m³/s / 0.00785 m² = 2.55 m/s

The velocity at the inlet section is 1.27 m/s.
The velocity at the outlet section is 2.55 m/s.

Note: The velocity increases due to conservation of mass in an incompressible flow with a constant cross-sectional area, which requires higher velocity to accommodate the increased volumetric flow rate.
