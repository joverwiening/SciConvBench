Flow Conditions:

Computational Method:
Apply thin-airfoil theory with the Kutta–Joukowski theorem
Assume completely irrotational flow
Use Kutta condition to compute circulation Γ

Solve the problem by determining the circulation Γ using thin-airfoil theory principles
Compute aerodynamic characteristics based on the calculated circulation
Ensure the solution adheres to the Kutta condition at the trailing edge of the airfoil
