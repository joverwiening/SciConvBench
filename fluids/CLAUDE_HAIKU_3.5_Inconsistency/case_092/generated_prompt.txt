Compute radiative heat loss using the following specification:

Emissivity (ε) = 0.8
Surface temperature (Ts) = 400 K
Surrounding temperature (Tsur) = 300 K
Surface area (A) = 1 m²
Use Stefan-Boltzmann constant (σ) = 5.67 × 10^-8 W/(m²·K^4)
Calculate radiative heat transfer using equation: q = ε * σ * A * (Ts^4 - Tsur^4)
Report the final heat transfer rate in watts (W)
