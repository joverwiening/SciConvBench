The specification for calculating the hydrodynamic entrance length for turbulent air flow in a smooth pipe is:

Fluid is air.
Pipe diameter is 40 mm.
Reynolds number is 50,000.
Flow is turbulent.
Calculate entrance length using the formula Le = 0.06 × Re × D.
The calculated entrance length represents the distance from the pipe inlet where the velocity profile becomes fully developed.
The result will be in the range of 10D to 60D, which is typical for turbulent pipe flow.
Entrance length calculation assumes a smooth pipe with uniform inlet conditions.
