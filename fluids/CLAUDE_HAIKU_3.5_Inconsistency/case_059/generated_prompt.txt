The rectangular open channel problem will be solved with the following specifications:

Discharge per unit width (q) is 1.0 m^2/s.
Initial depth (y) is 1.0 m.
Initial velocity (V) is 0.5 m/s.
Specific gravity will be assumed as that of water (1.0).
Channel slope will be assumed horizontal (0%).
Gravitational acceleration (g) will be 9.81 m/s².
The objective is to find the critical depth of the channel.
Calculations will use standard hydraulic engineering principles for critical flow determination.
The solution method will involve critical flow equations and dimensional analysis.
