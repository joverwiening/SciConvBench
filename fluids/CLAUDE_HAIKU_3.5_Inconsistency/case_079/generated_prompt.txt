A Carnot refrigeration cycle operates between TH = 300 K and TL = 250 K.
The cycle extracts 5 kW of heat from the low-temperature reservoir.
The Coefficient of Performance (COP) is 20.
Calculate the required work input to the refrigeration cycle.
Compute the work using the relationship between COP, QL, and W.
The work will be determined by W = QL / COP.
The final result should express the work input in kilowatts (kW).
