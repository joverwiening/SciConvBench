The specification for computing hydrostatic forces on a quarter-cylindrical gate is as follows:

The gate has a radius R = 2.0 m and width b = 1.0 m.
The gate is positioned with its curved surface vertically oriented.
The top edge of the gate is at the free water surface.
The total water depth will be 2.0 m to fully submerge the vertical projection.
Compute the horizontal hydrostatic force using the vertical projection of the curved surface.
Compute the vertical hydrostatic force using the vertical projection of the curved surface.
Use standard hydrostatic force calculation methods for the projection areas.
Assume water density and gravitational acceleration as standard values.
