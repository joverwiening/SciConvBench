Solve the steady-state heat conduction problem for a plane wall with the following specifications:

Initial temperature at x=0 is 350 K.
Boundary condition at x=0 is T(0) = 350 K.
Boundary condition at x=L is dT/dx = 0 (insulated end).
Thermal conductivity (k) = 50 W/m·K (typical value for steel).
Wall length (L) = 0.5 meters.
Heat generation rate (Q) = 0 W/m³ (no internal heat generation).
Solve the steady-state heat conduction equation to determine the temperature distribution across the wall.
