I'll use the standard water vapor pressure at 20°C, which is approximately 0.023 bar (2.3 kPa) or 0.23 m of water column height.

To calculate the cavitation factor sigma (σ), I'll use Thoma's equation:
σ = (Ha - Hv - hs - hloss) / H

Where:
- Ha (Available head) = 10.3 m
- Hv (Vertical head) = 0.3 m
- hs (Suction head) = Vapor pressure head = 0.23 m
- hloss (Head losses) = 0.309 m
- H (Total head) = 10.3 m

Calculation:
σ = (10.3 - 0.3 - 0.23 - 0.309) / 10.3
  = 9.461 / 10.3
  = 0.918

Since σ > 0.1 (typical cavitation threshold), cavitation is indeed impossible for this head.
