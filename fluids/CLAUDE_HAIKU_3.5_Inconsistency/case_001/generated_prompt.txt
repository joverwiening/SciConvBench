The task is to calculate the stagnation pressure of air flowing through a duct using the incompressible Bernoulli equation.

Input conditions:
Air velocity is 250 m/s.
Static pressure is 101.3 kPa.
Temperature is 300 K.
Air density is 1.176 kg/m³.

Calculation method:
Use the incompressible Bernoulli equation: P0 = P + 0.5 * rho * v^2.

Stagnation pressure calculation steps:
Substitute the known values into the equation.
P0 = 101.3 kPa + 0.5 * 1.176 kg/m³ * (250 m/s)^2.
Compute the result to determine the stagnation pressure.

The final output will be the calculated stagnation pressure in kPa.
