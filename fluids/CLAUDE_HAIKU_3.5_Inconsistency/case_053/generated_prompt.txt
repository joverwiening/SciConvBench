The lumped-capacitance method will be used to compute the transient cooling curve for a solid aluminum sphere with the following parameters:

Biot number (Bi) = 5
Specific heat capacity (c) = 900 J/(kg·K)
Density (ρ) = 2700 kg/m³
Initial temperature (T0) = 100°C
Ambient temperature (Ta) = 20°C

The simulation will track the sphere's temperature change over time using the lumped-capacitance method, assuming uniform temperature distribution within the sphere due to the low Biot number.

The cooling curve will be calculated using the exponential cooling equation: T(t) = Ta + (T0 - Ta) * exp(-ht/ρcL)

Where:
h is the convective heat transfer coefficient
t is time
L is the characteristic length of the sphere

The goal is to determine how the sphere's temperature decreases from its initial temperature to the ambient temperature over time.
