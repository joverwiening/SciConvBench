The thermal stress calculation will be performed for a steel rod under fully constrained conditions.
The temperature change is 50 K.
The coefficient of thermal expansion (alpha) is 11.5 × 10^-6 /K.
The Young's modulus (E) is 200 GPa.
Thermal stress (σ) will be calculated using the formula: σ = E * alpha * ΔT.
The calculation will result in the thermal stress experienced by the fully constrained steel rod.
The stress will represent the internal force generated by preventing the rod's natural thermal expansion.
