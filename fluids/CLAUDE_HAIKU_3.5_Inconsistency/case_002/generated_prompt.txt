Reynolds number is 99,800 (turbulent flow regime).

Calculation Method:
Use Blasius equation for friction factor in smooth pipes.
Apply Bernoulli's equation to determine pressure drop.
Consider turbulent flow correlation for friction factor calculation.

Computational Steps:
Determine Blasius friction factor using turbulent flow correlation.
Calculate head loss using Darcy-Weisbach equation.
Compute pressure drop between pipe inlet and outlet.

Constraints:
