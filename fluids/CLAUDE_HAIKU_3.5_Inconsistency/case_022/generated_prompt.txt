The system will calculate the pressure difference across a curved water streamline using the following specification:

Fluid properties: Water with density (rho) = 1000 kg/m^3
Flow speed (V) = 10 m/s
Radius of curvature (R) = 5 m
Normal distance (Δn) = 0.10 m

Calculate pressure difference using normal momentum balance equation:
dP/dn = rho V^2 / R
Pressure gradient is directed outward from the center of curvature.
Compute the pressure difference by integrating the pressure gradient across the specified normal distance.
The result will represent the pressure variation between the inner and outer sides of the streamline.
