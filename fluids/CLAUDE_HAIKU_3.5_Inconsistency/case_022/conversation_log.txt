Q: What is the specific equation or method you would like me to use to calculate the pressure difference across the curved streamline?
A: Use the normal momentum balance equation: dP/dn = rho V^2 / R, with n directed outward from the center of curvature. This will help you calculate the pressure gradient magnitude and pressure difference across the specified normal distance.
