Q: The problem states "fully developed laminar flow" (which has a parabolic velocity profile) but then says "assume the velocity profile is uniform (plug flow)."

Which velocity profile should be used to compute the kinetic-energy correction factor alpha: the actual parabolic (laminar) profile, or the uniform (plug flow) profile?
