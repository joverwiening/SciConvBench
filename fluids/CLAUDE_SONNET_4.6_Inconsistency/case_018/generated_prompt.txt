Compute the kinetic-energy correction factor alpha for fully developed laminar flow in a circular pipe.

The pipe is circular with diameter D = 20 mm.
The mean velocity V = 1.0 m/s.
The fluid is water.
Use the parabolic (fully developed laminar) velocity profile: u(r) = 2V(1 - (r/R)^2), where R is the pipe radius and r is the radial coordinate.
The kinetic-energy correction factor alpha is defined as alpha = (1/A) * integral over A of (u/V)^3 dA.
Substitute the parabolic profile into the definition of alpha.
Express the area element in polar coordinates as dA = 2*pi*r dr, and integrate from r = 0 to r = R.
Evaluate the integral analytically.
The expected result is alpha = 2.
Report the final value of alpha and show the step-by-step integration leading to alpha = 2.
