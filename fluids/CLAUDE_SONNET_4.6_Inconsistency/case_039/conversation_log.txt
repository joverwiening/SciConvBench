Q: The Colebrook equation is valid for turbulent flow, but Re=800 is in the laminar regime (Re < 2300), where the Colebrook equation does not apply.

Are you sure you want to use Re=800, or did you mean a turbulent Reynolds number (e.g., Re=80,000)?
A: Yes, Re=800 is correct. Since the flow is laminar, use f = 64/Re instead of the Colebrook equation.
