Model a compressible isentropic flow of air discharging through an orifice with a pressure ratio of 6:1 (upstream to downstream).

Assume the working fluid is air with a ratio of specific heats gamma = 1.4 and a gas constant R = 287 J/(kg·K).

Assume upstream stagnation pressure P0 = 600,000 Pa (6 bar absolute).

Assume downstream static pressure P_back = 100,000 Pa (1 bar absolute), giving the specified 6:1 pressure ratio.

Assume upstream stagnation temperature T0 = 293.15 K (20 degrees Celsius).

Assume an orifice diameter of 10 mm (0.01 m), giving an orifice throat area A = pi/4 * (0.01)^2 = 7.854e-5 m^2.

Assume a discharge coefficient Cd = 0.61, which is standard for a sharp-edged orifice.

Perform a choking check by computing the critical pressure ratio: P_critical = P0 * (2 / (gamma + 1))^(gamma / (gamma - 1)).

For gamma = 1.4, the critical pressure ratio evaluates to P_critical = P0 * (0.5283), giving P_critical = 316,980 Pa.

If P_back is less than or equal to P_critical, the flow is choked and the throat Mach number is set to M = 1.

If P_back is greater than P_critical, the flow is unchoked and the throat Mach number M is solved from the isentropic relation P_back / P0 = (1 + (gamma - 1)/2 * M^2)^(-(gamma / (gamma - 1))).

Since P_back = 100,000 Pa is less than P_critical = 316,980 Pa, the flow is choked at M = 1.

Compute the throat static temperature at choked conditions: T_throat = T0 * (2 / (gamma + 1)) = 293.15 * (2 / 2.4) = 244.29 K.

Compute the throat static pressure at choked conditions: P_throat = P0 * (2 / (gamma + 1))^(gamma / (gamma - 1)) = 316,980 Pa.

Compute the throat air density: rho_throat = P_throat / (R * T_throat).

Compute the speed of sound at the throat: a_throat = sqrt(gamma * R * T_throat).

Compute the ideal mass flow rate: m_dot_ideal = rho_throat * a_throat * A.

Apply the discharge coefficient to get the actual mass flow rate: m_dot_actual = Cd * m_dot_ideal.

Report the following outputs: the choking status (choked or unchoked), throat Mach number, throat static pressure, throat static temperature, throat density, throat speed of sound, ideal mass flow rate, and actual mass flow rate.
