The problem is steady-state one-dimensional heat conduction in a plane wall.
The wall spans from x = 0 to x = L, with L = 1 m.
There is no heat generation within the wall.
The boundary condition at x = 0 is a specified temperature: T(0) = 350 K.
The boundary condition at x = L is an insulated (zero flux) condition: dT/dx = 0 at x = L.
The governing equation is d²T/dx² = 0.
Integrate the governing equation twice to obtain the general solution T(x) = C1*x + C2.
Apply dT/dx = 0 at x = L to get C1 = 0.
Apply T(0) = 350 K to get C2 = 350.
The final solution is T(x) = 350 K for all x in [0, 1].
Report the solution as a constant temperature profile: T(x) = 350 K everywhere in the wall.
Verify that both boundary conditions are satisfied: T(0) = 350 K (satisfied) and dT/dx = 0 at x = 1 m (satisfied, since the derivative of a constant is zero).
