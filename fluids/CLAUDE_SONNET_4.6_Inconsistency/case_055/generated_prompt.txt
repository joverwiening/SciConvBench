Compute the Nusselt number using the standard formula Nu = (h · L) / k.
Use h = 40 W/m²·K, L = 0.10 m, and k = 0.026 W/m·K.
Calculate the numerator as h · L = 40 × 0.10 = 4.0 W/m·K.
Divide the numerator by k: Nu = 4.0 / 0.026.
