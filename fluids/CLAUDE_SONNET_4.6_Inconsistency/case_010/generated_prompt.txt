The wall surface is maintained at a fixed temperature Ts = 400 K.
The surrounding fluid temperature is T∞ = 300 K.
The convection coefficient is h = 25 W/m²·K.
The wall is non-adiabatic; heat transfer occurs between the wall and the fluid.
Calculate the convective heat flux at the surface using Newton's law of cooling: q'' = h × (Ts - T∞).
Substitute the values: q'' = 25 × (400 - 300).
Compute the temperature difference: 400 - 300 = 100 K.
Compute the heat flux: q'' = 25 × 100 = 2500 W/m².
The convective heat flux at the surface is q'' = 2500 W/m².
The positive value indicates heat is transferred from the wall to the fluid.
