The task is to verify the stagnation-point pressure coefficient using Bernoulli's equation for incompressible flow.

The pressure coefficient is defined as Cp = (p - p_inf) / (0.5 * rho * V_inf^2).

At a stagnation point, the local velocity is zero by definition.

Apply Bernoulli's equation for steady, incompressible, inviscid flow along a streamline: p_inf + 0.5 * rho * V_inf^2 = p_stag + 0.5 * rho * V_stag^2.

Substitute V_stag = 0 into Bernoulli's equation: p_inf + 0.5 * rho * V_inf^2 = p_stag.

Rearrange to find the pressure difference: p_stag - p_inf = 0.5 * rho * V_inf^2.

Substitute this result into the Cp definition: Cp = (0.5 * rho * V_inf^2) / (0.5 * rho * V_inf^2).

Simplify: Cp = 1.0.

The correct stagnation-point pressure coefficient for incompressible inviscid flow is Cp = 1.0.

A value of Cp exceeding 1.0 cannot occur in standard incompressible potential flow and would indicate one of the following: an error in the Cp definition used, the presence of compressibility effects, or a measurement or calculation error.
