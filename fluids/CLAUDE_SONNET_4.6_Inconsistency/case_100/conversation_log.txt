Q: The pressure coefficient Cp is defined as (p - p_inf) / (0.5 * rho * V_inf^2).

At a stagnation point, the velocity is zero by definition.

Applying Bernoulli's equation for incompressible flow:

p_inf + 0.5 * rho * V_inf^2 = p_stag + 0.5 * rho * 0^2

This gives:

p_stag - p_inf = 0.5 * rho * V_inf^2

Substituting into the Cp definition:

Cp = (0.5 * rho * V_inf^2) / (0.5 * rho * V_inf^2) = 1.0

The correct stagnation-point pressure coefficient for incompressible flow is **Cp = 1.0**, not 1.5.

The value Cp = 1.5 is incorrect. It does not follow from Bernoulli's equation. A value greater than 1.0 can occur in compressible flow or in certain 3D flow configurations (e.g., flow impinging on a surface with added effects), but for standard incompressible potential flow around a body, Bernoulli strictly gives Cp = 1.0 at the stagnation point.
