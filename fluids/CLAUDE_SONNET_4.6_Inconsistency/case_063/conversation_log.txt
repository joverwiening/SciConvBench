Q: The Froude number for a rectangular channel is Fr = V / sqrt(g * y), where V is velocity and y is the depth.

You stated the flow is critical (Fr = 1), but the given velocity and depth correspond to Fr = 0.2. These two conditions are contradictory — a single pair of (V, y) cannot simultaneously give Fr = 1 and Fr = 0.2.

Could you clarify what you mean: do you want to find the critical depth that has the **same specific energy** as the given flow state where Fr = 0.2?
A: The given state (Fr=0.2) is NOT critical — don't label it as such. Use the given V and y to compute the discharge per unit width q = V * y, then find the critical depth using yc = (q²/g)^(1/3).
