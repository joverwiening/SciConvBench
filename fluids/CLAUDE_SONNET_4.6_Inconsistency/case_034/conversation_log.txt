Q: The problem states the pipe is "frictionless with no pump or turbine," but then asks to apply Bernoulli "including a pump head of 20 m." These two conditions directly contradict each other.

Which condition should be used: no pump (frictionless pipe only), or include a pump head of 20 m?
A: Include a pump head of 20 m. The pipe is frictionless (no losses), but there is a pump adding 20 m of head.
