Apply the Bernoulli equation between the reservoir surface (point 1) and the pipe exit (point 2).
The reservoir is at elevation z1 = 0 m.
The discharge exit is at elevation z2 = 5 m.
The pipe is frictionless, so head loss hL = 0.
A pump adds a pump head of hp = 20 m.
Assume the reservoir surface is large, so the inlet velocity V1 ≈ 0 m/s.
Assume the pressure at the reservoir surface is atmospheric: P1 = Patm.
Assume the pressure at the pipe exit is also atmospheric: P2 = Patm.
Since P1 = P2 = Patm, the pressure terms cancel in the Bernoulli equation.
The Bernoulli equation with pump head is: z1 + V1²/2g + hp = z2 + V2²/2g + hL.
Substitute known values: 0 + 0 + 20 = 5 + V2²/2g + 0.
Rearrange to: V2²/2g = 20 - 5 = 15 m.
Solve for V2: V2 = sqrt(2 × 9.81 × 15).
Calculate: V2 = sqrt(294.3) ≈ 17.15 m/s.
Use g = 9.81 m/s².
The exit velocity V2 is approximately 17.15 m/s.
