Compute the pressure drop per unit length (ΔP/L) for air flowing through a packed bed using the Ergun equation.

Use the Ergun equation: ΔP/L = [150·μ·U·(1−ε)²] / [d_p²·ε³] + [1.75·ρ·U²·(1−ε)] / [d_p·ε³]

Use a porosity ε = 0.40.

Use a particle diameter d_p = 0.005 m (5 mm converted to SI units).

Assume standard atmospheric conditions: temperature = 20°C, pressure = 1 atm (101325 Pa).

Use air density ρ = 1.204 kg/m³ at standard conditions.

Use air dynamic viscosity μ = 1.825 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s at standard conditions.

Assume a superficial velocity U = 0.1 m/s, which is a typical representative value for gas flow through packed beds in engineering practice.

Compute the viscous (first) term and the inertial (second) term of the Ergun equation separately.

Sum both terms to obtain the total pressure drop per unit length in units of Pa/m.
