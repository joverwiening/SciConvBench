Calculate the Nusselt number for turbulent water flow in a smooth pipe using the Dittus-Boelter equation.

The pipe diameter is 40 mm.

The Reynolds number is Re = 50,000.

The Prandtl number is Pr = 5.

The flow regime is turbulent (Re = 50,000 >> 2300), confirming the Dittus-Boelter equation is appropriate.

The formula to use is Nu = 0.023 · Re^0.8 · Pr^n.

Use n = 0.4 because the fluid is being heated.

Step 1: Compute Re^0.8.
Re = 50,000.
Re^0.8 = 50,000^0.8.
Calculate: 50,000^0.8 ≈ 1,906.15.

Step 2: Compute Pr^0.4.
Pr = 5.
Pr^0.4 = 5^0.4.
Calculate: 5^0.4 ≈ 1.9037.

Step 3: Compute Nu.
Nu = 0.023 · 1,906.15 · 1.9037.
Nu = 0.023 · 3,629.2.
Nu ≈ 83.47.

The Nusselt number for turbulent water flow in the 40 mm diameter smooth pipe at Re = 50,000 and Pr = 5 under heating conditions is Nu ≈ 83.5.

The pipe diameter of 40 mm is not needed for the Nusselt number calculation itself but would be used subsequently to find the convective heat transfer coefficient h = Nu · k / D, if thermal conductivity k of water is provided.
