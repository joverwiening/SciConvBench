Q: The formula Nu = 3.66 is for **laminar flow** (used when Re < 2300) with constant wall temperature boundary conditions.

At Re = 50,000, the flow is fully **turbulent**, so Nu = 3.66 would give a physically incorrect result.

Which turbulent flow correlation would you like to use for this calculation?

For example:
- Dittus-Boelter: Nu = 0.023 · Re^0.8 · Pr^n
- Gnielinski: Nu = (f/8)(Re−1000)Pr / [1 + 12.7√(f/8)(Pr^(2/3)−1)]
- Petukhov, or another specific formula?
A: Use the Dittus-Boelter equation: Nu = 0.023 · Re^0.8 · Pr^n, with n = 0.4 (heating of the fluid).
