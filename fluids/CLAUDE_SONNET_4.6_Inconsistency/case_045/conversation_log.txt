Q: The fluid described is **not** a Newtonian fluid — it has a yield stress (τ_y = 20 Pa), which is the defining characteristic of a **Bingham plastic** (or similar viscoplastic model), not a Newtonian fluid.

A Newtonian fluid has zero yield stress and follows τ = μ · (du/dy) only.

A Bingham plastic follows τ = τ_y + μ_p · (du/dy), which uses both the yield stress and plastic viscosity you provided.

Which model should be used for the computation?
A: It's a Bingham plastic. Use τ = τ_y + μ_p · γ̇.
