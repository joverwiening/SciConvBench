The fluid is a Bingham plastic.
The yield stress is tau_y = 20 Pa.
The plastic viscosity is mu_p = 0.05 Pa·s.
The shear rate is gamma_dot = 50 1/s.
Use the Bingham plastic constitutive equation: tau = tau_y + mu_p * gamma_dot.
Substitute the values: tau = 20 + (0.05 * 50).
Compute the viscous term: 0.05 * 50 = 2.5 Pa.
Add the yield stress: tau = 20 + 2.5 = 22.5 Pa.
The shear stress at a shear rate of 50 1/s is 22.5 Pa.
Since the computed shear stress (22.5 Pa) exceeds the yield stress (20 Pa), the fluid is indeed flowing, and the result is physically valid.
