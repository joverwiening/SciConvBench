The task is to compute the Rayleigh number for natural convection from a vertical plate using air properties at the film temperature.

Given conditions are: surface temperature Ts = 80°C, ambient temperature T∞ = 20°C, characteristic length L = 0.5 m.

Compute the film temperature as Tf = (Ts + T∞) / 2 = (80 + 20) / 2 = 50°C.

Evaluate the thermal expansion coefficient as beta = 1 / Tf_C where Tf_C is the film temperature in Celsius, so beta = 1 / 50 = 0.02 °C⁻¹.

Compute the temperature difference as ΔT = Ts - T∞ = 80 - 20 = 60°C.

Use standard air property table values at 50°C: kinematic viscosity ν = 17.95 × 10⁻⁶ m²/s and thermal diffusivity α = 25.45 × 10⁻⁶ m²/s.

Apply the Rayleigh number formula: Ra = g × beta × ΔT × L³ / (ν × α).

Use g = 9.81 m/s².

Substitute values: Ra = 9.81 × 0.02 × 60 × (0.5)³ / (17.95 × 10⁻⁶ × 25.45 × 10⁻⁶).

Compute the numerator: 9.81 × 0.02 × 60 × 0.125 = 1.4715.

Compute the denominator: 17.95 × 10⁻⁶ × 25.45 × 10⁻⁶ = 456.8275 × 10⁻¹² = 4.568275 × 10⁻¹⁰.

Compute Ra = 1.4715 / 4.568275 × 10⁻¹⁰ ≈ 3.22 × 10⁹.

Report the final Rayleigh number as Ra ≈ 3.22 × 10⁹.

Note that this value exceeds 10⁹, indicating the boundary layer flow is in the turbulent regime.
