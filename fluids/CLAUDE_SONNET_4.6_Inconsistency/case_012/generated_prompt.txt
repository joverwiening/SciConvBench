The flow is simple Couette flow of an incompressible Newtonian oil between two infinite parallel plates.
The dynamic viscosity of the oil is mu = 0.10 Pa·s.
The gap between the plates is h = 5 mm (0.005 m).
The bottom plate at y = 0 is stationary (u = 0).
The top plate at y = h moves at U = 2.0 m/s in the x-direction.
The flow is steady, laminar, incompressible, and fully developed.
Gravity is neglected entirely.
The plates are infinite in x and z, so all flow variables depend only on y.
Use the steady incompressible Navier-Stokes equations in Cartesian coordinates.
Apply the x-momentum equation to derive the velocity profile u(y).

Show all steps of the derivation explicitly.
From the continuity equation for incompressible flow, since the plates are infinite and flow is fully developed, the wall-normal velocity component v = 0 everywhere.
Substitute v = 0 and the condition that all partial derivatives with respect to x and z are zero into the y-momentum Navier-Stokes equation.
The y-momentum equation reduces to dP/dy = 0, showing that pressure does not vary across the gap in the wall-normal direction.
State explicitly that pressure is constant across the gap (no pressure gradient in y) for simple Couette flow with gravity neglected.
From the x-momentum equation, with no imposed streamwise pressure gradient (dP/dx = 0) and no body forces, derive that d²u/dy² = 0.
Integrate twice to obtain the linear velocity profile u(y) = U times (y divided by h).
