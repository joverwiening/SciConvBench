Solve for the oblique shock wave angle beta for a wedge angle theta = 25 degrees at Mach number M = 3.

Use the full theta-beta-Mach relation: tan(theta) = 2 * cot(beta) * (M^2 * sin^2(beta) - 1) / (M^2 * (gamma + cos(2*beta)) + 2).

Use the standard value of gamma = 1.4 (air).

Solve the theta-beta-Mach equation numerically or iteratively for beta, given theta = 25 deg and M = 3.
