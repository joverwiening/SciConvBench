Compute the Reynolds number for water flowing in a circular pipe.
The pipe diameter is D = 0.10 m.
The mean flow velocity is V = 2.0 m/s.
The hydraulic diameter is Dh = D = 0.10 m, derived from Dh = 4A/P for a circular pipe.
Use the kinematic viscosity of water at standard conditions (20°C), ν = 1.004 × 10⁻⁶ m²/s.
Apply the Reynolds number formula Re = V × Dh / ν.
Substitute the values: Re = (2.0 m/s × 0.10 m) / (1.004 × 10⁻⁶ m²/s).
Calculate and report the resulting Reynolds number.
State whether the flow is laminar (Re < 2300), transitional (2300 ≤ Re ≤ 4000), or turbulent (Re > 4000).
