Q: The given depth y = 1.0 m and velocity V = 0.5 m/s imply q = V × y = 0.5 m²/s, but the stated discharge is q = 1.0 m²/s. Which value of q should be used to find the critical depth: q = 1.0 m²/s (the stated discharge) or q = 0.5 m²/s (implied by the given depth and velocity)?
A: Use q = 1.0 m²/s.
