The fluid is air flowing turbulently in a smooth pipe.
The Reynolds number is Re = 50,000.
The pipe diameter is D = 40 mm = 0.04 m.
The formula for hydrodynamic entrance length is Le = 0.06 × Re × D.
Substitute the values into the formula: Le = 0.06 × 50,000 × 0.04.
Calculate step one: 0.06 × 50,000 = 3,000.
Calculate step two: 3,000 × 0.04 = 120.
The hydrodynamic entrance length is Le = 120 m.
Note that this formula Le = 0.06 Re D is typically used for laminar flow, where the coefficient 0.06 applies to the laminar entrance length correlation.
For turbulent flow, the commonly accepted correlation is Le = 4.4 × Re^(1/6) × D, which gives a much shorter entrance length.
Apply the turbulent correlation as a comparison: Le = 4.4 × (50,000)^(1/6) × 0.04.
Calculate (50,000)^(1/6): take the sixth root of 50,000, which equals approximately 5.85.
Calculate: Le = 4.4 × 5.85 × 0.04 = 1.03 m.
