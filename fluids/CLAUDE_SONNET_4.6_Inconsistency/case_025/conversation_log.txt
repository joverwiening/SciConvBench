To resolve this contradiction, which approach would you like to take?

Option A: Keep Kn = 5 but use the **physically correct method** (e.g., DSMC, Boltzmann-based model, or a slip-flow model with high-order slip corrections).

Option B: Keep the **continuum no-slip Navier–Stokes** framework but reduce the Knudsen number to a valid range (Kn ≪ 0.001) by adjusting the channel size, pressure, or gas conditions.

Which option do you prefer?
A: Option A. The task is to identify that Kn = 5 is in the transition-to-free-molecular regime and explain that continuum no-slip Navier–Stokes is inappropriate here. The correct approach is kinetic theory or DSMC, not continuum no-slip NS.
