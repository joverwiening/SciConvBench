Calculate the drag coefficient of a sphere at a Reynolds number of Re = 10^5 using experimental drag curve data.

The flow regime at Re = 10^5 is in the Newton's Law (turbulent boundary layer, pre-drag-crisis) regime, where experimental data shows Cd is approximately constant.

Use the experimentally established value of Cd ≈ 0.5 for a smooth sphere at Re = 10^5, taken from the standard drag curve for spheres before the drag crisis.

Note that the drag crisis occurs at approximately Re ≈ 3×10^5 to 5×10^5, so Re = 10^5 is confirmed to be in the pre-drag-crisis regime.

Report the result as Cd ≈ 0.5 for a smooth sphere at Re = 10^5.

State clearly that Stokes' Law (Cd = 24/Re) is not applicable here, as it is only valid for Re << 1 (creeping flow), and applying it at Re = 10^5 would yield a physically meaningless result of Cd ≈ 0.00024.
