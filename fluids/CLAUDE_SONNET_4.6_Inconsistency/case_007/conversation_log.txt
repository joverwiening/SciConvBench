Q: Is the channel horizontal (zero slope), and should I treat the channel bed as the datum for Bernoulli's equation?
A: The channel is horizontal. However, do not use Bernoulli's equation — use the momentum equation (specific force/momentum function) to find the sequent depth y2, since energy is not conserved across a hydraulic jump.
