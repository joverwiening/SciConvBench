The working fluid is air treated as a calorically perfect ideal gas.
Use a ratio of specific heats (gamma) of 1.4 and a gas constant R of 287 J/(kg·K).
The static pressure is 101.3 kPa, the static temperature is 300 K, and the flow velocity is 250 m/s.
Calculate the speed of sound using a = sqrt(gamma * R * T).
Calculate the Mach number using M = V / a.
Calculate the stagnation pressure using the isentropic relation P0 = P * (1 + (gamma - 1) / 2 * M^2)^(gamma / (gamma - 1)).
Report the speed of sound in m/s, the Mach number (dimensionless), and the stagnation pressure in kPa.
Round all final results to four significant figures.
Do not use the incompressible Bernoulli equation at any step.
