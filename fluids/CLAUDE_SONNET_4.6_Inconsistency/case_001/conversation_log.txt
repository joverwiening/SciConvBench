Q: The incompressible Bernoulli equation is only valid for low-speed (low Mach number) flows, typically Mach < 0.3. At 250 m/s in air at 300 K, the speed of sound is approximately 347 m/s, giving a Mach number of about 0.72, which is in the compressible subsonic regime.

Using the incompressible formula here will give a significantly inaccurate result compared to the correct compressible isentropic relation.

Which approach would you like to use?

1. Proceed with the incompressible Bernoulli equation as stated (knowing it is physically incorrect at this Mach number)
2. Use the correct compressible isentropic stagnation pressure formula instead
A: Use the compressible isentropic stagnation pressure formula. First calculate the Mach number, and since M > 0.3, apply the isentropic flow relations for a compressible ideal gas.
