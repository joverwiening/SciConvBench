The system is a quarter-cylindrical gate with radius R = 2.0 m and width b = 1.0 m submerged in water.
The top edge of the curved gate is at the free surface (water surface), so the gate spans from depth 0 at the top to depth R = 2.0 m at the bottom.
Use water density rho = 1000 kg/m³ and gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².

Compute the horizontal hydrostatic force component using the vertical projection of the curved surface.
The vertical projection (for horizontal force) is a rectangle of height R = 2.0 m and width b = 1.0 m.
The centroid of this vertical projection is at depth h_c = R/2 = 1.0 m below the free surface.
Compute the horizontal force as F_H = rho * g * h_c * A_vertical, where A_vertical = R * b = 2.0 * 1.0 = 2.0 m².
F_H = 1000 * 9.81 * 1.0 * 2.0 = 19620 N = 19.62 kN.
The horizontal force acts at the center of pressure of the vertical projection, located at depth h_cp = h_c + I_c / (h_c * A_vertical), where I_c = b * R³ / 12 is the second moment of area of the rectangle about its centroid.
I_c = 1.0 * (2.0)³ / 12 = 8/12 = 0.6667 m⁴.
h_cp = 1.0 + 0.6667 / (1.0 * 2.0) = 1.0 + 0.3333 = 1.333 m below the free surface.

Compute the vertical hydrostatic force component using the horizontal projection of the curved surface.
The horizontal projection (for vertical force) is a rectangle of width R = 2.0 m and length b = 1.0 m, projected onto a horizontal plane.
The vertical force equals the weight of the water column above the curved surface up to the free surface.
For a quarter cylinder with the top edge at the free surface and curving downward and inward, the water volume above the curved surface is the volume of the quarter cylinder itself.
Compute the volume of the quarter cylinder as V = (1/4) * pi * R² * b = (1/4) * pi * (2.0)² * 1.0 = pi m³ ≈ 3.1416 m³.
Compute the vertical force as F_V = rho * g * V = 1000 * 9.81 * 3.1416 = 30819 N ≈ 30.82 kN.
The vertical force acts through the centroid of the quarter-circle volume, located at a horizontal distance of 4R/(3*pi) = 4*2.0/(3*pi) ≈ 0.8488 m from the vertical flat face of the gate.

State the final results clearly.
F_H = 19.62 kN, acting horizontally at a depth of 1.333 m below the free surface.
F_V = 30.82 kN, acting vertically downward through the centroid of the quarter-cylinder water volume at 0.8488 m from the axis.
