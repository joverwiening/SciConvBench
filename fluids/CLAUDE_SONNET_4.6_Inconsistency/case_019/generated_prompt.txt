Calculate the speed of sound in water using the formula c = sqrt(K / rho).
Use a bulk modulus K = 2.2 GPa (2.2 × 10⁹ Pa).
Use a density rho = 998 kg/m³.
Compute c = sqrt(2.2 × 10⁹ / 998).
Report the resulting speed of sound as a finite numerical value in meters per second.
Do not apply any incompressibility assumption, as that would yield an infinite result.
