Q: What value should be used for the angle of attack in the lift formula — the geometric angle of 20 degrees, or the effective angle accounting for the NACA 4412 zero-lift angle of attack (which is approximately -4 degrees for this cambered airfoil)?
A: Use a small angle of attack like 5 degrees instead of 20 degrees, since thin airfoil theory with Cl = 2π alpha is only valid for small angles before stall.
