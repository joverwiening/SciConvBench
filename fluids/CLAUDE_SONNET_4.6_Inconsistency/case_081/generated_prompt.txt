Compute the Reynolds number for water at 20°C using the formula Re = ρVD/μ.
Use dynamic viscosity μ = 1.0e-3 Pa·s (fixed constant, physically correct for water at 20°C).
Accept three user-specified inputs: fluid density ρ (kg/m³), flow velocity V (m/s), and pipe diameter D (m).
All inputs must be in SI units to ensure dimensional consistency.
Compute Re = ρ * V * D / μ and return the resulting dimensionless Reynolds number.
The formula may equivalently be expressed as Re = V * D / ν where kinematic viscosity ν = μ/ρ = 1.0e-6 m²/s (valid only when ρ = 1000 kg/m³, the standard density of water at 20°C).
Do not hard-code ρ; always use the user-provided value so the formula remains general.
Output the computed Reynolds number as a dimensionless scalar value.
