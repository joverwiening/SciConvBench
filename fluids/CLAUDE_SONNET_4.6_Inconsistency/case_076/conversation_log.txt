Q: The saturation temperature at 10 kPa from standard steam tables is approximately 45.8°C, not 100°C — these two values are contradictory since each pressure corresponds to a unique saturation temperature for water.

Which value should be used as the authoritative condition: the pressure of 10 kPa, or the saturation temperature of 100°C?
A: Use 10 kPa as the authoritative condition, with the correct saturation temperature of approximately 45.8°C. The 100°C figure in the incomplete requirement is wrong — if you want Tsat = 100°C, the pressure should be about 101.3 kPa, not 10 kPa.
