The fluid is water at standard conditions with a density (rho) of 1000 kg/m³.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
The pump delivers a head H = 30 m.
The flow rate Q = 50 L/s, which must be converted to SI units as 0.05 m³/s before use in the formula.
Compute hydraulic power using the formula P = rho * g * Q * H.
Substitute the values: P = 1000 * 9.81 * 0.05 * 30.
Calculate the result: P = 14715 W, which can also be expressed as approximately 14.715 kW.
Report the final answer as P ≈ 14.715 kW.
