Compute the pressure drop in a pipe using the Darcy–Weisbach equation.

The pipe length L is 30 m.
The pipe diameter D is 0.1 m.
The Fanning friction factor f is 0.005.
Use the formula Δp = f × (L/D) × (ρ × v² / 2).

Assume the fluid is water at 20°C with a density ρ of 998 kg/m³.
Assume a typical turbulent flow velocity v of 1.0 m/s, which is standard for water in industrial piping.

Compute L/D as 30 / 0.1 = 300.
Compute the dynamic pressure term as ρ × v² / 2 = 998 × (1.0)² / 2 = 499 Pa.
Compute Δp as 0.005 × 300 × 499 = 748.5 Pa.

Report the final pressure drop result as Δp = 748.5 Pa.
State the assumptions used: water at 20°C, ρ = 998 kg/m³, v = 1.0 m/s.
