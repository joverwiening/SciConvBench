A steel sphere has radius R = 10 mm = 0.01 m.
The convection coefficient is h = 80 W/m²·K.
The thermal conductivity of steel is k = 45 W/m·K.
The characteristic length for a sphere is L_c = R/3 = 0.01/3 = 0.003333 m.
Compute the Biot number as Bi = h · L_c / k = (80 × 0.003333) / 45.
Bi = 0.26667 / 45 = 0.005926 (approximately 5.93 × 10⁻³).
The lumped capacitance criterion requires Bi < 0.1.
Since Bi ≈ 0.00593 is much less than 0.1, the criterion is satisfied.
Conclude that the internal temperature of the solid steel sphere is spatially uniform at any given time.
Lumped capacitance method is valid for this problem.
