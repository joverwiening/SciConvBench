The fluid is Newtonian with a viscosity of 0.5 Pa·s.  
The viscometer uses a Couette geometry with coaxial cylinders.  
The inner cylinder has a radius of 0.02 m.  
The gap between the cylinders is 0.001 m.  
The height of the cylinder is 0.05 m.  
The rotational speed is 100 rpm, equivalent to 10.472 rad/s.  
The shear rate is calculated as (rotational speed × radius) / gap.  
The shear stress is calculated as viscosity × shear rate.  
The torque is calculated as (2 × π × radius² × height × shear stress) / (gap).  
Use SI units throughout the calculation.  
The torque is expressed in newton-meters (N·m).
