Calculate heat loss per meter of a horizontal steel pipe carrying steam at 150°C.  
The ambient temperature is 20°C.  
The pipe has a nominal diameter of 2 inches with an outer diameter of 60.3 mm.  
The pipe wall thickness is 3.91 mm, based on standard Schedule 40 steel pipe dimensions.  
The thermal conductivity of carbon steel is 50 W/(m·K).  
Heat transfer occurs under natural convection with an external heat transfer coefficient of 10 W/m²·K.  
The calculation is for steady-state heat loss per meter length of pipe.  
Include conduction through the pipe wall and convection from the outer surface to ambient air.  
Use cylindrical coordinate heat transfer equations for radial conduction and convection.  
Report the total heat loss in watts per meter (W/m).
