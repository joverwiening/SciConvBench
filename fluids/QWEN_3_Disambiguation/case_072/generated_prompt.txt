Use a net head of 300 meters to calculate the jet velocity.  
Calculate the jet velocity using the formula \( v = \sqrt{2 \times 9.81 \times 300} \), resulting in approximately 76.72 m/s.  
Assume the bucket speed is 0.48 times the jet velocity for optimal efficiency.  
Calculate the bucket speed as \( 0.48 \times 76.72 \), resulting in approximately 36.83 m/s.  
Assume mechanical efficiency is 100% for the purpose of calculating hydraulic efficiency.  
Calculate hydraulic efficiency using the formula \( \eta_h = \frac{2(v - u)u}{v^2} \), where \( v \) is jet velocity and \( u \) is bucket speed.  
Substitute \( v = 76.72 \) m/s and \( u = 36.83 \) m/s into the formula to compute hydraulic efficiency.  
The hydraulic efficiency is approximately 93.5%.
