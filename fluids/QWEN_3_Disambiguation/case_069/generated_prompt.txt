Use an ambient static temperature of 220 K.
Use a flight Mach number of 2.5.
Use a ratio of specific heats (γ) of 1.4.
Calculate the stagnation temperature using the isentropic flow relation: T0 = T * (1 + ((γ - 1)/2) * M^2).
The stagnation temperature is the temperature at the nose of the rocket where the flow is brought to rest isentropically.
Do not account for real-gas effects or chemical dissociation.
Assume calorically perfect gas behavior.
