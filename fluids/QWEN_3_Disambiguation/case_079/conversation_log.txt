Q: What is the net positive suction head (NPSH) available for the Francis turbine?
A: The net positive suction head (NPSH) available is calculated as:

NPSH = (Atmospheric pressure head - Vapor pressure head) - (Runner elevation above tailrace)

NPSH = (10.3 m - 0.3 m) - 2 m = 8.0 m

So, the NPSH available is 8.0 meters.
