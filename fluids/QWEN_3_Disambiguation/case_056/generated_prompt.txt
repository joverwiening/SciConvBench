Calculate the stagnation pressure using the isentropic flow relation for a perfect gas.
The stagnation pressure is determined at the nose of the jet where the flow is brought to rest isentropically.
Use the formula: P0 = P * (1 + ((γ - 1) / 2) * M^2)^(γ / (γ - 1)), where P is static pressure, M is Mach number, and γ is the ratio of specific heats.
Substitute M = 1.5, P = 20 kPa, and γ = 1.4 into the formula to compute the stagnation pressure.
