Calculate the Darcy–Weisbach head loss using the formula h_f = (f * L * v^2) / (2 * g * D).
Use a friction factor f of 0.02.
Use a pipe length L of 50 m.
Use a pipe diameter D of 0.05 m.
Use a flow velocity v of 2 m/s.
Use gravitational acceleration g of 9.81 m/s².
The head loss is calculated as h_f = (0.02 * 50 * 2^2) / (2 * 9.81 * 0.05).
