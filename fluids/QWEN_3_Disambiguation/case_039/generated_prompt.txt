Calculate the torque required to rotate a disk of radius 0.2 m in a viscous fluid.  
The disk is positioned 1 mm from a flat wall.  
The disk rotates at 60 rpm, which is equivalent to 2π radians per second.  
The dynamic viscosity of the fluid is 0.1 Pa·s.  
Assume the flow between the disk and the wall is laminar and dominated by viscous shear.  
Model the shear stress distribution across the disk surface using the linear velocity gradient assumption.  
Integrate the shear stress over the disk area to obtain the total torque.  
Neglect edge effects and inertial contributions.
