Assume the fluid is water with a density of 1000 kg/m³.  

  
The rectangular plate is 1 m wide and 2 m high.  
The plate is vertically submerged with its top edge at the water surface.  
The fluid is water with a density of 1000 kg/m³.  
The center of pressure is located at a depth of 1.333 meters below the water surface.  
The center of pressure is calculated using the formula for a vertically submerged plane surface: h_cp = h_c + (I_xx / (A * h_c)), where h_c is the depth to the centroid, I_xx is the second moment of area about the centroidal axis, and A is the area of the plate.  
The centroid of the plate is at a depth of 1 meter.  
The second moment of area I_xx for the plate about its centroidal axis is (1/12) * width * height³ = (1/12) * 1 * (2)³ = 0.6667 m⁴.  
The area A of the plate is 1 * 2 = 2 m².  
The depth to the center of pressure is 1 + (0.6667 / (2 * 1)) = 1.333 meters.
