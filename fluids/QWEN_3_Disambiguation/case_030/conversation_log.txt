Q: What is the dynamic viscosity of the air?
A: The kinematic viscosity (ν) of air is given as 1.5 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s. The dynamic viscosity (μ) can be calculated using the relationship μ = ν × ρ, where ρ is the density of air. For air at standard conditions, ρ ≈ 1.225 kg/m³. Therefore, μ ≈ (1.5 × 10⁻⁵) × 1.225 = 1.8375 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s.
