Calculate the lift force per unit span on a rotating cylinder using the Kutta-Joukowski theorem.
The cylinder has a radius of 0.1 m.
The free-stream air velocity is 15 m/s.
The cylinder rotates at an angular velocity of 200 rad/s.
The span length is 1 meter (per unit span).
Use standard sea-level air density of 1.225 kg/m³.
Compute the circulation Γ using the formula Γ = 2π × ω × r², where ω is the angular velocity and r is the radius.
Compute the lift per unit span using the Kutta-Joukowski theorem: L = ρ × V∞ × Γ × span.
Report the circulation value in m²/s and the final lift force in Newtons per meter of span.
