Find the terminal velocity of a spherical raindrop falling through air using the drag coefficient method.

The raindrop is spherical with a diameter of 2 mm (radius = 0.001 m).
The density of water is 1000 kg/m³.
The density of air is 1.2 kg/m³.
The drag coefficient Cd is 0.5.
The gravitational acceleration is 9.81 m/s².

Calculate the mass of the raindrop using m = (4/3) × π × r³ × ρ_water.
Calculate the cross-sectional area of the raindrop using A = π × r².
At terminal velocity, the gravitational force equals the drag force: m × g = 0.5 × Cd × ρ_air × A × v².
Solve for terminal velocity v = sqrt((2 × m × g) / (Cd × ρ_air × A)).
Substitute all values and compute the numerical result for v in meters per second.
Buoyancy force is neglected (ρ_water >> ρ_air, so the buoyancy correction is minor and not required).
Report the final terminal velocity in m/s.
