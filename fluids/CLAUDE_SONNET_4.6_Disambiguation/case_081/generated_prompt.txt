Calculate the force exerted by a water jet on a stationary curved vane using the momentum equation.

The water jet has a circular cross-section with a diameter of 50 mm (0.05 m).
The jet velocity is 20 m/s.
The vane is stationary.
The jet is deflected through an angle of 120°, meaning the jet turns 120° from its original direction.
Use the standard deflection angle convention where the angle of deflection is 120°, so the angle used in the momentum equation components is (180° - 120°) = 60° for the x-component calculation, or directly use 120° as the turning angle.
Assume the vane is smooth, so there is no friction and the speed of the jet remains 20 m/s after deflection.
Assume water density is 1000 kg/m³.
Calculate the cross-sectional area of the jet as A = π/4 × (0.05)².
Calculate the mass flow rate as ṁ = ρ × A × v.
Calculate the x-component of force as Fx = ṁ × v × (1 - cos(120°)).
Calculate the y-component of force as Fy = ṁ × v × sin(120°).
Calculate the resultant force as F = √(Fx² + Fy²).
State the magnitude and direction of the resultant force on the vane.
