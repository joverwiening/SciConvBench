Find the hydraulic efficiency of a Pelton wheel turbine operating under a net head of 300 meters.

Use a jet velocity coefficient Cv = 0.98, which is standard best practice for Pelton wheel design.
Calculate the actual jet velocity using V1 = Cv × sqrt(2 × g × H), where g = 9.81 m/s² and H = 300 m.
Use a speed ratio φ = 0.46, which is the optimal value for maximum hydraulic efficiency in a Pelton wheel.
Calculate the bucket speed using u = φ × V1.
Use a bucket friction coefficient k = 0.9, meaning the relative velocity leaving the bucket is 90% of the relative velocity entering the bucket.
Use a bucket exit angle β₂ = 165 degrees (measured from the direction of bucket motion), which is standard practice to avoid the exiting jet striking the back of the next bucket.
Calculate the relative velocity at inlet as Vr1 = V1 - u.
Calculate the relative velocity at outlet as Vr2 = k × Vr1.
Calculate the whirl velocity at inlet as Vw1 = V1.
Calculate the whirl velocity at outlet as Vw2 = u - Vr2 × cos(180 - β₂).
Calculate the work done per unit mass as W = u × (Vw1 + Vw2).
Calculate the kinetic energy of the jet per unit mass as KE = 0.5 × V1².
Calculate hydraulic efficiency as η_h = W / KE, expressed as a percentage.
Present all intermediate values including V1, u, Vr1, Vr2, Vw1, Vw2, and the final hydraulic efficiency.
