Calculate the buoyant lift force on a gas-filled balloon using Archimedes' principle.
The balloon has a volume of 500 m³.
The balloon is filled with helium gas with a density of 0.18 kg/m³.
The surrounding fluid is air with a density of 1.20 kg/m³.
Use standard gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Compute the buoyant force as F_buoy = rho_air × V × g.
Compute the weight of the helium gas as W_he = rho_he × V × g.
Compute the net lift force as F_lift = F_buoy - W_he = (rho_air - rho_he) × V × g.
Substitute values: F_lift = (1.20 - 0.18) × 500 × 9.81.
Calculate the density difference: 1.20 - 0.18 = 1.02 kg/m³.
Calculate the net lift force: F_lift = 1.02 × 500 × 9.81 = 5002.1 N.
Report the final net buoyant lift force as approximately 5002 N (or about 5.00 kN).
Do not account for the mass of the balloon envelope itself, as it is not provided.
