Calculate the force produced by a water jet striking a flat plate using the impulse-momentum principle.
The jet diameter is 10 mm (0.01 m).
The jet velocity is 15 m/s.
Use the density of water as 1000 kg/m³.
Calculate the cross-sectional area of the jet using A = π/4 × d².
A = π/4 × (0.01)² = 7.854 × 10⁻⁵ m².
Calculate the mass flow rate using ṁ = ρ × A × v.
ṁ = 1000 × 7.854 × 10⁻⁵ × 15 = 1.1781 kg/s.
Assume the flat plate is stationary and perpendicular to the jet.
Assume the water does not rebound and flows away along the plate after impact (velocity component normal to plate becomes zero).
Calculate the force using F = ṁ × v.
F = 1.1781 × 15 = 17.67 N.
The force exerted by the water jet on the flat plate is approximately 17.67 N, acting perpendicular to the plate surface.
