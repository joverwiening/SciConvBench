Calculate the thermal efficiency of a Carnot heat engine.
The hot reservoir (source) temperature is 800 K.
The cold reservoir (sink) temperature is 300 K.
Use the Carnot efficiency formula: η = 1 - (T_cold / T_hot).
Substitute the values: η = 1 - (300 / 800).
Compute the result: η = 1 - 0.375 = 0.625.
Express the thermal efficiency as a percentage: 62.5%.
State that this is the maximum possible efficiency for any heat engine operating between these two temperatures.
