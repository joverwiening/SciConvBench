Find the center of pressure on a rectangular plate submerged vertically in a fluid.
The plate dimensions are 1 m (width) x 2 m (height).
The top edge of the plate is at the free surface of the fluid (depth = 0).
The plate extends vertically downward from the surface to a depth of 2 m.
Assume the fluid is water with a specific gravity of 1 (density = 1000 kg/m³).
Assume standard gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Use the standard hydrostatic formula for center of pressure: h_cp = h_c + I_c / (A * h_c).
h_c is the depth of the centroid of the plate below the free surface.
The centroid of the plate is at a depth of 1 m (midpoint of the 2 m height).
I_c is the second moment of area of the plate about its centroidal axis parallel to the free surface.
Calculate I_c as (width * height³) / 12 = (1 * 2³) / 12 = 8/12 = 0.667 m⁴.
Calculate the area A = 1 * 2 = 2 m².
Calculate h_cp = 1 + 0.667 / (2 * 1) = 1 + 0.333 = 1.333 m.
Report the center of pressure as 1.333 m (4/3 m) below the free surface, measured along the vertical axis of the plate.
The center of pressure is located 0.333 m below the centroid of the plate.
