Calculate the convective heat transfer rate from a flat plate using Newton's Law of Cooling.
The plate surface area is 2 m².
The plate surface temperature is 60°C.
The surrounding fluid temperature is 20°C.
The temperature difference (ΔT) is 40 K.
The convective heat transfer coefficient (h) is 25 W/(m²·K).
Apply the formula Q = h × A × ΔT.
Compute Q = 25 × 2 × 40.
The resulting convective heat transfer rate Q is 2000 W (or 2 kW).
Report the final answer as 2000 W.
