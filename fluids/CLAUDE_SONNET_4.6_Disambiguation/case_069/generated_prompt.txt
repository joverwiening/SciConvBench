Calculate the stagnation temperature on the nose of a rocket using the isentropic flow relation for a calorically perfect gas.
Use the freestream static temperature T = 220 K.
Use the flight Mach number M = 2.5.
Use the ratio of specific heats γ = 1.4 (standard value for air).
Apply the isentropic stagnation temperature formula: T0 = T × (1 + (γ - 1) / 2 × M²).
Substitute values: T0 = 220 × (1 + 0.2 × 6.25).
Compute the term in parentheses: 1 + 1.25 = 2.25.
Multiply: T0 = 220 × 2.25 = 495 K.
Report the stagnation temperature at the rocket nose as 495 K (approximately 222 °C).
Note that this is the theoretical maximum (adiabatic) stagnation temperature assuming the nose brings the flow to rest isentropically, with no radiative or convective heat loss accounted for.
