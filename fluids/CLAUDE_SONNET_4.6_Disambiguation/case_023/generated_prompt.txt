Calculate the pressure drop per unit length in a packed bed using the Ergun equation.
The packing consists of spherical particles with a diameter of 5 mm (0.005 m).
The fluid is air at standard atmospheric pressure (101325 Pa) and temperature (20°C or 293.15 K).
Use standard air properties: density = 1.204 kg/m³ and dynamic viscosity = 1.825 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s.
The superficial velocity of the air is 0.5 m/s.
The void fraction (porosity) of the packed bed is 0.4.
Apply the Ergun equation: ΔP/L = [150 × μ × u × (1 - ε)²] / [dp² × ε³] + [1.75 × ρ × u² × (1 - ε)] / [dp × ε³].
Compute the viscous (Kozeny-Carman) term and the inertial (Burke-Plummer) term separately, then sum them.
Report the final result as pressure drop per unit length of bed in units of Pa/m.
Also report the Reynolds number for the packed bed to confirm the flow regime and justify use of the full Ergun equation.
