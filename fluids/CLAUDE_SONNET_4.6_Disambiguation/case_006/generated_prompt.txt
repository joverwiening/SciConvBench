Solve for the steady-state velocity profile in planar Couette flow between two infinite parallel plates.
The bottom plate is stationary (velocity = 0 m/s) and is located at y = 0.
The top plate moves at 5 m/s in the x-direction and is located at y = 10 mm (0.01 m).
The gap between the plates is 10 mm.
Assume the flow is incompressible, viscous, and fully developed (no pressure gradient in the x-direction).
Assume the fluid properties are constant throughout the domain.
Apply the no-slip boundary condition at both plates: u(0) = 0 m/s and u(0.01) = 5 m/s.
Solve the simplified Navier-Stokes equation, which reduces to d²u/dy² = 0, for the velocity field u(y).
The solution is a linear velocity profile: u(y) = (5 / 0.01) * y = 500y, where y is in meters and u is in m/s.
Express the final velocity profile as u(y) = 500y for 0 ≤ y ≤ 0.01 m.
The shear stress in the fluid is constant and equal to τ = μ * (du/dy) = μ * 500, where μ is the dynamic viscosity of the fluid.
No body forces (e.g., gravity in the flow direction) are considered.
