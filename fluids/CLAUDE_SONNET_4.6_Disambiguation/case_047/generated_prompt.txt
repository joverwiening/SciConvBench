Calculate the heat transfer coefficient (h) from the Nusselt number using the formula h = Nu × k / L.
Use Nu = 120, thermal conductivity k = 0.026 W/m·K, and characteristic length L = 0.10 m (diameter of the sphere).
Compute h = 120 × 0.026 / 0.10.
The result is h = 31.2 W/m²·K.
Report the heat transfer coefficient as h = 31.2 W/m²·K.
