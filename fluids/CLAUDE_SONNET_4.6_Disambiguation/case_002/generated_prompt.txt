Convert the input angle of attack from degrees to radians before applying the formula.

Import the math module to access pi and the radians conversion function.
Print the resulting lift coefficient to the console with a clear label.
Do not model camber or thickness effects; assume a flat plate (symmetric airfoil) as per basic thin airfoil theory.
The script should be self-contained and runnable as a single Python file.
