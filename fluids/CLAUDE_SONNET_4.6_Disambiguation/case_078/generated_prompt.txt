Calculate the power available in a wind stream using the standard kinetic power formula for wind energy.
Use the wind speed of 12 m/s.
Use a blade diameter of 40 meters to compute the rotor swept area as a circle: A = π × (D/2)².
Use the standard air density value of 1.225 kg/m³ at sea level and 15°C.
Apply the wind power formula: P = 0.5 × ρ × A × v³.
Report the final answer in watts (W) and kilowatts (kW).
Do not apply the Betz limit or any efficiency factor; compute the total available power in the wind stream only.
