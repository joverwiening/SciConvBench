Calculate the thermal efficiency of an ideal Otto cycle.
Use a compression ratio (r) of 8.5.
Use the standard thermal efficiency formula for the Otto cycle: η = 1 - (1 / r^(γ-1)).
Assume the working fluid is air using the air-standard assumption.
Use a specific heat ratio (γ) of 1.4 (diatomic ideal gas, standard air-standard cycle assumption).
Compute r^(γ-1) as 8.5^(0.4).
Compute the thermal efficiency as η = 1 - (1 / 8.5^0.4).
Express the final thermal efficiency as a percentage rounded to two decimal places.
