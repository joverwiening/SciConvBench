Find the terminal velocity of a 2 mm diameter spherical steel particle falling vertically through oil.
The particle diameter is 0.002 m (2 mm).
The particle is perfectly spherical.
The particle density is 7800 kg/m³.
The oil dynamic viscosity is 0.29 Pa·s.
The oil density is 890 kg/m³.
The particle falls under gravity with g = 9.81 m/s².
Assume steady-state terminal velocity (net force = 0).
Use Stokes' Law to calculate terminal velocity: v = (d² × (ρ_particle - ρ_fluid) × g) / (18 × μ).
Verify the result is valid for Stokes' Law by computing the Reynolds number Re = (ρ_fluid × v × d) / μ and confirming Re < 1.
Report the terminal velocity in m/s.
Report the Reynolds number to confirm the Stokes' Law regime is valid.
