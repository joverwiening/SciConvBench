Calculate the Weber number for a moving liquid droplet using the formula: We = (ρ * v² * d) / σ.
The droplet diameter is 2 mm, which must be converted to 0.002 m for use in the formula.
The droplet velocity is 30 m/s.
The liquid density is 800 kg/m³.
The surface tension of the liquid is 0.03 N/m.
Substitute the values into the formula: We = (800 * 30² * 0.002) / 0.03.
Compute the numerator: 800 * 900 * 0.002 = 1440.
Compute the Weber number: We = 1440 / 0.03 = 48000.
Report the final Weber number as 48000 (dimensionless).
