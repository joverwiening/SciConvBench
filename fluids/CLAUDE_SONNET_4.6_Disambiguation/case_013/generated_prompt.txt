Calculate the critical insulation thickness for a cylindrical pipe with insulation.

Use the formula for critical radius of insulation for a cylinder: r_critical = k / h.

The thermal conductivity of the insulation is k = 0.17 W/m·K.
The convective heat transfer coefficient on the outer surface is h = 8 W/m²·K.

Calculate the critical radius as r_critical = 0.17 / 8 = 0.02125 m = 21.25 mm.

The critical insulation thickness is determined by subtracting the pipe's outer radius from the critical radius.
Since no pipe outer radius was provided, report the critical radius value as 21.25 mm.
State that the critical insulation thickness equals 21.25 mm minus the outer radius of the bare pipe.
If the pipe outer radius is less than 21.25 mm, adding insulation up to this critical radius will increase heat loss.
If the pipe outer radius is greater than 21.25 mm, any insulation added will reduce heat loss.
