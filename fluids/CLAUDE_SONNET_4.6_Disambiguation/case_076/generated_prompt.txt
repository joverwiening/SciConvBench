Calculate the efficiency of a conical draft tube using the given data.
The inlet velocity of water V1 is 10 m/s.
The outlet velocity of water V2 is 2 m/s.
The actual pressure head recovery is 4.5 meters of water.
Use the value of g as 9.81 m/s².
Calculate the theoretical pressure head recovery using the formula: Theoretical recovery = (V1² - V2²) / (2g).
Substitute values: Theoretical recovery = (10² - 2²) / (2 × 9.81) = (100 - 4) / 19.62 = 96 / 19.62.
The theoretical pressure head recovery equals approximately 4.893 meters of water.
Calculate efficiency using the formula: Efficiency = Actual recovery / Theoretical recovery.
Substitute values: Efficiency = 4.5 / 4.893.
The efficiency of the conical draft tube is approximately 0.9197, or 91.97%.
