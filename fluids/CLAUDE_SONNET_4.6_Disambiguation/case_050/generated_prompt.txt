Calculate the power output of a horizontal-axis wind turbine using the standard wind power formula: P = 0.5 × ρ × A × Cp × v³.
The rotor diameter is 40 meters, giving a rotor swept area A = π × (20)² ≈ 1256.64 m².
Use standard air density ρ = 1.225 kg/m³.
The wind speed v is 12 m/s.
The power coefficient Cp is 0.35.
Compute v³ = 12³ = 1728 m³/s³.
Compute P = 0.5 × 1.225 × 1256.64 × 0.35 × 1728.
Report the final power output in watts (W) and kilowatts (kW).
The turbine is horizontal-axis with a single rotor plane perpendicular to the wind direction.
No mechanical or electrical drivetrain losses beyond the stated Cp are to be applied.
