Calculate the Biot number for a cooling sphere.
The sphere has a radius of 5 mm (0.005 m).
The convective heat transfer coefficient h = 150 W/m²·K.
The thermal conductivity of the sphere material k = 45 W/m·K (steel).
Use the characteristic length Lc = r/3 for a sphere, which gives Lc = 0.005/3 = 0.001667 m.
Calculate the Biot number using the formula Bi = h × Lc / k.
Substitute values: Bi = 150 × 0.001667 / 45.
Compute the final result: Bi ≈ 0.00556.
Report the Biot number as dimensionless.
Since Bi << 0.1, confirm that the lumped capacitance method is valid for this sphere.
