Calculate the hydraulic diameter of a rectangular duct with dimensions 30 cm x 10 cm.
Use the hydraulic diameter formula: Dh = 4A / P, where A is the cross-sectional area and P is the wetted perimeter.
Compute the cross-sectional area: A = 30 cm x 10 cm = 300 cm².
Compute the wetted perimeter: P = 2 x (30 cm + 10 cm) = 80 cm.
Compute the hydraulic diameter: Dh = 4 x 300 cm² / 80 cm = 15 cm.
Report the final answer as Dh = 15 cm.
