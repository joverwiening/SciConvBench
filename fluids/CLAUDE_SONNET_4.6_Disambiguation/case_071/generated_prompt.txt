Calculate the shaft power required for a centrifugal pump delivering water.
The fluid being pumped is water with a density of 1000 kg/m³.
Use a gravitational acceleration of 9.81 m/s².
The volumetric flow rate is 0.05 m³/s.
The total head the pump must deliver is 25 meters.
The overall pump efficiency is 75% (0.75).
Calculate the hydraulic power first using the formula: P_hydraulic = density × g × Q × H.
P_hydraulic = 1000 × 9.81 × 0.05 × 25 = 12,262.5 W (12.2625 kW).
Calculate the shaft power using the formula: P_shaft = P_hydraulic / efficiency.
P_shaft = 12,262.5 / 0.75 = 16,350 W (16.35 kW).
Report the final shaft power required as 16,350 W or 16.35 kW.
