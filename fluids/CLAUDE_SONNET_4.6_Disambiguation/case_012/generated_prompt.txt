Calculate the capillary rise of a liquid in a vertical cylindrical tube using the Jurin's Law formula: h = (2 * T * cos(θ)) / (ρ * g * r).
Use a tube diameter of 0.5 mm, giving a radius r = 0.00025 m.
Use a contact angle θ = 0°, so cos(0°) = 1.
Use a surface tension T = 0.072 N/m.
Use a liquid density ρ = 1000 kg/m³.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Substitute all values into the formula: h = (2 * 0.072 * 1) / (1000 * 9.81 * 0.00025).
Compute the numerator: 2 * 0.072 * 1 = 0.144.
Compute the denominator: 1000 * 9.81 * 0.00025 = 2.4525.
Divide to get the capillary rise: h = 0.144 / 2.4525 ≈ 0.05872 m.
Express the final result as h ≈ 58.72 mm.
