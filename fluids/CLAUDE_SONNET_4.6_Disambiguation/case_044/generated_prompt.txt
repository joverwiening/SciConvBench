Calculate the Stefan number (Ste) for a phase-change problem involving ice melting into water.
Use the formula: Ste = (cp × ΔT) / L
Use the specific heat of liquid water: cp = 4186 J/(kg·°C).
Use the latent heat of fusion for ice: L = 334,000 J/kg.
Use the given temperature difference: ΔT = 10°C.
Compute Ste = (4186 × 10) / 334,000.
The result is Ste ≈ 0.1253 (dimensionless).
Report the Stefan number as approximately 0.125, indicating that the sensible heat is about 12.5% of the latent heat for this problem.
