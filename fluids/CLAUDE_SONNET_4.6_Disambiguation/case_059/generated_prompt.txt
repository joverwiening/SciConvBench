Calculate the drag force on a flat billboard during a windstorm.
The billboard dimensions are 4 meters high by 10 meters wide.
The frontal area of the billboard is 40 square meters (4m x 10m).
The wind speed is 30 m/s.
The drag coefficient (Cd) is 1.2.
Use the standard air density at sea level, which is 1.225 kg/m³.
Apply the drag force formula: F = 0.5 x Cd x air density x A x v².
Substitute values: F = 0.5 x 1.2 x 1.225 x 40 x (30)².
Calculate the result: F = 0.5 x 1.2 x 1.225 x 40 x 900.
The drag force on the billboard is 26,460 Newtons (26.46 kN).
