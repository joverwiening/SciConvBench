Calculate the stagnation temperature of a compressible flow using the isentropic relation.
The freestream (static) temperature is 250 K.
The Mach number is 2.0.
Assume the flow is a calorically perfect ideal gas (constant specific heats).
Use the standard value of the ratio of specific heats, gamma = 1.4 (air).
Apply the isentropic stagnation temperature formula: T0 = T * (1 + ((gamma - 1) / 2) * M^2).
Substitute T = 250 K, gamma = 1.4, and M = 2.0 into the formula.
Compute the numerical result for stagnation temperature T0 in Kelvin.
Report the final answer in Kelvin.
