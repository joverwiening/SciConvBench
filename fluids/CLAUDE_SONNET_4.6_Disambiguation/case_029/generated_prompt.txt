Determine the efficiency of a straight rectangular fin made of aluminum.
The thermal conductivity of the fin material is k = 200 W/m·K.
The fin has a rectangular profile with a length L = 3 cm (0.03 m) and a thickness t = 2 mm (0.002 m).
The convective heat transfer coefficient is h = 20 W/m²·K applied along the fin surface.
The tip of the fin is adiabatic (insulated), meaning no heat loss occurs at the tip.
The fin efficiency is calculated using the standard adiabatic-tip formula: η = tanh(mL) / (mL).
The corrected fin parameter m is defined as m = sqrt(2h / (k · t)), using the rectangular cross-section approximation where the perimeter-to-cross-sectional-area ratio simplifies to 2/t for a thin fin of unit width.
Compute m = sqrt((2 × 20) / (200 × 0.002)) = sqrt(40 / 0.4) = sqrt(100) = 10 m⁻¹.
Compute mL = 10 × 0.03 = 0.3 (dimensionless).
Compute fin efficiency η = tanh(0.3) / 0.3 = 0.2913 / 0.3 ≈ 0.9709, or approximately 97.1%.
Report the final fin efficiency as approximately 97.1%, indicating the fin is highly effective under these conditions.
