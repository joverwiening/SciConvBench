Calculate the Nusselt number for turbulent pipe flow using the Dittus-Boelter correlation.
The Reynolds number is Re = 1.5 × 10^4.
The Prandtl number is Pr = 6.0.
The fluid is water and is being heated by the wall.
Use the Dittus-Boelter equation: Nu = 0.023 × Re^0.8 × Pr^0.4.
Compute Re^0.8 by raising 15000 to the power of 0.8.
Compute Pr^0.4 by raising 6.0 to the power of 0.4.
Multiply 0.023 × Re^0.8 × Pr^0.4 to obtain the final Nusselt number.
No entrance effects or L/D correction is required.
Report the final numerical value of Nu.
