Calculate the Mach number for a flow velocity of 500 m/s in air at an ambient temperature of -50°C (223.15 K).
Use the standard formula for the speed of sound in air: a = sqrt(γ * R * T), where γ (ratio of specific heats for air) = 1.4, R (specific gas constant for air) = 287 J/(kg·K), and T = 223.15 K.
Compute the speed of sound: a = sqrt(1.4 * 287 * 223.15).
Compute the Mach number: M = V / a = 500 / a.
Report the final Mach number as a dimensionless value rounded to four decimal places.
