Calculate the Mach number downstream of a normal shock wave.
The upstream Mach number (M1) is 2.2.
Use the standard normal shock relation: M2² = (M1² + 5) / (7·M1² − 1), which applies to a calorically perfect diatomic gas with specific heat ratio γ = 1.4.
Substitute M1 = 2.2 into the formula.
Compute M1² = 2.2² = 4.84.
Compute the numerator: 4.84 + 5 = 9.84.
Compute the denominator: 7 × 4.84 − 1 = 33.88 − 1 = 32.88.
Compute M2² = 9.84 / 32.88 ≈ 0.2993.
Compute M2 = √0.2993 ≈ 0.5471.
Report the downstream Mach number as M2 ≈ 0.547.
Confirm that M2 < 1, which is physically correct since flow downstream of a normal shock is always subsonic.
