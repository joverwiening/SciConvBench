Calculate the Darcy–Weisbach head loss in a pipe using the following parameters.
Use the Darcy–Weisbach equation: h_f = f * (L / D) * (v² / (2 * g)).
The pipe length L is 50 m.
The pipe diameter D is 0.05 m.
The flow velocity v is 2 m/s.
The Darcy friction factor f is 0.02.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Compute v² / (2 * g) first, then multiply by (L / D), then multiply by f.
Report the final head loss h_f in meters.
