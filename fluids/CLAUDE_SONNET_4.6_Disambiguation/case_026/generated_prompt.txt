Calculate the Knudsen number (Kn) for air flowing in a channel of width 100 nm at Standard Temperature and Pressure (0°C and 273.15 K, 1 atm = 101325 Pa).

Use the formula Kn = λ / L, where λ is the mean free path of the gas and L is the characteristic length (the channel width).

Use the mean free path formula λ = (k_B * T) / (sqrt(2) * π * d² * P), where k_B is the Boltzmann constant (1.380649 × 10⁻²³ J/K), T is the temperature in Kelvin (273.15 K), d is the effective diameter of an air molecule (use 3.7 × 10⁻¹⁰ m as the kinetic diameter for air, approximated as nitrogen), and P is the pressure (101325 Pa).

Compute λ using the values above.
Use L = 100 nm = 100 × 10⁻⁹ m = 1 × 10⁻⁷ m as the characteristic length.
Divide λ by L to obtain the Knudsen number Kn.
Report the numerical value of λ (in meters), the numerical value of Kn, and interpret the flow regime based on the result (Kn < 0.001 is continuum flow, 0.001 ≤ Kn < 0.1 is slip flow, 0.1 ≤ Kn < 10 is transitional flow, Kn ≥ 10 is free molecular flow).
