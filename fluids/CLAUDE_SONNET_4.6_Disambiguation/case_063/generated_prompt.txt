Calculate the Reynolds number for water flowing through a smooth pipe using the formula Re = (density × velocity × diameter) / dynamic viscosity.
Use a pipe diameter of 0.04 m.
Use a flow velocity of 1.5 m/s.
Use a dynamic viscosity of 0.001 Pa·s.
Use a density of 998 kg/m³.
Compute Re = (998 × 1.5 × 0.04) / 0.001.
Compare the resulting Reynolds number against the standard thresholds: Re < 2300 indicates laminar flow, 2300 ≤ Re ≤ 4000 indicates transitional flow, and Re > 4000 indicates turbulent flow.
