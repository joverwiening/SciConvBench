Calculate the head loss across a pipe fitting using the minor loss equation: h_L = K * V² / (2g).
Use a loss coefficient K = 0.9.
Use a fluid velocity V = 3 m/s.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
The fluid is water (density ≈ 1000 kg/m³), though density is not required for the head loss calculation in meters of fluid.
Compute V² = 3² = 9 m²/s².
Compute 2g = 2 × 9.81 = 19.62 m/s².
Compute V² / (2g) = 9 / 19.62 ≈ 0.4587 m.
Compute h_L = 0.9 × 0.4587 ≈ 0.413 m.
Report the head loss as approximately 0.413 meters of water.
