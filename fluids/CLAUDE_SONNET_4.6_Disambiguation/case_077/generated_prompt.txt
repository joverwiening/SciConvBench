Calculate the thermal efficiency of an ideal Brayton cycle gas turbine.
Use a pressure ratio (rp) of 12.
Use a ratio of specific heats (γ) of 1.4.
Apply the ideal Brayton cycle thermal efficiency formula: η = 1 - (1 / rp^((γ-1)/γ)).
Compute the exponent (γ-1)/γ = (1.4-1)/1.4 = 0.4/1.4 ≈ 0.2857.
Raise the pressure ratio to this exponent: 12^0.2857.
Divide 1 by the result, then subtract from 1 to obtain the thermal efficiency.
Express the final thermal efficiency as a percentage rounded to two decimal places.
