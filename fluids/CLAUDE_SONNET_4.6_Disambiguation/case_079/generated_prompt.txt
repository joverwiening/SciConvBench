Calculate Thoma's cavitation factor (sigma) for a Francis turbine using the formula: sigma = (Ha - Hv - Hs) / H.

Use the atmospheric pressure head Ha = 10.3 m.
Use the vapor pressure head Hv = 0.3 m.
Use the suction head Hs = 2 m (runner installed 2 meters above the tailrace water level).
Use the total net head H = 50 m.

Substitute into the formula: sigma = (10.3 - 0.3 - 2) / 50.

Compute the numerator: 10.3 - 0.3 - 2 = 8.0 m.
Compute sigma: 8.0 / 50 = 0.16.

Report Thoma's cavitation factor sigma = 0.16.
Interpret the result: this value indicates the margin against cavitation; the turbine installation is safe from cavitation if the plant sigma exceeds the critical sigma for the turbine type.
