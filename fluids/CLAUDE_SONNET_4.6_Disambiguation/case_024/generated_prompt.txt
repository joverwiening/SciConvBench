Calculate the Rayleigh number for natural convection along a vertical wall.
The wall is 0.5 m tall and oriented vertically.
The wall surface temperature is 80°C.
The surrounding fluid is air at 25°C.
The air is at atmospheric pressure at sea level (101,325 Pa).
Use the film temperature (average of wall and air temperatures) to evaluate all air properties: T_film = (80 + 25) / 2 = 52.5°C = 325.65 K.
At the film temperature and sea-level pressure, use the following standard air properties: density ρ ≈ 1.086 kg/m³, dynamic viscosity μ ≈ 1.963 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s, thermal conductivity k ≈ 0.02814 W/(m·K), specific heat cp ≈ 1007 J/(kg·K), and Prandtl number Pr ≈ 0.703.
Calculate kinematic viscosity as ν = μ / ρ.
Calculate thermal diffusivity as α = k / (ρ · cp).
Use the ideal gas approximation for the volumetric thermal expansion coefficient: β = 1 / T_film = 1 / 325.65 K⁻¹.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Use the wall height L = 0.5 m as the characteristic length.
Calculate the Grashof number as Gr = (g · β · ΔT · L³) / ν², where ΔT = 80 − 25 = 55°C.
Calculate the Rayleigh number as Ra = Gr × Pr.
Report the final Rayleigh number Ra as the result.
