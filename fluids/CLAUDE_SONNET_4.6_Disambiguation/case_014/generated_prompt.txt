Determine the oblique shock wave angle (β) for a wedge in a supersonic airflow using the following parameters and method.

The freestream Mach number is M = 3.0.
The wedge half-angle (flow deflection angle) is θ = 10 degrees.
The working fluid is air with a ratio of specific heats γ = 1.4.
Use the Theta-Beta-Mach (TBM) relation to solve for the shock angle β.
The TBM relation is: tan(θ) = 2 cot(β) × [M²sin²(β) − 1] / [M²(γ + cos(2β)) + 2].
Solve for the weak shock solution of β, which is the physically relevant attached oblique shock solution (the smaller of the two possible β values).
The valid range for β is between the Mach angle μ = arcsin(1/M) and 90 degrees.
Compute the Mach angle μ = arcsin(1/3.0) ≈ 19.47 degrees as the lower bound for β.
Iterate or use a numerical root-finding method to solve the TBM equation for β given θ = 10° and M = 3.0.
Report the resulting weak shock angle β in degrees, rounded to two decimal places.
Verify that the solution satisfies the attached shock condition (β < β_max for the given Mach number).
