Calculate the hydraulic power required to pump water from a lower reservoir to an upper reservoir.
Use a flow rate of 0.02 cubic meters per second.
Use a height difference (static head) of 30 meters between the lower and upper reservoir.
Use the density of water as 1000 kg per cubic meter.
Use gravitational acceleration as 9.81 m/s².
Use a pump efficiency of 80% (0.80).
Calculate the hydraulic power using the formula: P_hydraulic = density × g × Q × h.
Calculate the required input power using the formula: P_input = P_hydraulic / efficiency.
Report the hydraulic power in watts and kilowatts.
Report the required input power (accounting for pump efficiency) in watts and kilowatts.
