Calculate the excess pressure inside a soap bubble.
The diameter of the soap bubble is 10 mm, which gives a radius of 5 mm (0.005 m).
The surface tension of the soap solution is 0.035 N/m.
Use the formula for excess pressure inside a soap bubble: P = 4T/r, where T is surface tension and r is the radius.
A soap bubble has two free surfaces, so the factor of 4 (not 2) must be used in the formula.
Substitute the values: P = 4 × 0.035 / 0.005.
Compute the result and express the final answer in Pascals (Pa).
