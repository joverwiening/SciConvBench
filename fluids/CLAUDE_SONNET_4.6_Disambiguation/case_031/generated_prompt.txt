Calculate the pressure at a depth of 2000 m in the ocean.
Use a seawater density of 1025 kg/m³.
Use the standard acceleration due to gravity of 9.81 m/s².
Apply the hydrostatic pressure formula: P = P0 + ρgh, where P0 is atmospheric pressure at the surface (101325 Pa), ρ is the density of seawater, g is gravitational acceleration, and h is the depth.
Compute the gauge pressure (ρgh) and the absolute pressure (P0 + ρgh).
Express the final result in Pascals (Pa) and, if helpful, also in atmospheres (atm) or megapascals (MPa).
