Calculate the lift force on a wing using the standard aerodynamic lift equation: L = 0.5 * rho * V² * S * C_L.
Use air density rho = 1.1 kg/m³.
Use airspeed V = 80 m/s.
Use wing reference area S = 20 m².
Use lift coefficient C_L = 0.45.
Compute 0.5 * 1.1 * (80²) * 20 * 0.45 to obtain the lift force in Newtons.
The flight condition is steady (no acceleration), so the lift equation applies directly without correction factors.
Report the final result as a single numeric value in Newtons.
