Calculate the power output of a steam turbine using the given thermodynamic conditions.
The mass flow rate of steam entering the turbine is 10 kg/s.
The specific enthalpy of steam at the turbine inlet is 3200 kJ/kg.
The specific enthalpy of steam at the turbine outlet is 2400 kJ/kg.
Apply the steady-flow energy equation for an adiabatic turbine: Power = mass flow rate × (inlet enthalpy − outlet enthalpy).
Compute the specific enthalpy drop as 3200 kJ/kg − 2400 kJ/kg = 800 kJ/kg.
Compute the power output as 10 kg/s × 800 kJ/kg = 8000 kW (8 MW).
Assume no heat loss to surroundings (adiabatic process) and neglect changes in kinetic and potential energy.
Report the final power output as 8000 kW or 8 MW.
