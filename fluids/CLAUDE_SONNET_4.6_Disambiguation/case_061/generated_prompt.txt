Calculate the lift force generated by an airfoil using the lift equation: L = 0.5 × ρ × v² × A × CL.
Use air density ρ = 1.225 kg/m³ (standard sea-level conditions, ISA standard atmosphere).
Use airspeed v = 85 m/s.
Use wing reference area A = 12 m².
Use lift coefficient CL = 0.45.
Compute dynamic pressure as 0.5 × 1.225 × (85)² = 4423.4375 Pa.
Multiply dynamic pressure by area and lift coefficient: 4423.4375 × 12 × 0.45.
The resulting lift force is approximately 23,886.6 Newtons (≈ 23.89 kN).
Report the final answer as L ≈ 23,887 N or 23.89 kN.
