Calculate the shear stress for a Bingham plastic fluid using the Bingham plastic model equation: tau = tau_y + mu_p * gamma_dot.
Use a yield stress (tau_y) of 20 Pa.
Use a plastic viscosity (mu_p) of 0.05 Pa·s.
Use a shear rate (gamma_dot) of 50 s⁻¹.
Apply the formula: tau = 20 + (0.05 * 50).
Compute the plastic viscosity contribution as 0.05 * 50 = 2.5 Pa.
Add the yield stress to the viscous contribution: tau = 20 + 2.5 = 22.5 Pa.
Report the final shear stress as 22.5 Pa.
This calculation assumes the shear rate of 50 s⁻¹ exceeds the yield point, meaning the fluid is flowing and the Bingham model applies.
