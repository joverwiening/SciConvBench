Calculate the Peclet number for convective heat transfer over a small object.
The characteristic length is 5 cm, which is 0.05 m.
The flow velocity is 0.1 m/s.
The thermal diffusivity (alpha) is 1.2e-7 m²/s.
Use the thermal Peclet number formula: Pe = (U * L) / alpha.
Substitute the values: Pe = (0.1 * 0.05) / 1.2e-7.
Compute the numerator: 0.1 * 0.05 = 0.005 m²/s.
Compute the result: Pe = 0.005 / 1.2e-7 = 41,666.67 (approximately 4.17 × 10^4).
Report the Peclet number as approximately 41,667, indicating that convective heat transport strongly dominates over diffusive heat transport for this flow condition.
