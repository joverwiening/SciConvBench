Assume the steam turbine inlet pressure is 10 MPa and the inlet temperature is 540 °C.  
Assume the turbine exhaust (condenser) pressure is 0.1 MPa (saturated vapor condition).  
Assume an isentropic efficiency for the turbine of 85 %.  
Use standard steam tables to obtain the specific enthalpy at the inlet (h₁ ≈ 3440 kJ/kg) and the specific enthalpy of saturated vapor at the exhaust pressure (h₂s ≈ 2778 kJ/kg).  
Calculate the actual exhaust enthalpy: h₂ = h₁ – ηₜ·(h₁ – h₂s) ≈ 3440 kJ/kg – 0.85·(3440 kJ/kg – 2778 kJ/kg) ≈ 2877 kJ/kg.  
Determine the specific enthalpy drop: Δh = h₁ – h₂ ≈ 562 kJ/kg.  
Compute the turbine power output: P = ṁ·Δh = 10 kg/s × 562 kJ/kg = 5620 kW ≈ 5.6 MW.
