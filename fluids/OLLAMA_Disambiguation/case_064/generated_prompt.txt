Determine the rate of heat transfer through the concrete wall using steady‑state one‑dimensional conduction.

Assume the thermal conductivity of normal‑weight concrete is 1.4 W/(m·K).

Use the formula Q = k × A × ΔT / L, where:
- k is the thermal conductivity (1.4 W/(m·K)),
- A is the wall surface area (15 m²),
- ΔT is the temperature difference across the wall (20 °C),
- L is the wall thickness (0.2 m).

Calculate Q in watts (W) and report the result.
