Calculate the hydraulic efficiency of the Pelton wheel turbine using the formula η = (shaft power) / (ρ g Q H).  
Use water density ρ = 1000 kg/m³ and gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².  
Compute the hydraulic power: ρ g Q H = 1000 × 9.81 × 0.5 × 300 = 1,471,500 W (≈ 1471.5 kW).  
Divide the actual shaft power (1300 kW) by the hydraulic power to obtain η = 1300 / 1471.5 ≈ 0.8835.  
Express the efficiency as a percentage: hydraulic efficiency ≈ 88.3 %.
