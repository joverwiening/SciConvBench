Assume the water temperature at the turbine inlet is 20 °C, giving a water vapor pressure of approximately 2.34 kPa.  
Assume the inlet pressure equals atmospheric pressure (101.3 kPa).  
Use water density ρ = 1000 kg/m³ and gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².  
Apply Thoma’s cavitation factor formula: σ = (p_inlet – p_vapor) / (ρ · g · H), where H is the total head (50 m).  
Calculate σ = (101.3 kPa – 2.34 kPa) / (1000 kg/m³ · 9.81 m/s² · 50 m) ≈ 0.20.  
Report the Thoma’s cavitation factor σ as approximately 0.20 (dimensionless).
