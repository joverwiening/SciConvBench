Assume 1 kg of nitrogen gas at an initial temperature of 300 K and an initial pressure of 100 kPa.  
Use the specific gas constant for nitrogen R = 296.8 J · kg⁻¹ · K⁻¹.  
Treat the compression as a polytropic process with exponent n = 1.25, final pressure 600 kPa.  

1. Compute the initial specific volume: v₁ = R·T₁/P₁ = (296.8 J · kg⁻¹ · K⁻¹ × 300 K) / 100 000 Pa ≈ 0.890 m³ · kg⁻¹.  
2. Compute the final specific volume using the polytropic relation: v₂ = v₁·(P₁/P₂)^{1/n} ≈ 0.890 × (100 kPa/600 kPa)^{0.8} ≈ 0.212 m³ · kg⁻¹.  
3. Calculate the work for a polytropic process:  
   W = (P₂·v₂ − P₁·v₁) / (1 − n)  
   = [(600 000 Pa × 0.212 m³) − (100 000 Pa × 0.890 m³)] / (1 − 1.25)  
   = (127 440 J − 89 040 J) / (−0.25)  
   ≈ −1.54 × 10⁵ J.  

The negative sign indicates work is done on the gas.  
Therefore, the compression requires approximately 154 kJ of work.
