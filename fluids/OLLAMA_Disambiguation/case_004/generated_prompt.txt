Use the specific heat ratio for air γ = 1.4 and the specific gas constant R = 287 J/(kg·K).  
Calculate the speed of sound at the given temperature T = 223.15 K with a = sqrt(γ · R · T).  
Compute a = sqrt(1.4 × 287 × 223.15) ≈ 299.4 m/s.  
Determine the Mach number M by dividing the flow speed by the speed of sound: M = 500 m/s ÷ 299.4 m/s ≈ 1.67.  
Report the Mach number as approximately 1.67.
