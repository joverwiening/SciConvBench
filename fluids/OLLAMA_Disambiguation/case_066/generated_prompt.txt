Assume a standard vapor‑compression refrigeration cycle using refrigerant R‑134a.  
Assume the evaporator operates at –5 °C (268 K) and the condenser operates at 35 °C (308 K).  
Use the refrigerant property tables for R‑134a to obtain the saturated liquid enthalpy at the condenser pressure (h₁) and the saturated vapor enthalpy at the evaporator pressure (h₄).  
Assume an isentropic compressor efficiency of 80 %.  
Calculate the isentropic outlet enthalpy (h₂s) from the saturated vapor state at the evaporator pressure and the corresponding compressor outlet pressure (condenser pressure).  
Determine the actual compressor outlet enthalpy (h₂) using h₂ = h₁ + (h₂s – h₁)/η_isentropic.  
Compute the refrigeration effect per kilogram of refrigerant: Q_L = h₁ – h₄.  
Compute the compressor work per kilogram: W_comp = h₂ – h₁.  
Calculate the coefficient of performance: COP = Q_L / W_comp.  
Report the COP value rounded to two decimal places.
