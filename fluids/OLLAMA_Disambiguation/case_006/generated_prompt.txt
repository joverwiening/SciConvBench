Solve for the steady velocity profile in a planar Couette flow between two infinite parallel plates separated by a distance of 0.01 m (10 mm). Assume the fluid is incompressible, Newtonian, and the flow is laminar, fully developed, and steady with no pressure gradient in the streamwise direction. The lower plate is stationary (velocity = 0 m/s) and the upper plate moves in the streamwise direction with a constant velocity of 5 m/s. Use a Cartesian coordinate system with y = 0 at the stationary lower plate and y = 0.01 m at the moving upper plate. Derive the analytical expression for the streamwise velocity u(y) as a function of y, and provide the numerical form of the profile. State any derived quantities such as the constant shear stress if relevant. The final velocity profile should be expressed as u(y) = (5 / 0.01) · y = 500 y (with y in meters and u in meters per second).
