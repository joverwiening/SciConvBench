Find the torque required to rotate a disk of radius 0.2 m close to a wall in a viscous fluid. The disk rotates at an angular velocity of 2π rad/s. The fluid has a dynamic viscosity of 0.1 Pa·s. The gap between the disk surface and the wall is 1 mm. Use the shear‑stress approximation τ = μ ω r / gap and integrate the differential torque dT = τ · (2π r dr) · r over the disk radius. The resulting torque is T = (π/2) · μ · ω · R⁴ / gap, which evaluates to approximately 1.58 N·m. Provide this torque value as the answer.
