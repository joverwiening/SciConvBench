Assume the phase‑change material is water/ice, with a specific heat capacity cₚ of 4186 J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹ and a latent heat of fusion L of 334 000 J kg⁻¹.  
Use the given temperature difference ΔT of 10 °C (which equals 10 K).  
Calculate the Stefan number as Ste = (cₚ · ΔT) / L = (4186 × 10) / 334000 ≈ 0.125.  
Report the Stefan number as approximately 0.125.
