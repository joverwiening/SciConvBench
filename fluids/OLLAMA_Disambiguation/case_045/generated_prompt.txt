Assume the fluid is water at a typical ambient temperature (≈20 °C), for which the given vapor pressure of 2.5 kPa is appropriate.  
Use the standard cavitation index formula:  

σ = (P_downstream − P_vapor) / (P_upstream − P_vapor)  

where P_upstream = 400 kPa, P_downstream = 150 kPa, and P_vapor = 2.5 kPa.  

Calculate:  

σ = (150 kPa − 2.5 kPa) / (400 kPa − 2.5 kPa) = 147.5 kPa / 397.5 kPa ≈ 0.37.  

Report the cavitation index as approximately 0.37.  
Interpretation: Since σ is below 0.5, cavitation is likely to occur under these operating conditions.
