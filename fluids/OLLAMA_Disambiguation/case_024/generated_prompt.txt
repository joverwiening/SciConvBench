Find the Rayleigh number for natural convection along a vertical wall that is 0.5 m tall, with the wall surface temperature at 80 °C and the surrounding ambient air temperature at 25 °C.  

Use the average (film) temperature of 52.5 °C (≈ 325.65 K) to evaluate the air properties.  

Assume standard properties of air at 52.5 °C:  
- Density ρ ≈ 1.09 kg m⁻³  
- Dynamic viscosity μ ≈ 1.96 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s  
- Thermal conductivity k ≈ 0.030 W m⁻¹ K⁻¹  
- Specific heat at constant pressure cp ≈ 1006 J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹  

Compute derived properties:  
- Kinematic viscosity ν = μ/ρ ≈ 1.80 × 10⁻⁵ m² s⁻¹  
- Thermal diffusivity α = k/(ρ cp) ≈ 2.74 × 10⁻⁵ m² s⁻¹  
- Prandtl number Pr = ν/α ≈ 0.66  
- Thermal expansion coefficient for ideal gas β = 1/T_film ≈ 1/325.65 ≈ 3.07 × 10⁻³ K⁻¹  

Calculate the Grashof number:  
Gr = g β ΔT L³ / ν², where g = 9.81 m s⁻², ΔT = 80 °C − 25 °C = 55 K, L = 0.5 m.  
Gr ≈ 9.81 × 3.07 × 10⁻³ × 55 × (0.5)³ / (1.80 × 10⁻⁵)² ≈ 6.4 × 10⁸  

Finally, compute the Rayleigh number:  
Ra = Gr × Pr ≈ 6.4 × 10⁸ × 0.66 ≈ 4.2 × 10⁸.  

Thus, the Rayleigh number for the specified natural‑convection scenario is approximately 4.2 × 10⁸.
