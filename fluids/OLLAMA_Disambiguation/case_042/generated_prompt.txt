Calculate the cross‑sectional area of the jet: A = π · (d/2)² = π · (0.01 m / 2)² = 7.85 × 10⁻⁵ m².  
Determine the mass flow rate: ṁ = ρ · A · v = 1000 kg/m³ · 7.85 × 10⁻⁵ m² · 15 m/s = 1.18 kg/s.  
Assume the jet is stopped completely by the flat plate, so the momentum change per second equals the force: F = ṁ · v = 1.18 kg/s · 15 m/s = 17.7 N.  
State the final result: The water jet exerts a force of approximately 17.7 newtons on the flat plate.
