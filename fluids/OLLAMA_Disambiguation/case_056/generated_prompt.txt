Determine the stagnation pressure at the nose of a jet flying at Mach 1.5 in an atmosphere where the ambient static pressure is 20 kPa.  
Assume standard‑atmosphere conditions for the temperature corresponding to a static pressure of 20 kPa (approximately 12.5 km altitude, temperature ≈ 216.65 K).  
Assume air behaves as an ideal gas with ratio of specific heats γ = 1.4.  

Compute the stagnation pressure using the isentropic relation:  

p₀ = p · (1 + ((γ − 1)/2)·M²)^(γ/(γ‑1))  

where p = 20 kPa, M = 1.5, γ = 1.4.  

The term (1 + ((γ − 1)/2)·M²) equals 1 + 0.2·(1.5)² = 1.45.  

Raise 1.45 to the power γ/(γ‑1) = 3.5, giving (1.45)³·⁵ ≈ 3.666.  

Thus p₀ ≈ 20 kPa × 3.666 ≈ 73 kPa.  

Report the stagnation pressure as approximately 73 kPa (rounded to three significant figures).
