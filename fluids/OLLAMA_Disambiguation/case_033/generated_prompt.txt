Calculate the Peclet number using the formula Pe = (U × L) / D, where U is the flow velocity, L is the characteristic length, and D is the diffusion coefficient. Use U = 0.1 m/s, L = 0.05 m (converted from 5 cm), and D = 1.2 × 10⁻⁷ m²/s. Compute Pe = (0.1 × 0.05) / (1.2 × 10⁻⁷) ≈ 4.17 × 10⁴. Output the Peclet number value.
