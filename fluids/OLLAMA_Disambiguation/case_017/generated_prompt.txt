Calculate the speed of sound in the gas mixture at 300 K assuming ideal‑gas behavior and using γ ≈ 1.66 for helium and γ ≈ 1.67 for argon.  
Use the mole fractions He = 0.70 and Ar = 0.30.  
Determine the molar mass of the mixture as M = 0.70·4.0026 + 0.30·39.948 = 0.014786 kg mol⁻¹.  
Compute the specific gas constant for the mixture: Rₛ = 8.314462618 / 0.014786 = 562.5 J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹.  
For each component, calculate Cv = Rᵢ / (γ − 1) and Cp = γ·Cv, where Rᵢ = 8.314462618 / Mᵢ.  
Obtain the mixture heat capacities by mole‑fraction weighting:  
Cv,mix = 0.70·Cv,He + 0.30·Cv,Ar ≈ 2297.7 J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹.  
Cp,mix = 0.70·Cp,He + 0.30·Cp,Ar ≈ 3822.2 J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹.  
Compute the effective ratio of specific heats: γₘᵢₓ = Cp,mix / Cv,mix ≈ 1.663.  
Finally, calculate the speed of sound: c = √(γₘᵢₓ·Rₛ·T) = √(1.663 · 562.5 · 300) ≈ 5.30 × 10² m s⁻¹, i.e., about 529 m s⁻¹.
