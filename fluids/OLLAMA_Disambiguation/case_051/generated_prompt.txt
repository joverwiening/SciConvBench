Assume the balloon operates at sea level under standard atmospheric conditions: pressure = 101.325 kPa and temperature = 15 °C (288 K).  
Use the ideal gas law to calculate the densities of air and helium at these conditions.  
Compute the density of air (ρ_air) ≈ 1.225 kg/m³ and the density of helium (ρ_He) ≈ 0.1785 kg/m³.  
Calculate the buoyant lift force using the formula: Buoyant force = (ρ_air − ρ_He) × V × g, where V = 500 m³ and g = 9.80665 m/s².  
The resulting buoyant lift force is approximately 5.3 kN (≈ 540 kgf).
