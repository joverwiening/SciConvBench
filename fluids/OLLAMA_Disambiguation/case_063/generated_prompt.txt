Assume the water temperature is 20 °C, which gives a density of approximately 998 kg/m³ and a dynamic viscosity of about 1.0 × 10⁻³ Pa·s.  
Calculate the Reynolds number using Re = ρ · v · D / μ, where the pipe inner diameter D = 0.04 m and the average velocity v = 1.5 m/s.  
Re ≈ 998 kg/m³ × 1.5 m/s × 0.04 m / 1.0 × 10⁻³ Pa·s ≈ 6.0 × 10⁴.  
Since the Reynolds number is greater than 4,000, the flow in the smooth pipe is turbulent.
