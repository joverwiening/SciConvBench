Calculate the excess pressure inside a soap bubble with a diameter of 10 mm using a surface tension of 0.035 N/m.  
Convert the diameter to radius: radius = 10 mm / 2 = 5 mm = 0.005 m.  
Use the pressure‑difference formula for a soap bubble with two surfaces: ΔP = 4 γ / r.  
Substitute the values: ΔP = 4 × 0.035 N/m / 0.005 m = 0.14 / 0.005 = 28 Pa.  
The excess pressure inside the bubble is 28 Pa.
