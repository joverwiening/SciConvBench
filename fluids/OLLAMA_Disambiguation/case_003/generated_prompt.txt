Calculate the terminal velocity of the 2 mm spherical particle falling in oil using Stokes' law for low Reynolds number flow.

Use the formula  
v = ((ρ_particle – ρ_oil) · g · d²) / (18 · μ),

where  
ρ_particle = 7800 kg/m³ (particle density),  
ρ_oil = 890 kg/m³ (oil density),  
g = 9.81 m/s² (standard gravitational acceleration),  
d = 2 mm = 0.002 m (particle diameter),  
μ = 0.29 Pa·s (dynamic viscosity of the oil).

Compute v and report the result in meters per second.
