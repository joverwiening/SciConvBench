Solve the steady one‑dimensional heat conduction equation with uniform internal heat generation for a plane wall of thickness 0.20 m, thermal conductivity k = 15 W/(m·K), and volumetric heat generation rate q‴ = 2 × 10⁵ W/m³. Impose constant temperature boundary conditions T = 50 °C at both surfaces (x = 0 and x = 0.20 m).  

Derive the temperature distribution:  
T(x) = 50 + (q‴ / (2k)) · x · (L − x), where L = 0.20 m.  

Compute the maximum temperature at the wall mid‑plane (x = L/2):  
T_max = 50 + (q‴ L²) / (8k) ≈ 116.7 °C.  

Determine the heat flux at each surface:  
q''(x = 0) = −k (dT/dx)_{x=0} = −q‴ L / 2 ≈ −2.0 × 10⁴ W/m² (heat leaving the wall).  
q''(x = L) = −k (dT/dx)_{x=L} = +q‴ L / 2 ≈ +2.0 × 10⁴ W/m² (heat leaving the opposite surface).  

Provide the analytical solution, the expression for the temperature profile, the maximum temperature value, and the surface heat fluxes.
