Assume the packed‑bed column has a circular cross‑section with a diameter of 0.10 m, giving a cross‑sectional area of 7.85 × 10⁻³ m².  
Assume the bed length is 1.00 m.  
Assume the spheres have a uniform diameter of 5 mm (0.005 m).  
Assume a random packing void fraction (porosity) of ε = 0.40.  
Assume air at 20 °C and 1 atm with density ρ = 1.20 kg m⁻³ and dynamic viscosity μ = 1.80 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s.  
Assume a superficial gas velocity of 0.5 m s⁻¹, giving a volumetric flow rate Q = 0.5 m s⁻¹ × 7.85 × 10⁻³ m² = 3.93 × 10⁻³ m³ s⁻¹.  

Calculate the pressure drop using the Ergun equation:  

ΔP/L = [150 (1‑ε)² μ U] / [ε³ dₚ²] + [1.75 (1‑ε) ρ U²] / [ε³ dₚ]  

where U = 0.5 m s⁻¹, dₚ = 0.005 m, ε = 0.40, μ = 1.80 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s, ρ = 1.20 kg m⁻³.  

First term: 150 × 0.6² × 1.80 × 10⁻⁵ × 0.5 / (0.40³ × 0.005²) ≈ 3.04 × 10² Pa m⁻¹.  
Second term: 1.75 × 0.6 × 1.20 × 0.5² / (0.40³ × 0.005) ≈ 9.84 × 10² Pa m⁻¹.  

Total pressure‑drop gradient: ΔP/L ≈ 1.29 × 10³ Pa m⁻¹.  

For the assumed 1 m bed length, the pressure drop ΔP ≈ 1.29 kPa (≈0.013 atm).
