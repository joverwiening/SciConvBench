Find the center of pressure on a vertical rectangular plate that is 1 m wide and 2 m tall, with its top edge at the free surface of water. Use a water density of 1000 kg/m³ and gravitational acceleration of 9.81 m/s². Compute the centroid depth as 1 m, the area as 2 m², and the second moment of area about the centroidal horizontal axis as (1 m × (2 m)³)/12 = 0.667 m⁴. Then calculate the depth of the center of pressure as (I_G + A ȳ²)/(A ȳ) = (0.667 + 2 × 1²)/(2 × 1) ≈ 1.33 m below the free surface. The center of pressure is therefore located 1.33 m down from the top edge of the plate.
