Use the standard sea‑level air density ρ = 1.225 kg/m³.  
Calculate the dynamic pressure q = ½ ρ V², where V = 85 m/s.  
Compute q = 0.5 × 1.225 × 85² = 4 425.31 Pa.  
Multiply the dynamic pressure by the planform area S = 12 m² to obtain the aerodynamic force before coefficient application: q S = 4 425.31 × 12 = 53 103.75 N.  
Apply the lift coefficient Cl = 0.45: Lift L = q S Cl = 53 103.75 × 0.45 = 23 896.69 N.  
Report the lift force as approximately 2.39 × 10⁴ N.
