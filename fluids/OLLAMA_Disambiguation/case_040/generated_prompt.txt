Assume the gas behaves as an ideal diatomic gas with a specific heat ratio γ = 1.4.  
Use the stagnation temperature relation T₀ = T_static × [1 + ((γ − 1)/2) M²].  
Substitute the given static temperature T_static = 250 K and Mach number M = 2.0.  
Calculate the term ((γ − 1)/2) M² = ((1.4 − 1)/2) × (2.0)² = 0.2 × 4 = 0.8.  
Thus T₀ = 250 K × (1 + 0.8) = 250 K × 1.8 = 450 K.  
Report the stagnation temperature as 450 K.
