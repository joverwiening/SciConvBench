Determine the weak (attached) oblique shock angle β for a Mach 3.0 supersonic airflow over a wedge with a deflection angle θ = 10°.

Use the θ‑β‑M relation for a perfect gas with γ = 1.4:

tan θ = 2 cot β · (M² sin²β − 1) / [M²(γ + cos 2β) + 2]

Solve this equation for β, selecting the smaller (weak) solution.

The numerical solution is β ≈ 27.4° (rounded to one decimal place).
