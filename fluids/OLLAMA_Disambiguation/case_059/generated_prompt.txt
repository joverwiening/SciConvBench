Calculate the frontal area of the billboard as width multiplied by height (10 m × 4 m = 40 m²).  
Use the drag force formula F = ½ · ρ · V² · C_d · A, where ρ = 1.225 kg/m³, V = 30 m/s, C_d = 1.2, and A = 40 m².  
Compute: ½ · 1.225 = 0.6125; 0.6125 · 30² = 0.6125 · 900 = 551.25; 551.25 · 1.2 = 661.5; 661.5 · 40 = 26 460 N.  
The drag force on the billboard during the wind storm is 26 460 newtons.
