Assume the gas flowing through the 100 nm wide channel is dry air at standard laboratory conditions (temperature = 300 K, pressure = 1 atm).  
Use the molecular diameter of air (≈ 0.37 nm) to calculate the mean free path λ with the kinetic theory formula:  
λ = (k_B · T) / (√2 · π · d² · p), where k_B is Boltzmann’s constant (1.38 × 10⁻²³ J/K), T is temperature, d is molecular diameter, and p is pressure (converted to Pa).  
Compute λ using the assumed values.  
Calculate the Knudsen number as Kn = λ / L, where L is the characteristic length (the channel width, 100 nm).  
Report the numerical value of Kn and indicate whether the flow regime is continuum (Kn < 0.001), slip (0.001 ≤ Kn < 0.1), transition (0.1 ≤ Kn < 10), or free molecular (Kn ≥ 10).
