Determine the specific net work output of the ideal Brayton cycle using the given data.

Use the pressure ratio r = 10 and the specific heat ratio γ = 1.40 to compute the temperature rise factor:  
\( r^{(\gamma-1)/\gamma} = 10^{(1.40-1)/1.40} = 10^{0.285714} \approx 1.931 \).

Compute the compressor outlet temperature:  
\( T_2 = T_1 \times r^{(\gamma-1)/\gamma} = 300\ \text{K} \times 1.931 \approx 579.3\ \text{K} \).

Compute the turbine outlet temperature:  
\( T_4 = T_3 / r^{(\gamma-1)/\gamma} = 1200\ \text{K} / 1.931 \approx 621.5\ \text{K} \).

Calculate the specific net work:  
\( w_{net} = c_p \big[ (T_3 - T_4) - (T_2 - T_1) \big] \)  
\( = 1.005\ \text{kJ/(kg·K)} \times \big[ (1200 - 621.5) - (579.3 - 300) \big] \)  
\( = 1.005 \times (578.5 - 279.3) \)  
\( = 1.005 \times 299.2 \approx 300.7\ \text{kJ/kg} \).

Therefore, the specific work output of the Brayton cycle is approximately 301 kJ per kilogram of air.
