Assume the fluid is water at 20 °C with a density of 998 kg/m³.  
Assume a discharge coefficient (Cₙ) of 0.98 for the Venturi meter.  
Assume the upstream pipe diameter (D₁) is 0.10 m and the throat diameter (D₂) is 0.05 m.  
Calculate the cross‑sectional areas: A₁ = π D₁² / 4 and A₂ = π D₂² / 4.  
Compute the area ratio β = A₂ / A₁ (β = 0.25 for the assumed diameters).  
Use the pressure drop ΔP = 20 kPa (20 000 Pa) in the Venturi flow equation:  
v₂ = √[ 2 ΔP / (ρ (1 – β²)) ] where v₂ is the velocity at the throat.  
Determine the volumetric flow rate: Q = Cₙ · A₂ · v₂.  
With the assumed values, the flow rate Q ≈ 0.0126 m³/s (≈ 12.6 L/s).  
Report the calculated flow rate and state all assumptions used.
