Calculate the lift on a rotating cylinder in air using the Magnus effect.

Assume the cylinder length is 1 m (a typical value for engineering estimates).

Use the following parameters:
- Cylinder radius R = 0.1 m.
- Angular velocity ω = 200 rad/s.
- Free‑stream airflow speed V = 15 m/s relative to the cylinder.
- Air density ρ = 1.225 kg/m³ (sea‑level standard).
- Cylinder diameter D = 2 R = 0.2 m.
- Cylinder length L = 1 m (assumed).

Compute the spin ratio α = (ω R) / V.

Calculate the lift coefficient using the linear Magnus approximation C_L = 2 π α.

Determine the lift per unit length: L' = 0.5 · ρ · V² · C_L · D.

Obtain the total lift: Lift = L' · L.

Report the lift value in newtons (N).
