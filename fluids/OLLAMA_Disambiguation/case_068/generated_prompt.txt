\(W = m\,R\,T \,\ln\!\left(\dfrac{V_{2}}{V_{1}}\right)\).

Compute the volume ratio \(V_{2}/V_{1} = 5\) and its natural logarithm \(\ln(5) = 1.60944\).  
Calculate the work: \(W = 2 \times 0.2968 \times 300 \times 1.60944 \approx 2.87 \times 10^{2}\; \text{kJ}\).
