Assume the ambient air temperature is 25 °C (room temperature).  
Use the following air properties at 25 °C: k = 0.0262 W/(m·K), ν = 1.589 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s, Pr = 0.71.  
Compute the Reynolds number: Re = U·L/ν = 5 m/s × 1 m / 1.589 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s ≈ 3.15 × 10⁵.  
Since Re < 5 × 10⁵, treat the flow as laminar over the entire plate.  
Calculate the local Nusselt number at the plate end using the laminar correlation: Nu = 0.332 Re^{0.5} Pr^{1/3}.  
Compute Re^{0.5} ≈ √(3.15 × 10⁵) ≈ 5.62 × 10² and Pr^{1/3} ≈ 0.71^{1/3} ≈ 0.89.  
Thus Nu ≈ 0.332 × 5.62 × 10² × 0.89 ≈ 1.66 × 10².  
Determine the average convective heat‑transfer coefficient: h = Nu · k / L ≈ 1.66 × 10² × 0.0262 W/(m·K) / 1 m ≈ 4.4 W/(m²·K).  
Report the convective heat‑transfer coefficient as approximately 4.4 W/(m²·K).
