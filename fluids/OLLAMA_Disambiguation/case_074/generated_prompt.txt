Calculate the degree of reaction for the axial‑flow compressor stage using the supplied velocity data.

Use the absolute inlet and exit flow angles (α₁ = 42°, α₂ = 60°) to obtain the tangential components of the absolute velocities:
Cθ₁ = C₁ · sin α₁, Cθ₂ = C₂ · sin α₂.

Compute the change in tangential velocity:
ΔCθ = Cθ₂ − Cθ₁.

Apply the axial‑flow compressor degree‑of‑reaction formula:
R = 0.5 − (ΔCθ) ⁄ (2 U).
