Assume the rod is a solid circular steel rod with a diameter of 10 mm, giving a cross‑sectional area of approximately 78 mm².  
Use a typical coefficient of thermal expansion for steel of 12 × 10⁻⁶ /°C.  
Use a typical Young’s modulus for steel of 200 GPa.  
Treat the rod as fully constrained so that its total axial strain is zero.  
Calculate the thermal stress with σ = E · α · ΔT, where ΔT = −40 °C (temperature drop).  
Report the resulting stress value in pascals (or megapascals) and indicate that it is compressive.
