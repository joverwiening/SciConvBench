Determine the pressure at a depth of 2000 meters in the ocean and express the result in pascals (Pa). Assume a seawater density of 1025 kg/m³, standard gravitational acceleration of 9.80665 m/s², and a surface atmospheric pressure of 101 325 Pa. Calculate the hydrostatic pressure using the formula P = P₀ + ρ g h, where P₀ is the surface pressure, ρ is the water density, g is gravity, and h is the depth. Output the final pressure value in pascals.
