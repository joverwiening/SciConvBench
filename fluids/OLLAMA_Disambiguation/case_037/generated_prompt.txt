Calculate the efficiency of a Rankine cycle with the following conditions: the boiler operates at 4 MPa and the turbine inlet steam is saturated vapor at that pressure; the condenser operates at 10 kPa (saturation temperature ≈ 45.8 °C); both the turbine and the pump are assumed to be ideal (isentropic efficiencies of 100 %). Use steam‑water property data (e.g., from saturated steam tables) to determine the specific enthalpy at each state: h₁ at the turbine inlet (saturated vapor at 4 MPa), h₂ after isentropic expansion to 10 kPa, h₃ at the condenser outlet (saturated liquid at 10 kPa), and h₄ after isentropic pumping to 4 MPa. Compute the turbine specific work as w_t = h₁ – h₂, the pump specific work as w_p = h₄ – h₃, the net specific work as w_net = w_t – w_p, and the heat added in the boiler as q_in = h₁ – h₃. Finally, calculate the cycle thermal efficiency as η = w_net / q_in. Provide the numerical value of η (as a percentage) based on the obtained enthalpy values.
