Use an air density of 1.225 kg/m³ (standard sea‑level value).  
Calculate the rotor swept area as A = π × (D/2)² = π × 20² ≈ 1 256.64 m².  
Compute the theoretical wind power available: P₀ = 0.5 × ρ × A × V³ = 0.5 × 1.225 × 1 256.64 × 12³ ≈ 1.33 MW.  
Apply the overall efficiency (power coefficient) Cp = 0.35: P = P₀ × Cp ≈ 1.33 MW × 0.35 ≈ 0.466 MW.  
Report the estimated power output as approximately 0.47 MW (≈ 470 kW).
