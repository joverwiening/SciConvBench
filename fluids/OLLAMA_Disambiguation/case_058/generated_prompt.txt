Calculate the water velocity in the pipe: v = Q / A, where Q = 0.02 m³/s and A = π D²/4 with D = 0.05 m, giving v ≈ 10.19 m/s.  
Compute the Darcy–Weisbach friction head loss: h_f = f · (L/D) · (v²/(2g)), using f = 0.02, L = 50 m, D = 0.05 m, g = 9.81 m/s², resulting in h_f ≈ 105.9 m.  
Add the static head: H_total = static head + h_f = 30 m + 105.9 m ≈ 135.9 m.  
Determine the hydraulic power required: P_hyd = ρ · g · Q · H_total, with water density ρ = 1000 kg/m³, giving P_hyd ≈ 26.7 kW.  
Account for pump efficiency η = 0.80: P_input = P_hyd / η ≈ 26.7 kW / 0.80 ≈ 33.3 kW.  
Thus, the pump must be supplied with approximately 33 kW of electrical power to deliver the specified flow rate to the upper reservoir under the given conditions.
