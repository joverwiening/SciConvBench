Use water at 20 °C, assuming a density of 998 kg/m³, a vapor pressure of 2.34 kPa, and an ambient (static) pressure of 101.3 kPa.  
Take the flow velocity as 20 m/s.  
Compute the cavitation number σ using the formula σ = (p − p_v) / (½ ρ V²), where p is the ambient pressure, p_v is the vapor pressure, ρ is the density, and V is the velocity.  
The calculated cavitation number is approximately 0.5.
