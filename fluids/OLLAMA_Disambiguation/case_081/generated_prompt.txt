Calculate the cross‑sectional area of the jet: A = π · (d/2)² = π · (0.05 m / 2)² ≈ 1.963 × 10⁻³ m².  
Determine the mass flow rate: ṁ = ρ · A · V = 1000 kg/m³ · 1.963 × 10⁻³ m² · 20 m/s ≈ 39.27 kg/s.  
Compute the change in velocity magnitude due to deflection: ΔV = 2 · V · sin(Δθ / 2) where Δθ = 120°, so ΔV = 2 · 20 m/s · sin(60°) ≈ 34.64 m/s.  
Calculate the force magnitude exerted on the vane: F = ṁ · ΔV ≈ 39.27 kg/s · 34.64 m/s ≈ 1.36 × 10³ N.
