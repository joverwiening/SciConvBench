The convective heat transfer coefficient (h) is 25 W/(m²·K).
The surface area of the plate (A) is 2 m².
The plate surface temperature (Ts) is 60°C.
The surrounding fluid temperature (Tf) is 20°C.
The temperature difference (ΔT) is calculated as Ts - Tf.
The convective heat transfer rate (Q) is calculated using the formula Q = h * A * ΔT.
