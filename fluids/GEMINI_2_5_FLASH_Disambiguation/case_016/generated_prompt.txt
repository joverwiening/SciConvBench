The task is to calculate the center of pressure for a vertically submerged rectangular plate.
The plate has a width of 1 meter and a height of 2 meters.
The top edge of the plate is located at the free surface of the fluid.
The fluid is water, with a density of 1000 kg/m^3.
The calculation should determine the depth of the center of pressure from the free surface.
The calculation should determine the horizontal position of the center of pressure relative to the plate's vertical centerline.
