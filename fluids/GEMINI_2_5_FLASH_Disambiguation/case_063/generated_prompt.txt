The fluid is water.
The pipe is smooth.
The average flow velocity is 1.5 m/s.
The internal pipe diameter is 4 cm.
The water temperature is assumed to be 20°C.
The density of water at 20°C is 998.2 kg/m³.
The dynamic viscosity of water at 20°C is 1.002 x 10⁻³ Pa·s.
The Reynolds number is calculated as (density * velocity * diameter) / dynamic viscosity.
The calculated Reynolds number is approximately 59772.
The flow is determined to be turbulent because the Reynolds number is greater than 4000.
