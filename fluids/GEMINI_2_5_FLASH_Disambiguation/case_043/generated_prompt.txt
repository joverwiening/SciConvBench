The task is to determine the Taylor number for flow between rotating cylinders.
The inner cylinder has a radius of 5 cm.
The gap between the cylinders is 2 mm.
The inner cylinder rotates at an angular velocity of 100 rpm.
The outer cylinder is stationary.
The fluid is water at 20 degrees Celsius.
The kinematic viscosity of the fluid is 1.004 x 10^-6 m^2/s.
The Taylor number should be calculated using the formula Ta = (2 * omega^2 * R_in * d^3) / nu^2.
Here, omega is the angular velocity of the inner cylinder, R_in is the radius of the inner cylinder, d is the gap width, and nu is the kinematic viscosity of the fluid.
