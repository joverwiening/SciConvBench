The hydraulic diameter of a rectangular duct is calculated.
The duct has a width of 30 cm.
The duct has a height of 10 cm.
The hydraulic diameter is calculated using the formula Dh = 4 * Area / Perimeter.
The area is calculated as width * height.
The perimeter is calculated as 2 * (width + height).
The calculated hydraulic diameter is 15 cm.
