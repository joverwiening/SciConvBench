The rate of heat transfer through the concrete wall is calculated using Fourier's Law of Conduction.
The wall has a thickness of 0.2 meters.
The thermal conductivity of the concrete is 1.4 W/(m·K).
The surface area of the wall is 15 m².
The temperature difference across the wall is 20°C.
The heat transfer rate is determined by multiplying the thermal conductivity by the surface area and the temperature difference, then dividing by the thickness.
