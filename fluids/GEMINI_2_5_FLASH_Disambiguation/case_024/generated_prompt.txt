The Rayleigh number for natural convection along a 0.5 m tall vertical wall shall be determined.
The fluid involved in the natural convection is air.
The surface temperature of the vertical wall is 80°C.
The ambient temperature of the air is 25°C.
Fluid properties for air shall be evaluated at the film temperature, which is the average of the surface and ambient temperatures.
Standard properties for air shall be used for the calculation.
