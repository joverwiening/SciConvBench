The task is to calculate the net buoyant lift force.
The volume of the gas-filled balloon is 500 cubic meters.
The density of the surrounding air is 1.2 kilograms per cubic meter.
The density of the gas inside the balloon is 0.18 kilograms per cubic meter.
The acceleration due to gravity is 9.81 meters per second squared.
The net buoyant lift force is calculated as (density of surrounding air - density of gas inside balloon) * volume of balloon * acceleration due to gravity.
