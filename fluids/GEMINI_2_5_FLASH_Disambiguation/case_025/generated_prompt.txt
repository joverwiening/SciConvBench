The diameter of the soap bubble is 10 mm.
The surface tension of the soap solution is 0.035 N/m.
The radius of the soap bubble is 5 mm (0.005 m).
The excess pressure inside a soap bubble is calculated using the formula ΔP = 4σ/R.
Substitute the values: ΔP = 4 * 0.035 N/m / 0.005 m.
The excess pressure inside the soap bubble is 28 Pa.
