The task is to calculate the force exerted by a water jet on a flat plate.
The water jet has a diameter of 10 mm.
The water jet has a velocity of 15 m/s.
The water jet strikes the flat plate at an angle of 90 degrees, perpendicular to the plate surface.
The density of water is assumed to be 1000 kg/m^3.
The force is calculated using the formula F = ρ * A * V^2, where ρ is the water density, A is the cross-sectional area of the jet, and V is the jet velocity.
The calculated force represents the force normal to the plate.
