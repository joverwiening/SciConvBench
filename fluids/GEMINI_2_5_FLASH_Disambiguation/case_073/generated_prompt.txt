The specific speed of a Francis turbine is to be calculated.
The turbine operates at a rotational speed of 450 revolutions per minute.
The power output of the turbine is 5000 kilowatts.
The net head of the turbine is 120 meters.
The specific speed is calculated using the formula Ns = N * sqrt(P) / H^(5/4).
The calculated specific speed is 120.94.
