Calculate the steady-state heat loss per meter from a horizontal pipe.
The steam temperature inside the pipe is 150°C.
The ambient air temperature around the pipe is 20°C.
The pipe has a nominal size of 2 inches, with an assumed outer diameter of 60.3 mm.
The pipe is insulated with 25 mm of glass wool.
The thermal conductivity of the glass wool insulation is 0.04 W/m·K.
The emissivity of the outer surface of the insulation is assumed to be 0.9.
Assume the inner surface temperature of the pipe is equal to the steam temperature.
The calculation should account for heat transfer by conduction through the insulation, and by natural convection and radiation from the outer surface of the insulation to the ambient air.
