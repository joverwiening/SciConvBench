Calculate Thoma's cavitation factor (sigma) for a Francis turbine.
Use a total head (H) of 50 meters.
Use an atmospheric pressure head (Ha) of 10.3 meters of water.
Use a vapor pressure head (Hv) of 0.3 meters of water.
Use a suction head (Hs) of 2 meters of water.
The calculated Thoma's cavitation factor (sigma) is 0.16.
