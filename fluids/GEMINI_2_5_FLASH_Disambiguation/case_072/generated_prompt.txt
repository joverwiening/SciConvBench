The task is to calculate the hydraulic efficiency of a Pelton wheel turbine.
The Pelton wheel operates under a net head of 300 meters.
The velocity ratio (u/Vj) is assumed to be 0.47 based on best practices for optimal efficiency.
The bucket outlet angle (β) is assumed to be 15 degrees based on best practices.
The coefficient of velocity for the bucket (k) is assumed to be 0.97 based on best practices.
The hydraulic efficiency is calculated using the formula η_h = 2 * (u/Vj) * (1 - (u/Vj)) * (1 + k * cos(β)).
Substituting the assumed values, the hydraulic efficiency is approximately 96.76%.
