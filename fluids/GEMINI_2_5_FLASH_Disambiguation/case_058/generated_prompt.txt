The system shall calculate the power required to pump water.
The calculation shall use a vertical height difference of 30 meters.
The calculation shall use a desired flow rate of 0.02 cubic meters per second.
The calculation shall use a pump efficiency of 0.80.
The density of water shall be assumed as 1000 kg/m³.
The acceleration due to gravity shall be assumed as 9.81 m/s².
The calculated power shall be expressed in Watts.
