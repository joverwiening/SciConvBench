The Weber number for a 2 mm diameter moving liquid fuel droplet will be determined.
The relative velocity of the fuel droplet is 30 m/s.
The surface tension of the fuel droplet is 0.03 N/m.
The density of the surrounding fluid will be assumed to be 1.225 kg/m^3, representing air at standard conditions.
The Weber number will be calculated using the formula We = (rho * U^2 * L) / sigma.
