Calculate the lift force for a NACA 0012 airfoil at 4 degrees angle of attack.
The airfoil has a chord length of 1 meter.
The airfoil has a span of 10 meters.
The airfoil moves at 85 m/s through standard sea-level air.
Use standard sea-level air properties: density 1.225 kg/m³, temperature 15°C, pressure 101325 Pa.
Apply appropriate aerodynamic theory or computational methods to determine the lift coefficient.
Calculate lift force using the formula: Lift = 0.5 × density × velocity² × wing area × lift coefficient.
The wing area equals chord length multiplied by span.
Report the final lift force in Newtons.
