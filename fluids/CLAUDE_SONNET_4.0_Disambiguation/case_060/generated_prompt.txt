Calculate the thermal efficiency of a Carnot heat engine operating between a hot reservoir at 800 K and a cold reservoir at 300 K.
Use the Carnot efficiency formula: η = 1 - (T_cold / T_hot).
Substitute the given temperatures: η = 1 - (300 K / 800 K).
Perform the calculation to determine the efficiency as a percentage.
The thermal efficiency should be expressed as both a decimal and percentage value.
