Calculate the center of pressure on a rectangular plate submerged vertically in water.
The plate dimensions are 1 m wide (horizontal) by 2 m tall (vertical).
The top edge of the plate is at the water surface (depth = 0).
The fluid is water with density 1000 kg/m³.
Use standard gravitational acceleration of 9.81 m/s².
Find the vertical distance from the water surface to the center of pressure.
Apply the hydrostatic pressure distribution and moment equilibrium principles.
The center of pressure will be located below the centroid of the plate due to the non-uniform pressure distribution.
