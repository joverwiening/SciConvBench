Determine the viscosity of a fluid using a falling-ball viscometer with the following specifications.
Use a 3 mm diameter steel ball with density 7850 kg/m³.
The fluid has a density of 1000 kg/m³.
The ball falls through a distance of 100 mm, which is a standard height for small-scale falling-ball viscometers.
Measure the terminal velocity of the ball as it falls through the fluid.
Apply Stokes' law to calculate the dynamic viscosity using the formula: η = (2gr²(ρ_ball - ρ_fluid))/(9v), where g is gravitational acceleration (9.81 m/s²), r is the ball radius (0.0015 m), ρ_ball is ball density, ρ_fluid is fluid density, and v is terminal velocity.
Ensure the Reynolds number is less than 0.1 to validate that Stokes' law applies.
Record the temperature during measurement as viscosity is temperature-dependent.
Repeat the measurement at least three times and calculate the average viscosity value.
