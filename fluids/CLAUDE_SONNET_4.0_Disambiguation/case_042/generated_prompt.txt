Calculate the force produced by a water jet striking a flat plate perpendicularly.
The water jet has a diameter of 10 mm.
The water jet velocity is 15 m/s.
The water jet strikes the flat plate at a 90-degree angle (perpendicular impact).
Use the standard density of water at room temperature (1000 kg/m³).
Apply the momentum change principle where the water comes to a complete stop upon impact.
Calculate the mass flow rate using the jet cross-sectional area and velocity.
Determine the force using F = mass flow rate × velocity change.
The velocity change equals the initial jet velocity since the water stops completely.
Express the final result in Newtons.
