Calculate the stagnation pressure at the nose of a jet flying at Mach 1.5.
Assume a flight altitude of 35,000 feet (typical cruise altitude for commercial jets).
Use standard atmospheric conditions at 35,000 feet: ambient pressure of 498.4 psf and ambient temperature of 394.1°R.
Apply the isentropic stagnation pressure formula for compressible flow: P0 = P * (1 + ((γ-1)/2) * M²)^(γ/(γ-1)).
Use γ = 1.4 for air.
Calculate the stagnation pressure in both psf and psi units.
Present the result with appropriate significant figures.
