Calculate the Reynolds number for water flowing through a smooth pipe to determine if the flow is laminar or turbulent.
Use a pipe diameter of 4 cm (0.04 m).
Use a flow velocity of 1.5 m/s.
Assume water temperature of 20°C (room temperature) for determining fluid properties.
Use water density of 998 kg/m³ and dynamic viscosity of 1.002 × 10⁻³ Pa·s at 20°C.
Calculate Reynolds number using the formula Re = ρVD/μ where ρ is density, V is velocity, D is diameter, and μ is dynamic viscosity.
Compare the calculated Reynolds number to the critical value of 2300 for pipe flow.
If Re < 2300, classify the flow as laminar.
If Re > 2300, classify the flow as turbulent.
Report both the calculated Reynolds number value and the flow classification.
