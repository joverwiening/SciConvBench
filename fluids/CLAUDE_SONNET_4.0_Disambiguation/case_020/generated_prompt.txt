Calculate the cavitation number for water flowing at 20 m/s with local pressure of 50 kPa and vapor pressure of 2.3 kPa.
Use a water density of 1000 kg/m³ at standard conditions.
Apply the cavitation number formula: σ = (P - Pv) / (0.5 × ρ × V²).
Substitute the values: σ = (50,000 - 2,300) / (0.5 × 1000 × 20²).
Calculate the result: σ = 47,700 / 200,000 = 0.2385.
Report the cavitation number as 0.24 (rounded to two decimal places).
