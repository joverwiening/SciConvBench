Calculate the coefficient of performance (COP) for a vapor-compression refrigeration cycle.
The cycle removes 5 kW of heat from a cold space.
The compressor requires 1.5 kW of work input.
Assume the cold space temperature is 0°C (32°F), which is typical for commercial refrigeration applications.
Use the standard COP formula: COP = Cooling Effect / Work Input = 5 kW / 1.5 kW = 3.33.
The refrigeration cycle is a standard vapor-compression cycle, which is the most common type used in commercial and residential applications.
