Calculate the head loss across a pipe fitting using the minor loss equation.
Use a K factor of 0.9 for the pipe fitting.
Use a fluid velocity of 3 m/s.
Use standard gravitational acceleration of 9.81 m/s².
Apply the formula: h_L = K × (V²)/(2g) where h_L is head loss, K is the loss coefficient, V is velocity, and g is gravitational acceleration.
The calculated head loss is 0.413 meters.
