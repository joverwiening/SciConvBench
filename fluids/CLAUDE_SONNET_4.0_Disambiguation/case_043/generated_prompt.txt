Calculate the Taylor number for flow between two rotating cylinders.
The inner cylinder has a radius of 5 cm.
The outer cylinder has a radius of 5.2 cm.
The gap between cylinders is 2 mm.
The inner cylinder rotates at 100 rpm.
The outer cylinder is stationary (0 rpm angular velocity).
Use the standard Taylor number formula: Ta = (Ω₁ * R₁ * d^(3/2)) / (ν * √(R₂)).
Where Ω₁ is the inner cylinder angular velocity in rad/s, R₁ is inner radius, R₂ is outer radius, d is the gap width, and ν is the kinematic viscosity of the fluid.
Assume the working fluid is water at room temperature with kinematic viscosity of 1.0 × 10⁻⁶ m²/s.
Convert all units to SI base units before calculation.
Report the final Taylor number as a dimensionless value.
