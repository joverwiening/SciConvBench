Determine the drag coefficient of a sphere in creeping flow at Reynolds number 0.1.
Use a sphere diameter of 1 mm as a representative size for typical creeping flow applications.
Use water as the fluid with density 1000 kg/m³ and dynamic viscosity 0.001 Pa·s at room temperature.
Apply Stokes' law analytical solution for drag force calculation.
Calculate the drag coefficient using the formula Cd = Fd / (0.5 × ρ × V² × A) where Fd is drag force, ρ is fluid density, V is velocity, and A is sphere cross-sectional area.
Determine the required velocity from the Reynolds number definition Re = ρVD/μ where D is diameter and μ is dynamic viscosity.
Report the final drag coefficient value and compare it to the theoretical Stokes flow limit of Cd = 24/Re.
