Calculate the power output of a steam turbine using the following parameters.
The steam mass flow rate is 10 kg/s.
The steam inlet specific enthalpy is 3200 kJ/kg.
The steam outlet specific enthalpy is 2400 kJ/kg.
Use the formula: Power = mass flow rate × (inlet enthalpy - outlet enthalpy).
Express the final answer in kilowatts (kW).
