Calculate the buoyant lift force on a helium-filled balloon with a volume of 500 m³.
Use the density of air as 1.2 kg/m³.
Use the density of helium as 0.18 kg/m³.
Apply Archimedes' principle where buoyant force equals the weight of displaced air minus the weight of the helium.
The buoyant lift force equals (air density - helium density) × volume × gravitational acceleration.
Use standard gravitational acceleration of 9.81 m/s².
Express the final answer in Newtons.
