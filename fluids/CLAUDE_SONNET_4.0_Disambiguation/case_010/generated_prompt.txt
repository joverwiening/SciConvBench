Calculate the lift per unit span on a rotating cylinder using the Magnus effect.
The cylinder has a radius of 0.1 m.
The cylinder rotates at 200 rad/s counterclockwise when viewed from the positive z-direction.
The air flow velocity is 15 m/s in the positive x-direction.
Use standard air properties at sea level conditions (density = 1.225 kg/m³, viscosity = 1.81 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s).
Apply the Magnus effect formula or computational fluid dynamics to determine the lift force.
The result should be expressed as lift force per unit length in N/m.
Account for the circulation around the cylinder due to its rotation.
The Magnus force acts perpendicular to both the flow direction and the axis of rotation.
