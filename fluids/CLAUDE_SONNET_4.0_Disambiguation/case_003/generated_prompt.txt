Calculate the terminal velocity of a spherical particle falling through oil using the drag force balance equation.
The particle is a steel ball with a diameter of 2 mm (radius = 0.001 m).
The particle density is 7800 kg/m³.
The oil density is 890 kg/m³.
The oil dynamic viscosity is 0.29 Pa·s.
Use the force balance equation: gravitational force equals drag force at terminal velocity.
Apply Stokes' law for the drag force: F_drag = 6πμrv, where μ is dynamic viscosity, r is radius, and v is velocity.
The gravitational force is: F_gravity = (4/3)πr³(ρ_particle - ρ_oil)g, where g = 9.81 m/s².
Solve for terminal velocity: v_terminal = (2r²g(ρ_particle - ρ_oil))/(9μ).
Verify that the Reynolds number is less than 1 to confirm Stokes' law applies.
Report the final terminal velocity in m/s.
