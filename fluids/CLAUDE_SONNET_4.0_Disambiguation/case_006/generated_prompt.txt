Solve for the steady velocity profile in Couette flow between two parallel plates.
The plates are separated by a distance of 10 mm.
The top plate moves at a constant velocity of 5 m/s in the horizontal direction.
The bottom plate is stationary.
The fluid between the plates has a dynamic viscosity of 0.001 Pa·s (water at room temperature).
The flow is assumed to be laminar and fully developed.
The velocity profile is linear, varying from 0 m/s at the bottom plate to 5 m/s at the top plate.
The velocity at any height y from the bottom plate is given by u(y) = (5 m/s) × (y / 0.01 m).
The shear rate is constant throughout the fluid at 500 s⁻¹.
The shear stress is constant throughout the fluid at 0.5 Pa.
