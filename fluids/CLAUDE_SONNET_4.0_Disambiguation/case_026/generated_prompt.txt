Calculate the Knudsen number for gas flow in a 100 nm wide channel.
Use air at standard temperature and pressure (STP) conditions.
Use a mean free path of 68 nm for air at STP.
The characteristic length is the channel width of 100 nm.
Apply the formula Kn = λ/L where λ is the mean free path and L is the characteristic length.
The resulting Knudsen number is 0.68.
This indicates transitional flow regime since 0.1 < Kn < 10.
