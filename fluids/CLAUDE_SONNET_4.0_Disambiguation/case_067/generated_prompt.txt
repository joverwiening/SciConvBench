Calculate the terminal velocity of a spherical raindrop falling through air.
The raindrop has a diameter of 2 mm (radius of 1 mm).
The raindrop density is 1000 kg/m³.
The air density is 1.2 kg/m³.
Use a dynamic viscosity of air of 1.81 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s (standard value at 20°C and 1 atm).
Apply the force balance equation where gravitational force equals drag force at terminal velocity.
Use the appropriate drag coefficient for a sphere, accounting for the Reynolds number regime.
If the Reynolds number indicates turbulent flow, use CD = 0.47 for a sphere.
If the Reynolds number indicates laminar flow, use Stokes' law with CD = 24/Re.
Solve iteratively if necessary since the drag coefficient depends on the Reynolds number which depends on the terminal velocity.
Report the final terminal velocity in m/s.
