Calculate the pressure drop per unit length in a packed bed using the Ergun equation.
The packed bed contains spherical particles with diameter 5 mm.
The fluid is air at standard atmospheric conditions with density 1.225 kg/m³ and viscosity 1.81 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s.
The superficial velocity of air through the bed is 0.5 m/s.
The bed porosity is 0.4.
Apply the Ergun equation: ΔP/L = 150 × (μ × v × (1-ε)²)/(dp² × ε³) + 1.75 × (ρ × v² × (1-ε))/(dp × ε³).
Use μ = 1.81 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s, ρ = 1.225 kg/m³, v = 0.5 m/s, ε = 0.4, dp = 0.005 m.
Calculate both the viscous term and inertial term separately.
Report the total pressure drop per unit length in Pa/m.
