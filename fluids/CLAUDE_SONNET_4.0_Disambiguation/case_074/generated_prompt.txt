Calculate the degree of reaction for an axial flow compressor stage.
Air enters the rotor with a relative velocity of 150 m/s at 45 degrees.
The blade speed is 200 m/s.
The axial velocity component is 106.07 m/s and remains constant throughout the stage.
Air exits the rotor with a relative velocity of 100 m/s at 30 degrees.
Use the velocity triangles to determine the absolute velocities at rotor inlet and outlet.
Apply the degree of reaction formula using the enthalpy changes across the rotor and stator.
The degree of reaction equals the ratio of enthalpy rise in the rotor to the total enthalpy rise across the stage.
