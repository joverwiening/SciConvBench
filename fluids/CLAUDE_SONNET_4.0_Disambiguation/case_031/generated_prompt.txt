Calculate the pressure at a depth of 2000 m in the ocean.
Use a seawater density of 1025 kg/m³.
Use standard gravity of 9.81 m/s².
Apply the hydrostatic pressure formula: P = ρgh + P₀.
Add atmospheric pressure at sea level of 101,325 Pa to the hydrostatic pressure.
Express the final result in Pascals and convert to atmospheres for reference.
The calculation should yield approximately 2.02 × 10⁷ Pa or about 200 atmospheres total pressure.
