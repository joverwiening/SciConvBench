Calculate the Stefan number using the formula: Stefan number = (specific heat capacity × temperature difference) / latent heat of fusion.
Use ice as the material with a specific heat capacity of 2.1 kJ/kg·K.
Use a temperature difference of 10°C.
Use the latent heat of fusion for ice of 334 kJ/kg.
The Stefan number equals (2.1 × 10) / 334 = 0.0629.
