Calculate the rate of heat transfer through a concrete wall using Fourier's law of heat conduction.
The concrete wall has a thickness of 0.2 meters.
The total wall area is 15 square meters.
The temperature difference across the wall is 20°C.
The thermal conductivity of the concrete is 1.4 W/(m·K).
Use the formula Q = k × A × ΔT / t, where Q is heat transfer rate, k is thermal conductivity, A is area, ΔT is temperature difference, and t is thickness.
The calculation yields Q = 1.4 × 15 × 20 / 0.2 = 2100 watts.
Report the final heat transfer rate as 2100 W or 2.1 kW.
