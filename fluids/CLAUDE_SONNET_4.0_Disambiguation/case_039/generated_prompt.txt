Calculate the torque required to rotate a disk in a viscous fluid near a wall.
The disk has a radius of 0.2 m.
The fluid is oil with a viscosity of 0.1 Pa·s.
The disk rotates at 60 rpm (6.283 rad/s).
The gap distance between the disk and the wall is 1 mm (0.001 m).
Assume the disk thickness is negligible compared to its radius for torque calculations.
Use the viscous torque formula for a disk rotating near a wall: T = (μ × ω × π × R⁴) / (2 × h).
Where μ is viscosity, ω is angular velocity, R is disk radius, and h is gap distance.
The calculated torque accounts for viscous shear stress in the thin gap between the rotating disk and the stationary wall.
