Calculate the Mach number for a 500 m/s flow in air at -50°C temperature.
Assume standard atmospheric pressure at sea level (101,325 Pa) since no pressure was specified.
Use the ideal gas law and properties of air to determine the speed of sound.
The speed of sound in air is calculated using the formula: a = √(γRT) where γ = 1.4 for air, R = 287 J/(kg·K) for air, and T is absolute temperature in Kelvin.
Convert -50°C to Kelvin: T = -50 + 273.15 = 223.15 K.
Calculate the speed of sound: a = √(1.4 × 287 × 223.15) = 299.5 m/s.
Calculate the Mach number: M = V/a = 500/299.5 = 1.67.
Report the final Mach number as approximately 1.67.
