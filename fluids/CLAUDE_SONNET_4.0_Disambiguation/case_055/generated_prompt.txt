Calculate the work done during polytropic compression of nitrogen gas.
Initial pressure P1 is 100 kPa.
Final pressure P2 is 600 kPa.
Initial volume V1 is 0.2 m³.
Polytropic index n is 1.25.
Assume standard temperature of 298 K (25°C) for the process.
Use the polytropic work formula: W = (P2V2 - P1V1)/(1-n).
First calculate final volume V2 using P1V1^n = P2V2^n.
Treat nitrogen as an ideal gas with R = 296.8 J/(kg·K).
Express the final answer in kilojoules.
