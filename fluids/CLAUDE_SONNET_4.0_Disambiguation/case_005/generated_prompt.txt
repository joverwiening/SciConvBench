Calculate the Reynolds number at 0.5 m from the leading edge for flow over a flat plate in air.
Use a free stream velocity of 10 m/s.
Assume standard atmospheric conditions with air temperature of 15°C (288.15 K).
Use the kinematic viscosity of air at 15°C, which is approximately 1.46 × 10^-5 m²/s.
Apply the Reynolds number formula: Re = (velocity × distance) / kinematic viscosity.
The distance from the leading edge is 0.5 m.
Report the final Reynolds number value.
