Calculate the force exerted by a water jet on a stationary curved vane.
The water jet has a diameter of 50 mm.
The water jet has a velocity of 20 m/s.
The curved vane deflects the water jet by 120 degrees.
Assume the deflection is counterclockwise from the initial jet direction as this is the standard convention in fluid mechanics problems.
Use water density of 1000 kg/m³.
Apply the momentum equation for jet impingement on curved vanes.
Calculate both the magnitude and direction of the resultant force on the vane.
Present the force components in x and y directions if applicable.
