Calculate the hydraulic diameter of a rectangular duct with dimensions 30 cm x 10 cm.
Use the standard hydraulic diameter formula: Dh = 4A/P where A is cross-sectional area and P is wetted perimeter.
The cross-sectional area is 30 cm × 10 cm = 300 cm².
The wetted perimeter is 2 × (30 cm + 10 cm) = 80 cm.
The hydraulic diameter is 4 × 300 cm² ÷ 80 cm = 15 cm.
Express the final answer as 15 cm or 0.15 m.
