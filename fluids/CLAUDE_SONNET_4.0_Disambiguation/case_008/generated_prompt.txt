Calculate the thermal efficiency of an Otto cycle using air as the working fluid.
Use a compression ratio of 8.5.
Use a specific heat ratio (γ) of 1.4 for air.
Apply the Otto cycle thermal efficiency formula: η = 1 - (1/r)^(γ-1) where r is the compression ratio.
Substitute the values: η = 1 - (1/8.5)^(1.4-1) = 1 - (1/8.5)^0.4.
Calculate the numerical result and express as both a decimal and percentage.
