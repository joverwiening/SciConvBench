Calculate the drag force on a billboard during a wind storm.
The billboard dimensions are 4 meters high by 10 meters wide.
The wind speed is 30 m/s.
The drag coefficient is 1.2.
The air density is 1.225 kg/m³.
Use the drag force equation: F = 0.5 × ρ × v² × Cd × A.
The frontal area A is the width multiplied by the height.
Report the final drag force in Newtons.
