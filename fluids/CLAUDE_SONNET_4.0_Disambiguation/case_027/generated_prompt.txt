Calculate the lift force on a wing using the lift equation: L = 0.5 × ρ × V² × S × CL.
Use an air density (ρ) of 1.1 kg/m³.
Use an airspeed (V) of 80 m/s.
Use a wing area (S) of 20 m².
Use a coefficient of lift (CL) of 0.45.
Apply the formula to determine the total lift force in Newtons.
The calculation should yield: L = 0.5 × 1.1 × 80² × 20 × 0.45 = 158,400 N.
