Calculate the heat transfer coefficient using the formula h = Nu × k / L.
Use Nu = 120 as the Nusselt number.
Use k = 0.026 W/m·K as the thermal conductivity.
Use L = 0.1 m as the characteristic length scale (diameter of the sphere).
The resulting heat transfer coefficient will be h = 120 × 0.026 / 0.1 = 31.2 W/m²·K.
