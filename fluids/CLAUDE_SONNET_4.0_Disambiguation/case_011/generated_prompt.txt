Calculate the Biot number for a steel sphere with radius 5 mm and thermal conductivity 45 W/m·K.
The sphere is being cooled by convection in an oil bath with convective heat transfer coefficient 150 W/m²·K.
Use the formula Bi = hL_c/k where h is the convective heat transfer coefficient, L_c is the characteristic length, and k is the thermal conductivity.
For a sphere, the characteristic length L_c is radius/3, so L_c = 0.005 m / 3 = 0.00167 m.
Calculate Bi = (150 W/m²·K × 0.00167 m) / (45 W/m·K).
Report the final Biot number value.
