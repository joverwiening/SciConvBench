Calculate the efficiency of a straight rectangular aluminum fin with the following specifications.
The fin is made of aluminum with thermal conductivity k = 200 W/m·K.
The fin has a length of 3 cm (0.03 m).
The fin has a thickness of 2 mm (0.002 m).
Assume a reasonable fin width of 10 mm (0.01 m) based on typical engineering applications.
The convective heat transfer coefficient is 20 W/m²·K.
Assume a temperature difference of 50°C between the fin base and surrounding fluid.
The fin has a rectangular cross-sectional shape.
Use the standard fin efficiency formula for a straight fin with rectangular cross-section.
Calculate the fin parameter m = sqrt(hP/kA) where h is convective coefficient, P is perimeter, k is thermal conductivity, and A is cross-sectional area.
Calculate fin efficiency using η = tanh(mL)/(mL) where L is fin length.
