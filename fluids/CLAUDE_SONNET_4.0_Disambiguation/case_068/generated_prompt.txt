Calculate the boundary layer work done by an ideal gas expanding in a piston-cylinder device.
The gas is nitrogen with a mass of 2 kg and gas constant R = 0.2968 kJ/kg·K.
The initial pressure is 500 kPa.
The final pressure is 100 kPa.
The expansion process is isothermal at a constant temperature of 300 K.
Use the ideal gas law to determine the initial and final volumes from the given pressures and temperature.
Calculate the boundary work using the isothermal work formula: W = mRT ln(P1/P2) where m is mass, R is gas constant, T is temperature, P1 is initial pressure, and P2 is final pressure.
Express the final answer in kJ.
