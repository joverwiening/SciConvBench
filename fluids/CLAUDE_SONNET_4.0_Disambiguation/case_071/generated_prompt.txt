Calculate the shaft power required for a centrifugal pump delivering water at 0.05 m³/s flow rate.
The pump delivers a total head of 25 meters.
The pump efficiency is 75%.
Use water density of 1000 kg/m³ and gravitational acceleration of 9.81 m/s².
Calculate hydraulic power using the formula: P_hydraulic = ρ × g × Q × H.
Calculate shaft power using the formula: P_shaft = P_hydraulic / η.
Express the final answer in kilowatts.
