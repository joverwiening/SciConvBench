Calculate the excess pressure inside a soap bubble with a diameter of 10 mm.
Use a surface tension value of 0.035 N/m for the soap solution.
Apply the Young-Laplace equation for a soap bubble, which accounts for two surfaces (inner and outer).
The excess pressure formula is ΔP = 4γ/r, where γ is surface tension and r is the radius.
Convert the diameter to radius: r = 5 mm = 0.005 m.
Substitute the values: ΔP = 4 × 0.035 N/m ÷ 0.005 m.
