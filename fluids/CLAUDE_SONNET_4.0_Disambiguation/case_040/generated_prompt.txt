Calculate the stagnation temperature of a compressible flow with the following parameters.
The flow has a Mach number of 2.0.
The static temperature is 250 K.
Use a specific heat ratio (gamma) of 1.4 for air at standard conditions.
Apply the stagnation temperature formula: T0 = T * (1 + ((gamma-1)/2) * M^2).
The expected result should be 450 K.
