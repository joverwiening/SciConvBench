Calculate the heat loss per meter of pipe length from a horizontal 2-inch pipe carrying steam at 150°C.
The ambient air temperature is 20°C.
The pipe is insulated with 25mm thick glass wool insulation.
The thermal conductivity of the glass wool insulation is 0.04 W/m·K.
The outer surface heat transfer coefficient between the insulation surface and ambient air is 10 W/m²·K.
The outer diameter of the insulated pipe is the 2-inch pipe diameter plus 50mm total insulation thickness (25mm on each side).
Use standard thermal resistance calculations for cylindrical geometry including conduction through insulation and convection to ambient air.
Express the final result as heat loss in watts per meter of pipe length.
