Calculate the outlet temperature of a compressor using isentropic compression with 85% efficiency.
The inlet temperature is 300 K.
The pressure ratio is 8.
Assume the working fluid is air with a specific heat ratio of 1.4.
Use the isentropic relation T2_ideal = T1 × (P2/P1)^((γ-1)/γ) to find the ideal outlet temperature.
Calculate the actual outlet temperature using T2_actual = T1 + (T2_ideal - T1) / efficiency.
The efficiency is 0.85 (85%).
Report the final outlet temperature in Kelvin.
