Calculate the hydraulic efficiency of a Pelton wheel turbine with the following specifications.
The turbine operates under a net head of 300 meters.
The water discharge through the turbine is 0.5 cubic meters per second.
The runner develops 1300 kW of power output.
Use the standard formula for hydraulic efficiency which is the ratio of actual power output to theoretical power available from the water flow.
The theoretical power is calculated as rho times g times Q times H, where rho is water density (1000 kg/m³), g is gravitational acceleration (9.81 m/s²), Q is flow rate, and H is head.
Express the final hydraulic efficiency as a percentage.
