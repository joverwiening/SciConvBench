Solve the one-dimensional steady-state heat conduction equation with uniform internal heat generation in a plane wall.
The wall thickness is 0.20 m.
The thermal conductivity is 15 W/m·K.
The internal heat generation rate is 2×10⁵ W/m³.
Both surfaces of the wall are maintained at a constant temperature of 50°C.
The governing equation is d²T/dx² + q̇/k = 0 where T is temperature, x is position, q̇ is heat generation rate, and k is thermal conductivity.
Apply boundary conditions T(0) = 50°C and T(L) = 50°C where L = 0.20 m.
Determine the temperature distribution T(x) throughout the wall.
Find the maximum temperature and its location within the wall.
