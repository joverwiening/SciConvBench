Calculate the thermal efficiency of an ideal gas turbine operating on a Brayton cycle.
Use air as the working fluid with a specific heat ratio (γ) of 1.4.
Apply a pressure ratio of 12 across the compressor.
Use the ideal Brayton cycle thermal efficiency formula: η = 1 - (1/r_p)^((γ-1)/γ).
Substitute the given values: η = 1 - (1/12)^((1.4-1)/1.4) = 1 - (1/12)^(0.4/1.4) = 1 - (1/12)^(2/7).
Calculate the numerical result and express as a percentage.
