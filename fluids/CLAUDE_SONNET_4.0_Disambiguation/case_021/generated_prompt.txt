Calculate the thermal stress in a steel rod that is fully constrained against thermal contraction.
The rod is made of steel with Young's modulus E = 200 GPa.
The coefficient of thermal expansion is α = 12×10⁻⁶ /°C.
The temperature drops by 40°C.
Use the thermal stress formula: σ = α × E × ΔT.
The thermal stress will be compressive since the rod wants to contract but is prevented from doing so.
Report the final stress value in MPa.
