Design a counterflow heat exchanger simulation using the following specifications:
Assume water-to-water heat exchange as the most common industrial application.
Set hot fluid inlet temperature to 80°C and outlet temperature to 60°C.
Set cold fluid inlet temperature to 20°C and outlet temperature to 40°C.
Use hot fluid mass flow rate of 2 kg/s.
Use cold fluid mass flow rate of 2 kg/s.
Set overall heat transfer coefficient to 1000 W/m²K for water-to-water systems.
Calculate ΔT1 as Th,in - Tc,out = 80°C - 40°C = 40°C.
Calculate ΔT2 as Th,out - Tc,in = 60°C - 20°C = 40°C.
Calculate logarithmic mean temperature difference as LMTD = (ΔT1 - ΔT2) / ln(ΔT1/ΔT2).
Since ΔT1 equals ΔT2, use arithmetic mean: LMTD = 40°C.
Calculate heat duty as Q = mh × cp × (Th,in - Th,out) = 2 kg/s × 4180 J/kg·K × 20K = 167,200 W.
Calculate required heat transfer area using A = Q / (U × LMTD) = 167,200 W / (1000 W/m²K × 40K) = 4.18 m².
Verify energy balance by checking that hot fluid heat loss equals cold fluid heat gain.
Output the calculated area, LMTD, and heat duty values.
