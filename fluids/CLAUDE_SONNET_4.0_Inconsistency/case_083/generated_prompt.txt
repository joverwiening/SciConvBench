Calculate the shock angle beta for an oblique shock using the theta-beta-Mach relation.
Given conditions are wedge angle theta = 25 degrees, upstream Mach number M1 = 3, and specific heat ratio gamma = 1.4.
Use the exact theta-beta-Mach relation: tan(theta) = 2*cot(beta) * [(M1^2*sin^2(beta) - 1) / (M1^2*(gamma + cos(2*beta)) + 2)].
Solve this equation numerically to find the shock angle beta.
Convert the wedge angle from degrees to radians for the calculation.
Report the final shock angle beta in degrees.
