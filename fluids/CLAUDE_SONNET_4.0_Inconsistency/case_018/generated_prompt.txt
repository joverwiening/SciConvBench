The simulation models fully developed laminar flow of water in a circular pipe.
The pipe has a diameter D of 20 mm.
The mean velocity V is 1.0 m/s.
The velocity profile is assumed to be uniform (plug flow) across the pipe cross-section.
The water temperature is assumed to be 20°C (room temperature, standard for engineering calculations).
The water density is 998 kg/m³ (at 20°C).
The kinetic-energy correction factor alpha is calculated using the formula alpha = (1/A) * ∫(u³/V³)dA where u is the local velocity and V is the mean velocity.
For uniform (plug flow) velocity profile, u = V everywhere across the cross-section.
The kinetic-energy correction factor alpha equals 1.0 for plug flow since u/V = 1 at all points.
The calculation confirms that alpha = 1.0 when the velocity profile is perfectly uniform.
