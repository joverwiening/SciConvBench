The simulation models a thin airfoil using thin-airfoil theory.
The airfoil has a chord length c = 1.0 m.
The freestream velocity is U∞ = 50 m/s.
The angle of attack is α = 5°.
The flow is assumed to be completely irrotational everywhere.
The Kutta condition is applied at the trailing edge.
The circulation Γ around the airfoil is computed using thin-airfoil theory.
The fluid is air with standard properties.
