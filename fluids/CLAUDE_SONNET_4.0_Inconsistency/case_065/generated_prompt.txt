Calculate the cavitation factor sigma for a Francis turbine using Thoma's formula sigma = (Ha - Hv - hs - hloss)/H.
The Francis turbine runner is installed 20 meters above the tailrace.
Use Ha = 10.3 m (atmospheric pressure head).
Use Hv = 0.3 m (vapor pressure head).
Set hs = 20 m (suction head, equal to the height above tailrace).
Assume hloss = 1 m (typical head losses for Francis turbines).
Assume H = 100 m (total head, representing typical medium-head Francis turbine operation).
Substitute all values into the formula to calculate sigma.
Demonstrate that sigma is positive under these conditions.
Explain that a positive sigma value indicates cavitation is impossible.
Show that for any reasonable operating head H, the cavitation factor remains positive, proving cavitation cannot occur.
