Solve the steady-state 1D heat conduction equation d2T/dx2 = 0 in a slab.
Use a slab thickness of 1 meter in the x-direction.
Apply Dirichlet boundary conditions with T = 100°C at x = 0 and T = 0°C at x = 1.
Use thermal diffusivity alpha = 1e-6 m²/s for steel.
Solve using the analytical method since the steady-state equation is simple.
The analytical solution is T(x) = T0 - (T0 - T1) * x / L where T0 is temperature at x=0, T1 is temperature at x=L, and L is slab thickness.
Plot the temperature distribution across the slab.
Verify that the solution satisfies the boundary conditions.
