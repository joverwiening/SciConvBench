Calculate the mass flow rate through a converging nozzle with the following conditions.
Air discharges from a tank at 500 kPa absolute pressure to ambient conditions at 100 kPa absolute pressure.
Assume the tank air temperature is 300 K (approximately room temperature).
The nozzle exit cross-sectional area is 0.001 m² (10 cm²).
For a converging nozzle, the throat area equals the exit area, so the throat cross-sectional area is 0.001 m².
The exit pressure equals the ambient pressure of 100 kPa.
Use the isentropic flow equations for compressible flow through nozzles.
Assume air behaves as an ideal gas with specific heat ratio γ = 1.4.
Use the gas constant for air R = 287 J/(kg·K).
Check if the flow is choked by comparing the pressure ratio to the critical pressure ratio.
If choked, use the critical conditions at the throat for mass flow calculation.
If not choked, use the actual exit conditions for mass flow calculation.
