A cylindrical tank of water rotates as a forced vortex with tangential velocity v_theta = omega * r.
The angular velocity omega is assumed to be 2 rad/s, which is a typical moderate rotation rate for fluid mechanics problems.
Apply Bernoulli's equation along a radial line from r = 0.1 m to r = 0.3 m.
Assume inviscid flow conditions throughout the analysis.
The fluid is water with standard density properties.
Calculate the pressure difference between the two radial positions using the centripetal acceleration effects in the rotating reference frame.
Account for the hydrostatic pressure variation due to the curved free surface created by the rotation.
Present the final pressure difference in Pascals.
