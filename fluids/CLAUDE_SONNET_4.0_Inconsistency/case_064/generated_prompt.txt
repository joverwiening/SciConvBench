Calculate the pressure difference between inlet and outlet of a pipe using Bernoulli's equation.
The pipe outlet is 15 m higher than the inlet.
The fluid is water with density 1000 kg/m³.
Assume the pipe has constant diameter so inlet and outlet velocities are equal.
Apply Bernoulli's equation: P₁/ρ + v₁²/2 + gz₁ = P₂/ρ + v₂²/2 + gz₂.
Since v₁ = v₂, the velocity terms cancel out.
Rearrange to find pressure difference: P₁ - P₂ = ρg(z₂ - z₁).
Substitute values: P₁ - P₂ = 1000 kg/m³ × 9.81 m/s² × 15 m.
Calculate the result: P₁ - P₂ = 147,150 Pa or 147.15 kPa.
The pressure at the inlet is 147.15 kPa higher than at the outlet.
