Calculate the stagnation enthalpy of liquid water using h0 = h + v^2/2 where h0 is stagnation enthalpy and h is static enthalpy.
Use a jet velocity of 20 m/s.
Assume an initial water temperature of 20°C (293.15 K) as a standard reference condition.
Use the constant pressure specific heat capacity for liquid water, Cp = 4186 J/(kg·K).
Calculate the kinetic energy term as v^2/2 = (20 m/s)^2/2 = 200 J/kg.
Convert the kinetic energy to temperature rise using ΔT = (v^2/2)/Cp = 200/4186 = 0.048 K.
The stagnation temperature is T0 = T + ΔT = 20°C + 0.048°C = 20.048°C.
Report both the stagnation enthalpy increase and the corresponding temperature rise.
