Calculate the Rayleigh number for natural convection from a vertical plate.
Use air as the working fluid at atmospheric pressure.

Calculate the film temperature as the average of surface and ambient temperatures.
Evaluate the thermal expansion coefficient beta as 1/(film temperature in Kelvin).
Obtain the kinematic viscosity and thermal diffusivity of air at the film temperature from standard property tables.
Apply the Rayleigh number formula: Ra = g × beta × ΔT × L³ / (ν × α).
