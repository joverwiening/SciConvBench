The simulation models Couette flow of oil between two infinite parallel plates.
The oil has a dynamic viscosity of 0.10 Pa·s and a density of 900 kg/m³ (typical for motor oil).
The plates are separated by a gap height of 5 mm.
The bottom plate is stationary.
The top plate moves at a constant velocity of 2.0 m/s in the horizontal direction.
Apply Bernoulli's equation between points at the stationary bottom plate and the moving top plate.
Calculate and determine the pressure variation across the 5 mm gap.
The flow is assumed to be steady, incompressible, and fully developed.
The velocity profile is linear across the gap as characteristic of Couette flow.
Output the pressure difference between the top and bottom plates.
