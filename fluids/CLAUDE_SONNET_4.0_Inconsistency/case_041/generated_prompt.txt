Calculate the pressure drop in a pipe using the Darcy-Weisbach equation with the following parameters.
Use a pipe length of 30 meters and diameter of 0.1 meters.
Use a Fanning friction factor of 0.005.
Assume water as the fluid with density of 1000 kg/m³.
Assume a typical water velocity of 2 m/s for pipe flow applications.
Apply the Darcy-Weisbach equation: Δp = f (L/D) (ρ v²/2).
Calculate and report the pressure drop in Pascals.
