Use the Moody chart to find the friction factor for a pipe flow analysis.
The relative roughness is 0.05.
The Reynolds number is 1000.
Since the Reynolds number is 1000, the flow is in the laminar regime.
For laminar flow, the friction factor is calculated using the formula f = 64/Re, regardless of pipe roughness.
Calculate the friction factor as f = 64/1000 = 0.064.
Note that the Moody chart and relative roughness value are not needed for this calculation since laminar flow friction factor is independent of surface roughness.
The final answer is f = 0.064.
