For fully developed laminar flow in a circular pipe, assume a pipe diameter of 0.1 meters and an average fluid velocity of 1.0 m/s.
Use water as the working fluid with density 1000 kg/m³.
Set the momentum correction factor beta equal to 1.
Calculate the momentum flux using the formula: momentum flux = beta × density × velocity².
The momentum flux equals 1 × 1000 kg/m³ × (1.0 m/s)² = 1000 kg/(m·s²).
Express the final result in units of kg/(m·s²) or Pa.
