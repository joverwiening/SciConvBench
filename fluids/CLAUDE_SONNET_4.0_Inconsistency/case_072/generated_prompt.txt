Calculate the pressure drop per unit length through a packed bed using the Ergun equation.
The packed bed has porosity epsilon = 0.4 and particle diameter 5 mm.
Use air as the fluid at standard conditions (20°C, 1 atm).
Use a superficial velocity of 1 m/s.
Apply the Ergun equation: ΔP/L = (150 * μ * u * (1-ε)²)/(dp² * ε³) + (1.75 * ρ * u² * (1-ε))/(dp * ε³).
Use air properties: density = 1.2 kg/m³, dynamic viscosity = 1.8 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s.
Express the final result in Pa/m.
