Calculate the Reynolds number for water flow in a circular pipe.
Use pipe diameter D = 0.10 m.
Use mean velocity V = 2.0 m/s.
Use hydraulic diameter Dh = D = 0.10 m for a circular pipe.
Use kinematic viscosity ν = μ/ρ = (1.0e-3 Pa·s)/(998 kg/m³) = 1.002e-6 m²/s.
Apply the Reynolds number formula Re = V × Dh / ν.
Substitute the values: Re = (2.0 m/s) × (0.10 m) / (1.002e-6 m²/s).
