Calculate the velocity at the inlet and outlet sections of a steady incompressible flow in a constant-area pipe.
Use a volumetric flow rate of 0.01 m³/s at both the inlet and outlet sections.
Assume a pipe cross-sectional area of 0.01 m² (corresponding to a circular pipe with diameter of approximately 0.113 m).
Apply the continuity equation V = Q/A where V is velocity, Q is volumetric flow rate, and A is cross-sectional area.
Calculate the velocity at the inlet section using V_inlet = 0.01 m³/s ÷ 0.01 m² = 1.0 m/s.
Calculate the velocity at the outlet section using V_outlet = 0.01 m³/s ÷ 0.01 m² = 1.0 m/s.
Verify that both velocities are equal, confirming conservation of mass for steady incompressible flow in a constant-area pipe.
