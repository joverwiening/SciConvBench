Q: What is the camber line equation or maximum camber percentage and its chordwise location for the NACA 4412 airfoil that you want to use in the thin airfoil theory calculation?
A: For the NACA 4412 airfoil, the maximum camber is 4% of chord located at 40% chord from the leading edge. The camber line equation follows the standard NACA 4-digit series formulation with these parameters.
