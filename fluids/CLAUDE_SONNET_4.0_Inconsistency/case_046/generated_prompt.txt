The simulation models a normal shock wave with the following specifications:
Assume an upstream Mach number of 2.0 as a representative supersonic condition.
The flow upstream of the shock is isentropic.
The total pressure remains constant across the shock wave.
Use the constant total pressure assumption to calculate all downstream flow conditions.
Calculate downstream static pressure, temperature, density, and Mach number using normal shock relations with constant total pressure.
The working fluid is air with standard properties (gamma = 1.4, R = 287 J/kg-K).
Use standard atmospheric conditions upstream (p1 = 101325 Pa, T1 = 288.15 K).
