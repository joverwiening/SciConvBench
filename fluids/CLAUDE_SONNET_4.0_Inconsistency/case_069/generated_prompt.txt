Calculate the pressure difference between inlet and outlet of a pipe using Bernoulli's equation.
The pipe has an elevation difference of 15 meters with the outlet 15 meters higher than the inlet.
Assume the fluid is water with density 1000 kg/m³.
Assume the pipe has constant diameter so inlet and outlet velocities are equal.
Apply Bernoulli's equation: P₁/ρ + v₁²/2 + gz₁ = P₂/ρ + v₂²/2 + gz₂.
Since velocities are equal, the velocity terms cancel out.
The pressure difference is P₁ - P₂ = ρg(z₂ - z₁) = ρgh where h = 15 m.
Use standard gravity g = 9.81 m/s².
Calculate the final pressure difference in Pascals.
