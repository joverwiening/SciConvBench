Calculate the pressure difference across a curved streamline using the centripetal acceleration principle.

The water density is ρ = 1000 kg/m³.

The outer measurement point is at radius R + Δn/2 = 5.05 m from the center of curvature.
The inner measurement point is at radius R - Δn/2 = 4.95 m from the center of curvature.
Apply the radial momentum equation: dp/dr = ρV²/r where r is the local radius of curvature.
Integrate the pressure gradient from the inner radius to the outer radius to find the total pressure difference.
The pressure increases toward the center of curvature, so the inner side has higher pressure than the outer side.
