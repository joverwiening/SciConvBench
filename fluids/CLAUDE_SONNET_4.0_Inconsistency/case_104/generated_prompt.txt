Calculate the heat removed during steam condensation at 100°C using the correct thermodynamic approach.
Use the latent heat of vaporization for water at 100°C, which is approximately 2260 kJ/kg.
Assume a mass of 1 kg of steam for the calculation.
Apply the formula q = m × hfg where hfg is the latent heat of vaporization.
The calculation becomes q = 1 kg × 2260 kJ/kg = 2260 kJ.
Note that the given formula q = m cp ΔT is inappropriate for phase change processes where ΔT = 0.
The sensible heat formula only applies to temperature changes within the same phase.
For condensation at constant temperature, use the latent heat approach instead.
