Simulate 2D flow over a circular cylinder using Euler's equations.
The fluid is air with density 1.225 kg/m³ and dynamic viscosity 1.81e-5 Pa·s.
The cylinder has a diameter of 1 meter.
The freestream velocity is 1.81e-3 m/s to achieve Reynolds number of 0.1.
Apply far-field boundary conditions with uniform inlet velocity and zero gradient outlet.
Use no-slip boundary condition on the cylinder surface.
The computational domain extends 10 cylinder diameters upstream, downstream, and in the cross-flow directions.
Solve the steady-state Euler equations neglecting viscous terms.
Use second-order spatial discretization and appropriate numerical schemes for inviscid flow.
Monitor convergence of lift and drag coefficients.
