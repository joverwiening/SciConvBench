Calculate the Thoma sigma for a Francis turbine operating with water at 100°C.
Use vapor pressure head Hv = 0.3 m.
Use atmospheric head Ha = 10.3 m.
Assume a net head H = 50 m based on typical Francis turbine applications.
Assume elevation difference between turbine and tailwater level Hs = 2 m based on standard installation practices.
Apply the Thoma sigma formula: σ = (Ha - Hv - Hs) / H.
Substitute the values: σ = (10.3 - 0.3 - 2) / 50.
Calculate the final result: σ = 8.0 / 50 = 0.16.
Report the Thoma sigma value as 0.16.
