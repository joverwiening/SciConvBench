Calculate the capillary rise in a glass tube for mercury.
Use a contact angle of 0 degrees.
Use a surface tension of 0.48 N/m.
Use an inner tube diameter of 1 mm (0.001 m radius) as a typical laboratory capillary tube size.
Use a mercury density of 13,534 kg/m³ at room temperature.
Apply the Young-Laplace equation for capillary rise: h = (2σcosθ)/(ρgr).
Use standard gravitational acceleration of 9.81 m/s².
Report the result as the height change in the mercury column.
Note that mercury exhibits capillary depression rather than rise due to its non-wetting behavior with glass.
