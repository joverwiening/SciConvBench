Calculate the pressure rise in a water pipeline due to valve closure using steady Bernoulli equation.
The water velocity decreases from 2 m/s to 0 m/s when the valve closes in 0.05 s.
Use standard water density of 1000 kg/m³.
Assume initial gauge pressure of 0 Pa (atmospheric reference).
Assume pipeline length of 10 m from calculation point to valve.
Apply Bernoulli equation between the upstream point and valve location.
Account for the kinetic energy change as the primary source of pressure rise.
Neglect elevation changes and friction losses for this steady-state analysis.
Report the pressure rise in Pascals.
