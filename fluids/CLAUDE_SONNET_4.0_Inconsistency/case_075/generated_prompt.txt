Calculate the pressure difference in a horizontal pipe flow system using Bernoulli's equation.
Use liquid water with density 998 kg/m³ at 20°C.
Treat the water as incompressible.
Model a pipe with two sections: initial diameter 0.1 m, constricted diameter 0.05 m.
Set the initial flow velocity to 2 m/s.
Calculate the velocity in the constricted section using continuity equation.
Apply Bernoulli's equation between the two sections to find the pressure difference.
Assume the pipe is horizontal so gravitational potential energy terms cancel.
Use atmospheric pressure (101,325 Pa) as the reference pressure in the initial section.
