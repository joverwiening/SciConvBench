Calculate the hydraulic power of a pump using the formula P = rho * g * Q * H.
The pump delivers a head of H = 30 meters at a flow rate of Q = 50 liters per second.
Use water as the fluid with density rho = 1000 kg/m³.
Use standard gravity g = 9.81 m/s².
Convert the flow rate from liters per second to cubic meters per second (Q = 0.05 m³/s).
Apply the formula P = 1000 * 9.81 * 0.05 * 30 to calculate the hydraulic power.
Express the final result in watts.
