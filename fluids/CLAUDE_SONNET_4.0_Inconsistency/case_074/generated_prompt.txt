Air flows through a duct where pressure increases from 1 bar to 10 bar.
The density of air remains constant throughout the process.
Apply the incompressible continuity equation to relate velocities at different locations.
Assume an initial air velocity of 10 m/s at the 1 bar location as a typical moderate flow condition.
Assume the duct has a constant circular cross-sectional area of 0.01 m² throughout its length.
Use the continuity equation A₁V₁ = A₂V₂ where A is cross-sectional area and V is velocity.
Calculate the velocity at the 10 bar location using the given assumptions.
Present the solution showing the application of continuity principles despite the pressure change.
