Calculate the downstream depth y2 for a hydraulic jump in a horizontal rectangular open channel.
The channel width is 3 m.
The upstream depth y1 is 0.4 m.
The upstream velocity V1 is 6.0 m/s.
Use the momentum equation (specific force function) instead of Bernoulli's equation since energy is not conserved across the hydraulic jump.
Apply the momentum equation: y1 + V1²/(2g) = y2 + V2²/(2g) where V2 is determined from continuity equation V1*y1 = V2*y2.
Assume the channel is horizontal since no bed slope is specified.
Solve the momentum equation simultaneously with the continuity equation to find y2.
The downstream velocity V2 will be calculated as part of the solution using V2 = V1*y1/y2.
