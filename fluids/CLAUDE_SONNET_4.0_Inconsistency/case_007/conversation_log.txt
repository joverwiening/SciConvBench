Q: What is the downstream velocity V2 that you want to use in the Bernoulli equation calculation?
A: You should use the momentum equation (specific force function) instead of Bernoulli's equation, since mechanical energy is not conserved across a hydraulic jump. The downstream velocity V2 will be determined from the momentum equation along with the downstream depth y2.
