The wall extends from x=0 to some finite length L.
The temperature at x=0 is fixed at 350 K.
The wall is adiabatic, meaning there is no heat generation within the wall.
The boundary condition at x=L is dT/dx = 0 (insulated).
Solve the one-dimensional steady-state heat conduction equation d²T/dx² = 0.
The solution is T(x) = 350 K for all x from 0 to L.
The temperature distribution is uniform throughout the wall at 350 K.
