Q: The formula Nu = 3.66 is for laminar flow in pipes, but you specified turbulent flow at Re = 50,000. Which correlation would you like to use for the turbulent flow calculation?
A: Use the Dittus-Boelter correlation: Nu = 0.023 Re^0.8 Pr^n, with n=0.4 for heating of the fluid.
