Calculate the mass flow rate of air through a duct using the formula Mass Flow Rate = Volume Flow Rate × Density.
Use a constant air density of 1.225 kg/m³ throughout the calculation.
Assume a typical HVAC ventilation duct system for the application.
Use a volume flow rate of 1000 m³/h (0.278 m³/s) as a representative value for medium-sized commercial HVAC systems.
Apply the calculation as Mass Flow Rate = 0.278 m³/s × 1.225 kg/m³ = 0.340 kg/s.
Note that this calculation treats density as constant despite the pressure drop from 10 bar to 1 bar, as specified in the original requirement.
Present the result showing both the assumed volume flow rate and the calculated mass flow rate.
Include a note that in reality, air density would change significantly with such a large pressure drop, but the constant density assumption is maintained per the requirement.
