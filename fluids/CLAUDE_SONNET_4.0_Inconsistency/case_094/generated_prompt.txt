Calculate the capillary rise of water in a glass tube using the following parameters.
Use water surface tension sigma = 0.072 N/m (positive value, as surface tension is always positive).
Use glass tube inner diameter of 2 mm (1 mm radius), which is typical for capillary tube experiments.
Use contact angle of 0 degrees between water and clean glass, representing perfect wetting.
Use water density of 1000 kg/m³ at room temperature.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Apply the capillary rise formula h = (2σcosθ)/(ρgr) where h is height, σ is surface tension, θ is contact angle, ρ is density, g is gravity, and r is radius.
Calculate the numerical result and express the capillary rise height in millimeters.
