Calculate the shear stress for a Bingham plastic fluid using the equation tau = tau_y + mu_p * gamma_dot.
The yield stress tau_y is 20 Pa.
The plastic viscosity mu_p is 0.05 Pa·s.
The shear rate gamma_dot is 50 1/s.
Substitute the values into the equation to get tau = 20 + 0.05 * 50.
The final shear stress is 22 Pa.
