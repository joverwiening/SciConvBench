Calculate the mass flow rate for a converging nozzle under the assumption of choked flow conditions.
Use air as the working fluid with specific heat ratio gamma = 1.4 and gas constant R = 287 J/(kg·K).
Set stagnation pressure to 101.325 kPa (standard atmospheric pressure).
Set stagnation temperature to 288.15 K (standard atmospheric temperature).
Use a throat area of 0.001 m² (1 cm²) as a typical small nozzle size.
Apply the choked flow mass flow rate formula: m_dot = (P0 * A_throat / sqrt(T0)) * sqrt(gamma/R) * (2/(gamma+1))^((gamma+1)/(2*(gamma-1))).
Note that the given throat Mach number Mt = 0.3 contradicts the choked flow assumption, as choked flow requires Mt = 1.0 at the throat.
For the calculation, assume true choked conditions with Mt = 1.0 at the throat, as this is physically correct for choked flow.
Present the final mass flow rate in kg/s.
