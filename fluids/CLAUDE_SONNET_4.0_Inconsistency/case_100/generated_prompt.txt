Verify that the stagnation-point pressure coefficient Cp = 1.5 for incompressible flow around a body using Bernoulli's equation.
Use any free-stream velocity U_inf as the reference velocity.
Set the stagnation point velocity to zero since flow comes to rest at the stagnation point.
Apply Bernoulli's equation between the free-stream and stagnation point locations.
Use the pressure coefficient definition Cp = (p - p_inf) / (0.5 * rho * U_inf^2).
Show that this calculation yields Cp = 1.0, not 1.5 as stated in the problem.
Conclude that the given value of Cp = 1.5 is incorrect for incompressible flow, as Bernoulli's equation gives the theoretical maximum of Cp = 1.0 at a stagnation point.
