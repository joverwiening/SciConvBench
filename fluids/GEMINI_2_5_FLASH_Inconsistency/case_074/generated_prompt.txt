The simulation models air compression in a duct.
The inlet pressure is 1 bar.
The outlet pressure is 10 bar.
The simulation accounts for variable air density.
Mass continuity is used to relate inlet and outlet conditions.
The inlet cross-sectional area of the duct is 0.1 square meters.
The compression process is assumed to be adiabatic and isentropic.
The inlet velocity of the air is 15 meters per second.
The inlet temperature of the air is 25 degrees Celsius.
The duct has a constant cross-sectional area.
Air is modeled as an ideal gas with a specific gas constant of 287 J/(kg*K) and an adiabatic index of 1.4.
The simulation calculates the outlet velocity of the air.
