The pressure drop per unit length for air flowing through a packed bed will be computed using the Ergun equation.
The packed bed has a porosity of 0.40.
The particle diameter for the calculation is 5 meters.
The superficial velocity of the air is assumed to be 0.5 meters per second.
The density of the air is assumed to be 1.225 kilograms per cubic meter.
The viscosity of the air is assumed to be 1.8 x 10^-5 Pascal-seconds.
