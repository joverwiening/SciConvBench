The lumped-capacitance method will be used to compute the transient cooling curve of a solid sphere.
The Biot number for the sphere is 0.05.

The sphere is made of Aluminum with a density of 2700 kg/m^3, a specific heat capacity of 900 J/(kg*K), and a thermal conductivity of 205 W/(m*K).
The diameter of the solid sphere is 0.1 meters.
The heat transfer coefficient is 615.0 W/(m^2*K).
