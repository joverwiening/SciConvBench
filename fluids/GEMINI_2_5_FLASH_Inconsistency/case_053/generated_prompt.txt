The transient cooling curve of a solid sphere will be computed using the lumped-capacitance method.
The Biot number for the sphere is 0.05.
The initial temperature of the sphere is assumed to be 100 degrees Celsius.
The ambient temperature of the surroundings is assumed to be 20 degrees Celsius.
The material of the solid sphere is assumed to be Aluminum, with a density of 2700 kg/m^3, a specific heat of 900 J/(kg*K), and a thermal conductivity of 205 W/(m*K).
The radius of the solid sphere is assumed to be 0.05 meters.
The convective heat transfer coefficient will be calculated from the Biot number, material properties, and sphere radius.
The temperature of the sphere as a function of time will be provided.
