The heat exchanger is a counterflow unit.
The hot and cold fluids are assumed to be water.
The intended application is assumed to be general industrial process heat exchange.
The overall heat transfer coefficient (U) is assumed to be 1000 W/m²K.
The inlet temperature of the hot fluid (Th,in) is assumed to be 80°C.
The outlet temperature of the hot fluid (Th,out) is assumed to be 60°C.
The inlet temperature of the cold fluid (Tc,in) is assumed to be 20°C.
The outlet temperature of the cold fluid (Tc,out) is assumed to be 40°C.
The desired heat transfer rate (Q) is assumed to be 100 kW.
The required area will be computed using the Log Mean Temperature Difference (LMTD) method.
ΔT1 is defined as Th,in - Tc,out.
ΔT2 is defined as Th,out - Tc,in.
The LMTD is calculated as (ΔT1 - ΔT2) / ln(ΔT1 / ΔT2).
The required area (A) is calculated as Q / (U * LMTD).
