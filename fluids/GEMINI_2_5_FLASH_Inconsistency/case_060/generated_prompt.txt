The system involves a centrifugal pump drawing water from an open reservoir.
The pump centerline is 4.0 m above the reservoir free surface.
The suction-line head loss is 1.0 m.
The water temperature is 25°C.
The vapor pressure (Pv) is 3.2 kPa.
The density of water (rho) is 997 kg/m^3.
The acceleration due to gravity (g) is 9.81 m/s^2.
Atmospheric pressure should be neglected for the calculation.
The available Net Positive Suction Head (NPSHa) should be computed using the formula NPSHa = -hs - hL - Pv/(rho g).
