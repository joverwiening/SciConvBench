Water flows steadily through a sudden expansion from D1 = 50 mm to D2 = 100 mm at a flow rate Q = 0.010 m^3/s. Calculate the pressure rise across the expansion accounting for the Borda–Carnot head loss: h_L = (V1 - V2)^2/(2g). Use continuity to find V1 and V2, then use the energy equation to compute the pressure change.
