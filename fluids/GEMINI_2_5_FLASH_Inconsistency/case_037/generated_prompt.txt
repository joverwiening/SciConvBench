The task is to compute the pressure drop for oil flowing steadily in a horizontal pipe.
The pipe is 25 m long.
The pipe has an 80 mm diameter.
The mean velocity of the oil is 1.5 m/s.
The flow must be treated as viscous, and friction must be accounted for.
The pressure drop calculation must use the Darcy-Weisbach equation.
The friction factor must be determined based on the Reynolds number and the pipe's absolute roughness.
Assume the kinematic viscosity of the oil is 20 x 10^-6 m²/s.
Assume the density of the oil is 900 kg/m³.
Assume the absolute roughness of the pipe is 0.045 mm.
