The calculation will determine radiative heat loss.
The formula q = epsilon sigma A (Ts^4 - Tsur^4) will be used.
Emissivity (epsilon) is 0.8.
The Stefan-Boltzmann constant (sigma) is 5.67 x 10^-8 W/(m^2 K^4).
The surface area (A) is assumed to be 1 square meter.
The surface temperature (Ts) is assumed to be 100 degrees Celsius (373.15 Kelvin).
The surrounding temperature (Tsur) is assumed to be 25 degrees Celsius (298.15 Kelvin).
The radiative heat loss (q) will be computed in Watts.
