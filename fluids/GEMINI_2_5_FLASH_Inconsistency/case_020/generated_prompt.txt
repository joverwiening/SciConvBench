The specific heat ratio (gamma, γ) is assumed to be 1.4.
The Mach number (M) is 0.5.
The Rayleigh-Pitot formula P0/P = (1 + (γ-1)/2 * M^2)^(γ/(γ-1)) is applied.
The ratio of stagnation pressure (P0) to static pressure (P) is calculated as (1 + (1.4-1)/2 * 0.5^2)^(1.4/(1.4-1)).
The ratio P0/P is approximately 1.189.
