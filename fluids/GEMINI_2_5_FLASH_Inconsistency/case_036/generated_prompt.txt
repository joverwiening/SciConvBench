The problem is treated as a water-hammer event.
The initial water velocity is 2 m/s.
The final water velocity is 0 m/s.
The valve closure time is 0.05 s.
The density of water is assumed to be 1000 kg/m³.
The wave speed (celerity) in the pipeline is assumed to be 1200 m/s.
The pipeline length is assumed to be 100 m.
The closure is considered rapid because the closure time (0.05 s) is less than 2L/a (0.167 s).
The pressure rise is calculated using the Joukowsky equation, ΔP = ρ * a * ΔV.
The calculated pressure rise due to valve closure is 2.4 MPa.
