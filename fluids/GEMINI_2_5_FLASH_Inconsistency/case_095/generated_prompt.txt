The calculation will determine the momentum flux for fully developed laminar flow in a circular pipe.
The momentum correction factor beta is 4/3.
The density of the fluid is 1000 kg/m^3.
The average velocity of the fluid is 1 m/s.
The diameter of the circular pipe is 0.1 m.
The momentum flux is calculated as the product of the momentum correction factor, the mass flow rate, and the average velocity.
The mass flow rate is calculated as the product of the fluid density, the average velocity, and the cross-sectional area of the pipe.
The cross-sectional area of the pipe is calculated using the pipe's diameter.
