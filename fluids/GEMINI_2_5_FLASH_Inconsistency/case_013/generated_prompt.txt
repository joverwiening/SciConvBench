The initial static temperature of the liquid water jet is assumed to be 298.15 K.
The velocity of the liquid water jet is 20 m/s.
The liquid water jet is assumed to behave as an ideal gas.
The specific heat at constant pressure (Cp) for water, assuming ideal gas behavior, is taken as 1870 J/(kg·K).
The stagnation temperature is calculated using the formula T0 = T + (V^2) / (2 * Cp).
