Q: Should the 'f' in the pressure drop formula be the Fanning friction factor or the Darcy friction factor?
A: It should be the Darcy friction factor. The Fanning friction factor f_F=0.005 should be converted to the Darcy friction factor f_D = 4 * f_F = 0.02 before use in the formula.
