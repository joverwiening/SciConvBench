The density of water is assumed to be 1000 kg/m^3.
The angular velocity (omega) is assumed to be 10 rad/s.
The pressure difference along a radial line between r=0.1 m and r=0.3 m is computed using the radial equilibrium equation dp/dr = rho v_theta^2 / r.
The tangential velocity is given by v_theta = omega r.
The flow is assumed to be inviscid.
The pressure difference is 4000 Pa.
