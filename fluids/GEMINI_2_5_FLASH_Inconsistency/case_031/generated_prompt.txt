The quarter-cylindrical gate has a radius R = 2.0 m and a width b = 1.0 m.
The gate is submerged in water with its top edge at the free surface.
The density of the water is rho = 1000 kg/m^3.
The acceleration due to gravity is g = 9.81 m/s^2.
The horizontal hydrostatic force component is computed using the force on the vertical projection of the curved surface.
The vertical hydrostatic force component is computed as the weight of the imaginary column of water directly above the curved surface up to the free surface.
The direction of the vertical hydrostatic force component is upward (buoyancy).
