The Francis turbine operates with water at 100 C.
The vapor pressure head (Hv) is 0.3 m.
The atmospheric head (Ha) is 10.3 m.
The net head (H) is assumed to be 150 m.
The suction head (Hs) is assumed to be 0 m.
Thoma sigma (σ) is computed using the formula σ = (Ha - Hv - Hs) / H.
The computed Thoma sigma is 0.0667.
