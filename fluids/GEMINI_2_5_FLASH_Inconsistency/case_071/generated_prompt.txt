The task is to compute the capillary rise.
The fluid is mercury.
The tube material is glass.
The contact angle is 0 degrees.
The surface tension is 0.48 N/m.
The density of mercury is assumed to be 13534 kg/m^3.
The radius of the glass tube is assumed to be 0.001 meters.
The acceleration due to gravity is assumed to be 9.81 m/s^2.
The capillary rise will be calculated using the formula h = (2 * sigma * cos(theta)) / (rho * g * r).
