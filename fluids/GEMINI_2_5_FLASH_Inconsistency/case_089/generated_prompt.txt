The simulation models high-pressure air discharge through an orifice.
Compressible orifice/nozzle relations are used for the flow calculation.
The upstream to downstream absolute pressure ratio is 6:1.
The absolute upstream pressure is 10 bar (1 MPa).
The absolute upstream temperature is 293.15 K (20 °C).
The orifice area is 1 cm² (0.0001 m²).
The discharge coefficient (Cd) is 0.8.
The specific heat ratio for air is 1.4.
The specific gas constant for air is 287 J/(kg·K).
