The simulation will model a 5 mm diameter sphere falling in water at a velocity of 0.5 m/s.
The density of water will be assumed to be 1000 kg/m^3.
The dynamic viscosity of water will be assumed to be 0.001 Pa.s.
The Reynolds number (Re) will be calculated using the formula Re = (density * velocity * diameter) / dynamic viscosity.
The drag coefficient (Cd) for the sphere will be determined using the correlation Cd = 0.44, which is appropriate for the calculated large Reynolds number.
The projected area (A) of the sphere will be calculated as pi * (diameter/2)^2.
The drag force (Fd) will be computed using the formula Fd = (1/2) * density * velocity^2 * Cd * A.
