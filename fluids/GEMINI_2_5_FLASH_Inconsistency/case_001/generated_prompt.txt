The speed of sound for air is calculated using the ideal gas law.
The Mach number is calculated as the ratio of the air velocity to the speed of sound.
The specific heat ratio (gamma) for air is assumed to be 1.4.
The specific gas constant (R_specific) for air is assumed to be 287 J/(kg*K).
The stagnation pressure is calculated using the isentropic flow relations for a compressible ideal gas.
The stagnation pressure calculation uses the given static pressure, calculated Mach number, and assumed specific heat ratio.
