The heat removed during the condensation of steam will be calculated.
The steam is at 100 C and condenses to liquid water at 100 C.
The calculation will use the latent heat of vaporization (h_fg).
The mass of the steam will be assumed to be 1 kg.
The latent heat of vaporization (h_fg) for water at 100 C will be assumed to be 2260 kJ/kg.
The heat removed will be computed as the product of the mass and the latent heat of vaporization.
