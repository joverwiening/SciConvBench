Treat liquid water as incompressible with a density of 998 kg/m^3.
Apply Bernoulli's principle to determine the efflux velocity of water from a large open tank through a small orifice.
The orifice is located 1 meter below the free surface of the water in the tank.
Assume the pressure at the free surface and at the orifice exit is atmospheric pressure.
Assume the velocity of the water surface in the tank is negligible.
Use a standard gravitational acceleration of 9.81 m/s^2.
