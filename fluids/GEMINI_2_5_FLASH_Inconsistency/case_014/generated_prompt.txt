The flow has a Reynolds number of 0.1.
Viscous terms are not neglected.
The Stokes equations are solved.
The flow domain is a 2D channel.
The height of the 2D channel is 1 unit.
The length of the 2D channel is 10 units.
No-slip boundary conditions are applied at the top and bottom walls of the channel.
A uniform velocity profile of 1 unit/s is applied at the inlet of the channel.
A zero pressure boundary condition is applied at the outlet of the channel.
