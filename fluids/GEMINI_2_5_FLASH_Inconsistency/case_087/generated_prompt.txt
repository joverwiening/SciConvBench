The task is to compute hydraulic power.
The formula P = rho g Q H will be used.
The head H is 30 meters.
The flow rate Q is 50 liters per second.
The flow rate Q will be converted to 0.050 cubic meters per second.
The acceleration due to gravity g will be taken as 9.81 meters per second squared.
The density of the fluid rho will be assumed to be 1000 kilograms per cubic meter, representing water.
The hydraulic power P will be calculated as 1000 kg/m^3 * 9.81 m/s^2 * 0.050 m^3/s * 30 m.
The computed hydraulic power P is 14715 Watts.
