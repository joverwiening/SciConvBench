The problem involves natural convection from a vertical plate.
The surface temperature (Ts) is 80°C.
The ambient temperature (T∞) is 20°C.
The characteristic length (L) is 0.5 m.
The acceleration due to gravity (g) is 9.81 m/s^2.
The working fluid is air.
The film temperature (Tf) is 50°C.
The film temperature for calculating the thermal expansion coefficient (beta) is 323.15 K.
The thermal expansion coefficient (beta) is calculated as 1 divided by the film temperature in Kelvin.
The kinematic viscosity (ν) of air at the film temperature is 1.79 x 10^-5 m^2/s.
The thermal diffusivity (α) of air at the film temperature is 2.55 x 10^-5 m^2/s.
The Rayleigh number (Ra) is computed using the formula Ra = g beta ΔT L^3 /(ν α).
