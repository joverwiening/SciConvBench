The thermal stress in a fully constrained steel rod is to be computed.
The temperature change (ΔT) is 50 K.
The Young's Modulus (E) for steel is assumed to be 200 GPa.
The coefficient of thermal expansion (alpha) for steel is assumed to be 12 x 10^-6 /K.
The thermal stress (sigma) is calculated using the formula sigma = E * alpha * ΔT.
The computed thermal stress is 120 MPa.
