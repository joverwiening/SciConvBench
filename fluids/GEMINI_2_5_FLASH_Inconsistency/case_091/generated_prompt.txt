Design a counterflow heat exchanger.
The hot fluid is water, entering at 80 degrees Celsius and exiting at 40 degrees Celsius.
The cold fluid is water, entering at 20 degrees Celsius and exiting at 60 degrees Celsius.
The overall heat transfer coefficient (U) is 1000 Watts per square meter Kelvin.
The intended application is general industrial process cooling.
The required heat exchanger area will be computed using the counterflow LMTD expression.
The counterflow LMTD expression uses ΔT1 = Th,in - Tc,out and ΔT2 = Th,out - Tc,in.
