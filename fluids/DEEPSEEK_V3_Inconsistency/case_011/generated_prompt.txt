The fluid is water with a density of 1000 kg/m³.
The flow is steady.
The pipe diameters are 50 mm at the inlet and 100 mm at the outlet.
The volumetric flow rate is 0.010 m³/s.
Assume uniform velocity profiles at both the inlet and outlet cross-sections.
Apply the Bernoulli equation from the inlet to the outlet to calculate the pressure rise.
Neglect frictional losses in the expansion section for this calculation.
Calculate the inlet and outlet velocities from the flow rate and cross-sectional areas.
The pressure recovery is the outlet pressure minus the inlet pressure.
