A centrifugal pump draws water from an open reservoir. The pump centerline is 4.0 m above the reservoir free surface (suction lift), and the suction-line head loss is 1.0 m. Water temperature is 25°C with vapor pressure Pv = 3.2 kPa. Compute the available NPSH using absolute pressures: NPSHa = (p_atm/(rho g)) - hs - hL - (Pv/(rho g)). Take p_atm = 101.3 kPa, rho = 997 kg/m^3, and g = 9.81 m/s^2.
