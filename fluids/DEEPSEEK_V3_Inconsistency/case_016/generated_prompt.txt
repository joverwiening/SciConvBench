The task is to calculate the pressure distribution inside a centrifuge spinning at 10,000 RPM using a modified hydrostatic pressure formula.
The centrifuge is a cylindrical container rotating about its central axis.
The fluid inside the centrifuge is incompressible and has a constant density, rho.
The acceleration due to gravity, g, is negligible compared to the centrifugal acceleration.
The effective acceleration is the centrifugal acceleration, a = omega^2 * r, where omega is the angular velocity and r is the radial distance from the rotation axis.
The angular velocity, omega, is calculated from the given rotational speed of 10,000 RPM.
The pressure formula is adapted to P(r) = (1/2) * rho * omega^2 * r^2 + P0, where P0 is the pressure at the rotation axis (r=0).
The pressure distribution is calculated as a function of radial distance, r, from the axis of rotation.
The result is the pressure at any point within the fluid, increasing quadratically with radial distance.
