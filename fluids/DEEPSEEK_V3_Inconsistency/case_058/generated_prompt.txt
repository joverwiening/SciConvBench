Assume a rectangular open channel of unit width (1 meter).
Assume an upstream flow depth of 1 meter.
The upstream Froude number is given as Fr1 = 0.5.
Compute the sequent depth (downstream depth after the hydraulic jump) using the conjugate depth equation for a rectangular channel: y2/y1 = (1/2)*(sqrt(1 + 8*Fr1^2) - 1).
Perform the calculation with the assumed upstream depth of y1 = 1 m and Fr1 = 0.5.
The result is the sequent depth in meters.
