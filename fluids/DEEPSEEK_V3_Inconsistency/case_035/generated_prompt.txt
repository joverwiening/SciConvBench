Assume the fluid is water at standard conditions with a density of 1000 kg/m³.
Assume the angular velocity (omega) is 10 rad/s for a typical demonstration.
Assume the two radial points are at the same vertical height, so the height difference is zero.
Use the given velocity field: v_theta = omega * r.
Apply Bernoulli's equation along a streamline from r=0.1 m to r=0.3 m.
Since the flow is inviscid, incompressible, and steady, use the form: p/ρ + (v^2)/2 + gz = constant.
The pressure difference is calculated as: p2 - p1 = (ρ * omega^2 / 2) * (r1^2 - r2^2), where r1=0.1 m and r2=0.3 m.
Substitute the assumed values to compute the numerical pressure difference.
