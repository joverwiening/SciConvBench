Calculate the capillary rise using the Jurin's law formula: h = (2 * sigma * cos(theta)) / (rho * g * r).
Convert the tube diameter to radius in meters: r = (2.0 mm / 2) / 1000 = 0.001 m.
Substitute the values: sigma = 0.072 N/m, theta = 0 degrees (cos(0) = 1), rho = 1000 kg/m^3, g = 9.81 m/s^2, r = 0.001 m.
Compute the capillary rise: h = (2 * 0.072 * 1) / (1000 * 9.81 * 0.001) = 0.144 / 9.81 ≈ 0.01468 m.
