Assume the fluid is water at standard conditions.
Assume the fluid density is 1000 kg/m³.
Assume the Venturi meter inlet diameter is 0.1 m.
Assume the Venturi meter throat diameter is 0.05 m.
Assume a measured pressure difference (Δp) of 10,000 Pa.
Use the ideal Bernoulli equation (discharge coefficient Cd = 1) to compute the theoretical volumetric flow rate.
The calculation must use the specified diameters, density, and pressure difference.
The calculation must account for the area change between the inlet and throat.
