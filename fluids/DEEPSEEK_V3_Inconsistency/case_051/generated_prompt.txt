The task is to calculate the convective heat transfer coefficient (h) using the Dittus-Boelter correlation.
The Reynolds number (Re) is 800.
The Prandtl number (Pr) is 6.
The Dittus-Boelter correlation for heating is Nu = 0.023 * Re^0.8 * Pr^0.4.
Calculate the Nusselt number (Nu) using the given values.
The convective heat transfer coefficient h is found from the relation h = (Nu * k) / D, where k is the fluid thermal conductivity and D is the pipe diameter.
The user has not provided the fluid thermal conductivity (k) or the pipe diameter (D).
Therefore, the final answer for h must be expressed in terms of k and D.
The calculation proceeds as: Nu = 0.023 * (800)^0.8 * (6)^0.4.
Compute the numerical value: (800)^0.8 ≈ 191.0, (6)^0.4 ≈ 2.05.
Thus, Nu ≈ 0.023 * 191.0 * 2.05 ≈ 9.00.
The convective heat transfer coefficient is h ≈ (9.00 * k) / D.
