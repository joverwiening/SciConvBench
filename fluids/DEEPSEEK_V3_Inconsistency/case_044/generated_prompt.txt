The task is to compute the wall shear stress for a steady, inviscid flow of oil between parallel plates.
The oil has a dynamic viscosity (mu) of 0.1 Pa·s.
The shear stress (tau) is defined by the formula tau = mu * (du/dy).
For an inviscid flow, the velocity gradient (du/dy) is zero.
Therefore, the wall shear stress is 0 Pa.
