Assume the gas is air.
Use a specific gas constant (R) of 287 J/(kg·K).
Use a ratio of specific heats (gamma) of 1.4.
Assume a total pressure (p0) of 101325 Pa.
Assume a total temperature (T0) of 300 K.
Assume a throat area (A_t) of 0.01 m².
The throat Mach number (M_t) is 0.3.
Calculate the mass flow rate using the choked flow equation, substituting the given M_t.
The mass flow rate is computed as m_dot = (p0 / sqrt(T0)) * A_t * sqrt(gamma/R) * M_t * (1 + ((gamma-1)/2)*M_t^2)^(-(gamma+1)/(2*(gamma-1))).
