The task is to compute the pressure rise due to valve closure using steady Bernoulli.
Assume water density ρ = 1000 kg/m³.
Assume water bulk modulus K = 2.2 GPa.
Assume the pipeline length is 100 m.
Assume the pipeline diameter is 0.1 m.
Assume the pipeline material is steel.
Assume the pipeline wall thickness is 0.005 m.
Assume the initial pressure in the pipeline is 200 kPa.
Assume the flow regime is turbulent before valve closure.
Assume the modulus of elasticity of the steel pipeline is 200 GPa.
Use the steady Bernoulli equation to compute the pressure rise as Δp = ρ * L * (Δv/Δt), where L is pipeline length, Δv is velocity change (2 m/s to 0 m/s), and Δt is valve closure time (0.05 s).
Calculate the pressure rise Δp = 1000 kg/m³ * 100 m * (2 m/s / 0.05 s) = 4,000,000 Pa or 4 MPa.
Report the pressure rise as 4 MPa.
