The simulation models water flow along a curved streamline.
The water has a constant speed V of 10 meters per second.
The streamline has a constant radius of curvature R of 5 meters.
The fluid density rho is 1000 kilograms per cubic meter.
The pressure gradient points radially outward from the center of curvature.
The pressure increases toward the center of curvature.
Compute the magnitude of the pressure gradient using the formula (rho * V^2) / R.
Compute the pressure difference across a normal distance Δn of 0.10 meters.
The pressure difference is calculated as the computed pressure gradient magnitude multiplied by Δn.
The inner side of the streamline is toward the center of curvature.
The outer side of the streamline is away from the center of curvature.
The pressure on the inner side is higher than the pressure on the outer side.
The final output is the numerical value of the pressure difference (outer pressure minus inner pressure).
