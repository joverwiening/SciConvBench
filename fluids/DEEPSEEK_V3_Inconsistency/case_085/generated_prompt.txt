The hot fluid inlet temperature (Th,in) is 150°C.
The cold fluid inlet temperature (Tc,in) is 20°C.
The hot fluid outlet temperature (Th,out) is 50°C.
The cold fluid outlet temperature (Tc,out) is 80°C.
The mass flow rate of the hot fluid is 1.0 kg/s.
The mass flow rate of the cold fluid is 0.8 kg/s.
The specific heat capacity of the hot fluid is 4.2 kJ/(kg·K).
The specific heat capacity of the cold fluid is 4.2 kJ/(kg·K).
The overall heat transfer coefficient (U) is 500 W/(m²·K).
The heat exchanger is a counterflow type.
The log mean temperature difference (LMTD) is calculated using the formula: LMTD = (ΔT1 - ΔT2) / ln(ΔT1 / ΔT2), where ΔT1 = Th,in - Tc,out and ΔT2 = Th,out - Tc,in.
The required heat transfer area (A) is calculated using the formula: A = Q / (U * LMTD), where Q is the heat duty.
The heat duty (Q) is calculated from the hot fluid side as: Q = m_dot_h * Cp_h * (Th,in - Th,out).
The heat duty (Q) is also calculated from the cold fluid side as: Q = m_dot_c * Cp_c * (Tc,out - Tc,in).
The calculated heat duty from both sides must be consistent for energy balance.
The required area is computed using the parapression with the defined ΔT1 and ΔT2.
