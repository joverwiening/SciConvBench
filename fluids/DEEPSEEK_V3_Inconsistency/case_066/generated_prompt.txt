The rotor blade speed (U) is assumed to be 200 m/s, a typical value for axial compressor design.
The axial velocity (V_a) is constant through the rotor at 106.066 m/s.
The inlet relative velocity (W1) is 150 m/s at an inlet relative flow angle (β1) of 45 degrees.
The outlet relative velocity (W2) is 100 m/s at an outlet relative flow angle (β2) of 30 degrees.
Calculate the change in tangential relative velocity (ΔW_θ) as V_a * (tan(β1) - tan(β2)).
Calculate the degree of reaction (R) using the formula R = 1 - (ΔW_θ) / (2 * U).
