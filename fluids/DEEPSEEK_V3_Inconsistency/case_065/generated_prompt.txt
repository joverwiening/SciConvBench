The runner is installed 20 meters above the tailrace.
The atmospheric pressure head (Ha) is 10.3 meters.
The vapor pressure head (Hv) is 0.3 meters.
The net positive suction head available (NPSHa) is calculated as Ha - Hv - Hs, where Hs is the static suction head (20 m).
Therefore, NPSHa = 10.3 - 0.3 - 20 = -10 meters.
The cavitation factor (sigma) is defined as NPSHa / H, where H is the operating head.
For any positive operating head (H > 0), the cavitation factor sigma = (-10) / H.
Since the numerator (-10) is negative and the denominator (H) is positive, sigma is negative for any head.
A negative sigma indicates that cavitation is not just possible, but certain, as NPSHa is less than zero.
The statement to be shown is incorrect; cavitation is inevitable for any head under these conditions.
