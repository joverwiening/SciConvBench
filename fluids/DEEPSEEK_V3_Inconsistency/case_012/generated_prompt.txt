The task is to analyze Couette flow of oil with dynamic viscosity mu = 0.10 Pa·s.
The flow is between two infinite parallel plates separated by a gap h = 5 mm.
The top plate moves at velocity U = 2.0 m/s.
The bottom plate is stationary.
The flow is steady, laminar, and fully developed.
The fluid is incompressible Newtonian oil.
The pressure gradient in the flow direction is zero for simple Couette flow.
The velocity profile is linear: u(y) = (U/h) * y.
Bernoulli's equation is to be applied between points on the stationary bottom plate and the moving top plate.
Bernoulli's equation along a streamline is P/ρ + (1/2)v^2 + gz = constant.
For horizontal flow, the elevation term gz is constant and can be neglected in the comparison.
The pressure variation across the gap is to be determined from the application.
The analysis will show that the pressure is constant across the gap due to the linear velocity profile and zero pressure gradient.
