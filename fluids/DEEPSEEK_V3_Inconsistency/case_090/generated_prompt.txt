Assume the steel rod is made of mild steel.
Assume the Young's Modulus (E) for mild steel is 200 GPa.
Assume the coefficient of thermal expansion (alpha) for mild steel is 12e-6 per K.
Assume the temperature change (ΔT) is 50 K.
Assume the rod is fully constrained at both ends.
Compute the thermal stress using the formula sigma = E * alpha * ΔT.
The cross-sectional area and length of the rod are not needed for this calculation.
The initial temperature of the rod is not needed for this calculation.
The intended application is a general engineering analysis.
The thermal stress is calculated as 200e9 Pa * 12e-6 K^-1 * 50 K.
The result is 120e6 Pa or 120 MPa.
