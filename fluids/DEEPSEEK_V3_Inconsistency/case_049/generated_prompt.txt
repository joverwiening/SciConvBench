The slab length is 1 meter.
The thermal diffusivity (alpha) is 1e-5 m^2/s.
The left boundary (x=0) is a fixed temperature of 100 degrees Celsius.
The right boundary (x=L) is a fixed temperature of 0 degrees Celsius.
The governing equation is the steady-state form: d2T/dx2 = 0.
The solution is a linear temperature profile: T(x) = 100 - 100x.
