Assume the fluid is water at standard temperature and pressure (20°C, 1 atm).
Assume the Venturi meter has an inlet diameter of 100 mm and a throat diameter of 50 mm.
Assume the measured pressure difference (Δp) is 10 kPa.
Use the density of water at 20°C (998 kg/m³) for the calculation.
Apply the ideal Bernoulli equation (Cd = 1) to compute the volumetric flow rate.
The calculation must account for significant irreversible losses in the throat and diffuser sections.
The final output is the computed flow rate in cubic meters per second (m³/s).
