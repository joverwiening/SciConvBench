The task is to compute the Rayleigh number for natural convection from a vertical plate.
The plate surface temperature (Ts) is 80°C.
The ambient fluid temperature (T∞) is 20°C.
The characteristic length (L) is 0.5 m.
The fluid medium is air at atmospheric pressure (1 atm).
The volumetric thermal expansion coefficient (beta) is defined as 1/(T_film) where T_film is in degrees Celsius.
The film temperature (T_film) is the arithmetic mean of Ts and T∞, calculated as (Ts + T∞)/2.
The temperature difference (ΔT) is Ts - T∞.
The Rayleigh number (Ra) is calculated using the formula Ra = (g * beta * ΔT * L^3) / (ν * α).
The acceleration due to gravity (g) is 9.81 m/s².
The properties ν (kinematic viscosity) and α (thermal diffusivity) for air must be evaluated at the film temperature.
The final computed Rayleigh number must be provided.
