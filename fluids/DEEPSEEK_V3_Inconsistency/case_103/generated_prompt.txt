Assume a net head (H) of 100 meters for the Francis turbine.
Assume a specific speed (N_s) of 300 (metric units) for the Francis turbine.
Assume a rated power output (P) of 10,000 kilowatts for the Francis turbine.
Compute the Thoma cavitation coefficient (sigma) using the formula: sigma = (H_a - H_v) / H, where H_a is the atmospheric pressure head and H_v is the vapor pressure head.
Substitute the given values: H_a = 10.3 m, H_v = 0.3 m, and the assumed H = 100 m.
Calculate the result: sigma = (10.3 - 0.3) / 100 = 10.0 / 100 = 0.1.
Report the computed Thoma sigma as 0.1.
