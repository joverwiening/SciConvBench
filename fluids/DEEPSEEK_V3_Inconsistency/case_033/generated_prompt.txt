Assume the air in the tank is at a stagnation temperature of 300 K.
Assume the nozzle is a simple converging nozzle with a circular exit of 0.01 m diameter, giving an exit area of 7.854e-5 m².
Assume the air behaves as an ideal gas with a specific heat ratio (gamma) of 1.4 and a gas constant (R) of 287 J/(kg·K).
Calculate the mass flow rate using isentropic flow relations for a converging nozzle.
Check if the nozzle is choked by comparing the pressure ratio (P_exit/P_tank) to the critical pressure ratio.
If the nozzle is choked, the exit pressure equals the ambient pressure of 100 kPa, but the exit Mach number is 1.0, and the exit static pressure is the critical pressure.
If the nozzle is not choked, the exit static pressure equals the ambient pressure of 100 kPa, and the exit Mach number is subsonic.
Calculate the mass flow rate using the formula: m_dot = (P_tank * A_exit / sqrt(T_tank)) * sqrt(gamma/R) * (P_exit/P_tank)^(1/gamma) * sqrt( (2/(gamma-1)) * (1 - (P_exit/P_tank)^((gamma-1)/gamma)) ) for unchoked flow, or the choked flow formula: m_dot = (P_tank * A_exit / sqrt(T_tank)) * sqrt(gamma/R) * ( (2/(gamma+1))^((gamma+1)/(2*(gamma-1))) ) for choked flow.
Report the calculated mass flow rate in kg/s.
