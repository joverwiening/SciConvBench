Calculate the Weber number using the formula We = (ρ * v² * d) / σ, where ρ is the liquid density, v is the droplet velocity, d is the droplet diameter, and σ is the surface tension.

Use a droplet diameter of 2 mm, which equals 0.002 m.

Use a droplet velocity of 30 m/s.

Use a liquid density of 800 kg/m³.

Use a surface tension of 0.03 N/m.

Compute the Weber number as (800 kg/m³ * (30 m/s)² * 0.002 m) / 0.03 N/m = (800 * 900 * 0.002) / 0.03 = 1440 / 0.03 = 48000.

The Weber number for this liquid droplet is 48000.
