Calculate the pressure drop per unit length in a packed bed using the Ergun equation.

Use spherical particles with a diameter of 5 mm.

Use a superficial velocity of 0.5 m/s.

Use a void fraction (porosity) of 0.4.

Use air as the fluid with a density of 1.2 kg/m³ at standard atmospheric pressure and temperature (20°C, 1 atm).

Use a dynamic viscosity of 1.8 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s for air at standard atmospheric pressure and temperature (20°C, 1 atm).

Assume the packed bed column diameter is large enough that wall effects are negligible, or alternatively assume a column diameter of 0.1 m (10 cm) as a representative laboratory scale if needed for any secondary calculations.

Report the result as pressure drop per unit length (Pa/m).
