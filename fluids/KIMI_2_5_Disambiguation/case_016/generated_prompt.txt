Calculate the center of pressure on a vertically submerged rectangular plate with dimensions 1 m (horizontal width) by 2 m (vertical height).

Assume the top edge of the plate is located at the free surface of the fluid.

Assume the fluid is water with a density of 1000 kg/m³.

Assume the acceleration due to gravity is 9.81 m/s².

Use the formula for center of pressure on a vertical plane surface: h* = h̄ + Ī/(A·h̄), where h̄ is the depth of the centroid, Ī is the second moment of area about the centroidal axis, and A is the area.

Calculate the depth of the centroid: h̄ = 1.0 m (midpoint of the 2 m vertical height).

Calculate the area: A = 1 m × 2 m = 2 m².

Calculate the second moment of area about the centroidal horizontal axis: Ī = (width × height³)/12 = (1 × 2³)/12 = 8/12 = 0.667 m⁴.

Calculate the center of pressure depth: h* = 1.0 + 0.667/(2 × 1.0) = 1.0 + 0.333 = 1.333 m from the free surface.

Report the center of pressure location as 1.33 m below the free surface, or 0.67 m above the bottom edge of the plate.
