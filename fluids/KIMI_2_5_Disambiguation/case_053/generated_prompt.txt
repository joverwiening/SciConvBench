The torque measured by the rotational viscometer at 100 rpm is determined as follows.

The viscometer is a Couette type with concentric cylinder geometry.
The inner cylinder has radius R = 2 cm = 0.02 m.
The outer cylinder has radius R_o = 2.1 cm = 0.021 m.
The gap between cylinders is d = 0.001 m.
The immersion height of the inner cylinder is h = 5 cm = 0.05 m.
The fluid is Newtonian with constant viscosity μ = 0.5 Pa·s.
The rotational speed is N = 100 rpm = 100/60 rev/s = 10.472 rad/s angular velocity.

For a narrow-gap Couette viscometer with d/R << 1, the shear rate is approximated as γ̇ = Ω·R/d where Ω is angular velocity in rad/s.
The angular velocity is Ω = 2π·N/60 = 2π·100/60 = 10.472 rad/s.
The shear rate is γ̇ = (10.472 rad/s)·(0.02 m)/(0.001 m) = 209.4 s⁻¹.

The shear stress at the inner cylinder wall is τ = μ·γ̇ = 0.5 Pa·s · 209.4 s⁻¹ = 104.7 Pa.

The surface area of the inner cylinder in contact with fluid is A = 2π·R·h = 2π·(0.02 m)·(0.05 m) = 0.006283 m².

The tangential force on the inner cylinder is F = τ·A = 104.7 Pa · 0.006283 m² = 0.6578 N.

The torque measured is T = F·R = 0.6578 N · 0.02 m = 0.01316 N·m.

Alternatively, using the exact formula for Couette flow: T = 4π·μ·h·Ω·R²·R_o²/(R_o² - R²).
T = 4π·(0.5 Pa·s)·(0.05 m)·(10.472 rad/s)·(0.02 m)²·(0.021 m)²/((0.021 m)² - (0.02 m)²) = 0.0137 N·m.

The torque measured by the rotational viscometer at 100 rpm is approximately 0.0132 N·m (narrow-gap approximation) or 0.0137 N·m (exact solution).
