Calculate the power output of a steam turbine using the steady-flow energy equation.
Use a mass flow rate of 10 kg/s for the steam entering the turbine.
Use a specific enthalpy of 3200 kJ/kg for the steam at the turbine inlet.
Use a specific enthalpy of 2400 kJ/kg for the steam at the turbine outlet.
Apply the formula: Power Output = Mass Flow Rate × (Inlet Enthalpy − Outlet Enthalpy).
Compute the result: 10 kg/s × (3200 kJ/kg − 2400 kJ/kg) = 8000 kJ/s = 8000 kW = 8 MW.
Assume the turbine operates under steady-state conditions with negligible changes in kinetic and potential energy.
Neglect heat transfer to the surroundings.
Report the final power output as 8000 kW or 8 MW.
