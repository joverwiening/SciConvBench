Calculate the lift force generated by an airfoil using the standard lift equation.
Use an airspeed of 85 m/s.
Use standard sea-level air density of 1.225 kg/m³.
Use a planform area of 12 m².
Use a lift coefficient of 0.45.
Assume a chord length of 1.5 meters based on typical general aviation airfoil proportions.
Assume an angle of attack of 4 degrees based on typical cruise flight conditions for moderate lift coefficients.
Apply the lift force formula: L = 0.5 × ρ × v² × S × C_L.
Compute the dynamic pressure as 0.5 × 1.225 kg/m³ × (85 m/s)² = 4425.31 Pa.
Compute the lift force as 4425.31 Pa × 12 m² × 0.45 = 23,896.7 N.
Report the final lift force as approximately 23,900 N or 23.9 kN.
