Calculate the Darcy-Weisbach head loss using the formula: h_f = f × (L/D) × (v²/2g).

Use the following parameters:
- Pipe length L = 50 m
- Pipe diameter D = 0.05 m
- Flow velocity v = 2 m/s
- Darcy friction factor f = 0.02
- Gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s²

Assume the fluid is water at standard conditions with density ρ = 1000 kg/m³.

Assume the kinematic viscosity of water at 20°C: ν = 1.004 × 10⁻⁶ m²/s.

Perform the calculation:
- Calculate velocity head: v²/2g = (2)²/(2 × 9.81) = 4/19.62 = 0.2039 m
- Calculate L/D ratio: 50/0.05 = 1000
- Calculate head loss: h_f = 0.02 × 1000 × 0.2039 = 4.078 m

The Darcy-Weisbach head loss is approximately 4.08 m of water column.
