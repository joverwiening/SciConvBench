Calculate the terminal velocity of a spherical raindrop with a diameter of 2 mm falling through air.
Use an air density of 1.2 kg/m³.
Assume a constant drag coefficient of 0.5.
Assume standard gravity of 9.81 m/s².
Assume the density of water is 1000 kg/m³.
The raindrop is spherical in shape.
Terminal velocity is reached when gravitational force equals drag force.
Use the drag equation: F_d = 0.5 * ρ_air * v² * C_d * A, where A is the cross-sectional area.
Use the gravitational force: F_g = m * g, where m is the mass of the raindrop.
Equate F_d and F_g to solve for terminal velocity v.
Report the terminal velocity in m/s.
