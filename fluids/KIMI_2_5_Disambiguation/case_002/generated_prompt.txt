The Python script shall compute the lift coefficient for a NACA 4412 airfoil at 5 degrees angle of attack using thin airfoil theory.

The freestream Mach number shall be assumed to be 0.3, which is a reasonable subsonic value where thin airfoil theory remains valid and compressibility effects are moderate.

The camber line equation for NACA 4412 shall be implemented using the standard NACA 4-digit series formula: for x/c from 0 to the position of maximum camber (m), y_c = (m/p^2) * (2*p*(x/c) - (x/c)^2), and for x/c from m to the chord, y_c = (m/(1-p)^2) * ((1-2*p) + 2*p*(x/c) - (x/c)^2), where m = 0.04 and p = 0.4 for NACA 4412.

The script shall discretize the camber line into at least 100 points for numerical integration.

The Fourier coefficients A0, A1, and A2 shall be computed by integrating the camber line slope distribution using the transformation x/c = (1 - cos(theta))/2.

The lift coefficient shall be calculated as Cl = 2*pi*(alpha - alpha_L0), where alpha is in radians and alpha_L0 is the zero-lift angle of attack computed from the Fourier coefficients as alpha_L0 = A0 - A1/2.

The angle of attack shall be converted from 5 degrees to radians (0.0873 rad) for all calculations.

The script shall output the computed lift coefficient with at least 4 decimal places of precision.

The script shall include comments explaining each major step of the thin airfoil theory implementation.
