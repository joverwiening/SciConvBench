Calculate the Reynolds number at x = 0.5 m from the leading edge for flow over a flat plate in air.
Use a free stream velocity of U = 10 m/s.
Assume standard air temperature of T = 20°C (293.15 K).
Assume sea level standard atmospheric pressure of p = 101325 Pa.
Use the standard kinematic viscosity of air at these conditions: ν = 1.516 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s (or calculate from μ and ρ if showing full derivation).
Calculate Reynolds number using Re_x = (U × x) / ν.
The resulting Reynolds number is Re_x = (10 m/s × 0.5 m) / (1.516 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s) = 3.30 × 10⁵.
