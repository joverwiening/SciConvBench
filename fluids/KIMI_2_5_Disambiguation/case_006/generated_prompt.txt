Solve for the steady velocity profile in Couette flow between parallel plates separated by 10 mm.
The velocity of the moving plate is 5 m/s.
The velocity of the stationary plate is 0 m/s.
The fluid is Newtonian with constant viscosity.
The dynamic viscosity of the fluid is assumed to be 0.001 Pa·s, representing water at approximately 20°C.
The density of the fluid is assumed to be 1000 kg/m³, representing water at standard conditions.
The flow is assumed to be fully developed, steady, laminar, and incompressible.
There is no pressure gradient in the flow direction.
The velocity profile is linear between the two plates.
The solution should be expressed as u(y) = (U/h) * y, where U = 5 m/s is the moving plate velocity, h = 0.01 m is the plate separation, and y is the coordinate normal to the plates ranging from 0 to h.
