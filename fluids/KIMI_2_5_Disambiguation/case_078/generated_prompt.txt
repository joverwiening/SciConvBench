Calculate the power available in the wind stream using the formula for wind power: P = 0.5 * ρ * A * v^3, where ρ is air density, A is the swept area, and v is wind velocity.

Assume standard air density of 1.225 kg/m³.

Calculate the swept area A using the blade diameter: A = π * (d/2)^2 = π * (20 m)^2 = 1256.64 m².

Substitute values into the power equation: P = 0.5 * 1.225 kg/m³ * 1256.64 m² * (12 m/s)^3.

Compute the result: P = 0.5 * 1.225 * 1256.64 * 1728 = 1,330,000 W or approximately 1.33 MW.
