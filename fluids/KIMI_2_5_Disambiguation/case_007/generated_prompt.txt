Calculate the total heat loss per meter from a horizontal steel pipe carrying steam at 150°C to ambient air at 20°C.

Use standard 2-inch nominal pipe size with outer diameter of 60.3 mm.

Assume a standard wall thickness of 3.91 mm for Schedule 40 steel pipe, giving an inner diameter of 52.48 mm.

The pipe has 25 mm of glass wool insulation with thermal conductivity k = 0.04 W/m·K.

The outer diameter of the insulation layer is 110.3 mm (60.3 mm + 2 × 25 mm).

Use thermal conductivity of steel k_steel = 45 W/m·K.

Calculate heat loss through three thermal resistances in series: convective resistance inside the pipe, conductive resistance through steel wall, conductive resistance through insulation, and convective resistance from outer insulation surface.

Assume inner convective heat transfer coefficient h_inner = 10000 W/m²·K for condensing steam.

Use provided outer convective heat transfer coefficient h_outer = 10 W/m²·K.

Assume surface emissivity of insulation ε = 0.9 for radiation heat loss calculation.

Calculate radiation heat transfer coefficient h_rad = εσ(T_s^4 - T_amb^4)/(T_s - T_amb) where σ = 5.67×10^-8 W/m²·K^4 and T_s is outer surface temperature.

Solve iteratively for outer surface temperature since radiation depends on T_s.

Combine convection and radiation in parallel at outer surface: h_total = h_outer + h_rad.

Calculate total thermal resistance per meter: R_total = R_inner_conv + R_steel_cond + R_insulation_cond + R_outer_total.

Calculate heat loss per meter: q/L = (T_steam - T_ambient) / R_total.

Report the final heat loss value in W/m.
