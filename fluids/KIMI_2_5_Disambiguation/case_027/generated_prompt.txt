Calculate the lift force using the lift equation L = 0.5 * ρ * V² * S * CL.
Use air density ρ = 1.1 kg/m³.
Use airspeed V = 80 m/s.
Use wing area S = 20 m².
Use lift coefficient CL = 0.45.
Compute dynamic pressure q = 0.5 * 1.1 * (80)² = 3520 Pa.
Compute lift L = 3520 * 20 * 0.45 = 31680 N.
Report the final lift force as 31680 Newtons (or 31.68 kN).
