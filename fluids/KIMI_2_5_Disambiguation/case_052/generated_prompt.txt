Calculate the dynamic viscosity of the fluid using Stokes' law for a falling-ball viscometer.
Use a steel ball with diameter 3 mm (radius 1.5 mm or 0.0015 m).
Use fluid density of 1000 kg/m³.
Use steel ball density of 7850 kg/m³.
Use terminal velocity of 5 cm/s (0.05 m/s).
Apply the formula: viscosity = (2 * r² * g * (ρ_ball - ρ_fluid)) / (9 * v_terminal), where r is ball radius, g is gravitational acceleration (9.81 m/s²), ρ_ball is ball density, ρ_fluid is fluid density, and v_terminal is terminal velocity.
Report the final viscosity value in SI units (Pa·s or kg/(m·s)).
