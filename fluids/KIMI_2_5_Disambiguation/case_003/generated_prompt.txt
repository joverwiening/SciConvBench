Calculate the terminal velocity of a spherical particle using Stokes' Law.

The particle has a diameter of 2 mm, which is a radius of 0.001 m.

The particle density is 7800 kg/m³.

The oil density is 890 kg/m³.

The oil dynamic viscosity is 0.29 Pa·s.

Use the terminal velocity formula for Stokes' regime: v_t = (2/9) * (r² * g * (ρ_p - ρ_f)) / μ.

Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².

Substitute values: v_t = (2/9) * ((0.001 m)² * 9.81 m/s² * (7800 - 890) kg/m³) / 0.29 Pa·s.

Compute the density difference: 7800 - 890 = 6910 kg/m³.

Compute the terminal velocity: v_t = (2/9) * (1×10⁻⁶ m² * 9.81 m/s² * 6910 kg/m³) / 0.29 Pa·s.

Calculate the numerator: 2 * 1×10⁻⁶ * 9.81 * 6910 / 9 = 0.01506 kg/(m·s²) or 0.01506 Pa.

Divide by viscosity: 0.01506 / 0.29 = 0.0519 m/s.

Verify the Reynolds number to confirm Stokes' regime validity: Re = (ρ_f * v_t * d) / μ = (890 * 0.0519 * 0.002) / 0.29 = 0.32.

Since Re < 1, the Stokes' regime assumption is valid.

The terminal velocity is approximately 0.052 m/s or 5.2 cm/s.
