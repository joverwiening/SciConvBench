Calculate the hydraulic diameter of a rectangular duct using the formula Dh = 4A/P, where A is the cross-sectional area and P is the wetted perimeter.
Use a duct width of 30 cm and a duct height of 10 cm.
Compute the cross-sectional area as A = 30 cm × 10 cm = 300 cm².
Compute the wetted perimeter as P = 2 × (30 cm + 10 cm) = 80 cm.
Calculate the hydraulic diameter as Dh = 4 × 300 cm² / 80 cm = 15 cm.
Report the final answer as 15 cm or 0.15 m.
