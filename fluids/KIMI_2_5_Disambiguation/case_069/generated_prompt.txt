Calculate the stagnation temperature on the nose of the rocket using the isentropic flow relation for stagnation temperature.

Use the formula: T0 = T * (1 + (gamma - 1)/2 * M^2)

Assume gamma = 1.4 for air as the working fluid.

Substitute the given values: T = 220 K, M = 2.5.

Calculate the temperature ratio term: (1.4 - 1)/2 * 2.5^2 = 0.2 * 6.25 = 1.25.

Calculate the stagnation temperature: T0 = 220 K * (1 + 1.25) = 220 K * 2.25 = 495 K.

The stagnation temperature on the nose of the rocket is 495 K.
