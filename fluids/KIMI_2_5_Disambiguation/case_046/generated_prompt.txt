The hydrodynamic entrance length for laminar pipe flow shall be calculated using the established correlation for laminar flow in circular pipes.

The inner diameter of the pipe is 25 mm.

The working fluid is assumed to be water at approximately 20 degrees Celsius with a kinematic viscosity of 1.004 times 10 to the negative 6 meters squared per second.

The Reynolds number is specified as 1500, which confirms laminar flow conditions since it is below the critical value of 2300.

The hydrodynamic entrance length correlation for laminar pipe flow is L over D equals 0.05 times Re.

The entrance length is calculated as 0.05 times 1500 times 0.025 m equals 1.875 m.

The hydrodynamic entrance length is 1.875 meters or 1875 mm.
