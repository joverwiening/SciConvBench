Calculate the rate of heat transfer through a concrete wall using Fourier's Law of Heat Conduction.

Use the given thermal conductivity value of 1.4 W/(m·K) for the concrete material.

Use the given wall thickness of 0.2 m as the distance for heat conduction.

Use the given total surface area of 15 m² for heat transfer calculation (individual height and width dimensions are not required since area is provided).

Use the given temperature difference of 20°C between the two sides of the wall.

Apply the formula Q = k × A × ΔT / d, where Q is heat transfer rate (W), k is thermal conductivity (W/m·K), A is area (m²), ΔT is temperature difference (°C or K), and d is thickness (m).

Calculate Q = 1.4 × 15 × 20 / 0.2 = 2100 W.

Report the final rate of heat transfer as 2100 W or 2.1 kW.
