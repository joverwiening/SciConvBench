The convective heat transfer coefficient shall be computed for air flowing over a flat plate using the following parameters and methodology.

The air velocity is 5 m/s.

The air temperature is assumed to be 300 K (approximately 27°C), which is a standard reference temperature for room-temperature heat transfer calculations.

The surface temperature of the flat plate is assumed to be 350 K (approximately 77°C), which provides a typical temperature difference of 50 K for natural or forced convection scenarios.

The characteristic length for Reynolds number calculation is 1 m, representing the length of the plate in the flow direction.

The air pressure is assumed to be standard atmospheric pressure of 101325 Pa.

The analysis shall determine whether the flow is laminar or turbulent by computing the Reynolds number at the plate trailing edge using Re_L = (rho * u * L) / mu, where air properties at 300 K are: density approximately 1.177 kg/m³, dynamic viscosity approximately 1.846 × 10^-5 Pa·s, Prandtl number approximately 0.707, and thermal conductivity approximately 0.0262 W/m·K.

The Reynolds number at L = 1 m is approximately 319,000, indicating mixed boundary layer conditions with transition to turbulence.

For the laminar region (0 to x_critical where Re_x = 5 × 10^5), the local Nusselt number correlation Nu_x = 0.332 * Re_x^0.5 * Pr^(1/3) shall be used. For the turbulent region (x_critical to L), the local Nusselt number correlation Nu_x = 0.0296 * Re_x^0.8 * Pr^(1/3) shall be used.

The average heat transfer coefficient over the entire plate shall be computed by integrating the local heat transfer coefficients across both regions and dividing by the total length, or using appropriate average Nusselt number correlations for mixed boundary layers: Nu_L = (0.037 * Re_L^0.8 - 871) * Pr^(1/3) for 0.6 < Pr < 60 and 5 × 10^5 < Re_L < 10^8.

The final convective heat transfer coefficient h is obtained from h = Nu_L * k / L.
