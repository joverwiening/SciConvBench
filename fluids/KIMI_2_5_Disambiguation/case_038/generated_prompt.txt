The specific work output of a Brayton cycle shall be calculated using the following parameters and methodology.

The working fluid is air with a specific heat capacity at constant pressure (cp) of 1.005 kJ/kg·K.

The compressor inlet temperature (T1) is 300 K.

The turbine inlet temperature (T3) is 1200 K.

The pressure ratio (rp) is 10.

The specific heat ratio (k) for air shall be assumed to be 1.4 based on standard thermodynamic practice.

The temperature at the compressor outlet (T2) shall be calculated using the isentropic relation: T2 = T1 * (rp)^((k-1)/k).

The temperature at the turbine outlet (T4) shall be calculated using the isentropic relation: T4 = T3 / (rp)^((k-1)/k).

The specific work input to the compressor shall be calculated as: w_compressor = cp * (T2 - T1).

The specific work output from the turbine shall be calculated as: w_turbine = cp * (T3 - T4).

The net specific work output of the Brayton cycle shall be calculated as: w_net = w_turbine - w_compressor.

The final numerical result shall be presented in units of kJ/kg with appropriate significant figures.
