Determine the stagnation temperature of a compressible flow at Mach 2.0.

Assume the gas is air with a specific heat ratio (gamma) of 1.4, which is the standard value for diatomic gases at moderate temperatures.

Use the isentropic stagnation temperature relation: T0/T = 1 + (gamma-1)/2 * M^2.

Calculate the temperature ratio: T0/T = 1 + (1.4-1)/2 * (2.0)^2 = 1 + 0.2 * 4 = 1.8.

Multiply by the static temperature: T0 = 1.8 * 250 K = 450 K.

The stagnation temperature is 450 K.
