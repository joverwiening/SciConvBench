Calculate the convective heat transfer rate using Newton's Law of Cooling.
Use the formula Q = h × A × (T_surface - T_fluid).
Use a surface area of 2 m².
Use a plate surface temperature of 60°C.
Use a surrounding fluid temperature of 20°C.
Use a convective heat transfer coefficient of 25 W/(m²·K).
Calculate the temperature difference as 60°C - 20°C = 40°C.
Calculate the heat transfer rate as Q = 25 × 2 × 40 = 2000 W.
The convective heat transfer rate is 2000 W or 2 kW.
