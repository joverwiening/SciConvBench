Calculate the hydraulic power required using the formula: P_hydraulic = ρ × g × Q × H, where ρ = 1000 kg/m³ (density of water), g = 9.81 m/s² (acceleration due to gravity), Q = 0.02 m³/s (flow rate), and H = 30 m (head).

The hydraulic power is 1000 × 9.81 × 0.02 × 30 = 5886 W or 5.886 kW.

Calculate the actual shaft power required by dividing hydraulic power by pump efficiency: P_shaft = P_hydraulic / η, where η = 0.80 (80% efficiency).

The actual power required is 5886 / 0.80 = 7357.5 W or approximately 7.36 kW.

Report the final answer as approximately 7.4 kW (or 7.36 kW for higher precision).
