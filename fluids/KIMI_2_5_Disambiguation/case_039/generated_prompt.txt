The disk has a radius of 0.2 m.
The gap distance between the disk and the wall is 1 mm (0.001 m).
The dynamic viscosity of the fluid is 0.1 Pa·s.
The disk rotates at an angular velocity of 60 rpm (6.283 rad/s or 2π rad/s).
The flow in the gap is assumed to be laminar and fully developed.
The no-slip boundary condition applies at both the disk surface and the wall.
The torque is calculated by integrating the viscous shear stress over the disk area.
The shear stress at radius r is given by τ = μ * (ω * r) / h, where μ is viscosity, ω is angular velocity, and h is gap height.
The differential torque element is dT = r * τ * dA = r * (μ * ω * r / h) * (2πr * dr).
The total torque is T = ∫(0 to R) (2πμω/h) * r³ dr = (πμωR⁴) / (2h).
The calculated torque value is approximately 0.503 N·m.
