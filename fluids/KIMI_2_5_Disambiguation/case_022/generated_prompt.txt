The drag coefficient of a sphere in creeping flow at Re = 0.1 shall be determined using Stokes' law.

The Reynolds number is 0.1, which satisfies the creeping flow condition (Re << 1).

Use the Stokes drag formula for drag coefficient: Cd = 24 / Re.

Calculate Cd = 24 / 0.1 = 240.

The drag coefficient is 240.
