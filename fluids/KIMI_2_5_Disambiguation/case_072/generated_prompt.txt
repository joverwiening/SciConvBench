Calculate the hydraulic efficiency of a Pelton wheel turbine using the following parameters and methodology.

Assume a velocity coefficient (Cv) of 0.98 for the nozzle, based on typical values for well-designed Pelton nozzles.

Assume a jet ratio (m = D/d) of 12, which is a standard design value for Pelton wheels balancing efficiency and mechanical constraints.

Assume a speed ratio (φ = u/V1) of 0.46, which is near the theoretical optimum of 0.5 for maximum efficiency.

Assume a blade friction coefficient (k) of 0.90, representing moderate friction losses in the buckets.

Assume a deflection angle (β) of 165 degrees, which is typical for Pelton wheel buckets to ensure smooth water exit.

Calculate the jet velocity V1 using V1 = Cv × √(2gH), where g = 9.81 m/s² and H = 300 m.

Calculate the bucket speed u using u = φ × V1.

Calculate the relative velocity at inlet Vr1 = V1 - u.

Calculate the relative velocity at outlet Vr2 = k × Vr1.

Calculate the whirl velocity at inlet Vw1 = V1.

Calculate the whirl velocity at outlet Vw2 = Vr2 × cos(180° - β) - u.

Calculate the hydraulic efficiency using ηh = [2 × u × (Vw1 + Vw2)] / V1², or equivalently ηh = 2φ(1 - φ)(1 + kcosθ) where θ = 180° - β.

Report the final hydraulic efficiency as a percentage.
