Calculate the Peclet number using the formula Pe = (u * L) / α, where u is flow velocity, L is characteristic length, and α is thermal diffusivity.

Use a characteristic length of 5 cm, which equals 0.05 m.

Use a flow velocity of 0.1 m/s.

Use a thermal diffusivity of 1.2e-7 m²/s.

Compute the Peclet number as (0.1 m/s * 0.05 m) / 1.2e-7 m²/s = 0.005 / 1.2e-7 = 41666.67.

Report the final Peclet number as approximately 4.17 × 10⁴ or 41667.
