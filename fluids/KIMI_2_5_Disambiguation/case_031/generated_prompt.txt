Calculate the hydrostatic pressure at a depth of 2000 m in the ocean using the fluid statics equation.
Use a seawater density of 1025 kg/m³ as provided.
Use a standard gravitational acceleration of 9.80665 m/s² (standard gravity).
Use a standard atmospheric pressure at sea level of 101325 Pa (1 atm).
Apply the formula P = P_atm + ρgh, where P is total pressure, P_atm is atmospheric pressure, ρ is seawater density, g is gravitational acceleration, and h is depth.
Compute the gauge pressure (ρgh) as 1025 kg/m³ × 9.80665 m/s² × 2000 m = 20103632.5 Pa.
Add atmospheric pressure to obtain absolute pressure: 101325 Pa + 20103632.5 Pa = 20204957.5 Pa.
Report the final absolute pressure as approximately 20.2 MPa or 20205 kPa.
