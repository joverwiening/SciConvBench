Calculate the efficiency of a conical draft tube using the following parameters and methodology.

Use an inlet diameter of 1.0 meter based on standard hydraulic turbine draft tube design practices for medium-sized installations.

Use an outlet diameter of 2.0 meters based on typical expansion ratios of 2:1 to 3:1 for conical draft tubes, selecting the conservative 2:1 ratio.

The water velocity at the inlet is 10 m/s.

The water velocity at the outlet is 2 m/s.

The pressure head recovery between inlet and outlet is 4.5 meters of water.

Calculate the actual kinetic energy head recovered using the pressure head recovery value of 4.5 m.

Calculate the theoretical maximum kinetic energy head available for recovery as the difference between inlet and outlet kinetic energy heads: (V₁² - V₂²) / (2g) = (10² - 2²) / (2 × 9.81) = (100 - 4) / 19.62 = 96 / 19.62 = 4.893 meters.

Calculate the draft tube efficiency as the ratio of actual recovered head to theoretical available head: η = 4.5 / 4.893 = 0.9197 or approximately 92.0%.

Report the final efficiency as 92.0% or 0.92.
