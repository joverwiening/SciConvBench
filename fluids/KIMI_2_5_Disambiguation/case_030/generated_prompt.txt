Calculate the Reynolds number of a 1 mm diameter water droplet falling through air at terminal velocity.

Use a droplet diameter of d = 1 mm = 0.001 m.

Use air density of ρ_air = 1.225 kg/m³ (standard at sea level).

Use water density of ρ_water = 1000 kg/m³.

Use kinematic viscosity of air of ν = 1.5 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s as given.

Use gravitational acceleration of g = 9.81 m/s².

Assume surface tension of water of σ = 0.072 N/m at 20°C.

Use the standard drag coefficient for a spherical droplet of Cd = 0.47.

Calculate the terminal velocity using the force balance: drag force equals weight minus buoyancy.

The terminal velocity formula is: v_t = √[(4/3) × g × d × (ρ_water - ρ_air) / (Cd × ρ_air)].

Calculate v_t = √[(4/3) × 9.81 × 0.001 × (1000 - 1.225) / (0.47 × 1.225)] = √[(4/3) × 9.81 × 0.001 × 998.775 / 0.57575] = √[13.072 / 0.57575] = √22.70 = 4.76 m/s.

Calculate the Reynolds number using Re = v_t × d / ν.

Calculate Re = 4.76 × 0.001 / (1.5 × 10⁻⁵) = 0.00476 / 0.000015 = 317.3.

The Reynolds number is approximately 317 (or about 320 when rounded).
