The degree of reaction for an axial flow compressor stage shall be calculated using the standard definition: R = (Δh_rotor)/(Δh_stage) = (V_w1 - V_w2)/(2U) + 0.5, where V_w represents the whirl components of relative velocity.

The axial velocity is constant at 106.07 m/s, calculated from V_r1 × cos(β₁) = 150 × cos(45°).

The whirl component of relative velocity at rotor inlet is V_w1 = V_r1 × sin(β₁) = 150 × sin(45°) = 106.07 m/s.

The whirl component of relative velocity at rotor outlet is V_w2 = V_r2 × sin(β₂) = 100 × sin(30°) = 50 m/s. Note: The sign convention assumes V_w2 is in the same direction as V_w1 (both positive for a compressor).

The blade speed at mean radius is U = 200 m/s.

The degree of reaction is R = (106.07 - 50)/(2 × 200) + 0.5 = 56.07/400 + 0.5 = 0.1402 + 0.5 = 0.6402, or approximately 64.0%.

This indicates a moderately high degree of reaction, typical for axial compressor stages where the static enthalpy rise is shared between rotor and stator, with slightly more occurring in the rotor.
