Compute the Rayleigh number for natural convection from a vertical plate using the following inputs and procedure.

The surface temperature is Ts = 80°C.
The free-stream temperature is T∞ = 20°C.
The characteristic length is L = 0.5 m.
The film temperature is T_f = (Ts + T∞)/2 = (80 + 20)/2 = 50°C = 323.15 K.
The volumetric thermal expansion coefficient is beta = 1/T_f(K) = 1/323.15 K⁻¹.
The temperature difference is ΔT = Ts − T∞ = 80 − 20 = 60 K.
The gravitational acceleration is g = 9.81 m/s².
The kinematic viscosity of air at 50°C is ν = 1.798 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s (from Incropera).
The thermal diffusivity of air at 50°C is α = 2.097 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s (from Incropera).
The Rayleigh number formula is Ra = g × beta × ΔT × L³ / (ν × α).
Substitute all values into the formula and compute the numerical result.
Report the final Rayleigh number value.
