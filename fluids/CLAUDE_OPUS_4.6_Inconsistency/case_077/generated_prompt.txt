The simulation addresses a Brayton cycle and computes the temperature after compression for two cases.

Case 1: Isothermal Compression.
For isothermal compression, T2 equals T1 by definition.
T1 is 300 K.
Therefore T2 is 300 K for the isothermal case.

Case 2: Isentropic Compression (Standard Brayton Cycle).
For isentropic compression, use the relation T2/T1 = (p2/p1)^((gamma-1)/gamma).
T1 is 300 K.
The pressure ratio p2/p1 is 8.
The ratio of specific heats gamma is 1.4, corresponding to air as the working fluid.
Compute the exponent as (gamma-1)/gamma = (1.4-1)/1.4 = 0.4/1.4.
Compute T2 as T1 multiplied by (p2/p1) raised to the power of (gamma-1)/gamma.
Report T2 in Kelvin.

Both cases should be clearly presented with their respective T2 results.
