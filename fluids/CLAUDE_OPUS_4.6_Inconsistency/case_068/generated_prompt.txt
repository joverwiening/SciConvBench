Use the Darcy–Weisbach head loss equation: h_f = f × (L / D) × (V² / (2g)).
The Darcy–Weisbach friction factor is f = 0.02.
Assume a pipe length of L = 100 m.
Assume an inner pipe diameter of D = 0.1 m (100 mm).
Assume a flow velocity of V = 2 m/s.
Assume the fluid is water at standard conditions.
Use gravitational acceleration g = 9.81 m/s².
Compute the head loss h_f in meters.
The purpose is to demonstrate the Darcy–Weisbach head loss calculation with these standard textbook values.
