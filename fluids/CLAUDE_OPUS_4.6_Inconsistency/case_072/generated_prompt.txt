Use the Ergun equation to compute the pressure drop per unit length (ΔP/L) in a packed bed.
The Ergun equation is: ΔP/L = (150 × μ × U × (1 − ε)² ) / (D_p² × ε³) + (1.75 × ρ × U² × (1 − ε)) / (D_p × ε³).
The packed bed porosity is ε = 0.4.
The particle diameter is D_p = 5 mm (0.005 m).
Assume the fluid is air at standard conditions (approximately 20 °C and 1 atm), with density ρ ≈ 1.204 kg/m³ and dynamic viscosity μ ≈ 1.825 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s.
Assume a superficial velocity of U = 0.1 m/s, which is a typical moderate flow rate for air through a packed bed.
Express the result as pressure drop per unit length (ΔP/L) in units of Pa/m.
Compute both the viscous (Blake-Kozeny) term and the inertial (Burke-Plummer) term of the Ergun equation separately, then sum them to obtain the total ΔP/L.
Report the individual contributions of each term as well as the total pressure drop per unit length.
