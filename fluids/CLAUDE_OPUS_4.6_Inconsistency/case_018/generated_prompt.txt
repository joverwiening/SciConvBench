The task is to compute the kinetic-energy correction factor alpha for a specified flow condition in a circular pipe.

The pipe is circular with diameter D = 20 mm.
The mean velocity is V = 1.0 m/s.
The velocity profile is assumed to be uniform (plug flow), meaning the velocity is the same at every point across the cross-section.
The kinetic-energy correction factor alpha is defined as alpha = (1/A) * integral over A of (v/V_mean)^3 dA, where v is the local velocity, V_mean is the mean velocity, and A is the cross-sectional area.
For a uniform (plug flow) velocity profile, v = V_mean at every point in the cross-section.
Compute alpha using the standard definition of the kinetic-energy correction factor.
Report the numerical value of alpha.
