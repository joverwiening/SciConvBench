The task is to compute the convective heat transfer coefficient h for water flowing in a pipe under laminar, fully developed conditions with constant wall temperature.
The flow has Re = 800 and Pr = 6.
The Dittus-Boelter correlation is not applicable because Re = 800 is laminar (well below the turbulent threshold of Re > 10,000).
Instead, use the fully developed laminar flow correlation for constant wall temperature: Nu = 3.66.
Assume a standard water thermal conductivity of k = 0.607 W/(m·K), corresponding to water at approximately 25°C.
Assume a pipe inner diameter of D = 0.025 m (25 mm), which is a common nominal pipe size for internal flow problems.
Compute the convective heat transfer coefficient using h = Nu * k / D.
Report the final value of h with appropriate units (W/(m²·K)).
State all assumptions clearly in the output, including the laminar correlation used, the assumed thermal conductivity, the assumed temperature, and the assumed pipe diameter.
