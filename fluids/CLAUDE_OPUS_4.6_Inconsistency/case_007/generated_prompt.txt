A rectangular open channel has a width of 3 m.
The upstream depth is y1 = 0.4 m.
The upstream velocity is V1 = 6.0 m/s.
A hydraulic jump occurs in the channel.
Use the momentum equation (specific force / momentum function) combined with the continuity equation to find the downstream conjugate (sequent) depth y2.
Do not apply Bernoulli's equation across the jump, as mechanical energy is not conserved due to turbulent dissipation.
Use the standard conjugate depth relation for a rectangular channel: y2 = (y1 / 2) * (-1 + sqrt(1 + 8 * Fr1^2)), where Fr1 is the upstream Froude number.
The Froude number is defined as Fr1 = V1 / sqrt(g * y1), using g = 9.81 m/s^2.
Report the downstream depth y2 in meters.
