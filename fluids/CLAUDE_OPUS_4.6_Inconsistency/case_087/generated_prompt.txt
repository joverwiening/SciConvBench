Compute the hydraulic power of a pump using the formula P = rho × g × Q × H.
The fluid density (rho) is 1000 kg/m³, assuming standard water at approximately 4°C per common engineering practice.
The gravitational acceleration (g) is 9.81 m/s².
The volumetric flow rate (Q) is 50 L/s, which must be converted to 0.05 m³/s for use in SI units.
The pump head (H) is 30 m.
The output should report the hydraulic power in watts (W) and optionally in kilowatts (kW).
