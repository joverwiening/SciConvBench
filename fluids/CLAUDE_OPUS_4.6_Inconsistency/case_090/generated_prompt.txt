The rod is made of steel.
The Young's modulus E is 200 GPa.
The coefficient of linear thermal expansion alpha is 12 × 10⁻⁶ per K.
The temperature change delta-T is 50 K.

Case 1: Free to expand (unconstrained).
The rod expands freely with no external restraint.
The thermal stress is zero because no mechanical constraint opposes the expansion.

Case 2: Fully constrained (both ends fixed).
The rod cannot expand at all.
Compute the thermal stress using sigma = E × alpha × delta-T.
The expected result is sigma = 200 × 10⁹ Pa × 12 × 10⁻⁶ per K × 50 K = 120 × 10⁶ Pa = 120 MPa compressive.
The stress is compressive because the rod tries to expand but is prevented from doing so.

Present both cases clearly and note the distinction between them.
Note that the original problem statement contained a contradiction by saying the rod is free to expand while requesting the fully constrained formula, and explain that this specification resolves that contradiction by computing both scenarios.
