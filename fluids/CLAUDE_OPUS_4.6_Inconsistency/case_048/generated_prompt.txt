The problem is a 1D steady-state heat conduction problem through a plane wall with no internal heat generation.
The governing equation is d²T/dx² = 0.
The wall thickness L is assumed to be 0.1 m as a reasonable default.
The thermal conductivity is assumed to be 15 W/(m·K), representative of a typical steel, though its value does not affect the solution in this case.
The boundary condition at x = 0 is a fixed temperature: T(0) = 350 K.
The boundary condition at x = L is adiabatic (insulated): dT/dx = 0 at x = L.
There is no volumetric heat generation within the wall (q̇ = 0).
The analytical solution is T(x) = 350 K uniformly throughout the wall for all x in [0, L].
The simulation should solve the governing equation numerically and verify that the computed temperature field matches the uniform analytical solution T(x) = 350 K.
The result should be presented as a temperature distribution plot of T(x) versus x from x = 0 to x = L.
