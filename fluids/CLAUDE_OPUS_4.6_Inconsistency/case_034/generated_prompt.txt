The system consists of water flowing from a reservoir to a discharge point through a pipe with a pump.
The reservoir surface is at elevation z1 = 0 m.
The discharge exit is at elevation z2 = 5 m.
A pump adds 20 m of head to the fluid.
The pipe is frictionless, so friction head loss is zero.
There is no turbine in the system.
Both the reservoir surface and the discharge exit are open to atmosphere, so the pressure terms cancel out.
The velocity at the reservoir surface is zero (large reservoir assumption).
Apply the modified Bernoulli equation: z1 + (V1^2)/(2g) + hp = z2 + (V2^2)/(2g) + hf.
Substitute known values: 0 + 0 + 20 = 5 + (V2^2)/(2 * 9.81) + 0.
Solve for the exit velocity V2 at the discharge point.
Use g = 9.81 m/s^2.
