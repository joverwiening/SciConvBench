The fluid is water with a density of 999 kg/m³ (standard conditions).
The flow is steady and incompressible through a sudden expansion in a horizontal pipe.
The inlet diameter is D1 = 50 mm (0.050 m).
The outlet diameter is D2 = 100 mm (0.100 m).
The volumetric flow rate is Q = 0.010 m³/s.
Use the continuity equation to compute the inlet velocity: V1 = Q / A1, where A1 = π/4 × D1².
Use the continuity equation to compute the outlet velocity: V2 = Q / A2, where A2 = π/4 × D2².
Apply the modified Bernoulli equation (energy equation) between inlet and outlet, assuming a horizontal pipe so elevation terms cancel: P1/(ρg) + V1²/(2g) = P2/(ρg) + V2²/(2g) + h_L.
The Borda–Carnot head loss for the sudden expansion is h_L = (V1 − V2)² / (2g).
Rearrange to solve for the pressure rise: P2 − P1 = (ρ/2)(V1² − V2²) − (ρ/2)(V1 − V2)².
Report the pressure rise (P2 − P1) in Pascals.
