Compute the momentum flux for fully developed laminar flow in a circular pipe.
Use the momentum correction factor β = 4/3.
Express the momentum flux using the general formula: Momentum Flux = β × ρ × A × V², where ρ is the fluid density, A is the pipe cross-sectional area, and V is the mean (bulk) velocity.
Substitute β = 4/3 into the formula to obtain: Momentum Flux = (4/3) × ρ × A × V².
All variables (ρ, A, V) are to be left in symbolic form; no specific numerical values are assigned.
The result is a single closed-form symbolic expression for the momentum flux.
