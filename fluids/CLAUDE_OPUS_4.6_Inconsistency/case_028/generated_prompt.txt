The task is a 2D potential flow calculation for a thin airfoil using thin airfoil theory and the Kutta-Joukowski theorem.

The airfoil is a 2D thin airfoil (flat plate) with chord c = 1.0 m.
The freestream velocity is U∞ = 50 m/s.
The angle of attack is α = 5°.
The fluid is air with density ρ = 1.2 kg/m³.
The flow is assumed irrotational everywhere (potential flow).
The Kutta condition is applied at the trailing edge to determine circulation.
Use thin airfoil theory to compute the lift coefficient as Cl = 2πα, where α is expressed in radians.
Convert α from degrees to radians: α = 5 × π / 180.
Compute the lift per unit span using L' = 0.5 × ρ × U∞² × c × Cl.
Compute the circulation Γ from the Kutta-Joukowski theorem: L' = ρ × U∞ × Γ, solving for Γ.
Report the values of Cl, L' (in N/m), and Γ (in m²/s).
