The flow is steady, inviscid, and irrotational (zero vorticity).
The fluid density is ρ = 1000 kg/m³.
Point A has pressure pA = 200 kPa, velocity VA = 5 m/s, and elevation zA = 0 m.
Point B is on a different streamline and has velocity VB = 8 m/s and elevation zB = 0 m.
Because the flow is irrotational, the Bernoulli constant is the same across all streamlines.
Apply the Bernoulli equation pA + 0.5·ρ·VA² + ρ·g·zA = pB + 0.5·ρ·VB² + ρ·g·zB with g = 9.81 m/s² to solve for pB.
Since zA = zB = 0, the elevation terms cancel, reducing the equation to pA + 0.5·ρ·VA² = pB + 0.5·ρ·VB².
Solve for pB in Pa and convert to kPa.
