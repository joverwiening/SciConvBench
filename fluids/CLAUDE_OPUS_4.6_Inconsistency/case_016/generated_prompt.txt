The simulation computes the pressure distribution inside a centrifuge spinning at 10,000 RPM by integrating the rotating fluid equilibrium equation dP/dr = ρ·r·ω².
The integrated pressure formula is P(r) = P_inner + 0.5·ρ·ω²·(r² - r_inner²).
The angular velocity ω is computed from 10,000 RPM as ω = 10000 × 2π / 60 rad/s.
The fluid is water with a density of 998 kg/m³.
The inner radius of the fluid column is 0.05 m (5 cm).
The outer radius of the fluid column is 0.15 m (15 cm).
The boundary condition is P_inner = 101325 Pa (standard atmospheric pressure) at the inner radius.
The radial domain from 0.05 m to 0.15 m is discretized into at least 100 evenly spaced points.
The pressure is calculated at each discrete radial point using the integrated formula.
The output is both a plot and a printed table.
The plot shows pressure (in Pascals) on the y-axis versus radial position (in meters) on the x-axis, with labeled axes and a descriptive title.
The printed table displays radial position in meters and corresponding pressure in Pascals at approximately 10 evenly spaced radial points including the inner and outer boundaries.
The simulation prints the key parameters (RPM, ω, density, inner radius, outer radius, boundary pressure) before displaying results.
