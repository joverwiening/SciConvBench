The wall surface is maintained at a fixed temperature Ts = 400 K.
The surrounding fluid temperature is T_infinity = 300 K.
The convection heat transfer coefficient is h = 25 W/m^2·K.
The adiabatic condition mentioned in the original problem is ignored as it was an error.
Calculate the convective heat flux at the surface using Newton's law of cooling: q'' = h * (Ts - T_infinity).
The expected result is q'' = 25 * (400 - 300) = 2500 W/m^2.
