Compute the heat transfer from a straight rectangular fin with an adiabatic (insulated) tip using the standard adiabatic-tip fin formulas.

Fin material thermal conductivity k = 200 W/(m·K).
Fin length L = 30 mm = 0.030 m.
Fin thickness t = 2 mm = 0.002 m.
Fin width w = 20 mm = 0.020 m.
Convection coefficient on the lateral surfaces h = 25 W/(m²·K).
The fin tip at x = L is adiabatic (dT/dx = 0 at x = L). No convection is applied at the tip.
Convection acts only on the lateral (top, bottom, and side) surfaces of the fin.

Compute the fin parameter m = sqrt(h * P / (k * A_c)), where P is the perimeter of the fin cross-section P = 2*(w + t) and A_c is the cross-sectional area A_c = w * t.
Use the adiabatic-tip total heat transfer formula q = sqrt(h * P * k * A_c) * tanh(m * L) * theta_b, where theta_b = T_b - T_inf is the excess base temperature.
Also compute the fin efficiency eta = tanh(m * L) / (m * L), where the total fin surface area for the efficiency definition is A_s = P * L (lateral surface area only, since the tip is insulated and does not participate in heat transfer).

Express the final heat transfer rate q in terms of theta_b (since no specific base or ambient temperatures are given).
Report the numerical values of m, mL, tanh(mL), fin efficiency eta, and the expression for q with its numerical coefficient.
