The intent of the problem is for you to **use mass conservation (continuity equation) to show that for steady incompressible flow in a constant-area pipe, Q_in must equal Q_out.** The inlet flow rate is 0.01 m³/s. The outlet flow rate of 0.02 m³/s is intentionally inconsistent — you should recognize and correct it using continuity. So Q_out = Q_in = 0.01 m³/s. Then find the velocity at each section.
