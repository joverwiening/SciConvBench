If the drag force is negligible compared to the raindrop's weight, the raindrop would accelerate indefinitely under gravity and never reach a terminal velocity. Terminal velocity is, by definition, the velocity at which drag force exactly balances weight, meaning drag is equal to (not negligible compared to) weight.

Did you mean to calculate the terminal velocity by **equating** the drag force to the weight (the standard approach), or did you intend a different calculation, such as the velocity after falling a certain distance with no drag?
