Calculate the terminal velocity of a spherical raindrop by balancing buoyancy-corrected weight against aerodynamic drag.
Use the force balance equation: (ρ_w - ρ_air) · g · (π D³ / 6) = ½ · ρ_air · V_t² · C_d · (π D² / 4).
Solve this equation for V_t, the terminal velocity.
Use the following parameter values: D = 2 mm (0.002 m), ρ_w = 1000 kg/m³, ρ_air = 1.2 kg/m³, g = 9.81 m/s², C_d = 0.5.
Report the final terminal velocity V_t in m/s.
Show the algebraic rearrangement of the equation and the numerical substitution steps leading to the final answer.
