The task is to calculate the hydrodynamic entrance length for turbulent air flow in a smooth pipe.

The fluid is air flowing turbulently in a smooth pipe.
The Reynolds number is Re = 50,000.
The pipe diameter is D = 40 mm (0.04 m).
The formula to use is Le = 0.06 × Re × D.
Compute Le = 0.06 × 50,000 × 0.04 m.
Report the hydrodynamic entrance length in meters.
