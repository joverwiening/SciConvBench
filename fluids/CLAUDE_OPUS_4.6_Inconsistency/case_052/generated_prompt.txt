Solve the steady-state 1D heat conduction equation in a slab.
The governing equation is d²T/dx² = 0, which is the steady-state reduction of dT/dt = alpha * d²T/dx².
The slab has a thickness (length) of L = 1.0 meter.
The boundary condition at the left face (x = 0) is a fixed temperature of T = 100 degrees Celsius (Dirichlet condition).
The boundary condition at the right face (x = L) is a fixed temperature of T = 0 degrees Celsius (Dirichlet condition).
Use 11 equally spaced spatial grid points (nodes), giving 10 intervals and a grid spacing of dx = 0.1 meters.
Solve the resulting system of linear equations using a finite difference method (second-order central difference for d²T/dx²).
The expected analytical solution is a linear temperature profile: T(x) = 100 * (1 - x/L).
Compare the numerical solution against the analytical solution.
Output the temperature at each grid point and report the maximum absolute error between the numerical and analytical solutions.
