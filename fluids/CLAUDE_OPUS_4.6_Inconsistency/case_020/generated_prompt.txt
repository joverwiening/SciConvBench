Compute the stagnation-to-static pressure ratio (p0/p) for a subsonic flow at Mach number M = 0.5 using the isentropic flow relation (Saint-Venant equation), not the Rayleigh-Pitot formula.
Use the ratio of specific heats γ = 1.4, which is the standard value for air.
The isentropic relation to apply is p0/p = (1 + ((γ - 1) / 2) * M^2) ^ (γ / (γ - 1)).
Note explicitly that the Rayleigh-Pitot formula is not applicable here because M = 0.5 is subsonic and there is no detached bow shock ahead of the Pitot tube.
Report the final numerical value of p0/p.
