The pipe has a relative roughness (ε/D) of 0.05.
The Reynolds number is 1000.
Since Re = 1000 is below 2300, the flow is in the laminar regime.
In the laminar regime, the friction factor is independent of relative roughness.
The Darcy friction factor is calculated using the laminar flow formula: f = 64 / Re.
f = 64 / 1000 = 0.064.
The Moody chart confirms this value, as all roughness curves converge to the single laminar line f = 64/Re for Re < 2300.
