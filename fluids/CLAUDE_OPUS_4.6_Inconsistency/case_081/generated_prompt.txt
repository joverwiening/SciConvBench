The task is to compute the Reynolds number for water at 20 degrees Celsius.
Use the density of water rho = 998 kg/m3.
Use the dynamic viscosity mu = 1.0e-3 Pa·s (equivalently, kg/(m·s)).
Use the kinematic viscosity nu = 1.0e-6 m2/s.
The flow velocity V and characteristic diameter D are left as symbolic variables.
Compute the Reynolds number using Re = rho * V * D / mu.
Equivalently, compute Re = V * D / nu.
Both formulations must yield the same result.
V has units of m/s and D has units of m.
The resulting Reynolds number Re is dimensionless.
