Compute the volumetric flow rate Q through a Venturi meter that has significant irreversible losses in the throat and diffuser.
Do not use the ideal Bernoulli assumption (Cd=1); instead, account for losses using a discharge coefficient Cd < 1.
The final formula to derive and present is Q = Cd × A₂ × sqrt(2 Δp / (ρ (1 − β⁴))), where β = d₂/d₁.
A₂ is the cross-sectional area of the throat, equal to π d₂² / 4.
d₁ is the upstream pipe diameter, treated as a symbolic variable.
d₂ is the throat diameter, treated as a symbolic variable.
β is the diameter ratio, defined as β = d₂ / d₁, treated as a symbolic variable.
Δp is the measured pressure drop between the upstream section and the throat, treated as a symbolic variable.
ρ is the fluid density, treated as a symbolic variable.
Cd is the discharge coefficient, treated as a symbolic variable (given or to be determined from experimental data).
If a specific numerical value of Cd is needed for illustration, assume a reasonable value based on best practices for a Venturi meter with significant losses (e.g., Cd ≈ 0.95 or lower).
The main deliverable is the fully symbolic formula for Q with all variables defined.
A brief numerical example with assumed values for illustration is optional but acceptable.
