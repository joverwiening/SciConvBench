Compute the horizontal component of the hydrostatic force by finding the force on the vertical projection of the curved surface (a rectangle of height R and width b), using the standard planar hydrostatic force formula.
Compute the vertical component of the hydrostatic force by finding the weight of the water column above the horizontal projection of the curved surface, which is the weight of water contained in the volume between the free surface, the curved gate, and the horizontal projection area (a rectangle of length R and width b at the bottom).
