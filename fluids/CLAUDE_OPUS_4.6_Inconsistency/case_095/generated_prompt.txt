The task is to compute the momentum flux for fully developed laminar flow in a circular pipe.
Use the momentum correction factor beta equal to 4/3.
Express the momentum flux in general symbolic terms using standard fluid mechanics variables.
The momentum flux formula is M_dot equals beta times m_dot times V, where m_dot is the mass flow rate and V is the mean velocity.
Equivalently, the momentum flux can be written as M_dot equals beta times rho times V_squared times A, where rho is the fluid density, V is the mean velocity, and A is the pipe cross-sectional area.
Substituting beta equals 4/3, the momentum flux is M_dot equals (4/3) times m_dot times V.
Equivalently, M_dot equals (4/3) times rho times V_squared times A.
The cross-sectional area A equals pi times D_squared divided by 4, where D is the pipe inner diameter.
The velocity profile assumed is the standard parabolic Hagen-Poiseuille profile for fully developed laminar flow, which gives rise to beta equals 4/3.
