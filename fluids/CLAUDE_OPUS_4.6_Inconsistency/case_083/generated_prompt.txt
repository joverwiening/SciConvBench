Compute the oblique shock wave angle beta using the small-angle approximation for the following conditions.

The freestream Mach number is M = 3.
The wedge half-angle (flow deflection angle) is theta = 25 degrees.
The Mach angle mu is computed as mu = arcsin(1/M).
The approximate shock wave angle is computed as beta = mu + theta, where both mu and theta are in degrees.
Report the values of mu and beta in degrees.
