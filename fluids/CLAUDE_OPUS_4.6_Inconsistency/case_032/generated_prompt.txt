Compute the Biot number for a steel sphere cooling in air to determine whether the lumped capacitance method is applicable.
The sphere has a radius R = 10 mm (0.01 m).
The convection heat transfer coefficient is h = 80 W/(m²·K).
The thermal conductivity of steel is k_steel = 45 W/(m·K).
The characteristic length for the sphere is L_c = R/3.
Calculate the Biot number as Bi = h · L_c / k_steel.
The Biot number is used to assess whether the solid sphere's internal temperature can be treated as spatially uniform.
If Bi < 0.1, the internal temperature gradients in the sphere are negligible and the lumped capacitance method is valid.
If Bi >= 0.1, the internal temperature gradients are significant and the lumped capacitance method should not be used.
State the computed Biot number value and the resulting conclusion about the applicability of the lumped capacitance method.
