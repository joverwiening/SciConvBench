The heat exchanger is a counterflow configuration.
The hot fluid inlet temperature (Th,in) is 150 degrees Celsius.
The hot fluid outlet temperature (Th,out) is 90 degrees Celsius.
The cold fluid inlet temperature (Tc,in) is 30 degrees Celsius.
The cold fluid outlet temperature (Tc,out) is 80 degrees Celsius.
The overall heat transfer coefficient (U) is 500 W/(m^2·K).
The heat duty (Q) is 100,000 W (100 kW).
Compute ΔT1 as Th,in minus Tc,out, which equals 150 minus 80 equals 70 degrees Celsius.
Compute ΔT2 as Th,out minus Tc,in, which equals 90 minus 30 equals 60 degrees Celsius.
Compute the log-mean temperature difference as LMTD = (ΔT1 - ΔT2) / ln(ΔT1 / ΔT2), which equals (70 - 60) / ln(70 / 60).
Compute the required heat transfer area as A = Q / (U × LMTD).
Display the values of ΔT1, ΔT2, LMTD, and the required area A as output.
