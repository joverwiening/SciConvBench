The task is to verify whether a stagnation-point pressure coefficient of Cp = 1.5 is correct for incompressible flow around a body using Bernoulli's equation.

Apply Bernoulli's equation between a point in the freestream and the stagnation point on the body.
Assume steady, incompressible, inviscid flow along a streamline.
The freestream conditions are denoted by subscript infinity: pressure p_inf, velocity V_inf, and density rho.
At the stagnation point, the velocity is zero by definition.
Bernoulli's equation gives: p_inf + 0.5 * rho * V_inf^2 = p_stag + 0.5 * rho * (0)^2.
Therefore p_stag = p_inf + 0.5 * rho * V_inf^2.
The pressure coefficient is defined as Cp = (p - p_inf) / (0.5 * rho * V_inf^2).
Substituting the stagnation pressure: Cp = (p_stag - p_inf) / (0.5 * rho * V_inf^2) = (0.5 * rho * V_inf^2) / (0.5 * rho * V_inf^2) = 1.0.
The correct stagnation-point pressure coefficient from Bernoulli's equation is Cp = 1.0, not 1.5.
Conclude that the given value of Cp = 1.5 is incorrect for incompressible flow; Bernoulli's equation proves the maximum pressure coefficient at a stagnation point is exactly 1.0.
