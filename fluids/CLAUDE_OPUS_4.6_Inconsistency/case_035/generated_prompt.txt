One potential issue: Bernoulli's equation in its standard form applies along streamlines in irrotational flow, or along streamlines in rotational flow. A forced vortex has non-zero vorticity, so standard Bernoulli can only be applied along a streamline. In a forced vortex, streamlines are circles (constant r), not radial lines. Applying Bernoulli along a radial line in rotational flow is not strictly valid — one should use the radial momentum equation instead (which gives the same result in this case).
