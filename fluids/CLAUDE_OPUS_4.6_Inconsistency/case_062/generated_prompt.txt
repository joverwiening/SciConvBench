The task is to compute the sequent (conjugate) depth ratio for a hydraulic jump in a rectangular open channel.
The upstream Froude number is Fr1 = 2.0.
The flow is supercritical upstream of the jump.
Use the standard Belanger momentum equation for a rectangular channel: y2/y1 = (1/2) * (-1 + sqrt(1 + 8 * Fr1^2)).
Substitute Fr1 = 2.0 into the equation and compute the numerical value of y2/y1.
Express the final answer as the dimensionless ratio y2/y1.
