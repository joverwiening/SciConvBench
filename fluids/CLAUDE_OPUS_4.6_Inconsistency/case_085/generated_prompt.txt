The simulation is a counterflow heat exchanger area calculation.
The heat exchanger operates in counterflow configuration.
Define ΔT1 = Th,in - Tc,out.
Define ΔT2 = Th,out - Tc,in.
Compute LMTD = (ΔT1 - ΔT2) / ln(ΔT1 / ΔT2).
If ΔT1 equals ΔT2, set LMTD equal to ΔT1 to handle the special case where the logarithm is undefined.
Treat the hot-side inlet temperature (Th,in), hot-side outlet temperature (Th,out), cold-side inlet temperature (Tc,in), and cold-side outlet temperature (Tc,out) as general input parameters.
Treat the overall heat transfer coefficient (U) as a general input parameter.
Treat the heat duty (Q) as a general input parameter.
Compute the required heat exchanger area as A = Q / (U * LMTD).
Print the computed required area to the console.
