Solve the partial differential equation u_t = nu * u_xx + rho * u*(1 - u).
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundaries.
The time domain is t in [0, 1.0].
The parameter nu is 10.0.
The parameter rho is 2.0.
The boundary conditions are periodic.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = 0.25 + 0.1*sin(2*pi*x).
The solution will be computed numerically.
Use a uniform periodic spatial grid with Nx=512 points.
The grid points are x_j = j/Nx for j=0,...,Nx-1.
The spatial step size is dx = 1/Nx.
Use a fixed time step of dt=0.001.
Perform Nt=1000 time steps to reach the final time T=1.0.
Advance the solution in time using the Strang splitting method for each time step dt.
The Strang splitting consists of three steps: a half-step of diffusion, a full-step of reaction, and another half-step of diffusion.
The diffusion half-step of duration dt/2 is solved exactly in Fourier space.
The Fourier space update for the diffusion step is u_hat(k) <- u_hat(k) * exp(-nu*k^2*(dt/2)), where k are the angular wavenumbers.
The reaction full-step of duration dt is solved exactly for the logistic ODE at each grid point.
The update for the reaction step is u <- (u*exp(rho*dt)) / (1 + u*(exp(rho*dt) - 1)).
Compute the final solution u(x,T=1.0).
