Solve the partial differential equation u_t = nu * u_xx + rho * u*(1 - u).
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions.
The time domain is t in [0, 1.0].
The parameter nu is 0.5.
The parameter rho is 2.0.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = 0.25 + 0.1*sin(2*pi*x).
The PDE will be solved numerically using the Strang splitting method for time integration.
Each time step dt consists of a half-step of diffusion, a full step of reaction, and another half-step of diffusion.
The diffusion steps have a duration of dt/2 and are solved exactly in Fourier space using the update rule u_hat(k) <- u_hat(k) * exp(-nu*k^2*(dt/2)).
The wavenumbers k are defined as 2*pi*fftfreq(Nx, d=dx).
The reaction step has a duration of dt and is solved exactly for the logistic ODE using the update rule u <- (u*exp(rho*dt)) / (1 + u*(exp(rho*dt) - 1)).
Use a uniform periodic spatial grid with Nx=512 points, where x_j = j/Nx for j=0,...,Nx-1.
The spatial step size is dx = 1/Nx.
Use a fixed time step of dt = 0.001 for Nt = 1000 steps to reach the final time T = 1.0.
