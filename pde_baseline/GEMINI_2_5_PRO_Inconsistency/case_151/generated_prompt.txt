The time domain is t in [0, 1.0].
The viscosity parameter nu is 2.0.
The boundary conditions are periodic.

The numerical solution should be computed on a uniform periodic grid with Nx=512 points.

The spatial step size is dx = 1/Nx.
The time integration method is Strang splitting.
Each time step dt consists of a half-step of diffusion, a full step of advection, and another half-step of diffusion.
The diffusion half-step of duration dt/2 is solved exactly in Fourier space using the formula u_hat(k) <- u_hat(k) * exp(-nu*k^2*(dt/2)).
The angular wavenumbers are k = 2*pi*fftfreq(Nx, d=dx).
The advection full-step of duration dt is solved using a finite-volume method with a Rusanov flux.
The flux function is f(u) = 0.5*u^2.
The Rusanov flux between states u_left and u_right is F = 0.5*(f(u_left) + f(u_right)) - 0.5*alpha*(u_right - u_left), where alpha = max(abs(u_left), abs(u_right)).
The finite volume update for cell j is u_new[j] = u[j] - (dt/dx)*(F_plus - F_minus), where F_plus and F_minus are the fluxes at the right and left interfaces of the cell, respectively.

Finally, set the actual time step to dt = T/Nt to ensure the final time is reached exactly.
