Solve the 1D viscous Burgers' equation numerically.
The PDE is u_t + ∂_x( 0.5*u^2 ) = nu * u_xx.
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions.
The time domain is t in [0, 1.0].

The spatial step size is dx=1/Nx.
Use the Strang splitting method for time integration.
Each time step dt consists of a half-step of diffusion, a full step of advection, and another half-step of diffusion.
The diffusion step u_t = nu * u_xx is solved for a duration of dt/2 in Fourier space.
The Fourier transform of the solution u_hat(k) is updated by multiplying with exp(-nu*k^2*(dt/2)).
The wave numbers are k = 2*pi*fftfreq(Nx, d=dx).
The advection step u_t + ∂_x(0.5*u^2)=0 is solved for a duration of dt using a finite-volume method.
The numerical flux is the Rusanov (local Lax-Friedrichs) flux.
The flux between cells with values u_left and u_right is F = 0.5*(f(u_left) + f(u_right)) - 0.5*alpha*(u_right - u_left), where f(u)=0.5*u^2 and alpha = max(abs(u_left), abs(u_right)).
The finite volume update for cell j is u_new[j] = u[j] - (dt/dx)*(F_plus - F_minus), where F_plus is the flux at the right interface and F_minus is the flux at the left interface.
The time step dt is determined by a CFL condition.

The number of time steps is Nt = ceil(T/dt0).
The final time step is dt = T/Nt.
