Solve the partial differential equation u_t = nu * u_xx + rho * u*(1 - u).
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions.
The time domain is t in [0, 1.0].
The parameter nu is 10.0.
The parameter rho is 10.0.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = 0.25 + 0.1*sin(2*pi*x).
Use the Strang splitting method for time integration.
Each time step dt consists of a diffusion half-step of duration dt/2, a reaction full-step of duration dt, and a second diffusion half-step of duration dt/2.
Solve the diffusion steps exactly in Fourier space.
The Fourier space update for a half-step of duration dt/2 is u_hat(k) <- u_hat(k) * exp(-nu*k^2*(dt/2)).
The wave numbers are defined as k = 2*pi*fftfreq(Nx, d=dx).
Solve the reaction step du/dt = rho*u*(1-u) exactly for each grid point.
The update for the reaction step of duration dt is u <- (u*exp(rho*dt)) / (1 + u*(exp(rho*dt) - 1)).
Use a uniform periodic spatial grid with Nx=512 points.
The spatial grid points are x_j = j/Nx for j=0,...,Nx-1.
The spatial step size is dx = 1/Nx.
Use a fixed time step of dt = 0.001.
Perform Nt = 1000 time steps to reach the final time T = 1.0.
Compute the solution u(x,T) at the final time T=1.0.
