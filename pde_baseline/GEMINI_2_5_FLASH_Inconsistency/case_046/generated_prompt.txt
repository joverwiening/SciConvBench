The task is to solve the 1D heat equation numerically.
The PDE is u_t = 0.1 * u_xx + sin(2*pi*x).
The spatial domain is x in [0,1] and the time domain is t in [0,0.5].
The parameter alpha is 0.1.
The boundary conditions are Neumann at x=0 and x=1, specifically u_x(0,t)=0 and u_x(1,t)=0.
The initial condition is u(x,0)=0.
The spatial discretization uses a uniform grid on [0,1] with Nx=200 intervals (201 points including both boundaries).
Second-order central differences are used for u_xx in the interior.
The time integration uses the Crank–Nicolson method for diffusion.
Neumann boundary conditions are incorporated via ghost points: u[-1]=u[1] and u[Nx+1]=u[Nx-1].
The time step is dt=0.001, resulting in 600 steps.
The final simulation time T is 0.5.
The output requires computing u(x, T=0.5) on the grid.
The solution should be saved to "solution_agent_heat_17.npz" containing arrays x, u, and metadata scalars T, dx, dt.
A line plot of u(x,T) should be saved as "solution_agent_heat_17.png".
The minimum value of u, maximum value of u, and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at T should be printed.
