The task is to solve the 1D Viscous Burgers equation numerically.
The PDE is u_t + ∂_x( 0.5*u^2 ) = nu * u_xx.
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions.
The temporal domain is t in [0, 1.0].
The parameter nu is 0.2.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = sin(2*pi*x).
The spatial grid is uniform periodic with Nx=512 points on [0,1).
The grid points are x_j = j*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1 with dx=1/Nx.
The numerical method is Strang splitting for each full time step dt.
The splitting consists of a diffusion half-step (duration dt/2), an advection full-step (duration dt), and another diffusion half-step (duration dt/2).
The diffusion half-step is solved exactly in Fourier space.
The advection full-step for the inviscid Burgers equation u_t + ∂_x(0.5*u^2)=0 uses a finite-volume update with Rusanov (local Lax-Friedrichs) flux.
The time step dt is determined using CFL=0.4 based on an estimated max speed ≈ 1.
dt0 is calculated as CFL*dx/1.0.
Nt is chosen as ceil(T/dt0), and dt is reset to T/Nt to hit the final time T=1.0 exactly.
The final solution u(x,T=1.0) on the periodic grid must be computed.
The solution must be saved to "solution_pdebench_burgers_nu0p2.npz" containing arrays x, u, and scalars nu, T, dx, dt, Nx, Nt.
A line plot of u(x,T) must be saved as "solution_pdebench_burgers_nu0p2.png".
The minimum, maximum, discrete mean value, and discrete L2 norm of u at T must be printed.
