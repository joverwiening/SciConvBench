The task is to numerically solve the 1D Compressible Navier-Stokes equations with no heat conduction.
The unknown fields are rho(x,t), v(x,t), and p(x,t).
The PDE is given in conservative form: rho_t + (m)_x = 0, m_t + (m^2/rho + p - sigma)_x = 0, E_t + ( (E+p)*v - v*sigma )_x = 0.
Here, m = rho*v, total energy E = p/(Gamma-1) + 0.5*rho*v^2, and viscous stress sigma = (zeta + 4/3*eta) * v_x.
The spatial domain is x in [-1, 1] and the time domain is t in [0, 0.5].
The parameters are Gamma = 1.6666666666666667, eta = 0.1, and zeta = 0.1.
The boundary conditions are periodic at x=-1 and x=1.
The initial conditions are rho(x,0) = 1.0 + 0.2*sin(pi*x), v(x,0) = 0.1*cos(pi*x), and p(x,0) = 1.0 + 0.2*cos(pi*x).
The numerical method uses a uniform finite-volume grid with Nx=400 cells on [-1,1].
Cell centers are x_j = -1 + (j+0.5)*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1, with dx = 2/Nx.
Conservative variables U = [rho, m, E] (cell averages) are evolved.
The equation of state is p = (Gamma-1) * (E - 0.5*m^2/rho), with rho and p clipped to a floor of 1e-12 if needed.
The convective flux uses the Rusanov (local Lax-Friedrichs) numerical flux.
Viscous terms are computed by approximating v_x with a centered difference on cell-centered v values, then sigma = (zeta + 4/3*eta)*v_x.
Sigma at interfaces is approximated by averaging neighboring cell-centered sigma values.
Viscous contributions to momentum and energy fluxes are added at interfaces: F_visc = [ 0, -sigma, -v*sigma ] using interface-averaged v and sigma.
The total interface flux is F_total = F_hat + F_visc.
Time integration uses 2-stage SSP-RK2 (Heun's method).
The time step dt is chosen each step by dt_conv = CFL * dx / max_j( |v_j| + c_j ) and dt_visc = 0.5 * dx^2 / max( zeta + 4/3*eta, 1e-30 ), with dt = min(dt_conv, dt_visc).
The last time step is adjusted to hit the final time T exactly.
Periodic boundary conditions are implemented by wrapping indices.
The CFL number is 0.8.
The output requirements are to compute rho(x,T), v(x,T), p(x,T) on the cell centers.

Save a 3-panel line plot(s) of rho, v, p vs x at T as "solution_pdebench_cns1d_eta0p1_periodic_smooth.png".
Report the min/max of rho and p, and the mean of rho over the domain.
