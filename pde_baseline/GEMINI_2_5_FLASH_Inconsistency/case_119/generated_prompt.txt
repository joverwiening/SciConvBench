The spatial grid is a uniform periodic grid with Nx=512 points on [0,1).
Use x_j = j*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1 with dx=1/Nx.

The time step should use CFL=0.5: dt0 = CFL*dx/abs(q); set Nt=ceil(T/dt0) and then dt=T/Nt.
