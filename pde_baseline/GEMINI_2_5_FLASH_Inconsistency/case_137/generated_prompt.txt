The PDE to solve is u_t + beta * u_x = 0.
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions.
The time domain is t in [0, 2.0].
The parameter beta is 0.4.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = sin(2*pi*x) + 0.5*sin(4*pi*x).
The reference solution is u(x,t) = u0( (x - beta*t) mod 1 ), where u0(x) is the initial condition.
The spatial grid is uniform with Nx=512 points on [0,1), using x_j = j*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1 with dx=1/Nx.
The spatial discretization is 1st-order upwind finite difference for u_x, using the upwind direction determined by the sign of beta.
The time integration method is Explicit forward Euler.
The time step is determined by CFL=0.5, with dt = CFL * dx / abs(beta).
The number of time steps Nt is ceil(T/dt), and dt is then reset to T/Nt to hit the final time exactly.
The simulation computes u(x,T=2.0) on the periodic grid.
The solution is saved to "solution_pdebench_advection_beta0p4.npz" containing arrays x, u, and scalars beta, T, dx, dt, Nx, Nt.
A line plot of u(x,T) is saved as "solution_pdebench_advection_beta0p4.png".
The minimum, maximum, and discrete L2 norm (sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx)) of u at T are printed.
The maximum absolute error and L2 error against the reference solution at T are computed and printed.
