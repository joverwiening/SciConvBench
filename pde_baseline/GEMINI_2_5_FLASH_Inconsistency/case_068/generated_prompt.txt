The task is to numerically solve the KdV equation.
The PDE is u_t + 6*u*u_x + u_xxx = 0.
The spatial domain is x in [0,1) with periodic boundary conditions.
The time domain is t in [0,0.1].
The initial condition is u(x,0)=0.5*cos(2*pi*x).
Use a Fourier pseudo-spectral method for spatial derivatives.
Compute u_x and u_xxx by multiplying FFTs by (ik) and (ik)^3 respectively.
Use explicit RK4 for time integration with a fixed dt.
Compute the nonlinear term 6*u*u_x in physical space.
The grid has Nx=1024 spatial points.
The time step is dt=1e-5, for a total of 12000 steps.
Compute u(x, T=0.1) on the grid.
Save "solution_agent_other_03_kdv.npz" containing arrays x, u, and scalars T, dx, dt.
Save a line plot of u(x,T) as "solution_agent_other_03_kdv.png".
Print min(u), max(u), and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at T.
