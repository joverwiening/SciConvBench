The task is to solve the 1D Viscous Burgers equation numerically.
The PDE is u_t + ∂_x( 0.5*u^2 ) = nu * u_xx.
The parameter nu is 0.01.
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1).
The boundary conditions are periodic: u(0,t) = u(1,t) for all t.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = sin(2*pi*x).
The final time T for the simulation is 1.0.
The spatial grid is uniform with Nx=512 points.
The numerical method is Strang splitting each full time step dt.
The diffusion half-step (duration dt/2) is solved exactly in Fourier space.
The advection full-step (duration dt) for the inviscid Burgers equation is solved using a finite-volume update with Rusanov flux.
The time step is determined by CFL=0.4 based on an estimated max speed of 1.
The initial time step dt0 is CFL*dx/1.0.
The number of time steps Nt is ceil(T/dt0).
The final time step dt is T/Nt to hit the final time exactly.
The output requires computing u at the final time T on the grid.
The solution must be saved to "solution_pdebench_burgers_nu0p01.npz" containing arrays x, u, and scalars nu, T, dx, dt, Nx, Nt.
A line plot of u(x,T) must be saved as "solution_pdebench_burgers_nu0p01.png".
The report must print min(u), max(u), the discrete mean value mean(u), and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at T.
