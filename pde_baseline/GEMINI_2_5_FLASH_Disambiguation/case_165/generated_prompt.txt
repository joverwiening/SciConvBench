The second PDE equation is m_t plus (m squared divided by rho plus p minus sigma)_x equals 0.

For x less than 0, rho_L is 0.445, v_L is 0.698, and p_L is 3.528.
For x greater than or equal to 0, rho_R is 0.5, v_R is 0.0, and p_R is 0.571.

For viscous terms, v_x should be computed with a centered difference.
Sigma at interfaces should be approximated by averaging.
The numerical method for time integration is 2-stage SSP-RK2 (Heun's method) on the semi-discrete finite-volume system.

The output requirements include computing rho(x,T), v(x,T), and p(x,T) on the cell centers.
