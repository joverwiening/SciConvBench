The task is to solve the 1D KdV-type equation numerically.
The PDE is u_t + (q**2)*u_xxx + u*u_x = 0.
The unknown field is u(x,t).
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions.
The temporal domain is t in [0, 0.2].
The parameter q is 0.1.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = cos(2*pi*x).
The time integration method is RK4.
The spatial discretization method is Fourier spectral derivative for u_x and u_xxx.
The spatial resolution is Nx = 512.
The time step size is dt = 0.0001.
Compute u(x,T=0.2) on the periodic grid.
Save "solution_molk_017.npz" containing arrays: x (1D length Nx), u (1D length Nx), and scalars T, dx, Nx plus the parameter scalars.
Save a line plot of u(x,T) as "solution_molk_017.png".
Print min(u), max(u), mean(u), and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at T.
