The task is to solve the 1D reaction-diffusion PDE u_t = q1*u_xx + q2*u*(1-u) numerically.
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions, and the temporal domain is t in [0, 1.0].
The parameters are q1 = 0.01 and q2 = 5.0.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = 0.25 + 0.1*sin(2*pi*x).
The spatial discretization for the u_xx term is 2nd-order central differences.
The temporal discretization method is IMEX Euler, with diffusion handled implicitly and reaction handled explicitly.
The number of spatial grid points is Nx = 512.
The time step size is dt = 0.001.
Compute u(x,T=1.0) on the periodic grid.
Save "solution_molk_020.npz" containing arrays: x (1D length Nx), u (1D length Nx), and scalars T, dx, Nx plus the parameter scalars.
Save a line plot of u(x,T) as "solution_molk_020.png".
Print min(u), max(u), mean(u), and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at T.
