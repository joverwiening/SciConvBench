Q: What is the exact formula you want to use for the Taylor start to compute u^1?
A: The PDE is u_tt = c^2*u_xx - m^2*u.
For the Taylor start, u^1 = u^0 + dt*u_t^0 + 0.5*dt^2*u_tt^0.
Substitute u_tt^0 = c^2*u_xx^0 - m^2*u^0.
So, u^1 = u^0 + dt*u_t^0 + 0.5*dt^2*(c^2*u_xx^0 - m^2*u^0).
