The task is to solve the 1D Viscous Burgers equation numerically.
The PDE is u_t + ∂_x( 0.5*u^2 ) = nu * u_xx.
The parameter nu is 0.2.
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions u(0,t) = u(1,t) for all t.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = sin(2*pi*x).
The final time T for the simulation is 1.0.
The numerical method uses a uniform spatial grid with Nx=512 points.
The time integration uses Strang splitting for each full time step dt.
The diffusion half-step (duration dt/2) is solved exactly in Fourier space.
The advection full-step (duration dt) for the inviscid Burgers equation u_t + ∂_x(0.5*u^2)=0 uses a finite-volume update with Rusanov (local Lax-Friedrichs) flux.
The time step dt is determined by CFL=0.4 based on an estimated max speed of 1.0.
dt0 is calculated as CFL*dx/1.0.
Nt is ceil(T/dt0), and dt is reset to T/Nt to hit the final time exactly.
The output requires computing u at the final time T on the grid.
Save "solution_pdebench_burgers_nu0p2.npz" containing arrays: x (1D length Nx), u (1D length Nx), and scalars nu, T, dx, dt, Nx, Nt.
Save a line plot of u(x,T) as "solution_pdebench_burgers_nu0p2.png".
Report min(u), max(u), the discrete mean value mean(u), and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at T.
