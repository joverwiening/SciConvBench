The task is to solve the 1D porous medium equation u_t = ∂_xx( u^m ) numerically.
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions, and the temporal domain is t in [0, 0.2].
The parameter m is 2.0.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = 0.5 + 0.2*cos(2*pi*x).
The spatial discretization uses 2nd-order central differences for w_xx where w = u^m.
The temporal discretization uses Explicit RK4.
The number of spatial grid points is Nx = 256.
The time step size is dt = 0.0001.
The final solution u(x,T=0.2) on the periodic grid must be computed.
The results must be saved to "solution_molk_013.npz" containing arrays x, u, and scalars T, dx, Nx, and m.
A line plot of u(x,T) must be saved as "solution_molk_013.png".
The minimum, maximum, mean, and discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) of u at T must be printed.
