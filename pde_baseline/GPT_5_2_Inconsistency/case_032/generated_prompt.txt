Solve the 1D heat equation u_t = 0.2 u_xx for u(x,t) on x in [0,1] and t in [0,0.3] with alpha=0.2.

Discretize u_xx in the interior with 2nd-order central differences.
Integrate in time with Crank–Nicolson using dt=0.0005 for 720 time steps to reach T=0.3.

Compute the numerical solution u(x,T=0.3) on the grid.

Print diagnostics at T: min(u), max(u), discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx), the max absolute error vs u_exact, the L2 error vs u_exact, and r = alpha*dt/dx^2.
Save a NumPy .npz file named solution_agent_heat_03.npz containing arrays x (1D) and u (1D, same length as x) plus metadata scalars T, dx, dt.
