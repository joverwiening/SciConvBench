Q: What value should I use for the diffusion coefficient in the heat equation - should I use the given alpha = 0.05 or the coefficient 0.05 that appears directly in the PDE u_t = 0.05 * u_xx?
A: Use the coefficient 0.05 that appears directly in the PDE u_t = 0.05 * u_xx. The alpha = 0.05 parameter is just confirming that this is the diffusion coefficient value.
