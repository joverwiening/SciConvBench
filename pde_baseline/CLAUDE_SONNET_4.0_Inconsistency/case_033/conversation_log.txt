Q: What specific time-stepping scheme should I use for the Crank-Nicolson method - should I use a standard implicit-explicit approach or do you want me to solve the full implicit system at each time step?
A: Use the full implicit system at each time step. Crank-Nicolson is a fully implicit method where you solve a linear system (I - dt/2 * A) * u^{n+1} = (I + dt/2 * A) * u^n at each time step, where A is the discrete Laplacian operator.
