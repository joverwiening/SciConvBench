Use uniform spatial grid with Nx=400 intervals (Nx+1 points) and dx=1/Nx.
Apply 2nd-order central differences for spatial discretization of u_xx.
Use 2nd-order central difference in time (leapfrog) for time integration of u_tt.

Handle boundary conditions using zero Neumann via ghost points (mirror values).
Compute the final solution u(x, T=1.0) on the grid.
