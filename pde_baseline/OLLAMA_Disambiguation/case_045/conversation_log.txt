Q: What is the initial condition for u(x,y,0)?
A: The initial condition is  

\[
u(x,y,0)=\sin(\pi\,x)\,\cos(2\pi\,y)\,.
\]

Q: What Dirichlet boundary values should be used at x = 0 and x = 1?
A: The Dirichlet boundaries are homogeneous:

\[
u(0,y,t)=0,\qquad u(1,y,t)=0\;\;\text{for all }y\text{ and }t.
\]
