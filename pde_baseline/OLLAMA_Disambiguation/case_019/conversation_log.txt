Q: What boundary conditions should be applied for u on the edges of the [0, 1] × [0, 1] spatial domain?
A: Apply Dirichlet boundary conditions on all four edges of the square domain, setting u = 0 along the boundaries x = 0, x = 1, y = 0, and y = 1.

Q: What is the initial condition for u at t = 0?
A: The initial condition is  

\[
u(x,y,0)=\sin(\pi\,x)\,\sin(\pi\,y)\,,
\]  

for \((x,y)\) in the domain \([0,1]\times[0,1]\).
