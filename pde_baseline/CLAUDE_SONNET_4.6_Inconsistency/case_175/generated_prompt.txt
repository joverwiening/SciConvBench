Step B: Reaction full-step of duration dt solved exactly pointwise using the logistic ODE exact solution: u <- (u*exp(rho*dt)) / (1 + u*(exp(rho*dt) - 1)).
Step C: Repeat the diffusion half-step exactly as in Step A.
