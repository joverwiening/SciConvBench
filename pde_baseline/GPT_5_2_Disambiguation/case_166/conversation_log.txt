Q: What numerical flux (Riemann solver) should I use for the inviscid terms (e.g., Rusanov/Lax–Friedrichs, HLL, HLLC, Roe)?
A: Use the **Rusanov (local Lax–Friedrichs)** numerical flux for the inviscid (Euler) terms.

- Inviscid flux:
  \[
  F_{\text{conv}}(U)=\begin{bmatrix}
  m\\
  m^2/\rho + p\\
  (E+p)\,v
  \end{bmatrix},\quad v=m/\rho,\quad c=\sqrt{\Gamma p/\rho}
  \]
- Interface wave speed:
  \[
  a=\max\left(|v_L|+c_L,\ |v_R|+c_R\right)
  \]
- Numerical flux:
  \[
  \hat F=\tfrac12\left(F_{\text{conv}}(U_L)+F_{\text{conv}}(U_R)\right)-\tfrac12\,a\,(U_R-U_L)
  \]

Q: What time-step constraint should I use for the viscous term (i.e., the formula for \(dt_{\text{visc}}\) and how to combine it with \(dt_{\text{conv}}\))?
A: Use the viscous stability constraint exactly as:

\[
dt_{\text{visc}} = 0.5\,\frac{dx^2}{\max\!\left(\zeta+\frac{4}{3}\eta,\ 1\times 10^{-30}\right)}.
\]

Compute the convective constraint

\[
dt_{\text{conv}} = \text{CFL}\,\frac{dx}{\max_j\left(|v_j|+c_j\right)},
\quad c_j=\sqrt{\Gamma p_j/\rho_j},
\]

then take

\[
dt = \min\left(dt_{\text{conv}},\ dt_{\text{visc}}\right),
\]

and adjust the last step so you hit \(T\) exactly.

Q: What specific discretization should I use for the viscous stress term \(\sigma=(\zeta+\tfrac{4}{3}\eta)\,v_x\) at cell interfaces (e.g., compute \(v_x\) with a centered difference using neighboring cell-centered velocities)?
A: Compute \(v_x\) using a **centered difference on the cell-centered velocities**:
\[
(v_x)_j \approx \frac{v_{j+1}-v_{j-1}}{2\,dx},
\quad v_j=\frac{m_j}{\rho_j}.
\]
Then compute the **cell-centered** viscous stress
\[
\sigma_j = \left(\zeta+\frac{4}{3}\eta\right)(v_x)_j.
\]
Obtain the **interface** stress by **averaging neighboring cell-centered values**:
\[
\sigma_{j+1/2}=\tfrac12(\sigma_j+\sigma_{j+1}).
\]
Use interface-averaged velocity similarly,
\[
v_{j+1/2}=\tfrac12(v_j+v_{j+1}),
\]
and add viscous flux at interfaces as
\[
F_{\text{visc},\,j+1/2} = \big[0,\ -\sigma_{j+1/2},\ -v_{j+1/2}\sigma_{j+1/2}\big].
\]
