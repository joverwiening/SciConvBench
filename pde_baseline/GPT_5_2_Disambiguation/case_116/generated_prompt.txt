Solve the 1D sine-Gordon PDE u_tt + q*sin(u) = u_xx on x in [0,1) with periodic boundary conditions and t in [0,2.0].
Use q = 1.0.
Use periodic conditions u(0,t)=u(1,t) and u_x(0,t)=u_x(1,t).
Use the initial conditions u(x,0) = 0.1*sin(2*pi*x) and u_t(x,0) = 0.
Use a periodic grid with Nx = 512 points on x ∈ [0,1), with dx = 1/Nx and x_j = j*dx for j = 0,…,Nx−1.
Discretize u_xx with a 2nd-order central difference with periodic wrap-around: u_xx(x_j) ≈ (u_{j+1} − 2u_j + u_{j−1})/dx^2 with indices modulo Nx.
Rewrite the PDE as a first-order system with v = u_t: u_t = v and v_t = u_xx − q*sin(u).
Time integrate using classical RK4 with dt = 0.0005 for Nt = 4000 steps so that T = 2.0 exactly.
Use float64 for computation and for saved x and u arrays.
Compute u(x,T=2.0) on the periodic grid.
Save solution_molk_015.npz containing arrays x (1D length Nx) and u (1D length Nx at T), and scalars T, dx, Nx, and q.
Save a line plot of u(x,T) to solution_molk_015.png.
Print min(u), max(u), mean(u), and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u^2)*dx) at T.
