Q: What numerical method should be used to solve this PDE (e.g., upwind finite difference, Lax-Wendroff, spectral, etc.)?
A: Use 1st-order upwind finite difference for spatial discretization and Forward Euler for time integration. Use Nx=512 points, CFL=0.5: dt0 = 0.5*dx/abs(q), Nt=ceil(T/dt0), dt=T/Nt.
