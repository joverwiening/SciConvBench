Solve the 1D Cahn-Hilliard PDE numerically using a Fourier pseudo-spectral method with semi-implicit time integration.

PDE: u_t = -(0.01)**2 * u_xxxx + d^2/dx^2(u**3 - u), periodic boundary conditions.

Domain: x in [0,1) periodic, t in [0, 0.05].

Parameter: epsilon = 0.01.

Initial condition: u(x,0) = 0.1*cos(2*pi*x) + 0.01*cos(6*pi*x).

Use Nx=512 equally spaced points x_j = j/Nx for j=0..511, so dx=1/512.

Use dt=1e-5 and take 5000 time steps to reach T=0.05.

In Fourier space, the wavenumbers are k = 2*pi*n where n are the standard FFT integer frequencies.

The linear operator is L(k) = -epsilon**2 * k**4 + k**2.

The semi-implicit update is: u_hat_new = (u_hat_old + dt * FFT(d^2/dx^2(u**3))) / (1 - dt*L(k)).

Apply 2/3 dealiasing to the u**3 term: after computing FFT(u**3), zero out all Fourier modes with |n| > (2/3)*(Nx/2), then multiply by -(k**2) in Fourier space to get FFT(d^2/dx^2(u**3)).

At T=0.05, compute u(x,T) on the grid.

Save "solution_agent_other_02_cahn_hilliard.npz" containing arrays x (1D, length 512), u (1D, length 512), and scalar metadata T, dx, dt.

Save a line plot of u(x,T) as "solution_agent_other_02_cahn_hilliard.png".

Print min(u), max(u), and the discrete L2 norm sqrt(sum(u**2)*dx) at T=0.05.
