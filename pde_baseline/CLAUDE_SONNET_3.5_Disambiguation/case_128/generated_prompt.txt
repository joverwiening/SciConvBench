Task ID: molk_027
Solve 1D nonlinear convection-diffusion PDE with cubic flux and periodic boundary.
The PDE is u_t = -q1*∂_x((1/3)*u^3) + (q2/pi)*u_xx.
The domain is x in [0,1) periodic and t in [0,0.5].
Parameters are q1=1.0 and q2=0.05.
Initial condition is u(x,0) = 0.5*sin(2*pi*x).
Use Nx=1024 uniform grid points on [0,1).
Use Rusanov flux for convection term and 2nd-order central differences for diffusion.
Implement SSP-RK2 time integration.
Use CFL=0.2 with dt calculation based on max|u^2|<=0.25 and diffusion stability.
Save final solution at T=0.5 in "solution_molk_027.npz" with arrays x and u, and scalars T, dx, Nx, q1, q2.
Create line plot of final state as "solution_molk_027.png".
Print min(u), max(u), mean(u), and L2 norm at final time.
