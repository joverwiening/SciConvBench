Q: Which wave speed should be used in the numerical scheme: the 1.5 implied by the PDE (u_tt = (1.5)² u_xx) or the parameter c = 3 listed under Parameters?
A: Use the wave speed \(c = 1.5\) (the value that appears in the PDE as \((1.5)^2\) and is also given in the Parameters section).
