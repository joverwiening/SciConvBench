Use parameters c=1.0 and m=1.0.

Create a uniform grid with Nx=Ny=256 intervals, giving dx=dy=1/256 and grid points x_i=i*dx, y_j=j*dy for i,j=0,…,256.

Discretize the Laplacian u_xx+u_yy with the standard 5‑point stencil on the uniform grid.  
Integrate in time using a second‑order central difference scheme for u_tt with the time step dt.  
Initialize the solution at the first time level using a Taylor expansion that incorporates the Laplacian and the mass term.  
Advance the solution to the final time T=0.8.
