Q: Which grid convention should be used for the spatial domain: the one where dx = (b − a)/(Nx − 1) with both endpoints included, or the periodic convention where dx = (b − a)/Nx and the endpoint x = b is omitted?
A: Use the periodic convention: dx = (b − a)/Nx with Nx = 1024 points on the half‑open interval [0, 1) (the endpoint x = 1 is omitted).
