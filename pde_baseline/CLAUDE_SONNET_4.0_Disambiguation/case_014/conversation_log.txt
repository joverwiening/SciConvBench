Q: What time stepping scheme should be used for the IMEX method - specifically, should I use forward Euler for the explicit convection term, or a different explicit scheme?
A: Use forward Euler for the explicit convection term. The requirement specifies "1st-order IMEX" which means first-order explicit (forward Euler) for convection and first-order implicit (backward Euler) for diffusion.
