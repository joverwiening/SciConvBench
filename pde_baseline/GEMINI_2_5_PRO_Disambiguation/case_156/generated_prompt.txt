Solve the 1D Compressible Navier-Stokes PDE system without heat conduction.
The unknown fields are density rho(x,t), velocity v(x,t), and pressure p(x,t).
The PDE is given in conservative form for the variables rho, m, and E, where m = rho*v and E = p/(Gamma-1) + 0.5*rho*v^2.
The viscous stress is sigma = (zeta + 4/3*eta) * v_x.
The PDE system is rho_t + (m)_x = 0, m_t + (m^2/rho + p - sigma)_x = 0, and E_t + ( (E+p)*v - v*sigma )_x = 0.
The spatial domain is x in [-1, 1].
The time domain is t in [0, 0.5], so the final time T is 0.5.
The ratio of specific heats Gamma is 1.6666666666666667.
The dynamic viscosity eta is 0.1.
The bulk viscosity zeta is equal to eta, so zeta is 0.1.
Boundary conditions are periodic on x=-1 and x=1.
The initial condition for density is rho(x,0) = 1.0 + 0.2*sin(pi*x).
The initial condition for velocity is v(x,0) = 0.1*cos(pi*x).
The initial condition for pressure is p(x,0) = 1.0 + 0.2*cos(pi*x).
The numerical simulation will use a uniform finite-volume grid with 400 cells (Nx=400).
The cell width is dx = (1 - (-1)) / 400.
The cell centers are x_j = -1 + (j+0.5)*dx for j=0,...,399.
The conservative variables U = [rho, m, E] are evolved as cell averages.
The equation of state is p = (Gamma-1) * (E - 0.5*m^2/rho).
If rho or p become negative during the simulation, clip them to a small positive floor of 1e-12.
The convective part of the flux is handled by the Rusanov (local Lax-Friedrichs) numerical flux.
The viscous term v_x is computed using a centered difference on the cell-centered velocity values.
The viscous stress sigma is then computed at cell centers.
The viscous stress at cell interfaces is approximated by averaging the values from the two neighboring cells.
The total numerical flux at an interface is the sum of the Rusanov convective flux and the viscous flux.
Time integration is performed using the 2-stage SSP-RK2 (Heun's method).
The time step dt is calculated at each step as the minimum of a convective and a viscous time step.
The convective time step is dt_conv = CFL * dx / max_j( |v_j| + c_j ), where c_j is the sound speed in cell j.
The Courant-Friedrichs-Lewy (CFL) number is 0.8.
The viscous time step is dt_visc = 0.5 * dx^2 / max( zeta + 4/3*eta, 1e-30 ).
The final time step is adjusted to ensure the simulation stops exactly at T=0.5.
Periodic boundary conditions are implemented by wrapping indices for ghost cells.
Compute the final solution for rho(x,T), v(x,T), and p(x,T) on the cell centers.
