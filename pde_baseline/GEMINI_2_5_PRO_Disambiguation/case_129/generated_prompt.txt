Solve the partial differential equation u_t = -k*∂_x( (1/3)*u^3 ).
The spatial domain is x in [0, 1) with periodic boundary conditions.
The time domain is t in [0, 0.2].
The parameter k is 1.0.
The initial condition is u(x,0) = 0.5*sin(2*pi*x).
Use a uniform periodic spatial grid with Nx=1024 points.
The grid points are defined as x_j = j*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1, with dx=1/Nx.
Use a conservative finite-volume method for spatial discretization.
The numerical flux should be the Rusanov flux for the physical flux f(u)=(1/3)u^3.
Use the SSP-RK2 method for time integration.
The time step dt should be calculated based on a CFL number of 0.2.
First, estimate an initial time step dt0 = CFL*dx/max|f'(u)|, where f'(u) = u^2 and the maximum is estimated from the initial condition where |u|<=0.5.
Then, determine the number of time steps as Nt = ceil(T/dt0), where T=0.2.
The final time step is dt = T/Nt.
Compute the solution u(x,t) at the final time T=0.2.
