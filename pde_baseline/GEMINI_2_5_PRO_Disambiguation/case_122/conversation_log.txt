Q: What numerical method should be used for the time integration?
A: Use a deterministic operator-splitting method with a time step of dt=0.001. The steps are:
1) Diffusion Crank-Nicolson step: solve (I - 0.5*dt*q1*L) u_tilde^{n+1} = (I + 0.5*dt*q1*L) u^n, where L is the periodic 2nd-order Laplacian.
2) Reaction exact step: u^{n+1} = exp(q2*dt) * u_tilde^{n+1}.
