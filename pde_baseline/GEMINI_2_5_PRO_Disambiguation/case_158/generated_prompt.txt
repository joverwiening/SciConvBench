Solve the 1D Compressible Navier-Stokes equations without heat conduction.
The unknown fields are rho(x,t), v(x,t), and p(x,t).
The conservative variables are U = [rho, m, E], where m = rho*v and E = p/(Gamma-1) + 0.5*rho*v^2.
The viscous stress is sigma = (zeta + 4/3*eta) * v_x.
The PDE in conservative form is rho_t + (m)_x = 0.
The PDE in conservative form is m_t + (m^2/rho + p - sigma)_x = 0.
The PDE in conservative form is E_t + ( (E+p)*v - v*sigma )_x = 0.
The spatial domain is x in [-1, 1].
The time domain is t in [0, 0.5].
The final time T is 0.5.
The ratio of specific heats Gamma is 1.6666666666666667.
The dynamic viscosity eta is 1e-08.
The bulk viscosity zeta is equal to eta, 1e-08.
The boundary conditions are periodic on x=-1 and x=1.
The initial condition for density is rho(x,0) = 1.0 + 0.2*sin(pi*x).
The initial condition for velocity is v(x,0) = 0.1*cos(pi*x).
The initial condition for pressure is p(x,0) = 1.0 + 0.2*cos(pi*x).
Use a uniform finite-volume grid with Nx=400 cells.
The cell centers are x_j = -1 + (j+0.5)*dx for j=0,...,Nx-1.
Evolve the cell averages of the conservative variables U = [rho, m, E].
Use the equation of state p = (Gamma-1) * (E - 0.5*m^2/rho).
Enforce positivity by clipping rho and p to a minimum value of 1e-12 if they fall below it.
Use the Rusanov (local Lax-Friedrichs) numerical flux for the inviscid part of the equations.
Compute the viscous term v_x using a centered difference on the cell-centered velocity values.
Approximate the viscous stress sigma at cell interfaces by averaging the values from neighboring cells.
The total numerical flux at an interface is the sum of the Rusanov flux and the viscous flux contribution.
The viscous flux vector is [0, -sigma, -v*sigma], using interface-averaged v and sigma.
Use the 2-stage SSP-RK2 method (Heun's method) for time integration.
The time step dt is the minimum of a convective and a viscous time step criterion.
The convective time step is dt_conv = CFL * dx / max_j( |v_j| + c_j ).
The viscous time step is dt_visc = 0.5 * dx^2 / max( zeta + 4/3*eta, 1e-30 ).
Use a reasonable CFL number based on best practices for the SSP-RK2 scheme.
Adjust the final time step to land exactly at T=0.5.
