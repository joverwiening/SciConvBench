Solve the partial differential equation u_t + u*u_x = 0.005*u_xx.
The spatial domain is x in [0,1) with periodic boundary conditions.
The time domain is t in [0,0.2].
The initial condition is u(x,0) = 0.5 + sin(2*pi*x).
The equation is discretized in its conservative form u_t + (f(u))_x = nu*u_xx, where f(u)=0.5*u^2 and nu=0.005.
The convective term (f(u))_x is discretized using a finite-volume method with the Rusanov flux.
The Rusanov flux is F_{i+1/2} = 0.5*(f(u_i)+f(u_{i+1})) - 0.5*alpha_{i+1/2}*(u_{i+1}-u_i).
The wave speed estimate is alpha_{i+1/2} = max(|u_i|, |u_{i+1}|).
The discrete convective term is (F_{i+1/2}-F_{i-1/2})/dx.
The diffusive term u_xx is discretized using a second-order central difference, (u_{i+1}-2u_i+u_{i-1})/dx^2.
Time integration is performed using a first-order IMEX scheme.
The convection term is treated explicitly and the diffusion term is treated implicitly.
The time-stepping formula is (u^{n+1} - u^n)/dt + (F^n_{i+1/2}-F^n_{i-1/2})/dx = nu * (u^{n+1}_{i+1}-2u^{n+1}_i+u^{n+1}_{i-1})/dx^2.
The resulting sparse linear system for u^{n+1} is solved at each time step.
The spatial grid has Nx=1200 points, with dx=1/Nx.
The time step is dt=5e-5, and the simulation runs for Nt=4000 steps to reach the final time T=0.2.
